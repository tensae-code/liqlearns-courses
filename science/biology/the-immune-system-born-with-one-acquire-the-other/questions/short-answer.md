# The immune system — born with one, acquire the other — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: innate.
**Answer: The innate immune system is the part we are born with.**

**2.** Explain: acquired.
**Answer: The acquired immune system is acquired through vaccination among other routes.**

**3.** Explain: kinds of immunity (student book).
**Answer: The immune system gives different kinds of immunity, born and acquired.**
