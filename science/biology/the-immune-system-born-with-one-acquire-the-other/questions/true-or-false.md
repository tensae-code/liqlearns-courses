# The immune system — born with one, acquire the other — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** The immune system protects the body from outside invaders — bacteria, viruses, fungi and toxins (chemicals produced by microbes).
**Answer: True**

**2.** Working together has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** The innate immune system is the part we are born with.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Acquired has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**5.** Mothers are advised to breastfeed immediately after delivery because the first milk carries antibodies.
**Answer: True**

**6.** Vaccines has nothing to do with this lesson’s topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
