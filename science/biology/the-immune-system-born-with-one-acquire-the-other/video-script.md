Today: The immune system — born with one, acquire the other.

Remember this one thing: the immune system has two parts: the innate system we are born with and the acquired system, gained through vaccination among other routes.

The immune system protects the body from outside invaders — bacteria, viruses, fungi and toxins (chemicals produced by microbes).

The immune system works through organs, cells and proteins working together.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.


Student-book core: Immunity is introduced from the lymphatic system, with the immune system's own cells. The immune system gives different kinds of immunity, born and acquired.
