## The idea
This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only. The guide fixes the two-part framing. (teacher guide — evidence)

## The key facts
**Job.** The immune system protects the body from outside invaders — bacteria, viruses, fungi and toxins (chemicals produced by microbes).

**Working together.** The immune system works through organs, cells and proteins working together.

**Innate.** The innate immune system is the part we are born with.

**Acquired.** The acquired immune system is acquired through vaccination among other routes.

## From the student book — the pupil-taught core

### Student-book key facts
**From lymphatic base.** Immunity is introduced from the lymphatic system, with the immune system's own cells.

**Kinds of immunity.** The immune system gives different kinds of immunity, born and acquired.

**First milk.** Mothers are advised to breastfeed immediately after delivery because the first milk carries antibodies.

**Vaccines.** Vaccines — the COVID-19 vaccine named — give acquired protection.
