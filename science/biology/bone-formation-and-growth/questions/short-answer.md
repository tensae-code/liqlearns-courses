# Bone formation and growth — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: bones form by ossification — cartilage or membrane is gradually replaced by hard bone — and a long bone keeps growing in length at its growth plates until adulthood, while thickening and repair continue through life. formation and development of bones (ossification)**

**2.** Explain Greece's way of teaching this.
**Answer: formation and development of bones (ossification)**
