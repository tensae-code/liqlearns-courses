# Bone formation and growth — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (bones form by ossification — cartilage or membrane is gradually replaced by hard bone — and a long bone keeps growing in length at its growth plates until adulthood, while thickening and repair continue through life) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
