# PCR — polymerase chain reaction — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why the three temperatures must come in the PCR order — separate, bind, build — and what goes wrong if the order is changed.
**Model answer: The strands must separate first, because primers cannot bind a closed double strand; the primers must bind next, because Taq polymerase can only extend from a bound primer; and building comes last, at the temperature Taq works at. Changed in order, no step can do its work — there is nothing bound to extend, or nothing open to bind.**
