# PCR — polymerase chain reaction — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the three steps of one PCR cycle, with their temperatures.
**Answer: Denaturation at about 96°C separates the DNA into single strands; annealing at 55–65°C binds the primers to their matching sequences; extension at 72°C lets Taq polymerase build the new strands — so each cycle doubles the copies.**

**2.** Explain: Indonesia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: PCR is not in Indonesia's Grade 12 student book; the Grade 12 teacher guide's answer key (Bab 2, printed p. 67) states the mechanism in three steps — denaturation (96°C), annealing (55–65°C), extension by Taq polymerase (72°C) — with doubling per cycle.**
