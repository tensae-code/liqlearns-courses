# PCR — polymerase chain reaction — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. In PCR, denaturation happens at about…**
a) 55°C  b) 65°C  c) 72°C  d) 96°C
**Answer: d) 96°C**

**2. Primers bind to the DNA during the step called…**
a) denaturation  b) annealing  c) extension  d) centrifugation
**Answer: b) annealing**

**3. The enzyme that builds new strands in PCR is…**
a) amylase  b) catalase  c) Taq polymerase  d) lipase
**Answer: c) Taq polymerase**

**4. After each full PCR cycle, the number of copies…**
a) stays the same  b) halves  c) doubles  d) triples
**Answer: c) doubles**
