# PCR — polymerase chain reaction — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. PCR needs living cells to copy DNA.**
**Answer: False** — PCR copies DNA in a tube; no living cell is used.

**2. The copies double with every cycle.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Annealing happens at a higher temperature than denaturation.**
**Answer: False** — annealing (55–65°C) is cooler than denaturation (96°C) — cooling is what lets the primers bind.

**4. Taq polymerase works at 72°C because it is heat-stable.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
