# PCR — polymerase chain reaction — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. PCR stands for polymerase _____ reaction.**
**Answer: chain**

**2. At about _____ °C the DNA double strand separates.**
**Answer: 96**

**3. The primers bind during the _____ step.**
**Answer: annealing**

**4. Taq polymerase extends the new strands at _____ °C.**
**Answer: 72**
