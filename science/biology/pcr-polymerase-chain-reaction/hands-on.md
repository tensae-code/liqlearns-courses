# Hands-on — PCR — polymerase chain reaction

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Write the three steps on three cards — 96°C separate, 55–65°C primers bind, 72°C Taq builds. Shuffle them, then lay them in cycle order and repeat the round aloud three times, counting the copies after each round: 2, 4, 8. Then teach the cycle to a companion from the cards alone.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a tube in a thermal cycler shown at three stations in a circle — 96°C with the DNA ladder unzipping into two single strands, 55–65°C with two short primers binding, 72°C with Taq polymerase extending new strands — and below, 1 copy becoming 2, then 4, then 8 — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
