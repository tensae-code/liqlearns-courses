# Lesson plan — PCR — polymerase chain reaction

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** PCR — polymerase chain reaction
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — Grades 11–12 band (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** PCR copies one chosen piece of DNA over and over in a tube by cycling three temperature steps — denaturation at about 96°C, where the double strand separates; annealing at 55–65°C, where primers bind; and extension at 72°C, where Taq polymerase builds the new strands — so the number of copies doubles with every cycle.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier biology of living things, health and the human body, and builds the Grades 11–12 band understanding of this topic.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
