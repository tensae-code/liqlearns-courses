Today: PCR — polymerase chain reaction.

Remember this one thing: PCR copies a chosen piece of DNA by cycling three temperatures, and the copies double every cycle.

Ninety-six degrees separates the strands. Fifty-five to sixty-five lets the primers bind. Seventy-two is where Taq polymerase builds the new strands. Round and round — two copies, four, eight, millions.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
