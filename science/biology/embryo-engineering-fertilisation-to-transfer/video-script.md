Today: Embryo engineering — fertilisation to transfer.

Remember this one thing: embryo engineering works with the early embryo: sperm capacitation and fertilisation with its two blocks to polyspermy, cleavage to the morula and the blastocyst with its inner cell mass, then IVF, embryo transfer and embryo splitting to multiply valuable animals — and to save species down to their last females.

the theory base in full (sperm capacitation, the egg's two blocks to polyspermy, male and female pronuclei, cleavage, morula, blastocyst with inner cell mass and trophoblast, gastrula); then IVF (China's work from the late 1980s, human and cattle technique at world level), embryo transfer (3–4 calves per flush, a dozen or more offspring per donor cow per year against four or five in a lifetime), and embryo splitting (halves best; China's split-embryo work across mouse, rabbit, sheep, goat and cattle); the northern white rhino — two females left in 2018, frozen sperm saved — as the closing problem.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
