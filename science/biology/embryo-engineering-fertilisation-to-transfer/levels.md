# Embryo engineering — fertilisation to transfer — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (embryo engineering works with the early embryo: sperm capacitation and fertilisation with its two blocks to polyspermy, cleavage to the morula and the blastocyst with its inner cell mass, then IVF, embryo transfer and embryo splitting to multiply valuable animals — and to save species down to their last females) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
