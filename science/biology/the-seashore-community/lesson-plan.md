# Lesson plan — The seashore community

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** The seashore community
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — appended to Unit 16 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A seashore community is studied as a whole: the living things of a shore live in zones between the tides, each kind living where it can stand the covering and uncovering of the sea — who lives where on the shore, and why there.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier biology of living things and the cell, and builds the secondary band understanding of this topic.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: ecologys-key-words (what a community is) and relationships-where-someone-loses (how community members affect each other). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the one thing this lesson teaches, in their own words.
2. Explain each key fact of the lesson without looking.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from what the lesson itself taught.

## What you need
Paper and pencil; the learner's own surroundings for examples.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner tries the opening doing task before any explanation (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the idea, then the key facts one by one (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a Fijian seashore in three bands — the dry top shore, the middle shore the tide covers and uncovers, and the low shore by the water — with the living things of each band drawn in their band and labelled
   - ADD GIF: a shore through one tide in steps — the sea rises and covers the low and middle shore, then falls and uncovers them again — the student names each zone as the water leaves it — the student follows it step by step
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the key facts aloud — the student listens and repeats each one. (Audio is a described slot only; no audio file is made in this build.)
3. **Say it to your companion** — the learner explains the lesson aloud (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — made later, when videos are made.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, fill-in-the-blank, short-answer, writing (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the closing doing block (lesson/lesson.md).
