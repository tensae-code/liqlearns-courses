# Hands-on — The seashore community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
At a shore or beach, stand back and look for the bands: the dry top, the middle the tide reaches, and the wet low shore by the water. Name one living thing you can see in each band and say why it can live there. (Study the shore by looking — leave every living thing where it lives.)

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of a Fijian seashore in three bands — the dry top shore, the middle shore the tide covers and uncovers, and the low shore by the water — with the living things of each band drawn in their band and labelled — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
