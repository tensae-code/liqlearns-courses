# The seashore community — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (A seashore community is studied as a whole: the living things of a shore live in zones between the tides, each kind living where it can stand the covering and uncovering of the sea — who lives where on the shore, and why there.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
