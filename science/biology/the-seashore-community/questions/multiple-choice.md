# The seashore community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about the seashore community?
a) A seashore community is studied as a whole: the living things of a shore live in zones between the tides, each kind living where it can stand the covering and uncovering of the sea — who lives where on the shore, and why there.
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) A seashore community is studied as a whole: the living things of a shore live in zones between the tides, each kind living where it can stand the covering and uncovering of the sea — who lives where on the shore, and why there.**

**2.** What does this lesson say about a whole community, in its place?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) A community is all the living things living together in one place.
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) A community is all the living things living together in one place.**

**3.** What does this lesson say about zonation — the shore is banded?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) A shore between the tides is not the same from top to bottom.
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) A shore between the tides is not the same from top to bottom.**

**4.** What does this lesson say about who lives where follows the zone?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Each kind of shore life is found in the zone it can stand.
**Answer: d) Each kind of shore life is found in the zone it can stand.**
