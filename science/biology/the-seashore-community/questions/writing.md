# The seashore community — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining the seashore community, using the lesson's key facts.
**Model answer: A seashore community is studied as a whole: the living things of a shore live in zones between the tides, each kind living where it can stand the covering and uncovering of the sea — who lives where on the shore, and why there. A community is all the living things living together in one place. The seashore study takes one real community — the shore — and studies the whole of it together: not one animal or one plant alone, but every kind in its place, with the conditions of that place. The study is §6.2.2 of the Year 11 book's ecology chapter, and the community is Fiji's own shore — the rocky shore and the coral-reef shore that the same chapter names among Fiji's communities. The learner studies the shore outside the school, not a diagram of a shore in another country.**
