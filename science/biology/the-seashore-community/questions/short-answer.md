# The seashore community — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: a whole community, in its place.
**Answer: A community is all the living things living together in one place. The seashore study takes one real community — the shore — and studies the whole of it together: not one animal or one plant alone, but every kind in its place, with the conditions of that place.**

**2.** Explain: zonation — the shore is banded.
**Answer: A shore between the tides is not the same from top to bottom. It falls into zones: the top of the shore, uncovered for most of the day; the middle shore, covered and uncovered by each tide; and the low shore, uncovered only at the lowest tides. Each zone has its own conditions — how long it is dry, how hard the waves strike, how hot the sun makes it.**
