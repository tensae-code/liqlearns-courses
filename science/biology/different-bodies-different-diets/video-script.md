Today: Different bodies, different diets.

Remember this one thing: energy needs differ by sex, work, and life stage — one diet does not fit everyone.

Women generally need less energy than men, with higher body-fat proportion and lower metabolic rate.

Heavy physical work and athletics demand more energy and more protein.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
