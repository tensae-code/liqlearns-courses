# Different bodies, different diets — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about women and men?
a) Women generally need less energy than men, with higher body-fat proportion and lower metabolic rate
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Women generally need less energy than men, with higher body-fat proportion and lower metabolic rate**

**2.** What does this lesson say about heavy work?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Heavy physical work and athletics demand more energy and more protein
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Heavy physical work and athletics demand more energy and more protein**

**3.** What does this lesson say about particular needs?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Children, nursing mothers and people living with HIV/AIDS have particular needs
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Children, nursing mothers and people living with HIV/AIDS have particular needs**

**4.** What does this lesson say about no single diet?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) One diet does not fit everyone
**Answer: d) One diet does not fit everyone**
