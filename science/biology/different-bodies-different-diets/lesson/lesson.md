# Different bodies, different diets

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Different bodies, different diets. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Heavy work and athletics demand more. Children, nursing mothers and people living with HIV/AIDS have particular needs.

[Picture 21057 — four figures with differently sized plates]

## The key facts
**Women and men.** Women generally need less energy than men, with higher body-fat proportion and lower metabolic rate.

**Heavy work.** Heavy physical work and athletics demand more energy and more protein.

**Particular needs.** Children, nursing mothers and people living with HIV/AIDS have particular needs.

**No single diet.** One diet does not fit everyone.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words: energy needs differ by sex, work, and life stage — one diet does not fit everyone. Use each key fact once. Then let your companion explain it back to you.
