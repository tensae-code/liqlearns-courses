# Energy nutrients — carbohydrates, fats and oils

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Energy nutrients — carbohydrates, fats and oils. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Carbohydrates are made by green plants as starch through photosynthesis and stored in animals as glycogen.

[Picture 21054 — local energy foods with their energy values]

## The key facts
**Carbohydrates.** Carbohydrates (carbon, hydrogen, oxygen) are the main energy source, at about 16 kJ per gram, abundant in potatoes and cereals.

**Storage.** Plants store carbohydrate as starch; animals store it as glycogen.

**Fats and oils.** Fats and oils give about 37 kJ per gram; fats come from animals and are solid at room temperature, oils come from plants and are liquid.

**Sources.** Fat sources include meat, milk and oilseeds.
