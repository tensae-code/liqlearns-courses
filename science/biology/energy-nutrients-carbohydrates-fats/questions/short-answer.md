# Energy nutrients — carbohydrates, fats and oils — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: fats and oils.
**Answer: Fats and oils give about 37 kJ per gram; fats come from animals and are solid at room temperature, oils come from plants and are liquid.**

**2.** Explain: sources.
**Answer: Fat sources include meat, milk and oilseeds.**
