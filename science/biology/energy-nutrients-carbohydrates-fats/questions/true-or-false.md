# Energy nutrients — carbohydrates, fats and oils — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Carbohydrates (carbon, hydrogen, oxygen) are the main energy source, at about 16 kJ per gram, abundant in potatoes and cereals.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Storage has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Fats and oils give about 37 kJ per gram; fats come from animals and are solid at room temperature, oils come from plants and are liquid.
**Answer: True**
