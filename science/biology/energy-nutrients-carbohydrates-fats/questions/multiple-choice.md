# Energy nutrients — carbohydrates, fats and oils — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about carbohydrates?
a) Carbohydrates (carbon, hydrogen, oxygen) are the main energy source, at about 16 kJ per gram, abundant in potatoes and cereals
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Carbohydrates (carbon, hydrogen, oxygen) are the main energy source, at about 16 kJ per gram, abundant in potatoes and cereals**

**2.** What does this lesson say about storage?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Plants store carbohydrate as starch; animals store it as glycogen
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Plants store carbohydrate as starch; animals store it as glycogen**

**3.** What does this lesson say about fats and oils?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Fats and oils give about 37 kJ per gram; fats come from animals and are solid at room temperature, oils come from plants and are liquid
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Fats and oils give about 37 kJ per gram; fats come from animals and are solid at room temperature, oils come from plants and are liquid**

**4.** What does this lesson say about sources?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Fat sources include meat, milk and oilseeds
**Answer: d) Fat sources include meat, milk and oilseeds**
