Today: Energy nutrients — carbohydrates, fats and oils.

Remember this one thing: carbohydrates are the main energy source; fats and oils give about twice the energy per gram.

Carbohydrates (carbon, hydrogen, oxygen) are the main energy source, at about 16 kJ per gram, abundant in potatoes and cereals.

Plants store carbohydrate as starch; animals store it as glycogen.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
