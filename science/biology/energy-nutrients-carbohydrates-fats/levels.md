# Energy nutrients — carbohydrates, fats and oils — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (carbohydrates are the main energy source; fats and oils give about twice the energy per gram) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: comparing this lesson's facts with a neighbouring lesson's, and explaining them without looking.
