# Insect products — honey, silk, dyes and medicines — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Commercial insect products: honey and beeswax, silk, lac, cochineal colouring for food, drink, cosmetics and paint, and tannic acid from wasp galls for leather dyeing and ink; in medicine, honey for burns and wounds and bee and ant venom for joint pain are the traditional uses named, with bee products' wound-healing support noted.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Two locality investigations the student book does not set: whether silkworms are kept locally and how silk fibre is produced from them in Ethiopia, and interviewing traditional healers about whether they use insects or insect products to treat human and animal diseases and what indigenous knowledge lies behind it.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
