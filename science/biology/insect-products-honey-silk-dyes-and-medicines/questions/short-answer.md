# Insect products — honey, silk, dyes and medicines — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about beneficial insects?**
**Answer: Commercial insect products: honey and beeswax, silk, lac, cochineal colouring for food, drink, cosmetics and paint, and tannic acid from wasp galls for leather dyeing and ink; in medicine, honey for burns and wounds and bee and ant venom for joint pain are the traditional uses named, with bee products' wound-healing support noted.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added ethiopian investigations?**
**Answer: Two locality investigations the student book does not set: whether silkworms are kept locally and how silk fibre is produced from them in Ethiopia, and interviewing traditional healers about whether they use insects or insect products to treat human and animal diseases and what indigenous knowledge lies behind it.**
