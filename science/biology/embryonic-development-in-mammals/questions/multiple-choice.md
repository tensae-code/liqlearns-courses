# Embryonic development in mammals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the zygote?**
a) A newborn mammal  b) The single cell formed when sperm and egg join  c) The organ that feeds the embryo  d) The mother's milk
**Answer: b) The single cell formed when sperm and egg join**

**2. Where does a mammal embryo develop?**
a) In an egg laid outside the body  b) In water  c) Inside the mother's uterus  d) In a nest
**Answer: c) Inside the mother's uterus**

**3. What does the placenta do?**
a) It stores the mother's milk  b) It digests the embryo's food for it  c) It passes food and oxygen from the mother's blood to the embryo and takes wastes away  d) It becomes the young animal's skin
**Answer: c) It passes food and oxygen from the mother's blood to the embryo and takes wastes away**

**4. What is gestation?**
a) The time a young mammal develops inside its mother before birth  b) The act of fertilisation  c) The first meal of milk  d) The dividing of the zygote
**Answer: a) The time a young mammal develops inside its mother before birth**
