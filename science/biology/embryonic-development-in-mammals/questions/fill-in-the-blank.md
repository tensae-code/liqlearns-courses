# Embryonic development in mammals — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The single cell formed at fertilisation is called the _____.**
**Answer: zygote**

**2. The embryo settles into the wall of the _____.**
**Answer: uterus (womb)**

**3. Food and oxygen reach the embryo across the _____.**
**Answer: placenta**

**4. The time of development inside the mother before birth is called _____.**
**Answer: gestation**
