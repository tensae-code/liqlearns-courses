# Embryonic development in mammals — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: how the embryo is fed inside the mother.
**Answer: The embryo settles in the uterus and the placenta grows there. Across the placenta, food and oxygen pass from the mother's blood to the embryo, and the embryo's wastes pass back — the two bloods do not mix, and the cord joins the embryo to the placenta.**

**2.** Explain: North Macedonia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: North Macedonia's Grade 8 textbook closes its reproduction theme with embryonic development in mammals as a named lesson (Theme 2, printed p. 138), after asexual and sexual reproduction in animals and the development of young animals and birds, and before the theme on reproduction in humans.**
