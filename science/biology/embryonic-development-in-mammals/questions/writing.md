# Embryonic development in mammals — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining how a young mammal develops before it is born.
**Model answer: A mammal begins as one cell, the zygote, formed at fertilisation. The cell divides into an embryo, which settles in the wall of the mother's uterus; there the placenta passes it food and oxygen from the mother's blood and takes its wastes away. When the development time — the gestation — is complete, the young mammal is born and is fed on milk.**
