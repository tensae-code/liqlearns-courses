# Embryonic development in mammals — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. A mammal embryo develops inside its mother's body.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. The mother's blood and the embryo's blood mix freely in the placenta.**
**Answer: False** — the two bloods do not mix; food, oxygen and wastes pass across the placenta.

**3. The zygote is formed by fertilisation.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. After birth, a young mammal is fed on its mother's milk.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
