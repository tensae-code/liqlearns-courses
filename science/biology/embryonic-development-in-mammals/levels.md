# Embryonic development in mammals — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (In mammals the young develop inside the mother's body: after fertilisation the single cell divides again and again, the embryo settles in the uterus and is fed through the placenta until it is born.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a fresh example from the learner's own surroundings and saying why it fits, without looking.
