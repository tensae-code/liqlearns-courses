# Gaseous exchange in plants — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Describe how a plant's gaseous exchange differs between day and night.
**Answer: By day the plant photosynthesises and respires together; photosynthesis outruns respiration, so on balance carbon(IV) oxide is taken in and oxygen given out. At night photosynthesis stops, respiration alone continues, and on balance oxygen is taken in and carbon(IV) oxide given out (Ch. 2, printed p. 56).**

**2.** Explain Kenya's way of teaching gaseous exchange in plants.
**Answer: Kenya teaches it as a balance, not a single flow: the day/night balance of photosynthesis and respiration, and the balance is proved in class — a bicarbonate-indicator investigation for the carbon(IV) oxide, and an oxygen-collection investigation on a water plant for the gas given off in light.**
