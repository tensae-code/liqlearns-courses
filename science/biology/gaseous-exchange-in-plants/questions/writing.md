# Gaseous exchange in plants — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why a plant kept in the dark for a whole day would still be exchanging gases at midnight.
**Model answer: Exchange does not need light, because respiration never stops: the plant's cells go on taking in oxygen and giving out carbon(IV) oxide to release energy. What darkness removes is photosynthesis, the process that in daylight outruns respiration and reverses the net direction — so at midnight the exchange is respiration's own, oxygen in and carbon dioxide out.**
