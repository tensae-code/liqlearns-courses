# Gaseous exchange in plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. At night, a green plant's net gaseous exchange is…**
a) carbon dioxide in, oxygen out  b) oxygen in, carbon dioxide out  c) no exchange at all  d) nitrogen in, oxygen out
**Answer: b) oxygen in, carbon dioxide out — only respiration is running**

**2. The bicarbonate indicator in Kenya's investigation tracks…**
a) oxygen  b) carbon(IV) oxide around the plant  c) water vapour  d) light intensity
**Answer: b) carbon(IV) oxide around the plant**

**3. In bright daylight, photosynthesis compared with respiration…**
a) stops completely  b) runs slower  c) outruns it, so oxygen is given out on balance  d) is reversed
**Answer: c) outruns it, so oxygen is given out on balance**

**4. The gas collected from a water plant in the light is…**
a) carbon(IV) oxide  b) oxygen  c) nitrogen  d) hydrogen
**Answer: b) oxygen**
