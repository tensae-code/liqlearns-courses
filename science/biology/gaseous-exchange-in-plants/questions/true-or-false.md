# Gaseous exchange in plants — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. A plant only exchanges gases in daylight.**
**Answer: False** — respiration continues at night; the balance changes.

**2. Photosynthesis and respiration move the same gases in opposite directions.**
**Answer: True** — that is why the net exchange can reverse.

**3. The bicarbonate indicator proves exchange by changing colour with carbon(IV) oxide.**
**Answer: True** — Kenya's indicator investigation.

**4. At night a plant gives out oxygen on balance.**
**Answer: False** — at night it takes in oxygen and gives out carbon(IV) oxide.
