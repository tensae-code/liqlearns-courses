# Gaseous exchange in plants — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. By day, photosynthesis _____ respiration, so oxygen is given out on balance.**
**Answer: outruns**

**2. The indicator used to track carbon(IV) oxide is a _____ indicator.**
**Answer: bicarbonate**

**3. At night, only _____ continues in the plant.**
**Answer: respiration**

**4. The gas collected from a water plant in light is _____.**
**Answer: oxygen**
