# Synthetic biology and CRISPR — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Synthetic biology designs and builds biological systems as engineering — and CRISPR is its sharpest tool: a system borrowed from bacteria that can cut DNA at a chosen place, so a gene can be removed, changed or replaced with precision.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a fresh example from the learner's own surroundings and saying why it fits, without looking.
