# Hands-on — Synthetic biology and CRISPR

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
On paper, write a three-step 'editing plan' for a made-up example: a plant whose seeds lack a vitamin. Step 1 — find: which gene is the target? Step 2 — cut: what does the guide lead the cutter to? Step 3 — repair: what change do you want the repair to make? Label each step with the CRISPR part that does it.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a strand of DNA with a marked gene, a guide molecule leading a cutting protein to exactly that mark, the cut made, and the strand repaired with a changed letter — editing drawn as find, cut, repair — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
