Today: Synthetic biology and CRISPR.

Remember this one thing: Synthetic biology designs and builds biological systems as engineering — and CRISPR is its sharpest tool: a system borrowed from bacteria that can cut DNA at a chosen place, so a gene can be removed, changed or replaced with precision.

Biology's newest branch does not only study living systems — it builds them. That is synthetic biology.

Its sharpest tool is CRISPR, borrowed from bacteria: a guide leads a cutting protein to one chosen place in the DNA, the cut is made, and the cell's repair can remove, correct or replace a gene. Find, cut, repair — gene editing.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
