# Synthetic biology and CRISPR — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why CRISPR changed gene editing.
**Model answer: Older methods of changing DNA were slow, imprecise and expensive, and could not be aimed at one chosen gene. CRISPR, borrowed from bacteria, can be guided to a single place in any DNA and cut there, so a gene can be removed, corrected or replaced deliberately. Editing became a tool any laboratory can aim.**
