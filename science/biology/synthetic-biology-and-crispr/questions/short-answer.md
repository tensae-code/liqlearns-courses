# Synthetic biology and CRISPR — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: how CRISPR editing works, in three steps.
**Answer: A guide molecule is made to match one chosen place in the DNA and leads the cutting protein (Cas9) there — find. The protein cuts the DNA at that place — cut. The cell repairs the cut, and in the repair the gene can be knocked out, corrected or replaced — repair.**

**2.** Explain: North Macedonia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: North Macedonia's Biology II names synthetic biology and CRISPR technology as their own lesson (Theme 4.2, printed p. 247) at the head of its closing biotechnology and genetic-engineering theme, before plant tissue culture, fermentation production, forensics, bioinformatics and gene therapy.**
