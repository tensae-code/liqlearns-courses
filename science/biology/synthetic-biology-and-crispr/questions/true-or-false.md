# Synthetic biology and CRISPR — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. CRISPR was borrowed from a defence system of bacteria.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Gene editing with CRISPR can target one chosen place in the DNA.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Synthetic biology only describes living things; it never builds anything.**
**Answer: False** — synthetic biology designs and builds biological parts, circuits and organisms.

**4. Biosafety and ethics questions are part of this field's teaching.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
