# Synthetic biology and CRISPR — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is synthetic biology?**
a) The study of fossils  b) Designing and building biological systems as engineering  c) Naming new species  d) Growing plants without soil
**Answer: b) Designing and building biological systems as engineering**

**2. Where does the CRISPR system come from?**
a) It was invented from nothing in a computer  b) From bacteria, which use it against attacking viruses  c) From human blood  d) From yeast used in baking
**Answer: b) From bacteria, which use it against attacking viruses**

**3. What does the guide molecule do in CRISPR editing?**
a) It repairs the DNA  b) It leads the cutting protein to one chosen place in the DNA  c) It copies the whole genome  d) It feeds the cell
**Answer: b) It leads the cutting protein to one chosen place in the DNA**

**4. What can be done when the cell repairs a CRISPR cut?**
a) Nothing can be controlled  b) A gene can be knocked out, corrected or replaced  c) The cell always dies  d) Only whole chromosomes can be removed
**Answer: b) A gene can be knocked out, corrected or replaced**
