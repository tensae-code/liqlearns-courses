# Synthetic biology and CRISPR — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Designing and building biological systems as engineering is _____ biology.**
**Answer: synthetic**

**2. The gene-editing system borrowed from bacteria is called _____.**
**Answer: CRISPR**

**3. The _____ molecule leads the cutting protein to its target.**
**Answer: guide**

**4. Editing follows three steps: find, _____, repair.**
**Answer: cut**
