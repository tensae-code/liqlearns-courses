# Echinodermata and the water-vascular system — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Echinoderms are marine animals with pentaradial (five-sided) symmetry, an internal skeleton of calcareous plates carrying spines, and a water-vascular (ambulacral) system — a network of water canals found in no other phylum — with reduced circulation, no excretory organs, separate sexes and a larval metamorphosis.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by naming what is present and what is missing without looking — water-vascular system present and unique; circulation reduced; excretory organs absent — and the larval metamorphosis between the free larva and the five-sided adult.
