# Echinodermata and the water-vascular system — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining how an animal can move and feed using water pressure instead of muscles and blood alone.
**Model answer: The echinoderm's water-vascular system is a set of canals filled with seawater, ending in rows of tube feet. Water pressure in the canals extends and works the tube feet, so the animal grips, walks and handles food with hydraulic power — a whole system of movement run on water, found in no other phylum.**
