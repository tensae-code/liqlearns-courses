# Echinodermata and the water-vascular system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Echinoderm symmetry is…**
a) bilateral  b) pentaradial — five-sided  c) asymmetrical  d) six-sided
**Answer: b) pentaradial — five-sided**

**2. The water-vascular system is also called the…**
a) ambulacral system  b) radula system  c) nervous net  d) open circulation
**Answer: a) ambulacral system**

**3. The echinoderm skeleton is…**
a) an external shell of two valves  b) internal, of calcareous plates with spines  c) made of chitin  d) absent
**Answer: b) internal, of calcareous plates with spines**

**4. Echinoderms live…**
a) in fresh water  b) on land  c) in the sea  d) inside hosts
**Answer: c) in the sea**
