# Echinodermata and the water-vascular system — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Echinoderms have a well-developed excretory system.**
**Answer: False** — they have no excretory organs — circulation is reduced too.

**2. The sexes are separate in echinoderms.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. The adult echinoderm develops through a larval metamorphosis.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. The water-vascular system is found in molluscs as well.**
**Answer: False** — it is unique to the echinoderms.
