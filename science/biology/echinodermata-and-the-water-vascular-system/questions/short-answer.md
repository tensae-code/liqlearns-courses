# Echinodermata and the water-vascular system — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What is the water-vascular system, and what makes it special?
**Answer: It is a network of water-filled canals (the ambulacral system) that works the echinoderm's tube feet for movement and feeding. It is special because no other phylum has it — it is the echinoderms' signature system.**

**2.** Explain Mozambique's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Mozambique's PESD II animal module (Módulo 3 de Biologia), Lesson 13, teaches phylum Echinodermata by its marks: marine, pentaradial, internal skeleton of calcareous plates with spines, the water-vascular (ambulacral) system, reduced circulation, no excretory organs, separate sexes with larval metamorphosis — and its solutions name the phylum's five classes.**
