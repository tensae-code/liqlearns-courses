# Echinodermata and the water-vascular system — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The water-vascular system is also called the _____ system.**
**Answer: ambulacral**

**2. Echinoderm symmetry, arranged in five parts, is called _____.**
**Answer: pentaradial**

**3. The internal skeleton is made of _____ plates.**
**Answer: calcareous**

**4. Echinoderms are found only in the _____ — they are marine.**
**Answer: sea**
