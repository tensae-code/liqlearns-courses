Today: Echinodermata and the water-vascular system.

Remember this one thing: echinoderms are marine animals with pentaradial (five-sided) symmetry, an internal skeleton of calcareous plates carrying spines, and a water-vascular (ambulacral) system — a network of water canals found in no other phylum — with reduced circulation, no excretory organs, separate sexes and a larval metamorphosis.

The echinoderms live in the sea and they are built on the number five — five arms, five parts, around one centre. Under the skin is a skeleton of hard plates with spines. And they have a system no other animal has: canals full of water — the water-vascular system — that work their many little tube feet, so the animal can walk and feed.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
