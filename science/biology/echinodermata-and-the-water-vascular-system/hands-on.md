# Hands-on — Echinodermata and the water-vascular system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Spread one hand flat, fingers out: five arms around one centre — the pentaradial plan. Now imagine water pumped through tiny canals inside each finger making rows of little feet grip and release underneath: that is the water-vascular system at work.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — a starfish drawn from above with its five arms around a centre, its spiny plated skin shown in a cut corner, and the water canals drawn as a ring with branches running into each arm and down to rows of small tube feet — label its parts, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
