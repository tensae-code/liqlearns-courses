# Bacterial cells — pili, flagella and the three domains — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added cell detail?**
**Answer: Pili (short, hair-like, mainly for attachment to surfaces and so a virulence factor in infection) are distinguished from flagella (long, thin, extending from inside the cell body, for movement); a full prokaryote-vs-eukaryote difference table and a three-domain table (Bacteria / Archaea / Eukarya across nucleus, histones, chromosomes, plasmids, introns, mitochondria, chloroplasts and membrane lipids) consolidate the classification; true sexual reproduction is absent in bacteria, with genetic recombination by conjugation, transduction and transformation instead.**
