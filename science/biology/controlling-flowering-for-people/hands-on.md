# Hands-on — Controlling flowering for people

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: flowering time can be shaped for people — by temperature, day length, growth regulators and cultivation — so flowers and fruit arrive on the festival day and their seasons spread across the market.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: chrysanthemums blooming out of season under lamps in a greenhouse, beside a calendar with a festival day circled and flowers arriving exactly on it. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
