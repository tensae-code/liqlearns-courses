# Relationships where someone loses — competition, predation, parasitism

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Relationships where someone loses — competition, predation, parasitism. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Competition is minus/minus; predation and herbivory are plus/minus; parasitism is plus/minus on a living host.

[Picture 21080 — four small scenes, one per relationship, with its sign]

## The key facts
**Signs.** Interactions between species are signed plus, minus or zero by who gains and who loses.

**Competition.** Competition is minus/minus, when individuals of the same or different species use a limiting resource.

**Predation and herbivory.** Predation and herbivory are plus/minus, when one feeds on another — usually killing it, or grazing its parts.

**Parasitism.** Parasitism is plus/minus, when one feeds on or in a living host without immediately killing it.
