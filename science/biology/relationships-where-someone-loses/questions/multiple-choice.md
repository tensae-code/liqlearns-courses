# Relationships where someone loses — competition, predation, parasitism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about signs?
a) Interactions between species are signed plus, minus or zero by who gains and who loses
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Interactions between species are signed plus, minus or zero by who gains and who loses**

**2.** What does this lesson say about competition?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Competition is minus/minus, when individuals of the same or different species use a limiting resource
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Competition is minus/minus, when individuals of the same or different species use a limiting resource**

**3.** What does this lesson say about predation and herbivory?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Predation and herbivory are plus/minus, when one feeds on another — usually killing it, or grazing its parts
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Predation and herbivory are plus/minus, when one feeds on another — usually killing it, or grazing its parts**

**4.** What does this lesson say about parasitism?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Parasitism is plus/minus, when one feeds on or in a living host without immediately killing it
**Answer: d) Parasitism is plus/minus, when one feeds on or in a living host without immediately killing it**
