# Relationships where someone loses — competition, predation, parasitism — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: predation and herbivory.
**Answer: Predation and herbivory are plus/minus, when one feeds on another — usually killing it, or grazing its parts.**

**2.** Explain: parasitism.
**Answer: Parasitism is plus/minus, when one feeds on or in a living host without immediately killing it.**
