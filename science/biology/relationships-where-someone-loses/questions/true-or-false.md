# Relationships where someone loses — competition, predation, parasitism — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Interactions between species are signed plus, minus or zero by who gains and who loses.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Competition has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Predation and herbivory are plus/minus, when one feeds on another — usually killing it, or grazing its parts.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Parasitism has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
