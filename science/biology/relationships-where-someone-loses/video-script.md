Today: Relationships where someone loses — competition, predation, parasitism.

Remember this one thing: competition, predation, herbivory and parasitism are interactions signed by who gains and who loses.

Interactions between species are signed plus, minus or zero by who gains and who loses.

Competition is minus/minus, when individuals of the same or different species use a limiting resource.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
