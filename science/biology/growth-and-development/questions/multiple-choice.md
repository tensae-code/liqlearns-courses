# Growth and development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Growth is…**
a) any change in shape b) an irreversible increase in size and mass c) movement toward light d) the making of seeds
**Answer: b) an irreversible increase in size and mass**

**2. Development is…**
a) only increase in height b) the ordered change by which an organism matures — differentiation, organs forming, functions switching on c) shedding of leaves d) cell division only
**Answer: b) the ordered change by which an organism matures — differentiation, organs forming, functions switching on**

**3. In plants, growth is centred on the…**
a) flowers b) meristems at root and shoot tips c) bark d) stomata
**Answer: b) meristems at root and shoot tips**

**4. Punjab's Grade 12 book teaches growth and development…**
a) as Ch 19, its own chapter after Reproduction b) inside the ecosystem chapter c) not at all d) in Grade 9 only
**Answer: a) as Ch 19, its own chapter after Reproduction**
