# Growth and development — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: growth and development, and how they differ.
**Answer: Growth is an irreversible increase in size and mass — more and bigger cells, more material laid down — and it can be measured. Development is the ordered maturing of the organism: cells differentiate, organs form and functions switch on until it is a working adult. Size versus readiness: that is the difference.**

**2.** Explain: Pakistan (Punjab)'s way of teaching this.
**Answer: Punjab's Grade 12 SNC book gives growth and development a named chapter of its own, Ch 19 (PDF p. 159), set between Reproduction (Ch 18) and Chromosomes and DNA (Ch 20) — first how organisms are made, then how they grow and mature.**
