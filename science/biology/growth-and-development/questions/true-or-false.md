# Growth and development — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Growth can be measured with a ruler and a balance.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches

**2. Growth and development are exactly the same thing.**
**Answer: False** — growth is increase in size; development is maturing — new structures and abilities; they go together but are not the same

**3. A seed that has germinated can shrink back into the seed.**
**Answer: False** — growth is irreversible — one-way

**4. Punjab gives growth and development a chapter of its own in the second-year book.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches — Ch 19
