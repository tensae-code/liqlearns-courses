# Growth and development — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Growth is an irreversible increase in _____ and mass.**
**Answer: size**

**2. The change by which an organism matures is called _____.**
**Answer: development**

**3. When cells take on different kinds and jobs, they _____.**
**Answer: differentiate**

**4. Plant growth is centred on the dividing tissues called _____.**
**Answer: meristems**
