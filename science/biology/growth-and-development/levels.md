# Growth and development — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Growth is an irreversible increase in size — more cells, bigger cells, more dry mass — while development is the whole change by which a living thing matures: cells differentiate, organs form and take up their work, and the organism becomes able to live and reproduce as an adult. Growth can be measured with a ruler and a balance; development is seen in what the living thing can now do.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
