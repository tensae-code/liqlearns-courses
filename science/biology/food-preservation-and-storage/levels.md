# Food preservation and storage — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (harvests are wasted by incomplete and late harvesting, weather, pests and poor storage — and preservation fights the spoilage organisms: heating, freezing, and salting or sugaring, which preserve by osmosis, drawing water out of the food and its microbes) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: applying the lesson to a new example from the learner's own surroundings, and explaining Nigeria's way of teaching it (Ch. 9, printed p. 155), without looking.
