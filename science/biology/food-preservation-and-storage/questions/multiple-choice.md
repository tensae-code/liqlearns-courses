# Food preservation and storage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which is NOT a cause of harvest wastage in the book?
a) Late harvesting
b) Pests
c) Poor storage
d) Eating the harvest
**Answer: d) Eating the harvest**

**2.** How do salting and sugaring preserve food?
a) By osmosis — drawing water out of the food and its microbes
b) By heating the food
c) By freezing it
d) By adding colour
**Answer: a) By osmosis — drawing water out of the food and its microbes**

**3.** Heating preserves food by…
a) drawing out water
b) killing spoilage organisms
c) adding salt
d) drying it in the sun only
**Answer: b) killing spoilage organisms**

**4.** The book's picture of food storage is…
a) yams in barns
b) rice in silos only
c) fish in freezers
d) milk in bottles
**Answer: a) yams in barns**
