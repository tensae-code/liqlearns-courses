# Food preservation and storage — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Freezing preserves food by drawing water out of it.
**Answer: False** — Freezing halts the spoilage organisms' activity; drawing water out by osmosis is how salting and sugaring work.

**2.** Incomplete and late harvesting waste part of a harvest.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Salt preserves by osmosis.
**Answer: True**
