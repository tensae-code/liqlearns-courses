# Food preservation and storage — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the causes of harvest wastage and how preservation answers them.
**Answer: Harvests are wasted by incomplete and late harvesting, weather, pests and poor storage. Preservation fights the spoilage that follows: heating kills the spoilage organisms, freezing halts their activity, and salting and sugaring draw water out of the food and its microbes by osmosis so they cannot live.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book teaches food production and storage in Chapter 9 (printed p. 155): the causes of harvest wastage first (incomplete and late harvesting, weather, pests, poor storage — yams in barns), then the preservation methods with their principles — heating, freezing, salting and sugaring by osmosis.**
