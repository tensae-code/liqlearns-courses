# Hands-on — Food preservation and storage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find three preserved foods at home or in a shop — something salted, something sugared, something frozen or tinned. For each, say aloud its preservation principle from the book: osmosis, cold, or heat. Then check one stored food (yams, grains, dried goods) and judge its storage against the book's wastage causes.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — yams stacked in a traditional barn, beside preserved foods labelled with their principles: heated (sealed tin), frozen, salted fish, sugared preserve — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
