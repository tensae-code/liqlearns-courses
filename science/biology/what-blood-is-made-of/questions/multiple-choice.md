# What blood is made of — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about plasma and cells?
a) Blood is plasma plus cells  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Blood is plasma plus cells**

**2.** What does this lesson say about red cells?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Red blood cells carry oxygen, with adaptations listed point by point  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Red blood cells carry oxygen, with adaptations listed point by point**

**3.** What does this lesson say about white cells?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) White blood cells defend the body  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) White blood cells defend the body**

**4.** What does this lesson say about platelets and counts?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Platelets help blood clot, and the cell kinds are compared by their counts
**Answer: d) Platelets help blood clot, and the cell kinds are compared by their counts**
