# What blood is made of

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — What blood is made of. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Spin blood and it settles into layers: liquid plasma above, cells below. Each cell kind has its job — red cells fitted point by point for carrying oxygen.

[Picture 22258 — a test tube of settled blood — plasma above, cells below — with the three cell kinds drawn from it]

## The key facts
**Plasma and cells.** Blood is plasma plus cells.

**Red cells.** Red blood cells carry oxygen, with adaptations listed point by point.

**White cells.** White blood cells defend the body.

**Platelets and counts.** Platelets help blood clot, and the cell kinds are compared by their counts.
