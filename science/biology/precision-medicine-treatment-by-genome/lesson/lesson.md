# Precision medicine — treatment by genome

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Precision medicine — treatment by genome. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Precision medicine builds diagnosis and treatment on the patient's own genome: the same drug cures one patient and harms another, so targeted cancer drugs are chosen by the mutation present — and are useless, even harmful, without it — against the obstacles of data privacy, ethics and the sheer volume of sequence data. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from China's People's Education Press biology textbooks, which teach it as a named topic.
