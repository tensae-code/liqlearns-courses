Today: Cell theory — three statements, three scientists.

Remember this one thing: cell theory says organisms are made of cells, the cell is the basic unit of life, and new cells come from existing cells.

Schleiden (1838) established that plants are made of cells.

Schwann (1839) established that animals are made of cells.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
