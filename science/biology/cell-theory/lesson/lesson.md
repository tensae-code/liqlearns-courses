# Cell theory — three statements, three scientists

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Cell theory — three statements, three scientists. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Schleiden, Schwann and Virchow built the theory statement by statement.

[Picture 21026 — three portraits with their contributions in order]

## The key facts
**Schleiden.** Schleiden (1838) established that plants are made of cells.

**Schwann.** Schwann (1839) established that animals are made of cells.

**Virchow.** Virchow added that cells arise only from existing cells by division.

**The theory.** Cell theory: organisms are composed of one or more cells, the cell is the basic unit of life, and new cells come from pre-existing cells.
