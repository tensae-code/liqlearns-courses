# Cell theory — three statements, three scientists — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about Schleiden?
a) Schleiden (1838) established that plants are made of cells
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Schleiden (1838) established that plants are made of cells**

**2.** What does this lesson say about Schwann?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all

c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Schwann (1839) established that animals are made of cells**

**3.** What does this lesson say about Virchow?

**4.** What does this lesson say about the theory?
