# Hands-on — Fish adaptations to life in water

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Look at a picture of a fish — or a real fish, if one is being prepared for cooking — and find each adaptation in turn: the streamlined shape, the fins, the gill covers, and the lateral line along the side. Say the job of each one aloud.

## Teach it on paper
Draw a fish from memory and label its five adaptations — streamlined shape, fins, gills, swim bladder, lateral line — with the job of each written beside it. Check your drawing against the lesson and fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
