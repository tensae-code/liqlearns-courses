# Video script — Fish adaptations to life in water

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Fish adaptations to life in water.

Remember this one thing: a fish's body is shaped by the water it lives in. Every part this lesson names is an answer to living in water.

The shape comes first: streamlined, smooth and tapered, so the water slips past. Then the fins, which move the fish and hold it balanced. Then the gills, which take oxygen out of the water as it flows in through the mouth and out under the gill covers.

Inside, the swim bladder controls the depth at which the fish swims. Along the side, the lateral line senses vibrations and movement in the water — a sense organ made for life in water.

Finland's teachers pose the question before the dissection — how does the fish's structure show it is adapted to water? — and the pupils answer it after, from the real fish they have opened.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
