# Fish adaptations to life in water — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain how the gills are an adaptation to life in water.
**Answer: A fish cannot breathe air like a land animal, so its gills take oxygen out of the water itself: water flows in through the mouth, over the gill filaments, and out under the gill covers, and oxygen passes into the blood in the filaments' vessels.**

**2.** Explain Finland's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Finland's AOE fish materials (Kaitera, Särvä & Takalo 2020) build the whole perch-dissection lesson around one research question — how the fish's structure shows it is adapted to water. The question is posed before the dissection and answered after it; the pupil sheets ask it in writing, and the grades 7–9 guide closes by drawing streamline, fins, gills, swim bladder and lateral line together as one synthesis.**
