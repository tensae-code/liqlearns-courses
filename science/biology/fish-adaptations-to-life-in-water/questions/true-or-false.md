# Fish adaptations to life in water — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. The lateral line senses vibrations and movement in the water around the fish.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. A fish takes oxygen from the air above the water, like a land animal.**
**Answer: False** — a fish takes oxygen from the water itself, through its gills, as water flows over the gill filaments.

**3. Fins are only for decoration and play no part in movement or balance.**
**Answer: False** — the fins move the fish through the water and hold it balanced and steady.

**4. Finland's grades 7–9 guide draws streamline, fins, gills, swim bladder and lateral line together as a closing synthesis.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches — it is also set as homework.
