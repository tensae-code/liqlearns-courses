# Fish adaptations to life in water — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining how a fish is adapted to life in water, naming at least three adaptations and the job of each.
**Model answer: A fish's streamlined shape lets it move through water with little resistance, and its fins move it and hold it balanced. Its gills take oxygen out of the water as water flows over the gill filaments, and its swim bladder lets it control the depth at which it swims.**
