# Fish adaptations to life in water — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The sense organ running along a fish's side, sensing vibrations in the water, is the _____.**
**Answer: lateral line**

**2. The _____ lets a fish control the depth at which it swims.**
**Answer: swim bladder**

**3. A fish's smooth, tapered shape, letting water slip past, is called _____.**
**Answer: streamlined**

**4. Oxygen passes into the blood in the _____ of the gills.**
**Answer: filaments**
