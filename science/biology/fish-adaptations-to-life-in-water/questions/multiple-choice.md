# Fish adaptations to life in water — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which set names a fish's adaptations to life in water?**
a) Feathers, beak, wings and hollow bones  b) Streamlined shape, fins, gills, swim bladder and lateral line  c) Fur, whiskers, claws and good night vision  d) Shell, scales that change colour, and legs
**Answer: b) Streamlined shape, fins, gills, swim bladder and lateral line**

**2. What does the swim bladder do?**
a) It takes oxygen from the water  b) It senses vibrations  c) It lets the fish control the depth at which it swims  d) It pumps blood to the gills
**Answer: c) It lets the fish control the depth at which it swims**

**3. How do the gills take in oxygen?**
a) Water flows in through the mouth, over the gill filaments, and out under the gill covers; oxygen passes into the blood in the filaments  b) The fish swallows air at the surface  c) Oxygen enters through the fins  d) The lateral line absorbs oxygen
**Answer: a) Water flows in through the mouth, over the gill filaments, and out under the gill covers; oxygen passes into the blood in the filaments**

**4. In Finland's materials, when is the question “how does the fish's structure show it is adapted to water?” posed?**
a) Only in the examination  b) Never — the pupils only label a diagram  c) Before the dissection, and answered after it  d) After the course ends
**Answer: c) Before the dissection, and answered after it**
