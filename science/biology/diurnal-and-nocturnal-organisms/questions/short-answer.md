# Diurnal and nocturnal organisms — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: living things keep biological rhythms — some are active by day and some by night, and each is fitted to its time: flowers open and close at set hours, and nocturnal animals see in the dark with large eyes and a reflective eye layer. Moldova's Grade 6 book (2.2 Diurnal and nocturnal organisms) teaches: orientation in time / biological rhythms, flower opening-closing times table, nocturnal animals' reflective eye layer and large eyes (e.g. tarsier-like "maki"). (printed p. 16).**

**2.** Explain Moldova's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Moldova's Grade 6 book teaches the topic through orientation in time: a flower opening-closing times table, and the nocturnal animals' reflective eye layer and large eyes (e.g. the tarsier-like “maki”).**
