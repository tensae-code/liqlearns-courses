# Diurnal and nocturnal organisms — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Moldova's Grade 6 book (2.2 Diurnal and nocturnal organisms) teaches: orientation in time / biological rhythms, flower opening-closing times table, nocturnal animals' reflective eye layer and large eyes (e.g. tarsier-like "maki"). (printed p. 16).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Moldova's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Biologie: Manual pentru clasa a 6-a (Grade 6)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with diurnal and nocturnal organisms.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is diurnal and nocturnal organisms.
