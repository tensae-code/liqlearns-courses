Today: Diurnal and nocturnal organisms.

Remember this one thing: living things keep biological rhythms — some are active by day and some by night, and each is fitted to its time: flowers open and close at set hours, and nocturnal animals see in the dark with large eyes and a reflective eye layer.

Moldova's Grade 6 book (2.2 Diurnal and nocturnal organisms) teaches: orientation in time / biological rhythms, flower opening-closing times table, nocturnal animals' reflective eye layer and large eyes (e.g. tarsier-like "maki"). (printed p. 16).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
