# Hands-on — Stem cells

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Draw one round unspecialised cell, then draw it dividing: one daughter stays a stem cell, the other becomes a specialised cell of your choice. Label which ability each arrow shows — self-renewal or specialisation.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a single round unspecialised cell in the middle dividing into two, with three arrows leading out to a muscle cell, a nerve cell and a blood cell it can become — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
