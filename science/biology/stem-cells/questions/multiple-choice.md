# Stem cells — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A stem cell is a cell that…**
a) can only contract b) is not yet specialised and can produce specialised cells c) carries nerve signals d) has no nucleus
**Answer: b) is not yet specialised and can produce specialised cells**

**2. Self-renewal means a stem cell can…**
a) move by itself b) divide to make more stem cells like itself c) live without food d) become a whole plant
**Answer: b) divide to make more stem cells like itself**

**3. When a stem cell's daughter takes on one set job and structure, it has…**
a) died b) specialised c) become a stem cell again d) lost its DNA
**Answer: b) specialised**

**4. In Punjab's Grade 9 SNC book, stem cells are taught…**
a) in the statistics chapter b) as a named section, Ch 3.5, in the chapter on the cell c) only in Grade 12 d) inside the enzymes chapter
**Answer: b) as a named section, Ch 3.5, in the chapter on the cell**
