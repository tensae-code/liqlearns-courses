# Stem cells — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the two abilities that make a cell a stem cell.
**Answer: A stem cell can divide to make more cells like itself — self-renewal — and it can produce cells that specialise into the working cells of tissues. It is defined by being unspecialised while holding both abilities.**

**2.** Explain: Pakistan (Punjab)'s way of teaching this.
**Answer: Punjab's Grade 9 SNC book teaches the cell chapter in order — structure of the cell, structural advantages of plant and animal cells, cell specialisation — and then gives stem cells their own named section, Ch 3.5 (printed p. 53), so a grade-9 learner meets them as a topic in their own right.**
