# Stem cells — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. A cell that is not yet specialised and can produce specialised cells is a _____ cell.**
**Answer: stem**

**2. A stem cell renews itself by _____ to make more stem cells.**
**Answer: dividing**

**3. A daughter cell that takes on one set job has _____.**
**Answer: specialised**

**4. Punjab teaches stem cells in Chapter 3, section _____.**
**Answer: 3.5**
