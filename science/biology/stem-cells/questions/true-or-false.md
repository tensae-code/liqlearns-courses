# Stem cells — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Every cell in the body is a stem cell.**
**Answer: False** — most cells are specialised for one job; stem cells are the unspecialised source cells

**2. A stem cell can both copy itself and produce specialised cells.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches

**3. Specialisation comes before the stem cell stage.**
**Answer: False** — the stem cell is unspecialised first; specialisation is the step a daughter cell takes afterwards

**4. Punjab's Grade 9 book gives stem cells their own named section.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches — Ch 3.5
