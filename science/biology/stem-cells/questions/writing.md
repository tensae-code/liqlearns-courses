# Stem cells — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining what a stem cell is and why a body needs such cells.
**Model answer: A stem cell is an unspecialised cell that can divide to make more stem cells and can also produce cells that specialise for particular jobs. A body needs such a source because specialised cells are used up, damaged and must be replaced as the body grows and repairs itself.**
