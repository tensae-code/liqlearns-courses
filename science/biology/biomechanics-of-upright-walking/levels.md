# Biomechanics of upright walking — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (upright walking is a physical balancing act: the spine's curves, the arched foot, the pelvis and the five phases of a step fit the human skeleton and muscles to moving steadily on two legs) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
