# The brain's lymphatic vessels — a 2015 discovery — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. the brain was the one place the immune system was thought absent; in 2015 lymphatic vessels were found in mouse meninges, in 2017 imaged in humans — hidden, the discoverers say, by hugging a great blood vessel through a region MRI reads badly: a lesson in how much anatomy can still be missed.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in China's People's Education Press biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (普通高中教科书《生物学》选择性必修1 稳态与调节 (Senior High Biology, Selective Compulsory Book 1: Homeostasis and Regulation, People's Education Press, 2019-standard edition)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with the brain's lymphatic vessels — a 2015 discovery.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is the brain's lymphatic vessels — a 2015 discovery.
