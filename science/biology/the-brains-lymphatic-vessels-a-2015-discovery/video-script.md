Today: The brain's lymphatic vessels — a 2015 discovery.

Remember this one thing: the brain was the one place the immune system was thought absent — until 2015, when lymphatic vessels were found in mouse meninges, and imaged in humans in 2017, hidden by hugging a great blood vessel through a region MRI reads badly: a lesson in how much anatomy can still be missed.
