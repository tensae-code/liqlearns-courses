# Ozone depletion and acid rain — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about ozone depletion and acid rain?**
**Answer: The stratospheric ozone layer shields life from ultraviolet radiation; its depletion raises skin cancer, cataracts, immune and respiratory harm and damages plants and animals, while also increasing ground-level ozone; acid rain — rain made extra-acid by sulfur and nitrogen oxides from fuel burning and engines — stops seeds germinating, leaches soil nutrients and dissolved aluminium, harms fish, amphibians and soil microbes, and corrodes pipes, leaching heavy metals into drinking water.**
