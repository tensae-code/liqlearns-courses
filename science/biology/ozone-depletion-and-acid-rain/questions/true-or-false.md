# Ozone depletion and acid rain — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. The stratospheric ozone layer shields life from ultraviolet radiation; its depletion raises skin cancer, cataracts, immune and respiratory harm and damages plants and animals, while also increasing ground-level ozone; acid rain — rain made extra-acid by sulfur and nitrogen oxides from fuel burning and engines — stops seeds germinating, leaches soil nutrients and dissolved aluminium, harms fish, amphibians and soil microbes, and corrodes pipes, leaching heavy metals into drinking water.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
