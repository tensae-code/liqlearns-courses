# Ozone depletion and acid rain

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — ozone depletion and acid rain. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

The stratospheric ozone layer shields life from ultraviolet radiation; its depletion raises skin cancer, cataracts, immune and respiratory harm and damages plants and animals, while also increasing ground-level ozone; acid rain — rain made extra-acid by sulfur and nitrogen oxides from fuel burning and engines — stops seeds germinating, leaches soil nutrients and dissolved aluminium, harms fish, amphibians and soil microbes, and corrodes pipes, leaching heavy metals into drinking water.
