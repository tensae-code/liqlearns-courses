# How seeds travel — dispersal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about four ways?
a) Seeds travel by wind, by water, by animals and by explosive pods  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
