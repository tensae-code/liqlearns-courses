# How seeds travel — dispersal

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — How seeds travel — dispersal. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
If every seed fell under its parent, the young plants would crowd each other out. Seeds travel — and each way of travelling is matched to the seed's own features.

[Picture 22214 — four seeds, one per dispersal method, with their travelling features labelled]

[GIF 22215 — an explosive pod bursting and scattering seeds]

## The key facts
**Four ways.** Seeds travel by wind, by water, by animals and by explosive pods.

**Matched features.** Each method is matched to the seed features that make it work.

**The point.** Dispersal avoids crowding and competition with the parent plant.

**New ground.** Travelling seeds can grow in new places away from the parent.
