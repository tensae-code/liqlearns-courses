# Video script — Enzymes — the body's catalysts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Protein molecules that act as biological catalysts, speeding reactions by lowering the activation energy — the minimum energy a reaction needs to start — while themselves emerging unchanged; every cell carries its own enzyme set for its metabolism, the sum of breakdown (catabolism) and building (anabolism); a first lab has pupils chew bread slowly and notice it sweetening as salivary amylase works.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
