# Enzymes — the body's catalysts — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about what enzymes are?**
**Answer: Protein molecules that act as biological catalysts, speeding reactions by lowering the activation energy — the minimum energy a reaction needs to start — while themselves emerging unchanged; every cell carries its own enzyme set for its metabolism, the sum of breakdown (catabolism) and building (anabolism); a first lab has pupils chew bread slowly and notice it sweetening as salivary amylase works.**
