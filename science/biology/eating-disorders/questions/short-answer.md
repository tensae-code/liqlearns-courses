# Eating disorders — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: how does Ecuador's book teach eating disorders?
**Answer: Biología — 1ro BGU teaches them inside Sección 6, Tema 2, "Salud integral" (integral health), printed pp. 80–81. Eating disorders — the book names anorexia and bulimia — are placed in a cause→effect table together with drugs, alcohol, sedentarism and pollution: each cause is read by the effect it has on health, because the book's frame is that health is damaged by several causes together, not one at a time.**

**2.** Explain: what gap does the book itself name, and why does this lesson carry it without filling it?
**Answer: The book notes that Ecuador lacks updated statistics on anorexia and bulimia. The lesson carries that note exactly as the book states it and invents no figures: the disorders are named and taught, while the country's own current numbers for them are missing — that missing count is itself part of what the book teaches.**
