# Eating disorders — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Ecuador's book names anorexia and bulimia as eating disorders.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Eating disorders get a chapter of their own in Ecuador's book, separate from all other health topics.
**Answer: False** — they are taught inside "Salud integral" (Sección 6, Tema 2), in a cause→effect table with drugs, alcohol, sedentarism and pollution.

**3.** In the book's table, each cause is read by its effect on health.
**Answer: True**

**4.** The book gives Ecuador's current, updated statistics on anorexia and bulimia.
**Answer: False** — the book notes that Ecuador lacks updated statistics on anorexia and bulimia.
