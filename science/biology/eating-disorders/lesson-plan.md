# Lesson plan — Eating disorders

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Eating disorders
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — appended to Unit 16 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** eating disorders — anorexia and bulimia are the two Ecuador's Grade 11 biology book names — are health damage that belongs inside "Salud integral" (integral health): Ecuador's book teaches them in a cause→effect table alongside drugs, alcohol, sedentarism and pollution, and it says plainly that Ecuador lacks updated statistics on anorexia and bulimia (Ecuador, Grade 11 — *Biología — 1ro BGU*, student textbook, Sección 6, Tema 2, printed pp. 80–81).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. The substance-abuse and alcohol lessons are recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: **Substance abuse — when use becomes abuse** and **Alcohol, khat and cannabis**. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say, in their own words, the one thing this lesson teaches: eating disorders are part of integral health — Ecuador's book puts anorexia and bulimia in a cause→effect table with drugs, alcohol, sedentarism and pollution, as causes that damage health.
2. Name the two eating disorders the book names: anorexia and bulimia.
3. Explain Ecuador's way: the book's frame is "Salud integral" — health damaged by several causes together, read through a cause→effect table, not one cause at a time.
4. Say what the book itself notes about the numbers: Ecuador lacks updated statistics on anorexia and bulimia.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Go back to the one thing this lesson teaches, and re-read the key facts slowly, one at a time.
- Use the book's own table: cause on the left, effect on health on the right — where do eating disorders sit, and what sits beside them?
