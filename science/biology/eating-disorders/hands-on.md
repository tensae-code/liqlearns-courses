# Hands-on — Eating disorders

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Draw the book's cause→effect table yourself: two columns, "Cause" and "Effect on health". Fill the cause column with the book's five causes — eating disorders, drugs, alcohol, sedentarism, pollution. In the eating-disorders row, write the two disorders the book names: anorexia and bulimia.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the "Salud integral" table with its five causes — label it (anorexia and bulimia under eating disorders; the book's note that Ecuador lacks updated statistics on them written under the table), and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap. End with this one together: why does the book teach eating disorders in the same table as alcohol and pollution, instead of in a chapter of their own? Say why, using the "Salud integral" frame.
