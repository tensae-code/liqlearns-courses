The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: eating disorders.

Remember this one thing: eating disorders damage health — and Ecuador's biology book teaches them together with the other causes that damage health, in one table.

The book names two: anorexia and bulimia. And it does not put them in a chapter of their own. It puts them inside "Salud integral" — integral health — in a cause→effect table, side by side with drugs, alcohol, sedentarism and pollution. Cause on the left. Effect on health on the right.

And the book is honest about what it does not have: it notes that Ecuador lacks updated statistics on anorexia and bulimia. The disorders are named and taught; the country's current numbers are missing, and the book says so.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
