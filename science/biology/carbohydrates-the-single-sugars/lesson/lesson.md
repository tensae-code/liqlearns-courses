# Carbohydrates — the single sugars

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Carbohydrates — the single sugars. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Carbohydrates start simple: one sugar unit. Glucose, galactose and fructose are the three single sugars this lesson fixes.

## The key facts
**Single sugars.** Monosaccharides are the single sugars.

**The three.** Glucose, galactose and fructose are the monosaccharides taught here.

**Starting foods.** The section opens from carbohydrate-rich foods pupils know.

**Formulas.** The single sugars' formulas are fixed in this lesson's activities.
