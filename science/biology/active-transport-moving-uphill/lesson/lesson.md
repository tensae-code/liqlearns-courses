# Active transport — moving uphill, spending energy

## Do it first

## The idea
Sometimes a cell must gather what diffusion would carry away. It pays energy to move particles uphill.

[Picture 21033 — a membrane pump pushing particles into a crowded cell]

[GIF 21113 — the pump cycling and pushing particles against the flow]

## The key facts
**Against the gradient.** Active transport moves particles from lower to higher concentration — against the gradient.

**Energy.** Active transport uses the cell's stored energy.

**Purpose.** Active transport lets cells gather or expel substances that diffusion would move the other way.

**Contrast.** Active transport is contrasted point by point with passive transport.
