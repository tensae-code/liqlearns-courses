# Active transport — moving uphill, spending energy — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Active transport moves particles from lower to higher concentration — against the gradient.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Energy has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Active transport lets cells gather or expel substances that diffusion would move the other way.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Contrast has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
