# Bioinformatics — biology meets computing — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about bioinformatics?**
**Answer: Computing applied to biological data: storing, organising, retrieving and analysing sequence and other data at scales no manual method could handle, across genomics, proteomics and systems biology, and used to improve disease diagnosis, screen pathogen genomes for vaccine development, and trace evolution through sequence changes.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added bioinformatics example?**
**Answer: The guide's concrete example of bioinformatics in action is DNA sequencing, defined as determining the order of nucleotides within a DNA molecule, offered for pupils to elaborate with further applications.**
