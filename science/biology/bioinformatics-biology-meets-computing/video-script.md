# Video script — Bioinformatics — biology meets computing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Computing applied to biological data: storing, organising, retrieving and analysing sequence and other data at scales no manual method could handle, across genomics, proteomics and systems biology, and used to improve disease diagnosis, screen pathogen genomes for vaccine development, and trace evolution through sequence changes.

The guide's concrete example of bioinformatics in action is DNA sequencing, defined as determining the order of nucleotides within a DNA molecule, offered for pupils to elaborate with further applications.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
