# Telling plants from animals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the surest everyday difference between a plant and an animal?
a) A plant makes its own food and stays rooted; an animal eats and moves about
b) A plant is always bigger than an animal
c) An animal is always green
d) A plant never needs water
**Answer: a) A plant makes its own food and stays rooted; an animal eats and moves about**

**2.** Why can a plant stay in one place for its whole life?
a) Because it has legs it does not use
b) Because it makes its own food where it stands, using sunlight
c) Because animals carry food to it
d) Because it does not need food
**Answer: b) Because it makes its own food where it stands, using sunlight**

**3.** Where does Uganda's Senior One book teach the plant–animal distinction?
a) As the closing topic of Chapter 1, Introduction to Biology
b) In the last chapter, on flowering plants
c) It does not teach it
d) In the teacher's guide only
**Answer: a) As the closing topic of Chapter 1, Introduction to Biology**
