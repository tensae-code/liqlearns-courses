# Telling plants from animals — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A plant makes its own food using chlorophyll in its green parts.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Animals can make their own food by photosynthesis.
**Answer: False — animals have no chlorophyll; they must eat plants or other animals.**

**3.** An animal moves from place to place, above all to find food.
**Answer: True**
