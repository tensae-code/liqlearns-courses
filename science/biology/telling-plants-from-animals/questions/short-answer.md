# Telling plants from animals — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: how do a plant and an animal differ in the way they get food?
**Answer: A plant makes its own food: the chlorophyll in its green parts traps sunlight and the plant builds food by photosynthesis, so it can stay rooted in one place. An animal has no chlorophyll and cannot make food; it must eat plants, or eat other animals, and it moves about to find that food.**

**2.** Explain: Uganda's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Uganda's Senior One textbook makes differentiating between animals and plants a named topic of its own — the closing topic of Chapter 1, Introduction to Biology (printed p. 8) — so the plant–animal distinction is the first sorting a Ugandan learner is formally asked to make.**
