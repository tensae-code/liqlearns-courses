Today: Telling plants from animals.

Remember this one thing: a plant and an animal live in different ways, and you can tell them apart by those ways.

A plant makes its own food. Its green leaves hold chlorophyll, and with sunlight the plant builds its food — so a plant does not need to hunt or travel for food, and it stays rooted in one place.

An animal cannot make its own food. It eats plants, or it eats other animals — and to find that food, it moves about.

Uganda's Senior One book closes its very first chapter with this sorting: telling animals from plants (printed p. 8). It is the first distinction the book asks a learner to make.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
