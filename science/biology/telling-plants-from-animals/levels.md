# Telling plants from animals — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (plants and animals can be told apart by how they live: plants make their own food and stay rooted in one place, while animals eat other living things and move about — and Uganda's Senior One book closes its introduction chapter with exactly this distinction (Ch 1, printed p. 8)) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by sorting more living things — including hard cases like a mushroom (a fungus, neither plant nor animal) — and explaining each difference, without looking.
