# Hands-on — Telling plants from animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Go outside (or look out of the window) and find one plant and one animal. For each one, say aloud: how does it get its food, and does it move from place to place? Then say the verdict — plant or animal — and the reason.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a rooted green plant beside a moving animal, with the food arrows drawn in — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
