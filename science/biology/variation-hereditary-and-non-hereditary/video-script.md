Today: Variation — hereditary and non-hereditary.

Remember this one thing: organisms vary in two ways: hereditary variation, carried in their genes and passed to their offspring, and non-hereditary — modification — variation, shaped by living conditions and not inherited; telling the two apart is the first step of genetics.
