# Variation — hereditary and non-hereditary — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (organisms vary in two ways: hereditary variation, carried in their genes and passed to their offspring, and non-hereditary — modification — variation, shaped by living conditions and not inherited; telling the two apart is the first step of genetics) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
