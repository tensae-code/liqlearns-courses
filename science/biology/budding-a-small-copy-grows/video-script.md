Today: Budding — a small copy grows on the parent.

Remember this one thing: in budding an unequal outgrowth grows on the parent, then separates or stays as a colony.

In budding the offspring starts as an unequal outgrowth (bud) on the parent's side.

Budding happens in yeasts and in animals such as hydra.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
