# Budding — a small copy grows on the parent

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Budding — a small copy grows on the parent. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Parent and bud are deliberately unequal — that is what separates budding from fission.

[Picture 21039 — a hydra with a bud at its side at three growth stages]

[GIF 21117 — the bud swelling and detaching]

## The key facts
**Bud.** In budding the offspring starts as an unequal outgrowth (bud) on the parent's side.

**Examples.** Budding happens in yeasts and in animals such as hydra.

**Separating.** The bud grows and then separates, or stays attached as a colony.

**Unequal.** Parent and bud are unequal, unlike the equal halves of fission.
