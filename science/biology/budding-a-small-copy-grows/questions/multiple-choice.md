# Budding — a small copy grows on the parent — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about bud?
a) In budding the offspring starts as an unequal outgrowth (bud) on the parent's side
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) In budding the offspring starts as an unequal outgrowth (bud) on the parent's side**

**2.** What does this lesson say about examples?

**Answer: b) Budding happens in yeasts and in animals such as hydra**
