Today: Flatworms — planaria and tapeworms.

Remember this one thing: flatworms are among the first animals with three embryonic germ layers — ectoderm, endoderm and mesoderm — and the group runs from free-living planaria, whose eye spots are light-sensing cell clusters, to parasitic flukes and tapeworms with life cycles through their hosts.

Kuwait's Grade 10 Term 2 student book (Unit 2, Lesson 2-1, printed pp. 31–35) introduces flatworms as among the first animals with three embryonic germ layers: ectoderm, endoderm and mesoderm.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
