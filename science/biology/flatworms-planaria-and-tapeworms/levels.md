# Flatworms — planaria and tapeworms — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (flatworms are among the first animals with three embryonic germ layers — ectoderm, endoderm and mesoderm — and the group runs from free-living planaria, whose eye spots are light-sensing cell clusters, to parasitic flukes and tapeworms with life cycles through their hosts) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
