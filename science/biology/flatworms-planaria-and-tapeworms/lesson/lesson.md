## The key facts
**Three germ layers.** Kuwait's Grade 10 Term 2 student book (Unit 2, Lesson 2-1, printed pp. 31–35) introduces flatworms as among the first animals with three embryonic germ layers: ectoderm, endoderm and mesoderm.

**Planaria.** The free-living planaria is the group's worked example: its eye spots are light-sensing cell clusters, not true eyes.

**Parasitic flatworms.** The book works a parasitic fluke's life cycle in full, with the human as the primary host in which the fluke reproduces sexually.

**Kuwait's way.** Kuwait teaches the flatworms inside its worms lesson (Grade 10 Term 2, Unit 2 Lesson 2-1) as a step in body-plan history — the first three-layered animals — moving from planaria to the parasite life cycles.
