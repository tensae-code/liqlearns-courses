# Flatworms — planaria and tapeworms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) flatworms are among the first animals with three embryonic germ layers — ectoderm, endoderm and mesoderm — and the group runs from free-living planaria, whose eye spots are light-sensing cell clusters, to parasitic flukes and tapeworms with life cycles through their hosts  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
**Answer: a) flatworms are among the first animals with three embryonic germ layers — ectoderm, endoderm and mesoderm — and the group runs from free-living planaria, whose eye spots are light-sensing cell clusters, to parasitic flukes and tapeworms with life cycles through their hosts**

**2. Three germ layers — which statement is right?**
a) Kuwait's Grade 10 Term 2 student book (Unit 2, Lesson 2-1, printed pp. 31–35) introduces flatworms as among the first animals with three embryonic germ layers: ectoderm, endoderm and mesoderm.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) Kuwait's Grade 10 Term 2 student book (Unit 2, Lesson 2-1, printed pp. 31–35) introduces flatworms as among the first animals with three embryonic germ layers: ectoderm, endoderm and mesoderm.**

**3. Which statement about this lesson's topic is right?**
