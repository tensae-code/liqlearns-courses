# Flatworms — planaria and tapeworms — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: flatworms are among the first animals with three embryonic germ layers — ectoderm, endoderm and mesoderm — and the group runs from free-living planaria, whose eye spots are light-sensing cell clusters, to parasitic flukes and tapeworms with life cycles through their hosts. Kuwait's Grade 10 Term 2 student book (Unit 2, Lesson 2-1, printed pp. 31–35) introduces flatworms as among the first animals with three embryonic germ layers: ectoderm, endoderm and mesoderm.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait teaches the flatworms inside its worms lesson (Grade 10 Term 2, Unit 2 Lesson 2-1) as a step in body-plan history — the first three-layered animals — moving from planaria to the parasite life cycles.
