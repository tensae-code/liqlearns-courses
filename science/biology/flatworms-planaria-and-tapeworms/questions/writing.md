# Flatworms — planaria and tapeworms — writing

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: flatworms are among the first animals with three embryonic germ layers — ectoderm, endoderm and mesoderm — and the group runs from free-living planaria, whose eye spots are light-sensing cell clusters, to parasitic flukes and tapeworms with life cycles through their hosts. The free-living planaria is the group's worked example: its eye spots are light-sensing cell clusters, not true eyes.**
