# Hands-on — How roots absorb water and mineral salts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: a root does its absorbing through its zones — cap, division, growth, absorption and conduction — and in the absorption zone its root hairs take in water and mineral salts and pass them up into the plant.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a young root drawn long, its four zones labelled in bands — cap, division, growth, absorption with fine root hairs, conduction — with water and mineral arrows entering at the hairs and travelling upward. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
