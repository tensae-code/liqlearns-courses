# Ecological engineering — designing with nature — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: ecological engineering designs or repairs ecosystems on ecological principles — self-generation, cycling, coordination and wholeness — for less consumption, more benefit and sustainability, as the classical mulberry–silkworm–fishpond and modern village biogas, lake-restoration and mining-land systems show. from 1952 he took on China's locust plague with "combine habitat change with control, uproot the locust disaster"; he originated the ecological-engineering principles (wholeness, coordination, cycling, self-generation) and the social–economic–natural complex ecosystem idea that Ch. 4 §3 later teaches as doctrine.**

**2.** Explain China's way of teaching this.
**Answer: from 1952 he took on China's locust plague with "combine habitat change with control, uproot the locust disaster"; he originated the ecological-engineering principles (wholeness, coordination, cycling, self-generation) and the social–economic–natural complex ecosystem idea that Ch. 4 §3 later teaches as doctrine. the discipline defined (designing or repairing ecosystems on ecological principles, "less consumption, more benefit, sustainable"); its four principles from Ma Shijun — self-generation, cycling, coordination, wholeness — each with a Chinese case: wetland plant choice by niche, the ancient "waste-free agriculture" that kept China's soils fertile for millennia, the "grey Great Wall" of poplars planted where rainfall under 450 mm suits only shrubs and grasses, and the social–economic–natural complex system; the Ming–Qing mulberry–silkworm–fishpond （桑基鱼塘） of the Yangtze and Pearl deltas as the classical worked example, pond-mud ratios and all. three national instances: a Beijing-suburb village's biogas-centred multi-level cycling system relieving the rural "three shortages" (feed, fuel, fertiliser); Xiamen's Yundang Lake wetland restoration (tidal flushing and dredging after the 1982 clean-up of its shores); and the Yuanbaoshan mining-wasteland restoration at Chifeng (750 hectares of grassland in three years) — closing with the warning never to read ecological engineering as a licence to pollute first and repair later.**
