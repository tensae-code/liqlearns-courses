# Meiosis — halving the chromosomes

## Do it first

## The idea
Gametes cannot carry the full set. Meiosis divides twice, halving the chromosome number, so fertilisation can restore it.

## The key facts
**Two divisions.** Meiosis divides the cell twice.

**Halved.** Meiosis halves the chromosome number.

**Gametes.** Meiosis makes gametes — the sex cells.

**Four cells.** One cell gives four daughter cells in meiosis.
