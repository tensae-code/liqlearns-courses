# Hydroponics — growing plants without soil — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. In hydroponics, the plants' roots grow in…**
a) dry sand  b) a nutrient solution  c) pure distilled water with nothing dissolved  d) soil mixed with stones
**Answer: b) a nutrient solution**

**2. Which set names the parts of Vietnam's hydroponics model?**
a) tank, channels, pump, aeration  b) plough, seed drill, harvester  c) greenhouse glass only  d) pots and compost
**Answer: a) tank, channels, pump, aeration**

**3. Why are the plants flushed with clean water 2–3 days before harvest?**
a) to make the leaves wet  b) to clear nitrate from the crop  c) to cool the roots  d) to stop the pump
**Answer: b) to clear nitrate from the crop**

**4. How many lettuce crops a year does the model give, against soil?**
a) the same as soil  b) 1–2, against 5–6 in soil  c) 5–6, against 2–3 in soil  d) 12, against 1 in soil
**Answer: c) 5–6, against 2–3 in soil**
