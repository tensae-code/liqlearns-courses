# Hydroponics — growing plants without soil — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Hydroponics grows plants in a nutrient _____ instead of soil.**
**Answer: solution**

**2. Bubbling air into the solution for the roots is called _____.**
**Answer: aeration**

**3. Before harvest the plants are flushed with clean water to clear _____.**
**Answer: nitrate**

**4. The model gives _____ lettuce crops a year, against 2–3 in soil.**
**Answer: 5–6**
