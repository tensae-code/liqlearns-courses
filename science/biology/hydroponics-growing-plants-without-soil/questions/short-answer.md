# Hydroponics — growing plants without soil — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of the hydroponics system and the job of each, as Vietnam's lesson lays them out.
**Answer: Tank — holds the nutrient solution; channels — carry the solution past the roots; pump — keeps the solution moving; aeration — keeps air in the water for the roots. Around the machine sit the grower's disciplines: controlling the solution and its pH, disinfecting the channels, and flushing with clean water before harvest.**

**2.** Explain Vietnam's way of teaching hydroponics.
**Answer: Vietnam's Grade 11 study topics teach hydroponics inside the mineral-nutrition topic as the model for clean agriculture (Topic 1, Bài 2, printed p. 12): the full system anatomy, solution and pH control, channel disinfection, the 2–3 day clean-water flush that clears nitrate before harvest, the yield comparison (5–6 lettuce crops a year against 2–3 in soil), and Vietnamese home/rooftop and commercial greenhouse examples.**
