Today: Hydroponics — growing plants without soil.

Remember this one thing: Hydroponics grows plants with their roots in a nutrient solution instead of soil — Vietnam's Grade 11 study topics teach the full model (tank, channels, pump and aeration, solution and pH control, a clean-water flush before harvest) as the growing system of clean agriculture.

No soil at all: the roots sit in moving water that carries their food. A tank, channels, a pump, air bubbling in — that is the machine. The grower watches the solution and its pH, cleans the channels, and for the last two or three days runs plain clean water through, so the leaves carry no nitrate to the table. Lettuce five or six times a year, where soil gives two or three — on a rooftop in Vietnam, or in a big greenhouse.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
