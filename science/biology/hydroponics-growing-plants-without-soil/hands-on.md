# Hands-on — Hydroponics — growing plants without soil

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Take the lesson's key facts for a walk: find one example of this lesson's idea in your own surroundings — your home, street, market or school — and say which key fact it matches, and why.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — a hydroponics bench in cross-section: a tank of nutrient solution at one end, a pump pushing the solution along a sloping channel past the roots of lettuce plants held in holes along the channel, bubbles of air rising in the tank from an aeration pipe, and the solution returning to the tank — label its parts, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
