# Lesson plan — Hydroponics — growing plants without soil

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Hydroponics — growing plants without soil
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — secondary band (CURRICULUM.md Unit 16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Hydroponics grows plants with their roots in a nutrient solution instead of soil — Vietnam's Grade 11 study topics teach the full model (tank, channels, pump and aeration, solution and pH control, a clean-water flush before harvest) as the growing system of clean agriculture.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier biology lessons and builds the secondary-band understanding of hydroponics — growing plants without soil.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
