# Pheromones — chemical signals between animals — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. A pheromone can be read by any animal that smells it.**
**Answer: False** — a pheromone is species-specific — it is read by the sender's own species.

**2. Vietnam's behaviour lesson gives pheromones their own section.**
**Answer: True** — Bài 18, IV is the pheromone section of Sinh học 11.

**3. Pheromone traps catch the females of a pest species.**
**Answer: False** — the Grade 12 topics teach mass-trapping of males.

**4. The silkworm moth's scientific name is Bombyx mori.**
**Answer: True** — this is the case named in the book.
