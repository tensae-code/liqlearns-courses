# Pheromones — chemical signals between animals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A pheromone is…**
a) a chemical signal between animals of the same species  b) a sound only insects can hear  c) a colour change for camouflage  d) a hormone inside one animal's blood
**Answer: a) a chemical signal between animals of the same species**

**2. Vietnam's Grade 11 book uses which animal as its pheromone case?**
a) the honeybee  b) the silkworm moth (Bombyx mori)  c) the tiger  d) the flamingo
**Answer: b) the silkworm moth (Bombyx mori)**

**3. Pheromone traps work against pests because pheromones are…**
a) poisonous  b) species-specific, so only the pest species is lured  c) loud  d) visible from far away
**Answer: b) species-specific, so only the pest species is lured**

**4. In the Grade 12 study topics, pheromone mass-trapping is taught as part of…**
a) photosynthesis  b) biological control of pests  c) human reproduction  d) the water cycle
**Answer: b) biological control of pests**
