# Pheromones — chemical signals between animals — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. A chemical signal between animals of one species is called a _____.**
**Answer: pheromone**

**2. Vietnam's pheromone case is the _____ moth, Bombyx mori.**
**Answer: silkworm**

**3. Pheromone _____ of males is a biological-control method in the Grade 12 topics.**
**Answer: mass-trapping**

**4. A pheromone works only on the sender's own _____.**
**Answer: species**
