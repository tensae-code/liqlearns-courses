# Pheromones — chemical signals between animals — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Distinguish a pheromone from an ordinary smell, using Vietnam's teaching.
**Answer: An ordinary smell can be picked up by many kinds of animal; a pheromone is a chemical signal that works between animals of one species — species-specific — as in the silkworm moth (Bombyx mori) case in Sinh học 11, Bài 18, IV.**

**2.** Explain Vietnam's way of teaching pheromones.
**Answer: Vietnam teaches pheromones twice: in Grade 11 as the closing section of the animal-behaviour lesson (Bài 18, IV, printed p. 118), with the silkworm moth as the case and pheromone applications among the required outcomes; and in the Grade 12 study topics as a working tool — pheromone (chất dẫn dụ sinh học) mass-trapping of male pests inside biological control (Topic 2, Bài 5, printed p. 39).**
