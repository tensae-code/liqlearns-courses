# Pheromones — chemical signals between animals — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why a pheromone trap catches one pest species and leaves other insects alone.
**Model answer: A pheromone is species-specific: the chemical message is read only by the species that produces it. A trap baited with that species' pheromone therefore lures only its males. Other insects do not read the signal, so they are not drawn in — which is why Vietnam's study topics count pheromone mass-trapping among the clean, targeted methods of biological control.**
