# Hands-on — Pheromones — chemical signals between animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Take the lesson's key facts for a walk: find one example of this lesson's idea in your own surroundings — your home, street, market or school — and say which key fact it matches, and why.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — a female silkworm moth releasing an invisible scent trail through the air, drawn as a dotted plume, with a male moth of the same species following the plume toward her; beside it, a field trap baited with pheromone, with male pest moths caught inside — label its parts, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
