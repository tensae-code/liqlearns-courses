# Pheromones — chemical signals between animals — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Pheromones are chemical signals an animal releases that carry a message to others of its own species — Vietnam's Grade 11 behaviour lesson gives pheromones their own section (Bài 18, IV), with the silkworm moth as the case, and its Grade 12 study topics put pheromone traps to work against crop pests.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by giving the lesson's key facts with the books' own examples without looking.
