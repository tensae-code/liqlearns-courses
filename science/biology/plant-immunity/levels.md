# Plant immunity — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (plants defend themselves by structural immunity and biochemical immunity, and Egypt's Grade 12 book teaches immunity in plants first, in Ch. 4, printed pp. 85–88, before immunity in man) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: sorting a new defence into structural or biochemical without looking, and explaining why a tylose and canavanine are different kinds of defence.
