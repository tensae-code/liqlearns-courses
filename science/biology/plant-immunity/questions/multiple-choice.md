# Plant immunity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What are the two kinds of immunity Egypt's book teaches in plants?
a) Structural immunity and biochemical immunity
b) Natural immunity and acquired immunity
c) First-line and second-line defence
d) Innate immunity and antibody immunity
**Answer: a) Structural immunity and biochemical immunity**

**2.** Which of these is a structural defence a plant already has?
a) Canavanine
b) A cutinised epidermis, hairs and thorns, and thickened cell walls
c) Antimicrobial proteins
d) Detoxifying enzymes
**Answer: b) A cutinised epidermis, hairs and thorns, and thickened cell walls**

**3.** What does a tylose do?
a) It carries water up the stem faster
b) It attracts pollinators to the flower
c) It grows into the xylem and blocks the vessel, so the pathogen cannot spread through it
d) It stores food for the seed
**Answer: c) It grows into the xylem and blocks the vessel, so the pathogen cannot spread through it**

**4.** Which of these is a biochemical defence?
a) A thorn
b) Induced cork formation
c) Gum deposition in Acacia nilotica
d) Phenols and glycosides, and the non-protein amino acid canavanine
**Answer: d) Phenols and glycosides, and the non-protein amino acid canavanine**
