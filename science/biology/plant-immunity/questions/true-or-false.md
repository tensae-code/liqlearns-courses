# Plant immunity — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Egypt's Grade 12 book teaches immunity in plants before it teaches immunity in man.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Tyloses are a biochemical defence made of phenols.
**Answer: False** — tyloses are a structural defence: they grow into the xylem and block the vessels. Phenols are a biochemical defence.

**3.** In the hypersensitive response, the injured tissue itself dies, cutting the pathogen off.
**Answer: True**

**4.** A plant's biochemical defences get weaker after an infection.
**Answer: False** — after an infection the defences are strengthened (post-infection strengthening), so the plant is harder to attack the next time.
