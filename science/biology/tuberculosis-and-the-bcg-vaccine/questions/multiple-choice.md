# Tuberculosis — an airborne disease and the BCG vaccine — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about airborne?
a) TB is an airborne bacterial disease
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) TB is an airborne bacterial disease**

**2.** What does this lesson say about risk?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) TB is tied to weakened immunity and overcrowding
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) TB is tied to weakened immunity and overcrowding**

**3.** What does this lesson say about BCG?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) The BCG vaccine, from animal TB bacteria, is given in infancy, protects most recipients, and its protection fades with age
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) The BCG vaccine, from animal TB bacteria, is given in infancy, protects most recipients, and its protection fades with age**

**4.** What does this lesson say about HIV link?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Treating HIV helps control TB because the two diseases reinforce each other
**Answer: d) Treating HIV helps control TB because the two diseases reinforce each other**
