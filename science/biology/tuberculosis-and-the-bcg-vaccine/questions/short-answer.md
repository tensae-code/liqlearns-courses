# Tuberculosis — an airborne disease and the BCG vaccine — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: BCG.
**Answer: The BCG vaccine, from animal TB bacteria, is given in infancy, protects most recipients, and its protection fades with age.**

**2.** Explain: HIV link.
**Answer: Treating HIV helps control TB because the two diseases reinforce each other.**
