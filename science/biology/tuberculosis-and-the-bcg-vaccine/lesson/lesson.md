# Tuberculosis — an airborne disease and the BCG vaccine

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Tuberculosis — an airborne disease and the BCG vaccine. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Treating HIV helps control TB, because the two diseases reinforce each other.

[Picture 21068 — droplets travelling between people in a crowded room, with a vaccination card]

## The key facts
**Airborne.** TB is an airborne bacterial disease.

**Risk.** TB is tied to weakened immunity and overcrowding.

**BCG.** The BCG vaccine, from animal TB bacteria, is given in infancy, protects most recipients, and its protection fades with age.

**HIV link.** Treating HIV helps control TB because the two diseases reinforce each other.
