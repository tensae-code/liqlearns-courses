# What affects the cell membrane — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (the cell membrane is a delicate, partly permeable skin of fat and protein — heat and chemicals such as acids and alkalis can damage it, and a damaged membrane leaks: the cell's contents escape and substances rush in uncontrolled.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
