# What affects the cell membrane — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about what affects the cell membrane?
a) The cell membrane is a delicate, partly permeable skin of fat and protein — heat and chemicals such as acids and alkalis can damage it, and a damaged membrane leaks: the cell's contents escape and substances rush in uncontrolled.
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The cell membrane is a delicate, partly permeable skin of fat and protein — heat and chemicals such as acids and alkalis can damage it, and a damaged membrane leaks: the cell's contents escape and substances rush in uncontrolled.**

**2.** What does this lesson say about a skin that chooses?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) The cell membrane holds the cell together and controls what crosses it: some substances pass, others are kept in or out.
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The cell membrane holds the cell together and controls what crosses it: some substances pass, others are kept in or out.**

**3.** What does this lesson say about heat damages the membrane?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Heating a cell harms its membrane: the fats in the membrane melt and shift and the proteins lose their shape, so the membrane's careful choosing breaks down.
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Heating a cell harms its membrane: the fats in the membrane melt and shift and the proteins lose their shape, so the membrane's careful choosing breaks down.**

**4.** What does this lesson say about acids and alkalis damage it too?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Dilute acids and alkalis attack the membrane's fats and proteins in the same way, breaking its structure.
**Answer: d) Dilute acids and alkalis attack the membrane's fats and proteins in the same way, breaking its structure.**
