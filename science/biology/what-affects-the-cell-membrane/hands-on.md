# Hands-on — What affects the cell membrane

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Put a piece of raw beetroot in cold water and another in hot water (with a teacher's care). Compare the water after ten minutes: the hot-water piece leaks its red pigment, because heat damaged the membranes of its cells. Write what the leak proves.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of what affects the cell membrane — the cell membrane is a delicate, partly permeable skin of fat and protein — heat and chemicals such as acids and alkalis can damage it, and a damaged membrane leaks: the cell's contents escape and substances rush in uncontrolled. — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
