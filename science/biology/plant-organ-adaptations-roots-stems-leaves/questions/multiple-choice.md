# Plant organ adaptations — roots, stems and leaves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The maize's prop roots grow from…**
a) the leaf tips  b) stem nodes  c) the flowers  d) the seeds
**Answer: b) stem nodes**

**2. The banana's apparent trunk is really…**
a) a woody stem  b) rolled leaf sheaths  c) a bundle of roots  d) a rhizome above ground
**Answer: b) rolled leaf sheaths**

**3. Cladodes are…**
a) underground roots  b) flattened stems doing the leaf's work in plants that lost their leaves  c) climbing fruits  d) water-storage leaves
**Answer: b) flattened stems doing the leaf's work in plants that lost their leaves**

**4. Tendrils (gavinhas) serve a climbing plant for…**
a) storing food  b) holding on and climbing  c) catching insects  d) losing water
**Answer: b) holding on and climbing**
