# Plant organ adaptations — roots, stems and leaves — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Give one root, one stem and one leaf adaptation from the lesson.
**Answer: Roots: the maize's aerial prop roots from stem nodes (or fleshy storage roots). Stems: tendrils for climbing (or the banana's underground rhizome). Leaves: spines, or cladodes replacing leaves to save water.**

**2.** Explain Mozambique's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Mozambique's PESD I Módulo 1 de Biologia (2023), Lesson 11, teaches adaptation of plant organs to the environment organ by organ: aquatic roots, aerial/adventitious roots (maize prop roots from stem nodes), fleshy storage roots, tendrils (gavinhas), cladodes in plants that lost leaves to save water, spines, and the banana's rhizome — the apparent trunk being leaf sheaths — closing with plant care and the importance of plants.**
