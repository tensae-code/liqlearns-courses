**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why the banana's "trunk" is a good test of whether someone really looks at plants.
**Model answer: The banana's trunk looks like a stem but is only the rolled sheaths of its leaves; the true stem, the rhizome, is underground with its buds. The lesson uses it to teach that an organ is what it is by its structure and position, not by appearances — the eye must be corrected by the dissection.**
