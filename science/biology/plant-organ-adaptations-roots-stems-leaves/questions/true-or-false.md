# Plant organ adaptations — roots, stems and leaves — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. The banana's true stem is an underground rhizome with buds.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Spines are leaves reshaped to save water.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches — leaves shrink to spines that lose almost nothing.

**3. Prop roots are a kind of flower.**
**Answer: False** — prop roots are aerial/adventitious roots from stem nodes, as in maize.

**4. Fleshy storage roots store food reserves.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
