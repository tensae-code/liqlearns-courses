# Plant organ adaptations — roots, stems and leaves — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Climbing plants grip with curling _____.**
**Answer: tendrils**

**2. The banana's underground stem is called a _____.**
**Answer: rhizome**

**3. Flattened green stems that replace leaves are called _____.**
**Answer: cladodes**

**4. Maize is braced by _____ roots from its stem nodes.**
**Answer: prop**
