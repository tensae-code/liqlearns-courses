# Individual, population, community, ecosystem, biosphere — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about population?
a) A population is all members of the same species in an area
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) A population is all members of the same species in an area**

**2.** What does this lesson say about community?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) A community is all species in an area, interacting
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) A community is all species in an area, interacting**

**3.** What does this lesson say about ecosystem level?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) An ecosystem is a community plus its environment
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) An ecosystem is a community plus its environment**

**4.** What does this lesson say about biosphere?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) The biosphere is the widest ecological level, covering the whole living Earth
**Answer: d) The biosphere is the widest ecological level, covering the whole living Earth**
