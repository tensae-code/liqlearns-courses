## The key facts
**Population.** A population is all members of the same species in an area.

**Community.** A community is all species in an area, interacting.

**Ecosystem level.** An ecosystem is a community plus its environment.

**Biosphere.** The biosphere is the widest ecological level, covering the whole living Earth.
