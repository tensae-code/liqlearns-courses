Today: Individual, population, community, ecosystem, biosphere.

Remember this one thing: ecologists organise the living world into widening levels, from one organism to the biosphere.

A population is all members of the same species in an area.

A community is all species in an area, interacting.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
