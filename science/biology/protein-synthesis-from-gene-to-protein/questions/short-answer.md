# Protein synthesis — from gene to protein — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about protein synthesis?**
**Answer: The central dogma: DNA's instructions flow through RNA to protein (with reverse transcription named as the exception); in transcription a DNA section is copied into mRNA codons in the nucleus; in translation the mRNA docks on a ribosome, tRNAs bring amino acids matching codon to anticodon, and a polypeptide chain grows link by link into a protein; the genetic-code chart is read throughout.**
