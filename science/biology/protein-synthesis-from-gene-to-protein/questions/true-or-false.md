# Protein synthesis — from gene to protein — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. The central dogma: DNA's instructions flow through RNA to protein (with reverse transcription named as the exception); in transcription a DNA section is copied into mRNA codons in the nucleus; in translation the mRNA docks on a ribosome, tRNAs bring amino acids matching codon to anticodon, and a polypeptide chain grows link by link into a protein; the genetic-code chart is read throughout.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. In transcription a DNA section is copied into mRNA codons in the nucleus.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. In translation the mRNA docks on a ribosome, tRNAs bring amino acids matching codon to anticodon, and a polypeptide chain grows link by link into a protein.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. The genetic-code chart is read throughout.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
