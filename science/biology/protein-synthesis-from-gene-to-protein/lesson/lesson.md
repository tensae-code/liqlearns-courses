# Protein synthesis — from gene to protein

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — protein synthesis. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

The central dogma: DNA's instructions flow through RNA to protein (with reverse transcription named as the exception); in transcription a DNA section is copied into mRNA codons in the nucleus; in translation the mRNA docks on a ribosome, tRNAs bring amino acids matching codon to anticodon, and a polypeptide chain grows link by link into a protein; the genetic-code chart is read throughout.
