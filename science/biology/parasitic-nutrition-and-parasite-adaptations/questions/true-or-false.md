# Parasitic nutrition and parasite adaptations — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** The guinea-worm Dracunculus medinensis is one of the book's West African parasite examples.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A parasite benefits its host while feeding on it.
**Answer: False** — A parasite feeds at the host's expense and harms it.

**3.** Many parasites pass through an intermediate host before reaching their final host.
**Answer: True**
