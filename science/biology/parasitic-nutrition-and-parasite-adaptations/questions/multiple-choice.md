# Parasitic nutrition and parasite adaptations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which parasite causes elephantiasis?
a) Fasciola
b) Wuchereria bancrofti
c) Taenia solium
d) Dracunculus medinensis
**Answer: b) Wuchereria bancrofti**

**2.** Why do endoparasites produce millions of eggs?
a) To feed the host
b) Because most eggs perish on the way to a new host — numbers make some survive
c) To grow faster
d) Eggs are their food store
**Answer: b) Because most eggs perish on the way to a new host — numbers make some survive**

**3.** What do hooks and suckers do for a parasite?
a) Help it digest food
b) Anchor it to its host so it is not swept away
c) Help it breathe
d) Attract a mate
**Answer: b) Anchor it to its host so it is not swept away**

**4.** What are anti-enzymes for, in an endoparasite?
a) To digest the host's food for it
b) To protect the parasite from the host's digestive enzymes
c) To make eggs
d) To move the parasite along the gut
**Answer: b) To protect the parasite from the host's digestive enzymes**
