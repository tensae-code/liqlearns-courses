# Parasitic nutrition and parasite adaptations — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** The tapeworms in the book are Taenia solium and Taenia _____.
**Answer: saginata**

**2.** Wuchereria bancrofti causes the disease called _____.
**Answer: elephantiasis**

**3.** A parasite that lives on the outside of its host is an _____; one that lives inside is an endoparasite.
**Answer: ectoparasite**
