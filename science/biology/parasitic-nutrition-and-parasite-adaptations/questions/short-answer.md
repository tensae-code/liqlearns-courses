# Parasitic nutrition and parasite adaptations — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the adaptations of parasites, as the book lists them.
**Answer: Parasites are fitted to their life: hooks and suckers for attachment to the host; anti-enzymes that protect endoparasites from the host's digestive juices; the production of millions of eggs so some survive to reach a new host; life cycles that pass through intermediate hosts; and host specificity — each parasite adapted to its own host.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book builds parasitic nutrition (Chapter 6, printed p. 97) on West African parasites — Fasciola in sheep liver, the two Taenia tapeworms, Wuchereria bancrofti, the guinea-worm, and nematodes of tomato and banana roots — then lists the adaptations of ectoparasites and endoparasites one by one.**
