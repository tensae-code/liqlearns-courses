# Hands-on — Parasitic nutrition and parasite adaptations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Look at tomato or banana plants (or pictures of them) and say aloud where root nematodes would live and feed. Then list, from memory, the five parasite adaptations — attachment, anti-enzymes, millions of eggs, intermediate hosts, host specificity — and give one book example for each that you can.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a labelled tapeworm head with hooks and suckers holding to a gut wall, beside a guinea-worm emerging and a mosquito carrying Wuchereria — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
