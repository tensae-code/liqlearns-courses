# Climate change and global warming

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — climate change and global warming. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Climate change is a shift in a place's usual weather patterns, driven substantially by greenhouse gases that trap the sun's heat like greenhouse glass or a blanket; the result, global warming — a rapid rise in Earth's average temperature — brings sea-level rise, extinctions, flooding, heat stress and health risks, with its effects figured.
