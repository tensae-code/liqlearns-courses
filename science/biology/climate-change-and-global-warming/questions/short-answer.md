# Climate change and global warming — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about climate change and global warming?**
**Answer: Climate change is a shift in a place's usual weather patterns, driven substantially by greenhouse gases that trap the sun's heat like greenhouse glass or a blanket; the result, global warming — a rapid rise in Earth's average temperature — brings sea-level rise, extinctions, flooding, heat stress and health risks, with its effects figured.**
