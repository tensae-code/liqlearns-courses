# Climate change and global warming — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. ** _____ is a shift in a place's usual weather patterns, driven substantially by greenhouse gases that trap the sun's heat like greenhouse glass or a blanket; the result, global warming — a rapid rise in Earth's average temperature — brings sea-level rise, extinctions, flooding, heat stress and health risks, with its effects figured.
**Answer: Climate change**
