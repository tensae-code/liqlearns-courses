# Living and non-living parts of an ecosystem — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about biotic?
a) Biotic components are all the living members of an ecosystem
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Biotic components are all the living members of an ecosystem**

**2.** What does this lesson say about abiotic?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Abiotic components are non-living factors — light, temperature, water, soil and air
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Abiotic components are non-living factors — light, temperature, water, soil and air**

**3.** What does this lesson say about shaping?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Abiotic factors shape which organisms can live in a place
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Abiotic factors shape which organisms can live in a place**

**4.** What does this lesson say about sorting?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Pupils sort a real local scene into biotic and abiotic lists
**Answer: d) Pupils sort a real local scene into biotic and abiotic lists**
