The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: Living and non-living parts of an ecosystem.

Remember this one thing: every ecosystem mixes biotic (living) components with abiotic (non-living) factors that shape who can live there.

Biotic components are all the living members of an ecosystem.

Abiotic components are non-living factors — light, temperature, water, soil and air.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
