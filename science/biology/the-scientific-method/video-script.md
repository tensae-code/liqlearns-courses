Today: The scientific method — observe, question, hypothesise, test, conclude.

Remember this one thing: the scientific method moves from observation to question, hypothesis, experiment and conclusion.

Scientific study begins with careful observation, which raises a question.

A hypothesis is a testable possible answer to the question.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
