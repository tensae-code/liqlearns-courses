# The scientific method — observe, question, hypothesise, test, conclude

## Do it first

## The idea
When a torch will not light, you can work like a scientist: look carefully, ask why, guess a testable answer, test it, and decide from the result. That is the scientific method.

## The key facts
**Observation.** Scientific study begins with careful observation, which raises a question.

**Hypothesis.** A hypothesis is a testable possible answer to the question.

**Experiment.** An experiment only counts if others can repeat it and get similar results.

**Conclusion.** Conclusions either keep the hypothesis as a possible explanation or reject it and form a new one.
