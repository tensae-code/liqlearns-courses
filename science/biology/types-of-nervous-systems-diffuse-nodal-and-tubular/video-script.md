Today: Types of nervous systems — diffuse, nodal and tubular.

Remember this one thing: animal nervous systems climb an evolutionary ladder: the diffuse nerve net of a hydra, the nodal system of nerve knots and chains in worms and insects, and the tubular system of vertebrates — a cord with a brain at its head end.
