# Treating plants ethically in biology

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — treating plants ethically in biology. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Plants are producers feeding all life and work in a decentralised way with no brain; destructive testing, careless collecting of seeds, berries, roots, leaves, bark and flowers, and cutting terminals can erase species and harm the communities that depend on them, so plant material must be taken and tested with restraint.
