# Treating plants ethically in biology — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about ethical treatment of plants in experiments?**
**Answer: Plants are producers feeding all life and work in a decentralised way with no brain; destructive testing, careless collecting of seeds, berries, roots, leaves, bark and flowers, and cutting terminals can erase species and harm the communities that depend on them, so plant material must be taken and tested with restraint.**
