# Movement in unicellular organisms — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (single-celled organisms move with what they have: an amoeba flows by pseudopodia — temporary feet of streaming cytoplasm — while others row with many short cilia or lash one or two long flagella, each method fitted to life at microscopic size) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
