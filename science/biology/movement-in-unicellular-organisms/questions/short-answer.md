# Movement in unicellular organisms — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: single-celled organisms move with what they have: an amoeba flows by pseudopodia — temporary feet of streaming cytoplasm — while others row with many short cilia or lash one or two long flagella, each method fitted to life at microscopic size. support and movement in unicellular organisms: pseudopodia/amoeboid movement, cilia and flagella**
