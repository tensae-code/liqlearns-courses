Today: Movement in unicellular organisms.

Remember this one thing: single-celled organisms move with what they have: an amoeba flows by pseudopodia — temporary feet of streaming cytoplasm — while others row with many short cilia or lash one or two long flagella, each method fitted to life at microscopic size.
