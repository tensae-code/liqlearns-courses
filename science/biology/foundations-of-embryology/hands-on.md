# Hands-on — Foundations of embryology

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Draw the four stages of the lesson's picture from memory — zygote, cleavage ball, hollow ball folding in, layered embryo — and under the last, list which adult parts come from each germ layer. Check against the lesson; fix the layer lists until they are right.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a row of stages: one cell, the dividing ball of cleavage, the hollow ball, the ball folding in to make layers, and the young embryo with the three germ layers marked in three colours — each stage labelled — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
