**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why embryologists say the adult body can be 'read backwards' to three layers.
**Model answer: Every tissue and organ of the adult forms from one of the three germ layers laid down in gastrulation: skin and nerves from ectoderm, gut and its glands from endoderm, muscle, bone and blood from mesoderm. Because the mapping is constant, the whole adult body can be traced back — read backwards — to those three beginnings.**
