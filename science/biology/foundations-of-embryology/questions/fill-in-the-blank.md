# Foundations of embryology — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The study of development from the fertilised egg is _____.**
**Answer: embryology**

**2. The rapid early divisions of the zygote are called _____.**
**Answer: cleavage**

**3. The folding-in that lays down the germ layers is _____.**
**Answer: gastrulation**

**4. Muscle, bone and blood come from the _____ layer.**
**Answer: mesoderm**
