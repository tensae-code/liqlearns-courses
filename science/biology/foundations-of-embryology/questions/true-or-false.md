# Foundations of embryology — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. During cleavage the embryo divides without growing overall.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. The mesoderm lies between ectoderm and endoderm.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. The gut lining comes from the ectoderm.**
**Answer: False** — the gut and its glands come from the endoderm; ectoderm gives skin and the nervous system.

**4. Embryology studies development from the fertilised egg.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
