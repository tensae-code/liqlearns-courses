# Foundations of embryology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What does embryology study?**
a) Only adult anatomy  b) The development of the organism from the fertilised egg  c) The naming of species  d) Diseases of old age
**Answer: b) The development of the organism from the fertilised egg**

**2. What is cleavage?**
a) The folding-in of the embryo  b) The rapid early divisions of the zygote into more, smaller cells  c) The birth of the young  d) The making of gametes
**Answer: b) The rapid early divisions of the zygote into more, smaller cells**

**3. What does gastrulation produce?**
a) The gametes  b) The germ layers — ectoderm, endoderm, mesoderm  c) The placenta only  d) The adult body at once
**Answer: b) The germ layers — ectoderm, endoderm, mesoderm**

**4. Which germ layer gives the nervous system?**
a) Endoderm  b) Mesoderm  c) Ectoderm  d) None of them
**Answer: c) Ectoderm**
