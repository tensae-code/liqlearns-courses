# Foundations of embryology — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: from zygote to germ layers.
**Answer: The zygote divides rapidly in cleavage, making a solid then a hollow ball of cells without overall growth. In gastrulation the ball folds in and the cells organise into three germ layers — ectoderm outside, endoderm inside, mesoderm between — from which all tissues and organs will form.**

**2.** Explain: North Macedonia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: North Macedonia's Grade 13 elective textbook closes its reproduction chapter with foundations of embryology as a named part of its own (Ch. 3.III, printed pp. 145–151), after reproduction in plants and in animals.**
