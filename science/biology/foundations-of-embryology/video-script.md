Today: Foundations of embryology.

Remember this one thing: Embryology is the study of how an organism develops from the fertilised egg: cleavage divides the zygote, the cells organise into layers — the germ layers — and from those layers every tissue and organ is built.

Embryology asks how a body comes to be. It begins with one cell, the zygote, dividing fast — cleavage — into a ball of cells.

The ball folds in, and the cells sort into three germ layers: ectoderm, mesoderm, endoderm. From those three layers, every tissue and organ of the adult will be built. That is the founding of an animal.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
