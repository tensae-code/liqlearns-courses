# Foundations of embryology — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Embryology is the study of how an organism develops from the fertilised egg: cleavage divides the zygote, the cells organise into layers — the germ layers — and from those layers every tissue and organ is built.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a fresh example from the learner's own surroundings and saying why it fits, without looking.
