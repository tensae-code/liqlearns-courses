Today: Cell wall and extracellular matrix.

Remember this one thing: outside the cell membrane there can be further structure: plants carry a cellulose cell wall, and animal cells sit in an extracellular matrix — the lesson covers the structures that lie outside the cell itself.

Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 1-2, printed p. 45) teaches the structures outside the cell: the cellulose cell wall of plants — and its presence also in prokaryotes, some protists and fungi.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
