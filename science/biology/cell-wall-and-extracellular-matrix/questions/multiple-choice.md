# Cell wall and extracellular matrix — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) outside the cell membrane there can be further structure: plants carry a cellulose cell wall, and animal cells sit in an extracellular matrix — the lesson covers the structures that lie outside the cell itself  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life

**2. The cell wall — which statement is right?**
