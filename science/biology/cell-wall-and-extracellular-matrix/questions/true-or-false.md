# Cell wall and extracellular matrix — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 1-2, printed p. 45) teaches the structures outside the cell: the cellulose cell wall of plants — and its presence also in prokaryotes, some protists and fungi.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كتاب الطالب — الصف العاشر — الفصل الدراسي الأول (Biology — Student Book, Grade 10, Term 1), printed p. 45).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with cell wall and extracellular matrix.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is cell wall and extracellular matrix.
