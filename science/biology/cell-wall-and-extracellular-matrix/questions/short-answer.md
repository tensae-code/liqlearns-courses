# Cell wall and extracellular matrix — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: outside the cell membrane there can be further structure: plants carry a cellulose cell wall, and animal cells sit in an extracellular matrix — the lesson covers the structures that lie outside the cell itself. Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 1-2, printed p. 45) teaches the structures outside the cell: the cellulose cell wall of plants — and its presence also in prokaryotes, some protists and fungi.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's new Grade 10 book gives the outside of the cell its own headed place in the cell lesson (Lesson 1-2, printed p. 45): wall and matrix taught together as "structures outside the cell".**
