Today: Chromosome structure and karyotype.

Remember this one thing: a chromosome is DNA packaged with protein — wound into nucleosomes, folded into chromatin, pinched at the centromere.

Loose chromatin is euchromatin; dense chromatin is heterochromatin. The centromere's position names the chromosome's shape. And a karyotype is the whole set, arranged in pairs in size order.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
