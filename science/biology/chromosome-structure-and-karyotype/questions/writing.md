# Chromosome structure and karyotype — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining what a karyotype shows and how it is made.
**Model answer: A karyotype is made by photographing an individual's chromosomes during division and arranging them in homologous pairs in size order. It shows the number and shapes of the chromosomes in the set, so an abnormal number or shape can be seen at a glance.**
