# Chromosome structure and karyotype — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. DNA is packaged into chromosomes by winding around proteins called…**
a) nucleosomes  b) histones  c) ribosomes  d) centromeres
**Answer: b) histones**

**2. The loose, lighter chromatin whose genes can be read is called…**
a) heterochromatin  b) a karyotype  c) euchromatin  d) a polytene chromosome
**Answer: c) euchromatin**

**3. A chromosome's type is named by the position of its…**
a) telomere  b) gene  c) centromere  d) nucleosome
**Answer: c) centromere**

**4. A karyotype is…**
a) one giant chromosome  b) the whole set of chromosomes arranged in pairs in size order  c) a picture of a single gene  d) the DNA of a bacterium
**Answer: b) the whole set of chromosomes arranged in pairs in size order**
