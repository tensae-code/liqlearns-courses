# Chromosome structure and karyotype — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. A chromosome is made of DNA alone, with no protein.**
**Answer: False** — a chromosome is a macromolecule of DNA plus protein — the DNA is wound around histone proteins.

**2. Euchromatin is the looser kind of chromatin.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Every chromosome's centromere sits exactly in the middle.**
**Answer: False** — centromere position varies — middle, off-centre, near or at the end — and that position names the chromosome type.

**4. A karyotype arranges chromosomes in homologous pairs in size order.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
