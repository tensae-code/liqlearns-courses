# Chromosome structure and karyotype — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: how DNA is packaged, from nucleosome to chromosome.
**Answer: DNA winds around histone proteins to form nucleosomes; the beaded string folds into chromatin — loose euchromatin or dense heterochromatin — and packs down further at division into the chromosome, a DNA-plus-protein macromolecule whose chromatids are joined at the centromere.**

**2.** Explain: Indonesia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Indonesia's Grade 12 student book (Ch. 2A, printed p. 70) teaches DNA packaging, nucleosomes, chromosome structure, centromere-position types, euchromatin/heterochromatin and the karyotype; the teacher guide adds Drosophila melanogaster's giant polytene chromosomes (Gambar 2.1), observable by light microscope.**
