# Chromosome structure and karyotype — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. DNA wound around histone proteins forms bead-like units called _____.**
**Answer: nucleosomes**

**2. The dense, tightly packed chromatin is called _____.**
**Answer: heterochromatin**

**3. The pinched point where two chromatids are joined is the _____.**
**Answer: centromere**

**4. The giant chromosomes of Drosophila observed in the Indonesian guide are called _____ chromosomes.**
**Answer: polytene**
