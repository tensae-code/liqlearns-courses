# Genetic counselling and prenatal diagnosis — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why genetic counselling is advice and arithmetic, not only laboratory tests.
**Model answer: The tests answer one family's question, but the counsellor's work starts earlier and ends later: risk is calculated from the pedigree and from population genetics before any sample is taken, and the result only matters when it is turned into advice a family can act on — about marriage, about testing, about screening and treatment after the birth. The laboratory supplies the facts; counselling supplies what they mean.**
