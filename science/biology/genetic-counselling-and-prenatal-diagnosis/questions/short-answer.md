# Genetic counselling and prenatal diagnosis — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Describe amniocentesis as Vietnam's book draws it.
**Answer: Under ultrasound guidance, a needle takes about 20 mL of the fluid around the unborn child; the cells in the fluid are cultured, and the chromosomes are then analysed — the step-by-step figure in Sinh học 12, Bài 13, III (printed pp. 71–74).**

**2.** Explain Vietnam's way of teaching genetic counselling.
**Answer: Vietnam's Grade 12 book teaches medical genetic counselling as a full section of its human genetics lesson (Bài 13, III, printed pp. 71–74): risk calculated from pedigrees and population genetics, amniocentesis drawn step by step, chorionic villus sampling at 11–13 weeks, advice against consanguineous marriage, the age-35 Down-syndrome risk, and newborn screening with the galactosaemia diet — the same lesson goes on to gene therapy's achievements.**
