# Genetic counselling and prenatal diagnosis — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Risk is calculated from pedigrees and _____ genetics.**
**Answer: population**

**2. The fluid sampled in amniocentesis is about _____ mL.**
**Answer: 20**

**3. Chorionic _____ sampling is done at 11–13 weeks.**
**Answer: villus**

**4. Newborn screening catches galactosaemia, treated by _____.**
**Answer: diet**
