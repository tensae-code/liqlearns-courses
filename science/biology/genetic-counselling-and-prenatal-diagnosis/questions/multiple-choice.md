# Genetic counselling and prenatal diagnosis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. In Vietnam's lesson, a family's risk is first calculated from…**
a) blood pressure  b) pedigrees and population genetics  c) the mother's diet  d) a horoscope
**Answer: b) pedigrees and population genetics**

**2. Amniocentesis samples about how much fluid?**
a) 2 mL  b) 20 mL  c) 200 mL  d) 2 litres
**Answer: b) 20 mL**

**3. Chorionic villus sampling is done at…**
a) 5–6 weeks  b) 11–13 weeks  c) 30 weeks  d) after birth
**Answer: b) 11–13 weeks**

**4. Newborn screening in the book is worked through which case?**
a) the galactosaemia diet  b) the age-35 risk figure  c) consanguineous marriage  d) the pedigree
**Answer: a) the galactosaemia diet**
