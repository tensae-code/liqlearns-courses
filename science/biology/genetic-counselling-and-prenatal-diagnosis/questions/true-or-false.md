# Genetic counselling and prenatal diagnosis — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Amniocentesis is guided by ultrasound.**
**Answer: True** — the book draws it step by step under ultrasound.

**2. Genetic counselling in the lesson advises in favour of consanguineous marriage.**
**Answer: False** — it advises against consanguineous marriage.

**3. The Down-syndrome risk is stated in the lesson for a mother of 35.**
**Answer: True** — age-35 risk is part of the section's reckoning.

**4. Prenatal diagnosis ends the counsellor's work — nothing follows the birth.**
**Answer: False** — newborn screening continues the care after birth.
