# Hands-on — Genetic counselling and prenatal diagnosis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Take the lesson's key facts for a walk: find one example of this lesson's idea in your own surroundings — your home, street, market or school — and say which key fact it matches, and why.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — a counsellor's desk in three panels: a family pedigree drawn on paper with a risk figure worked beside it; an ultrasound-guided needle taking a small sample of fluid from around an unborn baby, the sample tube labelled for culture and analysis; and a newborn's heel-prick screening card — label its parts, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
