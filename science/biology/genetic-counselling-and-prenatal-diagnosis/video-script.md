Today: Genetic counselling and prenatal diagnosis.

Remember this one thing: Genetic counselling works out a family's risk of an inherited condition and advises on it before and during pregnancy — Vietnam's Grade 12 human-genetics lesson teaches it as a full section: risk calculated from pedigrees and population genetics, amniocentesis and chorionic villus sampling as the prenatal tests, and newborn screening after the birth.

A family knows a condition runs in it. The counsellor turns that knowledge into numbers — risk worked out from the family tree and from the population. Before birth, the child can be tested: fluid drawn under ultrasound, cells grown and read; or a villus sample at eleven to thirteen weeks. After birth, screening catches what can be treated — a special diet from the first days. That whole chain, risk to test to advice, is genetic counselling.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
