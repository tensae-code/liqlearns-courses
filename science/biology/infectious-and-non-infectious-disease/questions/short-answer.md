# Infectious and non-infectious disease — and breaking the chain of infection — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: routes.
**Answer: Infections spread by insects, contaminated water or food, air, and contact.**

**2.** Explain: breaking the chain.
**Answer: Prevention includes safe water, proper waste disposal in covered bins, and sewage treatment keeping toilets away from water pipes; giardia and cholera ride faeces-contaminated water.**
