# Infectious and non-infectious disease — and breaking the chain of infection — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about disease?
a) A disease is a condition in which the body does not function normally, with symptoms such as headache, raised temperature, pain and weakness
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) A disease is a condition in which the body does not function normally, with symptoms such as headache, raised temperature, pain and weakness**

**2.** What does this lesson say about infectious?

c) It happens only at night and nowhere else

**Answer: b) Infectious diseases are caused by organisms such as viruses and bacteria**
