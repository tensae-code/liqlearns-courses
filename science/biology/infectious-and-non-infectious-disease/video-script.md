Today: Infectious and non-infectious disease — and breaking the chain of infection.

Remember this one thing: infectious diseases are caused by organisms and spread by routes we can break; non-infectious diseases are not.

A disease is a condition in which the body does not function normally, with symptoms such as headache, raised temperature, pain and weakness.

Infectious diseases are caused by organisms such as viruses and bacteria.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
