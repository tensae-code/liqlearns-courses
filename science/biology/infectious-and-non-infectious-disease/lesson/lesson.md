# Infectious and non-infectious disease — and breaking the chain of infection

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Infectious and non-infectious disease — and breaking the chain of infection. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
A disease is a condition in which the body does not function normally. Prevention is a public-health chain: safe water, covered bins, sewage treatment keeping toilets away from water pipes.

[Picture 21066 — a chain of infection with break points marked]

## The key facts
**Disease.** A disease is a condition in which the body does not function normally, with symptoms such as headache, raised temperature, pain and weakness.

**Infectious.** Infectious diseases are caused by organisms such as viruses and bacteria.

**Routes.** Infections spread by insects, contaminated water or food, air, and contact.

**Breaking the chain.** Prevention includes safe water, proper waste disposal in covered bins, and sewage treatment keeping toilets away from water pipes; giardia and cholera ride faeces-contaminated water.
