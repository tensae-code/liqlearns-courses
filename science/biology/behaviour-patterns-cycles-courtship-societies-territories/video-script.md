# Video script — Behaviour patterns — cycles, courtship, societies, territories

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Recurring patterns across species: behavioural cycles answering periodic change (daily circadian rhythm, seasonal migration), reproductive behaviour (finding and choosing mates, courtship by sound, display or chemicals, rearing young), social behaviour (ants, termites, bees and wasps as the most developed), and competition, territory and communication.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
