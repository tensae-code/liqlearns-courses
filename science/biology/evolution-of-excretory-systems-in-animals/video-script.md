Today: Evolution of excretory systems in animals.

Remember this one thing: excretory systems evolve along the animal line — from contractile vacuoles and worm tubules to the vertebrate kidney — while the waste itself changes with habitat: ammonia where water is plentiful, urea, and uric acid where water must be saved.
