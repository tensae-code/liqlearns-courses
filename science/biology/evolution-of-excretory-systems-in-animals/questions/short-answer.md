# Evolution of excretory systems in animals — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: excretory systems evolve along the animal line — from contractile vacuoles and worm tubules to the vertebrate kidney — while the waste itself changes with habitat: ammonia where water is plentiful, urea, and uric acid where water must be saved. excretory structures compared along the animal evolutionary line**
