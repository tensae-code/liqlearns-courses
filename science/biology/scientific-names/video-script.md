Today: Scientific names — genus + epithet.

Remember this one thing: every species has a two-part Latin scientific name: genus with a capital, epithet in lower case.

Linnaeus set out the system in Systema Naturae in 1735 and described thousands of plants and animals.

A scientific name has the genus first with a capital letter and the specific epithet second in lower case.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
