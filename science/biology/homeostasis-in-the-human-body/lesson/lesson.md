# Homeostasis in the human body

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — homeostasis in the human body. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Vasoconstriction is described in skin terms (capillaries constrict, less blood near the surface, the skin looks paler, heat is kept) against vasodilation; pupils are tasked to investigate how kidneys control water and ion levels and — distinctively — to research traditional mechanisms used in their own locality to regulate body homeostasis; the drugs-and-homeostasis section closes the unit by examining smoking, alcohol, khat and cannabis effects on the nervous and endocrine systems together.
