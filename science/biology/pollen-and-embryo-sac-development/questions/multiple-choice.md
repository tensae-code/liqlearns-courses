# Pollen and embryo sac development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) before a flower can be pollinated, its gametes must be built: pollen grains develop in the anther, each carrying the male gametes, and the embryo sac develops inside the ovule, carrying the egg cell — the two developments that pollination and fertilisation join  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
**Answer: a) before a flower can be pollinated, its gametes must be built: pollen grains develop in the anther, each carrying the male gametes, and the embryo sac develops inside the ovule, carrying the egg cell — the two developments that pollination and fertilisation join**

**2. Pollen development — which statement is right?**
a) In the anther, mother cells divide by meiosis to form pollen grains. Each pollen grain is the male gametophyte: it will produce the male gametes that fertilisation needs.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) In the anther, mother cells divide by meiosis to form pollen grains. Each pollen grain is the male gametophyte: it will produce the male gametes that fertilisation needs.**

**3. Embryo sac development — which statement is right?**
a) It is unrelated to the lesson's topic  b) Inside the ovule, a mother cell divides by meiosis and one surviving cell builds the embryo sac — the female gametophyte — which holds the egg cell (the female gamete).  c) It is a myth the book corrects  d) It applies only outside Malaysia
**Answer: b) Inside the ovule, a mother cell divides by meiosis and one surviving cell builds the embryo sac — the female gametophyte — which holds the egg cell (the female gamete).**
