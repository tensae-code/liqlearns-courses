# Pollen and embryo sac development — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: before a flower can be pollinated, its gametes must be built: pollen grains develop in the anther, each carrying the male gametes, and the embryo sac develops inside the ovule, carrying the egg cell — the two developments that pollination and fertilisation join. Inside the ovule, a mother cell divides by meiosis and one surviving cell builds the embryo sac — the female gametophyte — which holds the egg cell (the female gamete).**
