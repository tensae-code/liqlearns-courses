# Pollen and embryo sac development — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: before a flower can be pollinated, its gametes must be built: pollen grains develop in the anther, each carrying the male gametes, and the embryo sac develops inside the ovule, carrying the egg cell — the two developments that pollination and fertilisation join. In the anther, mother cells divide by meiosis to form pollen grains. Each pollen grain is the male gametophyte: it will produce the male gametes that fertilisation needs.**

**2.** Explain Malaysia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Malaysia's KSSM Form 5 book orders its flower chapter this way (Ch.6): structure of a flower (p. 114), then development of pollen grains and the embryo sac (p. 116), then pollination and fertilisation (p. 120) — development before delivery.**
