Today: Pollen and embryo sac development.

Remember this one thing: before a flower can be pollinated, its gametes must be built: pollen grains develop in the anther, each carrying the male gametes, and the embryo sac develops inside the ovule, carrying the egg cell — the two developments that pollination and fertilisation join.

Malaysia's Form 5 book teaches the development of pollen grains and the embryo sac as its own subtopic (printed p. 116), before pollination: the making of the two gamete-carrying structures, anther-side and ovule-side.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
