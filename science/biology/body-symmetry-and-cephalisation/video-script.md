Today: Body symmetry and cephalisation.

Remember this one thing: animal body plans are read by their symmetry — radial in simple animals like sea anemones, bilateral in most others — and cephalisation concentrates sense organs and nerve cells at the front end.

Kuwait's Grade 10 Term 2 student book (Unit 2, Lesson 1-1, printed pp. 19–20) teaches radial symmetry in simple animals like sea anemones, set against bilateral symmetry.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
