# Body symmetry and cephalisation — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: animal body plans are read by their symmetry — radial in simple animals like sea anemones, bilateral in most others — and cephalisation concentrates sense organs and nerve cells at the front end. Kuwait's Grade 10 Term 2 student book (Unit 2, Lesson 1-1, printed pp. 19–20) teaches radial symmetry in simple animals like sea anemones, set against bilateral symmetry.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's animal-kingdom introduction (Grade 10 Term 2, Unit 2 Lesson 1-1) teaches body symmetry and cephalisation as the reading frame for every animal group that follows in the unit. (The held copy of this book is truncated — Unit 1 is absent from the file; Units 2 and 3 are complete, and this lesson stands in Unit 2.)**
