# Body symmetry and cephalisation — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's Grade 10 Term 2 student book (Unit 2, Lesson 1-1, printed pp. 19–20) teaches radial symmetry in simple animals like sea anemones, set against bilateral symmetry.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كتاب الطالب — الصف العاشر — الفصل الدراسي الثاني (Biology — Student Book, Grade 10, Term 2), printed pp. 19–20).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with body symmetry and cephalisation.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is body symmetry and cephalisation.
