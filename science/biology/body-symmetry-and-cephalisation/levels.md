# Body symmetry and cephalisation — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (animal body plans are read by their symmetry — radial in simple animals like sea anemones, bilateral in most others — and cephalisation concentrates sense organs and nerve cells at the front end) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
