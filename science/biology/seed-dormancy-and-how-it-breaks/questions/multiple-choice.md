# Seed dormancy and how it breaks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Dormancy means the seed is…**
a) dead  b) alive but not germinating  c) rotting  d) already a seedling
**Answer: b) alive but not germinating**

**2. The inhibitor hormone named in Kenya's book is…**
a) gibberellin  b) auxin  c) abscisic acid  d) ethene
**Answer: c) abscisic acid**

**3. Wattle seed dormancy is broken in agriculture by…**
a) freezing only  b) boiling, roasting or cracking the seed  c) soaking in oil  d) burying it deeper
**Answer: b) boiling, roasting or cracking the seed**

**4. Scarification breaks dormancy by…**
a) killing the embryo  b) breaking the hard seed coat  c) removing the endosperm  d) adding abscisic acid
**Answer: b) breaking the hard seed coat**
