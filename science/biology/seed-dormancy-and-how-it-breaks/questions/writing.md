# Seed dormancy and how it breaks — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why a farmer would deliberately boil or roast seed before planting it.
**Model answer: The seed is not being cooked — its armour is being broken. Wattle's hard coat keeps water out and the seed dormant past the planting moment; heat from boiling or roasting cracks that coat (scarification), so water enters, dormancy ends, and the whole planting germinates together instead of straggling over weeks.**
