# Seed dormancy and how it breaks — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the causes of seed dormancy in Kenya's Form 3 book.
**Answer: An immature embryo; inhibitors (abscisic acid); and hard seed coats, as in wattle (Ch. 4, printed p. 138).**

**2.** Explain Kenya's way of teaching dormancy.
**Answer: Kenya teaches it as a working agricultural topic, not a definition: the value of the wait, the three causes, then the keys matched to the causes — maturation, gibberellins/cytokinins, light, scarification — ending in the farm practice of boiling, roasting and cracking wattle seed.**
