# Seed dormancy and how it breaks — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The resting state of a viable seed is called _____.**
**Answer: dormancy**

**2. The inhibitor inside a dormant seed is _____ acid.**
**Answer: abscisic**

**3. Breaking a hard coat to wake a seed is _____.**
**Answer: scarification**

**4. Wattle seed is treated by boiling, roasting or _____.**
**Answer: cracking**
