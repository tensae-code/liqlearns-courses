# Seed dormancy and how it breaks — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. A dormant seed can be perfectly alive and viable.**
**Answer: True** — dormancy is rest, not death.

**2. Gibberellins help break dormancy.**
**Answer: True** — with cytokinins, against the inhibitors.

**3. A hard seed coat is one cause of dormancy.**
**Answer: True** — wattle is the book's case.

**4. Dormancy has no value to the plant.**
**Answer: False** — its value is timing — not sprouting into the wrong moment.
