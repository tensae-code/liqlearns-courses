# Video script — Technologies copied from living things

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Termite-mound buildings with constant temperature and humidity (the Eastgate Centre, Harare), swallow-nest building, spider-web weaving, aircraft wings from birds and bats, the camera from the human eye, bio-robot movement from the kangaroo, injection needles from the mosquito proboscis, device sensors from neurons, saws from animal teeth, bulletproof vests from spider silk, ceramics from shellfish shell, and a robotic arm from an elephant's trunk; pupils then build a jointed model human hand from local materials and judge what it still cannot do.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
