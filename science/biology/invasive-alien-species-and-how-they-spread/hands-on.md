# Hands-on — Invasive alien species and how they spread

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Look — without touching — for a garden lupin stand or another garden escapee growing wild near your home. Note where it grows and which native plants grow beside it. Never handle giant hogweed: its sap with sunlight burns skin.

## Teach it on paper
Draw Finland's four invaders from memory — mink, Spanish slug, garden lupin, giant hogweed — with the harm of each written beside it, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
