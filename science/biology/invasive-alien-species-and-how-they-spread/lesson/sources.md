Structural summary: A field-day pack built on five stations, each with a teacher info page, a pupil observation form and group reflection questions: (1) pollinators and insect hotels, (2) deadwood (lahopuu), (3) water bodies, (4) invasive alien species, (5) traditional biotopes (perinnebiotoopit), closing with a 1 m² quadrat species survey and whole-group discussion.
