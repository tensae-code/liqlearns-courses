# Video script — Invasive alien species and how they spread

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Invasive alien species and how they spread.

Remember this one thing: an invasive alien species crossed a barrier it could never cross alone — people carried it — and now it spreads and harms the nature that was there.

Finland's cases, one by one. The mink, escaped from fur farms, devastating the archipelago's birds. The Spanish slug, arrived in 1994, eating almost anything, in mass numbers. The garden lupin, a garden flower crowding out the native plants. And the giant hogweeds, whose sap with sunlight burns the skin like a burn.

The answer Finland teaches is early action: control starts at the first sighting, and people gather in removal work parties to clear the invaders out.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
