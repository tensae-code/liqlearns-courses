# Lesson plan — Invasive alien species and how they spread

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Invasive alien species and how they spread
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — secondary band (CURRICULUM.md Unit 16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** An invasive alien species is a species that has crossed a barrier it could never cross alone — with human help — and then spreads in its new home and harms the nature that was there. Finland's field pack names its cases: the mink, a fur-farm escapee devastating archipelago birds; the Spanish slug, arrived 1994, an omnivorous mass species; the garden lupin, outcompeting native plants; and the giant hogweeds, whose sap with sunlight causes burn-like skin damage. Control starts at the first sighting, with removal work parties.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier biology lessons and builds the secondary-band understanding of invasive alien species and how they spread.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
