# Invasive alien species and how they spread — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What makes a species an invasive alien species?**
a) It is rare and endangered  b) It crossed a naturally impassable barrier with human help, then spreads and harms native nature  c) It is any foreign-looking plant  d) It lives in water
**Answer: b) It crossed a naturally impassable barrier with human help, then spreads and harms native nature**

**2. How did the mink reach Finland's archipelago, and what does it harm?**
a) It swam from America; it harms fish  b) It escaped from fur farms; it devastates archipelago birds  c) It was planted by gardeners; it harms lupins  d) It arrived in 1994; it harms slugs
**Answer: b) It escaped from fur farms; it devastates archipelago birds**

**3. What is dangerous about the giant hogweeds?**
a) Their roots crack roads  b) Their smell drives birds away  c) Their sap, with sunlight, causes burn-like skin damage  d) They eat native plants
**Answer: c) Their sap, with sunlight, causes burn-like skin damage**

**4. When does control of an invasive species work best?**
a) After it has become a mass species  b) At the first sighting  c) Never — nothing can be done  d) Only in winter
**Answer: b) At the first sighting**
