# Invasive alien species and how they spread — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining what an invasive alien species is, using one Finnish case as your example.
**Model answer: An invasive alien species is a species people have carried across a barrier it could never cross alone, which then spreads and harms the nature that was there. In Finland the mink escaped from fur farms and now devastates the birds of the archipelago, which had no defence against it.**
