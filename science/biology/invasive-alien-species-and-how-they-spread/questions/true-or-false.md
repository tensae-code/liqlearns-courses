# Invasive alien species and how they spread — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. The Spanish slug arrived in Finland in 1994 and became an omnivorous mass species.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. The garden lupin helps native plants by giving them shade.**
**Answer: False** — the garden lupin outcompetes native plants and takes their place.

**3. An alien species that never spreads or harms anything is still taught as invasive.**
**Answer: False** — the lesson's definition has three parts: alien, human-carried across a natural barrier, and spreading with harm.

**4. Protective gear is needed when clearing giant hogweed.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches — its sap with sunlight causes burn-like skin damage.
