# Invasive alien species and how they spread — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The _____, a fur-farm escapee, devastates Finland's archipelago birds.**
**Answer: mink**

**2. The Spanish slug arrived in Finland in _____.**
**Answer: 1994**

**3. The garden _____ outcompetes native plants.**
**Answer: lupin**

**4. Control of an invasive species starts at the first _____.**
**Answer: sighting**
