# Invasive alien species and how they spread — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain why the mink is an invasive alien species in Finland, using the lesson's three-part test.
**Answer: The mink is alien to Finland's archipelago nature; it crossed there with human help, escaping from fur farms — a barrier crossing it did not make as a wild colonist of the islands; and it spreads and harms native nature by devastating the archipelago's birds. Alien, human-carried, spreading and harmful: all three parts hold.**

**2.** Explain Finland's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Finland's field pack teaches invasive alien species on a school field day, in the pupils' own near environment: it names Finland's real cases — mink, Spanish slug (arrived 1994), garden lupin and giant hogweeds — gives the harm of each, and teaches control as removal work parties starting at the first sighting, with protective gear for dangerous species.**
