# Hands-on — Agricultural classification of crops

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
List every crop or crop product in your kitchen or a nearby market stall, and sort your list into the book's twelve groups (leave a group empty if nothing fits). Say aloud, for three of them, which useful part put the crop in its group.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a market stall arrangement of Nigerian crops sorted into labelled groups: roots (cassava, yam), cereals (guinea corn), oil crops (oil palm), beverage (kola) — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
