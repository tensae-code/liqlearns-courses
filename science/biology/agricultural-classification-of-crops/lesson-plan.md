# Lesson plan — Agricultural classification of crops

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Agricultural classification of crops
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — appended to Unit 16 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** plants can be classified two ways: botanically, by their natural relationships, and agriculturally, by what they are grown for — Nigeria sorts crops agriculturally into twelve groups by product, useful part and economic importance, with Nigerian crops throughout.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own as a single-skill lesson built from Nigeria's Senior Secondary Biology 1, Ch. 9 (printed p. 137). Related lessons are recommended first where they help, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
