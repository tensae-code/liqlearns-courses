# Agricultural classification of crops — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Cassava and yam are sorted among the root crops.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Agricultural classification is the same thing as botanical classification.
**Answer: False** — Botanical classification sorts by natural relationship; agricultural classification sorts by product, useful part and economic importance.

**3.** Oil palm is the book's example of an oil crop.
**Answer: True**
