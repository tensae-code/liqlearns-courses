# Agricultural classification of crops — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: botanical against agricultural classification of plants.
**Answer: Botanical classification groups plants by their natural relationships. Agricultural classification groups crops by what they are grown for — product, useful part and economic importance — giving twelve groups: root, vegetable, cereal, legume, fibre, oil, latex, beverage, fruit, spice, forage and cash crops.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book opens Chapter 9, the relevance of Biology to agriculture (printed p. 137), by classifying plants twice — botanically and agriculturally — and filling the twelve agricultural groups with Nigerian examples: cassava, yam, guinea corn, benniseed, kola, oil palm.**
