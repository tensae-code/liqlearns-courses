# Agricultural classification of crops — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many agricultural crop groups does the book use?
a) Five
b) Eight
c) Twelve
d) Twenty
**Answer: c) Twelve**

**2.** Guinea corn belongs to which group?
a) Root crops
b) Cereals
c) Latex crops
d) Spices
**Answer: b) Cereals**

**3.** Agricultural classification sorts crops by…
a) flower colour
b) product, useful part and economic importance
c) height
d) leaf shape
**Answer: b) product, useful part and economic importance**

**4.** Kola is the book's example of which group?
a) Fibre
b) Forage
c) Beverage
d) Oil
**Answer: c) Beverage**
