Today: Proving photosynthesis — the starch test.

Remember this one thing: the iodine test sequence shows starch only where light reached green tissue — boil in water, boil in alcohol, rinse, then iodine

De-starching: The plant is de-starched first, so any starch found was made during the test.

The sequence: The test runs: boil in water, boil in alcohol, rinse, then add iodine.
