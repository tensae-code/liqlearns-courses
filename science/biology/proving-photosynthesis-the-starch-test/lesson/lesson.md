# Proving photosynthesis — the starch test

## The idea
How do we know a leaf made food? Test it for starch. This doing-lesson runs the standard proof: a de-starched plant, a covered or variegated leaf, then the four-step iodine test.

## The key facts
**De-starching.** The plant is de-starched first, so any starch found was made during the test.

**The sequence.** The test runs: boil in water, boil in alcohol, rinse, then add iodine.

**The result.** Starch shows only where light reached green tissue.

**What it proves.** The test proves light and green tissue are needed for the leaf to make starch.
