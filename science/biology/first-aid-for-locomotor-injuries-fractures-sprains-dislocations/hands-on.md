# Hands-on — First aid for locomotor injuries — fractures, sprains, dislocations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: locomotor injuries — muscle strains and tears among them — happen when a muscle or joint is stretched or loaded beyond its capacity; this lesson covers the injuries of the support-and-movement system and their first care.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: an arm with a torn muscle fibre bundle highlighted, beside small labelled sketches of a sprained joint and a bandaged fracture splint. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
