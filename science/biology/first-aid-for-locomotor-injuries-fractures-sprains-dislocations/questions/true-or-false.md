# First aid for locomotor injuries — fractures, sprains, dislocations — true or false

**1. Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-4, printed p. 136) teaches muscle strain and tear: over-stretching or overloading a muscle beyond its capacity tears its fibres.**

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كتاب الطالب — الصف العاشر — الفصل الدراسي الأول (Biology — Student Book, Grade 10, Term 1), printed p. 136).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with first aid for locomotor injuries — fractures, sprains, dislocations.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is first aid for locomotor injuries — fractures, sprains, dislocations.
