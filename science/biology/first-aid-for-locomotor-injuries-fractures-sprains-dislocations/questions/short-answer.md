# First aid for locomotor injuries — fractures, sprains, dislocations — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: locomotor injuries — muscle strains and tears among them — happen when a muscle or joint is stretched or loaded beyond its capacity; this lesson covers the injuries of the support-and-movement system and their first care. Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-4, printed p. 136) teaches muscle strain and tear: over-stretching or overloading a muscle beyond its capacity tears its fibres.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait closes its muscular-system lesson (Lesson 2-4) with the system's own injuries — strain and tear defined by their cause, load beyond capacity — so first care is taught where the anatomy that fails has just been learned.**
