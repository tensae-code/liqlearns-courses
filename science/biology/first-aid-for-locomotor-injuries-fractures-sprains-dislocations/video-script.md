Today: First aid for locomotor injuries — fractures, sprains, dislocations.

Remember this one thing: locomotor injuries — muscle strains and tears among them — happen when a muscle or joint is stretched or loaded beyond its capacity; this lesson covers the injuries of the support-and-movement system and their first care.

Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-4, printed p. 136) teaches muscle strain and tear: over-stretching or overloading a muscle beyond its capacity tears its fibres.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
