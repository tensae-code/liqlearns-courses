# Hands-on — Hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the nervous system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: the nervous system has its common disorders — multiple sclerosis among them — and its health is kept by named habits: the lesson covers the system's dysfunctions and how the system is kept healthy.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a neuron with its myelin sheath shown damaged in patches, labelled multiple sclerosis, beside a short list of habits that keep the nervous system healthy. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
