# Hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the nervous system — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: the nervous system has its common disorders — multiple sclerosis among them — and its health is kept by named habits: the lesson covers the system's dysfunctions and how the system is kept healthy. Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 1 student book (Lesson 5-1, printed p. 51) gives the nervous system a health lesson of its own: the system's common disorders, multiple sclerosis among them.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's Grade 12 Pearson frame teaches every system as structure, then physiology, then a closing "health of the system" lesson — Lesson 5-1 is the nervous system's: its disorders, multiple sclerosis named, and its hygiene.**
