# Hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the nervous system — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 1 student book (Lesson 5-1, printed p. 51) gives the nervous system a health lesson of its own: the system's common disorders, multiple sclerosis among them.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كتاب الطالب — الصف الثاني عشر — الفصل الدراسي الأول (Biology — Student Book, Grade 12, Term 1), printed p. 51).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the nervous system.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the nervous system.
