# Hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the nervous system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) the nervous system has its common disorders — multiple sclerosis among them — and its health is kept by named habits: the lesson covers the system's dysfunctions and how the system is kept healthy  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life

**2. Nervous system health — which statement is right?**
a) Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 1 student book (Lesson 5-1, printed p. 51) gives the nervous system a health lesson of its own: the system's common disorders, multiple sclerosis among them.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 1 student book (Lesson 5-1, printed p. 51) gives the nervous system a health lesson of its own: the system's common disorders, multiple sclerosis among them.**

**3. Which statement about this lesson's topic is right?**
