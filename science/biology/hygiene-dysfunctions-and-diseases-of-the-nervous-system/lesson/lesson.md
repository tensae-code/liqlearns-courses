## The key facts
**Nervous system health.** Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 1 student book (Lesson 5-1, printed p. 51) gives the nervous system a health lesson of its own: the system's common disorders, multiple sclerosis among them.

**Keeping it healthy.** The same lesson sets out how the system is kept healthy — disorders and care taught together, as the closing lesson of the nervous-system run.

**Kuwait's way.** Kuwait's Grade 12 Pearson frame teaches every system as structure, then physiology, then a closing "health of the system" lesson — Lesson 5-1 is the nervous system's: its disorders, multiple sclerosis named, and its hygiene.
