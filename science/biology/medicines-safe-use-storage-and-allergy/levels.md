# Medicines — safe use, storage and allergy — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Medicines need care like any powerful tool: they are stored away from bathrooms, sinks and cleaning materials, and anyone taking a medicine should know the symptoms of an allergic reaction to it — the closing lesson of Mozambique's PESD I Módulo 4 puts medicine care inside the biology course itself.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by reciting the three forbidden neighbours without looking — bathrooms, sinks, cleaning materials — and stating the lesson's second half: knowing the symptoms of an allergic reaction to medicines.
