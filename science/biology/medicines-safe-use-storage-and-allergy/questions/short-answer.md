# Medicines — safe use, storage and allergy — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the module's storage rule for medicines.
**Answer: Medicines are stored away from bathrooms, sinks and cleaning materials — kept apart from the home's moisture and its cleaning chemicals.**

**2.** Explain Mozambique's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Mozambique's PESD I Módulo 4 de Biologia (2023) closes — Lesson 17 — with medicines inside the biology course: care with medicines, their storage away from bathrooms, sinks and cleaning materials, and the symptoms of allergic reaction to medicines.**
