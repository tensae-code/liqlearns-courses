# Medicines — safe use, storage and allergy — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why a biology course ends with a lesson about the home medicine shelf.
**Model answer: The module has taught the body, its infections and its diseases; medicines are where that knowledge meets the home. Storing them wrongly — among bathroom moisture and cleaning chemicals — degrades or endangers them, and an unrecognised allergic reaction is a health risk. So the course closes by turning biology into household practice.**
