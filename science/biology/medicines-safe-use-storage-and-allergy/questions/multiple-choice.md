# Medicines — safe use, storage and allergy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Medicines should be stored away from…**
a) bedrooms  b) bathrooms, sinks and cleaning materials  c) food cupboards only  d) windows only
**Answer: b) bathrooms, sinks and cleaning materials**

**2. Why does the lesson name bathrooms and sinks?**
a) they are cold  b) they are the home's moisture places  c) they are dark  d) they are locked
**Answer: b) they are the home's moisture places**

**3. An allergic reaction to a medicine is…**
a) impossible  b) something whose symptoms the lesson teaches you to know  c) a sign the medicine is working  d) only a children's problem
**Answer: b) something whose symptoms the lesson teaches you to know**

**4. This lesson closes which module?**
a) PESD I Módulo 1 (2023)  b) PESD I Módulo 4 (2023)  c) PESD II Módulo 7  d) the chemistry course
**Answer: b) PESD I Módulo 4 (2023)**
