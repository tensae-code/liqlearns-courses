# Medicines — safe use, storage and allergy — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Storing medicines beside cleaning materials is good practice.**
**Answer: False** — the rule is the opposite: away from cleaning materials.

**2. Bathrooms are a recommended place for medicines.**
**Answer: False** — medicines are stored away from bathrooms and sinks.

**3. The symptoms of allergic reaction to medicines are part of this lesson.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. This is the closing lesson of its module.**
**Answer: True** — Lesson 17 closes PESD I Módulo 4.
