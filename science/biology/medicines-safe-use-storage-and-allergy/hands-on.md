# Hands-on — Medicines — safe use, storage and allergy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Audit your own home like the lesson teaches: find where medicines are kept, and check the place against the rule — is it away from the bathroom, the sink and the cleaning materials? Move nothing without the household's say-so, but report what you found.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — a home medicine box on a dry shelf with a tick beside it, and crossed-out drawings of a bathroom cabinet, a kitchen sink and a shelf of cleaning bottles below — with a small panel showing a person checking their skin and breathing for signs of allergy — label its parts, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
