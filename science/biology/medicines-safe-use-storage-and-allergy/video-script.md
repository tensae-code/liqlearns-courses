Today: Medicines — safe use, storage and allergy.

Remember this one thing: medicines need care like any powerful tool: they are stored away from bathrooms, sinks and cleaning materials, and anyone taking a medicine should know the symptoms of an allergic reaction to it — the closing lesson of Mozambique's PESD I Módulo 4 puts medicine care inside the biology course itself.

The last lesson of the module is about the medicines in your own home. Keep them away from bathrooms, away from sinks, away from cleaning materials — moisture and chemicals are no place for medicines. And know this: a body can react to a medicine with allergy. Learn the symptoms of an allergic reaction, so you recognise one when it comes.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
