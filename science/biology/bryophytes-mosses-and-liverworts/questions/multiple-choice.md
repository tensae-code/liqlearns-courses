# Bryophytes — mosses and liverworts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Bryophytes are called the amphibians of the plant kingdom because…**
a) they live in the sea  b) they are protected on land but still need liquid water for fertilisation  c) they have flowers in water  d) they eat insects
**Answer: b) they are protected on land but still need liquid water for fertilisation**

**2. A moss's three parts are…**
a) root, trunk, leaf  b) rhizoids, cauloids, phylloids  c) spore, gamete, seed  d) thallus, rhizome, frond
**Answer: b) rhizoids, cauloids, phylloids**

**3. Bryophytes stay small — under about…**
a) 30 cm  b) 3 m  c) 30 m  d) 3 cm
**Answer: a) 30 cm**

**4. The liverwort body is described as…**
a) woody  b) thalloid — a flat lobed sheet  c) a spiral shell  d) a feather
**Answer: b) thalloid — a flat lobed sheet**
