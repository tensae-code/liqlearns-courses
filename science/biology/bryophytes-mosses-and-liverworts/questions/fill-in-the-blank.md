# Bryophytes — mosses and liverworts — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Plants without conducting vessels are called _____.**
**Answer: avascular**

**2. The moss's root-like anchors are called _____.**
**Answer: rhizoids**

**3. The liverwort's flat body is called _____ .**
**Answer: thalloid**

**4. The module's example moss is _____.**
**Answer: Funaria**
