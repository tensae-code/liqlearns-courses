# Bryophytes — mosses and liverworts — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining what keeps the bryophytes small and close to the water.
**Model answer: Bryophytes have no conducting vessels, so water and food move only slowly through the plant — no bryophyte can grow tall, and all stay under about 30 cm. Their fertilisation also needs liquid water for the swimming gametes to meet. So they live low, in damp places, protected by a cuticle but never free of the wet.**
