# Bryophytes — mosses and liverworts — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Bryophytes have conducting vessels like the ferns.**
**Answer: False** — they are avascular — no conducting vessels.

**2. A cuticle protects bryophytes on land.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Funaria is the module's example of a liverwort.**
**Answer: False** — Funaria is the example of a moss; liverworts are the thalloid group.

**4. Mosses have an importance in ecosystems.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches — the module closes with it.
