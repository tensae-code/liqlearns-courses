# Stem cells and totipotency — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (a totipotent cell can make a whole organism, and stem cells carry that power at every scale: Steward's carrot grown from one cell (1958), Dolly and the cloned monkeys Zhongzhong and Huahua from a body cell's nucleus, haematopoietic stem cells rebuilding blood, and iPS cells — a patient's own cells reprogrammed in 2006 — promising the same without an embryo and without rejection, the tumour risk stated plainly) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
