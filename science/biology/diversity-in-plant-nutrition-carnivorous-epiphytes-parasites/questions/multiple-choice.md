# Diversity in plant nutrition — carnivorous, epiphytes and parasites — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) not every plant feeds the standard way: carnivorous plants trap and digest insects for minerals, epiphytes live on other plants for support while making their own food, and parasitic plants steal food or water from a host  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
**Answer: a) not every plant feeds the standard way: carnivorous plants trap and digest insects for minerals, epiphytes live on other plants for support while making their own food, and parasitic plants steal food or water from a host**

**2. Carnivorous plants — which statement is right?**
a) Pitcher plants and their kin grow where the soil is poor in minerals. They still photosynthesise, but they trap and digest insects to gain the nitrogen and minerals the soil lacks.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) Pitcher plants and their kin grow where the soil is poor in minerals. They still photosynthesise, but they trap and digest insects to gain the nitrogen and minerals the soil lacks.**

**3. Epiphytes — which statement is right?**
a) It is unrelated to the lesson's topic  b) An epiphyte grows on another plant — a fern or orchid on a tree trunk — using it only for support and a place in the light. It makes its own food and takes nothing from its host.  c) It is a myth the book corrects  d) It applies only outside Malaysia
**Answer: b) An epiphyte grows on another plant — a fern or orchid on a tree trunk — using it only for support and a place in the light. It makes its own food and takes nothing from its host.**
