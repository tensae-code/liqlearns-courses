# Diversity in plant nutrition — carnivorous, epiphytes and parasites — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: not every plant feeds the standard way: carnivorous plants trap and digest insects for minerals, epiphytes live on other plants for support while making their own food, and parasitic plants steal food or water from a host. An epiphyte grows on another plant — a fern or orchid on a tree trunk — using it only for support and a place in the light. It makes its own food and takes nothing from its host.**
