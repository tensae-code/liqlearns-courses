Today: Diversity in plant nutrition — carnivorous, epiphytes and parasites.

Remember this one thing: not every plant feeds the standard way: carnivorous plants trap and digest insects for minerals, epiphytes live on other plants for support while making their own food, and parasitic plants steal food or water from a host.

Malaysia's Form 5 book sets a whole subtopic on the diversity of plant nutrition (printed p. 68): beside the ordinary green plant stand three other ways of living — catching animals, perching on other plants, and stealing from a host.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
