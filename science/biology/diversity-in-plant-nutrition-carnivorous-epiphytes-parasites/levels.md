# Diversity in plant nutrition — carnivorous, epiphytes and parasites — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (not every plant feeds the standard way: carnivorous plants trap and digest insects for minerals, epiphytes live on other plants for support while making their own food, and parasitic plants steal food or water from a host) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
