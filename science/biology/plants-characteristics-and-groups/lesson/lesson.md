# Plants — characteristics and groups

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Plants — characteristics and groups. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
What makes a plant a plant? Plants share clear marks, and they sort into major groups — flowering and non-flowering first of all.

[Picture 22204 — a flowering plant and a conifer side by side with the shared plant marks labelled]

## The key facts
**Making food.** Plants make their own food.

**Rooted in place.** Plants are rooted in place and do not travel from spot to spot.

**Cell walls.** Plant cells have cell walls.

**The first sort.** Plants sort first into flowering and non-flowering groups.
