# Plants — characteristics and groups — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about making food?
a) Plants make their own food  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Plants make their own food**

**2.** What does this lesson say about rooted in place?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Plants are rooted in place and do not travel from spot to spot  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Plants are rooted in place and do not travel from spot to spot**

**3.** What does this lesson say about cell walls?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Plant cells have cell walls  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Plant cells have cell walls**

**4.** What does this lesson say about the first sort?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Plants sort first into flowering and non-flowering groups
**Answer: d) Plants sort first into flowering and non-flowering groups**
