# First aid basics — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: first help, given at once.
**Answer: First aid is the help given first, on the spot, to a person who is injured or suddenly ill. Its work is to keep the person alive and stop the harm growing worse until medical help takes over — it does not replace the doctor; it bridges the time before the doctor.**

**2.** Explain: safety and air first.
**Answer: The first-aider first makes the place safe — for the casualty and for themselves — then checks the person: are they conscious, is the airway open, are they breathing? A blocked airway or stopped breathing is dealt with before anything else, because the body can go only minutes without air.**
