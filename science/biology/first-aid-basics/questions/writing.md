# First aid basics — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining first aid basics, using the lesson's key facts.
**Model answer: First aid is the first help given to a person who is injured or suddenly taken ill, before medical help takes over: keep the person and yourself safe, keep the airway open and breathing going, stop bleeding, keep the person still and warm, and send for help. First aid is the help given first, on the spot, to a person who is injured or suddenly ill. Its work is to keep the person alive and stop the harm growing worse until medical help takes over — it does not replace the doctor; it bridges the time before the doctor. First aid stands in the book as §8.5, the closing section of the Social Biology chapter — Fiji teaches it as part of biology itself, the last thing the chapter's health teaching asks of the learner: not only to know the body, but to act for it in the first minutes.**
