# First aid basics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about first aid basics?
a) First aid is the first help given to a person who is injured or suddenly taken ill, before medical help takes over: keep the person and yourself safe, keep the airway open and breathing going, stop bleeding, keep the person still and warm, and send for help.
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) First aid is the first help given to a person who is injured or suddenly taken ill, before medical help takes over: keep the person and yourself safe, keep the airway open and breathing going, stop bleeding, keep the person still and warm, and send for help.**

**2.** What does this lesson say about first help, given at once?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) First aid is the help given first, on the spot, to a person who is injured or suddenly ill.
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) First aid is the help given first, on the spot, to a person who is injured or suddenly ill.**

A blocked airway or stopped breathing is dealt with before anything else, because the body can go only minutes without air..**

**4.** What does this lesson say about stop the bleeding, keep still and warm?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Bleeding is pressed on directly to slow the blood loss.
