# Hands-on — First aid basics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Practise the order on a companion (no real injury): say the steps aloud in order — safe place, check the person, airway and breathing, press on bleeding, keep still and warm, send for help — while your companion names what you are doing at each step. Then swap.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of a first-aid scene in order — a helper kneeling by a hurt person, one hand pressing a cloth on a bleeding arm, the person lying still and covered warm, and a second person running for help — each step numbered — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
