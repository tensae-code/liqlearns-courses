# Hands-on — Locomotion across animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Move like the chapter: first flow an arm forward slowly like a pseudopodium, then mime the earthworm — stretch long and thin, then shorten and grip — then stand and mime the duck's flapping flight. Say aloud, at each step, which engine you are using and which animal it belongs to.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a row of movers in order of size: an amoeba reaching out a pseudopodium, a Euglena with its flagellum, an earthworm stretching and gripping, a grasshopper's folded leg, a fish swimming, and a duck in flapping flight — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
