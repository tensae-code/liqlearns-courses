# Locomotion across animals — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the engines of movement in Iraq's Grade 11 locomotion chapter, in order.
**Answer: Unicellular organisms: the amoeba's pseudopodia (endoplasm streaming) and Euglena's flagellum. Then muscle as the engine: the earthworm's hydrostatic movement and the grasshopper's leg muscles. Then the vertebrates: swimming, crawling, and bird flight — the ordinary flapping flight of strong birds such as the duck (Ch. 4, printed p. 112).**
