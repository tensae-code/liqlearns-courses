# Locomotion across animals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The amoeba moves by…
a) flapping wings
b) pseudopodia, worked by endoplasm streaming
c) a hydrostatic skeleton only
d) fins
**Answer: b) pseudopodia, worked by endoplasm streaming**

**2.** Euglena's moving organ is its…
a) pseudopodium
b) flagellum
c) leg
d) fin
**Answer: b) flagellum**

**3.** The earthworm's movement is called…
a) flapping flight
b) hydrostatic movement
c) crawling on legs
d) jet movement
**Answer: b) hydrostatic movement**

**4.** Iraq's chapter gives the duck as an example of…
a) hydrostatic movement
b) ordinary flapping flight of strong birds
c) a unicellular mover
d) a crawler
**Answer: b) ordinary flapping flight of strong birds**
