# Locomotion across animals — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (animals move by many different engines — Iraq's Grade 11 Biology book traces movement from the amoeba's flowing pseudopodia and Euglena's flagellum, through the earthworm's hydrostatic body and the grasshopper's leg muscles, to vertebrate swimming, crawling and the flapping flight of strong birds such as the duck) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: applying the lesson to a new example from the learner's own surroundings, and explaining Iraq's way of teaching it, without looking.
