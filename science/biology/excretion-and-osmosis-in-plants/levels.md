# Excretion and osmosis in plants — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Plants have no kidneys and no single excretory system: they get rid of waste through several small mechanisms — excess water leaves by transpiration and guttation, and other wastes are stored away in leaves, bark and resins that are shed — and they keep their water balance by osmotic adjustments, changing the solute content of their cells so water moves the way the plant needs.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
