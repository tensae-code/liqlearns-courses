# Excretion and osmosis in plants — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Excess water leaving a plant as vapour is called _____.**
**Answer: transpiration**

**2. Water droplets at a leaf tip early in the morning are _____.**
**Answer: guttation**

**3. Wastes locked in old leaves leave the plant when the leaves _____.**
**Answer: fall (are shed)**

**4. Water moves by _____ toward the side with more dissolved solute.**
**Answer: osmosis**
