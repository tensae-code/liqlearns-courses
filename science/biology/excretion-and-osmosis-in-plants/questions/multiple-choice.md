# Excretion and osmosis in plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Plants get rid of waste…**
a) through kidneys b) through several mechanisms spread over the body, with no special excretory system c) only through roots d) never — plants make no waste
**Answer: b) through several mechanisms spread over the body, with no special excretory system**

**2. Guttation is…**
a) loss of water as vapour b) loss of excess water as liquid droplets at leaf tips and edges c) shedding of bark d) uptake of minerals
**Answer: b) loss of excess water as liquid droplets at leaf tips and edges**

**3. A plant stores some wastes in…**
a) its flowers only b) old leaves, bark, resins and vacuoles, shed with the part c) the soil d) its seeds only
**Answer: b) old leaves, bark, resins and vacuoles, shed with the part**

**4. Osmotic adjustment is a plant…**
a) making seeds b) changing the solute content of its cells to manage its water balance c) closing its flowers at night d) shedding leaves
**Answer: b) changing the solute content of its cells to manage its water balance**
