# Excretion and osmosis in plants — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: how a plant excretes without kidneys.
**Answer: A plant spreads excretion over its body: excess water leaves by transpiration and by guttation at leaf tips, and other wastes are stored where they do no harm — in old leaves, bark, resins and vacuoles — and leave when those parts are shed.**

**2.** Explain: Pakistan (Punjab)'s way of teaching this.
**Answer: Punjab's Grade 9 SNC book makes its ninth chapter Plant Physiology and closes it with two named sections — mechanisms for excretion in plants (9.7) and osmotic adjustments in plants (9.8), printed pp. 140–142 — teaching plant excretion and osmoregulation as topics of their own at grade 9.**
