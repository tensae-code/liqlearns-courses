# Excretion and osmosis in plants — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining how a plant keeps its water balance without any organ for the job.
**Model answer: A plant balances water by osmosis: by adjusting the solute content of its cells it draws water in or holds it against dry or salty surroundings, keeping its cells firm. The water it cannot use is not stored up — it leaves by transpiration and guttation.**
