# Excretion and osmosis in plants — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Plants have a pair of kidneys like animals.**
**Answer: False** — plants have no special excretory organs; excretion is spread over the whole body

**2. Shedding a leaf can be a way for a plant to get rid of stored waste.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches

**3. Osmotic adjustment needs a pump.**
**Answer: False** — it works by changing solute content so osmosis itself moves the water

**4. Punjab's Grade 9 book teaches plant excretion in the human-physiology chapter.**
**Answer: False** — it teaches it in the Plant Physiology chapter (Ch 9.7–9.8) — grade-9 physiology in Punjab is plant physiology
