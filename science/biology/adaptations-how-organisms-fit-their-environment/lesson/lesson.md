# Adaptations — how organisms fit their environment

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Adaptations — how organisms fit their environment. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Organisms carry adaptations — features that fit them to their environment — as Greece's Mediterranean plants show: pine and olive hold small, hard, needle-like leaves against dry summers while the plane tree by the water spreads broad leaves, so the same life function is fitted differently in dry and wet habitats. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from Greece's Gymnasio and Lykeio biology books, which teach it as a named topic.
