Today: Adaptations — how organisms fit their environment.

Remember this one thing: organisms carry adaptations — features that fit them to their environment — as Greece's Mediterranean plants show: pine and olive hold small, hard, needle-like leaves against dry summers while the plane tree by the water spreads broad leaves, so the same life function is fitted differently in dry and wet habitats.

interactions and adaptations: pine/olive needle-like hard leaves vs plane tree in dry vs wet habitats, taught by comparing Greek Mediterranean plants
