# Harmful traditional practices and health — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Practices violating the right to live free from harm: about twenty are recorded in Ethiopia, half involving cutting of skin/body parts, female genital mutilation alone accounting for a large share (the book gives 73%); the reproductive-health harms are worked through early marriage, other cutting practices and FGM — partial or total removal of external female genitalia.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Beyond the student book's FGM definition, the guide lists the unclassified forms teachers should be able to name: pricking, piercing or incising the clitoris or labia, cauterisation by burning, scraping tissue around the vaginal opening or cutting the vagina, and applying corrosive substances or herbs to tighten or narrow the opening.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
