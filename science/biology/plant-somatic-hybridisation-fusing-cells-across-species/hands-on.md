# Hands-on — Plant somatic hybridisation — fusing cells across species

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: plant somatic hybridisation fuses whole cells across species: walls removed by cellulase and pectinase, protoplasts fused by PEG, high-Ca²⁺/high-pH or an electric pulse, and the hybrid cell grown through callus into a plant — the tomato–potato that did neither job well told honestly among China's citrus successes.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: two wall-less plant cells of different colours merging into one cell with both colours inside, then a callus lump, then a small plant. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
