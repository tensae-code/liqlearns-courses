# The breathing system — air's path to the alveoli

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The breathing system — air's path to the alveoli. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Hold your breath — then trace where the next breath goes. Every station on air's path has a job, from the nose that warms it to the alveoli where gases swap.

[Picture 22260 — the whole air path drawn from nostril to one alveolar sac]

## The key facts
**The path.** Air's path runs nose and nasal cavity, pharynx, larynx, trachea, bronchi and bronchioles.

**Alveoli.** The path ends in alveolar sacs, where gas exchange occurs.

**Voice box.** Sound is formed in the larynx, at the vocal cords.

**Epiglottis.** The epiglottis guards the trachea when you swallow.
