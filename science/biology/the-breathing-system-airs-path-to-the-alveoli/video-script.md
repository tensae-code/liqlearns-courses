Today: The breathing system — air's path to the alveoli.

Remember this one thing: air travels nose, pharynx, larynx, trachea, bronchi and bronchioles to the alveolar sacs where gas exchange occurs — with the voice made in the larynx and the epiglottis guarding the trachea

The path: Air's path runs nose and nasal cavity, pharynx, larynx, trachea, bronchi and bronchioles.

Alveoli: The path ends in alveolar sacs, where gas exchange occurs.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
