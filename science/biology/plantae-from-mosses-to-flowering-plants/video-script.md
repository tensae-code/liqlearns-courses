Today: Plantae — from mosses to flowering plants.

Remember this one thing: plants make food by photosynthesis, and their divisions run from mosses and ferns to seed plants.

Plants make food by photosynthesis with chlorophyll in chloroplasts (some parasitic exceptions exist).

Simple non-seed plants include bryophytes (mosses, liverworts, hornworts) and ferns.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
