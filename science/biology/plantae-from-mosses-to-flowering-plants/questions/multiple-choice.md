# Plantae — from mosses to flowering plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about photosynthesis?
a) Plants make food by photosynthesis with chlorophyll in chloroplasts (some parasitic exceptions exist)
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Plants make food by photosynthesis with chlorophyll in chloroplasts (some parasitic exceptions exist)**

**2.** What does this lesson say about bryophytes and ferns?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Simple non-seed plants include bryophytes (mosses, liverworts, hornworts) and ferns
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Simple non-seed plants include bryophytes (mosses, liverworts, hornworts) and ferns**

**3.** What does this lesson say about seed plants?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Seed plants are gymnosperms and the flowering angiosperms
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Seed plants are gymnosperms and the flowering angiosperms**

**4.** What does this lesson say about angiosperms?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Angiosperms are the most diverse plant group and split into dicots and monocots
**Answer: d) Angiosperms are the most diverse plant group and split into dicots and monocots**
