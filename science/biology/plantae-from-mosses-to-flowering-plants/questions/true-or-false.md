# Plantae — from mosses to flowering plants — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Plants make food by photosynthesis with chlorophyll in chloroplasts (some parasitic exceptions exist).
**Answer: True**

**2.** Bryophytes and ferns has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Seed plants are gymnosperms and the flowering angiosperms.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Angiosperms has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
