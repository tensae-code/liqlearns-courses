# Plantae — from mosses to flowering plants

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Plantae — from mosses to flowering plants. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Plants are multicellular eukaryotes with chlorophyll in chloroplasts. Their story runs from tiny mosses to the flowering plants that dominate the land.

[Picture 21021 — a moss, a fern, a conifer and a flowering plant in a row]

## The key facts
**Photosynthesis.** Plants make food by photosynthesis with chlorophyll in chloroplasts (some parasitic exceptions exist).

**Bryophytes and ferns.** Simple non-seed plants include bryophytes (mosses, liverworts, hornworts) and ferns.

**Seed plants.** Seed plants are gymnosperms and the flowering angiosperms.

**Angiosperms.** Angiosperms are the most diverse plant group and split into dicots and monocots.
