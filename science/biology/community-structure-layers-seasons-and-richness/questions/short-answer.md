# Community structure — layers, seasons and richness — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: a community has a structure: its species are layered vertically, patterned horizontally and changed with the seasons, and its species richness — how many species it holds — is its measure. vertical layering, horizontal patterning and seasonal change (Poyang Lake's wintering birds) as the three faces of community structure, with species richness as its measure.**

**2.** Explain China's way of teaching this.
**Answer: vertical layering, horizontal patterning and seasonal change (Poyang Lake's wintering birds) as the three faces of community structure, with species richness as its measure.**
