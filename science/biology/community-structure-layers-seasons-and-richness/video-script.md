Today: Community structure — layers, seasons and richness.

Remember this one thing: a community has a structure: its species are layered vertically, patterned horizontally and changed with the seasons, and its species richness — how many species it holds — is its measure.

vertical layering, horizontal patterning and seasonal change (Poyang Lake's wintering birds) as the three faces of community structure, with species richness as its measure.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
