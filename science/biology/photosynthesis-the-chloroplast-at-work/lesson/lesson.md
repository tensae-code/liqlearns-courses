## The idea
The plant's central work — photosynthesis — happens in one small structure: the chloroplast. Its inside membranes are the working surface, and not all colours of light drive it equally.

[Picture 22218 — a chloroplast diagram with its internal membranes labelled]

## The key facts
**Where it happens.** Photosynthesis takes place in the chloroplast.

**Structure fits function.** The chloroplast's structure is matched to its function.

**Action spectrum.** The action spectrum shows which wavelengths of light drive photosynthesis hardest.

**Green tissue.** Photosynthesis happens in the plant's green tissue, where chloroplasts are.
