# Photosynthesis — the chloroplast at work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about where it happens?

**Answer: a) Photosynthesis takes place in the chloroplast**

**2.** What does this lesson say about structure fits function?

**Answer: b) The chloroplast's structure is matched to its function**

**Answer: c) The action spectrum shows which wavelengths of light drive photosynthesis hardest**
