# Techniques of molecular biology

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Techniques of molecular biology. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Molecular biology works through a bench of named techniques — extracting and cutting DNA, separating fragments by gel electrophoresis, multiplying them by PCR, reading their sequence and joining them anew — and Greece's Grade 12 appendix gathers twenty pages of them as the toolkit behind every genetics chapter. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from Greece's Gymnasio and Lykeio biology books, which teach it as a named topic.
