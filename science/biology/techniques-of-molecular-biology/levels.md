# Techniques of molecular biology — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (molecular biology works through a bench of named techniques — extracting and cutting DNA, separating fragments by gel electrophoresis, multiplying them by PCR, reading their sequence and joining them anew — and Greece's Grade 12 appendix gathers twenty pages of them as the toolkit behind every genetics chapter) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
