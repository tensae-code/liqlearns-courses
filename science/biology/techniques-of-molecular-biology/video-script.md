Today: Techniques of molecular biology.

Remember this one thing: molecular biology works through a bench of named techniques — extracting and cutting DNA, separating fragments by gel electrophoresis, multiplying them by PCR, reading their sequence and joining them anew — and Greece's Grade 12 appendix gathers twenty pages of them as the toolkit behind every genetics chapter.

"Techniques used in Biology" appendix (20 pages of named laboratory techniques)
