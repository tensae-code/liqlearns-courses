# Gaseous exchange in insects and fish — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences comparing how an insect and a fish bring the gas they need to their exchange surfaces.
**Model answer: The insect brings air to the tissues: spiracles open into tracheae that branch right to the cells, helped by abdominal pumping, so the medium travels the last stretch itself. The fish brings water to the blood instead: its pumps drive a one-way stream over the gill filaments, and counter-current blood flow squeezes the most out of every pass.**
