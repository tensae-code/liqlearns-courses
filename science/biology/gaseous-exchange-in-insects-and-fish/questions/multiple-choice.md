# Gaseous exchange in insects and fish — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Air enters an insect's tracheal system through…**
a) gills  b) spiracles with valves and hairs  c) the mouth only  d) the skin
**Answer: b) spiracles with valves and hairs**

**2. A mosquito larva breathes at the surface through a…**
a) plastron  b) siphon  c) gill bar  d) operculum
**Answer: b) siphon**

**3. Counter-current flow means…**
a) blood and water flow in the same direction  b) blood flows opposite to the water across the gill filaments  c) water stops while blood flows  d) the fish pumps air
**Answer: b) blood flows opposite to the water across the gill filaments**

**4. The parts of a bony fish's gill named in Kenya's book are…**
a) bar, filaments and rakers  b) spiracles, valves and hairs  c) siphon and trumpets  d) alveoli and bronchi
**Answer: a) bar, filaments and rakers**
