# Gaseous exchange in insects and fish — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. An insect's abdomen pumping helps move air through its tracheae.**
**Answer: True** — abdominal pumping is part of the system.

**2. Aquatic insects have no use for air.**
**Answer: False** — they breathe air through siphons, trumpets, tracheal gills or a plastron.

**3. In counter-current flow, blood and water move in the same direction.**
**Answer: False** — they move in opposite directions.

**4. Gill rakers are part of the bony fish's gill.**
**Answer: True** — bar, filaments and rakers.
