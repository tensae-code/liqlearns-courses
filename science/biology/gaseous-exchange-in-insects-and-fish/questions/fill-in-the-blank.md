# Gaseous exchange in insects and fish — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The openings of the insect tracheal system are called _____.**
**Answer: spiracles**

**2. A permanent air film held against an aquatic insect's body is a _____.**
**Answer: plastron**

**3. Water is driven over a fish's gills by the buccal and _____ pumps.**
**Answer: opercular**

**4. Blood flowing against the water in a gill is _____ flow.**
**Answer: counter-current**
