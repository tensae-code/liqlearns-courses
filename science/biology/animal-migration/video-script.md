# Video script — Animal migration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Animal migration.

Remember this one thing: animals migrate — they travel between places as the seasons change — and the journeys have causes and set routes, from the “explosive” migration of lemmings to the migration schemes of salmon and eels.

Moldova's Grade 7 book (5.2 Migrations in animals) teaches: migration causes, "explosive" migration of lemmings, salmon and eel migration schemes. (printed p. 139).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
