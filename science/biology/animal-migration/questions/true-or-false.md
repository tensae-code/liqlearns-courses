# Animal migration — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Moldova's Grade 7 book (5.2 Migrations in animals) teaches: migration causes, "explosive" migration of lemmings, salmon and eel migration schemes. (printed p. 139).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Moldova's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Biologie: manual pentru clasa a 7-a (Grade 7)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with animal migration.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is animal migration.
