# Animal migration — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: animals migrate — they travel between places as the seasons change — and the journeys have causes and set routes, from the “explosive” migration of lemmings to the migration schemes of salmon and eels. Moldova's Grade 7 book (5.2 Migrations in animals) teaches: migration causes, "explosive" migration of lemmings, salmon and eel migration schemes. (printed p. 139).**

**2.** Explain Moldova's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Moldova's Grade 7 book teaches migration through its causes and its schemes — lemmings, salmon and eels drawn as migration schemes.**
