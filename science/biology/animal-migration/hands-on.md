# Hands-on — Animal migration

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: animals migrate — they travel between places as the seasons change — and the journeys have causes and set routes, from the “explosive” migration of lemmings to the migration schemes of salmon and eels.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a map with arrows showing migration routes: lemmings moving over land, a salmon swimming up a river and an eel crossing the sea. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
