# Parts of a flower

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Parts of a flower. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
The flower is the angiosperm's reproductive structure. Name its parts first; pollination only makes sense once each part's role is fixed.

[Picture 22210 — a large flower with all parts labelled]

## The key facts
**Sepals and petals.** Sepals and petals are the flower's outer parts.

**Stamens.** Stamens are the flower's male parts, making pollen.

**Carpel.** The carpel is the flower's female part.

**Roles first.** Each part's role in reproduction is fixed before pollination is taught.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words: a flower's parts are sepals, petals, stamens and carpel — each with its role in reproduction Use each key fact once. Then let your companion explain it back to you.
