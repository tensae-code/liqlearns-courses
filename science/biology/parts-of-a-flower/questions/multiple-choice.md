# Parts of a flower — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about sepals and petals?
a) Sepals and petals are the flower's outer parts  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Sepals and petals are the flower's outer parts**

**2.** What does this lesson say about stamens?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Stamens are the flower's male parts, making pollen  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Stamens are the flower's male parts, making pollen**

**3.** What does this lesson say about carpel?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) The carpel is the flower's female part  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) The carpel is the flower's female part**

**4.** What does this lesson say about roles first?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Each part's role in reproduction is fixed before pollination is taught
**Answer: d) Each part's role in reproduction is fixed before pollination is taught**
