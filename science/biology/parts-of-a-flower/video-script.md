Today: Parts of a flower.

Remember this one thing: a flower's parts are sepals, petals, stamens and carpel — each with its role in reproduction

Sepals and petals: Sepals and petals are the flower's outer parts.

Stamens: Stamens are the flower's male parts, making pollen.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
