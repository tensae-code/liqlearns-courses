# Hands-on — Savannah grasslands and derived savannah

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find a piece of open grassy land near trees or bush. Ask: is this grassland here because of climate, or because people keep clearing and burning it? Look for evidence — cut stumps, burn marks, farm plots, young forest saplings at the edge. Say aloud your verdict and the observation that would prove it: what would grow here if people stopped.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — open savannah with scattered trees and tall grass, a farm plot and a line of bush fire at its edge, and behind them the rain forest the land would return to — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
