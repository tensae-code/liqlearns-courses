# Savannah grasslands and derived savannah — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (grassland is the world's great open habitat — prairies, steppes, pampas and veldt are its local names — and Nigeria's savannah is its home form; derived savannah is special: grassland that people maintain by farming and bush burning inside the rain-forest climate, which grows back to forest when the pressure stops) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: applying the lesson to a new example from the learner's own surroundings, and explaining Nigeria's way of teaching it (Ch. 14, printed p. 210), without looking.
