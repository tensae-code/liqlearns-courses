# Savannah grasslands and derived savannah — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which set are all names for grassland around the world?
a) Tundra, taiga, desert
b) Prairies, steppes, pampas, veldt
c) Lagoons and deltas
d) Sahel only
**Answer: b) Prairies, steppes, pampas, veldt**

**2.** Derived savannah is grassland that exists…
a) only in deserts
b) inside the rain-forest climate, maintained by farming and bush burning
c) under the sea
d) where no people have ever lived
**Answer: b) inside the rain-forest climate, maintained by farming and bush burning**

**3.** What happens to derived savannah when farming and burning stop?
a) It becomes desert
b) It reverts to forest
c) Nothing changes
d) It floods permanently
**Answer: b) It reverts to forest**

**4.** The savannah is…
a) Nigeria's own grassland form
b) a kind of forest
c) found only in Europe
d) the same as marsh
**Answer: a) Nigeria's own grassland form**
