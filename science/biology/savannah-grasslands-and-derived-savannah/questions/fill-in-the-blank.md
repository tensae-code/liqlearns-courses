# Savannah grasslands and derived savannah — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** Grassland is called _____ in North America and veldt in southern Africa.
**Answer: prairies**

**2.** Savannah kept open inside the forest climate by farming and burning is _____ savannah.
**Answer: derived**

**3.** Derived savannah _____ to forest when human pressure stops.
**Answer: reverts**
