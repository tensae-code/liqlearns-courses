# Savannah grasslands and derived savannah — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: what derived savannah is, and how we know it is human-made.
**Answer: Derived savannah is grassland standing inside the rain-forest climate — a climate that would naturally carry forest. It exists because farming and bush burning hold it open. The proof: when the farming and burning stop, the land reverts to forest.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book teaches grassland in Chapter 14 (printed p. 210) from the world to home: grassland characteristics and world names (prairies, steppes, pampas, veldt), then Nigeria's savannah types — and derived savannah, the grassland humans maintain by farming and bush burning inside the rain-forest climate, which reverts to forest when the pressure stops.**
