# Savannah grasslands and derived savannah — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Derived savannah is kept open by human activity, not by climate.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Pampas and veldt are names for forest types.
**Answer: False** — They are local names for grassland, with prairies and steppes.

**3.** If the pressure stops, derived savannah grows back towards forest.
**Answer: True**
