# Video script — The musculoskeletal system at a glance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Of the body's many organ systems this unit teaches two; the musculoskeletal system — bones, muscles, cartilage, tendons (muscle to bone), ligaments (bone to bone), joints and connective tissue — gives shape and form, holds bones in place, protects vital organs, allows movement, makes blood cells in marrow and stores minerals.

Pupils are sent to visit traditional bone healers (Wogesha) in their locality and report how fractures and bone injuries are treated traditionally, set against the unit's musculoskeletal teaching — an investigation that does not appear in the student book.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
