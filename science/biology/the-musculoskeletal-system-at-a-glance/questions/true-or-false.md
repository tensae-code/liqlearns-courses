# The musculoskeletal system at a glance — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Of the body's many organ systems this unit teaches two; the musculoskeletal system — bones, muscles, cartilage, tendons (muscle to bone), ligaments (bone to bone), joints and connective tissue — gives shape and form, holds bones in place, protects vital organs, allows movement, makes blood cells in marrow and stores minerals.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Pupils are sent to visit traditional bone healers (Wogesha) in their locality and report how fractures and bone injuries are treated traditionally, set against the unit's musculoskeletal teaching — an investigation that does not appear in the student book.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
