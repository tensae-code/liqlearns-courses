# The musculoskeletal system at a glance — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about the musculoskeletal system?**
**Answer: Of the body's many organ systems this unit teaches two; the musculoskeletal system — bones, muscles, cartilage, tendons (muscle to bone), ligaments (bone to bone), joints and connective tissue — gives shape and form, holds bones in place, protects vital organs, allows movement, makes blood cells in marrow and stores minerals.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added ethiopian practice?**
**Answer: Pupils are sent to visit traditional bone healers (Wogesha) in their locality and report how fractures and bone injuries are treated traditionally, set against the unit's musculoskeletal teaching — an investigation that does not appear in the student book.**
