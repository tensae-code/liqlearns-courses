# Fruit formation and diversity of fruits — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: after fertilisation the flower's ovary becomes the fruit — the ovary wall forms the pericarp around the seeds — and fruits are diverse: drupes such as apricot, peach and sour cherry, pomes such as apple and pear, and dry multi-seeded fruits. Moldova's Grade 8 book (3.4 From flower to fruits and seeds; the fruit and diversity of fruits) teaches: ovary to pericarp, drupes (apricot, peach, sour cherry), pome (apple, pear), dry multi-seeded fruits. (printed p. 30).**

**2.** Explain Moldova's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Moldova's Grade 8 book follows the flower forward: from flower to fruits and seeds, ovary to pericarp, then the diversity of fruits kind by kind.**
