# Fruit formation and diversity of fruits — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Moldova's Grade 8 book (3.4 From flower to fruits and seeds; the fruit and diversity of fruits) teaches: ovary to pericarp, drupes (apricot, peach, sour cherry), pome (apple, pear), dry multi-seeded fruits. (printed p. 30).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Moldova's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Biologie: Manual pentru clasa a VIII-a (Grade 8)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with fruit formation and diversity of fruits.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is fruit formation and diversity of fruits.
