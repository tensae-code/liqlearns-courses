# Video script — Fruit formation and diversity of fruits

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Fruit formation and diversity of fruits.

Remember this one thing: after fertilisation the flower's ovary becomes the fruit — the ovary wall forms the pericarp around the seeds — and fruits are diverse: drupes such as apricot, peach and sour cherry, pomes such as apple and pear, and dry multi-seeded fruits.

Moldova's Grade 8 book (3.4 From flower to fruits and seeds; the fruit and diversity of fruits) teaches: ovary to pericarp, drupes (apricot, peach, sour cherry), pome (apple, pear), dry multi-seeded fruits. (printed p. 30).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
