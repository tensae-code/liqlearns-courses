# Reproduction — one parent or two

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Reproduction — one parent or two. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Individuals die; the species continues. Reproduction is how.

[Picture 21035 — a single parent splitting into copies beside two parents]

## The key facts
**Purpose.** Reproduction produces new individuals of the same kind and keeps species going although individuals die.

**Asexual.** Asexual reproduction has one parent, no special reproductive cells, and offspring that are copies of the parent.

**Sexual.** Sexual reproduction usually has two parents contributing special germ cells — egg and sperm.

**Fertilisation.** In sexual reproduction egg and sperm unite in fertilisation.
