# Reproduction — one parent or two — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Reproduction produces new individuals of the same kind and keeps species going although individuals die.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Asexual has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Sexual reproduction usually has two parents contributing special germ cells — egg and sperm.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Fertilisation has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
