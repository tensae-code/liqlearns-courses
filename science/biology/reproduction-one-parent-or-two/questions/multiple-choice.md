# Reproduction — one parent or two — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about purpose?
a) Reproduction produces new individuals of the same kind and keeps species going although individuals die
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Reproduction produces new individuals of the same kind and keeps species going although individuals die**

**2.** What does this lesson say about asexual?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Asexual reproduction has one parent, no special reproductive cells, and offspring that are copies of the parent
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Asexual reproduction has one parent, no special reproductive cells, and offspring that are copies of the parent**

**3.** What does this lesson say about sexual?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Sexual reproduction usually has two parents contributing special germ cells — egg and sperm
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Sexual reproduction usually has two parents contributing special germ cells — egg and sperm**

**4.** What does this lesson say about fertilisation?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) In sexual reproduction egg and sperm unite in fertilisation
**Answer: d) In sexual reproduction egg and sperm unite in fertilisation**
