Today: Reproduction — one parent or two.

Remember this one thing: reproduction keeps species going: asexual from one parent, sexual from two parents' germ cells uniting.

Reproduction produces new individuals of the same kind and keeps species going although individuals die.

Asexual reproduction has one parent, no special reproductive cells, and offspring that are copies of the parent.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
