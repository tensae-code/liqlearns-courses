# Coevolution — arms races and partnerships — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is coevolution?
a) Two species in a close relationship shaping each other's evolution, generation after generation
b) One species evolving while everything around it stays still
c) It plays no part in this topic at all
d) It happens only at night and nowhere else
**Answer: a) Two species in a close relationship shaping each other's evolution, generation after generation**

**2.** In an antagonistic relationship, coevolution runs as —
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) An arms race — a better defence selects a better attack, and the other way round
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) An arms race — a better defence selects a better attack, and the other way round**

**3.** Where does Costa Rica's anthology teach Coevolución?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Inside Unit 1, the ecological-relationships unit — after the symbiotic relationships, at printed p. 28
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Inside Unit 1, the ecological-relationships unit — after the symbiotic relationships, at printed p. 28**

**4.** In a symbiotic relationship, what does coevolution do?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) It fits the two species ever more closely to each other — a partnership, like a flower and its pollinator
**Answer: d) It fits the two species ever more closely to each other — a partnership, like a flower and its pollinator**
