# Coevolution — arms races and partnerships — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** When two species in a close relationship shape each other's evolution, it is called _____ — Coevolución in Costa Rica's book.
**Answer: coevolution**

**2.** Between a parasite and its host, coevolution runs as an arms _____: each improvement selects the other side's counter-improvement.
**Answer: race**
