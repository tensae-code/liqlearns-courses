Today: Plant uses and library work.

Remember this one thing: group work and a library search extend the uses of plants beyond food — and they close the unit.

Plants feed us. They also heal us, clothe us, build for us and shade us.

Activity 2.20: in your group, gather every use beyond food that you know.
Activity 2.21: take your questions to the library. Find the use in a book. Note the plant, the use, and the page. Bring back evidence, not just opinion.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
