# Plant uses and library work — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: beyond food.
**Answer: Plants serve people in more ways than food — the unit's medicinal-plants table is one family of uses, and this lesson's activities extend the list further.**

**2.** Explain: the library search.
**Answer: Learners take their questions into the library, find plant uses in books, note each one with its book and page, and report back with evidence that others can check.**

**3.** Explain: closing the unit.
**Answer: Activities 2.20–2.21 are the last content of Unit 2; after them comes only the unit summary, so the learners themselves gather and test the unit's knowledge.**
