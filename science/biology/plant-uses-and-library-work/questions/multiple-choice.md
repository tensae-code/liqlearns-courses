# Plant uses and library work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about Activity 2.20?
a) It is group work that gathers the uses of plants beyond food
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) It is group work that gathers the uses of plants beyond food**

**2.** What does this lesson say about Activity 2.21?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It is a library search — finding plant uses in books and noting them as evidence
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) It is a library search — finding plant uses in books and noting them as evidence**

**3.** Where do Activities 2.20–2.21 stand in Unit 2?
a) They open the unit, before any plant content
b) They belong to a different unit entirely
c) They close the unit's content, just before its summary
d) They replace the unit summary and review
**Answer: c) They close the unit's content, just before its summary**

**4.** What must a proper library-search note always carry?
a) Only the plant's local name
b) The plant, the use, and where it was found — the book and the page
c) A drawing and nothing else
d) The searcher's own opinion
**Answer: b) The plant, the use, and where it was found — the book and the page**
