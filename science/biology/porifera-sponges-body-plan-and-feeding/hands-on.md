# Hands-on — Porifera — sponges: body plan and feeding

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Take a bath sponge or a kitchen sponge and hold it under a tap: watch the water enter through the many small holes and find its way out again — that through-flow of water is the sponge's feeding current, in through the pores and out through the biggest opening.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — a vase-shaped sponge with many small pores in its wall, arrows showing water entering through the pores and leaving through the large opening at the top, and the flagellated choanocyte cells lining the inside drawn as small collars with whips — label its parts, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
