# Porifera — sponges: body plan and feeding — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What drives the water current through a sponge?**
a) The beating of the choanocytes' flagella  b) Muscles in the sponge wall  c) The wind on the water surface  d) The sponge's heart
**Answer: a) The beating of the choanocytes' flagella**

**2. Water leaves a sponge through the…**
a) inhalant pores  b) osculum  c) choanocytes  d) spicules
**Answer: b) osculum**

**3. A sponge's skeleton can be made of…**
a) bone and cartilage  b) shell and horn  c) spicules or spongin  d) chitin plates
**Answer: c) spicules or spongin**

**4. Which of these is true of adult sponges?**
a) They swim freely all their lives  b) They have true tissues and organs  c) They are sessile — fixed in one place  d) They catch prey with stinging cells
**Answer: c) They are sessile — fixed in one place**
