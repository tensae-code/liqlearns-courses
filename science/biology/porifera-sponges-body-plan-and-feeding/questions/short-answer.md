# Porifera — sponges: body plan and feeding — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain how a sponge feeds, following the water.
**Answer: Water enters through the inhalant pores in the sponge's wall. The flagellated choanocytes beat their flagella and drive the current through the body, and food is taken from the water as it passes. The water leaves through the osculum.**

**2.** Explain Mozambique's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Mozambique's PESD II animal module (Módulo 3 de Biologia) surveys the animal phyla one by one and begins with Porifera: sponges as the simplest animals — no true tissues, sessile, inhalant pores, choanocyte flagellated cells driving water out through the osculum, a spicule or spongin skeleton, and sexual reproduction with a free-swimming larva.**
