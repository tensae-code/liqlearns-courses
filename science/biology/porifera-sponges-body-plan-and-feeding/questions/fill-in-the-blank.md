# Porifera — sponges: body plan and feeding — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Water leaves the sponge through the large opening called the _____.**
**Answer: osculum**

**2. The flagellated cells that drive the current are called _____.**
**Answer: choanocytes**

**3. A sponge skeleton of soft fibres is made of _____.**
**Answer: spongin**

**4. An adult sponge is _____ — it stays fixed in one place.**
**Answer: sessile**
