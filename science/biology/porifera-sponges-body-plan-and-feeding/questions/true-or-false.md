# Porifera — sponges: body plan and feeding — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Sponges have true tissues like other animals.**
**Answer: False** — sponges are the simplest animals — they have no true tissues.

**2. The free-swimming larva is the moving stage of a sponge's life.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches: the adult is sessile, the larva swims.

**3. Water enters a sponge through the osculum.**
**Answer: False** — water enters through the inhalant pores and leaves through the osculum.

**4. Choanocytes are flagellated cells that drive the water current.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
