# Porifera — sponges: body plan and feeding — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining how an animal with no mouth, no stomach and no movement can still feed itself.
**Model answer: A sponge feeds by moving water, not by moving itself. Water enters through many small pores in its wall, and flagellated choanocyte cells beat their flagella to keep the current flowing through the body. Food particles are taken from the water as it passes, and the used water leaves through the osculum.**
