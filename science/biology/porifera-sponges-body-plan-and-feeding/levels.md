# Porifera — sponges: body plan and feeding — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Sponges are the simplest animals: they have no true tissues, stay fixed in one place, and feed by driving water through their bodies — in through many small pores and out through one big opening, the osculum.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by tracing the water's path without looking — pores, choanocyte current, osculum — and naming the skeleton materials, spicules and spongin, and the one moving stage, the free-swimming larva.
