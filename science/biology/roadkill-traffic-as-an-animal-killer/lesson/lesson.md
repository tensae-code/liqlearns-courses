# Roadkill — traffic as an animal killer

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — roadkill. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Road accidents — a major public-health burden in developing countries including Ethiopia — kill and injure wild and domestic animals as well as people, including animals crossing highways in and near conservation areas (road-kills are pictured); pupils gather local accident data from transport and wildlife offices or traffic police and discuss how to reduce the toll.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: what roadkill means, the main ideas of this lesson, and one example from your own surroundings.

## Do It Yourself
1. Write the lesson's main idea in one sentence of your own.
2. Find one example of it in your home, street or school and note what you observed.
3. Teach the idea to someone at home and note the question they asked you.
