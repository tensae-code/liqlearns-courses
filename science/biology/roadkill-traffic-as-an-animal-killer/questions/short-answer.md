# Roadkill — traffic as an animal killer — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about road traffic accidents and animals?**
**Answer: Road accidents — a major public-health burden in developing countries including Ethiopia — kill and injure wild and domestic animals as well as people, including animals crossing highways in and near conservation areas (road-kills are pictured); pupils gather local accident data from transport and wildlife offices or traffic police and discuss how to reduce the toll.**
