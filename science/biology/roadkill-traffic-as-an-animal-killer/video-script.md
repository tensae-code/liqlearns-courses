# Video script — Roadkill — traffic as an animal killer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Road accidents — a major public-health burden in developing countries including Ethiopia — kill and injure wild and domestic animals as well as people, including animals crossing highways in and near conservation areas (road-kills are pictured); pupils gather local accident data from transport and wildlife offices or traffic police and discuss how to reduce the toll.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
