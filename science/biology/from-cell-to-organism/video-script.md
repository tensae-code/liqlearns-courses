# Video script — From cell to organism — the levels of life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: From cell to organism — the levels of life.

Remember this one thing: life is organised cell, tissue, organ, organ system, organism — each level built from the one below.

The levels are cell, tissue, organ, organ system and organism.

Each level is built from the one below it.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
