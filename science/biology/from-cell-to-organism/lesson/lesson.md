# From cell to organism — the levels of life

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — From cell to organism — the levels of life. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Each level does more than any part alone. Trace the staircase in a plant and in an animal.

[Picture 21034 — a five-step staircase from one cell up to a whole person or tree]

[GIF 21114 — zooming up the staircase level by level]

## The key facts
**The levels.** The levels are cell, tissue, organ, organ system and organism.

**Built up.** Each level is built from the one below it.

**More than parts.** Each level does more than any part alone.

**Two traces.** Examples are traced in both a plant and an animal.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words: life is organised cell, tissue, organ, organ system, organism — each level built from the one below. Use each key fact once. Then let your companion explain it back to you.
