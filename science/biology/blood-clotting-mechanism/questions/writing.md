# Blood clotting mechanism — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: when a blood vessel is cut, blood clots by a mechanism: platelets and plasma proteins build a fibrin net that traps blood cells and seals the wound. Damaged tissue and platelets start the change: soluble plasma protein is converted into insoluble fibrin threads. The threads form a net across the wound, blood cells are trapped in the net, and the trapped mass is the clot.**
