# Blood clotting mechanism — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: when a blood vessel is cut, blood clots by a mechanism: platelets and plasma proteins build a fibrin net that traps blood cells and seals the wound. An open vessel would bleed without limit and let microbes in. The clot is the seal: it forms at the wound, stops the loss, and covers the break while the vessel repairs.**

**2.** Explain Malaysia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: In Malaysia's KSSM Form 4 book the mechanism of blood clotting is a named subtopic of Chapter 10 (printed p. 183), taught in its own right between the mechanism of heartbeat (p. 180) and blood groups (p. 185).**
