# Blood clotting mechanism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) when a blood vessel is cut, blood clots by a mechanism: platelets and plasma proteins build a fibrin net that traps blood cells and seals the wound  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
**Answer: a) when a blood vessel is cut, blood clots by a mechanism: platelets and plasma proteins build a fibrin net that traps blood cells and seals the wound**

**2. Why blood must clot — which statement is right?**
a) An open vessel would bleed without limit and let microbes in. The clot is the seal: it forms at the wound, stops the loss, and covers the break while the vessel repairs.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) An open vessel would bleed without limit and let microbes in. The clot is the seal: it forms at the wound, stops the loss, and covers the break while the vessel repairs.**

**3. The mechanism — which statement is right?**
a) It is unrelated to the lesson's topic  b) Damaged tissue and platelets start the change: soluble plasma protein is converted into insoluble fibrin threads. The threads form a net across the wound, blood cells are trapped in the net, and the trapped mass is the clot.  c) It is a myth the book corrects  d) It applies only outside Malaysia
**Answer: b) Damaged tissue and platelets start the change: soluble plasma protein is converted into insoluble fibrin threads. The threads form a net across the wound, blood cells are trapped in the net, and the trapped mass is the clot.**
