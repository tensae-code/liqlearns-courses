Today: Noise and radiation pollution.

Remember this one thing: pollution has kinds the eye cannot see in air or water — Yemen's Grade 12 book names noise pollution (التلوث الضوضائي) and radiation pollution (التلوث الإشعاعي) as pollution kinds of their own, inside its environment unit beside air, water, food and soil pollution.

Pollution is not only dirt in the air and dirt in the water.

Noise pollution is unwanted, harmful sound — traffic, horns, drills and machines. Yemen's book teaches it under its own name: التلوث الضوضائي.

Radiation pollution is contamination by radioactive materials and their rays — التلوث الإشعاعي. You cannot see it or smell it; instruments find it.

The two stand in one unit with air pollution, water pollution, food pollutants and soil pollution — the unit sorts pollution by what carries the harm.

Yemen's Grade 12 book gives noise and radiation their own headed sections, instead of folding them into air pollution.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
