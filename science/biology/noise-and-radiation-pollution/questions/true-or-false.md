# Noise and radiation pollution — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Noise pollution and radiation pollution are the same kind of pollution.
**Answer: False** — Noise pollution is unwanted, harmful sound; radiation pollution is contamination by radioactive materials and their rays. Yemen's book teaches them as two separate kinds.

**2.** Yemen's Grade 12 book names noise pollution التلوث الضوضائي and radiation pollution التلوث الإشعاعي.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Radiation pollution can be seen with the naked eye if the light is good.
**Answer: False** — It cannot be seen, heard or smelled; instruments detect it.
