# Noise and radiation pollution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Noise pollution is…
a) any loud music at a wedding
b) unwanted, harmful sound
c) silence in a library
d) the sound of rain
**Answer: b) unwanted, harmful sound**

**2.** Radiation pollution is contamination by…
a) dust and smoke
b) sewage
c) radioactive materials and their rays
d) fertiliser runoff
**Answer: c) radioactive materials and their rays**

**3.** In Yemen's Grade 12 book, noise pollution and radiation pollution are taught…
a) inside the air-pollution section, unnamed
b) as named pollution kinds with headed sections of their own
c) only in the activities book
d) in the geology unit
**Answer: b) as named pollution kinds with headed sections of their own**

**4.** Which of these stands in the same unit, after radiation pollution?
a) Food pollutants and soil pollution
b) Photosynthesis
c) The cell cycle
d) Vaccines and serums
**Answer: a) Food pollutants and soil pollution**

**5.** Radiation pollution is found by…
a) looking at the colour of the water
b) smelling the air
c) instruments that detect radiation
d) tasting the soil
**Answer: c) instruments that detect radiation**

**6.** Yemen's environment unit closes with…
a) the depletion of environmental resources
b) Mendel's experiments
c) the five kingdoms
d) a microscope practical
**Answer: a) the depletion of environmental resources**

**7.** The unit sorts pollution by…
a) the year it was discovered
b) what carries the harm
c) the size of the country
d) alphabetical order only
**Answer: b) what carries the harm**
