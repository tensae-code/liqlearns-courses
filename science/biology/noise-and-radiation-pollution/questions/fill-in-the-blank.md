# Noise and radiation pollution — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** Unwanted, harmful sound is called _____ pollution.
**Answer: noise**

**2.** Contamination by radioactive materials and their rays is _____ pollution.
**Answer: radiation**

**3.** In Yemen's book the two kinds stand beside food pollutants and _____ pollution in the same unit.
**Answer: soil**
