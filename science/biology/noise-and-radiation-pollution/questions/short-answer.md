# Noise and radiation pollution — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: noise pollution against radiation pollution.
**Answer: Noise pollution is unwanted, harmful sound — traffic, horns, drills — heard as it happens and gone when it stops. Radiation pollution is contamination by radioactive materials and their rays — invisible and undetectable by the senses, found by instruments, and it stays until the radioactive material decays or is removed.**

**2.** Explain: Yemen's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Yemen's Grade 12 Biology and Earth Science book, in Unit 7 (The environment and its problems), gives noise pollution (التلوث الضوضائي, printed p. 173) and radiation pollution (التلوث الإشعاعي, printed p. 176) headed sections of their own — named pollution kinds beside air, water, food (p. 178) and soil (p. 180) pollution — instead of folding them into air pollution, and closes the unit with the depletion of environmental resources (p. 181).**
