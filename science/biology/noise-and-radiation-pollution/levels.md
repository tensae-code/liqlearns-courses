# Noise and radiation pollution — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (pollution has kinds the eye cannot see in air or water — Yemen's Grade 12 book names noise pollution (التلوث الضوضائي) and radiation pollution (التلوث الإشعاعي) as pollution kinds of their own, inside its environment unit beside air, water, food and soil pollution.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: applying the lesson to a new example from the learner's own surroundings, and explaining Yemen's way of teaching it (Unit 7, printed pp. 173–176), without looking.
