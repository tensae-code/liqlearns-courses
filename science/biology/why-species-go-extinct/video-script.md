# Video script — Why species go extinct

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Causes of species extinction are listed in teaching-note form: habitat degradation and destruction (elimination or change of living space), toxicity (killing directly through food and water, or indirectly by sterilisation, over one or several generations), volcanoes, floods and drought, predation, competition, disease and climatic heating.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
