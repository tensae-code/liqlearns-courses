# Information transfer in ecosystems — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: ecosystems run on information as well as energy and matter: physical, chemical and behavioural signals pass from a source through a channel to a receptor — the bee's dances, the silkworm moth's bombykol, a plant's phytochrome reading light, and the volatiles an attacked tobacco plant releases to summon parasitoids. physical, chemical and behavioural information on a source–channel–receptor chain; bee round and waggle dances; Butenandt's bombykol (20 years, 500,000 silkworm moths, 1959 — the first pheromone); phytochrome as a plant's information receptor; dolphin echolocation over 100 kHz and ship noise behind mass strandings; herbivore-attacked tobacco releasing volatiles that summon parasitoids (2001); and the farming uses — simulated pollinator signals, longer light for laying hens, pheromone traps and mating disruption.**
