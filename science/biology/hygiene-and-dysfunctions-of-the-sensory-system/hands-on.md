# Hands-on — Hygiene and dysfunctions of the sensory system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: every sensory analyser has its own hygiene and its own dysfunctions: risk factors are named analyser by analyser, and failures of sensation — anaesthesia as absence of cutaneous sensation, myopia — are studied as dysfunctions.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: the five sensory analysers of a human head and skin, each with a small hygiene card and a warning sign for its risk factors. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
