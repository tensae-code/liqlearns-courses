# Hygiene and dysfunctions of the sensory system — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (every sensory analyser has its own hygiene and its own dysfunctions: risk factors are named analyser by analyser, and failures of sensation — anaesthesia as absence of cutaneous sensation, myopia — are studied as dysfunctions) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
