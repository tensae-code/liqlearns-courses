Today: Hygiene and dysfunctions of the sensory system.

Remember this one thing: every sensory analyser has its own hygiene and its own dysfunctions: risk factors are named analyser by analyser, and failures of sensation — anaesthesia as absence of cutaneous sensation, myopia — are studied as dysfunctions.

Moldova's Grade 11 book (§15 Hygiene and dysfunctions of the human sensory system) teaches: risk factors by analyser, anaesthesia as absence of cutaneous sensation, myopia discussed as a possible adaptation to near work in the computer era. (printed p. 48).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
