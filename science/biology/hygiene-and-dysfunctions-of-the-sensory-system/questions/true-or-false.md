# Hygiene and dysfunctions of the sensory system — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Moldova's Grade 11 book (§15 Hygiene and dysfunctions of the human sensory system) teaches: risk factors by analyser, anaesthesia as absence of cutaneous sensation, myopia discussed as a possible adaptation to near work in the computer era. (printed p. 48).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Moldova's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Biologie — Procese și sisteme vitale: Manual pentru clasa a XI-a (Profil real / Profil umanist) (Grade 11)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with hygiene and dysfunctions of the sensory system.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is hygiene and dysfunctions of the sensory system.
