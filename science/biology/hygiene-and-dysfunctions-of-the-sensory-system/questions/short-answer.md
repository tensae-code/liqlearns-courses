# Hygiene and dysfunctions of the sensory system — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: every sensory analyser has its own hygiene and its own dysfunctions: risk factors are named analyser by analyser, and failures of sensation — anaesthesia as absence of cutaneous sensation, myopia — are studied as dysfunctions. Moldova's Grade 11 book (§15 Hygiene and dysfunctions of the human sensory system) teaches: risk factors by analyser, anaesthesia as absence of cutaneous sensation, myopia discussed as a possible adaptation to near work in the computer era. (printed p. 48).**

**2.** Explain Moldova's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Moldova's Grade 11 book closes its sensory chapter with hygiene and dysfunctions of the whole sensory system — risk factors by analyser, anaesthesia, and myopia discussed as a possible adaptation to near work in the computer era.**
