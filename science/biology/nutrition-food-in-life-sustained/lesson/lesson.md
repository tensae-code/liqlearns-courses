# Nutrition — food in, life sustained

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Nutrition — food in, life sustained. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Food, nutrients and nutrition are three distinct terms — this lesson fixes them precisely.

[Picture 21053 — a flow from plate to body systems]

## The key facts
**Nutrition.** Nutrition is the taking in of food and its conversion into energy and vital nutrients.

**Food.** Food is the material eaten.

**Nutrients.** Nutrients are the useful substances food contains, required for life.

**Three terms.** Food, nutrients and nutrition are three distinct terms.
