# Nutrition — food in, life sustained — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about nutrition?
a) Nutrition is the taking in of food and its conversion into energy and vital nutrients
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Nutrition is the taking in of food and its conversion into energy and vital nutrients**

**2.** What does this lesson say about food?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Food is the material eaten
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Food is the material eaten**

**3.** What does this lesson say about nutrients?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Nutrients are the useful substances food contains, required for life
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Nutrients are the useful substances food contains, required for life**

**4.** What does this lesson say about three terms?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Food, nutrients and nutrition are three distinct terms
**Answer: d) Food, nutrients and nutrition are three distinct terms**
