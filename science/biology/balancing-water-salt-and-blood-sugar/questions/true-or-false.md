# Balancing water, salt and blood sugar — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Water and salt balance is run by the kidneys under hypothalamus and ADH control — much dilute urine when water is plentiful, little concentrated urine when it is not; blood glucose is held steady by two pancreatic hormones working oppositely, insulin lowering it and glucagon raising it, with diabetes as the failure case pupils investigate.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Too little body water impairs temperature regulation, waste flushing and all major systems, while overhydration can cause water intoxication; excess sodium is linked to kidney stones, high blood pressure and cardiovascular disease; and the blood-sugar loop is retold with the pancreas itself named as the control centre that secretes insulin when glucose rises and stops when balance is restored.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
