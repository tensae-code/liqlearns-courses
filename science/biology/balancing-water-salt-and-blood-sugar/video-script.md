# Video script — Balancing water, salt and blood sugar

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Water and salt balance is run by the kidneys under hypothalamus and ADH control — much dilute urine when water is plentiful, little concentrated urine when it is not; blood glucose is held steady by two pancreatic hormones working oppositely, insulin lowering it and glucagon raising it, with diabetes as the failure case pupils investigate.

Too little body water impairs temperature regulation, waste flushing and all major systems, while overhydration can cause water intoxication; excess sodium is linked to kidney stones, high blood pressure and cardiovascular disease; and the blood-sugar loop is retold with the pancreas itself named as the control centre that secretes insulin when glucose rises and stops when balance is restored.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
