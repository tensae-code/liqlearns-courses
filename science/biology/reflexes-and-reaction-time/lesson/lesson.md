# Reflexes and reaction time

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — reflexes and reaction time. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

An investigation the guide adds in full: partners drop a metre ruler and catch it to measure reaction time, the whole class's data are graphed and summarised (average, median, mean), and people are noted to differ and to be trainable faster; reflex examples are listed (coughing/sneezing, knee jerk, hand withdrawal, blinking) and the answer key explains reflex speed — most reflexes are processed without travelling to the brain.
