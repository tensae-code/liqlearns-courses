# Investigation: temperature and seed germination

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Investigation: temperature and seed germination. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
In this investigation you state a hypothesis first, then germinate bean seeds in three places and compare what happens.

[Picture 29203 — three labelled seed trays — cold, room, warm]

[GIF 21101 — seedlings growing at three different speeds side by side]

## The key facts
**Three places.** Bean seeds are germinated at room temperature, in a cold place and in a warm place.

**Hypothesis first.** A hypothesis is stated before the seeds are tested.

**Measuring.** Germination times and seedling lengths are measured and drawn as a bar graph.

**Reporting.** Results are interpreted, a conclusion is drawn, and the work is reported to the class.
