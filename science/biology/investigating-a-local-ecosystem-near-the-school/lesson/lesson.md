# Investigating a local ecosystem near the school

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Investigating a local ecosystem near the school. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
An ecosystem is investigated where it actually is: go out into an ecosystem from the school's area, observe it on a field excursion, and complete a trophic chain for that local ecosystem. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from Moldova's Ministry of Education biology textbooks, which teach it as a named topic.

[Picture — pupils on a field excursion in a Moldovan temperate-climate ecosystem, notebook in hand, with a partly completed trophic chain drawn beside them]

## The key facts
**5.3 An ecosystem from the school's area.** Moldova's Grade 8 book (5.3 An ecosystem from the school's area) teaches: Moldova's temperate-climate ecosystem types, field excursion and completing a trophic chain for the local ecosystem. (printed p. 124).

**Moldova's way.** Moldova's Grade 8 book teaches ecosystems by field study: an ecosystem from the school's area, Moldova's temperate-climate ecosystem types, a field excursion and a trophic chain completed for the local ecosystem.
