# Hands-on — Investigating a local ecosystem near the school

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: an ecosystem is investigated where it actually is: go out into an ecosystem from the school's area, observe it on a field excursion, and complete a trophic chain for that local ecosystem.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: pupils on a field excursion in a Moldovan temperate-climate ecosystem, notebook in hand, with a partly completed trophic chain drawn beside them. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
