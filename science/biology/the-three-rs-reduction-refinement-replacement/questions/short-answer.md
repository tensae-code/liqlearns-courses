# The three Rs — reduction, refinement, replacement — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about ethical treatment of animals?**
**Answer: Animals are sentient and feel pain; research on them is governed by Reduction (use fewer animals through better technique and sharing results), Refinement (less invasive methods, better care and living conditions, proper handling) and Replacement (cell cultures, biosynthetic material and computer models instead of whole animals).**
