# The three Rs — reduction, refinement, replacement

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — the three rs. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Animals are sentient and feel pain; research on them is governed by Reduction (use fewer animals through better technique and sharing results), Refinement (less invasive methods, better care and living conditions, proper handling) and Replacement (cell cultures, biosynthetic material and computer models instead of whole animals).
