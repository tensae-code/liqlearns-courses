# Gene and chromosomal mutations — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 2 student book (Section One, printed p. 43) teaches chromosomal mutations: Robertsonian translocation between chromosomes 14 and 21, inversion, and numerical mutations causing congenital and mental abnormalities — with fruit-fly wing forms as the visible face of mutation.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كتاب الطالب — الصف الثاني عشر — الفصل الدراسي الثاني (Biology — Student Book, Grade 12, Term 2), printed p. 43).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with gene and chromosomal mutations.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is gene and chromosomal mutations.
