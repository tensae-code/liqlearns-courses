# Gene and chromosomal mutations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) mutations come at two scales: chromosomal mutations — Robertsonian translocation between chromosomes 14 and 21, inversion, and numerical mutations — and gene mutations: deletion and insertion shifting the reading frame, and the single base substitution in the haemoglobin gene  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
**Answer: a) mutations come at two scales: chromosomal mutations — Robertsonian translocation between chromosomes 14 and 21, inversion, and numerical mutations — and gene mutations: deletion and insertion shifting the reading frame, and the single base substitution in the haemoglobin gene**

**2. Chromosomal mutations — which statement is right?**
a) Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 2 student book (Section One, printed p. 43) teaches chromosomal mutations: Robertsonian translocation between chromosomes 14 and 21, inversion, and numerical mutations causing congenital and mental abnormalities — with fruit-fly wing forms as the visible face of mutation.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 2 student book (Section One, printed p. 43) teaches chromosomal mutations: Robertsonian translocation between chromosomes 14 and 21, inversion, and numerical mutations causing congenital and mental abnormalities — with fruit-fly wing forms as the visible face of mutation.**

**3. Which statement about this lesson's topic is right?**
a) It is unrelated to the lesson's topic  b) Gene mutations are taught beside them: deletion and insertion shifting the frame, and the single base substitution in the haemoglobin gene — one base changed, one protein changed.  c) It is a myth the book corrects  d) It applies only outside Kuwait
