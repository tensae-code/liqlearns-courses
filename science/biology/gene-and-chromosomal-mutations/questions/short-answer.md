# Gene and chromosomal mutations — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: mutations come at two scales: chromosomal mutations — Robertsonian translocation between chromosomes 14 and 21, inversion, and numerical mutations — and gene mutations: deletion and insertion shifting the reading frame, and the single base substitution in the haemoglobin gene. Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 2 student book (Section One, printed p. 43) teaches chromosomal mutations: Robertsonian translocation between chromosomes 14 and 21, inversion, and numerical mutations causing congenital and mental abnormalities — with fruit-fly wing forms as the visible face of mutation.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's Grade 12 molecular course teaches mutation as its own chapter step (Section One): visible mutation first (fruit-fly wings), then the chromosome-scale kinds, then the gene-scale kinds down to the single base.**
