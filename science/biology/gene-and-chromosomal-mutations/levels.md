# Gene and chromosomal mutations — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (mutations come at two scales: chromosomal mutations — Robertsonian translocation between chromosomes 14 and 21, inversion, and numerical mutations — and gene mutations: deletion and insertion shifting the reading frame, and the single base substitution in the haemoglobin gene) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
