Today: Gene and chromosomal mutations.

Remember this one thing: mutations come at two scales: chromosomal mutations — Robertsonian translocation between chromosomes 14 and 21, inversion, and numerical mutations — and gene mutations: deletion and insertion shifting the reading frame, and the single base substitution in the haemoglobin gene.

Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 2 student book (Section One, printed p. 43) teaches chromosomal mutations: Robertsonian translocation between chromosomes 14 and 21, inversion, and numerical mutations causing congenital and mental abnormalities — with fruit-fly wing forms as the visible face of mutation.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
