# Hands-on — Cloning and plant tissue culture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Draw two columns on a big sheet of paper. Head one "based on an egg" and the other "based on a somatic cell". In each column draw the starting cell, then the young copy it leads to, and label the single donor both copies match. Underneath, draw the natural-reproduction row: two parents, gametes meeting, a mixed offspring — and point at the difference.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory, label it (egg, somatic cell from the mammary glands, donor, copy), and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
