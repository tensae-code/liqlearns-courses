# Lesson plan — Cloning and plant tissue culture

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Cloning and plant tissue culture
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — appended to Unit 16 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** cloning copies one donor's heredity without fertilisation — by a technique based on an egg, or by one based on a specialised somatic cell (a body cell, in the guide's example from the mammary glands) — and the principle is deduced by comparing the two techniques and comparing cloning with natural reproduction (Morocco, Grade 8 — *Guide Almoufid SVT*, teacher's guide, Unit 2 closing sequences, printed pp. 85–87).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. Asexual reproduction — copies without gametes is recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: **Asexual reproduction — copies without gametes**. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say, in their own words, the one thing this lesson teaches: cloning copies one donor's heredity without fertilisation.
2. Describe the two techniques the guide compares — one based on an egg, the other on a specialised somatic cell from the mammary glands — and say what each starts from.
3. Set cloning against natural reproduction: two parents' heredity mixed at fertilisation, against one donor's heredity copied whole.
4. Explain Morocco's way as the placements describe it: the principle of cloning is deduced from the comparisons, document by document.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.
