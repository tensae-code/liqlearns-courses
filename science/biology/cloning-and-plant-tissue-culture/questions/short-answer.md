# Cloning and plant tissue culture — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the two cloning techniques Morocco compares.
**Answer: One technique is based on an egg — the work starts from an egg cell. The other is based on a specialised somatic cell, an ordinary body cell (in the guide's example taken from the mammary glands) that carries the donor's whole heredity, and the copy grows with that heredity.**

**2.** Explain: Morocco's way of teaching cloning.
**Answer: The Grade 8 guide closes its reproduction unit with cloning: learners compare the two techniques, compare cloning with natural reproduction, and deduce the principle of cloning from the comparisons — that a living thing can be copied because its heredity is carried in its cells.**
