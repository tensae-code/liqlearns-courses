# Cloning and plant tissue culture — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A clone's heredity comes from one single donor.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Cloning needs male and female gametes to meet at fertilisation.
**Answer: False** — cloning copies one donor's heredity with no fertilisation and no mixing.

**3.** One of the two techniques Morocco compares is based on a specialised somatic cell from the mammary glands.
**Answer: True**

**4.** In natural reproduction the offspring is an exact copy of one parent.
**Answer: False** — its heredity is a mixture of two parents, so it is not an exact copy of either.
