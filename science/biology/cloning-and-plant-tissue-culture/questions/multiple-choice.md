# Cloning and plant tissue culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is a clone?
a) A living copy whose heredity comes from one single donor
b) An offspring whose heredity mixes two parents at fertilisation
c) It plays no part in this topic at all
d) It happens only at night and nowhere else
**Answer: a) A living copy whose heredity comes from one single donor**

**2.** What does the somatic-cell cloning technique start from?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) A specialised somatic cell — a body cell, in the guide's example taken from the mammary glands
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) A specialised somatic cell — a body cell, in the guide's example taken from the mammary glands**

**3.** How does cloning differ from natural reproduction?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Natural reproduction mixes two parents' heredity at fertilisation; cloning copies one donor's heredity whole, with no fertilisation
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Natural reproduction mixes two parents' heredity at fertilisation; cloning copies one donor's heredity whole, with no fertilisation**

**4.** What principle do Morocco's learners deduce from comparing the two techniques?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) The heredity of the whole individual is carried in its cells, so a new individual grown from one donor cell's heredity is the donor's copy
**Answer: d) The heredity of the whole individual is carried in its cells, so a new individual grown from one donor cell's heredity is the donor's copy**
