# Harmful insects — pests and disease carriers

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — harmful insects. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

The damaging side: locust swarms that migrate for kilometres devouring green plants (with regional monitoring and control), stored-grain pests such as the rice weevil and lesser grain borer, and disease vectors pictured together — anopheles and culex mosquitoes and the tsetse fly — alongside other crop pests (caterpillars, bugs, hoppers, aphids).
