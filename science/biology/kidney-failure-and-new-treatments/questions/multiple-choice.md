# Kidney failure and new treatments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about what failure means?
a) Kidney failure means the kidneys can no longer filter the blood: urea and other wastes, salts and water build up in the body
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Kidney failure means the kidneys can no longer filter the blood: urea and other wastes, salts and water build up in the body**

**2.** What does this lesson say about what causes it?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Long-standing diabetes and high blood pressure, repeated infections, stones and some poisons are the common causes of kidney failure
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Long-standing diabetes and high blood pressure, repeated infections, stones and some poisons are the common causes of kidney failure**

**3.** What does this lesson say about signs and course?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Tiredness, swelling of feet and face, little or frothy urine, nausea and breathlessness are warning signs of failing kidneys
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Tiredness, swelling of feet and face, little or frothy urine, nausea and breathlessness are warning signs of failing kidneys**

**4.** What does this lesson say about dialysis — cleaning from outside?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Dialysis cleans the blood outside the kidney: in haemodialysis a machine filters the patient's blood through an artificial kidney
**Answer: d) Dialysis cleans the blood outside the kidney: in haemodialysis a machine filters the patient's blood through an artificial kidney**
