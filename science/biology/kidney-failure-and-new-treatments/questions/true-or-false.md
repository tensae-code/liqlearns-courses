# Kidney failure and new treatments — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Kidney failure means the kidneys can no longer filter the blood: urea and other wastes, salts and water build up in the body.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Dialysis repairs the patient's own kidneys so they heal..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.

**3.** Long-standing diabetes and high blood pressure, repeated infections, stones and some poisons are the common causes of kidney failure.
**Answer: True**

**4.** One donated kidney is not enough to clean the blood — two are always needed..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.
