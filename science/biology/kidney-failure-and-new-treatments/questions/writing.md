# Kidney failure and new treatments — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining kidney failure and new treatments, using the lesson's key facts.
**Model answer: When kidneys fail, wastes and water build up in the blood and poison the body — and the new treatments, dialysis and transplant, do the kidney's cleaning work from outside or replace the organ itself. Kidney failure means the kidneys can no longer filter the blood: urea and other wastes, salts and water build up in the body. Long-standing diabetes and high blood pressure, repeated infections, stones and some poisons are the common causes of kidney failure. Tiredness, swelling of feet and face, little or frothy urine, nausea and breathlessness are warning signs of failing kidneys.**
