# Kidney failure and new treatments — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: signs and course.
**Answer: Because the kidneys work silently, failure is often far advanced when it is felt. The signs track the undone jobs — fluid in the tissues (swelling), waste in the blood (nausea, exhaustion), and changes in the urine that a test can find long before the person feels ill.**

**2.** Explain: dialysis — cleaning from outside.
**Answer: In haemodialysis, blood leaves the body, passes along a membrane in the machine where wastes and extra water cross into a washing fluid, and returns cleaned — several hours at a time, several times a week, for as long as the kidneys cannot work. Peritoneal dialysis uses the patient's own abdominal lining as the membrane instead.**
