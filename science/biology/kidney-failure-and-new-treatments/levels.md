# Kidney failure and new treatments — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (When kidneys fail, wastes and water build up in the blood and poison the body — and the new treatments, dialysis and transplant, do the kidney's cleaning work from outside or replace the organ itself.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
