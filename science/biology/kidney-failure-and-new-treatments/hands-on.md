# Hands-on — Kidney failure and new treatments

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
The kidney's jobs can be listed on one hand: clean the blood, balance salts, shed extra water, help control blood pressure. Write the list from memory and say which of them dialysis does and which it does not.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of a haemodialysis machine at work, labelled — blood leaving the patient's arm, passing through the dialyser (artificial kidney) where wastes cross the membrane, and the cleaned blood returning — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
