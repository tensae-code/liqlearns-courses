# Immune system health — allergy and autoimmune diseases

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Immune system health — allergy and autoimmune diseases. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
hypersensitivity and allergy first, then the applied boxes: organ-transplant rejection, Alzheimer's disease, diabetes mellitus and autoimmune disease in the closing glossary discussions

[Picture — three panels: an allergen meeting an antibody labelled allergy, body tissue being attacked by its own immune cells labelled autoimmune disease, and a grafted organ under immune attack labelled rejection]

## The key facts
**Hypersensitivity and allergy.** Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 1 student book (Lesson 3-3, printed p. 120) opens the immune system's health lesson with hypersensitivity and allergy.

**Applied boxes.** The lesson's applied boxes run to organ-transplant rejection, Alzheimer's disease, diabetes mellitus and autoimmune disease, with the closing glossary discussions taking the same topics up again.

**Kuwait's way.** Kuwait closes the immune unit with its health lesson (Grade 12, Lesson 3-3): allergy first, then the applied boxes — transplant rejection, autoimmune disease — so the defence system is also taught by its failures.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words: the immune system itself can be the problem: hypersensitivity and allergy are its over-reactions, autoimmune diseases its attacks on the body's own tissues — and transplant rejection is the same system defending against a graft.
