# Hands-on — Immune system health — allergy and autoimmune diseases

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: the immune system itself can be the problem: hypersensitivity and allergy are its over-reactions, autoimmune diseases its attacks on the body's own tissues — and transplant rejection is the same system defending against a graft.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: three panels: an allergen meeting an antibody labelled allergy, body tissue being attacked by its own immune cells labelled autoimmune disease, and a grafted organ under immune attack labelled rejection. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
