# Immune system health — allergy and autoimmune diseases — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: the immune system itself can be the problem: hypersensitivity and allergy are its over-reactions, autoimmune diseases its attacks on the body's own tissues — and transplant rejection is the same system defending against a graft. Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 1 student book (Lesson 3-3, printed p. 120) opens the immune system's health lesson with hypersensitivity and allergy.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait closes the immune unit with its health lesson (Grade 12, Lesson 3-3): allergy first, then the applied boxes — transplant rejection, autoimmune disease — so the defence system is also taught by its failures.**
