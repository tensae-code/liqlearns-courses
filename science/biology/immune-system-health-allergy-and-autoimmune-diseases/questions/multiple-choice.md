Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) the immune system itself can be the problem: hypersensitivity and allergy are its over-reactions, autoimmune diseases its attacks on the body's own tissues — and transplant rejection is the same system defending against a graft  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
