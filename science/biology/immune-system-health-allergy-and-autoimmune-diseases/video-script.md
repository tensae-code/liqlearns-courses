Today: Immune system health — allergy and autoimmune diseases.

Remember this one thing: the immune system itself can be the problem: hypersensitivity and allergy are its over-reactions, autoimmune diseases its attacks on the body's own tissues — and transplant rejection is the same system defending against a graft.

Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 1 student book (Lesson 3-3, printed p. 120) opens the immune system's health lesson with hypersensitivity and allergy.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
