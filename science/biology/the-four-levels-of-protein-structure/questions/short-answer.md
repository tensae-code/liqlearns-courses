# The four levels of protein structure — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about protein structure?**
**Answer: Because enzymes are proteins, their building is taught in four levels: primary (the amino-acid sequence in the chain), secondary (folding into α-helix and β-sheet by hydrogen bonding), tertiary (the full three-dimensional shape held by side-chain interactions) and quaternary (several folded subunits packed together, as in haemoglobin); pupils draw all four as a wall poster.**
