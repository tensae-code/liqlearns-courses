# The four levels of protein structure — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1. ** Write two or three sentences explaining protein structure to a younger student. Use one example from the lesson.
**Model answer: Because enzymes are proteins, their building is taught in four levels: primary (the amino-acid sequence in the chain), secondary (folding into α-helix and β-sheet by hydrogen bonding), tertiary (the full three-dimensional shape held by side-chain interactions) and quaternary (several folded subunits packed together, as in haemoglobin)**
