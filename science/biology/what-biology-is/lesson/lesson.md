# What biology is

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — What biology is. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Biology comes from Greek words for life and study. It is the scientific study of life and living things, and it meets the other sciences: chemistry explains the substances and reactions inside organisms, and physics explains processes such as photosynthesis turning light energy into food.

## The key facts
**Living things.** Living things are made of cells, grow, reproduce, pass genetic information to offspring, use energy, keep internal conditions steady (homeostasis) and adapt over generations.

**Chemistry link.** Chemistry helps biology explain the substances and reactions inside organisms.

**Physics link.** Physics helps biology explain processes such as photosynthesis turning light energy into food.

**Homeostasis.** Homeostasis means keeping internal conditions steady.
