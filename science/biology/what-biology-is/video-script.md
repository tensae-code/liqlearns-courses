Today: What biology is.

Remember this one thing: biology is the scientific study of life and living things.

Living things are made of cells, grow, reproduce, pass genetic information to offspring, use energy, keep internal conditions steady (homeostasis) and adapt over generations.

Chemistry helps biology explain the substances and reactions inside organisms.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
