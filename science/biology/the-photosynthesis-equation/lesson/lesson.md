# The photosynthesis equation

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The photosynthesis equation. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
The whole of photosynthesis fits in one chemical summary. Learn what goes in, what comes out, and what powers it.

[Picture 22219 — the equation as a balance diagram — inputs left, outputs right, light and chlorophyll over the arrow]

## The key facts
**Inputs.** Carbon dioxide and water are the inputs of photosynthesis.

**Energy.** Light energy, caught by chlorophyll, powers the change.

**Outputs.** Glucose and oxygen are the outputs of photosynthesis.

**The oxygen.** The oxygen released comes from the water, and water itself is essential to the process.
