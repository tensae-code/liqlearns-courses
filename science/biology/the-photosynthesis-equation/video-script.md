# Video script — The photosynthesis equation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: The photosynthesis equation.

Remember this one thing: carbon dioxide + water, using light energy caught by chlorophyll, yields glucose + oxygen

Inputs: Carbon dioxide and water are the inputs of photosynthesis.

Energy: Light energy, caught by chlorophyll, powers the change.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
