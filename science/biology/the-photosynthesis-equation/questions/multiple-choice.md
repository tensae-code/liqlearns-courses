# The photosynthesis equation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about inputs?
a) Carbon dioxide and water are the inputs of photosynthesis  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Carbon dioxide and water are the inputs of photosynthesis**

**2.** What does this lesson say about energy?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Light energy, caught by chlorophyll, powers the change  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Light energy, caught by chlorophyll, powers the change**

**3.** What does this lesson say about outputs?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Glucose and oxygen are the outputs of photosynthesis  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Glucose and oxygen are the outputs of photosynthesis**

**4.** What does this lesson say about the oxygen?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) The oxygen released comes from the water, and water itself is essential to the process
**Answer: d) The oxygen released comes from the water, and water itself is essential to the process**
