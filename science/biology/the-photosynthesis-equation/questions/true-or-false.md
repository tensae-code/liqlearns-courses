# The photosynthesis equation — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Carbon dioxide and water are the inputs of photosynthesis.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Energy has nothing to do with this lesson’s topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Glucose and oxygen are the outputs of photosynthesis.
**Answer: True**

**4.** The oxygen has nothing to do with this lesson’s topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
