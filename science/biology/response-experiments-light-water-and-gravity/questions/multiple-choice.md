# Response experiments — light, water and gravity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about phototropism shown?
a) The light experiment demonstrates a shoot bending toward light  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The light experiment demonstrates a shoot bending toward light**

**2.** What does this lesson say about hydrotropism shown?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) The water experiment demonstrates roots growing toward water  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The water experiment demonstrates roots growing toward water**

**3.** What does this lesson say about the clinostat?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) A clinostat turns a seedling slowly, cancelling gravity's one-sided pull  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) A clinostat turns a seedling slowly, cancelling gravity's one-sided pull**

**4.** What does this lesson say about straight on the clinostat?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) A seedling on a turning clinostat grows straight, unlike one bent by gravity
**Answer: d) A seedling on a turning clinostat grows straight, unlike one bent by gravity**
