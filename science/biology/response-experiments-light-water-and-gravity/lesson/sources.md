Ch. 5 turns to plants: the auxin discovery chain from the Darwins to Went, the other hormones opened by a Song–Yuan dynasty text on ripening persimmons, growth regulators regulated as pesticides, and environmental control ending with flower-forcing for festivals and the 2008 Olympics.
