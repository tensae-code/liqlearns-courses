# Response experiments — light, water and gravity

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Response experiments — light, water and gravity. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
This doing-lesson runs the response experiments: light bending a shoot, water drawing roots, and a slowly turning clinostat that cancels gravity so a seedling grows straight.

[Picture 22228 — a seedling on a slowly turning clinostat staying straight beside one bent by gravity]

## The key facts
**Phototropism shown.** The light experiment demonstrates a shoot bending toward light.

**Hydrotropism shown.** The water experiment demonstrates roots growing toward water.

**The clinostat.** A clinostat turns a seedling slowly, cancelling gravity's one-sided pull.

**Straight on the clinostat.** A seedling on a turning clinostat grows straight, unlike one bent by gravity.
