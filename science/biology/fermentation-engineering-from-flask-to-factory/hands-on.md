# Hands-on — Fermentation engineering — from flask to factory

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: fermentation engineering runs microbes at factory scale as one chain — strain selection and breeding, scale-up culture, medium design, sterilisation, inoculation, the computer-monitored fermentation itself, then separation and purification — and the conditions steer what the microbes make, as glutamate fermentation's pH switch proves.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a chain of vessels growing left to right — flask, small tank, factory fermenter with gauges for temperature, pH and oxygen — ending in a separated, purified product. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
