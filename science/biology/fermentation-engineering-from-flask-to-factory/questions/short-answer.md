# Fermentation engineering — from flask to factory — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: fermentation engineering runs microbes at factory scale as one chain — strain selection and breeding, scale-up culture, medium design, sterilisation, inoculation, the computer-monitored fermentation itself, then separation and purification — and the conditions steer what the microbes make, as glutamate fermentation's pH switch proves. the process as a chain (strain selection and breeding, scale-up culture, medium design, sterilisation, inoculation, the fermentation itself under computer monitoring of temperature, pH, dissolved oxygen, pressure and foam, then separation and purification); penicillin's fall from priceless to about a yuan a vial as the scale story; the pH switch in glutamate fermentation (alkaline conditions accumulate glutamate, acid ones glutamine) as proof that conditions steer metabolism; China's fermentation industry at nearly ¥290 billion a year by 2015, the world's largest.**
