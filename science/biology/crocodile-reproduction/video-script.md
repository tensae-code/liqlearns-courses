# Video script — Crocodile reproduction

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Large semi-aquatic reptiles with internal fertilisation; mating is underwater in the July–August season, and the female buries 10–100 hard, leathery-shelled eggs in sand or mound vegetation — the hard shell, unlike the frog's jelly egg, protects the young on land; the life cycle is figured end to end.

The guide adds that only about 20% of a crocodile's eggs produce offspring, that hatching takes about 80 days (up to 100 in cooler temperatures), and — developed further in Unit Four — that crocodile sex is temperature-dependent: moderate incubation temperatures produce males, warm and cool extremes produce females.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
