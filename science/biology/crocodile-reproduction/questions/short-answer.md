# Crocodile reproduction — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about reproduction in crocodiles?**
**Answer: Large semi-aquatic reptiles with internal fertilisation; mating is underwater in the July–August season, and the female buries 10–100 hard, leathery-shelled eggs in sand or mound vegetation — the hard shell, unlike the frog's jelly egg, protects the young on land; the life cycle is figured end to end.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added crocodile detail?**
**Answer: The guide adds that only about 20% of a crocodile's eggs produce offspring, that hatching takes about 80 days (up to 100 in cooler temperatures), and — developed further in Unit Four — that crocodile sex is temperature-dependent: moderate incubation temperatures produce males, warm and cool extremes produce females.**
