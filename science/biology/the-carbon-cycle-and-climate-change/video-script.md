# Video script — The carbon cycle — and climate change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: The carbon cycle — and climate change.

Remember this one thing: carbon moves between air, living bodies, dead matter and fuels — photosynthesis takes carbon dioxide in, respiration and decomposition return it

The stations: Carbon moves between air, living bodies, dead matter and fuels.

Taken in: Photosynthesis takes carbon dioxide in from the air.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
