# The carbon cycle — and climate change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about the stations?
a) Carbon moves between air, living bodies, dead matter and fuels  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Carbon moves between air, living bodies, dead matter and fuels**

**2.** What does this lesson say about taken in?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Photosynthesis takes carbon dioxide in from the air  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Photosynthesis takes carbon dioxide in from the air**

**3.** What does this lesson say about returned?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Respiration and decomposition return carbon dioxide to the air  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Respiration and decomposition return carbon dioxide to the air**

**4.** What does this lesson say about climate link?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) The cycle closes by linking carbon dioxide to climate change
**Answer: d) The cycle closes by linking carbon dioxide to climate change**
