# The carbon cycle — and climate change

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The carbon cycle — and climate change. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Carbon goes round: out of the air into plants, through bodies, back to the air. This cycle's balance is where climate change enters the story.

[Picture 22273 — the carbon cycle with photosynthesis and respiration arrows in opposite colours]

## The key facts
**The stations.** Carbon moves between air, living bodies, dead matter and fuels.

**Taken in.** Photosynthesis takes carbon dioxide in from the air.

**Returned.** Respiration and decomposition return carbon dioxide to the air.

**Climate link.** The cycle closes by linking carbon dioxide to climate change.
