# Fruits — types and placentation — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why a maize grain counts as a fruit while a bean pod's seeds, not the pod, are what most people call the vegetable.
**Model answer: The fruit is the ripened ovary, whatever the kitchen calls it: in maize the grain itself is the fruit (a caryopsis) with the seed fused inside it, while in the bean the pod is the fruit and the beans inside are its seeds. Botanical classification follows the making, not the menu — which is exactly why Kenya teaches the kinds by structure.**
