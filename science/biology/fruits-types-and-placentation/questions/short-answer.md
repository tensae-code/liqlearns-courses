# Fruits — types and placentation — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Classify these fruits as Kenya's Form 3 book does: tomato, mango, maize, cotton, macadamia.
**Answer: Tomato — berry; mango — drupe; maize — caryopsis; cotton — capsule; macadamia — nut (Ch. 3, printed p. 100).**

**2.** Explain Kenya's way of teaching fruits.
**Answer: Kenya builds the topic in order: development first (gibberellin-driven growth, parthenocarpy in pineapple and banana), then the classification of fruits kind by kind with a Kenyan-market example for each, false fruits separated out, the five placentation types underneath — and a fruit-classification practical to prove the system on real fruits.**
