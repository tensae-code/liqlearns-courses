# Fruits — types and placentation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A mango is classified as a…**
a) berry  b) drupe  c) legume  d) caryopsis
**Answer: b) drupe — the stone fruit**

**2. Parthenocarpy is…**
a) fruit forming without fertilisation, as in pineapple and banana  b) seed dispersal by wind  c) double fertilisation  d) a placentation type
**Answer: a) fruit forming without fertilisation, as in pineapple and banana**

**3. Maize's fruit type is a…**
a) nut  b) cypsela  c) caryopsis  d) capsule
**Answer: c) caryopsis**

**4. Placentation describes…**
a) how fruits are dispersed  b) how ovules were attached inside the ovary  c) how seeds germinate  d) how petals are arranged
**Answer: b) how ovules were attached inside the ovary**
