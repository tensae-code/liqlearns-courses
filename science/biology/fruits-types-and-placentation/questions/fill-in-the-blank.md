# Fruits — types and placentation — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Fruit without fertilisation, as in banana, is called _____.**
**Answer: parthenocarpy**

**2. The hormone driving fruit growth is _____.**
**Answer: gibberellin**

**3. A tomato's fruit type is a _____.**
**Answer: berry**

**4. Macadamia's fruit type is a _____.**
**Answer: nut**
