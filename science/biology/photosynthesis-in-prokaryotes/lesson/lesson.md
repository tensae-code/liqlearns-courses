# Photosynthesis in prokaryotes

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Photosynthesis in prokaryotes. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Photosynthesis began in prokaryotes: cyanobacteria run it much as plants do and filled the air with oxygen, while other bacteria photosynthesise without releasing oxygen, using sulphur compounds instead of water — the process is older than the chloroplast, which is itself a captured prokaryote. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from Greece's Gymnasio and Lykeio biology books, which teach it as a named topic.

[Picture — a cyanobacterium chain in pond water releasing oxygen bubbles toward the sun, beside a purple sulphur bacterium depositing yellow sulphur grains]
