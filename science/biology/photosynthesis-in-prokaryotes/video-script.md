Today: Photosynthesis in prokaryotes.

Remember this one thing: photosynthesis began in prokaryotes: cyanobacteria run it much as plants do and filled the air with oxygen, while other bacteria photosynthesise without releasing oxygen, using sulphur compounds instead of water — the process is older than the chloroplast, which is itself a captured prokaryote.
