# Plant tissues — meristem and permanent tissues — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (A plant's tissues divide by one question — do the cells still divide? Meristem (творно) tissue is the dividing tissue at the growing points; permanent (трајни) tissues are its finished cells, divided no more, each specialised for its job.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a fresh example from the learner's own surroundings and saying why it fits, without looking.
