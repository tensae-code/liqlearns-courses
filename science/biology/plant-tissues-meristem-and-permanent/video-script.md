Today: Plant tissues — meristem and permanent tissues.

Remember this one thing: A plant's tissues divide by one question — do the cells still divide? Meristem (творно) tissue is the dividing tissue at the growing points; permanent (трајни) tissues are its finished cells, divided no more, each specialised for its job.

Ask of any plant tissue: do its cells still divide? That one question sorts them all.

At the tips and the cambium sits meristem — small cells, dividing without rest, the source of every cell the plant will ever have. Behind the growing fronts lie the permanent tissues: finished, specialised, no longer dividing. Growth at the fronts, finished work behind.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
