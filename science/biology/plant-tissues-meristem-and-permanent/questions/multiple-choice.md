# Plant tissues — meristem and permanent tissues — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is meristem?**
a) The tissue of dead cells in bark  b) The dividing tissue at a plant's growing points  c) The tissue that carries water  d) The skin of the leaf
**Answer: b) The dividing tissue at a plant's growing points**

**2. Where is meristem found?**
a) Only in flowers  b) At shoot and root tips and in the cambium  c) Spread evenly through the whole plant  d) Only in seeds
**Answer: b) At shoot and root tips and in the cambium**

**3. What are permanent tissues?**
a) Tissues that never die  b) Cells from meristem that have stopped dividing and specialised  c) The youngest tissues  d) Tissues found only in roots
**Answer: b) Cells from meristem that have stopped dividing and specialised**

**4. How does a plant grow longer?**
a) Every cell along the stem divides  b) Only the meristem at the tips divides, adding cells that become permanent tissue  c) The permanent cells stretch forever  d) Roots push the stem up
**Answer: b) Only the meristem at the tips divides, adding cells that become permanent tissue**
