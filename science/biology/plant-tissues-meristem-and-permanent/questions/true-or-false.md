# Plant tissues — meristem and permanent tissues — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Most of a plant's body is permanent tissue.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Permanent tissue cells keep dividing all their lives.**
**Answer: False** — permanent tissue is formed from meristem cells that have stopped dividing.

**3. The cambium is a meristem that thickens stems.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. A plant grows throughout its body the way an animal does.**
**Answer: False** — growth is at the fronts — tips and cambium — while finished parts stay as they are.
