# Plant tissues — meristem and permanent tissues — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: meristem and permanent tissue, and how one becomes the other.
**Answer: Meristem is the dividing tissue at the growing points — small thin-walled cells that keep dividing. Its cells, as they are left behind by the growing front, stop dividing, grow to their final size and shape, and specialise — becoming permanent tissue, the finished body of the plant.**

**2.** Explain: North Macedonia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: North Macedonia's Biology II opens plant histology with meristematic (творно) tissue and permanent (трајни) tissues as the first two lessons of the theme (Themes 3.1–3.2, printed pp. 180–184), before the permanent kinds — parenchyma, mechanical and conducting tissue — and the organs.**
