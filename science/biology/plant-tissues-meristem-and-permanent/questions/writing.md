# Plant tissues — meristem and permanent tissues — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining where a plant's growth happens and why the older stem does not keep lengthening.
**Model answer: A plant grows only where meristem divides: at the shoot and root tips for length, and at the cambium for thickness. The parts behind the tips are permanent tissue — finished cells that no longer divide. So an older stretch of stem cannot lengthen; new length is only ever added at the tip.**
