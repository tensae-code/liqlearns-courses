# Plant tissues — meristem and permanent tissues — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The dividing tissue of a plant is called _____.**
**Answer: meristem**

**2. Tissues whose cells have stopped dividing and specialised are _____ tissues.**
**Answer: permanent**

**3. The study of plant tissues is plant _____.**
**Answer: histology**

**4. Meristem at the stem's side that makes the stem thicker is the _____.**
**Answer: cambium**
