# Hands-on — Comparing invertebrate body systems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Choose two invertebrates you know — say an earthworm and a grasshopper. On paper, compare them across the five systems of this lesson, one row per system.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of a comparison table drawn as five animals in a row — sponge, hydra, flatworm, earthworm, grasshopper — with one label under each naming how it digests, breathes, transports, controls and excretes — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
