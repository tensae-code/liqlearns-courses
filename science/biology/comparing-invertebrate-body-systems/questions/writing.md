**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining comparing invertebrate body systems, using the lesson's key facts.
**Model answer: Across the invertebrate phyla the same life jobs — feeding, breathing, transport, control, excretion — are done by ever more complete systems, from diffusion alone in the simplest animals to closed vessels, brains and kidneys in the highest. Invertebrate digestion climbs from none (sponges filter food inside cells) through a sac gut with one opening (cnidarians, flatworms) to a tube gut with mouth and anus (roundworms and all higher phyla). Small and water-living invertebrates breathe through the body surface; larger and land forms evolve gills, tracheae or book lungs. Many invertebrates have no transport system at all; molluscs and arthropods have an open circulation, while annelids and cephalopods have closed vessels.**
