# Comparing invertebrate body systems — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Invertebrate digestion climbs from none (sponges filter food inside cells) through a sac gut with one opening (cnidarians, flatworms) to a tube gut with mouth and anus (roundworms and all higher phyla).
**Answer: True**

**2.** A hydra has a brain at the front of its nerve net..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.

**3.** Small and water-living invertebrates breathe through the body surface; larger and land forms evolve gills, tracheae or book lungs.
**Answer: True**

**4.** In an open circulation the blood never leaves the blood vessels..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.
