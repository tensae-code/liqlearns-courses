# Comparing invertebrate body systems — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: transport.
**Answer: In an open system the blood leaves the vessels and bathes the tissues directly — slow, but enough for a slug or a crab. In the closed system of the earthworm (and the octopus among molluscs), blood stays in vessels driven by hearts, and reaches every part faster and under control.**

**2.** Explain: control — nervous systems.
**Answer: A hydra's nerve net coordinates without any centre. Flatworms gather cords and a simple brain; earthworms and insects run a double ventral nerve cord with a ganglion in each segment and a brain in the head — faster, directed behaviour becomes possible at each step.**
