# Comparing invertebrate body systems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about feeding and digestion?
a) Invertebrate digestion climbs from none (sponges filter food inside cells) through a sac gut with one opening (cnidarians, flatworms) to a tube gut with mouth and anus (roundworms and all higher phyla)
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Invertebrate digestion climbs from none (sponges filter food inside cells) through a sac gut with one opening (cnidarians, flatworms) to a tube gut with mouth and anus (roundworms and all higher phyla)**

**2.** What does this lesson say about breathing?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Small and water-living invertebrates breathe through the body surface; larger and land forms evolve gills, tracheae or book lungs
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Small and water-living invertebrates breathe through the body surface; larger and land forms evolve gills, tracheae or book lungs**

**3.** What does this lesson say about transport?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Many invertebrates have no transport system at all; molluscs and arthropods have an open circulation, while annelids and cephalopods have closed vessels

**Answer: c) Many invertebrates have no transport system at all; molluscs and arthropods have an open circulation, while annelids and cephalopods have closed vessels**

**4.** What does this lesson say about control — nervous systems?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Nervous control climbs from the nerve net of cnidarians through the ladder system of flatworms to the brain and ventral nerve cord of annelids and arthropods
**Answer: d) Nervous control climbs from the nerve net of cnidarians through the ladder system of flatworms to the brain and ventral nerve cord of annelids and arthropods**
