# The renowned biologists — who found what

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The renowned biologists — who found what. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Biology was built by people, one discovery at a time. This lesson walks the procession of the field's founders and fixes the one discovery each one is remembered by. The discoveries table gathers them all into one revision spine.

[Picture 22201 — a timeline of portraits with each biologist's discovery in a caption strip]

## The key facts
**Early founders.** Aristotle and Galen are among the earliest founders remembered in this procession.

**Seeing small life.** Leeuwenhoek, with the microscope, first saw living things too small for the eye.

**Naming and evolving.** Linnaeus gave classification its system and Darwin gave evolution its explanation.

**Genes and DNA.** Mendel found the pattern of inheritance, and Watson and Crick found the shape of DNA.

**Germs and cloning.** Pasteur and Koch tied germs to disease, and Wilmut and Campbell cloned a mammal.
