# The renowned biologists — who found what — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about early founders?
a) Aristotle and Galen are among the earliest founders remembered in this procession  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Aristotle and Galen are among the earliest founders remembered in this procession**

**2.** What does this lesson say about seeing small life?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Leeuwenhoek, with the microscope, first saw living things too small for the eye  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Leeuwenhoek, with the microscope, first saw living things too small for the eye**

**3.** What does this lesson say about naming and evolving?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Linnaeus gave classification its system and Darwin gave evolution its explanation  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Linnaeus gave classification its system and Darwin gave evolution its explanation**

**4.** What does this lesson say about genes and dna?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Mendel found the pattern of inheritance, and Watson and Crick found the shape of DNA
**Answer: d) Mendel found the pattern of inheritance, and Watson and Crick found the shape of DNA**
