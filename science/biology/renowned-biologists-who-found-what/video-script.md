# Video script — The renowned biologists — who found what

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: The renowned biologists — who found what.

Remember this one thing: each renowned biologist is remembered by one discovery — from Aristotle and Linnaeus to Watson, Crick, Wilmut and Campbell

Early founders: Aristotle and Galen are among the earliest founders remembered in this procession.

Seeing small life: Leeuwenhoek, with the microscope, first saw living things too small for the eye.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
