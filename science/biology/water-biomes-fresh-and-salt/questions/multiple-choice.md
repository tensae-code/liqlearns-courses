# Water biomes — fresh and salt — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about coverage?
a) Water biomes cover about 75 percent of Earth's surface
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Water biomes cover about 75 percent of Earth's surface**

**2.** What does this lesson say about freshwater?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Freshwater biomes (lakes, streams, rivers, wetlands) have salt under 1 percent
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Freshwater biomes (lakes, streams, rivers, wetlands) have salt under 1 percent**

**3.** What does this lesson say about marine?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Marine biomes (oceans, intertidal zones, coral reefs) have salt about 3 percent
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Marine biomes (oceans, intertidal zones, coral reefs) have salt about 3 percent**

**4.** What does this lesson say about estuaries?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
