# Water biomes — fresh and salt

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Water biomes — fresh and salt. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Freshwater holds salt under 1 percent; marine water about 3 percent; estuaries are where the two meet.

[Picture 21078 — a river running through an estuary into an ocean]

## The key facts
**Coverage.** Water biomes cover about 75 percent of Earth's surface.

**Freshwater.** Freshwater biomes (lakes, streams, rivers, wetlands) have salt under 1 percent.

**Marine.** Marine biomes (oceans, intertidal zones, coral reefs) have salt about 3 percent.

**Estuaries.** Estuaries are where freshwater and marine water meet.
