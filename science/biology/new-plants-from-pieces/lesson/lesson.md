# New plants from pieces of the parent plant

## The idea
Farmers use vegetative propagation deliberately, for crops that must keep the parent's qualities.

## The key facts
**Parts.** Vegetative propagation grows new plants from stems, runners, tubers, bulbs and cuttings.

**Identical.** Plants grown vegetatively are identical to the parent.

**Farmers.** Farmers use vegetative propagation deliberately for crops that must keep the parent's qualities.

**Tuber.** A potato tuber sprouts shoots from its eyes.
