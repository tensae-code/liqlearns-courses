# Video script — New plants from pieces of the parent plant

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: New plants from pieces of the parent plant.

Remember this one thing: plants reproduce asexually from stems, runners, tubers, bulbs and cuttings — identical to the parent.

Vegetative propagation grows new plants from stems, runners, tubers, bulbs and cuttings.

Plants grown vegetatively are identical to the parent.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
