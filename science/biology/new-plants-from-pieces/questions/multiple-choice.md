# New plants from pieces of the parent plant — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about parts?
a) Vegetative propagation grows new plants from stems, runners, tubers, bulbs and cuttings
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Vegetative propagation grows new plants from stems, runners, tubers, bulbs and cuttings**

**2.** What does this lesson say about identical?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Plants grown vegetatively are identical to the parent
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Plants grown vegetatively are identical to the parent**

**3.** What does this lesson say about farmers?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Farmers use vegetative propagation deliberately for crops that must keep the parent's qualities
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Farmers use vegetative propagation deliberately for crops that must keep the parent's qualities**

**4.** What does this lesson say about tuber?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) A potato tuber sprouts shoots from its eyes
**Answer: d) A potato tuber sprouts shoots from its eyes**
