# Reptiles — the amniotic egg and embryonic membranes — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Reptiles are the first fully terrestrial vertebrates: a thick waterproof skin of keratin scales keeps water in, fertilisation is internal, development is direct, and the amniotic egg — with its four embryonic annexes, chorion, amnion, allantois and yolk sac — lets the embryo grow up inside its own private pond, away from water.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by naming the four annexes and one job of each without looking — chorion, amnion, allantois, yolk sac — and tying the whole set back to the one thing: reproduction freed from water.
