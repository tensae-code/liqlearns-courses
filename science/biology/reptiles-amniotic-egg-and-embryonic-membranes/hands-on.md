# Hands-on — Reptiles — the amniotic egg and embryonic membranes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Take a hen's egg and crack it into a bowl: find the yolk — the food sac the embryo would live on. The reptile egg adds what the bird kept: membranes around the embryo itself, the amnion holding it in fluid. Name the four annexes aloud as you point to where each would lie.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — a reptile egg cut open with the small curled embryo in the middle, wrapped in its fluid sac, and the four membranes labelled around it — chorion outside, amnion around the embryo, allantois and the yolk sac beside it — label its parts, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
