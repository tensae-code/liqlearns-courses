# Reptiles — the amniotic egg and embryonic membranes — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Name the four embryonic annexes of the reptile egg.
**Answer: The chorion, the amnion, the allantois and the yolk sac — the four embryonic annexes (membranes), each with its own function in the embryo's development.**

**2.** Explain Mozambique's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Mozambique's PESD II animal module (Módulo 3 de Biologia), Lesson 17, teaches the reptiles as the first fully terrestrial vertebrates: thick waterproof keratin-scale skin, ectothermy tying them to warm regions, internal fertilisation and direct development — and the four embryonic annexes, chorion, amnion, allantois and yolk sac, each with its function, the amniotic egg taught as the key to life away from water.**
