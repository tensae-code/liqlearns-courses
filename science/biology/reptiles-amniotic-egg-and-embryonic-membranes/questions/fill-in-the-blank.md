# Reptiles — the amniotic egg and embryonic membranes — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The waterproof scales of a reptile are made of _____.**
**Answer: keratin**

**2. The egg that carries its own fluid pond is called the _____ egg.**
**Answer: amniotic**

**3. The annexe that holds the embryo in fluid is the _____.**
**Answer: amnion**

**4. Reptiles are _____ — their temperature follows the surroundings.**
**Answer: ectotherms**
