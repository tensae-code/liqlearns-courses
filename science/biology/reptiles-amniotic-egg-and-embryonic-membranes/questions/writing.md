# Reptiles — the amniotic egg and embryonic membranes — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why the amniotic egg freed the reptiles from water.
**Model answer: Amphibians must return to water to breed, because their eggs and larvae dry out. The reptile's amniotic egg carries the water with it: the amnion holds the embryo in fluid, the yolk sac feeds it, and the other annexes protect and serve it inside a shell. Development can therefore finish on dry land, and the reptiles became the first fully terrestrial vertebrates.**
