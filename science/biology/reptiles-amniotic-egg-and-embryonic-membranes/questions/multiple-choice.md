# Reptiles — the amniotic egg and embryonic membranes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The reptile skin is…**
a) thin, moist and glandular  b) thick, waterproof, with keratin scales  c) covered in feathers  d) covered in hair
**Answer: b) thick, waterproof, with keratin scales**

**2. How many embryonic annexes does the module teach in the reptile egg?**
a) two  b) three  c) four  d) five
**Answer: c) four**

**3. The membrane that wraps the embryo in fluid is the…**
a) chorion  b) amnion  c) allantois  d) yolk sac
**Answer: b) amnion**

**4. Reptiles are tied to warm regions because they are…**
a) ectotherms — body temperature follows the surroundings  b) too heavy to move in cold water  c) nocturnal  d) unable to lay eggs in the cold
**Answer: a) ectotherms — body temperature follows the surroundings**
