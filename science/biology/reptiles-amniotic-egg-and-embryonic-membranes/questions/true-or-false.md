# Reptiles — the amniotic egg and embryonic membranes — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Reptile development includes a free-swimming larval stage.**
**Answer: False** — development is direct — the young hatch as small reptiles.

**2. Fertilisation in reptiles is internal.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. The yolk sac is one of the four embryonic annexes.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. Reptiles regulate their body temperature internally like mammals.**
**Answer: False** — they are ectotherms, tied to warm regions.
