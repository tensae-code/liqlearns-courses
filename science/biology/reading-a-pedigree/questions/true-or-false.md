# Reading a pedigree — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Family trees drawn with squares (males), circles (females), marriage lines and shaded affected members let scientists read a trait's history: worked pedigrees for an autosomal dominant and an autosomal recessive trait show how analysis reveals whether a trait is dominant or recessive, autosomal or sex-linked, the genotypes of family members, and the risks to future children.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Two fully narrated pedigrees beyond the student book's figures: freckles (dominant F) traced over three generations from a freckled grandmother, counting which children and grandchildren show the trait; and colour blindness (recessive, X-linked) over four generations, with the reasoning made explicit — unaffected parents with an affected child proves recessive, and males being exclusively affected points to X-linkage.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
