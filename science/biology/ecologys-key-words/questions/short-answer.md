# Ecology's key words — habitat, niche, producer, consumer, decomposer — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: producers and consumers.
**Answer: Producers (plants and algae) trap sunlight into food; consumers are animals eating producers or other consumers.**

**2.** Explain: decomposers.
**Answer: Decomposers (bacteria and fungi) return dead matter to reusable substances.**
