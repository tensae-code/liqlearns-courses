# Ecology's key words — habitat, niche, producer, consumer, decomposer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about ecology?
a) Ecology studies organisms' interactions with each other and their environment
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Ecology studies organisms' interactions with each other and their environment**

**2.** What does this lesson say about habitat and niche?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) A habitat is where an organism lives; a niche is its job plus its place
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) A habitat is where an organism lives; a niche is its job plus its place**

**3.** What does this lesson say about producers and consumers?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Producers (plants and algae) trap sunlight into food; consumers are animals eating producers or other consumers
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Producers (plants and algae) trap sunlight into food; consumers are animals eating producers or other consumers**

**4.** What does this lesson say about decomposers?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Decomposers (bacteria and fungi) return dead matter to reusable substances
**Answer: d) Decomposers (bacteria and fungi) return dead matter to reusable substances**
