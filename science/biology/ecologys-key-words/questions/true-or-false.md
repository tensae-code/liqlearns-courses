# Ecology's key words — habitat, niche, producer, consumer, decomposer — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Ecology studies organisms' interactions with each other and their environment.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Habitat and niche has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Producers (plants and algae) trap sunlight into food; consumers are animals eating producers or other consumers.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Decomposers has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
