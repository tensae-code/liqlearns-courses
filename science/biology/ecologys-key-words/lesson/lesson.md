## The key facts
**Ecology.** Ecology studies organisms' interactions with each other and their environment.

**Habitat and niche.** A habitat is where an organism lives; a niche is its job plus its place.

**Producers and consumers.** Producers (plants and algae) trap sunlight into food; consumers are animals eating producers or other consumers.

**Decomposers.** Decomposers (bacteria and fungi) return dead matter to reusable substances.
