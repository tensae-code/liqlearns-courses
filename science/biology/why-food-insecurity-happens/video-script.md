# Video script — Why food insecurity happens

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Global food supply is uneven because physical factors (climate, soil quality, gradient) and human factors (technology) control what is produced where; the causes-of-food-insecurity list adds climate change, poor nutrition, poor public policy, economy, food waste, gender inequality and forced migration; and hunger (the painful sensation of food deprivation) is distinguished from food insecurity — all hungry people are food insecure, but not all food-insecure people are hungry, since micronutrient-poor intake also counts.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
