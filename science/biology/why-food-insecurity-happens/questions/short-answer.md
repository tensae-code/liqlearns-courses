# Why food insecurity happens — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added food-insecurity analysis?**
**Answer: Global food supply is uneven because physical factors (climate, soil quality, gradient) and human factors (technology) control what is produced where; the causes-of-food-insecurity list adds climate change, poor nutrition, poor public policy, economy, food waste, gender inequality and forced migration; and hunger (the painful sensation of food deprivation) is distinguished from food insecurity — all hungry people are food insecure, but not all food-insecure people are hungry, since micronutrient-poor intake also counts.**
