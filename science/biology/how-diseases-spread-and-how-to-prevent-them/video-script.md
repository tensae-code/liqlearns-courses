# Video script — How Diseases Spread and How to Prevent Them

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

transmission by contact, droplet, airborne spread and common vehicles, organised in a worked table — droplet infection (common cold, flu, pneumonia), contaminated water (cholera, typhoid), contaminated food (salmonellosis, listeriosis, botulism), direct contact (athlete's foot, ringworm) and sexual intercourse (candidiasis, syphilis, AIDS) — with prevention matched to each route.
