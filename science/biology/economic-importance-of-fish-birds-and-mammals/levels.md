# Economic importance of fish, birds and mammals — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Fish, birds and mammals earn their keep in a household economy: they give food, materials, labour and services — and protecting them protects that income.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
