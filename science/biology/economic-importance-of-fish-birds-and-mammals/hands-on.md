# Hands-on — Economic importance of fish, birds and mammals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
List the fish, birds and mammals your household or neighbours keep or use. Beside each write what it gives — food, material, labour or service — and one thing people do, or should do, to keep it giving.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of a village economy in four panels — a fisher with his catch, hens and eggs in a yard, a sheep being shorn, and a donkey carrying a load on a mountain path — each panel labelled with what the animal gives — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
