# Economic importance of fish, birds and mammals — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Fish are a protein food and a cash income for the families that catch and sell them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Taking every fish from a river increases the income it gives in later years..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.

**3.** Birds give eggs and meat, and wild birds work free: they eat insect pests and clear carrion.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Domestic mammals give materials but never labour..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.
