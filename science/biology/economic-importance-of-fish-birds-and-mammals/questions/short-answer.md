# Economic importance of fish, birds and mammals — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: mammals give food and materials.
**Answer: Sheep, goats and cattle convert grass people cannot eat into milk and meat people can. Their wool and hair are spun and woven, their hides become leather — for Afghanistan, livestock products are household food and market goods at once.**

**2.** Explain: mammals give labour.
**Answer: Before engines, and still where roads and fuel run short, working mammals are transport and tractor in one: pack loads on mountain paths, carts and ploughs in the fields, and herding across the plains.**
