# Economic importance of fish, birds and mammals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about fish give food and income?
a) Fish are a protein food and a cash income for the families that catch and sell them
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Fish are a protein food and a cash income for the families that catch and sell them**

**2.** What does this lesson say about birds give food — and free work?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Birds give eggs and meat, and wild birds work free: they eat insect pests and clear carrion
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Birds give eggs and meat, and wild birds work free: they eat insect pests and clear carrion**

**3.** What does this lesson say about mammals give food and materials?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Mammals give milk, meat, wool, hair and leather — the raw materials of food, clothing and trade
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Mammals give milk, meat, wool, hair and leather — the raw materials of food, clothing and trade**

**4.** What does this lesson say about mammals give labour?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Horses, donkeys, mules, oxen and camels carry, pull and plough — animal labour moves goods and works fields
**Answer: d) Horses, donkeys, mules, oxen and camels carry, pull and plough — animal labour moves goods and works fields**
