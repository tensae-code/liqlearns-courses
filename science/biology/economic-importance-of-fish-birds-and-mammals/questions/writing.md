# Economic importance of fish, birds and mammals — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining economic importance of fish, birds and mammals, using the lesson's key facts.
**Model answer: Fish, birds and mammals earn their keep in a household economy: they give food, materials, labour and services — and protecting them protects that income. Fish are a protein food and a cash income for the families that catch and sell them. Birds give eggs and meat, and wild birds work free: they eat insect pests and clear carrion. Mammals give milk, meat, wool, hair and leather — the raw materials of food, clothing and trade.**
