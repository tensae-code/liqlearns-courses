# Causes of infertility — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about infertility?**
**Answer: Female infertility centres on ovulation failure — thyroid problems, pelvic-surgery damage to tubes and ovaries, cervical-mucus problems, fibroids, endometriosis; male infertility shows as immotile or too few sperm (under 15 million per ml), testicular pain or swelling, and risk factors including smoking, alcohol and drugs, overweight, toxins, past STIs, overheating and trauma, with cause percentages figured.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added infertility detail?**
**Answer: The guide extends the infertility lists: female causes add pelvic inflammatory disease, sterilisation as a chosen cause, and medicines and drugs; and a male-conditions block names hydrocele (a fluid-filled sac around the testis), hypospadias (the urinary opening below the penis tip), benign prostatic hypertrophy (prostate swelling causing difficult urination and night urination) and its surgical treatment by transurethral resection — none of which the student book names.**
