# Video script — Causes of infertility

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Female infertility centres on ovulation failure — thyroid problems, pelvic-surgery damage to tubes and ovaries, cervical-mucus problems, fibroids, endometriosis; male infertility shows as immotile or too few sperm (under 15 million per ml), testicular pain or swelling, and risk factors including smoking, alcohol and drugs, overweight, toxins, past STIs, overheating and trauma, with cause percentages figured.

The guide extends the infertility lists: female causes add pelvic inflammatory disease, sterilisation as a chosen cause, and medicines and drugs; and a male-conditions block names hydrocele (a fluid-filled sac around the testis), hypospadias (the urinary opening below the penis tip), benign prostatic hypertrophy (prostate swelling causing difficult urination and night urination) and its surgical treatment by transurethral resection — none of which the student book names.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
