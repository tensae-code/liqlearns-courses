# Video script — Preventing sexual abuse

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Preventing sexual abuse.

Remember this one thing: Prevention of sexual abuse: knowing that your body is your own, recognising behaviour that is not acceptable, refusing and getting away, and telling a trusted adult — abuse is always the offender's fault, never the victim's.

This lesson is about keeping your own body safe. Your body belongs to you — nobody may touch it in ways that make you uncomfortable, and nobody may ask you to keep such things secret.

If it happens: say no, get away, and tell a trusted adult — and keep telling until someone acts. It is never the victim's fault. That is the whole lesson, and it is worth knowing before it is ever needed.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
