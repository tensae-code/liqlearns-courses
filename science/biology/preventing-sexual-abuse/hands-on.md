# Hands-on — Preventing sexual abuse

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Without naming anyone: write three short lists — (1) three trusted adults you could tell, (2) three places you can go to be safe, (3) the exact first sentence you would say to tell an adult. Keep the paper somewhere private. Learning the words before they are needed is the prevention this lesson teaches.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a young person standing firm with one hand raised in a clear 'stop', and beside them the trusted adults of their life — a parent, a teacher, a doctor — drawn as the people who can be told — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
