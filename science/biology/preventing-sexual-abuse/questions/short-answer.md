# Preventing sexual abuse — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the three steps of prevention this lesson teaches.
**Answer: First know that your body is your own and what behaviour is not acceptable. Then, in the situation itself, refuse clearly and get away. Then tell a trusted adult, and keep telling until someone acts — abuse is always the offender's fault.**

**2.** Explain: North Macedonia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: North Macedonia's Grade 8 textbook teaches prevention of sexual abuse inside biology, as the closing lesson (Theme 3.12, printed p. 192) of its theme on reproduction and development in humans — after puberty, the menstrual cycle, reproductive health, family planning and rights and care.**
