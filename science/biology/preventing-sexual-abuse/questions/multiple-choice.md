# Preventing sexual abuse — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Who does your body belong to?**
a) To your parents  b) To you  c) To your teachers  d) To whoever is oldest
**Answer: b) To you**

**2. What should you do first if someone touches you in a way that is not acceptable?**
a) Keep it secret  b) Say no clearly and get away  c) Wait to see if it happens again  d) Blame yourself
**Answer: b) Say no clearly and get away**

**3. After getting away, what completes the protection?**
a) Telling a trusted adult — and telling another if the first does not act  b) Telling nobody  c) Posting it online first  d) Forgetting it
**Answer: a) Telling a trusted adult — and telling another if the first does not act**

**4. Whose fault is sexual abuse?**
a) The victim's  b) Nobody's — it just happens  c) The offender's, always  d) The family's
**Answer: c) The offender's, always**
