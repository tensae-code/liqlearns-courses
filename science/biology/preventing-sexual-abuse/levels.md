# Preventing sexual abuse — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Prevention of sexual abuse: knowing that your body is your own, recognising behaviour that is not acceptable, refusing and getting away, and telling a trusted adult — abuse is always the offender's fault, never the victim's.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a fresh example from the learner's own surroundings and saying why it fits, without looking.
