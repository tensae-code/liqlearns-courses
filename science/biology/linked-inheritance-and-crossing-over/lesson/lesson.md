## The key facts
**The cross.** Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 1 applications workbook (Activity 11, printed p. 40) builds a Punnett square for the cross BbMm × bbmm and writes down the expected offspring genotype ratios.

**What linkage gives instead.** The expected ratios are then compared against what linkage actually gives: linked genes travel together, so the offspring ratios depart from the independent-assortment expectation — crossing over is the exchange that can part them.

**Kuwait's way.** Kuwait teaches linkage as a comparison, not a definition (Grade 11 workbook, Activity 11): first the Mendelian expectation from the Punnett square, then the linked reality measured against it.
