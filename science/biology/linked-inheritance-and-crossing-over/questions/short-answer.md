# Linked inheritance and crossing over — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: genes on the same chromosome are linked and are inherited together more often than Mendel's independent assortment predicts — a Punnett square for BbMm × bbmm shows the expected ratios, and linkage is what the real offspring do instead. Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 1 applications workbook (Activity 11, printed p. 40) builds a Punnett square for the cross BbMm × bbmm and writes down the expected offspring genotype ratios.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait teaches linkage as a comparison, not a definition (Grade 11 workbook, Activity 11): first the Mendelian expectation from the Punnett square, then the linked reality measured against it.**
