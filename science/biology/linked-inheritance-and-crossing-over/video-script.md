Today: Linked inheritance and crossing over.

Remember this one thing: genes on the same chromosome are linked and are inherited together more often than Mendel's independent assortment predicts — a Punnett square for BbMm × bbmm shows the expected ratios, and linkage is what the real offspring do instead.

Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 1 applications workbook (Activity 11, printed p. 40) builds a Punnett square for the cross BbMm × bbmm and writes down the expected offspring genotype ratios.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
