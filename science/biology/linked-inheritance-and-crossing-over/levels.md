# Linked inheritance and crossing over — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (genes on the same chromosome are linked and are inherited together more often than Mendel's independent assortment predicts — a Punnett square for BbMm × bbmm shows the expected ratios, and linkage is what the real offspring do instead) and explain each key fact in their own words.
