# Hands-on — Insect moulting hormones

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Take the lesson's key facts for a walk: find one example of this lesson's idea in your own surroundings — your home, street, market or school — and say which key fact it matches, and why.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — a caterpillar drawn at a crossroads with two hormone taps above it — one labelled juvenile hormone, flowing from two small round corpora allata behind the brain, keeping the road sign pointing to 'larva' — and one labelled ecdysone, flowing from the prothoracic glands, opening the gate to 'pupa' and then 'adult', the butterfly at the road's end — label its parts, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
