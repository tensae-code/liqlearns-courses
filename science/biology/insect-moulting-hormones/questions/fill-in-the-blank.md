# Insect moulting hormones — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The moulting hormone is called _____.**
**Answer: ecdysone**

**2. Juvenile hormone flows from the corpora _____.**
**Answer: allata**

**3. Ecdysone flows from the _____ glands.**
**Answer: prothoracic**

**4. High juvenile hormone means the moult yields another _____.**
**Answer: larva**
