# Insect moulting hormones — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining how the same moulting hormone can produce first a bigger larva and later a pupa.
**Model answer: Ecdysone only gives the order to moult; it does not choose the result. The choice is made by juvenile hormone flowing alongside it — while juvenile hormone is present, the moult's product is another larva, and when it is withdrawn, the same ecdysone signal yields a pupa. One trigger, two outcomes, decided by the second hormone.**
