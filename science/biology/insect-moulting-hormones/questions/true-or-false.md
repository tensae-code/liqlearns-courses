# Insect moulting hormones — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Ecdysone is the moulting hormone.**
**Answer: True** — from the prothoracic glands.

**2. Juvenile hormone triggers the moult itself.**
**Answer: False** — ecdysone triggers the moult; juvenile hormone decides what the moult produces.

**3. The corpora allata produce juvenile hormone.**
**Answer: True** — the 'staying young' gland.

**4. With juvenile hormone withdrawn, a moult can produce a pupa.**
**Answer: True** — that withdrawal is the switch to metamorphosis.
