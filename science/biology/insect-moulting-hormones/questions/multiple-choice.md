# Insect moulting hormones — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Ecdysone is produced by the…**
a) corpora allata  b) prothoracic glands  c) Malpighian tubules  d) spiracles
**Answer: b) prothoracic glands**

**2. Juvenile hormone comes from the…**
a) prothoracic glands  b) corpora allata  c) gut  d) antennae
**Answer: b) corpora allata**

**3. While juvenile hormone is high, a moult produces…**
a) an adult  b) a pupa  c) another larva  d) an egg
**Answer: c) another larva**

**4. The adult stage is reached when…**
a) ecdysone stops entirely  b) juvenile hormone is withdrawn and ecdysone drives the final moults  c) the insect stops feeding  d) the corpora allata grow larger
**Answer: b) juvenile hormone is withdrawn and ecdysone drives the final moults**
