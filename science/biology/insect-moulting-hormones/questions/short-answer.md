# Insect moulting hormones — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the source and job of each insect moulting hormone.
**Answer: Ecdysone — from the prothoracic glands — triggers moulting. Juvenile hormone — from the corpora allata — holds the insect in the larval state at each moult while it lasts (Ch. 4, printed p. 152).**

**2.** Explain Kenya's way of teaching insect metamorphosis hormones.
**Answer: Kenya closes the growth chapter with the endocrine switchboard: two glands and two hormones named with their sources, and the whole drama of metamorphosis reduced to their balance — juvenile hormone high, larva; juvenile hormone gone, pupa and adult under ecdysone's drive.**
