# Hands-on — Traditional healing and medicinal plants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Ask an older person at home or in your village about one plant used as a medicine where you live. Write down the plant's name, what it is used for, and how it is prepared — the way Samoa's book names its plants and their jobs.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the taulasea's garden with the named plants and the sickness each one treats written beside it — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
