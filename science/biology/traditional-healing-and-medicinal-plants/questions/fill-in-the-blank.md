# Traditional healing and medicinal plants — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The local healer in Samoa is the _____.**
**Answer: taulasea**

**2. _____ (Psychotria insularum) is the most-used medicinal plant in Samoa.**
**Answer: Matalafi**

**3. The taulasea's hands-on treatment of massage and manipulation is called _____.**
**Answer: fofo**

**4. An infusion of _____ bark is used for mouth infections and coughs.**
**Answer: 'ava'avaaitu sosolo**
