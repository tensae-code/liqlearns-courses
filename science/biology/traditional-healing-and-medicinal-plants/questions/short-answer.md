# Traditional healing and medicinal plants — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: health as balance in Samoa's teaching.
**Answer: Samoa's Year 12 book teaches that health is a balance between natural, social and supernatural elements of life. Sickness is a break in that balance, and healing restores it — so treatment addresses more than the germ or the sore place alone.**

**2.** Explain: Samoa's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Samoa's Year 12 biology (MESC, 2004) gives traditional medicine a full section of its first unit — Unit 1: Use of Plants as Medicines (printed pp. 47–57) — in the same unit as micro-organisms, diseases, antiseptics, antibiotics and vaccination. It names the taulasea, the fofo treatment, and each plant by its Samoan and scientific name with what it treats.**
