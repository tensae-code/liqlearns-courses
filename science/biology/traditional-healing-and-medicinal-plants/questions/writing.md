# Traditional healing and medicinal plants — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining Samoa's idea of health as balance to a younger student. Use one named plant and what it treats.
**Model answer: In Samoa, health means balance — between the natural, the social and the supernatural parts of life — and sickness is a break in that balance. The taulasea, the local healer, restores it with treatments like fofo massage and with plant medicines, for example Matalafi (Psychotria insularum), the most-used medicinal plant.**
