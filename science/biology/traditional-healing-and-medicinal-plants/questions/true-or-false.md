# Traditional healing and medicinal plants — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. In Samoa's teaching, sickness is a break in the balance between natural, social and supernatural elements.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Fofo is a plant infusion drunk for coughs.**
**Answer: False** — fofo is massage and manipulation, a treatment of the taulasea's hands; the bark infusion in the lesson is 'ava'avaaitu sosolo, for mouth infections and coughs.

**3. 'ava'avaaitu (Macropiper puberulum) is used for “ghost sickness”.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. Samoa's Year 12 book teaches traditional plant medicine in a separate unit far from antiseptics, antibiotics and vaccination.**
**Answer: False** — it teaches them in the same first unit, side by side.
