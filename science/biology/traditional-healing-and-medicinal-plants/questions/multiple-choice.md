# Traditional healing and medicinal plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. In Samoa's Year 12 biology, what is health?**
a) The absence of germs only  b) A balance between natural, social and supernatural elements  c) A balance of chemicals in the blood only  d) Rest after work
**Answer: b) A balance between natural, social and supernatural elements**

**2. Who is the taulasea?**
a) A clinic doctor  b) A plant used for coughs  c) The local healer  d) A kind of massage oil
**Answer: c) The local healer**

**3. Which plant does the book name as Samoa's most-used medicinal plant?**
a) Moso'i (Cananga odorata)  b) Ginger  c) 'ava'avaaitu (Macropiper puberulum)  d) Matalafi (Psychotria insularum)
**Answer: d) Matalafi (Psychotria insularum)**

**4. What is Moso'i (Cananga odorata) used for?**
a) Constipation, stomachache and postpartum sickness  b) “Ghost sickness”  c) Mouth infections only  d) Broken bones
**Answer: a) Constipation, stomachache and postpartum sickness**
