# Video script — Teeth — four kinds, one structure

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: Teeth — four kinds, one structure.

Remember this one thing: incisors, canines, premolars and molars each do a shaped job — and every tooth has crown, enamel, dentine, pulp and root

Four kinds: The teeth are incisors, canines, premolars and molars.

Shaped for the job: Each tooth kind is shaped for its own job in eating.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
