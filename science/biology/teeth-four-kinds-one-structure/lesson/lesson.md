# Teeth — four kinds, one structure

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Teeth — four kinds, one structure. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Digestion's first mechanical work is done by the teeth. Four kinds, each shaped for its job, all built to one structure.

[Picture 22248 — a jaw with the four tooth kinds labelled, beside a cut-through tooth]

## The key facts
**Four kinds.** The teeth are incisors, canines, premolars and molars.

**Shaped for the job.** Each tooth kind is shaped for its own job in eating.

**Tooth structure.** A tooth has a crown, enamel, dentine, pulp and a root.

**First work.** Teeth do the first mechanical work of digestion.
