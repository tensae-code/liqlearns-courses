# Teeth — four kinds, one structure — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about four kinds?
a) The teeth are incisors, canines, premolars and molars  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The teeth are incisors, canines, premolars and molars**

**2.** What does this lesson say about shaped for the job?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Each tooth kind is shaped for its own job in eating  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Each tooth kind is shaped for its own job in eating**

**3.** What does this lesson say about tooth structure?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) A tooth has a crown, enamel, dentine, pulp and a root  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) A tooth has a crown, enamel, dentine, pulp and a root**

**4.** What does this lesson say about first work?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Teeth do the first mechanical work of digestion
**Answer: d) Teeth do the first mechanical work of digestion**
