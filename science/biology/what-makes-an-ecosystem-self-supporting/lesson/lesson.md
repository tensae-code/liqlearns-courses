## The key facts
**Definition.** An ecosystem is a self-supporting unit of community plus environment with materials recycled through it.

**Lake.** In a lake, sunlight, water and minerals grow plants that support animal life.

**Carp.** A carp is a top secondary consumer in the lake, competing for food and mates.

**Two kinds.** Terrestrial and aquatic ecosystems are compared.
