# Bilateral symmetry and the coelom — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (bilateral symmetry — a body with two matching sides and a front end — is an evolutionary advance tied to active movement, and bilateral animals are graded by the body cavity they carry: acoelomate (no coelom), pseudocoelomate (a false cavity) or coelomate (a true, lined coelom)) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
