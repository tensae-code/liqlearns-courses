Today: Bilateral symmetry and the coelom.

Remember this one thing: bilateral symmetry — a body with two matching sides and a front end — is an evolutionary advance tied to active movement, and bilateral animals are graded by the body cavity they carry: acoelomate (no coelom), pseudocoelomate (a false cavity) or coelomate (a true, lined coelom).

Cuba's Grade 8 programme (Unit 4) teaches bilateral symmetry as an adaptive and evolutionary advantage, together with the coelom and the pseudocoelom (printed p. 17, teacher programme — evidence)

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
