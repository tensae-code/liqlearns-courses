# Bilateral symmetry and the coelom — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Cuba's Grade 8 programme (Unit 4) teaches bilateral symmetry as an adaptive and evolutionary advantage, together with the coelom and the pseudocoelom (printed p. 17, teacher programme — evidence)**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Cuba's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Programa Biología 2 — Los animales y su conservación (octavo grado)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with bilateral symmetry and the coelom.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is bilateral symmetry and the coelom.
