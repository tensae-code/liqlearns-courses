# Bilateral symmetry and the coelom — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: Bilateral symmetry — a body with two matching sides and a front end — is an evolutionary advance tied to active movement, and bilateral animals are graded by the body cavity they carry: acoelomate (no coelom), pseudocoelomate (a false cavity) or coelomate (a true, lined coelom). Cuba's Grade 8 programme (Unit 4) teaches bilateral symmetry as an adaptive and evolutionary advantage, together with the coelom and the pseudocoelom (printed p. 17, teacher programme — evidence)**
