# Signs of a chemical reaction — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) a chemical reaction shows itself by observable signs — a colour change, a taste change, fizzing or a new substance forming — and naming the evidence is how you know a reaction, not just a mixture, has happened  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
