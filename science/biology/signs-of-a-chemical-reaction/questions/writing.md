# Signs of a chemical reaction — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: a chemical reaction shows itself by observable signs — a colour change, a taste change, fizzing or a new substance forming — and naming the evidence is how you know a reaction, not just a mixture, has happened. Activity 20 (printed p. 52) sets four home mixtures — baking-powder solution, concentrated vinegar, and the two combined two ways — and pupils predict in which mixture a chemical reaction occurs, and what happens to the components that do and do not react.**
