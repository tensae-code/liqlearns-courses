# Signs of a chemical reaction — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's Grade 10 applications workbook (Activity 15, printed p. 42) squeezes lemon quarters into a cup of dark tea step by step and records the colour and taste changes; the sour taste is citric acid, a chemical — and the changes are the evidence that a chemical reaction happened (a "link with chemistry" box).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كراسة التطبيقات — الصف العاشر — الجزء الأول (Biology — Applications Workbook, Grade 10, Term 1), printed p. 42).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with signs of a chemical reaction.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is signs of a chemical reaction.
