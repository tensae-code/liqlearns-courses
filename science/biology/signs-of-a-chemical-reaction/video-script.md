Today: Signs of a chemical reaction.

Remember this one thing: a chemical reaction shows itself by observable signs — a colour change, a taste change, fizzing or a new substance forming — and naming the evidence is how you know a reaction, not just a mixture, has happened.

Kuwait's Grade 10 applications workbook (Activity 15, printed p. 42) squeezes lemon quarters into a cup of dark tea step by step and records the colour and taste changes; the sour taste is citric acid, a chemical — and the changes are the evidence that a chemical reaction happened (a "link with chemistry" box).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
