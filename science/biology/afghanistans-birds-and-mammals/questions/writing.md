**Model answer: Afghanistan's own birds and mammals are its two warm-blooded vertebrate groups: the birds with feathers and wings, and the mammals with hair that feed their young on milk. Birds are warm-blooded vertebrates with feathers, wings and a beak, and they reproduce by laying hard-shelled eggs. Afghanistan's birds range from raptors of the high mountains to water birds of its rivers and lakes and the familiar birds of farm and garden. Mammals are warm-blooded vertebrates with hair or fur whose mothers feed the young on milk from mammary glands.**
