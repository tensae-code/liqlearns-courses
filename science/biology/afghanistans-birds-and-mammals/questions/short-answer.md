# Afghanistan's birds and mammals — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: mammals — the marks.
**Answer: Hair and milk are the mammal marks. The young are born alive (in nearly all mammals), are suckled on the mother's milk, breathe air with lungs, and keep a constant warm body temperature.**

**2.** Explain: wild mammals of afghanistan.
**Answer: The Hindu Kush and the Pamirs hold wild sheep and goats — the markhor with its corkscrew horns, Afghanistan's national animal, and the ibex — hunted in turn by the snow leopard. Foxes, wolves, hares and marmots range the same slopes and plains.**
