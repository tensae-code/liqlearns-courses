# Afghanistan's birds and mammals — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Birds are warm-blooded vertebrates with feathers, wings and a beak, and they reproduce by laying hard-shelled eggs.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Birds feed their young on milk..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.

**3.** Afghanistan's birds range from raptors of the high mountains to water birds of its rivers and lakes and the familiar birds of farm and garden.
**Answer: True**

**4.** The snow leopard is a bird of the high mountains..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.
