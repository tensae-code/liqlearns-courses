# Hands-on — Afghanistan's birds and mammals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Make two lists from what you can see or reliably hear about near home: birds and mammals. Beside each, write the mark that proves its group — feathers and beak, or hair and milk-fed young.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of an Afghan mountain scene labelled — a markhor on a crag, a snow leopard watching from above, and an eagle circling over both — each labelled bird or mammal with its group mark — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
