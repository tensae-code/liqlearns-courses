# Afghanistan's birds and mammals — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Afghanistan's own birds and mammals are its two warm-blooded vertebrate groups: the birds with feathers and wings, and the mammals with hair that feed their young on milk.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
