# Axial and appendicular skeletons

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — axial and appendicular skeletons. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

The skeleton's two divisions are tabled bone by bone (skull 22, ear ossicles 6, hyoid 1, rib cage and the rest of the axial set; the appendicular limbs and girdles), and bones' jobs are listed: support and posture, protection (skull for brain, ribs for heart and lungs), blood-cell production, and storing and releasing fat and minerals such as calcium.
