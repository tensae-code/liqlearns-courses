# Proteins — chains of amino acids

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Proteins — chains of amino acids. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
A protein is a chain, and its beads are amino acids. Each join — a peptide bond — is made by losing water, the same joining trick as the sugars.

[Picture 22238 — two amino acids joining with a water molecule leaving]

## The key facts
**Amino acids.** Proteins are chains of amino acids.

**Peptide bonds.** Amino acids are joined by peptide bonds.

**Water lost.** Peptide bonds form with the loss of water.

**Local foods.** Local protein foods, animal and plant, are collected from pupils' own localities.
