# Proteins — chains of amino acids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about amino acids?
a) Proteins are chains of amino acids  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Proteins are chains of amino acids**

**2.** What does this lesson say about peptide bonds?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Amino acids are joined by peptide bonds  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Amino acids are joined by peptide bonds**

**3.** What does this lesson say about water lost?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Peptide bonds form with the loss of water  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Peptide bonds form with the loss of water**
