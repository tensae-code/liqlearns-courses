# Video script — Barrier contraceptives

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Contraception prevents pregnancy for family planning, health and population reasons; barrier methods physically block sperm from egg — male and female condoms (the condom also protecting against STIs) and the diaphragm, a silicone dome placed over the cervix.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
