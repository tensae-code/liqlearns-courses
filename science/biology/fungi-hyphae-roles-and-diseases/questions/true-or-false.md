Say true or false.

**1. True fungi are characterised (eukaryotic, mostly filamentous hyphae branching into a mycelium with apical growth, some unicellular yeasts, rigid-walled protoplasm); hyphae grow from a germ tube out of a germinating spore; ecological roles run saprotrophic biodegradation, pathogens and mutualists (the guide attributes pathogenicity, circularly, to "the virulence gene"); fermentation and spoilage species are named (Saccharomyces cerevisiae for fermentation; Rhizopus, Penicillium and Aspergillus for spoilage, misprints noted); five-plus human fungal diseases are listed (candidiasis, cryptococcosis, aspergillosis, coccidioidomycosis, histoplasmosis, blastomycosis) plus plant diseases; and probiotics are defined as live beneficial bacteria/yeasts normally resident in the body.**
