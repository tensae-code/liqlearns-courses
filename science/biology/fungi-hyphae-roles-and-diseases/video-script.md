# Video script — Fungi — hyphae, roles and diseases

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

True fungi are characterised (eukaryotic, mostly filamentous hyphae branching into a mycelium with apical growth, some unicellular yeasts, rigid-walled protoplasm); hyphae grow from a germ tube out of a germinating spore; ecological roles run saprotrophic biodegradation, pathogens and mutualists (the guide attributes pathogenicity, circularly, to "the virulence gene"); fermentation and spoilage species are named (Saccharomyces cerevisiae for fermentation; Rhizopus, Penicillium and Aspergillus for spoilage, misprints noted); five-plus human fungal diseases are listed (candidiasis, cryptococcosis, aspergillosis, coccidioidomycosis, histoplasmosis, blastomycosis) plus plant diseases; and probiotics are defined as live beneficial bacteria/yeasts normally resident in the body.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
