# The Rh factor — why mothers and babies must match

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — the rh factor. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

The Rh (Rhesus D) system is inherited independently of ABO: Rh-positive people carry the D antigen, Rh-negative people do not; the danger case is an Rh-negative mother carrying an Rh-positive baby — her antibodies, raised in a first pregnancy, can attack later Rh-positive babies, a complication preventable with anti-D treatment.
