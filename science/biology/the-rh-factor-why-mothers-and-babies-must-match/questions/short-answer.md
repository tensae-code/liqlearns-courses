# The Rh factor — why mothers and babies must match — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about rh factor and its medical importance?**
**Answer: The Rh (Rhesus D) system is inherited independently of ABO: Rh-positive people carry the D antigen, Rh-negative people do not; the danger case is an Rh-negative mother carrying an Rh-positive baby — her antibodies, raised in a first pregnancy, can attack later Rh-positive babies, a complication preventable with anti-D treatment.**
