# The Rh factor — why mothers and babies must match — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. The Rh (Rhesus D) system is inherited independently of ABO: Rh-positive people carry the D antigen, Rh-negative people do not; the danger case is an Rh-negative mother carrying an Rh-positive baby — her antibodies, raised in a first pregnancy, can attack later Rh-positive babies, a complication preventable with anti-D treatment.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. The danger case is an Rh-negative mother carrying an Rh-positive baby — her antibodies, raised in a first pregnancy, can attack later Rh-positive babies, a complication preventable with anti-D treatment.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
