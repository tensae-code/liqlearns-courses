# Homeostasis and the Kidney — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about homeostasis and the human kidney (§5.3–5.3.1):?**
**Answer: homeostasis defined, with regulation of body temperature, sugar level and water balance as its outcomes; the kidney's structure — bean-shaped, cortex, medulla, renal pelvis, ureter to the bladder — and its filtering work: blood entering by the renal artery, fluid forced into millions of nephrons, needed sugar and ions reabsorbed, urea and excess water released as urine; an outcome sends students to investigate traditional local mechanisms for regulating body homeostasis.**
