# Video script — Homeostasis and the Kidney

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

homeostasis defined, with regulation of body temperature, sugar level and water balance as its outcomes; the kidney's structure — bean-shaped, cortex, medulla, renal pelvis, ureter to the bladder — and its filtering work: blood entering by the renal artery, fluid forced into millions of nephrons, needed sugar and ions reabsorbed, urea and excess water released as urine; an outcome sends students to investigate traditional local mechanisms for regulating body homeostasis.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
