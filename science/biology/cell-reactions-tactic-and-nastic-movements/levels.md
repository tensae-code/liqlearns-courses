# Cell reactions — tactic and nastic movements — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (cells and organisms answer their surroundings by moving: a tactic movement is a whole cell or organism moving towards or away from a stimulus, and nastic movements are the non-directional responses — movement is one of the ways a living cell reacts to its environment) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: applying the lesson to a new example from the learner's own surroundings, and explaining Nigeria's way of teaching it (Ch. 4, printed p. 65), without looking.
