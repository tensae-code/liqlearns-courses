# Cell reactions — tactic and nastic movements — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is a tactic movement?
a) A movement of a whole cell or organism towards or away from a stimulus
b) Growth of a root
c) Division of a cell into two
d) Movement of water across a membrane
**Answer: a) A movement of a whole cell or organism towards or away from a stimulus**

**2.** A cell swimming away from a harmful chemical shows…
a) A positive tactic response
b) A negative tactic response
c) Photosynthesis
d) No reaction
**Answer: b) A negative tactic response**

**3.** What sets the direction of a tactic movement?
a) The cell's age
b) The direction of the stimulus
c) The size of the cell
d) Nothing — it is random
**Answer: b) The direction of the stimulus**

**4.** How do nastic movements differ from tactic movements?
a) They happen only at night
b) Their direction does not depend on the stimulus's direction
c) They happen only in animals
d) They need no stimulus
**Answer: b) Their direction does not depend on the stimulus's direction**
