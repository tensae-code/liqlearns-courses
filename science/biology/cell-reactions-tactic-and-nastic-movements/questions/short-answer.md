# Cell reactions — tactic and nastic movements — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: tactic against nastic movements.
**Answer: A tactic movement is directional: the whole cell or organism moves towards a stimulus or away from it, and the stimulus's direction sets the direction of the move. A nastic movement is a response whose direction does not depend on where the stimulus comes from.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book teaches cell reactions to the environment in Chapter 4 (printed p. 65) through movement — tactic movements towards and away from stimuli at cell level, and the forms of movement in cells and organisms, nastic movements among them.**
