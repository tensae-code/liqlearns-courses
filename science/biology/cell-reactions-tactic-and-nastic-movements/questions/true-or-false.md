# Cell reactions — tactic and nastic movements — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A tactic movement can be towards a stimulus or away from it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In a tactic movement, the direction of movement has nothing to do with the stimulus.
**Answer: False** — The stimulus's direction decides the direction of the tactic movement.

**3.** Movement is one of the ways a living cell reacts to its environment.
**Answer: True**
