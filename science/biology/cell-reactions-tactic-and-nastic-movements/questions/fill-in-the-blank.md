# Cell reactions — tactic and nastic movements — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** Movement of a whole cell or organism towards or away from a stimulus is a _____ movement.
**Answer: tactic**

**2.** A tactic response away from a stimulus is called a _____ tactic response.
**Answer: negative**

**3.** Responses whose direction does not depend on the stimulus's direction are _____ movements.
**Answer: nastic**
