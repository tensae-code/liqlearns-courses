# Enzymes at work in the body — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about what enzymes do in the body?**
**Answer: Enzymes run respiration, digestion, liver, muscle and nerve work; each has a uniquely shaped active site and a turnover number; enzyme levels also mark disease states such as heart attack, jaundice, pancreatitis and cancer; a table of body enzymes and their jobs anchors the lesson (lipases on fats, amylase on starch, maltase, trypsin, lactase, helicase, DNA polymerase, acetylcholinesterase).**
