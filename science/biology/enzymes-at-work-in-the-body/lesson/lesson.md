# Enzymes at work in the body

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — enzymes at work in the body. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Enzymes run respiration, digestion, liver, muscle and nerve work; each has a uniquely shaped active site and a turnover number; enzyme levels also mark disease states such as heart attack, jaundice, pancreatitis and cancer; a table of body enzymes and their jobs anchors the lesson (lipases on fats, amylase on starch, maltase, trypsin, lactase, helicase, DNA polymerase, acetylcholinesterase).
