# Hands-on — Modified leaves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find an onion and peel it: the fleshy scales you pull apart are modified leaves, storing food. Then look at any climbing plant near you and find a tendril — a leaf reshaped into a gripping thread. Say aloud, for each one, what job its shape does.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the five reshaped leaves in a row — tendril, trap, scale, spine, swollen leaf — each labelled with its job — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
