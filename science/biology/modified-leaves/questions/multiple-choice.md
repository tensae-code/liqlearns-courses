# Modified leaves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** An onion's fleshy scales are…
a) Roots that store water
b) Modified leaves that store food
c) Flowers that have not opened
d) Stems underground
**Answer: b) Modified leaves that store food**

**2.** What job does a cactus spine — a modified leaf — do?
a) It makes food by photosynthesis
b) It catches insects
c) It loses almost no water and defends the plant
d) It climbs supports
**Answer: c) It loses almost no water and defends the plant**

**3.** How does Uganda's Senior One book teach modified leaves?
a) Scattered through every chapter
b) In one sorting activity — Activity 5.6 — sorted by the job each leaf does
c) Only in the teacher's guide
d) It does not teach them
**Answer: b) In one sorting activity — Activity 5.6 — sorted by the job each leaf does**
