# Modified leaves — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (a leaf's ordinary flat green shape is only its starting shape: leaves are modified — reshaped — for other jobs: tendrils that climb, traps that catch insects, scales that store food, spines that defend, and swollen leaves that store water — Uganda's Senior One book sorts them by job in Activity 5.6 (printed pp. 81–82)) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by naming the job of further modified leaves from their shape alone, and matching each of Uganda's named examples to its kind, without looking.
