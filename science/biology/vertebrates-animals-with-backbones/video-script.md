# Video script — Vertebrates — animals with backbones

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Animals with a defined internal skeleton and backbone: bilaterally symmetrical, with paired kidneys, in five groups — mammals, birds, fish, reptiles, amphibians — the first two warm-blooded, the rest cold-blooded; all vertebrates are chordates but not all chordates are vertebrates, a puzzle the book leaves pupils to reason out; a comparison activity sets the two great groups side by side across backbone, exoskeleton, symmetry, circulation, nervous system, nutrition and reproduction.

Two consolidation tables the student book asks for but does not fill: a row-by-row invertebrate/vertebrate comparison (backbone and internal skeleton first), and an asexual-vs-sexual table (one parent vs two, no fertilisation vs fertilisation, a few animals such as sponges vs most animals, identical vs different offspring, short vs long time).

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
