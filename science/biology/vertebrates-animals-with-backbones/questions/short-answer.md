# Vertebrates — animals with backbones — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about vertebrates?**
**Answer: Animals with a defined internal skeleton and backbone: bilaterally symmetrical, with paired kidneys, in five groups — mammals, birds, fish, reptiles, amphibians — the first two warm-blooded, the rest cold-blooded; all vertebrates are chordates but not all chordates are vertebrates, a puzzle the book leaves pupils to reason out; a comparison activity sets the two great groups side by side across backbone, exoskeleton, symmetry, circulation, nervous system, nutrition and reproduction.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added worked tables?**
**Answer: Two consolidation tables the student book asks for but does not fill: a row-by-row invertebrate/vertebrate comparison (backbone and internal skeleton first), and an asexual-vs-sexual table (one parent vs two, no fertilisation vs fertilisation, a few animals such as sponges vs most animals, identical vs different offspring, short vs long time).**
