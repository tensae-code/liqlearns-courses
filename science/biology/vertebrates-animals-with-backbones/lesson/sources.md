Verdict: COMPLETE (all 5 pages read; p. 5 is the pictured answer key for the pupil sheet). Structural summary: Teacher guide (same authors, 2020) for a lesson built around a teaching video in which a perch (ahven) is dissected in stages, in pairs. Covers lesson staging, equipment (dissecting scissors, forceps, board, gloves), safety (fish allergy, sharp tools), and the science content in teacher notes: fins and their jobs, scales and mucus, ageing a fish from scale growth rings, the lateral-line sense, gill breathing, fish senses (sight, inner ear, smell via nostril tunnels, taste), sexing by roe/milt sacs (a female perch may carry 4,000–300,000 eggs), and swim-bladder depth control. Finland's way: the research question (“how does the fish's structure show it is adapted to water?”) is posed BEFORE the dissection and answered after it, with self-assessment built in. Grade: grade-07 (guide states grades 7–9) — Kind: teacher guide (NOT a textbook) as verified — Repo path: grade-07/gov/finland/biology/teacher/finland-g07-aoe1063-kalan-preparointi-opettajan-ohje.pdf — Byte size: 1,355,171 (matches record) — PDF pages: 7 — Printed-page offset: 0. Structural summary: The grades 7–9 version of the perch-dissection lesson, deeper than the primary guide. Adds: countercurrent gas exchange in the gills (water and blood flowing in opposite directions), the two-chambered fish heart pumping oxygen-poor blood to the gills, stomach-content investigation (perch are predators — smaller fish may be found inside), and a dedicated eye dissection — the fish lens is round and pearl-like, bends light more strongly than the human lens, and focuses by moving in and out rather than changing shape. Model answers give the exact incision sequence (belly cut from vent to lower jaw, flap cut toward the lateral line, gill cover removed) and organ-finding order (heart under the gills, liver, stomach, intestine to the vent, gonad, swim bladder against the spine).
