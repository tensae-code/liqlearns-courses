# Leaf and root — the outside view

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Leaf and root — the outside view. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Look at a leaf and a root from the outside. The leaf is built to catch light and gases; the root is built to anchor the plant and take in water.

[Picture 22206 — a leaf and a root system with parts labelled]

## The key facts
**Leaf parts.** The leaf's parts are the blade, the veins and the stalk.

**The blade.** The blade is the leaf's broad feeding surface.

**Root forms.** Roots come in different forms, all anchoring the plant.

**Two surfaces.** Leaf and root are the plant's feeding and anchoring surfaces.
