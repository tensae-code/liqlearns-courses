Today: Leaf and root — the outside view.

Remember this one thing: the leaf's parts (blade, veins, stalk) and the root's forms are the plant's feeding and anchoring surfaces

Leaf parts: The leaf's parts are the blade, the veins and the stalk.

The blade: The blade is the leaf's broad feeding surface.
