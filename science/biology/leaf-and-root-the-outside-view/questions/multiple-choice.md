# Leaf and root — the outside view — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about leaf parts?
a) The leaf's parts are the blade, the veins and the stalk  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The leaf's parts are the blade, the veins and the stalk**

**2.** What does this lesson say about the blade?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) The blade is the leaf's broad feeding surface  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The blade is the leaf's broad feeding surface**

**3.** What does this lesson say about root forms?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Roots come in different forms, all anchoring the plant  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Roots come in different forms, all anchoring the plant**

**4.** What does this lesson say about two surfaces?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Leaf and root are the plant's feeding and anchoring surfaces
**Answer: d) Leaf and root are the plant's feeding and anchoring surfaces**
