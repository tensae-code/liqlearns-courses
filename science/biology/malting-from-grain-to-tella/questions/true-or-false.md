# Malting — from grain to tella — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Malting is controlled germination: steeping grain in water and air (24–48 hours), germinating, then kilning/drying, waking the grain's amylases and proteases to convert starch into maltose and other sugars; barley is the main malt grain for its starch-to-protein ratio and husk; in Ethiopia malting underlies tella and porridge and the local drinks booka, cheka, keribo, korefe, shameta, borde and teji made for holidays and weddings; the unit closes with the work of biochemists and renowned Ethiopian biochemists.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
