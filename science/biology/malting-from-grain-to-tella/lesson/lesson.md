# Malting — from grain to tella

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — malting. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Malting is controlled germination: steeping grain in water and air (24–48 hours), germinating, then kilning/drying, waking the grain's amylases and proteases to convert starch into maltose and other sugars; barley is the main malt grain for its starch-to-protein ratio and husk; in Ethiopia malting underlies tella and porridge and the local drinks booka, cheka, keribo, korefe, shameta, borde and teji made for holidays and weddings; the unit closes with the work of biochemists and renowned Ethiopian biochemists.
