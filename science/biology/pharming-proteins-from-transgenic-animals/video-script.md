Today: Pharming — proteins from transgenic animals.

Remember this one thing: pharming makes transgenic animals secrete human pharmaceutical proteins in their milk — a medicine factory on four legs — so the protein is harvested by milking, purified, and used to treat people.

pharmaceutical proteins secreted in the milk of transgenic animals

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
