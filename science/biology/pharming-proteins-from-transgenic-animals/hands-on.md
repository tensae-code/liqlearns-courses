# Hands-on — Pharming — proteins from transgenic animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: pharming makes transgenic animals secrete human pharmaceutical proteins in their milk — a medicine factory on four legs — so the protein is harvested by milking, purified, and used to treat people.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a goat drawn with a labelled udder and a churn beside it holding a medicine bottle, the chain from gene to milk to medicine marked in three steps. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
