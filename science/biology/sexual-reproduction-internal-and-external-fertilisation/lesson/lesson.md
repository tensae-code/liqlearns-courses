## Sub lesson 1 — Sexual reproduction, fertilisation and early development

Two-parent reproduction producing a diploid zygote; fertilisation is internal (egg fertilised inside the female's tubes) or external (eggs and sperm released into water); after fertilisation the embryo passes through cleavage and the early developmental stages shown in the book's sequence figures.

## Sub lesson 2 — Added development stages — blastula, cleavage, gastrula and germ layers

The guide names and defines the embryonic stages behind the student book's figures: cleavage as rapid repeated division of the zygote, the blastula it produces, and the gastrula with its three germ layers — ectoderm, mesoderm and endoderm — from which tissues, organs and organ systems develop.
