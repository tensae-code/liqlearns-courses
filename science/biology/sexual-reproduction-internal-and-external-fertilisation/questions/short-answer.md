# Sexual reproduction — internal and external fertilisation — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about sexual reproduction, fertilisation and early development?**
**Answer: Two-parent reproduction producing a diploid zygote; fertilisation is internal (egg fertilised inside the female's tubes) or external (eggs and sperm released into water); after fertilisation the embryo passes through cleavage and the early developmental stages shown in the book's sequence figures.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added development stages?**
**Answer: The guide names and defines the embryonic stages behind the student book's figures: cleavage as rapid repeated division of the zygote, the blastula it produces, and the gastrula with its three germ layers — ectoderm, mesoderm and endoderm — from which tissues, organs and organ systems develop.**
