# Sexual reproduction — internal and external fertilisation — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Two-parent reproduction producing a diploid zygote; fertilisation is internal (egg fertilised inside the female's tubes) or external (eggs and sperm released into water); after fertilisation the embryo passes through cleavage and the early developmental stages shown in the book's sequence figures.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The guide names and defines the embryonic stages behind the student book's figures: cleavage as rapid repeated division of the zygote, the blastula it produces, and the gastrula with its three germ layers — ectoderm, mesoderm and endoderm — from which tissues, organs and organ systems develop.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
