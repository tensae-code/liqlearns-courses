# Conserving Ethiopia's wildlife — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about conservation in ethiopia?**
**Answer: Conservation is the care, protection and wise use of resources for future generations; Ethiopia's wildlife is being lost to natural disasters and to farming expansion, deforestation, poaching, illegal trade, habitat change, climate change, overexploitation and pollution; the response is a protected-area network whose national parks, wildlife sanctuaries (Babile elephants, Senkele Swayne's hartebeest), reserves and community conservation areas (Abune Yosef, Guassa Menz, Tama) are tabled with region, year and area.**
