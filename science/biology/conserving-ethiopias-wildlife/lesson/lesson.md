# Conserving Ethiopia's wildlife

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — conserving ethiopia's wildlife. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Conservation is the care, protection and wise use of resources for future generations; Ethiopia's wildlife is being lost to natural disasters and to farming expansion, deforestation, poaching, illegal trade, habitat change, climate change, overexploitation and pollution; the response is a protected-area network whose national parks, wildlife sanctuaries (Babile elephants, Senkele Swayne's hartebeest), reserves and community conservation areas (Abune Yosef, Guassa Menz, Tama) are tabled with region, year and area.
