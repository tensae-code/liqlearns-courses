# The DNA double helix — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about dna structure?**
**Answer: A double helix of two polynucleotide strands: each nucleotide is sugar (deoxyribose), phosphate and one of four bases — purines adenine and guanine, pyrimidines cytosine and thymine — pairing A–T and C–G across hydrogen-bonded rungs, sugar-phosphate as the rails, strands running opposite (5′ to 3′) directions; Watson and Crick are credited; genes are the instruction-bearing sections, each at its own locus on a chromosome of DNA wound on histone protein — humans carry 46 chromosomes in 23 pairs.**
