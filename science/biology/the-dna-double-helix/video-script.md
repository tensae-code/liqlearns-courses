# Video script — The DNA double helix

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A double helix of two polynucleotide strands: each nucleotide is sugar (deoxyribose), phosphate and one of four bases — purines adenine and guanine, pyrimidines cytosine and thymine — pairing A–T and C–G across hydrogen-bonded rungs, sugar-phosphate as the rails, strands running opposite (5′ to 3′) directions; Watson and Crick are credited; genes are the instruction-bearing sections, each at its own locus on a chromosome of DNA wound on histone protein — humans carry 46 chromosomes in 23 pairs.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
