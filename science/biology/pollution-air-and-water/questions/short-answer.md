# Pollution — air and water — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about human impacts?**
**Answer: Human activity's environmental damage is listed (pesticides, incineration, sewage, construction, urban growth, mining and more) and pollution is worked in detail: air pollution and its main pollutants (carbon monoxide, nitrogen dioxide, ozone, lead, sulfur dioxide, smog, particles) from transport, industry and fuel burning, and water pollution including eutrophication and the water-hyacinth (emboch) invasion of lakes.**
