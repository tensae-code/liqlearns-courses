# Pollution — air and water

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — pollution. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Human activity's environmental damage is listed (pesticides, incineration, sewage, construction, urban growth, mining and more) and pollution is worked in detail: air pollution and its main pollutants (carbon monoxide, nitrogen dioxide, ozone, lead, sulfur dioxide, smog, particles) from transport, industry and fuel burning, and water pollution including eutrophication and the water-hyacinth (emboch) invasion of lakes.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: what pollution means, the main ideas of this lesson, and one example from your own surroundings.
