Today: Antibiotics and antibiotic resistance.

Remember this one thing: antibiotics kill bacteria — Fleming's discovery of penicillin in 1928 opened the antibiotic age — but bacteria evolve resistance, so an antibiotic that once cured can fail, as with MRSA.

Fleming's discovery of penicillin (1928) and bacterial resistance to antibiotics (incl. MRSA in the glossary) as its own teaching block

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
