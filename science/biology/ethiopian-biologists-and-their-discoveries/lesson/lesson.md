# Ethiopian biologists and their discoveries

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Ethiopian biologists and their discoveries. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
The unit closes with Ethiopia's own scientists. Each one is tied to a real problem in Ethiopia, and their work shows biology solving problems at home.

[Picture 22203 — portraits of Ethiopian biologists with the Ethiopian problem each one worked on]

## The key facts
**Yalemtsehay Mekonnen.** Yalemtsehay Mekonnen is one of the renowned Ethiopian biologists named in this lesson.

**Aklilu Lemma.** Aklilu Lemma is remembered for the endod (soapberry) work against schistosomiasis.

**Gebissa Ejeta.** Gebissa Ejeta is named among Ethiopia's renowned biologists, tied to the problem his work solved.

**The pattern.** Each Ethiopian biologist here is tied to the problem their work solved for Ethiopia.
