# Video script — Ethiopian biologists and their discoveries

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: Ethiopian biologists and their discoveries.

Remember this one thing: Ethiopia's own scientists — Yalemtsehay Mekonnen, Aklilu Lemma and Gebissa Ejeta — each tied to the problem their work solved for Ethiopia

Yalemtsehay mekonnen: Yalemtsehay Mekonnen is one of the renowned Ethiopian biologists named in this lesson.

Aklilu lemma: Aklilu Lemma is remembered for the endod (soapberry) work against schistosomiasis.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
