# Video script — Inhibition — competitive, noncompetitive, uncompetitive

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Inhibitors block enzyme activity: irreversible inhibitors permanently, reversible inhibitors by breakable binding — competitive (fighting the substrate for the active site), noncompetitive and uncompetitive (binding elsewhere or to the enzyme–substrate complex and lowering its efficacy); feedback inhibition, where a pathway's own product shuts its enzyme, and a drug that blocks a viral enzyme in HIV treatment are the worked examples.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
