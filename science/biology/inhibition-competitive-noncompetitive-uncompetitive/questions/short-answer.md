# Inhibition — competitive, noncompetitive, uncompetitive — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about enzyme inhibition?**
**Answer: Inhibitors block enzyme activity: irreversible inhibitors permanently, reversible inhibitors by breakable binding — competitive (fighting the substrate for the active site), noncompetitive and uncompetitive (binding elsewhere or to the enzyme–substrate complex and lowering its efficacy); feedback inhibition, where a pathway's own product shuts its enzyme, and a drug that blocks a viral enzyme in HIV treatment are the worked examples.**
