# Inhibition — competitive, noncompetitive, uncompetitive — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. ** _____ enzyme activity: irreversible inhibitors permanently, reversible inhibitors by breakable binding — competitive (fighting the substrate for the active site), noncompetitive and uncompetitive (binding elsewhere or to the enzyme–substrate complex and lowering its efficacy); feedback inhibition, where a pathway's own product shuts its enzyme, and a drug that blocks a viral enzyme in HIV treatment are the worked examples.
**Answer: Inhibitors block**
