# Inhibition — competitive, noncompetitive, uncompetitive — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Inhibitors block enzyme activity: irreversible inhibitors permanently, reversible inhibitors by breakable binding — competitive (fighting the substrate for the active site), noncompetitive and uncompetitive (binding elsewhere or to the enzyme–substrate complex and lowering its efficacy); feedback inhibition, where a pathway's own product shuts its enzyme, and a drug that blocks a viral enzyme in HIV treatment are the worked examples.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
