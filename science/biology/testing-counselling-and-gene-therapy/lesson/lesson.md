# Testing, counselling and gene therapy

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — testing, counselling and gene therapy. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Testing examines genetic material to identify a disorder; counsellors explain inheritance patterns, interpret risk and suggest testing, mindful of the psychological and social weight; gene therapy treats by introducing genes — replacing or inactivating a faulty gene or adding a new one — into somatic cells (effects not inherited) or germ-line cells (effects inherited), delivered by viral or bacterial vectors, gene editing, or the patient's own modified cells, with delivery, immune reaction and side-effect challenges stated honestly.
