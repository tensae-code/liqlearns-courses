# Breaking free — the steps of recovery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about admit?
a) The first step of recovery is to admit the problem to yourself and friends
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The first step of recovery is to admit the problem to yourself and friends**

**2.** What does this lesson say about support?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Recovery needs support from others
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Recovery needs support from others**

**3.** What does this lesson say about routine?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Recovery means rewriting the daily routine and enjoying the small successes
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Recovery means rewriting the daily routine and enjoying the small successes**

**4.** What does this lesson say about relapse?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Recovery means recognising and avoiding relapse, reaching freedom and staying there
**Answer: d) Recovery means recognising and avoiding relapse, reaching freedom and staying there**
