Today: Breaking free — the steps of recovery.

Remember this one thing: recovery from addiction follows steps: admit, find support, rewrite the routine, avoid relapse, stay free.

The first step of recovery is to admit the problem to yourself and friends.

Recovery needs support from others.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
