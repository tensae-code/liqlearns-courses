# Breaking free — the steps of recovery

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Breaking free — the steps of recovery. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Recovery is a path anyone can follow, one honest step at a time.

[Picture 21065 — a stepped path from admission to freedom]

## The key facts
**Admit.** The first step of recovery is to admit the problem to yourself and friends.

**Support.** Recovery needs support from others.

**Routine.** Recovery means rewriting the daily routine and enjoying the small successes.

**Relapse.** Recovery means recognising and avoiding relapse, reaching freedom and staying there.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words: recovery from addiction follows steps: admit, find support, rewrite the routine, avoid relapse, stay free. Use each key fact once. Then let your companion explain it back to you.
