# Video script — Renewable and non-renewable resources

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Resources are anything nature provides that people use — food, fuel, materials, air, sunlight, soil, water, animals and plants; renewable resources replace themselves (sunlight, wind, water, biomass, hydropower) yet can still be depleted by overuse or poor management; non-renewable resources (fossil fuels, metals and similar stocks) form too slowly to be replaced once used.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
