# Renewable and non-renewable resources — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Resources are anything nature provides that people use — food, fuel, materials, air, sunlight, soil, water, animals and plants; renewable resources replace themselves (sunlight, wind, water, biomass, hydropower) yet can still be depleted by overuse or poor management; non-renewable resources (fossil fuels, metals and similar stocks) form too slowly to be replaced once used.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
