# Renewable and non-renewable resources — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about natural resources?**
**Answer: Resources are anything nature provides that people use — food, fuel, materials, air, sunlight, soil, water, animals and plants; renewable resources replace themselves (sunlight, wind, water, biomass, hydropower) yet can still be depleted by overuse or poor management; non-renewable resources (fossil fuels, metals and similar stocks) form too slowly to be replaced once used.**
