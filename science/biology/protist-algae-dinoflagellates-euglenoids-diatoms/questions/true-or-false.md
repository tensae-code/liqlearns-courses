# Protist algae — dinoflagellates, euglenoids and diatoms — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Diatoms belong with the silica-walled Chrysophyta of the marine plankton.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Dinoflagellates have no flagella.**
**Answer: False** — they have two flagella.

**3. Algae are heterotrophs that eat other plankton.**
**Answer: False** — algae photosynthesise — they are the food base of the heterotrophs.

**4. Euglenophyta is one of the protist algal phyla in the module.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
