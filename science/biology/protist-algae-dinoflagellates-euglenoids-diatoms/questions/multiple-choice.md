# Protist algae — dinoflagellates, euglenoids and diatoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The Chrysophyta are known for walls made of…**
a) chitin  b) silica  c) cellulose only  d) lime scales
**Answer: b) silica**

**2. Dinoflagellates swim with…**
a) one flagellum  b) two flagella  c) cilia all over  d) a muscular foot
**Answer: b) two flagella**

**3. Bioluminescence on night wave-crests is produced by…**
a) diatoms  b) euglenoids  c) dinoflagellates  d) sponges
**Answer: c) dinoflagellates**

**4. The algae matter to aquatic animals because they are…**
a) parasites of fish  b) the food base of aquatic heterotrophs  c) makers of silica  d) night predators
**Answer: b) the food base of aquatic heterotrophs**
