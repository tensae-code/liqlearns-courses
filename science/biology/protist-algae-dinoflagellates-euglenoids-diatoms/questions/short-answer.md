# Protist algae — dinoflagellates, euglenoids and diatoms — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Give the three protist algal phyla of the module with one mark for each.
**Answer: Euglenophyta; Chrysophyta — silica walls, marine plankton (the diatoms); and Pyrrophyta — the dinoflagellates, with two flagella and bioluminescence seen as sparks on night wave-crests.**

**2.** Explain Mozambique's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Mozambique's PESD II Módulo 1 de Biologia, Lesson 9, teaches the protist algae and their importance inside the Protista sequence: the phyla Euglenophyta, Chrysophyta (silica walls, marine plankton) and Pyrrophyta/dinoflagellates (two flagella, bioluminescence on night wave-crests) — and algae as the food base of aquatic heterotrophs.**
