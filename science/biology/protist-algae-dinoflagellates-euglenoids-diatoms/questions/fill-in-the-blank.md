# Protist algae — dinoflagellates, euglenoids and diatoms — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The dinoflagellates belong to the phylum _____.**
**Answer: Pyrrophyta**

**2. Silica-walled algae of the marine plankton are the _____.**
**Answer: Chrysophyta**

**3. Light produced by a living organism is called _____.**
**Answer: bioluminescence**

**4. Algae are the food _____ of aquatic heterotrophs.**
**Answer: base**
