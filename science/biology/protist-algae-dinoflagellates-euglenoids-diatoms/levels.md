# Protist algae — dinoflagellates, euglenoids and diatoms — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (The protist algae are sorted into phyla by their pigments, walls and tails: the Euglenophyta; the Chrysophyta, silica-walled algae of the marine plankton (the diatoms); and the Pyrrophyta — the dinoflagellates, with two flagella and a bioluminescence that sparkles on night wave-crests — and together the algae are the food base on which aquatic heterotrophs live.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each phylum to its mark without looking — Euglenophyta, Chrysophyta/silica/plankton, Pyrrophyta/two flagella/bioluminescence — and closing with the algae's role as the food base of aquatic heterotrophs.
