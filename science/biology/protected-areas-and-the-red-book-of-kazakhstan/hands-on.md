# Hands-on — Protected areas and the Red Book of Kazakhstan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: Kazakhstan guards its nature through a network of specially protected areas — state reserves, national parks and sanctuaries, each with its own rules — and lists its rarest species, the snow leopard, the Caspian seal and Selevinia among them, in the Red Book of Kazakhstan, protected by law, by science and by practical measures.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a map of Kazakhstan dotted with reserve and national-park shields, beside an open Red Book showing a snow leopard, a Caspian seal and a small Selevinia, each named. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
