# Protected areas and the Red Book of Kazakhstan — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Kazakhstan guards its nature through a network of specially protected areas — state reserves, national parks and sanctuaries, each with its own rules — and lists its rarest species, the snow leopard, the Caspian seal and Selevinia among them, in the Red Book of Kazakhstan, protected by law, by science and by practical measures) and explain each key fact in their own words.
