# Theories of Evolution: Lamarck, Darwin and the Modern Synthesis

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — theories of evolution: lamarck, darwin and the modern synthesis. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Lamarckism (Jean-Baptiste de Lamarck, 1744–1829, seeking a naturalistic explanation of diversity); Darwin's and Wallace's natural selection; Neo-Darwinism defined as the accepted revision combining Darwin's theory, genetic theory and animal behaviour, taught through the gene pool concept and its three postulates — genetic variability, natural selection, reproductive isolation; a group activity discusses the postulates.
