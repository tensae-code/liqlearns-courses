# Video script — Human evolution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Humans emerge after the dinosaurs' disappearance about 70 million years ago, from monkey-like, lemur-like ancestors; brain size is staged (slow increase 6–2 million years ago as upright walking and simple tools began; brain and body increase 2 million–800,000 years ago; the modern brain the largest and most complex of any living primate, tripled over human evolution).
