# Hands-on — Embryonic development — segmentation, gastrulation and organogenesis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Draw the three panels yourself on paper — dividing ball, layered cup, small embryo with organs — and label each with its phase. The drawing is the lesson: development is not three separate events but one continuous building, as the module insists.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — an egg drawn with its two poles labelled and yolk at the vegetative end, then three panels in a row: a ball of dividing cells (segmentation), a cup-shaped embryo with layers moving in (gastrulation), and a small embryo with the first organs marked (organogenesis) — label its parts, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
