# Embryonic development — segmentation, gastrulation and organogenesis — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Development runs continuously from the egg through three phases — segmentation (cleavage divisions), gastrulation (the cell territories move into place and the germ layers form, the mesoderm arising from the primitive gut) and organogenesis (the organs are built) — and it starts from an egg with two poles, animal and vegetative, classified by how much yolk it carries.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by reciting the three phases in order with one event for each — segmentation/divisions, gastrulation/layers and territories, organogenesis/organs — and the ectoderm's three products: nervous system, sense organs, epidermis.
