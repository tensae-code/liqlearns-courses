# Embryonic development — segmentation, gastrulation and organogenesis — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Name the three phases of embryonic development and say what happens in each.
**Answer: Segmentation — the egg divides repeatedly, multiplying cells. Gastrulation — the cell territories move into place and the germ layers form, the mesoderm arising from the primitive gut. Organogenesis — the organs are built from the germ layers.**

**2.** Explain Mozambique's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Mozambique's PESD II Módulo 7 de Biologia, Lessons 14–15 (ontogenesis), teaches development as a continuous process from the egg — cell multiplication, differentiation, morphogenesis — starting from egg polarity (animal vs vegetative pole) and the classification of eggs by yolk, then the three phases segmentation, gastrulation and organogenesis, with the chorion forming the placenta in mammals and the ectoderm giving nervous system, sense organs and epidermis.**
