# Embryonic development — segmentation, gastrulation and organogenesis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The three phases of embryonic development, in order, are…**
a) gastrulation, segmentation, organogenesis  b) segmentation, gastrulation, organogenesis  c) organogenesis, segmentation, gastrulation  d) fertilisation, birth, growth
**Answer: b) segmentation, gastrulation, organogenesis**

**2. The egg's two poles are the…**
a) north and south poles  b) animal and vegetative poles  c) inner and outer poles  d) head and tail poles
**Answer: b) animal and vegetative poles**

**3. In gastrulation, the mesoderm arises from the…**
a) yolk sac  b) primitive gut  c) chorion  d) epidermis
**Answer: b) primitive gut**

**4. The ectoderm gives rise to…**
a) the gut lining  b) the muscles  c) the nervous system, sense organs and epidermis  d) the skeleton only
**Answer: c) the nervous system, sense organs and epidermis**
