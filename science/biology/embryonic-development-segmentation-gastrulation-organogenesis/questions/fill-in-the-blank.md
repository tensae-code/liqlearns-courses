# Embryonic development — segmentation, gastrulation and organogenesis — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The first phase of development, of repeated divisions, is called _____.**
**Answer: segmentation**

**2. The phase in which germ layers form is called _____.**
**Answer: gastrulation**

**3. The building of organs is called _____.**
**Answer: organogenesis**

**4. The germ layer that gives the nervous system is the _____.**
**Answer: ectoderm**
