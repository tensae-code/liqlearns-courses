# Embryonic development — segmentation, gastrulation and organogenesis — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why gastrulation is called the phase where the body's map is laid down.
**Model answer: Before gastrulation the embryo is a ball of similar cells with no arrangement. In gastrulation the cell territories move into their places and the germ layers form — ectoderm outside, mesoderm (from the primitive gut) and endoderm within. Every organ of the adult is then built from the layer it belongs to, so the embryo's geography in this phase decides the body's whole map.**
