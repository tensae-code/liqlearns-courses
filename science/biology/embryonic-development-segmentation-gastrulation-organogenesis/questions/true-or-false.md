# Embryonic development — segmentation, gastrulation and organogenesis — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Development from the egg is a continuous process.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches — multiplication, differentiation, morphogenesis.

**2. Segmentation is the phase in which organs are built.**
**Answer: False** — segmentation is the dividing phase; organs are built in organogenesis.

**3. In mammals the chorion forms the placenta.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. Eggs are classified by their yolk.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches — with the animal and vegetative poles.
