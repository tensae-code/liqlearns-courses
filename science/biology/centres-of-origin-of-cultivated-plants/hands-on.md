# Hands-on — Centres of origin of cultivated plants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: Vavilov showed that every cultivated plant comes from a centre of origin — a region where its wild relatives grow and its diversity is greatest — and the same mapping of centres was made for domestic animals.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a world map with Vavilov's centres of origin marked as glowing regions, a wheat ear, a potato and an apple each joined by a line to the centre it came from. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
