# Centres of origin of cultivated plants — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Vavilov showed that every cultivated plant comes from a centre of origin — a region where its wild relatives grow and its diversity is greatest — and the same mapping of centres was made for domestic animals) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
