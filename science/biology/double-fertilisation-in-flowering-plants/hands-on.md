# Hands-on — Double fertilisation in flowering plants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Draw the embryo sac from memory as the guide's process diagram shows it: mark the egg cell and the polar nuclei, draw the pollen tube arriving with two male gametes, and draw an arrow from each gamete to what it fuses with and what that fusion becomes — zygote to embryo, endosperm to food store. Check your drawing against the lesson and fix anything you missed.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a pollen tube entering an embryo sac, releasing two male gametes — one arrow to the egg cell labelled 'zygote → embryo', the other to the two polar nuclei labelled 'endosperm → food store' — inside a young seed — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
