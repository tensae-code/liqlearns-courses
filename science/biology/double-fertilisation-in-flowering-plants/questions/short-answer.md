# Double fertilisation in flowering plants — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the two fusions of double fertilisation and what each produces.
**Answer: One male gamete fuses with the egg cell to form the zygote, which develops into the embryo. The second male gamete fuses with the polar nuclei to form the endosperm, the food tissue that feeds the embryo in the seed.**

**2.** Explain: Indonesia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Indonesia's Grade 11 teacher guide (Ch. 3, printed p. 108) names double fertilisation explicitly and teaches it through the process diagram and a set teaching sequence, inside the plant life-cycle chapter.**
