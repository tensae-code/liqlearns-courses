# Double fertilisation in flowering plants — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. One male gamete plus the egg cell forms the _____.**
**Answer: zygote**

**2. The food tissue of the seed formed by the second fusion is the _____.**
**Answer: endosperm**

**3. The second male gamete fuses with the _____ nuclei.**
**Answer: polar**

**4. The zygote develops into the _____ inside the seed.**
**Answer: embryo**
