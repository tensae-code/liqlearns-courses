# Double fertilisation in flowering plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The second male gamete fuses with the polar nuclei to form the…**
a) zygote  b) embryo  c) endosperm  d) seed coat
**Answer: c) endosperm**

**2. The fusion of a male gamete with the egg cell produces the…**
a) endosperm  b) zygote  c) pollen tube  d) ovary wall
**Answer: b) zygote**

**3. Double fertilisation is the defining reproduction event of…**
a) mosses  b) ferns  c) flowering plants  d) conifers only
**Answer: c) flowering plants**

**4. The endosperm's job in the seed is to…**
a) protect the seed from insects  b) store food for the developing embryo  c) make pollen  d) open the seed
**Answer: b) store food for the developing embryo**
