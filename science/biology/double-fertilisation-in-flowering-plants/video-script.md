Today: Double fertilisation in flowering plants.

Remember this one thing: fertilisation in a flowering plant happens twice in the same embryo sac.

One male gamete joins the egg and makes the zygote — the future embryo. The second joins the polar nuclei and makes the endosperm — the food store. Embryo and food, made together: that is double fertilisation.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
