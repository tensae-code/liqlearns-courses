# Double fertilisation in flowering plants — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (In a flowering plant, fertilisation is double: of the two male gametes a pollen grain delivers into the embryo sac, one joins the egg cell to form the zygote that grows into the embryo, and the second joins the polar nuclei to form the endosperm — the food tissue that will feed the embryo inside the seed) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
