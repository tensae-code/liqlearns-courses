# Tree rings — reading past climates — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Rings grow _____ in warm, wet years.**
**Answer: thick**

**2. Rings grow _____ in cold, dry or harsh years.**
**Answer: thin**

**3. Scientists read past climates from ring _____.**
**Answer: widths**

**4. The science of reading climates from tree rings is called _____.**
**Answer: dendrochronology**
