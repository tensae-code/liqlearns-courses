# Tree rings — reading past climates — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** A trunk shows three wide rings, then two very thin rings, then a wide ring. Read this record.
**Answer: The tree lived through three warm, wet years in which it grew strongly, then two cold, dry or harsh years in which its growth was held back, then another warm, wet year. The widths, read in order, are the climate history of the tree's place for those six years.**

**2.** Explain Jordan's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Jordan's Grade 11 student book teaches tree rings as the enrichment closing its plant-response lesson: trees record local climate, rings growing thick in warm, wet years and thin in cold, dry or harsh years, so scientists read past climates from ring widths.**
