# Tree rings — reading past climates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What does a thick tree ring record?**
a) A cold, dry year  b) A warm, wet year  c) A year with no rain at all  d) The tree's age in months
**Answer: b) A warm, wet year**

**2. What does a thin tree ring record?**
a) A warm, wet year  b) A year the tree was cut  c) A cold, dry or harsh year  d) A year with extra fertiliser
**Answer: c) A cold, dry or harsh year**

**3. How do scientists use tree rings?**
a) They count rings to find the tree's weight  b) They read past climates from ring widths  c) They read the soil type from ring colour  d) They predict next year's weather exactly
**Answer: b) They read past climates from ring widths**

**4. Whose climate do a tree's rings record?**
a) The climate of the whole planet equally  b) The local climate of the place the tree stood in  c) Only the climate of the tropics  d) No climate — rings are random
**Answer: b) The local climate of the place the tree stood in**
