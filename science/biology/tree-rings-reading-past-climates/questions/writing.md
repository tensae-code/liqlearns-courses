# Tree rings — reading past climates — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining how a dead trunk can tell us about climates nobody measured.
**Model answer: A tree lays down one ring of wood as it grows, and the ring's width answers that year's climate — thick in a warm, wet year, thin in a cold, dry or harsh year. Because the rings stay in the trunk in order, measuring their widths reconstructs the climates of past years, including years before weather stations existed.**
