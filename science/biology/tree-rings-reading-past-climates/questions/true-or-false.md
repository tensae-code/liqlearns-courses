# Tree rings — reading past climates — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. A thin ring records a warm, wet year.**
**Answer: False** — a thin ring records a cold, dry or harsh year; a thick ring records the warm, wet year.

**2. Tree rings record the local climate where the tree grew.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Ring widths can be read for years before weather stations existed.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches — the widths reconstruct past climates.

**4. Every ring in a trunk has the same width.**
**Answer: False** — widths differ year by year, and the differences are the record.
