Today: Tree rings — reading past climates.

Remember this one thing: a tree writes its climate into its rings.

In a warm, wet year the tree grows strongly, and the ring it lays down is thick. In a cold, dry or harsh year growth is held back, and the ring is thin.

So the trunk is a record. Scientists measure the rings, thick and thin in order, and read from their widths the climates of years long past — years before anyone kept weather records.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
