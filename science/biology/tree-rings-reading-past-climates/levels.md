# Tree rings — reading past climates — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (A tree records its local climate in its rings: a ring grows thick in a warm, wet year and thin in a cold, dry or harsh year, so scientists read past climates from ring widths.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by reading a drawn ring sequence aloud — naming each wide ring a warm, wet year and each thin ring a cold, dry or harsh year, in order — without looking.
