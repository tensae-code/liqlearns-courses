# Hands-on — Tree rings — reading past climates

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
If a cut log, stump or firewood end is available, look at its rings without cutting anything new: find the widest ring and the thinnest ring and say what kind of year each one records. If no wood is available, draw a trunk face with ten rings of your own, mixing thick and thin.

## Teach it on paper
Draw a trunk face from memory with thick and thin rings, label one of each with the year it records, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
