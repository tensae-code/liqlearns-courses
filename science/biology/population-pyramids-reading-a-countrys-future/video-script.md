# Video script — Population pyramids — reading a country's future

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Demography's measures are defined (crude birth and death rates, infant mortality, fertility, life expectancy, migration) and read off population pyramids: expansive (broad base, young and growing), constrictive and stationary shapes, with Kenya, the USA and Italy (2015) as the three pictured types and a step-by-step method for building a pyramid from 5-year age-group data.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
