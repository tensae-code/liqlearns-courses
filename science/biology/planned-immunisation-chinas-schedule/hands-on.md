# Hands-on — Planned immunisation — China's schedule

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: planned immunisation gives each vaccine at its set age on a national schedule — in China's, BCG and hepatitis B at birth — and the child's vaccination certificate is the document that holds the record, reproduced in the book from Beijing's two-page schedule.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a child's vaccination certificate opened to a schedule table, with a birth-day row marked BCG and hepatitis B. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
