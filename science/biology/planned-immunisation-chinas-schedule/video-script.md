Today: Planned immunisation — China's schedule.

Remember this one thing: planned immunisation gives each vaccine at its set age on a national schedule — in China's, BCG and hepatitis B at birth — and the child's vaccination certificate is the document that holds the record, reproduced in the book from Beijing's two-page schedule.
