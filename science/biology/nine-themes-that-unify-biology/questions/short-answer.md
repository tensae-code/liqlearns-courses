# Nine themes that unify biology — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: energy and growth.
**Answer: Themes 5–6: energy, matter and organisation (living order is maintained by continuous metabolism), and growth, development and differentiation by a genetic plan influenced by environment.**

**2.** Explain: structure, regulation, environment.
**Answer: Themes 7–9: complementarity of structure and function, regulation and homeostasis, and complementarity of organism and environment.**
