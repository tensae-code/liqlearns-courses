# Nine themes that unify biology — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** Themes 1–2: change of living things with time (characteristics are products of evolution), _____.
**Answer: and genetic continuity (generations linked by genetic material passed parent to offspring)**

**2.** Themes 3–4: biological roots of behaviour (behaviour is partly innate and inherited), _____.
**Answer: and diversity of type with unity of pattern**
