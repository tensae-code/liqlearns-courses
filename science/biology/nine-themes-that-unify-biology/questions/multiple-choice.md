# Nine themes that unify biology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about change and continuity?

**Answer: a) Themes 1–2: change of living things with time (characteristics are products of evolution), and genetic continuity (generations linked by genetic material passed parent to offspring)**
