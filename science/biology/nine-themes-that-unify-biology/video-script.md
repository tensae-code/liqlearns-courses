The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: Nine themes that unify biology.

Remember this one thing: nine themes unify the whole of biology, from evolution to organism–environment complementarity.

Themes 1–2: change of living things with time (characteristics are products of evolution), and genetic continuity (generations linked by genetic material passed parent to offspring).

Themes 3–4: biological roots of behaviour (behaviour is partly innate and inherited), and diversity of type with unity of pattern.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
