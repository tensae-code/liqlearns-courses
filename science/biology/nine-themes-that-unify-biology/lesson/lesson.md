# Nine themes that unify biology

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Nine themes that unify biology. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only: the student book never lists these themes. The guide names nine themes that unify the whole subject. (teacher guide — evidence)

## The key facts
**Change and continuity.** Themes 1–2: change of living things with time (characteristics are products of evolution), and genetic continuity (generations linked by genetic material passed parent to offspring).

**Behaviour and diversity.** Themes 3–4: biological roots of behaviour (behaviour is partly innate and inherited), and diversity of type with unity of pattern.

**Energy and growth.** Themes 5–6: energy, matter and organisation (living order is maintained by continuous metabolism), and growth, development and differentiation by a genetic plan influenced by environment.

**Structure, regulation, environment.** Themes 7–9: complementarity of structure and function, regulation and homeostasis, and complementarity of organism and environment.
