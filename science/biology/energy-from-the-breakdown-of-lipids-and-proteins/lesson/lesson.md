# Energy from the breakdown of lipids and proteins

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Energy from the breakdown of lipids and proteins. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Cells also burn lipids and proteins for energy: fats are split into fatty acids and glycerol that enter respiration's pathways — gram for gram fat yields more than carbohydrate — and amino acids enter after their nitrogen is removed, tying energy release back to photosynthesis as the source of it all. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from Greece's Gymnasio and Lykeio biology books, which teach it as a named topic.
