# Energy from the breakdown of lipids and proteins — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (cells also burn lipids and proteins for energy: fats are split into fatty acids and glycerol that enter respiration's pathways — gram for gram fat yields more than carbohydrate — and amino acids enter after their nitrogen is removed, tying energy release back to photosynthesis as the source of it all) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
