# Video script — What regulates population growth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Growth is checked by density-dependent factors — predation, competition within and between species, waste accumulation and disease, all biting harder as density rises — and density-independent factors that strike regardless of density; pupils debate the two groups' relative weight.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
