# What regulates population growth — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about population regulation?**
**Answer: Growth is checked by density-dependent factors — predation, competition within and between species, waste accumulation and disease, all biting harder as density rises — and density-independent factors that strike regardless of density; pupils debate the two groups' relative weight.**
