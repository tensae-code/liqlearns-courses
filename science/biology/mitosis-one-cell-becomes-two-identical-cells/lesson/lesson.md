## Sub lesson 1 — Mitosis

One division producing two identical daughter cells, in four steps: prophase (chromosomes condense, envelope breaks down, spindle forms, centrosomes separate), metaphase (chromosomes line up), anaphase (sister chromatids pulled to opposite poles) and telophase (envelopes reform, chromosomes unravel), finished by cytokinesis.
