# Harmful substances and the developing fetus — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: the placenta feeds the fetus but does not seal it off: alcohol, nicotine and narcotic drugs pass through it and can harm the developing baby, which is why those substances are forbidden in pregnancy. 10.4 The effect of harmful substances on the fetus**

**2.** Explain Kazakhstan's way of teaching this.
**Answer: 10.4 The effect of harmful substances on the fetus §51 The effect of smoking, narcotic substances and alcohol on the development of the human fetus 15.2 Impacts of cigarettes, alcohol and drugs on fetal development Module 11: The placenta passes harmful substances too — alcohol, smoke and drugs reach the baby (in "The functions of the placenta")**
