# Animal cell culture — conditions and passage

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Animal cell culture — conditions and passage. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Animal cells can be grown outside the body in a dish, if the culture gives them a suitable medium with serum, sterility, body temperature, the right pH and the right gases — and a grown culture is passaged by trypsinising it into single cells that attach and divide again. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from China's People's Education Press biology textbooks, which teach it as a named topic.
