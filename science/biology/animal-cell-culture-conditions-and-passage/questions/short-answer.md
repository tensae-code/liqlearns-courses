# Animal cell culture — conditions and passage — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: animal cells can be grown outside the body in a dish, if the culture gives them a suitable medium with serum, sterility, body temperature, the right pH and the right gases — and a grown culture is passaged by trypsinising it into single cells that attach and divide again. opened on growing a burn patient's own skin; the conditions (synthetic medium plus serum, sterility, 36.5 ± 0.5 °C, pH 7.2–7.4, 95% air with 5% CO₂); trypsinising tissue to single cells; attachment and contact inhibition; primary culture against passage culture; and the cell-culture engineer career told through the HeLa line of 1951.**
