# Animal cell culture — conditions and passage — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (animal cells can be grown outside the body in a dish, if the culture gives them a suitable medium with serum, sterility, body temperature, the right pH and the right gases — and a grown culture is passaged by trypsinising it into single cells that attach and divide again) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
