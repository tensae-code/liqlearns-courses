# Mineral nutrition of green plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What makes a mineral element "essential" to a green plant?**
a) The plant can complete its life without it  b) The plant cannot grow and complete its life without it  c) It is the rarest element in the soil  d) It gives the leaves their green colour
**Answer: b) The plant cannot grow and complete its life without it**

**2. What is hydroponics?**
a) Growing plants in soil enriched with manure  b) Growing plants in water holding a measured mineral solution, with no soil  c) Growing plants in the dark  d) Growing plants only from cuttings
**Answer: b) Growing plants in water holding a measured mineral solution, with no soil**

**3. In a hydroponics test, one element is left out and the plant fails with a visible symptom. What does that prove?**
a) The element was not essential  b) The water was at fault  c) The left-out element was essential  d) Plants do not need minerals
**Answer: c) The left-out element was essential**

**4. What is nitrogen fixation?**
a) Turning air's nitrogen into a form plants can take in  b) The loss of nitrogen from the soil  c) The burning of nitrogen fertiliser  d) The green colour of leaves
**Answer: a) Turning air's nitrogen into a form plants can take in**
