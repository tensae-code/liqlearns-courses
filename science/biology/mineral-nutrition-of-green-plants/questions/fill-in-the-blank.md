# Mineral nutrition of green plants — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Growing a plant in a measured mineral solution with no soil is called _____.**
**Answer: hydroponics**

**2. The visible sign a plant shows when an essential element runs short is a _____ symptom.**
**Answer: deficiency**

**3. Turning air's nitrogen into a form plants can use is nitrogen _____.**
**Answer: fixation**

**4. An element the plant cannot complete its life without is called an _____ element.**
**Answer: essential**
