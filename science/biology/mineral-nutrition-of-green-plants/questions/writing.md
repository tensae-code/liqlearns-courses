# Mineral nutrition of green plants — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why a green plant needs mineral elements, and how we know which ones are essential.
**Model answer: A green plant makes its food by photosynthesis, but building the plant itself takes mineral elements taken in through the roots, and some of these elements are essential — the plant cannot complete its life without them. Hydroponics proves which ones they are: grown in a measured mineral solution with no soil, a plant left without one element fails with a deficiency symptom if that element was essential, and grows on if it was not.**
