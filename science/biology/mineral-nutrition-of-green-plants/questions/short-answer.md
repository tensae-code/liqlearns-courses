# Mineral nutrition of green plants — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain how hydroponics proves that an element is essential.
**Answer: The plant is grown with no soil, in water holding a measured mix of mineral salts, so every element it receives is known. If one element is left out and the plant fails, showing its deficiency symptom, that element was essential; if the plant grows on without it, it was not.**

**2.** Explain India's way of teaching this.
**Answer: NCERT's Class XI book gives mineral nutrition its own chapter (Chapter 12), standing between Transport in Plants and Photosynthesis in Higher Plants. It teaches the essential elements through the two evidences a grower can see — the deficiency symptom on the plant itself, and the hydroponics jar where leaving one element out makes the plant fail — and closes the nitrogen story with nitrogen fixation.**
