# Mineral nutrition of green plants — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. A green plant can grow fully on light, air and water alone, with no mineral elements.**
**Answer: False** — the plant must also take in mineral elements; the essential ones it cannot complete its life without.

**2. A deficiency symptom is a visible sign on the plant that points to a missing essential element.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Hydroponics grows plants in rich garden soil so the soil's contents stay unknown.**
**Answer: False** — hydroponics uses no soil: the plant grows in a measured mineral solution, so every element it gets is known.

**4. In leguminous plants, bacteria in root nodules fix nitrogen for the plant.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
