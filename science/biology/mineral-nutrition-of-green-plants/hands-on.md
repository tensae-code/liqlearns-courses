# Hands-on — Mineral nutrition of green plants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Look at real plants around you — a crop, a garden plant, a potted plant — and look for the visible signs this lesson names: healthy even growth against any yellowing, stunting or poor growth that could be a deficiency symptom. Say aloud what each sign might be telling you.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the hydroponics test from memory: a plant growing in a jar of mineral solution with no soil, one element left out, and the deficiency symptom it shows. Label each part — solution, missing element, symptom — and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
