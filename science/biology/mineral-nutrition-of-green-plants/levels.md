# Mineral nutrition of green plants — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (A green plant does not live on light, air and water alone: it must take in mineral elements, and only some elements are essential — leave one out and the plant shows it as a deficiency symptom; hydroponics, growing plants in a measured mineral solution with no soil, is the experiment that proves which elements are essential, and nitrogen fixation turns air's nitrogen into a form plants can use.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each part — essential elements, deficiency symptoms, hydroponics, nitrogen fixation — to what it shows or does, without looking.
