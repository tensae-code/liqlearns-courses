# Epigenetics — marks on the DNA — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: epigenetics is inheritance by marks on the DNA — methylation that switches a gene off without changing its base sequence — passed on without any change in the letters, as the changed toadflax flower and the agouti mouse's yellow-or-mottled coat show. toadflax flowers changed by methylation of the Lcyc gene and agouti mice whose identical Avy genes give yellow or mottled coats by methylation level, inherited without any change in base sequence; twins, queen and worker bees, and a society note that smoking raises methylation and damages sperm — epigenetics is in this book and in no existing lesson.**
