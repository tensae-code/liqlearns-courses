# Epigenetics — marks on the DNA — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. toadflax flowers changed by methylation of the Lcyc gene and agouti mice whose identical Avy genes give yellow or mottled coats by methylation level, inherited without any change in base sequence; twins, queen and worker bees, and a society note that smoking raises methylation and damages sperm — epigenetics is in this book and in no existing lesson.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in China's People's Education Press biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (普通高中教科书《生物学》必修2 遗传与进化 (Senior High Biology, Compulsory Book 2: Genetics and Evolution, People's Education Press, 2019-standard edition)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with epigenetics — marks on the dna.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is epigenetics — marks on the dna.
