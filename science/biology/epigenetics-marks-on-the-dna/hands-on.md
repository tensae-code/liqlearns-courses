# Hands-on — Epigenetics — marks on the DNA

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: epigenetics is inheritance by marks on the DNA — methylation that switches a gene off without changing its base sequence — passed on without any change in the letters, as the changed toadflax flower and the agouti mouse's yellow-or-mottled coat show.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: two identical mice, one yellow and one mottled, above the same DNA ladder with different small tags clipped onto it. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
