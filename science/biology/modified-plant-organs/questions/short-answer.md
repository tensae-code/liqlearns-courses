# Modified plant organs — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: a plant organ can be modified — changed in form so it does a special job besides, or instead of, its ordinary one: tendrils modified to climb and hold, thorns modified to defend, and storage organs modified to keep food and water for the plant. A modified organ keeps its identity as a root, stem or leaf but its form is changed — flattened, sharpened, swollen or curled — so it can do a special job. The organ is recognised by where it grows and what it grows from, not by the job it now does.**

**2.** Explain Bulgaria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Bulgaria's Pedagog 6 Grade 7 book does not scatter modifications inside the organ topics: it names modified plant organs as a topic in itself (Topic 19, printed p. 42) and teaches it as a discussion/project topic — learners argue and present cases of organs changed for special jobs.**
