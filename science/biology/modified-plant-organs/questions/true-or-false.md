# Modified plant organs — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. A modified organ keeps its identity as a root, stem or leaf but its form is changed — flattened, sharpened, swollen or curled — so it can do a special job. The organ is recognised by where it grows and what it grows from, not by the job it now does.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Bulgaria's Pedagog 6 biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Биология и здравно образование 7. клас (Pedagog 6), printed p. 42).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with modified plant organs.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is modified plant organs.
