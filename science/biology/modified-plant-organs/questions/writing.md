# Modified plant organs — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: a plant organ can be modified — changed in form so it does a special job besides, or instead of, its ordinary one: tendrils modified to climb and hold, thorns modified to defend, and storage organs modified to keep food and water for the plant. A tendril is a thin, curling modified organ that winds round a support and holds the plant up. Climbing plants reach the light by tendrils instead of by a thick self-supporting stem.**
