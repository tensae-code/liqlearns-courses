# Video script — Modified plant organs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Modified plant organs.

Remember this one thing: a plant organ can be modified — changed in form so it does a special job besides, or instead of, its ordinary one: tendrils modified to climb and hold, thorns modified to defend, and storage organs modified to keep food and water for the plant.

Bulgaria's Grade 7 book gives modified plant organs a topic of their own (Topic 19, printed p. 42), taught as a discussion/project topic right after the plant organs themselves (Topics 17–18): once the learner knows the ordinary organs, the book asks what happens when an organ's form is changed for a special job.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
