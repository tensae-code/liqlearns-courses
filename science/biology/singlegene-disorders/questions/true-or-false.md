# Single-gene disorders — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Disorders from one mutated gene, in three inheritance patterns: autosomal dominant (expressed from one copy — Huntington's disease), autosomal recessive (needing two copies) and X-linked (haemophilia, the blood-clotting disorder, as the example); mutation itself is defined as a change in DNA sequence from copying errors or environmental factors.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A disorder-by-disorder table the student book lacks, giving description, cause, symptoms and treatment for each — Huntington's disease (dominant mutation on chromosome 4; uncontrolled movements, emotional disturbance and cognitive decline; currently no way to stop or slow it) and albinism (inherited, greatly reduced pigment) among the entries worked in the guide.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
