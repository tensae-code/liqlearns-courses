# Video script — Single-gene disorders

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Disorders from one mutated gene, in three inheritance patterns: autosomal dominant (expressed from one copy — Huntington's disease), autosomal recessive (needing two copies) and X-linked (haemophilia, the blood-clotting disorder, as the example); mutation itself is defined as a change in DNA sequence from copying errors or environmental factors.

A disorder-by-disorder table the student book lacks, giving description, cause, symptoms and treatment for each — Huntington's disease (dominant mutation on chromosome 4; uncontrolled movements, emotional disturbance and cognitive decline; currently no way to stop or slow it) and albinism (inherited, greatly reduced pigment) among the entries worked in the guide.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
