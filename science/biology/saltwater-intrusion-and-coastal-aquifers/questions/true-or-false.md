# Saltwater intrusion and coastal aquifers — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Rain that soaks into coastal ground gathers as groundwater in an aquifer.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Rising seas push the boundary inland is the opposite of what this lesson teaches.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches this as stated.**

**3.** The balance can be broken from the land side too: draw too much fresh water out of a coastal aquifer and the salt water below rises toward the well to fill the space.
**Answer: True**

**4.** This lesson's topic plays no part in biology at all.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
