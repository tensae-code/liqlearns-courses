# Saltwater intrusion and coastal aquifers — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Beneath a coast, fresh groundwater sits on denser salt water like oil on water; when the sea level rises, the salt water pushes further inland and up into the fresh water — saltwater intrusion — and Fiji’s coastal wells and aquifers are where it strikes first.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
