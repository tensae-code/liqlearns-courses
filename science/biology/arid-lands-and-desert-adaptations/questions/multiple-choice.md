# Arid lands and desert adaptations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What defines arid lands?
a) Too much rain
b) Very little, unreliable rainfall
c) Permanent flooding
d) No sunlight
**Answer: b) Very little, unreliable rainfall**

**2.** An arid-land plant adaptation would be…
a) large thin leaves that lose water fast
b) storing water and cutting water loss
c) roots kept at the surface only
d) growing only in rivers
**Answer: b) storing water and cutting water loss**

**3.** Arid-land animals are fitted to…
a) life with little water and great heat
b) life underwater
c) life in forest canopy
d) life in snow
**Answer: a) life with little water and great heat**

**4.** In Nigeria, the arid lands belong to…
a) the lagoon coast
b) the dry north, towards the Sahel
c) the rain forest
d) the mangrove swamp
**Answer: b) the dry north, towards the Sahel**
