# Arid lands and desert adaptations — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: how plants and animals are fitted to arid lands.
**Answer: Both answer the scarcity of water. Plants cut water loss, store water, or reach deep for it with their roots. Animals economise on water and endure heat and drought in body and behaviour. The fits differ; the problem is one.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book closes Chapter 14, terrestrial habitats, with the arid lands (printed p. 213) — the dry lands and how their plants and animals are fitted to them.**
