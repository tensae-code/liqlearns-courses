# Arid lands and desert adaptations — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Every adaptation in the arid lands answers the scarcity of water.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Arid-land plants survive by losing water quickly.
**Answer: False** — They survive by cutting water loss, storing water, or reaching deep for it.

**3.** Nigeria's arid lands are continuous with the Sahel belt.
**Answer: True**
