# Arid lands and desert adaptations — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** Lands of very little rainfall are called _____ lands.
**Answer: arid**

**2.** Arid-land plants cut water _____ and store what they get.
**Answer: loss**

**3.** Plant and animal adaptations in the desert answer one problem: _____ is scarce.
**Answer: water**
