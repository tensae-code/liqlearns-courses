# Arid lands and desert adaptations — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (arid lands are lands of very little water, and everything living in them is fitted to scarcity: plants and animals shaped to find water, keep water, and endure heat and drought) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: applying the lesson to a new example from the learner's own surroundings, and explaining Nigeria's way of teaching it (Ch. 14, printed p. 213), without looking.
