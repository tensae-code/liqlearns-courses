Today: Food poisoning and food safety.

Remember this one thing: Food poisoning is illness caused by contaminated food — Vietnam's Grade 11 study topics define it by the country's 2010 Food Safety Law and work its causes group by group: bacteria and their toxins, food-borne viruses, and parasites such as the small liver fluke that reaches people through raw fish salad.

Food poisoning is not just 'bad food'. Vietnam starts from the law — the 2010 Food Safety Law — and then names the culprits. Bacteria, and the toxins some of them leave behind, toxins that can survive cooking. Viruses that ride in food. And parasites — one fluke that hides in raw fish salad and ends in the human liver. Know the agents, and the safety rules stop being rules and start being reasons.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
