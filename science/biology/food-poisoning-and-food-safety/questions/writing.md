# Food poisoning and food safety — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why 'cook it well' is not a complete answer to food poisoning, using the lesson's agents.
**Model answer: Cooking kills living agents, but some of the harm is not alive by the time the food is eaten: bacteria such as Bacillus cereus and Staphylococcus aureus leave toxins in food, and the lesson names heat-stable toxins that survive the cooking. Food safety therefore starts before the pot — in clean handling and storage that stop the bacteria making their poisons at all.**
