# Food poisoning and food safety — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Some bacterial toxins in food are heat-stable — cooking does not destroy them.**
**Answer: True** — Bacillus cereus is named with heat-stable and heat-labile toxins.

**2. Food poisoning in Vietnam's lesson is caused by bacteria alone.**
**Answer: False** — viruses and parasites are also named agents.

**3. The lesson's definition of food poisoning comes from the 2010 Food Safety Law.**
**Answer: True** — the law is the lesson's starting point.

**4. The liver fluke in the lesson reaches people through raw fish salad.**
**Answer: True** — gỏi cá is the book's worked case.
