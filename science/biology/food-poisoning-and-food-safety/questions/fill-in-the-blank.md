# Food poisoning and food safety — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Vietnam's food-poisoning lesson is defined by the 2010 Food _____ Law.**
**Answer: Safety**

**2. Staphylococcus aureus makes toxins called _____.**
**Answer: enterotoxins**

**3. Hepatitis A and _____ are food-borne viruses named in the lesson.**
**Answer: norovirus**

**4. The small liver fluke reaches humans through raw fish _____ (gỏi cá).**
**Answer: salad**
