# Food poisoning and food safety — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Sort the agents of food poisoning named in Vietnam's lesson into their groups.
**Answer: Bacteria and their toxins — Salmonella, Campylobacter, E. coli, Clostridium botulinum, Staphylococcus aureus (enterotoxins A–E), Bacillus cereus (heat-stable and heat-labile toxins). Food-borne viruses — hepatitis A and E, rotavirus, norovirus. Parasites — including the small liver fluke, which reaches humans through raw fish salad (gỏi cá).**

**2.** Explain Vietnam's way of teaching food poisoning.
**Answer: Vietnam's Grade 11 study topics teach food poisoning as the closing lesson of the food hygiene and safety topic (Topic 3, Bài 10, printed p. 45): defined by the 2010 Food Safety Law, causes in four groups, agents named one by one — bacteria with their toxins, food-borne viruses, and parasites, with the small liver fluke in raw fish salad (gỏi cá) and its 15-hour/28-day development timings as the home case.**
