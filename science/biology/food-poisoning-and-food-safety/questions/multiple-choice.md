# Food poisoning and food safety — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Vietnam's food-poisoning lesson defines its subject by…**
a) a proverb about cooking  b) the 2010 Food Safety Law  c) a WHO poster  d) the school rules
**Answer: b) the 2010 Food Safety Law**

**2. Which of these is a bacterial toxin named in the lesson?**
a) Staphylococcus aureus enterotoxins A–E  b) norovirus  c) the liver fluke  d) hepatitis A
**Answer: a) Staphylococcus aureus enterotoxins A–E**

**3. The small liver fluke reaches humans through…**
a) undercooked rice  b) raw fish salad (gỏi cá)  c) unwashed hands only  d) mosquito bites
**Answer: b) raw fish salad (gỏi cá)**

**4. Which pair are food-borne viruses in the lesson?**
a) Salmonella and E. coli  b) hepatitis A and norovirus  c) botulinum and cereus toxins  d) fluke and tapeworm
**Answer: b) hepatitis A and norovirus**
