# Food poisoning and food safety — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Food poisoning is illness caused by contaminated food — Vietnam's Grade 11 study topics define it by the country's 2010 Food Safety Law and work its causes group by group: bacteria and their toxins, food-borne viruses, and parasites such as the small liver fluke that reaches people through raw fish salad.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by giving the lesson's key facts with the books' own examples without looking.
