# Lesson plan — Cell signalling and communication

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Cell signalling and communication
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — secondary band (CURRICULUM.md Unit 16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Cells talk to each other. A cell releases a signalling molecule, the molecule reaches a matching receptor on a target cell, the signal is carried inside and amplified, and the cell responds — a gene switched on or off, an enzyme started, a shape changed. The United States' OpenStax texts give cell communication a chapter of its own in both their biology books: signalling molecules and cellular receptors, propagation of the signal, response to the signal, and signalling in single-celled organisms.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier biology lessons and builds the secondary-band understanding of cell signalling and communication.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
