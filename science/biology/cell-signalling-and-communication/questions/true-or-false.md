# Cell signalling and communication — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Any cell can respond to any signalling molecule, whether or not it has the matching receptor.**
**Answer: False** — a cell responds only if it carries the receptor shaped to bind that molecule.

**2. A relay chain inside the cell can amplify a signal, so one molecule bound outside can produce a large response inside.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Signalling happens only in animals and plants with many cells.**
**Answer: False** — single-celled organisms signal too, as in bacterial quorum sensing and yeast mating signals.

**4. A cell's response to a signal can be switching a gene on or off.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
