# Cell signalling and communication — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The protein on or in a target cell that binds one signalling molecule is called a _____.**
**Answer: receptor**

**2. Passing a signal along a chain of relay molecules inside the cell is called _____ of the signal.**
**Answer: propagation**

**3. Another name for a signalling molecule that binds a receptor is a _____.**
**Answer: ligand**

**4. Bacteria sensing how many neighbours surround them, by the concentration of a signalling molecule, is called _____ sensing.**
**Answer: quorum**
