# Cell signalling and communication — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining how a cell that never touches another cell can still send it a message, and why only some cells answer.
**Model answer: A cell releases a signalling molecule that travels — through the spaces between cells or carried through the whole body, as hormones are — until it reaches other cells. Only a cell carrying the matching receptor can bind that molecule, so only those cells hear the message and respond; cells without the receptor are deaf to it, however much of the molecule passes them.**
