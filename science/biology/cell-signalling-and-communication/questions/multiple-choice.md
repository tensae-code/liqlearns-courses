# Cell signalling and communication — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is a signalling molecule?**
a) A molecule a cell releases to carry a message to another cell  b) A molecule that builds the cell wall  c) The molecule that stores the genes  d) A waste molecule the cell throws away
**Answer: a) A molecule a cell releases to carry a message to another cell**

**2. Why does only one kind of cell respond to a given signalling molecule?**
a) Only that cell is alive  b) Only cells carrying the matching receptor can bind the molecule and hear the message  c) The molecule travels to one cell only  d) Other cells destroy the molecule on sight
**Answer: b) Only cells carrying the matching receptor can bind the molecule and hear the message**

**3. What happens during propagation of the signal?**
a) The signal stops at the cell surface  b) The molecule is eaten by the cell  c) The signal is passed along a chain of relay molecules inside the cell, and can be amplified on the way  d) The cell divides at once
**Answer: c) The signal is passed along a chain of relay molecules inside the cell, and can be amplified on the way**

**4. What is quorum sensing?**
a) Bacteria counting their neighbours by the concentration of a signalling molecule, and changing behaviour when the count is high enough  b) A bacterium measuring its own size  c) Cells counting their chromosomes  d) A plant sensing the season
**Answer: a) Bacteria counting their neighbours by the concentration of a signalling molecule, and changing behaviour when the count is high enough**
