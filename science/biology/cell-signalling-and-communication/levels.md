# Cell signalling and communication — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (cells communicate: a signalling molecule from one cell binds a matching receptor on a target cell, the signal is propagated inside the cell — usually through a chain of relay molecules that amplifies it — and the cell responds; single-celled organisms signal too, to coordinate with their neighbours) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each part — signalling molecules, receptors, propagation of the signal, response to the signal, signalling in single-celled organisms — to what it shows or does, without looking.
