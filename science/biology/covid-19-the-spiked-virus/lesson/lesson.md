## The idea
Prevention is the familiar set: distance, fresh air, handwashing, masks, and covering coughs.

[Picture 21070 — the virus particle with S, M and E labelled, beside prevention icons]

## The key facts
**The virus.** Coronavirus disease 2019 is caused by a coronavirus — an enveloped virus with club-shaped spike (S) proteins on a lipid bilayer with membrane (M) and envelope (E) proteins.

**Spread.** COVID-19 spreads mainly in droplets and airborne particles when an infected person breathes, talks, coughs or sneezes, and by touched surfaces.

**Hands.** Prevention includes handwashing for 20 seconds or 60 percent alcohol sanitiser.

**Distance and masks.** Prevention includes distance, avoiding crowded poorly ventilated places, masks, and covering coughs.
