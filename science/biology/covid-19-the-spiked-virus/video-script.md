Today: COVID-19 — the spiked virus and how to stop it.

Remember this one thing: COVID-19 is caused by a coronavirus with club-shaped spike proteins, spreading mainly in droplets and airborne particles.

Coronavirus disease 2019 is caused by a coronavirus — an enveloped virus with club-shaped spike (S) proteins on a lipid bilayer with membrane (M) and envelope (E) proteins.

COVID-19 spreads mainly in droplets and airborne particles when an infected person breathes, talks, coughs or sneezes, and by touched surfaces.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
