# COVID-19 — the spiked virus and how to stop it — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about the virus?
a) Coronavirus disease 2019 is caused by a coronavirus — an enveloped virus with club-shaped spike (S) proteins on a lipid bilayer with membrane (M) and envelope (E) proteins
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Coronavirus disease 2019 is caused by a coronavirus — an enveloped virus with club-shaped spike (S) proteins on a lipid bilayer with membrane (M) and envelope (E) proteins**

**2.** What does this lesson say about spread?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) COVID-19 spreads mainly in droplets and airborne particles when an infected person breathes, talks, coughs or sneezes, and by touched surfaces
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) COVID-19 spreads mainly in droplets and airborne particles when an infected person breathes, talks, coughs or sneezes, and by touched surfaces**

**3.** What does this lesson say about hands?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Prevention includes handwashing for 20 seconds or 60 percent alcohol sanitiser
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Prevention includes handwashing for 20 seconds or 60 percent alcohol sanitiser**

**4.** What does this lesson say about distance and masks?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Prevention includes distance, avoiding crowded poorly ventilated places, masks, and covering coughs
**Answer: d) Prevention includes distance, avoiding crowded poorly ventilated places, masks, and covering coughs**
