# COVID-19 — the spiked virus and how to stop it — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** _____ 2019 is caused by a coronavirus — an enveloped virus with club-shaped spike (S) proteins on a lipid bilayer with membrane (M) and envelope (E) proteins
**Answer: Coronavirus disease**

**2.** COVID-19 spreads mainly in droplets and airborne particles when an infected person breathes, _____.
**Answer: talks, coughs or sneezes, and by touched surfaces**
