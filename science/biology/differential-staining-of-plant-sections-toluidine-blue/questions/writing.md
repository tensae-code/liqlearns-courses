**Model answer: toluidine blue stains different plant tissues in different colours, so a thin hand-cut section of a plant stem under the microscope shows its tissues told apart by colour — differential staining. The stain's multiple colours in different tissues are the result to read: one stain, several colours — each tissue announcing itself by the colour it takes.**
