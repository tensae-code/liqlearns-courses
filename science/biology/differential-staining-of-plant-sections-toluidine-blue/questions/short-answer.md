# Differential staining of plant sections — toluidine blue — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: toluidine blue stains different plant tissues in different colours, so a thin hand-cut section of a plant stem under the microscope shows its tissues told apart by colour — differential staining. Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 1 applications workbook (Activity 1, printed p. 17) teaches the differential staining technique: preparing toluidine blue stain, and cutting thin hand sections of a plant stem with the blade drawn at 45°.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait opens its Grade 11 workbook with technique, not theory: the first activity is a bench skill — stain preparation and hand sectioning — and the reading of a section by its stain colours (Activity 1).**
