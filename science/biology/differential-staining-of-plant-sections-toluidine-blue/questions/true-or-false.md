# Differential staining of plant sections — toluidine blue — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 1 applications workbook (Activity 1, printed p. 17) teaches the differential staining technique: preparing toluidine blue stain, and cutting thin hand sections of a plant stem with the blade drawn at 45°.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كراسة التطبيقات — الصف الحادي عشر — الفصل الدراسي الأول (Biology — Applications Workbook, Grade 11, Term 1), printed p. 17).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with differential staining of plant sections — toluidine blue.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is differential staining of plant sections — toluidine blue.
