# Differential staining of plant sections — toluidine blue — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (toluidine blue stains different plant tissues in different colours, so a thin hand-cut section of a plant stem under the microscope shows its tissues told apart by colour — differential staining) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
