Today: Differential staining of plant sections — toluidine blue.

Remember this one thing: toluidine blue stains different plant tissues in different colours, so a thin hand-cut section of a plant stem under the microscope shows its tissues told apart by colour — differential staining.

Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 1 applications workbook (Activity 1, printed p. 17) teaches the differential staining technique: preparing toluidine blue stain, and cutting thin hand sections of a plant stem with the blade drawn at 45°.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
