# Hands-on — Differential staining of plant sections — toluidine blue

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: toluidine blue stains different plant tissues in different colours, so a thin hand-cut section of a plant stem under the microscope shows its tissues told apart by colour — differential staining.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a hand section of a plant stem under a microscope, its tissues showing in several different stain colours, with the razor held at 45 degrees in a small inset sketch. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
