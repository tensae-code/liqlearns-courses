# The liver — bile, storage, detox — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: storage and detox.
**Answer: The liver stores glycogen and vitamins A, B12 and D, and detoxifies harmful substances.**

**2.** Explain: two supplies.
**Answer: The liver uniquely receives two blood supplies — hepatic arteries bringing oxygenated blood and the portal vein bringing venous blood from the gut.**

**3.** Explain: bile emulsifies (student book).
**Answer: Bile from the liver, stored in the gall bladder, emulsifies fat.**
