Today: The liver — bile, storage, detox.

Remember this one thing: the liver makes bile, stores glycogen and vitamins, detoxifies, and uniquely receives two blood supplies.

The digestive organ order is mouth, oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, rectum, anus, with accessory organs pancreas, gall bladder and liver.

The liver continually produces bile, which emulsifies large fat globules.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.


Student-book core: The liver, gall bladder and pancreas pour their juices into the canal as accessory organs. Bile from the liver, stored in the gall bladder, emulsifies fat.
