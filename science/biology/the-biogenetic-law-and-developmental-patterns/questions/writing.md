# The biogenetic law and developmental patterns — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining the biogenetic law to a younger student. Use the words ontogeny and phylogeny once each.
**Model answer: Every living thing develops from a single fertilised egg — that one individual's development is its ontogeny. The biogenetic law says that during that development the organism briefly repeats stages from the long history of its whole kind, its phylogeny — which is why a fish embryo, a bird embryo and a human embryo look so alike at first before they grow apart.**
