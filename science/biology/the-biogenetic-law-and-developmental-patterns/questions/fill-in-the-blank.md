# The biogenetic law and developmental patterns — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. One individual's development from fertilised egg to adult is called _____.**
**Answer: ontogeny (ontogenesis)**

**2. The biogenetic law states: ontogeny _____ phylogeny.**
**Answer: recapitulates**

**3. The evolutionary history of a whole group of organisms is its _____.**
**Answer: phylogeny**

**4. Embryos of different vertebrates look remarkably alike at matching _____ stages and diverge later.**
**Answer: early**
