# The biogenetic law and developmental patterns — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. The biogenetic law says ontogeny recapitulates phylogeny.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Ontogeny is the evolutionary history of a whole group of organisms.**
**Answer: False** — ontogeny is one individual's development; the group's history is phylogeny.

**3. The repetition the law names is brief and compressed, not a perfect stage-by-stage replay of every ancestor.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. Embryos of land animals never show structures such as gill slits or a tail.**
**Answer: False** — such structures appear in the embryos of land animals as well as fish, and the law is read from those resemblances.
