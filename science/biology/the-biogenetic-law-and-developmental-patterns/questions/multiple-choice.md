# The biogenetic law and developmental patterns — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is ontogeny?**
a) The evolutionary history of a whole group  b) One individual's development from fertilised egg to adult  c) The making of gametes  d) The classification of living things
**Answer: b) One individual's development from fertilised egg to adult**

**2. What does the biogenetic law state?**
a) Every embryo becomes an exact copy of its parent  b) Development stops at birth  c) In its individual development, an organism briefly repeats the stages its group passed through in its phylogeny  d) All embryos stay identical until birth
**Answer: c) In its individual development, an organism briefly repeats the stages its group passed through in its phylogeny**

**3. What is phylogeny?**
a) The evolutionary history of a group of organisms  b) One embryo's growth  c) The embryonic period only  d) A kind of fertilisation
**Answer: a) The evolutionary history of a group of organisms**

**4. Which observation is the law read from?**
a) Adults of different species look alike  b) Embryos of different vertebrates look remarkably alike at matching early stages and diverge later  c) All species lay eggs  d) Embryos never change shape
**Answer: b) Embryos of different vertebrates look remarkably alike at matching early stages and diverge later**
