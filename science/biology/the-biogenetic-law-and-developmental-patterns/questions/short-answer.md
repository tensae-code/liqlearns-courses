# The biogenetic law and developmental patterns — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the biogenetic law, in one or two sentences.
**Answer: In its individual development (ontogeny), an organism briefly repeats — recapitulates — the stages its ancestors passed through in the group's evolutionary history (phylogeny). The repetition is short and compressed, not a perfect replay.**

**2.** Explain: Uzbekistan's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Uzbekistan's Grade 9 textbook (Биология — Основы цитологии и генетики, "Zar Qalam", Tashkent 2006) closes its development chapter with §42 (printed p. 101): after the embryonic period and postembryonic development (§§40–41) it draws the general pattern over both and states the biogenetic law as a law — ontogeny recapitulating phylogeny.**
