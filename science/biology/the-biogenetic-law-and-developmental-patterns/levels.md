# The biogenetic law and developmental patterns — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (The biogenetic law — in its individual development (ontogeny), an organism briefly repeats the stages its group passed through in its evolutionary history (phylogeny), stated as a law in Uzbekistan's Grade 9 textbook, Part IV Ch. 10 §42, printed p. 101.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by taking another vertebrate — a frog, a chick, a human — and saying which early embryo resemblances the law would predict for it, and why, without looking.
