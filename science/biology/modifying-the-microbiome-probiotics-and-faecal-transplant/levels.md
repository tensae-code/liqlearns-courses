# Modifying the microbiome — probiotics and faecal transplant — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (the microbiome can be modified as a treatment — faecal transplantation, and probiotics with effects on weight and glycaemia) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: separating the two modifications by scale — a whole-community transplant against adding chosen live micro-organisms — and explaining why the manual studies probiotics through measurable outcomes (weight, glycaemia) rather than through promises.
