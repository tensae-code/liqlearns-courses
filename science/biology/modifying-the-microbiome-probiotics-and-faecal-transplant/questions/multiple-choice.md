# Modifying the microbiome — probiotics and faecal transplant — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does France's manual teach faecal transplantation as?
a) A way to sterilise the gut
b) A treatment — transplanting the gut microbiote
c) A kind of antibiotic
d) A test for diabetes
**Answer: b) A treatment — transplanting the gut microbiote**

**2.** What are probiotics, in the manual's teaching?
a) Antibiotics that kill gut bacteria
b) Hormones that lower blood sugar
c) Live micro-organisms given deliberately, to shift the microbiote
d) Vaccines against gut diseases
**Answer: c) Live micro-organisms given deliberately, to shift the microbiote**

**3.** Through which two measurable effects does the manual study probiotics?
a) Weight and glycaemia (blood sugar)
b) Height and eyesight
c) Blood pressure and pulse
d) Temperature and breathing rate
**Answer: a) Weight and glycaemia (blood sugar)**

**4.** How does a faecal transplant differ from probiotics?
a) It uses no micro-organisms
b) It is a diet, not a treatment
c) It only works on the skin
d) It brings in a whole community of gut micro-organisms; probiotics add chosen ones
**Answer: d) It brings in a whole community of gut micro-organisms; probiotics add chosen ones**
