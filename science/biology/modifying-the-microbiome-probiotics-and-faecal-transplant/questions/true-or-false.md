# Modifying the microbiome — probiotics and faecal transplant — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** France's manual teaches the microbiote as something medicine can deliberately modify.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A faecal transplant removes all micro-organisms from the gut and leaves it empty.
**Answer: False** — it transplants a gut microbiote: a whole community is brought in to rebuild the patient's own.

**3.** Probiotics are dead micro-organisms used as a food colouring.
**Answer: False** — probiotics are live micro-organisms given deliberately to shift the microbiote.

**4.** The manual studies probiotics' effects on weight and on glycaemia.
**Answer: True**
