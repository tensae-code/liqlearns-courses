# Modifying the microbiome — probiotics and faecal transplant — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the two ways France's manual teaches for modifying the microbiome.
**Answer: Faecal transplantation transplants the gut microbiote — a whole community of gut micro-organisms brought in as a treatment to rebuild the patient's own. Probiotics add chosen live micro-organisms deliberately, a small selected addition rather than a whole transplant.**

**2.** Explain: why the manual studies probiotics through weight and glycaemia.
**Answer: Weight and glycaemia (blood sugar) can be measured. The manual's claim for probiotics is tested against those two measurable outcomes, so the effect is checked, not taken on trust.**
