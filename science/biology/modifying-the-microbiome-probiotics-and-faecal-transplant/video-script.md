Today: modifying the microbiome — probiotics and faecal transplant.

Remember this one thing: the micro-organisms living in and on you — your microbiote — are not fixed. They can be changed, and changing them can be a treatment.

France's manual teaches two ways. The first is faecal transplantation: the gut microbiote is transplanted, as a treatment, to rebuild a patient's own. The second is probiotics: live micro-organisms given on purpose, whose effects the manual studies on two measurable things — weight, and glycaemia, the blood sugar.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
