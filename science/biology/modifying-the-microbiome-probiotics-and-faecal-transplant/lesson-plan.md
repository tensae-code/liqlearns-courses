# Lesson plan — Modifying the microbiome — probiotics and faecal transplant

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Modifying the microbiome — probiotics and faecal transplant
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — appended to Unit 16 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the human microbiome can be deliberately modified as a treatment — France's manual teaches it in its "Microbiote humain et santé" (Human microbiote and health) chapter: faecal transplantation as a treatment, and probiotics — their effects on weight and on glycaemia (blood sugar) (France, Grade 10 — *SVT 2de GT — Manuel Libre de Sciences de la vie et de la Terre*, student manual, Thème 3 / "Microbiote humain et santé" — repository path as cited in the placements)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands beside the course's microbiome lessons — what the microbiote is, and what antibiotics do to it — and adds the half they do not teach: changing the microbiome on purpose, as medicine. Normal Microbiota, Germ Theory and Koch's Postulates is recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: **Normal Microbiota, Germ Theory and Koch's Postulates**. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say, in their own words, the one thing this lesson teaches: the microbiome can be modified as a treatment — by faecal transplantation and by probiotics.
2. Say what a faecal transplant is, as France's manual teaches it: a treatment that transplants the gut microbiote.
3. Say what probiotics are in the manual's teaching, and the two effects it studies: effects on weight and on glycaemia.
4. Say why modification belongs in the same chapter as the microbiote itself: you can only treat by changing a system you first understand.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.
