# Laboratory tools and what each one does — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: burner and hotplate.
**Answer: Bunsen or alcohol burners give flame for heating and sterilising; hotplates heat without a flame.**

**2.** Explain: autoclave and culturing.
**Answer: An autoclave sterilises media and samples with pressurised steam; culturing means growing micro-organisms in controlled conditions, used for penicillin, wine, beer, cheese and bio-fuel.**
