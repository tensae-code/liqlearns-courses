# Laboratory tools and what each one does — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about beaker and crucible?
a) Beakers hold solutions and water baths; crucibles withstand great heat
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Beakers hold solutions and water baths; crucibles withstand great heat**

**2.** What does this lesson say about measuring tools?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) pH meters measure acidity and thermometers measure temperature; forceps pick up small objects and spatulas lift small amounts
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) pH meters measure acidity and thermometers measure temperature; forceps pick up small objects and spatulas lift small amounts**

**3.** What does this lesson say about burner and hotplate?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Bunsen or alcohol burners give flame for heating and sterilising; hotplates heat without a flame
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Bunsen or alcohol burners give flame for heating and sterilising; hotplates heat without a flame**

**4.** What does this lesson say about autoclave and culturing?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) An autoclave sterilises media and samples with pressurised steam; culturing means growing micro-organisms in controlled conditions, used for penicillin, wine, beer, cheese and bio-fuel
**Answer: d) An autoclave sterilises media and samples with pressurised steam; culturing means growing micro-organisms in controlled conditions, used for penicillin, wine, beer, cheese and bio-fuel**
