Today: Laboratory tools and what each one does.

Remember this one thing: each laboratory tool has its own job — holding, heating, measuring, sterilising or culturing.

Beakers hold solutions and water baths; crucibles withstand great heat.

pH meters measure acidity and thermometers measure temperature; forceps pick up small objects and spatulas lift small amounts.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
