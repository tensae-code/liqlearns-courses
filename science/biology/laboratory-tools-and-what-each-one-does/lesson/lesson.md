# Laboratory tools and what each one does

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Laboratory tools and what each one does. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
A good biologist knows the bench tools by name and by job, and keeps living cultures clean and controlled.

[Picture 29205 — a labelled laboratory bench]

## The key facts
**Beaker and crucible.** Beakers hold solutions and water baths; crucibles withstand great heat.

**Measuring tools.** pH meters measure acidity and thermometers measure temperature; forceps pick up small objects and spatulas lift small amounts.

**Burner and hotplate.** Bunsen or alcohol burners give flame for heating and sterilising; hotplates heat without a flame.

**Autoclave and culturing.** An autoclave sterilises media and samples with pressurised steam; culturing means growing micro-organisms in controlled conditions, used for penicillin, wine, beer, cheese and bio-fuel.
