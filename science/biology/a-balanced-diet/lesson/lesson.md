# A balanced diet — every group, in proportion

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — A balanced diet — every group, in proportion. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Ethiopia's many cuisines differ in flavour and ingredients — any cuisine can be balanced if the proportions are right.

[Picture 21056 — an Ethiopian plate arranged by nutrient group]

## The key facts
**Definition.** A balanced diet contains all nutrient groups in the right proportions for the person eating it.

**Any cuisine.** Any cuisine can be balanced if the proportions are right.

**Auditing meals.** Pupils audit real meals, including vegetarian meals, against the nutrient groups.

**Proportions.** Balance is about proportions, not about one fixed menu.
