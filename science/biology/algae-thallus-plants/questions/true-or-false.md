# Algae — thallus plants — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. A thallus is a plant body that is not divided into root, stem and leaves. The whole body does the work of living — taking in water and light and making food — without separate organs for each job.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Bulgaria's Pedagog 6 biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Биология и здравно образование 7. клас (Pedagog 6), printed p. 33).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with algae — thallus plants.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is algae — thallus plants.
