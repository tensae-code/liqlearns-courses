# Hands-on — Algae — thallus plants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: algae are thallus plants: their whole body is a thallus — one simple plant body that is not divided into root, stem and leaves — the simplest body plan in the plant kingdom, set against the cormus plants whose bodies are built of tissues and true organs.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: two plants side by side: a flat green alga labelled as one thallus body, and a flowering plant labelled root, stem and leaves. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
