# Algae — thallus plants — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (algae are thallus plants: their whole body is a thallus — one simple plant body that is not divided into root, stem and leaves — the simplest body plan in the plant kingdom, set against the cormus plants whose bodies are built of tissues and true organs) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
