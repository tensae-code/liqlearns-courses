# Meristems and plant growth curves — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: a plant grows from its meristems — the tissues of dividing cells at root and shoot tips — and its growth, measured over time, draws curves: the sigmoid S-curve of steady growth and the stepped curve of growth that comes in stages. Meristematic tissue is the plant's growth tissue: cells that keep dividing. Apical meristems at shoot and root tips lengthen the plant (primary growth); lateral meristems thicken it (secondary growth).**

**2.** Explain Malaysia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Malaysia's KSSM Form 5 book teaches meristems and growth curves together at the start of its flowering-plant theme (Ch.1, printed p. 6): the tissue that grows, then the curves that measure the growing — the sigmoid and the stepped curve side by side.**
