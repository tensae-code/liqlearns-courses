# Meristems and plant growth curves — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Meristematic tissue is the plant's growth tissue: cells that keep dividing. Apical meristems at shoot and root tips lengthen the plant (primary growth); lateral meristems thicken it (secondary growth).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Malaysia's KSSM biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Biology Form 5 (DLP), Ch.1, printed p. 6).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with meristems and plant growth curves.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is meristems and plant growth curves.
