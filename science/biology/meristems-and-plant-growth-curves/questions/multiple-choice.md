# Meristems and plant growth curves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) a plant grows from its meristems — the tissues of dividing cells at root and shoot tips — and its growth, measured over time, draws curves: the sigmoid S-curve of steady growth and the stepped curve of growth that comes in stages  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
**Answer: a) a plant grows from its meristems — the tissues of dividing cells at root and shoot tips — and its growth, measured over time, draws curves: the sigmoid S-curve of steady growth and the stepped curve of growth that comes in stages**

**2. Meristems — which statement is right?**
a) Meristematic tissue is the plant's growth tissue: cells that keep dividing. Apical meristems at shoot and root tips lengthen the plant (primary growth); lateral meristems thicken it (secondary growth).  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) Meristematic tissue is the plant's growth tissue: cells that keep dividing. Apical meristems at shoot and root tips lengthen the plant (primary growth); lateral meristems thicken it (secondary growth).**

**3. Growth measured — which statement is right?**
a) It is unrelated to the lesson's topic  b) Growth is measured as increase — in length, mass or number — over time, and plotted as a growth curve, so the rate of growth can be read from the graph's slope.  c) It is a myth the book corrects  d) It applies only outside Malaysia
**Answer: b) Growth is measured as increase — in length, mass or number — over time, and plotted as a growth curve, so the rate of growth can be read from the graph's slope.**
