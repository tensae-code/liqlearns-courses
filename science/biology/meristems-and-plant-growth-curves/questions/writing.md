# Meristems and plant growth curves — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: a plant grows from its meristems — the tissues of dividing cells at root and shoot tips — and its growth, measured over time, draws curves: the sigmoid S-curve of steady growth and the stepped curve of growth that comes in stages. Growth is measured as increase — in length, mass or number — over time, and plotted as a growth curve, so the rate of growth can be read from the graph's slope.**
