Today: Meristems and plant growth curves.

Remember this one thing: a plant grows from its meristems — the tissues of dividing cells at root and shoot tips — and its growth, measured over time, draws curves: the sigmoid S-curve of steady growth and the stepped curve of growth that comes in stages.

Malaysia's Form 5 book opens its plant theme with meristematic tissues and growth (printed p. 6): where a plant's new cells are made, and how growth is measured — as curves. Growth here is not a vague 'getting bigger' but a quantity that can be plotted.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
