# Hands-on — Meristems and plant growth curves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: a plant grows from its meristems — the tissues of dividing cells at root and shoot tips — and its growth, measured over time, draws curves: the sigmoid S-curve of steady growth and the stepped curve of growth that comes in stages.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a shoot tip and root tip with their meristem regions shaded, beside two graphs: a smooth S-shaped sigmoid growth curve and a stepped growth curve rising in stairs. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
