# Meristems and plant growth curves — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (a plant grows from its meristems — the tissues of dividing cells at root and shoot tips — and its growth, measured over time, draws curves: the sigmoid S-curve of steady growth and the stepped curve of growth that comes in stages) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
