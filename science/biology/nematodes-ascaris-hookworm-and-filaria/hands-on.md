# Hands-on — Nematodes — Ascaris, hookworm and filaria

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Thread a string through a drinking straw: the string running the whole length is the nematode's complete gut — in at the mouth, out at the anus — the step up from the flatworm's one-opening gut in the lesson before.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — a cylindrical nematode drawn with its complete gut tube running mouth to anus, beside three labelled panels: Ascaris the big roundworm, the hookworm with its curved toothed head fastened to a gut wall, and a mosquito above a swollen leg for the filaria — label its parts, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
