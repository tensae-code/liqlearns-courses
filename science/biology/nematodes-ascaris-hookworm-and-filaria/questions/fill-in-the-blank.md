# Nematodes — Ascaris, hookworm and filaria — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The tough outer skin of a nematode is called the _____.**
**Answer: cuticle**

**2. The hookworm genus taught in the module is _____.**
**Answer: Ancilostoma**

**3. The filaria's scientific name is Wuchereria _____.**
**Answer: bancrofti**

**4. Ascaris is called the _____ in Portuguese — the big roundworm.**
**Answer: lombriga**
