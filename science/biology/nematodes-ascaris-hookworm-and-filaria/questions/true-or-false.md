# Nematodes — Ascaris, hookworm and filaria — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Nematodes are pseudocoelomate — they have a false body cavity.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. The hookworm's head is curved and carries teeth for its blood-sucking life.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Ascaris never causes symptoms in the lungs.**
**Answer: False** — its symptoms include cough, fever and lung lesions.

**4. A mosquito carries the filaria between people.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
