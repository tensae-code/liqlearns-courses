# Nematodes — Ascaris, hookworm and filaria — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A nematode's gut is…**
a) a cavity with one opening  b) a complete tube with mouth and anus  c) absent — it absorbs through the skin  d) a gastrovascular cavity
**Answer: b) a complete tube with mouth and anus**

**2. Hookworm larvae enter the human body by…**
a) the mosquito's bite  b) penetrating the skin  c) drinking water only  d) the lungs first
**Answer: b) penetrating the skin**

**3. Wuchereria bancrofti adults live in the…**
a) liver  b) lymph vessels  c) kidneys  d) brain
**Answer: b) lymph vessels**

**4. The filaria's larvae appear in the peripheral blood…**
a) at midday  b) in the morning  c) at night  d) only in children
**Answer: c) at night**
