# Nematodes — Ascaris, hookworm and filaria — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Nematodes are cylindrical pseudocoelomate worms with a complete gut — mouth and anus — and a tough cuticle; the module works three parasites one by one: Ascaris, the hookworm that sucks blood and enters through the skin, and Wuchereria bancrofti, the filaria carried by mosquitoes whose larvae appear in the peripheral blood at night.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by giving each parasite its route without looking — Ascaris by its cycle to the gut, hookworm larvae through the skin, filaria by mosquito to the lymph vessels — and the filaria's night-time blood appearance.
