# Hands-on — Biological weapons — and the ban

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: biological weapons are pathogenic bacteria, viruses and toxins used to harm people, and the world has banned them — the 1972 Convention, which China joined in 1984, forbids developing, producing or stockpiling them, a position China reaffirms: never develop, produce or stockpile, and oppose proliferation.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a row of sealed hazard vessels crossed by a bold prohibition sign, beside a scroll representing the 1972 Convention. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
