# Biological weapons — and the ban

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Biological weapons — and the ban. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Biological weapons are pathogenic bacteria, viruses and toxins used to harm people, and the world has banned them — the 1972 Convention, which China joined in 1984, forbids developing, producing or stockpiling them, a position China reaffirms: never develop, produce or stockpile, and oppose proliferation. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from China's People's Education Press biology textbooks, which teach it as a named topic.
