# Biological weapons — and the ban — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: biological weapons are pathogenic bacteria, viruses and toxins used to harm people, and the world has banned them — the 1972 Convention, which China joined in 1984, forbids developing, producing or stockpiling them, a position China reaffirms: never develop, produce or stockpile, and oppose proliferation. what they are (pathogenic bacteria, viruses, toxins) and how they spread; the history China teaches from its own wounds — Unit 731 and Unit 100, germ warfare across more than 20 regions, the 1950–52 allegations and the anti-germ-warfare campaign, the 2001 anthrax letters; gene technology's new dangers (engineered pathogens, ethnic susceptibility claims) and the standing smallpox stocks; the 1972 Convention, China's accession in 1984, and its reaffirmed position — never develop, produce or stockpile, and oppose proliferation.**
