Remember this one thing: biological weapons are pathogenic bacteria, viruses and toxins used to harm people, and the world has banned them — the 1972 Convention, which China joined in 1984, forbids developing, producing or stockpiling them, a position China reaffirms: never develop, produce or stockpile, and oppose proliferation.
