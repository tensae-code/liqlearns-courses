# Biological weapons — and the ban — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (biological weapons are pathogenic bacteria, viruses and toxins used to harm people, and the world has banned them — the 1972 Convention, which China joined in 1984, forbids developing, producing or stockpiling them, a position China reaffirms: never develop, produce or stockpile, and oppose proliferation) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
