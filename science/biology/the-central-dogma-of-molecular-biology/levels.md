# The central dogma of molecular biology — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (the central dogma states the flow of genetic information: DNA is transcribed into RNA and RNA is translated into protein, while DNA copies itself — information flows from the archive to the working copy to the machine — and Greece's Grade 12 volume opens its molecular genetics with it) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
