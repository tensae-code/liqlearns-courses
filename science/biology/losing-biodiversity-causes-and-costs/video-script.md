# Video script — Losing biodiversity — causes and costs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Biodiversity is lost mainly through habitat loss and fragmentation (named the most important cause), overexploitation, pollution and invasive species; the loss lowers ecosystem productivity and the services people depend on — food, water, fertile soil, pest control, building materials and medicines — and deforestation is pictured as a leading driver.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
