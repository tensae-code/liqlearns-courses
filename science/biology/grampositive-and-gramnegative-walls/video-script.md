# Video script — Gram-positive and Gram-negative walls

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Gram-positive and gram-negative walls are compared in a table and figured at peptidoglycan level (glycan backbone plus tetrapeptide side chains), with arrows marking where penicillin interferes with cross-bridge linkage; wall-less forms are distinguished (protoplast — one membrane; spheroplast — two membranes), and wall behaviour in hypertonic surroundings (plasmolysis in walled cells vs crenation in wall-less cells) is explained.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
