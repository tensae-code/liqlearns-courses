# Hands-on — Disorders of the muscular system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: the muscular system has its own named disorders — strains and cramps from overuse and diseases that waste or weaken the muscle fibres themselves — taught in Greece's Grade 10 book as their own section, with care and prevention.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a runner holding a cramped calf muscle, beside a small panel of a weakened arm muscle, each with its plain label. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
