# Stomata — opening, closing and transpiration — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The two cells that work a stoma are called _____ cells.**
**Answer: guard**

**2. A swollen, water-full guard cell is described as _____.**
**Answer: turgid**

**3. The green plastid where photosynthesis runs is the _____.**
**Answer: chloroplast**

**4. Plastids occur only in _____ organisms.**
**Answer: photosynthetic**
