# Stomata — opening, closing and transpiration — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Guard cells close the stoma when they swell with water.**
**Answer: False** — swollen turgid guard cells open the stoma; loss of water closes it.

**2. Plastids have a double membrane.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Transpiration is the plant's loss of water vapour.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. Chloroplasts are found in animal cells too.**
**Answer: False** — plastids occur only in photosynthetic organisms.
