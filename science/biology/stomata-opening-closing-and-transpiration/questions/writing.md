# Stomata — opening, closing and transpiration — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining the bargain a leaf makes every time it opens its stomata.
**Model answer: A leaf opens its stomata to take in the carbon dioxide photosynthesis needs. But the same open pore lets water vapour escape — transpiration. So the leaf is always trading: gas in for making food, water out as the cost, and the guard cells open and close the pore to strike the bargain hour by hour.**
