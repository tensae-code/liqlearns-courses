# Stomata — opening, closing and transpiration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A stoma opens when its guard cells…**
a) die  b) take up water and become turgid  c) divide  d) make seeds
**Answer: b) take up water and become turgid**

**2. The water vapour lost through open stomata is called…**
a) respiration  b) transpiration  c) germination  d) pollination
**Answer: b) transpiration**

**3. Plastids are found…**
a) in all cells  b) only in photosynthetic organisms  c) only in roots  d) only in animals
**Answer: b) only in photosynthetic organisms**

**4. The pore of the stoma is also called the…**
a) ostiole  b) radula  c) osculum  d) capsule
**Answer: a) ostiole**
