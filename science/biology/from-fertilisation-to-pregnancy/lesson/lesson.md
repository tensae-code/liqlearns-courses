# From fertilisation to pregnancy

## Do it first

## The idea
Twins come in two kinds: identical from one fertilised egg splitting, fraternal from two eggs fertilised separately.

## The key facts
**Fertilisation.** Fertilisation is one sperm fusing with the egg, usually in the oviduct.

**Journey.** The fertilised egg divides as it travels toward the uterus.

**Implantation.** The dividing ball implants in the prepared uterus lining and develops through pregnancy to birth.

**Twins.** Identical twins come from one fertilised egg splitting; fraternal twins from two eggs fertilised separately.
