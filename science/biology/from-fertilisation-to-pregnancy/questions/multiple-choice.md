# From fertilisation to pregnancy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about fertilisation?
a) Fertilisation is one sperm fusing with the egg, usually in the oviduct
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Fertilisation is one sperm fusing with the egg, usually in the oviduct**

**2.** What does this lesson say about journey?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) The fertilised egg divides as it travels toward the uterus
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The fertilised egg divides as it travels toward the uterus**

**3.** What does this lesson say about implantation?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) The dividing ball implants in the prepared uterus lining and develops through pregnancy to birth
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) The dividing ball implants in the prepared uterus lining and develops through pregnancy to birth**

**4.** What does this lesson say about twins?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Identical twins come from one fertilised egg splitting; fraternal twins from two eggs fertilised separately
**Answer: d) Identical twins come from one fertilised egg splitting; fraternal twins from two eggs fertilised separately**
