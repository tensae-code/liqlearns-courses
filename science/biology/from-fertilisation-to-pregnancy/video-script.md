# Video script — From fertilisation to pregnancy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: From fertilisation to pregnancy.

Remember this one thing: one sperm fusing with the egg begins pregnancy; the dividing ball implants in the uterus lining.

Fertilisation is one sperm fusing with the egg, usually in the oviduct.

The fertilised egg divides as it travels toward the uterus.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
