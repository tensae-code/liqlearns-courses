# Hands-on — Body cavities — acoelomate, pseudocoelomate, coelomate

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Draw the three cross-sections from memory — solid, false cavity, true coelom — and under each write one phylum that sits on that rung. Check your ladder against the lesson and fix any rung you placed wrong.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of the three body plans cut across — three worm-like bodies in cross-section: the first solid inside with the gut in packed tissue, the second with an unlined space around the gut, the third with the gut and organs lying in a lined cavity — each labelled acoelomate, pseudocoelomate, coelomate — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
