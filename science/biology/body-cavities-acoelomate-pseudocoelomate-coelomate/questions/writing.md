# Body cavities — acoelomate, pseudocoelomate, coelomate — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining body cavities — acoelomate, pseudocoelomate, coelomate, using the lesson's key facts.
**Model answer: Animals are sorted by the cavity inside the body wall: acoelomates have no cavity — the gut sits in solid tissue; pseudocoelomates have a cavity not lined by mesoderm; coelomates have a true coelom, a cavity fully lined by mesoderm, in which the organs lie and move. In acoelomate animals there is no body cavity at all: the space between the gut and the body wall is packed solid with tissue. The flatworms are the standing example — flat, with no room inside for organs to lie in a fluid-filled space, and every cell kept near the surface or the gut. The ladder is the organising spine of the Year 13 book's whole Animalia survey (§3.2.6): diploblastic animals and Cnidaria, then the triploblastic acoelomates, then the pseudocoelomate phyla — Rotifera, Nematomorpha, Loricifera, Acanthocephala among them — then the coelomates phylum by phylum. Learn the three rungs and the survey's order explains itself.**
