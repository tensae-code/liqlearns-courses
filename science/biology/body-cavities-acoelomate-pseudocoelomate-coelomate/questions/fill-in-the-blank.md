# Body cavities — acoelomate, pseudocoelomate, coelomate — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. An animal whose body cavity is a true cavity fully lined by mesoderm is called a _____.**
**Answer: coelomate**

**2. A flatworm, with no body cavity at all, is an _____.**
**Answer: acoelomate**
