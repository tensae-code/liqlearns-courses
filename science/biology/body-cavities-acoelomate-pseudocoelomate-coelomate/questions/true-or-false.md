# Body cavities — acoelomate, pseudocoelomate, coelomate — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** In acoelomate animals there is no body cavity at all: the space between the gut and the body wall is packed solid with tissue.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Pseudocoelomate — a false cavity is the opposite of what this lesson teaches.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches this as stated.**

**3.** In coelomate animals the cavity is a true coelom: a space fully lined by mesoderm, holding the organs in fluid so they can move, grow and work partly independently of the body wall.
**Answer: True**

**4.** This lesson's topic plays no part in biology at all.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
