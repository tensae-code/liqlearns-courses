# Body cavities — acoelomate, pseudocoelomate, coelomate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about body cavities — acoelomate, pseudocoelomate, coelomate?
a) Animals are sorted by the cavity inside the body wall: acoelomates have no cavity — the gut sits in solid tissue; pseudocoelomates have a cavity not lined by mesoderm; coelomates have a true coelom, a cavity fully lined by mesoderm, in which the organs lie and move.
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Animals are sorted by the cavity inside the body wall: acoelomates have no cavity — the gut sits in solid tissue; pseudocoelomates have a cavity not lined by mesoderm; coelomates have a true coelom, a cavity fully lined by mesoderm, in which the organs lie and move.**

**2.** What does this lesson say about acoelomate — no cavity?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) In acoelomate animals there is no body cavity at all: the space between the gut and the body wall is packed solid with tissue.
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) In acoelomate animals there is no body cavity at all: the space between the gut and the body wall is packed solid with tissue.**

**3.** What does this lesson say about pseudocoelomate — a false cavity?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Pseudocoelomate animals have a cavity between gut and body wall, but it is not lined all round by mesoderm tissue — a false coelom.
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Pseudocoelomate animals have a cavity between gut and body wall, but it is not lined all round by mesoderm tissue — a false coelom.**

**4.** What does this lesson say about coelomate — the true coelom?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) In coelomate animals the cavity is a true coelom: a space fully lined by mesoderm, holding the organs in fluid so they can move, grow and work partly independently of the body wall.
**Answer: d) In coelomate animals the cavity is a true coelom: a space fully lined by mesoderm, holding the organs in fluid so they can move, grow and work partly independently of the body wall.**
