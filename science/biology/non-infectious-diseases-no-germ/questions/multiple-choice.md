# Non-infectious diseases — no germ involved — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about no organism?
a) Non-infectious diseases are not caused by organisms
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Non-infectious diseases are not caused by organisms**

**2.** What does this lesson say about named groups?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Malnutrition diseases include kwashiorkor, scurvy, night blindness and marasmus; smoke causes lung cancer and chronic bronchitis
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Malnutrition diseases include kwashiorkor, scurvy, night blindness and marasmus; smoke causes lung cancer and chronic bronchitis**

**3.** What does this lesson say about inherited and other?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Sickle cell anaemia, cancer, allergies, diabetes and podoconiosis are named non-infectious examples
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Sickle cell anaemia, cancer, allergies, diabetes and podoconiosis are named non-infectious examples**

**4.** What does this lesson say about contrast?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Non-infectious diseases differ from infectious diseases because no germ is involved
**Answer: d) Non-infectious diseases differ from infectious diseases because no germ is involved**
