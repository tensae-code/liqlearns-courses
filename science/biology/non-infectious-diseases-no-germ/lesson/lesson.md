# Non-infectious diseases — no germ involved

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Non-infectious diseases — no germ involved. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Named examples include sickle cell anaemia, cancer, allergies, diabetes and podoconiosis.

[Picture 21071 — four cause panels — nutrient, smoke, inheritance, malfunction]

## The key facts
**No organism.** Non-infectious diseases are not caused by organisms.

**Named groups.** Malnutrition diseases include kwashiorkor, scurvy, night blindness and marasmus; smoke causes lung cancer and chronic bronchitis.

**Inherited and other.** Sickle cell anaemia, cancer, allergies, diabetes and podoconiosis are named non-infectious examples.

**Contrast.** Non-infectious diseases differ from infectious diseases because no germ is involved.
