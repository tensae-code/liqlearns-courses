Today: Non-infectious diseases — no germ involved.

Remember this one thing: non-infectious diseases are not caused by organisms — they come from missing nutrients, smoke, inheritance or body malfunction.

Non-infectious diseases are not caused by organisms.

Malnutrition diseases include kwashiorkor, scurvy, night blindness and marasmus; smoke causes lung cancer and chronic bronchitis.
