# Collecting in the field — nets — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about insect net?
a) An insect net is a cloth or fine-mesh bag on a wire loop and a pole
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) An insect net is a cloth or fine-mesh bag on a wire loop and a pole**

**2.** What does this lesson say about gentle catching?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) An insect net catches insects without crushing them
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) An insect net catches insects without crushing them**

**3.** What does this lesson say about fishing net?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
