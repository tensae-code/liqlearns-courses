Structural summary: A field-day pack built on five stations, each with a teacher info page, a pupil observation form and group reflection questions: (1) pollinators and insect hotels, (2) deadwood (lahopuu), (3) water bodies, (4) invasive alien species, (5) traditional biotopes (perinnebiotoopit), closing with a 1 m² quadrat species survey and whole-group discussion. Framing chapters cover biodiversity loss and its causes, and ecosystem services in three groups (provisioning, regulating/supporting, cultural). Field ethics are explicit: handle organisms gently, release everything back, respect everyman's rights (jokaisenoikeudet). Finland's way: biodiversity is learned standing in it — counting, catching and releasing — in the pupil's own near environment, not from species lists.
