Grade: grade-03 as filed (material is for school field days, primary/lower-secondary; designed for the Hyria natural-resources campus but usable anywhere) — Kind: teacher field-work pack (info pages + pupil field forms + discussion tasks) — NOT a textbook, as verified — Repo path: grade-03/gov/finland/biology/teacher/finland-g03-aoe6014-tutki-luonnon-monimuotoisuutta.pdf (GitHub Release mid-size-books-5) — Byte size: 5,668,126 (matches record) — PDF pages: 22 — Printed-page offset: 0 (no printed folios). Verdict: COMPLETE (all 22 pages read, ending with the Hyria campus map appendix). Structural summary: A field-day pack built on five stations, each with a teacher info page, a pupil observation form and group reflection questions: (1) pollinators and insect hotels, (2) deadwood (lahopuu), (3) water bodies, (4) invasive alien species, (5) traditional biotopes (perinnebiotoopit), closing with a 1 m² quadrat species survey and whole-group discussion. Framing chapters cover biodiversity loss and its causes, and ecosystem services in three groups (provisioning, regulating/supporting, cultural). Field ethics are explicit: handle organisms gently, release everything back, respect everyman's rights (jokaisenoikeudet). Finland's way: biodiversity is learned standing in it — counting, catching and releasing — in the pupil's own near environment, not from species lists.
