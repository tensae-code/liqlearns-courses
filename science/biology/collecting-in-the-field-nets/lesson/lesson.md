## The idea
Biology also happens outdoors. The collector's nets are made to catch living things gently.

## The key facts
**Insect net.** An insect net is a cloth or fine-mesh bag on a wire loop and a pole.

**Gentle catching.** An insect net catches insects without crushing them.

**Fishing net.** Fishing nets of woven fibre are used for collecting in water.

**Field work.** Field tools are used outside the laboratory to collect specimens.
