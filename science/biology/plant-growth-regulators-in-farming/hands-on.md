# Hands-on — Plant growth regulators in farming

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: plant growth regulators are artificial partners to the plant hormones — in China regulated as pesticides under rules from 1989 onward, sold only when registered — and each farming case, from gibberellin making barley produce amylase to fruit expanders and sprout suppressants, is judged on its benefit and its health questions.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a market stall of large glossy fruit beside a smaller flavourful one, with a registration label held up between them. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
