# Plant growth regulators in farming — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: plant growth regulators are artificial partners to the plant hormones — in China regulated as pesticides under rules from 1989 onward, sold only when registered — and each farming case, from gibberellin making barley produce amylase to fruit expanders and sprout suppressants, is judged on its benefit and its health questions. in China these are regulated as pesticides (rules of 1989, 1997, 2011) and only registered products may be sold; cases for evaluation include gibberellin making barley produce amylase without germinating, fruit "expanders" that swell size and thin flavour, and sprout suppressants with health questions; the pre-experiment （预实验） named as method; and two student inquiries — the best auxin-like concentration for rooting cuttings (soak against dip methods), and ethephon ripening of market fruit, with the seller's and the consumer's views set against each other.**
