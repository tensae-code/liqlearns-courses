Today: Plant growth regulators in farming.

Remember this one thing: plant growth regulators are artificial partners to the plant hormones — in China regulated as pesticides under rules from 1989 onward, sold only when registered — and each farming case, from gibberellin making barley produce amylase to fruit expanders and sprout suppressants, is judged on its benefit and its health questions.
