# Acceleration — earlier growth and maturation

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Acceleration — earlier growth and maturation. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Children in recent generations grow taller and mature faster than earlier generations — acceleration — read off height-by-age graphs for boys and girls: a change in the pace of development itself, not in its stages. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from Kazakhstan's Grade 7–9 biology books, which teach it as a named topic.
