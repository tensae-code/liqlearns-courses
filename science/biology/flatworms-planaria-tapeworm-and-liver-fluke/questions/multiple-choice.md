# Flatworms — planaria, tapeworm and liver fluke — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A flatworm's gut has…**
a) a mouth and a separate anus  b) a single opening  c) no opening at all  d) three openings
**Answer: b) a single opening**

**2. In the tapeworm cycle as the module teaches it, the human is the…**
a) intermediate host  b) definitive host  c) vector  d) first larval stage
**Answer: b) definitive host**

**3. Fasciola, the liver fluke, passes through which hosts?**
a) fish and humans  b) sheep and snails  c) pigs and mosquitoes  d) birds and cattle
**Answer: b) sheep and snails**

**4. Which is one of the module's prophylaxis rules against the liver fluke?**
a) boil all drinking milk  b) keep cattle faeces out of water  c) sleep under a bed net  d) drain all ponds
**Answer: b) keep cattle faeces out of water**
