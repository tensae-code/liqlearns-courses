# Flatworms — planaria, tapeworm and liver fluke — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Flatworms are hermaphrodites — both sexes in one body.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Flatworms have a body cavity (coelom) around the gut.**
**Answer: False** — they are acoelomate — no body cavity.

**3. Abdominal pain, fever and weight loss are among the liver fluke's symptoms.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. The planarian is a parasite of sheep.**
**Answer: False** — the planarian is the free-living example; Fasciola is the sheep parasite.
