# Flatworms — planaria, tapeworm and liver fluke — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Give the flatworm body plan in your own words.
**Answer: A flatworm is flattened from back to belly (dorsoventrally), is bilateral and acoelomate, has three germ layers, a gut with a single opening, and both sexes in one body — a hermaphrodite.**

**2.** Explain Mozambique's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Mozambique's PESD II animal module (Módulo 3 de Biologia), Lessons 4–5, teaches phylum Platyhelminthes by its plan — flattened, bilateral, acoelomate, three germ layers, single gut opening, hermaphroditism — then works the planarian's anatomy, the Taenia tapeworm cycle to the human definitive host, and the Fasciola liver fluke cycle through sheep and snail hosts with symptoms and prophylaxis.**
