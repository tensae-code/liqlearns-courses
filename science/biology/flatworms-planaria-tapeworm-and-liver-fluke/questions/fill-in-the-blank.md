# Flatworms — planaria, tapeworm and liver fluke — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Flatworms are flattened from back to belly — _____ flattened.**
**Answer: dorsoventrally**

**2. The tapeworm genus taught in the module is _____.**
**Answer: Taenia**

**3. The liver fluke's scientific name is _____.**
**Answer: Fasciola**

**4. Washing aquatic _____ is one rule that breaks the fluke cycle.**
**Answer: vegetables**
