# Ethiopian taxonomists and their work

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Ethiopian taxonomists and their work. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Ethiopia's biodiversity is still under-studied, partly for want of trained taxonomists. This lesson profiles Ethiopians who do that work.

[Picture 21024 — a botanist pressing a plant specimen in the field in Ethiopia]

## The key facts
**Under-studied.** Ethiopia's rich biodiversity is still under-studied, partly for want of trained taxonomists.

**The work.** Taxonomists identify, name and classify species.

**Endemics.** The work matters greatly for a country with so many endemic species.

**Profile.** The section profiles Ethiopians who have classified the country's species.
