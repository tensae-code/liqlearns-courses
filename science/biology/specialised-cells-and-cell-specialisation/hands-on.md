# Hands-on — Specialised cells and cell specialisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Gather or draw two cell kinds from the lesson — a cheek cell and a ciliated cell. Lay the drawings side by side, name each one, and point to the structure that tells them apart.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two cells side by side as seen under a microscope — a flat cheek cell and a ciliated cell with a fringe of cilia and a layer of mucus above it — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
