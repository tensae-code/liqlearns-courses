# Specialised cells and cell specialisation — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A specialised cell's structure is fitted to one particular job.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Cheek cells carry cilia.
**Answer: False** — the ciliated cell carries cilia; the cheek cell does not.

**3.** Mucus in the trachea traps dust and microbes.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Specialisation has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
