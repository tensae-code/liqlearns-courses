# Specialised cells and cell specialisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about a specialised cell?
a) A specialised cell is a cell whose structure is fitted to one particular job
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) A specialised cell is a cell whose structure is fitted to one particular job**

**2.** What does this lesson say about cheek cells and ciliated cells?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) They are distinguished by their structure: the ciliated cell carries cilia, the cheek cell does not
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) They are distinguished by their structure: the ciliated cell carries cilia, the cheek cell does not**

**3.** What does this lesson say about cilia and mucus in the trachea?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Mucus traps dust and microbes, and the beating cilia sweep the mucus along, away from the lungs
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Mucus traps dust and microbes, and the beating cilia sweep the mucus along, away from the lungs**

**4.** What does this lesson say about the advantage of specialisation?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Division of labour: each cell does one job well, so the whole organism works more efficiently
**Answer: d) Division of labour: each cell does one job well, so the whole organism works more efficiently**
