# Specialised cells and cell specialisation — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: cilia and mucus in the trachea.
**Answer: The trachea is lined with ciliated cells and mucus: the mucus traps dust and microbes, and the beating cilia sweep the mucus along, away from the lungs.**

**2.** Explain: why specialisation helps.
**Answer: Division of labour: when each cell does one job and does it well, the whole organism works more efficiently than if every cell tried to do everything.**
