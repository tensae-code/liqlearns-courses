# Specialised cells and cell specialisation — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences comparing a cheek cell and a ciliated cell, and say what the ciliated cell's cilia do in the trachea.
**Model answer: A cheek cell is a flat cell with no cilia, while a ciliated cell carries a fringe of tiny hairs called cilia, and the two are told apart by that structure. In the trachea, the cilia beat in waves and sweep the mucus — which traps dust and microbes — along, away from the lungs.**
