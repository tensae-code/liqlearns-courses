# Video script — Resistance — evolution in action

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Resistance — evolution in action.

Remember this one thing: Evolution you can watch within a lifetime: Kenya's Form 4 book teaches resistance — penicillin-breaking enzymes in bacteria, DDT-resistant mosquitoes, Plasmodium changing its coat antigens and resisting chloroquine, drug-resistant TB — as natural selection observed in action, in Kenyan health and agriculture.

Most evolution is too slow to watch. Resistance is not. Spray DDT and the few mosquitoes born resistant survive and inherit the field. Dose penicillin and the bacteria that make a penicillin-breaking enzyme are the ones left standing. Chloroquine meets a Plasmodium that changes its coat antigens and resists the drug; TB treatment meets bacteria that withstand the medicines. Kenya teaches all four as one lesson inside evolution: variation already present, selection applied by our own chemicals, and the resistant line taking over — Darwin's mechanism running at a speed a single generation of students can measure.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
