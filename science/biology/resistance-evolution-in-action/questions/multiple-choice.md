# Resistance — evolution in action — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Resistance develops because…**
a) the drug teaches each insect to resist  b) resistant individuals survive the drug and breed  c) the drug mutates every survivor the same way  d) resistant pests immigrate only
**Answer: b) resistant individuals survive the drug and breed**

**2. Penicillin-resistant bacteria survive by…**
a) hiding in bone  b) making enzymes that break penicillin down  c) stopping all growth  d) changing into viruses
**Answer: b) making enzymes that break penicillin down**

**3. Plasmodium resists chloroquine and also escapes immunity by…**
a) growing wings  b) changing its coat antigens  c) living in soil  d) killing the mosquito
**Answer: b) changing its coat antigens**

**4. Kenya teaches resistance inside which chapter?**
a) Genetics  b) Evolution  c) Transport  d) Reproduction
**Answer: b) Evolution — selection in action**
