# Resistance — evolution in action — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Bacteria can defeat penicillin with penicillin-breaking _____.**
**Answer: enzymes**

**2. Mosquitoes have evolved resistance to the insecticide _____.**
**Answer: DDT**

**3. Plasmodium changes its coat _____ to escape.**
**Answer: antigens**

**4. Resistance is natural _____ in action.**
**Answer: selection**
