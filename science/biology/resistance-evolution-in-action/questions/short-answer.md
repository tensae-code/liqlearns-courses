# Resistance — evolution in action — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain how a mosquito population becomes DDT-resistant.
**Answer: The population already varies: a few mosquitoes are born resistant. Spraying kills the susceptible majority; the resistant survivors breed, their offspring inherit resistance, and within generations the population is resistant — selection imposed by the insecticide (Ch. 2, printed p. 75).**

**2.** Explain Kenya's way of teaching resistance.
**Answer: Kenya gives resistance its own place in the evolution chapter as observable evolution: four working cases — penicillin-breaking enzymes, DDT-resistant mosquitoes, chloroquine-resistant Plasmodium changing its coat antigens, and drug-resistant TB — all drawn from Kenyan health and agriculture, where the students meet the problem outside school.**
