# Resistance — evolution in action — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Resistance is an example of natural selection that can be observed within years.**
**Answer: True** — the lesson's whole point.

**2. DDT creates resistance in a mosquito during its own lifetime.**
**Answer: False** — resistant mosquitoes are born, survive spraying, and breed.

**3. Drug-resistant TB is one of Kenya's resistance cases.**
**Answer: True** — with chloroquine-resistant Plasmodium.

**4. Penicillin-breaking enzymes are made by the patient, not the bacteria.**
**Answer: False** — the resistant bacteria make them.
