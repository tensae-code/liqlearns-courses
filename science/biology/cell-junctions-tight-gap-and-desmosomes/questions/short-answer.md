# Cell junctions — tight, gap and desmosomes — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: neighbouring cells are joined by long-lived junctions built from protein fibres — tight junctions, gap junctions and desmosomes — each kind joining cells in its own way. Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 1-4, printed pp. 76–77) teaches cell junctions as long-lived protein-fibre junctions between cells.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's new Grade 10 book teaches junctions comparatively (Lesson 1-4): a table to complete, then a labelling task on the digestive tract, so the three kinds are told apart by structure and place, not memorised as a list.**
