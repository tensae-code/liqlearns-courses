Today: Cell junctions — tight, gap and desmosomes.

Remember this one thing: neighbouring cells are joined by long-lived junctions built from protein fibres — tight junctions, gap junctions and desmosomes — each kind joining cells in its own way.

Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 1-4, printed pp. 76–77) teaches cell junctions as long-lived protein-fibre junctions between cells.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
