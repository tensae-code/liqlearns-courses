# Transport in the plant — xylem up, phloem both ways — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Water and minerals travel from root to stem to leaf in the xylem.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Phloem route has nothing to do with this lesson’s topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** The xylem route runs upward from the roots.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Both ways has nothing to do with this lesson’s topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
