# Transport in the plant — xylem up, phloem both ways — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about xylem route?
a) Water and minerals travel from root to stem to leaf in the xylem  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Water and minerals travel from root to stem to leaf in the xylem**

**2.** What does this lesson say about phloem route?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Food travels from the leaves to all parts of the plant in the phloem  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Food travels from the leaves to all parts of the plant in the phloem**

**3.** What does this lesson say about up in xylem?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) The xylem route runs upward from the roots  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) The xylem route runs upward from the roots**

**4.** What does this lesson say about both ways?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Phloem carries food to growing and storage parts, in the direction it is needed
**Answer: d) Phloem carries food to growing and storage parts, in the direction it is needed**
