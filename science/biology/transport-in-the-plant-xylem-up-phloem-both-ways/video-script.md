# Video script — Transport in the plant — xylem up, phloem both ways

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Transport in the plant — xylem up, phloem both ways.

Remember this one thing: water and minerals travel root to leaf in the xylem; food travels from the leaves to all parts in the phloem

Xylem route: Water and minerals travel from root to stem to leaf in the xylem.

Phloem route: Food travels from the leaves to all parts of the plant in the phloem.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
