## The idea
Roots take in water far from the leaves that need it, and leaves make food far from the roots that need feeding. Two tube systems solve both journeys.

## The key facts
**Xylem route.** Water and minerals travel from root to stem to leaf in the xylem.

**Phloem route.** Food travels from the leaves to all parts of the plant in the phloem.

**Up in xylem.** The xylem route runs upward from the roots.

**Both ways.** Phloem carries food to growing and storage parts, in the direction it is needed.
