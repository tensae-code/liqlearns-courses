# Incubation, hatching and caring for chicks

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — incubation, hatching and caring for chicks. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Incubation keeps eggs warm with body heat, helped by the female's brood patch of dense blood vessels; eggs are turned for even warmth; the chick cuts out with a temporary egg tooth after signalling its parents by sound; care ranges from mound chicks that dig themselves out and fend for themselves to species that feed and guard chicks for a long time, with the African paradise flycatcher pictured.
