# Cholera and typhoid — waterborne bacterial diseases — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Cholera and typhoid are both bacterial diseases.**
**Answer: True** — Vibrio cholerae and Salmonella typhi.

**2. Oral rehydration salts replace water and salts lost in cholera.**
**Answer: True** — the named treatment.

**3. Food-handler hygiene is part of typhoid control.**
**Answer: True** — with vaccination.

**4. Typhoid is spread only by mosquito bites.**
**Answer: False** — it travels through contaminated water and food.
