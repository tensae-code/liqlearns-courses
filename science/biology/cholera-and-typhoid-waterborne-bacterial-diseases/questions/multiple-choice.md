# Cholera and typhoid — waterborne bacterial diseases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Cholera is caused by…**
a) Salmonella typhi  b) Vibrio cholerae  c) Plasmodium  d) a virus
**Answer: b) Vibrio cholerae**

**2. The front-line treatment Kenya's book names for cholera is…**
a) antibiotics only  b) oral rehydration salts  c) blood transfusion  d) insulin
**Answer: b) oral rehydration salts**

**3. Typhoid is caused by…**
a) Vibrio cholerae  b) a fungus  c) Salmonella typhi  d) Entamoeba
**Answer: c) Salmonella typhi**

**4. A rural defence against both diseases is…**
a) shallow open pits  b) deep pit latrines  c) boiling soil  d) draining wells
**Answer: b) deep pit latrines**
