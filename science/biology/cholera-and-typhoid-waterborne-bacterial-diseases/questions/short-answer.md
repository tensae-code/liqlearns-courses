# Cholera and typhoid — waterborne bacterial diseases — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Give the cause and control of cholera as Kenya's Form 3 book teaches them.
**Answer: Cause: the bacterium Vibrio cholerae. Control and care: oral rehydration salts for the water and salts lost, and deep pit latrines in rural areas keeping waste away from drinking water (Ch. 2, printed p. 65).**

**2.** Explain Kenya's way of teaching cholera and typhoid.
**Answer: Kenya teaches the two together as the human bacterial diseases of its ecology chapter: each named by its bacterium — Vibrio cholerae, Salmonella typhi — then the defences: rehydration for cholera's losses, vaccination and food-handler hygiene against typhoid, and deep pit latrines as the shared sanitary foundation.**
