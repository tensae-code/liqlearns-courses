# Cholera and typhoid — waterborne bacterial diseases — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why a cheap packet of salts can be the difference between life and death in cholera.
**Model answer: Cholera kills by emptying the body of water and salts faster than they can be replaced by drinking alone, and the loss itself — not the bacterium's poison — is what collapses the patient. Oral rehydration salts put back exactly what is being lost, in the proportions the gut can absorb, so a packet and clean water hold the body up while the disease runs its course.**
