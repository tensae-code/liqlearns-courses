# Cholera and typhoid — waterborne bacterial diseases — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Cholera's bacterium is Vibrio _____.**
**Answer: cholerae**

**2. The salts given by mouth for cholera are oral _____ salts.**
**Answer: rehydration**

**3. Typhoid's bacterium is Salmonella _____.**
**Answer: typhi**

**4. Rural sanitation against cholera rests on deep _____ latrines.**
**Answer: pit**
