## The key facts
**The nephron.** The nephron is the kidney's working unit, inside its internal structure.

**Filtration.** Urine formation begins with filtration.

**Reabsorption and secretion.** Reabsorption and secretion follow filtration, in sequence.

**The journey.** Fluid travelling the tubule changes as useful substances return to the blood.
