# Hands-on — Methods of human genetics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: human genetics cannot make breeding experiments on people, so it reads heredity by its own methods — the genealogical method of pedigree charts, the twin method comparing identical and fraternal twins, and population methods — to find how traits and diseases are inherited.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a pedigree chart of squares and circles over three generations beside a pair of identical twins and a pair of fraternal twins, each pair with its comparison label. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
