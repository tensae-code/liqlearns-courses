# Hands-on — Alternation of generations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Draw the two circles of the lesson's picture from memory: moss and fern. On each circle mark where meiosis happens and where fertilisation happens, and shade the bigger generation in each. Check against the lesson and fix what you missed.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two circles side by side: the moss cycle with the green leafy gametophyte drawn large and the small sporophyte growing out of it, and the fern cycle with the large leafy sporophyte drawn large and the tiny heart-shaped gametophyte beneath — spores and gametes marked on each — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
