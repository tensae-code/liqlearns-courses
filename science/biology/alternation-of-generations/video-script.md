Today: Alternation of generations.

Remember this one thing: Plant life cycles alternate between two generations: the sporophyte (2n), which makes spores by meiosis, and the gametophyte (n), which makes gametes — in mosses the gametophyte is the main plant, in ferns the sporophyte is.

A spore plant lives twice over. One generation — the sporophyte — makes spores. The other — the gametophyte — makes gametes. They take turns, generation after generation.

In mosses the green gametophyte is the main plant and the sporophyte rides on it. In ferns it is the other way round: the big plant is the sporophyte, and the gametophyte is a tiny thing under it. That taking-turns is the alternation of generations.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
