# Alternation of generations — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences comparing the moss and fern life cycles.
**Model answer: In the moss, the green plant is the gametophyte and the sporophyte grows out of it, dependent on it, making spores in its capsule. In the fern, the large plant is the sporophyte and the gametophyte is reduced to a tiny prothallus that makes the gametes. Both cycles alternate the same two generations — in opposite proportions.**
