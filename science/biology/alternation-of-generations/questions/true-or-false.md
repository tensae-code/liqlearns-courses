# Alternation of generations — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Spores are produced by meiosis and are haploid.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. The gametophyte makes gametes by meiosis.**
**Answer: False** — the gametophyte is already haploid; it makes gametes by mitosis.

**3. The fern plant with fronds is the sporophyte.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. Fertilisation restores the diploid generation.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
