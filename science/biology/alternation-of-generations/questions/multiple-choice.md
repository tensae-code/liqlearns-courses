# Alternation of generations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What does the sporophyte generation produce?**
a) Gametes  b) Spores  c) Seeds  d) Flowers
**Answer: b) Spores**

**2. Which division makes the spores?**
a) Mitosis  b) Meiosis  c) Binary fission  d) Budding
**Answer: b) Meiosis**

**3. In mosses, which generation is the main green plant?**
a) The sporophyte  b) The gametophyte  c) Neither — mosses have no generations  d) The seed
**Answer: b) The gametophyte**

**4. In ferns, the tiny heart-shaped prothallus is the…**
a) Sporophyte  b) Gametophyte  c) Spore  d) Root
**Answer: b) Gametophyte**
