# Alternation of generations — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The generation that makes spores is the _____.**
**Answer: sporophyte**

**2. The generation that makes gametes is the _____.**
**Answer: gametophyte**

**3. In mosses the _____ is the main plant; in ferns the sporophyte is.**
**Answer: gametophyte**

**4. The two switches of the cycle are meiosis and _____.**
**Answer: fertilisation**
