# Alternation of generations — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the alternation of generations in one paragraph.
**Answer: The sporophyte (2n) makes spores by meiosis. A spore grows into the gametophyte (n), which makes gametes by mitosis. Two gametes join at fertilisation to form the zygote, which grows into the next sporophyte. The two generations alternate, handing over by spores one way and by gametes the other.**

**2.** Explain: North Macedonia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: North Macedonia's Grade 13 elective textbook teaches alternation of generations as a named topic (Ch. 3.I.2, printed pp. 104–110) inside its reproduction chapter, worked through mosses (Bryophyta) and ferns (Pteridophyta), between vegetative reproduction and generative reproduction in seed plants.**
