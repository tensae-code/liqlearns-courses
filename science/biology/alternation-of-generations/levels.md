# Alternation of generations — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Plant life cycles alternate between two generations: the sporophyte (2n), which makes spores by meiosis, and the gametophyte (n), which makes gametes — in mosses the gametophyte is the main plant, in ferns the sporophyte is.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a fresh example from the learner's own surroundings and saying why it fits, without looking.
