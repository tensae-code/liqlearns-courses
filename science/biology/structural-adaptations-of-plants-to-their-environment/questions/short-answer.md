# Structural adaptations of plants to their environment — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: a plant's structure is fitted to the place it lives — support structures hold it up, and in arid conditions a cactus turns its leaves into spines so it loses less water to evaporation. Moldova's Grade 6 book (5.1 Structural adaptations of plants) teaches: support structures, cactus leaves turned to spines reducing evaporation in arid conditions. (printed p. 68).**

**2.** Explain Moldova's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Moldova's Grade 6 book teaches plant adaptation as structure first: support structures, then the cactus's spines as leaves changed to cut evaporation in arid conditions.**
