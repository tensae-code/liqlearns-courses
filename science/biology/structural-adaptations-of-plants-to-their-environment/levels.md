# Structural adaptations of plants to their environment — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (a plant's structure is fitted to the place it lives — support structures hold it up, and in arid conditions a cactus turns its leaves into spines so it loses less water to evaporation) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
