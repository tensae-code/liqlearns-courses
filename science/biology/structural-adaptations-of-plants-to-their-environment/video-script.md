Today: Structural adaptations of plants to their environment.

Remember this one thing: a plant's structure is fitted to the place it lives — support structures hold it up, and in arid conditions a cactus turns its leaves into spines so it loses less water to evaporation.

Moldova's Grade 6 book (5.1 Structural adaptations of plants) teaches: support structures, cactus leaves turned to spines reducing evaporation in arid conditions. (printed p. 68).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
