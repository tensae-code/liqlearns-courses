# Plant and animal tissues — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (a tissue is a group of similar cells working together at one job — Yemen's Grade 11 book teaches the animal tissues first (epithelial, connective, muscular, nervous) and then the plant tissues (permanent, and compound permanent — the vascular tissues), and its activities book makes the learner look at each kind under the microscope.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: applying the lesson to a new example from the learner's own surroundings, and explaining Yemen's way of teaching it (Unit 2, printed pp. 25–49, with Activities 3–8 of the activities book), without looking.
