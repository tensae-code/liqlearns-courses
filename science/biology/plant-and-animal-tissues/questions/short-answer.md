# Plant and animal tissues — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the four animal tissues and the job of each.
**Answer: Epithelial tissue covers and lines. Connective tissue binds and supports (the skeletal tissues are studied among its kinds). Muscular tissue does the moving. Nervous tissue senses and signals.**

**2.** Explain: Yemen's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Yemen's Grade 11 Biology and Earth Science book teaches tissues as one unit (Unit 2) across both kingdoms — animal tissues first (epithelial printed p. 25, connective p. 31, muscular p. 39, nervous p. 42), then plant tissues (permanent p. 45, compound permanent/vascular p. 49) — and its Grade 11 activities book keys six microscope practicals (Activities 3–8, printed pp. 12–27) to the same ground.**
