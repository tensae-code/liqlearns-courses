# Plant and animal tissues — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A tissue is…
a) one very large cell
b) a group of similar cells doing one job together
c) a whole organ
d) a kind of microscope
**Answer: b) a group of similar cells doing one job together**

**2.** In Yemen's Grade 11 book, the animal tissues are taught in which order?
a) nervous, muscular, connective, epithelial
b) epithelial, connective, muscular, nervous
c) muscular first, epithelial last
d) all four on one page, unordered
**Answer: b) epithelial, connective, muscular, nervous**

**3.** Epithelial tissue is the…
a) moving kind
b) signalling kind
c) covering and lining kind
d) water-carrying kind
**Answer: c) covering and lining kind**

**4.** Which tissue does the moving?
a) Muscular tissue
b) Epithelial tissue
c) Permanent tissue
d) Meristematic tissue
**Answer: a) Muscular tissue**

**5.** The compound permanent plant tissues are the…
a) covering tissues
b) vascular tissues
c) growing tissues
d) nervous tissues
**Answer: b) vascular tissues**

**6.** The Grade 11 activities book's tissue practicals begin, on the plant side, from…
a) permanent tissues
b) vascular bundles
c) meristematic tissues
d) epithelial tissues
**Answer: c) meristematic tissues**

**7.** Yemen teaches tissues…
a) in two separate books, one per kingdom
b) as one unit across both kingdoms — animals first, then plants
c) plants only; animal tissues are left to Grade 12
d) without any practical work
**Answer: b) as one unit across both kingdoms — animals first, then plants**
