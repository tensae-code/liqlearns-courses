# Plant and animal tissues — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A tissue stands between the cell and the organ in the levels of organisation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Nervous tissue is a plant tissue.
**Answer: False** — Nervous tissue is one of the four animal tissues; the plant tissues in the unit are the permanent and compound permanent (vascular) tissues.

**3.** The activities book studies simple and compound (stratified) epithelial tissues as separate practicals.
**Answer: True**
