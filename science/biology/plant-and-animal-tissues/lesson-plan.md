# Lesson plan — Plant and animal tissues

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Plant and animal tissues
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — appended to Unit 16 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a tissue is a group of similar cells working together at one job — Yemen's Grade 11 book teaches the animal tissues first (epithelial, connective, muscular, nervous) and then the plant tissues (permanent, and compound permanent — the vascular tissues), and its activities book makes the learner look at each kind under the microscope.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own as a single-skill lesson built from Yemen's Grade 11 Biology and Earth Science textbook, Unit 2 (printed pp. 25–49), and its Grade 11 Activities and Practical Experiments book, Activities 3–8 (printed pp. 12–27). Related lessons are recommended first where they help, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say, in their own words, the one thing this lesson teaches: a tissue is a group of similar cells working together at one job — Yemen's Grade 11 book teaches the animal tissues first (epithelial, connective, muscular, nervous) and then the plant tissues (permanent, and compound permanent — the vascular tissues), and its activities book makes the learner look at each kind under the microscope.
2. Explain each key fact (What a tissue is; Epithelial tissue; Connective tissue; Muscle and nervous tissue; Plant tissues; Yemen's way) in their own words.
3. Explain Yemen's way as the placements describe it: the topic is taught in Unit 2 of the Grade 11 textbook (printed pp. 25–49), with six keyed practicals in the Grade 11 activities book.
