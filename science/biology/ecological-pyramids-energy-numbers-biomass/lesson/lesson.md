# Ecological pyramids — energy, numbers, biomass

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Ecological pyramids — energy, numbers, biomass. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Draw the levels as a pyramid and their sizes speak. Three pyramids — energy, numbers, biomass — tell the same chain three ways.

[Picture 22270 — the three pyramid kinds side by side for one food chain]

## The key facts
**Three kinds.** Ecological pyramids come as pyramids of energy, of number and of biomass.

**Energy pyramid.** The pyramid of energy is the idealised pyramid.

**Aquatic example.** Number and biomass pyramids are worked on an aquatic chain.

**Applied close.** The advantage-of-being-vegetarian question is the applied close of this lesson.
