# Masquerade and mimicry — the hoverfly that imitates a bee — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is Eristalis tenax?
a) A hoverfly — a true fly that imitates a bee
b) A bee with an extra-powerful sting
c) It plays no part in this topic at all
d) It happens only at night and nowhere else
**Answer: a) A hoverfly — a true fly that imitates a bee**

**2.** What does the hoverfly NOT have, that the bee has?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) A sting — it copies the bee's warning pattern without the weapon
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) A sting — it copies the bee's warning pattern without the weapon**

**3.** Why does the imitation protect the hoverfly?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Predators that have learned bees sting leave bee-patterned insects alone
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Predators that have learned bees sting leave bee-patterned insects alone**

**4.** Where does this case stand in Costa Rica's Biología Undécimo?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) At the very front — Unit 1's opening Lectura Reflexiva, "Un caso muy especial de mimetismo", printed p. 14
**Answer: d) At the very front — Unit 1's opening Lectura Reflexiva, "Un caso muy especial de mimetismo", printed p. 14**
