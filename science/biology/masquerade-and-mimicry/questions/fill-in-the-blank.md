# Masquerade and mimicry — the hoverfly that imitates a bee — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** Eristalis tenax is a _____ — a true fly that imitates a bee but has no sting.
**Answer: hoverfly**

**2.** Costa Rica's anthology calls the case "Un caso muy especial de _____" — a very special case of mimicry.
**Answer: mimetismo**
