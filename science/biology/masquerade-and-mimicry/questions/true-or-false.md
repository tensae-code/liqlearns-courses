# Masquerade and mimicry — the hoverfly that imitates a bee — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Eristalis tenax is a hoverfly, not a bee.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The hoverfly's sting is weaker than a bee's, but it can still sting.
**Answer: False** — the hoverfly has no sting at all; the imitation is its whole defence.

**3.** In mimicry, the model is genuinely defended and the mimic copies the warning without the weapon.
**Answer: True**

**4.** In Costa Rica's anthology, the mimicry case appears at the end of the course, after all the theory.
**Answer: False** — it opens the course: it is Unit 1's Lectura Reflexiva, printed p. 14, before the formal topics.
