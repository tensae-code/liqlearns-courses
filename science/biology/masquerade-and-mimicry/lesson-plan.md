# Lesson plan — Masquerade and mimicry — the hoverfly that imitates a bee

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Masquerade and mimicry — the hoverfly that imitates a bee
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — appended to Unit 16 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a harmless animal can survive by imitating a defended one — mimicry — and Costa Rica's Grade 11 anthology opens its whole ecology course with one case: Eristalis tenax, the hoverfly that imitates a bee.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. The relationships lessons are recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: **Relationships where someone loses** and **Relationships where no one loses**. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say, in their own words, the one thing this lesson teaches: a harmless animal can survive by imitating a defended one — mimicry — and Costa Rica's Grade 11 anthology opens its whole ecology course with one case: Eristalis tenax, the hoverfly that imitates a bee.
2. Say what Eristalis tenax is: a hoverfly — a fly with no sting — that imitates a bee.
3. Explain why the imitation protects it: predators that have learned bees sting leave bee-patterned insects alone.
4. Explain Costa Rica's way: the whole course opens with this case as a "Lectura Reflexiva" reading, before any formal topic.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck

- Use the book's own case: who is copying whom, and who is fooled?
