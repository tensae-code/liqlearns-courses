# Video script — Co-dominance, incomplete dominance and blood groups

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Patterns Mendel's rules do not cover: co-dominance, where both alleles show fully (red × white cattle giving roan; the A and B blood alleles giving AB blood); incomplete dominance, where the heterozygote is a blend (red × white flowers giving pink, 1:2:1 in F2); and multiple alleles, where a gene has more than two forms — the ABO blood system with its A, B and O alleles, antigens and antibodies, and why transfusion matching matters.

Complete parent-combination tables the student book does not print: for ABO, every parental pairing (O×O through A×B and A×AB) with the children's possible phenotypes and genotypes; and the three-row Rh table (Rh+×Rh+, Rh+×Rh−, Rh−×Rh−) with possible children's types.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
