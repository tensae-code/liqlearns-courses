# Co-dominance, incomplete dominance and blood groups — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Patterns Mendel's rules do not cover: co-dominance, where both alleles show fully (red × white cattle giving roan; the A and B blood alleles giving AB blood); incomplete dominance, where the heterozygote is a blend (red × white flowers giving pink, 1:2:1 in F2); and multiple alleles, where a gene has more than two forms — the ABO blood system with its A, B and O alleles, antigens and antibodies, and why transfusion matching matters.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Complete parent-combination tables the student book does not print: for ABO, every parental pairing (O×O through A×B and A×AB) with the children's possible phenotypes and genotypes; and the three-row Rh table (Rh+×Rh+, Rh+×Rh−, Rh−×Rh−) with possible children's types.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
