## The idea
Filamentous algae and some worms live this way: break, then regrow what is missing.

[Picture 21038 — an alga filament breaking, each piece growing to full length]

[GIF 21116 — a fragment regrowing its missing end]

## The key facts
**Breaking.** In fragmentation a multicellular body breaks into pieces.

**Regeneration.** Each fragment regenerates its missing parts to become a complete individual.

**Examples.** Filamentous algae and some worms reproduce by fragmentation.

**Complete.** Every fragment can become a complete individual.
