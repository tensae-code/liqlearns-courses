# Protozoa — malaria and its relatives

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — protozoa. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Babesia (tick-borne red-blood-cell parasite, over 100 species, discovered by Victor Babeș) and Theileria (T. annulata and T. parva as important cattle parasites) are introduced; the Plasmodium life cycle is narrated (infected female Anopheles inoculates sporozoites, liver schizonts rupture releasing merozoites, and onward); Giardia and amoeba transmission routes are given as drinking water and food; a Leishmania life-cycle figure is included.
