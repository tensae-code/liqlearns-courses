Today: Hormones in medicine and anabolic steroids.

Remember this one thing: a hormone can be a medicine — and the same kind of power, misused, becomes doping.

When a body makes too little insulin, insulin is given as medicine: the missing hormone replaced. Doctors use other hormones on purpose too, in treatment and in fertility care.

Anabolic steroids copy the male hormone to build muscle. Misused in sport they are banned — and they damage the liver, the heart and the body's own hormone balance.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
