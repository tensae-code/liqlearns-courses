# Hormones in medicine and anabolic steroids — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Hormones are medicines as well as messengers: the same chemistry that lets the body regulate itself lets doctors replace a missing hormone (insulin for diabetes is the standing example) and lets hormones be used to treat and to prevent — and anabolic steroids, synthetic versions of the male hormone, show the other face of that power, building muscle when misused in sport while damaging the body that uses them without need.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
