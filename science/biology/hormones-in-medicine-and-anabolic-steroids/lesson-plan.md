# Lesson plan — Hormones in medicine and anabolic steroids

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Hormones in medicine and anabolic steroids
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — Grades 11–12 band (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Hormones are medicines as well as messengers: the same chemistry that lets the body regulate itself lets doctors replace a missing hormone (insulin for diabetes is the standing example) and lets hormones be used to treat and to prevent — and anabolic steroids, synthetic versions of the male hormone, show the other face of that power, building muscle when misused in sport while damaging the body that uses them without need.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier biology of living things, health and the human body, and builds the Grades 11–12 band understanding of hormones in medicine and anabolic steroids.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio.
