# Hands-on — Hormones in medicine and anabolic steroids

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Sort a set of cards into two piles — medicine and misuse: insulin for a diabetic, hormone treatment prescribed by a doctor, steroids taken to win a race, steroids bought without a prescription. Say aloud what makes each card belong to its pile.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the insulin pen for the medicine side and the crossed-out steroid syringe for the misuse side, each labelled with why — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
