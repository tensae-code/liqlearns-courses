# Hormones in medicine and anabolic steroids

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Hormones in medicine and anabolic steroids. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Hormones are medicines as well as messengers: the same chemistry that lets the body regulate itself lets doctors replace a missing hormone (insulin for diabetes is the standing example) and lets hormones be used to treat and to prevent — and anabolic steroids, synthetic versions of the male hormone, show the other face of that power, building muscle when misused in sport while damaging the body that uses them without need. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from Bhutan's Class 10 book, which teaches it as a named topic.

[Picture — an insulin pen beside a vial labelled hormone medicine, and a crossed-out syringe labelled anabolic steroids beside a sports kit bag]

## The key facts
**Replacement — giving what the body lacks.** Where a gland makes too little of its hormone, the hormone itself can be given as medicine: insulin for diabetes is the standing example — the missing hormone supplied from outside so blood sugar can be regulated again.

**Hormones used on purpose.** Medicine also uses hormones deliberately — to treat conditions, and in fertility and contraception, where the reproductive hormones are managed to a plan. A hormone's medical use follows from what the hormone does in the body.

**Anabolic steroids — the misuse.** Anabolic steroids are synthetic substances modelled on the male sex hormone. They build muscle and strength, which is why some athletes misuse them to gain an advantage — doping by hormone.

**The cost of misuse.** Used without medical need, anabolic steroids damage the body — the liver, the heart and the body's own hormone balance among them — and their use in sport is banned as cheating. The same power that heals as a medicine harms as a shortcut.

**Bhutan's way.** Bhutan's Class 10 book teaches the two faces together (Ch. 4.2, printed pp. 104–106): anabolic steroids and the medical uses of hormones in one section, right after hormone regulation by feedback and the regulation of blood glucose — treatment and abuse as one topic.
