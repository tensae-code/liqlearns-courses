# Hormones in medicine and anabolic steroids — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Anabolic steroids are safe whenever they build muscle.**
**Answer: False** — used without medical need they damage the liver, the heart and the body's own hormone balance, and they are banned in sport.

**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Medicine uses hormones only for replacement.**
**Answer: False** — hormones are also used deliberately in treatment and in fertility and contraception.
