# Hormones in medicine and anabolic steroids — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the two faces of hormones in this lesson.
**Answer: As medicines, hormones replace what a body lacks — insulin for diabetes — and are used deliberately in treatment and fertility care. As anabolic steroids, synthetic male-hormone copies are misused to build muscle in sport: banned as cheating and damaging to the body.**

**2.** Explain: Bhutan's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Bhutan's Class 10 book (Ch. 4.2, printed pp. 104–106) puts anabolic steroids and the medical uses of hormones in one section, straight after feedback regulation and blood-glucose regulation.**
