# Hormones in medicine and anabolic steroids — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Supplying a hormone the body lacks is called hormone _____.**
**Answer: replacement**

**2. The standing example of a hormone medicine is _____ for diabetes.**
**Answer: insulin**

**3. Anabolic _____ are synthetic versions of the male hormone.**
**Answer: steroids**

**4. Steroid misuse can damage the liver, the heart and the body's hormone _____.**
**Answer: balance**
