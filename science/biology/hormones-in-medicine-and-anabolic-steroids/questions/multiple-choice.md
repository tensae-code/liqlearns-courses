# Hormones in medicine and anabolic steroids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Insulin given to a person with diabetes is an example of…**
a) doping  b) hormone replacement — supplying a hormone the body lacks  c) a vaccine  d) an antibiotic
**Answer: b) hormone replacement — supplying a hormone the body lacks**

**2. Anabolic steroids are modelled on…**
a) insulin  b) the male sex hormone  c) adrenaline  d) thyroxine
**Answer: b) the male sex hormone**

**3. Why are anabolic steroids banned in sport?**
a) They do not work  b) They give an unfair advantage and damage the body  c) They are too cheap  d) They are hard to make
**Answer: b) They give an unfair advantage and damage the body**

**4. A hormone's medical use follows from…**
a) its colour  b) what that hormone does in the body  c) its price  d) the gland's size
**Answer: b) what that hormone does in the body**
