# Polygenic inheritance — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A polygenic trait is controlled by many genes acting together.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Each gene in a polygenic trait decides the whole trait by itself.
**Answer: False** — each gene contributes a small part; the trait shown is the sum of all the contributions.

**3.** Polygenic traits tend to grade through a population instead of splitting into two or three neat kinds.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Polygenic inheritance is the same thing as multiple alleles.
**Answer: False** — multiple alleles are many forms of one gene; polygenic inheritance is many genes for one trait. Sudan's book teaches them as separate sections — multiple alleles in §4, polygenes in §5.
