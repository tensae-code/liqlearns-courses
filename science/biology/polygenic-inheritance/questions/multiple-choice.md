# Polygenic inheritance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What are polygenes?
a) Many genes that together control a single trait
b) One gene with many names
c) Genes that are never inherited
d) The genes of a plant only
**Answer: a) Many genes that together control a single trait**

**2.** How does a polygenic trait usually appear in a population?
a) In exactly two kinds, with nothing between
b) In many gradations, step by step from one extreme to the other
c) In one kind only, the same in everyone
d) It never appears at all
**Answer: b) In many gradations, step by step from one extreme to the other**

**3.** How is polygenic inheritance different from Mendel's pea traits?
a) Pea traits are not inherited at all
b) Pea traits are controlled by the environment only
c) Each pea trait is controlled by a single gene; a polygenic trait is controlled by many genes together
d) There is no difference
**Answer: c) Each pea trait is controlled by a single gene; a polygenic trait is controlled by many genes together**

**4.** Where does Sudan's Grade 12 book teach polygenes?
a) In §5 of its genetics unit, after multiple alleles and before sex determination
b) In the reproduction unit, before fertilisation
c) In the ecosystem unit, after soil
d) It does not teach polygenes
**Answer: a) In §5 of its genetics unit, after multiple alleles and before sex determination**
