Today: Polygenic inheritance.

Remember this one thing: some traits are not controlled by one gene. They are controlled by many genes together.

Those many genes are called polygenes. Each one adds a small part, and the trait you see is the sum of all the parts.

That is why a polygenic trait does not split into two neat kinds. It grades — step by step, from one extreme to the other, like a row of plants at every height.

Sudan's Grade 12 book teaches polygenes in §5 of its genetics unit — after Mendel's ratios have been modified, and after multiple alleles — the step from one gene for a trait to many genes for a trait.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
