# Hands-on — Polygenic inheritance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Line up a set of same-kind things that come in graded sizes — seedlings, stones sorted by size, or classmates by height (with permission) — from smallest to largest. Count how many steps the line makes. Then say aloud why a trait built by many genes grades like this line, instead of splitting into two neat heaps the way a single-gene trait does.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the row of plants at many graded heights — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
