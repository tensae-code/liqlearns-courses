Today: Energy requirements and BMR.

Remember this one thing: How much energy a person needs is not one number for everybody: Kenya's Form 1 book closes its nutrition chapter by working out what sets the requirement — basal metabolic rate, occupation, age, body size, sex, lactation and pregnancy — with a Kenyan labourer pulling a cart set against an office worker.

Start with the body at rest: even doing nothing, you burn energy to keep warm, breathe and keep your heart beating — that floor is your basal metabolic rate. Then pile the day on top: the labourer pulling a cart or mixing concrete burns far more than the office worker at a desk. Age matters, body size matters, sex matters — and a mother who is pregnant or breastfeeding needs more, for two. Kenya's book puts the numbers in a table, by age and sex, so the requirement is worked out, not guessed.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
