# Energy requirements and BMR — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why two healthy adults of the same age can honestly need very different amounts of food energy.
**Model answer: Food energy has to cover the basal rate plus everything the day demands, and days differ: the labourer pulling a cart burns in an hour what the office worker may not burn in a morning, so the same age and a similar body can still mean a much larger requirement. Pregnancy or breastfeeding raises it again, because the energy is feeding two bodies.**
