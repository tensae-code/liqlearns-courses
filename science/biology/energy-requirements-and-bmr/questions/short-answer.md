# Energy requirements and BMR — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** List the factors that determine a person's energy requirements, as Kenya's Form 1 book gives them.
**Answer: Basal metabolic rate; occupation (the day's work); age; body size; sex; and, for a woman, lactation and pregnancy (Ch. 5, printed p. 88).**

**2.** Explain Kenya's way of teaching energy requirements.
**Answer: The book does not give one number for everyone: it works the requirement out from the person and their life — the basal rate underneath, then occupation on top, with a labourer pulling a cart or mixing concrete set against an office worker, and an age/sex table (table 5.4) to read the values from.**
