# Energy requirements and BMR — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Basal metabolic rate is the energy the body uses…**
a) for hard physical work  b) at complete rest, just to stay alive  c) only during sleep  d) for growing taller
**Answer: b) at complete rest, just to stay alive**

**2. In Kenya's book, who needs the most energy for the same hours?**
a) an office worker  b) a person at rest  c) a labourer pulling a cart or mixing concrete  d) a school pupil sitting in class
**Answer: c) a labourer pulling a cart or mixing concrete**

**3. Which of these does NOT change a person's energy requirement, as the book lists the factors?**
a) age  b) body size  c) lactation  d) the colour of their clothes
**Answer: d) the colour of their clothes**

**4. A breastfeeding mother needs extra energy because…**
a) she is feeding another body as well as her own  b) milk is made of fat only  c) she sleeps less  d) her BMR falls to zero
**Answer: a) she is feeding another body as well as her own**
