# Energy requirements and BMR — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The energy a body uses at complete rest is its _____ metabolic rate.**
**Answer: basal**

**2. A labourer pulling a _____ is Kenya's example of heavy work.**
**Answer: cart**

**3. Feeding a baby with milk is called _____.**
**Answer: lactation**

**4. Energy requirements are set out by age and _____ in table 5.4.**
**Answer: sex**
