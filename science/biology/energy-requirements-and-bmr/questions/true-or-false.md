# Energy requirements and BMR — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Every person needs the same amount of energy each day.**
**Answer: False** — the requirement turns on BMR, occupation, age, body size, sex, pregnancy and lactation.

**2. BMR is measured at complete rest.**
**Answer: True** — it is the energy used just to stay alive.

**3. An office worker needs more energy than a labourer pulling a cart.**
**Answer: False** — the labourer's heavy work needs far more.

**4. The book gives energy use in a table by age and sex (table 5.4).**
**Answer: True** — the age/sex energy-use table.
