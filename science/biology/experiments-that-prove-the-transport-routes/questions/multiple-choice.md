# Experiments that prove the transport routes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about celery test?
a) Coloured water rises through celery, showing the xylem carrying dyed water upward  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Coloured water rises through celery, showing the xylem carrying dyed water upward**

**2.** What does this lesson say about dye in strands?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) The dye appears in the celery's cut strands — the xylem tubes  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The dye appears in the celery's cut strands — the xylem tubes**

**3.** What does this lesson say about ringing?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Removing a ring of bark and phloem makes the stem swell above the ring  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Removing a ring of bark and phloem makes the stem swell above the ring**

**4.** What does this lesson say about what ringing proves?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) The swelling above the ring proves food travels downward in the phloem
**Answer: d) The swelling above the ring proves food travels downward in the phloem**
