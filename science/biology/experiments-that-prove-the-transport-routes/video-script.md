Today: Experiments that prove the transport routes.

Remember this one thing: dyed water climbing celery proves the xylem route; a ringed stem swelling above the ring proves food travels in the phloem

Celery test: Coloured water rises through celery, showing the xylem carrying dyed water upward.

Dye in strands: The dye appears in the celery's cut strands — the xylem tubes.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
