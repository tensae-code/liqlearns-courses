# Experiments that prove the transport routes

## Do it first

## The idea
Two classic experiments make the invisible routes visible. Watch the dye climb, and read the swelling above a ring.

## The key facts
**Celery test.** Coloured water rises through celery, showing the xylem carrying dyed water upward.

**Dye in strands.** The dye appears in the celery's cut strands — the xylem tubes.

**Ringing.** Removing a ring of bark and phloem makes the stem swell above the ring.

**What ringing proves.** The swelling above the ring proves food travels downward in the phloem.
