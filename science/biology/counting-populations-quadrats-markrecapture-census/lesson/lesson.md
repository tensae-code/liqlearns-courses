# Counting populations — quadrats, mark-recapture, census

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — counting populations. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Ecology studies organisms with their environment through levels from individual to biosphere; a population is interbreeding members of one species in an area; its size and density are estimated by quadrat sampling for still organisms (with a worked density calculation), mark–recapture for moving animals (worked with marked birds), and for humans a census every ten years, taken de facto or de jure, yielding birth, death, fertility and growth measures.
