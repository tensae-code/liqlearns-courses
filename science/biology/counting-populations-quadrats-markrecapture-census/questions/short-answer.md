# Counting populations — quadrats, mark-recapture, census — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about population ecology?**
**Answer: Ecology studies organisms with their environment through levels from individual to biosphere; a population is interbreeding members of one species in an area; its size and density are estimated by quadrat sampling for still organisms (with a worked density calculation), mark–recapture for moving animals (worked with marked birds), and for humans a census every ten years, taken de facto or de jure, yielding birth, death, fertility and growth measures.**
