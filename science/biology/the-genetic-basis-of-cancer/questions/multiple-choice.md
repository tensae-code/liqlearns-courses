# The genetic basis of cancer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Cancer depends on mutations in which genes?**
a) Genes for eye colour  b) Genes that control cell division  c) Genes found only in bacteria  d) No genes at all
**Answer: b) Genes that control cell division**

**2. How many gene alterations contribute to a cancer, on the book's account?**
a) Exactly one  b) None — genes are not involved  c) Many gene alterations contribute  d) One per body system
**Answer: c) Many gene alterations contribute**

**3. How do altered genes do their damage?**
a) By leaving the cell  b) By yielding defective proteins that interfere with signal transduction  c) By changing the cell's colour  d) By stopping all protein synthesis
**Answer: b) By yielding defective proteins that interfere with signal transduction**

**4. What is signal transduction?**
a) The building of a ribosome  b) The passing of control messages from protein to protein inside the cell  c) The copying of DNA  d) The death of a cell
**Answer: b) The passing of control messages from protein to protein inside the cell**
