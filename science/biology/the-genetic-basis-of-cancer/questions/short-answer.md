# The genetic basis of cancer — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the genetic basis of cancer, in one or two sentences.
**Answer: Cancer depends on mutations in the genes that control cell division. Many gene alterations contribute, and they act through the proteins those genes produce: defective proteins interfere with signal transduction, the protein-to-protein signalling that tells a cell when to divide and when to stop.**

**2.** Explain: Italy's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Italy's Grade 11 Campbell (*Biologia: concetti e collegamenti, Secondo biennio*, Linx/Pearson Italia) closes Unità 1, the molecular biology of the gene, with a full run on the genetic bases of cancer (printed pp. 48–50) — joining protein synthesis and mutations into the genetics of the disease.**
