# The genetic basis of cancer — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Cancer depends on _____ in the genes that control cell division.**
**Answer: mutations**

**2. Altered genes act through defective _____.**
**Answer: proteins**

**3. The passing of control signals from protein to protein is called signal _____.**
**Answer: transduction**

**4. Not one but _____ gene alterations contribute to a cancer.**
**Answer: many**
