# The genetic basis of cancer — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining the genetic basis of cancer to a younger student. Use the words mutations and signal transduction once each.
**Model answer: A cell divides only when its control genes tell it to, and those genes work through signals passed from protein to protein. If mutations damage the genes that control division, they can produce defective proteins, and those proteins garble the signalling — signal transduction goes wrong, and division escapes control. Because many gene alterations can each add damage, cancer's genetic basis is an accumulation, not a single break.**
