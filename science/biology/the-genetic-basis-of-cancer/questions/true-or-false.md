# The genetic basis of cancer — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Cancer depends on mutations in genes that control cell division.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. A single gene alteration is the whole genetic basis of cancer.**
**Answer: False** — the book's account is plural — many gene alterations contribute.

**3. Defective proteins can interfere with signal transduction.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. Italy's Grade 11 Campbell teaches the genetic bases of cancer at the close of its gene unit (Unità 1).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (printed pp. 48–50).
