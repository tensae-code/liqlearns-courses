# The genetic basis of cancer — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (cancer depends on mutations in the genes that control cell division; many gene alterations contribute, acting through defective proteins that interfere with signal transduction.) and explain each key fact in their own words.
