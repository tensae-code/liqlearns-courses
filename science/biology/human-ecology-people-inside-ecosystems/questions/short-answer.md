# Human ecology — people inside ecosystems — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Give the city's weight on the planet in the figures Vietnam's book cites.
**Answer: Cities are human-made ecosystems covering about 3% of Earth's land, using about 75% of the world's energy and emitting about 85% of its greenhouse-gas emissions (IPCC 2022, cited in Chuyên đề học tập Sinh học 12, Topic 3, Bài 9, II, printed pp. 64–66).**

**2.** Explain Vietnam's way of teaching human ecology.
**Answer: Vietnam's Grade 12 study topics close with human ecology (Sinh thái nhân văn) as a topic of its own: Bài 8 sets out the concept and its value for sustainable development (printed p. 58); Bài 9 works the value in the field, its urban half treating the city as a human-made ecosystem with IPCC 2022 scale figures, Vietnam's own urbanisation (40.5% in 2021, heading to about 45% by 2026), Hanoi traffic and Ho Chi Minh City air-pollution photographs, the eco-city criteria, and Hanoi's 2030/2050 green-city plan with Hoàn Kiếm Lake and Đà Lạt as model images.**
