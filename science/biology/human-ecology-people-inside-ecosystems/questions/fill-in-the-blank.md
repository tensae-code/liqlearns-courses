# Human ecology — people inside ecosystems — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The ecology of people inside ecosystems is called _____ ecology.**
**Answer: human**

**2. Cities cover about _____% of Earth's land.**
**Answer: 3**

**3. Cities emit about _____% of greenhouse gases (IPCC 2022).**
**Answer: 85**

**4. Vietnam's urbanisation was _____% in 2021.**
**Answer: 40.5**
