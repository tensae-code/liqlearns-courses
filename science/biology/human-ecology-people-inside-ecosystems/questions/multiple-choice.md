# Human ecology — people inside ecosystems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Human ecology, as Vietnam's topics teach it, is…**
a) the study of ancient humans only  b) a named field studying people inside ecosystems, valued for sustainable development  c) a branch of medicine  d) the history of cities
**Answer: b) a named field studying people inside ecosystems, valued for sustainable development**

**2. Cities cover about what share of Earth's land, in the book's figures?**
a) 30%  b) 3%  c) 0.3%  d) 50%
**Answer: b) 3%**

**3. The book's IPCC 2022 figures give cities about what share of world energy use and greenhouse-gas emissions?**
a) 10% and 10%  b) 50% and 40%  c) 75% and 85%  d) 85% and 75%
**Answer: c) 75% and 85%**

**4. Vietnam's urbanisation in 2021 is given as…**
a) 25.5%  b) 40.5%, heading to about 45% by 2026  c) 60%  d) 12%
**Answer: b) 40.5%, heading to about 45% by 2026**
