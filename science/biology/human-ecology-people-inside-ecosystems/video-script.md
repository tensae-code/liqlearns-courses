# Video script — Human ecology — people inside ecosystems

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Human ecology — people inside ecosystems.

Remember this one thing: Human ecology studies people as part of ecosystems, not outside them — Vietnam's Grade 12 study topics name the field, tie it to sustainable development, and work it through the city: a human-made ecosystem on about 3% of Earth's land that uses about 75% of the world's energy and emits about 85% of its greenhouse gases.

Ecology is not only about forests and lakes. People make ecosystems too — cities. Three percent of the land, seventy-five percent of the energy, eighty-five percent of the emissions: that is the city's weight on the planet, in the figures Vietnam's book quotes. Human ecology studies that weight — and plans the lighter city: green corridors, cleaner transport, buildings and industry that fit the ecosystem they sit in.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
