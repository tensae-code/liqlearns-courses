# Hands-on — Human ecology — people inside ecosystems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Take the lesson's key facts for a walk: find one example of this lesson's idea in your own surroundings — your home, street, market or school — and say which key fact it matches, and why.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — a city drawn as an ecosystem diagram: apartment blocks, traffic and a lake park inside one boundary, with arrows entering labelled energy and food, and arrows leaving labelled emissions and waste; the figures 3% of land, 75% of energy, 85% of emissions written along the arrows — label its parts, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
