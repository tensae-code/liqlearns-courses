# Human ecology — people inside ecosystems

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Human ecology — people inside ecosystems. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Ecology usually studies other species' homes; human ecology turns the same science on our own. Vietnam's Grade 12 study topics close with a topic on human ecology (Sinh thái nhân văn, Topic 3). Bài 8 sets out the concept and its value for sustainable development (printed p. 58); Bài 9 then works the value in two fields, and its urban half (II, printed pp. 64–66) treats the city as a human-made ecosystem and gives the scale of it in IPCC 2022 figures: cities cover about 3% of Earth's land, use about 75% of world energy and emit about 85% of greenhouse-gas emissions. Vietnam's own urbanisation — 40.5% in 2021, heading to about 45% by 2026 — Hanoi's traffic and Ho Chi Minh City's air-pollution photographs, the eco-city criteria, and Hanoi's 2030/2050 green-city plan with Hoàn Kiếm Lake and Đà Lạt as model images carry the lesson home. No lesson in this course took human ecology as its one thing before, so this lesson is built for it.

## The key facts
**A named field.** Human ecology is taught as a field in its own right: the ecology of the human species and its made environments, valued because sustainable development cannot be planned without it — greenhouse gases are a glossary term of the topic.

**The city as an ecosystem.** A city is a human-made ecosystem: about 3% of Earth's land holds it, yet it uses about 75% of the world's energy and emits about 85% of its greenhouse gases (IPCC 2022, as the book cites).

**Vietnam's own figures.** Vietnam's urbanisation stood at 40.5% in 2021 and heads to about 45% by 2026; the book photographs Hanoi's traffic and Ho Chi Minh City's air pollution as its home evidence.

**The eco-city answer.** Against those figures the lesson sets the eco-city criteria — architecture, biodiversity corridors, transport, green industry and an eco-economy — with Hanoi's 2030/2050 green-city plan as the worked plan, and Hoàn Kiếm Lake and Đà Lạt as the model images.
