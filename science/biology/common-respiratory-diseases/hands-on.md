# Hands-on — Common respiratory diseases

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Check the air of the room you study or sleep in: does it get fresh air each day, and is there smoke in it? Write one change that would thin germs and smoke in that air.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of the air path from nose to alveoli with the common diseases pinned to the part each one strikes — cold and flu at the nose and throat, bronchitis at the bronchi, pneumonia and tuberculosis in the lungs, asthma at the narrowed bronchus — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
