# Common respiratory diseases — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining common respiratory diseases, using the lesson's key facts.
**Model answer: The breathing passages have common diseases of their own — colds, influenza, bronchitis, pneumonia, tuberculosis and asthma — spread mostly through the air, and preventable by air, hygiene and vaccination habits. The common cold and influenza are virus infections of the nose, throat and air passages, spread by coughs, sneezes and touch. Bronchitis inflames the bronchi; pneumonia fills the air sacs of the lungs themselves with fluid and pus. Tuberculosis (TB) is a bacterial disease of the lungs spread through the air from a coughing patient, cured by a full course of antibiotics.**
