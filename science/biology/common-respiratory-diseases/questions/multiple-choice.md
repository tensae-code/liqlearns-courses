# Common respiratory diseases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about colds and influenza?
a) The common cold and influenza are virus infections of the nose, throat and air passages, spread by coughs, sneezes and touch
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The common cold and influenza are virus infections of the nose, throat and air passages, spread by coughs, sneezes and touch**

**2.** What does this lesson say about bronchitis and pneumonia?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Bronchitis inflames the bronchi; pneumonia fills the air sacs of the lungs themselves with fluid and pus
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Bronchitis inflames the bronchi; pneumonia fills the air sacs of the lungs themselves with fluid and pus**

**3.** What does this lesson say about tuberculosis?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Tuberculosis (TB) is a bacterial disease of the lungs spread through the air from a coughing patient, cured by a full course of antibiotics
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Tuberculosis (TB) is a bacterial disease of the lungs spread through the air from a coughing patient, cured by a full course of antibiotics**

**4.** What does this lesson say about asthma?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Asthma narrows the air passages from inside — their walls swell and tighten — so breathing out becomes hard work
**Answer: d) Asthma narrows the air passages from inside — their walls swell and tighten — so breathing out becomes hard work**
