# Common respiratory diseases — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: tuberculosis.
**Answer: The TB bacterium travels in the fine spray of a patient's cough and settles in a new lung. It grows slowly — cough lasting weeks, fever, night sweats, weight loss and sometimes blood in the sputum — and it is curable, but only by the complete months-long course of antibiotics; stopping early breeds resistant germs.**

**2.** Explain: asthma.
**Answer: In asthma the bronchi are over-sensitive: dust, smoke, cold air or pollen makes them swell, tighten and fill with mucus, and the sufferer wheezes, worst on breathing out. It is not an infection and cannot be caught; attacks are eased by inhaled medicines that reopen the passages.**
