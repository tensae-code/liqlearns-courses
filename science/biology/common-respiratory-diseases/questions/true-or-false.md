# Common respiratory diseases — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** The common cold and influenza are virus infections of the nose, throat and air passages, spread by coughs, sneezes and touch.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Asthma is an infection you can catch from a classmate..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.

**3.** Bronchitis inflames the bronchi; pneumonia fills the air sacs of the lungs themselves with fluid and pus.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Stopping TB medicine as soon as you feel better is the safe way to take it..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.
