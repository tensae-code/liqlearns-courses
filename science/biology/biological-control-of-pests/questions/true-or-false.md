# Biological control of pests — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Biological control replaces integrated pest management entirely.**
**Answer: False** — it sits inside integrated pest management.

**2. Bt toxin genes have been moved into maize, cotton and potato.**
**Answer: True** — these are the book's resistance-gene cases.

**3. Conserving natural enemies is a single step: release them.**
**Answer: False** — the book gives a six-step research workflow, from survey to release.

**4. Trichoderma fungus mixed into organic fertiliser is a worked practice in the book.**
**Answer: True** — Bài 6's practice task, citing Monte et al. (2003).
