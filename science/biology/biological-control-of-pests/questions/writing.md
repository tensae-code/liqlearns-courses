# Biological control of pests — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why a slow method that cannot stop an outbreak still earns its place in pest management.
**Model answer: Most pest damage is not an outbreak but a steady pressure, season after season — and that is what biological control holds down, without poisoning the field, the food or the pest's own enemies. Its slowness only disqualifies it as the emergency answer; inside integrated pest management the emergency measures are used rarely precisely because the biological ones are working the rest of the time.**
