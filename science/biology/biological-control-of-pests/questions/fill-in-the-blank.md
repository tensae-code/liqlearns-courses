# Biological control of pests — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Using natural enemies to shrink a pest population is called _____ control.**
**Answer: biological**

**2. Sterile males are made by irradiation with X or _____ rays.**
**Answer: gamma**

**3. The oriental fruit fly's scientific name is Bactrocera _____.**
**Answer: dorsalis**

**4. A _____ toxin gene moved into maize makes the crop kill its own pests.**
**Answer: Bt**
