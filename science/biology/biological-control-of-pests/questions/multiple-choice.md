# Biological control of pests — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Biological control is defined in Vietnam's book as…**
a) spraying pests with stronger poisons  b) using natural enemies or biological products to shrink or wipe out a pest population  c) growing crops without any pest measures  d) fencing fields against animals
**Answer: b) using natural enemies or biological products to shrink or wipe out a pest population**

**2. Which is a limit of biological control the book states honestly?**
a) it kills the crop  b) it is slow and cannot answer an outbreak  c) it works only at night  d) it is banned in Vietnam
**Answer: b) it is slow and cannot answer an outbreak**

**3. The sterile-insect technique works by…**
a) poisoning male pests  b) releasing X/gamma-irradiated sterile males to mate the population down  c) training birds to eat the pest  d) flooding the field
**Answer: b) releasing X/gamma-irradiated sterile males to mate the population down**

**4. In Vietnam, SIT is used against the oriental fruit fly in…**
a) rice paddies of the Red River delta  b) the dragon-fruit regions of Vĩnh Long and the Central Highlands  c) coffee plantations only  d) fish ponds
**Answer: b) the dragon-fruit regions of Vĩnh Long and the Central Highlands**
