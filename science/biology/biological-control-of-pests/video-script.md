Today: Biological control of pests.

Remember this one thing: Biological control fights a pest with living things and biology — natural enemies, sterile males, pheromone traps and resistance genes — instead of poison alone: Vietnam's Grade 12 study topics define it, give its ecological, genetic and physiological bases, state its limits honestly, and set its measures out as a system inside integrated pest management.

Fight the pest with life, not only with poison. Natural enemies that hunt it. Sterile males, irradiated and released, so the next generation never hatches. Pheromone traps that call the males in. Crops carrying resistance genes of their own. Vietnam teaches all of it — and teaches its limits too: biological control is slow, and cannot stop an outbreak already burning. That honesty is why it sits inside integrated pest management, as one strong part of a system.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
