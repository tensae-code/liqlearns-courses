# Hands-on — Biological control of pests

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Take the lesson's key facts for a walk: find one example of this lesson's idea in your own surroundings — your home, street, market or school — and say which key fact it matches, and why.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — a dragon-fruit field worked as a system in four panels: a ladybird hunting aphids on a stem; a release tube of sterile male fruit flies; a pheromone trap hanging from a branch; and a maize plant carrying a resistance gene, drawn as a shield in its leaves — with a faded spray bottle crossed out in the corner — label its parts, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
