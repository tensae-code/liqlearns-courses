# Birds — structure and adaptations for flight — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (birds are built for flight: beaks matched to diet, no teeth, a highly efficient digestive system, air sacs driving air through the lungs in one path, nitrogen waste converted to uric acid, and long bones strong yet light with struts and air cavities) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
