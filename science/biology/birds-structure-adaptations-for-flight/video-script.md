Today: Birds — structure and adaptations for flight.

Remember this one thing: birds are built for flight: beaks matched to diet, no teeth, a highly efficient digestive system, air sacs driving air through the lungs in one path, nitrogen waste converted to uric acid, and long bones strong yet light with struts and air cavities.

Kuwait's Grade 10 Term 2 student book (Unit 3, Lesson 2-2, printed p. 93) teaches the birds through their shared characteristics and their evolution.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
