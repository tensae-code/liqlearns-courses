# Birds — structure and adaptations for flight — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: birds are built for flight: beaks matched to diet, no teeth, a highly efficient digestive system, air sacs driving air through the lungs in one path, nitrogen waste converted to uric acid, and long bones strong yet light with struts and air cavities. Kuwait's Grade 10 Term 2 student book (Unit 3, Lesson 2-2, printed p. 93) teaches the birds through their shared characteristics and their evolution.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait teaches birds as a flight-adaptation study (Grade 10 Term 2, Unit 3 Lesson 2-2): every structure is introduced by the job it does in the air. (The held copy of this book is truncated — Unit 1 is absent from the file; Units 2 and 3 are complete, and this lesson stands in Unit 3.)**
