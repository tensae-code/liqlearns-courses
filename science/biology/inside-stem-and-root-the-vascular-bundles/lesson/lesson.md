# Inside stem and root — the vascular bundles

## The idea
Inside the stem and the root run the vascular bundles — the plant's transport tubes. Their arrangement is not the same everywhere, and that pattern is the anatomy the transport lessons build on.

[Picture 22208 — stem and root cross-sections with xylem and phloem labelled]

## The key facts
**Vascular bundles.** Dicot stem and root cross-sections show bundles of vascular tissue.

**Xylem and phloem.** The bundles contain xylem and phloem, the transport tissues.

**Arrangement differs.** The bundles' arrangement differs between stem and root.

**Dicots and monocots.** The arrangement also differs between dicots and monocots.
