# Hands-on — Calculating the magnification of a microscope

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Read the numbers printed on your microscope's eyepiece and on each objective. Calculate the total magnification for every combination and write them as a table, lowest to highest. If there is no microscope, do the same from a labelled picture of one.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of calculating the magnification of a microscope — the total magnification of a microscope is the eyepiece magnification multiplied by the objective magnification — eyepiece × objective. — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
