# Calculating the magnification of a microscope — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (the total magnification of a microscope is the eyepiece magnification multiplied by the objective magnification — eyepiece × objective.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
