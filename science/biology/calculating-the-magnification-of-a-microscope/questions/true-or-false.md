# Calculating the magnification of a microscope — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A light microscope magnifies twice: the objective lens, close to the specimen, magnifies first, and the eyepiece lens magnifies that image again.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Worked example is the opposite of what this lesson teaches.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches this as stated.**

**3.** The same multiplication tells you a real size: if a cell's image measures 20 mm across at ×400, the cell itself is 20 ÷ 400 = 0.05 mm across.
**Answer: True**

**4.** This lesson's topic plays no part in science at all.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
