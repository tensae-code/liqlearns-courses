# Calculating the magnification of a microscope — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about calculating the magnification of a microscope?
a) The total magnification of a microscope is the eyepiece magnification multiplied by the objective magnification — eyepiece × objective.
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The total magnification of a microscope is the eyepiece magnification multiplied by the objective magnification — eyepiece × objective.**

**2.** What does this lesson say about two lenses multiply?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) A light microscope magnifies twice: the objective lens, close to the specimen, magnifies first, and the eyepiece lens magnifies that image again.
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) A light microscope magnifies twice: the objective lens, close to the specimen, magnifies first, and the eyepiece lens magnifies that image again.**

**3.** What does this lesson say about worked example?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) With a ×10 eyepiece and a ×40 objective, the total magnification is 10 × 40 = ×400: the image is four hundred times bigger than the specimen.
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) With a ×10 eyepiece and a ×40 objective, the total magnification is 10 × 40 = ×400: the image is four hundred times bigger than the specimen.**

**4.** What does this lesson say about magnification of a cell?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) The same multiplication tells you a real size: if a cell's image measures 20 mm across at ×400, the cell itself is 20 ÷ 400 = 0.05 mm across.
**Answer: d) The same multiplication tells you a real size: if a cell's image measures 20 mm across at ×400, the cell itself is 20 ÷ 400 = 0.05 mm across.**
