# Muscle fatigue and lactic acid — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: a hard-working muscle tires: when it works beyond its oxygen supply it builds up lactic acid and its force falls — fatigue — and rest lets it recover, as hand-clenching and endurance tests show. §17 Muscle fatigue processes under anaerobic and aerobic load**

**2.** Explain Kazakhstan's way of teaching this.
**Answer: §17 Muscle fatigue processes under anaerobic and aerobic load 6.2 Muscle fatigue Module 5: Activity 5.1 — showing that muscles working harder tire sooner (hand-clenching and bicep-curl protocols) Module 10: Muscle fatigue (with Activity 10.1, hand-endurance testing of dominant vs non-dominant hands)**
