# Hands-on — Muscle fatigue and lactic acid

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: a hard-working muscle tires: when it works beyond its oxygen supply it builds up lactic acid and its force falls — fatigue — and rest lets it recover, as hand-clenching and endurance tests show.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a hand squeezing a ball again and again above a falling force graph, with a muscle cross-section showing lactic acid building up and a rest symbol at the end. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
