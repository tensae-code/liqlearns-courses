Today: endocrine disruptors.

Remember this one thing: your hormones are chemical messengers. An endocrine disruptor is a substance from outside the body that interferes with that messaging.

France's manual teaches two cases by name. The first is bisphenol A — the manual teaches its effects, and that it has been banned in certain uses. The second is DDT, which the manual's documents present as a lure: a false signal that deceives the endocrine system.

That is why the lesson sits inside the chapter on hormones and human procreation: the disruptors act on the very system the chapter teaches.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
