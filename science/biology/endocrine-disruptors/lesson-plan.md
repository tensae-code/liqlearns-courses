# Lesson plan — Endocrine disruptors

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Endocrine disruptors
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — appended to Unit 16 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** some substances interfere with the body's hormone system — France's manual teaches them by name in its "Hormones et procréation humaine" (Hormones and human procreation) chapter, in the part "Perturbateurs endocriniens": bisphenol A — its effects, and its ban in certain uses — and DDT, presented in the chapter's documents as an endocrine-system lure (France, Grade 10 — *SVT 2de GT — Manuel Libre de Sciences de la vie et de la Terre*, student manual, Thème 3 / "Hormones et procréation humaine" / "Perturbateurs endocriniens" — repository path as cited in the placements)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands beside the course's hormone lessons and adds the half they do not teach: the substances from outside the body that disturb the hormone system, taught in France through two named cases. Hormone feedback in the menstrual cycle is recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: **Hormone feedback in the menstrual cycle**. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say, in their own words, the one thing this lesson teaches: an endocrine disruptor interferes with the hormone system, and France's manual teaches bisphenol A and DDT as its two cases.
2. Say what bisphenol A is in the manual's teaching, and what happened to some of its uses: it was banned in certain uses.
3. Say how the manual presents DDT: as an endocrine-system lure.
4. Say why disruptors are taught inside the hormones and human procreation chapter: they act on the very system that chapter teaches.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.
