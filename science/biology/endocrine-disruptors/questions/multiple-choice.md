# Endocrine disruptors — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is an endocrine disruptor?
a) A hormone made by the pituitary gland
b) A substance that interferes with the endocrine (hormone) system
c) A contraceptive pill
d) A kind of white blood cell
**Answer: b) A substance that interferes with the endocrine (hormone) system**

**2.** Which substance does France's manual teach first in its endocrine-disruptors part?
a) DDT
b) Insulin
c) Bisphenol A
d) Testosterone
**Answer: c) Bisphenol A**

**3.** What does the manual teach about bisphenol A, besides its effects?
a) It was banned in certain uses
b) It is a vitamin
c) It cures diabetes
d) It is made by the body
**Answer: a) It was banned in certain uses**

**4.** How do the chapter's documents present DDT?
a) As a hormone the body needs
b) As a vaccine
c) As a food
d) As an endocrine-system lure — a false signal that deceives the system
**Answer: d) As an endocrine-system lure — a false signal that deceives the system**
