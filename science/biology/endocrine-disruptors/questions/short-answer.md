# Endocrine disruptors — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: bisphenol A as France's manual teaches it.
**Answer: Bisphenol A is the manual's first endocrine-disruptor case: the part "Perturbateurs endocriniens" teaches its effects on the hormone system, and the response — bisphenol A was banned in certain uses.**

**2.** Explain: why a lure is the right picture for DDT's action.
**Answer: A lure deceives by resemblance. The manual's documents present DDT as an endocrine-system lure: it acts like a false signal, so the hormone system responds to something that is not its own message — the signalling is disturbed without the substance being one of the body's hormones.**
