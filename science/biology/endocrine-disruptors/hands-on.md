# Hands-on — Endocrine disruptors

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Take a key and its lock — or draw them large on paper. The key is the body's own hormone signal. Now cut a second, wrong key out of paper and label it "DDT — the lure", and hold a third card labelled "bisphenol A — banned in certain uses". Say, for each card, what the manual teaches about it.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the hormone signal as a key travelling to its lock, bisphenol A with its "banned in certain uses" tag, DDT as the false key — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap. End with this one together: why does France teach endocrine disruptors inside the hormones chapter and not inside a chapter on poisons? Say what the placement itself teaches.
