# Endocrine disruptors

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Endocrine disruptors. "Endocrine" is the hormone system. Write down, or say aloud, how you think a substance from outside the body could disturb a system that works by chemical messages, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Hormones are the body's chemical messengers: they are released in one place, travel, and deliver instructions in another. France's Seconde SVT manual teaches that system in the chapter "Hormones et procréation humaine" (Hormones and human procreation) — and in the same chapter it teaches the substances that disturb the system: the "Perturbateurs endocriniens", the endocrine disruptors. This lesson is that part of the chapter.

[Picture — a hormone signal drawn as a key travelling to its lock, with two outside substances beside it: one labelled bisphenol A with a small "banned in certain uses" tag, and one labelled DDT shown as a false key — the lure — slipping toward the lock]

## The key facts
**What an endocrine disruptor is.** An endocrine disruptor is a substance that interferes with the endocrine system — the hormone system. It does not have to poison the body outright; it disturbs the signalling itself.

**Bisphenol A — the first case.** The manual teaches bisphenol A (BPA) and its effects on the hormone system.

**Bisphenol A — the ban.** The manual also teaches the answer France gave: bisphenol A was banned in certain uses. A ban can name the uses it covers — the lesson keeps the manual's wording: banned in certain uses.

**DDT — the lure.** In the chapter's documents, DDT is presented as an endocrine-system lure (un leurre): a false signal, something that deceives the system by resembling what the system itself sends or receives.

**France's way.** In France this is taught inside the hormones chapter of the Seconde manual — the disruptors are not a separate pollution topic; they are taught where the system they disrupt is taught, with named substances, their effects, and the ban as part of the science.
