# Conscious citizens and sustainable development — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. A conscious global citizen knows what is happening in the world and how their own and others' actions affect it, valuing being fully human and the relationships among all living things; civic consciousness is the citizen's cognition of their own qualification and status, with equal civic rights and obligations, contrasted with a "servant consciousness".**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
