## Sub lesson 1 — Creating conscious citizens and ensuring sustainable development (§1.3):

a conscious citizen defined (one who values human life and all living things and takes responsibility for transforming skills into ethical action); the conscious global citizen; biology and biotechnology as tools for sustainable development — bio-based technologies that use fewer resources, recycle and reuse, and reduce waste.

## Sub lesson 2 — Added citizen definitions — conscious and civic consciousness

A conscious global citizen knows what is happening in the world and how their own and others' actions affect it, valuing being fully human and the relationships among all living things; civic consciousness is the citizen's cognition of their own qualification and status, with equal civic rights and obligations, contrasted with a "servant consciousness".
