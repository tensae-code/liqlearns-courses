# Video script — Conscious citizens and sustainable development

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A conscious global citizen knows what is happening in the world and how their own and others' actions affect it, valuing being fully human and the relationships among all living things; civic consciousness is the citizen's cognition of their own qualification and status, with equal civic rights and obligations, contrasted with a "servant consciousness".

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
