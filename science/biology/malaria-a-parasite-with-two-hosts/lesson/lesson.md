# Malaria — a parasite with two hosts

## Do it first

## The idea
Control works three ways: fewer mosquitoes, fewer bites, and treating the sick.

## The key facts
**Two hosts.** Malaria's Plasmodium parasite lives a two-host life cycle.

**In the mosquito.** The parasite reproduces sexually in the mosquito.

**In the human.** The parasite reproduces asexually in the human.

**Control.** Malaria control reduces mosquito numbers (removing breeding water, biological controls, insecticides), stops bites, and treats the sick.
