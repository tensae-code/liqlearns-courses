# Video script — Malaria — a parasite with two hosts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: Malaria — a parasite with two hosts.

Remember this one thing: malaria's Plasmodium parasite reproduces sexually in the mosquito and asexually in the human.

Malaria's Plasmodium parasite lives a two-host life cycle.

The parasite reproduces sexually in the mosquito.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
