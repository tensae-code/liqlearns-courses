# Malaria — a parasite with two hosts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about two hosts?
a) Malaria's Plasmodium parasite lives a two-host life cycle
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Malaria's Plasmodium parasite lives a two-host life cycle**

**2.** What does this lesson say about in the mosquito?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) The parasite reproduces sexually in the mosquito
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The parasite reproduces sexually in the mosquito**

**3.** What does this lesson say about in the human?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) The parasite reproduces asexually in the human
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) The parasite reproduces asexually in the human**

**4.** What does this lesson say about control?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Malaria control reduces mosquito numbers (removing breeding water, biological controls, insecticides), stops bites, and treats the sick
**Answer: d) Malaria control reduces mosquito numbers (removing breeding water, biological controls, insecticides), stops bites, and treats the sick**
