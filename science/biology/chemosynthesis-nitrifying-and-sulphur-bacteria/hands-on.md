# Hands-on — Chemosynthesis — food-making without light

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Take two handfuls of garden soil. Smell and look at both, then say aloud the invisible work happening inside: nitrifying bacteria oxidising ammonium compounds and nitrites, winning energy and leaving nitrates plants can use. Write the two steps in order: ammonium compounds → nitrites → nitrates, and name the bacterium on each step.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — soil bacteria around a root, with arrows showing ammonium compounds turning to nitrites and nitrites to nitrates, releasing the energy the bacteria live on — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
