# Chemosynthesis — food-making without light — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (some bacteria make their food without light at all: they win energy by oxidising chemicals — the nitrifying bacteria oxidise ammonium compounds and nitrites, and the sulphur bacteria oxidise sulphur compounds — and that energy builds their food; this is chemosynthesis, and it ties straight into the nitrogen cycle) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: applying the lesson to a new example from the learner's own surroundings, and explaining Nigeria's way of teaching it (Ch. 4, printed p. 49), without looking.
