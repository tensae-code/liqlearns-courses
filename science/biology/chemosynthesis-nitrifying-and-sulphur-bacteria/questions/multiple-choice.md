# Chemosynthesis — food-making without light — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What powers chemosynthesis?
a) Sunlight
b) Energy released by oxidising chemicals
c) Heat from the soil only
d) Energy from eating other bacteria
**Answer: b) Energy released by oxidising chemicals**

**2.** What does Nitrosomonas oxidise?
a) Nitrates
b) Ammonium compounds
c) Sulphur
d) Glucose
**Answer: b) Ammonium compounds**

**3.** What does Nitrobacter oxidise?
a) Ammonium compounds
b) Oxygen
c) Nitrites
d) Carbon dioxide
**Answer: c) Nitrites**

**4.** Why does chemosynthesis matter to the nitrogen cycle?
a) It removes all nitrogen from the soil
b) The nitrifying bacteria that run nitrification are chemosynthetic — they keep nitrates coming for plants
c) It turns nitrates back into air
d) It does not matter
**Answer: b) The nitrifying bacteria that run nitrification are chemosynthetic — they keep nitrates coming for plants**
