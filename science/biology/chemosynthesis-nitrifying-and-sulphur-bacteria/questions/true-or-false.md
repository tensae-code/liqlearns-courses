# Chemosynthesis — food-making without light — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Chemosynthetic bacteria need light to make their food.
**Answer: False** — They make food without light, using energy from oxidising chemicals.

**2.** Nitrosomonas and Nitrobacter are nitrifying bacteria.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Sulphur bacteria oxidise sulphur compounds to win energy.
**Answer: True**
