# Chemosynthesis — food-making without light — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: photosynthesis against chemosynthesis.
**Answer: Both are autotrophic — the organism builds its own food. Photosynthesis powers the building with light energy, as green plants, blue-green algae and chlorophyll bacteria do. Chemosynthesis powers it with energy released by oxidising chemicals, as the nitrifying bacteria (oxidising ammonium compounds and nitrites) and the sulphur bacteria do — no light is needed.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book places the chemosynthetic autotrophs directly after the photosynthetic autotrophs in Chapter 4 (printed p. 49): Nitrosomonas and Nitrobacter winning energy by oxidising ammonium compounds and nitrites, and the sulphur bacteria — food-making without light, tied to the nitrogen cycle.**
