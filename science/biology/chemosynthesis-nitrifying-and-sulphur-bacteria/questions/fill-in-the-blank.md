# Chemosynthesis — food-making without light — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** Food-making powered by chemical energy instead of light is called _____.
**Answer: chemosynthesis**

**2.** Nitrobacter oxidises _____ on the way to nitrates.
**Answer: nitrites**

**3.** The nitrifying bacteria are the engine of _____ in the nitrogen cycle.
**Answer: nitrification**
