# Haploid and diploid — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. A nucleus with two sets of chromosomes is called _____.**
**Answer: diploid**

**2. Gametes are _____, written n.**
**Answer: haploid**

**3. The division that halves the chromosome number is _____.**
**Answer: meiosis**

**4. At _____, two haploid gametes join to form a diploid zygote.**
**Answer: fertilisation**
