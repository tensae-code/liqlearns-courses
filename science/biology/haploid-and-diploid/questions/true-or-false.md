# Haploid and diploid — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Nearly all the cells of the body are diploid.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. A gamete carries both members of every chromosome pair.**
**Answer: False** — a gamete is haploid — it carries one member of each pair.

**3. The two members of a homologous pair come one from each parent.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. Without meiosis and fertilisation alternating, the chromosome number would double every generation.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
