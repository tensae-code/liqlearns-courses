# Haploid and diploid — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What does diploid (2n) mean?**
a) One set of chromosomes  b) Two sets of chromosomes — both members of every pair  c) Twice as many cells  d) No chromosomes
**Answer: b) Two sets of chromosomes — both members of every pair**

**2. Which cells are haploid?**
a) Skin cells  b) Muscle cells  c) Gametes — sperm and eggs  d) Root cells
**Answer: c) Gametes — sperm and eggs**

**3. What division makes haploid cells from diploid ones?**
a) Mitosis  b) Meiosis  c) Binary fission  d) Budding
**Answer: b) Meiosis**

**4. What happens to the chromosome number at fertilisation?**
a) It halves  b) It is lost  c) Two haploid sets join and restore the diploid number  d) It doubles twice
**Answer: c) Two haploid sets join and restore the diploid number**
