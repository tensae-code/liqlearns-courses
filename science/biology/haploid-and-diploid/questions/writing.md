# Haploid and diploid — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining haploid and diploid to a younger student, using a pair of shoes as your picture.
**Model answer: Model answer: Think of chromosomes as shoes that come in pairs. A body cell keeps the full set of pairs — that is diploid. A gamete keeps only one shoe from each pair — that is haploid. When two gametes join at fertilisation, the pairs are complete again.**
