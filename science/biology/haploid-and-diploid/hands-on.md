# Hands-on — Haploid and diploid

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Take 4 counters of two colours — say 2 red and 2 blue beans as two chromosome pairs. Arrange them as a diploid cell (both pairs whole). Now make two haploid gametes, one counter of each colour in each. Join the gametes and check: the diploid pairs are whole again.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a diploid cell with its chromosomes in matching pairs, one of each pair marked as from the mother and one from the father, beside a haploid gamete carrying only one of each kind, and the two gametes joining into a diploid zygote — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
