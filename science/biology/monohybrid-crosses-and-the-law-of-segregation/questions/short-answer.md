# Monohybrid crosses and the law of segregation — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about monohybrid cross, punnett square and segregation?**
**Answer: Crossing one trait at a time (tall TT × dwarf tt giving all tall Tt in F1, then a 3:1 pattern in F2) is worked through the Punnett square as a probability grid for fertilisation outcomes; Mendel's conclusions — paired factors, one from each parent, dominant masking recessive — become the law of segregation: allele copies separate in meiosis so each gamete carries one.**
