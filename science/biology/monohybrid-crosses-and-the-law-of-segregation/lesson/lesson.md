# Monohybrid crosses and the law of segregation

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — monohybrid crosses and the law of segregation. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Crossing one trait at a time (tall TT × dwarf tt giving all tall Tt in F1, then a 3:1 pattern in F2) is worked through the Punnett square as a probability grid for fertilisation outcomes; Mendel's conclusions — paired factors, one from each parent, dominant masking recessive — become the law of segregation: allele copies separate in meiosis so each gamete carries one.
