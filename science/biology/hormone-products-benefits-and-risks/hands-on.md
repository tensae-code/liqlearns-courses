# Hands-on — Hormone products — benefits and risks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: hormone products must be weighed case by case — the chemistry that treats dwarfism and diabetes also breeds fish, fattens livestock, masks fever and endangers athletes — so each of the book's ten real cases is judged on its own evidence.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a balance scale with a medicine bottle on one pan and a warning sign on the other, the pans level while a hand points 'judge each case'. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
