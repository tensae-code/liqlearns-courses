# Hormone products — benefits and risks — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: hormone products must be weighed case by case — the chemistry that treats dwarfism and diabetes also breeds fish, fattens livestock, masks fever and endangers athletes — so each of the book's ten real cases is judged on its own evidence. ten real cases set for judgement: insulin and glucocorticoid therapy, gonadotropins breeding the four great Chinese carps, hormones fattening pigs for lean meat, juvenile-hormone spray making silkworms eat longer and spin more, environmental endocrine disruptors, athletes' testosterone derivatives, growth hormone abused for beauty, fever-masking steroids, the oral contraceptive of Pincus and Chang Min-chueh （张明觉）, and sex-pheromone pest traps. No existing lesson teaches evaluating hormone use case by case.**
