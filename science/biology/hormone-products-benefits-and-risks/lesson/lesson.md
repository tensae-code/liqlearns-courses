# Hormone products — benefits and risks

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Hormone products — benefits and risks. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Hormone products must be weighed case by case — the chemistry that treats dwarfism and diabetes also breeds fish, fattens livestock, masks fever and endangers athletes — so each of the book's ten real cases is judged on its own evidence. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from China's People's Education Press biology textbooks, which teach it as a named topic.

[Picture — a balance scale with a medicine bottle on one pan and a warning sign on the other, the pans level while a hand points 'judge each case']

## The key facts
**Ch. 3 §2 evaluation feature "Weighing the use of hormone products".** ten real cases set for judgement: insulin and glucocorticoid therapy, gonadotropins breeding the four great Chinese carps, hormones fattening pigs for lean meat, juvenile-hormone spray making silkworms eat longer and spin more, environmental endocrine disruptors, athletes' testosterone derivatives, growth hormone abused for beauty, fever-masking steroids, the oral contraceptive of Pincus and Chang Min-chueh （张明觉）, and sex-pheromone pest traps.
