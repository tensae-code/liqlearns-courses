# Parts of a flowering plant

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Parts of a flowering plant. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
The flowering plant's body plan has two systems: the shoot above ground and the roots below. Name each part and fix its job.

[Picture 22205 — a whole plant with shoot and root systems labelled]

## The key facts
**Shoot system.** The shoot system — stem, leaves and flowers — is the part above ground.

**Root system.** The root system is the part below ground.

**Each part's job.** Each named part has its own job in the plant's life.

**Two systems.** Shoot and root together make the angiosperm body plan.
