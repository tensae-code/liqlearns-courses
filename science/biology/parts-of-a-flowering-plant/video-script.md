# Video script — Parts of a flowering plant

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Parts of a flowering plant.

Remember this one thing: a flowering plant has a shoot system above ground (stem, leaves, flowers) and a root system below, each part with its job

Shoot system: The shoot system — stem, leaves and flowers — is the part above ground.

Root system: The root system is the part below ground.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
