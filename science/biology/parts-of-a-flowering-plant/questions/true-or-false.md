# Parts of a flowering plant — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** The shoot system — stem, leaves and flowers — is the part above ground.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Root system has nothing to do with this lesson’s topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Each named part has its own job in the plant's life.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Two systems has nothing to do with this lesson’s topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
