# Parts of a flowering plant — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about shoot system?
a) The shoot system — stem, leaves and flowers — is the part above ground  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The shoot system — stem, leaves and flowers — is the part above ground**

**2.** What does this lesson say about root system?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) The root system is the part below ground  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The root system is the part below ground**

**3.** What does this lesson say about each part's job?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Each named part has its own job in the plant's life  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Each named part has its own job in the plant's life**

**4.** What does this lesson say about two systems?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Shoot and root together make the angiosperm body plan
**Answer: d) Shoot and root together make the angiosperm body plan**
