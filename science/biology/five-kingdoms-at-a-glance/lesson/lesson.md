# Five kingdoms at a glance

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Five kingdoms at a glance. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
The five kingdoms are separated mainly by cell type, body organisation and way of feeding.

[Picture 21017 — the five kingdoms as five panels]

## The key facts
**The five.** The five kingdoms are Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae and Animalia.

**Cell type.** Kingdoms are separated partly by cell type — prokaryotic or eukaryotic.

**Organisation.** Kingdoms are separated partly by body organisation — single-celled or multicellular.

**Feeding.** Kingdoms are separated partly by way of feeding — making food, absorbing it, or eating it.
