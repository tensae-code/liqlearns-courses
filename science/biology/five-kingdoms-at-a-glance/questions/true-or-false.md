# Five kingdoms at a glance — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** The five kingdoms are Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae and Animalia.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Cell type has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Kingdoms are separated partly by body organisation — single-celled or multicellular.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Feeding has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
