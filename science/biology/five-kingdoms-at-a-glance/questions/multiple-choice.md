# Five kingdoms at a glance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about the five?
a) The five kingdoms are Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae and Animalia
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The five kingdoms are Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae and Animalia**

**2.** What does this lesson say about cell type?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Kingdoms are separated partly by cell type — prokaryotic or eukaryotic
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Kingdoms are separated partly by cell type — prokaryotic or eukaryotic**

**3.** What does this lesson say about organisation?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Kingdoms are separated partly by body organisation — single-celled or multicellular
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Kingdoms are separated partly by body organisation — single-celled or multicellular**

**4.** What does this lesson say about feeding?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Kingdoms are separated partly by way of feeding — making food, absorbing it, or eating it
**Answer: d) Kingdoms are separated partly by way of feeding — making food, absorbing it, or eating it**
