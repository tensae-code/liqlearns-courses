# Hands-on — Breeding achievements in Kazakhstan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: Kazakhstan has its own breeding record — plant varieties and animal breeds selected by its own breeders for its steppe conditions — which its Grade 8 book teaches as a named section of the selection chapter, beside Vavilov's centres of origin.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a Kazakh steppe farm with a bred wheat variety and a bred sheep breed in front, each with a label naming it as a Kazakhstan breeding achievement, and a research institute building behind. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
