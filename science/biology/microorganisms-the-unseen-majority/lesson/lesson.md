# Microorganisms — the unseen majority

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — microorganisms. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Microbes are presented as the dominant organisms of most natural environments, woven through the cycles of all living things, without which life on Earth would not survive; bacteria are placed quantitatively (roughly 1,000 to 10 million bacteria per gram of soil; bacteria in milk, butter, cheese and fermented drinks) and by tolerance (thermophiles above 40 °C, psychrophiles in extreme cold, survival from below pH 1 to pH 13).
