# Biotechnology — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) biotechnology defined as using living organisms or their products to make products that benefit humans; recombinant DNA and genetically modified organisms; a gene moved from one species into another is a transgene and the receiver a transgenic organism; crop modification for yield and novel products.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) microbe-driven food production from bread, beer, tej, tella and wine fermentation to industrial single-cell protein — Pruteen grown from waste methanol by a bacterium, mycoprotein from a fungus sold as a meat substitute, and Spirulina harvested from lakes as a nutrient-rich food.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) micropropagation by tissue culture — tissue fragments grown in an artificial medium into many uniform, disease-free plants (palm, orchids, bananas, carrots) — and breed improvement, figured with a Borana bull and a dairy cow.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
