# Biotechnology — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about biotechnology, gmos and transgenic organisms (§1.4 opening):?**
**Answer: biotechnology defined as using living organisms or their products to make products that benefit humans; recombinant DNA and genetically modified organisms; a gene moved from one species into another is a transgene and the receiver a transgenic organism; crop modification for yield and novel products.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about traditional biotechnology and food production (§1.4(i)):?**
**Answer: microbe-driven food production from bread, beer, tej, tella and wine fermentation to industrial single-cell protein — Pruteen grown from waste methanol by a bacterium, mycoprotein from a fungus sold as a meat substitute, and Spirulina harvested from lakes as a nutrient-rich food.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about biotechnology in agriculture (§1.4):?**
**Answer: micropropagation by tissue culture — tissue fragments grown in an artificial medium into many uniform, disease-free plants (palm, orchids, bananas, carrots) — and breed improvement, figured with a Borana bull and a dairy cow.**
