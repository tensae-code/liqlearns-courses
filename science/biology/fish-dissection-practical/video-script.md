Today: Fish dissection — a practical.

Remember this one thing: A fish dissection is a guided practical that studies the fish from the outside in: first the external structure fitted to life in water, then — opened carefully, step by step — the internal organs in their places.

A dissection is a practical: you study a real fish, from the outside in, in a guided order.

Outside first: the scales, the fins, the lateral line, and the hard cover over the gills — every part fitted to life in water.

Then inside: the red gills, the heart, the stomach and intestine, and the swim bladder — each found, named and drawn where it lies.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
