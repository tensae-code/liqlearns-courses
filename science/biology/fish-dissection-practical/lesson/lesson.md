# Fish dissection — a practical

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Fish dissection — a practical. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Armenia's Grade 7 Biology book does not leave the fish on the page. After the fishes section, its laboratory work §21 is a guided dissection: the learner takes a real fish and studies its external and internal structure with their own eyes and hands.

## The key facts
**A practical, not a reading.** Armenia's Grade 7 Biology book teaches the fish twice: first its structure in the fishes section, then again as Laboratory work — a guided dissection. The lesson is done with the hands: the learner studies a real fish, outside and inside, in the book's own order.

**Outside first.** Before anything is cut, the external structure is studied: the streamlined body covered in scales, the fins and where each sits, the lateral line along the flank, and the hard gill cover (operculum) over the gills — every part fitted to moving, steering and breathing in water.

**Then inside, in order.** Opened carefully along the body wall, the fish shows its internal structure in place: the red feathery gills under their cover, the heart, the stomach and intestine of the digestive tract, and the swim bladder that holds the fish at its depth. Each organ is found, named and drawn where it lies — nothing is pulled out before it has been seen in its place.

**Guided and recorded.** A dissection is guided work: the steps are followed in order, sharp tools are handled as the guidance directs, and what is seen is recorded in labelled drawings. The drawing is the proof — the structure must be known well enough to draw it from the real fish, not from memory of a picture.

**Armenia's way.** The dissection stands in the book as §21, one of its numbered laboratory works, immediately after the fishes section §20 — Armenia teaches the group first, then makes the learner prove the structure on the fish itself.
