# Hands-on — Fish dissection — a practical

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
At a market or kitchen, look at a whole fish before it is cooked: find the scales, each fin, the lateral line and the gill cover, and say what each one is for. (The full dissection itself is guided laboratory work, done with a teacher as the book directs.)

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of a fish on a dissection tray, labelled outside and inside — fins, scales, lateral line and gill cover outside; gills, heart, stomach, intestine and swim bladder inside, each labelled — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
