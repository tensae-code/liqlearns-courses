# Fish dissection — a practical — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: outside first.
**Answer: Before anything is cut, the external structure is studied: the streamlined body covered in scales, the fins and where each sits, the lateral line along the flank, and the hard gill cover (operculum) over the gills — every part fitted to moving, steering and breathing in water.**

**2.** Explain: guided and recorded.
**Answer: A dissection is guided work: the steps are followed in order, sharp tools are handled as the guidance directs, and what is seen is recorded in labelled drawings.**
