# Fish dissection — a practical — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about a fish dissection?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) A fish dissection is a guided practical that studies the fish from the outside in
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) A fish dissection is a guided practical that studies the fish from the outside in**

**2.** What does this lesson say about outside first?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) The external structure — scales, fins, lateral line and gill cover — is studied before anything is cut
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) The external structure — scales, fins, lateral line and gill cover — is studied before anything is cut**

**3.** What does this lesson say about then inside, in order?
a) The swim bladder is the only organ inside a fish
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Opened carefully, the fish shows the gills, heart, stomach, intestine and swim bladder in their places
**Answer: d) Opened carefully, the fish shows the gills, heart, stomach, intestine and swim bladder in their places**

**4.** What does this lesson say about guided and recorded?
a) The dissection is guided work, followed in order, and what is seen is recorded in labelled drawings
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The dissection is guided work, followed in order, and what is seen is recorded in labelled drawings**
