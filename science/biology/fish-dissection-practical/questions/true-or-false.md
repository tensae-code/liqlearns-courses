# Fish dissection — a practical — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A fish dissection studies the fish from the outside in.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The fish is cut open before its external structure is studied.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**

**3.** The swim bladder holds the fish at its depth in the water.
**Answer: True**

**4.** In a dissection, organs are pulled out before they are seen in their places.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
