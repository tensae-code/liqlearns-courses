# Useful insects in farming and as food

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — useful insects in farming and as food. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Insects serve farming as pollinators and as natural pest regulators — predators and parasitoids such as mantises, ladybird beetles, ground beetles, lacewings and hoverflies used in biological control of crop pests; insects are also promoted as food — fast-growing, efficient converters, rich in protein, fat and minerals — and pupils are asked for their own community's attitude to eating insects in Ethiopia.
