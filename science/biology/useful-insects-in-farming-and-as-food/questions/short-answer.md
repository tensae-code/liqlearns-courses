# Useful insects in farming and as food — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about beneficial insects?**
**Answer: Insects serve farming as pollinators and as natural pest regulators — predators and parasitoids such as mantises, ladybird beetles, ground beetles, lacewings and hoverflies used in biological control of crop pests; insects are also promoted as food — fast-growing, efficient converters, rich in protein, fat and minerals — and pupils are asked for their own community's attitude to eating insects in Ethiopia.**
