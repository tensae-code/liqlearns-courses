# The biology of cancer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is C12 of Italy's Grade 11 human-body textbook?**
a) The nervous system  b) The sense organs  c) The biology of cancer  d) The muscular and skeletal systems
**Answer: c) The biology of cancer**

**2. What does the chapter's title itself say?**
a) Cancer is only a medical matter, not biology  b) Cancer has a biology of its own, studied as a subject in itself  c) Cancer belongs to one organ only  d) Cancer is a closing note, not a chapter
**Answer: b) Cancer has a biology of its own, studied as a subject in itself**

**3. Why can the cancer chapter draw on the whole course?**
a) Because it is the longest chapter  b) Because it stands after every body system has been taught  c) Because it repeats the first chapter  d) Because it is written for teachers
**Answer: b) Because it stands after every body system has been taught**

**4. How do most school books treat this chapter, as the placements record?**
a) They put it first  b) They give it two chapters  c) Most school books omit it  d) They teach it in chemistry
**Answer: c) Most school books omit it**
