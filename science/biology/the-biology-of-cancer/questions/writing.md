# The biology of cancer — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson to a younger student. Use the words biology of cancer and closing chapter once each.
**Model answer: When you study the human body, you learn its systems one by one — heart, lungs, digestion, nerves and the rest. Italy's school textbook then does something unusual: its last chapter is the biology of cancer, a full chapter on cancer as a subject of biology itself. It is a closing chapter most school books leave out, placed at the end because cancer can arise in any system the book has taught.**
