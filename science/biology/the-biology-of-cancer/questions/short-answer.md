# The biology of cancer — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the biology of cancer as Italy's book teaches it, in one or two sentences.
**Answer: Italy's Grade 11 human-body textbook (*La nuova biologia.blu PLUS — Il corpo umano*, Sadava et al., Zanichelli) closes its whole course with C12, The biology of cancer (PDF p. 267) — a full chapter treating cancer as a subject of biology in itself, standing after all eleven chapters on the body's architecture and systems.**

**2.** Explain: why the chapter stands last.
**Answer: Cancer can arise in any of the body's systems, so the book teaches it only after every system has been met; the closing chapter can then draw on the whole body the course has built. It is a closing chapter most school books omit.**
