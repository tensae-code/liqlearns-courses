# The biology of cancer — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Italy's Grade 11 human-body textbook closes with a full chapter on the biology of cancer.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (C12, PDF p. 267).

**2. The biology of cancer is the first chapter of the volume.**
**Answer: False** — it is C12, the closing chapter, after the eleven system chapters.

**3. Most school books include a closing chapter on the biology of cancer.**
**Answer: False** — the placements record it as a closing chapter most school books omit.

**4. The chapter's place after all the systems lets it draw on the whole body.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
