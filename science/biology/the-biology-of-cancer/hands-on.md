# Hands-on — The biology of cancer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
List the body systems you know — say them aloud in the order Italy's book teaches them — and finish by naming the closing chapter, The biology of cancer. Say, for two of the systems, one sentence each on why a chapter about cancer needs that system taught first.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the row of the body's systems with the final panel labelled “the biology of cancer (C12)” — and write under it “the closing chapter”. Check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
