Today: The biology of cancer.

Remember this one thing: cancer is not just an illness you hear about — it is a subject biology studies, and one Italian textbook ends its whole human-body course with it.

Think of the human-body course as a journey: first how the body is built, then system by system — the heart and blood, breathing, digestion, the kidneys' balance, defence, hormones, reproduction, the nervous system, the senses, muscle and bone. Eleven chapters.

Then comes a twelfth. Italy's Grade 11 textbook closes with The biology of cancer — a full chapter on cancer itself. Not a paragraph. Not a warning box. A chapter — the last one in the book.

Why last? Because cancer can arise in any system, so the book teaches it after you know them all. Most school books leave this chapter out. This one keeps it, and ends there.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
