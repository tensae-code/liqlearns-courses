# The biology of cancer

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The biology of cancer. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Most school books about the human body end with the last body system. Italy's Grade 11 textbook, *La nuova biologia.blu PLUS — Il corpo umano*, does something different: after its eleven chapters on the body's architecture and systems, it closes the whole volume with C12 (PDF p. 267) — The biology of cancer — a full chapter on cancer as a subject of biology in itself. No lesson in this course took cancer's own biology as its one thing before, so this lesson is built for it. (The course's lesson on cancer and tumours teaches uncontrolled cell division; this lesson teaches cancer as the closing subject of the human-body course.)

[Picture — the human-body course as a row of system diagrams — heart, lungs, gut, nerves — with a final panel after them all, labelled “the biology of cancer”]

## The key facts
**Cancer is taught as a biology, not as an appendix.** The chapter is titled The biology of cancer: cancer is presented as something biology studies in its own right — a subject with its own place in the science, not a footnote inside one organ's chapter.

**It closes the human-body course.** C12 is the twelfth and last chapter of the volume: architecture of the body, circulation, respiration, digestion, urinary balance, lymph and immunity, endocrine, reproduction, nervous system, sense organs, muscle and skeleton — and then cancer, after them all.

**Its place is the point.** A disease that can arise in any system is taught after every system has been met, so the learner brings the whole body to it — the chapter can draw on each system the course has built.

**A closing chapter most school books omit.** The placements record it plainly: this is a closing chapter most school books omit. Italy's book keeps it, ending the human-body course on the biology of cancer rather than on the last healthy system.

**Italy's way.** In Italy's Grade 11 course, the human body is not finished when its systems are described: the book's final word is a full chapter on the biology of cancer — the disease studied as biology, at the end of the body.
