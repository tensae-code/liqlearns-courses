Today: Hibernation, dormancy and active life.

Remember this one thing: animals meet the cold season two ways — active life, or hibernation and torpor, in which the animal burns the fat it laid down in the warm season until conditions return.

Moldova's Grade 7 book (5.1 Active life and hibernation) teaches: hibernation burning fat laid down in the warm season, torpor ("amorțire"), lesser horseshoe bat summer/winter. (printed p. 136).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
