# Hands-on — Hibernation, dormancy and active life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: animals meet the cold season two ways — active life, or hibernation and torpor, in which the animal burns the fat it laid down in the warm season until conditions return.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a lesser horseshoe bat shown twice: active in summer and hibernating in winter, with its fat store marked. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
