# Hibernation, dormancy and active life — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Moldova's Grade 7 book (5.1 Active life and hibernation) teaches: hibernation burning fat laid down in the warm season, torpor ("amorțire"), lesser horseshoe bat summer/winter. (printed p. 136).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Moldova's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Biologie: manual pentru clasa a 7-a (Grade 7)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with hibernation, dormancy and active life.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is hibernation, dormancy and active life.
