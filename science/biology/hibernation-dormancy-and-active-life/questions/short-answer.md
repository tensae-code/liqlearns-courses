# Hibernation, dormancy and active life — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: animals meet the cold season two ways — active life, or hibernation and torpor, in which the animal burns the fat it laid down in the warm season until conditions return. Moldova's Grade 7 book (5.1 Active life and hibernation) teaches: hibernation burning fat laid down in the warm season, torpor ("amorțire"), lesser horseshoe bat summer/winter. (printed p. 136).**

**2.** Explain Moldova's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Moldova's Grade 7 book opens its seasonal chapter with active life against hibernation: fat laid down in the warm season, torpor (“amorțire”), and the lesser horseshoe bat's summer and winter.**
