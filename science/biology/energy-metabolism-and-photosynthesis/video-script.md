# Video script — Energy, metabolism and photosynthesis

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A comparison table sets catabolism (all breakdown reactions, releasing energy) against anabolism (all building reactions, requiring energy); photosynthesis is argued essential as the atmosphere's number-one oxygen source and the primary energy route by which green plants and trees make food from sunlight, carbon dioxide and water.
