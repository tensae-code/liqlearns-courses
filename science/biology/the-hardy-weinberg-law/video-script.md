Today: The Hardy–Weinberg law.

Remember this one thing: if nothing pushes a population, its allele frequencies do not change.

No selection, no mutation, no migration, random mating, large numbers — then generation after generation keeps the same frequencies. That balance is Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium, and it is written p² + 2pq + q² = 1.

And when real frequencies do change, the law tells you where to look: one of those pushes is at work. That is evolution, caught in the act.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
