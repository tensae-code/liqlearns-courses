# The Hardy–Weinberg law — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The Hardy–Weinberg genotype frequencies sum as p² + 2pq + q² = _____.**
**Answer: 1**

**2. A population whose frequencies are not changing is in Hardy–Weinberg _____.**
**Answer: equilibrium**

**3. The frequency of the heterozygous genotype is written _____.**
**Answer: 2pq**

**4. Genetic _____ in a small population is one force that breaks the equilibrium.**
**Answer: drift**
