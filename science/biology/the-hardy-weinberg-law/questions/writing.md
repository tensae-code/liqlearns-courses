# The Hardy–Weinberg law — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why a law about populations that do not change is useful for studying evolution.
**Model answer: The law gives the balance point — the frequencies a population keeps when nothing is pushing it. When a real population's frequencies are found changing, the law's list of conditions tells you what to look for: selection, mutation, migration, non-random mating or drift. The change, measured against the balance, is evolution made visible.**
