# The Hardy–Weinberg law — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the Hardy–Weinberg law and its conditions.
**Answer: If a population has no selection, no mutation, no migration, random mating and large numbers, its allele and genotype frequencies stay constant generation to generation — p² + 2pq + q² = 1. Change in the frequencies means one of those conditions has failed.**

**2.** Explain: Bhutan's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Bhutan's Class 10 book (Ch. 6.4, printed pp. 166–167) teaches the Hardy–Weinberg law with the types of species, immediately before the factors responsible for speciation — selection, drift, isolation and migration.**
