# The Hardy–Weinberg law — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium means the population is evolving fast.**
**Answer: False** — equilibrium means the frequencies are not changing — the population is not evolving.

**2. p + q = 1 for two alleles.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Migration into a population can change its allele frequencies.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. The law is useless because real populations change.**
**Answer: False** — real populations are measured against the balance; a change points to the force causing it.
