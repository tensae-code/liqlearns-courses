# The Hardy–Weinberg law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The Hardy–Weinberg law says that, when a population is not evolving, allele frequencies…**
a) double each generation  b) stay the same from generation to generation  c) always become equal  d) disappear
**Answer: b) stay the same from generation to generation**

**2. Which condition is required for Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium?**
a) Strong selection  b) New mutations every generation  c) No selection, no mutation, no migration, random mating, large numbers  d) A very small population
**Answer: c) No selection, no mutation, no migration, random mating, large numbers**

**3. If q = 0.3, the homozygous recessive frequency q² is…**
a) 0.3  b) 0.09  c) 0.7  d) 0.49
**Answer: b) 0.09**

**4. Changing allele frequencies in a real population show that…**
a) the law is broken and useless  b) one of the pushes — selection, mutation, migration, drift, non-random mating — is at work  c) the counts were wrong  d) equilibrium is permanent
**Answer: b) one of the pushes — selection, mutation, migration, drift, non-random mating — is at work**
