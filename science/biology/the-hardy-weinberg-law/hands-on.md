# Hands-on — The Hardy–Weinberg law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Put 80 light beads and 20 dark beads in a bag — allele frequencies p = 0.8 and q = 0.2. Draw pairs at random, record each pair as a genotype, and tally: your counts should land near p² = 0.64, 2pq = 0.32, q² = 0.04 of the draws.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the p², 2pq, q² grid and the level balance across generations — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
