# The lymphatic system — drainage and defence

## Do it first

## The idea
Beside the blood's closed loop runs an open network. The lymphatic system drains tissue fluid back to the blood and defends the body on the way — which is why glands swell near an injury.

[Picture 22259 — a body outline with the main lymph vessels and node clusters marked]

## The key facts
**Open system.** The lymphatic system is an open system returning tissue fluid to the blood.

**Parts.** It is made of vessels, nodes and organs including the tonsils.

**Drainage and defence.** It works as the body's drainage and defence network.

**Swollen glands.** Glands at groin and armpit can swell after a limb injury, as the system responds.
