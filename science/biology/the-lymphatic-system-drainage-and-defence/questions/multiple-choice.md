# The lymphatic system — drainage and defence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about open system?
a) The lymphatic system is an open system returning tissue fluid to the blood  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The lymphatic system is an open system returning tissue fluid to the blood**

**2.** What does this lesson say about parts?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It is made of vessels, nodes and organs including the tonsils  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) It is made of vessels, nodes and organs including the tonsils**

**3.** What does this lesson say about drainage and defence?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It works as the body's drainage and defence network  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) It works as the body's drainage and defence network**

**4.** What does this lesson say about swollen glands?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Glands at groin and armpit can swell after a limb injury, as the system responds
**Answer: d) Glands at groin and armpit can swell after a limb injury, as the system responds**
