# The sub-fields of biology

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The sub-fields of biology. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Biology is a big subject, so it is divided into smaller fields. Each sub-field studies one slice of life. This map is the one you will use all year: every later unit sits somewhere on it.

[Picture 22200 — a branching map of biology with one example organism on each branch]

## The key facts
**Sub-fields.** Biology is divided into sub-fields, each studying one slice of life.

**One slice each.** Each sub-field studies one part of living things, not everything at once.

**The map.** The sub-fields map opens the year, so later units can be located on it.

**Sorting.** Sorting the fields is the first skill — later lessons sort them into pure and applied.
