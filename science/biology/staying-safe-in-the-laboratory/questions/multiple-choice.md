# Staying safe in the laboratory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about before starting?
a) Know the safety signs and check equipment before starting work
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Know the safety signs and check equipment before starting work**

**2.** What does this lesson say about dressing?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Dress for the laboratory: coat, goggles, gloves, covered feet — no sandals — and a mask for toxic chemicals
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Dress for the laboratory: coat, goggles, gloves, covered feet — no sandals — and a mask for toxic chemicals**

**3.** What does this lesson say about food rule?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Never eat, drink, taste or sniff in the laboratory, and never store food with experiments or chemicals
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Never eat, drink, taste or sniff in the laboratory, and never store food with experiments or chemicals**

**4.** What does this lesson say about care?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Handle glass, flames and cultures carefully and report accidents
**Answer: d) Handle glass, flames and cultures carefully and report accidents**
