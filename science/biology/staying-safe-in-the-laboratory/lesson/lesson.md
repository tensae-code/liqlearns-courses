# Staying safe in the laboratory

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Staying safe in the laboratory. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Safety is a habit, not a poster. Dress right, handle things carefully, and report accidents at once.

[Picture 29209 — a correctly dressed pupil at a bench with safety signs]

## The key facts
**Before starting.** Know the safety signs and check equipment before starting work.

**Dressing.** Dress for the laboratory: coat, goggles, gloves, covered feet — no sandals — and a mask for toxic chemicals.

**Food rule.** Never eat, drink, taste or sniff in the laboratory, and never store food with experiments or chemicals.

**Care.** Handle glass, flames and cultures carefully and report accidents.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words: laboratory safety means controlling hazards before small problems become large ones. Use each key fact once. Then let your companion explain it back to you.
