# Video script — Staying safe in the laboratory

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Staying safe in the laboratory.

Remember this one thing: laboratory safety means controlling hazards before small problems become large ones.

Know the safety signs and check equipment before starting work.

Dress for the laboratory: coat, goggles, gloves, covered feet — no sandals — and a mask for toxic chemicals.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
