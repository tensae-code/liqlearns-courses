# Specific defence and immunity — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: specific defence is immunity proper: the body recognises an antigen as foreign and answers with antibodies and dedicated cells — active immunity made by the body itself, lasting with memory, and passive immunity borrowed ready-made, as from mother to baby. specific defence mechanisms — immunity: antigens, antibodies, active/passive immunity**

**2.** Explain Greece's way of teaching this.
**Answer: specific defence mechanisms — immunity: antigens, antibodies, active/passive immunity**
