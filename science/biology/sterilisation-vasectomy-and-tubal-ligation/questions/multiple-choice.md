# Sterilisation — vasectomy and tubal ligation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about tubal ligation?

c) It happens only at night and nowhere else

**Answer: a) Tubal ligation seals or ties each oviduct in women**

**2.** What does this lesson say about vasectomy?

**Answer: b) Vasectomy cuts and ties each vas deferens in men**
