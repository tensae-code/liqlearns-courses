# Sterilisation — vasectomy and tubal ligation

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Sterilisation — vasectomy and tubal ligation. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Hormone production and sexual function are unaffected in both. Effectiveness is the highest of all methods — and the methods are not easily reversible.

[Picture 21049 — two small diagrams showing where each tube is tied]

## The key facts
**Tubal ligation.** Tubal ligation seals or ties each oviduct in women.

**Vasectomy.** Vasectomy cuts and ties each vas deferens in men.

**Unaffected.** Hormone production and sexual function are unaffected by sterilisation.

**Permanent.** Sterilisation has the highest effectiveness of all methods and is not easily reversible.
