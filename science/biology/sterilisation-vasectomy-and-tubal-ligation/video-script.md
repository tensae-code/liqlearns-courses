Today: Sterilisation — vasectomy and tubal ligation.

Remember this one thing: sterilisation permanently prevents gamete release — tubal ligation in women, vasectomy in men.

Tubal ligation seals or ties each oviduct in women.

Vasectomy cuts and ties each vas deferens in men.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
