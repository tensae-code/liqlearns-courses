# Gene regulation — switching genes on and off

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Gene regulation — switching genes on and off. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Cells switch genes on and off: prokaryotes group genes in operons a repressor can shut, and eukaryotes control expression at many levels, so the same genome makes a nerve cell and a liver cell — regulation is how one instruction book runs every cell kind. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from Greece's Gymnasio and Lykeio biology books, which teach it as a named topic.
