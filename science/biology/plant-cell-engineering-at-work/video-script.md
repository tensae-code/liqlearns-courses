Today: Plant cell engineering at work.

Remember this one thing: plant cell engineering at work: micropropagation multiplying orchids and medicinal plants in tubes, virus-free stock from shoot-tip culture yielding over 50% more potatoes, haploid breeding through anther culture, useful mutants selected from cultured cells, and cell-product factories growing shikonin, taxol and ginseng saponins.

micropropagation (the Dutch orchid industry; Dendrobium candidum tube plantlets at scale in China); virus-free stock from shoot-tip culture (potatoes yielding over 50% more); haploid breeding through anther culture — China's Danyu No. 1 tobacco of 1974, the world's first haploid crop variety; useful mutants selected from cultured cells; and cell-product factories — shikonin, the first marketed plant-cell product (1980s), taxol from cultured yew cells, ginseng saponins cultured in China since 1963.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
