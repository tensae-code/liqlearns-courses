# Hands-on — Plant cell engineering at work

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: plant cell engineering at work: micropropagation multiplying orchids and medicinal plants in tubes, virus-free stock from shoot-tip culture yielding over 50% more potatoes, haploid breeding through anther culture, useful mutants selected from cultured cells, and cell-product factories growing shikonin, taxol and ginseng saponins.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: shelves of culture tubes and jars holding tiny green plantlets, with a potato plant and a medicine bottle at the end of the shelf. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
