# Hands-on — Filter feeding and deposit feeding

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Pour muddy water slowly through a piece of fine cloth into a bowl. What stays on the cloth is what a filter feeder keeps; the water passes on. Say aloud which book animal does the same with gill rakers and which with baleen. Then look at a handful of soil and say what a deposit feeder would get from passing it through its body.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a fish head with gill rakers straining plankton from water flowing through its gills, beside a blue whale's baleen plates and an animal eating its way through soil — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
