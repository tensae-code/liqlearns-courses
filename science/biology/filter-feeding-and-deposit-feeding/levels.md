# Filter feeding and deposit feeding — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (some animals eat without hunting: filter feeders strain tiny food out of the water passing over their gills or baleen, and deposit feeders eat the soil or sediment itself and digest the living matter out of it) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: applying the lesson to a new example from the learner's own surroundings, and explaining Nigeria's way of teaching it (Ch. 6, printed p. 102), without looking.
