# Filter feeding and deposit feeding — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What do herrings and sardines use to filter feed?
a) Teeth
b) Gill rakers
c) Baleen plates
d) Their tails
**Answer: b) Gill rakers**

**2.** What does the blue whale strain from sea water?
a) Fish
b) Krill
c) Seaweed
d) Sand
**Answer: b) Krill**

**3.** What does a deposit feeder eat?
a) Only living prey it catches
b) Soil or sediment, digesting the organic matter out of it
c) Leaves
d) Plankton in the water
**Answer: b) Soil or sediment, digesting the organic matter out of it**

**4.** Filter feeding works because…
a) the animal runs fast
b) water carrying fine food particles passes over a straining surface
c) the food is poisonous to others
d) the animal sleeps in the current
**Answer: b) water carrying fine food particles passes over a straining surface**
