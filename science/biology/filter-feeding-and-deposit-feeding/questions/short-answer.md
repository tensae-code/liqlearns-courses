# Filter feeding and deposit feeding — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: filter feeding against deposit feeding.
**Answer: A filter feeder strains fine food particles out of water — herrings and sardines through their gill rakers, the blue whale through baleen. A deposit feeder instead swallows the deposit itself — soil or sediment — and digests the organic matter out of it as it passes through. One sieves the water; the other eats the ground.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book teaches feeding mechanisms in holozoic organisms in Chapter 6 (printed p. 102): filter feeding through the gill rakers of herrings and sardines and in the blue whale, and deposit feeding through soil and sediment.**
