# Filter feeding and deposit feeding — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** Herrings and sardines strain plankton through their _____ rakers.
**Answer: gill**

**2.** The blue whale strains krill through plates of _____.
**Answer: baleen**

**3.** An animal that eats soil or sediment for the organic matter in it is a _____ feeder.
**Answer: deposit**
