# Filter feeding and deposit feeding — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** The blue whale is a filter feeder.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Deposit feeders strain plankton from the water through gill rakers.
**Answer: False** — That is filter feeding (herrings and sardines). Deposit feeders take in soil or sediment and digest the organic matter from it.

**3.** Gill rakers sieve food from the same water a fish breathes through.
**Answer: True**
