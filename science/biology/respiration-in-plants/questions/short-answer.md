# Respiration in plants — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: plants respire as animals do — every living cell takes in oxygen and releases energy from food — but with no special breathing organs: air moves in through stomata, lenticels and root surfaces, day and night, even while photosynthesis runs the other way in the light. how plants take in oxygen without special organs**
