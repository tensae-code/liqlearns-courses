# Respiration in plants — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (plants respire as animals do — every living cell takes in oxygen and releases energy from food — but with no special breathing organs: air moves in through stomata, lenticels and root surfaces, day and night, even while photosynthesis runs the other way in the light) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
