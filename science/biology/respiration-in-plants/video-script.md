Today: Respiration in plants.

Remember this one thing: plants respire as animals do — every living cell takes in oxygen and releases energy from food — but with no special breathing organs: air moves in through stomata, lenticels and root surfaces, day and night, even while photosynthesis runs the other way in the light.
