# Tools technology gives biology

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — tools technology gives biology. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Technology serves biology in biochemical, biomedical (including medicinal-plant chemistry), biophysical and environmental studies; the tool catalogue runs from digital thermometers, pregnancy urine tests, diabetic blood-sugar tests and HIV tests, through microscopes, CT and PET scanning, to GPS and a handheld body-fat calculator; a lab has pupils measure classmates' temperatures and visit a health centre to discuss the tests.
