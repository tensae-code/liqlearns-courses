# Effects of agricultural activities on ecosystems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does land clearing do first to an ecosystem?
a) Adds new species
b) Removes the plant cover that protected the soil
c) Makes rain fall
d) Cools the soil permanently
**Answer: b) Removes the plant cover that protected the soil**

**2.** Which practice does the book single out for its ecosystem effects?
a) Irrigation
b) Bush burning
c) Fencing
d) Harvesting by hand
**Answer: b) Bush burning**

**3.** Why do farming systems have different ecosystem effects?
a) They are practised in different countries only
b) Each system works the cleared land in its own way
c) Some use no land
d) They all burn the bush
**Answer: b) Each system works the cleared land in its own way**

**4.** Exposed soil after clearing and burning is open to…
a) erosion by rain and wind
b) nothing — it is protected
c) frost
d) the sea
**Answer: a) erosion by rain and wind**
