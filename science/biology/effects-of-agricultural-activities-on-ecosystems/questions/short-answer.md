# Effects of agricultural activities on ecosystems — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: how clearing, farming systems and bush burning affect an ecosystem.
**Answer: Clearing strips away the plant cover that shaded and held the soil, exposing it to sun, rain and erosion. The farming systems then work that cleared land in different ways, with different effects on soil and organisms. Bush burning destroys vegetation and soil organisms and leaves the ground bare. Together they change the ecosystem's soil, water and living community.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book teaches the effects of agricultural activities on ecological systems in Chapter 9 (printed p. 139), working through clearing, the farming systems and bush burning crop by crop and practice by practice.**
