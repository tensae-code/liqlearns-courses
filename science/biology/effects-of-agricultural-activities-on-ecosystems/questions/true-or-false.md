# Effects of agricultural activities on ecosystems — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Bush burning leaves the soil protected by a thick plant cover.
**Answer: False** — Burning destroys the vegetation cover and soil organisms and leaves the soil exposed.

**2.** Clearing land changes an ecosystem before any crop is grown.
**Answer: True**

**3.** The book teaches agricultural effects practice by practice, not as a general slogan.
**Answer: True**
