# Effects of agricultural activities on ecosystems — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** Removing the plant cover from land is called _____.
**Answer: clearing**

**2.** The farming practice the book singles out, where fire sweeps the land, is bush _____.
**Answer: burning**

**3.** Soil left bare after clearing and burning is washed or blown away by _____.
**Answer: erosion**
