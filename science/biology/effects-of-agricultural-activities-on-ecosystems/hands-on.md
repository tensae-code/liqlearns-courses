# Hands-on — Effects of agricultural activities on ecosystems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find a piece of cleared or burnt ground and a piece of covered ground (grass or leaf litter) near each other. Compare the soil surface in both — cover, colour, hardness, what is living there. Say aloud which agricultural practices made the difference, using the book's three: clearing, the farming system, bush burning.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a hillside in three panels: forest being cleared, farmland under cultivation, and a bush fire burning through dry grass, with the soil exposed in each — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
