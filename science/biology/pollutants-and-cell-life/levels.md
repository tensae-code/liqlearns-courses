# Pollutants and cell life — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (pollutants act at the level of the cell: Greece's Grade 11 laboratory guide tests their effects on living cells directly — growth, division and survival change with what the cells are exposed to — which is how the damage pollution does to whole organisms begins) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
