# Hands-on — Pollutants and cell life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: pollutants act at the level of the cell: Greece's Grade 11 laboratory guide tests their effects on living cells directly — growth, division and survival change with what the cells are exposed to — which is how the damage pollution does to whole organisms begins.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: two microscope views of the same cells: one field green and dividing evenly, one field with shrunken and stopped cells under a grey haze. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
