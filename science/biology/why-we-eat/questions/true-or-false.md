# Why we eat — energy, building, protection — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Food provides energy for movement, growth and reproduction.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Building has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Food protects the body, since shortage of particular food groups causes deficiency diseases.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Fuel has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
