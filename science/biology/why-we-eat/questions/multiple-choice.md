# Why we eat — energy, building, protection — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about energy?
a) Food provides energy for movement, growth and reproduction
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Food provides energy for movement, growth and reproduction**

**2.** What does this lesson say about building?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Food supplies material to build new cells and replace damaged tissues
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Food supplies material to build new cells and replace damaged tissues**

**3.** What does this lesson say about protection?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Food protects the body, since shortage of particular food groups causes deficiency diseases
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Food protects the body, since shortage of particular food groups causes deficiency diseases**

**4.** What does this lesson say about fuel?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Food is the material that fuels all biological activity
**Answer: d) Food is the material that fuels all biological activity**

**5. In the Grade 3 wording, a child who misses meals cannot —**
a) play, learn or work  b) sleep  c) grow taller overnight
**Answer: a) play, learn or work** — Play, learn or work — the primary book's warning.
