Today: Why we eat — energy, building, protection.

Remember this one thing: food gives energy, builds and repairs the body, and protects it — shortage of a group causes deficiency disease.

Food provides energy for movement, growth and reproduction.

Food supplies material to build new cells and replace damaged tissues.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
