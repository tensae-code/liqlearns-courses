# Why we eat — energy, building, protection

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Why we eat — energy, building, protection. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Food is the material that fuels all biological activity. It does three jobs at once.

[Picture 21052 — three icons over one plate — flame, blocks, shield]

## The key facts
**Energy.** Food provides energy for movement, growth and reproduction.

**Building.** Food supplies material to build new cells and replace damaged tissues.

**Protection.** Food protects the body, since shortage of particular food groups causes deficiency diseases.

**Fuel.** Food is the material that fuels all biological activity.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words: food gives energy, builds and repairs the body, and protects it — shortage of a group causes deficiency disease. Use each key fact once. Then let your companion explain it back to you.
