Today: The pressure-flow hypothesis.

Remember this one thing: phloem sap flows because it is pushed — high pressure at the source, low pressure at the sink.

A leaf loads sugar into the phloem. Water rushes in after the sugar, and the pressure climbs. At the fruit or root, sugar is unloaded, water leaves, and the pressure falls. Between the two pressures, the sap flows — that is pressure flow.

No pump. Just loading, osmosis, and a pressure difference.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
