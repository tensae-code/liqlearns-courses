# The pressure-flow hypothesis — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Pressure flow needs a plant pump like a heart.**
**Answer: False** — the flow is driven by the pressure difference built by loading and osmosis — no pump.

**2. Phloem sap can flow up or down, as sources and sinks change.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Water follows sugar into the phloem at the source.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. Unloading sugar at the sink raises the pressure there.**
**Answer: False** — unloading drops the pressure at the sink — that drop is half of what drives the flow.
