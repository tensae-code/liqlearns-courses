# The pressure-flow hypothesis — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Food travels from a source to a _____ through the phloem.**
**Answer: sink**

**2. Loading sugar at the source draws in water by _____.**
**Answer: osmosis**

**3. The mechanism of phloem translocation is called the _____-flow hypothesis.**
**Answer: pressure**

**4. A fruit, a root and a growing tip can each act as a _____.**
**Answer: sink**
