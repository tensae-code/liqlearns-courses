# The pressure-flow hypothesis — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the pressure-flow hypothesis step by step.
**Answer: Sugar is loaded into the phloem at a source; water follows by osmosis and pressure rises there. At a sink, sugar is unloaded, water leaves and pressure falls. The pressure difference pushes the sap in bulk flow from source to sink.**

**2.** Explain: Bhutan's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Bhutan's Class 10 book (Ch. 2.2, printed p. 41) teaches the mechanism of translocation through phloem by name as the pressure-flow hypothesis, after the xylem and phloem paths and the evidence for translocation.**
