# The pressure-flow hypothesis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. In the pressure-flow hypothesis, phloem sap moves because of…**
a) gravity alone  b) a pressure difference — high at the source, low at the sink  c) wind shaking the stem  d) the heart of the plant
**Answer: b) a pressure difference — high at the source, low at the sink**

**2. A source is…**
a) a place where food is used  b) a place where food is made or released from store, usually a leaf  c) always a root  d) a flower only
**Answer: b) a place where food is made or released from store, usually a leaf**

**3. Water enters the phloem at the source because…**
a) sugar loaded there draws water in by osmosis  b) roots pump it in  c) rain falls on the leaf  d) the sink sucks it in
**Answer: a) sugar loaded there draws water in by osmosis**

**4. At the sink, pressure falls because…**
a) sugar is unloaded and water leaves the phloem  b) sugar is loaded  c) light increases  d) the tube widens
**Answer: a) sugar is unloaded and water leaves the phloem**
