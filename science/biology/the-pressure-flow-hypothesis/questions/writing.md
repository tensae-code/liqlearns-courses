# The pressure-flow hypothesis — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why girdling a tree trunk — removing a ring of bark — starves the roots.
**Model answer: The phloem lies in the bark, so a complete ring of removed bark cuts the pressure-flow path. Sugar made in the leaves can no longer reach the roots, the sink below the ring, so the roots starve even though the xylem still carries water up to the leaves.**
