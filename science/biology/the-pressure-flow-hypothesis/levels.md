# The pressure-flow hypothesis — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Food made in the leaves travels through the phloem by pressure flow: sugar is loaded into the phloem at a source (a leaf), water follows by osmosis and the pressure that builds pushes the sap along the tube to a sink (a root, fruit or growing tip), where sugar is unloaded and the pressure falls — a flow driven by pressure difference, not by the plant pumping.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
