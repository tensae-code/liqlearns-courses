# Alcohol, khat and drugs — body effects and risky sex — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Alcohol's dose ladder from euphoria at low blood levels to confusion, coma and death, with brain and liver damage, cancers, violence and accidents, and its association with risky sex and HIV transmission; khat chewing's immediate effects (alertness, raised blood pressure and heart rate, insomnia, suppressed appetite) and long-term harms (hypertension, gastritis, diabetes, liver disease, impotence); cannabis, cocaine, heroin and inhalants each sketched; the unit's through-line is that intoxication raises STI and unwanted-pregnancy risk, adolescents most of all.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The guide supplies the cannabis effects list the student book only gestures at: short term — quiet reflective mood, confusion, anxiety, altered vision, sleepiness, reduced coordination and balance, raised heart rate and lowered blood pressure; long term — impaired concentration, memory and learning, sore throat and asthma among the effects.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
