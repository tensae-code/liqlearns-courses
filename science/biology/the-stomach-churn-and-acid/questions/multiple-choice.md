# The stomach — churn and acid — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about chyme?
a) The stomach churns food into a soup called chyme  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The stomach churns food into a soup called chyme**

**2.** What does this lesson say about muscle and sphincters?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Muscle contractions move the food, and sphincters control its movement  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Muscle contractions move the food, and sphincters control its movement**

**3.** What does this lesson say about gastric juice?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Gastric juice does chemical digestion in the stomach  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Gastric juice does chemical digestion in the stomach**

**4.** What does this lesson say about self-protection?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) The stomach protects itself from its own strong chemicals
**Answer: d) The stomach protects itself from its own strong chemicals**
