# The stomach — churn and acid

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The stomach — churn and acid. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
The stomach is a muscular bag that squeezes, mixes and chemically attacks food. Peristalsis moves the food along, sphincters gate it, and the stomach must protect itself from its own strong juice.

## The key facts
**Chyme.** The stomach churns food into a soup called chyme.

**Muscle and sphincters.** Muscle contractions move the food, and sphincters control its movement.

**Gastric juice.** Gastric juice does chemical digestion in the stomach.

**Self-protection.** The stomach protects itself from its own strong chemicals.
