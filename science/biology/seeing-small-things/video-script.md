Today: Seeing small things — hand lens and microscope kinds.

Remember this one thing: a hand lens gives a first enlargement, and microscopes show the small world in far more detail.

A hand lens is a convex lens in a ring with a handle; it gives a first enlargement but cannot show cell detail.

Light microscopes may be simple (one lens) or compound (two or more lenses); the compound is the common school type.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
