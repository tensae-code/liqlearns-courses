# Seeing small things — hand lens and microscope kinds — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A hand lens is a convex lens in a ring with a handle; it gives a first enlargement but cannot show cell detail.
**Answer: True**

**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Electron microscopes give far higher magnification than light microscopes.
**Answer: True**
