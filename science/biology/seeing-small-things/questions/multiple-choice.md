# Seeing small things — hand lens and microscope kinds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about hand lens?
a) A hand lens is a convex lens in a ring with a handle; it gives a first enlargement but cannot show cell detail
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) A hand lens is a convex lens in a ring with a handle; it gives a first enlargement but cannot show cell detail**

**2.** What does this lesson say about light microscope?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Light microscopes may be simple (one lens) or compound (two or more lenses); the compound is the common school type
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Light microscopes may be simple (one lens) or compound (two or more lenses); the compound is the common school type**

**3.** What does this lesson say about electron microscope?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Electron microscopes give far higher magnification than light microscopes
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Electron microscopes give far higher magnification than light microscopes**

**4.** What does this lesson say about two gifts?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) A microscope provides magnification (making bigger) and resolution (making detail clear)
**Answer: d) A microscope provides magnification (making bigger) and resolution (making detail clear)**
