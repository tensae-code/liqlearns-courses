# Seeing small things — hand lens and microscope kinds — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** _____ lens is a convex lens in a ring with a handle; it gives a first enlargement but cannot show cell detail
**Answer: A hand**

**2.** _____ may be simple (one lens) or compound (two or more lenses); the compound is the common school type
**Answer: Light microscopes**
