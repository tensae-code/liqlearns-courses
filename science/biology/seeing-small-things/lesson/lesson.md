## The idea
Some things are too small for the eye alone. Tools that magnify let us see them, step by step.

## The key facts
**Hand lens.** A hand lens is a convex lens in a ring with a handle; it gives a first enlargement but cannot show cell detail.

**Light microscope.** Light microscopes may be simple (one lens) or compound (two or more lenses); the compound is the common school type.

**Electron microscope.** Electron microscopes give far higher magnification than light microscopes.

**Two gifts.** A microscope provides magnification (making bigger) and resolution (making detail clear).
