# Hands-on — Chromosome numbers in different organisms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: each species carries its own characteristic chromosome number, constant in every body cell and every generation — cattle 60, sheep 54, pig 38, chicken 78 — a fixed mark of the species.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a table of farm animals — cow, sheep, pig, chicken — each with a small nucleus beside it labelled with its chromosome number: 60, 54, 38, 78. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
