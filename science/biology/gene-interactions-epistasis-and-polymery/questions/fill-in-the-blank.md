# Gene interactions — epistasis and polymery — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The R gene alone gives a _____ comb in chickens.**
**Answer: rose**

**2. The masking gene in epistasis is called _____.**
**Answer: epistatic**

**3. Several genes adding small effects on one trait is called _____.**
**Answer: polymery**

**4. The Indonesian book works the anthocyanin interaction in the plant _____.**
**Answer: Linaria**
