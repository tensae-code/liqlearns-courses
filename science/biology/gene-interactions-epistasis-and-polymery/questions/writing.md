# Gene interactions — epistasis and polymery — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences distinguishing epistasis from polymery, with the chicken comb as your example of interaction.
**Model answer: In epistasis one gene masks a different gene's effect — the epistatic gene hides the hypostatic one. In polymery several genes act on the same trait in the same direction and their effects add up into graded amounts. The chicken comb shows interaction itself: R and P together produce the walnut comb, which neither gene produces alone.**
