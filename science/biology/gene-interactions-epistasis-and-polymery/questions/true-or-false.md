# Gene interactions — epistasis and polymery — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. The walnut comb is produced by the R gene alone.**
**Answer: False** — walnut needs both dominant alleles, R and P together — a form neither gene makes alone.

**2. A hypostatic gene is the one doing the masking.**
**Answer: False** — the hypostatic gene is the one masked; the epistatic gene does the masking.

**3. Polymery makes a trait come in graded amounts.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. Gene interactions keep the plain Mendelian ratios unchanged.**
**Answer: False** — interaction changes the ratios, because one gene's effect depends on another gene.
