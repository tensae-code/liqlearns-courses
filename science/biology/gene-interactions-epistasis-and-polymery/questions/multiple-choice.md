# Gene interactions — epistasis and polymery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A chicken carrying both dominant R and P alleles has a…**
a) rose comb  b) pea comb  c) walnut comb  d) single comb
**Answer: c) walnut comb**

**2. In epistasis, the gene whose effect is hidden is called…**
a) epistatic  b) hypostatic  c) dominant  d) polygenic
**Answer: b) hypostatic**

**3. Polymery is…**
a) one gene masking another  b) several genes adding their effects on one trait  c) a mutation in one gene  d) two genes making a new form
**Answer: b) several genes adding their effects on one trait**

**4. Gene interaction changes offspring ratios from…**
a) the plain single-gene Mendelian ratios  b) chromosome counts  c) karyotype order  d) protein shapes
**Answer: a) the plain single-gene Mendelian ratios**
