# Gene interactions — epistasis and polymery — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the chicken-comb cross as proof of gene interaction.
**Answer: Comb shape is controlled by two genes: R alone gives rose, P alone gives pea, both dominants together give walnut — a new form — and neither gives single. Because the outcome of one gene depends on the other gene present, the genes interact.**

**2.** Explain: Indonesia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Indonesia's Grade 12 student book (Ch. 2D, printed p. 104) teaches gene interaction through the chicken-comb R×P cross, Linaria anthocyanin, polymery, and epistasis–hypostasis crosses.**
