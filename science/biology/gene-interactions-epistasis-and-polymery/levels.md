# Gene interactions — epistasis and polymery — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Genes at different loci do not always act alone — they interact: two genes together can produce a form neither makes by itself (the chicken's comb shape from the R and P genes), one gene can mask another gene's effect (epistasis — the masking gene is epistatic, the masked one hypostatic), and several genes can add their small effects together on one trait (polymery)) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
