Today: Gene interactions — epistasis and polymery.

Remember this one thing: genes interact. Two genes together can make something new, one gene can mask another, and several genes can add up on one trait.

Rose comb from R, pea comb from P, walnut from both together, single from neither — that is interaction. A masking gene is epistatic, the masked one hypostatic, and polymery is many genes adding small effects.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
