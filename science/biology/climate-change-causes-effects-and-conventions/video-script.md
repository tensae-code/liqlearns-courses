# Video script — Climate change — causes, effects and conventions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Weather (the mix of atmospheric events, differing place to place over minutes to weeks) is distinguished from climate, climate variability and climate change; emitted carbon dioxide is followed as it distributes rapidly between atmosphere, upper ocean and vegetation, then moves slowly among Earth's reservoirs over long periods, with orbital changes credited for the comings and goings of ice ages; and the answer key supplies the climate-vs-global-warming distinction (warming is only surface temperature; climate change includes the side effects — melting glaciers, heavier rainstorms, frequent drought), health effects (more malaria and dengue, mental-health impacts, a minority of benefits), agricultural effects (availability, access, food quality), a definition of mitigation, and the complete COP conference list from COP 1 (Berlin, 1995) to COP 26 (Glasgow, 2021), including COP 3 Kyoto and COP 21 Paris.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
