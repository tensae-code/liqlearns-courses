# Hands-on — Autophagy — the cell eats itself

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: in autophagy the cell digests its own damaged parts inside lysosomes — survival rations in starvation and a clearance service for invading microbes and worn organelles in damage and ageing.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a cell wrapping a worn-out organelle in a double membrane bubble and delivering it to a lysosome that digests it. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
