# Autophagy — the cell eats itself — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. the cell digesting its own damaged parts in lysosomes: survival rations in starvation, clearance of invading microbes and worn organelles in damage and ageing, and its possible runaway into apoptosis — a named mechanism given a box of its own and no lesson of ours.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in China's People's Education Press biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (普通高中教科书《生物学》必修1 分子与细胞 (Senior High Biology, Compulsory Book 1: Molecules and Cells, People's Education Press, 2019-standard edition)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with autophagy — the cell eats itself.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is autophagy — the cell eats itself.
