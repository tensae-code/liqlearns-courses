# Autophagy — the cell eats itself

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Autophagy — the cell eats itself. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
In autophagy the cell digests its own damaged parts inside lysosomes — survival rations in starvation and a clearance service for invading microbes and worn organelles in damage and ageing. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from China's People's Education Press biology textbooks, which teach it as a named topic.

[Picture — a cell wrapping a worn-out organelle in a double membrane bubble and delivering it to a lysosome that digests it]
