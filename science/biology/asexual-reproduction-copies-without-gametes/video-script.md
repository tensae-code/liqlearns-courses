Today: Asexual reproduction — copies without gametes.

Remember this one thing: asexual reproduction makes genetically identical offspring without gametes or a second parent.

Asexual reproduction produces individuals without gametes or a second parent.

Asexual reproduction occurs in bacteria, single-celled eukaryotes, many invertebrates, fungi and plants.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
