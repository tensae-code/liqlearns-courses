## The idea
It multiplies a successful type quickly — but with no variation.

[Picture 21036 — one parent organism with identical offspring around it]

## The key facts
**No gametes.** Asexual reproduction produces individuals without gametes or a second parent.

**Where.** Asexual reproduction occurs in bacteria, single-celled eukaryotes, many invertebrates, fungi and plants.

**Identical.** Offspring are genetically identical to the parent.

**Trade-off.** Asexual reproduction multiplies a successful type quickly but with no variation.
