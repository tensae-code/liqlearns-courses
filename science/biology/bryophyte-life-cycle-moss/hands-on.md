# Hands-on — The bryophyte life cycle — the moss

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find real moss if any grows near home or school — on a wall, a stone or damp ground. Look for the thin stalks with capsules rising from the green plant, and name the two generations you are looking at.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a moss plant labelled as the book labels it — rhizoids below, the gametophyte body, and the sporophyte rising from it on its seta with the capsule at the top — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
