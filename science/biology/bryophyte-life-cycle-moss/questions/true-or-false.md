# The bryophyte life cycle — the moss — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** The green moss plant is the gametophyte.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The moss sporophyte lives independently, away from the gametophyte.
**Answer: False** — it is borne on the gametophyte and dependent on it.

**3.** Moss fertilisation depends on water.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Rhizoids are true roots.
**Answer: False** — rhizoids are root-like threads that anchor the plant; they are not true roots.
