# The bryophyte life cycle — the moss — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about bryophytes?
a) Bryophytes are small, simple land plants without true roots, stems or leaves; the moss is the example
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Bryophytes are small, simple land plants without true roots, stems or leaves; the moss is the example**

**2.** What does this lesson say about the gametophyte?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) The green moss plant itself is the gametophyte generation
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The green moss plant itself is the gametophyte generation**

**3.** What does this lesson say about fertilisation in moss?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Fertilisation depends on water: the male gamete must swim through water to the egg
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Fertilisation depends on water: the male gamete must swim through water to the egg**

**4.** What does this lesson say about the sporophyte?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) It grows out of the gametophyte and stays dependent on it — a seta lifting a capsule that makes spores
**Answer: d) It grows out of the gametophyte and stays dependent on it — a seta lifting a capsule that makes spores**
