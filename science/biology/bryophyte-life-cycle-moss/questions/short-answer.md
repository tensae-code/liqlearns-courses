# The bryophyte life cycle — the moss — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: antheridia and archegonia.
**Answer: They are the sex organs borne on the gametophyte — the antheridia are the male organs and the archegonia are the female organs.**

**2.** Explain: why fertilisation in moss depends on water.
**Answer: The male gamete must swim through water to reach the egg in the archegonium, so without water fertilisation cannot happen.**
