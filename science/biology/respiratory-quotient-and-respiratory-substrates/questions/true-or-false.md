# Respiratory quotient and respiratory substrates — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Carbohydrate respired aerobically gives an RQ of 1.0.**
**Answer: True** — CO₂ produced equals O₂ used.

**2. Protein's RQ is lower than fat's.**
**Answer: False** — protein is about 0.9, fat about 0.7.

**3. Surface-area-to-volume ratio affects the rate of respiration.**
**Answer: True** — it is one of the six factors.

**4. RQ measures the energy in a substrate directly in kJ/g.**
**Answer: False** — RQ is a ratio of gas volumes; the kJ/g figure is the substrate's energy value.
