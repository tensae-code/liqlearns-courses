# Respiratory quotient and respiratory substrates — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Define the respiratory quotient and give the values for the three substrates.
**Answer: RQ = CO₂ produced ÷ O₂ used in respiration. Carbohydrate ≈ 1.0, protein ≈ 0.9, fat ≈ 0.7 (Ch. 3, printed p. 92).**

**2.** List the six factors affecting the rate of respiration in Kenya's Form 2 book.
**Answer: Oxygen, substrate, hormones, surface-area-to-volume ratio, age and temperature (Ch. 3, printed p. 92).**
