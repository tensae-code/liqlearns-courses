# Respiratory quotient and respiratory substrates — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining how an RQ measurement can tell a biologist what fuel an animal is respiring.
**Model answer: Each substrate exchanges the two gases in its own proportion, because of what it is made of: carbohydrate trades them one for one, fat gives out noticeably less carbon dioxide per oxygen taken in, and protein falls between. So the single measured ratio — CO₂ out over O₂ in — is a signature: near 1.0 reads carbohydrate, near 0.7 reads fat.**
