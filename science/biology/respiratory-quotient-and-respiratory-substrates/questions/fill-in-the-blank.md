# Respiratory quotient and respiratory substrates — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. RQ stands for respiratory _____.**
**Answer: quotient**

**2. The RQ of carbohydrate is _____.**
**Answer: 1.0**

**3. Fats store about _____ kJ of energy per gram.**
**Answer: 38**

**4. One factor affecting respiration rate, alongside oxygen and temperature, is _____.**
**Answer: hormones**
