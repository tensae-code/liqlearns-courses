# Respiratory quotient and respiratory substrates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. RQ is calculated as…**
a) O₂ used ÷ CO₂ produced  b) CO₂ produced ÷ O₂ used  c) CO₂ produced × O₂ used  d) energy released ÷ glucose used
**Answer: b) CO₂ produced ÷ O₂ used**

**2. The RQ of fat is about…**
a) 1.0  b) 0.9  c) 0.7  d) 2.0
**Answer: c) 0.7**

**3. Fats yield about how much energy per gram?**
a) 4 kJ/g  b) 17 kJ/g  c) 38 kJ/g  d) 100 kJ/g
**Answer: c) 38 kJ/g**

**4. Which is NOT one of Kenya's six factors affecting the rate of respiration?**
a) temperature  b) hormones  c) age  d) fur colour
**Answer: d) fur colour**
