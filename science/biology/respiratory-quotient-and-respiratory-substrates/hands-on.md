# Hands-on — Respiratory quotient and respiratory substrates

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Take the lesson's key facts for a walk: find one example of this lesson's idea in your own surroundings — your home, street, market or school — and say which key fact it matches, and why.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — three fuel stores drawn as labelled tanks — carbohydrate, fat and protein — feeding one flame, with two measuring cylinders above it collecting the gases, and a balance card reading RQ = CO₂ out ÷ O₂ in, marked 1.0 for carbohydrate, 0.7 for fat and 0.9 for protein — label its parts, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
