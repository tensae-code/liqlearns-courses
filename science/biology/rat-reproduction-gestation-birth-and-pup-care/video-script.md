# Video script — Rat reproduction — gestation, birth and pup care

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Rats (black, brown, and Ethiopia's endemic giant mole rat) fertilise internally and gestate in the uterus — about 22–24 days in brown rats, 37–49 in giant mole rats; the zygote becomes a blastocyst, implants in the uterine horns and differentiates; pups are born into a nest, suckle, are weaned at about 45 days, reach sexual maturity at 3–6 months depending on species, and are cared for communally — females nurse pups that are not their own.

The guide fills in the rat's female structures the student book only figures: the uterus receiving fertilised eggs and supporting the embryo to birth, each uterine horn having its own cervix with strong thick walls and a small opening that expands at birth, and the vagina as the short muscular canal below the urethra.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
