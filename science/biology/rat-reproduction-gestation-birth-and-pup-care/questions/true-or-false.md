# Rat reproduction — gestation, birth and pup care — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Rats (black, brown, and Ethiopia's endemic giant mole rat) fertilise internally and gestate in the uterus — about 22–24 days in brown rats, 37–49 in giant mole rats; the zygote becomes a blastocyst, implants in the uterine horns and differentiates; pups are born into a nest, suckle, are weaned at about 45 days, reach sexual maturity at 3–6 months depending on species, and are cared for communally — females nurse pups that are not their own.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The guide fills in the rat's female structures the student book only figures: the uterus receiving fertilised eggs and supporting the embryo to birth, each uterine horn having its own cervix with strong thick walls and a small opening that expands at birth, and the vagina as the short muscular canal below the urethra.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
