# Video script — Evidence for evolution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fossil dating is quantified: living things hold a carbon-14 to carbon-12 ratio of about 1 to a trillion, maintained in life by eating/making food; at death carbon-14 decays to nitrogen unreplaced, halving every 5,730 years, so the ratio dates the fossil; comparative anatomy is extended (the embryonic tail reduced in humans to the coccyx) and comparative biochemistry defined as studying evolutionary relationships through the shared genetic code and the protein machines it specifies.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
