# Evidence for evolution — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added evidence detail?**
**Answer: Fossil dating is quantified: living things hold a carbon-14 to carbon-12 ratio of about 1 to a trillion, maintained in life by eating/making food; at death carbon-14 decays to nitrogen unreplaced, halving every 5,730 years, so the ratio dates the fossil; comparative anatomy is extended (the embryonic tail reduced in humans to the coccyx) and comparative biochemistry defined as studying evolutionary relationships through the shared genetic code and the protein machines it specifies.**

**2. Give one example from the lesson about the evidence for evolution (§4.1.3):.**
**Answer: comparative anatomy (§4.1.3.1) — structural similarities revealing common ancestry; embryology; the fossil record; biochemistry — molecules such as haemoglobin used to build evolutionary trees, with the stated limit that haemoglobin cannot place plants and algae; an activity staffs the class as palaeontologists, anatomists, embryologists and biochemists, each group finding and poster-presenting its evidence.**
