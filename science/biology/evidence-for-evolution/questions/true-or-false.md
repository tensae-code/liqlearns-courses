# Evidence for evolution — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Fossil dating is quantified: living things hold a carbon-14 to carbon-12 ratio of about 1 to a trillion, maintained in life by eating/making food; at death carbon-14 decays to nitrogen unreplaced, halving every 5,730 years, so the ratio dates the fossil; comparative anatomy is extended (the embryonic tail reduced in humans to the coccyx) and comparative biochemistry defined as studying evolutionary relationships through the shared genetic code and the protein machines it specifies.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Biochemistry — molecules such as haemoglobin used to build evolutionary trees, with the stated limit that haemoglobin cannot place plants and algae.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) An activity staffs the class as palaeontologists, anatomists, embryologists and biochemists, each group finding and poster-presenting its evidence.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
