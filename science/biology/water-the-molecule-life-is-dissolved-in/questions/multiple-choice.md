# Water — the molecule life is dissolved in — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about structure?
a) The water molecule's structure is the root of all its properties  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The water molecule's structure is the root of all its properties**

**2.** What does this lesson say about gathering ions?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Water molecules cluster around ions when substances dissolve  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Water molecules cluster around ions when substances dissolve**

**3.** What does this lesson say about cohesion and adhesion?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Water shows cohesion (sticking to itself) and adhesion (sticking to other surfaces)  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Water shows cohesion (sticking to itself) and adhesion (sticking to other surfaces)**

**4.** What does this lesson say about surface tension?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Water's surface tension is strong enough for a raft spider to walk on water
**Answer: d) Water's surface tension is strong enough for a raft spider to walk on water**
