# Water — the molecule life is dissolved in

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Water — the molecule life is dissolved in. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Life is mostly water, and that is no accident. One small molecule's structure gives it the properties every living thing depends on — strong enough at the surface for a spider to walk on.

[Picture 22231 — the water molecule beside a raft spider walking on a pond surface]

## The key facts
**Structure.** The water molecule's structure is the root of all its properties.

**Gathering ions.** Water molecules cluster around ions when substances dissolve.

**Cohesion and adhesion.** Water shows cohesion (sticking to itself) and adhesion (sticking to other surfaces).

**Surface tension.** Water's surface tension is strong enough for a raft spider to walk on water.

**Boiling and freezing.** Water's boiling and freezing behaviour is unusual, and fits it to be life's medium.
