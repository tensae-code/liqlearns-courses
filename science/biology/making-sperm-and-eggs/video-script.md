Making gametes by meiosis from diploid germ cells: spermatogenesis runs continuously from puberty in the seminiferous tubules of the testes, germ cells dividing with Sertoli cells nourishing the developing sperm, the testes also making androgen; oogenesis is its female counterpart, the two processes figured side by side.
