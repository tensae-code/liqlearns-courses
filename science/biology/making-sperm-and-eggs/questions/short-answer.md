# Making sperm and eggs — short answer

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about gametogenesis?**
**Answer: Making gametes by meiosis from diploid germ cells: spermatogenesis runs continuously from puberty in the seminiferous tubules of the testes, germ cells dividing with Sertoli cells nourishing the developing sperm, the testes also making androgen; oogenesis is its female counterpart, the two processes figured side by side.**
