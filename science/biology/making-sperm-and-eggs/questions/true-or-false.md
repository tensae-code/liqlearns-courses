# Making sperm and eggs — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Making gametes by meiosis from diploid germ cells: spermatogenesis runs continuously from puberty in the seminiferous tubules of the testes, germ cells dividing with Sertoli cells nourishing the developing sperm, the testes also making androgen; oogenesis is its female counterpart, the two processes figured side by side.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Oogenesis is its female counterpart, the two processes figured side by side.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Making gametes by meiosis from diploid germ cells: spermatogenesis runs continuously from puberty in the seminiferous tubules of the testes, germ cells dividing with Sertoli cells nourishing the developing sperm, the testes also making androgen.**
