Today: Parasitic roundworms — Ascaris, pinworm, Trichina.

Remember this one thing: the roundworms are the phylum best known for species parasitic on their hosts, though free-living roundworms also hunt as predators with grasping mouthparts.

Kuwait's Grade 10 Term 2 student book (Unit 2, Lesson 2-1, printed pp. 36–37) opens the roundworms with their free-living members: predators with grasping mouthparts.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
