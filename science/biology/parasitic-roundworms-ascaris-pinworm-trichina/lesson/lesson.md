## The key facts
**Free-living roundworms.** Kuwait's Grade 10 Term 2 student book (Unit 2, Lesson 2-1, printed pp. 36–37) opens the roundworms with their free-living members: predators with grasping mouthparts.

**The parasitic phylum.** The book then states the phylum's fame plainly: roundworms are the phylum best known for species parasitic on their hosts — the group of Ascaris, the pinworm and Trichina.

**Kuwait's way.** Kuwait teaches roundworms inside the worms lesson (Grade 10 Term 2, Unit 2 Lesson 2-1), free-living predators first, then the parasitic species the phylum is known for.
