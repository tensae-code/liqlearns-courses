# Parasitic roundworms — Ascaris, pinworm, Trichina — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (the roundworms are the phylum best known for species parasitic on their hosts, though free-living roundworms also hunt as predators with grasping mouthparts) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
