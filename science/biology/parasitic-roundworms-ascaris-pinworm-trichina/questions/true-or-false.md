# Parasitic roundworms — Ascaris, pinworm, Trichina — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's Grade 10 Term 2 student book (Unit 2, Lesson 2-1, printed pp. 36–37) opens the roundworms with their free-living members: predators with grasping mouthparts.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كتاب الطالب — الصف العاشر — الفصل الدراسي الثاني (Biology — Student Book, Grade 10, Term 2), printed pp. 36–37).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with parasitic roundworms — Ascaris, pinworm, Trichina.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is parasitic roundworms — Ascaris, pinworm, Trichina.
