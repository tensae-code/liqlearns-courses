# Parasitic roundworms — Ascaris, pinworm, Trichina — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: the roundworms are the phylum best known for species parasitic on their hosts, though free-living roundworms also hunt as predators with grasping mouthparts. Kuwait's Grade 10 Term 2 student book (Unit 2, Lesson 2-1, printed pp. 36–37) opens the roundworms with their free-living members: predators with grasping mouthparts.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait teaches roundworms inside the worms lesson (Grade 10 Term 2, Unit 2 Lesson 2-1), free-living predators first, then the parasitic species the phylum is known for.
