# Hands-on — Parasitic roundworms — Ascaris, pinworm, Trichina

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: the roundworms are the phylum best known for species parasitic on their hosts, though free-living roundworms also hunt as predators with grasping mouthparts.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a roundworm drawn with its smooth unsegmented body, beside a magnified view of a free-living roundworm's grasping mouthparts. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
