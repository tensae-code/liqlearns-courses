Today: Regulation of gene expression.

Remember this one thing: having a gene is not the same as using it — and the using is controlled.

Every cell in your body carries the same full set of genes. But a muscle cell is not a nerve cell. So what differs? Not the genes — the expression. A gene is expressed when the cell reads it and makes its product. In the muscle cell, the muscle genes are being read. In the nerve cell, a different set is being read, and the muscle set stays silent.

That control — which genes, in which cells, at what times, how strongly — is regulation of gene expression. Italy's Grade 11 textbook gives it an entire unit, right after the unit on the gene itself: first how genes work, then how their work is governed.

Same library in every cell. Different books open. That is regulation.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
