# Regulation of gene expression — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. A gene that is present but not being read is silent — present, but not expressed.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Regulation of gene expression changes which genes a cell has.**
**Answer: False** — regulation controls which genes are expressed; the cell's set of genes stays the same.

**3. Cells with identical genes can become different kinds of cell through regulation.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. In Italy's book, the regulation unit comes before the unit on the molecular biology of the gene.**
**Answer: False** — Unità 2 (regulation) follows Unità 1 (the molecular biology of the gene).
