# Regulation of gene expression — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. A gene being read and made into its product is called gene _____.**
**Answer: expression**

**2. The control of which genes are expressed, when and how much, is called _____ of gene expression.**
**Answer: regulation**

**3. Every body cell carries the same _____ — the full set of genes.**
**Answer: genome**

**4. A gene that is not being read is present but _____.**
**Answer: silent**
