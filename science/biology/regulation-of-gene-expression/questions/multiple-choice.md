# Regulation of gene expression — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is gene expression?**
a) A gene being copied into every cell  b) A gene being read and made into its product  c) A gene leaving the nucleus  d) A gene changing its sequence
**Answer: b) A gene being read and made into its product**

**2. What does regulation of gene expression control?**
a) Which genes exist in the body  b) Which genes are expressed, in which cells, when, and how much  c) The number of chromosomes  d) The speed of cell division only
**Answer: b) Which genes are expressed, in which cells, when, and how much**

**3. How can two cells with identical genes be different kinds of cell?**
a) They cannot  b) Each expresses a different set of its genes and keeps the rest silent  c) One of them loses its genes  d) Genes change places
**Answer: b) Each expresses a different set of its genes and keeps the rest silent**

**4. How much room does Italy's Grade 11 Campbell give regulation of gene expression?**
a) One sentence  b) A whole unit — Unità 2  c) Only a glossary entry  d) It omits it
**Answer: b) A whole unit — Unità 2**
