# Regulation of gene expression — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining regulation of gene expression to a younger student. Use the words expressed and silent once each.
**Model answer: Every cell in the body has the same genes, but it does not use them all. A gene is expressed when the cell reads it and makes its product; the genes a cell does not need stay silent. Regulation of gene expression is the control of that choosing — which genes are on, which are off, and how strong — and it is why a muscle cell and a nerve cell can be so different with the same genes inside.**
