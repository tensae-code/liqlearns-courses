# Regulation of gene expression — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: regulation of gene expression, in one or two sentences.
**Answer: Gene expression is a gene being read and made into its product. Its regulation is the control of which genes are expressed, in which cells, at which times and how strongly — so cells carrying identical genes can become different kinds of cell by expressing different sets.**

**2.** Explain: Italy's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Italy's Grade 11 Campbell (*Biologia: concetti e collegamenti, Secondo biennio*, Linx/Pearson Italia) gives regulation of gene expression a whole unit of its own — Unità 2 (PDF p. 46) — placed immediately after Unità 1, the molecular biology of the gene.**
