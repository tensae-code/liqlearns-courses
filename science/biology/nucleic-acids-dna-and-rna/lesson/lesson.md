# Nucleic acids — DNA and RNA

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Nucleic acids — DNA and RNA. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
The last organic family carries information. Nucleic acids are built from nucleotides, and their two forms — DNA and RNA — are compared point by point.

[Picture 22240 — a nucleotide taken apart into its three pieces, beside a DNA–RNA comparison panel]

## The key facts
**Nucleotides.** Nucleic acids are polymers of nucleotides.

**Three parts.** A nucleotide has three components.

**Two sugars.** DNA's sugar is deoxyribose and RNA's sugar is ribose.

**DNA vs RNA.** DNA and RNA are compared point by point in structure and function.
