# Population characteristics and structure — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Moldova's Grade 12 book (4.2 Organisation of living matter: populations) teaches: life at successive levels; population characteristics numbered, including sex structure (ratio of females to males); primary consumers as phytophages feeding on plant tissues. (printed p. 130).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Moldova's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Biologie: Manual pentru clasa a 12-a (Grade 12)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with population characteristics and structure.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is population characteristics and structure.
