# Population characteristics and structure — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: a population — living matter organised at its own level — is described by its characteristics, including its structure, and its sex structure is the ratio of females to males in it. Moldova's Grade 12 book (4.2 Organisation of living matter: populations) teaches: life at successive levels; population characteristics numbered, including sex structure (ratio of females to males); primary consumers as phytophages feeding on plant tissues. (printed p. 130).**

**2.** Explain Moldova's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Moldova's Grade 12 book teaches populations inside the levels of organisation of living matter: population characteristics numbered, including sex structure (ratio of females to males).**
