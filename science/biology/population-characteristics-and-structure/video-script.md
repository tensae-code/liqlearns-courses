Today: Population characteristics and structure.

Remember this one thing: a population — living matter organised at its own level — is described by its characteristics, including its structure, and its sex structure is the ratio of females to males in it.

Moldova's Grade 12 book (4.2 Organisation of living matter: populations) teaches: life at successive levels; population characteristics numbered, including sex structure (ratio of females to males); primary consumers as phytophages feeding on plant tissues. (printed p. 130).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
