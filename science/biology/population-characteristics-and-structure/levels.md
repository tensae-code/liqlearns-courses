# Population characteristics and structure — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (a population — living matter organised at its own level — is described by its characteristics, including its structure, and its sex structure is the ratio of females to males in it) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
