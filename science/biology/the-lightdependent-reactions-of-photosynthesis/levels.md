# The Light-Dependent Reactions of Photosynthesis — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: write the photolysis equation, name the carriers in order (plastoquinone, cytochromes, plastocyanin between the photosystems; ferredoxin after photosystem I), explain ATP-making by chemiosmosis from the lumen's proton build-up, name the three products, and contrast cyclic with non-cyclic photophosphorylation.
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to name or sort, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.
