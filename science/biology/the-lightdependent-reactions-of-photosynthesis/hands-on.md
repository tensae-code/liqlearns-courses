# Hands-on — The Light-Dependent Reactions of Photosynthesis

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Walk the chain on paper
Draw the thylakoid membrane as a long band. Place photosystem II at the left and photosystem I at the right, the three carriers between them in order, and ferredoxin after photosystem I. Under the band, shade the lumen and fill it with H+ marks. Check your drawing against Picture 36602 and fix anything out of order.

## The dam test
Explain the lumen to a companion using a dam or a held-back stream: water held high, one gate, work done as it flows through. Then say where the picture stops being exact.

## Teach it
Explain the lesson to someone at home, ending with the three products. Note the question they asked and your answer.
