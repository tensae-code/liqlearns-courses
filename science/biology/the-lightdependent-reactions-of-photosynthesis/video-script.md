# Video script — The Light-Dependent Reactions of Photosynthesis

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Light falls on the leaf. Here is what it does.

At photosystem II, water is split. That is photolysis.
Out comes oxygen — and electrons for the chain.

The electrons travel: plastoquinone, cytochromes, plastocyanin.
On the way, protons are pumped into the thylakoid lumen. They pile up.

The protons flow back through ATP synthase — and ATP is made.
That is photophosphorylation, by chemiosmosis.

At photosystem I the electrons are lifted again, pass to ferredoxin,
and make reduced NADP.

Three products: oxygen, ATP, and NADPH.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
