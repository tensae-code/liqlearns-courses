# The Light-Dependent Reactions of Photosynthesis — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. The electrons that replace those lost by photosystem II come from splitting water.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Plastocyanin carries electrons from photosystem II directly to ferredoxin, skipping photosystem I.**
**Answer: False** — plastocyanin delivers electrons to photosystem I; ferredoxin receives them after photosystem I.

**3. Protons are pumped into the thylakoid lumen as electrons pass through the chain.**
**Answer: True**

**4. Reduced NADP (NADPH) is formed by ferredoxin and NADP reductase after photosystem I.**
**Answer: True**
