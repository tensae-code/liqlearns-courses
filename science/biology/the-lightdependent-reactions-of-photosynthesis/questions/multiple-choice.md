# The Light-Dependent Reactions of Photosynthesis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What does photolysis split, and what equation shows it?**
a) It splits carbon dioxide: CO₂ → C + O₂
b) It splits water: 2H₂O → O₂ + 4H⁺ + 4e⁻
c) It splits glucose: C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2H₂O + CO₂
d) It splits ATP: ATP → ADP + phosphate
**Answer: b) It splits water: 2H₂O → O₂ + 4H⁺ + 4e⁻**

**2. In what order do electrons pass through the carriers between photosystem II and photosystem I?**
a) Plastocyanin, then cytochromes, then plastoquinone
b) Ferredoxin, then plastoquinone, then plastocyanin
c) Plastoquinone, then cytochromes, then plastocyanin
d) Cytochromes, then ferredoxin, then plastoquinone
**Answer: c) Plastoquinone, then cytochromes, then plastocyanin**

**3. How is ATP made in photophosphorylation?**
a) Protons pile up in the thylakoid lumen and flow back through ATP synthase, driving ATP formation — chemiosmosis
b) Oxygen is burned inside the chloroplast
c) Glucose is split in the membrane
d) NADP is broken down into ATP
**Answer: a) Protons pile up in the thylakoid lumen and flow back through ATP synthase, driving ATP formation — chemiosmosis**

**4. What does cyclic photophosphorylation produce, compared with the non-cyclic run?**
a) All three products — O₂, ATP and NADPH
b) ATP only — no water is split, no oxygen released, no NADPH made
c) NADPH only
d) Glucose directly
**Answer: b) ATP only — no water is split, no oxygen released, no NADPH made**
