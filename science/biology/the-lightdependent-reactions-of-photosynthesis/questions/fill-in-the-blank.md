# The Light-Dependent Reactions of Photosynthesis — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1.** The splitting of water in the light-dependent reactions is called _____.
**Answer: photolysis**

**2.** ATP is made as protons flow out of the thylakoid _____ through ATP synthase.
**Answer: lumen**

**3.** After photosystem I, electrons pass to _____, and NADP reductase then forms reduced NADP.
**Answer: ferredoxin**
