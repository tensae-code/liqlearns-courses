# The Light-Dependent Reactions of Photosynthesis — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. Name the three products of the light-dependent reactions and where each comes from.**
**Answer: Oxygen, from photolysis splitting water at photosystem II; ATP, from photophosphorylation as protons flow out of the lumen through ATP synthase; and reduced NADP (NADPH), formed via ferredoxin and NADP reductase after photosystem I.**

**2. What is chemiosmosis in this lesson?**
**Answer: Chemiosmosis is ATP-making driven by a proton gradient across a membrane: protons pumped into the thylakoid lumen flow back through ATP synthase, and that flow drives the formation of ATP.**

**3. How do cyclic and non-cyclic photophosphorylation differ?**
**Answer: In non-cyclic flow electrons run from split water through both photosystems to NADP, making O₂, ATP and NADPH; in cyclic flow electrons from photosystem I cycle back round the chain, so only ATP is made — no water split, no oxygen, no NADPH.**
