# Parasitic worms — Ascaris and bilharzia — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Ascaris's full name is Ascaris _____.**
**Answer: lumbricoides**

**2. Bilharzia is also called _____.**
**Answer: schistosomiasis**

**3. Bilharzia cercariae swim from _____ hosts.**
**Answer: snail**

**4. The canal holding the female fluke is the _____ canal.**
**Answer: gynacophoric**
