# Parasitic worms — Ascaris and bilharzia — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Describe the Ascaris larva's migration and the worm's prevention, as Kenya teaches them.
**Answer: Eggs are swallowed; the larva migrates through liver, heart and lungs before the adult settles in the intestine, and the book lists the worm's six adaptive characteristics alongside prevention that breaks transmission (Ch. 2, printed p. 69).**

**2.** Explain Kenya's way of teaching the parasitic worms.
**Answer: Kenya works the two worms as a pair inside ecology: for each, the road in (swallowed eggs for Ascaris, skin-piercing cercariae from Biomphalaria and Bulinus for Schistosoma), the effects on the host, the parasite's own adaptive characteristics listed one by one, and prevention stationed on the road — latrines for both, molluscicides and protective clothing for bilharzia.**
