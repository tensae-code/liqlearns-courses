# Parasitic worms — Ascaris and bilharzia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Ascaris enters the body by…**
a) piercing the skin  b) being swallowed as eggs  c) mosquito bite  d) breathing spores
**Answer: b) being swallowed as eggs**

**2. The Ascaris larva migrates through…**
a) skin, muscle, bone  b) liver, heart and lungs  c) kidney and bladder only  d) it does not migrate
**Answer: b) liver, heart and lungs**

**3. Bilharzia's cercariae leave which hosts to reach humans?**
a) fish  b) Biomphalaria and Bulinus snails  c) mosquitoes  d) dogs
**Answer: b) Biomphalaria and Bulinus snails**

**4. The gynacophoric canal is an adaptation of…**
a) Ascaris  b) Schistosoma (bilharzia)  c) the tapeworm  d) the liver fluke
**Answer: b) Schistosoma (bilharzia)**
