# Video script — Mendel and the seven pea traits

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Gregor Mendel, the Austrian monk who founded genetics, crossed garden peas pure-bred for seven pairs of contrasting traits (seed shape and colour, pod shape and colour, flower colour and position, stem height), dominant forms listed against recessive; self-crosses first proved the lines pure before any experimental cross was trusted.

The guide adds Mendel's reasons for choosing peas (easy to grow, many clearly distinct contrasting characters, an annual giving many generations quickly, naturally self-pollinating but cross-pollinatable) and the habits behind his success, including testing his results rigorously — material the student book omits.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
