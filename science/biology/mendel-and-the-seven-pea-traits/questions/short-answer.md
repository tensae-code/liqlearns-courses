# Mendel and the seven pea traits — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about mendel and his pea crosses?**
**Answer: Gregor Mendel, the Austrian monk who founded genetics, crossed garden peas pure-bred for seven pairs of contrasting traits (seed shape and colour, pod shape and colour, flower colour and position, stem height), dominant forms listed against recessive; self-crosses first proved the lines pure before any experimental cross was trusted.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added mendel detail?**
**Answer: The guide adds Mendel's reasons for choosing peas (easy to grow, many clearly distinct contrasting characters, an annual giving many generations quickly, naturally self-pollinating but cross-pollinatable) and the habits behind his success, including testing his results rigorously — material the student book omits.**
