# Mendel and the seven pea traits — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Gregor Mendel, the Austrian monk who founded genetics, crossed garden peas pure-bred for seven pairs of contrasting traits (seed shape and colour, pod shape and colour, flower colour and position, stem height), dominant forms listed against recessive; self-crosses first proved the lines pure before any experimental cross was trusted.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The guide adds Mendel's reasons for choosing peas (easy to grow, many clearly distinct contrasting characters, an annual giving many generations quickly, naturally self-pollinating but cross-pollinatable) and the habits behind his success, including testing his results rigorously — material the student book omits.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
