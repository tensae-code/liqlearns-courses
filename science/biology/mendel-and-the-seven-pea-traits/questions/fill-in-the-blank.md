# Mendel and the seven pea traits — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** _____ Mendel, the Austrian monk who founded genetics, crossed garden peas pure-bred for seven pairs of contrasting traits (seed shape and colour, pod shape and colour, flower colour and position, stem height), dominant forms listed against recessive; self-crosses first proved the lines pure before any experimental cross was trusted.
