Today: Fungi — threads that absorb their food.

Remember this one thing: fungi feed by absorbing food, and most bodies are threads (hyphae) woven into a mycelium.

Fungi include yeasts, moulds and mushrooms.

Fungi are eukaryotes with chitin cell walls that feed by absorbing food from dead or living matter.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
