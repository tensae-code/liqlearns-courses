# Fungi — threads that absorb their food — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about members?
a) Fungi include yeasts, moulds and mushrooms
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Fungi include yeasts, moulds and mushrooms**

**2.** What does this lesson say about walls and feeding?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Fungi are eukaryotes with chitin cell walls that feed by absorbing food from dead or living matter
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Fungi are eukaryotes with chitin cell walls that feed by absorbing food from dead or living matter**

**3.** What does this lesson say about hyphae?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Except for single-celled yeasts, fungal bodies are threads called hyphae woven into a mycelium
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Except for single-celled yeasts, fungal bodies are threads called hyphae woven into a mycelium**

**4.** What does this lesson say about two sides?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Fungi help by decomposing and food-making (bread, drinks) and harm by spoilage and disease
**Answer: d) Fungi help by decomposing and food-making (bread, drinks) and harm by spoilage and disease**
