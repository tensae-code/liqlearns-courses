# Fungi — threads that absorb their food — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: hyphae.
**Answer: Except for single-celled yeasts, fungal bodies are threads called hyphae woven into a mycelium.**

**2.** Explain: two sides.
**Answer: Fungi help by decomposing and food-making (bread, drinks) and harm by spoilage and disease.**
