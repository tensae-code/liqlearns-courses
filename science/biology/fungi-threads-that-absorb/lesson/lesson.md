# Fungi — threads that absorb their food

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Fungi — threads that absorb their food. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
The mushroom you see is only the tip. Below ground a woven network does the feeding.

[Picture 21020 — a mushroom above ground with its hyphae network below]

## The key facts
**Members.** Fungi include yeasts, moulds and mushrooms.

**Walls and feeding.** Fungi are eukaryotes with chitin cell walls that feed by absorbing food from dead or living matter.

**Hyphae.** Except for single-celled yeasts, fungal bodies are threads called hyphae woven into a mycelium.

**Two sides.** Fungi help by decomposing and food-making (bread, drinks) and harm by spoilage and disease.
