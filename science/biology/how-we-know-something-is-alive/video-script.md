# Video script — How we know something is alive

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: How we know something is alive.

Remember this one thing: living things share a set of characteristics that non-living matter does not have.

Living things show nutrition, respiration, movement, excretion, growth, reproduction and sensitivity to stimuli.

Living things keep internal conditions stable — body temperature held steady is the worked example.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
