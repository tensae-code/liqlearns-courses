# How we know something is alive — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about the marks?
a) Living things show nutrition, respiration, movement, excretion, growth, reproduction and sensitivity to stimuli
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Living things show nutrition, respiration, movement, excretion, growth, reproduction and sensitivity to stimuli**

**2.** What does this lesson say about homeostasis?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Living things keep internal conditions stable — body temperature held steady is the worked example
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Living things keep internal conditions stable — body temperature held steady is the worked example**

**3.** What does this lesson say about adaptation?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Living things adapt, and adaptation evolves over time
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Living things adapt, and adaptation evolves over time**

**4.** What does this lesson say about non-living?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Non-living matter does not show the full set of characteristics of life
**Answer: d) Non-living matter does not show the full set of characteristics of life**
