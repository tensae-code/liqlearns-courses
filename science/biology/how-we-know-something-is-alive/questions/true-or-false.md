# How we know something is alive — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Living things show nutrition, respiration, movement, excretion, growth, reproduction and sensitivity to stimuli.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Homeostasis has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Living things adapt, and adaptation evolves over time.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Non-living has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
