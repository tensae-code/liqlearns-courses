Today: Alcohol, khat and cannabis — stimulants and depressants in real life.

Remember this one thing: alcohol, khat and cannabis each change the body and behaviour in their own way.

Alcohol affects the body and behaviour as a depressant.

Khat is a stimulant leaf chewed socially in Ethiopia, with social and psychological effects.
