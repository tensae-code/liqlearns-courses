# Alcohol, khat and cannabis — stimulants and depressants in real life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about alcohol?
a) Alcohol affects the body and behaviour as a depressant
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Alcohol affects the body and behaviour as a depressant**

**2.** What does this lesson say about khat?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Khat is a stimulant leaf chewed socially in Ethiopia, with social and psychological effects
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Khat is a stimulant leaf chewed socially in Ethiopia, with social and psychological effects**

**3.** What does this lesson say about cannabis?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Cannabis (marijuana) contains psychoactive THC
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Cannabis (marijuana) contains psychoactive THC**

**4.** What does this lesson say about THC effects?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) THC alters mind, time sense, concentration and short-term memory, relaxes the body and increases appetite
**Answer: d) THC alters mind, time sense, concentration and short-term memory, relaxes the body and increases appetite**
