# Alcohol, khat and cannabis — stimulants and depressants in real life

## The idea
Khat is a stimulant leaf chewed socially in Ethiopia. Cannabis's psychoactive THC alters mind, time sense, concentration and short-term memory.

[Picture 21063 — khat leaves, an alcohol bottle and a cannabis plant in a warning panel]

## The key facts
**Alcohol.** Alcohol affects the body and behaviour as a depressant.

**Khat.** Khat is a stimulant leaf chewed socially in Ethiopia, with social and psychological effects.

**Cannabis.** Cannabis (marijuana) contains psychoactive THC.

**THC effects.** THC alters mind, time sense, concentration and short-term memory, relaxes the body and increases appetite.
