# Hands-on — Effect of exercise on heart rate

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: exercise raises the heart rate: count the pulse at rest, exercise, count again — and analyse the change.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a pulse being counted at the wrist, beside a simple two-bar chart: heart rate at rest and heart rate after exercise. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
