# Effect of exercise on heart rate

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Effect of exercise on heart rate. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
pulse counted at rest and after exercise and the change analysed

[Picture — a pulse being counted at the wrist, beside a simple two-bar chart: heart rate at rest and heart rate after exercise]

## The key facts
**The measurement.** Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 2 applications workbook (Activity 10, printed p. 38) examines the effect of exercise on heart rate: the pulse is counted at rest and after exercise.

**The analysis.** The change between the two counts is the activity's result, and it is analysed, not just recorded — why the rate rose is part of the write-up.

**Kuwait's way.** Kuwait's workbook makes the learners' own bodies the apparatus (Activity 10): their pulse, at rest and after exercise, measured and analysed by themselves.
