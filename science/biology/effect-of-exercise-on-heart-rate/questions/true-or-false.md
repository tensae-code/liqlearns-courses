# Effect of exercise on heart rate — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 2 applications workbook (Activity 10, printed p. 38) examines the effect of exercise on heart rate: the pulse is counted at rest and after exercise.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كراسة التطبيقات — الصف الحادي عشر — الفصل الدراسي الثاني (Biology — Applications Workbook, Grade 11, Term 2), printed p. 38).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with effect of exercise on heart rate.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is effect of exercise on heart rate.
