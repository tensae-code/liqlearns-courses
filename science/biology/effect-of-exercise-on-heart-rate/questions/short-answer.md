# Effect of exercise on heart rate — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: exercise raises the heart rate: count the pulse at rest, exercise, count again — and analyse the change. Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 2 applications workbook (Activity 10, printed p. 38) examines the effect of exercise on heart rate: the pulse is counted at rest and after exercise.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's workbook makes the learners' own bodies the apparatus (Activity 10): their pulse, at rest and after exercise, measured and analysed by themselves.**
