Today: Effect of exercise on heart rate.

Remember this one thing: exercise raises the heart rate: count the pulse at rest, exercise, count again — and analyse the change.

Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 2 applications workbook (Activity 10, printed p. 38) examines the effect of exercise on heart rate: the pulse is counted at rest and after exercise.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
