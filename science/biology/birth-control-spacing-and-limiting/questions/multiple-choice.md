# Birth control — spacing and limiting children — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about natural methods?
a) Natural methods include periodic abstinence (rhythm), the breastfeeding effect and withdrawal
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Natural methods include periodic abstinence (rhythm), the breastfeeding effect and withdrawal**

**2.** What does this lesson say about barrier methods?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Barrier methods include male and female condoms and the diaphragm/cap; condoms are the only methods that also block infections
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Barrier methods include male and female condoms and the diaphragm/cap; condoms are the only methods that also block infections**

**3.** What does this lesson say about hormonal methods?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Hormonal methods include the pill, injectable and implant
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Hormonal methods include the pill, injectable and implant**

**4.** What does this lesson say about IUD?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Intra-uterine devices sit in the uterus and prevent pregnancy over a long period
**Answer: d) Intra-uterine devices sit in the uterus and prevent pregnancy over a long period**
