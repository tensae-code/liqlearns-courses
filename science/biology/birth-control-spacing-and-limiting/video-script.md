Today: Birth control — spacing and limiting children.

Remember this one thing: families space or limit children with natural, barrier, hormonal and intra-uterine methods.

Natural methods include periodic abstinence (rhythm), the breastfeeding effect and withdrawal.

Barrier methods include male and female condoms and the diaphragm/cap; condoms are the only methods that also block infections.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
