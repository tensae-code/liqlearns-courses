# Birth control — spacing and limiting children

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Birth control — spacing and limiting children. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Each method family works differently, with its own advantages and disadvantages. Condoms alone also block infections.

[Picture 21048 — the method families as four labelled panels]

## The key facts
**Natural methods.** Natural methods include periodic abstinence (rhythm), the breastfeeding effect and withdrawal.

**Barrier methods.** Barrier methods include male and female condoms and the diaphragm/cap; condoms are the only methods that also block infections.

**Hormonal methods.** Hormonal methods include the pill, injectable and implant.

**IUD.** Intra-uterine devices sit in the uterus and prevent pregnancy over a long period.
