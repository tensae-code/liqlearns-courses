# Lesson plan — Skeletons — hydrostatic, exoskeleton and endoskeleton

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Skeletons — hydrostatic, exoskeleton and endoskeleton
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — secondary band (CURRICULUM.md Unit 16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Animals hold themselves up three ways: Kenya's Form 4 book compares the skeleton types — the hydrostatic skeleton of fluid under pressure, the exoskeleton of chitin outside the body (jointed, and shed in moulting/ecdysis), and the endoskeleton of living bone and cartilage inside — with a grasshopper, crab and millipede examination practical.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier biology lessons and builds the secondary-band understanding of skeletons — hydrostatic, exoskeleton and endoskeleton.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
