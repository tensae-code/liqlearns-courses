# Skeletons — hydrostatic, exoskeleton and endoskeleton — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. An exoskeleton is jointed so the animal can move.**
**Answer: True** — plates with joints, examined in the practical.

**2. A hydrostatic skeleton is made of bone.**
**Answer: False** — it is fluid under pressure.

**3. Moulting leaves the animal soft and vulnerable for a time.**
**Answer: True** — the price of the outside skeleton.

**4. Bone and cartilage of the endoskeleton are living tissue.**
**Answer: True** — they grow with the animal.
