# Skeletons — hydrostatic, exoskeleton and endoskeleton — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why an exoskeleton, for all its armour, sets a harder limit on an animal's growth than an endoskeleton does.
**Model answer: Chitin armour cannot stretch, so growth must wait for a moult: the skeleton is shed whole, the animal sits soft and defenceless while the new one hardens, and between moults its size is fixed by the shell it is wearing. Living bone inside the body grows continuously with its owner, so the endoskeleton never has to be taken off to get bigger.**
