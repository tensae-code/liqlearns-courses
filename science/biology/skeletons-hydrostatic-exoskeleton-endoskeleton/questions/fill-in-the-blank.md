# Skeletons — hydrostatic, exoskeleton and endoskeleton — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The outside skeleton of an insect is made of _____.**
**Answer: chitin**

**2. Shedding the exoskeleton is called moulting or _____.**
**Answer: ecdysis**

**3. A fluid-filled skeleton is a _____ skeleton.**
**Answer: hydrostatic**

**4. The internal skeleton is the _____ skeleton.**
**Answer: endo**
