# Skeletons — hydrostatic, exoskeleton and endoskeleton — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A hydrostatic skeleton works by…**
a) air in hollow bones  b) fluid under pressure inside the body  c) external chitin plates  d) cartilage rods
**Answer: b) fluid under pressure inside the body**

**2. The exoskeleton is made of…**
a) bone  b) cartilage  c) chitin  d) keratin horns
**Answer: c) chitin**

**3. An animal with an exoskeleton grows by…**
a) stretching the chitin  b) moulting — shedding the old skeleton (ecdysis)  c) adding rings of bone  d) inflating the shell
**Answer: b) moulting — shedding the old skeleton (ecdysis)**

**4. The endoskeleton differs from the exoskeleton in being…**
a) outside the body  b) dead and shed yearly  c) inside the body and living, growing with the animal  d) made of chitin
**Answer: c) inside the body and living, growing with the animal**
