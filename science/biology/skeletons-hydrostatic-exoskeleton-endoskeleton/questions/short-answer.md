# Skeletons — hydrostatic, exoskeleton and endoskeleton — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Compare the three skeleton types in one line each.
**Answer: Hydrostatic — fluid under pressure inside the body; exoskeleton — jointed chitin outside, shed in moulting; endoskeleton — living bone and cartilage inside, growing with the animal (Ch. 4, printed p. 126).**

**2.** Explain Kenya's way of teaching skeletons.
**Answer: Kenya teaches the types comparatively and by specimen — grasshopper, crab and millipede examined in the practical for the exoskeleton — before the mammal skeleton is taken bone by bone: the outside skeleton's joints and moulting set against the inside skeleton's living bone and cartilage, with the hydrostatic design completing the three.**
