## The idea
Primary succession starts from lifeless ground with pioneers first; secondary succession regrows where soil and some life remain.

## The key facts
**Sequence.** Ecological succession is species colonising in an orderly sequence, each gradually replaced by the next, ending in a stable community.

**Disturbance.** Succession follows a severe disturbance such as a volcanic eruption or glacier retreat.

**Primary.** Primary succession starts from lifeless ground with pioneer organisms first.

**Secondary.** Secondary succession regrows where soil and some life remain.
