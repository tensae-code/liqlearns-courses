Today: Succession — nature rebuilding in order.

Remember this one thing: after disturbance, species colonise in an orderly sequence until a stable community forms.

Ecological succession is species colonising in an orderly sequence, each gradually replaced by the next, ending in a stable community.

Succession follows a severe disturbance such as a volcanic eruption or glacier retreat.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
