# Succession — nature rebuilding in order — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about sequence?
a) Ecological succession is species colonising in an orderly sequence, each gradually replaced by the next, ending in a stable community
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Ecological succession is species colonising in an orderly sequence, each gradually replaced by the next, ending in a stable community**

**2.** What does this lesson say about disturbance?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Succession follows a severe disturbance such as a volcanic eruption or glacier retreat
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Succession follows a severe disturbance such as a volcanic eruption or glacier retreat**

**3.** What does this lesson say about primary?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Primary succession starts from lifeless ground with pioneer organisms first
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Primary succession starts from lifeless ground with pioneer organisms first**

**4.** What does this lesson say about secondary?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Secondary succession regrows where soil and some life remain
**Answer: d) Secondary succession regrows where soil and some life remain**
