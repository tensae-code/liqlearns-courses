Today: Plant adaptations to habitats.

Remember this one thing: plants are fitted to the water of their habitat: xerophytes hoard and guard water in dry places, hydrophytes float and breathe in water, halophytes manage salt in salty ground, and epiphytes live perched above the ground — each habitat answered by a set of adaptations.

Malaysia's Form 5 book surveys plant adaptations habitat by habitat (printed p. 134): the same problem — getting and keeping water, light and support — solved four different ways by four kinds of plant.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
