# Plant adaptations to habitats — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (plants are fitted to the water of their habitat: xerophytes hoard and guard water in dry places, hydrophytes float and breathe in water, halophytes manage salt in salty ground, and epiphytes live perched above the ground — each habitat answered by a set of adaptations) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
