# Plant adaptations to habitats — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) plants are fitted to the water of their habitat: xerophytes hoard and guard water in dry places, hydrophytes float and breathe in water, halophytes manage salt in salty ground, and epiphytes live perched above the ground — each habitat answered by a set of adaptations  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
**Answer: a) plants are fitted to the water of their habitat: xerophytes hoard and guard water in dry places, hydrophytes float and breathe in water, halophytes manage salt in salty ground, and epiphytes live perched above the ground — each habitat answered by a set of adaptations**

**2. Xerophytes — dry habitats — which statement is right?**
a) Plants of dry places cut water loss and store water: thick cuticle, sunken or few stomata, leaves reduced to spines, fleshy water-storing stems, deep or widespread roots.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) Plants of dry places cut water loss and store water: thick cuticle, sunken or few stomata, leaves reduced to spines, fleshy water-storing stems, deep or widespread roots.**

**3. Hydrophytes — water habitats — which statement is right?**
a) It is unrelated to the lesson's topic  b) Plants living in water float and breathe: air spaces in stems and leaves give buoyancy and carry air, stomata sit on the upper surface, and roots are reduced — water is everywhere, support and air are the problems.  c) It is a myth the book corrects  d) It applies only outside Malaysia
**Answer: b) Plants living in water float and breathe: air spaces in stems and leaves give buoyancy and carry air, stomata sit on the upper surface, and roots are reduced — water is everywhere, support and air are the problems.**
