# Plant adaptations to habitats — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: plants are fitted to the water of their habitat: xerophytes hoard and guard water in dry places, hydrophytes float and breathe in water, halophytes manage salt in salty ground, and epiphytes live perched above the ground — each habitat answered by a set of adaptations. Plants of dry places cut water loss and store water: thick cuticle, sunken or few stomata, leaves reduced to spines, fleshy water-storing stems, deep or widespread roots.**

**2.** Explain Malaysia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Malaysia's KSSM Form 5 book gives habitat adaptations a chapter of their own (Ch.7, printed p. 134) — xerophytes, hydrophytes, halophytes and epiphytes surveyed together as the close of its flowering-plant theme.**
