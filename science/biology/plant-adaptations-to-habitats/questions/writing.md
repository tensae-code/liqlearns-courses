# Plant adaptations to habitats — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: plants are fitted to the water of their habitat: xerophytes hoard and guard water in dry places, hydrophytes float and breathe in water, halophytes manage salt in salty ground, and epiphytes live perched above the ground — each habitat answered by a set of adaptations. Plants living in water float and breathe: air spaces in stems and leaves give buoyancy and carry air, stomata sit on the upper surface, and roots are reduced — water is everywhere, support and air are the problems.**
