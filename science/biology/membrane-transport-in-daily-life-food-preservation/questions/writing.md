**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: the movement of substances across a plasma membrane — osmosis above all — is put to work in daily life, and food preservation is its named application: salt and sugar draw water out of food and out of the microbes that would spoil it. Salt and sugar draw water out of the food and out of any microbe cells on it. A microbe that loses its water cannot grow, so the food keeps — preservation by osmosis.**
