# Membrane transport in daily life — food preservation — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: the movement of substances across a plasma membrane — osmosis above all — is put to work in daily life, and food preservation is its named application: salt and sugar draw water out of food and out of the microbes that would spoil it. The same crossing the chapter teaches in cells — water moving by osmosis towards the stronger solution — happens in food: salted or sugared food is a strong solution at its surface.**

**2.** Explain Malaysia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Malaysia's KSSM way is to end the membrane chapter (Ch.3) by applying it: after the plasma membrane, diffusion and osmosis in living organisms, the chapter's last step is the application in daily life, food preservation among it (printed p. 65).**
