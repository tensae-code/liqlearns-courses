# Membrane transport in daily life — food preservation — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (the movement of substances across a plasma membrane — osmosis above all — is put to work in daily life, and food preservation is its named application: salt and sugar draw water out of food and out of the microbes that would spoil it) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
