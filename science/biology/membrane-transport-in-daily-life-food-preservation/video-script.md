Today: Membrane transport in daily life — food preservation.

Remember this one thing: the movement of substances across a plasma membrane — osmosis above all — is put to work in daily life, and food preservation is its named application: salt and sugar draw water out of food and out of the microbes that would spoil it.

Malaysia's Form 4 book closes its membrane chapter with an application lesson: movement across a plasma membrane and its application in daily life (printed p. 65). The diffusion and osmosis the chapter has built are turned outward — to how food is preserved by controlling the water that crosses membranes.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
