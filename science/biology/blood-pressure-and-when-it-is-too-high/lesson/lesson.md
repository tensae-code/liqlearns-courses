# Blood pressure — and when it is too high

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Blood pressure — and when it is too high. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Pressure in the pipes is necessary — too much is dangerous. Hypertension harms without warning, and foods and habits are part of its story.

[Picture 22257 — a pressure gauge on an artery, normal band and high zone marked]

## The key facts
**What raises it.** Certain foods and habits raise blood pressure.

**Hypertension.** Hypertension is blood pressure that is too high.

**Silent damage.** Hypertension damages the body silently, without clear warnings.

**Compare and contrast.** This lesson compares normal pressure work against the high-pressure danger.
