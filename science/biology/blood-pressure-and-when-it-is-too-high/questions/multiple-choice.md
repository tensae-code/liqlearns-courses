# Blood pressure — and when it is too high — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about what raises it?
a) Certain foods and habits raise blood pressure  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Certain foods and habits raise blood pressure**

**2.** What does this lesson say about hypertension?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Hypertension is blood pressure that is too high  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Hypertension is blood pressure that is too high**

**3.** What does this lesson say about silent damage?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Hypertension damages the body silently, without clear warnings  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Hypertension damages the body silently, without clear warnings**

**4.** What does this lesson say about compare and contrast?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) This lesson compares normal pressure work against the high-pressure danger
**Answer: d) This lesson compares normal pressure work against the high-pressure danger**
