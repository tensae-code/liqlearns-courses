Today: The autonomic nervous system.

Remember this one thing: the autonomic nervous system runs the body's automatic life — heartbeat, digestion, pupil size, sweating — through two opposing branches, sympathetic speeding and preparing for action, parasympathetic slowing and restoring, without any conscious command.
