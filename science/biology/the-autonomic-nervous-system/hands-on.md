# Hands-on — The autonomic nervous system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: the autonomic nervous system runs the body's automatic life — heartbeat, digestion, pupil size, sweating — through two opposing branches, sympathetic speeding and preparing for action, parasympathetic slowing and restoring, without any conscious command.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a human outline with the heart, gut and eye connected by two coloured nerve paths, one labelled 'speed up — action' and one 'slow down — rest'. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
