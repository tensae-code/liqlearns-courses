# Video script — Using a microscope — carry, focus, put away

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Using a microscope — carry, focus, put away.

Remember this one thing: a microscope is carried upright by arm and base, focused from low power upward, and put away clean.

Carry the microscope upright, holding the arm and the base.

Clean lenses only with lens tissue, never with ordinary cloth or fingers.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
