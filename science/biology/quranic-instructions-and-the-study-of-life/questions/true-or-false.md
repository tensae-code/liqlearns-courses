# Quranic instructions and the study of life — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. The Quranic-instructions section is a named, numbered section of Punjab's Grade 9 SNC book.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches

**2. The framing appears only in the Grade 12 book.**
**Answer: False** — it is taught in Grade 9 — section 1.4 of the Grade 9 SNC book's first chapter, with epigraphs in the eLearn Grade 9 edition too

**3. The section replaces the scientific method in Punjab's book.**
**Answer: False** — the same chapter teaches the scientific method (sections 1.6–1.7) and the malaria example — the framing stands beside the method, not instead of it

**4. Bu Ali Sina (Avicenna) is named among the Muslim biologists in Punjab's first chapter.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches
