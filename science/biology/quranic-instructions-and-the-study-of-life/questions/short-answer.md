# Quranic instructions and the study of life — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: what Punjab's Grade 9 SNC book teaches in section 1.4.
**Answer: Section 1.4, “Quranic Instructions to Reveal the Study of Life” (printed p. 8), teaches that the Holy Quran instructs the study of life; the chapter's learner outcomes require the learner to state this, so the book grounds the study of biology in that instruction.**

**2.** Explain: Pakistan (Punjab)'s way of teaching this.
**Answer: Punjab's books open biology with framing before mechanism: the Quran's instruction to study life as a named section with its own learner outcome, Quranic epigraphs and Muslim scientists (Bu Ali Sina/Avicenna) in the eLearn edition's first chapter, science as the common heritage of mankind, and the professions of Pakistan.**
