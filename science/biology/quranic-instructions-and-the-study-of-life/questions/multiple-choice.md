# Quranic instructions and the study of life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. In Pakistan (Punjab)'s Grade 9 SNC book, the section “Quranic Instructions to Reveal the Study of Life” is…**
a) an appendix b) section 1.4 of Chapter 1 c) a glossary entry d) a practical exercise
**Answer: b) section 1.4 of Chapter 1**

**2. The chapter's learner outcomes require the learner to state that…**
a) biology began in Punjab b) the Holy Quran instructs the study of life c) malaria was solved in 1898 d) careers in biology pay well
**Answer: b) the Holy Quran instructs the study of life**

**3. The older eLearn.Punjab Biology 9 also carries the framing, with…**
a) no religious content at all b) Quranic epigraphs in Ch 1 and a Quranic verse on embryonic development c) only European scientists named d) a chapter on statistics
**Answer: b) Quranic epigraphs in Ch 1 and a Quranic verse on embryonic development**

**4. This framing is presented in the book as…**
a) a reason for studying biology, given inside the first chapter b) a replacement for the scientific method c) a topic for Grade 12 only d) an exam trick
**Answer: a) a reason for studying biology, given inside the first chapter**
