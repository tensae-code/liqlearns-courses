The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: Quranic instructions and the study of life.

In Punjab's Grade 9 book, one whole section — 1.4 — is given to the Quranic instructions to reveal the study of life, and the chapter's learner outcomes ask you to state that the Holy Quran instructs the study of life.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
