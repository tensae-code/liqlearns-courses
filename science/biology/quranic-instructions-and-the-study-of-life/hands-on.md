# Hands-on — Quranic instructions and the study of life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Open any biology book or lesson you have and find how it answers the question “why study life?” — then write Punjab's answer beside it in one sentence, from memory.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — an open school biology book whose first chapter shows a tree of the branches of biology beside a verse panel headed “Quranic Instructions to Reveal the Study of Life”, with small drawings of a plant, an animal and a human figure under it — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
