# Quranic instructions and the study of life — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Pakistan (Punjab)'s Grade 9 book gives the study of life a named religious framing of its own: its section “Quranic Instructions to Reveal the Study of Life” teaches that the Holy Quran instructs the study of life — the book's own answer to the question of why biology should be studied at all.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
