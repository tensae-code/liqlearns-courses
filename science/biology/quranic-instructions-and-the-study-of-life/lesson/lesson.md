# Quranic instructions and the study of life

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Quranic instructions and the study of life. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Pakistan (Punjab)'s Grade 9 book gives the study of life a named religious framing of its own: its section “Quranic Instructions to Reveal the Study of Life” teaches that the Holy Quran instructs the study of life — the book's own answer to the question of why biology should be studied at all. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from Pakistan (Punjab)'s Punjab Board books, which teach it as a named topic.

## The key facts
**A named section, not a passing mention.** In *Biology Class 9 SNC* (Punjab Board), Chapter 1 — The Science of Biology — carries a numbered section of its own, 1.4: “Quranic Instructions to Reveal the Study of Life” (printed p. 8). The section sits inside the book's opening run: the branches of biology (1.1), biology's relation with the other sciences, careers in biology, and science as a collaborative field.

**The learner outcome is stated outright.** The chapter's learner outcomes require the learner to state that the Holy Quran instructs the study of life. The instruction to observe and study living things is presented as a reason — given before the science itself — for learning biology.

**The older Punjab book carries the same framing.** The eLearn.Punjab edition of Biology 9 opens its first chapter with Quranic epigraphs, includes the history of Muslim biologists such as Bu Ali Sina (Avicenna), and prints a Quranic verse on embryonic development — the framing runs through Punjab's biology course, in both editions.

**Pakistan (Punjab)'s way.** Where other countries' books open with definitions or laboratory rules, Punjab's books open by grounding the subject: science as the common heritage of mankind (the eLearn edition quotes Dr Abdus Salam), the professions of Pakistan, and the Quran's instruction to study life. That framing is Pakistan (Punjab)'s own way of teaching why biology matters, and it is content in this lesson.
