# Brazilian biomes and the Mata Atlântica — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the Mata Atlântica?
a) The Atlantic Forest — the forest biome of Brazil's Atlantic coast
b) A desert in the middle of Brazil
c) It plays no part in this topic at all
d) It happens only at night and nowhere else
**Answer: a) The Atlantic Forest — the forest biome of Brazil's Atlantic coast**

**2.** Why does the Mata Atlântica get a whole chapter of its own in Brazil's textbook?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Because it is disappearing — so much of it has been cut down that what remains stands in patches
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Because it is disappearing — so much of it has been cut down that what remains stands in patches**

**3.** What does the chapter's title — "socorro!!! Cadê você???" — mean?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) "Help!!! Where are you???" — the forest is spoken to as someone who has gone missing
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) "Help!!! Where are you???" — the forest is spoken to as someone who has gone missing**

**4.** What question does the book's Biomas chapter (Ch. 11) ask about biomes?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) "Paraísos naturais ou recursos inesgotáveis?" — natural paradises, or inexhaustible resources?
**Answer: d) "Paraísos naturais ou recursos inesgotáveis?" — natural paradises, or inexhaustible resources?**
