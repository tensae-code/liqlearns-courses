# Brazilian biomes and the Mata Atlântica — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** The Mata Atlântica is the Atlantic Forest of Brazil's Atlantic coast.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Mata Atlântica chapter is a tour of the forest's plants and animals, with nothing about loss.
**Answer: False** — the chapter's whole subject is the forest's disappearance; its title is a cry for help.

**3.** In Brazil's textbook every chapter is framed as a question, in an inquiry style.
**Answer: True**

**4.** When a biome disappears, only its trees are lost and the rest of its living community stays.
**Answer: False** — a biome is the whole living community of a region, and that community goes with it.
