# Lesson plan — Brazilian biomes and the Mata Atlântica

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Brazilian biomes and the Mata Atlântica
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — appended to Unit 16 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Brazil has its own named biomes, and its Paraná public textbook gives one of them — the Mata Atlântica, the Atlantic Forest — a whole chapter of its own because that forest is disappearing: the chapter is framed as a cry for help, "socorro!!! Cadê você???" (Help!!! Where are you???) (Brazil, Grade 10 — *Biologia — Livro Didático Público, Ensino Médio*, student textbook, Ch. 12, printed p. 193).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. The great land biomes is recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: **The great land biomes**. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say, in their own words, the one thing this lesson teaches: Brazil's own biomes are real named homes of living things, and the Mata Atlântica is the one Brazil's textbook cries out for because it is disappearing.
2. Say where the Mata Atlântica sits in the book: inside the Biodiversity block, straight after the Biomas chapter that asks whether biomes are "natural paradises or inexhaustible resources?".
3. Explain Brazil's way: the chapter title is a cry for help — "socorro!!! Cadê você???" (Help!!! Where are you???) — the forest is spoken to as someone who has gone missing.
4. Say why a disappearing forest earns a whole chapter: what is lost with a biome is not only trees but the whole living community that biome holds.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## Add-ons for this lesson (the full list)
- IMAGE: a picture of Brazil's Atlantic coast and its forest band, mostly gone, in patches — the student names what the remaining patches are and what their loss means.
- AUDIO: a voice reading the key facts aloud — the student listens and repeats each one. (Audio is a described slot only; no audio file is made in this build.)
- VIDEO — the video-script filmed (made later) — the student follows the spoken walkthrough.

## If the learner is stuck
- Go back to the one thing this lesson teaches, and re-read the key facts slowly, one at a time.
- Use the chapter's own question: if you called "where are you?" to a forest, what would it mean that it does not answer?
