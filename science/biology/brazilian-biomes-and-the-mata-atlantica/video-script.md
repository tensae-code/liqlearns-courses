Today: Brazilian biomes and the Mata Atlântica.

Remember this one thing: a country has its own biomes — its own great living regions — and Brazil's textbook teaches them by crying out for the one that is disappearing.

First the book asks about all biomes at once: "paraísos naturais ou recursos inesgotáveis?" — natural paradises, or inexhaustible resources? Hold that question.

Then comes the Mata Atlântica, the Atlantic Forest, the forest of Brazil's Atlantic coast. It gets a whole chapter of its own, and the chapter's title is a shout: "socorro!!! Cadê você???" — Help!!! Where are you??? The forest is spoken to like someone loved who has gone missing — because so much of it has gone.

That is Brazil's way: do not just list the forest. Ask where it went.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
