# Hands-on — Brazilian biomes and the Mata Atlântica

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Draw a long coastline on a big sheet of paper. Colour a band of forest all along it, the way the Mata Atlântica once ran. Then rub out most of the band, leaving only small green patches. Under the drawing write the chapter's own words: "socorro!!! Cadê você???" — and next to it, in your own words, what has been lost.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the coast, the forest band, the remaining patches — label it (Brazil, Atlantic coast, Mata Atlântica, remaining patches), and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap. End by asking the Biomas question together: is a biome a natural paradise, an inexhaustible resource, or neither? Say why.
