# Video script — Germination — what a seed needs, two ways it grows

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: Germination — what a seed needs, two ways it grows.

Remember this one thing: a seed needs water, air (oxygen) and suitable warmth to germinate — epigeal in the bean, hypogeal in maize

Water: A seed needs water to germinate.

Air and warmth: A seed needs air (oxygen) and suitable warmth to germinate.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
