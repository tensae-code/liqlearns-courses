# Germination — what a seed needs, two ways it grows — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about water?
a) A seed needs water to germinate  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) A seed needs water to germinate**

**2.** What does this lesson say about air and warmth?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) A seed needs air (oxygen) and suitable warmth to germinate  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) A seed needs air (oxygen) and suitable warmth to germinate**

**3.** What does this lesson say about epigeal?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) In epigeal germination (bean) the cotyledons are carried above ground  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) In epigeal germination (bean) the cotyledons are carried above ground**

**4.** What does this lesson say about hypogeal?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) In hypogeal germination (maize) the cotyledons stay below ground
**Answer: d) In hypogeal germination (maize) the cotyledons stay below ground**
