# Germination — what a seed needs, two ways it grows

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Germination — what a seed needs, two ways it grows. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
A dry seed is waiting, not dead. Give it the right conditions and it germinates — but bean and maize do it in two different ways.

## The key facts
**Water.** A seed needs water to germinate.

**Air and warmth.** A seed needs air (oxygen) and suitable warmth to germinate.

**Epigeal.** In epigeal germination (bean) the cotyledons are carried above ground.

**Hypogeal.** In hypogeal germination (maize) the cotyledons stay below ground.
