# HIV and AIDS — transmission, prevention, treatment

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — HIV and AIDS — transmission, prevention, treatment. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Transmission routes and non-routes must be told apart plainly — fear grows where facts are missing.

[Picture 21067 — a shield panel over a virus outline]

## The key facts
**Attack.** HIV attacks the immune system and, untreated, progresses to AIDS.

**Transmission.** HIV passes by unprotected sex, infected blood and needles, and from mother to child.

**Prevention.** Prevention is testing all donated blood, sterile single-use needles, avoiding multiple partners, and correct condom use.

**Treatment.** Antiretroviral treatment for HIV-positive mothers protects the child, and the same drugs greatly lengthen the healthy years between infection and symptoms.
