# HIV and AIDS — transmission, prevention, treatment — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: prevention.
**Answer: Prevention is testing all donated blood, sterile single-use needles, avoiding multiple partners, and correct condom use.**

**2.** Explain: treatment.
**Answer: Antiretroviral treatment for HIV-positive mothers protects the child, and the same drugs greatly lengthen the healthy years between infection and symptoms.**
