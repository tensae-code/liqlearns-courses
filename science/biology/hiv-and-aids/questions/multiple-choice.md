# HIV and AIDS — transmission, prevention, treatment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about attack?
a) HIV attacks the immune system and, untreated, progresses to AIDS
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) HIV attacks the immune system and, untreated, progresses to AIDS**

**2.** What does this lesson say about transmission?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) HIV passes by unprotected sex, infected blood and needles, and from mother to child
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) HIV passes by unprotected sex, infected blood and needles, and from mother to child**

**3.** What does this lesson say about prevention?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Prevention is testing all donated blood, sterile single-use needles, avoiding multiple partners, and correct condom use
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Prevention is testing all donated blood, sterile single-use needles, avoiding multiple partners, and correct condom use**

**4.** What does this lesson say about treatment?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Antiretroviral treatment for HIV-positive mothers protects the child, and the same drugs greatly lengthen the healthy years between infection and symptoms
**Answer: d) Antiretroviral treatment for HIV-positive mothers protects the child, and the same drugs greatly lengthen the healthy years between infection and symptoms**
