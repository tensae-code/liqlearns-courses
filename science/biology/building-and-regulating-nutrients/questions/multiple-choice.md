# Building and regulating nutrients — protein, vitamins, minerals, water, fibre — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about proteins?
a) Proteins build and repair the body
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Proteins build and repair the body**

**2.** What does this lesson say about vitamins?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Vitamins are organic substances needed in small amounts for the cells' chemical reactions, each with its own deficiency disease when missing
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Vitamins are organic substances needed in small amounts for the cells' chemical reactions, each with its own deficiency disease when missing**

**3.** What does this lesson say about water?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Water is the body's solvent and transport medium
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Water is the body's solvent and transport medium**

**4.** What does this lesson say about fibre?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Fibre (roughage) keeps the gut working
**Answer: d) Fibre (roughage) keeps the gut working**
