# Building and regulating nutrients — protein, vitamins, minerals, water, fibre

## The idea
Vitamins are needed in small amounts, each with its own deficiency disease when missing.

[Picture 21055 — a nutrient wheel with a local food in each segment]

## The key facts
**Proteins.** Proteins build and repair the body.

**Vitamins.** Vitamins are organic substances needed in small amounts for the cells' chemical reactions, each with its own deficiency disease when missing.

**Water.** Water is the body's solvent and transport medium.

**Fibre.** Fibre (roughage) keeps the gut working.
