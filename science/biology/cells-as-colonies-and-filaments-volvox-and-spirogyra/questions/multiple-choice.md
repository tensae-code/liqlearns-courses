# Cells as colonies and filaments — Volvox and Spirogyra — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is a Volvox colony?
a) One very large single cell
b) A hollow ball of up to 20,000 cells living together
c) A thread of cells joined end to end
d) A tissue of specialised cells
**Answer: b) A hollow ball of up to 20,000 cells living together**

**2.** What is Spirogyra's recognisable feature?
a) A hollow ball shape
b) Flagella on every cell
c) A spiral chloroplast in each cell
d) A hard silica shell
**Answer: c) A spiral chloroplast in each cell**

**3.** How does Spirogyra reproduce by fragmentation?
a) The filament breaks and each piece grows into a new filament
b) It releases spores into the air
c) Two filaments fuse into one
d) It divides into two cells only
**Answer: a) The filament breaks and each piece grows into a new filament**

**4.** How do a colony's cells differ from a tissue's cells?
a) Colony cells are all dead
b) Colony cells are alike and can live apart; tissue cells are interdependent
c) Tissue cells are always green
d) There is no difference
**Answer: b) Colony cells are alike and can live apart; tissue cells are interdependent**
