# Cells as colonies and filaments — Volvox and Spirogyra — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** A hollow ball of many cells living together, like Volvox, is called a _____.
**Answer: colony (coenobium)**

**2.** Each Spirogyra cell carries a _____ chloroplast.
**Answer: spiral**

**3.** Spirogyra multiplies when its filament breaks — reproduction by _____.
**Answer: fragmentation**
