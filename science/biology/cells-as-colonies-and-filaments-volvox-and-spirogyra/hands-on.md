# Hands-on — Cells as colonies and filaments — Volvox and Spirogyra

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find a pond, a ditch or a container of standing water with green threads in it and look closely at the threads — a filament alga like Spirogyra. If a microscope is available, look for the spiral chloroplast. Then roll small balls of paper or clay into one hollow ball and say aloud: this is the colony idea — many units, one ball.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a hollow Volvox ball made of many small cells beside a Spirogyra filament — a thread of cells, each with a spiral green chloroplast — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
