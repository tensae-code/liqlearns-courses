## The key facts
**Definition.** Malnutrition is insufficient, excessive or imbalanced nutrient intake leading to health problems.

**Causes.** Malnutrition is caused by poverty, famine from drought or flood, soil erosion, war and too little land.

**Undernutrition forms.** Undernutrition forms include stunting, wasting and underweight.

**Hidden hunger.** Hidden micronutrient deficiencies are a form of undernutrition.
