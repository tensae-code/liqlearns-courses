# Malnutrition — too little, or the wrong balance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about definition?
a) Malnutrition is insufficient, excessive or imbalanced nutrient intake leading to health problems
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Malnutrition is insufficient, excessive or imbalanced nutrient intake leading to health problems**

**2.** What does this lesson say about causes?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Malnutrition is caused by poverty, famine from drought or flood, soil erosion, war and too little land
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Malnutrition is caused by poverty, famine from drought or flood, soil erosion, war and too little land**

**3.** What does this lesson say about undernutrition forms?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Undernutrition forms include stunting, wasting and underweight
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Undernutrition forms include stunting, wasting and underweight**

**4.** What does this lesson say about hidden hunger?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Hidden micronutrient deficiencies are a form of undernutrition
**Answer: d) Hidden micronutrient deficiencies are a form of undernutrition**
