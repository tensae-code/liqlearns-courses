# Malnutrition — too little, or the wrong balance — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** Malnutrition is insufficient, _____.
**Answer: excessive or imbalanced nutrient intake leading to health problems**

**2.** Malnutrition is caused by poverty, _____.
**Answer: famine from drought or flood, soil erosion, war and too little land**
