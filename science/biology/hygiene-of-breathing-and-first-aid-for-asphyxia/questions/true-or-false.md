# Hygiene of breathing and first aid for asphyxia — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Moldova's Grade 7 book (4.5 Hygiene of breathing) teaches: asphyxia causes (dust, drowning, strangling) and first aid / artificial respiration. (printed p. 105).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Moldova's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Biologie: manual pentru clasa a 7-a (Grade 7)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with hygiene of breathing and first aid for asphyxia.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is hygiene of breathing and first aid for asphyxia.
