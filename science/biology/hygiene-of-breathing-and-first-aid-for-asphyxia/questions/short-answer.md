# Hygiene of breathing and first aid for asphyxia — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: breathing is kept safe by hygiene of the airways, and when breathing stops in asphyxia — caused by dust, drowning or strangling — first aid and artificial respiration are the response. Moldova's Grade 7 book (4.5 Hygiene of breathing) teaches: asphyxia causes (dust, drowning, strangling) and first aid / artificial respiration. (printed p. 105).**

**2.** Explain Moldova's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Moldova's Grade 7 book gives breathing its own hygiene lesson: asphyxia causes (dust, drowning, strangling) and first aid / artificial respiration.**
