Today: Hygiene of breathing and first aid for asphyxia.

Remember this one thing: breathing is kept safe by hygiene of the airways, and when breathing stops in asphyxia — caused by dust, drowning or strangling — first aid and artificial respiration are the response.

Moldova's Grade 7 book (4.5 Hygiene of breathing) teaches: asphyxia causes (dust, drowning, strangling) and first aid / artificial respiration. (printed p. 105).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
