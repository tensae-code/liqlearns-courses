# Hygiene of breathing and first aid for asphyxia — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (breathing is kept safe by hygiene of the airways, and when breathing stops in asphyxia — caused by dust, drowning or strangling — first aid and artificial respiration are the response) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
