# Neurons: The Cells of the Nervous System — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about neurons and their functions (§5.1.1):?**
**Answer: the nervous system's two cell classes — neurons, its basic structural and functional units, and the supporting glial cells (neuroglia, about five times more numerous, feeding and supporting neurons); neuron anatomy — cell body/soma with nucleus, dendrites, the single axon branching into terminals, axons as nerve fibres and bundles of them as nerves, and the fatty myelin sheath.**
