# The menstrual cycle, phase by phase — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about the menstrual cycle?**
**Answer: A roughly 28-day cycle (normal range 20–45 days) of linked ovarian and uterine change: menstrual phase (days 1–4, lining shed), follicular phase (days 5–13, follicle matures, endometrium rebuilds under FSH and oestrogen), ovulation (day 14, egg released) and luteal phase (days 15–28), tabled by day and event.**
