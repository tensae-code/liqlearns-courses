# Video script — The menstrual cycle, phase by phase

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A roughly 28-day cycle (normal range 20–45 days) of linked ovarian and uterine change: menstrual phase (days 1–4, lining shed), follicular phase (days 5–13, follicle matures, endometrium rebuilds under FSH and oestrogen), ovulation (day 14, egg released) and luteal phase (days 15–28), tabled by day and event.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
