Today: Cycling of materials — decomposers and the nitrogen cycle.

Remember this one thing: without decomposers nutrients would stay locked in dead matter; bacteria run the nitrogen cycle's four processes.

Without decomposers, nutrients such as nitrogen, carbon and oxygen — which exist in limited amounts — would stay locked in dead matter.

The four bacterial processes of the nitrogen cycle are nitrogen fixation, nitrification, ammonification and denitrification.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.


Student-book core: Unlike energy, materials cycle — decomposers return dead matter's substances to the soil and air. Fixation, nitrification, ammonification and denitrification are the nitrogen cycle's bacterial processes.
