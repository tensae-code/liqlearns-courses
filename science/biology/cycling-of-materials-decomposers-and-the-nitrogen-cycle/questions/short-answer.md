# Cycling of materials — decomposers and the nitrogen cycle — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: carbon.
**Answer: Decomposition releases carbon through bacterial action.**

**2.** Explain: useful bacteria.
**Answer: Useful bacteria support biofuel and biofertiliser production and food processing such as milk, set against the harmful, pathogenic minority.**

**3.** Explain: four processes (student book).
**Answer: Fixation, nitrification, ammonification and denitrification are the nitrogen cycle's bacterial processes.**
