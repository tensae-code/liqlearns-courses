# Cycling of materials — decomposers and the nitrogen cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about without decomposers?
a) Without decomposers, nutrients such as nitrogen, carbon and oxygen — which exist in limited amounts — would stay locked in dead matter

**2.** What does this lesson say about four processes?
