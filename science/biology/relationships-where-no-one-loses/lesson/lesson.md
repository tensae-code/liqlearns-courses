# Relationships where no one loses — mutualism and commensalism

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Relationships where no one loses — mutualism and commensalism. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
A bee on a flower, a bird nesting in a tree — the gentler half of the interaction set.

[Picture 21081 — a bee on a flower and a bird nesting in a tree]

## The key facts
**Mutualism.** Mutualism is plus/plus, where both species benefit.

**Commensalism.** Commensalism is plus/zero, where one benefits and the other is neither helped nor harmed.

**Bee and flower.** A bee on a flower is mutualism — both gain.

**Bird in tree.** A bird nesting in a tree is commensalism — the bird gains, the tree is unaffected.
