# Relationships where no one loses — mutualism and commensalism — true or false

**1.** Mutualism is plus/plus, where both species benefit.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Commensalism has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.

**3.** A bee on a flower is mutualism — both gain.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Bird in tree has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
