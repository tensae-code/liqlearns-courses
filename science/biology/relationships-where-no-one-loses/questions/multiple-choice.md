# Relationships where no one loses — mutualism and commensalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about mutualism?
a) Mutualism is plus/plus, where both species benefit
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Mutualism is plus/plus, where both species benefit**

**2.** What does this lesson say about commensalism?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Commensalism is plus/zero, where one benefits and the other is neither helped nor harmed
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Commensalism is plus/zero, where one benefits and the other is neither helped nor harmed**

**3.** What does this lesson say about bee and flower?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) A bee on a flower is mutualism — both gain
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) A bee on a flower is mutualism — both gain**

**4.** What does this lesson say about bird in tree?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) A bird nesting in a tree is commensalism — the bird gains, the tree is unaffected
**Answer: d) A bird nesting in a tree is commensalism — the bird gains, the tree is unaffected**
