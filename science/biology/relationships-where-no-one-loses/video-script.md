Today: Relationships where no one loses — mutualism and commensalism.

Remember this one thing: mutualism benefits both species (plus/plus); commensalism benefits one and leaves the other unaffected (plus/zero).

Mutualism is plus/plus, where both species benefit.

Commensalism is plus/zero, where one benefits and the other is neither helped nor harmed.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
