Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: the body's first defence is non-specific — it answers any invader the same way: skin and membranes as barriers, inflammation flooding an injured place with blood, fever raising the temperature, and phagocyte cells engulfing microbes whole. non-specific defence mechanisms as their own section (skin, inflammation, fever, phagocytes)**
