Today: Peristalsis — how food moves through the gut.

Remember this one thing: food moves through the digestive canal by peristalsis: a wave-like muscular movement that pushes the food along, section by section.

Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 2 applications workbook (Activity 5, printed p. 26) asks "How does peristalsis work?" — the wave-like muscular movement that pushes food through the digestive canal, modelled and explained.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
