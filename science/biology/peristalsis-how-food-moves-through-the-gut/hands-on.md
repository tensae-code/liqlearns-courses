# Hands-on — Peristalsis — how food moves through the gut

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: food moves through the digestive canal by peristalsis: a wave-like muscular movement that pushes the food along, section by section.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a tube drawn with a bolus of food inside it and the muscular wall pinched behind the bolus and relaxed ahead of it, arrows showing the wave travelling along the tube. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
