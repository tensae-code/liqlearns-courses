# Peristalsis — how food moves through the gut — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: food moves through the digestive canal by peristalsis: a wave-like muscular movement that pushes the food along, section by section. Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 2 applications workbook (Activity 5, printed p. 26) asks "How does peristalsis work?" — the wave-like muscular movement that pushes food through the digestive canal, modelled and explained.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's workbook turns peristalsis into a modelling task (Activity 5): build the wave, watch it move the food, then explain it — the systems are learned by doing.**
