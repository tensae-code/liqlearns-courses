# Peristalsis — how food moves through the gut — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 2 applications workbook (Activity 5, printed p. 26) asks "How does peristalsis work?" — the wave-like muscular movement that pushes food through the digestive canal, modelled and explained.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كراسة التطبيقات — الصف الحادي عشر — الفصل الدراسي الثاني (Biology — Applications Workbook, Grade 11, Term 2), printed p. 26).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with peristalsis — how food moves through the gut.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is peristalsis — how food moves through the gut.
