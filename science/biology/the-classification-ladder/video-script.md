# Video script — The classification ladder — species to kingdom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: The classification ladder — species to kingdom.

Remember this one thing: species group into genera, then family, order, class, phylum and kingdom — each level wider than the one below.

Aristotle's early system grouped birds, bats and flying insects together for flight, joining unrelated organisms.

Linnaeus built the hierarchy still used today.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
