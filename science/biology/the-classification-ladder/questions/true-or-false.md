# The classification ladder — species to kingdom — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Aristotle's early system grouped birds, bats and flying insects together for flight, joining unrelated organisms.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Linnaeus has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Species group into genera, then family, order, class, phylum (or division) and kingdom.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Wider levels has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
