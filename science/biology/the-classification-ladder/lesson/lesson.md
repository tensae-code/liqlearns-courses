## The idea
Aristotle grouped plants as trees, shrubs and herbs and animals by red blood, which put some unrelated organisms together. Linnaeus built the ladder we still use.

## The key facts
**Aristotle.** Aristotle's early system grouped birds, bats and flying insects together for flight, joining unrelated organisms.

**Linnaeus.** Linnaeus built the hierarchy still used today.

**The ladder.** Species group into genera, then family, order, class, phylum (or division) and kingdom.

**Wider levels.** Each level up the ladder is wider and its members are less alike than the level below.
