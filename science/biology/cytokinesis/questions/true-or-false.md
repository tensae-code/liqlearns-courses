# Cytokinesis — dividing the cytoplasm — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Cytokinesis follows nuclear division.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. If cytokinesis fails after karyokinesis, one cell can hold two nuclei.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Plant cells divide their cytoplasm with a cleavage furrow.**
**Answer: False** — plant cells form a cell plate across the middle; the furrow is the animal way.

**4. Cytokinesis shares the cytoplasm and organelles between the two new cells.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
