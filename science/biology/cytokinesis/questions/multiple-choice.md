# Cytokinesis — dividing the cytoplasm — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is cytokinesis?**
a) The division of the nucleus  b) The division of the cytoplasm  c) The copying of DNA  d) The growth of the cell
**Answer: b) The division of the cytoplasm**

**2. What divides first in cell division?**
a) The cytoplasm  b) The nucleus — karyokinesis  c) The cell wall  d) The membrane only
**Answer: b) The nucleus — karyokinesis**

**3. How does an animal cell complete cytokinesis?**
a) A plate grows across the middle  b) A furrow pinches the cell in two  c) The wall dissolves  d) The nucleus leaves the cell
**Answer: b) A furrow pinches the cell in two**

**4. Why can't a plant cell pinch in two like an animal cell?**
a) It has no nucleus  b) Its stiff cell wall cannot pinch — a cell plate forms instead  c) It is too small  d) It divides only at night
**Answer: b) Its stiff cell wall cannot pinch — a cell plate forms instead**
