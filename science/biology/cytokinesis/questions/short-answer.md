# Cytokinesis — dividing the cytoplasm — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: cytokinesis in animal and plant cells, and why they differ.
**Answer: In an animal cell the membrane is drawn in as a cleavage furrow that deepens until the cell pinches in two. A plant cell's stiff wall cannot pinch, so a cell plate forms across the middle and grows into the new separating wall. Both share the cytoplasm between the two nuclei.**

**2.** Explain: North Macedonia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: North Macedonia's Biology I gives cytokinesis a separate lesson from nuclear division (Theme 2.11, printed p. 81), directly after karyokinesis (Theme 2.10) in its foundations-of-genetics theme — the two acts of division taught as two lessons.**
