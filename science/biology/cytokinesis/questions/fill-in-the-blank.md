# Cytokinesis — dividing the cytoplasm — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The division of the cytoplasm is called _____.**
**Answer: cytokinesis**

**2. The division of the nucleus is called _____.**
**Answer: karyokinesis**

**3. An animal cell is pinched in two by a cleavage _____.**
**Answer: furrow**

**4. A plant cell is separated by a growing cell _____.**
**Answer: plate**
