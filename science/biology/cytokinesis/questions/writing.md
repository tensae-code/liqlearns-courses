# Cytokinesis — dividing the cytoplasm — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why cell division needs cytokinesis as well as karyokinesis.
**Model answer: Karyokinesis divides only the nucleus, leaving one cell with two nuclei. A living cell also needs its cytoplasm and organelles shared out, one set around each nucleus. Cytokinesis does that sharing — by a furrow in animal cells, by a cell plate in plant cells — and only then has one cell become two.**
