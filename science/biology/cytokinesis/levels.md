# Cytokinesis — dividing the cytoplasm — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Cytokinesis is the division of the cytoplasm that follows nuclear division: in animal cells a furrow pinches the cell in two, in plant cells a plate grows across the middle — either way, each new cell receives its own nucleus and share of cytoplasm.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a fresh example from the learner's own surroundings and saying why it fits, without looking.
