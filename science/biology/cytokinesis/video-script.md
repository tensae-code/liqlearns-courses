Today: Cytokinesis — dividing the cytoplasm.

Remember this one thing: Cytokinesis is the division of the cytoplasm that follows nuclear division: in animal cells a furrow pinches the cell in two, in plant cells a plate grows across the middle — either way, each new cell receives its own nucleus and share of cytoplasm.

Cell division is two acts. The nucleus divides first — that is karyokinesis. Then the cytoplasm divides: cytokinesis.

An animal cell pinches in a furrow until it parts in two. A plant cell, walled and unable to pinch, grows a plate across its middle. Either way the end is two complete cells, each with its own nucleus.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
