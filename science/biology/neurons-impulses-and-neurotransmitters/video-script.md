# Video script — Neurons, impulses and neurotransmitters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A response needs four things (receptors detecting the stimulus, conduction, integration, effectors); once a receptor picks up a stimulus the information passes neuron to neuron across synapses; synapses between effector neurons and muscles — neuromuscular junctions — work like other synapses except the chemical crossing makes the muscle contract; and acetylcholine is introduced as the first neurotransmitter discovered, acting in both peripheral and central systems.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
