# Neurons, impulses and neurotransmitters — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added coordination detail?**
**Answer: A response needs four things (receptors detecting the stimulus, conduction, integration, effectors); once a receptor picks up a stimulus the information passes neuron to neuron across synapses; synapses between effector neurons and muscles — neuromuscular junctions — work like other synapses except the chemical crossing makes the muscle contract; and acetylcholine is introduced as the first neurotransmitter discovered, acting in both peripheral and central systems.**
