# Neurons, impulses and neurotransmitters — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. A response needs four things (receptors detecting the stimulus, conduction, integration, effectors); once a receptor picks up a stimulus the information passes neuron to neuron across synapses; synapses between effector neurons and muscles — neuromuscular junctions — work like other synapses except the chemical crossing makes the muscle contract; and acetylcholine is introduced as the first neurotransmitter discovered, acting in both peripheral and central systems.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Once a receptor picks up a stimulus the information passes neuron to neuron across synapses.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Synapses between effector neurons and muscles — neuromuscular junctions — work like other synapses except the chemical crossing makes the muscle contract.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. And acetylcholine is introduced as the first neurotransmitter discovered, acting in both peripheral and central systems.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**5. A response needs four things (receptors detecting the stimulus, conduction, integration, effectors).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**6. Synapses between effector neurons and muscles — neuromuscular junctions — work like other synapses except the chemical crossing makes the muscle contract.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
