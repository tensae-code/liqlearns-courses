# Hands-on — Transpiration and the transpiration stream

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Do the guide's investigation in simple form: tie a clear plastic bag over a leafy branch in sunlight and leave it for an hour. Note the water that collects inside the bag — that is transpiration made visible. Repeat on a cooler or shadier branch and compare the amounts.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the plant with water rising from roots to leaves and vapour leaving the leaf, the three jobs (support, transport, cooling) written beside it — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
