# Lesson plan — Transpiration and the transpiration stream

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Transpiration and the transpiration stream
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — Grades 11–12 band (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Transpiration is the loss of water vapour from a plant, and the pull it makes draws the transpiration stream up from the roots to the leaves; Samoa's Year 13 guide teaches transpiration for its three roles — support, transport and cooling — with the factors that change its rate investigated.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier biology of living things, health and the human body, and builds the Grades 11–12 band understanding of transpiration and the transpiration stream.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
