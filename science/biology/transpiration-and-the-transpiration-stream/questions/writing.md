# Transpiration and the transpiration stream — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining the transpiration stream to a younger student. Name its three jobs.
**Model answer: A plant loses water vapour from its leaves — that is transpiration — and the loss pulls a stream of water up from the roots to replace it. That stream does three jobs: it transports dissolved minerals up the plant, it keeps the cells full so the plant is supported, and evaporation cools the leaf.**
