# Transpiration and the transpiration stream — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Transpiration cools the leaf as water evaporates from it.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. The transpiration stream flows downwards, from the leaves to the roots.**
**Answer: False** — the stream flows upward, from the roots to the leaves, pulled by the water lost at the leaves.

**3. The transpiration stream carries dissolved minerals up the plant.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. Water filling the plant's cells helps hold the plant up.**
**Answer: True** — firm, water-filled cells give the plant support.
