# Transpiration and the transpiration stream — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The loss of water vapour from a plant is called _____.**
**Answer: transpiration**

**2. Water is pulled up the _____ from the roots to the leaves.**
**Answer: xylem**

**3. Transpiration has three roles: support, _____ and cooling.**
**Answer: transport**

**4. Hot, dry, moving air _____ the rate of transpiration.**
**Answer: speeds up (increases)**
