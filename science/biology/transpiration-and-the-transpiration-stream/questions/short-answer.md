# Transpiration and the transpiration stream — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the transpiration stream and how it is driven.
**Answer: Water vapour is lost from the leaves by transpiration. As it leaves, more water is pulled up behind it through the xylem from the roots, which take in more water from the soil. This continuous upward flow is the transpiration stream, and it is driven by the loss at the leaf.**

**2.** Explain: Samoa's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Samoa's Year 13 Learning Guide (MESC, 2004) teaches transpiration in its plant unit (Unit 4, printed p. 35) by its roles — support, transport and cooling — and sets the factors affecting its rate as an investigation for the learner, in the same unit as root tissues in transport and translocation.**
