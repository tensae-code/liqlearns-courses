# Transpiration and the transpiration stream — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is transpiration?**
a) Water entering the roots  b) The loss of water vapour from the plant, mainly through the leaves  c) The making of food in the leaf  d) Water stored in the stem
**Answer: b) The loss of water vapour from the plant, mainly through the leaves**

**2. What is the transpiration stream?**
a) A river beside the plant  b) Rain running off the leaves  c) The continuous upward flow of water from roots to leaves, pulled by transpiration  d) Water moving down the phloem
**Answer: c) The continuous upward flow of water from roots to leaves, pulled by transpiration**

**3. Which set names transpiration's three roles as Samoa's guide teaches them?**
a) Support, transport and cooling  b) Growth, flowering and seeding  c) Breathing, feeding and excretion  d) Support, seeding and shading
**Answer: a) Support, transport and cooling**

**4. Which change would speed up the rate of transpiration?**
a) Cool, damp, still air  b) Darkness  c) Hot, dry, moving air  d) Rain on the leaves
**Answer: c) Hot, dry, moving air**
