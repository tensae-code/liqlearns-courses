# Transpiration and the transpiration stream — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Transpiration is the loss of water vapour from a plant, and the pull it makes draws the transpiration stream up from the roots to the leaves; Samoa's Year 13 guide teaches transpiration for its three roles — support, transport and cooling — with the factors that change its rate investigated.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by predicting what happens to the transpiration rate when one condition changes — hotter, drier, windier — and saying why, without looking.
