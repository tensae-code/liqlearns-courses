# What speeds and slows enzymes — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about factors affecting enzyme action?**
**Answer: Activity peaks at an optimum and falls away with temperature (heat denatures, irreversibly), pH, substrate and enzyme concentration, end-product build-up, radiation, water, activators and inhibitors; each factor's curve is drawn and pupils test heat sensitivity by dropping hydrogen peroxide on raw vs boiled potato.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added experiment explanations?**
**Answer: The guide explains the student book's labs: potato bubbles in hydrogen peroxide because its catalase breaks the peroxide (a powerful oxidiser) down, the bubbling being the visible sign of metabolic decomposition into enzyme, substrate and product terms; and a cut apple browns because its polyphenol oxidase (phenolase), released and meeting air oxygen, catalyses formation of the brown pigment melanin — an oxidation the guide calls an enzyme reduction/oxidation reaction.**
