# Hands-on — Algae — plant-like protists

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Look at water from a pond, ditch or long-standing container — a green tinge or green slime on the sides is algae at work. Note where you found it and what the water gives it.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of four algae side by side — a single Chlamydomonas cell with its flagella, a green Spirogyra filament, a diatom in its glassy shell, and a brown kelp with holdfast and blades — each labelled — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
