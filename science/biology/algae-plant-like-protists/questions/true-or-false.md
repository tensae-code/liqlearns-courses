# Algae — plant-like protists — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Algae are the plant-like protists — they contain chlorophyll and photosynthesise like plants.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Algae have true roots, stems and leaves like a bean plant..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.

**3.** Algae have no true roots, stems or leaves, however large some of them grow.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Algae feed by catching and eating other living things..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.
