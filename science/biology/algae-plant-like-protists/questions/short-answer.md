# Algae — plant-like protists — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: from one cell to seaweed.
**Answer: Some algae are one cell alone, some live as filaments or colonies, and the brown algae of cold seas grow into kelp many metres long. Green, brown and red algae are told apart by the pigments that colour them.**

**2.** Explain: mostly in water.
**Answer: Water holds algae up, feeds them minerals and keeps them from drying out, so they are found wherever water or damp is: pond surfaces, wet rocks and soil, tree bark — and as the floating phytoplankton of lakes and seas.**
