# Modelling bile function — fat emulsification — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: bile's job on fats is emulsification: it breaks large fat globules into tiny droplets — and the action can be modelled with washing-up liquid and oil. Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 2 applications workbook (Activity 4, printed p. 24) models the function of bile: bile's emulsifying action on fats is modelled and observed — with washing-up liquid and oil standing in for bile and fat.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's workbook teaches digestion by doing: the bile activity (Activity 4) is a kitchen-materials model — washing-up liquid and oil — observed and recorded in the workbook's write-in sections.**
