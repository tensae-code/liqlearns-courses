bile's emulsifying action on fats modelled and observed

## The key facts
**The model.** Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 2 applications workbook (Activity 4, printed p. 24) models the function of bile: bile's emulsifying action on fats is modelled and observed — with washing-up liquid and oil standing in for bile and fat.

**What emulsification does.** Emulsification breaks the fat into tiny droplets, multiplying the surface the digestive enzymes can work on — the model shows the breaking-up directly.

**Kuwait's way.** Kuwait's workbook teaches digestion by doing: the bile activity (Activity 4) is a kitchen-materials model — washing-up liquid and oil — observed and recorded in the workbook's write-in sections.
