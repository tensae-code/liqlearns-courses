# Hands-on — Modelling bile function — fat emulsification

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: bile's job on fats is emulsification: it breaks large fat globules into tiny droplets — and the action can be modelled with washing-up liquid and oil.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: two jars of water and oil side by side: in the first the oil sits in one large layer, in the second, after the modelling agent is added, the oil is scattered as tiny droplets through the water. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
