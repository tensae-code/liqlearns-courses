Today: Modelling bile function — fat emulsification.

Remember this one thing: bile's job on fats is emulsification: it breaks large fat globules into tiny droplets — and the action can be modelled with washing-up liquid and oil.

Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 2 applications workbook (Activity 4, printed p. 24) models the function of bile: bile's emulsifying action on fats is modelled and observed — with washing-up liquid and oil standing in for bile and fat.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
