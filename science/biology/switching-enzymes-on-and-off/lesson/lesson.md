# Switching enzymes on and off

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — switching enzymes on and off. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Cells switch enzymes on and off as needed: allosteric enzymes carry a second, regulatory site where an effector molecule binds and reshapes the active site, raising or lowering activity; covalently modulated enzymes are switched by chemical modification, phosphorylation — adding phosphate groups — being the commonest.
