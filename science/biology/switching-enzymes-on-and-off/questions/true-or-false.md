# Switching enzymes on and off — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Cells switch enzymes on and off as needed: allosteric enzymes carry a second, regulatory site where an effector molecule binds and reshapes the active site, raising or lowering activity; covalently modulated enzymes are switched by chemical modification, phosphorylation — adding phosphate groups — being the commonest.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Covalently modulated enzymes are switched by chemical modification, phosphorylation — adding phosphate groups — being the commonest.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Cells switch enzymes on and off as needed: allosteric enzymes carry a second, regulatory site where an effector molecule binds and reshapes the active site, raising or lowering activity.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
