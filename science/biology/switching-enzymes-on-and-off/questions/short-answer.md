# Switching enzymes on and off — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about regulation?**
**Answer: Cells switch enzymes on and off as needed: allosteric enzymes carry a second, regulatory site where an effector molecule binds and reshapes the active site, raising or lowering activity; covalently modulated enzymes are switched by chemical modification, phosphorylation — adding phosphate groups — being the commonest.**
