# Gas-exchange organs across animals — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: animals exchange gases through organs fitted to where they live — skin in worms and frogs, feathery gills in water animals, branching tracheae opening at spiracles in insects, and lungs in land vertebrates — all of them thin, moist, wide-surfaced and well supplied. respiration across animal groups: skin, gills, tracheae and lungs compared**

**Answer: respiration across animal groups: skin, gills, tracheae and lungs compared respiration in animals, breathing organs matched to organisms, evolution and respiration (4.6, p. 57), different ways of breathing compared**
