Today: Gas-exchange organs across animals.

Remember this one thing: animals exchange gases through organs fitted to where they live — skin in worms and frogs, feathery gills in water animals, branching tracheae opening at spiracles in insects, and lungs in land vertebrates — all of them thin, moist, wide-surfaced and well supplied.
