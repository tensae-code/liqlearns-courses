# Gas-exchange organs across animals — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (animals exchange gases through organs fitted to where they live — skin in worms and frogs, feathery gills in water animals, branching tracheae opening at spiracles in insects, and lungs in land vertebrates — all of them thin, moist, wide-surfaced and well supplied) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
