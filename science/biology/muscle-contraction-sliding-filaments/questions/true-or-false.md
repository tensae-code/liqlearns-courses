# Muscle contraction — sliding filaments — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Muscle fibres are bundles of myofibrils built from thick myosin and thin actin filaments in repeating units (sarcomeres); a nerve impulse at the neuromuscular junction releases calcium, calcium binding to troponin uncovers actin's binding sites (normally blocked by tropomyosin), myosin heads form cross-bridges and the filaments slide past each other — the sliding-filament action — needing ATP; contractions are isometric (no length change), concentric (shortening) or eccentric (lengthening).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
