# Monera — the bacteria

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Monera — the bacteria. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Bacteria are tiny, numerous and varied: some helpful, some harmful, some making their own food.

[Picture 21018 — rod, sphere and spiral bacteria greatly magnified]

## The key facts
**Two groups.** Monera holds eubacteria (true bacteria, with strong cell walls, in varied shapes) and archaebacteria.

**No nucleus.** Bacteria have no nucleus.

**Feeding.** Some bacteria feed on other organisms, some make food by photosynthesis and others by chemical reactions (chemosynthesis).

**Habitats.** Bacteria live in almost every habitat.
