# Monera — the bacteria — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about two groups?
a) Monera holds eubacteria (true bacteria, with strong cell walls, in varied shapes) and archaebacteria
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Monera holds eubacteria (true bacteria, with strong cell walls, in varied shapes) and archaebacteria**

**2.** What does this lesson say about no nucleus?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Bacteria have no nucleus
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Bacteria have no nucleus**

**3.** What does this lesson say about feeding?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Some bacteria feed on other organisms, some make food by photosynthesis and others by chemical reactions (chemosynthesis)
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Some bacteria feed on other organisms, some make food by photosynthesis and others by chemical reactions (chemosynthesis)**

**4.** What does this lesson say about habitats?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Bacteria live in almost every habitat
**Answer: d) Bacteria live in almost every habitat**
