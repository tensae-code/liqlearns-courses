# Monera — the bacteria — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Monera holds eubacteria (true bacteria, with strong cell walls, in varied shapes) and archaebacteria.
**Answer: True**

**2.** No nucleus has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Some bacteria feed on other organisms, some make food by photosynthesis and others by chemical reactions (chemosynthesis).
**Answer: True**

**4.** Habitats has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
