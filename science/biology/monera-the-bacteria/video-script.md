The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: Monera — the bacteria.

Remember this one thing: Monera holds the bacteria — cells with no nucleus, living almost everywhere.

Monera holds eubacteria (true bacteria, with strong cell walls, in varied shapes) and archaebacteria.

Bacteria have no nucleus.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
