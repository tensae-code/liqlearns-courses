# Diseases of the circulatory system — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: stroke burst.
**Answer: A stroke can be a vessel in the brain bursting.**

**2.** Explain: classification.
**Answer: Cardiovascular diseases are classified into several types, with a detailed list in the student textbook.**

**3.** Explain: varicose veins (student book).
**Answer: Varicose veins come from faulty vein valves, drawn against a working valve.**
