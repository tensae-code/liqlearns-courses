## The idea
This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only. The guide frames the classification and explains a stroke plainly. (teacher guide — evidence)

## The key facts
**Examples.** Heart attack, stroke and heart failure are entry examples of cardiovascular disease.

**Stroke blocked.** A stroke can be a vessel supplying the brain blocked by a clot.

**Stroke burst.** A stroke can be a vessel in the brain bursting.

**Classification.** Cardiovascular diseases are classified into several types, with a detailed list in the student textbook.
