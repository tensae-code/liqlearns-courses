# Lamarck vs Darwin

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — lamarck vs darwin. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Lamarckian cases are worked (limbless snakes from lizard-like ancestors by disuse of limbs and body-stretching; the horse's legs and digits changing as soft ground gave way to dry); Darwin's giraffe case is worked in question form (short necks starved when food moved to upper branches; long necks survived and passed genes on); a comparison table sets the theories side by side (variation from environmental need vs natural heritable variation; survival by developing features vs environmental selection); and further variation sources are listed (gene recombination by crossing over, bivalent arrangement and gamete fusion; hybridisation between species; mutagens — base analogues, methylating agents, acridine dyes, and physical agents).
