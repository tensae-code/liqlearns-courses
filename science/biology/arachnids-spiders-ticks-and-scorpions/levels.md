# Arachnids — spiders, ticks and scorpions — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (arachnids — spiders, ticks and scorpions — are arthropods whose body is built in two parts, cephalothorax and abdomen, living on land, in soil and in burrows, some of them parasitising plants and animals) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
