# Arachnids — spiders, ticks and scorpions — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Moldova's Grade 7 book (2.9 Class Arachnids) teaches: spiders, ticks and scorpions; cephalothorax and abdomen; tick-borne encephalitis and mowing grass as prevention debated. (printed p. 44).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Moldova's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Biologie: manual pentru clasa a 7-a (Grade 7); Biologie: Clasa a X-a (Profil real/umanist) (Grade 10)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with arachnids — spiders, ticks and scorpions.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is arachnids — spiders, ticks and scorpions.
