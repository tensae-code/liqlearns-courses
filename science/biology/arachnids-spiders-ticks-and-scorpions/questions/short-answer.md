# Arachnids — spiders, ticks and scorpions — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: arachnids — spiders, ticks and scorpions — are arthropods whose body is built in two parts, cephalothorax and abdomen, living on land, in soil and in burrows, some of them parasitising plants and animals. Moldova's Grade 7 book (2.9 Class Arachnids) teaches: spiders, ticks and scorpions; cephalothorax and abdomen; tick-borne encephalitis and mowing grass as prevention debated. (printed p. 44).**

**2.** Explain Moldova's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Moldova teaches arachnids as their own class inside the arthropod march (Grade 7), returning at Grade 10 for their diversity and habitats; tick-borne encephalitis and mowing grass as prevention are debated in class.**
