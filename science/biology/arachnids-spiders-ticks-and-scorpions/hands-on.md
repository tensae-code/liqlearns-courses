# Hands-on — Arachnids — spiders, ticks and scorpions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: arachnids — spiders, ticks and scorpions — are arthropods whose body is built in two parts, cephalothorax and abdomen, living on land, in soil and in burrows, some of them parasitising plants and animals.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a spider, a tick and a scorpion side by side, each labelled with its cephalothorax and abdomen. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
