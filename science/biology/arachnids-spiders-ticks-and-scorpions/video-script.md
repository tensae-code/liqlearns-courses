Today: Arachnids — spiders, ticks and scorpions.

Remember this one thing: arachnids — spiders, ticks and scorpions — are arthropods whose body is built in two parts, cephalothorax and abdomen, living on land, in soil and in burrows, some of them parasitising plants and animals.

Moldova's Grade 7 book (2.9 Class Arachnids) teaches: spiders, ticks and scorpions; cephalothorax and abdomen; tick-borne encephalitis and mowing grass as prevention debated. (printed p. 44).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
