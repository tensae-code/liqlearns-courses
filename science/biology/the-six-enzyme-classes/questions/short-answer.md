# The six enzyme classes — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about classes of enzymes?**
**Answer: Structurally, enzymes divide into simple proteins and conjugated proteins (holoenzymes = protein apoenzyme plus non-protein cofactor, the cofactor being a firm prosthetic group or a loose coenzyme); by reaction, the six basic classes are oxidoreductases, transferases, hydrolases, lyases, isomerases and ligases, all named with the "-ase" ending; a lab tests enzyme browning reactions in cut apple or banana.**
