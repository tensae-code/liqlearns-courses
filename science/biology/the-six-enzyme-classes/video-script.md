# Video script — The six enzyme classes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Structurally, enzymes divide into simple proteins and conjugated proteins (holoenzymes = protein apoenzyme plus non-protein cofactor, the cofactor being a firm prosthetic group or a loose coenzyme); by reaction, the six basic classes are oxidoreductases, transferases, hydrolases, lyases, isomerases and ligases, all named with the "-ase" ending; a lab tests enzyme browning reactions in cut apple or banana.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
