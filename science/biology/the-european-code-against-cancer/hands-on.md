# Hands-on — The European Code Against Cancer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: the European Code Against Cancer is a short public list of everyday rules — do not smoke, keep a healthy weight, move daily, eat well, limit alcohol, respect the sun, and take the offered screenings and vaccinations — reproduced in Greece's Grades 8–9 appendix as the Greek and European way of teaching cancer prevention.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a poster page with a row of simple rule icons — a crossed cigarette, a plate of vegetables, a walking figure, a sun with a hat — above a set of numbered rule lines. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
