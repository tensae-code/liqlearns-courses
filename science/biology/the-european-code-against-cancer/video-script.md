Today: The European Code Against Cancer.

Remember this one thing: the European Code Against Cancer is a short public list of everyday rules — do not smoke, keep a healthy weight, move daily, eat well, limit alcohol, respect the sun, and take the offered screenings and vaccinations — reproduced in Greece's Grades 8–9 appendix as the Greek and European way of teaching cancer prevention.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
