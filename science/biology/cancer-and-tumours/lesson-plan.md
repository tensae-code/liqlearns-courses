# Lesson plan — Cancer and tumours

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Cancer and tumours
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — appended to Unit 16 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** cell division is normally controlled — cells divide when the body needs them and stop when it does not; when that control fails, cells divide without control and pile up into a tumour, and Azerbaijan's Grade 11 book closes its cell-division part with a section titled simply "Cancer" (Azerbaijan, Grade 11 — *Биология 11 (Biology 11)*, Russian-sector student textbook, Part VII §§46–47, printed pp. 175–178).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier biology of the cell cycle and mitosis, and builds the Grade 11 understanding of uncontrolled division. Mitosis — the phases in order is recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: **Mitosis — the phases in order** and **The cell cycle**. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say, in their own words, the one thing this lesson teaches: when the control of cell division fails, cells divide without control and form a tumour — the uncontrolled division Azerbaijan's book closes with, in its section titled "Cancer".
2. Say what a tumour (Опухоли) is: the mass built by uncontrolled division of cells.
3. Explain Azerbaijan's way as the placements describe it: the book's last part pairs controlled and uncontrolled cell division — mitosis first, then tumours (§46) and "Cancer" (§47), then the mitosis–meiosis comparison.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck

- Use the picture: compare the two cell families and say what the uncontrolled one builds.
