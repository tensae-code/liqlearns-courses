# Hands-on — Cancer and tumours

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Take two piles of counters or stones. In the first pile, add one counter at a time and stop when you reach the number you set — that is controlled division. In the second pile, keep adding counters with no stopping number, and watch the pile grow into a heap — that is uncontrolled division, and the heap stands for the tumour. Say aloud which pile shows the control and which shows the control failed.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the two cell families side by side, controlled against uncontrolled, the mass labelled tumour — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
