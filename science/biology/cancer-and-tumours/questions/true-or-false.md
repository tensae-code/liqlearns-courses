# Cancer and tumours — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Normal cell division is controlled: cells divide when needed and stop when the need is met.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A tumour is built by cells dividing under perfect control.
**Answer: False** — a tumour is the mass built by uncontrolled division, when the control has failed.

**3.** Azerbaijan's book gives cancer its own section, titled simply "Cancer" (Рак).
**Answer: True**

**4.** Uncontrolled division has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly: uncontrolled division forms tumours.
