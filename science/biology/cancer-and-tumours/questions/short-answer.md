# Cancer and tumours — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: controlled against uncontrolled division.
**Answer: Controlled division runs when the body needs new cells and stops when the need is met. In uncontrolled division the control has failed: the cell keeps dividing, its copies divide too, and the extra cells pile up into a tumour.**

**2.** Explain: Azerbaijan's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Azerbaijan's Grade 11 book closes with a part on controlled and uncontrolled cell division: after mitosis it teaches uncontrolled division — tumours (Опухоли, §46) — and then a section titled simply "Cancer" (Рак, §47), before the final mitosis–meiosis comparison.**
