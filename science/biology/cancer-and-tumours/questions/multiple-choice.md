# Cancer and tumours — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is controlled division?
a) Division that runs when new cells are needed and stops when the need is met
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Division that runs when new cells are needed and stops when the need is met**

**2.** What happens when the control of division fails?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) The cell divides without control and its copies pile up as extra cells
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The cell divides without control and its copies pile up as extra cells**

**3.** What is a tumour (Опухоли)?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) The mass of extra cells built by uncontrolled division — cells the body did not order and does not need
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) The mass of extra cells built by uncontrolled division — cells the body did not order and does not need**

**4.** How does Azerbaijan's book present cancer?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) As its own closing section, titled simply "Cancer" (Рак), straight after tumours
**Answer: d) As its own closing section, titled simply "Cancer" (Рак), straight after tumours**
