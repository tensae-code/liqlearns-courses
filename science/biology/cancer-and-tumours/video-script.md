Today: Cancer and tumours.

Remember this one thing: cell division is normally controlled. Cells divide when the body needs them — and stop when it does not.

Azerbaijan's Grade 11 book ends its course on exactly this pair: controlled and uncontrolled cell division.

When the control fails, a cell divides without control. Its copies divide too, and the pile of extra cells builds a mass. That mass is a tumour — Опухоли, in the book's §46.

And the book gives the illness its own closing section, titled simply: Cancer — Рак, §47.

Controlled division builds and repairs the body. Uncontrolled division builds a tumour. That difference is the lesson.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
