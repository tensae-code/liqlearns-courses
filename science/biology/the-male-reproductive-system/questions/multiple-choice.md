# The male reproductive system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about testes?
a) Paired testes produce sperm and the male hormone
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Paired testes produce sperm and the male hormone**

**2.** What does this lesson say about storage and carrying?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) The epididymis stores sperm and the vas deferens carries them
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The epididymis stores sperm and the vas deferens carries them**

**3.** What does this lesson say about semen?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Accessory glands add the fluid that makes semen
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Accessory glands add the fluid that makes semen**

**4.** What does this lesson say about penis?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) The penis delivers semen
**Answer: d) The penis delivers semen**
**5.** What does this lesson say about erection?
a) It is caused by muscles pulling the penis upright
b) Arousal dilates the arteries of the erectile tissues, blood engorges them, and compressed veins trap the blood
c) The urethra fills with urine
d) The scrotum contracts and lifts the testes
**Answer: b) Arousal dilates the arteries of the erectile tissues, blood engorges them, and compressed veins trap the blood**
