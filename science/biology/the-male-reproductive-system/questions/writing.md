# The male reproductive system — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1. ** Write two or three sentences explaining the male reproductive system to a younger student. Use one example from the lesson.
**Model answer: External organs (penis, testes, scrotum) and internal parts (prostate, vas deferens, urethra) working to produce and deposit sperm**
**2. ** Write two or three sentences explaining how an erection happens, mechanically, and name the urethra's two jobs.
**Model answer: On arousal, the arteries of the erectile tissues dilate, so blood rushes in and engorges the tissue; the swollen tissue compresses the veins, trapping the blood so the penis stays firm. The urethra carries urine out of the body and also carries semen out.**
