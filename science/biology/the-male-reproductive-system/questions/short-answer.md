# The male reproductive system — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: semen.
**Answer: Accessory glands add the fluid that makes semen.**

**2.** Explain: penis.
**Answer: The penis delivers semen.**
**3.** Explain: erection.
**Answer: Arousal dilates the arteries of the erectile tissues so blood engorges them; the swollen tissue compresses the veins, trapping the blood and keeping the penis firm.**
