The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: The male reproductive system.

Remember this one thing: the male system makes sperm in the testes, stores them, and delivers them in semen.

Paired testes produce sperm and the male hormone.

The epididymis stores sperm and the vas deferens carries them.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
