## The key facts
**Testes.** Paired testes produce sperm and the male hormone.

**Storage and carrying.** The epididymis stores sperm and the vas deferens carries them.

**Semen.** Accessory glands add the fluid that makes semen.

**Penis.** The penis delivers semen.

## The Grade 11 depth — the system part by part
The Grade 11 book sets the system out in full. The **external organs** are the penis, the testes and the scrotum — the sac that holds the testes outside the body. The **internal parts** include the prostate, the vas deferens and the urethra. Together the parts do one job in two halves: produce sperm, and deposit them where fertilisation can happen.

**Erection, explained mechanically.** The penis contains erectile tissue. On arousal, the arteries supplying that tissue dilate — they widen — so blood rushes in and engorges the tissue. As the tissue swells, it compresses the veins that would drain blood away, so the blood is trapped and the penis stays firm. Erection is therefore a blood-flow event: more blood in through widened arteries, less out through compressed veins.

**One tube, two jobs.** The urethra is the final shared passage: it carries urine out of the body, and it also carries semen out. The same tube serves both systems, at different times.
