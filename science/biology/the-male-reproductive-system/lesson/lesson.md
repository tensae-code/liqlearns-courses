# The male reproductive system

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The male reproductive system. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Trace a sperm cell's path from formation to exit. The teacher guide completes the structure–function table: epididymis (sperm finish maturing), prostate gland (alkaline fluid helping sperm move), scrotum (sac holding the testes), seminal vesicle (fructose-rich fluid), vas deferens (the carrying duct) (teacher guide — evidence).

## The key facts
**Testes.** Paired testes produce sperm and the male hormone.

**Storage and carrying.** The epididymis stores sperm and the vas deferens carries them.

**Semen.** Accessory glands add the fluid that makes semen.

**Penis.** The penis delivers semen.
