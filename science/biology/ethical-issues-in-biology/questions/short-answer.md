# Ethical issues in biology — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about ethical issues in biology?**
**Answer: Ethics is introduced as judging the rightness or wrongness of using biological discoveries, biotechnology products and devices where human health, lifestyles, livelihoods, medicine and the environment are at stake; local health and agriculture professionals are invited in to discuss it.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added ethics framing?**
**Answer: The guide gives teachers a three-part framing to add to the ethics lesson: ethical issues judge the rightness or wrongness of using biological discoveries for human health and life; unethical issues are wrong uses of technology against human rights; ethical questions are concerns about judgements that affect human rights — before the plant- and animal-treatment discussion and a zoologist's visit on sentient animals, suffering reduction and habitat/species/ecosystem balance.**
