## Sub lesson 1 — Ethical issues in biology

Ethics is introduced as judging the rightness or wrongness of using biological discoveries, biotechnology products and devices where human health, lifestyles, livelihoods, medicine and the environment are at stake; local health and agriculture professionals are invited in to discuss it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Added ethics framing — questions of right and wrong

The guide gives teachers a three-part framing to add to the ethics lesson: ethical issues judge the rightness or wrongness of using biological discoveries for human health and life; unethical issues are wrong uses of technology against human rights; ethical questions are concerns about judgements that affect human rights — before the plant- and animal-treatment discussion and a zoologist's visit on sentient animals, suffering reduction and habitat/species/ecosystem balance.
