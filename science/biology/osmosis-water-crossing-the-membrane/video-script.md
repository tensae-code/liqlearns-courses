Today: Osmosis — water crossing the membrane.

Remember this one thing: osmosis is water moving across a selectively permeable membrane from a dilute to a more concentrated solution.

Osmosis is the movement of water across a selectively permeable membrane from a dilute to a more concentrated solution.

In dilute (hypotonic) surroundings a plant cell fills its vacuole until it is firm and turgid — the strength of plant tissues.
