## The key facts
**Definition.** Osmosis is the movement of water across a selectively permeable membrane from a dilute to a more concentrated solution.

**Turgid.** In dilute (hypotonic) surroundings a plant cell fills its vacuole until it is firm and turgid — the strength of plant tissues.

**Water out.** In concentrated surroundings water leaves the plant cell and the cytoplasm pulls away from the wall.

**Animal cells.** Animal cells, lacking a wall, can swell and burst or shrink as water moves.
