# Cancer immunotherapy — checkpoints and engineered T cells — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. two strategies: T cells taken out, re-engineered to recognise a cancer, multiplied and returned (in clinical use for some lymphomas); and checkpoint blockade — tumours over-express PD-L1 to be "recognised as self" by PD-1, so antibodies block the handshake (broad-spectrum approvals by November 2018) — with the limits stated: engineered cells can hit healthy tissue, and an unbraked immune system can overreact. No existing lesson covers it.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in China's People's Education Press biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (普通高中教科书《生物学》选择性必修1 稳态与调节 (Senior High Biology, Selective Compulsory Book 1: Homeostasis and Regulation, People's Education Press, 2019-standard edition)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with cancer immunotherapy — checkpoints and engineered t cells.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is cancer immunotherapy — checkpoints and engineered t cells.
