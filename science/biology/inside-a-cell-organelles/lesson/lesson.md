# Inside a cell — organelles and their jobs

## Do it first

## The idea
Plant and animal cells share nucleus, cytoplasm and cell membrane. Around them the organelles divide the work.

## The key facts
**Nucleus.** The nucleus controls the cell and holds chromosomes of DNA and the nucleolus.

**Walls and jelly.** The cell wall gives support and shape outside the membrane in plants; the cytoplasm is the jelly where reactions happen.

**Power and packing.** Mitochondria release energy; the Golgi apparatus modifies, tags and dispatches proteins, and vesicles store and ship materials.

**Chloroplasts.** Chloroplasts in plant cells carry out photosynthesis.
