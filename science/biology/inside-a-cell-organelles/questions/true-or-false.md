# Inside a cell — organelles and their jobs — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** The nucleus controls the cell and holds chromosomes of DNA and the nucleolus.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Walls and jelly has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Mitochondria release energy; the Golgi apparatus modifies, tags and dispatches proteins, and vesicles store and ship materials.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Chloroplasts has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
