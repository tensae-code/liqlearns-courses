# Inside a cell — organelles and their jobs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about nucleus?
a) The nucleus controls the cell and holds chromosomes of DNA and the nucleolus
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The nucleus controls the cell and holds chromosomes of DNA and the nucleolus**

**2.** What does this lesson say about walls and jelly?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) The cell wall gives support and shape outside the membrane in plants; the cytoplasm is the jelly where reactions happen
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The cell wall gives support and shape outside the membrane in plants; the cytoplasm is the jelly where reactions happen**

**3.** What does this lesson say about power and packing?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Mitochondria release energy; the Golgi apparatus modifies, tags and dispatches proteins, and vesicles store and ship materials
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Mitochondria release energy; the Golgi apparatus modifies, tags and dispatches proteins, and vesicles store and ship materials**

**4.** What does this lesson say about chloroplasts?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Chloroplasts in plant cells carry out photosynthesis
**Answer: d) Chloroplasts in plant cells carry out photosynthesis**
