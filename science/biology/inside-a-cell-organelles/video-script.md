Today: Inside a cell — organelles and their jobs.

Remember this one thing: each organelle inside a cell has its own job, from control to making, packing and shipping proteins.

The nucleus controls the cell and holds chromosomes of DNA and the nucleolus.

The cell wall gives support and shape outside the membrane in plants; the cytoplasm is the jelly where reactions happen.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
