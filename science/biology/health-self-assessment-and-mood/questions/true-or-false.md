# Health self-assessment — and mood — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. health scored, not assumed: a 25-item self-test (which embeds the Chinese school-age BMI standard — for 13-year-olds, overweight above 21.9 for boys and 22.6 for girls) sorted with classmates into body, mind and social-adaptation items; the WHO definition drawn as a triangle; and three named methods for regulating mood — shift attention, vent appropriately, console yourself. No existing lesson covers self-assessment of health.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in China's People's Education Press biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (义务教育教科书《生物学》八年级下册 (Biology, Grade 8, Volume 2, People's Education Press, 2013)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with health self-assessment — and mood.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is health self-assessment — and mood.
