# Enzyme cofactors and coenzymes — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (many enzymes work only with a helper: a cofactor — often a metal ion — or an organic coenzyme, many of them made from vitamins, sits with the enzyme and takes part in the reaction it speeds) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
