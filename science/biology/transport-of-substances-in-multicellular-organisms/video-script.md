Today: Transport of substances in multicellular organisms.

Remember this one thing: a multicellular organism is too big for diffusion alone, so substances must be carried: in plants through their conducting tissues and in animals through blood and lymph, from where a substance is taken in or made to every cell that needs it.

§16 The importance of transport of substances for the life activity of living organisms
