# Transport of substances in multicellular organisms — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (a multicellular organism is too big for diffusion alone, so substances must be carried: in plants through their conducting tissues and in animals through blood and lymph, from where a substance is taken in or made to every cell that needs it) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
