# Cyanobacteria — the blue-green algae — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Cyanobacteria are true bacteria — prokaryotic cells of Kingdom Monera, with no nucleus and no membrane-bound organelles.
**Answer: True**

**2.** They photosynthesise is the opposite of what this lesson teaches.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches this as stated.**

**3.** They live as single cells, as colonies, and as filaments — chains of cells in a row — and some of their cells are specialised: in many filament forms, particular cells fix nitrogen from the air, turning it into a form living things can use, a service few organisms can do..
**Answer: True**

**4.** This lesson's topic plays no part in biology at all.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
