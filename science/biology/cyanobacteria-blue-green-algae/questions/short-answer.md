# Cyanobacteria — the blue-green algae — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: bacteria, not algae.
**Answer: Cyanobacteria are true bacteria — prokaryotic cells of Kingdom Monera, with no nucleus and no membrane-bound organelles. Their old name, blue-green algae, records the mistake: they are green, they live in water and light, and they were classed with the algae until their cell structure showed what they are.**

**2.** Explain: they photosynthesise.
**Answer: Cyanobacteria carry photosynthetic pigments inside the cell and make their own food from sunlight, water and carbon dioxide, giving out oxygen — the plant way of feeding, done by a bacterium. Mats and filaments of them colour ponds and wet rocks blue-green.**
