# Stomata — opening by guard-cell turgor — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A stoma is a small pore in the leaf's surface.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Guard cells open the stoma by losing water and going flaccid.
**Answer: False** — they open it by gaining water and becoming turgid; losing water closes it.

**3.** Turgid guard cells bulge outwards.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Osmosis has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly — the guard cells gain and lose water by osmosis.
