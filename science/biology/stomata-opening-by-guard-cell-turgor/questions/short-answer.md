# Stomata — opening by guard-cell turgor — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: how a stoma opens.
**Answer: The guard cells gain water by osmosis, become turgid — swollen and firm — and bulge outwards, pulling the pore open.**

**2.** Explain: how a stoma closes.
**Answer: The guard cells lose water and become flaccid, so the pore closes.**
