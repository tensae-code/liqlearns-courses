# Stomata — opening by guard-cell turgor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about a stoma?
a) A stoma is a small pore in the leaf's surface, framed by two guard cells
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) A stoma is a small pore in the leaf's surface, framed by two guard cells**

**2.** What does this lesson say about turgid guard cells?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Guard cells that have gained water by osmosis and become swollen and firm
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Guard cells that have gained water by osmosis and become swollen and firm**

**3.** What does this lesson say about how a stoma opens?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Turgid guard cells bulge outwards, and their bulging pulls the stoma open
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Turgid guard cells bulge outwards, and their bulging pulls the stoma open**

**4.** What does this lesson say about gases at an open stoma?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Through the open stoma, carbon dioxide moves in and oxygen moves out
**Answer: d) Through the open stoma, carbon dioxide moves in and oxygen moves out**
