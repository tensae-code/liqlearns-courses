# Hands-on — Stomata — opening by guard-cell turgor

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Examine a real leaf's underside — with a hand lens if one is at hand — and look for the tiny pores; then draw the stoma twice, closed and open, and label the guard cells in each drawing.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a stoma drawn twice — closed between two flaccid guard cells, and open between two swollen, turgid guard cells bulging outwards — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
