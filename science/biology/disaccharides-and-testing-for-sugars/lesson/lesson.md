# Disaccharides — and testing for sugars

## The idea
Two sugars join, water leaves, and a disaccharide is made. Then the doing part: Benedict's test, read by its colour change.

## The key facts
**Two joined.** A disaccharide is two monosaccharides joined together.

**Water lost.** The joining loses a molecule of water.

**The three.** Maltose, lactose and sucrose are the disaccharides taught here.

**Benedict's test.** Benedict's solution, heated in a water bath, tests for reducing sugars and is read by its colour change.
