# Disaccharides — and testing for sugars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about two joined?
a) A disaccharide is two monosaccharides joined together  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) A disaccharide is two monosaccharides joined together**

**2.** What does this lesson say about water lost?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) The joining loses a molecule of water  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The joining loses a molecule of water**

**3.** What does this lesson say about the three?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Maltose, lactose and sucrose are the disaccharides taught here  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Maltose, lactose and sucrose are the disaccharides taught here**

**4.** What does this lesson say about benedict's test?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Benedict's solution, heated in a water bath, tests for reducing sugars and is read by its colour change
**Answer: d) Benedict's solution, heated in a water bath, tests for reducing sugars and is read by its colour change**
