# Selective breeding and Ethiopia's genetic wealth

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — selective breeding and ethiopia's genetic wealth. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Selective breeding chooses parents with desirable traits; its forms include inbreeding (mating close relatives toward purebred, stable lines) and cross/outbreeding routes; Ethiopia is presented as a world centre of crop genetic diversity with Africa's largest livestock population (2020 figures are given for cattle, sheep, goats, camels and chickens), whose farmers select by growth rate, body size and disease resistance from accumulated indigenous knowledge.
