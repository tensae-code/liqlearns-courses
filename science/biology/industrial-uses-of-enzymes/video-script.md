Enzyme catalysis, hundreds to thousands of times more efficient than inorganic catalysts, is applied across food, animal feed (better use of starch, protein and minerals; breaking anti-nutritional factors), textiles, paper, leather, detergents and pharmaceuticals; most industrial enzymes now come from microbial fermentation rather than animal or plant organs; labs compare reaction rates in liver, potato and carrot.
