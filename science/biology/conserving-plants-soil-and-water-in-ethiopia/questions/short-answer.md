# Conserving plants, soil and water in Ethiopia — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about conservation in ethiopia?**
**Answer: Ethiopia is a centre of crop genetic diversity with rich endemic plants (mekmeko, African juniper, cabbage tree, kosso pictured); plant conservation uses botanical gardens, invasive-species control, restoration and the national gene bank's seed preservation; soil and water, threatened by deforestation, poor land husbandry and waste, are conserved by mechanical measures (bunds, terraces, check dams, micro-basins) and biological measures together.**
