# Conserving plants, soil and water in Ethiopia

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — conserving plants, soil and water in ethiopia. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Ethiopia is a centre of crop genetic diversity with rich endemic plants (mekmeko, African juniper, cabbage tree, kosso pictured); plant conservation uses botanical gardens, invasive-species control, restoration and the national gene bank's seed preservation; soil and water, threatened by deforestation, poor land husbandry and waste, are conserved by mechanical measures (bunds, terraces, check dams, micro-basins) and biological measures together.
