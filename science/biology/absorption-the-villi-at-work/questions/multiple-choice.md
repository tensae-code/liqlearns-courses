# Absorption — the villi at work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about where it finishes?
a) Digestion finishes in the small intestine  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Digestion finishes in the small intestine**

**2.** What does this lesson say about villi?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Villi are finger-like folds of the small intestine's wall  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Villi are finger-like folds of the small intestine's wall**

**3.** What does this lesson say about surface?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) The villi multiply the surface for absorption  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) The villi multiply the surface for absorption**

**4.** What does this lesson say about lacteal and blood?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Each villus holds blood vessels and a lacteal to carry absorbed food away
**Answer: d) Each villus holds blood vessels and a lacteal to carry absorbed food away**
