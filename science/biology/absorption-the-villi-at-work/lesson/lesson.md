# Absorption — the villi at work

## The idea
Digestion finishes in the small intestine, and there the food must cross into the body. The villi — millions of tiny fingers — give it the surface to cross on.

[Picture 22251 — an intestinal wall magnified step by step down to one villus with its blood vessels and lacteal]

## The key facts
**Where it finishes.** Digestion finishes in the small intestine.

**Villi.** Villi are finger-like folds of the small intestine's wall.

**Surface.** The villi multiply the surface for absorption.

**Lacteal and blood.** Each villus holds blood vessels and a lacteal to carry absorbed food away.

**Little at a time.** Only a little chyme is released from the stomach at a time.
