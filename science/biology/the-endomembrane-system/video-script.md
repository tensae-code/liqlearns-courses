Today: The endomembrane system.

Remember this one thing: the endomembrane system is the cell's inner production line: the endoplasmic reticulum makes proteins and lipids, vesicles carry them to the Golgi apparatus for finishing and addressing, and lysosomes digest — membranes flowing into one another as one working system.
