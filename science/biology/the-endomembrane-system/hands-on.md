# Hands-on — The endomembrane system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: the endomembrane system is the cell's inner production line: the endoplasmic reticulum makes proteins and lipids, vesicles carry them to the Golgi apparatus for finishing and addressing, and lysosomes digest — membranes flowing into one another as one working system.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a factory line inside a cell: a folded ER ribbon, small vesicle bubbles travelling, a stacked Golgi, and parcels leaving labelled for their destinations. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
