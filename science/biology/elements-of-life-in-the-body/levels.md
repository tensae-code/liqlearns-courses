# Elements of life in the body — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (a living body is built from chemical elements — macroelements taken in bulk and microelements in traces — each with its own role in the life of organisms, from the carbon and oxygen of every molecule to the iron in blood) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
