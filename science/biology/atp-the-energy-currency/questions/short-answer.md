# ATP — the energy currency — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: ATP is the cell's energy currency: its terminal phosphate bond stores energy that is spent by transferring the phosphate onto the working protein itself, changing its shape — and the cell turns over a huge amount of ATP every day, about half a kilogram a minute in hard exercise. opened by Du Mu's Tang-dynasty firefly poem; the A—P~P~P structure with 30.54 kJ released per mole hydrolysed; half a kilogram of ATP turned over per minute in hard exercise; and the mechanism shown honestly — the terminal phosphate transferred onto the calcium pump itself, changing its shape (phosphorylation), which is how the "currency" is actually spent. No existing lesson is given to ATP.**
