# ATP — the energy currency — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (ATP is the cell's energy currency: its terminal phosphate bond stores energy that is spent by transferring the phosphate onto the working protein itself, changing its shape — and the cell turns over a huge amount of ATP every day, about half a kilogram a minute in hard exercise) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
