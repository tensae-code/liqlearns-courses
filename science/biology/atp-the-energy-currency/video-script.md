Today: ATP — the energy currency.

Remember this one thing: ATP is the cell's energy currency: its terminal phosphate bond stores energy that is spent by transferring the phosphate onto the working protein itself, changing its shape — and the cell turns over a huge amount of ATP every day, about half a kilogram a minute in hard exercise.
