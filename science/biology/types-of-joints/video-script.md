# Video script — Types of joints

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Where bones meet and movement happens, in named types: ball-and-socket (movement in all directions — shoulder, hip), hinge (one plane — elbow, knee), condyloid (movement without rotation — fingers, jaw) and pivot (one bone swivelling in a ring — in the forearm/neck); a project has pupils build a model musculoskeletal system from local materials and count its bones.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
