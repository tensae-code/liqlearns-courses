# Types of joints — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about joints?**
**Answer: Where bones meet and movement happens, in named types: ball-and-socket (movement in all directions — shoulder, hip), hinge (one plane — elbow, knee), condyloid (movement without rotation — fingers, jaw) and pivot (one bone swivelling in a ring — in the forearm/neck); a project has pupils build a model musculoskeletal system from local materials and count its bones.**
