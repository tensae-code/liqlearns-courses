# Types of joints — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1. ** Write two or three sentences explaining joints to a younger student. Use one example from the lesson.
**Model answer: Where bones meet and movement happens, in named types: ball-and-socket (movement in all directions — shoulder, hip), hinge (one plane — elbow, knee), condyloid (movement without rotation — fingers, jaw) and pivot (one bone swivelling in a ring — in the forearm/neck)**
