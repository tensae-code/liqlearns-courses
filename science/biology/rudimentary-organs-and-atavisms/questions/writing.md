# Rudimentary organs and atavisms — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences arguing from rudimentary organs that humans share ancestry with other mammals.
**Model answer: Humans carry structures that no longer do their old jobs — the coccyx where other mammals anchor a tail, ear-moving muscles, an appendix like the gut pouch of plant-eating relatives. Such reduced leftovers make sense as inheritance: they are the remodelled remains of working organs in ancestors we share with those mammals.**
