# Rudimentary organs and atavisms — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Reduced, jobless inherited structures are called _____ organs.**
**Answer: rudimentary (vestigial)**

**2. The fused tail bones in humans are the _____.**
**Answer: coccyx**

**3. An ancestral feature reappearing in one individual is an _____.**
**Answer: atavism**

**4. Rudimentary organs and atavisms are evidence for _____.**
**Answer: evolution**
