# Rudimentary organs and atavisms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is a rudimentary (vestigial) organ?**
a) An organ that works twice as hard  b) A reduced structure that no longer does its old job, inherited from ancestors  c) A brand-new organ  d) An organ found only in humans
**Answer: b) A reduced structure that no longer does its old job, inherited from ancestors**

**2. Which of these is a human rudimentary organ?**
a) The heart  b) The coccyx (tail bone)  c) The lung  d) The tongue
**Answer: b) The coccyx (tail bone)**

**3. What is an atavism?**
a) A disease of the bones  b) An ancestral feature that reappears in an individual  c) A fossil  d) A reduced organ in every member of a kind
**Answer: b) An ancestral feature that reappears in an individual**

**4. What are rudimentary organs and atavisms evidence for?**
a) That organs are designed separately  b) Evolution — descent with modification from ancestors  c) That fossils are unreliable  d) That humans have no ancestors
**Answer: b) Evolution — descent with modification from ancestors**
