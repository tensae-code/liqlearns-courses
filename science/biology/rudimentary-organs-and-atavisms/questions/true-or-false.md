# Rudimentary organs and atavisms — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. The appendix is a classic human rudimentary organ.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. An atavism is present in every member of a kind.**
**Answer: False** — an atavism appears in a single individual; a rudimentary organ is the one carried by the kind.

**3. Flightless birds' shrunk wings are rudimentary organs.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. Rudimentary organs are best explained as fresh creations with no history.**
**Answer: False** — they are explained by descent — inherited, reduced structures of ancestors.
