# Rudimentary organs and atavisms — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the difference between a rudimentary organ and an atavism.
**Answer: A rudimentary organ is a reduced structure carried by the whole kind — like the human appendix or coccyx — left over from a working organ in ancestors. An atavism is an ancestral feature that reappears in one individual only — like a baby born with a short tail.**

**2.** Explain: North Macedonia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: North Macedonia's Biology I gives rudimentary organs and atavisms their own evidence lesson (Theme 4.6, printed p. 165), inside the theme on foundations of organic evolution, alongside fossils, transitional forms and comparative anatomy and embryology.**
