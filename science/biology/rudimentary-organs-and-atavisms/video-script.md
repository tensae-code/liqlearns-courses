Today: Rudimentary organs and atavisms.

Remember this one thing: Rudimentary (vestigial) organs are structures reduced and without their old function — leftovers of organs that worked in ancestors; atavisms are ancestral features that reappear in an individual — both are read as evidence of evolution.

Your body keeps some structures it no longer uses: the appendix, the tail bone, the wisdom teeth. Reduced and jobless, they are called rudimentary, or vestigial, organs.

And sometimes an old feature wakes up in one individual — a short tail, an extra toe on a horse. That is an atavism. Leftovers and reawakenings together are evidence: bodies are inherited, remodelled from ancestors.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
