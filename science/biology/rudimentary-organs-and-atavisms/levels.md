# Rudimentary organs and atavisms — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Rudimentary (vestigial) organs are structures reduced and without their old function — leftovers of organs that worked in ancestors; atavisms are ancestral features that reappear in an individual — both are read as evidence of evolution.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a fresh example from the learner's own surroundings and saying why it fits, without looking.
