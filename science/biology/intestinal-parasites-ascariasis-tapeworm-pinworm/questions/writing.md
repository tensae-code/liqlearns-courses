# Intestinal parasites — ascariasis, tapeworm and pinworm — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why a parasite that usually causes no symptoms is still worth teaching.
**Model answer: Ascariasis usually shows nothing, so carriers feel well while the worms live in the gut and eggs spread. But heavy infections bring pain, lost appetite and weight, diarrhoea and even a blocked intestine. Teaching the silent case is what makes people cook meat properly, wash hands and treat infections before they turn heavy.**
