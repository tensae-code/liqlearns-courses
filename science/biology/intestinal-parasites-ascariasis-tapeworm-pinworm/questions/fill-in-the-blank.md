# Intestinal parasites — ascariasis, tapeworm and pinworm — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The roundworm of ascariasis is called _____.**
**Answer: Ascaris**

**2. The pork tapeworm is Taenia _____.**
**Answer: solium**

**3. The beef tapeworm is Taenia _____.**
**Answer: saginata**

**4. Pinworm eggs can travel from anus to _____ after scratching.**
**Answer: mouth**
