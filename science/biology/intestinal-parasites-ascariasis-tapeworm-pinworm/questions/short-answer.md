# Intestinal parasites — ascariasis, tapeworm and pinworm — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** How are the two Taenia tapeworms told apart in the module?
**Answer: By the meat that carries them: Taenia solium comes from raw or undercooked pork, and Taenia saginata from raw or undercooked beef.**

**2.** Explain Mozambique's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Mozambique's PESD I Módulo 2 de Biologia (2023), Lesson 10, works the food-borne parasitoses one by one: ascariasis (Ascaris — usually asymptomatic; abdominal pain, appetite/weight loss, nausea, diarrhoea, intestinal obstruction), the tapeworms Taenia solium from raw/undercooked pork and T. saginata from beef, and pinworm-type transmission in three forms including direct anus-to-mouth transfer after scratching.**
