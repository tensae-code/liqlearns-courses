# Intestinal parasites — ascariasis, tapeworm and pinworm — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Ascariasis is usually…**
a) fatal within days  b) asymptomatic  c) marked by a rash  d) spread by mosquitoes
**Answer: b) asymptomatic**

**2. Taenia solium is acquired from…**
a) raw or undercooked beef  b) raw or undercooked pork  c) unwashed fruit only  d) contaminated water only
**Answer: b) raw or undercooked pork**

**3. Taenia saginata comes from…**
a) pork  b) chicken  c) beef  d) fish
**Answer: c) beef**

**4. The pinworm's most direct transmission route is…**
a) through the skin  b) anus to mouth after scratching  c) by mosquito bite  d) through the lungs
**Answer: b) anus to mouth after scratching**
