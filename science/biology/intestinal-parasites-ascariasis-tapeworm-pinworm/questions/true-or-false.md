# Intestinal parasites — ascariasis, tapeworm and pinworm — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Ascariasis can cause intestinal obstruction.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Taenia solium and T. saginata come from the same meat.**
**Answer: False** — solium from pork, saginata from beef.

**3. Pinworm transmission is only through food cooked by others.**
**Answer: False** — it includes the direct anus-to-mouth route after scratching.

**4. Loss of appetite and weight are among ascariasis's possible signs.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
