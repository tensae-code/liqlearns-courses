# Hands-on — Intestinal parasites — ascariasis, tapeworm and pinworm

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Wash your hands the thorough way — palms, backs, between fingers, under the nails — and say which of the lesson's parasites this one habit attacks, and at which step: the pinworm's anus-to-mouth route, broken at the hand.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — three panels: a long pale Ascaris roundworm coiled in a gut outline; a tapeworm's flat ribbon body with a line to a pig for Taenia solium and to a cow for T. saginata; and a small pinworm beside a hand with an arrow marked "scratching — hand to mouth" — label its parts, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
