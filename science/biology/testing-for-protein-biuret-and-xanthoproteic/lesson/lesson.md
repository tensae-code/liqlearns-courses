## The idea
Two laboratory tests name protein in a sample by colour. This doing-lesson runs both, after fixing how a protein's structure is built level by level from its chain.

[Picture 22239 — a Biuret colour change from blue to purple beside a test tube rack]

## The key facts
**Biuret test.** The Biuret test identifies protein, changing from blue to purple.

**Xanthoproteic test.** The xanthoproteic test is the second laboratory test for protein.

**Structure levels.** Protein structure is worked from the chain up through its levels.

**Two kinds.** Globular proteins such as haemoglobin are set against fibrous ones such as collagen.
