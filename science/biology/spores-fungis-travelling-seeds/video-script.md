Today: Spores — fungi's travelling seeds.

Remember this one thing: most fungi reproduce asexually by spores formed in a spore-sac at a hypha tip.

Spores form in a swelling spore-sac (sporangium) at a hypha tip.

Penicillium and Mucor on decaying food are pictured examples.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
