# Spores — fungi's travelling seeds

## Do it first

## The idea
Spores travel on air currents and, landing somewhere suitable, grow new hyphae — which is why sporulation colonises new habitats so well.

## The key facts
**Sporangium.** Spores form in a swelling spore-sac (sporangium) at a hypha tip.

**Pictured fungi.** Penicillium and Mucor on decaying food are pictured examples.

**Travel.** Spores travel on air currents.

**Growing.** Landing somewhere suitable, a spore grows new hyphae and a mycelium.
