# Spores — fungi's travelling seeds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about sporangium?
a) Spores form in a swelling spore-sac (sporangium) at a hypha tip
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Spores form in a swelling spore-sac (sporangium) at a hypha tip**

**2.** What does this lesson say about pictured fungi?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Penicillium and Mucor on decaying food are pictured examples
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Penicillium and Mucor on decaying food are pictured examples**

**3.** What does this lesson say about travel?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Spores travel on air currents
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Spores travel on air currents**

**4.** What does this lesson say about growing?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Landing somewhere suitable, a spore grows new hyphae and a mycelium
**Answer: d) Landing somewhere suitable, a spore grows new hyphae and a mycelium**
