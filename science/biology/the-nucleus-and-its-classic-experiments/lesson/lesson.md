# The nucleus — and its classic experiments

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The nucleus — and its classic experiments. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
The nucleus controls the cell, and its control role is proved by four classic experiments set side by side — axolotl nuclear transplant, salamander egg constriction, amoeba cutting and Acetabularia grafting — opened by a cloned-cow case, with chromatin against chromosome closing the account. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from China's People's Education Press biology textbooks, which teach it as a named topic.
