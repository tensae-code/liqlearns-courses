# The nucleus — and its classic experiments — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. its control role is proved by four classic experiments set side by side (axolotl nuclear transplant, salamander egg constriction, amoeba cutting, Acetabularia grafting), opened by a cloned-cow case; chromatin against chromosome closes it. No existing lesson is given to the nucleus itself.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in China's People's Education Press biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (普通高中教科书《生物学》必修1 分子与细胞 (Senior High Biology, Compulsory Book 1: Molecules and Cells, People's Education Press, 2019-standard edition)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with the nucleus — and its classic experiments.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is the nucleus — and its classic experiments.
