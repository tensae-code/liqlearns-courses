# Video script — Learned behaviour — habituation, conditioning, insight

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Learning changes behaviour through experience: habituation (stopping responding to a repeated harmless stimulus), classical conditioning (linking a new stimulus to an existing response), operant conditioning (reinforcers raise, punishers lower the chance a behaviour repeats), sensitisation (a response growing with repeated stimulation), latent learning and insight learning — solving a problem from past experience and reasoning.

The guide teaches classical conditioning through Pavlov's experiment in full: the accidental discovery while studying dogs' gastric systems, and the three stages — before conditioning (food alone causes salivation), during conditioning (bell paired with food over trials) and after conditioning (the bell alone, now a conditioned stimulus, produces the conditioned response).

Operant conditioning is taught through Skinner's hungry-rat experiment — the rat accidentally presses a lever, a food pellet drops, and it quickly learns to press deliberately — with Skinner's three response types (neutral operants, reinforcers positive and negative, punishers) and punishment explained both as applying an unpleasant stimulus and as removing a rewarding one (deducting pocket money is the human example).

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
