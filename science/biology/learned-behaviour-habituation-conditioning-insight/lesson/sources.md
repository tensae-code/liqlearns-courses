Ch. 1 builds the internal environment from a real blood-biochemistry report and extends homeostasis to every level of life. Ch. 4 is immunity: the system as an army with barracks and battlefields, the 2015 discovery of brain lymphatics, influenza and cancer immunotherapy features, HIV with China's free-treatment policy, and transplantation with China's computerised organ-allocation system. Ch. 5 turns to plants: the auxin discovery chain from the Darwins to Went, the other hormones opened by a Song–Yuan dynasty text on ripening persimmons, growth regulators regulated as pesticides, and environmental control ending with flower-forcing for festivals and the 2008 Olympics.
