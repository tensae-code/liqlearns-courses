# Epidemic curves and healthcare capacity — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the capacity line.
**Answer: The flat horizontal line is healthcare capacity — how many patients the hospitals can treat at the same time; keeping the curve's peak under it is the point of protective measures.**

**2.** Explain: Sweden's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Sweden teaches it as one touchable tactile graph (Platta ut kurvan — flatten the curve) on a raised-line sheet: the learner feels the two curves and the capacity line by touch and reads the point of protective measures off the picture.**
