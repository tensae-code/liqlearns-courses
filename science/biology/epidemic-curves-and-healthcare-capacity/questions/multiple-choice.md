# Epidemic curves and healthcare capacity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does an epidemic curve plot?
a) Cases against time — the number of sick people over days and weeks
b) Temperature against rainfall
c) It plays no part in this topic at all
d) It happens only at night and nowhere else
**Answer: a) Cases against time — the number of sick people over days and weeks**

**2.** What happens without protective measures?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Cases rise fast and the curve's high peak goes above the healthcare-capacity line
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Cases rise fast and the curve's high peak goes above the healthcare-capacity line**

**3.** What do protective measures do to the curve?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) They flatten it — a lower, slower peak that stays below the capacity line
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) They flatten it — a lower, slower peak that stays below the capacity line**

**4.** What is the flat horizontal line on the graph?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Healthcare capacity — how many patients hospitals can treat at the same time
**Answer: d) Healthcare capacity — how many patients hospitals can treat at the same time**
