# Hands-on — Epidemic curves and healthcare capacity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Draw the graph's two axes on a big sheet of paper: cases going up, time going across. Draw the flat healthcare-capacity line, then draw the two curves — the tall fast one without protective measures and the low flat one with them. Run a finger along each curve, as on Sweden's raised-line sheet, and say where each peak sits against the line.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory, label it (cases, time, the capacity line, the two curves), and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
