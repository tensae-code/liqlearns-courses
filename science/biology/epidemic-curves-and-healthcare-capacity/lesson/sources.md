An SPSM (Specialpedagogiska skolmyndigheten — Swedish Agency for Special Needs Education and Schools) tactile print original ("tryckoriginal … för egen utskrift" = print original for own printing): a raised-line / swell-paper teaching sheet for visually impaired learners, not a prose textbook. The sheet set is a tactile graph, cases (antal fall) against time (tid), with two curves — with protective measures (med skyddsåtgärder) and without (utan skyddsåtgärder) — against the horizontal line of healthcare capacity (sjukvårdens kapacitet). Sweden's way: the public-health "flatten the curve" idea taught as one touchable graph, so the point of protective measures is read off the picture: keep the peak under the capacity line.
