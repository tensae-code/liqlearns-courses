# How populations are spread out

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — how populations are spread out. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Individuals are spread through their area in three patterns — uniform (evenly spaced), random, and clumped — each figured, and density worked examples are calculated from quadrat tables for three plant species.
