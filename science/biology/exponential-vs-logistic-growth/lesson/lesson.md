# Exponential vs logistic growth

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — exponential vs logistic growth. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Population growth rate r can be positive, zero or negative; exponential growth keeps the same per-capita rate regardless of size (a J-curve, worked generation by generation, doubling 100 to 102,400 in ten generations at r = 1); logistic growth slows as the population nears carrying capacity K (an S-curve, worked to a ceiling of 250), because resources run short.
