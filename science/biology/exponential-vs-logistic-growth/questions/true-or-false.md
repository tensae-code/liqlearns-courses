# Exponential vs logistic growth — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Population growth rate r can be positive, zero or negative; exponential growth keeps the same per-capita rate regardless of size (a J-curve, worked generation by generation, doubling 100 to 102,400 in ten generations at r = 1); logistic growth slows as the population nears carrying capacity K (an S-curve, worked to a ceiling of 250), because resources run short.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
