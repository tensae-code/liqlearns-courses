# Exponential vs logistic growth — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about exponential and logistic growth?**
**Answer: Population growth rate r can be positive, zero or negative; exponential growth keeps the same per-capita rate regardless of size (a J-curve, worked generation by generation, doubling 100 to 102,400 in ten generations at r = 1); logistic growth slows as the population nears carrying capacity K (an S-curve, worked to a ceiling of 250), because resources run short.**
