# Exponential vs logistic growth — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. ** _____ rate r can be positive, zero or negative; exponential growth keeps the same per-capita rate regardless of size (a J-curve, worked generation by generation, doubling 100 to 102,400 in ten generations at r = 1); logistic growth slows as the population nears carrying capacity K (an S-curve, worked to a ceiling of 250), because resources run short.
