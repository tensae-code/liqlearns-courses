# Desertification

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Desertification. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Desertification is fertile land turning to desert under overgrazing, over-cultivation, tree loss and a drying climate — a named Mediterranean problem in Greece's Grade 11 book, fought by careful grazing, tree planting and care of water. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from Greece's Gymnasio and Lykeio biology books, which teach it as a named topic.
