# Hands-on — Desertification

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: desertification is fertile land turning to desert under overgrazing, over-cultivation, tree loss and a drying climate — a named Mediterranean problem in Greece's Grade 11 book, fought by careful grazing, tree planting and care of water.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a green field at the left cracking and paling step by step toward sand and a lone dead tree at the right, with a fence and young planted trees holding the middle. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
