Today: Desertification.

Remember this one thing: desertification is fertile land turning to desert under overgrazing, over-cultivation, tree loss and a drying climate — a named Mediterranean problem in Greece's Grade 11 book, fought by careful grazing, tree planting and care of water.

desertification (ερημοποίηση) as a named environmental problem, a Mediterranean/Greek priority topic

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
