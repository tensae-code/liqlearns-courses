# Hermaphroditism in animals — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: hermaphroditism, and where Iraq's Grade 12 book teaches it.
**Answer: A hermaphrodite carries male and female reproductive organs in one individual. Iraq's Grade 12 Biology book teaches hermaphroditic reproduction (التكاثر الخنثي) as the closing section of its reproduction chapter (Ch. 3 §11-3, printed p. 137), straight after parthenogenesis (§10-3, printed p. 135).**
