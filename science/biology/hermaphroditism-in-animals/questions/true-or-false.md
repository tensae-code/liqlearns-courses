# Hermaphroditism in animals — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Hermaphroditic reproduction has its own numbered section in Iraq's Grade 12 reproduction chapter.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A hermaphrodite carries only one sex's organs.
**Answer: False** — A hermaphrodite carries both male and female reproductive organs in one body.

**3.** The hermaphroditism section comes before the parthenogenesis section.
**Answer: False** — It closes the chapter, straight after parthenogenesis (§10-3).
