# Hermaphroditism in animals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A hermaphrodite is an animal that…
a) has no reproductive organs
b) carries both male and female reproductive organs in one body
c) reproduces only by fission
d) lays no eggs
**Answer: b) carries both male and female reproductive organs in one body**

**2.** In Iraq's Grade 12 book, hermaphroditic reproduction stands…
a) in the cell chapter
b) as the closing section of the reproduction chapter, after parthenogenesis
c) in the heredity chapter
d) only in the glossary
**Answer: b) as the closing section of the reproduction chapter, after parthenogenesis**

**3.** Parthenogenesis, the section just before it, is…
a) young from unfertilised eggs
b) reproduction in viruses
c) binary fission
d) pollination
**Answer: a) young from unfertilised eggs**
