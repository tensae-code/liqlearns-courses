# Hands-on — Hermaphroditism in animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
On paper, draw the reproduction chapter's closing pair as two boxes: parthenogenesis — young from an unfertilised egg — and hermaphroditism — both sexes in one body. Under each box write, from memory, the one line that defines it, then check against the lesson.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a simple diagram of one animal body carrying both a male and a female reproductive organ, the two labelled side by side in the single body — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
