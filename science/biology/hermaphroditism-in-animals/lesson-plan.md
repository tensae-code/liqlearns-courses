# Lesson plan — Hermaphroditism in animals

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Hermaphroditism in animals
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — appended to Unit 16 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a hermaphrodite is an animal that carries both male and female reproductive organs in one body — and Iraq's Grade 12 Biology capstone teaches hermaphroditic reproduction (التكاثر الخنثي) as the closing section of its reproduction chapter, straight after parthenogenesis.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own as a single-skill lesson built from Iraq's Grade 12 Biology textbook, as cited in lesson/sources.md. Related lessons are recommended first where they help, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

2. Explain each key fact (Both sexes, one body; A kind of reproduction; After parthenogenesis; Iraq's way) in their own words.
3. Explain Iraq's way as the placements describe it.
