# Hermaphroditism in animals — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (a hermaphrodite is an animal that carries both male and female reproductive organs in one body — and Iraq's Grade 12 Biology capstone teaches hermaphroditic reproduction (التكاثر الخنثي) as the closing section of its reproduction chapter, straight after parthenogenesis) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: applying the lesson to a new example from the learner's own surroundings, and explaining Iraq's way of teaching it, without looking.
