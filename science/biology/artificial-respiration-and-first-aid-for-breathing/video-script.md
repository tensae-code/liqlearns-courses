Today: Artificial respiration — first aid for breathing.

Remember this one thing: artificial respiration is rescue breathing: when a person's own breathing has stopped, another person breathes air into their lungs in a steady rhythm to keep oxygen reaching the blood until help comes or their own breathing returns.
