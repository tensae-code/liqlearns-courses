# Artificial respiration — first aid for breathing — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (artificial respiration is rescue breathing: when a person's own breathing has stopped, another person breathes air into their lungs in a steady rhythm to keep oxygen reaching the blood until help comes or their own breathing returns) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
