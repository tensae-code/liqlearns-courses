# Artificial respiration — first aid for breathing — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: artificial respiration is rescue breathing: when a person's own breathing has stopped, another person breathes air into their lungs in a steady rhythm to keep oxygen reaching the blood until help comes or their own breathing returns. artificial respiration (rescue breathing) as a practical skill**

**2.** Explain Greece's way of teaching this.
**Answer: artificial respiration (rescue breathing) as a practical skill**
