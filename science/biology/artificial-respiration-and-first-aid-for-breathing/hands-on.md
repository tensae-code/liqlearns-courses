# Hands-on — Artificial respiration — first aid for breathing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: artificial respiration is rescue breathing: when a person's own breathing has stopped, another person breathes air into their lungs in a steady rhythm to keep oxygen reaching the blood until help comes or their own breathing returns.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a trained helper kneeling beside a person lying flat with head tilted back, giving rescue breaths, with a steady rhythm marked beside them. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
