# Trophic levels — the steps of a food chain — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about trophic level?
a) Each feeding step in a chain is a trophic level  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Each feeding step in a chain is a trophic level**

**2.** What does this lesson say about a real chain?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Grass, grasshopper, lizard, snake and hawk form a worked chain  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Grass, grasshopper, lizard, snake and hawk form a worked chain**

**3.** What does this lesson say about field work?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Chains are built from real organisms, including on a field visit to the school compound  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Chains are built from real organisms, including on a field visit to the school compound**

**4.** What does this lesson say about steps in order?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Trophic levels are numbered from the producers upward
**Answer: d) Trophic levels are numbered from the producers upward**
