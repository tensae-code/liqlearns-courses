# Trophic levels — the steps of a food chain

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Trophic levels — the steps of a food chain. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
A food chain is a staircase. Each step — each feeding level — is a trophic level, and real chains from the school compound show the steps.

[Picture 22268 — a four-step staircase of organisms, one per trophic level]

## The key facts
**Trophic level.** Each feeding step in a chain is a trophic level.

**A real chain.** Grass, grasshopper, lizard, snake and hawk form a worked chain.

**Field work.** Chains are built from real organisms, including on a field visit to the school compound.

**Steps in order.** Trophic levels are numbered from the producers upward.
