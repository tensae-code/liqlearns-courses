Today: Parts of the light microscope.

Remember this one thing: each part of the compound light microscope has a job, and total magnification is eyepiece times objective.

The eyepiece (ocular) usually magnifies 10 times and sits in the eyepiece tube.

The rotating nosepiece carries objective lenses of about 4 to 100 times.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
