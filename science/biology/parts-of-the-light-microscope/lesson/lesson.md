# Parts of the light microscope

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Parts of the light microscope. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Hold the names part by part: some parts hold and support, others magnify and form the image.

[Picture 29207 — a full microscope diagram with every part labelled]

## The key facts
**Eyepiece.** The eyepiece (ocular) usually magnifies 10 times and sits in the eyepiece tube.

**Objectives.** The rotating nosepiece carries objective lenses of about 4 to 100 times.

**Focusing.** The coarse adjustment knob focuses roughly and the fine adjustment knob sharpens the image; the stage holds the slide.

**Total magnification.** Total magnification is eyepiece magnification multiplied by objective magnification.

## Say it to your companion
