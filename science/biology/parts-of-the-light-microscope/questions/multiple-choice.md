# Parts of the light microscope — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about eyepiece?
a) The eyepiece (ocular) usually magnifies 10 times and sits in the eyepiece tube
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The eyepiece (ocular) usually magnifies 10 times and sits in the eyepiece tube**

**2.** What does this lesson say about objectives?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) The rotating nosepiece carries objective lenses of about 4 to 100 times
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The rotating nosepiece carries objective lenses of about 4 to 100 times**

**3.** What does this lesson say about focusing?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) The coarse adjustment knob focuses roughly and the fine adjustment knob sharpens the image; the stage holds the slide
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) The coarse adjustment knob focuses roughly and the fine adjustment knob sharpens the image; the stage holds the slide**

**4.** What does this lesson say about total magnification?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Total magnification is eyepiece magnification multiplied by objective magnification
**Answer: d) Total magnification is eyepiece magnification multiplied by objective magnification**
