# Breathing in, breathing out — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about inspiration?
a) In inspiration the rib cage moves up and the diaphragm flattens  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) In inspiration the rib cage moves up and the diaphragm flattens**

**2.** What does this lesson say about expiration?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) In expiration the movements reverse — ribs down, diaphragm domed  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) In expiration the movements reverse — ribs down, diaphragm domed**

**3.** What does this lesson say about air compared?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Atmospheric and exhaled air are compared percentage by percentage  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Atmospheric and exhaled air are compared percentage by percentage**

**4.** What does this lesson say about feel it?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Placing fingertips between the ribs, you can feel the ribs move as you breathe
**Answer: d) Placing fingertips between the ribs, you can feel the ribs move as you breathe**
