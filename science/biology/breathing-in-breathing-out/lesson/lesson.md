# Breathing in, breathing out

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Breathing in, breathing out. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Breathing is movement you can feel: ribs up, diaphragm flat — air in. Reverse it — air out. Feel your own ribs do it, then compare the air going in with the air coming out.

## The key facts
**Inspiration.** In inspiration the rib cage moves up and the diaphragm flattens.

**Expiration.** In expiration the movements reverse — ribs down, diaphragm domed.

**Air compared.** Atmospheric and exhaled air are compared percentage by percentage.

**Feel it.** Placing fingertips between the ribs, you can feel the ribs move as you breathe.
