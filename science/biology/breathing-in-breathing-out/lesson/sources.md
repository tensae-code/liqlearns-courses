**Book 8:** Hyvinvointia mittaamalla — Työkortit ja oppimateriaali, Yläkoulun 8.–9. luokka (Wellbeing by Measuring — work cards and learning material, grades 8–9) — Finland. Grade: filed grade-07; the cover itself states grades 8–9 (ages 14–16) — Kind: student work-card pack / learning material (cross-curricular: health education, biology, physics, PE, psychology), adapted from University of Jyväskylä material (authors Moilanen, Koponen, Moilanen & Nevanpää) — NOT a textbook, as verified — Repo path: grade-07/gov/finland/biology/student/finland-g07-aoe5798-hyvinvointia-mittaamalla-ylakoulu.pdf — Byte size: 363,504 (matches record) — PDF pages: 56 — Printed-page offset: 0 (printed folios “Sivu 1–56”). Verdict: COMPLETE (all 56 pages read, cover to closing summary page). Structural summary: Fifteen measurement work cards, each following a Measure → Reflect → Learn (theory) → Try cycle (Kolb's experiential model, stated openly). Pupils measure their OWN bodies with wearables and instruments — grip dynamometer, Wattbike, activity tracker, sleep tracker, EEG headband, HRV/stress watch, Cooper/beep test, spirometer, PEF meter, countermovement jump, blood-glucose meter, blood-pressure cuff, a 5/9 recovery test, Finland's national Move! fitness battery, and smartwatch pulse oximetry — then read a theory section explaining the biology behind each number. Privacy rules are explicit: measurements are voluntary, results are never compared competitively, data is not collected centrally. Finland's way: human biology taught as self-measurement and data interpretation, not as diagrams to memorise.
