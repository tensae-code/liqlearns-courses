# Video script — Breathing in, breathing out

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Breathing in, breathing out.

Remember this one thing: inspiration lifts the ribs and flattens the diaphragm; expiration reverses both — and exhaled air differs from atmospheric air percentage by percentage

Inspiration: In inspiration the rib cage moves up and the diaphragm flattens.

Expiration: In expiration the movements reverse — ribs down, diaphragm domed.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
