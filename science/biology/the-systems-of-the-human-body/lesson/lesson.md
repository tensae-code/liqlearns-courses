# The systems of the human body

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The systems of the human body. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
The book's largest unit tours the body system by system, in the order food and blood move through it. This lesson is the map for the whole tour.

[Picture 22246 — a body outline with the five systems in five colours]

## The key facts
**The tour.** The unit tours digestive, circulatory, breathing, excretory and immune systems.

**The order.** The systems are taught in the order food and blood move through the body.

**Starting point.** The unit opens from what pupils already know about digestion.

**Woven through.** The immune system is woven through the other systems' lessons.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words: the human body unit tours the digestive, circulatory, breathing, excretory and immune systems in the order food and blood move Use each key fact once. Then let your companion explain it back to you.
