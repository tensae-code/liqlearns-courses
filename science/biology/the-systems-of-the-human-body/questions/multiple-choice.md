# The systems of the human body — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about the tour?
a) The unit tours digestive, circulatory, breathing, excretory and immune systems  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The unit tours digestive, circulatory, breathing, excretory and immune systems**

**2.** What does this lesson say about the order?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) The systems are taught in the order food and blood move through the body  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The systems are taught in the order food and blood move through the body**

**3.** What does this lesson say about starting point?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) The unit opens from what pupils already know about digestion  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) The unit opens from what pupils already know about digestion**

**4.** What does this lesson say about woven through?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) The immune system is woven through the other systems' lessons
**Answer: d) The immune system is woven through the other systems' lessons**
