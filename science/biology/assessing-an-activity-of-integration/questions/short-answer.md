# Assessing an activity of integration — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: what is an activity of integration, and how is it scored in Uganda?
**Answer: An activity of integration is the closing task of a chapter in Uganda's Lower Secondary Curriculum: one real-life task that makes the learner use the whole chapter at once. The Teacher's Guide scores it on an evaluation grid of four criteria — Accuracy (is it right?), Coherence (does it hold together?), Relevance (does it answer the real-life situation?) and Excellence (does it rise above the merely correct?).**

**2.** Explain: why does this lesson count for no country?
**Answer: Its only source is a teacher's guide — the Senior One Teacher's Guide — which is assessment machinery for teachers, not a pupil textbook. By the course's honesty rule a teacher's guide is cited as evidence only, outside the acceptance count, so the lesson is taught in 0 countries and Uganda is named here and counted nowhere.**
