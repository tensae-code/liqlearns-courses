Today: Hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the urinary system.

Remember this one thing: the urinary system is kept healthy by hygiene, and its dysfunctions and diseases are recognised early — kidney-stone (calculi) pain is often confused with radiculitis, which delays treatment.

Moldova's Grade 11 book (§40 Hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the urinary system) teaches: kidney-stone (calculi) pain often confused with radiculitis, delaying treatment. (printed p. 131).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
