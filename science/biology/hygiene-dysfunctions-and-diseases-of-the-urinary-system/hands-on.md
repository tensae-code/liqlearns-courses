# Hands-on — Hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the urinary system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: the urinary system is kept healthy by hygiene, and its dysfunctions and diseases are recognised early — kidney-stone (calculi) pain is often confused with radiculitis, which delays treatment.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: the kidneys, ureters and bladder with a kidney stone marked, beside a person holding their back in pain. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
