# Hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the urinary system — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: the urinary system is kept healthy by hygiene, and its dysfunctions and diseases are recognised early — kidney-stone (calculi) pain is often confused with radiculitis, which delays treatment. Moldova's Grade 11 book (§40 Hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the urinary system) teaches: kidney-stone (calculi) pain often confused with radiculitis, delaying treatment. (printed p. 131).**

**2.** Explain Moldova's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Moldova's Grade 11 book gives the urinary system its own hygiene section, with the clinical warning that kidney-stone (calculi) pain is often confused with radiculitis, delaying treatment.**
