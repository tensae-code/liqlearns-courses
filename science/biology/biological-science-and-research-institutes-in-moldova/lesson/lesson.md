# Biological science and research institutes in Moldova

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Biological science and research institutes in Moldova. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Biological science in the Republic of Moldova is carried on in Academy institutes and university centres — microbiology, the genetics and plant-protection institute, the zoology institute and the National Botanical Garden — each with named research projects. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from Moldova's Ministry of Education biology textbooks, which teach it as a named topic.

[Picture — four Moldovan research scenes in a row: microbes degrading plastic, drought-resistant lavender and sage varieties, a zoology fauna study, and in-vitro plant multiplication at a botanical garden]

## The key facts
**Tema 1.3 Development of the biological sciences in the Republic of Moldova.** Moldova's Grade 10 book (Tema 1.3 Development of the biological sciences in the Republic of Moldova) teaches: Academy institutes and university centres: microbiology project on biodegradation of non-recyclable plastic, genetics/plant-protection institute creating drought-resistant medicinal and aromatic plant varieties (clary sage, lavender, oregano, hyssop, chia, sesame), zoology institute fauna forecasts, National Botanical Garden in-vitro multiplication. (printed p. 9).

**Moldova's way.** Moldova's Grade 10 book devotes a full theme to biological science in the Republic of Moldova: a microbiology project on biodegradation of non-recyclable plastic, the genetics/plant-protection institute creating drought-resistant medicinal and aromatic plant varieties (clary sage, lavender, oregano, hyssop, chia, sesame), zoology institute fauna forecasts, and National Botanical Garden in-vitro multiplication.
