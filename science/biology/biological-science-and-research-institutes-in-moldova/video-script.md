# Video script — Biological science and research institutes in Moldova

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: Biological science and research institutes in Moldova.

Remember this one thing: biological science in the Republic of Moldova is carried on in Academy institutes and university centres — microbiology, the genetics and plant-protection institute, the zoology institute and the National Botanical Garden — each with named research projects.

Moldova's Grade 10 book (Tema 1.3 Development of the biological sciences in the Republic of Moldova) teaches: Academy institutes and university centres: microbiology project on biodegradation of non-recyclable plastic, genetics/plant-protection institute creating drought-resistant medicinal and aromatic plant varieties (clary sage, lavender, oregano, hyssop, chia, sesame), zoology institute fauna forecasts, National Botanical Garden in-vitro multiplication. (printed p. 9).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
