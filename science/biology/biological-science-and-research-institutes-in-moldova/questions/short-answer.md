# Biological science and research institutes in Moldova — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: biological science in the Republic of Moldova is carried on in Academy institutes and university centres — microbiology, the genetics and plant-protection institute, the zoology institute and the National Botanical Garden — each with named research projects. Moldova's Grade 10 book (Tema 1.3 Development of the biological sciences in the Republic of Moldova) teaches: Academy institutes and university centres: microbiology project on biodegradation of non-recyclable plastic, genetics/plant-protection institute creating drought-resistant medicinal and aromatic plant varieties (clary sage, lavender, oregano, hyssop, chia, sesame), zoology institute fauna forecasts, National Botanical Garden in-vitro multiplication. (printed p. 9).**

**2.** Explain Moldova's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Moldova's Grade 10 book devotes a full theme to biological science in the Republic of Moldova: a microbiology project on biodegradation of non-recyclable plastic, the genetics/plant-protection institute creating drought-resistant medicinal and aromatic plant varieties (clary sage, lavender, oregano, hyssop, chia, sesame), zoology institute fauna forecasts, and National Botanical Garden in-vitro multiplication.**
