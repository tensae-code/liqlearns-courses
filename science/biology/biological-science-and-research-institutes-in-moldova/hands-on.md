# Hands-on — Biological science and research institutes in Moldova

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: biological science in the Republic of Moldova is carried on in Academy institutes and university centres — microbiology, the genetics and plant-protection institute, the zoology institute and the National Botanical Garden — each with named research projects.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: four Moldovan research scenes in a row: microbes degrading plastic, drought-resistant lavender and sage varieties, a zoology fauna study, and in-vitro plant multiplication at a botanical garden. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
