# Primary and secondary growth in plants — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Lengthwise growth at the tips is _____ growth.**
**Answer: primary**

**2. The middle zone of a root tip, where cells stretch, is the zone of _____.**
**Answer: elongation**

**3. The wood of a tree is secondary _____.**
**Answer: xylem**

**4. Bark breathes through pores called _____.**
**Answer: lenticels**
