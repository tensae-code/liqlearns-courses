# Primary and secondary growth in plants — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining how one plant can keep growing in length and in thickness at the same time without the two growths interfering.
**Model answer: The two growths work in different places by different meristems: the apical meristems at the root and shoot tips add length cell by cell, while the cambium cylinders along the older stem add thickness around the outside of the wood. Tip and side never compete for the same tissue, so the tree climbs and thickens in the same season.**
