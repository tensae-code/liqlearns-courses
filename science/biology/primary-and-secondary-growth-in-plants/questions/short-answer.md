# Primary and secondary growth in plants — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Describe primary growth and how the ink-marking practical proves where it happens.
**Answer: Primary growth at the apical meristems lengthens root and shoot through the zones of division, elongation and differentiation; ink marks made at even intervals along a root spread apart only across the growing zone, and percentage growth is calculated from their spacing (Ch. 4, printed p. 143).**

**2.** Explain Kenya's way of teaching growth in plants.
**Answer: Kenya separates the two growths cleanly — primary for length at the tips, proved by the Indian-ink root practical with percentage-growth calculation; secondary for thickness from the two cambia, ending in bark, lenticels and the annual rings by which a tree is aged.**
