# Primary and secondary growth in plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Primary growth takes place at the…**
a) vascular cambium  b) apical meristems of root and shoot  c) cork cambium  d) lenticels
**Answer: b) apical meristems of root and shoot**

**2. The zones behind a root tip, in order, are…**
a) differentiation, division, elongation  b) division, elongation, differentiation  c) elongation, division, differentiation  d) division only
**Answer: b) division, elongation, differentiation**

**3. Secondary xylem is produced by the…**
a) apical meristem  b) cork cambium  c) vascular cambium  d) endodermis
**Answer: c) vascular cambium**

**4. A tree is aged by counting its…**
a) lenticels  b) leaves  c) annual rings in the wood  d) bark layers
**Answer: c) annual rings in the wood**
