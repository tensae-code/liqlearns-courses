# Primary and secondary growth in plants — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Ink marks on a root spread apart only where the root is growing.**
**Answer: True** — the marking practical's whole point.

**2. The cork cambium produces the secondary xylem.**
**Answer: False** — the vascular cambium does; cork cambium makes bark.

**3. Lenticels are pores in the bark.**
**Answer: True** — the bark's breathing pores.

**4. Secondary growth lengthens the shoot tip.**
**Answer: False** — it thickens; primary growth lengthens.
