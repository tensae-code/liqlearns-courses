# Interactions between populations — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining interactions between populations, using the lesson's key facts.
**Model answer: Populations sharing one place interact — competition, predation, parasitism, mutualism and commensalism — and every interaction can be read by what each side gains or loses. Competition harms both sides: populations needing the same limited resource — food, water, light, space — each grow less than they would alone. In predation one population eats the other: the predator gains, the prey loses — and the two populations rise and fall in linked cycles. A parasite lives on or in a host, feeding on it slowly — gaining food and shelter while the host is weakened but usually not killed outright.**
