# Interactions between populations — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Competition harms both sides: populations needing the same limited resource — food, water, light, space — each grow less than they would alone.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In competition, one side gains and the other is unaffected..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.

**3.** In predation one population eats the other: the predator gains, the prey loses — and the two populations rise and fall in linked cycles.
**Answer: True**

**4.** A parasite usually kills and eats its host at once, like a predator..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.
