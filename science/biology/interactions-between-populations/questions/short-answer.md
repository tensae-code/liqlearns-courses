# Interactions between populations — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: parasitism (+/−).
**Answer: Ticks, lice, worms and mistletoe all take their living from a host bigger than themselves. Unlike a predator, a parasite needs its host alive; unlike a mutualist, it gives nothing back — and heavy parasite loads still stunt, sicken and can kill.**

**2.** Explain: mutualism (+/+).
**Answer: Each partner supplies what the other lacks — food for pollination, cleaning for food, fixed nitrogen for shelter and sugars. Where both sides depend wholly on the partnership (obligate mutualism), neither population can live without the other.**
