# Hands-on — Interactions between populations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
List five pairs of living things that meet near home or school. For each pair, name the interaction and write its two signs, with one line of evidence for your call.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of five paired scenes, one per interaction — two flocks contesting one water hole, a fox with a hare, a tick on a dog, a bee on a flower, and a remora under a shark — each labelled with its two signs — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
