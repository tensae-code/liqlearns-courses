# The cell cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about the sequence?
a) The cell cycle is the cell's repeating sequence of growth, DNA copying, preparation and division  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The cell cycle is the cell's repeating sequence of growth, DNA copying, preparation and division**

**2.** What does this lesson say about modelling it?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Pupils model the cycle to fix its stages in order  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Pupils model the cycle to fix its stages in order**

**3.** What does this lesson say about uncontrolled?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) When the cycle runs uncontrolled, the result is the growth of cancer  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) When the cycle runs uncontrolled, the result is the growth of cancer**

**4.** What does this lesson say about replacement?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) The cycle connects to healing — skin replaces itself through cell division
**Answer: d) The cycle connects to healing — skin replaces itself through cell division**
