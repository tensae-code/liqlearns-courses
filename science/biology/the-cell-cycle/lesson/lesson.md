# The cell cycle

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The cell cycle. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
A cell's life goes round: grow, copy the DNA, prepare, divide — then begin again. This lesson also faces what happens when the cycle runs uncontrolled, and how skin replaces itself.

## The key facts
**The sequence.** The cell cycle is the cell's repeating sequence of growth, DNA copying, preparation and division.

**Modelling it.** Pupils model the cycle to fix its stages in order.

**Uncontrolled.** When the cycle runs uncontrolled, the result is the growth of cancer.

**Replacement.** The cycle connects to healing — skin replaces itself through cell division.
