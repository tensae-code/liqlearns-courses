# Video script — The nervous system — cranial and spinal nerves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A complete table of the twelve cranial nerves giving number, name, exit foramen, modality codes and functions (the trigeminal's maxillary and mandibular divisions, the abducens to the lateral rectus, the facial nerve's taste and muscle roles, the spinal accessory to trapezius and sternocleidomastoid, the hypoglossal to the tongue muscles, and the rest), followed by the statement that humans have 31 pairs of spinal nerves; a sheep-brain dissection figure shows the corpus callosum joining the hemispheres.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
