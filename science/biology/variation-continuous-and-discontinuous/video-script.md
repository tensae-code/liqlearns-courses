Today: Variation — continuous and discontinuous.

Remember this one thing: individuals of a species vary, and variation comes in two kinds: continuous variation, which grades smoothly between extremes (height, mass), and discontinuous variation, which falls into clear separate groups with nothing between (blood group) — caused by genes, by the environment, and by both together.

Malaysia's Form 5 book organises its variation chapter by the two kinds of variation (printed p. 256): types and factors of variation, with variation in humans as the working example — the organising frame is continuous against discontinuous.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
