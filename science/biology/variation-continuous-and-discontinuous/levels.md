# Variation — continuous and discontinuous — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (individuals of a species vary, and variation comes in two kinds: continuous variation, which grades smoothly between extremes (height, mass), and discontinuous variation, which falls into clear separate groups with nothing between (blood group) — caused by genes, by the environment, and by both together) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
