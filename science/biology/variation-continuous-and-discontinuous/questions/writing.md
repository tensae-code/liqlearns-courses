# Variation — continuous and discontinuous — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: individuals of a species vary, and variation comes in two kinds: continuous variation, which grades smoothly between extremes (height, mass), and discontinuous variation, which falls into clear separate groups with nothing between (blood group) — caused by genes, by the environment, and by both together. Blood group, tongue-rolling and earlobe shape fall into clear groups with no in-between values; plotted, they draw separate bars. Such traits are usually controlled by one gene, and the environment scarcely moves them.**
