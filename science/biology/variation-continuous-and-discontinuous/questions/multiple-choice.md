# Variation — continuous and discontinuous — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) individuals of a species vary, and variation comes in two kinds: continuous variation, which grades smoothly between extremes (height, mass), and discontinuous variation, which falls into clear separate groups with nothing between (blood group) — caused by genes, by the environment, and by both together  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life

**2. Continuous variation — which statement is right?**

**Answer: a) Height, mass and skin shade grade smoothly from one extreme to the other, with every step between; plotted, they draw a smooth bell-shaped curve. Such traits are usually built by many genes together, with the environment adding its effect.**
