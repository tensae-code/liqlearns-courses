# Variation — continuous and discontinuous — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: individuals of a species vary, and variation comes in two kinds: continuous variation, which grades smoothly between extremes (height, mass), and discontinuous variation, which falls into clear separate groups with nothing between (blood group) — caused by genes, by the environment, and by both together. Height, mass and skin shade grade smoothly from one extreme to the other, with every step between; plotted, they draw a smooth bell-shaped curve. Such traits are usually built by many genes together, with the environment adding its effect.**

**2.** Explain Malaysia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Malaysia's KSSM Form 5 book teaches variation as a topic in its own right (Ch.12, printed p. 256): types and factors of variation first, continuous against discontinuous as the organising frame, and mutation after it (p. 269) — not variation scattered inside single inheritance patterns.**
