Today: Digestion — the canal and its two kinds of breakdown.

Remember this one thing: digestion happens in a tube (the alimentary canal) plus accessory organs, by mechanical breakdown and chemical digestion by enzymes

The canal: The alimentary canal is the tube food travels through.

Accessory organs: Accessory organs pour their juices into the canal.
