# Digestion — the canal and its two kinds of breakdown

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Digestion — the canal and its two kinds of breakdown. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Food travels one long tube, with helper organs pouring in from the sides. All along it, two kinds of breakdown work together: chopping and chemistry.

[Picture 22247 — the canal drawn as one long tube with accessory organs attached]

## The key facts
**The canal.** The alimentary canal is the tube food travels through.

**Accessory organs.** Accessory organs pour their juices into the canal.

**Mechanical.** Mechanical breakdown — chewing and churning — breaks food into smaller pieces.

**Chemical.** Chemical digestion by enzymes breaks food down chemically.
