# Digestion — the canal and its two kinds of breakdown — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about the canal?
a) The alimentary canal is the tube food travels through  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The alimentary canal is the tube food travels through**

**2.** What does this lesson say about accessory organs?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Accessory organs pour their juices into the canal  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Accessory organs pour their juices into the canal**

**3.** What does this lesson say about mechanical?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Mechanical breakdown — chewing and churning — breaks food into smaller pieces  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Mechanical breakdown — chewing and churning — breaks food into smaller pieces**

**4.** What does this lesson say about chemical?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Chemical digestion by enzymes breaks food down chemically
**Answer: d) Chemical digestion by enzymes breaks food down chemically**
