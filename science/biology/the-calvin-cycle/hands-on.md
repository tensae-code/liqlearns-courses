# Hands-on — The Calvin Cycle

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Look and note
Find one example of this lesson's idea in your home, street or school. Draw it or describe it in three sentences.

## Compare
Put the lesson's two most alike ideas side by side and write one way they are the same and one way they are different.

## Teach it
Explain the lesson to someone at home using your example. Note the question they asked and your answer.
