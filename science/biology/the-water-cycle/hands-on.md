# Hands-on — The water cycle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Gather real things from your home or school compound that connect to this lesson: the water cycle drawn over a landscape — lake, hills, clouds. Lay them out, name each one, and say which key fact it shows.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory, label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
