# Video script — The water cycle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: The water cycle.

Remember this one thing: evaporation, condensation, precipitation and collection move water endlessly between land, living things and sky

Evaporation: Water evaporates from land and water surfaces into the air.

Condensation: Water vapour condenses into clouds.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
