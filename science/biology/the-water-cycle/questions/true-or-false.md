# The water cycle — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Water evaporates from land and water surfaces into the air.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Condensation has nothing to do with this lesson’s topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Water falls back as precipitation.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Collection and round again has nothing to do with this lesson’s topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
