# The water cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about evaporation?
a) Water evaporates from land and water surfaces into the air  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Water evaporates from land and water surfaces into the air**

**2.** What does this lesson say about condensation?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Water vapour condenses into clouds  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Water vapour condenses into clouds**

**3.** What does this lesson say about precipitation?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Water falls back as precipitation  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Water falls back as precipitation**

**4.** What does this lesson say about collection and round again?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Water collects and cycles endlessly between land, living things and sky
**Answer: d) Water collects and cycles endlessly between land, living things and sky**
