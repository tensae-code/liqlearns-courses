# The water cycle

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The water cycle. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
The water in a lake today was rain before and will be cloud again. Four steps move it round forever — and a small beaker model demonstrates them.

## The key facts
**Evaporation.** Water evaporates from land and water surfaces into the air.

**Condensation.** Water vapour condenses into clouds.

**Precipitation.** Water falls back as precipitation.

**Collection and round again.** Water collects and cycles endlessly between land, living things and sky.
