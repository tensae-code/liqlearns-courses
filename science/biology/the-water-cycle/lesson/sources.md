Structural summary: Five themes. (I) Organisation of the cell — inorganic and organic matter in organisms, water's structure and function, non-cellular forms (viruses), prokaryotic vs eukaryotic cell, plant vs animal cell, cell structures and specialisation, transport of matter in the cell, factors affecting the rate of diffusion. (II) Foundations of genetics — nucleic-acid structure and properties, DNA replication, RNA types and functions, genetic code/gene/genome, genotypic and phenotypic ratios in inheritance, chromosome structure, haploid and diploid nucleus, karyotype/karyogram/idiogram, cell cycle and interphase, nuclear division (karyokinesis), cytokinesis, variability (hereditary and non-hereditary), modifications and mutations, kinds of selection. (IV) Foundations of organic evolution — bioevolution of planet Earth, evolution theory and natural selection, fossils and biogeography as evidence, transitional forms and palaeontological series, comparative anatomy and embryology as evidence, rudimentary organs and atavisms, types of adaptation, models of evolution. (V) Organisms and their living environment — the Sun as primary energy source, energy flow, food chain and web, ecological pyramids, biogeochemical cycles (water; carbon and oxygen; nitrogen and the role of microorganisms), human influence on the cycles, population concept and characteristics, ecological niche, ecological factors and relationships.
