# Indigenous conservation — Konso terraces and the Guassa Qero

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — indigenous conservation. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Ethiopian communities' own conservation knowledge: Konso terracing against erosion, contour ploughing, crop rotation, mixed cropping/intercropping (maize with groundnuts the common mix), surface mulching with crop residues, and community-closed areas — above all the Guassa community conservation area of Menz, closed under the Qero communal system for three to five years at a time under elected headmen, a practice over 400 years old.
