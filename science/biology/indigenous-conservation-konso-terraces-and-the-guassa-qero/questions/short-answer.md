# Indigenous conservation — Konso terraces and the Guassa Qero — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about indigenous conservation practices in ethiopia?**
**Answer: Ethiopian communities' own conservation knowledge: Konso terracing against erosion, contour ploughing, crop rotation, mixed cropping/intercropping (maize with groundnuts the common mix), surface mulching with crop residues, and community-closed areas — above all the Guassa community conservation area of Menz, closed under the Qero communal system for three to five years at a time under elected headmen, a practice over 400 years old.**
