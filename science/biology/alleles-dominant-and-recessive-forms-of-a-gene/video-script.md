Today: Alleles — dominant and recessive forms of a gene.

Remember this one thing: a gene comes in alternative forms called alleles, and when two different alleles meet, the dominant one shows in the organism while the recessive one is carried hidden — the distinction Greece's Grades 8–9 book teaches as its own section before Mendel's laws.
