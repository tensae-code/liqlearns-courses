# Alleles — dominant and recessive forms of a gene — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (a gene comes in alternative forms called alleles, and when two different alleles meet, the dominant one shows in the organism while the recessive one is carried hidden — the distinction Greece's Grades 8–9 book teaches as its own section before Mendel's laws) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
