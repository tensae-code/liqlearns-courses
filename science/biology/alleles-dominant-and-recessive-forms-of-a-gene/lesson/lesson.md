# Alleles — dominant and recessive forms of a gene

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Alleles — dominant and recessive forms of a gene. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
A gene comes in alternative forms called alleles, and when two different alleles meet, the dominant one shows in the organism while the recessive one is carried hidden — the distinction Greece's Grades 8–9 book teaches as its own section before Mendel's laws. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from Greece's Gymnasio and Lykeio biology books, which teach it as a named topic.
