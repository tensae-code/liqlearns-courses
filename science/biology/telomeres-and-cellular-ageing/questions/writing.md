# Telomeres and cellular ageing — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining how the state of a chromosome's ends can tell us about the age of a cell.
**Model answer: Each chromosome end is capped by a telomere. The cap shortens over the cell's life, so a cell whose telomeres are still long is younger in its line than a cell whose caps have worn short. Reading the telomeres is reading the cell's age from its own chromosomes.**
