# Telomeres and cellular ageing — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Telomeres are found in the middle of chromosomes.**
**Answer: False** — telomeres are the caps at the ends of chromosomes.

**2. Telomeres shorten over the cell's life.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Telomere shortening has nothing to do with cellular ageing.**
**Answer: False** — Jordan's book files telomeres under cellular ageing: shortening and the ageing of the cell go together.

**4. Every chromosome end carries a telomere cap.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
