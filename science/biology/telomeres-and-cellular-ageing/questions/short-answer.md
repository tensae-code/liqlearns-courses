# Telomeres and cellular ageing — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain what a telomere is and what happens to it.
**Answer: A telomere is the cap at the end of a chromosome. It does not keep its full length: it shortens over the cell's life, and that shortening is bound up with the ageing of the cell.**

**2.** Explain Jordan's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Jordan's Grade 12 student book gives telomeres a stand-alone enrichment at the end of its cell-cycle and divisions unit (printed p. 102): chromosome-end caps and their shortening, taught as the chromosome's part in cellular ageing.**
