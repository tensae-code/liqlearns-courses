# Telomeres and cellular ageing — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Telomeres are the _____ at the ends of chromosomes.**
**Answer: caps**

**2. Over the cell's life, telomeres _____.**
**Answer: shorten**

**3. Telomere shortening is bound up with the _____ of the cell.**
**Answer: ageing**

**4. The telomere sits at the _____ of the chromosome, not in its middle.**
**Answer: end**
