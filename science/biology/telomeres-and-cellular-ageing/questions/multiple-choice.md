# Telomeres and cellular ageing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is a telomere?**
a) A gene in the middle of a chromosome  b) The cap at the end of a chromosome  c) The membrane of the nucleus  d) A kind of enzyme
**Answer: b) The cap at the end of a chromosome**

**2. What happens to telomeres over the cell's life?**
a) They lengthen without limit  b) They stay exactly the same  c) They shorten  d) They move to the middle of the chromosome
**Answer: c) They shorten**

**3. What is telomere shortening bound up with, in Jordan's Grade 12 book?**
a) The ageing of the cell  b) The colour of the cell  c) The size of the nucleus only  d) Photosynthesis
**Answer: a) The ageing of the cell**

**4. Where in the book does the telomere enrichment stand?**
a) In the ecology unit  b) In the cell-cycle unit, after the divisions  c) In the digestion unit  d) It is not in the book
**Answer: b) In the cell-cycle unit, after the divisions**
