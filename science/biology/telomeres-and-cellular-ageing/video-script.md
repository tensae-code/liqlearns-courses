Today: Telomeres and cellular ageing.

Remember this one thing: the ends of your chromosomes wear a cap, and the cap gets shorter.

The cap is called a telomere. It sits at the end of the chromosome, over the end of the thread. And it does not last forever at full length — as time passes and the cell's life goes on, the telomere shortens.

Jordan's Grade 12 book teaches that shortening as the cell's ageing written on the chromosome itself: shorter caps, older cell.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
