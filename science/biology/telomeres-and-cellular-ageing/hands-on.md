# Hands-on — Telomeres and cellular ageing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Take a shoelace or a length of string with a knot tied at each end: the string is the chromosome and the knots are its telomeres. Untie and re-tie one knot a little further in, so the end-piece is shorter — that is the lesson's shortening, shown with your hands.

## Teach it on paper
Draw a chromosome from memory with its two end caps labelled, then the same chromosome with shortened caps, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
