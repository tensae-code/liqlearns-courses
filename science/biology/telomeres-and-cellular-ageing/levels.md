# Telomeres and cellular ageing — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Telomeres are the caps at the ends of chromosomes, and they shorten as time passes — Jordan's Grade 12 book gives their shortening its own enrichment, as the chromosome's clock of cellular ageing.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by stating the lesson's chain in order — chromosome ends are capped by telomeres; the caps shorten; shortening goes with the ageing of the cell — without looking.
