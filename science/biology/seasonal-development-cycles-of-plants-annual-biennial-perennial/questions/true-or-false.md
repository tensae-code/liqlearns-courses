# Seasonal development cycles of plants — annual, biennial, perennial — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Moldova's Grade 7 book (5.3 Seasonal aspects of plant development cycles) teaches: annual, biennial and perennial plants, biorhythms. (printed p. 142).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Moldova's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Biologie: manual pentru clasa a 7-a (Grade 7)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with seasonal development cycles of plants — annual, biennial, perennial.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is seasonal development cycles of plants — annual, biennial, perennial.
