# Seasonal development cycles of plants — annual, biennial, perennial — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: a plant's seasonal development cycle is fitted to its lifespan: annual plants complete their life in one year, biennials in two years, and perennials live and develop over many years — biorhythms timed to the seasons. Moldova's Grade 7 book (5.3 Seasonal aspects of plant development cycles) teaches: annual, biennial and perennial plants, biorhythms. (printed p. 142).**

**2.** Explain Moldova's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Moldova's Grade 7 book closes its seasonal chapter with the development cycles of annual, biennial and perennial plants as biorhythms.**
