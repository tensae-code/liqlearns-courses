# Hands-on — Seasonal development cycles of plants — annual, biennial, perennial

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: a plant's seasonal development cycle is fitted to its lifespan: annual plants complete their life in one year, biennials in two years, and perennials live and develop over many years — biorhythms timed to the seasons.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: three plants in a row across the seasons: an annual completing its life in one year, a biennial over two years, and a perennial returning year after year. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
