# Seasonal development cycles of plants — annual, biennial, perennial — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (a plant's seasonal development cycle is fitted to its lifespan: annual plants complete their life in one year, biennials in two years, and perennials live and develop over many years — biorhythms timed to the seasons) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
