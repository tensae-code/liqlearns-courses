Today: Seasonal development cycles of plants — annual, biennial, perennial.

Remember this one thing: a plant's seasonal development cycle is fitted to its lifespan: annual plants complete their life in one year, biennials in two years, and perennials live and develop over many years — biorhythms timed to the seasons.

Moldova's Grade 7 book (5.3 Seasonal aspects of plant development cycles) teaches: annual, biennial and perennial plants, biorhythms. (printed p. 142).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
