# Cloning — debate and technique — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: cloning makes new living things that are genetic copies of a single parent — and because the technique copies life itself, it is taught together with the debate about what it should and should not be used for. Cloning produces living things that carry the same hereditary material as one parent — genetic copies, not new combinations. The copy is made from the parent's own cells or body material, so no second parent's genes are mixed in.**

**2.** Explain Bulgaria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Bulgaria's Pedagog 6 Grade 9 book is a cell-biology year that ends by applying the cell: its third section is 'Applying knowledge about the cell (biotechnology)', and cloning closes that section as a debate — the Bulgarian way is to hand the learner the technique and the argument together (Topic 32, printed p. 116).**
