# Food security and climate hazards — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (the world population's food supply is a biology topic, and drought and flood hazards can cut it — Mongolia's Grade 9 textbook, Ch. 2 §2.2.4, printed p. 37) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by taking a food the learner's own area grows or raises and tracing, without looking, what a drought year and what a flood year would each do to it, step by step.
