# Food security and climate hazards — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining food security to a younger student. Use the words drought and flood once each.
**Model answer: Food security means that all the people in a place have enough food, not just today but steadily. The food comes from living things — plants growing and animals feeding — so it is part of biology. A drought can stop the plants growing, and a flood can drown or wash them away, so both hazards can leave people with too little food.**
