# Food security and climate hazards — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Mongolia's Grade 9 biology book teaches the world population's food supply as a biology topic.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Ch. 2 §2.2.4, printed p. 37).

**2. Drought means too much water arriving at once.**
**Answer: False** — drought (ган гачиг) is too little water for too long; too much water at once is flood (үер усны аюул).

**3. Drought and flood are opposite hazards, but both can cut a population's food supply.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. The food supply has nothing to do with living things.**
**Answer: False** — food is grown by plants and raised from animals; the supply is biological at its root.
