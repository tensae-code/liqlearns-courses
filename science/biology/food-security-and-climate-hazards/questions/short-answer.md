# Food security and climate hazards — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: food security and its two climate hazards, in one or two sentences.
**Answer: Food security is a population having enough food, steadily. Its two climate hazards are drought (ган гачиг) — too little water for too long, so crops and grass fail — and flood (үер усны аюул) — too much water at once, drowning fields and washing away crops and soil; both cut the food supply.**

**2.** Explain: Mongolia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Mongolia's Grade 9 textbook (Биологи IX (Biology, Grade 9)) places the world population's food supply inside the biology course itself, at Ch. 2 §2.2.4 (printed p. 37) — closing the nutrition sequence after the digestive system (§2.2.1), food-composition experiments (§2.2.2) and enzymes as biocatalysts in digestion (§2.2.3) — and names drought and water disasters (ган гачиг, үер усны аюул) as the hazards over that supply.**
