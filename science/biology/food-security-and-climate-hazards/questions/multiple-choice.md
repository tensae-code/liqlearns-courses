# Food security and climate hazards — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is food security?**
a) Food kept in a locked store  b) A population having enough food, steadily  c) Food that costs a lot  d) Food grown in one season only
**Answer: b) A population having enough food, steadily**

**2. Why is the food supply a biology topic?**
a) Because food is made by living things growing  b) Because shops sell food  c) Because cooking is chemistry  d) Because populations are counted
**Answer: a) Because food is made by living things growing**

**3. What does drought (ган гачиг) do to the food supply?**
a) It fills the rivers  b) It leaves crops and grass without water, so harvests shrink and the supply falls  c) It washes soil away  d) Nothing — dry weather helps crops
**Answer: b) It leaves crops and grass without water, so harvests shrink and the supply falls**

**4. What does flood (үер усны аюул) do to the food supply?**
a) It waters the fields gently  b) It makes the soil richer  c) It drowns fields and washes away crops and soil, so the supply falls  d) It stores water for next year
**Answer: c) It drowns fields and washes away crops and soil, so the supply falls**
