# Food security and climate hazards — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. A population having enough food, steadily, is called food _____.**
**Answer: security**

**2. The long dry hazard that leaves crops without water is _____ (ган гачиг).**
**Answer: drought**

**3. The hazard of too much water at once, drowning fields, is _____ (үер усны аюул).**
**Answer: flood**

**4. In Mongolia's Grade 9 book, the food-supply section (§2.2.4) comes after the sections on digestion, food tests and _____ as biocatalysts.**
**Answer: enzymes**
