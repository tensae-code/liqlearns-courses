# Hands-on — Food security and climate hazards

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Walk to a place near you where food grows or is kept — a garden, a field, a market stall, a store room. Name the foods there, then say, for each one, what a long dry spell (drought) would do to it and what a flood would do to it.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two fields side by side, one green and watered, one cracked dry, and a flooded strip — and label them "enough rain", "drought — ган гачиг" and "flood — үер усны аюул". Check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
