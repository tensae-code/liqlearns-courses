Today: Food security and climate hazards.

Remember this one thing: food comes from living things, so feeding people is biology — and the weather can break the supply.

Every person in the world needs food, every day. That food is grown by plants and raised from animals. When we ask whether a whole population has enough food, steadily, we are asking about its food supply — its food security.

Mongolia's Grade 9 biology book puts this inside the biology course itself, right after digestion and the enzymes that break food down. First the book teaches what food does inside one body; then it steps back and asks about food for the whole world.

And it names the hazards. The first is drought — ган гачиг — a long time with too little rain, when crops and grass fail. The second is flood — үер усны аюул — too much water at once, drowning fields and washing the soil away. Too little water, or too much: both cut the food supply.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
