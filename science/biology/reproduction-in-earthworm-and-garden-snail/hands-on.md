# Hands-on — Reproduction in earthworm and garden snail

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
After rain, look for earthworms and for their casts, and (in snail season) for snails and any clusters of small round eggs under stones or in soil. Say aloud the chain for the worm: exchange at the clitellum → cocoon slips off → fertilisation inside. Do not disturb egg batches you find.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two earthworms joined at their clitella exchanging sperm, with a cocoon slipping off beside them; and two garden snails mating, with a batch of round eggs in the soil below — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
