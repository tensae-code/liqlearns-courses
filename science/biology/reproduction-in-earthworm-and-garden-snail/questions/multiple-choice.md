# Reproduction in earthworm and garden snail — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Hermaphrodite means an animal…
a) has no sex organs
b) carries both male and female organs
c) lays eggs only
d) lives alone always
**Answer: b) carries both male and female organs**

**2.** Where does earthworm fertilisation happen?
a) In the soil generally
b) Inside the cocoon that slips off the clitellum
c) In water
d) Inside the other worm's body only
**Answer: b) Inside the cocoon that slips off the clitellum**

**3.** What do mating earthworms exchange at the clitellum?
a) Eggs
b) Sperm
c) Cocoons
d) Food
**Answer: b) Sperm**

**4.** Where does the garden snail lay its eggs?
a) On leaves
b) In water
c) In the soil, in batches
d) Inside its shell
**Answer: c) In the soil, in batches**
