# Reproduction in earthworm and garden snail — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** The earthworm's sperm-exchange and cocoon-forming band is the _____.
**Answer: clitellum**

**2.** Fertilisation in the earthworm happens inside the slipped-off _____.
**Answer: cocoon**

**3.** An animal carrying both sexes is a _____.
**Answer: hermaphrodite**
