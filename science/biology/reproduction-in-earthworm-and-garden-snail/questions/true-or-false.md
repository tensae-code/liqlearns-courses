# Reproduction in earthworm and garden snail — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Earthworms fertilise themselves and never mate.
**Answer: False** — Earthworms are hermaphrodites but still cross-fertilise: two worms exchange sperm at the clitellum.

**2.** The earthworm's cocoon is formed from the clitellum's band slipping off.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Garden snails lay their egg batches in the soil.
**Answer: True**
