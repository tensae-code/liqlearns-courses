# Reproduction in earthworm and garden snail — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: earthworm reproduction, step by step.
**Answer: Two hermaphrodite earthworms join and exchange sperm at the clitellum, each storing the other's. Later the clitellum's band slips off as a cocoon; as it passes forward it collects the worm's eggs and the stored sperm, and fertilisation happens inside the cocoon, which is left in the soil.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book teaches reproduction in earthworm and garden snail in Chapter 15 (printed p. 217) as a pair: two hermaphrodites that still cross-fertilise — earthworms exchanging sperm at the clitellum with fertilisation inside the slipped-off cocoon; snails exchanging sperm and laying egg batches in the soil.**
