# The ecological niche — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (An organism's ecological niche is its whole way of life in its community — where it lives, what it eats and is eaten by, when it is active and what it does — and two kinds cannot hold the same niche in the same place for long.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a fresh example from the learner's own surroundings and saying why it fits, without looking.
