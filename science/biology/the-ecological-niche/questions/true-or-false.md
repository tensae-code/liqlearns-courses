# The ecological niche — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. A niche includes what an organism eats and what eats it.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Habitat and niche mean exactly the same.**
**Answer: False** — the habitat is the address; the niche is the whole way of life lived there.

**3. Two kinds can share one habitat while holding different niches.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. Competition is strongest when two kinds need the same niche.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
