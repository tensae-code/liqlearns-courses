# The ecological niche — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is an ecological niche?**
a) Only the place an organism lives  b) An organism's whole way of life in its community — place, food, enemies, activity  c) A kind of nest  d) The number of organisms in an area
**Answer: b) An organism's whole way of life in its community — place, food, enemies, activity**

**2. What happens when two kinds need the same niche in the same place?**
a) They share it easily forever  b) They compete, and the weaker is pushed out or changes  c) Both grow faster  d) Nothing
**Answer: b) They compete, and the weaker is pushed out or changes**

**3. Which pair is the right picture of habitat and niche?**
a) Habitat is the address; niche is the life lived at that address  b) Habitat is the food; niche is the weather  c) They are the same thing  d) Niche is the address; habitat is the job
**Answer: a) Habitat is the address; niche is the life lived at that address**

**4. Why can one tree hold many kinds of birds?**
a) Birds never compete  b) Each holds a different niche — different food, height or hours  c) The tree is very large only  d) They take turns being there
**Answer: b) Each holds a different niche — different food, height or hours**
