# The ecological niche — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why two similar bird kinds in one forest are usually found feeding in different places.
**Model answer: Two kinds that eat the same food in the same place at the same time hold the same niche, and so compete directly. The weaker competitor is pushed out, so the kinds that remain in one forest are those whose niches differ — by food, by height in the trees, or by hours of activity.**
