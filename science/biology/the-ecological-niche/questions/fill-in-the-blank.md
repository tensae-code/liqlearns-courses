# The ecological niche — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. An organism's whole way of life in its community is its ecological _____.**
**Answer: niche**

**2. The _____ is the address; the niche is the life lived there.**
**Answer: habitat**

**3. Two kinds needing the same niche in one place _____ with each other.**
**Answer: compete**

**4. Each kind's fittings — beak, teeth, roots — are fittings to its _____.**
**Answer: niche**
