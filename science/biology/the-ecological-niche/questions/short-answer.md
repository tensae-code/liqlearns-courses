# The ecological niche — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: niche and habitat, with an example.
**Answer: The habitat is the place — a tree. The niche is the whole way of life lived there: one bird hunts insects in the tree's top branches by day, another feeds on the trunk, a mouse eats fallen seeds below at night. One address, several niches.**

**2.** Explain: North Macedonia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: North Macedonia's Biology I gives the ecological niche a named lesson of its own (Theme 5.11, printed p. 224) in its closing living-environment theme, between the population lesson and the lesson on ecological factors and relationships.**
