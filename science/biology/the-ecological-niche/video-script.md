Today: The ecological niche.

Remember this one thing: An organism's ecological niche is its whole way of life in its community — where it lives, what it eats and is eaten by, when it is active and what it does — and two kinds cannot hold the same niche in the same place for long.

A habitat is where an organism lives. Its niche is the life it lives there — its food, its enemies, its hours, its work in the community.

Many kinds share one address but hold different niches. When two kinds truly need the same niche, they compete until one gives way. That is the niche: the organism's whole job in its community.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
