# Mitosis — the phases in order — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: cytokinesis.
**Answer: Cytokinesis follows the phases; the guide letters it D again, which is an error in the guide.**

**2.** Explain: plant difference.
**Answer: Cytokinesis differs in plant cells (cell plate formation) from animal cells.**

**3.** Explain: lining up and pulling apart (student book).
**Answer: The chromosomes line up, then are pulled apart toward the two ends.**
