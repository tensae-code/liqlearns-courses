Today: Mitosis — the phases in order.

Remember this one thing: the guide's answer key letters the mitosis figure: A Prophase, B Interphase, C Anaphase, D Telophase, then Cytokinesis.

In the guide's key, A is Prophase and B is Interphase.

In the guide's key, C is Anaphase and D is Telophase.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.


Student-book core: In mitosis the chromosomes condense and become visible. The chromosomes line up, then are pulled apart toward the two ends.
