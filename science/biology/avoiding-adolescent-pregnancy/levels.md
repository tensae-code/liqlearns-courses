# Avoiding adolescent pregnancy — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (adolescent pregnancy must be avoided — pregnancy while a young person is still developing carries health risks for the young mother and the baby and social consequences that can interrupt schooling and life plans, and healthy adolescent couple relationships are built on respect, responsibility and waiting) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
