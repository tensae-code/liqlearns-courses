# Avoiding adolescent pregnancy

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Avoiding adolescent pregnancy. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Adolescent pregnancy must be avoided — pregnancy while a young person is still developing carries health risks for the young mother and the baby and social consequences that can interrupt schooling and life plans, and healthy adolescent couple relationships are built on respect, responsibility and waiting. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from Cuba's Ministry of Education biology textbooks, which teach it as a named topic.

[Picture — two paths from one signpost, drawn simply and respectfully: one path keeps a student at school with books, family and future plans, the other path carries the heavy load of a pregnancy during adolescence]

## The key facts
**Why it must be avoided.** Cuba's Grade 9 student text (Ch. 7) teaches why adolescent pregnancy must be avoided — its health risks and its social consequences — together with healthy adolescent couple relationships (printed p. 340)

**Cuba's way.** Cuba's Grade 9 human-biology-and-health course places avoiding adolescent pregnancy inside its reproduction-and-development chapter, as health teaching in its own right — the risks and consequences themselves, and what a healthy adolescent couple relationship is — not as a list of contraception methods, which this course teaches in their own lessons.
