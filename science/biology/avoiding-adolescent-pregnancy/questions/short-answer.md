# Avoiding adolescent pregnancy — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: adolescent pregnancy must be avoided — pregnancy while a young person is still developing carries health risks for the young mother and the baby and social consequences that can interrupt schooling and life plans, and healthy adolescent couple relationships are built on respect, responsibility and waiting. Cuba's Grade 9 student text (Ch. 7) teaches why adolescent pregnancy must be avoided — its health risks and its social consequences — together with healthy adolescent couple relationships (printed p. 340)**

**2.** Explain Cuba's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Cuba's Grade 9 human-biology-and-health course places avoiding adolescent pregnancy inside its reproduction-and-development chapter, as health teaching in its own right — the risks and consequences themselves, and what a healthy adolescent couple relationship is — not as a list of contraception methods, which this course teaches in their own lessons.**
