# Avoiding adolescent pregnancy — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Cuba's Grade 9 student text (Ch. 7) teaches why adolescent pregnancy must be avoided — its health risks and its social consequences — together with healthy adolescent couple relationships (printed p. 340)**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Cuba's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Biología 3 — El organismo humano y la salud, Noveno grado (2025)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with avoiding adolescent pregnancy.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is avoiding adolescent pregnancy.
