Today: Avoiding adolescent pregnancy.

Remember this one thing: adolescent pregnancy must be avoided — pregnancy while a young person is still developing carries health risks for the young mother and the baby and social consequences that can interrupt schooling and life plans, and healthy adolescent couple relationships are built on respect, responsibility and waiting.

Cuba's Grade 9 student text (Ch. 7) teaches why adolescent pregnancy must be avoided — its health risks and its social consequences — together with healthy adolescent couple relationships (printed p. 340)

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
