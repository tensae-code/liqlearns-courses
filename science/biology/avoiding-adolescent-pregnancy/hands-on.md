# Hands-on — Avoiding adolescent pregnancy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: adolescent pregnancy must be avoided — pregnancy while a young person is still developing carries health risks for the young mother and the baby and social consequences that can interrupt schooling and life plans, and healthy adolescent couple relationships are built on respect, responsibility and waiting.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: two paths from one signpost, drawn simply and respectfully: one path keeps a student at school with books, family and future plans, the other path carries the heavy load of a pregnancy during adolescence. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
