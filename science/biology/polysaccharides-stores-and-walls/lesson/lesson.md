# Polysaccharides — stores and walls

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Polysaccharides — stores and walls. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Join hundreds of sugars and the chain changes jobs: starch and glycogen become stores, cellulose becomes walls. Iodine tells starch from the rest.

[Picture 22237 — a branching starch chain beside a cellulose fibre bundle]

## The key facts
**Long chains.** Polysaccharides are long chains of sugar units.

**Starch.** Starch is the plant's food store.

**Glycogen and cellulose.** Glycogen is the animal food store, and cellulose builds plant cell walls.

**Iodine test.** Iodine tests for starch and glycogen in the laboratory.
