# Polysaccharides — stores and walls — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about long chains?
a) Polysaccharides are long chains of sugar units  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Polysaccharides are long chains of sugar units**

**2.** What does this lesson say about starch?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Starch is the plant's food store  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Starch is the plant's food store**

**3.** What does this lesson say about glycogen and cellulose?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Glycogen is the animal food store, and cellulose builds plant cell walls  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Glycogen is the animal food store, and cellulose builds plant cell walls**

**4.** What does this lesson say about iodine test?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Iodine tests for starch and glycogen in the laboratory
**Answer: d) Iodine tests for starch and glycogen in the laboratory**
