Today: Protein engineering — designing proteins backwards.

Remember this one thing: protein engineering runs the central dogma in reverse: from the function wanted, design the protein's structure, read off the amino-acid sequence, then build the gene — the second-generation genetic engineering that made fast-acting insulin analogues and heat-proofed T4 lysozyme.
