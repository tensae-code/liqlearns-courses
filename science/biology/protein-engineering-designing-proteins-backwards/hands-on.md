# Hands-on — Protein engineering — designing proteins backwards

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: protein engineering runs the central dogma in reverse: from the function wanted, design the protein's structure, read off the amino-acid sequence, then build the gene — the second-generation genetic engineering that made fast-acting insulin analogues and heat-proofed T4 lysozyme.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: an arrow running backwards along the dogma — wanted job, folded protein shape, amino-acid chain, gene — each step drawn under its arrow. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
