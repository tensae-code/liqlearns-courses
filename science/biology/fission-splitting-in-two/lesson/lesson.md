# Fission — splitting in two (or many)

## The idea
First the hereditary material is copied; then the split. Each part grows into a complete new individual.

## The key facts
**Binary fission.** Binary fission divides the body into two roughly equal parts and is common in bacteria, algae and protozoa.

**Copy first.** In fission the hereditary material is copied before the division.

**Multiple fission.** Multiple fission divides the body into many parts at once.

**Each part.** Each part of a fission grows into a complete new individual.
