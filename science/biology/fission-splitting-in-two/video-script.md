Today: Fission — splitting in two (or many).

Remember this one thing: in fission the body divides into two roughly equal parts, or into many parts at once.

Binary fission divides the body into two roughly equal parts and is common in bacteria, algae and protozoa.

In fission the hereditary material is copied before the division.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
