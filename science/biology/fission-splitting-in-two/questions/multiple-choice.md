# Fission — splitting in two (or many) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about binary fission?
a) Binary fission divides the body into two roughly equal parts and is common in bacteria, algae and protozoa
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Binary fission divides the body into two roughly equal parts and is common in bacteria, algae and protozoa**

**2.** What does this lesson say about copy first?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) In fission the hereditary material is copied before the division
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) In fission the hereditary material is copied before the division**

**3.** What does this lesson say about multiple fission?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Multiple fission divides the body into many parts at once
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Multiple fission divides the body into many parts at once**

**4.** What does this lesson say about each part?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Each part of a fission grows into a complete new individual
**Answer: d) Each part of a fission grows into a complete new individual**
