# Video script — Fertilisation, implantation and pregnancy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fertilisation joins sperm and egg (23 chromosomes each) in the ampulla of the fallopian tube; the zygote divides as it travels, implants in the uterus lining at about six days (complete by nine to ten), and pregnancy — the fetus's development inside the womb — follows.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
