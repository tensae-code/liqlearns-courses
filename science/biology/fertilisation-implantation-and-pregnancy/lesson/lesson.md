# Fertilisation, implantation and pregnancy

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — fertilisation, implantation and pregnancy. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Fertilisation joins sperm and egg (23 chromosomes each) in the ampulla of the fallopian tube; the zygote divides as it travels, implants in the uterus lining at about six days (complete by nine to ten), and pregnancy — the fetus's development inside the womb — follows.
