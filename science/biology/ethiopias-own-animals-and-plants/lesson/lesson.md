# Ethiopia's own animals and plants

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Ethiopia's own animals and plants. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Endemic means found nowhere else on Earth. Ethiopia holds many such species, and your own surroundings are the place to start surveying.

[Picture 21015 — a map of Ethiopia ringed with its endemic animals and plants]

## The key facts
**Endemism.** An endemic species is one found nowhere else.

**Diversity.** Ethiopia has high species diversity and many endemic species.

**Local survey.** Pupils survey the common local animals and plants around them.

**Link.** The local survey is connected to the classification system.
