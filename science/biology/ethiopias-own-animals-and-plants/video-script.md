# Video script — Ethiopia's own animals and plants

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Ethiopia's own animals and plants.

Remember this one thing: Ethiopia is rich in species found nowhere else, and a local survey connects them to classification.

An endemic species is one found nowhere else.

Ethiopia has high species diversity and many endemic species.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
