# Food additives and preservatives — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (foods carry additives — preservatives that stop microbes and spoilage, colours, flavours and others — each with its job and its code on the label, and reading the label is how a buyer knows what is in their plate, Greece's workbook's own cry: 'Help! A preservative in my plate') and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
