# Lesson plan — Food additives and preservatives

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Food additives and preservatives
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — secondary band (CURRICULUM.md Unit 16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** foods carry additives — preservatives that stop microbes and spoilage, colours, flavours and others — each with its job and its code on the label, and reading the label is how a buyer knows what is in their plate, Greece's workbook's own cry: 'Help! A preservative in my plate'.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier biology lessons and builds the secondary-band understanding of this topic.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
