# Food additives and preservatives — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. "Help! A preservative in my plate": reading labels, food additives and preservatives**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Greece's Gymnasio and Lykeio biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Βιολογία Α΄ Γυμνασίου — Τετράδιο Εργασιών (Workbook), Grade 7).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with food additives and preservatives.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is food additives and preservatives.
