# Hands-on — Food additives and preservatives

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: foods carry additives — preservatives that stop microbes and spoilage, colours, flavours and others — each with its job and its code on the label, and reading the label is how a buyer knows what is in their plate, Greece's workbook's own cry: 'Help! A preservative in my plate'.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a food packet turned to show its ingredients label, a magnifier over the additive lines, and small icons for preservative, colour and flavour. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
