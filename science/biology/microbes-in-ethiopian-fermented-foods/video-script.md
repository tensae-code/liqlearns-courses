# Video script — Microbes in Ethiopian fermented foods

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The yeasts common to Ethiopian traditional fermented foods and beverages are named (Candida humilis, Meyerozyma guilliermondii, Pichia kudriavzevii and Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the last misprinted "servisiae"); primary metabolites (the same in every species, performing physiological functions) are tabled against secondary metabolites (different in every species).

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
