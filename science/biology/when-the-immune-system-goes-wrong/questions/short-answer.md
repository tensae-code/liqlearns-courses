# When the immune system goes wrong — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: the immune system itself can be the problem: allergies are its over-reaction to harmless things, autoimmune diseases its attack on the body's own tissues, and immunodeficiency its failure to defend at all — Greece's Grade 11 book gives the failures their own named section. problems in the action of the immune system (allergies, autoimmunity, immunodeficiency)**
