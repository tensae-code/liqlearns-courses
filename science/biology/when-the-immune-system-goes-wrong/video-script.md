Today: When the immune system goes wrong.

Remember this one thing: the immune system itself can be the problem: allergies are its over-reaction to harmless things, autoimmune diseases its attack on the body's own tissues, and immunodeficiency its failure to defend at all — Greece's Grade 11 book gives the failures their own named section.

problems in the action of the immune system (allergies, autoimmunity, immunodeficiency)

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
