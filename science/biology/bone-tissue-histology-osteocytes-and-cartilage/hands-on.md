# Hands-on — Bone tissue histology — osteocytes and cartilage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: bone tissue is organised: a periosteum wraps it, compact bone is built of Haversian systems and canals, spongy bone fills the ends, and red and yellow marrow sit inside — read on longitudinal and cross sections.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a long bone cut lengthwise and across, labelled: periosteum outside, compact bone with a Haversian system ringed around its canal, spongy bone, and red and yellow marrow. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
