## The key facts
**The bone's layers.** Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-3, printed pp. 117–119) teaches bone structure part by part: periosteum, compact bone with Haversian systems and canals, spongy bone, and red and yellow marrow.

**Reading the sections.** Every part is labelled on longitudinal and cross sections of the bone — the histology is learned by reading the two cuts, not from a list.

**Kuwait's way.** Kuwait's new Grade 10 book teaches bone as tissue under the microscope's logic: named systems (Haversian systems and canals) located on labelled sections, longitudinal and cross, inside the skeletal-system lesson (Lesson 2-3).
