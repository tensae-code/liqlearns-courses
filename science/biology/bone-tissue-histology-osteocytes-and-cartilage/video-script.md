Today: Bone tissue histology — osteocytes and cartilage.

Remember this one thing: bone tissue is organised: a periosteum wraps it, compact bone is built of Haversian systems and canals, spongy bone fills the ends, and red and yellow marrow sit inside — read on longitudinal and cross sections.

Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-3, printed pp. 117–119) teaches bone structure part by part: periosteum, compact bone with Haversian systems and canals, spongy bone, and red and yellow marrow.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
