# Bone tissue histology — osteocytes and cartilage — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: bone tissue is organised: a periosteum wraps it, compact bone is built of Haversian systems and canals, spongy bone fills the ends, and red and yellow marrow sit inside — read on longitudinal and cross sections. Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-3, printed pp. 117–119) teaches bone structure part by part: periosteum, compact bone with Haversian systems and canals, spongy bone, and red and yellow marrow.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's new Grade 10 book teaches bone as tissue under the microscope's logic: named systems (Haversian systems and canals) located on labelled sections, longitudinal and cross, inside the skeletal-system lesson (Lesson 2-3).**
