# Bone tissue histology — osteocytes and cartilage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) bone tissue is organised: a periosteum wraps it, compact bone is built of Haversian systems and canals, spongy bone fills the ends, and red and yellow marrow sit inside — read on longitudinal and cross sections  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
**Answer: a) bone tissue is organised: a periosteum wraps it, compact bone is built of Haversian systems and canals, spongy bone fills the ends, and red and yellow marrow sit inside — read on longitudinal and cross sections**

**2. The bone's layers — which statement is right?**
a) Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-3, printed pp. 117–119) teaches bone structure part by part: periosteum, compact bone with Haversian systems and canals, spongy bone, and red and yellow marrow.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-3, printed pp. 117–119) teaches bone structure part by part: periosteum, compact bone with Haversian systems and canals, spongy bone, and red and yellow marrow.**

**3. Which statement about this lesson's topic is right?**
