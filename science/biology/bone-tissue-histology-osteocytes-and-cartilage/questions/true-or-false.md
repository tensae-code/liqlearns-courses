# Bone tissue histology — osteocytes and cartilage — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-3, printed pp. 117–119) teaches bone structure part by part: periosteum, compact bone with Haversian systems and canals, spongy bone, and red and yellow marrow.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كتاب الطالب — الصف العاشر — الفصل الدراسي الأول (Biology — Student Book, Grade 10, Term 1), printed pp. 117–119).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with bone tissue histology — osteocytes and cartilage.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is bone tissue histology — osteocytes and cartilage.
