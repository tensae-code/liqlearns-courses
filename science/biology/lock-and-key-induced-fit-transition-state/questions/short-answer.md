# Lock and key, induced fit, transition state — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about how substrate meets enzyme?**
**Answer: The lock-and-key model (active site and substrate already match) and the induced-fit model (the enzyme moulds its active site around the substrate on binding, straining it toward the transition state — the high-energy arrangement from which products form and are released).**
