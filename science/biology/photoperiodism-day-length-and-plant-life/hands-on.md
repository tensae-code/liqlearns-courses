# Hands-on — Photoperiodism — day length and plant life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: plants measure the length of the day and the night — photoperiodism — and use it to time flowering and other seasonal steps, so a plant's calendar is set by light.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: one plant drawn twice: under long summer days flowering, under short winter days in leaves only, with a sun-and-moon day-length dial above each. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
