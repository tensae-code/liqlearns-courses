# Hands-on — Molecular and prenatal methods in human genetics — PCR and amniocentesis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: human genetics works at the molecular scale and before birth: the Human Genome Project mapped the whole set, sequencing reads it, and prenatal screening means genetic testing is not limited to adults.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a sequencing read-out strip beside a diagram of a prenatal test being taken, labelled as genetic testing before birth. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
