Today: Molecular and prenatal methods in human genetics — PCR and amniocentesis.

Remember this one thing: human genetics works at the molecular scale and before birth: the Human Genome Project mapped the whole set, sequencing reads it, and prenatal screening means genetic testing is not limited to adults.

Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 2 student book (Section Two, printed p. 91) teaches human molecular genetics through the Human Genome Project's goal and the NIH-led cooperation of advanced countries that carried it out, with a trigger-release sequencing technique pictured.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
