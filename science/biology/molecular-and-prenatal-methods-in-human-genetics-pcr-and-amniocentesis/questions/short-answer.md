# Molecular and prenatal methods in human genetics — PCR and amniocentesis — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: human genetics works at the molecular scale and before birth: the Human Genome Project mapped the whole set, sequencing reads it, and prenatal screening means genetic testing is not limited to adults. Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 2 student book (Section Two, printed p. 91) teaches human molecular genetics through the Human Genome Project's goal and the NIH-led cooperation of advanced countries that carried it out, with a trigger-release sequencing technique pictured.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's Grade 12 book ends its genetics course at home and in the clinic (Section Two): the Genome Project as international cooperation, sequencing pictured, prenatal screening — and then the Kuwait Medical Genetic Center itself as the next lesson's subject.**
