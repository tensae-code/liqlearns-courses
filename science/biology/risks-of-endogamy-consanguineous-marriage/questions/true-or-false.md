# Risks of endogamy — consanguineous marriage — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 2 student book (Section Two, printed p. 90) sets out the risks of endogamy as its own section closing the heredity-in-humans lesson: what can follow when two related individuals marry.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كتاب الطالب — الصف الثاني عشر — الفصل الدراسي الثاني (Biology — Student Book, Grade 12, Term 2), printed p. 90).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with risks of endogamy — consanguineous marriage.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is risks of endogamy — consanguineous marriage.
