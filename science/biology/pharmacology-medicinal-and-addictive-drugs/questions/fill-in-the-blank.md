# Pharmacology — medicinal and addictive drugs — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The study of drugs and their actions is called _____.**
**Answer: pharmacology**

**2. Drugs that kill or stop bacteria are _____.**
**Answer: antibiotics**

**3. A _____ trains the body's defence before the real infection arrives.**
**Answer: vaccine**

**4. Dependence on a harmful drug is called _____.**
**Answer: addiction**
