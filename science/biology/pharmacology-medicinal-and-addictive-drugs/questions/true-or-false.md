# Pharmacology — medicinal and addictive drugs — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Antibiotics act against bacteria.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches

**2. A vaccine is given after the disease has done its damage, to cure it.**
**Answer: False** — a vaccine is given beforehand — it trains the defence system before the real infection arrives

**3. Addictive drugs are harmless if the dose is small.**
**Answer: False** — addiction is dependence: the user craves the drug, needs more of it and is harmed by it

**4. Punjab teaches pharmacology inside its biology course.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches — it is the Grade 10 book's final chapter, in Applications of Biology
