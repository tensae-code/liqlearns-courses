# Pharmacology — medicinal and addictive drugs — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: medicinal drugs and addictive drugs, and the difference between them.
**Answer: Medicinal drugs are used to prevent or treat disease — antibiotics against bacteria, vaccines to train the body's defence beforehand. Addictive drugs are drugs the body and mind come to crave and depend on; they harm the user. The difference is the purpose and the grip: one is used on disease, the other takes hold of the user.**

**2.** Explain: Pakistan (Punjab)'s way of teaching this.
**Answer: Punjab's Grade 10 book ends its course with Ch 18, Pharmacology (printed pp. 150–155), in Section 6, Applications of Biology — medicinal drugs, addictive drugs, antibiotics and vaccines in one chapter, so the applications of biology include both faces of drugs.**
