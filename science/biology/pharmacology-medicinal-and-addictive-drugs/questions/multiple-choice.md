# Pharmacology — medicinal and addictive drugs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Pharmacology is the study of…**
a) plants b) drugs and their actions on the body c) rocks d) inheritance
**Answer: b) drugs and their actions on the body**

**2. An antibiotic is a drug that…**
a) trains the immune system b) kills or stops bacteria c) makes the user dependent d) cures viruses directly
**Answer: b) kills or stops bacteria**

**3. A vaccine works by…**
a) killing bacteria in the blood b) presenting the defence system with a harmless form of a germ so it is ready beforehand c) replacing blood d) sedating the patient
**Answer: b) presenting the defence system with a harmless form of a germ so it is ready beforehand**

**4. An addictive drug is one that…**
a) is prescribed for infection b) the body comes to crave and depend on, harming the user c) is made from plants only d) has no effect
**Answer: b) the body comes to crave and depend on, harming the user**
