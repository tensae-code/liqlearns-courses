**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining how a vaccine differs from an antibiotic.
**Model answer: An antibiotic is a drug that kills or stops bacteria, used when a bacterial infection is present. A vaccine is given before infection: it shows the body's defence system a harmless form of a germ, so the defence is trained and ready if the real germ arrives.**
