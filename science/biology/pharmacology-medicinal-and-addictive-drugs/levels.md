# Pharmacology — medicinal and addictive drugs — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Pharmacology is the study of drugs — what they are, how they act on the body, and how they are used: medicinal drugs that treat disease (antibiotics that kill bacteria, and vaccines that train the body's defence before disease comes) set against addictive drugs, the drugs a body comes to depend on, which harm the user and are taught as the other face of the same chapter.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
