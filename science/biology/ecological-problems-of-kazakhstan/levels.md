# Ecological problems of Kazakhstan — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Kazakhstan teaches its own ecological problems as a named topic — oil and mineral extraction and processing, invasive species such as ragweed and nutria, and pressure on its steppes, parks and waters — with their causes and consequences inside the country itself) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
