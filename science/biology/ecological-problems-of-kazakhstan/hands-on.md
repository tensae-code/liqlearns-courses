# Hands-on — Ecological problems of Kazakhstan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: Kazakhstan teaches its own ecological problems as a named topic — oil and mineral extraction and processing, invasive species such as ragweed and nutria, and pressure on its steppes, parks and waters — with their causes and consequences inside the country itself.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a map of Kazakhstan with its own cases pinned: an oil field, a mine, a ragweed plant marked invasive, and a national park shield, each with a small cause-and-consequence label. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
