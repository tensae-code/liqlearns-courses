# Heart dissection — structure of the mammalian heart — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: a mammalian heart dissected following pictured steps shows its plan directly: chambers and valves identified on a numbered diagram, and the wall thicknesses of the chambers compared. Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 2 applications workbook (Activity 11, printed p. 40) dissects a mammalian heart following pictured steps.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's workbook closes its circulation activities with the heart itself on the tray (Activity 11, printed pp. 40–44): pictured dissection steps, a numbered-part diagram, and wall thickness as a measured comparison.**
