# Heart dissection — structure of the mammalian heart

## The idea
a mammalian heart dissected following pictured steps; chambers and valves identified on a numbered diagram (parts 1–14) and the wall thicknesses compared

[Picture — a mammalian heart cut open with its chambers and valves numbered 1 to 14 on a diagram beside it, the thick wall of the left ventricle visibly thicker than the right]

## The key facts
**The dissection.** Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 2 applications workbook (Activity 11, printed p. 40) dissects a mammalian heart following pictured steps.

**Chambers, valves, walls.** The chambers and valves are identified on a numbered diagram (parts 1–14), and the wall thicknesses are compared — the heart's structure is read off the real organ.

**Kuwait's way.** Kuwait's workbook closes its circulation activities with the heart itself on the tray (Activity 11, printed pp. 40–44): pictured dissection steps, a numbered-part diagram, and wall thickness as a measured comparison.
