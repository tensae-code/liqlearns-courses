# Video script — Hormonal, IUD and permanent contraception

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Spermicides that kill sperm; combined and progesterone-only pills that stop ovulation, thicken cervical mucus and thin the womb lining; the copper or hormone IUD fitted in the uterus; implants and injections; the emergency post-pill taken within 72 hours; the vaginal ring releasing oestrogen and progesterone; and permanent sterilisation (vasectomy, tubal ligation); a summary table weighs each method's failure rate, action, advantages and disadvantages, and pupils interview health-centre staff about local use.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
