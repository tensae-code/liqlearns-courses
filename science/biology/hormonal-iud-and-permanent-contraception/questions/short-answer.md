# Hormonal, IUD and permanent contraception — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about contraceptives?**
**Answer: Spermicides that kill sperm; combined and progesterone-only pills that stop ovulation, thicken cervical mucus and thin the womb lining; the copper or hormone IUD fitted in the uterus; implants and injections; the emergency post-pill taken within 72 hours; the vaginal ring releasing oestrogen and progesterone; and permanent sterilisation (vasectomy, tubal ligation); a summary table weighs each method's failure rate, action, advantages and disadvantages, and pupils interview health-centre staff about local use.**
