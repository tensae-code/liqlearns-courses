# Hormonal, IUD and permanent contraception — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Spermicides that kill sperm; combined and progesterone-only pills that stop ovulation, thicken cervical mucus and thin the womb lining; the copper or hormone IUD fitted in the uterus; implants and injections; the emergency post-pill taken within 72 hours; the vaginal ring releasing oestrogen and progesterone; and permanent sterilisation (vasectomy, tubal ligation); a summary table weighs each method's failure rate, action, advantages and disadvantages, and pupils interview health-centre staff about local use.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. The copper or hormone IUD fitted in the uterus.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. The emergency post-pill taken within 72 hours.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. The vaginal ring releasing oestrogen and progesterone.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
