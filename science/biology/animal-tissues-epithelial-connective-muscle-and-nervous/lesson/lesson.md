## The key facts
**What a tissue is made of.** Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-1, printed pp. 89–92) defines a tissue by its parts: cells plus intercellular matrix plus fibres — thick collagen for tensile strength, elastin, and reticular fibres. Learners build a model tissue from slime, buttons, elastic bands and glue.

**Epithelial tissue.** Epithelial tissue (printed pp. 93–94) is tightly packed covering and lining tissue; the book's worked example is stratified squamous epithelium, the skin's friction-protecting outer layer.

**Connective tissue: cartilage.** For connective tissue the book works cartilage in detail (printed p. 97): cartilage cells in a semi-viscous matrix, located in the tracheal rings and the ear pinna.

**Kuwait's way.** Kuwait's new Grade 10 book builds the tissues lesson from a model the learners make themselves — slime, buttons, elastic bands and glue standing for matrix, cells and fibres — then takes each tissue kind in turn (Lesson 2-1).
