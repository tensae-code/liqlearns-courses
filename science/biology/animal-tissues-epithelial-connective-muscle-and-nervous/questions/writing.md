# Animal tissues — epithelial, connective, muscle and nervous — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: an animal tissue is cells plus the intercellular matrix and fibres between them, and animal tissues fall into four kinds — epithelial, connective, muscle and nervous — each built for its job. Epithelial tissue (printed pp. 93–94) is tightly packed covering and lining tissue; the book's worked example is stratified squamous epithelium, the skin's friction-protecting outer layer.**
