# Animal tissues — epithelial, connective, muscle and nervous — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-1, printed pp. 89–92) defines a tissue by its parts: cells plus intercellular matrix plus fibres — thick collagen for tensile strength, elastin, and reticular fibres. Learners build a model tissue from slime, buttons, elastic bands and glue.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كتاب الطالب — الصف العاشر — الفصل الدراسي الأول (Biology — Student Book, Grade 10, Term 1), printed pp. 89–92).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with animal tissues — epithelial, connective, muscle and nervous.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is animal tissues — epithelial, connective, muscle and nervous.
