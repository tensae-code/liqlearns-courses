# Animal tissues — epithelial, connective, muscle and nervous — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: an animal tissue is cells plus the intercellular matrix and fibres between them, and animal tissues fall into four kinds — epithelial, connective, muscle and nervous — each built for its job. Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-1, printed pp. 89–92) defines a tissue by its parts: cells plus intercellular matrix plus fibres — thick collagen for tensile strength, elastin, and reticular fibres. Learners build a model tissue from slime, buttons, elastic bands and glue.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's new Grade 10 book builds the tissues lesson from a model the learners make themselves — slime, buttons, elastic bands and glue standing for matrix, cells and fibres — then takes each tissue kind in turn (Lesson 2-1).**
