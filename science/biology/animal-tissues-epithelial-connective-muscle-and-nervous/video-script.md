Today: Animal tissues — epithelial, connective, muscle and nervous.

Remember this one thing: an animal tissue is cells plus the intercellular matrix and fibres between them, and animal tissues fall into four kinds — epithelial, connective, muscle and nervous — each built for its job.

Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-1, printed pp. 89–92) defines a tissue by its parts: cells plus intercellular matrix plus fibres — thick collagen for tensile strength, elastin, and reticular fibres. Learners build a model tissue from slime, buttons, elastic bands and glue.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
