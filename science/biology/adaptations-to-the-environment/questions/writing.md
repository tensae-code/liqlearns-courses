# Adaptations to the environment — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences comparing the adaptations of a fish and a desert plant.
**Model answer: A fish is adapted to water: gills take oxygen from the water and a streamlined shape with fins moves it through the water. A desert plant is adapted to dryness: it stores water, loses little through small or thick-skinned leaves, and reaches deep for water. Each is fitted to the abiotic factors of its own environment.**
