# Adaptations to the environment — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. A feature that fits an organism to its environment is an _____.**
**Answer: adaptation**

**2. Water supply, temperature and light are _____ factors.**
**Answer: abiotic**

**3. Fish take oxygen from water through their _____.**
**Answer: gills**

**4. Adaptations belong to a kind because they are _____.**
**Answer: inherited**
