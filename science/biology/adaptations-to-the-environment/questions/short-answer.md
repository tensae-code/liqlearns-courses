# Adaptations to the environment — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: adaptation to an abiotic factor, with an example.
**Answer: An abiotic factor is a non-living condition such as water supply, temperature or light. An organism is adapted to it when it carries features that answer that condition — for example a desert plant's water-storing stem and small leaves answer the shortage of water.**

**2.** Explain: North Macedonia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: North Macedonia's Grade 8 textbook gives adaptations a whole theme of their own (Theme 4, printed pp. 202–219) — adaptation to the environment, to abiotic factors, aquatic adaptations and adaptations in different environments — placed between the reproduction themes and the closing evolution theme.**
