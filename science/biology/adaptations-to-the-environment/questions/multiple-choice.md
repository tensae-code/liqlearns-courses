# Adaptations to the environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is an adaptation?**
a) A feature that fits an organism to its environment  b) A change an animal learns in its life  c) Any feature of an organism  d) A place where an organism lives
**Answer: a) A feature that fits an organism to its environment**

**2. Which of these is an abiotic factor?**
a) A predator  b) Temperature  c) A mate  d) A disease
**Answer: b) Temperature**

**3. Which set names adaptations for life in water?**
a) Thick fur and hibernation  b) Gills and a streamlined shape  c) Deep roots and small leaves  d) Wings and beaks
**Answer: b) Gills and a streamlined shape**

**4. How do adaptations come to belong to a kind of organism?**
a) Each individual learns them  b) They are inherited — those carrying them survived and reproduced  c) The environment writes them onto each animal at birth  d) They appear when the animal needs them
**Answer: b) They are inherited — those carrying them survived and reproduced**
