# Adaptations to the environment — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. An adaptation should be judged against the environment it answers.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Light and temperature are biotic factors.**
**Answer: False** — they are abiotic — non-living — factors.

**3. A desert plant's water-keeping features are adaptations to an abiotic factor.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. An individual animal can grow an adaptation during its own lifetime and pass it on.**
**Answer: False** — adaptations are inherited features of the kind, present because carriers survived and reproduced.
