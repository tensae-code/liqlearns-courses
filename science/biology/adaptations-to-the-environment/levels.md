# Adaptations to the environment — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (An adaptation is a feature of an organism that fits it to its environment: organisms are adapted to the abiotic factors around them — water, temperature, light — and every environment, water included, has its own adapted forms.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a fresh example from the learner's own surroundings and saying why it fits, without looking.
