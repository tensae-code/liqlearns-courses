# Properties of enzymes — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about physical and chemical properties of enzymes?**
**Answer: Enzymes are large, colloidal, high-molecular-weight proteins, active in tiny amounts, precipitable for analysis, heat- and pH-sensitive, regulatable, reversible in action, and enormously efficient — turning over hundreds to thousands of substrates a second, at rates many orders faster than uncatalysed reactions; denaturation — breaking the bonds that hold the shape — destroys the active site.**
