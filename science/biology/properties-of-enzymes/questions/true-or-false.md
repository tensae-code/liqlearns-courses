# Properties of enzymes — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Enzymes are large, colloidal, high-molecular-weight proteins, active in tiny amounts, precipitable for analysis, heat- and pH-sensitive, regulatable, reversible in action, and enormously efficient — turning over hundreds to thousands of substrates a second, at rates many orders faster than uncatalysed reactions; denaturation — breaking the bonds that hold the shape — destroys the active site.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
