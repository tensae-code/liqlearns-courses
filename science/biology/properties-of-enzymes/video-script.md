# Video script — Properties of enzymes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Enzymes are large, colloidal, high-molecular-weight proteins, active in tiny amounts, precipitable for analysis, heat- and pH-sensitive, regulatable, reversible in action, and enormously efficient — turning over hundreds to thousands of substrates a second, at rates many orders faster than uncatalysed reactions; denaturation — breaking the bonds that hold the shape — destroys the active site.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
