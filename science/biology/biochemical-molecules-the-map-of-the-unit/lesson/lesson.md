# Biochemical molecules — the map of the unit

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Biochemical molecules — the map of the unit. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Before the detail, the map. Every chemical a living thing is made of sits on one of two branches — inorganic or organic — and the organic branch has four families.

[Picture 22230 — a two-branch map — inorganic and organic — with the four organic families on the second branch]

## The key facts
**Two branches.** Life's chemicals sort into inorganic and organic molecules.

**Inorganic.** Water and ions are the inorganic molecules of this unit.

**Organic families.** The organic families are carbohydrates, lipids, proteins and nucleic acids.

**Sort first.** Pupils write and sort the biomolecules before the detail begins.
