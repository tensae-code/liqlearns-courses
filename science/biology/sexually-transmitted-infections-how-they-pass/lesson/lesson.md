# Sexually transmitted infections — how they pass and what they do

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Sexually transmitted infections — how they pass and what they do. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
The main infections named include HIV/AIDS, syphilis, gonorrhoea and herpes-type infections, caused by bacteria or viruses.

[Picture 21050 — a transmission-chain diagram]

## The key facts
**Transmission.** STIs pass mainly through sexual contact.

**Causes.** STIs are caused by bacteria or viruses.

**Named infections.** Named STIs include HIV/AIDS, syphilis, gonorrhoea and herpes-type infections.

**Untreated.** Untreated STIs can cause infertility and long-term damage.
