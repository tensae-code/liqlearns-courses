# Sexually transmitted infections — how they pass and what they do — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about transmission?
a) STIs pass mainly through sexual contact
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) STIs pass mainly through sexual contact**

**2.** What does this lesson say about causes?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) STIs are caused by bacteria or viruses
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) STIs are caused by bacteria or viruses**

**3.** What does this lesson say about named infections?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Named STIs include HIV/AIDS, syphilis, gonorrhoea and herpes-type infections
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Named STIs include HIV/AIDS, syphilis, gonorrhoea and herpes-type infections**

**4.** What does this lesson say about untreated?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Untreated STIs can cause infertility and long-term damage
**Answer: d) Untreated STIs can cause infertility and long-term damage**
