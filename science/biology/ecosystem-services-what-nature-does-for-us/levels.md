# Ecosystem services — what nature does for us — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Nature does work for people, for free — and that work has a name: ecosystem services. Finland's field pack groups them in three: provisioning services (food, timber), regulating and supporting services (pollination, water purification, nutrient cycling, carbon binding), and cultural services (recreation). The more diverse nature is, the better it adapts to climate change, disease and pests — so the services hold.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by sorting any named service — food, pollination, recreation — into its group: provisioning, regulating/supporting, or cultural, without looking.
