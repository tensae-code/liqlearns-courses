Today: Ecosystem services — what nature does for us.

Remember this one thing: nature works for us. The work is called ecosystem services, and it comes in three groups.

First, provisioning: nature hands things over — food, timber. Second, regulating and supporting: nature keeps the system running — pollination, water purification, nutrient cycling, carbon binding. Third, cultural: nature restores people — recreation, the walk in the forest.

And the services stand on diversity. The more kinds of living things there are, the better nature adapts to climate change, disease and pests — and the longer the services hold.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
