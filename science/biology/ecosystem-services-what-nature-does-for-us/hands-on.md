# Hands-on — Ecosystem services — what nature does for us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
On a walk, list every ecosystem service you can catch nature doing — a bee pollinating, a tree binding carbon and giving shade, a place people go to rest. Sort your list into the three groups: provisioning, regulating/supporting, cultural.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the three groups of ecosystem services from memory as three panels with two examples in each, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
