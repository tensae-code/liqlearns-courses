# Ecosystem services — what nature does for us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which group does pollination belong to?**
a) Provisioning  b) Cultural  c) Regulating and supporting  d) It is not a service
**Answer: c) Regulating and supporting**

**2. Food and timber are examples of which group?**
a) Provisioning services  b) Cultural services  c) Regulating services  d) Supporting services
**Answer: a) Provisioning services**

**3. Recreation in nature is which kind of service?**
a) Provisioning  b) Cultural  c) Regulating  d) Supporting
**Answer: b) Cultural**

**4. Why does diversity matter for ecosystem services?**
a) It does not  b) More diverse nature adapts better to climate change, disease and pests, so the services hold  c) Diversity only matters in zoos  d) Services come from machines, not nature
**Answer: b) More diverse nature adapts better to climate change, disease and pests, so the services hold**
