# Ecosystem services — what nature does for us — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain the three groups of ecosystem services, with one example of each.
**Answer: Provisioning services hand things over directly — food and timber. Regulating and supporting services keep the living system running — pollination, water purification, nutrient cycling and carbon binding. Cultural services restore people — recreation in nature is the named example.**

**2.** Explain Finland's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Finland's field pack (Luonnon monimuotoisuuden tutkiminen maastossa) teaches ecosystem services standing in nature itself, on a school field day: the framing chapters group the services as provisioning, regulating/supporting and cultural, and tie them to biodiversity — more diverse nature adapts better to climate change, disease and pests.**
