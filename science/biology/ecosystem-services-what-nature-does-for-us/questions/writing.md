# Ecosystem services — what nature does for us — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining ecosystem services to a younger student, naming the three groups and one example of each.
**Model answer: Nature does work for people, and that work is called ecosystem services. Provisioning services give us things like food and timber; regulating and supporting services keep the world running, like pollination and water purification; and cultural services restore us, like recreation in a forest.**
