# Ecosystem services — what nature does for us — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Water purification is a regulating/supporting ecosystem service.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Carbon binding is a cultural service.**
**Answer: False** — carbon binding is a regulating/supporting service; the cultural example in the lesson is recreation.

**3. Ecosystem services are work people do for nature.**
**Answer: False** — they are the other way round — work nature does that people depend on.

**4. Losing biodiversity weakens ecosystem services.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches — more diverse nature adapts better to change, disease and pests.
