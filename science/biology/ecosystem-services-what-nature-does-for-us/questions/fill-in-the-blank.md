# Ecosystem services — what nature does for us — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Food and timber are _____ services.**
**Answer: provisioning**

**2. Pollination, water purification, nutrient cycling and carbon binding are _____ and supporting services.**
**Answer: regulating**

**3. Recreation is a _____ service.**
**Answer: cultural**

**4. More diverse nature adapts better to climate change, disease and _____.**
**Answer: pests**
