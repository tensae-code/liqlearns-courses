# Plant growth hormones — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Abscisic acid and ethylene are plant growth hormones.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Apical dominance means the side buds hold the top bud back.
**Answer: False** — It is the other way: the top bud's auxin holds the side buds back.

**3.** Cutting a hedge's top releases its side buds to grow.
**Answer: True**
