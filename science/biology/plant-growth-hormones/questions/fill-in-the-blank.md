# Plant growth hormones — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** The hormone made at the shoot tip that holds side buds back is _____.
**Answer: auxin**

**2.** The top bud's control over the side buds is called _____ dominance.
**Answer: apical**

**3.** Gibberellins, cytokinins, abscisic acid and _____ complete the book's list of plant growth hormones alongside auxins.
**Answer: ethylene**
