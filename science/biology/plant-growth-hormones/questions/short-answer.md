# Plant growth hormones — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: apical dominance, using the hedge.
**Answer: Auxin made in the top (apical) bud travels down and holds the side buds back — apical dominance. When a gardener cuts the top off a hedge, the source of auxin is removed, the side buds are released, and they grow out sideways, so the hedge becomes bushy instead of tall.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book teaches the regulation of growth by hormones in Chapter 4 (printed p. 62): auxins, cytokinins, gibberellins, abscisic acid and ethylene are named as the growth regulators, and auxin and apical dominance are explained through hedge-cutting.**
