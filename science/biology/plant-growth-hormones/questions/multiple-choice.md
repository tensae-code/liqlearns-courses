# Plant growth hormones — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these is NOT one of the plant growth hormones the book names?
a) Auxins
b) Gibberellins
c) Insulin
d) Ethylene
**Answer: c) Insulin**

**2.** What is apical dominance?
a) Roots dominating the shoot
b) The top bud, through its auxin, holding the side buds back
c) The largest leaf shading the others
d) Flowers opening before leaves
**Answer: b) The top bud, through its auxin, holding the side buds back**

**3.** Why does a cut hedge grow bushy?
a) Cutting adds fertiliser
b) Removing the tip removes its auxin, so the side buds are released and grow out
c) The roots grow faster
d) Insects pollinate it more
**Answer: b) Removing the tip removes its auxin, so the side buds are released and grow out**

**4.** Where is auxin produced?
a) In the roots only
b) At the growing tip of the shoot
c) In the flower
d) In the seed coat
**Answer: b) At the growing tip of the shoot**
