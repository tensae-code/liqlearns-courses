# Hands-on — Plant growth hormones

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find a hedge or a bushy plant that has been cut. Look for where the cut was made and how many side shoots grew out below it. Say aloud the chain: tip cut → auxin gone → side buds released → bushy growth. Then find an uncut shoot and point to the top bud that is holding its side buds back.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a hedge before and after cutting: the uncut hedge growing tall from its top bud, the cut hedge bushing out sideways from released side buds — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
