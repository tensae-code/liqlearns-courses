# Food chains and food webs — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A trophic level is a feeding level in a food chain.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Three or four has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Energy is lost at each transfer between trophic levels.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Too little remains has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**5.** Producers, herbivores and primary and secondary carnivores are the roles in the web.
**Answer: True**

**6.** The sun has nothing to do with this lesson’s topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
