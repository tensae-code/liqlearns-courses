# Food chains and food webs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about trophic levels?
a) A trophic level is a feeding level in a food chain
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) A trophic level is a feeding level in a food chain**

**2.** What does this lesson say about three or four?
