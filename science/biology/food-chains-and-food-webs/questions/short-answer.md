# Food chains and food webs — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: energy loss.
**Answer: Energy is lost at each transfer between trophic levels.**

**2.** Explain: too little remains.
**Answer: Too little energy remains to support another level beyond three or four.**

**3.** Explain: webs (student book).
**Answer: Food chains interconnect into food webs.**
