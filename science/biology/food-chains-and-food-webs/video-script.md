Today: Food chains and food webs.

Remember this one thing: food chains usually stop at three or four trophic levels because energy is lost at each transfer.

A trophic level is a feeding level in a food chain.

Trophic level chains are usually limited to 3 or 4 levels.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.


Student-book core: Organisms are linked by who eats whom into food chains. Food chains interconnect into food webs.
