# Adaptation of organisms to their environment — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A terrestrial animal's body answers its home: thick fur where it is cold, a water-storing body where it is dry, strong legs where it must run, claws or a burrowing shape where it must dig.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Behaviour adapts too is the opposite of what this lesson teaches.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches this as stated.**

**3.** Given an unfamiliar animal, its features are clues to its home: webbed feet speak of water, a hump of the desert, white fur of snow.
**Answer: True**

**4.** This lesson's topic plays no part in science at all.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
