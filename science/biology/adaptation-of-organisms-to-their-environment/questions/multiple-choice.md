# Adaptation of organisms to their environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about adaptation of organisms to their environment?
a) An adaptation is a feature that fits a living thing to the place it lives — land animals carry, in their bodies and behaviour, the answers to their habitat's problems of water, temperature, food and enemies.

c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) An adaptation is a feature that fits a living thing to the place it lives — land animals carry, in their bodies and behaviour, the answers to their habitat's problems of water, temperature, food and enemies.**

**2.** What does this lesson say about features fit places?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) A terrestrial animal's body answers its home: thick fur where it is cold, a water-storing body where it is dry, strong legs where it must run, claws or a burrowing shape where it must dig.
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) A terrestrial animal's body answers its home: thick fur where it is cold, a water-storing body where it is dry, strong legs where it must run, claws or a burrowing shape where it must dig.**

**3.** What does this lesson say about behaviour adapts too?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all

**Answer: c) Adaptation is not only in the body.**

**4.** What does this lesson say about read the animal, read the home?
