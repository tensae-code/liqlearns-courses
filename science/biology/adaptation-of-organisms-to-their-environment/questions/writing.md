# Adaptation of organisms to their environment — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining adaptation of organisms to their environment, using the lesson's key facts.
**Model answer: An adaptation is a feature that fits a living thing to the place it lives — land animals carry, in their bodies and behaviour, the answers to their habitat's problems of water, temperature, food and enemies. A terrestrial animal's body answers its home: thick fur where it is cold, a water-storing body where it is dry, strong legs where it must run, claws or a burrowing shape where it must dig. The feature and the place explain each other. Adaptation is not only in the body. Sleeping through the cold season, hunting at night in the desert's heat, migrating when food fails — what an animal does can fit it to its environment as surely as what it is made of. Given an unfamiliar animal, its features are clues to its home: webbed feet speak of water, a hump of the desert, white fur of snow. Adaptation is the name for that fit between organism and environment, however it is achieved.**
