# Adaptation of organisms to their environment — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: features fit places.
**Answer: A terrestrial animal's body answers its home: thick fur where it is cold, a water-storing body where it is dry, strong legs where it must run, claws or a burrowing shape where it must dig. The feature and the place explain each other.**

**2.** Explain: behaviour adapts too.
**Answer: Adaptation is not only in the body. Sleeping through the cold season, hunting at night in the desert's heat, migrating when food fails — what an animal does can fit it to its environment as surely as what it is made of.**

**3.** Explain: read the animal, read the home.
**Answer: Given an unfamiliar animal, its features are clues to its home: webbed feet speak of water, a hump of the desert, white fur of snow. Adaptation is the name for that fit between organism and environment, however it is achieved.**
