# Adaptation of organisms to their environment — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (an adaptation is a feature that fits a living thing to the place it lives — land animals carry, in their bodies and behaviour, the answers to their habitat's problems of water, temperature, food and enemies.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
