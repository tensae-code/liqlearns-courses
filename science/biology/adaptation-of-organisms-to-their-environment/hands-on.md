# Hands-on — Adaptation of organisms to their environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Pick three land animals you know. For each, write its home's hardest problem (cold, dryness, hunters…) and the feature or behaviour that answers it. Then take one feature — thick fur — and name the home it predicts.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of adaptation of organisms to their environment — an adaptation is a feature that fits a living thing to the place it lives — land animals carry, in their bodies and behaviour, the answers to their habitat's problems of water, temperature, food and enemies. — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
