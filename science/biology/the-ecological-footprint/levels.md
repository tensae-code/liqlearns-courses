# The ecological footprint — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (the ecological footprint converts everything a person, city or country consumes and discards into the land and water area that must supply and absorb it, in six components — cropland, grazing land, forest, fishing grounds, built-up land and the carbon footprint) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
