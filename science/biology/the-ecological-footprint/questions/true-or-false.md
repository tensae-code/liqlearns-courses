# The ecological footprint — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. everything a person, city or country consumes and discards converted into the land and water area that must supply and absorb it, in six components (cropland, grazing land, forest, fishing grounds, built-up land and the carbon footprint); driving against walking and beef against vegetables as the worked comparisons; China's per-capita footprint 2.2 global hectares in 2007, below the world average, yet first in the world in total.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in China's People's Education Press biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (普通高中教科书《生物学》选择性必修2 生物与环境 (Senior High Biology, Elective Compulsory Book 2: Biology and Environment, People's Education Press, 2019-standard edition)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with the ecological footprint.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is the ecological footprint.
