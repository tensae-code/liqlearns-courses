**Answer: the ecological footprint converts everything a person, city or country consumes and discards into the land and water area that must supply and absorb it, in six components — cropland, grazing land, forest, fishing grounds, built-up land and the carbon footprint. everything a person, city or country consumes and discards converted into the land and water area that must supply and absorb it, in six components (cropland, grazing land, forest, fishing grounds, built-up land and the carbon footprint); driving against walking and beef against vegetables as the worked comparisons; China's per-capita footprint 2.2 global hectares in 2007, below the world average, yet first in the world in total.**
