# The ecological footprint

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The ecological footprint. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
The ecological footprint converts everything a person, city or country consumes and discards into the land and water area that must supply and absorb it, in six components — cropland, grazing land, forest, fishing grounds, built-up land and the carbon footprint. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from China's People's Education Press biology textbooks, which teach it as a named topic.
