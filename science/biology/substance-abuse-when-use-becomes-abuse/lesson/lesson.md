# Substance abuse — when use becomes abuse

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Substance abuse — when use becomes abuse. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Some substances are medicines properly used. Others hide in everyday products — tea and coffee carry caffeine, a stimulant that can addict.

[Picture 21061 — a spectrum from proper medicine use to abuse]

## The key facts
**Definition.** Substance abuse is using legal or illegal drugs in the wrong way or in excess.

**Medicines.** Some substances are medicines when properly used.

**Caffeine.** Tea and coffee carry caffeine, a stimulant that can addict.

**The cases.** The cases that follow are tobacco, alcohol, khat, cannabis and doping.
