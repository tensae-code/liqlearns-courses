Today: Animal tissues — epithelial, connective, muscle, nervous.

Remember this one thing: an animal's cells are organised into four tissue kinds — epithelial tissue covering and lining, connective tissue joining and supporting, muscle tissue moving, and nervous tissue carrying messages — each kind built of cells fitted to its one job.
