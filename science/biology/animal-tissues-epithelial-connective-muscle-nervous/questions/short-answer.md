# Animal tissues — epithelial, connective, muscle, nervous — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: an animal's cells are organised into four tissue kinds — epithelial tissue covering and lining, connective tissue joining and supporting, muscle tissue moving, and nervous tissue carrying messages — each kind built of cells fitted to its one job. the kinds of animal tissues as their own topic**

**2.** Explain Greece's way of teaching this.
**Answer: the kinds of animal tissues as their own topic observing plant and animal tissues (practical companion) tissues as a named topic (Lykeio treatment)**
