# Animal tissues — epithelial, connective, muscle, nervous — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. the kinds of animal tissues as their own topic**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Greece's Gymnasio and Lykeio biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Βιολογία Β΄ & Γ΄ Γυμνασίου (Biologia B' & C' Gymnasiou) — Vivlio Mathiti, Grades 8–9).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with animal tissues — epithelial, connective, muscle, nervous.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is animal tissues — epithelial, connective, muscle, nervous.
