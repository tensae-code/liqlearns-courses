# Organisation of the Nervous System: Brain and Spinal Cord — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about types of the nervous system; the brain and its protection (§5.1.4):?**
**Answer: central and peripheral organisation; why central nervous tissue must be protected — bony cranium and vertebral column, the three meninges (dura mater, arachnoid mater, pia mater) with the subdural and subarachnoid spaces, and cerebrospinal fluid cushioning the brain, equalising pressure and exchanging nutrients and wastes; the brain composed of cerebrum, cerebellum and brainstem (medulla).**
