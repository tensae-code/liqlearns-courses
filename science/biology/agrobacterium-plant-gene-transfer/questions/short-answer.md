# Agrobacterium — plant gene transfer — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: the soil bacterium Agrobacterium naturally carries genes into plant cells on its plasmid, and genetic engineers use that same delivery system to give crop plants new genes. Agrobacterium can carry genes into plants**
