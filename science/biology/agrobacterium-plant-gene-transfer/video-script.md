Today: Agrobacterium — plant gene transfer.

Remember this one thing: the soil bacterium Agrobacterium naturally carries genes into plant cells on its plasmid, and genetic engineers use that same delivery system to give crop plants new genes.

Agrobacterium can carry genes into plants

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
