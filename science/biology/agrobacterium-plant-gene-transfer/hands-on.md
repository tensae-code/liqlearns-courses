# Hands-on — Agrobacterium — plant gene transfer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: the soil bacterium Agrobacterium naturally carries genes into plant cells on its plasmid, and genetic engineers use that same delivery system to give crop plants new genes.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a soil bacterium touching a plant cell, its small circular plasmid drawn travelling across into the plant cell's nucleus. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
