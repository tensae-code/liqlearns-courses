# Growing up — puberty and the changes it brings

## The idea
Adolescence is treated whole — physical change, and growing economic and moral responsibility too.

[Picture 21043 — a neutral growth timeline from child to adult]

## The key facts
**Primary.** Primary sexual characteristics are the reproductive organs themselves maturing.

**Secondary.** Secondary characteristics appear under sex hormones — voice, hair and body shape changes in each sex.

**Adolescence.** Adolescence covers physical, economic and moral adulthood and responsibility for actions.

**Hormones.** Sex hormones drive the secondary characteristics of puberty.
