# Growing up — puberty and the changes it brings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about primary?
a) Primary sexual characteristics are the reproductive organs themselves maturing
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Primary sexual characteristics are the reproductive organs themselves maturing**

**2.** What does this lesson say about secondary?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Secondary characteristics appear under sex hormones — voice, hair and body shape changes in each sex
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Secondary characteristics appear under sex hormones — voice, hair and body shape changes in each sex**

**3.** What does this lesson say about adolescence?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Adolescence covers physical, economic and moral adulthood and responsibility for actions
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Adolescence covers physical, economic and moral adulthood and responsibility for actions**

**4.** What does this lesson say about hormones?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Sex hormones drive the secondary characteristics of puberty
**Answer: d) Sex hormones drive the secondary characteristics of puberty**
