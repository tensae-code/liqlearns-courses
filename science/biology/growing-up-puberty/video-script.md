Today: Growing up — puberty and the changes it brings.

Remember this one thing: puberty matures the reproductive organs (primary characteristics) and brings secondary characteristics under sex hormones.

Primary sexual characteristics are the reproductive organs themselves maturing.

Secondary characteristics appear under sex hormones — voice, hair and body shape changes in each sex.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
