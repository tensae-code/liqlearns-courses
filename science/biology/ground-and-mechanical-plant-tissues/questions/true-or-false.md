# Ground and mechanical plant tissues — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. The flesh of a fruit is mostly parenchyma.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Mechanical tissue's cells are built for support and strength.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Sclerenchyma cells are alive and thin-walled.**
**Answer: False** — sclerenchyma cells are dead, with thick lignified walls.

**4. Parenchyma in the leaf is the main food-making tissue.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
