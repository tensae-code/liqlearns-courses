# Ground and mechanical plant tissues — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The living ground tissue of the plant is _____.**
**Answer: parenchyma**

**2. The plant's supporting skeleton is _____ tissue.**
**Answer: mechanical**

**3. Living mechanical tissue with thickened corners is _____.**
**Answer: collenchyma**

**4. Dead mechanical tissue with thick lignified walls is _____.**
**Answer: sclerenchyma**
