# Ground and mechanical plant tissues — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is parenchyma?**
a) Dead, hard supporting tissue  b) The living ground tissue that fills the plant and does its everyday work  c) The tissue that carries water  d) The dividing tissue at the tips
**Answer: b) The living ground tissue that fills the plant and does its everyday work**

**2. Which jobs belong to parenchyma?**
a) Support against bending only  b) Photosynthesis in leaves and storage in roots and seeds  c) Dividing to make new cells  d) Carrying water up the stem
**Answer: b) Photosynthesis in leaves and storage in roots and seeds**

**3. What is mechanical tissue for?**
a) Making food  b) Storing water  c) Support and strength — the plant's skeleton  d) Absorbing minerals
**Answer: c) Support and strength — the plant's skeleton**

**4. How do collenchyma and sclerenchyma differ?**
a) Collenchyma is dead; sclerenchyma is alive  b) Collenchyma is living and flexible in growing parts; sclerenchyma is dead with thick lignified walls  c) They are the same tissue  d) Sclerenchyma photosynthesises; collenchyma stores
**Answer: b) Collenchyma is living and flexible in growing parts; sclerenchyma is dead with thick lignified walls**
