# Ground and mechanical plant tissues — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining what a plant would lose if it had no mechanical tissue, and what it would lose with no parenchyma.
**Model answer: Without mechanical tissue the plant could not hold itself up — stems would bend and break under their own weight and the wind. Without parenchyma there would be no living filling to make and store food — the frame would stand empty and starve. A plant needs both its fill and its frame.**
