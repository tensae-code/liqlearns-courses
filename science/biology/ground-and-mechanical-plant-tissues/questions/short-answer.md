# Ground and mechanical plant tissues — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: parenchyma and mechanical tissue as fill and frame.
**Answer: Parenchyma is the living ground tissue — it fills the soft parts and does the everyday work: photosynthesis in the leaf, storage in roots, stems and seeds. Mechanical tissue is the frame — collenchyma and sclerenchyma — giving support and strength. Together they make a working body: filling that feeds, framing that holds.**

**2.** Explain: North Macedonia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: North Macedonia's Biology II teaches the permanent tissues in order — parenchyma (Theme 3.3) and mechanical tissue (Theme 3.4, printed pp. 190–194) as their own lesson pair, after meristem and permanent tissues and before conducting tissue.**
