# Ground and mechanical plant tissues — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Among the permanent tissues, parenchyma is the ground tissue — living, thin-walled cells that fill the plant and do its everyday work of photosynthesis and storage — while mechanical tissue is the plant's skeleton: collenchyma and sclerenchyma, built to hold the plant up.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a fresh example from the learner's own surroundings and saying why it fits, without looking.
