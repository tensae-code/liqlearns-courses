# Learning from nature — biomimicry

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — learning from nature. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Nature is presented as a store of workable structures and mechanisms; scientists and engineers learn by imitating the structures, shapes, materials and functions of organisms, sketching the biology first, then designing and tuning a technology at large (macro) and very small (nano) scales.
