# Learning from nature — biomimicry — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about learning from nature?**
**Answer: Nature is presented as a store of workable structures and mechanisms; scientists and engineers learn by imitating the structures, shapes, materials and functions of organisms, sketching the biology first, then designing and tuning a technology at large (macro) and very small (nano) scales.**
