# Hands-on — Growth curves — sigmoid and intermittent growth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Measure a growing seedling (or a young plant) at the same time each day for a week and plot height against day on paper. Say aloud which part of a sigmoid curve your points are on. Then draw, beside it, the staircase curve an insect would give, and mark each step 'moult'.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two growth graphs side by side: a smooth S-shaped sigmoid curve, and a stepped staircase curve labelled with moults — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
