# Growth curves — sigmoid and intermittent growth — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** An insect's growth curve is a smooth S-shape.
**Answer: False** — It is stepped — growth happens in jumps at each moult, because the hard outer skeleton cannot stretch.

**2.** A sigmoid growth curve is slow at first, fast in the middle, then levels off.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Marking experiments can show where a root grows fastest.
**Answer: True**
