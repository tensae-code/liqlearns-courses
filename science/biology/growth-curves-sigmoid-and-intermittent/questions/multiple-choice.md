# Growth curves — sigmoid and intermittent growth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What shape is the growth curve of most organisms?
a) A straight line
b) An S-shaped sigmoid curve
c) A staircase
d) A circle
**Answer: b) An S-shaped sigmoid curve**

**2.** Why is an insect's growth curve stepped?
a) Insects grow only at night
b) The hard outer skeleton cannot stretch, so growth happens in jumps at each moult
c) Insects stop eating between moults
d) The curve is drawn wrong
**Answer: b) The hard outer skeleton cannot stretch, so growth happens in jumps at each moult**

**3.** What do marking experiments on a root show?
a) The root's colour
b) Which regions grow fastest — where the marks spread furthest apart
c) How much water the root holds
d) The root's age in years
**Answer: b) Which regions grow fastest — where the marks spread furthest apart**

**4.** What happens at the top of a sigmoid curve?
a) Growth speeds up forever
b) Growth levels off at full size
c) The organism shrinks
d) Growth restarts from zero
**Answer: b) Growth levels off at full size**
