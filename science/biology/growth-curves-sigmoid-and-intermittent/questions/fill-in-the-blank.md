# Growth curves — sigmoid and intermittent growth — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** The S-shaped growth curve is called the _____ curve.
**Answer: sigmoid**

**2.** An insect grows in jumps because it must _____ its hard outer skeleton.
**Answer: moult (shed)**

**3.** Growth that happens in steps, with level stretches between, is called _____ growth.
**Answer: intermittent**
