# Growth curves — sigmoid and intermittent growth — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: sigmoid against intermittent growth.
**Answer: Sigmoid growth is drawn as a smooth S-curve: slow, then fast, then levelling off at full size. Intermittent growth — the insect's — is a stepped curve: the hard outer skeleton cannot stretch, so the insect grows only in jumps when it moults, and the graph climbs like stairs.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book teaches growth in Chapter 4 (printed p. 56) by measuring and graphing it: the sigmoid curve, the stepped growth curve of insects (Fig. 4.12), the regions of fastest growth in root and stem found by marking experiments, and the environmental factors growth needs.**
