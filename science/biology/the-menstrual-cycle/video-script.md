Today: The menstrual cycle — a monthly rhythm.

Remember this one thing: the monthly cycle coordinates ovary and uterus: flow, follicle growth, ovulation, lining growth.

Day 1 is the start of the flow phase — shedding of the uterus lining with some blood.

FSH matures a follicle in the follicular phase.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
