# The menstrual cycle — a monthly rhythm — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about day 1?
a) Day 1 is the start of the flow phase — shedding of the uterus lining with some blood
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Day 1 is the start of the flow phase — shedding of the uterus lining with some blood**

**2.** What does this lesson say about follicular phase?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) FSH matures a follicle in the follicular phase
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) FSH matures a follicle in the follicular phase**

**3.** What does this lesson say about ovulation?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Ovulation releases the egg mid-cycle
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Ovulation releases the egg mid-cycle**

**4.** What does this lesson say about progesterone?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Progesterone thickens the uterus lining to receive a pregnancy
**Answer: d) Progesterone thickens the uterus lining to receive a pregnancy**
