# The menstrual cycle — a monthly rhythm

## Do it first

## The idea
Day 1 is the start of the flow phase. If no pregnancy begins, the lining is shed and the cycle restarts.

## The key facts
**Day 1.** Day 1 is the start of the flow phase — shedding of the uterus lining with some blood.

**Follicular phase.** FSH matures a follicle in the follicular phase.

**Ovulation.** Ovulation releases the egg mid-cycle.

**Progesterone.** Progesterone thickens the uterus lining to receive a pregnancy.
