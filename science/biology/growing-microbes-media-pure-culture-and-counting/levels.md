# Growing microbes — media, pure culture and counting — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (microbes are grown on media built from a carbon source, a nitrogen source, salts and water, tuned for pH and oxygen, and a pure culture is won by streak plate or dilution spread plate — then counted by colonies on the 30–300 rule, knowing the count runs low because chained and clumped cells count once) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
