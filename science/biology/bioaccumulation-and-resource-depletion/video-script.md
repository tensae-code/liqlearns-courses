# Video script — Bioaccumulation and resource depletion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Persistent toxic substances (DDT, halogenated chemicals, lead, mercury) build up in fatty tissue and concentrate up the food chain — DDT thinning the eggshells of birds of prey is the worked case; resource depletion, consumption outrunning recovery, is driven by overpopulation, poor farming (bad irrigation and soil care, heavy machinery, excess pesticides killing soil microbes) and overconsumption.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
