# Bioaccumulation and resource depletion — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about toxic bioaccumulation and resource depletion?**
**Answer: Persistent toxic substances (DDT, halogenated chemicals, lead, mercury) build up in fatty tissue and concentrate up the food chain — DDT thinning the eggshells of birds of prey is the worked case; resource depletion, consumption outrunning recovery, is driven by overpopulation, poor farming (bad irrigation and soil care, heavy machinery, excess pesticides killing soil microbes) and overconsumption.**
