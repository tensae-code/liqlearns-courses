# Agroecosystems and sustainable agriculture — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Moldova's Grade 12 book (4.5 Artificial ecosystems: agroecosystems) teaches: sedentary life and domestication as the origin of artificial ecosystems; sustainable agriculture needing ecological technologies and rational crop rotation. (printed p. 143).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Moldova's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Biologie: Manual pentru clasa a 12-a (Grade 12)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with agroecosystems and sustainable agriculture.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is agroecosystems and sustainable agriculture.
