# Agroecosystems and sustainable agriculture — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: agroecosystems are artificial ecosystems that began when sedentary life domesticated plants and animals, and sustainable agriculture keeps them productive with ecological technologies and rational crop rotation. Moldova's Grade 12 book (4.5 Artificial ecosystems: agroecosystems) teaches: sedentary life and domestication as the origin of artificial ecosystems; sustainable agriculture needing ecological technologies and rational crop rotation. (printed p. 143).**

**2.** Explain Moldova's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Moldova's Grade 12 book teaches agroecosystems as artificial ecosystems: sedentary life and domestication as their origin, and sustainable agriculture needing ecological technologies and rational crop rotation.**
