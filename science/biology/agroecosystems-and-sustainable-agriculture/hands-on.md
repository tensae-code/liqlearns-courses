# Hands-on — Agroecosystems and sustainable agriculture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: agroecosystems are artificial ecosystems that began when sedentary life domesticated plants and animals, and sustainable agriculture keeps them productive with ecological technologies and rational crop rotation.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a farm field laid out as an agroecosystem — crops, animals and farmer together — with a crop-rotation plan drawn beside it. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
