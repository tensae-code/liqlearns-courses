Today: Agroecosystems and sustainable agriculture.

Remember this one thing: agroecosystems are artificial ecosystems that began when sedentary life domesticated plants and animals, and sustainable agriculture keeps them productive with ecological technologies and rational crop rotation.

Moldova's Grade 12 book (4.5 Artificial ecosystems: agroecosystems) teaches: sedentary life and domestication as the origin of artificial ecosystems; sustainable agriculture needing ecological technologies and rational crop rotation. (printed p. 143).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
