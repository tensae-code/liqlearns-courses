# How exocrine glands are controlled — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Exocrine glands pour their products out through ducts — saliva, sweat, digestive juices — and their work is switched and timed by the nervous system and by hormones, so secretion happens when and where it is needed.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a fresh example from the learner's own surroundings and saying why it fits, without looking.
