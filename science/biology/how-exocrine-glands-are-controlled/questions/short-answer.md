# How exocrine glands are controlled — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the two control routes of exocrine glands.
**Answer: Nervous control is fast and direct: reflexes switch glands like the salivary and sweat glands in moments. Hormonal control is timed and sequential: hormones carried in the blood order the gut's glands in turn as food passes, so each secretion arrives at the right stage. Together they make secretion happen when and where it is needed.**

**2.** Explain: North Macedonia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: North Macedonia's Grade 13 elective textbook closes its regulation chapter with the functioning of the exocrine glands as its own topic (Ch. 4.11, printed pp. 232–239), after endocrine regulation and the regulation of reproduction — the ducted glands' control taught after the ductless glands'.**
