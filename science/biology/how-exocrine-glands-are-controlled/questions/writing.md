# How exocrine glands are controlled — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences contrasting exocrine and endocrine glands and their control.
**Model answer: Exocrine glands pour their products through ducts onto surfaces or into cavities — saliva, sweat, digestive juices — while endocrine glands release hormones into the blood without ducts. Exocrine secretion is switched by fast nervous reflexes and by timed hormonal signals, so each product arrives exactly when and where it is called for.**
