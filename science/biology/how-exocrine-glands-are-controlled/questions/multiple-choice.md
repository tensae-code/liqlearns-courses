# How exocrine glands are controlled — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What makes a gland exocrine?**
a) It releases hormones into the blood  b) It releases its product through a duct onto a surface or into a cavity  c) It has no product  d) It is found only in the brain
**Answer: b) It releases its product through a duct onto a surface or into a cavity**

**2. Which of these is an exocrine gland?**
a) The thyroid  b) The salivary gland  c) The pituitary  d) The adrenal gland
**Answer: b) The salivary gland**

**3. Your mouth waters at the thought of a lemon. Which control route acted?**
a) Hormonal — slowly, through the blood  b) Nervous — a fast reflex order to the salivary glands  c) No control — it is chance  d) The duct itself
**Answer: b) Nervous — a fast reflex order to the salivary glands**

**4. Why are the gut's digestive juices well suited to hormonal control?**
a) Hormones are faster than nerves  b) The juices must arrive in sequence as food passes — hormones time the sequence  c) Hormones are made in the gut only  d) Nerves cannot reach the gut
**Answer: b) The juices must arrive in sequence as food passes — hormones time the sequence**
