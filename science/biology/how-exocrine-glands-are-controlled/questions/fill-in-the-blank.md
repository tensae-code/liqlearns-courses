# How exocrine glands are controlled — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. A gland that pours its product through a duct is an _____ gland.**
**Answer: exocrine**

**2. Saliva reaches the mouth through a _____.**
**Answer: duct**

**3. Fast orders to glands travel by the _____ system.**
**Answer: nervous**

**4. Timed, sequential orders to the gut's glands travel as _____.**
**Answer: hormones**
