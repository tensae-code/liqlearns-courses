# How exocrine glands are controlled — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Endocrine glands release their products through ducts.**
**Answer: False** — endocrine glands release hormones into the blood, with no duct; ducts are the exocrine way.

**2. Sweat glands are exocrine glands.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Exocrine glands secrete at a constant, unchanging rate.**
**Answer: False** — secretion is switched and timed — by nerves and by hormones.

**4. The pancreas has an exocrine part that pours digestive juice through a duct.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
