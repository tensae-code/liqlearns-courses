# Hands-on — How exocrine glands are controlled

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Test the reflex yourself: think hard of biting into a lemon. Notice your mouth watering — that is nervous control of an exocrine gland, arriving before any food. Write down what you observed, which gland answered, and which control route (nerve or hormone) must have carried the order, and why.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a salivary gland with its duct opening into the mouth, arrows running to it from a nerve and from a blood vessel carrying a hormone — the two control routes arriving at one gland, and drops of saliva leaving by the duct — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
