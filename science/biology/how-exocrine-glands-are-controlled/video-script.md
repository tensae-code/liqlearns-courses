Today: How exocrine glands are controlled.

Remember this one thing: Exocrine glands pour their products out through ducts — saliva, sweat, digestive juices — and their work is switched and timed by the nervous system and by hormones, so secretion happens when and where it is needed.

Some glands pour through ducts: saliva into the mouth, sweat onto the skin, juices into the gut. These are the exocrine glands, and they do not pour steadily.

They are switched. Nerves give the fast orders — your mouth waters at the thought of a lemon. Hormones give the timed orders — the gut's juices arrive in sequence as food passes. Two control routes, one ducted gland.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
