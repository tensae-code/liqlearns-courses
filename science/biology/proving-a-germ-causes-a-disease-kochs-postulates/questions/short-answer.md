# Proving a germ causes a disease — Koch's postulates — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added disease detail?**
**Answer: Dead animals decay because microorganisms convert dead matter back to inorganic substances; gut bacteria's vitamin K-2 supports blood clotting and bone health and may protect against some cancers and heart disease; Koch's four postulates for proving a microbe causes a disease are stated in full (present in all diseased and no healthy animals; grown in pure culture; the culture causes the disease in healthy susceptible animals; re-isolated from the newly diseased animals); and an antibiotic-producer list is begun (penicillins from Penicillium, cephalosporins from Cephalosporium, carbapenems and aminoglycosides from Streptomyces/Micromonospora, ansamycin from Streptomyces mediterranei).**
