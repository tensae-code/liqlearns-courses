# Video script — Proving a germ causes a disease — Koch's postulates

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dead animals decay because microorganisms convert dead matter back to inorganic substances; gut bacteria's vitamin K-2 supports blood clotting and bone health and may protect against some cancers and heart disease; Koch's four postulates for proving a microbe causes a disease are stated in full (present in all diseased and no healthy animals; grown in pure culture; the culture causes the disease in healthy susceptible animals; re-isolated from the newly diseased animals); and an antibiotic-producer list is begun (penicillins from Penicillium, cephalosporins from Cephalosporium, carbapenems and aminoglycosides from Streptomyces/Micromonospora, ansamycin from Streptomyces mediterranei).

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
