# Choosing a healthy lifestyle — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (a healthy lifestyle is a plan a person designs and lives by — balanced meals, exercise, regular hours, no smoking or drinking, sensible screen time and group activities — and keeps long-term, not just for one week) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
