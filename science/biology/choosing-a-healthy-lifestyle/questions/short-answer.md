# Choosing a healthy lifestyle — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: a healthy lifestyle is a plan a person designs and lives by — balanced meals, exercise, regular hours, no smoking or drinking, sensible screen time and group activities — and keeps long-term, not just for one week. pupils design and carry out "a week of healthy living" (balanced meals, exercise, regular hours, no smoking or drinking, sensible screen time, group activities) and keep the habits long-term; the chapter's healthy-lifestyle content is a plan to live by, not a disease topic, and no existing lesson holds it.**
