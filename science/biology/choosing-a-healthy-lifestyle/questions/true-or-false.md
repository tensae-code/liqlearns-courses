# Choosing a healthy lifestyle — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. pupils design and carry out "a week of healthy living" (balanced meals, exercise, regular hours, no smoking or drinking, sensible screen time, group activities) and keep the habits long-term; the chapter's healthy-lifestyle content is a plan to live by, not a disease topic, and no existing lesson holds it.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in China's People's Education Press biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (义务教育教科书《生物学》八年级下册 (Biology, Grade 8, Volume 2, People's Education Press, 2013)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with choosing a healthy lifestyle.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is choosing a healthy lifestyle.
