# Hands-on — Choosing a healthy lifestyle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: a healthy lifestyle is a plan a person designs and lives by — balanced meals, exercise, regular hours, no smoking or drinking, sensible screen time and group activities — and keeps long-term, not just for one week.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a one-week planner page filled in with meals, exercise, sleep times and group activities, with a tick showing the week was actually lived. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
