# Choosing a healthy lifestyle

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Choosing a healthy lifestyle. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
A healthy lifestyle is a plan a person designs and lives by — balanced meals, exercise, regular hours, no smoking or drinking, sensible screen time and group activities — and keeps long-term, not just for one week. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from China's People's Education Press biology textbooks, which teach it as a named topic.
