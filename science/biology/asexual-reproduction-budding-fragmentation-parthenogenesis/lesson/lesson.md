# Asexual reproduction — budding, fragmentation, parthenogenesis

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — asexual reproduction. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Reproduction from a single individual with no fusion of gametes, commoner in invertebrates than vertebrates: budding and fragmentation, especially in aquatic animals, and parthenogenesis, in which an unfertilised egg develops into offspring, found in some insects and vertebrates.
