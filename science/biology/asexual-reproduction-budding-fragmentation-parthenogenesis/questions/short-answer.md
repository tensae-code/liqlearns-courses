# Asexual reproduction — budding, fragmentation, parthenogenesis — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about asexual reproduction in animals?**
**Answer: Reproduction from a single individual with no fusion of gametes, commoner in invertebrates than vertebrates: budding and fragmentation, especially in aquatic animals, and parthenogenesis, in which an unfertilised egg develops into offspring, found in some insects and vertebrates.**
