# Earthworms and the soil community

## Do it first

## The idea
Under your feet is a workforce. Earthworms and their neighbours tunnel, mix and enrich the soil — the living machinery of soil fertility.

[Picture 22274 — a soil cross-section with earthworms tunnelling through it]

## The key facts
**Soil organisms.** Larger soil organisms such as earthworms live and work in the soil.

**Mixing and aerating.** Earthworms mix and aerate the soil as they tunnel.

**Enriching.** Soil organisms enrich the soil the cycles depend on.

**Fertility machinery.** Their role is the living machinery of soil fertility.
