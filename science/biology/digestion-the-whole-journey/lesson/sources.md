**Book 9:** Kalan preparointi: yläkoulun video ja oppimateriaalit — kalan preparointi opettajan ohje (Fish dissection — teacher's guide, lower secondary) — Finland. Grade: grade-07 (guide states grades 7–9) — Kind: teacher guide (NOT a textbook) as verified — Repo path: grade-07/gov/finland/biology/teacher/finland-g07-aoe1063-kalan-preparointi-opettajan-ohje.pdf — Byte size: 1,355,171 (matches record) — PDF pages: 7 — Printed-page offset: 0. Verdict: COMPLETE (all 7 pages read, incl. pp. 6–7 model answers with the full cut-by-cut dissection sequence). Structural summary: The grades 7–9 version of the perch-dissection lesson, deeper than the primary guide. Adds: countercurrent gas exchange in the gills (water and blood flowing in opposite directions), the two-chambered fish heart pumping oxygen-poor blood to the gills, stomach-content investigation (perch are predators — smaller fish may be found inside), and a dedicated eye dissection — the fish lens is round and pearl-like, bends light more strongly than the human lens, and focuses by moving in and out rather than changing shape. Model answers give the exact incision sequence (belly cut from vent to lower jaw, flap cut toward the lateral line, gill cover removed) and organ-finding order (heart under the gills, liver, stomach, intestine to the vent, gonad, swim bladder against the spine).
