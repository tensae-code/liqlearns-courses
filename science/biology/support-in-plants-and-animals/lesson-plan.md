# Lesson plan — Support in plants and animals

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Support in plants and animals
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — appended to Unit 16 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** living things need support — a plant must hold itself up towards the light and an animal must hold its body up against gravity — and Sudan's Grade 10 book gives support in plants and animals a whole unit of its own, Unit Five: Support in Living Organisms, printed pp. 137–149.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands next to the movement lessons — movement is covered in the lesson before it in Sudan's book (Unit Four) — and adds the thing those lessons do not teach on its own: support itself. The musculoskeletal system at a glance is recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: **The musculoskeletal system at a glance**. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say, in their own words, the one thing this lesson teaches: living things need support, in plants and in animals, and Sudan teaches support as a whole unit.
2. Say what support does for a plant: it holds the plant up, so its leaves reach the light and its parts stay in place.
3. Say what support does for an animal: it holds the body up and gives the muscles something to pull against.
4. Explain Sudan's way as the placements describe it: support is the closing unit (Unit Five) of the Grade 10 book, after the movement unit, covering support in plants and support in animals, printed pp. 137–149.

## What you need

- Go back to the one thing this lesson teaches, and re-read the key facts slowly, one at a time.
- Use the picture: for the plant and for the animal, say out loud what would happen without support.
