Today: Support in plants and animals.

Remember this one thing: every living thing needs support — something that holds it up.

A plant holds itself up with its own grown body. Its stem keeps it upright, spreads its leaves towards the light, and holds its flowers and fruits in place.

An animal is held up by its supporting structures. They keep the body's shape, protect what is soft inside, and give the muscles something firm to pull against.

Sudan's Grade 10 biology book gives support a whole unit — Unit Five, Support in Living Organisms — right after its unit on movement, covering plants and animals together.

Same need. Two ways of meeting it: the plant stands still and grows its support; the animal carries its support as it moves.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
