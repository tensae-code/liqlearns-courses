# Support in plants and animals — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (living things need support — in plants and in animals — and Sudan's Grade 10 book gives support a whole unit, Unit Five, printed pp. 137–149) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: naming what holds a named plant or animal up, and explaining why Sudan places support after movement in its book, without looking.
