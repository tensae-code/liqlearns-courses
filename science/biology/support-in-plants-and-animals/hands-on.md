# Hands-on — Support in plants and animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find a growing plant and look at how it holds itself up — stem, leaves spread to the light. Then stand up yourself and feel what holds you up as you stand and walk. Say aloud, for the plant and for yourself, what is doing the supporting and what would happen without it.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a plant holding itself upright beside an animal standing on its legs, both labelled "support" — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
