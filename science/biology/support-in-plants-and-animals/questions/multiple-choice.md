# Support in plants and animals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is support, in this lesson?
a) The way a living thing holds itself up and keeps its parts in place
b) The food a living thing eats
c) The place where a living thing lives
d) The young a living thing produces
**Answer: a) The way a living thing holds itself up and keeps its parts in place**

**2.** What does support do for a plant?
a) It makes the plant's seeds
b) It holds the plant up so its leaves reach the light
c) It carries water from the soil
d) It attracts insects to the flowers
**Answer: b) It holds the plant up so its leaves reach the light**

**3.** What does support do for an animal?
a) It digests the animal's food
b) It pumps the animal's blood
c) It holds the body up and gives the muscles something to pull against
d) It keeps the animal warm
**Answer: c) It holds the body up and gives the muscles something to pull against**

**4.** Where does Sudan's Grade 10 book teach support?
a) In a whole closing unit — Unit Five: Support in Living Organisms — after the movement unit
b) Only in a footnote to the cell unit
c) It does not teach support
d) In the classification unit only
**Answer: a) In a whole closing unit — Unit Five: Support in Living Organisms — after the movement unit**
