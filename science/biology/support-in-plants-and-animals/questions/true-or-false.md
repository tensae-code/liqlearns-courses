# Support in plants and animals — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Both plants and animals need support.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A plant needs no support because it does not move from place to place.
**Answer: False** — a plant must hold itself up so its leaves reach the light; without support it would flop to the ground.

**3.** In Sudan's Grade 10 book, support is a whole unit taught after movement.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Support in an animal has nothing to do with movement.
**Answer: False** — the supporting structures give the muscles something firm to pull against, so support and movement work together.
