# Ethiopia's population — and survivorship curves

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — ethiopia's population. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Ethiopia's growth is given in census figures (42.6 million in 1984, 53.5 in 1994, 73.5 in 2007, 83.7 in 2012, over 110 million estimated at writing), a young population (45% under 15 in 2007) with its 1984 and 2017 pyramids compared; survivorship curves close the section — Type I (few young, high care, deaths late — humans), Type II (steady mortality) and Type III (many young, most dying early).
