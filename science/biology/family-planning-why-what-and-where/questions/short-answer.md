# Family planning — why, what, and where — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about family planning?**
**Answer: Planning the number, spacing and timing of children: risks of no planning include dangers of early and late motherhood, illness in mothers threatening the whole family, and complications of pregnancy and childbirth; planning actions include preventing unwanted pregnancy, STI protection, counselling and fertility management, waiting at least six months after miscarriage or abortion, and adoption; services run from pregnancy testing and counselling through infertility advice to breast and cervical screening.**
