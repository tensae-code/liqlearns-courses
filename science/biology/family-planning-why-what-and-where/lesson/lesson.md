# Family planning — why, what, and where

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — family planning. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Planning the number, spacing and timing of children: risks of no planning include dangers of early and late motherhood, illness in mothers threatening the whole family, and complications of pregnancy and childbirth; planning actions include preventing unwanted pregnancy, STI protection, counselling and fertility management, waiting at least six months after miscarriage or abortion, and adoption; services run from pregnancy testing and counselling through infertility advice to breast and cervical screening.
