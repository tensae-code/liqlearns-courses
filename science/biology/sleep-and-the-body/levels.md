# Sleep and the body — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Sleep is something the body does in stages — and it can be measured. Finland's wellbeing work cards have pupils measure their own sleep with a wearable: total sleep, deep sleep, REM sleep and awakenings through the night — and then learn what the stages do, because sleep drives next-day coping and concentration.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by reading a night's sleep record — total, deep, REM and awakenings — and saying what each part of it means, without looking.
