# Lesson plan — Sleep and the body

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Sleep and the body
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — secondary band (CURRICULUM.md Unit 16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Sleep is something the body does in stages — and it can be measured. Finland's wellbeing work cards have pupils measure their own sleep with a wearable: total sleep, deep sleep, REM sleep and awakenings through the night — and then learn what the stages do, because sleep drives next-day coping and concentration.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier biology lessons and builds the secondary-band understanding of sleep and the body.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
