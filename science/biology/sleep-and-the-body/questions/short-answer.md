# Sleep and the body — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain what Finland's sleep card asks a pupil to do.
**Answer: The pupil measures their own sleep with a wearable tracker — total sleep, deep sleep, REM sleep and awakenings — then reflects on the numbers and reads the theory of what the sleep stages do, in the pack's Measure → Reflect → Learn → Try cycle.**

**2.** Explain why sleep matters for the school day.
**Answer: The card teaches that sleep drives next-day coping and concentration: the stages and length of the night's sleep show the next day in how well a pupil manages the day and is able to concentrate.**
