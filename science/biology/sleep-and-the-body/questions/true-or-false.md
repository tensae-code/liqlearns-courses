# Sleep and the body — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. A night's sleep is one flat state with no stages.**
**Answer: False** — the tracker separates deep sleep and REM sleep and counts awakenings — the night has stages.

**2. REM sleep is one of the things Finland's sleep card measures.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. In the wellbeing pack, pupils' sleep results are ranked in a class competition.**
**Answer: False** — the pack's rules are explicit: measurements are voluntary, results are never compared competitively, and data is not collected centrally.

**4. Sleep affects how a pupil copes and concentrates the next day.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
