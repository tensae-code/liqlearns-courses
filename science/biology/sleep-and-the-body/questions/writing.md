# Sleep and the body — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining what a sleep tracker measures and why the night's sleep matters for the next day.
**Model answer: A sleep tracker measures the whole night: total sleep, deep sleep, REM sleep and the awakenings in between. The night matters because sleep drives next-day coping and concentration — a pupil who has slept in full stages manages the day and concentrates better than one whose night was broken.**
