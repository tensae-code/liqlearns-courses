# Sleep and the body — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. On Finland's sleep card, what does the wearable measure?**
a) Only the time the pupil went to bed  b) Total sleep, deep sleep, REM sleep and awakenings  c) Dreams and their meanings  d) Blood pressure during sleep
**Answer: b) Total sleep, deep sleep, REM sleep and awakenings**

**2. What does sleep drive, in the card's teaching?**
a) Appetite only  b) Hair growth  c) Next-day coping and concentration  d) Height in adults
**Answer: c) Next-day coping and concentration**

**3. Which of these is a stage the tracker separates out?**
a) Dreamless coma  b) Deep sleep  c) Day sleep  d) Half sleep
**Answer: b) Deep sleep**

**4. What are the wellbeing pack's rules for its measurements?**
a) Compulsory, ranked and published  b) Voluntary, never compared competitively, and not collected centrally  c) Done only by teachers  d) Sold to the tracker company
**Answer: b) Voluntary, never compared competitively, and not collected centrally**
