# Video script — Sleep and the body

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Sleep and the body.

Remember this one thing: sleep is work the body does at night, it comes in stages, and the stages show in the next day.

Finland's pupils do not only read this — they measure it. A wearable tracker records the night: the total sleep, the deep sleep, the REM sleep, and the awakenings in between.

Then comes the learning: sleep drives next-day coping and concentration. A night that is measured can be understood — and a night that is understood can be improved.

And the pack's rules protect the pupils: measuring is voluntary, results are never compared in competition, and no one's data is collected centrally.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
