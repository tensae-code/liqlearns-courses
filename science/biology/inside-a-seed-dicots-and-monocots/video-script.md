# Video script — Inside a seed — dicots and monocots

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Inside a seed — dicots and monocots.

Remember this one thing: a seed has a coat, a food store and an embryo — and dicot and monocot seeds differ point by point

Seed coat: The coat protects the seed from outside.

Food store: The seed carries a food store for the young plant.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
