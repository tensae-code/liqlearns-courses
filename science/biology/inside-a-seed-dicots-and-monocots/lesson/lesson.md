# Inside a seed — dicots and monocots

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Inside a seed — dicots and monocots. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Open a seed. Inside are the coat, the food store and the embryo — the young plant itself. Then set a bean (dicot) against maize (monocot) and compare them point by point.

[Picture 22213 — a bean seed opened beside a maize grain, parts labelled]

## The key facts
**Seed coat.** The coat protects the seed from outside.

**Food store.** The seed carries a food store for the young plant.

**Embryo.** The embryo is the young plant inside the seed.

**Dicot vs monocot.** Dicot and monocot seeds differ point by point, including their food-store leaves.
