# Inside a seed — dicots and monocots — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about seed coat?
a) The coat protects the seed from outside  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The coat protects the seed from outside**

**2.** What does this lesson say about food store?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) The seed carries a food store for the young plant  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The seed carries a food store for the young plant**

**3.** What does this lesson say about embryo?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) The embryo is the young plant inside the seed  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) The embryo is the young plant inside the seed**

**4.** What does this lesson say about dicot vs monocot?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Dicot and monocot seeds differ point by point, including their food-store leaves
**Answer: d) Dicot and monocot seeds differ point by point, including their food-store leaves**
