# DNA in mitochondria and chloroplasts — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: mitochondria and chloroplasts carry their own small circular DNA — a leftover of their origin as free bacteria — so they make some of their own proteins and, in animals, are inherited through the egg alone. mitochondria and chloroplasts have their own genetic material**

**2.** Explain Greece's way of teaching this.
**Answer: mitochondria and chloroplasts have their own genetic material**
