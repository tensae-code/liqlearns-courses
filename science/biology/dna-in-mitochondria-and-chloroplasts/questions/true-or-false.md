# DNA in mitochondria and chloroplasts — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. mitochondria and chloroplasts have their own genetic material**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Greece's Gymnasio and Lykeio biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Βιολογία Γ΄ Γενικού Λυκείου — Τεύχος Β΄ (Grade 12 orientation volume B)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with dna in mitochondria and chloroplasts.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is dna in mitochondria and chloroplasts.
