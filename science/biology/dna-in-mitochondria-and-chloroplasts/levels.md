# DNA in mitochondria and chloroplasts — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (mitochondria and chloroplasts carry their own small circular DNA — a leftover of their origin as free bacteria — so they make some of their own proteins and, in animals, are inherited through the egg alone) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
