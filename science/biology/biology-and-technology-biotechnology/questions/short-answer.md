# Biology + technology = biotechnology — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about biology, technology and biotechnology defined?**
**Answer: Biology is the study of living organisms; technology is the application of scientific knowledge, skills and processes to make devices and tools; their blend is biotechnology — using biological systems, organisms, cells or their parts to make useful products and services, with yeast making bread and beer as the everyday example.**
