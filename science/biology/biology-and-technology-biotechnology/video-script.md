# Video script — Biology + technology = biotechnology

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Biology is the study of living organisms; technology is the application of scientific knowledge, skills and processes to make devices and tools; their blend is biotechnology — using biological systems, organisms, cells or their parts to make useful products and services, with yeast making bread and beer as the everyday example.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
