# Alternation of generations — the Funaria life cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. In Funaria, the dominant generation is the…**
a) diploid sporophyte  b) haploid gametophyte  c) seed  d) flower
**Answer: b) haploid gametophyte**

**2. The antherozoids reach the archegonium by…**
a) the wind  b) flying insects  c) swimming in a drop of water  d) growing a pollen tube
**Answer: c) swimming in a drop of water**

**3. What attracts the antherozoids to the archegonium's neck?**
a) light  b) sugar  c) heat  d) salt
**Answer: b) sugar**

**4. The spores of Funaria are formed in the…**
a) archegonium  b) antheridium  c) capsule of the sporophyte  d) rhizoid
**Answer: c) capsule of the sporophyte**
