# Alternation of generations — the Funaria life cycle — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The swimming male gametes of the moss are called _____.**
**Answer: antherozoids**

**2. The zygote grows into the _____ generation.**
**Answer: sporophyte**

**3. Spores are released from the sporophyte's _____.**
**Answer: capsule**

**4. The familiar green moss plant is the _____ generation.**
**Answer: gametophyte**
