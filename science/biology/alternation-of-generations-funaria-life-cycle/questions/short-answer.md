# Alternation of generations — the Funaria life cycle — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Why does the Funaria sporophyte count as a separate generation if it never leaves the gametophyte?
**Answer: Because it is built from the diploid zygote, while the moss plant is haploid: the cycle alternates a haploid gamete-making generation with a diploid spore-making generation. In Funaria the sporophyte simply stays attached to and dependent on the gametophyte instead of becoming an independent plant.**

**2.** Explain Mozambique's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Mozambique's PESD II plant module (Módulo 2 de Biologia), Lesson 4, works the full Funaria life cycle: antherozoids swimming in a drop of water to the archegonium, attracted by sugar in its neck; the zygote starting the diploid sporophyte, which stays dependent on the dominant haploid gametophyte; spores formed in the capsule; and vegetative fragmentation noted beside the sexual cycle.**
