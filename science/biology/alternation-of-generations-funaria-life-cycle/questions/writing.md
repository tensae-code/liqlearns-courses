# Alternation of generations — the Funaria life cycle — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining what "alternation of generations" means, using Funaria as the example.
**Model answer: Alternation of generations means the life cycle takes turns between two body forms: a haploid gametophyte that makes gametes and a diploid sporophyte that makes spores. In Funaria the green moss plant is the gametophyte; after its swimming antherozoids fertilise the egg, the zygote grows into the sporophyte, which lives on the gametophyte and releases spores from its capsule to start new gametophytes.**
