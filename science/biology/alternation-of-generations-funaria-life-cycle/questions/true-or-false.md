# Alternation of generations — the Funaria life cycle — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. The sporophyte of Funaria lives independently of the gametophyte.**
**Answer: False** — it stays dependent on the gametophyte.

**2. The zygote is the first cell of the sporophyte generation.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Funaria can also multiply by vegetative fragmentation.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. The green moss plant is diploid.**
**Answer: False** — the green plant is the haploid gametophyte.
