# Lesson plan — Alternation of generations — the Funaria life cycle

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Alternation of generations — the Funaria life cycle
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — secondary band (CURRICULUM.md Unit 16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In Funaria the generations alternate with the haploid gametophyte on top: the familiar moss plant is the gametophyte; its antherozoids swim through a drop of water to the archegonium, and the zygote grows into the diploid sporophyte, which stays dependent on the gametophyte and makes the spores in its capsule that start the cycle again.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier biology lessons and builds the secondary-band understanding of alternation of generations — the funaria life cycle.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
