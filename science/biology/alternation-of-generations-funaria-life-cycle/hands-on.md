# Hands-on — Alternation of generations — the Funaria life cycle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Draw the cycle as a circle on paper from memory: moss plant, swimming antherozoid, zygote, sporophyte on its stalk, capsule, spores — then check it against the lesson and mark which generation is haploid and which diploid, and which one depends on the other.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — the Funaria cycle drawn as a circle: the green gametophyte plant with antheridia and archegonia, a swimming antherozoid crossing a water drop, then the sporophyte rising from the gametophyte on a stalk with its capsule, and spores falling from the capsule back to the ground — label its parts, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
