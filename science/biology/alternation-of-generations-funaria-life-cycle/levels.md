# Alternation of generations — the Funaria life cycle — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (In Funaria the generations alternate with the haploid gametophyte on top: the familiar moss plant is the gametophyte; its antherozoids swim through a drop of water to the archegonium, and the zygote grows into the diploid sporophyte, which stays dependent on the gametophyte and makes the spores in its capsule that start the cycle again.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by walking the circle without looking — gametophyte, antherozoid swimming, zygote, dependent sporophyte, capsule spores — and stating at each step whether the cells are haploid or diploid.
