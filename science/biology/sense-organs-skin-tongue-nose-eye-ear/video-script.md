# Video script — Sense organs — skin, tongue, nose, eye, ear

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Skin disorders are introduced (vitiligo — pale patches from lost melanin made by melanocytes — figured; acne, cold sore and eczema named); the retina's rods and cones convert light energy into electrical impulses carried by the optic nerve, and colour blindness is set as a task; eye defects are corrected optically (short sight by concave/diverging lenses, long sight by convex lenses, astigmatism — an egg-shaped eyeball or lens — by specially shaped lenses); and the hearing answer walks sound waves through the ear canal to electrical signals on the auditory nerve.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
