# Civil defence and emergency safety — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (civil defence (الدفاع المدني) is the organised public protection of people in emergencies — and Iraq teaches it as biology-course content, as the closing chapter of its Grade 7 Biology book, in the safety unit it shares with first aid) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: applying the lesson to a new example from the learner's own surroundings, and explaining Iraq's way of teaching it, without looking.
