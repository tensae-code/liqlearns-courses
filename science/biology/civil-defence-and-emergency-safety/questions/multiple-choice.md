# Civil defence and emergency safety — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Civil defence is…
a) a kind of microscope
b) the organised public protection of people in emergencies
c) a chapter about plant cells
d) a blood group
**Answer: b) the organised public protection of people in emergencies**

**2.** In Iraq's Grade 7 book, civil defence is taught…
a) in the genetics unit
b) as the closing chapter, in the safety unit with first aid
c) only in a footnote
d) in the Grade 12 book only
**Answer: b) as the closing chapter, in the safety unit with first aid**

**3.** First aid and civil defence differ as…
a) first aid is for plants, civil defence for animals
b) first aid is one person's help to one injured person; civil defence is the organised service for a whole community's emergency
c) they are the same thing
d) civil defence is only for soldiers in wartime
**Answer: b) first aid is one person's help to one injured person; civil defence is the organised service for a whole community's emergency**
