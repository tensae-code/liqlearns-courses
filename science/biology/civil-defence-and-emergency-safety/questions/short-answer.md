# Civil defence and emergency safety — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: why Iraq's Grade 7 book puts civil defence inside Biology.
**Answer: The book's Unit 4 is a safety unit — first aid (Ch. 8) and civil defence (Ch. 9, printed p. 87) — taught inside the biology book: protecting living people, individually and as an organised public service, is filed as part of the science course, and the book closes with the civil-defence review.**
