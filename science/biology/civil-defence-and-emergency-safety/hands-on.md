# Hands-on — Civil defence and emergency safety

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Walk through your home or school and list, on paper, what is already arranged for an emergency: exits, an extinguisher if there is one, the number you would call, the place people would gather. Say aloud which parts are one person's job (first aid) and which are the organised side (civil defence).

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a civil-defence scene in simple shapes: a uniformed rescue worker directing people to safety while a first-aider helps one injured person — the organised protection and the individual help side by side — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
