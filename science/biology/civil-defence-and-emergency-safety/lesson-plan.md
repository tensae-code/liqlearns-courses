# Lesson plan — Civil defence and emergency safety

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Civil defence and emergency safety
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — appended to Unit 16 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** civil defence (الدفاع المدني) is the organised public protection of people in emergencies — and Iraq teaches it as biology-course content, as the closing chapter of its Grade 7 Biology book, in the safety unit it shares with first aid.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own as a single-skill lesson built from Iraq's Grade 7 Biology textbook, as cited in lesson/sources.md. Related lessons are recommended first where they help, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- No forced prerequisite. Open it directly; the lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say, in their own words, the one thing this lesson teaches: civil defence (الدفاع المدني) is the organised public protection of people in emergencies — and Iraq teaches it as biology-course content, as the closing chapter of its Grade 7 Biology book, in the safety unit it shares with first aid.
2. Explain each key fact (What civil defence is; First aid's partner; The closing chapter; Iraq's way) in their own words.
3. Explain Iraq's way as the placements describe it.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

1. **Do it first** — the learner tries the opening doing task before any explanation (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the idea, then the key facts one by one (lesson/lesson.md).

4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, fill-in-the-blank, short-answer (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
