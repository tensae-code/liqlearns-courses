# The microscope — seeing the small world

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The microscope — seeing the small world. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Some living things are too small to see. The light microscope opened that small world, and whole sub-fields — microbiology and cell biology — stand on what it reveals.

[Picture 22202 — a labelled light microscope in use, with a slide under its lens]

## The key facts
**The tool.** The light microscope is introduced as the tool that opened the small world.

**What it opened.** With it, biologists could explore living things too small for the naked eye.

**Fields behind it.** Microbiology and cell biology stand on what the microscope reveals.

**Its place.** In this unit the microscope is the instrument behind the renowned biologists' small-world discoveries.
