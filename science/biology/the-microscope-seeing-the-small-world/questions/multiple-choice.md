# The microscope — seeing the small world — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about the tool?
a) The light microscope is introduced as the tool that opened the small world  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The light microscope is introduced as the tool that opened the small world**

**2.** What does this lesson say about what it opened?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) With it, biologists could explore living things too small for the naked eye  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) With it, biologists could explore living things too small for the naked eye**

**3.** What does this lesson say about fields behind it?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Microbiology and cell biology stand on what the microscope reveals  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Microbiology and cell biology stand on what the microscope reveals**

**4.** What does this lesson say about its place?
