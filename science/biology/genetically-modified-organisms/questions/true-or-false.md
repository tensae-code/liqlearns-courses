# Genetically modified organisms — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. genetically modified organisms as a named topic**

**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Βιολογία Β΄ & Γ΄ Γυμνασίου (Biologia B' & C' Gymnasiou) — Vivlio Mathiti, Grades 8–9).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with genetically modified organisms.**
