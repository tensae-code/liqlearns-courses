# Hands-on — Genetically modified organisms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: a genetically modified organism carries a gene deliberately moved into it — transgenic plants and animals made for production, which Greece's books teach as a named topic — with the new trait inherited by its offspring like any of its own genes.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a tomato plant with one glowing new gene segment marked in its DNA strip, and seedlings from its fruit carrying the same mark. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
