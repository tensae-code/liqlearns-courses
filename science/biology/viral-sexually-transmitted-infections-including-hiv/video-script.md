# Video script — Viral sexually transmitted infections — including HIV

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Genital herpes (HSV-1 and HSV-2), genital warts (HPV), molluscum contagiosum (a poxvirus spread skin to skin), HIV — pictured, attacking CD4 immune cells through three stages: acute (weeks, flu-like), chronic/latent (years) and AIDS — and hepatitis B, attacking the liver toward cirrhosis and liver cancer, vaccine-preventable, with WHO burden figures given.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
