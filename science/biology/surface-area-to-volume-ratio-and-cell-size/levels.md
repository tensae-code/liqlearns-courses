# Surface area to volume ratio and cell size — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (as a cell grows, its volume grows faster than its surface area, so the surface-area-to-volume ratio falls — which is why cells stay microscopic and why dividing beats growing for exchanging materials) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
