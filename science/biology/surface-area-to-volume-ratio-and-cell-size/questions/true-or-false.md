# Surface area to volume ratio and cell size — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. For a cube: surface area = 6 × side × side, and volume = length × width × height. Kuwait's Grade 10 workbook (Activity 6, printed p. 25) has pupils build cube models of cells from 1 cm cubes and work both measures out — a "link with mathematics" box in the activity.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كراسة التطبيقات — الصف العاشر — الجزء الأول (Biology — Applications Workbook, Grade 10, Term 1), printed p. 25).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with surface area to volume ratio and cell size.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is surface area to volume ratio and cell size.
