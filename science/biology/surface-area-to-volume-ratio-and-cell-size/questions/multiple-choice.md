# Surface area to volume ratio and cell size — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) as a cell grows, its volume grows faster than its surface area, so the surface-area-to-volume ratio falls — which is why cells stay microscopic and why dividing beats growing for exchanging materials  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
**Answer: a) as a cell grows, its volume grows faster than its surface area, so the surface-area-to-volume ratio falls — which is why cells stay microscopic and why dividing beats growing for exchanging materials**
