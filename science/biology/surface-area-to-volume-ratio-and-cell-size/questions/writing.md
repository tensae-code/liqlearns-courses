# Surface area to volume ratio and cell size — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: as a cell grows, its volume grows faster than its surface area, so the surface-area-to-volume ratio falls — which is why cells stay microscopic and why dividing beats growing for exchanging materials. Doubling the side multiplies the surface by four but the volume by eight, so the bigger the cube, the smaller its surface area for each unit of volume inside it.**
