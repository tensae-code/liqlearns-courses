# Mental health disorders

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Mental health disorders. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Biology is not only about the body. The human part of Azerbaijan's Grade 11 textbook — after embryonic development — turns to the psyche: the mind, with its feelings, thoughts and behaviour. The book first teaches the features of the development of the human psyche (§38), and then does something direct: it teaches the disorders of the psyche by name, one section each — anxiety disorders, depression, psychoses. Mental health stands inside human biology, named like any other part of it.

[Picture — the human part of Azerbaijan's book as a road: embryonic development, then the developing psyche, then the three named disorders — anxiety disorders, depression, psychoses — then healthy family relations]

## The key facts
**The developing psyche.** The psyche develops as the person grows — §38 teaches the features of the development of the human psyche, the mind that feels, thinks and acts, developing through life like the rest of the human organism.

**Anxiety disorders.** Тревожные расстройства (§39): the disorders where anxiety — fear and worry — itself becomes the illness: present strongly and lasting, beyond what the situation in front of the person calls for.

**Depression.** Депрессии (§40): the disorder of a lasting low mood that does not lift — mood, interest and energy all pulled down together, for a long stretch of time, not a single bad day.

**Psychoses.** Психозы (§41): the severe disorders of the psyche, where the person's contact with reality is disturbed — what is real and what is not stops being sorted correctly.

**Azerbaijan's way.** In Azerbaijan this is school biology: the mental-health sections sit in the human part of the Grade 11 book, between the embryonic development of the human and the closing sections on healthy relations in the family and a healthy lifestyle. The disorders are named directly — anxiety disorders, depression, psychoses — as illnesses of the developing psyche, taught to every pupil in the Russian-sector textbook.
