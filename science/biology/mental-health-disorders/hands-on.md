# Hands-on — Mental health disorders

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Draw the road of the human part of Azerbaijan's book on a big sheet of paper: embryonic development, the developing psyche, the three named disorders, healthy family relations. Point at each stop as you walk the road with a finger, and say at the disorders stop what marks each of the three — anxiety disorders, depression, psychoses.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the road with its four stops — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
