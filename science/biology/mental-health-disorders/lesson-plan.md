# Lesson plan — Mental health disorders

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Mental health disorders
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — appended to Unit 16 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the human psyche — the mind — develops as a person grows, and it can fall ill like the body can: Azerbaijan's Grade 11 biology teaches the disorders by name — anxiety disorders, depression and psychoses (Azerbaijan, Grade 11 — *Биология 11 (Biology 11)*, Russian-sector student textbook, Part VI §§38–41, printed pp. 144–155).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. The human nervous system is recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: **The human nervous system**. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say, in their own words, the one thing this lesson teaches: the psyche develops, and its disorders — anxiety disorders, depression and psychoses — are taught by name as illnesses of the mind.
2. Name the three disorder groups Azerbaijan's book teaches — anxiety disorders (Тревожные расстройства), depression (Депрессии) and psychoses (Психозы) — and say what marks each one.
3. Explain Azerbaijan's way as the placements describe it: a school biology course places mental health inside the human part of the book, between human embryonic development and healthy family relations, and names the disorders directly.

## What you need
