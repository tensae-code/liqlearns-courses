Today: Mental health disorders.

Remember this one thing: the human psyche — the mind — develops as a person grows, and it can fall ill like the body can. Azerbaijan's Grade 11 biology teaches the disorders by name.

First, anxiety disorders — Тревожные расстройства — where anxiety and fear themselves become the illness, strong and lasting beyond what the situation calls for.

Second, depression — Депрессии — a lasting low mood that does not lift, taking away interest and energy.

Third, psychoses — Психозы — the severe disorders, where a person's contact with reality is disturbed.

Azerbaijan teaches these inside the human part of its biology book — mental health is part of human biology, named directly, like any other part of the body.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
