# Mental health disorders — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Azerbaijan's Grade 11 biology teaches mental-health disorders by name.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Depression is a single bad day that lifts by itself the next morning.
**Answer: False** — depression is a lasting low mood that does not lift, pulling mood, interest and energy down together.

**3.** In psychoses, the person's contact with reality is disturbed.
**Answer: True**

**4.** The psyche has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the developing psyche and its disorders directly.
