# Mental health disorders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does §38 of Azerbaijan's book teach first, before the disorders?
a) The features of the development of the human psyche
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The features of the development of the human psyche**

**2.** What are anxiety disorders (Тревожные расстройства)?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Disorders where anxiety — fear and worry — itself becomes the illness, strong and lasting beyond what the situation calls for
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Disorders where anxiety — fear and worry — itself becomes the illness, strong and lasting beyond what the situation calls for**

**3.** What marks depression (Депрессии), as the book's section names it?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) A lasting low mood that does not lift, pulling mood, interest and energy down together
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) A lasting low mood that does not lift, pulling mood, interest and energy down together**

**4.** What are psychoses (Психозы)?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) The severe disorders where the person's contact with reality is disturbed
**Answer: d) The severe disorders where the person's contact with reality is disturbed**
