# Mental health disorders — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the three named disorders.
**Answer: Anxiety disorders (Тревожные расстройства) — anxiety and fear themselves become the illness, strong and lasting beyond what the situation calls for; depression (Депрессии) — a lasting low mood that does not lift, with interest and energy pulled down; psychoses (Психозы) — the severe disorders, where contact with reality is disturbed.**

**2.** Explain: Azerbaijan's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Azerbaijan teaches mental health inside the human part of its Grade 11 biology book: first the features of the development of the human psyche (§38), then the disorders by name — anxiety disorders (§39), depression (§40), psychoses (§41) — between human embryonic development and the sections on healthy family relations.**
