# Mollusca — snails, bivalves and cephalopods — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Molluscs are segmented like earthworms.**
**Answer: False** — the mollusc body is unsegmented — that is part of its plan.

**2. The gastropod has a single hermaphrodite gonad.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. The bivalve's foot is axe-shaped.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. All molluscs have internal fertilisation.**
**Answer: False** — fertilisation is internal in some and external in others.
