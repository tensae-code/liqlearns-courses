# Mollusca — snails, bivalves and cephalopods — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The scraping tongue of a mollusc is called the _____.**
**Answer: radula**

**2. The middle body region, holding the organs, is the _____ mass.**
**Answer: visceral**

**3. A bivalve's shell is made of two hinged _____.**
**Answer: valves**

**4. The snail's shell and visceral mass are twisted into a _____.**
**Answer: spiral**
