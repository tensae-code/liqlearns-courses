# Mollusca — snails, bivalves and cephalopods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The mollusc body has which three regions?**
a) head, thorax, abdomen  b) head, visceral mass, foot  c) cap, stalk, foot  d) head, mantle, tail
**Answer: b) head, visceral mass, foot**

**2. The radula is…**
a) a kind of shell  b) a scraping tongue  c) a stinging cell  d) a swimming larva
**Answer: b) a scraping tongue**

**3. A gastropod's eyes are…**
a) inside the shell  b) on the tips of tentacles  c) on its foot  d) absent
**Answer: b) on the tips of tentacles**

**4. A bivalve differs from a snail in having…**
a) a radula with more teeth  b) two hinged shell valves and no distinct head  c) a spiral shell  d) eyes on tentacles
**Answer: b) two hinged shell valves and no distinct head**
