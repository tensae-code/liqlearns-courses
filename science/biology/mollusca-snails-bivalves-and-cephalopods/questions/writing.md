**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining how the bivalve can be a mollusc even though it has no distinct head.
**Model answer: The mollusc plan is a soft unsegmented body in three regions with a radula in the feeding kinds and usually a shell. The bivalve keeps the visceral mass and the foot — axe-shaped, for digging — and protects itself with two hinged valves; it is the head region that is reduced away, because a filter-feeding animal shut inside shells has no use for a snail's head and tentacle eyes.**
