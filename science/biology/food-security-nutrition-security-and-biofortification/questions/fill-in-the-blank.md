# Food security, nutrition security and biofortification — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. ** _____ is defined as all people, at all times, having physical, social and economic access to sufficient, safe, nutritious food meeting dietary needs and preferences; nutrition security adds safe water, sanitation, health care and sound household practices in childcare and food storage/preparation, with a conceptual-framework figure; biofortification is breeding staple crops for higher nutrient levels (zinc wheat, golden rice) aiming at better protein, oil, vitamin and micronutrient content.
**Answer: Food security**
