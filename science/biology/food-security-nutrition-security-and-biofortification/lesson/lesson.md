# Food security, nutrition security and biofortification

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — food security, nutrition security and biofortification. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Food security is defined as all people, at all times, having physical, social and economic access to sufficient, safe, nutritious food meeting dietary needs and preferences; nutrition security adds safe water, sanitation, health care and sound household practices in childcare and food storage/preparation, with a conceptual-framework figure; biofortification is breeding staple crops for higher nutrient levels (zinc wheat, golden rice) aiming at better protein, oil, vitamin and micronutrient content.
