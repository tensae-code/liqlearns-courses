# Soil — properties and fertility — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (soil is a mixture whose layers can be separated and measured — shake soil with water in a bottle and the parts settle into layers whose heights you can read — and soil has chemical properties as well as physical ones: Uganda's Senior Two home-study booklet teaches soil as its own topic, Lesson 1 (PDF pp. 7–10)) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by comparing the layer heights of two different soils and saying what the difference means for each soil, without looking.
