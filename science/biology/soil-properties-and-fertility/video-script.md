Today: Soil — properties and fertility.

Remember this one thing: soil is not one single stuff. It is a mixture — and you can prove it with a bottle, water, and time.

Put soil in a bottle, add water, and shake. Then stand the bottle still and wait. The mixture settles, part by part, into layers — the heaviest parts at the bottom, the lightest at the top — and you can measure the height of every layer with a ruler.

Those layers are the soil's physical make-up made visible. And soil has a second side: its chemical properties — what is dissolved in it and how it feeds plants — which the same lesson teaches beside the physical ones.

Uganda's Senior Two home-study booklet — written for learners studying alone at home in the lockdown — makes soil its first topic, and its first lesson is exactly this: the physical and chemical properties of soil (PDF pp. 7–10).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
