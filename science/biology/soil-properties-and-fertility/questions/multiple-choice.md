# Soil — properties and fertility — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens when soil is shaken with water in a bottle and left to stand?
a) It dissolves completely
b) It settles into layers, heaviest at the bottom
c) It turns into one layer of sand
d) Nothing happens
**Answer: b) It settles into layers, heaviest at the bottom**

**2.** In the booklet's experiment, what is measured?
a) The weight of the bottle
b) The height of each settled layer
c) The temperature of the water
d) The colour of the soil only
**Answer: b) The height of each settled layer**

**3.** Which booklet teaches soil as its first topic, with properties as Lesson 1?
a) Uganda's Senior Two home-study booklet (NCDC, 2020)
b) The Senior One prototype textbook
c) The Senior One Teacher's Guide
d) No book teaches it
**Answer: a) Uganda's Senior Two home-study booklet (NCDC, 2020)**
