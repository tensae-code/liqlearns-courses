# Soil — properties and fertility — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the bottle sedimentation experiment and what it shows.
**Answer: Soil is put in a bottle with water, shaken, and left to stand. The parts of the mixture settle by weight into layers — heavy coarse parts at the bottom, finer parts above, lightest matter at the top — and the height of each layer is measured. The layers and their heights show what the soil is made of and in what proportions, so soils can be compared by number.**

**2.** Explain: Uganda's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Uganda's Senior Two home-study booklet (NCDC, 2020) — written for learners studying alone at home during the COVID-19 lockdown — makes Soil its first topic, and Lesson 1 of that topic is the physical and chemical properties of soil (PDF pp. 7–10), taught through the bottle-shake sedimentation experiment with measured layer heights.**
