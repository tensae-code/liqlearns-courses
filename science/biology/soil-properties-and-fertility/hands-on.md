# Hands-on — Soil — properties and fertility

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Do the booklet's experiment yourself: put a handful of soil in a clear bottle, add water, shake hard, and stand it still. When it has settled, measure the height of each layer with a ruler and write the heights down. Say aloud what each layer is, and which layer is thickest in your soil.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the clear bottle with its settled layers and the measured heights written beside it — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
