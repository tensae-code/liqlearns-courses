# Hands-on — Improving crop yield

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Look at a garden, farm plot or planted container near you. Judge it against the book's five ways: is the soil covered or conserved? fed? well tended? a good variety? Say aloud one improvement from the list that the plot is missing, and what it would do.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a terraced hillside with mulched beds, a farmer adding manure, and two labelled plants: disease-resistant cassava and a budded orange tree — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
