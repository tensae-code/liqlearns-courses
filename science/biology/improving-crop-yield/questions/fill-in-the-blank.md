# Improving crop yield — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** Building steps across a slope to hold the soil is called _____.
**Answer: terracing**

**2.** Cassava bred to resist disease is an _____ variety.
**Answer: improved (disease-resistant)**

**3.** _____ and manure feed the soil what crops take out.
**Answer: Fertilisers**
