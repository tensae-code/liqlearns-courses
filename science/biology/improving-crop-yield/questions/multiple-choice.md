# Improving crop yield — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these is a soil-conservation method in the book?
a) Bush burning
b) Terracing
c) Overcropping
d) Leaving soil bare
**Answer: b) Terracing**

**2.** The book's example of a disease-resistant improved variety is…
a) budded oranges
b) disease-resistant cassava
c) guinea corn
d) kola
**Answer: b) disease-resistant cassava**

**3.** Budded oranges are an example of improving yield by…
a) more land
b) plant breeding for improved varieties
c) mulching
d) rotation
**Answer: b) plant breeding for improved varieties**

**4.** Fertilisers and manure improve yield by…
a) returning to the soil what crops take out
b) killing all soil organisms
c) making rain fall
d) changing the crop's genes
**Answer: a) returning to the soil what crops take out**
