# Improving crop yield — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** The book lists 'no bush burning' among soil-conservation practices.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Putting more land under cultivation raises yield per hectare by itself.
**Answer: False** — It raises total production; the other four ways — soil conservation, fertilisers and manure, cultural practices, breeding — raise the yield from the land already farmed.

**3.** Mulching and rotation are soil-conservation methods.
**Answer: True**
