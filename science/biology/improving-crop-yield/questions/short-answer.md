# Improving crop yield — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the book's five ways of improving crop yield.
**Answer: Soil conservation (terracing, mulching, rotation, no bush burning); fertilisers and manure to feed the soil; better cultural practices; plant breeding for improved varieties such as disease-resistant cassava and budded oranges; and putting more land under cultivation.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book teaches ways of improving crop yield in Chapter 9 (printed p. 150) as a practical list — soil conservation, fertilisers and manure, cultural practices, plant breeding (disease-resistant cassava, budded oranges) and more land under cultivation.**
