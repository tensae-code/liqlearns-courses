# Video script — Improving crop yield

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Improving crop yield.

Remember this one thing: crop yield is raised in five ways Nigeria's book sets out: conserving the soil, feeding it with fertilisers and manure, better cultural practices, breeding improved varieties, and putting more land under cultivation.

Soil conservation heads the list: terracing sloping land, mulching the surface, rotating crops, and not burning the bush — keeping the soil there and fertile.

Fertilisers and manure return to the soil what crops take out, raising the yield the land can support.

Cultural practices — how the crop is planted, spaced, weeded and tended — change the harvest from the same land and seed.

Plant breeding produces improved varieties: the book's examples are disease-resistant cassava and budded oranges — yield protected by the plant's own constitution.

The fifth way is extent: putting more land under cultivation raises total production, alongside the four ways that raise yield per piece of land.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
