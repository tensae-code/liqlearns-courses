## The idea
Amoeba catches food, algae make it, slime moulds absorb it — yet all are protists, which is why they need a kingdom of their own.

## The key facts
**Where they live.** Protists are mostly single-celled eukaryotes living in water and damp places.

**Members.** Protista includes algae, protozoa such as amoeba and paramecium, and slime moulds.

**Three ways.** Some protists are plant-like (making food), some animal-like (catching food) and some fungus-like (absorbing it).

**Eukaryote.** Protists have a true nucleus, unlike bacteria.
