# Protista — the single-celled eukaryotes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about where they live?
a) Protists are mostly single-celled eukaryotes living in water and damp places
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Protists are mostly single-celled eukaryotes living in water and damp places**

**2.** What does this lesson say about members?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Protista includes algae, protozoa such as amoeba and paramecium, and slime moulds
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Protista includes algae, protozoa such as amoeba and paramecium, and slime moulds**

**3.** What does this lesson say about three ways?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Some protists are plant-like (making food), some animal-like (catching food) and some fungus-like (absorbing it)
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Some protists are plant-like (making food), some animal-like (catching food) and some fungus-like (absorbing it)**

**4.** What does this lesson say about eukaryote?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Protists have a true nucleus, unlike bacteria
**Answer: d) Protists have a true nucleus, unlike bacteria**
