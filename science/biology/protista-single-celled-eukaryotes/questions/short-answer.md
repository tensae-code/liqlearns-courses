# Protista — the single-celled eukaryotes — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: three ways.
**Answer: Some protists are plant-like (making food), some animal-like (catching food) and some fungus-like (absorbing it).**

**2.** Explain: eukaryote.
**Answer: Protists have a true nucleus, unlike bacteria.**
