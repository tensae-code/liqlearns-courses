Today: Ecosystem stability — resistance and resilience.

Remember this one thing: an ecosystem in balance holds three balances — structure, function and input–output — and its stability comes in two kinds: resistance stability, withstanding a disturbance, and resilience stability, recovering from one, rainforest and Arctic tundra standing at the two ends.

balance defined by three balances (structure, function, input–output); resistance stability against resilience stability, rainforest against Arctic tundra; raising stability by limiting use to the self-regulation range (closing hillsides to let them regreen) and by building "ecological barriers" — the Three-North Shelterbelt; the eucalyptus pure-plantation problem case, where undergrowth species fall as planting density rises from 750 to 1,000 stems per hectare.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
