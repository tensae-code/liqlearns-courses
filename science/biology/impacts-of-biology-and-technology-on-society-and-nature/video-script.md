# Video script — Impacts of biology and technology on society and nature

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Biological advance is credited with food security, better medicine and longer life, energy and clean water, industrial production by microbes and antibiotics; the unit frames impact as effects, good and bad, of biological knowledge and of devices on both society and the natural world, for pupils to separate and judge.

The guide extends the student book's impacts discussion with cases the student book does not name: practising a predetermined limit of choosing only XY (boy) children leads to the loss of females in society; industrial emissions and effluents polluting air and drinking water; and biological weapons as a destructive use of biology against society and nature.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
