# Impacts of biology and technology on society and nature — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Biological advance is credited with food security, better medicine and longer life, energy and clean water, industrial production by microbes and antibiotics; the unit frames impact as effects, good and bad, of biological knowledge and of devices on both society and the natural world, for pupils to separate and judge.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The guide extends the student book's impacts discussion with cases the student book does not name: practising a predetermined limit of choosing only XY (boy) children leads to the loss of females in society; industrial emissions and effluents polluting air and drinking water; and biological weapons as a destructive use of biology against society and nature.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
