# Genetic Drift — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about genetic drift (§4.1.7):?**
**Answer: drift as a mechanism of evolution in which allele frequencies change over generations by chance — a binomial sampling error of the gene pool, taught with the fair-coin analogy (runs of heads as alleles drift to fixation); average expected change is zero, and small populations drift fastest.**
