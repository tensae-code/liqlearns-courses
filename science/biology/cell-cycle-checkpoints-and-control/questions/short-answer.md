# Cell cycle checkpoints and control — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: the cell cycle is controlled at checkpoints: at the G1/S checkpoint and the M checkpoint the cell verifies that conditions are right — growth factors present, DNA doubling completed, chromosomes aligned — and halts the cycle if a check fails. Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 1-3, printed p. 52) tabulates the cell cycle's checkpoints: the G1/S checkpoint and the M checkpoint.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's new-curriculum Grade 10 book teaches cycle control as a table to be read exactly: checkpoint by checkpoint, what each one verifies, and the halt when a check fails (Lesson 1-3, printed p. 52).**
