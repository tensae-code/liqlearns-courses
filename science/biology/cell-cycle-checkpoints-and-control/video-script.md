Today: Cell cycle checkpoints and control.

Remember this one thing: the cell cycle is controlled at checkpoints: at the G1/S checkpoint and the M checkpoint the cell verifies that conditions are right — growth factors present, DNA doubling completed, chromosomes aligned — and halts the cycle if a check fails.

Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 1-3, printed p. 52) tabulates the cell cycle's checkpoints: the G1/S checkpoint and the M checkpoint.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
