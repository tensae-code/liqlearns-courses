## The key facts
**Checkpoints.** Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 1-3, printed p. 52) tabulates the cell cycle's checkpoints: the G1/S checkpoint and the M checkpoint.

**What each check verifies.** The table states what each checkpoint verifies: growth factors, whether DNA doubling has been completed, and whether the chromosomes are aligned on the metaphase plate.

**The halt.** If a check fails, the cycle halts — the checkpoint is a control, not a formality: the cell does not pass into the next phase until what the checkpoint verifies is in order.

**Kuwait's way.** Kuwait's new-curriculum Grade 10 book teaches cycle control as a table to be read exactly: checkpoint by checkpoint, what each one verifies, and the halt when a check fails (Lesson 1-3, printed p. 52).
