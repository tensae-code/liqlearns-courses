# Cnidaria — polyps, medusae and stinging cells — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. The polyp and the medusa are two forms of the same cnidarian body plan.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches — the medusa is an inverted polyp.

**2. Cnidarians have a complete gut with a mouth and a separate anus.**
**Answer: False** — they have one gastrovascular cavity; digestion is extracellular then intracellular.

**3. Nematocysts are found inside cnidoblasts.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. Hydra is a mollusc.**
**Answer: False** — hydra is a cnidarian — the module works its development in this lesson.
