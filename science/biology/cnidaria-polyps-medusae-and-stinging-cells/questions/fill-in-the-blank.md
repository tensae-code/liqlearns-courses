# Cnidaria — polyps, medusae and stinging cells — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The free-swimming cnidarian form is called the _____.**
**Answer: medusa**

**2. The stinging capsules inside cnidoblasts are called _____.**
**Answer: nematocysts**

**3. Digestion finishes inside the cells, so its second stage is called _____ digestion.**
**Answer: intracellular**

**4. The single inner space of a cnidarian is the _____ cavity.**
**Answer: gastrovascular**
