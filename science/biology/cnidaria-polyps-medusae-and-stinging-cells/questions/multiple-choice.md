# Cnidaria — polyps, medusae and stinging cells — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A medusa is best described as…**
a) a young polyp  b) an inverted polyp  c) a kind of sponge  d) a flatworm larva
**Answer: b) an inverted polyp**

**2. The stinging cells of cnidarians are the…**
a) choanocytes  b) cnidoblasts with nematocysts  c) spicules  d) radula
**Answer: b) cnidoblasts with nematocysts**

**3. Digestion in a cnidarian happens…**
a) only inside the gut cells  b) first extracellularly in the gastrovascular cavity, then intracellularly  c) in a complete tube with mouth and anus  d) in the blood
**Answer: b) first extracellularly in the gastrovascular cavity, then intracellularly**

**4. Cnidarian symmetry is…**
a) bilateral  b) radial  c) asymmetrical  d) five-sided
**Answer: b) radial**
