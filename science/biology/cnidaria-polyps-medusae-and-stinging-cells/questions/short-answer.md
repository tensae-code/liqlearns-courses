# Cnidaria — polyps, medusae and stinging cells — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** How are the polyp and the medusa related, as Mozambique teaches it?
**Answer: They are the two forms of one body plan: the polyp is fixed and tube-shaped, the medusa is free-swimming and bell-shaped — the medusa is an inverted polyp.**

**2.** Explain Mozambique's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Mozambique's PESD II animal module (Módulo 3 de Biologia), Lesson 3, teaches phylum Cnidaria (Coelenterata) by radial symmetry, the polyp and medusa forms with the medusa as an inverted polyp, the gastrovascular cavity with extracellular then intracellular digestion, and cnidoblasts/nematocysts as the phylum's signature stinging cells, with hydra development as the worked example.**
