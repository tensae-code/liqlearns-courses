# Cnidaria — polyps, medusae and stinging cells — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining how one animal plan can appear as both a fixed polyp and a swimming medusa.
**Model answer: The polyp and the medusa are the same radial body plan in two positions. The polyp is fixed, tube-shaped, with its mouth and tentacles up. Turn that plan over and free it to swim and you have the medusa, bell-shaped, with mouth and tentacles down — an inverted polyp, as Mozambique's module teaches it.**
