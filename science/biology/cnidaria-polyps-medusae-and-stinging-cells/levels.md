# Cnidaria — polyps, medusae and stinging cells — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Cnidarians are radially symmetrical animals with two body forms — the fixed polyp and the free-swimming medusa, a medusa being an inverted polyp — one gastrovascular cavity for digestion, and stinging cells, the cnidoblasts with their nematocysts, as the phylum's signature.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by restating the two forms and how they relate — medusa as inverted polyp — and the two stages of digestion, extracellular then intracellular, without looking.
