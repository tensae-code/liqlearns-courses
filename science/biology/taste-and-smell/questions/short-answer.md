# Taste and Smell — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about the tongue and the nose: taste and smell (§§5.2.2–5.2.3):?**
**Answer: taste sites on the tongue drawn and mapped; the nose's internal structure with smell receptors in the nasal passages; taste and smell compared — both detect chemicals that must dissolve, but smell receptors are more specialised for distant vapours and far more sensitive; an activity asks why food tastes flat with a cold.**
