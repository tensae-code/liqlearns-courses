# Brain science — mapping the brain

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Brain science — mapping the brain. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Brain science maps the brain three ways — its cell types, its connections and its activity — as national research programmes like the China Brain Project (launched 2018) set out to know the brain, simulate the brain, protect the brain and develop with the brain. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from China's People's Education Press biology textbooks, which teach it as a named topic.
