# Brain science — mapping the brain — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. launched 2018 under the aims "know the brain, simulate the brain, protect the brain, develop with the brain"; three maps to be drawn (cell types, connections, activity) and why cloned monkeys — genetically uniform model animals — are a brain-science instrument. No existing lesson treats brain science as a national research programme.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in China's People's Education Press biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (普通高中教科书《生物学》选择性必修1 稳态与调节 (Senior High Biology, Selective Compulsory Book 1: Homeostasis and Regulation, People's Education Press, 2019-standard edition)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with brain science — mapping the brain.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is brain science — mapping the brain.
