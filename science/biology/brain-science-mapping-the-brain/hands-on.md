# Hands-on — Brain science — mapping the brain

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: brain science maps the brain three ways — its cell types, its connections and its activity — as national research programmes like the China Brain Project (launched 2018) set out to know the brain, simulate the brain, protect the brain and develop with the brain.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a brain drawn three times side by side — once dotted with its many cell types, once threaded with connection lines, once lit up in active patches. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
