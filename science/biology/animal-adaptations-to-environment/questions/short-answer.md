# Animal adaptations to environment — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: how Iraq's Grade 10 book organises its animal-adaptation chapter.
**Answer: The chapter (Ch. 6, printed pp. 77–120 — the book's longest) opens with animal adaptation to the environment in form, structure and ways of life (§1-6, printed p. 77), then takes adaptation to life in water and adaptation to life on land (§§2-6/3-6, printed p. 88), and closes with a head-to-head comparison of a bony fish and the pigeon (§4-6, printed p. 110).**
