# Animal adaptations to environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Iraq's Grade 10 chapter on animal adaptation is organised by…
a) price of the animal
b) adaptation — form, structure and ways of life
c) alphabetical order of species
d) the size of the animal
**Answer: b) adaptation — form, structure and ways of life**

**2.** The chapter takes the two great homes in which order?
a) land first, then water
b) water first, then land
c) air first, then soil
d) it takes only one home
**Answer: b) water first, then land**

**3.** The chapter's closing comparison sets…
a) two fish against each other
b) a bony fish against the pigeon
c) a pigeon against a bat
d) a worm against a snail
**Answer: b) a bony fish against the pigeon**

**4.** Adaptation is read in which three registers?
a) colour, taste and smell
b) form, structure and ways of life
c) age, weight and height
d) speed, strength and size
**Answer: b) form, structure and ways of life**
