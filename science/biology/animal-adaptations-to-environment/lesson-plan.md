# Lesson plan — Animal adaptations to environment

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Animal adaptations to environment
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — appended to Unit 16 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an animal is fitted to the place it lives in three registers at once — its form, its structure and its ways of life — and life in water demands a different set of fittings from life on land; Iraq's Grade 10 Biology book gives animal adaptation its longest chapter (Ch. 6, printed pp. 77–120) and closes it with a head-to-head comparison of a bony fish and the pigeon.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own as a single-skill lesson built from Iraq's Grade 10 Biology textbook, as cited in lesson/sources.md. Related lessons are recommended first where they help, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say, in their own words, the one thing this lesson teaches: an animal is fitted to the place it lives in three registers at once — its form, its structure and its ways of life — and life in water demands a different set of fittings from life on land; Iraq's Grade 10 Biology book gives animal adaptation its longest chapter (Ch. 6, printed pp. 77–120) and closes it with a head-to-head comparison of a bony fish and the pigeon.
2. Explain each key fact (Adaptation in three registers; Adaptation to water; Adaptation to land; Fish against pigeon; Iraq's way) in their own words.
3. Explain Iraq's way as the placements describe it.
