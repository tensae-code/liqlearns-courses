# Hands-on — Animal adaptations to environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Pick one water animal and one land animal you know. For each, name one fitting of form, one of structure and one of ways of life that suits it to its home. Then set the two side by side, fish-and-pigeon style, and say aloud what the contrast shows.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a bony fish and a pigeon side by side, each labelled with the fittings that suit it to its home — the fish to water, the pigeon to land — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
