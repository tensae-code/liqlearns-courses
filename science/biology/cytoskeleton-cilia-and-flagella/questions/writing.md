# Cytoskeleton, cilia and flagella — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining the cell's movers to a younger student. Use the words cytoskeleton, cilia and flagellum once each.
**Model answer: Inside every cell there is a framework of fibres called the cytoskeleton, which holds the cell's shape and moves things around inside it. Some cells are covered in short hairs called cilia that beat together and move fluid past the cell. Other cells swim using a long tail called a flagellum, which whips back and forth and pushes the cell forward.**
