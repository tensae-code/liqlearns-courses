# Cytoskeleton, cilia and flagella — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The framework of fibres inside the cell is called the _____.**
**Answer: cytoskeleton**

**2. Short, numerous hair-like projections that beat are called _____.**
**Answer: cilia**

**3. A long whip-like tail that propels a cell is called a _____.**
**Answer: flagellum**

**4. In the Italian book, lesson A4.7 is titled by its idea: cells _____.**
**Answer: move**
