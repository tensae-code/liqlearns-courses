# Cytoskeleton, cilia and flagella — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. The cytoskeleton lies outside the cell, like a shell.**
**Answer: False** — the cytoskeleton is the framework of fibres inside the cell.

**2. Cilia are short and numerous, and they beat.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. A flagellum is one of hundreds of short hairs on a cell.**
**Answer: False** — a flagellum is a long whip-like tail, usually one or a few per cell; the short numerous hairs are cilia.

**4. Italy's Grade 9 textbook gives cell movement its own named lesson, A4.7.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (printed p. A80).
