# Cytoskeleton, cilia and flagella — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the cytoskeleton?**
a) A hard shell outside the cell  b) The framework of fibres inside the cell that holds its shape and moves things within it  c) A kind of flagellum  d) The cell's store of food
**Answer: b) The framework of fibres inside the cell that holds its shape and moves things within it**

**2. What are cilia?**
a) Long single tails that whip  b) Short, numerous hair-like projections that beat to move fluid or the cell  c) Fibres inside the nucleus  d) Openings in the cell membrane
**Answer: b) Short, numerous hair-like projections that beat to move fluid or the cell**

**3. What does a flagellum do?**
a) It holds the cell's shape  b) It stores energy  c) It lashes like a whip and propels the cell  d) It beats in rhythm with hundreds of others
**Answer: c) It lashes like a whip and propels the cell**

**4. Where does Italy's Grade 9 book teach the cytoskeleton, cilia and flagella?**
a) In the evolution chapter  b) As the lesson “cells move” (A4.7) inside the chapter “Observing the cell”  c) In the human-body volume  d) Only in a glossary
**Answer: b) As the lesson “cells move” (A4.7) inside the chapter “Observing the cell”**
