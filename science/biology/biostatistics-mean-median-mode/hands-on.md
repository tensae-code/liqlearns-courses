# Hands-on — Biostatistics — mean, median and mode

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Measure the heights of ten plants or the pulse rates of five companions. Line the numbers up, find the mean, the median and the mode, and draw the set as a bar chart.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a row of seedlings of different heights with a ruler beside them, and next to it a simple bar chart of the same heights, with the middle seedling ringed as the median — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
