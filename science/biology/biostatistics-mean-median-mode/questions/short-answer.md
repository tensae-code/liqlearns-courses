# Biostatistics — mean, median and mode — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: mean, median and mode, and how each is found.
**Answer: The mean is the total of the values divided by how many there are. The median is the middle value when the set is lined up in order. The mode is the value that occurs most often. Each sums a set of biological measurements up in one number.**

**2.** Explain: Pakistan (Punjab)'s way of teaching this.
**Answer: Punjab's Grade 9 SNC book closes with Ch 11, Biostatistics (printed pp. 163–167), teaching the mean, median and mode and the bar chart inside the biology book itself, as part of a biologist's training.**
