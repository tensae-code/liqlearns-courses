# Biostatistics — mean, median and mode — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** The leaf counts of five plants are 12, 15, 15, 18, 20. Write out the mean, median and mode in full sentences.
**Model answer: The total is 80 leaves over 5 plants, so the mean is 80 ÷ 5 = 16 leaves per plant. Lined up in order, the middle value is 15, so the median is 15. The value 15 occurs twice, more than any other, so the mode is 15.**
