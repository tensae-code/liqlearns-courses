# Biostatistics — mean, median and mode — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The _____ is the total of the values divided by how many values there are.**
**Answer: mean**

**2. The middle value of an ordered set is the _____.**
**Answer: median**

**3. The value that occurs most often is the _____.**
**Answer: mode**

**4. Biological data is presented at a glance in a _____ chart.**
**Answer: bar**
