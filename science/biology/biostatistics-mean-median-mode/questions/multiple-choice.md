# Biostatistics — mean, median and mode — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The mean of a set of values is…**
a) the middle value b) the total divided by how many values there are c) the commonest value d) the largest minus the smallest
**Answer: b) the total divided by how many values there are**

**2. To find the median you first…**
a) add all the values b) line the values up in order and take the middle one c) count the values d) draw a bar chart
**Answer: b) line the values up in order and take the middle one**

**3. The mode of the set 3, 5, 5, 7, 9 is…**
a) 3 b) 5 c) 7 d) 9
**Answer: b) 5**

**4. Punjab teaches biostatistics…**
a) only in the mathematics book b) as a chapter (Ch 11) inside its Grade 9 biology book c) at university only d) not at all
**Answer: b) as a chapter (Ch 11) inside its Grade 9 biology book**
