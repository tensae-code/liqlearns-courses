# Osteoporosis and bone health — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: in osteoporosis bones lose mass and mineral salts such as calcium, so fracture risk rises and the spine can curve — bone health is the health of the bone's material, not only its shape. Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-3, printed p. 124) teaches osteoporosis by what is lost: bones losing mass and mineral salts such as calcium.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's new Grade 10 book places osteoporosis inside the skeletal-system lesson itself (Lesson 2-3), right after bone structure — the disease follows the anatomy that explains it.**
