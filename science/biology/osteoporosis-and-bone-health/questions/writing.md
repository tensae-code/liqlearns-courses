# Osteoporosis and bone health — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: in osteoporosis bones lose mass and mineral salts such as calcium, so fracture risk rises and the spine can curve — bone health is the health of the bone's material, not only its shape. The consequences the book names are rising fracture risk and curvature of the spine — the disease is taught through what it does to the skeleton.**
