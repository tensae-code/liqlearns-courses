# Osteoporosis and bone health — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) in osteoporosis bones lose mass and mineral salts such as calcium, so fracture risk rises and the spine can curve — bone health is the health of the bone's material, not only its shape  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
**Answer: a) in osteoporosis bones lose mass and mineral salts such as calcium, so fracture risk rises and the spine can curve — bone health is the health of the bone's material, not only its shape**

**2. Osteoporosis — which statement is right?**
a) Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-3, printed p. 124) teaches osteoporosis by what is lost: bones losing mass and mineral salts such as calcium.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-3, printed p. 124) teaches osteoporosis by what is lost: bones losing mass and mineral salts such as calcium.**

**3. Which statement about this lesson's topic is right?**
a) It is unrelated to the lesson's topic  b) The consequences the book names are rising fracture risk and curvature of the spine — the disease is taught through what it does to the skeleton.  c) It is a myth the book corrects  d) It applies only outside Kuwait
**Answer: b) The consequences the book names are rising fracture risk and curvature of the spine — the disease is taught through what it does to the skeleton.**
