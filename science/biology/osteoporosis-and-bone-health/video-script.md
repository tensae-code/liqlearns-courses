Today: Osteoporosis and bone health.

Remember this one thing: in osteoporosis bones lose mass and mineral salts such as calcium, so fracture risk rises and the spine can curve — bone health is the health of the bone's material, not only its shape.

Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-3, printed p. 124) teaches osteoporosis by what is lost: bones losing mass and mineral salts such as calcium.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
