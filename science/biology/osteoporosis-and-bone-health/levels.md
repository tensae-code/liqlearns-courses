# Osteoporosis and bone health — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (in osteoporosis bones lose mass and mineral salts such as calcium, so fracture risk rises and the spine can curve — bone health is the health of the bone's material, not only its shape) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
