Today: Modelling the cell membrane.

Remember this one thing: a cell membrane can be modelled with simple materials — a glass dish of water, vegetable oil and fine sawdust — and each material in the model stands for one part of the real membrane's structure.

Kuwait's Grade 10 applications workbook (Activity 3, printed p. 20) has pupils design a sample — a model — for a cell membrane from a glass dish of water, vegetable oil and fine sawdust.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
