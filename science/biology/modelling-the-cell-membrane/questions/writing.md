# Modelling the cell membrane — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: a cell membrane can be modelled with simple materials — a glass dish of water, vegetable oil and fine sawdust — and each material in the model stands for one part of the real membrane's structure. After building the model, pupils state what the water, the oil and the sawdust each represent in the real membrane's structure — a model is only useful when every part of it is matched to the real thing it stands for.**
