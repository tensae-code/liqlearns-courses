# Modelling the cell membrane — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) a cell membrane can be modelled with simple materials — a glass dish of water, vegetable oil and fine sawdust — and each material in the model stands for one part of the real membrane's structure  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life

**2. The model — which statement is right?**
a) Kuwait's Grade 10 applications workbook (Activity 3, printed p. 20) has pupils design a sample — a model — for a cell membrane from a glass dish of water, vegetable oil and fine sawdust.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) Kuwait's Grade 10 applications workbook (Activity 3, printed p. 20) has pupils design a sample — a model — for a cell membrane from a glass dish of water, vegetable oil and fine sawdust.**

**3. Which statement about this lesson's topic is right?**
