# Modelling the cell membrane — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's Grade 10 applications workbook (Activity 3, printed p. 20) has pupils design a sample — a model — for a cell membrane from a glass dish of water, vegetable oil and fine sawdust.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كراسة التطبيقات — الصف العاشر — الجزء الأول (Biology — Applications Workbook, Grade 10, Term 1), printed p. 20).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with modelling the cell membrane.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is modelling the cell membrane.
