# Modelling the cell membrane — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: a cell membrane can be modelled with simple materials — a glass dish of water, vegetable oil and fine sawdust — and each material in the model stands for one part of the real membrane's structure. Kuwait's Grade 10 applications workbook (Activity 3, printed p. 20) has pupils design a sample — a model — for a cell membrane from a glass dish of water, vegetable oil and fine sawdust.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait teaches the membrane by building it: in the Grade 10 applications workbook the learners design the model themselves (Activity 3: "How Do You Design a Sample (Model) for a Cell Membrane?", printed p. 20), inside the workbook's fixed format — goal, materials, steps, group work, then written observation and analysis.**
