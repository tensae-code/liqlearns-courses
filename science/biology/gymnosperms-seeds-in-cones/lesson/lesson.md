# Gymnosperms — seeds in cones

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Gymnosperms — seeds in cones. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Not every plant hides its seeds in a fruit. Gymnosperms — the naked-seed plants — carry their seeds on cones. Follow the pine life cycle stage by stage.

[Picture 22209 — a pine cone open with seeds, beside a pine life-cycle circle]

## The key facts
**Naked seeds.** Gymnosperm seeds are borne exposed, not inside a fruit.

**Cones.** The seeds sit on cones, as in conifers.

**Needle leaves.** Conifers and their kin have needle-like leaves.

**Pine life cycle.** The pine life cycle is worked stage by stage in this lesson.
