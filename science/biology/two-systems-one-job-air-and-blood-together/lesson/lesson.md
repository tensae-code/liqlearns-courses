# Two systems, one job — air and blood together

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Two systems, one job — air and blood together. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Neither system finishes the job alone. The lungs swap the gases; the heart moves the carrier; digestion supplies the fuel. One job, three systems.

[Picture 22263 — lungs and heart linked by two arrows — oxygen in, carbon dioxide out]

## The key facts
**The lungs' part.** The lungs take in oxygen and release carbon dioxide.

**The heart's part.** The heart circulates the blood that carries both gases.

**Continually together.** The breathing and circulatory systems work continually together.

**The fuel.** The digestive system supplies the fuel for the partnership.
