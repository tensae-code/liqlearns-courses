# Two systems, one job — air and blood together — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (the lungs take in oxygen and release carbon dioxide while the heart circulates the blood that carries both — the two systems working continually together, fuelled by digestion) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: comparing this lesson's facts with a neighbouring lesson's, and explaining them without looking.
