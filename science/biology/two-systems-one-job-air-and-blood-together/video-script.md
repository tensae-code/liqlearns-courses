Today: Two systems, one job — air and blood together.

Remember this one thing: the lungs take in oxygen and release carbon dioxide while the heart circulates the blood that carries both — the two systems working continually together, fuelled by digestion

The lungs' part: The lungs take in oxygen and release carbon dioxide.

The heart's part: The heart circulates the blood that carries both gases.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
