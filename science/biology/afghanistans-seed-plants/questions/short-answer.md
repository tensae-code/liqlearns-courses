# Afghanistan's seed plants — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the conifers of the east.
**Answer: In the mountains of eastern Afghanistan the great forests are conifer forests. Their trees keep needle-like leaves, bear cones instead of flowers, and give timber, fuel and shelter on steep slopes — they are the country's clearest gymnosperms.**

**2.** Explain: field crops.
**Answer: The grain a family eats is itself a seed. Wheat and barley — the staple crops of Afghan fields — are flowering plants (angiosperms) farmed precisely for their seeds, and the same is true of maize, rice and the pulses.**
