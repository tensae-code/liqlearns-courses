# Afghanistan's seed plants — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Seed plants reproduce by seeds, which carry the young plant and its food store.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Gymnosperms enclose their seeds inside a fruit..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.

**3.** Seed plants divide into gymnosperms, whose seeds sit naked in cones, and angiosperms, whose seeds are enclosed in a fruit.
**Answer: True**

**4.** A seed carries no food store for the young plant..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.
