# Afghanistan's seed plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about seed plants bear seeds?
a) Seed plants reproduce by seeds, which carry the young plant and its food store
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else

**2.** What does this lesson say about two great groups?

**Answer: b) Seed plants divide into gymnosperms, whose seeds sit naked in cones, and angiosperms, whose seeds are enclosed in a fruit**
