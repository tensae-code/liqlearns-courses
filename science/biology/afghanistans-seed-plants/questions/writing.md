**Model answer: Afghanistan's own common seed plants — the conifers, field crops and fruit trees of the country — are seed plants: plants that reproduce by seeds, in cones or inside fruits. Seed plants reproduce by seeds, which carry the young plant and its food store. Seed plants divide into gymnosperms, whose seeds sit naked in cones, and angiosperms, whose seeds are enclosed in a fruit. Afghanistan's eastern mountain forests are conifer forests — pines, cedars and junipers whose seeds ripen in cones.**
