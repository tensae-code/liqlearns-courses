# Hands-on — Afghanistan's seed plants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Walk near home or school and list every seed plant you can find. Sort your list into gymnosperms (cones) and angiosperms (flowers and fruits), and mark the ones your household eats or uses.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of three Afghan seed plants side by side — a pine branch with cones, a wheat head heavy with grain, and a pomegranate branch with fruit — each labelled with its group, gymnosperm or angiosperm — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
