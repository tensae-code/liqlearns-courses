# Locomotion in a finned fish — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why the swim bladder is as much a locomotion organ as the tail.
**Model answer: Swimming is not only moving forward — it is holding a depth without wasting the effort on staying up. The swim bladder does that half of the job: by adjusting its gas the fish hangs weightless at its chosen level, so the myotomes and tail can spend all their power on thrust and steering.**
