# Locomotion in a finned fish — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The blocks of swimming muscle in a fish are called…**
a) myotomes  b) lenticels  c) alveoli  d) sarcomeres only
**Answer: a) myotomes**

**2. Buoyancy in a bony fish is adjusted by the…**
a) lateral line  b) swim bladder  c) gill rakers  d) scales
**Answer: b) swim bladder**

**3. The lateral line's job is…**
a) digestion  b) sensing movement in the water around the fish  c) making mucus  d) pumping the gills
**Answer: b) sensing movement in the water around the fish**

**4. Which fins are paired?**
a) dorsal and anal  b) tail and dorsal  c) pectoral and pelvic  d) anal and tail
**Answer: c) pectoral and pelvic**
