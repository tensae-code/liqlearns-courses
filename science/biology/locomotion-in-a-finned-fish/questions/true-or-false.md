# Locomotion in a finned fish — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Overlapping scales and mucus reduce resistance in water.**
**Answer: True** — the streamlined body's finish.

**2. The tail fin is a paired fin.**
**Answer: False** — it is unpaired; pectoral and pelvic fins are paired.

**3. A fish must swim constantly or sink, whatever its swim bladder does.**
**Answer: False** — the swim bladder holds its buoyancy at depth.

**4. Kenya's book sets a calculation practical on tail power.**
**Answer: True** — locomotion measured, not just described.
