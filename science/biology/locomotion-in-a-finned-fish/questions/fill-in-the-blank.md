# Locomotion in a finned fish — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The muscle blocks along a fish's body are _____.**
**Answer: myotomes**

**2. The gas float inside the fish is the _____ bladder.**
**Answer: swim**

**3. The sense organ reading water movement is the _____ line.**
**Answer: lateral**

**4. The main thrust comes from the _____ fin.**
**Answer: tail**
