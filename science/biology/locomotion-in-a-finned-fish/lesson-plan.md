# Lesson plan — Locomotion in a finned fish

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Locomotion in a finned fish
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — secondary band (CURRICULUM.md Unit 16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A fish swims with muscle blocks, a gas float and a kit of fins: Kenya's Form 4 book teaches locomotion in the tilapia — streamlining, overlapping scales and mucus, myotomes, the swim bladder and buoyancy, the lateral line, and paired against unpaired fins — with a fish-examination practical and a tail-power calculation.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier biology lessons and builds the secondary-band understanding of locomotion in a finned fish.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
