The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: How plants respond — light, water, gravity and auxin.

Remember this one thing: shoots grow toward light and roots toward water and gravity — steered by auxin, which speeds shoot growth and slows root growth

Toward light: Shoots grow toward light.

Roots respond: Roots grow toward water and toward gravity.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
