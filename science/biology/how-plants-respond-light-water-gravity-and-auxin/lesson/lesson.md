## The idea
Plants cannot walk, but they grow toward what they need. One hormone — auxin — steers the bending, and Darwin's experiments are the evidence history.

## The key facts
**Toward light.** Shoots grow toward light.

**Roots respond.** Roots grow toward water and toward gravity.

**Auxin.** Auxin is the plant hormone that steers these growth responses.

**Auxin's two effects.** Auxin affects shoot and root growth differently — it speeds the shoot and slows the root.

**Darwin's evidence.** Darwin's phototropism experiments are the classic evidence for the bending story.
