# How plants respond — light, water, gravity and auxin — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about toward light?
a) Shoots grow toward light  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Shoots grow toward light**

**2.** What does this lesson say about roots respond?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Roots grow toward water and toward gravity  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Roots grow toward water and toward gravity**

**3.** What does this lesson say about auxin?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Auxin is the plant hormone that steers these growth responses  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Auxin is the plant hormone that steers these growth responses**

**4.** What does this lesson say about auxin's two effects?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Auxin affects shoot and root growth differently — it speeds the shoot and slows the root
**Answer: d) Auxin affects shoot and root growth differently — it speeds the shoot and slows the root**
