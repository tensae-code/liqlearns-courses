# The phosphorus cycle

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The phosphorus cycle. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
One cycle never touches the sky. Phosphorus creeps from rock to soil to living things to sediment, slower than any other cycle in this unit.

[Picture 22275 — the phosphorus cycle drawn from rock weathering to lake sediment]

## The key facts
**No air stage.** Phosphorus cycles without an air stage.

**The stations.** Its route runs rock, soil, organisms and sediment.

**The slowest.** The phosphorus cycle is the slowest of the cycles taught here.

**Rock weathering.** The cycle begins as rock weathers into soil.
