# Compensation point — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) the compensation point is the light intensity at which a plant's photosynthesis exactly balances its respiration — the plant makes as much food as it burns, so there is no net gain and no net loss  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
**Answer: a) the compensation point is the light intensity at which a plant's photosynthesis exactly balances its respiration — the plant makes as much food as it burns, so there is no net gain and no net loss**

**2. Two opposite flows — which statement is right?**
a) Photosynthesis takes in carbon dioxide and builds food; respiration releases carbon dioxide and burns food. Both run in a living leaf — respiration day and night, photosynthesis only in light.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) Photosynthesis takes in carbon dioxide and builds food; respiration releases carbon dioxide and burns food. Both run in a living leaf — respiration day and night, photosynthesis only in light.**

**3. The balance point — which statement is right?**
a) It is unrelated to the lesson's topic  b) As light grows stronger, photosynthesis speeds up. At one light intensity the rate of photosynthesis exactly equals the rate of respiration: that intensity is the compensation point.  c) It is a myth the book corrects  d) It applies only outside Malaysia
**Answer: b) As light grows stronger, photosynthesis speeds up. At one light intensity the rate of photosynthesis exactly equals the rate of respiration: that intensity is the compensation point.**
