# Compensation point — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: the compensation point is the light intensity at which a plant's photosynthesis exactly balances its respiration — the plant makes as much food as it burns, so there is no net gain and no net loss. Photosynthesis takes in carbon dioxide and builds food; respiration releases carbon dioxide and burns food. Both run in a living leaf — respiration day and night, photosynthesis only in light.**

**2.** Explain Malaysia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Malaysia's KSSM Form 5 book gives the compensation point its own place in the leaf chapter (Ch.2, printed p. 52), after photosynthesis — the leaf's two gas flows set against each other and the balance named.**
