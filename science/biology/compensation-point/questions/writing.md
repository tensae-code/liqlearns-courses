# Compensation point — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: the compensation point is the light intensity at which a plant's photosynthesis exactly balances its respiration — the plant makes as much food as it burns, so there is no net gain and no net loss. As light grows stronger, photosynthesis speeds up. At one light intensity the rate of photosynthesis exactly equals the rate of respiration: that intensity is the compensation point.**
