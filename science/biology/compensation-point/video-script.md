Today: Compensation point.

Remember this one thing: the compensation point is the light intensity at which a plant's photosynthesis exactly balances its respiration — the plant makes as much food as it burns, so there is no net gain and no net loss.

Inside its leaf chapter, Malaysia's Form 5 book names a precise balance point (printed p. 52): the compensation point. A plant is always respiring and, in light, photosynthesising; the compensation point is where the two rates are equal.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
