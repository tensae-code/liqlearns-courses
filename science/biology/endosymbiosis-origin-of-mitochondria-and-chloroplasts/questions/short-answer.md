# Endosymbiosis: the origin of mitochondria and chloroplasts — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: endosymbiosis, in one or two sentences.
**Answer: Endosymbiosis is the hypothesis that mitochondria and chloroplasts began as free-living bacteria taken inside another cell, where they stayed: the guest supplied energy release or photosynthesis, the host supplied shelter and materials — an ancient pact that turned guests into organelles.**

**2.** Explain: Italy's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Italy's Grade 9 Campbell (*Biologia: concetti e collegamenti PLUS, Primo biennio*, Linx/Pearson Italia) closes Unità 2, its journey inside the cell, with the endosymbiosis hypothesis — Lynn Margulis, 1967 — for the origin of mitochondria and chloroplasts, taught as an “ancient pact” (printed p. 62).**
