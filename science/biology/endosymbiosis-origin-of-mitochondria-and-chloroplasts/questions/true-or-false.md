# Endosymbiosis: the origin of mitochondria and chloroplasts — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. On the endosymbiosis hypothesis, chloroplasts were once free-living bacteria.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Endosymbiosis says the host cell digested the bacterium for food.**
**Answer: False** — the bacterium was kept alive inside the host — a pact, not a meal.

**3. The hypothesis is credited to Lynn Margulis, 1967.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. Mitochondria look like small cells of their own, and the hypothesis explains that resemblance.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
