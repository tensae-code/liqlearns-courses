# Endosymbiosis: the origin of mitochondria and chloroplasts — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining endosymbiosis to a younger student. Use the words ancient pact and Margulis once each.
**Model answer: Mitochondria and chloroplasts were not always parts of the cell. Long ago they were bacteria living freely, until a larger cell took one inside and kept it — the guest made energy or caught sunlight, and the host gave it shelter, an ancient pact that never ended. The scientist who set out this account was Lynn Margulis, in 1967.**
