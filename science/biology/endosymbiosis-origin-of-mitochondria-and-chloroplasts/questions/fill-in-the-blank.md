# Endosymbiosis: the origin of mitochondria and chloroplasts — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The hypothesis that mitochondria and chloroplasts began as bacteria inside another cell is called _____.**
**Answer: endosymbiosis**

**2. The book teaches the partnership as an ancient _____.**
**Answer: pact**

**3. The hypothesis is credited to Lynn _____, 1967.**
**Answer: Margulis**

**4. The energy-releasing organelle that began as a bacterium is the _____.**
**Answer: mitochondrion**
