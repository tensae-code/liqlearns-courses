# Endosymbiosis: the origin of mitochondria and chloroplasts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What does the endosymbiosis hypothesis claim?**
a) Mitochondria were built by the nucleus  b) Mitochondria and chloroplasts began as free-living bacteria that took up life inside another cell  c) All organelles came from outside the cell  d) Bacteria evolved from mitochondria
**Answer: b) Mitochondria and chloroplasts began as free-living bacteria that took up life inside another cell**

**2. Why does the book call it an “ancient pact”?**
a) Because the two cells signed a treaty  b) Because guest and host each gained — energy or photosynthesis from the guest, shelter and materials from the host — so the partnership lasted  c) Because it happened in ancient Greece  d) Because the bacterium was eaten
**Answer: b) Because guest and host each gained — energy or photosynthesis from the guest, shelter and materials from the host — so the partnership lasted**

**3. Who proposed the hypothesis, and when, as the book gives it?**
a) Darwin, 1859  b) Lynn Margulis, 1967  c) Sadava, 2015  d) Margulis, 1900
**Answer: b) Lynn Margulis, 1967**

**4. Where does Italy's Grade 9 Campbell teach endosymbiosis?**
a) At the close of Unità 2, the journey inside the cell  b) In the ecology unit  c) In the human-body volume  d) In the PLUS heredity unit
**Answer: a) At the close of Unità 2, the journey inside the cell**
