# Hands-on — Endosymbiosis: the origin of mitochondria and chloroplasts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Tell the ancient pact as a story to your companion, in three scenes — the free bacterium, the taking-in, the partnership — without looking at the lesson. Your companion listens and names anything you left out; then swap.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the three panels of the ancient pact — and label them “free-living bacterium”, “taken inside” and “mitochondrion inside the host cell”. Check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
