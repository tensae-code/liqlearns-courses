# Doping — cheating with substances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about definition?
a) Doping is using prohibited substances or methods to gain unfair advantage
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Doping is using prohibited substances or methods to gain unfair advantage**

**2.** What does this lesson say about examples?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Doping includes anabolic agents and human growth hormone, beta blockers in shooting sports, diuretics, blood doping and gene manipulation
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Doping includes anabolic agents and human growth hormone, beta blockers in shooting sports, diuretics, blood doping and gene manipulation**

**3.** What does this lesson say about the list?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Prohibited substances are governed by a Prohibited List
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Prohibited substances are governed by a Prohibited List**

**4.** What does this lesson say about two of three?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) A substance joins the list if it meets two of three criteria: performance potential, health risk, and violating the spirit of sport
**Answer: d) A substance joins the list if it meets two of three criteria: performance potential, health risk, and violating the spirit of sport**
