# Doping — cheating with substances

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Doping — cheating with substances. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
A substance joins the Prohibited List if it meets two of three criteria: performance potential, health risk, violating the spirit of sport.

[Picture 21064 — a race finish line with one lane crossed out]

## The key facts
**Definition.** Doping is using prohibited substances or methods to gain unfair advantage.

**Examples.** Doping includes anabolic agents and human growth hormone, beta blockers in shooting sports, diuretics, blood doping and gene manipulation.

**The list.** Prohibited substances are governed by a Prohibited List.

**Two of three.** A substance joins the list if it meets two of three criteria: performance potential, health risk, and violating the spirit of sport.
