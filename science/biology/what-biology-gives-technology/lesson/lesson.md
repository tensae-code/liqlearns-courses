# What biology gives technology

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — what biology gives technology. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Biological knowledge feeds technology by supplying materials and designs to imitate, routes to curing genetic conditions and making biosynthetic repair materials for damaged tissues, termite-mound building design, and the kingfisher-beak nose of the bullet train; pupils research the natural source behind hexagonal structures, a new drone, live-vaccine preservation, repellent spray and cutting saws.
