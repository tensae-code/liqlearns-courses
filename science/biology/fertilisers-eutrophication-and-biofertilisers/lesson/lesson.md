# Fertilisers, eutrophication and biofertilisers

## Do it first

## The idea
The cycles meet the farm. Three applied ideas close the unit: the cell's own energy coin, what happens when fertiliser washes into water, and the living fertilisers that work without the damage.

[Picture 22276 — a green, algae-choked pond beside a clear one]

## The key facts
**ATP.** ATP is the cell's immediate energy source, harvested in cellular respiration.

**Eutrophication.** Eutrophication is excessive algal and plant growth in water bodies fed by leached nitrogen and phosphorus fertilisers.

**Biofertilisers.** Biofertilisers are cheap, non-toxic, eco-friendly substitutes for chemical fertilisers.

**Sustainable yield.** Biofertilisers raise yield while keeping farming sustainable.
