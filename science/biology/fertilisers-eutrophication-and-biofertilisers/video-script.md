Today: Fertilisers, eutrophication and biofertilisers.

Remember this one thing: ATP is the cell's immediate energy source; eutrophication is excessive growth in water fed by leached fertilisers; biofertilisers are cheap, non-toxic, eco-friendly substitutes

Atp: ATP is the cell's immediate energy source, harvested in cellular respiration.

Eutrophication: Eutrophication is excessive algal and plant growth in water bodies fed by leached nitrogen and phosphorus fertilisers.
