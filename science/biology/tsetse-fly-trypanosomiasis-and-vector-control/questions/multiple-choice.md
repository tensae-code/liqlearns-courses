# Tsetse fly, trypanosomiasis and vector control — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The tsetse fly transmits…
a) malaria
b) trypanosomiasis — sleeping sickness and nagana
c) cholera
d) typhoid
**Answer: b) trypanosomiasis — sleeping sickness and nagana**

**2.** Why does the book teach the tsetse's life history before its control?
a) For decoration
b) Control is built on how and where the fly lives, rests and breeds
c) To fill the chapter
d) Life history is not taught
**Answer: b) Control is built on how and where the fly lives, rests and breeds**

**3.** Which control measure uses the fly's resting behaviour?
a) Breeding resistant cattle
b) Clearing shade resting places
c) Treating patients
d) Draining swamps
**Answer: b) Clearing shade resting places**

**4.** Why can't the disease be ended by removing antelopes?
a) Antelopes are too fast
b) They carry the parasite harmlessly as reservoirs — control acts on the fly and on prompt treatment instead
c) Antelopes do not carry it
d) They are protected pets
**Answer: b) They carry the parasite harmlessly as reservoirs — control acts on the fly and on prompt treatment instead**
