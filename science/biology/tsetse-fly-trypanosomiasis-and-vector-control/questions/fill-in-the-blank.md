# Tsetse fly, trypanosomiasis and vector control — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** Sleeping sickness and nagana together are called _____.
**Answer: trypanosomiasis**

**2.** The tsetse rests in _____, which control programmes clear.
**Answer: shade (resting places)**

**3.** Cattle bred to resist nagana are _____ cattle.
**Answer: nagana-resistant**
