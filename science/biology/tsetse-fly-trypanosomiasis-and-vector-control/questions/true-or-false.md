# Tsetse fly, trypanosomiasis and vector control — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Nagana is the cattle form of trypanosomiasis carried by the tsetse.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Spraying resting sites is one of the book's tsetse controls.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Antelopes suffer severely from the trypanosomes they carry.
**Answer: False** — Antelopes carry the parasite harmlessly — that is what makes them reservoirs.
