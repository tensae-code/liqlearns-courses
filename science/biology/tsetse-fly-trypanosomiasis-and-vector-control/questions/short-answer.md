# Tsetse fly, trypanosomiasis and vector control — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: how tsetse control is built on the fly's life history.
**Answer: The tsetse rests in shade, so clearing shade resting places removes its shelter; spraying is aimed at vehicles and resting sites where the fly is found; nagana-resistant cattle withstand the cattle disease; and prompt treatment of sleeping sickness cuts the human reservoir the fly feeds on. Each measure answers a fact of the fly's life.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book teaches control of disease-causing microorganisms through their vectors in Chapter 12 (printed p. 174): the mosquito programme, then the tsetse fly's full life history and the control built on it — clearing shade resting places, spraying vehicles and resting sites, breeding nagana-resistant cattle, prompt treatment of sleeping sickness; antelopes as harmless reservoirs.**
