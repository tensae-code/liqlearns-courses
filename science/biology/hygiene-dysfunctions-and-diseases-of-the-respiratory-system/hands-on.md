# Hands-on — Hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the respiratory system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: the respiratory system is protected by hygiene against its risk factors and diseases, and infectious patients cover mouth and nose and use strictly individual utensils, linen and objects so the disease does not spread.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a person covering mouth and nose when coughing, with separate labelled utensils, linen and personal objects kept apart from others'. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
