Today: Hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the respiratory system.

Remember this one thing: the respiratory system is protected by hygiene against its risk factors and diseases, and infectious patients cover mouth and nose and use strictly individual utensils, linen and objects so the disease does not spread.

Moldova's Grade 11 book (§32 Hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the respiratory system) teaches: risk factors; infectious patients covering mouth and nose and using strictly individual utensils, linen and objects. (printed p. 105).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
