# Hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the respiratory system — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Moldova's Grade 11 book (§32 Hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the respiratory system) teaches: risk factors; infectious patients covering mouth and nose and using strictly individual utensils, linen and objects. (printed p. 105).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Moldova's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Biologie — Procese și sisteme vitale: Manual pentru clasa a XI-a (Profil real / Profil umanist) (Grade 11)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the respiratory system.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the respiratory system.
