# Hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the respiratory system — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: the respiratory system is protected by hygiene against its risk factors and diseases, and infectious patients cover mouth and nose and use strictly individual utensils, linen and objects so the disease does not spread. Moldova's Grade 11 book (§32 Hygiene, dysfunctions and diseases of the respiratory system) teaches: risk factors; infectious patients covering mouth and nose and using strictly individual utensils, linen and objects. (printed p. 105).**

**2.** Explain Moldova's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Moldova's Grade 11 book gives respiration its own hygiene section: risk factors, and the rule that infectious patients cover mouth and nose and use strictly individual utensils, linen and objects.**
