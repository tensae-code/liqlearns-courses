Today: Identifying plant families.

Remember this one thing: plant families are recognised by the build of their flowers and inflorescences — the grass family, Gramineae, by its raceme, panicle and spike, and the Compositae by many small florets packed into one head — a working key taught on Kazakhstan's grassland plants.

Kazakhstan's grassland agriculture as the teaching ground

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
