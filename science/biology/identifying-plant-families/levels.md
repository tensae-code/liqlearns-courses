# Identifying plant families — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (plant families are recognised by the build of their flowers and inflorescences — the grass family, Gramineae, by its raceme, panicle and spike, and the Compositae by many small florets packed into one head — a working key taught on Kazakhstan's grassland plants) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
