# Tropical rainforest — structure and adaptations — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** Rain-forest leaves shed water through pointed _____ tips.
**Answer: drip**

**2.** Trees hold tall trunks in thin soil with flaring _____ roots.
**Answer: buttress**

**3.** Animals that live in the trees are called _____.
**Answer: arboreal**
