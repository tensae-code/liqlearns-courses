# Tropical rainforest — structure and adaptations — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** The rain forest grows in storeys — layers from floor to canopy.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Shade plants in the rain forest are adapted to strong direct sunlight.
**Answer: False** — Shade plants are fitted to the dim light of the forest floor.

**3.** The book builds forest food chains from the species actually found in the study.
**Answer: True**
