# Tropical rainforest — structure and adaptations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is a drip tip for?
a) Catching insects
b) Shedding water from the leaf
c) Storing food
d) Attracting birds
**Answer: b) Shedding water from the leaf**

**2.** Buttress roots help a rain-forest tree by…
a) storing water
b) holding the tall trunk up in thin soil
c) making fruit
d) scaring animals
**Answer: b) holding the tall trunk up in thin soil**

**3.** Cauliflory means…
a) flowers and fruit borne directly on the trunk
b) leaves falling all year
c) roots above ground
d) climbing by tendrils
**Answer: a) flowers and fruit borne directly on the trunk**

**4.** How do climbers solve the forest's darkness?
a) They photosynthesise without light
b) They climb other plants to reach the light
c) They grow underground
d) They wait for trees to fall only
**Answer: b) They climb other plants to reach the light**
