# Tropical rainforest — structure and adaptations — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the rain forest's structure and three plant adaptations.
**Answer: The forest stands in storeys from floor to canopy. Its plants are fitted to the wet shade: drip tips shed water from leaves; buttress roots hold tall trunks in thin soil; cauliflory bears flowers and fruit on the trunk; climbers climb to reach light; shade plants live in the dim floor. (Any three, explained.)**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book studies the forest practically in Chapter 14 (printed p. 206): storeys, drip tips, buttress roots, cauliflory, climbers and shade plants, arboreal animal adaptations, and forest food chains built from the species found.**
