# Hands-on — Biological waste and lab accident management

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: biological waste from a biology laboratory is sorted and disposed of by set methods — never simply poured away — and a lab accident is met by a set order of steps.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a biology laboratory bench with labelled biological-waste containers beside the sink, a crossed-out arrow showing cultures must not be poured away, and the eye wash and emergency shower marked on the wall. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
