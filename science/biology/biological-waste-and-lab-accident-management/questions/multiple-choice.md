# Biological waste and lab accident management — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) biological waste from a biology laboratory is sorted and disposed of by set methods — never simply poured away — and a lab accident is met by a set order of steps  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
**Answer: a) biological waste from a biology laboratory is sorted and disposed of by set methods — never simply poured away — and a lab accident is met by a set order of steps**

**2. What may not go down the sink — which statement is right?**
a) Not everything from an experiment may be poured away. The book names what may and may not go down the sink — living material, cultures and contaminated waste are not sink waste.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) Not everything from an experiment may be poured away. The book names what may and may not go down the sink — living material, cultures and contaminated waste are not sink waste.**

**3. Biological waste management — which statement is right?**
a) It is unrelated to the lesson's topic  b) Biological waste is managed by set methods: it is collected, treated so it can no longer infect or spread, and disposed of by the prescribed route — the method is part of the experiment, not an afterthought.  c) It is a myth the book corrects  d) It applies only outside Malaysia
**Answer: b) Biological waste is managed by set methods: it is collected, treated so it can no longer infect or spread, and disposed of by the prescribed route — the method is part of the experiment, not an afterthought.**
