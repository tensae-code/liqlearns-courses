# Biological waste and lab accident management — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: biological waste from a biology laboratory is sorted and disposed of by set methods — never simply poured away — and a lab accident is met by a set order of steps. Not everything from an experiment may be poured away. The book names what may and may not go down the sink — living material, cultures and contaminated waste are not sink waste.**

**2.** Explain Malaysia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: In Malaysia's KSSM course this is named, examined content inside Chapter 1 of the Form 4 book (printed p. 6): lab safety equipment is taught as content, and waste disposal and accident procedure stand beside it as their own subtopic.**
