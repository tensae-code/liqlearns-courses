# Limiting factors and Blackman's law — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Photosynthesis needs several things at once — light, carbon dioxide, warmth — and its rate is set by whichever of them is in shortest supply: that is Blackman's law of limiting factors, which says the rate is governed by the factor nearest its minimum, so raising any other factor does nothing until the limiting one is raised.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
