# Hands-on — Limiting factors and Blackman's law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Put two identical potted seedlings on a windowsill; shade one with a card screen so it gets half the light. After a week compare growth — the shaded plant shows light as its limiting factor while everything else stayed the same.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the rising-then-flattening rate graph, with the flat part labelled with the new limiting factor — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
