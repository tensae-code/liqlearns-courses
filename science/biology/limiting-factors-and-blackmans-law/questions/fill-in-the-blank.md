# Limiting factors and Blackman's law — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The rate of a process is set by the factor nearest its _____ — that is Blackman's law.**
**Answer: minimum**

**2. When a graph of rate against light flattens, light is no longer the _____ factor.**
**Answer: limiting**

**3. Glucose made in photosynthesis can be stored as _____.**
**Answer: starch**

**4. Three factors that can limit photosynthesis are light, carbon dioxide and _____.**
**Answer: temperature**
