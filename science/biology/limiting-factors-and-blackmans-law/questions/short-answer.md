# Limiting factors and Blackman's law — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: Blackman's law in your own words.
**Answer: When a process needs several factors at once, its rate is governed by the factor nearest its minimum — the scarcest need. Raising any other factor does not raise the rate until the limiting one is raised.**

**2.** Explain: Bhutan's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Bhutan's Class 10 book (Ch. 2.1, printed pp. 37–38) states Blackman's law of limiting factors by name after the factors affecting the rate of photosynthesis, and pairs it with the utilisation of photosynthesis products.**
