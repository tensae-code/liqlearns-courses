# Limiting factors and Blackman's law — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining to a greenhouse grower how to use Blackman's law.
**Model answer: First find which factor is nearest its minimum — that factor alone is setting your plants' rate. Raise that one and growth can climb; raising light, warmth or carbon dioxide that is already plentiful buys nothing until the limiting factor is dealt with.**
