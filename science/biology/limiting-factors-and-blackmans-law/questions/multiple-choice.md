# Limiting factors and Blackman's law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Blackman's law says the rate of photosynthesis is set by…**
a) the most plentiful factor  b) the factor nearest its minimum  c) the temperature alone  d) the size of the leaf
**Answer: b) the factor nearest its minimum**

**2. A rate graph rises with light, then flattens. The flattening means…**
a) photosynthesis has stopped  b) light is no longer the limiting factor — another factor is  c) the plant is dead  d) carbon dioxide has run out everywhere
**Answer: b) light is no longer the limiting factor — another factor is**

**3. To raise the rate when carbon dioxide is limiting, you should…**
a) add more light only  b) raise the carbon dioxide  c) cool the plant  d) cover the leaves
**Answer: b) raise the carbon dioxide**

**4. Which of these is a factor that can limit photosynthesis?**
a) Light intensity  b) Carbon dioxide concentration  c) Temperature  d) All of these
**Answer: d) All of these**
