# Limiting factors and Blackman's law — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Raising a factor that is not limiting raises the rate.**
**Answer: False** — only raising the limiting factor raises the rate; the others cannot make up for it.

**2. Blackman's law is named for the factor in shortest supply setting the rate.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. A plant uses the products of photosynthesis only for storage.**
**Answer: False** — glucose is used for energy, stored as starch, and built into the plant's other substances.

**4. Temperature can be a limiting factor for photosynthesis.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
