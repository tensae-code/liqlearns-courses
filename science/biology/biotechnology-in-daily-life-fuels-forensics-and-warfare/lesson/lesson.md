# Biotechnology in daily life — fuels, forensics and warfare

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — biotechnology in daily life. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Model answers cover: biofuels made by fermenting sugar/starch crops to ethanol for blending with petrol, and oilseed crops (canola, sunflower) for biodiesel; biodegradable wastes (break down naturally) vs non-biodegradable (do not degrade easily); and a forensic-science/biological-warfare block defining germ warfare — using bacteria, viruses, insects or fungi to kill or incapacitate people, animals or plants — and asking why it is prohibited.
