# Hands-on — Screening microbes at scale — high-throughput screening

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: high-throughput screening searches the microbial kingdom at scale: thousands of mutagenised strains cultured in microplates and micro-bioreactors under automated handling and computer analysis — how industry searches a kingdom of which under 1% can yet be cultured.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a grid plate of hundreds of tiny wells in rows, a robot arm above it and a screen showing one well picked out in green. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
