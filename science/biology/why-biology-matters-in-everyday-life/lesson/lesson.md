# Why biology matters in everyday life

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Why biology matters in everyday life. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Biology is not only for the classroom. It helps make medicines, improves farming, solves crimes, and explains how ecosystems and our own bodies work.

[Picture 29201 — a four-panel strip of biology at work]

## The key facts
**Medicine.** Biology helps develop medicines and treatments for diseases such as AIDS, tuberculosis and some cancers.

**Agriculture.** Genetics and cell biology are changing medicine and agriculture.

**Forensic biology.** Forensic biology is used in crime investigation.

**Fermentation.** Fermentation, a biological process, is used to make drinks.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words: studying biology explains living things and touches daily life — medicine, farming, crime investigation and food-making. Use each key fact once. Then let your companion explain it back to you.
