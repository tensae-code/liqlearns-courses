# Why biology matters in everyday life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about medicine?
a) Biology helps develop medicines and treatments for diseases such as AIDS, tuberculosis and some cancers
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Biology helps develop medicines and treatments for diseases such as AIDS, tuberculosis and some cancers**

**2.** What does this lesson say about agriculture?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Genetics and cell biology are changing medicine and agriculture
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Genetics and cell biology are changing medicine and agriculture**

**3.** What does this lesson say about forensic biology?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Forensic biology is used in crime investigation
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Forensic biology is used in crime investigation**

**4.** What does this lesson say about fermentation?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Fermentation, a biological process, is used to make drinks
**Answer: d) Fermentation, a biological process, is used to make drinks**
