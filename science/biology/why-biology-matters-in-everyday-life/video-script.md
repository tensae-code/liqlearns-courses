Today: Why biology matters in everyday life.

Remember this one thing: studying biology explains living things and touches daily life — medicine, farming, crime investigation and food-making.

Biology helps develop medicines and treatments for diseases such as AIDS, tuberculosis and some cancers.

Genetics and cell biology are changing medicine and agriculture.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
