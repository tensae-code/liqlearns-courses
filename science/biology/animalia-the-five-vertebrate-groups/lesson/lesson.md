# Animalia — the five vertebrate groups

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Animalia — the five vertebrate groups. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
The five groups are told apart by skin covering, breathing, body temperature control and how their young develop.

[Picture 21023 — one fish, frog, lizard, bird and mammal in a labelled row]

## The key facts
**The five groups.** The vertebrate classes are fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds and mammals.

**Skin.** Vertebrate groups differ in skin covering.

**Temperature.** Vertebrate groups differ in body temperature control.

**Young.** Vertebrate groups differ in how their young develop.

## Say it to your companion
