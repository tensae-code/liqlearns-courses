# Sex-linked traits and phenocopies — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about sex-linked inheritance and environmental effects on phenotype?**
**Answer: Genes on the X chromosome show in males from a single copy (no second X to mask them), so X-linked recessive conditions such as haemophilia pass from carrier mothers to sons, worked as a full cross; phenotype is not genes alone — environment can mimic a genotype (the Himalayan rabbit's coat darkening in the cold, a phenocopy).**
