# Sex-linked traits and phenocopies — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Genes on the X chromosome show in males from a single copy (no second X to mask them), so X-linked recessive conditions such as haemophilia pass from carrier mothers to sons, worked as a full cross; phenotype is not genes alone — environment can mimic a genotype (the Himalayan rabbit's coat darkening in the cold, a phenocopy).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
