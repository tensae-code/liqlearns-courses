# Video script — Sex-linked traits and phenocopies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Genes on the X chromosome show in males from a single copy (no second X to mask them), so X-linked recessive conditions such as haemophilia pass from carrier mothers to sons, worked as a full cross; phenotype is not genes alone — environment can mimic a genotype (the Himalayan rabbit's coat darkening in the cold, a phenocopy).

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
