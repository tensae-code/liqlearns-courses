# Productivity of ecosystems — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (an ecosystem's productivity is the rate at which it builds living matter: primary production by its plants — gross, before their own respiration is subtracted, and net, after — and secondary production by the animals that eat them, the measure that decides how much life a place can support) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
