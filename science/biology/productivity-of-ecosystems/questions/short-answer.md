# Productivity of ecosystems — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: an ecosystem's productivity is the rate at which it builds living matter: primary production by its plants — gross, before their own respiration is subtracted, and net, after — and secondary production by the animals that eat them, the measure that decides how much life a place can support. the concept of productivity (primary/secondary production)**

**2.** Explain Greece's way of teaching this.
**Answer: the concept of productivity (primary/secondary production)**
