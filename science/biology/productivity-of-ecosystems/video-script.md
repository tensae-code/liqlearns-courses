Today: Productivity of ecosystems.

Remember this one thing: an ecosystem's productivity is the rate at which it builds living matter: primary production by its plants — gross, before their own respiration is subtracted, and net, after — and secondary production by the animals that eat them, the measure that decides how much life a place can support.

the concept of productivity (primary/secondary production)

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
