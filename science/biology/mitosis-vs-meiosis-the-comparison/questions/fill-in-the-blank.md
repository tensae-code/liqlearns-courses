# Mitosis vs meiosis — the comparison — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** _____ one division and makes two daughter cells
**Answer: Mitosis has**

**2.** Mitosis happens in somatic (body) cells; daughter cells are diploid (2n) and identical to the parent, _____.
**Answer: wherever growth and replacement of damaged or worn cells happen**

**3.** _____ keeps the chromosome number, making identical cells for growth and repair
**Answer: Mitosis**
