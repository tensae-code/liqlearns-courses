Today: Mitosis vs meiosis — the comparison.

Remember this one thing: mitosis makes two identical diploid body cells in one division; meiosis makes four haploid sex cells in two divisions.

Mitosis has one division and makes two daughter cells.

Mitosis happens in somatic (body) cells; daughter cells are diploid (2n) and identical to the parent, wherever growth and replacement of damaged or worn cells happen.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.


Student-book core: The student book's own table sets mitosis and meiosis side by side. The comparison covers divisions, daughter cells, where each happens, and what each is for.
