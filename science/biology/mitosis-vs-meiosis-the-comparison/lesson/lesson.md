## The key facts
**Mitosis divisions.** Mitosis has one division and makes two daughter cells.

**Mitosis cells.** Mitosis happens in somatic (body) cells; daughter cells are diploid (2n) and identical to the parent, wherever growth and replacement of damaged or worn cells happen.

**Meiosis divisions.** Meiosis has two divisions and makes four daughter cells.

**Meiosis cells.** Meiosis makes sex cells (gametes), haploid (n) with chromosome number halved, restricted to the gonads (testes and ovaries).
