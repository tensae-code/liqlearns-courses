# Global food production and the food gap — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: the world's food production does not reach everyone evenly — that unevenness is the food gap — and raising yields, as the green revolution did with new varieties and fertilisers, is humanity's main answer, with its costs and limits. Module 10: How can humans contribute to global food production? The green revolution; increasing yield; the food gap**
