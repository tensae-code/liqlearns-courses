# Global food production and the food gap

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Global food production and the food gap. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
The world's food production does not reach everyone evenly — that unevenness is the food gap — and raising yields, as the green revolution did with new varieties and fertilisers, is humanity's main answer, with its costs and limits. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from Kazakhstan's Grade 7–9 biology books, which teach it as a named topic.
