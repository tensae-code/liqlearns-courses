# Hands-on — Global food production and the food gap

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: the world's food production does not reach everyone evenly — that unevenness is the food gap — and raising yields, as the green revolution did with new varieties and fertilisers, is humanity's main answer, with its costs and limits.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a world map with full granaries in one region and empty bowls in another, joined by a green-revolution wheat field drawn between them. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
