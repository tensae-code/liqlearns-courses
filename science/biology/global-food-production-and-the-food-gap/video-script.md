Today: Global food production and the food gap.

Remember this one thing: the world's food production does not reach everyone evenly — that unevenness is the food gap — and raising yields, as the green revolution did with new varieties and fertilisers, is humanity's main answer, with its costs and limits.

Module 10: How can humans contribute to global food production? The green revolution; increasing yield; the food gap

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
