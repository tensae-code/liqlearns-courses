# Classification — grouping by shared characteristics

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Classification — grouping by shared characteristics. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
There are too many living things to study one by one. Grouping by what they share makes the variety manageable.

[GIF 21105 — the jumble sorting itself into groups]

## The key facts
**Purpose.** Classification organises the huge variety of organisms so they are easier to find, study and understand.

**Basis.** Organisms are grouped by shared characteristics — observable similarities and differences.

**Not one feature.** Grouping does not rest on a single striking feature alone.

**Shared characteristics.** Shared characteristics are features that members of a group have in common.
