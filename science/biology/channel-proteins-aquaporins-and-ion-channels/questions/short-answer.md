# Channel proteins — aquaporins and ion channels — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: channel proteins are fitted pores through the membrane — aquaporins pass water and ion channels pass particular ions — a whole class of membrane proteins with its own discovery story, from Agre's water channel (1988) to MacKinnon's potassium-channel structure (1998). Agre isolating the water channel in 1988 (13 aquaporins now known in human cells, 35 in Arabidopsis), Neher and Sakmann's patch clamp (Nobel 1991), MacKinnon's potassium-channel structure (1998), and the shared 2003 chemistry Nobel — a whole class of membrane proteins with its own discovery story, held by no existing lesson.**
