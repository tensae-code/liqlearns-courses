Today: Channel proteins — aquaporins and ion channels.

Remember this one thing: channel proteins are fitted pores through the membrane — aquaporins pass water and ion channels pass particular ions — a whole class of membrane proteins with its own discovery story, from Agre's water channel (1988) to MacKinnon's potassium-channel structure (1998).

Agre isolating the water channel in 1988 (13 aquaporins now known in human cells, 35 in Arabidopsis), Neher and Sakmann's patch clamp (Nobel 1991), MacKinnon's potassium-channel structure (1998), and the shared 2003 chemistry Nobel — a whole class of membrane proteins with its own discovery story, held by no existing lesson.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
