# Channel proteins — aquaporins and ion channels — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (channel proteins are fitted pores through the membrane — aquaporins pass water and ion channels pass particular ions — a whole class of membrane proteins with its own discovery story, from Agre's water channel (1988) to MacKinnon's potassium-channel structure (1998)) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
