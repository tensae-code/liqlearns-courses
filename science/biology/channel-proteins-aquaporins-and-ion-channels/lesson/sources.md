Structural summary: A "scientist interview" frontispiece (structural biologist Shi Yigong 施一公： leaving a Princeton chair for Tsinghua in 2008, his spliceosome cryo-EM structures) opens the volume. Every chapter teaches its science methods by name — induction, proposing hypotheses, control of variables, contrast experiments, model building — a signature of this edition.
