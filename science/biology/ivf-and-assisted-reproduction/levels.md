# IVF and assisted reproduction — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (assisted reproduction helps where natural conception fails — artificial insemination, IVF and embryo transfer, sperm and egg banks — the technology that gave the world Louise Brown in 1978 and mainland China its first test-tube baby in 1988, held under China's management measures and technical norms so embryo gene testing cannot slide into customised babies) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
