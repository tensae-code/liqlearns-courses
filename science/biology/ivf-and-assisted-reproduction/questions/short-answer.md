# IVF and assisted reproduction — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: assisted reproduction helps where natural conception fails — artificial insemination, IVF and embryo transfer, sperm and egg banks — the technology that gave the world Louise Brown in 1978 and mainland China its first test-tube baby in 1988, held under China's management measures and technical norms so embryo gene testing cannot slide into customised babies. Edwards and Steptoe, Louise Brown in 1978, mainland China's first IVF baby at Peking University Third Hospital in 1988, and roughly four million IVF births by 2010. No existing lesson covers assisted reproduction.**

**2.** Explain China's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Edwards and Steptoe, Louise Brown in 1978, mainland China's first IVF baby at Peking University Third Hospital in 1988, and roughly four million IVF births by 2010. No existing lesson covers assisted reproduction. artificial insemination, IVF and embryo transfer, sperm and egg banks, mainland China's first test-tube baby (1988, Peking University Third Hospital) and nearly 100,000 IVF births since — with the ethics stated plainly: gene testing of embryos could slide into "customised babies", and China's management measures and technical norms for assisted reproduction are cited by name.**
