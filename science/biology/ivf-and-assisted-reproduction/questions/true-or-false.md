# IVF and assisted reproduction — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Edwards and Steptoe, Louise Brown in 1978, mainland China's first IVF baby at Peking University Third Hospital in 1988, and roughly four million IVF births by 2010. No existing lesson covers assisted reproduction.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in China's People's Education Press biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (义务教育教科书《生物学》七年级下册 (Biology, Grade 7, Volume 2, People's Education Press, 2012)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with ivf and assisted reproduction.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is ivf and assisted reproduction.
