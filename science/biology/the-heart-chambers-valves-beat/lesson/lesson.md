# The heart — chambers, valves, beat

## Do it first

## The idea
Open the pump. Four chambers, valves that keep blood moving one way, and a beat you can count — with a mammalian heart dissection as the doing core.

## The key facts
**Four chambers.** The heart has four chambers.

**Valves.** Valves sit between the chambers and in the vessels, keeping flow one way.

**The beat.** The heart's own beat can be explored and heard as heart sounds.

**Dissection.** A mammalian (cow or sheep) heart is dissected in the laboratory activity.
