# Video script — The heart — chambers, valves, beat

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: The heart — chambers, valves, beat.

Remember this one thing: the heart is a live pump of four chambers with valves between them and in the vessels, beating in a rhythm you can hear

Four chambers: The heart has four chambers.

Valves: Valves sit between the chambers and in the vessels, keeping flow one way.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
