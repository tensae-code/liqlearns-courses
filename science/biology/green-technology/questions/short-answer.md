# Green technology — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: green technology is technology designed to protect the environment — producing energy and goods and handling waste in ways that use fewer resources and do less harm, so development can be sustained. A technology is green when it is designed to lessen harm to the environment — cleaner energy, less waste, fewer resources used — not merely when it is new or efficient for its owner.**

**2.** Explain Malaysia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Malaysia's KSSM Form 5 book closes its sustainability chapter (Ch.10) with green technology by name (printed p. 218) — the Malaysian course's own term, taught as the technological arm of sustainability.**
