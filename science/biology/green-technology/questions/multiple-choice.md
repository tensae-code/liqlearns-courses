# Green technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) green technology is technology designed to protect the environment — producing energy and goods and handling waste in ways that use fewer resources and do less harm, so development can be sustained  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
**Answer: a) green technology is technology designed to protect the environment — producing energy and goods and handling waste in ways that use fewer resources and do less harm, so development can be sustained**

**2. What 'green' means here — which statement is right?**
a) A technology is green when it is designed to lessen harm to the environment — cleaner energy, less waste, fewer resources used — not merely when it is new or efficient for its owner.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) A technology is green when it is designed to lessen harm to the environment — cleaner energy, less waste, fewer resources used — not merely when it is new or efficient for its owner.**

**3. Where it applies — which statement is right?**
a) It is unrelated to the lesson's topic  b) Green technology runs across the chapter's ground: renewable energy in place of fossil fuels, treatment and recycling of waste, buildings and transport that use less, and technology that helps restore damaged ecosystems.  c) It is a myth the book corrects  d) It applies only outside Malaysia
**Answer: b) Green technology runs across the chapter's ground: renewable energy in place of fossil fuels, treatment and recycling of waste, buildings and transport that use less, and technology that helps restore damaged ecosystems.**
