# Green technology — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: green technology is technology designed to protect the environment — producing energy and goods and handling waste in ways that use fewer resources and do less harm, so development can be sustained. Green technology runs across the chapter's ground: renewable energy in place of fossil fuels, treatment and recycling of waste, buildings and transport that use less, and technology that helps restore damaged ecosystems.**
