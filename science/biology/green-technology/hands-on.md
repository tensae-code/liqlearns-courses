# Hands-on — Green technology

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: green technology is technology designed to protect the environment — producing energy and goods and handling waste in ways that use fewer resources and do less harm, so development can be sustained.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a landscape combining solar panels, a wind turbine, a recycling plant and a replanted forest slope, labelled as green technology in practice. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
