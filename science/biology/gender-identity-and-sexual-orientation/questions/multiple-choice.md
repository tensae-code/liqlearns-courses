# Gender identity and sexual orientation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where does France's manual teach identities and orientations?
a) In the earth-science strand, with erosion
b) Inside "Cerveau, plaisir et sexualité" (Brain, pleasure and sexuality), in the part "Identités et orientations"
c) In the evolution chapter, with natural selection
d) It does not teach them
**Answer: b) Inside "Cerveau, plaisir et sexualité" (Brain, pleasure and sexuality), in the part "Identités et orientations"**

**2.** What is a gender stereotype, as the manual teaches it?
a) A chromosome pattern
b) A hormone of puberty
c) An expectation a society pins on a gender — how people of that gender are supposed to be and behave
d) A disease of the reproductive system
**Answer: c) An expectation a society pins on a gender — how people of that gender are supposed to be and behave**

**3.** Why does the manual bring in the Khasi society?
a) The Khasi are a matrilineal society — a counter-example showing the roles attached to a gender are not the same everywhere
b) The Khasi discovered the nerve cell
c) The Khasi have no genders
d) The Khasi live in France
**Answer: a) The Khasi are a matrilineal society — a counter-example showing the roles attached to a gender are not the same everywhere**

**4.** What does the manual trace at the close of the part?
a) The descent of the testes
b) The tick's development cycle
c) The history of vaccination
d) The evolution of LGBT rights in France
**Answer: d) The evolution of LGBT rights in France**
