# Gender identity and sexual orientation — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: how the Khasi counter-example answers a gender stereotype.
**Answer: A stereotype claims the roles attached to a gender are simply how things are. The Khasi are a matrilineal society — descent and the family line pass through the mother — so one whole society organises those roles the other way. That shows the roles are made by societies, not fixed by biology.**

**2.** Explain: France's way of teaching identity and orientation.
**Answer: France's Seconde manual (SVT 2de GT, Les Manuels Libres) teaches them in Thème 3, "Corps humain et santé", inside the chapter "Cerveau, plaisir et sexualité", in the part "Identités et orientations": what gender is, stereotypes (with the matrilineal Khasi society), attraction, and the evolution of LGBT rights in France.**
