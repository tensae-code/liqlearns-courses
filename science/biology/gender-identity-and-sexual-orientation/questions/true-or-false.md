# Gender identity and sexual orientation — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** France teaches identity and orientation inside its Seconde biology manual.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A stereotype is a fact of nature that biology fixes for every society.
**Answer: False** — the manual examines stereotypes as expectations societies pin on a gender, and the matrilineal Khasi society is its counter-example.

**3.** In a matrilineal society, the family line passes through the mother.
**Answer: True**

**4.** The manual presents LGBT rights in France as never having changed.
**Answer: False** — it traces their evolution over time.
