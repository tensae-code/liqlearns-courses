# Video script — Gender identity and sexual orientation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: gender identity and sexual orientation.

Remember this one thing: France's biology manual teaches this inside the chapter on the brain, pleasure and sexuality — as biology lessons, in a part called "Identités et orientations".

First, it asks: what is gender? Then it looks at stereotypes — the expectations a society pins on a gender. And it shows they are not the same everywhere: the Khasi people are a matrilineal society, where the family line passes through the mother.

Then it teaches attraction — sexual orientation, who a person is drawn to. And it closes by tracing how LGBT rights in France have evolved over time.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
