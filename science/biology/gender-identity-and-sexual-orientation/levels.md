# Gender identity and sexual orientation — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (France's manual teaches gender, stereotypes, attraction and the evolution of LGBT rights in France in its "Identités et orientations" part) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: explaining why one counter-example — a whole society organised differently — is enough to show a stereotype is not a law of nature, and separating the lesson's three ideas (gender, stereotype, orientation) without mixing them.
