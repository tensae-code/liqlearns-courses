# Radiometric dating — a geological clock — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (radiometric dating reads a rock's age from its radioactive clocks: uranium-238 halves in 4.5 billion years and carbon-14 in 5,730 years — which is why carbon-14 fails beyond about 70,000 years — and the lead–lead ratio dates a rock's formation) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
