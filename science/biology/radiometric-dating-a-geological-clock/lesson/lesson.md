# Radiometric dating — a geological clock

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Radiometric dating — a geological clock. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Radiometric dating reads a rock's age from its radioactive clocks: uranium-238 halves in 4.5 billion years and carbon-14 in 5,730 years — which is why carbon-14 fails beyond about 70,000 years — and the lead–lead ratio dates a rock's formation. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from China's People's Education Press biology textbooks, which teach it as a named topic.
