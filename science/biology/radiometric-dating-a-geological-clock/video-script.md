Today: Radiometric dating — a geological clock.

Remember this one thing: radiometric dating reads a rock's age from its radioactive clocks: uranium-238 halves in 4.5 billion years and carbon-14 in 5,730 years — which is why carbon-14 fails beyond about 70,000 years — and the lead–lead ratio dates a rock's formation.
