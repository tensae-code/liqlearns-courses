# Homeostasis — receptor, control centre, effector — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Animals hold their internal conditions near a set point despite outside change; every control loop has a receptor that detects the change, a control centre (often the hypothalamus) that decides the response, and an effector — kidney, liver, heart, muscle or gland — that carries it out and restores the normal condition.**
