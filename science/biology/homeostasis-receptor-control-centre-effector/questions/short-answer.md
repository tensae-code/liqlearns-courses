# Homeostasis — receptor, control centre, effector — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about homeostasis?**
**Answer: Animals hold their internal conditions near a set point despite outside change; every control loop has a receptor that detects the change, a control centre (often the hypothalamus) that decides the response, and an effector — kidney, liver, heart, muscle or gland — that carries it out and restores the normal condition.**
