(6) Ecology (pp. 266–295): levels of ecological integration, abiotic/biotic factors, ecosystem characteristics (succession, biorhythm), ecosystem metabolism — matter circulation and energy flow, autotroph and heterotroph roles, productivity and its analysis by trophic level, biogeochemical cycles, the biosphere's dependence on natural resources. Ch. 5 turns to plants: the auxin discovery chain from the Darwins to Went, the other hormones opened by a Song–Yuan dynasty text on ripening persimmons, growth regulators regulated as pesticides, and environmental control ending with flower-forcing for festivals and the 2008 Olympics.
