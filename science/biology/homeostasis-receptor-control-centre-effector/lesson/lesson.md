# Homeostasis — receptor, control centre, effector

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — homeostasis. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Animals hold their internal conditions near a set point despite outside change; every control loop has a receptor that detects the change, a control centre (often the hypothalamus) that decides the response, and an effector — kidney, liver, heart, muscle or gland — that carries it out and restores the normal condition.
