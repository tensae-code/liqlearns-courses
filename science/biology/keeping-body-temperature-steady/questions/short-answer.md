# Keeping body temperature steady — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about thermoregulation?**
**Answer: Cold-blooded (poikilothermic) animals behaviourally seek sun, shade, water and burrows; warm-blooded (homeothermic) animals generate their own heat and hold temperature by insulation (feathers puffed, hair raised), vasodilation and sweating to lose heat, vasoconstriction and shivering to keep it, and hibernation/estivation; heat moves by radiation, evaporation, convection and conduction, and small animals lose heat faster because their surface-area-to-volume ratio is larger (rabbit vs elephant).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added thermoregulation detail?**
**Answer: Hibernation in warm- and cold-blooded animals (bats, mammals, birds) against aestivation in cold-blooded animals (snails, earthworms, frogs); morphological regulation contrasted by habitat — desert animals with large heat-shedding features against the Arctic fox's small ears and thick fur; and a two-cube demonstration that surface-area-to-volume ratio falls as size grows, worked through the shrew as the small, fast heat-loser.**
