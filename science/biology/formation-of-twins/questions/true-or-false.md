# Formation of twins — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. One egg is fertilised by one sperm, and the early embryo then splits into two. Both twins grow from the same fertilised egg, so they carry the same genes and are the same sex.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Malaysia's KSSM biology books as named content.**

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with formation of twins.**
