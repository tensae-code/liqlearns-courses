# Formation of twins — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: twins form in two different ways: identical twins from one fertilised egg that splits, fraternal twins from two eggs fertilised separately — so identical twins share all their genes and fraternal twins share only what ordinary siblings share. Two eggs are released and each is fertilised by its own sperm. The two embryos are no more alike than ordinary brothers and sisters, and may be of different sexes.**
