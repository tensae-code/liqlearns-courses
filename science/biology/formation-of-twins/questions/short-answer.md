# Formation of twins — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: twins form in two different ways: identical twins from one fertilised egg that splits, fraternal twins from two eggs fertilised separately — so identical twins share all their genes and fraternal twins share only what ordinary siblings share. One egg is fertilised by one sperm, and the early embryo then splits into two. Both twins grow from the same fertilised egg, so they carry the same genes and are the same sex.**

**2.** Explain Malaysia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Malaysia's KSSM Form 4 book places the formation of twins inside Chapter 15 (printed p. 300), after the development of the foetus and before growth — twinning taught where fertilisation has just been learned, so the one-egg/two-egg split can be followed step by step.**
