Today: Formation of twins.

Remember this one thing: twins form in two different ways: identical twins from one fertilised egg that splits, fraternal twins from two eggs fertilised separately — so identical twins share all their genes and fraternal twins share only what ordinary siblings share.

At the end of its reproduction chapter, Malaysia's Form 4 book teaches the formation of twins (printed p. 300) — and examines it by comparison: identical against fraternal, one egg against two.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
