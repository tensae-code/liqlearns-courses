## Sub lesson 1 — Reproduction in frogs

Frogs — including Ethiopian species from the Bale Mountains and Shoa forests — reproduce sexually with figured male and female structures; they lay jelly-coated eggs in water (the jelly protects them), fertilisation is usually external, and the fertilised egg becomes a tadpole, a larval stage very different from the adult, before metamorphosis.
