# Video script — Frog reproduction — eggs, tadpoles, metamorphosis

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Frogs — including Ethiopian species from the Bale Mountains and Shoa forests — reproduce sexually with figured male and female structures; they lay jelly-coated eggs in water (the jelly protects them), fertilisation is usually external, and the fertilised egg becomes a tadpole, a larval stage very different from the adult, before metamorphosis.

Function lists for the frog's male and female reproductive organs (testes, sperm, seminal vesicles, oviducts, ova, and the cloaca as the shared exit for excretory, urinary and reproductive systems), and a seven-row tadpole-vs-frog table: gills vs lungs, tail and fins vs limbs, denticle teeth vs jaw teeth, water-only vs land-and-water, two-chambered vs three-chambered heart, herbivore vs carnivore, soft cartilage skull vs hardened skull.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
