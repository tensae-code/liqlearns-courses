# Hormone feedback in the menstrual cycle

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — hormone feedback in the menstrual cycle. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

The cycle is steered by hormone feedback: around days 12–14 oestrogen switches to positive feedback, driving the hypothalamus (GnRH) and pituitary (LH and FSH surge) to trigger ovulation; FSH grows the follicle and its oestradiol; progesterone then maintains the uterine lining for a possible pregnancy — negative feedback holding the rest of the system in check.
