# Hormone feedback in the menstrual cycle — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about feedback control of the menstrual cycle?**
**Answer: The cycle is steered by hormone feedback: around days 12–14 oestrogen switches to positive feedback, driving the hypothalamus (GnRH) and pituitary (LH and FSH surge) to trigger ovulation; FSH grows the follicle and its oestradiol; progesterone then maintains the uterine lining for a possible pregnancy — negative feedback holding the rest of the system in check.**
