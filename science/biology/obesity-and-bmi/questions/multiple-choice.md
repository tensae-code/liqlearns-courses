# Obesity and BMI — the other side of malnutrition — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about cause?
a) Obesity comes from taking in more energy than is used
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Obesity comes from taking in more energy than is used**

**2.** What does this lesson say about BMI?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Body mass index (BMI) measures mass against height and classifies obesity
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Body mass index (BMI) measures mass against height and classifies obesity**

**3.** What does this lesson say about control?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Obesity is controlled by eating less high-energy food and taking more exercise
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Obesity is controlled by eating less high-energy food and taking more exercise**

**4.** What does this lesson say about balanced lower energy?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) A balanced lower-energy diet helps control obesity
**Answer: d) A balanced lower-energy diet helps control obesity**
