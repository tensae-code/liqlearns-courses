# Obesity and BMI — the other side of malnutrition

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Obesity and BMI — the other side of malnutrition. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Control means eating less high-energy food, taking more exercise, and eating a balanced lower-energy diet.

[Picture 21060 — a BMI scale with the healthy band highlighted]

## The key facts
**Cause.** Obesity comes from taking in more energy than is used.

**BMI.** Body mass index (BMI) measures mass against height and classifies obesity.

**Control.** Obesity is controlled by eating less high-energy food and taking more exercise.

**Balanced lower energy.** A balanced lower-energy diet helps control obesity.
