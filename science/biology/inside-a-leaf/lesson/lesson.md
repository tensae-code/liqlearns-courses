# Inside a leaf

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Inside a leaf. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Cut a leaf open in your mind. Its tissues are arranged so that photosynthesis and gas exchange can happen — each layer matched to its function.

[Picture 22207 — a leaf cross-section with layers labelled]

## The key facts
**Inside layers.** A leaf cross-section shows tissues arranged in layers.

**Photosynthesis tissue.** Some leaf tissues carry out photosynthesis.

**Gas exchange.** Leaf tissues make gas exchange possible, letting gases in and out.

**Structure fits function.** Each inside layer is matched to the job it does.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words: a leaf's inside layers are matched layer by layer to photosynthesis and gas exchange Use each key fact once. Then let your companion explain it back to you.
