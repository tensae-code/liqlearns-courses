# Hands-on — Designing shade to reduce warming

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shade test
On a sunny day, put two matching objects — one in full sun, one under a shade you make from card or cloth. After twenty minutes, feel both and record which warmed more.

## Sketch a shade
Sketch a shade for a place that gets too hot — a seat, a plant, a pet's corner — and label what shades what, and when the shadow falls there.
