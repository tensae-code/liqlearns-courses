# Designing shade to reduce warming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Sunlight warms the area.
**Answer:** The problem is named first: sunlight warms the surfaces it falls on — the eating tables, the ground, a dog, a toy left in the sun (Kindergarten, K.1.4, printed p. 39).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Design and sketch or model a shade solution.
**Answer:** The solution is shade: something placed so its shadow falls on the area to be kept cool.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Test the shade.
**Answer:** A shade design is tested: put it in place and compare — the shaded area against the same area in full sun.
