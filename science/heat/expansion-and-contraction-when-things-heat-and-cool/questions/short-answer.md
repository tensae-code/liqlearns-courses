# Expansion and contraction — when things heat and cool — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: heated things expand.
**Answer:** Seychelles' Unit 10 Topic 2 states the rule plainly: metals and liquids expand when heated and contract when cooled.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What do metals and liquids do when heated, and when cooled?
**Answer:** They expand when heated and contract when cooled — the rule the bar-breaking apparatus proves and the thermometer uses.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Heated things expand → Cooled things contract — and the thermometer uses both — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
