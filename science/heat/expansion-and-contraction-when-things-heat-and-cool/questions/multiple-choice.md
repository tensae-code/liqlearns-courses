# Expansion and contraction — when things heat and cool — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) When a metal is heated, it…
a) expands  b) contracts  c) melts at once  d) disappears
**Answer: a) expands**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) When a liquid cools, it…
a) contracts  b) expands  c) boils  d) turns to metal
**Answer: a) contracts**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The bar-breaking apparatus shows that expansion…
a) has great force  b) is too weak to notice  c) only happens in water  d) stops a thermometer working
**Answer: a) has great force**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A thermometer works because its liquid…
a) expands as it warms and contracts as it cools  b) changes colour  c) freezes at room temperature  d) evaporates
**Answer: a) expands as it warms and contracts as it cools**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A heated ball that no longer fits its ring has…
a) expanded  b) contracted  c) rusted  d) melted
**Answer: a) expanded**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Gaps are left in bridges and rails because the material will…
a) expand in heat  b) shrink to nothing in summer  c) turn liquid  d) stop moving forever
**Answer: a) expand in heat**
