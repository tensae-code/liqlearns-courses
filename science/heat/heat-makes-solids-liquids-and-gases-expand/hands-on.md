# Hands-on — Heat makes solids, liquids and gases expand

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Fit a balloon over the neck of an empty bottle. Stand the bottle in warm water and watch the balloon rise as the air inside expands; move it to cold water and watch it fall. Write down what the balloon showed about heated air.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of heat makes solids, liquids and gases expand — when a solid, a liquid or a gas is heated, it expands — it takes up more space — and when it cools, it contracts; heat also drives the changes of state that turn ice to water and water to steam. — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
