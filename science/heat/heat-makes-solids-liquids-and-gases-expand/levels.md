# Heat makes solids, liquids and gases expand — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (when a solid, a liquid or a gas is heated, it expands — it takes up more space — and when it cools, it contracts; heat also drives the changes of state that turn ice to water and water to steam.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
