# Heat makes solids, liquids and gases expand — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A heated metal bar grows longer; a tight metal lid loosens in hot water because the metal has expanded more than the glass.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Liquids expand more is the opposite of what this lesson teaches.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches this as stated.**

**3.** Air in a bottle with a balloon over its neck inflates the balloon when warmed: gases expand most of all.
**Answer: True**

**4.** This lesson's topic plays no part in science at all.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
