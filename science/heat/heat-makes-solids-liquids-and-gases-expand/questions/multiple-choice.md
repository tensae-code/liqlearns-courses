# Heat makes solids, liquids and gases expand — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about heat makes solids, liquids and gases expand?
a) When a solid, a liquid or a gas is heated, it expands — it takes up more space — and when it cools, it contracts; heat also drives the changes of state that turn ice to water and water to steam.
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) When a solid, a liquid or a gas is heated, it expands — it takes up more space — and when it cools, it contracts; heat also drives the changes of state that turn ice to water and water to steam.**

**2.** What does this lesson say about solids expand?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) A heated metal bar grows longer; a tight metal lid loosens in hot water because the metal has expanded more than the glass.
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) A heated metal bar grows longer; a tight metal lid loosens in hot water because the metal has expanded more than the glass.**

**3.** What does this lesson say about liquids expand more?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Heated water rises in a narrow tube — the working of every thermometer: the liquid expands with heat and climbs, and its height reads the temperature.
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Heated water rises in a narrow tube — the working of every thermometer: the liquid expands with heat and climbs, and its height reads the temperature.**

**4.** What does this lesson say about gases expand most — and states change?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Air in a bottle with a balloon over its neck inflates the balloon when warmed: gases expand most of all.
**Answer: d) Air in a bottle with a balloon over its neck inflates the balloon when warmed: gases expand most of all.**
