# Hot and cold — temperature — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Why does Iran's Grade 5 book freeze a bottle of water?
**Answer: To show that water expands as it freezes with enough force to strain its container — the same force that, repeated in the cracks of rocks, splits them over long years.**

**2.** Sara's mother puts a hand on her forehead. What can that tell her, and what can it not?
**Answer: It can tell her roughly that Sara feels hotter than usual; it cannot say precisely how hot, or exactly which body is hotter — that needs a thermometer.**
