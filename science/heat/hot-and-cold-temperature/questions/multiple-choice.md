# Hot and cold — temperature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Hot and cold are…
a) Two boxes with nothing between  b) Degrees on one scale — a thing can be warmer than one thing and colder than another  c) The same thing  d) Only about weather
**Answer: b) Degrees on one scale — a thing can be warmer than one thing and colder than another**

**2.** A hand on a forehead…
a) Measures temperature exactly  b) Cannot say precisely which body is hotter  c) Is a thermometer  d) Always gives a number
**Answer: b) Cannot say precisely which body is hotter**

**3.** Water that freezes…
a) Takes less room  b) Takes more room and can split its container  c) Disappears  d) Turns to steam
**Answer: b) Takes more room and can split its container**

**4.** An ice figure left in a warm room…
a) Gets colder  b) Melts as it warms  c) Turns to stone  d) Stays the same forever
**Answer: b) Melts as it warms**
