# Hands-on — Hot and cold — temperature

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Warmer or colder?
Set out three bowls — cold water, room water, warm (not hot) water. Put a finger in each and order them. Then move a finger from the cold bowl into the room bowl: does the room bowl now feel warm? Say what that shows about judging by feeling.

## The freezing bottle
Fill a plastic bottle right up with water, close it, and freeze it overnight. Look at the bottle in the morning: the ice needed more room than the water did. Say where that same push acts in nature.
