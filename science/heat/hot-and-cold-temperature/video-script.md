Today: **Hot and cold — temperature** — Iran's way.

Iran's Grade 1 book opens the cold-and-hot world by asking where it is warmer, and sets a water-bottle figure in a freezer beside an ice figure and a snowman: cold and hot are not two boxes but a scale — a thing can be warmer than one thing and colder than another. When Sara tells her mother "I am very hot", her mother puts a hand on her forehead — Iran's Grade 4 book uses that moment to show the limit of touch: the sense of touch cannot say precisely which body is hotter than another. Change the temperature and the thing itself changes: ice figures melt as they warm, and water that freezes takes more room — Iran's Grade 5 book fills a plastic bottle with water and freezes it to show the force that, repeated in cracks, splits rocks apart over long years..

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
