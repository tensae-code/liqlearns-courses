# Lesson plan — Heating our homes — stoves, radiators and heaters

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Heat · **Lesson:** Heating our homes — stoves, radiators and heaters
**Place in the curriculum:** Heat — appended to Unit 22 (Heat) (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** stoves, radiators and batteries give the house heat when days turn cold; comparing heating in apartment blocks and village houses; why radiators sit under windows; water's high heat capacity in radiators
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Heating devices** — stoves, radiators and batteries give the house heat when days turn cold; comparing heating in apartment blocks and village houses; why radiators sit under windows; water's high heat capacity in radiators
2. **Heating appliances** — The books teach this part as radiators and other heaters as what we use to get heat; sorting pictured appliances into two groups — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.
3. **Heating our homes — stoves, radiators and heaters** — The books teach this part as heaters part: heating appliances are artificial heat sources that give out heat; heat from burning wood, oil or gas warms us and cooks food — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.
