# Hands-on — Heat — conduction, conductors and insulators

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Wax-spot rod race (Activity 7.18)
Wax spots at equal distances along aluminium, steel, copper and iron rods on one hot plate (adult runs the plate). Time each spot's melt, graph the times as bars, and write the full fair-test record: question, variables, aim, prediction, conclusion.

## Five-cup ranking (Activity 7.17)
Boiled water (adult pours) into aluminium, steel, plastic, glass and wood/wancha cups. Rank by touch at set times, then sort conductor/insulator from the ranking alone before reading the verdicts.
