# Heat — conduction, conductors and insulators — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Heat can flow from a cooler body to a hotter one on its own.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** All metals conduct heat at exactly the same rate.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A cloth used to lift a hot pan works as an insulator.
**Answer: True**
