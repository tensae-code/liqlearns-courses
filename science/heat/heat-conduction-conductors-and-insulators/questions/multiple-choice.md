# Heat — conduction, conductors and insulators — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Heat moves —
a) from cooler to hotter  b) from hotter to cooler, until temperatures equalise  c) only upwards
**Answer: b) from hotter to cooler, until temperatures equalise**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Conduction is heat transfer by —
a) direct contact  b) moving fluid  c) waves through empty space
**Answer: a) direct contact** — stove to pot; tea to cup and spoon.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Cold water on warm hands feels cold because —
a) cold flows in  b) heat flows out of the hands into the cooler water  c) the hands stop working
**Answer: b) heat flows out of the hands into the cooler water** — the flow is still hot to cold.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The best conductors in the rod test are —
a) wood and plastic  b) copper and aluminium  c) glass and wancha
**Answer: b) copper and aluminium** — hence cooking pots of metal.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Pot handles are made of wood or plastic because they —
a) conduct well  b) insulate — they slow heat reaching the hand  c) look better
**Answer: b) insulate — they slow heat reaching the hand**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Activity 7.18's write-up is a fair test, so it records —
a) only the result  b) question, variables, aim, prediction and conclusion  c) only a drawing
**Answer: b) question, variables, aim, prediction and conclusion**
