# Heat — conduction, conductors and insulators — fill in the blank

Fill the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Heat transfer through direct contact is called ______.
**Answer: conduction**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Materials that slow heat down are called ______.
**Answer: insulators**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The two best conductors in the rod test are copper and ______.
**Answer: aluminium**
