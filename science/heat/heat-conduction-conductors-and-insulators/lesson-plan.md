# Lesson plan — Heat — conduction, conductors and insulators

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Heat · **Lesson:** Heat — conduction, conductors and insulators
**Place in the curriculum:** Heat — a NEW title on the Science ladder (CURRICULUM.md). Unit 7, Physical Phenomena, at General Science Grade 8 depth.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** heat is transfer driven by temperature difference — from the hotter to the cooler until temperatures equalise — and conduction is its through-contact method: five cups ranked after holding boiled water and wax spots timed melting along four metal rods sort materials into conductors (metals, copper and aluminium best) and insulators (wood, plastic, cloth)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the primary Materials lesson Heat and how it travels: the three ways named there in one pass; now each way gets its own Grade 8 lesson, starting with conduction. It opens the Heat title.

## If the learner is stuck
- Draws an arrow cold to hot (‘the cold gets in’) → heat itself always moves hot to cold; cold water warms your hands' heat OUT — same rule, same direction.
- Calls glass a metal-class conductor from the cup test → rank from results: the metals warm fastest by far; glass, plastic and wood lag — that lag is insulating.
