# Hands-on — Heat — radiation and the black/white test

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Black/white bottle test (Activity 7.22)
Identical bottles, black and white cloth wraps, direct sun, readings every 30 minutes for two hours, both lines on one graph. The dress and car advice is written from the graph before the lesson's verdict is read.

## Radiation at the fire (Activity 7.21)
Near a home fire or a red-hot heater (adult present, hands NEAR, never touching): feel warmth arriving with no contact and still air. Name the method and rule out the other two, aloud, with reasons.
