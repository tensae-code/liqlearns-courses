# Heat — radiation and the black/white test — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Radiation transfers heat by —
a) direct contact  b) electromagnetic waves, needing no medium  c) circulating fluid
**Answer: b) electromagnetic waves, needing no medium** — it works even through a vacuum.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Sun's heat reaches Earth by radiation because the space between is —
a) full of water  b) nearly empty — a vacuum conduction and convection cannot cross  c) full of air
**Answer: b) nearly empty — a vacuum conduction and convection cannot cross**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Hands held NEAR a red-hot heater warm by —
a) conduction  b) convection  c) radiation
**Answer: c) radiation** — no contact, no fluid stream needed.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A black surface in the sun —
a) reflects most radiation and stays cool  b) absorbs well and heats fast  c) blocks radiation completely
**Answer: b) absorbs well and heats fast** — white and silver reflect and heat slowly.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the bottle test, readings are taken —
a) once, at the end  b) every 30 minutes for two hours, and line-graphed  c) only when the water boils
**Answer: b) every 30 minutes for two hours, and line-graphed** — identical bottles, black vs white cloth.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A white car's interior stays cooler than a black car's in the same sun because white —
a) makes cold  b) reflects radiation instead of absorbing it  c) is a better conductor
**Answer: b) reflects radiation instead of absorbing it**
