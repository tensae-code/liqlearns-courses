# Heat — radiation and the black/white test — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Radiation needs particles of matter to travel through.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Every hot object radiates heat.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Silver and white surfaces heat faster than black in the sun.
**Answer: False**
