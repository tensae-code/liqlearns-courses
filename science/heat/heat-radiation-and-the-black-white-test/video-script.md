The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The Sun warms you across empty space. No touch. No air out there.
That heat travels as waves. That is radiation.
Every hot thing radiates heat.
Black drinks the heat in — fast. White and silver throw it back — slow.
Two bottles, black cloth and white. The graph will prove it.
