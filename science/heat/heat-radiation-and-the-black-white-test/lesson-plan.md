# Lesson plan — Heat — radiation and the black/white test

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Heat · **Lesson:** Heat — radiation and the black/white test
**Place in the curriculum:** Heat — Unit 7, Physical Phenomena, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** radiation transfers heat by electromagnetic waves with no contact and no particles, working even through a vacuum — the Sun's heat crossing empty space — and surfaces differ: black absorbs well and heats fast while white and silver reflect and heat slowly, proved fairly by identical water bottles in black and white cloth in the sun, graphed over two hours
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Heat — convection currents: heat carried by moving fluid; now heat crossing where no fluid — and nothing — exists.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls the Sun's warming convection ‘through the air’ → between Sun and Earth lies vacuum; radiation is the only method that needs no medium.
- Predicts both bottles heat equally ‘under the same sun’ → the test exists precisely because surfaces differ: black absorbs, white/silver reflect.
