# Hands-on — Specific heat capacity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two spoons in one hot water
An adult's job, watched by the learner: stand a metal spoon and a wooden (or plastic) spoon in the same cup of hot water, handles up. After a minute, note which handle feels warmer and which material took the heat faster. Say, in the lesson's words, what the difference shows about the two materials' specific heat capacities. The learner does not carry or pour the hot water.
