# Hands-on — Insulation — slowing heat down

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## The two-ice-cubes test
Equal ice, same sun, one bare and one under a wool blanket; melt times recorded. Claims are re-read after the result and each pupil writes which claim their first reasoning matched — and the principle that settles it.

## Insulation survey
Homes and clothing surveyed for insulators: blankets, gabi, coats, cool boxes, pan handles, roof materials. Each is filed under ‘keeps heat in’, ‘keeps heat out’, or ‘both’ — the ‘both’ column is the lesson.
