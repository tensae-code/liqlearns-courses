# Lesson plan — Insulation — slowing heat down

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Heat · **Lesson:** Insulation — slowing heat down
**Place in the curriculum:** Heat — Unit 7, Physical Phenomena, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md). It closes the Heat title.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an insulator slows heat transfer in BOTH directions — the defended claim of the two ice cubes, one under a wool blanket — so feathers, fur, wool, clothing and blankets hold body heat in, houses leak heat through walls, ceiling, windows, doors and roof, and insulating materials (non-metals and trapped gases such as air) slow conduction, convection and radiation alike
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the three Heat lessons before it — conduction, convection, radiation: all three transfers named; now slowing all three down.

## If the learner is stuck
- Sides with Andualem (‘the blanket traps heat ON the ice’) → a blanket makes no heat; it only slows transfer. Around ice, what it slows is heat arriving.
- Thinks insulators only ‘keep warm things warm’ → the principle is direction-blind: slowing works both ways, which is why cool boxes keep cold things cold.
