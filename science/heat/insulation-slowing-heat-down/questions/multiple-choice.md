# Insulation — slowing heat down — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Of the two ice cubes in the sun, the one that melts first is —
a) the one under the wool blanket  b) the bare one  c) they melt together
**Answer: b) the bare one** — the blanket keeps the sun's heat OUT.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An insulator slows heat transfer —
a) only outwards  b) only inwards  c) in both directions
**Answer: c) in both directions** — that is the principle that settles the claim.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Andualem's error was to think the blanket —
a) is cold  b) traps heat around the ice — but a blanket makes no heat; it only slows transfer  c) reflects sunlight away completely
**Answer: b) traps heat around the ice — but a blanket makes no heat; it only slows transfer**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Warm-blooded animals hold body temperature with —
a) scales only  b) feathers, fur and wool  c) sweating alone
**Answer: b) feathers, fur and wool** — humans use clothing and blankets.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A house leaks heat through —
a) walls, ceiling, windows, doors and roof  b) only the floor  c) only the chimney
**Answer: a) walls, ceiling, windows, doors and roof** — five leak points.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Good insulators are —
a) metals  b) non-metals and trapped gases such as air  c) only liquids
**Answer: b) non-metals and trapped gases such as air** — metals conduct.
