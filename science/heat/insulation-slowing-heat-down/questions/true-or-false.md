# Insulation — slowing heat down — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A wool blanket makes its own heat to warm what it covers.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A cool box uses insulation to keep cold things cold.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Insulation slows conduction, convection and radiation alike.
**Answer: True**
