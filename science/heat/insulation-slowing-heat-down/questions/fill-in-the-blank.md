# Insulation — slowing heat down — fill in the blank

Fill the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An ______ slows heat transfer in both directions.
**Answer: insulator**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Houses leak heat through walls, ceiling, windows, doors and ______.
**Answer: roof**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Trapped ______ is a good insulator in blankets and cool-box walls.
**Answer: air (gas)**
