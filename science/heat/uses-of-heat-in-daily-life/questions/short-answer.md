# Uses of heat in daily life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Heat for drying and warming.
**Answer:** South Sudan's Primary 4 book teaches the **uses of heat** as its own topic: **drying, warming, cooking and ironing**. The guide's answers give drying its reason: grains are dried to remove moisture and stop them going bad — and name the sun as the main source behind the uses.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Heat at work — and a warning.
**Answer:** Cooking and ironing put heat to work on purpose, tool by tool. The guide adds the safety side of the same topic: **charcoal stoves in closed rooms give off harmful carbon monoxide** — the uses of heat are taught with the care heat demands.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Heat for drying and warming → Heat at work — and a warning — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
