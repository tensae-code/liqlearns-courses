The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Uses of heat in daily life** — South Sudan's way, from its Ministry of General Education and Instruction Primary Science books (see lesson/sources.md for the exact books and pages).

South Sudan's Primary 4 book teaches the **uses of heat** as its own topic: **drying, warming, cooking and ironing**. The guide's answers give drying its reason: grains are dried to remove moisture and stop them going bad — and name the sun as the main source behind the uses.

Cooking and ironing put heat to work on purpose, tool by tool. The guide adds the safety side of the same topic: **charcoal stoves in closed rooms give off harmful carbon monoxide** — the uses of heat are taught with the care heat demands.
