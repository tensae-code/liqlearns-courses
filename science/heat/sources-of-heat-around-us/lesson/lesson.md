# Sources of heat around us

## Do it first
Before you read: warm your hands in your mind — by the sun, by a fire, by rubbing them together. Where did each warmth come from? Say the sources. Then read on: this is Rwanda's way in — beside light, its Primary 2 book asks where the heat we feel comes from.

## Sub lesson 1 — Where the heat we feel comes from

The book asks the plain question: **where the heat we feel comes from** — the sources of heat around us, named from the learner's own world, so heat is never a mystery but a thing with sources that can be pointed to.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why heat matters

Then the **importance of heat**: heat is taught as something living things and daily work depend on — the sources are learned together with what the heat is for.

## The key ideas

- **Where the heat we feel comes from** — where heat comes from.
- **Why heat matters** — the importance of heat.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, in the lesson's own order — Where the heat we feel comes from first, then Why heat matters. Your companion may stop you once and ask: "What is this lesson's one thing?" Answer with the lesson's one thing, not by repeating the title.
