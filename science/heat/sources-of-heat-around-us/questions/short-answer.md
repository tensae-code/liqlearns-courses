# Sources of heat around us — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Where the heat we feel comes from" teach?
**Answer:** The book asks the plain question: **where the heat we feel comes from** — the sources of heat around us, named from the learner's own world, so heat is never a mystery but a thing with sources that can be pointed to.

**2.** What does the part "Why heat matters" teach?
**Answer:** Then the **importance of heat**: heat is taught as something living things and daily work depend on — the sources are learned together with what the heat is for.
