# Dangers of heat and staying safe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "How heat can harm" teach?
**Answer:** The book names the **harmful effects of heat** as their own topic: the same heat that serves can burn, scald, spoil and set alight — the harm is taught plainly, at the learner's own level, beside the heat they use every day.

**2.** What does the part "Staying safe around heat" teach?
**Answer:** A named danger carries its guard with it: knowing how heat harms is what **staying safe around heat** is built on — the learner who knows the harm handles fire, hot things and the sun with the care the book's order teaches: sources, importance, then dangers.
