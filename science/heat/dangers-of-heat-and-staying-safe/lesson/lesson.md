# Dangers of heat and staying safe

## Do it first
Before you read: heat cooks the food and warms the house — and heat can also hurt. Name every way heat can harm a person or a home that you can think of. Then read on: this is Rwanda's way in — its Primary 2 book does not leave heat at its uses; it teaches its harmful effects as their own topic.

## Sub lesson 1 — How heat can harm

The book names the **harmful effects of heat** as their own topic: the same heat that serves can burn, scald, spoil and set alight — the harm is taught plainly, at the learner's own level, beside the heat they use every day.

## Sub lesson 2 — Staying safe around heat

A named danger carries its guard with it: knowing how heat harms is what **staying safe around heat** is built on — the learner who knows the harm handles fire, hot things and the sun with the care the book's order teaches: sources, importance, then dangers.

## The key ideas

- **How heat can harm** — the harmful effects of heat.
- **Staying safe around heat** — staying safe around heat.
