# Heat — convection currents in liquids and gases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Convection happens in —
a) solids only  b) fluids — liquids and gases  c) metals only
**Answer: b) fluids — liquids and gases** — the particles must be free to move.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Warmed fluid rises because it —
a) expands and grows lighter  b) gets heavier  c) turns solid
**Answer: a) expands and grows lighter** — cooler, heavier fluid sinks in its place.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the beaker, sawdust sinks —
a) over the flame  b) at the cool side  c) nowhere
**Answer: b) at the cool side** — up the hot side, down the cool side.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the two-bottle test, the warm water —
a) stays at the bottom  b) rises into the upper bottle as the cold coloured water sinks  c) freezes
**Answer: b) rises into the upper bottle as the cold coloured water sinks**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The two-bottle test is done properly when the prediction is recorded —
a) after watching  b) before the sheet is drawn  c) never
**Answer: b) before the sheet is drawn** — predict, watch, conclude — in that order.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A convection current carries heat by —
a) particles handing it on without moving  b) the fluid itself circulating  c) waves crossing emptiness
**Answer: b) the fluid itself circulating**
