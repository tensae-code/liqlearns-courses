# Heat — convection currents in liquids and gases — fill in the blank

Fill the blank with the lesson's own word.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Warmed fluid expands, grows ______ and rises.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The moving loop that carries the heat is a convection ______.
