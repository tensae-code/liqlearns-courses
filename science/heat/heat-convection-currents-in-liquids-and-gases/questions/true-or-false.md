# Heat — convection currents in liquids and gases — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Convection can happen in a solid metal rod.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The sawdust loop rises over the flame and sinks at the cool side.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Warm fluid is lighter than the same fluid cold.
**Answer: True**
