# Lesson plan — Heat — convection currents in liquids and gases

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Heat · **Lesson:** Heat — convection currents in liquids and gases
**Place in the curriculum:** Heat — Unit 7, Physical Phenomena, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** in fluids the particles move freely, so warmed fluid expands, grows lighter and rises while cooler heavier fluid sinks — a convection current carries the heat — traced by sawdust circling a heated beaker and proved by the two-bottle coloured-water exchange
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Heat — conduction, conductors and insulators: heat handed particle to particle in solids; now heat carried by the fluid itself moving.

## If the learner is stuck
- Says ‘heat rises’ as if heat itself floats → it is the WARMED FLUID that rises, carrying its heat; the fluid is the carrier.
- Expects convection in a metal rod → convection needs particles free to move: fluids only. The rod's heat travels by conduction.
