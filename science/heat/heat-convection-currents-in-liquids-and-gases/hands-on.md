# Hands-on — Heat — convection currents in liquids and gases

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Sawdust loop (Activity 7.19)
Adult heats gently; pupils watch the sawdust, then draw the loop as a labelled diagram before any explanation is read. The diagram must show all four legs with the reason on the rising leg.

## Two-bottle exchange (Activity 7.20)
Cold coloured water inverted over hot through newspaper; prediction recorded first, sheet drawn, exchange observed, conclusion written from the observation alone.
