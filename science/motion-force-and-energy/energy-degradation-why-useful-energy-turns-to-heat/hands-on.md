# Hands-on — Energy — degradation: why useful energy is lost as heat

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Warmth hunt
Rub your palms together fast and feel the warmth rise — motion degraded to heat in your own skin. Then walk through your home and find three more warm machines: a charging phone, a working bulb, a running fridge or motor. For each one, say what job its energy was meant to do, and where the degraded part is going.

## The brake test
Spin a bicycle wheel (or watch one being ridden and braked). Touch the brake pad or rim just after a hard stop — carefully, it can be hot. The wheel's kinetic energy did not disappear: it degraded into the pad, the rim and the air. Trace the whole chain aloud, from the rider's muscles to the warm pad, naming the form at each step.
