# Energy — degradation: why useful energy is lost as heat — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** When a car finishes a trip, its fuel's energy —
a) has been destroyed  b) still exists, partly as motion done and partly as warmth spread around  c) turned back into fuel  d) became light
**Answer: b) still exists, partly as motion done and partly as warmth spread around** — energy is never destroyed; the useful part degraded

**2.** Degradation of energy means —
a) energy breaking the conservation law  b) energy vanishing  c) some energy spreading out as heat where it can no longer do the job  d) energy turning into matter
**Answer: c) some energy spreading out as heat where it can no longer do the job** — the booklet names this degradación de la energía

**3.** Rubbing your hands together makes them warm because —
a) heat is created from nothing  b) motion energy degrades into heat in your skin  c) your blood boils  d) friction destroys energy
**Answer: b) motion energy degrades into heat in your skin** — the form changed; the amount did not

**4.** Why can't the warmth from a car engine simply be collected and used to run the car again?
a) it is no longer energy  b) it has broken the law  c) it is spread too thin through the engine, road and air to gather back  d) heat can never move
**Answer: c) it is spread too thin through the engine, road and air to gather back** — degraded energy is scattered energy

**5.** A bulb takes in 100 units of electrical energy and gives 5 units of light. Its efficiency is —
a) 95%  b) 5%  c) 100%  d) 50%
**Answer: b) 5%** — useful out ÷ total in = 5 ÷ 100; the other 95 units degraded as heat

**6.** Which machine degrades the least energy for the same light?
a) an old glowing bulb that burns your fingers  b) a candle  c) a modern LED bulb that stays cool  d) they all degrade the same
**Answer: c) a modern LED bulb that stays cool** — better machines leak less, but none leak nothing
