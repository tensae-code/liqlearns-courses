# Energy — degradation: why useful energy is lost as heat — true or false

Say true or false.

**7.** Energy can be destroyed when a machine uses it up.
**Answer: False** — energy is never created or destroyed; it changes form and spreads

**8.** In every real conversion, some energy degrades as heat in the surroundings.
**Answer: True** — friction, resistance and sound always take a share

**9.** Degradation breaks the law of conservation of energy.
**Answer: False** — the law counts all the energy, useful and degraded together; degradation is about usefulness, not amount

**10.** A perfectly built machine could convert energy with no degradation at all.
**Answer: False** — real machines can leak less, never nothing
