# Energy — degradation: why useful energy is lost as heat — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**11.** The slipping of useful energy into spread-out heat is called ______.
**Answer: degradation** — degradación de la energía, as Argentina's booklet names it

**12.** A bulb takes in 100 units of energy and gives 5 units of light, so ______ units degraded as heat.
**Answer: 95** — 100 − 5; the count still balances

**13.** Degraded energy almost always ends as ______ spread through the surroundings.
**Answer: heat (warmth)** — warm pads, warm air, warm walls

**14.** The share of energy that reaches the job — useful out divided by total in — is called the machine's ______.
**Answer: efficiency** — better machines have a higher efficiency, never a perfect one
