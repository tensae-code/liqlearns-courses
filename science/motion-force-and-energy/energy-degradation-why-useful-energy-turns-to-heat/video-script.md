# Video script — Energy — degradation: why useful energy is lost as heat

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Rub your **hands** — faster! — they get **warm**.
The movement did not **vanish**. It turned into **warmth**.
Energy is **never destroyed**. But in every change, some of it **spreads out** as heat.
Spread-out warmth cannot be **gathered back**. That loss has a name: **degradation**.
Nothing is gone. It is just too **thin** to work again.
