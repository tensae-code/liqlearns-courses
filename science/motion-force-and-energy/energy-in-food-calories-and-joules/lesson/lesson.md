# Energy in food — calories and joules

## Do it first
A food label does not list weight or volume — it lists energy. Why does food carry an energy number at all, and in what units is that number given? Say your answer out loud. Then read on: this is Costa Rica's way in — its Grade 8 Ciencias teaches the unit of energy through the energy in food.

## Sub lesson 1 — The joule — the unit of energy

Energy is measured, and its scientific unit is the **joule**. Whenever this course speaks of an amount of energy — in motion, in heat, in a conversion — the joule is the unit the amount is counted in.

Meeting the unit inside the energy unit, as Costa Rica's book does, fixes it before the food measure arrives: first the unit, then its most familiar use.

## Sub lesson 2 — Calories — the energy in food

The energy stored in food is counted in **calories**: a food's calorie number says how much energy the food holds for the body that eats it.

So the two names belong to one lesson: the **joule** is the unit of energy, and the **calorie** is the measure on the food — both are counting the same thing, energy, the food's store of it set against the unit science counts it in.
