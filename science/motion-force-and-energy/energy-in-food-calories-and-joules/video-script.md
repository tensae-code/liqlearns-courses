Today: **Energy in food — calories and joules** — Costa Rica's way, from its Grade 8 Ciencias, Código 80008 (printed p. 54).

Energy is measured, and its scientific unit is the **joule**. Whenever this course speaks of an amount of energy — in motion, in heat, in a conversion — the joule is the unit the amount is counted in.

The energy stored in food is counted in **calories**: a food's calorie number says how much energy the food holds for the body that eats it.
