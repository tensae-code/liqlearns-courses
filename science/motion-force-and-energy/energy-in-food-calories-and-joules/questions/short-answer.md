# Energy in food — calories and joules — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The joule — the unit of energy.
**Answer:** Energy is measured, and its scientific unit is the **joule**. Whenever this course speaks of an amount of energy — in motion, in heat, in a conversion — the joule is the unit the amount is counted in.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Calories — the energy in food.
**Answer:** The energy stored in food is counted in **calories**: a food's calorie number says how much energy the food holds for the body that eats it.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The joule — the unit of energy → Calories — the energy in food — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
