# Video script — Force — gravity, weight and the newton meter

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Gravity** pulls at **every** point of the fall — low roof or high.
**Weight** is that pull on a mass: **W = m × g**, g is 9.8.
Hang the mass. The spring **stretches** — the stretch **is** the reading.
That instrument is the **newton meter**.
