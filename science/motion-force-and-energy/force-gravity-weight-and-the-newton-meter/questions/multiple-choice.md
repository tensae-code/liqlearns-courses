# Force — gravity, weight and the newton meter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Weight is —
a) the amount of matter in a body  b) gravity's force on a mass  c) the same as mass  d) measured in kilograms
**Answer: b) gravity's force on a mass** — W = mg, in newtons

**2.** With g = 9.8, a 2 kg mass weighs —
a) 2 N  b) 9.8 N  c) 19.6 N  d) 0.2 N
**Answer: c) 19.6 N** — 2 × 9.8 = 19.6

**3.** A newton meter measures force by —
a) its colour change  b) a spring stretching in proportion to the force  c) counting swings  d) weighing water
**Answer: b) a spring stretching in proportion to the force** — stretch read off the scale

**4.** Gravity acts on a falling ball —
a) only near the ground  b) only from high buildings  c) at every position of its path  d) only at the start
**Answer: c) at every position of its path** — that is why it speeds up all the way down

**5.** 100 g on the meter should read about —
a) 9.8 N  b) 0.98 N  c) 100 N  d) 0.1 N
**Answer: b) 0.98 N** — 0.1 kg × 9.8
