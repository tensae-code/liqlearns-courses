# Force — gravity, weight and the newton meter — true or false

Say true or false.

**6.** Mass changes when a body is carried where gravity is weaker.
**Answer: False** — mass stays; weight changes

**7.** The newton meter's parts include a spring, a hook, a support and a scale.
**Answer: True** — the four printed parts

**8.** A bouncing spring gives a trustworthy reading.
**Answer: False** — wait for the settle

**9.** Weight is measured in newtons.
**Answer: True** — it is a force
