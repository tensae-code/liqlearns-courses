# Force — gravity, weight and the newton meter — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**10.** W = m × ______, and its value is 9.8.
**Answer: g** — the gravity factor

**11.** 300 g weighs about ______ N.
**Answer: 2.94** — 0.3 × 9.8

**12.** The instrument that measures force is the ______ meter.
**Answer: newton** — the spring balance
