# Hands-on — Force — gravity, weight and the newton meter

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Measure and calculate
Hang 100 g, 200 g and 300 g masses on the newton meter in turn; record each reading. Then compute each weight from W = mg (0.1 kg × 9.8 ≈ 0.98 N; 0.2 kg ≈ 1.96 N; 0.3 kg ≈ 2.94 N). Lay reading beside calculation and reconcile any difference — meter zero, bouncing, reading angle.

## Gravity at every height
Drop a ball from knee height and from shoulder height over soft ground. At which point of either fall was gravity absent? (Nowhere — the pull acts at every position of the path, which is why the ball speeds up all the way down.)
