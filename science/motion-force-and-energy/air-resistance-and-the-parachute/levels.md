# Air resistance and the parachute — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner states what drag is and the two things it grows with (speed and area facing the air), builds the tissue-paper parachute and shows a bigger canopy descending more gently, and tells the falling story in order — weight wins, speed rises, drag rises to meet weight, steady gentle fall — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has three sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — feeling drag on a hand at different speeds and angles, timing drops with different canopy sizes, and reasoning about the balance point until the learner can predict a drop before making it.
