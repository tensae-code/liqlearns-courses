# Air resistance and the parachute — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The air's push-back on a body moving through it, always against the motion, is called air resistance or _____.
**Answer: drag**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A bigger canopy catches more air and descends more _____ .
**Answer: gently (slowly)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The steady fall begins at the moment drag _____ weight.
**Answer: balances (equals)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Once drag and weight balance, there is no _____ force, so the speed stops changing.
**Answer: net**
