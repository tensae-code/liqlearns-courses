# Air and water resistance — drag — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Air resistance is the air's push-back on anything moving through it — a force against the motion.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Water resistance is the water's push-back on anything moving through it — the same kind of force, felt more strongly.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A swimmer and a runner move at the same speed. Who meets the stronger push-back?
**Answer:** The swimmer — water resistance is the stronger push-back of the pair.
