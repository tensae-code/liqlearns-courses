# Air and water resistance — drag — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which sentence gives this lesson's first key idea?
a) Air resistance is the air's push-back on anything moving through it — a force against the motion.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Air resistance is the air's push-back on anything moving through it — a force against the motion.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson opens by asking the learner to…
a) Memorise the key ideas first  b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation  c) Skip to the questions  d) Copy the lesson out
**Answer: b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's second key idea?
a) Water resistance is the water's push-back on anything moving through it — the same kind of force, felt more strongly.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Water resistance is the water's push-back on anything moving through it — the same kind of force, felt more strongly.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's third key idea?
a) Something from a different lesson  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Iceland teaches the two together as one pair: the push-backs of air and of water.  d) None of these
**Answer: c) Iceland teaches the two together as one pair: the push-backs of air and of water.**
