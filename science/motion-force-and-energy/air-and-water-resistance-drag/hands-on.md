# Hands-on — Air and water resistance — drag

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel the pair
Wave a flat piece of card through the air, fast then slow. Then, with an adult, push the same card through a basin of water. Say which push-back was stronger and when it grew.

## Flat against edge
Push the card through the water flat-side first, then edge first. Which way met less resistance? Say why in your own words.
