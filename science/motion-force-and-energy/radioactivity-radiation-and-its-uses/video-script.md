Today: **Radioactivity — radiation and its uses** — Malaysia's way, from its book (Malaysia, Grade 10 (Form 4) — *Sains Tingkatan 4*, printed p. 256).

Malaysia's Form 4 book teaches that **more than 30 countries use nuclear energy, mainly to generate electricity**, its benefits weighed against other sources. The energy is produced two ways: **nuclear fission** — a heavy nucleus bombarded and splitting into two smaller, more stable nuclei — and **nuclear fusion** — two light nuclei (deuterium and tritium) combining into a heavier nucleus, releasing energy.

Malaysia's own reactor is the **1 MW TRIGA PUSPATI Reactor** of the Malaysian Nuclear Agency, operating since **28 June 1982, for research only**. The book also carries the impacts: the **Hiroshima and Nagasaki bombings (6 and 9 August 1945)**, nuclear tests and their effects. It closes with a **debate: justifications for and against a nuclear power station in Malaysia**.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
