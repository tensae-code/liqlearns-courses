# Radioactivity — radiation and its uses — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Nuclear energy — fission and fusion.
**Answer:** Malaysia's Form 4 book teaches that **more than 30 countries use nuclear energy, mainly to generate electricity**, its benefits weighed against other sources. The energy is produced two ways: **nuclear fission** — a heavy nucleus bombarded and splitting into two smaller, more stable nuclei — and **nuclear fusion** — two light nuclei (deuterium and tritium) combining into a heavier nucleus, releasing energy.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Malaysia's reactor, the impacts, and the debate.
**Answer:** Malaysia's own reactor is the **1 MW TRIGA PUSPATI Reactor** of the Malaysian Nuclear Agency, operating since **28 June 1982, for research only**. The book also carries the impacts: the **Hiroshima and Nagasaki bombings (6 and 9 August 1945)**, nuclear tests and their effects. It closes with a **debate: justifications for and against a nuclear power station in Malaysia**.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Nuclear energy — fission and fusion → Malaysia's reactor, the impacts, and the debate — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
