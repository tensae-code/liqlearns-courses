# The fastest animals and machines — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about the fastest animals and machines?
**Answer: How people chased speed: legs, then the caught horse, then cars that at first barely outran horses; the animal speed table (cheetah, lion, antelope) with Kazakhstan's own champion, the saiga, covering 80 km in an hour, and the baiga horse races Describing speed as fast and slow: the giraffe and hare run fast, tortoise and hedgehog move very slowly; the cheetah (qabylan) is the fastest of animals a**
