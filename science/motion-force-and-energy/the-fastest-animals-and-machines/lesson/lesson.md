## Sub lesson 1 — The fastest animals and machines

How people chased speed: legs, then the caught horse, then cars that at first barely outran horses; the animal speed table (cheetah, lion, antelope) with Kazakhstan's own champion, the saiga, covering 80 km in an hour, and the baiga horse races Describing speed as fast and slow: the giraffe and hare run fast, tortoise and hedgehog move very slowly; the cheetah (qabylan) is the fastest of animals and the snail among the slowest; a speedometer shows a vehicle's speed and an anemometer measures the wind's; an animal's speed hangs on its build — supple limbs and strong muscles move fast
