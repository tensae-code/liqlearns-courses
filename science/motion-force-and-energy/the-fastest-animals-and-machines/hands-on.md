# Hands-on — The fastest animals and machines

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — The fastest animals and machines. How people chased speed: legs, then the caught horse, then cars that at first barely outran horses; the animal speed table (cheetah, lion, antelope) with Kazakhstan's own champion, the saiga, covering 80 km in an hour, and the baiga horse races Describing speed as fast and slow: the giraffe and hare run fast, tortoise and hedgehog move very slowly; the cheetah (qabylan) is the fastest of animals a Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
