# Lesson plan — The fastest animals and machines

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Motion, Force and Energy · **Lesson:** The fastest animals and machines
**Place in the curriculum:** Motion, Force and Energy — appended to Unit 20 (Motion, Force and Energy) (CURRICULUM.md)

**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.
