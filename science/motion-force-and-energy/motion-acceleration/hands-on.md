# Hands-on — Motion — acceleration

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The three starts
Three runners start together: one sprints harder and harder, one starts fast and eases to a walk, one jogs one steady pace. The class calls each acceleration's sign as the difference shows.

## Table to graph
Take a runner's speed table (speed every second for 6 seconds). Plot it. Rising, falling or flat — the line's shape names the acceleration before any division is done; then compute a from the first and last speeds.
