# Motion — acceleration — true or false

Say true or false.

**6.** Steady velocity means zero acceleration.
**Answer: True** — nothing about the velocity is changing

**7.** A falling speed–time graph shows positive acceleration.
**Answer: False** — falling means slowing — negative

**8.** Acceleration's denominator is the time the change took.
**Answer: True** — a = Δv ÷ Δt

**9.** Only speeding up counts as acceleration.
**Answer: False** — braking and cornering count too
