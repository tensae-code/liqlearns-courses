# Motion — acceleration — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**10.** a = Δv ÷ ______.
**Answer: Δt (time)** — the time the change took

**11.** A body rising from 0 to 20 m/s in 8 s accelerates at ______ m/s².
**Answer: 2.5** — 20 ÷ 8

**12.** Steady velocity gives ______ acceleration.
**Answer: zero** — the flat graph
