# Motion — acceleration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Acceleration is —
a) velocity itself  b) the rate of change of velocity  c) distance ÷ time  d) the fastest speed reached
**Answer: b) the rate of change of velocity** — how velocity changes per second

**2.** From rest to 20 m/s in 8 s gives a = —
a) 20 m/s²  b) 2.5 m/s²  c) 0.4 m/s²  d) 160 m/s²
**Answer: b) 2.5 m/s²** — 20 ÷ 8 = 2.5

**3.** Braking to a stop is —
a) not acceleration  b) negative acceleration  c) zero acceleration  d) infinite acceleration
**Answer: b) negative acceleration** — velocity is shrinking

**4.** Cornering at a steady speed is acceleration because —
a) the engine works harder  b) velocity's direction changes  c) the road is curved  d) speed secretly grows
**Answer: b) velocity's direction changes** — velocity is size and direction together

**5.** The unit of acceleration is —
a) m/s  b) m  c) m/s²  d) s/m
**Answer: c) m/s²** — metres per second, per second
