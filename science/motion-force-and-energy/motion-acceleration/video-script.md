# Video script — Motion — acceleration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Velocity **changing** — that is **acceleration**.
Speed up: **positive**. Slow down: **negative**. Steady: **zero**.
Even turning the corner at steady speed — the **direction** changed.
**a = change of velocity ÷ time.** Unit: metre per second squared.
