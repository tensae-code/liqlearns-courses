# Lesson plan — Motion — four kinds of motion

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Motion, Force and Energy · **Lesson:** Motion — four kinds of motion
**Place in the curriculum:** Motion, Force and Energy — Unit 7, after the reference point (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** motions sort by their path into four kinds — linear, oscillatory, curvilinear and rotational — and the teacher guide adds two named sub-kinds, uniform circular and periodic motion (teacher evidence)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Motion — motion needs a reference point: motion is judged; now motions are sorted by the shape of their path.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sort a moving thing into its kind by its path: linear, oscillatory, curvilinear or rotational.
2. Give one home example of each kind.
3. Name the guide's two extra sub-kinds — uniform circular and periodic — as teacher evidence.

## What you need
A stone on a string (pendulum), a wheel or spinning top, a ball, and a curved track drawn on the ground.

## If the learner is stuck
- Sorts by the thing instead of the path → ask 'what shape does its path draw?' A car on a bend and a car on a straight are different kinds on the same trip.
- Calls every circle rotational → a point on the rim travels curvilinearly; the wheel as a whole rotates. Both namings appear in the sorting talk.
