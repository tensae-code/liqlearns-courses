# Motion — four kinds of motion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A pendulum swinging is —
a) linear motion  b) oscillatory motion  c) rotational motion  d) no motion
**Answer: b) oscillatory motion** — it travels to and fro along the same path

**2.** The Moon travelling around the Earth is —
a) curvilinear motion  b) linear motion  c) oscillatory motion  d) at rest
**Answer: a) curvilinear motion** — its path is a curve

**3.** Clock hands show —
a) linear motion  b) rotational motion  c) curvilinear fall  d) vibration only
**Answer: b) rotational motion** — they spin around an axis

**4.** Motions are sorted into kinds by —
a) their speed  b) the shape of their path  c) their size  d) their weight
**Answer: b) the shape of their path** — path is the sorting rule

**5.** Periodic motion (teacher-guide name) is motion that —
a) never repeats  b) repeats in equal times  c) only goes straight  d) only spins
**Answer: b) repeats in equal times** — a pendulum's equal swings are the example
