# Motion — four kinds of motion — true or false

Say true or false.

**6.** A falling object in still air shows linear motion.
**Answer: True** — it travels a straight path down

**7.** A car's wheels rotate while the car itself runs a curve.
**Answer: True** — one trip can hold several kinds

**8.** Uniform circular motion means the speed keeps changing.
**Answer: False** — uniform means steady speed around the circle

**9.** A vibrating spring is an oscillatory motion.
**Answer: True** — to-and-fro is the oscillatory path
