# Motion — four kinds of motion — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**10.** Straight-path motion is called ______ motion.
**Answer: linear (rectilinear)** — linear, also named rectilinear

**11.** Spinning around an axis is ______ motion.
**Answer: rotational** — the wheel and clock hands

**12.** A curved track gives ______ motion.
**Answer: curvilinear** — the path is a curve
