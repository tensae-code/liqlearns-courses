# Hands-on — Motion — four kinds of motion

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The yard sort
List ten moving things around home and school — a falling stone, a swing, a cart wheel, the Moon, clock hands, a vibrating rope. Sort each by its path and defend the sort aloud.

## Make all four
Roll a ball straight (linear), swing a stone on a string (oscillatory), roll the ball around a drawn curve (curvilinear), spin a top or wheel (rotational). A partner names each kind as it is made.
