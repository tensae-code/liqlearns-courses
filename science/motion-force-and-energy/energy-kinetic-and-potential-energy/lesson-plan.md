# Lesson plan — Energy — kinetic and potential energy

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Motion, Force and Energy · **Lesson:** Energy — kinetic and potential energy
**Place in the curriculum:** Motion, Force and Energy — Unit 7, the energy strand opens (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** energy is the ability to cause change — measured in joules — and it shows first in two forms: kinetic energy KE = ½mv², the energy of moving, and potential energy PE = mgh, the energy of position
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the laws of motion: bodies and forces are understood; now the change they can cause is measured as energy.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kinetic energy — the energy of moving** — KE = ½mv², worked at 32 J, and the squared speed's outsized effect
2. **Potential energy — the energy of position** — PE = mgh, worked forwards and backwards to the 2 m height

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Force and motion — Newton's third law (this title). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what energy is — the ability to cause change — and name its unit, the joule.
2. Work KE = ½mv²: 4 kg at 4 m/s = 32 J; and say which factor, mass or speed, counts double-squared.
3. Work PE = mgh both ways, including backwards: 40 J and 2 kg give a height of 2 m.

## What you need
A ball, a ramp or slope, a shelf at a measured height, and masses for the raise-and-compute set.

## If the learner is stuck
- Doubles the speed and doubles the KE → speed is squared in KE: double speed, four times the energy. Mass only doubles it.
- Confuses PE's height with distance travelled → h is height above the chosen ground, not the path taken to get there.
