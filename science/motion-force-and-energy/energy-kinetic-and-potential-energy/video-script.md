**Energy** — the ability to cause **change**.
Moving? **Kinetic**: KE = half, m, v-squared.
Raised? **Potential**: PE = m, g, h.
Four kilograms at four metres per second: **32 joules**.
Speed counts **squared** — double it, and the energy quadruples.
