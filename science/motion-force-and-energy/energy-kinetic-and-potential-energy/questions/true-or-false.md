# Energy — kinetic and potential energy — true or false

Say true or false.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** Doubling the mass doubles the kinetic energy.
**Answer: True** — mass enters plainly, not squared

**7. (Sub lesson 1)** Potential energy depends on the path taken up to the shelf.
**Answer: False** — it depends on the height, not the path

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** A body at rest on the floor holds kinetic energy.
**Answer: False** — no motion, no KE

**9. (Sub lesson 2)** PE can be computed backwards to find an unknown height.
**Answer: True** — h = PE ÷ (mg)
