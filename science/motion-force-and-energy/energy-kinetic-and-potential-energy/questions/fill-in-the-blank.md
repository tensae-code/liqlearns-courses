# Energy — kinetic and potential energy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**10. (Sub lesson 1)** KE = ½ m ______².
**Answer: v (speed)** — the squared factor

**11. (Sub lesson 2)** A 4 kg body at 4 m/s holds ______ J of kinetic energy.
**Answer: 32** — the worked example

**12. (Sub lesson 2)** Energy is measured in ______ (J).
**Answer: joules** — the unit for both forms
