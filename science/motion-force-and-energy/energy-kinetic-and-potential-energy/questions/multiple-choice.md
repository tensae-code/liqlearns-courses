# Energy — kinetic and potential energy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Energy is —
a) a kind of force  b) the ability to cause change  c) the same as speed  d) only heat
**Answer: b) the ability to cause change** — known by the changes it makes

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** KE = ½ × 4 kg × (4 m/s)² equals —
a) 16 J  b) 32 J  c) 64 J  d) 8 J
**Answer: b) 32 J** — ½ × 4 × 16

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Doubling a body's speed multiplies its KE by —
a) 2  b) 3  c) 4  d) 8
**Answer: c) 4** — speed enters squared

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** PE = mgh worked backwards: 40 J stored by 2 kg means a height of about —
a) 20 m  b) 2 m  c) 0.5 m  d) 8 m
**Answer: b) 2 m** — 40 ÷ (2 × 9.8) ≈ 2

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The unit of energy is the —
a) newton  b) joule  c) metre  d) watt-second only
**Answer: b) joule** — both KE and PE are counted in joules
