# Hands-on — Energy — kinetic and potential energy

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Slow ball, fast ball
Roll a ball gently into a stack of empty boxes; count the boxes it moves. Roll it fast from a ramp; count again. Same mass — the speed alone multiplied the doing. That doing, measured, is kinetic energy.

## Raise and compute
Raise a 2 kg mass onto shelves at 1 m and 2 m. Compute PE each time (19.6 J, 39.2 J ≈ 40 J). The higher shelf stores the more — position itself is the store.
