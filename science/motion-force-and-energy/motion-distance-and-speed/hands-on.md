# Hands-on — Motion — distance and speed

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The ball-run practical
Chalk a start line. Roll a ball, start the timer at the release and stop it where the ball stops. Measure the run. Compute distance ÷ time — that quotient is the ball's average speed. Repeat with a harder and a softer roll and compare.

## The three-part walk
Walk 40 m, then 20 m, then 40 m, timing the whole walk. Total distance 100 m. If the whole walk took 20 s, the average speed is 5 m/s — even though no single part need have been walked at exactly that speed.
