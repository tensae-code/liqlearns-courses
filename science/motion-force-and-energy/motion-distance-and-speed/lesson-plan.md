# Lesson plan — Motion — distance and speed

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Motion, Force and Energy · **Lesson:** Motion — distance and speed
**Place in the curriculum:** Motion, Force and Energy — Unit 7, the measuring lesson (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** distance is the total length of the path travelled, and average speed = total distance ÷ total time — measured for real in the ball-rolling practical before the formulas land
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Motion — four kinds of motion: paths are sorted; now a path is measured and timed.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Run the ball practical: roll, time and measure, then compute the ball's average speed.
2. Work the book's examples: 4,000 m in 50 s = 80 m/s; the 40+20+40 m walk = 100 m at 5 m/s.
3. Say why an average speed can hide fast and slow parts inside one journey.

## What you need
A ball, a tape or measured chalk line, and a watch or phone timer — the practical's own kit.
