# Motion — distance and speed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Average speed equals —
a) total time ÷ total distance  b) total distance ÷ total time  c) distance × time  d) time − distance
**Answer: b) total distance ÷ total time** — how much distance fits into each second

**2.** 4,000 m covered in 50 s gives an average speed of —
a) 8 m/s  b) 80 m/s  c) 800 m/s  d) 0.8 m/s
**Answer: b) 80 m/s** — 4,000 ÷ 50 = 80

**3.** The 40 m + 20 m + 40 m walk has a total distance of —
a) 60 m  b) 80 m  c) 100 m  d) 20 m
**Answer: c) 100 m** — distance adds every part of the path

**4.** The unit of average speed is —
a) m  b) s  c) m/s  d) m/s²
**Answer: c) m/s** — metres divided by seconds

**5.** An average speed of 5 m/s over 100 m means the journey took —
a) 5 s  b) 20 s  c) 100 s  d) 500 s
**Answer: b) 20 s** — time = distance ÷ speed = 100 ÷ 5
