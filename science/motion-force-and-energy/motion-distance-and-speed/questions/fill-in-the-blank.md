# Motion — distance and speed — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**10.** Average speed = total distance ÷ total ______.
**Answer: time** — the formula's second quantity

**11.** A body covering 900 m in 60 s averages ______ m/s.
**Answer: 15** — 900 ÷ 60 = 15

**12.** Distance is measured in ______ (m).
**Answer: metres** — the SI unit of length
