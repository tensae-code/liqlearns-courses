Today: **Centre of gravity and stability** — Fiji's way, from its book (Fiji, Year 4 — *Elementary Science Year 4 — Teacher Resource Material*, printed pp. 59-60).

Fiji's Year 4 Teacher Resource opens the idea with a balancing parrot: a figure that balances and stays upright instead of toppling. The parrot balances because its weight is arranged around one special point — the point this lesson names.
The book's second test is a ruler balanced on a point. A ruler balances at one place along its length: its centre of gravity — the point where the object's weight balances in every direction. Move the support away from that point, and the ruler tips; rest it there, and it stays.
The lesson's rule joins the two tests: lowering the centre of gravity makes an object stable. The parrot stays up because its centre of gravity sits low; a thing whose centre of gravity sits high tips easily. To make a thing stable, bring its balancing point down.
