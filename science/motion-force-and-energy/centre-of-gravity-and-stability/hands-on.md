# Hands-on — Centre of gravity and stability

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Balance a ruler or a straight stick on one finger and find the one place where it stays level — its centre of gravity. Mark the place. Then balance a bottle or a stool-shaped object two ways: with its heavy part low, and with its heavy part high. Which way is stable, and why?
