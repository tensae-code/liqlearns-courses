# Force and motion — Newton's first law — true or false

Say true or false.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** Inertia is a body's keeping its state — rest or steady motion.
**Answer: True** — the name of the keeping-on

**7. (Sub lesson 1)** The first law's condition is that NO forces act at all.
**Answer: False** — the condition is no NET force; balanced forces are fine

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** At a sudden start, passengers press back into their seats.
**Answer: True** — their bodies kept resting while the bus moved

**9. (Sub lesson 2)** The fast and slow cup pulls give the same result.
**Answer: False** — slow: follows; fast: stays
