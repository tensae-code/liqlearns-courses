# Force and motion — Newton's first law — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**10. (Sub lesson 1)** A body's keeping its state is called ______.
**Answer: inertia** — the first law's key word

**11. (Sub lesson 2)** The law turns on a ______ external force — balanced forces do not count.
**Answer: net** — net zero is the resting condition

**12. (Sub lesson 2)** When a bus turns, your body leans ______ — it keeps its straight line.
**Answer: outward** — the third lurch
