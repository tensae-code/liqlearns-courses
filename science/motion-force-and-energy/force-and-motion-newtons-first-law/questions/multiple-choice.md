# Force and motion — Newton's first law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the fast pull, the cup stays because —
a) the paper is magic  b) inertia: it keeps its state of rest when no net force drags it in time  c) the water holds it down  d) friction grows stronger
**Answer: b) inertia: it keeps its state of rest when no net force drags it in time** — the fast pull is gone before friction can drag it

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the slow pull, the cup follows because —
a) inertia stops working  b) friction has time to drag the cup with the paper  c) the cup is lighter  d) gravity pulls it along
**Answer: b) friction has time to drag the cup with the paper** — time is the difference between the pulls

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Newton's first law says a moving body keeps constant velocity unless —
a) it gets tired  b) a net external force acts on it  c) it meets a hill  d) a minute passes
**Answer: b) a net external force acts on it** — the law's exact condition

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** When the bus brakes suddenly, passengers lurch forward because —
a) the brakes push them  b) their bodies keep moving while the bus stops  c) gravity increases  d) the seats tilt
**Answer: b) their bodies keep moving while the bus stops** — inertia, felt

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A resting book with balanced forces on it —
a) breaks the first law  b) obeys it: net force is zero, so its state holds  c) has no forces on it at all  d) must fall
**Answer: b) obeys it: net force is zero, so its state holds** — net zero is the resting condition
