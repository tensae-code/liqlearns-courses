# Video script — Force and motion — Newton's first law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Set the cup on the paper. Pull **slow** — the cup follows.
Pull **fast** — the cup **stays**. That staying is **inertia**.
**Newton's first law:** a body keeps its state — rest, or steady motion —
unless a **net** force acts. The bus lurch is the law, felt.
