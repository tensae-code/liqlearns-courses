# Hands-on — Force and motion — Newton's first law

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Cup and paper, both ways
Fill a cup with water and stand it on hard paper on a smooth table. Pull the paper slowly: the cup rides with it. Reset; whip the paper out fast: the cup stays, the water barely stirs. Do each three times and say the difference in inertia's words.

## Lurch log
On the next bus or car ride, log every lurch: start, stop, turn. For each, write which way your body 'wanted' to keep going. Match each line to the law: your body kept its state; the vehicle changed its.
