# Conservation of energy and matter — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The law for matter — nothing is lost.
**Answer:** The **law of conservation of matter** says that in any change, matter is **not created and not destroyed**: it is rearranged. The wood, the ash, the smoke and the gases together account for all the matter that was there at the start.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The law for energy — nothing is lost.
**Answer:** The **law of conservation of energy** says the same of energy: energy is **not created and not destroyed** — it is **converted**, passed from form to form. The lamp's electrical energy becomes light and heat; nothing of it is annihilated, and nothing appears from nowhere.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: One law held together.
**Answer:** Costa Rica's book teaches the two conservations as **one law of conservation of matter and energy**: matter and energy alike are conserved — rearranged and converted, never created from nothing and never destroyed into nothing.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The law for matter — nothing is lost → The law for energy — nothing is lost → One law held together — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
