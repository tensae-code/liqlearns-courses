# Video script — Force and motion — Newton's third law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Blow up the balloon. Let go. The air **jets out** — the balloon **jets away**.
**Action** and **reaction**: equal, opposite —
and they never cancel, because they land on **different bodies**.
Your foot pushes the ground **back**. The ground pushes **you** forward.
