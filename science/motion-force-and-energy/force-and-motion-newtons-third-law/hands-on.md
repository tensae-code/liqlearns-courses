# Hands-on — Force and motion — Newton's third law

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The balloon jet
Blow up a balloon and release it untied, three times. Each time call out the pair: the balloon pushes air back (action on the air); the air pushes the balloon forward (reaction on the balloon). Note the balloon shrinks as it flies — its push spends its air.

## Walk by the law
Stand and take one slow step, attending to your foot: it pushes the ground backward; the ground's answering push carries you forward. Then jump: legs push down, ground pushes up. Name both bodies of each pair aloud.
