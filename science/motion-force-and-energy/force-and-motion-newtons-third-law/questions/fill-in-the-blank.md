# Force and motion — Newton's third law — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**10.** Every action has an equal and ______ reaction.
**Answer: opposite** — the law's two adjectives after equal

**11.** The pair never cancels because the forces act on ______ bodies.
**Answer: different** — each body answers its own force

**12.** Birds push air ______ to rise.
**Answer: down** — the reaction lifts them
