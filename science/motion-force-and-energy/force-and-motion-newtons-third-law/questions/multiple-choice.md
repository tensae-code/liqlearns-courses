# Force and motion — Newton's third law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Newton's third law pairs every action with —
a) a bigger reaction  b) an equal and opposite reaction  c) no reaction on hard ground  d) a delayed reaction
**Answer: b) an equal and opposite reaction** — same size, opposite direction

**2.** The balloon jets forward because —
a) the air pulls it  b) the air it pushes backward pushes it forward in return  c) gravity tilts  d) the room's air pushes it
**Answer: b) the air it pushes backward pushes it forward in return** — the reaction acts on the balloon

**3.** Action and reaction never cancel because —
a) they are not really equal  b) they act on different bodies  c) one comes later  d) they act on the same body
**Answer: b) they act on different bodies** — each body answers only its own force

**4.** A bird rises by —
a) pushing air up  b) pushing air down, and being pushed up in return  c) flapping without touching air  d) lightness alone
**Answer: b) pushing air down, and being pushed up in return** — the pair at the wings

**5.** Walking forward, your foot pushes the ground —
a) forward  b) backward  c) sideways only  d) not at all
**Answer: b) backward** — and the ground's answer carries you forward
