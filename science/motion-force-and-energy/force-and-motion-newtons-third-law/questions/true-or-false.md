# Force and motion — Newton's third law — true or false

Say true or false.

**6.** Action and reaction act on the same body.
**Answer: False** — different bodies — that is why they never cancel

**7.** A jump is powered by the ground pushing you up as you push it down.
**Answer: True** — the pair at the feet

**8.** The reaction is always weaker than the action on soft ground.
**Answer: False** — the pair is always equal

**9.** A rocket rises by pushing its gas downward.
**Answer: True** — the gas pushes the rocket up in return
