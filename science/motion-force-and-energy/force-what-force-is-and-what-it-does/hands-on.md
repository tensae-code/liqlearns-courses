# Hands-on — Force — what force is and what it does

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The kicked-ball story
Kick a resting ball gently across the yard. Call out each chapter as it happens: the kick starts it (contact force), it rolls, friction slows it, it stops. Repeat on grass and on smooth ground and compare how long friction takes.

## Shape test
Squeeze clay, stretch a rubber band, press soft fruit. Each change of shape or size is a force's signature. List which forces your hands applied and what each one changed.
