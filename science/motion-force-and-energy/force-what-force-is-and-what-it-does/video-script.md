# Video script — Force — what force is and what it does

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Force** — one body acting on another.
By **touch**: push, pull, kick. Across a **gap**: gravity, magnetism.
Force **starts** motion, **stops** it, **turns** it, **reshapes** it.
The kicked ball tells the whole story.
