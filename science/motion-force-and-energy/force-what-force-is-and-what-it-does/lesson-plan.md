# Lesson plan — Force — what force is and what it does

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Motion, Force and Energy · **Lesson:** Force — what force is and what it does
**Place in the curriculum:** Motion, Force and Energy — Unit 7, the force strand opens (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a force is an interaction between bodies that acts by contact (push, pull) or across a gap without contact (gravity, magnetism, electric force) — and its effects are to start or stop motion, change speed or direction, and change a body's shape or size
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Motion — acceleration: velocity changes are measured; now the thing that causes those changes is named.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sort forces into contact and non-contact, with the book's examples of each.
2. Recite force's effects on the kicked ball: starts it, friction stops it; and in general — speed, direction, shape and size.
3. Explain force as an interaction between two bodies, never a thing one body owns alone.

## What you need
A ball for the kick test, clay or soft fruit for the shape test, a magnet and a nail for the gap test.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a force must touch → gravity and the magnet act across a gap; those are the non-contact family.
- Thinks a resting body proves 'no force anywhere' → forces can balance; the law lessons ahead make that precise.
