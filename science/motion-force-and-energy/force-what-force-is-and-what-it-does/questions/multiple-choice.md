# Force — what force is and what it does — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A force is —
a) a thing a body owns  b) an interaction between bodies  c) a kind of speed  d) stored motion
**Answer: b) an interaction between bodies** — it always happens between two

**2.** Which of these is a non-contact force?
a) a push on a cart  b) a pull on a rope  c) magnetism drawing iron  d) a kick
**Answer: c) magnetism drawing iron** — it acts across a gap

**3.** In the kicked-ball story, the ball is stopped by —
a) gravity alone  b) friction  c) the kick  d) its own weight only
**Answer: b) friction** — the ground's grip slows and stops it

**4.** A force can change a body's —
a) speed, direction, shape or size  b) colour and name  c) age  d) unit
**Answer: a) speed, direction, shape or size** — the three printed effects

**5.** Gravity is classed as —
a) a contact force  b) a non-contact force  c) not a force  d) friction
**Answer: b) a non-contact force** — it pulls across empty space
