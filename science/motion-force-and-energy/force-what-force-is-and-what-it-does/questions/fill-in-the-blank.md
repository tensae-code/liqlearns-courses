# Force — what force is and what it does — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**10.** Throwing, holding, walking and cutting are all examples of ______ at work.
**Answer: force** — interactions between bodies

**11.** Forces that act by touch are ______ forces.
**Answer: contact** — push and pull

**12.** The force that stops the kicked ball is ______.
**Answer: friction** — the ground's grip
