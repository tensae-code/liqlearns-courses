# Force — what force is and what it does — true or false

Say true or false.

**6.** A force can happen with only one body involved.
**Answer: False** — a force is an interaction — it needs two

**7.** Squeezing clay shows a force changing shape.
**Answer: True** — one of the printed effects

**8.** Friction is a contact force.
**Answer: True** — it acts where surfaces touch

**9.** Electric force needs the charges to touch.
**Answer: False** — it acts across a gap
