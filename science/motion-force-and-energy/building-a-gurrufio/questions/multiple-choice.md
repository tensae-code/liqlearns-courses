# Building a gurrufío — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A gurrufío is a…
a) water toy  b) traditional spinning toy  c) kite  d) drum  
**Answer: b) traditional spinning toy**

**2.** The gurrufío's energy comes from…
a) the string itself  b) the disc itself  c) the person pulling the string  d) the wind only  
**Answer: c) the person pulling the string**

**3.** The disc's motion is…
a) falling  b) spinning  c) sliding  d) floating  
**Answer: b) spinning**
