# Building a gurrufío — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Trace the gurrufío's energy from start to spin.
**Answer: The person's body supplies the energy; the hands pull the string; the string passes the energy to the disc, which spins.**

**2.** Why does the book ask who supplies the energy, instead of just building the toy?
**Answer: Because the toy is the lesson's way into energy: every motion has a supplier, and the gurrufío's supplier is the player.**
