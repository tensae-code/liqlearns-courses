# Building a gurrufío

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's title — Building a gurrufío. Say out loud anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on: this is Venezuela's way in — its Grade 2 *Comunidad con Ciencia* teaches energy by making a traditional Venezuelan toy: the gurrufío, a spinning disc on a string.

## Sub lesson 1 — The gurrufío — a spinning toy

The **gurrufío** is a traditional Venezuelan toy: a disc threaded on a string. Pulling the string with both hands sets the disc **spinning**, faster and faster, humming as it turns. The book's activity is to **build one** and play with it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Who supplies its energy?

A spinning gurrufío is motion — and motion takes **energy**. The book's question is: **who supplies its energy?** Not the string, and not the disc: the **person** pulling — your own body's energy, passed through your hands and the string into the spin.
