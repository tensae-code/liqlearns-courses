# Force and motion — Newton's second law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the cart experiment, doubling the hanging force (same cart) —
a) halves the acceleration  b) doubles the acceleration  c) stops the cart  d) changes only the direction
**Answer: b) doubles the acceleration** — the graph's straight line is proportion

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Newton's second law is —
a) a = F × m  b) a = F/m  c) a = m/F  d) F = a/m only
**Answer: b) a = F/m** — proportional to force, inverse to mass

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** 20 N on 5 kg gives —
a) 4 m/s²  b) 25 m/s²  c) 100 m/s²  d) 0.25 m/s²
**Answer: a) 4 m/s²** — 20 ÷ 5 = 4

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Loading the cart heavier, same pull, gives —
a) more acceleration  b) less acceleration  c) the same acceleration  d) no motion ever
**Answer: b) less acceleration** — mass divides the force's effect

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The force–acceleration graph's straight rising line shows —
a) no relation  b) acceleration proportional to force  c) force falling  d) mass growing
**Answer: b) acceleration proportional to force** — the experiment's verdict, drawn
