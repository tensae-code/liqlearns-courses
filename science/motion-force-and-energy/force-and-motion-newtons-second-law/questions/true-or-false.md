# Force and motion — Newton's second law — true or false

Say true or false.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** Acceleration acts in the direction of the net force.
**Answer: True** — part of the law's statement

**7. (Sub lesson 1)** Doubling the mass at the same force doubles the acceleration.
**Answer: False** — it halves it

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** The cart experiment changes the cart's mass at every step of the first run.
**Answer: False** — first run: same cart, growing force

**9. (Sub lesson 2)** F = ma can be worked backwards to find an unknown force.
**Answer: True** — 4 kg at 3 m/s² needs 12 N
