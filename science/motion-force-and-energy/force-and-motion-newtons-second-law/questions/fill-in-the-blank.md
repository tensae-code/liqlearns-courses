# Force and motion — Newton's second law — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**10. (Sub lesson 1)** a = F ÷ ______.
**Answer: m (mass)** — the law divides by mass

**11. (Sub lesson 2)** 30 N on 6 kg gives ______ m/s².
**Answer: 5** — 30 ÷ 6

**12. (Sub lesson 2)** The graph of force against acceleration is a straight ______ line.
**Answer: rising** — proportion, drawn
