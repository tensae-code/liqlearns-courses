# Hands-on — Force and motion — Newton's second law

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Cart and hanging masses
Set the cart, string and pulley. Hang one mass; release; time the run over a measured length; compute the acceleration. Repeat with two and three masses, same cart. Tabulate force against acceleration and plot the graph — the straight rising line is the experiment's verdict.

## Load the cart
Keep one hanging mass and load the cart with extra mass instead. Release and time. The acceleration falls as the mass grows — the law's second half, felt in the timings.
