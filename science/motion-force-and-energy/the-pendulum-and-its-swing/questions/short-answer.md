# The pendulum and its swing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Describe the path of a swinging pendulum.
**Answer:** It swings down through the lowest point, up the other side, back down and up the first side again — back and forth, passing its hanging-still place in the middle of every swing.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What is one swing?
**Answer:** One complete journey — from one side, across, and back to where it started.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Write the steps of the pendulum experiment in order.
**Answer:** Build it (tie the bob and fix the string's top); pull the bob aside and let go without pushing; count one swing each time the bob returns to the release side; time how long 10 swings take; watch the swings shrink until the bob stops.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Why were pendulums used in clocks?
**Answer:** Because, counted and timed, the pendulum swings with a steady beat — the same time, swing after swing.
