# The pendulum and its swing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A pendulum is…
a) A wheel that rolls  b) A weight hanging from a string, free to swing  c) A spring that bounces  d) A ball that falls once
**Answer: b) A weight hanging from a string, free to swing**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The hanging weight of a pendulum is called…
a) The string  b) The beat  c) The bob  d) The swing
**Answer: c) The bob**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) One swing is…
a) Half the journey  b) The journey down only  c) One complete back-and-forth journey  d) The first movement after release
**Answer: c) One complete back-and-forth journey**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) To start the pendulum fairly, you…
a) Push it hard  b) Throw the bob  c) Pull it aside and let go without pushing  d) Spin the string
**Answer: c) Pull it aside and let go without pushing**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Timed swing after swing, the pendulum keeps…
a) A steady beat  b) A faster and faster beat  c) No beat at all  d) One swing per hour
**Answer: a) A steady beat**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Left alone, the pendulum's swings…
a) Grow bigger  b) Stay the same size forever  c) Jump sideways  d) Slowly get smaller until it stops
**Answer: d) Slowly get smaller until it stops**
