# The pendulum and its swing — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The weight hanging at the end of a pendulum's string is the _____.
**Answer: bob**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A pendulum swings back and forth through its _____ point.
**Answer: lowest**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Let go of the bob without _____ it.
**Answer: pushing**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Left alone, the swings get smaller and smaller until the bob hangs _____.
**Answer: still**
