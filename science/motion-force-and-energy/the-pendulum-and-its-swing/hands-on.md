# Hands-on — The pendulum and its swing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build and swing
Tie a stone or metal washer firmly to a string, about as long as your arm. Fix the top of the string to a table edge or a door frame so the bob hangs free. Pull the bob to one side and let go — no push. Write what the bob does, in the order it does it.

## Count and time
Start the pendulum again the same fair way. Count the swings out loud — one each time the bob returns to your release side — and time 10 swings. Write the count and the time. Start it once more and repeat: write whether the time for 10 swings is about the same.

## Watch it fade
Start the pendulum and do not touch it again. Watch until it stops. Write how the swings changed from the first to the last, and the lesson's word for what happened to them.
