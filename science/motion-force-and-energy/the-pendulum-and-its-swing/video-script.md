Today: **The pendulum and its swing** — Cabo Verde's way: build one, and try it.

Tie a weight to a string. That hanging weight is a pendulum, and the weight is its bob. Pull the bob to one side — and let go. Do not push. Watch: down through the middle, up the other side, back again. One full journey, there and back, is one swing. Count ten swings and time them: the beat is steady, like a clock ticking. But look closely — each swing is a little smaller than the one before, until the bob hangs still.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
