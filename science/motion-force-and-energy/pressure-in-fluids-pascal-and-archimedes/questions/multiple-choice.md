# Pressure in fluids — Pascal and Archimedes — multiple choice


**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Hydrostatics is the study of…
a) Fluids at rest and the pressure in them  b) Only gases  c) Electricity in water  d) Rivers in flood
**Answer: a) Fluids at rest and the pressure in them**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) As you go deeper in a fluid, the pressure…
a) Gets smaller  b) Stays the same  c) Grows  d) Disappears
**Answer: c) Grows**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A fluid presses on…
a) Only the bottom of its vessel  b) Everything it touches  c) Only floating things  d) Nothing
**Answer: b) Everything it touches**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Pascal's principle says pressure applied to a closed fluid is…
a) Lost at once  b) Passed through it undiminished  c) Turned into heat  d) Blocked by water
**Answer: b) Passed through it undiminished**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Archimedes' principle says the upthrust on a body equals…
a) The body's own weight always  b) The weight of the fluid it displaces  c) The depth of the water  d) The size of the vessel
**Answer: b) The weight of the fluid it displaces**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) A body floats when the upthrust…
a) Is zero  b) Is smaller than its weight  c) Matches its weight  d) Is a gas
**Answer: c) Matches its weight**
