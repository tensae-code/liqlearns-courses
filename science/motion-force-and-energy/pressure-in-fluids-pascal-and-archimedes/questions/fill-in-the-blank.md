# Pressure in fluids — Pascal and Archimedes — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The study of fluids at rest is called _____.
**Answer: hydrostatics**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Pressure in a fluid grows with _____.
**Answer: depth**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) _____ principle: pressure in a closed fluid is passed through it undiminished.
**Answer: Pascal's**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Upthrust equals the weight of the fluid a body _____.
**Answer: displaces**
