# Pressure in fluids — Pascal and Archimedes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does pressure grow with depth in a fluid?
**Answer: Because the fluid above weighs down on the fluid below — the deeper layer carries more weight pressing on it.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State Pascal's principle in your own words.
**Answer: Pressure applied to a fluid in a closed vessel is passed through the fluid, undiminished, to every part of it.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A stone sinks and a cork floats — explain with Archimedes' principle.
**Answer: Each is pushed up by the weight of the fluid it displaces: the cork's weight matches that upthrust so it floats; the stone is heavier than the fluid it displaces, so it sinks.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) What does a fluid press on? Name three things.
**Answer: Any three of: the sides of its vessel, the bottom of its vessel, and anything dipped into it.**
