# Hands-on — Pressure in fluids — Pascal and Archimedes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel Pascal's principle
Fill a plastic bottle with water, close it, and squeeze one part of it: feel the whole bottle firm up — the pressure has been passed through the water to every part. Say the principle out loud while you squeeze.

## Weigh the displaced water
Float a light bowl in a basin filled to the brim over a tray. Add small stones to the bowl one at a time and catch the water that spills — that spilled water is the displaced fluid, and its weight is the upthrust holding the bowl up.
