# Hands-on — Motion — motion needs a reference point

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The two-chair bus
Set two chairs as bus seats. One learner sits; another walks past slowly. The sitter answers: am I moving? First against the walker, then against the classroom wall, then against the chair. Three frames, three honest answers.

## Traffic-light watch
From a safe place by a road, pick one moving car. Judge it against a lamp post, then against another car moving beside it, then against a car coming the other way. Write the three judgements with their frames named.
