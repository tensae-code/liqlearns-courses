# Motion — motion needs a reference point — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**10.** Motion is change of ______ against a chosen reference point.
**Answer: position** — position is what changes

**11.** The chosen thing motion is judged against is the ______ point.
**Answer: reference** — reference point, reference frame

**12.** A passenger judged against two passing traffic lights is ______.
**Answer: moving** — his position beside each light changes
