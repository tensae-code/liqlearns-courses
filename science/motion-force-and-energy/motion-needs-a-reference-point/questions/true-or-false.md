# Motion — motion needs a reference point — true or false

Say true or false.

**6.** A body can be moving and at rest at the same time, in different frames.
**Answer: True** — the bus passenger proves it

**7.** 'It is moving' is a complete answer with no frame named.
**Answer: False** — the frame is half the sentence

**8.** Against himself, a bus passenger is moving.
**Answer: False** — against himself he is at rest

**9.** Rest means no change of position against the chosen frame.
**Answer: True** — that is the definition
