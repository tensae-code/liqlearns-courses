# Motion — motion needs a reference point — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Motion means —
a) standing very still  b) change of position against a chosen reference point  c) moving very fast  d) travelling a long way
**Answer: b) change of position against a chosen reference point** — the frame must be named before the judgement means anything

**2.** A passenger sits in a moving bus. Against the bus itself, the passenger is —
a) moving fast  b) at rest  c) accelerating  d) falling
**Answer: b) at rest** — he keeps his seat; against the bus his position does not change

**3.** The same passenger, judged against the ground, is —
a) at rest  b) moving  c) both at once in the same frame  d) unmeasurable
**Answer: b) moving** — the bus carries him along the road

**4.** A reference frame is —
a) a picture frame  b) the chosen point or set-up motion is judged against  c) the speed of a body  d) a kind of vehicle
**Answer: b) the chosen point or set-up motion is judged against** — every motion judgement is made inside a frame

**5.** Two drivers on the same road give opposite answers about the trip because —
a) one is lying  b) the road changed  c) they judge against different reference points  d) the car changed
**Answer: c) they judge against different reference points** — the facts are the same; the frames differ
