# Hands-on — Trajectory — the trail a moving body leaves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — Trajectory — the trail a moving body leaves. 15: trajectory defined as the trail a moving body leaves; straight and curved trails (jet trails, boat wake, tyre tracks), some trajectories invisible; drawing your home-to-school route; Almaty–Astana transport comparison map task Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
