# Trajectory — the trail a moving body leaves — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about trajectory — the trail a moving body leaves?
**Answer: 15: trajectory defined as the trail a moving body leaves; straight and curved trails (jet trails, boat wake, tyre tracks), some trajectories invisible; drawing your home-to-school route; Almaty–Astana transport comparison map task**

**2.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about what a movement trajectory is?
**Answer: The books teach this part as a trajectory can sometimes be seen (jet trail, boat wake) and sometimes not (car on a highway) — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.**
