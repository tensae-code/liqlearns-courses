# Trajectory — the trail a moving body leaves — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** 15: trajectory defined as the trail a moving body leaves; straight and curved trails (jet trails, boat wake, tyre tracks), some ______ invisible; drawing your home-to-school route; Almaty–Astana transport comparison map task
**Answer: trajectories**

**2.** The books teach this part as a trajectory can sometimes be seen (jet trail, boat wake) and sometimes not (car on a highway) — worked through in the books' own ______, at the printed pages named in Sources.
**Answer: presentation**

**3.** the line a body describes as it moves, straight (______) or curved (curvilinear); the path travelled is the length of the trajectory; toy-car path measured on flat and sloped surfaces; the marsh harrier's flight trajectory looks like a kite frozen in the air
**Answer: rectilinear**
