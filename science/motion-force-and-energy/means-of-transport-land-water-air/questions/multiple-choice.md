# Means of transport — land, water and air — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A ship is classified as…
a) land transport  b) water transport  c) air transport  d) no transport  
**Answer: b) water transport**

**2.** The question that classifies a means of transport is…
a) how fast is it?  b) what colour is it?  c) where does it travel?  d) who built it?  
**Answer: c) where does it travel?**

**3.** A train belongs with…
a) aeroplanes  b) land transport  c) boats  d) air transport  
**Answer: b) land transport**
