# Means of transport — land, water and air — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name one means of transport for each place.
**Answer: Land: a bus (or car, bicycle, train). Water: a boat (or ship, canoe). Air: an aeroplane (or helicopter).**

**2.** Why does the classification ask where a vehicle travels, and not how big it is?
**Answer: Because the three classes are the three places a journey can happen — land, water and air; size does not change the place a vehicle travels in.**
