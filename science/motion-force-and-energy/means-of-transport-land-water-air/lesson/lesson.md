# Means of transport — land, water and air

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's title — Means of transport — land, water and air. Say out loud anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. ## Sub lesson 1 — Three places to travel

Every journey happens in one of three places: on the **land**, on or in the **water**, or in the **air**. The means of transport — the vehicles and vessels that carry people and things — are classified by the place they travel in.

## Sub lesson 2 — Land, water and air

**Land** transport moves on roads, tracks and paths — cars, buses, bicycles and trains. **Water** transport moves on rivers, lakes and seas — boats, ships and canoes. **Air** transport moves through the air — aeroplanes and helicopters. To classify a means of transport, ask one question: where does it travel?
