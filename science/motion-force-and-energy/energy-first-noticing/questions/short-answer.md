# Energy — first noticing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is energy, as a first noticing?
**Answer:** Energy is the something at work when things shine, warm, sound or move — it is not held in the hand like a thing, it is noticed by what it does.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Name four signs that energy is at work, with one example of each.
**Answer:** Shining (a lamp), warming (the sun on skin), sounding (a drum being hit) and moving (a ball rolling).

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How does Zambia's Grade 1 module bring energy in, and why does that place make sense for a first noticing?
**Answer:** It brings energy in inside the materials unit, among the things learners handle every day, so the first meeting with energy is noticing it at work in familiar things before any lists of kinds or sources.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A stone sits on the ground all day. Is energy at work in it? Explain with the lesson's sorting.
**Answer:** Not in the lesson's noticing sense: the stone sits still and quiet until something acts on it — it belongs in the waiting pile, unlike a shining lamp, a warm fire, a sounding bell or a moving bicycle, which show energy at work.
