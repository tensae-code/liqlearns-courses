# Energy — first noticing — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The something at work when things shine, warm, sound or move is called _____.
**Answer: energy**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Energy is noticed by what it _____.
**Answer: does**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A torch gives _____, a cooking fire gives warmth, a bell gives sound.
**Answer: light**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Things that sit still and _____ are waiting until something acts on them.
**Answer: quiet**
