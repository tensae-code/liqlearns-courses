# Energy — first noticing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Energy is first noticed by…
a) Holding it in your hand  b) What it does — shining, warming, sounding, moving  c) Its colour  d) Its price
**Answer: b) What it does — shining, warming, sounding, moving**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these shows energy at work?
a) A stone sitting still  b) A lamp shining  c) A cup on a shelf  d) A spoon in a drawer
**Answer: b) A lamp shining**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The sun warming your skin is an example of…
a) Energy noticed at work  b) A material  c) A sound  d) Nothing happening
**Answer: a) Energy noticed at work**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Zambia's Grade 1 module meets energy inside its unit on…
a) Animals  b) Materials  c) Maps  d) Stories
**Answer: b) Materials**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A cup, a stone and a spoon, left alone, are…
a) Showing energy at work  b) Sitting still and quiet until something acts on them  c) Shining  d) Sounding
**Answer: b) Sitting still and quiet until something acts on them**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The kinds of energy and where energy comes from are…
a) This lesson's whole content  b) Never taught  c) Later lessons, standing on this first noticing  d) Only for teachers
**Answer: c) Later lessons, standing on this first noticing**
