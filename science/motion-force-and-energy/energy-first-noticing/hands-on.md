# Hands-on — Energy — first noticing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The noticing walk
With adult help, walk slowly around one room or the school yard. Stop at anything that shines, warms, sounds or moves. At each stop, say out loud what is happening: "The sun is warming." "The radio is sounding." "The bicycle is moving."

## The materials table
Gather five ordinary things — for example a cup, a stone, a torch, a ball and a spoon. Put each one in turn on the table and ask: does this thing give light, warmth, sound or movement by itself, or does it sit still and quiet until something acts on it? Sort the five into the two piles.

## The noticing chart
Make a chart: Thing | What it does | Light, warmth, sound or movement? Fill one row for each thing you noticed, then, pointing at your chart, explain in your own words what energy is, as a first noticing.
