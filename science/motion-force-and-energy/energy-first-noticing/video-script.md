Today: **Energy — first noticing** — Zambia's way, from its Grade 1 Mathematics and Science module.

Look around. The sun warms your skin. A lamp shines. A drum sounds. A ball rolls.
Something is at work in every one of those — and that something has a name: energy.
You cannot hold energy in your hand. You notice it by what it does.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
