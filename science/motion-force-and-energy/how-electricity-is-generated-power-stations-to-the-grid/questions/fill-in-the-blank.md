# How electricity is generated — power stations from source to grid — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**13.** (Sub lesson 1) The machine with blades that every power station is built around is the ______.
**Answer: turbine** — something must spin; the turbine is what spins

**14.** (Sub lesson 1) In a fuel-fired station, heat boils water into high-pressure ______ that pushes the turbine.
**Answer: steam** — chemical → thermal → kinetic of steam → kinetic of turbine → electrical

**15.** (Sub lesson 2) The spinning turbine drives the ______, which turns motion into electric current.
**Answer: generator** — motion to electricity, the motor run backwards

**16.** (Sub lesson 2) The web of wires that carries electricity across a whole country is called the ______.
**Answer: grid** — station → grid → street → socket
