# How electricity is generated — power stations from source to grid — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a coal-fired power station, the steam's job is to —
a) make the electricity directly  b) spin the turbine  c) cool the generator  d) charge the grid
**Answer: b) spin the turbine** — heat boils water, steam pushes the blades, the shaft spins

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A hydro station reaches the spinning turbine by a shorter road because —
a) it has no turbine  b) falling water pushes the turbine directly, skipping the steam step  c) it burns water  d) its generator makes steam
**Answer: b) falling water pushes the turbine directly, skipping the steam step** — gravitational → kinetic in one step

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The full chain of a fuel-fired station, in order, is —
a) electrical → thermal → kinetic → chemical  b) chemical → kinetic → thermal → electrical  c) chemical → thermal → kinetic of steam → kinetic of turbine → electrical  d) thermal → chemical → electrical → kinetic
**Answer: c) chemical → thermal → kinetic of steam → kinetic of turbine → electrical** — name the form at each stage

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The generator's one job is to —
a) spin the turbine  b) boil the water  c) turn spinning into electric current  d) store electricity
**Answer: c) turn spinning into electric current** — the turbine spins; the generator generates

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Electricity is stepped UP in voltage for the long journey across the country because —
a) high voltage travels with less energy degrading as heat in the wires  b) homes need very high voltage  c) generators cannot make low voltage  d) pylons are tall
**Answer: a) high voltage travels with less energy degrading as heat in the wires** — then it is stepped down again near the home

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The grid stores —
a) a week's electricity  b) almost nothing — electricity is made at the moment it is used  c) electricity in the pylons  d) only solar energy
**Answer: b) almost nothing — electricity is made at the moment it is used** — that is why stations spin day and night

**7.** (Sub lesson 1) A wind station and a nuclear station share exactly one stage —
a) burning  b) steam from a boiler only  c) a spinning turbine driving a generator  d) falling water
**Answer: c) a spinning turbine driving a generator** — the middle of the chain never changes
