# Video script — How electricity is generated — power stations from source to grid

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Burn** the fuel → **boil** the water → steam **pushes** the turbine.
Or the **river falls**, or the **wind blows** — the turbine **spins** all the same.
The spinning shaft turns the **generator** — and the generator makes **electricity**.
Up the voltage, across the **country**, down again — into your **home**.
Source → spin → electricity → **light**.
