# Work — and power — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: What work is in physics.
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's Standards Based Grade 8 guide opens its Work and Power lessons with the physics meaning of **work**: work is done when a force moves something through a distance — the relationship between **force, work and distance** is the lesson's core, and pushing without moving does no work in this sense, however tiring it feels.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Units of work — and calculating it.
**Answer:** The guide names the **units of force and of work**, then calculates: work is found from the force and the distance it moves through, in the force's direction. Force and distance are the two numbers every work calculation needs.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Power — how fast work is done.
**Answer:** The run closes with **power**: what power is — how fast work is done — and the **units of power**. Two people can do the same work; the one who does it faster has the greater power. Work prices the job; power prices the pace.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** What work is in physics → Units of work — and calculating it → Power — how fast work is done — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
