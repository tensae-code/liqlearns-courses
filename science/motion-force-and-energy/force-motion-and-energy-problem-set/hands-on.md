# Hands-on — Force, motion and energy — problem set

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Graph first, numbers after
Redraw the speed–time graph large. In words first: where does the journey speed up, hold steady, slow down? Then shade the area under the line and compute the distance from it. The story before the arithmetic, every time.

## The comparison bench
Compute both: a lorry of double mass at the car's speed, and the car at double speed. Set the two KEs side by side. The squared factor's verdict, in your own numbers.
