# Force, motion and energy — problem set — true or false

Say true or false.

**6.** A flat speed–time section means steady speed.
**Answer: True** — the middle interval

**7.** Doubling mass quadruples kinetic energy.
**Answer: False** — it doubles it; doubling speed quadruples

**8.** The goalkeeper's kick begins its chain with chemical energy in the muscles.
**Answer: True** — chemical → kinetic → the ball's flight

**9.** Distance under a speed–time graph is found from the area.
**Answer: True** — area = speed × time accumulated
