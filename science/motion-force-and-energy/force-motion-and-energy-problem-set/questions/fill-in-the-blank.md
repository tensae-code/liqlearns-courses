# Force, motion and energy — problem set — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**10.** A rising speed–time section is ______.
**Answer: accelerating** — the first interval

**11.** v² = 2 × KE ÷ ______.
**Answer: m (mass)** — the back-calculation's formula

**12.** At the bounce or catch, some energy ends as sound and ______.
**Answer: warmth (thermal)** — the chain's last forms
