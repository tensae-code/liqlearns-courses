# Force, motion and energy — problem set — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** On a speed–time graph, a falling section shows —
a) steady speed  b) decelerating  c) accelerating  d) rest
**Answer: b) decelerating** — speed shrinking with time

**2.** Distance from a speed–time graph is —
a) its highest point  b) the area under the graph  c) its final speed  d) its slope only
**Answer: b) the area under the graph** — speed × time, accumulated

**3.** KE = 16 J, m = 2 kg: the speed is —
a) 8 m/s  b) 4 m/s  c) 2 m/s  d) 16 m/s
**Answer: b) 4 m/s** — v² = 2 × 16 ÷ 2 = 16, v = 4

**4.** Double speed against double mass — the bigger KE growth is —
a) double mass (×4)  b) double speed (×4 against ×2)  c) they tie  d) double mass (×8)
**Answer: b) double speed (×4 against ×2)** — the squared factor outruns the plain one

**5.** Gravity acts on a falling ball —
a) only at the top  b) only at the ground  c) at every point of its path  d) only in the middle
**Answer: c) at every point of its path** — the unit's review answer
