# Lesson plan — Force, motion and energy — problem set

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Motion, Force and Energy · **Lesson:** Force, motion and energy — problem set
**Place in the curriculum:** Motion, Force and Energy — Unit 7 closes with its assessment set (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the unit's review problems worked as one set — reading a speed–time graph, gravity on a falling ball, kinetic energy calculated backwards, the lorry-versus-car comparison, and the energy transfers in a goalkeeper's kick
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the whole of Unit 7 in this title: every lesson before it is its preparation; it proves the unit rather than teaching it.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Read a speed–time graph in intervals — accelerating, steady, decelerating — and take the distance from it.
2. Back-calculate a speed from KE: 16 J and 2 kg.
3. Judge the lorry-versus-car KE question by the squared speed, and trace the goalkeeper's-kick energy transfers.

## What you need
Squared paper, a calculator if the class allows one, and the unit's formulas open beside the work.

## If the learner is stuck
- Hunts for a formula for the graph questions → the graph IS the data: slope for acceleration, area for distance.
- Judges lorry vs car by mass alone → run both numbers: mass doubles KE once; speed doubles it four times.
