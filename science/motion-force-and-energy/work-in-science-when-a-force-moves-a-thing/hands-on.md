# Hands-on — Work in science — when a force moves a thing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find the work
Pull a chair, a school bag, or a small cart of books across the floor (with permission, on a clear floor). Say out loud: the force is my pull — the thing moved — work was done. Then push gently against a wall: say what is different.

## Near and far
Pull the same bag a short way, then a long way, with the same steady pull. Which trip was more work in the science sense? Say why, using the lesson's own test.
