# Work in science — when a force moves a thing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Syria's book opens the lesson with the question…
a) Who does the moving of the farmer's cart — the farmer or the horse?  b) How fast is the cart?  c) What is the cart made of?  d) Where is the farm?
**Answer: a) Who does the moving of the farmer's cart — the farmer or the horse?**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In science, work is done when…
a) A person feels tired  b) A force moves a thing  c) A thing stays still  d) A machine is switched off
**Answer: b) A force moves a thing**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The horse pulling the moving cart does work because…
a) The cart is heavy  b) The farmer is watching  c) Its pull moves the cart  d) The road is long
**Answer: c) Its pull moves the cart**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Pushing hard on a cart that will not move does, in the science sense…
a) Double work  b) No work — the thing did not move  c) Work only for the farmer  d) Work without a force
**Answer: b) No work — the thing did not move**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The test for whether work was done is…
a) Did anyone sweat?  b) Was a machine used?  c) Did the force move the thing?  d) Was the thing heavy?
**Answer: c) Did the force move the thing?**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) More work is done when the force is stronger or…
a) The thing moves farther  b) The thing is older  c) The day is hotter  d) The cart is painted
**Answer: a) The thing moves farther**
