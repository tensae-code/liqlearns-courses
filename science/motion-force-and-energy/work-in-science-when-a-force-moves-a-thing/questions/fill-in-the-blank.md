# Work in science — when a force moves a thing — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** In science, a force does work when it _____ a thing.
**Answer: moves**

**2.** The horse's _____ moves the farmer's cart.
**Answer: pull**

**3.** A push that moves nothing does _____ work in the science sense.
**Answer: no**

**4.** More force, or a longer move, means _____ work.
**Answer: more**
