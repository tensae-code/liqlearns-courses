# Hands-on — Buoyancy — weight in different mediums

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Lift a stone or a full bottle in the air and feel its weight. Then lower it into a bucket of water, still holding it, and feel the weight again. Which feels lighter, and by how much? If oil is available, repeat in oil and compare all three: air, water, oil.
