# Buoyancy — weight in different mediums

## Sub lesson 1 — Weight in air

Fiji's Year 8 Basic Science book weighs objects first in the medium we live in: **air**. In air an object shows the weight we know — the pull we feel when we lift it, and the reading we expect.

## Sub lesson 2 — Weight in water and in oil

Then the book changes the medium: the same object is weighed in **water**, and in **oil**. The reading changes with the medium: oil, water and air do not treat the object's weight the same way, so the same object can weigh differently without itself changing.

## Sub lesson 3 — Apparent weight in fluids

The name for what the scale shows inside a fluid is **apparent weight**: the object's weight as the fluid leaves it, after the fluid has carried part of the load. Fiji's book teaches the comparison itself — air against water against oil — as the way into buoyancy.
