# Buoyancy — weight in different mediums — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Weight in air.
**Answer:** Fiji's Year 8 Basic Science book weighs objects first in the medium we live in: air.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Weight in water and in oil.
**Answer:** Then the book changes the medium: the same object is weighed in water, and in oil.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Apparent weight in fluids.
**Answer:** The name for what the scale shows inside a fluid is apparent weight: the object's weight as the fluid leaves it, after the fluid has carried part of the load.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Weight in air → Weight in water and in oil → Apparent weight in fluids — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
