# Buoyancy — weight in different mediums — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Weight in air  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Weight in air**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Weight in water and in oil  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Weight in water and in oil**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Apparent weight in fluids  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Apparent weight in fluids**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: how oil, water and air affect the weight and mass of objects — apparent weight in fluids. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Fiji, Year 8 — *Year 8 Basic Science*, printed pp. 105-125  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Fiji, Year 8 — *Year 8 Basic Science*, printed pp. 105-125**
