# Hands-on — Motion — displacement and velocity

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The two walks
Between two marks, walk a winding route counting paces; then walk straight back counting paces. The first count is distance-style; the second is the size of the displacement. Say the displacement fully: so many metres, in that direction.

## Graph two walkers
Two walkers' distance–time tables are given by the teacher. Plot both on one graph. The steeper line belongs to the faster walker — read both speeds off the slopes and check by dividing.
