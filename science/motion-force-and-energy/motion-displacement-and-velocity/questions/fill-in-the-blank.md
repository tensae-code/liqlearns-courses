# Motion — displacement and velocity — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**10.** ______ = change of displacement ÷ time.
**Answer: Velocity** — the directed rate

**11.** A flat distance–time graph shows a body at ______.
**Answer: rest** — distance is not changing

**12.** Displacement has size and ______.
**Answer: direction** — that is what separates it from distance
