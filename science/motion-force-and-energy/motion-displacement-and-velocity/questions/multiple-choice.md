# Motion — displacement and velocity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Displacement is —
a) the whole winding path  b) distance in a specified direction — the straight change from start to finish  c) the time taken  d) speed without direction
**Answer: b) distance in a specified direction — the straight change from start to finish** — size and direction together

**2.** Velocity equals —
a) change of displacement ÷ time  b) distance × time  c) time ÷ displacement  d) speed + mass
**Answer: a) change of displacement ÷ time** — speed's formula with direction kept

**3.** On a distance–time graph, the slope gives —
a) the mass  b) the speed  c) the direction home  d) the force
**Answer: b) the speed** — steep is fast, flat is rest

**4.** A flat distance–time line means the body is —
a) fastest  b) at rest  c) accelerating  d) falling
**Answer: b) at rest** — no distance is being added

**5.** A winding path ends 20 m from its start. The displacement is —
a) the full path length  b) 20 m, in the direction from start to finish  c) zero  d) the path length minus 20 m
**Answer: b) 20 m, in the direction from start to finish** — displacement is the straight arrow
