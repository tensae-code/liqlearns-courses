# Motion — displacement and velocity — true or false

Say true or false.

**6.** Distance and displacement always have the same size.
**Answer: False** — only a straight, one-direction path makes them equal

**7.** Two bodies can share a speed but hold different velocities.
**Answer: True** — different directions make different velocities

**8.** A steeper distance–time line means a slower body.
**Answer: False** — steeper means faster

**9.** Velocity's unit is m/s, stated with its direction.
**Answer: True** — same unit as speed, direction added
