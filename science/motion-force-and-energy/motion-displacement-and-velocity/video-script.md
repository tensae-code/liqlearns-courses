# Video script — Motion — displacement and velocity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Distance** walks the whole winding road.
**Displacement** takes the straight arrow — **with its direction**.
Twenty metres of path can end in a displacement far smaller.
**Velocity = displacement ÷ time.** The graph's slope is the speed.
