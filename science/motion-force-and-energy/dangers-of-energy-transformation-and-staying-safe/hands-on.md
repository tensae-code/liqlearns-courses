# Hands-on — Dangers of energy transformation — and staying safe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
List five appliances at home and write for each: the energy transformation it makes, the danger that travels with it, and the safety measure used in your home. Check one appliance's cord and plug for damage as you finish.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of dangers of energy transformation — and staying safe — every energy transformation that serves us can also harm us — burns from heat, shock from electricity, damage from loud sound — and each danger has its own safety measures, which are part of using the energy at all. — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
