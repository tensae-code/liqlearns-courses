# Dangers of energy transformation — and staying safe — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: heat transforms — and burns.
**Answer: Appliances that turn electricity into heat — cookers, irons, heaters — can burn skin, scorch materials and start fires. The safety measures match the danger: keep flammables away, never leave heating appliances unwatched, switch off after use, and treat every hot surface as able to burn until it has cooled.**

**2.** Explain: electricity transforms — and shocks.
**Answer: Electrical energy on its way to light, heat, sound and movement can pass through a body as an electric shock. Dry hands, undamaged insulation, no water near sockets, and never opening or repairing a live appliance are the measures that keep the transformation in the wires where it belongs.**

**3.** Explain: sound and movement transform — and injure.
**Answer: Very loud sound damages hearing over time and at once; moving machinery and falling loads carry kinetic energy that injures on contact. Distance, guards, ear protection and attention are the safety measures. The rule for all of them: name the transformation, and you can name its danger and its guard.**
