# Dangers of energy transformation — and staying safe — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Appliances that turn electricity into heat — cookers, irons, heaters — can burn skin, scorch materials and start fires.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Electricity transforms — and shocks is the opposite of what this lesson teaches.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches this as stated.**
