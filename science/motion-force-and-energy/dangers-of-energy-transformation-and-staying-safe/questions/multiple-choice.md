# Dangers of energy transformation — and staying safe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about dangers of energy transformation — and staying safe?
a) Every energy transformation that serves us can also harm us — burns from heat, shock from electricity, damage from loud sound — and each danger has its own safety measures, which are part of using the energy at all.
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Every energy transformation that serves us can also harm us — burns from heat, shock from electricity, damage from loud sound — and each danger has its own safety measures, which are part of using the energy at all.**

**2.** What does this lesson say about heat transforms — and burns?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Appliances that turn electricity into heat — cookers, irons, heaters — can burn skin, scorch materials and start fires.
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Appliances that turn electricity into heat — cookers, irons, heaters — can burn skin, scorch materials and start fires.**

**3.** What does this lesson say about electricity transforms — and shocks?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Electrical energy on its way to light, heat, sound and movement can pass through a body as an electric shock.
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Electrical energy on its way to light, heat, sound and movement can pass through a body as an electric shock.**

**4.** What does this lesson say about sound and movement transform — and injure?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Very loud sound damages hearing over time and at once; moving machinery and falling loads carry kinetic energy that injures on contact.
**Answer: d) Very loud sound damages hearing over time and at once; moving machinery and falling loads carry kinetic energy that injures on contact.**
