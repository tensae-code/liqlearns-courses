# Dangers of energy transformation — and staying safe — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (every energy transformation that serves us can also harm us — burns from heat, shock from electricity, damage from loud sound — and each danger has its own safety measures, which are part of using the energy at all.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
