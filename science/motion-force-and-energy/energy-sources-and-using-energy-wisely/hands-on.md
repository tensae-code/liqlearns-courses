# Hands-on — Energy — sources, and using energy wisely

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The source audit
List every energy source your home used this week — firewood, charcoal, electricity, kerosene, the sun for drying, water. Sort each as renewable or non-renewable by the lesson's rule, and mark which column your home depends on most.

## The conservation week
Choose three strategies from the guide's list — covered pots, taps never left running, lights and appliances off when unused, pulses soaked before cooking, shared rides. Run them for one week and log each day's savings you can see: less wood, less water, fewer trips.
