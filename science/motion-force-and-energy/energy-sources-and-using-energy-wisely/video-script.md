# Video script — Energy — sources, and using energy wisely

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Sources** store the energy we use.
**Finite**: fossil fuels, nuclear fuels — they end.
**Endless**: sun, wind, water — they return.
Ten sources the teachers name — and one skill at home:
**lid on, tap off, light off.** Conserve.
