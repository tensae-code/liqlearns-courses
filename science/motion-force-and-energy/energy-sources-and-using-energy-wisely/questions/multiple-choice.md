# Energy — sources, and using energy wisely — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A non-renewable source is one that —
a) returns every day  b) is finite and is used up  c) comes from the sun  d) is always water
**Answer: b) is finite and is used up** — fossil and nuclear fuels are the type

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is renewable?
a) coal  b) sunlight  c) natural gas  d) nuclear fuel
**Answer: b) sunlight** — it returns daily and replaces itself

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The teacher-guide list names how many sources?
a) three  b) eight  c) ten  d) five
**Answer: c) ten** — solar to fossil fuels, ten tiles

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ocean energy (teacher-guide evidence) includes —
a) wave, tidal, current and thermal  b) only waterfalls  c) coal from the sea  d) wind only
**Answer: a) wave, tidal, current and thermal** — the named ocean family

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Soaking pulses before cooking saves energy by —
a) making them heavier  b) shortening the cooking they need  c) changing their colour  d) using more water
**Answer: b) shortening the cooking they need** — less fuel for the same meal
