# Energy — sources, and using energy wisely — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**10. (Sub lesson 1)** Sun, wind and water are ______ sources.
**Answer: renewable** — the endless column

**11. (Sub lesson 2)** Fossil fuels are ______ — they will run out.
**Answer: finite (non-renewable)** — the other column

**12. (Sub lesson 2)** Cook vegetables ______ and with only the water needed.
**Answer: covered** — the lid-on strategy
