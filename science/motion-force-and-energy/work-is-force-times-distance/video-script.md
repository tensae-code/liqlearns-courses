Today: **Work is force times distance** — Saudi Arabia's way, from its book (Saudi Arabia, Grade 3, Part 2 — *Science, Grade 3, Part 2*, printed pp. 122–124).

Ch.
11, Lesson 1 "Work": work is the force exerted to move a body a certain distance, and it is calculated as work = force × distance — the more the force or the distance, the more the work; work is done only when the force and the body's movement are in the same direction, shown in three cases (lifting a box up is work; holding it still is not; carrying it forward is not, because the upward force differs from the forward movement)
