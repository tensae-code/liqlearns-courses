**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Work.
**Answer:** 11, Lesson 1 "Work": work is the force exerted to move a body a certain distance, and it is calculated as work = force × distance — the more the force or the distance, the more the work; work is done only when the force and the body's movement are in the same direction, shown in three cases (lifting a box up is work; holding it still is not; carrying it forward is not, because the upward force differs from the forward movement).
