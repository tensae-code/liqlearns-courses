| Ch. 6, Work done by a force (شغل تبذله قوة): §6-1 work and energy change — at an athletics meeting a sprinter pushes himself forward with a great force as he leaves the starting block: does he do any work on the block? If you spent the whole day lifting books from the floor and arranging them on a shelf you were, in the scientific meaning, doing work, and likewise if you push a bicycle and it moves you are doing work: work is done when a force moves a body in its own direction, and neither people nor machines can do work without a supply of energy (Fig. 6-2); a pulling force dragging a wooden box along a rough floor at constant speed must overcome the friction force opposing the sliding movement, and the pull over the distance moved generates heat energy; a lifting force raising a body increases the body's gravitational potential energy; a body released from a height gains speed, and so kinetic energy, as gravity pulls it down (Figs. 6-3 to 6-6); §6-2 work done by a force: the amount of work done = force × distance moved in the direction of the force (كمية الشغل المبذول = القوة × المسافة المقطوعة في اتجاه القوة), so the SI unit of work is the newton-metre (ن.م), whose common equivalent is the joule (جول): one joule is the work done when a force of one newton moves through one metre in the direction of the applied force; worked example: a traveller at Tripoli International Airport pushes his luggage with a horizontal force of 10 N for 500 m to the luggage-weighing office — work done = 10 × 500 = 5000 N·m = 5000 joules; the work is needed to overcome the friction of the trolley wheels to maintain the movement, and in the end the work done against friction is converted into heat energy in the wheels; §6-3 applying a force without doing work: no work is done if the applied force produces no transfer of energy — lifting a body upwards does work because the lifting force moves vertically and the body's potential energy changes, but a force that does not move the body in its direction does no work (Libya, G8 SB T1) — placement filed as NEW LESSON NEEDED — work-done-by-a-force-and-the-joule;
