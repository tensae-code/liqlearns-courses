# Lift — how a wing's pressure difference holds a glider up — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Lift is…
a) The leftover upward push from a pressure difference over a wing — lower pressure above, higher below  b) The air acting like a shelf under the wing  c) Gravity weakening near wings  d) The wing's lightness
**Answer: a) The leftover upward push from a pressure difference over a wing — lower pressure above, higher below**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A wing's top is shaped…
a) Flat, with a curved bottom  b) Pointed at both ends  c) Curved, with a much flatter bottom  d) The same as its bottom
**Answer: c) Curved, with a much flatter bottom**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) If a wing stops moving through the air, its lift…
a) Doubles  b) Stays the same  c) Turns into drag  d) Vanishes — no flow, no pressure difference
**Answer: d) Vanishes — no flow, no pressure difference**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A paper helicopter falls slowly because…
a) The paper is light  b) Its spinning blades are little wings whose lift holds the fall back  c) The paperclip is heavy  d) It falls in circles by chance
**Answer: b) Its spinning blades are little wings whose lift holds the fall back**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A glider with no engine pays for its forward motion with…
a) Fuel  b) Noise  c) Height — it sinks steadily as it travels  d) Speed only
**Answer: c) Height — it sinks steadily as it travels**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A glider launched from 2 m flies 18 m forward. Its glide ratio G is…
a) 36  b) 9 — it travels nine metres forward per metre sunk  c) 0.11  d) 20
**Answer: b) 9 — it travels nine metres forward per metre sunk**
