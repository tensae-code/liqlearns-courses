# Lift — how a wing's pressure difference holds a glider up — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The upward force made by a pressure difference over a wing is called _____.
**Answer: lift**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The pressure above a wing's curved top is _____ than the pressure below it.
**Answer: lower**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A glider pays for its forward motion with _____, sinking as it travels.
**Answer: height**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Glide ratio G = D / H divides distance forward by height _____.
**Answer: lost**
