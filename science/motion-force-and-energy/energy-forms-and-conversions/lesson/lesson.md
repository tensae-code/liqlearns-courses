# Energy — forms and conversions

## Do it first
Switch on a battery torch. Light pours out. Where was that light an hour ago, when the torch sat dark in a drawer? It was not light then. It was something else, stored — and switching on changed it. That changing is this lesson.

## The eight forms
Energy wears **eight forms**: **electrical, thermal, sound, radiant** (the Sun's kind), **chemical, nuclear, gravitational** and **mechanical**. One energy, many costumes — and the costumes change into each other.

## Conversions — the chains
The Sun's radiant energy becomes **chemical** energy stored as starch in a growing plant. In a **motor**, **electrical** energy becomes **mechanical**. In a **battery** torch, stored chemical energy becomes **light**. And the great chain: a **waterfall's** gravitational energy turns a generator, the generator makes **electrical** energy, and the wire delivers it to a bulb that shines.

## Trace it yourself
A **dropped ball**: gravitational → kinetic as it falls → sound and thermal at the bounce. **Clapped hands**: chemical (muscles) → mechanical → sound, with warmth left in the palms. A **ridden bicycle**: chemical → mechanical, all the way to the wheels. Every chain is read the same way: name the form at each stage, and let each arrow be one honest change.

## Say it to your companion
Name the eight forms, trace the waterfall chain, and trace the dropped ball without help.

## Do It Yourself
1. A wood fire under a pot: trace the chain from the wood to the steaming water.
2. A radio playing: trace from its batteries to the sound in the room, naming every form on the way.
3. Which single form begins the plant's chain, and which form does the plant store?
