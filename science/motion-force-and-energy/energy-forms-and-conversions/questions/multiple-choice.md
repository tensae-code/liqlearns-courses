# Energy — forms and conversions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which list holds only forms of energy?
a) torch, kettle, radio  b) electrical, thermal, sound, chemical  c) wire, bulb, battery  d) plant, sun, river
**Answer: b) electrical, thermal, sound, chemical** — devices are not forms

**2.** In a motor, the conversion is —
a) mechanical → electrical  b) electrical → mechanical  c) chemical → sound only  d) radiant → nuclear
**Answer: b) electrical → mechanical** — the printed motor chain

**3.** The plant stores the Sun's energy as —
a) electrical energy  b) chemical energy in starch  c) sound  d) gravitational energy
**Answer: b) chemical energy in starch** — sun → plant starch

**4.** The waterfall chain ends as —
a) sound only  b) generator electricity, delivered to light  c) chemical energy  d) nuclear energy
**Answer: b) generator electricity, delivered to light** — waterfall → generator → bulb

**5.** Clapped hands end their chain mainly as —
a) light  b) sound and warmth  c) gravitational energy  d) nuclear energy
**Answer: b) sound and warmth** — the clap is heard and the palms warm
