# Energy — forms and conversions — true or false

Say true or false.

**6.** A battery torch turns chemical energy into light.
**Answer: True** — the printed battery chain

**7.** There are eight forms of energy in the book's list.
**Answer: True** — electrical, thermal, sound, radiant, chemical, nuclear, gravitational, mechanical

**8.** In a conversion, the energy itself is destroyed.
**Answer: False** — the form changes; the energy goes on

**9.** A dropped ball's energy becomes sound and warmth at the bounce.
**Answer: True** — the last arrow of its chain
