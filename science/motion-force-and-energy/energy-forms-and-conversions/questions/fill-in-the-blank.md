# Energy — forms and conversions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**10.** The Sun's form of energy is called ______ energy.
**Answer: radiant** — the sunshine form

**11.** A waterfall's stored energy is ______ energy.
**Answer: gravitational** — the height form

**12.** In a chain, each arrow is one change of ______.
**Answer: form** — form changes, energy goes on
