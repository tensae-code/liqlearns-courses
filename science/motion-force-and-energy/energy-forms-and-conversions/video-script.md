The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

**Eight forms** wear one energy: electrical, thermal, **sound**, radiant,
chemical, nuclear, gravitational, mechanical.
Waterfall **falls** → generator **spins** → wire **carries** → bulb **shines**.
The form changes at every arrow. The **energy goes on**.
