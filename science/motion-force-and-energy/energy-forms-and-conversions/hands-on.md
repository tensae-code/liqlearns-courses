# Hands-on — Energy — forms and conversions

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Chain hunt at home
Pick three devices: a torch, a kettle or stove, a radio or phone. For each, write the conversion chain from its store to its effect — chemical → electrical → light; electrical → thermal; electrical → sound — one arrow per change of form.

## Clap and drop
Clap your hands hard: your muscles' chemical energy → motion → sound and warmth. Drop a ball: gravitational → kinetic → sound and warmth at the bounce. Trace both chains aloud with a partner checking each arrow.
