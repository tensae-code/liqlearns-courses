# Lesson plan — Energy — forms and conversions

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Motion, Force and Energy · **Lesson:** Energy — forms and conversions
**Place in the curriculum:** Motion, Force and Energy — Unit 7, the energy strand (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** energy wears eight named forms — electrical, thermal, sound, radiant, chemical, nuclear, gravitational and mechanical — and its story is conversion: form changing into form, as in the waterfall that ends as light in a bulb
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Energy — kinetic and potential energy: two forms are measured; now the full family of forms and their conversions are traced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the eight forms of energy.
2. Trace the printed chains: sun → plant starch; motor: electrical → mechanical; battery → light; waterfall → generator electricity.
3. Trace three fresh conversions unaided: a dropped ball, clapped hands, a ridden bicycle.

## What you need
A torch with batteries, a cup of hot water, and anything at home that plugs, burns or rings.

## If the learner is stuck
- Lists devices instead of forms → a torch is not a form; the light, the battery's chemical store and the warmth are forms. Name what the energy IS at each stage.
- Says energy is 'used up' in a chain → the book's teaching is conversion: form changes, energy goes on. (Its conservation paragraph is flagged in the next lesson's sources.)
