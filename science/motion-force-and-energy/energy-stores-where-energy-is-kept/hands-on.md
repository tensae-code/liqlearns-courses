# Hands-on — Energy stores — where energy is kept

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hunt the stores at home
Walk through your home and list everything that keeps energy for later: a battery, a fuel container, the food cupboard. For each one write its store's name and what the kept energy will be used for. Check your list against the lesson: fuels, foods, batteries and other stores.

## Draw the keeping and the release
Draw one store twice: first closed and keeping its energy, then open and releasing it (a torch switched on, a meal being eaten). Under each drawing write one line — 'kept' under the first, 'released' under the second.
