# Hands-on — Power — work done per unit time

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel power in the stairs
With a partner and a stopwatch: walk up one flight of stairs, then (only if an adult says it is safe) climb the same flight faster, timing both. The work against gravity is the same both times — the same body raised the same height. Say which climb was the more powerful one, and why, using power = work ÷ time. No running on stairs; the faster climb is a brisk walk only.
