# Couple forces — a pair of equal opposite forces — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) If a couple's forces cancel, why does anything happen at all?
**Answer: The forces cancel as pushes on the centre of mass, but they act on different lines, so their turning effects about any point point the same way and add: the body rotates without translating.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Show that a couple's moment does not depend on the pivot: take forces F at distances x and x + d from a point.
**Answer: Moments about the point: F(x + d) − Fx (the forces oppose, their moments about the point reinforce through the offset) = Fd — the x cancels, leaving F × d for every choice of point.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A tap is turned by two fingers applying 8 N each, 0.05 m apart. Find the couple's moment, and say how it changes if the handle were twice as wide.
**Answer: M = F × d = 8 × 0.05 = 0.40 N·m. Doubling the width doubles d, so the same finger force gives 0.80 N·m — wider handles turn more easily.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does the Lao book place couples after equilibrium in its statics part?
**Answer: Because couples complete the equilibrium account: Lessons 16–17 balance forces and single-force moments; a couple is the case where forces already balance yet turning remains, so equilibrium also needs the resultant moment — couples included — to be zero.**
