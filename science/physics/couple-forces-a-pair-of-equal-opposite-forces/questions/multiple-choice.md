# Couple forces — a pair of equal opposite forces — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A couple consists of two forces that are…
**Answer: a) equal, opposite and parallel, on different lines  b) equal and in the same direction  c) unequal and opposite  d) acting at the same point**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The resultant force of a couple is…
**Answer: a) zero — a couple cannot accelerate the centre of mass  b) twice one force  c) equal to one force  d) the product of the forces**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The moment of a couple equals…
**Answer: a) F × d, the force times the perpendicular distance between the lines  b) F ÷ d  c) F × the distance from an chosen pivot only  d) zero, because the forces cancel**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The moment of a couple measured about two different points is…
**Answer: a) the same both times  b) bigger about the centre  c) zero about one of them  d) impossible to measure**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A couple acting on a body can be balanced only by…
**Answer: a) an equal and opposite couple  b) one strong single force  c) the body's weight  d) friction alone, always**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In the Lao Grade 10 book, couple forces are taught…
**Answer: a) as a full lesson (Lesson 18) closing the statics part, after moments and equilibrium  b) in the heat part  c) inside the optics lessons  d) only in the Grade 12 book**
