# Couple forces — a pair of equal opposite forces — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Two equal, opposite, parallel forces form a _____.
**Answer: couple**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The moment of a couple is F × _____, where the missing quantity is the perpendicular distance between the lines of action.
**Answer: d**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A couple's moment has the units newton _____, like any moment.
**Answer: metres**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Full equilibrium needs resultant force zero and resultant _____ zero — couples included.
**Answer: moment**
