# Hands-on — Couple forces — a pair of equal opposite forces

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spin the ruler in place
Lay a ruler on a smooth table and mark its centre. With two fingertips, push one end one way and the other end the opposite way, as equally as you can: the ruler rotates about its middle without travelling — a couple with your hands. Then widen or narrow the gap between your fingers and repeat: a wider gap turns the ruler more easily for the same push, F × d growing with d. Ordinary objects only, exactly the local-materials demonstrations the Lao guides favour.
