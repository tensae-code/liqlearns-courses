# Combining and splitting motion — components move independently — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A motion can be split into _____ motions along two axes.
**Answer: component**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The components move _____ — each by its own law.
**Answer: independently**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Combined speed is the square root of vx² + _____.
**Answer: vy²**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The real path of the river boat is the _____ of the parallelogram.
**Answer: diagonal**
