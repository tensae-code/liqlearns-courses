# Combining and splitting motion — components move independently — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Splitting a motion replaces it with…
**Answer: a) two component motions along chosen axes, run in the same time  b) two halves of the time  c) a slower copy  d) a drawing only**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The split is done exactly using…
**Answer: a) the parallelogram rule for vectors  b) a ruler's edge  c) the tangent rule  d) averaging**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The independence principle says each component…
**Answer: a) answers only its own direction's forces, unaffected by the other  b) stops the other  c) doubles the other  d) shares the other's forces**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A dropped ball and a horizontally thrown ball from the same height…
**Answer: a) land together — identical vertical motions  b) the thrown one lands later  c) the dropped one lands later  d) never land**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Component velocities vx = 3 m/s and vy = 4 m/s combine to a speed of…
**Answer: a) 5 m/s  b) 7 m/s  c) 1 m/s  d) 12 m/s**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) To land exactly opposite, the ferryman must…
**Answer: a) aim upstream so the vector combination points across  b) aim downstream  c) cross more slowly  d) wait for the current to stop**
