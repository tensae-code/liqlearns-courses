# Wien's law and the Stefan–Boltzmann law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Wien's law says λmax × T is…
a) constant  b) zero  c) equal to σ  d) always 500 nm
**Answer: a) constant**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) As a body gets hotter, its peak wavelength…
a) gets shorter (bluer)  b) gets longer (redder)  c) stays fixed  d) leaves the spectrum
**Answer: a) gets shorter (bluer)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Doubling the kelvin temperature multiplies radiated power by…
a) 16  b) 2  c) 4  d) 8
**Answer: a) 16**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The Stefan–Boltzmann law uses temperature in…
a) kelvin  b) degrees Celsius  c) degrees Fahrenheit  d) any scale
**Answer: a) kelvin**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A star peaking at 290 nm has surface temperature about…
a) 10,000 K  b) 1000 K  c) 290 K  d) 5800 K
**Answer: a) 10,000 K**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A real surface, compared with a black body at the same temperature, radiates…
a) less  b) more  c) exactly the same  d) nothing at all
**Answer: a) less**
