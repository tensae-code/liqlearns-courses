# Wien's law and the Stefan–Boltzmann law — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Hotter bodies peak at _____ wavelengths.
**Answer: shorter**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) P/A = σT to the power _____.
**Answer: 4 (four)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) σ is called the _____ constant.
**Answer: Stefan–Boltzmann**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Sunlight peaks near 500 nm, pricing the Sun's surface near _____ K.
**Answer: 5800**
