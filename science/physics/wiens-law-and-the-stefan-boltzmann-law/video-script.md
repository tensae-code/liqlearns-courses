# Video script — Wien's law and the Stefan–Boltzmann law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **two laws read every glowing body**. Wien: the peak wavelength times the temperature is constant — hotter glows bluer, and the colour betrays the temperature. Stefan–Boltzmann: total power goes as temperature to the fourth — double the kelvin, sixteen times the radiation.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
