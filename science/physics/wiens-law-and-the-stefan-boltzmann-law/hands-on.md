# Hands-on — Wien's law and the Stefan–Boltzmann law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Colour is a thermometer
Watch (a teacher demonstrates; do not touch) a filament lamp or an iron object being heated, from off to full glow, in a darkened room. Note the order of colours — no glow, dull red, orange-red, yellow-white — and write beside each stage 'cooler' or 'hotter' with the peak's direction of travel: toward shorter wavelengths as it heats. Then recall a blue welding spark or a blue star picture: blue means hotter still. You have read Wien's law with your eyes.
