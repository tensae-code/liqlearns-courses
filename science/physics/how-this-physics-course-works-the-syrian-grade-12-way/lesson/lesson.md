# How this Physics course works — the Syrian Grade 12 way

## Do it first
Before you read: think of a physics rule you were simply told — a formula handed down with no reason. Now think of one you derived yourself, step by step. Which one could you rebuild if you forgot it? The Syrian Grade 12 book bets everything on the second kind: this lesson teaches its derivation-first shape.

## Sub lesson 1 — The book's shape — five units, university-leaning

The Grade 12 book (scientific track) is physics-only, in five units: **U1 Motion & dynamics, U2 Electricity & magnetism, U3 Standing waves, U4 Electronics & the solid state, and U5 Astronomical physics**. It is a university-leaning syllabus: the level assumes the learner will meet this material again at university and prepares them by showing where every result comes from.

## Sub lesson 2 — Derivation first — activity, conclusion, full mathematics

Each topic opens with an **activity** (نشاط) that puts the phenomenon in front of the learner, feeds an **"I conclude"** (أستنتج) box stating the finding — and then the book carries the **mathematics through in full**. Simple harmonic motion is derived as the projection of uniform circular motion; the elastic pendulum's period is derived from Newton's second law. Nothing central is merely stated: if the book uses a formula, the book has earned it.

## Sub lesson 3 — How to study with this book

The way to use a derivation-first book is to reproduce, not reread: cover the derivation, rebuild it from the activity's finding, and check each step against the book. The worked derivations are the exercises behind the exercises — a learner who can re-derive the period of the elastic pendulum owns it in a way no memorised formula gives. Unit by unit, that habit is the book's real curriculum.

## The key ideas

- The Grade 12 book is physics-only, in five units, and university-leaning — it prepares the learner by showing origins, not just results.
- Every topic runs activity → "I conclude" box → the mathematics carried through in full.
- Central results are derived, not stated: SHM from uniform circular motion, the elastic pendulum's period from Newton's second law.
- Study method for this book: reproduce each derivation from the finding, don't just reread it.
