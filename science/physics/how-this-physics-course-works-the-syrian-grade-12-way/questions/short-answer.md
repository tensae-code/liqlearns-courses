# How this Physics course works — the Syrian Grade 12 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the five units of the Grade 12 book in order.
**Answer: U1 Motion & dynamics; U2 Electricity & magnetism; U3 Standing waves; U4 Electronics & the solid state; U5 Astronomical physics.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What does "derivation-first" mean in this book?
**Answer: No central result is merely stated: an activity produces a finding, the "I conclude" box states it, and then the mathematics is carried through in full so the formula is earned from the phenomenon.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Give one example of a derived result in the book.
**Answer: Simple harmonic motion derived as the projection of uniform circular motion (or: the elastic pendulum's period derived from Newton's second law).**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does reproducing a derivation beat rereading it?
**Answer: Reproducing forces each step to be rebuilt from the finding, so gaps show immediately and the result can be reconstructed even if forgotten; rereading only creates familiarity, which fades.**
