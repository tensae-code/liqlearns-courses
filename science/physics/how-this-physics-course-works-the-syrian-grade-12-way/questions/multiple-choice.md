# How this Physics course works — the Syrian Grade 12 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Grade 12 Syrian book contains…
a) Physics and chemistry mixed  b) Physics only, in five units  c) Only astronomy  d) Only mechanics
**Answer: b) Physics only, in five units**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The book is described as university-leaning because…
a) It is printed at a university  b) It shows where every result comes from, as university study will demand  c) It has no pictures  d) It is the longest book
**Answer: b) It shows where every result comes from, as university study will demand**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The order inside a Grade 12 topic is…
a) Formula, test, activity  b) Activity, "I conclude" box, then the mathematics in full  c) Questions only  d) Story, then formula
**Answer: b) Activity, "I conclude" box, then the mathematics in full**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Simple harmonic motion is introduced in this book by…
a) Stating its equation  b) Deriving it as the projection of uniform circular motion  c) A historical anecdote  d) A table to memorise
**Answer: b) Deriving it as the projection of uniform circular motion**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The study method this book rewards is…
a) Rereading the derivations  b) Reproducing each derivation from the activity's finding  c) Skipping the mathematics  d) Memorising the contents page
**Answer: b) Reproducing each derivation from the activity's finding**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The elastic pendulum's period is…
a) Given without reason  b) Derived from Newton's second law  c) Left as an exercise with no treatment  d) Only in the teacher's guide
**Answer: b) Derived from Newton's second law**
