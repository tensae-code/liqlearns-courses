Today: **how the Syrian Grade 12 physics book works**. This is a derivation-first book. An activity shows you the phenomenon, the "I conclude" box states what it means — and then the book does not stop: it carries the mathematics through in full. Simple harmonic motion is not announced; it is derived, as the projection of uniform circular motion. Five units take you from motion and dynamics to electricity and magnetism, standing waves, electronics and the solid state, and finally astronomical physics. If you can follow a derivation, this book treats you as ready for university.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
