# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Syrian Grade 12 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rebuild one derivation
Take a result you know — for example, that a pendulum's period grows with the square root of its length. Swing a key on strings of two lengths, time ten swings each, and write the activity's finding in an "I conclude" sentence. Then state the formula and point to the exact observation each part of it comes from. Say aloud: activity first, conclusion second, mathematics earned.
