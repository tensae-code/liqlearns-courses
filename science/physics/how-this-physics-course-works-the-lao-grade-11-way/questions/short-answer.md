# How this Physics course works — the Lao Grade 11 (ມ.6) way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the five parts in order.
**Answer: I Structure and Properties of Solids, Liquids and Gases; II Electrostatics; III Direct Current; IV Electric Current in Various Media; V Geometrical Optics.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What does 'evidence first' mean in this course's opening?
**Answer: The book begins from something observable — Brownian motion, with Perrin's measurements — as evidence for molecules, and works Avogadro's number and molar mass from there, instead of asserting the particle picture by definition.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How is Part IV's treatment different from teaching current only in wires?
**Answer: It asks how charge moves in each kind of matter in turn — free electrons in metals, ions in electrolytes, ions and electrons in gases and rarefied air, electrons and holes in semiconductors — so conduction is a property of the medium, not of the wire alone.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) On what footing are this lesson's lesson titles cited?
**Answer: The student book's own TOC skeleton was read; because its legacy font encoding garbles its lesson titles in extraction, the titles are cited from the Grade 11 teacher guide's clean-Lao table of contents, which parallels the student book lesson for lesson.**
