# How this Physics course works — the Lao Grade 11 (ມ.6) way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Lao Grade 11 course has…
**Answer: a) 5 parts and 25 lessons  b) 7 parts and 28 lessons  c) 10 parts and 30 lessons  d) 2 parts and 40 lessons**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The year's five parts run…
**Answer: a) matter → electrostatics → direct current → current in various media → geometrical optics  b) mechanics → heat → sound → light → exams  c) optics first, matter last  d) only electricity, all year**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The opening lesson treats Brownian motion as…
**Answer: a) visible evidence that molecules exist and move  b) a kind of plant growth  c) an error in old microscopes  d) the cause of electric current**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Avogadro's number, as worked in the opening lesson, is about…
**Answer: a) 6.02×10²³  b) 6.02×10¹²  c) 9.8  d) 3×10⁸**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Part IV, Electric Current in Various Media, is distinctive because it…
**Answer: a) teaches conduction medium by medium — metals, electrolytes, gases, air, semiconductors — in six lessons  b) skips metals entirely  c) is one short lesson  d) covers only vacuum tubes**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The first medium taught in Part IV is…
**Answer: a) metals — the electron-sea picture  b) semiconductors  c) gases  d) electrolytes**
