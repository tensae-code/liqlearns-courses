# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Lao Grade 11 (ມ.6) way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch the dance
Stir a drop of milk or a pinch of fine pollen/ash into a shallow dish of water and watch the surface specks under a bright light or a phone magnifier: they tremble and wander without any push you can see. That restless motion — the course's opening evidence — is what moving water molecules do to anything small enough to feel their blows. Note what you see before reading any explanation, the Lao evidence-first way.
