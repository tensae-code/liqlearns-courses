# Lesson plan — Full-wave bridge rectifier

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** Full-wave bridge rectifier
**Place in the curriculum:** Grade 12 · Unit 5 — Basics of Electronics.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** full-wave and bridge rectification; smoothing — Four diodes in a bridge flip the negative half-cycles up, filling the gaps; a capacitor across the output smooths the pulses toward steady DC.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 12 Physics lessons before it in Unit 5 — Basics of Electronics — and on the learner's own world: the examples are the learner's home, street, school and body.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The bridge — both half-cycles used** — Four diodes in a bridge flip the negative half-cycles up, filling the gaps.
2. **Smoothing the output** — A capacitor across the output smooths the pulses toward steady DC.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
