# Torque — the twisting effect of a force — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. A force twists when it acts off-centre: torque grows with the force and with the lever arm (the door-handle idea), and its direction follows the rotation it causes.**
**Answer: True**

**2. A force twists when it acts off-centre: torque shrinks with the force and with the lever arm (the door-handle idea), and its direction follows the rotation it causes.**
**Answer: False**
