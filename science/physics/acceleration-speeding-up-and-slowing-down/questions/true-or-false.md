# Acceleration — speeding up and slowing down — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. Average and instantaneous acceleration as rate of change of velocity; the sign discussion is explicit: negative acceleration does not always mean slowing down, nor positive speeding up — it depends on the direction of motion.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Average and instantaneous acceleration as rate of change of velocity; the sign discussion is explicit: negative acceleration does not always mean slowing down, nor negative speeding up — it depends on the direction of motion.**
**Answer: False**
