# The Franck–Hertz experiment — direct proof of quantised energy levels — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — Below the excitation energy, electron–atom collisions are elastic — atoms refuse smaller amounts of energy; At 4.9 V (for mercury) electrons excite atoms inelastically, lose exactly 4.9 eV, and the collected current drops; The drops repeat at multiples of 4.9 V — one atom excited twice over, or two atoms once each; The excitation energy matches the light the excited atom later emits: E = hf; Franck–Hertz is direct electrical proof of quantised energy levels, confirming Bohr's picture by collision — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
