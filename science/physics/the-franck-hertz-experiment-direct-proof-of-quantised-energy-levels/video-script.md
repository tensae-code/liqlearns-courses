# Video script — The Franck–Hertz experiment — direct proof of quantised energy levels

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **the Franck–Hertz experiment**. Electrons fly through mercury vapour. Every 4.9 volts the current drops — the atoms take exactly 4.9 electron-volts, or nothing. Energy levels are real, and they are steps.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
