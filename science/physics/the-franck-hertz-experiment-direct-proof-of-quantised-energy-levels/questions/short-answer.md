# The Franck–Hertz experiment — direct proof of quantised energy levels — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the drop repeat at 9.8 V?
**Answer: At 9.8 eV an electron can excite two mercury atoms on its way (or excite one, be re-accelerated, and excite another near the grid) and again arrive too weak to pass.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) A mercury atom falls back from its first excited level. What does it emit, and what fixes the amount?
**Answer: A photon (ultraviolet light) carrying exactly the 4.9 eV step — its frequency is fixed by E = hf.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the job of the small retarding voltage on the collecting plate?
**Answer: It sets an energy threshold: only electrons arriving with at least that much energy register as current, so electrons that lost energy in inelastic collisions are filtered out — which is what makes the drops visible.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) How does the experiment support Bohr's model?
**Answer: Bohr's model says atoms have discrete energy levels; Franck–Hertz measures a discrete excitation energy directly, confirming the levels exist independently of the spectral evidence.**
