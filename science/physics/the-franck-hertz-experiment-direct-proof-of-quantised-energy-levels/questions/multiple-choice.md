# The Franck–Hertz experiment — direct proof of quantised energy levels — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the Franck–Hertz tube, the collected current registers electrons that…
**Answer: a) reach the plate still carrying at least the small retarding energy  b) collide elastically only  c) never leave the cathode  d) turn into light**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The vapour in the original tube was…
**Answer: a) mercury  b) hydrogen  c) neon  d) steam**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) For mercury, the current drops at accelerating voltages of…
**Answer: a) 4.9 V and its multiples  b) every 1.0 V  c) 13.6 V only  d) random voltages**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) At the first drop, an electron near the grid…
**Answer: a) collides inelastically, hands exactly 4.9 eV to an atom, and arrives too weak to pass  b) breaks apart  c) reverses direction forever  d) doubles its energy**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Below 4.9 eV, electron–atom collisions are…
**Answer: a) elastic — the atom refuses smaller energy offerings  b) always inelastic  c) impossible  d) light-emitting**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The experiment is called direct proof of quantised levels because…
**Answer: a) a level's energy is measured electrically, by collisions and meters, not only inferred from spectra  b) it photographs the atom  c) it uses no electricity  d) Bohr performed it**
