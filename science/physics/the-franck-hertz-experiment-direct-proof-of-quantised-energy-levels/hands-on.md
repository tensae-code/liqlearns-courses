# Hands-on — The Franck–Hertz experiment — direct proof of quantised energy levels

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Read a drawn curve: sketch the current–voltage curve to 15 V from the description (climb, drop at 4.9, climb, drop at 9.8, climb, drop at 14.7) and annotate each drop with what the electrons did there. (2) Energy bookkeeping: an electron accelerated through 12 V excites mercury atoms as late as possible on its path. How many excitations, and how much energy does it keep? (3) Predict: a different vapour has its first level at 2.1 eV. Sketch its curve to 7 V and mark the drops.
