# Lesson plan — Zeroth law of thermodynamics and temperature equilibrium

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** Zeroth law of thermodynamics and temperature equilibrium
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics — Thermodynamics neighbourhood, beside the heat and temperature lessons (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** when two bodies are left touching, heat flows until they reach the same temperature — thermal equilibrium — and the zeroth law (if each of two bodies is in equilibrium with a third, they are in equilibrium with each other) is what makes temperature measurable and thermometers possible.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own sense of hot and cold — a learner can land here directly. The heat lessons (What heat is; Temperature — how hot, how cold) deepen it, but nothing must come first.

## Sub lessons
1. **Hot, cold and thermal contact** — what temperature is, and what happens when a hot body and a cold body touch: heat flows from the hotter to the colder.
2. **Thermal equilibrium — when the flow stops** — the flow stops when both bodies reach the same temperature; that shared state is thermal equilibrium, and it is the state a thermometer waits for.
3. **The zeroth law and why thermometers work** — if A is in equilibrium with a third body C, and B is also in equilibrium with C, then A and B are in equilibrium with each other; the thermometer is the third body, and this law is why one reading can speak for every body it has matched.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: **What heat is** and **Temperature — how hot, how cold**. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Heat flows between touching bodies from the hotter to the colder, and it stops when both reach the same temperature.
2. That shared, settled state is called thermal equilibrium.
3. State the zeroth law: if two bodies are each in thermal equilibrium with a third body, they are in thermal equilibrium with each other.
4. Explain why a thermometer must be left in place until it settles: it has to reach equilibrium with the body it measures before its reading means that body's temperature.
5. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson text alone.

## If the learner is stuck
- Cannot start → go back to Do it first: a cold spoon in hot tea. Which way does the heat move, and how do you know when it has finished moving?
- Thinks temperature and heat are the same → heat is what flows; temperature is the level it flows between — the flow runs downhill from higher temperature to lower, and stops when the levels match.
- Thinks the law is too obvious to matter → without it, a thermometer matching one body would say nothing about any other body; the law is what lets one small glass tube speak for everything it touches.
