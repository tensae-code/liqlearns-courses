# Zeroth law of thermodynamics and temperature equilibrium — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A hot stone is dropped into cold water. Describe what happens to each, and in which direction the heat flows.
**Answer: Heat flows from the hotter stone to the colder water; the stone cools and the water warms, until both reach the same temperature.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is thermal equilibrium, in one sentence?
**Answer: Thermal equilibrium is the state of two (or more) bodies at the same temperature, with no heat flowing between them.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A thermometer settles in bath water and reads 38 °C. Later it settles against a hot-water bottle and also reads 38 °C. What does the zeroth law let you conclude about the bath water and the bottle?
**Answer: Both are in thermal equilibrium with the thermometer at the same reading, so by the zeroth law they are in thermal equilibrium with each other — they share the same temperature, and no heat would flow between them.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why is this law called the "zeroth" law rather than the first?
**Answer: The first and second laws of thermodynamics had already been named when this one was recognised — but it is more basic than both, sitting underneath them, so it was placed before them as number zero.**
