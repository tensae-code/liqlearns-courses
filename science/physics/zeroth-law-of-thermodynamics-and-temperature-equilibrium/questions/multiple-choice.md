# Zeroth law of thermodynamics and temperature equilibrium — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A cold metal spoon is put into hot tea. Which way does heat flow?
a) From the spoon into the tea  b) From the tea into the spoon  c) Both ways equally at all times  d) Heat does not flow between solids and liquids
**Answer: b) From the tea into the spoon**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Between two touching bodies, heat flows from…
a) The bigger body to the smaller  b) The colder to the hotter  c) The hotter to the colder  d) The solid to the liquid
**Answer: c) The hotter to the colder**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Two bodies are in thermal equilibrium when…
a) They have the same size  b) They reach the same temperature and heat stops flowing between them  c) They are both cold  d) One of them is a thermometer
**Answer: b) They reach the same temperature and heat stops flowing between them**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A cup of hot coffee is left standing in a cool room. What happens?
a) It stays hot forever  b) It cools until it reaches thermal equilibrium with the room  c) It gets hotter  d) Heat flows from the room into the coffee
**Answer: b) It cools until it reaches thermal equilibrium with the room**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The zeroth law of thermodynamics says: if body A is in thermal equilibrium with body C, and body B is also in equilibrium with C, then…
a) A is hotter than B  b) A and B are in thermal equilibrium with each other  c) C must be a liquid  d) A and B can never touch
**Answer: b) A and B are in thermal equilibrium with each other**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Why must a thermometer be left in place before it is read?
a) To warm up the water  b) So it can reach thermal equilibrium with the body it measures  c) Because glass is slow to see through  d) It need not be — the first reading is always right
**Answer: b) So it can reach thermal equilibrium with the body it measures**
