# Zeroth law of thermodynamics and temperature equilibrium — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Heat flows from the hotter body to the _____ body.
**Answer: colder**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) When two bodies reach the same temperature and heat stops flowing, they are in thermal _____.
**Answer: equilibrium**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In the zeroth law, the thermometer plays the part of the _____ body that the other two are each compared with.
**Answer: third**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A thermometer only reads a body's temperature truly after it has _____ into equilibrium with that body.
**Answer: settled**
