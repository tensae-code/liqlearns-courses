Today: **Zeroth law of thermodynamics and temperature equilibrium**.

Put a cold spoon in hot tea. Heat moves — from the tea into the spoon.
It keeps moving until both are the same temperature. Then it stops. That is thermal equilibrium.
Now the law. If the spoon matches the tea, and a thermometer matches the tea — then the spoon matches the thermometer too, even if they never touch.
That is the zeroth law. Small law. Huge job: it is why a thermometer can tell you the temperature of anything it settles with.
