# Video script — Project work in physics — the Armenian project method

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **each Armenian guide carries a chapter on educational project work: pupils take a real question, plan their own route to its answer in steps, do the work over time, and present what they found — with the teacher's functions named at every stage — because some physics is only learned by carrying an investigation of your own (teacher-guide evidence only)**. The guides define the project method by its essence: learning organised around a task the learners carry from question to presented result, over days or weeks, with real choices in their hands. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — what project work is — the essence; the steps — from topic choice to presentation; the teacher's functions — guide, not ghost — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
