# Hands-on — Project work in physics — the Armenian project method

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with your hands
Run the method in miniature: in one session, take the question 'which paper makes the best parachute?' — write the plan (what varies, what is measured, what is kept the same), do three timed drops, and present a two-sentence answer with its evidence. You have walked every guide-step — choice, plan, evidence, presentation — at classroom scale; a term project is this, longer.
