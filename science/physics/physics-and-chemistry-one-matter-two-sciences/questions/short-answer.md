# Physics and chemistry — one matter, two sciences — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. Give one key fact this lesson teaches about physics inside chemistry.**
**Answer: The two sciences meet in matter made of electrons and nuclei: atomic physics explains covalent and ionic bonding and which molecules are energetically favourable; heat/energy physics tells whether a reaction can run and where its equilibrium sits; spectroscopy — reading matter through its interaction with electromagnetic radiation — is named the shared tool that revealed atomic and molecular structure.**
