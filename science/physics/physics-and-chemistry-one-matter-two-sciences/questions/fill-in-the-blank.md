# Physics and chemistry — one matter, two sciences — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. The two sciences meet in matter made of electrons and nuclei: atomic physics explains covalent and ionic bonding and which molecules are energetically favourable; heat/energy physics tells whether a reaction can run and where its equilibrium sits; spectroscopy — reading matter through its interaction with electromagnetic ______ — is named the shared tool that revealed atomic and molecular structure.**
**Answer: radiation**
