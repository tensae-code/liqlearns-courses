# Physics and chemistry — one matter, two sciences — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. The two sciences meet in matter made of electrons and nuclei: atomic physics explains covalent and ionic bonding and which molecules are energetically favourable; heat/energy physics tells whether a reaction can run and where its equilibrium sits; spectroscopy — reading matter through its interaction with electromagnetic radiation — is named the shared tool that revealed atomic and molecular structure.**
**Answer: True**
