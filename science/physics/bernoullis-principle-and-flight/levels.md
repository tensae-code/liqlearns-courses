# Bernoulli's principle and flight — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner states Bernoulli's principle as a trade between speed and pressure, keeps the account in worked numbers with ΔP = ½ρ(v₂² − v₁²), explains wing lift and sail pull from the pressure difference, and explains the hurricane roof-lift with a force estimate, answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — doing the lesson's pressure accounts by hand, naming each idea aloud, and reasoning about new examples until the teaching is the learner's own.
