# Bernoulli's principle and flight — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The principle that faster flow means lower pressure is named after _____.
**Answer: Bernoulli**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In Bernoulli's equation, the term ½ρv² is the fluid's _____ energy per unit volume.
**Answer: kinetic**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The upward push on a wing from the pressure difference is called _____.
**Answer: lift**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) If a wing is tilted too steeply, its flow breaks away and lift collapses; this is called a _____.
**Answer: stall**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In a hurricane the roof is pushed off mainly by the still air _____ the house.
**Answer: inside**
