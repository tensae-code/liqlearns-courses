# Bernoulli's principle and flight — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Bernoulli's principle says that for level flow along a streamline, where a fluid's speed rises, its pressure…
a) Rises too  b) Falls  c) Stays exactly the same  d) Doubles
**Answer: b) Falls**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Two light balls hang side by side and you blow air steadily between them. The balls…
a) Swing apart  b) Stay still  c) Swing together  d) Rise straight up
**Answer: c) Swing together**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The pressure drop between air moving at 20 m/s and still air (density 1.2 kg/m³) is…
a) 24 Pa  b) 240 Pa  c) 480 Pa  d) 2,400 Pa
**Answer: b) 240 Pa**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A wing lifts because…
a) The engine pushes it up  b) Air below the wing is faster  c) Pressure above the wing is lower than pressure below it  d) The wing is lighter than air
**Answer: c) Pressure above the wing is lower than pressure below it**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A sail is best described as…
a) A bag that catches wind like a parachute only  b) A wing stood upright, driven by the pressure difference across it  c) A rudder for the wind  d) A flag that points the way
**Answer: b) A wing stood upright, driven by the pressure difference across it**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In a hurricane, a roof is most in danger because…
a) The wind presses it straight down  b) Fast wind over the roof lowers the pressure above it while the still air inside pushes up  c) Rain makes it heavier  d) The wind gets under the walls only
**Answer: b) Fast wind over the roof lowers the pressure above it while the still air inside pushes up**
