# Video script — Bernoulli's principle and flight

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **fast air pushes less**.

Hold a strip of paper under your lip and blow across the top. The free end rises — up into the fast air, not away from it. Moving air spends its push buying speed: where air races, its pressure drops. A wing is shaped so air hurries over the top — low pressure above, stronger push below, and the plane lifts. A sail is a wing stood upright. And when a hurricane races over a roof, the pressure above the roof falls while the still air inside keeps its full push — so roofs lift *off*. Fast flow, low pressure: that trade runs the sky and the storm alike.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
