## Unit 3 review

The summary restates acceleration, the kinematic equations, graph meanings and circular motion; end questions test whether displacement can exceed distance and whether velocity can change direction under constant acceleration.

Note for the build: despite the unit title, no projectile-motion section exists in this book — two-dimensional motion is circular motion only.
