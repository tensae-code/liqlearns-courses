# Hands-on — Measuring mass and time

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Balance two piles of stones by hand, swapping until your hands judge them equal — your hands are a rough balance. Then time a friend's walk across the yard in seconds.

## Apply it where you live
Weigh one shop item's mass in kilograms and time one daily task in seconds; record both with units.
