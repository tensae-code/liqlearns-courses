# Measuring mass and time — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is mass?
**Answer: The amount of matter in a body.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which instrument measures mass scientifically?
**Answer: A beam balance.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is time?
**Answer: The duration between an event's start and its end.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Is the hour an SI unit?
**Answer: No — the second is the SI unit; hour is a non-SI unit.**
