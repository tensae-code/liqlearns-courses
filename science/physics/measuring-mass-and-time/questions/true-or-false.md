# Measuring mass and time — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mass is the amount of matter in a body; its SI unit is the kilogram.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mass is measured scientifically with a beam balance.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Mass and weight are measured with a sundial.
**Answer: False**
