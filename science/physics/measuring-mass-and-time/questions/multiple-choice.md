# Measuring mass and time — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is mass?
a) The amount of matter in a body.  b) A beam balance.  c) The duration between an event's start and its end.  d) No — the second is the SI unit; hour is a non-SI unit.
**Answer: a) The amount of matter in a body.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which instrument measures mass scientifically?
a) The amount of matter in a body.  b) A beam balance.  c) The duration between an event's start and its end.  d) No — the second is the SI unit; hour is a non-SI unit.
**Answer: b) A beam balance.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is time?
a) The amount of matter in a body.  b) A beam balance.  c) The duration between an event's start and its end.  d) No — the second is the SI unit; hour is a non-SI unit.
**Answer: c) The duration between an event's start and its end.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Is the hour an SI unit?
a) The amount of matter in a body.  b) A beam balance.  c) The duration between an event's start and its end.  d) No — the second is the SI unit; hour is a non-SI unit.
**Answer: d) No — the second is the SI unit; hour is a non-SI unit.**
