# Video script — Measuring mass and time

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Mass is the amount of matter in a body.
Its SI unit is the kilogram.
Scientifically, mass is measured with a beam balance — the body on one side, known masses on the other, until the beam settles level.
Shop scales do the same job in daily trade, but the beam balance is the laboratory standard.
Time is the duration between an event's start and its end.
Its SI unit is the second; minute, hour and day are familiar non-SI units.
The sundial — reading time from a shadow — is the non-standard traditional device: clever, but it needs sunshine and gives only a rough reading.

Remember: **Mass is the amount of matter in a body; its SI unit is the kilogram.**
Remember: **Mass is measured scientifically with a beam balance.**
Remember: **Time's SI unit is the second; the sundial is a non-standard traditional device.**
