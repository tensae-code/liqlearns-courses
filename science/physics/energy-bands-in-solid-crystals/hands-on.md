# Hands-on — Energy bands in solid crystals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fill the staircase
Draw two staircases of 8 steps. On the first, put a dot (an electron) on every step of the bottom staircase and none above a gap of 3 blank steps: label it 'insulator — full band, big gap'. On the second, fill the bottom staircase, leave a gap of 1 step, and put two dots on the staircase above: 'semiconductor, warmed — two electrons promoted, two holes left behind'. Mark the holes as circled empty steps in the lower staircase. Your drawing is a band diagram — the picture engineers actually use.
