# Energy bands in solid crystals — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — In a crystal, atomic energy levels split and merge into continuous energy bands; electrons fill them from the bottom; A full band cannot conduct — no empty states for its electrons to move into; a partly filled band conducts freely; The forbidden gap between valence and conduction bands sorts solids: conductor (no gap/partly filled), semiconductor (small gap, Si ≈ 1.1 eV), insulator (large gap); In a semiconductor, heat or light promotes electrons across the small gap, leaving holes; conductivity rises with temperature — the reverse of a metal — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
