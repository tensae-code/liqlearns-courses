# Energy bands in solid crystals — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a solid, atomic energy levels merge into continuous energy _____.
**Answer: bands**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Between the valence band and the conduction band may lie a _____ gap, which no electron may occupy.
**Answer: forbidden**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) An electron promoted into the conduction band leaves behind a _____ in the valence band, which also carries current.
**Answer: hole**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Diamond, with a gap of about 5.5 eV, is an _____.
**Answer: insulator**
