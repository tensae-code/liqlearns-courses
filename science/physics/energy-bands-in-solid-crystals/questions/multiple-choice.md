# Energy bands in solid crystals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Energy bands form in a crystal because…
**Answer: a) neighbouring atoms perturb each atom's sharp levels, splitting each level into a crowd of close levels  b) electrons melt at room temperature  c) atoms share one single level  d) the crystal is heated**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The valence band is…
**Answer: a) the highest band that is (nearly) full at low temperature  b) the empty band above the gap in all solids  c) the band inside the nucleus  d) a band of valence electrons orbiting freely**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A completely full band cannot carry current because…
**Answer: a) its electrons have no empty states within reach to move into  b) its electrons are too heavy  c) full bands contain no electrons  d) the field cannot enter a solid**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Copper conducts because its conduction band is…
**Answer: a) partly filled, with empty states immediately above the filled ones  b) completely full  c) separated by a 5 eV gap  d) empty**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Silicon's band gap is about…
**Answer: a) 1.1 eV — small, the semiconductor case  b) 5.5 eV  c) 0 eV exactly, like a metal  d) 100 eV**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) When a semiconductor warms up, its conductivity…
**Answer: a) rises — more electrons are promoted across the small gap, leaving holes  b) falls to zero  c) never changes  d) becomes superconductivity**
