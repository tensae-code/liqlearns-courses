Today: **energy bands — the solid's energy landscape**. Crowd atoms into a crystal and their sharp levels smear into bands. A full band cannot conduct — its electrons have nowhere to move; a partly filled band conducts like a metal. The forbidden gap does the sorting: none for conductors, about an electronvolt for semiconductors like silicon, several for insulators. All of electronics is built in that small-gap middle kingdom.
