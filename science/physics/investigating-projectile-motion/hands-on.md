# Hands-on — Investigating projectile motion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Photograph a flat-throw with a phone
Set a ramp with a level lip at a table's edge, with a metre grid (chalk lines every 10 cm) on the wall or floor behind the flight and a second ball on a drop-release at the lip. (1) Film at the fastest frame rate your phone allows, launching the ball and dropping the control in the same frame (a partner's hand or a thread-release helps). (2) Step through the recording frame by frame; tabulate the projectile's horizontal position and height at each frame, and the control ball's height beside it. (3) Compute the horizontal steps between frames and the vertical falls from the launch height; test them against the two signatures — equal steps, and falls in 1 : 4 : 9. (4) From one frame pair, measure the launch speed. Write one paragraph: what the record shows, before any law is quoted — in the same order PEP's Compulsory Book 2 does it (§5.3 before §5.4).
