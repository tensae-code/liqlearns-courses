# Investigating projectile motion — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The launch leaves a level lip with a purely _____ velocity.
**Answer: horizontal**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Equal horizontal steps mean the horizontal motion is _____.
**Answer: uniform**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Total falls stand in the ratio 1 : _____ : 9.
**Answer: 4**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The control ball dropped beside the flight _____ together with the projectile.
**Answer: lands**
