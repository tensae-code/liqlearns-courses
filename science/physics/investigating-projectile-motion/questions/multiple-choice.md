# Investigating projectile motion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The launch in this investigation must start…
**Answer: a) purely horizontal, from a level lip  b) at 45 degrees  c) straight down  d) at any angle at all**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The flight is captured by…
**Answer: a) a flash photograph or camera frames at equal time intervals  b) memory  c) a stopwatch alone  d) guessing**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Horizontally, the record shows…
**Answer: a) equal steps in equal times — uniform motion  b) steps growing 1:3:5  c) steps shrinking  d) no motion**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The total vertical falls after 1, 2, 3 beats stand in the ratio…
**Answer: a) 1 : 4 : 9  b) 1 : 2 : 3  c) 1 : 1 : 1  d) 3 : 2 : 1**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The dropped control ball proves…
**Answer: a) the vertical motion is independent free fall — the two land together  b) gravity is weaker for moving balls  c) launches are faster  d) photographs lie**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The projectile laws of the next section are…
**Answer: a) this measured record written in symbols — x = v₀t, y = ½gt²  b) older than the experiment  c) unrelated to it  d) only for angled throws**
