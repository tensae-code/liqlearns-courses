# Investigating projectile motion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A projectile's photo (0.05 s beats) shows horizontal steps of 9 cm. Find v₀ and the expected total fall after 4 beats (g = 10 m/s²).
**Answer: v₀ = 0.09 ÷ 0.05 = 1.8 m/s. After 4 beats t = 0.2 s: y = ½ × 10 × 0.04 = 0.20 m = 20 cm (and the falls ratio check: after 1 beat 1.25 cm, ratio 1:16 over 4 beats = 1:4²).**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why must the ball be dense and the speeds modest in this investigation?
**Answer: So air resistance stays negligible — a light, fast ball would be slowed by the air and the horizontal steps would shrink, faking a force that the analysis does not include.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A team's vertical falls read 1 : 4 : 8 instead of 1 : 4 : 9. Name two checks before blaming physics.
**Answer: Frame timing (is the interval truly equal?), scale calibration of the photograph/grid, and whether the launch was truly horizontal — a downward-tilted lip adds initial vertical speed and distorts the ratio. Any two.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) State the two signatures of the flat-throw record in one line each.
**Answer: Horizontal: equal distances in equal times — uniform. Vertical: falls from rest in the ratio 1 : 4 : 9 for equal total times — uniformly accelerated at g.**
