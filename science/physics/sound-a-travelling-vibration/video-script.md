# Video script — Sound — a travelling vibration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Sound is produced by vibrating sources — a kirar string, a voice, a drum skin.
It travels as a longitudinal wave and it needs a medium: in a vacuum there is nothing to compress, so there is no sound.
Its speed differs by material — faster in solids and liquids than in air — and changes with the air's temperature.
Estimating echoes and timing thunder are everyday ways of measuring the speed of sound in air.

Remember: **Sound is made by vibrating sources and travels as a longitudinal wave.**
Remember: **Sound needs a medium — no sound in a vacuum.**
Remember: **Sound travels faster in solids and liquids than in air.**
