# Hands-on — Sound — a travelling vibration

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Press your ear to a table while a partner taps the far end softly. The tap arrives clearer through the wood than through the air — sound loves a medium.

## Apply it where you live
Estimate the speed of sound with an echo or a distant visible-and-audible event, showing your method.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21859 — vibrating kirar string with compressions and rarefactions spreading from it] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
