# Sound — a travelling vibration — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Why is there no sound in a vacuum?
**Answer: There is no medium to carry the compressions.**

**2.** Name the instrument the book uses as a vibrating source.
**Answer: The kirar.**

**3.** In which is sound fastest — air, water, or steel?
**Answer: Steel (solids fastest, then liquids, then gases).**

**4.** A sound travels 1020 m in air in 3 s. Speed?
**Answer: 1020 ÷ 3 = 340 m/s.**
