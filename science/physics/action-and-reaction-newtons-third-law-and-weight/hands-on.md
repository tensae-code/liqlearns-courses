# Hands-on — Action and reaction — Newton's third law; weight

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel inertia
Put a book on a sheet of paper and pull the paper out fast. The book stays. Then pull slowly — the book rides. Name the law that explains both.

## See it and say it
Study this lesson's picture — a book on a table with the paired force arrows drawn on both bodies — and name every part of it out loud. Then close the picture and draw it from memory; check your drawing against the lesson.

## Teach it back
Explain this lesson to someone at home using one thing in the room as your example. Their questions show you which part to re-read.
