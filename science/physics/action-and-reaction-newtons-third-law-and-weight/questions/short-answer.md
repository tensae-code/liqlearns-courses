# Action and reaction — Newton's third law; weight — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Give one key fact this lesson teaches about newton's third law and weight.**
**Answer: Action–reaction pairs act on different bodies.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Write down, in symbols, one rule or formula from this lesson, and say what it joins.**
**Answer: Weight is gravity's force on a mass (W = mg) and is distinguished from mass itself**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Give one key fact this lesson teaches about newton's third law and weight.**
**Answer: With normal force and tension introduced through supported and hanging bodies.**
