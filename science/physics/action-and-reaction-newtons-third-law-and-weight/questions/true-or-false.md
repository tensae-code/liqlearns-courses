# Action and reaction — Newton's third law; weight — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Action–reaction pairs act on different bodies.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Weight is gravity's force on a mass (W = mg) and is distinguished from mass itself.**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) With normal force and tension introduced through supported and hanging bodies.**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) For Newton's third law the guide works a group exercise explaining the pairs in: a man walking (foot pushes floor back, floor pushes him reverse); a car accelerating (the ground pushes the drive wheels forward against their backward push); a rocket (gas expelled backward at high speed); a helicopter (air pushed down, lift as the upward reaction); and an octopus or jet ski (water ejected through a funnel drives the body forward).**
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) For Newton's third law the guide works a group exercise explaining the pairs in: a man walking (foot pushes floor back, floor pushes him forward); a car accelerating (the ground pushes the drive wheels forward against their backward push); a rocket (gas expelled backward at high speed); a helicopter (air pushed down, lift as the upward reaction); and an octopus or jet ski (water ejected through a funnel drives the body forward).**
**Answer: True**
