# Action and reaction — Newton's third law; weight — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Weight is gravity's ______ on a mass (W = mg) and is distinguished from mass itself.**
**Answer: force**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) For Newton's third law the guide ______s a group exercise explaining the pairs in: a man walking (foot pushes floor back, floor pushes him forward); a car accelerating (the ground pushes the drive wheels forward against their backward push); a rocket (gas expelled backward at high speed); a helicopter (air pushed down, lift as the upward reaction); and an octopus or jet ski (water ejected through a funnel drives the body forward).**
**Answer: work**
