# Video script — Kirchhoff's rules — junctions and loops

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Junction rule: currents into a junction equal currents out (charge conservation).

Loop rule: voltages around any closed loop sum to zero (energy conservation).

A five-step routine (label branches, apply junction rule, apply loop rule, solve) is worked on multi-loop circuits.
