Today: **charging without touching**. In paper and other insulators, a near charge only stretches each atom — polarisation — enough to attract, never enough to keep. In a metal, the free electrons run to the far side. Earth that far side while the rod is still near, let the electrons escape, un-earth, then take the rod away — and the metal keeps the opposite charge, for free, while the rod loses nothing.
