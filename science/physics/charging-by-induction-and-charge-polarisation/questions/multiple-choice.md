# Charging by induction and charge polarisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Polarisation happens in an insulator because…
a) Electrons travel through the whole object  b) Each atom's electron cloud is stretched slightly by the nearby charge  c) The atoms gain extra electrons  d) The object is earthed
**Answer: b) Each atom's electron cloud is stretched slightly by the nearby charge**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) When the charged rod is taken away from a polarised insulator, the insulator…
a) Keeps a lasting charge  b) Returns to neutral — its charge clouds spring back  c) Becomes a conductor  d) Loses atoms
**Answer: b) Returns to neutral — its charge clouds spring back**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A negative rod near a neutral metal sphere pushes the sphere's free electrons…
a) To the near side  b) To the far side, leaving the near side positive  c) Out of the sphere into the air  d) Into the stand
**Answer: b) To the far side, leaving the near side positive**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) At the separation stage (rod near, nothing else done) the sphere is…
a) Positively charged overall  b) Negatively charged overall  c) Still neutral overall — charge has only separated  d) Fully discharged
**Answer: c) Still neutral overall — charge has only separated**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In charging by induction, the earthing connection must be removed…
a) After the rod is removed  b) Before the rod is removed, while it is still held near  c) Before the rod is brought near  d) It makes no difference
**Answer: b) Before the rod is removed, while it is still held near**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The lasting charge left by induction is…
a) The same sign as the rod's  b) Opposite in sign to the rod's  c) Always positive  d) Always neutral
**Answer: b) Opposite in sign to the rod's**
