# Charging by induction and charge polarisation — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A stretched atom with a positive near side and negative far side is a tiny _____.
**Answer: dipole**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The electrons that move freely through a conductor are called _____ electrons.
**Answer: free**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Charging a conductor using a nearby charge that never touches it is charging by _____.
**Answer: induction**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) During induction the repelled electrons escape to _____ through the earth connection.
**Answer: earth (the ground)**
