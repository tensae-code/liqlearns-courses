# Hands-on — Charging by induction and charge polarisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Charge a can without touching it
You need a clean empty drinks can on a plastic (insulating) surface, a plastic ruler or balloon, and a cloth. Rub the ruler to charge it. Roll the can toward the ruler without touching: bring the ruler near and watch the can roll as its charge separates (induction attracting it). Then, with a partner, try the full method on a metal object on an insulating stand: rod near, touch the far side briefly, remove your hand, then remove the rod — and test the result with tissue pieces. Record the order of steps; swap two steps and record how the result changes.
