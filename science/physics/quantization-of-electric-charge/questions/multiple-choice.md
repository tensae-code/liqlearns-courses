# Quantization of electric charge — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The elementary charge e is about…
**Answer: a) 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C  b) 1.6 × 10⁻⁹ C  c) 1 C  d) 9.1 × 10⁻³¹ C**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The quantisation law is…
**Answer: a) Q = ne with n a whole number  b) Q = e/n  c) Q = n²e  d) Q = eⁿ**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A body with charge −4.8 × 10⁻¹⁹ C holds…
**Answer: a) exactly 3 extra electrons  b) 4.8 electrons  c) 30 electrons  d) half an electron**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The quantisation unit was established by…
**Answer: a) Millikan's oil-drop experiment  b) Ohm's law  c) Coulomb's torsion balance  d) Faraday's cage**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Charge conservation and quantisation fit together because charging moves…
**Answer: a) whole electrons, each carrying −e  b) fractions of electrons  c) protons out of nuclei  d) charge created by friction**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) One coulomb is about…
**Answer: a) 6.25 × 10¹⁸ elementary charges  b) 6.25 charges  c) 1,000 charges  d) one electron**
