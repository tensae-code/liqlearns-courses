# Quantization of electric charge — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Charge is dealt in whole units of the _____ charge e.
**Answer: elementary**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Q equals _____ times e, with n a whole number.
**Answer: n**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The electron carries charge _____ e.
**Answer: minus**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) One coulomb is about 6.25 × 10¹⁸ _____ charges.
**Answer: elementary**
