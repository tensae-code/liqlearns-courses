# Quantization of electric charge — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — Every free charge is a whole multiple of the elementary charge e; Q = ne, with n a whole number; fractional charges do not occur on free bodies; e ≈ 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C; the electron carries −e and the proton +e; Millikan's oil drops established the unit experimentally; One coulomb is about 6.25 × 10¹⁸ elementary charges — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
