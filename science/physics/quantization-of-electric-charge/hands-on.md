# Hands-on — Quantization of electric charge

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Charge a balloon by rubbing and estimate: if it holds −1.6 × 10⁻¹³ C, how many electrons moved? (2) Sort a list of charges (−3.2, −2.4, +4.8, +1.6, −0.8 × 10⁻¹⁹ C) into possible and impossible, and defend the sorting. (3) Count the coins: work out how many electrons pass a point when 2 C flows in a circuit.
