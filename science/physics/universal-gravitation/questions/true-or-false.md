# Universal gravitation — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. Every mass attracts every other with F = Gm₁m₂/r²; a worked example weighs a 60 kg person by the formula (≈584 N, checked against mg ≈ 588 N) and distance-doubling questions drill the inverse square.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Every mass repels every other with F = Gm₁m₂/r²; a worked example weighs a 60 kg person by the formula (≈584 N, checked against mg ≈ 588 N) and distance-doubling questions drill the inverse square.**
**Answer: False**
