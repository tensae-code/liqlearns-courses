# Bohr's model of the hydrogen atom and energy levels — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — State the two failures of the classical Rutherford atom; Write Bohr's two postulates and the level formula E_n = −13.6/n² eV, and use both in jump calculations; Place the Lyman, Balmer and Paschen series by the level landed on; Name the model's limits honestly — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
