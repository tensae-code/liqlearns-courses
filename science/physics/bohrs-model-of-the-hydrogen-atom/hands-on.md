# Hands-on — Bohr's model of the hydrogen atom and energy levels

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the ladder
On paper draw a vertical energy axis and mark the rungs n = 1 (−13.6 eV), n = 2 (−3.4 eV), n = 3 (−1.5 eV), n = 4 (−0.85 eV) and 0 at the top. Draw arrows for falls to n = 2 and label the n = 3 → 2 arrow with 1.89 eV. Say aloud: the rungs crowd toward freedom — that crowding is why each spectral series crowds toward a limit.

## Barcode reading
Look at any printed picture of hydrogen's emission spectrum (in the lesson's described picture slot: four bright lines on black — red, blue-green, blue-violet, violet). Say aloud for the red line: which jump made it, and why there is black — not colour — everywhere else: no jump, no photon of that energy.
