# Video script — Bohr's model of the hydrogen atom and energy levels

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the atom as a ladder**. Rutherford's electron should have spiralled into the nucleus, radiating a smear. Instead hydrogen shines in exact lines — a barcode. Bohr's repair: the electron may only stand on certain rungs, **allowed orbits** with energies −13.6 divided by n² electron-volts — and on a rung it does not radiate at all. It shines only when it **jumps**: falling, it throws off one photon carrying exactly the energy difference; climbing, it swallows one. Every bright line is a named fall between named rungs. One integer — n — quantised the atom, and the barcode came out right.
