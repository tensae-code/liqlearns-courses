# Bohr's model of the hydrogen atom and energy levels — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Classically, Rutherford's orbiting electron should…
a) Radiate continuously and spiral into the nucleus — atoms would collapse  b) Radiate nothing  c) Orbit forever unchanged  d) Emit only spectral lines
**Answer: a) Radiate continuously and spiral into the nucleus — atoms would collapse**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Hydrogen's emission spectrum consists of…
a) Sharp lines at exact frequencies, grouped in series  b) A continuous rainbow  c) One white line  d) Infrared light only
**Answer: b) A continuous rainbow**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In a Bohr allowed orbit, the electron…
a) Radiates in bursts  b) Does not radiate — it is a stationary state  c) Radiates slowly  d) Falls gradually
**Answer: c) Radiates slowly**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Hydrogen's ground-state energy is…
a) −3.4 eV  b) −13.6 eV  c) +13.6 eV  d) 0 eV
**Answer: d) 0 eV**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The Balmer series lines are photons from electrons falling to…
a) n = 1  b) n = 2  c) n = 3  d) n = ∞
**Answer: a) n = 1**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Hydrogen's ionisation energy — the least energy that frees its electron from the ground state — is…
a) 10.2 eV  b) 1.89 eV  c) 13.6 eV  d) 0 eV
**Answer: b) 1.89 eV**
