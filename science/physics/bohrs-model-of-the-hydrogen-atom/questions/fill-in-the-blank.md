# Bohr's model of the hydrogen atom and energy levels — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Hydrogen's sharp emission lines are grouped into _____ such as Balmer's.
**Answer: series**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) An orbit in which the electron radiates nothing is called a _____ state.
**Answer: stationary**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The ground state of hydrogen is the level n = _____ .
**Answer: 1**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Dark lines in a star's spectrum are made by atoms _____ photons of exactly jump energies.
**Answer: absorbing**
