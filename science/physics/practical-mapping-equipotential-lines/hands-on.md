# Hands-on — Practical — mapping equipotential lines

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Do the practical: two pin electrodes on conductive paper, map the 2 V, 4 V and 6 V lines, then draw the field lines across them. (2) Repeat with a flat plate electrode facing a pin and compare the map's shape. (3) Use your map to predict the field direction at three named points, then check with the theory before measuring anything else.
