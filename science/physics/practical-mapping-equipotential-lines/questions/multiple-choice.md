# Practical — mapping equipotential lines — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The probe in this practical finds points of equal…
**Answer: a) potential  b) current  c) charge  d) resistance**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The sheet must be…
**Answer: a) conductive paper with electrodes fed by a supply  b) plain paper  c) insulating plastic  d) metal foil folded**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Equipotential lines never cross because…
**Answer: a) a point cannot have two different potentials  b) the paper is small  c) the probe is blunt  d) the supply is steady**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Field lines are drawn…
**Answer: a) at right angles to the equipotentials, from high to low potential  b) along the equipotentials  c) randomly  d) only near electrodes**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Work done moving a charge along an equipotential is…
**Answer: a) zero  b) maximum  c) qV  d) negative always**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Crowded equipotential lines indicate…
**Answer: a) a strong field  b) a weak field  c) no field  d) a broken probe**
