# Practical — mapping equipotential lines — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Points at one potential join into an _____ line.
**Answer: equipotential**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Equipotential lines never _____.
**Answer: cross**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Field lines meet equipotentials at _____ angles.
**Answer: right**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Moving along an equipotential, the work done is _____.
**Answer: zero**
