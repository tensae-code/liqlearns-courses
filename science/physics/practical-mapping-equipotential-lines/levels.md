# Practical — mapping equipotential lines — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — A probe and voltmeter hunt out the points at one chosen potential on conductive paper; Joining the marks draws the equipotential line; repeat for a family of lines; Equipotential lines never cross — one point cannot hold two potentials; Field lines cross equipotentials at right angles, from high to low potential; Crowded equipotentials mean a strong field; no work is done moving along an equipotential — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
