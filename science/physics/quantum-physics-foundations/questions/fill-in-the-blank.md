# Quantum physics foundations — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An ideal object that absorbs every ray falling on it and glows with a spectrum set only by its temperature is called a _____ body.
**Answer: black**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In E = hf, the constant h is named after _____.
**Answer: Planck**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) One packet of light energy is called a _____.
**Answer: photon**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The idea that quantum things show wave behaviour in some experiments and particle behaviour in others is called wave–particle _____.
**Answer: duality**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The principle that position and momentum cannot both be exact at once is the _____ principle.
**Answer: uncertainty**
