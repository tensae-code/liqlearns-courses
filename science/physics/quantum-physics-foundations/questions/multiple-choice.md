# Quantum physics foundations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The "ultraviolet catastrophe" was…
a) A solar flare  b) Classical physics predicting a heated body should emit unlimited ultraviolet energy, against all measurement  c) A broken telescope  d) The colour of hot iron
**Answer: b) Classical physics predicting a heated body should emit unlimited ultraviolet energy, against all measurement**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Planck's quantisation says the energy of one quantum of light of frequency f is…
a) E = mc²  b) E = hf  c) E = f/h  d) E = h/f²
**Answer: b) E = hf**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Bright red light frees no electrons from a metal, however bright, because…
a) Red light is blocked by air  b) Each red photon carries less than the work function, and electrons take photons one at a time  c) The metal is too cold  d) Red photons are waves, not particles
**Answer: b) Each red photon carries less than the work function, and electrons take photons one at a time**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Compton scattering proved that photons carry…
a) Charge  b) Mass at rest  c) Momentum  d) Temperature
**Answer: c) Momentum**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) De Broglie's wavelength for a particle of momentum p is…
a) λ = hp  b) λ = p/h  c) λ = h/p  d) λ = hf
**Answer: c) λ = h/p**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The uncertainty principle limits…
a) How carefully instruments can be built  b) How sharply position and momentum can both be known at once — by nature, not by instruments  c) The speed of electrons only  d) The size of laboratories
**Answer: b) How sharply position and momentum can both be known at once — by nature, not by instruments**
