# Hands-on — Quantum physics foundations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The glowing-coil colours
With a teacher, watch a heating element or a dimmer-controlled filament lamp rise in brightness: dull red, orange, yellow-white. Note the colour at each stage and the order it comes in — never blue first. Say aloud: the hump of the glow moves toward shorter wavelengths as temperature rises, and it is the packet size E = hf that stops the ultraviolet running away.

## Packets on the table
Spread coins as quanta: small coins are low-frequency packets, big coins are high-frequency packets, and price an "electron escape" at one big coin. Tip a whole jar of small coins onto the table — no electron escapes, however many coins land. Drop one big coin — one electron escapes. Now roll a coin into a marble so both recoil: that collision is Compton's photon, carrying momentum as well as energy. Say aloud: one packet, one electron, one account.
