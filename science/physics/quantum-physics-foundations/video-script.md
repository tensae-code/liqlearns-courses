Today: **energy comes in packets**.

Heat an iron bar and it glows red, then orange, then white. The old physics said it should blaze with endless ultraviolet — it does not. Planck's fix: energy is handed over in packets, quanta, each of size E = hf. Einstein showed the packets are real particles of light — photons — one photon knocking one electron out of a metal. Compton watched photons bounce off electrons like billiard balls. And yet light still makes wave patterns, and electrons do too: that is duality. Push nature for an exact position and an exact momentum at once and it refuses — that refusal, uncertainty, is what holds every atom up.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
