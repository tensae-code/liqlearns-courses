# Specific heat capacity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about heat capacity and specific heat capacity is correct?**
a) The teacher is told to support the class to conduct it properly and prepare the materials ahead of the lesson.  b) Bodies differ in how much heat raises their temperature: heat capacity per body, specific heat capacity per kilogram…  c) A pushed puck is compared on a table, on ice, and on an air-hockey table: the opposing (friction) force is greatest on…
**Answer: b) Bodies differ in how much heat raises their temperature: heat capacity per body, specific heat capacity per…**

**2. Bodies differ in how much heat raises their temperature: heat capacity per body, ______ capacity per kilogram per degree (Q = mcΔT), with water's unusually high value noted through examples.**
a) activity  b) specific heat  c) galvanometer
**Answer: b) specific heat**
