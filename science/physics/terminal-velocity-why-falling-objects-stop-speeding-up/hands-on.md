# Hands-on — Terminal velocity — why falling objects stop speeding up

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Same weight, two falls
Drop a flat sheet of paper and the same sheet crumpled tight, side by side, three times. Time each fall roughly (count aloud) and record which lands first every time. Then open the sheet flat again and hold it over the ball as you drop both — a paper “parachute”. Write two sentences: what stayed the same (weight) and what changed (area, drag, terminal velocity).
