# Terminal velocity — why falling objects stop speeding up — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Drag on a falling object grows when…
**Answer: a) its speed grows  b) its weight grows  c) time passes  d) it gets colder**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) At the very first instant of a fall, drag is…
**Answer: a) zero — the object is not yet moving  b) equal to weight  c) greater than weight  d) infinite**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Terminal velocity is reached when…
**Answer: a) drag exactly balances weight, so the resultant force is zero  b) weight runs out  c) drag disappears  d) the object hits the ground**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Crumpling a sheet of paper lowers its fall time because it…
**Answer: a) reduces the area facing the air, so drag balances weight at a higher speed  b) makes it heavier  c) removes gravity  d) adds weight**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Opening a parachute slows a skydiver because the canopy…
**Answer: a) hugely increases drag, which now beats weight until a lower terminal velocity is reached  b) reduces weight  c) turns gravity off  d) pushes the diver up forever**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A car’s top speed is a balance of the same kind: engine force finally equals…
**Answer: a) drag plus friction  b) weight  c) the brakes  d) gravity**
