# Terminal velocity — why falling objects stop speeding up — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The upward force resisting a falling object’s motion is called _____.
**Answer: drag (air resistance)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) When drag equals weight, the _____ force on the falling object is zero.
**Answer: resultant**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The steady speed of fall at that balance is the _____ velocity.
**Answer: terminal**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A parachute lowers terminal velocity by greatly increasing the _____ facing the air.
**Answer: area (canopy area)**
