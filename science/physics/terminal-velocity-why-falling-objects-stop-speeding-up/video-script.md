# Video script — Terminal velocity — why falling objects stop speeding up

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **why falling things stop speeding up**. Weight pulls down, steady. Air pushes back — and the push grows with speed. When the push finally equals the pull, the fall settles at one steady speed: terminal velocity. A parachute just makes that speed small.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
