# Magnets — poles and properties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** You cut a bar magnet in half. How many poles result?
a) Four — each half has its own N and S pair.  b) They repel.  c) Lodestone.  d) By rough treatment (dropping/hammering), heating, or time.
**Answer: a) Four — each half has its own N and S pair.**

**2.** Two N poles approach. What happens?
a) Four — each half has its own N and S pair.  b) They repel.  c) Lodestone.  d) By rough treatment (dropping/hammering), heating, or time.
**Answer: b) They repel.**

**3.** Name a natural magnet.
a) Four — each half has its own N and S pair.  b) They repel.  c) Lodestone.  d) By rough treatment (dropping/hammering), heating, or time.
**Answer: c) Lodestone.**

**4.** How can a magnet lose its power?
a) Four — each half has its own N and S pair.  b) They repel.  c) Lodestone.  d) By rough treatment (dropping/hammering), heating, or time.
**Answer: d) By rough treatment (dropping/hammering), heating, or time.**
