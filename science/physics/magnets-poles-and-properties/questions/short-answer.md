# Magnets — poles and properties — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** You cut a bar magnet in half. How many poles result?
**Answer: Four — each half has its own N and S pair.**

**2.** Two N poles approach. What happens?
**Answer: They repel.**

**3.** Name a natural magnet.
**Answer: Lodestone.**

**4.** How can a magnet lose its power?
**Answer: By rough treatment (dropping/hammering), heating, or time.**
