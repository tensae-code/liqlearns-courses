# Magnets — poles and properties — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1.** Magnets attract iron and nickel materials; strength crowds at the poles.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Like poles repel; unlike poles attract.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Cutting a magnet in half separates its N pole from its S pole into two single-pole pieces.
**Answer: False**
