# Hands-on — Magnets — poles and properties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
If you have any magnet, hunt what it attracts around the house and sort the attracted from the ignored. No magnet? Two paper clips and a borrowed fridge magnet will teach the same list.

## Apply it where you live
Write the magnet properties as a buyer's test: how to check a magnet in a market stall.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21911 — bar magnet being sawn in half, each half growing its own N and S] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
