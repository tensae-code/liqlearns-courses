# Video script — Magnets — poles and properties

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Magnets began as lodestone, the natural magnetic stone known since ancient Greece.
Today's magnets come shaped — bar, horseshoe, needle — and share strict properties: they attract iron and nickel materials; their strength crowds at two poles; like poles repel and unlike poles attract; and poles always come in pairs — saw a magnet in half and each half grows its own N and S.
Permanent magnets keep their power; temporary magnets hold it only while influenced; and rough treatment, heat and time can rob a magnet of its strength.

Remember: **Magnets attract iron and nickel materials; strength crowds at the poles.**
Remember: **Like poles repel; unlike poles attract.**
Remember: **Poles always pair — cutting a magnet makes two complete magnets.**
