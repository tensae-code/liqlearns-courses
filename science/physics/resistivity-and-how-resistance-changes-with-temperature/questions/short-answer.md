# Resistivity and how resistance changes with temperature — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Distinguish resistance from resistivity in one line each.
**Answer:** Resistance belongs to a specimen (size and shape included); resistivity belongs to the material at a temperature.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is nichrome chosen for heating elements?
**Answer:** Its resistivity is far higher than copper's, so a practical length gives a large resistance and strong heating.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What two effects compete in a heated semiconductor?
**Answer:** More collisions (raises ρ) against more freed carriers (lowers ρ) — the carriers win.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A copper ρ rises 0.4% per °C. By what fraction does it rise over 50 °C?
**Answer:** About 20% (αΔT = 0.004 × 50 = 0.20).
