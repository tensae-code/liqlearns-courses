# Hands-on — Resistivity and how resistance changes with temperature

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Measure a coil of copper wire's resistance cold, then in warm water (sealed coil, thermometer in the bath)
Measure a coil of copper wire's resistance cold, then in warm water (sealed coil, thermometer in the bath): plot R against T and estimate α. Repeat with a thermistor and compare the two curves' directions and steepness — one lesson, two opposite graphs, from your own bench.
