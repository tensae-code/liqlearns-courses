# Video script — Resistivity and how resistance changes with temperature

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **resistance belongs to a specimen, resistivity to its material**. Two wires of the same metal differ in resistance if their lengths or areas differ; the quantity that does not differ is the resistivity ρ, defined by R = ρL/A and measured in ohm-metres. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — resistivity — the material's own number; metals warm up — resistance rises; semiconductors cool down — resistance falls — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.
