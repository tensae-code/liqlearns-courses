# Video script — Models of the atom — Dalton, Thomson, Rutherford, Bohr

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **four atoms in a hundred years**. Dalton's solid ball. Thomson finds the electron inside it and serves up plum pudding. Rutherford fires alphas at gold foil, one in eight thousand bounces back — and the atom becomes a tiny hard nucleus in emptiness. Bohr freezes the electrons into fixed orbits, lets them jump with photons E = hf, and prices hydrogen's rainbow exactly. Each model died by experiment; that is how models earn their keep.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
