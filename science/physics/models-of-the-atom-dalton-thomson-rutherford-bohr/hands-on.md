# Hands-on — Models of the atom — Dalton, Thomson, Rutherford, Bohr

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Scattering with marbles
You need marbles, a tray, and hidden obstacles (a single heavy ball bearing taped under a card in the middle, versus several soft foam pieces spread out). Roll marbles blindly across the tray from one edge, many times, and tally how many pass straight, deflect slightly, or bounce back, for each hidden arrangement. Compare the tallies: the spread-out soft obstacles mimic plum-pudding, the single hard centre mimics the nucleus. Write which tally matched Rutherford's foil results and what you concluded from it — the same inference, with marbles.
