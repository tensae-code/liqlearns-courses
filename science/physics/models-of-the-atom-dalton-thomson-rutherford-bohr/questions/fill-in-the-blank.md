# Models of the atom — Dalton, Thomson, Rutherford, Bohr — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Thomson's model is nicknamed the _____-pudding model.
**Answer: plum**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The particles fired at gold foil were _____ particles.
**Answer: alpha**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The atom's positive charge and most of its mass sit in the _____.
**Answer: nucleus**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A jump's photon has energy E = h_____.
**Answer: f**
