# Models of the atom — Dalton, Thomson, Rutherford, Bohr — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Dalton's atoms were…
a) Mostly empty space  b) Solid, indivisible spheres, identical within an element  c) Electrons in a positive pudding  d) Electrons in fixed orbits
**Answer: b) Solid, indivisible spheres, identical within an element**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Thomson's model was forced by his discovery/measurement of…
a) The nucleus  b) The electron, a light negative particle coming out of atoms  c) The neutron  d) Spectral lines
**Answer: b) The electron, a light negative particle coming out of atoms**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In the gold-foil experiment, about 1 alpha in 8,000…
a) Was absorbed by the foil  b) Bounced back toward the source  c) Split in two  d) Turned into light
**Answer: b) Bounced back toward the source**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Rutherford concluded the atom is…
a) Solid throughout  b) Mostly empty space, with mass and positive charge in a tiny dense nucleus  c) A pudding of charge  d) A single particle
**Answer: b) Mostly empty space, with mass and positive charge in a tiny dense nucleus**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In Bohr's model, an electron emits a photon when it…
a) Spins faster in its orbit  b) Jumps to a lower-energy orbit, the photon carrying exactly the energy difference  c) Leaves the atom  d) Collides with the nucleus
**Answer: b) Jumps to a lower-energy orbit, the photon carrying exactly the energy difference**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Bohr's model's great success was…
a) Explaining every element's spectrum  b) Calculating hydrogen's spectral lines to experimental accuracy  c) Finding the neutron  d) Disproving the nucleus
**Answer: b) Calculating hydrogen's spectral lines to experimental accuracy**
