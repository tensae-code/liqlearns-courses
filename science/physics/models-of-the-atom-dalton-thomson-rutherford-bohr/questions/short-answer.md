# Models of the atom — Dalton, Thomson, Rutherford, Bohr — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What chemical laws did Dalton's model explain well?
**Answer: The laws of definite and multiple proportions — elements combining in fixed whole-number mass ratios, as identical atoms combining in whole numbers would give.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why could plum-pudding never bounce an alpha particle straight back?
**Answer: Its positive charge is spread thinly through the whole atom, so nowhere is the electric repulsion strong enough to reverse a fast, heavy alpha particle.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What classical problem did Rutherford's model leave unsolved?
**Answer: Orbiting electrons should radiate energy continuously, spiral into the nucleus and collapse the atom almost instantly — yet atoms are stable.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Name one failure of Bohr's model that the quantum model had to fix.
**Answer: It could not handle multi-electron atoms (or line intensities / fine structure) — any one such named limit.**
