# Video script — Inclined plane, wedge and screw

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

The inclined plane is a ramp: you trade a longer, gentler climb for a smaller effort.
Its VR = slope length ÷ height — a ramp three times as long as it is high has VR 3.
The wedge is a moving inclined plane — knife, axe, nail, spear — that splits or separates.
Its VR = penetration length ÷ thickness: a long thin wedge drives deep while opening a narrow split with multiplied force.
The screw is an inclined plane wrapped into a helix around a cylinder.
Turning it once advances it by one pitch, so MA = circumference ÷ pitch (πd/p).
Nuts and bolts are screws working in pairs.

Remember: **Inclined plane VR = slope length ÷ height.**
Remember: **Wedge VR = penetration length ÷ thickness.**
Remember: **Screw MA = circumference ÷ pitch = πd/p.**
Keep the formula: **Plane VR = length / height; Wedge VR = penetration / thickness; Screw MA = πd / p**
