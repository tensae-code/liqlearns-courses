# Inclined plane, wedge and screw — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A ramp is 6 m long and 2 m high. VR?
a) VR = 6 ÷ 2 = 3.  b) It splits or separates it.  c) VR = 10 ÷ 2 = 5.  d) MA = πd/p = (3.14 × 2) ÷ 0.5 ≈ 12.6.
**Answer: a) VR = 6 ÷ 2 = 3.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a wedge do to material?
a) VR = 6 ÷ 2 = 3.  b) It splits or separates it.  c) VR = 10 ÷ 2 = 5.  d) MA = πd/p = (3.14 × 2) ÷ 0.5 ≈ 12.6.
**Answer: b) It splits or separates it.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A wedge penetrates 10 cm while opening 2 cm. VR?
a) VR = 6 ÷ 2 = 3.  b) It splits or separates it.  c) VR = 10 ÷ 2 = 5.  d) MA = πd/p = (3.14 × 2) ÷ 0.5 ≈ 12.6.
**Answer: c) VR = 10 ÷ 2 = 5.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A screw's diameter is 2 cm and pitch 0.5 cm. MA (ideal)?
a) VR = 6 ÷ 2 = 3.  b) It splits or separates it.  c) VR = 10 ÷ 2 = 5.  d) MA = πd/p = (3.14 × 2) ÷ 0.5 ≈ 12.6.
**Answer: d) MA = πd/p = (3.14 × 2) ÷ 0.5 ≈ 12.6.**
