# Inclined plane, wedge and screw — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A ramp is 6 m long and 2 m high. VR?
**Answer: VR = 6 ÷ 2 = 3.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a wedge do to material?
**Answer: It splits or separates it.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A wedge penetrates 10 cm while opening 2 cm. VR?
**Answer: VR = 10 ÷ 2 = 5.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A screw's diameter is 2 cm and pitch 0.5 cm. MA (ideal)?
**Answer: MA = πd/p = (3.14 × 2) ÷ 0.5 ≈ 12.6.**
