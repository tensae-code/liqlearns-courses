# Hands-on — Inclined plane, wedge and screw

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Roll a stone up a plank instead of lifting it straight. Then tap a wedge-shaped piece of wood into a log's crack. Then turn a screw slowly and watch it advance — three forms of one slope.

## Apply it where you live
Find the pitch of a real screw or bolt by measuring, and compute its ideal MA.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21844 — ramp, wedge splitting wood, and a screw unwrapped into its ramp spiral] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using Plane VR = length / height; Wedge VR = penetration / thickness; Screw MA = πd / p: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
