# Lesson plan — How this Physics course works — the Ethiopian Grade 10 way

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** How this Physics course works — the Ethiopian Grade 10 way
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics — Course opening (Grade 10 band, CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Grade 10 course runs in six units — arrows, then faster motion, then stretch, spark, magnet, light — every unit closing with PhET virtual labs, and every lesson staged the same way.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 9 Physics course and on everyday observation; a learner can still land here directly and follow it, because the map itself is the lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. The Grade 10 course has six units, in order: Vector Quantities; Uniformly Accelerated Motion; Elasticity and Static Equilibrium of Rigid Body; Static and Current Electricity; Magnetism; Electromagnetic Waves and Geometrical Optics.
2. Every unit closes with Virtual Labs — named PhET simulations (Geometric Optics, Bending Light, Color Vision, Radio Waves) run on the soft copy under the teacher's guidance.
3. The mathematics steps up from Grade 9: components, equations of motion, moduli, Coulomb's law, and mirror and lens calculations.
4. Lessons are staged the same way every time: Starting Off → Input and Model → Guided Practice → Check for Understanding → Where next → Closure.
5. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson text alone.
