# How this Physics course works — the Ethiopian Grade 10 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** List the six units in order.
**Answer: Unit 1 Vector Quantities; Unit 2 Uniformly Accelerated Motion; Unit 3 Elasticity and Static Equilibrium of Rigid Body; Unit 4 Static and Current Electricity; Unit 5 Magnetism; Unit 6 Electromagnetic Waves and Geometrical Optics.**

**2.** What is the virtual-lab habit?
**Answer: Every unit closes with named PhET simulations run on the soft copy under the teacher's guidance — change one thing at a time, watch, and check against the unit.**

**3.** Name two Ethiopian anchors in the Grade 10 book.
**Answer: The Entoto Observatory telescope question and birds on Ethiopian transmission lines.**

**4.** How does Grade 10 step up from Grade 9?
**Answer: The mathematics steps up: components, equations of motion, moduli, Coulomb's law, and mirror and lens calculations.**
