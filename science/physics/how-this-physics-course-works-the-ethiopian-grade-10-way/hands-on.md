# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Ethiopian Grade 10 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Walk the journey
Lay six pieces of paper on the floor as stepping stones. Write one unit name on each, in order, and walk the road saying what each unit is about as you step on it. A stone you cannot explain is a unit to revisit.

## Run the virtual-lab habit on paper
Choose one PhET simulation named in the lesson. On paper, write the experiment you would run in it: what you would change, what you would keep the same, and what you would watch. That plan is the virtual-lab habit — one change at a time.

## Spot it in daily life
Find birds on a wire, a mirror, or a magnet in your home or street, name its unit, and describe what you observed in three sentences.
