# Teaching the Lao way — lesson concept inventories and worked-example lessons — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is a concept inventory for, in the Lao guide's system?
**Answer: It states the part's whole conceptual cargo up front, so the teacher can plan and check each lesson against it — every named concept must appear, taught in the book's concept → worked examples → exercises shape.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How do we know the guide and student book match?
**Answer: The guide's table of contents is the same 7-part / 28-lesson structure as the student book, read in full in the reading wave; the guide's identity was proven from its own cover ("Teacher PHYSICS", 2014).**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does the worked-example lesson matter to this teaching way?
**Answer: It shows the system's centre: problem-solving is taught whole, in a lesson, rather than left as homework — the guide's notes and keys exist to support that worked-example spine through every lesson.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) State this lesson's evidence footing exactly.
**Answer: It stands on the Grade 10 teacher guide only — a teacher guide, cited as teacher-guide evidence, named for Laos in the countries line and counted in no acceptance count.**
