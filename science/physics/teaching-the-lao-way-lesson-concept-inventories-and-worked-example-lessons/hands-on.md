# Hands-on — Teaching the Lao way — lesson concept inventories and worked-example lessons

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build a concept inventory
Take one chapter of any book you are studying and write its concept inventory the Lao way: every named concept in the chapter, one per line, before you reread it. Then work the chapter's exercises and tick each inventory line where an exercise uses it. Any unticked line is a concept the chapter named but never exercised — exactly the gap the Lao guide's inventory is built to expose before teaching starts.
