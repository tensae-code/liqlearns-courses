# Video script — The magnetic field of a current — Biot–Savart and Ampère's laws

Today: **a current builds a field, and two laws price it**. Biot and Savart price it piece by piece: every short element of wire adds its own small field — current times length, times the sine of the angle, over distance squared — all summed with the right-hand rule. Add up a straight wire's pieces and the field stands in circles around it: mu-zero I over two pi r. Ampère prices the same field whole: walk a closed loop, add the field along your path, and the total is mu-zero times the current threading the loop. One law element-wise, one law whole-wise — choose by symmetry.
