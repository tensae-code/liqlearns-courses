# The magnetic field of a current — Biot–Savart and Ampère's laws — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — State the Biot–Savart law with the meaning of each symbol, and give dB's direction by the right-hand rule; Sum the elements for a long straight wire to B = μ₀I/(2πr), and quote the loop-centre and solenoid results; State Ampère's law, ∮B·dl = μ₀ I_through, and use symmetry to recover the wire's field in one line; Choose the right law for a geometry: Ampère where symmetry fixes B on a loop, Biot–Savart otherwise; Keep the direction discipline: grip rule for the wire, cross-product sense for the element — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
