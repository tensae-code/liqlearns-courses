# Hands-on — The magnetic field of a current — Biot–Savart and Ampère's laws

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map a wire's field with a compass
You need: a battery pack, a length of wire, a switch or crocodile clips, a small plotting compass, a sheet of paper — and a teacher's eye, with the circuit closed only for a few seconds at a time (the wire warms). Thread the wire vertically through the paper's centre, close the circuit briefly, and mark the compass needle's direction at several points around the wire; join the marks — circles around the wire. Reverse the battery and watch every direction flip. Move the compass further out: the swing weakens with distance, the 1/r of B = μ₀I/(2πr). Say aloud: the current is the magnet; its field circles it, priced by two laws.
