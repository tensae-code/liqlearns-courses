# The magnetic field of a current — Biot–Savart and Ampère's laws — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the Biot–Savart law, dB grows with…
a) r²  b) I and dl, and falls with r² — dB = (μ₀/4π) I dl sinθ / r²  c) The wire's colour  d) Voltage alone
**Answer: b) I and dl, and falls with r² — dB = (μ₀/4π) I dl sinθ / r²**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The direction of an element's dB is…
a) Along the wire  b) Toward the element  c) Perpendicular to both the element and the line from element to point (right-hand rule)  d) Random
**Answer: c) Perpendicular to both the element and the line from element to point (right-hand rule)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The field at distance r from a long straight wire is…
a) μ₀I r / 2π  b) μ₀I / (2πr)  c) μ₀I / (2r²)  d) Zero
**Answer: b) μ₀I / (2πr)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Inside a long solenoid the field is approximately…
a) μ₀ n I, uniform  b) Zero  c) Circular around the axis  d) Falling as 1/r
**Answer: a) μ₀ n I, uniform**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Ampère's law states that ∮B·dl around a closed loop equals…
a) The flux through the loop  b) μ₀ times the total current threading the loop  c) The loop's area  d) Zero always
**Answer: b) μ₀ times the total current threading the loop**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Ampère's law gives a field immediately when…
a) The wire is bent  b) Symmetry makes B constant (or zero) along a chosen loop  c) The current is alternating  d) No current flows
**Answer: b) Symmetry makes B constant (or zero) along a chosen loop**
