**1. Which statement about biasing the diode; its i–v characteristic is correct?**

**Answer: a) Forward bias (positive to P) shrinks the barrier and current flows; reverse bias widens it and current is…**

**2. Forward bias (positive to P) shrinks the barrier and current flows; reverse bias widens it and current is blocked (until breakdown); the current–______ graph's knee captures both behaviours.**
a) voltage  b) generator  c) neutron
**Answer: a) voltage**
