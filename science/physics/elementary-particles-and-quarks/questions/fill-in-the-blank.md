# Elementary particles and quarks — the fundamental interactions — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A proton is two up quarks and one _____ quark.
**Answer: down**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The electron's family — elementary particles that feel no strong force — are the _____ .
**Answer: leptons**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Electromagnetism is carried by the _____ .
**Answer: photon**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Particles gain mass by interacting with the _____ field.
**Answer: Higgs**
