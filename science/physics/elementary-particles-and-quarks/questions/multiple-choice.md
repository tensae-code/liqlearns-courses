# Elementary particles and quarks — the fundamental interactions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The proton's quark content is…
a) udd  b) uud  c) uuu  d) uds
**Answer: a) udd**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Particles built from quarks are called…
a) Hadrons  b) Leptons  c) Photons  d) Bosons
**Answer: b) Leptons**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Ordinary matter is built entirely from which generation?
a) The third  b) The first — up, down and the electron  c) The second  d) All three equally
**Answer: c) The second**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The antiparticle of the electron is the…
a) Positron  b) Neutrino  c) Muon  d) Proton
**Answer: d) Proton**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The strong interaction between quarks is carried by…
a) Photons  b) Gluons  c) W and Z bosons  d) Gravitons, already detected
**Answer: a) Photons**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The Higgs boson, found at CERN in 2012, completed the Standard Model's account of…
a) Mass — particles acquire it through the Higgs field  b) Charge  c) Spin  d) Antimatter
**Answer: b) Charge**
