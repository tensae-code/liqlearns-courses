# Video script — Elementary particles and quarks — the fundamental interactions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the family album of everything**. Peel matter: atoms, nuclei, protons and neutrons — and inside those, **quarks**. A proton is two ups and a down; a neutron, two downs and an up; their third-charges add to wholes. Beside the quarks sit the **leptons** — the electron and its heavier cousins, with their neutrinos — in three generations, of which ordinary matter uses only the first. Every particle has an antiparticle; forces themselves travel as particles — photon, gluons, W and Z; and mass comes from the Higgs field, whose boson CERN found in 2012. That roster — the Standard Model — is the cast list of the universe.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
