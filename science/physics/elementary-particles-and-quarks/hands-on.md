# Hands-on — Elementary particles and quarks — the fundamental interactions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Charge the triplets
With nine cards labelled u (+2/3), u, u, d (−1/3), d, d, s (−1/3), c (+2/3), b (−1/3), deal out triplets and add their charges: find uud = +1 (proton) and udd = 0 (neutron), then hunt for any other whole-charge triplet. Say aloud: whole charges from thirds of charges — the arithmetic that made physicists believe in quarks before any were 'seen'.

## Read a decay
Write the beta decay of a neutron as the textbooks give it: n → p + e⁻ + antineutrino. Check the accounts aloud: charge 0 → +1 −1 +0; a down quark in the neutron has become an up quark — and name the interaction (weak) and its carrier family (W) responsible.
