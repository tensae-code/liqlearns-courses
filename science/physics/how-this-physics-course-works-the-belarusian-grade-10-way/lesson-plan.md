## Sub lessons
1. **The frontispiece — the method before the physics** — The frontispiece — «How to work with this textbook» — teaches the study method itself, before any physics: first read the paragraph quickly, to get its shape; then read it deeply, slowly; reproduce every derivation in your own notebook, in your own hand, until the steps are yours; and repeat the book's experiments at home with what you have. The answers to the exercises are printed at the back, so the book can check you — but the method says work first, check after.
2. **The design of every paragraph** — The book is built from numbered paragraphs (§). Every paragraph ends the same way: a «Main conclusions» (Галоўныя вывады) block that compresses the paragraph to its points, then control questions to check them. The exercises are graded by difficulty, and the hardest carry a star. Material beyond the core is printed in small print and marked as optional, so the main road through the book is never blocked by an extra.
3. **The Grade 10 map — what the year holds** — The Grade 10 study aid runs in two great halves. Molecular physics: Ch.1 Foundations of molecular-kinetic theory (§§1–8) and Ch.2 Foundations of thermodynamics (§§9–12). Electrodynamics: Ch.3 Electrostatics (§§13–21), Ch.4 Direct current (§§22–23), Ch.5 Current in different media (§§24–28), and Ch.6 Magnetic field and electromagnetic induction (§§29–35). The laboratory experiment block sits at printed p. 249, with an appendix at p. 260 and the answers at p. 264 — and the frontispiece, «How to work with the study aid» (printed p. 3), hands the pupil the same series method to run it with.
