# How this Physics course works — the Belarusian Grade 10 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State the study method the Grade 10 frontispiece teaches.
**Answer: Read quickly, then deeply; reproduce every derivation in your own notebook; repeat the experiments at home — the series method, restated for the study aid.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How is a paragraph in this book built, end to end?
**Answer: A numbered paragraph (§) carries the material, then closes with «Main conclusions» (Галоўныя вывады) and control questions; its exercises are graded, the hardest starred.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) List the six Grade 10 chapters in order.
**Answer: Ch.1 Foundations of molecular-kinetic theory; Ch.2 Foundations of thermodynamics; Ch.3 Electrostatics; Ch.4 Direct current; Ch.5 Current in different media; Ch.6 Magnetic field and electromagnetic induction.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Which half of the book carries Ohm's law for the complete circuit?
**Answer: Electrodynamics — Ch.4 Direct current (§23), in the second half of the book.**
