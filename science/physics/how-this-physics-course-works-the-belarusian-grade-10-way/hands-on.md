# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Belarusian Grade 10 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run the method on one paragraph
Choose one short paragraph's worth of physics — from this lesson, the Grade 10 map paragraph will do. (1) Read it once, quickly, and close it: say its shape in one sentence. (2) Read it again, deeply. (3) Take a notebook and reproduce its reasoning in your own hand, without looking, then check. (4) Name one experiment or test from it you could repeat at home with household things, and say what you would watch for. You have just done the frontispiece — the Belarusian Grade 10 way.
