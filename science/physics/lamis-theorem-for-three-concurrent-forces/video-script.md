Today: **Lami's theorem — three forces at a point, priced by sines**. When a point is held still by three forces, each force divided by the sine of the angle between the other two gives one and the same number. Hang a weight from two strings: the weight sits over the sine of the angle between the strings, and each tension sits over the sine of the angle facing it. Spread the strings wider and the sines shrink — so the tensions grow, which is exactly what your fingers feel.
