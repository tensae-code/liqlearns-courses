# Lami's theorem for three concurrent forces — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Three forces are concurrent when they…
a) Are equal in size  b) All act through a single point  c) Are parallel  d) Act on different bodies
**Answer: b) All act through a single point**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Three concurrent forces in equilibrium, drawn head-to-tail, form…
a) A straight line  b) An open chain  c) A closed triangle  d) A circle
**Answer: c) A closed triangle**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In Lami's theorem, each force is divided by the sine of the angle…
a) Next to it on the left  b) Between itself and the largest force  c) Between the other two forces  d) At the triangle's base
**Answer: c) Between the other two forces**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The three angles between the forces at the point must add to…
a) 180°  b) 270°  c) 360°  d) 90°
**Answer: c) 360°**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A weight W hangs from two strings; the angle between the tensions is 90°. In the Lami chain, W is divided by…
a) sin 90°  b) sin of the angle between W and a tension  c) 1, with no sine  d) The larger tension
**Answer: a) sin 90°**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Spreading two supporting strings wider apart, for the same hanging weight, makes the tensions…
a) Smaller  b) Larger  c) Unchanged  d) Zero
**Answer: b) Larger**
