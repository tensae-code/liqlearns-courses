# Lami's theorem for three concurrent forces — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Forces that all act through one point are called _____ forces.
**Answer: concurrent**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In equilibrium the vector _____ of the three forces is zero.
**Answer: sum**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Lami's theorem pairs each force with the _____ of the angle between the other two.
**Answer: sine**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The string nearer the _____ carries the larger share of a hanging weight.
**Answer: vertical**
