# Hands-on — Lami's theorem for three concurrent forces

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel Lami's theorem in your fingers
Tie a bunch of keys to the middle of a metre of string. Hold one end in each hand, hands level, and let the keys hang from a narrow V — note the gentle pull. Keeping the keys at the same height, spread your hands slowly apart so the V widens toward a straight line: the pull in your fingers climbs steeply even though the keys' weight never changed. Bring your hands close again and feel it fall. Say aloud what the theorem says about the moment: the angle between the tensions grew toward 180°, its sine shrank toward zero, so the tensions — weight divided by that shrinking sine — had to grow. Never spread to a truly straight line: the theorem says the tension would need to be endless.
