# Lesson plan — Energy stored in a stretched wire

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** Energy stored in a stretched wire
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18) — a new lesson built for the Sri Lanka placement named in lesson/sources.md
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Stretching a wire stores energy in it: because the stretching force grows from zero with the extension, the work stored is the **average force times the extension, ½Fx** — the area under the force–extension graph — which, written in stress and strain, is **½ × stress × strain per unit volume**; the wire gives it back when it contracts, as long as it stayed within its elastic limit
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Earlier lessons make a good preparation and are recommended, never forced
