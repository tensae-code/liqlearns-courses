Today: **the energy inside a stretch**. A wire's pull grows from nothing as you stretch it, so the work you store is the average force — half the final force — times the extension: one-half F x, the triangle under the graph. In material terms it is half stress times strain in every unit of volume, growing with the square of the strain. Stay elastic and the wire pays it all back; overstretch it and part of your work stays behind as heat and a permanent bend.
