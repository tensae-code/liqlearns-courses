# Hands-on — Energy stored in a stretched wire

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Graph the store
Hang increasing weights on a spring or elastic cord, plot force against extension, and shade the triangle under the line up to one chosen load. Measure the triangle's area in graph units. Say aloud: that shaded area is joules — work I did, stored, waiting.

## Give it back
Stretch an elastic band, hold it against your lip as you let it contract slowly, then snap-release a second stretch and feel the band warm after repeated stretching past easy limits. Say aloud: returned within the limit, spent as heat beyond it.
