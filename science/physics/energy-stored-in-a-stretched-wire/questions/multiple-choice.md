# Energy stored in a stretched wire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The work stored stretching a wire to force F and extension x (Hooke's law) is…
a) Fx  b) ½Fx  c) 2Fx  d) F/x
**Answer: b) ½Fx**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) On a force–extension graph, the stored energy is…
a) The slope  b) The area under the line  c) The intercept  d) The maximum force
**Answer: b) The area under the line**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Energy stored per unit volume equals…
a) Stress × strain  b) ½ × stress × strain  c) Stress ÷ strain  d) Y × strain
**Answer: b) ½ × stress × strain**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Doubling the strain (elastic) multiplies the stored energy per unit volume by…
a) 2  b) 3  c) 4  d) 8
**Answer: c) 4**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A wire stretched beyond its elastic limit and released…
a) Returns all the energy  b) Keeps a permanent set, and part of the work is lost as heat  c) Stores more energy than the work done  d) Breaks Hooke's law backwards only
**Answer: b) Keeps a permanent set, and part of the work is lost as heat**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The stored energy is returned as work when…
a) The wire is cut  b) The load is removed and the wire contracts (within its elastic limit)  c) The wire is heated  d) The extension is measured
**Answer: b) The load is removed and the wire contracts (within its elastic limit)**
