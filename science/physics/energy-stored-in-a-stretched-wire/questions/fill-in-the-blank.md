# Energy stored in a stretched wire — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Stored energy in a stretched wire equals one-half F times _____.
**Answer: x**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Energy per unit volume is ½ times stress times _____.
**Answer: strain**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The store per unit volume grows with the _____ of the strain.
**Answer: square**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Beyond the elastic limit a wire keeps a permanent _____.
**Answer: set**
