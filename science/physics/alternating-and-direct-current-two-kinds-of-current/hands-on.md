# Hands-on — Alternating and direct current — two kinds of current

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One way or back-and-forth?
Take a torch battery circuit in your mind — or a real torch: current leaves one end of the battery, lights the bulb, returns to the other end, always the same way round. Now picture the mains: the push flips 50 times a second. For five devices at home, write which kind each uses and how you know (battery inside = d.c.; plugs into the mains and has no charger block = a.c.; plugs in through a charger block = a.c. in, d.c. inside).
