# Video script — Alternating and direct current — two kinds of current

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **two kinds of current**. Batteries push charge one way, steady — that is d.c. The mains pushes it back and forth, wave after wave — that is a.c. Chargers turn a.c. into d.c. so your phone can drink it.
