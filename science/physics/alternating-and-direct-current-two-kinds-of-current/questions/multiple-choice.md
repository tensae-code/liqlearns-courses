# Alternating and direct current — two kinds of current — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Direct current (d.c.) is current that…
**Answer: a) flows steadily in one direction  b) reverses direction repeatedly  c) flows only in gases  d) has no direction**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these supplies d.c.?
**Answer: a) a battery  b) the wall mains  c) a power-station generator  d) a transformer**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The graph of a.c. against time is…
**Answer: a) a wave crossing zero again and again  b) a flat line  c) a staircase  d) a single spike**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Generators naturally produce a.c. because…
**Answer: a) a spinning coil cuts the field first one way, then the other  b) they contain batteries  c) wires are long  d) magnets are hot**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Power grids use a.c. chiefly because…
**Answer: a) transformers can step its voltage up and down  b) it is cheaper to make  c) it never loses energy  d) devices prefer it**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A phone charger contains a rectifier, which…
**Answer: a) converts mains a.c. into d.c.  b) converts d.c. into a.c.  c) stores charge  d) steps voltage up**
