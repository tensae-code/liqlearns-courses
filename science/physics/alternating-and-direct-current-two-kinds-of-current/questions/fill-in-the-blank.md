# Alternating and direct current — two kinds of current — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Current that flows steadily in one direction is called _____ current.
**Answer: direct**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The mains kind of current, reversing again and again, is called _____ current.
**Answer: alternating**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A _____ converts a.c. into d.c., as inside a phone charger.
**Answer: rectifier**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) _____ can step a.c. voltage up and down, which is why grids carry a.c.
**Answer: Transformers**
