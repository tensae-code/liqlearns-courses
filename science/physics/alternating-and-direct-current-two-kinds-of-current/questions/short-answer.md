# Alternating and direct current — two kinds of current — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State the difference between d.c. and a.c. in one sentence.
**Answer: D.c. flows steadily in one direction; a.c. reverses direction repeatedly, its graph a wave crossing zero.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A supply reverses direction 100 times per second. How many complete cycles is that per second, and what kind of current is it?
**Answer: A.c. — each full cycle contains two reversals, so 100 reversals make 50 cycles per second.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does a phone not plug straight into mains a.c.?
**Answer: Its battery and electronics need steady one-way d.c.; the charger rectifies the mains a.c. to d.c. first.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) What does an inverter do, and when is one needed?
**Answer: It converts d.c. into a.c. — needed when a battery or solar panel (d.c.) must run a mains (a.c.) device.**
