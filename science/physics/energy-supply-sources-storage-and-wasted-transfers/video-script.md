# Video script — Energy supply — sources, storage and wasted transfers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **where energy comes from, and where it leaks**. We burn fuels that finish, or catch flows — sun, wind, water — that vary. We carry energy along wires, pipes and roads, and store it in batteries, tanks and raised water for when it is wanted. And at every step some of it escapes as spread-out heat: not destroyed, just wasted. Efficiency — useful out divided by total in — is the number that tells the truth about the leak.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
