# Hands-on — Energy supply — sources, storage and wasted transfers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The warm lamp audit
With a teacher's permission, switch on a lamp and, after five minutes, hold your hand near — never on — the bulb or housing, and feel the warmth: that is wasted energy leaving as heat. Read the lamp's power rating and, if it is an old filament type, compare it with an LED lamp of the same brightness in another room: same useful output, far smaller input. Write the two ratings down and work out how many LED lamps the filament lamp's power could run. You have just done an efficiency audit with your hand and two numbers.
