# Energy supply — sources, storage and wasted transfers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does a renewable supply still need storage? Give the Sun as your example.
**Answer: Sunlight arrives in the daytime and not at night, while demand continues; storage holds the surplus from the bright hours for use in the dark ones.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name two ways energy is transported from where it is found to where it is used.
**Answer: Any two of: electricity along wires from power stations; fuels by train, truck or pipeline; heat in insulated water pipes (district heating).**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) 'Wasted energy breaks the law of conservation of energy.' Say why this is wrong.
**Answer: Energy is conserved — the wasted part still exists, but spread out as thermal energy in the surroundings, too thin to gather back for the job; nothing is destroyed.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) What does a Sankey diagram show that a pair of numbers does not show as quickly?
**Answer: It draws the input, useful output and wasted output as arrows whose widths are in proportion, so the sizes of the flows — and how big the waste is — are seen at a glance.**
