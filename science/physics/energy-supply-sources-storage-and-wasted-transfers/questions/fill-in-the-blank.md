# Energy supply — sources, storage and wasted transfers — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Coal, oil and natural gas are called _____ sources because they finish.
**Answer: non-renewable**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Electricity travels from power stations to towns along _____.
**Answer: wires**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Efficiency equals useful output divided by total _____.
**Answer: input**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A diagram of energy flows drawn with arrows of proportional width is a _____ diagram.
**Answer: Sankey**
