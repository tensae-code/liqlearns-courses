# Energy supply — sources, storage and wasted transfers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which set contains only renewable energy sources?
a) Coal, oil, natural gas  b) Sunlight, wind, moving water  c) Gas, coal, sunlight  d) Oil, wind, coal
**Answer: b) Sunlight, wind, moving water**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Non-renewable fuels are a problem of supply because they…
a) Cannot be burned  b) Are used up far faster than they form  c) Arrive only at night  d) Cannot be transported
**Answer: b) Are used up far faster than they form**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Pumping water uphill into a reservoir is a way of…
a) Wasting energy on purpose  b) Storing energy, to be released through turbines later  c) Making electricity disappear  d) Cooling the power station
**Answer: b) Storing energy, to be released through turbines later**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Energy stored in a battery is held as…
a) Light  b) Chemical energy  c) Sound  d) Movement
**Answer: b) Chemical energy**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A machine takes in 80 J and gives 20 J of useful work. Its efficiency is…
a) 400%  b) 25%  c) 60%  d) 100%
**Answer: b) 25%**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Wasted energy in a transfer is most often…
a) Destroyed completely  b) Thermal energy spread to the surroundings  c) Turned back into fuel  d) Stored in a battery
**Answer: b) Thermal energy spread to the surroundings**
