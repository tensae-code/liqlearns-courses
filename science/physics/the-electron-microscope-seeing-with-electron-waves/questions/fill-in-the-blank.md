# The electron microscope — seeing with electron waves — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) No instrument resolves detail much finer than about half the _____ of its illumination.
**Answer: wavelength**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) An electron's wavelength is given by de Broglie's λ = h/_____.
**Answer: mv**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Electron beams are focused by _____ lenses made of coils.
**Answer: magnetic**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Specimens in an electron microscope must withstand vacuum and bombardment, so they are never _____ when imaged.
**Answer: alive (living)**
