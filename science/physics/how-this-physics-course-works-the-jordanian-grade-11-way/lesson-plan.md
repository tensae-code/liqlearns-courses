# Lesson plan — How this physics course works — the Jordanian Grade 11 way

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** How this physics course works — the Jordanian Grade 11 way
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18), inside the energy-and-thermodynamics strand — the doorway lesson for Jordan's Grade 11 academic-track book
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Jordan's Grade 11 academic-track book continues the NCCD pattern into the senior years — two units per term, an introductory experiment per unit, English terms glossed, 'Physics and life' and STEAM enrichment spreads (wind energy, the refrigerator) — and adds a working tool at the back: a trigonometric-ratios table beside the glossary, because senior physics runs on components and angles
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Any earlier lesson named below is recommended first, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Two units per term — the senior shape** — The academic track settles into two units a term: Term 1 carries Work and Energy, then Thermodynamics; Term 2 carries Static Electricity, then Oscillatory Motion and Waves. The shape is the same as Grades 9–10 — introductory experiment, general idea, lessons, unit review — at senior depth, with enrichment spreads chosen from real technology: wind farms for energy, the refrigerator for thermodynamics.
2. **The pattern holds — experiment, gloss, application** — Nothing about the way changes: the work unit opens by calculating work with a spring balance, the thermodynamics unit by testing how mass changes a temperature rise. English terms stay glossed at first use, and the applications stay Jordanian and practical — the course's promise from Grade 9 is kept word for word in the academic track.
3. **The back-of-book tools — glossary and trig table** — The senior book's back matter adds a trigonometric-ratios table to the glossary: a quiet admission that from Grade 11 the physics runs on resolved components, angled forces and wave phases, and the ratios must be at the learner's elbow. The tools at the back are part of the way — the course equips the mathematics it demands.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
