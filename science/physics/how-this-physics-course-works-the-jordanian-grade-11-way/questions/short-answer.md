# How this physics course works — the Jordanian Grade 11 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the four units of Grade 11 in order.
**Answer: Term 1: Work and Energy; Thermodynamics. Term 2: Static Electricity; Oscillatory Motion and Wave Motion.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the two enrichment spreads named for Term 1 and the unit each closes.
**Answer: Wind energy and wind farms close the Work and Energy unit; the refrigerator closes the Thermodynamics unit.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In what way is the Grade 11 course's pattern identical to Grade 9's, and in what way does it grow?
**Answer: Identical: introductory experiment per unit, general idea, glossed English terms, lesson and unit reviews, application spreads. It grows in depth and equipment: two units per term at senior level and a trigonometric-ratios table added to the back matter.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) What does the trig table's presence tell you about Grade 11 physics?
**Answer: That its problems routinely resolve angled quantities — components of forces and displacements, phases of oscillations — so the ratios are a working tool, kept beside the glossary for constant use.**
