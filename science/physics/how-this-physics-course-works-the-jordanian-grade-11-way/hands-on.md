# Hands-on — How this physics course works — the Jordanian Grade 11 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Re-run the work unit's opener: pull an object with a spring balance at an angle, read the force, and compute the work for two angles — using the trig table for the components. (2) Do the thermodynamics opener: heat equal and double masses of water with the same heater and compare temperature rises. (3) Find three glossed English terms in the Grade 11 book and add them to your glossary from the Grade 9 and 10 way lessons.
