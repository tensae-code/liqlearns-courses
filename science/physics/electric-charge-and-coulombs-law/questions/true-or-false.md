# Electric charge and Coulomb's law — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. Atoms hold positive protons and negative electrons; bodies charge by gaining or losing electrons, like charges repel and unlike attract, and Coulomb's law gives the force between two point charges (inverse-square in distance, proportional to both charges) — with the meaning of one coulomb drilled.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Atoms hold negative protons and negative electrons; bodies charge by gaining or losing electrons, like charges repel and unlike attract, and Coulomb's law gives the force between two point charges (inverse-square in distance, proportional to both charges) — with the meaning of one coulomb drilled.**
**Answer: False**
