# Electric charge and Coulomb's law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about charging and coulomb's law is correct?**
a) Heating lengthens, widens and bulks solids: linear, area and volume expansion each with its coefficient; gaps in rails…  b) MRI uses magnetism and radio waves, and the X-ray CT scan builds cross-sections into a 3-D view.  c) Atoms hold positive protons and negative electrons; bodies charge by gaining or losing electrons, like charges repel…
**Answer: c) Atoms hold positive protons and negative electrons; bodies charge by gaining or losing electrons, like…**

**2. Atoms hold positive protons and negative ______s; bodies charge by gaining or losing electrons, like charges repel and unlike attract, and Coulomb's law gives the force between two point charges (inverse-square in distance, proportional to both charges) — with the meaning of one coulomb drilled.**
a) temperature  b) binary  c) electron
**Answer: c) electron**
