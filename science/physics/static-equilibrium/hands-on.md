# Hands-on — Static equilibrium — balanced forces and balanced turning

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Balance a ruler on a pencil with coins on both sides. Move a coin and watch the second condition fail in a slow tip — then fix it.

## Apply it where you live
Solve a ladder or beam problem imposing both equilibrium conditions step by step.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21893 — ladder against a wall with all forces and moments marked, balanced] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using ΣF = 0; Στ = 0: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
