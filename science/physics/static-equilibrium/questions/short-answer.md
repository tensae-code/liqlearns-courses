# Static equilibrium — balanced forces and balanced turning — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A beam carries 300 N down at its centre; supports push up 150 N each. Net force?
**Answer: 150 + 150 − 300 = 0 — first condition satisfied.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A body has net force zero but a net turning effect. Is it in equilibrium?
**Answer: No — the second condition fails; it will rotate.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is torque's other name in this unit?
**Answer: Moment (turning effect of a force).**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A ladder leans in equilibrium. Which conditions hold?
**Answer: Both — net force zero and net moment zero.**
