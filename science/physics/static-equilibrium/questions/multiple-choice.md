# Static equilibrium — balanced forces and balanced turning — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A beam carries 300 N down at its centre; supports push up 150 N each. Net force?
a) 150 + 150 − 300 = 0 — first condition satisfied.  b) No — the second condition fails; it will rotate.  c) Moment (turning effect of a force).  d) Both — net force zero and net moment zero.
**Answer: a) 150 + 150 − 300 = 0 — first condition satisfied.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A body has net force zero but a net turning effect. Is it in equilibrium?
a) 150 + 150 − 300 = 0 — first condition satisfied.  b) No — the second condition fails; it will rotate.  c) Moment (turning effect of a force).  d) Both — net force zero and net moment zero.
**Answer: b) No — the second condition fails; it will rotate.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is torque's other name in this unit?
a) 150 + 150 − 300 = 0 — first condition satisfied.  b) No — the second condition fails; it will rotate.  c) Moment (turning effect of a force).  d) Both — net force zero and net moment zero.
**Answer: c) Moment (turning effect of a force).**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A ladder leans in equilibrium. Which conditions hold?
a) 150 + 150 − 300 = 0 — first condition satisfied.  b) No — the second condition fails; it will rotate.  c) Moment (turning effect of a force).  d) Both — net force zero and net moment zero.
**Answer: d) Both — net force zero and net moment zero.**
