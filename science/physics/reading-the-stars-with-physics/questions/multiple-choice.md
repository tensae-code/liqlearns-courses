# Reading the stars with physics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about physics and astronomy is correct?**
a) Launched at an angle, the velocity splits into components; at the top the vertical component is momentarily zero while…  b) Newton's laws govern the heavens; electromagnetic waves are astronomy's messenger and atomic emission spectra tell…  c) The summary runs Newton's laws, friction, work–energy, and momentum; end questions include inertia vs mass, effects of…
**Answer: b) Newton's laws govern the heavens; electromagnetic waves are astronomy's messenger and atomic emission spectra…**

**2. Newton's laws govern the heavens; electro______ic waves are astronomy's messenger and atomic emission spectra tell astronomers what distant bodies are made of and how they move — the astronomers' only information about a star is its light.**
a) acceleration  b) magnet  c) activity
**Answer: b) magnet**
