# Reading the stars with physics — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: Newton's laws govern the heavens; electromagnetic waves are astronomy's messenger and atomic emission spectra tell astronomers what distant bodies are made of…
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to name or work, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.
