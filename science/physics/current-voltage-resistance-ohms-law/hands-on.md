# Hands-on — Current, voltage, resistance — Ohm's law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Wire a battery and bulb; add a longer, thinner wire in series and watch the bulb dim. Resistance just announced itself.

## Apply it where you live
For three home devices, state which of V, I, R you would change to make each safer or brighter, and why.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21905 — circuit with current, voltage and resistance labelled; birds safe on one wire] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using V = IR: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
