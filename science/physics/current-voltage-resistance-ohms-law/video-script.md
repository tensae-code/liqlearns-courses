# Video script — Current, voltage, resistance — Ohm's law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Three quantities run every circuit.
Current is the rate of flow of charge, measured in amperes (A).
Voltage (potential difference) is the energy given to each unit charge, measured in volts (V).
Resistance opposes the flow, measured in ohms (Ω) — and a conductor's resistance depends on its material, length and thickness.
Ohm's law binds them: V = IR.
The book's puzzle: birds sit unharmed on one transmission wire because both feet stand at the same potential — no potential difference across the bird, so no current through it.

Remember: **Current = rate of charge flow (A); voltage = energy per charge (V); resistance opposes flow (Ω).**
Remember: **Ohm's law: V = IR.**
Remember: **Resistance depends on material, length and cross-section (thickness).**
Keep the formula: **V = IR**
