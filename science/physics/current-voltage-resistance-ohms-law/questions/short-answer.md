# Current, voltage, resistance — Ohm's law — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A 12 V battery drives a 4 Ω resistor. Current?
**Answer: I = V/R = 12 ÷ 4 = 3 A.**

**2.** Current 2 A through 5 Ω. Voltage?
**Answer: V = IR = 2 × 5 = 10 V.**

**3.** Why are birds safe on one wire?
**Answer: Both feet are at the same potential — no voltage across the bird, no current through it.**

**4.** A longer wire of the same material has — more or less resistance?
**Answer: More resistance.**
