# Current, voltage, resistance — Ohm's law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A 12 V battery drives a 4 Ω resistor. Current?
a) I = V/R = 12 ÷ 4 = 3 A.  b) V = IR = 2 × 5 = 10 V.  c) Both feet are at the same potential — no voltage across the bird, no current through it.  d) More resistance.
