# Current, voltage, resistance — Ohm's law — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1.** Current = rate of charge flow (A); voltage = energy per charge (V); resistance opposes flow (Ω).
**Answer: True**

**2.** Ohm's law: V = IR.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Birds are shocked unless they touch two wires at different potentials — and a bird on one wire is in danger.
**Answer: False**
