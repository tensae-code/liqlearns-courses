# Beats — when two frequencies mix — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — Superpose two waves of close frequencies and their sum alternates between agreement (loud) and opposition (quiet) as the faster wave gains on the slower; Because the gain of one full cycle takes 1/|f₁ − f₂| seconds, the pulsing repeats at f_beat = |f₁ − f₂|: 256 Hz against 254 Hz gives 2 beats per second; identical frequencies give none; A tuner sounds a reference and the instrument together and listens: beats mean out of tune, and their slowing tracks the approach to unison; when the beats vanish, the frequencies match — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
