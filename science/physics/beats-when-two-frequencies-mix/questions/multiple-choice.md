# Beats — when two frequencies mix — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Beats arise from two tones whose frequencies are…
a) Identical only  b) Close but not equal  c) A thousand hertz apart  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Close but not equal**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) During a beat's quiet moment the two waves are…
a) In agreement  b) In opposition, cancelling  c) Both stopped  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) In opposition, cancelling**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The beat frequency equals…
a) f₁ + f₂  b) |f₁ − f₂|, the difference  c) The average exactly  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) |f₁ − f₂|, the difference**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) 256 Hz with 254 Hz gives beats at…
a) 510 per second  b) 2 per second  c) 0 per second  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) 2 per second**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A tuner knows unison is reached when…
a) The beats are fastest  b) The beats vanish  c) The sound is loudest  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) The beats vanish**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Wax on a fork's prong changes a beat test by…
a) Silencing the fork  b) Lowering the fork's frequency so its difference shows as beats  c) Doubling its frequency  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Lowering the fork's frequency so its difference shows as beats**
