# Beats — when two frequencies mix — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain a beat in wave language.
**Answer:** The faster wave steadily gains phase on the slower; agreement and opposition alternate, so the sum pulses at the difference frequency.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A piano string beats 3 times a second against a 256 Hz fork. What are the string's two possible frequencies?
**Answer:** 253 Hz or 259 Hz — the difference is 3 Hz either way.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is beat-counting a precision method?
**Answer:** A slow rhythm is easy to count exactly, yet it measures a tiny frequency difference.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) What does a slowing beat rate tell a tuner?
**Answer:** The frequencies are approaching each other — the difference is shrinking.
