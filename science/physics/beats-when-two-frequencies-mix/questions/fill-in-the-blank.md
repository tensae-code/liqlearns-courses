# Beats — when two frequencies mix — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Beat frequency = |f₁ − ___|.
**Answer:** f₂

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Beats are a case of two waves ___ — superposing.
**Answer:** interfering (superposing)

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) At ___ the beats vanish.
**Answer:** unison

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A tuner listens for beats to bring an instrument into ___.
**Answer:** tune
