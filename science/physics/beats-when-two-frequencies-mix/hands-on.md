# Hands-on — Beats — when two frequencies mix

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with your hands
Sound two tuning forks (or two phone tone generators) a few hertz apart and count the swells in ten seconds; divide and compare with the known difference. Then detune one fork slightly with a lump of wax on a prong and hear its own tone beat against its untouched twin — slower beats as you scrape the wax toward matching.
