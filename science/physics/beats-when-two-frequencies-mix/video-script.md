# Video script — Beats — when two frequencies mix

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **play two tones of slightly different frequencies together and the sound swells and fades in a slow rhythm — beats — at a rate equal to the difference of the frequencies, f_beat = |f₁ − f₂|, the effect tuners use to bring instruments into tune**. Superpose two waves of close frequencies and their sum alternates between agreement (loud) and opposition (quiet) as the faster wave gains on the slower. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — two tones, one pulsing sound; the beat frequency is the difference; tuning by beats — the musician's instrument — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
