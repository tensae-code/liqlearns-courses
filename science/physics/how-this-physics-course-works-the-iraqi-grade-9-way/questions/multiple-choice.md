# How this Physics course works — the Iraqi Grade 9 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Grade 9 book's shape is…
a) One long chapter  b) 9 chapters  c) A dictionary  d) Exam papers only
**Answer: b) 9 chapters**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Every Iraqi lesson or chapter opens with…
a) The answers  b) A hands-on activity or a chapter vocabulary list (مفردات الفصل)  c) A song  d) The next book
**Answer: b) A hands-on activity or a chapter vocabulary list (مفردات الفصل)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Technical terms in the Iraqi books are printed…
a) Only in Arabic  b) Only in English  c) In Arabic and English side by side  d) Never printed
**Answer: c) In Arabic and English side by side**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Worked examples in the Iraqi way are there to…
a) Fill pages  b) Show a real problem solved step by step with the lesson's idea  c) Replace all questions  d) Test the teacher
**Answer: b) Show a real problem solved step by step with the lesson's idea**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Every chapter closes with…
a) Nothing  b) A vocabulary/concept review plus short-answer and multiple-choice chapter tests  c) Only a picture  d) The contents page
**Answer: b) A vocabulary/concept review plus short-answer and multiple-choice chapter tests**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The chapter test matters because…
a) It checks the chapter's words and ideas together, in the book's own two question kinds  b) It is marked twice  c) It replaces the lessons  d) It is in English only
**Answer: a) It checks the chapter's words and ideas together, in the book's own two question kinds**
