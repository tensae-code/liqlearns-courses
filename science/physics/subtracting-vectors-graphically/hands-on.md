# Hands-on — Subtracting vectors graphically

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw it to scale
On paper, choose a scale (1 cm = 10 units). Draw two arrows at an angle, join them head-to-tail, and measure the resultant with ruler and protractor. Check your drawing by components.

## See it and say it
Study this lesson's picture — a walker's three-leg path with the straight-line resultant — and name every part of it out loud. Then close the picture and draw it from memory; check your drawing against the lesson.

## Teach it back
Explain this lesson to someone at home using one thing in the room as your example. Their questions show you which part to re-read.
