# Hands-on — Pendulum and spring — timing oscillations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Hang a stone on a string, time 20 swings, halve the string, time again. The shorter pendulum hurries — exactly as the formula promises.

## Apply it where you live
Use a pendulum of known length to determine g where you live, showing all working.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21853 — swinging pendulum and a bouncing mass on a spring, timers beside both] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using Pendulum T = 2π√(l/g); Spring T = 2π√(m/k): invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
