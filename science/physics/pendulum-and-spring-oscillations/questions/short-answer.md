# Pendulum and spring — timing oscillations — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A pendulum's length is quadrupled. Its period changes how?
**Answer: It doubles (period goes with √l).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A pendulum has l = 1.0 m and T = 2.0 s. Find g.
**Answer: g = 4π²l/T² = 4π² × 1.0 ÷ 4.0 ≈ 9.87 m/s².**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** On a spring, a heavier mass bounces — faster or slower?
**Answer: Slower — T grows with √m.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why time 20 swings instead of 1?
**Answer: Dividing the total by 20 shrinks the timing error per swing.**
