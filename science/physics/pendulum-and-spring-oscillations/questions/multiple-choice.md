# Pendulum and spring — timing oscillations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A pendulum's length is quadrupled. Its period changes how?
a) It doubles (period goes with √l).  b) g = 4π²l/T² = 4π² × 1.0 ÷ 4.0 ≈ 9.87 m/s².  c) Slower — T grows with √m.  d) Dividing the total by 20 shrinks the timing error per swing.
**Answer: a) It doubles (period goes with √l).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A pendulum has l = 1.0 m and T = 2.0 s. Find g.
a) It doubles (period goes with √l).  b) g = 4π²l/T² = 4π² × 1.0 ÷ 4.0 ≈ 9.87 m/s².  c) Slower — T grows with √m.  d) Dividing the total by 20 shrinks the timing error per swing.
**Answer: b) g = 4π²l/T² = 4π² × 1.0 ÷ 4.0 ≈ 9.87 m/s².**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** On a spring, a heavier mass bounces — faster or slower?
