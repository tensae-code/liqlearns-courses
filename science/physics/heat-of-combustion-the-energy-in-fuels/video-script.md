# Video script — Heat of combustion — the energy in fuels

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **a fuel is a store of energy that burning unlocks, and fuels are priced by one number — the specific heat of combustion, the heat released by burning one kilogram completely: Q = qm, the figure that lets a kilogram of petrol, gas, wood or coal be compared honestly**. Combustion is a chemical reaction with oxygen that turns the fuel's stored chemical energy into heat (and gases that carry some away). Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — burning releases what the fuel stores; specific heat of combustion — heat per kilogram; measuring it, and choosing fuels by it — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
