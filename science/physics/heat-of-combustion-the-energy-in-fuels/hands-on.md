# Hands-on — Heat of combustion — the energy in fuels

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with your hands
Heat 200 g of water in a metal can with a small spirit burner (or candle) whose mass you weigh before and after two minutes of burning. From the water's temperature rise, find the heat it gained; from the burner's lost mass, estimate q for the fuel. Your value will fall short of the table's — hunt the missing heat: the can, the air, the flame's own gases. Naming the losses is the experiment's real result.
