# Heat of combustion — the energy in fuels — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) The specific heat of combustion is the heat released when…
a) 1 kg of fuel burns completely  b) Any amount of fuel warms up  c) Water boils  d) 1 kg of water cools by 1 °C
**Answer: a) 1 kg of fuel burns completely**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The unit of specific heat of combustion is…
a) J  b) J/kg  c) J/(kg·°C)  d) kg/J
**Answer: b) J/kg**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Burning mass m of a fuel with specific heat of combustion q releases…
a) Q = q ÷ m  b) Q = qm  c) Q = m ÷ q  d) Q = q + m
**Answer: b) Q = qm**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Petrol's q is roughly…
a) 4 × 10³ J/kg  b) 4 × 10⁷ J/kg  c) 4 × 10¹⁰ J/kg  d) Exactly zero
**Answer: b) 4 × 10⁷ J/kg**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A school measurement of q comes out too small because…
a) Water lies  b) Some of the heat escapes to the can, the air and the exhaust gases instead of the water  c) The fuel is shy  d) Scales are wrong
**Answer: b) Some of the heat escapes to the can, the air and the exhaust gases instead of the water**

**6.** (Sub lesson 1) Combustion turns the fuel's stored ___ energy into heat.
a) Kinetic  b) Chemical  c) Nuclear  d) Elastic
**Answer: b) Chemical**
