# Heat of combustion — the energy in fuels — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) The heat from mass m of fuel is Q = ___ × m.
**Answer:** q

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Specific heat of combustion is measured in joules per ___.
**Answer:** kilogram

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Burning is a ___ reaction of the fuel with oxygen.
**Answer:** chemical

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A calorimeter measures q by heating a known mass of ___.
**Answer:** water
