# Measuring the volume of a gas — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Unlike a solid, which keeps its own shape and volume, and a liquid, which keeps its volume but takes its container's shape, a gas expands until it fills the whole space available to it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** You measure a gas by the space it fills is the opposite of what this lesson teaches.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches this as stated.**

**3.** Because gas particles are far apart, a gas can be compressed into a smaller volume and will spring back out when released.
**Answer: True**

**4.** This lesson's topic plays no part in science at all.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
