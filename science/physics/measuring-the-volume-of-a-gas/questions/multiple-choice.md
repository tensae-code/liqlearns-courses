# Measuring the volume of a gas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about measuring the volume of a gas?
a) A gas has no volume of its own — it spreads out to fill the whole of whatever holds it, so the volume of a gas is the volume of its container.

**2.** What does this lesson say about a gas fills its container?
