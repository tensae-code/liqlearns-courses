# Measuring the volume of a gas — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (a gas has no volume of its own — it spreads out to fill the whole of whatever holds it, so the volume of a gas is the volume of its container.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
