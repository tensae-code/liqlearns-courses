# Hands-on — Resolving and combining forces — resultant and the parallelogram law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two ropes, one pull
Tie two spring balances (or two people's hands pulling steadily) to one ring at an angle, and a third balance pulling the ring back to its starting mark. Compare the third reading with the parallelogram prediction from the first two. Say aloud: the ring answers to one resultant — the third balance is measuring exactly that.

## Resolve your own weight on a slope
Stand on a ramp or lean a board and feel: part of you presses into the board, part slides you down it. State the two components of your weight for a 30° slope as fractions of mg (sin 30° = 0.5 down, cos 30° ≈ 0.87 in). Say aloud: the steeper the slope, the more of me is in the sliding part.
