# Resolving and combining forces — resultant and the parallelogram law — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The component of F at angle θ across the chosen direction is F _____ θ.
**Answer: sin**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The resultant of two forces is the _____ of the parallelogram they form.
**Answer: diagonal**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Forces at 180° to each other give a resultant equal to the _____ of their sizes.
**Answer: difference**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The sums of resolved components are written ΣFx and _____.
**Answer: ΣFy**
