# Resolving and combining forces — resultant and the parallelogram law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A force F at angle θ to a direction has component along that direction…
a) F sin θ  b) F cos θ  c) F tan θ  d) F
**Answer: b) F cos θ**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Resolving a force into perpendicular components…
a) Changes its effect  b) Loses the part at an angle  c) Replaces it exactly — the pair has the same effect  d) Works only on slopes
**Answer: c) Replaces it exactly — the pair has the same effect**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Forces 6 N and 8 N at right angles have resultant…
a) 14 N  b) 2 N  c) 10 N  d) 48 N
**Answer: c) 10 N**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In the formula R = √(A² + B² + 2AB cos θ), θ is the angle…
a) Of the resultant  b) Between the two forces placed tail to tail  c) To the vertical  d) Of each force to the diagonal
**Answer: b) Between the two forces placed tail to tail**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) When combining many forces by components, you first…
a) Draw many parallelograms  b) Resolve every force and sum all x- and all y-components  c) Add all the sizes  d) Ignore the small forces
**Answer: b) Resolve every force and sum all x- and all y-components**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) If ΣFx = 0 and ΣFy = 0, the resultant is…
a) The largest force  b) Zero — the forces balance  c) The sum of all forces  d) Undefined
**Answer: b) Zero — the forces balance**
