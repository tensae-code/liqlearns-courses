# Resolving and combining forces — resultant and the parallelogram law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is weight on a slope resolved along and into the slope, rather than horizontally and vertically?
**Answer: Because the slope's surface controls the motion: the component along the slope (mg sin θ) is what accelerates or must be held, and the component into the slope (mg cos θ) sets the normal reaction and friction — those are the directions the problem actually asks about.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Two forces of 50 N each act at 120° between them. Find the resultant.
**Answer: R = √(50² + 50² + 2·50·50·cos 120°) = √(2500 + 2500 − 2500) = √2500 = 50 N — equal forces at 120° give a resultant equal to each of them, directed along their bisector.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) State the parallelogram law in words.
**Answer: If two forces acting at a point are represented in size and direction by the two sides of a parallelogram drawn from that point, their resultant is represented by the diagonal of the parallelogram through that point.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A resultant comes out at tan φ = ΣFy/ΣFx with ΣFx negative and ΣFy positive. Why does the calculator's angle alone mislead?
**Answer: The tangent repeats every 180°, so the inverse tangent gives an angle in the wrong quadrant unless the signs of the components are used to place φ — here the resultant points into the second quadrant (backward and sideways), not the fourth.**
