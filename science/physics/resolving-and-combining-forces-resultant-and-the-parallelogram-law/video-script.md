# Video script — Resolving and combining forces — resultant and the parallelogram law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **one force doing two jobs, two forces acting as one**. Tilt a force and it splits — cosine along, sine across — nothing lost. Put two forces tail to tail, build their parallelogram, and the diagonal is the single resultant that replaces them both: R equals the square root of A squared plus B squared plus 2AB cos theta. For a crowd of forces, resolve them all, add the pieces each way, and combine once. If the pieces sum to zero, the forces balance.
