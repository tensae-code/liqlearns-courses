# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Bulgarian Grade 9 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two covers, one course
Lay the two Grade 9 parts side by side — or picture them. On Part 1, find the big numeral, then find the subtitle under it and read what the subtitle actually says. On Part 2, find where its lessons begin and what its entry test assumes you already know. Write one sentence of verdict: which book is which grade, and what evidence settled it. This is the identity check the whole course depends on — covers over file labels, every time.
