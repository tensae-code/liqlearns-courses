# How this Physics course works — the Bulgarian Grade 9 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why must Part 1 and Part 2 be read as one course?
**Answer: Part 2's entry test and contents assume Part 1's mechanics and heat; alone, neither part is the whole Grade 9 course.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A record labels Part 1 as Grade 8. What is the correct citation, and why?
**Answer: Grade 9 Part 1 — because the book's own cover subtitle prints «Първа част за 9. клас»; the printed identity outranks the file label.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How does a laboratory lesson inside the sequence differ from an appendix practical?
**Answer: It is met on schedule as a numbered lesson type, so measuring is part of the course's rhythm, not an optional extra at the end.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Where do Part 2's glossary and answers sit?
**Answer: The glossary at printed p. 125 and the answers at printed p. 128.**
