# How this Physics course works — the Bulgarian Grade 9 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The two Grade 9 parts together carry…
a) Two different grades  b) One Grade 9 course — Part 1 mechanics and heat, Part 2 electricity, oscillations, waves and laboratories  c) A Grade 8 and a Grade 9 course  d) A student book and a teacher guide
**Answer: b) One Grade 9 course — Part 1 mechanics and heat, Part 2 electricity, oscillations, waves and laboratories**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Part 1's printed identity is settled by…
a) The file label  b) The big cover numeral alone  c) The cover's own subtitle, «Първа част за 9. клас» — Part 1 for Grade 9  d) Its page count
**Answer: c) The cover's own subtitle, «Първа част за 9. клас» — Part 1 for Grade 9**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) At Grade 9, laboratory works sit…
a) Only in an appendix  b) Inside the numbered lesson sequence itself  c) In a separate book  d) Only in Part 2
**Answer: b) Inside the numbered lesson sequence itself**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The lesson types in the Bulgarian design are…
a) Reading and writing only  b) New knowledge, problem-solving, laboratory work, summary, and "I know and I can" self-checks  c) Lecture and exam  d) Theory and homework
**Answer: b) New knowledge, problem-solving, laboratory work, summary, and "I know and I can" self-checks**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) An entry-test miss tells you…
a) To skip the part  b) Exactly which numbered lesson teaches what you missed  c) That the test was unfair  d) Nothing until the exit test
**Answer: b) Exactly which numbered lesson teaches what you missed**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Answers at the back are used…
a) Before attempting  b) Instead of attempting  c) Only after your own attempt is written down  d) Never
**Answer: c) Only after your own attempt is written down**
