# Hands-on — Chemical cells — the simple cell and the dry cell

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two plates and a fading lamp
Stand zinc and copper strips in a beaker of dilute acid (teacher handles the acid; eye protection on) and connect a lamp or LED through a switch. Note the brightness the moment the circuit closes, then watch for five minutes and note it again. Rest the cell with the switch open for five minutes, close the switch, and note what returns. Stirring the acid gently or wiping the copper plate revives it at once. Write the two faults you have just seen — polarisation for the fading, and (from the zinc's pitted surface afterwards) local action — and beside each, the cure the dry cell builds in.
