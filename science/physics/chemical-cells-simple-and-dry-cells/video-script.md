Today: **chemical cells — the simple cell and the dry cell**. Two different metals in an acid make a push of about a volt — and then two faults steal it: hydrogen bubbles blanket the copper, and impure zinc eats itself. The dry cell in your torch is the same cell with both faults engineered out and its acid turned to paste. Chemistry in, electricity out.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
