# Chemical cells — the simple cell and the dry cell — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The liquid (or paste) that acts chemically on the plates of a cell is called the _____.
**Answer: electrolyte**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The fading of a simple cell as hydrogen blankets its copper plate is called _____.
**Answer: polarisation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Local action is cured by using pure zinc or by coating the zinc with mercury, called _____.
**Answer: amalgamation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The depolariser packed around the dry cell's carbon rod is _____ dioxide.
**Answer: manganese**
