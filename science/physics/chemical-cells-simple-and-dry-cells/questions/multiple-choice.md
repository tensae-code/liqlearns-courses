# Chemical cells — the simple cell and the dry cell — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A chemical cell turns…
**Answer: a) chemical energy into electrical energy  b) electrical energy into light only  c) heat into chemical energy  d) magnetism into electricity**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In the simple cell, the positive terminal is the…
**Answer: a) copper plate  b) zinc plate  c) acid surface  d) connecting wire**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The simple (zinc–copper) cell's e.m.f. is about…
**Answer: a) 1.1 V  b) 12 V  c) 0.1 V  d) 230 V**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Polarisation is caused by…
**Answer: a) hydrogen bubbles gathering on the copper plate  b) the zinc dissolving too fast  c) the acid evaporating  d) the wires heating up**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A depolariser's job is to…
**Answer: a) remove the hydrogen before it blankets the plate  b) make the acid stronger  c) cool the cell  d) thicken the zinc plate**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In a dry cell, the negative terminal is…
**Answer: a) the zinc case itself  b) the carbon rod  c) the brass cap  d) the paste**
