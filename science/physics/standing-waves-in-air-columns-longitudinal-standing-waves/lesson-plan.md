# Lesson plan — Standing waves in air columns — longitudinal standing waves

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** Standing waves in air columns — longitudinal standing waves
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18) — a new lesson built for the Syria placement named in lesson/sources.md
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** how sound stands still inside a pipe — a **longitudinal standing wave** with nodes where the air cannot move and antinodes where it swings widest, giving a **closed pipe** only odd harmonics (fundamental λ = 4L) and an **open pipe** every harmonic (fundamental λ = 2L)**
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Earlier lessons make a good preparation and are recommended, never forced

## Sub lessons
1. **Longitudinal standing waves — the pattern** — Sound in air is **longitudinal**: particles oscillate back and forth along the direction the wave travels.
2. **The closed pipe — odd harmonics only** — A pipe closed at one end forces a **displacement node at the closed end** (the air cannot move there) and an **antinode at the open end**.
3. **The open pipe — every harmonic** — A pipe open at both ends has a **displacement antinode at each open end**.
