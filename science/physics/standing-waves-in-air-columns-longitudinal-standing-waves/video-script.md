# Video script — Standing waves in air columns — longitudinal standing waves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **sound standing still**. Blow across a bottle and it sings one note — the air inside is not travelling, it is standing: a longitudinal standing wave, particles jiggling back and forth, frozen at the nodes and swinging widest at the antinodes. A pipe closed at one end fits a quarter wavelength in its fundamental and sings only odd harmonics. A pipe open at both ends fits a half wavelength and sings them all. That is why organ pipes and clarinets obey different arithmetic.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
