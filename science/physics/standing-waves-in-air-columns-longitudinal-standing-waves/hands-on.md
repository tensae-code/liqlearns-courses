# Hands-on — Standing waves in air columns — longitudinal standing waves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The bottle and the tube
Blow steadily across the mouth of an empty bottle (a closed pipe — closed by its base) and note the pitch. Add water a little at a time: the air column shortens, and the pitch rises, exactly as f = v/4L says. Then blow across a tube open at both ends of about the bottle's length and compare — the open pipe sings higher. Say aloud: the ends decide which wavelengths may stand.
