# Standing waves in air columns — longitudinal standing waves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a standing wave, the nodes are places where…
a) The air moves widest  b) The air does not move  c) The wave travels fastest  d) The pressure is zero always
**Answer: b) The air does not move**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The distance from a node to the next antinode is…
a) λ  b) λ/2  c) λ/4  d) 2λ
**Answer: c) λ/4**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A closed pipe's fundamental wavelength is…
a) 2L  b) L  c) 4L  d) L/4
**Answer: c) 4L**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A closed pipe sounds…
a) All harmonics  b) Only even harmonics  c) Only odd harmonics  d) Only the fundamental
**Answer: c) Only odd harmonics**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) An open pipe's fundamental wavelength is…
a) 4L  b) 2L  c) L  d) L/2
**Answer: b) 2L**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) For pipes of equal length, the open pipe's fundamental is…
a) Half the closed pipe's  b) The same  c) Twice the closed pipe's  d) Four times the closed pipe's
**Answer: c) Twice the closed pipe's**
