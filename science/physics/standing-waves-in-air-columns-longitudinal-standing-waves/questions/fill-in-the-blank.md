# Standing waves in air columns — longitudinal standing waves — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a standing wave, particles at a _____ never move.
**Answer: node**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A closed pipe's fundamental wavelength equals _____ times its length.
**Answer: four**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) An open pipe has a displacement _____ at each open end.
**Answer: antinode**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The open pipe sounds the complete _____ series.
**Answer: harmonic**
