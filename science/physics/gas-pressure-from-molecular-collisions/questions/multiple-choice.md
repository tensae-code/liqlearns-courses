# Gas pressure — pressure from molecular collisions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Gas pressure on a container's walls is caused by…
a) The gas's weight only  b) Molecules colliding with the walls, billions of times a second  c) Static electricity  d) The container squeezing itself
**Answer: b) Molecules colliding with the walls, billions of times a second**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Gas particles move…
a) Only downward  b) Slowly in circles  c) Freely in all directions at high speed  d) Not at all until shaken
**Answer: c) Freely in all directions at high speed**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Squeezing a gas into half the volume (same temperature) makes its pressure…
a) Halve  b) Stay the same  c) Rise — the particles hit the walls more often  d) Vanish
**Answer: c) Rise — the particles hit the walls more often**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Heating a sealed gas raises its pressure because…
a) Particles grow bigger  b) Particles move faster and hit harder and more often  c) The container shrinks  d) Air is created
**Answer: b) Particles move faster and hit harder and more often**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Gas pressure pushes…
a) Only downward  b) Only sideways  c) In all directions at once  d) Only toward the opening
**Answer: c) In all directions at once**

**6.** (Sub lesson 1) The Armenian Grade 7 book explains gas pressure…
a) By a formula first  b) Molecularly — from particle collisions — in its own paragraph  c) Not at all  d) As a kind of liquid pressure
**Answer: b) Molecularly — from particle collisions — in its own paragraph**
