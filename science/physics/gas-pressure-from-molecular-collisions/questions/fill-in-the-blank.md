# Gas pressure — pressure from molecular collisions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Gas pressure is the summed effect of molecular ___ with the walls.
**Answer:** collisions

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Gas particles fly ___ in all directions — they never rest.
**Answer:** freely (fast)

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Halving a gas's volume makes its particles hit the walls more ___.
**Answer:** often

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Heating a gas makes its particles move ___, so pressure rises.
**Answer:** faster
