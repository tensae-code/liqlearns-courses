# Gas pressure — pressure from molecular collisions — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — The Grade 7 book gives gas pressure a paragraph of its own because a gas is not a quiet filling: its molecules fly freely in every direction at hundreds of metres per second, crossing the container and striking its walls again and again; One molecular hit is nothing; the pressure we feel is the sum of an uncountable rain of hits on every square centimetre of wall, so steady that it reads as a smooth push; Halve the volume and the same particles cross a smaller space, hitting the walls more often: pressure rises — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
