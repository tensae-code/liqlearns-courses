# Video script — Gas pressure — pressure from molecular collisions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **a gas pushes on its container because its particles never stop flying: billions of molecular collisions with the walls every second add up to the steady push we call gas pressure — squeeze the gas or heat it and the collisions answer at once**. The Grade 7 book gives gas pressure a paragraph of its own because a gas is not a quiet filling: its molecules fly freely in every direction at hundreds of metres per second, crossing the container and striking its walls again and again. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — gas particles never rest; pressure is the collisions, added up; squeeze it or heat it — the collisions answer — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
