# Hands-on — Gas pressure — pressure from molecular collisions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with your hands
Take a bicycle pump or a syringe and seal the outlet with a finger. Push the piston slowly: the air inside resists more and more, and pushes back when you let go. You are feeling the collisions multiply as the same particles are crowded into less space. Now try to pull the piston out from the sealed, pushed-in position — the outside air's pressure fights you. Both directions of the fight are particle collisions, counted in billions.
