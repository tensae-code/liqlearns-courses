# Hands-on — Teaching the Lao Grade 12 (ມ.7) year — a ten-part final-year overview for teachers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Overview your own year
Take the largest course or book you teach or study and write its one-arc overview the Lao way: a single paragraph walking start to finish, naming the two or three places where the content changes character, plus the hours you actually have for it. Pin it where you plan lessons from. If your overview cannot be written, the guide's lesson-by-lesson help will be working without a map — the gap this introduction exists to close.
