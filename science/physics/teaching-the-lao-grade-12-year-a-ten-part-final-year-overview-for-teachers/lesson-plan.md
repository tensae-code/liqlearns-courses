## Sub lessons
1. **The overview — one arc for the year** — The guide's content overview (general introduction, printed pp. 2–4) walks the year's ten parts as a single arc before any lesson guidance begins: rotation and oscillation, mechanical waves and sound, the magnetic field and alternating current, electromagnetic and light waves, atomic physics and quantum mechanics, nuclear physics. It names the year's strands in English inside the Lao text — the Doppler effect, scattering of light, Quantum Mechanics — marking the points where the final year reaches modern physics. A teacher reads the overview first and holds the whole year in view while teaching any one lesson.
2. **Aims, hours and the series' method** — The introduction also states the subject's character and aims and the hours table for ມ.5–ມ.7 — the final year carries the heaviest load of the series (ມ.7 at 3 hours a week, against 2 hours in ມ.5/ມ.6, as the Grade 11 guide's timetable states) — and it carries the series' learner-centred teaching method, with classroom-activity photographs: observation, experiment, discussion and demonstration as the stated routes to knowledge (stocked in this course's 'Doing science honestly' lesson). The overview is therefore a pacing instrument as well as a map: arc, hours and method on the first pages.
3. **Guidance for thirty lessons, answers at the back** — After the introduction, the guide gives per-lesson guidance for all 30 lessons (guide printed pp. 10–242, in the guide's own pagination of the same 10 parts / 30 lessons as the student book), and closes with an answers/guidance section beginning at p. 247 and running to the file's end at 257 pages. The structure is the series' system at its largest scale: overview first, lesson guidance throughout, answers at the back — the same inventory-and-keys apparatus the Grade 10 guide uses, here spanning the whole of modern-physics school content.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
