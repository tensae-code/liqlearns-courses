# Energy in simple harmonic motion — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) At the centre of the swing the energy is all _____.
**Answer: kinetic**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A stretched spring stores _____ potential energy.
**Answer: elastic**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) With no friction, the total energy of an oscillator is _____.
**Answer: conserved (constant)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) For a spring oscillator the total energy is E = ½k_____².
**Answer: A (amplitude)**
