# Energy in simple harmonic motion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) At the extreme of its swing, a pendulum bob has…
**Answer: a) all potential energy and no kinetic energy  b) all kinetic energy  c) no energy at all  d) equal kinetic and potential energy**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A pendulum moves fastest…
**Answer: a) at the centre of its swing  b) at the extremes  c) halfway up each side  d) it moves at one speed throughout**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) In a mass on a spring, the potential store is…
**Answer: a) elastic potential energy in the stretched or squeezed spring  b) gravitational energy only  c) heat in the spring  d) the mass's kinetic energy**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) With no friction, the total energy of an oscillator…
**Answer: a) stays the same at every point  b) grows each period  c) is zero at the centre  d) doubles at the extremes**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Doubling a spring oscillator's amplitude multiplies its total energy by…
**Answer: a) four  b) two  c) one — it is unchanged  d) eight**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) In one full period, the kinetic energy reaches its peak…
**Answer: a) twice  b) once  c) four times  d) never — it is constant**
