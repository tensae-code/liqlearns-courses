# Hands-on — Energy in simple harmonic motion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Follow the energy on a swing
Watch a playground swing or a pendulum you hang from a string. At the top of each swing the bob stops for an instant — all potential, no kinetic. Through the bottom it moves fastest — all kinetic. Mark the height at the extremes and check: swing after swing (before friction steals much), the bob climbs back to nearly the same height, because the total energy is conserved. Then let it run for a minute and watch the extremes shrink — the leak is friction and air resistance doing negative work.
