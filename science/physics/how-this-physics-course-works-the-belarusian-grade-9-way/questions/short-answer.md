# How this Physics course works — the Belarusian Grade 9 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does the Grade 9 book add before Ch.1 that Grades 7–8 did not have?
**Answer: An Introduction on matter, space and time and on scalar and vector quantities — the formal language the mechanics year runs on.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the four moves of the study method the frontispiece teaches.
**Answer: Quick read; deep read; reproduce every derivation in your notebook; repeat the experiments at home.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) List the Grade 9 chapters in order.
**Answer: Introduction; Ch.1 Kinematics; Ch.2 Dynamics; Ch.3 Conservation laws; Ch.4 Laboratory experiment.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Where does the Grade 9 laboratory chapter sit, and where are its answers?
**Answer: Ch.4 Laboratory experiment opens at printed p. 183; the answers to the exercises are at the back, from printed p. 209.**
