# How this Physics course works — the Belarusian Grade 9 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Grade 9 book's frontispiece and Introduction sit at…
**Answer: a) printed p. 5  b) printed p. 50  c) the back cover  d) Ch.4**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The series design carried into Grade 9 includes…
**Answer: a) «Main conclusions» and control questions closing every paragraph  b) no questions at all  c) ungraded exercises  d) no laboratory work**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Optional material beyond the core is marked by…
**Answer: a) small print  b) a star  c) a new chapter  d) the appendix**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The star in this book marks…
**Answer: a) the hardest exercises  b) the laboratory chapter  c) the Introduction  d) the answers section**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Grade 9's physics is, in one word…
**Answer: a) mechanics — kinematics, dynamics, conservation laws  b) optics  c) thermodynamics  d) electronics**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Before Kinematics, the Introduction establishes…
**Answer: a) matter, space and time, and scalars and vectors  b) lenses  c) circuits  d) heat engines**
