# How far, how high — the range and height formulas — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Range R = v₀² sin 2θ / g (greatest at 45°) and height H = v₀² sin²θ / 2g, their relation H = R tanθ / 4.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) And a fully worked 40 m/s at 37° example (H ≈ 28.8 m, R ≈ 153.8 m).**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) And a fully worked 80 m/s at 37° example (H ≈ 28.8 m, R ≈ 153.8 m).**
**Answer: False**
