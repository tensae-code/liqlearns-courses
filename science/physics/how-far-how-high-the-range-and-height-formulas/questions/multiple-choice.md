# How far, how high — the range and height formulas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about range and maximum height formulas is correct?**
a) Range R = v₀² sin 2θ / g (greatest at 45°) and height H = v₀² sin²θ / 2g, their relation H = R tanθ / 4.  b) The summary runs Newton's laws, friction, work–energy, and momentum; end questions include inertia vs mass, effects of…  c) The strong nuclear force is a very short-range attraction between nucleons strong enough to beat proton repulsion; the…
**Answer: a) Range R = v₀² sin 2θ / g (greatest at 45°) and height H = v₀² sin²θ / 2g, their relation H = R tanθ / 4.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ______ R = v₀² sin 2θ / g (greatest at 45°) and height H = v₀² sin²θ / 2g, their relation H = R tanθ / 4.**
a) range  b) momentum  c) series
**Answer: a) range**
