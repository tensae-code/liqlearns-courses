## Sub lessons
1. **The range formula** — Range R = v₀² sin 2θ / g (greatest at 45°) and height H = v₀² sin²θ / 2g, their relation H = R tanθ / 4.
2. **Maximum height — and how height and range join** — And a fully worked 40 m/s at 37° example (H ≈ 28.8 m, R ≈ 153.8 m).
