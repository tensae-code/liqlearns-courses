# What temperature change does — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What do most materials do when heated?
a) Expand (and contract when cooled).  b) Any of: changed growth, activity, or health.  c) No — matter and life respond to it.  d) The metal expands when heated.
**Answer: a) Expand (and contract when cooled).**

**2.** Name one effect of temperature change on living things.
a) Expand (and contract when cooled).  b) Any of: changed growth, activity, or health.  c) No — matter and life respond to it.  d) The metal expands when heated.
**Answer: b) Any of: changed growth, activity, or health.**

**3.** Is temperature change only a number moving?
a) Expand (and contract when cooled).  b) Any of: changed growth, activity, or health.  c) No — matter and life respond to it.  d) The metal expands when heated.
**Answer: c) No — matter and life respond to it.**

**4.** A cold metal lid on a jar loosens under hot water. Why?
a) Expand (and contract when cooled).  b) Any of: changed growth, activity, or health.  c) No — matter and life respond to it.  d) The metal expands when heated.
**Answer: d) The metal expands when heated.**
