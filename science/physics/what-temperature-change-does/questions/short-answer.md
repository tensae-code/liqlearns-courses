# What temperature change does — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** What do most materials do when heated?
**Answer: Expand (and contract when cooled).**

**2.** Name one effect of temperature change on living things.
**Answer: Any of: changed growth, activity, or health.**

**3.** Is temperature change only a number moving?
**Answer: No — matter and life respond to it.**

**4.** A cold metal lid on a jar loosens under hot water. Why?
**Answer: The metal expands when heated.**
