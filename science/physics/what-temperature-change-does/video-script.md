# Video script — What temperature change does

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Heating and cooling change matter and change environments.
A rising temperature in the environment has causes we can name and effects we can watch; materials respond to temperature change — most visibly by expanding when heated and contracting when cooled, the bridge into this unit's last topic.
Living things respond too: growth, activity and health all shift with temperature.
Temperature change is never just a number moving.

Remember: **Heating and cooling change matter, environments and living things.**
Remember: **Most materials expand when heated and contract when cooled.**
Remember: **Environmental temperature rise has nameable causes and effects.**
