# What temperature change does

## Do it first
Watch a cold glass 'sweat' in warm air and a balloon shrink in a cold place. Two materials answering temperature change in front of you.

[Picture 21866 — heated rail, warming room and rising thermometer in one strip]

## What temperature change does — the teaching
Heating and cooling change matter and change environments. A rising temperature in the environment has causes we can name and effects we can watch; materials respond to temperature change — most visibly by expanding when heated and contracting when cooled, the bridge into this unit's last topic. Living things respond too: growth, activity and health all shift with temperature. Temperature change is never just a number moving.

## The key ideas

- Heating and cooling change matter, environments and living things.
- Most materials expand when heated and contract when cooled.
- Environmental temperature rise has nameable causes and effects.

## Say it to your companion
Explain what temperature change does to your companion in your own words. Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
