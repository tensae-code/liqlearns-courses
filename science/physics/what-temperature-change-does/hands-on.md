# Hands-on — What temperature change does

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Watch a cold glass 'sweat' in warm air and a balloon shrink in a cold place. Two materials answering temperature change in front of you.

## Apply it where you live
Collect three local effects of temperature change and explain each in one line.
