# Gravitational waves and how we detect them — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Gravitational waves travel at the speed of ___.
**Answer:** light

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A wave stretches one direction while ___ the perpendicular.
**Answer:** squeezing

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) LIGO's arms form a laser ___.
**Answer:** interferometer

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Two ___ far apart confirm a real event against local noise.
**Answer:** detectors
