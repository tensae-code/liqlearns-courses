# Gravitational waves and how we detect them — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) What is strain, in this context?
**Answer:** The fractional change in length a passing wave causes.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) How does an interferometer turn a length change into a signal?
**Answer:** The arms' differing lengths shift the interference pattern of the recombined light.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why must the arms be kilometres long?
**Answer:** The absolute length change grows with arm length, lifting it toward measurability.

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the strongest sources of gravitational waves.
**Answer:** Spiralling, merging pairs of black holes or neutron stars.
