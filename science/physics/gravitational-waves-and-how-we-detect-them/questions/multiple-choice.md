# Gravitational waves and how we detect them — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Gravitational waves are ripples in…
a) Air  b) Space-time itself  c) Water only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Space-time itself**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) They are radiated by…
a) Any stationary mass  b) Accelerating masses  c) Electric charges only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Accelerating masses**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A passing wave makes distances…
a) Grow in all directions at once  b) Stretch in one direction and squeeze in the perpendicular one, alternately  c) Freeze  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Stretch in one direction and squeeze in the perpendicular one, alternately**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The strain reaching Earth from a distant merger is about…
a) One metre per kilometre  b) 10⁻²¹ — far smaller than an atom over kilometres  c) 10%  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) 10⁻²¹ — far smaller than an atom over kilometres**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) LIGO detects the waves with…
a) Radio dishes  b) Laser interferometers with kilometre arms  c) Seismometers only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Laser interferometers with kilometre arms**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The first detected signal, in 2015, came from…
a) The Moon's orbit  b) Two merging black holes  c) A laboratory mass  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Two merging black holes**
