# Hands-on — Gravitational waves and how we detect them

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Model the measurement's audacity with numbers
Model the measurement's audacity with numbers: take a 4 km arm and a strain of 10⁻²¹ and compute the length change; compare it with an atom's diameter from your book. Then model the stretch-squeeze with a grid drawn on a balloon's skin, inflated unevenly by a partner's squeeze, and describe what a ruler drawn on the grid would report.
