# Video script — Gravitational waves and how we detect them

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **accelerating masses ripple space-time itself, and the ripples — gravitational waves — travel at the speed of light; laser interferometers like LIGO catch them by measuring length changes thousands of times smaller than an atomic nucleus**. Just as an accelerated charge radiates electromagnetic waves, an accelerated mass radiates gravitational waves — disturbances of space-time's geometry travelling outward at c. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — ripples from accelerated masses; what a passing wave does; interferometers — hearing space breathe — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.
