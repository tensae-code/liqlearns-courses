Coulomb's law measures the force between two point charges: F = kq₁q₂/r².
The force grows with the product of the charges and falls with the square of their separation — double the distance and the force drops to a quarter.
Like charges repel, unlike charges attract; the law gives the force's size, the signs give its direction.
It is the electrostatic twin of the gravity law's shape: a product on top, a square below.

Remember: **F = kq₁q₂/r² — force grows with charge product, falls with distance squared.**
Remember: **Double the distance → force falls to a quarter.**
Remember: **Like charges repel; unlike charges attract.**
Keep the formula: **F = kq₁q₂ / r²**
