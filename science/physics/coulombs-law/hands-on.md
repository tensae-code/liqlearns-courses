# Hands-on — Coulomb's law — how strong the charge force is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Bring two rubbed balloons near each other and watch them push apart; move them slowly closer and feel the push grow — Coulomb's law tightening with distance, in your hands.

## Apply it where you live
Calculate the force between two stated charges at two distances and compare, showing the r² effect.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21901 — two charges with force arrows growing and shrinking as distance changes] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using F = kq₁q₂ / r²: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
