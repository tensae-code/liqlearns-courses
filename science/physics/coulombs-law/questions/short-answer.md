# Coulomb's law — how strong the charge force is — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Two charges are moved twice as far apart. The force becomes — ?
**Answer: A quarter of what it was (1/2²).**

**2.** One charge doubles, distance unchanged. Force?
**Answer: It doubles.**

**3.** Both charges double and distance doubles. Net change?
**Answer: Force × (2×2)/(2²) = unchanged.**

**4.** Charges +3 units and −2 units: attract or repel, and proportional to what product?
**Answer: Attract; the force is proportional to 3 × 2 = 6 units of product.**
