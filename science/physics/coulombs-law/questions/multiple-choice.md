# Coulomb's law — how strong the charge force is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Two charges are moved twice as far apart. The force becomes — ?
a) A quarter of what it was (1/2²).  b) It doubles.  c) Force × (2×2)/(2²) = unchanged.  d) Attract; the force is proportional to 3 × 2 = 6 units of product.
**Answer: a) A quarter of what it was (1/2²).**

**2.** One charge doubles, distance unchanged. Force?
a) A quarter of what it was (1/2²).  b) It doubles.  c) Force × (2×2)/(2²) = unchanged.  d) Attract; the force is proportional to 3 × 2 = 6 units of product.
**Answer: b) It doubles.**

**3.** Both charges double and distance doubles. Net change?
a) A quarter of what it was (1/2²).  b) It doubles.  c) Force × (2×2)/(2²) = unchanged.  d) Attract; the force is proportional to 3 × 2 = 6 units of product.
**Answer: c) Force × (2×2)/(2²) = unchanged.**

**4.** Charges +3 units and −2 units: attract or repel, and proportional to what product?
a) A quarter of what it was (1/2²).  b) It doubles.  c) Force × (2×2)/(2²) = unchanged.  d) Attract; the force is proportional to 3 × 2 = 6 units of product.
**Answer: d) Attract; the force is proportional to 3 × 2 = 6 units of product.**
