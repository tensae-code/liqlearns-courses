# Video script — Electrostatics at work — xerography and inkjet printers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the photocopier and the inkjet printer are electrostatics earning its keep**. In the photocopier a drum coated with a photoconductor is charged uniformly in the dark; light reflected from the document discharges it where the page is white, leaving a charged image where the page is dark. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — xerography — writing with charge and light; the inkjet — droplets steered by fields; one physics, two machines — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.
