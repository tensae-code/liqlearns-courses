# Electrostatics at work — xerography and inkjet printers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) List xerography's steps in order.
**Answer:** Charge the drum; expose (light discharges the white areas); develop with toner; transfer to paper; fuse with heat.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why must the drum coating be a photoconductor?
**Answer:** It must hold charge in the dark yet lose it where light falls — conductivity switched by light.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What chapter fact do both machines exploit about fields?
**Answer:** A charged body in an electric field experiences a force that can be controlled.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Name electrostatics' uninvited version of the same physics.
**Answer:** Static hazards — sparks igniting vapour, dust attraction fouling equipment.
