# Electrostatics at work — xerography and inkjet printers — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Xerography means writing with ___ and light on a drum.
**Answer:** charge

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The drum's coating is a ___ — it conducts only when lit.
**Answer:** photoconductor

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Toner is fixed to paper by ___.
**Answer:** heat (fusing)

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Inkjet droplets pass between ___ plates.
**Answer:** deflecting
