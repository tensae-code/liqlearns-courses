# Electrostatics at work — xerography and inkjet printers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Xerography's drum is first…
a) Heated red hot  b) Charged uniformly in the dark  c) Washed in ink  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Charged uniformly in the dark**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Light from the document's white areas…
a) Charges the drum more  b) Discharges the drum there, leaving the dark image charged  c) Melts the toner  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Discharges the drum there, leaving the dark image charged**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Toner sticks to…
a) The whole drum evenly  b) The still-charged parts of the drum — the image  c) The paper before transfer  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) The still-charged parts of the drum — the image**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The copy is fixed to the paper by…
a) Glue sprayed on  b) Heat fusing the toner  c) Pressing with cold rollers only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Heat fusing the toner**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) An inkjet droplet is steered by…
a) A tiny propeller  b) An electric field between deflecting plates, acting on its charge  c) Gravity alone  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) An electric field between deflecting plates, acting on its charge**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The amount of bending of a droplet is set by…
a) The paper's colour  b) Its charge (and the field)  c) The room temperature  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Its charge (and the field)**
