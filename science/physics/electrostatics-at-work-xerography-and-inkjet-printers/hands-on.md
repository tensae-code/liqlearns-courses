# Hands-on — Electrostatics at work — xerography and inkjet printers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Meet the forces with safe statics
Meet the forces with safe statics: charge a comb by running it through dry hair and use it to deflect a thin stream of water from a tap (the water is polarised and drawn) and to pick up toner-like dust (fine ash or pepper) in patterns. Map where the charged comb attracts and where discharged spots fail to — the copier's image logic in miniature.
