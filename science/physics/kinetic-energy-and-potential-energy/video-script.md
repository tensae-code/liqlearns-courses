# Video script — Kinetic energy and potential energy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Energy is the capacity to do work.
Kinetic energy is the energy of a moving body: KE = ½mv².
Double the speed and the kinetic energy grows four times, because the speed is squared — that is why fast vehicles are so much harder to stop.
Potential energy here is gravitational: the energy stored by raising a body, PE = mgh.
A raised stone holds PE quietly; let it fall and the PE trades into KE as it speeds up, until it can do work on whatever it lands on.

Remember: **Kinetic energy KE = ½mv² — the energy of motion.**
Remember: **Gravitational potential energy PE = mgh — the energy of height.**
Remember: **Falling trades PE into KE.**
Keep the formula: **KE = ½mv²; PE = mgh**
