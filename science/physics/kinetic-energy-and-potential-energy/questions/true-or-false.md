# Kinetic energy and potential energy — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kinetic energy KE = ½mv² — the energy of motion.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Gravitational potential energy PE = mgh — the energy of height.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Doubling a body's speed doubles its kinetic energy.
**Answer: False**
