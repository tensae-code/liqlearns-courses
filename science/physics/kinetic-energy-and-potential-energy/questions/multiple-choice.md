# Kinetic energy and potential energy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A 2 kg ball moves at 3 m/s. Its KE?
a) KE = ½ × 2 × 3² = 9 J.  b) Four times (speed is squared).  c) PE = 4 × 10 × 5 = 200 J.  d) PE decreases, KE increases — PE trades into KE.
**Answer: a) KE = ½ × 2 × 3² = 9 J.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Speed doubles — KE grows by what factor?
a) KE = ½ × 2 × 3² = 9 J.  b) Four times (speed is squared).  c) PE = 4 × 10 × 5 = 200 J.  d) PE decreases, KE increases — PE trades into KE.
**Answer: b) Four times (speed is squared).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A 4 kg stone sits 5 m up (g = 10 m/s²). Its PE?
a) KE = ½ × 2 × 3² = 9 J.  b) Four times (speed is squared).  c) PE = 4 × 10 × 5 = 200 J.  d) PE decreases, KE increases — PE trades into KE.
**Answer: c) PE = 4 × 10 × 5 = 200 J.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** As the stone falls, what happens to its PE and KE?
a) KE = ½ × 2 × 3² = 9 J.  b) Four times (speed is squared).  c) PE = 4 × 10 × 5 = 200 J.  d) PE decreases, KE increases — PE trades into KE.
**Answer: d) PE decreases, KE increases — PE trades into KE.**
