# Kinetic energy and potential energy — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A 2 kg ball moves at 3 m/s. Its KE?
**Answer: KE = ½ × 2 × 3² = 9 J.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Speed doubles — KE grows by what factor?
**Answer: Four times (speed is squared).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A 4 kg stone sits 5 m up (g = 10 m/s²). Its PE?
**Answer: PE = 4 × 10 × 5 = 200 J.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** As the stone falls, what happens to its PE and KE?
**Answer: PE decreases, KE increases — PE trades into KE.**
