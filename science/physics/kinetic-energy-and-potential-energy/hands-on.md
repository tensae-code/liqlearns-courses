# Hands-on — Kinetic energy and potential energy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Roll a ball slowly, then fast, into an empty plastic bottle and watch how much further the fast one knocks it. More speed, much more energy.

## Apply it where you live
Calculate the PE of a water tank load raised to a roof, choosing mass and height.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21833 — rolling ball (KE) and a raised stone above a peg (PE) side by side] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using KE = ½mv²; PE = mgh: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
