# Kinetic energy and potential energy

## Do it first
Roll a ball slowly, then fast, into an empty plastic bottle and watch how much further the fast one knocks it. More speed, much more energy.

[Picture 21833 — rolling ball (KE) and a raised stone above a peg (PE) side by side]

## Sub lesson 1 — Kinetic energy
Energy is the capacity to do work. Kinetic energy is the energy of a moving body: KE = ½mv². Double the speed and the kinetic energy grows four times, because the speed is squared — that is why fast vehicles are so much harder to stop.

**Keep the formulas:** KE = ½mv²; PE = mgh

## Sub lesson 2 — Potential energy
Potential energy here is gravitational: the energy stored by raising a body, PE = mgh. A raised stone holds PE quietly; let it fall and the PE trades into KE as it speeds up, until it can do work on whatever it lands on.

## The key ideas

- Kinetic energy KE = ½mv² — the energy of motion.
- Gravitational potential energy PE = mgh — the energy of height.
- Falling trades PE into KE.
