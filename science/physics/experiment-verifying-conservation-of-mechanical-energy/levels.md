# Experiment — verifying conservation of mechanical energy — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — A falling weight drags a ticker tape: dot positions give h fallen, dot spacings give v at each point; Conservation predicts ½mv² gained = mgh lost at every point of the fall, not only at the end; The kinetic column lands a few percent low: tape drag and air resistance convert a small share to heat; The honest verdict: within the experiment's error, mechanical energy is conserved — with the shortfall reported, not hidden — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
