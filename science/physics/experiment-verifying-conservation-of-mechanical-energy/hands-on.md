# Hands-on — Experiment — verifying conservation of mechanical energy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Put the conservation law on trial
Rig a ticker timer vertically with a tape falling through it, a 1 kg weight taped to the tape's end, and a cushion below. (1) Hold the weight at the timer, switch on, release, and catch the tape as it lands. (2) Mark the first clear dot as zero; choose four points spread down the tape; for each, measure h (distance fallen from zero) and v (spacing of the dots around the point ÷ the beat time). (3) Tabulate mgh and ½mv² side by side and compute the percentage shortfall at each point. (4) Write the verdict in the book's own form — conserved within error — and name your two largest loss or error sources with their likely sizes. Keep the tape: it is the evidence, and another team may audit your columns from it.
