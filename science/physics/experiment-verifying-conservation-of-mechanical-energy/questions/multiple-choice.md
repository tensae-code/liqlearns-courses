# Experiment — verifying conservation of mechanical energy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The falling weight's tape gives, at each point…
**Answer: a) h from dot positions and v from dot spacings  b) only the time  c) only the mass  d) the temperature**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Points very near the release are excluded because…
**Answer: a) speeds there are small and spacings cramped — poor evidence  b) the law fails there  c) the tape is blank there  d) gravity is off there**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Conservation predicts the two columns agree…
**Answer: a) at every point of the fall, not only at the end  b) only at the end  c) only at the start  d) never**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The kinetic energy at a point is computed as…
**Answer: a) ½mv²  b) mgh  c) mv  d) m/gh**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The kinetic column sits a few percent low because…
**Answer: a) tape drag and air resistance convert a small share to heat  b) the law is false  c) mass was lost  d) time runs backward**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The correct verdict sentence is…
**Answer: a) within the error the experiment allows, mechanical energy is conserved  b) the law was proved perfectly  c) the law failed  d) errors do not matter**
