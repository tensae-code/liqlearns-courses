# Gauss’s law — electric flux and the charge inside — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How much field passes through a surface is measured as electric _____.
**Answer: flux**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Gauss’s law: net flux = enclosed charge divided by _____, the permittivity of free space.
**Answer: ε₀ (epsilon-zero)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Charges _____ a closed surface contribute zero net flux through it.
**Answer: outside**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Gauss’s law finds fields directly when the charge arrangement has _____.
**Answer: symmetry**
