# Gauss’s law — electric flux and the charge inside — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Electric flux through a surface is greatest when the field…
**Answer: a) strikes it square-on (along its normal)  b) skims along it  c) is weakest  d) is changing fastest**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The flux formula for a flat area in a uniform field is…
**Answer: a) Φ = EA cos θ  b) Φ = E/A  c) Φ = EA²  d) Φ = E + A**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Gauss’s law says the net outward flux through a closed surface equals…
**Answer: a) the enclosed charge divided by ε₀  b) all charge everywhere  c) the surface area  d) zero always**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A charge outside a closed surface contributes what net flux through it?
**Answer: a) zero — its lines enter and leave again  b) q/ε₀  c) twice q/ε₀  d) it depends on its size**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Choosing a sphere around a point charge works because…
**Answer: a) the field has the same size everywhere on the sphere  b) spheres are round  c) the charge is round  d) flux is zero**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The electric field inside a hollow charged conductor is…
**Answer: a) zero — a surface inside encloses no charge  b) doubled  c) equal to the surface field  d) infinite**
