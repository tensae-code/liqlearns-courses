# Gauss’s law — electric flux and the charge inside — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A field of 100 N/C passes through 2 m² at 60° to the normal. Find the flux.
**Answer: Φ = EA cos θ = 100 × 2 × cos 60° = 100 × 2 × 0.5 = 100 N·m²/C.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State Gauss’s law in words.
**Answer: The net electric flux out of any closed surface equals the total charge enclosed by the surface divided by ε₀.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A surface encloses no charge, yet strong fields cross it from an outside charge. What is its net flux, and why?
**Answer: Zero — every field line that enters also leaves, so inward and outward flux cancel; only enclosed charge gives net flux.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) How does Gauss’s law recover Coulomb’s law for a point charge?
**Answer: On a sphere of radius r the field is uniform in size, so E × 4πr² = q/ε₀, giving E = q/(4πε₀r²) — Coulomb’s field.**
