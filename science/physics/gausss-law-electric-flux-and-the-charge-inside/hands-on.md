# Hands-on — Gauss’s law — electric flux and the charge inside

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Flux with water and a hoop
Hold a hoop under a tap: the water through it is greatest when the hoop faces the flow square-on, less when tilted, and (nearly) none when edge-on — flux in your hands. Write the three cases with the angle each time and match them to Φ = EA cos θ. Then close the idea: a sealed bag around the tap’s nozzle catches the same total flow whatever its shape — the picture of Gauss’s law.
