# General properties of matter — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A block measures 10 cm × 5 cm × 2 cm and has a mass of 400 g. State its volume, and say which property the 400 g measures.
**Answer: Volume = 10 × 5 × 2 = 100 cm³. The 400 g measures its mass — how much matter the block holds.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A diver's bell keeps its air pocket under water. Which general property keeps the water from simply taking the air's place?
**Answer: Impenetrability — the air and the water cannot occupy the same place at the same time, so the water stays out of the space the air holds.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A loaf is cut into slices, and a slice into crumbs. Which property is at work, and what stays true of every crumb?
**Answer: Divisibility — and every crumb is still matter, with its own small mass and volume and all six general properties.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A sealed syringe of air pushes in easily; a sealed syringe of water barely moves. Name the property being tested and explain the difference.
**Answer: Compressibility. The gas's particles have wide spaces between them and can close up; the liquid's particles are already close, so there is almost no room to squeeze away.**
