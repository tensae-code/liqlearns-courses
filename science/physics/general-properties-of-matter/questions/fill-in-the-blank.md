# General properties of matter — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How much matter a body holds is its _____.
**Answer: mass**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The space a body takes up is its _____.
**Answer: volume**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Two bodies can never occupy the same place at the same time — this property is called _____.
**Answer: impenetrability**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Of the three states, a _____ is the most compressible.
**Answer: gas**
