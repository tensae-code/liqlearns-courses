# General properties of matter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The mass of a body tells you…
**Answer: a) how much matter it holds  b) how much space it takes up  c) how hot it is  d) how fast it moves**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A stone's volume is best found by…
**Answer: a) the rise it makes in a measuring cylinder of water  b) weighing it  c) measuring its temperature  d) timing its fall**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A sack of feathers and a sack of stones can share volume yet differ in…
**Answer: a) mass  b) colour  c) place  d) name**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Impenetrability means…
**Answer: a) two bodies can never occupy the same place at the same time  b) matter can be cut forever  c) gases squeeze easily  d) springs come back**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Water spills from a full glass when a stone is lowered in because…
**Answer: a) the stone takes the water's place — impenetrability  b) the stone is heavier than water  c) the glass shrank  d) the water evaporated**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Which squeezes most easily?
**Answer: a) a gas  b) a liquid  c) a solid  d) they all squeeze equally**
