# Video script — General properties of matter

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the general properties of matter**. Every body — stone, water, air — has mass and takes up space. Two bodies never share one place. Matter cuts smaller and smaller, squeezes a little, and springs back. Six properties, and every thing you can touch has all six.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
