# Hands-on — General properties of matter

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Six properties, six quick tests
Work through the set with real objects. (1) Mass and volume: weigh a stone on a balance, then find its volume by the water it pushes up in a measuring cylinder — two numbers, two different facts. (2) Impenetrability: fill a glass exactly to the brim and lower the stone in slowly — the water that spills is the water whose place the stone took. (3) Divisibility: tear a sheet of paper in half, then in half again, four rounds — each piece is still paper. (4) Compressibility: seal a syringe of air with a finger and push — it gives; try the same with a syringe of water — it barely gives. (5) Elasticity: stretch a rubber band and let go; press a sponge and release. Finish by writing the six properties as a checklist and ticking each one against a chair, a cup of water and the air in the syringe: all three bodies pass all six tests.
