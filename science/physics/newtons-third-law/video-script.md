Newton's third law: forces come in pairs.
If body A pushes body B, then B pushes A back with an equal force in the opposite direction.
The two forces act on different bodies, so they never cancel on one body.
Push a wall and the wall pushes your hand back; walking works because your foot pushes the ground backward and the ground pushes you forward; a recoiling gun is the same pair read from the other side.

Remember: **Action and reaction are equal, opposite, and act on different bodies.**
Remember: **Push a wall and the wall pushes back equally.**
Remember: **Walking: foot pushes ground back, ground pushes walker forward.**
