# Newton's third law — action and reaction — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** When you push a car, what does the car do to you?
a) Pushes back on you with an equal, opposite force.  b) They act on different bodies.  c) The ground's reaction push on your foot.  d) The gas's reaction on the rocket.
**Answer: a) Pushes back on you with an equal, opposite force.**

**2.** Why don't action and reaction cancel out?
a) Pushes back on you with an equal, opposite force.  b) They act on different bodies.  c) The ground's reaction push on your foot.  d) The gas's reaction on the rocket.
**Answer: b) They act on different bodies.**

**3.** In walking, which force actually moves you forward?
a) Pushes back on you with an equal, opposite force.  b) They act on different bodies.  c) The ground's reaction push on your foot.  d) The gas's reaction on the rocket.
**Answer: c) The ground's reaction push on your foot.**

**4.** A rocket pushes gas down. What pushes the rocket up?
a) Pushes back on you with an equal, opposite force.  b) They act on different bodies.  c) The ground's reaction push on your foot.  d) The gas's reaction on the rocket.
**Answer: d) The gas's reaction on the rocket.**
