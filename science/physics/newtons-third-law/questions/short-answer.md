# Newton's third law — action and reaction — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** When you push a car, what does the car do to you?
**Answer: Pushes back on you with an equal, opposite force.**

**2.** Why don't action and reaction cancel out?
**Answer: They act on different bodies.**

**3.** In walking, which force actually moves you forward?
**Answer: The ground's reaction push on your foot.**

**4.** A rocket pushes gas down. What pushes the rocket up?
**Answer: The gas's reaction on the rocket.**
