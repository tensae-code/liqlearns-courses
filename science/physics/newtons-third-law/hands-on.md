# Hands-on — Newton's third law — action and reaction

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Press your palm hard against a wall and notice the wall pressing back — you cannot push without being pushed. Then walk and attend to the ground's answer at each step.

## Apply it where you live
Find three action–reaction pairs in sport or farm work and name both bodies in each pair.
