# Saturated and unsaturated vapour, phase diagrams, triple point and critical state — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Vapour in dynamic equilibrium with its liquid is called ___ vapour.
**Answer:** saturated

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The pressure of a saturated vapour depends only on its ___.
**Answer:** temperature

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The curve on a phase diagram between solid and gas is the ___ curve.
**Answer:** sublimation

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Above the ___ temperature, no pressure can liquefy a gas.
**Answer:** critical

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) For water the triple point is at ___ °C.
**Answer:** 0.01
