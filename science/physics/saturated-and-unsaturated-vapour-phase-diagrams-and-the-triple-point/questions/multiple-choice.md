# Saturated and unsaturated vapour, phase diagrams, triple point and critical state — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A vapour is saturated when…
a) No more liquid is present  b) Evaporation and condensation rates are equal  c) Its temperature reaches 100 °C  d) It fills the whole room
**Answer: b) Evaporation and condensation rates are equal**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Compressing a saturated vapour at fixed temperature…
a) Raises its pressure  b) Lowers its pressure  c) Leaves its pressure unchanged — extra vapour condenses  d) Turns it all to liquid at once always
**Answer: c) Leaves its pressure unchanged — extra vapour condenses**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) On a phase diagram, a point on the boiling curve means…
a) Only gas exists  b) Liquid and gas coexist in equilibrium  c) The substance is frozen  d) Pressure is zero
**Answer: b) Liquid and gas coexist in equilibrium**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Water boils at a lower temperature on a mountain because…
a) There is less gravity  b) Lower air pressure moves the boiling point down the boiling curve  c) The water is purer  d) It is colder outside
**Answer: b) Lower air pressure moves the boiling point down the boiling curve**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) At the triple point…
a) Only solid exists  b) Solid, liquid and gas coexist  c) The substance disappears  d) Pressure is at its maximum
**Answer: b) Solid, liquid and gas coexist**
