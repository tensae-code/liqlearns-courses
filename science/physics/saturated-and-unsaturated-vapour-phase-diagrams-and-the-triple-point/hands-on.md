# Saturated and unsaturated vapour, phase diagrams, triple point and critical state — hands-on

Do this before reading the lesson. Steam above boiling water in a sealed pan is compressed at the same temperature. What happens to its pressure, and why? Work with your hands and your eyes first: gather the ordinary items the question needs, make the measurement or the comparison yourself, and write down what you actually found — not what you expected. Then read the lesson and compare: where your finding and the lesson agree, you have learned it twice; where they differ, the difference is the lesson. Keep your numbers — the questions at the end will ask for them.
