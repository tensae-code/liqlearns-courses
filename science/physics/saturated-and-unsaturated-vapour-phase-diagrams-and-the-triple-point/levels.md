# Saturated and unsaturated vapour, phase diagrams, triple point and critical state — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — a vapour above its liquid settles into a balance — saturated vapour, whose pressure no squeezing can raise — and the whole map of a substance's states fits on one phase diagram, where three states meet at the triple point and the liquid–gas line ends at the critical point — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
