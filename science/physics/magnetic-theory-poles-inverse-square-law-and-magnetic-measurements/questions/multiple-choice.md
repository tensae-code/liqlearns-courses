# Magnetic theory — poles, inverse-square law and magnetic measurements — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The force between two magnetic poles varies with distance as…
a) 1/r  b) 1/r²  c) r²  d) It is constant
**Answer: b) 1/r²**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Cutting a bar magnet in two produces…
a) A separate north piece and south piece  b) Two smaller magnets, each with both poles  c) Unmagnetised iron  d) One pole in each hand, permanently
**Answer: b) Two smaller magnets, each with both poles**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Magnetic field strength at a point is defined as the force on…
a) Any magnet  b) A unit north pole placed at the point  c) An electric charge  d) A compass needle's south pole
**Answer: b) A unit north pole placed at the point**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The field of a single pole at distance r is proportional to…
a) m × r²  b) m ÷ r²  c) m ÷ r  d) r ÷ m
**Answer: b) m ÷ r²**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In the tangent law, tan θ equals…
a) H ÷ B  b) B ÷ H (B perpendicular to the Earth's horizontal field H)  c) B × H  d) B − H
**Answer: b) B ÷ H (B perpendicular to the Earth's horizontal field H)**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A deflection magnetometer measures magnets by…
a) Weighing them  b) Comparing their fields through compass deflections and distances  c) Timing their swings only  d) Heating them
**Answer: b) Comparing their fields through compass deflections and distances**
