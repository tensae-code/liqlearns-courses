# Hands-on — Magnetic theory — poles, inverse-square law and magnetic measurements

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel the square law
Bring two strong magnets slowly together against a ruler's marks, feeling the repulsion at 4 cm, 2 cm and 1 cm gaps (hold them in line with a wooden spacer). Say aloud: halving the gap does not double the push — the inverse-square law makes it roughly four times fiercer.

## Deflect a compass
Set a compass, note its rest direction (the Earth's field alone), then bring a bar magnet up side-on at a measured distance and read the deflection. Move it to twice the distance and read again. Say aloud: the needle is a magnetometer — it is comparing the magnet's field with the Earth's by the tangent law.
