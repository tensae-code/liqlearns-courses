Today: **magnetism with numbers, the classical way**. Treat a magnet as two poles, and the force between poles follows an inverse-square law, like gravity: double the distance, quarter the force. Field strength is the force a unit north pole would feel, and fields add as vectors. Lay a magnet's field across the Earth's and a compass needle strikes the balance — tangent of the deflection equals B over H. That one ratio is the magnetometer, the instrument that weighed magnets against the Earth.
