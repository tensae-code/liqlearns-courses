# Hands-on — The equations of uniformly accelerated motion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Write the five letters v, u, a, s, t on paper. For one journey, fill in what you know and circle what you want — then notice only one equation fits the four.

## Apply it where you live
Solve three motion problems, each time writing the knowns-unknown-equation choice first.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21883 — equation-choice flowchart — what you know, what you want, which equation] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using v = u + at; s = ut + ½at²; v² = u² + 2as: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
