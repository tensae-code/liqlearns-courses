# The equations of uniformly accelerated motion — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** From rest, a = 2 m/s² for 5 s. Final velocity?
**Answer: v = u + at = 0 + 2 × 5 = 10 m/s.**

**2.** From rest, a = 3 m/s² for 4 s. Displacement?
**Answer: s = ut + ½at² = 0 + ½ × 3 × 16 = 24 m.**

**3.** u = 5 m/s, v = 13 m/s, a = 2 m/s². Find s.
**Answer: v² = u² + 2as → 169 = 25 + 4s → s = 36 m.**

**4.** Which equation avoids t?
**Answer: v² = u² + 2as.**
