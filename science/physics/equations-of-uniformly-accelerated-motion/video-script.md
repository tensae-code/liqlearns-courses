# Video script — The equations of uniformly accelerated motion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Four equations link v (final velocity), u (initial velocity), a (acceleration), s (displacement) and t (time) when acceleration is constant: v = u + at; s = ut + ½at²; v² = u² + 2as; and s = ½(u + v)t.
The skill is choosing: list what you know, name what you want, and pick the equation that contains exactly those four letters and not the fifth.
Every worked example in the unit is that one choice, made well.

Remember: **v = u + at; s = ut + ½at²; v² = u² + 2as; s = ½(u + v)t.**
Remember: **The equations hold only for constant acceleration.**
Remember: **Choose the equation containing your three knowns and your one unknown.**
Keep the formula: **v = u + at; s = ut + ½at²; v² = u² + 2as**
