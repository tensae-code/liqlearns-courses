# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Lao Grade 12 (ມ.7) way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the three-year staircase
On one sheet, draw the three Lao years as three staircases — Grade 10 (7 parts), Grade 11 (5 parts), Grade 12 (10 parts) — and label each part in a few words. Then trace one thread across all three years (for example: motion, or electricity, or light) and mark how its treatment deepens year by year. The staircase is the Lao series' own architecture, made visible with paper and pen.
