# How this Physics course works — the Lao Grade 12 (ມ.7) way

## Do it first
Before you read: lay the three Lao years side by side in your mind — Grade 10 was mechanics and heat, Grade 11 was matter, electricity and light's geometry. What is left for a final year to do? Laos' answer fills ten parts. This lesson is that year's map, and the logic that orders it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ten parts, thirty lessons — the final-year map

The parts: I Rotation of a Rigid Body (Lessons 1–2), II Mechanical Oscillations (3–5), III Mechanical Waves (6–8), IV Sound (9–11), V The Magnetic Field (12–15), VI Alternating Current (16–19), VII Electromagnetic Waves (20–21), VIII Light Waves (22–24), IX Atomic Physics (25–27), X Nuclear Physics (28–30). The book runs 305 pages and its last lesson ends at the file's end — a complete final year, read in full table of contents in the expansion wave. The series' lesson shape holds: concept, worked examples, exercises, part by part.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mechanics returns only as rotation and oscillation

Against Grades 10–11, straight-line mechanics does not return. Mechanics appears as rotation (angular displacement, speed and acceleration; moment of inertia; the rotational equations) and as oscillation deepened beyond the pendulum: kinds of oscillation — damped, forced, resonance — and combining oscillations as a lesson of its own. The year assumes the Grade 10 mechanics and spends its mechanics time turning it: from translation to rotation, from one oscillation to systems of them.

## Sub lesson 3 — The modern-physics weight — waves, AC, the atom, the nucleus

The year's centre of gravity is new territory: mechanical waves and sound close with the Doppler effect (named in English in the body); magnetism climbs to electromagnetic induction; alternating current gets four lessons ending in the series RLC circuit ('RLC' printed in Latin letters in the contents itself); electromagnetic and light waves bring interference, diffraction and colour; and the book closes with atomic physics — Bohr, Planck and Einstein named, the photoelectric strand worked — and nuclear physics ending in the uses of radiation and protection from it, with a dose table in sieverts. It is a school course that ends where modern physics begins.

## The key ideas

- The Lao Grade 12 course: 10 parts / 30 lessons, from Rotation (Part I) to Nuclear Physics (Part X)
- Mechanics returns only as rotation and oscillation — translation mechanics is assumed from Grade 10
- The year's weight: waves and sound (Doppler), magnetism to induction, four AC lessons to the series RLC circuit, atomic and nuclear physics
- The final lesson joins uses of radiation to protection from it, with a sievert dose table
