# How this Physics course works — the Lao Grade 12 (ມ.7) way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give the ten parts with their lesson counts.
**Answer: I Rotation (2); II Mechanical Oscillations (3); III Mechanical Waves (3); IV Sound (3); V The Magnetic Field (4); VI Alternating Current (4); VII Electromagnetic Waves (2); VIII Light Waves (3); IX Atomic Physics (3); X Nuclear Physics (3) — 30 lessons.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does Grade 12 treat the Grade 10 mechanics?
**Answer: It assumes it. Straight-line motion, forces and energy are not re-taught; mechanics returns only in its turning forms — rotation of a rigid body and mechanical oscillations, deepened to damped/forced/resonance and combining oscillations.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What shows the year's modern-physics weight?
**Answer: Four AC lessons to the series RLC circuit, electromagnetic waves and the spectrum, wave optics with interference and diffraction, an atomic part naming Bohr, Planck and Einstein with the photoelectric strand, and a nuclear part ending in radiation uses and protection.**
