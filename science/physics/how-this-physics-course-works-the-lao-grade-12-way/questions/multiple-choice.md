# How this Physics course works — the Lao Grade 12 (ມ.7) way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Lao Grade 12 course has…
**Answer: a) 10 parts and 30 lessons  b) 7 parts and 28 lessons  c) 5 parts and 25 lessons  d) 12 parts and 40 lessons**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The course opens with…
**Answer: a) rotation of a rigid body  b) nuclear physics  c) geometrical optics  d) heat**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Mechanics in Grade 12 returns as…
**Answer: a) rotation and oscillation only  b) a full re-teach of Grade 10  c) statics and couples again  d) nothing at all**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Lesson 5 of the course teaches…
**Answer: a) combining oscillations  b) the photoelectric effect  c) transformers  d) radioactivity**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The alternating-current part ends with…
**Answer: a) the series RLC circuit and electrical resonance  b) Ohm's law  c) Kirchhoff's rules  d) the heating effect of current**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The whole course closes with…
**Answer: a) uses of radiation and protection from radioactivity, with a sievert dose table  b) a revision test  c) geometrical optics  d) the gas laws**
