# Hands-on — Measuring the speed of light

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Your own Rømer delay
You cannot time light, but you can time its stand-in: stand a friend 170 m away (count paces) with a flag and a drum or clap. You see the flag drop instantly; the sound arrives about half a second later. Time ten claps with a phone stopwatch to beat reaction error. Write Rømer's sentence for your case: the delay grows with distance, so the messenger — sound for you, light for him — travels at a finite speed.
