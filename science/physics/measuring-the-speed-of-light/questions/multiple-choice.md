# Measuring the speed of light — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Galileo's lantern experiment failed because…
**Answer: a) human reaction time swamps light's flight time over kilometres  b) lanterns were too dim  c) hills are too round  d) light is infinitely fast**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) To measure light's speed, Rømer needed…
**Answer: a) a regular clock far away, watched from changing distances  b) a faster horse  c) a microscope  d) two hills**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Io is…
**Answer: a) a moon of Jupiter, eclipsed every 42½ hours  b) a star  c) a comet  d) Jupiter's ring**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) When Earth moved away from Jupiter, Io's eclipses appeared…
**Answer: a) later and later, by minutes  b) earlier  c) unchanged  d) to stop**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The modern value of the speed of light is about…
**Answer: a) 300,000 km/s  b) 300 km/s  c) 300,000 km/h  d) infinite**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Sunlight takes about how long to reach Earth?
**Answer: a) About 8 minutes  b) 8 seconds  c) 8 hours  d) No time at all**
