# Charge distribution and the electric field inside conductors — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Excess charge on a conductor resides on its _____ surface.
**Answer: outer**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Charge leaks into the air most easily from a _____ part of a conductor.
**Answer: pointed (sharp)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In electrostatic equilibrium the field inside a conductor is _____.
**Answer: zero**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A hollow conductor used as a shield is called a _____ cage.
**Answer: Faraday**
