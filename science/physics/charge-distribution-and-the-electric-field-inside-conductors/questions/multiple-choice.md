# Charge distribution and the electric field inside conductors — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Excess charge placed on a conductor ends up…
**Answer: a) entirely on its outer surface  b) spread evenly through its volume  c) only at its centre  d) only on its inner surfaces**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Surface charge is most crowded where the surface is…
**Answer: a) sharpest / most pointed  b) flattest  c) thickest  d) painted**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Lightning rods are pointed because at a point…
**Answer: a) the charge density is greatest, so charge leaks to the air there  b) metal is stronger  c) rain runs off faster  d) the rod weighs less**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In electrostatic equilibrium, the electric field inside a conductor is…
**Answer: a) zero  b) at its maximum  c) equal to the surface field  d) always changing direction**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) If the field inside a conductor were not zero, the free charges would…
**Answer: a) keep moving — so it would not be equilibrium  b) freeze in place  c) leave the conductor  d) turn into heat instantly**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) A hollow conductor shields its cavity because…
**Answer: a) external fields end on the outer surface, leaving the inside field-free  b) metal absorbs all electricity forever  c) the cavity is a vacuum  d) charge cannot exist near a cavity**
