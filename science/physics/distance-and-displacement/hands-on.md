# Hands-on — Distance and displacement

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Walk from your door to a tree by the longest winding way, counting steps. Then walk straight back, counting again. Two numbers, one journey pair — name each.

## Apply it where you live
Sketch one real journey with a bend; label its distance path and its displacement arrow.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21814 — winding path and a straight arrow between the same start and finish — distance vs displacement] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
