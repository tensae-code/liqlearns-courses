# Distance and displacement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A pupil walks 30 m east then 40 m east. Distance and displacement?
a) Distance 70 m; displacement 70 m east.  b) Distance 100 m; displacement 0 m.  c) Displacement.  d) A quantity with size only, no direction.
**Answer: a) Distance 70 m; displacement 70 m east.**

**2.** A pupil walks 50 m east, then 50 m back west. Distance and displacement?
a) Distance 70 m; displacement 70 m east.  b) Distance 100 m; displacement 0 m.  c) Displacement.  d) A quantity with size only, no direction.
**Answer: b) Distance 100 m; displacement 0 m.**

**3.** Which one needs a direction to be complete — distance or displacement?
a) Distance 70 m; displacement 70 m east.  b) Distance 100 m; displacement 0 m.  c) Displacement.  d) A quantity with size only, no direction.
**Answer: c) Displacement.**

**4.** What is a scalar?
a) Distance 70 m; displacement 70 m east.  b) Distance 100 m; displacement 0 m.  c) Displacement.  d) A quantity with size only, no direction.
**Answer: d) A quantity with size only, no direction.**
