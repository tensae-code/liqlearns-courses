# Distance and displacement — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A pupil walks 30 m east then 40 m east. Distance and displacement?
**Answer: Distance 70 m; displacement 70 m east.**

**2.** A pupil walks 50 m east, then 50 m back west. Distance and displacement?
**Answer: Distance 100 m; displacement 0 m.**

**3.** Which one needs a direction to be complete — distance or displacement?
**Answer: Displacement.**

**4.** What is a scalar?
**Answer: A quantity with size only, no direction.**
