# Video script — Distance and displacement

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Motion is described from a reference position.
Distance is the full length of the path travelled — every bend included — and it is a scalar: it has size only.
Displacement is the straight change of position from start to finish, with its direction — a vector.
Walk a winding path between two points and your distance is long, while your displacement is the one straight arrow between the same start and finish.

Remember: **Distance is the full path length travelled; it is a scalar.**
Remember: **Displacement is the straight change of position, with direction; it is a vector.**
Remember: **On a winding journey, distance is longer than the displacement's size.**
