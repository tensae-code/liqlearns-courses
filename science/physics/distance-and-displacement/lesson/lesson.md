# Distance and displacement

## Do it first
Walk from your door to a tree by the longest winding way, counting steps. Then walk straight back, counting again. Two numbers, one journey pair — name each.

[Picture 21814 — winding path and a straight arrow between the same start and finish — distance vs displacement]

## Distance and displacement — the teaching
Motion is described from a reference position. Distance is the full length of the path travelled — every bend included — and it is a scalar: it has size only. Displacement is the straight change of position from start to finish, with its direction — a vector. Walk a winding path between two points and your distance is long, while your displacement is the one straight arrow between the same start and finish.

## The key ideas

- Distance is the full path length travelled; it is a scalar.
- Displacement is the straight change of position, with direction; it is a vector.
- On a winding journey, distance is longer than the displacement's size.

## Say it to your companion
Explain distance and displacement to your companion in your own words. Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
