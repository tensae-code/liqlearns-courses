Today: **modulation — how sound rides a radio wave**. Sound alone fades in metres, so radio writes it onto a strong high-frequency carrier. AM writes on the carrier's height — amplitude follows the sound, simple but crackly, because noise changes heights too. FM writes on the carrier's spacing — frequency follows the sound, height stays fixed, so noise gets clipped away and the music comes out clean. The receiver tunes to one carrier, strips it away, and hands you back the sound.
