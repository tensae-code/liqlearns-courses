# Hands-on — Modulation — AM and FM

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Write a message on a wave
Draw a long strip of identical sine waves on paper — your carrier. Now take a slow message wave (draw your name's rhythm as a wavy line: loud parts high, quiet parts low). Make the AM version: redraw the carrier strip, but scale each peak's height to match the message at that point — the outline spells your message. Make the FM version beside it: keep every peak the same height, but squeeze the waves closer where the message is high and stretch them where it is low. Hold the two strips up: in one, read the height; in the other, read the spacing. Say aloud: AM writes on height, FM writes on spacing, and the carrier itself carries no message at all until it is modulated.
