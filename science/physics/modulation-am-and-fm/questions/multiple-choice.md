# Modulation — AM and FM — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Modulation is needed mainly because audio signals on their own…
a) Travel too fast  b) Cannot travel far, would need huge aerials, and would all overlap  c) Are too loud  d) Cannot be produced by microphones
**Answer: b) Cannot travel far, would need huge aerials, and would all overlap**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The wave that actually makes the journey from transmitter to receiver is the…
a) Audio wave  b) Carrier wave  c) Sound wave in air  d) Envelope alone
**Answer: b) Carrier wave**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In AM, the audio signal varies the carrier's…
a) Frequency only  b) Amplitude, while the frequency stays fixed  c) Speed  d) Aerial length
**Answer: b) Amplitude, while the frequency stays fixed**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) AM reception suffers from crackle because…
a) The carrier is too weak to reach  b) Noise from lightning and motors also changes amplitudes, which is exactly where AM writes the sound  c) FM stations interfere  d) The receiver has a limiter
**Answer: b) Noise from lightning and motors also changes amplitudes, which is exactly where AM writes the sound**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In FM, the quantity held strictly constant is the carrier's…
a) Frequency  b) Amplitude  c) Wavelength pattern  d) Station name
**Answer: b) Amplitude**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) FM needs a wider channel than AM, so FM broadcasting is placed…
a) On long wave  b) In the VHF band, about 88–108 MHz  c) Underwater  d) Below 1 kHz
**Answer: b) In the VHF band, about 88–108 MHz**
