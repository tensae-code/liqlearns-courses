# Modulation — AM and FM — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The high-frequency wave that carries the sound on its journey is the _____ wave.
**Answer: carrier**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Reading the audio back off the carrier at the receiver is called _____.
**Answer: demodulation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) AM stands for _____ modulation.
**Answer: amplitude**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) FM stands for _____ modulation.
**Answer: frequency**
