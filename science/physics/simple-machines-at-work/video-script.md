# Video script — Simple machines at work

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Workplaces run on the same family.
On farms and building sites: the hoe and the axe (wedge and lever together), the crowbar (a lever for prising), and the wheelbarrow (a lever on a wheel — load between fulcrum and effort).
Local work is the test bench of this unit: each tool earns its place by multiplying force, multiplying speed, or changing direction for a real job.

Remember: **A crowbar is a lever for prising loads loose.**
Remember: **A wheelbarrow is a lever on a wheel, load in the middle.**
Remember: **An axe combines a lever handle with a wedge head.**
