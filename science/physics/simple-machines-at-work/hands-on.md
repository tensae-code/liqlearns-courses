# Hands-on — Simple machines at work

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
List the tools used in one real job near you — digging, building, fetching water — and name the machine inside each tool.

## Apply it where you live
Visit (or picture) a workshop and catalogue its simple machines by purpose.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21838 — farm and workshop scene with machines ringed — hoe, axe, crowbar, wheelbarrow] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
