# Magnetic properties of iron — permeability and hysteresis — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Permeability equals B divided by _____.
**Answer: H**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) When iron can magnetise no further it has reached _____.
**Answer: saturation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Flux remaining at zero magnetising force is called _____.
**Answer: remanence**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Electromagnet cores use _____ iron — narrow loop, low coercivity.
**Answer: soft**
