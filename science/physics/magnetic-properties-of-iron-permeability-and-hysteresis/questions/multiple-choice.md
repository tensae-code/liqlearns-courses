# Magnetic properties of iron — permeability and hysteresis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Permeability μ is defined as…
a) B × H  b) B ÷ H  c) H ÷ B  d) B − H
**Answer: b) B ÷ H**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Relative permeability μᵣ of soft iron is typically…
a) About 1  b) Less than 1  c) A few hundred to several thousand  d) Negative
**Answer: c) A few hundred to several thousand**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Remanence is…
a) The field at saturation  b) The flux density remaining when the magnetising force returns to zero  c) The reverse field needed to wipe out B  d) The loop's area
**Answer: b) The flux density remaining when the magnetising force returns to zero**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The area of a hysteresis loop represents…
a) The remanence  b) Energy lost as heat per unit volume per cycle  c) The coercive force  d) Saturation flux
**Answer: b) Energy lost as heat per unit volume per cycle**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) For a transformer core (AC), the right iron has…
a) A wide loop and high coercivity  b) A narrow loop, low coercivity and high permeability  c) The largest remanence  d) No permeability
**Answer: b) A narrow loop, low coercivity and high permeability**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Permanent magnets are made of hard steel because it has…
a) Low remanence  b) High remanence and high coercivity — it resists demagnetisation  c) A narrow loop  d) Zero hysteresis
**Answer: b) High remanence and high coercivity — it resists demagnetisation**
