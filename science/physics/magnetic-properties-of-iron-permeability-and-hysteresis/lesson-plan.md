# Lesson plan — Magnetic properties of iron — permeability and hysteresis

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** Magnetic properties of iron — permeability and hysteresis
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18) — a new lesson built for the Sri Lanka placement named in lesson/sources.md
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Iron does not merely sit in a magnetic field — it multiplies it: its **permeability** says by how much, and it remembers: taken round a cycle of magnetising force, its magnetisation lags behind in a **hysteresis loop** whose width (remanence, coercivity) and area (energy lost per cycle) decide whether an iron belongs in an electromagnet, a transformer core or a permanent magnet
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Earlier lessons make a good preparation and are recommended, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.
