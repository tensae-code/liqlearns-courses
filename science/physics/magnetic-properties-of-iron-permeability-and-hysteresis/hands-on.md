# Hands-on — Magnetic properties of iron — permeability and hysteresis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Amplify and remember
Wind a coil round a large nail, connect a battery, and count the paper clips the electromagnet lifts; disconnect and count what the nail alone still holds. Say aloud: while the current ran, permeability multiplied the field — now remanence is showing me the loop's memory.

## Hard versus soft
Try to magnetise a nail (soft) and a steel needle or screwdriver tip by stroking with a strong magnet; test each an hour later, or after a tap. Say aloud: the soft one lets go easily — low coercivity — and the hard steel holds on: that stubbornness is what a permanent magnet is bought for.
