# Video script — Magnetic properties of iron — permeability and hysteresis

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **iron, the field's amplifier with a memory**. Slip iron into a coil's field and the flux density soars — permeability is the measure of that multiplication, thousands-fold in soft iron, until saturation calls a halt. Cycle the magnetising force and the flux lags behind it, tracing the hysteresis loop: flux remaining at zero force is remanence, the reverse force that wipes it is coercivity, and the loop's area is heat you pay for every cycle. Narrow loop — soft iron for cores and electromagnets. Wide loop — hard steel for magnets that must never forget.
