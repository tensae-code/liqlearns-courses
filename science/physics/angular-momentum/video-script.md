Today: **the quantity of turning**. A circling mass carries angular momentum — m times v times r — and a whole rigid body carries I times omega, its moment of inertia times its spin. Torque is the only thing that changes it, at exactly the rate tau equals dL by dt. So with no outside torque it cannot change: the skater pulls in, her I halves, her spin doubles, and the account balances. Tuck, spin, planets — one law.
