# Angular momentum — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) For a rigid body, angular momentum L equals _____ times ω.
**Answer: I**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Torque is the rate of change of angular _____.
**Answer: momentum**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) With no external torque, angular momentum is _____.
**Answer: conserved**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Halving a spinner's moment of inertia (L fixed) _____ its angular velocity.
**Answer: doubles**
