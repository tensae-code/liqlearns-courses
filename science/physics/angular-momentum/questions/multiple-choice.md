# Angular momentum — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The angular momentum of a particle about an axis is…
a) mv  b) mvr (r the perpendicular distance)  c) Iω only for particles  d) mr²
**Answer: b) mvr (r the perpendicular distance)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) For a rigid body about a fixed axis, L equals…
a) Iω  b) Iα  c) ½Iω²  d) τt
**Answer: a) Iω**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Torque equals the rate of change of…
a) Kinetic energy  b) Angular momentum  c) Moment of inertia  d) Angular velocity
**Answer: b) Angular momentum**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An angular impulse τ × t equals…
a) The torque alone  b) The change in angular momentum  c) Iω²  d) Zero always
**Answer: b) The change in angular momentum**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) With zero external torque, a skater halving her I will…
a) Halve her ω  b) Keep the same ω  c) Double her ω  d) Stop
**Answer: c) Double her ω**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In the skater's spin-up, the quantity that is NOT conserved is…
a) Angular momentum  b) Rotational kinetic energy — her muscles do work pulling in  c) Total mass  d) The axis direction
**Answer: b) Rotational kinetic energy — her muscles do work pulling in**
