# Hands-on — Angular momentum

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spin and tuck
Sit on a swivel chair with arms (or two heavy books) held wide, get a gentle spin started, then pull everything in tight. Feel the spin-up no one gave you. Say aloud: I went down, ω went up, and Iω — the angular momentum — never changed.

## String through a tube
Whirl a small mass on a string passed through a short tube, then pull the string down to shorten the radius. Watch the circling speed rise as r falls. Say aloud: mvr is being kept — smaller r, larger v.
