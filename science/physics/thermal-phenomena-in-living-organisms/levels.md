# Thermal phenomena in living organisms — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — living things survive heat and frost with the same physics as the laboratory — conduction slowed by trapped air in fur and feathers, radiation managed by colour and surface, and cooling bought by evaporation of sweat — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
