# Thermal phenomena in living organisms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Fur keeps an animal warm mainly because it…
a) Makes heat  b) Traps still air, a poor conductor  c) Reflects sunlight  d) Stops the wind only
**Answer: b) Traps still air, a poor conductor**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Fluffing feathers in frost works by…
a) Making the bird bigger  b) Thickening the trapped-air insulating layer  c) Cooling the skin  d) Shedding water
**Answer: b) Thickening the trapped-air insulating layer**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Sweating cools the body because…
a) Sweat is cold  b) Evaporation takes latent heat from the skin  c) Sweat blocks sunlight  d) It washes heat off
**Answer: b) Evaporation takes latent heat from the skin**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Panting cools a dog by…
a) Breathing faster to make heat  b) Evaporating water from its wet tongue and airways  c) Shivering  d) Drinking air
**Answer: b) Evaporating water from its wet tongue and airways**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Plant sugars before winter help survival because they…
a) Feed the roots  b) Act as antifreeze, lowering the freezing point of cell fluid  c) Make leaves grow  d) Attract pollinators
**Answer: b) Act as antifreeze, lowering the freezing point of cell fluid**
