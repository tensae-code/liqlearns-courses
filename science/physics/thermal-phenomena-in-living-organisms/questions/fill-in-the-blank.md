# Thermal phenomena in living organisms — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Still ___ trapped in fur is a poor conductor and slows heat loss.
**Answer:** air

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A layer of ___ under the skin insulates an animal from inside.
**Answer:** fat

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Cooling by sweating uses the process of ___.
**Answer:** evaporation

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Pale colours in the desert ___ the sun's radiation.
**Answer:** reflect

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Ice ___ forming inside cells can rupture them in frost.
**Answer:** crystals
