# Thermal phenomena in living organisms — video script

**Opening (the hook):** Living things survive heat and frost with the same physics as the laboratory — conduction slowed by trapped air in fur and feathers, radiation managed by colour and surface, and cooling bought by evaporation of sweat.

**Walk-through:** Take the sub lessons in order. For 'Keeping warm — insulation that grows': Animals in the cold carry their insulation with them. For 'Keeping cool — evaporation and shade': In heat the problem reverses: heat must be dumped. For 'Plants, frost and the edge cases': Frost threatens from inside: ice crystals rupture cells, so plants harden by draining water from cells and loading sugars that act as antifreeze, and animals from frogs to insects use matching tricks — some even survive partial freezing.

**Close:** Say the one thing once more, plainly: living things survive heat and frost with the same physics as the laboratory — conduction slowed by trapped air in fur and feathers, radiation managed by colour and surface, and cooling bought by evaporation of sweat.
