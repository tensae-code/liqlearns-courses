# Hands-on — How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 12 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work one chapter the PTB way
Work one chapter the PTB way: tick each numbered Learning Objective as you can honestly do it, collect every Do You Know? box's fact on one card, read the SUMMARY box aloud, then do three PROBLEMS and mark them against the printed answers.
