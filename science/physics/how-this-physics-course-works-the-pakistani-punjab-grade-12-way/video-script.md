# Video script — How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 12 way

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **each chapter of the PTB's Physics 12 opens with a numbered Learning Objectives list, carries Do You Know? and For Your Information boxes through the text, and closes with a bullet SUMMARY box, then QUESTIONS and PROBLEMS with printed answers — the older Punjab pattern, running from Electrostatics (Ch. 12) to Nuclear Physics (Ch. 21)**. The PTB book numbers its promises: each chapter's opening list tells the learner, item by item, what the chapter will enable — state Coulomb's law, apply Gauss's law, explain Millikan's method — so progress can be checked against the list as the chapter proceeds. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — numbered learning objectives open the chapter; do you know? boxes carry the chapter's asides; a summary box, then questions and problems with answers — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.
