# How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 12 way

## Do it first
Work one chapter the PTB way — begin with the hands-on opening in hands-on.md before reading on. This lesson is the story of that activity.

## Sub lesson 1 — Numbered Learning Objectives open the chapter

The PTB book numbers its promises: each chapter's opening list tells the learner, item by item, what the chapter will enable — state Coulomb's law, apply Gauss's law, explain Millikan's method — so progress can be checked against the list as the chapter proceeds. The chapters themselves continue the old scheme's numbering, 12 to 21.

## Sub lesson 2 — Do You Know? boxes carry the chapter's asides

Through the text, Do You Know? and For Your Information boxes hold the applications and the striking facts — xerography in the electrostatics chapter, the NAVSTAR system in relativity — the same widening voice the newer Punjab books keep, under the older book's own headings.

## Sub lesson 3 — A SUMMARY box, then QUESTIONS and PROBLEMS with answers

The close is a bullet SUMMARY box that compresses the chapter to its load-bearing statements, followed by QUESTIONS and PROBLEMS whose answers are printed. The learner's last act in every chapter is therefore self-checked problem work against a summary they can hold in one view.
