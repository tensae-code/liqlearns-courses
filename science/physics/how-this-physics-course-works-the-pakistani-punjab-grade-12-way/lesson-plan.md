# Lesson plan — How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 12 way

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 12 way
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18), inside the electrostatics strand — Pakistan (Punjab)'s older PTB pattern that Physics 12 still carries
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** each chapter of the PTB's Physics 12 opens with a numbered Learning Objectives list, carries Do You Know? and For Your Information boxes through the text, and closes with a bullet SUMMARY box, then QUESTIONS and PROBLEMS with printed answers — the older Punjab pattern, running from Electrostatics (Ch. 12) to Nuclear Physics (Ch. 21)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Any earlier lesson named below is recommended first, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Numbered Learning Objectives open the chapter** — The PTB book numbers its promises: each chapter's opening list tells the learner, item by item, what the chapter will enable — state Coulomb's law, apply Gauss's law, explain Millikan's method — so progress can be checked against the list as the chapter proceeds. The chapters themselves continue the old scheme's numbering, 12 to 21.
2. **Do You Know? boxes carry the chapter's asides** — Through the text, Do You Know? and For Your Information boxes hold the applications and the striking facts — xerography in the electrostatics chapter, the NAVSTAR system in relativity — the same widening voice the newer Punjab books keep, under the older book's own headings.
3. **A SUMMARY box, then QUESTIONS and PROBLEMS with answers** — The close is a bullet SUMMARY box that compresses the chapter to its load-bearing statements, followed by QUESTIONS and PROBLEMS whose answers are printed. The learner's last act in every chapter is therefore self-checked problem work against a summary they can hold in one view.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
