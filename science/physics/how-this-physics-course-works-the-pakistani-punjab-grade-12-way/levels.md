# How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 12 way — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — The PTB book numbers its promises: each chapter's opening list tells the learner, item by item, what the chapter will enable — state Coulomb's law, apply Gauss's law, explain Millikan's method — so progress can be checked against the list as the chapter proceeds; Through the text, Do You Know? and For Your Information boxes hold the applications and the striking facts — xerography in the electrostatics chapter, the NAVSTAR system in relativity — the same widening voice the newer Punjab books keep, under the older book's own headings; The close is a bullet SUMMARY box that compresses the chapter to its load-bearing statements, followed by QUESTIONS and PROBLEMS whose answers are printed — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
