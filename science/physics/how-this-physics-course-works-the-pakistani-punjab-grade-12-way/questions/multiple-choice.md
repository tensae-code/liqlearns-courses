# How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 12 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Physics 12's chapters are numbered…
a) 1 to 10  b) 12 to 21, continuing the old scheme  c) 100 to 110  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) 12 to 21, continuing the old scheme**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Each chapter opens with…
a) The summary box  b) A numbered Learning Objectives list  c) Problem answers  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) A numbered Learning Objectives list**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The book's aside boxes are headed…
a) Keep Out  b) Do You Know? and For Your Information  c) Appendix  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Do You Know? and For Your Information**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Xerography appears as an application in…
a) The Nuclear chapter  b) The Electrostatics chapter  c) The Optics chapter  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) The Electrostatics chapter**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Each chapter closes with…
a) Nothing — the next chapter starts  b) A SUMMARY box, then QUESTIONS and PROBLEMS with answers  c) A song  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) A SUMMARY box, then QUESTIONS and PROBLEMS with answers**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The SUMMARY is written as…
a) Full paragraphs repeated  b) Bullets compressing the chapter's key statements  c) Questions only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Bullets compressing the chapter's key statements**
