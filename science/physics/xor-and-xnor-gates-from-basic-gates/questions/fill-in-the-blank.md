# XOR and XNOR gates — from basic gates — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) XOR is short for ___-OR.
**Answer:** exclusive

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) XNOR is XOR followed by ___.
**Answer:** NOT

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) In an adder, XOR gives the ___ bit.
**Answer:** sum

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Build any truth table by ___-ing the cases that give 1.
**Answer:** OR
