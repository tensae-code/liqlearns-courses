# XOR and XNOR gates — from basic gates — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What jobs does XOR do in real circuits?
**Answer:** It forms the sum bit in adders and detects changed bits in parity checking.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) State XNOR's sum-of-cases expression.
**Answer:** (A AND B) OR (NOT A AND NOT B) — the two agreeing cases.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How do XOR and XNOR together serve a comparator?
**Answer:** XOR flags difference, XNOR flags agreement — a comparison answered both ways.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why teach XOR as a combination rather than a new primitive?
**Answer:** Because any logic can be assembled from the basic gates; combinations are how real control circuits are built.
