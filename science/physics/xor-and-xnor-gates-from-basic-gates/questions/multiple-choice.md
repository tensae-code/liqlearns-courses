# XOR and XNOR gates — from basic gates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) XOR's output is 1 when its inputs…
a) Agree  b) Differ  c) Are both 0  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Differ**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) XOR of 1 and 1 is…
a) 1  b) 0  c) Unchanged 1s only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) 0**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) XNOR's output is 1 when its inputs…
a) Differ  b) Agree  c) Are both 1 only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Agree**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) XNOR is related to XOR as…
a) Its square  b) Its exact inversion (XOR then NOT)  c) An unrelated gate  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Its exact inversion (XOR then NOT)**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) XOR from basic gates is…
a) A AND B only  b) (A AND NOT B) OR (NOT A AND B)  c) NOT (A AND B)  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) (A AND NOT B) OR (NOT A AND B)**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The general building rule the lesson teaches is…
a) Always use NAND alone  b) OR together the input cases that must give 1  c) Copy the nearest circuit  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) OR together the input cases that must give 1**
