# XOR and XNOR gates — from basic gates — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — Exclusive-OR gives output 1 when exactly one input is 1 — when the inputs differ; Exclusive-NOR is XOR followed by NOT: output 1 when the inputs agree (00→1, 11→1) and 0 when they differ; XOR assembles as (A AND NOT B) OR (NOT A AND B) — an OR of the two differing cases — and XNOR as its inversion, or as (A AND B) OR (NOT A AND NOT B), the two agreeing cases — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
