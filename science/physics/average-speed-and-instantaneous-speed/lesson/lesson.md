# Average speed and instantaneous speed

## Do it first
Time a walk across the yard, then a run back. Work out each average speed from distance ÷ time.

## Average speed and instantaneous speed — the teaching
Average speed is the total distance travelled divided by the total time taken — one number for the whole journey, however uneven it was. Instantaneous speed is the speed at one particular moment, the number a speedometer shows right now. A journey can crawl through a town and race outside it; the instantaneous speed keeps changing while the average speed summarises it all.

**Keep the formula:** Average speed = total distance / total time

## The key ideas

- Average speed = total distance ÷ total time.
- Instantaneous speed is the speed at a particular moment.
- A speedometer shows instantaneous speed.

## Say it to your companion
Explain average speed and instantaneous speed to your companion in your own words. Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
