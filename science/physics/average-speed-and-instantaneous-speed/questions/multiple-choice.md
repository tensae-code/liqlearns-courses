# Average speed and instantaneous speed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A car travels 150 km in 3 h. Its average speed?
a) 150 ÷ 3 = 50 km/h.  b) 400 ÷ 80 = 5 m/s.  c) Instantaneous speed — the speed at that moment.  d) No — only the whole-journey summary.
**Answer: a) 150 ÷ 3 = 50 km/h.**

**2.** A runner covers 400 m in 80 s. Average speed?
a) 150 ÷ 3 = 50 km/h.  b) 400 ÷ 80 = 5 m/s.  c) Instantaneous speed — the speed at that moment.  d) No — only the whole-journey summary.
**Answer: b) 400 ÷ 80 = 5 m/s.**

**3.** What does a speedometer show?
a) 150 ÷ 3 = 50 km/h.  b) 400 ÷ 80 = 5 m/s.  c) Instantaneous speed — the speed at that moment.  d) No — only the whole-journey summary.
**Answer: c) Instantaneous speed — the speed at that moment.**

**4.** Does average speed tell you the speed at every moment?
a) 150 ÷ 3 = 50 km/h.  b) 400 ÷ 80 = 5 m/s.  c) Instantaneous speed — the speed at that moment.  d) No — only the whole-journey summary.
**Answer: d) No — only the whole-journey summary.**
