# Average speed and instantaneous speed — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A car travels 150 km in 3 h. Its average speed?
**Answer: 150 ÷ 3 = 50 km/h.**

**2.** A runner covers 400 m in 80 s. Average speed?
**Answer: 400 ÷ 80 = 5 m/s.**

**3.** What does a speedometer show?
**Answer: Instantaneous speed — the speed at that moment.**

**4.** Does average speed tell you the speed at every moment?
**Answer: No — only the whole-journey summary.**
