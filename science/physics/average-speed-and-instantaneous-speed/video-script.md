# Video script — Average speed and instantaneous speed

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Average speed is the total distance travelled divided by the total time taken — one number for the whole journey, however uneven it was.
Instantaneous speed is the speed at one particular moment, the number a speedometer shows right now.
A journey can crawl through a town and race outside it; the instantaneous speed keeps changing while the average speed summarises it all.

Remember: **Average speed = total distance ÷ total time.**
Remember: **Instantaneous speed is the speed at a particular moment.**
Remember: **A speedometer shows instantaneous speed.**
Keep the formula: **Average speed = total distance / total time**
