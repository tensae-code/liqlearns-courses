# Hands-on — Average speed and instantaneous speed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Time a walk across the yard, then a run back. Work out each average speed from distance ÷ time.

## Apply it where you live
Find the average speed of one real journey you know — distance and time from family or a timetable.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21816 — car journey timeline with one overall average and a speedometer snapshot] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using Average speed = total distance / total time: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
