# Evaporation and boiling — how they differ — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give the particle explanation of evaporative cooling.
**Answer: Particles have a spread of energies; the fastest surface particles escape, so the average energy of those remaining falls — and average particle energy is the temperature.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why can't boiling happen below the boiling point, though bubbles of dissolved air appear earlier?
**Answer: A vapour bubble below the boiling point is crushed by the outside pressure, because its vapour pressure is too low to push back; only at the boiling point can bubbles survive and grow throughout the liquid.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) State the comparison in four paired lines.
**Answer: Place: surface only / throughout. Temperature: any / fixed at the boiling point. Rate: slow / vigorous. Liquid's temperature: falls / stays constant.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Clothes dry faster on a windy day even when it is cold. Which process is this, and why does wind help?
**Answer: Evaporation — wind carries away the vapour just above the surface, keeping the air there dry and letting more fast particles escape instead of returning.**
