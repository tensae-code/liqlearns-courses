# Evaporation and boiling — how they differ — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Evaporation happens…
**Answer: a) only at the surface, at any temperature  b) throughout, at the boiling point  c) only at 100 °C  d) only in vacuum**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Evaporation cools the liquid because the escapees are…
**Answer: a) the highest-energy particles  b) the coldest particles  c) the heaviest  d) the surface itself**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Boiling begins when the vapour pressure in the bubbles…
**Answer: a) can push back the outside pressure  b) is zero  c) exceeds the melting point  d) equals the latent heat**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) During boiling, the liquid's temperature…
**Answer: a) stays at the boiling point  b) keeps rising  c) falls  d) doubles**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Evaporation is faster with…
**Answer: a) heat, wind, larger surface and drier air  b) a lid  c) still humid air  d) a smaller surface**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A pressure cooker cooks faster because it…
**Answer: a) raises the boiling point under higher pressure  b) removes water  c) cools the food  d) boils at a lower temperature**
