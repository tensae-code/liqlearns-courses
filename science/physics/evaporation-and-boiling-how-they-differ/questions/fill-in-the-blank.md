# Evaporation and boiling — how they differ — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Evaporation happens only at the _____.
**Answer: surface**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Evaporation _____ the liquid left behind.
**Answer: cools**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Boiling happens at one fixed temperature, the boiling _____.
**Answer: point**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) During boiling the supplied heat becomes _____ heat of vaporisation.
**Answer: latent**
