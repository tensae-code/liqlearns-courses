# Hands-on — Evaporation and boiling — how they differ

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Put equal water drops on two plates; fan one. Time the drying and record the difference. (2) Wet the back of one hand and blow on it; compare the two hands' feel — evaporation cooling measured by skin. (3) Heat water in a clear vessel and note, in order: the first tiny bubbles (dissolved air), vapour forming, the rolling boil — and the thermometer's refusal to pass the boiling point.
