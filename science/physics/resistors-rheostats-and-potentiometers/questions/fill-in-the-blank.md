# Resistors, rheostats and potentiometers — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Current through a resistor follows Ohm's law: I = _____/R.
**Answer: V**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The heat a resistor sheds is P = I²_____.
**Answer: R**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Sliding the contact to include more wire _____ the resistance.
**Answer: increases**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A radio volume knob is usually a _____ feeding an amplifier.
**Answer: potentiometer**
