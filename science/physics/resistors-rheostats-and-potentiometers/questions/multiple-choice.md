# Resistors, rheostats and potentiometers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A resistor's opposition to current is measured in…
**Answer: a) ohms (Ω)  b) amperes  c) volts  d) watts**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A series resistor across an LED is there to…
**Answer: a) limit the current to a safe value  b) make it brighter without limit  c) store charge  d) change AC to DC**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A rheostat changes resistance by…
**Answer: a) a sliding contact choosing how much wire is in the circuit  b) turning the battery  c) heating the lamp  d) adding bulbs**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A rheostat is wired…
**Answer: a) in series with the load it governs  b) in parallel with the battery  c) instead of the load  d) only in AC circuits**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A potentiometer uses how many terminals?
**Answer: a) Three — the whole coil plus the slider  b) Two  c) One  d) Four**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The potentiometer's slider delivers…
**Answer: a) a chosen share of the total voltage  b) a chosen share of the current only  c) heat  d) a magnetic field**
