# Hands-on — Resistors, rheostats and potentiometers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Slide and watch
Build the book's lab: battery, lamp, ammeter and a rheostat (or a long pencil 'lead' — graphite is a resistor — with two crocodile clips, one sliding) all in series. Slide the contact and record current and brightness at five positions. Then rewire the graphite as a divider: battery across its whole length, lamp from one end to the slider, and record the lamp's share. Two wirings, two jobs — label each with its right name.
