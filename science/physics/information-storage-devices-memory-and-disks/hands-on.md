# Hands-on — Information storage devices — memory and disks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Inventory the storage you can touch
Inventory the storage you can touch: a computer's memory size versus its disk size (find both in its settings), a phone's storage screen, one flash drive. Write which of your own school files live in exactly one place, and copy the most important to a second device — the survey's rule, applied.
