# Information storage devices — memory and disks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Primary memory is best pictured as…
a) A national archive  b) The machine's desk — fast and close to the processor  c) A printer tray  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) The machine's desk — fast and close to the processor**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) RAM's contents are…
a) Kept forever  b) Lost when the power dies  c) Printed automatically  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Lost when the power dies**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A hard disk stores information as…
a) Punched holes  b) Patterns of magnetisation on spinning platters  c) Coloured light  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Patterns of magnetisation on spinning platters**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Audio and video cassettes store magnetically on…
a) Glass  b) Tape  c) Paper  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Tape**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Flash drives store information by…
a) Spinning platters  b) Holding charge in solid-state memory, with no moving parts  c) Burning pits only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Holding charge in solid-state memory, with no moving parts**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The storage family's safety rule is…
a) Keep everything in RAM  b) Keep information in more than one place  c) Use one device for everything  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Keep information in more than one place**
