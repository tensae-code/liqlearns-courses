# The piston liquid pump — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — The machine is simple enough to name in one breath: a barrel (cylinder) dipping into the water, a piston that slides in it, and two one-way valves — one in the base of the barrel where the water enters, one in the piston itself; Raise the piston and the air trapped under it spreads and thins — its pressure drops below the atmosphere's; Push the piston down and the base valve slams shut while the piston's own valve opens, letting the water pass above the piston; next up-stroke lifts that trapped water out of the spout while fresh water rises below — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
