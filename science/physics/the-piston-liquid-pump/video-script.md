# Video script — The piston liquid pump

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the piston pump is atmospheric pressure put to work: the piston only empties the barrel of air, and it is the air outside — pressing on the well's surface — that pushes the water up, through valves that let it pass one way and never back, with a hard ceiling of about ten metres on the lift**. The machine is simple enough to name in one breath: a barrel (cylinder) dipping into the water, a piston that slides in it, and two one-way valves — one in the base of the barrel where the water enters, one in the piston itself. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — the parts — barrel, piston, two valves; the up-stroke — the atmosphere does the lifting; the down-stroke, the trapped water, and the ten-metre ceiling — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
