# Hands-on — The piston liquid pump

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with your hands
Model the pump with a syringe in a bowl of water, thumb over nothing: draw the plunger up and watch water chase it — that is the atmosphere pushing, not the syringe 'sucking'. Now lift the syringe tip just out of the water and draw again: air rushes in and no water climbs. You have found, with your hands, why pumps are primed and why leaks defeat them.
