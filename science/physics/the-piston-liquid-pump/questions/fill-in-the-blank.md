# The piston liquid pump — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) The force that pushes water up a pump's barrel is ___ pressure.
**Answer:** atmospheric

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A pump's valves pass water in ___ direction only.
**Answer:** one

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) One atmosphere can support a water column of about ___ metres.
**Answer:** 10.3

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Filling a pump with water before starting is called ___ it.
**Answer:** priming
