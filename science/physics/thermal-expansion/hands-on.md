# Hands-on — Thermal expansion — things grow when heated

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Run hot water over a tight metal jar lid: the lid expands and loosens. Expansion just did a job for you.

## Apply it where you live
Calculate the expansion gap a 10 m concrete slab needs for a 40 °C rise, using a stated α.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21871 — metal bar lengthening into an expansion gap; ring whose hole grows on heating] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using ΔL = αL₀ΔT: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
