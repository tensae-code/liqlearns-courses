# Thermal expansion — things grow when heated — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A rod with α = 1.2×10⁻⁵ /°C, L₀ = 2 m, warms by 50 °C. ΔL?
a) ΔL = 1.2×10⁻⁵ × 2 × 50 = 1.2×10⁻³ m = 1.2 mm.  b) Bigger — the hole expands as if it were solid.  c) So expansion in heat has room and the slab does not crack.  d) Its two metals expand by different amounts.
**Answer: a) ΔL = 1.2×10⁻⁵ × 2 × 50 = 1.2×10⁻³ m = 1.2 mm.**
