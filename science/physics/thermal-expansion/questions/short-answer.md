# Thermal expansion — things grow when heated — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A rod with α = 1.2×10⁻⁵ /°C, L₀ = 2 m, warms by 50 °C. ΔL?
**Answer: ΔL = 1.2×10⁻⁵ × 2 × 50 = 1.2×10⁻³ m = 1.2 mm.**

**2.** A ring is heated. Its hole gets bigger or smaller?
**Answer: Bigger — the hole expands as if it were solid.**

**3.** Why do concrete slabs have gaps?
**Answer: So expansion in heat has room and the slab does not crack.**

**4.** Why does a bimetallic strip curl?
**Answer: Its two metals expand by different amounts.**
