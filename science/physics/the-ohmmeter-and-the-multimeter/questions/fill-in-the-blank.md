# The ohmmeter and the multimeter — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An ohmmeter carries its own internal _____ to drive the test current.
**Answer: battery**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) With the probes open-circuit, an ohmmeter reads _____ ohms.
**Answer: infinity (∞)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The ohms scale is crowded at the high end because deflection follows the _____ of the total resistance.
**Answer: reciprocal (1/R)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The multimeter is also called an _____ (amp–volt–ohm meter).
**Answer: avometer**
