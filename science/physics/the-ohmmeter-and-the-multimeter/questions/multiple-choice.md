# The ohmmeter and the multimeter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Inside a series ohmmeter, the unknown resistor is connected…
a) In parallel with the battery only  b) In series with the internal battery, the meter movement and the zero-adjust resistor  c) Across the mains supply  d) In series with a standard lamp
**Answer: b) In series with the internal battery, the meter movement and the zero-adjust resistor**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) On an ohmmeter scale, zero ohms is found…
a) At the left, where the pointer rests  b) At mid-scale  c) At the right, at full deflection — because shorted probes give maximum current  d) Nowhere — ohms start at 1
**Answer: c) At the right, at full deflection — because shorted probes give maximum current**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Before each measurement you short the probes and use the zero-ohms adjuster to…
a) Set the pointer to ∞  b) Set the pointer exactly on 0 Ω at full deflection, correcting for battery ageing and range changes  c) Zero the voltage ranges  d) Discharge the resistor
**Answer: b) Set the pointer exactly on 0 Ω at full deflection, correcting for battery ageing and range changes**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An ohmmeter must never be connected to a live circuit because…
a) Live circuits have no resistance  b) Its own battery and delicate movement would meet the circuit's voltage, damaging the meter and falsifying the reading  c) The probes would melt instantly  d) Ohmmeters only work in the dark
**Answer: b) Its own battery and delicate movement would meet the circuit's voltage, damaging the meter and falsifying the reading**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A multimeter's single selector switch chooses between…
a) Different batteries  b) Voltmeter, ammeter and ohmmeter functions and their AC/DC ranges  c) Analogue and digital displays  d) Series and parallel circuit layouts
**Answer: b) Voltmeter, ammeter and ohmmeter functions and their AC/DC ranges**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) For the most trustworthy ohmmeter reading, choose the range that puts the pointer…
a) Hard against the left stop  b) Near the centre of the ohms scale  c) At exactly full deflection  d) Anywhere — all parts of the scale are equally precise
**Answer: b) Near the centre of the ohms scale**
