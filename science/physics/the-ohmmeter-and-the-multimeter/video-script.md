Today: **the ohmmeter and the multimeter**. An ohmmeter carries its own battery. It pushes a test current through your resistor, and the less current gets through, the bigger the resistance — so its scale runs backwards: zero ohms at full swing, infinity at rest, and everything crowded at the top end. Short the probes and re-zero it on every range, and never touch it to a live circuit. Add voltmeter and ammeter ranges behind one switch and the ohmmeter becomes the multimeter — the one instrument that does every job on the bench.
