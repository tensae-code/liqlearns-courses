# Hands-on — The ohmmeter and the multimeter

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Zero, then measure
On each ohms range of a multimeter: short the probes, set the zero-ohms adjuster to exactly 0 Ω, then measure three known resistors, choosing for each the range that lands the pointer nearest mid-scale. Compare with the colour-code values. Say aloud the rule: re-zero on every range; read near the centre of the scale.

## The backwards scale
Plot it: measure resistors of 10, 47, 100, 220, 470, 1000 Ω on one range and note each pointer position as a fraction of full deflection. The equal resistance steps squeeze together at the high end. Say aloud the rule: deflection follows 1/(R_internal + R), never R itself — that is why the scale runs backwards and crowds.
