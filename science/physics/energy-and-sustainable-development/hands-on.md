# Hands-on — Energy and sustainable development

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Audit your own energy day
Keep a one-day energy diary. (1) List everything you used that ran on energy — lights, phone charging, cooking, travel, hot water — and mark each with its supply: grid electricity, gas, petrol, battery, muscle. (2) For the grid items, estimate the energy in kWh from power × time (a kettle: 2 kW × 0.05 h = 0.1 kWh). (3) Sort your list: which uses could shrink with efficiency (a shorter shower, an LED, a walked trip), and which supplies behind them are finite or renewable. (4) Finish with one sentence of sustainable development applied to your own street: name one supply to grow and one waste to cut, with the physics reason for each.
