# Energy and sustainable development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Coal, oil and gas are called finite because…
**Answer: a) they are burned once and gone, formed over millions of years  b) they are expensive  c) they are heavy  d) they are found underground**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Burning fossil fuels loads the air chiefly with…
**Answer: a) carbon dioxide, which warms the climate  b) oxygen  c) nitrogen  d) water only**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is a renewable energy flow?
**Answer: a) wind  b) coal  c) natural gas  d) peat**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Nuclear energy's place in the account is…
**Answer: a) dense and powerful, but its waste must be carried safely for a very long time  b) renewable like sunlight  c) free of all problems  d) already finished**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The cheapest, cleanest joule is…
**Answer: a) the one never used — saved by efficiency  b) the one from coal  c) the one imported  d) the one used at night**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Sustainable development means meeting today's needs…
**Answer: a) without spending the world the next generations will need  b) as fast as possible  c) only with fossil fuels  d) without any energy at all**
