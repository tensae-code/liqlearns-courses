# Energy and sustainable development — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name two costs of fossil fuels that do not appear on the fuel bill itself.
**Answer: Climate warming from carbon dioxide; air pollution harming health in cities (also ash/waste disposal, or the finiteness itself — any two).**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is solar called “the root of most sources” in the junior book's energy chapters?
**Answer: Sunlight drives the water cycle (hydro), the winds, and grew the plants that became fossil fuels — most supplies are stored or flowing sunshine.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A grid built for steady fossil power must “learn” what, to live with sun and wind?
**Answer: To handle supplies that come and go with weather and daylight — storage, flexible demand and interconnection — instead of power that can be ordered at any hour.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Give one thrift measure and the physics chapter that prices it.
**Answer: E.g. LED lamps (electric power: same light for a fifth of the watts); insulation (heat transfer slowed); efficient engines (heat-engine efficiency caps the waste). Any measure tied to its physics.**
