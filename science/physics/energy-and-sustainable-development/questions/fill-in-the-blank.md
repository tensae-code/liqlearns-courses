# Energy and sustainable development — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Coal, oil and gas are _____ fuels — burned once, gone.
**Answer: fossil**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Sources nature refills on a human timescale are called _____.
**Answer: renewable**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Development that lasts is called _____ development.
**Answer: sustainable**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The joule never used is the _____ and cleanest.
**Answer: cheapest**
