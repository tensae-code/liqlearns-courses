# How this Physics course works — the Yemeni Grade 12 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the nine Grade 12 units in order.
**Answer: Momentum and projectiles; alternating current; electronics; electronic devices; atomic physics; radiation and matter; nuclear physics; solar energy; cosmic physics.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does stating each unit's applications in the preface help the student?
**Answer: The student knows from the start what each unit's physics is for, so the theory is learned as a tool with a named job, not as abstract facts.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Give two companion-notebook experiments and the units they prove.
**Answer: Any two, e.g. the resonance-circuit practical for alternating current; the visible solar spectrum for atomic spectra; collecting solar energy for the solar-energy unit; the Geiger characteristic curve for radioactivity.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) How does the Grade 12 package repeat the Grade 10 pattern?
**Answer: In both years the student book is paired with a separate activities-and-experiments notebook whose experiments prove the book's theory with the learner's own measurements.**
