# How this Physics course works — the Yemeni Grade 12 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Grade 12 preface does what, unusually?
a) Lists only the authors  b) Summarises each unit of the year, unit by unit  c) Gives the exam answers  d) Skips the units
**Answer: b) Summarises each unit of the year, unit by unit**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The Grade 12 course has…
a) 8 units  b) 9 units  c) 10 units  d) 11 units
**Answer: b) 9 units**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Every Grade 12 unit carries…
a) No applications  b) An applications strand — what its physics is for  c) Only theory  d) Only history
**Answer: b) An applications strand — what its physics is for**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The year's last unit is…
a) Nuclear physics  b) Solar energy  c) Cosmic physics  d) Electronics
**Answer: c) Cosmic physics**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The Grade 12 companion notebook has…
a) 11 experiments  b) 10 experiments tracking the hardest units  c) No experiments  d) Only solar experiments
**Answer: b) 10 experiments tracking the hardest units**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) One companion experiment uses a Geiger counter to study…
a) Sound  b) Radioactivity  c) Light  d) Heat
**Answer: b) Radioactivity**
