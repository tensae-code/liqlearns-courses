# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Yemeni Grade 12 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Walk the year on one page
Take one large page and draw nine boxes in a row, one per Grade 12 unit, in order. Under each, write its application in a few words — generator, devices, reactors, solar energy, the Universe. Pin the page where you study. Every time you start a unit, stand at its box first: read what the year promised there, and when the unit ends, write one line under the box — the promise kept, in your own words.
