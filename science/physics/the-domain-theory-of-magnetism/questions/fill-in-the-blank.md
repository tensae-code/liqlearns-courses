# The domain theory of magnetism — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The tiny magnetised regions inside iron are called _____.
**Answer: domains**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A bar is fully magnetised when its domains are all lined up; it is then said to be _____.
**Answer: saturated**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) _____ iron's domains line up and fall out of line easily, so it makes temporary magnets.
**Answer: Soft**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Heating, hammering and a fading alternating field are all ways of _____ a magnet.
**Answer: demagnetising**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Soft-iron pieces placed across a stored magnet's poles are called _____.
**Answer: keepers**
