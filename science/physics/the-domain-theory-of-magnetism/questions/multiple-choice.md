# The domain theory of magnetism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A domain is…
a) A kind of magnet sold in pairs  b) A tiny region of a magnetic material, magnetised in one direction  c) The space around a magnet  d) A pole of extra strength
**Answer: b) A tiny region of a magnetic material, magnetised in one direction**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) An unmagnetised iron bar shows no magnetism because its domains…
a) Are missing  b) Have melted  c) Point every which way, so their effects cancel  d) Are all at one end
**Answer: c) Point every which way, so their effects cancel**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Stroking a bar to magnetise it must be done…
a) Back and forth as fast as possible  b) In one direction only, lifting the magnet clear at the end of each stroke  c) With both poles together  d) Under water
**Answer: b) In one direction only, lifting the magnet clear at the end of each stroke**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Steel is used for permanent magnets because its domains…
a) Are bigger  b) Are hard to turn but hold their line once lined up  c) Never line up  d) Melt at low temperature
**Answer: b) Are hard to turn but hold their line once lined up**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Which of these demagnetises a magnet?
a) Storing it with keepers  b) Painting it  c) Heating it strongly  d) Stroking it one way
**Answer: c) Heating it strongly**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In the alternating-current method, the magnet is demagnetised because the fading reversing field leaves the domains…
a) All pointing one way  b) Pointing at random  c) Melted together  d) Moved to one end
**Answer: b) Pointing at random**
