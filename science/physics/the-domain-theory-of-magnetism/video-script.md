Today: **the domain theory of magnetism**.

Inside iron there are domains — tiny regions, each one a miniature magnet. In an ordinary nail they point every which way, so the nail is not a magnet. Stroke it the same way again and again and the domains line up: now the nail lifts paper clips. Heat it, hammer it, or fade an alternating field over it, and the line breaks up — the magnetism is gone. Soft iron lines up easily and lets go easily; steel holds its line, and that is why steel makes permanent magnets.
