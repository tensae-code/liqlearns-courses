# Hands-on — The domain theory of magnetism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Magnetise a nail by stroking
Test an iron nail on paper clips and record that it lifts none. Stroke the nail with one pole of a bar magnet, from one end to the other, lifting the magnet clear at the end of every stroke, twenty times, always the same direction. Test again and count the clips in the longest chain the nail lifts. Say aloud: the domains have lined up — nothing was added to the nail.

## Spoil it again
Drop the magnetised nail several times onto a hard surface (or tap it with a hammer on a bench, with care and an adult present), testing after each few drops. Watch the chain shorten as the magnetism fades. Say aloud: each knock shook some domains out of line; enough knocks leave them pointing at random, and the nail is a nail again.
