# Hands-on — Semiconductors — intrinsic and extrinsic, the p-n junction diode

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One way only
With a diode (or an LED), a battery, a resistor and a lamp or the LED itself: build the circuit and note it lights. Reverse the battery and note it does not. Reverse the diode instead and test again. Then, if the kit has one, test a plain resistor both ways round and compare. Record the diode's rule in one sentence, and under it the bias names: the lighting way is forward bias, the dark way reverse bias. Finish by finding one device at home whose plug or charger depends on this one-way rule and writing its name.
