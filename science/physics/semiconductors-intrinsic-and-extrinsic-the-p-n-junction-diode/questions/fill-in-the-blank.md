# Semiconductors — intrinsic and extrinsic, the p-n junction diode — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The missing electron left behind when a bond breaks in silicon behaves as a positive carrier called a _____.
**Answer: hole**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Adding impurity atoms to a semiconductor on purpose is called _____.
**Answer: doping**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) An impurity atom that accepts electrons and leaves holes is an _____ impurity.
**Answer: acceptor**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The carrier-free layer formed at a p-n junction is the _____ region.
**Answer: depletion**
