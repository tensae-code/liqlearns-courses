# Semiconductors — intrinsic and extrinsic, the p-n junction diode — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An intrinsic semiconductor is…
**Answer: a) a pure semiconductor  b) a doped semiconductor  c) a metal  d) an insulator**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In an intrinsic crystal, carriers are born as…
**Answer: a) electron–hole pairs, equal in number  b) electrons only  c) holes only  d) ions**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Doping silicon with a donor impurity (one extra electron) makes…
**Answer: a) n-type material  b) p-type material  c) an insulator  d) a magnet**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The majority carriers in p-type material are…
**Answer: a) holes  b) electrons  c) protons  d) ions**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Both n-type and p-type materials are, as a whole…
**Answer: a) electrically neutral  b) negatively charged  c) positively charged  d) radioactive**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A p-n junction diode conducts…
**Answer: a) one way only — under forward bias  b) equally both ways  c) only under reverse bias  d) never**
