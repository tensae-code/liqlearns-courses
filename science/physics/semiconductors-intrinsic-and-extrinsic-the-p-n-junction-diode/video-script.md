Today: **semiconductors — intrinsic, extrinsic, and the p-n junction diode**. Pure silicon carries a few electron–hole pairs. A pinch of donor impurity floods it with electrons — n-type; a pinch of acceptor floods it with holes — p-type. Join the two kinds and current passes one way only. That join is the diode, and electronics starts there.
