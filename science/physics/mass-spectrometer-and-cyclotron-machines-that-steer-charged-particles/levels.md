# Mass spectrometer and cyclotron — machines that steer charged particles — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — Mass spectrometer: ions circle at r = mv/qB in a known field — landing position reads out mass per charge; a velocity selector (v = E/B) makes it exact; This machine separated and weighed the isotopes — atoms of one element with different masses; Cyclotron: an alternating voltage kicks the particle at every gap crossing; lap time is speed-independent, so one rhythm works for hundreds of laps; Energy adds per crossing: hundreds of small kicks equal millions of volts; relativity sets the cyclotron's ceiling, where synchrotrons take over — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
