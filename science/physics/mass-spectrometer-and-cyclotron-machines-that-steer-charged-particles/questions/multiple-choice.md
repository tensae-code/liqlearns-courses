# Mass spectrometer and cyclotron — machines that steer charged particles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a mass spectrometer, ions of different mass in the same field…
**Answer: a) circle with different radii — landing position reads the mass  b) land together  c) do not circle  d) change charge**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A velocity selector passes only ions with…
**Answer: a) v = E/B, where the electric and magnetic forces cancel  b) zero speed  c) the largest mass  d) two charges**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The cyclotron's alternating voltage keeps working lap after lap because…
**Answer: a) the lap time does not depend on the particle's speed  b) the field grows  c) the Ds rotate  d) the gap widens**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Each gap crossing adds…
**Answer: a) a small fixed energy — hundreds of crossings sum to millions of volts' worth  b) a charge  c) a magnet  d) a lap**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The cyclotron's ceiling is set by…
**Answer: a) relativity — the lap time stretches as effective mass grows near light speed  b) the paint  c) the vacuum  d) the weather**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Hospitals use cyclotrons chiefly to…
**Answer: a) make radioisotopes and therapy beams  b) weigh patients  c) take X-ray photos  d) store blood**
