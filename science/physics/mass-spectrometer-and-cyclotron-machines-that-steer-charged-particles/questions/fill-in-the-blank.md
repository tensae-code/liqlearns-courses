# Mass spectrometer and cyclotron — machines that steer charged particles — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The spectrometer sorts ions by their mass-to-_____ ratio.
**Answer: charge**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Only ions with v = E/B pass a velocity _____.
**Answer: selector**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The cyclotron's two hollow electrodes are shaped like the letter _____.
**Answer: D**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Hundreds of small kicks can sum to _____ of volts' worth of energy.
**Answer: millions**
