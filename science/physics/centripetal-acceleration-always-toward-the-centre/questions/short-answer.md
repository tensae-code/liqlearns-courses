# Centripetal acceleration — always toward the centre — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**Answer: Even at constant speed the velocity's direction changes, so the object accelerates toward the centre; the acceleration is derived graphically by vector subtraction and given by speed-squared over radius (or angular speed squared times radius).**
