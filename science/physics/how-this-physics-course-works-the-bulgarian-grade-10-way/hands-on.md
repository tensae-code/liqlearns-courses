# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Bulgarian Grade 10 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map the year before you start it
Take a page and draw four boxes, one per strand: electromagnetic phenomena, light, from the atom to the cosmos, practical activities. From any contents list in front of you, drop eight lesson titles into their boxes — two per strand, with the practical block taking the laboratory titles. Then draw one arrow: a Grade 9 idea that a Grade 10 strand stands on (current → induction is the classic). Keep the map where you study; every new lesson gets pinned to it before you read it.
