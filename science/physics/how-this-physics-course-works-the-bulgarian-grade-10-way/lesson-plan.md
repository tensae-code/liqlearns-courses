A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Four strands in one numbered course** — The Bulgarian Grade 10 course (Pedagog 6) runs as one numbered sequence in four strands: electromagnetic phenomena — electric and magnetic fields, charged-particle motion, induction, alternating current and electromagnetic waves; light — its propagation, its wave properties and its quantum properties; from the atom to the cosmos (От атома до Космоса) — atoms, the nucleus, nuclear reactions, elementary particles, stars, galaxies and the Universe; and a closing block of practical activities — projects and laboratory works that cash out the whole course. The orientation page at printed p. 6 is the map: read it first and you know where every lesson lives.
2. **The same lesson design, at full depth** — The Bulgarian way holds at Grade 10: typed lessons — new knowledge, problem-solving, laboratory work, summary and "I know and I can" self-checks — each opening with an "I already know…" (Вече знам) recall box, carrying a worked example, and closing its teaching with "What I learned" (Какво научих), a "Curious" (Любопитно) box and questions and tasks. At this depth the recall box matters most: a Grade 10 lesson on induction stands on Grade 9's currents and fields, and the box is where that standing is checked before the new work begins.
3. **Practical activities, glossary and the two doors** — The book opens with an entry test and closes with the practical activities block (to printed p. 250) — projects and laboratories on induction, the transformer, refractive index, diffraction and spectra, and registering ionising radiation — then a glossary (Речник) at printed p. 252 and answers at p. 256. The practical block is not decoration: it is where the course's claims are measured by the learner's own hands, so a topic is not finished until its practical has been done or honestly pictured.
