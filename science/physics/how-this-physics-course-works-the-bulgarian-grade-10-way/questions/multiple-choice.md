# How this Physics course works — the Bulgarian Grade 10 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Grade 10 strands are…
a) Mechanics, heat, sound, optics  b) Electromagnetic phenomena, light, from the atom to the cosmos, and practical activities  c) Electricity only  d) Astronomy only
**Answer: b) Electromagnetic phenomena, light, from the atom to the cosmos, and practical activities**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The orientation page is at printed p.…
a) 4  b) 5  c) 6  d) 252
**Answer: c) 6**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) At Grade 10 depth, the box carrying the most weight is…
a) "Curious"  b) "I already know…" — the recall box, because each lesson stands on earlier learning  c) The glossary  d) The answers
**Answer: b) "I already know…" — the recall box, because each lesson stands on earlier learning**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The typed-lesson design at Grade 10…
a) Is abandoned  b) Continues unchanged from Grades 7 and 9  c) Applies only to laboratories  d) Applies only to summaries
**Answer: b) Continues unchanged from Grades 7 and 9**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The practical activities block contains…
a) Only projects  b) Only examinations  c) Projects and laboratory works across the course's topics  d) The glossary
**Answer: c) Projects and laboratory works across the course's topics**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A Grade 10 topic counts as finished when…
a) Its lesson is read  b) Its practical has been done or honestly pictured  c) Its answers are copied  d) The year ends
**Answer: b) Its practical has been done or honestly pictured**
