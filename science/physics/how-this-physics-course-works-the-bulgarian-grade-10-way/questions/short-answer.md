# How this Physics course works — the Bulgarian Grade 10 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which strand holds the photoelectric effect, and why there?
**Answer: Light — its quantum properties strand: the effect is light behaving as particles, taught after light's wave properties.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the four strands in order.
**Answer: Electromagnetic phenomena; light; from the atom to the cosmos; practical activities.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What does a worked example do in a Grade 10 lesson?
**Answer: It shows a task solved in full before the learner solves their own, at the course's deepest level.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Where do the glossary and answers sit in the Grade 10 book?
**Answer: The glossary at printed p. 252 and the answers at printed p. 256.**
