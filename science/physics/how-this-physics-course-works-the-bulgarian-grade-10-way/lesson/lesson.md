# How this Physics course works — the Bulgarian Grade 10 way

## Do it first
Before you read: a year's physics is about to run from electric fields to the evolution of the Universe. Before the first lesson, the book prints one orientation page. What should that page tell you so the year never feels like a pile of separate topics? This lesson reads the Grade 10 map the Bulgarian way.

## Sub lesson 1 — Four strands in one numbered course

The Bulgarian Grade 10 course (Pedagog 6) runs as one numbered sequence in four strands: electromagnetic phenomena — electric and magnetic fields, charged-particle motion, induction, alternating current and electromagnetic waves; light — its propagation, its wave properties and its quantum properties; from the atom to the cosmos (От атома до Космоса) — atoms, the nucleus, nuclear reactions, elementary particles, stars, galaxies and the Universe; and a closing block of practical activities — projects and laboratory works that cash out the whole course. The orientation page at printed p. 6 is the map: read it first and you know where every lesson lives.

## Sub lesson 2 — The same lesson design, at full depth

The Bulgarian way holds at Grade 10: typed lessons — new knowledge, problem-solving, laboratory work, summary and "I know and I can" self-checks — each opening with an "I already know…" (Вече знам) recall box, carrying a worked example, and closing its teaching with "What I learned" (Какво научих), a "Curious" (Любопитно) box and questions and tasks. At this depth the recall box matters most: a Grade 10 lesson on induction stands on Grade 9's currents and fields, and the box is where that standing is checked before the new work begins.

## Sub lesson 3 — Practical activities, glossary and the two doors

The book opens with an entry test and closes with the practical activities block (to printed p. 250) — projects and laboratories on induction, the transformer, refractive index, diffraction and spectra, and registering ionising radiation — then a glossary (Речник) at printed p. 252 and answers at p. 256. The practical block is not decoration: it is where the course's claims are measured by the learner's own hands, so a topic is not finished until its practical has been done or honestly pictured.

## The key ideas

- Grade 10 runs in four strands: electromagnetic phenomena; light; from the atom to the cosmos; and a closing block of practical activities
- The typed-lesson design and the lesson boxes continue unchanged — at this depth the "I already know…" recall box carries the most weight
- The course closes with practical activities, a glossary and answers — a topic is finished only when its practical has been done or honestly pictured
