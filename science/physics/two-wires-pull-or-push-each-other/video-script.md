# Video script — Two wires pull or push each other

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Two parallel wires, each carrying current, meet through their fields.
Wire one's field circles it; wire two, sitting in that field, feels the motor-effect push.
Chain the reasoning and the verdict is: currents in the same direction attract; currents in opposite directions repel.
Hang the wires loosely and switch on — same-way currents bow the wires toward each other; reverse one and they bow apart.

Remember: **Each wire's field acts on the other wire's current.**
Remember: **Same-direction currents attract.**
Remember: **Opposite-direction currents repel.**
