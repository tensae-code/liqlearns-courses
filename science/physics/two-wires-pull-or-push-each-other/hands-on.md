# Hands-on — Two wires pull or push each other

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Hold two pencils as model wires and act the story: draw field circles around one, push the other with the motor rule, and check the verdict matches the lesson.

## Apply it where you live
Explain, step by step, why opposite currents repel, chaining field then force.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21922 — two hanging wires bowing toward (same current) and away (opposite)] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
