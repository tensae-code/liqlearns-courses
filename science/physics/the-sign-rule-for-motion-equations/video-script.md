# Video script — The sign rule for motion equations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the sign rule — one choice that signs a whole motion**. Before any equation, choose a positive direction. Everything moving that way is plus; everything moving against it is minus. Throw a ball up and call up positive: gravity is minus 9.8 the whole flight, the velocity shrinks through zero at the top and turns negative on the way down — one rule, and the equations never lie to you. Remember: a minus sign means a direction, never a smaller speed.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
