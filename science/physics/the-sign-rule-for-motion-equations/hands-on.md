# Hands-on — The sign rule for motion equations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Signs on a line
Draw a chalk line on the ground (or a tape line on the floor) and mark one end with a big + sign — that direction is positive. Stand on a marked start point. Walk three steps in the + direction counting aloud '+1, +2, +3' for your displacement, then walk five steps back past the start, counting your displacement down through zero into '−1, −2'. A partner calls 'velocity!' at random moments and you must answer with a signed description — 'positive, walking toward +' or 'negative, walking back'. Repeat with the + sign moved to the other end of the line and feel every sign flip while the walking itself stays exactly the same: the signs live in the choice, not in the legs.
