# The sign rule for motion equations — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A displacement, velocity or acceleration pointing against the chosen positive direction is given a _____ sign.
**Answer: negative**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Distance, speed and time are _____ quantities, so they never take a sign from the chosen direction.
**Answer: scalar**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) At the very top of its flight a thrown ball's velocity is _____ m/s for an instant.
**Answer: 0 (zero)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Feeding _____ values into v = u + at and the other motion equations lets the answer's sign state the direction.
**Answer: signed**
