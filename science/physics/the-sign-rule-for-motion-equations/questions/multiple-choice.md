# The sign rule for motion equations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The first step in using the sign rule is to…
a) Write down all the numbers first  b) Choose one direction to be positive  c) Take gravity as positive  d) Remove every minus sign
**Answer: b) Choose one direction to be positive**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) With upward chosen as positive, the acceleration due to gravity during a ball's whole flight is…
a) +9.8 m/s² on the way up and −9.8 on the way down  b) Zero at the top  c) −9.8 m/s² throughout  d) +9.8 m/s² throughout
**Answer: c) −9.8 m/s² throughout**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A velocity of −20 m/s means…
a) A speed of 20 m/s in the negative direction  b) A speed smaller than 20 m/s  c) The object is slowing down  d) The object has stopped
**Answer: a) A speed of 20 m/s in the negative direction**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An object is slowing down exactly when…
a) Its acceleration is negative  b) Its velocity is negative  c) Velocity and acceleration have opposite signs  d) Velocity and acceleration have the same sign
**Answer: c) Velocity and acceleration have opposite signs**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A ball thrown up at 15 m/s (up positive, g = 9.8) has, after 1 s, velocity…
a) +24.8 m/s  b) +5.2 m/s, still rising  c) −5.2 m/s  d) 0 m/s
**Answer: b) +5.2 m/s, still rising**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In a finished calculation a displacement answer of −5 m means…
a) You made a sign error  b) The object is 5 m from the start in the negative direction  c) The object travelled a distance of −5 m  d) Time ran backwards
**Answer: b) The object is 5 m from the start in the negative direction**
