# Special relativity — basic principles — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner states both postulates, derives the direction of time dilation from the light-clock, describes length contraction and relative simultaneity in words, and uses E = mc² in a worked number, answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — reasoning through the thought experiments aloud, restating each consequence in the learner's own frame examples, and checking new examples against the postulates until the teaching is the learner's own.
