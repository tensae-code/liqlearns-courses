Today: **time itself depends on how you move**.

Throw a ball from a moving bicycle and its speed is the bicycle's speed plus the throw. Shine a torch from that bicycle and the light does not add — everyone, moving or still, measures light at the same speed, c. Follow that one fact honestly and clocks that move tick slower, rods that move measure shorter, and two far-apart events that are "at the same time" for you are not at the same time for a rider passing by. And mass? Mass is frozen energy: E = mc², which is why a tiny loss of mass lights the Sun.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
