# Hands-on — Special relativity — basic principles

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The corridor light-clock
Walk a corridor bouncing a ball between your two hands, one hand above the other: each catch is one tick, and the ball's path is straight up and down for you. Now walk forward while a companion watches from the side: the companion sees the ball trace a longer zig-zag, yet the ball leaves your hands no faster. Say aloud: a longer path at the same speed means a longer tick — that is the light-clock, and that is why the watcher counts the moving clock slow.

## Two torches, one speed
Stand two people with torches at one end of a field, one still and one running forward as both switch on. Everyone at the far end receives both beams together — running did not push the light ahead. Say aloud: speeds add for balls, never for light; c is the same for everyone, and time and length bend to keep it so.
