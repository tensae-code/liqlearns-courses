# Special relativity — basic principles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Einstein's first postulate says…
a) Light slows down in moving frames  b) The laws of physics are the same in every inertial frame  c) Trains are the fastest frames  d) Motion is absolute
**Answer: b) The laws of physics are the same in every inertial frame**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A torch on a train moving at half light-speed shines forward. A platform observer measures the beam's speed as…
a) 1.5c  b) 0.5c  c) c  d) It depends on the train's colour
**Answer: c) c**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In the light-clock, the platform watcher sees the moving clock tick slower because…
a) The moving clock is faulty  b) The light travels a longer diagonal path at the same speed c  c) Mirrors shrink when moving  d) Time stops on trains
**Answer: b) The light travels a longer diagonal path at the same speed c**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Length contraction happens…
a) In every direction equally  b) Only across the direction of motion  c) Only along the direction of motion  d) Only at rest
**Answer: c) Only along the direction of motion**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Two observers in relative motion can disagree about…
a) The speed of light  b) Whether cause comes before effect  c) Which distant events happen at the same time  d) The laws of physics
**Answer: c) Which distant events happen at the same time**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The energy stored in 1 kg of mass is about…
a) 9 × 10¹⁶ J  b) 9 J  c) 3 × 10⁸ J  d) 1 J
**Answer: a) 9 × 10¹⁶ J**

**7.** (Sub lesson 4) The Lorentz factor γ = 1/√(1 − v²/c²) at v = 0.8c is…
a) 0.6  b) 1.25  c) 1.67  d) 0.8
**Answer: c) 1.67**

**8.** (Sub lesson 4) A massive body can never reach c because…
a) Its fuel always runs out  b) Its relativistic momentum and energy grow without limit as v approaches c  c) Light pushes it back  d) γ becomes zero
**Answer: b) Its relativistic momentum and energy grow without limit as v approaches c**
