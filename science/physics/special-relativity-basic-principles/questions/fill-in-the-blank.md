# Special relativity — basic principles — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A frame moving at steady speed in a straight line, neither speeding up nor turning, is called an _____ frame.
**Answer: inertial**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The speed of light in vacuum is written with the letter _____.
**Answer: c**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A moving clock ticking slower than a resting one is called time _____.
**Answer: dilation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Space and time woven into one four-dimensional whole are called _____.
**Answer: spacetime**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In E = mc², the quantity that makes a small mass worth a huge energy is c raised to the power _____.
**Answer: two (c²)**

**6.** (Sub lesson 4) In relativistic dynamics, momentum is p = _____ mv, where the blank is the Lorentz factor.
**Answer: γ (gamma)**
