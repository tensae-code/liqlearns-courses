# Barometers and manometers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about measuring pressure is correct?**
a) One diode passes only the positive half-cycles: AC in, pulsating DC out — half the wave thrown away.  b) The mercury barometer reads atmospheric pressure as a column height (P = ρgh); open and closed manometers read gas…  c) A moving-coil galvanometer detects tiny currents; a low-resistance shunt in parallel converts it into an ammeter, and a…
**Answer: b) The mercury barometer reads atmospheric pressure as a column height (P = ρgh); open and closed manometers…**

**2. The mercury barometer reads atmospheric pressure as a column height (P = ρgh); open and closed ______s read gas pressure from level differences, adding or subtracting ρgh against atmospheric.**
a) binary  b) manometer  c) binding energy
**Answer: b) manometer**
