# Hands-on — The structure of matter — Democritus, Razi, Beruni and Avicenna

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Squeeze the void
Fill a syringe (no needle) with air, seal the tip with a finger, and push: the air compresses. Fill it with water and try again: it barely moves. On paper, draw both as particles with gaps (air) and particles packed tight (water). Write the rule: a gas squeezes because its particles have void between them — Razi's argument, in your hands.
