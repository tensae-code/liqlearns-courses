# The structure of matter — Democritus, Razi, Beruni and Avicenna — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Democritus' indivisible particles are called _____.
**Answer: atoms**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The empty space atoms move in is the _____.
**Answer: void**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Al-Razi held that bodies are built from indivisible particles with _____ space between them.
**Answer: empty**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Beruni put his question to Avicenna in a famous _____ of letters.
**Answer: correspondence**
