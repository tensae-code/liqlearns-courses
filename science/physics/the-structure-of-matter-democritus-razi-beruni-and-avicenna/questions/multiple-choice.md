# The structure of matter — Democritus, Razi, Beruni and Avicenna — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Democritus taught that matter is made of…
**Answer: a) atoms — indivisible particles moving in empty space  b) four elements only  c) water  d) continuous stuff with no parts**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) For how long did the atom remain an argument rather than a measurement?
**Answer: a) About two thousand years  b) Ten years  c) One century  d) It was measured at once**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Al-Razi used atoms and the void to explain…
**Answer: a) why bodies compress and mix  b) the motion of the Moon  c) electricity  d) the seasons**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In Razi's picture, a gas can be squeezed because…
**Answer: a) its particles have empty space between them  b) its particles melt  c) it has no particles  d) it is alive**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In the correspondence, Avicenna's position was that…
**Answer: a) matter is divisible without end — the atom a fiction  b) atoms are solid and indivisible  c) Beruni should stop asking  d) atoms are made of light**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) When experiment finally settled the question, it found…
**Answer: a) atoms are real, and built of smaller parts still  b) no atoms at all  c) Democritus right in every detail  d) matter is a liquid**
