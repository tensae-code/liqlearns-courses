# Motors and generators — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) One family from one principle: magnetic ______s switch big circuits with small ones.**
**Answer: relay**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The DC motor turns ______-in-a-field into rotation.**
**Answer: current**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) And the AC generator turns ______ back into alternating current under Faraday's law.**
**Answer: rotation**
