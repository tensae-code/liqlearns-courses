# Motors and generators — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) One family from one principle: magnetic relays switch big circuits with small ones.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The electric bell's electromagnet pulls its hammer repeatedly.**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The DC motor turns current-in-a-field into rotation.**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) And the AC generator turns rotation back into alternating current under Faraday's law.**
**Answer: True**
