# Motors and generators — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Give one key fact this lesson teaches about applications.**
**Answer: One family from one principle: magnetic relays switch big circuits with small ones.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Give one key fact this lesson teaches about applications.**
**Answer: The DC motor turns current-in-a-field into rotation.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Give one key fact this lesson teaches about applications.**
**Answer: And the AC generator turns rotation back into alternating current under Faraday's law.**
