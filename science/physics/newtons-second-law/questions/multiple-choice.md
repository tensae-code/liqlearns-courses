# Newton's second law — force, mass, acceleration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A net force of 20 N acts on a 4 kg mass. Acceleration?
a) a = F/m = 20 ÷ 4 = 5 m/s².  b) F = ma = 2 × 3 = 6 N.  c) W = mg = 5 × 10 = 50 N.  d) It halves.
**Answer: a) a = F/m = 20 ÷ 4 = 5 m/s².**

**2.** What force accelerates 2 kg at 3 m/s²?
a) a = F/m = 20 ÷ 4 = 5 m/s².  b) F = ma = 2 × 3 = 6 N.  c) W = mg = 5 × 10 = 50 N.  d) It halves.
**Answer: b) F = ma = 2 × 3 = 6 N.**

**3.** A 5 kg body on Earth (g = 10 m/s²) has what weight?
a) a = F/m = 20 ÷ 4 = 5 m/s².  b) F = ma = 2 × 3 = 6 N.  c) W = mg = 5 × 10 = 50 N.  d) It halves.
**Answer: c) W = mg = 5 × 10 = 50 N.**

**4.** Same force, double the mass — what happens to acceleration?
a) a = F/m = 20 ÷ 4 = 5 m/s².  b) F = ma = 2 × 3 = 6 N.  c) W = mg = 5 × 10 = 50 N.  d) It halves.
**Answer: d) It halves.**
