# Newton's second law — force, mass, acceleration — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A net force of 20 N acts on a 4 kg mass. Acceleration?
**Answer: a = F/m = 20 ÷ 4 = 5 m/s².**

**2.** What force accelerates 2 kg at 3 m/s²?
**Answer: F = ma = 2 × 3 = 6 N.**

**3.** A 5 kg body on Earth (g = 10 m/s²) has what weight?
**Answer: W = mg = 5 × 10 = 50 N.**

**4.** Same force, double the mass — what happens to acceleration?
**Answer: It halves.**
