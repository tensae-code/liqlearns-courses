## Newton's second law — force, mass, acceleration — the teaching
Newton's second law ties force, mass and acceleration together: acceleration is directly proportional to the net force and inversely proportional to the mass, F = ma. Double the force and you double the acceleration; double the mass and the same force gives half the acceleration. The newton is defined from this law: 1 N is the force that accelerates 1 kg by 1 m/s². Weight follows at once: W = mg.

**Keep the formula:** F = ma; W = mg

## The key ideas

- F = ma — acceleration grows with net force and shrinks with mass.
- 1 N accelerates 1 kg by 1 m/s².
- Weight W = mg.
