# Hands-on — Newton's second law — force, mass, acceleration

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Push an empty box across the floor, then the same box loaded with books, pushing just as hard. The loaded box answers more slowly — F = ma with your own arms.

## Apply it where you live
Calculate the force needed to accelerate a loaded cart in your compound, choosing its mass and a gentle acceleration.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21828 — same push on a light and a heavy cart, accelerations compared] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using F = ma; W = mg: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
