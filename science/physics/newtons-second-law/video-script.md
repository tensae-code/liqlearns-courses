Newton's second law ties force, mass and acceleration together: acceleration is directly proportional to the net force and inversely proportional to the mass, F = ma.
Double the force and you double the acceleration; double the mass and the same force gives half the acceleration.
The newton is defined from this law: 1 N is the force that accelerates 1 kg by 1 m/s².
Weight follows at once: W = mg.

Remember: **F = ma — acceleration grows with net force and shrinks with mass.**
Remember: **1 N accelerates 1 kg by 1 m/s².**
Remember: **Weight W = mg.**
Keep the formula: **F = ma; W = mg**
