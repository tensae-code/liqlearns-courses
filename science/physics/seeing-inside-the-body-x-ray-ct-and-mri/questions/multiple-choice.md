# Seeing inside the body — X-ray, CT and MRI — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about medical physics is correct?**
a) A body is in equilibrium when the vector sum of forces is zero; problems balance several forces — including a mass…  b) A nucleus weighs less than its separate nucleons; the missing mass is the binding energy via E = mc² (worked in MeV…  c) Roentgen's first X-ray image of a hand opens the story.
**Answer: c) Roentgen's first X-ray image of a hand opens the story.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Which statement about medical physics is correct?**

**Answer: b) MRI uses magnetism and radio waves.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) MRI uses ______ism and radio waves.**

**Answer: b) magnet**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) Which statement about medical physics is correct?**
