# Hands-on — Seeing inside the body — X-ray, CT and MRI

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spot the physics
Walk through a clinic, a workshop or a market in your mind and list five places physics is at work in another field — then name the field for each.

## See it and say it
Study this lesson's picture — Roentgen's hand X-ray beside a modern MRI scanner (the book's own cover uses one) — and name every part of it out loud. Then close the picture and draw it from memory; check your drawing against the lesson.

## Teach it back
Explain this lesson to someone at home using one thing in the room as your example. Their questions show you which part to re-read.
