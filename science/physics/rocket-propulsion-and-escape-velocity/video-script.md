# Video script — Rocket propulsion and escape velocity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **rockets push on their own exhaust**. Throw mass backward, and momentum throws you forward — no air needed, which is why rockets work in space. Throw something up at 11.2 kilometres a second and it never comes back — that's escape velocity. Throw it sideways, slower, and it falls around the Earth forever: a satellite. Three speeds, one story.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
