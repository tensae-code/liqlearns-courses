# Hands-on — Rocket propulsion and escape velocity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Balloon rocket on a string
Thread a long string through a drinking straw and tie it across a room. Tape a blown-up (untied) balloon to the straw, hold the neck shut at one end, then let go. The balloon races along the string as its air rushes out the back. Repeat with the balloon half full and fully blown, and note which travels farther and why — more exhaust mass thrown backward, more forward momentum gained. You have run the rocket principle on a string.
