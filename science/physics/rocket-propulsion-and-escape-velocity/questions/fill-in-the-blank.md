# Rocket propulsion and escape velocity — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A rocket throws its _____ gases backward.
**Answer: exhaust**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The rocket principle is conservation of _____.
**Answer: momentum**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Escape velocity from Earth is about _____ km/s.
**Answer: 11.2**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A satellite's orbital speed is fixed by its _____.
**Answer: height**
