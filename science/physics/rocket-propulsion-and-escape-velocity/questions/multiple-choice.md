# Rocket propulsion and escape velocity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A rocket moves forward because…
a) It pushes on the air  b) Momentum is conserved — exhaust thrown backward pushes the rocket forward  c) Gravity pulls it up  d) Its fins steer it upward
**Answer: b) Momentum is conserved — exhaust thrown backward pushes the rocket forward**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A rocket can work in empty space because…
a) Space has hidden air  b) It pushes on its own exhaust, needing nothing outside itself  c) The Sun pulls it  d) It is lighter there
**Answer: b) It pushes on its own exhaust, needing nothing outside itself**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Escape velocity from Earth's surface is about…
a) 8 km/s  b) 11.2 km/s  c) 300 km/s  d) 1 km/s
**Answer: b) 11.2 km/s**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A body launched upward well below escape velocity will…
a) Leave Earth forever  b) Fall back (or orbit if launched sideways)  c) Speed up as it rises  d) Stop in mid-air
**Answer: b) Fall back (or orbit if launched sideways)**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A satellite in orbit is best described as…
a) Floating free of gravity  b) Forever falling toward Earth and forever missing it  c) Held up by its engine  d) Outside Earth's gravity
**Answer: b) Forever falling toward Earth and forever missing it**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A lower satellite orbit needs…
a) A slower speed  b) A faster speed  c) No speed  d) Escape velocity exactly
**Answer: b) A faster speed**
