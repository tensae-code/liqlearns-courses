# Video script — Earth the magnet — and the compass

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

The Earth itself behaves as a giant magnet, wrapped in a field that reaches far into space.
A compass is a small, free-turning magnetised needle: released, it swings until it settles along the Earth's field — that settling is what 'north' on a compass means.
The field also shields life, deflecting harmful charged particles from space.
The magnetic poles and the geographic poles are near each other but not the same points, and the angle between their directions at a place is its declination — the compass's small, known lie.

Remember: **Earth behaves as a giant magnet with a field reaching into space.**
Remember: **A compass needle aligns with Earth's field.**
Remember: **Magnetic and geographic poles differ; the local angle between them is declination.**
