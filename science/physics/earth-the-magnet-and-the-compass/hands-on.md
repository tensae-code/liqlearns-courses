# Hands-on — Earth the magnet — and the compass

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Watch any compass (or a phone's) while you turn in a circle: the needle refuses to turn with you — it keeps faith with the Earth instead.

## Apply it where you live
Explain to a traveller why a compass works anywhere on Earth but needs a declination correction for exact maps.

Study [Picture 21914 — Earth with field lines and a compass needle aligning to them] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
