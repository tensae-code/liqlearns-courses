# Earth the magnet — and the compass — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Why does a compass needle settle pointing north-ish?
**Answer: It aligns with Earth's magnetic field.**

**2.** What life-serving job does Earth's field do?
**Answer: It deflects harmful charged particles from space.**

**3.** What is declination?
**Answer: The angle between magnetic north and geographic (true) north at a place.**

**4.** Is a compass needle itself a magnet?
**Answer: Yes — a small permanent magnet free to turn.**
