# Earth the magnet — and the compass — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why does a compass needle settle pointing north-ish?
a) It aligns with Earth's magnetic field.  b) It deflects harmful charged particles from space.  c) The angle between magnetic north and geographic (true) north at a place.  d) Yes — a small permanent magnet free to turn.
**Answer: a) It aligns with Earth's magnetic field.**

**2.** What life-serving job does Earth's field do?
a) It aligns with Earth's magnetic field.  b) It deflects harmful charged particles from space.  c) The angle between magnetic north and geographic (true) north at a place.  d) Yes — a small permanent magnet free to turn.
**Answer: b) It deflects harmful charged particles from space.**

**3.** What is declination?
a) It aligns with Earth's magnetic field.  b) It deflects harmful charged particles from space.  c) The angle between magnetic north and geographic (true) north at a place.  d) Yes — a small permanent magnet free to turn.
**Answer: c) The angle between magnetic north and geographic (true) north at a place.**

**4.** Is a compass needle itself a magnet?
a) It aligns with Earth's magnetic field.  b) It deflects harmful charged particles from space.  c) The angle between magnetic north and geographic (true) north at a place.  d) Yes — a small permanent magnet free to turn.
**Answer: d) Yes — a small permanent magnet free to turn.**
