# Three-part assessment — diagnostic, formative, summative, with test specifications — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Assessment before teaching is called ___ assessment.
**Answer:** diagnostic

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Assessment that steers teaching while it happens is ___ assessment.
**Answer:** formative

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The end-of-topic certifying assessment is ___ assessment.
**Answer:** summative

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A test's blueprint matching items to outcomes is its ___.
**Answer:** specification
