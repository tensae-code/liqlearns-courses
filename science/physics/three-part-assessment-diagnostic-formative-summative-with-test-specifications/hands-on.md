# Hands-on — Three-part assessment — diagnostic, formative, summative, with test specifications

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with your hands
Take one topic and build its three assessments on one page: three diagnostic questions for the day before it starts; two formative checkpoints mid-topic with 'if they fail this, I will…' written beside each; and a specification grid for the end test — outcomes down the side, item numbers across. The page you have made is exactly the apparatus the Armenian guides ask for.
