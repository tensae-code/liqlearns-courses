# What heat is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about the concept of heat is correct?**
a) A bullet embeds in a suspended wooden block; momentum conservation through the hit plus energy conservation through the…  b) A compass needle turns beside a current-carrying wire: current makes a circular magnetic field (B = μ₀I/2πr for a long…  c) Heat is energy in transit between bodies at different temperatures; heating changes temperature, size and state — and…
**Answer: c) Heat is energy in transit between bodies at different temperatures; heating changes temperature, size and…**

**2. Heat is energy in transit between bodies at different ______s; heating changes temperature, size and state — and heat is distinguished from work and from internal energy, with the similarities/differences question set as an exercise.**
a) gravity  b) bridge  c) temperature
**Answer: c) temperature**
