Today: **storing magnets and keepers**. A magnet left open to the air spends its strength on itself. Put bar magnets away in pairs, unlike poles together, with soft-iron keepers across the ends, and the field runs in a closed iron circuit and the strength stays. One keeper does the same for a horseshoe. Keeping is half of magnetism.
