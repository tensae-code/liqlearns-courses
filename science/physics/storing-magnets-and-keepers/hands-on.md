# Hands-on — Storing magnets and keepers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Test the keeper
With a bar magnet, a keeper and a box of paper clips: first lift as many clips in a chain as the magnet will hold and count them. Store the magnet for the lesson with its keeper on, and a second magnet (if the kit has two) without. At the end of the lesson — and, better, at the start of next week's — test both magnets again on the clips and compare counts. Then try the pair arrangement: two magnets unlike-pole-to-unlike-pole with keepers at both ends, and feel how the keepers are held. Record which storage your hands would trust for a year, and why.
