# Storing magnets and keepers — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A magnet's strength leaks away in store when its _____ are shaken out of line.
**Answer: domains**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The soft-iron pieces laid across the ends of a stored pair of bar magnets are called _____.
**Answer: keepers**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Bar magnets are stored with _____ poles together — north beside south.
**Answer: unlike**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Heat, hammering and dropping all _____ a magnet.
**Answer: demagnetise (weaken)**
