# Storing magnets and keepers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A lone bar magnet left in a drawer weakens mainly by…
**Answer: a) self-demagnetisation — its own field working back through the bar  b) rust alone  c) the Earth's field  d) gravity**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these demagnetises a magnet?
**Answer: a) heating it and hammering it  b) wrapping it in cloth  c) keeping it cold  d) labelling its poles**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Bar magnets are stored in pairs with…
**Answer: a) unlike poles together  b) like poles together  c) poles pointing north  d) one magnet inside the other**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A keeper is made of…
**Answer: a) soft iron  b) steel  c) copper  d) plastic**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The keepers' job is to…
**Answer: a) close the magnetic circuit through iron instead of air  b) make the magnets heavier  c) mark the poles  d) stop the magnets touching**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A horseshoe magnet is stored with…
**Answer: a) one keeper across both its poles  b) two keepers along its back  c) its poles wrapped in paper  d) another horseshoe on top of it**
