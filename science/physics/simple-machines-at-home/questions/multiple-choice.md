# Simple machines at home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What machine is a knife?
a) A wedge.  b) A lever (bottle opener).  c) Levers and wedges.  d) At the broom's head — the far end from your top hand.
**Answer: a) A wedge.**

**2.** What machine opens a bottle cap?
a) A wedge.  b) A lever (bottle opener).  c) Levers and wedges.  d) At the broom's head — the far end from your top hand.
**Answer: b) A lever (bottle opener).**

**3.** Scissors contain which machines?
a) A wedge.  b) A lever (bottle opener).  c) Levers and wedges.  d) At the broom's head — the far end from your top hand.
**Answer: c) Levers and wedges.**

**4.** Where is the load when you sweep with a broom?
a) A wedge.  b) A lever (bottle opener).  c) Levers and wedges.  d) At the broom's head — the far end from your top hand.
**Answer: d) At the broom's head — the far end from your top hand.**
