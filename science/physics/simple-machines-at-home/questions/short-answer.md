# Simple machines at home — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** What machine is a knife?
**Answer: A wedge.**

**2.** What machine opens a bottle cap?
**Answer: A lever (bottle opener).**

**3.** Scissors contain which machines?
**Answer: Levers and wedges.**

**4.** Where is the load when you sweep with a broom?
**Answer: At the broom's head — the far end from your top hand.**
