# Video script — Simple machines at home

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Your home is full of simple machines hiding in plain sight.
A knife is a wedge, scissors are two levers sharing a pivot — and a wedge each — a bottle opener is a lever, and a broom is a lever you swing.
Naming the machine inside each tool tells you how it helps: where the effort goes in, where the load is, and which of the three purposes it serves.

Remember: **A knife is a wedge; scissors combine levers and wedges.**
Remember: **A bottle opener is a lever.**
Remember: **A broom is a lever used as a speed multiplier.**
