# Hands-on — Simple machines at home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Open three kitchen drawers in your mind and hunt: find a wedge, a lever and a wheel. Name the load each one moves.

## Apply it where you live
Draw your kitchen and ring every simple machine, labelling its type.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21837 — kitchen scene with machines ringed — knife, scissors, bottle opener, broom] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
