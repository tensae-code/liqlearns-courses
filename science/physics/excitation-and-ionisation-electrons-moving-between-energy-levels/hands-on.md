# Hands-on — Excitation and ionisation — electrons moving between energy levels

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The ladder with your hands
Draw the ladder on the floor with chalk or tape: four rungs, spacing shrinking upward, and a line at the top marked FREE. One learner is the electron, standing on the ground rung. The class calls out energy deliveries — 'one small photon', 'a hard collision', 'heat' — and the electron must jump: up one rung for excitation (and straight back down, calling out 'light!' as the energy returns), or off the ladder entirely for ionisation, naming the ion left behind. The class judges each call: was that energy enough for what the electron did?
