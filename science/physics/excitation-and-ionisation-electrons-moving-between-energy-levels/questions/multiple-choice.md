# Excitation and ionisation — electrons moving between energy levels — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The lowest energy level of an atom is called…
**Answer: a) the ground state  b) the ionised state  c) the free state  d) the valence state**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) On an energy-level diagram, higher levels are drawn…
**Answer: a) closer together  b) further apart  c) below the ground state  d) as circles**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Excitation is when an electron…
**Answer: a) absorbs energy and jumps to a higher level, staying in the atom  b) leaves the atom  c) falls to the ground state  d) splits in two**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) When an excited electron falls back down, the energy is given out…
**Answer: a) often as a photon of light equal to the level difference  b) as sound  c) never  d) as a new electron**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Ionisation is when the electron…
**Answer: a) leaves the atom altogether  b) jumps one level up  c) jumps one level down  d) changes into a proton**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) After ionisation, the atom is left as…
**Answer: a) a positive ion  b) a neutral atom  c) a negative ion  d) a molecule**
