# Excitation and ionisation — electrons moving between energy levels — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An electron can sit on a level but never _____ levels.
**Answer: between**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) An atom whose electron has been raised to a higher level is in an _____ state.
**Answer: excited**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The minimum energy needed to remove an electron from the ground state is the _____ energy.
**Answer: ionisation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Excitation energy can arrive by photon, by collision, or by _____.
**Answer: heat**
