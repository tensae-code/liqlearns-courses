# Video script — Excitation and ionisation — electrons moving between energy levels

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **excitation and ionisation**. An atom's electron lives on a ladder of fixed energy levels. A measured gift of energy lifts it a rung — excitation — and it falls back, paying the energy out as light. A big enough gift lifts it clean off the ladder — ionisation — and the atom is left an ion. Learn these two words and the whole quantum topic that follows is bookkeeping.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
