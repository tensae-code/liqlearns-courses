# Refraction and total internal reflection — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1.** Refraction: light bends as its speed changes at a boundary.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Refractive index n = c ÷ speed in the medium.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Total internal reflection happens when light enters a denser medium from air.
**Answer: False**
