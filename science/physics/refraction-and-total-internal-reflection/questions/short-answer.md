# Refraction and total internal reflection — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Light enters water from air (slowing down). It bends — toward or away from the normal?
**Answer: Toward the normal.**

**2.** A medium slows light to 2.0×10⁸ m/s. Its refractive index?
**Answer: n = 3.00×10⁸ ÷ 2.0×10⁸ = 1.5.**

**3.** What is the critical angle?
**Answer: The incidence angle at which the refracted ray would skim the surface (90° out).**

**4.** Name one use of total internal reflection.
**Answer: Optical fibres (or diamond sparkle).**
