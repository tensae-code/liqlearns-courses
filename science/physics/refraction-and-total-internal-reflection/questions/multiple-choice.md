# Refraction and total internal reflection — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Light enters water from air (slowing down). It bends — toward or away from the normal?
a) Toward the normal.  b) n = 3.00×10⁸ ÷ 2.0×10⁸ = 1.5.  c) The incidence angle at which the refracted ray would skim the surface (90° out).  d) Optical fibres (or diamond sparkle).
