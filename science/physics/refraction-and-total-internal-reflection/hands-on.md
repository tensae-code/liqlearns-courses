# Hands-on — Refraction and total internal reflection

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Lay a coin under a glass of water and hunt the angle where it vanishes and reappears as you move your head — the boundary is negotiating with your light.

## Apply it where you live
Explain an optical fibre's working in four steps using TIR.
