## Refraction and total internal reflection — the teaching
Crossing into a medium where its speed changes, light bends: entering a denser medium it bends toward the normal; leaving, away from it. The refractive index measures the slowing: n = c ÷ (speed in the medium). Steepen the angle inside the denser medium and you reach the critical angle, where the ray would skim the surface; beyond it, no light escapes at all — total internal reflection bounces it back inside. Diamonds sparkle by it, and optical fibres pipe light around corners by it.

**Keep the formula:** n = c / v

## The key ideas

- Refraction: light bends as its speed changes at a boundary.
- Refractive index n = c ÷ speed in the medium.
- Beyond the critical angle: total internal reflection — light stays inside.
