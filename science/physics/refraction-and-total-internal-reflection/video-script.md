# Video script — Refraction and total internal reflection

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Crossing into a medium where its speed changes, light bends: entering a denser medium it bends toward the normal; leaving, away from it.
The refractive index measures the slowing: n = c ÷ (speed in the medium).
Steepen the angle inside the denser medium and you reach the critical angle, where the ray would skim the surface; beyond it, no light escapes at all — total internal reflection bounces it back inside.
Diamonds sparkle by it, and optical fibres pipe light around corners by it.

Remember: **Refraction: light bends as its speed changes at a boundary.**
Remember: **Refractive index n = c ÷ speed in the medium.**
Remember: **Beyond the critical angle: total internal reflection — light stays inside.**
Keep the formula: **n = c / v**
