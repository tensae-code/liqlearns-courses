# WALT and WILF — the Lao guide's learner-centred lesson framing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) WALT stands for…
**Answer: a) We Are Learning To  b) Write And Learn Together  c) What A Lesson Teaches  d) Weekly Assessment Learning Target**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A good WALT line names…
**Answer: a) the learning, not just the activity  b) the page number  c) the homework  d) the teacher's name**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) WILF stands for…
**Answer: a) What I am Looking For  b) Work In Lesson Files  c) Why I Learn Facts  d) Write It, Learn Fast**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) WILF is used by learners to…
**Answer: a) check themselves against the success signs during and after the lesson  b) mark each other's books for grades  c) choose the next textbook  d) replace the lesson's exercises**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In the guide's method, the teacher works mainly as a…
**Answer: a) facilitator of the learners' activity and thinking  b) reader of the textbook aloud  c) examiner only  d) note dictator**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) This lesson's source footing is…
**Answer: a) the Grade 11 teacher guide's teaching-methods section — teacher-guide evidence only  b) the Grade 11 student book  c) the Grade 12 student book  d) a Lao ministry website**
