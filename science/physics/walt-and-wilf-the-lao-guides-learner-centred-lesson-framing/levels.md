# WALT and WILF — the Lao guide's learner-centred lesson framing — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — WALT = 'We are learning to…' — the lesson's aim, written on the board for learners; WILF = 'What I am looking for…' — the success signs learners can check themselves against; The teacher works as facilitator; mind maps and higher-order thinking are the guide's named methods — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
