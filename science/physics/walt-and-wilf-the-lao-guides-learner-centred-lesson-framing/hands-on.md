# Hands-on — WALT and WILF — the Lao guide's learner-centred lesson framing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Frame your next study session
Before your next study session, write its WALT line at the top of your page — 'I am learning to…' — and three WILF signs beneath it: the things you will be able to show at the end. Study, then check yourself against the WILF list honestly and tick only what you can actually do. The Lao guide's framing, run on your own learning, with nothing but paper and a pen.
