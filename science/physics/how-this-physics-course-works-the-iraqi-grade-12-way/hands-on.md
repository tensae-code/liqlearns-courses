# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Iraqi Grade 12 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run a chapter the Iraqi way
Take one small topic you already know. Write its vocabulary list first — each term in Arabic and English side by side — then do one hands-on activity for it, work one example aloud step by step, and finish by writing yourself a two-question test: one short-answer, one multiple-choice. Say aloud: vocabulary first, activity, worked example, review, test — that is the Iraqi order.
