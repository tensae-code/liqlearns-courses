# Lesson plan — How this Physics course works — the Iraqi Grade 12 way

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** How this Physics course works — the Iraqi Grade 12 way
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18) — a new lesson built for the Iraq placement named in lesson/sources.md
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Iraqi Grade 12 book's own way of teaching physics — the Tenth edition, 2024 / 1446H, whose contents-at-the-back design (printed p. 284) and per-chapter vocabulary openers state the design; every lesson or chapter opens with a hands-on activity or a chapter vocabulary list (مفردات الفصل), runs through worked examples with bilingual Arabic–English technical terms printed side by side, and closes with a vocabulary/concept review plus short-answer and multiple-choice chapter tests
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Earlier lessons make a good preparation and are recommended, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The book's shape — 9 chapters** — The Grade 12 (Sixth Scientific) book runs in 9 chapters: capacitors; electromagnetic induction; alternating current; electromagnetic waves; physical optics; modern physics; solid-state electronics; atomic spectra and the laser; and nuclear physics. Its contents table is printed at the back of the book, on the last page.
2. **How every lesson is built — activity or vocabulary first, bilingual terms, worked examples** — Each Grade 12 chapter opener lists its vocabulary (مفردات الفصل) with English titles alongside the Arabic, lessons open with activity or vocabulary, and worked examples — like the worked gold-nucleus example in the nuclear chapter — carry the teaching step by step.
3. **How every chapter closes — vocabulary review and a chapter test** — The Grade 12 book closes each chapter with its vocabulary/concept review and a chapter test of short-answer and multiple-choice questions, chapter by chapter through to nuclear physics at the book's end.
