# How this Physics course works — the Iraqi Grade 7 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Describe the shape of the Iraqi Grade 7 book in one sentence.
**Answer: The Grade 7 book runs in 5 chapters and 12 lessons: matter — its states, measuring its volume and its density; force and its kinds; pressure, liquid and gas pressure and Archimedes' principle; heat and how it travels; and heat's effects — expansion and changes of state.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A learner opens a new Iraqi chapter. What two things does the book put in front of them first, and why?
**Answer: A hands-on activity or the chapter vocabulary list (مفردات الفصل): the activity gives the idea a real start in the hands, and the vocabulary names the chapter's technical words — in Arabic and English side by side — before they are used, so no term arrives unexplained.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How does an Iraqi chapter end, and what is each closing part for?
**Answer: With a vocabulary/concept review, which fixes the chapter's words and ideas, and a chapter test of short-answer and multiple-choice questions, which checks them.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does printing terms in two languages help a learner?
**Answer: The learner meets the everyday Arabic word and the international English term together, so they can read either one later — in another book, an exam or a laboratory — and know it is the same idea.**
