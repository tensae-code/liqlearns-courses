Today: **attenuation — why signals fade**. Geometry taxes every spreading wave with the inverse square; the medium takes more by absorbing and scattering; cables bleed signal into heat. Engineers count the losses in decibels and answer three ways: amplify the signal, repeat it fresh, or guide it — in fibre — so it never gets to spread.
