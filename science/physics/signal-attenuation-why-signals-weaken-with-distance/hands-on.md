# Hands-on — Signal attenuation — why signals weaken with distance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Square-law with a torch
In a dark room, shine a torch at a wall from 1 m and note the bright disc's diameter; step back to 2 m and 3 m and note again: the disc's diameter grows in proportion to distance — its area with distance squared — while the same light paints it, so the brightness per patch falls as 1/distance². Your three observations are the spreading law: at 2 m each patch gets a quarter of the light, at 3 m a ninth. A radio receiver lives inside the same arithmetic.
