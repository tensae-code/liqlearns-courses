# Signal attenuation — why signals weaken with distance — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The weakening of a signal on its journey is called _____.
**Answer: attenuation**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Losses are measured in _____, where −3 of them halve the power.
**Answer: decibels**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In an optical fibre, light is guided by total internal _____.
**Answer: reflection**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A _____ receives a weakened signal and re-transmits it at full strength.
**Answer: repeater**
