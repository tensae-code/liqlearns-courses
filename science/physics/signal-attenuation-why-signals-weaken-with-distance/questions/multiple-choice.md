# Signal attenuation — why signals weaken with distance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A wave spreading equally in all directions loses intensity with distance by…
**Answer: a) the inverse-square law — double the distance, a quarter of the intensity  b) simple halving per metre  c) no law — it is constant  d) the cube of the distance**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A dish aerial helps against attenuation because it…
**Answer: a) concentrates the radiation into a narrow beam instead of a whole sphere  b) amplifies noise  c) absorbs rain  d) shortens the distance**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In a copper cable the signal's lost energy mostly becomes…
**Answer: a) heat in the wire's resistance  b) light  c) sound  d) static charge**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A loss of 10 dB leaves the signal power at…
**Answer: a) a tenth of what it was  b) a half  c) a hundredth  d) nine tenths**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A repeater's advantage over a simple amplifier is that it…
**Answer: a) receives, cleans up and re-transmits the signal at full strength  b) needs no power  c) removes the inverse-square law  d) works only for analogue signals**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Digital signals survive long chains of repeaters best because…
**Answer: a) their 0/1 pattern can be recognised and regenerated exactly at each repeater  b) they travel faster than light  c) they are not attenuated at all  d) they use no carrier**
