# Hands-on — The eye — and fixing its focus

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Hold a finger at arm's length, then bring it close while feeling your eyes work — that effort is accommodation, live.

## Apply it where you live
For each defect, draw the eye's ray diagram before and after its correcting lens.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21937 — eye cross-section with rays focusing on, before and behind the retina, plus the correcting lens for each] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
