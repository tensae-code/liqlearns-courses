# Video script — The eye — and fixing its focus

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

The eye is a camera: the cornea and the lens focus light onto the retina at the back.
The lens changes shape to focus near and far objects — accommodation — keeping the image on the retina.
In short sight (myopia), the eye focuses in front of the retina — distant objects blur.
The correction is a diverging (concave) lens, which spreads the rays so focus falls back onto the retina.
In long sight (hyperopia), focus falls behind the retina — near objects blur.
The correction is a converging (convex) lens, adding focusing power.
Lenses are prescribed by power and focal length — the right lens moves the image exactly onto the retina.

Remember: **The eye focuses with cornea and lens onto the retina; changing shape is accommodation.**
Remember: **Short sight: focus in front of the retina; corrected with a diverging lens.**
Remember: **Long sight: focus behind the retina; corrected with a converging lens.**
