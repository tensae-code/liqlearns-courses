# The eye — and fixing its focus

## Do it first
Hold a finger at arm's length, then bring it close while feeling your eyes work — that effort is accommodation, live.

[Picture 21937 — eye cross-section with rays focusing on, before and behind the retina, plus the correcting lens for each]

## Sub lesson 1 — How the eye focuses
The eye is a camera: the cornea and the lens focus light onto the retina at the back. The lens changes shape to focus near and far objects — accommodation — keeping the image on the retina.

## Sub lesson 2 — Short sight and its correction
In short sight (myopia), the eye focuses in front of the retina — distant objects blur. The correction is a diverging (concave) lens, which spreads the rays so focus falls back onto the retina.

## Sub lesson 3 — Long sight and its correction
In long sight (hyperopia), focus falls behind the retina — near objects blur. The correction is a converging (convex) lens, adding focusing power. Lenses are prescribed by power and focal length — the right lens moves the image exactly onto the retina.

## The key ideas

- The eye focuses with cornea and lens onto the retina; changing shape is accommodation.
- Short sight: focus in front of the retina; corrected with a diverging lens.
- Long sight: focus behind the retina; corrected with a converging lens.
