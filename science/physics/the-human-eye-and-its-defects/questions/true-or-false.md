# The eye — and fixing its focus — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The eye focuses with cornea and lens onto the retina; changing shape is accommodation.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Short sight: focus in front of the retina; corrected with a diverging lens.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Short sight is corrected with a converging lens.
**Answer: False**
