# The eye — and fixing its focus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where must the image fall for clear vision?
a) On the retina.  b) The lens changing shape to focus near and far objects.  c) In front of the retina; a diverging (concave) lens.  d) Behind the retina; a converging (convex) lens.
**Answer: a) On the retina.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is accommodation?
a) On the retina.  b) The lens changing shape to focus near and far objects.  c) In front of the retina; a diverging (concave) lens.  d) Behind the retina; a converging (convex) lens.
**Answer: b) The lens changing shape to focus near and far objects.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A short-sighted eye focuses where, and is fixed by which lens?
a) On the retina.  b) The lens changing shape to focus near and far objects.  c) In front of the retina; a diverging (concave) lens.  d) Behind the retina; a converging (convex) lens.
**Answer: c) In front of the retina; a diverging (concave) lens.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A long-sighted eye focuses where, and is fixed by which lens?
a) On the retina.  b) The lens changing shape to focus near and far objects.  c) In front of the retina; a diverging (concave) lens.  d) Behind the retina; a converging (convex) lens.
**Answer: d) Behind the retina; a converging (convex) lens.**
