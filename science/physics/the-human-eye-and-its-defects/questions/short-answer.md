# The eye — and fixing its focus — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where must the image fall for clear vision?
**Answer: On the retina.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is accommodation?
**Answer: The lens changing shape to focus near and far objects.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A short-sighted eye focuses where, and is fixed by which lens?
**Answer: In front of the retina; a diverging (concave) lens.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A long-sighted eye focuses where, and is fixed by which lens?
**Answer: Behind the retina; a converging (convex) lens.**
