# Staying safe in extreme heat and cold — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** At 45 °C heat, what does the book's band say?
a) Exhaustion likely — limit activity (40–54 °C band).  b) About 54 °C.  c) Any two of: hydrate, dress suitably, sunscreen, rest/shade.  d) Cramps and exhaustion are possible.
**Answer: a) Exhaustion likely — limit activity (40–54 °C band).**

**2.** Above roughly what temperature does heat stroke threaten?
a) Exhaustion likely — limit activity (40–54 °C band).  b) About 54 °C.  c) Any two of: hydrate, dress suitably, sunscreen, rest/shade.  d) Cramps and exhaustion are possible.
**Answer: b) About 54 °C.**

**3.** Name two precautions against heat harm.
a) Exhaustion likely — limit activity (40–54 °C band).  b) About 54 °C.  c) Any two of: hydrate, dress suitably, sunscreen, rest/shade.  d) Cramps and exhaustion are possible.
**Answer: c) Any two of: hydrate, dress suitably, sunscreen, rest/shade.**

**4.** What is the first band's warning (32–40 °C)?
a) Exhaustion likely — limit activity (40–54 °C band).  b) About 54 °C.  c) Any two of: hydrate, dress suitably, sunscreen, rest/shade.  d) Cramps and exhaustion are possible.
**Answer: d) Cramps and exhaustion are possible.**
