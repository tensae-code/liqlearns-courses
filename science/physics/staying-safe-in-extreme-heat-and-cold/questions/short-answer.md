# Staying safe in extreme heat and cold — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** At 45 °C heat, what does the book's band say?
**Answer: Exhaustion likely — limit activity (40–54 °C band).**

**2.** Above roughly what temperature does heat stroke threaten?
**Answer: About 54 °C.**

**3.** Name two precautions against heat harm.
**Answer: Any two of: hydrate, dress suitably, sunscreen, rest/shade.**

**4.** What is the first band's warning (32–40 °C)?
**Answer: Cramps and exhaustion are possible.**
