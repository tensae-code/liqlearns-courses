# Staying safe in extreme heat and cold — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1.** 32–40 °C: heat cramps and exhaustion possible.
**Answer: True**

**2.** 40–54 °C: exhaustion likely — limit activity; above ~54 °C: heat stroke risk.
**Answer: True**

**3.** At 50 °C the right response is to keep working at full pace without water.
**Answer: False**
