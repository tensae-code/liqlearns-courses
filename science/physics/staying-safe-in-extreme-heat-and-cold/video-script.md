# Video script — Staying safe in extreme heat and cold

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Extreme heat harms in bands.
Around 32–40 °C, cramps and exhaustion become possible.
From 40–54 °C, exhaustion is likely — limit activity, rest and drink.
Above about 54 °C, heat stroke threatens, which can kill.
The defences are plain: hydrate before you feel desperate, dress for the environment, use sunscreen, and respect shade and rest.
Extreme cold demands its own care — cover skin, keep dry, and keep moving warmly.

Remember: **32–40 °C: heat cramps and exhaustion possible.**
Remember: **40–54 °C: exhaustion likely — limit activity; above ~54 °C: heat stroke risk.**
Remember: **Defences: hydrate, dress for the environment, sunscreen, rest in shade.**
