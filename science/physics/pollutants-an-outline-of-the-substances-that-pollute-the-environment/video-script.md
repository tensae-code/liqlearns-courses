# Video script — Pollutants — an outline of the substances that pollute the environment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the pollutants, by medium**. Air gets combustion's family — carbon monoxide, sulfur and nitrogen oxides, particulates, ozone, and the greenhouse gases. Water gets fertiliser runoff that suffocates by eutrophication, heavy metals that climb food chains, oil, sewage and heat. Land keeps the persistent solids — plastics, pesticides, metals. Name the substance, name the source: that is the whole outline.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
