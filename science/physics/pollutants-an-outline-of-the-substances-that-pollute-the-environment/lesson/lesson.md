# Pollutants — an outline of the substances that pollute the environment

## Do it first
Before you read: read the label of any cleaning product or spray in your home, and look at the exhaust end of any engine nearby after it runs. One lists chemicals by name; the other shows you a plume you can smell. Both are this lesson's subject — substances in the wrong place at the wrong amount. The lesson's job is to name them by medium, with their sources, so they can be argued about precisely.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pollutants of water and land

Heat and **noise** complete the outline as the energy-form pollutants: not substances, but energy released in the wrong place — a reminder that pollution is defined by harm and excess, not by chemistry alone. Vietnam's sequence then turns from the catalogue of harms to the survey of remedies' tools — renewable harvesting — in the topic's last lesson.

## The key ideas

- A pollutant is any substance — or energy form such as noise or waste heat — released beyond the environment's power to absorb it, where it does harm
- Air: carbon monoxide, sulfur dioxide, nitrogen oxides, particulates, ground-level ozone, and the greenhouse gases CO₂ and methane — mostly combustion's children
- Water: fertiliser runoff (eutrophication), heavy metals, industrial chemicals, oil, sewage pathogens and waste heat
- Land: persistent plastics, pesticide residues and heavy metals — materials no natural cycle reclaims

## Say it to your companion
Tell your companion: A pollutant is a substance (or an energy form, like noise and excess heat) released into air, water or land in amounts the environment cannot absorb, where it harms living things or the systems they depend on: air pollutants such as carbon monoxide, sulfur and nitrogen oxides, particulates and greenhouse gases; water pollutants such as fertiliser runoff, industrial chemicals, heavy metals and pathogens carried in waste; land pollutants such as persistent plastics, pesticides and heavy-metal residues — each named with its main source. If your companion can work the Do It Yourself tasks without looking back, the lesson has landed.
