# Pollutants — an outline of the substances that pollute the environment — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Smoke and soot particles fine enough to enter lungs are called _____.
**Answer: particulates**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The two main greenhouse gases named are carbon dioxide and _____.
**Answer: methane**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Fertiliser _____ carries nitrate and phosphate from fields into water.
**Answer: runoff**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Plastics are land pollutants because of their _____ — no cycle reclaims them.
**Answer: persistence**
