# Pollutants — an outline of the substances that pollute the environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A pollutant is best defined as…
**Answer: a) a substance or energy form released in amounts the environment cannot absorb, where it does harm  b) anything artificial  c) anything with a chemical name  d) only gases**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Carbon monoxide is dangerous mainly because it…
**Answer: a) interferes with the blood's carrying of oxygen  b) causes acid rain  c) warms the planet directly  d) blocks sunlight**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Acid rain's parent pollutants are…
**Answer: a) sulfur dioxide and nitrogen oxides from burning fossil fuels  b) carbon dioxide and methane  c) ozone and dust  d) lead and mercury**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Eutrophication is…
**Answer: a) overfeeding of water plants and algae by nitrate/phosphate runoff, ending in oxygen-starved water  b) poisoning by mercury  c) heating of river water  d) oil coating a surface**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Heavy metals grow more dangerous up a food chain because they…
**Answer: a) are stored in bodies and concentrated at each feeding step  b) evaporate  c) decay quickly  d) dissolve completely**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Noise and waste heat count as pollutants in this outline because pollution is defined by…
**Answer: a) harm and excess, not by being a chemical substance  b) colour  c) smell  d) radioactivity**
