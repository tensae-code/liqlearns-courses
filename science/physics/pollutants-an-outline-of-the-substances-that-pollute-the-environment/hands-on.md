# Hands-on — Pollutants — an outline of the substances that pollute the environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hunt the sources in one street
On a short walk (or from your door), list every visible source that releases something into air, water or land: a motorbike exhaust, a drain running to a stream, a rubbish pile, a kitchen chimney, a construction pile of dust. For each, write the medium polluted and your best name for the pollutant (smoke/particulates, exhaust gases, runoff, dust). Compare your list with the lesson's catalogue: most streets produce most of the catalogue within a hundred metres.
