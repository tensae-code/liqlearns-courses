# Doping — making N-type and P-type — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about doping is correct?**
a) The two sciences meet in matter made of electrons and nuclei: atomic physics explains covalent and ionic bonding and…  b) Adding impurities makes carriers: donor atoms (arsenic among the examples) free extra electrons (N-type); acceptor…  c) MRI uses magnetism and radio waves, and the X-ray CT scan builds cross-sections into a 3-D view.
**Answer: b) Adding impurities makes carriers: donor atoms (arsenic among the examples) free extra electrons (N-type);…**

**2. Adding impurities makes carriers: donor atoms (arsenic among the examples) free extra ______s (N-type); acceptor atoms (boron, gallium) leave holes (P-type).**
a) summary  b) electron  c) specific heat
**Answer: b) electron**
