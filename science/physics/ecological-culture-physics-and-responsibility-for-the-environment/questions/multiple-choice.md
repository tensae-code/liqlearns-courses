# Ecological culture — physics and responsibility for the environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Semipalatinsk site appears in the Kazakh closing chapter as…
a) A triumph of physics  b) A nuclear test site whose damage shows physics' ecological responsibility  c) A solar farm  d) A nature reserve
**Answer: b) A nuclear test site whose damage shows physics' ecological responsibility**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Vernadsky's noosphere is…
a) The layer of air  b) The sphere of human thought reshaping the planet  c) The Earth's core  d) A kind of radiation
**Answer: b) The sphere of human thought reshaping the planet**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Smog over cities is built largely from…
a) Steam only  b) Heat-engine exhaust gases  c) Moonlight  d) Pollen
**Answer: b) Heat-engine exhaust gases**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The greenhouse warming from engines is driven mainly by…
a) Carbon dioxide added to the air  b) Oxygen  c) Nitrogen  d) Water vapour from radiators
**Answer: a) Carbon dioxide added to the air**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Comparing energy sources honestly means comparing their ecological price…
a) Per factory  b) Per joule delivered  c) Per worker  d) Per year only
**Answer: b) Per joule delivered**
