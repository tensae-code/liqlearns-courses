# Ecological culture — physics and responsibility for the environment — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Kazakh course's final section is titled "___ culture".
**Answer:** Ecological

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Physics as part of ___ means its responsibilities are inside the subject.
**Answer:** culture

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Exhaust gases gathering over a city form ___.
**Answer:** smog

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Raising an engine's ___ wastes less fuel for the same work.
**Answer:** efficiency

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Wind and sun are ___ energy sources Kazakhstan is developing.
**Answer:** renewable
