# Ecological culture — physics and responsibility for the environment — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — the Kazakh course ends by handing the learner the bill: every joule physics delivers has an ecological price — exhaust, smog, warming, testing grounds — and an ecological culture is knowing the price, counting it honestly, and choosing how to pay less of it — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
