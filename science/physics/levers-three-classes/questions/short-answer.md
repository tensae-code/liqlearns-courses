# Levers — three classes — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A seesaw is which class?
**Answer: First class — fulcrum in the middle.**

**2.** Effort arm 1.2 m, load arm 0.3 m. VR?
**Answer: VR = 1.2 ÷ 0.3 = 4.**

**3.** A bottle opener is which class?
**Answer: Second class — load in the middle.**

**4.** Third-class levers trade what for what?
**Answer: They trade force for speed/distance of the load.**
