# Hands-on — Levers — three classes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Make a lever from a ruler over a pencil fulcrum and lift a heavy book with one finger. Slide the fulcrum and feel the VR change.

## Apply it where you live
Classify every lever in your home — scissors, opener, broom, tweezers — by which point sits in the middle.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21842 — three lever classes with F, L, E marked in different orders] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using VR = effort arm / load arm: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
