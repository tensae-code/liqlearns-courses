# Video script — Levers — three classes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

A lever is a rigid bar turning about a fixed point, the fulcrum.
Three points matter: fulcrum (F), load (L) and effort (E) — and the class is set by which sits in the middle.
First class, fulcrum in the middle: seesaw, scissors, crowbar.
Second class, load in the middle: wheelbarrow, nutcracker, bottle opener.
Third class, effort in the middle: the load moves faster and farther, at the price of force.
Lever ratios come from the arms: VR = effort arm ÷ load arm.
Archimedes boasted that with a place to stand and a long enough lever he could move the Earth.

Remember: **The lever's class is set by which of F, L, E sits in the middle.**
