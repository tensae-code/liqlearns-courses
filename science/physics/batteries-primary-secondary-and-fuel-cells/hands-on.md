# Hands-on — Batteries — primary, secondary and fuel cells

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the cells
Collect or name six real cells or batteries — torch cell, remote-control cell, phone battery, car battery, laptop battery, and any fuel cell you can find described. Write each one's kind (primary, secondary, fuel) and the evidence you used: its voltage, any 'rechargeable' marking, or its fuel. Say aloud the rule: spent and thrown away — primary; spent and charged again — secondary; fed while it runs — fuel cell.
