# Batteries — primary, secondary and fuel cells — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The push a cell gives each coulomb of charge is its electromotive _____.
**Answer: force**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A dry cell is a _____ cell: use once, then throw away.
**Answer: primary**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The car battery is a _____ cell battery — it is recharged by the car's charging system.
**Answer: secondary**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A hydrogen fuel cell takes in hydrogen and oxygen and gives out _____ and electric current.
**Answer: water**

**5.** (Sub lesson 1) Cells joined in _____ have their voltages add together.
**Answer: series**
