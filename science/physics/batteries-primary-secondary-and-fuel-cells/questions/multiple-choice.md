# Batteries — primary, secondary and fuel cells — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A cell's electromotive force (EMF) is…
a) Its size  b) The push it gives each coulomb of charge, in volts  c) Its colour  d) The current in a wire
**Answer: b) The push it gives each coulomb of charge, in volts**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Joining cells in series…
a) Cancels their pushes  b) Adds their pushes together  c) Halves the push  d) Stops the current
**Answer: b) Adds their pushes together**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A primary cell is…
a) Rechargeable hundreds of times  b) Used once and thrown away when its chemicals are spent  c) Fed with hydrogen  d) A kind of wire
**Answer: b) Used once and thrown away when its chemicals are spent**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The dry cell in a torch is…
a) A secondary cell  b) A fuel cell  c) A primary cell  d) Not a cell
**Answer: c) A primary cell**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A secondary cell is recharged by…
a) Heating it  b) Passing a current backwards through it, restoring its chemistry  c) Shaking it  d) Adding water only
**Answer: b) Passing a current backwards through it, restoring its chemistry**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A fuel cell differs from both other kinds because…
a) It has no electrodes  b) It makes electricity continuously from fuel fed in from outside, for as long as it is fed  c) It is thrown away after one use  d) It stores no energy at all in any form
**Answer: b) It makes electricity continuously from fuel fed in from outside, for as long as it is fed**
