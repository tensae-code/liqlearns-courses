Today: **batteries — primary, secondary and fuel cells**.

A battery is stored chemical push. A primary cell spends its chemicals once and is thrown away. A secondary cell takes a backwards current and is restored, again and after again. And a fuel cell is fed — hydrogen and oxygen in, water and current out — for as long as the feeding lasts.
