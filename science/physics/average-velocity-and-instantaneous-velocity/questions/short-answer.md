# Average velocity and instantaneous velocity — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A body moves 60 m east in 12 s. Average velocity?
**Answer: 60 ÷ 12 = 5 m/s east.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is a round trip's average velocity zero?
**Answer: The displacement is zero — start and finish are the same place.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How is instantaneous velocity read from a position–time graph?
**Answer: From the slope of the tangent at that point.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Average speed on a round trip — zero or not?
**Answer: Not zero — distance was travelled; only displacement is zero.**
