# Average velocity and instantaneous velocity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A body moves 60 m east in 12 s. Average velocity?
a) 60 ÷ 12 = 5 m/s east.  b) The displacement is zero — start and finish are the same place.  c) From the slope of the tangent at that point.  d) Not zero — distance was travelled; only displacement is zero.
**Answer: a) 60 ÷ 12 = 5 m/s east.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is a round trip's average velocity zero?
a) 60 ÷ 12 = 5 m/s east.  b) The displacement is zero — start and finish are the same place.  c) From the slope of the tangent at that point.  d) Not zero — distance was travelled; only displacement is zero.
**Answer: b) The displacement is zero — start and finish are the same place.**
