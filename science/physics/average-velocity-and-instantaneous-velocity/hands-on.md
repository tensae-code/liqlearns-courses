# Hands-on — Average velocity and instantaneous velocity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Walk 10 steps east, then 10 steps west back to your mark, timed. Compute both average speed and average velocity and feel why they differ.

## Apply it where you live
For one journey, write its average speed and average velocity side by side and explain any difference.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21817 — out-and-back journey with the displacement arrow shrinking to zero; position–time graph with chord and tangent] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using Average velocity = displacement / time: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
