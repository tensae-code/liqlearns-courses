# The strength of solids — Beruni the mineralogist — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Beruni identified a mineral's hardness by…
**Answer: a) scratching — which stone cuts which  b) its colour alone  c) its price  d) its weight in the hand**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Beruni measured a stone's density by…
**Answer: a) overflow weighing — mass over displaced water volume  b) melting it  c) counting its faces  d) asking the seller**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) An elastic body, when the force lets go…
**Answer: a) returns to its shape  b) stays deformed  c) always breaks  d) melts**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A brittle body, pushed harder and harder…
**Answer: a) barely deforms, then breaks  b) stretches like rubber  c) flows like water  d) bends double safely**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Stress is defined as…
**Answer: a) force divided by area  b) force times area  c) mass divided by volume  d) stretch times length**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Engineers divide ultimate strength by a safety factor because…
**Answer: a) real loads surprise — the working load must stay far below failure  b) it makes numbers prettier  c) the law demands smaller bridges  d) materials get stronger under load**
