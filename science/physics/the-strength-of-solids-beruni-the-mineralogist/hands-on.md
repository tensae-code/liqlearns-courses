# Hands-on — The strength of solids — Beruni the mineralogist

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Scratch and stretch
Scratch-test like Beruni: try marking a coin with a nail, the nail with a file, glass with the file — rank the four by hardness from what scratches what. Then hang masses from a rubber band and a spring, recording stretch against load: plot both. The band that returns and the plot that straightens are elasticity; the wire you bend and stays bent is plasticity. Same tests, every sample.
