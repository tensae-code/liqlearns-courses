# Hands-on — Alternating-current circuits — reactance, impedance and resonance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tune by numbers
Take L = 0.10 H and C = 10 µF. Compute X_L and X_C at 20 Hz, at the resonance frequency from the DIY (≈ 159 Hz), and at 1,000 Hz, in a small table. Say aloud: the inductor's wall grows with frequency, the capacitor's shrinks — they must cross once, and the crossing is the resonance.

## Phase with your hands
Clap a steady beat for the voltage. For a resistor, clap the current on the same beats — in phase. For a capacitor, clap a quarter-beat early; for an inductor, a quarter-beat late. Say aloud: reactance is not delay of energy loss — over a full cycle the borrowed energy returns; only the resistor's share is spent.
