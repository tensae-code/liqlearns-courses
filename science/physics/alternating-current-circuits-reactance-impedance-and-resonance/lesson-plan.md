# Lesson plan — Alternating-current circuits — reactance, impedance and resonance

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** Alternating-current circuits — reactance, impedance and resonance
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18), beside the AC and electronics lessons — the full reactance/impedance/resonance treatment; the narrower Haiti-stocked lesson AC circuits — RC and RL dipoles (`physics/ac-circuits-rc-and-rl-dipoles`) treats the two dipoles separately and is cross-referenced, not duplicated
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In an AC circuit, capacitors and inductors oppose current with **reactance** that depends on frequency — X_C = 1/(2πfC) falling, X_L = 2πfL rising — resistance and reactance combine (at right angles, by phase) into **impedance Z**, and where X_L = X_C the circuit **resonates**: impedance minimum, current maximum, the tuning principle of every radio
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Earlier lessons make a good preparation and are recommended, never forced

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Describe an alternator's output and use RMS versus peak correctly.
2. Compute X_C and X_L and state each one's phase and frequency behaviour.
3. Compute Z for a series RLC circuit and the current it draws.
4. Compute a resonance frequency and explain resonance's uses and hazards.
5. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson text alone.
