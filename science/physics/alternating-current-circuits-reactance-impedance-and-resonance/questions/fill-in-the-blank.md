# Alternating-current circuits — reactance, impedance and resonance — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An alternator generates EMF by rotating a coil in a _____ field.
**Answer: magnetic**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The opposition of a capacitor to AC is called capacitive _____ .
**Answer: reactance**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Resistance and net reactance combine into the circuit's _____ .
**Answer: impedance**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The frequency at which X_L = X_C is the _____ frequency.
**Answer: resonance**
