# Alternating-current circuits — reactance, impedance and resonance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Quoted mains voltage (230 V) is the…
a) Peak value  b) RMS (effective) value — peak is √2 times larger  c) Average of zero  d) DC equivalent of the peak
**Answer: a) Peak value**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In a pure resistor on AC, current and voltage are…
a) In phase  b) 90° out of phase  c) 180° out of phase  d) Unrelated
**Answer: b) 90° out of phase**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Capacitive reactance X_C as frequency rises…
a) Falls — X_C = 1/(2πfC)  b) Stays constant  c) Rises  d) Becomes negative
**Answer: c) Rises**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In an inductor, the current…
a) Is in phase with the voltage  b) Lags the voltage by 90°  c) Does not flow  d) Leads the voltage by 90°
**Answer: d) Leads the voltage by 90°**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) For R = 30 Ω, X_L − X_C = 40 Ω, the impedance Z is…
a) 70 Ω  b) 10 Ω  c) 50 Ω  d) 1,200 Ω
**Answer: a) 70 Ω**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) At resonance in a series RLC circuit…
a) X_L = X_C, impedance is minimum (= R) and current is maximum  b) Impedance is maximum  c) Current is zero  d) The capacitor blocks everything
**Answer: b) Impedance is maximum**
