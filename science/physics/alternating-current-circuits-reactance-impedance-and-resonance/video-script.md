# Video script — Alternating-current circuits — reactance, impedance and resonance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the circuit that listens to one frequency**. Spin a coil in a magnet's field and the current alternates — fifty full swings a second from your wall socket. A resistor takes it in step. A capacitor fights it less the faster it swings, and its current runs a quarter-cycle *ahead*. An inductor fights it more the faster it swings, its current a quarter-cycle *behind*. Add the three fights as a right triangle and you get **impedance**. And at exactly one frequency the two reactances cancel: **resonance** — impedance collapses to the resistance alone, current peaks, and a radio picks its station out of the air.
