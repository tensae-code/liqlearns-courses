Today: **science definitions — the physics word bank**. In physics, a word is a tool with one exact edge: velocity is speed *in a direction*, weight is a *force* in newtons, latent heat changes state *without* changing temperature. Libya's Grade 11 book prints these definitions in English at the back of the Arabic book, so the words are there when you need them. Learn a word the bank's way: find it, say it exactly, use it, check it.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
