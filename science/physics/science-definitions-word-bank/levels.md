# Science definitions — the physics word bank — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — A physics definition fixes a word to one exact, measurable meaning, usually with its unit (velocity is speed in a stated direction; weight is gravity's force on a mass, in newtons); The motion-and-force words are defined exactly: scalar, vector, displacement, velocity, acceleration, force, mass, weight, friction, pressure, work, energy, power; The heat, waves and electricity words are defined exactly: temperature, heat, latent heat, wave, wavelength, frequency, refraction, current, voltage, resistance; A word bank is used by a method — find the word, say the definition exactly with its unit, use it in a sentence of your own, check against the bank — the method Libya's Grade 11 book builds in with its English "Definitions" appendix — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
