# Hands-on — Science definitions — the physics word bank

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Start your own word bank
Rule a notebook page into two columns: WORD | DEFINITION (with its unit). Take five words from today's schoolwork that you could not define exactly — from any subject, not only physics. For each one, use the bank's method: find the definition (textbook glossary, the back of the book, or your teacher), write it in one exact line with its unit, say it aloud once, and write one sentence of your own underneath that uses the word about a real thing in the room. Cover the definition column and test yourself; the words you can still say exactly are yours. Add five more words each week — a word bank is built the way Libya's book builds it: at the back, always there, in the language the science is shared in.
