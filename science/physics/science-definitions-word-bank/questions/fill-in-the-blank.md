# Science definitions — the physics word bank — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A quantity with size only is a _____; a quantity with size and direction is a vector.
**Answer: scalar**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Weight is measured in _____, mass in kilograms.
**Answer: newtons**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The distance from one crest of a wave to the next is its _____.
**Answer: wavelength**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Current is the rate of flow of _____, measured in amperes.
**Answer: charge**
