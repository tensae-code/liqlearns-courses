# Science definitions — the physics word bank — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A physics definition is…
**Answer: a) a word fixed to one exact meaning that can be measured or tested  b) a rough everyday description  c) a translation of the word  d) an example of the word in use**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Weight, defined exactly, is…
**Answer: a) the force of gravity on a mass, measured in newtons  b) how heavy something feels  c) the amount of matter in a body, in kilograms  d) mass times distance**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A vector is a quantity that has…
**Answer: a) size and direction  b) size only  c) direction only  d) a unit but no size**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Acceleration is defined as…
**Answer: a) the rate of change of velocity  b) distance divided by time  c) force divided by area  d) the rate of doing work**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Latent heat is heat that…
**Answer: a) changes a body's state without changing its temperature  b) is hidden inside a thermometer  c) warms a body by one degree  d) travels without a medium**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The word-bank method, in order, is…
**Answer: a) find the word → say the definition exactly, with its unit → use it in your own sentence → check against the bank  b) copy the word ten times → translate it → forget it  c) guess the meaning → ask a friend → move on  d) read the whole glossary once → close the book**
