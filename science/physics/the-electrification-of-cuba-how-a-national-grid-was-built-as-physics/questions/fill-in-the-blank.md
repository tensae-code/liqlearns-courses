# The electrification of Cuba — how a national grid was built, as physics — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The current in a grid is made by electromagnetic _____.
**Answer: induction**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Line losses grow as _____ — the square of the current times the resistance.
**Answer: I²R**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A _____ steps voltage up at the station and down again near the user.
**Answer: transformer**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Stations linked into one system form a national _____.
**Answer: grid**
