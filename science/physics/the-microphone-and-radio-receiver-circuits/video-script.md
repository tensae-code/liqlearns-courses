Today: **the microphone and the receiver — sound to current, and one station out of many**. A microphone's diaphragm shakes a coil in a magnet's field, and the induced current copies the sound exactly. At the radio, a resonance circuit — a coil and a variable capacitor — answers only one carrier frequency, the way a swing answers only pushes in its rhythm. Then a detector's diode strips the carrier away, leaving the sound pattern, which is amplified and spoken by the loudspeaker. Four jobs, always in that order.
