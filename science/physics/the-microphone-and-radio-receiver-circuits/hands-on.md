# Hands-on — The microphone and radio receiver circuits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tune by resonance, detect by hand
First, resonance as selection: swing a long pendulum (string and keys) with tiny pushes — pushes timed to its own rhythm build it up, pushes at any other rhythm cancel out; the pendulum 'tuned' to your pushes is the LC circuit tuned to one carrier. Second, detection as envelope-reading: on paper, draw a fast carrier wave whose peaks swell and shrink in a slow pattern, then draw a smooth line joining only the top peaks — you have just done the detector's job with a pencil: the smooth line is the audio. Say aloud the receiver's chain in order — select by resonance, separate by detection, strengthen by amplification, speak by loudspeaker.
