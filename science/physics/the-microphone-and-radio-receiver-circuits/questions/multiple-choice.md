# The microphone and radio receiver circuits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a moving-coil microphone, the electrical signal is produced by…
a) A battery inside the microphone  b) Electromagnetic induction as the diaphragm's coil moves in a magnetic field  c) Static electricity  d) Heating of the coil
**Answer: b) Electromagnetic induction as the diaphragm's coil moves in a magnetic field**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The microphone's output current copies the sound in that…
a) It is constant while the sound lasts  b) Strong sounds make larger currents and high notes make faster variations  c) Loud sounds reverse it  d) It flows only for low notes
**Answer: b) Strong sounds make larger currents and high notes make faster variations**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The tuning circuit selects one station by…
a) Blocking all aerial signals  b) Resonance — an LC circuit responding strongly only at its resonant frequency  c) Amplifying the loudest station  d) Spinning the aerial
**Answer: b) Resonance — an LC circuit responding strongly only at its resonant frequency**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Turning a traditional radio's tuning knob changes the…
a) Diode's direction  b) Capacitance of the variable capacitor, sliding the resonant frequency  c) Length of the loudspeaker cone  d) Aerial's height
**Answer: b) Capacitance of the variable capacitor, sliding the resonant frequency**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The detector circuit's job is to…
a) Select the station  b) Strip the carrier away and recover the audio signal  c) Make the signal louder  d) Turn current into sound
**Answer: b) Strip the carrier away and recover the audio signal**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The one-way component at the heart of a simple detector is the…
a) Coil  b) Diode  c) Capacitor alone  d) Loudspeaker
**Answer: b) Diode**
