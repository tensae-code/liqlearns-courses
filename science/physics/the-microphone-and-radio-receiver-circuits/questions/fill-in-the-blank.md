# The microphone and radio receiver circuits — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The microphone part that the sound waves strike first is the _____.
**Answer: diaphragm**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The tuning circuit combines a coil with a variable _____.
**Answer: capacitor**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The tuning circuit picks out one station by the principle of _____.
**Answer: resonance**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The detector recovers the audio pattern, which in AM is the carrier's _____.
**Answer: envelope (amplitude pattern)**
