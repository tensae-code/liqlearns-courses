# Relative velocity — motion seen by a moving observer — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Cars move the same way at 25 m/s and 18 m/s. Relative velocity?
**Answer: 25 − 18 = 7 m/s.**

**2.** Two trains approach at 30 m/s and 20 m/s. Closing speed?
**Answer: 30 + 20 = 50 m/s.**

**3.** A passenger walks forward in a bus at 1 m/s; the bus moves at 12 m/s. Ground speed of passenger?
**Answer: 12 + 1 = 13 m/s (same direction — the ground observer adds).**

**4.** Why do roadside and driver disagree about a car's velocity?
**Answer: Velocity is relative to the observer, and the driver is moving.**
