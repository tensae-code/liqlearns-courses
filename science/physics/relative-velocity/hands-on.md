# Hands-on — Relative velocity — motion seen by a moving observer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Walk toward a partner who walks toward you, both slowly. Your closing speed feels bigger than either walk — your bodies just added velocities.

## Apply it where you live
Work a boat-on-a-river problem: boat speed, current speed, upstream and downstream relative to the bank.
