# The electroscope — seeing charge — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Why do the leaves spread apart?
**Answer: They carry the same kind of charge and repel each other.**

**2.** What does a bigger spread tell you?
**Answer: More charge.**

**3.** Can an electroscope alone always tell the sign of a charge?
**Answer: Not alone — you compare against a known charge to identify kind.**

**4.** Name the parts of a simple homemade electroscope.
**Answer: A jar case, a metal rod/wire through the top, and light foil leaves.**
