# The electroscope — seeing charge — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why do the leaves spread apart?
a) They carry the same kind of charge and repel each other.  b) More charge.  c) Not alone — you compare against a known charge to identify kind.  d) A jar case, a metal rod/wire through the top, and light foil leaves.
**Answer: a) They carry the same kind of charge and repel each other.**

**2.** What does a bigger spread tell you?
a) They carry the same kind of charge and repel each other.  b) More charge.  c) Not alone — you compare against a known charge to identify kind.  d) A jar case, a metal rod/wire through the top, and light foil leaves.
**Answer: b) More charge.**

**3.** Can an electroscope alone always tell the sign of a charge?
a) They carry the same kind of charge and repel each other.  b) More charge.  c) Not alone — you compare against a known charge to identify kind.  d) A jar case, a metal rod/wire through the top, and light foil leaves.
**Answer: c) Not alone — you compare against a known charge to identify kind.**

**4.** Name the parts of a simple homemade electroscope.
