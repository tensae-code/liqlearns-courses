# Video script — The electroscope — seeing charge

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

An electroscope is a sensitive charge detector: a metal rod ending in two light leaves (or a needle) inside a case.
Bring charge to its cap — or touch it with a charged body — and both leaves receive the same kind of charge, repel each other, and diverge.
The wider the spread, the more the charge.
Used carefully, it not only detects charge but helps identify its kind.
The book sets you to design and build one from locally available materials — a jar, wire and foil will do.

Remember: **An electroscope detects charge by its leaves diverging.**
Remember: **Both leaves get the same charge and repel each other apart.**
Remember: **Wider divergence means more charge.**
