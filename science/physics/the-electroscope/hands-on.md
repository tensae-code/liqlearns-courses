# Hands-on — The electroscope — seeing charge

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Build the book's electroscope from a jar, wire and foil. Charge a ruler and bring it near — watch leaves answer something invisible.

## Apply it where you live
Use your electroscope to test three rubbed objects and rank their charges by leaf spread.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21897 — homemade electroscope, leaves apart under a charged rod] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
