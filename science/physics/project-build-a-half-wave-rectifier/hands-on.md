# Hands-on — Project: build a half-wave rectifier

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the practical
1. A lab project sends pupils to construct a half-wave rectification circuit and present it — the unit's hands-on close.

Work with a partner, write down what you measured or observed, and say what the result proves. If apparatus is missing, walk through the steps with drawings and say what you would expect to see.

## One-way street
Join a diode, a cell and a small lamp in a loop. The lamp lights one way round; turn the diode around and it stays dark. Draw both loops and mark the bias each time.

## See it and say it
Study this lesson's picture — the pupil-built circuit on a bench with its output trace — and name every part of it out loud. Then close the picture and draw it from memory; check your drawing against the lesson.

## Teach it back
Explain this lesson to someone at home using one thing in the room as your example. Their questions show you which part to re-read.
