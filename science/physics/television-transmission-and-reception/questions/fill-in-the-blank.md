# Television transmission and reception — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Breaking a picture into lines for sending is called _____.
**Answer: scanning**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The signal that carries the picture's brightness pattern is the _____ signal.
**Answer: vision**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) One television channel carries its picture and _____ on two separate carriers.
**Answer: sound**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The eye's brief keeping of an image, which helps the repainted lines look steady, is called persistence of _____.
**Answer: vision**
