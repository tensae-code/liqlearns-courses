# Television transmission and reception — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A television camera turns a scene into a signal by…
a) Photographing it once per minute  b) Scanning it line by line and turning brightness along each line into a varying signal  c) Sending the whole picture in one pulse  d) Recording it on film
**Answer: b) Scanning it line by line and turning brightness along each line into a varying signal**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In the system taught in this lesson, one complete picture is scanned into…
a) 100 lines  b) 240 lines  c) 625 lines  d) 10,000 lines
**Answer: c) 625 lines**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Synchronising pulses in the vision signal are there to…
a) Make the picture brighter  b) Mark where each line and each picture begins, so the receiver keeps in step  c) Add the sound  d) Delay the colours
**Answer: b) Mark where each line and each picture begins, so the receiver keeps in step**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The picture and sound of one TV channel travel as…
a) One combined signal on one carrier  b) Two signals modulating two separate carriers within the channel  c) Sound only, with the picture sent by cable  d) Alternating seconds of picture and sound
**Answer: b) Two signals modulating two separate carriers within the channel**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The television tuner selects one channel by the same principle a radio uses — …
a) Detection  b) Resonance  c) Amplification  d) Scanning
**Answer: b) Resonance**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The viewer sees a steady whole picture rather than a travelling spot because…
a) The screen stores the picture for a week  b) The picture is repainted many times a second and the eye keeps each image briefly — persistence of vision  c) The lines are painted permanently  d) Sound fills the gaps
**Answer: b) The picture is repainted many times a second and the eye keeps each image briefly — persistence of vision**
