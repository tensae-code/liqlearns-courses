# Hands-on — Television transmission and reception

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Scan and rebuild a picture by lines
Draw a simple bold picture — a house with a sun — on squared paper, using 10 rows as your 'lines'. Working row by row from the top, call out (or write) a brightness number for each square: 0 for white, 1 for light, 2 for dark. A partner, with blank squared paper and without seeing your picture, fills squares from your numbers alone, row by row in the same order — when you call 'new line!' they must drop a row, exactly a synchronising pulse's job. Compare the rebuilt picture with the original: it is coarse at 10 lines; say how 625 lines would change it. You have scanned, transmitted and rebuilt a television picture with voices for carriers.
