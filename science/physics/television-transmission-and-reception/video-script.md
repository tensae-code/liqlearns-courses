# Video script — Television transmission and reception

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **television — a picture sent as lines**. The camera scans the scene into 625 lines, turning brightness along each line into a signal, with pulses to mark where lines and pictures begin. That signal rides its own carrier, the sound rides another, and your receiver tunes both in, prises them apart, and repaints the picture on the screen line by line in the same order — so fast that your eye sees one steady, moving scene. Television is the radio idea, grown from sound to pictures.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
