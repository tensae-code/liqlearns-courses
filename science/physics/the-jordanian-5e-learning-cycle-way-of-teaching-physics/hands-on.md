# Hands-on — The Jordanian 5E learning-cycle way of teaching physics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Take one lesson you know and map it onto the five stages in a table; mark where each stage happens. (2) Teach a five-minute micro-lesson to a partner following the cycle exactly; have them name each stage as it passes. (3) Rewrite one recall question as an evaluation-stage question that asks for the idea used somewhere new.
