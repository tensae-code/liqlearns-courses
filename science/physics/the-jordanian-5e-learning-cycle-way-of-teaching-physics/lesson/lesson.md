# The Jordanian 5E learning-cycle way of teaching physics

## Do it first
Before you read: work the opening task of the first sub lesson with real objects, real numbers or a real observation — the explanation that follows is the story of what you just did. Keep your result beside you as you read.

## Sub lesson 1 — Engage and explore — the lesson starts in the learner's hands

Engagement opens with the phenomenon or question that makes the learner want the idea — in these books, the unit's introductory experiment. Exploration follows: learners work the activity, gather the pattern and argue about it in their own words before any formal statement is given. The teacher's job in these stages is to set the task and protect the struggle, not to explain early.

## Sub lesson 2 — Explain and elaborate — formal words after the pattern

Explanation comes third, deliberately late: the teacher names the law, the term and the equation for the pattern the learners have already met, connects their wording to the book's, and works the first examples. Elaboration then pushes the idea into new ground — applications, the 'Physics and life' spread, STEAM tasks — where it must work in an unfamiliar setting, not merely repeat.

## Sub lesson 3 — Evaluate — evidence woven through, not bolted on

Evaluation is the fifth stage but runs through all of them: the guides pair each lesson with checks at every phase (present, teach, assess), worksheets, and the activities book's answers appendix. Assessment in the 5E way asks whether the idea survived elaboration — can the learner use it somewhere new — rather than only whether the explanation can be recited.

## The key ideas

- Jordan's guides teach inside the 5E cycle: engage, explore, explain, elaborate, evaluate
- Exploration precedes explanation — the pattern is met before the law is named
- Lessons are delivered in three phases: present, teach, assess
- Elaboration pushes the idea into applications and STEAM tasks
- Evaluation asks whether the idea works somewhere new, not only whether it can be recited

## Say it to your companion
Tell your companion, in your own words: Jordan's Grade 10 teacher guides teach every lesson inside the five-stage learning cycle — engagement, exploration, explanation, elaboration, evaluation (دورة التعلم الخماسية, the 5E cycle) — delivered in a three-phase lesson (present, teach, assess) with STEAM integration: the country's own stated way of teaching physics. If your companion can work the Do It Yourself tasks without looking back, the lesson has landed.
