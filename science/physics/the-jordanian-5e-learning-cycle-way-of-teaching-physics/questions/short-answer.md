# The Jordanian 5E learning-cycle way of teaching physics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is exploration placed before explanation in this way of teaching?
**Answer: So the formal law names a pattern the learners have already met and argued about — the explanation organises their experience instead of arriving as words about something unseen.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the teacher's discipline during exploration?
**Answer: To set the task and protect the struggle — guiding with questions, not explaining early; the naming of the law is saved for the explanation stage.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How is evaluation different here from a single end-of-lesson test?
**Answer: It is woven through every phase — checks in presenting, teaching and assessing, worksheets and worked answers — and it tests transfer (the idea used somewhere new), not recall alone.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Name the two frames the guides combine, and how they fit.
**Answer: The 5E learning cycle (the pedagogy) runs inside a three-phase lesson delivery — present, teach, assess (the lesson's timetable) — with STEAM integration in the elaboration work.**
