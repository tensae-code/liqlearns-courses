# The Jordanian 5E learning-cycle way of teaching physics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The 5E cycle's stages in order are…
**Answer: a) engage, explore, explain, elaborate, evaluate  b) explain, engage, explore, evaluate, elaborate  c) evaluate first  d) engage, explain, examine, end, exit**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The unit's introductory experiment belongs chiefly to…
**Answer: a) engagement and exploration  b) evaluation  c) elaboration only  d) no stage**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Explanation comes…
**Answer: a) after exploration, deliberately late  b) first, always  c) never  d) only in the guide**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Elaboration pushes the idea into…
**Answer: a) new applications and STEAM tasks  b) the glossary  c) the same examples again  d) silence**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The guides deliver lessons in three phases:…
**Answer: a) present, teach, assess  b) read, copy, test  c) plan, mark, file  d) open, close, shelve**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Evaluation in the 5E way chiefly asks whether the learner…
**Answer: a) can use the idea somewhere new  b) can recite the explanation  c) sat quietly  d) copied the notes**
