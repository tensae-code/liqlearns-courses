# Lesson plan — The Jordanian 5E learning-cycle way of teaching physics

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** The Jordanian 5E learning-cycle way of teaching physics
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18), a teaching-way lesson — Jordan's official pedagogy for its Grade 10 books
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Jordan's Grade 10 teacher guides teach every lesson inside the five-stage learning cycle — engagement, exploration, explanation, elaboration, evaluation (دورة التعلم الخماسية, the 5E cycle) — delivered in a three-phase lesson (present, teach, assess) with STEAM integration: the country's own stated way of teaching physics
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Any earlier lesson named below is recommended first, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Engage and explore — the lesson starts in the learner's hands** — Engagement opens with the phenomenon or question that makes the learner want the idea — in these books, the unit's introductory experiment. Exploration follows: learners work the activity, gather the pattern and argue about it in their own words before any formal statement is given. The teacher's job in these stages is to set the task and protect the struggle, not to explain early.
2. **Explain and elaborate — formal words after the pattern** — Explanation comes third, deliberately late: the teacher names the law, the term and the equation for the pattern the learners have already met, connects their wording to the book's, and works the first examples. Elaboration then pushes the idea into new ground — applications, the 'Physics and life' spread, STEAM tasks — where it must work in an unfamiliar setting, not merely repeat.
3. **Evaluate — evidence woven through, not bolted on** — Evaluation is the fifth stage but runs through all of them: the guides pair each lesson with checks at every phase (present, teach, assess), worksheets, and the activities book's answers appendix. Assessment in the 5E way asks whether the idea survived elaboration — can the learner use it somewhere new — rather than only whether the explanation can be recited.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

5. Evaluation asks whether the idea works somewhere new, not only whether it can be recited
