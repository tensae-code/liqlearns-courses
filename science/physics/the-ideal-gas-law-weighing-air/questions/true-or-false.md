# The ideal gas law — weighing air — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. PV = nRT, rewritten with the specific gas constant to give density from pressure and temperature; a worked example finds the mass of air in a 4 m × 5 m × 6 m room.**
**Answer: True**

**2. PV = nRT, rewritten with the specific gas constant to give density from pressure and temperature; a worked example finds the mass of air in a 8 m × 5 m × 6 m room.**
**Answer: False**
