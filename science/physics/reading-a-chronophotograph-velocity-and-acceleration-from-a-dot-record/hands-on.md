# Hands-on — Reading a chronophotograph — velocity and acceleration from a dot record

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

['(1) Make a record: pull a paper strip through a ticker timer attached to a toy car (or film a rolling ball against a ruler with a phone at fixed frame rate and plot frame positions). (2) Measure neighbour distances and compute the velocity at three points; draw the velocity vectors to scale. (3) Compute the acceleration between two velocities, then predict where the next dot should fall — and test the prediction against the record.']
