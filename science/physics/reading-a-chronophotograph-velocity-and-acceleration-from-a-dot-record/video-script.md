# Video script — Reading a chronophotograph — velocity and acceleration from a dot record

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **reading a chronophotograph**. A chronophotograph stamps the moving point at equal time steps τ — spacing is speed drawn out. Velocity at Mᵢ = Mᵢ₋₁Mᵢ₊₁ / 2τ — from the two neighbours, tangent to the path

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
