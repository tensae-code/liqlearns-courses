# Reading a chronophotograph — velocity and acceleration from a dot record — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — A chronophotograph stamps the moving point at equal time steps τ — spacing is speed drawn out; Velocity at Mᵢ = Mᵢ₋₁Mᵢ₊₁ / 2τ — from the two neighbours, tangent to the path; Acceleration = Δv / 2τ — from the change between two velocity vectors; The air table (puck on air jets) makes the record nearly friction-free; Run backwards, a record yields forces: friction f = m·a read from the deceleration in the dots — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
