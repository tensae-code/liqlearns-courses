# Reading a chronophotograph — velocity and acceleration from a dot record — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a chronophotograph, the dots are stamped…
**Answer: a) at equal time intervals  b) at equal distances  c) whenever the point turns  d) randomly**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Growing spacing between dots means the point is…
**Answer: a) accelerating  b) slowing down  c) at rest  d) moving in a circle**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The velocity at Mᵢ is computed from…
**Answer: a) its two neighbours: Mᵢ₋₁Mᵢ₊₁ over 2τ  b) the first dot only  c) the whole record's length over one τ  d) the next dot over τ**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The velocity vector at a point of the record points…
**Answer: a) tangent to the path at that point  b) toward the centre always  c) along the acceleration  d) backwards along the path**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Acceleration from a record is a = …
**Answer: a) Δv / 2τ  b) Δv × 2τ  c) v / τ²  d) Δx / Δv**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A record's steady deceleration of a puck of mass m gives the friction force as…
**Answer: a) f = m·a  b) f = m/a  c) f = a/m  d) f = m·a²**
