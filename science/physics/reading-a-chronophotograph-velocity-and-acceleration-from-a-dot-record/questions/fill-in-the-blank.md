# Reading a chronophotograph — velocity and acceleration from a dot record — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The equal time between stamped dots is written _____ .
**Answer: τ (tau)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Velocity at a dot is measured from its two _____ .
**Answer: neighbours**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Acceleration is the change of velocity, Δv, divided by _____ .
**Answer: 2τ**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A puck glides nearly without friction on a table of _____ jets.
**Answer: air**
