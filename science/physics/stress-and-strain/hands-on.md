# Hands-on — Stress and strain

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Pull a thick rubber band and a thin one with the same force. The thin one stretches much more — same force, smaller area, greater stress.

## Apply it where you live
Compute the stress in a loaded rope from its load and cross-section, with working.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21891 — wire under load with force, area, original length and extension labelled] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using Stress = F / A; Strain = extension / original length: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
