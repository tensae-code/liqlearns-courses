# Stress and strain — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A force of 500 N acts on 0.01 m². Stress?
a) 500 ÷ 0.01 = 50,000 Pa.  b) 0.002 ÷ 2 = 0.001 (no unit).  c) It doubles.  d) It slides one layer of material over another.
**Answer: a) 500 ÷ 0.01 = 50,000 Pa.**

**2.** A 2 m wire extends 0.002 m. Strain?
a) 500 ÷ 0.01 = 50,000 Pa.  b) 0.002 ÷ 2 = 0.001 (no unit).  c) It doubles.  d) It slides one layer of material over another.
**Answer: b) 0.002 ÷ 2 = 0.001 (no unit).**

**3.** Same force on half the area — stress does what?
a) 500 ÷ 0.01 = 50,000 Pa.  b) 0.002 ÷ 2 = 0.001 (no unit).  c) It doubles.  d) It slides one layer of material over another.
**Answer: c) It doubles.**

**4.** Shear stress acts how?
a) 500 ÷ 0.01 = 50,000 Pa.  b) 0.002 ÷ 2 = 0.001 (no unit).  c) It doubles.  d) It slides one layer of material over another.
**Answer: d) It slides one layer of material over another.**
