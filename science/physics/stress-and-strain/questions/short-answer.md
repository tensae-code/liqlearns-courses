# Stress and strain — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A force of 500 N acts on 0.01 m². Stress?
**Answer: 500 ÷ 0.01 = 50,000 Pa.**

**2.** A 2 m wire extends 0.002 m. Strain?
**Answer: 0.002 ÷ 2 = 0.001 (no unit).**

**3.** Same force on half the area — stress does what?
**Answer: It doubles.**

**4.** Shear stress acts how?
**Answer: It slides one layer of material over another.**
