# Projectiles launched horizontally — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about horizontal projection is correct?**
a) Launched flat, a projectile keeps its horizontal velocity while its vertical fall starts from zero — equations for each…  b) Whole circuits — diodes, transistors, resistors, capacitors — fabricated on one chip: small, cheap and reliable, but…  c) Two cans hang from a beam, one filled with sand, one empty; pupils give each a slight push to start it swinging, then…
**Answer: a) Launched flat, a projectile keeps its horizontal velocity while its vertical fall starts from zero —…**

**2. Launched flat, a ______ keeps its horizontal velocity while its vertical fall starts from zero — equations for each component separately, worked with a dropped package; an activity places two tennis balls to test the prediction.**
a) projectile  b) energy  c) radiation
**Answer: a) projectile**
