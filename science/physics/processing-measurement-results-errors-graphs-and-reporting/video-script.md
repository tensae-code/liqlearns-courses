# Video script — Processing measurement results — errors, graphs and reporting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **finishing a measurement honestly**. Readings scatter — random error — so we repeat and take the mean. Instruments push — systematic error — so we zero and calibrate. Tables and graphs show the pattern, the line of best fit runs through the middle, and its gradient can be the physics itself. Then we report the result with its uncertainty attached, because a number that hides its spread has told only half the truth.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
