# Processing measurement results — errors, graphs and reporting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Random errors are best reduced by…
a) Using the instrument once, carefully  b) Repeating the measurement and taking the mean  c) Zeroing the balance  d) Ignoring small readings
**Answer: b) Repeating the measurement and taking the mean**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A balance that reads 2 g with nothing on it causes…
a) Random error  b) Systematic error — every reading pushed the same way  c) No error  d) An anomaly only
**Answer: b) Systematic error — every reading pushed the same way**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) One reading far from all the others in a set is called…
a) A mean  b) An anomaly  c) A gradient  d) A calibration
**Answer: b) An anomaly**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) On a graph, the quantity the investigator changed goes on…
a) The vertical axis  b) The horizontal axis  c) Only in the title  d) The line of best fit
**Answer: b) The horizontal axis**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A line of best fit should…
a) Join every point dot to dot  b) Run as a single smooth line through the middle of the points  c) Pass through every point exactly  d) Avoid all the points
**Answer: b) Run as a single smooth line through the middle of the points**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A result reported as 1.24 s ± 0.06 s tells the reader…
a) The exact time  b) The mean and how far the readings spread — how far to trust it  c) That the watch was new  d) That five readings were taken, all equal
**Answer: b) The mean and how far the readings spread — how far to trust it**
