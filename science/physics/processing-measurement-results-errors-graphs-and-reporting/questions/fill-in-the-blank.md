# Processing measurement results — errors, graphs and reporting — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Errors that scatter readings on both sides of the true value are _____ errors.
**Answer: random**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The average of repeated readings is called the _____.
**Answer: mean**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A smooth line through the middle of plotted points is the line of best _____.
**Answer: fit**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A reported result should always carry its _____, so the reader knows how far to trust it.
**Answer: uncertainty**
