# Hands-on — Processing measurement results — errors, graphs and reporting

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five drops, one honest number
Drop the same book from the same height five times and time each fall, writing every reading down — no crossings out. Find any anomaly and circle it with its reason. Work out the mean of the good readings and the range, and write the finished result as mean ± half-range. Then look at a partner's set: whose result would you trust further, and what in their reporting — repeats, range, stated anomaly — earns that trust? Say it in one sentence each.
