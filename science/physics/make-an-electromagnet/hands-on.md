# Hands-on — Make an electromagnet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the practical
1. Pupils build the model electromagnet (coil on an iron core, battery), following the printed materials list and procedure — magnetism you can switch.

Work with a partner, write down what you measured or observed, and say what the result proves. If apparatus is missing, walk through the steps with drawings and say what you would expect to see.

## Make a magnet work
Wrap wire many times around a large nail, join the ends to a cell, and see how many paper clips the nail can lift. Unwrap ten turns and try again. More turns, stronger magnet.

## See it and say it
Study this lesson's picture — the coil-wrapped nail lifting paper clips — and name every part of it out loud. Then close the picture and draw it from memory; check your drawing against the lesson.

## Teach it back
Explain this lesson to someone at home using one thing in the room as your example. Their questions show you which part to re-read.
