# Capacitor networks and stored energy — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Series capacitors combine reciprocally, parallel ones add.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) parallel capacitors combine reciprocally, parallel ones add.**
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A charged capacitor stores energy (½CV²) in its field, released on discharge.**
**Answer: True**
