# Capacitor networks and stored energy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about capacitors in combination and stored energy is correct?**
a) A compass needle turns beside a current-carrying wire: current makes a circular magnetic field (B = μ₀I/2πr for a long…  b) Bench experiment: place a block on a board, load and pull it, and find coefficients from the forces at slipping and…  c) Series capacitors combine reciprocally, parallel ones add.
**Answer: c) Series capacitors combine reciprocally, parallel ones add.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Series capacitors combine reciprocally, ______ ones add.**
a) acceleration  b) proton  c) parallel
**Answer: c) parallel**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which statement about capacitors in combination and stored energy is correct?**
a) Gravity supplies the centripetal force of orbits: orbital speed from GM/r, recovering Kepler's third law (K = 4π²/GM)…  b) Subtracting a vector is adding its negative; worked and exercise problems combine several displacement vectors…  c) A charged capacitor stores energy (½CV²) in its field, released on discharge.
**Answer: c) A charged capacitor stores energy (½CV²) in its field, released on discharge.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A charged capacitor stores ______ (½CV²) in its field, released on discharge.**
a) experience  b) component  c) energy
**Answer: c) energy**
