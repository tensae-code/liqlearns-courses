# Capacitor networks and stored energy — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Give one key fact this lesson teaches about capacitors in combination and stored energy.**
**Answer: Series capacitors combine reciprocally, parallel ones add.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Give one key fact this lesson teaches about capacitors in combination and stored energy.**
**Answer: A charged capacitor stores energy (½CV²) in its field, released on discharge.**
