# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Iranian two-branch way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work one problem the Iranian four-step way
Take any problem from another Physics lesson and work it on paper under four written headings, exactly as every Iranian student book prints them: 1) Identify the concepts — write what the problem is about and what is being asked. 2) Set up — write the knowns, the unknowns and the equations that connect them. 3) Solve — do the working. 4) Evaluate — check the answer's size, units and sense. Compare with how you usually work: the Iranian way spends two of its four steps before and after the calculation itself.
