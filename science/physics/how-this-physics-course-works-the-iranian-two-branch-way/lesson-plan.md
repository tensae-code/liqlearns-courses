# Lesson plan — How this Physics course works — the Iranian two-branch way

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** How this Physics course works — the Iranian two-branch way
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18) — a new lesson built for the Iran placement named in lesson/sources.md
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Iran teaches one national Physics course in two branch depths — a shared core in both the Experimental Sciences and Mathematical Sciences books, with whole topics (thermodynamics, fission and fusion, circular motion, diffraction, free fall) reserved to the Mathematical branch — ordered measurement-first, and worked throughout with a printed four-step problem-solving method

## Sub lessons
1. **One national course, two branches** — Every Iranian pupil studies the same national Physics course — but from Grade 10 the course splits into two branches. The Experimental Sciences book and the Mathematical Sciences (Mathematics-and-Physics) book share one core: chapter for chapter, the Mathematical book contains everything the Experimental book teaches. On top of the core, the Mathematical branch adds whole territories the Experimental branch never meets: thermodynamics at Grade 10, free fall, uniform circular motion, wave diffraction, and nuclear fission and fusion at Grade 12. One course, two depths — and the branch difference is written into the books themselves, not into separate syllabuses.
2. **The order of the journey, and the four-step method** — The journey is deliberately ordered: physics begins at Grade 10 with measurement and modelling, electricity and magnetism fill Grade 11, and mechanics returns — with waves and modern physics (photoelectric effect, the Bohr atom, the laser, core for BOTH branches) — at Grade 12. Every book opens with the same apparatus: a word to students, a word to teachers, and a printed four-step problem-solving method — identify the concepts, set up the problem, solve it, then evaluate the answer. Chapter openers are anchored in Iranian life and craft: measurement through Iranian architecture, capacitors through the touchscreen, so the physics starts from what the learner already knows.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
