# How this Physics course works — the Iranian two-branch way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name two topics reserved to the Mathematical branch and the grade at which each appears.
**Answer: Any two of: thermodynamics (Grade 10); free fall or uniform circular motion (Grade 12); wave diffraction (Grade 12); nuclear fission and fusion (Grade 12).**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What does "one course, two depths" mean for a pupil who changes branch?
**Answer: The core she has already learned is the same core in the other branch's book — nothing is relearned or lost; only the extra territories differ.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) State the four steps of the Iranian problem-solving method in order.
**Answer: Identify the concepts → set up the problem → solve it → evaluate the answer.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Give one example of an Iranian chapter opener anchored in everyday life, and say what it introduces.
**Answer: The Grade 11 electrostatics chapter opens with capacitive touchscreens (introducing capacitors/charge); or Grade 10's measurement chapter opens through Iranian architecture (introducing measurement).**
