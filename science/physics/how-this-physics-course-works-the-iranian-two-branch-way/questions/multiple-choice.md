Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Iran's two Physics branches are…
**Answer: a) Experimental Sciences and Mathematical Sciences  b) Northern and Southern  c) Day and Evening  d) Public and Private**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The relationship between the two branch books is…
**Answer: a) the Mathematical book contains the shared core plus extra topics  b) they share nothing  c) the Experimental book contains the Mathematical book plus extras  d) only the covers differ**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Thermodynamics at Grade 10 is met by…

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Physics in Iran begins at Grade 10 with…
**Answer: a) measurement and modelling  b) nuclear physics  c) thermodynamics for everyone  d) astronomy**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Grade 11 in both branches is filled mainly by…
