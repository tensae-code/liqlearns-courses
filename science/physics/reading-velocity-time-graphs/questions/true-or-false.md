# Reading velocity–time graphs — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. Slope of a v–t graph is acceleration and the area under it is displacement; area-finding from graphs is practised directly, and mixed graph-interpretation exercises close the topic.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Slope of a v–t graph is acceleration and the area under it is displacement; area-finding from graphs is practised inversely, and mixed graph-interpretation exercises close the topic.**
**Answer: False**
