# The first law of thermodynamics — heat, work and internal energy in one account — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The total energy stored in a system's particles is its _____ energy.
**Answer: internal**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The first law is the conservation of _____ written as bookkeeping.
**Answer: energy**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Work done on a system carries a _____ sign in ΔU = Q − W.
**Answer: negative (it enters as −W, raising ΔU)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A process with no heat flow in or out is called _____.
**Answer: adiabatic**
