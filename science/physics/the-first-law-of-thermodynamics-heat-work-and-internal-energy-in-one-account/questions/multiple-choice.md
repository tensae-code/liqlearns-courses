# The first law of thermodynamics — heat, work and internal energy in one account — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A system's internal energy can be changed by…
a) Heat only  b) Work only  c) Heat and work — the only two routes across the border  d) Neither; it never changes
**Answer: c) Heat and work — the only two routes across the border**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) For a gas, the internal energy is fixed by…
a) Its volume alone  b) Its temperature alone  c) The colour of its container  d) The pressure gauge's position
**Answer: b) Its temperature alone**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The first law of thermodynamics states…
a) ΔU = Q + W in all conventions  b) ΔU = Q − W, with W the work done by the system  c) Q = W always  d) ΔU = 0 always
**Answer: b) ΔU = Q − W, with W the work done by the system**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A gas absorbs 500 J of heat and does 200 J of work. ΔU is…
a) 700 J  b) −300 J  c) 300 J  d) 0 J
**Answer: c) 300 J**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In a fast, insulated compression (adiabatic, Q = 0), the gas's temperature…
a) Falls, because work was done  b) Stays the same  c) Rises, because work done on the gas raises its internal energy  d) Drops to zero
**Answer: c) Rises, because work done on the gas raises its internal energy**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Over one complete cycle of a heat engine, ΔU is…
a) Equal to the heat taken in  b) Zero — the system returns to its starting state, so net work out equals net heat in  c) Always negative  d) Infinite
**Answer: b) Zero — the system returns to its starting state, so net work out equals net heat in**
