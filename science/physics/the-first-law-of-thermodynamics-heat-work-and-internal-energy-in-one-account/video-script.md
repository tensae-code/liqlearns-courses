Today: **the first law of thermodynamics — one account for heat, work and the energy inside**. A gas in a cylinder stores internal energy. Energy can cross its border only two ways: heat flowing in, or work being done. The law keeps the books: the change in internal energy equals the heat added minus the work the gas does — ΔU = Q − W. Pump a bicycle tyre fast and the pump warms: that is work going in and raising the internal energy, with no time for heat to escape. Nothing is created, nothing vanishes — every joule is counted.
