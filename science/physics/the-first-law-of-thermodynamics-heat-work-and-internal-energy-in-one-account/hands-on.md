# Hands-on — The first law of thermodynamics — heat, work and internal energy in one account

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The warming pump
Take a bicycle pump, block its outlet with a finger, and pump quickly ten or fifteen times. Now feel the barrel near the outlet: it is warm. No flame touched it and no heat flowed in — you did work compressing the air, the compression was too fast for heat to escape (nearly adiabatic, Q ≈ 0), so the work went straight into the air's internal energy and its temperature rose: ΔU = −W with W negative. Say aloud: work in, internal energy up — the first law, felt in the hand.
