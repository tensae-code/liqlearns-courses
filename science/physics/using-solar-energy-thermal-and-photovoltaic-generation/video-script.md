# Video script — Using solar energy — thermal and photovoltaic generation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **two ways to use sunlight**. Collect it as heat — dark surfaces, warm water, and, concentrated by mirrors, steam for a turbine: light to heat to motion to electricity. Or skip the heat entirely — a photovoltaic cell turns light straight into electricity, silently, at any scale. Heat where heat is wanted; cells where electricity must come straight from the light.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
