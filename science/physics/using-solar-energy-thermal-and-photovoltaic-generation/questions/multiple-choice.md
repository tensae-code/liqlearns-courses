# Using solar energy — thermal and photovoltaic generation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A dark solar collector works by…
a) Reflecting sunlight away  b) Absorbing sunlight and warming up, trapping heat like a greenhouse  c) Making electricity directly  d) Burning fuel
**Answer: b) Absorbing sunlight and warming up, trapping heat like a greenhouse**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Direct thermal uses of sunlight include…
a) Heating water, warming air, drying crops and cooking  b) Only power stations  c) Only calculators  d) Only satellites
**Answer: a) Heating water, warming air, drying crops and cooking**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In thermal generation, the steam is raised by…
a) Burning coal  b) Concentrated sunlight heating a fluid until it boils  c) Photovoltaic cells  d) Wind
**Answer: b) Concentrated sunlight heating a fluid until it boils**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The thermal-generation energy chain is…
a) Light → electricity → heat  b) Light → heat → motion → electricity  c) Heat → light → sound  d) Motion → light only
**Answer: b) Light → heat → motion → electricity**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A photovoltaic cell converts…
a) Heat into light  b) Light straight into electricity in a semiconductor junction  c) Electricity into heat only  d) Motion into light
**Answer: b) Light straight into electricity in a semiconductor junction**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A photovoltaic panel has…
a) A turbine  b) Steam  c) No moving parts, and works at any scale from a calculator to a power plant  d) Only one fixed size
**Answer: c) No moving parts, and works at any scale from a calculator to a power plant**
