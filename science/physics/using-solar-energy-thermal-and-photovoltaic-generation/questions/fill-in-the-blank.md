# Using solar energy — thermal and photovoltaic generation — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A collector's absorber is made _____ to soak up sunlight.
**Answer: dark**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Thermal generation drives a turbine with _____.
**Answer: steam**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The Sun does the job of _____ in a solar-thermal station — no fuel is burnt.
**Answer: burning**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) “Photovoltaic” generation comes from solar _____.
**Answer: cells**
