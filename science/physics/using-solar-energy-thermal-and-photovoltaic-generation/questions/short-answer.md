# Using solar energy — thermal and photovoltaic generation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is a collector's surface dark, and why is it covered?
**Answer: Dark surfaces absorb sunlight best and warm fastest; the cover traps the warmed air like a greenhouse, stopping the collected heat escaping before it is used.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How is solar-thermal generation like a fuel power station, and how is it different?
**Answer: Like it: steam drives a turbine and generator. Different: the heat that raises the steam comes from concentrated sunlight, not from burning fuel.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What stage does the photovoltaic route skip, and why does that matter?
**Answer: The heat stage — light becomes electricity directly in the cell, so no boiler, turbine or moving parts are needed, and the same cell works at any scale.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A village needs hot water and a little night lighting (with storage). Which routes, and why?
**Answer: Thermal collectors for the water — heat is what is wanted, collected directly. Photovoltaic panels (charging storage) for the lighting — electricity straight from light, with no fuel or moving parts to maintain.**
