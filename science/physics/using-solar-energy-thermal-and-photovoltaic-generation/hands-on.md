# Hands-on — Using solar energy — thermal and photovoltaic generation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Collect and exploit — a pocket solar collector
Take a shallow box, line it with dark material, and cover it with clear plastic or glass if you have it. Put a small cup of water inside, set the box in full sun angled toward it, and feel (or measure) the water after 30–60 minutes, comparing it with an identical cup left in shade. Record both, and state in one sentence what your collector collected and how you exploited it — this is the Grade 12 notebook's solar experiment, run at pocket scale.
