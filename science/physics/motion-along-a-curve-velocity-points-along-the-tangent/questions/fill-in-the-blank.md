# Motion along a curve — velocity points along the tangent — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Velocity on a curve points along the _____ to the curve.
**Answer: tangent**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A released stone flies off along the _____ at its release point.
**Answer: tangent**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The force component _____ the velocity is what curves the path.
**Answer: across**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A force purely along the velocity changes only the _____.
**Answer: speed**
