# Motion along a curve — velocity points along the tangent — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) On a curved path, the velocity at an instant points…
**Answer: a) along the tangent to the curve at that point  b) toward the centre  c) outward  d) along the chord**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The tangent line at a point on a curve…
**Answer: a) just touches the curve there and points the way the body is heading  b) cuts through the centre  c) is the path itself  d) is always vertical**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A whirled stone, released, flies…
**Answer: a) along the tangent at the release point  b) radially outward  c) on around the circle  d) straight down**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Sparks leaving a grinding wheel demonstrate…
**Answer: a) tangential departure — velocity along the tangent  b) gravity  c) friction only  d) magnetism**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A path curves exactly when…
**Answer: a) the net force has a component across the velocity  b) the speed changes  c) no force acts  d) the force is along the motion**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A force purely along the velocity changes…
**Answer: a) the speed only, never the direction  b) the direction only  c) neither  d) the mass**
