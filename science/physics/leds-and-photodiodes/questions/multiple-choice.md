# LEDs and photodiodes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about diodes at work is correct?**
a) Light-emitting diodes turn current into light.  b) Lines leave the north pole, enter the south, form continuous closed loops, never cross, and crowd where the field is…  c) Organised groups of physicists promote the subject, run conferences, share information and support teaching; the…
**Answer: a) Light-emitting diodes turn current into light.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Light-emitting diodes turn ______ into light.**
a) current  b) displacement  c) rotation
**Answer: a) current**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Photo______s turn light into current.**
a) knowledge  b) diode  c) laminar
**Answer: b) diode**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Which statement about diodes at work is correct?**
a) Linear momentum p = mv ("the effort needed to stop a body"); impulse is force × time and equals the change of momentum,…  b) Diodes guard circuits against over-voltage — the practical-uses run of §5.2/5.3.  c) Bench experiment: place a block on a board, load and pull it, and find coefficients from the forces at slipping and…
**Answer: b) Diodes guard circuits against over-voltage — the practical-uses run of §5.2/5.3.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Diodes guard circuits against over-______ — the practical-uses run of §5.2/5.3.**
a) junction  b) voltage  c) specific heat
**Answer: b) voltage**
