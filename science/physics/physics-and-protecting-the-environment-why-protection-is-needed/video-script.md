Today: **why the environment needs protecting — physics' own reasons**. Air, water, land and climate are a working system of cycles, and our energy use now runs at the cycles' own scale: every conversion sheds heat, combustion sheds gases, and persistent materials pile up where no cycle collects them. Physics measures the change and traces it to its source — that is why this topic opens a physics book.
