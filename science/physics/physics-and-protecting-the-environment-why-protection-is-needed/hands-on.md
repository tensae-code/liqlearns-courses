# Hands-on — Physics and protecting the environment — why protection is needed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One day of outputs — where does each go?
On paper, draw three columns: OUTPUT, NEXT STOP, TAKEN BACK BY A CYCLE? Fill in at least six outputs of one day in your home (cooking smoke, plastic wrapping, vegetable peelings, bath water, a battery, motorbike exhaust). Peelings and bath water are reclaimed by cycles; plastic and batteries are not, on any human timescale. Your table is the lesson in one page: protection is needed exactly at the rows where the third column says no.
