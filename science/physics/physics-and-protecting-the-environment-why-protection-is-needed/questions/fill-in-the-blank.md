# Physics and protecting the environment — why protection is needed — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The environment's matter is recycled by natural _____, such as the water and carbon cycles.
**Answer: cycles**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Burning fossil fuels releases stored _____ far faster than cycles can reabsorb it.
**Answer: carbon**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Every energy conversion sheds some energy as waste _____.
**Answer: heat**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Protection judgements in physics are made from _____ — measured quantities, not slogans.
**Answer: measurements**
