# Physics and protecting the environment — why protection is needed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In this lesson the environment is best described as…
**Answer: a) a working system of air, water, land and climate cycles  b) scenery behind human life  c) everything outside cities only  d) a list of endangered animals**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What has fundamentally changed in the human impact on the environment?
**Answer: a) its scale — human activity now moves matter and energy at the cycles' own scale  b) humans started using fire  c) the cycles stopped working  d) population stopped growing**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The core driver of most environmental damage, in physics terms, is…
**Answer: a) the production and conversion of energy, with its waste heat, gases and materials  b) gravity  c) the Moon's tides  d) static electricity**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A physicist's first contribution to a protection debate is…
**Answer: a) measurement — concentrations, energies, temperatures, doses  b) slogans  c) voting  d) building fences**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Combustion of fossil fuels is singled out because it…
**Answer: a) releases in years carbon that took millions of years to store, faster than cycles reabsorb it  b) produces no energy  c) is the only energy source  d) cools the planet**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) What can physics NOT do in the protection question?
**Answer: a) choose society's values and decisions for it  b) measure a gas concentration  c) compute an energy balance  d) trace a pollutant to its source**
