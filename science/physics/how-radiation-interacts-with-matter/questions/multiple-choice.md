# How radiation interacts with matter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Alpha radiation in air travels about…
a) Kilometres  b) A few centimetres before stopping  c) One metre exactly  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) A few centimetres before stopping**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Alpha spends its energy by…
a) Passing through untouched  b) Ionising atoms densely along its path  c) Splitting nuclei  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Ionising atoms densely along its path**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A photon in matter…
a) Slows gradually to rest  b) Survives whole until one event removes it  c) Always reflects  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Survives whole until one event removes it**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Above 1.02 MeV, a photon near a nucleus can vanish into…
a) Two neutrons  b) Pair production — an electron and a positron  c) A sound wave  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Pair production — an electron and a positron**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Beam intensity through an absorber follows…
a) A straight line to zero at a fixed range  b) I = I₀e^(−μx) — exponential fall  c) No change  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) I = I₀e^(−μx) — exponential fall**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The half-value thickness is the thickness that…
a) Stops the beam completely  b) Halves the beam's intensity  c) Doubles the beam  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Halves the beam's intensity**
