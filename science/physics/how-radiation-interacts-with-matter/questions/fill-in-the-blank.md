# How radiation interacts with matter — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Charged particles stop after a definite ___.
**Answer:** range

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Photons are removed by the photoelectric effect, ___ scattering or pair production.
**Answer:** Compton

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) I = I₀e^(−μx): μ is the ___ coefficient.
**Answer:** absorption

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Each half-value thickness ___ the beam.
**Answer:** halves
