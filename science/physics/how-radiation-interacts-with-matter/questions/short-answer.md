# How radiation interacts with matter — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) Why is shielding against gamma a matter of fractions, not a stopping thickness?
**Answer:** Each photon is removed by chance in a single event; layers remove fractions of the survivors, so intensity fades exponentially without a fixed range.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Compare alpha and beta ranges and the reason.
**Answer:** Alpha: centimetres of air — heavy, doubly charged, ionises densely. Beta: metres of air — lighter, singly charged, ionises thinly.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What is fluorescence in this lesson's terms?
**Answer:** Absorbed radiation energy re-emitted as visible light — the interactions used in screens and detectors.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) What does μ in the absorption law depend on?
**Answer:** The material and the radiation's type/energy.
