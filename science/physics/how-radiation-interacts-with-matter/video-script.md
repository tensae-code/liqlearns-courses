# Video script — How radiation interacts with matter

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **each radiation spends itself differently in matter**. A charged particle ploughing through matter ionises atom after atom, paying energy for every ionisation until it stops: alpha, heavy and doubly charged, spends itself in a few centimetres of air or a sheet of paper; beta, lighter and faster, ranges metres in air and millimetres in metal. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — alpha and beta — ionisation with a range; photons — removed whole, one event at a time; the exponential law of absorption — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
