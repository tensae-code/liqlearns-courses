# Hands-on — How radiation interacts with matter

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Model the exponential law with dice or coins as photons
Model the exponential law with dice or coins as photons: throw 100, remove every one showing a chosen face (an 'absorption'), count the survivors, repeat — plot survivors against rounds and see the halving pattern of I = I₀e^(−μx) emerge from pure chance, exactly as photons are removed.
