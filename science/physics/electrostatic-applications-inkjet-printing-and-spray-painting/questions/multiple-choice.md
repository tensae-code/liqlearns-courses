# Electrostatic applications — inkjet printing and spray painting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In an inkjet printer, a drop's landing place is set by…
**Answer: a) the amount of charge it is given  b) its colour  c) its size only  d) gravity**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Uncharged drops in the printer…
**Answer: a) fly straight into a gutter and are recycled  b) print black  c) stop mid-air  d) charge themselves**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In spray painting, the workpiece is…
**Answer: a) earthed, attracting the charged droplets  b) insulated  c) heated  d) charged the same sign as the paint**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Charged droplets repel each other, which…
**Answer: a) spreads them into a fine, even cloud  b) clumps them  c) dries them  d) removes their charge**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The force steering the drops is…
**Answer: a) F = qE  b) F = ma only  c) friction  d) buoyancy**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Wrap-around coating happens because droplets follow…
**Answer: a) field lines to all faces of the earthed object  b) straight lines only  c) the wind  d) gravity**
