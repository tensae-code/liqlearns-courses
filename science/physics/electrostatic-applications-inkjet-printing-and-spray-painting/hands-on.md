# Hands-on — Electrostatic applications — inkjet printing and spray painting

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Rub a balloon and hold it near a thin stream of water from a tap: watch the stream bend — a field steering charged matter, the printer's principle at kitchen scale. (2) Charge a comb and pick up paper bits, then try to 'print' a pattern by moving the comb — feel the control problem the electronics solve. (3) Compare an ordinary spray bottle's cone with the charged cloud's described behaviour: list what mutual repulsion does to droplet size and spread.
