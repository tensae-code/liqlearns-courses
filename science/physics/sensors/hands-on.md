# Hands-on — Sensors

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A sensor you already own
Open a free sensor app (or the phone's built-in diagnostics) and find the light sensor's readout in lux. Record it in three places: facing a window, under a table, covered by your hand. Then find the orientation/magnetic sensor and watch its numbers swing as you rotate. For each, write: quantity sensed, signal's form (a number the processor reads), and one automatic action the phone takes from it (screen dimming, screen rotation, compass). You have surveyed two sensors and their characteristics without opening anything.
