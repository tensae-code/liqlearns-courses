# Sensors — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A sensor is a device that…
**Answer: a) converts a physical quantity into an electrical signal  b) amplifies a signal  c) stores charge  d) produces light**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) An LDR's resistance…
**Answer: a) falls as the light on it grows  b) rises with light  c) never changes  d) measures temperature**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A resistive sensor is usually read by placing it in…
**Answer: a) a potential divider, whose output voltage tracks the resistance  b) a transformer  c) a capacitor  d) an aerial**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A piezoelectric sensor is a generating sensor: it…
**Answer: a) makes a voltage when it is squeezed  b) needs its resistance heated  c) changes colour  d) stores a magnetic field**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Sensitivity means…
**Answer: a) the size of the signal change per unit change of the quantity sensed  b) the biggest quantity the sensor survives  c) how fast the sensor breaks  d) the sensor's price**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) An NTC thermistor's resistance as temperature rises…
**Answer: a) falls steeply — a known, repeatable, non-linear curve  b) rises linearly forever  c) stays constant  d) becomes zero at once**
