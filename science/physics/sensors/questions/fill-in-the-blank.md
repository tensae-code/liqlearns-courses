# Sensors — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A _____ changes its resistance with temperature; an LDR changes its with light.
**Answer: thermistor**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A strain gauge senses _____ — it changes resistance as it is stretched.
**Answer: strain**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The span of quantity a sensor can follow is its _____.
**Answer: range**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A good sensor gives the same signal for the same quantity every time — that quality is _____.
**Answer: repeatability**
