# Video script — Sensors

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **sensors — the circuit's senses**. A thermistor feels temperature as resistance, an LDR feels light, a strain gauge feels stretch, a piezo crystal answers pressure with a voltage. Resistive sensors speak through a potential divider; generating sensors whisper a voltage of their own. Judge any of them by sensitivity, range and response — then hand its signal to the amplifier, and the machine can react to the world.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
