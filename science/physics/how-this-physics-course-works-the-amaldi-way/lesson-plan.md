# Lesson plan — How this Physics course works — the Italian (Amaldi) way

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** How this Physics course works — the Italian (Amaldi) way
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18), beside the other how-this-course-works lessons — the Ethiopian grade ways, the Zambia CDC module way and the NERDC theme way: this is Italy's way, the three-volume Amaldi course for the licei scientifici
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Italy's Physics course (Ugo Amaldi's «L'Amaldi per i licei scientifici.blu», Zanichelli) is built as three volumes whose every chapter splits theory from exercises, solves **model problems** in full before the student's own sets, plants **“stop and think”** prompts inside the theory, teaches the maths exactly when the physics needs it, and closes each chapter with a formula map, a test and a **“ready for the check?”** self-test — the whole course mapped onto the state examination (esame di Stato)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Earlier lessons make a good preparation and are recommended, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the three Amaldi volumes in order and the territory each carries.
2. List a chapter's parts in order — theory, stop-and-think prompts, model problems, exercises, formula map, test, self-test — and say what each part is for.
3. Say where the course's mathematics comes from and when it is taught.
4. Name the examination the course is mapped onto, and the Agenda-2030 strand the 2020 Vol. 1 adds.
5. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson text alone.
