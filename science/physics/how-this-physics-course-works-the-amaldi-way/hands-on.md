# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Italian (Amaldi) way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rebuild a chapter's shape
Take a topic you know well. On one page, lay out the Amaldi chapter machine for it: a theory section heading, one stop-and-think question written at the hardest point, one model problem (write only its opening line — the bridge, not the solution), three exercise headings in rising difficulty, and the five formulas its formula map would carry. Say aloud: the shape is the same every time, so the student's whole effort goes into the physics, never into guessing the book's habits.

## Sit a ready-for-the-check self-test
From the lesson, write yourself four check questions — one on the volumes, one on the chapter parts, one on the maths boxes, one on the examination frame. Close the lesson, answer them in writing, then mark yourself honestly against the text. Say aloud: the self-test is private and comes before the teacher's test — that order is the design.
