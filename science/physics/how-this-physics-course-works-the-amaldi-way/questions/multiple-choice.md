# How this Physics course works — the Italian (Amaldi) way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Italy's Physics course for the licei scientifici is…
a) Ugo Amaldi's three-volume «L'Amaldi per i licei scientifici.blu» (Zanichelli)  b) Three unrelated books by different authors  c) A single workbook  d) A set of examination papers only
**Answer: a) Ugo Amaldi's three-volume «L'Amaldi per i licei scientifici.blu» (Zanichelli)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Volume 1 of the Amaldi course carries…
a) Relativity and quanta  b) Mechanics and thermodynamics  c) Waves and the magnetic field only  d) Particles and frontiers only
**Answer: b) Mechanics and thermodynamics**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Problemi modello — model problems — are…
a) Complete problems solved in full before the student's own exercise sets  b) Problems with no solutions anywhere  c) Problems the teacher keeps secret  d) The final examination
**Answer: c) Problems the teacher keeps secret**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) “Fermati a pensare” prompts sit…
a) Inside the theory, at the point a careless reader would slide past a difficulty  b) Only in the examination  c) In the teacher's guide only  d) At the back of the book
**Answer: d) At the back of the book**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The mathematics the physics needs is taught…
a) In a separate maths course the student must finish first  b) In “La matematica per la fisica” boxes, exactly when the physics needs it  c) Never — the course avoids mathematics  d) Only in Volume 3
**Answer: a) In a separate maths course the student must finish first**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The whole Amaldi course is mapped onto…
a) A university entrance test in another country  b) The esame di Stato, Italy's state examination  c) No examination at all  d) The Agenda 2030 conference
**Answer: b) The esame di Stato, Italy's state examination**
