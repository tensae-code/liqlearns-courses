# How this Physics course works — the Italian (Amaldi) way — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Amaldi's course is published by _____ .
**Answer: Zanichelli**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The fully solved problems that come before the student's own sets are called Problemi _____ .
**Answer: modello (model problems)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The prompts planted inside the theory tell the reader to stop and _____ .
**Answer: think (Fermati a pensare)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The state examination the whole course is mapped onto is the esame di _____ .
**Answer: Stato**
