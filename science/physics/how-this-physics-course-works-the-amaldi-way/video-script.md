# Video script — How this Physics course works — the Italian (Amaldi) way

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **how Italy builds its whole Physics course — Amaldi's three blue volumes**. Volume 1 is mechanics and thermodynamics. Volume 2 is waves, and the electric and magnetic fields. Volume 3 is induction, relativity and quanta. Inside, every chapter runs the same machine: theory pages first, with little “stop and think” prompts planted in them; then **model problems**, solved in full, step by step; only then your own exercises. The maths you need arrives in its own boxes, exactly when the physics needs it. Each chapter ends with a map of its formulas and a private self-test — *are you ready for the check?* — and the whole course points at one destination: the state examination, the esame di Stato.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
