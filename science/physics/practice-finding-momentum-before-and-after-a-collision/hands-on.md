# Hands-on — Practice — finding momentum before and after a collision

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Film a collision, measure the hand-over
On a smooth floor, film a phone-video of a rolling ball colliding with an identical resting ball (use a metre rule laid beside the path as your scale). Play the video frame by frame: count frames for the incoming ball to cross 20 cm before impact, and for the outgoing ball to cross 20 cm after. Speeds are distance ÷ (frames × frame time); with equal masses, compare incoming momentum (mass × v) with outgoing. Then repeat with the target ball wrapped in sticky tape so they stick — and measure the pair's slower speed. Your two comparisons are the whole lesson in miniature.
