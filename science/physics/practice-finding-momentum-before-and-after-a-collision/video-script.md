# Video script — Practice — finding momentum before and after a collision

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **momentum, measured before and after**. Weigh the trolleys, time them in, multiply m by v — that is the momentum before. Collide them — sticking together or bouncing apart — time them out, and add the signed momenta. The totals match, apart from a small shortfall that friction explains. The conservation law stops being a sentence and becomes a measurement.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
