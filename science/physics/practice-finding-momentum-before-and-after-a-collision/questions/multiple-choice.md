# Practice — finding momentum before and after a collision — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) To find a trolley's momentum before the collision you must measure…
**Answer: a) its mass and its velocity — p = mv needs both  b) its mass only  c) its velocity only  d) its kinetic energy**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The sign convention matters because momentum is…
**Answer: a) a vector — direction is part of the quantity  b) always positive  c) a kind of energy  d) measured in newtons**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) After a sticking collision the momentum is computed with…
**Answer: a) the combined mass moving at the pair's single velocity  b) each trolley's old velocity  c) the heavier trolley's velocity only  d) the sum of the two old speeds**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A trolley rebounding back along the track after a bounce carries…
**Answer: a) negative momentum in the chosen convention  b) no momentum  c) double momentum  d) positive momentum by rule**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In a careful school run the after-total is usually…
**Answer: a) slightly less than the before-total, because friction steals momentum throughout  b) exactly equal to the last digit  c) much larger than the before-total  d) zero**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The law is supported when the totals agree…
**Answer: a) within the measured errors, at more than one incoming speed  b) for one special speed only  c) on paper without measuring  d) after rounding away the difference**
