# Practice — finding momentum before and after a collision — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Momentum is mass times _____.
**Answer: velocity**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Trolleys are _____ on a balance to get their masses in kilograms.
**Answer: weighed**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In a _____ collision the pair moves off as one mass at one velocity.
**Answer: sticking**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) _____ on the track steadily steals momentum during the run.
**Answer: Friction**
