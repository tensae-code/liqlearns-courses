# Electric current in metals — resistance and temperature, superconductivity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The carriers of current in a metal are…
a) Protons moving through the lattice  b) Free electrons forming a gas (sea) through the whole metal  c) Atoms drifting to the positive terminal  d) Holes only
**Answer: a) Protons moving through the lattice**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) When a lamp is switched on it lights at once, although electron drift is…
a) About light speed  b) Faster than light  c) A fraction of a millimetre per second — the signal, not the drift, travels fast  d) Zero
**Answer: b) Faster than light**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A metal's resistivity rises with temperature because…
a) The hotter lattice vibrates more and scatters the drifting electrons harder  b) Electrons evaporate from hot metal  c) The wire expands and lengthens only  d) The battery weakens when warm
**Answer: c) The wire expands and lengthens only**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) With α = 0.004 /°C, warming a wire by 100 °C multiplies its resistance by about…
a) 1.4  b) 4  c) 100  d) 1.04
**Answer: d) 1.04**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Onnes's 1911 discovery was that mercury at ≈ 4.2 K has resistance…
a) Half its room value  b) Too small to matter  c) Exactly zero  d) Infinite
**Answer: a) Half its room value**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The Meissner effect is a superconductor…
a) Expelling magnetic fields from its interior  b) Melting at its critical temperature  c) Glowing when current flows  d) Attracting all metals
**Answer: b) Melting at its critical temperature**
