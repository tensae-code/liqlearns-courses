# Electric current in metals — resistance and temperature, superconductivity — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The free electrons in a metal are pictured as an electron _____ .
**Answer: gas / sea**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In R = ρL/A, the material property ρ is called _____ .
**Answer: resistivity**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The temperature below which a superconductor loses all resistance is its _____ temperature.
**Answer: critical**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Superconductivity in mercury was discovered in 1911 by Kamerlingh _____ .
**Answer: Onnes**
