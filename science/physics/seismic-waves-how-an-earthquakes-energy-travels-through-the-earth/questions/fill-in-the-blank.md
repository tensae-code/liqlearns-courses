# Seismic waves — how an earthquake's energy travels through the Earth — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The surface point directly above an earthquake's focus is called the _____.
**Answer: epicentre**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) P waves are _____ waves — the rock squeezes and stretches along the direction of travel.
**Answer: longitudinal**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) S waves are transverse and can travel through _____ only, never through liquids.
**Answer: solids**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The instrument that records the ground's motion from an earthquake is a _____.
**Answer: seismograph**
