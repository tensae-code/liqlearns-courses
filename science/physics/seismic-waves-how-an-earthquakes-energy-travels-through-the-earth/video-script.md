# Video script — Seismic waves — how an earthquake's energy travels through the Earth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **an earthquake speaks in three waves**. Underground, rock slips at the focus; the epicentre sits on the surface above it. Fast squeezing P waves race through rock and liquid alike, slower sideways S waves follow through solid rock only, and last come the surface waves, rolling along the ground — the ones that knock the buildings down.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
