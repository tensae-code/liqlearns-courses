# Hands-on — Seismic waves — how an earthquake's energy travels through the Earth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two waves in one spring
Stretch a long spring (a slinky) along a smooth table, one person holding each end. First, send a P wave: push one end quickly towards the other and pull it back — watch the squeezing pulse race along the spring's length. Then send an S wave: flick one end sideways — watch the sideways wave travel, slower and swinging across the line. Both started at one "focus" (your hand); the longitudinal one always wins the race, exactly as P beats S to every seismograph.
