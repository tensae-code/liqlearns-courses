# Faraday's law; Lenz's law — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Induced emf equals the rate of change of flux (with the coil's turns multiplying it).**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Lenz's law gives its direction — the induced effect opposes the change causing it, as energy conservation demands.**
**Answer: True**
