# Lesson plan — Faraday's law; Lenz's law

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** Faraday's law; Lenz's law
**Place in the curriculum:** Grade 12 · Unit 4 — Electromagnetism.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** faraday's law and lenz's law — Induced emf equals the rate of change of flux (with the coil's turns multiplying it).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 12 Physics lessons before it in Unit 4 — Electromagnetism — and on the learner's own world: the examples are the learner's home, street, school and body.

## Sub lessons
1. **Faraday's law — emf from changing flux** — Induced emf equals the rate of change of flux (with the coil's turns multiplying it).
2. **Lenz's law — the direction opposes the change** — Lenz's law gives its direction — the induced effect opposes the change causing it, as energy conservation demands.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
