# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Bulgarian Grade 7 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a lesson by its type
Take any textbook lesson in front of you. First, label it: is it teaching something new, practising problems, measuring in a lab, summarising, or self-checking? Write the label at the top of a page. Under it, write the three boxes you would build for it — one line of "I already know…", one worked example you will copy carefully, and one line you expect in "What I learned". Work the lesson, then compare: did your expected harvest match the real one? The mismatch is exactly what to re-read.
