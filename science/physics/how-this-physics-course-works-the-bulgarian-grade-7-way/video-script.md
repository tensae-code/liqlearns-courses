# Video script — How this Physics course works — the Bulgarian Grade 7 way

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **how a Bulgarian Grade 7 physics book is built**. Forty-seven numbered lessons, each with a printed job — new knowledge, problem-solving, laboratory work, summary, or a self-check called "I know and I can". Every lesson opens with "I already know…", shows a worked example, and closes with "What I learned", a "Curious" box and tasks. An entry test at the front door, an exit test and a glossary at the back. Learn the shape once, and every lesson is familiar before you read a word of it.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
