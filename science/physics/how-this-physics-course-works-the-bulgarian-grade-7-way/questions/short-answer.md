# How this Physics course works — the Bulgarian Grade 7 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does each lesson carry a printed type?
**Answer: It tells the learner the lesson's job before starting — teach, practise, measure, summarise or self-check — so the work is done the right way.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What goes in the "Curious" box?
**Answer: A striking extra fact that widens the lesson — interest beyond the required teaching.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What should you do when your "What I learned" harvest does not match what a lesson taught?
**Answer: Re-read the parts the mismatch points to — the box is a check on your own learning.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) How do the entry and exit tests work together?
**Answer: The entry test measures what you can do before the year; the exit test measures the same ground after it — the difference is the year's growth.**
