# How this Physics course works — the Bulgarian Grade 7 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A laboratory lesson's job is to…
a) Teach a new idea  b) Measure and investigate with apparatus  c) Summarise a strand  d) Check yourself without help
**Answer: b) Measure and investigate with apparatus**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The self-check lessons are titled…
**Answer: a) "What I learned"  b) "Curious"  c) "I know and I can"  d) "I already know…"**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A lesson opens with the box…
**Answer: a) "What I learned"  b) "I already know…" — recall of earlier learning  c) The glossary  d) The exit test**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The worked example is for…
a) Copying without thinking  b) Seeing a task solved in full before solving your own  c) Skipping the tasks  d) The teacher only
**Answer: b) Seeing a task solved in full before solving your own**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The exit test sits…
a) At the front of the book  b) Inside every lesson  c) At the end of the book, closing the year  d) In the glossary
**Answer: c) At the end of the book, closing the year**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The glossary (Речник) collects…
a) The answers  b) The year's words with their meanings  c) The lesson types  d) Laboratory safety rules
**Answer: b) The year's words with their meanings**
