# How this Physics course works — the Bulgarian Grade 7 way

## Do it first
Before you read: picture a course where lesson 23 is not just "the next page" but a labelled job — its type is printed on it. What five jobs could a lesson have, and how would knowing the job change how you read it? This lesson walks through the Bulgarian Grade 7 book's design piece by piece, so you can use it instead of skipping it.

## Sub lesson 1 — A numbered course of typed lessons

The Bulgarian Grade 7 course (Pedagog 6) is one numbered sequence of lessons — Lessons 1 to 47 — and every lesson carries a type: new knowledge, problem-solving (решаване на задачи), laboratory work, summary (обобщение), or a self-check titled "I know and I can" (Знам и мога). The type tells you the job before you start: a new-knowledge lesson teaches, a problem-solving lesson practises on set tasks, a laboratory lesson measures, a summary lesson gathers a whole strand, and an "I know and I can" lesson is where you prove the strand to yourself. The year's strands run electric current (Lessons 1–16), light and sound (17–38), and from the atom to the cosmos (39–46), closing with a year review (47).

## Sub lesson 2 — The boxes inside every lesson

Every lesson is built from the same boxes, in the same places. It opens with "I already know…" (Вече знам) — a recall box that wakes up the earlier learning the new lesson stands on. A worked example shows a task solved in full before you solve your own. "What I learned" (Какво научих) closes the teaching with the lesson's harvest in a few lines, a "Curious" (Любопитно) box adds the striking extra fact, and questions and tasks finish the lesson. Learn the boxes once and every lesson in the book is already familiar in shape — only the physics changes.

## Sub lesson 3 — Tests at the doors, and the glossary at the back

The book opens with an entry test — what you can already do before the year starts — and closes with an exit test at printed p. 109, so the year's growth is measured at both doors. A glossary (Речник) at printed p. 110 collects the year's words with their meanings, and answers follow at p. 112. Use them in that order: entry test honestly at the start, glossary whenever a word wobbles, exit test at the end — and any miss points back to the numbered lesson that teaches it.

## The key ideas

- The course is a numbered sequence (Lessons 1–47) and every lesson has a type: new knowledge, problem-solving, laboratory work, summary, or an "I know and I can" self-check
- Every lesson opens with an "I already know…" recall box and a worked example, and closes its teaching with "What I learned", a "Curious" box, and questions and tasks
- An entry test opens the book and an exit test closes it; the glossary at the back collects the year's words
