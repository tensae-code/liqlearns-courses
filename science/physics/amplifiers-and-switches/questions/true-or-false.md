# Amplifiers and switches — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The transistor enlarges a signal (the common-emitter amplifier inverts the phase).**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) As switch, it turns devices fully on or off — the two jobs behind its use in computers, satellites, phones and control systems.**
**Answer: True**
