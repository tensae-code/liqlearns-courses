# Hands-on — History of physics — the Central Asian scholars

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The horizon dip
Stand on a high place (a hill, a rooftop with a teacher, a tall building's window) and look at the horizon: it sits slightly below eye level, and the higher you stand, the further you see. On paper, draw the Earth as a circle, yourself at two heights, and the sight-line to the horizon each time. Write the rule your drawing shows: the horizon drops and retreats as the observer rises — the effect Beruni turned into a measurement of the whole Earth.
