# History of physics — the Central Asian scholars — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the two Greek founders the book sets at the start of the story, and one thing each did.
**Answer: Aristotle sorted motion and the heavens into a system; Archimedes turned physics into measurement — levers, floating bodies and the value of π.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What did Beruni and Ibn Sina exchange that shows physics being done, not just stored?
**Answer: Letters arguing real questions of physics — whether the heavens could be otherwise, and how heat and light behave — argued between two working scholars.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does the lesson call physics a relay rather than an invention?
**Answer: Because each age received the science, tested and corrected it, and handed it on further: Greece to Central Asia to Galileo and Newton — no single country or person invented it whole.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Give one dated fact that places Central Asian physics before Galileo.
**Answer: Any of: al-Khwarizmi c. 780–850 founding algebra's method; Beruni (973–1048) measuring the Earth; Ibn Sina (980–1037) arguing the inertia idea; Khayyam's calendar reform (1048–1131) — all centuries before Galileo (1564–1642).**
