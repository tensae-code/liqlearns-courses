# History of physics — the Central Asian scholars — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The scholar from Khwarizm who gave algebra its method is al-_____.
**Answer: Khwarizmi**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Beruni measured the _____ of the Earth from a mountain and the horizon's dip.
**Answer: radius**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A claim is worth what you can _____ of it — Beruni's rule of measurement.
**Answer: measure**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Ibn Sina's idea that a thrown body keeps moving unless stopped points toward Newton's first law, the law of _____.
**Answer: inertia**
