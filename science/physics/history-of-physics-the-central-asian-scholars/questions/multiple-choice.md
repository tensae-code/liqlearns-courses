# History of physics — the Central Asian scholars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the Grade 6 book's founding story, the Central Asian scholars stand…
**Answer: a) beside Aristotle, Archimedes, Galileo and Newton  b) only after Newton  c) in a separate subject  d) nowhere — the book is Greek only**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Al-Khwarizmi's gift to every later physics student is…
**Answer: a) algebra — solving by rules  b) the telescope  c) the steam engine  d) the calendar**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Beruni measured the Earth's radius by…
**Answer: a) watching how far the horizon dips from a mountain  b) sailing around it  c) weighing it  d) counting its cities**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) For Beruni, a claim in physics is worth…
**Answer: a) what you can measure of it  b) what the oldest book says  c) what the ruler orders  d) nothing until Galileo**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Ibn Sina argued that a body thrown through empty space would…
**Answer: a) keep moving unless something stopped it  b) stop at once  c) fall straight down  d) turn into light**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Omar Khayyam's physics work included…
**Answer: a) weighing bodies in air and water to test alloys, and a calendar reform  b) inventing the printing press  c) discovering electricity  d) building the first telescope**
