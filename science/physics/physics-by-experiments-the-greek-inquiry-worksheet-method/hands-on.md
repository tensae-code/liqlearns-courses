# Hands-on — Physics by experiments — the Greek inquiry worksheet method

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run one worksheet yourself
You need: a spring or elastic band, a few coins or masses, a ruler, paper for a table and graph. Follow the book's five steps exactly. 1) Observe the spring stretching under one load; write what interests you. 2) Write a hypothesis about extension versus load before measuring. 3) Measure the extension for at least five loads, tabulate, and plot extension against load. 4) Write your conclusion under the graph — does it agree with your hypothesis? 5) Apply: say what your graph predicts for a load you did not test, then test that load and check. Say aloud: the conclusion came from my numbers, not from the back of the book.
