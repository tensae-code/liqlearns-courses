# Physics by experiments — the Greek inquiry worksheet method — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does the method insist the hypothesis is written before the experiment?
**Answer: So the experiment has a definite claim to test, and so the pupil cannot quietly rewrite their thinking afterwards to match the result. The written guess makes the comparison at the conclusion step honest.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A pupil says 'the recording is just paperwork; the science was the experiment.' Answer as the Greek book would.
**Answer: In this method the table, the graph and the written conclusion ARE the result: without them there is nothing to conclude from and nothing to compare with the hypothesis. Recording is a step of the science itself.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Give the five steps in order, one line each.
**Answer: 1 Observe and get interested. 2 Discuss, wonder, hypothesise. 3 Act and experiment (measure, tabulate, graph). 4 Conclude and record. 5 Apply, explain, generalise.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Name one worksheet-to-world jump the book itself makes.
**Answer: The covered-flask greenhouse worksheet generalises to the planet's greenhouse effect; equally, the short-circuit worksheet ends at the fuse protecting the home, and the generator worksheet ends in a pupil-designed wind generator.**
