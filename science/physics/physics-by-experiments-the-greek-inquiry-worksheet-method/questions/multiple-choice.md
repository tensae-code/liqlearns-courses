# Physics by experiments — the Greek inquiry worksheet method — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The first step of every Greek worksheet is…
a) Read the explanation  b) Observe the phenomenon and get interested  c) Answer questions  d) Copy the conclusion
**Answer: b) Observe the phenomenon and get interested**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A hypothesis in this method is written…
a) After the experiment, to match the results  b) By the teacher only  c) Before the experiment — a testable guess the experiment will answer  d) Only when the class is sure it is right
**Answer: c) Before the experiment — a testable guess the experiment will answer**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) At the experiment step, the pupil produces…
a) A copied table from the book  b) Their own measurements, table and graph  c) A drawing only  d) The teacher's conclusion
**Answer: b) Their own measurements, table and graph**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) If the conclusion contradicts the hypothesis, the method treats this as…
a) A failed worksheet to redo until it agrees  b) Cheating  c) The experiment answering — a result, honestly recorded  d) A reason to change the data
**Answer: c) The experiment answering — a result, honestly recorded**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The final step — apply, explain, generalise — asks the pupil to…
a) Memorise the definition  b) Carry the conclusion to a new, everyday or larger case  c) Start the next worksheet  d) Hand in the book
**Answer: b) Carry the conclusion to a new, everyday or larger case**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The Grade 7 book teaches its topics as…
a) Twelve prose chapters  b) Lectures  c) Twelve inquiry worksheets following one five-step method  d) Videos
**Answer: c) Twelve inquiry worksheets following one five-step method**
