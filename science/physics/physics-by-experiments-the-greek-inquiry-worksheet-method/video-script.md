# Video script — Physics by experiments — the Greek inquiry worksheet method

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **physics you do, not physics you read**. Greece's Grade 7 book has no chapters to memorise. It has twelve worksheets, and every one walks the same five steps. First, observe — watch the thing happen and get interested. Second, hypothesise — write your guess before anyone touches the apparatus. Third, experiment — measure it yourself, table it, graph it. Fourth, conclude — write what YOUR numbers say, even if they kill your guess. Fifth, apply — carry the conclusion somewhere new: the flask becomes the planet, the bench circuit becomes the fuse at home. One method, every topic. That is the Greek way.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
