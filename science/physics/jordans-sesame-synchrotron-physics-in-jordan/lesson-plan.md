## Sub lessons
1. **The machine — a ring of fields doing the unit's jobs** — SESAME (Synchrotron-light for Experimental Science and Applications in the Middle East) stores electrons at 2.5 GeV. Around its ring, dipole magnets bend the beam's path into a circle — the magnetic force on a moving charge, F = qvB, as the centripetal force; accelerating cavities give the electrons energy back with electric fields; and the bending itself makes the electrons radiate the synchrotron light that is the machine's product.
2. **The light — a tool for every science** — The radiated light spans infrared to X-rays, brighter and more tuneable than laboratory sources. Beamlines use it as physics' gift to the other sciences: crystallography reading protein and material structures, archaeology and heritage science examining artefacts without harming them, environmental and materials research — the reason a physics machine serves biologists, chemists and historians.
3. **Why Jordan's book opens here — physics with an address** — The placement is Jordan's own way: the unit on magnetic force, fields of currents and induction begins not with an abstraction but with a machine on Jordanian soil, run by an international centre Jordan hosts and its scientists use. Every topic of the coming unit — force on a charge, force on a conductor, fields of coils, induction — can be pointed at somewhere in the ring.
