# Hands-on — Jordan's SESAME synchrotron — physics in Jordan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Hold a magnet near (never touching) the side of an old-style screen or use a simulation: watch a beam deflect — the ring's bending in miniature. (2) On a map of a synchrotron ring, mark where bending, acceleration and light extraction must happen and justify each placement. (3) Find one published SESAME result in any science and write three lines: the light used, the sample, the finding.
