# Jordan's SESAME synchrotron — physics in Jordan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which force keeps the electrons circling, and what supplies it?
**Answer: The magnetic part of the Lorentz force, F = qvB, from the ring's dipole magnets — always perpendicular to the velocity, so it bends the path into a circle without speeding the electrons up.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) If the magnets bend but do not energise, what replaces the energy the electrons radiate away?
**Answer: The accelerating cavities: electric fields in the radio-frequency cavities give the electrons back, each lap, the energy they lost as synchrotron light, holding the beam at 2.5 GeV.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why is synchrotron light a better tool than a laboratory X-ray tube for crystallography?
**Answer: It is far brighter, spans a continuous range from infrared to X-rays, and can be tuned to the needed wavelength — so smaller crystals and faster, finer measurements become possible.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Name SESAME's full English expansion.
**Answer: Synchrotron-light for Experimental Science and Applications in the Middle East.**
