# Jordan's SESAME synchrotron — physics in Jordan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) SESAME is hosted in…
**Answer: a) Balqa, Jordan  b) Geneva only  c) Cairo  d) Riyadh**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Its stored electrons reach an energy of…
**Answer: a) 2.5 GeV  b) 2.5 eV  c) 25 MeV  d) 250 GeV**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The beam is bent around the ring by…
**Answer: a) the magnetic force on moving charges  b) gravity  c) friction  d) electric attraction to the centre**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The synchrotron light is produced because the electrons…
**Answer: a) are bent (accelerated) in their path  b) collide  c) stop  d) cool down**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) SESAME's light is used for…
**Answer: a) crystallography and heritage science, among many fields  b) lighting the ring  c) heating samples only  d) power generation**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Jordan's book opens the magnetism unit with SESAME because…
**Answer: a) every topic of the unit is at work somewhere in the ring, on Jordanian soil  b) it is the unit's exam  c) it replaces the unit  d) it is a chemistry machine**
