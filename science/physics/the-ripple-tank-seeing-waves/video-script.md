# Video script — The ripple tank — seeing waves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **a shallow tray of water, lit from above and rippling from a vibrating bar, projects waves you can watch, measure and steer**. A shallow glass-bottomed tray holds a thin layer of water; a lamp above casts the ripples' shadows onto a white screen below, and a motor-driven bar (or a dipper for single pulses) sets the surface moving. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — the apparatus — water, light and a shadow screen; straight and circular waves on demand; barriers and shallows — steering the waves — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
