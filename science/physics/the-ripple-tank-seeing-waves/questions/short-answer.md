# The ripple tank — seeing waves — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the tank's main parts and each one's job.
**Answer:** Tray of shallow water (the medium), lamp (projects the pattern), screen (shows it), vibrating bar/dipper (makes the waves).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How is wavelength measured in the tank?
**Answer:** From the projected pattern — the spacing of bright crest lines, read against a ruler (often averaged over several waves).

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What does the barrier-gap experiment show?
**Answer:** Diffraction: waves spread behind a gap, more strongly as the gap approaches the wavelength.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does Punjab meet wave rules in the tank before stating them?
**Answer:** So reflection, refraction and diffraction are first seen as behaviour, then believed as rules.
