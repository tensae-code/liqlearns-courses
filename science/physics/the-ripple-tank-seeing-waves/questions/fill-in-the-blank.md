# The ripple tank — seeing waves — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Crests project as ___ lines on the screen.
**Answer:** bright

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A point dipper makes ___ waves.
**Answer:** circular

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Waves spreading behind a gap show ___.
**Answer:** diffraction

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) In shallow water the waves slow and bend — ___.
**Answer:** refraction
