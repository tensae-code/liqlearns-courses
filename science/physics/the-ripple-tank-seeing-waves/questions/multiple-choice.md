# The ripple tank — seeing waves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a ripple tank, wave crests appear on the screen as…
a) Dark holes  b) Bright lines in a projected shadow pattern  c) Coloured bands  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Bright lines in a projected shadow pattern**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The water depth in the tank is…
a) As deep as possible  b) Shallow — a thin layer  c) Irrelevant, no water is used  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Shallow — a thin layer**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A straight vibrating bar produces…
a) Circular waves  b) Plane waves with parallel crests  c) No waves  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Plane waves with parallel crests**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Wave speed in the tank is checked against…
a) v = mgh  b) v = fλ, measured on the visible pattern  c) Guesswork  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) v = fλ, measured on the visible pattern**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Waves through a narrow gap…
a) Stop completely  b) Diffract — spreading out behind the gap  c) Speed up without spreading  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Diffract — spreading out behind the gap**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A shallow region laid in the tank makes waves…
a) Disappear  b) Slow down and bend — refraction  c) Turn into sound  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Slow down and bend — refraction**
