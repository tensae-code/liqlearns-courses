# The ripple tank — seeing waves — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — A shallow glass-bottomed tray holds a thin layer of water; a lamp above casts the ripples' shadows onto a white screen below, and a motor-driven bar (or a dipper for single pulses) sets the surface moving; The straight bar makes plane waves — parallel crests marching together; a point dipper makes circular waves spreading from a centre; Place a barrier and the waves reflect with angle in equal to angle out; leave a gap and they diffract through it, spreading wider as the gap narrows toward the wavelength; lay a plate under part of the tray to make a shallow region and the waves slow and bend — refraction, seen — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
