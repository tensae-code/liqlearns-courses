# Hands-on — The ripple tank — seeing waves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the school ripple tank (or a baking tray under a bright lamp)
Build the school ripple tank (or a baking tray under a bright lamp): set the bar rippling, freeze the pattern with a phone photo over a ruler and measure the wavelength; time ten vibrations for the frequency and test v = fλ. Then open and narrow a barrier gap and sketch the diffraction at three widths.
