# Work–energy theorem; power — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about the work–energy theorem and power is correct?**
a) Moving a magnet near a coil (or the coil near the magnet) induces a current — electricity from magnetism, reversing…  b) Average and instantaneous acceleration as rate of change of velocity; the sign discussion is explicit: negative…  c) Net work equals the change in kinetic energy.
**Answer: c) Net work equals the change in kinetic energy.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Net work equals the change in kinetic ______.**
a) vector  b) expansion  c) energy
**Answer: c) energy**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which statement about the work–energy theorem and power is correct?**
a) A force twists when it acts off-centre: torque grows with the force and with the lever arm (the door-handle idea), and…  b) Power is the rate of doing work (watts), applied to machines and people in worked examples.  c) Forward bias (positive to P) shrinks the barrier and current flows; reverse bias widens it and current is blocked…
**Answer: b) Power is the rate of doing work (watts), applied to machines and people in worked examples.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ______ is the rate of doing work (watts), applied to machines and people in worked examples.**
a) rectification  b) power  c) displacement
**Answer: b) power**

**5.** Two pupils of nearly equal mass carry out the same upstairs climb; one takes half the time. Compared with the slower pupil, the faster one's power is…
a) Half  b) The same  c) Double — same work in half the time  d) Zero
**Answer: c) Double — same work in half the time**

**6.** In the guide's waterfall/dam example, what does the power plant's output measure?
a) The height of the dam only  b) The rate at which the falling water's potential energy is delivered  c) The volume of the reservoir only  d) The speed of the river before the dam
**Answer: b) The rate at which the falling water's potential energy is delivered**
