# Inside the nucleus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about the nucleus is correct?**
a) Summary and end-of-unit problems: resultants parallel and antiparallel, unit vectors from components, dot/cross…  b) Motion round a circle at constant speed: angular displacement, angular velocity and tangential (linear) speed are…  c) The atom's tiny dense core holds protons and neutrons (nucleons); notation A/Z X, isotopes as same-Z different-N family…
**Answer: c) The atom's tiny dense core holds protons and neutrons (nucleons); notation A/Z X, isotopes as same-Z…**

**2. The atom's tiny dense core holds protons and ______s (nucleons); notation A/Z X, isotopes as same-Z different-N family members, and nuclear radius growing with the cube root of A (R ≈ 1.2 fm × A^⅓).**
a) compared  b) resistance  c) neutron
**Answer: c) neutron**
