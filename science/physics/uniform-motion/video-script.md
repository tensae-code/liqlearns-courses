# Video script — Uniform motion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Uniform motion is motion at constant velocity in a straight line: equal displacements in equal times, again and again.
Nothing speeds up, nothing slows down, nothing turns.
It is the baseline case of motion — the calm pattern against which accelerated motion is compared.
Mark a uniformly moving body's position every second and the marks sit evenly spaced along the track.

Remember: **Uniform motion means constant velocity in a straight line.**
Remember: **Equal displacements happen in equal times.**
Remember: **Position marks every second sit evenly spaced.**
