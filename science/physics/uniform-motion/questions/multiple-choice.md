# Uniform motion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What makes motion uniform?
a) Constant velocity in a straight line.  b) Yes, uniform; velocity = 3 m/s.  c) No — its velocity is changing.  d) Evenly spaced.
**Answer: a) Constant velocity in a straight line.**

**2.** A body covers 6 m every 2 s steadily. Uniform? Its velocity?
a) Constant velocity in a straight line.  b) Yes, uniform; velocity = 3 m/s.  c) No — its velocity is changing.  d) Evenly spaced.
**Answer: b) Yes, uniform; velocity = 3 m/s.**

**3.** Is a car speeding up from a stop in uniform motion?
a) Constant velocity in a straight line.  b) Yes, uniform; velocity = 3 m/s.  c) No — its velocity is changing.  d) Evenly spaced.
**Answer: c) No — its velocity is changing.**

**4.** How do second-by-second position marks look for uniform motion?
a) Constant velocity in a straight line.  b) Yes, uniform; velocity = 3 m/s.  c) No — its velocity is changing.  d) Evenly spaced.
**Answer: d) Evenly spaced.**
