# Uniform motion — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** What makes motion uniform?
**Answer: Constant velocity in a straight line.**

**2.** A body covers 6 m every 2 s steadily. Uniform? Its velocity?
**Answer: Yes, uniform; velocity = 3 m/s.**

**3.** Is a car speeding up from a stop in uniform motion?
**Answer: No — its velocity is changing.**

**4.** How do second-by-second position marks look for uniform motion?
**Answer: Evenly spaced.**
