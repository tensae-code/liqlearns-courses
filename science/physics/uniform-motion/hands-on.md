# Hands-on — Uniform motion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Walk as steadily as you can while a partner claps each second; mark where each clap finds you. Even marks mean you nearly managed uniform motion.

## Apply it where you live
Find one near-uniform motion at home (a dripping tap's fall is not — a moving belt or escalator is) and justify it.
