# Lesson plan — How this Physics course works — the Iraqi Grade 11 way

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** How this Physics course works — the Iraqi Grade 11 way
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18) — a new lesson built for the Iraq placement named in lesson/sources.md
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Iraqi Grade 11 book's own way of teaching physics — the Eleventh edition, 2024 / 1446H, whose contents page (printed p. 4) evidences the bilingual chapter design; every lesson or chapter opens with a hands-on activity or a chapter vocabulary list (مفردات الفصل), runs through worked examples with bilingual Arabic–English technical terms printed side by side, and closes with a vocabulary/concept review plus short-answer and multiple-choice chapter tests
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Earlier lessons make a good preparation and are recommended, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The book's shape — 10 chapters** — The Grade 11 (Fifth Scientific) book runs in 10 chapters: vectors; motion; laws of motion; torque and equilibrium; work, power, energy and momentum; thermodynamics; circular and rotational motion; wave and vibration motion and sound; electric current; and magnetism.
2. **How every lesson is built — activity or vocabulary first, bilingual terms, worked examples** — In the Grade 11 book the bilingual presentation is at its strongest: Arabic text carries English chapter and section titles printed alongside throughout, so the learner reads every heading in both languages. Chapters open with their vocabulary (مفردات الفصل) or an opening experiment — Oersted's 1820 experiment opens the magnetism chapter — and worked examples carry the teaching.
3. **How every chapter closes — vocabulary review and a chapter test** — The Grade 11 book closes each chapter with its vocabulary/concept review, exercises and a chapter test of short-answer and multiple-choice questions; the whole book's Chapter 10 exercises run to the last page.
