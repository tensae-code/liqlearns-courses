# Maxwell’s equations — the laws that unite electricity, magnetism and light — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the four Maxwell laws in one line each.
**Answer: Gauss-electric (charges source E fields); Gauss-magnetic (no monopoles, magnetic flux always zero); Faraday (changing B makes E); Ampère–Maxwell (currents and changing E make B).**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Iron is ferromagnetic. What does that mean inside the material?
**Answer: Its atomic magnetic domains align strongly with an applied field and can stay aligned — the material magnetises strongly and can keep its magnetism.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How do the equations make a wave self-carrying in empty space?
**Answer: A changing electric field creates a magnetic field (Ampère–Maxwell) and a changing magnetic field creates an electric field (Faraday); each change breeds the next, so the wave propagates without charges or wires.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does the non-existence of monopoles matter to the shape of magnetic field lines?
**Answer: Because lines can never start or end on a pole, magnetic field lines must form closed loops, unlike electric lines which begin and end on charges.**
