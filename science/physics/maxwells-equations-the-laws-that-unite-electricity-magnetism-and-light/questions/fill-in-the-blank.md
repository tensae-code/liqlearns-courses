# Maxwell’s equations — the laws that unite electricity, magnetism and light — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Maxwell gathered the laws of electricity and magnetism into _____ equations.
**Answer: four**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) There are no magnetic _____ — cutting a magnet never isolates a pole.
**Answer: monopoles**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A changing magnetic field creates an electric field — that is _____ law inside the set.
**Answer: Faraday’s**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The equations predict that light itself is an _____ wave.
**Answer: electromagnetic**
