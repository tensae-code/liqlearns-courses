# Maxwell’s equations — the laws that unite electricity, magnetism and light — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How many equations make up Maxwell’s set?
**Answer: a) four  b) two  c) seven  d) one**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Gauss’s law for magnetism says the net magnetic flux out of a closed surface is…
**Answer: a) always zero — there are no magnetic monopoles  b) equal to the pole inside  c) doubled  d) negative**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Cutting a bar magnet in half gives…
**Answer: a) two smaller magnets, each with both poles  b) a separate north pole and south pole  c) unmagnetised iron  d) one pole only**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Faraday’s law, in Maxwell’s list, says a changing magnetic field creates…
**Answer: a) an electric field  b) a charge  c) a monopole  d) gravity**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The Ampère–Maxwell law adds that a changing _____ field also creates a magnetic field.
**Answer: a) electric  b) gravitational  c) sound  d) temperature**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Maxwell’s equations predict EM waves travelling at…
**Answer: a) exactly the measured speed of light — so light is an EM wave  b) the speed of sound  c) half light speed  d) no fixed speed**
