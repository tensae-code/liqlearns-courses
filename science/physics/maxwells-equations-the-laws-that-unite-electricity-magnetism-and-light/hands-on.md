# Hands-on — Maxwell’s equations — the laws that unite electricity, magnetism and light

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The uncuttable magnet
With a bar magnet and iron filings (or a compass), trace the field lines and note they form closed loops with no beginning or end. Try the thought experiment in writing: saw the magnet in half — draw the two pieces and their poles, and state which Maxwell law your drawing obeys. Finish by listing the four laws from memory, one line each.
