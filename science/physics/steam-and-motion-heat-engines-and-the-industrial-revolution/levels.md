# Steam and motion — heat engines and the industrial revolution — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner states in their own words how a heat engine works (heat in, part to work, rest thrown away cold), follows the steam engine's cycle, explains how steam power drove the industrial revolution, and states that no heat engine reaches 100% efficiency — and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has three sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — more engines to trace (a car engine, a power station turbine), harder judgements about which changes belong to steam power and which do not, and simple efficiency reckonings: work out ÷ heat in.
