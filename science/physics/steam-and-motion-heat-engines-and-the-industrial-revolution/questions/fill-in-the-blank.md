# Steam and motion — heat engines and the industrial revolution — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A heat engine turns part of a flow of heat into _____.
**Answer: work**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In the cylinder, expanding steam pushes the _____.
**Answer: piston**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The rod joins the piston to the _____, so the straight push becomes turning.
**Answer: flywheel**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The share of an engine's heat that becomes work is called its _____.
**Answer: efficiency**
