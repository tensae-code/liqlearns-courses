# Steam and motion — heat engines and the industrial revolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A heat engine is a machine that…
a) Makes heat from cold  b) Turns part of a flow of heat into work  c) Destroys heat completely  d) Stores heat forever
**Answer: b) Turns part of a flow of heat into work**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these must every heat engine have?
a) A cold side to throw waste heat away to  b) A river beside it  c) Sails  d) A horse
**Answer: a) A cold side to throw waste heat away to**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In a steam engine, what pushes the piston?
a) Cold water shrinking  b) Expanding steam  c) The flywheel pulling it  d) Wind
**Answer: b) Expanding steam**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) After pushing the piston, the steam is…
a) Thrown away as steam forever  b) Cooled and condensed back to water, and the cycle repeats  c) Turned into coal  d) Used to stop the engine
**Answer: b) Cooled and condensed back to water, and the cycle repeats**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Why did steam power let factories leave the riverbank?
a) Rivers became too warm  b) Work could now be made wherever coal could be carried  c) Workers preferred towns  d) Water power was banned
**Answer: b) Work could now be made wherever coal could be carried**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) An engine takes in 100 units of heat and does 30 units of work. Its efficiency is…
a) 130%  b) 100%  c) 30%, with 70 units thrown away as waste heat  d) 70%, with nothing wasted
**Answer: c) 30%, with 70 units thrown away as waste heat**
