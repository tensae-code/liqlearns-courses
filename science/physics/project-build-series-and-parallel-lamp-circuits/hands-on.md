# Hands-on — Project: build series and parallel lamp circuits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Strip two short wires carefully and make any complete one-battery-one-lamp loop first. The projects are this loop, arranged two ways.

## Apply it where you live
Complete both projects and present a comparison table: brightness, independence, current path, voltage sharing.
