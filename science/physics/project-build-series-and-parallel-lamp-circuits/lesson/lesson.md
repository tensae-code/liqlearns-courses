# Project: build series and parallel lamp circuits

## Do it first
Strip two short wires carefully and make any complete one-battery-one-lamp loop first. The projects are this loop, arranged two ways.

[Picture 21909 — both finished circuits lit, side by side]

## Project: build series and parallel lamp circuits — the teaching
Two builds make the resistor rules visible. Project 4.1 wires two lamps in series — strip the wires, join the loop, check with a multimeter: both lamps glow dimmer than one alone, and removing one kills both, because one path carries everything. Project 4.2 wires the same lamps in parallel: each has its own branch across the supply, both glow bright, and removing one leaves the other lit. Build both, measure both, and the series/parallel rules stop being arithmetic and become memory.

## The key ideas

- Series lamps: dimmer, and one removed kills both (one path).
- Parallel lamps: brighter and independent (own branches).
- Multimeter checks verify each build before power is trusted.

## Say it to your companion
Explain project: build series and parallel lamp circuits to your companion in your own words. Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
