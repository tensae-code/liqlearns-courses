# Project: build series and parallel lamp circuits — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** In the series build, why are the lamps dimmer?
**Answer: They share the supply voltage and the same current path — each gets only part of the voltage.**

**2.** Unscrew one parallel lamp. The other — ?
**Answer: Stays lit — its branch is complete without the first.**

**3.** What instrument checks the builds?
**Answer: A multimeter.**

**4.** Which build matches house wiring, and why?
**Answer: Parallel — each load gets full voltage and works independently.**
