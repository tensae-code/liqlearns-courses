# Light — a wave you can see — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** What is a ray?
**Answer: The straight-line path of light, drawn as a line with an arrow.**

**2.** What is c in vacuum, approximately?
**Answer: 3.00×10⁸ m/s.**

**3.** Does light travel faster or slower in glass than in vacuum?
**Answer: Slower.**

**4.** How long does light take to travel 9.0×10⁸ m in vacuum?
**Answer: t = 9.0×10⁸ ÷ 3.00×10⁸ = 3 s.**
