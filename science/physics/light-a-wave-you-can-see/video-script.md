Visible light is the one EM band our eyes detect.
It travels through vacuum as happily as through air, water or glass, and in a uniform medium it travels in straight lines — the ray model, where a ray is the path of light drawn as a straight line with an arrow.
Its speed in vacuum is c ≈ 3.00×10⁸ m/s — exactly 2.99792458×10⁸ m/s — and it slows in materials, the fact refraction is built on.

Remember: **Light is the visible band of the EM spectrum.**
Remember: **In a uniform medium light travels in straight lines (the ray model).**
Remember: **c ≈ 3.00×10⁸ m/s in vacuum; light slows in materials.**
