# Hands-on — Light — a wave you can see

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
In a dusty or smoky room, watch a sunbeam or torch beam hold its straight edge. You are seeing the ray model with your own eyes.

## Apply it where you live
Calculate how far light travels in one minute in vacuum, showing working.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21929 — ray from a candle to an eye, straight-line path drawn] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using c ≈ 3.00×10⁸ m/s: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
