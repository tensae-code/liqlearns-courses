# Practice — synthesising forces experimentally — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In this experiment the two spring balances give…
**Answer: a) two real forces with measured sizes and directions, pulling one ring  b) the resultant directly  c) only the angle between the forces  d) the ring's weight**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The traced lines on the paper record…
**Answer: a) the directions of the two pulls  b) the ring's path  c) the balances' calibration  d) the table's slope**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The parallelogram's diagonal gives…
**Answer: a) the predicted resultant — the single force that should replace both  b) the sum of the two readings in every case  c) the friction force  d) the difference of the forces**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A drawing at the scale 1 cm = 1 N has F₁ as a 3.2 cm arrow. F₁ is…
**Answer: a) 3.2 N — the scale converts length back to force  b) 1 N  c) 3.2 cm of force  d) 32 N**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The check uses a single balance to…
**Answer: a) hold the ring in the same place the pair held it, reading the resultant directly  b) weigh the ring  c) zero the other balances  d) stretch the cords**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A small mismatch between diagonal and single balance is handled honestly by…
**Answer: a) naming the error sources — zero errors, cord friction, the ring's weight  b) rubbing out the diagonal  c) repeating until the numbers match exactly  d) ignoring it**
