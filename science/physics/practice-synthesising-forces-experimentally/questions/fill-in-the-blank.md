# Practice — synthesising forces experimentally — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The two balances pull the ring at a measured _____ to each other.
**Answer: angle**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The _____ of the parallelogram predicts the resultant force.
**Answer: diagonal**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Forces are drawn to a stated _____, such as 1 cm = 1 N.
**Answer: scale**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) When the single balance agrees with the diagonal within error, the parallelogram rule is _____, not just assumed.
**Answer: verified**
