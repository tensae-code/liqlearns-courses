Today: **the parallelogram rule, tested on the bench**. Two spring balances pull one ring at an angle; draw them to scale, complete the parallelogram, and its diagonal predicts the single force that replaces both. Then one balance holds the ring in the same place — and its reading matches the diagonal. A rule you have verified is a rule you own.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
