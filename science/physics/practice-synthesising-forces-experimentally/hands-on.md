# Hands-on — Practice — synthesising forces experimentally

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two strings, one book, one prediction
Loop two strings around a heavy book on a smooth table. Pull both strings gently at about 60° to each other and watch the book's slide direction — mark it with a chalk line or a ruler laid along the slide. Now use one string only and hunt for the single pull direction that slides the book along the same line. Compare: the single direction lies between the two pulls, closer to the stronger one if your pulls differed. You have just done qualitatively what the balances do with numbers.
