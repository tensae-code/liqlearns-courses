Today: **how this physics course works — the uganda competency-based way**.

Uganda's new Physics course is built on competences, not coverage. Learners do activities first and practise thinking, communicating and solving problems inside every topic. The teacher plans backwards — outcome first, then activity, then evidence — checks learning while it happens, that is formative assessment, and certifies it at the end, summative assessment, using rubrics that show what quality looks like.
