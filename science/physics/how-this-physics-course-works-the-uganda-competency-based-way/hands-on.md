# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Uganda competency-based way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Outcome first
Take any small topic and write one sentence beginning "By the end, the learner can…". Then list one activity that would build exactly that ability and one question that would prove it. Say aloud: plan backwards — outcome, activity, evidence.

## Rubric in four lines
For the same topic, write a tiny rubric: three levels (not yet, meeting, exceeding) with one observable sign for each. Swap with a companion and mark a sample answer with it. Say aloud: a rubric makes quality visible before the work is judged.
