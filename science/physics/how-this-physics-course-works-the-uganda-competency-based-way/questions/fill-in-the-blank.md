# How this Physics course works — the Uganda competency-based way — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Skills such as communication and problem-solving that run across all subjects are called _____ skills.
**Answer: generic**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Planning that starts from the learning outcome works _____ from what the learner must be able to do.
**Answer: backwards**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Assessment during learning that steers teaching is called _____ assessment.
**Answer: formative**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Assessment at the end of a stretch that certifies achievement is called _____ assessment.
**Answer: summative**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Sample _____ in the guide show markers what each level of performance looks like.
**Answer: rubrics**
