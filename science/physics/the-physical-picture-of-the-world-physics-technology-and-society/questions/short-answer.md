# The physical picture of the world — physics, technology and society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why must a 'physical picture of the world' remain revisable?
**Answer: Because its authority is experiment: when finer measurement disagrees with the picture — as quanta disagreed with the clockwork picture — the picture must change. A picture that cannot be corrected has left physics.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the two-way chain between physics and technology in one example each way.
**Answer: Physics → technology: electromagnetism → generators and the grid. Technology → physics: the telescope (an optical product) → modern astronomy and the expanding-Universe picture; or accelerators → particle physics.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Name two modern Uzbek physics works the chapter points to, and one historical root.
**Answer: Modern: solar/heliotechnical work (the solar furnace tradition on its sunny plateau) and nuclear research at its institutes (accept also university physics). Historical root: Beruni and the Central Asian scholars with whom the series opened.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) What responsibilities ride with the picture's powers, as the chapter frames them?
**Answer: Safety (radiation, power), environment (what generation and industry cost the land and air) and access (who the benefits reach) — because society chooses and funds what gets built, the choices are moral as well as technical.**
