# The physical picture of the world — physics, technology and society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The physical picture describes the world as…
**Answer: a) matter in motion, interacting through fields, under laws  b) a machine wound once forever  c) four elements at rest  d) pure mathematics**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is the picture called a picture and not the final truth?
**Answer: a) Experiment keeps correcting it — it is the best current map  b) It is drawn with pencils  c) Physicists dislike truth  d) It changes daily**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Instruments like the telescope and accelerator show…
**Answer: a) technology feeding physics back, opening the next picture  b) physics finished  c) society stopping physics  d) laws changing yearly**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Society's place in the loop is…
**Answer: a) choosing, funding and using what gets built — with responsibilities attached  b) none — physics is separate  c) only to watch  d) to forbid new pictures**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The book's closing claim about Uzbekistan is…
**Answer: a) physics is one of its oldest trades — from Beruni to its modern institutes  b) physics arrived last year  c) Uzbekistan has no physics  d) the course ends abroad**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Uzbekistan's sunny plateau hosts work in…
