# Hands-on — The physical picture of the world — physics, technology and society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Your picture, one page
Draw the whole course as one map on a single page: matter (particles → atoms → bodies) on one side, fields and waves on the other, and arrows for three technologies you used today, each labelled with the physics it runs on. Under it write the Uzbekistan line: one place in your country where that physics is worked professionally. If your map fits a page, you hold the picture — keep it for the next course.
