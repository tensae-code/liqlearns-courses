# Electrical energy consumed — the meter, E = P·t and the electricity bill — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The energy an appliance consumes is worked out as…
a) E = P ÷ t  b) E = P·t  c) E = P + t  d) E = t ÷ P
**Answer: b) E = P·t**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A 2 kW appliance runs for 3 hours. The energy consumed is…
a) 5 kWh  b) 6 kWh  c) 0.67 kWh  d) 6 joules
**Answer: b) 6 kWh**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) An electricity meter counts a home's consumption in…
a) Watts  b) Amperes  c) Kilowatt-hours — the “units”  d) Joules per second
**Answer: c) Kilowatt-hours — the “units”**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Consumption between two meter readings is found by…
a) Adding the readings  b) Subtracting the earlier reading from the later one  c) Multiplying the readings  d) Reading the meter once
**Answer: b) Subtracting the earlier reading from the later one**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The energy charge on a bill is mainly…
a) The power of the biggest appliance  b) The number of units × the price per unit  c) A fixed sum that never changes  d) The meter reading itself
**Answer: b) The number of units × the price per unit**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A 0.5 kW appliance used 10 hours a week and a 2 kW appliance used 1 hour a week: which consumes more energy in the week?
a) The 2 kW appliance  b) The same  c) The 0.5 kW appliance (5 kWh against 2 kWh)  d) It cannot be worked out
**Answer: c) The 0.5 kW appliance (5 kWh against 2 kWh)**
