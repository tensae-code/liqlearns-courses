# Electrical energy consumed — the meter, E = P·t and the electricity bill — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Energy consumed = power × _____.
**Answer: time**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The practical unit of household electrical energy is the _____-hour.
**Answer: kilowatt**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The meter's counts of kilowatt-hours are called the bill's _____.
**Answer: units**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Energy charge = number of units × _____ per unit.
**Answer: price**
