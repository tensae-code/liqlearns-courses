# Hands-on — Electrical energy consumed — the meter, E = P·t and the electricity bill

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Audit one room, then read the meter
With an adult's permission, list every electrical appliance in one room of your home with the power printed on its rating plate, and estimate how many hours each one runs in a day. Work out each one's daily consumption with E = P·t (power in kilowatts) and add them up for the room's daily total in kilowatt-hours. Then, together with an adult, read the home's electricity meter at the same time on two days running: subtract, and compare the home's real daily units with your one-room estimate. Say in one sentence where the rest of the home's energy went, and which single appliance on your list you would cut first to save money — and whether your cut works on its power or its time.
