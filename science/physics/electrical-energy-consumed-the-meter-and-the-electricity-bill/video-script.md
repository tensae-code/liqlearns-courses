# Video script — Electrical energy consumed — the meter, E = P·t and the electricity bill

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **following electricity all the way to the bill**. An appliance's power tells you how fast it eats energy. How much it eats is power times time: E = P times t. Count it in kilowatts and hours and you get kilowatt-hours — the units on your bill. The meter on the wall counts those units for the whole home, day and night, appliance by appliance. The bill simply multiplies units by the price per unit. So the costly things are the powerful ones and the ones left running — and that is exactly where the saving is.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
