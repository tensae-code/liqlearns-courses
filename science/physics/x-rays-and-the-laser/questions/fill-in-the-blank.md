# X-rays and the laser — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) X-rays are _____ when fast electrons strike a metal target.
**Answer: produced**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) LASER stands for Light Amplification by _____ Emission of Radiation.
**Answer: Stimulated**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Laser waves all travel in step — the light is _____.
**Answer: coherent**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) X-rays are _____ radiation, so exposure dose is kept low.
**Answer: ionising**
