# X-rays and the laser — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) X-rays are produced when…
a) A laser warms a gas  b) Fast electrons strike a metal target in an X-ray tube  c) Atoms absorb light  d) A prism splits sunlight
**Answer: b) Fast electrons strike a metal target in an X-ray tube**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In a medical X-ray image, bones appear because they…
a) Emit X-rays  b) Absorb X-rays more strongly than soft tissue  c) Reflect visible light  d) Are hotter
**Answer: b) Absorb X-rays more strongly than soft tissue**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Stimulated emission produces a second photon that is…
a) Random in colour  b) An exact copy — same energy, direction and phase  c) Slower  d) Absorbed at once
**Answer: b) An exact copy — same energy, direction and phase**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Population inversion means…
a) More atoms in the ground state  b) No excited atoms  c) More atoms in the excited state than the ground state  d) Atoms changing element
**Answer: c) More atoms in the excited state than the ground state**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Laser light differs from torch light in being…
a) Coherent, monochromatic and collimated  b) Hotter always  c) Visible only  d) Made of different particles
**Answer: a) Coherent, monochromatic and collimated**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The main radiation caution with X-rays is that they are…
a) Loud  b) Ionising, so dose must be kept low and shielded  c) Cold  d) Magnetic
**Answer: b) Ionising, so dose must be kept low and shielded**
