# Video script — X-rays and the laser

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **two machines that weaponise the atom's light — kindly**. Slam fast electrons into a metal target and out come X-rays: wavelengths so short they pass through flesh and stop at bone, letting us photograph the inside of a body. Coax excited atoms differently — let one photon stimulate them to release its exact twin, again and again — and you get laser light: one colour, one direction, every wave in step, a beam that can cut steel or carry a concert down a glass fibre. Penetration and precision: the pair that runs modern medicine and modern light.
