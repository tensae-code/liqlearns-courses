# Hands-on — X-rays and the laser

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spread is the difference
In a dark room, shine a torch and then a laser pointer (borrowed, used with an adult, never pointed at any face or eye — aim only at a wall) at a wall one metre away, then three metres away. Measure both spots each time. The torch spot grows and fades with distance; the laser spot barely changes. Say aloud: same light, different organisation — in step, one way, one colour.
