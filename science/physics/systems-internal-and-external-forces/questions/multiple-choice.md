# Systems — internal and external forces — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A 'system' in this lesson is…
a) A machine  b) The bodies chosen, inside a boundary the reasoner draws and holds fixed  c) Everything in the universe  d) One body only, always
**Answer: b) The bodies chosen, inside a boundary the reasoner draws and holds fixed**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The same force can be internal for one choice and external for another because…
a) Forces change size  b) It depends on where the system boundary was drawn  c) Of the time of day  d) It cannot — a force has one fixed kind
**Answer: b) It depends on where the system boundary was drawn**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Internal forces cannot change a system's total momentum because…
a) They are small  b) They act for no time  c) They occur in equal-and-opposite third-law pairs inside the boundary and cancel in the total  d) They are frictionless
**Answer: c) They occur in equal-and-opposite third-law pairs inside the boundary and cancel in the total**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Internal forces CAN change a system's…
a) Total momentum  b) Kinetic energy — as in a collision losing energy to heat and deformation  c) Mass  d) Boundary
**Answer: b) Kinetic energy — as in a collision losing energy to heat and deformation**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A system's total momentum stays constant when…
a) It is large  b) The net external impulse is zero (or negligible during the event)  c) The bodies are equal  d) The collision is elastic
**Answer: b) The net external impulse is zero (or negligible during the event)**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The change in a system's total momentum equals…
a) The internal impulse  b) The net external impulse — external force × time  c) Zero always  d) The kinetic energy change
**Answer: b) The net external impulse — external force × time**
