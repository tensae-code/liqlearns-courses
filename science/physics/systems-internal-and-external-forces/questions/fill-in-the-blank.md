# Systems — internal and external forces — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Every momentum claim is made relative to a chosen _____.
**Answer: system**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Forces between parts of the system are called _____ forces.
**Answer: internal**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Internal forces occur in equal-and-opposite pairs by Newton's _____ law.
**Answer: third**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Total momentum changes only through the net _____ impulse.
**Answer: external**
