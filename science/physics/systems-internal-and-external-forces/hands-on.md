# Hands-on — Systems — internal and external forces

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Push off in pairs
You need: two chairs on wheels or two skateboards (or two trolleys on a track), and a partner. Sit facing each other, feet against your partner's feet, and push off together — both of you glide backwards though no outside push acted on the pair. Roll back and try pushing with unequal effort: the pair's centre stays where it was. Now have a third person push one chair from outside during the push-off and watch the pair's centre drift. Say aloud: inside pushes moved us apart; only the outside push moved the pair.
