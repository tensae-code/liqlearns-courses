# Video script — Systems — internal and external forces

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **draw the boundary first**. Two trolleys crash. Is 'momentum conserved' true? It depends — on what you chose as your system. Greece's book teaches the choice before the law. Forces between the parts inside your boundary are internal: they come in equal opposite pairs, they cancel in the total, they can shuffle momentum between the parts and waste energy as heat — but they can never change the whole system's momentum. Forces from outside are external, and their impulse is exactly how much the total changes. No net outside impulse — the total stands. The boundary is not bookkeeping. It is the physics.
