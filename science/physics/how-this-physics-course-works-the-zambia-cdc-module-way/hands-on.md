# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Zambia CDC module way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build a moment tool
Like the module's own activity designs: make a simple tool that uses a moment — a lever from a ruler, a pencil as a pivot and a small load. Press the long arm and lift the load. Say aloud where the pivot is, where the effort acts, and why the long arm makes the lift easier.

## Plan a term like the module
Take one topic you know. On one page, write its scope and sequence the module way: the sub-topics in order, one activity learners build for each, one formative check for each, and the unit test, project or practical assessment that closes it.

## Formative check, on yourself
After each activity above, stop and check formatively: write one sentence on what landed and one on what did not — the module's rhythm, used on your own learning.
