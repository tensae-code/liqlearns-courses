# How this Physics course works — the Zambia CDC module way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What kind of book is the Zambia CDC Physics module?
a) A student textbook  b) A Teaching Module written for teachers  c) An exam paper  d) A story book
**Answer: b) A Teaching Module written for teachers**

**2.** How much of the syllabus does one term module carry?
a) The whole year's syllabus  b) Two topics  c) One syllabus topic  d) Half a sub-topic
**Answer: c) One syllabus topic**

**3.** Which topic does the Form 1 Term 3 module carry?
a) Topic 2 — Waves  b) Topic 5 — Mechanics 1  c) Topic 1 — Measurement  d) Topic 4 — Sound
**Answer: b) Topic 5 — Mechanics 1**

**4.** What does the module's front matter lay out first?
a) The exam answers  b) The scope and sequence — what is taught, in what order  c) A story  d) The price list
**Answer: b) The scope and sequence — what is taught, in what order**

**5.** Why is equilibrium not covered in the Form 1 Term 3 module?
a) It is too hard  b) It was covered in module 1  c) It is not physics  d) The pages were lost
**Answer: b) It was covered in module 1**

**6.** Which of these is one of the module's activity designs?
a) Copying notes silently  b) Designing tools that use moments  c) Memorising the contents page  d) Watching only
**Answer: b) Designing tools that use moments**
