# How this Physics course works — the Zambia CDC module way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the six sub-topics of Topic 5 in the Form 1 Term 3 module, in order.
**Answer: Scalar and vector quantities; linear motion; forces; circular motion; moment of a force — with equilibrium expressly excluded, as it was covered in module 1. (Five taught sub-topics plus the excluded one make the module's six named stops.)**

**2.** What is the difference between the module's formative checks and its unit tests?
**Answer: Formative checks are small checks during learning, showing what has landed while there is still time to fix it; unit tests belong to the summative side, gathering and judging the term's learning at the close, alongside projects and practical assessments.**

**3.** Who is the CDC module written for, and what does it give them?
**Answer: It is written for teachers. It gives them the scope and sequence, the learning-activity designs, worked teaching notes, and the formative and summative assessment guidance for one term's topic.**

**4.** Why does the module teach one topic per term-book instead of spreading topics across the year?
**Answer: So the one topic is taught whole in its term — nothing spread thin, and nothing already taught (like equilibrium in module 1) taught again.**
