# How this Physics course works — the Zambia CDC module way — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** The module opens with a scope and _____ that lays out what is taught, in what order.
**Answer: sequence**

**2.** Small checks during learning, while there is still time to fix what has not landed, are called _____ checks.
**Answer: formative**

**3.** Learners design tools that use _____ — learning the principle by building a tool that works by it.
**Answer: moments**

**4.** The module's summative side includes unit tests, projects and _____ assessments.
**Answer: practical**
