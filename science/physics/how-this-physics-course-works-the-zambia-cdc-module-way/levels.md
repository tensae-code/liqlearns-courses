# How this Physics course works — the Zambia CDC module way — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner states the lesson's key ideas in their own words, gives the module's shape — one topic per term-book, scope and sequence first, activities learners build, assessment designed in — and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to work, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling a teacher module apart from a student textbook, and formative checks apart from unit tests — before the learner moves on.
