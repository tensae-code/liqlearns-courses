## Density and specific gravity — the teaching
Density is mass per unit volume, ρ = m/V — a characteristic property of a substance: same substance, same density, whatever the sample size. It decides floating: a rock (denser than water) sinks; wood and oil (less dense) float. Specific gravity is density measured against water at 4 °C — a pure ratio with no unit. The same ratio thinking scales up: the book even turns it on the Earth itself, computing a whole planet's density from its mass and volume.

**Keep the formula:** ρ = m / V

## The key ideas

- Density ρ = m/V, a characteristic property of a substance.
- Denser than water sinks; less dense floats.
- Specific gravity = density ÷ density of water at 4 °C (unitless).
