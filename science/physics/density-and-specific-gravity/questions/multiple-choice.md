# Density and specific gravity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A block has mass 240 g and volume 80 cm³. Density?
a) ρ = 240 ÷ 80 = 3 g/cm³.  b) No — it is denser than water, so it sinks.  c) 0.8 ÷ 1.0 = 0.8 (no unit).  d) No — mass and volume both double; the ratio stays.
**Answer: a) ρ = 240 ÷ 80 = 3 g/cm³.**

**2.** Will that block float on water (1 g/cm³)?
a) ρ = 240 ÷ 80 = 3 g/cm³.  b) No — it is denser than water, so it sinks.  c) 0.8 ÷ 1.0 = 0.8 (no unit).  d) No — mass and volume both double; the ratio stays.
**Answer: b) No — it is denser than water, so it sinks.**

**3.** A substance has density 0.8 g/cm³. Its specific gravity?
a) ρ = 240 ÷ 80 = 3 g/cm³.  b) No — it is denser than water, so it sinks.  c) 0.8 ÷ 1.0 = 0.8 (no unit).  d) No — mass and volume both double; the ratio stays.
**Answer: c) 0.8 ÷ 1.0 = 0.8 (no unit).**

**4.** Does doubling the sample change a substance's density?
a) ρ = 240 ÷ 80 = 3 g/cm³.  b) No — it is denser than water, so it sinks.  c) 0.8 ÷ 1.0 = 0.8 (no unit).  d) No — mass and volume both double; the ratio stays.
**Answer: d) No — mass and volume both double; the ratio stays.**
