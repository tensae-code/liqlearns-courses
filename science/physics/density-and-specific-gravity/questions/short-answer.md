# Density and specific gravity — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A block has mass 240 g and volume 80 cm³. Density?
**Answer: ρ = 240 ÷ 80 = 3 g/cm³.**

**2.** Will that block float on water (1 g/cm³)?
**Answer: No — it is denser than water, so it sinks.**

**3.** A substance has density 0.8 g/cm³. Its specific gravity?
**Answer: 0.8 ÷ 1.0 = 0.8 (no unit).**

**4.** Does doubling the sample change a substance's density?
**Answer: No — mass and volume both double; the ratio stays.**
