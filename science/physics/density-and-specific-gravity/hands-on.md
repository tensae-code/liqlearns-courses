# Hands-on — Density and specific gravity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Drop a stone, a piece of wood and a drop of oil into water — predict first, from density, then watch the tank give the verdict.

## Apply it where you live
Measure a stone's mass and volume (by overflow) and compute its density and specific gravity.

## Read the picture

## Calculate by hand
Using ρ = m / V: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
