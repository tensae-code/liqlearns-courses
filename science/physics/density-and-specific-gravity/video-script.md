Density is mass per unit volume, ρ = m/V — a characteristic property of a substance: same substance, same density, whatever the sample size.
It decides floating: a rock (denser than water) sinks; wood and oil (less dense) float.
Specific gravity is density measured against water at 4 °C — a pure ratio with no unit.
The same ratio thinking scales up: the book even turns it on the Earth itself, computing a whole planet's density from its mass and volume.

Remember: **Density ρ = m/V, a characteristic property of a substance.**
Remember: **Denser than water sinks; less dense floats.**
Remember: **Specific gravity = density ÷ density of water at 4 °C (unitless).**
Keep the formula: **ρ = m / V**
