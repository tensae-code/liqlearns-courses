# Teaching physics the Kurikulum Merdeka way — schemes, misconception checks and LKPD — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The per-chapter learning scheme is the Skema _____.
**Answer: Pembelajaran**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The guide's word for a listed misconception is _____.
**Answer: miskonsepsi**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The worksheets that carry the learners' activity work are called _____.
**Answer: LKPD**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Routes for learners the first route missed are called _____ learning.
**Answer: alternative**
