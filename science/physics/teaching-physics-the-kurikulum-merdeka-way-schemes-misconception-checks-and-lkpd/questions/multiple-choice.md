# Teaching physics the Kurikulum Merdeka way — schemes, misconception checks and LKPD — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A Skema Pembelajaran lays out, for a whole chapter…
a) Only the examination questions  b) The meetings, the hours and the strategy for each meeting  c) The textbook's prices  d) The parents' names
**Answer: b) The meetings, the hours and the strategy for each meeting**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The scheme is written…
a) After the chapter is taught  b) During the final meeting  c) Before teaching starts, so the chapter is planned whole  d) By the learners
**Answer: c) Before teaching starts, so the chapter is planned whole**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) “Miskonsepsi” in the guide are…
a) Spelling mistakes  b) The misconceptions learners commonly carry into a topic, listed per chapter  c) Apparatus faults  d) Examination grades
**Answer: b) The misconceptions learners commonly carry into a topic, listed per chapter**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Teaching planned against a misconception first…
a) Hides it  b) Surfaces it — learners commit to an answer before teaching  c) Tests it only at the year end  d) Ignores it
**Answer: b) Surfaces it — learners commit to an answer before teaching**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) LKPD are…
a) Student worksheets that structure each activity — what to do, record and conclude  b) Parents' letters  c) Examination papers  d) Apparatus catalogues
**Answer: a) Student worksheets that structure each activity — what to do, record and conclude**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A chapter's teaching closes with…
a) Nothing  b) Teacher reflection, and alternative-learning routes for learners the first route missed  c) A new scheme for a different subject  d) The parents' meeting
**Answer: b) Teacher reflection, and alternative-learning routes for learners the first route missed**
