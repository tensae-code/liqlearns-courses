# Teaching physics the Kurikulum Merdeka way — schemes, misconception checks and LKPD — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why plan the chapter whole before teaching any of it?
**Answer: So the teacher knows in advance what later meetings must be ready to do, what apparatus to book and where the checks fall — learners meet one planned journey instead of a sequence of surprises.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why must a misconception be named before it can be taught against?
**Answer: An unnamed misconception survives the chapter untouched — learners fit the new teaching around it. Named, it can be surfaced, argued against by the activity's evidence, and checked at the end.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What three jobs does an LKPD do in an activity?
**Answer: It tells the learner what to do, gives them the structure for what to record, and poses the question they must conclude from — so the planned investigation is the one actually carried out.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) What does teacher reflection add that a scheme alone cannot?
**Answer: Judgement from the taught chapter: what actually happened, who the first route missed, and what to change — feeding alternative routes now and a better scheme next time.**
