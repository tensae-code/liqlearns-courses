# Hands-on — Teaching physics the Kurikulum Merdeka way — schemes, misconception checks and LKPD

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Plan one chapter like the guide
Take a topic and a blank page divided in three. Top: the scheme — three meetings, their jobs and strategies. Middle: the misconception list — two wrong ideas, each with the activity whose evidence argues against it. Bottom: the LKPD front — aim, recording table, concluding question — plus one parent-interaction line. Teach a partner from your page for two minutes; if they can say where the chapter is going and what wrong idea it is hunting, the plan holds.
