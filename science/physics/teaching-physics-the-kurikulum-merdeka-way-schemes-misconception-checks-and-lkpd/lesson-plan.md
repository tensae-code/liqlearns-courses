# Lesson plan — Teaching physics the Kurikulum Merdeka way — schemes, misconception checks and LKPD

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** Teaching physics the Kurikulum Merdeka way — schemes, misconception checks and LKPD
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18) — a new lesson built for the Indonesia placement named in lesson/sources.md
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Indonesian teacher-guide apparatus for teaching physics the Kurikulum Merdeka way — every chapter planned whole in a **Skema Pembelajaran** (meetings, hours, strategies), its common **misconceptions (miskonsepsi)** listed and taught against, and the work carried on **LKPD** worksheets, with parent interaction, alternative-learning routes and teacher reflection around it — teacher-guide evidence only
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Earlier lessons make a good preparation and are recommended, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The scheme first — Skema Pembelajaran** — Before a chapter is taught, its learning scheme is laid out whole: the meetings, the hours, the strategies for each meeting — so the teacher walks in holding the chapter as one planned journey, not a pile of pages.
2. **Misconception checks — teach against the named wrong idea** — Each chapter's guide apparatus lists the misconceptions (miskonsepsi) learners commonly carry for that topic, and the teaching is planned against them: surface the wrong idea, let the activity argue with it, then check it has moved.
3. **LKPD and the people around the lesson — worksheets, parents, reflection** — Learning is carried on LKPD worksheets that structure each activity; the guide adds parent-interaction notes that bring the home into the chapter, alternative-learning routes for learners the first route misses, and a teacher reflection that closes the chapter and feeds the next one.

## If the learner is stuck
- Cannot start → return to Do it first and do the opening activity with real objects or examples before reading any explanation; the explanation is the story of that activity.
- Mixes the sub lessons up → re-read the key ideas in order; each key idea is one sub lesson compressed to a line.
