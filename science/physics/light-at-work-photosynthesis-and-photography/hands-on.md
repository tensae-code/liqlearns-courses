# Hands-on — Light at work — photosynthesis and photography

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Light writes on a leaf
Cover part of a living leaf with a small opaque strip (foil, tape) for two sunny days, leaving the rest exposed. Then (with a teacher) test the whole leaf for starch with iodine after boiling the leaf in water and alcohol: the covered part stays pale, the lit part turns blue-black. Light wrote the difference — photosynthesis only where photons fell. Record it as the book's claim, proved.
