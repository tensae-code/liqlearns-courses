# Light at work — photosynthesis and photography — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A photon's energy is E = _____f.
**Answer: h**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The green pigment that catches light in the leaf is _____.
**Answer: chlorophyll**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Photosynthesis releases _____ as its by-product.
**Answer: oxygen**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Film crystals struck by light are silver _____ crystals.
**Answer: halide**
