# Light at work — photosynthesis and photography — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is meant by calling photosynthesis 'stored sunlight'?
**Answer: The light's energy is not lost: it is banked in the chemical bonds of sugar, and released later when the sugar is eaten and respired — food is sunlight in storage.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does photosynthesis stop in darkness though water and CO₂ remain?
**Answer: Its driving energy is light itself; without photons the reaction has no energy source at ordinary temperatures — the raw materials alone cannot run it.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Follow one bright part of a scene through film photography.
**Answer: Bright scene → many photons strike that film area → many silver specks form in the latent image → developer builds them into dense black silver — so the negative is dark exactly where the scene was bright; printing reverses it back.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Name the modern electronic heir of the film process named in the lesson, and its shared principle.
**Answer: The photodiode/phone sensor: it too counts photons place by place and turns the count into an image — the same 'light writes place by place' principle, electronically instead of chemically.**
