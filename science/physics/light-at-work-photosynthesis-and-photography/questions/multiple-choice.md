# Light at work — photosynthesis and photography — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Light drives chemical change because…
**Answer: a) its photons carry energy that can drive reactions  b) it is warm like fire  c) it pushes molecules mechanically  d) darkness poisons reactions**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The two great examples the book teaches are…
**Answer: a) photosynthesis and photography  b) rusting and burning  c) melting and boiling  d) echoes and shadows**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Photosynthesis stores light energy as…
**Answer: a) sugar (chemical energy in food)  b) heat in the soil  c) electricity in roots  d) light in the leaf forever**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The oxygen released in photosynthesis comes from…
**Answer: a) the splitting of water  b) the air's CO₂  c) the soil  d) the chlorophyll itself**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The latent image on film is…
**Answer: a) invisible specks of silver where photons struck  b) the finished photograph  c) a pattern in the lens  d) made by the fixer**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The fixer's job is to…
**Answer: a) dissolve unstruck crystals so the image stops changing  b) darken the whole film  c) develop the image  d) reverse the negative**
