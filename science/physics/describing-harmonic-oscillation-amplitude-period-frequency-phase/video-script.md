# Video script — Describing harmonic oscillation — amplitude, period, frequency, phase

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the four numbers that describe any oscillation**. Amplitude — how far from the centre it gets. Period — seconds per cycle; frequency — cycles per second, its inverse, and ω = 2πf for the equations. Phase — where in the cycle it has got to, carried inside x = A cos(ωt + φ). Size from the start, rhythm from the apparatus, position from the phase.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
