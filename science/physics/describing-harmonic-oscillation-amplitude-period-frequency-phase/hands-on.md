# Hands-on — Describing harmonic oscillation — amplitude, period, frequency, phase

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The rhythm does not care about the size
Hang a pendulum about 50 cm long. Time 10 full swings starting from a small pull-back (about 10 cm sideways); divide by 10 for the period. Repeat from a bigger pull-back (about 20 cm). The periods should agree closely — you have measured that amplitude does not set the rhythm. Now shorten the string to about 25 cm and repeat: the period drops (by about √2). Length sets rhythm; launch sets size. Record all three periods and the frequency f = 1/T for each.
