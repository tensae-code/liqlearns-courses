# Describing harmonic oscillation — amplitude, period, frequency, phase — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The greatest displacement from equilibrium is the _____.
**Answer: amplitude**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Frequency is measured in _____, cycles per second.
**Answer: hertz**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Angular frequency ω equals 2π times the _____.
**Answer: frequency**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Oscillators moving identically, cycle for cycle, are said to be in _____.
**Answer: phase**
