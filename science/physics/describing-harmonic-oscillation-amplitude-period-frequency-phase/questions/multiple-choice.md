# Describing harmonic oscillation — amplitude, period, frequency, phase — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The amplitude of an oscillation is…
**Answer: a) the greatest displacement from the equilibrium position  b) the full travel from one extreme to the other  c) the time of one cycle  d) the number of cycles per second**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A pendulum's amplitude is set by…
**Answer: a) how far it was pulled back at the start  b) the length of the string  c) the mass of the bob  d) gravity alone**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) An oscillation with period 0.25 s has frequency…
**Answer: a) 4.0 Hz — f = 1/T = 1 ÷ 0.25  b) 0.25 Hz  c) 2.5 Hz  d) 25 Hz**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The angular frequency ω of a 2.0 Hz oscillator is…
**Answer: a) 4π rad/s — ω = 2πf = 2π × 2.0  b) 2π rad/s  c) 2.0 rad/s  d) 0.50 rad/s**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In x = A cos(ωt + φ), the quantity (ωt + φ) is called the…
**Answer: a) phase  b) amplitude  c) frequency  d) displacement**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Two oscillators in antiphase differ in phase by…
**Answer: a) half a cycle (π radians)  b) a quarter cycle  c) one full cycle  d) two cycles**
