# Charged particles circling in a uniform magnetic field — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Lorentz force on a particle moving across a uniform field is always…
**Answer: a) perpendicular to its velocity  b) along its velocity  c) opposite its velocity  d) zero**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Because the force is perpendicular to the motion, the particle's…
**Answer: a) speed stays constant — the force does no work  b) speed grows  c) speed falls  d) mass changes**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The circle's radius is…
**Answer: a) r = mv/qB  b) r = qB/mv  c) r = mvqB  d) r = B/mv**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A faster particle in the same field circles…
**Answer: a) wider — r grows with v  b) tighter  c) in the same radius  d) not at all**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The lap time T = 2πm/qB…
**Answer: a) does not depend on the speed  b) doubles with speed  c) halves with speed  d) depends on the radius only**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A particle fired along the field lines feels…
**Answer: a) no force — it travels straight  b) the maximum force  c) a circling force  d) friction**
