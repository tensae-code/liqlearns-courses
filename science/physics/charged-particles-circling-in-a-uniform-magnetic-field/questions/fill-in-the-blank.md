# Charged particles circling in a uniform magnetic field — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Lorentz force acts as the _____ force for the circle.
**Answer: centripetal**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Radius r = mv / _____.
**Answer: qB**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The lap time does not depend on the particle's _____.
**Answer: speed**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Angled entry gives a corkscrew path called a _____.
**Answer: helix**
