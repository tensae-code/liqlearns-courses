# Charged particles circling in a uniform magnetic field — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Derive r = mv/qB in one line from the two laws it joins.
**Answer: The Lorentz force supplies the centripetal force: qvB = mv²/r; cancel one v and rearrange: r = mv/qB.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A proton and an alpha particle (q = 2e, m ≈ 4 proton masses) enter at the same speed. Compare their radii.
**Answer: r ∝ m/q: the alpha's m/q is 4/2 = 2× the proton's, so its radius is twice the proton's at the same speed.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does the speed-independent period matter to the cyclotron's design?
**Answer: Its accelerating voltage can alternate at one fixed frequency and still arrive in step with the particle every half-lap, however fast the particle has grown — until relativistic mass growth at very high energy breaks the constancy.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A negative particle circles clockwise seen from above, where a positive one circles anticlockwise. Why?
**Answer: The Lorentz force flips sign with the charge: q changes sign, the force at each point reverses, and the circling sense reverses with it — track curvature direction reads the charge's sign.**
