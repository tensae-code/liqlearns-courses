# How molecular speeds are distributed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) At a given temperature, the molecules of a gas…
**Answer: a) have speeds spread in a fixed statistical distribution  b) all move at one speed  c) are mostly still  d) move only at the average speed**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The distribution's peak marks the…
**Answer: a) most probable speed  b) fastest speed  c) average energy  d) temperature**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The landmark tied directly to temperature is the…
**Answer: a) root-mean-square speed — temperature prices the mean of v²  b) slowest speed  c) most probable speed alone  d) speed of sound**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) All the landmark speeds grow with temperature as…
**Answer: a) √T  b) T  c) T²  d) 1/T**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Warming a gas makes its distribution…
**Answer: a) shift right, lower and broader, with a fatter fast tail  b) shift left  c) taller and narrower  d) unchanged**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Evaporation from a liquid's surface is carried by…
**Answer: a) the distribution's fast tail escaping  b) the slowest molecules  c) all molecules equally  d) no molecules**
