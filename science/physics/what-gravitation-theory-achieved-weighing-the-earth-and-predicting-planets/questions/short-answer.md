# What gravitation theory achieved — weighing the Earth, predicting planets — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is “weighing the Earth” not just a figure of speech here?
**Answer: Because the computation M = gR²/G returns an actual mass in kilograms from three ground-based measurements — the planet's mass obtained exactly as a scale obtains any mass, by its gravitational effect.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A moon orbits a planet in 8 days at 5 × 10⁸ m. Without calculating, state what else you need to weigh the planet and the law you would use.
**Answer: Only G (a universal constant): M = 4π²r³/(GT²) — Kepler's third law with the constant supplied by gravitation.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Mercury's orbit drift did NOT yield a new planet. Why is that failure part of the achievements story too?
**Answer: It marks the law's honest limit: the same method that crowned the theory at Neptune flagged, at Mercury, the small discrepancy that Newtonian gravitation could not pay — later settled by relativity; an audit reports debts as well as assets.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Name three working achievements of gravitation beyond the two showpieces.
**Answer: Tide prediction from the Moon's pull; Halley's comet's scheduled return; planetary/navigation tables (and later, every orbit and cosmic velocity of the spaceflight section). Any three.**
