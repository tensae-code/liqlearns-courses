# What gravitation theory achieved — weighing the Earth, predicting planets — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Earth's mass is found from the surface by setting…
**Answer: a) mg = GMm/R², giving M = gR²/G  b) M = gR/G²  c) M = v²R  d) guessing from its size**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Cavendish's torsion balance contributed…
**Answer: a) the first laboratory measure of G  b) the Earth's radius  c) the value of g  d) a telescope**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Neptune was discovered by…
**Answer: a) computing its position from Uranus's drift, then looking there  b) accident  c) a comet's tail  d) photographing the whole sky**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The astronomers who computed Neptune's place were…
**Answer: a) Adams and Le Verrier  b) Kepler and Galileo  c) Newton and Halley  d) Hubble and Einstein**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A planet with a moon can be weighed from…
**Answer: a) the moon's orbit radius and period  b) its colour  c) its ring size  d) its name**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Halley's comet returning in 1758 on schedule showed…
**Answer: a) gravitation could price even a comet's appointment  b) comets are planets  c) tides work  d) the Earth is flat**
