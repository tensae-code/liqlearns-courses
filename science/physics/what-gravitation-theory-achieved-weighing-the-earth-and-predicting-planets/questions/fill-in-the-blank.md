# What gravitation theory achieved — weighing the Earth, predicting planets — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Earth's mass: M = gR² / _____.
**Answer: G**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) G was first measured in the laboratory by _____.
**Answer: Cavendish**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The planet predicted from Uranus's drift was _____.
**Answer: Neptune**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A planet is weighed by its moon's period and orbit _____.
**Answer: radius**
