# Three ways to charge a body — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A comb rubbed on hair pulls electrons from the hair. The comb's sign?
a) Negative (it gained electrons); the hair is left positive.  b) Induction.  c) When the bodies share the charge (reach the same state).  d) It lets charge escape to, or enter from, the earth so the induced charge is trapped when the inducer leaves.
**Answer: a) Negative (it gained electrons); the hair is left positive.**

**2.** Which method never touches the body being charged?
a) Negative (it gained electrons); the hair is left positive.  b) Induction.  c) When the bodies share the charge (reach the same state).  d) It lets charge escape to, or enter from, the earth so the induced charge is trapped when the inducer leaves.
**Answer: b) Induction.**

**3.** In conduction, when does charge stop flowing?
a) Negative (it gained electrons); the hair is left positive.  b) Induction.  c) When the bodies share the charge (reach the same state).  d) It lets charge escape to, or enter from, the earth so the induced charge is trapped when the inducer leaves.
**Answer: c) When the bodies share the charge (reach the same state).**

**4.** In induction, what does grounding do?
a) Negative (it gained electrons); the hair is left positive.  b) Induction.  c) When the bodies share the charge (reach the same state).  d) It lets charge escape to, or enter from, the earth so the induced charge is trapped when the inducer leaves.
**Answer: d) It lets charge escape to, or enter from, the earth so the induced charge is trapped when the inducer leaves.**
