# Three ways to charge a body — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A comb rubbed on hair pulls electrons from the hair. The comb's sign?
**Answer: Negative (it gained electrons); the hair is left positive.**

**2.** Which method never touches the body being charged?
**Answer: Induction.**

**3.** In conduction, when does charge stop flowing?
**Answer: When the bodies share the charge (reach the same state).**

**4.** In induction, what does grounding do?
**Answer: It lets charge escape to, or enter from, the earth so the induced charge is trapped when the inducer leaves.**
