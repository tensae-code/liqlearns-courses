# Hands-on — Three ways to charge a body

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Charge a ruler by rubbing, then pick up paper bits (friction at work). Touch the ruler to a metal tap and feel its charge leave — conduction and grounding in one gesture.

## Apply it where you live
Describe charging a metal ball negatively by induction, step by step, naming each stage's charge movement.
