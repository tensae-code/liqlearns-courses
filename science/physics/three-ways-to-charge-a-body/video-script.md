# Video script — Three ways to charge a body

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Bodies are charged three ways.
By friction: rub a comb through hair and electrons transfer from one surface to the other — the receiver goes negative, the giver positive.
By conduction: touch a charged body to a neutral one and charge flows across the contact until they share it.
By induction: bring a charged body near (never touching) a neutral one, let the near charge drive electrons to one side, ground the far side so charge escapes or enters, then remove the ground and the charged body — the neutral body is left charged, oppositely to the inducer.

Remember: **Friction charging transfers electrons between rubbed surfaces.**
Remember: **Conduction charging shares charge by contact.**
Remember: **Induction charges without touching, using a nearby body and grounding.**
