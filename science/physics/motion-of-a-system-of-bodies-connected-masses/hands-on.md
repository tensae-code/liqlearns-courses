# Hands-on — Motion of a system of bodies — connected masses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two bottles and a string
Tie a full and a half-full water bottle together with a metre of string; rest the half-full bottle on a smooth table and hang the full one over the edge, holding both still. Release and watch: the pair accelerates gently, far slower than a dropped bottle — the system is sharing one acceleration, exactly as the lesson says. Repeat with the bottles swapped in size and feel (do not measure) how the acceleration changes with the mass balance.
