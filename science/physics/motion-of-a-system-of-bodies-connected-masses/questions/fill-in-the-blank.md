# Motion of a system of bodies — connected masses — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A string's fixed length forces connected bodies to share one _____.
**Answer: acceleration**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Tension is an _____ force of the whole system, so it cancels out of the system equation.
**Answer: internal**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) For a mass m₁ on a smooth table pulled by tension alone, T = m₁ × _____.
**Answer: a (the acceleration)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The arrangement of two hanging masses on opposite sides of a pulley is called the _____ arrangement.
**Answer: Atwood**
