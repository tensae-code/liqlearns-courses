# Motion of a system of bodies — connected masses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Two bodies joined by a taut string must have…
**Answer: a) the same acceleration  b) the same weight  c) the same tension pulling on each from outside  d) zero acceleration**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) For the whole connected system, Newton's second law reads…
**Answer: a) F_net (external) = (m₁ + m₂) a  b) T = (m₁ + m₂) a  c) F_net = m₁ a only  d) each body needs its own unrelated acceleration**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The tension is found by…
**Answer: a) isolating one body and applying F = ma to it alone  b) adding the two weights  c) dividing the total mass by g  d) it always equals the heavier weight**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A light, smooth pulley in the string…
**Answer: a) keeps the same tension throughout and changes only its direction  b) doubles the tension  c) removes the tension  d) makes the two sides accelerate differently**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In an Atwood arrangement with masses 5 kg and 3 kg, the acceleration is…
**Answer: a) (5 − 3)g/(5 + 3) = g/4, the 5 kg side falling  b) g  c) (5 + 3)g/(5 − 3)  d) zero, the masses balance**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In the Lao Grade 10 book, systems of bodies are taught…
**Answer: a) as their own lesson in Part III (Lesson 13, Motion of the Matter System)  b) inside the heat part  c) only in exercises, never in a lesson  d) in the Grade 12 book only**
