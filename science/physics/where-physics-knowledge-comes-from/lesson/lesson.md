## How physics knowledge is made — experiential sources

Knowledge begins in experience: sensory perception (seeing, touching, hearing), introspection (examining one's own feelings and thoughts, as when describing pain to a doctor), memory (the mind's recording of events in order) and testimony (what others report, written testimony usually the more reliable).
