## Splitting a vector — components — the teaching
Any vector can be split into two perpendicular components, usually x and y. For a vector A at angle θ to the x-axis: Ax = A cos θ along x and Ay = A sin θ along y. The components together do exactly the original vector's job. Recombining runs backward: A = √(Ax² + Ay²) and tan θ = Ay/Ax. This is the tool the rest of physics reaches for — forces on slopes, motion in two directions, fields. A worked case: a 40 N force at 30° gives Ax = 40 cos 30° ≈ 34.6 N and Ay = 40 sin 30° = 20 N.

**Keep the formula:** Ax = A cos θ; Ay = A sin θ

## The key ideas

- Ax = A cos θ and Ay = A sin θ for a vector at angle θ.
- Recombine: A = √(Ax² + Ay²), tan θ = Ay/Ax.
- A 40 N force at 30°: Ax ≈ 34.6 N, Ay = 20 N.
