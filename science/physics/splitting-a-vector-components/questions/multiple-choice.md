# Splitting a vector — components — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Split 40 N at 30° into components.
a) Ax = 40 cos 30° ≈ 34.6 N; Ay = 40 sin 30° = 20 N.  b) Ax = 10 cos 90° = 0; Ay = 10 sin 90° = 10 m/s.  c) A = √(36+64) = 10; θ = arctan(8/6) ≈ 53° from east.  d) The one along the axis the angle is measured from (x here).
**Answer: a) Ax = 40 cos 30° ≈ 34.6 N; Ay = 40 sin 30° = 20 N.**

**2.** Split 10 m/s at 90° (straight up). Components?
a) Ax = 40 cos 30° ≈ 34.6 N; Ay = 40 sin 30° = 20 N.  b) Ax = 10 cos 90° = 0; Ay = 10 sin 90° = 10 m/s.  c) A = √(36+64) = 10; θ = arctan(8/6) ≈ 53° from east.  d) The one along the axis the angle is measured from (x here).
**Answer: b) Ax = 10 cos 90° = 0; Ay = 10 sin 90° = 10 m/s.**
