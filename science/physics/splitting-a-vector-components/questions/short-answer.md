# Splitting a vector — components — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Split 40 N at 30° into components.
**Answer: Ax = 40 cos 30° ≈ 34.6 N; Ay = 40 sin 30° = 20 N.**

**2.** Split 10 m/s at 90° (straight up). Components?
**Answer: Ax = 10 cos 90° = 0; Ay = 10 sin 90° = 10 m/s.**

**3.** Components are 6 east and 8 north. Magnitude and direction?
**Answer: A = √(36+64) = 10; θ = arctan(8/6) ≈ 53° from east.**

**4.** Which component uses cosine?
**Answer: The one along the axis the angle is measured from (x here).**
