# Hands-on — Splitting a vector — components

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Push a book diagonally across a table. Feel both jobs your push is doing at once — across and forward. Components are that feeling, written down.

## Apply it where you live
Resolve a 1,900 km displacement at 40° (the guide's model) into north and east components.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21878 — one diagonal arrow dissolving into its horizontal and vertical partners] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using Ax = A cos θ; Ay = A sin θ: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
