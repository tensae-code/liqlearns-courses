# Video script — Splitting a vector — components

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Any vector can be split into two perpendicular components, usually x and y.
For a vector A at angle θ to the x-axis: Ax = A cos θ along x and Ay = A sin θ along y.
The components together do exactly the original vector's job.
Recombining runs backward: A = √(Ax² + Ay²) and tan θ = Ay/Ax.
This is the tool the rest of physics reaches for — forces on slopes, motion in two directions, fields.

Remember: **Ax = A cos θ and Ay = A sin θ for a vector at angle θ.**
Remember: **Recombine: A = √(Ax² + Ay²), tan θ = Ay/Ax.**
Remember: **A 40 N force at 30°: Ax ≈ 34.6 N, Ay = 20 N.**
Keep the formula: **Ax = A cos θ; Ay = A sin θ**
