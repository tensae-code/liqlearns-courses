# Hands-on — Fields — interacting objects and field characteristics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map a magnetic field with a compass
Put a bar magnet under a sheet of paper and a small compass (or a phone compass) on the paper. Move the compass slowly in a grid — near the poles, at the sides, far away — and at each spot draw a short arrow on the paper showing which way the needle points, longer where the needle swings hard and settles fast, shorter where it wanders weakly. When the grid is done, join the arrows into lines with your eye: check the three rules — lines leave one pole and enter the other, they never cross, and they crowd near the poles where your arrows were longest. Say aloud: "The crowding is the strength; the arrows are the direction — that is the field, characterised."
