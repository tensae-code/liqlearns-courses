# Video script — Fields — interacting objects and field characteristics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **fields — how objects interact without touching**. A magnet pulls a clip across a gap; the Earth holds the Moon across empty space. The secret is the field: the source object fills the space around it, and anything that enters that space feels the force. Every field has a strength — force divided by the mass or charge that feels it — and a direction, drawn as lines that crowd together where the field is strong. Gravity only pulls and cannot be blocked; electric fields push and pull and can be shut out by a metal cage. Name the field, its strength and its pattern, and the interaction is fully described.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
