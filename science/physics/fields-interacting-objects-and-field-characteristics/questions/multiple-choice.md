# Fields — interacting objects and field characteristics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the field picture, a falling apple is best described as…
a) The Earth reaching out and grabbing it  b) The apple sitting in the Earth's gravitational field, which exerts the force on it  c) Air pushing it down  d) The apple's own field pulling the Earth up only
**Answer: b) The apple sitting in the Earth's gravitational field, which exerts the force on it**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) If a field's source suddenly changes, the change spreads through the field at…
a) Instantly, everywhere  b) The speed of sound  c) The speed of light  d) 9.8 m/s
**Answer: c) The speed of light**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Electric field strength E is defined as…
a) Charge ÷ force  b) Force ÷ charge, E = F/q, in newtons per coulomb  c) Force × charge  d) Charge ÷ distance
**Answer: b) Force ÷ charge, E = F/q, in newtons per coulomb**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) On a field-line drawing, the field is strongest where…
a) The lines are longest  b) The lines crowd closest together  c) The lines are straightest  d) There are no lines
**Answer: b) The lines crowd closest together**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Inside a hollow conductor, the electric field is…
a) Doubled  b) Zero — the conductor's charges rearrange to cancel it (the Faraday cage effect)  c) The same as outside  d) Turned into a magnetic field
**Answer: b) Zero — the conductor's charges rearrange to cancel it (the Faraday cage effect)**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The one field that can never be screened off by any material is…
a) The electric field  b) The magnetic field  c) Gravity — it only attracts and no wall blocks it  d) Light
**Answer: c) Gravity — it only attracts and no wall blocks it**
