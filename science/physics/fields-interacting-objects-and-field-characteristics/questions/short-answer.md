# Fields — interacting objects and field characteristics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Tell the two-step field story for the Moon staying in orbit.
**Answer: The Earth (the source) sets up a gravitational field in the space around it; the Moon, sitting in that field, feels a force toward the Earth — the field, not contact or a tie, carries the interaction.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A charge of 0.001 C in a field feels 0.02 N. Find E, with its unit.
**Answer: E = F/q = 0.02 ÷ 0.001 = 20 N/C.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) State the three field-line rules.
**Answer: Lines point the way a visitor object is pushed; lines never cross; lines crowd where the field is strong and spread where it is weak.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Two fields overlap at a point. How is the resultant field found, and why does that method work?
**Answer: By vector addition — superposition: each field acts independently at the point, so their strengths and directions add as vectors to give the single resultant field a visitor would feel.**
