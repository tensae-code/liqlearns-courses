# Electric phenomena — Beruni and Ibn Sina on lightning — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The word electricity comes from the Greek word for…
**Answer: a) amber  b) lightning  c) iron  d) cloud**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Beruni's way with marvellous reports was to…
**Answer: a) gather them, compare them, refuse the marvellous  b) believe them all  c) ban them  d) sell them**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Ibn Sina placed lightning…
**Answer: a) inside the storm cloud, as a natural event with natural causes  b) in the underworld  c) inside the Earth  d) in the Sun**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In Ibn Sina's account, thunder is…
**Answer: a) the crash of the cloud's tearing discharge  b) the voice of a spirit  c)  an earthquake in the sky  d) the sound of rain**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) What did Beruni and Ibn Sina lack, that the modern course has?
**Answer: a) Instruments — electrometer, battery, wire  b) Curiosity  c) The phenomena themselves  d) Books**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The stance the history hands to the electricity course is…
**Answer: a) electric phenomena in nature are physics, continuous with bench experiments  b) lightning is an omen  c) nature and laboratory are separate  d) only moderns may ask questions**
