# Electric phenomena — Beruni and Ibn Sina on lightning — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Rubbed _____ attracts straw and chaff — the founding electric observation.
**Answer: amber**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Beruni treated electric wonders as _____ to be collected.
**Answer: facts**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Ibn Sina wrote on lightning in his Book of _____.
**Answer: Healing**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Charge on a rubbed ruler fades faster in _____ air.
