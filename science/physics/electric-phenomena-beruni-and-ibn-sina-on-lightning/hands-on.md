# Hands-on — Electric phenomena — Beruni and Ibn Sina on lightning

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Amber's trick
Rub a plastic comb or ruler briskly on wool or dry hair, then hold it near tiny paper scraps, a thin stream of tap water, and a friend's hair. Record which are attracted and how the effect fades with time and dampness. You have repeated the founding observation of electricity — the one Beruni collected — and seen its two school facts: charge leaks away, and damp air leaks it faster.
