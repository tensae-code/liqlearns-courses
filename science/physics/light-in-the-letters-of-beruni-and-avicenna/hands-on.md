# Hands-on — Light in the letters of Beruni and Avicenna

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The water-sphere lens
Fill a round glass jar or a clear bottle completely with water and cap it. Hold it over newsprint: the letters swell — your water lens magnifies. Then, on a sunny day with a teacher, hold it so the Sun's bright spot falls on dark paper: the spot is small, bright and hot. Draw the rays bending in and out. You have rebuilt the letters' exhibit.
