# Light in the letters of Beruni and Avicenna — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The eye _____ light — Avicenna's side, and the modern one.
**Answer: receives**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A rainbow stands _____ the Sun in the sky.
**Answer: opposite**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Each droplet bends and bounces light — the bow is geometry plus _____.
**Answer: droplets**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Bending of light at a boundary is called _____.
**Answer: refraction**
