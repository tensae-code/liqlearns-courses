# Light in the letters of Beruni and Avicenna — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The old Greek theory of vision said…
**Answer: a) rays leave the eye and touch the object  b) light enters the eye  c) the eye is a camera  d) we see by smell**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Avicenna's argument against rays leaving the eye:…
**Answer: a) we would then see in the dark and tire like lamps  b) the eye is too small  c) rays are too slow  d) the Greeks forbade it**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A rainbow stands…
**Answer: a) opposite the Sun  b) next to the Sun  c) only at midnight  d) under the rain cloud's centre**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In the scholars' frame, a rainbow needs…
**Answer: a) sunlight meeting rain — geometry plus droplets  b) a full Moon  c) snow  d) a mirror**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A water-filled sphere acts as…
**Answer: a) a lens — bending light to a focus  b) a mirror  c) a prism only  d) a light source**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The sphere's bright spot can kindle tinder because…
**Answer: a) it concentrates the Sun's rays into a small area  b) water burns  c) glass is hot  d) the Sun aims at it**
