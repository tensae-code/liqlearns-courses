# The electric motor and back EMF — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Continuous rotation in a DC motor is maintained by…
a) The split-ring commutator, reversing the coil current each half-turn so the torque stays one-way  b) Increasing the supply voltage  c) The brushes heating the coil  d) Gravity on the coil's sides
**Answer: a) The split-ring commutator, reversing the coil current each half-turn so the torque stays one-way**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The turning effect on the motor coil comes from…
a) Electrostatic attraction to the poles  b) Opposite forces F = BIL on the two current-carrying sides, forming a couple  c) The coil's weight  d) Induced currents in the magnet
**Answer: b) Opposite forces F = BIL on the two current-carrying sides, forming a couple**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The back EMF in a motor…
a) Aids the supply voltage  b) Opposes the supply and grows in proportion to the motor's speed  c) Is constant at all speeds  d) Exists only at switch-on
**Answer: b) Opposes the supply and grows in proportion to the motor's speed**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The current in a running motor is…
a) I = V/R always  b) I = E′/R  c) I = (V − E′)/R  d) I = (V + E′)/R
**Answer: c) I = (V − E′)/R**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) At the instant of starting, the motor current is largest because…
a) The resistance is highest when cold  b) The back EMF is zero — the coil is not yet spinning — so the current is V/R  c) The commutator doubles the voltage  d) The field is strongest at rest
**Answer: b) The back EMF is zero — the coil is not yet spinning — so the current is V/R**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A stalled motor burns out because…
a) Its brushes wear instantly  b) With E′ = 0 the current stays at the huge starting value and overheats the windings  c) The supply voltage doubles  d) The magnetic field reverses
**Answer: b) With E′ = 0 the current stays at the huge starting value and overheats the windings**
