# The electric motor and back EMF — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The device that reverses the coil current each half-turn in a DC motor is the _____.
**Answer: commutator (split-ring commutator)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The EMF a spinning motor generates against its own supply is called the _____ EMF.
**Answer: back**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Motor current obeys I = (V − E′)/_____.
**Answer: R**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A large motor is started with a _____ resistance in series, removed as speed builds.
**Answer: starter**
