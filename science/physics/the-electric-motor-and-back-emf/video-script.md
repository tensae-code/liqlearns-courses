Today: **the electric motor and back EMF**. Push current through a coil in a magnetic field and the two sides are forced opposite ways — the coil turns: a motor. But a turning coil in a field is also a generator, and it generates against you. That opposing voltage is the back EMF. It grows with speed until it nearly cancels the supply, leaving only the small current the load needs. Hold the shaft still and there is no back EMF at all — and the current explodes. That is why motors need starters, and why a jammed motor burns.
