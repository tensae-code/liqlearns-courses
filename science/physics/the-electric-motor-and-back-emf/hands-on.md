# Hands-on — The electric motor and back EMF

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch the current fall as it spins
Connect a small DC motor through an ammeter to a variable supply. Hold the shaft, switch on briefly and note the current; release and note the running current; then load the shaft gently with a finger and watch the current climb as the speed dips. Say aloud the rule: current = (V − E′)/R — anything that lowers the speed lowers E′ and lets the current rise.

## Spin it and measure the back EMF
Disconnect the supply and spin the same motor's shaft by hand (or by a falling weight on a string) with a voltmeter across its terminals. The motor generates; the faster the spin, the larger the reading. Say aloud the rule: motoring and generating are one machine read in two directions — the back EMF is the generator voltage fighting the supply.
