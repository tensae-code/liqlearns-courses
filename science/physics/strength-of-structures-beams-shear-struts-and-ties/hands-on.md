# Hands-on — Strength of structures — beams, shear, struts and ties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Bend it, notch it, frame it
Three stations. (1) A strip of wood or stiff card across two books: load the middle with coins and see the sag; flip it on edge and repeat. (2) Two identical strips, one notched halfway through at mid-span: load both and record which fails, where, and at what load — the notch effect measured. (3) Build a square frame from strips and pins: push a corner and watch it distort; add one diagonal (a tie of string or a strut of card) and push again. Write one sentence per station naming the physics that decided the result.
