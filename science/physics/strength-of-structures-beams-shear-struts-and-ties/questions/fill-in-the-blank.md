# Strength of structures — beams, shear, struts and ties — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The unstressed layer between a bending beam's squeezed top and stretched bottom is the _____ layer.
**Answer: neutral**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Sliding failure between the layers of a beam is failure by _____.
**Answer: shear**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A member built to be pulled, working in tension, is called a _____.
**Answer: tie**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Putting a frame's members into _____ makes it hold its shape, because that shape cannot distort.
**Answer: triangles (triangulation)**
