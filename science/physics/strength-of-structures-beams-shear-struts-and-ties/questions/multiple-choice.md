# Strength of structures — beams, shear, struts and ties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a bending beam under a central load, the top face is in…
**Answer: a) compression  b) tension  c) shear only  d) neither**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A beam is made stiffer mainly by making it…
**Answer: a) deeper  b) longer  c) narrower  d) lighter in colour**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Shear force in a beam is greatest…
**Answer: a) near the supports  b) at mid-span  c) on the top face  d) outside the beam**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The notch effect says a cut or nick in a member…
**Answer: a) concentrates stress at its root and starts cracks  b) strengthens the member  c) spreads stress evenly  d) only matters for metals**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A strut is a member that works in…
**Answer: a) compression — it is pushed  b) tension — it is pulled  c) bending  d) torsion**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Which shape holds its form under load without any rigid joints?
**Answer: a) the triangle  b) the square  c) the circle alone  d) the rectangle**
