Today: **strength of structures — beams, shear, struts and ties**. Bend a beam and its top squeezes while its bottom stretches; shear slides its layers; a notch gathers all its stress into one weak point. Build a frame instead and the load divides politely — struts push, ties pull, triangles keep the shape. Standing up is Physics.
