# Video script — Electric charge — two kinds, conserved

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Electric charge comes in two kinds: positive, carried by protons, and negative, carried by electrons.
A neutral object holds equal amounts of both.
Charge is conserved in an isolated system: charging never creates charge from nothing — it moves charge from one place to another, so what one body gains, another loses.
Every charging story in this unit is bookkeeping of electrons on the move.

Remember: **Positive charge: protons. Negative charge: electrons.**
Remember: **A neutral object holds equal positive and negative charge.**
Remember: **Charge is conserved — charging moves charge, never creates it.**
