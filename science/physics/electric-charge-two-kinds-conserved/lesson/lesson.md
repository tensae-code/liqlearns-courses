# Electric charge — two kinds, conserved

## Electric charge — two kinds, conserved — the teaching
Electric charge comes in two kinds: positive, carried by protons, and negative, carried by electrons. A neutral object holds equal amounts of both. Charge is conserved in an isolated system: charging never creates charge from nothing — it moves charge from one place to another, so what one body gains, another loses. Every charging story in this unit is bookkeeping of electrons on the move.

## The key ideas

- Positive charge: protons. Negative charge: electrons.
- A neutral object holds equal positive and negative charge.
- Charge is conserved — charging moves charge, never creates it.

## Say it to your companion

Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
