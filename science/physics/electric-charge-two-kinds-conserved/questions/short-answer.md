# Electric charge — two kinds, conserved — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Which particle carries negative charge?
**Answer: The electron.**

**2.** A body gains electrons. Its charge?
**Answer: Negative — it now has more negative than positive.**

**3.** Can rubbing create charge from nothing?
**Answer: No — charge is conserved; rubbing only moves it.**

**4.** What makes an object neutral?
**Answer: Equal amounts of positive and negative charge.**
