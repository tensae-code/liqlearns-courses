# Electric charge — two kinds, conserved — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which particle carries negative charge?
a) The electron.  b) Negative — it now has more negative than positive.  c) No — charge is conserved; rubbing only moves it.  d) Equal amounts of positive and negative charge.
**Answer: a) The electron.**

**2.** A body gains electrons. Its charge?
a) The electron.  b) Negative — it now has more negative than positive.  c) No — charge is conserved; rubbing only moves it.  d) Equal amounts of positive and negative charge.
**Answer: b) Negative — it now has more negative than positive.**

**3.** Can rubbing create charge from nothing?
a) The electron.  b) Negative — it now has more negative than positive.  c) No — charge is conserved; rubbing only moves it.  d) Equal amounts of positive and negative charge.
**Answer: c) No — charge is conserved; rubbing only moves it.**

**4.** What makes an object neutral?
a) The electron.  b) Negative — it now has more negative than positive.  c) No — charge is conserved; rubbing only moves it.  d) Equal amounts of positive and negative charge.
**Answer: d) Equal amounts of positive and negative charge.**
