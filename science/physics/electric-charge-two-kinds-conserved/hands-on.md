# Hands-on — Electric charge — two kinds, conserved

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Rub a balloon or a plastic ruler and hold it near small paper scraps. Something moved between surfaces — this lesson names what.

## Apply it where you live
Explain, with charge conservation, where a charged comb's charge came from.
