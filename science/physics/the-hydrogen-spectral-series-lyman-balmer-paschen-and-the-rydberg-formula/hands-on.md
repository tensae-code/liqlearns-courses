# Hands-on — The hydrogen spectral series — Lyman, Balmer, Paschen and the Rydberg formula

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

['(1) Look at a hydrogen (or helium) discharge tube through a grating (teacher-led) and list the visible lines by colour — the Balmer family in person. (2) Compute the first three Balmer wavelengths from the Rydberg formula and match them to the colours seen. (3) On a printed level diagram, draw every fall to p = 1, 2 and 3 from n ≤ 4 and sort your arrows into the three series by landing level.']
