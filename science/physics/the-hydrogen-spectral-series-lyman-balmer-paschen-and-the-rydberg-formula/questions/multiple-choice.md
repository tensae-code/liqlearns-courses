# The hydrogen spectral series — Lyman, Balmer, Paschen and the Rydberg formula — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A hydrogen line is emitted when the electron…
**Answer: a) falls from a higher level n to a lower level p, emitting the difference as one photon  b) climbs a level  c) leaves the atom only  d) circles faster**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A photon can be absorbed by hydrogen only if its energy…
**Answer: a) exactly equals a level difference  b) is any value  c) exceeds 13.6 eV always  d) is below 1 eV**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The Rydberg formula reads 1/λ = …
**Answer: a) R_H(1/p² − 1/n²)  b) R_H(n² − p²)  c) R_H/(n − p)  d) R_H × p² × n²**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In the formula, p is…
**Answer: a) the landing (lower) level  b) the starting level  c) the photon count  d) the wavelength**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The Balmer series is the family of falls to…
**Answer: a) p = 2 — the visible series  b) p = 1 — ultraviolet  c) p = 3 — infrared  d) p = 6**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The Lyman series lies in the…
**Answer: a) ultraviolet  b) visible  c) infrared  d) radio**
