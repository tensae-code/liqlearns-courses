# The hydrogen spectral series — Lyman, Balmer, Paschen and the Rydberg formula — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Hydrogen's levels follow Eₙ = −13.6/_____ eV.
**Answer: n²**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The constant R_H in the Rydberg formula is the _____ constant.
**Answer: Rydberg**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Falls to p = 2 form the _____ series.
**Answer: Balmer**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Falls to p = 1 form the _____ series, in the ultraviolet.
