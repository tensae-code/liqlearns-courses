# The hydrogen spectral series — Lyman, Balmer, Paschen and the Rydberg formula — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Compute the Hα wavelength (3 → 2) with R_H = 1.097×10⁷ m⁻¹.
**Answer: 1/λ = 1.097×10⁷ × (1/4 − 1/9) = 1.097×10⁷ × 0.1389 ≈ 1.524×10⁶ m⁻¹, so λ ≈ 6.56×10⁻⁷ m = 656 nm — the red Hα line.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name all six series with their landing levels, as the 7AS-D chapter lists them.
**Answer: Lyman (p = 1), Balmer (p = 2), Paschen (p = 3), Brackett (p = 4), Pfund (p = 5), Humphreys (p = 6).**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What is a series limit, physically?
**Answer: The line of the fall from n = ∞ to that series' landing level — the ionisation edge from that level: beyond it (shorter wavelength) the electron is freed rather than landed, and the spectrum becomes continuous.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A photon of 15 eV meets a ground-state hydrogen atom. What happens, and where does the extra energy go?
**Answer: 15 eV exceeds the 13.6 eV ionisation energy, so the atom is ionised; the surplus 1.4 eV becomes the freed electron's kinetic energy.**
