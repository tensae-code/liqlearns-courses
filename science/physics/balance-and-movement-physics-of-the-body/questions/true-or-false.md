# Balance and movement — physics of the body — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. Newtonian mechanics explains animal and human movement: stability is centre of mass over the base of support (a load-carrier bends to keep it there; standing on one foot and sleeping are the discussion cases), and the same mechanics explains athletes and the cheetah's speed; biomechanics/kinesiology are named.**
**Answer: True**
