# Balance and movement — physics of the body — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. Give one key fact this lesson teaches about physics of the living body.**
**Answer: Newtonian mechanics explains animal and human movement: stability is centre of mass over the base of support (a load-carrier bends to keep it there; standing on one foot and sleeping are the discussion cases), and the same mechanics explains athletes and the cheetah's speed; biomechanics/kinesiology are named.**
