Today: **the force between two parallel current-carrying wires**. Every current wraps its wire in a magnetic field. Put a second current inside that field and it is pushed — and the second wire pushes back equally. Currents flowing the same way attract; currents flowing against each other repel. The force per metre is μ₀I₁I₂ over 2πd — so trusted that the ampere itself was once defined by it: the current that pulls two wires, one metre apart, with exactly 2 × 10⁻⁷ newtons per metre.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
