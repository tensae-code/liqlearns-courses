# Hands-on — Force between two parallel current-carrying wires

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch wires attract and repel
Hang two long flexible leads vertically, parallel and a centimetre apart, from retort stands. Pass a few amperes from a low-voltage supply through both in series-aiding (same direction down both) and watch them bow together; reverse one lead's connections and watch them spring apart. Say aloud the rule: same direction attracts, opposite repels — the field of each wire pushes the current of the other.

## Feel the scaling
Repeat with the wires twice as far apart and note the weaker bow; then double the current and note the stronger one. Say aloud the rule: F/L = μ₀I₁I₂/(2πd) — the force follows the product of the currents and fades as 1/d.
