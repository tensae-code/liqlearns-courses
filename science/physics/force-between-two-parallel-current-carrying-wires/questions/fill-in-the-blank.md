# Force between two parallel current-carrying wires — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A current-carrying wire in a magnetic field feels a force F = B × I × _____ for a wire at right angles to the field.
**Answer: L (its length in the field)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Parallel currents in the same direction _____ each other.
**Answer: attract**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The force per unit length between two wires varies inversely with their _____.
**Answer: separation (distance apart, d)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) In F/L = μ₀I₁I₂/(2πd), the constant μ₀ is the _____ of free space.
**Answer: permeability**
