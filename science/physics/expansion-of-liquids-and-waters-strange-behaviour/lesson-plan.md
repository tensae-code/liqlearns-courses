# Lesson plan — Expansion of liquids; water's strange 0–4 °C behaviour

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** Expansion of liquids; water's strange 0–4 °C behaviour
**Place in the curriculum:** Grade 11 · Unit 5 — Heat Conduction and Calorimetry.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** expansion of liquids and gases; the water anomaly — Liquids expand more than solids (the thermometer works by it) and gases most of all; water breaks the rule between 0 °C and 4 °C, contracting as it warms to its densest…
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 11 Physics lessons before it in Unit 5 — Heat Conduction and Calorimetry — and on the learner's own world: the examples are the learner's home, street, school and body.

## Sub lessons
1. **Liquids and gases expand more** — Liquids expand more than solids (the thermometer works by it) and gases most of all.
2. **Water's strange 0–4 °C behaviour** — Water breaks the rule between 0 °C and 4 °C, contracting as it warms to its densest point — why lakes freeze from the top.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
