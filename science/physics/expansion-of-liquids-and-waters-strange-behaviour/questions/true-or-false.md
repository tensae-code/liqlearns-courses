# Expansion of liquids; water's strange 0–4 °C behaviour — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Liquids expand more than solids (the thermometer works by it) and gases most of all.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Liquids expand less than solids (the thermometer works by it) and gases most of all.**
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Water breaks the rule between 0 °C and 4 °C, contracting as it warms to its densest point — why lakes freeze from the top.**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Water breaks the rule between 2 °C and 4 °C, contracting as it warms to its densest point — why lakes freeze from the top.**
**Answer: False**
