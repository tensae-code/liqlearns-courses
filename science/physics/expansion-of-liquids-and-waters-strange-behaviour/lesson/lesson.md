## Sub lesson 1 — Liquids and gases expand more

Liquids expand more than solids (the thermometer works by it) and gases most of all.

## Sub lesson 2 — Water's strange 0–4 °C behaviour

Water breaks the rule between 0 °C and 4 °C, contracting as it warms to its densest point — why lakes freeze from the top.
