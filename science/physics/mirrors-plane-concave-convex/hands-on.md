# Hands-on — Mirrors — plane, concave, convex

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Face a mirror and raise your right hand; the person facing you raises their left. Lateral inversion, demonstrated by your own body.

## Apply it where you live
Draw ray diagrams for one object position at each mirror type and state each image's nature.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21933 — ray diagrams for the three mirrors with the same object] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using 1/f = 1/v + 1/u; m = v/u; f = R/2: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
