# Video script — Mirrors — plane, concave, convex

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

A plane mirror gives an image that is virtual (behind the mirror), erect, the same size as the object, and laterally inverted — left and right swapped.
Image distance equals object distance.
A concave mirror curves inward and gathers rays: parallel rays reflect through its focal point, with focal length f = R/2, half the radius of curvature.
It serves in car headlights, shaving mirrors and solar furnaces.
A convex mirror curves outward and spreads rays, giving a wide field of view with small, erect, virtual images — the rear-view mirror's bargain.
Both curved mirrors are computed with the mirror equation 1/f = 1/v + 1/u and magnification m = v/u, with ray diagrams to check the arithmetic.

Remember: **Plane mirror image: virtual, erect, same size, laterally inverted.**
Remember: **Concave mirror focuses rays; f = R/2.**
Remember: **Convex mirror spreads rays — wide view, small erect images.**
Keep the formula: **1/f = 1/v + 1/u; m = v/u; f = R/2**
