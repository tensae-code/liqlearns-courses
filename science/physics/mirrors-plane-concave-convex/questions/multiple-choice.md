# Mirrors — plane, concave, convex — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A plane mirror's image is real or virtual?
a) Virtual — behind the mirror.  b) f = R/2 = 20 cm.  c) 1/v = 1/20 − 1/30 = 1/60, so v = 60 cm.  d) They spread rays to give a wide field of view.
**Answer: a) Virtual — behind the mirror.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A concave mirror has R = 40 cm. Its focal length?
a) Virtual — behind the mirror.  b) f = R/2 = 20 cm.  c) 1/v = 1/20 − 1/30 = 1/60, so v = 60 cm.  d) They spread rays to give a wide field of view.
**Answer: b) f = R/2 = 20 cm.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mirror equation: f = 20 cm, u = 30 cm. Find v.
a) Virtual — behind the mirror.  b) f = R/2 = 20 cm.  c) 1/v = 1/20 − 1/30 = 1/60, so v = 60 cm.  d) They spread rays to give a wide field of view.
**Answer: c) 1/v = 1/20 − 1/30 = 1/60, so v = 60 cm.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Why do rear-view mirrors use convex mirrors?
a) Virtual — behind the mirror.  b) f = R/2 = 20 cm.  c) 1/v = 1/20 − 1/30 = 1/60, so v = 60 cm.  d) They spread rays to give a wide field of view.
**Answer: d) They spread rays to give a wide field of view.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Magnification m = v/u with v = 60, u = 30 gives — ?
a) m = 2 — the image is twice the object's size.  b) Virtual — behind the mirror.  c) f = R/2 = 20 cm.  d) 1/v = 1/20 − 1/30 = 1/60, so v = 60 cm.
**Answer: a) m = 2 — the image is twice the object's size.**
