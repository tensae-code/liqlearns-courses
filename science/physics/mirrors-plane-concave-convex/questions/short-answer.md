# Mirrors — plane, concave, convex — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A plane mirror's image is real or virtual?
**Answer: Virtual — behind the mirror.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A concave mirror has R = 40 cm. Its focal length?
**Answer: f = R/2 = 20 cm.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mirror equation: f = 20 cm, u = 30 cm. Find v.
**Answer: 1/v = 1/20 − 1/30 = 1/60, so v = 60 cm.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Why do rear-view mirrors use convex mirrors?
**Answer: They spread rays to give a wide field of view.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Magnification m = v/u with v = 60, u = 30 gives — ?
**Answer: m = 2 — the image is twice the object's size.**
