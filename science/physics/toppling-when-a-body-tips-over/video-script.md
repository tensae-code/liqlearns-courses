# Video script — Toppling — when a body tips over

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **toppling — gravity changing sides**. A body stands while the straight-down line from its centre of gravity lands inside its base. Tilt it and that line walks toward the edge; on the edge, weight has no moment at all; one step past, and the same weight that used to restore the body now overturns it. Low centre, wide base — that is the whole art of not falling over.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
