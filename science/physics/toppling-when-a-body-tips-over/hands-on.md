# Hands-on — Toppling — when a body tips over

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find the tipping angle
You need a rectangular box (a cereal box works), a protractor or phone angle measure, and a board to tilt slowly. Stand the box on the board, raise one end gradually, and note the angle at which the box topples. Measure the box's width and the height of its middle (its centre of gravity, if it is evenly packed). Compare the measured angle with tan θ = half-width ÷ centre height. Repeat with the box on its wider face, and with weights (books) taped low versus high, recording which change bought the most stability.
