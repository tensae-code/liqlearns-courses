# Toppling — when a body tips over — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A body topples when…
a) Its mass doubles  b) The line of gravity falls outside its base  c) It is pushed at all  d) Its centre of gravity rises
**Answer: b) The line of gravity falls outside its base**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) While the line of gravity falls well inside the base, a small tilt…
a) Topples the body at once  b) Is corrected — weight rotates the body back onto its base  c) Has no effect of any kind  d) Makes the body slide
**Answer: b) Is corrected — weight rotates the body back onto its base**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) At the exact tipping point, the weight's moment about the pivot edge is…
a) At its maximum  b) Zero — the line of gravity passes through the pivot  c) Doubled  d) Unknown
**Answer: b) Zero — the line of gravity passes through the pivot**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Pushing a body over requires work because…
a) Friction must be melted  b) The centre of gravity must first be lifted to the tipping position  c) The base must be broken  d) Air resistance resists
**Answer: b) The centre of gravity must first be lifted to the tipping position**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Which change makes a body hardest to topple?
a) Higher centre of gravity, narrow base  b) Lower centre of gravity, wider base  c) Lighter body, same shape  d) Smoother floor
**Answer: b) Lower centre of gravity, wider base**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A tall body pushed sideways on a high-grip floor will usually…
a) Slide  b) Topple, because grip holds while the line of gravity escapes the base  c) Float  d) Compress
**Answer: b) Topple, because grip holds while the line of gravity escapes the base**
