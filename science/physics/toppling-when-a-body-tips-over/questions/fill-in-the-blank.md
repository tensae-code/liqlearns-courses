# Toppling — when a body tips over — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The area joining a body's contact points with the ground is its _____.
**Answer: base**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) At the tipping point the body balances on its pivot _____.
**Answer: edge**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Past the tipping point, weight's moment about the edge is _____ (restoring / overturning).
**Answer: overturning**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Ballast is placed _____ in a body to make it stable.
**Answer: low down (at the bottom)**
