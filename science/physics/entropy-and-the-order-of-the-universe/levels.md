# Entropy and the order of the universe — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner states in their own words that real processes run one way, defines entropy as the measure of disorder/spreading, states the second law (the universe's total entropy never decreases), and explains local order as bought with greater disorder exported elsewhere — and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has three sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — more one-way processes to spot in the home, entropy judgements comparing pairs of states, and the hard cases argued carefully: the fridge that cools its inside, the plant that builds itself from air and sunlight.
