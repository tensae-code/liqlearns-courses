# Entropy and the order of the universe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these processes runs one way only?
a) Heat flowing by itself from a cold body to a hot one  b) Ice melting in a warm room  c) Perfume gathering itself back into its bottle  d) A still ball starting to bounce higher on its own
**Answer: b) Ice melting in a warm room**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Heat flows from a hot cup into a cool room. What never happens by itself?
a) The cup cools  b) The room warms slightly  c) The heat gathers back from the room into the cup, making it hot again  d) The cup reaches the room's temperature
**Answer: c) The heat gathers back from the room into the cup, making it hot again**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Entropy is a measure of…
a) How heavy a body is  b) How disordered and spread out energy and matter have become  c) How fast something moves  d) How much charge a body holds
**Answer: b) How disordered and spread out energy and matter have become**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does a shuffled pack of cards end up jumbled, every time?
a) Cards dislike order  b) There are vastly more jumbled arrangements than the one ordered arrangement  c) Shuffling heats the cards  d) Gravity pulls the order out
**Answer: b) There are vastly more jumbled arrangements than the one ordered arrangement**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The second law of thermodynamics says the total entropy of the universe…
a) Always decreases  b) Stays exactly zero  c) Never decreases  d) Changes only inside fridges
**Answer: c) Never decreases**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A living body builds and keeps a great deal of internal order. How does it obey the second law?
a) It does not — living things are exempt  b) It takes in concentrated energy and throws out more disorder (heat and waste) than the order it keeps  c) It freezes entropy in place  d) It borrows entropy from the Moon
**Answer: b) It takes in concentrated energy and throws out more disorder (heat and waste) than the order it keeps**
