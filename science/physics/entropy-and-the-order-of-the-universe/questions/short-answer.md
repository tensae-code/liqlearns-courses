# Entropy and the order of the universe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give two everyday processes that run one way only, and state the reverse that never happens.
**Answer: Any two, e.g.: heat flows hot to cold — never cold to hot by itself; ink/perfume spreads through water/air — never gathers back into the drop or bottle by itself; ice melts in a warm room — the water never freezes itself in the warm room.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A drop of ink is evenly spread through a glass of water. Is its entropy higher or lower than when it was one drop — and why?
**Answer: Higher. The ink is spread out and disordered through the whole glass; the single drop was concentrated and ordered, which is the low-entropy state.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) State the second law of thermodynamics.
**Answer: In every real process, the total entropy of the universe never decreases — it grows, or in idealised cases stays the same.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A crystal grows in perfect order from a solution. Does this break the second law? Explain briefly.
**Answer: No. Growing, the crystal releases heat that spreads disorder into its surroundings; the surroundings' entropy grows by more than the crystal's falls, so the universe's total entropy still does not decrease.**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) What is the far-end picture the second law gives for the universe?
**Answer: Everything keeps spreading and cooling toward an even, uniform state — no hot spots or concentrated stores left to run engines or processes — the order of the universe runs down and the direction never reverses.**
