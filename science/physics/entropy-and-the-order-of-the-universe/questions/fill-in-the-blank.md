# Entropy and the order of the universe — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Heat flows from hot to cold; the reverse never happens by _____.
**Answer: itself**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The quantity that measures how spread out and disordered things have become is called _____.
**Answer: entropy**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A concentrated, ordered state has _____ entropy than a spread out, jumbled one.
**Answer: lower**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The second law says the total entropy of the universe never _____.
**Answer: decreases**
