# Video script — Entropy and the order of the universe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Entropy and the order of the universe**.

Ink spreads in water. It never climbs back into the drop.
Heat leaves the coffee. It never gathers back in.
Entropy is the measure of that spreading — of disorder. And the law of the universe says: total entropy never goes down. It grows, or it stays. Never down.
You can build order — a crystal, a house, a living body. But you always pay: more disorder spread around you than the order you built.
That is the order of the universe: everything, slowly, spreads out.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
