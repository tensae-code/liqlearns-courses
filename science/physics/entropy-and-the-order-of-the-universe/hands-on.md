# Hands-on — Entropy and the order of the universe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shuffle the pack
Order a pack of cards perfectly by suit and number. Photograph it in your mind: that is low entropy. Now shuffle it well. Say aloud: the ordered arrangement was one special arrangement among countless jumbled ones — shuffling did not aim at disorder; disorder is simply almost everywhere the pack can land.

## Ink in water
Drop one drop of ink or food colouring into a still glass of water. Do not stir. Watch it spread until the glass is even. Time it. Then answer honestly: how long would you wait for the drop to come back? Write the sentence the second law gives you: the entropy of ink plus water grew, and it will not fall by itself.

## Order at a price
Build one small order near you — stack books neatly, fold clothes, tidy a drawer. Then list what disorder you spread to do it: your body's heat, your breathing, the energy you spent, the wear on things. The lesson in your hands: local order rose, total entropy rose faster.
