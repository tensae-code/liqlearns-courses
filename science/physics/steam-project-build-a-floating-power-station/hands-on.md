# Hands-on — STEAM project — build a floating power station

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Build a platform from a sealed bottle raft or foam hull; load it with masses until it floats level and record the load. (2) Fit a small DC motor as generator to a water wheel or crank; spin it and measure the voltage; light an LED. (3) Float the combined station, generate on water, and write the one-page presentation: physics, design choices, measurements, one failure and its fix.
