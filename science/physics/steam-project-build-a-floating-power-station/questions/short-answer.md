# STEAM project — build a floating power station — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is the hull tested loaded before the generator is fitted?
**Answer: Because flotation and stability are the station's foundation — if the platform cannot carry the equipment's weight and stay upright, generation is moot; the fluids problem is solved first, on its own.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Trace the energy chain of the station in four steps.
**Answer: Moving water's kinetic energy → turbine/float mechanism's rotation → generator coil turning in a magnetic field (induction) → electrical energy in the circuit.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A station lights its LED on the bench crank but not on water. Give two design-level causes to investigate.
**Answer: The water drive delivers less turning (smaller force or slower motion) than the hand crank, or added friction/leakage in the linkage wastes it — the fix is in the drive gearing or the float mechanism, found by measuring the on-water voltage.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) What makes this a STEAM project rather than only a physics practical?
**Answer: The artefact must be designed and built (technology, engineering), presented with form and clarity (art), and sized and measured quantitatively (mathematics) — the physics alone is one of five joined strands.**
