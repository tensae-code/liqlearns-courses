# STEAM project — build a floating power station — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A floating platform floats when it displaces…
**Answer: a) its own weight of water  b) half its weight  c) no water  d) its volume in air**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Stability is helped by keeping the centre of mass…
**Answer: a) low and centred  b) high  c) at the edge  d) moving**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The generator turns motion into electricity by…
**Answer: a) electromagnetic induction  b) friction  c) chemical action  d) gravity**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A DC motor spun by a water wheel acts as…
**Answer: a) a generator  b) a battery  c) a resistor  d) a switch**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The project's presentation must include…
**Answer: a) the physics, the design choices and the measurements  b) only a drawing  c) only the cost  d) no results**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) STEAM joins science with…
**Answer: a) technology, engineering, art and mathematics  b) history only  c) languages  d) sport**
