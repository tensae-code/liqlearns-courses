# Discharge, lightning and grounding — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** What is lightning, in this unit's words?
**Answer: A giant electrical discharge from charge built up in clouds.**

**2.** What does a lightning rod do?
**Answer: It gives lightning's charge a safe path to the ground (earthing).**

**3.** A charged body touches a grounded wire. What happens?
**Answer: Its charge drains to earth — it discharges safely.**

**4.** Why does a charged object stay charged in dry air on an insulator?
**Answer: It has no escape path for its charge.**
