# Hands-on — Discharge, lightning and grounding

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Shuffle across a mat in socks and touch a metal handle: the tiny spark is a discharge you can feel. Lightning is that same event at sky scale.

## Apply it where you live
Explain how a fuel tanker or a building uses grounding for safety.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21899 — cloud-to-ground lightning path beside a building's lightning rod and earth wire] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
