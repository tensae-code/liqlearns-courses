# Video script — Discharge, lightning and grounding

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

A charged object stays charged until it is given an escape path.
A sudden, rapid transfer along that path is a discharge.
Lightning is the giant case: charge builds up within clouds and between cloud and ground until the air itself gives way and the charge flashes down in a spark kilometres long.
Grounding (earthing) is the controlled version: give charge a safe wire path to the earth — a building's lightning rod and earth wire — and it drains away without harm.

Remember: **Discharge is rapid charge transfer along an escape path.**
Remember: **Lightning is a giant discharge from charge built up in clouds.**
Remember: **Grounding drains charge safely to earth — lightning rods use it.**
