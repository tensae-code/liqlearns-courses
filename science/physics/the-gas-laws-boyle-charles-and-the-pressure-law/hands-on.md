# Hands-on — The gas laws — Boyle, Charles and the pressure law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Syringe squeeze
Block a syringe's tip at 30 cm³ of air. Push to 15 cm³ and hold: feel the doubled push-back. Say aloud: half the volume, twice the pressure — Boyle.

## Balloon in warm water
Put a half-inflated balloon in warm water and watch it swell; move it to cold water and watch it shrink. Say aloud: at the same pressure, volume follows the Kelvin temperature — Charles.
