# The gas laws — Boyle, Charles and the pressure law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Boyle's law holds when this quantity stays constant:
a) Volume  b) Pressure  c) Temperature  d) Mass of the container
**Answer: Temperature**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A gas's volume is halved at constant temperature. Its pressure…
a) Halves  b) Stays the same  c) Doubles  d) Quadruples
**Answer: Doubles**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Charles's law links volume and temperature at constant…
a) Volume  b) Pressure  c) Mass  d) Density
**Answer: Pressure**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Gas-law temperatures must be measured in…
a) Celsius  b) Fahrenheit  c) Kelvin  d) Any scale
**Answer: Kelvin**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The pressure law says that at constant volume, pressure is proportional to…
a) Volume  b) Kelvin temperature  c) Mass  d) Area
**Answer: Kelvin temperature**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The combined gas law is…
a) P₁V₁ = P₂V₂ only  b) V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂ only  c) P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂  d) P = V × T
**Answer: P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂**
