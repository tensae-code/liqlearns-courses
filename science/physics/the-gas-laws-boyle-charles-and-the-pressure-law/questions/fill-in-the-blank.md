# The gas laws — Boyle, Charles and the pressure law — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) At constant temperature, pressure × _____ is constant.
**Answer: volume**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) 27 °C is _____ K.
**Answer: 300**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Charles's law: at constant pressure, _____ / T is constant.
**Answer: volume**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) In the combined law, the quantity that must never be in Celsius is _____.
**Answer: temperature**
