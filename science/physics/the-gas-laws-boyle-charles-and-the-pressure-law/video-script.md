Today: **The gas laws — Boyle, Charles and the pressure law**.

Trap air in a syringe and squeeze: less room, harder push-back. That is Boyle's law — at steady temperature, pressure times volume is constant. Heat a gas free to expand and it grows: Charles's law, volume proportional to Kelvin temperature. Heat a gas that cannot expand and its pressure climbs instead: the pressure law. And when everything changes at once, one combined law — P₁V₁ over T₁ equals P₂V₂ over T₂ — does the whole job, always in kelvin.
