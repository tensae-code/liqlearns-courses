# Resistors in series and parallel — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. Series resistors share current and add up; parallel resistors share voltage and combine reciprocally — with brightness comparisons of lamps in each arrangement worked as examples.**
**Answer: True**

**2. parallel resistors share current and add up; parallel resistors share voltage and combine reciprocally — with brightness comparisons of lamps in each arrangement worked as examples.**
**Answer: False**
