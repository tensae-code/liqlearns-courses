# Hands-on — Renewable energy — evaluating energy resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run the four tests on your own street
Walk your street or compound with a notebook. List every energy flow you can actually see or feel: sun on roofs, wind in trees, water moving anywhere, plants and garden waste, shade and shelter. For each flow, write one line per test — what energy could it return, how steady is it across a day, what would harvesting it take, what would it leave? Then pick the one flow your street is richest in and say aloud: "Here, the resource to evaluate first is ___ because ___." Finish by naming the strongest point against your own pick — evaluation means arguing both sides with evidence.
