# Renewable energy — evaluating energy resources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A resource counts as renewable when…
a) It is modern  b) Nature refills it as fast as, or faster than, it is used  c) It produces electricity  d) It is found underground
**Answer: b) Nature refills it as fast as, or faster than, it is used**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is a stock that finishes, not a flow?
a) Wind  b) Sunlight  c) Coal  d) Falling water
**Answer: c) Coal — formed over millions of years and gone once burned**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Capacity factor means…
a) The price per unit of electricity  b) Average output ÷ rated (best possible) output  c) The size of the plant  d) The number of windy days
**Answer: b) Average output ÷ rated (best possible) output**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A forest cut much faster than it regrows is, by the refill-rate test…
a) Still fully renewable  b) Being mined like a stock — it fails the test while the cutting lasts  c) Geothermal  d) A capacity factor
**Answer: b) Being mined like a stock — it fails the test while the cutting lasts**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The same wind turbines earn their keep on a windy ridge but not in a sheltered valley. This shows evaluation verdicts are…
a) Random  b) Local — a match between resource, place and job  c) Decided by colour  d) Permanent once made
**Answer: b) Local — a match between resource, place and job**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A solar farm's power is needed in the evening, after sunset. The steadiness problem is answered by…
a) Bigger panels alone  b) Storage (batteries, pumped storage) and grid links that carry power across places and times  c) Burning the panels  d) Ignoring it
**Answer: b) Storage (batteries, pumped storage) and grid links that carry power across places and times**
