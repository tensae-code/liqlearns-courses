# Renewable energy — evaluating energy resources — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Energy sources like sunlight and wind, which arrive continuously, are called _____; coal and oil are stocks.
**Answer: flows**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Average output divided by rated output is the capacity _____.
**Answer: factor**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Heat drawn from inside the Earth is _____ energy.
**Answer: geothermal**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Batteries and pumped storage answer a flow's weakness in _____ — its coming and going.
**Answer: steadiness**
