---

Today: **renewable energy — and how to judge it like a physicist**. Some energy arrives as a flow that refills itself — sunlight, wind, falling water, the Earth's heat, regrowing plants. Some sits in a stock that finishes — coal, oil, gas. But "renewable" is only the first word. A good judge runs four tests: how much energy comes back for the energy put in, how steady the flow is, what land and materials it takes, and what waste it leaves. Then the verdict is always local: the right resource, in the right place, for the right job — argued with numbers.
