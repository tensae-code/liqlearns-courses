Today: **Newton's second law, turning**. Resultant torque equals moment of inertia times angular acceleration: τ = Iα. Inertia here is not just mass — it is mass times distance squared from the axis, so rim mass rules. Add the twins — energy ½Iω², power τω — keep your angles in radians, and every spinning problem is the roundabout: same push, different spread of mass, different spin-up.
