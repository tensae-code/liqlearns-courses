# Rotational dynamics — torque, inertia and angular acceleration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Rotational dynamics' central law is…
a) τ = Iω  b) τ = Iα  c) F = Iα  d) τ = mα
**Answer: b) τ = Iα**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) With zero resultant torque on a spinning body…
a) It stops at once  b) α = 0, so ω stays constant  c) I doubles  d) It must fall over
**Answer: b) α = 0, so ω stays constant**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A hoop and a solid disc have the same mass and radius. The hoop's moment of inertia is…
a) Half the disc's  b) Equal to the disc's  c) Twice the disc's (mR² against ½mR²)  d) Zero
**Answer: c) Twice the disc's (mR² against ½mR²)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Moving mass closer to the axis…
a) Increases I  b) Decreases I, because I counts mr²  c) Leaves I unchanged  d) Increases the torque
**Answer: b) Decreases I, because I counts mr²**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Rotational kinetic energy is…
a) Iω  b) ½Iω²  c) τω  d) Iα
**Answer: b) ½Iω²**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In rotational calculations, angles must be measured in…
a) Degrees  b) Radians  c) Revolutions only  d) Minutes
**Answer: b) Radians**
