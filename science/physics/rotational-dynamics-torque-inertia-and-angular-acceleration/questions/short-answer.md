# Rotational dynamics — torque, inertia and angular acceleration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A torque of 12 N m acts on a body with I = 3 kg m². Find α.
**Answer: α = τ/I = 12/3 = 4 rad/s².**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is a flywheel built as a heavy rim rather than a solid block?
**Answer: Concentrating mass at large r maximises I for its mass, so it stores the most rotational energy and resists changes in ω most strongly.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) State the four-step order for a rotational-dynamics problem.
**Answer: Fix the axis and find I; find the resultant torque about that axis; apply τ = Iα and the rotation equations; check units/energy — angles in radians.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A disc's I is 2 kg m² and it spins at 10 rad/s. Calculate its rotational kinetic energy.
**Answer: KE = ½Iω² = ½ × 2 × 100 = 100 J.**
