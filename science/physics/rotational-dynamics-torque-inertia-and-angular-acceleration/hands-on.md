# Hands-on — Rotational dynamics — torque, inertia and angular acceleration

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Race the hoop and the disc
You need a hoop (a bicycle wheel, hula hoop or rolled card ring) and a solid disc or wide tin lid of similar size and mass, plus a gentle ramp (a tilted board). Release both from rest at the top, together, without pushing, and watch which reaches the bottom first — the disc wins, because less of its mass sits at full radius, so its I is smaller and gravity's torque spins it up faster. Time three runs each, record them in a table, and write the τ = Iα explanation of the result in your own words.
