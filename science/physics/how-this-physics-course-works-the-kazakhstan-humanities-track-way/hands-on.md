# How this physics course works — the Kazakhstan humanities-track way — hands-on

Do this before reading the lesson. Pick one discovery from this year's physics. Name one writer, one artist and one ruler of its epoch. Work with your hands and your eyes first: gather the ordinary items the question needs, make the measurement or the comparison yourself, and write down what you actually found — not what you expected. Then read the lesson and compare: where your finding and the lesson agree, you have learned it twice; where they differ, the difference is the lesson. Keep your numbers — the questions at the end will ask for them.
