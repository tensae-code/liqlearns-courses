# How this physics course works — the Kazakhstan humanities-track way — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — the humanities track learns physics by rebuilding the world a discovery was made in: for each great step, you reconstruct its epoch — its writers, artists and rulers — keep a project growing all year, and defend it at the end, so the physics is remembered as a human story with the equations inside it — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
