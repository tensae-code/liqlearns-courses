# How this physics course works — the Kazakhstan humanities-track way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The humanities track teaches physics through the scientific method plus…
a) Sport  b) History  c) Music only  d) Memorisation
**Answer: b) History**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) To rebuild the epoch of a discovery you gather its…
a) Equations only  b) Writers, artists and rulers  c) Textbook prices  d) Exam dates
**Answer: b) Writers, artists and rulers**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The year-long project grows…
a) In one evening  b) Lesson by lesson, all year  c) Only in the holidays  d) By copying a model
**Answer: b) Lesson by lesson, all year**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The project reconstructs…
a) The teacher's notes  b) The historical-physical picture of the world  c) A single experiment  d) The school timetable
**Answer: b) The historical-physical picture of the world**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The course ends by…
a) A multiple-choice test only  b) Defending the project as coursework  c) Burning the project  d) A dictation
**Answer: b) Defending the project as coursework**
