# Hands-on — Experiment: series vs parallel circuits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the practical
1. A bench experiment measuring currents and voltages around series and parallel resistor circuits and checking that parallel branch voltages match the supply and series voltages add up to it.

Work with a partner, write down what you measured or observed, and say what the result proves. If apparatus is missing, walk through the steps with drawings and say what you would expect to see.

## Build the loop
With a cell, wires and a small lamp, build one complete loop so the lamp lights. Break the loop — the lamp dies. Draw the circuit and mark where the ammeter and voltmeter would go.

## See it and say it
Study this lesson's picture — a circuit board with ammeter and voltmeter connected — and name every part of it out loud. Then close the picture and draw it from memory; check your drawing against the lesson.

## Teach it back
Explain this lesson to someone at home using one thing in the room as your example. Their questions show you which part to re-read.
