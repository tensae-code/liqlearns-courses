# Calorimetry — mixing hot and cold — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about calorimetry is correct?**
a) Hot and cold bodies mixed in an insulated calorimeter exchange heat until they settle together: heat lost = heat…  b) A slide-wire potentiometer compares and measures a cell's emf without drawing current from it, balancing the unknown…  c) The inward acceleration needs an inward net force — tension, friction or gravity supplies it in different settings; the…
**Answer: a) Hot and cold bodies mixed in an insulated calorimeter exchange heat until they settle together: heat lost =…**

**2. Hot and cold bodies mixed in an insulated calorimeter exchange heat until they settle together: heat lost = heat gained, the equation that finds unknown ______s, masses and final temperatures; experiments with the calorimeter are set.**
a) specific heat  b) voltage  c) component
**Answer: a) specific heat**
