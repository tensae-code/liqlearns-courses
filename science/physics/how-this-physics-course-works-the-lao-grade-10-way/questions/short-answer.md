# How this Physics course works — the Lao Grade 10 (ມ.5) way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the seven parts in order.
**Answer: I Kinematics; II Dynamics; III Different Types of Motion; IV Statics; V Work and Energy; VI Momentum; VII Change of Matter due to Heat.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What does the teacher guide add to the student book, and on what footing is it cited in this course?
**Answer: Per-part concept inventories, teaching notes and per-lesson answer keys, mirroring the student book lesson for lesson. It is cited as teacher-guide evidence — it shows how the course is taught; the student book carries the counted teaching.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why is Lesson 5 described as Laos' own way of teaching?
**Answer: Because it makes problem-solving itself the lesson: instead of leaving worked methods scattered as examples and setting solving as homework, the book devotes a full lesson inside Part I to worked examples of solving motion problems, taught in class time.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) How does the book open, and why does that matter?
**Answer: It opens with position, motion vs rest and reference frames — defining where a thing is before any formula — so every later quantity (velocity, acceleration) is built on a stated frame of reference.**
