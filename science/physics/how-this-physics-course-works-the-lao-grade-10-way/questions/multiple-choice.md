# How this Physics course works — the Lao Grade 10 (ມ.5) way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Lao Grade 10 Physics course is organised as…
**Answer: a) 7 parts of numbered lessons, 28 lessons in all  b) 28 unrelated chapters  c) 3 terms with no lesson numbers  d) one long mechanics text, then an exam**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Part III of the course is unusual because it…
**Answer: a) re-teaches motion type by type — horizontal, vertical, incline, systems, throwing, circular  b) teaches heat early  c) contains only experiments  d) is written by students**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Every Lao lesson is built in the shape…
**Answer: a) concept → worked examples → exercises  b) exercises first, concept last  c) test, then answers only  d) story, then song**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The teacher guide adds, for each part, a…
**Answer: a) concept inventory naming the part's concepts  b) second student book  c) set of exam marks  d) list of page errors**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Lesson 5 of the course is…
**Answer: a) a whole lesson of worked examples of solving motion problems  b) the first heat lesson  c) a holiday break  d) the course's only test**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Teaching problem-solving as a lesson, as Laos does in Lesson 5, means…
**Answer: a) the solving method is shown whole, in teaching time, as content to learn  b) problems are only ever homework  c) answers are given without methods  d) learners skip the exercises**
