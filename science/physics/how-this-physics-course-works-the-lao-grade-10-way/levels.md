# How this Physics course works — the Lao Grade 10 (ມ.5) way — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — The Lao Grade 10 course: 7 parts / 28 lessons — Kinematics, Dynamics, Types of Motion, Statics, Work and Energy, Momentum, Heat; Every lesson is built concept → worked examples → exercises; Lesson 5 is a whole lesson of worked problem-solving — solving taught as content — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
