# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Lao Grade 10 (ມ.5) way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map your own course the Lao way
Take any textbook you own and draw its course map the Lao way: numbered parts, numbered lessons inside them, one line per lesson. Then check its lesson shape against the Lao shape — concept, worked examples, exercises — and mark any lesson that is only worked examples. The comparison makes the Lao choices (a types-of-motion part, a worked-problems lesson) stand out against your own book's choices.
