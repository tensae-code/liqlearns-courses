# Free-body diagrams — drawing all the forces on an object — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A free-body diagram shows forces…
**Answer: a) acting ON the chosen object only  b) the object exerts on others  c) inside the object  d) in the whole scene**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The chosen object is drawn…
**Answer: a) isolated, as a box or dot, away from its surroundings  b) inside its scene  c) three times  d) not at all**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Weight is drawn…
**Answer: a) from the centre of the body, straight down  b) along the surface  c) at the contact point, upward  d) along the rope**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Tension arrows point…
**Answer: a) along the rope, away from the body  b) into the body  c) downward always  d) opposite the rope**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) If the arrows balance in every direction, the body…
**Answer: a) has no acceleration  b) must be moving fast  c) falls  d) gains mass**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The working order in a force problem is…
**Answer: a) diagram, equations, numbers  b) numbers, diagram, equations  c) answers first  d) equations only**
