# Free-body diagrams — drawing all the forces on an object — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The diagram shows forces acting _____ the chosen object.
**Answer: on**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Weight acts from the body's _____, straight down.
**Answer: centre**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Friction acts _____ the surface, opposing slip.
**Answer: along**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A leftover resultant means acceleration by F = _____.
**Answer: ma**
