# Hands-on — Free-body diagrams — drawing all the forces on an object

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Take five objects in the room — resting, hanging, sliding, leaning — and draw each one's free-body diagram; compare with a partner and hunt for missing or extra arrows. (2) Hold a mass from two spring balances at angles; draw the diagram first, then check your predicted balance against the readings. (3) Redraw one diagram choosing a different body (the rope instead of the mass) and list what changed.
