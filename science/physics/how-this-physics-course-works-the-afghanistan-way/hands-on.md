# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Afghanistan way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map the four years
On one large sheet, draw four columns headed Grade 9, Grade 10, Grade 11, Grade 12. From this lesson only, write each year's chapters into its column — no books open. Then draw one arrow across the top: 'light and electricity' over Grades 9–10, 'mechanics' over Grade 11. Show the sheet to a companion and have them find, from your map alone, where mirrors live and where Newton's laws live. If they find both in under a minute, the map — and the Afghanistan way — has landed.
