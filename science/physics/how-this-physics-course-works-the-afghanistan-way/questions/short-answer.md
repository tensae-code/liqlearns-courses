# How this Physics course works — the Afghanistan way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the four chapters of the Grade 9 book in order.
**Answer: One-dimensional motion; oscillations, waves and sound; current electricity; electromagnetism.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is reversed in Afghanistan's order, compared with most countries in this library?
**Answer: Afghanistan teaches the full optics + electricity + magnetism year (Grade 10) before the mechanics year (Grade 11); most countries teach mechanics first.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Which grade carries Kirchhoff's laws in Afghanistan's course, and in which strand?
**Answer: Grade 10, in the circuits strand (Ch. 7, Circuits and Current) — in the electricity year, before mechanics.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) What two closing chapters does Grade 12 give its own full chapters to?
**Answer: Atomic physics (Ch. 5) and nuclear physics (Ch. 6).**
