# How this Physics course works — the Afghanistan way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Afghanistan's first physics book is the…
a) Grade 9 book  b) Grade 10 book  c) Grade 7 book  d) Grade 12 book
**Answer: a) Grade 9 book**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The Grade 9 book closes with…
a) electromagnetism — dynamo and doorbell  b) nuclear physics  c) relativity  d) astrophysics
**Answer: a) electromagnetism — dynamo and doorbell**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Grade 10 opens with a chapter titled…
a) “What is physics?”  b) “Forces and motion”  c) “The atom”  d) “Energy”
**Answer: a) “What is physics?”**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In Afghanistan's order, lenses and the microscope come in…
a) Grade 10 — before the mechanics year  b) Grade 11 — inside the mechanics year  c) Grade 9  d) Grade 12 only
**Answer: a) Grade 10 — before the mechanics year**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Newton's laws are carried by Afghanistan's…
a) Grade 11 book  b) Grade 10 book  c) Grade 9 book  d) Grade 12 book
**Answer: a) Grade 11 book**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Grade 12 closes the course with, among other strands…
a) atomic physics and nuclear physics  b) mechanics of machines  c) geometrical optics only  d) electronics
**Answer: a) atomic physics and nuclear physics**
