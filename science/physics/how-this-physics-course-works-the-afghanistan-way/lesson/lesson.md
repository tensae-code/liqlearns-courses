# How this Physics course works — the Afghanistan way

## Do it first
Before you read: most countries in this course teach mechanics first — forces and motion — and leave light and circuits for later. Afghanistan does it the other way round. Which year carries the mirrors and lenses? Which year carries Newton's laws? And what opens the very first physics chapter of Grade 10? Sort the four years here.

## Sub lesson 1 — Grade 9 — the first physics book

Afghanistan's physics begins in Grade 9 with one-dimensional motion (position, average velocity, acceleration — with Pythagoras used to find displacement), then oscillations, waves and sound in one chapter, then a long current-electricity chapter — current, potential difference, resistance, Ohm's law applied, joining resistors, the battery, and a full section on protecting against electrical hazards — closing with electromagnetism: the magnetic effect of a current, the galvanometer, induction, the electromagnet, and the bicycle dynamo and electric doorbell as worked applications.

## Sub lesson 2 — Grade 10 — light and electricity before mechanics

Grade 10 opens with an explicit “What is physics?” chapter — introduction, brief history and the language of physics — then measurement, and then goes straight to light: properties of light, reflection and spherical mirrors, refraction, prisms and the rainbow, lenses with the eye, the camera and the microscope. The year continues with static electricity (charge, field, potential, capacitors), circuits (resistance, EMF, Kirchhoff's laws), magnetism, and electromagnetic induction with alternating current — RL, RC and LC circuits, transformers and generators. Mechanics is deliberately NOT in this book.

## Sub lesson 3 — Grade 11 — the mechanics year — and the Grade 12 finale

Grade 11 is a full year of mechanics and fluids: mechanical equilibrium (force as a vector, torque, couples, the conditions of equilibrium, taught with levers, cranes and building machines), one- and two-dimensional motion, Newton's laws with friction, gravitation, lift and satellite orbits, work, energy and power, linear momentum and collisions, fluids at rest (pressure, the hydraulic press, Archimedes) and moving fluids (continuity, Bernoulli, the Venturi tube, viscosity). Grade 12 then closes the course with oscillations and simple harmonic motion, waves and their motion (including diffraction and polarisation of light), mechanical and thermal properties of matter (including Wien's law and the Stefan–Boltzmann law), a full atomic-physics chapter and a full nuclear chapter.

## The key ideas

- Afghanistan teaches optics + electricity + magnetism (Grade 10) BEFORE the mechanics year (Grade 11) — the reverse of most countries
- Grade 10 opens with an explicit “What is physics?” chapter before measurement
- Grade 9 is the first physics book (motion, waves and sound, current electricity, electromagnetism); Grade 12 closes with oscillations, waves, thermal properties, atomic and nuclear physics
