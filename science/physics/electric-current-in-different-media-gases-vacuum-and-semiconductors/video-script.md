# Video script — Electric current in different media — gases, vacuum and semiconductors

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **one current, three carriers**. In a gas, a strong field smashes electrons into molecules until ionisation makes an avalanche — that is a discharge, and lightning is its giant. In a vacuum, a hot cathode boils electrons off and they cross emptiness itself. In a semiconductor, electrons and holes share the carrying, doping chooses which one leads, and where the two kinds meet you get the diode. Always ask: what is moving, and what set it free?
