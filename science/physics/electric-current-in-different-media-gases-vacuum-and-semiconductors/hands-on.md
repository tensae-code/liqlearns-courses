# Hands-on — Electric current in different media — gases, vacuum and semiconductors

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Carriers on cards
Take three large cards and label them GAS, VACUUM and SEMICONDUCTOR. For each card, write in your own words: what the carriers are, what sets them free, and one device or event that uses the current. Then close the lesson and, from memory, sort six shuffled statements — 'ionisation by collision', 'thermionic emission', 'electrons and holes', 'lightning', 'old television tube', 'diode junction' — two to each card, defending each sort aloud. A statement you cannot defend goes back to the lesson text until you can.
