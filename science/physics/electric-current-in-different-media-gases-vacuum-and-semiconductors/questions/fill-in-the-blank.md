# Electric current in different media — gases, vacuum and semiconductors — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A conducting gas carrying a current, often with a glow, is called a gas _____.
**Answer: discharge**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Lightning is a gas discharge at _____ scale.
**Answer: sky**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Electrons escape a hot cathode by _____ emission.
**Answer: thermionic**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Where n-type and p-type material meet, the junction that passes current easily one way is the _____.
**Answer: diode**
