# Electric current in different media — gases, vacuum and semiconductors — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A gas at ordinary pressure does not conduct because…
a) It has no atoms  b) Its molecules are neutral, with almost no free charge carriers  c) It is too cold to melt  d) Electrons cannot move in air at all
**Answer: b) Its molecules are neutral, with almost no free charge carriers**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In ionisation by collision, the gas is made to conduct when…
a) The gas is heated until it melts  b) Accelerated electrons strike molecules and knock electrons off them, multiplying the carriers  c) The electrodes touch  d) The gas is compressed into a liquid
**Answer: b) Accelerated electrons strike molecules and knock electrons off them, multiplying the carriers**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Thermionic emission is…
a) Electrons knocked off gas molecules  b) Electrons escaping from a hot metal surface into the space around it  c) Holes moving through silicon  d) Light emitted by a discharge
**Answer: b) Electrons escaping from a hot metal surface into the space around it**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The carriers of a current in a vacuum are…
a) Ions of the vacuum  b) Electrons supplied by the hot cathode, crossing empty space  c) Holes only  d) Protons
**Answer: b) Electrons supplied by the hot cathode, crossing empty space**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In a semiconductor, the two kinds of carrier are…
a) Protons and neutrons  b) Electrons and holes  c) Ions and atoms  d) Molecules and crystals
**Answer: b) Electrons and holes**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Doping silicon with atoms that have one fewer outer electron makes…
a) n-type material, carried by electrons  b) p-type material, carried by holes  c) A metal  d) An insulator for ever
**Answer: b) p-type material, carried by holes**
