# Hands-on — Tension force in ropes and connectors — practical

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Hang masses from a rope with a spring balance in series at two different points; record both readings for three masses. (2) Run the rope over a rough edge and repeat — record how the readings now differ. (3) Join two dynamics trolleys with a rope over a pulley, predict the tension from the masses, then measure it with a balance in the line.
