# Tension force in ropes and connectors — practical — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Tension is the _____ transmitted along a rope.
**Answer: pull**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A rope can pull but never _____.
**Answer: push**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Tension is _____ along a light, inextensible rope.
**Answer: uniform**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Bodies joined by one rope share one _____.
**Answer: tension**
