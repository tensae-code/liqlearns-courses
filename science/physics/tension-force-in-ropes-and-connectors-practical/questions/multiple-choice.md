# Tension force in ropes and connectors — practical — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Tension is…
**Answer: a) the pull transmitted along a rope or connector  b) a push from a rope  c) the rope's weight  d) friction in a rope**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A rope can exert…
**Answer: a) a pull only, never a push  b) pushes only  c) both equally  d) neither**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Balances at several points of a light rope holding a mass read…
**Answer: a) the same value  b) decreasing values  c) zero  d) random values**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A balance reads tension when placed…
**Answer: a) in series with the rope  b) beside the rope  c) on the mass  d) under the support**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Bodies joined by one light rope share…
**Answer: a) one tension in their diagrams  b) one mass  c) no forces  d) different tensions always**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Readings along a rope differ when the rope…
**Answer: a) has mass or runs over a rough surface  b) is light and smooth-running  c) is short  d) is vertical**
