# Current as moving charge — I = nAvq — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Current equals charge passing per unit ___.
**Answer:** time

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The carriers' slow average motion is their ___ speed.
**Answer:** drift

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) I = nAvq: raising A at fixed I lowers ___.
**Answer:** v (drift speed)

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Copper's carrier density n is about 10²⁸–10²⁹ per cubic ___.
**Answer:** metre
