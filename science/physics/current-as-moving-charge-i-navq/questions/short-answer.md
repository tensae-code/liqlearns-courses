# Current as moving charge — I = nAvq — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Write the meaning of each symbol in I = nAvq.
**Answer:** n carriers per unit volume; A cross-sectional area; v drift speed; q charge per carrier.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Show the counting that leads to the equation.
**Answer:** Carriers in AvΔt number nAvΔt; their charge is nAvqΔt; dividing charge by time gives I = nAvq.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is current called a rate, not a substance?
**Answer:** It measures charge passing per unit time — nothing accumulates or is used up.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Name an instrument that trades on carriers drifting at v.
**Answer:** The Hall probe (and the velocity selector).
