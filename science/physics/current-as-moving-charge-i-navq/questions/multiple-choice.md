# Current as moving charge — I = nAvq — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In I = nAvq, n counts…
a) Newtons per volt  b) Carriers per unit volume  c) Wires in parallel  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Carriers per unit volume**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The charge counted in time Δt fills the volume…
a) The whole wire  b) A × vΔt — a length vΔt of wire  c) Zero volume  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) A × vΔt — a length vΔt of wire**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Drift speeds in household wires are of order…
a) The speed of light  b) Fractions of a millimetre per second  c) Kilometres per second  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Fractions of a millimetre per second**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A lamp lights instantly because…
a) Electrons race from the switch  b) The field is set up along the wire at once, moving all carriers together  c) The filament pre-heats  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) The field is set up along the wire at once, moving all carriers together**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Doubling a wire's area at fixed current makes the drift speed…
a) Double  b) Halve  c) Unchanged  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Halve**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Materials differ in the equation mainly through…
a) The colour of insulation  b) n — their carrier density  c) Length alone  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) n — their carrier density**
