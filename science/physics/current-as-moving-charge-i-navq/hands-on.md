# Hands-on — Current as moving charge — I = nAvq

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Compute before believing
Compute before believing: take a copper wire of cross-section 1 mm² carrying 1 A, with n ≈ 8.5 × 10²⁸ m⁻³ and q = e. Find the drift speed, then time how long one electron would take to travel a metre of the wire — and reconcile that with a lamp's instant response, in writing.
