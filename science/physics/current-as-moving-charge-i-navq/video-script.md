# Video script — Current as moving charge — I = nAvq

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **a current is charge on the move, and its size is set by how many carriers pass**. In time Δt, every carrier within a length vΔt of a cross-section drifts past it: volume AvΔt, carriers nAvΔt, charge nAvqΔt. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — counting charge past a point; why drift is slow yet light is instant; reading circuits with the carrier equation — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
