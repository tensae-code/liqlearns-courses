# Video script — Centripetal force at work — banking, dryers and separators

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **wherever something turns, a real inward force must supply the centripetal pull — and Punjab's Grade 9 book shows the same supply at work in three machines of everyday life**. On a flat curve, friction alone must pull the car toward the centre. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — banked roads — the slope lends a hand; the dryer — what cannot turn, leaves; the cream separator — turning sorts by mass — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
