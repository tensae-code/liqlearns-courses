# Hands-on — Centripetal force at work — banking, dryers and separators

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spin a bucket of water in a vertical circle (outside, over grass)
Spin a bucket of water in a vertical circle (outside, over grass): fast enough, the water stays in at the top — the bucket's base supplies the centripetal force. Slow down until it spills and note the speed where supply fails. Then spin a salad spinner with wet leaves and watch the water leave tangentially, exactly as in the dryer.
