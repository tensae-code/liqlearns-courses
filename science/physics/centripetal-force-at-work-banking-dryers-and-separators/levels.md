# Centripetal force at work — banking, dryers and separators — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — On a flat curve, friction alone must pull the car toward the centre; In a washing-machine dryer the drum spins fast and its wall pushes the clothes inward, supplying their centripetal force; Spin milk in a separator and every part is pulled toward the centre with the force its circular path demands — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
