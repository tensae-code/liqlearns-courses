# Centripetal force at work — banking, dryers and separators — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) On a banked road the ___ reaction tilts inward and helps pull the car to the centre.
**Answer:** normal

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Drops leave the dryer along the ___ to the circle.
**Answer:** tangent

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In the separator, ___ gathers near the axis.
**Answer:** cream

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The centre-pulling force is called the ___ force.
**Answer:** centripetal
