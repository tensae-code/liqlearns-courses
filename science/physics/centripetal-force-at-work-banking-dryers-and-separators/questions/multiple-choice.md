# Centripetal force at work — banking, dryers and separators — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Banking a road helps because…
a) It makes the road shorter  b) The tilted normal reaction gains an inward component  c) It removes gravity  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) The tilted normal reaction gains an inward component**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) At the design speed on a banked bend…
a) The car needs maximum friction  b) The car needs no friction to turn  c) The car cannot turn  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) The car needs no friction to turn**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Water leaves a dryer drum because at the holes…
a) Water is repelled by metal  b) No wall supplies the inward force, so drops fly off tangentially  c) The drum heats the water  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) No wall supplies the inward force, so drops fly off tangentially**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The clothes stay in the dryer because…
a) They are glued  b) The drum wall keeps supplying their centripetal force  c) They are heavier than water in the drum's frame  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) The drum wall keeps supplying their centripetal force**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A cream separator sorts milk by…
a) Colour  b) Density — heavier skim milk takes the outer path, lighter cream the axis  c) Temperature  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Density — heavier skim milk takes the outer path, lighter cream the axis**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) All three applications run on the same bookkeeping: the inward force must supply…
a) mg only  b) mv²/r for the turning mass  c) Zero force  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) mv²/r for the turning mass**
