# Centripetal force at work — banking, dryers and separators — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What supplies the centripetal force on a flat (unbanked) curve?
**Answer:** Friction between tyres and road, alone.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is drying described as centripetal force failing on purpose?
**Answer:** The water is denied the inward force at the holes, so it leaves tangentially while the clothes are kept turning.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What property of the milk parts does the separator sort by?
**Answer:** Density (mass for the path each part can be held to).

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) State the centripetal force a mass m needs at speed v on a circle of radius r.
**Answer:** F = mv²/r, directed toward the centre.
