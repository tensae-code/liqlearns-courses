# Centripetal force at work — banking, dryers and separators

## Do it first
Spin a bucket of water in a vertical circle (outside, over grass) — begin with the hands-on opening in hands-on.md before reading on. This lesson is the story of that activity.

## Sub lesson 1 — Banked roads — the slope lends a hand

On a flat curve, friction alone must pull the car toward the centre. Bank the road — raise its outer edge — and the normal reaction tilts inward, so a component of it points to the centre and helps supply mv²/r. At the banking's design speed the car can take the bend with no friction at all; the road itself does part of the centre-pulling.

## Sub lesson 2 — The dryer — what cannot turn, leaves

In a washing-machine dryer the drum spins fast and its wall pushes the clothes inward, supplying their centripetal force. The water drops in the clothes need the same inward pull — but where a hole in the drum faces them, no wall pushes, so they fly out straight along the tangent. Drying is centripetal force failing, on purpose, for the water only.

## Sub lesson 3 — The cream separator — turning sorts by mass

Spin milk in a separator and every part is pulled toward the centre with the force its circular path demands. The heavier skim milk can be held to the wider, outer path; the lighter cream gathers nearer the axis. Turning has sorted the mixture by density — the same mv²/r bookkeeping, used as a sieve.

## The key ideas

- On a flat curve, friction alone must pull the car toward the centre.
- In a washing-machine dryer the drum spins fast and its wall pushes the clothes inward, supplying their centripetal force.
- Spin milk in a separator and every part is pulled toward the centre with the force its circular path demands.

## Say it to your companion
Tell your companion the lesson's one thing in your own words: wherever something turns, a real inward force must supply the centripetal pull — and Punjab's Grade 9 book shows the same supply at work in three machines of everyday life: the banked road, the washing-machine dryer and the cream separator. If your companion can work the Do It Yourself items without looking back, the lesson has landed.
