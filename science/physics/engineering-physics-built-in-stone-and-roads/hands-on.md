# Hands-on — Engineering — physics built in stone and roads

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spot the physics
Walk through a clinic, a workshop or a market in your mind and list five places physics is at work in another field — then name the field for each.

## See it and say it
Study this lesson's picture — the obelisk, the rock church and the interchange as a three-photo strip — and name every part of it out loud. Then close the picture and draw it from memory; check your drawing against the lesson.

## Teach it back
Explain this lesson to someone at home using one thing in the room as your example. Their questions show you which part to re-read.
