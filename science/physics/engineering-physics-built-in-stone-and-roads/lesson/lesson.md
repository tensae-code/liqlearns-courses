## Physics and engineering — with Ethiopian monuments

Civil engineering is illustrated from Ethiopia itself: the Axum obelisks, Lalibela's Bete Giorgis rock church, and the Gotera interchange road in Addis Ababa; then mechanical, electrical and chemical engineering each get their physics basis, and measurement accuracy in civil work is singled out.
