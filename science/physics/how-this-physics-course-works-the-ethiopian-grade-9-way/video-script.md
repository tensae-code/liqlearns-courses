# Video script — How this Physics course works — the Ethiopian Grade 9 way

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **How this Physics course works — the Ethiopian Grade 9 way**.

Seven stops. One: physics in human society — physics is already in your life.
Two: physical quantities — measure properly.
Three: motion in a straight line — describe how things move.
Four: force, work, energy and power — pushes and what they do.
Five: simple machines — helpers that multiply force.
Six: oscillation and sound — swings, vibrations, the kirar.
Seven: temperature — hot, cold, and measuring them.
Every unit starts the same way: brainstorm from your own life first. Think first. Then learn.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
