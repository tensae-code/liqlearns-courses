# How this Physics course works — the Ethiopian Grade 9 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many units does the Grade 9 Physics course have, and what is the last one?
a) Five — Basics of Electronics  b) Six — Geometrical Optics  c) Seven — Temperature and Thermometry  d) Seven — Magnetism
**Answer: c) Seven — Temperature and Thermometry**

**2.** Which unit comes first in the Grade 9 course?
a) Physical Quantities  b) Physics and Human Society  c) Motion in a Straight Line  d) Simple Machines
**Answer: b) Physics and Human Society**

**3.** How does every unit in this book open?
a) With a test  b) With Brainstorming Questions drawn from daily life  c) With a formula list  d) With a Unit Review
**Answer: b) With Brainstorming Questions drawn from daily life**

**4.** Which pair is in the book's order?
a) Simple Machines before Physical Quantities  b) Motion in a Straight Line before Force, Work, Energy and Power  c) Temperature before Motion  d) Sound Wave before Simple Machines
**Answer: b) Motion in a Straight Line before Force, Work, Energy and Power**

**5.** Which of these is one of the book's Ethiopian anchors?
a) The kirar among oscillating instruments  b) A snow plough  c) A lighthouse  d) A space rocket launch
**Answer: a) The kirar among oscillating instruments**

**6.** What closes every unit?
a) A brainstorm only  b) Nothing — the next unit starts at once  c) A Unit Summary and a multi-part Unit Review  d) A laboratory exam
**Answer: c) A Unit Summary and a multi-part Unit Review**
