# How this Physics course works — the Ethiopian Grade 9 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** List the seven units in order.
**Answer: Unit 1 Physics and Human Society; Unit 2 Physical Quantities; Unit 3 Motion in a Straight Line; Unit 4 Force, Work, Energy and Power; Unit 5 Simple Machines; Unit 6 Mechanical Oscillation and Sound Wave; Unit 7 Temperature and Thermometry.**

**2.** What do the words at the start of each numbered section tell you?
**Answer: They state what you should be able to do at the end of that section — the section's outcomes.**

**3.** Name two Ethiopian anchors the book uses.
**Answer: Any two of: Harar Jugol, Fasilides Castle, the Axum obelisks, the Lalibela churches, local-market measuring, the Afar region's heat, the kirar.**

**4.** Why does the course start with society and measuring before force?
**Answer: Because each unit builds on the ones before it: you cannot talk about force before you can measure, and the course starts from physics in daily life.**
