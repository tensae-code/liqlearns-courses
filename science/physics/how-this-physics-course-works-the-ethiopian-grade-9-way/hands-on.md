# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Ethiopian Grade 9 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Walk the journey
Lay seven pieces of paper on the floor as stepping stones. Write one unit name on each, in order, and walk the road saying what each unit is about as you step on it. A stone you cannot explain is a unit to revisit.

## Brainstorm like the book
Pick tomorrow's unit. Before opening anything, write three questions about it from your own daily life — the book's brainstorm-first way. After the unit, check which of your questions it answered.

## Spot it in daily life
Find one place where each of two different units shows up on the same street — for example a simple machine at a workshop and sound from a kirar — and describe both in three sentences.
