# How this Physics course works — the Ethiopian Grade 9 way — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner states the lesson's key ideas in their own words, gives the units in the book's order, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to work, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.
