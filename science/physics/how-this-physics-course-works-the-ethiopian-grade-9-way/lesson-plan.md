# Lesson plan — How this Physics course works — the Ethiopian Grade 9 way

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** How this Physics course works — the Ethiopian Grade 9 way
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics — Course opening (Grade 9 band, CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Grade 9 course runs in seven units — society first, then measure, move, push, machines, waves, heat — and every unit is taught brainstorm-first, from daily life.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the learner's own world — it is the opening map for the whole Grade 9 band, and a learner can land here directly and follow it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. The Grade 9 course has seven units, in order: Physics and Human Society; Physical Quantities; Motion in a Straight Line; Force, Work, Energy and Power; Simple Machines; Mechanical Oscillation and Sound Wave; Temperature and Thermometry.
2. Every unit opens with Brainstorming Questions drawn from daily life — think first, then learn.
3. Each section opens with "at the end of this section you should be able to…", and units close with a Unit Summary and a Unit Review.
4. Ethiopian anchors run throughout: Harar Jugol, Fasilides Castle, the Axum obelisks, Lalibela, market measuring, Afar heat and the kirar.
5. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson text alone.
