# Hands-on — Stability and the position of the centre of gravity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stand a bottle upright, half-full of water, and tip it gently both ways
Stand a bottle upright, half-full of water, and tip it gently both ways: it returns. Empty it and repeat — the higher centre of gravity tips easier and falls sooner. Fill it fully: the centre rises again and it is less steady than half-full. Then race two paper towers of the same height, one on a wide base, one narrow, under a fan.
