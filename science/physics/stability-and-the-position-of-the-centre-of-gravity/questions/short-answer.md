# Stability and the position of the centre of gravity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State the deciding test for whether a tipped body returns.
**Answer:** Whether the vertical line from its centre of gravity falls inside or outside its base.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the two design rules for a stable object.
**Answer:** Keep the centre of gravity low; make the base wide.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does a stable body's centre of gravity rise when it is tipped a little?
**Answer:** Tipping lifts it against gravity — which is why gravity pulls it back.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Give one everyday object built low-and-wide for stability.
**Answer:** A table lamp (heavy wide base), a racing car, a baby's walker.
