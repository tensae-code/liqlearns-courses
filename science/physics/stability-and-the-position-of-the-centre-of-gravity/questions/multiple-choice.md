# Stability and the position of the centre of gravity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A tipped body returns upright while…
a) Its centre of gravity is highest  b) The vertical from its centre of gravity falls inside its base  c) Its base is narrowest  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) The vertical from its centre of gravity falls inside its base**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Once the line falls outside the base…
a) The body returns faster  b) Gravity's moment topples the body  c) Nothing changes  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Gravity's moment topples the body**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Stability is increased by…
a) Raising the centre and narrowing the base  b) Lowering the centre of gravity and widening the base  c) Painting the body  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Lowering the centre of gravity and widening the base**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A racing car is built low and wide because…
a) It looks faster  b) Its centre of gravity stays over its base in hard cornering  c) Low cars use less fuel only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Its centre of gravity stays over its base in hard cornering**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A ball on a flat table is in ___ equilibrium.
a) Stable  b) Neutral — its centre of gravity neither rises nor falls when moved  c) Unstable  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Neutral — its centre of gravity neither rises nor falls when moved**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In unstable equilibrium, a small tip makes the centre of gravity…
a) Rise, so it returns  b) Fall, so the body falls further  c) Vanish  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Fall, so the body falls further**
