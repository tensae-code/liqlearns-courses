# Stability and the position of the centre of gravity — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The body topples once the line from the centre of ___ falls outside the base.
**Answer:** gravity

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) ___ the centre of gravity and widening the base both add stability.
**Answer:** Lowering

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A body that stays where it is left is in ___ equilibrium.
**Answer:** neutral

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) In ___ equilibrium a small tip makes the centre of gravity drop.
**Answer:** unstable
