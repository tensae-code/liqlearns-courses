# Lesson plan — Stability and the position of the centre of gravity

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** Stability and the position of the centre of gravity
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18), inside the equilibrium strand — Punjab Grade 9's named section on when a body stands and when it falls
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a body is stable while a vertical line from its centre of gravity falls inside its base; lower the centre of gravity and widen the base and the body stands harder to tip — Punjab's Grade 9 section makes the position of the centre of gravity the whole story of stability

## Sub lessons
1. **The line that decides — centre of gravity over the base** — Tip a body a little and watch its centre of gravity: while the weight's line of action falls inside the base, gravity itself pulls the body back upright. Tip past the edge, the line falls outside the base, and the same gravity now topples it. Stability is geometry: where the line lands decides which way the moment acts.
2. **Lower and wider stand firmer** — Two changes make the line harder to push out: lowering the centre of gravity (the body must be lifted further before the line escapes, and the restoring moment grows) and widening the base (the line has further to travel). Racing cars, table lamps and loaded trucks are all built on this pair of rules — weight low, feet wide.
3. **Stable, unstable and neutral** — Set a body on a slope of its own shape and three fates appear: displaced, a stable body returns (its centre of gravity rises when tipped); an unstable body falls further (its centre of gravity drops); a neutral body stays where left (a ball, a cylinder on its side — the centre of gravity neither rises nor falls). Punjab's section names all three by what the centre of gravity does.
