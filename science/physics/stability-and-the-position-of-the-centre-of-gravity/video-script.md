# Video script — Stability and the position of the centre of gravity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **a body is stable while a vertical line from its centre of gravity falls inside its base; lower the centre of gravity and widen the base and the body stands harder to tip — Punjab's Grade 9 section makes the position of the centre of gravity the whole story of stability**. Tip a body a little and watch its centre of gravity: while the weight's line of action falls inside the base, gravity itself pulls the body back upright. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — the line that decides — centre of gravity over the base; lower and wider stand firmer; stable, unstable and neutral — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
