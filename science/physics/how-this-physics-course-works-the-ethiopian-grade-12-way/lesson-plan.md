# Lesson plan — How this Physics course works — the Ethiopian Grade 12 way

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. The Grade 12 course has five units, in order: Application of Physics in Other Fields; Two-Dimensional Motion; Fluid Mechanics; Electromagnetism; Basics of Electronics.
2. Applications come first and electronics last; the three machinery units — motion, fluids, electromagnetism — sit between them.
3. Every unit addresses the pupil directly ("Dear students…"), opens with Brainstorming, and uses Discussion questions in tinted boxes and bench Activities with materials lists.
4. Ethiopian anchors: the Axum obelisks, Lalibela's Bete Giorgis church and Addis Ababa's Gotera interchange illustrate civil engineering.
5. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson text alone.
