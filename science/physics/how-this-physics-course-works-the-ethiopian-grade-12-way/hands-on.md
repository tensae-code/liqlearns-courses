# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Ethiopian Grade 12 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Walk the journey
Lay five pieces of paper on the floor as stepping stones. Write one unit name on each, in order, and walk the road saying what each unit is about as you step on it. A stone you cannot explain is a unit to revisit.

## Brainstorm like the book
Pick tomorrow's unit. Before opening anything, write three brainstorming questions about it from your own daily life — the book's direct-address way. After the unit, check which of your questions it answered.

## Spot it in daily life
Find one application of physics in medicine, agriculture or building near you, name the physics it uses, and describe it in three sentences.
