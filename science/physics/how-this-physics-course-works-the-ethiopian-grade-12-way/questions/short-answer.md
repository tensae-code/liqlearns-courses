# How this Physics course works — the Ethiopian Grade 12 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** List the five units in order.
**Answer: Unit 1 Application of Physics in Other Fields; Unit 2 Two-Dimensional Motion; Unit 3 Fluid Mechanics; Unit 4 Electromagnetism; Unit 5 Basics of Electronics.**

**2.** What closes the book?
**Answer: A full Index, so any topic can be found again by name.**

**3.** Name two Ethiopian anchors the book uses for civil engineering.
**Answer: Any two of: the Axum obelisks, Lalibela's Bete Giorgis church, Addis Ababa's Gotera interchange.**

**4.** Describe one unit's rhythm from opening to close.
**Answer: Introduction and Brainstorming addressed to the pupil, stated outcomes, numbered sections with Discussion boxes and Activities (some bench experiments with materials lists), worked examples, Exercises, a bullet-point summary, and End-of-unit questions and problems.**
