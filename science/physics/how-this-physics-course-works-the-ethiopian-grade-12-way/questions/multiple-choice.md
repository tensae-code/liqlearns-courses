# How this Physics course works — the Ethiopian Grade 12 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many units does the Grade 12 Physics course have, and what is the last one?
a) Five — Basics of Electronics  b) Six — Geometrical Optics  c) Five — Fluid Mechanics  d) Seven — Nuclear Physics
**Answer: a) Five — Basics of Electronics**

**2.** Which unit comes first in Grade 12?
a) Two-Dimensional Motion  b) Application of Physics in Other Fields  c) Electromagnetism  d) Fluid Mechanics
**Answer: b) Application of Physics in Other Fields**

**3.** How does each Grade 12 unit address the pupil?
a) In Latin  b) Only through the teacher  c) Directly — "Dear students…" — with Brainstorming questions  d) Through examination questions only
**Answer: c) Directly — "Dear students…" — with Brainstorming questions**

**4.** What does Unit 2 teach that Grade 11's identically titled unit never taught?
a) Projectile motion  b) Heat conduction  c) Logic gates  d) Sound waves
**Answer: a) Projectile motion**

**5.** Which of these illustrates civil engineering in the book?
a) The Gotera interchange in Addis Ababa  b) A snow bridge  c) A lighthouse  d) A desert dune
**Answer: a) The Gotera interchange in Addis Ababa**

**6.** Where do the Discussion questions appear in each unit?
a) Only in the Index  b) In tinted boxes inside the sections  c) Nowhere  d) In the teacher's guide only
**Answer: b) In tinted boxes inside the sections**
