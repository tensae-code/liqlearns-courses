# The electronvolt — the energy unit of charged particles — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) 1 eV = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ _____ .
**Answer: joules (J)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) 1 MeV is 10⁶ _____ .
**Answer: electronvolts (eV)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Energy gained in eV = charge (in units of e) × _____ crossed.
**Answer: volts (the potential difference)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Hydrogen's levels go as −13.6/_____ eV.
**Answer: n²**
