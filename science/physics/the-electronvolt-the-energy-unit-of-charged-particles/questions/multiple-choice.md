# The electronvolt — the energy unit of charged particles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) One electronvolt is the energy gained by…
**Answer: a) one elementary charge crossing 1 V  b) one coulomb crossing 1 V  c) one electron crossing 1,000 V  d) any charge crossing any voltage**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) 1 eV in joules is about…
**Answer: a) 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ J  b) 1.6×10¹⁹ J  c) 1 J  d) 3.6×10⁶ J**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) 1 MeV equals…
**Answer: a) 10⁶ eV  b) 10³ eV  c) 10⁹ eV  d) 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ J**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An α particle (charge 2e) accelerated through 10⁶ V gains…
**Answer: a) 2 MeV  b) 1 MeV  c) 2 eV  d) 10⁶ J**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The hydrogen atom's ground level is −13.6 eV. The 13.6 eV is…
**Answer: a) the ionisation energy — the price of removing the electron  b) the atom's mass  c) the electron's speed  d) the nucleus's charge**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) An electron accelerated through 500 V has kinetic energy…
**Answer: a) 500 eV  b) 500 J  c) 5 eV  d) 500 keV**
