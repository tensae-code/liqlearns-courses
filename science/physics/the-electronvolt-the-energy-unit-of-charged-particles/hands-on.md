# Hands-on — The electronvolt — the energy unit of charged particles

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

["(1) Price a torch battery's work: one electron crossing a 1.5 V cell gains 1.5 eV — compute how many electrons per second a 0.5 A current sends through it, and the total in joules, to feel both scales. (2) From a printed hydrogen level diagram (in eV), compute the photon energy of the n = 3 → n = 2 jump in eV and in joules. (3) Look up three particle energies (a medical X-ray photon, a cosmic-ray proton, the LHC beam) and sort them on a keV–MeV–GeV ladder you draw."]
