# Lesson plan — Misconception-first teaching — the Indonesian guide way

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** Misconception-first teaching — the Indonesian guide way
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18) — a new lesson built for the Indonesia placement named in lesson/sources.md
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** misconception-first teaching in the Indonesian guide way — every chapter carries a named **Miskonsepsi** section listing this topic's specific wrong ideas **before** the teaching plan; teaching is chosen to confront them (predict by the misconception, watch it fail, with **Pembelajaran Alternatif** as a second route), and checks, teacher reflection and follow-up track whether the wrong idea actually moved — teacher-guide evidence only
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Earlier lessons make a good preparation and are recommended, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Name the misconception first — a Miskonsepsi section in every chapter** — Every chapter of the guide carries its own named Miskonsepsi section: the specific wrong ideas this topic provokes, written down before the teaching plan — so the chapter's first target is the error, not the content.
2. **Teach against it — activities and alternative routes** — The teaching that follows is chosen to confront the named misconception: demonstrations and activities whose evidence the wrong idea cannot explain, and Pembelajaran Alternatif — alternative-learning activities — as a second route when the first does not dislodge it.
3. **Check, reflect, follow up — did the wrong idea move?** — Answer keys and LKPD checks reveal whether learners still answer the misconception's way; the teacher's reflection names what remains, and follow-up (tindak lanjut) carries the unfinished work into the next teaching — the misconception is tracked, not just mentioned.

## If the learner is stuck
- Cannot start → return to Do it first and do the opening activity with real objects or examples before reading any explanation; the explanation is the story of that activity.
- Mixes the sub lessons up → re-read the key ideas in order; each key idea is one sub lesson compressed to a line.
