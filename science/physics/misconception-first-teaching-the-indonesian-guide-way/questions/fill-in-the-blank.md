# Misconception-first teaching — the Indonesian guide way — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Every chapter's named misconception section is headed _____.
**Answer: Miskonsepsi**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The alternative-learning route is called Pembelajaran _____.
**Answer: Alternatif**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In a confrontation, learners first _____ by the misconception, then watch it fail.
**Answer: predict**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Planned next teaching that carries unfinished work forward is the _____.
**Answer: follow-up**
