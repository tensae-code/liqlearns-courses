# Misconception-first teaching — the Indonesian guide way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A Miskonsepsi section lists…
a) The chapter's formulae  b) The specific wrong ideas this topic provokes, named per chapter  c) The learners' names  d) The examination dates
**Answer: b) The specific wrong ideas this topic provokes, named per chapter**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The misconception list is written…
a) After the examination  b) Before the teaching plan, so teaching can be planned against it  c) Only if learners ask  d) By the parents
**Answer: b) Before the teaching plan, so teaching can be planned against it**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A confrontation activity works by…
a) Telling learners they are wrong  b) Making the misconception predict, then showing a result it cannot explain  c) Skipping the topic  d) Giving more homework
**Answer: b) Making the misconception predict, then showing a result it cannot explain**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Pembelajaran Alternatif are…
a) Alternative-learning activities — a second route into the same idea when the first route has not dislodged the misconception  b) Optional chapters  c) Another school's lessons  d) Sports activities
**Answer: a) Alternative-learning activities — a second route into the same idea when the first route has not dislodged the misconception**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) At the chapter's end, the key misconception question is…
a) Was the chapter finished on time?  b) Did the wrong idea move — do learners still answer its way?  c) Were the worksheets collected?  d) Was the board clean?
**Answer: b) Did the wrong idea move — do learners still answer its way?**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Follow-up (tindak lanjut) carries…
a) Next year's enrolment  b) The unfinished misconception work into the next teaching, instead of building on a standing error  c) The apparatus back to storage  d) Grades to the office
**Answer: b) The unfinished misconception work into the next teaching, instead of building on a standing error**
