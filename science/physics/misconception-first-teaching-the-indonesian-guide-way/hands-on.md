# Hands-on — Misconception-first teaching — the Indonesian guide way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hunt one misconception
Choose a topic and someone to teach for five minutes. First, write the Miskonsepsi box: two wrong ideas, as a learner would say them. Make your partner commit to a prediction from one of them, out loud. Then run or describe the activity whose result that prediction cannot explain. Finish by asking one new question wearing different clothing over the same trap. Write down, in one line, whether the wrong idea moved — that line is your reflection.
