The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **misconception-first teaching**. Before the content, the errors. Every chapter names the wrong ideas this topic provokes — in its own Miskonsepsi section — and the teaching aims at them: you predict by your wrong idea, you watch it fail, and if it still stands, there is a second route ready. At the end, nobody asks only “did we finish the chapter?” They ask: did the wrong idea move?
