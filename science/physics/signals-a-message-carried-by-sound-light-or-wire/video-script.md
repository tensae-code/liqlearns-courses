Today: **Signals**.

A whistle blows and a whole game stops. No word, no touch — a message travelled. That is a signal, and every signal has three parts: an emitter who sends, a carrier that travels, a receiver who catches it. Sound carries the whistle, the drum and the bell. Light carries the flare, the lighthouse and the traffic light. Electricity in a wire carries the telephone and the doorbell, guided straight to one receiver. And when the message is help, signals become patterns no one can miss — a red flare, or the famous SOS. Emitter, carrier, receiver: spot the three, and you understand every signal on Earth.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
