# Signals — a message carried by sound, light or wire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A signal is…
a) Any loud noise  b) A sign sent on purpose to carry a message from an emitter to a receiver  c) A kind of wire  d) Only an electric current
**Answer: b) A sign sent on purpose to carry a message from an emitter to a receiver**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The three parts of every signal chain are…
a) Wire, plug, battery  b) Emitter, carrier, receiver  c) Sender, paper, reader  d) Bell, rope, tower
**Answer: b) Emitter, carrier, receiver**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) In the referee's whistle, the carrier is…
a) The referee  b) Sound travelling through the air  c) The players  d) The ball
**Answer: b) Sound travelling through the air**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A lighthouse sends its signal by…
a) Sound  b) Light — seen as far as sight reaches at night  c) Wire  d) Drum
**Answer: b) Light — seen as far as sight reaches at night**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The telephone's advantage as a signal system is…
a) It needs no receiver  b) Its wire guides the signal exactly to one receiver, over any distance, in any weather  c) It is the loudest signal  d) It works with no emitter
**Answer: b) Its wire guides the signal exactly to one receiver, over any distance, in any weather**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) SOS is a distress signal built as…
a) One long blast  b) A pattern — three short, three long, three short — that no receiver can mistake  c) A red colour only  d) A whispered word
**Answer: b) A pattern — three short, three long, three short — that no receiver can mistake**
