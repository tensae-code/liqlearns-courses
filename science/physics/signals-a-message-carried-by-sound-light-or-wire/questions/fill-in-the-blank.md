# Signals — a message carried by sound, light or wire — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The one who sends a signal is called the _____.
**Answer: emitter**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The sound, light or electricity that travels is the _____.
**Answer: carrier**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A traffic light sends its message by _____.
**Answer: light**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The distress pattern of three short, three long, three short is called _____.
**Answer: SOS**
