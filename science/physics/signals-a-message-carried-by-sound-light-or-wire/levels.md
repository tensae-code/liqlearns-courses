# Signals — a message carried by sound, light or wire — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner defines a signal and breaks any example into emitter, carrier and receiver, sorts signals by their three carriers (sound, light, wire-electricity) with the strength and limit of each, and explains distress signals as patterns built to be unmissable, answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 2 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — doing the lesson's own experiments and sorts by hand, naming each idea aloud, and reasoning about new examples until the teaching is the learner's own.
