# Hands-on — Signals — a message carried by sound, light or wire

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Whistle, flare, bell
With a whistle, a torch and a bell, send the same message ('come here') three ways across the playground at dusk. Compare: which carried farthest, which was clearest, which failed in noise or darkness? Say aloud: same message, three carriers.

## Break the chain
Run the string telephone, then break each link in turn — no speaker (emitter), string cut (carrier), listener walks away (receiver). Say aloud after each: which link broke, and why the message died with it.
