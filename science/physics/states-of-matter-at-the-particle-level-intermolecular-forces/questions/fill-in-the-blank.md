# States of matter at the particle level — intermolecular forces — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a solid, particles vibrate in _____ places.
**Answer: fixed**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The pulls between particles are called _____ forces.
**Answer: intermolecular**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The pull between particles of the same substance is called _____.
**Answer: cohesion**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A liquid's surface behaving like a stretched skin is called surface _____.
**Answer: tension**
