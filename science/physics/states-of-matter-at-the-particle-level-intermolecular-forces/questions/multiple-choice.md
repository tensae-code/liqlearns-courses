# States of matter at the particle level — intermolecular forces — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a liquid, the particles…
**Answer: a) are close together but can slide past each other  b) sit in fixed places and only vibrate  c) are far apart and fly freely  d) do not move at all**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A gas fills all the space it is given because its particles…
**Answer: a) are far apart and move freely  b) are locked in a grid  c) pull each other into the corners  d) are heavier than a liquid's particles**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Intermolecular forces are…
**Answer: a) the pulls between particles of a substance  b) the weight of the particles  c) forces that exist only in gases  d) the pushes that keep particles apart**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A solid melts when its particles…
**Answer: a) vibrate hard enough that the pulls can no longer hold the pattern  b) stop moving  c) lose their forces completely at any temperature  d) become heavier**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Cohesion is the pull between particles…
**Answer: a) of the same substance  b) of different substances  c) of a substance and gravity only  d) inside one particle**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Water climbs a thin glass tube mainly because of…
**Answer: a) adhesion between water and glass, with cohesion pulling more water up  b) the tube sucking the water up  c) air pressure pushing only inside tubes  d) the water evaporating upward**
