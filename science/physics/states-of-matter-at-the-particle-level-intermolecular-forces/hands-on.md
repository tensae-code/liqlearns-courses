# Hands-on — States of matter at the particle level — intermolecular forces

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Drops and climbing water
Put one drop of water and one drop of cooking oil on a clean plate: the water drop holds together in a bead (cohesion), the oil spreads flatter. Dip the corner of a paper towel into a glass of water and watch the water climb into the dry paper against gravity — adhesion between water and paper, then cohesion pulling more water up. Stand a thin drinking straw next to a wide straw in the same glass: the water climbs higher in the thinner tube, because the surface effects are stronger where the surface is a bigger share of the water.
