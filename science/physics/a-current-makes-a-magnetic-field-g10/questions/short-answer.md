# A current makes a magnetic field — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** What shape is the field around a straight current-carrying wire?
**Answer: Concentric circles around the wire.**

**2.** State the right-hand grip rule.
**Answer: Thumb along the current; fingers curl in the field's direction.**

**3.** How do you strengthen a solenoid's field?
**Answer: More current, more turns (and an iron core helps).**

**4.** A solenoid's field resembles which magnet's?
**Answer: A bar magnet's.**
