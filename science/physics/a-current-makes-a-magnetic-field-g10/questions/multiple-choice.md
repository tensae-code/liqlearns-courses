# A current makes a magnetic field — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What shape is the field around a straight current-carrying wire?
a) Concentric circles around the wire.  b) Thumb along the current; fingers curl in the field's direction.  c) More current, more turns (and an iron core helps).  d) A bar magnet's.
**Answer: a) Concentric circles around the wire.**

**2.** State the right-hand grip rule.
a) Concentric circles around the wire.  b) Thumb along the current; fingers curl in the field's direction.  c) More current, more turns (and an iron core helps).  d) A bar magnet's.
**Answer: b) Thumb along the current; fingers curl in the field's direction.**

**3.** How do you strengthen a solenoid's field?
a) Concentric circles around the wire.  b) Thumb along the current; fingers curl in the field's direction.  c) More current, more turns (and an iron core helps).  d) A bar magnet's.
**Answer: c) More current, more turns (and an iron core helps).**

**4.** A solenoid's field resembles which magnet's?
a) Concentric circles around the wire.  b) Thumb along the current; fingers curl in the field's direction.  c) More current, more turns (and an iron core helps).  d) A bar magnet's.
**Answer: d) A bar magnet's.**
