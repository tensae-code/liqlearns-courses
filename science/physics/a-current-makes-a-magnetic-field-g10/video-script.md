# Video script — A current makes a magnetic field

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Pass a current through a straight wire and a magnetic field appears in concentric circles around the wire.
The right-hand grip rule gives its direction: thumb along the current, fingers curl the way the field runs.
Wind the wire into a coil — a solenoid — and the circles add up into a field like a bar magnet's, with poles at its ends, strength growing with current and with turns.
Electricity has just manufactured magnetism: the bridge this whole unit walks on.

Remember: **A current's field forms concentric circles around the wire.**
Remember: **Right-hand grip rule: thumb = current, curled fingers = field direction.**
Remember: **A solenoid's field resembles a bar magnet's, with poles.**
