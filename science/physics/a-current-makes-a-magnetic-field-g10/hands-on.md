# Hands-on — A current makes a magnetic field

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Grip your right fist with thumb up: that hand is now the rule. Point the thumb along any imagined current and read the field off your fingers.

## Apply it where you live
Draw a wire through card with compasses around it, current on and off, and label both states.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21916 — wire through card, compasses circling it; solenoid with bar-magnet-like poles] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
