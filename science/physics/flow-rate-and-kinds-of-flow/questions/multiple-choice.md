# Flow rate and kinds of flow — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about fluid flow is correct?**
a) Friction opposes relative motion or its tendency: static friction adjusts up to a maximum, kinetic friction is roughly…  b) Flow is steady (laminar) or turbulent; flow rate Q = V/t counts volume per time through a pipe.  c) The traffic runs both ways: new instruments and technology open new physics (telescopes, accelerators, computers), just…
**Answer: b) Flow is steady (laminar) or turbulent; flow rate Q = V/t counts volume per time through a pipe.**
