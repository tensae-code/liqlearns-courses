Today: **Uniform circular motion** — how to describe a thing that goes round and round at a steady pace.

A ball on a string, a point on a wheel, a horse on a merry-go-round: the path is a circle and the speed never changes. Two counts describe it. The period is the time for one full turn. The frequency is the turns in one second. They are flips of each other: frequency equals one over period.
In one turn the body travels the whole rim — two pi times the radius — so its speed along the circle is that distance divided by the period. And the angle sweeps too: the angular velocity is two pi over the period, and the two speeds join in one line — v equals omega times r. The farther out you sit, the faster you travel, though you turn at the very same rate.
