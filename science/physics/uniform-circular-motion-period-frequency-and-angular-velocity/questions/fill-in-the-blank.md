# Uniform circular motion — period, frequency and angular velocity — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Frequency and period are flips of each other: f = _____ / T.
**Answer: 1**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Frequency is measured in _____, one per turn-per-second.
**Answer: hertz (Hz)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The linear velocity points along the _____ to the circle, so its direction changes at every instant.
**Answer: tangent**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The two speeds of circular motion are joined by v = _____ × r.
**Answer: ω (the angular velocity)**
