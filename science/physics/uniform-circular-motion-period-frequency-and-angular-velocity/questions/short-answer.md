# Uniform circular motion — period, frequency and angular velocity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A fan blade completes 30 turns in 10 seconds. Find its frequency and its period.
**Answer: f = 30/10 = 3 Hz; T = 1/f = 1/3 s ≈ 0.33 s per turn.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why do a big frequency and a small period always go together?
**Answer: They are flips of each other (f = 1/T): the more turns are packed into each second, the less time each single turn can take.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A point on a wheel of radius 0.5 m has period 2 s. Compute its linear velocity.
**Answer: v = 2πr/T = 2π × 0.5 / 2 ≈ 1.6 m/s.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A body moves in a circle at perfectly constant speed. Is its velocity constant? Say why or why not.
**Answer: No. The velocity points along the tangent, and the tangent turns as the body rounds the circle — the direction changes at every instant, so the velocity changes even though the speed does not.**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) For the wheel of question 3, compute the angular velocity, and check the answer against v = ωr.
**Answer: ω = 2π/T = 2π/2 = π ≈ 3.14 rad/s. Check: v = ωr = 3.14 × 0.5 ≈ 1.6 m/s — the same linear velocity as before.**
