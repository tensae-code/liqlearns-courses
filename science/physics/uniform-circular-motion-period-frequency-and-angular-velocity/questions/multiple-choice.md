# Uniform circular motion — period, frequency and angular velocity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The period T of a body in uniform circular motion is…
a) The number of turns it makes in one second  b) The time it takes to complete one full turn  c) The length of the circle's rim  d) The angle swept in one second
**Answer: b) The time it takes to complete one full turn**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A wheel has period T = 0.25 s. Its frequency is…
a) 0.25 Hz  b) 2 Hz  c) 4 Hz  d) 25 Hz
**Answer: c) 4 Hz — f = 1/T = 1/0.25**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In one period, a point on the rim travels a distance of…
a) r  b) πr  c) 2πr — the whole circumference  d) The diameter
**Answer: c) 2πr — the whole circumference**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The linear (tangential) velocity of a circling body…
a) Points toward the centre at every instant  b) Points along the tangent, so its direction changes at every instant while its size stays constant  c) Is zero because the body returns to its start  d) Points away from the centre
**Answer: b) Points along the tangent, so its direction changes at every instant while its size stays constant**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The angular velocity ω is measured in… and equals…
a) Metres per second; ω = vr  b) Hertz; ω = 1/T  c) Turns; ω = 2πf only in degrees  d) Radians per second; ω = 2π/T = 2πf
**Answer: d) Radians per second; ω = 2π/T = 2πf**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Two points on the same turning wheel, one at the rim and one halfway in, share the same… but the rim point has twice the…
a) Angular velocity; linear velocity — v = ωr, and its radius is double  b) Linear velocity; angular velocity  c) Period; frequency  d) Radius; period
**Answer: a) Angular velocity; linear velocity — v = ωr, and its radius is double**
