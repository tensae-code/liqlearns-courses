## Physics communities and what they do

Organised groups of physicists promote the subject, run conferences, share information and support teaching; the Ethiopian Physical Society (EPS) is the home example, with its printed objectives: promote physics education and research in Ethiopia, organise conferences and exchange of scientific information, popularise physics, involve physicists and the public in curriculum design, and disseminate scientific information — with member benefits such as equipment access, computing facilities and scholarships.
