# Planned revision — how the Armenian Grade 12 guide teaches repeating the course — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — Every guide in the Armenian set shares one architecture — but the Grade 12 guide adds §3.6: the methodology of repeating the study material in the second semester; Planned revision revisits the course by structure, not by panic: topics come back in their families — mechanics, molecular physics, electricity, optics, the quantum and nuclear close — with problem-solving and questions doing the revisiting, so each return is also a retrieval; A taught revision does three things raw re-reading does not: it forces recall (the pupil must produce the physics, not recognise it), it reconnects topics that were learned in separate months into one subject, and it diagnoses — every failed retrieval is a located gap with time still attached to it — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
