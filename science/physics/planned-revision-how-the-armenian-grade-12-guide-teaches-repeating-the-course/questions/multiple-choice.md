# Planned revision — how the Armenian Grade 12 guide teaches repeating the course — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Grade 12 guide's §3.6 is a methodology for…
a) Discipline  b) Repeating (revising) the course material in the second semester  c) Buying apparatus  d) Entrance interviews
**Answer: b) Repeating (revising) the course material in the second semester**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In the Armenian set, this revision chapter is found in…
a) Every guide  b) Only the Grade 12 guide  c) Only the Grade 8 guide  d) No guide
**Answer: b) Only the Grade 12 guide**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Planned revision revisits topics…
a) In the order pupils fear them  b) By structure — in their families, with problem-solving doing the revisiting  c) Alphabetically  d) Only the easy ones
**Answer: b) By structure — in their families, with problem-solving doing the revisiting**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Revision questions should mix topics because…
a) It saves paper  b) Examinations mix them, and mixing forces real retrieval and connection  c) Teachers enjoy surprises  d) Chapters are boring
**Answer: b) Examinations mix them, and mixing forces real retrieval and connection**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A failed retrieval during revision is valuable because…
a) It lowers the grade early  b) It locates a gap while there is still time to close it  c) It proves revision is useless  d) It entertains the class
**Answer: b) It locates a gap while there is still time to close it**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Taught revision differs from re-reading chiefly because…
a) It is louder  b) It forces the pupil to produce the physics from memory, reconnect topics, and diagnose gaps  c) It uses more ink  d) It is done at home only
**Answer: b) It forces the pupil to produce the physics from memory, reconnect topics, and diagnose gaps**
