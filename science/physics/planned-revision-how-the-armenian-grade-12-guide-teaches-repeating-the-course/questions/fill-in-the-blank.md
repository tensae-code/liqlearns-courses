# Planned revision — how the Armenian Grade 12 guide teaches repeating the course — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Grade 12 guide's extra chapter is a methodology of ___ the material.
**Answer:** repeating (revising)

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The planned repetition is scheduled for the ___ semester.
**Answer:** second

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Revision must force ___ — producing the physics from memory, not recognising it.
**Answer:** recall (retrieval)

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Every failed retrieval in revision is a located ___ with time still left to close it.
**Answer:** gap
