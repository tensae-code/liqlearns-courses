# Hands-on — Planned revision — how the Armenian Grade 12 guide teaches repeating the course

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with your hands
Build the guide's move for yourself, at small scale: take three topics you studied months apart, and — book shut — write everything you can still produce about each (laws, formulas, one worked example). Mark what would not come. Your marked page is a revision plan: the gaps are located, and they are where the next study hour goes. That is §3.6 in one sitting.
