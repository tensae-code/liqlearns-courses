Every guide in the Armenian set shares one architecture — but the Grade 12 guide adds §3.6: the methodology of repeating the study material in the second semester. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — revision is taught, not left to chance; how the repetition is organised; what good revision does — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.
