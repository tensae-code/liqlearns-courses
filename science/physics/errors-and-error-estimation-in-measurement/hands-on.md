# Hands-on — Errors and error estimation in measurement

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Scatter on purpose
Time the same short fall (or a pendulum's one swing) ten times with a stopwatch and write every value. Find the mean and the spread. Say aloud: my reaction is a random error — the mean is my honest answer and half the spread is my honest error.

## The least-count limit
Measure one book's thickness with a metre ruler, then with a vernier caliper, and write each result with its ± error. Say aloud: the finer instrument did not just change the number — it shrank the error I must honestly claim.
