# Video script — Errors and error estimation in measurement

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **how wrong might I be?** Every measurement misses the truth a little. Some misses lean all one way — systematic, fix them. Some scatter — random, average them away. And a plain misread is a mistake, not data. State the miss in units — absolute error; as a fraction — relative error; add lengths and their absolute errors add; multiply quantities and their percentage errors add. A result without its error is only half an answer.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
