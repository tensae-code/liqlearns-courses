# Errors and error estimation in measurement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A clock that runs steadily slow causes a…
a) Random error  b) Systematic error  c) Gross error  d) Least count
**Answer: b) Systematic error**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Random errors are best reduced by…
a) Recalibrating  b) Taking the mean of many repeated readings  c) Reading once, carefully  d) Discarding the largest reading
**Answer: b) Taking the mean of many repeated readings**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A mass is 40.0 ± 0.5 g. Its percentage error is…
a) 0.5%  b) 1.25%  c) 5%  d) 0.0125%
**Answer: b) 1.25%**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Relative error is defined as…
a) Value ÷ absolute error  b) Absolute error ÷ measured value  c) Error × 100  d) Least count ÷ value
**Answer: b) Absolute error ÷ measured value**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Lengths 10.0 ± 0.2 cm and 5.0 ± 0.1 cm are added. The total and its error are…
a) 15.0 ± 0.1 cm  b) 15.0 ± 0.3 cm  c) 15.0 ± 0.2 cm  d) 15 ± 1 cm
**Answer: b) 15.0 ± 0.3 cm**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) When two quantities are multiplied, their errors combine by adding…
a) Absolute errors  b) Percentage errors  c) The values  d) Least counts only
**Answer: b) Percentage errors**
