# Errors and error estimation in measurement — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An error that pushes every reading the same way is called _____.
**Answer: systematic**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The _____ of many repeated readings stands closest to the true value when errors are random.
**Answer: mean**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Percentage error is the relative error multiplied by _____.
**Answer: 100**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Quantities that are added combine their _____ errors by adding them.
**Answer: absolute**
