# Hands-on — Diffraction of waves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Bending water waves round a barrier
Fill a shallow baking tray with water about 1 cm deep. Stand two waterproof blocks (or folded card wrapped in plastic) across the middle as a barrier, leaving a gap about 2 cm wide. Dip the edge of a ruler into the water on one side, again and again at a steady rhythm, to send straight waves at the barrier. Watch beyond the gap: the waves fan out in curves and reach the tray's far corners — diffraction. Now widen the gap to most of the tray's width and repeat: the middle runs straight and only the edges curl. Say aloud: narrow gap, full spread; wide gap, straight middle — the gap priced against the wavelength.
