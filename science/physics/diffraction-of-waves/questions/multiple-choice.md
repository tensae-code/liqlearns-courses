# Diffraction of waves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Diffraction is…
a) Waves bouncing off a surface  b) Waves spreading out into the shadow region after a gap or an edge  c) Waves speeding up in deep water  d) Two waves cancelling
**Answer: b) Waves spreading out into the shadow region after a gap or an edge**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In a ripple tank, straight waves through a narrow gap emerge as…
a) Straighter waves  b) No waves  c) Semicircular wavefronts centred on the gap  d) Waves travelling backwards
**Answer: c) Semicircular wavefronts centred on the gap**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Diffraction is strongest when the gap width is…
a) Much larger than the wavelength  b) About equal to the wavelength or smaller  c) Exactly ten wavelengths  d) It does not depend on the gap
**Answer: b) About equal to the wavelength or smaller**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Keeping the gap fixed, diffraction increases when the wavelength…
a) Decreases  b) Stays the same  c) Increases  d) Becomes zero
**Answer: c) Increases**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) You can hear a person around a corner but not see them because…
a) Sound is faster than light  b) Sound's wavelength matches the sizes of walls and doors, so it diffracts strongly; light's wavelength is far too small  c) Light is stopped by air  d) Sound travels through walls
**Answer: b) Sound's wavelength matches the sizes of walls and doors, so it diffracts strongly; light's wavelength is far too small**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Sea waves entering a narrow harbour mouth…
a) Stop at the entrance  b) Spread into semicircular wavefronts that reach the whole harbour  c) Become straight only  d) Double their wavelength
**Answer: b) Spread into semicircular wavefronts that reach the whole harbour**
