# Diffraction of waves — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Waves bend into the region behind an obstacle called the _____ region.
**Answer: shadow**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Diffraction is priced by comparing the gap width with the _____.
**Answer: wavelength**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Through a wide gap, the middle of the wave train passes almost straight and spreading shows only at the _____.
**Answer: edges**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Light's diffraction is hard to notice because its wavelength is extremely _____.
**Answer: small (tiny)**
