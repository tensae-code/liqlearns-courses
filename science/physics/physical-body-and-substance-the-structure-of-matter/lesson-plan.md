# Lesson plan — Physical body and substance — the structure of matter

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** Physical body and substance — the structure of matter
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18), inside the structure-of-matter strand — Armenia's Grade 7 book opens its matter chapter with body and substance before atoms (Ch. II §7)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** every thing you can point to is a physical body, every body is made of some substance, and substances themselves are built of tiny particles — the three-step ladder (body, substance, structure) on which the whole Armenian matter chapter stands
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Any earlier lesson named below is recommended first, never forced

## Sub lessons
1. **Physical bodies — the things you can point to** — A physical body is any single object with a place in space: a cup, a ruler, a stone, a drop of water, the Moon. Bodies can be big or small, solid, liquid or gas, made by people or found in nature — what makes each one a body is that it is one thing, separate from the things around it. Armenia's book names the body first, before any property is measured on it.
2. **Substance — what a body is made of** — Ask what the body is made of and you name a substance: the cup is plastic, the ruler wood, the ring gold. One substance makes countless bodies (iron — nails, ships, bridges), and the same body-shape comes in many substances (a cup of glass, of clay, of steel). Body says which thing; substance says what it is of. The properties of the substance — density, melting point — are what later lessons measure.
3. **Structure of matter — the step underneath** — The chapter's real destination: substances are not solid through and through — they are built of tiny particles, atoms and molecules, with spaces between them. That single picture, opened here in §7 and proved in §8, is what will explain diffusion, temperature, pressure and every change of state later in the book. Body and substance are the visible rungs; structure is the rung underneath that does the explaining.
