# Video script — Physical body and substance — the structure of matter

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **every thing you can point to is a physical body, every body is made of some substance, and substances themselves are built of tiny particles — the three-step ladder (body, substance, structure) on which the whole Armenian matter chapter stands**. A physical body is any single object with a place in space: a cup, a ruler, a stone, a drop of water, the Moon. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — physical bodies — the things you can point to; substance — what a body is made of; structure of matter — the step underneath — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
