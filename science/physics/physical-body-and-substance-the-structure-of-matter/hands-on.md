# Hands-on — Physical body and substance — the structure of matter

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with your hands
Lay out five objects (a cup, a coin, a wooden ruler, a rubber ball, a spoon). For each, say two sentences: 'The body is…' and 'The substance is…'. Then find two bodies made of the same substance and one body-shape made of two different substances. The sorting is the lesson: body and substance are different questions.
