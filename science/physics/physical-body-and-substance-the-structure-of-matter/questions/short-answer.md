# Physical body and substance — the structure of matter — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sort these into bodies and substances: a brick, clay, a drop of water, water, a nail, iron.
**Answer:** Bodies: a brick, a drop of water, a nail. Substances: clay, water, iron — the materials the bodies are made of.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A glass cup and a clay cup: what is the same and what is different?
**Answer:** The body-shape (a cup) is the same kind of body; the substances differ — glass against clay — so their properties (strength, density, breaking) differ too.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What question does 'structure of matter' answer that 'substance' does not?
**Answer:** Substance names what a thing is made of; structure says how that substance is built underneath — of atoms and molecules with spaces between them.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Give one case where the same body-shape in a different substance behaves differently, and say why it matters.
**Answer:** Example: a steel spoon and a wooden spoon in hot soup — steel conducts the heat to your hand, wood does not; the substance, not the shape, decides the property.
