# Physical body and substance — the structure of matter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A physical body is…
a) Any single object with its own place in space  b) Only a living thing  c) Only something solid  d) A kind of force
**Answer: a) Any single object with its own place in space**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The substance of a gold ring is…
a) The ring  b) Gold  c) Roundness  d) Its price
**Answer: b) Gold**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which pair is body — substance?
a) Water — a glass of water  b) A nail — iron  c) Iron — a nail  d) Hot — heat
**Answer: b) A nail — iron**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) One substance, iron, can appear as…
a) Only nails  b) Nails, ships and bridges — many different bodies  c) No bodies at all  d) Only a liquid
**Answer: b) Nails, ships and bridges — many different bodies**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The structure of matter says substances are built of…
a) Tiny particles — atoms and molecules — with spaces between them  b) Tiny solid bricks with no gaps  c) Light  d) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Tiny particles — atoms and molecules — with spaces between them**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does the Armenian book teach body and substance before atoms?
a) Atoms are not in the course  b) The ladder body → substance → structure lets each new idea rest on a visible one  c) It is a printing error  d) To delay the hard part
**Answer: b) The ladder body → substance → structure lets each new idea rest on a visible one**
