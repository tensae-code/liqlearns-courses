# Physical body and substance — the structure of matter — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Any single object with its own place in space is a physical ___.
**Answer:** body

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What a body is made of is called its ___.
**Answer:** substance

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Substances are built of tiny particles — atoms and ___ — with spaces between them.
**Answer:** molecules

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) One substance can make many different ___: iron makes nails, ships and bridges.
**Answer:** bodies
