# Video script — Work — force times distance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Work is force times displacement in the force's direction; angled forces contribute only their along-motion component, and work can be zero or negative.
