# Fundamental and derived quantities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Is length fundamental or derived?
a) Fundamental.  b) Length × length; the unit is m².  c) Length and time (speed = length ÷ time, unit m/s).  d) No — it is measured directly.
**Answer: a) Fundamental.**

**2.** How is area built, and what is its unit?
a) Fundamental.  b) Length × length; the unit is m².  c) Length and time (speed = length ÷ time, unit m/s).  d) No — it is measured directly.
**Answer: b) Length × length; the unit is m².**

**3.** Speed is built from which two fundamentals?
a) Fundamental.  b) Length × length; the unit is m².  c) Length and time (speed = length ÷ time, unit m/s).  d) No — it is measured directly.
**Answer: c) Length and time (speed = length ÷ time, unit m/s).**

**4.** Can a fundamental quantity be described through others?
a) Fundamental.  b) Length × length; the unit is m².  c) Length and time (speed = length ÷ time, unit m/s).  d) No — it is measured directly.
**Answer: d) No — it is measured directly.**
