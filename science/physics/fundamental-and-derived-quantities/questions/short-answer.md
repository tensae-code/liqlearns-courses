# Fundamental and derived quantities — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Is length fundamental or derived?
**Answer: Fundamental.**

**2.** How is area built, and what is its unit?
**Answer: Length × length; the unit is m².**

**3.** Speed is built from which two fundamentals?
**Answer: Length and time (speed = length ÷ time, unit m/s).**

**4.** Can a fundamental quantity be described through others?
**Answer: No — it is measured directly.**
