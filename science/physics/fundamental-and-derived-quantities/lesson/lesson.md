# Fundamental and derived quantities

## Do it first
Take a book cover: you measure only its length and width directly. Its area you never measure — you build it. That is the difference in your hands.

## Fundamental and derived quantities — the teaching
Quantities split into two families. Fundamental quantities are measured directly and cannot be described through others — they are the seven base quantities, like length, mass and time. Derived quantities are built by combining fundamentals: area comes from length × length, speed from length ÷ time. Every derived quantity carries a derived unit built the same way, such as m² for area and m/s for speed.

## The key ideas

- Fundamental quantities are measured directly — the seven base quantities.
- Derived quantities are built by combining fundamentals.
- Area is derived: length × length, with unit m².
