The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Quantities split into two families.
Fundamental quantities are measured directly and cannot be described through others — they are the seven base quantities, like length, mass and time.
Derived quantities are built by combining fundamentals: area comes from length × length, speed from length ÷ time.
Every derived quantity carries a derived unit built the same way, such as m² for area and m/s for speed.

Remember: **Fundamental quantities are measured directly — the seven base quantities.**
Remember: **Derived quantities are built by combining fundamentals.**
Remember: **Area is derived: length × length, with unit m².**
