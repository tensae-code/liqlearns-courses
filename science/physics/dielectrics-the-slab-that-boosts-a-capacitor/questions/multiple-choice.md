# Dielectrics — the slab that boosts a capacitor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about dielectrics is correct?**
a) Lenz's law gives its direction — the induced effect opposes the change causing it, as energy conservation demands.  b) Sliding an insulator between the plates raises capacitance (dielectric constant) and must withstand the field…  c) Rotation's resistance to change: I = Σmr² for point masses (a three-mass example totalling 164 kg·m² is worked),…
**Answer: b) Sliding an insulator between the plates raises capacitance (dielectric constant) and must withstand the field…**

**2. Sliding an insulator between the plates raises ______ (dielectric constant) and must withstand the field (dielectric strength); a table of common materials' constants is printed, and the isolated-vs-connected cases are distinguished in a worked example.**
a) hole  b) capacitance  c) laminar
**Answer: b) capacitance**
