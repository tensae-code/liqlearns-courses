# Thermometers and temperature scales — including making your own — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a thermometric property?
**Answer: A property that changes steadily with temperature, e.g. a liquid column's length.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Celsius steam point?
**Answer: 100 °C.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Fahrenheit ice point?
**Answer: 32 °F.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** How do you make your own scale agree with reality?
**Answer: Mark true fixed points, divide evenly, and calibrate against a real thermometer.**
