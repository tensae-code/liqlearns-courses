# Hands-on — Thermometers and temperature scales — including making your own

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Hold a thermometer in cold water, then warm. Watch the thermometric property — the column — do the measuring for you.

## Apply it where you live
Design and calibrate a homemade liquid thermometer, recording your two fixed points.
