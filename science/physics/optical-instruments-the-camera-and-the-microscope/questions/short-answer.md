# Optical instruments — the camera and the microscope — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why must a camera's box be light-tight except at the lens?
**Answer: So that the only light reaching the sensor is the light the lens has formed into the image; stray light would fog the picture.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A student wants ×200 from a ×20 objective. What eyepiece is needed?
**Answer: A ×10 eyepiece — total = 20 × 10 = ×200.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) State the two big differences between the camera's image and the microscope's final image.
**Answer: The camera's is real and diminished (single stage, caught on a surface); the microscope's is virtual and enlarged (two stages, seen by the eye).**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why is the microscope's objective made with a very short focal length?
**Answer: A short-focus lens placed close to the specimen gives a large first-stage magnification, which the eyepiece then magnifies again.**
