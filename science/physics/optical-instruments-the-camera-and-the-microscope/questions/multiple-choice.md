# Optical instruments — the camera and the microscope — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The image on a camera's sensor is…
a) real, inverted and diminished  b) virtual, upright and enlarged  c) real, upright and same size  d) virtual and diminished
**Answer: a) real, inverted and diminished**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A camera is focused on a nearer object by…
a) moving the lens farther from the sensor  b) moving the lens closer to the sensor  c) widening the aperture  d) closing the shutter
**Answer: a) moving the lens farther from the sensor**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In a compound microscope, the objective lens forms…
a) a real enlarged image inside the tube  b) the final virtual image  c) an image on a screen outside  d) no image at all
**Answer: a) a real enlarged image inside the tube**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A ×10 objective with a ×15 eyepiece gives total magnification…
a) ×150  b) ×25  c) ×10  d) ×15
**Answer: a) ×150**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The microscope's final image is…
a) virtual and enlarged  b) real and diminished  c) caught on film  d) smaller than the object
**Answer: a) virtual and enlarged**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The human eye is most like which instrument here?
a) The camera — one lens, real image on a screen (the retina)  b) The microscope — two lens stages  c) Neither — the eye uses no lens  d) A telescope
**Answer: a) The camera — one lens, real image on a screen (the retina)**
