# Optical instruments — the camera and the microscope — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A camera doses light with its aperture and its _____.
**Answer: shutter**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Total magnification = objective magnification × _____ magnification.
**Answer: eyepiece**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The microscope magnifies in _____ stages.
**Answer: two**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The camera's image is _____ — it can land on a surface.
**Answer: real**
