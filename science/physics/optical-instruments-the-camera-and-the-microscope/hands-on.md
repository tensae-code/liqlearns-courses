# Hands-on — Optical instruments — the camera and the microscope

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A one-lens camera
Darken a small box (a shoebox works). Cut a window in one end and tape tracing paper over it as the screen; hold a magnifying glass in front of the opposite end facing a bright window, and move the lens until the window's picture appears upside down on the tracing paper. You have built the camera's heart: one converging lens, one real inverted image on a screen. Note what happens to the image size when you step closer to the window.
