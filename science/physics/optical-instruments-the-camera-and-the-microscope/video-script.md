# Video script — Optical instruments — the camera and the microscope

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **two instruments, one lens rule**. The camera: one lens throws a real, upside-down, smaller image onto the sensor — that is a photograph. The microscope: two lenses in a row — the objective enlarges once, the eyepiece enlarges again, and the magnifications multiply.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
