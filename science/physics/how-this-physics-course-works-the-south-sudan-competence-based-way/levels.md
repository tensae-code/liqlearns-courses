# How this Physics course works — the South Sudan competence-based way — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — South Sudan's Physics is competence-based: outcomes are competences — knowledge and understanding, skills, and attitudes and values together (teacher-guide evidence); Every topic opens with a Learn about / Key inquiry questions table and closes, in the student book, with a Topic summary, new words list and Topic Test; Assessment in the guides works three ways: Observation, Conversation, Product; Cross-cutting issues (environment, peace education, life skills) and special-needs inclusion are written into the topics and activities themselves — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
