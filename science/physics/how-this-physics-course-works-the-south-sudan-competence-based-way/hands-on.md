# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the South Sudan competence-based way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a topic the South Sudan way
Take any stocked lesson in this course whose sources name a South Sudan book. On paper, rebuild its South Sudan shape: write the key inquiry question its topic opens with (from the lesson's own opening), list its outcomes in the three strands, and mark which of Observation / Conversation / Product would best prove each one. Close your page the student book's way: a three-line topic summary, a new-words list of five terms, and one Topic Test question with its answer. You have just taught the topic the competence-based way.
