# How this Physics course works — the South Sudan competence-based way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does this lesson stand on teacher-guide evidence only, counted in no country?
**Answer: The competence-based framing lives in Part 1 of the four Teacher's Guides, not in any student book; following the Zambia and Uganda course-way lessons, it is named as guide evidence and counted nowhere.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A topic's outcomes promise more than facts. Give one outcome of each strand for a pressure topic.
**Answer: Knowledge and understanding: state that pressure = force ÷ area. Skills: measure pressure in a liquid at depths and compare. Attitudes and values: value care with high-pressure equipment / share apparatus fairly (any sensible strand-matched set).**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How is inclusion handled in the South Sudan way — inside or outside the Physics activities? Give the guide's example.
**Answer: Inside: adaptations are written into the activities themselves — e.g. the S2 guide's equilibrium work includes touch-based activities so learners with sight problems reach the same competence by a different sense.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Trace one topic's shape from opening to closing as the guides and student books run it.
**Answer: Opens with the Learn about / Key inquiry questions table; outcomes set in three strands; taught activity by activity (assessed by observation, conversation, product); closes in the student book with Topic summary, new-words list and Topic Test.**
