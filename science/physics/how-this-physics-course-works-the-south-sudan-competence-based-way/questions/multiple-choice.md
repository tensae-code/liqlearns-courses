# How this Physics course works — the South Sudan competence-based way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) South Sudan's curriculum for Physics is…
**Answer: a) competence-based — outcomes are competences to be used  b) memorisation-based  c) examination-only  d) activity-free**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The learning outcomes of each topic are written in three strands: knowledge and understanding, skills, and…
**Answer: a) attitudes and values  b) homework  c) examinations  d) discipline**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Every topic in the guides opens with…
**Answer: a) a Learn about / Key inquiry questions table  b) the Topic Test  c) the answers  d) a glossary only**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The three assessment modes named in the guides are Observation, Conversation, and…
**Answer: a) Product  b) Punishment  c) Attendance  d) Speed**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) In the student books, every topic closes with a Topic summary, a new-words list, and…
**Answer: a) a Topic Test  b) an advertisement  c) a poem  d) nothing**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Cross-cutting issues in the South Sudan way are…
**Answer: a) written into the Physics topics themselves  b) taught in a separate week  c) left to other subjects  d) optional extras**
