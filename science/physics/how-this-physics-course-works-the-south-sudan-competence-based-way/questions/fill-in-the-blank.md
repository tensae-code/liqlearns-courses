# How this Physics course works — the South Sudan competence-based way — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The curriculum builds _____ — knowledge used in skills and carried in attitudes and values.
**Answer: competences**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The questions that open each topic are called key _____ questions.
**Answer: inquiry**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Environment, peace education and life skills are the guides' _____ issues.
**Answer: cross-cutting**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The S2 guide adapts equilibrium activities for learners with sight problems by making them _____-based.
**Answer: touch**
