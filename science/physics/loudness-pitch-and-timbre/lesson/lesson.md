## Loudness, pitch and timbre — and noise pollution — the teaching
Heard sound is described by three qualities, each tied to a wave property. Loudness follows amplitude — bigger vibrations sound louder. Pitch follows frequency — higher frequency, higher pitch. Timbre, the quality that tells a kirar from a voice singing the same note, follows the waveform's shape. Sound in the wrong place or amount becomes noise pollution, judged against sound standards — loudness that harms hearing, sleep and peace is no longer just sound.

## The key ideas

- Loudness follows amplitude; pitch follows frequency.
- Timbre follows the waveform and identifies the source.
- Noise pollution is sound judged harmful against sound standards.
