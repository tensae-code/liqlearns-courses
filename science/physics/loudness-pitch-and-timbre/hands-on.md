# Hands-on — Loudness, pitch and timbre — and noise pollution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Hum a low note, then a high one; speak softly, then loudly. Four combinations — name which wave property you changed each time.

## Apply it where you live
Survey your neighbourhood's sounds for one day and judge the worst noise pollution with reasons.
