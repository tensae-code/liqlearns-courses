# Loudness, pitch and timbre — and noise pollution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A note sounds higher. Which wave property changed?
a) Frequency increased.  b) Timbre — their waveforms differ.  c) Amplitude (loudness).  d) When it is harmful or unwanted against sound standards — harming hearing, sleep or peace.
**Answer: a) Frequency increased.**

**2.** Two instruments play the same note at the same loudness, yet sound different. Why?
a) Frequency increased.  b) Timbre — their waveforms differ.  c) Amplitude (loudness).  d) When it is harmful or unwanted against sound standards — harming hearing, sleep or peace.
**Answer: b) Timbre — their waveforms differ.**

**3.** Turning up a speaker increases which property?
a) Frequency increased.  b) Timbre — their waveforms differ.  c) Amplitude (loudness).  d) When it is harmful or unwanted against sound standards — harming hearing, sleep or peace.
**Answer: c) Amplitude (loudness).**

**4.** When does sound become noise pollution?
a) Frequency increased.  b) Timbre — their waveforms differ.  c) Amplitude (loudness).  d) When it is harmful or unwanted against sound standards — harming hearing, sleep or peace.
**Answer: d) When it is harmful or unwanted against sound standards — harming hearing, sleep or peace.**
