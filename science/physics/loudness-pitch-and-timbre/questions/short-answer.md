# Loudness, pitch and timbre — and noise pollution — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A note sounds higher. Which wave property changed?
**Answer: Frequency increased.**

**2.** Two instruments play the same note at the same loudness, yet sound different. Why?
**Answer: Timbre — their waveforms differ.**

**3.** Turning up a speaker increases which property?
**Answer: Amplitude (loudness).**

**4.** When does sound become noise pollution?
**Answer: When it is harmful or unwanted against sound standards — harming hearing, sleep or peace.**
