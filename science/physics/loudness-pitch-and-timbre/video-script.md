# Video script — Loudness, pitch and timbre — and noise pollution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Heard sound is described by three qualities, each tied to a wave property.
Loudness follows amplitude — bigger vibrations sound louder.
Pitch follows frequency — higher frequency, higher pitch.
Timbre, the quality that tells a kirar from a voice singing the same note, follows the waveform's shape.
Sound in the wrong place or amount becomes noise pollution, judged against sound standards — loudness that harms hearing, sleep and peace is no longer just sound.

Remember: **Loudness follows amplitude; pitch follows frequency.**
Remember: **Timbre follows the waveform and identifies the source.**
Remember: **Noise pollution is sound judged harmful against sound standards.**
