# Resistors in series and parallel — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 4 Ω, 8 Ω and 12 Ω in series on 24 V. Equivalent R and circuit current?
**Answer: R = 4 + 8 + 12 = 24 Ω; I = V/R = 24 ÷ 24 = 1 A.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In series, what is the same through every resistor?
**Answer: The current.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 6 Ω and 3 Ω in parallel. Equivalent R?
**Answer: 1/R = 1/6 + 1/3 = 1/2, so R = 2 Ω.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In parallel, what is the same across every branch?
**Answer: The voltage.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** First move on a combination circuit?
**Answer: Collapse one clear series or parallel group into its equivalent and redraw.**
