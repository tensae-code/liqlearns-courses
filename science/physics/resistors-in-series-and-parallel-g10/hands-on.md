# Hands-on — Resistors in series and parallel

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Wire two lamps in series and feel their dimness with your eyes; then in parallel and see them brighten. The arithmetic of this lesson has a visible face.

## Apply it where you live
Reduce a three-resistor combination circuit to one equivalent resistance, showing every step.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21906 — two lamps in series vs parallel, brightness compared] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using Series: R = R₁ + R₂; Parallel: 1/R = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
