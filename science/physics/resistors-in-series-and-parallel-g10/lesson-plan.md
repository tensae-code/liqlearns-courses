# Lesson plan — Resistors in series and parallel

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** Resistors in series and parallel
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Static and Current Electricity — Grade 10 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Series: resistances add, current the same, voltage divides.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 10 lessons before it in Unit 4 and on everyday observation; it needs nothing else to start.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## If the learner is stuck
- Cannot start → return to Do it first and do it with real objects, slowly, naming each step out loud.
- Mixes up the key ideas → go back to the key-ideas list in lesson/lesson.md and test each one against the picture [Picture 21906].
- Calculation slips → the formula is Series: R = R₁ + R₂; Parallel: 1/R = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂; write the knowns first, substitute, then compute, keeping units at every step.
