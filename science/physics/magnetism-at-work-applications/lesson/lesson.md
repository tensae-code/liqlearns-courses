## Magnetism at work — applications — the teaching
The unit's ideas earn their keep in applications. Electric motors turn the motor effect into rotation in fans, pumps and vehicles. Generators run the family in reverse — motion in a field making current. Compasses navigate by Earth's field. Magnetic separators lift iron from grain, scrap and ore streams. Medicine and transport use strong controlled fields of their own. Every application is one of the unit's principles, dressed for work.

## The key ideas

- Motors turn the motor effect into rotation.
- Generators make current from motion in a field.
- Magnetic separators pull iron from grain, scrap and ore.
