# Magnetism at work — applications — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Which application sorts iron from a grain stream?
**Answer: A magnetic separator.**

**2.** A motor is built on which effect?
**Answer: The motor effect — force on a current-carrying wire in a field.**

**3.** What does a generator produce, and from what?
**Answer: Electric current, from motion in a magnetic field.**

**4.** Name two magnetism applications in transport or medicine.
**Answer: Any two applications the lesson surveys (e.g. motors in vehicles; medical magnetic imaging/scanning as presented).**
