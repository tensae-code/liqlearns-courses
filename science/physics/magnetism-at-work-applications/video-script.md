# Video script — Magnetism at work — applications

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

The unit's ideas earn their keep in applications.
Electric motors turn the motor effect into rotation in fans, pumps and vehicles.
Generators run the family in reverse — motion in a field making current.
Compasses navigate by Earth's field.
Magnetic separators lift iron from grain, scrap and ore streams.
Medicine and transport use strong controlled fields of their own.
Every application is one of the unit's principles, dressed for work.
