# Dipole characteristics and the generator/receiver sign conventions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A dipole's characteristic is the curve of…
**Answer: a) voltage against current  b) power against time  c) resistance against temperature  d) charge against voltage**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why trace the characteristic in both directions?
**Answer: a) to test symmetry — whether the dipole behaves the same with the current reversed  b) to double the marks  c) because meters only work one way  d) to heat the component**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A dipole is passive exactly when its characteristic…
**Answer: a) passes through the origin — U = 0 when I = 0  b) is a straight line  c) is symmetric  d) never reaches zero current**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The lamp's characteristic is…
**Answer: a) passive, non-linear and symmetric  b) active and linear  c) passive and perfectly straight  d) asymmetric with an avalanche knee**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In the receiver convention, the voltage arrow points…
**Answer: a) against the current arrow  b) with the current arrow  c) downward always  d) along the wire's length**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The Zener diode's reverse behaviour shows…
**Answer: a) a sharp avalanche threshold  b) no current ever  c) the same slope as forward  d) a curve through the origin both ways equally**
