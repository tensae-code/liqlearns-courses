# Dipole characteristics and the generator/receiver sign conventions — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The U–I curve of a component is called its _____ .
**Answer: characteristic**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A passive dipole has U = 0 when _____ = 0.
**Answer: I (the current)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The Zener diode conducts sharply in reverse at its _____ threshold.
**Answer: avalanche**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) In the _____ convention the voltage arrow points against the current.
**Answer: receiver**
