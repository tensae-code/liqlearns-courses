# Hands-on — Dipole characteristics and the generator/receiver sign conventions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

["(1) Trace a small torch bulb's characteristic with a variable supply, ammeter and voltmeter — plot both directions and test the origin, the symmetry and the straightness. (2) Repeat for a resistor and compare the two curves' shapes. (3) Take a battery's open-circuit voltage with a voltmeter (I = 0): record that U ≠ 0, and state what the origin test concludes."]
