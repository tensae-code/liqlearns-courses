# Engineered materials — composites, smart materials and fullerenes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A composite material is…
**Answer: a) two or more materials combined so the whole has properties neither part has alone  b) any very pure single material  c) a material that has been melted and re-frozen  d) a natural material used without processing**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In fibreglass, the fibres and the resin play the roles of…
**Answer: a) reinforcement and matrix  b) matrix and reinforcement  c) pores and smart material  d) fullerene and cage**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Porous materials are chosen for insulation mainly because their pores…
**Answer: a) trap still air, a poor heat conductor, while removing heavy solid material  b) conduct heat away quickly  c) make the material stronger than solid  d) reflect all radiation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A shape-memory alloy is a smart material because it…
**Answer: a) returns to a remembered shape when warmed  b) remembers electrical data  c) is the hardest material known  d) never changes temperature**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The fullerene C60 is built from…
**Answer: a) 60 carbon atoms closing a cage of pentagons and hexagons  b) 60 silicon atoms in a crystal  c) carbon atoms in flat unconnected sheets only  d) a chain of 60 carbon atoms in a line**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Piezoelectric crystals are smart because they…
**Answer: a) trade a squeeze for a voltage, in both directions  b) darken in sunlight  c) soak up water like a sponge  d) melt at room temperature**
