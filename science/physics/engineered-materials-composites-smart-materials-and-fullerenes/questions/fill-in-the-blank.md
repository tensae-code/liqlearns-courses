# Engineered materials — composites, smart materials and fullerenes — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a composite, the fibres act as the _____ and the resin or concrete as the matrix.
**Answer: reinforcement**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A material full of tiny holes is called a _____ material.
**Answer: porous**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The C60 fullerene molecule is nicknamed the _____ after its cage shape.
**Answer: buckyball**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Materials that change a property on cue — like darkening in sunlight — are called _____ materials.
**Answer: smart (intelligent)**
