# Hands-on — Engineered materials — composites, smart materials and fullerenes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A composite on the kitchen table
Make two thin strips: one of plain plaster (or dried clay/mud) and one of the same plaster with straw, hair or fabric fibres mixed through it — a home-made composite. When both are fully dry, rest each across two books and load the middle with coins until it snaps. The plain strip breaks early and cleanly; the fibre strip bends, cracks slowly and holds far more — the reinforcement carries the pull while the matrix holds the shape, exactly the composite bargain. Then hold a sponge (a porous material) beside a solid block of the same stuff: squeeze, weigh and insulate-test both, and name what the pores buy and what they cost.
