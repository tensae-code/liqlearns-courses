# Hands-on — Measuring force — the dynamometer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read three forces
With a school dynamometer (or a spring balance from a kitchen): (1) check and note the zero with nothing hanging. (2) Hang three household objects one at a time — a book, a shoe, a full water bottle — let each settle, and read each weight at eye level, writing the newtons down. (3) For the lightest object, predict its reading from its mass first (about 1 N per 100 g), then compare. Any reading that disagrees with the prediction by more than the zero error you noted sends you back to the zero check — that is the instrument teaching its own habits.
