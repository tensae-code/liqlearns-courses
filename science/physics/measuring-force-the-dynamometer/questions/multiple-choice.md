# Measuring force — the dynamometer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The unit of force is the…
**Answer: a) newton  b) kilogram  c) metre  d) joule**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) One newton is about the weight of a mass of…
**Answer: a) 100 g  b) 1 kg  c) 10 g  d) 10 kg**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A dynamometer's scale can be evenly divided because…
**Answer: a) the spring's stretch is proportional to the force  b) the case is rectangular  c) the hook is metal  d) the pointer is thin**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Inside a dynamometer, the part that translates force into movement is…
**Answer: a) the spring  b) the case  c) the printed scale  d) the hook alone**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Before hanging anything, you must check that the pointer sits on…
**Answer: a) zero  b) the middle mark  c) the largest mark  d) any mark**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Stretching a dynamometer past its scale limit…
**Answer: a) spoils the spring, and its later readings lie  b) makes it more accurate  c) resets the zero  d) has no effect**
