# Hands-on — Young's modulus — how stiff a material is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Stretch two different 'wires' — a rubber band and a string — under the same hanging mass and compare extensions. Different stiffness, felt by hand: the idea of a modulus.

## Apply it where you live
From a table of load and extension for a wire, compute Y with full working.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21892 — copper and steel wires under the same load stretching by different amounts] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using Y = stress / strain: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
