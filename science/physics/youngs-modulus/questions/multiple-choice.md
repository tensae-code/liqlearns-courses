# Young's modulus — how stiff a material is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Stress 2×10⁸ Pa produces strain 0.001. Y?
a) Y = 2×10⁸ ÷ 0.001 = 2×10¹¹ Pa.  b) No — Y is the material's property.  c) Copper's — more strain for the same stress means smaller Y.  d) The force (for stress) and the extension (for strain).
**Answer: a) Y = 2×10⁸ ÷ 0.001 = 2×10¹¹ Pa.**

**2.** Does a thicker wire of the same steel have a larger Y?
a) Y = 2×10⁸ ÷ 0.001 = 2×10¹¹ Pa.  b) No — Y is the material's property.  c) Copper's — more strain for the same stress means smaller Y.  d) The force (for stress) and the extension (for strain).
**Answer: b) No — Y is the material's property.**

**3.** Two wires, same size and load: copper stretches more than steel. Whose Y is smaller?
a) Y = 2×10⁸ ÷ 0.001 = 2×10¹¹ Pa.  b) No — Y is the material's property.  c) Copper's — more strain for the same stress means smaller Y.  d) The force (for stress) and the extension (for strain).
**Answer: c) Copper's — more strain for the same stress means smaller Y.**

**4.** What two measurements does the Young experiment need per load step?
a) Y = 2×10⁸ ÷ 0.001 = 2×10¹¹ Pa.  b) No — Y is the material's property.  c) Copper's — more strain for the same stress means smaller Y.  d) The force (for stress) and the extension (for strain).
**Answer: d) The force (for stress) and the extension (for strain).**
