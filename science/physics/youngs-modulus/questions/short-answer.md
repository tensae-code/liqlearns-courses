# Young's modulus — how stiff a material is — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Stress 2×10⁸ Pa produces strain 0.001. Y?
**Answer: Y = 2×10⁸ ÷ 0.001 = 2×10¹¹ Pa.**

**2.** Does a thicker wire of the same steel have a larger Y?
**Answer: No — Y is the material's property.**

**3.** Two wires, same size and load: copper stretches more than steel. Whose Y is smaller?
**Answer: Copper's — more strain for the same stress means smaller Y.**

**4.** What two measurements does the Young experiment need per load step?
**Answer: The force (for stress) and the extension (for strain).**
