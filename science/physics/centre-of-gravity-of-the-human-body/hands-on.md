# Hands-on — Centre of gravity of the human body

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find your own centre of gravity
You need a friend, a plank or long board, two bathroom scales (or one, used twice), and care. Reaction-board method: lie straight on the board, heels at one end supported on a scale, the other end on a fixed support at the same height; from the scale reading and the board's balance, your teacher can work out the centre of gravity's distance from your heels — compare it with 55% of your height. Then do the wall test and a feet-together / feet-apart sway test, writing each result as a line-of-gravity sentence. No lifting of people, and stop any test that feels unsafe.
