# Centre of gravity of the human body — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The body's centre of gravity stands at about _____ % of a person's height.
**Answer: 55**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The area between and around the feet is the base of _____.
**Answer: support**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A walking stick enlarges the base by adding a third _____ point.
**Answer: contact**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Walking is described as controlled _____.
**Answer: falling**
