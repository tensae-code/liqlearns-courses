# Centre of gravity of the human body — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The standing body's centre of gravity is roughly…
a) In the chest, at the heart  b) In the pelvis, a little below the navel, at about 55% of height  c) In the head  d) Fixed in the feet
**Answer: b) In the pelvis, a little below the navel, at about 55% of height**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) When a person bends forward to touch their toes, their centre of gravity…
a) Stays exactly in the pelvis  b) Moves forward and may lie outside the body, in the air in front of the hips  c) Drops to the floor  d) Disappears
**Answer: b) Moves forward and may lie outside the body, in the air in front of the hips**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Balance while standing requires…
a) The line of gravity to fall inside the base of support  b) Zero muscle activity  c) The centre of gravity to be in the feet  d) Standing on one foot
**Answer: a) The line of gravity to fall inside the base of support**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The wall test fails (you cannot lift the outside foot) because…
a) The wall is magnetic  b) The wall stops you shifting your centre of gravity over the standing foot, so its line would fall outside the base  c) Your outside leg is weaker  d) Friction holds the foot down
**Answer: b) The wall stops you shifting your centre of gravity over the standing foot, so its line would fall outside the base**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In the Fosbury flop the jumper's centre of gravity can pass…
a) Over the bar with the body  b) Under the bar while the arched body passes over it  c) Through the bar  d) Nowhere — it stays in the stadium
**Answer: b) Under the bar while the arched body passes over it**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Carrying a heavy load on your back makes you lean forward because…
a) It is fashionable  b) The load shifts the combined centre of gravity backward; leaning forward brings its line back over the feet  c) The load is hot  d) Your legs shorten
**Answer: b) The load shifts the combined centre of gravity backward; leaning forward brings its line back over the feet**
