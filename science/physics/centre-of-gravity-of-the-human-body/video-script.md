# Video script — Centre of gravity of the human body

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **the balance point inside you**. It hides in your pelvis, just below your navel, at about 55% of your height — and it will not sit still: bend and it moves, sometimes clean out of your body into the air. Stand, walk, jump, carry — all of it is keeping that point's line inside the base of your feet. Even standing against a wall and failing to lift a foot is that same geometry, proving itself on you.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
