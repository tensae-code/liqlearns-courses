# The Doppler effect — pitch shifts when source or listener moves — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Meeting wavefronts more often raises the heard _____.
**Answer: frequency (pitch)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Behind a moving source the wavefronts are stretched, so the wavelength is _____.
**Answer: longer**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Approach between source and listener always _____ the pitch.
**Answer: raises**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Light from a receding galaxy is shifted toward the red end — a _____.
**Answer: redshift**
