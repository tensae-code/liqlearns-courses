# The Doppler effect — pitch shifts when source or listener moves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A listener moving toward a steady sound hears a higher pitch because she…
**Answer: a) meets the wavefronts more often per second  b) makes the waves faster  c) shortens the air  d) hears louder sound**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) When a listener moves away from a source, the heard frequency…
**Answer: a) falls  b) rises  c) is unchanged  d) doubles**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Ahead of a moving source, the wavefronts in the air are…
**Answer: a) squeezed closer together — a shorter wavelength  b) stretched apart  c) unchanged  d) stopped**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The siren’s pitch drops as it passes you because you move from the…
**Answer: a) squeezed (front) side to the stretched (behind) side  b) loud side to the quiet side  c) high gear to low gear  d) near side to far side**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Redshift of a galaxy’s light means the galaxy is…
**Answer: a) receding from us  b) approaching us  c) standing still  d) cooling down**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Police radar measures a car’s speed from…
**Answer: a) the Doppler shift of the reflected waves  b) the loudness of the echo  c) the car’s colour  d) the time of the echo alone**
