# Hands-on — The Doppler effect — pitch shifts when source or listener moves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hear the shift
Stand safely by a quiet road (with a teacher) and listen to a single vehicle approach, pass and recede; note the moment the pitch drops and write what changed at that moment in wavefront words. No road? Use a phone playing a steady tone swung gently on a string in a circle (teacher-supervised, clear space): listeners around the circle hear the pitch rise and fall as the phone approaches and recedes.
