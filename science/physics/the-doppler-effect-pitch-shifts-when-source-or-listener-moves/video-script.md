# Video script — The Doppler effect — pitch shifts when source or listener moves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the passing-siren secret**. Move toward sound and you meet its waves more often — pitch up. Let the source move and the waves themselves bunch ahead and stretch behind. Do it to light and galaxies confess their motion: redshift, an expanding universe.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
