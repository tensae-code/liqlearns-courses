# Electronic tuning — the varactor diode — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The insulating region at a p–n junction, emptied of mobile carriers, is the _____ layer.
**Answer: depletion**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Junction capacitance behaves like a parallel-plate capacitor: widening the layer _____ the capacitance.
**Answer: lowers (decreases)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The resonant frequency follows f = 1/(2π_____).
**Answer: √(LC)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Several tuned stages driven by one control voltage are said to be _____ together, replacing the old ganged capacitor.
**Answer: ganged (tracked)**
