# Hands-on — Electronic tuning — the varactor diode

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tune by voltage alone
With a simple varactor-tuned radio kit or the tuner of an old TV board (described where unavailable), vary only the tuning voltage from a potentiometer and log the stations received against the voltage. No part moves, yet the whole band is crossed. Say aloud the rule: voltage → depletion width → capacitance → resonant frequency — four links, all electronic.

## Station-keeping with AFC
On a receiver with AFC, tune slightly off a strong station, then switch AFC on and hear the set pull itself onto the station's centre. Say aloud the rule: the AFC turns tuning into a feedback loop, steering the varactor voltage until the answer is strongest — something no mechanical knob could do for itself.
