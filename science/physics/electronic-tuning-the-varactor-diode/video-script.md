Today: **electronic tuning — the varactor diode**. Reverse-bias a p–n junction and its depletion layer widens — and since that layer is the insulator of a tiny capacitor, widening it shrinks the capacitance. A voltage now controls a capacitor. Drop that diode into an LC circuit and the resonant frequency follows the voltage: no knobs, no moving plates — tuning by wire, ready for remote controls, presets and automatic fine tuning. That is how every modern radio and television finds its stations.
