# Multipliers and direction changers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A hammer pulling a nail — force or speed effect at the claw?
a) Force multiplication (claw prises with multiplied force).  b) Direction change only.  c) Effort travels far; the split opens by a smaller, multiplied force.  d) No — the trades go opposite ways.
**Answer: a) Force multiplication (claw prises with multiplied force).**

**2.** A fixed pulley gives which effect?
a) Force multiplication (claw prises with multiplied force).  b) Direction change only.  c) Effort travels far; the split opens by a smaller, multiplied force.  d) No — the trades go opposite ways.
**Answer: b) Direction change only.**

**3.** A wedge driven into wood — what is traded?
a) Force multiplication (claw prises with multiplied force).  b) Direction change only.  c) Effort travels far; the split opens by a smaller, multiplied force.  d) No — the trades go opposite ways.
**Answer: c) Effort travels far; the split opens by a smaller, multiplied force.**

**4.** Can one machine multiply both force and speed at once?
a) Force multiplication (claw prises with multiplied force).  b) Direction change only.  c) Effort travels far; the split opens by a smaller, multiplied force.  d) No — the trades go opposite ways.
**Answer: d) No — the trades go opposite ways.**
