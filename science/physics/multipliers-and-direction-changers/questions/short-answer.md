# Multipliers and direction changers — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A hammer pulling a nail — force or speed effect at the claw?
**Answer: Force multiplication (claw prises with multiplied force).**

**2.** A fixed pulley gives which effect?
**Answer: Direction change only.**

**3.** A wedge driven into wood — what is traded?
**Answer: Effort travels far; the split opens by a smaller, multiplied force.**

**4.** Can one machine multiply both force and speed at once?
**Answer: No — the trades go opposite ways.**
