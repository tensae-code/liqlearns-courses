# Hands-on — Multipliers and direction changers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Mime the three effects with your arms: a slow strong lift, a fast sweeping broom, a downward pull that raises a bucket. Your body knows the three trades.

## Apply it where you live
Sort six local tools into force multipliers, speed multipliers and direction changers.
