---

Machines can also be sorted by their effect.
A force multiplier: the load is bigger than the effort, but the load moves less far than the effort — you trade distance for force.
A speed multiplier: the load moves farther and faster than the effort, and the load force is smaller — you trade force for speed.
A direction changer: the load moves a different way from the effort, like a fixed pulley where you pull down and the load rises.
No machine gives both trades at once — that is the unit's honest bargain.

Remember: **Force multiplier: load > effort, but the load moves less.**
Remember: **Speed multiplier: load moves farther/faster than the effort.**
Remember: **Direction changer: load moves a different way to the effort.**
