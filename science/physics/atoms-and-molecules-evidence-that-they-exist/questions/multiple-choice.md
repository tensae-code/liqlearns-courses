# Atoms and molecules — evidence that they exist — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An atom is…
a) The smallest particle carrying a substance's chemical identity  b) A tiny solar system you can see  c) A drop of water  d) A unit of temperature
**Answer: a) The smallest particle carrying a substance's chemical identity**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A molecule is…
a) One atom only, always  b) Two or more atoms joined — the smallest particle keeping a substance's ordinary properties  c) A kind of microscope  d) A gas only
**Answer: b) Two or more atoms joined — the smallest particle keeping a substance's ordinary properties**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Ink spreading through still water by itself is called…
a) Boiling  b) Diffusion  c) Friction  d) Condensation
**Answer: b) Diffusion**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Brownian motion is…
a) Pollen grains jiggling endlessly in water, struck by moving molecules  b) Dust settling in sunlight  c) A river's current  d) Brown paint drying
**Answer: a) Pollen grains jiggling endlessly in water, struck by moving molecules**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does the book call these phenomena 'confirming' atoms and molecules?
a) They are old traditions  b) Only ceaselessly moving invisible particles can explain all of them together  c) Atoms were photographed in colour  d) A famous person said so
**Answer: b) Only ceaselessly moving invisible particles can explain all of them together**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A puddle drying on a still day is evidence that…
a) Water climbs into the sky whole  b) The fastest particles escape the surface — evaporation by particle motion  c) The ground drinks it all instantly  d) Sunlight eats water
**Answer: b) The fastest particles escape the surface — evaporation by particle motion**
