# Atoms and molecules — evidence that they exist — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why can nobody simply look at an atom to settle whether atoms exist?
**Answer:** Atoms are about a ten-millionth of a millimetre across — far below what light microscopes (or eyes) can resolve; so physics proves them by their effects, the phenomena they alone explain.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain dissolving in particle language.
**Answer:** The solid's particles separate and move in among the liquid's particles, spreading through the whole volume — which is why the sweetness reaches every part of the tea.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the difference between splitting a molecule of water and dividing a drop of water?
**Answer:** Dividing a drop gives smaller drops of water; splitting the molecule destroys water itself, giving hydrogen and oxygen — different substances.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A sealed perfume bottle is opened across a still room and soon smelt everywhere. Which idea does this prove, and how?
**Answer:** Diffusion in a gas: the perfume's molecules move ceaselessly and mix through the air's molecules until they reach every corner — no draught needed.
