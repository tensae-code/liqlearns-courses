# Atoms and molecules — evidence that they exist — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The smallest particle carrying a substance's chemical identity is the ___.
**Answer:** atom

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Atoms joined together form a ___.
**Answer:** molecule

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Ink spreading through still water by itself is called ___.
**Answer:** diffusion

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The endless jiggling of pollen grains in water is called ___ motion.
**Answer:** Brownian
