# Video script — Atoms and molecules — evidence that they exist

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **atoms and molecules are far too small to see, yet the Grade 7 book does not ask for belief: it lines up the phenomena — diffusion, Brownian motion, dissolving, evaporation — that only moving invisible particles can explain, and the particles earn their place by explaining**. Break a substance finer and finer and the Armenian book stops the breaking at the atom: the smallest particle that still carries the substance's chemical identity. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — the atom idea — matter in tiny particles; molecules — atoms joined, substances built; the evidence — phenomena that confirm they exist — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
