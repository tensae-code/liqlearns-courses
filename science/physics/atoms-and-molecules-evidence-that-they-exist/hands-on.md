# Hands-on — Atoms and molecules — evidence that they exist

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with your hands
Put one drop of ink or strong tea colour into a tall glass of still water and do not touch it. Watch the colour spread over the lesson and into the next day. Nothing pushed it — draw the conclusion the book draws: the water and the colour are made of moving particles, mixing by their own motion. That spreading is diffusion, and it is evidence, not magic.
