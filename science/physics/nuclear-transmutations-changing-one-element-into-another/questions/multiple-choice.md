# Nuclear transmutations — changing one element into another — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An element is decided by its…
a) Neutron count  b) Proton number Z  c) Electron shells only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Proton number Z**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Alpha decay changes Z by…
a) −1  b) −2 (and A by −4)  c) 0  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) −2 (and A by −4)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Beta decay raises Z by 1 because…
a) A proton leaves  b) A neutron becomes a proton  c) An electron is captured from outside  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) A neutron becomes a proton**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In every transmutation equation, the totals that balance are…
a) Mass in grams  b) A and Z on both sides  c) Charge of electrons only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) A and Z on both sides**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The first artificial transmutation was Rutherford's, turning…
a) Lead into gold  b) Nitrogen into oxygen with alpha particles  c) Hydrogen into helium in a bottle  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Nitrogen into oxygen with alpha particles**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Artificial transmutations are run today to make…
a) Ordinary table salt  b) Radioisotopes for medicine and industry  c) Glass  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Radioisotopes for medicine and industry**
