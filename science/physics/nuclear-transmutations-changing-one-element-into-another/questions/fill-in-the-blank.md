# Nuclear transmutations — changing one element into another — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The element is fixed by the ___ number Z.
**Answer:** proton

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Alpha decay lowers A by ___ and Z by 2.
**Answer:** four (4)

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Beta decay turns a ___ into a proton.
**Answer:** neutron

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Transmutation equations must ___ in both A and Z.
**Answer:** balance
