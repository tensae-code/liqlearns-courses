# Nuclear transmutations — changing one element into another — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What change transmutes, and what change does not?
**Answer:** Any change in Z transmutes; gamma emission (Z unchanged) does not.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How can an unknown decay product be identified from an equation?
**Answer:** Balance A and Z to find the product's numbers, then read the element from Z in the periodic table.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What did Rutherford bombard, and with what?
**Answer:** Nitrogen nuclei, with alpha particles.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Where are made radioisotopes used?
**Answer:** In medicine (tracers and therapy) and industry.
