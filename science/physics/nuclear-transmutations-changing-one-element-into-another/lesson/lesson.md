# Nuclear transmutations — changing one element into another

## Do it first
Practise the bookkeeping before the belief — begin with the hands-on opening in hands-on.md before reading on. This lesson is the story of that activity.

## Sub lesson 1 — The rule of change — protons decide the element

An element is its proton number Z. Any nuclear change that alters Z — alpha decay (Z falls by 2), beta decay (Z rises by 1 as a neutron becomes a proton) — transmutes the nucleus into a different element, whatever happens to its neutrons. Changes that keep Z (gamma emission) change the energy, not the element.

## Sub lesson 2 — Balancing the books — A and Z must match

Every transmutation equation balances both numbers: total nucleon number A and total proton number Z are the same on both sides. Written in the form ᴬzX, the equation can be checked arithmetically before it is believed — and an unknown product can be found from the balance alone.

## Sub lesson 3 — Made transmutations — bombarding nuclei

People transmute deliberately by firing particles at nuclei: Rutherford's alpha particles turned nitrogen into oxygen — the first artificial transmutation. Accelerators and reactors run such reactions to make radioisotopes for medicine and industry: the alchemists' dream, done by bookkeeping and bombardment.

## The key ideas

- An element is its proton number Z.
- Every transmutation equation balances both numbers: total nucleon number A and total proton number Z are the same on both sides.
- People transmute deliberately by firing particles at nuclei: Rutherford's alpha particles turned nitrogen into oxygen — the first artificial transmutation.
