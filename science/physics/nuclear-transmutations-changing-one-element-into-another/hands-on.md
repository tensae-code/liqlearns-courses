# Hands-on — Nuclear transmutations — changing one element into another

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Practise the bookkeeping before the belief
Practise the bookkeeping before the belief: take five decay steps from any natural series (your teacher or book provides them) and check A and Z balance on each; then complete three equations with a missing product, finding the unknown element from the balance and the periodic table.
