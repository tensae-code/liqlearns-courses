# Electrical safety at home — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Why are wet hands at a switch dangerous?
**Answer: Wet skin has low resistance, so more current can pass through the body.**

**2.** What does a fuse do in a fault?
**Answer: It melts and breaks the circuit when current is too high.**

**3.** A frayed cord is dangerous because — ?
**Answer: Damaged insulation can expose live wire to touch.**

**4.** Overloading one socket risks what?
**Answer: Excess current, overheating and fire.**
