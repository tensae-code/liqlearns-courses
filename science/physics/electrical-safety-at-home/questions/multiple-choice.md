# Electrical safety at home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why are wet hands at a switch dangerous?
a) Wet skin has low resistance, so more current can pass through the body.  b) It melts and breaks the circuit when current is too high.  c) Damaged insulation can expose live wire to touch.  d) Excess current, overheating and fire.
**Answer: a) Wet skin has low resistance, so more current can pass through the body.**

**2.** What does a fuse do in a fault?
a) Wet skin has low resistance, so more current can pass through the body.  b) It melts and breaks the circuit when current is too high.  c) Damaged insulation can expose live wire to touch.  d) Excess current, overheating and fire.
**Answer: b) It melts and breaks the circuit when current is too high.**

**3.** A frayed cord is dangerous because — ?
a) Wet skin has low resistance, so more current can pass through the body.  b) It melts and breaks the circuit when current is too high.  c) Damaged insulation can expose live wire to touch.  d) Excess current, overheating and fire.
**Answer: c) Damaged insulation can expose live wire to touch.**

**4.** Overloading one socket risks what?
a) Wet skin has low resistance, so more current can pass through the body.  b) It melts and breaks the circuit when current is too high.  c) Damaged insulation can expose live wire to touch.  d) Excess current, overheating and fire.
**Answer: d) Excess current, overheating and fire.**
