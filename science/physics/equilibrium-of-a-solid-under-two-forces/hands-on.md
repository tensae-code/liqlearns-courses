# Hands-on — Equilibrium of a solid under two forces

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

['(1) Pull a light card with two spring balances from opposite sides; adjust until the card rests, and record both readings and the line — then shift one pull sideways and watch the card turn. (2) Hang a known mass from a thread over a spring balance and read the tension at rest. (3) Float a wooden block in a basin, mark the waterline, add small masses and mark again — the thrust grows to meet the weight each time.']
