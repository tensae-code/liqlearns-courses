# Equilibrium of a solid under two forces — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In two-force equilibrium the vector sum F₁ + F₂ equals _____ .
**Answer: zero (0)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The two forces must share the same _____ of action.
**Answer: line**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A hanging body pairs its weight with the thread's _____ .
**Answer: tension**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) For a spring, T = K·Δl, where K is the _____ constant.
**Answer: stiffness**
