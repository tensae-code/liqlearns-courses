# Equilibrium of a solid under two forces — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A solid under two forces is in equilibrium when the forces…
**Answer: a) share one line, oppose each other and are equal  b) are both downward  c) act at the same point only  d) are perpendicular**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Written as vectors, the two-force condition is…
**Answer: a) F₁ + F₂ = 0  b) F₁ = 2F₂  c) F₁ − F₂ = F₁  d) F₁ × F₂ = 0**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A body rests on a table. The two forces on it are…
**Answer: a) its weight and the table's reaction  b) its weight twice  c) friction and weight only  d) tension and weight**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A 8 N body hangs at rest from a thread. The tension is…
**Answer: a) 8 N  b) 0 N  c) 16 N  d) 4 N**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A spring's tension follows T = …
**Answer: a) K·Δl  b) K/Δl  c) Δl/K²  d) K·Δl²**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A floating pirogue is in equilibrium because…
**Answer: a) Archimedes' thrust equals its weight  b) no force acts on it  c) its weight is zero  d) the water pushes sideways**
