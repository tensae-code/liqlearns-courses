# Power of a lens and combining lenses — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Power is measured in _____.
**Answer: dioptres**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A lens of focal length 25 cm has power _____ D.
**Answer: +4 (four)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Lenses in contact add their _____.
**Answer: powers**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A small combined power means a _____ focal length.
**Answer: long**
