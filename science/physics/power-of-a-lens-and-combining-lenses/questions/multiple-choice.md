# Power of a lens and combining lenses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The power of a lens is…
a) 1 ÷ focal length in metres  b) focal length in metres  c) focal length × 2  d) the magnification
**Answer: a) 1 ÷ focal length in metres**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A convex lens of focal length 0.5 m has power…
a) +2 D  b) −2 D  c) +0.5 D  d) +5 D
**Answer: a) +2 D**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A concave lens has…
a) positive power  b) negative power  c) zero power  d) power in metres
**Answer: b) negative power**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Two lenses in contact, +4 D and +1 D, act as…
a) +5 D  b) +3 D  c) +4 D  d) a weaker lens than either
**Answer: a) +5 D**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The combined focal length of a +5 D pair is…
a) 0.20 m  b) 5 m  c) 0.5 m  d) 25 m
**Answer: a) 0.20 m**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A +2 D lens in contact with a −1 D lens gives a pair that…
a) still converges, with F = 1 m  b) diverges, with F = 1 m  c) has zero power  d) has F = 0.5 m
**Answer: a) still converges, with F = 1 m**
