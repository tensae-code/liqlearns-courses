# Hands-on — Power of a lens and combining lenses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two magnifiers together
Take two magnifying glasses (or reading glasses a teacher lends). Use each alone to project a window's image onto a sheet of paper and pace out roughly how far the paper sits from the lens — that distance is near the focal length. Now hold the two lenses together, in contact, and project again: the paper sits much closer. The pair is stronger than either lens alone — their powers have added. Note which single lens was stronger (shorter focal length) and check that the pair beats even that one.
