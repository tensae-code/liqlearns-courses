# Video script — Power of a lens and combining lenses

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **a lens is priced by its power**. Power is one over the focal length in metres, in dioptres — short focus, strong lens, big number; convex positive, concave negative. Stack lenses in contact and the powers simply add, so two lenses together out-bend either one alone.
