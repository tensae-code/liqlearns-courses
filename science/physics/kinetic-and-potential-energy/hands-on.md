# Hands-on — Kinetic and potential energy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel inertia
Put a book on a sheet of paper and pull the paper out fast. The book stays. Then pull slowly — the book rides. Name the law that explains both.

## See it and say it
Study this lesson's picture — a ball at a hilltop (PE) and rolling fast at the bottom (KE) — and name every part of it out loud. Then close the picture and draw it from memory; check your drawing against the lesson.

## Teach it back
Explain this lesson to someone at home using one thing in the room as your example. Their questions show you which part to re-read.
