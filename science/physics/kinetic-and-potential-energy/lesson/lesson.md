## Kinetic and potential energy

Moving bodies carry kinetic energy (½mv²); raised bodies carry gravitational potential energy (mgh); a stretched/compressed spring stores elastic potential energy — with the check question whether kinetic energy can ever be negative.

## The teacher's demonstrations — watch energy change its form (teacher guide — evidence)

Your teacher's guide prescribes two demonstrations for exactly this exchange of energies. Do them, or picture them carefully:

- **The pendulum before a meter stick.** Hang a pendulum in front of a vertical meter stick and release it from a marked height on one side. Watch it rise on the other side to the same height mark. Height becomes speed becomes height again: kinetic energy and potential energy trade into each other, and the meter stick makes the trade visible.
- **The spring-powered toy.** Wind up a spring-powered toy and let it go. The work you did winding it was stored as potential energy in the spring; released, it comes back out as kinetic energy as the toy moves. Stored quietly, spent in motion — the same trade as the falling stone, in a spring instead of a height.
