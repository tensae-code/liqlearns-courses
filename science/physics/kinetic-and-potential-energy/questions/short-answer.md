**Answer: Moving bodies carry kinetic energy (½mv²); raised bodies carry gravitational potential energy (mgh); a stretched/compressed spring stores elastic potential energy — with the check question whether kinetic energy can ever be negative.**

**9.** Why is the meter stick placed behind the pendulum in the guide's demonstration?
**Answer: So the rise on the other side can be read against a height mark — making the KE↔PE trade visible: the pendulum rises to the same height it was released from.**

**10.** Where is the energy while a spring-powered toy sits wound up and still?
**Answer: It is stored as potential energy in the wound spring, put there by the work of winding; it is released as kinetic energy when the toy moves.**
