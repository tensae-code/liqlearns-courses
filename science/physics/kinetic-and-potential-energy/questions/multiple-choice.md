# Kinetic and potential energy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about kinetic and potential energy is correct?**
a) The upthrust equals the weight of fluid displaced (F_B = ρ_fluid g V), derived from the pressure difference between a…  b) Moving bodies carry kinetic energy (½mv²); raised bodies carry gravitational potential energy (mgh); a…  c) A transistor sandwiches one type between two of the other (NPN or PNP), giving emitter, base and collector; the base is…
**Answer: b) Moving bodies carry kinetic energy (½mv²); raised bodies carry gravitational potential energy (mgh); a…**

**2. Moving bodies carry kinetic energy (½mv²); raised bodies carry gravitational ______ energy (mgh); a stretched/compressed spring stores elastic potential energy — with the check question whether kinetic energy can ever be negative.**
a) parallel  b) potential  c) energy
**Answer: b) potential**

**3.** A pendulum is released from a marked height before a vertical meter stick. Ignoring friction, how high does it rise on the other side?
a) Half as high  b) To the same height mark  c) Twice as high  d) It does not rise
**Answer: b) To the same height mark**

**4.** In the spring-powered toy demonstration, what happens when the wound spring is released?
a) Potential energy stored in the spring is released as kinetic energy  b) Kinetic energy turns into mass  c) The spring creates energy from nothing  d) Energy disappears
**Answer: a) Potential energy stored in the spring is released as kinetic energy**
