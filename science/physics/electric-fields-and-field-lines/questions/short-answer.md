# Electric fields and field lines — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. Give one key fact this lesson teaches about electric field and field lines.**
**Answer: A charge fills its surroundings with a field; field strength is force per unit positive charge, and field lines picture the field — they start on positive, end on negative, never cross, and crowd where the field is strong.**
