# Electric fields and field lines — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. A charge fills its surroundings with a field; field strength is force per unit positive charge, and field lines picture the field — they start on positive, end on negative, never cross, and crowd where the field is strong.**
**Answer: True**

**2. A charge fills its surroundings with a field; field strength is force per unit negative charge, and field lines picture the field — they start on positive, end on negative, never cross, and crowd where the field is strong.**
**Answer: False**
