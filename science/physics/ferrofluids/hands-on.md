# Hands-on — Ferrofluids

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ferrofluid itself is a bought material (handle per its safety sheet, keep off skin and clothes)
Ferrofluid itself is a bought material (handle per its safety sheet, keep off skin and clothes): rest its sealed bottle or a drop in a dish on a strong magnet and sketch the spike pattern; move the magnet and watch the pattern follow. Compare with iron filings on card — same field made visible, once in a solid scatter, once in a living liquid.
