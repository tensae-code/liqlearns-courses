Today: **a ferrofluid is a liquid carrying tiny magnetic particles that stays liquid yet answers a magnet**. A ferrofluid is a stable suspension of nanoscale iron-oxide particles, each coated so they cannot clump, in a carrier oil or water. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — a liquid full of magnets; spikes along the field; uses — seals, bearings and speakers — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.
