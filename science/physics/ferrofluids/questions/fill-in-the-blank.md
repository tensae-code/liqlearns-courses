# Ferrofluids — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ferrofluid particles are made of iron ___.
**Answer:** oxide

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The spikes align with the field ___.
**Answer:** lines

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) With the magnet removed, the fluid goes ___ again.
**Answer:** flat (smooth)

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A ferrofluid seal is a liquid ___.
**Answer:** O-ring (seal)
