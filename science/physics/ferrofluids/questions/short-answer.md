# Ferrofluids — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What two natures does a ferrofluid combine?
**Answer:** It flows like a liquid and responds like a magnetic material.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain the spikes in field language.
**Answer:** The fluid is drawn toward stronger field and its surface breaks into peaks aligned with the field lines.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is the magnetisation called temporary?
**Answer:** Remove the magnet and the fluid demagnetises and flattens — nothing stays magnetised.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Give the seal use in one sentence.
**Answer:** A magnet holds ferrofluid in a rotating shaft's gap, sealing out dust and gas while the shaft turns.
