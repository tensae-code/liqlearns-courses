# Ferrofluids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A ferrofluid is…
a) A solid magnet melted down  b) A liquid carrying nanoscale magnetic particles  c) Iron filings in glue (a solid)  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) A liquid carrying nanoscale magnetic particles**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The particles stay suspended because…
a) The liquid is frozen  b) They are nanoscale and coated against clumping; thermal motion spreads them  c) They are lighter than air  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) They are nanoscale and coated against clumping; thermal motion spreads them**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Near a magnet, a ferrofluid's surface…
a) Stays perfectly flat  b) Heaps into spikes tracing the field  c) Turns to gas  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Heaps into spikes tracing the field**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Its magnetisation is like a soft magnetic material's: …
a) Permanent forever  b) Temporary — gone when the field is removed  c) Only at red heat  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Temporary — gone when the field is removed**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A ferrofluid shaft seal works because…
a) The fluid hardens into metal  b) A magnet holds the fluid in the gap as a liquid O-ring  c) Friction welds it  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) A magnet holds the fluid in the gap as a liquid O-ring**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In loudspeakers, ferrofluid…
a) Makes the sound louder by itself  b) Centres the voice coil and carries its heat away  c) Replaces the magnet  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Centres the voice coil and carries its heat away**
