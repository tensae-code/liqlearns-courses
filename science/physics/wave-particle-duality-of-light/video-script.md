Today: **Wave–particle duality of light**.

Ask light a wave question — through two slits — and it answers like a wave: bright bands, dark bands, ripples overlapping.
Ask light a particle question — shine it on metal — and it answers like particles: packets called photons, one packet knocking out one electron.
Which is it really? Both. That is the duality. Light is not a wave, not a stream of grains — it is light, and it shows the face your experiment asks for.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
