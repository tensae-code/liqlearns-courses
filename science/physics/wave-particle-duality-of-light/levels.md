# Wave–particle duality of light — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner states in their own words the wave evidence (diffraction, interference), the particle evidence (the photoelectric effect, one photon knocking out one electron with energy E = hf), and the duality itself — light answers as a wave or as particles according to the experiment — and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has three sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — sorting new phenomena into wave-evidence and particle-evidence, E = hf comparisons across the spectrum, and the gentle extension the book opens: electrons, too, can be made to paint interference bands.
