# Hands-on — Wave–particle duality of light

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rainbow on a disc
Tilt a CD or DVD in sunlight and watch the rainbow bands crawl as you tilt. Say aloud: those bands are light spreading and overlapping — interference — the wave face of light, in your hand.

## Slits from two cards
Stand two cards edge to edge with a hair-thin gap, hold them close to one eye and look at a bright window or lamp through the slit. Notice the fringes — stripes — fringing the light. Grains flying straight could not paint stripes; waves spreading through the gap can.

## Argue both sides
With a companion, run the court case: one of you argues "light is a wave" (evidence: diffraction, interference bands), the other "light is particles" (evidence: photons, the photoelectric effect). Then deliver the verdict together, in one sentence: light shows wave–particle duality.
