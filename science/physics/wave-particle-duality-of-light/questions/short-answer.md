# Wave–particle duality of light — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give two behaviours of light that only a wave picture explains.
**Answer: Diffraction — light spreading after a narrow gap; and interference — two slits producing bright and dark bands, where waves arriving out of step cancel to make darkness.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is a photon, and what sets its energy?
**Answer: A photon is one packet of light energy. Its energy is set by the light's frequency: E = hf, with h Planck's constant.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In the photoelectric effect, what does raising the light's frequency do, and what does raising its brightness do?
**Answer: Raising the frequency raises the energy of each ejected electron (each photon carries more); raising the brightness raises the number of electrons ejected (more photons arrive), not each one's energy.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why can't physics simply choose "light is a wave" as the final answer?
**Answer: Because the photoelectric effect contradicts the pure wave picture: light there arrives in separate packets, one photon freeing one electron, with a threshold frequency brightness cannot overcome. Both sets of experiments are right, so the answer must be the duality.**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) What shows that duality is not only about light?
**Answer: Electrons — particles of matter with mass — can be sent through slits and also build up interference bands, the wave behaviour; wave and particle are two faces nature's things can show.**
