# Wave–particle duality of light — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Bright and dark bands from two slits are a pattern of _____.
**Answer: interference**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) One packet of light energy is called a _____.
**Answer: photon**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A photon's energy is E = _____, proportional to the frequency.
**Answer: hf**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The two-faced behaviour of light is called wave–particle _____.
**Answer: duality**
