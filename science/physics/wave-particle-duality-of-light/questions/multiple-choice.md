# Wave–particle duality of light — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Two narrow slits lit by one light source produce a pattern of bright and dark bands. The dark bands are the strongest wave evidence because…
a) Grains would land there too  b) Only waves can cancel each other — darkness made by adding light to light  c) The screen is dirty  d) Light stops at slits
**Answer: b) Only waves can cancel each other — darkness made by adding light to light**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Light spreading out after passing through a narrow gap is called…
a) Reflection  b) Diffraction  c) Refraction  d) Conduction
**Answer: b) Diffraction**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In the photoelectric effect, light falling on a metal…
a) Melts it  b) Knocks electrons out of it  c) Charges it like a battery  d) Bends around it
**Answer: b) Knocks electrons out of it**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A metal frees no electrons under bright red light, but frees electrons under faint ultraviolet light. Why?
a) Ultraviolet is warmer  b) Each photon's energy is set by frequency (E = hf); red photons are individually too weak, and brightness cannot pool their energy  c) Red light is slower  d) The metal dislikes red
**Answer: b) Each photon's energy is set by frequency (E = hf); red photons are individually too weak, and brightness cannot pool their energy**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Making a light brighter (at the same frequency) increases…
a) The energy of each photon  b) The number of photons arriving  c) The frequency  d) Planck's constant
**Answer: b) The number of photons arriving**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Wave–particle duality means…
a) Light is a wave on weekdays and particles on weekends  b) Half of light is waves and half is grains  c) Light shows wave behaviour in wave experiments and particle behaviour in particle experiments — neither picture alone is complete  d) Scientists have given up on light
**Answer: c) Light shows wave behaviour in wave experiments and particle behaviour in particle experiments — neither picture alone is complete**
