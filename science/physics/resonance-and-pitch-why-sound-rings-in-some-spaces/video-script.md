Today: **resonance and pitch — why sound rings in some spaces**.

Fast vibration makes a high note; slow vibration makes a low one. And everything — a string, a drum, the air in a room — has a favourite rate of shaking. Sound that arrives at that favourite rate gets a big answer: the note rings. Sound at any other rate gets almost nothing. That big answer is resonance, and it is why a horn sings, a drum booms, and one room rings while the next one swallows every note.
