# Resonance and pitch — why sound rings in some spaces — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The pitch of a sound is set by…
a) Its amplitude  b) Its frequency — how many vibrations happen each second  c) How far it travels  d) The listener's distance
**Answer: b) Its frequency — how many vibrations happen each second**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A string is tightened. Its note becomes…
a) Louder only  b) Lower  c) Higher, because it vibrates faster  d) Unchanged
**Answer: c) Higher, because it vibrates faster**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Striking a drum harder changes…
a) Its pitch to a higher note  b) Its loudness, not its pitch  c) Its natural frequency  d) Nothing at all
**Answer: b) Its loudness, not its pitch**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Resonance happens when…
a) Any loud sound is played  b) Pushes arrive at exactly the object's natural frequency, each in step with the motion  c) An object is pushed once, very hard  d) The air is removed
**Answer: b) Pushes arrive at exactly the object's natural frequency, each in step with the motion**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Small pushes can build a swing very high when they are…
a) As strong as possible  b) Given at random moments  c) Timed to the swing's own natural rhythm  d) Given only once
**Answer: c) Timed to the swing's own natural rhythm**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A hall rings strongly at some notes and swallows others because…
a) The walls are painted  b) The air in the hall has natural frequencies, and only matching notes are reinforced  c) The audience is quiet  d) Sound travels faster indoors
**Answer: b) The air in the hall has natural frequencies, and only matching notes are reinforced**
