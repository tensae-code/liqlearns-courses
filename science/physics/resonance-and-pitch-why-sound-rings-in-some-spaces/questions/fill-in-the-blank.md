# Resonance and pitch — why sound rings in some spaces — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Pitch is set by _____: the faster the vibration, the higher the note.
**Answer: frequency**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Loudness is set by the _____ of the vibration, not by its frequency.
**Answer: amplitude**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The rate at which an object vibrates most easily, left to itself, is its _____ frequency.
**Answer: natural**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) An untouched string set vibrating by a matching note nearby shows _____ resonance.
**Answer: sympathetic**
