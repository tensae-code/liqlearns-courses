# Hands-on — Resonance and pitch — why sound rings in some spaces

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Blow the bottles
Take three empty bottles of different sizes, or one bottle you can fill to different levels. Blow steadily across each mouth. The air inside each bottle answers at its own natural frequency: less air space gives a higher note, more air space a lower one. Change the water level and hear the pitch climb as the air space shrinks. Say aloud the rule: the pitch you hear is the resonant frequency of the air in the bottle — you are not blowing the note, the bottle is choosing it.

## Push the swing in step
Push a swing (or a hanging weight on a string) twice: first with small pushes timed to land just as it swings back toward you, then with bigger pushes at random moments. The small in-step pushes build the swing high; the big mistimed pushes spoil it. Say aloud the rule: resonance is timing — pushes at the natural frequency build the vibration up.
