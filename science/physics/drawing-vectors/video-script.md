# Video script — Drawing vectors — arrows with scale and direction

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

A vector is drawn as an arrow.
The arrow's length is its magnitude, drawn to a stated scale — for instance 1 cm stands for 10 N — and its direction is given by an angle or a bearing from a stated reference.
In symbols, vectors are written bold or with an arrow over the letter, so a reader always knows direction is part of the quantity.
A drawing without a scale is a sketch; with a scale and bearing it is data.

Remember: **A vector is drawn as an arrow: length = magnitude, to a stated scale.**
Remember: **Direction is given by angle or bearing.**
Remember: **Vector symbols are written bold or with an arrow over the letter.**
