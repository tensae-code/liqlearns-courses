# Hands-on — Drawing vectors — arrows with scale and direction

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Draw a 40 N force at a bearing you choose, using 1 cm = 10 N. Measure your own arrow back and recover 40 N — your drawing is now data.

## Apply it where you live
Draw three real vectors (a walk, a push, a wind) to one stated scale.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21874 — one force drawn three ways — freehand, to scale, with its bearing marked] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
