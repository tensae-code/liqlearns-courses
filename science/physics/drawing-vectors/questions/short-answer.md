# Drawing vectors — arrows with scale and direction — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Scale 1 cm = 10 N. How long is the arrow for 35 N?
**Answer: 3.5 cm.**

**2.** An arrow 6 cm long at 1 cm = 5 m/s stands for what velocity?
**Answer: 30 m/s, in the arrow's direction.**

**3.** What makes a vector drawing data rather than a sketch?
**Answer: A stated scale and a stated direction (angle/bearing).**

**4.** How are vectors marked in symbols?
**Answer: Bold type or an arrow over the letter.**
