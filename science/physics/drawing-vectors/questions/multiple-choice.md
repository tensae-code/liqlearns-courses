# Drawing vectors — arrows with scale and direction — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Scale 1 cm = 10 N. How long is the arrow for 35 N?
a) 3.5 cm.  b) 30 m/s, in the arrow's direction.  c) A stated scale and a stated direction (angle/bearing).  d) Bold type or an arrow over the letter.
**Answer: a) 3.5 cm.**

**2.** An arrow 6 cm long at 1 cm = 5 m/s stands for what velocity?
a) 3.5 cm.  b) 30 m/s, in the arrow's direction.  c) A stated scale and a stated direction (angle/bearing).  d) Bold type or an arrow over the letter.
**Answer: b) 30 m/s, in the arrow's direction.**

**3.** What makes a vector drawing data rather than a sketch?
a) 3.5 cm.  b) 30 m/s, in the arrow's direction.  c) A stated scale and a stated direction (angle/bearing).  d) Bold type or an arrow over the letter.
**Answer: c) A stated scale and a stated direction (angle/bearing).**

**4.** How are vectors marked in symbols?
a) 3.5 cm.  b) 30 m/s, in the arrow's direction.  c) A stated scale and a stated direction (angle/bearing).  d) Bold type or an arrow over the letter.
**Answer: d) Bold type or an arrow over the letter.**
