# Thermoelectricity — Seebeck effect and thermocouples — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The heat-to-EMF effect at unlike-metal junctions is the _____ effect.
**Answer: Seebeck**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The junction held at a known temperature is the _____ junction.
**Answer: reference**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Traditionally the reference junction sits in melting _____.
**Answer: ice**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A driven current heating or cooling a junction is the _____ effect.
**Answer: Peltier**
