# Video script — Thermoelectricity — Seebeck effect and thermocouples

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **electricity straight out of heat**. Join copper to iron at two junctions and keep them at different temperatures, and an EMF appears — the Seebeck effect, bigger the wider the temperature gap and the further apart the metals stand in the thermoelectric series. That is a thermometer: one junction in the furnace, one in ice, and the EMF tells the temperature — tiny, fast, and tough enough for places no glass thermometer survives. Drive a current back through a junction and it heats or cools: Peltier, the same trade in reverse.
