# Hands-on — Thermoelectricity — Seebeck effect and thermocouples

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Warm one twist
If a school thermocouple and millivoltmeter are available: hold the measuring junction between warm fingers while the reference sits in cool water and watch the EMF rise; move your hand away and watch it fall. Say aloud: the meter is reading a temperature difference, not a temperature.

## Swap the heat
Warm the reference junction instead and watch the sign of the EMF reverse. Say aloud: the effect belongs to the difference and its direction — swap hot and cold and the current turns around.
