# Video script — Kirchhoff's laws — branching circuits

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **two conservation laws solve any circuit**. At a junction, currents in equal currents out — charge cannot pile up. Round a loop, gains and drops sum to zero — energy cannot appear from nowhere. Label the branches, write the equations, and branching circuits open up.
