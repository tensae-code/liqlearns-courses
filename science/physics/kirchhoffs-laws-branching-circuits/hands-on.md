# Hands-on — Kirchhoff's laws — branching circuits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Junction sums with real meters
A teacher wires a low-voltage source across two different lamps in parallel, with an ammeter in the main line and one in each branch (or one ammeter moved between the three places). Read all three currents: the main current matches the sum of the two branch currents, every time, whichever lamp is brighter. Then swap one lamp for a different one and repeat. You have measured the junction rule — charge neither appears nor disappears at the split.
