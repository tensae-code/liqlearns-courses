# Kirchhoff's laws — branching circuits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Kirchhoff's junction rule rests on conservation of…
a) charge  b) energy  c) momentum  d) mass of the wires
**Answer: a) charge**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) 4 A and 1 A enter a junction; one branch leaves with 3 A. The other leaves with…
a) 2 A  b) 5 A  c) 3 A  d) 7 A
**Answer: a) 2 A**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Kirchhoff's loop rule rests on conservation of…
a) energy  b) charge  c) current  d) resistance
**Answer: a) energy**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Round a loop, the signed sum of source rises and resistor drops is…
a) zero  b) the EMF  c) the total current  d) always positive
**Answer: a) zero**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A negative solved branch current means…
a) the current really runs opposite to the assumed direction  b) an arithmetic error for certain  c) the battery is flat  d) the junction rule failed
**Answer: a) the current really runs opposite to the assumed direction**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A 12 V source across parallel 2 Ω and 4 Ω branches gives branch currents…
a) 6 A and 3 A  b) 3 A and 6 A  c) 2 A and 4 A  d) 9 A and 9 A
**Answer: a) 6 A and 3 A**
