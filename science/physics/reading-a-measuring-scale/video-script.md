# Video script — Reading a measuring scale

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

A scale is the set of marks on a measuring device.
The more marks there are, the finer the precision — you can read smaller differences.
To read a scale, find the last numbered mark the pointer or edge has passed, then estimate one extra decimal place for how far it sits between that mark and the next.
That estimated last digit is part of the reading; length, mass and temperature scales are all read this way.

Remember: **A scale is the set of marks on a measuring device.**
Remember: **More marks on a scale means finer precision.**
Remember: **A reading takes one extra estimated decimal place between two marks.**
