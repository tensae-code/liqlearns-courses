# Reading a measuring scale — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** What is a scale?
**Answer: The set of marks on a measuring device.**

**2.** What do more marks on a scale give you?
**Answer: Finer precision.**

**3.** The edge sits just past the 7 mark, about halfway to 8. How do you read it?
**Answer: About 7.5 — the last digit is estimated between the marks.**

**4.** Which digit of a careful reading is estimated?
**Answer: The last digit.**
