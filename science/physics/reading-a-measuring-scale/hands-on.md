# Hands-on — Reading a measuring scale

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Take a ruler. Lay a pencil along it so its end falls between two marks. Read the length out loud, including your estimated last digit.

## Apply it where you live
Read three scales at home — a ruler, a jug, a thermometer if you have one — estimating the last digit each time.
