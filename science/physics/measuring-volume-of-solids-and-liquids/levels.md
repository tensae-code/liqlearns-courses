# Measuring volume of solids and liquids — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — volume is measured three honest ways — by formula for regular shapes, by reading a measuring cylinder for liquids, and by displacement for irregular solids — and every way starts by finding the scale's division value — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
