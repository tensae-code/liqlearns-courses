# Measuring volume of solids and liquids — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The volume of a cylinder is πr² × ___.
**Answer:** height (h)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The curved surface of a liquid in a cylinder is called the ___.
**Answer:** meniscus

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Reading a scale from above or below causes ___ error.
**Answer:** parallax

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) V₂ − V₁, the rise in level, equals the ___ of the immersed body.
**Answer:** volume

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A body too large for a cylinder is measured with an ___ vessel.
**Answer:** overflow
