# Measuring volume of solids and liquids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The volume of a cuboid is found by…
a) Adding its three sides  b) Multiplying length × width × height  c) Dividing length by width  d) Measuring its mass
**Answer: b) Multiplying length × width × height**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The volume of a sphere of radius r is…
a) πr²  b) 2πr³  c) (4/3)πr³  d) 4πr²
**Answer: c) (4/3)πr³**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Before reading a measuring cylinder you first find…
a) Its mass  b) The division value of its scale  c) Its temperature  d) The colour of the liquid
**Answer: b) The division value of its scale**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A liquid's level in a cylinder is read…
a) From above, quickly  b) At the top of the meniscus  c) At the bottom of the meniscus, eye level with it  d) Through the glass from below
**Answer: c) At the bottom of the meniscus, eye level with it**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) An irregular solid's volume is measured by…
a) Weighing it  b) Displacement — the rise of water level  c) Melting it  d) Counting its corners
**Answer: b) Displacement — the rise of water level**
