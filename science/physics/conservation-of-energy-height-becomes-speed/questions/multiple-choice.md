**1. Which statement about conservation of mechanical energy is correct?**
a) Without friction, KE + PE stays constant: problems follow blocks on frictionless tracks and inclines and a diver/swing…  b) Output is 1 only when all inputs are 1 (the multiplication of logic), pictured as switches in series.  c) Gravity supplies the centripetal force of orbits: orbital speed from GM/r, recovering Kepler's third law (K = 4π²/GM)…
**Answer: a) Without friction, KE + PE stays constant: problems follow blocks on frictionless tracks and inclines and a…**

**2. Without ______, KE + PE stays constant: problems follow blocks on frictionless tracks and inclines and a diver/swing through points B and C, finding speeds from heights alone.**
a) friction  b) vector  c) acceleration
**Answer: a) friction**
