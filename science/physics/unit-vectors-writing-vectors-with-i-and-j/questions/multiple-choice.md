# Unit vectors — writing vectors with i and j — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about unit vectors and the i, j notation is correct?**
a) A unit vector has magnitude one and only points; the special unit vectors i and j along the x and y axes let any vector…  b) Four diodes in a bridge flip the negative half-cycles up, filling the gaps; a capacitor across the output smooths the…  c) Summary: charges are quantised, Coulomb's law, fields, Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's rules, capacitance; end questions span…
**Answer: a) A unit vector has magnitude one and only points; the special unit vectors i and j along the x and y axes let…**

**2. A unit vector has magnitude one and only points; the special unit vectors i and j along the x and y axes let any vector be written as a combination of ______s, and resultants are found by adding matching components.**
a) component  b) radar  c) energy
**Answer: a) component**
