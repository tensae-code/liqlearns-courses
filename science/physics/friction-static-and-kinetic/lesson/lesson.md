## Friction — what it is and its two kinds

Friction opposes relative motion or its tendency: static friction adjusts up to a maximum, kinetic friction is roughly constant once sliding; both are proportional to the normal force through coefficients of friction.

A pushed puck is compared on a table, on ice, and on an air-hockey table: the opposing (friction) force is greatest on the table and least on the air cushion, and the puck travels farthest where the opposition is least — the guide's printed answer makes the surface–friction–distance chain explicit.
