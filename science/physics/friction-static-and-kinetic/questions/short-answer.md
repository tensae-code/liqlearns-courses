# Friction — static and kinetic — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. Give one key fact this lesson teaches about friction.**
**Answer: Friction opposes relative motion or its tendency: static friction adjusts up to a maximum, kinetic friction is roughly constant once sliding; both are proportional to the normal force through coefficients of friction.**
