# Falling and thrown objects — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. Objects thrown up or dropped move with acceleration g; the kinematic equations are applied to rises, falls and meeting points, with the sign convention for upward motion made explicit.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Objects thrown up or dropped move with acceleration g; the kinematic equations are applied to falls, falls and meeting points, with the sign convention for upward motion made explicit.**
**Answer: False**
