# Hands-on — Unit 4 review — electromagnetism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make a magnet work
Wrap wire many times around a large nail, join the ends to a cell, and see how many paper clips the nail can lift. Unwrap ten turns and try again. More turns, stronger magnet.

## See it and say it
Study this lesson's picture — the scene the lesson describes — and name every part of it out loud. Then close the picture and draw it from memory; check your drawing against the lesson.

## Teach it back
Explain this lesson to someone at home using one thing in the room as your example. Their questions show you which part to re-read.
