# Hands-on — Multiplying vectors — dot and cross products

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel cos θ and sin θ with a door
Use a door and a gentle push (with permission, and never slamming). Push at the handle, at right angles to the door's face: it swings easily — sin θ at its largest for the turning effect. Push the same handle toward the hinges, along the door's face: no swing at all. Now do work against a dragging box or a heavy book across a table, pushing first along its motion and then at a steep angle: feel how only the along-motion part moves it. Write both experiences as one dot-product sentence and one cross-product sentence.
