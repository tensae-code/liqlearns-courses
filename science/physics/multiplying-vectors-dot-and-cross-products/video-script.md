Today: **two ways to multiply vectors**. The dot product, A·B = AB cos θ, gives a number — how much they work together — and it is hiding inside work, W = F·s. The cross product gives a whole new vector, size AB sin θ, pointing perpendicular to both — find it with your right hand — and it is hiding inside torque. Number needed? Dot. New direction needed? Cross.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
