# Multiplying vectors — dot and cross products — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A·B for vectors of sizes 5 and 4 at 60° between them is…
a) 20  b) 10  c) 0  d) 17.3
**Answer: b) 10**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Two perpendicular non-zero vectors have a dot product of…
a) AB  b) −AB  c) Exactly zero  d) AB/2
**Answer: c) Exactly zero**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The size of A×B is largest when the vectors are…
a) Parallel  b) Perpendicular  c) Opposite  d) Equal
**Answer: b) Perpendicular**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Swapping a cross product's order, B×A instead of A×B,…
a) Changes nothing  b) Doubles it  c) Reverses its direction  d) Makes it a scalar
**Answer: c) Reverses its direction**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Torque τ = r×F is a…
a) Dot product, giving a scalar  b) Cross product, giving a vector perpendicular to both r and F  c) Sum of two vectors  d) Division of vectors
**Answer: b) Cross product, giving a vector perpendicular to both r and F**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) To test whether two vectors are perpendicular, the quickest product check is…
a) Cross product equals AB  b) Dot product equals zero  c) Dot product equals AB  d) Cross product equals zero
**Answer: b) Dot product equals zero**
