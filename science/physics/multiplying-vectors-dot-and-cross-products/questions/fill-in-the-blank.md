# Multiplying vectors — dot and cross products — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) W = F·s is a _____ product.
**Answer: dot (scalar)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The direction of A×B is found with the _____-hand rule.
**Answer: right**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) î × ĵ = _____.
**Answer: k̂**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A cross product is always _____ to both of its parent vectors.
**Answer: perpendicular**
