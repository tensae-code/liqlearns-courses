# Multiplying vectors — dot and cross products — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A force does no work on a body that moves at right angles to the force. Which product explains this, and how?
**Answer: The dot product: W = Fs cos 90° = 0, because only the component of force along the displacement does work.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State the size and direction of A×B when A and B are parallel.
**Answer: Size zero (sin 0° = 0), so there is no cross-product vector at all — parallel vectors have no perpendicular product.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why must a student state a direction for τ but not for W?
**Answer: Work is a dot product — a scalar with no direction; torque is a cross product — a vector, meaningless until its direction (its axis) is stated.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Calculate A·B for A = 2î + 3ĵ and B = 5î − ĵ.
**Answer: A·B = (2)(5) + (3)(−1) = 10 − 3 = 7.**
