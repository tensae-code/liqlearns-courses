# Half-wave rectification — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about half-wave rectification is correct?**
a) The teacher is told to support the class to conduct it properly and prepare the materials ahead of the lesson.  b) The second product gives a new vector perpendicular to both parents, with magnitude from the sine of the angle between…  c) One diode passes only the positive half-cycles: AC in, pulsating DC out — half the wave thrown away.
**Answer: c) One diode passes only the positive half-cycles: AC in, pulsating DC out — half the wave thrown away.**

**2. One ______ passes only the positive half-cycles: AC in, pulsating DC out — half the wave thrown away.**
a) acceleration  b) magnet  c) diode
**Answer: c) diode**
