# Hands-on — Electrical power; which lamp is efficient

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the loop
With a cell, wires and a small lamp, build one complete loop so the lamp lights. Break the loop — the lamp dies. Draw the circuit and mark where the ammeter and voltmeter would go.

## See it and say it
Study this lesson's picture — the three lamp types with their wattages for the same light output — and name every part of it out loud. Then close the picture and draw it from memory; check your drawing against the lesson.

## Teach it back
Explain this lesson to someone at home using one thing in the room as your example. Their questions show you which part to re-read.
