# Hands-on — The uniform electric field

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel a push that changes, then imagine it even
Charge a comb by running it through dry hair and bring it slowly toward small paper scraps on a table: far away nothing happens, nearer they tremble, closer still they leap. The push grew as you approached — a non-uniform field. Now picture the same scraps between two big charged plates: the push would be identical at every point of the gap, scraps anywhere would leap identically. Write down one place this evenness matters (think of the electron beam in an old television, steered by plates) — the lesson names it and works it.
