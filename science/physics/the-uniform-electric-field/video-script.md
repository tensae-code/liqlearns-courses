Today: **the uniform electric field**. Between two parallel plates the field is the same everywhere — E = V/d, lines straight, parallel and evenly spaced. A charge inside feels a constant force F = qE, accelerates constantly, and a charge fired across the field traces a parabola — the physics inside the cathode-ray tube and Millikan's oil-drop experiment.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
