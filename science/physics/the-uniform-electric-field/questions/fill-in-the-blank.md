# The uniform electric field — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Between parallel plates the field lines are straight, _____ and evenly spaced.
**Answer: parallel**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The unit V/m for field strength equals the unit _____ per coulomb.
**Answer: newton**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A charge in a uniform field moves with _____ acceleration, because the force on it is constant.
**Answer: constant**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The path of a charge fired across a uniform field is a _____.
**Answer: parabola**
