# The uniform electric field — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A uniform electric field has…
**Answer: a) the same strength and direction at every point  b) lines that spread out from a point  c) strength that grows toward the plates  d) no direction**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The field between plates at potential difference V separated by d is…
**Answer: a) E = V/d  b) E = Vd  c) E = d/V  d) E = V²/d**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A positive charge in a uniform field feels a force…
**Answer: a) along the field, from the positive plate toward the negative  b) toward the positive plate  c) in circles  d) only when it moves**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) 100 V across a 2.0 cm gap gives a field of…
**Answer: a) 5,000 V/m — E = 100 ÷ 0.020  b) 200 V/m  c) 2 V/m  d) 50,000 V/m**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A charge fired across a uniform field follows a parabola because…
**Answer: a) its forward velocity stays constant while a constant force accelerates it sideways  b) the field curves  c) gravity bends it  d) the plates attract it in turns**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) One electron crossing a potential difference of 1 V gains…
**Answer: a) 1 eV of energy  b) 1 volt of charge  c) 1 coulomb  d) 1 newton**
