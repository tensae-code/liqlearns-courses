# How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 10 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which unit opens Physics X and what is it about?
**Answer:** Unit 10 — Simple Harmonic Motion and Waves.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the Class X pattern help a learner arriving from Class IX?
**Answer:** Only the physics changes; the way each unit is built stays the same.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What does an index let a reader do that a contents list does not?
**Answer:** Look up any named idea directly and find every page that treats it.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) What earlier Punjab book does the bibliography name in this series' lineage?
**Answer:** The Punjab Textbook Board Physics 10 lineage, from its 2003 first edition.
