# How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 10 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Physics X's units are numbered…
a) 1 to 9 again  b) 10 to 18, continuing Physics IX  c) 19 to 27  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) 10 to 18, continuing Physics IX**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The continued numbering shows that Punjab treats Grades 9–10 as…
a) Unrelated books  b) One two-year course  c) An examination only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) One two-year course**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Class X keeps from Class IX…
a) Only the cover design  b) The same unit pattern of outcomes, boxes and question sets  c) None of the pattern  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) The same unit pattern of outcomes, boxes and question sets**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Unit 17 of Physics X is…
a) Thermodynamics  b) Information and Communication Technology  c) Mechanics  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Information and Communication Technology**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Physics X closes with…
a) A blank page  b) A full index and a bibliography  c) The Grade 9 summary  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) A full index and a bibliography**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The bibliography's value to the learner is that it…
a) Lists examination marks  b) Names the book's sources and lineage  c) Replaces the index  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Names the book's sources and lineage**
