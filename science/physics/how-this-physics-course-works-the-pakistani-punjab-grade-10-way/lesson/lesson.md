# How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 10 way

## Do it first
Open Physics X at its index and bibliography before any unit — begin with the hands-on opening in hands-on.md before reading on. This lesson is the story of that activity.

## Sub lesson 1 — One course, two books — units 10 to 18

Class X does not start again at Unit 1. Its nine units are numbered 10–18, continuing Class IX's 1–9, because Punjab treats the two years as one course: Simple Harmonic Motion and Waves is Unit 10 precisely because Thermal Properties was Unit 8 and Transfer of Heat Unit 9. The numbering itself is the Conceptual Linkage of the Grade 9 pattern, written across two books.

## Sub lesson 2 — The same unit pattern, carried through

Each Class X unit keeps the Grade 9 architecture — outcomes and major concepts first, investigation and society boxes through the text, sidebars for quick checks — so a learner moving from Class IX to Class X changes only the physics, never the way of learning it. Sound, geometrical optics, electrostatics, current electricity, electromagnetism, basic electronics, ICT and nuclear physics all arrive in the same shape.

## Sub lesson 3 — An index and a bibliography close the course

The book ends with a full index (printed p. 199) and a bibliography (printed pp. 200–201) that names its lineage, including the Punjab Textbook Board Physics 10 line back to its 2003 first edition. A course that shows its sources and lets you look anything up by name is treating the learner as a reader of physics, not only a passer of units.
