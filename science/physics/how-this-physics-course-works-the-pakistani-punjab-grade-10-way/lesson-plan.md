## Sub lessons
1. **One course, two books — units 10 to 18** — Class X does not start again at Unit 1. Its nine units are numbered 10–18, continuing Class IX's 1–9, because Punjab treats the two years as one course: Simple Harmonic Motion and Waves is Unit 10 precisely because Thermal Properties was Unit 8 and Transfer of Heat Unit 9. The numbering itself is the Conceptual Linkage of the Grade 9 pattern, written across two books.
2. **The same unit pattern, carried through** — Each Class X unit keeps the Grade 9 architecture — outcomes and major concepts first, investigation and society boxes through the text, sidebars for quick checks — so a learner moving from Class IX to Class X changes only the physics, never the way of learning it. Sound, geometrical optics, electrostatics, current electricity, electromagnetism, basic electronics, ICT and nuclear physics all arrive in the same shape.
3. **An index and a bibliography close the course** — The book ends with a full index (printed p. 199) and a bibliography (printed pp. 200–201) that names its lineage, including the Punjab Textbook Board Physics 10 line back to its 2003 first edition. A course that shows its sources and lets you look anything up by name is treating the learner as a reader of physics, not only a passer of units.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
