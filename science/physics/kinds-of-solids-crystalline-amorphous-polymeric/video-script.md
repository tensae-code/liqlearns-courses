# Video script — Kinds of solids — crystalline, amorphous, polymeric

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **solids sort by how their particles are arranged**. In a crystal, atoms, ions or molecules sit in a pattern that repeats in three dimensions — the lattice (salt, metals, quartz). Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — crystalline solids — the repeating lattice; amorphous solids — order that never formed; polymeric solids — the chain-built kind — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.
