# Hands-on — Kinds of solids — crystalline, amorphous, polymeric

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort a tray of solids by structure before by name
Sort a tray of solids by structure before by name: a salt or sugar crystal (look for flat faces), a piece of glass or candle wax (softens gradually over a flame-warmed knife, teacher-supervised), and a polythene strip (stretches). Record which soften sharply, which gradually, which stretch — and match each behaviour to its arrangement.
