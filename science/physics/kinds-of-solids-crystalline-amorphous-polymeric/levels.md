# Kinds of solids — crystalline, amorphous, polymeric — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — In a crystal, atoms, ions or molecules sit in a pattern that repeats in three dimensions — the lattice (salt, metals, quartz); Glass, rubber-like solids and many plastics have particles arranged without long-range order — a liquid's disorder, frozen; Polymers are solids whose molecules are long chains of repeating units — natural (rubber, cellulose) and synthetic (polythene, nylon) — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
