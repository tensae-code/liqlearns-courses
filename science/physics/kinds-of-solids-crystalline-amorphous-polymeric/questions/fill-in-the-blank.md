# Kinds of solids — crystalline, amorphous, polymeric — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The repeating pattern inside a crystal is its ___.
**Answer:** lattice

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Amorphous means without long-range ___.
**Answer:** order

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Polymers are built of long ___ of repeating units.
**Answer:** chains

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Glass softens over a ___ of temperatures, not at a point.
**Answer:** range
