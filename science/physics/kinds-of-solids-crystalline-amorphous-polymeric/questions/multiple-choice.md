# Kinds of solids — crystalline, amorphous, polymeric — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A crystalline solid's particles sit in…
a) Frozen disorder  b) A regular repeating lattice  c) Long chains only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) A regular repeating lattice**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A sharp melting point belongs to…
a) Amorphous solids  b) Crystalline solids  c) All solids equally  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Crystalline solids**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Glass is the standard example of…
a) A crystal  b) An amorphous solid  c) A polymer crystal  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) An amorphous solid**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An amorphous solid on heating…
a) Melts at one sharp temperature  b) Softens over a range of temperatures  c) Sublimes always  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Softens over a range of temperatures**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Polymeric solids are built from…
a) Single atoms only  b) Long chains of repeating units  c) Ions in a lattice  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Long chains of repeating units**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Polythene and nylon are…
a) Crystalline salts  b) Synthetic polymers  c) Amorphous metals  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Synthetic polymers**
