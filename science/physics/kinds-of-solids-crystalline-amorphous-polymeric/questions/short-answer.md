# Kinds of solids — crystalline, amorphous, polymeric — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What decides a solid's kind in Punjab's classification?
**Answer:** How its particles are arranged — lattice, frozen disorder, or chains.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why do crystals cleave along flat planes?
**Answer:** The lattice has regular planes of weakness along which it splits cleanly.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the heating test that separates amorphous from crystalline.
**Answer:** Amorphous softens gradually over a range; crystalline melts sharply at one temperature.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why can polymers stretch?
**Answer:** Their long chains can slide and uncoil past one another (unless cross-linked).
