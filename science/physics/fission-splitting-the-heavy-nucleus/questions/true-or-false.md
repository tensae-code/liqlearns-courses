# Fission — splitting the heavy nucleus — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. A neutron slamming into a heavy nucleus splits it into two smaller fission products plus more neutrons and energy; the released neutrons can trigger a chain reaction, controlled inside a nuclear reactor.**
**Answer: True**

**2. A neutron slamming into a heavy nucleus splits it into two smaller fission products plus less neutrons and energy; the released neutrons can trigger a chain reaction, controlled inside a nuclear reactor.**
**Answer: False**
