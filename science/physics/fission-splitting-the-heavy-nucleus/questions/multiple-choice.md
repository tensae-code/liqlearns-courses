# Fission — splitting the heavy nucleus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about nuclear fission is correct?**
a) Melting, freezing, boiling, evaporation and condensation swap heat without changing temperature while they last; the…  b) A force twists when it acts off-centre: torque grows with the force and with the lever arm (the door-handle idea), and…  c) A neutron slamming into a heavy nucleus splits it into two smaller fission products plus more neutrons and energy; the…
**Answer: c) A neutron slamming into a heavy nucleus splits it into two smaller fission products plus more neutrons and…**

**2. A neutron slamming into a heavy nucleus splits it into two smaller fission products plus more neutrons and energy; the released neutrons can trigger a ______, controlled inside a nuclear reactor.**
a) potential  b) component  c) chain reaction
**Answer: c) chain reaction**
