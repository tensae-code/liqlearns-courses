# Video script — How this Physics course works — the Syrian Grade 9 way

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **how a Syrian Grade 9 physics lesson works**. Before any teaching, the page tells you the objectives — what you will be able to do — and the key words. Then you do not read the answer first: you try the experiment yourself, watch what happens, and only then write what you conclude, in the book's own conclusion box. Worked applications show the idea earning its keep, enrichment boxes stretch it further, and every unit ends with questions and a project you build with your hands. Learn this shape once, and every lesson in the book opens the same way.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
