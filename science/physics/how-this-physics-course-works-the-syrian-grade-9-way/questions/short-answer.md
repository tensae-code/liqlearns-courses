# How this Physics course works — the Syrian Grade 9 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does printing the objectives first help the learner?
**Answer: The learner knows the exact target before starting — what they will be able to do — so every activity in the lesson can be judged against it, and success at the end is checkable rather than a feeling.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the difference between the experiment and the "I conclude" box?
**Answer: The experiment is what the learner does and observes with their hands; the "I conclude" box is where the observed result is stated as the lesson's law, in the learner's own finding — doing first, law second.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What do enrichment boxes (إثراء) do?
**Answer: They stretch the lesson's idea further for learners ready for more — extra depth around the same conclusion, not a different lesson.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) How is a unit project different from the unit questions?
**Answer: The questions test the unit's ideas one by one; the project makes them work together in a single designed, built thing — application of the whole unit at once.**
