# How this Physics course works — the Syrian Grade 9 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Syrian book's units are organised…
a) Alphabetically by topic  b) As a course journey — each unit a stage of the year's physics  c) By exam date  d) Randomly
**Answer: b) As a course journey — each unit a stage of the year's physics**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Every Syrian lesson opens with…
a) A poem  b) Printed objectives and key words  c) The answers  d) A story
**Answer: b) Printed objectives and key words**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) "I try and I conclude" means the learner…
a) Copies the law from the board  b) Runs the bench experiment, then states the finding in the "I conclude" box  c) Guesses before reading  d) Skips the experiment
**Answer: b) Runs the bench experiment, then states the finding in the "I conclude" box**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A worked application (تطبيق محلول) is…
a) An unsolved exercise  b) A real problem solved step by step with the lesson's idea  c) A glossary  d) A picture caption
**Answer: b) A real problem solved step by step with the lesson's idea**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Every unit closes with…
a) Nothing  b) Unit questions plus a hands-on project  c) Only a test  d) A song
**Answer: b) Unit questions plus a hands-on project**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The unit project matters because…
a) It fills the last page  b) The unit's separate conclusions must work together in one made thing  c) It is marked twice  d) It replaces the questions
**Answer: b) The unit's separate conclusions must work together in one made thing**
