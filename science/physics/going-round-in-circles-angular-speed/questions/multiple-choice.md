# Going round in circles — angular speed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about uniform circular motion is correct?**
a) For incompressible flow A₁v₁ = A₂v₂: narrow pipe, faster flow — related by pupils to rivers and hoses in the section's…  b) Motion round a circle at constant speed: angular displacement, angular velocity and tangential (linear) speed are…  c) Blood circulation runs on fluid-flow physics (viscosity, continuity, turbulence) and soft-bodied animals like the…
**Answer: b) Motion round a circle at constant speed: angular displacement, angular velocity and tangential (linear) speed…**

**2. Motion round a circle at constant speed: angular ______, angular velocity and tangential (linear) speed are defined and related, with period and frequency in the background of the worked examples.**
a) potential  b) displacement  c) pressure
**Answer: b) displacement**
