# Heat in the eyes of Farabi, Beruni and Avicenna — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does classifying heat matter — what does it change?
**Answer: It moves heat from magic and omen into a system of topics with causes, where claims can be argued, taught and tested — the precondition for heat becoming physics.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State one strength and one limit of Beruni's comparison method for heat.
**Answer: Strength: fair side-by-side comparisons give true orderings (which spring is hotter, which season warmer) with no instruments. Limit: without a scale he could not say how much hotter, or compare results across distant places and years.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In what way does Avicenna's medical heat face the same facts as the modern theory?
**Answer: He worked with friction warming, fever burning and compresses drawing heat away — the same phenomena the kinetic theory explains as particle motion and energy transfer; his framework differed, the observations were real.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Which of the three scholars' heat work is closest to a modern physics laboratory habit, and why?
**Answer: Beruni's — measuring and comparing under controlled likeness (same vessels, same conditions) is the laboratory method itself, lacking only the scaled instrument.**
