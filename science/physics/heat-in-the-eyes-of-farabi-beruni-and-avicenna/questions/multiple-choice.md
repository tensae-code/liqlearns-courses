# Heat in the eyes of Farabi, Beruni and Avicenna — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Farabi's contribution to the study of heat was…
**Answer: a) placing heat and cold in a classification of the sciences, as topics with causes  b) inventing the thermometer  c) discovering particles  d) building a steam engine**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In Farabi's map, heat belonged to…
**Answer: a) natural philosophy  b) astrology  c) music only  d) law**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Without a thermometer, Beruni studied heat by…
**Answer: a) comparison — spring against spring, season against season  b) guessing  c) reading omens  d) weighing it**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) One question Beruni pressed on Avicenna was…
**Answer: a) whether heat can travel through empty space, as light seems to  b) how to build a car  c) what colour heat is  d) who invented fire**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In Avicenna's Canon, fever is treated as…
**Answer: a) a disorder of the body's own heat  b) a punishment  c) a kind of light  d) unrelated to heat**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Avicenna used heating and cooling…
**Answer: a) as treatments, dosed like drugs  b) never in medicine  c) only for cooking  d) only in winter**
