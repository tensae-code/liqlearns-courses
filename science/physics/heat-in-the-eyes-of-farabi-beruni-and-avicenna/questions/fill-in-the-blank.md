# Heat in the eyes of Farabi, Beruni and Avicenna — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Farabi wrote the Classification of the _____.
**Answer: Sciences**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Beruni's instrument for heat was _____: this spring against that.
**Answer: comparison**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Beruni described and compared hot _____ in his writings on heat.
**Answer: springs**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Avicenna's great medical book is called the _____.
**Answer: Canon**
