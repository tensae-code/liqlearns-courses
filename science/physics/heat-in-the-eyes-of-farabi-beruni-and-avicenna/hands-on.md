# Hands-on — Heat in the eyes of Farabi, Beruni and Avicenna

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Beruni's comparison
Fill two identical cups with warm water. Wrap one in cloth, leave the other bare. Every two minutes, feel both (or use a thermometer if you have one) and record which is warmer. At the end, write Beruni's sentence: which lost heat faster, and what carried it away? You have done his method — comparison, honestly recorded — with better instruments.
