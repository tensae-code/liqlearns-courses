# Hands-on — Thermodynamics — the laws and heat engines

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work becomes heat — the pump
Seal a bicycle pump's outlet with a finger and pump fast, twenty strokes. Feel the barrel: it is warm. No fire, no electricity — your work on the air became the air's internal energy, and the barrel shows it. Say aloud: first law — the energy had to come from somewhere, and it came from me.

## Heat runs downhill — the two bowls
Put a metal spoon with its handle in a bowl of warm water and its bowl-end in a bowl of cold water. Wait two minutes, then feel both ends. The warmth travelled one way only — hot to cold — and it will never travel back by itself. Say aloud: second law — heat runs downhill and does not climb back.

## Hunt the cold sink
Find one real engine — a car, a motorcycle, a generator, a refrigerator — and find where it dumps its rejected heat: the radiator, the exhaust pipe, the warm coils at the fridge's back. Point at it and say: that is the cold sink; the engine could not work without somewhere cold to throw heat into.
