# Thermodynamics — the laws and heat engines — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The first law of thermodynamics says…
a) Heat always flows from cold to hot  b) The change in internal energy equals heat added minus work done by the system — energy is conserved  c) Engines can create energy from nothing  d) Temperature and heat are the same thing
**Answer: b) The change in internal energy equals heat added minus work done by the system — energy is conserved**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A gas absorbs 400 J of heat and does 150 J of work. Its internal energy changes by…
a) 550 J  b) −250 J  c) 250 J  d) 150 J
**Answer: c) 250 J**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The second law of thermodynamics says heat, by itself, flows…
a) From cold to hot  b) In whichever direction balances the books  c) From hot to cold — and no engine can turn all its heat into work  d) Only inside engines
**Answer: c) From hot to cold — and no engine can turn all its heat into work**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A refrigerator must be plugged in because…
a) Its light needs power  b) Moving heat from cold to hot never happens by itself — work must be paid in  c) Cold air is heavy  d) The second law does not apply without electricity
**Answer: b) Moving heat from cold to hot never happens by itself — work must be paid in**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A heat engine takes in 200 J of heat and does 50 J of work. Its efficiency is…
a) 50%  b) 25%  c) 75%  d) 4%
**Answer: b) 25%**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The best possible efficiency of a heat engine (Carnot's limit) depends only on…
a) The engine's size  b) The brand of fuel  c) The temperatures of the hot source and the cold sink  d) How fast it runs
**Answer: c) The temperatures of the hot source and the cold sink**
