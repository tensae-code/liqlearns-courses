# Thermodynamics — the laws and heat engines — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The first law is written ΔU = Q − _____, where Q is heat added and the missing symbol is work done by the system.
**Answer: W**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The quantity that measures spreading-out or disorder, and never decreases for the universe as a whole, is called _____.
**Answer: entropy**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The third law concerns the temperature that can be approached but never reached: _____ zero.
**Answer: absolute**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) An engine's efficiency is work out divided by _____ in.
**Answer: heat**
