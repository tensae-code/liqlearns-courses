**2. Unstable nuclei disintegrate spontaneously: alpha emission drops A by 4 and Z by 2, beta-minus raises Z by 1 (beta-plus lowers it), and gamma rays are high-energy electromagnetic ______ that usually accompanies the others; the decay equations are written in A/Z notation.**
