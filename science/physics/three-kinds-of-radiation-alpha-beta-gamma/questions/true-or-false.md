# Three kinds of radiation — alpha, beta, gamma — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. Unstable nuclei disintegrate spontaneously: alpha emission drops A by 4 and Z by 2, beta-minus raises Z by 1 (beta-plus lowers it), and gamma rays are high-energy electromagnetic radiation that usually accompanies the others; the decay equations are written in A/Z notation.**
**Answer: True**
