# Three kinds of radiation — alpha, beta, gamma — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. Give one key fact this lesson teaches about radioactivity.**
**Answer: Unstable nuclei disintegrate spontaneously: alpha emission drops A by 4 and Z by 2, beta-minus raises Z by 1 (beta-plus lowers it), and gamma rays are high-energy electromagnetic radiation that usually accompanies the others; the decay equations are written in A/Z notation.**
