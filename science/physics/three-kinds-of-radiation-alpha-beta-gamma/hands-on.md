# Hands-on — Three kinds of radiation — alpha, beta, gamma

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Halving with coins
Start with 64 coins. Shake and remove half, again and again, writing the count each round: 64, 32, 16, 8… Each round is one half-life. Plot the counts.

## See it and say it
Study this lesson's picture — one nucleus emitting each radiation type in three panels, stopped by paper, aluminium and lead respectively — and name every part of it out loud. Then close the picture and draw it from memory; check your drawing against the lesson.

## Teach it back
Explain this lesson to someone at home using one thing in the room as your example. Their questions show you which part to re-read.
