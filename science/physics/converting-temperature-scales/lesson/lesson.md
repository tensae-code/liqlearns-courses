# Converting Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin

## Converting Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin — the teaching
The three scales are tied by their fixed points. Celsius to Fahrenheit: °F = (9/5 × °C) + 32. Fahrenheit to Celsius: °C = (5/9) × (°F − 32). Kelvin shares Celsius's step size and starts at absolute zero: K = °C + 273.15. Work both ways until the relations are tools, not spells — and the same fixed-point method converts even a scale you invent yourself, like the review's pupil-designed °X.

**Keep the formula:** °F = (9/5)°C + 32; K = °C + 273.15

## The key ideas

- °F = (9/5)°C + 32 and °C = (5/9)(°F − 32).
- K = °C + 273.15; Kelvin's floor is absolute zero.
- Kelvin and Celsius have the same step size.

## Say it to your companion
Explain converting celsius, fahrenheit and kelvin to your companion in your own words. Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
