# Converting Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Convert 25 °C to Fahrenheit.
**Answer: °F = (9/5 × 25) + 32 = 45 + 32 = 77 °F.**

**2.** Convert 98 °F to Celsius.
**Answer: °C = (5/9)(98 − 32) = (5/9)(66) ≈ 36.7 °C.**

**3.** Convert 27 °C to kelvin.
**Answer: K = 27 + 273.15 = 300.15 K.**

**4.** What is absolute zero in Celsius?
**Answer: −273.15 °C (0 K).**
