# Converting Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Convert 25 °C to Fahrenheit.
a) °F = (9/5 × 25) + 32 = 45 + 32 = 77 °F.  b) °C = (5/9)(98 − 32) = (5/9)(66) ≈ 36.7 °C.  c) K = 27 + 273.15 = 300.15 K.  d) −273.15 °C (0 K).
**Answer: a) °F = (9/5 × 25) + 32 = 45 + 32 = 77 °F.**

**2.** Convert 98 °F to Celsius.
a) °F = (9/5 × 25) + 32 = 45 + 32 = 77 °F.  b) °C = (5/9)(98 − 32) = (5/9)(66) ≈ 36.7 °C.  c) K = 27 + 273.15 = 300.15 K.  d) −273.15 °C (0 K).
**Answer: b) °C = (5/9)(98 − 32) = (5/9)(66) ≈ 36.7 °C.**

**3.** Convert 27 °C to kelvin.
a) °F = (9/5 × 25) + 32 = 45 + 32 = 77 °F.  b) °C = (5/9)(98 − 32) = (5/9)(66) ≈ 36.7 °C.  c) K = 27 + 273.15 = 300.15 K.  d) −273.15 °C (0 K).
**Answer: c) K = 27 + 273.15 = 300.15 K.**

**4.** What is absolute zero in Celsius?
a) °F = (9/5 × 25) + 32 = 45 + 32 = 77 °F.  b) °C = (5/9)(98 − 32) = (5/9)(66) ≈ 36.7 °C.  c) K = 27 + 273.15 = 300.15 K.  d) −273.15 °C (0 K).
**Answer: d) −273.15 °C (0 K).**
