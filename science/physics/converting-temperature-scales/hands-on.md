# Hands-on — Converting Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Take today's temperature in Celsius from a phone or radio. Convert it to Fahrenheit and kelvin by hand, then check with any converter you trust.

## Apply it where you live
Convert a full day's weather — dawn, noon, night — into all three scales.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21870 — three scales as parallel thermometers with ice and steam points aligned] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using °F = (9/5)°C + 32; K = °C + 273.15: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
