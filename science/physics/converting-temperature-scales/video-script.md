# Video script — Converting Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

The three scales are tied by their fixed points.
Celsius to Fahrenheit: °F = (9/5 × °C) + 32.
Fahrenheit to Celsius: °C = (5/9) × (°F − 32).
Kelvin shares Celsius's step size and starts at absolute zero: K = °C + 273.15.
Work both ways until the relations are tools, not spells — and the same fixed-point method converts even a scale you invent yourself, like the review's pupil-designed °X.

Remember: **°F = (9/5)°C + 32 and °C = (5/9)(°F − 32).**
Remember: **K = °C + 273.15; Kelvin's floor is absolute zero.**
Remember: **Kelvin and Celsius have the same step size.**
Keep the formula: **°F = (9/5)°C + 32; K = °C + 273.15**
