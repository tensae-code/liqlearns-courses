# Grouping generators — series and parallel equivalents — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Three generators (E, r each) in series act as one of…
**Answer: a) EMF 3E and internal resistance 3r  b) EMF 3E and resistance r/3  c) EMF E and resistance 3r  d) EMF E and resistance r**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A flat 4.5 V battery contains…
**Answer: a) three 1.5 V cells in series  b) one 4.5 V cell  c) three cells in parallel  d) nine cells**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Identical generators in parallel give an equivalent of…
**Answer: a) the same EMF and internal resistance r/n  b) n times the EMF  c) the same EMF and resistance nr  d) zero internal resistance**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Parallel grouping is chosen when the load needs…
**Answer: a) the same voltage with more current or longer life  b) a higher voltage  c) a lower voltage  d) no current**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The equivalent generator's current into a load R is…
**Answer: a) I = E_eq/(R + r_eq)  b) I = E_eq·R  c) I = R/E_eq  d) I = E_eq/R²**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Unequal generators in parallel are avoided because…
**Answer: a) they drive a wasteful circulating current through each other  b) parallel needs AC  c) their EMFs add wrongly  d) the wires overheat by law**
