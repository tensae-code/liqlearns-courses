# Grouping generators — series and parallel equivalents — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In series, the equivalent EMF is the _____ of the EMFs.
**Answer: sum (ΣEᵢ)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In series the internal resistances also _____ .
**Answer: add (Σrᵢ)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In parallel (identical generators), r_eq = r/_____ .
**Answer: n**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Series grouping buys _____ ; parallel buys staying power.
**Answer: voltage**
