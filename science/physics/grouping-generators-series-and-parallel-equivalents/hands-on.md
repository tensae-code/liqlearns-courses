# Hands-on — Grouping generators — series and parallel equivalents

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

["(1) Measure one AA cell's voltage, then two, three and four in series with a voltmeter — record the stacking. (2) Power a small lamp from one cell and from two in parallel; compare brightness now and after ten minutes, and relate the difference to sagging (r) rather than EMF. (3) Open a flat 4.5 V battery (dead one, teacher-led) and count the 1.5 V cells in series inside."]
