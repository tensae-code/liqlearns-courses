# Car safety physics — seat belts, airbags and crumple design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a crash, the quantity that cannot be negotiated is…
**Answer: a) the impulse — the momentum change  b) the force  c) the time  d) the contact area**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Stretching the stopping time for the same impulse…
**Answer: a) reduces the average force  b) increases the force  c) changes the momentum  d) removes the impulse**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A seat belt protects by stretching time and…
**Answer: a) spreading force across the pelvis and ribcage  b) heating the body  c) adding momentum  d) stiffening the car**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An airbag works by…
**Answer: a) lengthening the head's stopping time and spreading its force  b) stopping the car  c) replacing the belt  d) inflating the tyres**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A crumple zone protects because folding structure…
**Answer: a) turns kinetic energy into deformation work over time and distance  b) makes the car lighter  c) bounces the car back  d) hides the engine**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The passenger cell, by contrast with the crumple zone, is built…
**Answer: a) rigid, to keep its shape around the occupants  b) to fold first  c) soft  d) detachable**
