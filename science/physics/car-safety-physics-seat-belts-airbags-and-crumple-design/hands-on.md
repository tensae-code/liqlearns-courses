# Hands-on — Car safety physics — seat belts, airbags and crumple design

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Drop an egg onto concrete and onto a foam pad from the same height; relate the outcomes to stopping time. (2) Drop a mass on a spring balance and on a rigid block from the same height and compare the peak readings. (3) Design a paper crumple nose for a toy car, run it into a wall three times, and improve the fold pattern between runs.
