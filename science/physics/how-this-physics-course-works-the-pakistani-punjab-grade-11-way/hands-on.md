# Hands-on — How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 11 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Take one chapter of Physics 11 and work it the PECTAA way
Take one chapter of Physics 11 and work it the PECTAA way: read only its Learning Objectives box and predict the chapter; work two worked examples with the book covered after the first line; then attempt one question from each of the five closing sets and mark yourself against the given answers.
