# How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 11 way — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — PECTAA's 2023-curriculum book states each chapter's promises up front, in a box, as things the student will be able to do; Inside the chapter, worked examples show the method on real numbers before the learner is asked to perform it, and For Your Information / Interesting Information side boxes carry the extensions and curiosities; The close is graded in five steps: MCQs, Short Answer Questions, Constructed Response Questions, Comprehensive Questions and Numerical Problems — from recognition to full written argument to calculation — and the answers are given, so the learner can check, not guess, whether the chapter has landed — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
