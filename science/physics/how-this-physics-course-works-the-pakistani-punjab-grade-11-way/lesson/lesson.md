# How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 11 way

## Do it first
Take one chapter of Physics 11 and work it the PECTAA way — begin with the hands-on opening in hands-on.md before reading on. This lesson is the story of that activity.

## Sub lesson 1 — A Learning Objectives box opens every chapter

PECTAA's 2023-curriculum book states each chapter's promises up front, in a box, as things the student will be able to do. Measurements, force and motion, rotation, energy, fluids, thermodynamics, waves, optics, electricity, electromagnetism, relativity and nuclear physics all begin the same way: the destination printed before the journey.

## Sub lesson 2 — Worked examples and information boxes carry the middle

Inside the chapter, worked examples show the method on real numbers before the learner is asked to perform it, and For Your Information / Interesting Information side boxes carry the extensions and curiosities. The chapter's teaching is therefore two-voiced: the line of the text, and the boxes that pause it to show or to widen.

## Sub lesson 3 — Five question sets close the chapter — with answers

The close is graded in five steps: MCQs, Short Answer Questions, Constructed Response Questions, Comprehensive Questions and Numerical Problems — from recognition to full written argument to calculation — and the answers are given, so the learner can check, not guess, whether the chapter has landed. The five sets are the PECTAA signature: harder kinds of answer, demanded in order.

## Say it to your companion
Tell your companion the lesson's one thing in your own words: every chapter of PECTAA's Physics 11 opens with a boxed Learning Objectives list written as student outcomes, runs worked examples and For Your Information side boxes through the chapter, and closes with five graded question sets — MCQs, Short Answer, Constructed Response, Comprehensive and Numerical Problems, with answers given. If your companion can work the Do It Yourself items without looking back, the lesson has landed.
