# How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 11 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) Name PECTAA's five closing question sets in order.
**Answer:** MCQs, Short Answer, Constructed Response, Comprehensive and Numerical Problems.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What is a Learning Objectives box for?
**Answer:** It states, before the chapter, what the student will be able to do after it.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How do the side boxes change the chapter's voice?
**Answer:** They pause the main line to show a worked case or widen the idea.

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Which curriculum is this book built on?
**Answer:** The Updated/Revised National Curriculum of Pakistan-2023, approved by PECTAA.
