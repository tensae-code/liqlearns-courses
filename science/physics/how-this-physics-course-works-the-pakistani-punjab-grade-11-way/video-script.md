# Video script — How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 11 way

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **every chapter of PECTAA's Physics 11 opens with a boxed Learning Objectives list written as student outcomes, runs worked examples and For Your Information side boxes through the chapter, and closes with five graded question sets — MCQs, Short Answer, Constructed Response, Comprehensive and Numerical Problems, with answers given**. PECTAA's 2023-curriculum book states each chapter's promises up front, in a box, as things the student will be able to do. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — a learning objectives box opens every chapter; worked examples and information boxes carry the middle; five question sets close the chapter — with answers — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.
