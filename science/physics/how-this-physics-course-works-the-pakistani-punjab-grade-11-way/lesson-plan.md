# Lesson plan — How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 11 way

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 11 way
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18), inside the measurement strand — Pakistan (Punjab)'s PECTAA pattern for the 2023 curriculum
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** every chapter of PECTAA's Physics 11 opens with a boxed Learning Objectives list written as student outcomes, runs worked examples and For Your Information side boxes through the chapter, and closes with five graded question sets — MCQs, Short Answer, Constructed Response, Comprehensive and Numerical Problems, with answers given
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Any earlier lesson named below is recommended first, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **A Learning Objectives box opens every chapter** — PECTAA's 2023-curriculum book states each chapter's promises up front, in a box, as things the student will be able to do. Measurements, force and motion, rotation, energy, fluids, thermodynamics, waves, optics, electricity, electromagnetism, relativity and nuclear physics all begin the same way: the destination printed before the journey.
2. **Worked examples and information boxes carry the middle** — Inside the chapter, worked examples show the method on real numbers before the learner is asked to perform it, and For Your Information / Interesting Information side boxes carry the extensions and curiosities. The chapter's teaching is therefore two-voiced: the line of the text, and the boxes that pause it to show or to widen.
3. **Five question sets close the chapter — with answers** — The close is graded in five steps: MCQs, Short Answer Questions, Constructed Response Questions, Comprehensive Questions and Numerical Problems — from recognition to full written argument to calculation — and the answers are given, so the learner can check, not guess, whether the chapter has landed. The five sets are the PECTAA signature: harder kinds of answer, demanded in order.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
