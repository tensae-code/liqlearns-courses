# Hands-on — Thin-film interference — colours in soap films

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Blow a soap film in a wire loop, hold it vertical against a dark background, and watch the bands descend and the top turn black; time the changes. (2) Put a drop of oil on wet dark paper and sketch its colour map; mark where you judge the film thinnest. (3) Shine one colour of LED light on the film: the rainbow collapses to bright and dark bands of that colour — record what that proves about the white-light colours.
