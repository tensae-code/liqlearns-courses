# Thin-film interference — colours in soap films — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The path difference is _____ the film's thickness.
**Answer: twice**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Reflection off a denser medium flips the phase by _____ a cycle.
**Answer: half**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The thinnest part of a draining film looks _____.
**Answer: black**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Each thickness reinforces its own _____.
**Answer: colour**
