# Thin-film interference — colours in soap films — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The two interfering reflections come from…
**Answer: a) the film's front and back surfaces  b) two lamps  c) the eye  d) the soap and the water**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The path difference between them is…
**Answer: a) twice the film's thickness  b) the thickness once  c) zero  d) the film's width**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Reflection off a denser medium adds…
**Answer: a) a half-cycle phase change  b) no change  c) a full cycle  d) a colour**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The black patch at a draining film's top means the film there is…
**Answer: a) so thin the reflections cancel  b) thickest  c) dry  d) opaque**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The colour bands on the film are a map of…
**Answer: a) the film's thickness  b) its temperature  c) its age  d) the light's brightness**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Under one-colour light the film shows…
**Answer: a) bright and dark bands of that colour  b) a rainbow  c) nothing  d) black only**
