# Lesson plan — Binding energy and mass defect

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** Binding energy and mass defect
**Place in the curriculum:** Grade 11 · Unit 7 — Nuclear Physics.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** mass defect and binding energy — A nucleus weighs less than its separate nucleons; the missing mass is the binding energy via E = mc² (worked in MeV with the 931.5 MeV-per-atomic-mass-unit conversion),…
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 11 Physics lessons before it in Unit 7 — Nuclear Physics — and on the learner's own world: the examples are the learner's home, street, school and body.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.
