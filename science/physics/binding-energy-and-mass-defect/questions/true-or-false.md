# Binding energy and mass defect — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. A nucleus weighs less than its separate nucleons; the missing mass is the binding energy via E = mc² (worked in MeV with the 931.5 MeV-per-atomic-mass-unit conversion), and binding energy per nucleon measures stability.**
**Answer: True**

**2. A nucleus weighs less than its separate nucleons; the missing mass is the binding energy via E = mc² (worked in MeV with the 1863 MeV-per-atomic-mass-unit conversion), and binding energy per nucleon measures stability.**
**Answer: False**
