# Lenses — bending light to form images — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A convex lens has f = 10 cm; the object is 30 cm away. Find v.
**Answer: 1/v = 1/10 − 1/30 = 2/30, so v = 15 cm.**

**2.** Which lens spreads parallel rays?
**Answer: A concave (diverging) lens.**

**3.** Half a lens is covered. The image — ?
**Answer: Complete but dimmer.**

**4.** A real image differs from a virtual one how?
**Answer: A real image can be formed on a screen; a virtual one cannot.**
