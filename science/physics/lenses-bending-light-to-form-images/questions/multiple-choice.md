# Lenses — bending light to form images — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A convex lens has f = 10 cm; the object is 30 cm away. Find v.
a) 1/v = 1/10 − 1/30 = 2/30, so v = 15 cm.  b) A concave (diverging) lens.  c) Complete but dimmer.  d) A real image can be formed on a screen; a virtual one cannot.
**Answer: a) 1/v = 1/10 − 1/30 = 2/30, so v = 15 cm.**

**Answer: b) A concave (diverging) lens.**
