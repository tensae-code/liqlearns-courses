# Hands-on — Lenses — bending light to form images

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Use any magnifying lens (or a phone lens and screen) to catch a window's image on paper: a real image, caught by a school lens.

## Apply it where you live
For a stated object position at a convex lens, draw the ray diagram and compute v and m.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21935 — both lens kinds with ray diagrams and image positions] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using 1/f = 1/v + 1/u; m = v/u: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
