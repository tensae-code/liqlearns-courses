# Video script — Lenses — bending light to form images

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Lenses form images by refraction.
A convex (converging) lens bends parallel rays to its focal point; a concave (diverging) lens spreads them as if from its focal point.
Ray diagrams locate the image, and the lens equation 1/f = 1/v + 1/u with magnification m = v/u sizes it — real images can land on a screen, virtual ones cannot.
The half-covered-lens puzzle settles what a lens really does: cover half of it and the image stays complete, only dimmer — every part of the lens serves the whole image.

Remember: **Convex lens converges; concave lens diverges.**
Remember: **Lens equation 1/f = 1/v + 1/u; magnification m = v/u.**
Remember: **Half-covering a lens dims the image but keeps it complete.**
Keep the formula: **1/f = 1/v + 1/u; m = v/u**
