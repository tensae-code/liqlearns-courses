## Lenses — bending light to form images — the teaching
Lenses form images by refraction. A convex (converging) lens bends parallel rays to its focal point; a concave (diverging) lens spreads them as if from its focal point. Ray diagrams locate the image, and the lens equation 1/f = 1/v + 1/u with magnification m = v/u sizes it — real images can land on a screen, virtual ones cannot. The half-covered-lens puzzle settles what a lens really does: cover half of it and the image stays complete, only dimmer — every part of the lens serves the whole image.

**Keep the formula:** 1/f = 1/v + 1/u; m = v/u

## The key ideas

- Convex lens converges; concave lens diverges.
- Lens equation 1/f = 1/v + 1/u; magnification m = v/u.
- Half-covering a lens dims the image but keeps it complete.
