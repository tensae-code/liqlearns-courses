# Hands-on — Newton's first law — inertia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Balance a coin on a card over a cup and flick the card away sideways. The coin drops into the cup — it stayed while the card left. That staying is inertia.

## Apply it where you live
Explain a seat belt using Newton's first law in two sentences.
