Newton's first law: a body stays at rest, or keeps moving in a straight line at constant velocity, unless an unbalanced force acts on it.
Bodies resist changes to their state — that resistance is inertia, and mass is its measure: the bigger the mass, the bigger the inertia.
You feel it in a car: brake suddenly and your body lurches forward, still trying to keep its old motion; pull away and you press back into the seat.

Remember: **A body keeps its state unless an unbalanced force acts — Newton's first law.**
Remember: **Inertia is resistance to change of state; mass measures it.**
Remember: **A braking car throws passengers forward because their bodies keep the old motion.**
