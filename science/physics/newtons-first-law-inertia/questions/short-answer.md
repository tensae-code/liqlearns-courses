# Newton's first law — inertia — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** What keeps a rolling ball rolling on a smooth floor?
**Answer: No unbalanced force stops it — it keeps its state (first law).**

**2.** Which has more inertia, a truck or a bicycle?
**Answer: The truck — more mass means more inertia.**

**3.** Why do you lurch forward when a car brakes?
**Answer: Your body tends to keep its forward motion — inertia.**

**4.** What is inertia?
**Answer: Matter's resistance to a change in its state of motion or rest.**
