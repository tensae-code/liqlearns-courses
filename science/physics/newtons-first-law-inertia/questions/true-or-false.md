# Newton's first law — inertia — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1.** A body keeps its state unless an unbalanced force acts — Newton's first law.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Inertia is resistance to change of state; mass measures it.
**Answer: True**

**3.** A moving body always needs a force pushing it to keep moving.
**Answer: False**
