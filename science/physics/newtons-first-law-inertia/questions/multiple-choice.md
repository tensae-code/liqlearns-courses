# Newton's first law — inertia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What keeps a rolling ball rolling on a smooth floor?
a) No unbalanced force stops it — it keeps its state (first law).  b) The truck — more mass means more inertia.  c) Your body tends to keep its forward motion — inertia.  d) Matter's resistance to a change in its state of motion or rest.
**Answer: a) No unbalanced force stops it — it keeps its state (first law).**

**2.** Which has more inertia, a truck or a bicycle?
a) No unbalanced force stops it — it keeps its state (first law).  b) The truck — more mass means more inertia.  c) Your body tends to keep its forward motion — inertia.  d) Matter's resistance to a change in its state of motion or rest.
**Answer: b) The truck — more mass means more inertia.**

**3.** Why do you lurch forward when a car brakes?
a) No unbalanced force stops it — it keeps its state (first law).  b) The truck — more mass means more inertia.  c) Your body tends to keep its forward motion — inertia.  d) Matter's resistance to a change in its state of motion or rest.
**Answer: c) Your body tends to keep its forward motion — inertia.**

**4.** What is inertia?
a) No unbalanced force stops it — it keeps its state (first law).  b) The truck — more mass means more inertia.  c) Your body tends to keep its forward motion — inertia.  d) Matter's resistance to a change in its state of motion or rest.
