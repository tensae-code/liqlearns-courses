# Apparent weight — overweight and weightlessness — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A bathroom scale under your feet directly measures…
**Answer: a) the normal force pushing back on you  b) gravity itself  c) your mass  d) your volume**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Your apparent weight is…
**Answer: a) the normal force N a surface exerts on you  b) always equal to mg  c) your mass in kilograms  d) zero in a lift**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A lift accelerates upward. The scale reading…
**Answer: a) rises — N = m(g + a), overweight  b) falls  c) stays exactly mg  d) reads zero**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A downward-moving lift brakes to a stop. During braking you feel…
**Answer: a) heavier — the acceleration is upward, so N > mg  b) lighter  c) weightless  d) unchanged**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In free fall (a = g) the scale reads…
**Answer: a) zero — weightlessness, though gravity still acts fully  b) mg  c) double  d) your mass**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Astronauts in orbit float because…
**Answer: a) they and their station are in continuous free fall together  b) there is no gravity in space  c) the station has no mass  d) they are beyond Earth's pull**
