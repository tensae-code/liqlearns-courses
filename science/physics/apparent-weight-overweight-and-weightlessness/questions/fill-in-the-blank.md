# Apparent weight — overweight and weightlessness — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A scale reads the _____ force pushing back on you.
**Answer: normal**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Accelerating upward, you are in the state the Chinese books call _____ (超重) — overweight.
**Answer: overweight**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In free fall the apparent weight is _____ newtons.
**Answer: zero**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The Chinese word 失重 names the state of _____.
**Answer: weightlessness**
