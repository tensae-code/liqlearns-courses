# Hands-on — Apparent weight — overweight and weightlessness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel apparent weight in a lift and on a spring
(1) Lift ride with a scale: take a bathroom scale into a lift (with permission) and stand on it through one full ride. Note the reading at rest, as the lift starts upward, mid-ride, as it slows to stop, and on the way down. Mark each phase overweight, normal or light, and match each to the lift's acceleration direction. (2) Spring version without a lift: hang a mass from a spring balance, lift it sharply, then let it drop a short distance while watching the pointer — the reading climbs as you accelerate it up and falls as it falls. (3) Free-fall test: drop a paper cup of water with holes in its side from a safe height onto soft ground — in flight, no water jets out: in free fall there is no apparent weight to press the water through the holes.
