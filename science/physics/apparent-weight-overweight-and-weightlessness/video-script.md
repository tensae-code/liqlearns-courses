# Video script — Apparent weight — overweight and weightlessness

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **apparent weight — overweight and weightlessness**. A scale reads how hard the floor pushes back, not gravity itself. Accelerate up and the push grows — overweight. Fall freely and the push vanishes — weightlessness, with gravity still pulling.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
