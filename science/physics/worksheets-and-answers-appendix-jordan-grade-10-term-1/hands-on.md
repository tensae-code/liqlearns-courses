# Hands-on — Worksheets and answers appendix — Jordan Grade 10 Term 1

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Draft a six-question worksheet for one Term 1 unit in the appendix's pattern — two recall, two application, two problem questions. (2) Write its answer key in the key's style: result, working, concluding statement. (3) Swap with a partner and mark against each other's keys; note where the standard of a complete answer differed.
