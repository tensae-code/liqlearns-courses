# Adding vectors with drawings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about graphical addition is correct?**
a) Vectors are added head-to-tail (triangle rule), by the parallelogram rule for two vectors at a point, and by the…  b) Every mass attracts every other with F = Gm₁m₂/r²; a worked example weighs a 60 kg person by the formula (≈584 N,…  c) For Newton's third law the guide works a group exercise explaining the pairs in: a man walking (foot pushes floor back,…
**Answer: a) Vectors are added head-to-tail (triangle rule), by the parallelogram rule for two vectors at a point, and by…**

**2. Vectors are added head-to-tail (triangle rule), by the parallelogram rule for two vectors at a point, and by the polygon method for several; a scale diagram converts measured lengths and angles back into a ______ — the brainstorming questions drill why a scale is needed and how a vector differs from its negative.**
a) resultant  b) temperature  c) manometer
**Answer: a) resultant**

**3.** In the A-to-B walking demonstration, two pupils walk from A to B by different paths. What is the same for both walks?
a) The distance walked  b) The displacement — the straight arrow from A to B  c) The time taken  d) The speed
**Answer: b) The displacement — the straight arrow from A to B**

**4.** Three non-zero vectors are drawn head-to-tail and the last head lands on the first tail. What is their resultant?
a) The longest vector  b) Zero — the three vectors add to zero  c) It cannot be found by drawing  d) Three times the first vector
**Answer: b) Zero — the three vectors add to zero**
