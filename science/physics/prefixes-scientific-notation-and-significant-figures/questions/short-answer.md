# Prefixes, scientific notation and significant figures — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Write 3,000 m using a prefix.
**Answer: 3 km (kilo = 10³).**

**2.** Write 45,000,000 in scientific notation.
**Answer: 4.5 × 10⁷.**

**3.** What does pico mean as a power of ten?
**Answer: 10⁻¹².**

**4.** In 7.50 cm, how many significant figures are there?
**Answer: Three — 7, 5 and 0 all carry information.**
