# Prefixes, scientific notation and significant figures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Write 3,000 m using a prefix.
a) 3 km (kilo = 10³).  b) 4.5 × 10⁷.  c) 10⁻¹².  d) Three — 7, 5 and 0 all carry information.
**Answer: a) 3 km (kilo = 10³).**

**2.** Write 45,000,000 in scientific notation.
a) 3 km (kilo = 10³).  b) 4.5 × 10⁷.  c) 10⁻¹².  d) Three — 7, 5 and 0 all carry information.
**Answer: b) 4.5 × 10⁷.**

**3.** What does pico mean as a power of ten?
a) 3 km (kilo = 10³).  b) 4.5 × 10⁷.  c) 10⁻¹².  d) Three — 7, 5 and 0 all carry information.
**Answer: c) 10⁻¹².**

**4.** In 7.50 cm, how many significant figures are there?
a) 3 km (kilo = 10³).  b) 4.5 × 10⁷.  c) 10⁻¹².  d) Three — 7, 5 and 0 all carry information.
**Answer: d) Three — 7, 5 and 0 all carry information.**
