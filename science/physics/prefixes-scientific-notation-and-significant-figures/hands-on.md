# Hands-on — Prefixes, scientific notation and significant figures

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Write your height in metres, then in centimetres, then try scientific notation for the distance to the Moon if a teacher gives it. Notice which form is easiest to read each time.

## Apply it where you live
Convert five measures from adverts or labels into prefixed or scientific-notation form.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21807 — prefix ladder from pico to tera with a familiar object on each rung] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
