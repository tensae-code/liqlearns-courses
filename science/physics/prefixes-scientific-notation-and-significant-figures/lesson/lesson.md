# Prefixes, scientific notation and significant figures

## Prefixes, scientific notation and significant figures — the teaching
SI prefixes shrink or stretch a unit by powers of ten: tera (10¹²), giga (10⁹), mega (10⁶), kilo (10³), then below one — centi (10⁻²), milli (10⁻³), micro (10⁻⁶), nano (10⁻⁹) and pico (10⁻¹²). Scientific notation writes any number as a number between 1 and 10 times a power of ten, so very large and very small measurements stay short. Significant figures are the digits of a measurement that really carry information, including the one estimated digit from reading a scale.

## The key ideas

- Kilo means 10³ and milli means 10⁻³.
- Scientific notation writes a number as a value between 1 and 10 times a power of ten.
- Significant figures are the digits that carry real information, including the estimated digit.

## Say it to your companion
Explain prefixes, scientific notation and significant figures to your companion in your own words. Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
