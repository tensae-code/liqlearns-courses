SI prefixes shrink or stretch a unit by powers of ten: tera (10¹²), giga (10⁹), mega (10⁶), kilo (10³), then below one — centi (10⁻²), milli (10⁻³), micro (10⁻⁶), nano (10⁻⁹) and pico (10⁻¹²).
Scientific notation writes any number as a number between 1 and 10 times a power of ten, so very large and very small measurements stay short.
Significant figures are the digits of a measurement that really carry information, including the one estimated digit from reading a scale.

Remember: **Kilo means 10³ and milli means 10⁻³.**
