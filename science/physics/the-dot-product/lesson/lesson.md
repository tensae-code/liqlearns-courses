## The scalar (dot) product

Multiplying two vectors can give a scalar: the dot product combines magnitudes with the cosine of the angle between them, is commutative, gives a vector's squared magnitude when dotted with itself, and is zero for perpendicular vectors — with the angle conditions for maximum, zero and negative results drilled in exercises.
