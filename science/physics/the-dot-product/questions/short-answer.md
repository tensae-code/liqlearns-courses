# The dot product — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. Give one key fact this lesson teaches about the scalar (dot) product.**
**Answer: Multiplying two vectors can give a scalar: the dot product combines magnitudes with the cosine of the angle between them, is commutative, gives a vector's squared magnitude when dotted with itself, and is zero for perpendicular vectors — with the angle conditions for maximum, zero and negative results drilled in exercises.**
