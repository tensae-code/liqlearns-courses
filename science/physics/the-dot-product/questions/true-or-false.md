# The dot product — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. Multiplying two vectors can give a scalar: the dot product combines magnitudes with the cosine of the angle between them, is commutative, gives a vector's squared magnitude when dotted with itself, and is zero for perpendicular vectors — with the angle conditions for maximum, zero and negative results drilled in exercises.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Multiplying two vectors can give a scalar: the dot product combines magnitudes with the cosine of the angle between them, is commutative, gives a vector's squared magnitude when dotted with itself, and is zero for perpendicular vectors — with the angle conditions for minimum, zero and negative results drilled in exercises.**
**Answer: False**
