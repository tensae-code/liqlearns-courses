# Hands-on — Unit 5 review — heat

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three ways heat travels
Hold a metal spoon and a wooden spoon in the same hot water. Feel which handle warms first, then watch steam rise and feel warmth from the side of the cup. Name conduction, convection and radiation in what you felt.

## See it and say it
Study this lesson's picture — the scene the lesson describes — and name every part of it out loud. Then close the picture and draw it from memory; check your drawing against the lesson.

## Teach it back
Explain this lesson to someone at home using one thing in the room as your example. Their questions show you which part to re-read.
