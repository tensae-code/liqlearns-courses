# Non-inertial frames and apparent forces — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A frame where Newton's laws hold as written is called _____ .
**Answer: inertial**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Apparent forces are also called _____ forces.
**Answer: fictitious**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The outward apparent force in a rotating frame is the _____ force.
**Answer: centrifugal**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The apparent force that deflects winds on the rotating Earth is the _____ force.
**Answer: Coriolis**
