# Video script — Non-inertial frames and apparent forces

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the pushes nobody makes**. Brake hard in a bus and you lurch forward — nobody pushed you. Turn a car and you slide to the outer door — no hand shoved you. Those pushes appear when your viewpoint itself accelerates: your frame is **non-inertial**. From the road, the story is just inertia — your body keeps its speed while the bus or car changes its own. Inside the vehicle, keeping Newton's laws working costs extra, imaginary forces: a phantom push against the frame's acceleration, an outward **centrifugal** push in anything rotating, and the **Coriolis** bend that steers Earth's winds. The forces are bookkeeping — but inside the frame, the bookkeeping is law.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
