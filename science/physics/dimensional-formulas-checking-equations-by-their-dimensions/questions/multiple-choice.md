# Dimensional formulas — checking equations by their dimensions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The dimensional formula of force is…
a) M L T⁻¹  b) M L T⁻²  c) M L² T⁻²  d) L T⁻²
**Answer: b) M L T⁻²**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The dimensional formula of energy (and work) is…
a) M L T⁻²  b) M L⁻¹ T⁻²  c) M L² T⁻²  d) M L² T⁻³
**Answer: c) M L² T⁻²**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which equation is dimensionally WRONG?
a) v = u + at  b) s = ut + ½at²  c) v² = u² + 2as  d) v = u + a/t
**Answer: d) v = u + a/t**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In the check of s = ut + ½at², the factor ½…
a) Has dimensions L⁰ T¹  b) Is a pure number with no dimensions, so it does not affect the check  c) Makes the term a different kind  d) Must be removed before checking
**Answer: b) Is a pure number with no dimensions, so it does not affect the check**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Two quantities with the same dimensional formula M L² T⁻² are…
a) Force and energy  b) Work and torque — dimensions alone cannot tell them apart  c) Pressure and energy  d) Power and work
**Answer: b) Work and torque — dimensions alone cannot tell them apart**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) An equation that PASSES the dimensional check is…
a) Certainly correct  b) Certainly wrong  c) Possibly correct — the check is necessary, not sufficient  d) Correct only if it has no fractions
**Answer: c) Possibly correct — the check is necessary, not sufficient**
