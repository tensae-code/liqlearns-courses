# Dimensional formulas — checking equations by their dimensions — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Speed has dimensional formula L _____ (a negative power of time).
**Answer: T⁻¹**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) An equation whose terms all carry the same dimensional formula is called _____.
**Answer: homogeneous**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The period of a pendulum must vary as the square root of length divided by _____.
**Answer: g**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Pure numbers such as ½ and 2π are _____ — they carry no dimensions.
**Answer: dimensionless**
