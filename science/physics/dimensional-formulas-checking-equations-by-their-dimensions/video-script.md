Today: **dimensional formulas — checking equations by their dimensions**. Every quantity is built from a few base kinds — mass, length, time. Force is mass times length over time squared. And here is the power of it: a true equation must balance in these kinds on both sides, term by term. So before you trust any formula, check its dimensions. If they fail, the formula is wrong — no numbers, no experiment, no argument. If they pass, the formula has earned the right to be tested further.
