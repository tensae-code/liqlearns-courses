# Hands-on — Dimensional formulas — checking equations by their dimensions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Check three formulas by hand
On paper, work the dimensional check for v = u + at, for E = ½mv², and for the wrong guess P = F × v² (pressure from force and speed). Say aloud the rule: each side, and every added term, must carry the same dimensional formula — that sameness is called homogeneity.

## Rebuild a law from dimensions
Take the period of a simple pendulum. List what it could depend on — length of string, mass of bob, g — and try each combination against the dimension T. Find that mass can never appear (nothing cancels its M), and that only √(L/g) gives T. Say aloud the rule: dimensions decide which quantities a law may contain at all.
