# Lesson plan — How this Physics course works — the Yemeni Grade 10 way

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** How this Physics course works — the Yemeni Grade 10 way
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md) — a new lesson built for the Yemen placement named in lesson/sources.md
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** every Yemeni physics unit opens with its objectives printed first and its unit and key section titles printed in both Arabic and English, lessons carry short hands-on activities inside them, every unit closes with a unit evaluation, and the Grade 10 student book is paired with a separate activities-and-experiments notebook whose experiments use materials from the local environment
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Earlier lessons make a good preparation and are recommended, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Objectives first, titles in two languages** — Every Yemeni unit opens with its printed unit objectives (أهداف الوحدة) — a short list of what the learner will be able to do — before the teaching starts. The unit title and the key section titles are also printed in English, so the learner meets the English name of each idea beside its Arabic name from the start. Grade 10 runs in eight units: physics and measurement; motion in a straight line; properties of solids and fluids; work, power and energy; electrostatics; electric current; thermal measurements; and the effect of heat on bodies. Read the objectives first and the whole unit has a destination before it has a first step.
2. **Activities inside the lessons, an evaluation at the end** — The teaching itself is not read-only: short hands-on activities (نشاط) are embedded inside the lessons, so a new idea is tried with the hands soon after it is met. And every unit closes the same way — with a unit evaluation (تقويم الوحدة), a set of questions that checks the unit's objectives one by one. The shape is a promise: objectives at the front, activities in the middle, evaluation at the back — a unit is finished when its evaluation can be answered, not when its last page is reached.
3. **The companion notebook — theory proved in the lab** — Grade 10 physics in Yemen is a package of two books. Beside the student book sits a separate activities-and-experiments notebook (الأنشطة والتجارب العملية) with eleven named experiments, one per topic of the student book — Hooke's law, Archimedes' principle, Ohm's law, specific heat, thermal conductivity, linear expansion and the gas laws among them. Each experiment is laid out the same way: aim, theory, apparatus, steps, conclusion — and the apparatus deliberately uses materials from the local environment and the school. The design is the point: theory is proved in the lab notebook, not only stated in the textbook.

## If the learner is stuck
- Cannot start → return to Do it first and do the opening activity with real objects or numbers before reading any explanation; the explanation is the story of that activity.
- Mixes the sub lessons up → re-read the key ideas in order; each key idea is one sub lesson compressed to a line.
