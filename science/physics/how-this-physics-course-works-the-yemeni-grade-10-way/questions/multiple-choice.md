# How this Physics course works — the Yemeni Grade 10 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Every Yemeni unit opens with…
a) Its answers  b) Its printed objectives (أهداف الوحدة)  c) A test  d) A glossary
**Answer: b) Its printed objectives (أهداف الوحدة)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Unit and key section titles are printed…
a) In Arabic only  b) In English only  c) In Arabic and English  d) In three languages
**Answer: c) In Arabic and English**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The short activities embedded inside lessons are called…
a) تقويم الوحدة  b) نشاط  c) أهداف الوحدة  d) Experiments
**Answer: b) نشاط**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Every unit closes with…
a) A unit evaluation (تقويم الوحدة)  b) A new unit  c) The objectives again  d) A glossary
**Answer: a) A unit evaluation (تقويم الوحدة)**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The Grade 10 companion notebook contains…
a) Eleven named experiments, one per topic of the student book  b) Only exam papers  c) Only answers  d) Teacher notes
**Answer: a) Eleven named experiments, one per topic of the student book**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Each experiment in the notebook is laid out as…
a) Questions only  b) Aim → theory → apparatus → steps → conclusion  c) Theory only  d) Steps with no conclusion
**Answer: b) Aim → theory → apparatus → steps → conclusion**
