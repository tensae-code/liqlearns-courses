# How this Physics course works — the Yemeni Grade 10 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name three of the eight Grade 10 units.
**Answer: Any three of: physics and measurement; motion in a straight line; properties of solids and fluids; work, power and energy; electrostatics; electric current; thermal measurements; the effect of heat on bodies.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the evaluation at the end of a unit matter in this design?
**Answer: It checks the unit's objectives one by one — the objectives promised at the front are tested at the back, so finishing a unit means being able to do what it promised.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Give one example of a companion-notebook experiment and the topic it proves.
**Answer: Any one, e.g. verifying Hooke's law for elasticity, Ohm's law for electric current, or determining the specific heat of iron for thermal measurements.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) What is meant by saying theory is proved in the lab notebook, not the textbook?
**Answer: The student book states the laws; the companion notebook makes the learner measure them — each experiment ends in a conclusion drawn from the learner's own results.**
