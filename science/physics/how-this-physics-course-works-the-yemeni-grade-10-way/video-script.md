# Video script — How this Physics course works — the Yemeni Grade 10 way

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the Yemeni Grade 10 way**. Objectives first — every unit opens by telling you what you will be able to do, with its titles in Arabic and English together. Activities inside the lessons, an evaluation closing every unit. And a second book beside the first: eleven experiments, each in five parts — aim, theory, apparatus, steps, conclusion — built from materials around you. Theory is not finished until your hands have proved it.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
