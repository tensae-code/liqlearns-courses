# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Yemeni Grade 10 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Prove the design with one experiment plan
Take a page and rule five boxes: aim, theory, apparatus, steps, conclusion. Plan the notebook's specific-heat experiment — determining the specific heat of iron — filling each box: the aim in one sentence, the theory (heat lost = heat gained), apparatus you could actually find locally (iron piece, balance, thermometer, water, a heat source, an insulated cup), the steps in order, and the conclusion you would draw from your numbers. The plan is the Yemeni way in miniature: nothing is only read — it is set up, done and concluded.
