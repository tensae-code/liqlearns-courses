Today: **Brownian motion and the kinetic theory of matter**.

Drop ink in still water and it wanders on its own. Why? Because matter is made of particles, and the particles never stop moving. Pollen grains in water, dust in sunlight — they jiggle forever, kicked from every side by invisible molecules. That jiggle is Brownian motion, and it is our proof of the kinetic theory: particles flying in gases, sliding in liquids, vibrating in solids. Heat them and they move faster — that is temperature. Their random wandering mixes ink and smells — that is diffusion. Their blows on a container's walls — that is pressure. One moving picture explains it all.
