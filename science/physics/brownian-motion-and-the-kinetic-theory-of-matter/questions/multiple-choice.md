# Brownian motion and the kinetic theory of matter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Brownian motion is…
a) The ceaseless random jiggling of tiny visible particles in a fluid  b) The flow of a river  c) The settling of dust to the bottom  d) The growth of pollen grains
**Answer: The ceaseless random jiggling of tiny visible particles in a fluid**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The specks in Brownian motion jiggle because…
a) They are alive  b) Uneven blows from the fluid's invisible moving particles kick them  c) The container vibrates  d) Heat rises through the fluid
**Answer: Uneven blows from the fluid's invisible moving particles kick them**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In the kinetic theory, the particles of a gas…
a) Sit still in rows  b) Fly freely, colliding with each other and the walls  c) Vibrate about fixed places only  d) Move only when stirred
**Answer: Fly freely, colliding with each other and the walls**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In a solid, the particles of the kinetic theory…
a) Fly freely  b) Slide past each other  c) Vibrate about fixed places  d) Do not exist
**Answer: Vibrate about fixed places**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Temperature, in the particle picture, measures…
a) How many particles there are  b) The particles' average kinetic energy  c) The size of the particles  d) The weight of the fluid
**Answer: The particles' average kinetic energy**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Gas pressure on a container's walls comes from…
a) Particles sticking to the walls  b) Particles colliding with the walls  c) Particles falling to the bottom  d) The container shrinking
**Answer: Particles colliding with the walls**
