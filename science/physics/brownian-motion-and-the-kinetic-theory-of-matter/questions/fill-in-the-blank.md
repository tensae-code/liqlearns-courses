# Brownian motion and the kinetic theory of matter — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The restless motion of tiny visible particles suspended in a fluid is called _____ motion.
**Answer: Brownian**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The theory that matter is made of particles in constant random motion is the _____ theory of matter.
**Answer: kinetic**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Ink spreading through still water without stirring is called _____.
**Answer: diffusion**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Heating a fluid makes its particles move _____, so Brownian motion grows livelier.
**Answer: faster**
