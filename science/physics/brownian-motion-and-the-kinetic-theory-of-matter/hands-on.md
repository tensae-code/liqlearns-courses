# Hands-on — Brownian motion and the kinetic theory of matter

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Zigzag watch
Watch ink threads or dust motes for one full minute and sketch one speck's path as a zigzag of short straight pieces. Say aloud: every turn is a kick I cannot see.

## Warm versus cold
Put a drop of ink into two glasses, one cold and one warm, at the same moment. Time how long each takes to spread. Say aloud: the warm water's particles move faster, so the mixing is quicker.
