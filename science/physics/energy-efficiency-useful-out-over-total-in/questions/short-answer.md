# Energy efficiency — useful out over total in — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Write the efficiency formula in words.
**Answer:** Efficiency = useful energy output divided by total energy input.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Where does a filament bulb's wasted energy go?
**Answer:** Almost all of it leaves as heat.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does saving energy at the point of use beat generating more?
**Answer:** Chain losses multiply, so a unit saved at the end saves several units at the start.

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Can a real conversion reach 100% efficiency? Why does Punjab's ratio say no?
**Answer:** No — some input always degrades to waste, mostly heat, so useful out is always less than total in.
