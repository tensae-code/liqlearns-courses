# Energy efficiency — useful out over total in — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Efficiency is defined as…
a) Total input ÷ useful output  b) Useful output ÷ total input  c) Useful output × total input  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Useful output ÷ total input**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) An efficiency of 0.35 as a percentage is…
a) 3.5%  b) 35%  c) 350%  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) 35%**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In most machines the wasted energy leaves mainly as…
a) Light  b) Heat  c) Stored energy  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Heat**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A 100 J input giving 5 J of useful output wastes…
a) 5 J  b) 95 J  c) 105 J  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) 95 J**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Efficiencies along a conversion chain…
a) Add together  b) Multiply together  c) Cancel out  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Multiply together**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A chain of 0.35 × 0.90 × 0.05 delivers about…
a) About 40%  b) Under 2% as useful output  c) Exactly 100%  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Under 2% as useful output**
