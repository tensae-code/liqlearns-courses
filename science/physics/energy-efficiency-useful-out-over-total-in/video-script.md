# Video script — Energy efficiency — useful out over total in

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **no conversion is perfect**. Efficiency compares what a conversion was for with what it cost: η = useful output / total input. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — the ratio every conversion is judged by; where the rest goes — wasted energy; chains multiply their losses — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.
