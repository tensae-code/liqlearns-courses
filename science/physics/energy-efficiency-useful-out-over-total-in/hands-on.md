# Hands-on — Energy efficiency — useful out over total in

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Audit one device at home with its label
Audit one device at home with its label: a kettle's power and the time it takes to boil a measured litre, against the physics of heating that water (4,200 J per kg per °C). Compute the efficiency of your own kettle and account for the missing energy. Repeat for any device whose input and useful output you can both measure.
