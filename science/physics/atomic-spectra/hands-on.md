# Hands-on — Atomic spectra

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make a pocket spectroscope
Cut a narrow slit in card, tape it over one end of a cardboard tube, and fix a piece of old CD (its grooved surface acts as a grating) at an angle at the other end. Look through it at a white bulb and then at any coloured lamp or flame test your teacher provides. The bulb smears into a rainbow; the gas lamp breaks into separate bright lines. Say aloud: same light-spreading tool, different atoms — the lines belong to the element.
