# Atomic spectra — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A hot thin gas gives an _____ spectrum of bright lines.
**Answer: emission**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Electrons occupy fixed energy _____ and never sit between them.
**Answer: levels**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A falling electron releases the energy difference as one _____.
**Answer: photon**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Each element's line pattern is its unique _____.
**Answer: fingerprint**
