# Atomic spectra — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A hot, thin gas produces…
a) A continuous spectrum  b) An emission line spectrum  c) No spectrum  d) Only infrared
**Answer: b) An emission line spectrum**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) An absorption spectrum shows…
a) Bright lines on darkness  b) Dark lines at the element's own wavelengths, cut from a continuous spectrum  c) A rainbow with extra colours  d) Nothing
**Answer: b) Dark lines at the element's own wavelengths, cut from a continuous spectrum**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A photon is emitted when an electron…
a) Rises to a higher level  b) Falls to a lower level  c) Leaves the atom  d) Circles faster
**Answer: b) Falls to a lower level**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The emitted photon's energy equals…
a) The upper level alone  b) h times the level number  c) The difference between the two levels, E₂ − E₁  d) Zero
**Answer: c) The difference between the two levels, E₂ − E₁**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Line spectra can identify an element because…
a) All elements share one pattern  b) Each element's level spacings — and so its lines — are unique  c) Lines are always red  d) Gases glow the same colour
**Answer: b) Each element's level spacings — and so its lines — are unique**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Helium was first discovered…
a) In a mine  b) In the Sun's spectrum  c) In seawater  d) In a meteorite
**Answer: b) In the Sun's spectrum**
