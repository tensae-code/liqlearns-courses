Today: **atomic spectra — light with a signature**. White light through a prism gives a rainbow with no gaps. But the light from a hot gas of one element gives only bright lines of exact colours, each element with its own pattern — its fingerprint. Bohr's model explains why: electrons live only in fixed energy levels, and a photon is born when an electron falls between levels, its energy exactly the difference. Read the lines, and you can name the element — even in a star.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
