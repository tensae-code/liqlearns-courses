# Hands-on — Scalars and vectors

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Call out quantities as a partner names them — scalar or vector, fast, no thinking time. Then slow down on the one you missed and say why.

## Apply it where you live
Sort twelve quantities from Grade 9 physics into scalars and vectors.
