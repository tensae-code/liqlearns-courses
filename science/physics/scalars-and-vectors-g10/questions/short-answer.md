# Scalars and vectors — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Is speed a scalar or a vector?
**Answer: A scalar — magnitude only.**

**2.** What two things must a vector give?
**Answer: Magnitude and direction.**

**3.** Classify force.
**Answer: A vector.**

**4.** Why is '5 m' an incomplete displacement?
**Answer: No direction is given.**
