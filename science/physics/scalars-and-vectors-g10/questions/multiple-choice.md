# Scalars and vectors — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Is speed a scalar or a vector?
a) A scalar — magnitude only.  b) Magnitude and direction.  c) A vector.  d) No direction is given.
**Answer: a) A scalar — magnitude only.**

**2.** What two things must a vector give?
a) A scalar — magnitude only.  b) Magnitude and direction.  c) A vector.  d) No direction is given.
**Answer: b) Magnitude and direction.**

**3.** Classify force.
a) A scalar — magnitude only.  b) Magnitude and direction.  c) A vector.  d) No direction is given.
**Answer: c) A vector.**

**4.** Why is '5 m' an incomplete displacement?
a) A scalar — magnitude only.  b) Magnitude and direction.  c) A vector.  d) No direction is given.
**Answer: d) No direction is given.**
