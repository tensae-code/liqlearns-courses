# Scalars and vectors

## Do it first
Call out quantities as a partner names them — scalar or vector, fast, no thinking time. Then slow down on the one you missed and say why.

## Scalars and vectors — the teaching
Physical quantities divide by one question: is direction part of the answer? Scalars have magnitude only — mass, time, speed: '5 kg' is complete. Vectors have magnitude and direction — displacement, velocity, force: '5 m' is incomplete until you add 'east'. Grade 9 met this split inside distance and displacement; Grade 10 builds the whole course on it.

## The key ideas

- Scalars have magnitude only: mass, time, speed.
- Vectors have magnitude and direction: displacement, velocity, force.
- A vector answer without direction is incomplete.

## Say it to your companion

Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
