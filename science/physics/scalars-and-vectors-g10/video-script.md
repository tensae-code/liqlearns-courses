# Video script — Scalars and vectors

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Physical quantities divide by one question: is direction part of the answer?
Scalars have magnitude only — mass, time, speed: '5 kg' is complete.
Vectors have magnitude and direction — displacement, velocity, force: '5 m' is incomplete until you add 'east'.
Grade 9 met this split inside distance and displacement; Grade 10 builds the whole course on it.

Remember: **Scalars have magnitude only: mass, time, speed.**
Remember: **Vectors have magnitude and direction: displacement, velocity, force.**
Remember: **A vector answer without direction is incomplete.**
