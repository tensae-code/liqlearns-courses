# The teacher-book pattern — chapter analysis, teaching suggestions and worked answers (China PEP) — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the guide pattern's four layers in order.
**Answer: Chapter overview (本章教材概述); section-by-section textbook analysis and teaching suggestions (教材分析与教学建议); reference answers and hints for the student book's Review and Improve sets; reference materials (参考资料).**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What do the reference answers answer, and why does that matter for marking?
**Answer: The student book's own “Review and Improve” problem sets — the teacher marks from the book's intended solutions and hints rather than improvising, keeping marking faithful to the course's design.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) State the truncation facts exactly as this lesson carries them.
**Answer: The guide's contents run to printed p. ~131 (Ch.8 guidance pp. 102–131), but the secured scan stops at printed p. 60 (PDF p. 66): Ch.5 and Ch.6 guidance are held complete; Ch.7–8 guidance (printed pp. 61–131) is missing — a library debt.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why is this lesson's country footing “evidence only” rather than counted?
**Answer: Its source is a teacher's guide, not a student textbook: teacher guides are cited as the teaching-way layer, named in countries as evidence, and never raise the acceptance count.**
