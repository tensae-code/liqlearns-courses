# Lesson plan — The teacher-book pattern — chapter analysis, teaching suggestions and worked answers (China PEP)

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** The teacher-book pattern — chapter analysis, teaching suggestions and worked answers (China PEP)
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18), among the how-this-course-works lessons — the teacher-side companion pattern of the PEP senior books, on teacher-guide evidence only
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** for every PEP senior student volume, China publishes a **teacher's teaching book (教师教学用书)** built to one pattern: a chapter overview that analyses the chapter's place and pitfalls, section-by-section teaching suggestions that route the lesson, reference answers to the student book's own “Review and Improve” sets, and reference materials that extend the teacher's bench — this lesson teaches that pattern, from the Compulsory Book 2 guide, on the evidence of its held (truncated) part
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Any earlier lesson named below is recommended first, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The chapter overview — the map before the march** — Each chapter of the guide opens with a **chapter overview (本章教材概述)**: what the chapter is for, where it sits in the course's long argument, which ideas are its load-bearing walls and which are scenery, and where students are known to stumble. Read before teaching, it converts the student book from a sequence of pages into a campaign with objectives. In the held part of the Compulsory Book 2 guide, the overviews for the projectile-motion chapter (Ch.5, guide pp. 1–27) and the circular-motion chapter (Ch.6, pp. 28–60) show the form at work: goals stated, misconceptions named, time budgeted across sections.
2. **Section by section — analysis with teaching suggestions** — The guide's core is its per-section **textbook analysis and teaching suggestions (教材分析与教学建议)**: for every section of the student book, what the section's experiment is really for, which demonstration to lead with, which question to open with, where the mathematics should be earned and where merely used, and how to handle the section's side columns. The Ch.6 treatment of centripetal force (guide printed p. 35) is the held part's clear specimen: the guide routes the teacher into the law through the conical-pendulum demonstration — the same experiment its reference materials then extend — so suggestion and apparatus speak with one voice.
3. **Worked answers and reference materials — the teacher's back room** — Two closing layers complete the pattern. **Reference answers and hints** work the student book's “Review and Improve” problem sets, so the teacher marks from the book's own intentions, not from improvisation. And **reference materials (参考资料)** stock the bench with extensions beyond the student text: for Ch.6, build-it-yourself verification experiments — an electric conical pendulum for verifying the centripetal-force formula, and arch-bridge and concave-bridge pressure experiments with a spring balance fitted with a “memory pointer”. The pattern's honesty note rides with this lesson: the secured guide scan is **truncated at printed p. 60 of about 131** (Ch.5–6 guidance only) — the pattern is taught from what is held, and the missing Ch.7–8 guidance is named, never invented.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
