## Read a guide the way its pattern intends
Take any teacher's guide available to you (or the held PEP Compulsory Book 2 guide's contents, printed pp. 5–6 of the scan). (1) Contents audit: list the guide's own chapter spans from its contents page — for the PEP guide: Ch.5 guidance pp. 1–27, Ch.6 pp. 28–60, Ch.7 pp. 61–101, Ch.8 pp. 102–131 — and mark which spans the secured file actually holds (to p. 60) and which are missing. (2) Pattern hunt: inside one held chapter, find and label the four layers — overview, per-section analysis-and-suggestions, reference answers, reference materials. (3) Route test: from the §2 centripetal-force suggestions (guide p. 35), write the lesson route the guide intends in three lines, and name the reference-material experiment that extends it. Your notes are the pattern, proved on a real book — evidence, not summary.
