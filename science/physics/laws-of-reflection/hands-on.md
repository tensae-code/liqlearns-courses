# Hands-on — Laws of reflection — mirror bounce and rough scatter

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Bounce torchlight or sunlight off a mirror onto a wall, then off crumpled foil. Same laws, two scatterings — name each one.

## Apply it where you live
Draw a two-mirror periscope path, marking equal angles at each bounce.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21930 — ray bouncing off a mirror at equal angles; the same ray scattering off rough paper] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
