# Video script — Laws of reflection — mirror bounce and rough scatter

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Reflection obeys two laws: the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection (both measured from the normal), and the incident ray, reflected ray and normal all lie in one plane.
What happens next depends on the surface.
A smooth surface reflects rays in an orderly way — specular reflection, mirror-like, images possible.
A rough surface reflects each ray lawfully but in scattered directions — diffuse reflection, which is why paper shows no image yet can be read from many angles.

Remember: **Angle of incidence = angle of reflection, measured from the normal.**
Remember: **Incident ray, reflected ray and normal lie in one plane.**
Remember: **Smooth surface: specular reflection. Rough surface: diffuse reflection.**
