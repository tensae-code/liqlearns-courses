# Laws of reflection — mirror bounce and rough scatter

## Do it first
Bounce torchlight or sunlight off a mirror onto a wall, then off crumpled foil. Same laws, two scatterings — name each one.

## Laws of reflection — mirror bounce and rough scatter — the teaching
Reflection obeys two laws: the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection (both measured from the normal), and the incident ray, reflected ray and normal all lie in one plane. What happens next depends on the surface. A smooth surface reflects rays in an orderly way — specular reflection, mirror-like, images possible. A rough surface reflects each ray lawfully but in scattered directions — diffuse reflection, which is why paper shows no image yet can be read from many angles.

## The key ideas

- Angle of incidence = angle of reflection, measured from the normal.
- Incident ray, reflected ray and normal lie in one plane.
- Smooth surface: specular reflection. Rough surface: diffuse reflection.
