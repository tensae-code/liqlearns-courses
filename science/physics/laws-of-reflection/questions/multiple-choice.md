# Laws of reflection — mirror bounce and rough scatter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A ray strikes a mirror at 35° to the normal. Reflection angle?
a) 35° — the angles are equal.  b) The normal.  c) Its rough surface reflects diffusely — rays scatter in many directions.  d) No — each ray obeys them; the surface points many ways.
**Answer: a) 35° — the angles are equal.**

**2.** Angles of reflection are measured from — ?
a) 35° — the angles are equal.  b) The normal.  c) Its rough surface reflects diffusely — rays scatter in many directions.  d) No — each ray obeys them; the surface points many ways.
**Answer: b) The normal.**

**3.** Why does paper give no mirror image?
a) 35° — the angles are equal.  b) The normal.  c) Its rough surface reflects diffusely — rays scatter in many directions.  d) No — each ray obeys them; the surface points many ways.
**Answer: c) Its rough surface reflects diffusely — rays scatter in many directions.**

**4.** Does diffuse reflection break the reflection laws?
