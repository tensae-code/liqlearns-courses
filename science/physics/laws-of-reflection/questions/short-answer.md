# Laws of reflection — mirror bounce and rough scatter — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A ray strikes a mirror at 35° to the normal. Reflection angle?
**Answer: 35° — the angles are equal.**

**2.** Angles of reflection are measured from — ?
**Answer: The normal.**

**3.** Why does paper give no mirror image?
**Answer: Its rough surface reflects diffusely — rays scatter in many directions.**

**4.** Does diffuse reflection break the reflection laws?
**Answer: No — each ray obeys them; the surface points many ways.**
