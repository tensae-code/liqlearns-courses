# The operational amplifier — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An op-amp amplifies…
a) The sum only  b) The difference between its two inputs  c) Nothing without valves  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) The difference between its two inputs**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Its open-loop gain is of order…
a) Exactly 1  b) 10⁵ or more  c) About 10  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) 10⁵ or more**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In the inverting amplifier, the − input is called a virtual earth because…
a) It is connected to the soil  b) Feedback holds it at 0 V while it draws no current  c) The chip is earthed inside  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Feedback holds it at 0 V while it draws no current**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The inverting gain is…
a) Rf + Rin  b) −Rf/Rin  c) Rin/Rf  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) −Rf/Rin**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The non-inverting gain is…
a) −Rf/Rin  b) 1 + Rf/Rin  c) Always 1  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) 1 + Rf/Rin**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Without feedback, the op-amp works as a…
a) Linear amplifier  b) Comparator, snapping between rails at the crossing  c) Oscillator only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Comparator, snapping between rails at the crossing**
