# The operational amplifier — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The op-amp's two inputs are inverting and ___-inverting.
**Answer:** non

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A virtual earth draws no ___.
**Answer:** current

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Inverting gain = −Rf / ___.
**Answer:** Rin

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) An open-loop op-amp used as a threshold switch is a ___.
**Answer:** comparator
