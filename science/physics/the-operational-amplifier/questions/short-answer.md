# The operational amplifier — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does feedback turn wild gain into precision?
**Answer:** The output is forced to keep the inputs matched, so the circuit's behaviour is set by the feedback network (the resistors), not by the chip's uncontrolled gain.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State the two ideal op-amp rules used in these circuits.
**Answer:** Inputs draw no current; with negative feedback, the inputs sit at equal voltage.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How does the night switch use a comparator?
**Answer:** An LDR divider's voltage crosses the reference as light falls; the comparator's output snaps and switches the lamp on.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) What does the minus sign in the inverting gain mean?
**Answer:** The output is inverted relative to the input — a positive input swing drives the output negative.
