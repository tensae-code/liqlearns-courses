# Video script — The operational amplifier

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the operational amplifier is a high-gain difference amplifier on a chip**. The op-amp has inverting (−) and non-inverting (+) inputs and amplifies their difference by an open-loop gain of 10⁵ or more. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — two inputs, one enormous gain; virtual earth and the inverting amplifier; non-inverting amplifier and comparator — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
