# Hands-on — The operational amplifier

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with your hands
If a trainer board or simulator is available, build the inverting amplifier with Rin = 10 kΩ and Rf = 100 kΩ and verify the −10 gain and the inversion on an oscilloscope or meter; then rewire as a comparator with an LDR divider on one input and watch the output snap as you shade the LDR — the night switch, working.
