## Work in physics — force times distance — the teaching
In physics, work has a strict meaning: work is done when a force moves its point of application in the force's own direction. Work W = F × s, force times the displacement in that direction, measured in joules (J). Push a wall all afternoon and, if it does not move, you have done no work in the physics sense, however tired you are. Carrying a load flat also does no work against gravity, because the movement is perpendicular to the weight.

**Keep the formula:** W = F × s

## The key ideas

- Work W = F × s, measured in joules.
- No displacement means no work, however great the effort.
- Movement perpendicular to the force means no work by that force.
