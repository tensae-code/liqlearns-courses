# Work in physics — force times distance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A force of 50 N pushes a box 4 m along the force. Work done?
a) W = 50 × 4 = 200 J.  b) Zero — no displacement.  c) Still zero — displacement is zero.  d) The joule (J).
**Answer: a) W = 50 × 4 = 200 J.**

**2.** You push a wall for an hour and it never moves. Work done on the wall?
a) W = 50 × 4 = 200 J.  b) Zero — no displacement.  c) Still zero — displacement is zero.  d) The joule (J).
**Answer: b) Zero — no displacement.**

**3.** A 100 N force moves nothing but is doubled to 200 N and still nothing moves. Work?
a) W = 50 × 4 = 200 J.  b) Zero — no displacement.  c) Still zero — displacement is zero.  d) The joule (J).
**Answer: c) Still zero — displacement is zero.**

**4.** What is the unit of work?
a) W = 50 × 4 = 200 J.  b) Zero — no displacement.  c) Still zero — displacement is zero.  d) The joule (J).
**Answer: d) The joule (J).**
