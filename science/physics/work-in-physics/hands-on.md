# Hands-on — Work in physics — force times distance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Drag a chair two metres across the room — work done. Then push the wall with the same effort — none. Say the difference out loud.

## Apply it where you live
Estimate the work you do lifting a jerrycan onto a step: choose force and height, then multiply.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21832 — pushing a box across the floor (work) vs pushing a wall (no work)] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using W = F × s: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
