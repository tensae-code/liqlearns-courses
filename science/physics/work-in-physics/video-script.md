In physics, work has a strict meaning: work is done when a force moves its point of application in the force's own direction.
Work W = F × s, force times the displacement in that direction, measured in joules (J).
Push a wall all afternoon and, if it does not move, you have done no work in the physics sense, however tired you are.
Carrying a load flat also does no work against gravity, because the movement is perpendicular to the weight.

Remember: **Work W = F × s, measured in joules.**
Remember: **No displacement means no work, however great the effort.**
Remember: **Movement perpendicular to the force means no work by that force.**
Keep the formula: **W = F × s**
