# The spectrometer — reading spectra — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The telescope and prism table share readings from a graduated _____ scale.
**Answer: circular (angle)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A wide slit ruins purity because the overlapping slit-images _____ into one another.
**Answer: smear (overlap)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A spectrum of discrete bright lines from a discharge tube is called an _____ spectrum.
**Answer: emission**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Dark lines across a continuum, where a gas has swallowed its own wavelengths, form an _____ spectrum.
**Answer: absorption**
