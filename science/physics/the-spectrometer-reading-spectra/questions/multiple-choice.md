# The spectrometer — reading spectra — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The collimator's job in a spectrometer is to…
a) Spread the light into colours  b) Deliver a parallel beam from the slit to the prism  c) Magnify the spectrum  d) Measure angles
**Answer: b) Deliver a parallel beam from the slit to the prism**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The telescope measures the deviated light by…
a) Timing it  b) Forming a sharp image of the slit for each wavelength and reading its angular position  c) Heating a sensor  d) Splitting it again
**Answer: b) Forming a sharp image of the slit for each wavelength and reading its angular position**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A pure spectrum requires all of these EXCEPT…
a) A narrow slit  b) A parallel beam  c) The prism near minimum deviation  d) The widest possible slit for brightness
**Answer: d) The widest possible slit for brightness**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) For measurement, the prism is set at minimum deviation because…
a) The deviation there obeys the exact symmetric formulas and changes slowest with setting, giving the most reliable readings  b) The spectrum is brightest there  c) No deviation occurs there  d) The apex angle vanishes there
**Answer: a) The deviation there obeys the exact symmetric formulas and changes slowest with setting, giving the most reliable readings**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) An element is identified by its spectrum because…
a) All elements emit the same lines  b) Each element's set of emitted wavelengths is unique — a fingerprint  c) The brightest line gives its atomic number directly  d) Only metals emit lines
**Answer: b) Each element's set of emitted wavelengths is unique — a fingerprint**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Helium was first detected…
a) In a mine  b) In the Sun's spectrum, by its lines, before it was isolated on Earth  c) In seawater  d) By its smell
**Answer: b) In the Sun's spectrum, by its lines, before it was isolated on Earth**
