# Frontiers of physics — Planck units and the scientific worldview — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The gravitational constant is written with the letter _____ .
**Answer: G**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The Planck length is about 1.6 × 10⁻³⁵ _____ .
**Answer: metres**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The unmerged theory awaited at the Planck frontier is quantum _____ .
**Answer: gravity**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Physics judges a model by testing its _____ against the world.
**Answer: predictions**
