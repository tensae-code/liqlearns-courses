# Frontiers of physics — Planck units and the scientific worldview — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The constant c belongs to which theory's heart?
a) Gravitation  b) Relativity  c) Quantum theory  d) Thermodynamics
**Answer: a) Gravitation**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Planck's constant h sets the grain-size of…
a) Action and energy exchange — the quantum scale  b) Length  c) Mass  d) Timekeeping
**Answer: b) Length**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The Planck length is about…
a) 10⁻¹⁸ m  b) 1.6 × 10⁻³⁵ m  c) 10⁻¹⁰ m  d) 10⁻²⁰ m
**Answer: c) 10⁻¹⁰ m**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The Planck mass is surprising because it is…
a) About 2.2 × 10⁻⁸ kg — a flea-egg scale, almost human  b) Exactly zero  c) Equal to an electron's mass  d) Larger than a star
**Answer: d) Larger than a star**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) At the Planck scale, the two theories that must merge — and still contradict — are…
a) Thermodynamics and optics  b) General relativity and quantum theory  c) Classical mechanics and electromagnetism  d) Chemistry and biology
**Answer: a) Thermodynamics and optics**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In the closing essay's account, when a model fails against the world, that is…
a) The method working — the failure directs the repair, as Rutherford's failure directed Bohr's  b) A scandal ending the subject  c) Proof that nothing can be known  d) A reason to keep the model anyway
**Answer: b) A scandal ending the subject**
