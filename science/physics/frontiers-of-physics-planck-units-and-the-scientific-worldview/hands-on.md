# Hands-on — Frontiers of physics — Planck units and the scientific worldview

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stand at the scale
Write the sizes in a column: atom ≈ 10⁻¹⁰ m, nucleus ≈ 10⁻¹⁴ m, the smallest structures accelerators resolve ≈ 10⁻¹⁸ m, Planck length ≈ 10⁻³⁵ m. Count the powers of ten from the last probed scale to the Planck line. Say aloud: seventeen orders of magnitude of frontier — the map's edge is not near, and it is exactly placed.

## Audit one model
Take the perfect gas from this course. List what it keeps (point particles, elastic collisions) and what it drops (particle size, attractions), then name the book-fact from Amaldi's own gas chapter — real gases — where the dropped flesh returns. Say aloud: a model is a tool with its limits printed on it; that honesty is the worldview.
