Today: **the edge of the map**. Three constants rule everything you have learned: c from relativity, G from gravity, h from the quantum. Planck combined them into nature's own measuring scale — a length of 10⁻³⁵ metres, a time of 10⁻⁴⁴ seconds, where quantum theory and gravity collide and neither yet wins. That collision is the living frontier. And the last word belongs to the method itself: models, tested against the world, repaired when they fail — the same regularities answering in every country that asks. This is where the course ends: knowing what is known, and exactly where it runs out.
