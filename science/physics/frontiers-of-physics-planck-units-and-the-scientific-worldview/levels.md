# Frontiers of physics — Planck units and the scientific worldview — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — Name the three governing constants and their theories; Give the Planck length, time, mass and energy values and how the units are built; Explain why the Planck scale is the quantum-gravity frontier; State the model-and-test account of how physics advances, with one course example — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
