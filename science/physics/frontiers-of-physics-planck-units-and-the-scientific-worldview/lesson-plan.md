# Lesson plan — Frontiers of physics — Planck units and the scientific worldview

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** Frontiers of physics — Planck units and the scientific worldview
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18), the capstone of the Physics title — beside the modern-physics lessons, closing the Amaldi course and the course's own arc
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Combining c, G and h builds nature's own measuring scale — the **Planck units** (length ≈ 1.6 × 10⁻³⁵ m, time ≈ 5.4 × 10⁻⁴⁴ s, mass ≈ 2.2 × 10⁻⁸ kg, energy ≈ 2 × 10⁹ J), at whose frontier general relativity and quantum theory must merge and still have not — and the course closes by naming the **scientific worldview** itself: models tested against the world, revised or replaced, as the course's last lesson
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Earlier lessons make a good preparation and are recommended, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the three governing constants and their theories.
2. Give the Planck length, time, mass and energy values and how the units are built.
3. Explain why the Planck scale is the quantum-gravity frontier.
4. State the model-and-test account of how physics advances, with one course example.
5. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson text alone.
