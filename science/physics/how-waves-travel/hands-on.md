# Hands-on — How waves travel — transverse and longitudinal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Flick a rope tied to a post: the bump travels, the rope stays. Then push a slinky or spring forward in pulses: crowds and gaps travel — two wave kinds with your hands.

## Apply it where you live
Demonstrate both wave kinds to a companion and have them name each correctly.
