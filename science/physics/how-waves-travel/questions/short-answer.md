# How waves travel — transverse and longitudinal — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Does the water in a water wave travel with the wave?
**Answer: No — energy travels; the matter oscillates in place.**

**2.** Sound is which kind of wave?
**Answer: Longitudinal (and mechanical).**

**3.** A rope wave is which kind?
**Answer: Transverse.**

**4.** What are the crowded and stretched parts of a longitudinal wave called?
**Answer: Compressions and rarefactions.**
