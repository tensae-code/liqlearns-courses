# How waves travel — transverse and longitudinal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Does the water in a water wave travel with the wave?
a) No — energy travels; the matter oscillates in place.  b) Longitudinal (and mechanical).  c) Transverse.  d) Compressions and rarefactions.
**Answer: a) No — energy travels; the matter oscillates in place.**

**2.** Sound is which kind of wave?
a) No — energy travels; the matter oscillates in place.  b) Longitudinal (and mechanical).  c) Transverse.  d) Compressions and rarefactions.
**Answer: b) Longitudinal (and mechanical).**

**3.** A rope wave is which kind?
a) No — energy travels; the matter oscillates in place.  b) Longitudinal (and mechanical).  c) Transverse.  d) Compressions and rarefactions.
**Answer: c) Transverse.**

**4.** What are the crowded and stretched parts of a longitudinal wave called?
a) No — energy travels; the matter oscillates in place.  b) Longitudinal (and mechanical).  c) Transverse.  d) Compressions and rarefactions.
**Answer: d) Compressions and rarefactions.**
