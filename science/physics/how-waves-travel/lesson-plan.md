# Lesson plan — How waves travel — transverse and longitudinal

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** How waves travel — transverse and longitudinal
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Mechanical Oscillation and Sound Wave — Grade 9 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A wave carries energy without carrying matter.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 9 lessons before it in Unit 6 and on everyday observation; it needs nothing else to start.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. A wave carries energy without carrying matter.
2. Mechanical waves need a medium; electromagnetic waves do not.
3. Transverse: vibration perpendicular to travel. Longitudinal: vibration parallel — compressions and rarefactions.
4. Answer the lesson's questions, including any calculation, with working shown.
