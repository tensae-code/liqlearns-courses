# Moment of inertia — rotation's mass — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. Rotation's resistance to change: I = Σmr² for point masses (a three-mass example totalling 164 kg·m² is worked), depending on mass and its distance from the axis.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Rotation's resistance to change: I = Σmr² for point masses (a three-mass example totalling 328 kg·m² is worked), depending on mass and its distance from the axis.**
**Answer: False**
