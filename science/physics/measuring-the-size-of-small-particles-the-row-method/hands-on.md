# Hands-on — Measuring the size of small particles — the row method

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with your hands
Pour a spoonful of millet, lentils or small beads. Build a straight row of 30–50 of them, tight together, against a ruler. Read the row's length, count again to be sure, and divide. Then measure one grain alone and compare the two answers — and say which one you trust, and why. Repeat with a second row: real measurements are repeated.
