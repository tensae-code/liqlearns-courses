# Measuring the size of small particles — the row method — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) In the row method, one particle's size = row length ÷ ___ of particles.
**Answer:** number (count)

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Laying many particles in a line shares the reading ___ over all of them.
**Answer:** error

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The Grade 11 lab spreads oil exactly one ___ thick to size a molecule.
**Answer:** molecule

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Particles in a row must be equal and packed without ___.
**Answer:** gaps
