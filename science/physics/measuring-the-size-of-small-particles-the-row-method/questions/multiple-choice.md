# Measuring the size of small particles — the row method — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) The row method finds one particle's size as…
a) L × N  b) L ÷ N  c) N ÷ L  d) L + N
**Answer: b) L ÷ N**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) One grain alone is hard to measure because…
a) It rolls away  b) The ruler's reading error is as large as the grain itself  c) Grains are invisible  d) Rulers cannot touch grains
**Answer: b) The ruler's reading error is as large as the grain itself**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A row of 40 grains measures 100 mm. One grain is…
a) 4 mm  b) 0.4 mm  c) 2.5 mm  d) 25 mm
**Answer: c) 2.5 mm**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Dividing by N also divides the…
a) Particles  b) Reading error — it is shared over all N particles  c) Ruler  d) Length
**Answer: b) Reading error — it is shared over all N particles**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The Grade 11 molecule-size lab works by…
a) Counting molecules under a microscope  b) Spreading oil one molecule thick and dividing volume by area  c) Weighing one molecule  d) Freezing molecules in a row
**Answer: b) Spreading oil one molecule thick and dividing volume by area**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A molecule's size from the school oil-film method is about…
a) 1 mm  b) 1 cm  c) 10⁻⁹ m  d) 1 m
**Answer: c) 10⁻⁹ m**
