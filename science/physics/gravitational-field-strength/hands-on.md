# Hands-on — Gravitational field strength

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Same you, different worlds
Take a mass you know — say 60 kg. Its weight on Earth is mg = 60 × 9.8 ≈ 590 N. Work out its weight where g = 1.6 N/kg (the Moon), g = 3.7 N/kg (Mars) and g = 25 N/kg (Jupiter): multiply each time. The mass never changed — 60 kg everywhere — only the field strength of the place did. Write the three weights beside Earth's and label each with its g: your table is the definition g = F/m, read backwards.
