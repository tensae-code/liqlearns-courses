# Gravitational field strength — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Gravitational field strength is force per unit _____.
**Answer: mass**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) For a spherical mass, g = GM divided by _____ squared.
**Answer: distance**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) At Earth's surface g is about _____ N/kg.
**Answer: 9.8**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Gravitational field lines always point _____ the mass creating the field.
**Answer: toward**
