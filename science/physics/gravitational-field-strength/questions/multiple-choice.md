# Gravitational field strength — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Gravitational field strength at a point is defined as…
**Answer: a) the force exerted on each kilogram placed at that point  b) the total force on any mass there  c) the mass of the planet  d) the acceleration of the planet**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Field lines around a mass point…
**Answer: a) toward the mass, crowding closer where the field is stronger  b) away from the mass  c) in circles  d) only downward**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The unit of gravitational field strength is…
**Answer: a) N/kg  b) kg/N  c) N·m  d) m/s only, never N/kg**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Doubling the distance from a mass's centre makes its field strength…
**Answer: a) a quarter as great — g = GM/r² is an inverse-square law  b) half as great  c) twice as great  d) unchanged**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A 10 kg mass and a 1 kg mass are placed at the same point in a field. The field strength they experience is…
**Answer: a) the same for both — g belongs to the place  b) ten times greater for the 10 kg mass  c) ten times greater for the 1 kg mass  d) zero for both**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Field strength and free-fall acceleration at the same place are…
**Answer: a) numerically equal — a = F/m = g  b) unrelated quantities  c) always different by a factor of 9.8  d) equal only on Earth**
