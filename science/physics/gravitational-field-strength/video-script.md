Today: **gravitational field strength**. A mass fills the space around it with a field; g measures that field as force per kilogram, g = F/m = GM/r² — 9.8 N/kg at Earth's surface, 1.6 on the Moon, quartering every time you double the distance. It belongs to the place, not to the visitor — which is why it also equals the acceleration of anything dropped there.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
