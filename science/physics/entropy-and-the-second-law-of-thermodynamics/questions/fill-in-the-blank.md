# Entropy and the second law of thermodynamics — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The quantity that never decreases in any real process is _____.
**Answer: entropy**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) An engine that turns heat into work while rejecting some heat to a cold sink is a heat _____.
**Answer: engine**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) No heat engine can be 100% _____ — some heat must always be rejected.
**Answer: efficient**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A _____ pays work in to move heat from cold to hot.
**Answer: refrigerator**
