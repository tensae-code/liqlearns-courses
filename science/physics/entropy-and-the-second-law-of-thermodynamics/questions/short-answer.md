# Entropy and the second law of thermodynamics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A gas expands to fill a whole room and never gathers back into its corner. Which law explains the one-way behaviour, and what happens to entropy?
**Answer: The second law of thermodynamics; the total entropy increases as the gas spreads out, and the reverse would decrease it, which never happens.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State the second law in its heat-engine form.
**Answer: No heat engine can convert all the heat it takes in into work; some heat must be rejected to a colder sink.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does the back of a working fridge feel warm?
**Answer: It dumps the heat removed from inside plus the energy of the work paid in to move that heat against its natural direction.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Give one more everyday irreversible process and name its entropy change.
**Answer: Any one-way example (ice melting in a warm room, perfume spreading, a bouncing ball settling); in each the total entropy increases.**
