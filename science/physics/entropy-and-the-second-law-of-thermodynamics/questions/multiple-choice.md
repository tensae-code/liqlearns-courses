# Entropy and the second law of thermodynamics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In every real process, the total entropy of the universe…
**Answer: a) increases  b) decreases  c) stays exactly zero  d) is destroyed**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Heat flows by itself…
**Answer: a) from hotter to colder only  b) from colder to hotter only  c) equally both ways  d) never**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A heat engine must reject some of its heat because…
**Answer: a) the second law forbids turning all the heat into work  b) engines are badly built  c) fuel is impure  d) work leaks**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The best possible engine efficiency depends only on…
**Answer: a) the temperatures of the hot source and cold sink  b) the size of the engine  c) the brand of fuel  d) the speed of the pistons**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A refrigerator can move heat from cold to hot because…
**Answer: a) work is paid in to drive the cycle  b) cold is heavier  c) heat likes to climb  d) the door is shut**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A fridge running with its door open in a sealed room…
**Answer: a) warms the room — the dumped heat includes the motor’s work  b) cools the room  c) leaves it unchanged  d) freezes it**
