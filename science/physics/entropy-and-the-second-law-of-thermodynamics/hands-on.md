# Hands-on — Entropy and the second law of thermodynamics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch a one-way process
Put a drop of food colouring in a glass of still water and watch, without stirring, for five minutes, sketching the spread each minute. Then answer in writing: what would the reverse film look like, why do you never see it, and which quantity — named in the lesson — grows as the colour spreads?
