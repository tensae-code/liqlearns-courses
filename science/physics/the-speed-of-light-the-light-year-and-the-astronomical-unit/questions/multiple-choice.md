# The speed of light, the light-year and the astronomical unit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The speed of light in vacuum is about…
**Answer: a) 3×10⁸ m/s  b) 300 m/s  c) 3×10⁵ m/s  d) infinite**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) We see the Sun as it was…
**Answer: a) about 8 minutes ago  b) instantly  c) yesterday  d) one year ago**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) One light-year is…
**Answer: a) a distance — what light travels in one year  b) a time of one year  c) the Sun's age  d) a speed**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) One light-year is about…
**Answer: a) 9.46×10¹⁵ m  b) 1.50×10¹¹ m  c) 3×10⁸ m  d) 1.5×10⁸ km exactly one**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) One astronomical unit is…
**Answer: a) the mean Earth–Sun distance  b) the Moon's distance  c) one light-year  d) the Earth's diameter**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Light crosses 1 AU in about…
**Answer: a) 8 minutes  b) 8 seconds  c) 8 hours  d) 1 year**
