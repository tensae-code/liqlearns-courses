# The speed of light, the light-year and the astronomical unit — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Light travels at c ≈ 3×10⁸ _____ per second.
**Answer: metres**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The distance light travels in one year is called a _____ .
**Answer: light-year**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) One light-year ≈ 9.46×10¹⁵ _____ .
**Answer: metres**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The Earth–Sun distance, 1 _____ , is about 8 light-minutes.
**Answer: astronomical unit (AU)**
