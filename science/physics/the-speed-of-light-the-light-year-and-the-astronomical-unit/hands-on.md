# Hands-on — The speed of light, the light-year and the astronomical unit

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

["(1) With a stopwatch app and a torch across a dark field, try to time light's journey — record honestly that no delay can be measured at this scale, and say why. (2) Pace out a scale model: if 1 step = 1 AU, mark the Sun and Earth; then compute how many steps one light-year would need (about 63,000) and write the number down instead of walking it. (3) Find tonight's brightest star in a sky app and read its distance in light-years; write what year the light you see left it."]
