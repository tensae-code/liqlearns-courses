# Hands-on — Physics in everyday life and society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ask at home
Ask the people at home: where does physics work in this house? List five places — a lamp, a phone, a stove, a bicycle, a water pump — and name the physics inside each one.

## See it and say it
Study this lesson's picture — a split scene — a bus, a phone, a hospital scanner and a power line, each tagged with the physics inside it — and name every part of it out loud. Then close the picture and draw it from memory; check your drawing against the lesson.

## Teach it back
Explain this lesson to someone at home using one thing in the room as your example. Their questions show you which part to re-read.
