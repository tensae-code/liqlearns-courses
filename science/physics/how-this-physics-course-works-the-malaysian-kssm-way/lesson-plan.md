# Lesson plan — How this Physics course works — the Malaysian KSSM way

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** How this Physics course works — the Malaysian KSSM way
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18) — a new lesson built for the Malaysia placement named in lesson/sources.md
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Malaysia's KSSM Form 4 book organises the whole of physics by **themes** — Elementary Physics, Newtonian Mechanics, Heat, and Waves, Light and Optics — works each chapter through activities with **Career Info** boxes and 21st-century-skills tasks, and closes every chapter with a **Performance Evaluation in SPM examination format**
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Earlier lessons make a good preparation and are recommended, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The book's shape — four themes, six chapters** — The Form 4 book (Sasbadi, for the Ministry of Education Malaysia, KSSM, first published 2019) is organised by **themes**, not by a single topic ladder. **Theme 1, Elementary Physics:** Ch. 1 Measurement — physical quantities and scientific investigation. **Theme 2, Newtonian Mechanics:** Ch. 2 Force and Motion I (motion, inertia, momentum, force, weight) and Ch. 3 **Gravitation** — Newton's universal law, Kepler's laws and man-made satellites given a chapter of their own. **Theme 3, Heat:** Ch. 4 Heat — thermal equilibrium, specific heat, latent heat and the gas laws inside the heat chapter. **Theme 4, Waves, Light and Optics:** Ch. 5 Waves (fundamentals, damping and resonance, reflection, refraction, diffraction, interference, electromagnetic waves) and Ch. 6 Light and Optics — where lenses and optical instruments come **before** spherical mirrors, mirrors last. Answers, a glossary and an index close the book (pp. 283–290).
2. **Inside the chapters — activities, Career Info and 21st-century skills** — The chapters are worked, not just told: sections open the physics through activities and investigations in the KSSM manner — measurement begins with physical quantities and a taught section on **scientific investigation** itself, so the method of physics is the book's second section, before any motion is taught. Two pieces of furniture recur through the Form 4 book: **Career Info** boxes, which tie the chapter's physics to real occupations, and activities tagged for **21st-century skills** — collaboration, communication and critical thinking practised on the physics itself. The book also exists, whole, in two languages at once: the Malay edition (Fizik Tingkatan 4) and the English DLP edition (Physics Form 4) are the same book — same themes, chapters, sections and pagination plan — issued in parallel.
3. **How every chapter closes — the SPM-format Performance Evaluation** — Every chapter closes with a **Performance Evaluation / Summative Practice set in SPM examination format** — SPM being Malaysia's national school-leaving examination. The rehearsal is therefore built into the book's rhythm: learn a chapter, then immediately answer for it in the exact style of the final examination, six times over before the book ends. That closes the Malaysian way as a system: themes give the map, activities and Career Info give the working and the point of it, and the SPM-format evaluation gives the test — the same test, in the same format, at the end of every chapter. A learner who knows this shape can open the book at any chapter and know what each part of it is for.
