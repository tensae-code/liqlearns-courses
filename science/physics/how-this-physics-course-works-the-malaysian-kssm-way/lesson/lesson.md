# How this Physics course works — the Malaysian KSSM way

## Do it first
Before you read: here is a puzzle of organisation. Six chapters — Measurement, Force and Motion, Gravitation, Heat, Waves, Light and Optics. Another country might file Gravitation inside mechanics and mirrors before lenses. Malaysia gives gravitation a chapter of its own, and puts mirrors last of all, after lenses. This lesson is the map of that book: its four themes, the furniture inside its chapters, and the examination-format evaluation that closes every chapter.

## Sub lesson 1 — The book's shape — four themes, six chapters

The Form 4 book (Sasbadi, for the Ministry of Education Malaysia, KSSM, first published 2019) is organised by **themes**, not by a single topic ladder. **Theme 1, Elementary Physics:** Ch. 1 Measurement — physical quantities and scientific investigation. **Theme 2, Newtonian Mechanics:** Ch. 2 Force and Motion I (motion, inertia, momentum, force, weight) and Ch. 3 **Gravitation** — Newton's universal law, Kepler's laws and man-made satellites given a chapter of their own. **Theme 3, Heat:** Ch. 4 Heat — thermal equilibrium, specific heat, latent heat and the gas laws inside the heat chapter. **Theme 4, Waves, Light and Optics:** Ch. 5 Waves (fundamentals, damping and resonance, reflection, refraction, diffraction, interference, electromagnetic waves) and Ch. 6 Light and Optics — where lenses and optical instruments come **before** spherical mirrors, mirrors last. Answers, a glossary and an index close the book (pp. 283–290).

## Sub lesson 2 — Inside the chapters — activities, Career Info and 21st-century skills

The chapters are worked, not just told: sections open the physics through activities and investigations in the KSSM manner — measurement begins with physical quantities and a taught section on **scientific investigation** itself, so the method of physics is the book's second section, before any motion is taught. Two pieces of furniture recur through the Form 4 book: **Career Info** boxes, which tie the chapter's physics to real occupations, and activities tagged for **21st-century skills** — collaboration, communication and critical thinking practised on the physics itself. The book also exists, whole, in two languages at once: the Malay edition (Fizik Tingkatan 4) and the English DLP edition (Physics Form 4) are the same book — same themes, chapters, sections and pagination plan — issued in parallel.

## Sub lesson 3 — How every chapter closes — the SPM-format Performance Evaluation

Every chapter closes with a **Performance Evaluation / Summative Practice set in SPM examination format** — SPM being Malaysia's national school-leaving examination. The rehearsal is therefore built into the book's rhythm: learn a chapter, then immediately answer for it in the exact style of the final examination, six times over before the book ends. That closes the Malaysian way as a system: themes give the map, activities and Career Info give the working and the point of it, and the SPM-format evaluation gives the test — the same test, in the same format, at the end of every chapter. A learner who knows this shape can open the book at any chapter and know what each part of it is for.

## The key ideas

- The KSSM Form 4 book organises physics by four themes: Elementary Physics; Newtonian Mechanics; Heat; Waves, Light and Optics
- Gravitation is a chapter of its own (Newton, Kepler, satellites), the gas laws sit inside the Heat chapter, and in optics mirrors come last, after lenses
- Career Info boxes and 21st-century-skills activities recur through the book, and the same book is issued in Malay and in English (DLP) in parallel
- Every chapter closes with a Performance Evaluation / Summative Practice in SPM examination format; answers, glossary and index close the book
