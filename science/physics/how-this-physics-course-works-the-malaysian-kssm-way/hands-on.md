# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Malaysian KSSM way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Drawing the book's own map
Take a large sheet and draw the Form 4 book as a map from memory of Sub lesson 1: four theme banners across the top, the six chapters hung under their themes, and under Gravitation its three sections (Newton's law, Kepler, satellites). Mark with a star the two orderings that make this book Malaysian: gravitation with its own chapter, and mirrors last in optics. Under the whole map, write the sentence that closes every chapter: 'Performance Evaluation — SPM format.' Then check your map against the lesson and repair what is missing. Say aloud: themes are the map; the evaluation is the test at every chapter's end.
