# How this Physics course works — the Malaysian KSSM way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Form 4 KSSM book organises physics by…
a) Alphabetical topic order  b) Themes  c) Examination dates  d) The teacher's choice
**Answer: b) Themes**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which theme holds the Gravitation chapter?
a) Elementary Physics  b) Heat  c) Newtonian Mechanics  d) Waves, Light and Optics
**Answer: c) Newtonian Mechanics**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The book's second section (§1.2) teaches…
a) Free fall  b) Scientific investigation — the method of physics itself  c) Lenses  d) Satellites
**Answer: b) Scientific investigation — the method of physics itself**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The Malay and DLP English editions of the Form 4 book are…
a) Two different books  b) The same book in two languages, with the same chapters and pagination plan  c) A book and its answer key  d) A student book and a teacher guide
**Answer: b) The same book in two languages, with the same chapters and pagination plan**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Every chapter of the book closes with…
a) A glossary  b) A Career Info box only  c) A Performance Evaluation / Summative Practice in SPM examination format  d) Blank pages
**Answer: c) A Performance Evaluation / Summative Practice in SPM examination format**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In this book's optics, spherical mirrors are taught…
a) First, before refraction  b) In the waves chapter  c) Last — after lenses and optical instruments  d) Not at all
**Answer: c) Last — after lenses and optical instruments**
