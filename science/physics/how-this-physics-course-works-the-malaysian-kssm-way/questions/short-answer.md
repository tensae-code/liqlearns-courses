# How this Physics course works — the Malaysian KSSM way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the four themes of the Form 4 book and the chapters under Newtonian Mechanics.
**Answer: Answer: Elementary Physics; Newtonian Mechanics; Heat; Waves, Light and Optics. Newtonian Mechanics holds Ch. 2 Force and Motion I and Ch. 3 Gravitation.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What two kinds of furniture recur inside the chapters, and what is each for?
**Answer: Answer: Career Info boxes, which tie the chapter's physics to real occupations, and activities tagged for 21st-century skills, which practise collaboration, communication and critical thinking on the physics itself.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does closing every chapter with an SPM-format evaluation change how the book is used, compared with a single practice paper at the end?
**Answer: Answer: The examination rehearsal is distributed through the year: after each chapter the learner immediately answers in the final examination's exact format, so exam technique is built chapter by chapter instead of being crammed at the end.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A learner lands on §6.6, Image Formation by Spherical Mirrors, and is surprised mirrors come so late. Explain Malaysia's order.
**Answer: Answer: The KSSM optics chapter builds refraction of light first, then lenses and optical instruments, and only then spherical mirrors — mirrors close the chapter and the book's physics, the reverse of the order many other countries use.**
