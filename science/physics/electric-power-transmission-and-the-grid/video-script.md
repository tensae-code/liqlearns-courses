Today: **electric power transmission and the grid**.

Moving electricity is a battle against heat: a wire wastes I squared R, so current is costly. Send the same power at ten times the voltage and the current drops ten times — and the waste drops a hundred times. So the grid steps power up for its long journey on pylons, then steps it down, stage by stage, until it is safe at your socket.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
