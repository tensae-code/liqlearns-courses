# Hands-on — Electric power transmission and the grid

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Square-law losses on paper
For a fixed 10,000 W and a line resistance of 5 Ω, work the loss I²R at 250 V (I = 40 A) and at 10,000 V (I = 1 A): 8,000 W against 5 W. Say aloud: same power delivered, a loss a thousand-plus times smaller — voltage is the lever, current is the cost.

## Trace the journey
Draw the grid as a chain of labelled boxes — power station, step-up transformer, transmission pylon line, substation, distribution line, home meter — and write the voltage rising or falling at each transformer. Say aloud: up for the journey, down for the use.
