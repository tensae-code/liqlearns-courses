# Electric power transmission and the grid — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The heating power wasted in a transmission wire is…
a) V × I  b) I²R  c) R / I  d) V² × I
**Answer: b) I²R**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Electrical energy billed to homes is measured in…
a) Watts  b) Kilowatt-hours  c) Amps  d) Ohms
**Answer: b) Kilowatt-hours**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) For a fixed power, stepping the voltage up ten times makes the current…
a) Ten times larger  b) The same  c) One tenth, and the I²R loss one hundredth  d) One hundredth, and the loss one tenth
**Answer: c) One tenth, and the I²R loss one hundredth**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Long-distance transmission uses very high voltage mainly to…
a) Make appliances faster  b) Keep the current — and so the heating loss — low for the power carried  c) Raise the resistance  d) Avoid using transformers
**Answer: b) Keep the current — and so the heating loss — low for the power carried**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In the grid journey, the voltage is stepped down…
a) At the power station  b) In stages at substations, ending near the user at a safe supply voltage  c) Only inside appliances  d) Never — homes receive transmission voltage
**Answer: b) In stages at substations, ending near the user at a safe supply voltage**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) If demand exceeds supply at a moment, the grid may show…
a) Higher voltages everywhere  b) Brown-outs (sagging voltage) and blackouts  c) Free electricity  d) Colder wires
**Answer: b) Brown-outs (sagging voltage) and blackouts**
