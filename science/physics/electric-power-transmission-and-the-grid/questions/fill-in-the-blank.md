# Electric power transmission and the grid — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Electrical power is voltage multiplied by _____.
**Answer: current**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Heating loss in a wire grows with the _____ of the current.
**Answer: square**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A transformer that raises voltage is called a _____-up transformer.
**Answer: step**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The network linking power stations to all users is called the _____.
**Answer: grid**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Voltage is reduced near towns at a _____.
**Answer: substation**
