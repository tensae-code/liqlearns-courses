# Lesson plan — How this physics course works — the Jordanian Grade 10 way

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** How this physics course works — the Jordanian Grade 10 way
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18), inside the vectors-and-motion strand — the doorway lesson for Jordan's Grade 10 book
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Jordan's Grade 10 book continues the NCCD pattern of Grade 9 — introductory experiment, general idea, glossed English terms, Physics-and-life and unit review — and adds two things: an air-track introductory experiment for motion, and a STEAM enrichment in every unit that builds the unit's physics into a designed thing
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Any earlier lesson named below is recommended first, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The same NCCD pattern, one year on** — The Grade 10 terms keep the Grade 9 architecture exactly: each unit opens with an introductory experiment — Term 1's vectors unit opens by finding the resultant of two forces practically, Term 2's motion work opens on the air track — followed by the general idea, lessons with the English terms glossed at first use, and the closing review and application spreads.
2. **New tools for a harder course** — Grade 10 is where Jordan's course turns mathematical: vectors are drawn, added and resolved; motion is graphed and written in equations; Newton's laws are applied through free-body diagrams. The book's pattern carries the load — every new tool is first met in the introductory experiment or a worked practical before it is used in problems.
3. **STEAM enrichment in every unit** — Each unit closes with a STEAM enrichment that joins the physics to design and making — in the workbook, the wave unit's enrichment builds a floating electricity-generation station. The enrichment is the course's way of saying the physics is finished only when it has been built into something.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
