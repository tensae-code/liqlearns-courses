# How this physics course works — the Jordanian Grade 10 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the Grade 9 pattern parts that Grade 10 keeps.
**Answer: Introductory experiment first; general-idea page; English terms glossed beside the Arabic at first use; lesson reviews; unit review; 'Physics and life' application spread.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the course introduce vectors practically before algebraically?
**Answer: Following its pattern, the tool is first met in the hand — the resultant found with real pulls — so the algebra that follows describes an experienced thing, not a symbol game.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What is the STEAM enrichment for, in the course's own logic?
**Answer: To finish the unit by building its physics into a designed thing — the physics is complete when it has been used, joining science to technology, engineering, art and mathematics.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which three mathematical tools does Grade 10 add?
**Answer: Vectors (drawn, added, resolved into components, with dot and cross products); motion graphs; and the equations of uniformly accelerated motion.**
