# Hands-on — How this physics course works — the Jordanian Grade 10 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Repeat the vectors unit opener: pull a ring with two spring balances at an angle and find the single pull that balances them — the resultant, found practically. (2) Glide a cart on a levelled track or a smooth board and record its motion with phone video; check whether the spacing of positions is even. (3) Sketch a STEAM build of your own for one unit and label the physics in it.
