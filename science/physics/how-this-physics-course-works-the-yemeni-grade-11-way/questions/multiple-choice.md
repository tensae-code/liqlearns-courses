# How this Physics course works — the Yemeni Grade 11 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) At Grade 11, the English unit titles are printed…
a) Only in a glossary  b) On the unit openers themselves  c) Only in the Grade 10 book  d) Nowhere
**Answer: b) On the unit openers themselves**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The Grade 11 design compared with Grade 10 is…
a) Completely different  b) The same design — objectives, activities, evaluation — at a wider level  c) Objectives removed  d) Evaluation removed
**Answer: b) The same design — objectives, activities, evaluation — at a wider level**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The Grade 11 course has…
a) 8 units  b) 9 units  c) 10 units  d) 12 units
**Answer: c) 10 units**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The first Grade 11 unit is…
a) Heat engines  b) Equilibrium of rigid bodies  c) Electrical measurements  d) Sound waves
**Answer: b) Equilibrium of rigid bodies**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In the Yemeni way, a unit's finish line is…
a) Its last page  b) Its closing evaluation, answered against its objectives  c) Its first activity  d) Its English title
**Answer: b) Its closing evaluation, answered against its objectives**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The embedded activities (نشاط) should be done…
a) After the whole book  b) As they come inside the lessons  c) Never  d) Only in examinations
**Answer: b) As they come inside the lessons**
