# How this Physics course works — the Yemeni Grade 11 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is new about the English titles at Grade 11?
**Answer: They are printed on the unit openers themselves — the English name stands as the unit's own heading beside the Arabic.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name four of the ten Grade 11 units in order.
**Answer: Any four in order, e.g. equilibrium of rigid bodies; periodic motions; sound waves; sound tones and resonance (then light and vision devices; thermodynamics; heat engines; direct current; magnetism and magnetic effects of current; electrical measurements).**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How should a learner use a unit's objectives page?
**Answer: Read it first as the list of things they will be able to do, study with it in view, and count the unit finished only when the closing evaluation shows those things can be done.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why keep the same design from Grade 10 to Grade 11?
**Answer: The learner's study method does not have to be relearned — the same shape carries harder content, so all effort goes to the physics, not to figuring out a new book.**
