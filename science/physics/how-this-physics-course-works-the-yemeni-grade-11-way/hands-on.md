# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Yemeni Grade 11 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read an opener like a contract
Take any unit opener you can find — from this course or a book at hand — and copy out three things onto a card: its objectives (what you will be able to do), its title in both languages if it has them, and the place where its closing check sits. Keep the card at the front of your notes for that unit; tick each objective off only when you can do it without looking. That ticking-off is the Yemeni design working as intended.
