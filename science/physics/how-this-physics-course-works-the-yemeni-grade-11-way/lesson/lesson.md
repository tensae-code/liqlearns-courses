# How this Physics course works — the Yemeni Grade 11 way

## Do it first
Before you read: a Grade 11 unit opener carries the unit's title in English — “Equilibrium of Rigid Bodies” — beside its Arabic title and its objectives. The design you met in Grade 10 is still running, but the map is wider. This lesson reads the Grade 11 course the Yemeni way.

## Sub lesson 1 — The same design, one level up

Grade 11 keeps the Yemeni shape exactly: every unit opens with its printed objectives (أهداف الوحدة), lessons embed short activities (نشاط), and every unit closes with a unit evaluation (تقويم الوحدة). What changes is the print on the opener: at this level the English unit titles are printed on the unit openers themselves — a learner opening Unit 1 reads “Equilibrium of Rigid Bodies” in English as the unit's own heading, not as a translation added later.

## Sub lesson 2 — A wider map — ten units

The ten units run: equilibrium of rigid bodies; periodic motions; sound waves; sound tones and resonance; light and vision devices; thermodynamics; heat engines; direct current; magnetism and the magnetic effects of current; and electrical measurements. The year moves from still bodies through waves and heat to circuits, magnets and the instruments that measure them — each unit still announced by its objectives before its first lesson.

## Sub lesson 3 — Reading a unit the Yemeni way

The design tells you how to study: start at the objectives and read them as promises; meet each English title as the name you will use for the idea in any language; do the embedded activities as they come, not after; and treat the closing evaluation as the unit's finish line. A learner who follows the shape never has to guess what a unit wants — the book states it on the first page of every unit.

## The key ideas

- Grade 11 keeps the design: objectives first, bilingual titles, activities inside lessons, a unit evaluation closing every unit
- The English unit titles are printed on the unit openers themselves (e.g. “Equilibrium of Rigid Bodies”)
- Ten units run from equilibrium of rigid bodies through waves, heat and heat engines to direct current, magnetism and electrical measurements

## Do It Yourself
1. Write the ten Grade 11 units in order and mark the one that sounds hardest — then write what its objectives page would need to promise to make it learnable.
2. Take one unit opener title in English (for example “Equilibrium of Rigid Bodies”) and write, in your own words, what the unit is about before reading any of it. Check yourself after the unit.
3. Plan how you would study one unit the Yemeni way: objectives first, activities as they come, evaluation last — three lines, one per stage.
