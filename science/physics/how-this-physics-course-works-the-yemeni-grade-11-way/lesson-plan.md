**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Earlier lessons make a good preparation and are recommended, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The same design, one level up** — Grade 11 keeps the Yemeni shape exactly: every unit opens with its printed objectives (أهداف الوحدة), lessons embed short activities (نشاط), and every unit closes with a unit evaluation (تقويم الوحدة). What changes is the print on the opener: at this level the English unit titles are printed on the unit openers themselves — a learner opening Unit 1 reads “Equilibrium of Rigid Bodies” in English as the unit's own heading, not as a translation added later.
2. **A wider map — ten units** — The ten units run: equilibrium of rigid bodies; periodic motions; sound waves; sound tones and resonance; light and vision devices; thermodynamics; heat engines; direct current; magnetism and the magnetic effects of current; and electrical measurements. The year moves from still bodies through waves and heat to circuits, magnets and the instruments that measure them — each unit still announced by its objectives before its first lesson.
3. **Reading a unit the Yemeni way** — The design tells you how to study: start at the objectives and read them as promises; meet each English title as the name you will use for the idea in any language; do the embedded activities as they come, not after; and treat the closing evaluation as the unit's finish line. A learner who follows the shape never has to guess what a unit wants — the book states it on the first page of every unit.

## If the learner is stuck
- Cannot start → return to Do it first and do the opening activity with real objects or numbers before reading any explanation; the explanation is the story of that activity.
- Mixes the sub lessons up → re-read the key ideas in order; each key idea is one sub lesson compressed to a line.
