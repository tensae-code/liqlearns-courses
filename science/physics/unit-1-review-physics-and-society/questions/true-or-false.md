# Unit 1 review — physics and society — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. The unit summary restates society's gains from physics, the role of communities, how knowledge is constructed and the career list; the end-of-unit questions ask pupils to argue the importance of learning physics, name community benefits, and report whether their own school has a physics community.**
**Answer: True**

**2. The unit summary restates society's loses from physics, the role of communities, how knowledge is constructed and the career list; the end-of-unit questions ask pupils to argue the importance of learning physics, name community benefits, and report whether their own school has a physics community.**
**Answer: False**
