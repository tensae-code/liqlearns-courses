# Hands-on — Nuclear stability and the stability valley

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Plot ten given nuclei on N–Z graph paper and shade the valley from a data table; mark each nucleus stable or radioactive and check against the table. (2) Start from a neutron-rich nucleus and step it by beta-minus decays, plotting each step, until it lands in the valley. (3) Count, for three stable nuclei of increasing Z, the ratio N/Z and graph the bend yourself.
