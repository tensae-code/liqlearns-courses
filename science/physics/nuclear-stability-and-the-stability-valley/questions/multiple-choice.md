# Nuclear stability and the stability valley — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) On the N–Z chart, stable nuclei…
**Answer: a) lie in a narrow band, the stability valley  b) cover the whole chart  c) lie on the axes  d) form a circle**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Light stable nuclei have…
**Answer: a) N about equal to Z  b) twice as many neutrons  c) no neutrons  d) N = 0**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The valley bends upward because heavier nuclei need…
**Answer: a) more neutrons per proton to dilute proton repulsion  b) fewer neutrons  c) more electrons  d) larger volume only**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) No nucleus is stable beyond…
**Answer: a) bismuth, Z = 83  b) iron  c) uranium, because it is mined  d) Z = 20**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A neutron-rich nucleus moves toward the valley by…
**Answer: a) beta-minus decay, turning a neutron into a proton  b) alpha decay only  c) beta-plus decay  d) fission always**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The very heaviest nuclei decay chiefly by…
**Answer: a) alpha emission, cutting both N and Z  b) beta-minus only  c) electron capture only  d) gamma only**
