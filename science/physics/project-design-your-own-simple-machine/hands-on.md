# Hands-on — Project: design your own simple machine

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Walk your compound with the project's eyes: find one job that hurts — a heavy lift, a long carry — and hold it in mind as your design brief.

## Apply it where you live
Complete the full design-build-test cycle and present sketch, machine and measurements together.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21850 — pupil-made machine sketch beside the built version] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
