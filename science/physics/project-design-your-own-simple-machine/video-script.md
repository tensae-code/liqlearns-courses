# Video script — Project: design your own simple machine

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

This project lesson closes the unit by designing.
Choose a real local need — lifting water, moving stones, splitting wood, opening, carrying.
Sketch a simple machine for it, naming its type, its purpose (force, speed or direction), and its predicted MA and VR from your measurements.
Build it from local materials, test it against the need, measure its real efficiency, and say honestly where friction taxed you.
A design that is measured beats a design that is only admired.

Remember: **Start a design from a real local need.**
Remember: **Predict MA and VR before building; measure efficiency after.**
Remember: **Friction explains the gap between ideal and real performance.**
