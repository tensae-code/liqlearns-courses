# Project: design your own simple machine — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** What is the first step of the design project?
**Answer: Choose a real local need the machine will serve.**

**2.** Which two ratios do you predict before building?
**Answer: MA and VR.**

**3.** After building, what do you measure to judge the machine honestly?
**Answer: Its real efficiency (and actual MA).**

**4.** A built machine reaches 70% efficiency. The missing 30% went where?
**Answer: To friction (work input lost against it).**
