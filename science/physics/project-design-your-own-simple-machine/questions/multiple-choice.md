# Project: design your own simple machine — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the first step of the design project?
a) Choose a real local need the machine will serve.  b) MA and VR.  c) Its real efficiency (and actual MA).  d) To friction (work input lost against it).
**Answer: a) Choose a real local need the machine will serve.**

**2.** Which two ratios do you predict before building?
a) Choose a real local need the machine will serve.  b) MA and VR.  c) Its real efficiency (and actual MA).  d) To friction (work input lost against it).
**Answer: b) MA and VR.**

**3.** After building, what do you measure to judge the machine honestly?
a) Choose a real local need the machine will serve.  b) MA and VR.  c) Its real efficiency (and actual MA).  d) To friction (work input lost against it).
**Answer: c) Its real efficiency (and actual MA).**

**4.** A built machine reaches 70% efficiency. The missing 30% went where?
a) Choose a real local need the machine will serve.  b) MA and VR.  c) Its real efficiency (and actual MA).  d) To friction (work input lost against it).
**Answer: d) To friction (work input lost against it).**
