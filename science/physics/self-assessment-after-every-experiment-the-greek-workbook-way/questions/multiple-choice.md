# Self-assessment after every experiment — the Greek workbook way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Each Greek workbook worksheet accompanies…
a) A chapter of the student book  b) One of the thirteen labs of the lab guide, in the same order  c) An examination  d) A video
**Answer: b) One of the thirteen labs of the lab guide, in the same order**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) On the worksheet, the tables, graph space and conclusion prompts are completed by…
a) The teacher beforehand  b) The book's printed answers  c) The pupil — the pupil authors the whole page  d) Nobody
**Answer: c) The pupil — the pupil authors the whole page**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The closing block of every worksheet asks the pupil to…
a) Copy the conclusion neatly  b) Evaluate their own effort — what went well, where they struggled, what to change  c) Predict the next lab  d) Grade their partner
**Answer: b) Evaluate their own effort — what went well, where they struggled, what to change**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The self-assessment carries no marks because…
a) There is no time  b) Graded self-judgement stops being honest  c) Pupils dislike marks  d) The ministry forbids marks
**Answer: b) Graded self-judgement stops being honest**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Self-assessment appears…
a) Once, at the end of the year  b) After two chosen labs  c) At the end of all thirteen worksheets — a habit, not an event  d) Only for weak pupils
**Answer: c) At the end of all thirteen worksheets — a habit, not an event**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The teacher gains from the block…
a) Extra marks to average  b) Knowledge of where the class actually struggled  c) Less marking  d) Attendance data
**Answer: b) Knowledge of where the class actually struggled**
