# Hands-on — Self-assessment after every experiment — the Greek workbook way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Judge your last experiment
You need: the worksheet or notes from the last hands-on activity you did in this course, and a pen. Rule a fresh block at the foot of the page with three lines: 'Went well:', 'I struggled with:', 'Next time I will:'. Fill it in honestly and in writing, referring to the actual table, graph or conclusion above it — quote one detail from your own work in each line. Then do the named 'next time' improvement once, on the spot, if it is quick (re-plot the graph, complete the table). Say aloud: the experiment ends when I have judged it.
