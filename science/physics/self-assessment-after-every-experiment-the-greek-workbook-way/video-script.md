# Video script — Self-assessment after every experiment — the Greek workbook way

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the last box on the page is about you**. Greece's Grade 9 workbook walks with its lab guide, one worksheet per experiment. You fill the tables. You plot the graph. You write the conclusion. And then — every time, all thirteen labs — the page asks one more thing: evaluate your effort. What did you do well? Where did you get stuck? What will you change? No marks. That is the point — a graded mirror gets polished answers, not honest ones. Judge your work after every experiment, and judging becomes a habit. And a learner who can judge their work can improve it.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
