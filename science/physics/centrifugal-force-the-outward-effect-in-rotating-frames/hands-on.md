# Hands-on — Centrifugal force — the outward effect in rotating frames

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tangent, not outward
You need a marble or small ball and a large plate or tray, or a ball on a string (outdoors, with space and care). Roll the ball across the spinning plate (turn the plate as the ball rolls) and watch its curved path across the plate against its straight path across the table. Or whirl the string ball gently and let go: mark where it lands — along the tangent, not along the string's line. Write the two-frame account of what you saw in four sentences.
