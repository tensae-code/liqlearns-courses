# Video script — Centrifugal force — the outward effect in rotating frames

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the outward push that isn't there — from the ground**. Turning riders feel flung outward, mv²/r worth, and centrifuges earn their living from that feeling. But watch from the ground and nobody is pushed out: bodies go straight, cars and drums turn under them, and released mud flies along the tangent. Pick one frame and stay in it, and both stories agree.
