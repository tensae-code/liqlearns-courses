# Centrifugal force — the outward effect in rotating frames — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The centrifugal effect felt in a rotating frame has size…
a) mv/r  b) mv²/r outward  c) mr²  d) Zero in every frame
**Answer: b) mv²/r outward**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A centrifuge separates a mixture because, in the rotating frame…
a) Everything is pushed to the centre  b) The denser parts are flung outward hardest  c) Gravity switches off  d) The liquid evaporates
**Answer: b) The denser parts are flung outward hardest**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In the ground's inertial frame, a turning car's passenger is pressed to the door because…
a) An outward force pushes the passenger  b) The passenger's body travels straight (inertia) while the car turns under it, until the door pushes inward  c) The door pulls the passenger  d) Friction reverses
**Answer: b) The passenger's body travels straight (inertia) while the car turns under it, until the door pushes inward**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Mud released from a spinning wheel flies off…
a) Radially outward, along the radius  b) Along the tangent, in a straight line  c) Toward the centre  d) Backwards against the spin
**Answer: b) Along the tangent, in a straight line**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Centrifugal force is called a pseudo force because…
a) It is too small to matter  b) In an inertial frame no agent exerts it and it has no third-law partner  c) It only works at night  d) It is a kind of friction
**Answer: b) In an inertial frame no agent exerts it and it has no third-law partner**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In a ground-frame free-body diagram of a circling body you should…
a) Draw centrifugal force outward and centripetal inward so they cancel  b) Draw only real forces, whose resultant must be mv²/r inward  c) Draw no forces at all  d) Draw centrifugal force only
**Answer: b) Draw only real forces, whose resultant must be mv²/r inward**
