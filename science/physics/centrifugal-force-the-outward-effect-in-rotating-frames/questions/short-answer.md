# Centrifugal force — the outward effect in rotating frames — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does the spin dryer remove water but keep the clothes?
**Answer: In the rotating frame the water is flung outward and escapes through the drum's holes; the clothes are too large to pass and are held by the drum wall's inward force.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What actually provides the centripetal force on the car passenger once contact with the door is made?
**Answer: The door's inward normal push (with seat friction) — a real force, acting toward the centre of the turn.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) State the frame rule for using centrifugal force correctly.
**Answer: Use it only inside a rotating-frame account, added outward at mv²/r; in an inertial-frame diagram omit it and require the real forces to supply mv²/r inward.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Doubling the speed at the same radius multiplies the centrifugal effect by what factor?
**Answer: Four — the effect goes as v², so 2² = 4.**
