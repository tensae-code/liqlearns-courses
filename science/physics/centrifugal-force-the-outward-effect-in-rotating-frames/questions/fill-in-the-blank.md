# Centrifugal force — the outward effect in rotating frames — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A machine that separates mixtures by spinning them is a _____.
**Answer: centrifuge**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A released circling object flies off along the _____.
**Answer: tangent**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The passenger's straight-line tendency is Newton's _____ law at work.
**Answer: first**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Centrifugal force is classed as a _____ (apparent) force.
**Answer: pseudo**
