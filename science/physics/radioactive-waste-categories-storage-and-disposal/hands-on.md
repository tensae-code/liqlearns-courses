# Hands-on — Radioactive waste — categories, storage and the long-term problem

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Barriers in a bucket
Model the layered-barrier idea with no radioactivity at all: put a spoon of strongly coloured drink powder (the "waste") inside a small sealed plastic pot (the canister), set the pot in a tub of sand (the buffer), and stand the tub somewhere dry. The powder can only reach the room if every layer fails in turn — pot, then sand, then tub. Now think like the repository designers in the book: which layer does water attack first, which layer does an earthquake test, and which layer is still working in a thousand years when the others are forgotten? List the barriers of a real deep repository in order, from the waste form outward.
