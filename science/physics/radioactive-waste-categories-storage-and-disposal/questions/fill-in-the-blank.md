# Radioactive waste — categories, storage and the long-term problem — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Spent reactor fuel belongs to the _____-activity category of waste.
**Answer: high**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Waste is sorted by its _____, how strongly it radiates.
**Answer: activity**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Setting liquid high-activity waste into glass is called _____.
**Answer: vitrification**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) For high-activity waste, the final barrier in a deep repository is the stable _____ itself.
**Answer: rock (geology)**
