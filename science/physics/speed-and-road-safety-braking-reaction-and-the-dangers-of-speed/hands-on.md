# Hands-on — Speed and road safety — braking, reaction and the dangers of speed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The ruler-drop reaction test
Work with a partner, with a ruler and a table. Your partner holds the ruler by its top end so it hangs just above your open thumb and finger, which wait at the ruler's zero mark without touching it. Without warning, your partner lets go; you catch the ruler as fast as you can. Read off where your fingers caught it — that distance is how far the ruler fell during your reaction time, and doing it three times shows your reaction is not instant and not always the same. Then talk it through as drivers: at walking pace that delay costs centimetres; scale the same delay up to a car at 20 m/s with a one-second reaction and you get 20 m of road travelled before the brakes are even touched.
