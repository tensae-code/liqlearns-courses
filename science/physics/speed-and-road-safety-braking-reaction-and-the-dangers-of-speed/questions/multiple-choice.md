# Speed and road safety — braking, reaction and the dangers of speed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) During a driver's reaction time, the car…
a) Slows down steadily  b) Travels at full speed, covering the reaction distance  c) Stops instantly  d) Speeds up
**Answer: b) Travels at full speed, covering the reaction distance**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) If a car's speed doubles and reaction time stays the same, the reaction distance…
a) Stays the same  b) Halves  c) Doubles  d) Quadruples
**Answer: c) Doubles**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) If a car's speed doubles, its braking distance becomes…
a) Twice as long  b) Three times as long  c) Four times as long  d) The same
**Answer: c) Four times as long**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Braking distance grows with the square of the speed because…
a) Brakes get hotter  b) Kinetic energy grows with the square of the speed, and the brakes must shed it all  c) Tyres get bigger  d) The driver reacts more slowly
**Answer: b) Kinetic energy grows with the square of the speed, and the brakes must shed it all**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The stopping distance is…
a) The braking distance alone  b) The reaction distance alone  c) Reaction distance + braking distance  d) Speed × reaction time only
**Answer: c) Reaction distance + braking distance**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Which change makes a stopping distance longer?
a) A dry road  b) New tyres  c) A wet road and a tired driver  d) A slower speed
**Answer: c) A wet road and a tired driver**
