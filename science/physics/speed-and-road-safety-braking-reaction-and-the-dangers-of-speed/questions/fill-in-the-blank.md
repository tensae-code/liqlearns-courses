# Speed and road safety — braking, reaction and the dangers of speed — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The distance travelled while the driver is still reacting is called the _____ distance.
**Answer: reaction**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Braking distance grows with the _____ of the speed.
**Answer: square**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Stopping distance = reaction distance + _____ distance.
**Answer: braking**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) On a wet road the tyres grip less, so the braking distance gets _____.
**Answer: longer**
