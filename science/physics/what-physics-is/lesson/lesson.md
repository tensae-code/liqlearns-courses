# What physics is

## Do it first
Look at one old, strong building and one modern device near you. Ask: what keeps the building standing, and what makes the device work? Both answers start in physics.

## What physics is — the teaching
Physics comes from the Greek word phusis, which means nature. Physics is the branch of natural science that describes the fundamental workings of the universe — what things are, the properties we can notice, and the processes they go through. It sits underneath everyday devices, and it also explains why Ethiopia's heritage structures — Harar Jugol, Fasilides Castle, the Axum obelisks and the Lalibela churches — still stand after so many centuries.

## The key ideas

- Physics comes from Greek phusis, meaning nature.
- A person who studies physics is called a physicist.
- Physics-based careers include transport, aviation and space, medicine, forensics and meteorology.

Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
