# Hands-on — What physics is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Look at one old, strong building and one modern device near you. Ask: what keeps the building standing, and what makes the device work? Both answers start in physics.

## Apply it where you live
Find physics at work in one heritage building and one device at home, and say what physics does in each.
