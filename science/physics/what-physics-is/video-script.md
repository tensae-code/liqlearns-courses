# Video script — What physics is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Physics comes from the Greek word phusis, which means nature.
Physics is the branch of natural science that describes the fundamental workings of the universe — what things are, the properties we can notice, and the processes they go through.
It sits underneath everyday devices, and it also explains why Ethiopia's heritage structures — Harar Jugol, Fasilides Castle, the Axum obelisks and the Lalibela churches — still stand after so many centuries.

Remember: **Physics comes from Greek phusis, meaning nature.**
Remember: **A person who studies physics is called a physicist.**
Remember: **Physics-based careers include transport, aviation and space, medicine, forensics and meteorology.**
