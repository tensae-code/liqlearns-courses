# What physics is — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** What does the word physics come from, and what does it mean?
**Answer: It comes from the Greek word phusis, meaning nature.**

**2.** What is a person who studies physics called?
**Answer: A physicist.**

**3.** Name two careers built on physics.
**Answer: Any two of: transport, aviation/space, medicine, forensics/military, meteorology.**

**4.** Name one Ethiopian heritage structure the lesson uses as physics at work.
**Answer: Harar Jugol, Fasilides Castle, an Axum obelisk, or a Lalibela church.**
