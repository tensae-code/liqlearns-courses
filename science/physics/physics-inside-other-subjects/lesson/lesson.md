# Physics inside other subjects

## Do it first
Think of a hospital scan, a bridge, and a bag of cement with a quality mark. Each one leans on physics. Say how, in one sentence each.

## Physics inside other subjects — the teaching
Physics is the foundation under many other fields. Chemistry rests on the physics of atoms and molecules. Engineering builds with physics, geology uses it to study the Earth, and joining physics to living things and to medicine gives biophysics, geophysics and medical physics. Physics also serves applied areas such as transport, traffic, quality control and standards — checking that products and measures are what they claim to be.

## The key ideas

- Chemistry rests on atomic and molecular physics.
- Biophysics, geophysics and medical physics join physics to life, Earth and medicine.
- Applied physics areas include transport, traffic, quality control and standards.

## Say it to your companion
Explain physics inside other subjects to your companion in your own words. Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
