# Video script — Physics inside other subjects

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Physics is the foundation under many other fields.
Chemistry rests on the physics of atoms and molecules.
Engineering builds with physics, geology uses it to study the Earth, and joining physics to living things and to medicine gives biophysics, geophysics and medical physics.
Physics also serves applied areas such as transport, traffic, quality control and standards — checking that products and measures are what they claim to be.

Remember: **Chemistry rests on atomic and molecular physics.**
Remember: **Biophysics, geophysics and medical physics join physics to life, Earth and medicine.**
Remember: **Applied physics areas include transport, traffic, quality control and standards.**
