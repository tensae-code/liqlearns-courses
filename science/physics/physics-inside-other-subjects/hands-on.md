# Hands-on — Physics inside other subjects

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Think of a hospital scan, a bridge, and a bag of cement with a quality mark. Each one leans on physics. Say how, in one sentence each.

## Apply it where you live
Pick one other school subject and write how physics holds it up from underneath.
