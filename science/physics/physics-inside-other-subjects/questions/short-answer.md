# Physics inside other subjects — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** What does chemistry rest on?
**Answer: The physics of atoms and molecules.**

**2.** What is medical physics?
**Answer: Physics applied to medicine.**

**3.** Name two applied areas physics serves.
**Answer: Any two of: transport, traffic, quality control, standards.**

**4.** Which field joins physics to the study of the Earth?
**Answer: Geophysics (and geology uses physics).**
