# Physics inside other subjects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does chemistry rest on?
a) The physics of atoms and molecules.  b) Physics applied to medicine.  c) Any two of: transport, traffic, quality control, standards.  d) Geophysics (and geology uses physics).
**Answer: a) The physics of atoms and molecules.**

**2.** What is medical physics?
a) The physics of atoms and molecules.  b) Physics applied to medicine.  c) Any two of: transport, traffic, quality control, standards.  d) Geophysics (and geology uses physics).
**Answer: b) Physics applied to medicine.**

**3.** Name two applied areas physics serves.
a) The physics of atoms and molecules.  b) Physics applied to medicine.  c) Any two of: transport, traffic, quality control, standards.  d) Geophysics (and geology uses physics).
**Answer: c) Any two of: transport, traffic, quality control, standards.**

**4.** Which field joins physics to the study of the Earth?
a) The physics of atoms and molecules.  b) Physics applied to medicine.  c) Any two of: transport, traffic, quality control, standards.  d) Geophysics (and geology uses physics).
**Answer: d) Geophysics (and geology uses physics).**
