# Video script — Measuring length

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Measurement is comparing an unknown quantity with a unit, using a chosen tool.
Length is the distance between two points; its SI unit is the metre.
Rulers and tapes measure ordinary lengths, and finer instruments measure small diameters accurately.
Choose the tool to fit the job — a tape for a blackboard, a ruler for an exercise book — then area follows from length × width for a rectangle.

Remember: **Measurement is comparing an unknown quantity with a unit.**
Remember: **Length is the distance between two points; its SI unit is the metre.**
Remember: **Area of a rectangle = length × width.**
