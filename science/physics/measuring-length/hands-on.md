# Hands-on — Measuring length

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Measure your exercise book's length and width with a ruler, in metres, then multiply to get its cover area.

## Apply it where you live
Measure a door and a table; record lengths in metres and compute one area.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21808 — tape, ruler and a caliper-style instrument each measuring a different object] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using Area = length × width: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
