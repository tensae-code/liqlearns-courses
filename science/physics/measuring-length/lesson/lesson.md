# Measuring length

## Do it first
Measure your exercise book's length and width with a ruler, in metres, then multiply to get its cover area.

[Picture 21808 — tape, ruler and a caliper-style instrument each measuring a different object]

## Measuring length — the teaching
Measurement is comparing an unknown quantity with a unit, using a chosen tool. Length is the distance between two points; its SI unit is the metre. Rulers and tapes measure ordinary lengths, and finer instruments measure small diameters accurately. Choose the tool to fit the job — a tape for a blackboard, a ruler for an exercise book — then area follows from length × width for a rectangle.

**Keep the formula:** Area = length × width

## The key ideas

- Measurement is comparing an unknown quantity with a unit.
- Length is the distance between two points; its SI unit is the metre.
- Area of a rectangle = length × width.

## Say it to your companion
Explain measuring length to your companion in your own words. Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
