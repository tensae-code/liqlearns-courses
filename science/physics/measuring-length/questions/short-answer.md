# Measuring length — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** What is measurement?
**Answer: Comparing an unknown quantity with a unit, using a chosen tool.**

**2.** What is the SI unit of length?
**Answer: The metre (m).**

**3.** A book cover is 0.30 m long and 0.20 m wide. Its area?
**Answer: 0.30 × 0.20 = 0.060 m². Working: multiply length by width.**

**4.** Which tool fits a blackboard's length best — ruler or tape?
**Answer: A tape.**
