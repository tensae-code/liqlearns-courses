# Integrated circuits — whole circuits on a chip — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about integrated circuits is correct?**
a) A moving-coil galvanometer detects tiny currents; a low-resistance shunt in parallel converts it into an ammeter, and a…  b) Whole circuits — diodes, transistors, resistors, capacitors — fabricated on one chip: small, cheap and reliable, but…  c) The teacher is told to support the class to conduct it properly and prepare the materials ahead of the lesson.
**Answer: b) Whole circuits — diodes, transistors, resistors, capacitors — fabricated on one chip: small, cheap and…**

**2. Whole circuits — diodes, ______s, resistors, capacitors — fabricated on one chip: small, cheap and reliable, but they cannot handle large currents or voltages and cannot be repaired, only replaced.**
a) voltage  b) transistor  c) junction
**Answer: b) transistor**
