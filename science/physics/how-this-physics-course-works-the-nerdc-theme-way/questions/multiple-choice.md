# How this Physics course works — the NERDC theme way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Nigeria's Physics course is organised by…
a) Apparatus, from the simplest instrument to the hardest  b) Six themes — the big ideas that keep returning  c) The alphabet  d) Examination years
**Answer: b) Six themes — the big ideas that keep returning**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Theme 3, Waves, carries its whole idea in its fuller title. That title is…
a) Motion with material transfer  b) Motion without material transfer  c) Motion at the speed of light  d) Motion in a straight line
**Answer: b) Motion without material transfer**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The human eye is taught inside Theme 3 because…
a) The eye is a musical instrument  b) Light is a wave, and the eye is the receiver of that wave  c) Eyes are measured in Theme 1 units  d) The eye conserves energy
**Answer: b) Light is a wave, and the eye is the receiver of that wave**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Musical instruments close the course inside…
a) Theme 1 — Interaction  b) Theme 4 — Fields  c) Theme 5 — Quantization  d) Theme 6 — Physics in technology
**Answer: d) Theme 6 — Physics in technology**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Performance objectives are…
a) The teacher's private notes  b) Plain statements of what the learner will be able to do by the end of a topic  c) The examination timetable  d) Marks given at the end of the year
**Answer: b) Plain statements of what the learner will be able to do by the end of a topic**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The first book carries no Theme 3 because…
a) The pages were lost in printing  b) The NERDC curriculum deals Waves to the second year, and the book says so openly  c) Waves are not physics  d) Theme 3 does not exist
**Answer: b) The NERDC curriculum deals Waves to the second year, and the book says so openly**
