# How this Physics course works — the NERDC theme way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the six NERDC themes in order.
**Answer: (1) Interaction of matter, space and time; (2) Conservation principles; (3) Waves — motion without material transfer; (4) Fields at rest and in motion; (5) Energy quantization and duality of matter; (6) Physics in technology.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the Fields theme teach "fields" as a concept before any particular field?
**Answer: Because every particular field — gravitational, electric — is one case of the same idea: an influence that reaches across space. Learning the concept first means each field that follows is recognised as family, not met as a stranger.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What is a scheme of work, and what does it deal out?
**Answer: The scheme of work is the plan that deals the year's topics out in order, theme by theme, with the time each topic is given — so the whole year's teaching is arranged before it begins.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Which theme does each of the first two books leave out, and why is that not a defect in the books?
**Answer: Book 1 leaves out Theme 3 (Waves) and Book 2 leaves out Theme 4 (Fields). It is not a defect because the curriculum deals the themes across the years — each book names its missing theme openly, and across the years all six are taught.**
