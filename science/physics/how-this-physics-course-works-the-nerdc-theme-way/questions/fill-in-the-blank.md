# How this Physics course works — the NERDC theme way — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The big ideas that group Nigeria's whole Physics course are called _____ .
**Answer: themes**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Theme 2, the one about quantities nature never loses, is _____ principles.
**Answer: Conservation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A ripple crosses a pond while the water stays behind: a wave is motion without _____ transfer.
**Answer: material**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The statements that tell a learner what they will be able to do at the end of a topic are its performance _____ .
**Answer: objectives**
