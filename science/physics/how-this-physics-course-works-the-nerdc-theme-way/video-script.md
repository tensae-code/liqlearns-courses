Today: **how Nigeria builds its whole Physics course — out of six big ideas**.

Most courses list topics one after another. Nigeria's course, set by the NERDC, groups everything under six **themes**. First, **interaction** — how matter, space and time meet, which is where motion lives. Then **conservation** — the quantities nature never loses, like energy and momentum. Then **waves** — motion that travels without any matter travelling. Then **fields** — gravity and electricity reaching across empty space. Then **quantization** — energy and matter coming in tiny grains, both wave and particle at once. And last, **Physics in technology** — the subject put to work, from lenses to musical instruments. Every topic you will ever meet in this course has a home in one of those six themes — and each one is taught against **performance objectives**: plain statements of what you will be able to *do*.
