# Hands-on — Internal energy and how it changes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two warmings, two roads
Road one (work): rub your palms together fast for fifteen seconds and feel the warmth — then rub slowly and compare. Road two (heat transfer): stand a cold metal spoon in a mug of warm water for a minute and feel the handle end warm with no rubbing at all. Write one line for each road naming what did the changing (a moving force, or contact with a hotter body) and one line for what is identical in the two endings (the particles of palms and spoon end moving faster). The pair of lines is §3 in your own hands.
