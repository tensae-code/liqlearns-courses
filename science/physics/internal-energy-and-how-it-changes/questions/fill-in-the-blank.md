# Internal energy and how it changes — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Internal energy counts the particles' motion and their mutual _____ — the potential part.
**Answer: interaction**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Do work _____ a body and its internal energy grows.
**Answer: on**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Energy passing from hotter to colder with no work done is called heat _____.
**Answer: transfer**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Internal energy belongs to the body's _____, not to the road taken to reach it.
**Answer: state**
