# Internal energy and how it changes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Internal energy is the sum of the particles'…
**Answer: a) kinetic energy of random motion and potential energy of interaction  b) heights above the floor  c) speeds in one direction  d) charges**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Warming a body raises its internal energy chiefly because its particles…
**Answer: a) move faster  b) grow bigger  c) become heavier  d) stop pulling on each other**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Rubbing your palms warms them. The internal energy grew by…
**Answer: a) work  b) heat transfer from a hotter body  c) radiation only  d) melting**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A gas expanding and pushing a piston outwards…
**Answer: a) does work, and its internal energy falls — it cools  b) gains internal energy  c) is unchanged  d) melts**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A cold spoon in hot tea warms by…
**Answer: a) heat transfer  b) friction  c) its own work  d) compression**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The same internal-energy change can be produced by work alone, heat transfer alone, or a mix, because internal energy belongs to…
**Answer: a) the body's state, not the road taken  b) the hotter body  c) the thermometer  d) the road taken**
