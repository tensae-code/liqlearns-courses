# Hands-on — Friction — the grip that resists

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Slide a book across a rough mat and then across a smooth table or tray. The same book, two journeys — the difference is friction.

## Apply it where you live
Explain how a car stuck in mud could be freed using friction ideas — and why spinning wheels fail.
