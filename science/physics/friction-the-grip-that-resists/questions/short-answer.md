# Friction — the grip that resists — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Why can you walk without slipping?
**Answer: Friction grips your shoe to the ground.**

**2.** Which surface gives more friction — ice or rough stone?
**Answer: Rough stone.**

**3.** Name one cost of friction.
**Answer: Wear of parts (or energy lost as heat, or getting stuck).**

**4.** Does friction push a moving box forward or oppose it?
**Answer: It opposes the motion.**
