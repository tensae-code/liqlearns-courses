# Friction — the grip that resists — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why can you walk without slipping?
a) Friction grips your shoe to the ground.  b) Rough stone.  c) Wear of parts (or energy lost as heat, or getting stuck).  d) It opposes the motion.
**Answer: a) Friction grips your shoe to the ground.**

**2.** Which surface gives more friction — ice or rough stone?
a) Friction grips your shoe to the ground.  b) Rough stone.  c) Wear of parts (or energy lost as heat, or getting stuck).  d) It opposes the motion.
**Answer: b) Rough stone.**

**3.** Name one cost of friction.
a) Friction grips your shoe to the ground.  b) Rough stone.  c) Wear of parts (or energy lost as heat, or getting stuck).  d) It opposes the motion.
**Answer: c) Wear of parts (or energy lost as heat, or getting stuck).**

**4.** Does friction push a moving box forward or oppose it?
a) Friction grips your shoe to the ground.  b) Rough stone.  c) Wear of parts (or energy lost as heat, or getting stuck).  d) It opposes the motion.
**Answer: d) It opposes the motion.**
