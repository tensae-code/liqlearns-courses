# Video script — Friction — the grip that resists

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Friction is the surface resistance that opposes motion — or attempted motion — where two surfaces touch.
It grows with roughness and shrinks on smooth, slippery surfaces.
Friction is both friend and cost: it lets you walk without slipping and gives vehicles their grip, yet it wears parts down and can mire a car in mud, where the wheels find too little useful grip to bite with.

Remember: **Friction opposes motion where surfaces touch.**
Remember: **Rougher surfaces give more friction; smooth ones less.**
Remember: **Friction is useful (walking, grip) and costly (wear, stuck in mud).**
