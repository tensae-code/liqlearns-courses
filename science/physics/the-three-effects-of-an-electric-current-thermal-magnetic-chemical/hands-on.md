# Hands-on — The three effects of an electric current — thermal, magnetic, chemical

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

["(1) Thermal: run a current from a battery through a short length of nichrome wire (teacher-supervised) and feel the warmth grow with the current. (2) Magnetic: lay a compass beside a straight wire, switch the current on and off, and record the needle's swing and its reversal when the current reverses. (3) Chemical: electrolyse water with two inverted tubes over electrodes (teacher-led) and compare the two gas volumes collected."]
