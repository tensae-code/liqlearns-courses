# The three effects of an electric current — thermal, magnetic, chemical — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The thermal effect of a current is also called the…
**Answer: a) Joule effect  b) Oersted effect  c) photoelectric effect  d) Seebeck effect**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A fuse protects a circuit by using…
**Answer: a) the thermal effect — it melts when current is too large  b) the magnetic effect  c) electrolysis  d) induction**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Oersted's 1820 discovery was that a current…
**Answer: a) creates a magnetic field that swings a compass needle  b) heats any wire  c) splits water  d) makes light**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An electromagnet is made stronger by…
**Answer: a) coiling the wire and adding an iron core  b) straightening the wire  c) removing the current  d) using plastic wire**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Electrolysis of water produces…
**Answer: a) hydrogen and oxygen  b) hydrogen only  c) steam only  d) ozone and hydrogen**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Gold-plating a ring uses which effect?
**Answer: a) the chemical effect  b) the thermal effect  c) the magnetic effect  d) none — plating is mechanical**
