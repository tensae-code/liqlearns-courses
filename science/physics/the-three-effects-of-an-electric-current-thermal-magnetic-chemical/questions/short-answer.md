# The three effects of an electric current — thermal, magnetic, chemical — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give one useful appliance and one protective device that both rest on the thermal effect.
**Answer: Useful: the toaster/iron/radiator — a resistance made to warm. Protective: the fuse — a wire chosen to melt by the same Joule heating when the current exceeds its rating.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Describe Oersted's experiment and what it founded.
**Answer: A compass needle placed beside a current-carrying wire swings when the current flows (and reverses when the current reverses): a current creates a magnetic field. The observation founded electromagnetism.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In water electrolysis the hydrogen volume is twice the oxygen volume. Which electrode collects the hydrogen?
**Answer: The cathode (the negative electrode) collects hydrogen — twice the volume of the oxygen collected at the anode.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why do the Mauritanian literary manuals teach the three effects as one chapter?
**Answer: Because they are the three signatures of a single thing — wherever a current flows it can be detected and used by its heat, its magnetism or its chemistry; the chapter's frame (Experience → Explanation → Applications for each) makes the unity visible.**
