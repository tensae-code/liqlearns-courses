# The three effects of an electric current — thermal, magnetic, chemical — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The heating of a wire by a current is the _____ effect.
**Answer: Joule (thermal)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A _____ melts by the thermal effect to break an over-large current.
**Answer: fuse**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) _____ discovered the magnetic effect of a current in 1820.
**Answer: Oersted**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Splitting a compound with a current is called _____ .
**Answer: electrolysis**
