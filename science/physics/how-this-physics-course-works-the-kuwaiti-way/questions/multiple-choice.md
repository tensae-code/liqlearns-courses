# How this Physics course works — the Kuwaiti way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The objectives at a unit's opening are best used as…
a) A decoration to skip  b) A checklist you tick only when you can do each thing unaided  c) The answers to the review  d) A summary to memorise word for word
**Answer: b) A checklist you tick only when you can do each thing unaided**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) An objective you cannot yet do tells you…
a) That the unit is too hard  b) Exactly which lesson to revisit  c) That you should skip the unit  d) Nothing useful
**Answer: b) Exactly which lesson to revisit**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The "discover it yourself" box comes…
a) After the review questions  b) Before the explanation, so you try the phenomenon first  c) Only in the workbook  d) Instead of the lessons
**Answer: b) Before the explanation, so you try the phenomenon first**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A wrong first guess in the inquiry box is…
a) A failure to erase  b) Useful — it is the thing the lesson then corrects  c) A reason to stop  d) Not allowed
**Answer: b) Useful — it is the thing the lesson then corrects**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) "Physics in the lab" sidebars point to…
a) Real devices in industry  b) The practical experiment that belongs with the lesson  c) Historical biographies  d) Exam dates
**Answer: b) The practical experiment that belongs with the lesson**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) "Physics and technology" sidebars point to…
a) Where the same idea works in real devices and the world  b) How to build the lab kit  c) The unit's objectives  d) Safety rules only
**Answer: a) Where the same idea works in real devices and the world**
