# How this Physics course works — the Kuwaiti way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why read the objectives before starting a unit?
**Answer: They state exactly what you will be able to do at the end, so they set the target for your work and later tell you what to revisit.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What should you write down when you meet a "discover it yourself" box?
**Answer: What you think will happen — your own prediction or first result — so the coming explanation can be compared with it.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Give one "Physics and technology" example named in the first Kuwaiti unit.
**Answer: Any one of: physics in medicine, wind turbines, solar cells, satellites.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) What closes a Kuwaiti unit, after the lessons?
**Answer: A sustainable-development feature and the unit review questions.**
