# Video script — How this Physics course works — the Kuwaiti way

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **how a Kuwaiti physics unit is built**. First the objectives — promises you tick off only when you can really do them. Then "discover it yourself": try the thing before anyone explains it, and keep your first guess, because the lesson is its correction. Alongside, two sidebars: "Physics in the lab" for the experiment, "Physics and technology" for the device in the real world. Read the opening page properly and the whole unit becomes a map you drew yourself.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
