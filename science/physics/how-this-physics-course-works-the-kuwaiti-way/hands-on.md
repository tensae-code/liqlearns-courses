# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Kuwaiti way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Use an opening page like a map
Take any textbook unit opening (this course's books, or any school book you have). Copy its objectives into a notebook as a checklist with empty boxes. Work one lesson, then return and tick only what you can demonstrate — do a ticked objective once, out loud or on paper, to prove the tick is honest. Keep the unticked boxes as your revision list for the week.
