# Hands-on — Radiation and living things — protection

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Distance quarters the dose
Use a torch as your 'source' and a phone light-meter app (or judge by eye on white paper) as the detector. Measure brightness at 0.5 m, 1 m and 2 m from the torch and tabulate: each doubling of distance quarters the reading. Light is not ionising radiation, but the inverse-square geometry is the same — write the rule and one job where tongs are 'distance in disguise'.
