# Radiation and living things — protection — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Radiation that knocks electrons off atoms is called _____ radiation.
**Answer: ionising**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Everyone receives a few millisieverts a year from _____ radiation.
**Answer: background**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) No dose without a _____ — ALARA's first demand.
**Answer: reason**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Intensity falls with the _____ of the distance.
**Answer: square**
