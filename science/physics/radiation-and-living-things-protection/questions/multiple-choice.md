# Radiation and living things — protection — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ionising radiation harms tissue by…
**Answer: a) knocking electrons from atoms and breaking molecules, DNA included  b) heating it like an oven  c) shaking cells apart mechanically  d) blocking the blood**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A cell meeting a small radiation dose usually…
**Answer: a) repairs the damage  b) dies at once  c) turns into another cell  d) glows**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Dose in protection work is counted in…
**Answer: a) sieverts  b) newtons  c) ohms  d) candelas**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) ALARA means keeping every dose…
**Answer: a) as low as reasonably achievable  b) at zero, whatever the cost  c) secret  d) equal for everyone**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Doubling your distance from a point source cuts the intensity to…
**Answer: a) one quarter  b) one half  c) one eighth  d) zero**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The right shielding for gamma rays is…
**Answer: a) lead (dense matter)  b) a sheet of paper  c) a magnet  d) coloured glass**
