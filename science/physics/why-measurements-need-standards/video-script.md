# Video script — Why measurements need standards — the SI base quantities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Local and traditional measures vary from place to place: six pupils in different parts of Ethiopia measuring the same object with local measures get different values.
So measurement needs standards — measures that are accessible, reliable, and give the same result anywhere.
The world standard system is SI, with seven base quantities: length, mass, time, temperature, electric current, amount of substance and luminous intensity.
The metre is defined by how far light travels in a set time, the kilogram through the Planck constant, and the second by caesium-133 vibrations.

Remember: **A standard must be accessible, reliable, and give the same result anywhere.**
