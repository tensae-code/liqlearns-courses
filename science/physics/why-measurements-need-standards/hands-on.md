# Hands-on — Why measurements need standards — the SI base quantities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Measure the same table in hand-spans with a friend. Compare numbers. You have just felt why the world needed one standard.

## Apply it where you live
Measure one object with a local measure and with an SI unit; write both and say which a stranger could trust.
