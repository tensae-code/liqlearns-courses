# Viscous flow and Stokes' law — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — a viscous liquid resists flowing — its layers drag on each other, fastest at a pipe's centre and slowest at its walls — and a small sphere falling through it is held back by Stokes' drag, 6πηrv, until it sinks at a steady terminal speed the Kazakh laboratory measures — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
