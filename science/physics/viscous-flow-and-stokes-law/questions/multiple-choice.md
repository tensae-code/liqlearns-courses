# Viscous flow and Stokes' law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a pipe, a viscous liquid moves fastest…
a) At the walls  b) At the centre  c) Everywhere equally  d) Only at bends
**Answer: b) At the centre**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Viscosity is…
a) A liquid's density  b) Friction between a liquid's layers  c) A liquid's colour  d) Pressure in a liquid
**Answer: b) Friction between a liquid's layers**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Stokes' drag on a sphere is F =…
a) 6πηrv  b) ½ρv²  c) mgh  d) πr²ρ
**Answer: a) 6πηrv**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A falling ball reaches terminal velocity when…
a) Its weight vanishes  b) Weight = upthrust + drag  c) Drag vanishes  d) It reaches the bottom
**Answer: b) Weight = upthrust + drag**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In Lab 5, balls of larger radius falling through the same liquid…
a) Sink slower  b) Sink at the same speed  c) Sink faster (once falling steadily)  d) Float
**Answer: c) Sink faster (once falling steadily)**
