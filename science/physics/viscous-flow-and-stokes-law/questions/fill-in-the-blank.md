# Viscous flow and Stokes' law — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A body shaped so flow closes smoothly behind it is called ___.
**Answer:** streamlined

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Torricelli's formula gives the speed of an ideal liquid leaving a hole as v = √(___).
**Answer:** 2gh

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In Stokes' law the symbol η stands for the liquid's ___.
**Answer:** viscosity

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The steady speed a falling ball settles at is called its ___ velocity.
**Answer:** terminal

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Before timing a falling ball you check it falls equal distances in equal ___.
**Answer:** times
