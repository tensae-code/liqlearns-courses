# Hands-on — When waves meet — superposition and standing waves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Two people shake a rope from opposite ends with the same rhythm. Watch the pattern settle into place — big swing spots and still spots you can point to.

## Apply it where you live
Sketch a standing wave and mark two nodes and two antinodes with the λ/4 distance.
