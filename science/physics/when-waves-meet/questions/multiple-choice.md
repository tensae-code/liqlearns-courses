# When waves meet — superposition and standing waves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Two identical crests meet in phase. Result?
a) A crest twice as high — constructive interference.  b) They cancel — destructive interference.  c) A point that never moves.  d) A quarter of a wavelength (λ/4).
**Answer: a) A crest twice as high — constructive interference.**

**2.** A crest meets an equal trough. Result?
a) A crest twice as high — constructive interference.  b) They cancel — destructive interference.  c) A point that never moves.  d) A quarter of a wavelength (λ/4).
**Answer: b) They cancel — destructive interference.**

**3.** In a standing wave, what is a node?
a) A crest twice as high — constructive interference.  b) They cancel — destructive interference.  c) A point that never moves.  d) A quarter of a wavelength (λ/4).
**Answer: c) A point that never moves.**

**4.** How far is a node from the next antinode?
a) A crest twice as high — constructive interference.  b) They cancel — destructive interference.  c) A point that never moves.  d) A quarter of a wavelength (λ/4).
**Answer: d) A quarter of a wavelength (λ/4).**
