# When waves meet — superposition and standing waves — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Two identical crests meet in phase. Result?
**Answer: A crest twice as high — constructive interference.**

**2.** A crest meets an equal trough. Result?
**Answer: They cancel — destructive interference.**

**3.** In a standing wave, what is a node?
**Answer: A point that never moves.**

**4.** How far is a node from the next antinode?
**Answer: A quarter of a wavelength (λ/4).**
