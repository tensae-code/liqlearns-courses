# Build your own radio receiver — the Armenian Grade 12 lab — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The tuning circuit of the simplest receiver is made of…
a) A battery and a lamp  b) A coil and a variable capacitor (or sliding contact) — an LC circuit  c) Two earphones  d) A magnet and a needle
**Answer: b) A coil and a variable capacitor (or sliding contact) — an LC circuit**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The detector's job is to…
a) Amplify the signal  b) Strip the sound (audio) from the high-frequency carrier  c) Catch the wave  d) Power the set
**Answer: b) Strip the sound (audio) from the high-frequency carrier**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The simplest receiver works without a battery because…
a) It stores sunlight  b) The energy it uses arrives with the radio wave itself  c) Earphones make electricity  d) It is wound very tightly
**Answer: b) The energy it uses arrives with the radio wave itself**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A station is found when the tuning circuit…
a) Is disconnected  b) Resonates at that station's carrier frequency  c) Is earthed twice  d) Gets warm
**Answer: b) Resonates at that station's carrier frequency**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A longer aerial and a good earth connection matter because…
a) They look professional  b) The received energy is tiny — the aerial must catch as much of the wave as possible  c) The diode demands it  d) Regulations require it
**Answer: b) The received energy is tiny — the aerial must catch as much of the wave as possible**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) If the assembled set is silent, the first things to check are…
a) The weather  b) The chain of connections — aerial, earth, detector direction, earphone — joint by joint  c) The colour of the wire  d) Nothing; silence is normal
**Answer: b) The chain of connections — aerial, earth, detector direction, earphone — joint by joint**
