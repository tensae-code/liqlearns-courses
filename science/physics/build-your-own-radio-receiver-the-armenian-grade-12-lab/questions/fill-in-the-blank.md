# Build your own radio receiver — the Armenian Grade 12 lab — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The simplest receiver's detector was historically a ___ (crystal) or a diode.
**Answer:** crystal

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) Tuning works by ___: the LC circuit answers strongly at one frequency.
**Answer:** resonance

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The wire that catches the wave is the ___.
**Answer:** aerial (antenna)

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) With no battery, the earphone's energy arrives with the ___ itself.
**Answer:** radio wave (wave)
