# Build your own radio receiver — the Armenian Grade 12 lab — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Trace a station's journey through the four parts of the receiver.
**Answer:** Aerial catches all stations' waves together → tuning circuit resonates to one carrier and selects it → detector strips the audio pattern from the carrier → earphone turns the varying audio current back into sound.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) Why is the sound in a crystal receiver faint even at best?
**Answer:** Its only energy source is the fraction of the passing radio wave the aerial intercepts — there is no battery or amplifier adding energy, so the earphone gets microwatts and must be sensitive and close to the ear.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The tuning contact is slid along the coil. What quantity changes, and how does the station change?
**Answer:** Sliding changes the number of turns in use, hence the inductance L, hence the resonant frequency f = 1/(2π√(LC)) — the set resonates with a different carrier, and a different station is heard.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) What does this lab teach that the chapter's reading alone cannot?
**Answer:** That a circuit is physical: joints fail, earths matter, components have a right way round — the theory is proved by a working object the pupil assembled and debugged themselves.
