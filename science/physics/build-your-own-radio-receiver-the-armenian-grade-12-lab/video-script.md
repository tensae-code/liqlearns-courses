# Video script — Build your own radio receiver — the Armenian Grade 12 lab

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Armenia's Grade 12 pupils do not only read about radio: their first laboratory work of the year is to assemble the simplest receiver themselves — aerial, tuning coil, detector and earphone — and hear a real station in a circuit with no battery, powered by the radio wave itself**. The simplest receiver (the crystal set) has one part per job. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — the parts — four jobs, four components; assembling it — winding, joining, earthing; tuning and testing — finding the station — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
