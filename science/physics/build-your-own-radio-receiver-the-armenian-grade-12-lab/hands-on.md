# Hands-on — Build your own radio receiver — the Armenian Grade 12 lab

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with your hands
This lesson IS the laboratory work: build the receiver as the book directs (coil of enamelled wire on a cardboard former, diode detector, high-impedance earphone or crystal earpiece, long wire aerial, earth to a water pipe or buried metal). Work the tuning slowly in the evening, when distant stations strengthen. Log each station found and the tuner setting at which it appeared — your log is the lab report.
