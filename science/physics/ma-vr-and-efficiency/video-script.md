# Video script — MA, VR and efficiency — measuring a machine

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Three numbers measure any machine.
Mechanical advantage, MA = load ÷ effort: how many times the machine multiplies your force — in a real machine friction makes the actual MA smaller than the ideal.
Velocity ratio, VR = distance moved by effort ÷ distance moved by load: fixed by the machine's geometry.
Efficiency η = work output ÷ work input, and it also equals MA ÷ VR.
All three are unitless ratios.
An efficiency under 100% is not failure — it is friction's honest tax.

Remember: **MA = load ÷ effort; VR = effort distance ÷ load distance.**
Remember: **Efficiency η = work output ÷ work input = MA ÷ VR.**
Remember: **Friction makes actual MA less than ideal and efficiency less than 100%.**
Keep the formula: **MA = load / effort; VR = effort distance / load distance; η = MA / VR**
