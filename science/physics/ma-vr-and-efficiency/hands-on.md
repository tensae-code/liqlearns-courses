# Hands-on — MA, VR and efficiency — measuring a machine

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Lift a book by hand (effort = its weight), then with a lever. You cannot feel MA as a number yet — but you can feel the trade the numbers describe.

## Apply it where you live
Measure a real lever's MA and VR with weights and a tape, then compute its efficiency.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21841 — machine diagram with load, effort and both travel distances labelled] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using MA = load / effort; VR = effort distance / load distance; η = MA / VR: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
