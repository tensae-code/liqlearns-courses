# MA, VR and efficiency — measuring a machine — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A machine lifts a 600 N load with 150 N of effort. MA?
**Answer: MA = 600 ÷ 150 = 4.**

**2.** The effort moves 2 m while the load rises 0.5 m. VR?
**Answer: VR = 2 ÷ 0.5 = 4.**

**3.** A machine has MA 3 and VR 4. Efficiency?
**Answer: η = MA/VR = 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75 = 75%.**

**4.** Work input 500 J, work output 400 J. Efficiency?
**Answer: 400 ÷ 500 = 80%.**
