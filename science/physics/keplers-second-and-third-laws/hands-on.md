# Hands-on — Kepler's second and third laws

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two balls, one moment
With a partner: at the same moment, one of you drops a ball straight down and the other throws an identical ball sideways. Watch: they land together. Say why, component by component.

## See it and say it
Study this lesson's picture — two equal-area slices of one orbit, one long and thin, one short and fat — and name every part of it out loud. Then close the picture and draw it from memory; check your drawing against the lesson.

## Teach it back
Explain this lesson to someone at home using one thing in the room as your example. Their questions show you which part to re-read.
