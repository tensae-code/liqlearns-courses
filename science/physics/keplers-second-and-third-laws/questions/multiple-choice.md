# Kepler's second and third laws — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about kepler's second and third laws is correct?**
a) Equal areas in equal times (planets speed up near the Sun).  b) Joining P and N material makes a one-way device; its symbol and the depletion region at the junction are introduced.  c) Two cans hang from a beam, one filled with sand, one empty; pupils give each a slight push to start it swinging, then…
**Answer: a) Equal areas in equal times (planets speed up near the Sun).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Which statement about kepler's second and third laws is correct?**
a) Group activities interrogate both laws.  b) A charge fills its surroundings with a field; field strength is force per unit positive charge, and field lines picture…  c) Lenz's law gives its direction — the induced effect opposes the change causing it, as energy conservation demands.
**Answer: a) Group activities interrogate both laws.**
