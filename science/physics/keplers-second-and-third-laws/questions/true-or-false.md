# Kepler's second and third laws — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Equal areas in equal times (planets speed up near the Sun).**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) unequal areas in equal times (planets speed up near the Sun).**
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) And T² ∝ R³ with a printed data table of planets checking the ratio.**
