# Hands-on — The thin lens formula and magnification

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Finding f, then pricing an image with the formula
Use a magnifying glass as your convex lens and a sunlit window as a distant object. Hold a sheet of paper behind the lens and slide it until the window's image is sharpest and smallest: the lens-to-paper distance is (very nearly) the focal length f, because a distant object images at the focus. Suppose you measure f = 10 cm. Now stand a book 30 cm from the lens on a table (u = 30 cm), predict the image distance with 1/v = 1/f − 1/u (you should get v = 15 cm) and the magnification (m = 0.5). Then hunt with the paper screen: the image appears where the formula said, upside down and half size. Say aloud: the formula named the place before the screen found it.
