# The thin lens formula and magnification — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In 1/f = 1/u + 1/v, the letter u stands for the _____ distance.
**Answer: object**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A virtual image takes a _____ sign for v in the real-is-positive convention.
**Answer: negative**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) An object at 2f forms its image at _____, the same size as the object.
**Answer: 2f**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A camera's image is real, inverted and _____ — which is why it fits on a small sensor.
**Answer: diminished**
