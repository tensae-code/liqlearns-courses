# The thin lens formula and magnification — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An object is placed 30 cm from a convex lens of focal length 10 cm. Find v and describe the image.
**Answer: Answer: 1/v = 1/10 − 1/30 = 2/30 = 1/15, so v = 15 cm. Positive v, so the image is real and inverted; m = 15/30 = 0.5, so it is diminished — the camera case.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why must the sign convention be stated before the lens formula is used?
**Answer: Answer: The formula's answers are read through the signs: a negative v means a virtual image on the object's side, and a concave lens must enter with a negative f. Without the convention (real-is-positive here), the same arithmetic could be read as the wrong kind of image in the wrong place.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) An object 4 cm tall stands 12 cm from a convex lens of focal length 8 cm. Find the image height.
**Answer: Answer: 1/v = 1/8 − 1/12 = 1/24, so v = 24 cm; m = v/u = 24/12 = 2; image height = 2 × 4 cm = 8 cm (real and inverted).**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A learner puts an object exactly at the focal point of a convex lens and finds no image on the screen at any distance. Explain with the formula.
**Answer: Answer: With u = f, 1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 0, so v is infinite: the rays leave the lens parallel and meet 'at infinity' — no finite screen position can catch the image.**
