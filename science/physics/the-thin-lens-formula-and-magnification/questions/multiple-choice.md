# The thin lens formula and magnification — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The thin lens formula is…
a) f = u + v  b) 1/f = 1/u + 1/v  c) 1/f = 1/u − 1/v  d) f = 1/(uv)
**Answer: b) 1/f = 1/u + 1/v**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Under the real-is-positive convention, a concave lens has…
a) Positive f  b) Zero f  c) Negative f  d) Infinite f
**Answer: c) Negative f**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Magnification is given by…
a) m = u/v  b) m = v/u  c) m = u × v  d) m = f/u
**Answer: b) m = v/u**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An image with m = 0.25 is…
a) Enlarged and upright  b) Diminished — a quarter of the object's size  c) Same size  d) Virtual, always
**Answer: b) Diminished — a quarter of the object's size**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A convex lens (f = 10 cm) has an object at u = 15 cm. The image distance is…
a) 6 cm  b) 30 cm  c) 25 cm  d) 150 cm
**Answer: b) 30 cm**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) For the lens of the previous question, the image is…
a) Real, inverted and enlarged (m = 2)  b) Virtual, upright and enlarged  c) Real, inverted and diminished  d) At the focus
**Answer: a) Real, inverted and enlarged (m = 2)**
