Today: **the thin lens formula — one equation, every lens**. A lens makes an image at a place you can calculate. Measure u, the object distance, and f, the focal length, and the image distance v is fixed by one equation: 1/f = 1/u + 1/v. Real distances count positive — that is the real-is-positive rule — and a concave lens carries a negative f. Then the size: magnification is v divided by u, the same number as image height over object height. Object far, image near and small — a camera. Object near the focus, image far and large — a projector. Two formulas, and every lens in the lesson is priced.
