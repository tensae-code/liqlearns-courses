# Photons and the photoelectric effect — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner states the quantum hypothesis and E = hf, describes the photoelectric effect and its three wave-defying facts (threshold frequency, instant ejection, intensity counting electrons not speeds), and keeps the energy account with Einstein's equation hf = φ + KE_max in worked numbers, answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — doing the lesson's own experiments and sorts by hand, naming each idea aloud, and reasoning about new examples until the teaching is the learner's own.
