# Hands-on — Photons and the photoelectric effect

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The zinc plate test
If your school has the classic kit: charge an electroscope negatively with a clean zinc plate on top. Shine ordinary torch light on the plate — the leaf stands. Shine ultraviolet light — the leaf falls as electrons are knocked off the zinc. Say aloud: the dim lamp beat the bright one, because each of its photons carried more.

## Coins and the price of escape
Lay counters on the table as electrons and give the work function a price — say 3 tokens. 'Red photons' carry 2 tokens, 'ultraviolet photons' carry 5. Fire photons one at a time and pay out electrons: the 2-token photons never free one, however many you fire; each 5-token photon frees one and leaves 2 tokens of speed. Say aloud: one photon, one electron, one account.
