# Photons and the photoelectric effect — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The energy of one photon is…
a) Set by the brightness of the light  b) E = hf — set by its frequency  c) The same for all colours  d) Measured in newtons
**Answer: b) E = hf — set by its frequency**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Making a lamp brighter increases…
a) The energy of each photon  b) The frequency of the light  c) The number of photons per second  d) The work function
**Answer: c) The number of photons per second**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Below a metal's threshold frequency, electrons are ejected…
a) Slowly, after a long wait  b) Only if the light is very bright  c) Never, however bright the light  d) With very low speeds
**Answer: c) Never, however bright the light**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Ejection begins the instant the light arrives because…
a) The metal was already hot  b) Each electron takes its energy from one photon in a single event  c) Waves travel fast  d) The surface is polished
**Answer: b) Each electron takes its energy from one photon in a single event**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Photons of 5.0 eV strike a metal of work function 2.0 eV. KE_max of the ejected electrons is…
a) 7.0 eV  b) 2.0 eV  c) 5.0 eV  d) 3.0 eV
**Answer: d) 3.0 eV**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Doubling the frequency of the light (staying above threshold) makes the fastest ejected electrons…
a) Twice as numerous at the same speed  b) Faster, because each photon pays more over the same work function  c) Slower  d) Stop entirely
**Answer: b) Faster, because each photon pays more over the same work function**
