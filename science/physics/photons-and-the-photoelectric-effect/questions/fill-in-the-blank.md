# Photons and the photoelectric effect — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) One packet of light is called a _____.
**Answer: photon**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In E = hf, the constant h is named after _____.
**Answer: Planck**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The lowest frequency that can eject electrons from a metal is its _____ frequency.
**Answer: threshold**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The minimum energy needed to free an electron from a metal is the _____ function.
**Answer: work**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In Einstein's equation, hf = φ + KE_max, the energy left after paying the work function becomes the electron's _____ energy.
**Answer: kinetic**
