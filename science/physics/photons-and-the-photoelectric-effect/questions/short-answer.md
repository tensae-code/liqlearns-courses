# Photons and the photoelectric effect — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State the quantum hypothesis and who proposed it, and say what Einstein added.
**Answer: Planck proposed that energy is exchanged in packets (quanta) of size E = hf. Einstein applied it to light itself: a light beam is a stream of photons, each carrying hf.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the three facts about the photoelectric effect that the wave picture cannot explain.
**Answer: A threshold frequency below which nothing is ejected however bright the light; ejection that is instant, with no build-up time; and brightness raising the number of electrons ejected but never their maximum speed.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A metal's work function is 2.3 eV. Photons of 3.1 eV strike it. Find KE_max, and state what photons of exactly 2.3 eV would do.
**Answer: KE_max = hf − φ = 3.1 − 2.3 = 0.8 eV. Photons of exactly 2.3 eV just pay the work function: electrons escape with (almost) zero kinetic energy — this is the threshold.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Name one device that works by the photoelectric effect and say what it does.
**Answer: Any one of: a photocell / light meter — light frees electrons, and the resulting current measures the light; a solar cell — photons free charge carriers that are gathered as electric current; automatic-door sensors and burglar alarms that respond when a photoelectric beam is broken.**
