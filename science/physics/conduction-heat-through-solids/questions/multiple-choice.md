# Conduction — heat through solids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about conduction is correct?**
a) Pressure applied to an enclosed fluid transmits undiminished: the hydraulic press multiplies force by piston areas…  b) Heat travels through a solid by particle vibration passing energy along; the rate depends on area, thickness,…  c) Without friction, KE + PE stays constant: problems follow blocks on frictionless tracks and inclines and a diver/swing…
**Answer: b) Heat travels through a solid by particle vibration passing energy along; the rate depends on area, thickness,…**

**2. Heat travels through a solid by particle vibration passing energy along; the rate depends on area, thickness, ______ difference and the material (thermal conductivity) — metals fast, wood/air slow (insulators).**
a) electron  b) temperature  c) voltage
**Answer: b) temperature**
