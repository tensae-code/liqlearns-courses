# Projectiles launched at an angle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about angled (inclined) projectile motion is correct?**
a) Even at constant speed the velocity's direction changes, so the object accelerates toward the centre; the acceleration…  b) Launched at an angle, the velocity splits into components; at the top the vertical component is momentarily zero while…  c) A bullet embeds in a suspended wooden block; momentum conservation through the hit plus energy conservation through the…
**Answer: b) Launched at an angle, the velocity splits into components; at the top the vertical component is momentarily…**

**2. Launched at an angle, the velocity splits into components; at the top the vertical component is momentarily zero while the horizontal one continues; ______s are tracked component by component.**
a) energy  b) displacement  c) gravity
**Answer: b) displacement**
