# Video script — The nuclear industry — power stations and other uses

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the nuclear industry**. In the power station, controlled fission is the fire under an ordinary steam chain — reactor, turbine, generator — burning fuel so dense a year of it fits in trucks. Beyond electricity, radioisotopes trace, image, treat and measure in hospitals, farms and factories. And the lesson opens with the debate: density and no carbon, against waste that outlives us and safety that must never fail.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
