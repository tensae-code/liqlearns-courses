# The nuclear industry — power stations and other uses — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the station's chain, the reactor's heat boils water to make _____, which drives the turbine.
**Answer: steam**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) _____ rods absorb neutrons to control the chain reaction.
**Answer: Control**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A _____ is a radioactive substance followed through the body or a plant by its radiation.
**Answer: tracer**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Nuclear waste must be isolated for thousands of years because of the long _____ of some isotopes in it.
**Answer: half-lives**
