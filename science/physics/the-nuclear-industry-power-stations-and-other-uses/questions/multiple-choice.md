# The nuclear industry — power stations and other uses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a nuclear power station the correct order of the energy chain is…
**Answer: a) reactor → steam → turbine → generator  b) generator → turbine → reactor  c) turbine → reactor → generator  d) reactor → generator → turbine**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Control rods in a reactor…
**Answer: a) absorb neutrons to hold the chain reaction at a steady rate  b) burn as extra fuel  c) cool the turbine  d) make the steam**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A radioactive tracer in medicine works because it…
**Answer: a) can be detected in tiny amounts from outside the body, showing where it travels  b) cures by its heat  c) is invisible to detectors  d) has an infinite half-life**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) PET/CT imaging and radiotherapy appear in this lesson as…
**Answer: a) branches of the nuclear industry beyond electricity  b) kinds of reactor  c) waste products  d) reactor fuels**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The strongest burden of nuclear power, in physical terms, is…
**Answer: a) radioactive waste that must be isolated for thousands of years  b) the stations are noisy  c) the steam is wet  d) the fuel is bulky**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Compared with a coal station, a nuclear station in operation…
**Answer: a) produces no carbon dioxide from generation and needs vastly less fuel mass  b) needs more coal  c) has no turbine  d) cannot run at night**
