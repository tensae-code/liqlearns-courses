# Hands-on — The nuclear industry — power stations and other uses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One chain, four fires
Draw the energy chain of a steam power station as five boxes: FIRE → HEAT → STEAM → TURBINE → GENERATOR → ELECTRICITY. Under it, write four ways to fill the FIRE box (coal, gas, uranium fission, concentrated sunlight) and, for each, one line: what the fuel costs in transport (a year's supply in trains, trucks or mirrors). The diagram shows why engineers call the nuclear station 'a kettle with a nuclear fire' — and why its debate is about the fire, never about the kettle.
