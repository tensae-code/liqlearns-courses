# Ocean currents and water density — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Dissolved salt makes water _____ than fresh water at the same temperature.
**Answer: denser**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) An instrument that floats at different depths to measure a liquid's density is a _____.
**Answer: hydrometer**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In thermohaline circulation, haline refers to _____.
**Answer: salt**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Cold, dense water spreads along the ocean _____ after sinking.
**Answer: floor**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Deep water rising to the surface is called _____.
**Answer: upwelling**
