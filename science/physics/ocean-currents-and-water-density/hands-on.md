# Hands-on — Ocean currents and water density

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The egg and the brine
Float an egg in fresh water and note that it sinks. Add salt a spoon at a time, stirring gently, until the egg floats mid-water or at the surface. Say aloud: the egg did not change — the water's density rose past the egg's density, and floating is a density contest.

## Layered water
Half-fill a clear glass with cold water tinted with a drop of food colouring. Pour warm, untinted water very slowly down the inside of the tilted glass. Watch the warm layer sit on top of the cold layer instead of mixing at once. Say aloud: warm water is lighter, so it floats — the ocean layers the same way.
