Today: **ocean currents and water density**.

Drop an egg in fresh water and it sinks; salt the water and it rises. The egg never changed — the water's density did. Warm water is lighter and floats; cold, salty water is heavier and sinks to the ocean floor and creeps away, while warm water spreads across the top. That endless sinking and spreading is a current — one that carries heat to cold coasts and brings deep, rich water up to feed the fish.
