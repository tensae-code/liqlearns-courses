**2. P = F/A with the sharp-pencil-on-fingers demonstration; a worked example finds the ______ under a 55 kg person's shoe (300 cm² sole).**

**Answer: a) pressure**

**3. Pressure units are converted among ______, atmosphere, mmHg, torr and psi.**
