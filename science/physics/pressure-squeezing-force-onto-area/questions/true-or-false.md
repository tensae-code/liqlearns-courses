# Pressure — squeezing force onto area — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. P = F/A with the sharp-pencil-on-fingers demonstration; a worked example finds the pressure under a 55 kg person's shoe (300 cm² sole).**
**Answer: True**

**2. P = F/A with the sharp-pencil-on-fingers demonstration; a worked example finds the pressure under a 110 kg person's shoe (300 cm² sole).**
**Answer: False**

**3. Pressure units are converted among pascal, atmosphere, mmHg, torr and psi.**
**Answer: True**
