# How this physics course works — the Jordanian Grade 9 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In Jordan's Grade 9 book, every unit opens with…
**Answer: a) an introductory experiment  b) a unit review  c) a glossary  d) an exam**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The introductory experiment comes…
**Answer: a) before any theory  b) after the unit review  c) only in Term 2  d) in the teacher guide only**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) English physics terms in the book are…
**Answer: a) glossed beside the Arabic at first use  b) never used  c) listed only at the back  d) left untranslated**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The general-idea page states…
**Answer: a) the unit's big idea in plain words  b) the exam marks  c) the answers  d) the glossary**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The 'Physics and life' spread…
**Answer: a) applies the unit to daily life  b) repeats the glossary  c) is the unit review  d) is written in English only**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Each unit closes with…
**Answer: a) a unit review and a Physics-and-life spread  b) a new experiment only  c) the next unit's opener  d) nothing**
