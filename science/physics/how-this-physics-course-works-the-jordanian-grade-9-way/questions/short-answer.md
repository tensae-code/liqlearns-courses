# How this physics course works — the Jordanian Grade 9 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does the Jordanian pattern put the experiment before the theory?
**Answer: Because the theory then explains something the learner has already seen and done — the explanation is the story of the learner's own activity, not words about an unseen thing.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What two languages does the learner build at once, and how?
**Answer: Arabic understanding and the English physics vocabulary — every English term is glossed beside the Arabic at its first use, so both names attach to the same idea.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Name the four fixed parts of a Jordanian unit in order.
**Answer: Introductory experiment; general-idea page and lessons with review; unit review; the 'Physics and life' application spread.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Give one 'Physics and life' example from the Grade 9 book and the idea it applies.
**Answer: Balancing a metre rule applies the centre of mass (Unit 2); the eye and its defects apply thin lenses (Unit 5); blood-pressure measurement applies fluid pressure (Unit 4).**
