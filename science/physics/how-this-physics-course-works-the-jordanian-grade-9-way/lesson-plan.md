# Lesson plan — How this physics course works — the Jordanian Grade 9 way

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** How this physics course works — the Jordanian Grade 9 way
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18), inside the measurement-and-mechanics strand — the doorway lesson for Jordan's Grade 9 book
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Jordan's Grade 9 physics book runs every unit the same way: an introductory experiment first, a general-idea page, every English physics term glossed in Arabic at first use, lessons ending in review, and the unit closing with a unit review and a 'Physics and life' spread that puts the unit to work in daily life
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Any earlier lesson named below is recommended first, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The introductory experiment comes first** — Every Jordanian unit opens with a تجربة استهلالية — an introductory experiment the learner does before any theory. Unit 1 opens by measuring, Unit 2 by pushing and watching motion, Unit 3 by calculating work with real objects. The experiment is not an illustration of the lesson; it is the lesson's first page, and the theory that follows is the story of what the hands just did.
2. **The general idea and the glossed terms** — After the experiment comes a general-idea page that states the unit's big idea in plain words, and throughout the book every English physics term is glossed beside the Arabic at its first use — measurement, force, work and power each arrive with their English names attached, so the learner builds the Arabic understanding and the international vocabulary together.
3. **Review, unit review and Physics and life** — Each lesson ends with review questions, each unit closes with a full unit review, and a الفيزياء والحياة — 'Physics and life' — spread applies the unit to daily life: the centre of mass met by balancing a metre rule, pressure met in the body, lenses met in the eye. The pattern is the course's promise: try it, name it, check it, use it.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
