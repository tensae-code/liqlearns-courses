# Hands-on — How this physics course works — the Jordanian Grade 9 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Run the pattern yourself: before reading any explanation of pressure, press your thumb into a sponge with the flat and then the edge, and write what changed — your own introductory experiment. (2) Make a two-column glossary of five English physics terms from the book with their Arabic glosses. (3) Find a 'Physics and life' application at home (a balance, a lens, a lever) and name the unit it belongs to.
