# Hands-on — Temperature — how hot, how cold

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Put one hand in warm water and the other in cool water for a minute, then both in the same lukewarm bowl. The bowl gives two verdicts — your hands are not thermometers.

## Apply it where you live
Record one day's temperatures in your life — morning air, cooking, a forehead — and note how each was judged.
