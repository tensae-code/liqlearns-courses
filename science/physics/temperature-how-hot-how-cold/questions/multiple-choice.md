# Temperature — how hot, how cold — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why can't touch be trusted for temperature?
a) The same water feels different to a warmed hand and a chilled hand.  b) The degree of hotness or coldness of a body.  c) Any two of: weather, body/fever, cooking.  d) On a thermometer — measurement beats guessing.
**Answer: a) The same water feels different to a warmed hand and a chilled hand.**

**2.** What does temperature measure?
a) The same water feels different to a warmed hand and a chilled hand.  b) The degree of hotness or coldness of a body.  c) Any two of: weather, body/fever, cooking.  d) On a thermometer — measurement beats guessing.
**Answer: b) The degree of hotness or coldness of a body.**

**3.** Name two daily-life uses of temperature.
a) The same water feels different to a warmed hand and a chilled hand.  b) The degree of hotness or coldness of a body.  c) Any two of: weather, body/fever, cooking.  d) On a thermometer — measurement beats guessing.
**Answer: c) Any two of: weather, body/fever, cooking.**

**4.** Does a healthy person's fever show on a thermometer or by touch more reliably?
a) The same water feels different to a warmed hand and a chilled hand.  b) The degree of hotness or coldness of a body.  c) Any two of: weather, body/fever, cooking.  d) On a thermometer — measurement beats guessing.
**Answer: d) On a thermometer — measurement beats guessing.**
