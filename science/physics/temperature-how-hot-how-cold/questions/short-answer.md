# Temperature — how hot, how cold — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Why can't touch be trusted for temperature?
**Answer: The same water feels different to a warmed hand and a chilled hand.**

**2.** What does temperature measure?
**Answer: The degree of hotness or coldness of a body.**

**3.** Name two daily-life uses of temperature.
**Answer: Any two of: weather, body/fever, cooking.**

**4.** Does a healthy person's fever show on a thermometer or by touch more reliably?
**Answer: On a thermometer — measurement beats guessing.**
