# Video script — Temperature — how hot, how cold

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Temperature is the degree of hotness or coldness of a body.
Touch alone judges it unreliably — a hand warmed by fire finds the same water cold that a chilled hand finds warm — so temperature must be measured, not guessed.
It runs through daily life: the weather that plans a market day, the body temperature that signals fever, the heat that cooks food properly.

Remember: **Temperature is the degree of hotness or coldness.**
Remember: **Touch alone judges temperature unreliably.**
Remember: **Temperature matters in weather, body health and cooking.**
