# Temperature — how hot, how cold

## Do it first
Put one hand in warm water and the other in cool water for a minute, then both in the same lukewarm bowl. The bowl gives two verdicts — your hands are not thermometers.

[Picture 21864 — one day at three temperatures — cold morning, hot afternoon, fever]

## Temperature — how hot, how cold — the teaching
Temperature is the degree of hotness or coldness of a body. Touch alone judges it unreliably — a hand warmed by fire finds the same water cold that a chilled hand finds warm — so temperature must be measured, not guessed. It runs through daily life: the weather that plans a market day, the body temperature that signals fever, the heat that cooks food properly.

## The key ideas

- Temperature is the degree of hotness or coldness.
- Touch alone judges temperature unreliably.
- Temperature matters in weather, body health and cooking.

## Say it to your companion
Explain temperature — how hot, how cold to your companion in your own words. Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
