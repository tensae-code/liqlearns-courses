# Hands-on — Red, green, blue — how eyes build colour

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Stare at a bright red patch for half a minute, then look at a white wall: a greenish ghost appears — your red cones, tired, letting their partners answer louder.

## Apply it where you live
Predict what colour the brain reads for red+green cones only, green+blue only, blue+red only.
