# Red, green, blue — how eyes build colour — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many cone kinds are there, and what do they sense?
a) Three kinds — red, green and blue.  b) They work in dim light, without colour.  c) White.  d) Every seen colour is built from their combinations.
**Answer: a) Three kinds — red, green and blue.**

**2.** What do rods do?
a) Three kinds — red, green and blue.  b) They work in dim light, without colour.  c) White.  d) Every seen colour is built from their combinations.
**Answer: b) They work in dim light, without colour.**

**3.** All three cone kinds answer equally. The brain reads — ?
a) Three kinds — red, green and blue.  b) They work in dim light, without colour.  c) White.  d) Every seen colour is built from their combinations.
**Answer: c) White.**

**4.** Why are red, green and blue called primary?
a) Three kinds — red, green and blue.  b) They work in dim light, without colour.  c) White.  d) Every seen colour is built from their combinations.
**Answer: d) Every seen colour is built from their combinations.**
