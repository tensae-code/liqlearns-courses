# Red, green, blue — how eyes build colour — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** How many cone kinds are there, and what do they sense?
**Answer: Three kinds — red, green and blue.**

**2.** What do rods do?
**Answer: They work in dim light, without colour.**

**3.** All three cone kinds answer equally. The brain reads — ?
**Answer: White.**

**4.** Why are red, green and blue called primary?
**Answer: Every seen colour is built from their combinations.**
