# Video script — Red, green, blue — how eyes build colour

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

The retina carries two kinds of light sensors.
Rods work in dim light without colour; cones work in bright light and come in three kinds, sensing red, green and blue.
The brain builds every colour you see from how strongly those three cone kinds answer: equal answers from all three read as white; red and green together read as yellow.
Red, green and blue are therefore the primary colours of light — the eye's own alphabet of colour.

Remember: **Rods sense dim light; cones sense colour in bright light.**
Remember: **Three cone kinds sense red, green and blue.**
Remember: **Red, green and blue are the primary colours of light.**
