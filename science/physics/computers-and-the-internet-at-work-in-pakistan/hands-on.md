# Hands-on — Computers and the internet at work in Pakistan (Punjab)

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Survey your own surroundings for one day
Survey your own surroundings for one day: list every computer application and internet service you actually use or meet, naming the hand process each replaced. End the list with one risk you personally saw (a discarded device, a privacy choice, an access gap) — the unit's three parts, from your own day.
