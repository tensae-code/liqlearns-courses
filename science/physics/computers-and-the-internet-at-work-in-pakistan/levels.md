# Computers and the internet at work in Pakistan (Punjab) — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — The unit surveys what computers actually do in Punjab's working life: word processing for documents, records kept and searched, calculations, communication and control; On connected machines come the internet's services: the web, electronic mail, messaging, shared files and information search — information flowing between people and institutions at a distance and a speed the standalone computer cannot offer; The same unit refuses to end on praise: it names ICT's risks — to society (dependence, misuse, unequal access, privacy) and to the environment (power demand, electronic waste) — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
