# Computers and the internet at work in Pakistan (Punjab) — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does the survey name applications plainly?
**Answer:** So learners can recognise the computer's work in any real office or service they enter.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What do internet services add to standalone computers?
**Answer:** Distance communication and shared information between people and institutions.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Name two societal risks of ICT.
**Answer:** Dependence, misuse, unequal access, loss of privacy (any two).

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) What habit does teaching uses and risks together build?
**Answer:** Judgement that grows with skill — the learner weighs a tool while learning it.
