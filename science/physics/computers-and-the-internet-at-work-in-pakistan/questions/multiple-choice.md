# Computers and the internet at work in Pakistan (Punjab) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Punjab's named office application is…
a) Only gaming  b) Word processing  c) Satellite repair  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Word processing**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Computers at work mainly replace…
a) All human judgement  b) Slower hand processes for documents, records and calculation  c) Electricity  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Slower hand processes for documents, records and calculation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Internet services are what users meet as…
a) The cables under the street  b) The web, mail, messaging and search over connected machines  c) The power station  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) The web, mail, messaging and search over connected machines**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A standalone computer cannot offer…
a) Word processing  b) Communication at a distance between institutions  c) Calculation  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Communication at a distance between institutions**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The unit teaches ICT's risks…
a) Never  b) In the same unit as its uses  c) Only at university  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) In the same unit as its uses**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) An environmental risk of ICT named by the unit's approach is…
a) Rainfall  b) Electronic waste and power demand  c) Soil erosion by rivers  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Electronic waste and power demand**
