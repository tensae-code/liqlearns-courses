# Video script — Computers and the internet at work in Pakistan (Punjab)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Punjab Grade 10 closes its ICT unit where the technology lands**. The unit surveys what computers actually do in Punjab's working life: word processing for documents, records kept and searched, calculations, communication and control. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — computers at work — the applications; internet services — the network on top; risks to society and the environment — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
