## What holds the nucleus together — nuclear forces

The strong nuclear force is a very short-range attraction between nucleons strong enough to beat proton repulsion; the weak force acts inside nucleons and drives beta decay.

The guide's unit introduction states that nuclear forces are produced from the interaction of mesons, which are independent of charge — a particle-exchange account of the strong force that goes beyond the student book's statement of the force itself, and explains its charge-independence (protons and neutrons bound alike).
