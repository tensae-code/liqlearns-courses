# The strong force — the nucleus's glue — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. The strong nuclear force is a very short-range attraction between nucleons strong enough to beat proton repulsion; the weak force acts inside nucleons and drives beta decay.**
**Answer: True**

**2. The guide's unit introduction states that nuclear forces are produced from the interaction of mesons, which are independent of charge — a particle-exchange account of the strong force that goes beyond the student book's statement of the force itself, and explains its charge-independence (protons and neutrons bound alike).**
**Answer: True**
