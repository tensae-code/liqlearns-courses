# The strong force — the nucleus's glue — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about what holds the nucleus together is correct?**
a) Vectors are added head-to-tail (triangle rule), by the parallelogram rule for two vectors at a point, and by the…  b) The strong nuclear force is a very short-range attraction between nucleons strong enough to beat proton repulsion; the…  c) The second product gives a new vector perpendicular to both parents, with magnitude from the sine of the angle between…
**Answer: b) The strong nuclear force is a very short-range attraction between nucleons strong enough to beat proton…**

**2. The strong nuclear force is a very short-range attraction between nucleons strong enough to beat ______ repulsion; the weak force acts inside nucleons and drives beta decay.**
a) electron  b) proton  c) resistance
**Answer: b) proton**

**3. The guide's unit introduction states that nuclear forces are produced from the interaction of mesons, which are independent of charge — a particle-exchange account of the ______ that goes beyond the student book's statement of the force itself, and explains its charge-independence (protons and neutrons bound alike).**
a) capacitance  b) centripetal  c) strong force
**Answer: c) strong force**
