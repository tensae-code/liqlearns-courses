# The strong force — the nucleus's glue — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. Give one key fact this lesson teaches about what holds the nucleus together.**
**Answer: The strong nuclear force is a very short-range attraction between nucleons strong enough to beat proton repulsion; the weak force acts inside nucleons and drives beta decay.**
