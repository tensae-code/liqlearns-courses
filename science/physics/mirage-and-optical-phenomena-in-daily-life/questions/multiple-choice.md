# Mirage and optical phenomena in daily life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Air near sun-heated ground is…
**Answer: a) hotter and less dense than the air above  b) colder and denser  c) the same density throughout  d) a vacuum**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Crossing into rarer air, light bends…
**Answer: a) away from the normal  b) toward the normal  c) not at all  d) backward**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The bright patch in a road mirage is really…
**Answer: a) sky light bent up into the eye  b) water on the road  c) oil on the road  d) the Sun's reflection in dust**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The ray's turning in a mirage is…
**Answer: a) total internal reflection in gradual, layered form  b) diffraction  c) dispersion  d) absorption**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Stars twinkle because their light…
**Answer: a) crosses moving air layers that bend it unevenly  b) blinks at the source  c) reflects off clouds  d) is absorbed by the Moon**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A superior mirage appears when…
**Answer: a) the temperature gradient is reversed, e.g. over cold water  b) the ground is hottest  c) it rains  d) there is no air**
