# Mirage and optical phenomena in daily life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain in two sentences why a mirage looks like water.
**Answer: Sky light bends upward through the hot layers and enters the eye from below; the eye projects it straight back to the road, and a bright sky-image on a road is read by the brain as water reflecting the sky.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is the bending smooth in a mirage but sharp at a water surface?
**Answer: At a water surface the density changes in one step, so the ray kinks once; in air the density changes gradually through many layers, so the ray bends a little at each layer and its path curves.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Name two daily phenomena, besides the mirage, caused by the same bending.
**Answer: Shimmering air above a stove or hot roof; twinkling stars; the flattened, lifted image of the setting Sun; a superior mirage over cold water.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Why can you never reach the mirage pool?
**Answer: The pool's position depends on the geometry between the Sun-heated ground, the light and your eye — as you move, the place where the bending sends sky light to your eye moves too.**
