# Hands-on — Mirage and optical phenomena in daily life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Look across a hot stove top or sun-baked wall and watch the shimmering — note that objects behind it wobble. (2) Half-fill a glass with water, add a spoon: connect the broken-spoon bend (one boundary) with the mirage's smooth curve (many boundaries). (3) On a cold morning over water, look for raised or doubled images at the horizon and record the weather that goes with them.
