# Joule's law — the heat a current makes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Joule's law states the heat made in a resistor as…
**Answer: a) Q = I²Rt  b) Q = IRt  c) Q = VIt only, never I²R  d) Q = I/Rt**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Doubling the current in the same resistor multiplies the heating rate by…
**Answer: a) four  b) two  c) eight  d) one — it stays the same**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) For a pure resistor, P = I²R and P = VI are…
**Answer: a) the same account — all the delivered energy lands as heat  b) rival laws  c) both wrong  d) unrelated**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A lamp filament is best described as…
**Answer: a) a resistor driven to white heat, paying most energy out as heat  b) a device with no resistance  c) a battery  d) a magnet**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A fuse protects a circuit because…
**Answer: a) its wire is the first victim of I²R heating and melts to break the circuit  b) it stores charge  c) it lowers the voltage  d) it cools the wires**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The square in Joule's law means an overloaded circuit…
**Answer: a) heats far faster than the current increase suggests  b) heats more slowly  c) cannot heat at all  d) becomes safer**
