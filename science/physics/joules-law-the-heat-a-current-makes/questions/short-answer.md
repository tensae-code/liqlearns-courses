# Joule's law — the heat a current makes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A 0.5 A current flows in a 20 Ω resistor for 100 s. Find the heat made.
**Answer: Q = I²Rt = 0.25 × 20 × 100 = 500 J.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the experiment vary current, resistance and time one at a time?
**Answer: So each factor's effect can be read cleanly — the control-variables design: the square on I is only visible when R and t are held still.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Two identical heaters run on the same supply, one for 5 minutes, one for 20. Compare their heat output.
**Answer: Four times the time gives four times the heat — Q ∝ t: the 20-minute heater makes four times as much.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Name one device where Joule heat is the product and one where it is the danger.
**Answer: Product: electric kettle / heater / soldering iron (any one). Danger: an overloaded extension lead or unfused circuit whose wires overheat toward fire.**
