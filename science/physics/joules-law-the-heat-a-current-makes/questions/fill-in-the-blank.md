# Joule's law — the heat a current makes — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Joule's law: Q = _____ × R × t.
**Answer: I²**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Heat is measured in _____ in Joule's law.
**Answer: joules**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Dividing Q by time gives the heating _____, P = I²R.
**Answer: power**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Because heat follows the _____ of the current, overloads are dangerous.
**Answer: square**
