# Acceleration — velocity changing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A car's velocity changes from 5 m/s to 25 m/s in 4 s. Its acceleration?
a) (25 − 5) ÷ 4 = 5 m/s².  b) (4 − 12) ÷ 2 = −4 m/s² — deceleration of 4 m/s².  c) m/s².  d) Yes — its direction, so its velocity, is changing.
**Answer: a) (25 − 5) ÷ 4 = 5 m/s².**

**2.** A cyclist slows from 12 m/s to 4 m/s in 2 s. Acceleration?
a) (25 − 5) ÷ 4 = 5 m/s².  b) (4 − 12) ÷ 2 = −4 m/s² — deceleration of 4 m/s².  c) m/s².  d) Yes — its direction, so its velocity, is changing.
**Answer: b) (4 − 12) ÷ 2 = −4 m/s² — deceleration of 4 m/s².**

**3.** What is the unit of acceleration?
a) (25 − 5) ÷ 4 = 5 m/s².  b) (4 − 12) ÷ 2 = −4 m/s² — deceleration of 4 m/s².  c) m/s².  d) Yes — its direction, so its velocity, is changing.
**Answer: c) m/s².**

**4.** A car turns a corner at steady speed. Is it accelerating?
a) (25 − 5) ÷ 4 = 5 m/s².  b) (4 − 12) ÷ 2 = −4 m/s² — deceleration of 4 m/s².  c) m/s².  d) Yes — its direction, so its velocity, is changing.
**Answer: d) Yes — its direction, so its velocity, is changing.**
