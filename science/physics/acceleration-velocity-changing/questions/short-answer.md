# Acceleration — velocity changing — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A car's velocity changes from 5 m/s to 25 m/s in 4 s. Its acceleration?
**Answer: (25 − 5) ÷ 4 = 5 m/s².**

**2.** A cyclist slows from 12 m/s to 4 m/s in 2 s. Acceleration?
**Answer: (4 − 12) ÷ 2 = −4 m/s² — deceleration of 4 m/s².**

**3.** What is the unit of acceleration?
**Answer: m/s².**

**4.** A car turns a corner at steady speed. Is it accelerating?
**Answer: Yes — its direction, so its velocity, is changing.**
