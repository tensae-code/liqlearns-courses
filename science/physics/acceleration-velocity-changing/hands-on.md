# Hands-on — Acceleration — velocity changing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Roll a ball down a gentle slope and watch the gaps it covers grow each second. That growing-gap pattern is acceleration you can see.

## Apply it where you live
Describe two everyday accelerations and one deceleration, with what changed in each.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21818 — cart with velocity arrows growing longer each second] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using Acceleration = change of velocity / time: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
