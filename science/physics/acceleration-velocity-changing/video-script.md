Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity: how fast velocity itself is changing, each second.
Speeding up is acceleration; slowing down is deceleration — acceleration in the opposite sense; and changing direction is also a change of velocity, so it is acceleration too.
Acceleration = change of velocity ÷ time taken, and its unit is m/s² — metres per second, per second.

Remember: **Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity.**
Remember: **Slowing down is deceleration; changing direction also accelerates a body.**
Remember: **Acceleration's unit is m/s².**
Keep the formula: **Acceleration = change of velocity / time**
