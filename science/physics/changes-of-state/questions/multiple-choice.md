# Changes of state — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about change of phase is correct?**
a) Melting, freezing, boiling, evaporation and condensation swap heat without changing temperature while they last; the…  b) For ohmic conductors current is proportional to voltage (the straight-line graph); non-ohmic devices (the curved graph,…  c) Output is 1 only when all inputs are 1 (the multiplication of logic), pictured as switches in series.
**Answer: a) Melting, freezing, boiling, evaporation and condensation swap heat without changing temperature while they…**

**2. Melting, freezing, boiling, evaporation and condensation swap heat without changing ______ while they last; the heating curve's flat stretches are the picture of it.**
a) temperature  b) pressure  c) half-life
**Answer: a) temperature**
