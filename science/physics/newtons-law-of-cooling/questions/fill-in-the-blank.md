# Newton's law of cooling — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Cooling is driven by the temperature _____ between the body and its surroundings.
**Answer: gap (difference)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The rate of loss of heat is directly _____ to the temperature difference.
**Answer: proportional**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In the law's symbols, T_s stands for the temperature of the _____.
**Answer: surroundings**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) As a body nears room temperature, its cooling curve _____ out.
**Answer: flattens**
