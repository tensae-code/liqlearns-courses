# Newton's law of cooling — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A hot body in a cooler room cools fastest when…
**Answer: a) the temperature gap between it and the room is widest  b) it is nearly at room temperature  c) the room is exactly as hot as it is  d) it is covered so no heat can leave**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Heat leaves a cooling body by…
**Answer: a) conduction, convection and radiation  b) radiation only  c) evaporation only  d) it does not leave — it is used up**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Newton's law of cooling says the rate of loss of heat is proportional to…
**Answer: a) the difference in temperature between the body and its surroundings  b) the body's temperature alone  c) the square of the body's mass  d) the room's temperature alone**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In dT/dt = −k(T − T_s), the minus sign says…
**Answer: a) the temperature falls  b) the body heats up  c) k is negative  d) time runs backwards**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The temperature–time graph of a cooling body…
**Answer: a) falls steeply at first, then flattens toward the surroundings' temperature  b) is a straight line to zero  c) rises, then falls  d) crosses below the room temperature and keeps falling**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The same soup cools faster in a wide shallow bowl than in a narrow mug because the bowl has…
**Answer: a) a larger surface, so a larger k  b) a larger temperature gap  c) colder soup  d) a smaller volume of air above it**
