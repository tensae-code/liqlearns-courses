# Newton's law of cooling — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does a cup of tea cool quickly in the first minutes and slowly later?
**Answer: At first the gap between the tea and the room is wide, so heat flows out fast; as the tea nears room temperature the gap narrows, and every heat flow driven by that gap weakens with it.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State Newton's law of cooling, and name the condition the lesson puts on it.
**Answer: The rate of loss of heat of a body is directly proportional to the difference in temperature between the body and its surroundings — provided that difference is small.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A body is only 2 °C warmer than the room. What does the law predict about its cooling?
**Answer: It will cool very slowly — the gap is tiny, so the rate of heat loss, being proportional to the gap, is tiny too.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A body at 80 °C in a 30 °C room has an excess of 50 °C. Later its excess is 25 °C. Compare its cooling rates at the two moments.
**Answer: The later rate is half the earlier one: the excess halved, and the rate is proportional to the excess.**
