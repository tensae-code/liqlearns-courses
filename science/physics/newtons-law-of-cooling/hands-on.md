# Hands-on — Newton's law of cooling

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch a cup cool, minute by minute
With a teacher's eye on the hot water: fill a mug with hot (not boiling) water, note the room temperature, and read the water's temperature every minute for ten minutes, using a kitchen thermometer. Write the excess over room temperature beside each reading. Plot temperature against time: your curve should fall steeply at first and flatten — and the excess should roughly halve over equal steps of time while the law holds.
