# Convection — heat carried by moving fluid — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. In fluids, heated portions expand, lighten and rise while cooler fluid sinks, setting up convection currents that carry the heat — the mechanism behind land/sea breezes and room heating patterns.**
**Answer: True**

**2. In fluids, heated portions expand, lighten and rise while cooler fluid sinks, setting down convection currents that carry the heat — the mechanism behind land/sea breezes and room heating patterns.**
**Answer: False**
