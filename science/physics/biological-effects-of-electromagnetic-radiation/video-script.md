# Video script — Biological effects of electromagnetic radiation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **what electromagnetic radiation does to living things**. Every photon carries energy set by its frequency. Low-frequency photons — radio, microwaves, light itself — can warm you but cannot break the bonds in your cells. High-frequency photons — strong ultraviolet, X-rays, gamma rays — can knock electrons off atoms and snap DNA, and that is where the harm lives: sickness at high doses, a raised cancer risk as dose builds. The protection is beautifully simple: less time, more distance, something solid in the way. Respect the ionising end; do not fear the rest.
