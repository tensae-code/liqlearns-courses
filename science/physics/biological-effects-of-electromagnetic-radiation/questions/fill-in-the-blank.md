# Biological effects of electromagnetic radiation — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A photon's energy is E = _____, Planck's constant times frequency.
**Answer: hf**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Radiation that can knock electrons off atoms is called _____ radiation.
**Answer: ionising**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Absorbed energy per kilogram of tissue is the absorbed _____.
**Answer: dose**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Doubling your distance from a small source cuts the intensity to one _____.
**Answer: quarter**
