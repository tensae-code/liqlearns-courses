# Biological effects of electromagnetic radiation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Whether a kind of EM radiation is ionising is decided by…
a) Its brightness  b) Each photon's energy, E = hf — its frequency  c) How long it has travelled  d) Whether it is artificial
**Answer: b) Each photon's energy, E = hf — its frequency**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is ionising?
a) Radio waves  b) Microwaves  c) Visible light  d) Gamma rays
**Answer: d) Gamma rays**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A deterministic effect of ionising radiation is one that…
a) Happens by chance at any dose  b) Appears above a threshold dose and grows worse as dose grows — cell death, burns, sickness  c) Only affects DNA  d) Cannot be measured
**Answer: b) Appears above a threshold dose and grows worse as dose grows — cell death, burns, sickness**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The stochastic worry from a surviving, mutated cell is…
a) Sunburn  b) A later cancer, with probability rising as dose rises  c) Warming  d) A blurred X-ray
**Answer: b) A later cancer, with probability rising as dose rises**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Moving from 1 m to 2 m from a small source changes the intensity to…
a) One half  b) One quarter — the inverse square law  c) One eighth  d) Nothing
**Answer: b) One quarter — the inverse square law**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A phone's radio waves cannot ionise tissue, however many photons arrive, because…
a) Phones are shielded  b) Each photon's energy is far below the ionisation threshold — the limit is per photon, not per crowd  c) Radio waves are not radiation  d) Skin blocks all waves
**Answer: b) Each photon's energy is far below the ionisation threshold — the limit is per photon, not per crowd**
