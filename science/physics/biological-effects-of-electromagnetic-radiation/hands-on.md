# Hands-on — Biological effects of electromagnetic radiation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel the inverse square with a lamp
In a dim room, hold this page at arm's length from a bare lamp or bright torch and read a line comfortably. Now have a partner hold the lamp three times as far away (pace it out: one pace, then three) while you keep the page in the same place. The page is dramatically dimmer — the same light spread over nine times the area. Say aloud: "Three times the distance, one ninth the intensity — that is why distance protects." Finish with the sun rule in the same words you would tell a younger child: shade, clothing and sunscreen are shielding; the middle of the day is the strong-dose time; and no tan is worth a burn.
