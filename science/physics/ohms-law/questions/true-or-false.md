# Ohm's law — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. For ohmic conductors current is proportional to voltage (the straight-line graph); non-ohmic devices (the curved graph, e.g. a filament lamp) break the rule — both graphs are printed side by side.**
**Answer: True**

**2. For ohmic conductors current is inversely proportional to voltage (the straight-line graph); non-ohmic devices (the curved graph, e.g. a filament lamp) break the rule — both graphs are printed side by side.**
**Answer: False**
