# Video script — The electromagnetic spectrum — uses and dangers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

One family, ordered by frequency, wavelength and energy: radio waves (longest wavelength), then microwaves, infrared, visible light, ultraviolet, X-rays and gamma rays (shortest, most energetic).
Each band has its work — radio carries broadcasts, microwaves cook and communicate, infrared is felt as heat, ultraviolet tans and sterilises, X-rays image bones, gamma rays treat and endanger.
The rule of dangers: the higher the frequency, the deeper the harm it can do to living tissue.

Remember: **Order by shrinking wavelength: radio, microwave, infrared, visible, ultraviolet, X-ray, gamma.**
Remember: **Higher frequency means higher energy and greater danger to tissue.**
Remember: **Each band has named uses: broadcasts, heat, imaging, sterilising.**
