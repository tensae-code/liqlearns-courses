# The electromagnetic spectrum — uses and dangers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which band has the longest wavelength?
a) Radio waves.  b) X-rays.  c) By frequency (low→high): infrared, ultraviolet, gamma.  d) Far higher frequency and energy — they damage living tissue.
**Answer: a) Radio waves.**

**2.** Which band images bones?
a) Radio waves.  b) X-rays.  c) By frequency (low→high): infrared, ultraviolet, gamma.  d) Far higher frequency and energy — they damage living tissue.
**Answer: b) X-rays.**

**3.** Put in order: ultraviolet, infrared, gamma.
a) Radio waves.  b) X-rays.  c) By frequency (low→high): infrared, ultraviolet, gamma.  d) Far higher frequency and energy — they damage living tissue.
**Answer: c) By frequency (low→high): infrared, ultraviolet, gamma.**

**4.** Why are gamma rays more dangerous than radio waves?
a) Radio waves.  b) X-rays.  c) By frequency (low→high): infrared, ultraviolet, gamma.  d) Far higher frequency and energy — they damage living tissue.
**Answer: d) Far higher frequency and energy — they damage living tissue.**
