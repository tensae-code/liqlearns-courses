# The electromagnetic spectrum — uses and dangers — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Which band has the longest wavelength?
**Answer: Radio waves.**

**2.** Which band images bones?
**Answer: X-rays.**

**3.** Put in order: ultraviolet, infrared, gamma.
**Answer: By frequency (low→high): infrared, ultraviolet, gamma.**

**4.** Why are gamma rays more dangerous than radio waves?
**Answer: Far higher frequency and energy — they damage living tissue.**
