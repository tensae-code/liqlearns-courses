# Hands-on — The electromagnetic spectrum — uses and dangers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Lay out seven objects or cards — radio, phone, stove heat, lamp, sun, clinic, the sky's most violent events — and order them by the spectrum before checking.

## Apply it where you live
Make a spectrum strip for the classroom wall with uses and dangers under each band.
