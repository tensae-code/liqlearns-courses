# Hands-on — The vernier caliper and travelling microscope

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Close, check, measure
Close a vernier caliper gently and read what it says shut — that is its zero error, sign and all. Then measure a coin's thickness at four places around its rim, correct each reading, and say aloud: no single reading is the coin; the set of corrected readings is.

## Line it up
On any printed vernier diagram (or the real instrument), find by eye the one vernier line that continues a main-scale line without a step. Say aloud: I am not guessing the fraction — the coinciding line is the fraction, counted.
