# Video script — The vernier caliper and travelling microscope

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **reading finer than the ruler**. Lay a short sliding scale against a ruler, its ten divisions squeezed into the space of nine. Slide it a hair, and exactly one pair of lines meets — count to that line and you have the fraction of a millimetre the ruler could never show. Put jaws around the scale and it is a **vernier caliper**; put a microscope on it and it becomes a **travelling microscope** that can stop its cross-wires on a hair. First check the zero, then main scale, then the coinciding line times the least count.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
