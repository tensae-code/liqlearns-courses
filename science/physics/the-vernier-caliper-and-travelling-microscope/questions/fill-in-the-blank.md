# The vernier caliper and travelling microscope — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The smallest length an instrument can read is its _____ count.
**Answer: least**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A caliper reading is the main-scale reading plus the coinciding division times the least _____.
**Answer: count**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In a travelling microscope the eye places _____ -wires exactly on the feature being measured.
**Answer: cross**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) If the zeros do not coincide when the instrument is closed, the instrument has a _____ error.
**Answer: zero**
