# The vernier caliper and travelling microscope — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) With 10 vernier divisions spanning 9 main divisions of 1 mm, the least count is…
a) 1 mm  b) 0.1 mm  c) 0.9 mm  d) 0.01 mm
**Answer: b) 0.1 mm**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A closed caliper reads +0.3 mm. This is a…
a) Random error to ignore  b) Zero error of +0.3 mm, subtracted from every reading  c) Least count  d) Broken instrument
**Answer: b) Zero error of +0.3 mm, subtracted from every reading**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Main scale 31 mm, 8th vernier line coincides, least count 0.1 mm. The reading is…
a) 31.8 mm  b) 38 mm  c) 31.08 mm  d) 39 mm
**Answer: a) 31.8 mm**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The depth of a small cup is measured with the caliper's…
a) Large outside jaws  b) Small upper jaws  c) Thin rod sliding from the end  d) Vernier zero
**Answer: c) Thin rod sliding from the end**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A length from a travelling microscope is always found as…
a) One reading alone  b) The difference of two readings, one at each end  c) The microscope's magnification  d) The least count times ten
**Answer: b) The difference of two readings, one at each end**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The microscope's job in a travelling microscope is to…
a) Make the scale longer  b) Place cross-wires exactly on tiny features  c) Remove zero error  d) Magnify the vernier numbers only
**Answer: b) Place cross-wires exactly on tiny features**
