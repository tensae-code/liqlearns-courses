# Electric potential and potential difference — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Electric potential at a point is…
a) work done per unit charge  b) force per unit charge  c) charge per second  d) energy of the whole body
**Answer: a) work done per unit charge**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The volt is the same as…
a) joule per coulomb  b) coulomb per second  c) newton per metre  d) joule per second
**Answer: a) joule per coulomb**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Current flows through the outside circuit from…
a) higher potential to lower potential  b) lower to higher  c) positive terminal only at night  d) it does not flow
**Answer: a) higher potential to lower potential**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A voltmeter is connected…
a) in parallel across the component  b) in series in the wire  c) across the battery only  d) in place of the switch
**Answer: a) in parallel across the component**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Plates 0.1 m apart at 50 V p.d. have field strength…
a) 500 V/m  b) 5 V/m  c) 50 V/m  d) 5000 V/m
**Answer: a) 500 V/m**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Moving a charge along an equipotential line costs…
a) no work  b) work equal to QV  c) double work  d) the charge gains energy
**Answer: a) no work**
