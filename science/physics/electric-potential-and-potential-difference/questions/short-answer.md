# Electric potential and potential difference — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A point has potential 6 V. How much work is needed to bring 3 C there from earth?
**Answer: W = QV = 3 × 6 = 18 J.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why can a bird sit on a high-voltage wire unharmed?
**Answer: Both feet are at the same potential — there is no potential difference across the bird, so no current flows through it.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A battery's p.d. is 9 V. What does the 9 price, exactly?
**Answer: The energy given to each coulomb: 9 joules per coulomb delivered by the source.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Two points are at 40 V and 15 V. What is the p.d. between them, and which way does positive charge flow between them in a wire?
**Answer: 25 V; charge flows from the 40 V point toward the 15 V point.**
