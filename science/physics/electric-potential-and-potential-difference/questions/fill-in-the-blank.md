# Electric potential and potential difference — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) V = W ÷ _____.
**Answer: Q (charge)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The instrument that measures potential difference is the _____.
**Answer: voltmeter**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In a uniform field, E = V ÷ _____.
**Answer: d (distance)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Lines of equal potential are called _____.
**Answer: equipotentials**
