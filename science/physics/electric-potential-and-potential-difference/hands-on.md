# Hands-on — Electric potential and potential difference

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feeling potential difference
With a low-voltage battery pack, two leads and a small lamp (a teacher checks the setup first): light the lamp from one cell, then from two cells in series, then three. The lamp brightens each time — the same lamp, the same resistance, but a bigger potential difference pushing each coulomb through with more energy. Write beside each setup the battery's volts and the lamp's brightness rank: p.d. is the push you have been changing.
