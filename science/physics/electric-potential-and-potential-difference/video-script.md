# Video script — Electric potential and potential difference

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **volts price the push**. Potential is work per coulomb at a point; the difference between two points is what drives current — charge falls from high to low, spending its energy on the way. And the field is just that difference spread over distance: E = V/d.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
