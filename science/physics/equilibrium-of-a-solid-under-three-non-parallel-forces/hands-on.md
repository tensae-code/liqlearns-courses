# Hands-on — Equilibrium of a solid under three non-parallel forces

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

["(1) Hang a mass from two spring balances tied at angles, with the mass's weight as the third force; record the three readings and angles, then draw the force triangle to scale and check it closes. (2) Rest a plank against a wall on a smooth floor with a rough wall contact removed/added — find when it slips and name the three forces. (3) Put a toy car on a tilted book, raise the tilt until it just slides, and compare the angle's sine with the friction you can measure by a spring balance pull."]
