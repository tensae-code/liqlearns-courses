# Equilibrium of a solid under three non-parallel forces — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) For three non-parallel forces to balance, their lines of action must…
**Answer: a) all pass through one point  b) be parallel  c) form a square  d) be equal in size**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The vector condition for three-force equilibrium is…
**Answer: a) F₁ + F₂ + F₃ = 0  b) F₁ + F₂ = F₃²  c) F₁·F₂·F₃ = 0  d) F₁ = F₂ = F₃ always**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Drawn head-to-tail, three balancing forces form…
**Answer: a) a closed triangle — the force polygon  b) an open chain  c) a straight line only  d) a circle**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The Mauritanian name for the polygon construction is…
**Answer: a) the 'dynamique des forces'  b) the parallelogram  c) the lever rule  d) the node law**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) For a car resting on an incline of angle α, friction equals…
**Answer: a) mg·sin α  b) mg·cos α  c) mg·tan α  d) mg**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The normal reaction on the incline car equals…
**Answer: a) mg·cos α  b) mg·sin α  c) zero  d) mg/sin α**
