# Equilibrium of a solid under three non-parallel forces — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Three balancing forces must be _____ — their lines meet at one point.
**Answer: concurrent**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Three balancing arrows drawn head-to-tail close into a _____ .
**Answer: triangle (force polygon)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Projection gives two equations: ΣFx = 0 and _____ = 0.
**Answer: ΣFy**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) On an incline, the weight's component along the slope is mg·_____ α.
**Answer: sin**
