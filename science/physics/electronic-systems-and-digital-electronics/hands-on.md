# Hands-on — Electronic systems and digital electronics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the truth table with two switches
With a battery, a small lamp or buzzer and two switches in series on a table (a teacher checks the circuit before power), you have built an AND gate with your hands: the lamp lights only when switch A AND switch B are both closed. Run all four combinations and write the truth table from what the lamp actually does. Rewire the switches in parallel and repeat: now the lamp is an OR gate. Finish by holding one switch closed and working the other in the series circuit: the lamp now follows the free switch exactly — one input in control, the other held at 1.
