# Electronic systems and digital electronics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the three blocks of an electronic system and give one component that can sit in each.
**Answer: Input — a sensor such as a thermistor, light-dependent resistor, microphone or switch; process — a transistor/comparator circuit or a processor; output — a lamp, buzzer, motor, heater or display.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why are two-level signals hard for noise to spoil?
**Answer: A clear high level stays recognisably high and a clear low stays low even with a little noise added, because the circuit only has to decide '1 or 0', not read an exact value.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) An OR gate has inputs A = 0, B = 1. What is its output, and what would an AND gate give for the same inputs?
**Answer: OR gives 1 (at least one input is 1); AND gives 0 (both inputs must be 1).**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) What is a truth table?
**Answer: A complete list of every possible combination of a gate's inputs with the output the gate gives for each combination.**
