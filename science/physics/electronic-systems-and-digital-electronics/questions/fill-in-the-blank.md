# Electronic systems and digital electronics — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A device that turns a physical quantity into an electrical signal is a _____.
**Answer: sensor**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Digital signals are written with the two digits 1 and _____.
**Answer: 0**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Numbers inside digital systems are written in the _____ number system.
**Answer: binary**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The gate whose output is the opposite of its input is the _____ gate.
**Answer: NOT**
