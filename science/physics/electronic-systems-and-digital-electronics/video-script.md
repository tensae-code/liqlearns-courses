# Video script — Electronic systems and digital electronics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **systems that sense, decide and act — in 1s and 0s**. Every electronic system is input, process, output: a sensor feels the world, a circuit decides, a lamp or motor answers. Digital circuits decide with signals that have only two levels, 1 and 0, counted in binary, and the deciding is done by logic gates — AND needs both, OR needs either, NOT flips. A truth table writes the whole rule in four rows.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
