Today: **the machine inside every engine**. Draw a border around a gas: that is a **system**. Its particles' hidden energies add up to its **internal energy** — and only two doors change it: heat in, and work out. That is the first law: ΔU = Q − W. Walk the system around a closed loop on a pressure–volume picture and you have a **heat engine**: it drinks heat from a hot source, turns part into work, and must pour the rest into a cold sink. Carnot found the best possible share — one minus the cold temperature over the hot, in kelvin — and the second law, kept by **entropy**, says no one, ever, does better.
