# Thermodynamic systems and the heat engine — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — Define a system and its internal energy, and state the first law with its sign convention; Name the five transformation kinds, what each holds constant, and read work as area on a p–V diagram; Compute an engine's work and efficiency and a Carnot ceiling from two temperatures; State the second law in engine form and say what entropy counts, macroscopically and microscopically — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
