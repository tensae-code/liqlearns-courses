# Thermodynamic systems and the heat engine — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The bicycle pump warms though no heat was supplied. Account for it with the first law.
**Answer: The pumping does work on the trapped air — near-adiabatic compression: Q ≈ 0 and W (by the gas) is negative, so ΔU = Q − W > 0; the internal energy, and so the temperature, rises.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does heating a gas at constant pressure cost more heat than at constant volume for the same temperature rise?
**Answer: At constant pressure the gas expands and does work, so part of the heat leaves as work; at constant volume no work is done and all the heat lands in internal energy. The constant-pressure specific heat is therefore larger.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) State the second law in its engine form.
**Answer: No cyclic heat engine can convert all the heat it absorbs into work; some heat must be discharged to a cold sink — efficiency is always less than 1.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) What does entropy count, in the microscopic reading?
**Answer: How the energy is spread among the particles' possible arrangements: more arrangements — more disorder — means higher entropy; the universe's entropy never decreases in any real process.**
