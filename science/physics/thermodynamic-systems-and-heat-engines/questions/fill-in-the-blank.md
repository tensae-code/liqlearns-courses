# Thermodynamic systems and the heat engine — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Heat and work are energy in _____ across the system's border.
**Answer: transit**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A transformation with no heat exchanged is called _____ .
**Answer: adiabatic**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A transformation that returns to its start state is called _____ .
**Answer: cyclic**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The theoretical ceiling on any engine's efficiency is set by the _____ cycle.
**Answer: Carnot**
