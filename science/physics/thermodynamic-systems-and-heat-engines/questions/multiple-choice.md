# Thermodynamic systems and the heat engine — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A system's internal energy U is…
a) The heat it contains  b) The sum of its particles' microscopic kinetic and potential energies — a property of its state  c) The work stored in it  d) Its temperature in joules
**Answer: a) The heat it contains**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In the convention ΔU = Q − W, W is…
a) Work done by the system on its surroundings  b) Work done on the system  c) Waste heat  d) The weight of the system
**Answer: b) Work done on the system**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In an isothermal transformation of a perfect gas…
a) ΔU = 0, so all absorbed heat leaves as work  b) W = 0  c) Q = 0 and the gas cools  d) Pressure is constant
**Answer: c) Q = 0 and the gas cools**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The work done by a system in a transformation is read from the p–V diagram as…
a) The area under the path  b) The starting pressure  c) The final volume  d) The length of the path
**Answer: d) The length of the path**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) An engine absorbs 900 J and discharges 600 J per cycle. Its efficiency is…
a) 1.5  b) 0.67  c) 0.33  d) 0.50
**Answer: a) 1.5**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The Carnot efficiency between 600 K and 300 K is…
a) 0.50  b) 0.25  c) 1.00  d) 2.00
**Answer: b) 0.25**
