# Sinusoidal alternating current — period, frequency and rms values with the oscilloscope — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) One complete pattern of a sine wave — up, down and back to the start — is called one _____.
**Answer: cycle**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Period and frequency are joined by f = 1/_____.
**Answer: T**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The mains supply in Morocco alternates at _____ Hz.
**Answer: 50**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The instrument that draws a voltage wave on a screen is the _____.
**Answer: oscilloscope**
