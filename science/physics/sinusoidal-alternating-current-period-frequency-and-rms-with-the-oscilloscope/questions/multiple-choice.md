# Sinusoidal alternating current — period, frequency and rms values with the oscilloscope — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An alternating current is a current that…
a) Flows steadily in one direction  b) Keeps reversing direction, tracing a sine wave  c) Flows only in short bursts  d) Never reaches zero
**Answer: b) Keeps reversing direction, tracing a sine wave**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The peak value of a sinusoidal voltage is…
a) Its average value  b) The largest value the wave reaches  c) Half its rms value  d) The value at zero crossing
**Answer: b) The largest value the wave reaches**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A wave has a period of 0.02 s. Its frequency is…
a) 0.02 Hz  b) 2 Hz  c) 20 Hz  d) 50 Hz
**Answer: d) 50 Hz**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Frequency is measured in…
a) Seconds  b) Volts  c) Hertz  d) Amperes
**Answer: c) Hertz**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) On an oscilloscope screen, the period of a wave is read from…
a) The vertical scale  b) The horizontal (time) scale, counting the divisions of one complete cycle  c) The brightness control  d) The peak height
**Answer: b) The horizontal (time) scale, counting the divisions of one complete cycle**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A sinusoidal voltage peaks at 100 V. Its rms value is about…
a) 100 V  b) 141 V  c) 71 V  d) 50 V
**Answer: c) 71 V**
