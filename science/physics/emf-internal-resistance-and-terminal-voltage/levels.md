# EMF, internal resistance and terminal voltage of a source — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner defines EMF as energy per unit charge and reads it as the open-circuit terminal voltage, explains internal resistance and the lost volts Ir, uses V = E − Ir and I = E/(R + r) in worked circuits, and reads E and r off a terminal-voltage–current graph, answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — doing the lesson's own experiments and sorts by hand, naming each idea aloud, and reasoning about new examples until the teaching is the learner's own.
