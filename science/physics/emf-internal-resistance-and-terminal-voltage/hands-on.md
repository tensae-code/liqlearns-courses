# Hands-on — EMF, internal resistance and terminal voltage of a source

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The sagging voltmeter
Connect a voltmeter across a cell with nothing else attached and read the EMF. Now connect a lamp across the cell and read again while it glows. Say aloud: same cell, smaller reading — the missing volts are being spent inside the cell.

## The V–I line
Wire the cell to a variable resistor with an ammeter in series and a voltmeter across the cell. Take five pairs of readings, changing the resistor each time, and plot terminal voltage against current. Draw the straight line back to zero current: where it lands is the EMF; how steeply it falls is the internal resistance. Say aloud: the graph measures what my hands cannot separate.
