# EMF, internal resistance and terminal voltage of a source — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) EMF is the energy a source gives to each _____ of charge.
**Answer: coulomb**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The resistance inside a real source is called its _____ resistance.
**Answer: internal**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The volts spent inside the source, Ir, are often called the _____ volts.
**Answer: lost**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Terminal voltage equals EMF minus _____, the lost volts.
**Answer: Ir**
