# EMF, internal resistance and terminal voltage of a source — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The EMF of a source is…
a) A force measured in newtons  b) The energy the source gives to each coulomb of charge  c) The current it can supply  d) The voltage across a lamp
**Answer: b) The energy the source gives to each coulomb of charge**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A voltmeter across a source reads the EMF itself when…
a) The lamp is brightest  b) No current is being drawn from the source  c) The source is short-circuited  d) The current is at its largest
**Answer: b) No current is being drawn from the source**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The 'lost volts' in a working source are…
a) Lost from the circuit entirely  b) The part of the EMF spent on the source's own internal resistance, Ir  c) A meter error  d) Stored in the wires
**Answer: b) The part of the EMF spent on the source's own internal resistance, Ir**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A 1.5 V cell with r = 0.5 Ω drives a 2.5 Ω lamp. The current is…
a) 0.6 A  b) 3.0 A  c) 0.5 A  d) 1.5 A
**Answer: c) 0.5 A**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In that same circuit, the terminal voltage is…
a) 1.5 V  b) 1.25 V  c) 0.25 V  d) 3.0 V
**Answer: b) 1.25 V**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) On a graph of terminal voltage against current, the intercept and the slope's size give…
a) r and E  b) E and r  c) I and V  d) R and r
**Answer: b) E and r**
