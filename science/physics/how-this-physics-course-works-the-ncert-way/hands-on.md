# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the NCERT way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run one chapter the NCERT way
Take one short chapter of any textbook you own. Read only the first section. The moment you meet a worked example, cover its working and try it yourself first, then uncover and compare. At the end of the section, write one Points to Ponder question of your own in the margin. Then work the chapter's end questions in two rings — the direct ones first, the hardest ones last — and mark honestly which ring stopped you.
