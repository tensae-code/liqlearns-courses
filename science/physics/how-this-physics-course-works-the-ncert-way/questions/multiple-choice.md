# How this Physics course works — the NCERT way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In an NCERT chapter, a worked example sits…
**Answer: a) inside the text, at the moment the idea lands  b) only in a separate solutions book  c) all together in an appendix at the book's end  d) nowhere — there are no worked examples**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) An NCERT chapter is built as…
**Answer: a) numbered sections, one idea per section, in order  b) one long undivided essay  c) questions only, with no text  d) a story with the physics hidden in it**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A "Points to Ponder" box is for…
**Answer: a) stopping to think about questions the text did not answer outright  b) decoration, to be skipped  c) the chapter's answers  d) the list of symbols**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The blue boxes down the margin carry…
**Answer: a) margin notes — a caution, a shortcut, a historical aside  b) advertisements  c) the next chapter's text  d) blank space for doodling**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The exercises at a chapter's end practise…
**Answer: a) exactly what the chapter taught, in the order it taught it  b) next year's syllabus  c) only arithmetic  d) topics from other subjects**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The additional exercises are…
**Answer: a) a harder second ring — combined ideas and unfamiliar settings  b) a repeat of the exercises with the numbers changed  c) optional pictures  d) the teacher's private questions**
