# How this Physics course works — the NCERT way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does NCERT put the worked example inside the text instead of saving examples for the end?
**Answer: So the example teaches the method while the idea is fresh — use follows statement immediately, and the reader never has to hold a new law through pages of theory before meeting its first use.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What should a reader actually do at a Points to Ponder box?
**Answer: Stop, and answer the ponder question in their own words before reading on — the box is where the chapter tests understanding rather than mere following.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What is the difference between the exercises and the additional exercises?
**Answer: The exercises practise exactly what the chapter taught, in its order; the additional exercises reach further — combined ideas, unfamiliar settings, estimation and reasoning.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Give one example of an NCERT chapter's everyday or historical hook named in the lesson.
**Answer: Aristotle's fallacy opens Laws of Motion (Galileo opening kinematics is also accepted).**
