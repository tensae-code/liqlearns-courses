# The generator as an active dipole — EMF, internal resistance and the operating point — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A generator's U–I line intercepts at 9 V with slope −3 Ω. State E and r, and the terminal voltage at 2 A.
**Answer: E = 9 V (the I = 0 intercept); r = 3 Ω. At 2 A: U = E − rI = 9 − 6 = 3 V.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Derive the power balance from U = E − rI.
**Answer: Multiply by I: UI = EI − rI², so EI = UI + rI² — total power generated = useful power delivered + Joule heat lost in the internal resistance.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Solve the operating point for E = 12 V, r = 2 Ω feeding R = 200 Ω, both ways in outline.
**Answer: Algebraically: I = E/(R + r) = 12/202 ≈ 0.059 A, U = RI ≈ 11.9 V. Graphically: draw the falling line from (0, 12) with slope −2 and the rising line U = 200I; they cross at the same (11.9 V, 0.059 A).**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does efficiency ρ = U/E fall as more current is drawn?
**Answer: U = E − rI shrinks as I grows — a bigger share of the EMF is dropped inside the generator as rI (and burnt as rI² heat), so the surviving share U/E falls.**
