# The generator as an active dipole — EMF, internal resistance and the operating point — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A real generator's terminal voltage as current rises…
**Answer: a) falls: U = E − rI  b) rises: U = E + rI  c) stays exactly E  d) falls to zero at once**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The EMF E is measured as the terminal voltage when…
**Answer: a) the current is zero  b) the current is maximum  c) the generator is short-circuited  d) the load is removed and replaced**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In the balance EI = UI + rI², the rI² term is…
**Answer: a) heat lost inside the generator  b) useful output  c) the receiver's stored energy  d) the EMF**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The generator's efficiency is ρ = …
**Answer: a) U/E  b) E/U  c) rI/E  d) UI/r**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The operating point of a generator feeding a resistor is found where…
**Answer: a) the two characteristics cross  b) the generator's line meets the axis  c) the current is largest  d) the voltage is E**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) For E = 6 V, r = 1 Ω, R = 5 Ω, the operating current is…
**Answer: a) 1 A  b) 6 A  c) 1.2 A  d) 0.83 A**
