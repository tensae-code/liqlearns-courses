# The generator as an active dipole — EMF, internal resistance and the operating point — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A generator's characteristic is U = E − _____ .
**Answer: rI**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The voltage at zero current is the _____ , E.
**Answer: electromotive force (EMF)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The power lost as heat inside the generator is _____ .
**Answer: rI²**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Generator and receiver settle at their _____ point.
**Answer: operating**
