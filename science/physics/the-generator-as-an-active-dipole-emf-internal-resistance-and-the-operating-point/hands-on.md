# Hands-on — The generator as an active dipole — EMF, internal resistance and the operating point

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

["(1) Measure a battery's terminal voltage open-circuit, then across three different resistors; plot U against I, fit the falling line and read off E (intercept) and r (slope). (2) Add a 200 Ω receiver (or the class's nearest), predict the operating point algebraically, then measure U and I and compare. (3) Feel a small battery warm during a heavy discharge and name the rI² term it demonstrates."]
