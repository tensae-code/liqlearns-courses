# Electric circuits — the complete loop — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Name the four parts of a basic circuit.
**Answer: Source, conductors, load, control (switch).**

**2.** A wire is disconnected at one point. What happens?
**Answer: Current stops everywhere — the circuit is open.**

**3.** Why do diagrams use symbols instead of drawings?
**Answer: Standard symbols let anyone rebuild the same circuit exactly.**

**4.** The lamp in a series loop is removed and the wires joined. The circuit is — ?
**Answer: Closed again — the loop is complete.**
