# Video script — Electric circuits — the complete loop

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

An electric circuit is a complete loop: a source (battery) gives energy, conductors (wires) carry it, a load (a lamp) uses it, and a control (a switch) rules it.
Circuits are drawn as diagrams with standard symbols, so anyone can rebuild the loop from the drawing.
Break the loop anywhere — an open circuit — and the current stops everywhere; close it and current flows all the way round.
One gap is enough to kill the whole circuit.

Remember: **A circuit needs source, conductors, load and control in a complete loop.**
Remember: **Open circuit: a break stops current everywhere. Closed: current flows.**
Remember: **Circuit diagrams use standard symbols.**
