# Hands-on — Electric circuits — the complete loop

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Build the smallest real loop: battery, wire, small bulb. Light it. Break one connection and watch a single gap command the whole circuit.

## Apply it where you live
Draw a symbol diagram of a torch's circuit and label all four parts.
