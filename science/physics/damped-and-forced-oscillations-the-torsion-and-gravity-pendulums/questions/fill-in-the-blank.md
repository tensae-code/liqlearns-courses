# Damped and forced oscillations — the torsion and gravity pendulums — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The torsion pendulum measures moments of _____.
**Answer: inertia**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A gravity pendulum's period is 2π√(L/_____).
**Answer: g**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Friction makes a real oscillator's amplitude _____ away.
**Answer: decay**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Driving an oscillator at its natural frequency produces _____.
**Answer: resonance**
