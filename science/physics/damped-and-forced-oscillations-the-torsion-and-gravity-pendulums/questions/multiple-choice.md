# Damped and forced oscillations — the torsion and gravity pendulums — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The torsion pendulum's restoring torque is…
a) Constant  b) Proportional to the twist, τ = −κθ  c) Proportional to the mass only  d) Zero
**Answer: b) Proportional to the twist, τ = −κθ**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The torsion pendulum's period is…
a) 2π√(κ/I)  b) 2π√(I/κ)  c) 2π√(L/g)  d) κI
**Answer: b) 2π√(I/κ)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A gravity pendulum's period does NOT depend on…
a) Its length  b) g  c) The swinging mass  d) Nothing — it depends on all
**Answer: c) The swinging mass**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) To double a gravity pendulum's period, its length must…
a) Double  b) Quadruple  c) Halve  d) Stay the same
**Answer: b) Quadruple**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Damping makes an oscillator's amplitude…
a) Grow forever  b) Stay constant  c) Decay as energy drains each cycle  d) Double each swing
**Answer: c) Decay as energy drains each cycle**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Resonance happens when the driving frequency…
a) Is zero  b) Matches the natural frequency  c) Is twice any frequency  d) Is random
**Answer: b) Matches the natural frequency**
