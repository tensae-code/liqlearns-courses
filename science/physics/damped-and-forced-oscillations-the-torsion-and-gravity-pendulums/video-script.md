Today: **two pendulums, and the life of every oscillation**. Twist a bar hung on a wire and let go: it twists back and forth with period two-pi root I-over-kappa — the torsion pendulum, a machine for measuring moments of inertia. Swing a mass on a string: period two-pi root L-over-g — the gravity pendulum, a machine for measuring g. Left alone, friction shrinks every swing — that is damping. Push in rhythm with the natural frequency and tiny pushes build huge swings — that is resonance. Measure, drain, drive: one lesson.
