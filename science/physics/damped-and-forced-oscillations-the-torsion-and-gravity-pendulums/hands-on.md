# Hands-on — Damped and forced oscillations — the torsion and gravity pendulums

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Time a homemade gravity pendulum
Tie a heavy key to a string, measure the string's length to the key's centre, and time 20 full swings with a phone timer; divide by 20 for the period. Repeat with the string halved. Check: the shorter pendulum's period should be about √2 ≈ 1.41 times smaller, not half. Then give the key one push per swing, exactly in rhythm, and watch the amplitude grow — resonance with your own hand. Say aloud: the period belongs to the pendulum; the pushes only feed it.
