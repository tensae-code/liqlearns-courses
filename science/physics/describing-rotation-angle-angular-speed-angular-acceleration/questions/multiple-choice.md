# Describing rotation — angle, angular speed, angular acceleration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about rotational kinematics is correct?**
a) Rotation is described in radians: angular displacement Δθ, angular velocity ω, angular acceleration α, with the…  b) A bullet embeds in a suspended wooden block; momentum conservation through the hit plus energy conservation through the…  c) A slide-wire potentiometer compares and measures a cell's emf without drawing current from it, balancing the unknown…
**Answer: a) Rotation is described in radians: angular displacement Δθ, angular velocity ω, angular acceleration α, with…**

**2. Rotation is described in radians: angular displacement Δθ, angular velocity ω, angular ______ α, with the direction of ω and α fixed by the right-hand rule.**
a) acceleration  b) magnet  c) resistance
**Answer: a) acceleration**
