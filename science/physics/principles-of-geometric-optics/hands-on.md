# Hands-on — Principles of geometric optics — independence and reversibility of light rays

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cross them, then send one back
Independence first: cross the beams of two torches in a darkened, slightly dusty room (chalk dust clapped from two board rubbers works). Note where each beam lands before the crossing, then cross them and check: the landing spots do not move — each ray passed through the crossing unchanged. Reversibility next: set a torch so its beam strikes a plane mirror and lands on a marked spot on a card. Without moving the mirror or the card, swap them round — put the torch exactly where the spot was and aim it along the returning line. The beam lands where the torch first stood. Finish by writing the two principles as two testable sentences and the result of each test beside them.
