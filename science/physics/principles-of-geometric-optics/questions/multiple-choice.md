# Principles of geometric optics — independence and reversibility of light rays — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The principle of independence says that rays which cross…
**Answer: a) pass through each other unchanged  b) merge into one ray  c) bend away from each other  d) cancel each other out**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Independence is what allows a ray diagram to be read…
**Answer: a) one ray at a time, each traced alone  b) only all rays together  c) backwards only  d) without straight lines**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The principle of reversibility says a ray sent back along its path will…
**Answer: a) retrace the same path exactly  b) take a shorter new path  c) spread out in all directions  d) stop at the first surface**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) You see a person's eyes in a mirror at the same moment they see yours because…
**Answer: a) the light path between you works in both directions — reversibility  b) mirrors store light  c) eyes emit rays that push  d) the mirror doubles the light**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A finished ray construction cannot be travelled backwards from image to object. You conclude…
**Answer: a) the construction is drawn wrong  b) reversibility has failed  c) the mirror is faulty  d) nothing — backward travel never works**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In Mozambique's PESD I Módulo 4, the principles lesson (Lição 14) is placed…
**Answer: a) after rectilinear propagation and before shadow and penumbra  b) at the very end of the book  c) before light is introduced  d) inside the heat unit**
