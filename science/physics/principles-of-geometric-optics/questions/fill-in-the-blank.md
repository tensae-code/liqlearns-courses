# Principles of geometric optics — independence and reversibility of light rays — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Rays that cross pass through each other unchanged — this is the principle of _____.
**Answer: independence**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A ray sent back along its path retraces it exactly — this is the principle of _____.
**Answer: reversibility**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) On the return journey, a ray meets each surface at the _____ angle as on the way out.
**Answer: same**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A construction that cannot be travelled backwards from image to object is drawn _____.
**Answer: wrong**
