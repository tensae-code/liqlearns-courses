# Elasticity and plasticity — bounce back or stay bent — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A spring returns after stretching. Elastic or plastic?
a) Elastic.  b) Plastic.  c) The greatest deformation from which a body still fully recovers.  d) No — every real body deforms under force.
**Answer: a) Elastic.**

**2.** Clay keeps a fingerprint. Elastic or plastic?
a) Elastic.  b) Plastic.  c) The greatest deformation from which a body still fully recovers.  d) No — every real body deforms under force.
**Answer: b) Plastic.**

**3.** What is the elastic limit?
a) Elastic.  b) Plastic.  c) The greatest deformation from which a body still fully recovers.  d) No — every real body deforms under force.
**Answer: c) The greatest deformation from which a body still fully recovers.**

**4.** Is any real body perfectly rigid?
a) Elastic.  b) Plastic.  c) The greatest deformation from which a body still fully recovers.  d) No — every real body deforms under force.
**Answer: d) No — every real body deforms under force.**
