# Elasticity and plasticity — bounce back or stay bent — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A spring returns after stretching. Elastic or plastic?
**Answer: Elastic.**

**2.** Clay keeps a fingerprint. Elastic or plastic?
**Answer: Plastic.**

**3.** What is the elastic limit?
**Answer: The greatest deformation from which a body still fully recovers.**

**4.** Is any real body perfectly rigid?
**Answer: No — every real body deforms under force.**
