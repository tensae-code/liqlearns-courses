# Elasticity and plasticity — bounce back or stay bent — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1.** A deforming force changes a body's shape or size.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Elastic bodies recover when the force is removed; plastic bodies keep the change.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Elastic bodies keep their new shape after the force is removed.
**Answer: False**
