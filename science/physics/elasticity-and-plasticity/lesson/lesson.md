# Elasticity and plasticity — bounce back or stay bent

## Do it first
Stretch a rubber band gently and release — recovery. Then fold a strip of clay or soft wire — it stays. Two materials, two verdicts, one minute.

## Elasticity and plasticity — bounce back or stay bent — the teaching
No real body is perfectly rigid: a deforming force changes its shape or size. An elastic body recovers its shape when the force is removed — a released spring returns. A plastic body keeps the change — bent clay stays bent. The elastic limit is the boundary between the two behaviours: stretch an elastic material past it and it stops recovering fully.

## The key ideas

- A deforming force changes a body's shape or size.
- Elastic bodies recover when the force is removed; plastic bodies keep the change.
- The elastic limit marks where full recovery ends.
