# Video script — Elasticity and plasticity — bounce back or stay bent

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

No real body is perfectly rigid: a deforming force changes its shape or size.
An elastic body recovers its shape when the force is removed — a released spring returns.
A plastic body keeps the change — bent clay stays bent.
The elastic limit is the boundary between the two behaviours: stretch an elastic material past it and it stops recovering fully.

Remember: **A deforming force changes a body's shape or size.**
Remember: **Elastic bodies recover when the force is removed; plastic bodies keep the change.**
Remember: **The elastic limit marks where full recovery ends.**
