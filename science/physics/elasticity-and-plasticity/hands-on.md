# Hands-on — Elasticity and plasticity — bounce back or stay bent

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Stretch a rubber band gently and release — recovery. Then fold a strip of clay or soft wire — it stays. Two materials, two verdicts, one minute.

## Apply it where you live
Classify six household materials as mainly elastic or plastic under hand forces.
