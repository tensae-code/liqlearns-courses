# Magnetic properties of substances — dia-, para- and ferromagnetism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does no substance escape diamagnetism?
**Answer: The field acts on the orbital motion of electrons in every atom, inducing a moment against the field — so the weak opposing response is universal.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Aluminium is pulled only weakly to a magnet, and keeps nothing. Name the behaviour and the reason.
**Answer: Paramagnetism: its atoms' permanent moments align only partly with the field, and they randomise again when the field is removed.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What is remanence, and which device depends on it?
**Answer: The magnetisation that stays after the field is removed; a permanent magnet depends on it (as does magnetic recording).**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why is soft iron chosen for a transformer core rather than hard steel?
**Answer: It magnetises and demagnetises easily with little energy lost per cycle, following the alternating field; hard steel would resist the reversal and waste energy as heat.**
