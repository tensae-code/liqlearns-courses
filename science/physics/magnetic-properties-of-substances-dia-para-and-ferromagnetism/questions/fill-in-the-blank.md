# Magnetic properties of substances — dia-, para- and ferromagnetism — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Bismuth is the classic _____ substance.
**Answer: diamagnetic**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A paramagnet's susceptibility is small and _____.
**Answer: positive**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Iron's Curie temperature is about _____ °C.
**Answer: 770**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Magnetisation that stays after the field leaves is called _____.
**Answer: remanence**
