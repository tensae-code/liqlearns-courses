# Magnetic properties of substances — dia-, para- and ferromagnetism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Diamagnetism is found in…
a) Iron only  b) Every substance  c) Gases only  d) No real substance
**Answer: b) Every substance**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A diamagnetic material in a field is…
a) Pulled strongly in  b) Magnetised weakly against the field and pushed toward the weaker field  c) Permanently magnetised  d) Unaffected in any way
**Answer: b) Magnetised weakly against the field and pushed toward the weaker field**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Paramagnetism weakens as temperature rises because…
a) The atoms dissolve  b) Thermal shaking breaks up the alignment of the atomic moments  c) The field weakens  d) Domains melt
**Answer: b) Thermal shaking breaks up the alignment of the atomic moments**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) When the field is removed, a paramagnet…
a) Stays fully magnetised  b) Keeps half its magnetisation  c) Loses its magnetisation as the moments randomise  d) Becomes diamagnetic
**Answer: c) Loses its magnetisation as the moments randomise**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A ferromagnetic material's moments are pre-aligned in regions called…
a) Shells  b) Domains  c) Cells  d) Grains of charge
**Answer: b) Domains**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Above the Curie temperature, a ferromagnet…
a) Melts  b) Becomes merely paramagnetic as domain order is destroyed  c) Becomes a superconductor  d) Doubles its magnetisation
**Answer: b) Becomes merely paramagnetic as domain order is destroyed**
