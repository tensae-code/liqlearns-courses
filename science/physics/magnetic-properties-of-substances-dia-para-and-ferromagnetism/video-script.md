# Video script — Magnetic properties of substances — dia-, para- and ferromagnetism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **three answers to a magnet**. Diamagnets — every substance — push weakly against the field and forget it instantly. Paramagnets pull weakly toward it while their atomic moments partly line up, and forget it just as fast. Ferromagnets are the drama: whole domains of moments swing into line, the field is multiplied a thousandfold, magnetisation stays when the field leaves — until heat past the Curie point shakes the domains apart. Iron, nickel, cobalt: the short list that runs every motor and transformer on Earth.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
