# Wave words — period, frequency, wavelength, amplitude — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A wave has frequency 5 Hz and wavelength 2 m. Its speed?
a) v = fλ = 5 × 2 = 10 m/s.  b) f = 1/T = 1 ÷ 0.25 = 4 Hz.  c) The greatest displacement from the rest position.  d) Two matching points — e.g. crest to crest or trough to trough.
**Answer: a) v = fλ = 5 × 2 = 10 m/s.**

**Answer: b) f = 1/T = 1 ÷ 0.25 = 4 Hz.**
