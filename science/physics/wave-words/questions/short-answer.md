# Wave words — period, frequency, wavelength, amplitude — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A wave has frequency 5 Hz and wavelength 2 m. Its speed?
**Answer: v = fλ = 5 × 2 = 10 m/s.**

**2.** An oscillation has period 0.25 s. Frequency?
**Answer: f = 1/T = 1 ÷ 0.25 = 4 Hz.**

**3.** What is amplitude?
**Answer: The greatest displacement from the rest position.**

**4.** Wavelength is measured between which points?
**Answer: Two matching points — e.g. crest to crest or trough to trough.**
