## Wave words — period, frequency, wavelength, amplitude — the teaching
Waves speak in a small, exact vocabulary. The period T is the time for one complete oscillation; frequency f is how many complete oscillations happen each second, measured in hertz (Hz), with f = 1/T. Amplitude is the greatest displacement from rest. On a travelling wave, the wavelength λ is the distance between two matching points — crest to crest, trough to trough. Wave speed joins them all: v = fλ.

**Keep the formula:** v = fλ; f = 1 / T

## The key ideas

- Period is the time for one complete oscillation; frequency is oscillations per second, in hertz.
- f = 1/T, and wave speed v = fλ.
- Amplitude is the greatest displacement from rest; wavelength is crest to crest.

## Say it to your companion
Explain wave words — period, frequency, wavelength, amplitude to your companion in your own words. Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
