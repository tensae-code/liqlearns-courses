# Hands-on — Wave words — period, frequency, wavelength, amplitude

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Swing a pendulum or bounce a mass and count: time 10 swings, divide by 10 — that is a period measured with your own hands.

## Apply it where you live
Measure the period and frequency of a swinging object at home, timing 10 oscillations.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21851 — wave train with crest, trough, wavelength and amplitude all measured] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using v = fλ; f = 1 / T: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
