# Video script — Wave words — period, frequency, wavelength, amplitude

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Waves speak in a small, exact vocabulary.
The period T is the time for one complete oscillation; frequency f is how many complete oscillations happen each second, measured in hertz (Hz), with f = 1/T.
Amplitude is the greatest displacement from rest.
On a travelling wave, the wavelength λ is the distance between two matching points — crest to crest, trough to trough.
Wave speed joins them all: v = fλ.

Remember: **Period is the time for one complete oscillation; frequency is oscillations per second, in hertz.**
Remember: **f = 1/T, and wave speed v = fλ.**
Remember: **Amplitude is the greatest displacement from rest; wavelength is crest to crest.**
Keep the formula: **v = fλ; f = 1 / T**
