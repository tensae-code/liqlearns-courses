# Lesson plan — Wave words — period, frequency, wavelength, amplitude

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** Wave words — period, frequency, wavelength, amplitude
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Mechanical Oscillation and Sound Wave — Grade 9 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Period is the time for one complete oscillation; frequency is oscillations per second, in hertz.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 9 lessons before it in Unit 6 and on everyday observation; it needs nothing else to start.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Period is the time for one complete oscillation; frequency is oscillations per second, in hertz.
2. f = 1/T, and wave speed v = fλ.
3. Amplitude is the greatest displacement from rest; wavelength is crest to crest.
4. Answer the lesson's questions, including any calculation, with working shown.

## If the learner is stuck
- Cannot start → return to Do it first and do it with real objects, slowly, naming each step out loud.
- Mixes up the key ideas → go back to the key-ideas list in lesson/lesson.md and test each one against the picture [Picture 21851].
- Calculation slips → the formula is v = fλ; f = 1 / T; write the knowns first, substitute, then compute, keeping units at every step.
