# Hands-on — Adding and subtracting vectors in a line

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Take 5 steps east, 2 steps west. Your body did signed addition. Write it as numbers with east as + and check the sum against where you stand.

## Apply it where you live
Solve three collinear problems from daily journeys, stating the positive direction first each time.

Study [Picture 21875 — two arrows nose-to-tail on one line making one longer or shorter arrow] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
