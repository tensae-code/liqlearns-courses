# Adding and subtracting vectors in a line — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1.** Choose a positive direction; vectors along it are +, against it −.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The signed sum is the resultant; its sign gives direction.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Vectors in a line are added by adding their sizes whatever their directions.
**Answer: False**
