# Adding and subtracting vectors in a line — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** East is +. Forces: +30 N and −12 N. Resultant?
**Answer: 30 − 12 = +18 N — 18 N east.**

**2.** Displacements: +5 m, +3 m, −10 m. Resultant?
**Answer: 5 + 3 − 10 = −2 m — 2 m in the negative direction.**

**3.** Subtract: 7 m east minus 4 m east.
**Answer: 7 − 4 = 3 m east.**

**4.** What does a negative resultant tell you?
**Answer: The resultant points in the chosen negative direction.**
