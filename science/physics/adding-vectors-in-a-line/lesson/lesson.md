# Adding and subtracting vectors in a line

## Do it first
Take 5 steps east, 2 steps west. Your body did signed addition. Write it as numbers with east as + and check the sum against where you stand.

[Picture 21875 — two arrows nose-to-tail on one line making one longer or shorter arrow]

## Adding and subtracting vectors in a line — the teaching
Vectors on one line add algebraically. Choose a positive direction, give vectors along it a plus sign and vectors against it a minus sign, and add the signed values. The sign of the answer gives the resultant's direction; its size gives the magnitude. Subtraction is the same game: subtracting a vector means reversing it and adding — change the sign, then sum.

## The key ideas

- Choose a positive direction; vectors along it are +, against it −.
- The signed sum is the resultant; its sign gives direction.
- Subtracting a vector = adding its reverse.

## Say it to your companion
Explain adding and subtracting vectors in a line to your companion in your own words. Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
