# Video script — Adding and subtracting vectors in a line

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Vectors on one line add algebraically.
Choose a positive direction, give vectors along it a plus sign and vectors against it a minus sign, and add the signed values.
The sign of the answer gives the resultant's direction; its size gives the magnitude.
Subtraction is the same game: subtracting a vector means reversing it and adding — change the sign, then sum.

Remember: **Choose a positive direction; vectors along it are +, against it −.**
Remember: **The signed sum is the resultant; its sign gives direction.**
Remember: **Subtracting a vector = adding its reverse.**
