# Self-induction and mutual induction — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Self-induction is the EMF induced…
a) In a nearby coil by a magnet  b) In a coil by the change of its own current and flux  c) In a wire by Earth's field  d) Only in iron cores
**Answer: b) In a coil by the change of its own current and flux**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A steady direct current in a coil induces…
a) A constant EMF  b) An EMF proportional to the current  c) No EMF — only a changing flux induces  d) An EMF that grows with time
**Answer: c) No EMF — only a changing flux induces**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A coil has L = 2 H. A current changing at 3 A/s induces an EMF of…
a) 1.5 V  b) 5 V  c) 6 V  d) 0.67 V
**Answer: c) 6 V**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The energy stored in an inductor carrying current I is…
a) ½L I  b) L I²  c) ½L I²  d) ½I²/L
**Answer: c) ½L I²**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Mutual induction requires…
a) The coils to touch electrically  b) Part of one coil's flux to link the other, and that flux to change  c) A permanent magnet between the coils  d) Direct current only
**Answer: b) Part of one coil's flux to link the other, and that flux to change**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The induction coil produces its very high secondary voltage by…
a) A very large battery  b) Chopping the primary current so rapid flux changes induce EMF in a secondary of very many turns  c) Electrostatic charging  d) Rectifying AC
**Answer: b) Chopping the primary current so rapid flux changes induce EMF in a secondary of very many turns**
