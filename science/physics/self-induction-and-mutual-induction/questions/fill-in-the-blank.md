# Self-induction and mutual induction — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The EMF of self-induction is ε = −L × (rate of change of _____).
**Answer: current**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The unit of inductance, one volt per ampere-per-second, is the _____.
**Answer: henry**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Energy stored in a coil's magnetic field is U = ½L _____.
**Answer: I²**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Mutual induction is quantified by the _____ inductance M, also in henrys.
**Answer: mutual**
