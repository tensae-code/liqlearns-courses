Today: **self-induction and mutual induction**. A coil whose current changes induces a voltage in itself, fighting the change — that is self-induction, priced by the inductance in henrys, ε = −L dI/dt. The energy is stored in the field and paid back when you switch off: that is the spark. Let a second coil share the field and the change induces a voltage there too — mutual induction, the secret of the induction coil and the transformer.
