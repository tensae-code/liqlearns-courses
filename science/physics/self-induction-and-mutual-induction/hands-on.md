# Hands-on — Self-induction and mutual induction

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The switch-off flash
Wire a coil (or a small transformer primary) in series with a torch lamp and a battery. Close the circuit and note the lamp's normal brightness; open it and watch the flash at break. Repeat with an iron core in the coil and compare. Say aloud the rule: the flash is the collapsing field paying back U = ½LI² — a bigger L stores more, and pays back harder.

## Induction with no connection
Place a second coil near the first, its ends to a sensitive galvanometer (or LED pair). Make and break the first coil's circuit and watch the needle kick at both moments — and sit still while the current merely flows. Say aloud the rule: only change induces; the second coil answers the field's change, not its presence.
