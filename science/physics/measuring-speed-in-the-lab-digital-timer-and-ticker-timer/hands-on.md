# Hands-on — Measuring speed in the lab — digital timer and ticker timer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Time the same run three ways
Roll a toy car down the same ramp three times, timing a fixed 1 m stretch near the bottom with a stopwatch. Write the three times down: they will differ, because human reaction time (~0.2 s) sits inside every one. Now average them and work out the speed. Then imagine the same stretch timed by two photogates: no thumb, no reaction time — the class discussion is exactly this: which of your three stopwatch times would the gates have beaten, and why does the average of three human timings come closer to the gate's answer?
