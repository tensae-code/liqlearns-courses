# Video script — Measuring speed in the lab — digital timer and ticker timer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **two machines that time motion honestly**. A digital timer's light gates clock a card through one gate — width divided by blocking time is the speed right there — or between two gates for an average. A ticker timer stamps 50 dots a second on a tape the moving object pulls: wide-spaced dots, fast; crowded dots, slow. The tape and the timer turn 'it looked fast' into a number you can defend.
