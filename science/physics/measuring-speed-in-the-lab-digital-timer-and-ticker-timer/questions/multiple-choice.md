# Measuring speed in the lab — digital timer and ticker timer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A photogate timer in mode A↔B measures…
**Answer: a) the time for the object to travel between two gates  b) the period of a pendulum  c) the width of the card  d) the mains frequency**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A card 2.0 cm wide blocks a gate for 0.040 s. Its speed at the gate is…
**Answer: a) 0.50 m/s — speed = width ÷ blocking time = 0.020 ÷ 0.040  b) 2.0 m/s  c) 0.08 m/s  d) 5.0 m/s**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A ticker timer at 50 Hz prints one dot every…
**Answer: a) 0.02 s — the period of a 50 Hz vibration  b) 0.50 s  c) 2 s  d) 50 s**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) On a ticker tape, dots that spread further and further apart show…
**Answer: a) the object speeding up — each equal 0.02 s carries it further  b) the object slowing down  c) constant speed  d) a faulty timer**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Ten tape gaps span 24 cm on a 50 Hz tape. The average speed is…
**Answer: a) 1.2 m/s — 0.24 m in 10 × 0.02 = 0.20 s  b) 0.24 m/s  c) 12 m/s  d) 0.12 m/s**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A measured speed is honest lab work when it…
**Answer: a) names the method and the stretch it was measured over, from repeated runs  b) has the most decimal places  c) comes from a single run  d) matches the expected answer**
