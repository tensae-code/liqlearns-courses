# Measuring speed in the lab — digital timer and ticker timer — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A photogate starts and stops the timer when its light _____ is blocked.
**Answer: beam**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) At 50 Hz the time between neighbouring ticker-tape dots is _____ s.
**Answer: 0.02**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Crowded dots on a ticker tape mean the object was moving _____.
**Answer: slowly**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Two gates give the _____ speed between them, not the speed at a point.
**Answer: average**
