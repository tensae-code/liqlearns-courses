# AC circuits — RC and RL dipoles — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner states what a dipole is and why AC answers with reactance and phase rather than resistance alone, works X_C = 1/(2πfC) and X_L = 2πfL in numbers, gives the phase of each dipole (current leads in RC, lags in RL) with its reason, and prices a series dipole with Z = √(R² + X²) and I = V/Z, checking the account by V² = V_R² + V_X², answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — doing the lesson's reactance and impedance accounts by hand, naming each idea aloud, and reasoning about new examples until the teaching is the learner's own.
