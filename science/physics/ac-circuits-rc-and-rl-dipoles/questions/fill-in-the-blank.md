# AC circuits — RC and RL dipoles — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The number of full AC cycles in one second is the _____ , measured in hertz.
**Answer: frequency**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The opposition a capacitor offers to alternating current is called capacitive _____ .
**Answer: reactance**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In an RC dipole the current _____ the voltage by a quarter of a cycle.
**Answer: leads**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) In an RL dipole the current _____ the voltage by a quarter of a cycle.
**Answer: lags**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The total opposition of a series dipole, combining resistance and reactance by squares, is its _____ .
**Answer: impedance**
