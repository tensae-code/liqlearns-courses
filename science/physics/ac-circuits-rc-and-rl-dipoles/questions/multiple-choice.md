# AC circuits — RC and RL dipoles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A dipole is…
a) A magnet with two poles  b) Any two-terminal part the current must pass through  c) A pair of batteries  d) A double switch
**Answer: b) Any two-terminal part the current must pass through**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The frequency of a 50 Hz mains supply means…
a) 50 volts  b) 50 amperes  c) 50 full cycles every second  d) 50 switches in the house
**Answer: c) 50 full cycles every second**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The capacitive reactance X_C = 1/(2πfC)…
a) Rises as the frequency rises  b) Falls as the frequency rises  c) Never changes  d) Equals the capacitance
**Answer: b) Falls as the frequency rises**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In an RC dipole, the current…
a) Lags the voltage by 90°  b) Is exactly in step with the voltage  c) Leads the voltage by 90°  d) Stops completely
**Answer: c) Leads the voltage by 90°**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In an RL dipole, the current lags the voltage because…
a) The wire is too long  b) The inductor fights every change of current with its back-EMF  c) The resistor heats up  d) The supply is weak
**Answer: b) The inductor fights every change of current with its back-EMF**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A series dipole has R = 30 Ω and reactance X = 40 Ω. Its impedance is…
a) 70 Ω  b) 10 Ω  c) 50 Ω  d) 1,200 Ω
**Answer: c) 50 Ω**
