Today: **alternating current, and the two parts that answer it out of step**.

Mains current does not flow one way — it swings, back and forth, fifty times a second. Push that swinging current through a plain resistor and the two swing together. But push it through a capacitor and the current rushes in *before* the voltage builds — the current **leads**. Push it through a coil of wire and the coil fights every change, so the current trails behind — the current **lags**. Each part opposes the swing with its own **reactance**: the capacitor's fades as the swing speeds up, the coil's grows. And when a resistor shares the circuit, the oppositions join like the sides of a right triangle — that total is the **impedance**, and it, not resistance alone, sets how much current flows.
