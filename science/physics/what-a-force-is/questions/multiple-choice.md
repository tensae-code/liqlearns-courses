# What a force is — pushes, pulls and weight — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What instrument measures force?
a) A spring balance.  b) The newton (N).  c) Non-contact — it acts across a gap.  d) Toward the centre of the Earth.
**Answer: a) A spring balance.**

**2.** What is the unit of force?
a) A spring balance.  b) The newton (N).  c) Non-contact — it acts across a gap.  d) Toward the centre of the Earth.
**Answer: b) The newton (N).**

**3.** Is gravity a contact or non-contact force?
a) A spring balance.  b) The newton (N).  c) Non-contact — it acts across a gap.  d) Toward the centre of the Earth.
**Answer: c) Non-contact — it acts across a gap.**

**4.** Toward where is weight directed?
a) A spring balance.  b) The newton (N).  c) Non-contact — it acts across a gap.  d) Toward the centre of the Earth.
**Answer: d) Toward the centre of the Earth.**
