# What a force is — pushes, pulls and weight — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** What instrument measures force?
**Answer: A spring balance.**

**2.** What is the unit of force?
**Answer: The newton (N).**

**3.** Is gravity a contact or non-contact force?
**Answer: Non-contact — it acts across a gap.**

**4.** Toward where is weight directed?
**Answer: Toward the centre of the Earth.**
