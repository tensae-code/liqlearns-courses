# What a force is — pushes, pulls and weight

## Do it first
Push a box, pull a rope, stretch a rubber band, squash a sponge. Four forces, four different results — name what each force did.

## What a force is — pushes, pulls and weight — the teaching
A force is a push or a pull. A force can start a body moving, stop it, speed it up, slow it down, change its direction, or deform it — squash or stretch its shape. Contact forces touch the body; non-contact forces act across a gap, like gravity and magnetism. Force is measured in newtons (N) with a spring balance. Weight is a special force: the gravitational pull on a body, directed toward the centre of the Earth.

## The key ideas

- A force is a push or a pull, measured in newtons.
- Force can start, stop, speed, slow, redirect or deform a body.
- Weight is the gravitational force on a body, directed toward Earth's centre.
