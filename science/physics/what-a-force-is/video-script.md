# Video script — What a force is — pushes, pulls and weight

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

A force is a push or a pull.
A force can start a body moving, stop it, speed it up, slow it down, change its direction, or deform it — squash or stretch its shape.
Contact forces touch the body; non-contact forces act across a gap, like gravity and magnetism.
Force is measured in newtons (N) with a spring balance.
Weight is a special force: the gravitational pull on a body, directed toward the centre of the Earth.

Remember: **A force is a push or a pull, measured in newtons.**
Remember: **Force can start, stop, speed, slow, redirect or deform a body.**
Remember: **Weight is the gravitational force on a body, directed toward Earth's centre.**
