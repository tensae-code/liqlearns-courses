# Hands-on — What a force is — pushes, pulls and weight

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Push a box, pull a rope, stretch a rubber band, squash a sponge. Four forces, four different results — name what each force did.

## Apply it where you live
List five forces acting around your home right now and say what each one does.
