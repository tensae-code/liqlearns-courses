# Hands-on — Waves reflect, refract and diffract

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three behaviours in one tray
Use a ripple tank (or a shallow tray with a vibrating dipper and a steady hand). (1) Reflection: place a straight barrier at an angle, send straight waves, and measure incidence and reflection angles against a drawn normal — check their equality at two different angles. (2) Refraction: lay a glass plate in half the tank to make a shallow region; send waves across the border first square-on (watch the wavelength shorten, direction unchanged) then at an angle (watch the bend). (3) Diffraction: set two barriers with an adjustable gap; start wide, then narrow toward the wavelength, sketching the pattern at three gap widths. Record each behaviour with its rule in one sentence — the same three sentences the Selective Compulsory 1 book prints at §3.3.
