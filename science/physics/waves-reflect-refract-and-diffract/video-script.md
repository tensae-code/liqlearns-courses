# Video script — Waves reflect, refract and diffract

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **waves reflect, refract and diffract**. Off a barrier they bounce with equal angles; into shallower water they slow, shorten and bend; through a narrow gap they spread like a new source. Bounce, bend, spread — a wave's manners at every border.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
