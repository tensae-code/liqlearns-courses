# Waves reflect, refract and diffract — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Angle of reflection equals angle of _____.
**Answer: incidence**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) At a border the wave's _____ never changes.
**Answer: frequency**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Waves spreading through a gap are showing _____.
**Answer: diffraction**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Diffraction is strongest when the gap is about a _____ wide.
**Answer: wavelength**
