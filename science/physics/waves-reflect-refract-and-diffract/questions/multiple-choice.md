# Waves reflect, refract and diffract — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) On reflection, a wave keeps its…
**Answer: a) wavelength, frequency and speed — only direction changes  b) direction  c) nothing  d) medium**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The reflection law for waves says…
**Answer: a) angle of reflection = angle of incidence  b) angles double  c) the wave stops  d) the wave speeds up**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) At a refraction border, the quantity that never changes is…
**Answer: a) frequency  b) wavelength  c) speed  d) direction**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Water waves entering shallow water…
**Answer: a) slow down, shorten in wavelength, and bend toward the normal if angled  b) speed up  c) keep their wavelength  d) stop**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Diffraction is strongest when…
**Answer: a) the gap is about a wavelength wide  b) the gap is huge  c) the wave is fastest  d) the barrier is soft**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Sea waves spreading into all corners inside a harbour mouth show…
**Answer: a) diffraction at the gap  b) reflection only  c) refraction only  d) interference**
