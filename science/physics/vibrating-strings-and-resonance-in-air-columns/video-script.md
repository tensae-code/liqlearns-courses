# Video script — Vibrating strings and resonance in air columns

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **strings and air columns sing by segments**. A string fixed at both ends vibrates in equal segments — shorten it and the note climbs. A closed pipe fits a quarter-wave and gives odd harmonics only; an open pipe fits a half-wave and gives them all. Every string and wind instrument you have ever heard is those two fits, built in wood and metal.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
