# Vibrating strings and resonance in air columns — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The still points in a vibrating string are called _____.
**Answer: nodes**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lowest note of a string is its _____.
**Answer: fundamental**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A closed column fits a _____-wavelength in its simplest pattern.
**Answer: quarter**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) An open column sounds the _____ harmonic series.
**Answer: full**
