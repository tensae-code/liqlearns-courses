# Vibrating strings and resonance in air columns — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A string fixed at both ends vibrates in…
a) One loose loop only  b) Segments, with nodes at the fixed ends  c) Random curves  d) No pattern
**Answer: b) Segments, with nodes at the fixed ends**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Shortening a stretched string makes its note…
a) Lower  b) Higher  c) Unchanged  d) Silent
**Answer: b) Higher**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) At the closed end of an air column there is a…
a) Antinode  b) Node — the air cannot move there  c) Speaker  d) Valve
**Answer: b) Node — the air cannot move there**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A closed air column resounds at…
a) All harmonics  b) Odd harmonics only  c) Even harmonics only  d) One frequency with no harmonics
**Answer: b) Odd harmonics only**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) An open air column's simplest pattern fits…
a) A quarter-wavelength  b) A half-wavelength  c) Two wavelengths  d) No wavelength
**Answer: b) A half-wavelength**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Musical instruments, in this lesson's terms, are…
a) Magic  b) Strings and pipes sized so their segments and columns give the wanted notes  c) Only electronic  d) Unrelated to physics
**Answer: b) Strings and pipes sized so their segments and columns give the wanted notes**
