# Hands-on — Vibrating strings and resonance in air columns

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Bottle pipes — measure resonance with your ears
Line up four bottles and pour different depths of water into them, leaving air columns of clearly different lengths. Blow steadily across each mouth (or tap the bottle and listen) and order the notes from lowest to highest. Write the order of the air-column lengths beside the order of the notes and state the rule your ears found. Then add water to the lowest bottle until its note matches its neighbour — you have tuned an instrument by changing a column length, exactly as the lesson describes.
