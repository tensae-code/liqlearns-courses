# Experiment — measuring a metal's resistivity — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Resistivity's symbol is the Greek letter _____.
**Answer: ρ**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The diameter gauge reads to _____ millimetres.
**Answer: 0.01**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Area depends on the _____ of the diameter.
**Answer: square**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Extract ρ by computing R × A ÷ _____.
**Answer: L**
