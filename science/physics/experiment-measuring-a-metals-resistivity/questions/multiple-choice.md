# Experiment — measuring a metal's resistivity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Resistivity is defined by…
**Answer: a) R = ρL/A  b) R = ρA/L  c) ρ = RLA  d) V = ρI**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The unit of resistivity is the…
**Answer: a) ohm-metre (Ω·m)  b) ohm  c) ohm per metre  d) ampere**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The wire's diameter is measured with a micrometer because…
**Answer: a) it is a fraction of a millimetre and its error counts double through d²  b) rulers are banned  c) it is very long  d) it is hot**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Diameter readings are taken at several places and averaged because…
**Answer: a) real wires are neither perfectly round nor even  b) the gauge is broken  c) time must be filled  d) the wire moves**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The length L in ρ = RA/L is…
**Answer: a) the length between the voltmeter's contacts  b) the whole reel  c) the wire's diameter  d) always 1 m by law**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The measuring current is kept small and brief so that…
**Answer: a) the wire does not warm and change its resistivity mid-measurement  b) the battery lasts  c) the meters stay cold  d) the wire glows nicely**
