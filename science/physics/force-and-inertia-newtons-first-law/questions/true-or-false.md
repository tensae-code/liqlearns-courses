# Force and inertia — Newton's first law — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. Force as push/pull that changes motion or shape; inertia is the resistance to change, measured by mass; the first law states the natural state of uniform motion and is argued from everyday examples (seat belts and airbags appear as safety applications in the section's questions).**
**Answer: True**

**2. Two cans hang from a beam, one filled with sand, one empty; pupils give each a slight push to start it swinging, then try to stop the already-swinging cans, comparing the resistance felt in starting and stopping — mass as inertia, experienced by hand.**
**Answer: True**
