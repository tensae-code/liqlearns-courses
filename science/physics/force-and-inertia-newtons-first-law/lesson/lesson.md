## Force and Newton's first law — inertia

Force as push/pull that changes motion or shape; inertia is the resistance to change, measured by mass; the first law states the natural state of uniform motion and is argued from everyday examples (seat belts and airbags appear as safety applications in the section's questions).

Two cans hang from a beam, one filled with sand, one empty; pupils give each a slight push to start it swinging, then try to stop the already-swinging cans, comparing the resistance felt in starting and stopping — mass as inertia, experienced by hand.
