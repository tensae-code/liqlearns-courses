# Hands-on — Force on a current-carrying conductor and torque on a coil

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The jumping wire
A teacher sets this up and switches: a loose length of wire (or foil strip) hangs between the poles of a strong horseshoe magnet, connected through a switch to a low-voltage supply. Close the switch — the wire kicks sideways. Reverse the battery leads and close again — it kicks the other way. Turn the magnet over and repeat. Three kicks, three direction calls: make each call with your left hand before you switch, and let the wire mark you right or wrong.
