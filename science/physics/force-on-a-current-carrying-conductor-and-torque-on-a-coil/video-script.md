# Video script — Force on a current-carrying conductor and torque on a coil

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **a field pushes a current sideways**. The force is F = BIL sinθ, the left hand reads its direction, and along the field it vanishes. Wind the wire into a coil and the two sides are pushed opposite ways — a turning couple. Add a commutator and you have built the motor.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
