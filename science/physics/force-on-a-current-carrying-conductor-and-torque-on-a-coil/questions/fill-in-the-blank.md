# Force on a current-carrying conductor and torque on a coil — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) F = BIL sin_____.
**Answer: θ (theta)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Currents in the same direction in parallel wires _____ each other.
**Answer: attract**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The turning effect on a coil is called its _____ (torque).
**Answer: moment**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Without a commutator, the coil in a meter settles across the field — the moving-coil _____.
**Answer: galvanometer**
