# Force on a current-carrying conductor and torque on a coil — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A 0.2 m wire carries 3 A across a 0.5 T field. Find the force.
**Answer: F = BIL = 0.5 × 3 × 0.2 = 0.3 N.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does reversing both the current and the field leave the force unchanged?
**Answer: Each reversal flips the force once; two flips cancel, so the force points the same way as before.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why is a coil's torque zero when its plane stands across the field?
**Answer: The two side forces then pull along the same line through the axis — equal, opposite and in line, so their turning effects cancel (sinθ = 0 in τ = NIBA sinθ).**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Name the two school instruments/machines this one law runs.
**Answer: The electric motor (coil + commutator) and the moving-coil galvanometer/meter (coil settling against a spring).**
