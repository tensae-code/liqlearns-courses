# Force on a current-carrying conductor and torque on a coil — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The force on a conductor is largest when the conductor is…
a) across the field (θ = 90°)  b) along the field (θ = 0°)  c) at 45° to the field  d) carrying no current
**Answer: a) across the field (θ = 90°)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Fleming's left-hand rule assigns the thumb to…
a) the force (motion)  b) the field  c) the current  d) the voltage
**Answer: a) the force (motion)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Doubling both the current and the length in the field multiplies the force by…
a) 4  b) 2  c) 1 (unchanged)  d) 8
**Answer: a) 4**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A conductor lying along the field lines, carrying a large current, feels…
a) no force  b) the maximum force  c) a force toward the north pole  d) a turning moment only
**Answer: a) no force**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Two parallel wires carrying currents in opposite directions…
a) repel each other  b) attract each other  c) feel nothing  d) both stop carrying current
**Answer: a) repel each other**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A motor keeps turning because its commutator…
a) reverses the coil's current each half-turn  b) increases the field  c) removes the coil's resistance  d) stops the torque at the top
**Answer: a) reverses the coil's current each half-turn**
