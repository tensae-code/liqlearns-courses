# Hands-on — Surface tension and capillarity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The floating clip
Float a dry paper clip on still water using the tissue trick, then touch one drop of soapy water to the surface away from the clip and watch it sink. Say aloud: soap weakened the surface skin — surface tension was holding the clip, not floating it like a boat.

## The climbing towel
Hold three strips — a wide strip of paper towel, a narrow strip, and a strip of plastic — with their ends in coloured water. Time how high the colour climbs in each in one minute. Say aloud: the liquid climbs in the paper's fine spaces and not in the plastic it does not wet — narrow, wetted spaces climb highest.
