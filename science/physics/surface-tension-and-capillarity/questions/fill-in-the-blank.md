# Surface tension and capillarity — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The pulls between neighbouring particles are called _____ forces.
**Answer: intermolecular**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The pull of a liquid's particles for each other is called _____.
**Answer: cohesion**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The pull of a liquid's particles for the wall of a tube is called _____.
**Answer: adhesion**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The curved surface of water in a tube is called the _____.
**Answer: meniscus**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Mercury in a glass capillary is pushed _____ the outside level because cohesion beats adhesion.
**Answer: below**
