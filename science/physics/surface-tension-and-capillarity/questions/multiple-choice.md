# Surface tension and capillarity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Surface tension happens because surface particles of a liquid…
a) Have no neighbours at all  b) Are pulled inward and sideways, with no pull from above  c) Are heavier than inner particles  d) Turn into gas
**Answer: b) Are pulled inward and sideways, with no pull from above**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A small water drop pulls itself into a sphere because a sphere…
a) Holds the most water  b) Has the least surface for its volume, and the tight surface pulls it round  c) Is the heaviest shape  d) Freezes fastest
**Answer: b) Has the least surface for its volume, and the tight surface pulls it round**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Detergent added to water usually…
a) Raises surface tension  b) Lowers surface tension  c) Turns water to mercury  d) Stops all forces
**Answer: b) Lowers surface tension**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Water rises in a narrow glass tube mainly because…
a) Air pressure pushes it  b) Adhesion to the glass beats cohesion, and the tight surface pulls the column up  c) The tube is magnetic  d) Water expands
**Answer: b) Adhesion to the glass beats cohesion, and the tight surface pulls the column up**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The narrower the capillary tube, the…
a) Lower the rise  b) Higher the rise  c) Same the rise  d) Wider the meniscus only
**Answer: b) Higher the rise**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Oil climbing a lamp wick is an example of…
a) Evaporation  b) Capillarity through the wick's fine spaces  c) Convection  d) Radiation
**Answer: b) Capillarity through the wick's fine spaces**
