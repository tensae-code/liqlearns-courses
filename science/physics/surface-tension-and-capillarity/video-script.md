Today: **surface tension and capillarity**.

Water has a skin. The particles at its surface are pulled inward by their neighbours, and that tight skin carries a paper clip and pulls drops round. Put a thin tube in water and the skin, helped by the water sticking to the glass, hauls the water up the tube — higher in a thinner tube. That climb, capillarity, lifts oil in a wick and water through soil to roots.
