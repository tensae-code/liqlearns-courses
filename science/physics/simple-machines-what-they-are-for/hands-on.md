# Hands-on — Simple machines — what they are for

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Lift a heavy book with your hand alone, then slide it up a ramp of stacked books. Same load, easier effort — a machine has been at work.

## Apply it where you live
Name one machine at home for each of the three purposes.
