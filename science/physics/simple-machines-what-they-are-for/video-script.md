# Video script — Simple machines — what they are for

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

A simple machine does work with one movement: a single effort works against a single load.
Machines in general do one or more of three jobs — multiply force (a small effort moves a big load), multiply speed or distance (a small movement of the load's point travels far), or change the direction of a force (pull down to lift up).
Every machine you meet later in this unit is judged by these three purposes.

Remember: **A simple machine works with one movement — one effort against one load.**
Remember: **Machines multiply force, multiply speed/distance, or change direction.**
Remember: **A pulley that lets you pull down to lift a load is a direction changer.**
