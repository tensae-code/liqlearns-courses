# Simple machines — what they are for — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What are the three purposes of machines?
a) Multiply force, multiply speed/distance, change direction.  b) Force multiplication.  c) Changes the direction of the force.  d) One movement — a single effort against a single load.
**Answer: a) Multiply force, multiply speed/distance, change direction.**

**2.** A crowbar lets a small push lift a heavy stone. Which purpose?
a) Multiply force, multiply speed/distance, change direction.  b) Force multiplication.  c) Changes the direction of the force.  d) One movement — a single effort against a single load.
**Answer: b) Force multiplication.**

**3.** Pulling down on a rope to raise a flag does which job?
a) Multiply force, multiply speed/distance, change direction.  b) Force multiplication.  c) Changes the direction of the force.  d) One movement — a single effort against a single load.
**Answer: c) Changes the direction of the force.**

**4.** How many movements does a simple machine use?
a) Multiply force, multiply speed/distance, change direction.  b) Force multiplication.  c) Changes the direction of the force.  d) One movement — a single effort against a single load.
**Answer: d) One movement — a single effort against a single load.**
