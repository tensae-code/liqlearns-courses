# Simple machines — what they are for — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** What are the three purposes of machines?
**Answer: Multiply force, multiply speed/distance, change direction.**

**2.** A crowbar lets a small push lift a heavy stone. Which purpose?
**Answer: Force multiplication.**

**3.** Pulling down on a rope to raise a flag does which job?
**Answer: Changes the direction of the force.**

**4.** How many movements does a simple machine use?
**Answer: One movement — a single effort against a single load.**
