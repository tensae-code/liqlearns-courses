# Simple machines — what they are for

## Do it first
Lift a heavy book with your hand alone, then slide it up a ramp of stacked books. Same load, easier effort — a machine has been at work.

## Simple machines — what they are for — the teaching
A simple machine does work with one movement: a single effort works against a single load. Machines in general do one or more of three jobs — multiply force (a small effort moves a big load), multiply speed or distance (a small movement of the load's point travels far), or change the direction of a force (pull down to lift up). Every machine you meet later in this unit is judged by these three purposes.

## The key ideas

- A simple machine works with one movement — one effort against one load.
- Machines multiply force, multiply speed/distance, or change direction.
- A pulley that lets you pull down to lift a load is a direction changer.

## Say it to your companion
Explain simple machines — what they are for to your companion in your own words. Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
