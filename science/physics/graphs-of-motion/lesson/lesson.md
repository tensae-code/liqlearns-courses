## Graphs of motion — reading slopes and areas — the teaching
Motion can be drawn. On a position–time graph, the slope gives velocity: steep means fast, flat means at rest. On a velocity–time graph, the slope gives acceleration, and the area under the graph gives displacement. Uniform motion draws a straight rising line on position–time and a flat line on velocity–time; accelerated motion bends the first and slopes the second. Read a journey three ways — track, position–time, velocity–time — and each drawing tells the same story in a different voice.

## The key ideas

- Slope of a position–time graph = velocity.
- Slope of a velocity–time graph = acceleration.
- Area under a velocity–time graph = displacement.
