# Hands-on — Graphs of motion — reading slopes and areas

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Walk a journey in three acts — fast, stopped, slow — while a partner sketches your position–time graph from watching. Compare the sketch with your memory.

## Apply it where you live
Sketch position–time and velocity–time graphs for a bus journey with one stop.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21822 — same journey three ways — track, position–time graph, velocity–time graph] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
