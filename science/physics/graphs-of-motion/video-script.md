# Video script — Graphs of motion — reading slopes and areas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Motion can be drawn.
On a position–time graph, the slope gives velocity: steep means fast, flat means at rest.
On a velocity–time graph, the slope gives acceleration, and the area under the graph gives displacement.
Uniform motion draws a straight rising line on position–time and a flat line on velocity–time; accelerated motion bends the first and slopes the second.
Read a journey three ways — track, position–time, velocity–time — and each drawing tells the same story in a different voice.

Remember: **Slope of a position–time graph = velocity.**
Remember: **Slope of a velocity–time graph = acceleration.**
Remember: **Area under a velocity–time graph = displacement.**
