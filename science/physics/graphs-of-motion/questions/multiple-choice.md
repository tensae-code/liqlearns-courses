# Graphs of motion — reading slopes and areas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A velocity–time graph is a rectangle 8 m/s high and 5 s long. Displacement?
a) Area = 8 × 5 = 40 m.  b) Slope = 20 ÷ 4 = 5 m/s.  c) The body is at rest — position not changing.  d) 2 m/s² — the slope is the acceleration.
**Answer: a) Area = 8 × 5 = 40 m.**

**2.** A position–time graph rises 20 m in 4 s steadily. Velocity?
a) Area = 8 × 5 = 40 m.  b) Slope = 20 ÷ 4 = 5 m/s.  c) The body is at rest — position not changing.  d) 2 m/s² — the slope is the acceleration.
**Answer: b) Slope = 20 ÷ 4 = 5 m/s.**

**3.** What does a flat position–time graph mean?
a) Area = 8 × 5 = 40 m.  b) Slope = 20 ÷ 4 = 5 m/s.  c) The body is at rest — position not changing.  d) 2 m/s² — the slope is the acceleration.
**Answer: c) The body is at rest — position not changing.**

**4.** A velocity–time graph slopes up at 2 (m/s) per s. Acceleration?
a) Area = 8 × 5 = 40 m.  b) Slope = 20 ÷ 4 = 5 m/s.  c) The body is at rest — position not changing.  d) 2 m/s² — the slope is the acceleration.
**Answer: d) 2 m/s² — the slope is the acceleration.**
