# Graphs of motion — reading slopes and areas — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A velocity–time graph is a rectangle 8 m/s high and 5 s long. Displacement?
**Answer: Area = 8 × 5 = 40 m.**

**2.** A position–time graph rises 20 m in 4 s steadily. Velocity?
**Answer: Slope = 20 ÷ 4 = 5 m/s.**

**3.** What does a flat position–time graph mean?
**Answer: The body is at rest — position not changing.**

**4.** A velocity–time graph slopes up at 2 (m/s) per s. Acceleration?
**Answer: 2 m/s² — the slope is the acceleration.**
