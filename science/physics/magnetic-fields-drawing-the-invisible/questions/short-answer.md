# Magnetic fields — drawing the invisible — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Outside a magnet, field lines run from — to — ?
**Answer: From N to S.**

**2.** Can two field lines cross?
**Answer: Never.**

**3.** What do iron filings do over a magnet?
**Answer: They align along the field lines, showing the pattern.**

**4.** Where is the field strongest in a drawing?
**Answer: Where the lines crowd.**
