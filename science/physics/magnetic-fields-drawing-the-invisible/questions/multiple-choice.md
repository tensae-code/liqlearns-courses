# Magnetic fields — drawing the invisible — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Outside a magnet, field lines run from — to — ?
a) From N to S.  b) Never.  c) They align along the field lines, showing the pattern.  d) Where the lines crowd.
**Answer: a) From N to S.**

**2.** Can two field lines cross?
a) From N to S.  b) Never.  c) They align along the field lines, showing the pattern.  d) Where the lines crowd.
**Answer: b) Never.**

**3.** What do iron filings do over a magnet?
a) From N to S.  b) Never.  c) They align along the field lines, showing the pattern.  d) Where the lines crowd.
**Answer: c) They align along the field lines, showing the pattern.**

**4.** Where is the field strongest in a drawing?
a) From N to S.  b) Never.  c) They align along the field lines, showing the pattern.  d) Where the lines crowd.
**Answer: d) Where the lines crowd.**
