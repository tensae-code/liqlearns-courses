## Magnetic fields — drawing the invisible — the teaching
A magnetic field is the region around a magnet where its influence acts. Field lines draw it with rules: outside the magnet they run from N to S; they never cross; and where they crowd, the field is strong. Iron filings sprinkled on card over a magnet swing into the pattern by themselves; plotting compasses trace the same lines arrow by arrow. Between unlike poles the lines run across the gap; between like poles they bend away, leaving a neutral emptiness.

## The key ideas

- Field lines run N → S outside the magnet and never cross.
- Crowding lines mean a strong field.
- Iron filings and plotting compasses reveal the pattern.

## Say it to your companion
Explain magnetic fields — drawing the invisible to your companion in your own words. Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
