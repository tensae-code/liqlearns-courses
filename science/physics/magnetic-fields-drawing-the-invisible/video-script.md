# Video script — Magnetic fields — drawing the invisible

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

A magnetic field is the region around a magnet where its influence acts.
Field lines draw it with rules: outside the magnet they run from N to S; they never cross; and where they crowd, the field is strong.
Iron filings sprinkled on card over a magnet swing into the pattern by themselves; plotting compasses trace the same lines arrow by arrow.
Between unlike poles the lines run across the gap; between like poles they bend away, leaving a neutral emptiness.

Remember: **Field lines run N → S outside the magnet and never cross.**
Remember: **Crowding lines mean a strong field.**
Remember: **Iron filings and plotting compasses reveal the pattern.**
