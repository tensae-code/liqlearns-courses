Today: **the oscillating LC circuit and electrical resonance**. Connect a charged capacitor to a coil and the energy starts to dance: out of the capacitor's field, into the coil's magnetism, and back again, twice per cycle, at one natural frequency — one over two-pi-root-LC. Push that circuit at exactly its frequency and the answer builds up huge: resonance. A radio is just this circuit with a variable capacitor — turn it until its frequency matches one station, and that station alone rings out of the whole sky.
