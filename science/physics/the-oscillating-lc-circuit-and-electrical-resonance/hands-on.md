# Hands-on — The oscillating LC circuit and electrical resonance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hear resonance select
On a simple radio (or phone radio app with manual tuning), tune slowly across a crowded band and note how each station swells to a peak and dies within a small turn of the dial — the resonance curve walked by hand. Say aloud the rule: the circuit answers strongly only within a narrow band around f = 1/(2π√(LC)); the dial moves C, and C moves f.

## The electrical pendulum
Where a school oscilloscope and signal generator are available, drive an LC circuit from the generator and watch the response peak sharply as the generator frequency passes the calculated 1/(2π√(LC)). Say aloud the rule: maximum response at equality of frequencies is resonance — the same response a pushed swing gives at its natural rate.
