# The oscillating LC circuit and electrical resonance — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The energy stored in a charged capacitor is Q²/_____ .
**Answer: 2C**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The period of an LC oscillation is T = 2π_____ .
**Answer: √(LC)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The strong response of a circuit driven at its natural frequency is called electrical _____.
**Answer: resonance**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) In a receiver, the component most often varied to tune is the _____.
**Answer: capacitor (variable capacitor)**
