# The oscillating LC circuit and electrical resonance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In an oscillating LC circuit, when the capacitor's charge is maximum, the current is…
a) Maximum  b) Zero — all the energy sits in the capacitor's field  c) Half its maximum  d) Reversed
**Answer: b) Zero — all the energy sits in the capacitor's field**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) At the instant the capacitor is fully discharged, the circuit's energy is…
a) Lost as heat  b) Stored entirely in the inductor's magnetic field, with the current at its maximum  c) Stored in the wires  d) Zero
**Answer: b) Stored entirely in the inductor's magnetic field, with the current at its maximum**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The natural frequency of an LC circuit is…
a) f = 1/(2π√(LC))  b) f = 2π√(LC)  c) f = √(LC)/(2π)  d) f = LC/2π
**Answer: a) f = 1/(2π√(LC))**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) If both L and C are doubled, the natural frequency becomes…
a) Double  b) Half  c) One half — √(LC) doubles, so f is halved  d) Unchanged
**Answer: c) One half — √(LC) doubles, so f is halved**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A radio is tuned to a station by…
a) Amplifying that station more  b) Adjusting C (or L) until 1/(2π√(LC)) equals the station's frequency, so resonance selects it  c) Filtering the sound after detection  d) Shortening the aerial to the station's wavelength only
**Answer: b) Adjusting C (or L) until 1/(2π√(LC)) equals the station's frequency, so resonance selects it**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Lower resistance in a tuned circuit gives…
a) A lower resonant frequency  b) Sharper tuning — a narrower, taller resonance peak  c) No resonance at all  d) A higher resonant frequency
**Answer: b) Sharper tuning — a narrower, taller resonance peak**
