# Hands-on — Power — how fast work is done

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Carry the same bag up the same steps twice — walking, then briskly — timing both. Same work, different power: calculate both.

## Apply it where you live
Compare two ways of lifting water to a tank (by hand, by pump) as a power comparison.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21835 — two pupils carrying the same load upstairs — one running, one walking, with a clock] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using P = W / t: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
