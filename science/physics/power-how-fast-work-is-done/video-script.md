# Video script — Power — how fast work is done

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Power is the rate of doing work: P = W/t, work divided by the time it takes, measured in watts (W).
Two pupils can carry the same load upstairs — the same work — but the one who runs does it in less time and so has the greater power.
Machines are judged the same way: a powerful engine is not just strong, it does its work fast.

Remember: **Power P = W/t, measured in watts.**
Remember: **Same work in less time means more power.**
Remember: **Power joins force ideas to time — fast work, not just work.**
Keep the formula: **P = W / t**
