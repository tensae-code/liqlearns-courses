# Power — how fast work is done — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A pupil does 600 J of work in 20 s. Power?
a) P = 600 ÷ 20 = 30 W.  b) 2000 ÷ 4 = 500 W.  c) The faster one — twice the power.  d) The watt (W).
**Answer: a) P = 600 ÷ 20 = 30 W.**

**2.** A motor does 2000 J in 4 s. Power?
a) P = 600 ÷ 20 = 30 W.  b) 2000 ÷ 4 = 500 W.  c) The faster one — twice the power.  d) The watt (W).
**Answer: b) 2000 ÷ 4 = 500 W.**

**3.** Two workers do equal work; one takes half the time. Who is more powerful?
a) P = 600 ÷ 20 = 30 W.  b) 2000 ÷ 4 = 500 W.  c) The faster one — twice the power.  d) The watt (W).
**Answer: c) The faster one — twice the power.**

**4.** What is the unit of power?
a) P = 600 ÷ 20 = 30 W.  b) 2000 ÷ 4 = 500 W.  c) The faster one — twice the power.  d) The watt (W).
**Answer: d) The watt (W).**
