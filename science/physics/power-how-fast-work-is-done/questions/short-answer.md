# Power — how fast work is done — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A pupil does 600 J of work in 20 s. Power?
**Answer: P = 600 ÷ 20 = 30 W.**

**2.** A motor does 2000 J in 4 s. Power?
**Answer: 2000 ÷ 4 = 500 W.**

**3.** Two workers do equal work; one takes half the time. Who is more powerful?
**Answer: The faster one — twice the power.**

**4.** What is the unit of power?
**Answer: The watt (W).**
