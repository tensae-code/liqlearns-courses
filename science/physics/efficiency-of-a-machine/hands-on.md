# Hands-on — Efficiency of a machine

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Price a pulley lift
Lift a known load through a measured height with a pulley system (useful output = mgh) while measuring the pull force and the rope distance pulled (input = force × distance). Divide. Say aloud: my system delivered this fraction — the rest is friction's bill.

## Feel the waste
Rub your palms together fast for ten seconds, then crank any stiff handle or drag a block with string. Where did the spent work go? Say aloud: efficiency fell because work I paid for turned into heat I did not order.
