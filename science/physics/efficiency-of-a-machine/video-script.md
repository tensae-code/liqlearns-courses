# Video script — Efficiency of a machine

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **how much of your work the machine keeps**. Feed in a hundred joules; a machine hands back only part as useful work — friction pockets the rest as heat and sound. Efficiency is useful out divided by total in, times a hundred for the percentage. It is never over a hundred — that would be creating energy. Lubricate, align, use bearings, and the fraction climbs; it never reaches one.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
