# Efficiency of a machine — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Efficiency equals useful output divided by total _____.
**Answer: input**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Input energy always equals useful output plus _____ output.
**Answer: wasted**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The chief thief of useful work in machines is _____.
**Answer: friction**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) No machine's efficiency can exceed _____ percent.
**Answer: 100**
