# Efficiency of a machine — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An electric motor takes in 1,000 J and delivers 820 J of useful work. Find its efficiency and the energy wasted.
**Answer: Efficiency = 820 ÷ 1,000 = 0.82 = 82%. Wasted = 1,000 − 820 = 180 J, spent mainly as heat and sound.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why must percent and decimal forms of efficiency not be mixed in one calculation?
**Answer: Because 60% and 60 differ a hundredfold: using '60' where 0.60 belongs inflates the answer 100 times. Convert the percentage to a decimal (÷ 100) before multiplying or dividing.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Name two practical ways to raise a pulley system's efficiency, and the limit on all such efforts.
**Answer: Lubricate the pulley bearings and use free-running, well-aligned pulleys (rollers/bearings instead of rubbing surfaces). The limit: friction can be reduced but never removed, so efficiency stays below 100%.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) A machine's input is 800 J and its efficiency is 0.65. Find the useful output.
**Answer: Useful output = efficiency × input = 0.65 × 800 = 520 J (the remaining 280 J is wasted).**
