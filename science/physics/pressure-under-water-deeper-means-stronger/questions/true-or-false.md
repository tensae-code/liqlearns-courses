# Pressure under water — deeper means stronger — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. P = P₀ + ρgh derived from a fluid column's weight; worked for a diver at 25 m (absolute pressure ≈ 354 kPa) and the net force on a surface.**
**Answer: True**

**2. P = P₀ + ρgh derived from a fluid column's weight; worked for a diver at 50 m (absolute pressure ≈ 354 kPa) and the net force on a surface.**
**Answer: False**
