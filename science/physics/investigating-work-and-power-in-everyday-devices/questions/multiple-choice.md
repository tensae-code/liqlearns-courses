# Investigating work and power in everyday devices — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A device's rating tells you…
a) Its price  b) The power it is designed to draw  c) Its mass  d) How loud it is
**Answer: b) The power it is designed to draw**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A fair investigation states, before measuring…
a) The answer  b) A hypothesis and a plan with fair comparisons  c) The teacher's opinion  d) Nothing
**Answer: b) A hypothesis and a plan with fair comparisons**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) To check a kettle's rating you would compare it with…
a) Its colour  b) Energy delivered to the water divided by the time taken  c) Its weight  d) The size of its plug
**Answer: b) Energy delivered to the water divided by the time taken**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Vehicles are compared for quickness in this investigation by…
a) Top speed only  b) Their 0–100 km/h acceleration times  c) Engine noise  d) Fuel tank size
**Answer: b) Their 0–100 km/h acceleration times**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A small-power device can use more energy than a big one when…
a) It is newer  b) It runs for much longer — energy = power × time  c) It is plugged in abroad  d) It is switched off
**Answer: b) It runs for much longer — energy = power × time**
