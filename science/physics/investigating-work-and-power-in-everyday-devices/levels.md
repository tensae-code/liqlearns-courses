# Investigating work and power in everyday devices — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — power stops being a formula when you go and look: read the ratings of the devices heating and lighting your home, time what they do, and compare vehicles by the time they take to reach 100 km/h — the investigation the NIS book sets as its own section — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
