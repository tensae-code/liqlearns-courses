# The branches of physics — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Which branch studies sound?
**Answer: Acoustics.**

**2.** Which branch studies light?
**Answer: Optics.**

**3.** Mechanics splits into which two parts?
**Answer: Classical mechanics and quantum mechanics.**

**4.** A falling stone belongs to which branch?
**Answer: Mechanics.**
