# The branches of physics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which branch studies sound?
a) Acoustics.  b) Optics.  c) Classical mechanics and quantum mechanics.  d) Mechanics.
**Answer: a) Acoustics.**

**2.** Which branch studies light?
a) Acoustics.  b) Optics.  c) Classical mechanics and quantum mechanics.  d) Mechanics.
**Answer: b) Optics.**

**3.** Mechanics splits into which two parts?
a) Acoustics.  b) Optics.  c) Classical mechanics and quantum mechanics.  d) Mechanics.
**Answer: c) Classical mechanics and quantum mechanics.**

**4.** A falling stone belongs to which branch?
a) Acoustics.  b) Optics.  c) Classical mechanics and quantum mechanics.  d) Mechanics.
**Answer: d) Mechanics.**
