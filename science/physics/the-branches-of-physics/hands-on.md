# Hands-on — The branches of physics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Name six things happening around you — a falling cup, music, a lamp, a hot pot. Try to give each one a branch before you read on.

## Apply it where you live
Sort five phenomena from your street into branches and defend each sort in one line.
