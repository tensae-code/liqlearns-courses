# Video script — The branches of physics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Physics is organised into branches, and each branch has its own territory.
Mechanics studies motion, with and without force, and splits into classical and quantum mechanics.
Acoustics studies sound, optics studies light, thermodynamics studies heat and energy, electromagnetism studies electricity and magnetism together, and nuclear physics studies the centre of the atom.
When you meet a new phenomenon, the first physics question is: which branch does it belong to?

Remember: **Mechanics studies motion and splits into classical and quantum mechanics.**
Remember: **Acoustics is the branch about sound; optics is the branch about light.**
Remember: **Thermodynamics studies heat, and nuclear physics studies the centre of the atom.**
