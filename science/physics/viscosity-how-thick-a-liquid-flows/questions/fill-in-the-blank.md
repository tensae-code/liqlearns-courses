# Viscosity — how thick a liquid flows — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The drag between sliding layers of a liquid is called its _____.
**Answer: viscosity**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In F = ηA × (velocity gradient), the Greek letter η is the _____ of viscosity.
**Answer: coefficient**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A small ball sinking steadily in a liquid is moving at its _____ speed.
**Answer: terminal**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Cold honey pours more slowly than warm honey because cooling _____ its viscosity.
**Answer: raises (increases)**
