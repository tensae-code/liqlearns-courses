# Hands-on — Viscosity — how thick a liquid flows

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Race the liquids
Tilt a smooth board and release a spoonful of water, cooking oil and honey at the same line. Time each to the bottom. Say aloud the rule: same slope, same gravity — the race is decided by viscosity alone, the liquid whose layers grip hardest flows slowest.

## Falling-ball viscometer
Drop the same small steel ball into tall jars of water and of oil and time its fall between two marks well below the surface. The ball falls at its terminal speed there; the slower fall means the larger viscosity. Say aloud the rule: at terminal speed weight = upthrust + viscous drag, so the timing prices the drag.
