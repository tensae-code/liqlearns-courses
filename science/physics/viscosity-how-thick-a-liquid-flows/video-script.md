Today: **viscosity — how thick a liquid flows**. A flowing liquid is layers sliding over each other, and the layers grip. That grip is viscosity. Honey grips hard and crawls; water grips weakly and races. Scientists price the grip with one number, the viscosity coefficient, and that number decides which oil protects an engine and how hard a heart must push blood through a narrow vessel. Warm a liquid and the grip loosens — that is why warm honey pours.
