Today: **warming a gas has two prices**. Seal the gas so it cannot expand, and every joule of heat becomes internal energy — that price per mole per kelvin is Cv. Let it expand under a free piston instead, and the bill grows: the same internal energy, plus the work of pushing the piston out. That bigger price is Cp. For an ideal gas the difference is exact — Cp minus Cv equals R, the cost of one mole expanding through one kelvin. And their ratio, gamma, is the exponent that governs every adiabatic change. Two capacities, one gas — the difference is work you can see a piston do.
