# Molar heat capacities of gases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Cv is defined for warming at constant…
a) Pressure  b) Volume — Q = n Cv ΔT, all the heat becoming internal energy  c) Temperature  d) Mass
**Answer: b) Volume — Q = n Cv ΔT, all the heat becoming internal energy**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) For an ideal gas, ΔU = n Cv ΔT is valid…
a) Only at constant volume  b) For any change of the gas  c) Only adiabatically  d) Never
**Answer: b) For any change of the gas**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Cp is larger than Cv because at constant pressure the heat must also pay for…
a) Friction  b) The work of expansion, p ΔV  c) Heating the vessel  d) Sound
**Answer: b) The work of expansion, p ΔV**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Mayer's relation states…
a) Cp + Cv = R  b) Cp × Cv = R  c) Cp − Cv = R  d) Cp = Cv
**Answer: c) Cp − Cv = R**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) For a diatomic gas such as air, γ = Cp/Cv is about…
a) 1.00  b) 1.40  c) 1.67  d) 2.50
**Answer: b) 1.40**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In the adiabatic law p V^γ = constant, γ is…
a) The gas constant  b) The number of moles  c) The ratio Cp/Cv  d) The temperature ratio
**Answer: c) The ratio Cp/Cv**
