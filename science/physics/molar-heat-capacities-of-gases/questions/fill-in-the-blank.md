# Molar heat capacities of gases — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) At constant volume, all the heat absorbed becomes _____ energy.
**Answer: internal**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The heat capacity at constant pressure is _____ than at constant volume.
**Answer: larger**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Cp − Cv = _____ for an ideal gas.
**Answer: R**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The ratio Cp/Cv is given the symbol _____.
**Answer: γ**
