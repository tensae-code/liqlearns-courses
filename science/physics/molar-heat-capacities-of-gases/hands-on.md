# Hands-on — Molar heat capacities of gases

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel the expansion work
You need: a bicycle pump with its outlet partly blocked by your thumb, and care — pump slowly and stop if it gets hot to hold. Pump air quickly into the blocked pump: the barrel warms noticeably. You compressed the gas — work went in and, with no time to escape as heat, became internal energy (the adiabatic partner of this lesson). Now let the compressed air escape suddenly through a loosened thumb: the escaping air feels cold — it paid for its expansion work out of its internal energy. Say aloud: expanding gas does work, and the bill is settled in temperature — that is why Cp, which pays the bill with heat, is larger than Cv.
