# Matter waves and the uncertainty principle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) De Broglie's wavelength for a particle of momentum p is…
a) λ = p/h  b) λ = h/p  c) λ = h·p²  d) λ = c/p
**Answer: a) λ = p/h**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Electron waves were proved by…
a) Electron diffraction from a crystal (Davisson and Germer, 1927)  b) The photoelectric effect  c) Bohr's spectrum  d) The oil-drop experiment
**Answer: b) The photoelectric effect**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Bohr's allowed orbits survive in the wave picture because…
a) Only waves that fit a whole number of wavelengths around the confinement stand; others cancel  b) The nucleus selects them  c) Electrons are too small to interfere  d) Photons hold them in place
**Answer: c) Electrons are too small to interfere**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In the full quantum picture, the electron wave's intensity at a place gives…
a) The probability of finding the electron there  b) The electron's speed there  c) Nothing physical  d) The electron's charge there
**Answer: d) The electron's charge there**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The uncertainty principle says Δx·Δp cannot be…
a) Larger than h/4π  b) Smaller than h/4π  c) Exactly zero only for electrons  d) Measured at all
**Answer: a) Larger than h/4π**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The principle's floor exists because…
a) A particle of exact momentum is a wave spread through space — exact place and exact momentum cannot both belong to one wave  b) Instruments always disturb what they measure  c) Electrons are too fast to track  d) Heisenberg guessed it
**Answer: b) Instruments always disturb what they measure**
