# Matter waves and the uncertainty principle — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — Compute a de Broglie wavelength and judge when wave behaviour can show; Explain Bohr's allowed states as standing-wave fits, and probability as wave intensity; State Δx·Δp ≥ h/4π, use it in estimates, and refute the “clumsy instruments” reading; Explain zero-point motion from confinement — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
