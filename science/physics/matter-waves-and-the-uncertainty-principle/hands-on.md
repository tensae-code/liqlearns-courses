# Hands-on — Matter waves and the uncertainty principle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fit the wave to the loop
Draw a circle and sketch a wave around its rim twice: once with exactly 4 whole wavelengths closing smoothly, once with 4½ wavelengths, the ends mismatching. Say aloud: the first pattern can stand forever; the second cancels itself — that mismatch is every “forbidden orbit” in Bohr's atom.

## Squeeze and spread
Whistle or hum a very short *pip* and then a long steady note (or imagine a drum tap versus an organ note). The short pip has a definite *when* but no definite pitch; the long note a definite pitch but no definite *when*. Say aloud: position–momentum uncertainty is this same trade, written for waves of matter.
