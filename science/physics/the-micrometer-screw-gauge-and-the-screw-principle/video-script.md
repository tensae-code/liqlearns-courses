# Video script — The micrometer screw gauge and the screw principle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the screw that measures a hair**. One turn of a screw moves it forward by its pitch — half a millimetre. Divide the turn into fifty marked steps and each step is a hundredth of a millimetre: that is a **micrometer screw gauge**. Object between anvil and spindle, sleeve first, then the thimble line, always finishing on the ratchet so your hand cannot crush the truth. Check the zero, measure in several places, and average.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
