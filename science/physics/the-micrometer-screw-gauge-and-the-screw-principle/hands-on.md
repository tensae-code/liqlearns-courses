# Hands-on — The micrometer screw gauge and the screw principle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel the ratchet
Close a micrometer on a sheet of paper using only the ratchet until it slips, then try to pull the paper out — it should drag, not tear and not fall. Say aloud: the ratchet, not my strength, sets the measuring force.

## Fifty steps of a hair
Turn a micrometer thimble through exactly 10 divisions against a fixed mark and state the advance (10 × 0.01 mm = 0.10 mm) before checking it against a ruler's millimetre. Say aloud: I counted a tenth of a millimetre without ever seeing it move.
