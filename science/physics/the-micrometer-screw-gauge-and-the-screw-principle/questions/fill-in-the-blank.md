# The micrometer screw gauge and the screw principle — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) One full turn advances a screw by its _____.
**Answer: pitch**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Least count equals pitch divided by the number of _____ divisions.
**Answer: thimble**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The object is placed between the anvil and the _____.
**Answer: spindle**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The part that slips at the right measuring pressure is the _____.
**Answer: ratchet**
