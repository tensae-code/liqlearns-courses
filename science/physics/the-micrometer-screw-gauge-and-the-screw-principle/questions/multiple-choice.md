# The micrometer screw gauge and the screw principle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Pitch 0.5 mm, 50 thimble divisions: the least count is…
a) 0.5 mm  b) 0.05 mm  c) 0.01 mm  d) 0.001 mm
**Answer: c) 0.01 mm**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The pitch of a screw is…
a) Its length  b) The distance between neighbouring threads — the advance in one turn  c) Its diameter  d) The number of divisions
**Answer: b) The distance between neighbouring threads — the advance in one turn**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Sleeve 2.5 mm, thimble division 41, least count 0.01 mm. The reading is…
a) 2.91 mm  b) 43.5 mm  c) 2.54 mm  d) 6.6 mm
**Answer: a) 2.91 mm**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The final turns when measuring are made with the ratchet because it…
a) Turns faster  b) Slips at the right pressure, so the object is not crushed  c) Locks the scale  d) Removes zero error
**Answer: b) Slips at the right pressure, so the object is not crushed**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A wire is measured at several places and averaged mainly because…
a) The gauge changes each time  b) The wire itself is not the same thickness everywhere  c) Averaging removes zero error  d) The ratchet slips differently
**Answer: b) The wire itself is not the same thickness everywhere**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A school micrometer's measuring range is typically…
a) 1 m  b) About 25 mm  c) Exactly 1 mm  d) Unlimited
**Answer: b) About 25 mm**
