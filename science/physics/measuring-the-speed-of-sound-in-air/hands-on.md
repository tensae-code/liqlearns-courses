# Hands-on — Measuring the speed of sound in air

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Echo method: find a large wall, pace out 100 m, clap in rhythm with your echoes and time 50 claps; compute v. (2) Repeat on a warmer or colder day and compare. (3) Estimate your reaction-time error and state its percentage of your measured interval.
