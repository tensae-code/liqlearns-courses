# Measuring the speed of sound in air — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Speed equals distance divided by _____.
**Answer: time**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In the echo method the sound travels _____ the wall distance.
**Answer: twice**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The main error is _____ time.
**Answer: reaction**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Sound travels at about _____ metres per second in air.
**Answer: 340**
