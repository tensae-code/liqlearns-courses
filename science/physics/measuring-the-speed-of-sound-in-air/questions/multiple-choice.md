# Measuring the speed of sound in air — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the direct method, timing starts at…
**Answer: a) the visible signal, which arrives effectively instantly  b) the sound  c) a guess  d) the echo**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The speed is found as…
**Answer: a) distance ÷ travel time  b) time ÷ distance  c) distance × time  d) frequency × time**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In the echo method the sound travels…
**Answer: a) twice the distance to the wall  b) the distance once  c) no distance  d) the wall's height**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Clapping in rhythm with the echoes lets you…
**Answer: a) time many return trips at once, shrinking reaction-time error  b) make louder sound  c) change the speed  d) cancel the echo**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The main error in both methods is…
**Answer: a) reaction time  b) the wall  c) the ruler  d) the sound's pitch**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The speed of sound in air near room temperature is about…
**Answer: a) 340 m/s  b) 34 m/s  c) 3,400 m/s  d) 300,000 km/s**
