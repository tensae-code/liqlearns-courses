# How a refrigerator works — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The refrigerant's loop order is…
**Answer: a) compressor, condenser, expansion valve, evaporator  b) evaporator, valve, condenser, compressor  c) condenser first  d) valve only**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Expansion through the narrow valve makes the refrigerant…
**Answer: a) suddenly cold  b) hotter  c) solid  d) luminous**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Heat moves from the cold cabinet to the warm kitchen because…
**Answer: a) the compressor pays work to carry it uphill  b) heat flows that way naturally  c) the coils are magic  d) cold flows out**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The heat shed at the back equals…
**Answer: a) the heat removed from the food plus the compressor's work  b) only the food's heat  c) only the work  d) nothing**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Inside the cabinet the refrigerant…
**Answer: a) evaporates, absorbing the food's heat  b) condenses, shedding heat  c) freezes solid  d) stops moving**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The enrichment judges a fridge by…
**Answer: a) heat moved per unit of work paid  b) its colour  c) its size only  d) its price**
