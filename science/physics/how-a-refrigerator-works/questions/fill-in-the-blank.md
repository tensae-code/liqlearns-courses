# How a refrigerator works — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The _____ squeezes the refrigerant into a hot gas.
**Answer: compressor**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The refrigerant cools suddenly at the expansion _____.
**Answer: valve**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Moving heat from cold to hot requires paid _____.
**Answer: work**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Inside the cabinet the refrigerant _____, absorbing heat.
**Answer: evaporates**
