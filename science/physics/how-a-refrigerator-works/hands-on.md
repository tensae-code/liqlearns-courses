# Hands-on — How a refrigerator works

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Feel a working fridge's back or side coils (warm) and the inside back wall (cold) — the loop's two heat exchanges found by hand. (2) Spray a little water on your skin and blow: the evaporation inside the cabinet is the same cooling, felt directly. (3) Read a fridge's energy label and find its efficiency rating; relate it to heat moved per unit of work.
