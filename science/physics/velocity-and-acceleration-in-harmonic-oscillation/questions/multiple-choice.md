# Velocity and acceleration in harmonic oscillation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The velocity of a harmonic oscillator is greatest…
**Answer: a) at the centre (equilibrium)  b) at the extremes  c) halfway out, permanently  d) it is constant**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) v_max for amplitude A and angular frequency ω is…
**Answer: a) ωA  b) A/ω  c) ω²A  d) ωA²**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Acceleration in SHM obeys a = −ω²x. The minus sign means the acceleration…
**Answer: a) always points opposite the displacement — back toward the centre  b) is always negative in value  c) decreases with time  d) acts only at the extremes**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The acceleration is greatest in size…
**Answer: a) at the extremes, where x = ±A — a_max = ω²A  b) at the centre  c) a quarter cycle after the start, always  d) nowhere — it is constant**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The velocity curve is ahead of the displacement curve in phase by…
**Answer: a) a quarter of a cycle  b) half a cycle  c) a full cycle  d) nothing — they are in phase**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A motion with a = −9x (SI units) is SHM with ω =…
**Answer: a) 3 rad/s — ω² = 9, so ω = 3  b) 9 rad/s  c) 81 rad/s  d) 4.5 rad/s**
