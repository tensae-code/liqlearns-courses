# Velocity and acceleration in harmonic oscillation — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) At the extremes of the oscillation the velocity is _____.
**Answer: zero**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Acceleration in SHM is _____ to the displacement and opposite to it.
**Answer: proportional**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) At the centre, where x = 0, the acceleration is _____.
**Answer: zero**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The acceleration curve is in _____ with the displacement curve — one is the other flipped.
**Answer: antiphase**
