# Hands-on — Velocity and acceleration in harmonic oscillation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Slow-motion the swing's three stories
Film a pendulum in slow motion and step through the frames: at the extremes the bob hangs still for several frames (velocity zero) while the string's angle is greatest (the restoring pull is strongest); at the centre the bob flashes across the most distance between frames (velocity greatest) while the string is vertical (no sideways pull — acceleration zero). Note one frame count for the crossing speed near the centre and near an extreme. Your table of 'far between frames' vs 'still for frames' is the v and a curves, measured.
