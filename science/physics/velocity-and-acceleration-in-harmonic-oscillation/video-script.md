# Video script — Velocity and acceleration in harmonic oscillation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the oscillator's velocity and acceleration**. Velocity — zero at the extremes, greatest at the centre, v = −ωA sin(ωt + φ). Acceleration — a = −ω²x: always back toward the centre, greatest at the extremes, and that proportionality-with-a-minus-sign is the very definition of simple harmonic motion.
