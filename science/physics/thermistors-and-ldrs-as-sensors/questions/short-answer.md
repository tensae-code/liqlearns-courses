# Thermistors and LDRs as sensors — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does NTC stand for and describe?
**Answer:** Negative temperature coefficient — resistance falls as temperature rises.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the LDR's resistance in darkness versus bright light, in order of size.
**Answer:** Very high in the dark (megohms), low in bright light (hundreds of ohms).

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How does a divider convert resistance change to voltage change?
**Answer:** The supply splits between the two arms in proportion to resistance; as the sensor's share changes, the output voltage swings.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why must the fixed resistor's value be chosen near the sensor's working value?
**Answer:** So the output swing is largest in the range where the sensor is meant to act.
