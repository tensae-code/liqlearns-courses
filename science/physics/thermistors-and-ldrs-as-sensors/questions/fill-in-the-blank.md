# Thermistors and LDRs as sensors — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) NTC means negative ___ coefficient.
**Answer:** temperature

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) LDR stands for light-dependent ___.
**Answer:** resistor

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A sensor in series with a fixed resistor forms a potential ___.
**Answer:** divider

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Photons free ___ in the LDR's track.
**Answer:** carriers (electrons)
