# Thermistors and LDRs as sensors — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An NTC thermistor's resistance as temperature rises…
a) Rises steeply  b) Falls steeply  c) Stays fixed  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Falls steeply**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Thermistor action is explained by…
a) The wire expanding  b) Heat freeing more charge carriers in the semiconductor  c) Magnetism  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Heat freeing more charge carriers in the semiconductor**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) An LDR in bright light has…
a) Its highest resistance  b) Low resistance  c) Infinite resistance  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Low resistance**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An LDR's resistance falls because photons…
a) Heat the room  b) Free charge carriers in its track  c) Reflect off its surface  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Free charge carriers in its track**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A sensor becomes a signal-maker when placed in…
a) A fuse box  b) A potential divider with a fixed resistor  c) Series with a battery only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) A potential divider with a fixed resistor**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A street light's dusk switch uses…
a) A thermistor only  b) An LDR divider triggering a switch as light falls  c) A stopwatch  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) An LDR divider triggering a switch as light falls**
