# Hands-on — Thermistors and LDRs as sensors

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build both dividers on a breadboard with a multimeter on the output
Build both dividers on a breadboard with a multimeter on the output: warm the thermistor between fingers and log output against a thermometer's reading; shade and light the LDR with a torch and log again. Set a threshold with the fixed resistor so a transistor switches an LED at your chosen temperature or darkness.
