# Damping and resonance — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The biggest displacement of an oscillation from its rest position is its _____.
**Answer: amplitude**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Energy leaves a swinging pendulum mainly through friction and _____ resistance.
**Answer: air**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The frequency at which an oscillator swings when left to itself is its _____ frequency.
**Answer: natural**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Driving an oscillator at its natural frequency, so its amplitude reaches a maximum, is called _____.
**Answer: resonance**
