# Damping and resonance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Damping is…
a) The growth of amplitude when pushes are in step  b) The steady shrinking of amplitude as energy leaves the oscillation  c) A change of frequency to zero  d) The swing stopping instantly
**Answer: b) The steady shrinking of amplitude as energy leaves the oscillation**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A pendulum swinging in oil, compared with the same pendulum in air, is…
a) Lightly damped  b) Undamped  c) Heavily damped — it dies in a swing or two  d) Resonant
**Answer: c) Heavily damped — it dies in a swing or two**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A pendulum's natural frequency is set by…
a) How hard it was first pushed  b) Its length  c) The pusher's rhythm  d) The air temperature
**Answer: b) Its length**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Pushes build an oscillation up only when they arrive…
a) As hard as possible  b) At random times  c) In step with the motion, once per cycle  d) Against the motion
**Answer: c) In step with the motion, once per cycle**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Resonance occurs when the driving frequency…
a) Is zero  b) Equals the natural frequency  c) Is twice the natural frequency  d) Is much lower than the natural frequency
**Answer: b) Equals the natural frequency**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Soldiers break step when crossing a bridge so that…
a) The bridge is not worn out  b) Their marching cannot drive the bridge at its natural frequency and build a dangerous sway  c) They cross faster  d) The bridge's damping increases
**Answer: b) Their marching cannot drive the bridge at its natural frequency and build a dangerous sway**
