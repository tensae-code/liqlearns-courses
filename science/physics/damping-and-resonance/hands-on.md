# Hands-on — Damping and resonance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The dying swing and the timed push
Tie a washer to 40 cm of string and hang it from a door frame or a shelf edge. Pull it 15 cm to one side, let go, and count aloud: watch the swings shrink — that is damping, and the energy is going into the air and the pivot. Start it again and, with one fingertip, give the tiniest push each time the washer passes the lowest point, always in the direction it is already moving. Count ten pushes: the swing grows far beyond your fingertip's effort — that is resonance, energy arriving in step at the natural frequency. Finally, push deliberately out of step — against the motion — and feel the swing die faster than damping alone could kill it. Say aloud: in step builds, out of step kills, friction drains.
