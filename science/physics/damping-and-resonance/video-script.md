Today: **damping and resonance — the dying swing and the timed push**. Set a swing going and leave it: every swing is smaller than the one before, because friction and the air steal a little energy each time — that shrinking is damping. Now push instead, one small push per swing, timed to the swing's own rhythm: the swings grow and grow — that is resonance, driving at the natural frequency. Damping takes energy out a little at a time; resonance puts it in, in step, a little at a time — and in step is what makes it powerful.
