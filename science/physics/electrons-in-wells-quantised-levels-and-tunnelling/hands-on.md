# Hands-on — Electrons in wells — quantised levels and tunnelling

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The string that teaches wells
Stretch a skipping rope or long string between two fixed points (or have two people hold it taut) and shake standing waves: first the single hump, then two, then three — count the half-wavelengths each time. Write beside each sketch its “n” and, using E ∝ n², its relative energy (1, 4, 9). The rope is the well, your hands are its walls.
