# Electrons in wells — quantised levels and tunnelling — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does confinement quantise energy? Answer in standing-wave words.
**Answer: The trapped matter wave must fit the well as a standing wave; only discrete patterns fit, and each pattern carries one definite energy, so the energies form a discrete ladder.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Level energies go as n². If E₁ = 2 eV, find E₂ and E₃.
**Answer: E₂ = 4 × 2 = 8 eV; E₃ = 9 × 2 = 18 eV.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why do quantum dots show quantum effects so clearly?
**Answer: They are extremely narrow wells, and level energies rise as 1/L² — so in a dot the levels are pushed far apart, making the quantisation large and visible (e.g. in the colours dots emit).**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) What must be true of a barrier for tunnelling to be noticeable?
**Answer: It must be thin (and finite in height): the wave decays inside the barrier, so only a thin barrier lets a measurable part of the wave emerge on the far side.**
