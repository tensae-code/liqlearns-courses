# Electrons in wells — quantised levels and tunnelling — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A trapped electron’s allowed energies are _____ — a ladder, not a ramp.
**Answer: quantised**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The lowest allowed energy, present even at n = 1, is the _____-point energy.
**Answer: zero**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Level energies grow as _____ squared — 1, 4, 9 times the ground energy.
**Answer: n (the quantum number)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Passing through an unclimbable thin barrier is called _____.
**Answer: tunnelling**
