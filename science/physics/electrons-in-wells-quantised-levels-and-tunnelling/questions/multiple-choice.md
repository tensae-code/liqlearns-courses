# Electrons in wells — quantised levels and tunnelling — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Only certain wave patterns survive in a well because the wave must…
**Answer: a) fit the well as a standing wave — a whole number of half-wavelengths  b) touch one wall only  c) be round  d) move fastest**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The allowed states of a trapped electron are labelled by…
**Answer: a) a quantum number n = 1, 2, 3, …  b) its colour  c) its temperature  d) its charge sign**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The ground-state (n = 1) energy of a trapped electron is…
**Answer: a) not zero — a zero-point energy remains  b) exactly zero  c) negative  d) infinite**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Making a well narrower pushes its energy levels…
**Answer: a) up and further apart  b) down and closer  c) nowhere  d) to zero**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Tunnelling means a particle can…
**Answer: a) pass through a thin barrier it has not the energy to climb  b) dig under any wall  c) travel faster than light  d) lose its charge**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The scanning tunnelling microscope works by…
**Answer: a) a tunnelling current that changes with distance to single atoms  b) bouncing light off atoms  c) X-ray photographs  d) magnets**
