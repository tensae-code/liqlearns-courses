# Video script — Heat transfer at home — the thermos flask and radiation absorbers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the thermos flask defeats all three heat transfers at once, and the Leslie cube shows why surface decides radiation**. Fill a cube with hot water whose four faces are finished differently — dull black, shiny black, dull white, polished metal — and hold a detector at equal distances: the dull black face radiates most, the polished face least. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — the leslie cube — surfaces vote on radiation; the thermos flask — three transfers, three defeats; everyday consequences — clothing and houses — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
