# Hands-on — Heat transfer at home — the thermos flask and radiation absorbers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run the Leslie test with cans
Run the Leslie test with cans: paint four identical cans black-dull, black-shiny, white and leave one polished; fill with equal hot water, and record temperatures every two minutes. Rank the cooling rates and match them to the radiation rule. Then test a flask against an open jug of the same hot water over an hour.
