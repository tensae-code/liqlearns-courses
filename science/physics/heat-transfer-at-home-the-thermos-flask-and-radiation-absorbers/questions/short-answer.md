# Heat transfer at home — the thermos flask and radiation absorbers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the three transfers and the flask feature that defeats each.
**Answer:** Conduction/convection — the vacuum (and poor-conductor stopper); radiation — the silvered walls.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What does the Leslie cube prove that a thermometer alone cannot?
**Answer:** That at equal temperature the surface finish decides the rate of radiation.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does a flask keep cold drinks cold with the same features?
**Answer:** The three defeats work in both directions — heat is kept out as well as in.

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) State the absorber–emitter rule.
**Answer:** A surface that absorbs radiation well also emits it well (and a poor absorber is a poor emitter).
