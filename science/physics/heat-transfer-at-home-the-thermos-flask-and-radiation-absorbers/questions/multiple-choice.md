# Heat transfer at home — the thermos flask and radiation absorbers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a Leslie cube test, the face radiating most is…
a) Polished metal  b) Dull black  c) Dull white  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Dull black**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Good absorbers of radiation are also…
a) Poor emitters  b) Good emitters  c) Unable to emit  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Good emitters**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The flask's vacuum defeats…
a) Radiation only  b) Conduction and convection  c) Evaporation only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Conduction and convection**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Silvering on the flask's walls works by…
a) Conducting heat out  b) Reflecting radiation back  c) Making the glass stronger  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Reflecting radiation back**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Summer clothing in Punjab's application is…
a) Black and tight  b) Light-coloured and loose  c) Silvered and sealed  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Light-coloured and loose**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The stopper and narrow neck mainly cut losses by…
a) Reflection  b) Conduction through the top and convection at the opening  c) Radiation from the base  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Conduction through the top and convection at the opening**
