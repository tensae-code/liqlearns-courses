## Scalars and vectors; kinds of vectors

Some quantities need only a size (scalars), others a size and a direction (vectors); vectors are drawn as arrows whose length is the magnitude.

Kinds met here: equal vectors, negative (opposite) vectors, parallel and collinear vectors, coplanar vectors and the zero vector.
