# Hands-on — Investigating acceleration against force and mass

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Earn the law with a trolley, a tape and a pulley
Set a trolley on a track with a ticker timer, thread its tape, and hang a small weight over a pulley at the track's end. (1) Compensate friction: tilt the track slightly until the trolley, given a gentle nudge with no hanging weight, prints evenly spaced dots. (2) Run A: keep the trolley mass fixed; hang 1, 2, then 3 units of pull (move masses from the trolley to the hanger so the total mass of the system never changes) and take a tape each time; find each acceleration from the tapes. (3) Run B: restore one pull; load the trolley with extra masses and repeat. (4) Plot a against F, and a against 1/m. Write the two proportionalities in words before you are told any law — you have just done what PEP's Compulsory Book 1 does in §4.2, one section before the law is stated.
