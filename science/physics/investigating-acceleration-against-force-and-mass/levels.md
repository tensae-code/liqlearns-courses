# Investigating acceleration against force and mass — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — Control variables: change one suspect at a time, hold the other fixed; compensate friction first; Fixed mass: acceleration is proportional to the force — a ∝ F; Fixed force: acceleration is proportional to 1/mass — a ∝ 1/m; Joined: a ∝ F/m — and choosing the force unit to make the constant 1 gives F = ma, stated only after the experiment — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
