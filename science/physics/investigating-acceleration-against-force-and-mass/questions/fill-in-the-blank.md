# Investigating acceleration against force and mass — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Change one variable, hold the rest: that design is called _____ of variables.
**Answer: control**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) At fixed mass, acceleration is _____ to the force.
**Answer: proportional**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) At fixed force, doubling the mass _____ the acceleration.
**Answer: halves**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Joined, the pattern reads a ∝ F/_____.
**Answer: m**
