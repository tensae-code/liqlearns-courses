# Investigating acceleration against force and mass — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The design that separates the two suspects, force and mass, is called…
**Answer: a) control of variables — change one, hold the other  b) trial and error  c) the control group dance  d) guessing**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Before measuring, friction on the track is…
**Answer: a) compensated, so the measured acceleration belongs to the pull alone  b) ignored completely  c) doubled  d) painted over**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) At fixed mass, doubling the pull…
**Answer: a) doubles the acceleration — a ∝ F  b) halves it  c) leaves it unchanged  d) quadruples it**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) At fixed force, doubling the mass…
**Answer: a) halves the acceleration — a ∝ 1/m  b) doubles it  c) leaves it unchanged  d) stops the trolley**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The law F = ma appears in the book…
**Answer: a) after this experiment, as the summary of its pattern  b) before any measurement  c) in a different subject  d) never**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) An a–F graph for this experiment should be…
**Answer: a) a straight line through the origin  b) a curve bending down  c) a horizontal line  d) a circle**
