# Video script — The six simple machines — and compound machines

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

The six simple machines fall into two families.
The inclined-plane family: the inclined plane itself, the wedge, and the screw — each is a slope, still or moving or wrapped.
The lever family: the lever, the wheel and axle, and the pulley — each turns about a point or axle.
A compound machine combines simple machines into one tool: a bicycle joins wheel and axle, levers and a screw or two; wire cutters join levers to wedges; a car is a compound machine of many parts.

Remember: **Inclined-plane family: inclined plane, wedge, screw.**
Remember: **Lever family: lever, wheel and axle, pulley.**
Remember: **A compound machine combines simple machines — bicycle, wire cutters, car.**
