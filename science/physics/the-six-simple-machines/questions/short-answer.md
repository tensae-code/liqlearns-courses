# The six simple machines — and compound machines — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** The screw belongs to which family?
**Answer: The inclined-plane family — it is a wrapped slope.**

**2.** Name the lever family.
**Answer: Lever, wheel and axle, pulley.**

**3.** Is a bicycle simple or compound?
**Answer: Compound — it combines several simple machines.**

**4.** Wire cutters combine which machines?
**Answer: Levers with wedges.**
