# The six simple machines — and compound machines — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The screw belongs to which family?
a) The inclined-plane family — it is a wrapped slope.  b) Lever, wheel and axle, pulley.  c) Compound — it combines several simple machines.  d) Levers with wedges.
**Answer: a) The inclined-plane family — it is a wrapped slope.**

**2.** Name the lever family.
a) The inclined-plane family — it is a wrapped slope.  b) Lever, wheel and axle, pulley.  c) Compound — it combines several simple machines.  d) Levers with wedges.
**Answer: b) Lever, wheel and axle, pulley.**

**3.** Is a bicycle simple or compound?
a) The inclined-plane family — it is a wrapped slope.  b) Lever, wheel and axle, pulley.  c) Compound — it combines several simple machines.  d) Levers with wedges.
**Answer: c) Compound — it combines several simple machines.**

**4.** Wire cutters combine which machines?
a) The inclined-plane family — it is a wrapped slope.  b) Lever, wheel and axle, pulley.  c) Compound — it combines several simple machines.  d) Levers with wedges.
**Answer: d) Levers with wedges.**
