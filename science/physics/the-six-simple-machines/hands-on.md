# Hands-on — The six simple machines — and compound machines

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Take a bicycle in your mind and strip it down: wheels, pedals, brakes, chain. Name the simple machine in each part.

## Apply it where you live
Choose one compound machine and list every simple machine inside it.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21839 — six types in a two-family tree; bicycle with component machines labelled] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
