# Limits of validity of Newton's laws — where classical mechanics stops working — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Newton's laws hold when speeds are far below the speed of _____.
**Answer: light**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The theory that takes over near light speed is _____.
**Answer: relativity**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The theory that governs inside atoms is _____ physics.
**Answer: quantum**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Newton's laws are the _____ case of the deeper theories at everyday speeds.
**Answer: limiting**
