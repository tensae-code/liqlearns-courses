# Limits of validity of Newton's laws — where classical mechanics stops working — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Newton's laws are fully reliable when…
**Answer: a) speeds are far below c, sizes far above atoms and gravity is not extreme  b) always, without exception  c) only for planets  d) only in a laboratory**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The speed of light c is about…
**Answer: a) 3 × 10⁸ m/s  b) 3 × 10⁵ m/s  c) 340 m/s  d) 1,000 m/s**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A clock moving very fast relative to you…
**Answer: a) runs slow compared with yours  b) runs fast  c) stops being a clock  d) is unaffected**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A massive body can reach the speed of light if pushed long enough.
**Answer: a) False — the energy needed grows without limit as v approaches c  b) True  c) Only in space  d) Only rockets can**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Inside an atom, Newton's smooth trajectories fail because…
**Answer: a) energy is exchanged in fixed quanta and only probabilities can be stated  b) atoms are too hot  c) gravity is absent  d) electrons are too heavy**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Mercury's orbit is evidence for general relativity because…
**Answer: a) its perihelion precesses by an extra 43 arc-seconds per century that Newton cannot explain  b) Mercury is the fastest planet  c) Mercury has no atmosphere  d) its year is short**
