# Limits of validity of Newton's laws — where classical mechanics stops working — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State the three edges of Newton's territory.
**Answer: Speeds far below c; sizes far above the atomic scale; gravitational fields far weaker than a black hole's.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why do muons created high in the atmosphere reach the ground even though their lifetime seems too short?
**Answer: Their moving clocks run slow (time dilation), so in Earth time they live long enough to complete the fall.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In what sense is Newton 'not wrong' even where his laws fail?
**Answer: The deeper theories reduce to Newton's laws at everyday speeds and sizes — his laws are their limiting case, exact enough wherever the territory holds.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) A GPS satellite's clock must be corrected for relativity. Which edge of the territory is involved?
**Answer: The speed edge (with a contribution from the gravity edge): the satellite's motion and its weaker gravity field both shift its clock rate measurably.**
