# Hands-on — Limits of validity of Newton's laws — where classical mechanics stops working

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Three-territory sort: take ten situations — a falling apple, an electron in an atom, a GPS satellite clock, a bridge in wind, a particle in an accelerator, Mercury's orbit, a kicked football, light bending near the Sun, a muon from space, a swinging pendulum. Sort them into Newton's territory / relativity needed / quantum needed, and write the deciding feature (speed, size or gravity strength) beside each. (2) Muon arithmetic: at 0.98c a muon covers about 650 m in one 2.2 µs lifetime by Earth clocks. How many lifetimes would a 10 km fall take on Newton's account — and what does the muon's own slowed clock say instead?
