# Measuring area — counting squares for irregular surfaces — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A rectangle's area rule, length × width, is a collapsed count of unit _____.
**Answer: squares**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A part square more than half covered is counted as _____ in the more-than-half rule.
**Answer: one**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The reported area is the _____ of repeated counts.
**Answer: mean**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Area is reported in _____ units, such as cm².
**Answer: squared**
