# Hands-on — Measuring area — counting squares for irregular surfaces

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The area of your hand
You need: squared (centimetre) paper, a pencil, a calculator. Draw around your hand, fingers together. Count the whole squares inside (mark each with a dot). Count the part squares on the border. Work out the area twice: once counting every part square as a half, once with the more-than-half rule. Take the mean as your hand's area in cm². Say aloud: my hand covers about this many centimetre squares — and my two counts tell me how much to trust the number.
