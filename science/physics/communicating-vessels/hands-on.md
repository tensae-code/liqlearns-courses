# Hands-on — Communicating vessels

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with your hands
Make communicating vessels from a clear U-tube, or two bottles joined at the base by a tube. Pour coloured water in and mark the levels: tilt nothing, just look — both surfaces settle on one horizontal line. Add water to one side only and watch it share itself out. Then float a drop of oil into one arm and measure the two column heights from the join: the oil stands taller — its density is the smaller, in the ratio of the heights.
