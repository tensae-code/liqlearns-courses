# Communicating vessels — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Communicating vessels are vessels…
a) That hold the same amount  b) Joined so liquid can pass between them below the surface  c) Made of glass only  d) With lids
**Answer: b) Joined so liquid can pass between them below the surface**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) One liquid at rest in communicating vessels stands…
a) Higher in the wider vessel  b) Higher in the narrower vessel  c) At the same level in all arms  d) At random heights
**Answer: c) At the same level in all arms**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The level is the same because at the joining depth…
a) The volumes are equal  b) The pressures from both sides must be equal, and pressure depends on depth alone  c) The temperatures match  d) Surface tension pulls it flat
**Answer: b) The pressures from both sides must be equal, and pressure depends on depth alone**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Oil stands higher than the water it balances because oil is…
a) Heavier per volume  b) Less dense — a taller column is needed for the same pressure  c) Warmer  d) Thicker
**Answer: b) Less dense — a taller column is needed for the same pressure**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) For two balancing liquids, the column heights obey…
a) h₁ = h₂ always  b) ρ₁h₁ = ρ₂h₂  c) ρ₁ + h₁ = ρ₂ + h₂  d) The taller column is always water
**Answer: b) ρ₁h₁ = ρ₂h₂**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A water tower supplies a whole district because…
a) It has a strong pump running all day  b) Water seeks one level: the tower's height sets the pressure in every joined pipe  c) Pipes are wide  d) Towers are traditional
**Answer: b) Water seeks one level: the tower's height sets the pressure in every joined pipe**
