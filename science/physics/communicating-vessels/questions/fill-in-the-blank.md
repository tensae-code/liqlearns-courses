# Communicating vessels — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Vessels joined below the liquid surface are called ___ vessels.
**Answer:** communicating

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) At rest, one liquid's free surface stands at one ___ in all the arms.
**Answer:** level (height)

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) At the joining depth the ___ from both sides must be equal.
**Answer:** pressure

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Balancing liquids obey ρ₁h₁ = ρ₂___.
**Answer:** h₂
