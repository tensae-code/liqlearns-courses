# Video script — Communicating vessels

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **join two vessels at the bottom and pour liquid in: at rest the free surface stands at exactly one level in both, however different their shapes — because at the joining depth the pressure from the two sides must balance, and pressure in a liquid depends on depth alone**. Vessels that connect below the liquid's surface are communicating vessels. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — joined at the bottom, level at the top; why one level — pressure at the join; different liquids, and the vessels at work — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
