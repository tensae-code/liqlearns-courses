# Holography — recording the whole wavefront — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Film records intensity, which is proportional to the wave's _____ squared.
**Answer: amplitude**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The beam that travels straight to the plate, untouched by the object, is the _____ beam.
**Answer: reference**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Holography requires light that is _____, with a fixed phase relation between the beams.
**Answer: coherent**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Moving the head while viewing a hologram shows true _____: near parts shift against far parts.
**Answer: parallax**
