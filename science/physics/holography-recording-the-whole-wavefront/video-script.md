Today: **holography — recording the whole wavefront**. A photo keeps only brightness and throws the wave's phase away — and the depth goes with it. A hologram keeps both. Split a laser: one beam bounces off the object, the other comes straight as a reference, and their interference pattern on the plate writes the phase into microscopic fringes. Play the reference beam through that plate later and the original wavefront is reborn: an image in true three dimensions, that you can look around.
