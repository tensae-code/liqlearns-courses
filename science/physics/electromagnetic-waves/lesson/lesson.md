## Electromagnetic waves — light's family — the teaching
An electromagnetic wave is a pair of oscillating fields — electric and magnetic — travelling outward together, each one sustaining the other. It carries energy without carrying matter and, unlike mechanical waves, needs no medium: it crosses empty space at the speed of light. That is how sunlight reaches us through vacuum, and why a bell in an evacuated jar can be seen vibrating but not heard.

## The key ideas

- EM waves are oscillating electric and magnetic fields travelling together.
- They carry energy without matter and need no medium.
- They travel at the speed of light, even through vacuum.
