# Video script — Electromagnetic waves — light's family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

An electromagnetic wave is a pair of oscillating fields — electric and magnetic — travelling outward together, each one sustaining the other.
It carries energy without carrying matter and, unlike mechanical waves, needs no medium: it crosses empty space at the speed of light.
That is how sunlight reaches us through vacuum, and why a bell in an evacuated jar can be seen vibrating but not heard.

Remember: **EM waves are oscillating electric and magnetic fields travelling together.**
Remember: **They carry energy without matter and need no medium.**
Remember: **They travel at the speed of light, even through vacuum.**
