# Electromagnetic waves — light's family — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** What two fields make an EM wave?
**Answer: Electric and magnetic fields.**

**2.** Does an EM wave need a medium?
**Answer: No — it can travel through vacuum.**

**3.** Why can sunlight cross empty space but sound cannot?
**Answer: Light is an EM wave needing no medium; sound is mechanical and needs one.**

**4.** What speed do EM waves travel at?
**Answer: The speed of light.**
