# Electromagnetic waves — light's family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What two fields make an EM wave?
a) Electric and magnetic fields.  b) No — it can travel through vacuum.  c) Light is an EM wave needing no medium; sound is mechanical and needs one.  d) The speed of light.
**Answer: a) Electric and magnetic fields.**

**2.** Does an EM wave need a medium?
a) Electric and magnetic fields.  b) No — it can travel through vacuum.  c) Light is an EM wave needing no medium; sound is mechanical and needs one.  d) The speed of light.
**Answer: b) No — it can travel through vacuum.**

**3.** Why can sunlight cross empty space but sound cannot?
a) Electric and magnetic fields.  b) No — it can travel through vacuum.  c) Light is an EM wave needing no medium; sound is mechanical and needs one.  d) The speed of light.
**Answer: c) Light is an EM wave needing no medium; sound is mechanical and needs one.**

**4.** What speed do EM waves travel at?
a) Electric and magnetic fields.  b) No — it can travel through vacuum.  c) Light is an EM wave needing no medium; sound is mechanical and needs one.  d) The speed of light.
**Answer: d) The speed of light.**
