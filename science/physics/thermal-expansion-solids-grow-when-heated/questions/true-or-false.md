# Thermal expansion — solids grow when heated — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. Heating lengthens, widens and bulks solids: linear, area and volume expansion each with its coefficient; gaps in rails and bridges and the bimetallic strip are the standard applications, and the thin-ring question (does its hole widen or narrow?) is set as a thinking exercise.**
**Answer: True**
