# Thermal expansion — solids grow when heated — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. Give one key fact this lesson teaches about thermal expansion of solids.**
**Answer: Heating lengthens, widens and bulks solids: linear, area and volume expansion each with its coefficient; gaps in rails and bridges and the bimetallic strip are the standard applications, and the thin-ring question (does its hole widen or narrow?) is set as a thinking exercise.**
