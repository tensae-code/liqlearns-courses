# Video script — Microscopes and telescopes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Optical instruments chain the unit's lenses and mirrors.
A simple microscope is one converging lens used as a magnifier; a compound microscope stacks two — an objective making a magnified real image and an eyepiece magnifying it again.
Telescopes gather distant light: refracting telescopes use lenses, reflecting telescopes use a concave mirror — the kind at work at Ethiopia's Entoto Observatory.
Cameras and projectors are the same family: lenses placing real images where they can be kept or shown.

Remember: **A compound microscope uses two lenses — objective then eyepiece.**
Remember: **Refracting telescopes use lenses; reflecting telescopes use mirrors.**
Remember: **The Entoto Observatory telescope is this lesson's Ethiopian instrument case.**
