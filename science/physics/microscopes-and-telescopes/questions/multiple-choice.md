# Microscopes and telescopes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which two lenses make a compound microscope?
a) The objective and the eyepiece.  b) A concave mirror.  c) A single converging lens (a magnifier).  d) The Entoto Observatory.
**Answer: a) The objective and the eyepiece.**

**2.** A reflecting telescope gathers light with a — ?
a) The objective and the eyepiece.  b) A concave mirror.  c) A single converging lens (a magnifier).  d) The Entoto Observatory.
**Answer: b) A concave mirror.**

**3.** What does a simple microscope use?
a) The objective and the eyepiece.  b) A concave mirror.  c) A single converging lens (a magnifier).  d) The Entoto Observatory.
**Answer: c) A single converging lens (a magnifier).**

**4.** Which Ethiopian observatory does the book ask about?
a) The objective and the eyepiece.  b) A concave mirror.  c) A single converging lens (a magnifier).  d) The Entoto Observatory.
**Answer: d) The Entoto Observatory.**
