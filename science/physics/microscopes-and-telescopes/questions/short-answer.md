# Microscopes and telescopes — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Which two lenses make a compound microscope?
**Answer: The objective and the eyepiece.**

**2.** A reflecting telescope gathers light with a — ?
**Answer: A concave mirror.**

**3.** What does a simple microscope use?
**Answer: A single converging lens (a magnifier).**

**4.** Which Ethiopian observatory does the book ask about?
**Answer: The Entoto Observatory.**
