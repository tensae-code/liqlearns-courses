## Microscopes and telescopes — the teaching
Optical instruments chain the unit's lenses and mirrors. A simple microscope is one converging lens used as a magnifier; a compound microscope stacks two — an objective making a magnified real image and an eyepiece magnifying it again. Telescopes gather distant light: refracting telescopes use lenses, reflecting telescopes use a concave mirror — the kind at work at Ethiopia's Entoto Observatory. Cameras and projectors are the same family: lenses placing real images where they can be kept or shown.

## The key ideas

- A compound microscope uses two lenses — objective then eyepiece.
- Refracting telescopes use lenses; reflecting telescopes use mirrors.
- The Entoto Observatory telescope is this lesson's Ethiopian instrument case.

## Say it to your companion
Explain microscopes and telescopes to your companion in your own words. Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
