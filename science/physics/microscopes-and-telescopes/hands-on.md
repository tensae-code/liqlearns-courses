# Hands-on — Microscopes and telescopes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Stack two magnifiers to glimpse the compound microscope's trick: one lens makes an image, the second magnifies the image itself.

## Apply it where you live
Compare microscopes and telescopes in a table: object, lenses/mirrors, image.
