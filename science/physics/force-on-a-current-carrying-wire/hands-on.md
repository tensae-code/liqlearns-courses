# Hands-on — Force on a current-carrying wire — the motor effect

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
If a battery, wire and magnet are available, rest the wire across the magnet's poles and close the circuit for an instant: the wire kicks. That kick is a motor clearing its throat.

## Apply it where you live
Explain how the motor effect becomes a spinning motor, in three sentences.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21920 — wire between magnet poles kicking sideways when current flows] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using F = BIL: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
