# Force on a current-carrying wire — the motor effect — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** B = 0.5 T, I = 4 A, L = 0.2 m, perpendicular. Force?
a) F = BIL = 0.5 × 4 × 0.2 = 0.4 N.  b) It reverses direction.  c) The motor effect.  d) Zero (or minimal) — the wire must cross the field.
**Answer: a) F = BIL = 0.5 × 4 × 0.2 = 0.4 N.**

**2.** What happens to the force if the current reverses?
a) F = BIL = 0.5 × 4 × 0.2 = 0.4 N.  b) It reverses direction.  c) The motor effect.  d) Zero (or minimal) — the wire must cross the field.
**Answer: b) It reverses direction.**

**3.** Name this effect.
a) F = BIL = 0.5 × 4 × 0.2 = 0.4 N.  b) It reverses direction.  c) The motor effect.  d) Zero (or minimal) — the wire must cross the field.
**Answer: c) The motor effect.**

**4.** The wire lies parallel to the field. Force?
a) F = BIL = 0.5 × 4 × 0.2 = 0.4 N.  b) It reverses direction.  c) The motor effect.  d) Zero (or minimal) — the wire must cross the field.
**Answer: d) Zero (or minimal) — the wire must cross the field.**
