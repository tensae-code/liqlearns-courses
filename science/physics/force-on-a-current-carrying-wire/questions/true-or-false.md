# Force on a current-carrying wire — the motor effect — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1.** The motor effect: a current-carrying wire across a field is pushed.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Force grows with current, length in field and field strength — F = BIL (perpendicular).
**Answer: True**

**3.** The push on a current-carrying wire runs along the wire.
**Answer: False**
