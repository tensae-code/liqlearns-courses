# Blood, voice and eye — more physics in the body — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Blood circulation runs on fluid-flow physics (viscosity, continuity, turbulence) and soft-bodied animals like the earthworm move by Pascal's principle.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The voice is vibrating vocal cords setting air in motion to the ear.**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Nerve/muscle electricity and the eye as an optical imaging system complete the survey.**
**Answer: True**
