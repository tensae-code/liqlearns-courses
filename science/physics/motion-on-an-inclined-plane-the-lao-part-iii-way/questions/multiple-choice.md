# Motion on an inclined plane — the Lao Part III way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The component of a body's weight that pulls it down an inclined plane at angle θ is…
**Answer: a) mg sin θ  b) mg cos θ  c) mg tan θ  d) the whole mg**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) On an inclined plane the normal reaction equals…
**Answer: a) mg cos θ  b) mg sin θ  c) mg  d) zero**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A body slides down a smooth 30° plane. Its acceleration is…
**Answer: a) g sin 30° = about 4.9 m/s², whatever its mass  b) g, like free fall  c) bigger for a heavier body  d) zero until it is pushed**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) With kinetic friction μ on the plane, the acceleration is…
**Answer: a) g(sin θ − μ cos θ)  b) g(sin θ + μ cos θ)  c) g cos θ  d) μ g sin θ**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In the Lao Grade 10 book, incline motion is taught as…
**Answer: a) its own lesson in Part III, the part that re-teaches motion type by type  b) a footnote inside free fall  c) only in the teacher guide  d) part of circular motion**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) For slope distance s, the vertical height fallen is…
**Answer: a) s sin θ  b) s cos θ  c) s tan θ  d) s / sin θ**
