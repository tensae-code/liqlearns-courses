# Motion on an inclined plane — the Lao Part III way — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The weight component along the plane is mg _____ θ.
**Answer: sin**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) On a smooth plane the acceleration is g sin θ, so the body's _____ cancels out of the equation.
**Answer: mass**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A body begins to slide when tan θ exceeds the coefficient of _____ friction.
**Answer: static**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) In the Lao book's fixed lesson shape, worked _____ come between the concept and the exercises.
