# Hands-on — Motion on an inclined plane — the Lao Part III way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Roll it down the ruler ramp
Rest a ruler or stiff card against a pile of books to make a ramp and let a marble or toy car roll down from a marked start. Time it with a phone over a fixed ramp length, then double the height of the pile and time again. The steeper ramp (bigger sin θ) gives the shorter time every run — the down-slope component at work. School-bench work in the Lao book's local-materials spirit: ruler, books and a phone timer are the whole kit.
