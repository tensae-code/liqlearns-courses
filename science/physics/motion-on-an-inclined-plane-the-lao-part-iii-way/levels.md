# Motion on an inclined plane — the Lao Part III way — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — On a plane at angle θ the weight splits into mg sin θ along the slope and mg cos θ into it; the normal reaction is N = mg cos θ; On a smooth plane a = g sin θ — the mass cancels; With friction, a = g(sin θ − μ cos θ), and sliding starts only when tan θ > μ (static) — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
