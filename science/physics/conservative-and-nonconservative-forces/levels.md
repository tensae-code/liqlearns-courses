# Conservative and nonconservative forces — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — A conservative force's work between two points is the same on every path, and zero around a closed loop; Gravity and the spring force are conservative; Only conservative forces can store potential energy — mgh and ½kx²; Friction is nonconservative: its work grows with the path's length and is not recoverable; Mechanical energy is conserved when only conservative forces work; otherwise it changes by the nonconservative work — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
