# Conservative and nonconservative forces — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A conservative force's work is independent of the _____.
**Answer: path**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Around a closed loop its work is _____.
**Answer: zero**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Only _____ forces can store potential energy.
**Answer: conservative**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Friction is _____.
**Answer: nonconservative**
