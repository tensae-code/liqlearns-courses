# Conservative and nonconservative forces — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A conservative force's work between two points…
**Answer: a) is the same on every path  b) grows with the path  c) is always zero  d) depends on speed**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Around a closed loop, a conservative force's total work is…
**Answer: a) zero  b) mgh  c) the loop's length × force  d) infinite**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Potential energy can be stored against…
**Answer: a) conservative forces only  b) friction  c) any force  d) no force**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Elastic potential energy ½kx² belongs to…
**Answer: a) the spring force, a conservative force  b) friction  c) air resistance  d) the normal reaction**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Friction's work on a sliding body depends on…
**Answer: a) the length of the path travelled  b) the endpoints only  c) the height  d) nothing**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Mechanical energy is conserved when…
**Answer: a) only conservative forces do work  b) friction acts  c) the path is long  d) the body stops**
