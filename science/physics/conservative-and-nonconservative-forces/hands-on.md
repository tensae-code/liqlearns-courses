# Hands-on — Conservative and nonconservative forces

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Lift a mass to a shelf directly and via a ramp with a spring balance and ruler; compute gravity's work both ways. (2) Drag the mass around a chalked loop on the floor, measuring the drag force and path length; total friction's work. (3) Stretch a spring to the same mark by slow and jerky pulls; compare the force–extension area each time.
