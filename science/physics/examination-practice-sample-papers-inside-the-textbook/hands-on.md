# Hands-on — Examination practice — sample papers inside the textbook

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sit one honestly
Choose one sample paper (or one full practice set). Set the full allowed time on a clock in front of you, clear the desk of books and notes, and sit the paper in one stretch. Attempt every question; show all working. When the time ends, stop writing, even mid-sentence. Say aloud: this score is the true one — it is the one the revision plan is built from.

## Mark it and log it
Mark the paper strictly, question by question. In a notebook, log every lost mark in three columns: topic, marks lost, kind of loss (did not know it / knew it but slipped / ran out of time). Circle the biggest topic loss — that is the first lesson you revisit. Say aloud: the paper is finished; the log is the work that remains.
