# Examination practice — sample papers inside the textbook — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A sample paper inside a textbook is…
a) A list of topics  b) A complete mock examination, in examination form, spanning the course  c) The answers to the real examination  d) A summary chapter
**Answer: b) A complete mock examination, in examination form, spanning the course**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) This course's closing chapter carries how many full sample papers?
a) One  b) Two  c) Four  d) Ten
**Answer: c) Four**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) To sit a sample paper honestly you should…
a) Keep the textbook open for hard questions  b) Pause the clock when stuck  c) Take the full allowed time in one stretch, with no book or notes  d) Do only the questions you like
**Answer: c) Take the full allowed time in one stretch, with no book or notes**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Showing the working in a calculation matters because…
a) It makes the page look full  b) A correct method earns marks even when the final number slips  c) It uses up time  d) Markers require it for neatness only
**Answer: b) A correct method earns marks even when the final number slips**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The log of a marked paper records, for every mark lost…
a) Only the total score  b) The topic and the kind of loss  c) The time of day  d) Who sat nearby
**Answer: b) The topic and the kind of loss**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) After marking and logging Paper One, the next step is…
a) Sit Paper Two at once  b) Revisit the lessons the log names, redo their questions, then sit the next paper  c) Throw the paper away  d) Memorise the paper's answers
**Answer: b) Revisit the lessons the log names, redo their questions, then sit the next paper**
