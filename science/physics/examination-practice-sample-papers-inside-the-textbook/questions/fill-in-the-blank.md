# Examination practice — sample papers inside the textbook — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A complete mock examination printed for practice is called a _____ paper.
**Answer: sample**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) This course closes with _____ full sample papers inside the book.
**Answer: four**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A sample paper must be sat in one _____ , against the clock, with no book open.
**Answer: stretch**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In a calculation, always show your _____ — a right method earns marks even if the last number slips.
**Answer: working**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The written record of lost marks, by topic and kind of loss, is the _____.
**Answer: log**
