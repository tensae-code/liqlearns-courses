Today: **examination practice — sample papers inside the textbook**.

Some courses close with one more topic. This one closes with the examination itself: four full sample papers, printed inside the book. Sit each one honestly — full time, one stretch, no book, working shown. Then mark it strictly and write down every lost mark: which topic, and what kind of loss it was. That log tells you exactly what to revise before the next paper. Paper by paper, the surprises run out.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
