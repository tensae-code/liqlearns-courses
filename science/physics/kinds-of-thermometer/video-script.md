# Video script — Kinds of thermometer — which for which job

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Thermometers form a family, each bred for a job.
The laboratory thermometer measures general temperatures in experiments.
The clinical thermometer reads body temperature over a narrow range, precisely where health needs it.
The maximum–minimum thermometer remembers the highest and lowest temperatures since it was last reset — the weather watcher's tool.
Digital thermometers read electronically and display the number directly.
Choosing a thermometer means matching its range and its talent to the job.

Remember: **Clinical thermometers read body temperature over a narrow, precise range.**
Remember: **Maximum–minimum thermometers record the extremes since reset.**
Remember: **Laboratory thermometers serve general experiment work; digital ones display readings directly.**
