# Kinds of thermometer — which for which job — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Which thermometer suits a fever check?
**Answer: A clinical thermometer.**

**2.** A weather station wants last night's coldest point. Which thermometer?
**Answer: A maximum–minimum thermometer.**

**3.** Why not use a clinical thermometer for boiling water?
**Answer: Its narrow range does not reach boiling — match range to job.**

**4.** What is a digital thermometer's advantage?
**Answer: It displays the reading directly.**
