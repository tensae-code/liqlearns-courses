# Kinds of thermometer — which for which job — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which thermometer suits a fever check?
a) A clinical thermometer.  b) A maximum–minimum thermometer.  c) Its narrow range does not reach boiling — match range to job.  d) It displays the reading directly.
**Answer: a) A clinical thermometer.**

**2.** A weather station wants last night's coldest point. Which thermometer?
a) A clinical thermometer.  b) A maximum–minimum thermometer.  c) Its narrow range does not reach boiling — match range to job.  d) It displays the reading directly.
**Answer: b) A maximum–minimum thermometer.**

**3.** Why not use a clinical thermometer for boiling water?
a) A clinical thermometer.  b) A maximum–minimum thermometer.  c) Its narrow range does not reach boiling — match range to job.  d) It displays the reading directly.
**Answer: c) Its narrow range does not reach boiling — match range to job.**

**4.** What is a digital thermometer's advantage?
a) A clinical thermometer.  b) A maximum–minimum thermometer.  c) Its narrow range does not reach boiling — match range to job.  d) It displays the reading directly.
**Answer: d) It displays the reading directly.**
