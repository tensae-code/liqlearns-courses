# Hands-on — Kinds of thermometer — which for which job

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
List five temperature jobs around home and school, then assign each its thermometer from the family — and defend the one hard choice.

## Apply it where you live
Write a buying guide: three thermometers, three jobs, one line each.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21869 — thermometer family laid out with its jobs captioned] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
