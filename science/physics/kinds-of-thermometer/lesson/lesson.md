## Kinds of thermometer — which for which job — the teaching
Thermometers form a family, each bred for a job. The laboratory thermometer measures general temperatures in experiments. The clinical thermometer reads body temperature over a narrow range, precisely where health needs it. The maximum–minimum thermometer remembers the highest and lowest temperatures since it was last reset — the weather watcher's tool. Digital thermometers read electronically and display the number directly. Choosing a thermometer means matching its range and its talent to the job.

## The key ideas

- Clinical thermometers read body temperature over a narrow, precise range.
- Maximum–minimum thermometers record the extremes since reset.
- Laboratory thermometers serve general experiment work; digital ones display readings directly.
