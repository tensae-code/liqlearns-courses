# Converting units — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Convert 2.5 km to metres.
**Answer: 2.5 × 1000 = 2500 m.**

**2.** Convert 4500 g to kilograms.
**Answer: 4500 ÷ 1000 = 4.5 kg.**

**3.** Convert 2 h to seconds.
**Answer: 2 × 3600 = 7200 s.**

**4.** Convert 720 cm to metres.
**Answer: 720 ÷ 100 = 7.2 m.**
