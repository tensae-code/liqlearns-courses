# Converting units — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Convert 2.5 km to metres.
a) 2.5 × 1000 = 2500 m.  b) 4500 ÷ 1000 = 4.5 kg.  c) 2 × 3600 = 7200 s.  d) 720 ÷ 100 = 7.2 m.
**Answer: a) 2.5 × 1000 = 2500 m.**

**2.** Convert 4500 g to kilograms.
a) 2.5 × 1000 = 2500 m.  b) 4500 ÷ 1000 = 4.5 kg.  c) 2 × 3600 = 7200 s.  d) 720 ÷ 100 = 7.2 m.
**Answer: b) 4500 ÷ 1000 = 4.5 kg.**

**3.** Convert 2 h to seconds.
a) 2.5 × 1000 = 2500 m.  b) 4500 ÷ 1000 = 4.5 kg.  c) 2 × 3600 = 7200 s.  d) 720 ÷ 100 = 7.2 m.
**Answer: c) 2 × 3600 = 7200 s.**

**4.** Convert 720 cm to metres.
a) 2.5 × 1000 = 2500 m.  b) 4500 ÷ 1000 = 4.5 kg.  c) 2 × 3600 = 7200 s.  d) 720 ÷ 100 = 7.2 m.
**Answer: d) 720 ÷ 100 = 7.2 m.**
