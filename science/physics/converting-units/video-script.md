Converting units means re-expressing the same quantity in a different unit of the same kind — the quantity does not change, only its clothing.
Multiply or divide by the correct factor: 1 km = 1000 m, 1 m = 100 cm, 1 kg = 1000 g, 1 h = 3600 s.
Going to a smaller unit makes the number bigger; going to a bigger unit makes it smaller.
The same method works for prefixed units, non-SI units and derived units like m/s to km/h.

Remember: **Conversion changes the unit, never the quantity itself.**
Remember: **1 km = 1000 m, 1 kg = 1000 g, 1 h = 3600 s.**
Remember: **To a smaller unit the number grows; to a bigger unit it shrinks.**
