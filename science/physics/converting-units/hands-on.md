# Hands-on — Converting units

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
 Pace out a distance and call it metres. Now say it in centimetres without measuring again — you converted. Check by measuring a small part.

## Apply it where you live
Convert the measures on three food packets and one journey distance into SI base units.
