# Converting units

## Do it first
 Pace out a distance and call it metres. Now say it in centimetres without measuring again — you converted. Check by measuring a small part.

## Converting units — the teaching
Converting units means re-expressing the same quantity in a different unit of the same kind — the quantity does not change, only its clothing. Multiply or divide by the correct factor: 1 km = 1000 m, 1 m = 100 cm, 1 kg = 1000 g, 1 h = 3600 s. Going to a smaller unit makes the number bigger; going to a bigger unit makes it smaller. The same method works for prefixed units, non-SI units and derived units like m/s to km/h.

## The key ideas

- Conversion changes the unit, never the quantity itself.
- 1 km = 1000 m, 1 kg = 1000 g, 1 h = 3600 s.
- To a smaller unit the number grows; to a bigger unit it shrinks.

## Say it to your companion
Explain converting units to your companion in your own words. Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
