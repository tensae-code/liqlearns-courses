# Measuring resistance with a voltmeter and ammeter — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The method is named the _____ method.
**Answer: volt-ampere**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) On a V against I graph, the _____ of the line is the resistance.
**Answer: gradient**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Report the _____ of the R values from your pairs of readings.
**Answer: average**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A lamp's measured resistance grows with voltage because its filament gets _____.
**Answer: hotter**
