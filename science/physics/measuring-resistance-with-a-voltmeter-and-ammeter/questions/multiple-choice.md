# Measuring resistance with a voltmeter and ammeter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the volt-ampere circuit, the ammeter goes…
**Answer: a) in series with the resistor  b) in parallel across it  c) across the supply only  d) anywhere at all**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The voltmeter goes…
**Answer: a) in parallel across the resistor  b) in series  c) in place of the switch  d) across the ammeter**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Resistance is found from each pair of readings as…
**Answer: a) R = V/I  b) R = VI  c) R = I/V  d) R = V + I**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Several (V, I) pairs are taken, using a sliding rheostat, so that…
**Answer: a) the values can be averaged and checked on a V–I graph whose gradient is R  b) the resistor gets hotter  c) the meters wear evenly  d) the supply lasts longer**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Readings are taken quickly, with the switch opened between them, because…
**Answer: a) a warming resistor changes its own resistance mid-measurement  b) the battery recharges  c) the meters need rest  d) the rheostat cools the room**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A V–I plot for a fixed resistor should be…
**Answer: a) a straight line through the origin  b) a curve flattening out  c) a horizontal line  d) a scatter with no pattern**
