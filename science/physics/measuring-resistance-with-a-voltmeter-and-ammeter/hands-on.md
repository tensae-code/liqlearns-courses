# Hands-on — Measuring resistance with a voltmeter and ammeter

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Measure three unknown resistors
You are given three resistors with their markings covered. For each: wire supply, switch, rheostat, ammeter (series) and the unknown, with a voltmeter across the unknown only. (1) Set the rheostat to its safest (largest) value, close the switch, and note V and I. (2) Move the rheostat to take two more pairs, opening the switch between readings so the resistor stays cool. (3) Compute R from each pair and average; then plot V against I and check the gradient against your average. (4) Swap in the next resistor and repeat. Finish by uncovering the markings and comparing — your measured value should sit within a few percent, and your notes should say which reading you trusted least, and why.
