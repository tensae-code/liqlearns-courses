# Supercapacitors — storing far more charge — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Supercapacitor capacitance is measured in _____.
**Answer: farads**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The electrodes are porous _____ with enormous area.
**Answer: carbon**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Stored energy is ½C_____.
**Answer: V²**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Beside a battery's marathon, the supercapacitor runs the _____.
**Answer: sprint**
