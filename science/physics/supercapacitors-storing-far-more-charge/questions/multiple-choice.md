# Supercapacitors — storing far more charge — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A supercapacitor's capacitance is measured in…
**Answer: a) farads and thousands of farads  b) microfarads  c) picofarads  d) ohms**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Its huge capacitance comes from enormous electrode area and…
**Answer: a) a nanometre-scale charge separation  b) thicker plates  c) higher voltage  d) colder operation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Its stored energy is given by…
**Answer: a) ½CV², as for any capacitor  b) mgh  c) QV²  d) I²R**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) On discharge, a supercapacitor's voltage…
**Answer: a) falls steadily  b) stays on a plateau  c) rises  d) oscillates**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A supercapacitor's best work is…
**Answer: a) bursts of power and bridging beside a battery  b) replacing all batteries  c) storing a city's yearly energy  d) generating charge**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) It survives on the order of…
**Answer: a) hundreds of thousands of charge cycles  b) ten cycles  c) one cycle  d) no cycles**
