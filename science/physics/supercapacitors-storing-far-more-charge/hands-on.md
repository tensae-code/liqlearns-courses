# Hands-on — Supercapacitors — storing far more charge

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Charge a small supercapacitor (from a solar-garden-light or a kit) for ten seconds and run an LED or a small motor from it; time the run. (2) Repeat with an ordinary electrolytic capacitor of the largest capacitance available and compare the run times. (3) Log voltage against time as the supercapacitor discharges through the LED and sketch the falling curve against a battery's plateau.
