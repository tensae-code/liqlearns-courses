# Supercapacitors — storing far more charge — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — A supercapacitor stores charge on facing surfaces, like any capacitor — but in farads, not microfarads; Its electrodes are porous carbon: enormous area in a small volume; Its charge separation is a layer of ions — nanometres thick; Energy is still ½CV²; charging takes seconds and cycles number in the hundreds of thousands; Its work is bursts and bridges beside a battery: the sprint beside the marathon — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
