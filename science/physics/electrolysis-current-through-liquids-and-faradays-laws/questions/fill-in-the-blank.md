# Electrolysis — current through a liquid splits the substance — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A liquid that conducts electricity through moving ions is called an _____.
**Answer: electrolyte**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Positive ions are called _____ and move to the cathode.
**Answer: cations**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The splitting of a substance by an electric current is called _____.
**Answer: electrolysis**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) One mole of electrons carries a charge of about _____ coulombs — the Faraday constant.
**Answer: 96,500**
