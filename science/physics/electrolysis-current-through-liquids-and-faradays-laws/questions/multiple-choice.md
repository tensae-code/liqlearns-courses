# Electrolysis — current through a liquid splits the substance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does solid salt not conduct electricity while salt solution does?
**Answer: a) in the solid the ions are locked in place; dissolved, they are free to move  b) the solid has no ions  c) water creates new charges  d) the solid's ions are all neutral**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In an electrolysis cell, cations move toward…
**Answer: a) the cathode, the negative electrode  b) the anode, the positive electrode  c) neither electrode  d) the battery's positive terminal directly**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) When water is electrolysed, the gases at the cathode and anode are…
**Answer: a) hydrogen at the cathode, oxygen at the anode, in the volume ratio 2:1  b) oxygen at the cathode, hydrogen at the anode  c) hydrogen at both electrodes  d) steam at both electrodes**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Electroplating uses electrolysis to…
**Answer: a) coat one metal with a thin skin of another metal  b) melt metals together into an alloy  c) magnetise a metal bar  d) dissolve a metal completely**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Faraday's first law says the mass changed at an electrode is proportional to…
**Answer: a) the charge passed  b) the voltage alone  c) the area of the beaker  d) the temperature of the liquid**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Passing 96,500 C through a solution of Ag⁺ ions (z = 1) deposits…
**Answer: a) one mole of silver — 108 g  b) half a mole of silver  c) two moles of silver  d) 1 g of silver**
