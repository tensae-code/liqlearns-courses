# Collisions — bouncing and sticking — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. Collisions are sorted by whether kinetic energy survives: elastic collisions conserve both momentum and KE, perfectly inelastic ones stick together (the loaded dump-truck example) and lose the maximum KE.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Collisions are sorted by whether kinetic energy survives: elastic collisions conserve both momentum and KE, perfectly inelastic ones stick together (the loaded dump-truck example) and lose the minimum KE.**
**Answer: False**
