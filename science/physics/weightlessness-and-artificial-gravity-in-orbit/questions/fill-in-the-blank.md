# Weightlessness and artificial gravity in orbit — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Orbit is continuous ___ fall that never lands.
**Answer:** free

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Weightlessness is the absence of the ___ force, not of gravity.
**Answer:** support (normal)

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Artificial gravity at a rim equals ___ / r.
**Answer:** v²

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A rotating station's fake gravity grows with distance from the ___.
**Answer:** hub (axis)
