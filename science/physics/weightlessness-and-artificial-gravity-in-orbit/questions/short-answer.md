# Weightlessness and artificial gravity in orbit — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Distinguish felt weight from gravity.
**Answer:** Felt weight is the support force on a body; gravity is the Earth's pull — orbit removes the first, not the second.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does water stop leaking from a falling holed cup?
**Answer:** Water and cup accelerate together at g; no relative pressure drives the leak while both fall freely.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How could a station's spin be set to fake Earth gravity?
**Answer:** Choose ω so that ω²r = 9.8 m/s² at the rim — larger radius lets it spin slower.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Name one oddity of artificial gravity.
**Answer:** It decreases toward the hub (a ∝ r) and moving in it brings Coriolis effects.
