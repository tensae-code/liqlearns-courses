# Weightlessness and artificial gravity in orbit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Astronauts in orbit feel weightless because…
a) Gravity is zero in space  b) They and their craft fall together, so no support force acts  c) The station has no mass  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) They and their craft fall together, so no support force acts**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Gravity at space-station height is…
a) Exactly zero  b) Still most of its surface value  c) Negative  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Still most of its surface value**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In orbit, a massive tool's inertia is…
a) Zero  b) Unchanged — as hard to accelerate as on Earth  c) Doubled  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Unchanged — as hard to accelerate as on Earth**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) What a scale measures, and what vanishes in orbit, is…
a) Mass  b) The support (normal) force  c) Gravity itself  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) The support (normal) force**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Artificial gravity in a rotating station comes from…
a) Real gravity increasing  b) The rim floor's inward push supplying centripetal force  c) Magnets in the floor  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) The rim floor's inward push supplying centripetal force**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The artificial gravity at the rim is a = …
a) mg  b) v²/r = ω²r  c) r/v  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) v²/r = ω²r**
