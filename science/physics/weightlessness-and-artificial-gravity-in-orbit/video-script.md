# Video script — Weightlessness and artificial gravity in orbit

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **orbiting astronauts feel weightless not because gravity has stopped but because they and their craft fall together, freely, all the time — and a rotating station can manufacture a substitute gravity from centripetal acceleration, sized by v²/r**. A satellite is a projectile that never lands: gravity bends its path into orbit while it falls continuously toward the Earth. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — free fall all the way round; what weightlessness does and does not change; artificial gravity — spin the station — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
