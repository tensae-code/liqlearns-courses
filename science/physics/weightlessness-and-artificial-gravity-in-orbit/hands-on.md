# Hands-on — Weightlessness and artificial gravity in orbit

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel the principle on Earth in miniature
Feel the principle on Earth in miniature: drop a cup with a hole in its side, filled with water — while it falls, the leak stops, because water and cup fall together (do it over a tray, outside). Then swing a bucket of water overhead: at the top, the bucket's push on the water is its artificial gravity — slow down until the water falls out and estimate the failing speed.
