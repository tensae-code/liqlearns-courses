# Transformers — stepping voltage up and down — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. Mutual induction between two coils on one core steps AC voltage up or down by the turns ratio (V_s/V_p = N_s/N_p), with efficiency as output over input power; home examples: phone/laptop chargers step down, a microwave steps up.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Mutual induction between two coils on one core steps AC voltage down or down by the turns ratio (V_s/V_p = N_s/N_p), with efficiency as output over input power; home examples: phone/laptop chargers step down, a microwave steps up.**
**Answer: False**
