# Hands-on — Adding light, subtracting light — colour mixing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Overlap coloured cellophane or phone torches with colour filters on a wall: add lights toward white, then stack the filters toward darkness — both mixings in one minute.

## Apply it where you live
Predict the appearance of red, green and blue objects under magenta light, with reasons.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21940 — stage lights mixing to white on one side; stacked filters darkening to black on the other] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using R+G=Y; G+B=C; B+R=M; R+G+B=white: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
