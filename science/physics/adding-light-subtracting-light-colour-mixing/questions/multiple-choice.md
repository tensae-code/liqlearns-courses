# Adding light, subtracting light — colour mixing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Red and green lights overlap. What colour appears?
a) Yellow.  b) White.  c) Blue.  d) Red and blue (it absorbs green).
**Answer: a) Yellow.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** All three primaries of light together make — ?
a) Yellow.  b) White.  c) Blue.  d) Red and blue (it absorbs green).
**Answer: b) White.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A yellow filter absorbs which primary?
a) Yellow.  b) White.  c) Blue.  d) Red and blue (it absorbs green).
**Answer: c) Blue.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A magenta book cover under white light reflects what?
a) Yellow.  b) White.  c) Blue.  d) Red and blue (it absorbs green).
**Answer: d) Red and blue (it absorbs green).**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The same magenta cover under pure green light looks — ?
a) Black/dark — it absorbs green and there is no red or blue to reflect.  b) Yellow.  c) White.  d) Blue.
**Answer: a) Black/dark — it absorbs green and there is no red or blue to reflect.**
