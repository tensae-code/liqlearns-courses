# Force on a moving charge in a magnetic field — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** q = 2 C, v = 3 m/s, B = 4 T, perpendicular. Force?
a) F = qvB = 2 × 3 × 4 = 24 N.  b) Zero — no motion, no force.  c) Zero.  d) Doubles it.
**Answer: a) F = qvB = 2 × 3 × 4 = 24 N.**

**2.** A stationary charge sits in a strong field. Force?
a) F = qvB = 2 × 3 × 4 = 24 N.  b) Zero — no motion, no force.  c) Zero.  d) Doubles it.
**Answer: b) Zero — no motion, no force.**

**3.** A charge moves parallel to the field lines. Force?
a) F = qvB = 2 × 3 × 4 = 24 N.  b) Zero — no motion, no force.  c) Zero.  d) Doubles it.
**Answer: c) Zero.**

**4.** Doubling v does what to the force?
a) F = qvB = 2 × 3 × 4 = 24 N.  b) Zero — no motion, no force.  c) Zero.  d) Doubles it.
**Answer: d) Doubles it.**
