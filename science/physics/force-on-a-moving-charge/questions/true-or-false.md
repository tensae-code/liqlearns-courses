# Force on a moving charge in a magnetic field — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1.** F = qvB when velocity is perpendicular to the field.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The force is perpendicular to both velocity and field — paths bend sideways.
**Answer: True**

**3.** A magnetic field pushes a stationary charge sideways.
**Answer: False**
