# Force on a moving charge in a magnetic field — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** q = 2 C, v = 3 m/s, B = 4 T, perpendicular. Force?
**Answer: F = qvB = 2 × 3 × 4 = 24 N.**

**2.** A stationary charge sits in a strong field. Force?
**Answer: Zero — no motion, no force.**

**3.** A charge moves parallel to the field lines. Force?
**Answer: Zero.**

**4.** Doubling v does what to the force?
**Answer: Doubles it.**
