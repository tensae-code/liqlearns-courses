# Video script — Force on a moving charge in a magnetic field

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

A charge moving through a magnetic field feels a force — but only when it moves across the field.
In the clean case, velocity perpendicular to a uniform field, the force is F = qvB: proportional to the charge, its velocity and the field strength.
Its direction is perpendicular to both the velocity and the field, so the charge's path bends sideways.
Two zero cases matter: a charge at rest feels nothing, and a charge moving along the field feels nothing — crossing is everything.

Remember: **F = qvB when velocity is perpendicular to the field.**
Remember: **The force is perpendicular to both velocity and field — paths bend sideways.**
Remember: **No force on a charge at rest, or moving along the field.**
Keep the formula: **F = qvB**
