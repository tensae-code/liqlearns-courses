# Hands-on — Force on a moving charge in a magnetic field

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Roll a ball straight across the floor, then roll it again while a partner gently fans air across its path. The sideways drift is the picture of qvB's direction — always across, never along.

## Apply it where you live
For three cases (rest, along field, across field), state the force and sketch the path.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21918 — charge's path bending sideways as it enters a field region] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using F = qvB: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
