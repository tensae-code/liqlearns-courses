# Video script — Hooke's law — springs stretch in proportion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Hooke's law: within the elastic limit, a spring's extension is proportional to the applied force, F = kx.
Double the load, double the stretch — a straight-line promise you can test with a spring, masses and a ruler.
Pass the elastic limit and the promise breaks: the spring is permanently damaged.
The same oscillator family includes the stretched string: flick it and a pulse travels along it, the string itself staying in place.

Remember: **Hooke's law: F = kx, within the elastic limit.**
Remember: **Extension is proportional to load — double load, double stretch.**
Remember: **Beyond the elastic limit the spring is permanently damaged.**
Keep the formula: **F = kx**
