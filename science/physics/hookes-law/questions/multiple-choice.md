# Hooke's law — springs stretch in proportion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A spring with k = 200 N/m stretches 0.05 m. Force?
a) F = kx = 200 × 0.05 = 10 N.  b) k = F/x = 6 ÷ 0.03 = 200 N/m.  c) The greatest stretch from which the spring still returns to its original length.  d) The pulse travels; the string stays in place.
**Answer: a) F = kx = 200 × 0.05 = 10 N.**
