# Hooke's law — springs stretch in proportion — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A spring with k = 200 N/m stretches 0.05 m. Force?
**Answer: F = kx = 200 × 0.05 = 10 N.**

**2.** A 6 N load stretches a spring 3 cm. k?
**Answer: k = F/x = 6 ÷ 0.03 = 200 N/m.**

**3.** What is the elastic limit?
**Answer: The greatest stretch from which the spring still returns to its original length.**

**4.** A flick sends a pulse along a string. What travels — string or pulse?
**Answer: The pulse travels; the string stays in place.**
