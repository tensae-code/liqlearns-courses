# Hooke's law — springs stretch in proportion

## Do it first
Stretch a rubber band with one coin's weight, then two, then three — measure each stretch. Equal added weights, equal added stretch: Hooke in your fingers.

## Hooke's law — springs stretch in proportion — the teaching
Hooke's law: within the elastic limit, a spring's extension is proportional to the applied force, F = kx. Double the load, double the stretch — a straight-line promise you can test with a spring, masses and a ruler. Pass the elastic limit and the promise breaks: the spring is permanently damaged. The same oscillator family includes the stretched string: flick it and a pulse travels along it, the string itself staying in place.

**Keep the formula:** F = kx

## The key ideas

- Hooke's law: F = kx, within the elastic limit.
- Extension is proportional to load — double load, double stretch.
- Beyond the elastic limit the spring is permanently damaged.
