# Hands-on — Hooke's law — springs stretch in proportion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Stretch a rubber band with one coin's weight, then two, then three — measure each stretch. Equal added weights, equal added stretch: Hooke in your fingers.

## Apply it where you live
Plot force against extension for a real spring or band and find k from the slope.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21855 — spring stretching in equal steps under equal added masses] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using F = kx: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
