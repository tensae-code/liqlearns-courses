# Ammeters and voltmeters — where they go — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. Ammeters go in series (near-zero resistance), voltmeters in parallel (near-infinite resistance); their connections are figured and exercised.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Ammeters go in parallel (near-zero resistance), voltmeters in parallel (near-infinite resistance); their connections are figured and exercised.**
**Answer: False**
