# Pulleys — fixed, movable, compound — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** How many supporting ropes give ideal MA 4?
**Answer: Four supporting rope segments.**

**2.** A fixed pulley's MA is — ?
**Answer: 1 — direction change only.**

**3.** A load of 400 N hangs on a movable pulley (ideal). Effort?
**Answer: About 200 N — MA 2.**

**4.** In a compound system, what do you count to predict MA?
**Answer: The rope segments supporting the moving part.**
