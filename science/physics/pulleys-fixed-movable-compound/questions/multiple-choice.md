# Pulleys — fixed, movable, compound — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many supporting ropes give ideal MA 4?
a) Four supporting rope segments.  b) 1 — direction change only.  c) About 200 N — MA 2.  d) The rope segments supporting the moving part.
**Answer: a) Four supporting rope segments.**

**2.** A fixed pulley's MA is — ?
a) Four supporting rope segments.  b) 1 — direction change only.  c) About 200 N — MA 2.  d) The rope segments supporting the moving part.
**Answer: b) 1 — direction change only.**

**3.** A load of 400 N hangs on a movable pulley (ideal). Effort?
a) Four supporting rope segments.  b) 1 — direction change only.  c) About 200 N — MA 2.  d) The rope segments supporting the moving part.
**Answer: c) About 200 N — MA 2.**

**4.** In a compound system, what do you count to predict MA?
a) Four supporting rope segments.  b) 1 — direction change only.  c) About 200 N — MA 2.  d) The rope segments supporting the moving part.
**Answer: d) The rope segments supporting the moving part.**
