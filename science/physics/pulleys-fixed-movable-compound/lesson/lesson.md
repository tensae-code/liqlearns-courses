# Pulleys — fixed, movable, compound

## Do it first
Rig a fixed pulley over a beam and lift a bucket by pulling down. Then rig the pulley to the bucket itself and lift again — feel the movable pulley's gift.

[Picture 21848 — three pulley setups lifting the same load with different efforts]

## Pulleys — fixed, movable, compound — the teaching
A pulley is a grooved wheel that turns freely on its axle, guiding a rope. A fixed pulley changes direction only — pull down, load up, MA 1. A movable pulley travels with the load and multiplies force. Compound systems combine fixed and movable pulleys, and the MA follows the number of rope segments supporting the moving part. Build each system and count the supporting ropes — the count predicts the effort before you ever lift.

## The key ideas

- A fixed pulley changes direction only (MA 1).
- A movable pulley multiplies force and moves with the load.
- Compound MA follows the number of supporting rope segments.

## Say it to your companion
Explain pulleys — fixed, movable, compound to your companion in your own words. Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
