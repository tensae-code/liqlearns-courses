# Video script — Pulleys — fixed, movable, compound

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

A pulley is a grooved wheel that turns freely on its axle, guiding a rope.
A fixed pulley changes direction only — pull down, load up, MA 1.
A movable pulley travels with the load and multiplies force.
Compound systems combine fixed and movable pulleys, and the MA follows the number of rope segments supporting the moving part.
Build each system and count the supporting ropes — the count predicts the effort before you ever lift.

Remember: **A fixed pulley changes direction only (MA 1).**
Remember: **A movable pulley multiplies force and moves with the load.**
Remember: **Compound MA follows the number of supporting rope segments.**
