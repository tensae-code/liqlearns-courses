# Hands-on — Pulleys — fixed, movable, compound

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Rig a fixed pulley over a beam and lift a bucket by pulling down. Then rig the pulley to the bucket itself and lift again — feel the movable pulley's gift.

## Apply it where you live
Construct a compound pulley from local materials and verify its MA with a spring balance.
