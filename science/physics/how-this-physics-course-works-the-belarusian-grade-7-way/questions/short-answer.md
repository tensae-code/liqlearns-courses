# How this Physics course works — the Belarusian Grade 7 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the four moves of the frontispiece method, in order.
**Answer: Quick read; deep read; reproduce every derivation in your own notebook; repeat the experiments at home.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is small print used for in this book, and must you do it?
**Answer: Small print marks optional material beyond the core; it is not required — the main road through the paragraph never depends on it.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Where in the book do you check your exercise answers, and when should you look?
**Answer: At the back of the book, where the answers to the exercises are printed (from printed p. 178) — after working the exercise, not before.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) List Grade 7's three physics chapters in order.
**Answer: Ch.1 Motion and forces; Ch.2 Work and energy, simple mechanisms; Ch.3 Pressure — then Ch.4 Laboratory experiment.**
