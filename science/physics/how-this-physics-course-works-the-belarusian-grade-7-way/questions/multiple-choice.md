# How this Physics course works — the Belarusian Grade 7 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The frontispiece's first move with a new paragraph is to…
**Answer: a) read it quickly, to get its shape  b) memorise it word for word  c) copy it into a notebook  d) skip straight to the exercises**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) «Reproduce every derivation in your notebook» means…
**Answer: a) write the derivation out yourself, in your own hand, until its steps are yours  b) photocopy the page  c) read the derivation aloud once  d) skip derivations entirely**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Every paragraph of the book ends with…
**Answer: a) «Main conclusions» and control questions  b) a poem  c) the next chapter's title  d) a star**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A star beside an exercise marks it as…
**Answer: a) the hardest — graded at the top of the difficulty scale  b) optional  c) wrong  d) a laboratory task**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Grade 7's third chapter is…
**Answer: a) Pressure  b) Light  c) Electricity  d) The atom**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The book's last chapter, Ch.4, is given over to…
**Answer: a) laboratory experiment — a practicum of hands-on work  b) revision poems  c) biographies  d) the index**
