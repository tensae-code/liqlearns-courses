# Wheel and axle — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A windlass lifts a bucket by effort on the — wheel or axle?
**Answer: The wheel (the handle) — force multiplication.**

**2.** In a bicycle, effort drives the axle and speed comes at the — ?
**Answer: The wheel's rim — speed multiplication.**

**3.** Name two wheel-and-axle devices.
**Answer: Any two of: screwdriver, windmill, doorknob, windlass.**

**4.** What sets the size of the trade?
**Answer: The ratio of wheel radius to axle radius.**
