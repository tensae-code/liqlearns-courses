# Wheel and axle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A windlass lifts a bucket by effort on the — wheel or axle?
a) The wheel (the handle) — force multiplication.  b) The wheel's rim — speed multiplication.  c) Any two of: screwdriver, windmill, doorknob, windlass.  d) The ratio of wheel radius to axle radius.
**Answer: a) The wheel (the handle) — force multiplication.**

**2.** In a bicycle, effort drives the axle and speed comes at the — ?
a) The wheel (the handle) — force multiplication.  b) The wheel's rim — speed multiplication.  c) Any two of: screwdriver, windmill, doorknob, windlass.  d) The ratio of wheel radius to axle radius.
**Answer: b) The wheel's rim — speed multiplication.**

**3.** Name two wheel-and-axle devices.
a) The wheel (the handle) — force multiplication.  b) The wheel's rim — speed multiplication.  c) Any two of: screwdriver, windmill, doorknob, windlass.  d) The ratio of wheel radius to axle radius.
**Answer: c) Any two of: screwdriver, windmill, doorknob, windlass.**

**4.** What sets the size of the trade?
a) The wheel (the handle) — force multiplication.  b) The wheel's rim — speed multiplication.  c) Any two of: screwdriver, windmill, doorknob, windlass.  d) The ratio of wheel radius to axle radius.
**Answer: d) The ratio of wheel radius to axle radius.**
