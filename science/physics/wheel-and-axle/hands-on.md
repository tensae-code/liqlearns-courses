# Hands-on — Wheel and axle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Wind a string around a pencil (axle) using a bottle cap as the wheel and lift a small weight. Then build the same from local materials as the book directs.

## Apply it where you live
Build a working windlass from local materials and test what it lifts.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21846 — windlass drawing water from a well, wheel and axle labelled] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
