**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** Wheel and axle
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Simple Machines — Grade 9 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Wheel and axle: a large wheel fixed to a smaller axle, turning together.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 9 lessons before it in Unit 5 and on everyday observation; it needs nothing else to start.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Wheel and axle: a large wheel fixed to a smaller axle, turning together.
2. Effort on the wheel multiplies force (windlass).
3. Effort on the axle multiplies speed (bicycle wheel).
4. Answer the lesson's questions, including any calculation, with working shown.

## If the learner is stuck
- Cannot start → return to Do it first and do it with real objects, slowly, naming each step out loud.
- Mixes up the key ideas → go back to the key-ideas list in lesson/lesson.md and test each one against the picture [Picture 21846].
