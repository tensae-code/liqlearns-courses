# Video script — Wheel and axle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

A wheel and axle is a large wheel fixed to a smaller axle, turning together.
Put the effort on the wheel and the axle winds or lifts with multiplied force — screwdriver, windmill, doorknob, and the windlass that draws water from a well.
Put the effort on the axle instead and the wheel's rim travels fast — the speed-multiplying arrangement in bicycles, car wheels and fans.
The ratio of wheel radius to axle radius sets the trade.

Remember: **Wheel and axle: a large wheel fixed to a smaller axle, turning together.**
Remember: **Effort on the wheel multiplies force (windlass).**
Remember: **Effort on the axle multiplies speed (bicycle wheel).**
