# Absolute and gauge pressure — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Gas pressure from trapped, squeezed air (the crush-the-air activity).**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Absolute pressure counts from vacuum, gauge pressure from atmospheric.**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) And the two convert by adding/subtracting atmospheric pressure.**
**Answer: True**
