# Absolute and gauge pressure — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about pressure in gases; absolute and gauge pressure is correct?**
a) Flux Φ = BA cosθ counts field through an area and is greatest face-on, zero edge-on (the 0°/45°/90° cases are…  b) Gas pressure from trapped, squeezed air (the crush-the-air activity).  c) Force as push/pull that changes motion or shape; inertia is the resistance to change, measured by mass; the first law…
**Answer: b) Gas pressure from trapped, squeezed air (the crush-the-air activity).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Gas ______ from trapped, squeezed air (the crush-the-air activity).**

**Answer: b) pressure**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) And the two convert by adding/subtracting atmospheric ______.**
