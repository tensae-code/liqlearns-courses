# Hands-on — Electric fields and field lines

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Shake pepper or small seeds in oil over a charged object as the book's demonstrations suggest, or simply draw the radial pattern from memory — then check the arrows' direction twice.

## Apply it where you live
Draw field patterns for a lone negative charge and a +/− pair, and mark where each field is strongest.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21902 — field lines radiating from + and into − charges, and the dipole pattern] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using E = F / q: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
