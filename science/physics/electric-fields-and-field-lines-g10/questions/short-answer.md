# Electric fields and field lines — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A test charge of 2 C feels 10 N in a field. E?
**Answer: E = F/q = 10 ÷ 2 = 5 N/C.**

**2.** Which way do field lines point around a lone positive charge?
**Answer: Radially outward, away from the charge.**

**3.** What does a test charge do?
**Answer: It samples the field's force without disturbing it much.**

**4.** Where is a field strongest on a field-line drawing?
**Answer: Where the lines crowd closest together.**
