# Electric fields and field lines — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A test charge of 2 C feels 10 N in a field. E?
a) E = F/q = 10 ÷ 2 = 5 N/C.  b) Radially outward, away from the charge.  c) It samples the field's force without disturbing it much.  d) Where the lines crowd closest together.
**Answer: a) E = F/q = 10 ÷ 2 = 5 N/C.**

**2.** Which way do field lines point around a lone positive charge?
a) E = F/q = 10 ÷ 2 = 5 N/C.  b) Radially outward, away from the charge.  c) It samples the field's force without disturbing it much.  d) Where the lines crowd closest together.
**Answer: b) Radially outward, away from the charge.**

**3.** What does a test charge do?
a) E = F/q = 10 ÷ 2 = 5 N/C.  b) Radially outward, away from the charge.  c) It samples the field's force without disturbing it much.  d) Where the lines crowd closest together.
**Answer: c) It samples the field's force without disturbing it much.**

**4.** Where is a field strongest on a field-line drawing?
a) E = F/q = 10 ÷ 2 = 5 N/C.  b) Radially outward, away from the charge.  c) It samples the field's force without disturbing it much.  d) Where the lines crowd closest together.
**Answer: d) Where the lines crowd closest together.**
