# Amount of substance — counting molecules with the mole — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) One mole of any substance contains…
a) One particle  b) 6.02 × 10²³ particles  c) A different number for each substance  d) 1000 particles
**Answer: b) 6.02 × 10²³ particles**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The amount of substance is measured in…
a) Kilograms  b) Moles  c) Litres  d) Particles per gram
**Answer: b) Moles**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The amount of substance in a sample is found by…
a) ν = m × M  b) ν = m/M — mass divided by molar mass  c) ν = M/m  d) ν = m + M
**Answer: b) ν = m/M — mass divided by molar mass**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The molar mass of water (H₂O) is…
a) 10 g/mol  b) 18 g/mol  c) 36 g/mol  d) 2 g/mol
**Answer: b) 18 g/mol**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) 54 g of water is…
a) 1 mol  b) 2 mol  c) 3 mol  d) 0.5 mol
**Answer: c) 3 mol**

**6.** (Sub lesson 1) Molecules must be counted by weighing because…
a) Balances are traditional  b) They are far too small and far too numerous to count directly  c) Molecules fear being counted  d) Only chemists may count
**Answer: b) They are far too small and far too numerous to count directly**
