# Hands-on — Amount of substance — counting molecules with the mole

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with your hands
Weigh a full glass of water, then the empty glass: the difference is near 200 g. Water's molar mass is 18 g/mol, so the glass holds about 11 mol — about 6.6 × 10²⁴ molecules. Now divide by the number of pupils in the class and give each their share to imagine. The exercise is the lesson: a kitchen balance just counted more particles than anyone could count grains of sand on Earth.
