# How this Physics course works — the Belarusian Grade 8 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State the frontispiece method in one sentence.
**Answer: Read each paragraph quickly, then deeply; reproduce every derivation in your notebook; and repeat the experiments at home.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How do you know an exercise is one of the hardest before you attempt it?
**Answer: It carries a star — the exercises are graded by difficulty and the hardest are starred.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Name Grade 8's three physics chapters and the closing chapter.
**Answer: Ch.1 Thermal phenomena; Ch.2 Electromagnetic phenomena; Ch.3 Light phenomena; and Ch.4 Laboratory experiment.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A pupil wants the density of aluminium mid-year. Where does the Grade 8 book keep it?
**Answer: In the density appendix at the back of the book (printed p. 179).**
