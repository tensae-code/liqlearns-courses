Today: **the Malaysian KSSM Form 5 way — the arc ends in quantum**. Form 5 finishes what Form 4 began. Mechanics returns harder — resultant forces, equilibrium, elasticity — then one chapter sweeps up all of pressure: liquids, air, gas, Pascal, Archimedes and Bernoulli in a single run. Electricity and electromagnetism take two chapters; electronics opens, unusually, with the electron itself. And then the point of the whole arc: the course ends in modern physics, with nuclear physics and a full chapter of quantum physics — photons and the photoelectric effect — taught as examination core for every student, not as enrichment for a few. Malaysia's school physics ends where physics itself is newest.
