# Lesson plan — How this Physics course works — the Malaysian KSSM Form 5 way

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** How this Physics course works — the Malaysian KSSM Form 5 way
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18) — a new lesson built for the Malaysia placement named in lesson/sources.md
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Form 5 book completes the two-year KSSM arc in four themes — Newtonian Mechanics, Electricity and Electromagnetism, Applied Physics, and **Modern Physics** — gathering all of pressure into one applied sweep and ending school physics with **nuclear and quantum physics as examined core**, quantum theory of light and the photoelectric effect included

## Sub lessons
1. **The Form 5 map — four themes, seven chapters** — The Form 5 book (Penerbit Bestari, for the Ministry of Education Malaysia, KSSM, first published 2020) runs in four themes. **Theme 1, Newtonian Mechanics:** Ch. 1 Force and Motion II — resultant force, resolution of forces, equilibrium, elasticity — and Ch. 2 Pressure. **Theme 2, Electricity and Electromagnetism:** Ch. 3 Electricity (current, resistance, e.m.f. and internal resistance, electrical power) and Ch. 4 Electromagnetism (force on a current-carrying conductor, induction, transformers). **Theme 3, Applied Physics:** Ch. 5 Electronics. **Theme 4, Modern Physics:** Ch. 6 Nuclear Physics and Ch. 7 Quantum Physics. Like the Form 4 book, it is issued whole in Malay (Fizik Tingkatan 5) and in English DLP in parallel, and its chapters close with SPM-format evaluations as Form 4's do.
2. **Malaysia's own sweeps — all of pressure in one chapter; electronics from the electron** — Two arrangements are this book's signature. First, **pressure is one applied sweep**: Ch. 2 takes pressure in liquids, atmospheric pressure, gas pressure, Pascal's principle, Archimedes' principle and Bernoulli's principle in a single chapter (§§2.1–2.6) — one idea, squeezed force, followed through every fluid and every instrument without leaving the chapter. Second, **electronics begins with the electron**: Ch. 5 opens with §5.1 Electron — thermionic emission and the cathode-ray oscilloscope — before the semiconductor diode (§5.2) and the transistor (§5.3): the particle first, the devices built from it after. Both sweeps show the KSSM habit of organising by the idea, not by the apparatus.
3. **The ending — nuclear and quantum as examined core** — The two-year KSSM arc ends in **Theme 4, Modern Physics**, and it is core, not decoration. Ch. 6 Nuclear Physics teaches radioactive decay (with half-life) and nuclear energy — fission, chain reaction, fusion. Then Ch. 7 **Quantum Physics** closes the book and the course: §7.1 Quantum Theory of Light (black-body radiation and photons), §7.2 the Photoelectric Effect, §7.3 Einstein's Photoelectric Theory — the quantum theory of light taught and examined for every SPM candidate. Where many school courses end at circuits, Malaysia's ends at the photon: the newest physics is the final, examined word of the two-year course.
