# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Malaysian KSSM Form 5 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Drawing the two-year arc
On one long sheet, draw the whole KSSM journey as a road with two year-marks. Year one (Form 4): Measurement → Force and Motion I → Gravitation → Heat → Waves → Light and Optics. Year two (Form 5): Force and Motion II → Pressure → Electricity → Electromagnetism → Electronics → Nuclear Physics → Quantum Physics. Circle the two signature stops — the Pressure sweep and the Quantum ending — and under the last stop write: 'examined core, not enrichment.' Say aloud: the road ends at the photon, and the examination walks the whole road.
