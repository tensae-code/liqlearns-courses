# How this Physics course works — the Malaysian KSSM Form 5 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) List the Form 5 themes in order, with the chapters under Theme 2.
**Answer: Answer: Newtonian Mechanics; Electricity and Electromagnetism; Applied Physics; Modern Physics. Theme 2 holds Ch. 3 Electricity and Ch. 4 Electromagnetism.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the six sections of the Pressure chapter in order and say what makes the chapter a 'sweep'.
**Answer: Answer: Pressure in liquids; atmospheric pressure; gas pressure; Pascal's principle; Archimedes' principle; Bernoulli's principle. It is a sweep because one idea — pressure — is followed through every fluid, instrument and application inside a single chapter.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Which sections make up Ch. 7 Quantum Physics, and why does their position matter?
**Answer: Answer: §7.1 Quantum Theory of Light, §7.2 the Photoelectric Effect and §7.3 Einstein's Photoelectric Theory. They are the closing chapter of the whole two-year course and are examined SPM core, so the course's final word is its newest physics.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) How does the Form 5 book's structure complete the promise of the Form 4 book?
**Answer: Answer: Form 4 laid the classical foundations by themes — measurement, mechanics, heat, waves and optics. Form 5 deepens mechanics and electricity, applies physics in electronics, and then goes beyond the classical world entirely, ending in nuclear and quantum physics — the arc runs from measuring a length to the photon.**
