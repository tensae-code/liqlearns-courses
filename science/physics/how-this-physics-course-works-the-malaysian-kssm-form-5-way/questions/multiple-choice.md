# How this Physics course works — the Malaysian KSSM Form 5 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How many themes organise the Form 5 book?
a) Two  b) Four  c) Six  d) Seven
**Answer: b) Four**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Ch. 2 Pressure ends (§2.6) with…
a) Pascal's principle  b) Archimedes' principle  c) Bernoulli's principle  d) Gas pressure
**Answer: c) Bernoulli's principle**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Electronics (Ch. 5) opens with…
a) The transistor  b) The diode as rectifier  c) The electron — thermionic emission and the cathode-ray oscilloscope  d) Logic gates
**Answer: c) The electron — thermionic emission and the cathode-ray oscilloscope**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The pressure sweep of Ch. 2 is an example of the KSSM habit of organising by…
a) The apparatus  b) The idea — one idea followed through every case in one chapter  c) The examination date  d) Alphabetical order
**Answer: b) The idea — one idea followed through every case in one chapter**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The final chapter of Form 5 — and of the two-year course — is…
a) Electricity  b) Nuclear Physics  c) Quantum Physics  d) Electronics
**Answer: c) Quantum Physics**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In the Malaysian course, quantum physics is…
a) Enrichment for a few students  b) Not taught  c) Examined SPM core for every candidate  d) Taught in Form 4
**Answer: c) Examined SPM core for every candidate**
