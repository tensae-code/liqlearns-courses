# The diode — a one-way gate for current — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about the p-n junction diode is correct?**
a) Roentgen's first X-ray image of a hand opens the story.  b) Joining P and N material makes a one-way device; its symbol and the depletion region at the junction are introduced.  c) Even at constant speed the velocity's direction changes, so the object accelerates toward the centre; the acceleration…
**Answer: b) Joining P and N material makes a one-way device; its symbol and the depletion region at the junction are…**

**2. Joining P and N material makes a one-way device; its symbol and the ______ region at the junction are introduced.**
a) displacement  b) depletion  c) vector
**Answer: b) depletion**
