## Sub lessons
1. **The worksheets — consolidation for Units 4–6** — The worksheet part (A1 onward) mirrors the Term 2 student book's three units: Applications of Newton's Laws (gravitation, connected bodies, circular motion), Fluids (static and moving), and Wave Motion (properties and behaviours). Each worksheet rehearses its unit's core moves in the appendix pattern of the Term 1 guide.
2. **The activities answers — the practical key for Term 2** — From A15, the answers to the Term 2 workbook: the friction-factor experiment, the centripetal enrichment, the buoyancy and flow practicals, the wave investigations and the speed-of-sound measurement — each with its expected results, workings and conclusions, the term's practical standard in worked form.
3. **Why it is kept — the record of Term 2's expectations** — Like its Term 1 twin, this appendix has no student-course home; it stands here as teacher evidence of Jordan's consolidation standard for Term 2, kept so the stock — worksheet topics and the answer key's expectations for the practical course — is not lost.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
