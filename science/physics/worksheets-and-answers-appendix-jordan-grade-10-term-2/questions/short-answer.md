# Worksheets and answers appendix — Jordan Grade 10 Term 2 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How does this appendix relate to the Term 1 appendix?
**Answer: It is its twin: same two parts (worksheets A1, activities answers A15), same evidential role, but covering Term 2's units and practicals instead of Term 1's.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the Term 2 practicals the key standardises.
**Answer: The tension-force and friction-factor experiments, the centripetal-force enrichment, the Archimedes/buoyancy and moving-fluid practicals, the wave reflection/refraction and interference/diffraction investigations, and the speed-of-sound measurement.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why is the key part of the course's evidence about Jordan?
**Answer: It shows what Jordan counts as a complete practical answer for this term — the measurements, workings and conclusions expected — which is part of how the country's course actually runs.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Which student-book units does the Term 2 guide follow, at which printed pages?
**Answer: Units 4–6 of the Term 2 student book, at its own printed pp. 7 (Applications of Newton's Laws), 55 (Fluids) and 91 (Wave Motion) in the guide's pagination.**
