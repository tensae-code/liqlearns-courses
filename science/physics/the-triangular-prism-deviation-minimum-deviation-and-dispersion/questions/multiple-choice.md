# The triangular prism — deviation, minimum deviation and dispersion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) At each face of a prism, the ray's bend is…
a) Toward the apex at both faces  b) Toward the base at both faces  c) Toward the base entering, toward the apex leaving  d) Random, depending on the glass
**Answer: b) Toward the base at both faces**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) For a prism of apex angle A, the deviation obeys…
a) δ = (i₁ + i₂) + A  b) δ = (i₁ + i₂) − A  c) δ = A − (i₁ + i₂)  d) δ = i₁ × i₂ − A
**Answer: b) δ = (i₁ + i₂) − A**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) At minimum deviation, the ray inside the prism…
a) Runs along the first face  b) Runs parallel to the base, with i₁ = i₂  c) Is normal to the second face  d) Has zero refraction at both faces
**Answer: b) Runs parallel to the base, with i₁ = i₂**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A prism has A = 60° and δm = 40°. Its refractive index is…
a) sin 50° ÷ sin 30° ≈ 1.53  b) sin 40° ÷ sin 60° ≈ 0.74  c) sin 100° ÷ sin 60° ≈ 1.14  d) sin 30° ÷ sin 50° ≈ 0.65
**Answer: a) sin 50° ÷ sin 30° ≈ 1.53**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In dispersion through a prism, the colour deviated MOST is…
a) Red  b) Yellow  c) Green  d) Violet
**Answer: d) Violet**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Newton recombined a spectrum into white light with a second inverted prism to prove…
a) Prisms create colour from nothing  b) White light is a mixture of colours that the prism only separates  c) Glass colours light permanently  d) Spectra exist only in sunlight
**Answer: b) White light is a mixture of colours that the prism only separates**
