# The triangular prism — deviation, minimum deviation and dispersion — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The total bend a prism gives a ray is called the angle of _____.
**Answer: deviation**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) At minimum deviation the two internal refraction angles are each equal to _____ of the apex angle.
**Answer: one half (A/2)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) For a thin prism, the deviation is approximately (n − 1) × _____.
**Answer: A (the apex angle)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The fanning of white light into colours by a prism is called _____.
**Answer: dispersion**
