# Hands-on — The triangular prism — deviation, minimum deviation and dispersion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace the ray through a prism
On paper, pin a glass prism over its outline, aim a ray box (or sunbeam through a slit) at the first face and mark the entering and emerging rays with pins. Measure i₁, i₂ and δ with a protractor and check δ = (i₁ + i₂) − A against your numbers. Say aloud the rule: both refractions bend the ray toward the base.

## Hunt the minimum
Repeat the trace at several incidence angles and tabulate δ. Watch δ fall, bottom out and rise again. At the flattest point the internal ray runs parallel to the base — mark it. Say aloud the rule: at minimum deviation the path is symmetric, and only there does n = sin((A + δm)/2) ÷ sin(A/2) apply.
