Today: **the triangular prism — deviation, minimum deviation and dispersion**. A ray crossing a prism bends twice, both times toward the base. Swing the ray and the total bend shrinks to a least value — minimum deviation — where the ray crosses in perfect symmetry, and one formula turns that angle into the refractive index of the glass. And because glass bends violet more than red, white light fans out into the spectrum: that is dispersion, the prism's rainbow.
