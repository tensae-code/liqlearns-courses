# The particle model — a body as a point — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the particle model, a body is replaced by…
**Answer: a) a single point carrying the body's whole mass  b) a smaller copy of the body  c) its centre of area drawn to scale  d) the strongest force acting on it**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) On a particle diagram, the forces on the body are drawn…
**Answer: a) all as arrows starting at the one point  b) at the places they really touch the body  c) only the largest one  d) underneath the body, in a list**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The particle model fits a motion when…
**Answer: a) the body only translates and the distances travelled are far larger than the body  b) the body is small and round  c) the body moves slowly  d) only one force acts on the body**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Treating the Earth as a point on its orbit round the Sun is honest because…
**Answer: a) the orbit is enormously larger than the Earth, and the Earth only travels along it  b) the Earth has no size  c) gravity acts at a point  d) the Sun is a point too**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A spinning ball curves in flight. The particle model cannot show this because…
**Answer: a) a point has no surface for the air to grip and no spin  b) the ball is too fast  c) gravity is missing from the diagram  d) the ball's mass changes**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) When turning matters, the point returns in a richer role as…
**Answer: a) the centre of mass, with moments counted separately  b) the centre of the drawing  c) the hinge  d) the resultant force**
