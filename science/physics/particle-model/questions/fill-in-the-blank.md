# The particle model — a body as a point — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the particle model, the point carries the body's whole _____.
**Answer: mass**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The law read off the arrows at the point is F = _____.
**Answer: ma**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The model fits when the body only _____ — it does not spin, roll or tip.
**Answer: translates (travels)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A body tips when the line of its _____ falls outside its base — something a point does not have.
**Answer: weight**
