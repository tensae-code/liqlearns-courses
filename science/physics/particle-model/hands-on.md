# Hands-on — The particle model — a body as a point

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One book, two models
Slide a book flat across a table with one push and watch it travel: every corner moves the same way — the book is, for this journey, a particle. Draw it as one dot on paper and put the arrows on the dot: your push, friction, weight. Now stand the same book on its edge and push its top corner gently until it tips instead of sliding. The dot cannot tip — the second motion needs the book's real size. Write one sentence for each motion: which model the motion allows, and why. Then spin a coin on the table and name, in one more sentence, exactly what the particle model would leave out.
