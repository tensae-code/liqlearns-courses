# The particle model — a body as a point — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — The particle model treats a body as a point particle: one point carrying the body's whole mass, with every force drawn acting at that point; The model fits when the body only translates and the distances travelled are far larger than the body (bus on a highway, planet on its orbit, ball in flight); F = ma is applied to the point: the resultant of the arrows at the dot, divided by the mass at the dot; The model breaks when rotation, spin, rolling contact or tipping decides the motion — where a force acts is then part of the physics, and the point returns as the centre of mass with moments counted separately — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
