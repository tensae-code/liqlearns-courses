# Video script — The particle model — a body as a point

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the particle model — a whole body as one point**. A bus is ten metres of metal, glass and people — but for its journey you may shrink it to one dot that carries all its mass, and draw every force at that dot. The dot is enough while the body only travels and the road is far longer than the body. The moment it spins, rolls or tips, give the body its size back. One dot, honestly used, is half of mechanics.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
