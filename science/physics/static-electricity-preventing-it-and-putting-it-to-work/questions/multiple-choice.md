# Static electricity — preventing it and putting it to work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A static discharge is dangerous at a fuel pump because…
**Answer: a) the spark can ignite fuel vapour  b) it is loud  c) it uses electricity  d) it attracts dust**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) An earthing strap on a tanker works by…
**Answer: a) draining charge continuously so none can gather to spark  b) hiding the charge  c) making more charge  d) cooling the fuel**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Electrostatic copying prints because…
**Answer: a) toner is attracted to the charged parts of a drum where light has not discharged it  b) ink is magnetic  c) the drum is hot  d) paper is charged by hand**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In electrostatic spraying, the even coat comes from…
**Answer: a) charged droplets seeking the earthed workpiece along field lines  b) thicker paint  c) spraying twice  d) wind**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) An electrostatic precipitator cleans smoke by…
**Answer: a) charging the particles and collecting them on plates  b) burning it  c) washing it with water only  d) filtering through paper**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The rule underneath both faces is…
**Answer: a) control where charge gathers and choose the path it takes  b) ban all static  c) always add more charge  d) fear it**
