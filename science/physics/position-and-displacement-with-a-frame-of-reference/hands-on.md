# Hands-on — Position and displacement — with a frame of reference

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Mark a 'home' stone as origin. Give the positions of three landmarks from it; then move the origin and watch every position change while journeys between them stay the same.

## Apply it where you live
Describe one moving car from two different origins and prove the displacement agrees.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21880 — one moving car located from two different origins] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using Displacement = final position − initial position: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
