# Video script — Position and displacement — with a frame of reference

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Position is meaningless until you fix a frame of reference — an origin and directions to measure from.
The same car sits at different positions in two frames that use different origins, yet its displacement — the vector change of position, final minus initial — tells the journey's true step.
Grade 10 restates Grade 9's ideas at exactly the level the accelerated-motion unit needs: fix the frame, read positions, subtract for displacement.

Remember: **Position needs a frame of reference: origin plus directions.**
Remember: **Displacement = final position − initial position (a vector).**
Remember: **Changing the origin changes positions, not the displacement.**
Keep the formula: **Displacement = final position − initial position**
