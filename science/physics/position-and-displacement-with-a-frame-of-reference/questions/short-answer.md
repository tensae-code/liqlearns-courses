# Position and displacement — with a frame of reference — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** A car moves from x = 3 m to x = 11 m. Displacement?
**Answer: 11 − 3 = +8 m.**

**2.** Why do two observers with different origins report different positions for one car?
**Answer: Position is measured from each frame's own origin.**

**3.** Does changing the origin change a journey's displacement?
**Answer: No — final minus initial is the same in both frames.**

**4.** Displacement is scalar or vector?
**Answer: Vector.**
