# Electric potential — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. Potential energy per unit charge defines electric potential (the volt); points of equal potential form equipotential surfaces, which never intersect and meet field lines at right angles.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Potential energy per unit charge defines electric potential (the volt); points of unequal potential form equipotential surfaces, which never intersect and meet field lines at right angles.**
**Answer: False**
