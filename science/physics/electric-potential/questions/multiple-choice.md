# Electric potential — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which statement about electric potential and equipotentials is correct?**
a) Whole circuits — diodes, transistors, resistors, capacitors — fabricated on one chip: small, cheap and reliable, but…  b) Newton's laws govern the heavens; electromagnetic waves are astronomy's messenger and atomic emission spectra tell…  c) Potential energy per unit charge defines electric potential (the volt); points of equal potential form equipotential…
**Answer: c) Potential energy per unit charge defines electric potential (the volt); points of equal potential form…**

**2. Potential energy per unit charge defines electric potential (the volt); points of equal potential form equipotential surfaces, which never intersect and meet ______ at right angles.**
a) convection  b) pressure  c) field lines
**Answer: c) field lines**
