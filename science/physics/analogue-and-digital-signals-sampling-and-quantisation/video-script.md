# Video script — Analogue and digital signals — sampling rate and sampling precision

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **a computer cannot swallow a smooth wave whole — it takes bites**. It samples the wave at regular instants and rounds each bite to a level. Sample fast enough and fine enough, and the numbers can rebuild the wave; too slow or too coarse, and the error is baked in forever.
