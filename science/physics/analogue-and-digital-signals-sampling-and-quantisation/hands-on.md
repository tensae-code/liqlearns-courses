# Hands-on — Analogue and digital signals — sampling rate and sampling precision

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sample a wave on graph paper
Draw a smooth sine-like wave across a page of squared paper. Now "sample" it: read its height only every 4 squares and mark the heights as dots; join the dots with straight steps (a staircase) — that is your digital version. Sample again every 1 square and rebuild. Compare the two staircases with the original curve: the slower sampling misses the wave's turns and invents flats that were never there — sampling error made visible. Finally round every height to the nearest whole square (coarse quantisation) and then to the nearest half square (finer): the rounding error halves as the levels double, the quantisation bargain from the lesson.
