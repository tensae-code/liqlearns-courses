# Analogue and digital signals — sampling rate and sampling precision — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sampling an analogue signal means…
**Answer: a) reading its value at regular instants and storing those readings as numbers  b) copying the whole continuous wave exactly  c) amplifying the signal  d) removing all noise from the signal**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The sampling rate is measured in…
**Answer: a) hertz — samples per second  b) bits  c) volts  d) decibels**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) To keep a signal whose highest frequency is 15 kHz, the sampling rate must be at least…
**Answer: a) 30 kHz  b) 15 kHz  c) 7.5 kHz  d) 1 kHz**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Quantisation is…
**Answer: a) rounding each sample to the nearest of the levels the system can store  b) reading the signal more often  c) converting numbers back into a wave  d) storing the signal on tape**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A precision of 16 bits per sample gives how many quantisation levels?
**Answer: a) 65,536  b) 16  c) 256  d) 1,600**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The job of a DA converter is to…
**Answer: a) rebuild a smooth analogue signal from stored digital numbers  b) sample an analogue signal  c) count the bits in a sample  d) remove quantisation error from old recordings**
