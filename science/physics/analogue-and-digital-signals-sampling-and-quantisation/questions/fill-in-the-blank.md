# Analogue and digital signals — sampling rate and sampling precision — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The number of samples taken per second is the sampling _____.
**Answer: rate (frequency)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Sampling too slowly can produce a false slower wave, an error called _____.
**Answer: aliasing**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Rounding a sample to the nearest storable level is called _____.
**Answer: quantisation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) With n bits per sample, the number of quantisation levels is _____ (write the power).
**Answer: 2^n**
