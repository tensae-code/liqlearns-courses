Today: **satellites and escape velocity — falling around the Earth, and leaving it**. A satellite is held by gravity itself: gravity does the centre-pulling, and the speed for a circle is fixed by height — about 8 km/s just above the Earth, slower higher up. To escape altogether takes root-two times that speed — about 11.2 km/s from the surface. Orbit: falling forever and missing. Escape: enough energy to climb out of the well and never return.
