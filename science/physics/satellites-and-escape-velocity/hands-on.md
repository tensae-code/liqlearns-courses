# Hands-on — Satellites and escape velocity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The falling-around-the-Earth throw
Roll a marble gently off the edge of a table: it describes a short curve and lands close. Roll it faster: the curve stretches and it lands farther out — same gravity, wider arc. Now draw the idea on paper at planet scale: draw a circle for the Earth, a mountain on top, and three dashed paths — one curving down to the surface nearby, one whose curve matches the circle so it returns to the mountain from behind, and one leaving the page. Label the three throws 'falls', 'orbits', 'escapes' and write the two lesson speeds beside the last two paths: about 8 km/s and about 11.2 km/s. Say aloud: an orbit is falling that keeps missing the ground.
