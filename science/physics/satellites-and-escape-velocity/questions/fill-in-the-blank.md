# Satellites and escape velocity — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a circular orbit, _____ provides the centripetal force.
**Answer: gravity**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The orbital speed just above Earth's surface, about 8 km/s, is called the first _____ velocity.
**Answer: cosmic**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Escape velocity equals _____ times the orbital speed at the same height.
**Answer: √2 (root two)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A satellite whose period matches Earth's rotation and which stays over one equatorial spot is called _____.
**Answer: geostationary**
