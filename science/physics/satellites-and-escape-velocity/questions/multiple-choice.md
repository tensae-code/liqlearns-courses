# Satellites and escape velocity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The centripetal force that holds a satellite in a circular orbit is provided by…
a) Its engine  b) Gravity  c) Air pressure  d) The satellite's spin
**Answer: b) Gravity**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The orbital speed v = √(GM/r) depends on the orbit's height and…
a) The satellite's mass  b) The satellite's shape  c) Not on the satellite's mass at all  d) The satellite's colour
**Answer: c) Not on the satellite's mass at all**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Compared with a low satellite, a satellite in a higher circular orbit moves…
a) Faster  b) At the same speed  c) Slower  d) Backwards
**Answer: c) Slower**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Escape velocity at a given height is the orbital speed there multiplied by…
a) 2  b) √2 ≈ 1.41  c) ½  d) 10
**Answer: b) √2 ≈ 1.41**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The escape velocity from Earth's surface is about…
a) 7.9 km/s  b) 8 km/s  c) 11.2 km/s  d) 35.8 km/s
**Answer: c) 11.2 km/s**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A geostationary satellite has a period of…
a) 90 minutes  b) 24 hours, matching Earth's turning  c) 7 days  d) 1 year
**Answer: b) 24 hours, matching Earth's turning**
