# Video script — How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 9 way

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **every Punjab Grade 9 unit is built the same way**. Every unit of Physics IX opens by telling the learner exactly what they will be able to do: a Student's Learning Outcomes list, then the unit's Major Concepts in one place. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — outcomes and concepts first, linkage around them; investigation, society and sidebars while you learn; five ways of being questioned at the close — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
