# How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 9 way

## Do it first
Take any unit of Physics IX and map it before reading it — begin with the hands-on opening in hands-on.md before reading on. This lesson is the story of that activity.

## Sub lesson 1 — Outcomes and concepts first, linkage around them

Every unit of Physics IX opens by telling the learner exactly what they will be able to do: a Student's Learning Outcomes list, then the unit's Major Concepts in one place. Around them sits the Conceptual Linkage box, which names what the unit is built on from earlier classes and what it leads to in later ones — the learner always knows where the unit stands in the whole course before meeting any detail.

## Sub lesson 2 — Investigation, society and sidebars while you learn

Through the unit run Investigation Skills boxes, where the learner does the measuring and testing, and Science–Technology–Society boxes, where the physics meets the world it serves. Quick Quiz checks, Tidbits and For Your Information sidebars ride alongside the main text — small, named pauses that test, surprise and extend without breaking the unit's line.

## Sub lesson 3 — Five ways of being questioned at the close

Each unit closes with a summary and then questions in five registers: multiple-choice questions, review questions, conceptual questions and numerical questions — the same physics asked as recognition, as recall, as understanding and as calculation. A learner who can answer in all the registers owns the unit; the Punjab pattern makes that the test of finishing.
