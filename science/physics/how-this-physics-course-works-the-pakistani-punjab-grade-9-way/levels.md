# How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 9 way — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — Every unit of Physics IX opens by telling the learner exactly what they will be able to do: a Student's Learning Outcomes list, then the unit's Major Concepts in one place; Through the unit run Investigation Skills boxes, where the learner does the measuring and testing, and Science–Technology–Society boxes, where the physics meets the world it serves; Each unit closes with a summary and then questions in five registers: multiple-choice questions, review questions, conceptual questions and numerical questions — the same physics asked as recognition, as recall, as understanding and as calculation — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
