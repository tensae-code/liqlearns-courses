# How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 9 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A Punjab Grade 9 unit opens with…
a) The answers to the exercises  b) Student's Learning Outcomes and Major Concepts  c) A formula sheet only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Student's Learning Outcomes and Major Concepts**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The Conceptual Linkage box tells the learner…
a) The examination dates  b) What the unit builds on and what it leads to  c) The teacher's name  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) What the unit builds on and what it leads to**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Investigation Skills boxes are where the learner…
a) Copies the summary  b) Does the measuring and testing  c) Memorises definitions  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Does the measuring and testing**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) STS in the Punjab pattern stands for…
a) Speed–Time–Space  b) Science–Technology–Society  c) Series–Total–Sum  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Science–Technology–Society**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A Punjab unit's closing questions come in…
a) One register only  b) Five registers incl. MCQ, conceptual and numerical  c) No questions — a summary only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Five registers incl. MCQ, conceptual and numerical**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Quick Quiz, Tidbits and For Your Information are…
a) Separate textbooks  b) Sidebars riding alongside the main text  c) Examination papers  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Sidebars riding alongside the main text**
