# How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 9 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What two lists open every Punjab Grade 9 unit?
**Answer:** The Student's Learning Outcomes and the Major Concepts list.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the work of the Science–Technology–Society boxes?
**Answer:** They connect the unit's physics to the technology and society it serves.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Name the closing question kinds.
**Answer:** Multiple-choice, review, conceptual and numerical questions (over a summary).

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) How does a learner know a unit is finished, in this pattern?
**Answer:** By answering its ideas in every register — recognition, recall, understanding and calculation.
