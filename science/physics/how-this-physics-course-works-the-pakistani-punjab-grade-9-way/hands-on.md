# Hands-on — How this physics course works — the Pakistani (Punjab) Grade 9 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Take any unit of Physics IX and map it before reading it
Take any unit of Physics IX and map it before reading it: copy its Learning Outcomes, list its Major Concepts, fill the Conceptual Linkage box in your own words (built on… / leads to…), and tick each sidebar kind as you meet it. Then attempt one question of each closing kind. You have used the book the Punjab way: outcomes first, linkage always, five registers at the end.
