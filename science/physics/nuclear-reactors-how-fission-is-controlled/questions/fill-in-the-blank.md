# Nuclear reactors — how fission is controlled — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The steady point is one onward _____ per fission.
**Answer: fission**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The _____ slows neutrons without swallowing them.
**Answer: moderator**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Control rods contain boron or _____.
**Answer: cadmium**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The _____ carries the fission heat away.
**Answer: coolant**
