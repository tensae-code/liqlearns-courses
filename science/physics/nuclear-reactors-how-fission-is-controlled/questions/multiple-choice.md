# Nuclear reactors — how fission is controlled — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A power reactor holds its chain reaction at…
**Answer: a) exactly one onward fission per fission  b) maximum multiplication  c) zero  d) two per fission**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) If multiplication stays above one, the reaction…
**Answer: a) runs away — power climbs uncontrollably  b) stops  c) cools  d) makes no heat**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The moderator's job is to…
**Answer: a) slow the neutrons so U-235 fissions readily  b) absorb neutrons  c) cool the fuel only  d) shield radiation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Control rods absorb neutrons because they contain…
**Answer: a) boron or cadmium  b) uranium  c) graphite  d) steam**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The heat that a power reactor sells is carried out by…
**Answer: a) the coolant, to raise steam  b) the control rods  c) the moderator only  d) the containment**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Reactor safety is designed so failures tend toward…
**Answer: a) shutdown  b) higher power  c) melting  d) silence**
