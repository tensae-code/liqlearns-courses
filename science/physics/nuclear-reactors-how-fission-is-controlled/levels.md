# Nuclear reactors — how fission is controlled — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — A reactor holds the chain reaction at exactly one onward fission per fission — the steady point; The moderator slows fast neutrons so U-235 fissions readily, without absorbing them; Control rods of boron or cadmium absorb neutrons and trim the reaction; The coolant carries the fission heat away to raise steam for generation; Safety is layered — fuel, cladding, vessel, containment — and designed to fail toward shutdown — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
