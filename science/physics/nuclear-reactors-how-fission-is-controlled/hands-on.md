# Hands-on — Nuclear reactors — how fission is controlled

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Model the chain with dominoes or a simulation: set branching just below, at, and above one onward event per event, and record the three fates. (2) On a reactor diagram, label moderator, rods, coolant path and containment, and write each part's one-line job. (3) Debate in pairs: 'the heat is the product; the reaction is only the fire' — list what follows for how a station is run and judged.
