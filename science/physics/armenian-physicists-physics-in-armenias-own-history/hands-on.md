# Hands-on — Armenian physicists — physics in Armenia's own history

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with your hands
Take a map of Armenia (or sketch one). Mark a high mountain area and the capital. Cosmic-ray detectors and observatory domes are put high up, away from city air and lights — find where you would put them and say why. Then make a 'physicist card' for one Armenian physicist: name, what they studied, and the instrument or observatory their work used.
