# Video script — Armenian physicists — physics in Armenia's own history

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the first physics book an Armenian pupil opens begins not with a formula but with the physicists of Armenia itself — the science belongs to every nation, and Armenia's own scientists, observatories and cosmic-ray stations are part of its story**. The Grade 7 book's §2, right after saying what physics studies, presents famous Armenian physicists. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — a small country, its own scientists; stars and cosmic rays — the work armenia is known for; why the book starts here — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
