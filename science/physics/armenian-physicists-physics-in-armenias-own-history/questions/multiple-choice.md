# Armenian physicists — physics in Armenia's own history — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Armenian Grade 7 book opens its physics with…
a) A list of formulas to memorise  b) Famous Armenian physicists  c) The periodic table  d) Examination rules
**Answer: b) Famous Armenian physicists**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Viktor Ambartsumian is known as the founder of…
a) The Byurakan Observatory  b) The first nuclear reactor  c) The Aragats railway  d) The Yerevan metro
**Answer: a) The Byurakan Observatory**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Cosmic-ray research in Armenia is carried out…
a) Deep underground in mines  b) Under the sea  c) High on Mount Aragats  d) Inside a volcano
**Answer: c) High on Mount Aragats**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A cosmic-ray station is placed high on a mountain because…
a) The air is thinner and clearer, so more particles and cleaner signals reach the detectors  b) It is colder, and cosmic rays like cold  c) Mountains attract lightning  d) Rent is cheaper
**Answer: a) The air is thinner and clearer, so more particles and cleaner signals reach the detectors**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The purpose of opening the course with national physicists is…
a) To fill pages before the real work  b) To show pupils the science is also theirs — done by people of their own country  c) To replace experiments  d) To teach history instead of physics
**Answer: b) To show pupils the science is also theirs — done by people of their own country**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Ambartsumian's field was mainly…
a) Astrophysics — stars and galaxies  b) Oceanography  c) Botany  d) Geology of oil
**Answer: a) Astrophysics — stars and galaxies**
