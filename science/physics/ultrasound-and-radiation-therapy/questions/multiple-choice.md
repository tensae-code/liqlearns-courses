# Ultrasound and radiation therapy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about medical physics is correct?**
a) Junction rule: currents into a junction equal currents out (charge conservation).  b) The stethoscope carries body sounds to the ear.  c) The summary runs Newton's laws, friction, work–energy, and momentum; end questions include inertia vs mass, effects of…
**Answer: b) The stethoscope carries body sounds to the ear.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ______ images the body with reflected sound (anechoic/hypoechoic regions named in the index).**

**Answer: b) ultrasound**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which statement about medical physics is correct?**

**Answer: a) Radiation therapy turns ionising radiation on tumours.**
