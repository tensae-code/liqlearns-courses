# Ultrasound and radiation therapy — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The stethoscope carries body sounds to the ear.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Ultrasound images the body with reflected sound (anechoic/hypoechoic regions named in the index).**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Radiation therapy turns ionising radiation on tumours.**
**Answer: True**
