# Video script — How this Physics course works — the Indonesian Kurikulum Merdeka way

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **how an Indonesian physics chapter works**. It never starts with definitions. First a scene from daily life, then a question that flushes out the wrong idea you walked in with — and you must commit to an answer. Then you investigate, with your own hands, in numbered activities. Small checks — Ayo, Cek Pemahaman, come on, check your understanding — close every stretch, a full assessment closes the chapter, and all the while the chapter is also growing your character: reasoning, independence, cooperation.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
