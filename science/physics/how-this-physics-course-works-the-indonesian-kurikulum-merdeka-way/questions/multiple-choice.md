# How this Physics course works — the Indonesian Kurikulum Merdeka way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A Kurikulum Merdeka physics chapter opens with…
a) A page of definitions  b) Apperception and a misconception check  c) The chapter assessment  d) A formula list
**Answer: b) Apperception and a misconception check**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The numbered investigations learners carry out themselves are called…
a) Asesmen  b) Aktivitas  c) Apperception  d) LKPD
**Answer: b) Aktivitas**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) “Ayo, Cek Pemahaman” is…
a) The final examination  b) A short check that closes each stretch of work inside a chapter  c) A project option  d) The teacher's scheme
**Answer: b) A short check that closes each stretch of work inside a chapter**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A chapter closes with…
a) A new topic  b) An apperception  c) An Asesmen — a full assessment of the chapter  d) Nothing; chapters just stop
**Answer: c) An Asesmen — a full assessment of the chapter**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The Profil Pelajar Pancasila dimension in a chapter names…
a) The apparatus list  b) The character the work is meant to grow — reasoning, independence, cooperation  c) The examination board  d) The chapter's page count
**Answer: b) The character the work is meant to grow — reasoning, independence, cooperation**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A misconception check works because learners…
a) Read the answer first  b) Commit to an answer before teaching, so the wrong idea is out in the open  c) Skip the question  d) Copy the teacher
**Answer: b) Commit to an answer before teaching, so the wrong idea is out in the open**
