# How this Physics course works — the Indonesian Kurikulum Merdeka way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does the concept come after the Aktivitas, not before it?
**Answer: So the definition is a name for something the learner has already seen happen in their own investigation — the word lands on an experience, not on empty memory.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the check–build–check-again shape find a lost learner early?
**Answer: The Ayo, Cek Pemahaman check after each stretch tests exactly what was just built, so a gap shows while that step is still current, instead of surfacing for the first time in the chapter Asesmen.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Give one Profil Pelajar Pancasila dimension and an Aktivitas that would grow it.
**Answer: For example independence: an investigation the group plans and carries out without being led, reaching and defending its own conclusion; or critical reasoning: an investigation whose result argues back against the class's first prediction.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) What is a misconception check, in one sentence?
**Answer: A question, asked before any teaching, that names the wrong idea learners commonly bring and makes them commit to an answer, so the chapter can work on that idea openly.**
