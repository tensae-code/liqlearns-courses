# Lesson plan — How this Physics course works — the Indonesian Kurikulum Merdeka way

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** How this Physics course works — the Indonesian Kurikulum Merdeka way
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18) — a new lesson built for the Indonesia placement named in lesson/sources.md
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Indonesian Kurikulum Merdeka (Fase F) way of teaching physics — **activity-first chapters**: every chapter opens with **apperception** and a **misconception check**, builds its concepts through numbered **Aktivitas** investigations learners carry out themselves, closes each stretch with **“Ayo, Cek Pemahaman”** checks and the chapter with an **Asesmen**, and carries a **Profil Pelajar Pancasila** character dimension and project options throughout
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Earlier lessons make a good preparation and are recommended, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Activity first — every chapter opens by doing** — A chapter does not begin with definitions. It opens with apperception — connecting the new topic to what the learner already meets in daily life — and a misconception check that surfaces the wrong idea learners commonly carry in, then a numbered Aktivitas investigation the learners carry out themselves, so the concept is built from something they have done.
2. **The shape of a chapter — check, build, check again** — Inside the chapter, concepts grow through the numbered Aktivitas investigations in order; short “Ayo, Cek Pemahaman” (“Come on, check your understanding”) checks close each stretch of work, and a chapter Asesmen — a full assessment — closes the chapter, so no idea is left untested and no test arrives unpractised.
3. **The learner in the chapter — character and projects** — Every chapter also carries a Profil Pelajar Pancasila dimension — the character profile the work is meant to grow (reasoning, independence, cooperation) — and project options that take the physics out of the page, so the course teaches a way of being a learner, not only a list of topics.
