# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Indonesian Kurikulum Merdeka way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run a chapter the Merdeka way
Pick a topic you know well. On one page, build its chapter opening in order: (1) write the apperception — a daily-life scene, two sentences; (2) write the misconception check — the common wrong idea as a choose-an-answer question; (3) list three Aktivitas investigations in the order a learner should meet them, one line each saying what the learner does and what they should notice. Read your page to a partner: if they can say, from your page alone, what the chapter will teach and how it will start, your opening works.
