# The active receiver — counter-EMF of an electrolyser — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An electrolyser's characteristic, extended back, meets the U axis at…
**Answer: a) E′, above the origin  b) the origin  c) below the origin  d) it never meets the axis**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The E′ of an active receiver is called its…
**Answer: a) counter-EMF  b) internal resistance  c) efficiency  d) threshold current**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The receiver's law is…
**Answer: a) U = E′ + r′I  b) U = E′ − r′I  c) U = r′I only  d) U = E′·I**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The slope of the receiver's characteristic gives…
**Answer: a) its internal resistance r′  b) its counter-EMF  c) the current  d) the stored energy**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In UI = E′I + r′I², the term E′I is…
**Answer: a) power stored as chemical energy  b) heat lost  c) the supply's power  d) the counter-EMF itself**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Compared with a generator's characteristic, a receiver's…
**Answer: a) rises from E′ instead of falling from E  b) is identical  c) passes through the origin  d) is vertical**
