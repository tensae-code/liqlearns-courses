# Hands-on — The active receiver — counter-EMF of an electrolyser

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

['(1) Electrolyse water with a variable supply (teacher-led), noting the voltage at which bubbles first stream steadily — the neighbourhood of E′. (2) Record U and I above threshold, plot the characteristic, extend the line back and read E′ off the axis; take r′ from the slope. (3) Run the cell at two currents and compare gas collected per minute against the heat felt — the E′I versus r′I² shares made visible.']
