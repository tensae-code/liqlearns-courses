# Hands-on — Motion of a body thrown vertically upward

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with your hands
Throw a ball straight up and clap the rhythm: catch the instants — launch, top, return to your hand. Time the whole flight with a phone stopwatch (several tries, keep the best): half the time is the rise, so v₀ = g × (T/2) prices your throw, and h_max = v₀²/(2g) its height. Compare throws with a partner: the stopwatch has turned your arm into a measuring instrument.
