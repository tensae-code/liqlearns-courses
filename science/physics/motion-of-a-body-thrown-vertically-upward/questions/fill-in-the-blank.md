# Motion of a body thrown vertically upward — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) On the way up, velocity obeys v = v₀ − ___ × t.
**Answer:** g

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) At the top of the throw the velocity is ___ but the acceleration is still g.
**Answer:** zero

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The greatest height of a vertical throw is v₀²/(2___).
**Answer:** g

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The total flight back to launch level lasts ___ × v₀/g.
**Answer:** 2 (two)
