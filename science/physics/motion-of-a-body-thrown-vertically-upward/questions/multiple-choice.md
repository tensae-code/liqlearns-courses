# Motion of a body thrown vertically upward — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A body thrown up at 30 m/s (g = 10 m/s²) has speed after 2 s of…
a) 30 m/s  b) 20 m/s  c) 10 m/s  d) 0 m/s
**Answer: c) 10 m/s**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The rise time of a vertical throw is…
a) v₀/g  b) 2v₀/g  c) v₀²/2g  d) g/v₀
**Answer: a) v₀/g**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) At the top of the throw…
a) Velocity and acceleration are both zero  b) Velocity is zero, acceleration is still g downward  c) Acceleration is zero, velocity is greatest  d) The body stays until pushed
**Answer: b) Velocity is zero, acceleration is still g downward**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The maximum height is…
a) h = v₀t  b) h = v₀²/(2g)  c) h = gt²  d) h = 2gv₀
**Answer: b) h = v₀²/(2g)**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Returning to launch level, the body's speed is…
a) Zero  b) Half the launch speed  c) Equal to the launch speed v₀ (air set aside)  d) Double the launch speed
**Answer: c) Equal to the launch speed v₀ (air set aside)**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) With up taken as positive, the acceleration in every vertical-throw equation is written…
a) +g  b) −g  c) 0  d) +v₀
**Answer: b) −g**
