# Video script — Motion of a body thrown vertically upward

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **throw a body straight up and gravity edits its speed by the same g every second: v = v₀ − gt on the way up, one still instant at the top (h_max = v₀²/2g), then the mirror-image fall — one motion, signed carefully, whose whole story is free fall read upwards first**. The throw's only force is gravity, pulling down at g, so the upward speed falls in a straight line: v = v₀ − gt. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — up against gravity — speed spent at g per second; the top, and the mirror-image fall; solving vertical throws — one sign convention, held — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
