# Video script — The greenhouse effect — trapping heat, the Greek experiment way

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **a jar that keeps its heat**. Two flasks under one lamp. The open one warms a little — its heat escapes as fast as it arrives. The covered one warms much more: the glass lets the light in, then slows the heat going out. Same lamp, same minutes — only the cover differs. Now make the flask as big as a planet. Earth's cover is not glass; it is a layer of gases — carbon dioxide, methane, water vapour. They let sunlight in and slow the heat leaving. That is the greenhouse effect: life needs some of it, and thickening the cover warms the world.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
