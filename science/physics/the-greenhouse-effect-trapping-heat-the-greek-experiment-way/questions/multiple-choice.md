# The greenhouse effect — trapping heat, the Greek experiment way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the Greek worksheet experiment, the covered flask warms more than the open one because…
a) The cover makes extra heat  b) The lamp shines harder on it  c) The cover slows the escape of heat while the inflow stays the same  d) Glass creates energy
**Answer: c) The cover slows the escape of heat while the inflow stays the same**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) For the comparison to be a fair test, everything except the cover must be…
a) Different  b) The same — lamp, distance, time, starting temperature  c) Unrecorded  d) Warmer
**Answer: b) The same — lamp, distance, time, starting temperature**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Glass traps heat in the flask by…
a) Reflecting all light back out  b) Letting light in easily but passing heat radiation poorly, and stopping warm air rising away  c) Absorbing the lamp's light and re-emitting it doubled  d) Cooling the outside air
**Answer: b) Letting light in easily but passing heat radiation poorly, and stopping warm air rising away**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The covered flask's temperature stops rising when…
a) The lamp runs out  b) The slowed outflow grows to balance the inflow  c) The glass melts  d) The air inside is used up
**Answer: b) The slowed outflow grows to balance the inflow**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In the planet version of the model, the 'cover' is…
a) The ozone layer only  b) Clouds only  c) Greenhouse gases — carbon dioxide, methane, water vapour  d) The oceans
**Answer: c) Greenhouse gases — carbon dioxide, methane, water vapour**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) With no greenhouse effect at all, the Earth would be…
a) The same temperature  b) Far colder — below freezing on average  c) Hotter  d) Without seasons
**Answer: b) Far colder — below freezing on average**
