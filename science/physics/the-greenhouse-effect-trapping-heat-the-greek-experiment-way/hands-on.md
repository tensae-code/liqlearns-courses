# Hands-on — The greenhouse effect — trapping heat, the Greek experiment way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Covered against open
You need: two identical jars or flasks, two thermometers, a desk lamp, a glass plate or clear film cover, a timer. Stand both jars the same distance from the lamp, thermometers inside, and record the starting temperatures. Cover one jar. Switch the lamp on and record both temperatures every two minutes for ten minutes. Plot both curves on one graph. Say aloud: same energy in — the covered jar loses less, so it warms more. Repeat with the cover on the other jar to check the jars themselves were not the difference.
