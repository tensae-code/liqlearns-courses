# Gravitational potential and gravitational potential energy — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Potential energy in a gravitational field is written E_p = _____ (the sign matters).
**Answer: −GMm/r**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Gravitational potential is the potential energy per unit _____.
**Answer: mass**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Field strength g is the _____ of the potential — how steeply it changes with distance.
**Answer: slope**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Earth's escape velocity is about _____ km/s.
**Answer: 11.2**
