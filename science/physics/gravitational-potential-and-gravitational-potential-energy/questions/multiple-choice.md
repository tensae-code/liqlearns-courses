# Gravitational potential and gravitational potential energy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The zero of gravitational potential energy is set at…
**Answer: a) infinity  b) the planet's centre  c) sea level, universally  d) the top of the atmosphere**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) E_p = −GMm/r is negative at finite r because…
**Answer: a) the mass is bound — work must be added to free it to infinity  b) gravity is negative  c) energy cannot be positive  d) r is negative**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Gravitational potential is measured in…
**Answer: a) joules per kilogram — energy per unit mass  b) newtons per kilogram  c) joules  d) newtons**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The potential at a point between two masses is found by…
**Answer: a) adding the two potentials as scalars  b) resolving them as vectors  c) multiplying them  d) taking the larger only**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Escape from a planet requires the total energy to reach…
**Answer: a) zero — kinetic energy exactly paying the potential well's depth  b) infinity  c) −GMm/r  d) the value mgh**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The Moon has no atmosphere largely because…
**Answer: a) its escape velocity (2.4 km/s) is so low that gas molecules leak away over time  b) it has no gravity  c) the solar wind glued the air down  d) its surface is too hot at all times**
