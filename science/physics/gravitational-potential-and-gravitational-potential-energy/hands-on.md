# Hands-on — Gravitational potential and gravitational potential energy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the well, price the climb
On graph paper, plot V = −GM/r in units where GM = 1: compute V at r = 1, 2, 4, 10 (getting −1, −0.5, −0.25, −0.1) and sketch the curve rising toward zero. Your curve is the potential well. Mark Earth's surface value conceptually and shade the depth a rocket must climb from r = 1 to infinity: exactly 1 unit of energy per kilogram — that area of effort is what 'bound by gravity' means, drawn.
