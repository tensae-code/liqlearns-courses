Today: **gravity's energy account**. Potential energy in a field is E_p = −GMm/r — negative because infinity is the zero and every finite place is a well you must pay to climb out of. The potential V = −GM/r maps energy per kilogram, the partner of g's force per kilogram. And when kinetic energy exactly pays the well's depth, total energy is zero — that speed is escape velocity: 11.2 km/s for Earth, a leaky 2.4 for the Moon.
