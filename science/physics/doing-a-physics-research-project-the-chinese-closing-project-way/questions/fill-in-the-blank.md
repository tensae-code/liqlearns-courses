# Doing a physics research project — the Chinese closing-project way — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Every PEP senior volume ends with a _____ project unit.
**Answer: research**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In Chinese, the closing unit is called _____ (课题研究).
**Answer: 课题研究**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Measure honestly: record what the instruments say and keep the _____ numbers.
**Answer: raw**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A conclusion the results table cannot support is not a _____.
**Answer: conclusion**
