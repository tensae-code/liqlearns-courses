# Doing a physics research project — the Chinese closing-project way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Rewrite “sound” as a project question a measurement can answer.
**Answer: Any question naming what changes, what is measured and what stays the same, e.g. “How does the loudness of a drum fall as I walk away from it, in the same hall?” — accept equivalents.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why are repeats built into the plan before measuring begins?
**Answer: One reading can be mistimed or misread; repeats show whether a value is trustworthy and expose the strange readings instead of hiding them.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A team's graph shows no pattern at all. State two honest closing moves they can still make.
**Answer: Say plainly that the evidence shows no pattern (that is a result), name the likeliest error source, and state what they would change — wider range, more repeats, better instrument.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Name the six senior PEP volumes whose closing unit stocks this lesson.
**Answer: Compulsory Books 1, 2 and 3 and Selective Compulsory Books 1, 2 and 3 — every senior volume carries the same closing research-project unit.**
