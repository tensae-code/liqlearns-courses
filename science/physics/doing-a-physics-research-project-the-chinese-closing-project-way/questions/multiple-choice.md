# Doing a physics research project — the Chinese closing-project way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Every PEP senior physics volume in China ends with…
**Answer: a) a research project unit (课题研究)  b) a formula summary  c) an examination  d) a biography of a physicist**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A good project question must be…
**Answer: a) small, safe and testable by measurement  b) as broad as possible  c) about famous scientists  d) impossible to measure**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In the plan, what is designed before the first reading is taken?
**Answer: a) the results table, with variables, instruments and repeats chosen  b) the conclusion  c) the poster colours  d) the grade**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A measurement comes out strange. The project way says…
**Answer: a) keep it, repeat the measurement, and report the doubt honestly  b) delete it quietly  c) change it to match the others  d) stop the project**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The closing part of a project turns measurements into knowledge by…
**Answer: a) graphing, stating the pattern, comparing with taught physics, naming errors and presenting  b) copying the textbook  c) adding more apparatus  d) memorising the table**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The book helps students start by carrying…
**Answer: a) a worked sample project and a list of suggested topics  b) the answers to every project  c) a list of forbidden topics  d) last year's grades**
