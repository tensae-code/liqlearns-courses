# Hands-on — Doing a physics research project — the Chinese closing-project way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run a one-lesson mini project
Work in a small team and run the whole Chinese closing-project cycle in miniature. (1) Choose: pick one testable question from the room around you — for example, how the bounce height of a ball depends on the drop height. (2) Plan: write the variables, the instrument (a metre rule), the repeats (three drops per height) and draw the results table before touching the ball. (3) Measure: take every reading honestly, repeats included, and keep the raw numbers. (4) Argue: plot bounce height against drop height, state the pattern, name your biggest error, and present the poster to another team for questions. You have done, in one lesson, what every PEP senior volume ends with.
