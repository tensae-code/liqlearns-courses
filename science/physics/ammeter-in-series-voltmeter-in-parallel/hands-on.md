# Hands-on — Ammeter in series, voltmeter in parallel

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Draw one circuit twice: meters correct, meters swapped. Under each, write what would actually happen — the second drawing is the cautionary tale.

## Apply it where you live
Given a faulty circuit diagram, find and fix the meter placements, explaining each fault.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21907 — circuit with both meters correctly placed, ticked; the swapped version crossed] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
