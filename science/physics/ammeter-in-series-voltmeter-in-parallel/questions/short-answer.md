# Ammeter in series, voltmeter in parallel — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Where does an ammeter go, and why is its resistance low?
**Answer: In series; low resistance so it hardly changes the current it measures.**

**2.** Where does a voltmeter go?
**Answer: In parallel, across the two points whose difference it reads.**

**3.** A voltmeter is wired in series by mistake. Result?
**Answer: Its high resistance nearly stops the current — the circuit barely works and readings are wrong.**

**4.** An ammeter across a battery (parallel) is dangerous because — ?
**Answer: Its low resistance is almost a short circuit.**
