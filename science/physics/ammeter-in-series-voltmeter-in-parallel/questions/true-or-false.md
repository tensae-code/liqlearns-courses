# Ammeter in series, voltmeter in parallel — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1.** Ammeter in series, with low resistance, measures current.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Voltmeter in parallel, with high resistance, measures voltage.
**Answer: True**

**3.** A voltmeter is connected in series like an ammeter.
**Answer: False**
