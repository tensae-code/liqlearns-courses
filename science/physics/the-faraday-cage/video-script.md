Today: **a closed conducting shell keeps its inside free of external electric fields**. Put a conductor in an external field and its free charges move until they cancel the field inside the material — which piles the charge on the outer surface. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — charge lives on the outside; why the inside is exactly zero; cages at work — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.
