# Hands-on — The Faraday cage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Test a household cage
Test a household cage: put a playing radio or a phone (watching its signal bars) inside a closed metal biscuit tin with the lid on, then inside a metal mesh (a sieve or colander dome) — compare reception. The tin is the fuller cage; discuss which signals each arrangement weakens and the hole-size rule behind the difference.
