# The Faraday cage — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The cage is named after ___.
**Answer:** Faraday

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Charge induced on a cage gathers on its ___ surface.
**Answer:** outer

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Inside the cavity, E = ___.
**Answer:** 0 (zero)

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A metal ___ shields sensitive electronics.
**Answer:** case (enclosure)
