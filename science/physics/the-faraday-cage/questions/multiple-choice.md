# The Faraday cage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Inside a closed conductor in an external field, the electric field is…
a) Doubled  b) Zero  c) Equal to the outside field  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Zero**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The cancellation is done by…
a) The metal absorbing the field like a sponge  b) Charges rearranged on the outer surface  c) Air inside the cage  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Charges rearranged on the outer surface**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Induced charges on the cage sit…
a) Evenly through the metal  b) On the outer surface only  c) On the inner surface only  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) On the outer surface only**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A mesh works as a cage when its holes are…
a) Larger than the cage  b) Much smaller than the job requires  c) Square rather than round  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Much smaller than the job requires**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A car in lightning protects occupants because…
a) Rubber tyres block lightning entirely  b) Charge runs over the outer skin; the inside stays field-free  c) The engine absorbs the strike  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Charge runs over the outer skin; the inside stays field-free**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Aircraft and instrument cases use the same principle to…
a) Increase the field inside  b) Shield their insides from external fields and strikes  c) Store charge for later  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Shield their insides from external fields and strikes**
