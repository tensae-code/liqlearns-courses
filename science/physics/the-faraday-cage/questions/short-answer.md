# The Faraday cage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain the cage without the word 'block'.
**Answer:** Surface charges rearrange until their field cancels the applied field everywhere inside, leaving the cavity field-free.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is there no charge on the cavity's inner surface (with nothing inside)?
**Answer:** Any inner charge would make a field in the cavity; equilibrium leaves the interior field zero, so charge resides outside.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What decides how fine a mesh must be?
**Answer:** The size of the holes relative to the fields/waves it must shield against — smaller holes, better shielding.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Name the cage's inventor-evidence: how do we know the inside is safe?
**Answer:** Faraday himself sat inside a charged cage with detectors showing no field inside.
