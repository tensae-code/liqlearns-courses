# Video script — Graphs of accelerated motion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Accelerated motion has its own graph family.
Position–time bends into a curve that steepens as velocity grows.
Velocity–time is a straight sloping line: its slope is the acceleration, and the area beneath it is the displacement.
Acceleration–time is flat for this unit's constant acceleration.
The three graphs stack in time — one car's story told in three aligned drawings, each readable both ways: graph to motion, motion to graph.

Remember: **Position–time for accelerated motion is a steepening curve.**
Remember: **Velocity–time slope = acceleration; area under it = displacement.**
Remember: **Constant acceleration draws a flat acceleration–time graph.**
