# Hands-on — Graphs of accelerated motion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Walk a speeding-up journey across the yard while a partner counts seconds aloud. Sketch all three graphs from memory of your own legs.

## Apply it where you live
Given one accelerating car's velocity–time graph, find its acceleration and displacement with working.

Study [Picture 21884 — one accelerating car with its three graphs stacked and aligned in time] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
