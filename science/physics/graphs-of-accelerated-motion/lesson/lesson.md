# Graphs of accelerated motion

## Graphs of accelerated motion — the teaching
Accelerated motion has its own graph family. Position–time bends into a curve that steepens as velocity grows. Velocity–time is a straight sloping line: its slope is the acceleration, and the area beneath it is the displacement. Acceleration–time is flat for this unit's constant acceleration. The three graphs stack in time — one car's story told in three aligned drawings, each readable both ways: graph to motion, motion to graph.

## The key ideas

- Position–time for accelerated motion is a steepening curve.
- Velocity–time slope = acceleration; area under it = displacement.
- Constant acceleration draws a flat acceleration–time graph.
