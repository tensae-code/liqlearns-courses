# Graphs of accelerated motion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A velocity–time line rises from 0 to 12 m/s in 3 s. Acceleration?
a) Slope = 12 ÷ 3 = 4 m/s².  b) ½ × 3 × 12 = 18 m.  c) A curve that steepens.  d) Flat (horizontal) line.
**Answer: a) Slope = 12 ÷ 3 = 4 m/s².**

**2.** The area under that triangle (base 3 s, height 12 m/s) is the displacement. Value?
a) Slope = 12 ÷ 3 = 4 m/s².  b) ½ × 3 × 12 = 18 m.  c) A curve that steepens.  d) Flat (horizontal) line.
**Answer: b) ½ × 3 × 12 = 18 m.**

**3.** What shape is position–time for a speeding-up body?
a) Slope = 12 ÷ 3 = 4 m/s².  b) ½ × 3 × 12 = 18 m.  c) A curve that steepens.  d) Flat (horizontal) line.
**Answer: c) A curve that steepens.**

**4.** Constant acceleration on an acceleration–time graph is a — ?
a) Slope = 12 ÷ 3 = 4 m/s².  b) ½ × 3 × 12 = 18 m.  c) A curve that steepens.  d) Flat (horizontal) line.
**Answer: d) Flat (horizontal) line.**
