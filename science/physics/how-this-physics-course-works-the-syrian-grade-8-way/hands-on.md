# Hands-on — How this Physics course works — the Syrian Grade 8 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Learn the shape on a real lesson
Take one short physics test you can do at home — for example, timing a falling object or balancing a ruler on a pencil. Before you start, write the Syrian page furniture yourself: an objectives line ("By the end I will be able to…"), three key words, then run the test, and finish by writing your finding in a boxed "I conclude" sentence. Say aloud: objectives first, experiment second, conclusion mine.
