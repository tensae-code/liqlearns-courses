# Lesson plan — How this Physics course works — the Syrian Grade 8 way

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** How this Physics course works — the Syrian Grade 8 way
**Place in the curriculum:** Physics (CURRICULUM.md Unit 18) — a new lesson built for the Syria placement named in lesson/sources.md
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Syrian Grade 8 book's own way of teaching physics — a research-and-experiment course tied to daily life and the local environment, where every lesson opens with printed objectives and key words, is built around an "I try and I conclude" bench experiment with an explicit "I conclude" box, gives worked applications and enrichment boxes, and closes its units with unit questions plus a hands-on project
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly. Earlier lessons make a good preparation and are recommended, never forced

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The book's shape — the units** — The Grade 8 book is a combined Physics & Chemistry volume in four units, with physics and chemistry alternating by unit: **U1 is structural chemistry, then U2 Motion & forces, U3 Electricity and U4 Light are physics**.
2. **How every lesson is built — objectives, key words, "I try and I conclude"** — Every lesson opens with printed **objectives** (الأهداف) — the exact things the learner will be able to do — and **key words** (الكلمات المفتاحية), the lesson's vocabulary named up front.
3. **Unit endings — questions and a project** — Each unit closes with **unit questions** that test the whole unit together, and a **hands-on project** (مشروع) — something real to design and build that uses the unit's physics: the electricity unit closes with a physics project on the effect of resistance value on electrical-energy consumption — the unit's law tested against a real household cost.
