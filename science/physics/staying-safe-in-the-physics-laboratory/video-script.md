# Video script — Staying safe in the physics laboratory

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Laboratory safety rules protect everyone in the room.
Bring only the books you need.
Read the experiment beforehand so you know what is coming.
Never eat, drink, use cosmetics or chew gum in the lab, and never taste chemicals or touch them with bare hands.
Report every accident at once, however small.
Take care with electrical equipment, do only authorised experiments, and wash your hands afterwards.
