# Hands-on — Staying safe in the physics laboratory

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Read the rules again and act out three of them with a partner: reporting an accident, refusing food in the lab, washing hands after.

## Apply it where you live
Write the lab rules as a poster list for your classroom wall, in your own words.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21811 — lab bench with rule icons — no food, wash hands, report accidents] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.
