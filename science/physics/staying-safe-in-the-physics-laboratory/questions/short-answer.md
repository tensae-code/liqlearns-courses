# Staying safe in the physics laboratory — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** What must you do after any accident in the lab?
**Answer: Report it at once.**

**2.** May you taste a chemical to identify it?
**Answer: Never.**

**3.** What should you do before starting an experiment?
**Answer: Read it beforehand.**

**4.** What is the last safety step after lab work?
**Answer: Wash your hands.**
