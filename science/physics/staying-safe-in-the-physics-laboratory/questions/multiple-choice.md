# Staying safe in the physics laboratory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What must you do after any accident in the lab?
a) Report it at once.  b) Never.  c) Read it beforehand.  d) Wash your hands.
**Answer: a) Report it at once.**

**2.** May you taste a chemical to identify it?
a) Report it at once.  b) Never.  c) Read it beforehand.  d) Wash your hands.
**Answer: b) Never.**

**3.** What should you do before starting an experiment?
a) Report it at once.  b) Never.  c) Read it beforehand.  d) Wash your hands.
**Answer: c) Read it beforehand.**

**4.** What is the last safety step after lab work?
a) Report it at once.  b) Never.  c) Read it beforehand.  d) Wash your hands.
**Answer: d) Wash your hands.**
