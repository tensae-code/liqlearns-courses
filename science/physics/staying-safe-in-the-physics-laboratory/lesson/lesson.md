# Staying safe in the physics laboratory

## Do it first
Read the rules again and act out three of them with a partner: reporting an accident, refusing food in the lab, washing hands after.

[Picture 21811 — lab bench with rule icons — no food, wash hands, report accidents]

## Staying safe in the physics laboratory — the teaching
Laboratory safety rules protect everyone in the room. Bring only the books you need. Read the experiment beforehand so you know what is coming. Never eat, drink, use cosmetics or chew gum in the lab, and never taste chemicals or touch them with bare hands. Report every accident at once, however small. Take care with electrical equipment, do only authorised experiments, and wash your hands afterwards.

## The key ideas

- Never taste chemicals, and never eat or drink in the laboratory.
- Report every accident at once, however small.
- Read the experiment beforehand and do only authorised experiments.

## Say it to your companion
Explain staying safe in the physics laboratory to your companion in your own words. Use the key ideas above, give one example from your own home or street, and finish with the hardest point — the one you would have got wrong before this lesson. If your companion can repeat the main idea back to you, the lesson has landed.
