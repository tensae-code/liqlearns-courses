## Acceleration — steady change of velocity — the teaching
Grade 10's acceleration is a vector: the rate of change of velocity, direction included. This unit's case is uniform (constant) acceleration — the same change of velocity in each equal time. Deceleration is acceleration directed against the motion. The standard example is free fall: near Earth a dropped body accelerates downward under gravity at a steady rate, its velocity growing by the same amount each second — visible in a strobe photograph where the gaps between flashes grow in a steady pattern.

**Keep the formula:** a = (v − u) / t

## The key ideas

- Acceleration is a vector — rate of change of velocity.
- Uniform acceleration: equal velocity changes in equal times.
- Free fall is steady acceleration under gravity; strobe gaps grow steadily.
