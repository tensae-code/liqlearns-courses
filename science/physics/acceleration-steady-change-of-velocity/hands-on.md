# Hands-on — Acceleration — steady change of velocity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Drop a stone and a crumpled paper together and listen: one clink or two? Then imagine flash photos of the fall — draw the growing gaps yourself.

## Apply it where you live
From a strobe-style sketch, compute a falling body's acceleration from two gaps.

## Read the picture
Study [Picture 21881 — falling ball photographed at equal time flashes, gaps growing] and reproduce it from memory on paper — every label and arrow. Compare with the original and fix what you missed.

## Calculate by hand
Using a = (v − u) / t: invent one example from your own home (your own masses, distances or times), solve it on paper with all working shown, and check the answer's unit makes sense.
