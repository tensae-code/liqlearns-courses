# Acceleration — steady change of velocity — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Velocity grows 0, 10, 20, 30 m/s each second. Acceleration?
**Answer: 10 m/s² — a steady change of 10 m/s per second.**

**2.** What is deceleration?
**Answer: Acceleration directed against the motion, slowing the body.**

**3.** In a strobe photo of a falling ball, what do the gaps do?
**Answer: They grow — each flash interval covers more distance.**

**4.** Is acceleration present when direction changes at steady speed?
**Answer: Yes — velocity includes direction.**
