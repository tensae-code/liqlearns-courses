# Video script — Acceleration — steady change of velocity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Grade 10's acceleration is a vector: the rate of change of velocity, direction included.
This unit's case is uniform (constant) acceleration — the same change of velocity in each equal time.
Deceleration is acceleration directed against the motion.
The standard example is free fall: near Earth a dropped body accelerates downward under gravity at a steady rate, its velocity growing by the same amount each second — visible in a strobe photograph where the gaps between flashes grow in a steady pattern.

Remember: **Acceleration is a vector — rate of change of velocity.**
Remember: **Uniform acceleration: equal velocity changes in equal times.**
Remember: **Free fall is steady acceleration under gravity; strobe gaps grow steadily.**
Keep the formula: **a = (v − u) / t**
