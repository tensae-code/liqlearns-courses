# Hands-on — Adding vectors by drawing — triangle and parallelogram

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
On paper, add a walk of 3 units east and 4 units north by both methods. Measure: the resultant should come out 5 units by the scale in both drawings.

## Apply it where you live
Add two real forces by drawing, by both methods, and compare the two resultants.
