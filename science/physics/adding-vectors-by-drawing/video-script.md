# Video script — Adding vectors by drawing — triangle and parallelogram

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Vectors at angles are added by drawing to scale.
The triangle method: draw the first vector, set the second vector's tail at the first's head (nose-to-tail), and the resultant runs from the first tail to the last head.
The parallelogram method: draw both from one point, complete the parallelogram, and the diagonal from that point is the resultant.
Measure its length for magnitude and its angle for direction.
Subtraction joins in as adding the negative (reversed) vector.

Remember: **Triangle method: nose-to-tail; resultant from first tail to last head.**
Remember: **Parallelogram method: resultant is the diagonal from the common start.**
Remember: **Measure the resultant's length (magnitude) and angle (direction).**
