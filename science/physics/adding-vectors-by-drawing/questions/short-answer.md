# Adding vectors by drawing — triangle and parallelogram — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** In the triangle method, where does the resultant start and end?
**Answer: From the first vector's tail to the last vector's head.**

**2.** In the parallelogram method, the resultant is the — ?
**Answer: The diagonal from the vectors' common starting point.**

**3.** How do you subtract vector B by drawing?
**Answer: Reverse B and add it (add the negative vector).**

**4.** Why must the drawing be to scale?
**Answer: So measuring the resultant gives true magnitude.**
