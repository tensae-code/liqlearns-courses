Today: **eddy currents and their uses**. Change the flux through solid metal and the induced current does not run in a wire — it swirls inside the metal itself, in eddies. Those eddies fight whatever change made them. They brake a swinging plate dead and slow a magnet falling through a copper tube. In transformer cores they waste energy as heat, so cores are sliced into insulated laminations. But hire them and they brake trains without contact, calm a galvanometer's needle, melt steel in induction furnaces and find coins in the soil.
