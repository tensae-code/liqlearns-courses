# Eddy currents and their uses — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Induced currents swirling inside a solid conductor are called _____ currents.
**Answer: eddy**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A core built from thin insulated sheets is said to be _____.
**Answer: laminated**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) High-frequency transformers often use _____ cores, a barely conducting ceramic, to defeat eddies.
**Answer: ferrite**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Eddy braking of a train disc is contact-free, so the brake parts suffer no _____.
**Answer: wear**
