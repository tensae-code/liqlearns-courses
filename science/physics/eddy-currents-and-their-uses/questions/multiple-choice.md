# Eddy currents and their uses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Eddy currents are…
a) Currents in the primary winding  b) Induced currents circulating in closed loops inside a bulk conductor  c) Static charges on a metal surface  d) Currents in the Earth's core only
**Answer: b) Induced currents circulating in closed loops inside a bulk conductor**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A magnet falling inside a copper tube descends slowly because…
a) The tube is magnetic  b) Eddies induced in the tube wall create a field opposing the magnet's motion, by Lenz's law  c) Air pressure builds beneath it  d) Copper repels all magnets permanently
**Answer: b) Eddies induced in the tube wall create a field opposing the magnet's motion, by Lenz's law**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Transformer cores are laminated in order to…
a) Increase the flux  b) Slice the eddy-current loops into high-resistance strips, cutting the eddy loss  c) Make the core heavier  d) Let oil circulate for cooling
**Answer: b) Slice the eddy-current loops into high-resistance strips, cutting the eddy loss**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Eddy-current loss in a core grows with…
a) Frequency only, linearly  b) The square of the frequency (and of flux density)  c) The core's colour  d) The square of the resistance
**Answer: b) The square of the frequency (and of flux density)**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Eddy currents are deliberately used for damping in…
a) A galvanometer's aluminium coil frame, which brakes the pointer's swings  b) A transformer's windings  c) A battery's plates  d) A microphone's diaphragm
**Answer: a) A galvanometer's aluminium coil frame, which brakes the pointer's swings**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Induction heating works by…
a) Passing the mains current directly through the workpiece  b) Inducing strong eddy currents in the metal with a high-frequency field, heating it by its own resistance  c) Radiant heat from a glowing coil  d) Friction from a spinning magnet
**Answer: b) Inducing strong eddy currents in the metal with a high-frequency field, heating it by its own resistance**
