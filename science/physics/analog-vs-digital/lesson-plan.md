# Lesson plan — Analog vs digital

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Physics · **Lesson:** Analog vs digital
**Place in the curriculum:** Grade 12 · Unit 5 — Basics of Electronics.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** analog and digital signals; logic levels — Analog signals vary continuously; digital signals live at two levels — high/low, 1/0 — with positive and negative logic assigning the 1 differently, the binary basis of…
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 12 Physics lessons before it in Unit 5 — Basics of Electronics — and on the learner's own world: the examples are the learner's home, street, school and body.
