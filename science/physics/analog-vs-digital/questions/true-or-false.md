# Analog vs digital — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. Analog signals vary continuously; digital signals live at two levels — high/low, 1/0 — with positive and negative logic assigning the 1 differently, the binary basis of all the gates that follow.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Analog signals vary continuously; digital signals live at two levels — high/low, 1/0 — with negative and negative logic assigning the 1 differently, the binary basis of all the gates that follow.**
**Answer: False**
