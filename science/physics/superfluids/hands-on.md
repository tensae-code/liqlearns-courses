# Hands-on — Superfluids

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Superfluids need temperatures no school lab reaches, so work the comparison
Superfluids need temperatures no school lab reaches, so work the comparison: time identical funnels draining water, oil and glycerine, and rank their viscosities; then read the superfluid as the list's limit — the drain time falling to the frictionless floor. Write what would break in your ranking's assumptions if a zero-viscosity liquid were poured.
