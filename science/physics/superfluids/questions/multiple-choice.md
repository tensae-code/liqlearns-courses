# Superfluids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A superfluid's defining property is…
a) Zero mass  b) Zero viscosity  c) Infinite density  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Zero viscosity**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Superfluidity appears in helium below about…
a) 220 K  b) 2.2 K  c) −2 °C only in winter  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) 2.2 K**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Set swirling, a superfluid…
a) Stops instantly  b) Keeps swirling without fading  c) Boils  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Keeps swirling without fading**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The creeping film lets a superfluid…
a) Freeze solid  b) Climb walls and escape an open container  c) Turn into gas at once  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Climb walls and escape an open container**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Helium II also conducts heat…
a) Not at all  b) Astonishingly well — temperature differences vanish almost at once  c) Only downward  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) Astonishingly well — temperature differences vanish almost at once**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The two-fluid picture describes helium II as…
a) Two gases mixing  b) A normal fluid and a superfluid interpenetrating  c) Ice floating in water  d) The question has no answer
**Answer: b) A normal fluid and a superfluid interpenetrating**
