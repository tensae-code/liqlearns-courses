# Superfluids — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Why can a superfluid not be kept like water in an open cup?
**Answer:** Its creeping surface film climbs the walls and drains it over the rim.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens to drag and pressure loss in superfluid flow?
**Answer:** Both vanish — flow meets no viscous resistance.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What second marvel follows the two-fluid picture?
**Answer:** Extremely effective heat conduction through the liquid.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does PECTAA place superfluidity at the end of fluids?
**Answer:** It is the limiting case the unit's ideas point to — viscosity taken to zero — and a doorway to low-temperature physics.
