## Archimedes' principle and buoyant force

The upthrust equals the weight of fluid displaced (F_B = ρ_fluid g V), derived from the pressure difference between a submerged block's bottom and top faces; buoyancy activities (3.9, 3.10) measure it and find a liquid's density from it.

For the Archimedes/density teaching the guide prescribes a classroom experiment the student book does not print in this form: pupils prepare in advance two glass cups, table salt, a spoon, two eggs and water — an egg sinks in fresh water and floats as salt is dissolved in, making density and buoyant force visible with kitchen materials.

The teacher is told to support the class to conduct it properly and prepare the materials ahead of the lesson.
