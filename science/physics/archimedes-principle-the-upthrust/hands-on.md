# Hands-on — Archimedes' principle — the upthrust

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The floating egg
Put an egg in a cup of fresh water — it sinks. Stir in salt spoon by spoon until the egg rises and floats. The water's density grew until the upthrust could hold the egg.

## See it and say it
Study this lesson's picture — a submerged block with bigger pressure arrows underneath than on top — and name every part of it out loud. Then close the picture and draw it from memory; check your drawing against the lesson.

## Teach it back
Explain this lesson to someone at home using one thing in the room as your example. Their questions show you which part to re-read.
