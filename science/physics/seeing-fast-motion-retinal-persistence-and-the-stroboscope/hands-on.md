# Hands-on — Seeing fast motion — retinal persistence and the stroboscope

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

["(1) Spin a marked disc (a dot near the rim) under a phone's strobe/torch app or a flickering LED; tune the flash rate until the dot freezes, and record the rate. (2) Lower the rate to half and count the fixed images of the dot. (3) Detune slightly both ways and record the slow forward and slow reversed drifts — the wagon-wheel effect on your own bench."]
