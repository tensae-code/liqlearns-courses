# Seeing fast motion — retinal persistence and the stroboscope — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The eye's holding of an image is called retinal _____ .
**Answer: persistence**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) At N′ = N the moving object appears in apparent _____ .
**Answer: rest**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Flashing at a sub-multiple gives _____ fixed images.
**Answer: k (several)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) N′ slightly greater than N makes the motion appear _____ .
**Answer: reversed**
