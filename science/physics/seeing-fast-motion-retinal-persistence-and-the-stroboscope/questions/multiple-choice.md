# Seeing fast motion — retinal persistence and the stroboscope — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retinal persistence lasts about…
**Answer: a) 1/15 to 1/20 of a second  b) one second  c) 1/1000 of a second  d) one minute**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Because of persistence, a fast fan's blades appear as…
**Answer: a) a blurred disc  b) separate still blades  c) nothing  d) a single blade**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A stroboscope freezes motion when its flash frequency N′…
**Answer: a) matches the motion's frequency N (once per period)  b) is maximum  c) is random  d) equals zero**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Flashing at a sub-multiple of N shows…
**Answer: a) k fixed images at k phases  b) a blur  c) the object reversed always  d) nothing at all**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) If N′ is slightly above N, the motion appears…
**Answer: a) slowed and reversed  b) slowed forwards  c) speeded up  d) frozen**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The stroboscope measures a frequency by…
**Answer: a) tuning N′ until apparent rest appears  b) timing one flash  c) counting the blur  d) photographing the object**
