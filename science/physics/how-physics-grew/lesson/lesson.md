## How physics grew — the people who pushed it — the teaching
Physics knowledge did not arrive all at once. It grew over centuries as scientists in different periods asked sharper questions, measured more carefully, and replaced older ideas with better ones when evidence demanded it. Each generation stood on the work before it: ideas were tested, corrected and extended. That is why physics is a growing body of knowledge, not a fixed list of facts — and why the people who pushed it are remembered by name.

## The key ideas

- Physics ideas evolved and changed over many centuries.
- Prominent scientists of different periods advanced the subject.
- New ideas replaced older ones when evidence demanded it.
