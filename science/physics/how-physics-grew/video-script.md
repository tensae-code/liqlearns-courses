# Video script — How physics grew — the people who pushed it

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
---

Physics knowledge did not arrive all at once.
It grew over centuries as scientists in different periods asked sharper questions, measured more carefully, and replaced older ideas with better ones when evidence demanded it.
Each generation stood on the work before it: ideas were tested, corrected and extended.
That is why physics is a growing body of knowledge, not a fixed list of facts — and why the people who pushed it are remembered by name.

Remember: **Physics ideas evolved and changed over many centuries.**
Remember: **Prominent scientists of different periods advanced the subject.**
Remember: **New ideas replaced older ones when evidence demanded it.**
