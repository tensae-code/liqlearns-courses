# How physics grew — the people who pushed it — short answer

Answer in a few words, or with full working where numbers are given.

**1.** Did physics arrive all at once?
**Answer: No — it grew over centuries.**

**2.** What happens to an old physics idea when evidence goes against it?
**Answer: It is corrected or replaced by a better idea.**

**3.** Why are past scientists remembered in this lesson?
**Answer: Their work advanced physics, and later work stands on theirs.**

**4.** Is physics a fixed list of facts?
**Answer: No — it is a growing body of knowledge.**
