# Hands-on — How physics grew — the people who pushed it

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening, for real
Think of one tool that changed in your lifetime — a phone, a lamp, a plough. Knowledge grows the same way: step by step, person by person.

## Apply it where you live
Draw a short timeline of one technology you know, marking who or what pushed each step.
