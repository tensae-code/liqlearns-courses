# Experiment — estimating a molecule's size with an oil film — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A film one molecule thick is called a _____.
**Answer: monolayer**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The oil is carried in a _____ that evaporates away.
**Answer: solvent**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The powder dusted on the water is _____ powder.
**Answer: lycopodium**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The school result lands near 10⁻¹⁰ to 10⁻⁹ _____.
**Answer: metres**
