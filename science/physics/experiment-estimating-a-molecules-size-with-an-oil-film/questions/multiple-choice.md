# Experiment — estimating a molecule's size with an oil film — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The oil film's limiting thickness is…
**Answer: a) one molecule — a monolayer  b) one millimetre  c) ten molecules  d) set by the tray**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) If the film is one molecule thick, its thickness equals…
**Answer: a) the molecule's size  b) the drop's diameter  c) the water's depth  d) nothing useful**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The oil is diluted before the experiment so that…
**Answer: a) one drop carries a tiny enough volume to spread one molecule deep  b) it smells nicer  c) it mixes with water  d) it sinks**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) One drop's volume is measured by…
**Answer: a) counting how many drops make 1 mL  b) weighing the tray  c) a micrometer  d) guessing**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The film's area is made visible by…
**Answer: a) lycopodium powder pushed aside at the film's edge  b) dye in the oil  c) freezing the water  d) a magnet**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The final computation is…
**Answer: a) d = V/S  b) d = VS  c) d = S/V  d) d = V − S**
