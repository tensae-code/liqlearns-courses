# Video script — Experiment — estimating a molecule's size with an oil film

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **estimating a molecule's size with an oil film**. Dilute the oil, count the drops, let one drop spread one molecule thick on dusted water, measure the patch — and divide. A tray, a ruler, and the size of a molecule.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
