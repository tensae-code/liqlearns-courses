# Hands-on — Reading displacement–time graphs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Time a falling object
Drop a small stone from a measured height, time the fall three times, and compare with the fall equation. Say why the times differ a little each drop.

## See it and say it
Study this lesson's picture — three s–t curves — steady, speeding, stopped — with moving dots beside them — and name every part of it out loud. Then close the picture and draw it from memory; check your drawing against the lesson.

## Teach it back
Explain this lesson to someone at home using one thing in the room as your example. Their questions show you which part to re-read.
