# Turning a galvanometer into an ammeter or voltmeter — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Give one key fact this lesson teaches about the galvanometer and converting it.**
**Answer: A moving-coil galvanometer detects tiny currents.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Give one key fact this lesson teaches about the galvanometer and converting it.**
**Answer: And a high resistance in series converts it into a voltmeter — both conversions are worked numerically (e.g. a 5 mA movement into a 10 A ammeter.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which two conversions does the book work numerically?**
**Answer: A 5 mA movement turned into a 10 A ammeter (with a shunt), and a 40 Ω, 3 mA movement turned into a 0–10 V voltmeter (with a series resistor).**
