# Turning a galvanometer into an ammeter or voltmeter — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A moving-coil galvanometer detects tiny ______s.**
**Answer: current**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) And a high resistance in ______ converts it into a voltmeter — both conversions are worked numerically (e.g. a 5 mA movement into a 10 A ammeter.**
**Answer: series**
