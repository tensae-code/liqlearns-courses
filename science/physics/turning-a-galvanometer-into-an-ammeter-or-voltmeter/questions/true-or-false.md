# Turning a galvanometer into an ammeter or voltmeter — true or false

Say if each one is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A moving-coil galvanometer detects tiny currents.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A low-resistance shunt in parallel converts it into an ammeter.**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) And a high resistance in series converts it into a voltmeter — both conversions are worked numerically (e.g. a 5 mA movement into a 10 A ammeter.**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) And a high resistance in parallel converts it into a voltmeter — both conversions are worked numerically (e.g. a 5 mA movement into a 10 A ammeter.**
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) A 40 Ω, 3 mA movement into a 0–10 V voltmeter).**
**Answer: True**
