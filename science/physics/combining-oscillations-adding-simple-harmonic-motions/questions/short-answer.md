# Combining oscillations — adding simple harmonic motions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Compute the resultant amplitude for A₁ = 6 cm, A₂ = 8 cm, φ = 90°.
**Answer: A = √(A₁² + A₂² + 2A₁A₂ cos φ) = √(36 + 64 + 0) = √100 = 10 cm (cos 90° = 0).**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why do beats slow down as a string comes into tune?
**Answer: The beat frequency is |f₁ − f₂|; tuning brings f₂ toward f₁, so the difference — and with it the throbbing rate — falls toward zero, and the tuner stops when the beats vanish.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How can a Lissajous figure measure an unknown frequency?
**Answer: Feed a known frequency to one axis and the unknown to the other on an oscilloscope: the figure's shape fixes the ratio of the two frequencies (counting its lobes), so the unknown follows from the known one.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Where does combining oscillations sit in the Lao Grade 12 course, and what comes just before it?
**Answer: Part II (Mechanical Oscillations), Lesson 5 — after Lesson 3 (Simple Harmonic Oscillation) and Lesson 4 (The Various Kinds of Oscillation: damped, forced, resonance), so learners combine motions they have already met one at a time.**
