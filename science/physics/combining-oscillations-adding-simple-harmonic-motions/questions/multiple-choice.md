# Combining oscillations — adding simple harmonic motions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Two same-frequency motions along a line combine by…
**Answer: a) adding their displacements at every instant  b) adding their frequencies  c) multiplying their amplitudes  d) taking the larger one only**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Two in-phase motions of amplitudes 3 cm and 4 cm give a resultant amplitude of…
**Answer: a) 7 cm  b) 5 cm  c) 1 cm  d) 12 cm**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Beats arise when the two frequencies are…
**Answer: a) slightly different — the motions drift in and out of step  b) exactly equal  c) one double the other  d) both zero**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The beat frequency equals…
**Answer: a) |f₁ − f₂|, the difference of the two frequencies  b) f₁ + f₂  c) f₁ × f₂  d) the average of the two**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Two equal-frequency motions at right angles, a quarter-cycle apart in phase, trace…
**Answer: a) a circle or ellipse — a Lissajous figure  b) a straight horizontal line  c) a single point  d) a parabola**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In the Lao Grade 12 book, combining oscillations is…
**Answer: a) its own lesson (Part II, Lesson 5), after simple harmonic motion and the kinds of oscillation  b) part of the nuclear part  c) taught only through beats in the sound part  d) not taught**
