# Combining oscillations — adding simple harmonic motions — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In antiphase, equal amplitudes _____ each other to rest.
**Answer: cancel**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The resultant amplitude formula contains the term 2A₁A₂ cos _____, the phase difference.
**Answer: φ (phi)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The swelling and fading of two close frequencies is called _____.
**Answer: beats**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Curves traced by oscillations combined at right angles are called _____ figures.
**Answer: Lissajous**
