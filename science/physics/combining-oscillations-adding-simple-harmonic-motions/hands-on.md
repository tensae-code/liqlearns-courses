# Hands-on — Combining oscillations — adding simple harmonic motions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two pendulums, one trace
Hang two pendulums of nearly equal length side by side, set both swinging in the same plane, one slightly ahead of the other, and watch a mark on one bob against the other: they drift together, oppose, and come together again — the slow beat of two almost-equal frequencies, visible. Then set one pendulum swinging across the other's line and watch the combined path curve — a first Lissajous figure made with string and weights, the Lao guides' local-materials way.
