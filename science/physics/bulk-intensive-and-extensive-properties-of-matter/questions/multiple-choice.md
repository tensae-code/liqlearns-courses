# Bulk, intensive and extensive properties of matter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A bulk property is a property of…
**Answer: a) a whole amount of matter taken together  b) a single atom  c) a single molecule  d) the container**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is extensive?
**Answer: a) mass  b) temperature  c) density  d) melting point**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Halve a sample: which property halves with it?
**Answer: a) volume  b) temperature  c) density  d) pressure at a point**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Which of these is intensive?
**Answer: a) density  b) mass  c) volume  d) total internal energy**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A cup of water drawn from a tank at 40 °C is at…
**Answer: a) 40 °C — temperature is intensive  b) half the tank's temperature  c) 20 °C  d) a temperature depending on the cup**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Density is intensive because it is…
**Answer: a) a ratio of two extensive properties, mass ÷ volume  b) measured in kilograms  c) always small  d) a property of solids only**
