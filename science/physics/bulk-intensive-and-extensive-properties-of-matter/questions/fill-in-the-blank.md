# Bulk, intensive and extensive properties of matter — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Properties of the whole amount of matter, taken together, are called _____ properties.
**Answer: bulk**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A property that doubles when the amount doubles is called _____.
**Answer: extensive**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A property that stays the same however much of the sample you take is called _____.
**Answer: intensive**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Mass divided by volume gives _____, an intensive property that identifies a material.
**Answer: density**
