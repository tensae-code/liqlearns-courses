# Hands-on — Bulk, intensive and extensive properties of matter

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The splitting test
Take a container of water (warm, dyed if possible), a thermometer, a balance and measuring cylinders. Measure the whole amount: mass, volume, temperature. Pour off exactly half into a second container and measure all three again for each half. Tabulate whole / half / half. The numbers that halved are extensive; the number that refused is intensive. Finish by computing density for the whole and for a half from your own masses and volumes, and write the sentence the table proves: a ratio of two extensive properties is intensive.
