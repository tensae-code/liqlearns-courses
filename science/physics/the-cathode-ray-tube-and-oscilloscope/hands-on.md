# Hands-on — The cathode ray tube and oscilloscope

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Reading a trace on paper
On squared paper, draw a sine trace 4 divisions high (peak) and 5 divisions wide per cycle. With the settings 2 V per division and 1 ms per division, work the peak voltage (8 V) and the period (5 ms) and frequency (200 Hz). Say aloud: height is voltage, width is time — the settings turn divisions into numbers.

## Fields bend the beam — in words
Bring (in imagination or with a teacher's demonstration tube) a bar magnet near a cathode ray beam and describe the spot moving; remove it and the spot returns. Say aloud: a magnetic field deflects the beam, an electric field can too — the beam is a stream of moving charges, not a ray of light.
