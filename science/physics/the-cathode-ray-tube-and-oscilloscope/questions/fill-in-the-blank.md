# The cathode ray tube and oscilloscope — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Cathode rays travel in _____ lines and cast sharp shadows.
**Answer: straight**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The control _____ sets the brightness of the spot by controlling the beam current.
**Answer: grid**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Two pairs of deflection plates bend the beam sideways and up-and-_____.
**Answer: down**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The steady sweep that moves the spot across the screen is produced by the _____ base circuit.
**Answer: time**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Frequency is one divided by the _____.
**Answer: period**
