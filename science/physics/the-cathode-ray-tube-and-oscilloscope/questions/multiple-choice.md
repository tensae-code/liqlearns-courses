# The cathode ray tube and oscilloscope — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Cathode rays are…
a) Rays of light  b) Streams of electrons  c) Positive rays  d) X-rays
**Answer: b) Streams of electrons**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Electrons leave a heated cathode by…
a) Radioactive decay  b) Thermionic emission  c) Refraction  d) Induction
**Answer: b) Thermionic emission**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Cathode rays are deflected by…
a) Only gravity  b) Electric and magnetic fields  c) Sound  d) Glass alone
**Answer: b) Electric and magnetic fields**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The part of the tube that accelerates and focuses the beam is the…
a) Screen coating  b) Electron gun  c) Time base  d) Grid only
**Answer: b) Electron gun**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The beam glows on the screen because the screen is coated with a _____ material.
a) Magnetic  b) Fluorescent  c) Transparent  d) Metallic
**Answer: b) Fluorescent**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) On a CRO trace, the period of a wave is read from…
a) The height of the trace  b) The width of one cycle × the time-per-division setting  c) The brightness  d) The number of traces
**Answer: b) The width of one cycle × the time-per-division setting**
