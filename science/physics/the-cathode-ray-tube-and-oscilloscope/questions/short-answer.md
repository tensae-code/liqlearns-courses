# The cathode ray tube and oscilloscope — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give two pieces of evidence that cathode rays are not light.
**Answer: They are deflected by electric and magnetic fields, which light is not; and they carry negative charge / consist of particles identical from every cathode metal — light carries no charge and is not bent by a magnet.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A spot sits at the centre of the screen. The vertical plates receive a steady positive voltage. What happens and why?
**Answer: The spot moves upward (toward the positive plate's side of the deflection, as the electron beam bends toward the positive plate) and stays there while the voltage lasts — a steady deflection voltage holds a steady displacement.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A trace stands 3 divisions high from zero to peak at 4 V/div, and one cycle is 2.5 divisions wide at 4 ms/div. Find the peak voltage, period and frequency.
**Answer: Peak voltage = 3 × 4 = 12 V. Period = 2.5 × 4 = 10 ms = 0.010 s. Frequency = 1/0.010 = 100 Hz.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why must the tube be evacuated for the beam to reach the screen cleanly?
**Answer: Air molecules would collide with the electrons, scattering and stopping the beam and glowing along its path; a vacuum gives the electrons a clear run from gun to screen.**
