Today: **the cathode ray tube and oscilloscope**.

Heat a cathode in an empty tube and electrons boil off; a high voltage fires them forward as cathode rays. The tube's gun focuses the beam, two sets of plates steer it, and it glows where it hits the screen. Add a steady sweep and the beam becomes a drawing machine — the oscilloscope — tracing voltage against time so you can measure both from the picture.
