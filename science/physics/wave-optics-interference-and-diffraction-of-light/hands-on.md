# Hands-on — Wave optics — interference and diffraction of light

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## See light spread, then split
First, the slit: hold two straight-edged cards so a hair-thin vertical gap is left between them, and look through the gap at a distant bright lamp. The lamp stretches sideways into a spread band with darker stripes inside it — that is diffraction through your slit; narrow the gap and the spread widens. Second, the grating: tilt the shiny side of a CD toward a white lamp and move it slowly. The surface is a spiral of fine grooves — a diffraction grating — and it fans the white light into rainbow spectra at definite angles. Say aloud: one slit spreads the light; many grooves sort it by wavelength.
