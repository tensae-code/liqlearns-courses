Today: **wave optics — light caught behaving as a wave**. Shine one colour of light through two narrow slits and the screen does not show two bright lines — it shows many: bright, dark, bright, dark. The two waves are adding where they arrive in step and cancelling where they arrive out of step, and the path difference decides which. Squeeze light through one narrow slit and it spreads out — diffraction — strongest when the slit is about one wavelength wide. Use hundreds of slits at once, a grating, and each colour bends to its own angle: interference becomes a ruler that measures the wavelength of light.
