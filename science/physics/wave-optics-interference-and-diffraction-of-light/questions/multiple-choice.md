# Wave optics — interference and diffraction of light — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Two light sources produce a steady interference pattern only if they are…
a) Very bright  b) Coherent — same wavelength, constant phase difference  c) Different colours  d) Pointing at each other
**Answer: b) Coherent — same wavelength, constant phase difference**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) At a point where the path difference from two slits is exactly 2λ, the screen is…
a) Dark  b) Bright — constructive interference  c) Half-lit  d) Coloured
**Answer: b) Bright — constructive interference**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) In Young's experiment, the fringe width w = λD/a grows when…
a) The slit separation a grows  b) The screen distance D grows  c) The wavelength gets shorter  d) The slits are blocked
**Answer: b) The screen distance D grows**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A single slit is made narrower (still wider than λ). The diffraction pattern's central maximum…
a) Gets narrower  b) Stays the same  c) Gets wider  d) Vanishes
**Answer: c) Gets wider**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Light shows obvious diffraction only through very fine slits because…
a) Light travels too fast  b) Its wavelength is very small, and diffraction is strong only when the opening is comparable to the wavelength  c) Slits absorb light  d) Eyes cannot see it otherwise
**Answer: b) Its wavelength is very small, and diffraction is strong only when the opening is comparable to the wavelength**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) For a diffraction grating, the first-order maximum of wavelength λ sits where…
a) d sin θ = λ/2  b) sin θ = dλ  c) d sin θ = λ  d) θ = λ/d²
**Answer: c) d sin θ = λ**
