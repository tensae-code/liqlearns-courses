# Wave optics — interference and diffraction of light — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sources that keep a constant phase difference are called _____ sources.
**Answer: coherent**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Crest meeting trough, cancelling to darkness, is _____ interference.
**Answer: destructive**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The spreading of light as it passes a narrow slit is called _____.
**Answer: diffraction**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A single slit's first dark minimum sits where a sin θ = _____.
**Answer: λ**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In the grating formula d sin θ = nλ, the whole number n is called the _____ of the maximum.
**Answer: order**

**6.** (Sub lesson 4) The rule I = I₀ cos²θ for light passing a second polarizing filter is called _____ law.
**Answer: Malus's**
