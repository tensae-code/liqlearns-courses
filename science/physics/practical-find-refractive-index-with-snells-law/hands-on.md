# Hands-on — Practical — find the refractive index with Snell's law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(1) Do the practical: trace a block, send rays at 30°, 45° and 60°, measure, and compute n each time. (2) Plot sin i against sin r and read n from the slope. (3) List your three biggest error sources and one fix for each.
