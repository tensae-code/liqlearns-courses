# Practical — find the refractive index with Snell's law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Angles in this practical are measured from…
**Answer: a) the normal  b) the surface  c) the block's edge  d) the ray**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The refracted ray inside the block is found by…
**Answer: a) joining the entry and exit marks of the traced ray  b) guessing  c) measuring the block  d) the normal alone**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Snell's law for the index is…
**Answer: a) n = sin i / sin r  b) n = sin r / sin i  c) n = i / r  d) n = i − r**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) For glass, the result should land near…
**Answer: a) 1.5  b) 1.0  c) 2.5  d) 0.5**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Repeating at several angles and averaging…
**Answer: a) reduces the effect of one misread measurement  b) changes the glass  c) is forbidden  d) removes the need for a normal**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) On a sin i against sin r graph, n is…
**Answer: a) the slope  b) the intercept  c) the area  d) the curvature**
