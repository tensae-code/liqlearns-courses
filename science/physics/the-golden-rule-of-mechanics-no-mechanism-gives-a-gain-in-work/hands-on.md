# Hands-on — The golden rule of mechanics — no mechanism gives a gain in work

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trade force for distance
Lift a heavy book 20 cm directly and note the effort. Then slide it up a long ramp (a plank on stacked books) to the same height, pulling with a spring scale or judging by feel: the pull is smaller, the path longer. Measure both distances and multiply pull × distance each way — the products come out near-equal, the ramp's a little bigger. That difference is friction's share: your own efficiency measurement.
