# The golden rule of mechanics — no mechanism gives a gain in work — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What you save in force you pay in _____.
**Answer: distance**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The share of work friction turns into _____ is why no real machine reaches 100%.
**Answer: heat**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Efficiency η equals useful work divided by _____ work.
**Answer: total**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A block system at 96% efficiency delivers 96 J usefully from every _____ J put in.
**Answer: 100**
