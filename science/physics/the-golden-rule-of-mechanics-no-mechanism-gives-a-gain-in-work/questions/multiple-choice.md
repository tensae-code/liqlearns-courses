# The golden rule of mechanics — no mechanism gives a gain in work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The golden rule of mechanics states that…
**Answer: a) no mechanism gives a gain in work  b) levers create work from nothing  c) friction helps every machine  d) work is measured in newtons**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A machine that halves the force needed will…
**Answer: a) double the distance the effort moves  b) halve the distance too  c) need no work  d) lift for free**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Efficiency is defined as…
**Answer: a) η = A_useful/A_total  b) η = A_total/A_useful  c) η = F × s  d) η = mass/volume**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In the book's table, a block system keeps about…
**Answer: a) 94–98% of the work  b) 30–40%  c) 10%  d) exactly 100%**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A screw jack's low efficiency comes from…
**Answer: a) strong friction in its threads  b) its great height  c) its colour  d) the load's weight**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) That same friction makes the screw jack good for…
**Answer: a) holding a load safely when you let go  b) lifting fastest  c) lifting for free  d) measuring mass**
