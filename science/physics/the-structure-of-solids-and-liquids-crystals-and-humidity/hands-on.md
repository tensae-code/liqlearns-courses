# Hands-on — The structure of solids and liquids — crystals and humidity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Float it, then break the skin
Fill a clean glass with still water. Dry a steel sewing needle, lay it across the tines of a fork, and lower it flat onto the surface: it rests on the water's skin. Look from the side at the dent it makes. Now touch the surface at one edge with a drop of washing-up liquid — the film fails and the needle sinks. Write the two observations (floats on a dented film; sinks when the film is weakened) and the one-line cause (surface tension) — then dry your hands and note how fast they dry, and whether the room air feels dry or damp: that rate is humidity working on you.
