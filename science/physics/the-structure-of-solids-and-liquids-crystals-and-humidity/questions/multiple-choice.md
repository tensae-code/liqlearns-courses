# The structure of solids and liquids — crystals and humidity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A crystalline solid is told by…
**Answer: a) a strict repeating lattice and one sharp melting point  b) softening over a range  c) having no order at all  d) flowing at room temperature**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Glass softens over a range of temperature instead of melting at a point because it is…
**Answer: a) amorphous — no long-range order  b) a single crystal  c) a polycrystal  d) saturated**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Surface tension exists because a liquid's surface particles…
**Answer: a) are pulled inwards with no outward partner, so the surface shrinks like a stretched film  b) are hotter than the rest  c) have evaporated  d) are charged**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A liquid rises in a thin capillary tube when it…
**Answer: a) wets the tube's wall  b) boils  c) is saturated  d) freezes**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Vapour over a liquid in a closed vessel is saturated when…
**Answer: a) evaporation and condensation balance — as many particles leave as return  b) all the liquid has evaporated  c) boiling starts  d) the vessel is opened**

**6.** (Sub lesson 4) Relative humidity is…
**Answer: a) the actual vapour density as a percentage of the density that would saturate the air at that temperature  b) the mass of all the air's water  c) the dew point in degrees  d) rainfall in millimetres**
