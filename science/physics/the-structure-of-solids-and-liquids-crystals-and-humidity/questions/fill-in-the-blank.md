# The structure of solids and liquids — crystals and humidity — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The strict repeating order of particles in a crystal is its _____.
**Answer: lattice**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A liquid's surface behaves like a stretched _____ — surface tension.
**Answer: film**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Evaporation happens at any temperature, from the _____ only.
**Answer: surface**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Vapour in dynamic equilibrium with its liquid is called _____ vapour.
**Answer: saturated**

**5.** (Sub lesson 4) Cool air to its _____ point and its vapour begins to condense as dew.
**Answer: dew**
