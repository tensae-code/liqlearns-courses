Today: **order, skin, and invisible water**. A crystal is particles in a strict lattice — that is why it melts at one sharp temperature. A liquid's surface is a stretched skin, surface tension, strong enough to float a needle. And the air around you holds water vapour — humidity is how full it is, priced against the most it could hold.
