# Hands-on — Density — the Beruni and Khazin way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Beruni's vessel at the sink
Fill a jug exactly to its spout (or to the brim of a cup set in a tray). Lower a stone in gently and catch the overflow in a measuring cup. Weigh the stone on a kitchen scale. Divide mass by overflow volume: you have Beruni's number. Test a metal spoon and a wooden block the same way and compare — wood floats, so hold it just under with a pin and say why that works.
