# Density — the Beruni and Khazin way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A body of mass 78 g displaces 10 cm³ of water. Find its density and name a likely metal (iron ≈ 7.8 g/cm³).
**Answer: ρ = 78/10 = 7.8 g/cm³ — iron (or steel), matching iron's table value.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the overflow method work for a ring when a ruler does not?
**Answer: A ring's shape defeats length × width × height, but the water it pushes aside takes exactly the ring's volume whatever its shape — catch and measure that water and the volume is known.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What error does weighing in air introduce, in Khazin's analysis?
**Answer: Air buoys the body slightly, like water buoys a swimmer, so the balance reads a little less than the true mass — the finest work corrects for the displaced air or weighs in vacuum.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) State Beruni's full procedure for an unknown stone, in order.
**Answer: Weigh the stone (mass). Fill the overflow vessel to the spout. Lower the stone gently in and catch all the overflow. Measure the overflow's volume. Divide mass by volume — the density names the stone against his tables.**
