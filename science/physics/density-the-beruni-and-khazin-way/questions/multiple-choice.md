# Density — the Beruni and Khazin way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Density is defined as…
**Answer: a) ρ = m/V — mass divided by volume  b) ρ = m × V  c) ρ = V/m  d) mass plus volume**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The density of pure gold is about…
**Answer: a) 19.3 g/cm³  b) 1.0 g/cm³  c) 0.9 g/cm³  d) 100 g/cm³**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Beruni's overflow vessel measures a body's…
**Answer: a) volume, as the water it displaces  b) mass directly  c) temperature  d) hardness**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The overflow water's volume equals…
**Answer: a) the volume of the body lowered in  b) the mass of the body  c) half the body's volume  d) the vessel's volume**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Khazin's vacuum weighing corrects for…
**Answer: a) the buoyancy of air on the body  b) the colour of the metal  c) the temperature of the room  d) the pull of the Moon**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The modern school method for an irregular solid's density is…
**Answer: a) Beruni's method: balance for mass, displaced water for volume  b) guessing from its shine  c) cutting it into cubes  d) melting it first**
