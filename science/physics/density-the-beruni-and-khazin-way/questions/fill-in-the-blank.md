# Density — the Beruni and Khazin way — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Density is mass divided by _____.
**Answer: volume**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A pure substance's density acts as its _____ — the number names the material.
**Answer: fingerprint**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The water a body pushes aside is called the _____ water.
**Answer: displaced**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Al-Khazin set out his refined weighing in the Book of the Balance of _____.
**Answer: Wisdom**
