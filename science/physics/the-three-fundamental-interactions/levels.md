# The three fundamental interactions — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner states in their own words the three fundamental interactions, what each acts between and rules, and reduces everyday forces (weight, friction, pushes, magnetic pull, light itself) to the interaction underneath — and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more everyday forces to undress to their interaction, harder comparisons of strength, reach and sign (attraction only vs attraction and repulsion), and the scale game — nucleus, atom, table, planet — naming the ruling interaction at each size.
