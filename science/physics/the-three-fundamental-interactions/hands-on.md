# Hands-on — The three fundamental interactions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three forces in your hand
Hold a small object. Feel its weight — gravity, acting between the object and the whole Earth. Now press it between your fingers: the push-back you feel is atoms repelling atoms — electromagnetism. Say aloud: two of the three interactions are already in my hand; the third is inside every atom of it, holding its nuclei together.

## The magnet census
With a magnet, test the things around you: which does it pull? Those pulls are electromagnetism showing itself directly. List three it pulls and three it does not — and for one it does not, name the interaction (if any) it still exerts on the magnet. (Gravity: it pulls everything, magnet included.)

## Undress a force
Choose one everyday force — the friction that stops a sliding book, the tension in a pulled rope, the spring in a pen. Write its story down to the atoms: which interaction is doing the work, and between what?
