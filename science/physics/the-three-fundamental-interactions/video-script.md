Today: **The three fundamental interactions**.

Everything that pushes, pulls, holds or shines does it by one of three interactions.
Gravity: every mass pulls every mass. Weak — but it never lets go, and it rules the planets.
Electromagnetism: the force between charges. It holds atoms together, it is every push and pull of daily life — and light itself is its wave.
The nuclear interaction: the strongest, the shortest reach. It glues the nucleus together, across a distance smaller than an atom.
Three interactions. The whole of physics, gathered.
