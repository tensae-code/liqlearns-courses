# The three fundamental interactions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many fundamental interactions does this lesson gather everything into?
a) Two  b) Three  c) Seven  d) One for every force
**Answer: b) Three**

**2.** The gravitational interaction acts between…
a) Charges only  b) Masses — every mass attracts every other mass  c) Nuclei only  d) Magnets only
**Answer: b) Masses — every mass attracts every other mass**

**3.** Why does gravity, the weakest interaction, rule the planets?
a) It is secretly the strongest  b) It only attracts and never cancels, and its reach is unlimited, so it adds up wherever matter gathers  c) Planets are too far away for the other interactions to matter at all  d) It travels faster than light
**Answer: b) It only attracts and never cancels, and its reach is unlimited, so it adds up wherever matter gathers**

**4.** The push of a chair against you, friction, and tension in a rope are all, underneath, the…
a) Gravitational interaction  b) Nuclear interaction  c) Electromagnetic interaction — atoms' electric forces at the surfaces  d) A fourth interaction, the contact interaction
**Answer: c) Electromagnetic interaction — atoms' electric forces at the surfaces**

**5.** What holds the protons of a nucleus together against their electric repulsion?
a) Gravity  b) The nuclear interaction — the strongest of the three, across the smallest distance  c) Friction  d) Nothing — nuclei fly apart at once
**Answer: b) The nuclear interaction — the strongest of the three, across the smallest distance**

**6.** Which interaction has unlimited reach?
a) The nuclear interaction  b) The electromagnetic interaction only  c) The gravitational interaction  d) None of them
**Answer: c) The gravitational interaction**
