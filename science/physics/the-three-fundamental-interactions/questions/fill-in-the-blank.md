# The three fundamental interactions — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Every mass attracts every other mass through the _____ interaction.
**Answer: gravitational**

**2.** The interaction between electric charges, which also underlies light, is the _____ interaction.
**Answer: electromagnetic**

**3.** The strongest interaction, with a reach of only one nucleus, is the _____ interaction.
**Answer: nuclear**

**4.** Gravity rules the planets although it is the weakest, because it never repels and never _____.
**Answer: cancels**
