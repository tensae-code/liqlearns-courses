# The three fundamental interactions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the three fundamental interactions, and for each, what it acts between.
**Answer: Gravitational — between masses. Electromagnetic — between electric charges. Nuclear — between the particles inside the nucleus.**

**2.** Light itself belongs to which interaction, and why does that fit?
**Answer: The electromagnetic interaction — light is that interaction travelling as a wave, the same interaction that acts between charges and holds atoms together.**

**3.** Why do we never feel the nuclear interaction directly in daily life, even though it is the strongest?
**Answer: Its reach is only about one nucleus wide and it is nothing beyond that, so it never acts across the distances of daily life — it works only inside atomic nuclei.**

**4.** A rope pulls a loaded sledge. Undress the tension in the rope down to its fundamental interaction.
**Answer: Tension is the electromagnetic interaction: along the rope, atoms pull on their neighbouring atoms through electric forces between their charges, link by link, from the puller to the load.**
