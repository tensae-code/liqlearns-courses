# Video script — The pinhole camera

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the pinhole camera**.

A dark box, one tiny hole, a paper screen — and a picture appears, upside down. Light travels straight, so rays from the top of the scene cross at the hole and land low, and rays from the bottom land high. A smaller hole makes the picture sharper but dimmer; a bigger hole makes it brighter but blurred. And the size is a simple trade of distances: how far the screen is behind the hole, divided by how far the object is in front of it.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
