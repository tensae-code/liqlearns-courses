# The pinhole camera — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The image in a pinhole camera is upside down because…
a) A lens turns it over  b) Straight rays from the object cross at the hole, so top lands low and bottom lands high  c) The screen is curved  d) Light bends at the hole
**Answer: b) Straight rays from the object cross at the hole, so top lands low and bottom lands high**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Left and right in the pinhole image are…
a) Unchanged  b) Reversed, for the same crossing reason  c) Doubled  d) Erased
**Answer: b) Reversed, for the same crossing reason**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Making the hole smaller makes the image…
a) Brighter and sharper  b) Dimmer and blurred  c) Sharper but dimmer  d) Bigger and brighter
**Answer: c) Sharper but dimmer**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Moving the screen farther from the hole makes the image…
a) Smaller and brighter  b) Bigger and dimmer  c) Upright  d) Disappear
**Answer: b) Bigger and dimmer**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) An object 5 m from the hole is imaged on a screen 0.25 m behind it. The magnification is…
a) 20  b) 0.05  c) 5.25  d) 1.25
**Answer: b) 0.05**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) At magnification 0.02, a 6 m tree makes an image of height…
a) 3 m  b) 0.12 m  c) 300 m  d) 0.06 m
**Answer: b) 0.12 m**
