# The pinhole camera — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Light travels in _____ lines, which is why the rays cross at the hole.
**Answer: straight**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The pinhole image is _____ — top and bottom swapped, left and right swapped.
**Answer: inverted**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A smaller hole gives a sharper but _____ image.
**Answer: dimmer**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Magnification = image distance ÷ _____ distance.
**Answer: object**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) An image with magnification below 1 is _____ than the object.
**Answer: smaller**
