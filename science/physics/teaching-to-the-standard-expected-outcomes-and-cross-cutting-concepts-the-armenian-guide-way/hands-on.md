# Hands-on — Teaching to the standard — expected outcomes and cross-cutting concepts, the Armenian guide way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with your hands
Take any topic you teach (or are learning) and write its outcome the Armenian way: not 'Chapter 4: Friction' but 'the learner will be able to… measure friction, predict its effect and argue from evidence'. Then list which cross-cutting concepts the topic carries (cause and effect? energy? systems?). If the outcome cannot be written as an ability, the guides' first chapter says the planning has not started yet.
