# Video script — Teaching to the standard — expected outcomes and cross-cutting concepts, the Armenian guide way

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **every Armenian teacher guide opens the same way, before a single topic: Chapter I states the standard — what the state standard asks of physics teaching, the expected outcomes for that grade named as abilities, and the cross-cutting concepts that run through the whole course — so the year's teaching is planned from outcomes backwards (teacher-guide evidence only)**. The shared architecture of the Armenian guides is itself the content: Chapter I is never a topic. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — the standard first — the guide's opening move; expected outcomes — abilities named before topics; cross-cutting concepts — the threads through every topic — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.
