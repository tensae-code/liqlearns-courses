# Teaching to the standard — expected outcomes and cross-cutting concepts, the Armenian guide way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Chapter I of the Armenian guides is always…
a) The first physics topic  b) Conceptual provisions — the standard, expected outcomes, cross-cutting concepts  c) A test paper  d) The answer key
**Answer: b) Conceptual provisions — the standard, expected outcomes, cross-cutting concepts**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Expected outcomes name…
a) The textbook's chapters  b) What the learner will be able to do by the grade's end  c) The teacher's holidays  d) Exam dates
**Answer: b) What the learner will be able to do by the grade's end**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Planning 'from outcomes backwards' means…
a) Teaching the last chapter first  b) Fixing the promised abilities first, then choosing lessons and topics that serve them  c) Skipping planning  d) Letting pupils choose everything
**Answer: b) Fixing the promised abilities first, then choosing lessons and topics that serve them**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A cross-cutting concept is…
a) A hard formula  b) A big idea — like energy or cause and effect — that runs through every topic  c) A chapter heading  d) A grading rule
**Answer: b) A big idea — like energy or cause and effect — that runs through every topic**

**5.** (Sub lesson 1) The guides repeat the standard chapter at every grade because…
a) Paper must be filled  b) Each grade's teaching must be re-anchored to the standard and its own outcomes  c) The authors forgot the earlier guides  d) It is the easiest chapter to write
**Answer: b) Each grade's teaching must be re-anchored to the standard and its own outcomes**

**6.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson stands on…
a) Student textbooks  b) Teacher-guide evidence only — the methodological guides' own teaching about teaching  c) A curriculum website  d) An exam board
**Answer: b) Teacher-guide evidence only — the methodological guides' own teaching about teaching**
