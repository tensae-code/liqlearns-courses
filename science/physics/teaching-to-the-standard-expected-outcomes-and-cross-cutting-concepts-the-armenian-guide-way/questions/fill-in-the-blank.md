# Teaching to the standard — expected outcomes and cross-cutting concepts, the Armenian guide way — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Every Armenian guide opens with the state ___ before any topic.
**Answer:** standard

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Expected outcomes are written as ___ the learner will have.
**Answer:** abilities

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Ideas like energy that run through all topics are called ___ concepts.
**Answer:** cross-cutting

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The guides' planning runs from outcomes ___ to lessons.
**Answer:** backwards
