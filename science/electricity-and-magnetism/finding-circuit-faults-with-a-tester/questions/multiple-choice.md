# Finding circuit faults with a tester — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A fault in a circuit is…
a) A break somewhere in the closed loop  b) Extra brightness  c) A bigger battery  d) A second switch
**Answer: a) A break somewhere in the closed loop**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The fault hunt starts by…
a) Replacing every part at once  b) Guessing which part could be broken, naming the loop's parts one by one  c) Shaking the torch  d) Adding more batteries
**Answer: b) Guessing which part could be broken, naming the loop's parts one by one**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these could be a fault in a torch?
a) A clean lens  b) A new bulb  c) A flat battery  d) A closed switch
**Answer: c) A flat battery**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A circuit tester is…
a) A kind of battery  b) A torch with no bulb  c) A switch  d) A simple working circuit with two loose ends, used to test parts
**Answer: d) A simple working circuit with two loose ends, used to test parts**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Touched across a sound part, the tester's bulb…
a) Lights, because its loop closes through the part  b) Stays dark  c) Blows at once  d) Changes colour
**Answer: a) Lights, because its loop closes through the part**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The tester finds the fault by…
a) Guessing louder  b) Testing parts one by one until it stays dark across the broken part  c) Lighting the room  d) Heating the wires
**Answer: b) Testing parts one by one until it stays dark across the broken part**
