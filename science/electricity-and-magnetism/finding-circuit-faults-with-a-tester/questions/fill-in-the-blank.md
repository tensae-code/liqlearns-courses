# Finding circuit faults with a tester — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** A _____ is a break somewhere in a circuit's closed loop.
**Answer: fault**

**2.** The tester is touched _____ the part being tested.
**Answer: across**

**3.** If the part is sound, the tester's _____ lights.
**Answer: bulb**

**4.** A torch may not light because its battery is _____.
**Answer: flat**
