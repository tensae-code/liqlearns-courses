Today: **Finding circuit faults with a tester** — China's way, from its 科学 (Science) textbooks.

A torch that will not light has a fault — a break somewhere in its closed loop. Guess the fault first: name the parts one by one — battery, bulb, wires, switch.
Then build a tester: a battery, a bulb and wires, with two loose ends. Touch it across each part. Where the tester's bulb lights, the part is sound. Where it stays dark — there is your fault.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
