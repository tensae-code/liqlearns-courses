# Hands-on — Finding circuit faults with a tester

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build a tester (with an adult)
With adult help, make a simple tester: a battery, a small bulb in a holder, and wires, with two wire ends left free. Check it works by touching the two ends together — the bulb must light.

## Clear the sound parts
Touch the tester across different sound parts — a good bulb, a piece of wire. Say each time what the tester shows, and what it proves about that part.

## Corner a fault
Ask an adult to hide one fault in a simple circuit — a loose wire is enough — then use the tester part by part until you find it. Write the order you tested in, and the part where the tester stayed dark.
