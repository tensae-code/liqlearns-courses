# Video script — Motors and generators — electricity to motion, motion to electricity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A full build-along / walkthrough script, as the placement names a video need for this lesson.

---

We build a motor. A coil of wire. Two paper clips hold it.
The magnet sits under the coil. Connect the battery.
Nothing yet. Give the coil a small nudge. Look — it spins! And it keeps spinning!
Why? The current makes the coil a magnet. The big magnet pushes and pulls it, round and round.
Wind more turns to make it stronger. That is how real motors work — in fridges, in trains.
Your turn. Build it. Nudge it. Watch it spin. Then explain it.
