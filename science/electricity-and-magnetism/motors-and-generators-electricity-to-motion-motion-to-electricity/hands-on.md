# Hands-on — Motors and generators — electricity to motion, motion to electricity

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Paper-clip motor (Activity 7.39)
Coil balanced on paper-clip supports over a magnet; battery fitted; nudge-start; each group must answer aloud: what turns it, where the energy comes from, how to speed it up — from their own spinning build.

## Cardboard generator (Activity 7.40)
Tube, nail, clay and disc magnets assembled as the book directs; output read on a compass needle at two spin speeds; needle behaviour recorded as the current's evidence.
