# Motors and generators — electricity to motion, motion to electricity — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A motor converts electrical energy into motion.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A generator and a motor run the same machine in opposite energy directions.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Alternating current always flows in one direction only.
**Answer: False**
