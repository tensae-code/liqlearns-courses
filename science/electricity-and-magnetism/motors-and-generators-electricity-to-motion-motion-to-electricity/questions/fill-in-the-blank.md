# Motors and generators — electricity to motion, motion to electricity — fill in the blank

Fill the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The motor's spinning part carries its motion out through the ______.
**Answer: shaft**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A generator's loop is turned by a ______.
**Answer: turbine**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Current that flows back and forth is ______ current.
**Answer: alternating (AC)**
