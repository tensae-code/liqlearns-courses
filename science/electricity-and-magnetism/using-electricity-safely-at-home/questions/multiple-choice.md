# Using electricity safely at home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The power that lights a lamp can also…
a) Sing  b) Hurt a person who meets it the wrong way  c) Turn into water  d) Make food
**Answer: b) Hurt a person who meets it the wrong way**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Djibouti's guide treats the dangers of electricity as…
a) Nothing worth teaching  b) A lesson of their own  c) A joke  d) Only for electricians
**Answer: b) A lesson of their own**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Protection from electricity's dangers is…
a) Luck  b) Careful knowledge and careful habits around wires, sockets and appliances  c) Never using light  d) Holding wires tightly
**Answer: b) Careful knowledge and careful habits around wires, sockets and appliances**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Where is this lesson meant to live?
a) Only in the exam  b) At home, where the electricity is  c) In another country  d) Nowhere
**Answer: b) At home, where the electricity is**
