# Using electricity safely at home — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does the guide name electricity's dangers in their own lesson?
**Answer: Because a pupil who learns electricity must know what its power can do to a body — the danger named plainly is what the protection stands on.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What does protecting yourself mean in this lesson?
**Answer: Carrying careful knowledge and careful habits around wires, sockets and appliances — treating electricity with the care its danger demands, every time.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is the lesson taught inside the electricity module, not somewhere else?
**Answer: Because safety is part of learning electricity itself: the guide places it right after the circuit and conductor lessons.**
