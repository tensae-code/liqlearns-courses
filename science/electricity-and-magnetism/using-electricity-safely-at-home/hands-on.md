# Hands-on — Using electricity safely at home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The home danger map
Draw a simple plan of one room at home. Mark every place electricity lives — lamp, socket, wire, appliance — with a star. Under the drawing, write for each star: the danger if treated carelessly, and the protection. Show the map to your family.

## Teach it at home
Choose the person at home who most needs this lesson — a younger brother or sister is perfect. Teach them the two halves in order: the dangers, then the protections. Afterwards, write what question they asked, and how you answered it from the lesson.
