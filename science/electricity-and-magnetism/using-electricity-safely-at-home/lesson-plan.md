# Lesson plan — Using electricity safely at home

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Electricity and Magnetism · **Lesson:** Using electricity safely at home
**Place in the curriculum:** Electricity and Magnetism — appended to Unit 23 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the dangers of electricity and how to protect yourself — Djibouti's own way, in its Grade 4 Sciences Expérimentales teacher guide, is to teach the danger and the protection as one lesson, right after the circuit and conductor lessons, so safety is part of learning electricity itself
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The dangers of electricity** — Electricity does real work in the home — and it can do real harm. Djibouti's Grade 4 guide names the dangers plainly as a lesson of their own: the same power that lights the lamp can hurt the person who meets it the wrong way.
2. **How to protect yourself** — The lesson's second half is the protection: the careful ways of being around electricity that keep the danger from becoming harm — taught by the guide as knowledge every pupil carries home.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: see the lesson's own opening. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain sub lesson 1 in their own words: The dangers of electricity.
2. Explain sub lesson 2 in their own words: How to protect yourself.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson text alone.
