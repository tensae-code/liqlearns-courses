# Electromagnets — a magnet you switch on — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A current-carrying wire has a magnetic field around it.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An electromagnet keeps its magnetism after the current is switched off.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Winding wire into a coil strengthens the field the current makes.
**Answer: True**
