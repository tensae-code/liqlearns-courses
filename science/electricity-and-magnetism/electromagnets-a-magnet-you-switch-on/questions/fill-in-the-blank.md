# Electromagnets — a magnet you switch on — fill in the blank

Fill the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A ______ creates a magnetic field around a wire.
**Answer: current**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A coil on an iron core, with current flowing, is an ______.
**Answer: electromagnet**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** When the current stops, the electromagnet's ______ stops with it.
**Answer: magnetism**
