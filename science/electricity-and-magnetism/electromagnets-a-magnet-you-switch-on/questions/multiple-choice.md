# Electromagnets — a magnet you switch on — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When current flows in wire loops around a compass, the needle —
a) never moves  b) swings — the current creates a magnetic field around the wire  c) melts
**Answer: b) swings — the current creates a magnetic field around the wire** — it settles on disconnect and reverses with the battery.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Reversing the battery makes the needle —
a) swing the other way  b) stop forever  c) spin continuously
**Answer: a) swing the other way**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The field around a wire is strengthened by —
a) less current  b) more current, and by winding the wire into a coil  c) painting the wire
**Answer: b) more current, and by winding the wire into a coil** — each loop adds its pull in the same direction.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An electromagnet is —
a) a permanent magnet painted red  b) a coil wrapped on a metal core such as an iron bar  c) a compass in a box
**Answer: b) a coil wrapped on a metal core such as an iron bar**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The iron core becomes a magnet —
a) forever, after one use  b) only while the current flows  c) before the current flows
**Answer: b) only while the current flows** — stop the current, stop the magnetism.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The electromagnet's great advantage is that it —
a) never needs a battery  b) can be switched on and off  c) is the strongest magnet possible
**Answer: b) can be switched on and off** — lift with it on, drop with it off.
