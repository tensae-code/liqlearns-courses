# Lesson plan — Electromagnets — a magnet you switch on

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Electricity and Magnetism · **Lesson:** Electromagnets — a magnet you switch on
**Place in the curriculum:** Electricity and Magnetism — Unit 7, Physical Phenomena, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a current creates a magnetic field around a wire — a compass needle swings while current flows, settles when disconnected, reverses with the battery — more current strengthens the field, winding the wire into a coil strengthens it further, and a coil on an iron core is an electromagnet: the core's particles line up, the core becomes a magnet, and only while the current flows
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Magnetic fields and on the circuit lessons together: current and field, each known alone; now current MAKES field, on demand.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the iron core is a magnet on its own → disconnect the current: the clips fall. The core is magnetic only while the coil's field lines its particles up.
- Expects a stronger battery to be the ONLY strengthener → coiling is the second: each loop adds its pull in the same direction.
