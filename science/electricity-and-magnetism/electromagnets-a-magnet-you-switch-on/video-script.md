# Video script — Electromagnets — a magnet you switch on

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script in the super-simple voice; the placement names no full video need for this lesson, so this is the standard short script.

---

Current flows in a wire. A compass needle swings!
No magnet nearby. The current made a field.
Wind the wire into a coil — stronger. Add an iron core — stronger still.
That is an electromagnet. Current on: magnet. Current off: nothing.
A magnet you can switch. That is the invention.
