Today: **Making a magnet by stroking** — Papua New Guinea's way, from its Department of Education science books (see lesson/sources.md for the exact books and pages).

Papua New Guinea's Grade 3 textbook teaches the surprising step after magnets attracting objects: an object that a magnet attracts — an iron or steel object, such as a nail or a needle — can itself **become a magnet**. The magnet does not only pull it; it can pass magnetism into it.

The making is done by **stroking**: the magnet is drawn along the object again and again, in the same direction. Then comes the test — bring the stroked object near paper clips or pins. If it attracts them, the object is now a magnet itself, made by stroking.
