# Hands-on — Static electricity and lightning

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Test the rule
Charge two balloons by rubbing and hang them side by side on strings: watch them repel. Then bring a differently charged object near one: watch it attract. Record both, labelled with the rule.

## Two sizes of discharge
Make a two-panel drawing: a small spark from a rubbed object, and lightning over a landscape. Under each, write the same three words in order — build-up, charge held, discharge.
