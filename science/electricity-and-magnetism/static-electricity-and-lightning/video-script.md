Today: **Static electricity and lightning**.

Rub two things together and charge builds up — and stays. That is static electricity. Like charges push apart, unlike charges pull together — and when the build-up in a storm cloud lets go all at once, that giant discharge is lightning. Pakistan (Punjab)'s Grade 5 book teaches the spark and the storm as one lesson.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
