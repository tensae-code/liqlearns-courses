# Static electricity and lightning — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does 'static' mean in static electricity?
**Answer: The charge stays where it was built up, instead of flowing as a current — it is held on the rubbed object until it discharges or is attracted away.**

**2.** State the charge rule and give the sky's example of it.
**Answer: Like charges repel and unlike charges attract; in the sky, charge built up in a storm is released as lightning, a giant discharge.**
