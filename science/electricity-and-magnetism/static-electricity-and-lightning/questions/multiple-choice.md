# Static electricity and lightning — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How does static charge build up?
a) By rubbing two things together  b) By heating water  c) By switching on a bulb  d) By magnetism  
**Answer: a) By rubbing two things together**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Two objects with like charges will…
a) Attract each other  b) Melt  c) Repel each other  d) Turn into magnets  
**Answer: c) Repel each other**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Lightning is…
a) A kind of rain  b) A giant discharge of built-up static charge  c) A circuit breaker working  d) Sound only  
**Answer: b) A giant discharge of built-up static charge**
