# Hands-on — Electricity around us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The electricity walk
With an adult, walk from room to room and count the things that use electricity. Keep the count room by room, then say the total out loud — that total is how much electricity surrounds one home.

## Jobs sort
Take your list and sort the devices by the job electricity does in them: light, heat, movement, sound, messages. Say which job is biggest in your home.
