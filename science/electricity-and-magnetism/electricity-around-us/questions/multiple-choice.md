# Electricity around us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which sentence gives this lesson's first key idea?
a) Electricity around us — lamps, screens, refrigerators, phones and machines all run on it, all day.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Electricity around us — lamps, screens, refrigerators, phones and machines all run on it, all day.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson opens by asking the learner to…
a) Memorise the key ideas first  b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation  c) Skip to the questions  d) Copy the lesson out
**Answer: b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's second key idea?
a) Electricity does many jobs: light, movement, heat, sound and messages.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Electricity does many jobs: light, movement, heat, sound and messages.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's third key idea?
