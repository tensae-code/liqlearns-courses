# Electricity around us — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Electricity around us — lamps, screens, refrigerators, phones and machines all run on it, all day.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Electricity does many jobs: light, movement, heat, sound and messages.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name three jobs electricity is doing in your home right now.
**Answer:** Any three real devices at work — for example lighting a room, keeping food cold, and charging a phone — each one electricity at work around us.
