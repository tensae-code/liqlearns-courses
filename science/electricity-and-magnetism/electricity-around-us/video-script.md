# Video script — Electricity around us

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Electricity around us** — Albania's way, opening electricity with the pupil's own surroundings.
Look around: lamps, television, refrigerator, phone — electricity is at work everywhere, all day.
Name its jobs — light, heat, movement, sound, messages — and you have met electricity before ever building a circuit.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
