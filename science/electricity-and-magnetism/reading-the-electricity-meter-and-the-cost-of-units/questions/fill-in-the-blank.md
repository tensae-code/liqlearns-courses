# Reading the electricity meter and the cost of units — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Complete from the lesson: the answer to "Units used are found by…" is ______.
**Answer: subtracting the earlier meter reading from the later one**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Complete from the lesson: the answer to "Electricity is paid for by the…" is ______.
**Answer: unit**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Complete from the lesson: the answer to "The month's cost is…" is ______.
