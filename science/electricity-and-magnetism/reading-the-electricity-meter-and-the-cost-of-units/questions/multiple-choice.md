# Reading the electricity meter and the cost of units — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Units used are found by…
a) subtracting the earlier meter reading from the later one  b) reading the meter once  c) counting the rooms  d) asking a neighbour
**Answer: a) subtracting the earlier meter reading from the later one**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Electricity is paid for by the…
a) unit  b) watt of the house  c) colour of the meter  d) day of the week
**Answer: a) unit**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The month's cost is…
a) units used × cost per unit  b) units plus the meter number  c) watts × rooms  d) the first reading only
**Answer: a) units used × cost per unit**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An appliance's power is measured in…
a) watts  b) units of rain  c) grams  d) metres
**Answer: a) watts**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A high-watt appliance uses units…
a) fast  b) never  c) only at night  d) backwards
**Answer: a) fast**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The meter is…
a) read, never touched, for its numbers  b) opened by children  c) a toy  d) a battery
**Answer: a) read, never touched, for its numbers**
