# Reading the electricity meter and the cost of units — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: reading the meter and counting units.
**Answer:** Seychelles' Unit 11 Topic 5, "The cost of energy", teaches the electricity meter as a measuring instrument: read the meter, read it again a month later, and the difference is the units of electrical energy the house has used.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How are the units used in a month found, and in what is an appliance's power measured?
**Answer:** By subtracting the earlier meter reading from the later one; power is measured in watts.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Reading the meter and counting units → The cost of a unit — and an appliance's power in watts — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
