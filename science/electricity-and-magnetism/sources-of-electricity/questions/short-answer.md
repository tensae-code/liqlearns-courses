# Sources of electricity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the two families of electricity sources and give one example of each.
**Answer: Sources that store electricity — a cell or battery; and sources that make it as it is needed — the mains supply from power stations, a generator, or a solar cell.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does a torch die on its own while the room lamps still work?
**Answer: The torch's stored source — its cell — has run out; the lamps are fed by the mains supply, which is still arriving. A store runs out by itself; a supply stops only when the supply is cut.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How does Bhutan make most of its mains electricity?
**Answer: From its rivers — falling water turns the machines (generators) in its power stations, as the placement's own country note records in the lesson.**
