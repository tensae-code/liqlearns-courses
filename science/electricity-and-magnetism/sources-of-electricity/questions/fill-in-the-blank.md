# Sources of electricity — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A _____ stores electricity and gives it out when it is connected into a circuit.
**Answer: cell**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Two or more cells joined together make a _____.
**Answer: battery**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A _____ makes electricity by turning.
**Answer: generator**
