# Sources of electricity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A dry cell gives a torch electricity by…
a) Making it in a power station  b) Storing it inside and giving it out when connected  c) Catching it from the air  d) Burning wood
**Answer: b) Storing it inside and giving it out when connected**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Joined cells working together are called a…
a) Circuit  b) Battery  c) Generator  d) Socket
**Answer: b) Battery**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The mains supply is electricity that…
a) Is stored in every wall  b) Is made in power stations and carried by wires to sockets  c) Comes only from the sun  d) Never stops for any reason
**Answer: b) Is made in power stations and carried by wires to sockets**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A generator makes electricity by…
a) Storing chemicals  b) Being kept in the dark  c) Turning — a machine is spun and electricity comes out  d) Waiting
**Answer: c) Turning — a machine is spun and electricity comes out**
