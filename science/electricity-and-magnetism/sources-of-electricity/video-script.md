Today: **Sources of electricity** — Bhutan's way: before the circuit, the source.

Every electrical thing begins somewhere. A torch begins in a cell — electricity stored inside, ready to be carried, ready also to run out. A lamp in a house begins far away, in a power station, and arrives by wire as the mains supply. A generator makes electricity by turning; a solar cell makes it from light. Two families: sources that store, and sources that make. Bhutan asks its youngest pupils to name the source first — then the circuit makes sense.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
