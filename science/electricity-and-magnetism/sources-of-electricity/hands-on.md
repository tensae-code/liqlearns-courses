# Hands-on — Sources of electricity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The source hunt
Walk through your home or classroom with a notebook. For every thing that works by electricity, find where its electricity comes from and write the source's name: cell, battery, mains, generator or sunlight. Count how many things each source feeds, and say which source your home leans on most.

## Two failures
With an adult's help, compare two failures safely: a torch whose cell is spent, and what happens to mains things when the supply goes off (wait for a real cut, or remember the last one — do not touch switches or sockets for this). Write what stopped, what kept working, and what each failure teaches about the source behind it.
