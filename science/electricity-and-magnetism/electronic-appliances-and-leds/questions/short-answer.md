# Electronic appliances and LEDs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Electronic appliances in daily life.
**Answer:** The appliances around us that run on electricity increasingly work through **electronic components** — small parts that control the current rather than only carrying it. Sri Lanka's Grade 6 book meets them where the learner lives: among the **electronic appliances used in daily life**, named as part of the electricity chapter's close.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Electronic components — the LED.
**Answer:** One component the book names is the **LED** — a small part that gives out light when current passes through it. LEDs appear inside appliances and lamps as indicators and as the light source itself: a component, not a whole appliance — the book's own distinction, and the one this lesson keeps.

**Answer:** Electronic appliances in daily life → Electronic components — the LED — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
