Today: **Electronic appliances and LEDs** — Sri Lanka's way, from the books cited in the sources.

The appliances around us that run on electricity increasingly work through electronic components — small parts that control the current rather than only carrying it.

One component the book names is the LED — a small part that gives out light when current passes through it.
