# Magnetic fields — field lines, Earth's field and the compass — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Field lines can cross each other where fields are strong.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Belts around Earth trap charged particles from the Sun.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Some trapped particles escape down field lines and make light shows near the poles.
**Answer: True**
