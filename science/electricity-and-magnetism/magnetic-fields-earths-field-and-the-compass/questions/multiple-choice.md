# Magnetic fields — field lines, Earth's field and the compass — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A magnetic field is —
a) the magnet's paint  b) the space around a magnet where its force acts  c) a kind of wire
**Answer: b) the space around a magnet where its force acts** — a non-contact field force.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Field lines outside a magnet run —
a) south to north  b) north to south  c) in straight lines only
**Answer: b) north to south** — closed loops that never cross.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Filings crowd thickest where —
a) the field is weakest  b) the field is strong — near the poles  c) the paper is thick
**Answer: b) the field is strong — near the poles** — lines thin with distance.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Earth's magnetism is probably driven by —
a) the Moon  b) the rotating liquid iron of Earth's core  c) ocean currents
**Answer: b) the rotating liquid iron of Earth's core** — pictured as a bar magnet through the core.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The book pictures Earth's internal magnet with its —
a) north pole under geographic north  b) south pole under geographic north  c) poles at the equator
**Answer: b) south pole under geographic north**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A home-made compass needle is —
a) hung from a string only  b) a magnetised needle floated on foam in water, lining up with Earth's field  c) a plain needle that points anywhere
**Answer: b) a magnetised needle floated on foam in water, lining up with Earth's field**
