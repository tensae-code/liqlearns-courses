# Video script — Magnetic fields — field lines, Earth's field and the compass

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A full build-along / walkthrough script, as the placement names a video need for this lesson.

---

We make a compass. A needle, a magnet, foam, and water.
Stroke the needle with the magnet. Same direction, many times. Now the needle is a magnet.
Lay the needle on a small piece of foam. Float the foam on water.
Watch. The needle turns — slowly — and stops. It points north and south.
Earth is a big magnet. Your little needle lines up with Earth's field.
Bring a bar magnet near. The needle swings to it. The nearer field wins.
Your turn. Make it float. Make it point.
