# Lesson plan — Magnetic fields — field lines, Earth's field and the compass

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Electricity and Magnetism · **Lesson:** Magnetic fields — field lines, Earth's field and the compass
**Place in the curriculum:** Electricity and Magnetism — Unit 7, Physical Phenomena, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the field — the space around a magnet where its force acts — is made visible by iron filings over one magnet, then two (unlike facing, like facing); field lines codify the pictures (closed loops, never crossing, north to south outside, crowded where strong); Earth itself is a magnet with particle-trapping belts and polar light shows, and the compass — a magnetised needle floated on foam — is built and tested
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Magnets — poles attract and repel: the force characterised; now the space it fills, mapped, and the planet itself read as a magnet.

## If the learner is stuck
- Draws field lines crossing → field lines NEVER cross; at any point the field has one direction.
- Places Earth's magnetic north pole under geographic north by name-matching → the book pictures a bar with its SOUTH pole under geographic north — which is why a compass needle's north is drawn there.
