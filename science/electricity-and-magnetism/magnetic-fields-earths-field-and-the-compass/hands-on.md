# Hands-on — Magnetic fields — field lines, Earth's field and the compass

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Make a compass (Activity 7.37)
Needle magnetised by stroking, floated on foam in a bowl of water; settling direction marked, bowl rotated and re-checked; then tested against a bar magnet brought near, and the astronaut question argued from the results.

## Filings patterns (Activity 7.36)
Three arrangements sprinkled and tapped in turn (adult handles filings; no blowing). Each pattern drawn before the next is made, field lines overlaid on the single-magnet drawing.
