# Hands-on — Drawing circuits — with symbols

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Real beside symbol
With an adult, lay out a real battery, bulb and switch (not connected to any mains electricity — a battery circuit only). Beside each real part, place a card with its symbol. Mix the cards, then match them back.

## Draw and build
Draw a battery circuit in symbols, closed loop and all. Hand the drawing to a companion: they must say what each part is and where the loop closes, from the drawing alone.
