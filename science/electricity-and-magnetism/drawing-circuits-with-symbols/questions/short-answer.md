# Drawing circuits — with symbols — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** A circuit symbol is an agreed picture-sign standing for one real circuit part.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Battery, bulb, switch and wires each have their symbol — the same sign for everyone, everywhere.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Your drawing's loop has a gap. What does the paper tell you?
**Answer:** That the circuit would not work — a circuit needs a closed loop, and the drawing must close it too.
