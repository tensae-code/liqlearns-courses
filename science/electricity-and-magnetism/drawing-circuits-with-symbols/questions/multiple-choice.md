# Drawing circuits — with symbols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which sentence gives this lesson's first key idea?
a) A circuit symbol is an agreed picture-sign standing for one real circuit part.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) A circuit symbol is an agreed picture-sign standing for one real circuit part.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson opens by asking the learner to…
a) Memorise the key ideas first  b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation  c) Skip to the questions  d) Copy the lesson out
**Answer: b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's second key idea?
a) Battery, bulb, switch and wires each have their symbol — the same sign for everyone, everywhere.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Battery, bulb, switch and wires each have their symbol — the same sign for everyone, everywhere.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's third key idea?
a) Something from a different lesson  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Draw the symbols in the real circuit's order, in a closed loop, and read the drawing back part by part.  d) None of these
**Answer: c) Draw the symbols in the real circuit's order, in a closed loop, and read the drawing back part by part.**
