# Hands-on — Using household electrical appliances safely

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Iron study — cold and unplugged
With an adult, and with the iron cold and unplugged, name its parts in turn — soleplate, body, handle, cord — and say each part's care aloud. Nothing is switched on.

## Appliance audit
Walk the home and list every electrical appliance. For each, write one line: its job, and the one care its using demands most. Read the list aloud to an adult and correct it together.
