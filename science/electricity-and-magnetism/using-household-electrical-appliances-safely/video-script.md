# Video script — Using household electrical appliances safely


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


Today: **Using household electrical appliances safely** — Mauritania's way: the home's machines, used with knowledge.

The appliances of the home run on mains electricity, and using them is a skill with an order: know the appliance, connect it rightly, work it for its job, disconnect it when done.

The Grade 4 book studies one appliance part by part — the electric iron: hot soleplate, handle, cord — because safe handling is simply the parts, known, each with its care.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
