# Using household electrical appliances safely — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State the using order the lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Know the appliance, connect it rightly, work it for its job, and finish properly — disconnected and put away.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the iron's studied parts and one care for each.
**Answer:** Soleplate — hot: never touched, never left standing on cloth; handle and body — the hand's safe hold; cord — kept sound and dry, and disconnected when the work is done.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Why are these lessons in the experimentation field?
**Answer:** Because they are practical skills learned by doing: using the home's appliances correctly and studying one appliance part by part, in the home where they live.
