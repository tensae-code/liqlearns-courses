# Using household electrical appliances safely — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Using an appliance ends by _____ it when its work is done.
**Answer: disconnecting**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The hot part of the iron that presses cloth is the _____.
**Answer: soleplate**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Safe handling is the _____, known — each part with its care.
**Answer: parts**
