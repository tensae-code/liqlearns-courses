# Video script — Series and parallel circuits — one path, many paths

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A full build-along / walkthrough script, as the placement names a video need for this lesson.

---

Same battery. Same three bulbs. Two ways to wire them.
First way: one loop. Bulb, bulb, bulb, all in a line. This is series.
Watch the brightness. Now I take one bulb out. All dark! One break stops everything.
Second way: three branches. Each bulb has its own road. This is parallel.
Watch. I take one bulb out. The others still shine!
That is why houses are wired in parallel.
Your turn. Build both. Measure both. Write what you see.
