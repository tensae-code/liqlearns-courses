# Hands-on — Series and parallel circuits — one path, many paths

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Paired build-and-measure (Activities 7.27–7.28)
Both wirings built from the same kit; brightness graded and ammeter current recorded for one, two and three bulbs; batteries and bulbs then varied together. The paired table is the lesson's product.

## The removal demonstration
With both circuits lit side by side, one bulb is removed from each in turn, the class calling the result before each removal. Any wrong call is re-argued from the road pictures, not from authority.
