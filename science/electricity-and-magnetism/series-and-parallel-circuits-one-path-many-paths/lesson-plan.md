# Lesson plan — Series and parallel circuits — one path, many paths

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Electricity and Magnetism · **Lesson:** Series and parallel circuits — one path, many paths
**Place in the curriculum:** Electricity and Magnetism — Unit 7, Physical Phenomena, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a series circuit is a one-way circular road — every part on a single loop, break any part and everything stops — while a parallel circuit is many roads to one place: branches divide the current, each carries its share at the same time, and a bulb out on one branch leaves the others glowing, which is how home lighting must be wired; both forms are built and measured, brightness and ammeter current compared
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Circuits — source, load, wires, and open vs closed: one loop mastered; now the loop split two ways and the split measured.

## If the learner is stuck
- Expects a dead parallel branch to kill the rest, like series → each branch is its own road to the source; only the broken branch stops.
- Reads ‘current divides’ as current being used up → the branches carry their shares at the same time; the division is of the flow, not a loss of it.
