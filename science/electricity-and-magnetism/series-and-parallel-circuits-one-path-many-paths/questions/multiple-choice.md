# Series and parallel circuits — one path, many paths — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a series circuit, the parts sit —
a) on separate branches  b) on a single loop, current passing through each in turn  c) nowhere in particular
**Answer: b) on a single loop, current passing through each in turn** — the one-way circular road.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Removing one bulb from a series circuit —
a) dims the rest  b) stops everything  c) does nothing
**Answer: b) stops everything**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** As more bulbs are added in series, they generally —
a) brighten  b) dim  c) explode
**Answer: b) dim** — the chain lengthens; the sharing shows.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In a parallel circuit, the current —
a) passes through one branch only  b) divides between the branches, each carrying its share at the same time  c) stops at the junction
**Answer: b) divides between the branches, each carrying its share at the same time** — many roads to one place.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A bulb burning out on one parallel branch —
a) kills all branches  b) leaves the others glowing  c) doubles their current forever
**Answer: b) leaves the others glowing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Home lighting must be wired in parallel because —
a) series is prettier  b) one dead bulb must not darken the whole house  c) parallel uses no wire
**Answer: b) one dead bulb must not darken the whole house** — deduced from the removal test.
