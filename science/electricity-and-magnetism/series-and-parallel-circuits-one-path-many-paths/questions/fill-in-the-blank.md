# Series and parallel circuits — one path, many paths — fill in the blank

Fill the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A circuit with every part on one loop is a ______ circuit.
**Answer: series**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A circuit whose current divides into branches is a ______ circuit.
**Answer: parallel**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Current in the builds is read on an ______.
**Answer: ammeter**
