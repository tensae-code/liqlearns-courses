# Hands-on — Fuses and circuit breakers — keeping circuits safe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Guard the circuit (drawing test)
Draw the circuit from the Do It Yourself block twice: once with the fuse before the bulb, once after it. For each drawing, trace the current's path and say whether the fuse can still break the whole circuit.

## Teach the guard
Teach a younger learner the fuse story in five sentences: what it guards, how it works, what its rating means, and what the circuit breaker does instead.
