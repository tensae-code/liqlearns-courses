Today: **Fuses and circuit breakers — keeping circuits safe**.

A circuit needs a guard. Pakistan (Punjab)'s Grade 5 book teaches the fuse — a wire that melts when the current grows too large, breaking the circuit before harm is done — and its rating, and then the circuit breaker doing the same job as a switch that can be reset. Two guards, one job: open the circuit and stop the dangerous current.
