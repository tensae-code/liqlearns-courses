# Fuses and circuit breakers — keeping circuits safe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why does a circuit need a fuse or a breaker at all?
**Answer: Because too much current can harm the wires and appliances and the people near them; the guard breaks the circuit first, stopping the dangerous current.**

**2.** Fuse or breaker — what is the same, and what is different?
**Answer: The same job: opening the circuit when current is too large. Different working: the fuse melts and must be replaced; the breaker switches open and can be reset.**
