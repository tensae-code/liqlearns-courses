# Fuses and circuit breakers — keeping circuits safe — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** A ______ melts to break a circuit when the current is too large.
**Answer: fuse**

**2.** Fuses carry ______, which must fit the circuit they protect.
**Answer: ratings**

**3.** A circuit ______ does the fuse's job as a resettable switch.
**Answer: breaker**
