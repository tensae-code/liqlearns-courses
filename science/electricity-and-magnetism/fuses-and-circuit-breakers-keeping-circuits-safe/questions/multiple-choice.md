# Fuses and circuit breakers — keeping circuits safe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does a fuse do when the current grows too large?
a) It makes the bulb brighter  b) Its wire melts and breaks the circuit  c) It stores the extra current  d) Nothing  
**Answer: b) Its wire melts and breaks the circuit**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What is a fuse's rating?
a) The current it allows before it melts  b) Its colour  c) Its price  d) The size of its box  
**Answer: a) The current it allows before it melts**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How does a circuit breaker differ from a fuse?
a) It never breaks the circuit  b) It makes current larger  c) It does the same job as a switch that can be reset, instead of melting  d) It replaces the battery  
**Answer: c) It does the same job as a switch that can be reset, instead of melting**
