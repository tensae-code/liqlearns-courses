# Hands-on — Circuits — source, load, wires, and open vs closed

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Light the bulb (Activity 7.24)
Open tinkering exactly as the Do-it-first: every trial drawn and recorded, working arrangements compared across the class, the count of different working arrangements agreed as a class.

## Safe handling and symbol drawing (teacher evidence)
Before the build counts as done: the class states the safe-handling rules for electrical appliances aloud, and each pupil files the same circuit drawn as objects and as symbols, switch shown open in one copy and closed in the other.
