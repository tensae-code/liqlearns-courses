# Circuits — source, load, wires, and open vs closed — fill in the blank

Fill the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Charge flowing continuously is ______ electricity.
**Answer: current**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The part of a circuit that uses the energy (the bulb) is the ______.
**Answer: load**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** One ampere is one ______ of charge per second.
**Answer: coulomb**
