# Circuits — source, load, wires, and open vs closed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Current electricity differs from static charge because it —
a) builds up and discharges once  b) flows continuously, like a river  c) only exists in lightning
**Answer: b) flows continuously, like a river**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The three basic parts of a circuit are —
a) bulb, glass, wood  b) power source, load, connectors (wires)  c) switch, magnet, string
**Answer: b) power source, load, connectors (wires)** — often plus a switch.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In Activity 7.24, every working bulb arrangement shares —
a) exactly two wires  b) a complete, unbroken loop  c) the same battery position
**Answer: b) a complete, unbroken loop**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A burnt-out bulb stops its circuit because —
a) it uses up the current  b) its snapped filament makes the circuit open  c) it gets too hot
**Answer: b) its snapped filament makes the circuit open** — any break makes an open circuit.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Current's rate is measured in —
a) volts  b) amperes — one coulomb of charge per second  c) ohms
**Answer: b) amperes — one coulomb of charge per second**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's parts list for a circuit is — (teacher evidence)
a) cell or battery, bulb, switch and conductor  b) generator, motor, turbine and shaft  c) coil, core, needle and foam
**Answer: a) cell or battery, bulb, switch and conductor**
