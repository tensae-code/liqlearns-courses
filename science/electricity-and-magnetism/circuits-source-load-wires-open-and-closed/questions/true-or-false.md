# Circuits — source, load, wires, and open vs closed — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A single wire from a battery to a bulb is a complete circuit.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An unbroken circuit is called a closed circuit.
**Answer: True**
