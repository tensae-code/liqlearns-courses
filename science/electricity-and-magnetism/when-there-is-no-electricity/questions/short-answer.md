# When there is no electricity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** When the electricity goes, its jobs stop together: light, machines, cold food, charging.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Life goes on the older way: candles for light, fire for cooking and warmth, cool places for food, work by daylight.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The refrigerator stops in a long cut. What is the older way to keep food?
**Answer:** Keep it in the coolest place and use it sooner — cold-keeping without a machine, as homes did before electricity.
