# When there is no electricity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which sentence gives this lesson's first key idea?
a) When the electricity goes, its jobs stop together: light, machines, cold food, charging.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) When the electricity goes, its jobs stop together: light, machines, cold food, charging.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson opens by asking the learner to…
a) Memorise the key ideas first  b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation  c) Skip to the questions  d) Copy the lesson out
**Answer: b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's second key idea?
a) Life goes on the older way: candles for light, fire for cooking and warmth, cool places for food, work by daylight.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Life goes on the older way: candles for light, fire for cooking and warmth, cool places for food, work by daylight.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's third key idea?
a) Something from a different lesson  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Imagining a whole evening without electricity shows exactly what electricity does for us.  d) None of these
**Answer: c) Imagining a whole evening without electricity shows exactly what electricity does for us.**
