# When there is no electricity — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Key ideas) Life goes on the older way: _____ for light, fire for cooking and warmth, cool places for food, work by daylight.
**Answer: candles**

**2.** (Key ideas) Life goes on the older way: candles for light, _____ for cooking and warmth, cool places for food, work by daylight.
**Answer: fire**
