# Hands-on — When there is no electricity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One hour dark
With your family, spend one hour with the lights and screens off (lamps you control only — never touch switches or wires beyond ordinary room switches, and keep an adult with you). Use candles or a torch as your family decides, and afterwards say what was hardest and what was fine.

## Job by job
Take the jobs list — light, cooking, keeping food, messages, washing — and for each one, act or mime the no-electricity way while your companion names the electric way it replaces.
