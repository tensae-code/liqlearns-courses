# Video script — When there is no electricity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **When there is no electricity** — Albania's way, asking the shocked question: what? There is no electricity?
Everything it was doing stops: lights, screens, machines, the cold box, the charging phone.
But life goes on the older way — candles, fire, cool places, daylight work. That is the lesson: find the old way for every job.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
