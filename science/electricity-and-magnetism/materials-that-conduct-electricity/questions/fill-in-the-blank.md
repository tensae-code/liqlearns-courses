# Materials that conduct electricity — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Metals are _____ of electricity.
**Answer: conductors**

**2.** Rubber and plastic are _____.
**Answer: insulators**

**3.** Wires are usually made of _____.
**Answer: copper**

**4.** In the test, a lit _____ means the material conducts.
**Answer: lamp**
