# Materials that conduct electricity — multiple choice


**1.** A conductor is a material that…
a) Stops electricity  b) Lets electricity flow through it  c) Makes light  d) Stores water
**Answer: b) Lets electricity flow through it**

**2.** Wires are made of copper because copper…
a) Is an insulator  b) Conducts electricity  c) Is cheap wood  d) Melts easily
**Answer: b) Conducts electricity**

**3.** Which is an insulator?
a) Iron nail  b) Copper wire  c) Rubber  d) Steel spoon
**Answer: c) Rubber**

**4.** The plastic coat on a wire is there to…
a) Make it pretty  b) Insulate — keep the current in and our hands safe  c) Make it heavier  d) Help it conduct
**Answer: b) Insulate — keep the current in and our hands safe**

**5.** In the gap test, the lamp lights when the material…
a) Is an insulator  b) Conducts  c) Is wet wood only  d) Is plastic
**Answer: b) Conducts**

**6.** Electricity is most dangerous with…
a) Dry hands  b) Wet hands and water nearby  c) Rubber shoes  d) Wooden floors
**Answer: b) Wet hands and water nearby**
