# Materials that conduct electricity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** How can you test whether a material conducts electricity?
**Answer: Put it across a gap in a battery circuit: if the lamp lights, the material conducts.**

**2.** Name two conductors and two insulators.
**Answer: Conductors: copper and iron. Insulators: rubber and plastic (wood and glass too).**

**3.** Why does every wire need both a conductor and an insulator?
**Answer: The metal core carries the current; the insulating coat keeps the current in the wire and protects our hands.**

**4.** Why is water a danger near electricity?
**Answer: Water with salts conducts electricity, so wet hands can carry the current into the body.**
