# Hands-on — Magnets — poles attract and repel; what is magnetic

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Prediction-first pole play (Activity 7.32)
All pairings predicted in writing before touching, then tested and repeated; strongest parts hunted with paper clips on bar, horseshoe and disc magnets.

## Sort and barrier test (Activities 7.34–7.35)
The tray sorted by the magnet alone (names hidden where possible); then clips tested through paper, thin/thick wood and foil, results tabled with a weakening note.
