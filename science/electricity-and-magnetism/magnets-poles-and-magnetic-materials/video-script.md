# Video script — Magnets — poles attract and repel; what is magnetic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script in the super-simple voice; the placement names no full video need for this lesson, so this is the standard short script.

---

Bring the magnets together. Sometimes they jump and lock.
Sometimes they push away. You cannot force them!
Unlike poles attract. Like poles repel.
Cut a magnet? Two small magnets! Each has two poles.
Iron, nickel, cobalt — magnetic. Aluminium? A metal, but NOT magnetic.
