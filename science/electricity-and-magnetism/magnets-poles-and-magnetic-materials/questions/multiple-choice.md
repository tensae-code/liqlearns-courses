# Magnets — poles attract and repel; what is magnetic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A magnet's force is strongest —
a) in its middle  b) at its two poles  c) at one end only
**Answer: b) at its two poles**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Like poles brought together —
a) attract  b) repel  c) do nothing
**Answer: b) repel** — unlike poles attract.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Cutting a bar magnet in two gives —
a) a lone north piece and a lone south piece  b) two whole magnets, each with fresh poles at the cut  c) two non-magnets
**Answer: b) two whole magnets, each with fresh poles at the cut** — a pole can never be isolated.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The magnetic elements are —
a) aluminium, copper, lead  b) iron, nickel and cobalt  c) tin, zinc and brass
**Answer: b) iron, nickel and cobalt** — plus alloys containing them.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Steel is magnetic because —
a) all metals are  b) it contains iron  c) it is shiny
**Answer: b) it contains iron**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A magnet's force through paper or thin wood —
a) is completely stopped  b) acts through the substance in its way  c) turns into light
**Answer: b) acts through the substance in its way** — weakened, but through.
