# Magnets — poles attract and repel; what is magnetic — fill in the blank

Fill the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Unlike poles ______; like poles repel.
**Answer: attract**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The three magnetic elements are iron, nickel and ______.
**Answer: cobalt**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** ‘Metal’ and ‘______’ are not the same list.
**Answer: magnetic**
