# Magnets — poles attract and repel; what is magnetic — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A magnet must touch iron to pull it.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Magnetic force weakens with distance.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Aluminium is a metal, so it must be magnetic.
**Answer: False**
