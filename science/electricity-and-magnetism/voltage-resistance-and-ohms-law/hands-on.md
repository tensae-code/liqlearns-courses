# Hands-on — Voltage, resistance and Ohm's law — V = IR

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Design the battery test (Activity 7.29)
Groups state their own hypothesis about batteries and current, design the test (including the parallel-batteries repeat), run it, and report whether the hypothesis survived.

## Graph and drill (Activities 7.30–7.31)
V–I graphs plotted from the printed radio/CD-player tables with slopes read as resistances; then three rearrangement calculations, one per form, checked by a partner substituting back into V = IR.
