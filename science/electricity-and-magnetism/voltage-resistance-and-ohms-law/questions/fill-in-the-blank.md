# Voltage, resistance and Ohm's law — V = IR — fill in the blank

Fill the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Ohm's law: V = I × ______.
**Answer: R**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A dry cell's voltage as printed is ______ volts.
**Answer: 1.5**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The unit of resistance is the ______.
**Answer: ohm**
