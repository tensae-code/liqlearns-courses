# Voltage, resistance and Ohm's law — V = IR — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Voltage is —
a) the opposition to flow  b) the strength of the power source — raising it pushes more current  c) the current itself
**Answer: b) the strength of the power source — raising it pushes more current** — dry cell 1.5 V; outlet 120 V as printed.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Resistance turns electrical energy into —
a) nothing  b) light and heat  c) cold
**Answer: b) light and heat** — a bulb's glow is resistance at work.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Of copper and rubber, the huge resistance belongs to —
a) copper  b) rubber  c) both equally
**Answer: b) rubber** — copper's is tiny — hence copper cores, rubber coats.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ohm's law states —
a) V = I / R  b) V = I × R  c) R = V × I
**Answer: b) V = I × R** — found by Ohm from experiment.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** To find the current from voltage and resistance: —
a) I = V × R  b) I = V / R  c) I = R / V
**Answer: b) I = V / R**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** On the V–I graph of Activity 7.30, the slope of each line is read as the device's —
a) voltage  b) resistance  c) weight
**Answer: b) resistance** — radio vs CD player, from the printed tables.
