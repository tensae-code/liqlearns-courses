## Sub lesson 1 — Modern technologies — the astronaut's tools

Iraq's Grade 6 book sets a reading on **modern technologies: the tools astronauts use**. Astronauts work where no unprotected person can work — in space — and their work is possible because of their tools: the reading studies the tools as the technology that carries the person.

The book's frame is its own order: the reading stands among the book's later chapters, where energy and the wider world beyond Earth are taught — technology is read as part of the science, the instruments of the space age named for the people who use them.

## The key ideas

- Astronauts work in space through their **tools** — the reading's subject is the tools themselves.
- The book files it as a reading on **modern technologies**.
- Technology is part of the science book: the instruments of the space age, named for their users.
