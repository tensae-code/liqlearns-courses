# Video script — Tools astronauts use

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Tools astronauts use** — a modern-technologies reading in Iraq's Grade 6 book.

Astronauts work where no unprotected person can work. Their tools carry them — and the tools are the reading's subject: the instruments of the space age, named for the people who use them. Technology, read as part of the science.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
