# Tools astronauts use — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The reading's subject is…
a) The astronauts' names  b) The tools astronauts use  c) The Moon's phases  d) Rocket prices
**Answer: b) The tools astronauts use**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The book files the reading under…
a) Ancient crafts  b) Modern technologies  c) Safety rules  d) Weather
**Answer: b) Modern technologies**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) An astronaut's tools…
a) Are decoration  b) Make work in space possible — they carry the person  c) Are not technology  d) Replace all science
**Answer: b) Make work in space possible — they carry the person**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The reading stands among the book's…
