# The aurora — how northern lights form — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner tells the aurora's chain in order — charged particles from the Sun, Earth's magnetic field funnelling them to the poles, collisions with upper-air gases, the gases glowing in their colours — and explains both why Finland sees auroras and why a stormy Sun brightens them, answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has three sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — tracing the field-line picture, matching colours to gases and heights, and reasoning from a solar storm to the sky two nights later until the chain is the learner's own.
