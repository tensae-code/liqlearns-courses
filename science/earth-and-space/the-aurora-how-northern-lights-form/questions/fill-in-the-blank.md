# The aurora — how northern lights form — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Sun's constant stream of charged particles is called the solar _____.
**Answer: wind**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Earth's magnetic _____ funnels aurora particles down at the poles.
**Answer: field**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Colliding particles hand energy to the air's gases, which shed it as _____.
**Answer: light**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The aurora's commonest colour, green, is the signature of _____.
**Answer: oxygen**
