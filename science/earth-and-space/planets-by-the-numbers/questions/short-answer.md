# Planets by the numbers — the data-table investigation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is Saturn the only planet that would float?
**Answer: its density, 687 kg/m³, is below water's 1,000 kg/m³ — it is a huge ball of light gas; every other planet is denser than water and would sink.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the distance-cubed/period-squared plot show?
**Answer: the points for all eight planets fall on one pattern — period-squared grows in step with distance-cubed, the relationship Kepler's third law states.**
