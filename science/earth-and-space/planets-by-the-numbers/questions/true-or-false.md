# Planets by the numbers — the data-table investigation — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every planet except Saturn would sink in water.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The giants spin more slowly than the terrestrial planets.
**Answer: False — the giants spin fastest; their days are the shortest.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Distance-cubed plotted against period-squared gives one straight pattern for all eight planets.
**Answer: True**
