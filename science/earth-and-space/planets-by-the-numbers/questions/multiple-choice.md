# Planets by the numbers — the data-table investigation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The only planet less dense than water is —
a) Jupiter  b) Saturn  c) Neptune  d) Earth
**Answer: b) Saturn.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Saturn's density, as printed, is about —
a) 687 kg/m³  b) 1,326 kg/m³  c) 5,514 kg/m³  d) 1,000 kg/m³
**Answer: a) 687 kg/m³.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The denser family is —
a) the giants  b) the terrestrial planets  c) equal  d) neither
**Answer: b) the terrestrial planets.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The planet that orbits the Sun fastest is —
a) Neptune  b) Earth  c) Mercury  d) Venus
**Answer: c) Mercury.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The planet whose year is shorter than its day is —
a) Mars  b) Mercury  c) Jupiter  d) Venus
**Answer: d) Venus.**
