# Hands-on — Planets by the numbers — the data-table investigation

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Interrogate the table
Write five questions the density/diameter/day/year table can answer, swap with a companion, and answer theirs from the table only.

## Float test
Drop a piece of wood (floats) and a stone (sinks) in water. Wood's density is below water's, stone's above — the same test Saturn passes and every other planet fails.
