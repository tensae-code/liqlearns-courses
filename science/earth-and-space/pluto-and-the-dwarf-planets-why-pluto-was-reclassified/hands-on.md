# Hands-on — Pluto and the dwarf planets — why Pluto was reclassified

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: science, technology and society: Pluto, from planet to dwarf planet — found in 1930 and counted the ninth planet, it was reclassified when the International Astronomical Union set the planet test (circles the sun; round by its own gravity; has cleared its orbit); Pluto fails the third, and with Eris, Makemake, Haumea and Ceres forms the dwarf planets Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
