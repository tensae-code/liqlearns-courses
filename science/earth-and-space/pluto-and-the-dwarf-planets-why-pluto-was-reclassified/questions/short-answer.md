# Pluto and the dwarf planets — why Pluto was reclassified — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Science, technology and society.
**Answer:** science, technology and society: Pluto, from planet to dwarf planet — found in 1930 and counted the ninth planet, it was reclassified when the International Astronomical Union set the planet test (circles the sun; round by its own gravity; has cleared its orbit); Pluto fails the third, and with Eris, Makemake, Haumea and Ceres forms the dwarf planets.

**2.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Science, technology and society — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
