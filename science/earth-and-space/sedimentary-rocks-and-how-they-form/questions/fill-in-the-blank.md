# Sedimentary rocks and how they form — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sedimentary rocks are laid down in horizontal layers called _____.
**Answer: strata**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A sedimentary rock is formed at the Earth's _____.
**Answer: surface**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The wearing away of older rock and land at the surface is called _____.
**Answer: erosion**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Material let fall and settling in water is called _____.
**Answer: sedimentation**
