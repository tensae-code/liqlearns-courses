# Sedimentary rocks and how they form — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give the lesson's definition of a sedimentary rock, naming both where it forms and how it lies.
**Answer:** A sedimentary rock is formed at the Earth's surface, in oceans, seas, lakes and rivers, and it is laid down in horizontal layers (strata).

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why are strata a sedimentary rock's birthmark?
**Answer:** Because the rock was made by material settling in water flat, layer on layer — the horizontal layers survive in the finished rock as the visible record of that making.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Put the formation cycle in order and say what happens at its two named ends.
**Answer:** Erosion first — older rock and land are worn away and the material is carried off — then sedimentation — the material is let fall in water and settles in layers, which burial hardens into rock.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A rock shows clear flat layers. What does the layering tell you about its past?
**Answer:** That it is sedimentary: its material settled in water — an ocean, sea, lake or river — stratum upon stratum, through the cycle from erosion to sedimentation.
