# Sedimentary rocks and how they form — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A sedimentary rock is formed…
a) Deep inside the Earth  b) At the Earth's surface  c) Only in volcanoes  d) Only in the air
**Answer: b) At the Earth's surface**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Sedimentary rocks form in…
a) Oceans, seas, lakes and rivers  b) Deserts only  c) The clouds  d) Mines only
**Answer: a) Oceans, seas, lakes and rivers**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The horizontal layers a sedimentary rock is laid down in are called…
a) Cores  b) Strata  c) Crusts  d) Veins
**Answer: b) Strata**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The formation cycle of sedimentary rocks runs from…
a) Sedimentation to erosion  b) Melting to cooling  c) Erosion to sedimentation  d) Freezing to thawing
**Answer: c) Erosion to sedimentation**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Sedimentation is…
a) Material worn away from older rock  b) Material let fall and settling in water, layer on layer  c) Rock remade by heat  d) Water evaporating
**Answer: b) Material let fall and settling in water, layer on layer**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Erosion is…
a) Older rock and land worn away at the surface  b) Layers hardening underground  c) A layer of rock  d) A kind of river
**Answer: a) Older rock and land worn away at the surface**
