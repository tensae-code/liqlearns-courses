Today: **Sedimentary rocks and how they form** — Haiti's way: a rock taught by the cycle that made it.

A sedimentary rock is made at the Earth's surface — not deep underground. Its material settles in oceans, seas, lakes and rivers, and it settles flat: layer on horizontal layer, and those layers are called strata.
The cycle starts with erosion: older rock and land are worn away. The worn material is carried, then let fall in the water — that letting-fall is sedimentation. Layer buries layer, and in time the buried layers become rock.
So when you see a rock in flat layers, read it backwards: strata on the outside, a whole cycle of wearing, carrying and settling inside.
