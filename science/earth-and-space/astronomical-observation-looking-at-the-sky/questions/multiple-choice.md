# Astronomical observation — looking at the sky — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An astronomical observation must always record…
a) How the observer felt  b) The direction, height in the sky, and the time  c) The names of all the stars  d) The temperature only
**Answer: b) The direction, height in the sky, and the time**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does the same patch of sky show more stars after ten minutes in the dark?
a) More stars switch on  b) The observer's eyes adapt to the dark  c) The sky rotates new stars in  d) Street lights dim themselves
**Answer: b) The observer's eyes adapt to the dark**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these must never be done?
a) Sketching the Moon  b) Looking at the Sun directly or through an instrument  c) Observing with binoculars  d) Keeping a log
**Answer: b) Looking at the Sun directly or through an instrument**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The first job of binoculars or a telescope is to…
a) Make everything bigger only  b) Gather more light than the pupil can, so fainter objects appear  c) Take photographs  d) Block the Moon's light
**Answer: b) Gather more light than the pupil can, so fainter objects appear**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A star chart is used to…
a) Tell the observer where to point, stepping from known bright stars to the target  b) Predict the weather  c) Measure the temperature of a star  d) Replace the observing log
**Answer: a) Tell the observer where to point, stepping from known bright stars to the target**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Repeating the same observation night after night reveals…
a) Nothing new  b) Changes — the Moon's face and place, planets wandering, the sky turning  c) That the log was wrong  d) Only the weather
**Answer: b) Changes — the Moon's face and place, planets wandering, the sky turning**
