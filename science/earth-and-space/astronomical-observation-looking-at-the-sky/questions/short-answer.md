# Astronomical observation — looking at the sky — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How can you tell a planet from a star with the unaided eye, as taught in this lesson?
**Answer: A planet shines steadily and does not twinkle like a star; it also wanders against the stars from night to night.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why can too much magnification make a telescope view worse instead of better?
**Answer: It magnifies the wobble of the air and the shake of the hand along with the object, so the image blurs and dances.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) List four things an observing log records.
**Answer: Any four of: date and time, place, weather and darkness, direction and height of the object, a sketch of what was seen, the instrument used.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why is a written log needed at all — why not just remember what you saw?
**Answer: Memory cannot be checked, compared or believed later; the written record lets observations on different nights be compared, which is how changes and motions are proved.**
