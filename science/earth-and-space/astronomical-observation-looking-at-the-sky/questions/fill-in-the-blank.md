# Astronomical observation — looking at the sky — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The unaided eye, fully adapted to the dark, is the observer's first _____.
**Answer: instrument**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Binoculars and telescopes gather more _____ than the pupil can.
**Answer: light**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A map of the sky for a date and time is called a star _____.
**Answer: chart**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The written record of an observation is kept in an observing _____.
**Answer: log**
