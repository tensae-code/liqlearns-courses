# Hands-on — Astronomical observation — looking at the sky

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The ten-minute dark eye
Choose a clear evening and a place as dark as you can safely reach with a teacher or family member. Stand still for ten full minutes without looking at any phone or light. At minute one and at minute ten, count the stars inside one hand-span patch of sky and write both counts in a log with the time, place and direction. The second count is larger because your eyes adapted — you have just measured your own first instrument improving. Finish the log with a sketch of the brightest pattern you saw.
