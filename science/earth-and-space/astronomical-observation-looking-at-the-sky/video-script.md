# Video script — Astronomical observation — looking at the sky

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **looking at the sky like an astronomer**. An observation is not a glance: you name the direction, the height and the time, and you write it down. Your eyes, given ten dark minutes, see thousands of stars; binoculars and a telescope gather still more light, so fainter things appear. And the log you keep tonight is what lets tomorrow night prove that the sky has moved.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
