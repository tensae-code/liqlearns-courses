# Seas and oceans — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The seas and oceans are…
a) The Earth's great salt waters  b) Fresh lakes only  c) Underground only  d) Made by people
**Answer: a) The Earth's great salt waters**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Iraq's book teaches them inside the unit on…
a) Magnets  b) The Earth and its resources  c) The human body  d) Sound
**Answer: b) The Earth and its resources**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The activity book's part is…
a) An activity on the same ground  b) Nothing  c) A test only  d) A song
**Answer: a) An activity on the same ground**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Seas and oceans are the greater part of the Earth's…
a) Land  b) Water environments  c) Air  d) Rocks
**Answer: b) Water environments**
