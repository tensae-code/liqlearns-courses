# Hands-on — Seas and oceans

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The Earth's waters
Draw the Earth and shade its waters. Label the seas and oceans as the great salt waters, and mark where fresh waters stand against them.

## Resources frame
Write the unit's frame: the Earth and its resources. Under it, place the seas and oceans, and one line on why they belong inside that frame.
