# Video script — Motions of the Earth, Moon and Sun

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **three motions at once**. The Earth spins once a day — that is day and night. It journeys around the Sun once a year on a tilted axis — that tilt, not distance, makes the seasons. The Moon circles us once a month, showing its phases and, at exact line-ups, eclipses. And the Sun's slow march against the stars is not its journey at all — it is ours.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
