# Motions of the Earth, Moon and Sun — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Earth's axis tilts about _____ degrees.
**Answer: 23.5**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) One full journey of the Earth around the Sun is one _____.
**Answer: year**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The Moon shines only by reflected _____.
**Answer: sunlight**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) At new Moon, the Moon's shadow crossing the Earth can give a _____ eclipse.
**Answer: solar**
