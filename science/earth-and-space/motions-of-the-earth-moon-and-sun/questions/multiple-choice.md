# Motions of the Earth, Moon and Sun — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Earth's revolution around the Sun takes about…
a) 24 hours  b) 27 days  c) 365¼ days — a year  d) 12 hours
**Answer: c) 365¼ days — a year**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The seasons are caused mainly by…
a) The Earth moving closer to the Sun in summer  b) The 23.5° tilt of the Earth's axis, held pointing the same way all year  c) The Moon pulling the oceans  d) The Sun getting hotter
**Answer: b) The 23.5° tilt of the Earth's axis, held pointing the same way all year**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The Moon completes one revolution around the Earth in about…
a) 27 days  b) 365 days  c) 24 hours  d) 7 days
**Answer: a) 27 days**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) At full Moon, the Earth's shadow falling on the Moon gives a…
a) Solar eclipse  b) Lunar eclipse  c) New Moon  d) Phase of Venus
**Answer: b) Lunar eclipse**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The Sun appears to move against the background stars over a year because…
a) The Sun orbits the Earth  b) The Earth journeys around the Sun, so our view direction changes  c) The stars push it  d) The axis spins faster
**Answer: b) The Earth journeys around the Sun, so our view direction changes**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Winter constellations are not seen on summer evenings because…
a) The stars switch off  b) The Earth's night side faces the opposite direction in space half a year later  c) Clouds always cover them  d) The Moon hides them
**Answer: b) The Earth's night side faces the opposite direction in space half a year later**
