# Motions of the Earth, Moon and Sun — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why do the two hemispheres have opposite seasons at the same time of year?
**Answer: The axis tilt leans one hemisphere toward the Sun while the other leans away; half a year later, at the opposite side of the orbit, the leaning is reversed.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why do eclipses not happen every month at new and full Moon?
**Answer: The Moon's orbit is tilted a little to the Earth's orbit, so at most new and full Moons the three bodies do not line up exactly and the shadows miss.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What are the Moon's phases, in one sentence?
**Answer: The changing amounts of the Moon's sunlit half that we can see from Earth as the Moon travels around us.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A classmate says the Sun travels through the constellations during the year. Correct them.
**Answer: The Sun stays put relative to the nearby stars for this purpose; it is the Earth's yearly journey that changes our viewing direction, making the Sun appear to move eastward against the stars.**
