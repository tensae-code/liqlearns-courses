# Hands-on — Motions of the Earth, Moon and Sun

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lamp, ball and Moon ball
In a dark room, use a lamp as the Sun, your head as the Earth and a small ball held at arm's length as the Moon. Turn slowly on the spot: that is rotation and the day. Walk one full circle around the lamp, keeping your face toward the same wall: that is revolution and the year — note the lamp appearing in front, beside and behind you. Finally, circle the ball around your head as you face the lamp, then away from it: watch how much of its lit half you can see at each place, and name the phases as it travels.
