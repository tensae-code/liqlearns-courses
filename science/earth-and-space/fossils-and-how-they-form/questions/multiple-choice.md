# Fossils and how they form — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Diagenesis is…
a) The wearing away of rock  b) The changing of buried sediments into solid sedimentary rock  c) A kind of fossil  d) The settling of sediment in water
**Answer: b) The changing of buried sediments into solid sedimentary rock**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Before diagenesis, the material is…
a) Solid rock  b) Loose sediments, buried layer on layer  c) Melted  d) Frozen
**Answer: b) Loose sediments, buried layer on layer**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Fossilisation is…
a) The preserving of a living thing's remains as the sediments around them become rock  b) The carving of shapes into stone  c) The melting of rock  d) The naming of fossils
**Answer: a) The preserving of a living thing's remains as the sediments around them become rock**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A fossil is…
a) Any old stone  b) A living animal  c) The remains of a living thing, preserved in sedimentary rock  d) A layer of sediment
**Answer: c) The remains of a living thing, preserved in sedimentary rock**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A fossil and its rock are related because…
a) The fossil was placed in the rock afterwards  b) They were made together — the remains were preserved in the same turning that made the rock  c) They are always the same colour  d) Fossils make the rock melt
**Answer: b) They were made together — the remains were preserved in the same turning that made the rock**
