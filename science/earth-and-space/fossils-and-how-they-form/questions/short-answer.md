# Fossils and how they form — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What changes, and into what, during diagenesis?
**Answer:** Buried sediments — loose, settled layers — are changed into solid sedimentary rock.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does a shell become a fossil? Give the steps in order.
**Answer:** The shell is buried among sediments; layers bury it deeper; diagenesis turns the sediments into sedimentary rock; and the shell caught inside is preserved in that turning — fossilisation — as a fossil in the rock.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the lesson say "no diagenesis, no fossil"?
**Answer:** Because fossilisation is the preserving of remains inside the sediments-to-rock turning itself: if the sediments never became rock, the remains buried in them would not be preserved as a fossil in rock.

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does Haiti's sequence name diagenesis and fossilisation in one séance?
**Answer:** Because they are one event seen two ways: from sediments to sedimentary rock is diagenesis, and the remains preserved in that same turning are fossilisation — the fossil and the rock are made together.
