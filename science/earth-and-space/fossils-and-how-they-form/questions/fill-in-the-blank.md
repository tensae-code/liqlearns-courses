# Fossils and how they form — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The changing of buried sediments into solid sedimentary rock is called _____.
**Answer: diagenesis**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The preserving of a living thing's remains as sediments become rock is called _____.
**Answer: fossilisation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Preserved remains held in sedimentary rock are a _____.
**Answer: fossil**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Fossils are found in _____ rock, the rock diagenesis makes from sediments.
**Answer: sedimentary**
