Today: **Fossils and how they form** — Haiti's way: fossils taught inside the sedimentary story, from sediments to sedimentary rock.

Start with loose sediments, settling in layers. Buried deeper and deeper, those sediments change — they become solid sedimentary rock. That change has a name: diagenesis.
Now put a living thing's remains in those sediments before they harden. As diagenesis turns the sediments to rock, the remains caught inside are preserved with it. That preserving is fossilisation — and the preserved remains, held in rock, are a fossil.
So a fossil is not placed in rock afterwards. It is made with the rock, in the same turning.
