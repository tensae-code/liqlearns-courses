## Sub lesson 1 — The means of watching the universe

the means of watching the universe: the refracting telescope gathers light with a convex lens; the reflecting telescope with a concave mirror (the great European reflector's mirror is 8 m across); optical telescopes are housed in observatories — five Saudi ones are named — while the Hubble space telescope sits outside the atmosphere, away from the distortion it causes; radio telescopes gather radio waves day and night in all weathers, feeding receivers and computers that map the sky and search for signs of life — with the home experiment counting stars through a tube to feel the effect of light pollution
