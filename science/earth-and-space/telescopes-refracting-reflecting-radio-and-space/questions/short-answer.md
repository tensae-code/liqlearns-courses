# Telescopes — refracting, reflecting, radio and space — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The means of watching the universe.
**Answer:** the means of watching the universe: the refracting telescope gathers light with a convex lens; the reflecting telescope with a concave mirror (the great European reflector's mirror is 8 m across); optical telescopes are housed in observatories — five Saudi ones are named — while the Hubble space telescope sits outside the atmosphere, away from the distortion it causes; radio telescopes gather radio waves day and night in all weathers, feeding receivers and computers that map the sky and search for signs of life — with the home experiment counting stars through a tube to feel the effect of light pollution.

**Answer:** The means of watching the universe — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
