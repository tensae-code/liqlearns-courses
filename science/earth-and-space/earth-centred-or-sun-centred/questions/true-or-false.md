# Earth-centred or Sun-centred? — judging two models — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the heliocentric model, the Moon orbits the Sun directly.
**Answer: False — the Moon orbits Earth; Earth orbits the Sun.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mercury and Venus are never seen far from the Sun.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The heliocentric model was popular at once because it kept Earth at the centre.
**Answer: False — it was unpopular because it displaced Earth from the centre.**
