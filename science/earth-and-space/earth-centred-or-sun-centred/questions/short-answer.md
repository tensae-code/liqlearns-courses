# Earth-centred or Sun-centred? — judging two models — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the Sun-centred model explain retrograde motion better?
**Answer: Earth moves faster and overtakes the outer planet; during the overtake, the planet appears against the distant stars to fall back and loop, though both planets keep moving forward.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one observation besides retrograde motion that fits the Sun-centred model.
**Answer: Mercury and Venus never stray far from the Sun, as expected if their orbits lie inside Earth's (or: things rise in the east because Earth rotates).**
