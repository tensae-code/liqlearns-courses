# Earth-centred or Sun-centred? — judging two models — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Planet' comes from a Greek word meaning —
a) wanderer  b) bright one  c) round one  d) fire ball
**Answer: a) wanderer.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the geocentric model, the centre is —
a) the Sun  b) the Moon  c) Earth  d) a star
**Answer: c) Earth.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In Copernicus's model, the daily motion of the sky comes from —
a) the stars revolving daily  b) Earth's own rotation  c) the Sun's rotation  d) the Moon's orbit
**Answer: b) Earth's own rotation.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Retrograde motion is —
a) a planet really reversing its orbit  b) a planet seeming to loop backwards against the stars  c) a planet stopping  d) the Sun moving backwards
**Answer: b) a planet seeming to loop backwards against the stars.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Retrograde motion happens when —
a) Mars speeds up  b) Earth overtakes an outer planet  c) the Moon passes Mars  d) a planet is at its farthest
**Answer: b) Earth overtakes an outer planet.**
