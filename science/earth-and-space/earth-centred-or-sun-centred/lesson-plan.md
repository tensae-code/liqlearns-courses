# Lesson plan — Earth-centred or Sun-centred? — judging two models

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Earth and Space · **Lesson:** Earth-centred or Sun-centred? — judging two models
**Place in the curriculum:** Earth and Space — the title opened by the Grade 7 Earth lessons (CURRICULUM.md). Unit 6 — The Solar System, at General Science Grade 8 depth.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** two models of the heavens — Earth at the centre, or the Sun at the centre — are judged on evidence, and the Sun-centred model wins because it explains why Mars sometimes seems to loop backwards.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Two families of planets: the learner knows the layout; this lesson asks how we know the layout is real.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State both models and what sits at each centre.
2. Give the three deciding observations.
3. Explain retrograde motion as Earth overtaking an outer planet.
4. Say why the Sun-centred model was unpopular when proposed.
