# Hands-on — Earth-centred or Sun-centred? — judging two models

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Overtake and watch
Walk past a slow-walking companion outdoors, watching them against distant trees. Note the moment they seem to drift backwards against the trees. That seeming loop is retrograde motion.

## Draw your own model first
Before checking the picture, draw the Sun, Earth and Moon as YOU think they move. Then compare with the two models in the lesson and say what you changed.
