# Hands-on — Ocean waters and currents

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read your own coast (map work)
On any map of Mexico, trace the waters around the country. List, from the map alone, every place where land meets ocean water. Write one sentence: what changes for a country that has ocean waters on more than one side?

## The sea as a resource — a class list
In a group, make two columns: 'the sea as nature' and 'the sea as an economic resource'. Fill each column from the lesson, then read the second column aloud: that column is Mexico's own way in this lesson — the ocean inside the country's development.
