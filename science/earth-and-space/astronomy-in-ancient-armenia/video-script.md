# Video script — Astronomy in ancient Armenia — a country's own sky history

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the Armenian Grade 9 book opens its astronomy chapter at home: Armenians watched, timed and wrote about the sky for well over a thousand years — a calendar built from the stars, and in Anania Shirakatsi a 7th-century scholar who set down the cosmos in Armenian — before the chapter turns to planets and galaxies**. A farming highland people lives by the sky's clock: sowing, harvest, festivals and travel all need the year measured, and measuring the year means watching the Sun, Moon and stars return. Then the lesson builds its three parts in order — watching the sky in ancient armenia; anania shirakatsi — the cosmos written in armenian; why the chapter starts at home — each one first met in the hand, then said in words.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
