# Hands-on — Astronomy in ancient Armenia — a country's own sky history

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with your hands
Keep a one-week sky log: at the same time each clear evening, sketch the Moon's shape and its place among the stars, and note the time of sunset. At the week's end, read your own log the way an ancient calendar-keeper would: the Moon has changed shape and moved against the stars, and the Sun's setting has shifted. You have repeated, in miniature, the observations Armenia's calendar was built from.
