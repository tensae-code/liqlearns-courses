# Astronomy in ancient Armenia — a country's own sky history — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Armenian Grade 9 astronomy chapter opens with…
a) The Big Bang  b) Astronomy in ancient Armenia — the country's own sky history  c) Telescope construction  d) Space rockets
**Answer: b) Astronomy in ancient Armenia — the country's own sky history**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Ancient calendars are built from…
a) Guessing  b) Careful repeated watching of the Sun, Moon and stars  c) Trade records  d) Dreams
**Answer: b) Careful repeated watching of the Sun, Moon and stars**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Anania Shirakatsi was…
a) A 7th-century Armenian scholar who wrote on the cosmos, calendar and mathematics  b) A Roman emperor  c) A telescope maker of 1600  d) A modern cosmonaut
**Answer: a) A 7th-century Armenian scholar who wrote on the cosmos, calendar and mathematics**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Shirakatsi's writings treated, among other things…
a) Steam engines  b) The Earth's sphericity, the Moon's borrowed light and eclipses  c) Electricity  d) Ocean tides only
**Answer: b) The Earth's sphericity, the Moon's borrowed light and eclipses**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Opening the chapter with Armenia's own astronomy is…
a) A delay before real content  b) The book's course-way: the subject arrives as the pupils' own inheritance first  c) A printing accident  d) Required by law to be skipped
**Answer: b) The book's course-way: the subject arrives as the pupils' own inheritance first**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) From the local sky the chapter walks outward in the order…
a) Galaxies first, then the Moon  b) Sun and solar system, Moon, stars, galaxies, the Universe  c) Only constellations, nothing else  d) Planets of other stars first
**Answer: b) Sun and solar system, Moon, stars, galaxies, the Universe**
