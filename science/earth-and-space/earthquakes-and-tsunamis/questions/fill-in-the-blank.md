# Earthquakes and tsunamis — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An earthquake is a sudden _____ of the ground.
**Answer: shaking**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The shaking travels through the rock as seismic _____.
**Answer: waves**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A great sea wave set off by an undersea earthquake is a _____.
**Answer: tsunami**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The instrument that records an earthquake's shaking is a _____.
**Answer: seismograph**
