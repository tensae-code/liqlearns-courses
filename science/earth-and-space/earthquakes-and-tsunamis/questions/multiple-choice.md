# Earthquakes and tsunamis — multiple choice


**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An earthquake is…
a) A slow warming of the ground  b) A sudden shaking of the ground when rock slips  c) A wave on the sea only  d) A kind of volcano
**Answer: b) A sudden shaking of the ground when rock slips**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A fault is…
a) A break in the rock where movement happens  b) A kind of seismograph  c) A sea wave  d) A mountain
**Answer: a) A break in the rock where movement happens**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A seismograph…
a) Stops earthquakes  b) Records the shaking as a wavy line  c) Makes seismic waves  d) Measures the sea
**Answer: b) Records the shaking as a wavy line**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A tsunami can be set off by…
a) Wind on a calm day  b) Rain over the island  c) An earthquake under the sea  d) A full moon
**Answer: c) An earthquake under the sea**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) In the Dominican Republic's books a tsunami is also called a…
a) Maremoto  b) Cordillera  c) Seismograph  d) Fault
**Answer: a) Maremoto**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) As a tsunami reaches shallow water at a coast it…
a) Disappears at once  b) Turns into rain  c) Grows tall and rushes inland  d) Stops moving
**Answer: c) Grows tall and rushes inland**
