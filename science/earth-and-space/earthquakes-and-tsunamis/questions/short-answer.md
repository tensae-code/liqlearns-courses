# Earthquakes and tsunamis — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens along a fault in an earthquake?
**Answer: Rock that has been strained slips suddenly along the break, and the ground shakes.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What do seismic waves do, and what records them?
**Answer: Seismic waves carry the shaking out through the rock from the break; a seismograph records the shaking as a wavy line.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How does an undersea earthquake make a tsunami?
**Answer: The sudden movement of the sea floor pushes the water above it and sets off a great sea wave that travels across the ocean.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Why do the Dominican Republic's books teach earthquakes and tsunamis together?
**Answer: Because for an island they are one joined event: the earthquake under the sea and the wave that reaches the shore.**
