# Hands-on — Earthquakes and tsunamis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## From shaking to record
Hold a ruler flat on a table and bend it gently until it slips and snaps back — feel the sudden release. That slip is the fault's slip in miniature. Then drag a pencil slowly across paper, then jerk it: the smooth line and the jagged line are what a seismograph's trace looks like quiet and shaking.

## The wave that grows
In a basin of water, push the water at one end with a flat hand, once, sharply. Watch the ripple travel and what it does when it reaches the shallow far edge. Say out loud how this is like a tsunami growing as the sea shallows — and how it is different (a real tsunami is the whole sea moving, not a ripple on top).
