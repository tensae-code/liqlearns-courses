# Rhythms in nature — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A change that comes back in the same order is a _____.
**Answer: rhythm**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The _____ rhythm brings the seasons back each year.
**Answer: yearly**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Rhythms longer than a century are called _____ rhythms.
**Answer: multi-century**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) All of nature's rhythms together make nature's _____.
**Answer: calendar**
