# Rhythms in nature — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What two short rhythms does the lesson open with, and what drives each?
**Answer:** The daily rhythm, driven by the Earth's turning, and the yearly rhythm of the seasons, driven by the Earth's yearly travel round the Sun.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is a rhythm more than just a change?
**Answer:** Because it comes back — the same change returns in the same order, again and again.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the two long rhythms in the lesson.
**Answer:** Century rhythms and multi-century rhythms.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) What is nature's calendar?
**Answer:** The full pattern-book of when things happen in nature, made of its short and long rhythms together.
