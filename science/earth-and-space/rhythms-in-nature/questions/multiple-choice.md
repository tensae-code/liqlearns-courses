# Rhythms in nature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A rhythm in nature is…
a) A change that happens once  b) A change that comes back in the same order, again and again  c) Only the weather  d) A plant's name
**Answer: b) A change that comes back in the same order, again and again**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The daily rhythm is driven by…
a) The Moon's phases  b) The wind  c) The Earth's turning  d) Rivers flowing
**Answer: c) The Earth's turning**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The yearly rhythm brings back…
a) The tides only  b) Earthquakes  c) New continents  d) The seasons, in their order
**Answer: d) The seasons, in their order**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Century and multi-century rhythms are…
a) Slow swings in nature taking lifetimes for one turn  b) Everyday changes  c) Faster than daily rhythms  d) Made by people
**Answer: a) Slow swings in nature taking lifetimes for one turn**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Nature's calendar is…
a) A paper calendar  b) The whole pattern of when things happen in nature  c) One season  d) A list of holidays
**Answer: b) The whole pattern of when things happen in nature**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Living things use nature's rhythms to…
a) Stop changing  b) Avoid the seasons  c) Grow, breed and rest at the right times  d) Live without light
**Answer: c) Grow, breed and rest at the right times**
