# Hands-on — Rhythms in nature

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A rhythm diary
For one week, record the same three things morning and evening: light, warmth, and one living thing's activity. Read the week back: which rhythm do your records show?

## Draw nature's calendar
Draw a circle for the year. Mark the seasons and one nature event you know in each. Your circle is a first page of nature's calendar.
