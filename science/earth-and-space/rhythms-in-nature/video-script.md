# Video script — Rhythms in nature

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Rhythms in nature** — Armenia's way: nature keeps time.

Every day, light and dark come back. Every year, the seasons come back in order. And beyond them, slow rhythms turn over centuries.

A change that comes back, in the same order, is a rhythm — and all of them together are nature's calendar.

Now you. Name a daily rhythm and a yearly rhythm. Say it back — in your own words.
