# Rock types — igneous, sedimentary, metamorphic — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Igneous rocks — born from melted rock.
**Answer:** **Igneous rocks** form when **melted rock cools and hardens**. Melted rock below the surface, or lava poured out above it, loses its heat, solidifies, and becomes igneous rock.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Sedimentary rocks — built from settled layers.
**Answer:** **Sedimentary rocks** form from **sediment**: material worn from older rock, carried, and let fall in layers, which are then buried and pressed into solid rock.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Metamorphic rocks — changed by heat and pressure.
**Answer:** **Metamorphic rocks** form when an **existing rock is changed** — buried, squeezed and heated until its material is remade into a new rock, without melting back into liquid.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Igneous rocks — born from melted rock → Sedimentary rocks — built from settled layers → Metamorphic rocks — changed by heat and pressure — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
