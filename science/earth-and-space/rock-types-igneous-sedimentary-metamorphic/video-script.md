Today: **Rock types — igneous, sedimentary, metamorphic** — Costa Rica's way, from its Grade 8 Ciencias, Código 80008 (printed p. 118).

**Igneous rocks** form when **melted rock cools and hardens**. Melted rock below the surface, or lava poured out above it, loses its heat, solidifies, and becomes igneous rock.

**Sedimentary rocks** form from **sediment**: material worn from older rock, carried, and let fall in layers, which are then buried and pressed into solid rock.

**Metamorphic rocks** form when an **existing rock is changed** — buried, squeezed and heated until its material is remade into a new rock, without melting back into liquid.
