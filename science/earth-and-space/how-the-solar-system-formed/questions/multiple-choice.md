# How the solar system formed — from cloud to Sun — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The solar system is —
a) only the Sun and the eight planets  b) the Sun plus everything that orbits it — planets, moons, asteroids, comets, dust and gas  c) the Sun and its nine planets  d) all the stars in our galaxy
**Answer: b) the Sun plus everything that orbits it — planets, moons, asteroids, comets, dust and gas.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A nebula is —
a) a small rocky planet  b) an enormous cloud of gas and dust between the stars  c) a ring around Saturn  d) the Sun's outer layer
**Answer: b) an enormous cloud of gas and dust between the stars.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The nebula the solar system formed from was mostly —
a) oxygen and nitrogen  b) iron and rock  c) hydrogen and helium  d) water and carbon dioxide
**Answer: c) hydrogen and helium.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Fusion in the young Sun began when the core reached about —
a) 1,000 °C  b) 10,000 °C  c) 10 million °C  d) 100 °C
**Answer: c) 10 million °C.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In fusion, the Sun's core joins —
a) helium nuclei into hydrogen  b) four hydrogen nuclei into one helium nucleus  c) oxygen and carbon into water  d) iron nuclei into gold
**Answer: b) four hydrogen nuclei into one helium nucleus.**
