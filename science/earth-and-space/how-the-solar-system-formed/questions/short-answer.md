# How the solar system formed — from cloud to Sun — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why can the solar system's origin only be a theory, not a witnessed fact?
**Answer: nobody was there to watch it happen 4.5 billion years ago; the account is built from present-day observation of clouds, disks and stars.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things happened to the cloud fragment as gravity shrank it?
**Answer: it flattened into a spinning disk with a dense centre, and it spun faster the smaller it got.**
