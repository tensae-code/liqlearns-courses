# Hands-on — How the solar system formed — from cloud to Sun

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Spin like the cloud
Stand with your arms stretched wide and turn slowly. Pull your arms in as you turn. You spin faster — the same thing that happened to the shrinking cloud.

## The four-panel story
Fold a sheet of paper into four boxes and draw the nebula story, one step per box. Show it to someone and tell the story without reading.
