# Make and launch a model rocket — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name a model rocket's three parts and the job of each.
**Answer:** The nose — pointed, to part the air; the body — to hold the rocket's power; the fins — to keep it flying straight.

**2.** Why does the book end the space unit with a build?
**Answer:** Because making a rocket teaches what pictures cannot: a rocket is a designed thing whose every part serves the flight — the unit's knowledge becomes the learner's own machine.

**3.** What does the launch prove, and what does a bad flight teach?
**Answer:** The launch tests the build: a straight climb proves the parts balanced, and a veer or tumble names what to change in the next build.

**4.** What safety rules belong to a launch?
**Answer:** Adults run it, outdoors in an open place, with watchers standing well back and the rocket checked before it flies.
