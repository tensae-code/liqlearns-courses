# Make and launch a model rocket — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** The rocket's pointed _____ parts the air.
**Answer: nose**

**2.** The _____ keep a rocket flying straight.
**Answer: fins**

**3.** The launch is the _____ of the build.
**Answer: test**

**4.** A veering flight names what to _____ in the next build.
**Answer: change**
