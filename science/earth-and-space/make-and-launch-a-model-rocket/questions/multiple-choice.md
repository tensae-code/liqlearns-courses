# Make and launch a model rocket — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The model rocket's nose is pointed to…
a) Part the air as it climbs  b) Hold the fins  c) Store water  d) Make it heavier
**Answer: a) Part the air as it climbs**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The fins on a model rocket…
a) Power it  b) Keep it flying straight  c) Paint it  d) Slow it on purpose
**Answer: b) Keep it flying straight**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Making the model teaches what pictures cannot — that a rocket is…
a) A natural object  b) Only a drawing  c) A designed thing, each part there for the flight  d) Impossible to understand
**Answer: c) A designed thing, each part there for the flight**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The launch is best described as…
a) A decoration  b) The end of designing  c) Unrelated to the model  d) The test of the build
**Answer: d) The test of the build**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A rocket that veers or tumbles tells its maker…
a) What to change in the build  b) That rockets never work  c) To launch it indoors  d) That fins are useless
**Answer: a) What to change in the build**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) This build closes which unit?
a) The soil unit  b) The space unit — from knowing the sky to flying a machine of the class's own  c) The food unit  d) The magnets unit
**Answer: b) The space unit — from knowing the sky to flying a machine of the class's own**
