# Hands-on — Make and launch a model rocket

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the model
With adult help, make a simple model rocket from safe materials — a card or plastic body, a cone nose, and fins. Check it stands straight and its fins are even before any launch.

## Launch and observe (adults lead)
Adults run the launch outdoors in an open place. Watchers stand well back. Note how the rocket flies — straight, veering or tumbling — and write one change the flight suggests for the next build.
