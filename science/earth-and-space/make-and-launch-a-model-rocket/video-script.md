# Video script — Make and launch a model rocket

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Make and launch a model rocket** — China's way, from its 科学 (Science) textbooks.

Body, nose, fins — build a rocket with your own hands, each part there for the flight: the nose to part the air, the fins to fly it straight.
Then launch it — safely, outdoors, adults in charge. Straight up means your build was balanced. A tumble is not failure; it is the rocket telling you what to change.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
