# Inside the Earth — crust, mantle, core — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Continental crust is thicker than oceanic crust.
**Answer: True** — 20–90 km against 5–10 km.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The mantle holds 16% of the Earth's volume.
**Answer: False** — the mantle holds 83%; 16% is the core's share.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The outer core is solid and the inner core is liquid.
**Answer: False** — it is the other way: liquid outer core, solid inner core.
