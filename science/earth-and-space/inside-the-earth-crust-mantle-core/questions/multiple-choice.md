# Inside the Earth — crust, mantle, core — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The outermost layer of the Earth is the —
a) mantle  b) core  c) crust  d) yolk
**Answer: c) crust** — the shell of the egg.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Oceanic crust is —
a) 20–90 km thick and light  b) 5–10 km thick and denser than continental crust  c) 2,900 km thick  d) liquid
**Answer: b).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The thickest layer of the Earth is the —
a) crust  b) mantle  c) inner core  d) outer core
**Answer: b) mantle** — about 2,900 km thick, 83% of the volume.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The core is made mainly of —
a) silicon and aluminium  b) water  c) iron and nickel  d) magnesium and air
**Answer: c) iron and nickel.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The Earth's magnetic field comes from the —
a) crust  b) oceans  c) core  d) mantle's surface
**Answer: c) core.**
