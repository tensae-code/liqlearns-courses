# Inside the Earth — crust, mantle, core — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the boiled-egg comparison, the egg's shell stands for the ______.
**Answer: crust.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The mantle is about ______ km thick.
**Answer: 2,900.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The core holds ______ % of the Earth's volume.
**Answer: 16.**
