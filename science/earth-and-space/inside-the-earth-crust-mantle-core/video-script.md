# Video script — Inside the Earth — crust, mantle, core

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cut a boiled **egg** in half. Look.
A thin **shell**. A thick **white**. A **yolk** in the middle.
The Earth is built the same way.

Outside: the **crust**. The skin we stand on.
Thick under the land. Thin under the sea.

Middle: the **mantle**. The thickest layer of all.
About **2,900 kilometres** thick. Most of the whole Earth.

Centre: the **core**. **Iron and nickel**.
Outside liquid, inside solid. It gives the Earth its **magnet**.

Shell, white, yolk. Crust, mantle, core. Now you say it.
