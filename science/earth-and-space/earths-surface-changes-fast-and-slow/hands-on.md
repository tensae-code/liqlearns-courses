# Hands-on — Earth's surface changes — fast and slow

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fast or slow?
List six changes to land you know of — a flood deposit, a cracked road, a worn step, a new volcanic island, a dune moving, a gully deepening. Sort each as fast or slow, and name its process.

## Rate of erosion test
Pour water at two different rates over matching piles of soil in trays. Compare how much soil each moves in one minute — the Grade 5 investigation of erosion's rate, in miniature.
