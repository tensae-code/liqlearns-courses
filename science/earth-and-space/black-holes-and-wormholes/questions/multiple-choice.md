# Black holes and wormholes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A black hole traps everything because inside its horizon the escape velocity…
**Answer: a) exceeds the speed of light, and nothing travels faster  b) is zero  c) equals the speed of sound  d) is negative**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The event horizon is…
**Answer: a) the surface of no return around a black hole  b) its glowing centre  c) a ring of planets  d) a kind of telescope**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Astronomers detect black holes chiefly by…
**Answer: a) their effects — glowing infalling gas and stars orbiting an invisible mass  b) photographing their black surfaces directly in daylight  c) their sound  d) their light**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) To a distant watcher, a clock falling toward a horizon appears to…
**Answer: a) tick ever slower, freezing at the edge  b) tick faster  c) stop at once  d) run backwards**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A wormhole, as science stands, is…
**Answer: a) a hypothetical tunnel allowed by the equations but never observed  b) a photographed shortcut  c) a kind of black hole seen daily  d) a tunnel dug in the Earth**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Holding a wormhole open would require…
**Answer: a) exotic matter with negative energy, never yet found in the needed form  b) ordinary steel  c) a very strong magnet  d) darkness**
