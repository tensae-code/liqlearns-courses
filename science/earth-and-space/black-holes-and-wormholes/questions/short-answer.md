# Black holes and wormholes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why can light not escape a black hole, in one or two sentences?
**Answer: Inside the event horizon the escape velocity is greater than the speed of light; since nothing — light included — can travel faster than light, nothing can escape.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) How was the black hole at our galaxy’s centre weighed if it cannot be seen?
**Answer: By watching stars orbit it: their orbital speeds and periods give the mass of the invisible object they circle — about four million Suns.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What is a singularity, and what does its appearance in the equations honestly mean?
**Answer: A point of infinite density at the black hole’s centre; it means the known theory breaks down there — a sign of incompleteness, not a photographed object.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) State the difference in scientific status between black holes and wormholes.
**Answer: Black holes were predicted and are now observed through their effects (and imaged by their glowing surroundings); wormholes remain purely hypothetical solutions with no observation.**
