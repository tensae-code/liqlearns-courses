# Black holes and wormholes — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The surface of no return around a black hole is its event _____.
**Answer: horizon**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A black hole is black because its _____ velocity exceeds the speed of light.
**Answer: escape**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The stretching force that differs between head and feet near a black hole is the _____ force.
**Answer: tidal**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A hypothetical tunnel through spacetime joining distant regions is a _____.
**Answer: wormhole**
