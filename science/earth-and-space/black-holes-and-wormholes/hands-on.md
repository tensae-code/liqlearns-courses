# Hands-on — Black holes and wormholes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Escape velocity grows
On paper, draw three circles — a planet, a dense star, a black hole — and for each draw arrows of “escape effort” for a thrown ball and a beam of light. Under each, write whether the ball and the light escape. Then write the rule your three drawings show: escape depends on how much mass is packed into how small a space.
