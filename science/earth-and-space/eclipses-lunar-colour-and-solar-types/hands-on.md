# Hands-on — Eclipses — lunar colour and solar types

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three-ball line-up
With lamp, big ball and small ball, make first a lunar eclipse (Earth in the middle) then a solar eclipse (Moon in the middle), naming each aloud as you build it. Then tilt the Moon's path a little and show a month with no eclipse.

## Ring eclipse model
Hold a coin between your eye and a lamp at two distances: near, it covers the lamp; farther, a rim of light shows round it. Say aloud: near is total, far is annular — the Moon's changing distance does the same.
