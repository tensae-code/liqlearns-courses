# Eclipses — lunar colour and solar types — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a _____ eclipse, the Earth stands between the Sun and the Moon.
**Answer: lunar**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The blue part of sunlight bent into Earth's shadow is lost by _____.
**Answer: scattering**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The eclipsed Moon's colour is described as _____-red.
**Answer: copper**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A solar eclipse with a bright ring of Sun left visible is called _____.
**Answer: annular**
