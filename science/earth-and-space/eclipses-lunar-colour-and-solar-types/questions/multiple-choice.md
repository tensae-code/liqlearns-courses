# Eclipses — lunar colour and solar types — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a lunar eclipse, the order in a straight line is…
a) Sun – Moon – Earth  b) Moon – Sun – Earth  c) Sun – Earth – Moon  d) Earth – Sun – Moon
**Answer: Sun – Earth – Moon**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The dark core of a shadow, where the Sun is wholly hidden, is the…
a) Penumbra  b) Umbra  c) Node  d) Ecliptic
**Answer: Umbra**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The totally eclipsed Moon turns copper-red because…
a) The Moon glows with its own heat  b) Earth's ash colours it  c) Earth's atmosphere bends sunlight into the shadow and scatters the blue away, leaving red  d) The shadow is red
**Answer: Earth's atmosphere bends sunlight into the shadow and scatters the blue away, leaving red**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) An annular solar eclipse happens when…
a) The Moon is near enough to cover the Sun fully  b) The Moon is too far away for its disc to cover the Sun, leaving a bright ring  c) The Earth is between Sun and Moon  d) Clouds ring the Sun
**Answer: The Moon is too far away for its disc to cover the Sun, leaving a bright ring**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Eclipses do not happen every month mainly because…
a) The Moon changes size  b) The Moon's orbit is tilted about 5° to the ecliptic, so the shadows usually miss  c) The Sun moves away  d) Earth's shadow is too small
**Answer: The Moon's orbit is tilted about 5° to the ecliptic, so the shadows usually miss**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Eclipses can only occur when the Moon is near…
a) The Earth  b) A node — where its orbit crosses the ecliptic  c) Its farthest point  d) The celestial pole
**Answer: A node — where its orbit crosses the ecliptic**
