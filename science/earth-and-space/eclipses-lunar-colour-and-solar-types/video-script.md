Today: **Eclipses — lunar colour and solar types**.

Line up Sun, Earth and Moon and something must give. Earth in the middle: its shadow swallows the Moon — but the Moon does not vanish. It turns copper-red, lit by sunlight bent through Earth's air, its blue scattered away. Moon in the middle: its shadow races across the Earth — total where the Moon covers the Sun, a bright ring where it is too far to cover it. And most months? The Moon's tilted orbit makes the shadows miss. Eclipses wait for the nodes.
