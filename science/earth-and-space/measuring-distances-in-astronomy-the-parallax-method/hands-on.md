# Measuring distances in astronomy — the parallax method — hands-on

Do this before reading the lesson. Why does a near star shift more than a distant one between January and July photographs? Work with your hands and your eyes first: gather the ordinary items the question needs, make the measurement or the comparison yourself, and write down what you actually found — not what you expected. Then read the lesson and compare: where your finding and the lesson agree, you have learned it twice; where they differ, the difference is the lesson. Keep your numbers — the questions at the end will ask for them.
