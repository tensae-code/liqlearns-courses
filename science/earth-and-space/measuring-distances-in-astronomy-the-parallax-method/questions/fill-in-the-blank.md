# Measuring distances in astronomy — the parallax method — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The known separation between the two viewpoints is called the ___.
**Answer:** baseline

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Parallax turns a measured angle into a distance by ___.
**Answer:** triangulation

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The Sun's horizontal parallax is under nine ___.
**Answer:** arcseconds

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Annual parallax uses photographs taken ___ months apart.
**Answer:** six

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) One parsec is about ___ light-years.
**Answer:** 3.26
