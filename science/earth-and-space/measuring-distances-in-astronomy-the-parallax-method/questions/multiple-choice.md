# Measuring distances in astronomy — the parallax method — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Parallax is…
a) A star's colour  b) The apparent shift of a near object seen from two viewpoints  c) A telescope fault  d) The bending of light
**Answer: b) The apparent shift of a near object seen from two viewpoints**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A bigger parallax angle means the body is…
a) Hotter  b) Nearer  c) Bigger  d) Older
**Answer: b) Nearer**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Horizontal parallax is measured using a baseline of…
a) Earth's orbit  b) Earth's radius (observatories a known distance apart)  c) The Moon's orbit  d) One kilometre
**Answer: b) Earth's radius (observatories a known distance apart)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The Moon's distance was measured early because its parallax is…
a) Zero  b) Large — nearly a degree  c) Too small to see  d) Negative
**Answer: b) Large — nearly a degree**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) One parsec is the distance at which annual parallax is…
a) One degree  b) One arcminute  c) One arcsecond  d) One radian
**Answer: c) One arcsecond**
