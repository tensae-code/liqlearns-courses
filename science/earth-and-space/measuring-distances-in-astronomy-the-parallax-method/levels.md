# Measuring distances in astronomy — the parallax method — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can do all of these — you cannot pace out the sky, but you can triangulate it: look at a near body from two places, measure its tiny apparent shift — its parallax — and the baseline you walked turns that angle into a distance, out to the nearest stars — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — restating each part in the learner's own examples, working its numbers, and checking new cases against it until the teaching is the learner's own.
