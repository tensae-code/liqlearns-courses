The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Weightlessness and floating in orbit**.

You feel your weight because the floor pushes back. Let everything fall together — the bottle and its water, the craft and its crew — and nothing pushes back: that is weightlessness. Pakistan (Punjab)'s Grade 7 book proves it with a falling bottle, and orbit is that same fall, kept going around the Earth.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
