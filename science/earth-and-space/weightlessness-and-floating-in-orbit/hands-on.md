# Hands-on — Weightlessness and floating in orbit

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The falling bottle
Repeat the bottle drop and draw three panels — bottle at rest (water pouring), bottle falling (what you saw), bottle caught again. Label each panel with what presses on what.

## Teach the fall
Teach the lesson to a companion with a book and a soft ball: drop them together from a small height onto a cushion, and explain why neither pressed on the other while they fell.
