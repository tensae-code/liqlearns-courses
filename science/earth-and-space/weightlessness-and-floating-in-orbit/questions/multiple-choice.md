# Weightlessness and floating in orbit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Weight is felt because…
a) Gravity stops  b) The ground or chair pushes back against gravity's pull  c) Mass disappears  d) Air holds you up  
**Answer: b) The ground or chair pushes back against gravity's pull**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In the falling-bottle activity, bottle and water…
a) Fall together at the same rate, so the water does not pour as at rest  b) Fall at different rates  c) Float upward  d) Stop being affected by gravity  
**Answer: a) Fall together at the same rate, so the water does not pour as at rest**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Weightlessness in orbit happens because…
a) Gravity is switched off in space  b) The craft has no mass  c) The craft and crew fall together around the Earth, with nothing pushing back  d) There is no Earth nearby  
**Answer: c) The craft and crew fall together around the Earth, with nothing pushing back**
