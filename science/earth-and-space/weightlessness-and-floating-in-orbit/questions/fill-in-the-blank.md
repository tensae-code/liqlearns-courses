# Weightlessness and floating in orbit — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** ______ is how much matter a thing has; it does not change in orbit.
**Answer: Mass**

**2.** When bottle and water ______ together, weight is not felt.
**Answer: fall**

**3.** Orbit is a fall kept going ______ the Earth.
**Answer: around**
