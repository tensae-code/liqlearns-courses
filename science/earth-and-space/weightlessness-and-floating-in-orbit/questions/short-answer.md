# Weightlessness and floating in orbit — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** How do mass and weight differ, in this lesson's terms?
**Answer: Mass is how much matter a thing has and stays the same; weight is gravity's pull on that mass, felt through the push-back of what holds the thing up.**

**2.** Why does the falling bottle show weightlessness?
**Answer: Because bottle and water fall together at the same rate: the water no longer presses on the bottle or pours as at rest — nothing pushes back, so weight is not felt, though gravity still pulls.**
