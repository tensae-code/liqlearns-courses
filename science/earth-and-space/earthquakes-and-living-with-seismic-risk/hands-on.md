# Hands-on — Earthquakes and living with seismic risk

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A safety walk of your own school (observation only)
Walk your school or home with a notebook and list: the open place you would go to, the routes out, and anything heavy that stands where it could fall. Write the list as your own safety lesson, in the spirit of the book's: risk is answered by preparation.

## One date, two earthquakes — a timeline
Draw a timeline with two marks: 1985 and 2017, both 19 September. Under each, write one line only: what the book teaches from that earthquake. Then write the lesson's closing line in your own words: what living with seismic risk asks of a country that knows its dates.
