# Rocks and minerals — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A _____ is one natural solid stuff, the same through and through.
**Answer: mineral**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Most rocks are made of several _____ joined together.
**Answer: minerals**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) We classify samples by colour, size and _____ .
**Answer: shape**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Hard rock is cut and crushed for building and for _____ .
**Answer: roads**
