# Rocks and minerals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A mineral is…
a) A stone made by people in a factory  b) One natural solid stuff of the Earth, the same through and through  c) Any mixture of grains joined together  d) Only the shiny part of a rock
**Answer: b) One natural solid stuff of the Earth, the same through and through**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A rock is…
a) Always one stuff throughout  b) A liquid found underground  c) A tool for breaking minerals  d) A natural solid mass made of one mineral or of several minerals joined together
**Answer: d) A natural solid mass made of one mineral or of several minerals joined together**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Most rocks are…
a) Made of several minerals joined together  b) Pure water  c) Alive  d) Made by people
**Answer: a) Made of several minerals joined together**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is a feature used to classify rocks and minerals in this lesson?
a) The sound they make when dropped  b) Their price in a shop  c) Colour, size and shape  d) The day they were found
**Answer: c) Colour, size and shape**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Sorting the same samples by colour, then by size, then by shape…
a) Is not allowed — one sorting only  b) Gives the same groups every time  c) Changes the groups each time, and each sorting tells something true  d) Destroys the samples
**Answer: c) Changes the groups each time, and each sorting tells something true**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Hard rock is used for…
a) Building and roads  b) Making drinking water  c) Growing plants in  d) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Building and roads**
