# Rocks and minerals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Say the one key difference between a mineral and a rock.
**Answer: A mineral is one stuff, the same through and through; a rock is a joined mass of one or more minerals.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What do rocks and minerals have in common?
**Answer: Both are natural solids of the Earth — found in and on the ground, not manufactured by people.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the three features this lesson sorts by, and say what happens to the groups when the feature changes.
**Answer: Colour, size and shape. The groups change each time the feature changes — the same samples sort differently, and each sorting tells something true about them.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Give two uses of rocks or minerals.
**Answer: Any two of: hard rock crushed or cut for building; rock for roads; stone built into walls; minerals taken from the ground for the things people make and use.**
