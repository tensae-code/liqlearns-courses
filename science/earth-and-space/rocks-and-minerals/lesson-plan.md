# Lesson plan — Rocks and minerals

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Earth and Space · **Lesson:** Rocks and minerals
**Place in the curriculum:** Earth and Space — appended to Unit 19 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** rocks and minerals compared and told apart, rocks and minerals sorted by colour, size and shape, and what rocks and minerals are used for — the Bahamas' own way, in its Grade 2 Primary Science Curriculum Guidelines, is to teach the ground through samples a child can hold, sort and use
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Rocks and minerals — the same, and different** — A mineral is one natural solid stuff of the Earth, the same through and through; a rock is a natural solid mass made of one mineral or of several minerals joined together — most rocks are mixtures, which is how the two are told apart.
2. **Sorting rocks and minerals, and what they are used for** — Rocks and minerals are classified by what can be seen and felt — colour, size and shape — and each kind has its uses: hard rock for building and roads, softer stone for carving and walls, minerals dug from the ground for the things people make and use.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the main idea → return to the first sub lesson and the Do-it-first questions: every later part hangs on that frame.
- Mixes this lesson up with a neighbour lesson → compare the two titles and say what each one is about; each lesson here teaches one thing.
- Thinks a rock and a mineral are the same thing → return to sub lesson 1: a mineral is one stuff, the same throughout; a rock is made of one or more minerals joined.
- Sorts samples by one feature only → return to sub lesson 2: classify by colour, size and shape together, and check the sorting again.
