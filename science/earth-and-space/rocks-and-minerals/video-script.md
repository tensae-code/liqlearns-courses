Today: **Rocks and minerals** — the Bahamas' way: hold the ground in your hand, and sort it.

A **mineral** is one natural solid stuff of the Earth — the same stuff all the way through. A **rock** is a solid mass made of one mineral, or of several minerals joined together. That is the difference.
And we sort them the way scientists start: by **colour**, by **size**, by **shape**. Then we ask what each is good for — hard rock builds walls and roads, and minerals from the ground go into the things people make and use every day.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
