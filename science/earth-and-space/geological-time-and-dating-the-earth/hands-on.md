# Hands-on — Geological time and dating the Earth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Evidence, controversy, person — a three-box record
Draw three boxes labelled 'the evidence', 'the controversy' and 'the named person'. Fill each from the lesson in one sentence each — the remains in Mexican territory, the Man of Tepexpan controversy, and Naia. Read the three sentences in order: that order is the lesson.

## What can a remain prove? (discussion)
In a group, take one imaginary find — a single old bone found in the ground — and list: two things it could prove, and two things it could not prove by itself. Compare your list with the Tepexpan sub lesson: that gap between prove and not-prove is where controversies live.
