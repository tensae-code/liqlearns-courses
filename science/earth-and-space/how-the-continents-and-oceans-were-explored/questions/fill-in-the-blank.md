# How the continents and oceans were explored — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** History of the mastering and study of the ______ and parts of the world — taught as its own lesson in Kazakhstan's books; the books and pages are named in Sources.
**Answer: continents**

**2.** History of the study of the oceans — taught as its own lesson in ______'s books; the books and pages are named in Sources.
**Answer: Kazakhstan**

**3.** The books teach this part as "______" (practical work: marking continents, oceans and mountain systems on an outline map) — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.
**Answer: Exploration of the oceans**
