# Lesson plan — How the continents and oceans were explored

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Earth and Space · **Lesson:** How the continents and oceans were explored
**Place in the curriculum:** Earth and Space — appended to Unit 19 (Earth and Space) (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** History of the mastering and study of the continents and parts of the world — taught as its own lesson in Kazakhstan's books; the books and pages are named in Sources.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **History of the mastering and study of the continents and parts of the world** — Kazakhstan's books teach this as their own lesson — "History of the mastering and study of the continents and parts of the world".
2. **History of the study of the oceans** — Kazakhstan's books teach this as their own lesson — "History of the study of the oceans".
3. **Exploration of the oceans** — The books teach this part as "Exploration of the oceans" (practical work: marking continents, oceans and mountain systems on an outline map) — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.
4. **How the modern map took shape** — The books teach this part as "How the modern map took shape" (the forming of the continents' outlines) — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.
5. **Who made the modern map?** — Kazakhstan's books teach this as their own lesson — "Who made the modern map?".
