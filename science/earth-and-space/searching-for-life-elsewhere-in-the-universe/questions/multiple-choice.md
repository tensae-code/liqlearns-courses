# Searching for life elsewhere in the universe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Life as we know it needs which three together?
**Answer: a) liquid water, carbon chemistry and an energy source  b) oxygen, soil and sunlight  c) warmth, rain and air  d) gravity, water and wind**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A planet in the habitable zone is at a distance where…
**Answer: a) its surface could hold liquid water  b) it is safe from meteors  c) its star looks biggest  d) it has the most moons**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Europa and Enceladus are prized targets because they hold…
**Answer: a) oceans of liquid water under their ice shells  b) breathable air  c) warm beaches  d) forests**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Most exoplanets have been found by…
**Answer: a) the dip in a star’s light as a planet crosses it, and the star’s wobble  b) landing probes on them  c) their own light alone  d) radio messages**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does oxygen alone in an atmosphere not prove life?
**Answer: a) non-living chemistry can also produce it  b) oxygen is invisible  c) telescopes cannot see oxygen  d) life never makes oxygen**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The honest present status of the search is…
**Answer: a) no confirmed evidence of life beyond Earth — yet  b) life confirmed on Mars  c) life ruled out everywhere  d) proof is being hidden**
