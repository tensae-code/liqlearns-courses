# Searching for life elsewhere in the universe — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The distance band where surface water can stay liquid is the _____ zone.
**Answer: habitable**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Life’s shopping list: liquid water, carbon chemistry and a source of _____.
**Answer: energy**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Enceladus sprays its hidden ocean into space through _____.
**Answer: geysers**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) So far there is no _____ evidence of life beyond Earth — the search reports that honestly.
**Answer: confirmed**
