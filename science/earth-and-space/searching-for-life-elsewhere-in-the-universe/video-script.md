# Video script — Searching for life elsewhere in the universe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **the search done honestly**. Life needs water, chemistry and energy together — so we hunt where they meet: ancient wet Mars, the hidden oceans of Europa and Enceladus, rocky worlds in other stars’ habitable zones. And we say plainly what would count as proof — and that it has not come yet.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
