# Hands-on — Searching for life elsewhere in the universe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the search map
Draw our Solar System and mark the three near targets from the lesson (Mars, Europa, Enceladus) with one line each on why it qualifies under the shopping list. Then add one far target — a rocky exoplanet in a habitable zone — and write what instrument method found it and what measurement could strengthen a life claim for it.
