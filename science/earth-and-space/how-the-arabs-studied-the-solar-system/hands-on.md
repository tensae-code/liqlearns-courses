# Hands-on — How the Arabs studied the solar system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Write it like science
Take one fact from the solar-system lessons and write it as a short piece of scientific writing: plain, ordered, and true to the lesson.

## An interest timeline
Draw a line: Arab scholars studying the solar system — your Grade 4 book studying it — you studying it. Write one line under each point on what is carried forward.
