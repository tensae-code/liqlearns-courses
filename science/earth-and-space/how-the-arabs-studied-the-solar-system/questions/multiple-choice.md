# How the Arabs studied the solar system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The closing writing tells how…
a) The Arabs took an interest in the solar system  b) Rockets are built  c) The Moon was visited  d) Telescopes are sold
**Answer: a) The Arabs took an interest in the solar system**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The piece is the book's…
a) Contents page  b) Scientific writing  c) Safety rules  d) Word list
**Answer: b) Scientific writing**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) It closes the book's run on…
a) Magnets  b) The solar system  c) Soil  d) Sound
**Answer: b) The solar system**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Science keeps what it learns by…
a) Forgetting it  b) Writing it down  c) Hiding it  d) Singing it only
