# How the Arabs studied the solar system — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What two things does the placing of this writing teach?
**Answer: That the solar system was studied by Arab scholars long before this book, and that science carries its learning as written accounts.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is the form — scientific writing — part of the lesson?
**Answer: Because a written account is how science keeps what it learns and hands it on.**
