# Hands-on — The rock cycle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the cycle
Draw the rock cycle as a round diagram: the three families as stations, arrows between them labelled with what moves the matter — melts, cools, settles and presses, heats and squeezes.

## Place a stone
Find three stones. For each, argue which family it belongs to and name the journey that could carry its matter into another family.
