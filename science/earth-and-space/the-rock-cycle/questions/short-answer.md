# The rock cycle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Three families of rock.
**Answer:** Every rock belongs to one of three families, told by how it formed: igneous rock, born when melted rock cools and hardens; sedimentary rock, built from settled layers of material pressed together; metamorphic rock, an older rock remade by heat and pressure without melting (Grade 7, 7.2.1, printed p. 49).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The cycle — energy flow and matter cycling.
**Answer:** What moves rock between the families is the lesson's engine: energy flow and matter cycling create igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic rocks — and destroy and remake them.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Three families of rock → The cycle — energy flow and matter cycling — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
