# Hands-on — Karst — limestone caves and sinkholes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dissolving you can watch (a glass, water, a sugar cube or a lump of chalk-free candy)
Drop a sugar cube into a glass of water and watch without stirring. Time how long the cube takes to disappear into the water, and write one sentence: the cube was not broken or washed away in pieces — it was dissolved. Say how that is the same kind of action water performs on limestone, and why the limestone version needs far longer.

## Cave and sinkhole in one drawing (paper and pencil)
Draw a block of limestone in cross-section: rain arrows on top, a cave hollowed inside, and a sinkhole opening at the surface. Label the three parts and write under the drawing, in one sentence, what joins them: one solvent action of water.
