# Karst — limestone caves and sinkholes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The solvent action of water — water dissolves limestone.
**Answer:** Water does not only wash rock away grain by grain; on limestone it works as a **solvent**: it **dissolves** the rock itself.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Karst — the caves and sinkholes the dissolving shapes.
**Answer:** A landscape shaped by that dissolving is called **karst**.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The solvent action of water — water dissolves limestone → Karst — the caves and sinkholes the dissolving shapes — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
