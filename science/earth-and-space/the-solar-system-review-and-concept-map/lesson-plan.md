# Lesson plan — The solar system — review and concept map

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Earth and Space · **Lesson:** The solar system — review and concept map
**Place in the curriculum:** Earth and Space — the title opened by the Grade 7 Earth lessons (CURRICULUM.md). Unit 6 — The Solar System, at General Science Grade 8 depth.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the whole unit, gathered: fill-ins, choices and defended answers — ordering Moon, Saturn, Earth, star and Sun by size; comparing every planet against Earth; judging what is inside the solar system and what is not; and completing the solar-system concept map.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on every Grade 8 Earth and Space lesson before it: this is the unit's own assessment, taught as a lesson so the learner can run it alone.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier Grade 8 Earth and Space lessons in this title, in unit order. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Order Moon, Saturn, Earth, star and Sun by size, with reasons.
2. Compare each planet against Earth on the unit's rows.
3. Defend the solar system's boundary against galaxies and constellations.
4. Complete the solar-system concept map.

## What you need
Paper for the concept map and the defended answers.
