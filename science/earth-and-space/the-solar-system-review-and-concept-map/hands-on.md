# Hands-on — The solar system — review and concept map

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Concept map on paper
Copy the map skeleton large, and complete it from memory. Then reopen the unit's lessons and add anything you missed in another colour.

## Boundary walk (with the ESSTI anchor, teacher evidence)
List fifteen things — from Lake Tana and airplanes to galaxies and constellations — and sort them: inside the solar system / outside. Then talk about Ethiopia's two satellites (teacher evidence, from the guide): they orbit Earth, deep inside the system you have just bounded.
