# The solar system — review and concept map — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In size order, smallest first: Moon, Earth, Saturn, Sun — a star belongs —
a) below the Moon  b) with the Sun — the Sun is a star  c) between Earth and Saturn  d) below Saturn only
**Answer: b) with the Sun — the Sun is a star.**

**2.** Which of these is INSIDE the solar system?
a) a galaxy  b) a constellation  c) a comet  d) the Milky Way's centre
**Answer: c) a comet.**

**3.** The Sun is mostly —
a) oxygen  b) hydrogen  c) iron  d) carbon
**Answer: b) hydrogen.**

**4.** The solar system has how many stars at its centre?
a) eight  b) two  c) one  d) none
**Answer: c) one.**

**5.** If the Sun were a basketball, the seed-sized Earth would be about —
a) 1 m away  b) 26 m away  c) 305 m away  d) touching it
**Answer: b) 26 m away.**
