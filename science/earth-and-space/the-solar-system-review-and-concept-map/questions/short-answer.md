# The solar system — review and concept map — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** A classmate says Moon, Saturn, Earth, star and Sun cannot be ordered by size. Defend the ordering.
**Answer: they can — Moon (smallest), Earth, Saturn, then the Sun, with a star at the Sun's level because the Sun is itself a star; each step is far bigger than the last, so the order is clear.**

**2.** Why is a galaxy not inside the solar system?
**Answer: the solar system is only the Sun and what its gravity binds in orbit around it; a galaxy is an enormous system of billions of stars far beyond, containing the solar system, not contained by it.**
