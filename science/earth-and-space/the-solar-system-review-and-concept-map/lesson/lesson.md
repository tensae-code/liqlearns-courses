# The solar system — review and concept map

## Do it first

Take the size-ordering question cold: Moon, Saturn, Earth, star, Sun — order them and write WHY for each step. Then work the rest of the lesson as your own examination, and only then check your answers in the question files.

## The review set

[Picture 34510 — the concept map skeleton]

Work the set the way the unit taught: from evidence, with reasons. **Order by size** — Moon, Saturn, Earth, star, Sun — and defend the ordering (a classmate may claim it cannot be done; it can: Moon, Earth, Saturn, Sun, and a star sits with the Sun, since the Sun IS a star). **Compare every planet against Earth** — size, distance, moons, temperature. **Judge the boundary:** which of these are inside the solar system — Lake Tana, airplanes, galaxies, constellations, the eight planets, comets? The system holds the Sun and everything its gravity binds in orbit; galaxies and constellations lie far outside it. **Scale judgement:** if the Sun were a basketball, a seed-sized Earth would sit not 1 m away but about **26 m** away — scale is the unit's recurring lesson. Finish by **completing the concept map**: Sun at the centre, planets, moons and small bodies as its branches.

## Say it to your companion

Explain the lesson's main facts to your companion in your own words. Then let them ask you one hard question — and answer it from the lesson, not from memory of the words.
