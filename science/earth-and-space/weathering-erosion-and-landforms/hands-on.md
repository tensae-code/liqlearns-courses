# Hands-on — Weathering, erosion and landforms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two hands of relief — a labelled sketch
Draw one landform — a hill above a plain, a coast, a valley. Label it twice: with an arrow from below marked 'internal factors generate relief', and an arrow from outside marked 'external factors model relief'. Write one sentence under each arrow saying what that work does.

## Continental and ocean relief — one story
Sketch the same profile twice: once as land you can stand on, once as the floor under the sea. Write under the pair: relief forms belong to one story in two settings — continental and ocean.
