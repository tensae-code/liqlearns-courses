# Weathering, erosion and landforms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Internal factors that generate relief.
**Answer:** Relief — the shapes of the land and the sea floor — is first generated from inside the Earth: the internal factors are the relief-makers.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Continental and ocean relief forms.
**Answer:** The generated relief takes forms in two places: continental relief — the shapes on the land — and ocean relief — the shapes under the sea.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: External factors that model relief.
**Answer:** Generated relief does not stay as it was made: external factors model it — working on it from outside, reshaping the forms over time.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Internal factors that generate relief → Continental and ocean relief forms → External factors that model relief — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
