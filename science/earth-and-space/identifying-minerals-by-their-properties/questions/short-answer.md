# Identifying minerals by their properties — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the five properties the book uses to tell minerals apart.
**Answer: Lustre, streak, hardness, fracture and magnetism.**

**2.** Why does the book not identify a mineral by a first look alone?
**Answer: Because minerals are told apart by their properties — each property is tested in its own way and matched against the properties table; a first look tests none of them.**
