# Identifying minerals by their properties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Streak is…
a) how the surface shines  b) the colour of the mark the mineral leaves  c) how the mineral breaks  d) whether a magnet pulls it  
**Answer: b) the colour of the mark the mineral leaves**

**2.** Which property asks whether a magnet pulls the mineral?
a) Lustre  b) Streak  c) Hardness  d) Magnetism  
**Answer: d) Magnetism**

**3.** Identification is made by matching tested properties against…
a) a photograph  b) the table of common minerals' properties  c) the rock cycle  d) a map  
**Answer: b) the table of common minerals' properties**
