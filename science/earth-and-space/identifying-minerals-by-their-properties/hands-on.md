# Hands-on — Identifying minerals by their properties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The five-test card
Make an identification card with five rows — lustre, streak, hardness, fracture, magnetism — and beside each, the test and what result you would record for a sample.

## Use the table the book's way
Take two different stones or mineral samples your teacher provides. Run the tests you can safely run, record each property, and say what a properties table would need to show for you to name each one.
