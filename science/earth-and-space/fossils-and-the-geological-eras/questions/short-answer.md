# Fossils and the geological eras — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Honduras's books teach about fossils defined?
**Answer: fossils defined: a fossil is the remains of a living being, or of its biological activity, that has been embedded in a rock — the marks the first living beings left of their passage.**

**2.** What do Honduras's books teach about how the Earth's age is known?
**Answer: how the Earth's age is known: radioactivity is the proof — geologists calculate the age of rocks directly from their uranium and lead content, and the planet's age was estimated by lead-lead isotope dating on meteorites, including the Canyon Diablo meteorite, in work published in 1956.**

**3.** What do Honduras's books teach about geological time is divided by great changes?
**Answer: geological time is divided by great changes — movement of the continental masses, climate changes, formation of mountain ranges and the predominant type of life — into eras of very different durations.**

**Answer: Fossils and the geological eras.**
