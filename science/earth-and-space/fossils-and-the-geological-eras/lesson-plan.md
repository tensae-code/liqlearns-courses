# Lesson plan — Fossils and the geological eras

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Earth and Space · **Lesson:** Fossils and the geological eras
**Place in the curriculum:** Earth and Space — appended to Unit 19 (Earth and Space) (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** fossils defined: a fossil is the remains of a living being, or of its biological activity, that has been embedded in a rock — the marks the first living beings left of their passage.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Fossils defined** — fossils defined: a fossil is the remains of a living being, or of its biological activity, that has been embedded in a rock — the marks the first living beings left of their passage.
2. **How the Earth's age is known** — how the Earth's age is known: radioactivity is the proof — geologists calculate the age of rocks directly from their uranium and lead content, and the planet's age was estimated by lead-lead isotope dating on meteorites, including the Canyon Diablo meteorite, in work published in 1956.
3. **Geological time is divided by great changes** — geological time is divided by great changes — movement of the continental masses, climate changes, formation of mountain ranges and the predominant type of life — into eras of very different durations.
