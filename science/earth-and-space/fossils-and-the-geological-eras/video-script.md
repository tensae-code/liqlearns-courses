Today: **Fossils and the geological eras** — Honduras's way.

fossils defined: a fossil is the remains of a living being, or of its biological activity, that has been embedded in a rock — the marks the first living beings left of their passage. how the Earth's age is known: radioactivity is the proof — geologists calculate the age of rocks directly from their uranium and lead content, and the planet's age was estimated by lead-lead isotope dating on meteorites, including the Canyon Diablo meteorite, in work published in 1956. geological time is divided by great changes — movement of the continental masses, climate changes, formation of mountain ranges and the predominant type of life — into eras of very different durations.
