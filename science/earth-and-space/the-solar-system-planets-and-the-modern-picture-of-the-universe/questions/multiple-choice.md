# The Solar system — planets and the modern picture of the Universe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The rocky family's planets are…
**Answer: a) Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars  b) Jupiter and Saturn only  c) all eight  d) the four giants**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The giants are made mostly of…
**Answer: a) gas and ice  b) rock and metal  c) water  d) lava**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Our Moon is remarkable because…
**Answer: a) it is unusually large for its planet and tidally locked — one face always to us  b) it is the largest moon known  c) it has its own light  d) it circles the Sun alone**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The asteroid belt lies between…
**Answer: a) Mars and Jupiter  b) Earth and Mars  c) Saturn and Uranus  d) the Sun and Mercury**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The Sun's share of the Solar system's mass is…
**Answer: a) 99.86%  b) 50%  c) 10%  d) 99.86% belongs to Jupiter**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The modern picture dates the Universe's hot dense beginning to about…
**Answer: a) 13.8 billion years ago  b) 4.6 million years ago  c) 13.8 million years ago  d) 6000 years ago**
