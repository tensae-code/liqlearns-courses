# Hands-on — The Solar system — planets and the modern picture of the Universe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The emptiness, walked
On a long corridor or field, pace out the system at 1 metre = 10 million km: Sun at 0, Earth at 15 m, Jupiter at 78 m, Neptune at 450 m — you will run out of field before Neptune, which is the lesson. Mark each planet with a stone sized to feel (Earth a peppercorn at this scale is still too big — say so honestly). Write the sentence the walk taught you that no wall-chart could.
