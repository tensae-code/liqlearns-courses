# Landforms — hills, plains and valleys — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: What relief is — and height above sea level.
**Answer:** Serbia's book begins with the word itself: **relief** is the shape of the land — all its rises and hollows together.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The high forms — hillocks, hills and mountains.
**Answer:** The land rises in steps of size: a small rise is a **hillock**, a bigger rise a **hill**, and the greatest rises are **mountains**.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: The hollows and flat lands — river beds, gorges, canyons, plains, basins, valleys and plateaus.
**Answer:** Against the rises stand the low forms: a **river bed**, where the water runs; deep cuts — **gorges** and **canyons**; wide flat **plains**; **basins**, low ground gathered inside higher ground; **valleys**, the low road a river makes between rises; and **plateaus**, high flat ground.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** What relief is — and height above sea level → The high forms — hillocks, hills and mountains → The hollows and flat lands — river beds, gorges, canyons, plains, basins, valleys and plateaus — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
