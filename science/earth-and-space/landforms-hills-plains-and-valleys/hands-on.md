# Hands-on — Landforms — hills, plains and valleys

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build your region in relief (tray, sand or clay)
In a tray, shape sand or clay into one hillock, one hill and one mountain, then cut a river bed and a valley, and leave one corner as a plain. Label each form with a paper flag. Read your tray back: rises in climbing order, then the hollows and flats.
