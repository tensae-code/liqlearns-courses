Today: **Landforms — hills, plains and valleys** — Serbia's way, from its Grade 3 *Природа и друштво* (Рељеф мога краја (The relief of my region), printed p. 4).

Serbia's book begins with the word itself: **relief** is the shape of the land — all its rises and hollows together. A place's height is measured as its **height above sea level**: how high the land stands above the sea. That measure is what lets one place be called higher or lower than another.

The land rises in steps of size: a small rise is a **hillock**, a bigger rise a **hill**, and the greatest rises are **mountains**. Hillock, hill, mountain — the same kind of form, growing: Serbia's way is to name them in that climbing order, on the learner's own region.

Against the rises stand the low forms: a **river bed**, where the water runs; deep cuts — **gorges** and **canyons**; wide flat **plains**; **basins**, low ground gathered inside higher ground; **valleys**, the low road a river makes between rises; and **plateaus**, high flat ground. Rises and hollows together — that full set is the relief of a region.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
