# Astronomical unit and light-year — measuring space — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: The astronomical unit (Sun to Earth, 1.5 × 10¹¹ m; Mars at about 1.5 AU) and the light-year (9.46 × 10¹⁵ m, worked from the speed of light; Proxima Centauri at 4.248 light-years)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science and Technology Grade 9 (Nepali, revised 2082)*, printed p. 202.**
