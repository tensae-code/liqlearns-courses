# Stars — classification and evolution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Sun's spectral class is G. What does that fix about it?
**Answer: It is a middling-temperature yellow star — cooler than O, B, A and F stars, hotter than K and M stars.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What two quantities does the H–R diagram plot?
**Answer: Luminosity against (surface) temperature.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does mass decide a star's lifetime?
**Answer: Mass sets the core's temperature and pressure, hence the fusion rate: massive stars burn their hydrogen fast and die young; small stars burn slowly and live far longer.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) What is left after the most massive stars' supernovae, and when?
**Answer: A black hole — when the collapsing core is so massive that not even a neutron star can hold it up.**
