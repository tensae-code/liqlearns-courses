# Stars — classification and evolution — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The hottest spectral class is _____.
**Answer: O**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The diagram plotting luminosity against temperature is the _____–Russell diagram.
**Answer: Hertzsprung**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A star is born in a cold cloud called a _____.
**Answer: nebula**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A massive star's explosion is a _____.
**Answer: supernova**
