# Stars — classification and evolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The spectral sequence O, B, A, F, G, K, M is a sequence of…
a) Size, largest first  b) Temperature, hottest first  c) Age, oldest first  d) Distance, nearest first
**Answer: b) Temperature, hottest first**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) On the H–R diagram, most stars lie…
a) In the giant region  b) Scattered at random  c) On the main sequence, where they burn hydrogen in their cores  d) Among the white dwarfs
**Answer: c) On the main sequence, where they burn hydrogen in their cores**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A star spends most of its life…
a) As a protostar  b) On the main sequence, gravity balanced against fusion pressure  c) As a red giant  d) Collapsing
**Answer: b) On the main sequence, gravity balanced against fusion pressure**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A massive star, compared with a small one, lives…
a) Longer, burning slowly  b) The same length  c) A much shorter life, burning fast  d) Forever
**Answer: c) A much shorter life, burning fast**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A Sun-like star ends as…
a) A black hole  b) A neutron star  c) A white dwarf, after a red-giant stage and a planetary nebula  d) A supernova with nothing left
**Answer: c) A white dwarf, after a red-giant stage and a planetary nebula**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A supernova's importance for later generations is that it…
a) Cools the galaxy  b) Scatters heavy elements that new stars and planets are made from  c) Stops star formation forever  d) Makes black holes only
**Answer: b) Scatters heavy elements that new stars and planets are made from**
