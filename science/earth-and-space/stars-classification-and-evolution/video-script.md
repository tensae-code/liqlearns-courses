Today: **reading stars**. Their spectra sort them O, B, A, F, G, K, M — hottest to coolest, blue to red; the Sun is a yellow G. Plot luminosity against temperature and the H–R diagram appears: the main-sequence band where stars burn hydrogen, giants above, white dwarfs below. A star is born in a nebula, lives in balance on the main sequence, and dies by its mass: gently, as a white dwarf — or in a supernova that forges the elements and leaves a neutron star or a black hole.
