# Hands-on — Stars — classification and evolution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Colours are temperatures
On a clear night, with no apparatus at all, find the brightest stars you can and sort them by colour: blue-white, white, yellow, orange, red. Write the list in that order — you have just rebuilt the spectral sequence by eye, hottest to coolest, because colour is a star's surface temperature showing. If the sky is too bright where you live, do the same with a printed star chart or photograph and say honestly that it was pictured, not seen. Check one against the sequence: an orange star sits between the yellow Sun-like stars and the red ones — K in the letters O B A F G K M.
