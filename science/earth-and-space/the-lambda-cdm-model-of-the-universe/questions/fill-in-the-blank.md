# The Lambda-CDM model of the universe — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Light stretched toward the red by the expansion of space is called _____.
**Answer: redshift**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The universe began about 13.8 _____ years ago in a hot, dense state.
**Answer: billion**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Dark matter is known only by its _____, since it neither emits nor blocks light.
**Answer: gravity**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The observation that revealed the accelerating expansion came from distant exploding stars called _____.
**Answer: supernovae**
