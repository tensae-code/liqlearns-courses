# The Lambda-CDM model of the universe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The light of distant galaxies is redshifted. This tells us…
a) The galaxies are hot  b) Space itself is expanding — the light was stretched on its journey  c) The galaxies are painted red by dust  d) Our telescopes are faulty
**Answer: b) Space itself is expanding — the light was stretched on its journey**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The cosmic microwave background is…
a) A radio station in space  b) Heat from the Sun spread thin  c) Static from our own galaxy  d) The cooled afterglow of the universe's hot, dense beginning, arriving from every direction
**Answer: d) The cooled afterglow of the universe's hot, dense beginning, arriving from every direction**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) We know dark matter exists because…
a) Photographs show it clearly  b) It blocks starlight like dust  c) Galaxies spin and clusters bind as if far more mass were present than the matter we can see  d) It was made in a laboratory
**Answer: c) Galaxies spin and clusters bind as if far more mass were present than the matter we can see**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In "CDM", the word "cold" means the dark matter particles…
a) Are frozen solid  b) Move slowly compared with light, so gravity could gather them into clumps that seeded galaxies  c) Sit only in cold places  d) Absorb heat
**Answer: b) Move slowly compared with light, so gravity could gather them into clumps that seeded galaxies**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Lambda (Λ) in the model's name stands for…
a) The first galaxy  b) The cosmological constant — the dark energy driving the accelerating expansion  c) A type of telescope  d) The speed of light
**Answer: b) The cosmological constant — the dark energy driving the accelerating expansion**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In the Lambda-CDM recipe, the largest share of the universe is…
a) Ordinary matter — stars, gas and us  b) Dark matter  c) Light  d) Dark energy
**Answer: d) Dark energy**
