# Video script — The Lambda-CDM model of the universe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **The Lambda-CDM model of the universe** — the best picture science has of everything.

The name is a recipe, in three parts. One: the universe began hot and tiny and has been growing ever since — every galaxy is flying away from every other, and we can see the leftover warmth of that beginning in every direction of the sky.
Two: CDM — cold dark matter. Most of the matter in the universe does not shine at all. We know it is there because galaxies spin too fast to hold together without it.
Three: Lambda — the Greek letter Λ — dark energy, pushing the whole expansion faster and faster.
Put it together: a beginning, invisible matter, and a push. Lambda-CDM.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
