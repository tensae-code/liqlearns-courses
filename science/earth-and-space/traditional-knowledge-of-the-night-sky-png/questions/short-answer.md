# Traditional knowledge of the night sky — Papua New Guinea — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching the stars.
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's Standards Based Grade 6 guide gives the night sky a lesson of its own kind: **traditional knowledge of the night sky** (lesson 95), taught beside the constellations lessons. The guide treats the knowledge communities already hold about the stars — learned, named and passed down outside school — as science content in its own right.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Knowledge that stands beside the school lessons.
**Answer:** The lesson's place in the guide is its point: traditional star knowledge is not a preface to the 'real' lessons — it sits in the Space unit as a lesson itself, at the same level as the movement of stars and the constellations. Papua New Guinea's own way of knowing the sky is itself course content.
