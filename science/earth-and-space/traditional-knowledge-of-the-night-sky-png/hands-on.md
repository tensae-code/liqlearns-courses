# Hands-on — Traditional knowledge of the night sky — Papua New Guinea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's thing with your hands
Ask an older person at home what they know about the stars — a name, a pattern, or something the stars were used to tell. Write down one thing they tell you, exactly as they say it, and bring it to the lesson's question: what does this knowledge know about the sky?
