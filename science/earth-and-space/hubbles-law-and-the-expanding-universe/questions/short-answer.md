# Hubble's law and the expanding universe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Galaxies are moving away from each other, and the farther apart they are the faster they recede — recession speed proportional to distance — shown by the dotted-balloon analogy and a distance-against-speed graph; the distance units of the lesson are the light-year and the parsec, with the mega-parsec used in Hubble's work; Hubble's result is presented as evidence for the Big Bang.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Shikshak Nirdeshika Vigyan Grade 10 (guide)*, printed p. 392.**
