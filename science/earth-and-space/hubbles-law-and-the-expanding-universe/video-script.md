# Video script — Hubble's law and the expanding universe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Hubble's law and the expanding universe** — Nepal's way, from its Curriculum Development Centre books.

Galaxies are moving away from each other, and the farther apart they are the faster they recede — recession speed proportional to distance — shown by the dotted-balloon analogy and a distance-against-speed graph; the distance units of the lesson are the light-year and the parsec, with the mega-parsec used in Hubble's work; Hubble's result is presented as evidence for the Big Bang.
