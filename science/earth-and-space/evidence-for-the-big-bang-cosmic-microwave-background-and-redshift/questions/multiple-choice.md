# Evidence for the Big Bang — cosmic microwave background and redshift — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Distant galaxies’ light is shifted toward…
**Answer: a) the red end of the spectrum — redshift  b) the blue end  c) green  d) no shift at all**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The redshift of a galaxy grows with its…
**Answer: a) distance  b) size  c) brightness  d) age of its stars**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The cosmic microwave background is…
**Answer: a) the cooled afterglow of the hot beginning, glowing faintly from every direction  b) light from the Sun reflected  c) radio noise from Earth  d) starlight averaged out**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The CMB’s temperature today is about…
**Answer: a) 2.7 kelvin  b) 270 kelvin  c) 2,700 kelvin  d) zero**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The two evidences are powerful together because they are…
**Answer: a) independent — different instruments, different principles, one converging account  b) both about microwaves  c) both very old  d) both American discoveries**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Running the expansion backwards, the universe becomes…
**Answer: a) smaller, denser and hotter  b) larger and colder  c) unchanged  d) empty**
