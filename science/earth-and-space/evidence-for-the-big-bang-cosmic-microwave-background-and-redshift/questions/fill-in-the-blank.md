# Evidence for the Big Bang — cosmic microwave background and redshift — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Light from receding galaxies is shifted to longer wavelengths — a _____.
**Answer: redshift**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The farther a galaxy, the _____ its recession speed.
**Answer: greater (faster)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The sky-wide afterglow of the hot beginning is the cosmic _____ background.
**Answer: microwave**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Independent lines of evidence _____ on one account — that convergence is the account’s strength.
**Answer: converge**
