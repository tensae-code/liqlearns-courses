# Evidence for the Big Bang — cosmic microwave background and redshift — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does galaxy redshift tell us, and what does the distance pattern add?
**Answer: Galaxies are receding from us; because the recession speed grows with distance, the whole universe is expanding — every galaxy recedes from every other, not only from us.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the CMB, in one sentence, and when was its light released?
**Answer: It is the cooled afterglow of the hot early universe — light released when the universe first became transparent, now stretched by expansion into microwaves at about 2.7 K.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) State the age the converging evidence gives for the universe, and name the two chief evidences.
**Answer: About 13.8 billion years; the redshift–distance expansion and the cosmic microwave background.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Is the Big Bang an explosion into empty space? Correct the picture in one sentence.
**Answer: No — it is the expansion of space itself from a hot dense state; there is no centre within the universe from which everything flies.**
