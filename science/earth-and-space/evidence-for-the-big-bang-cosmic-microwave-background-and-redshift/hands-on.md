# Hands-on — Evidence for the Big Bang — cosmic microwave background and redshift

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run the film backwards
Mark dots for galaxies on a balloon’s surface (or on a strip of elastic) and inflate (stretch) slowly: every dot recedes from every other, farther dots faster — no centre on the surface. Write three lines: what the balloon models well (expansion of space, recession growing with distance), and one thing it models badly (the balloon has an inside and a centre in the room; the universe’s expansion needs neither).
