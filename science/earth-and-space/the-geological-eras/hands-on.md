# Hands-on — The geological eras

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The eras on a washing line (string, paper, pegs or tape)
Hang a line across a wall. Pin three large labels along it in order — Palaeozoic, Mesozoic, Cenozoic — under one long label above them all: Phanerozoic eon. At the very end of the line, add a small final label: Quaternary — the last 2,600,000 years. Stand back and say, in one sentence, why the last label is so small on a line that holds an eon.

## Order from memory (pencil only)
Write the three era names on three scraps of paper, mix them, and lay them in the book's order without looking. Check against the lesson, then write one sentence naming the method the book uses to read the Quaternary: isotope stratigraphy.
