# The geological eras — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The Phanerozoic eon — the Palaeozoic era.
**Answer:** Italy's history of the Earth divides deep time into named stretches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The Mesozoic era and the Cenozoic era.
**Answer:** The Phanerozoic continues: after the Palaeozoic comes the **Mesozoic era**, and after it the **Cenozoic era** — the era that runs up to the present.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: The Quaternary — the last 2,600,000 years, and the geological history of Italy.
**Answer:** The book closes the eon with its most recent stretch: the **Quaternary — the last 2,600,000 years**.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The Phanerozoic eon — the Palaeozoic era → The Mesozoic era and the Cenozoic era → The Quaternary — the last 2,600,000 years, and the geological history of Italy — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
