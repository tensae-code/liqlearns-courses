# Hands-on — Fossils — evidence of life and environments long ago

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a fossil as evidence
Take a fossil picture or a shell in rock. Write two claims it supports — one about the organism, one about its environment — and the feature of the fossil behind each claim.

## Argue from layers
Draw three rock layers, each with a different fossil kind in it. Write the argument the pattern supports about how the environment changed.
