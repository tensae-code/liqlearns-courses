# Video script — Fossils — evidence of life and environments long ago

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Fossils — evidence of life and environments long ago** — the United States' way, from its Utah SEEd Standards books (Grade 4).

A **fossil** is read as **evidence**: the book (4.1.3, printed p. 41) has pupils **analyse fossil data as evidence of organisms and environments from long ago** — its worked example is the **trilobites and their modern relatives**. Match a fossil to the living things it resembles, and the fossil tells you both what lived then and what its world was like: the organism and its environment are read together.

One fossil is a fact; **patterns in rock layers and the fossils in them** are an argument (4.1.4, printed p. 56): argue from those patterns that **environments change over time**. The book's own case is the **tropical plant fossils in the Arctic** — plants of warm places, fossilised where no such plants can live now. The layers order the story, and the fossils inside them prove the place itself was once different.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
