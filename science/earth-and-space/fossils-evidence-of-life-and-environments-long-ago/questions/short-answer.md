# Fossils — evidence of life and environments long ago — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Fossil data — organisms and environments of long ago.
**Answer:** A fossil is read as evidence: the book (4.1.3, printed p. 41) has pupils analyse fossil data as evidence of organisms and environments from long ago — its worked example is the trilobites and their modern relatives.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Rock layers and their fossils — environments change over time.
**Answer:** One fossil is a fact; patterns in rock layers and the fossils in them are an argument (4.1.4, printed p. 56): argue from those patterns that environments change over time.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Fossil data — organisms and environments of long ago → Rock layers and their fossils — environments change over time — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
