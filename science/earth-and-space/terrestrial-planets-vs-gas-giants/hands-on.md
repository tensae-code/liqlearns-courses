# Hands-on — Terrestrial planets vs gas giants — the twelve-row contrast

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Build the table yourself
Draw a two-column table with twelve empty rows. Fill it from the picture, close the picture, and fill it again from memory. Mark the rows you missed and learn those.

## Opposites game
A companion names a row — 'moons!' — and you must answer with BOTH sides: 'terrestrial: few or none; giants: many.' Swap roles.
