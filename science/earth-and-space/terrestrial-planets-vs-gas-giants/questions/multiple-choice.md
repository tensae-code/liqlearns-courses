# Terrestrial planets vs gas giants — the twelve-row contrast — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which family has a solid surface?
a) gas giants  b) terrestrial planets  c) both  d) neither
**Answer: b) terrestrial planets.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Terrestrial planets are —
a) far out and widely spaced  b) close in and tightly spaced  c) ringed and cold  d) huge and light
**Answer: b) close in and tightly spaced.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Gas giants rotate —
a) more slowly than terrestrial planets  b) faster than terrestrial planets  c) not at all  d) backwards only
**Answer: b) faster than terrestrial planets.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which family has rings?
a) terrestrial planets  b) gas giants  c) neither  d) only Earth
**Answer: b) gas giants.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** For their size, gas giants are —
a) very dense  b) low in density  c) made of iron  d) solid throughout
**Answer: b) low in density.**
