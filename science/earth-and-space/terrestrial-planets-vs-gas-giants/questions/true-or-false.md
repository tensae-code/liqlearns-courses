# Terrestrial planets vs gas giants — the twelve-row contrast — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Terrestrial planets have thick atmospheres.
**Answer: False — their atmospheres are thin; the giants' are thick.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Terrestrial planets are denser than gas giants.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Gas giants have few moons or none.
**Answer: False — they have many moons; the terrestrial planets have few or none.**
