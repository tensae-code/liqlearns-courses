# Terrestrial planets vs gas giants — the twelve-row contrast — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four rows on which the two families are exact opposites.
**Answer: any four of — distance, mass, density, rotation speed, moons, rings, atmosphere thickness, temperature, surface, composition.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the formation story predict this table?
**Answer: near the hot young Sun only dense rock and metal could condense into small solid planets; far out, light gases gathered into huge, low-density, fast-spinning giants that could hold many moons and rings.**
