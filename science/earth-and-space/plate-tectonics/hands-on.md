# Hands-on — Plate tectonics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three movements with two hands (no materials)
Hold your two flat hands edge to edge on a table. Move them toward each other — convergent. Move them apart — divergent. Slide them past each other — transform. Say each name as you do it, then have a partner name the movement while you do it without speaking.

## Draw Mexico on the plate map
On a blank world outline (or in a notebook, copied from any atlas), shade where the book places the plates around Mexico. Write under it, in one sentence: why a country in Mexico's position must teach plate tectonics as local knowledge, not only world knowledge.
