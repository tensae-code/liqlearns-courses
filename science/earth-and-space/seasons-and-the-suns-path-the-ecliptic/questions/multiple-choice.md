# Seasons and the Sun's path — the ecliptic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The ecliptic is…
a) The Moon's orbit  b) The Sun's apparent yearly path on the celestial sphere  c) The Earth's equator  d) A ring of the Sun itself
**Answer: The Sun's apparent yearly path on the celestial sphere**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The ecliptic is tilted to the celestial equator by…
a) 5°  b) 23.5°  c) 45°  d) 90°
**Answer: 23.5°**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) At an equinox, the Sun…
a) Is at declination +23.5°  b) Is at declination −23.5°  c) Stands on the celestial equator, and day and night are equal worldwide  d) Stops moving
**Answer: Stands on the celestial equator, and day and night are equal worldwide**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) At the June solstice the Sun's declination is about…
a) 0°  b) +23.5°  c) −23.5°  d) +90°
**Answer: +23.5°**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The seasons are caused by…
a) The Earth moving closer to and farther from the Sun  b) The fixed tilt of the Earth's axis as it orbits  c) The Sun growing hotter and cooler  d) The Moon's pull
**Answer: The fixed tilt of the Earth's axis as it orbits**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) When a hemisphere leans toward the Sun, it gets…
a) A lower Sun and shorter days  b) A higher Sun and longer days  c) The same as the other hemisphere  d) No sunlight at noon
**Answer: A higher Sun and longer days**
