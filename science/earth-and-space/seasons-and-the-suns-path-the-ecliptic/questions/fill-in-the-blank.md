# Seasons and the Sun's path — the ecliptic — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Sun's apparent yearly road among the stars is called the _____.
**Answer: ecliptic**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The Sun crosses the celestial equator at the two _____.
**Answer: equinoxes**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) At the December solstice the Sun's declination is about _____ degrees.
**Answer: −23.5**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Through the whole year, the Earth's axis keeps pointing toward _____.
**Answer: Polaris**
