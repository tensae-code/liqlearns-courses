# Hands-on — Seasons and the Sun's path — the ecliptic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Carry the tilted Earth
Carry a tilted ball on a stick round a lamp, keeping the stick pointing at one wall the whole way. Stop at four stations and say which hemisphere has summer at each. Say aloud: the stick never turned — the lean moved.

## Noon shadow year
Measure a stick's noon shadow on the first of each month (or use recorded values) and graph its length. Say aloud: the longest noon shadow comes with the Sun's lowest road — the December solstice side of the ecliptic.
