Today: **Seasons and the Sun's path — the ecliptic**.

Through the year the Sun travels one great road in the sky — the ecliptic, tilted 23.5 degrees to the sky's equator. That tilt is the Earth's own axis, leaning as we orbit. In June the Sun stands 23.5 degrees north: our hemisphere leans toward it, the Sun climbs high, the days stretch — summer. In December it stands 23.5 degrees south and we lean away — winter. Not distance. The lean. The axis never turns; the seasons swap because we travel.
