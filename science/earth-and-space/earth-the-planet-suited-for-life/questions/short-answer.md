# Earth — the planet suited for life — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the nine factors in any order.
**Answer: liquid water in the right amount; the right distance from the Sun; the right temperature; the right moon; the right Sun; the right core; Jupiter the huge neighbour; an atmosphere rich in oxygen, nitrogen, carbon dioxide and other life-essential compounds; and the ozone layer.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does Earth hold life while Venus and Mars do not?
**Answer: Earth is suited on every count at once — liquid water, a temperature that keeps it liquid, and an atmosphere with life's gases and the ozone shield; Venus fails on temperature and air, Mars on air and warmth.**
