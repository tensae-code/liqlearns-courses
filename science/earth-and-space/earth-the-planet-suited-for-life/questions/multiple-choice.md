# Earth — the planet suited for life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many suitability factors does the guide's key list?
a) three  b) five  c) nine  d) twelve
**Answer: c) nine.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Jupiter is on the list as —
a) the right moon  b) a huge neighbour whose gravity guards the inner system  c) the right core  d) a second sun
**Answer: b) a huge neighbour whose gravity guards the inner system.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The ozone layer's gift to life is —
a) oxygen to breathe  b) shielding the surface  c) rain  d) warmth
**Answer: b) shielding the surface.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The nine factors gather into three unique characteristics: liquid water, temperature and —
a) volcanoes  b) atmosphere  c) rings  d) craters
**Answer: b) atmosphere.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's acceptance count is 0 because it stands on —
a) no evidence  b) teacher-guide evidence only  c) a supporting book  d) the student book in full
**Answer: b) teacher-guide evidence only.**
