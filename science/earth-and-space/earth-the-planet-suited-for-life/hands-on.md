# Hands-on — Earth — the planet suited for life

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Nine-factor poster
Draw Earth large and ring it with the nine factors as callouts, each with a one-line reason. Present it: a companion points at a callout, you explain it.

## Suited or not?
Take Venus and Mars in turn. For each, go through the nine factors and mark which fail. The marks show why only Earth, of the three, holds life.
