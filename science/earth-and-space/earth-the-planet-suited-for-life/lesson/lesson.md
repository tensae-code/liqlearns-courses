# Earth — the planet suited for life

## Do it first

Look at the picture: nine callouts around one blue planet. Cover the words and guess as many of the nine as you can from the unit you have finished. Then read and count how many you held already.

## Sub lesson 1 — Nine reasons Earth can hold life

[Picture 34512 — Earth centred, the nine suitability factors as nine labelled callouts around it, Jupiter drawn large at the edge as the neighbour]

The guide's key lists **nine suitability factors**. **Liquid water** in the right amount. The **right distance from the Sun**, and with it the **right temperature** for liquid water to persist. The **right moon** and the **right Sun**. The **right core**. A **huge neighbour — Jupiter** — whose gravity guards the inner system. An **atmosphere rich in oxygen, nitrogen, carbon dioxide** and other life-essential compounds. And an **ozone layer** shielding the surface.

## Sub lesson 2 — The unique three

The nine reasons gather into three unique characteristics: **liquid water**, the **temperature** that keeps it liquid, and the **atmosphere** that holds the gases of life and the ozone shield. No other planet in the unit has all three — Venus's air crushes and bakes, Mars's is thin and cold. Earth is not lucky on one count; it is suited on every count at once.
