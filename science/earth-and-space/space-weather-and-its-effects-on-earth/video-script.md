Today: **Space weather and its effects on Earth** — Malaysia's way, from its book (Malaysia, Grade 9 (Form 3) — *Sains Tingkatan 3*, printed p. 263).

Malaysia's Form 3 book defines **space weather** and teaches its effects on Earth: **auroras** — the visible lights it paints in the sky — and **disruption to satellites and power grids**, the machines and supplies people's lives run on. Space weather is the part of space science that reaches down and touches daily life.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
