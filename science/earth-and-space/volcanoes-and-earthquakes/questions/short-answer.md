# Volcanoes and earthquakes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Volcanoes — fire from below.
**Answer:** A **volcano** is an opening in the Earth's surface where melted rock from below pushes up and erupts — flowing out as lava, or bursting out with ash and gas.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Earthquakes — the ground lets go suddenly.
**Answer:** An **earthquake** is the ground shaking suddenly.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Together at the chapter's end — what movement does.
**Answer:** Australia's order is the lesson: moving continents (10.1) say the ground travels; plate movements (10.2) say how; volcanoes and earthquakes (10.3) are what that travelling does where people live — one by opening the ground to fire, the other by shaking it.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Volcanoes — fire from below → Earthquakes — the ground lets go suddenly → Together at the chapter's end — what movement does — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
