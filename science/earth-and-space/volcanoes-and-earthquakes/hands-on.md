# Hands-on — Volcanoes and earthquakes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stick and slip (two hands, no materials)
Press your two flat palms firmly together and push them slowly sideways against each other. They stick — then slip suddenly. Say which part was the slow plate movement and which part was the earthquake, and why the slip came all at once.

## Build the volcano (paper or clay)
Make a simple cone from paper or clay. Mark the opening at the top and the melted rock below it, and label the two ways out from the lesson — flowing lava, and bursting ash and gas. Under the model write one sentence: why Australia's book puts this model inside a chapter about moving ground.
