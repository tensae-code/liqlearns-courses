# Measuring the too-big and the too-small — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: explain indirect measurement and work both of the lesson's cases: a too-big quantity (the Earth's size, by string-around-a-globe style reasoning and proportional working) and a too-small, too-slow quantity (continental drift in centimetres per year, measured as change over long times).
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to name or work out, new examples from the learner's own surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing, before the learner moves on.
