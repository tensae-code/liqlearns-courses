# Video script — Measuring the too-big and the too-small

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

You cannot wrap a tape around the **Earth**. Too big.
You cannot watch a **continent** move. Too slow.
So measure **indirectly**.

For the too-big: measure a **model**. String around a globe. Then scale it up.
For the too-small: measure the **change**. Positions now, positions years later.
A few **centimetres a year** — counted over years, it shows.

Measure what you **can** reach. Work out the rest. Now you try.
