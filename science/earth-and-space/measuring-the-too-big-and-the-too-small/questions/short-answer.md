# Measuring the too-big and the too-small — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Why can the Earth's circumference not be measured directly with a tape?
**Answer: the Earth is too big to reach around — no tape can be wrapped around the planet itself, so it is measured indirectly through a model and proportion.**

**2.** Give one too-big and one too-small quantity from daily life, and the indirect way to measure each.
**Answer: any sound pair, e.g. too big: a mountain's height — measured from shadows and angles at a reachable distance; too small/slow: a child's height growth — measured as the change between marks months apart, divided by the months.**
