# Measuring the too-big and the too-small — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1.** "Too slow to watch" means "impossible to measure."
**Answer: False** — measure the change over a long time and divide by the years.

**2.** A scale model keeps the same proportions as the real thing, so measurements on the model can be scaled up.
**Answer: True.**

**3.** The string around a globe, by itself with no scaling, gives the Earth's circumference.
**Answer: False** — the string gives the model's circumference; the scaling step turns it into the Earth's.

**4.** Continental drift can be stated in centimetres per year even though no one can see it move.
**Answer: True** — positions measured years apart give the yearly rate.
