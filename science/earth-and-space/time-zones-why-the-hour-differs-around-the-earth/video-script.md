# Video script — Time zones — why the hour differs around the Earth

---

Today: **Time zones**.

Phone a friend halfway round the world at your lunchtime and you may wake them at midnight. Nobody's clock is wrong: the Earth spins once in 24 hours, and each place's hour is simply where it stands on the turn — sunrise here, noon there, midnight on the far side. So the world is cut into time zones, bands keeping one shared time, one hour different from their neighbours. Travel east and you add an hour; travel west and you take one away. One spinning Earth — many hours, all of them true.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
