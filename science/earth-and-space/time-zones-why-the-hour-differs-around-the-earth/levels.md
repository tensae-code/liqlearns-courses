# Time zones — why the hour differs around the Earth — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner explains differing hours from the 24-hour spin alone (noon and midnight at the same moment on opposite sides), states the time-zone system (about 24 bands, one hour apart, borders bent to real boundaries), and applies the traveller's rule — east adds an hour, west takes one away — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 2 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — doing the lesson's own experiments and sorts by hand, naming each idea aloud, and reasoning about new examples until the teaching is the learner's own.
