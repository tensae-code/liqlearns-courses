# Time zones — why the hour differs around the Earth — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Earth spins once every _____ hours.
**Answer: 24**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A place's hour is its _____ time — it belongs to that place.
**Answer: local**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Travelling east, you _____ an hour to your clock.
**Answer: add**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The bands that keep one shared time are called time _____.
**Answer: zones**
