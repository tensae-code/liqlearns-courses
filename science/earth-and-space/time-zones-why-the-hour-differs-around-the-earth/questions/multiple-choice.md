# Time zones — why the hour differs around the Earth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The deep reason different places keep different hours is…
a) Their clocks are broken  b) The Earth spins, so each place faces the Sun at a different moment  c) The Sun moves round quickly  d) Governments chose at random
**Answer: b) The Earth spins, so each place faces the Sun at a different moment**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) When it is noon where you are, a place halfway round the Earth is having…
a) Morning  b) Noon too  c) Midnight  d) Evening
**Answer: c) Midnight**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The Earth makes one full spin in…
a) 12 hours  b) 24 hours  c) 7 days  d) 365 days
**Answer: b) 24 hours**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Neighbouring time zones differ by…
a) Ten minutes  b) One hour  c) One day  d) Five hours
**Answer: b) One hour**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A traveller flying east into the next zone must…
a) Take an hour off the clock  b) Add an hour to the clock  c) Change nothing  d) Turn the clock back a day
**Answer: b) Add an hour to the clock**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Time zone borders on maps bend because…
a) Map-makers are careless  b) They follow country and region borders so neighbours share one time  c) The Earth is bent  d) Rivers move them
**Answer: b) They follow country and region borders so neighbours share one time**
