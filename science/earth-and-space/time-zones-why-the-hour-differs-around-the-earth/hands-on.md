# Hands-on — Time zones — why the hour differs around the Earth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three cities on a ball
Mark three dots around a ball's equator, lamp shining from one side. Rotate the ball a quarter turn at a time and record each dot's 'hour' (dawn, noon, dusk, midnight) in a table. Say aloud: the ball has one spin but three different hours — that is the whole lesson.

## The traveller's clock
Walk eastward across a floor map (or a drawn globe line), moving an hour hand forward one hour per zone; then walk west and move it back. Say aloud: east adds, west takes away.
