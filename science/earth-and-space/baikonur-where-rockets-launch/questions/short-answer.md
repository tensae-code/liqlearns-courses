# Baikonur — where rockets launch — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about where rockets are launched from?
**Answer: The books teach this part as Baikonur, Kazakhstan's own cosmodrome — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.**

**2.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about toktar Aubakirov — the first Kazakh cosmonaut?
**Answer: The books teach this part as "Toktar Aubakirov — the first Kazakh cosmonaut" (his flight launched from Baikonur) — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.**
