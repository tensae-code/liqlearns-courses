# Metamorphic rocks and the rock cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Metamorphic rocks are made by…
a) Cooling lava  b) Heat and pressure changing rock without melting  c) Settling in water  d) Freezing
**Answer: b) Heat and pressure changing rock without melting**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) If a buried rock melts completely, its next rock is…
a) Metamorphic  b) Magmatic  c) Sedimentary  d) Still the same rock
**Answer: b) Magmatic**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The worked example of the family in Grade 9 is…
a) Marble  b) Gneiss  c) Basalt  d) Sandstone
**Answer: b) Gneiss**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) In the rock cycle, sedimentary rock is made from…
a) Worn rock material pressed in layers  b) Melted granite  c) Changed gneiss only  d) Ice
**Answer: a) Worn rock material pressed in layers**
