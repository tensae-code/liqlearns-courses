# Metamorphic rocks and the rock cycle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What separates metamorphic change from melting?
**Answer:** Metamorphic change remakes the rock in the solid state — minerals and texture change under heat and pressure, but the rock never becomes melt; melted rock restarts as magmatic.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What does gneiss show that makes it the family's worked example?
**Answer:** Its light and dark mineral banding — the visible signature of intense change under pressure in a rock that was once another rock.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Trace the rock cycle in one round.
**Answer:** Magma cools into magmatic rock; wearing breaks it to sediment, pressed into sedimentary rock; burial, heat and pressure change any rock into metamorphic rock; melting returns any rock to magma, and the wheel turns again.
