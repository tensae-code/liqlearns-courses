# Metamorphic rocks and the rock cycle — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Rocks changed by heat and pressure without melting are _____ rocks.
**Answer: metamorphic**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The banded worked example is _____.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The turning system linking all three rock families is the rock _____.
