Today: **Metamorphic rocks and the rock cycle** — Mauritania's way: the changed rocks, gneiss as the example, then the wheel that joins all rocks.

Bury a rock, heat it, squeeze it — but do not melt it — and it changes in the solid state: new minerals, new texture, bands like those in gneiss. That is a metamorphic rock.

And no rock family is the end of the story. Melt any rock and it returns to magma; wear it and it becomes sediment; change it and it is metamorphic. The rock cycle turns through all three families, forever.
