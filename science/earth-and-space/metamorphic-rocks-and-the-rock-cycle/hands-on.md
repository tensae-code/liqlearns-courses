# Hands-on — Metamorphic rocks and the rock cycle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Banding by pressure
Layer strips of differently coloured soft clay or dough, then press and twist the stack firmly with your hands. Slice it and look at the bent, banded pattern — a hand model of the pressure that bands a gneiss. (The model shows the pattern, not the heat or the time.)

## Cycle wheel
Draw the rock cycle as a wheel with three stops — magmatic, sedimentary, metamorphic — and arrows labelled with the changes: melting, cooling, wearing and pressing, heat and pressure. Turn one full circle aloud, naming each arrow.
