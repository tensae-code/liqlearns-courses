# Landforms and water on Earth — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Landforms — valleys, canyons, floodplains.
**Answer:** The land's shapes are its landforms, and the book names its pattern through examples: valleys, low land between higher ground; canyons, deep cuts through the land; floodplains, the flat land beside rivers (Grade 2, 2.1.1, printed p. 13).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Water on Earth — solid and liquid.
**Answer:** Earth's water belongs in the same model: water is held across the planet in its places, and in two states — solid and liquid.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Models show the patterns.
**Answer:** Utah's way is to develop and use models of the whole: a model that shows the patterns of landforms and water together — the shapes of the land and the places and states of the water in one view.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Landforms — valleys, canyons, floodplains → Water on Earth — solid and liquid → Models show the patterns — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
