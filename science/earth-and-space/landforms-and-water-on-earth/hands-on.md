# Hands-on — Landforms and water on Earth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the pattern model
Model a piece of Earth in a tray or box: raise and cut the land into a valley, a canyon and a floodplain, and place water — including one piece of ice for solid water. Label every part.

## Read a model
Look at a globe or relief map. Find one valley, one canyon or gorge, and one floodplain, and point to water in each of its two states.
