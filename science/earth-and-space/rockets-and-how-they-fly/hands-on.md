# Hands-on — Rockets — and how they fly

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The balloon rocket
With a partner: thread a long string through a straw, tie the string taut between two chairs, tape an inflated (unknotted) balloon to the straw with its neck pointing back along the string — then let go. Watch which way the air rushes and which way the balloon travels, and say the pair in Newton's words: gases one way, rocket the other. Repeat with a half-full balloon and compare the flight.
