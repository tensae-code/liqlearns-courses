# Rockets — and how they fly — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) What a rocket is — a machine that carries its push with it  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) What a rocket is — a machine that carries its push with it**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) How a rocket flies — gases down, rocket up  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) How a rocket flies — gases down, rocket up**

**3.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: rockets. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**4.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Eritrea's *Science for Grade 8 Textbook* (Ministry of Education, State of Eritrea)  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Eritrea's *Science for Grade 8 Textbook* (Ministry of Education, State of Eritrea)**
