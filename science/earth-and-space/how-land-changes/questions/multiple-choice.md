# How land changes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The land's shape…
a) Never changes  b) Changes  c) Changes only on maps  d) Is made by people only
**Answer: b) Changes**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Sand dunes are formed by…
a) Rivers  b) Wind  c) Ice only  d) People's hands only
**Answer: b) Wind**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The causes of land change named here are…
a) Natural events  b) Spelling rules  c) Seasons' names  d) Tests
**Answer: a) Natural events**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A dune is…
a) A hole dug in sand  b) Sand carried and piled by wind into a new land shape  c) A kind of rock  d) A lake
**Answer: b) Sand carried and piled by wind into a new land shape**
