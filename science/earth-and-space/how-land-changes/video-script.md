Today: **How land changes** — the close of Iraq's Grade 1 Earth unit.

The land's shape changes. Natural events change it — and the book's example is the wind's work: sand dunes formed by wind, sand carried and piled into a new shape of land. The shape a place has today is not the shape it must keep.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
