# How we know the Earth turns and travels round the Sun — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these proves rotation rather than merely agreeing with it?
**Answer: a) The Foucault pendulum's turning swing plane  b) Day following night  c) The Sun rising in the east  d) Stars circling the pole**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The Coriolis effect deflects winds…
**Answer: a) right in the northern hemisphere, left in the southern  b) always toward the equator  c) always westward everywhere  d) only at sea**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Stellar parallax is…
**Answer: a) a near star's small shift against distant stars across six months  b) the star's real motion  c) a telescope fault  d) the Moon's shadow**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Aberration of starlight is compared in the lesson to…
**Answer: a) rain slanting on a moving walker  b) a bending river  c) a swinging pendulum  d) a spinning top**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Earth is nearest the Sun in…
**Answer: a) January  b) July  c) March  d) September**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The seasons are caused by…
**Answer: a) the tilt of Earth's axis carried round the Sun  b) Earth's changing distance from the Sun  c) the Moon's pull  d) the Sun's changing heat**
