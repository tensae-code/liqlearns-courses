# How we know the Earth turns and travels round the Sun — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A free pendulum keeps its plane while the Earth turns beneath it — the _____ pendulum.
**Answer: Foucault**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The Earth bulges at the equator and flattens at the _____ — a spinning ball's shape.
**Answer: poles**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Bessel first measured stellar _____ in 1838.
**Answer: parallax**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Kepler's first law: the orbit is an _____ with the Sun at one focus.
**Answer: ellipse**
