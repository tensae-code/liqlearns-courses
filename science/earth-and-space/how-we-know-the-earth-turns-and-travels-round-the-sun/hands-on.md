# Hands-on — How we know the Earth turns and travels round the Sun

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Coriolis in a basin
Put a rotating plate (a lazy susan, a turntable, a friend turning slowly in a swivel chair holding a tray) to work: roll a marble straight across the spinning tray and watch its path curve across the surface. Draw the path as seen from above the room and as 'felt' on the tray. That curve, written across an ocean of air, is the trade winds' bend — the spin signing its name. Record both views and label which frame each belongs to.
