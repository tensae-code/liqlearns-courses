# Hands-on — Two families of planets — rocky inside, giants outside

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Sort the planets
Write the eight planet names on scraps of paper. Sort them into two piles — rocky inside, giants outside — then lay each pile in order from the Sun and check yourself against the picture.

## Hot pan, cold room
Hold a wet cloth near (not on) a hot pan: the water leaves as vapour. Far from the heat, vapour can settle and gather. That is the young disk in miniature: heat drives the light stuff away, cold lets it collect.
