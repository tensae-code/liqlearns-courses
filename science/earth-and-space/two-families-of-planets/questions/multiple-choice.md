# Two families of planets — rocky inside, giants outside — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The planets formed from —
a) pieces broken off the Sun  b) disk matter that collided and stuck together  c) comets captured from other stars  d) the Moon breaking apart
**Answer: b) disk matter that collided and stuck together.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The inner planets are —
a) Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars  b) Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune  c) Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn  d) Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter
**Answer: a) Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Near the hot young Sun, light elements —
a) froze solid at once  b) could not condense — they were vaporised away  c) turned into iron  d) gathered into huge planets
**Answer: b) could not condense — they were vaporised away.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The outer planets are made mostly of —
a) iron and rock  b) hydrogen, helium, methane and ammonia  c) water and soil  d) carbon dioxide
**Answer: b) hydrogen, helium, methane and ammonia.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The inner planets have cores of —
a) helium  b) ice  c) iron  d) hydrogen
**Answer: c) iron.**
