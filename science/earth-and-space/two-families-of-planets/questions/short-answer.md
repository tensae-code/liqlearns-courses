# Two families of planets — rocky inside, giants outside — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are there no small rocky planets far from the Sun and no giants close to it?
**Answer: temperature sorted the material — near the Sun only rock and iron could condense, while far out light gases and ices could also gather, building giants.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which family is richer in hydrogen and helium, and why?
**Answer: the outer giants — far from the young Sun's heat, those light substances were not vaporised and could collect in huge amounts.**
