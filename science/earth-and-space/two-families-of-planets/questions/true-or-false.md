# Two families of planets — rocky inside, giants outside — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Asteroids formed from the same leftover disk matter as the planets.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The outer planets are smaller than the inner planets.
**Answer: False — the outer planets grew much larger.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** One temperature gradient — hot inside, cold outside — explains the two families.
**Answer: True**
