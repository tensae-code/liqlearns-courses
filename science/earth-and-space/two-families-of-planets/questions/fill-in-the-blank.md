# Two families of planets — rocky inside, giants outside — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The four inner planets are called ______ in type: small and rocky.
**Answer: terrestrial (rocky).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The planet family poor in light elements is the ______ family.
**Answer: inner (rocky).**
