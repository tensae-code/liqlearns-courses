# Planetary motion — ellipses and Kepler's laws — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State Kepler's third law and where you met its pattern before.
**Answer: period-squared is proportional to distance-cubed — the same pattern plotted from the data table in Planets by the numbers.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do two nails and a string draw a true ellipse?
**Answer: the nails are the two foci; a string loop kept tight by the pencil holds the total distance to the foci constant as the pencil walks round, which is exactly an ellipse.**
