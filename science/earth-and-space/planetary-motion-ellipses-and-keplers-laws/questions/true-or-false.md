# Planetary motion — ellipses and Kepler's laws — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Copernicus's circles were the final truth about orbit shapes.
**Answer: False — Kepler found the orbits are ellipses.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Equal areas in equal times means a planet's speed is the same all around its orbit.
**Answer: False — it sweeps the same area by moving faster near the Sun and slower far away.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Period-squared is proportional to distance-cubed.
**Answer: True**
