# Planetary motion — ellipses and Kepler's laws — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the marble model, the stars are fixed in clay because —
a) stars are small  b) real stars keep their patterns while planets wander through them  c) clay is heavy  d) the model is indoors
**Answer: b) real stars keep their patterns while planets wander through them.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A planet can be told from a star because the planet —
a) is brighter  b) wanders against the star patterns  c) twinkles more  d) is nearer the Moon
**Answer: b) wanders against the star patterns.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kepler's first law: orbits are —
a) perfect circles  b) ellipses with the Sun at the centre  c) ellipses with the Sun at one focus  d) spirals
**Answer: c) ellipses with the Sun at one focus.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** By the second law, a planet moves fastest when —
a) farthest from the Sun  b) nearest the Sun  c) at the top of its orbit  d) crossing a star
**Answer: b) nearest the Sun.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Eccentricity is computed as —
a) major axis ÷ focus distance  b) focus distance ÷ major-axis length  c) period ÷ distance  d) distance ÷ period
**Answer: b) focus distance ÷ major-axis length.**
