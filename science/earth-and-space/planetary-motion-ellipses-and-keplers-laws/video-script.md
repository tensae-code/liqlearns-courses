# Video script — Planetary motion — walkthrough of the two-nails-and-string ellipse

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). FULL script, as the placement names a video need: a step-by-step walkthrough. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two nails. A loop of string. A pencil. That is all.

Push the two nails into the board. They are the **two foci**.
Loop the string around them. Pull it tight with the pencil.
Now walk the pencil around — slowly — keeping the string **tight** all the way.
The curve you draw is an **ellipse**. The true shape of a planet's path.

Move the nails farther apart. Draw again. The ellipse stretches.
Its **eccentricity** grows — focus distance divided by the major-axis length.
Bring the nails together. The ellipse rounds toward a circle.

And the Sun? The Sun sits at **one focus** — never the centre.
Draw it. Measure it. Now you.
