# Lesson plan — Planetary motion — ellipses and Kepler's laws

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Earth and Space · **Lesson:** Planetary motion — ellipses and Kepler's laws
**Place in the curriculum:** Earth and Space — the title opened by the Grade 7 Earth lessons (CURRICULUM.md). Unit 6 — The Solar System, at General Science Grade 8 depth.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** planets wander against the fixed stars — modelled with marbles, then stated in Kepler's three laws: elliptical orbits with the Sun at one focus, equal areas in equal times, and period-squared proportional to distance-cubed — and the ellipse itself is drawn with two nails, a string loop and a pencil.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Earth-centred or Sun-centred? and Planets by the numbers: the learner has met retrograde motion and the distance–period pattern; this lesson gives both their full working form.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Use the marble model to show retrograde drift and to tell a planet from a star.
2. State Kepler's three laws.
3. Draw a true ellipse with two nails and a string, and compute its eccentricity.
4. Say where the Sun sits in a planet's orbit: at one focus.

## What you need
Marbles and clay for the sky model; two nails, a loop of string, a board and a pencil for drawing ellipses.
