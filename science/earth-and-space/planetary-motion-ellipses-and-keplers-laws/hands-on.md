# Hands-on — Planetary motion — ellipses and Kepler's laws

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Venus watch (teacher evidence — the guide's Project 6.1)
The teacher first finds Venus in the evening or morning sky, tells the class when and where to look, and debriefs what everyone saw the next day. Venus never strays far from the Sun — the model lesson's fact, seen with your own eyes.

## The marble sky (the book's Activity 6.9)
Set star-marbles in clay. Move Earth and planet X month by month, March to September, drawing the sight-line to the stars each month. Watch planet X drift, loop and drift again.

## Two nails and a string (the book's Activity 6.10)
Draw ellipses with two nails, a string loop and a pencil. Change ONE thing at a time — nail separation, then string length — and compute eccentricity = focus distance ÷ major-axis length for each.
