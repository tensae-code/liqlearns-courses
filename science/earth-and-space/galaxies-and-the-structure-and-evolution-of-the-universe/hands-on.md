# Hands-on — Galaxies and the structure and evolution of the Universe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Raisins in the rising dough
You need a bowl of bread dough with raisins folded through it (or a balloon and a marker, if dough is not to hand). Pick one raisin as home. Measure its distance to three neighbours, then let the dough rise (or inflate the balloon part-way) and measure again. Every distance grew, and the farthest neighbour's distance grew most — yet no raisin moved through the dough and there is no centre on the surface. Write Hubble's law in your own words from the measurements: recession speed grows in proportion to distance, because the space itself is stretching.
