# Video script — Galaxies and the structure and evolution of the Universe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **islands of stars, in expanding space**. A galaxy: hundreds of billions of stars bound by gravity — spirals like our Milky Way, smooth ellipticals, ragged irregulars — gathered into groups and clusters. Their light is redshifted, farther meaning faster: Hubble's law. Run the film backwards and the whole Universe lands in a hot, dense beginning 13.8 billion years ago, whose afterglow we still measure. Gravity wrote the rest — clusters, filaments, voids: the cosmic web.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
