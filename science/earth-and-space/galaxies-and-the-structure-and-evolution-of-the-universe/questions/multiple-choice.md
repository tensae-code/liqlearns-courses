# Galaxies and the structure and evolution of the Universe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A galaxy is…
a) A single large star  b) A gravitationally bound system of stars, gas, dust and dark matter  c) A cloud of gas only  d) A cluster of planets
**Answer: b) A gravitationally bound system of stars, gas, dust and dark matter**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) An elliptical galaxy is typically…
a) Rich in gas with many new stars  b) A smooth ball of mostly old stars with little gas  c) Always the smallest kind  d) A spiral seen edge-on
**Answer: b) A smooth ball of mostly old stars with little gas**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Hubble's law states that a galaxy's recession speed…
a) Falls with distance  b) Is the same for all galaxies  c) Grows in proportion to its distance, v = H₀d  d) Depends on its size
**Answer: c) Grows in proportion to its distance, v = H₀d**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The cosmic microwave background is…
a) Light from the Sun's corona  b) The leftover heat of the hot, dense beginning, measurable in every direction  c) Radio noise from satellites  d) Light from the first telescope
**Answer: b) The leftover heat of the hot, dense beginning, measurable in every direction**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The early Universe was…
a) Already web-like  b) Nearly uniform, with slightly denser patches that gravity grew into structure  c) Empty  d) Made only of galaxies
**Answer: b) Nearly uniform, with slightly denser patches that gravity grew into structure**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The expansion of the Universe is now…
a) Stopped  b) Slowing steadily  c) Accelerating  d) Reversing
**Answer: c) Accelerating**
