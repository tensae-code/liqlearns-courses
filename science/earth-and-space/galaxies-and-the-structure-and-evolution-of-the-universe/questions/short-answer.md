# Galaxies and the structure and evolution of the Universe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the galaxy group the Milky Way belongs to and its other giant member.
**Answer: The Local Group; the Andromeda galaxy.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A galaxy shows twice the redshift of another. What does Hubble's law say about the pair?
**Answer: It is about twice as far away and receding about twice as fast.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How old is the Universe on the expansion account, and what measurement supports the beginning?
**Answer: About 13.8 billion years; the cosmic microwave background — the cooled leftover heat of the hot, dense beginning — supports it.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why did structure form at all, if the early Universe was nearly uniform?
**Answer: Slightly denser patches pulled harder by gravity, drawing in more matter and growing into galaxies, clusters and the cosmic web.**
