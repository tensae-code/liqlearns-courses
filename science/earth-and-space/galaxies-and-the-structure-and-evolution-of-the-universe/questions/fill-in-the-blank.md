# Galaxies and the structure and evolution of the Universe — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Milky Way is a _____ galaxy.
**Answer: spiral**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Galaxies gather into groups and _____.
**Answer: clusters**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In Hubble's law, v = H₀ × _____.
**Answer: distance**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Most of the Universe's matter is _____, felt by gravity but never seen.
**Answer: dark**
