# Hands-on — Identifying rocks by colour, grain and hardness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read the marks
Take a collected rock and record its four marks on a card: colour, grain size, texture, and — with adult help — its hardness by a gentle scratch test. Lay the card beside the rock.

## Name it against the set
Using the five-rock set (granite, sandstone, shale, limestone, marble), compare your rock's marks with what you know of each, and give your rock a name with its evidence: 'I name it … because …'.
