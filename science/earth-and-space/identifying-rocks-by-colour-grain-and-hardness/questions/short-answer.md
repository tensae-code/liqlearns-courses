# Identifying rocks by colour, grain and hardness — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the four marks by which rocks are identified.
**Answer:** Colour, grain (the size of the bits it is made of), texture (how it feels and its surface is put together), and hardness.

**2.** Name the book's five-rock identification set.
**Answer:** Granite, sandstone, shale, limestone and marble.

**3.** How does the scratch test help where looking cannot?
**Answer:** It tries hardness: a rock that scratches easily is softer, one that resists is harder — and hardness separates rocks whose colours and grains look alike.

**4.** Why must the identification give evidence, not just a name?
**Answer:** Because the name is a conclusion from the marks: colour, grain, texture and hardness read together are the evidence that this rock is that rock.
