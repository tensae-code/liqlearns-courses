# Identifying rocks by colour, grain and hardness — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** The size of the bits a rock is made of is its _____.
**Answer: grain**

**2.** Hardness is tried by the _____ test.
**Answer: scratch**

**3.** Granite, sandstone, shale, limestone and _____ are the book's set.
**Answer: marble**

**4.** A rock that scratches easily is _____ than one that resists.
**Answer: softer**
