# Identifying rocks by colour, grain and hardness — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A rock's grain is…
a) The size of the bits it is made of  b) Its colour  c) Its weight  d) Its age
**Answer: a) The size of the bits it is made of**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which list is the book's identification set?
a) Only granite  b) Granite, sandstone, shale, limestone, marble  c) Sand, soil and clay  d) Iron, copper and gold
**Answer: b) Granite, sandstone, shale, limestone, marble**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Texture describes…
a) Only its colour  b) Where it was found  c) How a rock feels and how its surface is put together  d) Its price
**Answer: c) How a rock feels and how its surface is put together**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The scratch test tries a rock's…
a) Colour  b) Grain  c) Temperature  d) Hardness
**Answer: d) Hardness**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A rock that scratches easily is…
a) Softer than one that resists scratching  b) Harder  c) Not a rock  d) Younger
**Answer: a) Softer than one that resists scratching**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Identification is complete when the learner…
a) Guesses a name quickly  b) Reads the four marks and names the rock against the set, with evidence  c) Weighs the rock only  d) Paints the rock
**Answer: b) Reads the four marks and names the rock against the set, with evidence**
