# Video script — Identifying rocks by colour, grain and hardness

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Identifying rocks by colour, grain and hardness** — China's way, from its 科学 (Science) textbooks.

Pick up a rock. Before you name it, read it: its colour, its grain, how it feels. Those are its marks.
Still not sure? Scratch it. Hard or soft is a mark too. Four marks together — and the rock has a name, with evidence.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
