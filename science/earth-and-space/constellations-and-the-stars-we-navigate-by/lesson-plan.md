# Lesson plan — Constellations and the stars we navigate by

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Earth and Space · **Lesson:** Constellations and the stars we navigate by
**Place in the curriculum:** Earth and Space — appended to Unit 19 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the constellations and the first stars island children learn — the Southern Cross, the Morning Star and the Evening Star, and Sirius — the stars people know the sky by and navigate by, as Vanuatu's Year 3 guide teaches them in its Term 3 sky lessons
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Constellations — the sky's named patterns** — Stars are learned in patterns: a constellation is a named group of stars, and knowing their names and places is how a learner first reads the sky.
2. **The first stars — the Southern Cross, the Morning and Evening Stars, and Sirius** — Island children learn some stars first: the Southern Cross (Saoten kros), the Morning Star and Evening Star (Moning sta mo Ivening sta), and Sirius, the bright star of the Big Dog — the stars people steer and tell their way by.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the main idea → return to the first sub lesson and the Do-it-first questions: every later part hangs on that frame.
- Mixes this lesson up with a neighbour lesson → compare the two titles and say what each one is about; each lesson here teaches one thing.
- Thinks a constellation is one star → return to sub lesson 1: a constellation is a named pattern of stars, learned together.
- Cannot see why the first stars matter → return to sub lesson 2: they are the stars people find their way by — known stars are the sky's signposts.
