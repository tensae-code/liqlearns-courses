Today: **Constellations and the stars we navigate by** — Vanuatu's way: the Term 3 sky lessons name the stars island children learn first.

The night sky is full of stars, and nobody learns them one by one. We learn them in patterns — constellations — each pattern with its own name.
Some stars are learned first of all. The Southern Cross — Saoten kros. The Morning Star and the Evening Star — Moning sta mo Ivening sta — the bright star seen at morning and at evening. And Sirius, the bright star of the Big Dog.
These are the sky's signposts. People who travel by sea find their way by stars they know — so the first science of the sky is learning their names.
