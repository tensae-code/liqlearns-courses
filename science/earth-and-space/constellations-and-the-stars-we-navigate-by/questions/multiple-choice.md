# Constellations and the stars we navigate by — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A constellation is…
a) One single star  b) A named pattern of stars in the sky  c) A cloud shape  d) A kind of moon
**Answer: b) A named pattern of stars in the sky**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Stars are learned…
a) One by one, counting them all  b) In patterns, by their shapes and names  c) Only from books, never the sky  d) By closing your eyes
**Answer: b) In patterns, by their shapes and names**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The Southern Cross is…
a) A pattern of stars in the southern sky, Saoten kros  b) A star seen only at noon  c) The Moon's other name  d) A boat
**Answer: a) A pattern of stars in the southern sky, Saoten kros**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The Morning Star and the Evening Star are told apart by…
a) Their colour  b) The time they show — near morning, and near evening  c) Their size  d) Nothing; they are never seen
**Answer: b) The time they show — near morning, and near evening**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Sirius is…
a) A dim star nobody can find  b) Among the brightest stars in the sky, in the Big Dog  c) A constellation of the north only  d) The Sun
**Answer: b) Among the brightest stars in the sky, in the Big Dog**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) People learn these first stars because…
a) They are the sky's signposts — people navigate by stars they know  b) They are the only stars there are  c) They never move or change place in the year  d) A book says to, for no reason
**Answer: a) They are the sky's signposts — people navigate by stars they know**
