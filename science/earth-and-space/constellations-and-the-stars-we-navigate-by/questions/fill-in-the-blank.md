# Constellations and the stars we navigate by — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A named pattern of stars in the sky is a _____.
**Answer: constellation**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In Bislama, the Southern Cross is _____ kros.
**Answer: Saoten**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The bright star of the Big Dog is called _____.
**Answer: Sirius**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) People _____ by stars they know — the stars are their signposts.
**Answer: navigate**
