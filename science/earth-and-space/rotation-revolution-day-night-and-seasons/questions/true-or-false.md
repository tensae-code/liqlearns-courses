# The Earth — rotation, revolution, day-night and seasons — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Day and night happen because the Earth rotates.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The seasons happen because the Earth moves much nearer the Sun in summer.
**Answer: False** — the seasons come from the fixed 23.5° tilt leaning the hemispheres toward and away from the Sun during revolution.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Spring in the north is autumn in the south.
**Answer: True** — the in-between seasons are opposite too.
