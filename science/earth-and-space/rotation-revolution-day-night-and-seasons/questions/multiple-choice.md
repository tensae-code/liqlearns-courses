# The Earth — rotation, revolution, day-night and seasons — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rotation is —
a) the Earth's journey around the Sun  b) the Earth spinning on its axis  c) the Moon's journey  d) the axis changing direction
**Answer: b).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** One full rotation takes —
a) 365¼ days  b) 24 hours  c) one month  d) 23.5 hours
**Answer: b) 24 hours.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Earth's axis is tilted at —
a) 90°  b) 45°  c) 23.5°  d) 10°
**Answer: c) 23.5°.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** One revolution takes —
a) 24 hours  b) 365¼ days  c) 30 days  d) 23.5 days
**Answer: b) 365¼ days.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** When the northern hemisphere has summer, the southern hemisphere has —
a) summer too  b) winter  c) spring  d) no season
**Answer: b) winter** — the tilt leans it away from the Sun at the same time.
