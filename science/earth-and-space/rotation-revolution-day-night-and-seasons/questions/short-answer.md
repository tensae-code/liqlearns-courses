# The Earth — rotation, revolution, day-night and seasons — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does every place on Earth get both day and night?
**Answer: because rotation carries each place in turn onto the Sun-facing half (day) and the half facing away (night), once in every 24 hours.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A pupil says: "Summer comes when the Earth is closest to the Sun." Correct them.
**Answer: the seasons come from the tilt, not the distance: with the axis tilted 23.5° the same way all year, revolution leans the northern hemisphere toward the Sun for its summer while the south leans away into winter, then the reverse.**
