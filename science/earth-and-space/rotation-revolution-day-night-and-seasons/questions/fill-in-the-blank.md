# The Earth — rotation, revolution, day-night and seasons — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The face of the Earth turned away from the Sun is in ______.
**Answer: night.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Earth's journey around the Sun is called ______.
**Answer: revolution.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The axis keeps pointing ______ all through the journey.
**Answer: the same way.**
