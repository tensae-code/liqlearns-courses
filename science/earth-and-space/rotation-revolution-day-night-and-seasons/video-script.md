# Video script — The Earth — rotation, revolution, day-night and seasons

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Earth **spins**. Slowly. All the time.
One full spin takes **24 hours**. That spin is **rotation**.
The side facing the Sun has **day**. The other side has **night**.
Spin, day. Spin, night.

The Earth also **travels**. Around the Sun.
One full journey takes **365¼ days**. That journey is **revolution**.

And the Earth leans. Its axis tilts **23.5 degrees**, always the same way.
Leaning toward the Sun: **summer**. Leaning away: **winter**.
North summer, south winter. Then they swap.

Spin makes the day. The journey and the lean make the seasons. Now you.
