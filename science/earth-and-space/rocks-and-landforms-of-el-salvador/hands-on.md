# Hands-on — Rocks and landforms of El Salvador

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's own work
Do the work El Salvador's book sets for this lesson, as the sources describe it: El Salvador's own geology. Record what you did and what it showed.

## Say it and show it
Draw or build the lesson's central thing, label its parts, and explain it to someone at home in your own words.
