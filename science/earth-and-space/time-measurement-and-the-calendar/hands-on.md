# Hands-on — Time measurement and the calendar

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Count your own year
Mark a shadow-stick's noon shadow on the same date each month for a school term (or use last year's photos of the same view at the same clock time): the Sun's noon height marches and returns — the orbit made visible. Beside your marks, write the leap rule and check: is this year a leap year by the Gregorian rule? Your calendar page is astronomy; prove it from your own marks.
