# Time measurement and the calendar — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The unit every clock ultimately inherits is…
**Answer: a) the day — one turn of the Earth  b) the Moon's month  c) the King's decree  d) the pendulum**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The 60-minute hour and 60-second minute come from…
**Answer: a) Babylonian counting in sixties  b) the Romans  c) the Gregorian reform  d) quartz clocks**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The tropical year is about…
**Answer: a) 365.2422 days  b) exactly 365 days  c) 365.5 days  d) 364 days**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The Julian calendar's average year is too long by…
**Answer: a) about 11 minutes — a day every 128 years  b) a day a year  c) an hour a day  d) nothing — it is exact**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Under the Gregorian rule, the year 2100 will…
**Answer: a) not be a leap year  b) be a leap year  c) have 364 days  d) start the calendar again**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A purely lunar calendar…
**Answer: a) drifts through the seasons year by year  b) stays fixed to spring  c) has 365 days  d) was invented in 1582**
