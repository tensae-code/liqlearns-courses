# Time measurement and the calendar — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sunrise to sunrise is the _____ day.
**Answer: solar**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The spring-to-spring year is called the _____ year.
**Answer: tropical**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The 1582 reform that deleted ten days and refined the leap rule is the _____ reform.
**Answer: Gregorian**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Century years are leap only if divisible by _____.
**Answer: 400**
