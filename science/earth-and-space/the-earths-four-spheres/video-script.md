# Video script — The Earth — four spheres, one system

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Earth works in **four spheres**. Four circles, all touching.

**Lithosphere** — the rock. The land, the mountains, the soil.
**Hydrosphere** — the water. Seas, lakes, rivers, even water under the ground.
**Biosphere** — the living things. Plants, animals, you.
**Atmosphere** — the air, all around everything.

Touch one, and the others move.
Cut the forest — the soil washes away.
Smoke in the air — lungs suffer.
Four spheres. **One system**. Now you map your town.
