# The Earth — four spheres, one system — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Glaciers are part of the hydrosphere.
**Answer: True** — ice is water, held solid.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The four spheres are separate: a change in one leaves the others alone.
**Answer: False** — they are interdependent; a change in one changes the others.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A lake evaporating is a change between the hydrosphere and the atmosphere.
**Answer: True.**
