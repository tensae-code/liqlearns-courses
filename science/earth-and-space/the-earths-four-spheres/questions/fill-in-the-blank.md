# The Earth — four spheres, one system — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right sphere name.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ______ is the sphere of air around the Earth.
**Answer: atmosphere.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil belongs to the ______.
**Answer: lithosphere.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** When a dam is built, the river it holds back belongs to the ______.
**Answer: hydrosphere.**
