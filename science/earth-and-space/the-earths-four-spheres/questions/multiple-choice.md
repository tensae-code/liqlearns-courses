# The Earth — four spheres, one system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Groundwater belongs to the —
a) lithosphere  b) hydrosphere  c) biosphere  d) atmosphere
**Answer: b) hydrosphere** — all the Earth's water, under the ground too.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The lithosphere is —
a) all living things  b) the air  c) rock, landforms and soil  d) the oceans
**Answer: c).**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The biosphere holds —
a) all living things  b) only people  c) only plants  d) only animals
**Answer: a) all living things.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Cutting a forest leads to soil erosion because —
a) trees make rain  b) roots that held the soil are gone, so rain washes the soil away  c) soil dislikes shade  d) animals leave
**Answer: b)** — a biosphere change changing the lithosphere.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Smoke harming people's lungs is a change passing from —
a) hydrosphere to lithosphere  b) atmosphere to biosphere  c) biosphere to atmosphere  d) lithosphere to hydrosphere
**Answer: b).**
