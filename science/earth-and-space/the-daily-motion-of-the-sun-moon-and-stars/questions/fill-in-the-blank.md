# The daily motion of the Sun, Moon and stars — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Earth rotates once in about _____ hours.
**Answer: 24**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The Sun rises in the _____ and sets in the west.
**Answer: east**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Each night the Moon is found further _____ against the stars than the night before.
**Answer: east**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Long-exposure photographs of the night sky show stars as arcs called star _____.
**Answer: trails**
