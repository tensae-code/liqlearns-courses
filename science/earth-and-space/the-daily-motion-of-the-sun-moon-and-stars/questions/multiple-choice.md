# The daily motion of the Sun, Moon and stars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Sun appears to cross the sky east to west because…
a) The Sun orbits the Earth daily  b) The Earth rotates west to east once in about 24 hours  c) The sky is a turning dome  d) The wind pushes it
**Answer: b) The Earth rotates west to east once in about 24 hours**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) At local noon in the northern hemisphere, the Sun stands…
a) Due east and lowest  b) Highest in the sky, due south  c) Due west  d) Below the horizon
**Answer: b) Highest in the sky, due south**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The Moon rises roughly how much later each day, and why?
a) It does not change  b) About 50 minutes later, because it has stepped eastward in its own orbit  c) Three hours later, because it spins  d) Ten minutes earlier, because the Earth slows
**Answer: b) About 50 minutes later, because it has stepped eastward in its own orbit**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The Moon's changing lit face is called its…
a) Trail  b) Phases  c) Arc  d) Pole
**Answer: b) Phases**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Star-trail photographs show arcs centred on…
a) The Moon  b) The celestial pole, near the Pole Star in the northern hemisphere  c) The horizon  d) The Sun
**Answer: b) The celestial pole, near the Pole Star in the northern hemisphere**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Stars close to the celestial pole…
a) Set at midnight  b) Never set — they circle the pole  c) Rise in the west  d) Only appear in summer
**Answer: b) Never set — they circle the pole**
