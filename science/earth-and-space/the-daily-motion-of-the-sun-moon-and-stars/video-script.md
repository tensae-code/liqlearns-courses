# Video script — The daily motion of the Sun, Moon and stars

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the daily parade of the sky**. The Sun climbs from the east and sets in the west, the Moon and stars wheel the same way all night — but nothing up there is doing the travelling. The Earth itself is spinning, west to east, once a day, and we ride it under the sky. The Moon adds one trick of its own: each night it has stepped a little further east, so it rises later and shows a new face.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
