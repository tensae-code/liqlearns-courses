# Landforms and geological features of our island — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does the Grade 5 book put first when it teaches the island's geology?
**Answer: The island as a whole and its mountain systems — the joined ranges of high ground that run across Hispaniola.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) How is a mountain system different from one mountain?
**Answer: A system is a whole family of peaks, ridges and valleys formed together and standing together.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name three landforms and say where each sits.
**Answer: Any three of: high ground (the mountain systems), slopes (between high and low), low ground, and the flat land near the coasts.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book teach geology from the learner's own island?
**Answer: Because the island's own mountain systems and features are the geology the learner can see and point to — the country's own way into the subject.**
