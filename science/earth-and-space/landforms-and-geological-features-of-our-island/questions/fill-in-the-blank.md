# Landforms and geological features of our island — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Dominican Republic shares the island of _____.
**Answer: Hispaniola**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Long joined ranges of high ground are called mountain _____.
**Answer: systems**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The shapes of the Earth's surface are called _____.
**Answer: landforms**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) High ground, slopes and coastal flats are geological _____.
**Answer: features**
