# Landforms and geological features of our island — multiple choice


**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The island the Dominican Republic shares is…
a) Cuba  b) Hispaniola  c) Puerto Rico  d) Jamaica
**Answer: b) Hispaniola**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A mountain system is…
a) One single peak  b) A volcano only  c) A whole joined family of peaks, ridges and valleys  d) A flat plain
**Answer: c) A whole joined family of peaks, ridges and valleys**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The Grade 5 book reads the island…
a) Only by its rivers  b) Geologically — by its mountain systems and features  c) Only by its weather  d) By its cities
**Answer: b) Geologically — by its mountain systems and features**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Landforms are…
a) Kinds of cloud  b) The shapes of the Earth's surface  c) Kinds of rock only  d) Sea waves
**Answer: b) The shapes of the Earth's surface**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is a landform? 
a) A slope between high and low ground  b) A seismograph  c) A nutrient  d) A circuit
**Answer: a) A slope between high and low ground**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Landforms are geological features because they are…
a) Drawn on maps  b) What the island's long history of movement and wearing has left standing  c) Always flat  d) Made by people
**Answer: b) What the island's long history of movement and wearing has left standing**
