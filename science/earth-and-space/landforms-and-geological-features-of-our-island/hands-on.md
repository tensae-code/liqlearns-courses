# Hands-on — Landforms and geological features of our island

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read your own ground
Walk to a place where you can see both high and low ground. Name what you see in the lesson's words — mountain system, slope, low ground, coast — and sketch the view, labelling each landform.

## The island in clay or sand
With clay, mud or wet sand, build a model island: mountain systems as long joined ridges across it, low ground and a flat coast around them. Point to each system and say what makes it one system.
