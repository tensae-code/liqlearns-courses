# The Sun — its scale, layers, spots and storms — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Sun looks small only because it is small.
**Answer: False — it is huge; apparent size shrinks with distance.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The corona reaches millions of kilometres from the Sun.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The whole Sun rotates in the same time everywhere.
**Answer: False — the equator takes about 25 days, the poles about 35.**
