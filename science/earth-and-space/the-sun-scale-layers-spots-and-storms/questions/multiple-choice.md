# The Sun — its scale, layers, spots and storms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Sun's diameter is about —
a) 10 Earth diameters  b) 108 Earth diameters  c) 1,000 Earth diameters  d) equal to Earth's
**Answer: b) 108 Earth diameters.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Sun holds about what share of the solar system's mass?
a) 50%  b) 75%  c) 98%  d) 10%
**Answer: c) 98%.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Sun's visible light-giving layer is the —
a) corona  b) chromosphere  c) photosphere  d) core
**Answer: c) photosphere.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Sunspots come and go on a cycle of about —
a) 1 year  b) 10–11 years  c) 100 years  d) 25 days
**Answer: b) 10–11 years.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Sun's equator rotates in about —
a) 35 days  b) 25 days  c) 365 days  d) 24 hours
**Answer: b) 25 days.**
