# The Sun — its scale, layers, spots and storms — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the Sun's gravity hold the whole system in orbit?
**Answer: it holds 98% of the system's mass, so its gravitational pull is by far the strongest — everything else falls around it.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are solar flares and prominences?
**Answer: prominences are huge arching loops of gas rising from the surface (some blasting material out at 600–1,000+ km/s); flares are sudden bright bursts — both driven by the magnetic fields around sunspots.**
