# Video script — The Sun — walkthrough of the two scale activities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). FULL script, as the placement names a video need: a step-by-step walkthrough. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Chalk in hand. Outside. We measure the Sun without touching it.

First: the chalk circles. Draw a small circle. That is Earth.
Now — walk. And walk. The Sun's circle is **one hundred and eight times wider**.
Stand back. THAT is the Sun's true size against Earth's.

Second: the football and the grain. Hold the ball close — it fills your view.
Walk away. Farther. Farther. It shrinks to a grain.
The ball did not change. The **distance** changed what you see.
That is why the huge Sun looks like a small disk in the sky.

Big — and far. Both true. Now you measure.
