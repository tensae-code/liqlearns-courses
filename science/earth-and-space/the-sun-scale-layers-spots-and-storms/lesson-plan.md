# Lesson plan — The Sun — its scale, layers, spots and storms

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Earth and Space · **Lesson:** The Sun — its scale, layers, spots and storms
**Place in the curriculum:** Earth and Space — the title opened by the Grade 7 Earth lessons (CURRICULUM.md). Unit 6 — The Solar System, at General Science Grade 8 depth.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Sun is 108 Earth-diameters wide and holds 98% of the system's mass; its air layers run photosphere, chromosphere, corona — and its face carries sunspots, arching prominences and flares.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the model lessons before it: the learner knows the Sun sits at the centre; this lesson measures the centre itself.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier Grade 8 Earth and Space lessons in this title, in unit order. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Give the Sun's diameter as 108 Earth diameters and its mass share as 98%.
2. Explain why the Sun looks small: apparent size shrinks with distance.
3. Name the photosphere, chromosphere and corona in order.
4. Describe sunspots, prominences and flares, with the spot cycle and the two rotation times.

## What you need
Chalk, open ground, a football and a grain of rice or sand for the scale activities.
