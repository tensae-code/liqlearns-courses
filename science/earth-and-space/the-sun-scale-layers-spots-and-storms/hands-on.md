# Hands-on — The Sun — its scale, layers, spots and storms

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Chalk circles (the book's Activity 6.4)
Chalk an Earth circle 10 cm wide on the ground. Now pace out the Sun's circle at the same scale — 108 times wider is nearly 11 metres across. Stand in it: that is the scale.

## Football and grain (the book's Activity 6.5)
Hold a football and a grain of sand. Walk away from a companion holding the ball until the ball looks as small as the grain held near your eye. Measure the distance — distance is what shrinks the Sun to a disk.
