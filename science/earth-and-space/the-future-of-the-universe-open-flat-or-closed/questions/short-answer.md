# The future of the universe — open, flat or closed — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: If the universe's average density is below the critical density it is open and galaxies recede forever; if equal, flat, with expansion slowing to a stop; if above, closed, shrinking back to a point in a great contraction.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Shikshak Nirdeshika Vigyan Grade 10 (guide)*, printed p. 396.**
