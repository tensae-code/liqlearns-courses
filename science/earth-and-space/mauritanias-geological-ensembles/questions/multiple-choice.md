# Mauritania's geological ensembles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A geological ensemble is…
a) A single rock  b) A great region of rock sharing one character and history  c) A mine  d) A fossil
**Answer: b) A great region of rock sharing one character and history**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Mauritania's ensembles are taught in…
a) Grade 3  b) Grade 11, série D  c) Grade 6  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: b) Grade 11, série D**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The ensembles lesson stands between…
a) Volcanoes and earthquakes  b) The geological eras and Mauritania's mining resources  c) Rivers and seas  d) Plants and animals
**Answer: b) The geological eras and Mauritania's mining resources**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) After the ensembles, the book teaches…
a) The country's mining resources and its oil and gas  b) Cooking fuels  c) The weather  d)  microscopy
**Answer: a) The country's mining resources and its oil and gas**
