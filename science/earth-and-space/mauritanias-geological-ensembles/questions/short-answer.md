# Mauritania's geological ensembles — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is a geological ensemble?
**Answer:** A vast region of a country's ground sharing one geological character and history — the regional frame from which smaller studies of that ground begin.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the book's teaching order around this lesson.
**Answer:** The geological eras first (the Earth's long ages), then Mauritania's own geological ensembles (this country's ground), then what that ground holds — its mining resources and its oil and gas.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Why teach a country's own geology in its own book?
**Answer:** Because the learner's land is the ground every other lesson must be placed on: knowing its ensembles lets the learner locate the country's minerals, water and fuels in the frame that holds them.
