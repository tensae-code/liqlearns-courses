# Video script — Mauritania's geological ensembles


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


Today: **Mauritania's geological ensembles** — a country reading its own ground.

An ensemble is geology at the scale of a whole country: a great region of rock sharing one character and one history. Mauritania's Grade 11 book sorts the national ground into its great ensembles — the frame for everything else.

The order teaches: first the geological eras, the Earth's long ages; then Mauritania's own ground; then the mining resources and oil and gas that ground holds. World time first, home ground second, treasure third.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
