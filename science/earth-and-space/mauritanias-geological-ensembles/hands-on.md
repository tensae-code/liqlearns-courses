# Hands-on — Mauritania's geological ensembles

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ensemble sketch
On a blank outline map of Mauritania (or your own country), shade the great regions differently and write for each what kind of ground it is — old foundation or younger basin — as the lesson's frame describes.

## Place the resources
On the same map, mark where the book's next lessons turn: mining resources and oil and gas. Say, for each mark, which ensemble's ground it sits in and why that order — ensembles first — matters.
