# Hands-on — Patterns of Earth's features

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Plot the pattern
On an outline world map, mark a band where continents meet oceans around the Pacific. Shade it and label it: most earthquakes and volcanoes occur in bands like this.

## Chains, not peaks
On the same map, draw two mountain chains — one inside a continent, one near an edge — as lines, not single peaks, the way the pattern runs.
