# The lunar month — sidereal and synodic — and Nepal's calendar — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: The sidereal month of 27⅓ days, one true circuit of the Earth, against the synodic lunar month of 29.5 days from new moon to new moon; the bright fortnight (Shukla Paksha) and the dark fortnight (Krishna Paksha); the solar year of 365.25 days against the lunar year of 354.37 days, and the leap year; Adhik Maas, the doubled lunar month that falls about every three years (as in Asoj BS 2077), and Kshaya Maas, the lunar month that drops out, mostly between Kartik and Magh — the reckoning by which festivals are kept.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science and Technology Grade 7*, printed pp. 244–246.**
