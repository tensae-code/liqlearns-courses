distances in space: the solar system holds together (eight planets and other bodies in elliptical orbits) because of the sun's huge gravity; kilometres are too small inside it, so astronomers use the astronomical unit — the average earth–sun distance, 150 million km — worked through a body 3 units away at 450,000,000 km
