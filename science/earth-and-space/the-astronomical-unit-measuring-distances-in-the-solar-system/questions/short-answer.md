# The astronomical unit — measuring distances in the solar system — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Distances in space.
**Answer:** distances in space: the solar system holds together (eight planets and other bodies in elliptical orbits) because of the sun's huge gravity; kilometres are too small inside it, so astronomers use the astronomical unit — the average earth–sun distance, 150 million km — worked through a body 3 units away at 450,000,000 km.

**Answer:** Distances in space — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
