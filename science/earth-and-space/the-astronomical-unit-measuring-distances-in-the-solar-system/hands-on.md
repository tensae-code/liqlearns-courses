# Hands-on — The astronomical unit — measuring distances in the solar system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: distances in space: the solar system holds together (eight planets and other bodies in elliptical orbits) because of the sun's huge gravity; kilometres are too small inside it, so astronomers use the astronomical unit — the average earth–sun distance, 150 million km — worked through a body 3 units away at 450,000,000 km Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
