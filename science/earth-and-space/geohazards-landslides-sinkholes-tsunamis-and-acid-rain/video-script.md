Today: **Geohazards — landslides, sinkholes, tsunamis and acid rain** — Malaysia's way, from its book (Malaysia, Grade 7 (Form 1) — *Sains Tingkatan 1*, printed p. 265).

Malaysia's Form 1 book names the **geohazard phenomena**: **landslides, sinkholes (tanah jerlus in swamp areas), tsunamis, and acid rain in industrial areas** — hazards that rise from the Earth's own processes and from what people do to the air and land.

Against the hazards the book sets **science and technology: early-warning instruments for landslides and tsunamis** — watching the Earth so people can be warned before the hazard strikes.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
