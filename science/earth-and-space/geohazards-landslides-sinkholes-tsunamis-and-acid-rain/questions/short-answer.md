# Geohazards — landslides, sinkholes, tsunamis and acid rain — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Geohazard phenomena.
**Answer:** Malaysia's Form 1 book names the **geohazard phenomena**: **landslides, sinkholes (tanah jerlus in swamp areas), tsunamis, and acid rain in industrial areas** — hazards that rise from the Earth's own processes and from what people do to the air and land.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Science and technology for facing geohazards.
**Answer:** Against the hazards the book sets **science and technology: early-warning instruments for landslides and tsunamis** — watching the Earth so people can be warned before the hazard strikes.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Geohazard phenomena → Science and technology for facing geohazards — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
