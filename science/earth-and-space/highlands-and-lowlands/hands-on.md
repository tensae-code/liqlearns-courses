# Hands-on — Highlands and lowlands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the views
A partner describes views — a farm on flat ground by the sea, a bare plateau high above the farms, a town in a low valley, sheep country on high slopes. Sort each view: highland or lowland, and say why.

## Two-level map
Draw a simple side view of a country: sea, lowland, then highland rising behind it. Label the two lands and the line where one becomes the other.
