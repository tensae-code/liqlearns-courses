# Video script — Highlands and lowlands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Highlands and lowlands** — Iceland's way, from its Grade 3 Earth chapter on the volcanic island.
Low ground near the sea is lowland. The raised country above it is highland.
One country, two lands, told apart by a single thing: height above the sea.
Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
