# Highlands and lowlands — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Key ideas) This lesson's first key idea, in the lesson's own words, begins: "Lowland is low ground" — write the whole sentence out from memory.
**Answer: Lowland is low ground — flat or gently rolling land down near the sea, small in height above it.**

**2.** (Key ideas) This lesson's third key idea, in the lesson's own words, begins: "A country's land sorts by height" — write the whole sentence out from memory.
**Answer: A country's land sorts by height: lowland below, highland above — knowing the difference is the lesson.**
