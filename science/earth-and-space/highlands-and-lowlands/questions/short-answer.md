# Highlands and lowlands — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Lowland is low ground — flat or gently rolling land down near the sea, small in height above it.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Highland is high ground — the raised country above the lowland.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What single thing tells highland from lowland?
**Answer:** Height — how high the ground stands above the sea. Low ground is lowland; the raised country above it is highland.
