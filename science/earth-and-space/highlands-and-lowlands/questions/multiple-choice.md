# Highlands and lowlands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which sentence gives this lesson's first key idea?
a) Lowland is low ground — flat or gently rolling land down near the sea, small in height above it.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Lowland is low ground — flat or gently rolling land down near the sea, small in height above it.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson opens by asking the learner to…
a) Memorise the key ideas first  b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation  c) Skip to the questions  d) Copy the lesson out
**Answer: b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's second key idea?
a) Highland is high ground — the raised country above the lowland.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Highland is high ground — the raised country above the lowland.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's third key idea?
a) Something from a different lesson  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) A country's land sorts by height: lowland below, highland above — knowing the difference is the lesson.  d) None of these
**Answer: c) A country's land sorts by height: lowland below, highland above — knowing the difference is the lesson.**
