# Hands-on — Ulugbek's astronomical school

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A noon-mark arc
On a sunny day, mark where a vertical stick's shadow tip falls at local noon, and repeat for a week: the noon shadow shortens and lengthens with the seasons. Measure the stick and its noon shadow and compute the Sun's noon height as a ratio. Ulugbek's arc was this, perfected: the same shadow, a 40-metre scale, and a school writing down every day. Your week's marks are the method in miniature.
