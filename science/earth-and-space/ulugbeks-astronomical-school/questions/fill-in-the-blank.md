# Ulugbek's astronomical school — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ulugbek was the grandson of _____.
**Answer: Timur**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) On a 40-metre arc, an arc-_____ is over a centimetre — size is precision.
**Answer: minute**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The star catalogue is called the Zij-i _____.
**Answer: Sultani**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The trench of the great arc was rediscovered in _____.
**Answer: 1908**
