# Ulugbek's astronomical school — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ulugbek's observatory stood in…
**Answer: a) Samarkand  b) Bukhara  c) Istanbul  d) Baghdad**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The observatory's heart was…
**Answer: a) a giant meridian arc of about 40 m radius  b) a telescope  c) a water clock  d) a dome of mirrors**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Ulugbek's star catalogue lists…
**Answer: a) 1,018 stars, measured afresh  b) 101 stars  c) only the planets  d) the stars of Greece, copied**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The school measured the year as about…
**Answer: a) 365 days 6 hours 10 minutes — within a minute of the modern value  b) exactly 360 days  c) 365 days even  d) 366 days 2 hours**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) After Ulugbek's death in 1449, his student Ali Qushji…
**Answer: a) carried the school's tables to Istanbul  b) burned the catalogue  c) built a telescope  d) closed the madrasa**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The observatory was a school, meaning…
**Answer: a) astronomers lived, taught, computed and argued there  b) it was only a monument  c) pupils visited once a year  d) it taught no one**
