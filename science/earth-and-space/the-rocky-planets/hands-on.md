# Hands-on — The rocky planets — Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Four planet cards
Make a card for each rocky planet: name, one drawing, three facts. Shuffle them, then lay them in order from the Sun and read each card aloud.

## Greenhouse jar
Put two thermometers (or two hands) — one in open air, one under a clear jar in the sun. The jarred one warms more: a small taste of how Venus's thick air traps heat.
