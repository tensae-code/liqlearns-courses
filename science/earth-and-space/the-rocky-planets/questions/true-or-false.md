# The rocky planets — Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mercury has a thick atmosphere borrowed from the solar wind.
**Answer: False — it has effectively no atmosphere; its trace gases are borrowed from the solar wind.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Earth is the only planet with abundant liquid water and life.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mars's red colour comes from iron oxide in its soil.
**Answer: True**
