# The rocky planets — Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mercury's 3 km cliffs are read as —
a) old river valleys  b) wrinkles from its shrinking iron core  c) volcano rims  d) cracks from impacts only
**Answer: b) wrinkles from its shrinking iron core.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mercury's daytime temperature reaches about —
a) 100 °C  b) 425 °C  c) 475 °C  d) −170 °C
**Answer: b) 425 °C.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Venus is hotter than Mercury because —
a) it is nearer the Sun  b) its carbon-dioxide air traps the heat — a strong greenhouse effect  c) it spins faster  d) it has volcanoes everywhere
**Answer: b) its carbon-dioxide air traps the heat — a strong greenhouse effect.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Earth's distance from the Sun is —
a) 1 AU — 150 million km  b) 0.4 AU  c) 10 AU  d) 150 thousand km
**Answer: a) 1 AU — 150 million km.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Olympus Mons, the system's largest volcano, is on —
a) Venus  b) Earth  c) Mars  d) Mercury
**Answer: c) Mars.**
