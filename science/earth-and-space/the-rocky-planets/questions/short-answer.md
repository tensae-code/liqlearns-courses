# The rocky planets — Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does Mercury swing from 425 °C to −170 °C while Venus stays hot?
**Answer: Mercury has almost no atmosphere to hold or move heat, so its face bakes and freezes in turn; Venus's thick carbon-dioxide air traps heat everywhere, all the time.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What evidence suggests water once flowed on Mars?
**Answer: channels that look like old water-carved valleys, and frozen water held in its polar caps today.**
