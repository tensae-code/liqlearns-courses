# The Moon — Earth's natural satellite and its phases — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What holds the Moon on its path round the Earth?
**Answer: Earth's gravity. The pull balances the Moon's motion so it keeps circling — neither flying away into space nor falling to Earth.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is moonlight so much gentler than sunlight?
**Answer: Moonlight is sunlight reflected off grey rock and dust — a poor mirror. Most of the light is lost in the bouncing, so what reaches us is weak and gentle.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Put the four main phases in order, starting from new moon.
**Answer: New moon (dark face toward us), then crescent (a sliver of the lit half), then half moon, then full moon (the whole lit face toward us) — then half, crescent and new again.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) How long does the whole cycle of phases take, and what calendar idea comes from it?
**Answer: About 29½ days from new moon to new moon. The calendar month is built on the Moon's cycle — 'month' and 'Moon' are the same old word.**
