# The Moon — Earth's natural satellite and its phases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Moon is called Earth's natural satellite because…
a) It was launched long ago  b) It circles the Earth, and nobody built or launched it  c) It is made of light  d) It stands still
**Answer: b) It circles the Earth, and nobody built or launched it**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) One full journey of the Moon round the Earth takes about…
a) One day  b) Seven days  c) 27 days  d) One year
**Answer: c) 27 days**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Moonlight really is…
a) Light the Moon makes itself  b) Sunlight reflected off the Moon's rocky surface  c) Light from the stars  d) Earth's light
**Answer: b) Sunlight reflected off the Moon's rocky surface**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) At any moment, how much of the Moon is lit by the Sun?
a) All of it  b) None of it  c) Exactly half — the half facing the Sun  d) A quarter
**Answer: c) Exactly half — the half facing the Sun**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) At new moon, we see nothing because…
a) The Moon has left its path  b) The Moon is between Earth and Sun, so we face its dark side  c) The Sun is switched off  d) Clouds always cover it
**Answer: b) The Moon is between Earth and Sun, so we face its dark side**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The phases of the Moon are caused by…
a) The Moon changing shape  b) Our changing view of its sunlit half as it circles the Earth  c) Earth's shadow every night  d) The Moon spinning like a top
**Answer: b) Our changing view of its sunlit half as it circles the Earth**
