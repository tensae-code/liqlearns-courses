# The Moon — Earth's natural satellite and its phases — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A body that circles a bigger body is called a _____.
**Answer: satellite**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The Moon shines by _____ sunlight.
**Answer: reflecting**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) When the whole lit face of the Moon faces us, it is a _____ moon.
**Answer: full**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The changing faces of the Moon are called its _____.
**Answer: phases**
