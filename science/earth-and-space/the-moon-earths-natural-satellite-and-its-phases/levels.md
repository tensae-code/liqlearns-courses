# The Moon — Earth's natural satellite and its phases — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner states what a natural satellite is and the Moon's key facts (about 27 days to circle Earth, rocky, airless, held by gravity), proves with ball and lamp that exactly half the Moon is always sunlit, and walks the phase cycle — new, crescent, half, full and back in about 29½ days — explaining each phase by the Moon's position, answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — doing the lesson's own experiments and sorts by hand, naming each idea aloud, and reasoning about new examples until the teaching is the learner's own.
