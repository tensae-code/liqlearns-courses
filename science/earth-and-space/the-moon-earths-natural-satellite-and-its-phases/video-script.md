Today: **The Moon**.

The Moon wears many faces — a sliver, a half, a full round disc. Is it changing? No. The Moon is Earth's natural satellite, a rocky world circling us once a month, held by gravity. It makes no light of its own: the Sun lights exactly half of it, always the half facing the Sun, and moonlight is that sunlight reflected to us. As the Moon travels round the Earth, we see more or less of its lit half — dark new moon, growing crescent, half, blazing full moon, then back again in 29½ days. The Moon never changes. Our view does.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
