# Hands-on — The Moon — Earth's natural satellite and its phases

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Head, ball and lamp
In a dark room, hold a ball at arm's length and turn slowly with a lamp at one side. Watch the ball's lit part grow and shrink exactly like the Moon's phases. Say aloud: the ball never changed — my view did.

## Shadow theatre of phases
Draw the Sun, Earth and Moon in four positions (new, crescent/growing, full, crescent/shrinking) on paper with the lit halves shaded. Say aloud: shade tells the story — the lit half always faces the Sun.
