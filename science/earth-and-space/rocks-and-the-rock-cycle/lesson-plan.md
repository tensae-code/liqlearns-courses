**The one thing this lesson teaches:** rocks are solid structures made of crystallised minerals (some of glass or solidified organic remains) and are classified by origin into igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic.

## Sub lessons
1. **Rocks are solid structures made of crystallised minerals (some** — rocks are solid structures made of crystallised minerals (some of glass or solidified organic remains) and are classified by origin into igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic.
2. **Igneous rocks form when magma cools** — igneous rocks form when magma cools: intrusive rocks cool slowly underground and grow large crystals (granite), extrusive rocks cool fast on the surface and grow small crystals (basalt); magma that reaches the surface is called lava.
3. **Sedimentary rocks form by lithification** — sedimentary rocks form by lithification: weathering breaks rock into sediments, wind and water carry them to low areas, and accumulating layers are compacted and cemented under pressure.
