# Rocks and the rock cycle — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Honduras's books teach about rocks are solid structures made of crystallised minerals (some?
**Answer: rocks are solid structures made of crystallised minerals (some of glass or solidified organic remains) and are classified by origin into igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic.**

**2.** What do Honduras's books teach about igneous rocks form when magma cools?
**Answer: igneous rocks form when magma cools: intrusive rocks cool slowly underground and grow large crystals (granite), extrusive rocks cool fast on the surface and grow small crystals (basalt); magma that reaches the surface is called lava.**

**3.** What do Honduras's books teach about sedimentary rocks form by lithification?
**Answer: sedimentary rocks form by lithification: weathering breaks rock into sediments, wind and water carry them to low areas, and accumulating layers are compacted and cemented under pressure.**

**4.** Name this lesson's one thing.
**Answer: Rocks and the rock cycle.**
