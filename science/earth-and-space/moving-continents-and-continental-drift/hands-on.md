# Hands-on — Moving continents and continental drift

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The torn page (paper and pencil)
Draw one big joined land shape on a page. Tear the page into three or four continent pieces, then drift the pieces slowly apart on the table. Lay them back together and say what the fit tells you — then drift them apart again and draw today's separated map.

## Drift in slow motion (two books on a table)
Stand two books edge to edge. Move one a fingernail's width while a partner counts slowly to sixty. Say how far it went, then say what the same slowness adds up to over spans of time no one can watch.
