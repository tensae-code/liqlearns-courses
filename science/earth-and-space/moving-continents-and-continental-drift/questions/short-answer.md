# Moving continents and continental drift — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Moving continents — the ground itself travels.
**Answer:** The continents are not fixed to the Earth like pins in a board.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Continental drift — continents out of place.
**Answer:** The name for that movement is **continental drift**: the continents have drifted — travelled slowly — from earlier positions to the ones on today's map.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Australia's order — drift first, then plates.
**Answer:** Australia's own way is the order: moving continents and drift first (10.1), plate movements second (10.2), volcanoes and earthquakes last (10.3).

**Answer:** Moving continents — the ground itself travels → Continental drift — continents out of place → Australia's order — drift first, then plates — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
