## Sub lesson 1 — Folds — rock layers bent

The chapter starts from the plates (Lesson 1): the Earth's crust is carried on moving plates, and where plates press, the rock layers caught between them are squeezed. Rock that is squeezed slowly **folds** — the layers bend into waves, up-folds and down-folds, without breaking. A fold is the crust answering pressure by bending.

## Sub lesson 2 — Faults — rock layers broken and shifted

Where the pressure is too great, or the rock too hard, the crust answers the other way: it **faults** — the layers crack and the blocks on either side of the crack shift, up, down or sideways. Folds and faults are the two answers of one crust to the plates' push, and the chapter reads them together before it goes on to volcanoes (Lesson 3) and earthquakes (Lesson 4) — the sudden answers of the same moving ground.
