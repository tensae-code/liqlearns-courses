# Hands-on — Faults and folds in the Earth's crust

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Bend it, break it
Press a stack of soft cloth layers slowly from both ends and watch them fold. Then push a stiff cracker until it snaps and shifts — a fault. Say which crust answer each model shows, and why.

## The chapter in order
Write Earth Ch. 2's lessons in order — plates, faults and folds, volcanoes, earthquakes — and one line on what each adds. Check against the lesson.
