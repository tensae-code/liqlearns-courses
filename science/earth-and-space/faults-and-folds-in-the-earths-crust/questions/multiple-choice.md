# Faults and folds in the Earth's crust — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A fold is…
a) rock layers bent by pressure without breaking  b) a crack in the crust  c) a volcano  d) a kind of mineral
**Answer: a) rock layers bent by pressure without breaking** — Squeezed layers bend into up-folds and down-folds.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A fault is…
a) the crust cracked, with blocks shifted along the crack  b) bent layers  c) a river bend  d) a cloud shape
**Answer: a) the crust cracked, with blocks shifted along the crack** — Too much pressure, or too-hard rock: the crust breaks and shifts.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Folds and faults are both answers to…
a) the plates' pressure on the crust  b) the wind  c) the tides  d) the seasons
**Answer: a) the plates' pressure on the crust** — One crust, one push — two ways of answering.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The chapter teaches this lesson straight after…
a) plate tectonics  b) the Moon  c) food chains  d) electricity
**Answer: a) plate tectonics** — Earth Ch. 2: Lesson 1 plates, Lesson 2 faults and folds.

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The lessons that follow in the chapter are…
a) volcanoes, then earthquakes  b) seasons, then soil  c) plants, then animals  d) circuits, then magnets
**Answer: a) volcanoes, then earthquakes** — Lessons 3 and 4 — the sudden answers of the same moving ground.

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Up-folds and down-folds are shapes of…
a) folded rock layers  b) fault blocks  c) clouds  d) waves at sea
**Answer: a) folded rock layers** — The bent layers wave upward and downward.
