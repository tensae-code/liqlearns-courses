# Faults and folds in the Earth's crust — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words, how a fold forms and how a fault forms.
**Answer:** Under the plates' pressure, rock layers squeezed slowly bend into folds; where the pressure is too great or the rock too hard, the layers crack into a fault and the blocks shift along the crack.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book teach faults and folds between plate tectonics and volcanoes?
**Answer:** Because they are the crust's quiet answers to the plates' push — the chapter builds from the plates, through bending and breaking, to the sudden answers, volcanoes and earthquakes.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Pressed rock layers bend into folds — up-folds and down-folds — without breaking → pressed too hard, the layers crack into faults and the blocks shift — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
