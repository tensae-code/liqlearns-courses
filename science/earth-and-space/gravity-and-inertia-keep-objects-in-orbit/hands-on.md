# Hands-on — Gravity and inertia keep objects in orbit

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ball-and-string model
Whirl a soft ball on a string in a clear space. Feel the inward pull in your hand (gravity's part) and notice the ball's urge to fly straight when released (inertia's part). Say which is which.

## Draw the balance
Draw a planet, its star, a straight arrow for inertia's onward path and a curved arrow for gravity's inward pull; combine them into the orbit line.
