Today: **Plate tectonics and shaping the land** — Canada's way: the land is shaped from below, at the surface, and by people's long care.

British Columbia was built by tectonic plates. The Juan de Fuca plate slides under the coast — subduction — and volcanism belongs to that same slow building. Ice and water then rework the surface: glaciation and erosion. And a moving land shakes: earthquakes, tsunamis, landslides and flooding — so we learn the hazards and prepare for them.
But plates are not the only shapers. For thousands of years, First Peoples shaped this land by care: clam gardens at the shore, shell middens where people lived, and burning done on purpose to manage the landscape. The land you see is geology and care together.
