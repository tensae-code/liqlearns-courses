# Lesson plan — Plate tectonics and shaping the land

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Earth and Space · **Lesson:** Plate tectonics and shaping the land
**Place in the curriculum:** Earth and Space — appended to Unit 19 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** how the land is shaped — built from below by tectonic plates and reworked by ice, water and people's long care — Canada's two Science First Peoples guides teach it as one thing: the Grades 5–9 guide's Shaking and Flooding unit (a plate-built land and its hazards) and the Secondary guide's Shaping the Land unit (landscapes shaped by millennia of First Peoples land management)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Shaking and flooding — how plates build and shake the land** — British Columbia was built by tectonic plates: one great piece of Earth's outer shell slides under another — the Juan de Fuca plate subducting — and that slow building is still at work in volcanism, and is reworked at the surface by glaciation and erosion; the same moving ground brings earthquakes, tsunamis, landslides and flooding, so the unit closes on preparedness — knowing the hazards of a plate-built land and being ready for them.
2. **Shaping the land by care — clam gardens, middens and burning** — landscapes are also shaped by people over thousands of years: clam gardens built at the shore, shell middens laid down where people lived and ate, and landscape burning done on purpose — millennia of land management that the Secondary guide studies as the way the land was shaped.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the main idea → return to the first sub lesson and the Do-it-first questions: every later part hangs on that frame.
- Mixes this lesson up with a neighbour lesson → compare the two titles and say what each one is about; each lesson here teaches one thing.
- Thinks plates only build mountains far away → return to sub lesson 1: the Juan de Fuca plate is subducting off this coast now, and its hazards are local.
- Thinks people never shape land → return to sub lesson 2: clam gardens, shell middens and landscape burning are shaping done by care, over millennia.
