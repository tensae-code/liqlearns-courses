# Plate tectonics and shaping the land — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Earth's ground is made of great pieces called tectonic _____.
**Answer: plates**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Glaciation and _____ rework the surface the plates raised.
**Answer: erosion**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Deep shell layers where people lived and ate for generations are shell _____.
**Answer: middens**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Clam gardens, middens and burning together are millennia of land _____.
**Answer: management**
