# Plate tectonics and shaping the land — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How is British Columbia still being built and shaken today?
**Answer: The Juan de Fuca plate is still subducting off the coast, volcanism belongs to that plate building, and the moving ground brings earthquakes, tsunamis, landslides and flooding.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does the unit end with preparedness rather than with the hazards themselves?
**Answer: Because the hazards belong to the land people live on — knowing which hazards a plate-built land carries, and being ready for them before they come, is part of the science.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the three kinds of shaping by care and say what each one is.
**Answer: Clam gardens — shore places built and tended so clams grow well; shell middens — deep shell layers laid down where people lived and ate over generations; landscape burning — fire used deliberately to manage what grows.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the lesson's whole point when the two sub lessons are read together?
**Answer: The land is shaped from below by plates, at the surface by ice and water, and by people's long care over millennia — and a science of the land has to hold all three.**
