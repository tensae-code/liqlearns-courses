# Plate tectonics and shaping the land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) British Columbia was built by…
a) Wind alone  b) Tectonic plates meeting  c) Rivers digging it out of flat land  d) People piling up soil
**Answer: b) Tectonic plates meeting**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Subduction is…
a) Ice melting  b) A volcano erupting  c) One plate sliding under another  d) A wave crossing the ocean
**Answer: c) One plate sliding under another**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The plate subducting off the BC coast is…
a) The Pacific plate named for a river  b) The Juan de Fuca plate  c) The ice plate  d) The midden plate
**Answer: b) The Juan de Fuca plate**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The Shaking and Flooding unit closes on…
a) Map drawing  b) Preparedness for earthquakes, tsunamis, landslides and flooding  c) Volcano names  d) Shell collecting
**Answer: b) Preparedness for earthquakes, tsunamis, landslides and flooding**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A clam garden is…
a) A garden of flowers shaped like clams  b) A shore place built and tended so clams grow well  c) A shell museum  d) A fishing boat
**Answer: b) A shore place built and tended so clams grow well**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Landscape burning, as the Secondary guide teaches it, is…
a) An accident to study after it happens  b) Fire damage with no purpose  c) A wildfire warning system  d) Fire used deliberately to manage what grows on the land
**Answer: d) Fire used deliberately to manage what grows on the land**
