# Hands-on — Plate tectonics and shaping the land

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read your own ground
Walk your street or picture the nearest hill, valley, shore or river flat. List every mark of shaping you can find or remember — a slope cut by water, a shore built up, a bank that slips after rain — and beside each one write which shaper made it: the plates below, ice, water, or people's hands.

## The preparedness card
Write a card for your home: the four hazards of a plate-built land from the lesson — earthquake, tsunami, landslide, flooding. Beside each, write one thing a family does to be ready for it. Keep the card where the family keeps its important papers.

## Draw the coast in section
Draw the coast in cross-section: the ocean plate sliding under the land plate (subduction), a volcano above the sliding place, ice and water reworking the surface, and at the shore a clam garden wall. Label every part and every shaper.
