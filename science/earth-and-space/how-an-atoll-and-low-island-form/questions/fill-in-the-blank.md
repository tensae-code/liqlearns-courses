# How an atoll and low island form — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A ring reef is called an _____.
**Answer: atoll**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Inside the reef ring lies a sheltered _____.
**Answer: lagoon**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The island is built from piles of coral and _____ sand.
**Answer: shell**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Low islands are narrow and _____.
**Answer: flat**
