# How an atoll and low island form — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is an atoll?
**Answer: A ring of coral reef in the sea, holding a sheltered lagoon inside its ring — the foundation on which a low island is built.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Tell how a low island forms, in order.
**Answer: A ring reef (atoll) grows in the sea; piles of coral and shell sand gather on the ring and rise above the water; those piles grow into narrow, flat islands.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How is a low island's building different from a high island's?
**Answer: A low island is built up from reef and sand piled on a reef ring; a high island is built by lava from an underwater volcano rising above the sea.**
