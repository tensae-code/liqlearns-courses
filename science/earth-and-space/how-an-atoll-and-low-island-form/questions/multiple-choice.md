# How an atoll and low island form — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A low island begins with…
a) A volcano erupting on land  b) A ring reef — an atoll  c) A river delta  d) A glacier
**Answer: b) A ring reef — an atoll**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Inside an atoll's ring lies…
a) A mountain  b) A sheltered lagoon  c) A taro pit  d) A cloud forest
**Answer: b) A sheltered lagoon**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The piles that rise on the ring are made of…
a) Lava only  b) Coral and shell sand  c) River mud only  d) Ice
**Answer: b) Coral and shell sand**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Low islands are…
a) High and mountainous  b) Narrow and flat  c) Round and rocky  d) Covered in cloud forest
**Answer: b) Narrow and flat**
