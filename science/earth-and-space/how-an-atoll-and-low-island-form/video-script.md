Today: **How an atoll and low island form** — Micronesia's way: an island built of reef and sand.

First comes a ring of coral reef in the sea — an atoll — with a calm lagoon inside it. Then the sea piles coral and shell sand on the ring, higher and higher, until the piles stand out of the water. Those piles grow into islands: narrow, flat strips of land, lagoon on one side, ocean on the other.
