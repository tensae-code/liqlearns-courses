# Hands-on — How an atoll and low island form

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build a ring island
In a tray of water, make a ring of stones or packed wet sand for the reef. Add spoonfuls of loose sand on one part of the ring until a small strip rises above the water. Say which part of your model is the atoll, which is the lagoon, and which is the low island.

## Flat against high
Draw two islands side by side: a high island (a mountain rising from the sea) and a low island (a narrow flat strip on a reef ring). Label what each is made of and how each was built, from this lesson and from the high-island way you know.
