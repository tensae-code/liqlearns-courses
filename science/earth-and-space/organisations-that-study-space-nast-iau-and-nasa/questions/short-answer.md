# Organisations that study space — NAST, IAU and NASA — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: NAST, Nepal's own Academy of Science and Technology (established BS 2039, its four objectives, and its planned National Space Research Centre); the IAU (founded 1919; the body that defined planet and dwarf planet and reclassified Pluto and Ceres in 2006); and NASA (founded 1958; the 1969 Moon landing, the space station, the Parker Solar Probe, the Perseverance rover, Hubble and the James Webb telescope)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science and Technology Grade 9 (Nepali, revised 2082)*, printed pp. 203–205.**
