# Continents and oceans of the Earth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Earth's great land masses are called…
a) Seas  b) Continents  c) Islands only  d) Currents
**Answer: b) Continents**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The oceans are…
a) The great water masses, covering most of the Earth's surface  b) Small lakes  c) Only the water near Cuba  d) Frozen land
**Answer: a) The great water masses, covering most of the Earth's surface**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Are lands and waters equally distributed on the planet?
a) Yes, exactly half and half  b) No — the land is larger  c) No — the waters are the larger share  d) Nobody can tell
**Answer: c) No — the waters are the larger share**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) An island is…
a) A small ocean  b) A kind of current  c) A sea's deepest part  d) Land surrounded by water
**Answer: d) Land surrounded by water**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Seas are…
a) Waters at the continents' edges, smaller than oceans  b) Lands surrounded by water  c) The same as continents  d) Frozen oceans
**Answer: a) Waters at the continents' edges, smaller than oceans**
