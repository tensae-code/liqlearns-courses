# Continents and oceans of the Earth — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Earth's great land masses are the _____.
**Answer: continents**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The great water masses are the _____.
**Answer: oceans**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Land surrounded by water is an _____.
**Answer: island**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Waters at the continents' edges, smaller than oceans, are _____.
**Answer: seas**
