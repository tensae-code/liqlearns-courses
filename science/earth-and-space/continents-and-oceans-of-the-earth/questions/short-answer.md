# Continents and oceans of the Earth — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is a continent?
**Answer:** One of the Earth's great land masses — every country stands on a continent.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is an ocean, and how much of the Earth do the oceans cover?
**Answer:** An ocean is one of the great water masses; together the oceans cover most of the Earth's surface — the larger share.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Tell an island from a sea.
**Answer:** An island is land surrounded by water; a sea is water at a continent's edge, smaller than an ocean.
