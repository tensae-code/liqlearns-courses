# Video script — Continents and oceans of the Earth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Continents and oceans of the Earth** — Cuba's way.

The Earth's land is gathered into continents: the great land masses.

Facing the continents stand the oceans: the great water masses that cover most of the Earth's surface.

Between the great masses sit the smaller pieces: islands — land surrounded by water, Cuba itself among them — and seas — the waters at the continents' edges, smaller than oceans.

Now you. How does this lesson sort the Earth's surface? Say it back — in your own words.
