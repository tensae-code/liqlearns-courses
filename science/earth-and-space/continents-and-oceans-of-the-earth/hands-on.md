# Hands-on — Continents and oceans of the Earth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort a globe by eye
Turn a globe or a world map slowly and sort what you see out loud: continent, ocean, island or sea — name each piece as it passes, and say which kind it is and why.

## Your own island in the frame
Cuba is an island in a sea, at the edge of an ocean, near a continent. Draw that frame from memory — continent, ocean, sea, island — and place your own home inside the same frame.
