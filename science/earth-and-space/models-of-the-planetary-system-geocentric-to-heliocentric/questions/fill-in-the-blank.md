# Models of the planetary system — geocentric to heliocentric — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the geocentric model, the _____ stands still at the centre of everything.
**Answer: Earth**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A planet's backward loop against the stars is called _____ motion.
**Answer: retrograde**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Ptolemy saved the geocentric picture with the _____: a small circle riding on a larger circle.
**Answer: epicycle**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) In Copernicus's model the Earth circles the Sun once in one _____ .
**Answer: year**
