# Models of the planetary system — geocentric to heliocentric — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the geocentric model, what stands at the centre of everything?
a) The Sun  b) The Earth, still and motionless, with the Moon, Sun, planets and stars circling it  c) Mars  d) Nothing — the model has no centre
**Answer: b) The Earth, still and motionless, with the Moon, Sun, planets and stars circling it**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The geocentric model lasted so long mainly because…
a) Nobody ever looked at the sky  b) It was forbidden to question it  c) It matched what the senses show and predicted the sky well for over a thousand years  d) It was the simplest possible picture
**Answer: c) It matched what the senses show and predicted the sky well for over a thousand years**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Retrograde motion is…
a) A planet speeding up  b) A planet's temporary backward loop against the fixed stars  c) The Moon's monthly path  d) The daily rising of the Sun
**Answer: b) A planet's temporary backward loop against the fixed stars**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Ptolemy's epicycle was…
a) A small circle, carrying the planet, whose centre travels a larger circle round the Earth — circles on circles that reproduce the backward loops  b) A telescope  c) A new planet  d) The Earth's yearly path
**Answer: a) A small circle, carrying the planet, whose centre travels a larger circle round the Earth — circles on circles that reproduce the backward loops**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In Copernicus's heliocentric model, the daily rising and setting of the Sun and stars is caused by…
a) The sphere of the fixed stars wheeling round daily  b) The Sun circling the Earth  c) The Moon's motion  d) The Earth spinning on its axis once a day
**Answer: d) The Earth spinning on its axis once a day**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In the heliocentric model, a planet's backward loop happens because…
a) The planet really reverses its path  b) The faster Earth overtakes the slower outer planet, so it seems to drift backward against the far stars — no epicycle needed  c) An epicycle carries it backward  d) The Sun pulls it back
**Answer: b) The faster Earth overtakes the slower outer planet, so it seems to drift backward against the far stars — no epicycle needed**
