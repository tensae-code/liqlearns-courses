# Video script — Comparing the planets — build-along for the scale model (teacher evidence)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). FULL script, as the placement names a video need: a step-by-step walkthrough. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

String. Beads. Balls. And one long line. We build the whole solar system to scale.

First — **fix the scale**. One step stands for one set distance.
Say it aloud. Everyone uses the SAME scale. That is what makes it a model.

The Sun goes here — the big ball. Now walk Mercury. A bead, close in.
Venus. Earth. Mars. Still close — these four are neighbours.
Now walk. And walk. **Jupiter** is far. Saturn farther. Uranus. Neptune.
Feel the gaps. The outer system is mostly empty road.

Sizes too: bead, bead, bead — then the big balls.
One model. Every comparison lives in it: distance, size, position, behaviour.
Walk it once more, and name each planet as you pass. Now you.
