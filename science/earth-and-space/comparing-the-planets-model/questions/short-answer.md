# Comparing the planets — a model of distance, size and behaviour — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why fix the scale before placing any planet?
**Answer: without one agreed scale for distance and size, the spacings and sizes mean nothing and cannot be compared; the scale is what makes the model evidence.**
