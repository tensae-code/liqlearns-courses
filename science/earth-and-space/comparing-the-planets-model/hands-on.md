# Hands-on — Comparing the planets — a model of distance, size and behaviour

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Playground scale model
On the playground, mark the Sun, agree the scale as a class, and space the planets along a string at their scaled distances, each pupil holding their planet's scaled size. Photograph or sketch the line.

## String and bead model
Indoors: one bead on a knotted string per planet, knots at the scaled distances. Roll it up, carry it out, and unroll it to show the whole system in a corridor.
