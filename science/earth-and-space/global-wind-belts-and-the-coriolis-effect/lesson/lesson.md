## Sub lesson 1 — Global air currents

global air currents: the sun's rays fall vertically on the equator and slanting on the poles, so equatorial air heats more, rises and moves poleward while cold polar air sinks equatorward; the earth's rotation bends the moving air right in the northern hemisphere and left in the southern — the Coriolis effect
