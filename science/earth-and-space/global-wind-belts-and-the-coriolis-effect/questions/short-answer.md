# Global wind belts and the Coriolis effect — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Global air currents.
**Answer:** global air currents: the sun's rays fall vertically on the equator and slanting on the poles, so equatorial air heats more, rises and moves poleward while cold polar air sinks equatorward; the earth's rotation bends the moving air right in the northern hemisphere and left in the southern — the Coriolis effect.

**2.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Global air currents — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
