# Hands-on — Designing a space colony — choosing a planet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: real-life inquiry closing the chapter: the space colony — choose a planet, study its surface conditions (solid or gas; hot or cold; a thick, thin or absent atmosphere), and design on paper a colony in which people could live on it, drawing every part and explaining how it keeps the colony alive Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
