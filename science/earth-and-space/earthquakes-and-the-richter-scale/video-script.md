Today: **Earthquakes and the Richter scale** — Costa Rica's way, from its Grade 8 Ciencias, Código 80008 (printed p. 120).

An **earthquake** — a **sismo** — is a sudden shaking of the ground: rock below the surface moves, and the shaking spreads through the ground to the surface above.

Earthquakes are **classified** — sorted — so that one quake can be compared with another: by their strength, the shaking they cause, and the effects they leave.

The **Richter scale** is the scale that measures an earthquake's **magnitude** — its size as a number. A quake's Richter number lets any two earthquakes, anywhere, be compared on the one scale.
