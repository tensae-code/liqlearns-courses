# Earthquakes and the Richter scale — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Earthquakes — phenomena that alter the geosphere.
**Answer:** An **earthquake** — a **sismo** — is a sudden shaking of the ground: rock below the surface moves, and the shaking spreads through the ground to the surface above.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Classifying earthquakes.
**Answer:** Earthquakes are **classified** — sorted — so that one quake can be compared with another: by their strength, the shaking they cause, and the effects they leave.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: The Richter scale — measuring magnitude.
**Answer:** The **Richter scale** is the scale that measures an earthquake's **magnitude** — its size as a number. A quake's Richter number lets any two earthquakes, anywhere, be compared on the one scale.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Earthquakes — phenomena that alter the geosphere → Classifying earthquakes → The Richter scale — measuring magnitude — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
