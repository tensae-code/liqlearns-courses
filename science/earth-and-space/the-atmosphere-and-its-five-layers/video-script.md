# Video script — The Earth — the atmosphere and its five layers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Breathe in. What did you breathe?
Mostly **nitrogen** — about **78** parts in every 100.
Then **oxygen** — about **21**. The part your body uses.
A little **argon**. A tiny part **carbon dioxide**. Traces of other gases.

Now climb. The air is stacked in **five layers**.
**Troposphere** — your layer. Clouds and weather live here.
**Stratosphere** — planes cruise in its lower part, above the weather.
**Mesosphere** — its top is the coldest place in the air: about **−90 degrees**.
**Thermosphere**. Then **exosphere**, thinning into space.

Five layers, one blanket of air. Now you climb it.
