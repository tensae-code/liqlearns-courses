# The Earth — the atmosphere and its five layers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The biggest share of the air is —
a) oxygen  b) nitrogen  c) carbon dioxide  d) argon
**Answer: b) nitrogen** — about 78%.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Oxygen makes up about —
a) 78%  b) 0.03%  c) 21%  d) 50%
**Answer: c) 21%.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The layer we live in is the —
a) stratosphere  b) troposphere  c) mesosphere  d) exosphere
**Answer: b) troposphere.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Planes cruise in the lower part of the —
a) troposphere  b) mesosphere  c) stratosphere  d) thermosphere
**Answer: c) stratosphere** — above the weather.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The coldest place in the atmosphere, about −90 °C, is the top of the —
a) mesosphere  b) troposphere  c) exosphere  d) stratosphere
**Answer: a) mesosphere.**
