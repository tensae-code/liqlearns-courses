# The Earth — the atmosphere and its five layers — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Carbon dioxide is about 0.03% of the air.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Weather happens in the stratosphere.
**Answer: False** — weather happens in the troposphere, the layer we live in; planes cruise above it in the stratosphere.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The boundary at the top of the troposphere is called the tropopause.
**Answer: True.**
