# The Earth — the atmosphere and its five layers — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Besides nitrogen, oxygen, argon and carbon dioxide, the air holds traces of neon, helium, ______ and xenon.
**Answer: krypton.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The outermost of the five layers is the ______.
**Answer: exosphere.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The boundary between the stratosphere and the mesosphere is the ______.
**Answer: stratopause.**
