# The celestial sphere and sky coordinates — declination and right ascension — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The sky's equator, Earth's equator projected outward, is the _____ equator.
**Answer: celestial**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The great circle through the poles and the point overhead is the _____.
**Answer: meridian**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Declination is the sky's equivalent of _____ on Earth.
**Answer: latitude**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A full circle of right ascension is _____ hours.
**Answer: 24**
