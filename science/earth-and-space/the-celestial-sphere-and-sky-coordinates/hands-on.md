# Hands-on — The celestial sphere and sky coordinates — declination and right ascension

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find your meridian
On a clear evening, face south (in the northern hemisphere) and sweep your arm overhead from north to south: that arc is your meridian's path across the sky. Say aloud: a star is highest when it crosses this line.

## Pole star anchor
Find Polaris (the end of the Little Bear's handle, found from the Big Dipper's pointer stars). Note it stays put while nearby stars wheel. Say aloud: the sky turns about the celestial pole, and Polaris sits almost on it.
