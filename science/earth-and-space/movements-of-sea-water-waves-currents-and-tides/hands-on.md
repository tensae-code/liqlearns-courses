# Hands-on — Movements of sea water — waves, currents and tides

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three movements in a basin
Fill a wide basin with water. Make waves by blowing across the surface. Make a current by stirring steadily in one direction at one corner and watching a floating leaf travel. Then raise and lower one end of the basin gently and watch the water line climb and retreat at the other end — your tide. Name each movement as you make it.

## Read the shore
If you can visit a shore (or recall one), look for the tide's line: the band of sand, shells or weed that marks where the water reached. Say what left it there, in the lesson's words.
