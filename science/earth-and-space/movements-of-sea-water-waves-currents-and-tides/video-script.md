# Video script — Movements of sea water — waves, currents and tides

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Movements of sea water — waves, currents and tides** — Cuba's way.

The sea's surface rises and falls in waves: the movement you can see.

Below and beyond the waves, the sea water itself travels in currents: great movements of water through the ocean, carrying water — and what floats in it — from place to place across the sea..

At the shore, the sea rises and falls in tides: the water line climbs the beach and then retreats, in a regular coming and going.

Now you. What are the sea's three movements? Say it back — in your own words.
