# Movements of sea water — waves, currents and tides — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Where do waves move?
**Answer:** On the sea's surface — it rises and falls in waves that travel and break at the shore.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How is a current different from a wave?
**Answer:** A wave is the surface rising and falling; a current is the sea water itself travelling through the ocean from place to place.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What does the tide do at the shore?
**Answer:** It makes the sea rise and fall regularly — the water line climbs the beach and then retreats.
