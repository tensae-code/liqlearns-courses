# Movements of sea water — waves, currents and tides — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The sea's surface rises and falls in _____.
**Answer: waves**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Great movements of water travelling through the sea are called _____.
**Answer: currents**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) At the shore the sea rises and falls in _____.
**Answer: tides**
