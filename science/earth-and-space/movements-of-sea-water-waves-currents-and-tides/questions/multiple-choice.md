# Movements of sea water — waves, currents and tides — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Waves are the movement of…
a) Only the deep sea  b) The sea's surface, rising and falling  c) The sand  d) The Moon only
**Answer: b) The sea's surface, rising and falling**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Currents are…
a) Great movements of water travelling through the sea  b) Only surface ripples  c) Frozen water  d) Beach sand moving
**Answer: a) Great movements of water travelling through the sea**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Tides are…
a) Waves in a storm only  b) Currents stopping  c) The sea rising and falling at the shore, regularly  d) Rivers flowing inland
**Answer: c) The sea rising and falling at the shore, regularly**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Together, waves, currents and tides are…
a) Three kinds of fish  b) Three kinds of cloud  c) Three sea colours  d) The movements of sea water
**Answer: d) The movements of sea water**
