# Video script — Oceanography — the world ocean

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Oceanography — the world ocean** — Malaysia's way, from its book (Malaysia, Grade 11 (Form 5) — *Sains Tambahan Tingkatan 5*, printed p. 164).

Malaysia's Additional Science Form 5 book opens oceanography with **early ocean exploration and its purposes** — the earliest explorers named are **Ibn Battuta, Christopher Columbus and Vasco da Gama** — and sets out **the main oceans on the world map and the main seas of Southeast Asia**. The **ocean floor's structure** is sketched and tabulated zone by zone, and **mapping the ocean floor** is taught with what seafloor maps give science. Continental drift and plate tectonics are linked to how the ocean floor evolves.

The book's physical oceanography plots **ocean depth against water temperature** and identifies the **surface temperatures of the Pacific, Atlantic and Southern Oceans**. **Pressure and density differences in seawater** are followed into the living world: the **adaptations of creatures in the ocean's dark zone**. Ocean life itself is classified by **size, mobility and location** — pleuston and the other groups. The chapter closes with **careers in oceanology** and a design task: invent a product solving an ocean problem.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
