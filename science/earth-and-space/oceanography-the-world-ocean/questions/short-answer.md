# Oceanography — the world ocean — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The oceans, their exploration and the ocean floor.
**Answer:** Malaysia's Additional Science Form 5 book opens oceanography with **early ocean exploration and its purposes** — the earliest explorers named are **Ibn Battuta, Christopher Columbus and Vasco da Gama** — and sets out **the main oceans on the world map and the main seas of Southeast Asia**. The **ocean floor's structure** is sketched and tabulated zone by zone, and **mapping the ocean floor** is taught with what seafloor maps give science. Continental drift and plate tectonics are linked to how the ocean floor evolves.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Seawater — temperature, pressure, density — and ocean life.
**Answer:** The book's physical oceanography plots **ocean depth against water temperature** and identifies the **surface temperatures of the Pacific, Atlantic and Southern Oceans**. **Pressure and density differences in seawater** are followed into the living world: the **adaptations of creatures in the ocean's dark zone**. Ocean life itself is classified by **size, mobility and location** — pleuston and the other groups. The chapter closes with **careers in oceanology** and a design task: invent a product solving an ocean problem.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The oceans, their exploration and the ocean floor → Seawater — temperature, pressure, density — and ocean life — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
