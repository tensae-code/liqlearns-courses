# Video script — Space junk and satellites

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Space junk and satellites** — Iceland's way, from its Grade 3 aims sheet, which opens space study with the debris problem.
Satellites are machines people launched. They circle the Earth and work there.
But broken pieces, dead machines and leftovers circle too. That crowd is space debris — space junk.
Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
