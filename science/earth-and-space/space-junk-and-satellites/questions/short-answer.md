# Space junk and satellites — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Satellites are machines people launched that circle the Earth and work there.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Space debris (space junk) is the broken, dead and leftover things that stay circling the Earth.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Is a working satellite space junk?
**Answer:** No — space junk is the broken, dead and leftover things; a working satellite is a machine still doing its job.
