# Space junk and satellites — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which sentence gives this lesson's first key idea?
a) Satellites are machines people launched that circle the Earth and work there.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Satellites are machines people launched that circle the Earth and work there.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson opens by asking the learner to…
a) Memorise the key ideas first  b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation  c) Skip to the questions  d) Copy the lesson out
**Answer: b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's second key idea?
a) Space debris (space junk) is the broken, dead and leftover things that stay circling the Earth.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Space debris (space junk) is the broken, dead and leftover things that stay circling the Earth.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's third key idea?
a) Something from a different lesson  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Iceland opens space study with the debris problem — it is one of the first aims of its Grade 3 space chapter.  d) None of these
**Answer: c) Iceland opens space study with the debris problem — it is one of the first aims of its Grade 3 space chapter.**
