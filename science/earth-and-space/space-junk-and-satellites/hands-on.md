# Hands-on — Space junk and satellites

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the crowd
Draw the Earth. Around it draw three working satellites and three pieces of space junk. Label which is which and say out loud how you told them apart.

## Junk or working?
A partner names things that circle the Earth — a weather satellite, a broken rocket piece, a dead machine, a leftover tool. Sort each one: working machine or space debris. Stand by each sorting out loud.
