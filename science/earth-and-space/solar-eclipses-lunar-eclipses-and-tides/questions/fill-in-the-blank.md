# Solar eclipses, lunar eclipses and tides — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The dark core of a shadow is the _____; the lighter fringe is the penumbra.
**Answer: umbra**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A solar eclipse can only happen at _____ Moon.
**Answer: new**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A lunar eclipse can only happen at _____ Moon.
**Answer: full**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) At the quarter Moons the tidal range is smallest — these are _____ tides.
**Answer: neap**
