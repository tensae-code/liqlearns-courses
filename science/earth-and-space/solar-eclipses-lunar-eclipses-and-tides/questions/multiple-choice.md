# Solar eclipses, lunar eclipses and tides — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A solar eclipse happens when…
**Answer: a) the Moon passes between the Sun and the Earth, casting its shadow on the Earth  b) the Earth passes between the Sun and the Moon  c) the Moon enters the Earth's shadow  d) the Sun passes behind the Moon's dark side permanently**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Inside the umbra of the Moon's shadow an observer sees…
**Answer: a) a total solar eclipse  b) a partial solar eclipse  c) a lunar eclipse  d) an ordinary day**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A totally eclipsed Moon looks copper-red because…
**Answer: a) sunlight bent through the Earth's atmosphere, robbed of its blue, lights the shadow  b) the Moon glows with its own heat  c) the shadow is made of red light  d) city lights reflect onto it**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A lunar eclipse is visible from…
**Answer: a) the whole night side of the Earth  b) a narrow track only  c) one city at a time  d) only the equator**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The two tidal bulges exist because the Moon's gravity…
**Answer: a) pulls the near side of the Earth harder than the centre, and the centre harder than the far side  b) pulls only on water, not on land  c) reverses twice a day  d) pushes the oceans sideways**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Spring tides — the biggest tidal range — happen at…
**Answer: a) new and full Moon, when Sun and Moon pulls line up  b) the quarter Moons  c) lunar eclipses only  d) midday**
