# Video script — Solar eclipses, lunar eclipses and tides

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **one alignment, three shows**. New Moon: the Moon's narrow shadow races across the Earth — total eclipse inside the umbra track. Full Moon: the Earth's broad shadow swallows the Moon, turning it copper-red for the whole night hemisphere. And the Moon's uneven pull stretches the oceans into two bulges — two high tides a day, biggest at spring tides, at those same new and full alignments.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
