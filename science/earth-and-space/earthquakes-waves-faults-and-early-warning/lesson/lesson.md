## Sub lesson 1 — Seismic parameters

Seismic parameters; how P, S, Love and Rayleigh waves propagate and which destroy most; geological faults in El Salvador; early-warning systems and forecasting by impact.

## Sub lesson 2 — Earthquakes

Earthquakes: the 13 January 2001 magnitude-7.8 quake that destroyed many municipalities; geological faults as fractures of the crust with appreciable displacement, whose rupture releases the energy; seismic waves and how their propagation forms relate to surface effects; El Salvador's seismic sources, above all the Cocos–Caribbean subduction zone; the country's historic quakes (San Vicente 1936, Jucuapa–Chinameca 1951, the 1982 offshore quake) and its tsunami history, including the 1902 tsunami not born of a quake; seismic-alert technology as implemented in Mexico.
