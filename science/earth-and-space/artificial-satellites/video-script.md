Today: **Artificial satellites** — Sri Lanka's way, from the books cited in the sources.

An artificial satellite is a machine people have made and placed in orbit around the Earth — artificial, because it is built, not born like the Moon, the Earth's natural satellite.

Sri Lanka's books give satellites their uses: they watch the weather, carry communications and broadcasts, help with navigation and mapping, and observe the Earth and space.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
