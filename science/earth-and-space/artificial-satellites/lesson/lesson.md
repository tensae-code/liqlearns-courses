# Artificial satellites

## Sub lesson 1 — Artificial satellites — machines in orbit

An **artificial satellite** is a machine people have made and placed in **orbit** around the Earth — artificial, because it is built, not born like the Moon, the Earth's natural satellite. It stays up by travelling fast enough to keep falling around the Earth, and it is powered and directed from the ground and by its own instruments.

## Sub lesson 2 — What artificial satellites are used for

Sri Lanka's books give satellites their **uses**: they watch the weather, carry communications and broadcasts, help with navigation and mapping, and observe the Earth and space. The 1986 Tamil book already ends its sky unit with them — a Sri Lankan classroom has met satellites beside the Moon and the stars for decades.
