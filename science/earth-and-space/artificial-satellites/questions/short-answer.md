# Artificial satellites — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Artificial satellites — machines in orbit.
**Answer:** An **artificial satellite** is a machine people have made and placed in **orbit** around the Earth — artificial, because it is built, not born like the Moon, the Earth's natural satellite. It stays up by travelling fast enough to keep falling around the Earth, and it is powered and directed from the ground and by its own instruments.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: What artificial satellites are used for.
**Answer:** Sri Lanka's books give satellites their **uses**: they watch the weather, carry communications and broadcasts, help with navigation and mapping, and observe the Earth and space. The 1986 Tamil book already ends its sky unit with them — a Sri Lankan classroom has met satellites beside the Moon and the stars for decades.
