# Hands-on — The Earth — shape, evidence and dimensions

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## The bottle-cap ship
Put a bottle cap or a small toy boat on a big ball — a football will do. Slide it slowly away from your eye over the top of the ball, keeping your eye low and still. Watch which part you lose first: the bottom, then the top. That is the hull-first evidence, in your hands.

## The round shadow
In a dark room, hold a ball between a lamp and a wall. Turn the ball slowly. The shadow stays round from every side. Now hold a flat plate the same way and turn it: the shadow changes shape. Only the ball's shadow is always round — like the Earth's shadow on the Moon.

## String around the ball
Wrap a string around the ball's middle and mark where it meets; then wrap it over the top and bottom and mark again. Measure both marks against a ruler. On a slightly squashed ball (press it gently as you wrap), the middle string is longer — the bulge and the flattening you can feel.
