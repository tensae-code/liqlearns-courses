# The Earth — shape, evidence and dimensions — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two evidences from the longer list (teacher evidence) that are not among the student book's four.
**Answer: any two of: circumnavigation (sailing right around the world), the curved horizon, sunrise/sunset at different times in different places, poles driven on level ground, the changing altitude of the sun, different constellations at different latitudes.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The equatorial circumference is 40,075 km and the polar circumference is 40,007 km. How big is the difference, and what does it show?
**Answer: 68 km; it shows the equator bulges out beyond the flattened polar circle — the Earth is not a perfect sphere.**
