# The Earth — shape, evidence and dimensions — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The equator way around the Earth is longer than the pole way around.
**Answer: True** — 40,075 km against 40,007 km.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The polar diameter is longer than the equatorial diameter.
**Answer: False** — the polar diameter (12,713.6 km) is shorter; the Earth is flattened at the poles.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ancient Chinese pictured the Earth as a flat square under round heavens. (teacher evidence)
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Photographs from space are one of the four evidences for the Earth's shape.
**Answer: True.**
