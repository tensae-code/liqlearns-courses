# The Earth — shape, evidence and dimensions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Earth's true shape is —
a) a perfect sphere  b) a sphere slightly flattened at the poles and bulged at the equator  c) a flat disc  d) a cube
**Answer: b)** — an oblate sphere.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The equatorial circumference is —
a) 40,007 km  b) 12,756.4 km  c) 40,075 km  d) 12,713.6 km
**Answer: c) 40,075 km** — the way around the bulging middle (Table 6.1).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The polar diameter is —
a) 12,756.4 km  b) 12,713.6 km  c) 40,007 km  d) 40,075 km
**Answer: b) 12,713.6 km** — pole to pole, the shorter way through (Table 6.1).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A ship sailing away disappears —
a) all at once  b) mast first  c) hull first, mast last  d) from the middle
**Answer: c)** — the sea curves away beneath it.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In a lunar eclipse, the Earth's shadow on the Moon is —
a) square  b) round-edged  c) a straight line  d) invisible
**Answer: b)** — only a sphere casts a round shadow from every direction.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Distant places keeping different times is evidence because —
a) clocks are badly made  b) different faces of a turning sphere meet the Sun at different times  c) people wake at different times  d) the Sun moves around the Earth
**Answer: b).**
