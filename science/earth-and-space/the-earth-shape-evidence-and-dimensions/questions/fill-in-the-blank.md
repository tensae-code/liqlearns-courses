# The Earth — shape, evidence and dimensions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A sphere flattened at the poles and bulged at the equator is called an ______ sphere.
**Answer: oblate.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The polar circumference is ______ km.
**Answer: 40,007.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A departing ship vanishes ______ first and ______ last.
**Answer: hull; mast.**
