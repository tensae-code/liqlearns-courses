# Video script — The Earth — shape, evidence and dimensions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Earth is not flat. It is a **ball** — a sphere.
A sphere a little **flattened** at the top and bottom. A little **bulged** in the middle.

How do we know? Watch a **ship** sail away.
The bottom goes first. The **hull** sinks out of sight. The mast goes last.
The sea itself is **curved**.

Look at the Moon in an **eclipse**. The Earth's shadow on the Moon is **round**.
Only a ball makes a round shadow every time.

Around the equator: **40,075 kilometres**.
Pole to pole: **40,007 kilometres**. A little less. That little less is the flattening.

Now you. Name the shape. Give one proof. That is the Earth.
