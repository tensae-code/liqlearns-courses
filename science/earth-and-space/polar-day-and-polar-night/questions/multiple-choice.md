# Polar day and polar night — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A polar day is…
a) A very hot day at the equator  b) A day near a pole when the Sun never sets  c) A day with two sunrises  d) The longest school day
**Answer: b) A day near a pole when the Sun never sets**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A polar night is the season when, at that pole…
a) The Sun rises twice a day  b) The Sun never rises at all  c) It rains without stopping  d) The Moon replaces the Sun
**Answer: b) The Sun never rises at all**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Polar days and nights happen because…
a) The Earth stops turning  b) The Moon blocks the Sun for months  c) The Earth's axis is tilted as it travels round the Sun, so a pole can stay in light or dark through every turning  d) The poles are closer to the Sun
**Answer: c) The Earth's axis is tilted as it travels round the Sun, so a pole can stay in light or dark through every turning**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) When the North Pole has its polar day, the South Pole has…
a) Its polar day too  b) Its polar night  c) Ordinary days only  d) No season at all
**Answer: b) Its polar night**
