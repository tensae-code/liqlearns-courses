# Polar day and polar night — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A day near a pole when the Sun never sets is called a _____.
**Answer: polar day**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) When the Sun never rises at a pole for days on end, it is the _____.
**Answer: polar night**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Polar days and nights are caused by the Earth's tilted _____.
**Answer: axis**
