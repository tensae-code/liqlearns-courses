# Video script — Polar day and polar night


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


Today: **Polar day and polar night** — Bhutan's way: the strangest days on Earth, at its two ends.

Travel to the North Pole in summer and wait for night. It never comes. The Sun circles the sky and does not set — a day months long, the polar day. Come back in winter: now the Sun never rises, and the darkness lasts for months — the polar night. The Earth's tilted axis makes both: tilted toward the Sun, a pole stays in the light through every turning; tilted away, it stays in the dark. And the two poles always take opposite turns.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
