# Hands-on — Polar day and polar night

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Torch and tilted ball
Stick a skewer through a ball or an orange as its axis and mark the two poles. In a darkened room, hold a torch still as the Sun. First tilt the top pole toward the torch and turn the ball slowly on its axis: watch whether any turning carries the top pole into shadow. Then tilt the top pole away and turn again. Record, for each tilt, whether the top pole gets day, night, or an unbroken one of the two — the model of the polar day and the polar night.

## A day that never ends, a night that never ends
Write two short diary entries: one from a child living through a polar day (what would you do at 'midnight' in full light?) and one from a polar night (how would you know the days are passing?). Under each, add one sentence naming the tilt that makes it so.
