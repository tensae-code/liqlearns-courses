# Volcanoes — benefits and harms

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — What volcanoes give — ash that fertilises

Nicaragua's Grade 8 book teaches its own country's volcanoes from both sides. On the giving side: **volcanic ash can fertilise** — ash fallen on the land feeds the soil, one reason farms thrive near volcanoes.

## Sub lesson 2 — What volcanoes harm — fresh ash and health

On the harming side, the same book sets the **harm of fresh ash for health** — the ash that has just fallen is a danger to breathe and to live under, even though weathered ash later enriches the ground. Benefits and harms come from the same ash, at different times.
