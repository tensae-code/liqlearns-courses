# Volcanoes — benefits and harms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: What volcanoes give — ash that fertilises.
**Answer:** Nicaragua's Grade 8 book teaches its own country's volcanoes from both sides. On the giving side: **volcanic ash can fertilise** — ash fallen on the land feeds the soil, one reason farms thrive near volcanoes.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: What volcanoes harm — fresh ash and health.
**Answer:** On the harming side, the same book sets the **harm of fresh ash for health** — the ash that has just fallen is a danger to breathe and to live under, even though weathered ash later enriches the ground. Benefits and harms come from the same ash, at different times.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** What volcanoes give — ash that fertilises → What volcanoes harm — fresh ash and health — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
