# Volcanoes — benefits and harms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) What volcanoes give — ash that fertilises  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) What volcanoes give — ash that fertilises**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) What volcanoes harm — fresh ash and health  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) What volcanoes harm — fresh ash and health**

**3.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: Nicaragua's volcanoes — what they give and what they harm: volcanic ash that can fertilise the soil, set against fresh ash that harms health. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**4.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Nicaragua, Grade 8 — *Ciencias Naturales, Octavo grado*, printed pp. 92–94  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Nicaragua, Grade 8 — *Ciencias Naturales, Octavo grado*, printed pp. 92–94**
