Today: **The geologic time scale** — the United States' way, from its Utah SEEd Standards books (Grade 7).

The evidence is in the ground: **rock strata** — the layered beds of rock, younger lying on older — and the **fossils** held inside each layer (Grade 7, 7.2.6, printed p. 98). A layer's fossils are the remains of the living world of that layer's time, so the stack of layers is a stack of times, each with its own life.

From that evidence scientists build the **geologic time scale**: the ordered scale that **shows Earth's age and history** — the whole past divided and named, so any fossil or layer can be placed in its chapter. Utah's task is to **argue** it: from rock strata and fossil evidence, show how the time scale is known, not merely announced. The scale is an argument that the rocks themselves support.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
