# The geologic time scale — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Rock strata and fossils — the evidence.
**Answer:** The evidence is in the ground: rock strata — the layered beds of rock, younger lying on older — and the fossils held inside each layer (Grade 7, 7.2.6, printed p. 98).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The time scale — Earth's age and history.
**Answer:** From that evidence scientists build the geologic time scale: the ordered scale that shows Earth's age and history — the whole past divided and named, so any fossil or layer can be placed in its chapter.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Rock strata and fossils — the evidence → The time scale — Earth's age and history — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
