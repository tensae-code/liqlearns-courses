# Hands-on — The geologic time scale

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stack of times
Draw five rock layers, oldest at the bottom, each with a different simple fossil shape. Label the stack as a time scale and write what changed between two layers.

## Place the fossil
Given a fossil kind found in the second layer from the bottom of your drawing, state its place in the scale and the evidence you used.
