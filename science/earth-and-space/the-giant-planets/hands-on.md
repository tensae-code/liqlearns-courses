# Hands-on — The giant planets — Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Ring the planet
Draw Saturn and shade its ring band; then write what the rings are really made of and how big the pieces are — dust-specks to tens of metres.

## Roll like Uranus
Walk across the room spinning a ball upright; then walk again 'rolling' an imaginary ball on its side. Say which walk was Uranus's and what may have knocked it over.
