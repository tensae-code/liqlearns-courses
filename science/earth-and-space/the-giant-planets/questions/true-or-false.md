# The giant planets — Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Saturn is the lowest-density planet.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Uranus was found in 1846 and Neptune in 1781.
**Answer: False — Uranus in 1781, Neptune in 1846.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Neptune's Great Dark Spot is about the size of Earth.
**Answer: True**
