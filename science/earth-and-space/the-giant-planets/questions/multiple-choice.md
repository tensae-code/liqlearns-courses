# The giant planets — Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The four Galilean moons belong to —
a) Saturn  b) Jupiter  c) Uranus  d) Neptune
**Answer: b) Jupiter.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Titan, larger than Mercury, is a moon of —
a) Jupiter  b) Neptune  c) Saturn  d) Uranus
**Answer: c) Saturn.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Saturn's rings are —
a) one solid sheet  b) thousands of ringlets of ice and rock  c) burning gas  d) frozen water only, in one piece
**Answer: b) thousands of ringlets of ice and rock.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Uranus's axis is —
a) perfectly upright  b) tipped nearly onto its side  c) pointing at the Sun  d) reversed every year
**Answer: b) tipped nearly onto its side.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The blue-green colour of Uranus and Neptune comes from —
a) their oceans  b) methane absorbing red and yellow light  c) their rings  d) frozen nitrogen on the surface
**Answer: b) methane absorbing red and yellow light.**
