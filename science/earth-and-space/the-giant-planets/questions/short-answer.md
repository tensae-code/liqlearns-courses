# The giant planets — Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are this lesson's moon counts labelled 'as printed'?
**Answer: both the student book's counts and the guide's keyed counts are dated figures from when the books were made; the lesson teaches the book's figures as the book's, not as today's counts.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What makes Titan stand out among Saturn's moons?
**Answer: it is larger than the planet Mercury and carries its own atmosphere of nitrogen, argon and methane.**
