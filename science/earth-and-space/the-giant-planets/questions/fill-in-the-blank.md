# The giant planets — Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** As the book prints them, Jupiter has ______ moons.
**Answer: 16 (the book's figure, as of the book).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The giant that rolls on its side is ______.
**Answer: Uranus.**
