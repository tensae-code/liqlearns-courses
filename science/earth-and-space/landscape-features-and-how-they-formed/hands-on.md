# Hands-on — Landscape features — and how they formed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map your landscape
Draw a map of your own area and mark its special features — natural and people-made — the Samoa way: features first, named one by one.

## One feature, two questions
Choose one feature on your map and write its two answers: how it formed, and what is changing it now, with the evidence you can point to.
