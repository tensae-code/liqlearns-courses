# Landscape features — and how they formed — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Samoa's special features and environments.
**Answer:** First, the mapping: Samoa's special landscape features — waterfalls, cliffs, caves, lava flows, the chain of volcanoes, blowholes, Sliding Rock, bays, lakes, pools, rivers and streams — and its special environments — rainforest, swamp forest, mangrove swamps and cloud forest.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The field trip — evidence of forming and changing.
**Answer:** Then the class goes out and hunts evidence: volcanic ash and lava say a volcano built this; water-worn rock says water erosion is still cutting it; roots in cracks say plants are splitting rock; trees bent one way say the wind shapes growth; and cleared, built or damaged places say people have changed the landscape, for better and for worse.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Samoa's special features and environments → The field trip — evidence of forming and changing — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
