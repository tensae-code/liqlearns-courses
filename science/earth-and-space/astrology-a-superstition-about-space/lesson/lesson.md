# Astrology — a superstition about space

## Sub lesson 1 — A claim about the sky

Astrology claims that the positions of the stars and planets shape people's lives and fortunes. It is a prevailing superstition about space — widely believed, and taught in Bangladesh's Class 6 book as a claim to be tested, not a fact to be learned.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tested against astronomy

Astronomy studies the sky by observation and measurement; astrology offers no testable link between a star's place and a person's life. Set against astronomy — what can be observed, measured and checked — astrology's claims fail the test that science asks of every claim.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why the book teaches it

The lesson's point is critical thinking about the sky: a claim is not made true by being old, or by being believed by many. Bangladesh teaches the superstition itself so learners can practise telling a tested claim from an untested one.
