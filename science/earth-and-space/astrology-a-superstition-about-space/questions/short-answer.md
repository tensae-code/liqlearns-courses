# Astrology — a superstition about space — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: A claim about the sky.
**Answer:** Astrology claims that the positions of the stars and planets shape people's lives and fortunes.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Tested against astronomy.
**Answer:** Astronomy studies the sky by observation and measurement; astrology offers no testable link between a star's place and a person's life.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Why the book teaches it.
**Answer:** The lesson's point is critical thinking about the sky: a claim is not made true by being old, or by being believed by many.

**Answer:** A claim about the sky → Tested against astronomy → Why the book teaches it — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
