## Sub lesson 2 — Interactions in the oceans

Interactions in the oceans: the Sun heats the tropics with vertical rays and the poles with oblique ones, so the ocean–atmosphere system transports the surplus heat; surface and deep currents follow planet-wide routes, carrying heat and exchanging chemicals between surface and deep water, and so shape climate and weather.
