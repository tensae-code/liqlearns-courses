# Hands-on — Seeing the past — light travel time from the stars

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: light coming from the past: even travelling at light's speed, starlight takes years to reach us, so when you look at a star you see it as it was when its light set out — the distances between bodies in space are so huge that some starlight takes millions of years to arrive Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
