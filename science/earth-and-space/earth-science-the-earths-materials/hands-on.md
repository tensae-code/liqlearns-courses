# Hands-on — Earth science — the Earth's materials

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The handful test
Go outside and collect: one stone, one handful of soil, one cup of water. Hold each one and say which of the Earth's materials it is. For the fourth material — air — wave your hand and feel it push back: it is there even though you cannot pick it up.

## One material's journey
Choose water or soil. Follow it: where is it now, where was it before, where does it go next? Draw its journey as a circle, not a line — Earth's materials travel in cycles.
