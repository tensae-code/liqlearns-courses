# Video script — Earth science — the Earth's materials

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Earth science — the Earth's materials** — the science of the ground under your feet.

Every science has its own thing to study. Earth science studies the Earth itself — the whole planet: its land, its waters and its air.
And the Earth is made of materials. Pick up a stone: that is rock. Pick up a handful of soil: rock broken small, mixed with the remains of living things. The water in the river and the rain, and the air you cannot see — these too are the Earth's materials.
Earth's materials do not sit still. Water cycles, rock breaks down into soil, soil washes to the sea. That is why Ghana files Earth science under Cycles.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
