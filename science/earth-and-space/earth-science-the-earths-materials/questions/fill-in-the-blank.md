# Earth science — the Earth's materials — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The science that studies the Earth itself is called _____ science.
**Answer: Earth**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Ghana files Earth science in Strand 2, _____.
**Answer: Cycles**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The four great materials of the Earth are rock, soil, water and _____.
**Answer: air**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) _____ is rock broken small over long ages, mixed with the remains of living things.
**Answer: Soil**
