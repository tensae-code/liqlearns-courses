# Earth science — the Earth's materials — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Earth science studies…
a) Only the stars  b) The Earth itself — its land, waters and air  c) Only living things  d) Only weather
**Answer: b) The Earth itself — its land, waters and air**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In Ghana's JHS curriculum, Earth science is filed inside which strand?
a) Strand 1 Diversity of Matter  b) Strand 3 Systems  c) Strand 2 Cycles  d) Strand 4 Forces and Energy
**Answer: c) Strand 2 Cycles**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is NOT one of the Earth's four great materials named in this lesson?
a) Rock  b) Soil  c) Plastic  d) Air
**Answer: c) Plastic**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Soil is…
a) Rock broken small, mixed with the remains of living things  b) Melted rock  c) A kind of air  d) Water with sand only
**Answer: a) Rock broken small, mixed with the remains of living things**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does Earth science belong in the Cycles strand?
a) Because it is only taught in one term  b) Because the Earth's materials keep changing and going round  c) Because it uses bicycles  d) Because it has no materials
**Answer: b) Because the Earth's materials keep changing and going round**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The material you cannot pick up, but can feel push back when you wave your hand, is…
a) Rock  b) Soil  c) Water  d) Air
**Answer: d) Air**
