# Earth science — the Earth's materials — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does Earth science study?
**Answer:** Earth science studies the Earth itself — the whole planet: its land and what the land is made of, its waters, and the air wrapped around it.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is Earth science placed in the Cycles strand in Ghana's curriculum?
**Answer:** Because the Earth's story is things going round — water rising and falling as rain, rock breaking down and building up again; its materials cycle.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the Earth's four great materials.
**Answer:** Rock, soil, water and air.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Give one way the Earth's materials change into each other's business.
**Answer:** Any one of: water wears rock down; broken rock becomes soil; soil washes to the sea and settles; air moves water as clouds and rain.
