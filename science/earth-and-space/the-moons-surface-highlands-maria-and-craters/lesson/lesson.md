## Sub lesson 1 — Earth's Moon

Earth's Moon: until Galileo's telescope in 1609 the moon was thought smooth; its highlands are pocked with craters from crashing meteorites, and its dark flat areas, the maria, formed when lava flowed into low ground and cooled; in 2018 high-resolution pictures of the moon's surface were taken by the Saudi imaging system carried on the Chinese lunar exploration mission — the moon circles the earth every 27.3 days at an average 384,400 km
