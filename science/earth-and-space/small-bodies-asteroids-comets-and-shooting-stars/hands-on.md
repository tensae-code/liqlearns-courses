# Hands-on — Small bodies — asteroids, comets, and shooting stars that land

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Tail test
Walk a strip of tissue paper around a fan (the Sun): whichever way you walk, the strip streams AWAY from the fan. A comet's tail does the same around the real Sun.

## Crater drops
Drop stones of different sizes into soft mud or flour and measure the craters — the Moon's cratered face was made the same way, by meteoroid impacts.
