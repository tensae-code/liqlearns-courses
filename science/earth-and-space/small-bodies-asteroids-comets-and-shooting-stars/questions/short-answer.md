# Small bodies — asteroids, comets, and shooting stars that land — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Were scientists 'wrong' about Pluto before 2006? Explain.
**Answer: not careless — with more evidence, Pluto was found to be one of many similar small icy bodies, so the category was redrawn; science corrects its categories as evidence grows.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One rock, three names — give them in order with their places.
**Answer: meteoroid in space, meteor burning in the atmosphere, meteorite on the ground — the name follows the location.**
