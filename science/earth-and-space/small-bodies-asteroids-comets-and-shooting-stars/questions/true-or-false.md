# Small bodies — asteroids, comets, and shooting stars that land — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pluto's demotion shows scientific categories can change as evidence grows.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Halley's comet returns about every 75–76 years.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A meteor is the rock while it is still travelling in space.
**Answer: False — in space it is a meteoroid; the meteor is the bright streak in the atmosphere.**
