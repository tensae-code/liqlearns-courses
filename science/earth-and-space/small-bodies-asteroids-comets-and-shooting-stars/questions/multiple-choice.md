# Small bodies — asteroids, comets, and shooting stars that land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pluto was reclassified a dwarf planet in —
a) 1930  b) 2006  c) 1758  d) 1996
**Answer: b) 2006.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Most asteroids orbit in the belt between —
a) Earth and Mars  b) Venus and Mercury  c) Mars and Jupiter  d) Jupiter and Saturn
**Answer: c) Mars and Jupiter.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The largest asteroid is about —
a) the size of Earth  b) a quarter of the Moon's diameter  c) the size of Jupiter  d) 10 km across exactly
**Answer: b) a quarter of the Moon's diameter.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A comet's tail always points —
a) behind its motion  b) toward the Sun  c) away from the Sun  d) downward
**Answer: c) away from the Sun.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A rock that survives the atmosphere and strikes the ground is a —
a) meteoroid  b) meteor  c) meteorite  d) comet
**Answer: c) meteorite.**
