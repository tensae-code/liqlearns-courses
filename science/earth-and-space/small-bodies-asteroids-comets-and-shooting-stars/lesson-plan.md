# Lesson plan — Small bodies — asteroids, comets, and shooting stars that land

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Earth and Space · **Lesson:** Small bodies — asteroids, comets, and shooting stars that land
**Place in the curriculum:** Earth and Space — the title opened by the Grade 7 Earth lessons (CURRICULUM.md). Unit 6 — The Solar System, at General Science Grade 8 depth.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the solar system's smaller members: Pluto, reclassified a dwarf planet in 2006; the asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter; comets whose tails always point away from the Sun; and one rock with three names — meteoroid in space, meteor in the sky, meteorite on the ground.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the planet lessons: the learner knows the eight planets; this lesson clears up everything else that orbits the Sun — and why Pluto is no longer counted among the eight.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State Pluto's 2006 reclassification and what it shows about scientific categories.
2. Locate the asteroid belt and give the largest asteroid's size.
3. Explain a comet's tail and Halley's period.
4. Use meteoroid, meteor and meteorite correctly by location.
