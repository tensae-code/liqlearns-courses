# Hands-on — Natural and human-made landscapes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — Lesson 12. Lesson 12: a natural landscape holds nature's elements (pine, mahogany and cedar trees, mountains, plateaus, valleys, rivers, lakes, seas, rocks, soil); a human-made (artificial) landscape is transformed by houses, buildings, hydroelectric dams, farming, roads and bridges; hurricanes and tropical storms also change landscapes. Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
