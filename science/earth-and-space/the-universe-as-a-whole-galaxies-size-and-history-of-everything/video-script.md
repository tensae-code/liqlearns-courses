# Video script — The universe as a whole — galaxies, size and history of everything

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **the universe as a whole**. Stars do not wander alone — billions of them gather into galaxies, and our Sun sits in one spiral galaxy, the Milky Way, among billions of others. The distances are so large we count them in light-years — and because light takes time to travel, looking far away means looking back in time. Every galaxy's light is stretched red, which tells us the whole universe is expanding — and run the story backwards, it all began hot and dense, about 13.8 billion years ago. The faint afterglow of that beginning still fills the sky today.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
