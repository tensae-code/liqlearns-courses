# Hands-on — The universe as a whole — galaxies, size and history of everything

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The balloon universe
Draw six or seven dots on an uninflated balloon with a marker — these are galaxies. Now blow the balloon up slowly and watch: every dot moves away from every other dot, the gaps all grow, and no dot is the centre of the expansion on the surface. That is the expanding universe in your hands — space itself stretching, carrying the galaxies with it. Say aloud: the galaxies are not flying through space; the space between them is growing.
