# The universe as a whole — galaxies, size and history of everything — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A galaxy is…
a) A single very large star  b) A gathering of billions of stars held together by gravity  c) A cloud of gas with no stars  d) Another name for a solar system
**Answer: b) A gathering of billions of stars held together by gravity**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The Sun is…
a) At the centre of the Milky Way  b) One ordinary star among hundreds of billions in the Milky Way  c) The largest star in its galaxy  d) Outside any galaxy
**Answer: b) One ordinary star among hundreds of billions in the Milky Way**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) One light-year is…
a) The time light takes to cross the solar system  b) About 9.5×10¹⁵ m — the distance light travels in one year  c) Exactly one year of time  d) The distance to the nearest star
**Answer: b) About 9.5×10¹⁵ m — the distance light travels in one year**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Seeing a galaxy 2.5 million light-years away means seeing it…
a) As it is now  b) As it will be in 2.5 million years  c) As it was 2.5 million years ago  d) Not at all
**Answer: c) As it was 2.5 million years ago**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The redshift of distant galaxies tells us…
a) The galaxies are heating up  b) The universe is expanding — galaxies are receding, farther ones faster  c) The galaxies are falling toward us  d) Space is shrinking
**Answer: b) The universe is expanding — galaxies are receding, farther ones faster**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The cosmic microwave background is…
a) Radiation from the Sun's corona  b) Interference from satellites  c) The leftover afterglow of the hot, dense beginning of the universe  d) Light reflected by the Moon
**Answer: c) The leftover afterglow of the hot, dense beginning of the universe**
