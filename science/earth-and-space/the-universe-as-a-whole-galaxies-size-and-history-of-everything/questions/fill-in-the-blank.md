# The universe as a whole — galaxies, size and history of everything — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Our own galaxy is called the _____ Way.
**Answer: Milky**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part of the universe we can in principle see is called the _____ universe.
**Answer: observable**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The stretching of a receding galaxy's light toward longer wavelengths is called _____.
**Answer: redshift**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The universe began about 13.8 _____ years ago, hot and dense.
**Answer: billion**
