# Hands-on — Plate tectonics and its patterns

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four layers, one pattern
On tracing paper over a world map, mark volcanoes on one sheet, earthquakes on a second, mountain chains on a third. Stack them: write what the agreement shows.

## Predict from the pattern
Given a blank map showing plate boundaries only, predict where you would put a volcano belt and an earthquake belt — then check against a real map.
