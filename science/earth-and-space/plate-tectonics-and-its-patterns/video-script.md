Today: **Plate tectonics and its patterns** — the United States' way, from its Utah SEEd Standards books (Grade 7).

The lesson works from **data analysed for patterns** (Grade 7, 7.2.5, printed p. 87): plot the volcanoes, the earthquakes, the mountains and the seafloor features, and they do not scatter — they **fall in lines and belts**. A pattern in four separate datasets, all agreeing, is evidence asking for one explanation.

The explanation is **plate tectonics**: the lines the features draw are the **plates and their boundaries**. Where plates meet, the Earth erupts, shakes, crumples into mountains and tears new seafloor. The pattern is the plates made visible — each feature a sign of the same moving system underneath.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
