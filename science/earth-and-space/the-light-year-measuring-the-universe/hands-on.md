# Hands-on — The light year — measuring the universe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: the light year: light travels at 300,000 km/s, circling the earth about seven times a second; the light year is the distance it covers in one year, about 9.5 trillion km — the unit fit for distances between galaxies, whose light shows them as they were millions of years ago Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
