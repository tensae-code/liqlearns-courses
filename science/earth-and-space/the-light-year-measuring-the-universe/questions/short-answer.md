# The light year — measuring the universe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The light year.
**Answer:** the light year: light travels at 300,000 km/s, circling the earth about seven times a second; the light year is the distance it covers in one year, about 9.5 trillion km — the unit fit for distances between galaxies, whose light shows them as they were millions of years ago.
