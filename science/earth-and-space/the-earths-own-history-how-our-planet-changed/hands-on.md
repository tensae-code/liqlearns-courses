# Hands-on — The Earth's own history — how our planet changed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The rope of Earth's history
Lay out a long rope: one end is the Earth's formation, the other is today. Mark where the Solar System chapter (§9.3) ends and the Earth's own story (§9.4) runs. Walk the rope and say, at three points, 'the Earth was still changing' — the rope's lesson is that the change never stopped.

## Then and now
Draw one place you know twice: as it might have been in the young Earth's time, and as it is now. Under each, write one thing that changed. Your two drawings are the evolution of the Earth in miniature.
