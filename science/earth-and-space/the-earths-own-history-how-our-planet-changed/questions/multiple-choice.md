# The Earth's own history — how our planet changed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Section 9 of Armenia's book runs in the order…
a) Earth first, then the Big Bang  b) Big Bang; galaxies and stars; Solar System; Earth's evolution  c) Stars only  d) Solar System, then nothing
**Answer: b) Big Bang; galaxies and stars; Solar System; Earth's evolution**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson's first point about the Earth is…
a) It has never changed  b) It is older than the Universe  c) It has a history — it formed and has changed ever since  d) Its story is unknowable in principle
**Answer: c) It has a history — it formed and has changed ever since**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The Earth's surface, air and waters today are…
a) Exactly as at its formation  b) Fixed since people arrived  c) Changed only by people  d) The present state of a long change
**Answer: d) The present state of a long change**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) 'Evolution of the Earth' means…
a) The planet's long change from its formation to today, told as one story  b) Only the evolution of animals  c) One earthquake  d) The Moon's history
**Answer: a) The planet's long change from its formation to today, told as one story**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The Earth chapter closes the section because…
a) It is the shortest  b) The story narrows from the Universe to the planet the learner stands on  c) It is unrelated to the rest  d) The book ran out of space topics
**Answer: b) The story narrows from the Universe to the planet the learner stands on**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The Earth's change, in the lesson's close,…
a) Stopped at its formation  b) Happened in one day  c) Never stopped — the planet is still changing  d) Applies only to its surface
**Answer: c) Never stopped — the planet is still changing**
