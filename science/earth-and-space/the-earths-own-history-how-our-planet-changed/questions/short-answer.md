# The Earth's own history — how our planet changed — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name Section 9's four chapters in order.
**Answer:** The Big Bang; formation and evolution of galaxies and stars; formation of the Solar System; evolution of the Earth.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What two claims make up the lesson's frame?
**Answer:** The Earth formed, and it has been changing ever since — its present state is one state in a long change.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the evolution of the Earth?
**Answer:** The planet's long change from the newly formed Earth to the Earth of today, told as one story.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is placing the Earth last 'deliberate', in the lesson's words?
**Answer:** The Universe's vast story narrows chapter by chapter until it arrives at the one planet the learner stands on — and that planet, too, has a past.
