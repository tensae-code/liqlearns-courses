# Iceland — the volcanic island and its landscapes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Iceland is a volcanic island — raised and still shaped by eruptions, its ground made of lava.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** The island's landscapes carry the marks of that volcanic making.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does Iceland's book start Earth study with Iceland itself?
**Answer:** Because it is the land the children stand on — a volcanic island being built still — so its making is the first Earth fact they can see around them.
