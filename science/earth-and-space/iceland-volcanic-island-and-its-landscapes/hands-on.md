# Hands-on — Iceland — the volcanic island and its landscapes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read your own ground
With an adult, look at the ground and hills near your home. Is any of it volcanic where you live? Say what you see and whether your land is still being made, like Iceland's, or long finished.

## Island portrait
Draw an island being built by a volcano: the eruption, the new lava ground, the sea around it. Label the newest land on your drawing.
