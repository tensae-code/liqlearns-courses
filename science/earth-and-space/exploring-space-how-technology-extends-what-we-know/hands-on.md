# Hands-on — Exploring space — how technology extends what we know

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Instrument ladder
On one page, draw a ladder with four rungs — eye, telescope, satellite telescope, probe. Beside each rung write one thing it can discover that the rung below cannot, using the lesson’s examples. Finish with a prediction rung: name one instrument being built or flown now and the news it is meant to bring.
