# Exploring space — how technology extends what we know — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A telescope’s wide mirror _____ far more light than a pupil can.
**Answer: gathers (collects)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Saturn’s _____ were among the first sights of the telescope age.
**Answer: rings**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Orbiting telescopes escape the atmosphere’s _____ and blocking.
**Answer: blurring**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A spacecraft that travels to another world to study it closely is a _____.
**Answer: probe (space probe)**
