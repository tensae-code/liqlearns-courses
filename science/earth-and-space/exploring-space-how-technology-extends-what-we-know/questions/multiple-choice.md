# Exploring space — how technology extends what we know — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A telescope’s first job is…
**Answer: a) gathering much more light than the eye can  b) making objects nearer  c) colouring stars  d) replacing the eye entirely**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The first telescopes extended knowledge by showing, among other things…
**Answer: a) Jupiter’s moons and Saturn’s rings  b) atoms  c) the Earth’s core  d) radio waves**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A space telescope beats a ground telescope because it is above…
**Answer: a) the blurring and blocking atmosphere  b) the clouds only  c) gravity  d) the Moon**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these also rides on the satellite step?
**Answer: a) weather watching, mapping and navigation  b) nothing else  c) only weapons  d) only television**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A probe adds what no telescope can:
**Answer: a) close-up, on-the-spot knowledge of another world — landings, samples, surfaces  b) bigger magnification  c) cheaper pictures  d) faster light**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The pattern of space knowledge is: it grows exactly with…
**Answer: a) our instruments — eyes, telescopes, satellites, probes  b) our wishes  c) the number of stars  d) the calendar**
