# Exploring space — how technology extends what we know — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is “gathering light” more important than “magnifying” for discovering faint objects?
**Answer: A faint object sends too little light for the eye to register at any magnification; a wide mirror collects enough of it to be seen at all — magnification without light only enlarges darkness.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name one band of radiation the atmosphere blocks and say how astronomers study it.
**Answer: Ultraviolet / X-rays / much infrared (any one); they study it with telescopes on satellites above the atmosphere.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What have probes done that no Earth-based instrument could?
**Answer: Travelled to other worlds — orbited and landed on planets, moons and asteroids, photographed surfaces close up and returned samples.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) In one sentence, state this lesson’s pattern linking technology and knowledge.
**Answer: What we know about space extends exactly as far as our instruments extend our senses — each new technology (telescope, satellite, probe) has redrawn the map.**
