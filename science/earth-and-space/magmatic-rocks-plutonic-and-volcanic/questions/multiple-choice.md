# Magmatic rocks — plutonic and volcanic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Magmatic rocks are born from…
a) Settled layers  b) Molten rock cooled and hardened  c) Changed old rocks  d) Sand
**Answer: b) Molten rock cooled and hardened**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Plutonic rocks cooled…
a) Fast, at the surface  b) Slowly, below the surface  c) In rivers  d) In ice
**Answer: b) Slowly, below the surface**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Slow cooling gives crystals…
a) No time to grow  b) Time to grow large and visible  c) Colour only  d) Holes
**Answer: b) Time to grow large and visible**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Volcanic rocks form from…
a) Lava cooled quickly at the surface  b) Magma cooled slowly deep down  c) Pressed sediment  d) Changed limestone
**Answer: a) Lava cooled quickly at the surface**
