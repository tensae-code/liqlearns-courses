# Magmatic rocks — plutonic and volcanic — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Rocks born from molten rock are _____ rocks.
**Answer: magmatic**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The magmatic rocks that cooled below the surface are _____ rocks.
**Answer: plutonic**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Lava cooled fast at the surface makes _____ rocks.
**Answer: volcanic**
