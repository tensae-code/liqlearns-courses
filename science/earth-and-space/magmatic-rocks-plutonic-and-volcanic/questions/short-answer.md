# Magmatic rocks — plutonic and volcanic — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What makes the magmatic rocks one family?
**Answer:** Their birth: every one of them formed from molten rock — magma or lava — that cooled and hardened.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does a plutonic rock's crystal size prove its cooling place?
**Answer:** Its visible, well-grown crystals needed time — time only slow cooling deep below the surface, insulated in hot rock, can give.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) State the pair's difference in one sentence.
**Answer:** Plutonic rocks cooled slowly below the surface and grew visible crystals; volcanic rocks cooled quickly at the surface from lava and stayed fine-grained or glassy.
