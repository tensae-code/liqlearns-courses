Today: **Magmatic rocks — plutonic and volcanic** — Mauritania's way: one family of melt-born rocks, split by where the melt cooled.

Every magmatic rock was once molten. Cool that melt deep underground, slowly, and crystals grow big — a plutonic rock. Pour it out as lava at the surface, where it cools fast, and the rock stays fine or glassy — a volcanic rock.

Same birth, two cooling places. Read a magmatic rock's crystals and they tell you where it cooled.
