# Hands-on — Magmatic rocks — plutonic and volcanic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read the crystals
Find or look at pictures of two igneous rocks — a coarse-grained one (like granite) and a fine or glassy one (like basalt or obsidian). For each, write its cooling story: where it cooled, how fast, and how the crystals prove it.

## Slow and fast cooling
Melt a little candle wax in a spoon over a candle (adult help). Let one drop cool in cold water and another cool slowly in warm sand. Compare the two solids' texture — your model of fast volcanic and slow plutonic cooling.
