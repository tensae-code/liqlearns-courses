# Hands-on — Matter — the particle theory

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The taste test
Dissolve a spoon of sugar in a glass of water. Taste before and after. The sweetness spreads through the whole glass — the sugar's particles have spread with it.

## Sand and water
Measure 50 mL of dry sand and 50 mL of water. Mix and measure again. The shortfall is the water hiding in the spaces between the sand particles.

## Your own dilution line
Line up five cups of water. Carry one spoon of ink-coloured water from cup to cup. Keep the line — it is Experiment 2.1 on your own table.
