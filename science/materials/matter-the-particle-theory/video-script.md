# Video script — Matter — the particle theory

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Grind the sugar. Finer. Finer.
Stir it in water. Gone? Taste it. Sweet!
The sugar is still there — in pieces too small to see.
**Matter** is what takes space and has mass.
Stone — matter. Water — matter. Air — matter.
Sound? Light? A smell? Not matter. No mass. No space of their own.
Long ago, **Democritus** said it first: all things are tiny **particles**.
Now the proof. Colour the water deep purple.
Pour a little into clear water. Paler. Again. Paler.
Again, again, again — the colour fades… but it never quite goes.
Particles. Always moving. With spaces between them.
Heat them, and they move faster. That is the particle theory.
