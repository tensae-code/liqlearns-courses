# Matter — the particle theory — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sound is matter.
**Answer: False** — sound has no mass and occupies no space of its own.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The particles of every substance are the same.
**Answer: False** — each substance's particles are its own.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Dissolved sugar is gone for good.
**Answer: False** — the taste proves it is still there.
