# Matter — the particle theory — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Particles are always ______.
**Answer: moving** — postulate 4.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Experiment 2.1 dilutes ______ (or ink) beaker by beaker.
**Answer: potassium permanganate** — the book's colour.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Matter exists as solid, ______ or gas.
**Answer: liquid** — the three states.
