# Matter — the particle theory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is matter?
a) light  b) sound  c) air  d) a smell
**Answer: c) air** — air occupies space and has mass.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Matter is anything that —
a) can be seen  b) occupies space and has mass  c) makes a sound
**Answer: b) occupies space and has mass** — that is the definition.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which postulate says heat matters?
a) particles attract  b) temperature speeds particles  c) spaces lie between particles
**Answer: b) temperature speeds particles** — heating makes particles move faster.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Who first had the particle idea?
a) Democritus  b) a textbook printer  c) no one knows
**Answer: a) Democritus** — teacher-guide evidence, printed pp. 16–18.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In Experiment 2.1, beaker by beaker the colour —
a) vanishes at beaker 2  b) gets darker  c) fades but never quite goes
**Answer: c) fades but never quite goes** — the particles spread thinner without end.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Sand + water measuring less than the sum proves —
a) water evaporated  b) spaces lie between particles  c) sand dissolved
**Answer: b) spaces lie between particles** — water slips into the spaces.
