The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Air has mass** — China's way, from its 科学 (Science) textbooks.

Air feels like nothing. So weigh it: balance a flat ball, then blow it up and balance it again — you must add beans to the other side. The ball got heavier, and all you added was air.
Galileo proved it first with a bottle and a pump. Invisible does not mean weightless: air has mass.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
