# Hands-on — Air has mass

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Balance the ball (with an adult)
Balance an uninflated ball against sand or beans on a simple balance. Inflate the ball, balance again, and count what must be added to the other side. Say what the added weight is measuring.

## Read the scale
Put a ball on an electronic scale uninflated, note the reading, inflate it and read again. Write both readings and the difference — the mass of the added air, as nearly as the scale can show it.
