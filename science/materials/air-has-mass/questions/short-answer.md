# Air has mass — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Describe the ball proof from start to finish.
**Answer:** Balance an uninflated ball against sand; inflate the ball with more air; balance again — soybeans must be added to the other side, so the inflated ball is heavier, and the only thing added was air: air has mass.

**2.** What does the electronic scale add to the proof?
**Answer:** Numbers: the mass can be read before and after inflating, and 'more air in, more mass shown' is measured, not just balanced.

**3.** How did Galileo make the first proof?
**Answer:** He pumped extra air into a sealed bottle and weighed the bottle again; the bottle with more air in it weighed more.

**4.** Why does trapping air in a container let us weigh it?
**Answer:** Because the container can sit on a balance: weigh it with less air and with more air, and the difference between the two weighings is the added air's mass.
