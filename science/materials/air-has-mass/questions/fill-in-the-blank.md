# Air has mass — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Air has _____, proved by weighing it.
**Answer: mass**

**2.** The inflated ball needed _____ added to balance it.
**Answer: soybeans**

**3.** More air in means more _____ on the scale.
**Answer: mass**

**4.** _____ was the first to prove air has mass, with a sealed bottle.
**Answer: Galileo**
