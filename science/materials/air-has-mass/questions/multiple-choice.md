# Air has mass — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The ball experiment proves that air…
a) Has mass  b) Has colour  c) Is a liquid  d) Weighs nothing
**Answer: a) Has mass**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) After the ball is inflated, the balance needs…
a) Nothing added  b) Soybeans added on the other side to balance it  c) The ball removed  d) Less sand
**Answer: b) Soybeans added on the other side to balance it**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) What was added to the ball to make it heavier?
a) Water  b) Sand inside the ball  c) Air — nothing else  d) A stone
**Answer: c) Air — nothing else**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) On an electronic scale, more air in the ball shows as…
a) Less mass  b) No change  c) A temperature  d) More mass
**Answer: d) More mass**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Galileo proved air has mass by…
a) Pumping extra air into a sealed bottle and weighing it again  b) Looking at the sky  c) Weighing the wind outdoors  d) Heating air
**Answer: a) Pumping extra air into a sealed bottle and weighing it again**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The shared method of ball and bottle is to…
a) Weigh the air directly on the pan  b) Trap more air in a container and weigh the container  c) Count the air  d) Colour the air
**Answer: b) Trap more air in a container and weigh the container**
