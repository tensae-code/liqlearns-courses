# Hands-on — Symbols — writing the elements

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Symbol hunt at home
Find five things at home made of one element — an iron nail, a copper wire, aluminium foil, a gold or silver ring, charcoal (carbon). Write each one's symbol beside it.

## Two-letter care drill
Write these pairs slowly, saying both letters aloud: Ca/Cu, He/Hg, Cl/C. Check each: capital first, small second.
