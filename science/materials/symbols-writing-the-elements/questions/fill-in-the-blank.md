# Symbols — writing the elements — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or formula.

**1.** The symbol for potassium is ___ . (K)
**Answer: K**

**2.** One kind of atom that cannot be broken down by ordinary means is called an ___ . (element)
**Answer: element**

**3.** The symbol for lead, from the Latin plumbum, is ___ . (Pb)
**Answer: Pb**
