# Symbols — writing the elements — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The symbol for iron is —
a) Ir  b) Fe  c) Io
**Answer: b) Fe** — — from the Latin name ferrum.

**2.** Which symbol is written correctly?
a) hE  b) HE  c) He
**Answer: c) He** — — first letter capital, second letter small.

**3.** Hg is the symbol for —
a) helium  b) mercury  c) hydrogen
**Answer: b) mercury** — — from the Latin hydrargyrum.

**4.** An element is —
a) a mixture of atoms  b) one kind of atom, not breakable by ordinary means  c) any shiny solid
**Answer: b) one kind of atom, not breakable by ordinary means**

**5.** Ca and Cu are —
a) the same element  b) different elements: calcium and copper  c) both copper
**Answer: b) different elements: calcium and copper**
