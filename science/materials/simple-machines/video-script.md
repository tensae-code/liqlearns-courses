# Video script — Simple machines

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Muscle is limited — **machines multiply it**.
They multiply **force**, or **speed**, or change **direction** — never **work**.
Six kinds: **lever, pulley, inclined plane, wheel and axle, gears, screw**.
A lever's class is the **order** of support, effort and load.
A screw is a **slope wrapped round**; a wedge is **two slopes** back to back.
