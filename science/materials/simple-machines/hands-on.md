# Hands-on — Simple machines

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The stone, lifted
Set a plank over a log as a first-class lever and lift the stone that defeated bare hands. Move the log (support) nearer the stone and feel the effort change.

## Gear teeth count
Draw two meshed gear wheels, 12 teeth and 6. Turn the big one once on paper: the small one turns twice. Equal teeth pass the meeting point — the book's own reasoning, drawn.
