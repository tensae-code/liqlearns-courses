# Simple machines — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A simple machine can multiply —**
a) work itself  b) force  c) matter
**Answer: b) force**

**2. In a second-class lever, the middle one is the —**
a) support  b) effort  c) load
**Answer: c) load**

**3. A fixed pulley's gift is —**
a) multiplying work  b) changing force's direction  c) removing friction
**Answer: b) changing force's direction**

**4. A wedge is —**
a) a wrapped slope  b) two inclined planes joined at a sharp edge  c) a kind of gear
**Answer: b) two inclined planes joined at a sharp edge**

**5. A screw is —**
a) an inclined plane wound round a shaft  b) a plain rod  c) a pulley without rope
**Answer: a) an inclined plane wound round a shaft**

**6. Where gear teeth meet, the smaller-toothed disc —**
a) spins slower  b) spins faster  c) stops
**Answer: b) spins faster**
