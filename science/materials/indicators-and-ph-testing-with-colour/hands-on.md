# Hands-on — Indicators and pH — testing with colour

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Plant indicator test
Make beetroot juice. Put a spoonful in three cups; add vinegar to one, nothing to the second, soap water to the third. Record the three colours — your own indicator chart.

## pH placing
Place these on a drawn 0–14 strip: lemon juice, rain water, pure water, baking-soda water, ash water, stomach acid. Defend each placing in one sentence.
