# Indicators and pH — testing with colour — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An indicator tells acid from base by —
a) smell  b) colour change  c) fizzing
**Answer: b) colour change**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Experiment 3.3, indicators are tested against —
a) metals  b) common acids and bases  c) salts only
**Answer: b) common acids and bases**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A solution of pH 3 is —
a) a weak base  b) neutral  c) an acid
**Answer: c) an acid**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which pair are local plant indicators named in this lesson?
a) beetroot and petunia  b) litmus and phenolphthalein  c) ash and lime
**Answer: a) beetroot and petunia**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Pure water sits at pH —
a) 0  b) 7  c) 14
**Answer: b) 7**
