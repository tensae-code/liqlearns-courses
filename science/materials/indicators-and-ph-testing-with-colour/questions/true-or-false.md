# Indicators and pH — testing with colour — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The same indicator can show different colours in acid and in base.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** pH 12 is a weaker base than pH 8.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A pH below 7 means the solution is a base.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Plant indicators are an eco-friendly way to test.
**Answer: True**
