# Hands-on — Formulas — the two meanings of a formula

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Two-readings drill
For each of 2HBr, 5H₂O, 3Cl₂, Ca(OH)₂, HNO₃, 4Br₂: say the qualitative reading in one breath, then count the quantitative reading on paper. Six formulas, both readings.

## Counters check
Lay out bottle tops for 3Cl₂ and Ca(OH)₂. If your counters and your written counts disagree, find which one missed the brackets or the coefficient.
