# Formulas — the two meanings of a formula — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or formula.

**1.** In 5H₂O there are ___ oxygen atoms in total. (5)
**Answer: 5**

**2.** The reading that names which elements are present is the ___ reading. (qualitative)
**Answer: qualitative**

**3.** 4Br₂ holds ___ bromine atoms. (8)
**Answer: 8**
