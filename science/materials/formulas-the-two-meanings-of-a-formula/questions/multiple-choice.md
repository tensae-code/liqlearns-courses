# Formulas — the two meanings of a formula — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In 3Cl₂, the total number of chlorine atoms is —
a) 3  b) 2  c) 6
**Answer: c) 6** — — 3 units × 2 atoms.

**2.** The qualitative reading of HNO₃ says —
a) it has 5 atoms  b) it contains hydrogen, nitrogen and oxygen  c) it has 3 oxygens
**Answer: b) it contains hydrogen, nitrogen and oxygen**

**3.** Ca(OH)₂ contains —
a) 1 Ca, 1 O, 2 H  b) 1 Ca, 2 O, 1 H  c) 1 Ca, 2 O, 2 H
**Answer: c) 1 Ca, 2 O, 2 H**

**4.** The number in front of a formula is called the —
a) subscript  b) coefficient  c) valence
**Answer: b) coefficient**

**5.** In 2HBr there are in total —
a) 2 H and 2 Br  b) 2 H and 1 Br  c) 4 H and 2 Br
**Answer: a) 2 H and 2 Br**
