# Formulas — the two meanings of a formula — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** A subscript counts atoms inside one unit; a coefficient counts whole units.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In 4Br₂ there are 4 bromine atoms.
**Answer: False**

**3.** The qualitative reading names which elements are present.
**Answer: True**

**4.** A subscript outside brackets multiplies everything inside them.
**Answer: True**
