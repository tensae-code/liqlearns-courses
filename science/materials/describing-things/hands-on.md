# Hands-on — Describing things: matter and its properties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The property hunt
Pick five things in the room. For each, fill a property line: colour, feel, weight, smell. Read one line aloud — can the class name the thing?

## Alike pairs
Find two things at home that look alike. Write the property that parts them, as the book parts salt from sugar.

## Air is matter proof
Blow up a balloon. The air filled it and stretched it — nothing cannot do that. Say the conclusion in the lesson's words.
