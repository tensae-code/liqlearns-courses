# Video script — Describing things: matter and its properties

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Everything around you is **matter**.
Colour. Shape. Size. Taste. Smell. Rough or smooth. Heavy or light.
Salt or sugar? Taste tells. Orange or lemon? Taste tells.
List the properties — and the thing names itself.
