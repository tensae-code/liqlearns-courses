# Describing things: matter and its properties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Everything around us — chair, stone, book, oil, air — is called —**
a) matter  b) shadow  c) music
**Answer: a) matter**

**2. How do we tell salt from sugar?**
a) by colour only  b) by taste  c) by size only
**Answer: b) by taste** — both are white; taste parts them.

**3. Which property pair parts a clay jebena from a metal pot?**
a) weight and feel  b) taste  c) smell
**Answer: a) weight and feel**

**4. Which sense reports whether a thing is rough or smooth?**
a) smell  b) touch (skin)  c) hearing
**Answer: b) touch (skin)**
