# Hands-on — Physical changes of matter

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Melt it, freeze it
Freeze a small cup of water, then let it melt. Measure or mark the water before freezing and after melting, and write what changed (state) and what did not (the substance).

## Two columns of changes
List six changes you know — melting ice, burning wood, boiling water, rusting iron, condensing steam, cooking an egg. Sort them: can be reversed, or makes something new. Check against the lesson's rule.
