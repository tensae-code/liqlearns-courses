Today: **Physical changes of matter** — Cambodia's way, from its Grade 6 book *វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រ ថ្នាក់ទី៦ (Science, Grade 6)*.

Matter comes as solid, liquid and gas, and it can change from one state to another. Ice **melts** to water; water **freezes** to ice; water **evaporates** to vapour; vapour **condenses** back to water. Cambodia's Grade 6 book teaches these as the **changes of matter** (Lesson 12) — the changes a substance goes through as it is heated and cooled.

The mark of these changes is that they can be **reversed**: melt the ice and freeze the water and you have the same water you started with. Nothing new is made — the substance only changes its state, the way its particles are held and move. A change that can be undone this way, leaving the same substance, is a **physical change** — set against changes that make a new substance and cannot simply be run backwards.
