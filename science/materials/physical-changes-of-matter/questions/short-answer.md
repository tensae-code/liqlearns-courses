# Physical changes of matter — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words, what a physical change is.
**Answer:** A physical change is a change — of state, for example — that can be reversed, where the substance stays itself and nothing new is made.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the four changes of state named in the lesson and say what each one does.
**Answer:** Melting (solid to liquid), freezing (liquid to solid), evaporating (liquid to vapour) and condensing (vapour back to liquid).

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Matter changes state — melting, freezing, evaporating, condensing → those changes can be reversed and the substance stays itself, which is what makes them physical changes — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
