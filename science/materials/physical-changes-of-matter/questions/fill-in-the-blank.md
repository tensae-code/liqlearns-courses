# Physical changes of matter — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ice changing to water is called ______.
**Answer: melting**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A change that can be reversed, with the substance staying itself, is a ______ change.
**Answer: physical**

**3.** (Whole lesson) Vapour changing back to water is called ______.
**Answer: condensing**
