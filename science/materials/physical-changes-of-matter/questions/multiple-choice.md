# Physical changes of matter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ice melting to water is a change of…
a) state  b) kind  c) colour only  d) owner
**Answer: a) state** — Melting changes the state — solid to liquid — not the substance.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A physical change can be…
a) reversed, leaving the same substance  b) never undone  c) only done in a laboratory  d) seen only once
**Answer: a) reversed, leaving the same substance** — Reversibility with the same substance is the mark of the physical change.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Water vapour turning back to water is called…
a) condensing  b) melting  c) burning  d) rusting
**Answer: a) condensing** — Vapour condenses back to liquid water.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) After ice melts and the water freezes again, you have…
a) the same water  b) a new substance  c) steam  d) nothing
**Answer: a) the same water** — The substance stays itself through the change of state.

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Cambodia's Grade 6 book teaches changes of matter as…
a) Lesson 12  b) Lesson 1  c) the last lesson  d) an Earth lesson
**Answer: a) Lesson 12** — Lesson 12 — straight before pure substances and mixtures (Lesson 13).

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is a physical change?
a) water evaporating  b) wood burning  c) iron rusting  d) food cooking
**Answer: a) water evaporating** — Evaporation is a change of state; the others make new substances.
