Today: **Gases in the air — oxygen and carbon dioxide** — Seychelles' way, from its Integrated Science unit booklets (Unit 2).

Seychelles' Unit 2 Topic 5, "Some gases in the air", prepares oxygen by heating potassium permanganate and collects the gas that burning things need. Air is a mixture, and its gases can be made, collected and tested one at a time — oxygen is the part of the air that keeps a flame alive.

Burning carbon makes a different gas: carbon dioxide, which turns limewater milky — the test that names it. The same topic shows charcoal made by burning wood in a limited supply of air: with little air the wood does not burn away to ash, but chars into charcoal. One element, carbon, and the amount of air decide what burning gives.
