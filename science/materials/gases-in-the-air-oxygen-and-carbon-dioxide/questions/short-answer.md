# Gases in the air — oxygen and carbon dioxide — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: oxygen — the gas burning needs.
**Answer:** Seychelles' Unit 2 Topic 5, "Some gases in the air", prepares oxygen by heating potassium permanganate and collects the gas that burning things need.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What makes limewater turn milky, and how is charcoal made?
**Answer:** Carbon dioxide turns limewater milky; charcoal is made by burning wood in a limited supply of air.
