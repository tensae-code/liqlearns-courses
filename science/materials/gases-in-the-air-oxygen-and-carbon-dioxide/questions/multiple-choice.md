# Gases in the air — oxygen and carbon dioxide — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Oxygen is prepared in this lesson by heating…
a) potassium permanganate  b) limewater  c) charcoal  d) sand
**Answer: a) potassium permanganate**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The gas that turns limewater milky is…
a) carbon dioxide  b) oxygen  c) nitrogen  d) steam
**Answer: a) carbon dioxide**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Charcoal is made by burning wood with…
a) a limited supply of air  b) no wood  c) extra oxygen  d) water
**Answer: a) a limited supply of air**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Burning carbon makes…
a) carbon dioxide  b) oxygen  c) limewater  d) charcoal only
**Answer: a) carbon dioxide**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Oxygen is the part of air that…
a) keeps burning going  b) turns limewater milky  c) makes wood char  d) dissolves in limewater
**Answer: a) keeps burning going**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Air is…
a) a mixture of gases  b) one single gas  c) a liquid  d) pure oxygen
**Answer: a) a mixture of gases**
