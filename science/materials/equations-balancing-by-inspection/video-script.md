# Video script — Equations — balancing by inspection

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This is a FULL script — the placement names the need.

---

Watch my hands. We balance by looking — inspection.

K + O2 -> K2O. Count with me.
Left: potassium 1. Oxygen 2.
Right: potassium 2. Oxygen 1.
Nothing matches. We fix it — with numbers in FRONT only.
Never touch the small numbers. They belong to the substance.

Oxygen first. Right side needs 2 oxygens.
Put 2 in front of K2O: K + O2 -> 2K2O.
Count again. Oxygen: 2 and 2. Good.
Potassium: left 1, right 4. Not yet.

Put 4 in front of K: 4K + O2 -> 2K2O.
Count everything, one last time.
Potassium: 4 and 4. Oxygen: 2 and 2.
Equal. Balanced. Done.

Now you. Same game: try, count, fix, count again.
