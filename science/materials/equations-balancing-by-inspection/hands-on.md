# Hands-on — Equations — balancing by inspection

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Stage-by-stage balancing
Balance K + O₂ → K₂O on paper in stages, writing the atom tally under the equation after EVERY change, exactly as the picture does. Then do C + O₂ → CO₂ and Mg + 2HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂ the same way (the last is balanced as written — prove it by tally).

## Why-not-a-subscript
Write what H₂O₂ is (a different substance — hydrogen peroxide) beside H₂O. One sentence: why balancing may never touch subscripts.
