# Hands-on — Oxides, acids, bases and salts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The comparison bench (adult-led)
With an adult, run the book's Experiment 1 shape on the three safe samples — vinegar, lemon juice, filtered ash water — with indicator paper. Record each sample's colour change in a table.

## Ash water, filtered
Filter ash water through cloth as the book's sample is made, and look at its slipperiness between (washed) fingers — the base's feel, met on a weak home base only.
