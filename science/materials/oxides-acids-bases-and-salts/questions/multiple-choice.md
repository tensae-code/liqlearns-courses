# Oxides, acids, bases and salts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Every oxide must contain —**
a) hydrogen  b) oxygen  c) salt
**Answer: b) oxygen**

**2. CO₂ + H₂O makes —**
a) a base  b) carbonic acid, an acid  c) a salt directly
**Answer: b) carbonic acid, an acid**

**3. Acids turn blue indicator paper —**
a) blue  b) green  c) red
**Answer: c) red**

**4. Bases turn red indicator paper —**
a) blue  b) red  c) yellow
**Answer: a) blue**

**5. Acid + base gives —**
a) only water  b) salt + water  c) only gas
**Answer: b) salt + water**

**6. A strong acid spills on skin. First —**
a) taste it to check  b) wash at once with plenty of clean water  c) cover it with ash
**Answer: b) wash at once with plenty of clean water**
