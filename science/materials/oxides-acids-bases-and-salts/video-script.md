# Video script — Oxides, acids, bases and salts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An **oxide** — an element joined with **oxygen**.
Four characters: **basic, acidic, amphoteric, neutral**.
Acidic oxide and water — an **acid**. Sour! Blue paper turns **red**.
Basic oxide and water — a **base**. Bitter! Red paper turns **blue** — and **never taste it**.
**Acid + base = salt + water.** That meeting is **neutralisation**.
A strong acid or base spills? **Wash at once, with plenty of clean water.**
