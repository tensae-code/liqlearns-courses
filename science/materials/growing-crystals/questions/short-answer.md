# Growing crystals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: making crystals grow.
**Answer:** Seychelles' Unit 3 Project, "Making crystals", grows crystals of magnesium sulphate or copper sulphate from solution.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What shape do crystals have, and from what are these crystals grown?
**Answer:** Regular shapes with straight edges; they are grown from a solution of magnesium sulphate or copper sulphate.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
