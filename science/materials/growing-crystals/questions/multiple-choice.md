# Growing crystals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Crystals in this project are grown from…
a) a solution of magnesium sulphate or copper sulphate  b) dry sand  c) pure water only  d) a flame
**Answer: a) a solution of magnesium sulphate or copper sulphate**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A crystal's shape is…
a) regular, with straight edges  b) always round  c) shapeless  d) different every time for the same substance
**Answer: a) regular, with straight edges**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Crystals form as a strong solution…
a) cools or dries  b) is shaken hard  c) is boiled away at once  d) is frozen solid
**Answer: a) cools or dries**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Growing crystals takes…
a) time — they grow slowly  b) one second  c) a shout  d) darkness only
**Answer: a) time — they grow slowly**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The dish of solution should be…
a) left undisturbed  b) stirred all day  c) kept on a flame  d) covered in soil
**Answer: a) left undisturbed**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) A crystal grows bigger as…
a) more of the substance joins it in order  b) it melts  c) it is broken up  d) water is poured off
**Answer: a) more of the substance joins it in order**
