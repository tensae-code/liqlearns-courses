Today: **Growing crystals** — Seychelles' way, from its Integrated Science unit booklets (Unit 3).

Seychelles' Unit 3 Project, "Making crystals", grows crystals of magnesium sulphate or copper sulphate from solution. A solution that holds as much dissolved salt as it can gives its salt back slowly as solid crystals when it cools or dries — growing, not just appearing: the crystals get bigger as more of the substance joins them in order.

What marks a crystal is its shape: crystals have regular shapes with straight edges. The regularity is the sign that the substance is building itself in an ordered way, the same order in every crystal of that substance. The project is a making lesson — prepare the solution, let the crystals form, and observe their shapes closely.
