# Video script — Dissolving — when solids disappear into liquids

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Dissolving — when solids disappear into liquids** — Serbia's way, from its Grade 3 *Природа и друштво* (Вода и друге течности као растварачи (Water and other liquids as solvents), printed p. 112).

Some solids **dissolve** in a liquid: they disappear into it, spread through it, and are still there — the sweet taste proves the sugar. A liquid that dissolves things this way is a **solvent**, and **water and other liquids** work as solvents. Dissolving is disappearing without being gone.

Serbia's book settles what changes the speed by racing pairs: **fine sugar against cubes** — the fine, with more surface touching the water, against the lump; and **stirring against still** — the stirred glass raced against the glass left alone. Each race changes one thing only, so the race itself is the proof.

The final pair races the water itself: the same solid dissolved in **warm water against cold**. Three races together — size, stirring, warmth — give the whole lesson: dissolving has a speed, and the speed can be changed.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
