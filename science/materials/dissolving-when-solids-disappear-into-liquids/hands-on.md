# Hands-on — Dissolving — when solids disappear into liquids

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run one dissolving race (two glasses, sugar, water)
Set up ONE of the book's races fairly: same water, same amount of sugar, only one thing different — fine sugar in one glass and a cube in the other, or one glass stirred and one left still. Time which glass clears first and write the result in one sentence: what was changed, and which was faster.
