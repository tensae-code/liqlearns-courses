Today: **How gases change with temperature and pressure** — South Sudan's way, from its Ministry of General Education and Instruction Primary Science books (see lesson/sources.md for the exact books and pages).

South Sudan's Primary 8 book teaches the relationship between **temperature, pressure and volume of air** as its own topic. The guide's model answers give its rules: **heated air expands** — and heated water vapour expanding will burst a sealed container.

The second rule runs the other way: **increasing pressure reduces volume**. Air can be squeezed into a smaller space — and pushes back — in a way solids and liquids do not.

Temperature, pressure and volume move together: the guide's answers use them to explain **wind — air moving from high pressure to low pressure**. One relationship, three names, and the weather itself as its biggest example.
