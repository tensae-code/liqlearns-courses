# How gases change with temperature and pressure — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Heated air expands.
**Answer:** South Sudan's Primary 8 book teaches the relationship between **temperature, pressure and volume of air** as its own topic. The guide's model answers give its rules: **heated air expands** — and heated water vapour expanding will burst a sealed container.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Pressure squeezes air smaller.
**Answer:** The second rule runs the other way: **increasing pressure reduces volume**. Air can be squeezed into a smaller space — and pushes back — in a way solids and liquids do not.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: The three together — and where wind comes from.
**Answer:** Temperature, pressure and volume move together: the guide's answers use them to explain **wind — air moving from high pressure to low pressure**. One relationship, three names, and the weather itself as its biggest example.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Heated air expands → Pressure squeezes air smaller → The three together — and where wind comes from — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
