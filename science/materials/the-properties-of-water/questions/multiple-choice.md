# The properties of water — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Pure water is…
a) Blue
b) Colourless, with no taste and no smell of its own
c) Sweet
d) Thick like oil
**Answer: b) Colourless, with no taste and no smell of its own**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Water in a bottle takes…
a) The bottle's colour
b) The shape of the vessel that holds it
c) A solid shape
d) No shape at all
**Answer: b) The shape of the vessel that holds it**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) When water is poured into a wider bowl, its amount…
a) Doubles
b) Stays the same while its shape changes
c) Halves
d) Disappears
**Answer: b) Stays the same while its shape changes**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Water that is coloured or has a smell…
a) Is purer
b) Carries something in it — it fails water's own marks
c) Is always safe
d) Is frozen
**Answer: b) Carries something in it — it fails water's own marks**
