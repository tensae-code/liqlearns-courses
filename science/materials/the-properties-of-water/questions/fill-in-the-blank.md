# The properties of water — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Pure water has no colour, no taste and no _____ of its own.
**Answer: smell**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Water _____ and takes the shape of the vessel that holds it.
**Answer: flows**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Poured from vessel to vessel, water's _____ stays the same.
**Answer: amount**
