# The properties of water — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the physical properties of water the lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Pure water is colourless, has no taste and no smell of its own, and is a liquid — it flows and takes the shape of its vessel while keeping its amount.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why are water's properties a first test of drinking water?
**Answer:** Because water that is coloured, tasting or smelling carries something in it; water showing only water's own marks — colourless, tasteless, without smell — passes the first test.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book set this lesson inside its water run?
**Answer:** So the learner can read every later water — sea water, dam water, drinking water — by these marks of water itself.
