Today: **The properties of water** — Angola's way: know water by its own marks.

Pure water is colourless, with no taste and no smell of its own. It flows, and it takes the shape of the vessel that holds it — while its amount stays its own.

So coloured or smelling water tells you something is carried in it. The properties are water's name — and a first test.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
