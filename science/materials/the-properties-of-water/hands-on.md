# Hands-on — The properties of water

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shape of the vessel
Pour one measured amount of water into a tall thin bottle, then a wide bowl, then back into the measure. Draw each shape and check the amount returns the same.

## The marks of water
Set out water and two other clear liquids an adult provides (for example salt water and sugared water). Sort them by the lesson's marks — colour, smell, taste where safe — and say which is pure water.
