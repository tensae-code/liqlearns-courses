# Lesson plan — Designing and modelling machines

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Materials · **Lesson:** Designing and modelling machines
**Place in the curriculum:** appended to Unit 4 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** designing and modelling machines — machine designs and models, as Zimbabwe's Grade 6 guide teaches the unit
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Machine designs** — Zimbabwe's Grade 6 guide teaches **machine designs and models** as a named unit: a machine begins as a design — the plan of what it will be and do — before it is a thing.
2. **Making a model** — A **model** is the design made small and touchable: building the model tests the design before the real machine is built, and shows what the drawing could not.
3. **Design, model, improve** — The unit's rhythm is design, then model, then improve what the model shows. That rhythm is the lesson's whole point — no machine in the topic is finished at the first drawing.
