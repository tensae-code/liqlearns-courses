## Sub lesson 2 — Making a model

A **model** is the design made small and touchable: building the model tests the design before the real machine is built, and shows what the drawing could not.

## Sub lesson 3 — Design, model, improve

The unit's rhythm is design, then model, then improve what the model shows. That rhythm is the lesson's whole point — no machine in the topic is finished at the first drawing.

## The key ideas

- **Machine designs** — a machine begins as a design.
- **Making a model** — the model tests the design.
- **Design, model, improve** — design, model, improve.
