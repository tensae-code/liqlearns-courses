# Designing and modelling machines — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Machine designs" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's Grade 6 guide teaches **machine designs and models** as a named unit: a machine begins as a design — the plan of what it will be and do — before it is a thing.

**2.** What does the part "Making a model" teach?
**Answer:** A **model** is the design made small and touchable: building the model tests the design before the real machine is built, and shows what the drawing could not.

**3.** What does the part "Design, model, improve" teach?
**Answer:** The unit's rhythm is design, then model, then improve what the model shows.
