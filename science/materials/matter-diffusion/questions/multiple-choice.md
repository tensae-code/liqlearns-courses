# Matter — diffusion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Diffusion is particles spreading from —
a) low to high concentration  b) high to low concentration  c) cold to hot only
**Answer: b) high to low concentration** — until evenly spread.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** You smell perfume across the room because —
a) sound carries it  b) its particles diffuse through the air  c) light carries it
**Answer: b) its particles diffuse through the air**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Ink in still water colours the whole jar by —
a) dissolving the jar  b) diffusion  c) boiling
**Answer: b) diffusion**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Diffusion is fastest in —
a) solids  b) liquids  c) gases
**Answer: c) gases** — fast particles, weak attraction.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A solid's particles —
a) travel freely  b) only vibrate in place  c) do not move at all
**Answer: b) only vibrate in place** — so solids essentially do not diffuse.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Liquids diffuse more slowly than gases because their particles —
a) are heavier in colour  b) move slower with stronger attraction  c) are bigger
**Answer: b) move slower with stronger attraction**
