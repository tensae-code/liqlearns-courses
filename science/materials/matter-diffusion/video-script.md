# Video script — Matter — diffusion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Spray! Perfume in the corner.
Wait. You smell it — far away. Who carried it? Nobody.
The particles spread. Crowded here — few there.
They wander until they are even. That is **diffusion**.
In water, ink blooms and spreads — slower.
In a solid? The particles only shake in place. No spreading.
Fast in gas. Slow in liquid. In a solid — still.
