# Hands-on — The candle trick — air and burning

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch the trick, adult-led
An adult lights a candle on a safe surface and covers it with a glass while you watch from a safe place. Before the glass goes on, say your prediction out loud. After the flame goes out, say what changed — and what the flame ran out of.

## Air or no air chart
Make a two-column chart: With new air | Trapped under the glass. Draw the flame in each column and write one sentence under each drawing saying what happens and why.

## Explain the trick
Draw the candle and glass in three steps — flame burning, glass on, flame out — and, pointing at your drawings, explain the trick to someone at home, as Finland's activity asks the watcher to do: what happens to a flame under a glass, and why.
