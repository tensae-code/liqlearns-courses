Today: **The candle trick — air and burning** — Finland's way, from its Luova LUMA activity bank.

An adult lights a candle and puts a glass over it. Watch: the flame changes, and then it goes out. Nothing blew on it.
The flame was burning, and burning needs air. Under the glass it had only the trapped air — and when that air was used up, the burning stopped.
So the trick is not magic. A flame with air burns. A flame trapped away from new air goes out.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
