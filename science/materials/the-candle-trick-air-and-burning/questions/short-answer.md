# The candle trick — air and burning — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Describe the candle trick: what is done, and what is seen?
**Answer:** An adult puts a glass over a lit candle so no new air can get in; the flame changes and then goes out, though nothing blew on it and nothing touched it.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is the trick worth predicting before it is done?
**Answer:** Because the prediction — keep burning, grow, or go out, with a reason — is what the result then tests: the flame going out proves the idea about air, not just a surprising sight.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the flame go out under the glass?
**Answer:** Burning needs air. The flame uses the small store of air trapped under the glass, and when that air is used up and no new air can get in, the burning stops.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An adult lifts the glass and lights the candle again, and it burns. What does that show?
**Answer:** It shows the flame stopped for lack of air, not because the candle was spoiled: with new air around it, the same candle burns again.
