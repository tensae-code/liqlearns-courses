# The candle trick — air and burning — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the candle trick, a glass is put over a lit _____.
**Answer: candle**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Under the glass, no new _____ can get in to the flame.
**Answer: air**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Burning needs _____.
**Answer: air**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The flame goes out when the trapped air is _____ up.
**Answer: used**
