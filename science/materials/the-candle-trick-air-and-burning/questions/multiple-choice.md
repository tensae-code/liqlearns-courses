# The candle trick — air and burning — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the candle trick, an adult puts a glass over…
a) A cup of water  b) A lit candle  c) An unlit candle  d) A paper flower
**Answer: b) A lit candle**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens to the flame under the glass?
a) It grows bigger  b) It changes and then goes out  c) It turns blue forever  d) Nothing at all
**Answer: b) It changes and then goes out**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) What blew the flame out under the glass?
a) Wind  b) A hand  c) Nothing — no new air could get in  d) Water
**Answer: c) Nothing — no new air could get in**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Burning needs…
a) Glass  b) Air  c) Milk  d) Sand
**Answer: b) Air**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The flame goes out because…
a) The glass is cold  b) The trapped air is used up and no new air can get in  c) The candle is finished  d) The flame is frightened
**Answer: b) The trapped air is used up and no new air can get in**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Who handles the lit candle in this lesson?
a) The youngest child  b) Nobody — it lights itself  c) An adult, while learners watch safely  d) The companion, blindfolded
**Answer: c) An adult, while learners watch safely**
