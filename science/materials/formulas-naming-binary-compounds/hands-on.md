# Hands-on — Formulas — naming binary compounds

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Name-and-build pairs
One partner writes a formula (from NaCl, MgO, FeCl₂, FeCl₃, Cu₂O, CO, CO₂, SO₃, N₂O), the other names it aloud; swap. Any name missing a needed Roman numeral is replayed.

## Common-name hunt
List compounds at home known by common names — water, table salt, limestone, ammonia cleaner. Write the systematic name beside each one you can.
