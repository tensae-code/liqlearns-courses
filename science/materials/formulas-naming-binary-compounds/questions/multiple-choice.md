# Formulas — naming binary compounds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** FeCl₂ is named —
a) iron chloride  b) iron(II) chloride  c) iron(III) chloride
**Answer: b) iron(II) chloride** — — two chlorines of valence 1 make iron's valence 2.

**2.** CO₂ is named —
a) carbon oxide  b) carbon monoxide  c) carbon dioxide
**Answer: c) carbon dioxide**

**3.** The non-metal chlorine in a compound name becomes —
a) chlorate  b) chloride  c) chlorous
**Answer: b) chloride**

**4.** Cu₂O is named —
a) copper(II) oxide  b) copper oxide  c) copper(I) oxide
**Answer: c) copper(I) oxide** — — two coppers share oxygen's valence 2, so each copper is 1.

**5.** N₂O is named —
a) nitrogen oxide  b) dinitrogen monoxide  c) nitrogen dioxide
**Answer: b) dinitrogen monoxide**
