# Water in three states — melting and evaporating — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about water in three states — melting and evaporating?
**Answer: Water's three aggregate states — liquid, ice, vapour — with a burner experiment melting ice toward steam, and the named changes (melting, evaporation, cooling back), plus twin ice cubes proving melting speeds up with warmth Water's aggregate states — solid, liquid and gas — watched in a heated test tube and in a bottle of water left in the freezer: frozen to ice, melted back to liquid, heated away**

**2.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about the wonderful transformations of water?
**Answer: Kazakhstan's books teach this as their own lesson — "The wonderful transformations of water".**
