# Water in three states — melting and evaporating — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** Water's three aggregate states — liquid, ice, vapour — with a burner experiment melting ice toward steam, and the named changes (melting, ______, cooling back), plus twin ice cubes proving melting speeds up with warmth
**Answer: evaporation**

**2.** The wonderful ______ of water — taught as its own lesson in Kazakhstan's books; the books and pages are named in Sources.
**Answer: transformations**

**3.** How water can change — taught as its own lesson in ______'s books; the books and pages are named in Sources.
**Answer: Kazakhstan**
