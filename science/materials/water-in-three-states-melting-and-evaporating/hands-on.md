# Hands-on — Water in three states — melting and evaporating

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — Water in three states — melting and evaporating. Water's three aggregate states — liquid, ice, vapour — with a burner experiment melting ice toward steam, and the named changes (melting, evaporation, cooling back), plus twin ice cubes proving melting speeds up with warmth Water's aggregate states — solid, liquid and gas — watched in a heated test tube and in a bottle of water left in the freezer: frozen to ice, melted back to liquid, heated away Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
