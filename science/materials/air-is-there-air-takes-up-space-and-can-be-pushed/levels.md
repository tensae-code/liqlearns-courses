# Air is there — air takes up space and can be pushed — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner proves air is matter that takes up space — running the cup-and-paper experiment straight (dry) and tipped (bubbles, wet) — and shows air pushed and pushing back with a balloon or pump, naming wind as moving air's push, answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 2 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — doing the lesson's own experiments and sorts by hand, naming each idea aloud, and reasoning about new examples until the teaching is the learner's own.
