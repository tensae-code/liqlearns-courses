# Hands-on — Air is there — air takes up space and can be pushed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The dry-paper proof
Paper fixed in a cup, cup pushed straight down into water, paper felt dry; then tipped, bubbles watched, paper felt wet. Say aloud: the air was the cup's invisible filling, and the bubbles are it, leaving.

## Hard with air
Pump a bicycle tyre (or squeeze a sealed empty bottle with its cap on) and feel it resist and harden as more air is pushed in. Say aloud: the hardness is air — pushed in, pushing back.
