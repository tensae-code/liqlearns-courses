# Air is there — air takes up space and can be pushed — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A friend says an empty bottle contains nothing. Answer them from this lesson.
**Answer: The bottle is full of air. Air is invisible, but it is real matter and takes up space — the bottle is empty of water, not empty of everything.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What two things happen, in order, when the cup is tipped under the water?
**Answer: First the trapped air escapes as bubbles; then the water rushes in to take the space the air left. One thing leaves, another enters.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name two everyday jobs done by pushed air.
**Answer: Any two of: filling a balloon, making a tyre or ball hard, the wind filling a sail / turning a windmill / drying clothes / carrying seeds, a fan's breeze cooling a room.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) You wave a stiff card quickly and feel resistance. What are you feeling?
**Answer: Air pushing back against the card. The air was there all along; the fast card makes its push impossible to miss.**
