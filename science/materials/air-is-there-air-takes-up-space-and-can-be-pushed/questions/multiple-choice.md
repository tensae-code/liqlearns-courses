# Air is there — air takes up space and can be pushed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The paper in the cup stayed dry because…
a) The paper was waterproof  b) Air trapped in the cup took up the space, so water could not enter  c) The cup was magic  d) Water cannot wet paper
**Answer: b) Air trapped in the cup took up the space, so water could not enter**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) When the cup is tipped under water…
a) Nothing happens  b) Bubbles of air escape and water rushes in  c) The paper dries further  d) The air turns to water
**Answer: b) Bubbles of air escape and water rushes in**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Air takes up space. This shows air is…
a) Nothing at all  b) Real matter, even though invisible  c) A kind of light  d) Only a feeling
**Answer: b) Real matter, even though invisible**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A bicycle tyre pumped full feels hard because…
a) The rubber hardens itself  b) So much air is pushed in that it pushes back hard on every side  c) The pump heats it  d) Water fills it
**Answer: b) So much air is pushed in that it pushes back hard on every side**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Wind is…
a) Air standing still  b) Moving air, and its push can drive sails and dry clothes  c) A sound  d) Warm water
**Answer: b) Moving air, and its push can drive sails and dry clothes**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Blowing up a balloon works because…
a) Breath is pushed air, and it pushes the balloon's skin outward as it fills  b) The balloon grows by itself  c) Air disappears inside  d) Rubber makes air
**Answer: a) Breath is pushed air, and it pushes the balloon's skin outward as it fills**
