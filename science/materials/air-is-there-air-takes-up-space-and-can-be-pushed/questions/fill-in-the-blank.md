# Air is there — air takes up space and can be pushed — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Air is real _____ — it takes up space even though we cannot see it.
**Answer: matter**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) When the cup tips, the escaping air rises as _____.
**Answer: bubbles**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Air that moves is called _____.
**Answer: wind**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A pump pushes air into a tyre until the tyre is _____ with air.
**Answer: hard**
