# Video script — Air is there — air takes up space and can be pushed

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Air is there**.

Is an empty cup really empty? Push it, upside down, straight into water — the paper inside stays dry! The cup was full all along: full of air. Air is invisible, but it is real stuff, and it takes up space — that is why the water could not get in. Tip the cup: out come the bubbles, in rushes the water. And air can be pushed: blow up a balloon, pump a tyre hard with nothing but air, feel the wind — moving air — pushing a sail. Air is there. You cannot see it, but now you can prove it.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
