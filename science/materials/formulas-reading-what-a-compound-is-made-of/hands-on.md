# Hands-on — Formulas — reading what a compound is made of

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Formula reading walk
Write these formulas on cards: NaCl, Al₂O₃, CaCO₃, C₆H₈O₇. Read each aloud to a partner as a shopping list of atoms; your partner checks the counts.

## Counters for a formula
Use stones or bottle tops: lay out the atoms of Al₂O₃ — 2 of one colour, 3 of another. Then lay out CaCO₃. Photograph or sketch both.
