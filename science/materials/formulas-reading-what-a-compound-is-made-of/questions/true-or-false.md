# Formulas — reading what a compound is made of — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** The subscript in a formula counts the atoms of the element just before it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A mixture has a fixed ratio like a compound does.
**Answer: False**

**3.** Sulfur can travel as S₈, a polyatomic molecule.
**Answer: True**

**4.** In CaCO₃ the subscript 3 counts the calcium atoms.
**Answer: False**
