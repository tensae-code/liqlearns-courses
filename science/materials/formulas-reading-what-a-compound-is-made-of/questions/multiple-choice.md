# Formulas — reading what a compound is made of — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In Al₂O₃ there are —
a) 2 aluminium and 3 oxygen atoms  b) 2 oxygen and 3 aluminium atoms  c) 5 aluminium atoms
**Answer: a) 2 aluminium and 3 oxygen atoms**

**2.** A compound is different elements joined —
a) in any ratio  b) in a fixed ratio  c) only by mixing
**Answer: b) in a fixed ratio**

**3.** In NaCl, the number of chlorine atoms is —
a) 0  b) 1  c) 2
**Answer: b) 1** — — no subscript means one.

**4.** Which of these travels as a diatomic molecule?
a) He  b) S₈  c) O₂
**Answer: c) O₂**

**5.** Citric acid C₆H₈O₇ contains —
a) 3 elements and 21 atoms  b) 21 elements  c) 3 atoms
**Answer: a) 3 elements and 21 atoms** — — 6 + 8 + 7 = 21.
