# Formulas — reading what a compound is made of — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or formula.

**1.** In H₂O there are ___ hydrogen atoms and 1 oxygen atom. (2)
**Answer: 2**

**2.** A formula's small low number is called a ___ . (subscript)
**Answer: subscript**

**3.** Oxygen gas is written ___ because its molecules hold two atoms. (O₂)
**Answer: O₂**
