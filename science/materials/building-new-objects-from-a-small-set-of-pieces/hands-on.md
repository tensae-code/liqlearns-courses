# Hands-on — Building new objects from a small set of pieces

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Take apart, rebuild
Build a small object from a set of pieces — blocks, sticks, clips. Sketch it, take it apart, and build a different object with a different job from the same pieces. Sketch that too.

## Count the pieces
Before and after rebuilding, count and list the pieces. Write the proof that nothing was added or lost.
