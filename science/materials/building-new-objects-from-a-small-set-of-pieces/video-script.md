# Video script — Building new objects from a small set of pieces

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Building new objects from a small set of pieces** — the United States' way, from its Utah SEEd Standards books (Grade 2).

Start by seeing the **pieces**: many objects are **made of a small set of pieces** joined in one arrangement (Grade 2, 2.3.3, printed p. 98). The object is not one solid lump of stuff — it is pieces, plus the way they are put together. Name the pieces and you have the object's whole material.

The pieces can come apart: **disassemble** the object, and the same set of pieces can be **reshaped into a new object with a different function**. The tower's blocks become the bridge. Matter is conserved — the pieces are unchanged — while arrangement and function change completely. That is the lesson's model, and the book has pupils model it with their own hands.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
