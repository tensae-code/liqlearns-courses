# Video script — How materials behave: stretchy, breakable, shapable

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Pull it. Does it spring back? **Stretchy.**
Drop it — no! Some things **break**. Carry them with care.
Beat it flat, draw it thin — the metals **shape**.
Snap! **Brittle** things do none of these.
Ask the three questions, and every material tells its family.
