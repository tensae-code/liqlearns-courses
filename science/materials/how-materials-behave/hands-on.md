# Hands-on — How materials behave: stretchy, breakable, shapable

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The three-question sort
For any material ask three questions in order: Does it spring back? Can it be drawn or beaten into shape? Or does it snap? Sort ten home materials by the answers.

## Careful hands
Choose the three most breakable things in your home. Move them — with permission — to safer places: off edges, wrapped, low down.

## Wire and sheet hunt
Find one wire thing and one flat-sheet thing at home (a fence wire, a metal roof sheet). Say which behaviour made each possible.
