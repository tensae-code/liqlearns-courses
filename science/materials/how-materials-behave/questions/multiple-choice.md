# How materials behave: stretchy, breakable, shapable — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these breaks easily?**
a) a rubber band  b) a drinking glass  c) a sock
**Answer: b) a drinking glass**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) What does a stretchy material do when you let go?**
a) stays stretched forever  b) springs back  c) breaks
**Answer: b) springs back** — that springing back is elasticity.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is stretchy?**
a) a stone  b) a rubber band  c) a glass
**Answer: b) a rubber band**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) Beating a metal into a flat sheet is called —**
a) elasticity  b) malleability  c) brittleness
**Answer: b) malleability**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) Which material is brittle?**
a) gold  b) charcoal  c) copper
**Answer: b) charcoal** — charcoal neither stretches nor flattens — it snaps.
