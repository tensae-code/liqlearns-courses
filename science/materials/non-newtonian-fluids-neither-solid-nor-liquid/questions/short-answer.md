# Non-Newtonian fluids — neither solid nor liquid — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: What a solid does, and what a liquid does.
**Answer:** A solid keeps its own shape: you can hold it, and it stays as it is.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The dough that is neither.
**Answer:** Indonesia's Grade 3 book (Bab 8, Rahasia Tiga Wujud Zat — the secret of the three states of matter) sets a challenge its states chapter cannot settle with the two clear cases: a soil/clay dough that is neither solid nor liquid.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Testing it against both.
**Answer:** The book's own way is a test, not a label: the dough is tested against the properties of both — held against what a solid must do and against what a liquid must do, property by property.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** What a solid does, and what a liquid does → The dough that is neither → Testing it against both — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
