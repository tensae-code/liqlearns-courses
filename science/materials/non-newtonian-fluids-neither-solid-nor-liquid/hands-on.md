# Hands-on — Non-Newtonian fluids — neither solid nor liquid

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Test the dough against both (kitchen materials)
Make a simple clay or soil dough (soil or flour with a little water, mixed thick). Test it as the book does: (1) Shape it and leave it — does it keep its shape like a solid? (2) Tip it — does it pour like a liquid? (3) Press it slowly, then quickly — does it answer the same way both times? Write one line for each test: solid-like, liquid-like, or neither. Your three lines are the lesson's verdict, reached by testing, not by being told.
