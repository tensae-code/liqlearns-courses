Today: **Making things — choosing materials** — Iran's way.

Every making starts with the thing itself, named first: Iran's Grade 1 book opens the making lesson by asking "what do I want to make?", and its Grade 2 book has pupils choose something to make by looking around them — the made thing comes first, the material second.. A material is chosen because of what the thing must do. Iran's Grade 6 book runs the full maker's round in its "let us design and build" lesson: look at the kinds there are, decide what yours will do, build it, and watch how it goes.
