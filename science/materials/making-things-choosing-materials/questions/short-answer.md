# Making things — choosing materials — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Say, in your own words, the two questions Iran's Grade 1 book starts every maker with.
**Answer: What do I want to make? And: what can / should it be made from — which material fits its job?**

**2.** Why did humans use wood and stone for shelter for thousands of years?
**Answer: Because a shelter's job is to keep out weather and danger, and wood and stone were the natural materials at hand whose properties fit that job.**
