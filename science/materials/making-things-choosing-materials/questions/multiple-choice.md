# Making things — choosing materials — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A maker's first question is…
a) What do I want to make?  b) What is the cheapest material?  c) What did I make last year?  d) What colour is it?
**Answer: a) What do I want to make?**

**2.** A cooking pot is not made of wood because…
a) Wood is too pretty  b) The material must stand heat without burning  c) Pots are always round  d) Wood cannot be cut
**Answer: b) The material must stand heat without burning**

**3.** A mould is used to…
a) Weigh a material  b) Give a made thing the shape its maker chose  c) Dry a material faster  d) Test a material's colour
**Answer: b) Give a made thing the shape its maker chose**

**4.** For thousands of years, shelter was built from…
a) Only plastic  b) Natural materials like wood and stone  c) Only glass  d) Paper and cloth only
**Answer: b) Natural materials like wood and stone**

**5.** When a built thing fails its test, the maker…
a) Throws away the idea of making  b) Goes back and chooses again  c) Blames the material  d) Stops designing forever
**Answer: b) Goes back and chooses again**
