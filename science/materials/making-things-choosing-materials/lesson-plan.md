# Lesson plan — Making things — choosing materials

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Materials · **Lesson:** Making things — choosing materials
**Place in the curriculum:** Materials — appended to Unit 4 (Materials) (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** how a maker starts from the thing they want to make and chooses the material that fits its job — Iran's way, from the Grade 1 question "what can a ruler be made from, and what does mother cook with?" through choosing something to make by looking around us (Grade 2), shaping a mould for a chocolate of your own shape (Grade 2), designing and building a car (Grade 6), to the building materials humans have used for a safe shelter for thousands of years (Grade 7)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **What do I want to make?** — Every making starts with the thing itself, named first: Iran's Grade 1 book opens the making lesson by asking "what do I want to make?", and its Grade 2 book has pupils choose something to make by looking around them — the made thing comes first, the material second.
2. **Choosing the material that fits the job** — A material is chosen because of what the thing must do. A ruler must be straight and stiff; a cooking pot must stand heat and not melt; a shelter must keep out weather and danger — for thousands of years humans used natural materials like wood and stone for shelter, as Iran's Grade 7 book tells it, and choosing still follows the job. Even a chocolate is shaped by choice: Iran's Grade 2 makers draw the shape they want and make a mould for it.
3. **Design it, build it, test it** — Iran's Grade 6 book runs the full maker's round in its "let us design and build" lesson: look at the kinds there are, decide what yours will do, build it, and watch how it goes. A design is a choosing plan; building tests the choices; what fails sends you back to choose again.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: see the lesson's own opening. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain sub lesson 1 in their own words: What do I want to make?.
2. Explain sub lesson 2 in their own words: Choosing the material that fits the job.
3. Explain sub lesson 3 in their own words: Design it, build it, test it.
