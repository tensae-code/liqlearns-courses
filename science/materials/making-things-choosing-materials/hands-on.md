# Hands-on — Making things — choosing materials

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Want it, then choose
Pick something small to make — a pencil holder, a card, a toy boat. Write the thing's name first. Then handle three materials you could use and choose one, saying the property that decided you: stiff, soft, waterproof, heat-standing, easy to cut.

## The mould test
Press a shape into soft clay or dough, lift it out, and fill the hollow with something that sets or melts and cools. Look at the shape that comes out: the mould carried your chosen shape into the new material.
