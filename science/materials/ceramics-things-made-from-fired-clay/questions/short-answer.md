# Ceramics — things made from fired clay — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Ceramics: shapes moulded from a special clay (kaolin for pure white ware), sun-dried and kiln-fired hard and porous, then glazed — salt dust, or a coating of tin oxide or lead oxide, melted on in the kiln — so the ware is non-porous, smooth and lasting; porcelain insulators, cups and plates, false teeth and bone joints, bricks and roof tiles.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science Grade 10*, printed p. 148.**
