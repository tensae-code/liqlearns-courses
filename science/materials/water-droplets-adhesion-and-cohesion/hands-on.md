# Hands-on — Water droplets — adhesion and cohesion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Round or flat
Place drops of water on a coin, on foil and on a leaf. Sketch each drop's shape and write which force won on each surface.

## The detergent touch
Touch one round drop with the tip of a toothpick dipped in detergent. Draw the drop before and after, and explain the change with the two forces.
