# Water droplets — adhesion and cohesion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Two forces in every drop.
**Answer:** Water pulls on itself: cohesive forces hold water molecules to each other.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Round or flat — and the detergent test.
**Answer:** When cohesion beats adhesion, the drop pulls itself into the roundest shape it can — a round drop.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Two forces in every drop → Round or flat — and the detergent test — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
