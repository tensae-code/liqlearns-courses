# Hands-on — Making natural dyes from plants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Try the parts
With adult help, put different plant parts — leaves, flowers, roots, bark, fruit skins — each in its own cup of water. Wait, then look: which cups have coloured water, and which plant part gave each colour?

## Watch it dissolve
Take the part that colours best. Watch the water as the colour leaves the plant part and spreads: say where the colour starts, how it spreads, and when the water is evenly coloured.

## Dye a piece
Dip a piece of plain material into the strongest coloured water, leave it, then lift it out and let it dry. Make a chart: Plant part | Colour in the water? | Colour on the material? Fill one row for each part, then, pointing at your chart, explain which plant parts make dyes and how, in your own words.
