# Video script — Making natural dyes from plants

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Making natural dyes from plants** — Timor-Leste's way, inside its Grade 3 mixtures unit.

Some plant parts hold colour. Try a part in water and watch: if the colour leaves the part and spreads through the water, that water is now a dye — it can colour materials.
Try part after part. The parts that give up their colour are the dye-makers; the parts that do not are a finding too.
That investigation is the lesson: which plant parts make colour, and how their colour dissolves in water.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
