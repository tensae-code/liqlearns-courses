# Making natural dyes from plants — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A natural _____ is a colour taken from plants and used to colour materials.
**Answer: dye**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The investigation finds which plant _____ make colour.
**Answer: parts**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The colour leaves the plant part and _____ in the water.
**Answer: dissolves**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The coloured water is the _____ that colours the material.
**Answer: dye**
