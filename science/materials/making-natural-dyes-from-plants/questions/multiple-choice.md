# Making natural dyes from plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A natural dye is…
a) A colour bought in a tin  b) A colour taken from plants and used to colour materials  c) Plain water  d) A kind of plant
**Answer: b) A colour taken from plants and used to colour materials**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The investigation starts by…
a) Guessing which parts colour  b) Trying plant parts and finding which ones make colour  c) Colouring the material first  d) Throwing the plants away
**Answer: b) Trying plant parts and finding which ones make colour**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A plant part that gives no colour is…
a) A wasted try  b) A finding too — it sorts that part out of the colour-makers  c) Proof the water is broken  d) Always a dye anyway
**Answer: b) A finding too — it sorts that part out of the colour-makers**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The colour gets from the plant part to the material by…
a) Jumping directly  b) Dissolving in water — the coloured water is the dye  c) Wind  d) It never moves
**Answer: b) Dissolving in water — the coloured water is the dye**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Put in water, a colour-making part's colour…
a) Stays locked in the part  b) Leaves the part and spreads through the whole cup  c) Disappears  d) Turns the water solid
**Answer: b) Leaves the part and spreads through the whole cup**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Coloured dye water is taught inside the mixtures unit because…
a) It is a mixture — colour mixed into water — made on purpose  b) It is not a mixture  c) Mixtures are a different subject  d) The book ran out of pages
**Answer: a) It is a mixture — colour mixed into water — made on purpose**
