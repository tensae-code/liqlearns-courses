# Colours dancing in milk — surface tension — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Drops of colour rest on milk's surface _____.
**Answer: film**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) At first the colours sit barely _____.
**Answer: moving**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Soap _____ the milk's surface film.
**Answer: breaks**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Soap breaks the film and chases the _____.
**Answer: fat**
