# Colours dancing in milk — surface tension — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What holds the colour drops almost still on the milk at first?
**Answer:** Milk's surface film — the skin-like top of the liquid — holds the surface together, and the drops rest on it where they were dropped.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) How is the dish set up before the soap touches it?
**Answer:** Milk is poured into a shallow dish and left to settle, then drops of colour are put on its surface.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What does the soap do, in the activity's own words?
**Answer:** Soap breaks the milk's surface film and chases the fat.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain the dance: why do the colours move when the soap touches?
**Answer:** The soap breaks the surface film and chases the fat, so the surface pulls and swirls instead of holding in one piece — and the colours resting on it are carried along, racing and mixing.
