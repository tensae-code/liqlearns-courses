# Colours dancing in milk — surface tension — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The drops of colour at first rest on…
a) The bottom of the dish  b) Milk's surface film  c) A spoon  d) The soap
**Answer: b) Milk's surface film**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Before the soap, the colours…
a) Dance at once  b) Sink straight down  c) Sit barely moving where they were dropped  d) Vanish
**Answer: c) Sit barely moving where they were dropped**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The touch that starts the dance is…
a) More colour  b) Soap  c) Cold water  d) A spoon stirring
**Answer: b) Soap**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The soap does what to the milk?
a) Freezes it  b) Breaks its surface film and chases the fat  c) Turns it into cheese  d) Heats it
**Answer: b) Breaks its surface film and chases the fat**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The colours dance because…
a) The soap paints them  b) The dish is shaken  c) The broken surface pulls and swirls, carrying them  d) They melt
**Answer: c) The broken surface pulls and swirls, carrying them**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The dancing proves the surface film was…
a) Unbreakable  b) Broken by the soap  c) Made of colour  d) Under the milk
**Answer: b) Broken by the soap**
