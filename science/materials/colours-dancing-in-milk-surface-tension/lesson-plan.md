# Lesson plan — Colours dancing in milk — surface tension

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Materials · **Lesson:** Colours dancing in milk — surface tension
**Place in the curriculum:** Materials — appended to Unit 4 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** colours dance in milk — drops of colour sit on milk's surface film, and a touch of soap breaks that film and chases the fat, so the colours swirl and dance — Finland's own way, in its Luova LUMA activity bank, is the milk dish itself: soap breaks the milk's surface film and chases the fat

## Sub lessons
1. **The film on milk** — Drops of colour rest on the surface of milk, held on its surface film, barely moving.
2. **Soap breaks the film** — A touch of soap breaks the milk's surface film and chases the fat, and the colours dance as the surface pulls and swirls.
