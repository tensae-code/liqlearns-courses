# Hands-on — Colours dancing in milk — surface tension

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The milk dish
With adult help, pour milk into a shallow dish, let it settle, and add drops of colour. Draw the dish. Then touch the surface with a little soap and watch closely: say the moment the colours start to move, and what you think broke.

## Before and after drawings
Draw the dish twice — colours resting, and colours dancing. Under the first write what holds the colours still; under the second write what the soap did to the film and the fat.

## Explain the dance
Pointing at your two drawings, explain to someone at home why the colours danced — the film, the soap breaking it, and the fat being chased — in your own words.
