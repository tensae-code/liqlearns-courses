# Video script — Colours dancing in milk — surface tension

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Colours dancing in milk — surface tension** — Finland's way, from its Luova LUMA activity bank.

Drops of colour sit on a dish of milk, resting on its surface film, almost still.
Then a touch of soap: the soap breaks the milk's surface film and chases the fat — and the colours dance, racing and swirling across the dish.
The soap did not paint them. It broke the film, and the moving surface carried the colours.
