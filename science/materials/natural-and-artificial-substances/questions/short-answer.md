# Natural and artificial substances — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about formation of substances in nature; artificial substances?
**Answer: Kazakhstan's books teach this as their own lesson — "Formation of substances in nature; artificial substances".**

**2.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about what kinds of substances there are?
**Answer: The books teach this part as classifying substances by origin and by state — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.**
