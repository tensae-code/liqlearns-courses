# Salts — preparation, names and uses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is NOT a salt-preparation route?
a) acid + base  b) acid + metal  c) salt + salt
**Answer: c) salt + salt**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Acid + carbonate gives salt + water + —
a) hydrogen  b) oxygen  c) carbon dioxide
**Answer: c) carbon dioxide**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Experiment 3.6 prepares —
a) baking soda  b) common salt  c) plaster
**Answer: b) common salt**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The salt of nitric acid is a —
a) nitride  b) nitrate  c) nitrite
**Answer: b) nitrate**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** (NH₄)₂HPO₄ is used as —
a) table salt  b) plaster  c) fertiliser
**Answer: c) fertiliser**
