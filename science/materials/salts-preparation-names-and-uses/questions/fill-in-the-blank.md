# Salts — preparation, names and uses — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or formula.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Zn + 2HCl → ___ + H₂ — the salt made is zinc chloride.
**Answer: ZnCl₂**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The salt of sulphuric acid is called a ___.
**Answer: sulphate**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Baking soda's formula is ___.
**Answer: NaHCO₃**
