# Video script — Salts — preparation, names and uses

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A salt is an acid with its hydrogen replaced.

Make one three ways: acid plus base, acid plus metal, acid plus carbonate.

Name it from its acid: binary acids give -ide, oxyacids give -ate.

NaCl seasons. CaCO3 builds. KNO3 feeds the field.

Six jars — and now you can read every label.
