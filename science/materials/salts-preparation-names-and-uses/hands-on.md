# Hands-on — Salts — preparation, names and uses

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Vinegar rock test (the book's project)
Drop vinegar on local stones, one drop each. Fizz = carbonate rock. Record each stone, its place, and its result in a table, and write the acid + carbonate word equation under it.

## Jar labelling
Write labels for the six use-table salts: full name, formula, one-line use, and the route you would use to prepare it. Check each formula's brackets and subscripts twice.
