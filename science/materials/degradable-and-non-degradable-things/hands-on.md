# Hands-on — Degradable and non-degradable things

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The burying test
Choose a safe patch of soil. Bury a leaf, a scrap of paper, a small piece of wood and a piece of plastic at finger depth, each with a marker. Water the patch like the rest of the ground. After two weeks, dig each one up gently and compare it with how it went in. Record which things the soil had started to take back and which came out unchanged.

## The litter sort
With an adult, collect the litter in one small area into two bags or piles: things that would break down in the soil, and things that would not. Weigh or count each pile. Say which pile your area would be rid of by itself, and which pile only people can clear.
