# Degradable and non-degradable things — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Things that break down and become part of the soil are called _____.
**Answer: degradable**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Plastic, glass and metal are _____ things — they stay for years without breaking down.
**Answer: non-degradable**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A fallen leaf is a _____ thing.
**Answer: degradable**
