# Degradable and non-degradable things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A degradable thing is one that…
a) Breaks down in the soil until it becomes part of the soil  b) Never changes at all  c) Is always made of plastic  d) Cannot be touched by water
**Answer: a) Breaks down in the soil until it becomes part of the soil**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is degradable?
a) A glass bottle  b) A tin can  c) A fallen leaf  d) A nylon cloth
**Answer: c) A fallen leaf**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A plastic bag thrown on the ground is still itself years later because it is…
a) Too heavy  b) Non-degradable  c) Made of soil  d) Alive
**Answer: b) Non-degradable**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Non-degradable things leave a place only when they are…
a) Rained on  b) Looked at  c) Collected, reused or recycled by people  d) Buried deeper
**Answer: c) Collected, reused or recycled by people**
