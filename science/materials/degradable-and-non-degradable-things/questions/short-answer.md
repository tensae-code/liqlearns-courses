# Degradable and non-degradable things — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens to a degradable thing left on the ground?
**Answer: It slowly breaks down — water, air and tiny living things in the soil rot and crumble it until it becomes part of the soil itself.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name two non-degradable things and say why they are a problem.
**Answer: Any two of: plastic bag, glass bottle, tin can, nylon cloth. They do not break down, so they pile up wherever they are thrown and only people can clear them.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the burying test?
**Answer: Bury the thing in soil, wait, and dig it up: if the soil has started breaking it down it is degradable; if it comes out unchanged it is non-degradable.**
