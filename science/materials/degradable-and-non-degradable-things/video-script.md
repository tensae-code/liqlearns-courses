Today: **Degradable and non-degradable things** — Bhutan's way: sorting things by what the soil does to them.

Drop a fruit peel on the ground and come back in some weeks: it has softened, darkened and crumbled — the soil is taking it back. That is a degradable thing. Now drop a plastic bag: come back in a year and it is still a plastic bag. Nothing in the soil breaks it down. That is a non-degradable thing. One test tells them apart: time in the soil.
