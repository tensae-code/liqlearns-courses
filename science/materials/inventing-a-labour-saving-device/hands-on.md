# Hands-on — Inventing a labour-saving device

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Design the saving
Pick a real job — lifting, carrying, opening, moving — and draw a device to save labour at it. Label the simple machines in your drawing and say what effort each one saves.

## Test and improve
Build the simplest working version with adult help if needed. Test it three times, note where it strains or slips, improve that one place, and test three more times. Compare the two tests in two sentences.
