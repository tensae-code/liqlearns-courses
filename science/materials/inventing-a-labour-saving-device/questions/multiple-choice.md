# Inventing a labour-saving device — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A labour-saving device is built to…
a) Do a job with less effort  b) Use more effort  c) Replace all workers  d) Weigh the load
**Answer: a) Do a job with less effort**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The device's parts are…
a) Only gears  b) The simple machines — lever, pulley, wheel and axle, inclined plane  c) Only engines  d) Only rope
**Answer: b) The simple machines — lever, pulley, wheel and axle, inclined plane**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The design begins by naming…
a) The paint colour  b) The price  c) The job — what work is to be saved  d) The inventor's name
**Answer: c) The job — what work is to be saved**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An invention is finished when it is…
a) Drawn once  b) Named  c) Stored away  d) Tested at its job
**Answer: d) Tested at its job**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Testing asks…
a) Does it really save labour — and where does it strain or slip?  b) Is it the heaviest?  c) Is it the oldest design?  d) Does it look new?
**Answer: a) Does it really save labour — and where does it strain or slip?**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Each failure found in testing becomes…
a) A reason to stop  b) An improvement to make, then a new test  c) A secret  d) A decoration
**Answer: b) An improvement to make, then a new test**
