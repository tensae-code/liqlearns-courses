# Inventing a labour-saving device — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What is a labour-saving device, and what is it made from?
**Answer:** A device that does a job with less effort, made from the simple machines — lever, pulley, wheel and axle, inclined plane — used alone or joined together.

**2.** Why does the design name the job first?
**Answer:** Because the job decides the machines: what work is to be saved tells the inventor which simple machine or machines the device needs.

**3.** What two questions does testing answer?
**Answer:** Does the device really save labour at its job — and where does it strain, slip or waste effort, which is where it can be improved.

**4.** Describe the invention circle the book teaches.
**Answer:** Design, make, test, improve: the device is designed, built, tested at its job, and each failure the test finds becomes an improvement — then it is tested again.
