# Inventing a labour-saving device — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** A labour-saving device does a job with less _____.
**Answer: effort**

**2.** The lever, pulley, wheel and axle and inclined plane are the simple _____.
**Answer: machines**

**3.** The design names the _____ first.
**Answer: job**

**4.** An invention is finished by testing and _____.
**Answer: improving**
