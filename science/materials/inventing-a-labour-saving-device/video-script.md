# Video script — Inventing a labour-saving device

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Inventing a labour-saving device** — China's way, from its 科学 (Science) textbooks.

Here is a job that takes effort. Now invent: which simple machines could save that labour — a lever, a pulley, a wheel, a slope, joined together? Design it, then build it.
But an invention is not done when it is built. Test it at its job. Where it strains — improve it. Test again. That circle is inventing.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
