# Soluble and insoluble substances — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A substance that dissolves in water is called _____.
**Answer: soluble**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Sand is _____ in water — it never dissolves.
**Answer: insoluble**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Dissolving is a _____ change, because no new substance is made.
**Answer: physical**
