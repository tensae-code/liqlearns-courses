# Soluble and insoluble substances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A soluble substance is one that…
a) Sinks at once  b) Dissolves in a liquid and spreads through it  c) Can never be tasted  d) Floats on every liquid
**Answer: b) Dissolves in a liquid and spreads through it**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Salt stirred into water seems to vanish, but we know it is still there because…
a) The glass gets heavier than the room  b) The water tastes salty  c) The water changes colour forever  d) The salt climbs out
**Answer: b) The water tastes salty**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Sand is insoluble because…
a) It is too light  b) It never dissolves, however long you stir  c) It is a liquid  d) It melts in water
**Answer: b) It never dissolves, however long you stir**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Dissolving is a physical change because…
a) A new substance is made  b) The substance stays itself and can be won back unchanged  c) The water disappears  d) Stirring is hard work
**Answer: b) The substance stays itself and can be won back unchanged**
