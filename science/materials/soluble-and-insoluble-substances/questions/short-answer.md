# Soluble and insoluble substances — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does a soluble substance do in water?
**Answer: It dissolves — it breaks into pieces too small to see and spreads evenly through the water, so it seems to vanish while still being there.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How can an insoluble substance be taken back out of water, and why does that work?
**Answer: By settling and filtering — it works because the substance never dissolved; it stayed as separate grains that a filter can hold back.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How could you prove dissolved salt is still salt?
**Answer: Let the water evaporate (or boil it away with an adult): the salt is left behind, unchanged — dissolving made no new substance.**
