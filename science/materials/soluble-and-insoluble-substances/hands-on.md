# Hands-on — Soluble and insoluble substances

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The four-glass test
Fill four glasses with the same amount of water. Add a spoon of salt to the first, sugar to the second, clean sand to the third, and flour or chalk dust to the fourth. Stir each glass twenty times, wait, and look from the side and the top. Sort the four substances into soluble and insoluble from what your eyes report — vanished into the water, or still itself.

## Winning the salt back
Dissolve two spoons of salt in half a glass of warm water. Pour the salty water into a shallow dish and leave it where sun or moving air reaches it. Look each day. When the water has gone, scrape up what remains, taste a grain carefully if an adult allows, and say what came back — and what that proves.
