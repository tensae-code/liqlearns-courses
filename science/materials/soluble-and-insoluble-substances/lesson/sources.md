Placement choice, on the record: this NEW LESSON was built for the Bhutan placements "NEW LESSON NEEDED — materials/soluble-and-insoluble-substances" — bhutan-placements.md, Class IV printed p. 36 and Class V printed p. 29 (two placements, one lesson). No existing general-science lesson covers soluble and insoluble substances as its own thing (checked the live repo at merge time — the dissolving lesson teaches solvents and dissolving speed by races, and the separating lessons use insolubility inside filtration, but neither names and sorts the soluble/insoluble pair as the thing itself), so it gets its own lesson. It sits in materials/ and is registered in CURRICULUM.md Unit 4 (Materials).

Bhutan's own way — Bhutan's Class IV book places soluble and insoluble substances inside its mixtures chapter, straight after pure substances and mixtures; its Class V book returns to the same glasses of water a year later and asks the sharper question — is dissolving a physical change? — IS part of this lesson's content, carried exactly as the placements describe it.
