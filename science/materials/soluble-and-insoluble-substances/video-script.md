Today: **Soluble and insoluble substances** — Bhutan's way: the stirring test, and the question its Class V book asks about it.

Salt in water: stir, and it vanishes — but taste, and it is there. Salt is soluble. Sand in water: stir all day, and the sand is still sand at the bottom. Sand is insoluble. And Bhutan's older pupils ask one more question: is dissolving a change that makes something new? No — evaporate the water and the salt returns, itself. Dissolving is a physical change.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
