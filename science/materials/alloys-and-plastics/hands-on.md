# Hands-on — Alloys and plastics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Made or not made?
List ten materials around you. Sort them: made materials (alloys, plastics and their kin) against materials met as they are. Say the rule you sorted by.

## One alloy, one plastic
Find one alloy-made thing and one plastic thing at home. For each, write what it is used for and why a made material serves there.
