# Alloys and plastics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Alloys and plastics are…
a) Living things  b) Made materials  c) Kinds of rock only  d) Weather
**Answer: b) Made materials**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The book's Arabic word for plastics is…
a) Lada'in  b) Pens  c) Suckers  d) Marshes
**Answer: a) Lada'in**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The reading stands…
a) Before the atom chapters  b) After the matter and reaction chapters  c) In the contents  d) In Grade 1
**Answer: b) After the matter and reaction chapters**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) An alloy is made by…
a) Growing  b) Mixing  c) Freezing only  d) Burning away
**Answer: b) Mixing**
