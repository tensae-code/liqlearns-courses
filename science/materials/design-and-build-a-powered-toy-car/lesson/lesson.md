# Design and build a powered toy car

## Do it first
Before you read: say out loud what you already think this lesson's one thing is — designing, building, testing and improving a powered toy car — its power chosen strong enough to run at least 1 metre, its weakness found by testing and fixed. Then read on: this is China's way in — its 科学 (Science) textbooks teach this thing at the places named in the sources, and this lesson follows their order.

## Sub lesson 1 — Designing a powered car — choosing its power

China's Grade 3 book closes its course with a design project: a **powered toy car**. First the class studies how toy cars are powered — **propeller** cars, **motor** cars, **sail** cars — because the design's first decision is the power: the device chosen must be **strong enough to run the car at least 1 metre**.

Power chosen, the materials are gathered and the car is built to the design: body, wheels, and the power device fitted so its push becomes the car's motion.

## Sub lesson 2 — Testing and improving the powered car

The built car is **shown, tested and measured**: its distance and time are recorded against the 1-metre target, and the class reviews each other's cars. A car that falls short has a findable reason — its power is too weak for its weight or its friction.

Improvement answers the test: a **thicker rubber band**, a **balloon that inflates** bigger — the power strengthened until the car meets its target. Design, build, test, improve: the year's last lesson is the whole method in one small car.

## The key ideas

- A powered car's first design decision is its **power** — propeller, motor or sail — chosen **strong enough to run at least 1 metre**.
- The built car is **tested and measured** — distance and time against the target — and peer-reviewed.
- Improvement strengthens what the test exposed: a **thicker rubber band**, a bigger **balloon** — power fixed until the car meets its target.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, in the lesson's own order — Designing a powered car — choosing its power, Testing and improving the powered car. Your companion may stop you once and ask one question about the hardest part; answer from the lesson, not by repeating it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Design a powered car on paper: name its power device and write why you judge it strong enough for 1 metre.
2. Build a simple powered car at home with adult help, measure its best run's distance and time, and write them beside your design.
3. Improve one thing the test exposed — the power first — run the car again, and write whether it now beats its first measure.
