# Design and build a powered toy car — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The design's first decision is the car's…
a) Power — propeller, motor or sail  b) Colour  c) Name  d) Price
**Answer: a) Power — propeller, motor or sail**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The chosen power must be strong enough to…
a) Lift the car  b) Run the car at least 1 metre  c) Stop the car  d) Turn the car around
**Answer: b) Run the car at least 1 metre**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Before building, the class…
a) Races finished cars  b) Buys a car  c) Studies how toy cars are powered  d) Paints the track
**Answer: c) Studies how toy cars are powered**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Testing records the car's…
a) Weight only  b) Colour in sunlight  c) Driver's name  d) Distance and time against the 1-metre target
**Answer: d) Distance and time against the 1-metre target**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A car that falls short is improved by…
a) Strengthening its power — a thicker rubber band, a bigger balloon  b) Making it heavier  c) Removing its wheels  d) Shortening the track secretly
**Answer: a) Strengthening its power — a thicker rubber band, a bigger balloon**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The project's full circle is…
a) Buy, race, discard  b) Design, build, test, improve  c) Draw, colour, hang  d) Guess, run, hope
**Answer: b) Design, build, test, improve**
