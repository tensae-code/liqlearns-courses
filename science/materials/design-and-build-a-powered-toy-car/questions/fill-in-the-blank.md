# Design and build a powered toy car — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** A powered car's first design decision is its _____.
**Answer: power**

**2.** The car must run at least _____ metre.
**Answer: 1**

**3.** Testing records distance and _____ against the target.
**Answer: time**

**4.** A thicker rubber _____ is one way to strengthen the power.
**Answer: band**
