# Design and build a powered toy car — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the three power kinds the class studies, and the rule the choice must meet.
**Answer:** Propeller, motor and sail cars; the device chosen must be strong enough to run the car at least 1 metre.

**2.** What is measured in testing, and against what?
**Answer:** The car's distance and time, recorded against the 1-metre target the design was set.

**3.** A car runs only half a metre. What does the book's method do next?
**Answer:** Find the reason in the test — power too weak for the car's weight or friction — and improve the power (a thicker rubber band, a balloon that inflates bigger), then test again.

**4.** Why does peer review belong in the project?
**Answer:** Because showing the car and having the class review it exposes weaknesses the builder alone may miss — each found weakness becomes an improvement.
