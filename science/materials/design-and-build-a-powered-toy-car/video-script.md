# Video script — Design and build a powered toy car

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Design and build a powered toy car** — China's way, from its 科学 (Science) textbooks.

Propeller, motor or sail — choose a power strong enough to run your car a full metre. Gather, build, and then comes the truth: the test.
Measure its distance and time. Short of the target? Thicken the rubber band, blow a bigger balloon — improve the power, and run it again.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
