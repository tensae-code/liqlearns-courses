# Hands-on — Design and build a powered toy car

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Choose the power
Look at the three power kinds — propeller, motor, sail (and elastic or balloon drives if your class uses them). Pick one for your car and defend the choice in two sentences against the 1-metre target.

## Measure, review, improve
Run your built car three times on a measured track; record distance and time for each run. With a partner reviewing, name the weakest point, improve it, and run three more times. Compare the two sets of measures.
