# Hands-on — Electricity and magnets

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Filings map (Experiment 10)
Lay paper over a bar magnet and sprinkle iron filings. Tap gently: the class watches the field draw itself, thickest at the poles.

## The coil motor (Experiment 12, build-along)
Build the book's motor in groups, following the lesson step by step — winding, stripping the axle ends, balancing the coil — with the video script as the build-along narration.
