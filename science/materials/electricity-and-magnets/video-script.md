**Dry cells and generators** — other energy becomes electric. The **GERD** is our great water source.
Current needs a **closed loop**: source, bulb, wires, switch.
Magnets pull **iron, steel, nickel, cobalt**.
Two poles only. **Like repel — unlike attract.**
A hung magnet settles **north–south**. A current makes a **field**.
A **motor** turns electricity into movement. A **generator** turns it back.
