# Electricity and magnets — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Both dry cells and generators —**
a) make energy from nothing  b) convert other kinds of energy into electric energy  c) only store water
**Answer: b) convert other kinds of energy into electric energy**

**2. Current needs —**
a) an open circuit  b) a closed circuit  c) no source
**Answer: b) a closed circuit**

**3. Which of these is magnetic?**
a) aluminium  b) plastic  c) iron
**Answer: c) iron**

**4. Like poles —**
a) attract  b) repel  c) do nothing
**Answer: b) repel**

**5. A hung magnet settles pointing —**
a) east–west  b) any way at all  c) north–south
**Answer: c) north–south**

**6. A motor converts —**
a) mechanical to electric  b) electric to mechanical  c) heat to sound
**Answer: b) electric to mechanical**
