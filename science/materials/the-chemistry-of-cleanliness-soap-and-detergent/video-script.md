Today: **The chemistry of cleanliness — soap and detergent** — Bangladesh's way, from its book (Bangladesh, Grade 8 — *Science Investigative Study Class 8*, printed p. 168).

Class 8 in Bangladesh gives cleanliness and hygiene a chemistry of their own: washing is not just water and rubbing — the cleaning agents themselves do chemical work.
Cleaning with soap and detergent is the chapter's centre: these are the substances that lift the dirt and grease that water alone leaves behind, so that the water can carry them away.
The book files this with its everyday chemistry, beside household chemistry and chemistry in agriculture and industry: keeping people clean is one of chemistry's daily jobs, done with named substances, used properly.
