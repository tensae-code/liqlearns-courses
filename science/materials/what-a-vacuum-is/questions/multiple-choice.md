# What a vacuum is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) A vacuum — a space with no matter  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page

**3.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: what a vacuum is. That is…
