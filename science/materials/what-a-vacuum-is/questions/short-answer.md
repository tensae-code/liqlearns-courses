# What a vacuum is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: A vacuum — a space with no matter.
**Answer:** South Sudan's Primary 8 book teaches the **vacuum** as its own topic: a vacuum is **a space with no matter in it** — the guide's answers add the consequence: no life can be there, because there is nothing there to live on or breathe.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The vacuum at work — keeping heat in.
**Answer:** The guide uses the vacuum to explain the **vacuum flask**: with no matter in its walls' gap, there is **no conduction or convection** to carry heat away — the same reason it sets beside the whale's fat and the clay lining of an improved charcoal stove as insulation. Emptiness itself doing a job: that is the lesson.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** A vacuum — a space with no matter → The vacuum at work — keeping heat in — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
