## Sub lesson 1 — A vacuum — a space with no matter

South Sudan's Primary 8 book teaches the **vacuum** as its own topic: a vacuum is **a space with no matter in it** — the guide's answers add the consequence: no life can be there, because there is nothing there to live on or breathe.

## Sub lesson 2 — The vacuum at work — keeping heat in

The guide uses the vacuum to explain the **vacuum flask**: with no matter in its walls' gap, there is **no conduction or convection** to carry heat away — the same reason it sets beside the whale's fat and the clay lining of an improved charcoal stove as insulation. Emptiness itself doing a job: that is the lesson.
