# Hands-on — Bodies and substances — what things are

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Body and substance pairs
Collect six small objects. For each, write two labels: the body (the thing) and its substance (what it is made of). Mix the labels and match them again.

## One substance, many bodies
Choose one substance at home — wood, metal, cloth or plastic. Hunt for every body in one room made of it and list them.
