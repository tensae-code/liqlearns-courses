# Bodies and substances — what things are — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Anything that takes up space is a _____.
**Answer: body**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What a body is made of is its _____.
**Answer: substance**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The table is a body; the _____ is its substance.
**Answer: wood**
