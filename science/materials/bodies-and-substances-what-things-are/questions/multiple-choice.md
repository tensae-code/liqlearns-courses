# Bodies and substances — what things are — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A body is…
a) Only a human body
b) Anything that takes up space
c) Only a living thing
d) A shadow
**Answer: b) Anything that takes up space**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is a body?
a) The idea of a chair
b) A drop of water
c) A dream
d) Yesterday
**Answer: b) A drop of water**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A substance is…
a) The shape of a thing
b) What a body is made of — its material
c) The place of a thing
d) The name of a thing
**Answer: b) What a body is made of — its material**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Wood making a table, a chair and a spoon shows that…
a) Substances disappear
b) The same substance can form many bodies
c) Bodies make themselves
d) Shape is substance
**Answer: b) The same substance can form many bodies**
