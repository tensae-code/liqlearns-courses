# Bodies and substances — what things are — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does Angola's Grade 5 book open with body and substance?
**Answer:** Because all the nature science that follows studies bodies — their properties, changes and states — and those are read through this first pair: the thing, and the material of the thing.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Can two bodies of the same kind be made of different substances? Give an example.
**Answer:** Yes — a spoon (a body) can be made of wood, metal or plastic; the kind of body is the same, the substances differ.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Is air in a balloon a body? Why?
**Answer:** Yes — it takes up space inside the balloon, and anything that takes up space is a body.
