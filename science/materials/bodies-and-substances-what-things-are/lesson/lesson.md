# Bodies and substances — what things are

## Do it first
Look around and name five things. Each one takes up space — but each is made of something. Can you name, for one of them, the thing itself and the material it is made of, as two different answers? Say them out loud. Then read on: this is Angola's way in — its Grade 5 *Ciências da Natureza* book opens the whole subject with the idea of a body and a substance (Noção de corpo e de substância).

## Sub lesson 1 — What a body is

A **body** is anything that **takes up space**: the stone, the chair, the drop of water, the air in the balloon. Angola's Grade 5 book opens with this idea because all the nature science that follows studies bodies — their properties, their changes, their states. If a thing is there, taking up its place in the world, it is a body, whatever its size.

## Sub lesson 2 — What a substance is

A **substance** is **what a body is made of**. The table is a body; the wood is its substance. The same substance can form many bodies — wood makes the table, the chair and the spoon — and bodies of the same shape can be made of different substances. Body and substance are the book's first pair: the thing, and the material of the thing; the properties of bodies, taught next in the book, are read off this pair.

## The key ideas

- A **body** is anything that takes up space.
- A **substance** is what a body is made of — the material of the thing.
- The **same substance** can form many bodies, and the same kind of body can be made of **different substances**.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, in the lesson's own order. Your companion may stop you once and ask: "The table and its wood — which is the body and which the substance?" Answer from the lesson: The table is the body — the thing taking up space; the wood is its substance — what the body is made of.

## Do It Yourself
1. Name five bodies around you and the substance of each.
2. Find two bodies made of the same substance, and two bodies of the same kind made of different substances. Say both pairs aloud.
3. Teach the hardest part of this lesson to someone at home in five sentences.
