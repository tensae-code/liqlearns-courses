Today: **Bodies and substances — what things are** — the opening idea of Angola's Grade 5 nature-science book.

A body is anything that takes up space: the stone, the chair, the drop of water, the air in the balloon.

A substance is what a body is made of. The table is a body; the wood is its substance — and one substance can form many bodies.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
