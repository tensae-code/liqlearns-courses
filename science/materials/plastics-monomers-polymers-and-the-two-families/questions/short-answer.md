# Plastics — monomers, polymers and the two families — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Plastics: man-made polymers built by polymerisation, small monomer units joining into long chains; thermoplastics with linear chains that soften and can be reshaped again and again (polythene, PVC, polystyrene) against thermosetting plastics with cross-linked chains that set hard for good (Bakelite from phenol and formaldehyde, melamine); the uses table and the properties — light, cheap, waterproof, non-reactive, insulators of heat and electricity.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science Grade 10*, printed pp. 148–150.**
