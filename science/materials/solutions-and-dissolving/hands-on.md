# Hands-on — Solutions and dissolving

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Stir a spoon of salt into a glass of water until it vanishes; taste one drop — the salt is still there. Now stir in sand and compare: which one dissolved, and how do you know the salt did not disappear?

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of solutions and dissolving — when a solid dissolves in water it spreads through the water as particles too small to see, making a solution — the solid has not vanished, it is still there; substances that will not dissolve are insoluble, and warming and stirring make dissolving faster and greater. — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
