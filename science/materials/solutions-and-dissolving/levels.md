# Solutions and dissolving — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (when a solid dissolves in water it spreads through the water as particles too small to see, making a solution — the solid has not vanished, it is still there; substances that will not dissolve are insoluble, and warming and stirring make dissolving faster and greater.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
