# Solutions and dissolving — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Stir salt or sugar into water and the grains disappear — but taste the water and the solid is still there, spread evenly through every drop.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Some substances are insoluble is the opposite of what this lesson teaches.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches this as stated.**

**3.** A solution that will take no more solid is saturated, but warming the water usually lets it dissolve more, and stirring and crushing make dissolving faster.
**Answer: True**

**4.** This lesson's topic plays no part in science at all.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
