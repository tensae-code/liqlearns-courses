# Solutions and dissolving — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: dissolving makes a solution.
**Answer: Stir salt or sugar into water and the grains disappear — but taste the water and the solid is still there, spread evenly through every drop. That even mixture is a solution: the dissolved solid is the solute, the water is the solvent.**

**2.** Explain: some substances are insoluble.
**Answer: Sand, stones and oil do not dissolve: stirred into water they stay themselves, sinking, floating or separating out again. Insoluble is a property of the pair — this solid in this liquid — not a failure of stirring.**

**3.** Explain: more can dissolve.
**Answer: A solution that will take no more solid is saturated, but warming the water usually lets it dissolve more, and stirring and crushing make dissolving faster. Cooling a hot saturated solution can bring the solid back out as crystals — proof it was there all along.**
