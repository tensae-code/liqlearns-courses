# Solutions and dissolving — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about solutions and dissolving?
a) When a solid dissolves in water it spreads through the water as particles too small to see, making a solution — the solid has not vanished, it is still there; substances that will not dissolve are insoluble, and warming and stirring make dissolving faster and greater.
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) When a solid dissolves in water it spreads through the water as particles too small to see, making a solution — the solid has not vanished, it is still there; substances that will not dissolve are insoluble, and warming and stirring make dissolving faster and greater.**

**2.** What does this lesson say about dissolving makes a solution?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Stir salt or sugar into water and the grains disappear — but taste the water and the solid is still there, spread evenly through every drop.
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Stir salt or sugar into water and the grains disappear — but taste the water and the solid is still there, spread evenly through every drop.**

**3.** What does this lesson say about some substances are insoluble?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Sand, stones and oil do not dissolve: stirred into water they stay themselves, sinking, floating or separating out again.
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Sand, stones and oil do not dissolve: stirred into water they stay themselves, sinking, floating or separating out again.**

**4.** What does this lesson say about more can dissolve?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) A solution that will take no more solid is saturated, but warming the water usually lets it dissolve more, and stirring and crushing make dissolving faster.
**Answer: d) A solution that will take no more solid is saturated, but warming the water usually lets it dissolve more, and stirring and crushing make dissolving faster.**
