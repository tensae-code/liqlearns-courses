# Water expands when it freezes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does water do when it freezes, that most things do not?
**Answer:** It expands — it takes more room as ice than it did as water.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why can a full, closed bottle crack in a freezer?
**Answer:** The water inside expands as it freezes and pushes outward against the closed bottle.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does ice float?
**Answer:** The ice takes more room than the same water liquid, so it is lighter for its size and floats.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Name one thing outdoors that water's expansion changes.
**Answer:** It widens cracks in rock, one freezing at a time (and it makes lakes freeze from the top, leaving liquid water below).
