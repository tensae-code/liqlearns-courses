# Water expands when it freezes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) When water freezes, it…
a) Shrinks like most things  b) Expands — takes more room  c) Disappears  d) Turns into air
**Answer: b) Expands — takes more room**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The expansion of water is also called its…
a) Dilatation  b) Evaporation  c) Circulation  d) Solution
**Answer: a) Dilatation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A brim-full closed bottle of water in a freezer can crack because…
a) The cold breaks all bottles  b) Water turns to gas  c) The freezing water pushes outward as it expands  d) The bottle shrinks
**Answer: c) The freezing water pushes outward as it expands**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Ice floats on water because…
a) It is full of air only  b) It is warmer  c) It is pushed up by fish  d) It takes more room than the water it came from, so it is lighter for its size
**Answer: d) It takes more room than the water it came from, so it is lighter for its size**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A lake freezes…
a) From the top down  b) From the bottom up  c) All at once  d) Never, anywhere
**Answer: a) From the top down**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Water freezing inside a crack in rock…
a) Heals the crack  b) Pushes the crack wider  c) Colours the rock  d) Makes the rock lighter
**Answer: b) Pushes the crack wider**
