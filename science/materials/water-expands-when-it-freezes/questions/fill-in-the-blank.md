# Water expands when it freezes — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) When water freezes, it _____.
**Answer: expands**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The expansion of water is called its _____.
**Answer: dilatation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Because ice takes more room, it _____ on liquid water.
**Answer: floats**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A lake freezes from the _____ down.
**Answer: top**
