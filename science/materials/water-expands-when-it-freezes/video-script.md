The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Water expands when it freezes** — Cuba's way.

Most things shrink as they cool and harden.

Because the ice takes more room, it is lighter for its size than liquid water — so ice floats, and a lake freezes from the top down, leaving liquid water below where living things survive the cold.

Now you. What is strange about water when it freezes? Say it back — in your own words.
