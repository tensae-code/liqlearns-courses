# Hands-on — Water expands when it freezes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The bottle test
With an adult, fill a strong plastic bottle completely with water, close it, mark the water level, and freeze it overnight. In the morning, look: the ice stands higher than your mark — measure how much more room it takes. (Use plastic, never glass.)

## Ice floats — check it
Drop an ice cube into a glass of water. It floats, with most of itself under the surface. Say why, in the lesson's words: the ice takes more room than the water it came from, so it is lighter for its size.
