# Reactions — seven kinds of reaction around the home

## Do it first

A battery lights a torch. Soap lifts grease. A boiled egg shows a green-grey ring. Three happenings — before reading, guess: is each one a chemical reaction, and what kind? Write your guesses.

## Seven kinds, named (teacher-guide evidence)

The teacher guide names seven reaction kinds in everyday life. **Combustion**: fuel burning with oxygen, giving carbon dioxide and water — propane burning is its worked example: C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O (count it: C 3 = 3, H 8 = 8, O 10 = 10 — balanced). **Double replacement (metathesis)**: partners swap — the vinegar-and-baking-soda volcano, and baking powder working in recipes. **Electrochemistry**: batteries — spontaneous galvanic cells giving electricity, against non-spontaneous electrolytic cells driven by it.

## Body, basin and pan (teacher-guide evidence)

**Digestion**: salivary amylase starts on sugars, stomach acid works, enzymes cleave proteins and fats so the body can absorb them. **Acid–base reactions**: acid + base → salt + water. **Soap and detergent action**: soap emulsifies grime so water can carry it away; detergents are surfactants that lower water's surface tension. **Cooking changes**: the green-grey ring in a boiled egg, where the white's sulfur meets the yolk's iron — a reaction you can slice open and see.
