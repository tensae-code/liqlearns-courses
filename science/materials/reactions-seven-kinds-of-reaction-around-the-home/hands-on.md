# Hands-on — Reactions — seven kinds of reaction around the home

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Seven-kind home survey
Find one real example of each of the seven kinds in your home or street this week. Write the kind, the happening, and the evidence a reaction occurred.

## The egg ring
With a parent, boil an egg hard, cut it open, and find the green-grey ring. Write one sentence: which two substances met to make it?
