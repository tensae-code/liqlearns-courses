# Reactions — seven kinds of reaction around the home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Combustion of propane is written —
a) C₃H₈ + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O  b) C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O  c) C₃H₈ + 3O₂ → 5CO₂ + 4H₂O
**Answer: b) C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O** — — C 3 = 3, H 8 = 8, O 10 = 10.

**2.** The vinegar-and-baking-soda volcano belongs to the kind called —
a) combustion  b) double replacement (metathesis)  c) digestion
**Answer: b) double replacement (metathesis)**

**3.** Acid + base gives —
a) salt + water  b) only water  c) a metal
**Answer: a) salt + water**

**4.** A battery in use is a —
a) electrolytic cell  b) galvanic cell  c) cooking change
**Answer: b) galvanic cell** — — spontaneous, giving electricity.

**5.** The green-grey ring in a boiled egg forms where —
a) shell meets white  b) the white's sulfur meets the yolk's iron  c) air meets the yolk
**Answer: b) the white's sulfur meets the yolk's iron**
