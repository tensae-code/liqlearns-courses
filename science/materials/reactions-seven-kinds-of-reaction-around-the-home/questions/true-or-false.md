# Reactions — seven kinds of reaction around the home — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Detergents lower the surface tension of water.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Digestion is only a physical change.
**Answer: False**

**3.** Soap lifts grime by emulsifying it.
**Answer: True**

**4.** C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O is a balanced equation.
**Answer: True**
