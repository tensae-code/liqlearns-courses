# Reactions — seven kinds of reaction around the home — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or formula.

**1.** Fuel burning with oxygen is called ___ . (combustion)
**Answer: combustion**

**2.** The enzyme in saliva that starts on sugars is salivary ___ . (amylase)
**Answer: amylase**

**3.** Vinegar plus baking soda is a double ___ reaction. (replacement)
**Answer: replacement**
