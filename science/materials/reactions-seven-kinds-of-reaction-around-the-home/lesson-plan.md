# Lesson plan — Reactions — seven kinds of reaction around the home

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Materials · **Lesson:** Reactions — seven kinds of reaction around the home
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Elements, Compounds and Chemical Reactions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** seven named kinds of reaction run the home — combustion, double replacement, electrochemistry, digestion, acid–base, soap action and cooking changes (teacher-guide evidence only)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Reactions — chemistry in everyday life (same unit) and is built ONLY on teacher-guide evidence at syllabus level — it has no student-book counterpart. The Chemistry title names reaction kinds again at secondary depth; this first-depth lesson is new here, cross-referenced not copied.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier Unit 3 lessons in order — symbols, then formulas, then reactions. Recommended, never forced; this lesson stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the seven reaction kinds from the guide.
2. Give one home example of each kind.
3. Write the guide's combustion example balanced: C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O.
4. Explain the boiled-egg ring as a cooking reaction (sulfur meeting iron).

## If the learner is stuck
Mixes up galvanic and electrolytic → galvanic gives electricity out (a battery in use); electrolytic takes electricity in. Calls digestion 'just chewing' → chewing is physical; the enzyme and acid work is the chemical kind.
