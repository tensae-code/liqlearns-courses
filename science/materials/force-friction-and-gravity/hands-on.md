# Hands-on — Force, friction and gravity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The three-effects test bench
With the book's kit — plastic, clay, a lemon, a ball, a stone — groups demonstrate one effect each: squeeze for shape, kick against a wall for direction, roll and stop for speed.

## Roughness under the lens
Look at a 'smooth' desk edge and a rough stone under a magnifier if the class has one. Draw the jaggedness you see: friction's cause, made visible.
