# Video script — Force, friction and gravity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **force** is a push or a pull.
Some forces touch. Some reach **across a gap** — the magnet, gravity.
Force changes **shape**, **direction** and **speed**.
**Friction** fights sliding — and the still kind, static, is the stronger.
**Gravity** pulls all things to the Earth's centre — and without air, all fall **together**.
