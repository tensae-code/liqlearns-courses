# Force, friction and gravity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A force is —**
a) only a push  b) a push or a pull  c) a kind of machine
**Answer: b) a push or a pull**

**2. The magnet pulling a nail across a gap is a —**
a) contact force  b) non-contact force  c) friction only
**Answer: b) non-contact force**

**3. Which is NOT one of force's three effects?**
a) changing shape  b) changing direction  c) changing colour by itself
**Answer: c) changing colour by itself**

**4. Friction comes from —**
a) surface roughness  b) the body's colour  c) sound
**Answer: a) surface roughness**

**5. The greater kind of friction is —**
a) kinetic  b) static  c) neither exists
**Answer: b) static**

**6. Without air resistance, bodies of different masses dropped together —**
a) the heavy lands first  b) land together  c) the light lands first
**Answer: b) land together**
