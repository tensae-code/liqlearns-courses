# Rust and corrosion — how air and water attack iron — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner describes rust as transformed, crumbly metal that thins and holes iron, proves with the three-nail experiment that rust needs air and water together (each alone harmless, salt speeding it), and lists the protections — paint, oil and grease, galvanising, tin, stainless steel, dry storage — as applications of that proof, answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — doing the lesson's own experiments and sorts by hand, naming each idea aloud, and reasoning about new examples until the teaching is the learner's own.
