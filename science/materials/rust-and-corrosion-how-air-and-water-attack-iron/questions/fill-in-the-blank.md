# Rust and corrosion — how air and water attack iron — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The slow chemical eating-away of a metal is called _____.
**Answer: corrosion**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Rust needs air and _____ together.
**Answer: water**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Iron coated with zinc is called _____ iron.
**Answer: galvanised**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Tools are wiped with _____ before storage to keep air and water off.
**Answer: oil**
