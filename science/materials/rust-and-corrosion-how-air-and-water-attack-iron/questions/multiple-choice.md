# Rust and corrosion — how air and water attack iron — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Rust is…
a) Dirt that washes off iron  b) The reddish-brown, crumbly substance iron is transformed into by corrosion  c) A kind of paint  d) Polished iron
**Answer: b) The reddish-brown, crumbly substance iron is transformed into by corrosion**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) As rusting continues, iron…
a) Gets stronger  b) Stays the same under the rust  c) Thins, weakens and can be holed through  d) Turns to wood
**Answer: c) Thins, weakens and can be holed through**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The three-nail experiment shows rust needs…
a) Water alone  b) Air alone  c) Air (oxygen) and water together  d) Sunlight
**Answer: c) Air (oxygen) and water together**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A nail under boiled water covered with oil stays bright because…
a) Oil feeds the nail  b) The air is shut out, and rust needs air as well as water  c) The water is hot  d) Nails never rust in water
**Answer: b) The air is shut out, and rust needs air as well as water**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Galvanised iron is protected by…
a) A coat of zinc over the iron  b) Soaking in salt water  c) Leaving it in rain  d) Painting with water
**Answer: a) A coat of zinc over the iron**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Rust on a painted gate starts first…
a) In the middle of sound paint  b) Exactly where the paint is chipped, letting air and water reach the iron  c) Nowhere — paint stops rust forever even when broken  d) On the hinges only
**Answer: b) Exactly where the paint is chipped, letting air and water reach the iron**
