Today: **Rust and corrosion**.

Scratch an old nail: brown crumbs, thin pitted metal underneath. That is rust — iron being slowly eaten. Eaten by what? The three-nail experiment knows: a nail in dry air stays bright; a nail in water shut away from air stays bright; only the nail with air AND water together rusts. Oxygen and water are rust's two ingredients, and salt hurries them. So every protection is a keeping-away: paint, oil, zinc coating — galvanising — stainless steel, or simply storing iron dry. Keep the two thieves off, and iron keeps its strength.
