# Hands-on — Rust and corrosion — how air and water attack iron

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The three jars
Run the dry / water-without-air / water-with-air nail test for a week, labels on, untouched. Say aloud on judging day: only the jar with both thieves present shows rust — the recipe is proved, not quoted.

## Chip the paint
On a painted scrap (not a valued object), compare a sound painted patch with a chipped patch after days in damp air — or examine a real gate's chips. Say aloud: protection fails exactly where it is broken, and rust moves in through the break.
