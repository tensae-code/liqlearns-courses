# Lesson plan — Acids and bases — definitions and naming

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Materials · **Lesson:** Acids and bases — definitions and naming
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Classification of Compounds (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** acids release H⁺ in water (carried as H₃O⁺) and bases release OH⁻ — soluble bases are alkalis — and an acid's name is built from its anion: -ide → hydro-…-ic, -ate → -ic, -ite → -ous, with the guide's oxoacid ladder (per- / hypo-) completing the machine
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Oxides — basic, acidic and in-between (same unit): oxides plus water make the acids and bases named here. The primary lesson Oxides, acids, bases and salts meets acids and bases at first depth; this is the Grade 8 naming machine.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **What acids and bases are** — An **acid** is a substance that releases **hydrogen ions, H⁺**, when dissolved in water — in water the ion rides on a water molecule as **H₃O⁺**.
2. **The naming machine — and the oxoacid ladder (teacher guide — evidence)** — Read the acid's **anion** and turn its ending: **-ide → hydro-…-ic** (chloride → **hydrochloric** acid); **-ate → -ic** (sulphate → **sulphuric** acid); **-ite → -ous** (sulphite → **sulphurous** acid).
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define acid, base and alkali by the ions they release.
2. Name an acid from its anion using the three suffix rules.
3. Climb the chlorine oxoacid ladder: perchloric, chloric, chlorous, hypochlorous.
