The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

An acid lets go of H+ in water. A base lets go of OH-.

A base that dissolves is an alkali.

Now the naming machine: look at the anion's ending.

Ide becomes hydro-ic. Ate becomes ic. Ite becomes ous.

Chloride — hydrochloric. Sulphate — sulphuric. Sulphite — sulphurous.
