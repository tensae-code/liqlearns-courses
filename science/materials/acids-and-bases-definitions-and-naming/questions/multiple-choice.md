# Acids and bases — definitions and naming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In water, an acid releases —
a) OH⁻ ions  b) H⁺ ions  c) salt
**Answer: b) H⁺ ions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An alkali is —
a) any base  b) a base that dissolves in water  c) an acid that dissolves
**Answer: b) a base that dissolves in water**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In water, H⁺ is carried as —
a) H₂O  b) OH⁻  c) H₃O⁺
**Answer: c) H₃O⁺**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The acid of the sulphite anion is —
a) sulphuric acid  b) sulphurous acid  c) hydrosulphuric acid
**Answer: b) sulphurous acid**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** One oxygen less than chloric acid gives —
a) perchloric acid  b) chlorous acid  c) hydrochloric acid
**Answer: b) chlorous acid**
