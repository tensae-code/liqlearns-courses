# Acids and bases — definitions and naming — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or formula.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A base releases ___ ions in water.
**Answer: hydroxide (OH⁻)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Chloride gives ___ acid.
**Answer: hydrochloric**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The anion H₂PO₄⁻ is named ___ phosphate.
**Answer: dihydrogen**
