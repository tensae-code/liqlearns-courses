# Acids and bases — definitions and naming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every base is an alkali.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** HCl gas and hydrochloric acid are the same substance in different states.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The anion of an -ic acid ends in -ate.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** HPO₄²⁻ is correctly named dihydrogen phosphate.
**Answer: False**
