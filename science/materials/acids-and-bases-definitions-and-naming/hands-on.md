# Hands-on — Acids and bases — definitions and naming

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Naming drill
Name the acids of: bromide, nitrate, nitrite, carbonate, fluoride. Then reverse: write the anion behind phosphoric, hydrobromic and carbonous-style names you invent — and check each ending.

## Ladder drawing
Draw the chlorine ladder as four rungs, perchloric at the top losing one oxygen per rung down to hypochlorous, with each rung's name change marked.
