# Hands-on — Separating mixtures — magnet and evaporation

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The clip hunt
Bury metal clips in sand or grain. A magnet in a thin plastic bag finds and lifts them; peel the bag inside-out to drop them cleanly.

## Sun salt dish
A shallow dish of salt water on a sunny sill. Mark the water line each day. The shrinking line and the growing white crust are evaporation at work.
