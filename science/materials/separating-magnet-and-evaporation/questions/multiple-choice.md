# Separating mixtures — magnet and evaporation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Experiment 2.5 the magnet lifts —
a) the sulfur  b) the iron filings  c) both
**Answer: b) the iron filings** — only iron is magnetic.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The differing property used in Experiment 2.5 is —
a) colour  b) magnetism  c) boiling point
**Answer: b) magnetism**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** After the magnet passes, the sulfur —
a) is gone  b) stays on the paper  c) turns black
**Answer: b) stays on the paper**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Experiment 2.6, heating salt water leaves —
a) water  b) salt  c) nothing
**Answer: b) salt** — the water leaves as vapour.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Table salt is won from sea water by —
a) magnetism  b) evaporation under the sun  c) filtration
**Answer: b) evaporation under the sun**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The state change that separates in Experiment 2.6 is —
a) liquid to gas  b) solid to liquid  c) gas to solid
**Answer: a) liquid to gas** — evaporation.
