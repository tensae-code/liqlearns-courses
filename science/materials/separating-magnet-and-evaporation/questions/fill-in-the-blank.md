# Separating mixtures — magnet and evaporation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Only ______ is magnetic in the filings-and-sulfur mixture.
**Answer: iron**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Driving water off as vapour is called ______.
**Answer: evaporation**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The yellow powder left behind in Experiment 2.5 is ______.
**Answer: sulfur**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The white solid left in the dish is ______.
