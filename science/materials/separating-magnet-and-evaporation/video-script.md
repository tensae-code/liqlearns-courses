# Video script — Separating mixtures — magnet and evaporation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mix the filings and the sulfur. One powder. Sorted? Never by eye.
Now — the magnet. Pass it over. The black filings LEAP up!
The yellow sulfur stays. Only iron answers the magnet.
Salt water in the dish. Heat it. The water flies off as vapour.
Look — white salt, left behind. The sun does this to the whole sea.
