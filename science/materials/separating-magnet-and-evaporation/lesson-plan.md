# Lesson plan — Separating mixtures — magnet and evaporation

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Materials · **Lesson:** Separating mixtures — magnet and evaporation
**Place in the curriculum:** Materials — Grade 7, Unit 2 "Matter in Our Surroundings" (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** two separations worked by hand: a magnet lifts iron out of a mixture, and heating drives water off a solution leaving the solid behind
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Separating mixtures — why and how we choose (grown in Mixtures and how to separate them); here two of its methods are done for real

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Experiment 2.5 — the magnet lifts the iron out** — a magnet passed over an iron-filings-and-sulfur mixture lifts the filings clean out
2. **What you observe, and what it proves** — Experiment 2.6: heating a salt solution drives the water off as vapour and leaves the salt — the sun does the same to sea water

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Materials — Mixtures and how to separate them. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Run or describe Experiment 2.5: the magnet lifting iron filings from sulfur.
2. Say why the magnet works: only the iron is magnetic — the differing property.
3. Run or describe Experiment 2.6: evaporation leaving salt from salt water.
4. Connect evaporation to salt-making from sea water under the sun.

## What you need
iron filings, sulfur powder, a magnet, paper; then a salt solution, a heat source and an evaporating dish (adult work wherever heat is used)

## If the learner is stuck
- Tries the magnet on wet mixtures first → dry mixtures separate cleanly; water hides the filings and rusts them.
- Thinks the salt 'burned away' in Experiment 2.6 → the salt is what is LEFT; the water left as vapour.
