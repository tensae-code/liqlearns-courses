Today: **Changing state — melting, freezing, evaporating, condensing** — Vietnam's way, from its book (Vietnam, Grade 5 — *Khoa học 5*, printed p. 19).

Vietnam's Grade 4 *Khoa học 4* names the changes of water's state and diagrams them: **melting**, **freezing** (solidifying), **evaporating** and **condensing** — ice trays hardening in the freezer, and steam condensing on a plate held above boiling water. The same lesson applies the changes: fog is tiny water droplets (vapour condensed in the air), coastal people make sea salt by evaporation, and rain forms as vapour condenses high up and falls.

Vietnam's Grade 5 *Khoa học 5* watches the changes beyond water: candle wax **melts** under heat and **sets again** as it cools; the alcohol in hand sanitiser **evaporates** off the skin; and liquid oxygen kept in hospital tanks turns back to **gas** for patients to breathe. A change of state changes how a substance looks and behaves — the substance itself stays itself.
