| §1-4, Changes in states of matter (التغيرات في حالات المادة): almost all substances can exist as solids, liquids or gases, and the state of a substance is set by temperature — water, for example, is a liquid, ice a solid, and carbon dioxide a gas at room temperature (Fig. 1-10, the change triangle: melting/freezing, evaporation or boiling/condensation, sublimation);
