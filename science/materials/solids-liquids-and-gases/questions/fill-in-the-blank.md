# Solids, liquids and gases — fill in the blank

Fill in each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Pouring water from bottle to bowl is the book's Experiment ___.**
**Answer: 1**

**2. (Sub lesson 3) There are three states of matter: solid, liquid and ___.**
**Answer: gas**
**3. (Sub lesson 5) Pupils measure the local boiling point of water with a ___, with adult help.**
**Answer: thermometer**
**4. Iodine and solid carbon dioxide change by ______ (ዝልትነት).**
**Answer: sublimation**

**5. (Sub lesson 6)** Of the three states, only ______ compresses easily.
**Answer: gas** — Experiment 2.2.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** A gas changing straight into a solid is ______ (teacher guide — evidence).
**Answer: deposition**
