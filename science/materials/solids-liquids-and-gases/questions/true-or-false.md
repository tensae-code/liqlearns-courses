# Solids, liquids and gases — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A stone keeps its shape in a cup and in a bowl.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Water takes the shape of the bowl it is poured into.**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) An air-filled ball weighs more than an empty one.**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4) Breaking wood is a change of shape, not a new substance.**
**Answer: True**
**5. (Sub lesson 5) Ice, water and steam are one material in three states.**
**Answer: True**
**6. Gaining heat energy speeds a state's particles into the next state.**
**Answer: True**

**7. The deodorant in Experiment 4 stayed in its corner.**
**Answer: False**
