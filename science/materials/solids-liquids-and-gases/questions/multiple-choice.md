# Solids, liquids and gases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A solid has —**
a) no fixed shape  b) fixed shape and fixed volume  c) fixed shape only in a box
**Answer: b) fixed shape and fixed volume**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A liquid has —**
a) fixed shape and volume  b) fixed volume but no fixed shape  c) no volume at all
**Answer: b) fixed volume but no fixed shape**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) A gas has —**
a) fixed shape  b) no fixed shape or volume — it spreads to fill its container  c) fixed volume
**Answer: b) no fixed shape or volume — it spreads to fill its container**

**4. (Sub lesson 4) When ice melts, what happens?**
a) new matter is created  b) the state changes but no new matter is created  c) the water disappears forever
**Answer: b) the state changes but no new matter is created**
**5. (Sub lesson 5) Boiling point is —**
a) the temperature at which a liquid boils  b) the size of a pot  c) the colour of steam
**Answer: a) the temperature at which a liquid boils**

**6. (Sub lesson 5) The candle in the flame melts and then —**
a) vanishes  b) re-solidifies as it cools  c) becomes water
**Answer: b) re-solidifies as it cools**
**7. In a gas, the particles are —**
a) tightly ordered  b) very far apart and nearly free  c) frozen still
**Answer: b) very far apart and nearly free**

**8. Sublimation is a solid jumping straight to —**
a) liquid  b) gas  c) another solid
**Answer: b) gas**

**9. Water poured between vessels keeps its —**
a) shape  b) volume  c) temperature always
**Answer: b) volume**

**10. The reverse of sublimation is —**
a) melting  b) deposition  c) boiling
**Answer: b) deposition**
