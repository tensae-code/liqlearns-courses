# Hands-on — Solids, liquids and gases

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The three-container test
Take one solid, one liquid, one gas container (balloon). Put each in two different places. Which kept its shape? Write the three rules from your own test.

## Collect and group
The book's own task: collect solids from outside the classroom and group them by your own criteria. Defend one group by a property from Describing things.

## State-change watch
With an adult, watch a kettle or a pot come to the boil. Name each state you see, in order, and say the moment steam appears.
