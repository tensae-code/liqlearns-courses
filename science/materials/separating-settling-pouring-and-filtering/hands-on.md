# Hands-on — Separating mixtures — settling, pouring off and filtering

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The three glasses
Glass 1: muddy. Glass 2: settled and poured off. Glass 3: filtered. Line them up against the light — the stages you can see.

## Kitchen decantation
Wash lentils or peas: swirl, settle, pour off. Name each move with its science word as you do it.
