# Lesson plan — Separating mixtures — settling, pouring off and filtering

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Materials · **Lesson:** Separating mixtures — settling, pouring off and filtering
**Place in the curriculum:** Materials — Grade 7, Unit 2 "Matter in Our Surroundings" (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** muddy water is cleaned in stages: let the solid settle, pour the clear layer off, then filter what remains through paper
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Separating mixtures — why and how we choose; here the water-cleaning methods are done for real, in the book's order

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Run or describe Experiment 2.7: sedimentation, then decantation.
2. Run or describe Experiment 2.8: filtration, naming residue and filtrate.
3. Say why filtration catches what settling misses.
4. Connect the methods to washing lentils and peas, and to real water filters.

## What you need
muddy water in a clear glass or jar; filter paper (or clean folded cloth), a funnel, a second glass; chalk powder and water for Experiment 2.8

## If the learner is stuck
- Pours too fast and stirs the sediment back in → decantation is slow and tilted gently; the last cloudy part is left for the filter.
- Calls the water on the paper the residue → the residue stays ON the paper; what passes through is the filtrate.
