# Separating mixtures — settling, pouring off and filtering — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The solid that sinks in standing muddy water forms the ______.
**Answer: sediment**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Chalk stays on the paper as the ______.
**Answer: residue**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Clear water passing through paper is the ______.
**Answer: filtrate**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Pouring off the clear layer is called ______.
**Answer: decantation**
