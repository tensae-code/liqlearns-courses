# Separating mixtures — settling, pouring off and filtering — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Letting muddy water stand so the solid sinks is —
a) filtration  b) sedimentation  c) evaporation
**Answer: b) sedimentation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Pouring the clear layer off the sediment is —
a) decantation  b) distillation  c) diffusion
**Answer: a) decantation**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Decantation must be done —
a) quickly  b) slowly and gently  c) while stirring
**Answer: b) slowly and gently** — so the sediment stays.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In filtration, the solid caught on the paper is the —
a) filtrate  b) residue  c) solvent
**Answer: b) residue**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The clear liquid that passes through the paper is the —
a) residue  b) filtrate  c) sediment
**Answer: b) filtrate**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Real water filters can trap particles as small as —
a) stones  b) bacteria  c) grains of sand only
**Answer: b) bacteria**
