# Video script — Separating mixtures — settling, pouring off and filtering

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Set up the funnel. Fold the paper so it sits in the funnel with no gaps.
Wet the paper with a little clean water so it sticks to the funnel walls.
Stand the funnel in a clean glass. The stem should touch the glass side, so the filtrate runs down quietly.
Stir the chalk water. Pour it into the paper a little at a time — never above the paper's edge.
Watch: cloudy water goes in at the top. Clear filtrate drips below.
What stays on the paper is the residue. What passes is the filtrate. That is filtration, set up the book's way.
