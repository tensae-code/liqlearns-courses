# Oxides — basic, acidic and in-between — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A basic oxide is —
a) a non-metal oxide that forms an acid  b) a metal oxide that forms a base  c) any oxide that does nothing
**Answer: b) a metal oxide that forms a base**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which oxide is amphoteric?
a) CaO  b) CO₂  c) Al₂O₃
**Answer: c) Al₂O₃**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** CO is an example of —
a) a neutral oxide  b) an acidic oxide  c) a basic oxide
**Answer: a) a neutral oxide**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Burning magnesium in air gives —
a) Mg(OH)₂ directly  b) white ash, MgO  c) no product — magnesium cannot burn
**Answer: b) white ash, MgO**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Sulphur dioxide in water tests —
a) neutral  b) basic  c) acidic
**Answer: c) acidic**
