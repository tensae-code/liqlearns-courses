# Oxides — basic, acidic and in-between — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or formula.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** CaO + H₂O → ___ , a base.
**Answer: Ca(OH)₂**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The gas made when sulphur burns is sulphur ___.
**Answer: dioxide (SO₂)**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Oxides that react with both acids and bases are called ___.
**Answer: amphoteric**
