# Video script — Oxides — basic, acidic and in-between

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An oxide is oxygen plus one other element.

Metal oxide plus water makes a base.

Non-metal oxide plus water makes an acid.

Magnesium burns white-hot and leaves MgO, a basic oxide.

Sulphur burns to SO2, an acidic oxide.

Two oxides do both. Two do neither. Four characters — that is the lesson.
