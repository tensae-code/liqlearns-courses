# What air is made of — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Air is…
a) One single gas
b) A mixture of gases
c) Only oxygen
d) A liquid
**Answer: b) A mixture of gases**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The largest part of air is…
a) Oxygen
b) Carbon dioxide
c) Nitrogen
d) Water vapour
**Answer: c) Nitrogen**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Oxygen is the gas that…
a) Plants give out only
b) Keeps breathing and burning going
c) Makes air blue
d) Fills balloons at parties only
**Answer: b) Keeps breathing and burning going**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Plants take in which gas to make their food?
a) Nitrogen
b) Oxygen
c) Carbon dioxide
d) Smoke
**Answer: c) Carbon dioxide**
