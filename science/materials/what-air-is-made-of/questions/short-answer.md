# What air is made of — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the components of air the lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Nitrogen (the largest part), oxygen, and in small parts carbon dioxide, water vapour and other gases.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book teach the components before the importance of the gases?
**Answer:** Because the gases matter one by one — each does its own work — so they must be named and told apart before what each one does can be taught.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the importance of two gases in air.
**Answer:** Oxygen keeps breathing and burning going; carbon dioxide is taken in by plants to make their food (nitrogen, the largest part, feeds soils and plant growth).
