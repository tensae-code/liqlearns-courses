# What air is made of — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Air is a _____ of gases.
**Answer: mixture**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The largest component of air is _____.
**Answer: nitrogen**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) _____ is the gas that keeps breathing and burning going.
