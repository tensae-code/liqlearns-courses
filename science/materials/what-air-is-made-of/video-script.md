Today: **What air is made of** — Angola's way: meet air in nature, then take it apart.

Air is a mixture of gases. Nitrogen is the largest part; oxygen is the part we breathe; carbon dioxide, water vapour and others are there in small parts.

And each gas matters in its own way — oxygen for breathing and burning, carbon dioxide for plants making food. Name the parts first; then their work makes sense.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
