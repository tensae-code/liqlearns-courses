# Hands-on — What air is made of

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The mixture drawing
Draw a big jar labelled AIR. Fill it with parts: a large part for nitrogen, a smaller part for oxygen, and tiny parts for carbon dioxide, water vapour and the others. Say the shares as you draw.

## Gas jobs hunt
Walk the home and garden and find each gas at work: breathing (oxygen), a flame (oxygen), a green plant taking in air (carbon dioxide). Name the gas at each stop.
