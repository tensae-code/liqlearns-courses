# Hands-on — Solutions and concentration — how much is dissolved

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: concentration: the property that describes the amount of material dissolved in the solvent — the more sugar dissolved, the higher the solution's concentration (two pictured glasses, little sugar and much sugar) Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
