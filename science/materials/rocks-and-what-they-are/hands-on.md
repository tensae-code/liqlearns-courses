# Hands-on — Rocks — and what they are

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hold a rock
Hold a real rock in your hand. Feel its weight, its coldness and its hardness. Tap it gently against another rock and listen: that hard, ringing knock is the Earth's solid material announcing itself.

## Rock or made thing?
Lay out a rock beside a brick, a piece of concrete and a tile. Sort them: which came from the Earth as it is, and which were made by people? Stand by each sorting out loud.
