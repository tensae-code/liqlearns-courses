# Video script — Rocks — and what they are

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Rocks — and what they are** — Albania's way, from its Grade 2 guide's question: what are rocks?
A rock is hard, natural and solid — the Earth itself is made of it. Not alive. Not made by people.
The cliff, the river stone, the pebble in your hand — all rock, in different sizes and places.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
