# Rocks — and what they are — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** A rock is a hard, natural solid — the material the Earth itself is made of.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Rocks are not alive and were not made by people; the cliff, the river stone and the pebble are all rock.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Is a brick a rock?
**Answer:** No — a brick is made by people; a rock is the Earth's own natural material. That made-by-nature line is the lesson's test.
