# Rocks — and what they are — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which sentence gives this lesson's first key idea?
a) A rock is a hard, natural solid — the material the Earth itself is made of.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) A rock is a hard, natural solid — the material the Earth itself is made of.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson opens by asking the learner to…
a) Memorise the key ideas first  b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation  c) Skip to the questions  d) Copy the lesson out
**Answer: b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's second key idea?
a) Rocks are not alive and were not made by people; the cliff, the river stone and the pebble are all rock.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Rocks are not alive and were not made by people; the cliff, the river stone and the pebble are all rock.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's third key idea?
a) Something from a different lesson  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Rocks differ in colour, hardness and grains — sorting those differences belongs to later lessons.  d) None of these
**Answer: c) Rocks differ in colour, hardness and grains — sorting those differences belongs to later lessons.**
