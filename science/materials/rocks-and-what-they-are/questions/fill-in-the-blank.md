# Rocks — and what they are — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Key ideas) A _____ is a hard, natural solid — the material the Earth itself is made of.
**Answer: rock**

**2.** (Key ideas) Rocks are _____ and were not made by people; the cliff, the river stone and the pebble are all rock.
**Answer: not alive**
