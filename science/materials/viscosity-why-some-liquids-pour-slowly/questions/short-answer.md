# Viscosity — why some liquids pour slowly — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The steel-ball test.
**Answer:** Yemen's Grade 9 Science book measures pouring slowness with a sinking ball: a steel ball sinks slowly through car oil, faster through cooking oil, and fastest through water.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Inner friction — and why engines are oiled.
**Answer:** Viscosity is the inner friction between a liquid's own layers as they slide over each other.

**Answer:** The steel-ball test → Inner friction — and why engines are oiled — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
