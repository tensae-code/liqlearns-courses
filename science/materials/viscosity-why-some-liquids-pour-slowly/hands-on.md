# Hands-on — Viscosity — why some liquids pour slowly

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Time a marble or steel ball sinking through three liquids in tall jars — water, cooking oil, and the thickest oil you can find. Order the liquids by sinking time, and write which is most viscous and how the test proves it.
