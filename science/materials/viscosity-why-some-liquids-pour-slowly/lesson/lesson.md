## Sub lesson 1 — The steel-ball test

Yemen's Grade 9 Science book measures pouring slowness with a sinking ball: a steel ball sinks slowly through car oil, faster through cooking oil, and fastest through water. The three liquids hold the ball back by three different amounts — that hold-back is viscosity at work.

## Sub lesson 2 — Inner friction — and why engines are oiled

Viscosity is the inner friction between a liquid's own layers as they slide over each other. The book closes the lesson at the engine: engines are oiled to cut wear, because the oil's viscous film stands between the moving parts — the same property that slows the ball protects the machine.
