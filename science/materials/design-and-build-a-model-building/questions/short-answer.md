# Design and build a model building — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What three things does the design decide before building starts?
**Answer:** What the model will be (a strong bridge or a tall steady tower), which strong shapes will hold it, and which materials will serve.

**2.** How is a finished model judged?
**Answer:** By testing: a bridge by the load it carries, a tower by whether it stands tall and steady.

**3.** Why does showing and testing come before improving?
**Answer:** Because the test finds the weakness — where the bridge sagged or the tower leaned — and improving means changing that found weakness, then building and testing again.

**4.** Who is Lu Ban, and why does the book close the unit with him?
**Answer:** The master carpenter of ancient China, said to have invented the saw and the plane; he stands as the model of the builder — one who designs, makes, and makes better.
