# Design and build a model building — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** A model building is _____ first, before any building starts.
**Answer: designed**

**2.** A bridge is judged by the _____ it carries.
**Answer: load**

**3.** Each weakness found in testing becomes a _____ to make.
**Answer: change**

**4.** Lu Ban invented the saw and the _____.
**Answer: plane**
