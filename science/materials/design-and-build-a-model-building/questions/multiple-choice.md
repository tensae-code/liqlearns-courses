# Design and build a model building — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The build begins with…
a) A design — the model, its shapes and materials decided first  b) Glueing pieces at random  c) Painting the finished model  d) Testing before building
**Answer: a) A design — the model, its shapes and materials decided first**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A model bridge is judged by…
a) Its colour  b) The load it carries  c) Its price  d) How fast it was built
**Answer: b) The load it carries**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A tall model tower is judged by…
a) Being the heaviest  b) Having the most parts  c) Standing tall and steady  d) Its decoration
**Answer: c) Standing tall and steady**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) After a model is shown and tested, the builder…
a) Throws it away  b) Never changes it  c) Hides the test results  d) Finds its weaknesses and improves it
**Answer: d) Finds its weaknesses and improves it**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Lu Ban is remembered as…
a) The master carpenter who invented the saw and the plane  b) A bridge that fell  c) A kind of wood  d) The book's publisher
**Answer: a) The master carpenter who invented the saw and the plane**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Improving a model means…
a) Only painting it  b) Changing what the test showed was weak, then testing again  c) Making it smaller every time  d) Adding weight until it breaks
**Answer: b) Changing what the test showed was weak, then testing again**
