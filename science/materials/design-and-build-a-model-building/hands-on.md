# Hands-on — Design and build a model building

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Design on paper
Draw your model building and label every strong shape you plan to use — triangles, wide base, columns, arch — and say what each one is for.

## Build, show, improve
Build the model with adult help if needed. Show it, test it, find its weakest point, improve that one thing, and test it again. Keep a two-line record: what failed, what I changed.
