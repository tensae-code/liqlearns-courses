# Video script — Design and build a model building

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Design and build a model building** — China's way, from its 科学 (Science) textbooks.

Design it first: a strong bridge, a tall steady tower — draw the shapes that will hold it. Then build it, and test it: load the bridge, stand the tower.
Where it sags or leans, improve it and build again. That is the builder's circle — and its ancient master is Lu Ban, who invented the saw and the plane.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
