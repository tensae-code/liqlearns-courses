# Hands-on — Reactions — five signs to look for

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The fizz test, safely
With a parent or teacher: a spoon of baking soda in a cup, add vinegar. Watch, fan the smell to your nose carefully, feel the cup. Write down every sign you observed.

## Sign hunt
Walk your home and list five changes. Mark each: which of the five signs shows, and is a new substance formed? Defend one 'not a reaction' verdict.
