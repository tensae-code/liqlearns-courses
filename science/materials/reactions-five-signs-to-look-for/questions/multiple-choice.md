# Reactions — five signs to look for — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which is NOT one of the five signs?
a) gas forming  b) colour change  c) the substance getting bigger when heated
**Answer: c) the substance getting bigger when heated**

**2.** In Experiment 3.2 the cup cooling is the sign of —
a) a solid forming  b) an energy/temperature change  c) a colour change
**Answer: b) an energy/temperature change**

**3.** Dough becoming baked bread shows the sign of —
a) texture/hardness change  b) gas forming from solutions  c) a solid forming
**Answer: a) texture/hardness change**

**4.** One sign alone does not prove a reaction because —
a) signs are invisible  b) some signs also happen in physical changes  c) reactions never show signs
**Answer: b) some signs also happen in physical changes**

**5.** Fizz when vinegar meets baking soda is the sign of —
a) gas forming  b) colour change  c) texture change
**Answer: a) gas forming**
