# Reactions — five signs to look for — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** A solid forming where two solutions meet is a sign of a reaction.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Bubbles from boiling water prove a chemical reaction.
**Answer: False**

**3.** Heat or light given out is a sign of a possible reaction.
**Answer: True**

**4.** If any one sign appears, a reaction is proved.
**Answer: False**
