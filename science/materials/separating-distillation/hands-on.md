# Hands-on — Separating mixtures — distillation

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Draw the home still
From a visit or an elder's description, draw the local araki (katikala) still large, and label: heat, vessel, vapour path, cooling, receiver.

## Two vessels, two answers
For salt water under distillation, write what lands in the receiver and what stays in the flask — then check against the lesson.
