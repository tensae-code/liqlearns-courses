# Video script — Separating mixtures — distillation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Build the train in order. Flask on the heat. Condenser sloping down. Receiver at the end.
Check the condenser hoses: water IN at the bottom, OUT at the top — the jacket must stay full.
Drop in the boiling chips before heating. They stop the liquid bumping and leaping.
Heat gently — an adult's job. Watch the thermometer and the first drips.
The lower-boiling part comes over first. That is your distillate.
Safety: never heat a sealed system, never leave the flame, and keep faces away from the vapour path.
