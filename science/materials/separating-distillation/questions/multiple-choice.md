# Separating mixtures — distillation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The local araki still and the laboratory still share —
a) nothing  b) the same principle: vaporise, condense, collect  c) only the heat
**Answer: b) the same principle: vaporise, condense, collect**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the local still, the vapour is —
a) thrown away  b) cooled and collected  c) burned
**Answer: b) cooled and collected**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Distillation separates liquids by —
a) colour  b) boiling point  c) magnetism
**Answer: b) boiling point**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Condenser water runs —
a) top to bottom  b) bottom to top
**Answer: b) bottom to top** — so the jacket stays full.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Boiling chips are added to —
a) flavour the liquid  b) prevent bumping  c) raise the boiling point
**Answer: b) prevent bumping**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The liquid collected in the receiver is the —
a) residue  b) distillate  c) filtrate
**Answer: b) distillate**
