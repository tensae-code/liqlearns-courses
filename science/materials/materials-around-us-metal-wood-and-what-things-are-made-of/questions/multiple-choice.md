# Materials around us — metal, wood and what things are made of — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The quickest test that catches iron and steel among metals is…
a) Tasting  b) A magnet — it takes iron and steel, but not aluminium or copper  c) Weighing  d) Smelling
**Answer: b) A magnet — it takes iron and steel, but not aluminium or copper**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Wood is recognised by…
a) Its transparency  b) Its grain — the lines of the tree — and its warm feel  c) Its ring when struck  d) Its stretchiness
**Answer: b) Its grain — the lines of the tree — and its warm feel**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Glass's family marks are…
a) Soft and bendy  b) Hard, smooth, transparent — and breaking into sharp edges  c) Warm and grainy  d) Stretchy and springy
**Answer: b) Hard, smooth, transparent — and breaking into sharp edges**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A knife's blade is steel and its handle wood or plastic because…
a) Makers are careless  b) The blade must be hard and stay sharp; the handle must be warm, grippy and not carry heat to the hand  c) Wood is cheaper everywhere  d) Steel cannot be shaped
**Answer: b) The blade must be hard and stay sharp; the handle must be warm, grippy and not carry heat to the hand**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The full answer to 'what is a bicycle made of?' is…
a) Metal  b) Part by part: metal frame, rubber tyres, plastic-and-leather seat, glass in the lamp — each material fits its part's job  c) Bicycle material  d) Iron only
**Answer: b) Part by part: metal frame, rubber tyres, plastic-and-leather seat, glass in the lamp — each material fits its part's job**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) A spoon and a coin can be made of the same material. This shows…
a) Objects and materials are the same thing  b) The material is not the object — one material serves in many objects  c) Coins are spoons  d) Metal has no other use
**Answer: b) The material is not the object — one material serves in many objects**
