# Materials around us — metal, wood and what things are made of — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Wood shows the tree's lines, called its _____.
**Answer: grain**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Iron and steel are taken by a _____, aluminium is not.
**Answer: magnet**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A knife's _____ is steel; its handle is wood or plastic.
**Answer: blade**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Each part's material is chosen for the _____ that part does.
**Answer: job**
