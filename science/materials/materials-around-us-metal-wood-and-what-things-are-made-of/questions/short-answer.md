# Materials around us — metal, wood and what things are made of — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give three marks by which you can name a metal without being told.
**Answer: Any three: shiny surface, cold feeling to the touch, hard, rings when tapped/struck, (for iron and steel) taken by a magnet.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is 'the chair is made of chair' a failed answer — what should replace it?
**Answer: It names the object, not the material. The answer must name what the thing is made from — wood, metal, plastic, fabric — part by part if the parts differ.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Take a cooking pot: name its likely materials part by part and one reason for each.
**Answer: Body: metal (conducts heat to the food, strong, does not burn). Handles: wood, plastic or thick insulated material (poor heat conductor, safe grip). Lid knob: same reasoning as handles; lid itself usually metal or glass (glass lets you see the food).**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Your magnet ignores an aluminium cup but takes a steel spoon. What have you learned about metals?
**Answer: Metals are a family with different members, not one single stuff: iron and steel are magnetic; aluminium (and copper) are metals too but are not taken by a magnet.**
