# Hands-on — Materials around us — metal, wood and what things are made of

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The touch-and-tap sort
Blindfold a partner (or close your own eyes) and identify six samples by touch, temperature-feel and tap-sound alone. Say aloud for each: the marks that named it.

## Take one thing apart — on paper
Choose a bicycle, a lamp or a cooking pot and draw it large, labelling every part with its material and its reason. Say aloud: one object, many materials, each with a job.
