# Materials around us — metal, wood and what things are made of — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner identifies the common materials by their family marks — metal (cold, shiny, ringing, some magnetic), wood (grain, warm), glass (transparent, sharp-breaking), plastic, fabric, clay, stone, rubber, paper — analyses a multi-material object part by part, and gives the job-reason for each part's material, answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 2 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — doing the lesson's own experiments and sorts by hand, naming each idea aloud, and reasoning about new examples until the teaching is the learner's own.
