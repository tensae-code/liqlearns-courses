# Video script — Materials around us — metal, wood and what things are made of

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Materials around us**.

What is the thing nearest you made of? Not what it is — what it is MADE of. Around us stand the materials: metal, cold and shiny, ringing when tapped, some of it caught by a magnet; wood, warm, wearing its grain; glass, see-through and sharp when broken; plastic, fabric, clay, stone, rubber, paper — each with its own marks. And most things are built from several: a knife is steel where it cuts and wood where the hand holds. Learn to name the materials, part by part, and the whole room becomes readable.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
