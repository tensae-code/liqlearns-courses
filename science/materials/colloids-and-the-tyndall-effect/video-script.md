Today: **Colloids and the Tyndall effect** — Costa Rica's way, from its Grade 7 Ciencias, Código 80002 (printed p. 92).

A **colloid** is a mixture in which tiny particles of one substance are spread evenly through another substance — and, unlike the particles in a suspension, they are too small and too well spread to settle out on standing.

Shine a narrow beam of light through a colloid in a dark place and the beam itself becomes **visible** inside the mixture: the colloid's particles **scatter** the light — they throw it in many directions as it passes. That scattering is the **Tyndall effect**.
