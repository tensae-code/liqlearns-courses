# Colloids and the Tyndall effect — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: What a colloid is.
**Answer:** A **colloid** is a mixture in which tiny particles of one substance are spread evenly through another substance — and, unlike the particles in a suspension, they are too small and too well spread to settle out on standing.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The Tyndall effect — light scattered by a colloid.
**Answer:** Shine a narrow beam of light through a colloid in a dark place and the beam itself becomes **visible** inside the mixture: the colloid's particles **scatter** the light — they throw it in many directions as it passes. That scattering is the **Tyndall effect**.
