# Hands-on — Colloids and the Tyndall effect

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The beam test
In a dim room, shine a torch beam through a glass of water with a little milk stirred in, and then through a glass of plain salt water. Draw both glasses and the beam in each. Write under the pair which one showed the Tyndall effect, and what that proves about each mixture.
