# Video script — Working with acids and bases — properties, uses, precautions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zinc in acid fizzes — that gas is hydrogen.

Acids taste sour in food. Bases feel soapy. But in the lab, nobody tastes and nobody touches.

Eight rules keep you safe. The biggest: add acid to water — never water to acid.

Splash in the eye? Flood it with water, then get help.

The rules are not a footnote. The rules are the lesson.
