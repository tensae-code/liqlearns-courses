# Working with acids and bases — properties, uses, precautions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Zinc in hydrochloric acid releases —
a) oxygen  b) hydrogen  c) carbon dioxide
**Answer: b) hydrogen**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A base feels —
a) gritty  b) soapy  c) dry
**Answer: b) soapy**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Experiment 3.5 tests which base against indicators?
a) ammonia  b) lime  c) sodium hydroxide
**Answer: a) ammonia**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** When diluting concentrated acid, you add —
a) water to acid  b) acid to water  c) either way
**Answer: b) acid to water**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A chemical splash in the eye is first —
a) wiped with a dry cloth  b) washed with plenty of water  c) left until it stops hurting
**Answer: b) washed with plenty of water**
