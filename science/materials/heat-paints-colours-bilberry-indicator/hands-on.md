# Hands-on — Heat paints colours — bilberry indicator

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The two cups
With an adult handling the warm water, put the same bilberry juice into a cup of warm water and a cup of cold water. Watch each cup closely and say the colour you see in each, out loud, before you draw anything.

## Colour record
Draw both cups and colour your drawings to match the cups. Label them warm and cold. Under the pair, write the fair-test sentence: same juice, same amount — only the water's warmth was different.

## Explain heat's painting
Pointing at your drawings, explain to someone at home how heat paints colours with bilberry juice — Finland's own way, with a local berry — in your own words.
