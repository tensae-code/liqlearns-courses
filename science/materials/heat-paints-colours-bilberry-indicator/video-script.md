Today: **Heat paints colours — bilberry indicator** — Finland's way, from its Luova LUMA activity bank.

The same bilberry juice goes into two cups: warm water, and cold water.
Watch the colours. The juice does not show the same colour in the two cups — heat paints colours.
Same juice, same amount: only warm against cold is different. Finland teaches it with its own local berry, the bilberry.
