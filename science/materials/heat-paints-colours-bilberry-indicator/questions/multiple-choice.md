# Heat paints colours — bilberry indicator — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The juice in this lesson comes from…
a) Beetroot  b) Bilberries — Finland's local berry  c) Petunia flowers  d) Lemons
**Answer: b) Bilberries — Finland's local berry**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In the first cup, bilberry juice is put into…
a) Cold water  b) Warm water  c) Milk  d) Empty air
**Answer: b) Warm water**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) What must be done with the colour in the warm cup?
a) Ignored  b) Watched and recorded — it is half the experiment  c) Poured away at once  d) Covered so nobody sees it
**Answer: b) Watched and recorded — it is half the experiment**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The second cup holds the same juice in…
a) Warm water again  b) Cold water  c) Soap water  d) No water
**Answer: b) Cold water**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Comparing the cups, the juice's colour is…
a) Exactly the same  b) Not the same in warm and cold water  c) Gone in both  d) Black in both
**Answer: b) Not the same in warm and cold water**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The fair test keeps the same juice and amount, and changes only…
a) The cups' colour  b) Warm against cold water  c) The berry  d) The watcher
**Answer: b) Warm against cold water**
