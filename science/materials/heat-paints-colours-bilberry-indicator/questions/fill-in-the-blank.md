# Heat paints colours — bilberry indicator — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The juice in this lesson is made from _____, Finland's local berry.
**Answer: bilberry (bilberries)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Bilberry juice is first put into _____ water.
**Answer: warm**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The same juice in cold water shows a _____ colour from the warm cup.
**Answer: different**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The lesson's name says it: _____ paints colours.
**Answer: heat**
