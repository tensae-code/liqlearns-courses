# Dangerous gases in the air — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "The components of air that can harm" teach?
**Answer:** The book names the danger plainly: **dangers of components of air — carbon dioxide, water vapour and carbon monoxide**.

**2.** What does the part "Knowing the danger is the protection" teach?
**Answer:** The lesson's point is knowledge as guard: a gas you cannot see is a danger you must **know** — the book teaches these dangers inside the air unit itself, so the learner meets the harm in the same place they meet the air's composition and uses.
