# Dangerous gases in the air

## Do it first
Before you read: air is mostly safe to breathe — you are breathing it now. But the book names some parts of air as dangerous. Which parts could those be, and when? Say your guesses. Then read on: this is Rwanda's way in — after the composition of air, its Primary 4 book turns to the dangers of its components.

## Sub lesson 1 — The components of air that can harm

The book names the danger plainly: **dangers of components of air — carbon dioxide, water vapour and carbon monoxide**. Air is a mixture, and knowing its parts includes knowing which of them can harm, and that each is dangerous in its own way and measure.

## Sub lesson 2 — Knowing the danger is the protection

The lesson's point is knowledge as guard: a gas you cannot see is a danger you must **know** — the book teaches these dangers inside the air unit itself, so the learner meets the harm in the same place they meet the air's composition and uses.

## The key ideas

- **The components of air that can harm** — the dangerous components.
- **Knowing the danger is the protection** — knowledge as the guard.
