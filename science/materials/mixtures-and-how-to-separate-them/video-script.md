# Video script — Mixtures and how to separate them

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two kinds: **seen** and **hidden** — ልይ ዘር and ዋህድ ዘር.
The dissolved part is the **solute**; the dissolver, the **solvent**.
Four ways back apart: **filter** it, **funnel** it, **evaporate** it, **distil** it.
A mixture is only **physically** joined — so physics can unjoin it.
