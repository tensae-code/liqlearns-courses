# Mixtures and how to separate them — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. The traditional areke still works by ______ (ንጥረት).**
**Answer: distillation**

**2. In a solution the dissolver is the ______ (አሟሚ).**
**Answer: solvent**

**3.** Separation ______ the components of a mixture.
**Answer: purifies**
