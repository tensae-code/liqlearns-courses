# Mixtures and how to separate them — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Oil and water together are a —**
a) homogeneous mixture  b) heterogeneous mixture  c) compound
**Answer: b) heterogeneous mixture**

**2. Salt solution is a —**
a) heterogeneous mixture  b) element  c) homogeneous mixture
**Answer: c) homogeneous mixture**

**3. In a solution, the part that dissolves is the —**
a) solvent  b) solute  c) component layer
**Answer: b) solute**

**4. Muddy water is separated by —**
a) distillation first  b) filtration  c) a magnet
**Answer: b) filtration**

**5. Salt is got back from salt water by —**
a) evaporation  b) the separating funnel  c) freezing it solid
**Answer: a) evaporation**

**6. Could the separating funnel separate salt water?**
a) yes, easily  b) no — it has no layers  c) only when frozen
**Answer: b) no — it has no layers**
