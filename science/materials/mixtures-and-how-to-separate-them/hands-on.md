# Hands-on — Mixtures and how to separate them

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Filtration, run
Filter muddy water through paper in a funnel, the book's figure 3.4 way. Hold the before and after cups to the light for the class.

## The bottle funnel
Cut the book's funnel from a plastic bottle with a pierced cap. Shake oil and water, let them settle into layers in the funnel, and drain the lower layer first — naming which it is and why it sits below.
