# Early ideas of the atom — Democritus against Aristotle — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The thinker who said matter is built of indivisible atoms is ___ .
**Answer: Democritus**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Under Democritus, foil-cutting stops at the smallest indivisible particles, called ___ .
**Answer: atoms**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The atomic theory that settled the argument on evidence in the early 19th century was ___'s.
**Answer: Dalton**
