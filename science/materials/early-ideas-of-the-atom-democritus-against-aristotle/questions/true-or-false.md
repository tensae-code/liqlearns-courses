# Early ideas of the atom — Democritus against Aristotle — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For Democritus, an atom is indivisible — it cannot be cut into smaller pieces of the same substance.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Aristotle's picture has empty space between atoms.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Matter looks continuous because its particles are invisible to the naked eye.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Dalton settled the argument in ancient Greek times.
**Answer: False**
