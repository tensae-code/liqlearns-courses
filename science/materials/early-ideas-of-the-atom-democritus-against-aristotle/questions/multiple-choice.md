# Early ideas of the atom — Democritus against Aristotle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Democritus held that matter is —
a) continuous, divisible without end  b) discontinuous — indivisible atoms with empty space between  c) made of fire and water
**Answer: b) discontinuous — indivisible atoms with empty space between**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Democritus's picture, the empty space is —
a) inside each atom  b) between the atoms  c) only in gases
**Answer: b) between the atoms**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Aristotle and Plato held that a piece of matter can be —
a) divided only ten times  b) divided until it vanishes  c) divided again and again without end
**Answer: c) divided again and again without end**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Cutting aluminium foil in half again and again, Aristotle's picture says the pieces —
a) stay foil forever, smaller without limit  b) stop at single atoms  c) turn into a different substance
**Answer: a) stay foil forever, smaller without limit**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The atom idea was finally accepted because —
a) atoms were seen with the naked eye  b) Dalton's atomic theory settled the argument on evidence  c) Aristotle withdrew his picture
**Answer: b) Dalton's atomic theory settled the argument on evidence**
