# Hands-on — Early ideas of the atom — Democritus against Aristotle

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The foil debate
Take a strip of aluminium foil. Cut it in half. Cut one half in half again. Keep going until the pieces are too small to cut. Half the group argues Aristotle: "we stopped only because our scissors and fingers stopped — the foil could go on forever." The other half argues Democritus: "somewhere there is a smallest piece that is still foil." Each side must answer the other side's best point.

## Break and look
Break a dry stick, tear paper, crush a grain of salt. After each one ask: is the smallest piece still the same stuff? Where would it stop being that stuff? Write the stopping idea in one sentence.
