# Reactions — conservation of mass — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The law of conservation of mass says mass in a reaction is —
a) always lost as ash  b) neither created nor destroyed  c) created by heat
**Answer: b) neither created nor destroyed**

**2.** Burnt paper's ash weighs less because —
a) mass was destroyed  b) some products escaped as gases  c) paper has no mass
**Answer: b) some products escaped as gases**

**3.** Magnesium ash weighs more than the ribbon because —
a) mass was created  b) the ash is bigger  c) oxygen from the air joined the product
**Answer: c) oxygen from the air joined the product**

**4.** In 2Na + Cl₂ → 2NaCl, the chlorine atoms number —
a) 2 on the left, 1 on the right  b) 2 on each side  c) 1 on each side
**Answer: b) 2 on each side**

**5.** Experiment 3.3 heats iron and sulfur in a closed tube so that —
a) it heats faster  b) nothing can escape or enter  c) the tube does not break
**Answer: b) nothing can escape or enter**
