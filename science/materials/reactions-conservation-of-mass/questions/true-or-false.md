# Reactions — conservation of mass — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Atoms are rearranged in a reaction, never created or destroyed.
**Answer: True**

**2.** If ash weighs less than the wood, the law of conservation is broken.
**Answer: False**

**3.** Total mass of products equals total mass of reactants.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Dissolving sugar in water destroys its mass.
**Answer: False**
