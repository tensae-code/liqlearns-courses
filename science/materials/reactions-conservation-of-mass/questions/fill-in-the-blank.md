# Reactions — conservation of mass — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or formula.

**1.** Mass is neither ___ nor destroyed in a reaction. (created)
**Answer: created**

**2.** Iron filings and sulfur heated in a closed tube weigh the ___ before and after. (same)
**Answer: same**

**3.** A reaction rearranges ___ ; it does not make new ones. (atoms)
**Answer: atoms**
