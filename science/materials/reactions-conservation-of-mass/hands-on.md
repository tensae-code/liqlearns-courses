# Hands-on — Reactions — conservation of mass

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Closed-bag fizz weighing
Put a cup of vinegar and a spoon of baking soda, separately, inside a sealed plastic bag on a kitchen scale. Note the mass. Tip the soda in without opening the bag. Note the mass again — equal, though the bag puffs with gas.

## Analogy audit
For ground teff, dissolved sugar and burnt paper: write where every part of the starting mass is at the end. No part may be written off as 'gone'.
