# Organic and inorganic compounds — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** All acids, bases and salts are inorganic compounds.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Every compound containing carbon is organic.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Before 1828, chemists believed organic compounds came only from living things.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Wöhler synthesised urea from a living plant.
**Answer: False**
