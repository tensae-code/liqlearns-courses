# Organic and inorganic compounds — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or formula.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Calcium carbonate, CaCO₃, is counted as an ___ compound.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Wöhler made ___ in a laboratory in 1828.
