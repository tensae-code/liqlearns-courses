# Organic and inorganic compounds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A compound is organic when it —
a) is made by a living thing  b) contains carbon  c) contains oxygen
**Answer: b) contains carbon**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is inorganic?
a) sugar  b) cooking oil  c) salt (NaCl)
**Answer: c) salt (NaCl)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Carbon dioxide, CO₂, is counted —
a) organic, because it has carbon  b) inorganic — it is a named exception  c) neither
**Answer: b) inorganic — it is a named exception**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The old belief that organic compounds needed an invisible force was called —
a) the carbon rule  b) the life-force theory  c) neutralisation
**Answer: b) the life-force theory**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Wöhler's 1828 synthesis of urea showed —
a) urea is inorganic  b) an organic compound can be made in a laboratory  c) carbonates are organic
**Answer: b) an organic compound can be made in a laboratory**
