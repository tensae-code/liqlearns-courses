# Video script — Organic and inorganic compounds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Organic means carbon — mostly.

Sugar, oil, wood: organic.

Water, salt, lime: inorganic.

Careful: carbon oxides and carbonates break the rule — they count inorganic.

And once, people thought only living things could make organic compounds — until Wöhler made urea in 1828.
