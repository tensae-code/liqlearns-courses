# Hands-on — Organic and inorganic compounds

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Home-shelf sort
List ten substances at home. Sort each as organic or inorganic with the carbon rule, and mark any exception you find. Bring the hardest one to class and defend your sorting.

## Exception hunt
Find one carbonate at home or nearby (limestone, chalk, an eggshell is calcium carbonate). Explain in two sentences why it counts as inorganic even though it contains carbon.
