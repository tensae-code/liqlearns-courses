# Caring for and storing materials safely — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Materials that are kept _____ stay ready for their work.
**Answer: properly**

**2.** A tool is _____ in its proper place so it is safe and can be found.
**Answer: stored**

**3.** What you have made is kept whole by _____ it.
**Answer: maintaining**
