# Caring for and storing materials safely — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) Maintaining and storing agricultural tools  b) Maintaining the objects we make and learn with  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Keeping materials properly — at home and at school
**Answer: d) Keeping materials properly — at home and at school**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about keeping home and school materials properly?
a) Maintaining the objects we make and learn with  b) Keeping materials properly — at home and at school  c) Maintaining and storing agricultural tools
**Answer: b) Keeping materials properly — at home and at school**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about maintaining and storing farm tools?
a) Maintaining and storing agricultural tools  b) Keeping materials properly — at home and at school  c) Maintaining the objects we make and learn with
**Answer: a) Maintaining and storing agricultural tools**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about maintaining made objects?
a) Keeping materials properly — at home and at school  b) Maintaining and storing agricultural tools  c) Maintaining the objects we make and learn with
