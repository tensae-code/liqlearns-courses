# Caring for and storing materials safely — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Keeping materials properly — at home and at school" teach?
**Answer:** Rwanda's Primary 2 book teaches **keeping and maintaining home materials properly**, and then the same care for **school materials**: materials that are kept properly stay ready for their work and last; materials left carelessly are lost, broken or spoiled.

**2.** What does the part "Maintaining and storing agricultural tools" teach?
**Answer:** In Primary 4 the same care reaches the farm: **maintenance of agricultural tools** and **storage of agricultural tools** are taught as their own topics — a tool is maintained so it keeps working, and stored in its proper place so it is safe and can be found.

**3.** What does the part "Maintaining the objects we make and learn with" teach?
**Answer:** The care continues for the things learners make themselves: **maintenance of utility and learning objects** is taught in Primary 4, Primary 5 and Primary 6 alike — what you have made is kept working and kept whole by maintaining it, not by making it again.
