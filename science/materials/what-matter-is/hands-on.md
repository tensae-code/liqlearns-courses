# Hands-on — What matter is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the world
Make two lists from the things around you: Matter and Not Matter. Put in a stone, water, air in a balloon, wood — and then light from a lamp, a shadow, a sound, warmth from the sun. Defend each Not Matter choice with the two-part test: no mass, takes up no space.
