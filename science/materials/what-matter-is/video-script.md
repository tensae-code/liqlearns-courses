# Video script — What matter is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **What matter is** — Kuwait's way: what matter is — everything around us is matter: it has mass and takes up space, from a stone to the air itself.

Matter is everything that has mass and takes up space. The chair, the water in the cup, the book and your own body are matter. Even the air — which you cannot see — is matter: blow up a balloon and the air inside pushes the rubber out, taking up space. Kuwait's Grade 1 Term 2 book opens its Matter and Energy unit here: before states of matter or changes, the child first sorts the world into matter — the stuff everything is made of. Not everything is matter. Light is not matter: it has no mass and takes up no space, though it travels through matter and shows it to us. Heat, sound and shadows are not matter either — they are things matter does or things that happen to it. The test the book gives is simple enough for a first-year child: can you weigh it, and does it take up room? If yes to both, it is matter. Everything else in this course's Materials title — states, particles, changes — is built on this one sorting.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
