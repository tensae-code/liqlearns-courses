# What matter is — multiple choice


**1.** Matter is everything that…
a) Shines  b) Has mass and takes up space  c) Moves fast  d) Makes sound
**Answer: b) Has mass and takes up space**

**2.** Which of these is matter?
a) Light  b) A shadow  c) Air in a balloon  d) Heat
**Answer: c) Air in a balloon**

**3.** Is light matter?
a) Yes — it fills a room  b) No — it has no mass and takes up no space  c) Only sunlight  d) Only lamplight
**Answer: b) No — it has no mass and takes up no space**

**4.** A balloon blows up because the air inside…
a) Is not matter  b) Takes up space  c) Disappears  d) Turns to light
**Answer: b) Takes up space**

**5.** Your own body is…
a) Not matter  b) Matter  c) Only water  d) A shadow
**Answer: b) Matter**

**6.** The test for matter asks: can it be weighed, and does it take up…?
a) Time  b) Room (space)  c) Light  d) Sound
**Answer: b) Room (space)**
