# What matter is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What is matter?
**Answer: Everything that has mass and takes up space.**

**2.** How do we know air is matter if we cannot see it?
**Answer: It takes up space — it fills and stretches a balloon — and it can be weighed.**

**3.** Name two things that are not matter.
**Answer: Light and heat (sound and shadows are also not matter).**

**4.** Why does this lesson come before states of matter?
**Answer: Because first we must know what matter is; only then can we sort it into solids, liquids and gases.**
