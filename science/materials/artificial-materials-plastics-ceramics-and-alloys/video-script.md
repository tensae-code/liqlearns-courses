Today: **Artificial materials — plastics, ceramics and alloys** — Libya's way, from its العلوم (Science) textbooks.

Nature gives us wood, stone and metals — but not everything we need. So people invent materials: plastics from oil chemicals, in a kind for every job; ceramics from clay, fired hard in a kiln, strong enough to shield a space shuttle; and alloys — metals mixed together, harder and stronger than any one metal alone. Steel, bronze, brass: all invented, all mixed.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
