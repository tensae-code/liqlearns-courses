# Hands-on — Artificial materials — plastics, ceramics and alloys

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Natural or invented?
Collect ten household things. For each one, name its material and sort it: found in nature, or invented by people? For every invented material, say what natural things it was made from (oil chemicals, clay, mixed metals).

## Match the kind to the job
Take three plastic things at home. For each, name the property that fits its job — light, flexible, transparent, heat-resistant — the way the book matches each kind of plastic to its use. Then do the same for one ceramic thing and one alloy thing at home.
