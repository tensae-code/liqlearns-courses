# Artificial materials — plastics, ceramics and alloys — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Artificial materials are…
a) Found ready in nature  b) Invented by people from mixtures of natural things  c) Grown on trees  d) Dug up unchanged
**Answer: b) Invented by people from mixtures of natural things**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Plastics are made mostly from…
a) Clay  b) Sand  c) Oil chemicals  d) Wood sap
**Answer: c) Oil chemicals**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Why do plastics come in different kinds?
a) Each kind is made to match its use  b) By accident  c) Only for colour  d) They do not differ
**Answer: a) Each kind is made to match its use**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Ceramics are hardened by…
a) Freezing  b) Heating in a kiln  c) Soaking in oil  d) Hammering
**Answer: b) Heating in a kiln**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) An alloy is…
a) A pure metal  b) A kind of plastic  c) Fired clay  d) A mixture of two or more metals, sometimes with non-metals
**Answer: d) A mixture of two or more metals, sometimes with non-metals**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Steel is…
a) Iron + carbon  b) Copper + tin  c) Copper + zinc  d) Pure iron
**Answer: a) Iron + carbon**
