# Artificial materials — plastics, ceramics and alloys — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why do people invent new materials?
**Answer:** Because our needs change and natural materials no longer suit every job; invented materials are made from mixtures of natural things to meet the new needs.

**2.** Give two uses of plastics and the property that fits each use.
**Answer:** Any two from the lesson, e.g. flexible plastic for bags and food film; transparent plastic used instead of glass; light, heat-resistant plastic for food packaging and dishes; hard plastic for furniture covers.

**3.** What are ceramics made from, and how are they hardened?
**Answer:** From clay and other materials, hardened by heating in a kiln until hard and strong.

**4.** Why were alloys invented, and name one alloy with its mixture.
**Answer:** Pure metals are usually too soft to use, so alloys mix metals (sometimes with non-metals) to be harder and stronger — e.g. steel (iron + carbon), bronze (copper + tin) or brass (copper + zinc).
