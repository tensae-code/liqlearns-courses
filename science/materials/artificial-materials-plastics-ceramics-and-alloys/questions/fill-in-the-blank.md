# Artificial materials — plastics, ceramics and alloys — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Materials invented by people are called _____ materials.
**Answer: artificial (human-made)**

**2.** Plastics are made mostly from _____ chemicals.
**Answer: oil**

**3.** Ceramics are hardened by heating in a _____.
**Answer: kiln**

**4.** Bronze is copper mixed with _____.
**Answer: tin**

**5.** Pure gold is _____ carats.
**Answer: 24**
