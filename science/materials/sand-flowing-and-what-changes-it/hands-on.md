# Hands-on — Sand flowing — and what changes it

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## First pour
With adult help, pour dry sand slowly from a cup onto a tray. Watch the stream and the heap: say how the grains move, where the heap grows, and when the flowing stops.

## One change at a time
Pour three times, changing one thing each time — pour height, tipping speed, amount of sand — and keep everything else the same. After each pour, say whether the flow changed and how.

## The flow chart
Make a chart: Pour | What I changed | What the flow did. Fill one row for each pour, then, pointing at your chart, explain what affects the flowing of sand, in your own words.
