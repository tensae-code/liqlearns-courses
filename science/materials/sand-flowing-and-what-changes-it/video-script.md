# Video script — Sand flowing — and what changes it

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Sand flowing — and what changes it** — Finland's way, from its Luova LUMA activity bank.

Tip a cup of dry sand. It does not fall in a lump — it pours, grain over grain, and piles into a heap. Sand flows.
Now change one thing — the height, the tip, the amount — and pour again. Compare the pours.
That comparison is the lesson: the flowing of sand, and what affects it.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
