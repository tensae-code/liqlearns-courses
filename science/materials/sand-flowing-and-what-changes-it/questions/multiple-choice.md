# Sand flowing — and what changes it — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Tipped from a cup, dry sand…
a) Falls in one lump like a stone  b) Pours — grain over grain — and piles into a heap  c) Floats upward  d) Stays in the cup like a cake
**Answer: b) Pours — grain over grain — and piles into a heap**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) One grain of sand is a solid, but thousands together…
a) Melt  b) Flow like a very slow, grainy liquid  c) Become water  d) Stop moving forever
**Answer: b) Flow like a very slow, grainy liquid**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Watching the flow means watching…
a) Only the cup  b) How fast it runs, how the heap grows, and when it stops  c) The colour of the tray  d) Nothing — eyes closed
**Answer: b) How fast it runs, how the heap grows, and when it stops**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The activity's own question is…
a) What colour is sand?  b) What affects the flowing of sand?  c) Who owns the sand?  d) How old is sand?
**Answer: b) What affects the flowing of sand?**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) To find what affects the flow, you change…
a) Everything at once  b) One thing at a time, keeping the rest the same  c) Nothing, ever  d) Only the watcher
**Answer: b) One thing at a time, keeping the rest the same**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The answer about what affects the flow comes from…
a) Guessing  b) Comparing pours  c) A dream  d) The cup's label
**Answer: b) Comparing pours**
