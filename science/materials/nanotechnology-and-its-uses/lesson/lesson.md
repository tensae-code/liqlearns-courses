## Sub lesson 1 — Nanotechnology — working at the smallest scale

**Nanotechnology** works with matter at the **nanometre scale** — so small that the pieces are built and measured atom-cluster by atom-cluster, far below anything an eye or an ordinary microscope resolves. At that scale materials behave in new ways, and those new behaviours are the technology.
