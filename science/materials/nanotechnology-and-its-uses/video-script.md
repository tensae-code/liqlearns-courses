Today: **Nanotechnology and its uses** — Sri Lanka's way, from the books cited in the sources.

Nanotechnology works with matter at the nanometre scale — so small that the pieces are built and measured atom-cluster by atom-cluster, far below anything an eye or an ordinary microscope resolves.

Sri Lanka's guide introduces nanotechnology by its applications: the new materials and devices the smallest scale makes possible, across medicine, electronics and materials.
