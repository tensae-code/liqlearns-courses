# Nanotechnology and its uses — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Nanotechnology — working at the smallest scale.
**Answer:** **Nanotechnology** works with matter at the **nanometre scale** — so small that the pieces are built and measured atom-cluster by atom-cluster, far below anything an eye or an ordinary microscope resolves. At that scale materials behave in new ways, and those new behaviours are the technology.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Applications of nanotechnology.
**Answer:** Sri Lanka's guide introduces nanotechnology **by its applications**: the new materials and devices the smallest scale makes possible, across medicine, electronics and materials. This lesson stands on the guide as evidence, exactly as filed — the student book that teaches it in class is a library debt, named in the sources.
