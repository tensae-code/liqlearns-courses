# Changes — useful and harmful changes in daily life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Grinding teff is a —
a) chemical change  b) physical change  c) harmful change only
**Answer: b) physical change** — form changes, substance stays.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dough rising is a —
a) physical change  b) chemical change
**Answer: b) chemical change** — fermentation forms new substances.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Salt is won from sea water by —
a) freezing  b) evaporation  c) rusting
**Answer: b) evaporation** — a useful change.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Tella, tej and bread are made by —
a) fermentation  b) filtration  c) freezing
**Answer: a) fermentation**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which is a harmful change?
a) freezing food  b) making soap  c) rusting iron
**Answer: c) rusting iron**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In the tella project, fermenting is tagged —
a) physical  b) chemical
**Answer: b) chemical**
