# Video script — Changes — useful and harmful changes in daily life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Grind the teff. Chop the wood. Physical — same stuff, new shape.
The dough rises. The injera bakes. Chemical — new stuff!
Freezing keeps food. The sun makes salt from the sea.
Fermentation makes tella, tej and bread. Useful!
But rust eats iron. Food decays. Explosions harm.
Every day is full of changes. Sort them. Judge them.
