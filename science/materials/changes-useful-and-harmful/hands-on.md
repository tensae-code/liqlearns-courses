# Hands-on — Changes — useful and harmful changes in daily life

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The change diary
One day, one page: write every change you see at home and tag it — physical or chemical, useful or harmful. Count which kind wins the day.

## Interview the maker
Ask a tella, tej or araki maker to walk you through their process. Write each step and tag it. Bring the list to class.
