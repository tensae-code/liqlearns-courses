# Hands-on — Strong shapes — triangles, arches and columns

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rack the frames
Make a square frame and a triangle frame from strips of card joined with split pins. Push each one sideways: say which keeps its shape, and what a diagonal does to the square.

## Test bases and arches
Stand the same block tower on a narrow base and then a wide base and say which tips first. Then rest a strip of card as an arch between two books, load it gently in the middle, and say where its weight travels.
