# Strong shapes — triangles, arches and columns — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A square frame pushed sideways…
a) Racks into a diamond shape  b) Cannot move at all  c) Turns into a triangle  d) Gets stronger
**Answer: a) Racks into a diamond shape**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A triangle frame pushed sideways…
a) Racks into a diamond  b) Holds its shape — its sides fix its angles  c) Collapses flat  d) Stretches longer
**Answer: b) Holds its shape — its sides fix its angles**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A diagonal steadies a square frame because it…
a) Makes the square heavier  b) Paints the frame  c) Divides the square into triangles  d) Removes a side
**Answer: c) Divides the square into triangles**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which tower stands steadier?
a) One on a narrow base  b) One with no base  c) They are always equal  d) One on a wide base
**Answer: d) One on a wide base**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A column's job is to…
a) Carry weight downward  b) Span a gap  c) Hold water  d) Block the wind
**Answer: a) Carry weight downward**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) An arch carries the weight on it…
a) Straight through its middle  b) Along its curve, out and down into its feet  c) Into the air above it  d) Nowhere — it holds no weight
**Answer: b) Along its curve, out and down into its feet**
