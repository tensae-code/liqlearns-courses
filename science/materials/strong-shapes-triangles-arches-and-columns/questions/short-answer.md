# Strong shapes — triangles, arches and columns — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why does a triangle hold its shape when a square frame does not?
**Answer:** A triangle's three sides fix its three angles, so it cannot lean; a square frame can rack sideways into a diamond without any of its sides changing length.

**2.** How can a square frame be made steady?
**Answer:** By adding a diagonal — which divides the square into triangles, and triangles hold their shape.

**3.** Why does a wide base stand steadier than a narrow one?
**Answer:** Because the weight sits inside the wide base instead of tipping past it; on a narrow base a small lean carries the weight outside the base and the structure falls.

**4.** How does an arch carry weight across a gap?
**Answer:** The load presses along the arch's curve, travelling outwards and down into the arch's feet, so the weight reaches the ground along the arch's shape instead of needing a straight beam.
