# Strong shapes — triangles, arches and columns — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** A square frame pushed sideways _____ into a diamond.
**Answer: racks**

**2.** A _____ frame holds its shape because its sides fix its angles.
**Answer: triangle**

**3.** A _____ carries weight downward; its shape decides its strength.
**Answer: column**

**4.** An arch carries weight along its _____ into its feet.
**Answer: curve**
