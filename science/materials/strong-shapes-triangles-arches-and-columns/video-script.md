Today: **Strong shapes — triangles, arches and columns** — China's way, from its 科学 (Science) textbooks.

Push a square frame and it leans — it racks. Push a triangle and nothing moves: three sides fix its shape. That is why builders trust triangles.
Give a tower a wide base, let columns carry weight down, and let an arch carry its load along its curve into its feet — shape itself is strength.
