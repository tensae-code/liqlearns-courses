## Sub lesson 2 — Solid, liquid and gas together

Unmelted wax stands solid below; around the wick, wax melts into a liquid pool; and at the flame, wax vapour burns as a gas. One small object holds the whole chapter: solid, liquid and gas, side by side, at the same moment.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why a demonstration proves more than a list

The states are usually taught as a list to memorise. The candle teaches them as a sight: point, and each state is there. The book's own demonstration is the lesson.
