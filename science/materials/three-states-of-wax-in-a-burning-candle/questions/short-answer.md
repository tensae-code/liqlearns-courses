# Three states of wax in a burning candle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: One candle, three states.
**Answer:** A burning candle shows all three states of wax at once.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Solid, liquid and gas together.
**Answer:** Unmelted wax stands solid below; around the wick, wax melts into a liquid pool; and at the flame, wax vapour burns as a gas.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Why a demonstration proves more than a list.
**Answer:** The states are usually taught as a list to memorise.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** One candle, three states → Solid, liquid and gas together → Why a demonstration proves more than a list — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
