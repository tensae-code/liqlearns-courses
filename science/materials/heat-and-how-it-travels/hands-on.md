# Hands-on — Heat and how it travels

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Wax on the rod (adult-led)
The book's conduction test, run by an adult: wax blobs fixed along a metal rod, the end heated, the blobs dropping in sequence from the hot end — heat's travel made visible and timed.

## The circulation, drawn live
As a pot warms (adult-led), pupils draw the loop they infer — arrows up through the middle heat, down at the cooler edges — then compare with the lesson's GIF.
