# Heat and how it travels — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Heat flows from —**
a) colder to hotter  b) hotter to colder  c) either way equally
**Answer: b) hotter to colder**

**2. Heat flow requires —**
a) a temperature difference  b) total darkness  c) a vacuum always
**Answer: a) a temperature difference**

**3. Which is a conductor?**
a) wood  b) plastic  c) copper
**Answer: c) copper**

**4. Convection happens in —**
a) solids only  b) liquids and gases  c) metals
**Answer: b) liquids and gases**

**5. In convection, the hot part —**
a) sinks  b) rises  c) freezes
**Answer: b) rises**

**6. The Sun's heat reaches Earth by —**
a) conduction  b) convection  c) radiation
**Answer: c) radiation**
