# Lesson plan — Heat and how it travels

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Materials · **Lesson:** Heat and how it travels
**Place in the curriculum:** Materials — heat, energy on the move (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** heat is energy flowing from a hotter body to a colder one — a temperature difference is required — and it travels three ways: conduction (handed along a body; iron, aluminium, nickel and copper conduct, wood and plastic do not), convection (circulation in liquids and gases only) and radiation (through air and even vacuum, as the Sun's heat reaches Earth)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Measuring: temperature — temperature is measured; now its movement is followed.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State what heat is and the condition it needs: a temperature difference.
2. Separate the three ways with the book's examples and tests: the wax blobs on a metal rod (conduction), the pot's circulation (convection), the Sun's heat across emptiness (radiation).
3. Name conductors and non-conductors, and state Experiment 8's safety shape: focusing sunlight with a lens is a teacher demonstration, never a pupil task.

## What you need
