# Video script — Heat and how it travels

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Heat** flows from hotter to colder. It needs a **difference**.
**Conduction** — handed along a body. Metal yes, wood no.
**Convection** — liquids and gases **circulate**: hot rises, cold sinks.
**Radiation** — across air, across **emptiness** — the Sun warms the Earth.
The lens experiment? **Teacher's hands only.**
