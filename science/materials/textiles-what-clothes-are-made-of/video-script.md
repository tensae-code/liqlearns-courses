Today: **Textiles — what clothes are made of** — Angola's way: start from the material, not the garment.

Every piece of clothing begins as a textile material: fibres worked into thread, thread woven or knitted into cloth, cloth cut and sewn. The material decides whether the cloth is soft or rough, warm or cool, strong or quick to tear.

Angola's Grade 2 book then reads each garment twice — the kind of clothing, and the fabric it is made of.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
