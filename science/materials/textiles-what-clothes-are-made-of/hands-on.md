# Hands-on — Textiles — what clothes are made of

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the clothing pile
Gather a small pile of real clothes. Sort them twice: first by kind of clothing, then by the fabric each is made from. Say aloud what changes between the two sortings.

## Feel and compare
With eyes closed, feel two different fabrics and describe each — soft or rough, thick or thin, warm or cool. Then open your eyes and name the clothing each belongs to.
