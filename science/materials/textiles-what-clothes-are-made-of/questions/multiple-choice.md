# Textiles — what clothes are made of — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A textile material is…
a) One finished shirt
b) The material cloth is made from, before cutting and sewing
c) A colour for dyeing cloth
d) A needle and thread
**Answer: b) The material cloth is made from, before cutting and sewing**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The material a cloth is made from decides…
a) Only its price
b) Whether it is soft or rough, warm or cool, strong or quick to tear
c) Who may wear it
d) Nothing at all
**Answer: b) Whether it is soft or rough, warm or cool, strong or quick to tear**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In Angola's Grade 2 way, reading a piece of clothing means naming…
a) Its owner
b) The kind of clothing, and the fabric it is made of
c) The shop it came from
d) Its size only
**Answer: b) The kind of clothing, and the fabric it is made of**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Cloth is made when…
a) Fibres are worked into thread, and thread is woven or knitted into cloth
b) Paper is folded
c) Wood is carved
d) Metal is melted
**Answer: a) Fibres are worked into thread, and thread is woven or knitted into cloth**
