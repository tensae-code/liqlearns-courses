# Textiles — what clothes are made of — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does the material come first, before the cutting and sewing?
**Answer:** Because the material decides how the cloth behaves — soft or rough, warm or cool, strong or quick to tear — so every later step works on what the material already is.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the Grade 2 book sort clothing?
**Answer:** By kinds of clothing together with their fabrics — each garment is named by what it is and by the textile material it is made of.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the chain from fibre to clothes.
**Answer:** Fibres are worked into thread, thread is woven or knitted into cloth, and cloth is cut and sewn into clothes.
