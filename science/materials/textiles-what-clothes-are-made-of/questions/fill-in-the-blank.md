# Textiles — what clothes are made of — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The materials cloth is made from are called _____ materials.
**Answer: textile**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Fibres are worked into _____, and that is woven or knitted into cloth.
**Answer: thread**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The _____ is what the body feels and what washing and wear act on.
**Answer: fabric**
