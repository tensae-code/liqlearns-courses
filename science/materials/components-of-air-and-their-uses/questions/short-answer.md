# Components of air and their uses — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Air is a mixture — the percentages.
**Answer:** South Sudan's Primary 6 book gives the composition of air by percentage: **nitrogen 78, oxygen 21, carbon dioxide 0.03, inert gases 0.97**. Air is not one gas — it is a mixture, and the percentages are the mixture's recipe.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Oxygen — the active part.
**Answer:** **Oxygen is the active part of air that supports burning** — proved with a candle under a gas jar: the candle uses the oxygen, and when that part is gone the flame dies, though most of the air is still there.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Carbon dioxide and the other gases — tests and uses.
**Answer:** Carbon dioxide is named by the **limewater test** on breathed-out air — the air we breathe out carries more of it. The unit closes with **the uses of each gas**: each component of the mixture has its own work, and the lesson names them gas by gas.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Air is a mixture — the percentages → Oxygen — the active part → Carbon dioxide and the other gases — tests and uses — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
