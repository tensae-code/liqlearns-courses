## Sub lesson 1 — Air is a mixture — the percentages

South Sudan's Primary 6 book gives the composition of air by percentage: **nitrogen 78, oxygen 21, carbon dioxide 0.03, inert gases 0.97**. Air is not one gas — it is a mixture, and the percentages are the mixture's recipe.

## Sub lesson 2 — Oxygen — the active part

**Oxygen is the active part of air that supports burning** — proved with a candle under a gas jar: the candle uses the oxygen, and when that part is gone the flame dies, though most of the air is still there.

## Sub lesson 3 — Carbon dioxide and the other gases — tests and uses

Carbon dioxide is named by the **limewater test** on breathed-out air — the air we breathe out carries more of it. The unit closes with **the uses of each gas**: each component of the mixture has its own work, and the lesson names them gas by gas.
