# Hands-on — Motion, distance and speed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Oscillation and rotation, made
Swing a stone on a string gently (oscillating) and spin a top or a wheel (rotational). The class names each path kind as it is made — the four kinds sorted live.

## The two-route walk
Walk a winding path between two marks, counting paces; then walk the straight line between the same marks. Distance and displacement, measured with your own feet.
