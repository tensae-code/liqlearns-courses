# Motion, distance and speed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Motion is change of place —**
a) absolutely  b) relative to something else  c) only forwards
**Answer: b) relative to something else**

**2. A swing's back-and-forth motion is —**
a) rotational  b) oscillating  c) straight-line
**Answer: b) oscillating**

**3. Displacement is —**
a) the whole road travelled  b) the straight change from start to finish  c) the time taken
**Answer: b) the straight change from start to finish**

**4. Velocity differs from speed because velocity has —**
a) a bigger number  b) a direction  c) no unit
**Answer: b) a direction**

**5. The world unit of speed is —**
a) km/h always  b) the metre per second (m/s)  c) m/s²
**Answer: b) the metre per second (m/s)**

**6. Acceleration's unit is —**
a) m/s  b) m/s²  c) kg/m³
**Answer: b) m/s²**
