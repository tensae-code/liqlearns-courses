# Video script — Motion, distance and speed

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Motion** — change of place, **relative** to something else.
Four paths: **straight, circular, oscillating, rotational**.
**Distance** walks the road. **Displacement** takes the straight line.
**Speed** is distance over time. **Velocity** adds the direction.
**Acceleration** — velocity changing, metre per second squared.
