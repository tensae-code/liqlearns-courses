# Uses of air and its gases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Air and wind at work  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Air and wind at work**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Oxygen and nitrogen — two gases at work  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Oxygen and nitrogen — two gases at work**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Carbon dioxide — fizz and fire  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Carbon dioxide — fizz and fire**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: air and wind at work — turbines, drying, sailboats, tyres — and air's own gases at work one by one: oxygen in cylinders, nitrogen for fertilizer and food packets, carbon dioxide for fizzy drinks and fire extinguishers, as Bangladesh's Class 5 book teaches them. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Bangladesh, Grade 5 — *Elementary Science Class 5*, printed p. 26  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
