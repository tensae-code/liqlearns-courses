# Uses of air and its gases — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Air and wind at work.
**Answer:** Moving air works: it turns turbines, dries clothes and hair, drives sailboats and inflates tyres.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Oxygen and nitrogen — two gases at work.
**Answer:** Air's own gases are also used one by one.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Carbon dioxide — fizz and fire.
**Answer:** Carbon dioxide makes fizzy drinks fizz, and fills fire extinguishers, where it smothers flame.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Air and wind at work → Oxygen and nitrogen — two gases at work → Carbon dioxide — fizz and fire — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
