Today: **Uses of air and its gases** — Bangladesh's way, from its book (Bangladesh, Grade 5 — *Elementary Science Class 5*, printed p. 26).

Moving air works: it turns turbines, dries clothes and hair, drives sailboats and inflates tyres. Class 5 opens with air as a worker before it opens air as a mixture.
Air's own gases are also used one by one. Oxygen is carried in cylinders for patients, divers and climbers. Nitrogen goes into urea fertilizer and into food packets, where it keeps the food fresh.
Carbon dioxide makes fizzy drinks fizz, and fills fire extinguishers, where it smothers flame. One gas, two opposite jobs — the book teaches the uses gas by gas, and that sort is the lesson.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
