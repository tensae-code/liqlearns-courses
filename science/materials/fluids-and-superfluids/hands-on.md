# Hands-on — Fluids and superfluids

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The flow test
Take three materials — water, dry sand or flour, and a stone. Tip each one in a cup. Which ones flow and take the cup's shape? Say which are fluids by the lesson's test, and why the grainy one is the tricky case (its grains flow, but it is not one flowing material).

## Drag, made visible
Stir water in a bowl, then stir something thick. Feel the difference in resistance with the spoon. That resistance is the drag a superfluid does not have — say out loud what would be different if the bowl held a superfluid.
