# Fluids and superfluids — multiple choice


**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A fluid is a material that…
a) Holds its own shape always  b) Flows and takes its container's shape  c) Is always wet  d) Cannot be a gas
**Answer: b) Flows and takes its container's shape**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is a fluid? 
a) A stone  b) Air  c) Wood  d) Ice
**Answer: b) Air**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The test for a fluid is…
a) Its colour  b) Its wetness  c) Whether it flows  d) Its weight
**Answer: c) Whether it flows**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Everyday fluids, as they flow…
a) Have no drag at all  b) Drag a little against themselves and their vessel  c) Flow only upward  d) Stop at once
**Answer: b) Drag a little against themselves and their vessel**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A superfluid flows…
a) Very slowly  b) Only when heated  c) Without friction at all  d) Only in rivers
**Answer: c) Without friction at all**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Honey pours slowly because…
a) It is not a fluid  b) Its inner drag is strong  c) It is a superfluid  d) It is a solid
**Answer: b) Its inner drag is strong**
