# Fluids and superfluids — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why are both liquids and gases fluids?
**Answer: Because both flow and take the shape of what holds them — the test is flowing, not wetness or being seen.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Is a stone a fluid? Say why or why not.
**Answer: No — a stone holds its own shape and does not flow, so it fails the fluid test.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What makes a superfluid different from an ordinary fluid?
**Answer: A superfluid flows without friction — the inner drag that every everyday fluid has is gone.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) What does honey show about ordinary fluids?
**Answer: That they drag as they flow: honey's strong drag makes it pour slowly, where water's weaker drag lets it pour freely.**
