# Fluids and superfluids — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A material that flows and takes its container's shape is a _____.
**Answer: fluid**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Both liquids and _____ are fluids.
**Answer: gases**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A superfluid flows without _____ at all.
**Answer: friction**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Honey pours slowly because its inner _____ is strong.
**Answer: drag**
