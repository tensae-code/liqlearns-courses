# Glass and its kinds — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Glass: a transparent supercooled liquid made from silicates; silica glass, water glass that dissolves in water, ordinary soda-lime glass (the batch mixed 75 percent silica, 15 percent sodium carbonate, 10 percent calcium carbonate and 25 percent broken glass as printed, and annealed so it does not crack), hard potash-lime glass, borosilicate or Pyrex glass, and lead crystal; coloured glass from metal oxides — blue from cobalt oxide, green from chromium oxide, red from copper oxide, white from tin oxide.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science Grade 10*, printed pp. 145–147.**
