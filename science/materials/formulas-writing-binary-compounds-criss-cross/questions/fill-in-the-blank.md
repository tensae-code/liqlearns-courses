# Formulas — writing binary compounds (criss-cross) — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or formula.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Crossing calcium's 2 and nitrogen's 3 gives the formula ___ . (Ca₃N₂)
**Answer: Ca₃N₂**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A compound of exactly two elements is called a ___ compound. (binary)
**Answer: binary**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Aluminium and chlorine criss-cross to ___ . (AlCl₃)
**Answer: AlCl₃**
