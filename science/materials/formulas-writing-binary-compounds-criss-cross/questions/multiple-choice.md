# Formulas — writing binary compounds (criss-cross) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Calcium (2) + nitrogen (3) gives —
a) Ca₂N₃  b) Ca₃N₂  c) CaN
**Answer: b) Ca₃N₂** — — each valence crosses to the other element.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Magnesium (2) + oxygen (2) gives —
a) Mg₂O₂  b) MgO  c) MgO₂
**Answer: b) MgO** — — simplify by the common factor 2 first.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Sodium (1) + iodine (1) gives —
a) NaI  b) Na₂I  c) INa
**Answer: a) NaI** — — metal first, subscripts of 1 are not written.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Aluminium (3) + chlorine (1) gives —
a) AlCl  b) Al₃Cl  c) AlCl₃
**Answer: c) AlCl₃**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In criss-cross, after crossing you must —
a) change the symbols  b) check atoms × valence balance both sides  c) add the valences
**Answer: b) check atoms × valence balance both sides**
