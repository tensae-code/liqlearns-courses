# Hands-on — Formulas — writing binary compounds (criss-cross)

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Criss-cross cards
Make element cards with valences on top corners. Pair cards, draw the crossing arrows on paper, write the formula, then check it by multiplication. Five pairs minimum.

## The simplifying trap
Deliberately write Mg₂O₂ and Ca₂O₂-style answers, then fix each by its common factor. Say the rule aloud each time: simplify first, then cross.
