# Video script — Formulas — writing binary compounds (criss-cross)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Described slot: a short telling of this lesson — the criss-cross method writes a binary compound's formula: write the symbols, put the valences on top, simplify, then cross each valence down as the other element's subscript — with the learner doing the lesson's Do It Yourself steps along with it.
