# Reactions — reactants become products — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or formula.

**1.** Starting substances in a reaction are called ___ . (reactants)
**Answer: reactants**

**2.** Hydrogen + oxygen → ___ is the word equation for making water. (water)
**Answer: water**

**3.** Burning magnesium leaves a white ash of magnesium ___ . (oxide)
**Answer: oxide**
