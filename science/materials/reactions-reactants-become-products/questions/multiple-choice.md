# Reactions — reactants become products — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O, the reactants are —
a) water only  b) hydrogen and oxygen  c) hydrogen and water
**Answer: b) hydrogen and oxygen**

**2.** The arrow in an equation reads —
a) equals  b) yields / becomes  c) plus
**Answer: b) yields / becomes**

**3.** Magnesium ash proves a reaction because —
a) it weighs the same  b) its properties differ from the metal's  c) it is hot
**Answer: b) its properties differ from the metal's**

**4.** Which of these is a chemical reaction at home?
a) melting ice  b) chopping wood  c) brewing tella
**Answer: c) brewing tella**

**5.** The substances made by a reaction are called —
a) reactants  b) products  c) coefficients
**Answer: b) products**
