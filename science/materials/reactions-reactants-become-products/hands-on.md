# Hands-on — Reactions — reactants become products

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Property comparison table
Take two home reaction pairs — a raw egg and a boiled egg; dough and baked bread. List two properties before and two after for each; underline what is new.

## Word equations of the day
Write word equations for three reactions you saw today (a fire lit, food cooked, a candle burned): reactants → products, in words only.
