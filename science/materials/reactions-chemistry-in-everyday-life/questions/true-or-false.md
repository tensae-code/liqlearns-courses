# Reactions — chemistry in everyday life — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Brewing and cheese-making are chemical reactions.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Filtering finished tella can turn it back into barley and water.
**Answer: False**

**3.** Gesho is one of tella's raw materials.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Cooking food involves chemical reactions.
**Answer: True**
