# Reactions — chemistry in everyday life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these runs on a chemical reaction?
a) boiling water  b) smelting iron  c) chopping wood
**Answer: b) smelting iron**

**2.** In tella-making, the malted barley is called —
a) asharo  b) bikil  c) difdif
**Answer: b) bikil** — — teacher-guide evidence.

**3.** The dark roasted maize that sets tella's colour is —
a) gesho  b) tejet  c) asharo
**Answer: c) asharo** — — teacher-guide evidence.

**4.** Fermentation is a chemical change because —
a) it takes a long time  b) new substances form  c) it happens in a vessel
**Answer: b) new substances form**

**5.** The vessel tella ferments in is the —
a) jebena  b) insera  c) mitad
**Answer: b) insera** — — teacher-guide evidence.
