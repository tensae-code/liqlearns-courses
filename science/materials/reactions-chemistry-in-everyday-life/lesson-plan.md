# Lesson plan — Reactions — chemistry in everyday life

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Materials · **Lesson:** Reactions — chemistry in everyday life
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Elements, Compounds and Chemical Reactions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** chemical reactions run everyday life — burning, cooking, brewing, smelting, making — and tella-making shows one complete traditional process, stage by stage, as chemistry
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the whole of Unit 3 — symbols, formulas and reactions — gathered into the world outside the classroom. The Chemistry title's everyday-chemistry lessons treat this ground at secondary depth; this is the first-depth lesson, new here, cross-referenced not copied.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name everyday reactions across burning, cooking, industry and living bodies.
2. Run a home energy audit: reaction, reactants, what it gives.
3. Tell the tella process in its six stages, in order (teacher-guide evidence).
4. Argue why fermentation is a chemical change, not a physical one.

## If the learner is stuck
Lists physical changes as reactions (boiling water, chopping wood) → apply the products test: is a new substance formed? Calls fermentation 'just soaking' → soaking is physical; the fermentation stages form new substances — that is why tella cannot be filtered back into barley and water.
