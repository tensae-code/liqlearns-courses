# Hands-on — Reactions — chemistry in everyday life

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Home energy audit
Audit your home: list every reaction that gives energy (fire, stove, lamp, candle), its reactants, and what it gives. Bring the list to class and merge it into the group audit.

## Stage cards for tella
Write the six tella stages on six cards, shuffle, and order them. Beside each, write P (physical) or C (chemical) for the main change in that stage, with the fermentation stages marked C.
