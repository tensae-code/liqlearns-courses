# Video script — Reactions — chemistry in everyday life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This is a FULL script — the placement names the need.

---

This is tella being made — chemistry in a home vessel. Stage by stage.

Stage one. Barley soaks in water, about a day. It wakes up — it becomes malt. Bikil.
Stage two. The malt is dried in the sun and ground. Gesho leaves and stems are dried and ground too.
Stage three. Malt and gesho meet water in the smoked vessel, the insera. It ferments two days. This is tejet.
Stage four. Dough is baked flat — ye-tella kita — sliced in, and the vessel is sealed. No air. It ferments on. This is tenses.
Stage five. Maize, soaked, dried, roasted dark and ground — asharo. Asharo gives the tella its colour.
Stage six. Difdif goes in. The tella rests five, six hours. Then it is filtered, and served.

Soaking was physical. Grinding was physical.
But fermentation made NEW substances — and no filter can unmake them.
That is a chemical change, running a home.
