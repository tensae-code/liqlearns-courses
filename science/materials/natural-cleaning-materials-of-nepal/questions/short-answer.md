# Natural cleaning materials of Nepal — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Ritha (soapnut), pina (mustard oil-cake), ash, sajivan (moringa) and lemon juice are the natural cleaning materials used traditionally in Nepal, harming neither health nor environment, set against factory-made cleaners that pollute.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Shikshak Nirdeshika Vigyan Grade 10 (guide)*, printed p. 548.**
