# Hands-on — Materials and their properties — hard, strong, flexible

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The scratch ladder
With care, try a fingernail, a coin and a nail in turn on chalk, wood, plastic and stone offcuts. Rank the samples from the marks. Say aloud: the harder scratches, the softer is scratched — never the reverse.

## Bend, spring or snap
Bend a rubber band, a plastic ruler, a green twig and a dry twig (gently, away from faces). Sort them: springs back, bends and stays, snaps. Say aloud: flexibility, and its two neighbours — elasticity and brittleness.
