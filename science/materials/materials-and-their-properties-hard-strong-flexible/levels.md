# Materials and their properties — hard, strong, flexible — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner defines and tests the three mechanical properties — hardness by the scratch contest, strength by loading to failure, flexibility by bending (with elastic and brittle as its neighbours) — ranks real samples, and chooses materials for named jobs by the property the job needs, answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — doing the lesson's own experiments and sorts by hand, naming each idea aloud, and reasoning about new examples until the teaching is the learner's own.
