Today: **Materials and their properties**.

Bend a rubber band, a ruler and a dry twig. Three materials, three answers: spring, bend, snap. Those answers are properties, and three of them rule the workshop. Hardness — resisting scratches; the harder material always scratches the softer. Strength — resisting breaking under a load, the property bridges and ropes live by. Flexibility — bending without breaking, with the elastic springing back and the brittle snapping instead. The rule for choosing anything: name the property the job needs — then pick the material that owns it.
