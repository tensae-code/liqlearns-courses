# Materials and their properties — hard, strong, flexible — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Hardness is a material's resistance to…
a) Bending  b) Scratches, dents and wear  c) Melting  d) Stretching
**Answer: b) Scratches, dents and wear**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In a scratch test between two materials…
a) The softer scratches the harder  b) The harder scratches the softer  c) Both scratch equally  d) Nothing can be learned
**Answer: b) The harder scratches the softer**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Strength is tested by…
a) Scratching the sample  b) Loading or pulling the sample until it fails  c) Bending it once  d) Weighing it
**Answer: b) Loading or pulling the sample until it fails**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A rope's most important property is…
a) Hardness  b) Strength under pulling  c) Transparency  d) Flexibility only
**Answer: b) Strength under pulling**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A material that bends without breaking is…
a) Rigid  b) Brittle  c) Flexible  d) Magnetic
**Answer: c) Flexible**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Glass is best described as…
a) Flexible and elastic  b) Rigid and brittle — it holds its shape, then snaps instead of bending  c) Soft and weak  d) Strong in pulling
**Answer: b) Rigid and brittle — it holds its shape, then snaps instead of bending**
