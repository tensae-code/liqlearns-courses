# Materials and their properties — hard, strong, flexible — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The property that resists scratches and dents is _____.
**Answer: hardness**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A sample is loaded until it fails to measure its _____.
**Answer: strength**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Bending without breaking is called _____.
**Answer: flexibility**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A rigid material that snaps instead of bending is called _____.
**Answer: brittle**
