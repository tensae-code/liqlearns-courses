Today: **Solutions — solute and solvent** — Bhutan's way: the two names every solution carries.

Stir salt into water until it vanishes, and you hold a solution: the salt spread evenly through the water, the same in every drop. That mixture has two named parts. The salt — the thing that dissolved — is the solute. The water — the thing it dissolved in — is the solvent. Solute plus solvent: a solution. Every solution you will ever meet can be taken apart into that pair.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
