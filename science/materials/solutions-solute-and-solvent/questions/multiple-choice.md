# Solutions — solute and solvent — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A solution is…
a) A substance settled at the bottom of a liquid  b) One substance dissolved evenly in another, the same in every drop  c) Any two things mixed anyhow  d) A liquid that can never hold anything
**Answer: b) One substance dissolved evenly in another, the same in every drop**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In sugar water, the solute is…
a) The water  b) The glass  c) The sugar  d) The spoon
**Answer: c) The sugar**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The solvent is…
a) The substance that dissolves  b) The liquid the solute dissolves in  c) The solid left at the bottom  d) The taste of the solution
**Answer: b) The liquid the solute dissolves in**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is NOT a solution?
a) Salt water  b) Sweet tea  c) Sand stirred in water, with sand at the bottom  d) Sugar dissolved in water
**Answer: c) Sand stirred in water, with sand at the bottom**
