# Solutions — solute and solvent — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The substance that dissolves is called the _____.
**Answer: solute**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The liquid the solute dissolves in is called the _____.
**Answer: solvent**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Solute + solvent = a _____.
**Answer: solution**
