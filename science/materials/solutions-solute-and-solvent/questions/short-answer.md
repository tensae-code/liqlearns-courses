# Solutions — solute and solvent — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the solute and the solvent in a glass of salt water, and say how you tell them apart.
**Answer: The salt is the solute — the substance that dissolved; the water is the solvent — the liquid it dissolved in. The solute is the one that vanishes into the other; the solvent is the one it vanishes into.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What makes a solution different from a mixture with sand in water?
**Answer: A solution is even — the dissolved substance is spread through the liquid in pieces too small to see and nothing settles out; sand in water stays as grains that sink and can be filtered back out.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Can a liquid other than water be a solvent? Explain with the lesson's rule.
**Answer: Yes — whichever liquid does the dissolving is the solvent of that solution; water is only the commonest solvent, not the only one.**
