# Hands-on — Using water — state changes in daily life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Find three places at home where water changes its state in one day — ice melting, water boiling, vapour settling on a cold surface. For each one, write the change (from what state to what state) and the benefit: what the change does for the people in the house.
