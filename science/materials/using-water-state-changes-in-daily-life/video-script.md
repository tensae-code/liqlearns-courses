Today: **Using water — state changes in daily life** — Yemen's way, from its book (Yemen, Grade 3 — *Science, Grade 3, Part 1*, printed p. 84).

Yemen's Grade 3 Science book teaches matter inside its own unit order: matter has varied properties, the states of matter change — and then the book turns to water by itself. Water is the state-changing material the child meets every day: it runs as a liquid, hardens into ice, and rises as vapour.
The lesson's own frame is benefit: the changes of water's states are not only changes to name — each one is used in daily life. Ice is water's solid state put to work, boiling is water driven to its vapour-making change, and vapour settling back to water is a change the child can watch. Yemen's own way is to teach the states through what they do for us, in the child's own day.
