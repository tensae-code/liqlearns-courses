## Sub lesson 1 — A creator in science: Dalton

Iraq's Grade 6 book builds matter lesson by lesson — the atom, elements and compounds, chemical reactions — and then stops for a person, in a piece of **scientific writing: creators in science — Dalton**.

Dalton stands in the book where the atomic idea belongs: after the atom chapter itself. The placing is the teaching — the ideas in the chapters have makers. The atom the pupil has just studied is not ownerless knowledge; it is an idea a named creator in science put forward, and the book writes him into the science at exactly that place.
