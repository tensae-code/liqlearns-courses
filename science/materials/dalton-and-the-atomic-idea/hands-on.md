# Hands-on — Dalton and the atomic idea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A creator card
Make a creator card: DALTON — creator in science — placed after the atom chapters. On the back, write the chapter ideas his name stands beside: the atom, elements and compounds.

## Ideas have makers
Pick one idea from your science book. Write who could be written into the book for it, and where the piece would stand — the Dalton placing, practised.
