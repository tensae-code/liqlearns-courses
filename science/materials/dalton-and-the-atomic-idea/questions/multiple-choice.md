# Dalton and the atomic idea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Dalton is presented as…
a) A king  b) A creator in science  c) A kind of atom  d) A safety rule
**Answer: b) A creator in science**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) His piece is a piece of…
a) Scientific writing  b) Poetry  c) A contents page  d) A test
**Answer: a) Scientific writing**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) It stands…
a) Before all chapters  b) After the atom chapters  c) Inside the contents  d) In another book
**Answer: b) After the atom chapters**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The placing teaches that the chapters' ideas…
a) Have no makers  b) Have makers — named creators in science  c) Are guesses  d) Are finished forever
**Answer: b) Have makers — named creators in science**
