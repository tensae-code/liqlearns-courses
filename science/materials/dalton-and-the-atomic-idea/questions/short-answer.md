# Dalton and the atomic idea — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does Dalton's piece stand after the atom chapters?
**Answer: Because that is where his idea was taught: the book writes the maker in at the place of the idea — the atom the pupil has just studied.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What form does the book use for the piece?
**Answer: Scientific writing, under the heading 'creators in science'.**
