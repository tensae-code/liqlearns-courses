# Hands-on — Traditional Chinese joinery — mortise and tenon

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## See the fit
Look at wooden furniture or a wooden box at home. Find where one piece fits into another, and say which part is the tongue and which is the hole.

## Make a paper joint
Cut a tongue in one strip of stiff card and a matching slot in another. Push them together and lift gently by one strip: say what holds them, and what would make the fit stronger.
