Today: **Traditional Chinese joinery — mortise and tenon** — China's way, from its 科学 (Science) textbooks.

Two pieces of wood, joined with no nails at all. One is cut with a tongue — the tenon. The other is cut with a hole — the mortise. Push the tongue into the hole, and they hold.
That is the joinery of old Chinese buildings and bridges — strength from shape and fit, standing for centuries.
