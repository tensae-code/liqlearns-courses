# Traditional Chinese joinery — mortise and tenon — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** A tongue of wood that sticks out is a _____.
**Answer: tenon**

**2.** The hole a tenon fits into is a _____.
**Answer: mortise**

**3.** The joint holds by _____ alone.
**Answer: fit**

**4.** Old Chinese buildings and _____ were built with this joinery.
**Answer: bridges**
