# Traditional Chinese joinery — mortise and tenon — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A tenon is…
a) A tongue of wood that sticks out  b) A hole cut in wood  c) A kind of nail  d) A metal bracket
**Answer: a) A tongue of wood that sticks out**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A mortise is…
a) A wooden tongue  b) A hole or slot cut to match a tenon  c) A screw  d) A coat of glue
**Answer: b) A hole or slot cut to match a tenon**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A mortise-and-tenon joint holds by…
a) Nails  b) Screws  c) The fit of its two parts  d) Glue alone
**Answer: c) The fit of its two parts**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The joint needs no…
a) Wood  b) Cutting  c) Measuring  d) Nails, screws or glue
**Answer: d) Nails, screws or glue**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) China's book meets this joinery in…
a) Old Chinese buildings and bridges  b) Steel towers  c) Plastic toys  d) Ships' sails
**Answer: a) Old Chinese buildings and bridges**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Structures built with this joinery…
a) Fall within a year  b) Still stand centuries later  c) Cannot be repaired  d) Are made of iron
**Answer: b) Still stand centuries later**
