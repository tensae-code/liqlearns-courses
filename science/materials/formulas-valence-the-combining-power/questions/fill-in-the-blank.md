# Formulas — valence, the combining power — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or formula.

**1.** The combining power of an atom is called its ___ . (valence)
**Answer: valence**

**2.** Calcium and magnesium both have valence ___ . (2)
**Answer: 2**

**3.** In FeCl₂ iron uses valence ___ , in FeCl₃ valence 3. (2)
**Answer: 2**
