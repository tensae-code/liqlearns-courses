# Formulas — valence, the combining power — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The valence of oxygen in water is —
a) 1  b) 2  c) 3
**Answer: b) 2** — — one oxygen combines with two hydrogens.

**2.** Valence means —
a) the mass of an atom  b) an atom's combining power  c) the number of atoms in a molecule
**Answer: b) an atom's combining power**

**3.** In FeCl₃, iron's valence is —
a) 2  b) 3  c) 1
**Answer: b) 3**

**4.** Which group is all valence 2?
a) Na, K, H  b) Ca, Mg, O  c) Al, N, P
**Answer: b) Ca, Mg, O**

**5.** Copper in Cu₂O has valence —
a) 1  b) 2  c) 3
**Answer: a) 1** — — but in CuO it is 2; copper varies.
