# Hands-on — Formulas — valence, the combining power

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Hand-count valences
Hold up fingers for partners: one finger for Na, two for Mg, three for Al. A partner names an element from the valence groups; you show its number before speaking.

## Valence detective
Given NaCl, H₂O, MgCl₂, AlCl₃, FeCl₂, FeCl₃ — write each first element's valence and how you know. Circle the element that changes its answer.
