# Hands-on — Chemical change: burning and gas-making

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The balloon experiment
Lemon juice in a plastic bottle, yeast in a balloon over the neck — the book's Experiment 3, done at home with an adult's help. Watch, wait, draw the balloon as it rises.

## Describe–predict–compare
The scientist's three steps from Experiment 2, practised on paper (the describing) with the burn done only by an adult, or watched in the video: describe first, predict, then compare the ash.
