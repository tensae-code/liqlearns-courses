# Chemical change: burning and gas-making — fill in the blank

Fill in each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Burning, rusting and ___ forming are signs of chemical change.**
**Answer: gas**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The balloon is stretched over the neck of a plastic ___.**
**Answer: bottle**
**3. Liquid → gas is ______ (ፈሳሽ→ጋዝ).**
**Answer: evaporation**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The fizz when vinegar meets chalk is a ______ forming.
**Answer: gas**
