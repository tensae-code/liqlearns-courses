# Chemical change: burning and gas-making — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does Experiment 2 prove a chemical change?**
**Answer: the ash is compared with the paper's description and is a new body — it cannot be made back into paper**
**2. Name three of the seven signs of a chemical change.**
**Answer: Any three of: gas forms, smell forms, colour changes, sound is made, temperature changes, light is made, a precipitate forms.**
