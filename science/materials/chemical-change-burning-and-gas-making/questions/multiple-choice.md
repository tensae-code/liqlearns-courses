# Chemical change: burning and gas-making — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A chemical change is when —**
a) a body is moved  b) a body changes into a new body  c) a body is measured
**Answer: b) a body changes into a new body**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In Experiment 2, the paper is burned held with —**
a) bare hands  b) tongs  c) a spoon
**Answer: b) tongs**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The ash after burning is —**
a) still paper  b) a new body  c) unburned
**Answer: b) a new body**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) In Experiment 3, the balloon inflates because —**
a) a gas is formed in the bottle  b) someone blows it  c) the bottle grows
**Answer: a) a gas is formed in the bottle**
**5. Which is a physical change?**
a) paper burning  b) paper tearing  c) iron rusting
**Answer: b) paper tearing**

**6. Gas → liquid is —**
a) evaporation  b) condensation  c) freezing
**Answer: b) condensation**

**7. Which is a sign of a chemical change?**
a) a precipitate forms  b) the thing gets bigger only  c) it is moved
**Answer: a) a precipitate forms**

**8. Ash can be turned back into paper —**
a) yes, by cooling  b) no, never  c) only by freezing
**Answer: b) no, never**

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** Crushed chalk is still chalk, so crushing is a —
a) chemical change  b) physical change
**Answer: b) physical change** — form only; nothing new forms.

**10. (Sub lesson 3)** Chalk fizzing in vinegar shows a chemical change because —
a) it gets wet  b) a gas forms and new substances appear  c) it changes shape
**Answer: b) a gas forms and new substances appear**

**11. (Sub lesson 3)** Which change is hard to reverse?
a) a physical change  b) a chemical change
**Answer: b) a chemical change** — Table 2.3.
