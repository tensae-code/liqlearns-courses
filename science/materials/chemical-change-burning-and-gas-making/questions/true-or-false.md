# Chemical change: burning and gas-making — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Rusting is one of the book's signs of chemical change.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The balloon in Experiment 3 is filled by a person's breath.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Lemon juice and yeast together form a gas.**
**Answer: True**

**Answer: True**

**5. Souring is a physical change.**
