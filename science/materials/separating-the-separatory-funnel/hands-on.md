# Hands-on — Separating mixtures — the separatory funnel

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The bottle rehearsal
Oil and coloured water in a clear plastic bottle with a hole-free cap: shake, settle, and practise spotting the boundary line as it sharpens.

## Boundary calling
One learner watches the draining funnel (or a picture sequence); the other calls 'close!' — swap and compare who stopped nearest the boundary.
