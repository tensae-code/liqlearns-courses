# Separating mixtures — the separatory funnel — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The separatory funnel separates liquids that —
a) mix fully  b) do not mix  c) are both solids
**Answer: b) do not mix** — they form layers.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the funnel, oil settles —
a) below the water  b) above the water
**Answer: b) above the water** — oil is the upper layer.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** After shaking, the funnel is vented to —
a) add air  b) release pressure  c) mix more
**Answer: b) release pressure**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The layer drained first through the stopcock is —
a) the upper layer  b) the lower layer
**Answer: b) the lower layer** — the water.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The stopcock is closed —
a) after everything drains  b) exactly when the boundary arrives  c) before draining
**Answer: b) exactly when the boundary arrives** — timing the close is the skill.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The funnel cannot separate —
a) oil and water  b) salt water (a solution)
**Answer: b) salt water (a solution)** — no layers, no boundary.
