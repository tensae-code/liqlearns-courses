# Separating mixtures — the separatory funnel — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The tap of the funnel is called the ______.
**Answer: stopcock**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The line between the two layers is the ______.
**Answer: boundary**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Shaking builds pressure, so the funnel is ______.
**Answer: vented**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The oil is caught ______ after the water is drained.
**Answer: separately**
