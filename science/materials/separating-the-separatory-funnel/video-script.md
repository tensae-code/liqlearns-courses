# Video script — Separating mixtures — the separatory funnel

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Oil and water in the funnel. Stopper in.
Shake. Vent the pressure. Set it down.
Two layers come back — oil on top, water below.
Open the stopcock. The water runs down into the beaker.
Watch the boundary fall… close! Exactly there.
Water in the beaker. Oil in the funnel. Parted — clean.
