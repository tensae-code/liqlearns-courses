# Equations — balancing by LCM, and modelling with circles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The balanced equation for aluminium + oxygen is —
a) Al + O₂ → Al₂O₃  b) 2Al + 3O₂ → Al₂O₃  c) 4Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃
**Answer: c) 4Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃** — — Al: 4 = 4, O: 6 = 6.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** LCM stands for —
a) least common multiple  b) largest counted mass  c) least counted molecules
**Answer: a) least common multiple**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the circles model of 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O, the left side holds —
a) 2 H and 1 O  b) 4 H and 2 O  c) 4 H and 1 O
**Answer: b) 4 H and 2 O**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** When the circles do not balance, you may —
a) change a subscript  b) remove a circle  c) add a whole molecule group (a coefficient)
**Answer: c) add a whole molecule group (a coefficient)**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The LCM for the aluminium–oxygen totals in this lesson is —
a) 6  b) 12  c) 24
**Answer: b) 12** — — as the book works it: divide 12 back to get the coefficients.
