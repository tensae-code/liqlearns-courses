# Equations — balancing by LCM, and modelling with circles — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 4Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃ has 6 oxygen atoms on each side.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the circles model, a subscript may be changed to make the sides match.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The LCM method ends with checking both sides.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Circles rearranging shows atoms are never lost in a reaction.
