# Hands-on — Equations — balancing by LCM, and modelling with circles

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Circles on the table
With two colours of bottle tops, build H₂ pairs and an O₂ pair; rearrange them into water molecules. If atoms are left over or missing, add whole pairs (coefficients) until both sides of your table-arrow match. Sketch the before and after.

## LCM on paper
Work Al + O₂ → Al₂O₃ by the LCM steps in the lesson, writing the divide-back line (12 ÷ 3 = 4, 12 ÷ 4 = 3, 12 ÷ 6 = 2 in the book's working) and the final tally beneath the finished equation.
