# Video script — Properties that identify a thing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Everything has **properties** — colour, length, hardness, shape.
Properties are a thing's identity card: they tell it from every other thing.
**Physical**: you can measure it, the thing stays itself. **Chemical**: you see it when a new thing is made.
