# Properties that identify a thing — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: each sub lesson is proved before the next opens, and practice inside a sub lesson deepens — more items, harder corners — before the learner moves on.

## Sub lesson 1 — Physical properties — the pencil's identity card
Pass mark: the learner can the Grade 4 book's worked example: one pencil, its properties listed — proved by this sub lesson's own questions (the gate pool is the whole set; no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

## Sub lesson 2 — Chemical properties — when a thing becomes a new thing (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can the guides' contrast: properties seen only when a material changes into a new material — proved by this sub lesson's own questions (the gate pool is the whole set; no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

Grade 5 depth (added 2026-11-12): the pass mark grows — the learner also separates physical from chemical properties by the book's test, names solute/solvent/solution from Experiment 3.1, and states alcohol's boiling point (78 °C) beside water's.
