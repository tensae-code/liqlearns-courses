# Properties that identify a thing — fill in the blank

Fill in each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The properties of a thing are how we ___ it from other things.**
**Answer: tell**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Properties seen without changing the thing are ___ properties.**
**Answer: physical**
**3. Solute + solvent = ______.**
**Answer: solution**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ is a chemical property: how a substance burns.
**Answer: Flammability**
