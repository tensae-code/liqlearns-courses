# Hands-on — Properties that identify a thing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The pencil, fully described
Take the book's worked example yourself: one pencil, and list every property you can find — aim for the book's eight. Measure, look, feel, compare.

## Mystery thing
Hide a small thing in a cloth bag. A companion feels it and lists properties — hard or soft, long or short, smooth or rough — then guesses. Properties did the identifying.
