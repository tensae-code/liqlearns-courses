# Compound machines — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is a compound machine?
**Answer:** A compound machine is two or more simple machines joined together so they work as one tool or device, each simple machine keeping its own job.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) How can you test whether a tool is a simple or a compound machine?
**Answer:** Count the simple machines inside it: only one means it is simple; two or more joined means it is compound.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the simple machines joined in a pair of scissors and the job of each.
**Answer:** Two levers — the handles and blades turning on the pivot — joined to two wedges, the sharp blade edges that cut.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Name three simple machines you can find in a bicycle.
**Answer:** Wheel and axle at each wheel, levers at the pedals and brakes, and the chain drive carrying the turning from the pedals to the back wheel (any three of these, with their places).
