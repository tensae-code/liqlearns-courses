# Compound machines — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A compound machine joins _____ or more simple machines.
**Answer: two**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Each simple machine inside a compound machine keeps its own _____.
**Answer: job**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The handles and blades of scissors turn as _____.
**Answer: levers**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The sharp edges of scissors' blades are _____.
**Answer: wedges**
