# Compound machines — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A compound machine is…
a) One simple machine  b) Two or more simple machines joined together  c) A machine with no moving parts  d) The biggest simple machine
**Answer: b) Two or more simple machines joined together**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Inside a compound machine, each simple machine…
a) Stops working  b) Keeps its own job  c) Becomes a new kind of machine  d) Works only at night
**Answer: b) Keeps its own job**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The test for a compound machine is…
a) Its colour  b) Its weight  c) Counting two or more simple machines joined in it  d) Its price
**Answer: c) Counting two or more simple machines joined in it**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Scissors are a compound machine because they join…
a) Levers and wedges  b) Screws and pulleys  c) Wheels and planes  d) Ropes and chains
**Answer: a) Levers and wedges**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) On a bicycle, the pedals work as…
a) Wedges  b) Levers  c) Screws  d) Inclined planes
**Answer: b) Levers**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) A compound machine is…
a) A seventh simple machine  b) Not a simple machine at all — simple machines, joined  c) A machine that needs electricity  d) A machine with one part
**Answer: b) Not a simple machine at all — simple machines, joined**
