Today: **Compound machines** — Afghanistan's way: machines, then simple machines, then the joined ones.

A simple machine works on its own: a lever, a wedge, a wheel and axle. Join two or more simple machines together so they work as one tool, and you have a compound machine.
Look at scissors: two levers, joined at a pivot, ending in two wedges that cut. Look at a bicycle: wheels on axles, lever pedals, and a chain carrying the turning from one wheel of teeth to another. Nothing new was invented inside them — the simple machines are still there, working together.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
