# Hands-on — Compound machines

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pull the scissors apart in words
With an adult, look at a pair of scissors without cutting anything. Find the two levers (the handles and blades turning on the pivot) and the two wedges (the sharp blade edges). Write each simple machine you find and the job it does inside the whole tool.

## Bicycle parts hunt
If a bicycle is near, stand beside it (do not ride it for this task). List what you can find: wheel and axle at each wheel, levers at the pedals and brake handles, and the chain drive carrying turning from the pedals to the back wheel. Say out loud which parts working together make the bicycle a compound machine.

## Design a joining
Draw a tool of your own that joins exactly two simple machines. Label each simple machine and write one sentence on what the joined tool can do that neither part does as well alone.
