# Soap and detergents on water

## Do it first
Before you read: say, in one sentence, what you think this lesson's one thing is, from its title alone — **Soap and detergents on water**. Then read on: this is Fiji's way in — its book teaches this thing at the place cited in the sources, in the order below.

## Sub lesson 1 — Soap and detergents meet water

Fiji's Year 9 Basic Science book tests what soap and detergents **do to water** — and what water does to them. Soap and detergent are not dropped into water as an afterthought: the book makes their meeting with water the investigation itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — Different waters, different effects

The book's question is a comparison: the **same soap or detergent** is tried on **different water types**, and the effect is not the same in each. Water is not one single thing in this lesson — the kind of water decides what the soap can do in it.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Fiji island context

Fiji teaches this as an island country: different waters are close at hand — the water of home, of wells and of the sea around the islands — so the effect of soap and detergents on each kind of water is a question Fiji learners can test with the waters they actually use.
