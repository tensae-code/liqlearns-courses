# Hands-on — Soap and detergents on water

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Take the same small piece of soap and the same amount of water from two different sources your home uses. Wash or shake each the same way and compare: which water lets the soap work better, and what do you see in each? Record the two waters and the difference, as Fiji's book compares them.
