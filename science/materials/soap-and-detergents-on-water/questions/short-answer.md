# Soap and detergents on water — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Soap and detergents meet water.
**Answer:** Fiji's Year 9 Basic Science book tests what soap and detergents do to water — and what water does to them.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Different waters, different effects.
**Answer:** The book's question is a comparison: the same soap or detergent is tried on different water types, and the effect is not the same in each.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: The Fiji island context.
**Answer:** Fiji teaches this as an island country: different waters are close at hand — the water of home, of wells and of the sea around the islands — so the effect of soap and detergents on each kind of water is a question Fiji learners can test with the waters they actually use..

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Soap and detergents meet water → Different waters, different effects → The Fiji island context — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
