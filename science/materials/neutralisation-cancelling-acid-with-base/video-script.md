# Video script — Neutralisation — cancelling acid with base

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Acid meets base — and both disappear.

What is left? A salt, and water. That is neutralisation.

NaOH plus HCl gives NaCl plus H2O. Count the atoms: one water, not two.

On the farm, lime lifts acid soil toward the pH the crop wants.

At home, acidic conditioner cancels basic shampoo. Same reaction, smaller stage.
