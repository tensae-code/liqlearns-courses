# Hands-on — Neutralisation — cancelling acid with base

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Cancel test
Put plant indicator in vinegar (acid colour). Add ash water a spoon at a time, watching the colour walk toward neutral. Stop at neutral and write what happened in the word equation's words.

## Crop decision
Soil A tests pH 5.0, Soil B pH 6.8. Using the crop table (potatoes 5.5–6.5), say which soil needs liming for potatoes, which material you would use, and what you are doing chemically.
