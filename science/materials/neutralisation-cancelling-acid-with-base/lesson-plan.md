# Lesson plan — Neutralisation — cancelling acid with base

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Materials · **Lesson:** Neutralisation — cancelling acid with base
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Classification of Compounds (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** acid + base → salt + water: neutralisation, put to work treating wastewater, farming acid soils back toward the pH a crop needs (ash, limestone or quicklime), and at home — where acidic conditioner cancels basic shampoo (teacher guide)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Working with acids and bases — properties, uses, precautions (same unit): the substances handled there cancel each other here. The primary lesson Oxides, acids, bases and salts states neutralisation at first depth; this is the Grade 8 applications lesson.
