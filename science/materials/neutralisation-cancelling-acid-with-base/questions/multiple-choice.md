# Neutralisation — cancelling acid with base — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Neutralisation gives —
a) salt + hydrogen  b) salt + water  c) acid + base
**Answer: b) salt + water**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The balanced equation NaOH + HCl → NaCl + H₂O has how many water molecules on the right?
a) two  b) one  c) none
**Answer: b) one**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An acid soil is lifted toward neutral by adding —
a) vinegar  b) limestone, ash or quicklime  c) more acid
**Answer: b) limestone, ash or quicklime**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Potatoes grow best at about pH —
a) 3.0–3.5  b) 5.5–6.5  c) 9.0–10.0
**Answer: b) 5.5–6.5**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the guide's home example, conditioner is —
a) basic, cancelling acidic shampoo  b) acidic, cancelling basic shampoo  c) a salt
**Answer: b) acidic, cancelling basic shampoo**
