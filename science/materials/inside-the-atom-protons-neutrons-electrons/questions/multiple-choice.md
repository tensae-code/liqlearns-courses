# Inside the atom — protons, neutrons, electrons — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The particles in the nucleus of an atom are —
a) protons and electrons  b) protons and neutrons  c) neutrons and electrons
**Answer: b) protons and neutrons**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** One atomic mass unit (amu) is —
a) the mass of a hydrogen atom  b) 1/12 of the mass of a carbon-12 atom  c) the mass of an electron
**Answer: b) 1/12 of the mass of a carbon-12 atom**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An atom has Z = 8 and A = 16. Its neutrons number —
a) 8  b) 16  c) 24
**Answer: a) 8** — 16 − 8 = 8

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The atomic number Z counts the —
a) neutrons  b) protons  c) protons plus neutrons
**Answer: b) protons**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Atoms are imaged with —
a) a magnifying glass  b) the naked eye  c) modern microscopes — TEM, AFM, STM
**Answer: c) modern microscopes — TEM, AFM, STM**
