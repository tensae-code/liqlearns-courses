# Inside the atom — protons, neutrons, electrons — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The centre of the atom, holding the protons and neutrons, is the ___ .
**Answer: nucleus**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mass number A minus atomic number Z gives the number of ___ .
**Answer: neutrons**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The scanning tunnelling microscope is shortened to ___ .
**Answer: STM**
