# Hands-on — Inside the atom — protons, neutrons, electrons

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Lentils and peas — build an atom
The book's home model: lentils and peas stand for the particles. Cluster lentils (protons) and peas (neutrons) tightly in the middle for a nucleus — build carbon: 6 lentils, 6 peas. Then place 6 small seeds in a wide ring around it for the electrons. Count aloud: Z is the lentils, A is lentils plus peas. Now rebuild it as oxygen (8, 8, 8) and as neon (10, 10, 10) — read the counts off the lesson's table first, then check yourself against it.

## The weight trick
Hold one lentil. The electron beside it would be about 1,836 times lighter — too light for any kitchen scale to notice next to the lentil. That is why the mass number counts only the nucleus. Say that reason aloud while you hold the lentil.
