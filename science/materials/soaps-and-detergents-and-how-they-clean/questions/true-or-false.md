# Soaps and detergents — and how they clean — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Soaps come as hard bars for washing bodies and clothes and as softer forms for dishes and floors; detergents are the made cleaners sold as powders and liquids, built to work even in hard water where soap struggles.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Why water alone is not enough is the opposite of what this lesson teaches.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches this as stated.**

**3.** Rubbed on with water, soap surrounds the grease in tiny balls, loosens its hold on the skin, cloth or dish, and the rinse water carries the balls away.
**Answer: True**

**4.** This lesson's topic plays no part in science at all.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
