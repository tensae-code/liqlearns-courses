# Soaps and detergents — and how they clean — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (soap and detergents clean because each of their particles holds water with one end and grease with the other — they lift dirt and grease off a surface and let the water carry it away.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
