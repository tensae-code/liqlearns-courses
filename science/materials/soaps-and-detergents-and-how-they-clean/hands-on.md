# Hands-on — Soaps and detergents — and how they clean

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Put a drop of cooking oil on a plate and try to wash it off with water alone — watch the water slide off. Then wash with soap and water and feel the difference. Write what the soap did that the water could not.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of soaps and detergents — and how they clean — soap and detergents clean because each of their particles holds water with one end and grease with the other — they lift dirt and grease off a surface and let the water carry it away. — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
