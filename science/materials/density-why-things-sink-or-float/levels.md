# Density: why things sink or float — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: say density is the amount of matter packed into a body's volume, and use it to explain the sinking iron ball and the floating sponge.
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to sort or name, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.

Grade 5 depth (added 2026-11-12): the pass mark grows — the learner also computes with density = mass ÷ volume, states water's density both ways, and explains pressure = force ÷ area with the pen and hoof examples.
