# Video script — Density: why things sink or float

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An iron ball **sinks**. A sponge **floats**. Same water!
**Density** is the matter packed into a body's space.
Packed tight — it sinks. Packed loose — it floats.
