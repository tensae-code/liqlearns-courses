# Lesson plan — Density: why things sink or float

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Materials · **Lesson:** Density: why things sink or float
**Place in the curriculum:** Materials — inside the properties study: density, the property that decides sinking and floating (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** density is the amount of matter packed into a body's volume — and it decides whether a thing sinks or floats: the iron ball sinks, the sponge floats
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Properties that identify a thing — density is one property, the one that answers to water.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks heavy things sink and light things float, whatever their size → the book's word is packing: matter packed into the volume. A big light-packed thing floats; a small dense one sinks.
- Thinks the sponge floats because it is 'used to water' → its volume holds mostly air, so little matter is packed in.
