# Density: why things sink or float — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. A dense body sinks in water in the book's test.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Two bodies of the same size always behave the same way in water.**
**Answer: False**
**3. Oil floats on water because it is less dense.**
**Answer: True**

**4. A tractor's wide tyres increase the pressure on soft ground.**
**Answer: False**
