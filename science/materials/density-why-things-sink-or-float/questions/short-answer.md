# Density: why things sink or float — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. Why did the iron ball sink and the sponge float?**
**Answer: the ball packs much matter into its volume (dense); the sponge packs very little (not dense)**
**2. Why does the pen's sharp end hurt the palm more?**
**Answer: The same force lands on a smaller area, so the pressure is greater.**
