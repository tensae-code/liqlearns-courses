# Density: why things sink or float — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Density is —**
a) the colour of a body  b) the amount of matter packed into a body's volume  c) the size of a body
**Answer: b) the amount of matter packed into a body's volume**

**2. In the book's test, the iron ball —**
a) floated  b) sank  c) melted
**Answer: b) sank**

**3. The sponge floated because —**
a) it is magic  b) very little matter is packed into its volume  c) it is bigger than the bucket
**Answer: b) very little matter is packed into its volume**

**4. Density helps tell —**
a) what a body is made of  b) what time it is  c) who owns a body
**Answer: a) what a body is made of**
