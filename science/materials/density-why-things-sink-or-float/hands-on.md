# Hands-on — Density: why things sink or float

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The bucket test
The book's own pair first — iron ball and sponge — then your own five things. A drawn table: THING / SANK / FLOATED.

## Packing in the hand
Hold a stone in one hand and a same-sized piece of dry wood or sponge in the other. The heavier-for-its-size one is the denser one — feel density before you name it.
