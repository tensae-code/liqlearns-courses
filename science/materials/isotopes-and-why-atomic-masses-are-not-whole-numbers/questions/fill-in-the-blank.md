# Isotopes — and why atomic masses are not whole numbers — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hydrogen's isotope with two neutrons is called ___ .
**Answer: tritium**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An average atomic mass weights each isotope's mass by how ___ it is.
**Answer: common**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's worked average atomic mass for carbon is ___ amu.
**Answer: 12.01**
