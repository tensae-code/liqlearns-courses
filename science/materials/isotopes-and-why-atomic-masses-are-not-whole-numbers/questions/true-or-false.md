# Isotopes — and why atomic masses are not whole numbers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Protium has no neutrons.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Two atoms with different Z can still be isotopes of one element.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Carbon's average atomic mass is 12.01 amu.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Every magnesium atom weighs 24.31 amu.
**Answer: False**
