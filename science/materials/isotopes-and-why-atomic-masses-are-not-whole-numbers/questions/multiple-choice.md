# Isotopes — and why atomic masses are not whole numbers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Isotopes of an element have the same —
a) number of neutrons  b) atomic number Z  c) mass number A
**Answer: b) atomic number Z**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Deuterium is hydrogen with —
a) 0 neutrons  b) 1 neutron  c) 2 neutrons
**Answer: b) 1 neutron**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Tritium's mass number A is —
a) 1  b) 2  c) 3
**Answer: c) 3**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The average atomic mass of magnesium, as worked in the book, is —
a) 24.31 amu  b) exactly 25 amu  c) 26.00 amu
**Answer: a) 24.31 amu**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Atomic masses are not whole numbers because they are —
a) measured badly  b) weighted averages over an element's isotopes  c) rounded up
**Answer: b) weighted averages over an element's isotopes**
