# Video script — Formulas — polyatomic ions and their compounds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Described slot: a short telling of this lesson — polyatomic ions are charged groups of atoms with their own formulas and valences; their compounds criss-cross like binary ones, with brackets when a group repeats — with the learner doing the lesson's Do It Yourself steps along with it.
