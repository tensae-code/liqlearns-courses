# Hands-on — Formulas — polyatomic ions and their compounds

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The ion card game (100-card game, home version)
Make cards: metal cards (Na, K, Ca, Mg, Al, Cu, Fe, NH₄) and ion cards (OH, NO₃, HCO₃, CO₃, SO₄, PO₄), each marked with its valence. Draw one of each, build the neutral compound by criss-cross, name it, score a point. First to ten wins.

## Bracket practice
Write Ca(OH)₂, Al₂(SO₄)₃ and Fe₂(SO₄)₃ slowly, saying for each: which group repeats, and why the brackets are there.
