# Formulas — polyatomic ions and their compounds — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or formula.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ion PO₄³⁻ is called ___ . (phosphate)
**Answer: phosphate**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Potassium nitrate is written ___ . (KNO₃)
**Answer: KNO₃**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** CuCO₃ is named copper(___) carbonate. (II)
**Answer: II**
