# Formulas — polyatomic ions and their compounds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The formula of sodium carbonate is —
a) NaCO₃  b) Na₂CO₃  c) Na(CO₃)₂
**Answer: b) Na₂CO₃** — — sodium 1 crosses with carbonate 2.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The valence of the sulfate ion SO₄²⁻ is —
a) 1  b) 2  c) 3
**Answer: b) 2**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Aluminium sulfate is written —
a) Al₂(SO₄)₃  b) AlSO₄  c) Al₂SO₄₃
**Answer: a) Al₂(SO₄)₃** — — brackets hold the repeating group.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** KNO₃ is named —
a) potassium nitrate  b) potassium nitrogen oxide  c) potassium nitrite
**Answer: a) potassium nitrate**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Fe₂(SO₄)₃ is named —
a) iron(II) sulfate  b) iron(III) sulfate  c) iron sulfate
**Answer: b) iron(III) sulfate** — — three sulfates of valence 2 balance two irons of valence 3.
