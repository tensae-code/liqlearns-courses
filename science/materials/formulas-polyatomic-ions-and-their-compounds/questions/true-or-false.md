# Formulas — polyatomic ions and their compounds — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ammonium ion NH₄⁺ has a positive charge.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Brackets are used when a polyatomic ion appears more than once.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Hydroxide OH⁻ has valence 2.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** NaOH is sodium hydroxide.
**Answer: True**
