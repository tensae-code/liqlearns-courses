# Air can be squeezed — compressing air and its spring back — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Squeezing air into a smaller space is called _____ it.
**Answer: compressing**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Crowded air presses harder: its _____ grows as its volume shrinks.
**Answer: pressure**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Air always springs back to its first volume, so air is _____.
**Answer: elastic**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Unlike air, _____ cannot be squeezed by hand — its particles are already close.
**Answer: water**
