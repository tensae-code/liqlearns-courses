# Air can be squeezed — compressing air and its spring back — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Air can be squeezed into a smaller space because…
a) Air disappears when pushed  b) Air's particles are far apart, and squeezing closes the spaces between them  c) Air turns into water  d) The syringe stretches
**Answer: b) Air's particles are far apart, and squeezing closes the spaces between them**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The further you push the plunger, the air…
a) Pushes back less  b) Pushes back harder — its pressure grows as it is crowded  c) Stops pushing  d) Gets colder always
**Answer: b) Pushes back harder — its pressure grows as it is crowded**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) When you release the plunger, it flies back because…
a) Your finger pulls it  b) The compressed air expands, springing back to its first volume  c) The syringe is broken  d) Water enters
**Answer: b) The compressed air expands, springing back to its first volume**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A syringe full of water cannot be squeezed by hand because…
a) Water is heavier than the hand  b) Water's particles are already close-packed, with no spaces to close  c) Water is always cold  d) The seal leaks
**Answer: b) Water's particles are already close-packed, with no spaces to close**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Air is called elastic because…
a) It is made of rubber  b) It returns to its former volume when the squeezing stops  c) It stretches into threads  d) It never moves
**Answer: b) It returns to its former volume when the squeezing stops**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these works by air's spring?
a) A stone bridge  b) A bicycle pump filling a tyre hard with air  c) A wooden ruler  d) A glass window
**Answer: b) A bicycle pump filling a tyre hard with air**
