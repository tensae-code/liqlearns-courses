# Air can be squeezed — compressing air and its spring back — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is happening to air's particles as the plunger is pushed in?
**Answer: They are crowded closer together. Air's particles are naturally far apart with wide spaces between; compressing closes those spaces, and the crowded particles press harder on the plunger, walls and finger.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) How does the spring-back prove the squeezed air did not vanish?
**Answer: On release, the plunger is driven all the way back to its start — the air expands to fill exactly its old volume. Vanished air could not push anything back; all of it was there, compressed.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why can air carry weight in a cushion or tyre if it is 'only air'?
**Answer: Compressed air pushes back with real pressure on every side. Sealed in a tyre or cushion, that push-back supports the load — softly, because the air can still give a little, spring-fashion.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Name two tools or machines that use compressed air's push, and say what the push does in each.
**Answer: Any two: pump/tyre (stored pressure holds the tyre hard and carries the bicycle's weight); spray bottle (compressed air drives the liquid out as spray); lorry/train air brakes (compressed air's pressure works the brakes); air cushion/mattress (the spring carries weight softly).**
