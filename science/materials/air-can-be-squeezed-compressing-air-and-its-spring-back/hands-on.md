# Hands-on — Air can be squeezed — compressing air and its spring back

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The finger-sealed syringe
Air in, tip sealed by finger: push in slowly noting the growing push-back, release and watch the return. Then water: push and feel the refusal. Say aloud: same syringe, same push — the difference is in the stuff inside.

## Cushion of air
Blow a plastic bag full of air, tie it shut, and press a book down on it; feel the bag resist and spring back when the book lifts. Say aloud: this soft spring is why air cushions and tyres can carry weight.
