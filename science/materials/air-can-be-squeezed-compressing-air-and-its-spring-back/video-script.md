# Video script — Air can be squeezed — compressing air and its spring back

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Air can be squeezed**.

Seal a syringe full of air with your finger and push. The plunger moves — the air squeezes into a smaller space, pushing back harder the further you go. Let go: whoosh — the plunger springs back to its mark. Air is elastic: crowded particles, wide-spaced by nature, always insist on their room again. Try water and nothing moves at all — its particles already sit close. That one difference fills our tyres, softens our cushions, drives our sprays and stops great lorries: air can be squeezed, and air springs back.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
