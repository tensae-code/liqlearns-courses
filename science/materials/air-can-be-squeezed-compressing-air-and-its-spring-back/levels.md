# Air can be squeezed — compressing air and its spring back — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner compresses air in a sealed syringe (feeling the growing push-back), releases it and observes the elastic spring-back, contrasts it with water's refusal to compress, explains both by particle spacing, and names working uses of air's spring — pumps/tyres, cushions, sprayers, air brakes — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 2 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — doing the lesson's own experiments and sorts by hand, naming each idea aloud, and reasoning about new examples until the teaching is the learner's own.
