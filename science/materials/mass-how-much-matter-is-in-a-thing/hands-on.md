# Hands-on — Mass — how much matter is in a thing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Same size, different mass
Find pairs of things the same size but different mass — a full and an empty bottle, a wooden and a metal spoon. Hold one in each hand and order them by mass before weighing. Then check with a kitchen scale or balance and record both masses in grams. Say what made the masses differ when the sizes matched.
