# Mass — how much matter is in a thing — multiple choice


**1.** Mass is…
a) How big a thing looks  b) The amount of matter in a thing  c) How fast it falls  d) Its colour
**Answer: b) The amount of matter in a thing**

**2.** Mass is measured in…
a) Litres  b) Grams and kilograms  c) Metres  d) Seconds
**Answer: b) Grams and kilograms**

**3.** The instrument for mass is the…
a) Ruler  b) Thermometer  c) Balance  d) Clock
**Answer: c) Balance**

**4.** A small stone can have more mass than a big balloon because…
a) Balloons are light colours  b) The stone packs more matter into less space  c) Stones are older  d) Air has no matter
**Answer: b) The stone packs more matter into less space**

**5.** A full bottle and an empty bottle have the same size but different…
a) Shape  b) Mass  c) Colour  d) Name
**Answer: b) Mass**

**6.** Weight differs from mass because weight is…
a) Gravity's pull on the mass  b) The same thing  c) Measured in grams  d) Inside the thing
**Answer: a) Gravity's pull on the mass**
