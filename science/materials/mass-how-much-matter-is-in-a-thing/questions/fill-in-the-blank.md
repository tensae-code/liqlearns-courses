# Mass — how much matter is in a thing — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Mass is the amount of _____ in a thing.
**Answer: matter**

**2.** Mass is measured in grams and _____.
**Answer: kilograms**

**3.** We measure mass with a _____.
**Answer: balance**

**4.** Weight is gravity's _____ on the mass.
**Answer: pull**
