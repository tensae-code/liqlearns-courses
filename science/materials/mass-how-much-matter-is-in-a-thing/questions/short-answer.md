# Mass — how much matter is in a thing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What is mass?
**Answer: The amount of matter in a thing.**

**2.** How is mass measured?
**Answer: With a balance, in grams and kilograms, comparing the thing against known masses.**

**3.** How can a small thing have more mass than a big thing?
**Answer: By packing more matter into less space — like a small stone against a big balloon.**

**4.** Mass or weight: which stays the same wherever the thing goes?
**Answer: Mass stays the same; weight is gravity's pull and changes with the pull.**
