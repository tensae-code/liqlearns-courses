# Capillarity — water climbing narrow tubes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Water rises in narrow tubes.
**Answer:** Yemen's Grade 9 Science book shows water climbing inside narrow tubes against gravity — and the narrower the tube, the higher the water climbs.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The same climb in the plant.
**Answer:** The book carries the same climb into the living world: soil water rises through the plant's root hairs and up through the whole plant by this same narrow-tube climb — capillarity is how water travels where no pump pushes it..

**Answer:** Water rises in narrow tubes → The same climb in the plant — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
