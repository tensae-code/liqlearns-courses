## Sub lesson 1 — Water rises in narrow tubes

Yemen's Grade 9 Science book shows water climbing inside narrow tubes against gravity — and the narrower the tube, the higher the water climbs. The climb is strongest exactly where the space is tightest.

## Sub lesson 2 — The same climb in the plant

The book carries the same climb into the living world: soil water rises through the plant's root hairs and up through the whole plant by this same narrow-tube climb — capillarity is how water travels where no pump pushes it.
