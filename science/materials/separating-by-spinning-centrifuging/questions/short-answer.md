# Separating by spinning — centrifuging — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Separating by spinning (centrifuging): butter coming out of curd and blood being tested, with the theki-and-madani churn as Nepal's own local way of spinning.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science and Technology Grade 6*, printed pp. 178–180.**
