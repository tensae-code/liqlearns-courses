# Surface tension — the skin on liquids — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The stretched skin on a liquid's surface.
**Answer:** Yemen's Grade 9 Science book teaches the surface of a liquid as a stretched skin: a needle and a razor blade — both denser than water — can be floated on water, held up by that skin, though left to themselves they would sink..

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Walking on the film — and breaking it.
**Answer:** Pond insects walk on the film without breaking through it, living on the skin's strength.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: The pull, measured.
**Answer:** The skin's pull is a real force and can be measured: surface tension is counted in newtons per metre — the pull along each metre of the surface's edge.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The stretched skin on a liquid's surface → Walking on the film — and breaking it → The pull, measured — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
