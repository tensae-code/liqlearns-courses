# Hands-on — Surface tension — the skin on liquids

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Float a dry needle or a razor blade flat on a bowl of water — lay it on a small piece of tissue first, then let the tissue sink away. Write what holds it up. Then touch the water with a soapy finger nearby and write what happens to the skin.
