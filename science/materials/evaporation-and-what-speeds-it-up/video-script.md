Today: **Evaporation — and what speeds it up** — Albania's way, from its Grade 5 guide's learning situation Avullimi.
Evaporation is water leaving a surface as vapour — no boiling, a little at a time, like a puddle disappearing.
Three things speed it up: warmth, moving air, and a wider surface. Cold, still, narrow — and it slows down.
