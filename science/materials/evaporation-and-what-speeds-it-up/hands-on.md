# Hands-on — Evaporation — and what speeds it up

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The two cloths
With adult help, wet two cloths equally. Hang one where air moves and one where it is still. Visit both every half hour and record which dries first, saying the factor you changed and the one you kept the same.

## Wide against narrow
Pour equal water into a wide plate and a narrow cup and leave both in the same place. Mark the levels, check later, and say which surface let its water leave faster.
