# Evaporation — and what speeds it up — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which sentence gives this lesson's first key idea?
a) Evaporation is a liquid turning into vapour from its surface, without boiling.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Evaporation is a liquid turning into vapour from its surface, without boiling.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson opens by asking the learner to…
a) Memorise the key ideas first  b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation  c) Skip to the questions  d) Copy the lesson out
**Answer: b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's second key idea?
a) Evaporation speeds up with warmth, with moving air, and with a wider surface open to the air.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Evaporation speeds up with warmth, with moving air, and with a wider surface open to the air.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's third key idea?
a) Something from a different lesson  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Cold, still air and a small surface slow evaporation down.  d) None of these
**Answer: c) Cold, still air and a small surface slow evaporation down.**
