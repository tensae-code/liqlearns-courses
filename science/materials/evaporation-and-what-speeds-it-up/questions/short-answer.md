# Evaporation — and what speeds it up — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Evaporation is a liquid turning into vapour from its surface, without boiling.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Evaporation speeds up with warmth, with moving air, and with a wider surface open to the air.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why did the cloth in the wind dry first?
**Answer:** Moving air carried the vapour away from its surface, so more water could leave — wind is one of evaporation's speed factors.
