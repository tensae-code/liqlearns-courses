# Hands-on — Hydrocarbons — alkanes, alkenes, alkynes

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Formula ladder
Write the first six alkanes from CₙH₂ₙ₊₂, then the matching alkenes and alkynes. Mark where each family starts (alkenes and alkynes cannot start at one carbon — say why in one sentence).

## Biogas sketch
Sketch a household biogas digester fed with dung: inlet, sealed tank, gas pipe to a stove. Label what goes in and what gas comes out.
