# Hydrocarbons — alkanes, alkenes, alkynes — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or formula.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The alkyne with two carbons, C₂H₂, is called ___.
**Answer: ethyne**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The prefix dec- means ___ carbons.
**Answer: ten (10)**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A series whose members follow one formula pattern is called a ___ series.
**Answer: homologous**
