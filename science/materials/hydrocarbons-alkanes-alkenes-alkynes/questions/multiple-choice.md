# Hydrocarbons — alkanes, alkenes, alkynes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A hydrocarbon contains —
a) carbon and oxygen only  b) carbon and hydrogen only  c) carbon, hydrogen and oxygen
**Answer: b) carbon and hydrogen only**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The general formula of the alkanes is —
a) CₙH₂ₙ  b) CₙH₂ₙ₋₂  c) CₙH₂ₙ₊₂
**Answer: c) CₙH₂ₙ₊₂**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Butane, C₄H₁₀, belongs to —
a) the alkenes  b) the alkanes  c) the alkynes
**Answer: b) the alkanes**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The prefix for three carbons is —
a) eth-  b) prop-  c) pent-
**Answer: b) prop-**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The main gas made by a dung biogas digester is —
a) ethyne  b) propane  c) methane
**Answer: c) methane**
