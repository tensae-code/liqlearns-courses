# Lesson plan — Hydrocarbons — alkanes, alkenes, alkynes

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Materials · **Lesson:** Hydrocarbons — alkanes, alkenes, alkynes
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Classification of Compounds (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** compounds of carbon and hydrogen only fall into three homologous series — alkanes CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ (single bonds), alkenes CₙH₂ₙ (one double bond), alkynes CₙH₂ₙ₋₂ (one triple bond) — named by the prefixes meth- to dec- for one to ten carbons
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Organic and inorganic compounds (same unit): hydrocarbons are the first big organic family. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly who already knows what a compound is.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Three families, three formulas** — A **hydrocarbon** contains **carbon and hydrogen only**.
2. **Naming by counting carbons — and what hydrocarbons are for** — The name's first part counts the carbons: **meth- 1, eth- 2, prop- 3, but- 4, pent- 5, hex- 6, hept- 7, oct- 8, non- 9, dec- 10**; the ending names the family: **-ane, -ene, -yne**.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the general formula of each series and the bond type behind it.
2. Name and write formulas for the first members of each series using meth- to dec-.
3. Give two uses of hydrocarbons and say what gas a dung biogas digester makes.
