# Video script — Sound and vibration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Sound is energy** — made when bodies **vibrate**.
It travels through **matter** only. A vacuum stops it.
The drum's **skin** vibrates. Your **throat** vibrates.
Fastest in **solids**, then liquids, slowest in **gases**.
In cold air: **three hundred thirty-one and a half metres a second**.
