# Hands-on — Sound and vibration

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The instrument table, live
Make sound from whatever instruments the class can borrow — drum first for the worked answer — and build Experiment 5's table row by row: instrument, what vibrates, how you know.

## Throat laboratory
Hum low, hum loud, speak, whisper — hand on throat throughout. Write what the fingers feel in each case; the table is the voice's own experiment record.
