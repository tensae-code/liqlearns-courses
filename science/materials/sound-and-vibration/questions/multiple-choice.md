# Sound and vibration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Sound is energy made when bodies —**
a) rest  b) vibrate  c) freeze
**Answer: b) vibrate**

**2. Sound can travel through —**
a) a vacuum  b) matter only  c) nothing at all
**Answer: b) matter only**

**3. In the drum, what vibrates?**
a) the stick  b) the skin  c) the air only
**Answer: b) the skin**

**4. Sound travels fastest in —**
a) gases  b) liquids  c) solids
**Answer: c) solids**

**5. In air at 0 °C, sound travels about —**
a) 331.5 m/s  b) 3 m/s  c) 1000 km/s
**Answer: a) 331.5 m/s**

**6. As air warms, sound in it —**
a) slows down  b) speeds up  c) stops
**Answer: b) speeds up**
