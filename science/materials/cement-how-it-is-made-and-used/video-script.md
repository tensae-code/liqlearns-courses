Today: **Cement — how it is made and used** — Nepal's way, from its Curriculum Development Centre books.

Cement: limestone and a special clay ground to powder, mixed about 3:1 as printed, made into slurry and burned in a rotary kiln until it clinkers, then ground with about 2.5 percent gypsum to slow the setting; cement production in Nepal began at Hetauda in 1951 as the book's own note says; mortar for joining bricks and plastering, concrete, and reinforced cement concrete for roofs, pillars and bridges.
