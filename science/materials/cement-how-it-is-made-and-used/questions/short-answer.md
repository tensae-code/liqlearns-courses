# Cement — how it is made and used — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Cement: limestone and a special clay ground to powder, mixed about 3:1 as printed, made into slurry and burned in a rotary kiln until it clinkers, then ground with about 2.5 percent gypsum to slow the setting; cement production in Nepal began at Hetauda in 1951 as the book's own note says; mortar for joining bricks and plastering, concrete, and reinforced cement concrete for roofs, pillars and bridges.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science Grade 10*, printed pp. 144–145.**
