# Springs and elastic force — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** The refill of a ballpoint pen is moved by a small _____.
**Answer: spring**

**2.** A spring's pull back to its first shape is its _____ force.
**Answer: elastic**

**3.** Hang more weight and the spring _____ more.
**Answer: lengthens**

**4.** The spring's measured stretch is what the spring force _____ is built on.
**Answer: meter**
