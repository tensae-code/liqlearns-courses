# Springs and elastic force — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Inside a click ballpoint pen sits a…
a) Spring that makes the refill spring in and out  b) Magnet  c) Battery  d) Wheel
**Answer: a) Spring that makes the refill spring in and out**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A spring let go after being stretched…
a) Stays stretched  b) Returns to its own length  c) Breaks at once  d) Turns into wire
**Answer: b) Returns to its own length**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The spring's pull back toward its first shape is its…
a) Weight  b) Friction  c) Elastic force  d) Magnetism
**Answer: c) Elastic force**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Hang more weight on a spring and it…
a) Shortens  b) Does not change  c) Disappears  d) Lengthens more
**Answer: d) Lengthens more**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Take the weights off and the spring…
a) Shortens back toward its first length  b) Stays stretched  c) Grows longer still  d) Falls apart
**Answer: a) Shortens back toward its first length**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The spring force meter is built on…
a) A magnet's pull  b) A spring's stretch reading the force hung on it  c) A battery's power  d) A wheel's turning
**Answer: b) A spring's stretch reading the force hung on it**
