# Springs and elastic force — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Where does the book first meet the spring, and what does it do there?
**Answer:** Inside a ballpoint pen, taken apart in class: the spring makes the refill spring in and out at a click.

**2.** What is elastic force?
**Answer:** The pull-back of a spring (or elastic material) toward its first shape after being stretched or pressed out of shape.

**3.** What rule does hanging weights on a spring show?
**Answer:** Stretch follows load, step for step: more weight, more lengthening; weight removed, the spring shortens back.

**4.** Why does the spring meter work as a measuring instrument?
**Answer:** Because its spring's stretch is regular — the lengthening reads the force hung on it, so a scale beside the spring can be marked in force.
