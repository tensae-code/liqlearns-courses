Today: **Springs and elastic force** — China's way, from its 科学 (Science) textbooks.

Click a pen. Inside it, a tiny spring does the work — stretch it, press it, let go, and it pulls back to its own shape. That pull-back is elastic force.
Hang weights on a spring and watch: more weight, more stretch, step for step. That measured stretch is so regular we build a force meter on it.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
