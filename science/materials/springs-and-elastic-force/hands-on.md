# Hands-on — Springs and elastic force

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stretch and press
With a pen spring or a safe loose spring, stretch it gently and let go; press it shorter and let go. Say each time what the spring does when released, and what that pull-back is called.

## Weights on a spring
Hang a spring or strong elastic from a support and hang light weights on it one by one (adult help; nothing heavy). Measure and record its length after each weight, then remove them in reverse and watch it shorten back.
