# Video script — Molecules — atoms joined

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Described slot: a short telling of this lesson — atoms joining into molecules — singles, pairs and clusters — and compound molecules such as water, the smallest particle that still behaves as the substance — with the learner doing the lesson's Do It Yourself steps along with it.

ADD AUDIO slots (described only; no audio files are made in this build): a voice reads the lesson's key terms aloud, one by one, and the learner repeats each one.
