# Hands-on — Molecules — atoms joined

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Build the three types
With bottle tops: lay out one top alone (monoatomic), two tops touching (diatomic), and a cluster of four or more (polyatomic). Label each group with a scrap of paper. Now rebuild the diatomic group twice — once with two tops of the same colour (an element molecule) and once with two colours (a compound molecule) — and say aloud what makes the second one a compound.

## Water, by hand
Take two small white tops and one larger red top. Join them as the GIF joins them — the red one in the middle, the two white ones attached at an angle. Count the atoms before joining and after: 3 and 3. Nothing was created and nothing vanished — they only joined. That joined group is one water molecule, the smallest piece of water that is still water.
