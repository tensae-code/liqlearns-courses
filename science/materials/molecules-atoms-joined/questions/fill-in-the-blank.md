# Molecules — atoms joined — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Atoms join together to form ___ .
**Answer: molecules**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Water is a ___ molecule, because it joins different elements.
**Answer: compound**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Divide a water molecule and you no longer have ___ .
**Answer: water**
