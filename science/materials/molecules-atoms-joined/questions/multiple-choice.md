# Molecules — atoms joined — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A diatomic molecule holds —
a) one atom  b) two atoms  c) many atoms only
**Answer: b) two atoms**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Oxygen in the air, O₂, is a —
a) monoatomic molecule  b) diatomic molecule  c) compound molecule
**Answer: b) diatomic molecule**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A molecule made of one atom only is called —
a) monoatomic  b) diatomic  c) polyatomic
**Answer: a) monoatomic**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A compound molecule is made of —
a) atoms of one element only  b) atoms of different elements joined  c) atoms that never join
**Answer: b) atoms of different elements joined**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The smallest particle of a substance that still behaves as that substance is —
a) the atom  b) the nucleus  c) the molecule
**Answer: c) the molecule**
