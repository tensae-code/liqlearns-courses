# Molecules — atoms joined — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A polyatomic molecule holds many atoms joined together.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A monoatomic molecule holds two atoms.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A water molecule joins two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Half a water molecule still behaves as water.
**Answer: False**
