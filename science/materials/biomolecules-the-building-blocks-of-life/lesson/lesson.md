## Sub lesson 2 — The biomolecules survey

The biomolecules survey: carbohydrates (mono-, di- and polysaccharides joined by glycosidic bonds, Haworth projections, a properties laboratory), lipids (fatty acids, triglycerides, phospholipids extracted from egg yolk, terpenes), proteins (amino acids joined by peptide bonds, structure levels, denaturation watched as egg white coagulates) and nucleic acids (nucleotides, nitrogen bases, building RNA and DNA helix models, ATP as the cell's energy carrier).

## Sub lesson 3 — The food-tests workbook

The food-tests workbook: a binary results matrix for Biuret, Lugol and Sudan III reagents across egg white, milk, starch, oil, water and tortilla (plain and fried); listing the day's meals with their natural sources; foods that can cause overweight and their main components.
