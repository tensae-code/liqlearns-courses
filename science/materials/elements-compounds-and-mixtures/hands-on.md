# Hands-on — Elements, compounds and mixtures

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The great sort
In groups, sort twelve home substances onto three cloths — ELEMENTS, COMPOUNDS, MIXTURES — and defend each placing to the class in one sentence.

## Fixed-ratio mixing
Mix sand and cement-dust (or flour) 4:1 beside a careless unmeasured mix. Compare the two heaps: the fixed ratio is the same in every spoonful; the other is not.
