# Lesson plan — Elements, compounds and mixtures

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Materials · **Lesson:** Elements, compounds and mixtures
**Place in the curriculum:** Materials — what matter is built of, at Grade 5–6 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** matter is anything that takes up space and has mass, built of atoms; pure matter splits into elements (one kind of atom) and compounds (elements chemically joined in a fixed ratio), while mixtures are only physically joined with no fixed ratio
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Describing things and Solids, liquids and gases — matter named and stated; now its building is opened.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define matter and say what it is built of: atoms.
2. Tell elements from compounds: one kind of atom, fixed make-up — against elements chemically joined in a fixed ratio (water always 2 hydrogen : 1 oxygen).
3. Tell a mixture from both: only physically joined, no fixed ratio — teff with wheat, salt solution, water with oil.
4. Say what is NOT matter: feelings and thoughts — they take no space and have no mass.

## What you need
Things to sort: salt, sugar, water, oil, teff and wheat grains, an iron nail, a coin.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls salt water a compound → no fixed ratio and no chemical joining: it is a mixture, and the salt can be got back by physical means.
- Thinks thoughts are matter because they are real → real is not the test; the test is space and mass, and thoughts have neither.
