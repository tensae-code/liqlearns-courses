# Elements, compounds and mixtures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Matter is anything that —**
a) can be seen  b) takes up space and has mass  c) is alive
**Answer: b) takes up space and has mass**

**2. An element is built of —**
a) many kinds of atom  b) one kind of atom  c) no atoms
**Answer: b) one kind of atom**

**3. Water's fixed ratio is —**
a) 1 hydrogen : 2 oxygen  b) 2 hydrogen : 1 oxygen  c) any ratio at all
**Answer: b) 2 hydrogen : 1 oxygen**

**4. Teff mixed with wheat is a —**
a) compound  b) element  c) mixture
**Answer: c) mixture**

**5. Which of these is NOT matter?**
a) a thought  b) salt  c) air in a balloon
**Answer: a) a thought**

**6. Mercury is the —**
a) liquid metal exception  b) only metalloid  c) a compound
**Answer: a) liquid metal exception**
