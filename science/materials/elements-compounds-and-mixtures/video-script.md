# Video script — Elements, compounds and mixtures

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Matter** takes space and has mass. It is built of **atoms**.
An **element** — one kind of atom. Iron. Oxygen. Carbon.
A **compound** — elements joined in a **fixed ratio**. Water: two to one, always.
A **mixture** — only joined, no fixed ratio. Teff with wheat.
Feelings and thoughts? **Not matter** — no space, no mass.
