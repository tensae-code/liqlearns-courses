## Sub lessons

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Materials — Mixtures and how to separate them. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain winnowing: density parts grain from chaff in moving air.
2. Explain sieving as separation by size.
3. Match the jebena's clear pour to decantation, and clarifying tella/tej to settling.
4. Recognise the local araki still as distillation.

## What you need
a winnowing tray with grain and chaff, a sieve, a jebena with brewed coffee, a vessel of tella or tej

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks winnowing works by size → the grain and chaff part by DENSITY in moving air; size is the sieve's property.
- Calls the jebena pour 'filtering' → nothing passes through a barrier; the grounds settle and the clear coffee is poured off — decantation.
