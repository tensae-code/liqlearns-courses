# Hands-on — Separating mixtures — local methods

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Winnow for real
With grain and chaff (or paper bits and stones of like size), toss from a tray in a breeze or a fan's air. Collect the two landings separately and weigh or count them.

## The jebena test
Let brewed coffee stand. Pour two cups: the first pour and the last. Compare their clarity — settling did the work.
