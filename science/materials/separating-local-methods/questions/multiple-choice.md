# Separating mixtures — local methods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Winnowing separates by —
a) size  b) density  c) magnetism
**Answer: b) density** — grain falls, chaff blows.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In winnowing, the chaff —
a) falls straight  b) blows aside  c) dissolves
**Answer: b) blows aside** — it is light.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Sieving separates by —
a) size  b) boiling point  c) colour
**Answer: a) size**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pouring clear coffee off settled grounds in the jebena is —
a) distillation  b) decantation  c) evaporation
**Answer: b) decantation**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Clarifying tella and tej rests on —
a) settling  b) magnetism  c) sieving
**Answer: a) settling**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The local araki still is the home form of —
a) filtration  b) distillation
**Answer: b) distillation**
