# Separating mixtures — local methods — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ______ teff in the wind parts grain from chaff.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Coffee is decanted clear in the ______.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A sieve separates by ______.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Tella clarifies by ______.
