# Population of my country — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) How many people live in my country  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) How many people live in my country**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) A young country and its workforce  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) A young country and its workforce**

**3.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: the population of my country — how many people live here, and what that means. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**4.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Vietnam, Grade 5 — *Khoa học 5*, printed p. 78  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Vietnam, Grade 5 — *Khoa học 5*, printed p. 78**
