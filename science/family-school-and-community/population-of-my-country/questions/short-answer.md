# Population of my country — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: How many people live in my country.
**Answer:** Vietnam's Grade 5 *Khoa học 5* gives its own country's number (an extension box, from UN figures): Vietnam's population reached **about 100 million in April 2023** — the **15th largest population in the world**. A country's population is the count of everyone who lives in it, and the count changes as babies are born and people live longer.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: A young country and its workforce.
**Answer:** The same box gives the country's average age — **about 34 years** — and reads the two numbers together: a large population at a young average age is a **young, plentiful workforce**, many people of working age to build, farm, make and teach. Knowing your own country's numbers is the start of knowing your country.

**Answer:** How many people live in my country → A young country and its workforce — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
