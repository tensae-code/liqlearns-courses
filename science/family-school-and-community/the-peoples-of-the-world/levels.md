# The peoples of the world — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (The racial composition of the world's population, How racial features formed; equality of races, Human races, Why are all people different?, Formation of racial features. Equality of races), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 5 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks.
