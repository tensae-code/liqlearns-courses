# East Africa's Population and Density — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) East Africa's share of the world's population is —**
a) 0.57%  b) 5.71%  c) 57%
**Answer: b) 5.71%** — 5.71% of the world lives in this region, on the printed figures.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) By population, East Africa stands —**
a) last in Africa  b) first among Africa's regions  c) third in Africa
**Answer: b) first among Africa's regions** — First among Africa's regions by population.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Population density equals —**
a) area ÷ population  b) population ÷ area  c) population × area
**Answer: b) population ÷ area** — Total population divided by total area gives people per km².

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The densest country in the table is —**
a) Zambia  b) Ethiopia  c) Mauritius
**Answer: c) Mauritius** — Mauritius packs 624.23 people into each km².

**5. (Sub lesson 3) The region's highest densities sit —**
a) in the driest lowlands  b) on high land with fertile soil and better rain  c) on the sea coasts only
**Answer: b) on high land with fertile soil and better rain** — Comfortable temperatures, fertile soil, fewer harmful insects and rain draw the crowds.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) Fast growth presses on —**
a) food, education, housing and jobs  b) only farming  c) nothing
**Answer: a) food, education, housing and jobs** — Those four services are the book's named pressure points.
