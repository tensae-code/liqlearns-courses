# East Africa's Population and Density — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Give the region's total population and its world share.**
**Answer:** 445,638,590 people; 5.71% of the world's population (printed UN/Worldometer 2021 figures).

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Compute: a country of 10,000,000 people on 100,000 km² — what is its density?**
**Answer:** 10,000,000 ÷ 100,000 = 100 people per km².

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Give two reasons high places carry more people in this region.**
**Answer:** Any two of: comfortable temperatures; fertile soil; freedom from harmful insects; better rainfall than the lowlands.
