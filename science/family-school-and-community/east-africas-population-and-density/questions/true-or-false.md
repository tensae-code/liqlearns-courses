# East Africa's Population and Density — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Fast population growth is no problem when economic growth is low.**
**Answer: False** — The book names exactly that pairing as a worrying problem.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Zambia is denser than Rwanda.**
**Answer: False** — Zambia is 25.14/km²; Rwanda is 504.08/km² — twenty times denser.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) People crowd most where rainfall is worst.**
**Answer: False** — Better rainfall than the lowlands is one of the highlands' draws.
