# Friends — Playing and Learning Together

## Do it first

Think of your best school friend. What do you do together that helps you both? Hold that picture, then read what good friendship means.

## Sub lesson 1 — What good relations mean

Good friendship means understanding and helping: friends listen to each other, explain to each other, and carry each other through hard work and hard days.

[Picture 12020 — two children studying together at one desk]

## Sub lesson 2 — Friends at work and on the road

Friendship, in pictures, is practical: studying together at one desk, doing homework together, and walking to and from school together. Friendship is not only talk — it is shared work and a shared road.

## Say it to your companion

Explain this lesson's one thing to your companion in your own words: good friendship at school — understanding and helping each other: studying together, doing homework together, and walking to and from school together. Then answer one question they ask you about it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Study one hard page with a friend today, each explaining half.
2. Walk home with a friend and talk about today's lesson on the way.
3. Write a thank-you note to a friend who helped you learn.
