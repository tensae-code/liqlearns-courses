# Friends — Playing and Learning Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Good friendship at school means —**
a) understanding and helping each other  b) hiding answers always  c) leaving friends behind
**Answer: a) understanding and helping each other** — Understanding and helping define good relations.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Which is friendship in action?**
a) Studying together and doing homework together  b) Copying without learning  c) Walking away alone always
**Answer: a) Studying together and doing homework together** — Shared study and homework are the book's examples.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A classmate falls behind in reading. A good friend —**
a) laughs  b) practises reading with them  c) hides their book
**Answer: b) practises reading with them** — Helping is friendship's work.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Studying together helps because —**
a) two minds explain to each other  b) it is noisier  c) homework vanishes
**Answer: a) two minds explain to each other** — Friends explain to each other.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Good relations at school make children —**
a) afraid  b) understood and helped  c) alone
**Answer: b) understood and helped** — Understanding and help are the fruit.
