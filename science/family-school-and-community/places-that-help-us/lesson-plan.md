# Lesson plan — Our Community — Places That Help Us

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Family, School and Community · **Lesson:** Our Community — Places That Help Us
**Place in the curriculum:** the Science course's Ethiopia Environmental Science stock, primary levels (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the places and institutions that serve a community — health station, church and mosque, bank, police station, school, iddir, court — what each one gives, from kebele to sub-city.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the location lessons — now the important places on the map get their jobs explained (the Grade 1 and teacher-guide parts are evidence; the Grade 2 student book carries the lesson).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Important places of the kebele (teacher guide — evidence)** — even the smallest area has its significant places and institutions, named and located along its roads: the school, the health post, the church or mosque, the market.
2. **Institutions of the woreda and what each gives** — at woreda scale the list grows, and each place gives its own service: the health station treats the sick; the bank keeps money safely; the police station keeps order; the school teaches; the iddir helps families in sorrow and need; the court settles disputes fairly; the church and mosque serve the faithful.
3. **Main places of the sub-city (teacher guide — evidence)** — in the city, the same learning grows again: a sub-city's main places — stadium, market, hospital, bus station — are pinned on its map, each found by the service people go to it for.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- The words are new → go back to the lesson's picture and say what it shows, in home words first, then in the lesson's words.
- The parts blur together → take one part at a time and answer that part's questions before moving on; the gate is per part where sub lessons exist.
- The learner cannot do the Do It Yourself → do the first item together, then let them finish the rest alone.
