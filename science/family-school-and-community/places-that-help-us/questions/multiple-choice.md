# Our Community — Places That Help Us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which places are significant in a kebele?**
a) School, health post, church or mosque, market  b) Only houses  c) Only trees
**Answer: a) School, health post, church or mosque, market** — These institutions are the kebele's significant places.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The iddir is —**
a) a bank  b) a community association that helps families in need and sorrow  c) a court
**Answer: b) a community association that helps families in need and sorrow** — The iddir is the community's own mutual-help association.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) A hospital in a sub-city is the place people go to for —**
a) sport  b) treatment when ill  c) buying grain
**Answer: b) treatment when ill** — Hospitals treat the sick.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where do you go to keep money safe?**
a) The bank  b) The river  c) The market roof
**Answer: a) The bank** — Banks keep money safe.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) A dispute that needs fair settling goes to —**
a) the court  b) the stadium  c) the shop
**Answer: a) the court** — Courts settle disputes.

**6. (Sub lesson 1) The police station's service is —**
a) keeping order and safety  b) selling food  c) teaching classes
**Answer: a) keeping order and safety** — Police keep order.
