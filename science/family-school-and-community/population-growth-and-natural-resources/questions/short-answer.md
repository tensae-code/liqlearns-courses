# Population growth and natural resources — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: More people, the same resources.
**Answer:** Population grows; natural resources do not grow with it.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: What the pressure does.
**Answer:** Class 4 names the chain: forests cleared, habitats destroyed, soil eroded, landslides, and more natural disasters.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: A chain that can be read backwards.
**Answer:** Each harm in the chain has a human step before it — clearing, building, burning, over-using chemicals.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** More people, the same resources → What the pressure does → A chain that can be read backwards — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
