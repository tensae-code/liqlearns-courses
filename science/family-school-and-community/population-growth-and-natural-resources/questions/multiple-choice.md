# Population growth and natural resources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) More people, the same resources  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) More people, the same resources**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) What the pressure does  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) What the pressure does**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) A chain that can be read backwards  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) A chain that can be read backwards**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: population growth set against limited natural resources — forest clearing, habitat destruction, soil erosion, landslides and more natural disasters — as Bangladesh's Classes 4 and 5 teach it. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Bangladesh, Grade 4 — *Elementary Science Class 4*, printed p. 104  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
