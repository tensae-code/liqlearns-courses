# Population growth and natural resources

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — More people, the same resources

Population grows; natural resources do not grow with it. Bangladesh's books set the two side by side: every added person needs food, housing and land from the same forests, soil and water as before.

## Sub lesson 2 — What the pressure does

Class 4 names the chain: forests cleared, habitats destroyed, soil eroded, landslides, and more natural disasters. Class 5 adds the rest: excess farm chemicals polluting soil and water, and fossil fuels burnt for power and transport polluting the air.

## Sub lesson 3 — A chain that can be read backwards

Each harm in the chain has a human step before it — clearing, building, burning, over-using chemicals. Reading the chain backwards shows where care can break it: protecting forest, soil and water protects the people who depend on them.
