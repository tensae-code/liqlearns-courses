# Our Community — Market, Clinic and Play Places — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The market is the place for —**
a) buying and selling  b) only sleeping  c) only praying
**Answer: a) buying and selling** — The market is for buying and selling.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) At the health station —**
a) mothers give birth and the sick are treated  b) grain is sold  c) cloth is woven
**Answer: a) mothers give birth and the sick are treated** — The health station is the place of birth care and treatment.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A mother about to give birth goes to —**
a) the market  b) the health station  c) the recreation ground
**Answer: b) the health station** — Mothers give birth at the health station.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which place belongs to rest?**
a) Recreation place  b) Bank  c) Court
**Answer: a) Recreation place** — Recreation places serve rest.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) On market day you would expect —**
a) an empty town  b) buying, selling and meeting  c) silence
**Answer: b) buying, selling and meeting** — Market day is busy with trade and meeting.
