# Our Community — Market, Clinic and Play Places — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three shared places of this lesson and what each is for.**
**Answer:** Market — buying and selling; health station — births and treating the sick; recreation places — rest and play.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why does a community need a place for rest?**
**Answer:** Rest restores people for work and learning (recreation is one of the shared community places).
