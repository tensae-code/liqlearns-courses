# Our Community — Market, Clinic and Play Places

## Do it first

Saturday comes. Where does everyone go? Think of the busiest place in your community, the place for the sick, and the place for rest. Read on, and name them yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The market — where the community meets to trade

The market is the community's shared trading place: buying and selling happen there, farmers and traders meet there, and news travels there. On market day the whole area flows into one place.

## Sub lesson 2 — The health station and places of rest

The health station is where mothers give birth and where the sick are treated — the community's place of care. And a community also shares recreation places: open places where people rest and children play in their free time. Work, care and rest each have their shared place.

## Say it to your companion

Explain this lesson's one thing to your companion in your own words: the places the whole community shares — the market for buying and selling, the health station where mothers give birth and the sick are treated, and recreation places for rest time. Then answer one question they ask you about it.
