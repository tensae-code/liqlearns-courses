# Hands-on — Children's Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The ability line
Draw a line across a page: 'a child can do this' on one side, 'too much for a child' on the other. Place ten household jobs on the line as a class and argue the hard ones.

## Who protects?
Make a protectors' poster for the classroom wall: the child in the middle, and around them — family, teachers and school, kebele leaders, community, government, NGOs — with one line under each saying what that protector does.

## Four shield-cards
Make four paper shields, one for each printed right. Take turns: one pupil reads a short scene, the others raise the shield that protects the child in it.
