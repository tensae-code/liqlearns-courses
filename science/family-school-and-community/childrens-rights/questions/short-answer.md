# Children's Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) List the six things every child should have.**
**Answer:** Love, a name, care, food, clothing and education.

**2. (Sub lesson 3) Name four children's-rights violations from the chapter.**
**Answer:** Any four of: labour exploitation, early marriage, beating, school dropout, sexual abuse, illegal trafficking.

**3. (Sub lesson 4) Name two rights of the child from the 1989 convention.**
**Answer:** Any two of: life, a name and a nationality; to know one's parents or caregivers and receive their care; protection from exploitative work; special protection from cruel or inhuman treatment.
