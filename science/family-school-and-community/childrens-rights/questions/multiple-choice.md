# Children's Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is a child's right?**
a) education  b) a car  c) a shop
**Answer: a) education** — Love, a name, care, food, clothing and education are the named rights.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The rights song መብታችን ይከበር means —**
a) 'Let our rights be respected'  b) 'Time to work'  c) 'Good morning'
**Answer: a) 'Let our rights be respected'** — The song's title, carried as theme only.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A child should do work that is —**
a) beyond their ability  b) within their ability  c) all the house's work
**Answer: b) within their ability** — Only work within their ability at home.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Who must care for orphans and children without food, clothes or schooling?**
a) no one  b) the community, teachers, school, kebele leaders, government and NGOs  c) other children
**Answer: b) the community, teachers, school, kebele leaders, government and NGOs** — Protection has named protectors.

**5. (Sub lesson 3) A child is anyone —**
a) under 12  b) under 18  c) at school
**Answer: b) under 18** — The Grade 3 book's definition.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) In the story, the hired-out children —**
a) went to school  b) worked beyond their strength and missed school  c) played all day
**Answer: b) worked beyond their strength and missed school** — Loads beyond strength, and no schooling — their rights were violated.

**7. (Sub lesson 4) Under the 1989 convention, a child is every person under —**
a) 12  b) 16  c) 18
**Answer: c) 18** — The convention counts every person under 18 as a child.

**8. (Sub lesson 4) Which of these is a printed right of the child?**
a) to work instead of school  b) a name and a nationality  c) to be treated cruelly
**Answer: b) a name and a nationality** — Life, a name and a nationality stand first among the printed rights.
