# Children's Rights — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A name is one of a child's rights.**
**Answer: True** — Love, a name, care, food, clothing, education.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Early marriage is named as a children's-rights violation.**
**Answer: True** — It stands in the chapter's violations list.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Children's rights are gifts that adults may give or withhold.**
**Answer: False** — They are rights — owed to every child, not favours.
