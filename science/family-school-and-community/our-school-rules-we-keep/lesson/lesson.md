# Our School — Rules We Keep

## Do it first

What happens if everyone arrives at school at a different time, and no one does homework? Think about one school rule you know. Why does it exist? Read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — The rules of our school

Our school's rules keep the day fair and safe: arrive on time; wear the uniform; do your homework; respect the teachers; and do not break the school's things — desks, books and the blackboard are for everyone.

[Picture 12015 — pupils in uniform arriving at the school gate on time]

## Sub lesson 2 — Caring for property — the school's and your own

A care table sets them side by side: school property and personal property, and how each is cared for. A desk cared for serves the next class; a book cared for (you learned this in Care for Your Book) teaches all year.

## Say it to your companion

Explain this lesson's one thing to your companion in your own words: the school's rules — arriving on time, the uniform, homework, respect for teachers, and caring for school property — and how we care for what is ours and the school's. Then answer one question they ask you about it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write your school's rules as a poster list of five.
2. Check your own things: one thing to mend or clean today.
3. Arrive on time every day this week — mark each day.
