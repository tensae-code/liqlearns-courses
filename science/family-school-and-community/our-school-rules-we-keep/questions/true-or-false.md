# Our School — Rules We Keep — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Respecting teachers is one of the school's rules.**
**Answer: True** — Respect is listed among the rules.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Caring for your own things and the school's things are both your duty.**
**Answer: True** — The care table covers school and personal property together.
