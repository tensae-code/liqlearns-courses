# Video script — Family Needs — Food, Clothes and a Home

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A family needs three things to live.

Food — for growth, health and strength.

Clothes — to protect us, changing with the weather.

And a home — shelter from animals, sun, thieves, cold and rain.
