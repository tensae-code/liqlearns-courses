# Hands-on — Family Needs — Food, Clothes and a Home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Needs sorting
Sort picture cards into 'basic need' and 'not a basic need' — a meal, a toy, a house, a song; defend each sort.

## Weather clothes
Lay out clothes for a cold morning and a hot afternoon; say why each set fits its weather.
