# Family Needs — Food, Clothes and a Home — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three basic needs of a family.**
**Answer:** Food, clothing and shelter (a home).

**2. (Sub lesson 3) Give one way a house protects a family.**
**Answer:** Keeps out wild animals / sun / thieves / cold / rain (any one).
