# Family Needs — Food, Clothes and a Home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Food gives the body —**
a) growth, health and strength  b) only sweetness  c) nothing needed
**Answer: a) growth, health and strength** — Food serves growth, health and strength.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why do we wear different clothes in cold and hot weather?**
a) Clothes protect us, so they must fit the weather  b) Clothes are only for beauty  c) No reason
**Answer: a) Clothes protect us, so they must fit the weather** — Clothing protects; its kind follows the weather.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) A home protects the family from —**
a) wild animals, sun, thieves, cold and rain  b) food  c) each other only
**Answer: a) wild animals, sun, thieves, cold and rain** — Shelter protects from animals, weather and thieves.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) The family's three basic needs are —**
a) food, clothing and shelter  b) toys, songs and games  c) books, pens and bags
**Answer: a) food, clothing and shelter** — Food, clothing and shelter are the basic three.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In rainy, cold weather a child needs —**
a) thin clothes only  b) warm, protecting clothes  c) no clothes
**Answer: b) warm, protecting clothes** — Clothes must fit the weather to protect.

**6. (Sub lesson 1) The food song teaches that food is for —**
a) health, growth and energy  b) sleeping only  c) selling only
**Answer: a) health, growth and energy** — The song's theme: food for health, growth and energy.
