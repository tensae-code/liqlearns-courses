# Family Needs — Food, Clothes and a Home — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Children who eat well can grow and have strength to play and learn.**
**Answer: True** — Food feeds growth, health and energy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Clothes protect us from cold, rain and sun.**
**Answer: True** — Protection is clothing's job.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) All houses everywhere are built of exactly the same materials.**
**Answer: False** — Houses are built of different materials in different places.
