# Family Needs — Food, Clothes and a Home

## Do it first

You ate today. You are wearing clothes. You will sleep somewhere tonight. Which of these could a family live without? Think carefully, then read.

## Sub lesson 1 — Food — for growth, health and strength

Food is a basic need: it makes children grow, keeps the body healthy and gives strength for work and play. There is a food song, and its theme is this: food is for our health, our growth and our energy — sing it together in class.

[Picture 12013 — a meal, weather clothes and three different houses]

## Sub lesson 2 — Clothes — protection that changes with the weather

Clothes protect us from cold, from rain and from the sun. Because weather changes, clothes change with it: warm and thick in the cold, light in the heat.

## Sub lesson 3 — A home — shelter and safety

A house shelters the family: it protects from wild animals, from the sun, from thieves, from cold and from rain. Houses are built of different materials in different places — but every family's shelter does the same protecting work.

## Say it to your companion

Explain this lesson's one thing to your companion in your own words: the family's three basic needs — food for growth, health and strength; clothing that protects and changes with the weather; and shelter that keeps us safe. Then answer one question they ask you about it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Draw the three needs in three boxes: a meal, clothes for two weathers, your home.
2. Ask at home: what is our house built of?
3. Sing or hum the food song's idea in your own words: food for health, growth and energy.
