# Population — How a Country's People Grow — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Population grows by births minus deaths, plus —**
a) in-migration minus out-migration  b) shops minus schools  c) rain minus sun
**Answer: a) in-migration minus out-migration** — Migration's difference is the third mover's contribution.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Ethiopia's growth rate in the book is given as —**
a) under 1%  b) above 2.5%  c) exactly 0%
**Answer: b) above 2.5%** — Above 2.5% a year, on the survey the book cites.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The average birth rate measures —**
a) deaths per year  b) the average children a woman has in her lifetime  c) migrants per town
**Answer: b) the average children a woman has in her lifetime** — It counts lifetime children per woman.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) One printed reason births stay high is —**
a) children counted as wealth  b) too many hospitals  c) small families preferred
**Answer: a) children counted as wealth** — Children as wealth, sex preference and weak reproductive-health support are the printed reasons.

**5. (Sub lesson 3) Average life expectancy measures —**
a) how long a person is expected to live  b) how many children are born  c) how far people migrate
**Answer: a) how long a person is expected to live** — Life expectancy is the average length of a life.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) Death rates vary with —**
a) age, sex, work and health  b) only the weather  c) only town size
**Answer: a) age, sex, work and health** — Those four are the book's named factors.

**7. (Sub lesson 4) Countryside→town is which kind of migration?**
a) international  b) internal  c) neither
**Answer: b) internal** — Both places are inside one country — internal migration.

**8. (Sub lesson 4) Internal migration in Ethiopia runs in —**
a) one direction only  b) all four directions  c) no direction
**Answer: b) all four directions** — Countryside and town exchange people both ways, and each exchanges with itself.

**9. (Sub lesson 5) ፍልሰት (migration) can —**
a) only raise a population  b) raise or lower it  c) do nothing
**Answer: b) raise or lower it** — Movers arriving raise it; movers leaving lower it.

**10. (Sub lesson 5) A birth —**
a) lowers the number  b) raises the number  c) changes nothing
**Answer: b) raises the number** — Children born alive raise the place's number.
