# Population — How a Country's People Grow — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Deaths raise a country's population.**
**Answer: False** — Deaths lower it; births raise it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Rural birth rates in Ethiopia are low.**
**Answer: False** — The book says rural birth rates are high.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) The child death rate measures deaths among old people.**
**Answer: False** — It measures deaths among children.

**4. (Sub lesson 4) Migration only ever gains a place people.**
**Answer: False** — It raises one place's number and lowers another's; the book weighs its gains and losses.

**5. (Sub lesson 5) International migration stays inside one country.**
**Answer: False** — International migration crosses a border; internal migration stays inside.
