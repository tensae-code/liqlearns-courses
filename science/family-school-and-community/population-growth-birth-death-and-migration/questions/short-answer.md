# Population — How a Country's People Grow — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) State the growth rule in words.**
**Answer:** A population grows by the difference between births and deaths, plus the difference between people moving in and people moving out.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name two reasons the book gives for high births.**
**Answer:** Any two of: children counted as a source of wealth; preference for one sex; weak reproductive-health support.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Name the three measures of death the book gives.**
**Answer:** The crude death rate; the child death rate; average life expectancy.

**4. (Sub lesson 4) Give the four internal migration directions.**
**Answer:** Countryside→countryside; countryside→town; town→town; town→countryside.

**5. (Sub lesson 5) Say the three movers and what each does to a place's number.**
**Answer:** Birth raises it; death lowers it; migration raises it where people arrive and lowers it where they leave.
