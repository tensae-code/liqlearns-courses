# Our Community — People Young and Old; People's Jobs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In the book's grouping, a youth is —**
a) 0–18  b) 19–30  c) over 64
**Answer: b) 19–30** — Youth: 19–30 years.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A potter's products are —**
a) jebena and pots  b) sickles and ploughs  c) cloth and dresses
**Answer: a) jebena and pots** — The potter makes jebena and pots (the blacksmith makes sickle and plough).

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A 10-year-old in the age grouping is a —**
a) youth  b) child  c) elder
**Answer: b) child** — 0–18 is a child.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Who makes cloth on a loom?**
a) The weaver  b) The potter  c) The carpenter
**Answer: a) The weaver** — The weaver works the loom.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) A carpenter works mainly with —**
a) wood  b) clay  c) iron only
**Answer: a) wood** — Carpenters work wood.
