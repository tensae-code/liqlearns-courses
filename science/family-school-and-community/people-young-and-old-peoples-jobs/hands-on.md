# Hands-on — Our Community — People Young and Old; People's Jobs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trade table live
Bring one product per trade to class (a pot, a cloth, a tool) and build the trade–product table from the real objects.

## Age-group line
Stand in a line by age group — children, youth, adults, elders (use family photos or role cards) — and say what each group gives the community.
