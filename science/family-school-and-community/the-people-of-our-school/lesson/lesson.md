# Our School — The People of Our School

## Do it first

Look around your school in your mind. The teacher is easy to name. Who else works here? Who guards the gate? Who keeps the library? Count them before you read.

## Sub lesson 1 — Many people make one school

A school is a community of people with different jobs: the pupils and their teachers; the head and the deputy who lead; the supervisor; the guards at the gate; the library staff; the cleaning staff; and the secretaries. Remove any one of them and the school day limps.

## Sub lesson 2 — Each person, a different job

The teacher teaches; the guard watches the gate; the librarian cares for the books; the cleaner makes the rooms ready; the secretary keeps the school's papers. Knowing each job teaches us to respect each person — and to know whom to ask for what.

## Say it to your companion

Explain this lesson's one thing to your companion in your own words: the members of a school community — pupils, teachers, head, deputy, supervisor, guards, library and cleaning staff, secretaries — and the different job each one does. Then answer one question they ask you about it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Draw your school compound and put each person at their post.
2. Write the names of your own school's head and your teacher.
3. Thank one school worker this week, naming their job as you thank them.
