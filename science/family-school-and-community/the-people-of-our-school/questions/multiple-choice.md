# Our School — The People of Our School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Who leads a school?**
a) The head, with the deputy  b) Only the pupils  c) The guards alone
**Answer: a) The head, with the deputy** — The head leads, with the deputy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) If you need a book from the school library, you ask —**
a) the guard  b) the library staff  c) the cook
**Answer: b) the library staff** — Library staff care for the books.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which list is all school community members?**
a) Teacher, head, guard, cleaner  b) Farmer, trader, potter only  c) Doctor, driver, baker only
**Answer: a) Teacher, head, guard, cleaner** — All four work in the school.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) The supervisor's place in the school is to —**
a) help watch over the school's work  b) cook lunch  c) sell goods
**Answer: a) help watch over the school's work** — The supervisor is named among the school's leading staff.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why learn the school people's jobs?**
a) To respect each person and know whom to ask  b) To laugh at them  c) No reason
**Answer: a) To respect each person and know whom to ask** — Knowing jobs builds respect and helps us ask the right person.
