# Our School — The People of Our School — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Cleaning staff and library staff are members of the school community.**
**Answer: True** — Every worker in the school belongs to its community.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Every member of the school does exactly the same job.**
**Answer: False** — Each member has a different job — that is the point.
