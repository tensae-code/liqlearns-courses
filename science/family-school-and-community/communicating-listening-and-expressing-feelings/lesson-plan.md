# Lesson plan — Communicating — listening and expressing feelings

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Family, School and Community · **Lesson:** Communicating — listening and expressing feelings
**Place in the curriculum:** Family, School and Community — appended to Unit 12 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** communicating at school — listening carefully without interrupting, expressing feelings and thoughts, and reading non-verbal messages — Turkey's own way, in its Grade 2 Hayat Bilgisi book, under the heading İletişim Var, Sorun Yok (there is communication, there is no problem)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Listening carefully and expressing feelings and thoughts** — Good communication at school starts with listening: listen carefully, without interrupting, until the other person has finished. Then express your own feelings and thoughts clearly, in words — say what you feel and what you think, so the other person does not have to guess.
2. **Non-verbal messages** — Not every message uses words. Faces, gestures and posture also send messages — non-verbal messages. A communicator watches those messages as well as listening to words, and notices the non-verbal messages they themselves are sending.
