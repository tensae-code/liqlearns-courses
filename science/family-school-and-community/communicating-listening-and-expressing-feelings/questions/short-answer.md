# Communicating — listening and expressing feelings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the two halves of communicating at school in Turkey's book.
**Answer:** Listening carefully without interrupting, and expressing your own feelings and thoughts clearly in words.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is a non-verbal message? Give one example.
**Answer:** A message sent without words — for example a face, gesture or posture that shows how a person feels.
