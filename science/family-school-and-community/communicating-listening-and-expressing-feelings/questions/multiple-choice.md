# Communicating — listening and expressing feelings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Listening carefully means listening…
a) While planning your reply  b) Without interrupting, until the other person has finished  c) Only to the first word  d) With your back turned
**Answer: b) Without interrupting, until the other person has finished**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) To express feelings and thoughts well, you should…
a) Make others guess  b) Say what you feel and think clearly in words  c) Stay silent  d) Shout them
**Answer: b) Say what you feel and think clearly in words**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A non-verbal message is a message sent by…
a) A written note  b) Faces, gestures and posture, without words  c) A loudspeaker  d) A book
**Answer: b) Faces, gestures and posture, without words**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A good communicator watches non-verbal messages…
a) Only in other people  b) Only in themselves  c) In others and in themselves  d) Never
**Answer: c) In others and in themselves**
