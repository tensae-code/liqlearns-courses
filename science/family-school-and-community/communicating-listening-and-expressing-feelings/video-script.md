# Video script — Communicating — listening and expressing feelings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Communicating — listening and expressing feelings**.

There is communication, there is no problem — that is Turkey's heading. First, listen: carefully, without interrupting, until the other person finishes. Then speak: say your feelings and your thoughts clearly, so nobody has to guess.
And watch the messages without words. A face, a gesture, a posture — they all speak. Notice them in others, and notice the ones you are sending yourself.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
