# Hands-on — Communicating — listening and expressing feelings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Listen without interrupting
Work in a pair. One person speaks for one minute about their day; the other listens without interrupting at all, then repeats back what they heard — feelings as well as facts. Swap roles.

## Say the feeling
Practise three sentences that express a feeling and a thought clearly: "I feel … because …" / "I think … because …" Say each one to your partner, and let your partner name the feeling and the thought they heard.

## Read the non-verbal message
Without words, one partner shows a feeling with face and posture alone. The other names the feeling and says which non-verbal messages gave it away. Swap and repeat with a different feeling.
