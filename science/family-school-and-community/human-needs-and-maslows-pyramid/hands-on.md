# Hands-on — Human needs and Maslow's pyramid

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — Maslow's pyramid of needs in five levels. Maslow's pyramid of needs in five levels: physiological (base: food, water, survival), safety (body, belongings, housing, work, health), belonging (acceptance, friendship, family), esteem (self-respect, success, confidence) and self-realisation at the top (goals, values, fulfilment) Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
