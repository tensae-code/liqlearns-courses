# Human needs and Maslow's pyramid — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Honduras's books teach about maslow's pyramid of needs in five levels?
**Answer: Maslow's pyramid of needs in five levels: physiological (base: food, water, survival), safety (body, belongings, housing, work, health), belonging (acceptance, friendship, family), esteem (self-respect, success, confidence) and self-realisation at the top (goals, values, fulfilment)**

**2.** What do Honduras's books teach about a need (something required to live and develop) is?
**Answer: a need (something required to live and develop) is not a desire (given importance by each person); conflicts arise when people's needs compete and are best solved by dialogue, with solidarity and respect as the values that hold a group together.**

**3.** What do Honduras's books teach about atkinson's theory?
**Answer: Atkinson's theory: people's conduct depends on the motive, the expectation and the incentive; motivating states drive action (hunger is a motivating state that pushes us to seek food); Maslow's levels with contents — physiological (breathing, eating, sleep, homeostasis), security, affiliation (friendship, affection, communication), recognition (confidence, respect, prestige) and self-actualisation (morality, spontaneity, creativity, problem-solving) (guide evidence)**

**4.** Name this lesson's one thing.
**Answer: Human needs and Maslow's pyramid.**
