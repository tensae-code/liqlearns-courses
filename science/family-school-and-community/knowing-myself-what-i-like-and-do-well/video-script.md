# Video script — Knowing myself: what I like and do well

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Knowing myself: what I like and do well** — Suriname's way, from its Grade 6 *Natuuronderwijs* book.

Suriname's book does not start with plants or animals. It starts with you. *Ik ben uniek* — I am unique.
No other child is exactly you. So say who you are: what do you like? What do you not like? And what are you proud that you can do well?
Three lists. All yours. That is knowing yourself.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
