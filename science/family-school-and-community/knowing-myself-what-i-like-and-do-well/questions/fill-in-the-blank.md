# Knowing myself: what I like and do well — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) *Ik ben _____* — I am unique.
**Answer: uniek**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Unique means one of a _____ — no other child is exactly like you.
**Answer: kind**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Each child lists what they like, what they _____, and what they are proud of being good at.
**Answer: dislike**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Suriname's book starts the year with the self before the _____.
