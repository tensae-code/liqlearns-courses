# Knowing myself: what I like and do well — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) *Ik ben uniek* means…
a) I am the teacher  b) I am unique  c) I am first  d) I am finished
**Answer: b) I am unique**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Unique means…
a) The oldest  b) The tallest  c) One of a kind — no other child is exactly like you  d) The same as everyone
**Answer: c) One of a kind — no other child is exactly like you**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Suriname's Grade 6 book opens the year with…
a) Plants  b) The self — knowing yourself first  c) The Moon  d) Computers
**Answer: b) The self — knowing yourself first**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The three lists of this lesson are…
a) Names, ages, heights  b) What I like, what I dislike, and what I am proud of being good at  c) Foods, games, songs  d) Yesterday, today, tomorrow
**Answer: b) What I like, what I dislike, and what I am proud of being good at**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Why can't you copy a friend's lists?
a) The teacher forbids paper  b) Your friend writes too fast  c) The lists describe your friend, a different unique child — yours must be your own  d) Lists cannot be copied
**Answer: c) The lists describe your friend, a different unique child — yours must be your own**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Naming what you dislike is…
a) Complaining, and never allowed  b) Part of knowing yourself — honesty about who you are  c) Only for teachers  d) The whole lesson
**Answer: b) Part of knowing yourself — honesty about who you are**
