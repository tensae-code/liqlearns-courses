# Knowing myself: what I like and do well — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does *Ik ben uniek* mean, and what does it say about you?
**Answer:** It means "I am unique" — one of a kind: no other child is exactly like you, in face, voice, thoughts, likes and skills together.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does Suriname's Grade 6 book put this lesson first, before the body and before nature?
**Answer:** Its opening lessons are a social-skills pair that starts the year with the self before the body — every later lesson is learned by a person, so the book first asks the person to know themselves.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the three lists this lesson asks each child to make.
**Answer:** What I like; what I dislike; and what I am proud of being good at.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A child says: "I will write down what my best friend likes, because we are friends." Why does that fail this lesson?
**Answer:** The lists must describe the child who writes them. The friend is a different unique child, so the friend's lists say who the friend is, not who this child is.
