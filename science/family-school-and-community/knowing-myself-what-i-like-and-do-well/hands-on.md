# Hands-on — Knowing myself: what I like and do well

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## My three lists
Take one sheet of paper and fold it into three columns. Head them: **I like** · **I do not like** · **I am good at, and proud of it**. Fill each column with at least three true entries of your own — nobody else's lists can do this work, because the lesson is *Ik ben uniek*: I am unique.

## The unique check
With a partner, read one entry from each of your columns out loud. Your partner says back one way your lists show you are different from every other child in the room, and one way the two of you are the same. Swap roles.

## The proud-skill minute
Pick the entry you are proudest of from your third column. Show it, do it, or explain it for one minute — a drawing, a sum, a kick, a song, a kind act — so the class sees the skill, not only its name on the list.
