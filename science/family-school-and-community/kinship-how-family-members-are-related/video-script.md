Today: **Kinship — how family members are related** — Angola's way: gather the family first, then name the relations.

Kinship is how the members of a family are related to one another. Each person stands in a named relation: parent, grandparent, brother or sister, uncle or aunt, cousin.

Given any two members of the family, kinship lets you say how they are related — that is the skill Angola's Grade 2 book teaches.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
