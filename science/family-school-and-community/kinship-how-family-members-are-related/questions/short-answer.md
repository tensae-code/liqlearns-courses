# Kinship — how family members are related — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is a family more than a list of people?
**Answer:** Because each person stands in a relation to the others — parent, grandparent, uncle, cousin — and kinship names those relations, not just the people.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How is a grandparent related to a cousin in the same family?
**Answer:** The grandparent is also the cousin's grandparent (or great-grandparent through the other line): both are grandchildren of that grandparent — relations are read through the shared parents and grandparents.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name four relations and say who each joins.
**Answer:** Parents join a child to mother and father; grandparents join the child to the parents' parents; uncles and aunts are the parents' brothers and sisters; cousins are the uncles' and aunts' children.
