# Kinship — how family members are related — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) _____ is how the members of a family are related to one another.
**Answer: Kinship**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The children of my uncles and aunts are my _____.
**Answer: cousins**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) My mother and father together are my _____.
**Answer: parents**
