# Kinship — how family members are related — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Kinship is…
a) The house a family lives in
b) How the members of a family are related to one another
c) The food a family eats
d) A list of names only
**Answer: b) How the members of a family are related to one another**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Angola's Grade 2 book teaches kinship relations…
a) Before the family members
b) Straight after the lesson that gathers my family and its other members
c) Only in Grade 6
d) In the road-safety lesson
**Answer: b) Straight after the lesson that gathers my family and its other members**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Your mother's brother is your…
a) Cousin
b) Grandfather
c) Uncle
d) Brother
**Answer: c) Uncle**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Your grandparents are…
a) Your cousins' children
b) Your parents' parents
c) Your aunts' friends
d) Your teachers
**Answer: b) Your parents' parents**
