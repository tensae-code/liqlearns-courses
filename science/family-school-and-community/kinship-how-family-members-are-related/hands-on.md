# Hands-on — Kinship — how family members are related

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the relation tree
On paper, write the names of your family members and draw lines between them, labelling each line with the relation — parent, grandparent, uncle, aunt, cousin, brother, sister.

## Relation detective
Have someone name two members of your family. Answer with the relation between them, then check together by walking the tree line by line.
