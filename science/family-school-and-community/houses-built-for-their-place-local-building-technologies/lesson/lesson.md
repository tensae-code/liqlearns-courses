## Sub lesson 1 — Place shapes the house

In Many Types of Houses, Bangladesh's Class 9 Exercise Book teaches that the environment and water availability shape a place's houses: a house is an answer to its place — its rain, its floods, its heat and its materials.

## Sub lesson 2 — Local building technologies

Each area develops specific technologies for the characteristics of the area — the book's own way of teaching technology as an answer to place, taught inside the same unit.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why mud houses stay cool

The book's test case is the traditional mud house: thick mud walls take the day's heat slowly and give it back slowly, so the inside stays cool. A technology does not need to be new, or bought, to be clever.
