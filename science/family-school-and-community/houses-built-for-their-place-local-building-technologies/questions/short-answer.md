# Houses built for their place — local building technologies — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Place shapes the house.
**Answer:** In Many Types of Houses, Bangladesh's Class 9 Exercise Book teaches that the environment and water availability shape a place's houses: a house is an answer to its place — its rain, its floods, its heat and its materials..

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Local building technologies.
**Answer:** Each area develops specific technologies for the characteristics of the area — the book's own way of teaching technology as an answer to place, taught inside the same unit..

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Why mud houses stay cool.
**Answer:** The book's test case is the traditional mud house: thick mud walls take the day's heat slowly and give it back slowly, so the inside stays cool.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Place shapes the house → Local building technologies → Why mud houses stay cool — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
