# Hands-on — Planning my time

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## List and order your day
Write down everything you will do tomorrow, as it comes to mind. Then number the list in the order you will actually do it. Cross out nothing — ordering is the work.

## Keep the week's plan
Copy your ordered list onto a week's page: one column for each day. Each evening, tick what was done as planned and mark what moved. At the week's end, review the page: which days went as planned, and what kept moving?

## Review and re-plan
From your review, write one sentence: "Next week I will plan differently by …" — and make next week's first day's list using that sentence.
