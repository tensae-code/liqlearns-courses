Today: **Planning my time**.

Being planned — Planlı Olmak — starts with the day itself. List what you do in a day. Put the list in order. Now the day is work you can do, one piece after another.
Then keep that plan every day for a week. At the end of the week, look back: what went as planned? What did not? That review is part of planning — it makes the next plan better.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
