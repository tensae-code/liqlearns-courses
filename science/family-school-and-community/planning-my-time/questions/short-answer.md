# Planning my time — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What are the first two steps of being planned?
**Answer:** Name what we do in a day — list the day's work — and put that work in order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How long is the daily plan kept in Turkey's book, and what happens at the end?
**Answer:** It is kept for a week, then reviewed: what was done as planned and what was not is looked back over, and that review shapes the next plan.
