# Planning my time — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Being planned starts by…
a) Guessing the day  b) Listing the day's work and putting it in order  c) Doing the hardest thing first without a list  d) Copying a friend's day
**Answer: b) Listing the day's work and putting it in order**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A list helps because it turns a jumbled day into…
a) A longer day  b) Work that can be done one piece after another  c) No work at all  d) A drawing
**Answer: b) Work that can be done one piece after another**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Turkey's book asks the learner to keep a daily plan for…
a) One hour  b) One day only  c) A week  d) A year
**Answer: c) A week**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Reviewing the week's plan means…
a) Throwing it away unread  b) Looking back at what was done as planned and what was not  c) Making the list longer  d) Asking someone else to do it
**Answer: b) Looking back at what was done as planned and what was not**
