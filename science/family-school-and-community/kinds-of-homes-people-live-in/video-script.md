# Video script — Kinds of homes people live in


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


Today: **Kinds of homes people live in** — Angola's way: the home is a family need, and it comes in kinds.

Homes differ in what they are built from, in shape and size, and in where they stand. Each kind is shaped by the materials at hand, the place, and the family it shelters.

The lesson sorts the kinds. It does not rank them — every kind is still a home.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
