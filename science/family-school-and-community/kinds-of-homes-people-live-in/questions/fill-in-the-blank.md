# Kinds of homes people live in — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The different kinds of homes people live in are kinds of _____.
**Answer: housing**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A home is built from the _____ at hand.
**Answer: materials**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The home is one of the things a _____ needs.
**Answer: family**
