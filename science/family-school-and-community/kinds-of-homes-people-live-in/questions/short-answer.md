# Kinds of homes people live in — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why do homes come in different kinds?
**Answer:** Because each home is built from the materials at hand, in the place where the family lives, for the people it must shelter — and those differ from place to place and family to family.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Where does this lesson sit in Angola's Grade 2 book, and why?
**Answer:** Inside the family run, after what the family needs — because the home is one of the family's needs, and it comes in kinds.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Name two ways two homes can be of different kinds.
**Answer:** They can be built from different materials, and they can differ in shape, size or where they stand.
