# Kinds of homes people live in — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Angola's Grade 2 book teaches kinds of housing as…
a) A history lesson
b) Their own thing — homes sorted into kinds
c) A kind of food
d) A road rule
**Answer: b) Their own thing — homes sorted into kinds**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Homes of different kinds differ in…
a) Nothing at all
b) What they are built from, shape and size, and where they stand
c) Only their colour of paint
d) The day they were built only
**Answer: b) What they are built from, shape and size, and where they stand**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The kind of a home is shaped by…
a) Chance alone
b) The materials at hand, the place, and the family it shelters
c) The weather of one day
d) The size of the street only
**Answer: b) The materials at hand, the place, and the family it shelters**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The lesson sorts kinds of homes; it does not…
a) Name them
b) Rank one home above another as better people's homes
c) Draw them
d) Count them
**Answer: b) Rank one home above another as better people's homes**
