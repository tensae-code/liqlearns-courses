# Family — Who Is in a Family and What Each One Does

## Do it first

Think of your own home. Who lives there? What does each person do in a day? Name them in your head, then read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — The members of a family

A family holds father, mother and children — and sometimes grandparents, uncles and aunts live with them too. Each family is its own mix, but these are the members we meet first.

[Picture 12012 — a family at home, each member doing a different task]

## Sub lesson 2 — Every member has work to do

Every member works for the family's living — the grown-ups with their full work, and the children helping as they are able: small, safe tasks that fit a child's strength. No member only receives; each one gives what they can.

## Say it to your companion

Explain this lesson's one thing to your companion in your own words: who is in a family — father, mother, children, sometimes grandparents, uncles and aunts — and that every member works for the family's living, with children helping as they are able. Then answer one question they ask you about it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Draw your family, each person doing their work.
2. Write one line under each person: what they do for the family.
3. Do one helping task at home today, sized for you.
