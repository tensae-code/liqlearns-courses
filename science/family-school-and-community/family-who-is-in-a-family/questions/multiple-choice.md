# Family — Who Is in a Family and What Each One Does — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which people can be members of a family?**
a) Only a father  b) Father, mother, children — sometimes grandparents, uncles and aunts  c) Only teachers
**Answer: b) Father, mother, children — sometimes grandparents, uncles and aunts** — Families hold parents and children, and often grandparents, uncles and aunts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) How do children help the family?**
a) By doing work beyond their strength  b) By helping as they are able  c) By not helping at all
**Answer: b) By helping as they are able** — Children help as they are able.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A mother, a father and their children together are —**
a) a market  b) a family  c) a school
**Answer: b) a family** — Together they are a family.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which task fits a young child helping at home?**
a) Ploughing alone  b) Carrying a small water cup to the table  c) Working all night
**Answer: b) Carrying a small water cup to the table** — Children help with small, safe tasks.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) An uncle who lives in the home is —**
a) a stranger  b) a family member  c) a visitor forever
**Answer: b) a family member** — Uncles can be family members in the home.
