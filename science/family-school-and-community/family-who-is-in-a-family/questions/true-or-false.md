# Family — Who Is in a Family and What Each One Does — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Grandparents can be members of a family.**
**Answer: True** — The book names grandparents, uncles and aunts as possible members.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Every family member works for the family's living in some way.**
**Answer: True** — Each member contributes what they can.
