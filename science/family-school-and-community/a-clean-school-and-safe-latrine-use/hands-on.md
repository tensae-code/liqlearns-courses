# Hands-on — Our School — A Clean School and Safe Latrine Use

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Compound clean-up
In groups, clean one part of the compound each — classroom, yard, latrine area — then inspect each other's work.

## Hand-wash station check
Check the school's hand-washing place: is there water? Is there soap or ash? Report what is missing to a teacher.
