# Our School — A Clean School and Safe Latrine Use — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The latrine is part of what the school community keeps clean.**
**Answer: True** — Classroom, walls, compound and latrine are all named.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Leaving the latrine clean for the next person is part of using it properly.**
**Answer: True** — Proper use includes leaving it clean.
