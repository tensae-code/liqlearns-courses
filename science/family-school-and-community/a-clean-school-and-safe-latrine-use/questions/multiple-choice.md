# Our School — A Clean School and Safe Latrine Use — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Keeping the school clean is the job of —**
a) only the cleaning staff  b) everyone in the school  c) visitors
**Answer: b) everyone in the school** — Cleanliness is everyone's responsibility.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) After using the latrine you must —**
a) run to class  b) wash your hands  c) eat at once
**Answer: b) wash your hands** — Hands are washed after the latrine.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A wrapper drops in the compound. The clean-school thing to do is —**
a) leave it  b) pick it up and bin it  c) kick it away
**Answer: b) pick it up and bin it** — Everyone keeps the compound clean.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Washing hands after the latrine stops —**
a) sickness travelling between children  b) learning  c) play
**Answer: a) sickness travelling between children** — It blocks sickness spreading.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Dirty walls and classrooms make a school —**
a) proud  b) unhealthy and unpleasant  c) faster
**Answer: b) unhealthy and unpleasant** — Dirt harms health and the school's face.
