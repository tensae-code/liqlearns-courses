# Hands-on — Bullying — what it is and how to stop it

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — School bullying (acoso escolar) defined as physical or verbal. school bullying (acoso escolar) defined as physical or verbal harassment and mistreatment among peers; a victim does not feel safe, and schools should promote an agreeable atmosphere of respect inside and outside the classroom (guide evidence) Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
