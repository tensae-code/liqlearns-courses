# Video script — Famine, and how it differs from drought

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Famine** is when food and water run short for people and animals.
But drought alone is **not** famine — a full **grain store** can carry a village through.
Low production and war can starve a land too. Stores, production, peace: famine is fought on all three.
