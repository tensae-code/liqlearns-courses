# Hands-on — Famine, and how it differs from drought

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The bridge of stores
Look at your household's stored food — grain, dried goods. Talk with your family: how many weeks is the bridge? Stores are famine-fighting you can see.

## Production talk
Ask a farmer what 'improved crops and animals' means on their farm — better seed? a stronger breed? The book's second root, in a real voice.
