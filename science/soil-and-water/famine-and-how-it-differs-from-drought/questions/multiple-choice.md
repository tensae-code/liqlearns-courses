# Famine, and how it differs from drought — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Famine is when —**
a) one meal is late  b) food and water for animals and people run short as drought spreads  c) the market closes for a day
**Answer: b) food and water for animals and people run short as drought spreads**

**2. Drought alone does not mean famine because —**
a) drought is pleasant  b) stored food reserves can carry a community through  c) rain always returns in a week
**Answer: b) stored food reserves can carry a community through**

**3. Which is one of famine's other roots in the book?**
a) modern farming not spreading  b) too many grain stores  c) terraced fields
**Answer: a) modern farming not spreading**

**4. Political instability and ___ are named among famine's roots.**
a) civil war  b) festivals  c) rains
**Answer: a) civil war**
