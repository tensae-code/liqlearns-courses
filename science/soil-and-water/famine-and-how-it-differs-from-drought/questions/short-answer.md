# Famine, and how it differs from drought — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. Name famine's three other roots from the book.**
**Answer: not producing enough with improved crops and animals; modern farming not spreading; political instability and civil war**
**2. Name three indigenous coping methods.**
**Answer: Any three of: fast/drought-resistant crops; market links; small animals, bees and fodder plots; community resource management and planting; rainwater harvesting; caring for waters; sparing varied irrigation; fuel-saving stoves; hearing forecasts.**
