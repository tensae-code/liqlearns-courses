# Chemical fertilisers and what each one does — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Chemical (inorganic) fertilisers by the nutrient they give: nitrogen fertilisers (urea, ammonium sulphate, ammonium nitrate — fast leafy growth; lack yellows the leaves and shrinks flower, fruit and seed), phosphatic fertilisers (ammonium phosphate, superphosphates — root growth and early, full grain; lack weakens roots and delays ripening), and potassic fertilisers (potassium chloride, sulphate, nitrate and carbonate, and ash — strong stems and roots and disease resistance; lack shrinks and sheds leaves), opening with the book's own story of a farmer whose maize failed until a soil test showed each part of his field lacked a different element.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science and Technology Grade 9 (Nepali, revised 2082)*, printed pp. 292–296.**
