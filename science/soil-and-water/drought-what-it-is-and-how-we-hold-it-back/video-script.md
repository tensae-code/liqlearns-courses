# Video script — Drought: what it is and how we hold it back

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

**Drought**: the rain fails a long time, and the water dries up.
It comes with a broken climate — and it follows the cutting of forests.
Terraces, trees, harvested water, mulch: the land can be held. Work is the answer.
