# Lesson plan — Drought: what it is and how we hold it back

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Soil and Water · **Lesson:** Drought: what it is and how we hold it back
**Place in the curriculum:** Soil and Water — when the water fails: the long dry harm, and the works that answer it (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** drought is when rain fails for a long time and water sources dry up — caused chiefly by an irregular climate, and also following deforestation — and it is held back by forest care, tree planting, terraces and water harvesting, stopping free grazing, and, in dry-prone places, drought-resisting trees, low-water plants and mulch
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Water: why every living thing needs it — the uses of water named, now its absence faced.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what drought is: rain fails for a long time in an area and water sources dry up.
2. Say its main cause — an irregular climate — and that it can also follow deforestation.
3. Name the holding-back works: forest care, planting many trees for every one cut, soil and stone terraces, gabion works, water harvesting, stopping free grazing.
4. Name the dry-prone place rules: drought-resisting trees, plants that use little water, and mulch to cut evaporation.

## What you need
A dry season memory; a terraced hillside or the picture to study.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls one dry month a drought and panics — or one rain a drought ended → the book's marks are length: rain failed a LONG time, sources dried; and the works are long works too.
- Thinks drought is only weather, nothing people do matters → the book names deforestation among its causes and a whole page of human works against it.
