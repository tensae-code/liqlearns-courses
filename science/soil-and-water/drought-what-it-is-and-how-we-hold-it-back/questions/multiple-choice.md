# Drought: what it is and how we hold it back — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Drought is —**
a) one hot day  b) when rain fails for a long time and water sources dry up  c) a kind of flood
**Answer: b) when rain fails for a long time and water sources dry up**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The main cause of drought is —**
a) an irregular climate  b) too many trees  c) cold weather
**Answer: a) an irregular climate**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Drought can also follow —**
a) planting trees  b) deforestation  c) terracing
**Answer: b) deforestation**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Which is one of the book's works against drought?**
a) soil and stone terraces  b) free grazing everywhere  c) cutting the forest
**Answer: a) soil and stone terraces**
