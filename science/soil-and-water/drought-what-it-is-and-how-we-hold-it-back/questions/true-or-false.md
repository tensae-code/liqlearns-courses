# Drought: what it is and how we hold it back — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Drought's harm lasts a long time, striking people, plants, animals and the land.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Mulch covers the ground to cut evaporation in high heat.**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The book's rule is to plant one tree for every many cut.**
**Answer: False**
**4. The Rift Valley's low, hot floor leaves bordering areas drought-prone.**
**Answer: True**

**5. Ethiopia's forest cover has passed 10% through conservation work.**
**Answer: True**
