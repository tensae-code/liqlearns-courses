# Hands-on — Drought: what it is and how we hold it back

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Mulch trial
Two patches of ground, one mulched with straw and remains, one bare. Feel them after a hot afternoon — the mulched one keeps its moisture, the book's rule under your fingers.

## Water harvesting at home
Where does rain run off your roof and yard? With your family, count the places a jar or a trench could catch it. Harvesting begins as a looking.
