# Video script — Ethiopia's rivers and lakes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ethiopia — **Africa's water tower**.
Three systems: **west to the Nile**, **south-east to the ocean**, **inland to the Rift**.
**Tana** the largest lake. **Shala** the deepest. **Ziway** the shallowest.
And the wealth sleeps — for **policy**, for **backwardness**, under **outside pressure**.
