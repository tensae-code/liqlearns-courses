# Ethiopia's rivers and lakes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The western drainage system joins to become —**
a) the Indian Ocean  b) the Nile in Sudan  c) Lake Tana
**Answer: b) the Nile in Sudan**

**2. The Awash belongs to the —**
a) western system  b) south-eastern system  c) inland (Rift Valley) system
**Answer: c) inland (Rift Valley) system**

**3. Ethiopia's largest lake is —**
a) Ziway  b) Tana  c) Shala
**Answer: b) Tana**

**4. The deepest Rift lake named is —**
a) Shala  b) Ziway  c) Langano
**Answer: a) Shala**

**5. The GERD reservoir is a —**
a) highland lake  b) Rift lake  c) man-made lake
**Answer: c) man-made lake**

**6. Which is NOT one of the three under-use reason-groups?**
a) policy and strategy  b) backwardness  c) too much rain
**Answer: c) too much rain**
