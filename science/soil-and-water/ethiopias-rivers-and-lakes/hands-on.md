# Hands-on — Ethiopia's rivers and lakes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three colours on the map
On a big outline map, trace the three systems in three colours — west to the Nile, south-east to the ocean, inland to the Rift lakes — the teacher guide's own map work, done at pupil scale.

## Lake cards
Make a card per named lake: name, kind, one fact. Shuffle and sort them into the three kinds against the clock; the superlative lakes earn a star on their cards.
