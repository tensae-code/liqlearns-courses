# Desalination — making seawater drinkable — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Seawater holds salt — the problem by the sea.
**Answer:** Sea water is full of **dissolved salt**.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Desalination — taking the salt out.
**Answer:** **Desalination** is getting fresh water from seawater: separating the water from the salt it holds, so what remains can be drunk and used like any fresh water.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Seawater holds salt — the problem by the sea → Desalination — taking the salt out — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
