# Hands-on — Desalination — making seawater drinkable

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Taste the difference — without drinking the sea
Put a pinch of salt in a cup of water and stir until it disappears. Say why you can no longer see it, and why a cloth filter would not take it out.

## Design a Water by the Sea answer
Draw your own answer to Egypt's project: a way for a family living by the sea to get a cup of fresh water from seawater. Label where the salt ends up.
