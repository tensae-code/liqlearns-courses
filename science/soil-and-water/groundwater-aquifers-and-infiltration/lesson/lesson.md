# Groundwater, aquifers and infiltration

## Sub lesson 1 — Groundwater

Groundwater: testing the school soil's infiltration capacity; the unsaturated zone (pores partly full) and saturated zone below; El Salvador's aquifers and the well water much of the country drinks; protecting a resource sensitive to overuse and contamination.

## Sub lesson 2 — The infiltration race

The infiltration race: materials ranked from fastest to non-infiltrating, an infiltration-capacity graph over 50 minutes, an aquifer model showing the clay layer's role, texture vs permeability and porosity, El Salvador's highest-production aquifers and the aquifer under your own home; your water footprint calculated.

## Sub lesson 3 — An aquifer built in a jar (stones, sand, soil and a dispenser for a well)

An aquifer built in a jar (stones, sand, soil and a dispenser for a well): pumping the well dry in a drought simulation, then raining on the model to refill it; the permeable zone, the saturated zone and the impermeable rock below; cenotes as natural groundwater windows.
