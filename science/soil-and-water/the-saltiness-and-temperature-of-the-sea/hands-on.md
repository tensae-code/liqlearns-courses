# Hands-on — The saltiness and temperature of the sea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Taste with instruments
If you can, compare rainwater, tap water and seawater by evaporation: one spoonful of each dried on a dark plate — which leaves a salt crust, and how much?

## Read a lagoon graph
Sketch a two-line graph — temperature and salinity with depth — from the lesson's numbers: warm 26–28°C at the top, sunlight to about 60 metres, cold below.
