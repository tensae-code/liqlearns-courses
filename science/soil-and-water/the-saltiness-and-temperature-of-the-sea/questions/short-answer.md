# The saltiness and temperature of the sea — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Salinity — how salty, and why it changes.
**Answer:** Salinity is the sea's dissolved load: the ocean is 96.5% water and 3.5% dissolved solids, measured in parts per thousand, and the dissolved part is a mix of common ions.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Ocean temperature — warm top, cold deep.
**Answer:** Around Samoa the sea sits at 26–28°C all year.
