Today: **Water as a coolant** — Sri Lanka's way, from the books cited in the sources.

A coolant is a liquid that takes heat away from something hot and carries it off, so the hot thing stays at a safe temperature.

In an engine, moving parts and burning fuel make heat faster than the engine can lose it.

The body uses water the same way: blood — mostly water — carries heat from the working inside of the body to the skin, and sweat, which is water, takes heat away as it evaporates.
