# Watersheds and wetlands of Nepal — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Water gathered from ponds to great rivers — Jagdishpur, Ghodaghodi, Bishajari, Gosaikunda and Rara — and land covered with water all year, like the Koshi Tappu, She-Phoksundo and the Pokhara valley lakes; and why they matter: food, homes and breeding grounds for water plants and animals, birds nesting and sheltering, rainwater stored, temperature balanced and the water cycle kept turning.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science and Technology Grade 7*, printed pp. 86–87.**
