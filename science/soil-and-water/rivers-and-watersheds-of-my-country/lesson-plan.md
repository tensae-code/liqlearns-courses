# Lesson plan — Rivers and watersheds of my country

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Soil and Water · **Lesson:** Rivers and watersheds of my country
**Place in the curriculum:** Soil and Water — appended to Unit 7 (Soil and Water) (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A watershed (cuenca hidrográfica) is the territory at the sides of a river's course — mountains, valleys, plateaus and gorges; the book maps Honduras's watersheds (Ulúa, Patuca, Lempa, Choluteca, Goascorán, Nacaome, Aguán, Plátano and more)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

2. **Lesson 15** — Lesson 15: the Ulúa river — born in the mountains of Intibucá as the Río Grande de Otoro, some 400 km through Santa Bárbara, Cortés, Yoro and Atlántida, fed by the Higuito, Mejocote, Lindo, Jicatuyo, Humuya and Sulaco, irrigating the Sula valley's banana, sugar cane, plantain, African palm, citrus, pasture and basic grains.
