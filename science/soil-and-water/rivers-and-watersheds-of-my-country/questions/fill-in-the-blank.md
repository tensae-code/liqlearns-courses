# Rivers and watersheds of my country — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** Honduras's *Ciencias Naturales* books teach this lesson's one thing: ______.
**Answer: rivers and watersheds of my country**

**2.** The first part of this lesson is about ______.
**Answer: a watershed (cuenca hidrográfica) is the territory at the sides of a river's course**

**3.** This lesson's sources name the Honduras book ______.
**Answer: HN CN3**
