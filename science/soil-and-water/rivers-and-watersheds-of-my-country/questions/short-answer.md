# Rivers and watersheds of my country — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Honduras's books teach about a watershed (cuenca hidrográfica) is the territory at the sides of a river's course?
**Answer: A watershed (cuenca hidrográfica) is the territory at the sides of a river's course — mountains, valleys, plateaus and gorges; the book maps Honduras's watersheds (Ulúa, Patuca, Lempa, Choluteca, Goascorán, Nacaome, Aguán, Plátano and more)**

**2.** What do Honduras's books teach about lesson 15?
**Answer: Lesson 15: the Ulúa river — born in the mountains of Intibucá as the Río Grande de Otoro, some 400 km through Santa Bárbara, Cortés, Yoro and Atlántida, fed by the Higuito, Mejocote, Lindo, Jicatuyo, Humuya and Sulaco, irrigating the Sula valley's banana, sugar cane, plantain, African palm, citrus, pasture and basic grains.**

**3.** Name this lesson's one thing.
**Answer: Rivers and watersheds of my country.**
