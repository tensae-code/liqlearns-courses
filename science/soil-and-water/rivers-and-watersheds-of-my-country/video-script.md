Today: **Rivers and watersheds of my country** — Honduras's way.

A watershed (cuenca hidrográfica) is the territory at the sides of a river's course — mountains, valleys, plateaus and gorges; the book maps Honduras's watersheds (Ulúa, Patuca, Lempa, Choluteca, Goascorán, Nacaome, Aguán, Plátano and more) Lesson 15: the Ulúa river — born in the mountains of Intibucá as the Río Grande de Otoro, some 400 km through Santa Bárbara, Cortés, Yoro and Atlántida, fed by the Higuito, Mejocote, Lindo, Jicatuyo, Humuya and Sulaco, irrigating the Sula valley's banana, sugar cane, plantain, African palm, citrus, pasture and basic grains.
