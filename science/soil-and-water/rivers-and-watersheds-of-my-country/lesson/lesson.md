# Rivers and watersheds of my country

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's subject — rivers and watersheds of my country. Then read on: this is Honduras's way in — its *Ciencias Naturales* books teach this thing at the places named in Sources, in the order below.

## Sub lesson 1 — A watershed (cuenca hidrográfica) is the territory at the sides of a river's course

A watershed (cuenca hidrográfica) is the territory at the sides of a river's course — mountains, valleys, plateaus and gorges; the book maps Honduras's watersheds (Ulúa, Patuca, Lempa, Choluteca, Goascorán, Nacaome, Aguán, Plátano and more)

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 15

Lesson 15: the Ulúa river — born in the mountains of Intibucá as the Río Grande de Otoro, some 400 km through Santa Bárbara, Cortés, Yoro and Atlántida, fed by the Higuito, Mejocote, Lindo, Jicatuyo, Humuya and Sulaco, irrigating the Sula valley's banana, sugar cane, plantain, African palm, citrus, pasture and basic grains.
