# Hands-on — Rivers and watersheds of my country

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — A watershed (cuenca hidrográfica) is the territory at the sides of a river's course. A watershed (cuenca hidrográfica) is the territory at the sides of a river's course — mountains, valleys, plateaus and gorges; the book maps Honduras's watersheds (Ulúa, Patuca, Lempa, Choluteca, Goascorán, Nacaome, Aguán, Plátano and more) Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
