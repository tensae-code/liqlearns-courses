# Sources of water around us

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's one thing — sources of water — where the water around us comes from, as Zimbabwe's guides teach the topic from ECD level up. Say what you already know about it out loud. Then read on: this is Zimbabwe's way in — its PlusOne Science and Technology guides teach this topic as a unit of their own, returning to it across the grades named in this lesson's sources.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sources of water

Zimbabwe's ECD A guide and its Grade 3 guide both teach **sources of water** as a named topic: the water people and animals use comes from somewhere, and the sources can be named and visited.

## Sub lesson 2 — The sources around us

The topic is local: the sources **around us** — the ones a learner can point to near home and school — are the lesson's ground, taught before any use of water is studied.

## Sub lesson 3 — Knowing a source means caring for it

A source you can name is a source you can look after. The guides place sources of water inside their water and soil topics, so knowing the source and caring for it are taught together.

## The key ideas

- **Sources of water** — water comes from named sources.
- **The sources around us** — the sources near home and school.
- **Knowing a source means caring for it** — named sources can be cared for.
