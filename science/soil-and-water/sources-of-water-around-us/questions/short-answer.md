# Sources of water around us — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Sources of water" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's ECD A guide and its Grade 3 guide both teach **sources of water** as a named topic: the water people and animals use comes from somewhere, and the sources can be named and visited.

**2.** What does the part "The sources around us" teach?
**Answer:** The topic is local: the sources **around us** — the ones a learner can point to near home and school — are the lesson's ground, taught before any use of water is studied.

**3.** What does the part "Knowing a source means caring for it" teach?
**Answer:** A source you can name is a source you can look after.
