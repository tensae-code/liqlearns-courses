# Sources of water around us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) The sources around us  b) Knowing a source means caring for it  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Sources of water
**Answer: d) Sources of water**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about water comes from named sources?
a) Sources of water  b) The sources around us  c) Knowing a source means caring for it
**Answer: a) Sources of water**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about the sources near home and school?
a) Sources of water  b) The sources around us  c) Knowing a source means caring for it
**Answer: b) The sources around us**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about named sources can be cared for?
a) Sources of water  b) The sources around us  c) Knowing a source means caring for it
**Answer: c) Knowing a source means caring for it**
