# Community water supply and rural aqueducts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Rural aqueducts — a community's own water system.
**Answer:** A **rural aqueduct** is a water system that brings **drinking water** to a rural community: water is taken from a source, carried through pipes, and supplied to the homes of the community it serves.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Run under official rules — Costa Rica's own way.
**Answer:** Costa Rica teaches its rural aqueducts as systems with **official rules**: the ASADAS supply community drinking water under the country's own regulations, so the taking, carrying and sharing of the water is organised and answerable, not left to chance.
