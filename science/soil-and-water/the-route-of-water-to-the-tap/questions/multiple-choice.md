# The route of water to the tap — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Water reaches the tap through…
a) wires  b) pipes, tanks and pumps  c) roads  d) clouds only  
**Answer: b) pipes, tanks and pumps**

**2.** Water used in a home is called…
a) industrial consumption  b) rain  c) domestic consumption  d) stored water  
**Answer: c) domestic consumption**

**3.** A pump's job on the route is to…
a) clean the water  b) lift or move the water  c) colour the water  d) store the water  
**Answer: b) lift or move the water**
