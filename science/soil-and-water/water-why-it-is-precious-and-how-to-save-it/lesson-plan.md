# Lesson plan — Water — why it is precious and how to save it

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Soil and Water · **Lesson:** Water — why it is precious and how to save it
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Ecosystem and Conservation of Natural Resources, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** water covers 71% of the Earth's surface yet over 96% of it is saline, and the human body is about 60% water — so the fresh water living things can use is a small share, saved by a six-part practice: prevent pollution, reuse wastewater, plug leaks, harvest rainwater, dam and store, use irrigation water efficiently, protect groundwater, and plant trees.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Water — why every living thing needs it (this title), where water's uses were named one by one, and on Cycles — the water cycle (Our Environment): the loop is known; this Grade 8 lesson counts the usable share and teaches the saving practice.

## Sub lessons
1. **How much water — and how little of it we can use** — 71% cover, over 96% saline; the body about 60% water.
2. **Saving it — the conservation practice** — the book's conservation list: homes, farms and the land itself.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Water — why every living thing needs it (Soil and Water)
- Recommended first: Cycles — the water cycle (Our Environment) — recommended, never forced.
## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what share of Earth's water is saline, and what share of the body is water.
2. Explain why fresh water is precious even on a water-covered planet.
3. Name the conservation practices and pick the ones a home and a farm can each do.
## What you need
A jar of water and a small cup to show the usable share; the home's taps, jars and roof to audit.
