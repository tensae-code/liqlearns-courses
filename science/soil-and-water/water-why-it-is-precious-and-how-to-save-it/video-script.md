# Video script — Water — why it is precious and how to save it

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Earth looks **blue** — water covers most of it.
But almost all of it is **salty**. We cannot drink the sea.
Your body is **mostly water** — about sixty in a hundred.
So guard the **fresh** water: keep it clean, **use it twice**, fix the leak.
Catch the rain. Store it. Water crops **carefully**.
Plant trees. The small cup must serve everyone.
