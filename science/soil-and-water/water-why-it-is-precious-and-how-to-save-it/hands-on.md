# Hands-on — Water — why it is precious and how to save it

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Home water audit
Walk your home as an inspector with three questions: Is any tap or jar leaking? Is any used water thrown away that could water a plant? Does rain run off the roof uncaught? Write one fix for each fault you find, from the lesson's practice list.

## The small cup
Fill a big jar with water and colour it with a pinch of salt stirred in — the seas. Now take a small cup of clean water beside it — all the fresh water. Say in one sentence why the cup, not the jar, is what conservation guards.
