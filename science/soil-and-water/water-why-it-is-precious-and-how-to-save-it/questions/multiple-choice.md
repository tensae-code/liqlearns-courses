# Water — why it is precious and how to save it — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Water covers about what share of the Earth's surface? 
a) 10%  b) 40%  c) 71%  d) 96%
**Answer: c) 71%**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Over 96% of Earth's water is —
a) fresh  b) saline (salty)  c) underground  d) ice
**Answer: b) saline (salty)** — that is why the usable share is small.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The human body is about what share water? 
a) 10%  b) 30%  c) 60%  d) 96%
**Answer: c) 60%**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Reusing wastewater is a conservation practice because —
a) it makes new rain  b) water used twice is water not drawn fresh again  c) it cleans the sea  d) it stops leaks
**Answer: b) water used twice is water not drawn fresh again**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Harvesting rainwater means —
a) waiting for floods  b) catching roof or surface rain and storing it for dry times  c) boiling sea water  d) digging only in rivers
**Answer: b) catching roof or surface rain and storing it for dry times**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Planting trees helps water conservation because trees —
a) drink all the water  b) hold water in the land and help springs and rain  c) stop all rain  d) make water salty
**Answer: b) hold water in the land and help springs and rain**
