# Water — why it is precious and how to save it — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain in two lines why water is precious even though it covers 71% of the surface.
**Answer:** Over 96% is saline and unusable as it is, so the fresh share living things can use is small — while every body and crop depends on it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three conservation practices a home can do.
**Answer:** Any three of: prevent pollution, reuse wastewater, plug leaks, harvest rainwater (and store it).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two conservation practices at farm or community scale.
**Answer:** Any two of: dam and store water, use irrigation water efficiently, protect groundwater, plant trees, prevent pollution.
