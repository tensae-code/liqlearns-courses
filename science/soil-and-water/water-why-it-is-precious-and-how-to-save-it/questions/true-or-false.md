# Water — why it is precious and how to save it — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Because Earth is mostly covered in water, fresh water is plentiful for everyone.
**Answer: False** — Over 96% is saline; the usable fresh share is small.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Plugging a leaking tap is a real conservation practice.
**Answer: True** — Water never dripping away is water saved.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Groundwater needs no protection because it is underground.
**Answer: False** — Groundwater is the dry-season reserve and must be protected from waste and pollution.
