# Water problems: sharing, pollution and the lake weed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Problem 1 is about transboundary rivers with —**
a) too many boats  b) no agreement between the countries  c) no rain
**Answer: b) no agreement between the countries**

**2. Ethiopia's printed position on using its water is —**
a) silence  b) equality and fairness  c) giving it all away
**Answer: b) equality and fairness**

**3. Which is NOT a pollution source the book names?**
a) industrial chemical waste  b) farm pesticide chemicals  c) moonlight
**Answer: c) moonlight**

**4. Siltation does what to the water itself?**
a) cleans it  b) shrinks it  c) freezes it
**Answer: b) shrinks it**

**5. The water hyacinth harms a lake by —**
a) blocking sunlight and starving oxygen  b) deepening it  c) feeding the fish
**Answer: a) blocking sunlight and starving oxygen**

**6. The hyacinth is a named problem on —**
a) Lake Tana and Lake Victoria  b) only desert ponds  c) no real lake
**Answer: a) Lake Tana and Lake Victoria**
