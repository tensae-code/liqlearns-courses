# Hands-on — Water problems: sharing, pollution and the lake weed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Clean above, dirty below
Draw one river in two reaches — above the town, below it — labelling every pollutant the book names where it enters. The drawing is the lesson's field report in miniature.

## The debate, staged
Stage the book's Nile discussion as a formal class debate with a chair, timed speeches and the printed positions only — equality and fairness against the downstream obstruction, as the book frames them.
