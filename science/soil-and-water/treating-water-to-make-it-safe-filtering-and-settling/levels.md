# Treating water to make it safe — filtering and settling — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner runs the whole treatment chain in order — settling muddy water undisturbed and pouring the clear layer off gently, filtering it slowly through cloth or a gravel-and-sand filter, and storing it covered — and states the chain's honest limit (dirt removed, germs only partly; boiling surest), answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — doing the lesson's own experiments and sorts by hand, naming each idea aloud, and reasoning about new examples until the teaching is the learner's own.
