Today: **Treating water to make it safe**.

Muddy water looks undrinkable — and mostly it is, until you treat it. Gabon's way is a chain of three. First, settling: leave the water perfectly still, and gravity pulls the heavy dirt down to the bottom all by itself; pour the clear top off gently. Second, filtering: pour that water slowly through clean cloth, sand and gravel — the water passes, the fine dirt stays trapped. Third, keep it safe: a clean, covered container and a clean cup, because treated water dipped with dirty hands is dirty again. And remember the honest end: clear is cleaner, not germ-proof — boiling is the surest last step.
