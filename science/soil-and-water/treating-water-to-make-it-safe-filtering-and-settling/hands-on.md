# Hands-on — Treating water to make it safe — filtering and settling

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The five-minute settle
Fill a clear bottle with muddy water, mark the dirt layer's height with a rubber band at the start, and again every five minutes without touching the bottle. Watch the settled layer grow and the clear layer deepen. Say aloud: nobody filtered anything — gravity did this work for free.

## Cloth against sand
Filter two identical cups of settled water — one through a single cloth, one through the gravel-and-sand bottle filter. Hold both results up to the light. Say aloud: the finer, slower trap catches more.
