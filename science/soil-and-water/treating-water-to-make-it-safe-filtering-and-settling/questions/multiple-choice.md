# Treating water to make it safe — filtering and settling — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Settling cleans muddy water because…
a) The dirt dissolves  b) The heavy dirt grains sink to the bottom when the water is left still  c) The water evaporates  d) Shaking mixes the dirt away
**Answer: b) The heavy dirt grains sink to the bottom when the water is left still**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Settling works only if the water is…
a) Boiled first  b) Shaken often  c) Left undisturbed  d) Frozen
**Answer: c) Left undisturbed**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A filter works by…
a) Killing all germs  b) Letting water pass while trapping dirt in its layers or cloth  c) Changing water into rain  d) Cooling the water
**Answer: b) Letting water pass while trapping dirt in its layers or cloth**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In the bottle filter, the layers from bottom to top are…
a) Cloth, sand, gravel  b) Sand, cloth, gravel  c) Gravel, then clean sand, then cloth on top  d) Gravel only
**Answer: c) Gravel, then clean sand, then cloth on top**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Treated water should be stored…
a) In an open bucket  b) In a clean, covered container, drawn out with a clean cup  c) Anywhere — treatment is permanent  d) In the sun
**Answer: b) In a clean, covered container, drawn out with a clean cup**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The surest final home treatment, after filtering, is…
a) More settling  b) Boiling the water  c) Adding sand  d) Letting it stand a week
**Answer: b) Boiling the water**
