# Treating water to make it safe — filtering and settling — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Leaving muddy water perfectly still so its dirt sinks is called _____.
**Answer: settling**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Water poured through cloth, sand and gravel is being _____.
**Answer: filtered**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Treated water is stored in a clean container with a _____ so dust and flies stay out.
**Answer: cover**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The surest final treatment that kills germs is _____.
**Answer: boiling**
