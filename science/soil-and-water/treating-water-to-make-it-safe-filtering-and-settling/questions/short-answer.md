# Treating water to make it safe — filtering and settling — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why must the settled water be poured off gently?
**Answer: Pouring gently — tilting slowly — leaves the settled dirt layer behind at the bottom. A rough pour stirs the layer back up and undoes the settling.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does a dirty filter stop being a help?
**Answer: A filter choked with old dirt can no longer trap new dirt, and the old dirt washes into the water instead — the filter starts adding dirt. Cloth must be washed and sand replaced.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the two rules that make filtering work well.
**Answer: Pour slowly, so the water does not shove dirt through the filter's holes; and keep the filter clean, washing the cloth and replacing the sand.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Filtered water looks perfectly clear. Why does the lesson still recommend boiling it where possible?
**Answer: Filtering removes dirt, but the smallest germs are too tiny to see and can pass through a home filter. Boiling kills the germs the filter missed — it is the surest final step.**
