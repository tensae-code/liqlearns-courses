# Groundwater and springs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Groundwater is…
a) River water at night  b) Water collected underground, in soil and rock  c) Water in clouds  d) Only well water
**Answer: b) Water collected underground, in soil and rock**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Groundwater begins as…
a) Sea water rising  b) Ice melting on mountains  c) Rain soaking into the ground  d) Water made by roots
**Answer: c) Rain soaking into the ground**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Underground, groundwater moves…
a) Faster than rivers  b) Not at all, ever  c) Upwards always  d) Only very slowly
**Answer: d) Only very slowly**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A spring is…
a) Groundwater coming out where the surface cuts into it  b) A fountain built in a park  c) Rain falling in spring season  d) A deep well only
**Answer: a) Groundwater coming out where the surface cuts into it**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Artesian water rises because…
a) It is pumped by roots  b) It lies under pressure between layers  c) The Sun pulls it  d) It is lighter than air
**Answer: b) It lies under pressure between layers**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Groundwater matters to surface waters because it…
a) Never reaches them  b) Replaces rain entirely  c) Feeds them from below  d) Flows only in deserts
**Answer: c) Feeds them from below**
