# Groundwater and springs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How does rain become groundwater?
**Answer:** Part of the rain soaks into the ground and collects in the spaces of soil and rock.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is groundwater called the land's hidden store?
**Answer:** It lies underground, out of sight, held in the ground itself.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the difference between a spring and an artesian well?
**Answer:** A spring is groundwater coming out by itself where the surface cuts into it; an artesian well is bored into pressurised groundwater, which then rises on its own.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the two doors by which groundwater reaches people and rivers.
**Answer:** Springs and artesian wells.
