# Groundwater and springs — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Water collected underground in soil and rock is _____.
**Answer: groundwater**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Groundwater begins as rain _____ into the ground.
**Answer: soaking**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Groundwater coming out by itself at the surface is a _____.
**Answer: spring**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Water that rises under pressure from a bored well is _____ water.
**Answer: artesian**
