# Video script — Groundwater and springs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Groundwater and springs** — Armenia's way: the water under the ground.

Rain soaks in. Underground, it collects — groundwater, the land's hidden store.

Where the surface cuts into it, out it comes: a spring. Held under pressure, it rises in an artesian well.

Now you. Tell where groundwater comes from and how it comes back out. Say it back — in your own words.
