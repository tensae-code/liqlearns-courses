# Hands-on — Groundwater and springs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch water soak in
Pour a measured cup of water slowly onto bare soil and onto a paved surface. Where did each cup go? The soil's share is on its way to becoming groundwater.

## Make a spring
Fill a sponge with water and set it on a slope of stones in a tray. Press gently upslope: where the water emerges is your spring — say why it comes out there.
