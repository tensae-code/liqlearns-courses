# Video script — Swamps and glaciers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Swamps and glaciers** — Armenia's way: the land's two slow water stores.

A swamp is land where water stands — not a lake, not dry ground, but the wet ground between.

A glacier is a great mass of ice, holding fresh water frozen, and feeding rivers as it melts.

One store holds water wet. The other holds it frozen.

Now you. Tell the difference between a swamp and a glacier. Say it back — in your own words.
