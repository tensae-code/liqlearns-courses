# Swamps and glaciers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is a swamp neither a lake nor dry land?
**Answer:** Its water stands in the ground itself — the land is soaked and wet, not covered by open water, and never properly dry.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What is a swamp's work in the land's waters?
**Answer:** It holds water back and releases it slowly.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How does a glacier form, in the lesson's words?
**Answer:** From snow pressed over long years into a great mass of ice.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) State the significance the lesson gives to both.
**Answer:** Both are slow stores of the land's water — the swamp holding it wet, the glacier frozen — feeding the waters around them over time.
