# Swamps and glaciers — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Land where water stands and soaks the ground is a _____.
**Answer: swamp**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A swamp releases the water it holds _____.
**Answer: slowly**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A great mass of ice built from pressed snow is a _____.
**Answer: glacier**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A glacier holds fresh water _____ in place.
**Answer: frozen**
