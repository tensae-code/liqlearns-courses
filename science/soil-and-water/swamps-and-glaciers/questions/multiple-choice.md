# Swamps and glaciers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A swamp is…
a) A deep lake  b) Land where water stands and soaks the ground  c) A fast river  d) Dry ground after rain
**Answer: b) Land where water stands and soaks the ground**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Swamp plants and animals are…
a) All fish  b) Unable to live in water  c) Fitted to standing water  d) The same as desert plants
**Answer: c) Fitted to standing water**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A swamp holds water back and…
a) Boils it away  b) Turns it to ice  c) Sends it uphill  d) Releases it slowly
**Answer: d) Releases it slowly**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A glacier is…
a) A great mass of ice on the land, built from pressed snow  b) A frozen lake surface  c) Winter frost  d) Snow that melts in a day
**Answer: a) A great mass of ice on the land, built from pressed snow**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Glaciers hold…
a) Salt water  b) Fresh water, frozen in place  c) Only air  d) No water at all
**Answer: b) Fresh water, frozen in place**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Swamp and glacier are alike because both…
a) Are rivers  b) Are dry places  c) Store the land's water and give it back slowly  d) Never change
**Answer: c) Store the land's water and give it back slowly**
