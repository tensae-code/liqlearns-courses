# Hands-on — Swamps and glaciers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A swamp in a tray
Fill a tray with soil, pour in water until it stands in the soil, and plant nothing — just watch for days: what stays wet, what grows, what visits? Say what makes your tray a swamp and not a lake.

## The glacier store
Freeze a large block of ice and set it in the sun over a bowl. Time how long its meltwater keeps coming. That slow giving-back is the glacier's work for a river.
