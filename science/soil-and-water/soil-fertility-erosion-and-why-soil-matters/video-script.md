# Video script — Soil fertility, erosion, and why soil matters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Six things decide if soil is **fertile** — rock, chemicals, land shape, climate, remains, tiny life.
**Fine** soil is in danger: rain runs off it, wind lifts it. **Coarse** soil drinks the rain and stands.
No **soil** — no plants. No plants — no animals. No animals — no food.
