# Hands-on — Soil fertility, erosion, and why soil matters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The agriculture worker interview
The book's fieldwork: prepare three questions — what makes our soil fertile, what carries it away, what do farmers do about it — interview, write the answers, report.

## Gully walk
After rain, walk a slope with an adult: where did the water run, where did soil move, where did grass hold the ground? Erosion leaves a diary after every storm.
