# Soil fertility, erosion, and why soil matters — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. Name three things that decide fertility.**
**Answer: parent rock, chemical content, land shape, climate, amount of remains, small living things (any three)**
**2. Name the six methods for holding soil.**
**Answer: Afforestation, reforestation, contour ploughing, agroforestry, terracing, and stone-and-soil bunds.**
