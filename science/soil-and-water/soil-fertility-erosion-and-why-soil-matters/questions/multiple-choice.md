# Soil fertility, erosion, and why soil matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which of these decides a soil's fertility?**
a) parent rock  b) the day of the week  c) the colour of the farmer's shirt
**Answer: a) parent rock**

**2. Fine, smooth soil is eroded more easily because —**
a) rain cannot soak in, so floods wash it off, and its light particles blow away  b) it is heavier  c) it is darker
**Answer: a) rain cannot soak in, so floods wash it off, and its light particles blow away**

**3. Coarse soil resists erosion because —**
a) it is magic  b) it lets water in  c) it is smooth
**Answer: b) it lets water in**

**4. In the book's chain, no soil means —**
a) no plants, then no animals, then no food  b) more food  c) nothing changes
**Answer: a) no plants, then no animals, then no food**
**5. About how much soil is washed away in Ethiopia every year?**
a) 960 tonnes  b) 960 million tonnes  c) 18 tonnes
**Answer: b) 960 million tonnes**

**6. Which is NOT one of erosion's four causes?**
a) slope  b) the soil's own nature  c) the moon
**Answer: c) the moon**

**7. Contour ploughing means ploughing —**
a) down the slope  b) across the slope  c) only at night
**Answer: b) across the slope**

**8. The Konso people are praised for —**
a) check-dams of concrete  b) traditional terracing  c) deep wells
**Answer: b) traditional terracing**

**9. Reforestation is —**
a) planting where no forest stood  b) replanting land once forested  c) burning stubble
**Answer: b) replanting land once forested**
