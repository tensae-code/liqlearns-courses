# Soil fertility, erosion, and why soil matters — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: name the six fertility deciders, explain why fine soil erodes more easily, and recite the soil-to-food chain.
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to sort or name, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.

Grade 5/6 depth (added 2026-11-12): the pass mark grows — the learner also states the 960-million-tonnes emergency, erosion's four causes, and the six holding methods with their Amharic names, Konso terracing among them.
