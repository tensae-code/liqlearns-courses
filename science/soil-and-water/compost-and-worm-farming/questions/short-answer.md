# Compost and worm farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Compost — organic fertiliser built layer by layer.
**Answer:** Nicaragua's Grade 9 book teaches organic fertiliser (abono orgánico) as the way to feed the soil in ecological agriculture: build a **compost pile layer by layer**, with an **air column** through it so the material can breathe and rot down into fertiliser instead of waste.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Worm farming — vermiculture and worm humus.
**Answer:** The same book sets out **vermiculture (lombricultura)**: worm beds of **1 × 1.5 m and 60 cm high**. The Grade 9 Módulo Autoformativo adds the module's own way of producing **worm humus**: beds on **dry, well-drained ground near organic waste** — the humus is a blackish acidic substance that **improves soil structure and supplies nutrients**.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Compost — organic fertiliser built layer by layer → Worm farming — vermiculture and worm humus — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
