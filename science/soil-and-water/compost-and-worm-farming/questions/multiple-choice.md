# Compost and worm farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Compost — organic fertiliser built layer by layer  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Compost — organic fertiliser built layer by layer**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Worm farming — vermiculture and worm humus  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Worm farming — vermiculture and worm humus**

**3.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: feeding the soil with organic fertiliser — building a compost pile layer by layer, and farming worms (vermiculture) whose humus improves the soil. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**4.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Nicaragua, Grade 9 — *Ciencias Naturales, Noveno grado*, printed pp. 90–93  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Nicaragua, Grade 9 — *Ciencias Naturales, Noveno grado*, printed pp. 90–93**
