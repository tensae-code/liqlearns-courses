# Compost and worm farming

## Sub lesson 1 — Compost — organic fertiliser built layer by layer

Nicaragua's Grade 9 book teaches organic fertiliser (abono orgánico) as the way to feed the soil in ecological agriculture: build a **compost pile layer by layer**, with an **air column** through it so the material can breathe and rot down into fertiliser instead of waste.

## Sub lesson 2 — Worm farming — vermiculture and worm humus

The same book sets out **vermiculture (lombricultura)**: worm beds of **1 × 1.5 m and 60 cm high**. The Grade 9 Módulo Autoformativo adds the module's own way of producing **worm humus**: beds on **dry, well-drained ground near organic waste** — the humus is a blackish acidic substance that **improves soil structure and supplies nutrients**.
