Today: **How soil is degraded — five forms** — Honduras's way.

a degraded soil in the FAO definition presents changes in its physical, chemical and biological characteristics that affect its productivity and its capacity to support the ecosystem; main causes: deforestation and land-use change, overgrazing (trampling compacts the upper layers) and agricultural overexploitation (guide evidence) the five forms defined: erosion (washing or removal of surface layers, hydric or eolic), salinisation (accumulation of salts altering the soil's chemistry, harming soil fauna and vegetation — e.g. irrigation with brackish water), compaction (reduction of pore spaces, cutting water availability, root penetration and organism activity), contamination and desertification (climate factors with bad soil management) (guide evidence)
