# Landslides and earth slips — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Landslides and earth slips — what happens.
**Answer:** A **landslide** — an **earth slip** — is a mass of soil and rock that breaks loose from a slope and slides downhill. It can bury houses, roads and fields in minutes, and it is one of the natural disasters Sri Lanka's Grade 8 book lists for its own country.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Causes of landslides.
**Answer:** Slopes give way when the ground is **soaked by heavy rain**, when the trees and roots that held the soil are removed, and when slopes are cut or loaded by building. Water is the great loosening agent: saturated soil grows heavy and loses its grip at the same time.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Staying safe from landslides.
**Answer:** Safety begins before the slip: know the slopes that have slipped before, watch for cracks, leaning trees and muddy springs after long rain, and move away from a suspect slope in time. Sri Lanka's book teaches causes and safety as one lesson — the science is for the staying safe.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Landslides and earth slips — what happens → Causes of landslides → Staying safe from landslides — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
