Today: **Landslides and earth slips** — Sri Lanka's way, from the books cited in the sources.

A landslide — an earth slip — is a mass of soil and rock that breaks loose from a slope and slides downhill.

Slopes give way when the ground is soaked by heavy rain, when the trees and roots that held the soil are removed, and when slopes are cut or loaded by building.

Safety begins before the slip: know the slopes that have slipped before, watch for cracks, leaning trees and muddy springs after long rain, and move away from a suspect slope in time.
