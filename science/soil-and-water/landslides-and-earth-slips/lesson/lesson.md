# Landslides and earth slips

## Do it first

Then read on: this is Sri Lanka's way in — its Grade 8 Science Part 2 teaches landslides inside its natural-disasters chapter, beside drought and floods, in a hill country where earth slips are a lived hazard.

## Sub lesson 1 — Landslides and earth slips — what happens

A **landslide** — an **earth slip** — is a mass of soil and rock that breaks loose from a slope and slides downhill. It can bury houses, roads and fields in minutes, and it is one of the natural disasters Sri Lanka's Grade 8 book lists for its own country.

## Sub lesson 2 — Causes of landslides

Slopes give way when the ground is **soaked by heavy rain**, when the trees and roots that held the soil are removed, and when slopes are cut or loaded by building. Water is the great loosening agent: saturated soil grows heavy and loses its grip at the same time.

## Sub lesson 3 — Staying safe from landslides

Safety begins before the slip: know the slopes that have slipped before, watch for cracks, leaning trees and muddy springs after long rain, and move away from a suspect slope in time. Sri Lanka's book teaches causes and safety as one lesson — the science is for the staying safe.
