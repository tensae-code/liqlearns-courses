# Hands-on — Landslides and earth slips

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a slope
On a walk (with an adult), find a slope — a bank, a cutting, a hillside. Sketch it and mark: where water would soak in, what holds the soil (roots, grass, walls), and where a slip would go. Write one line on the safest and the riskiest place in your sketch, and why.
