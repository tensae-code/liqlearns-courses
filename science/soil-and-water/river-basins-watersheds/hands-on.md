# Hands-on — River basins — watersheds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Where does the rain go? (tray and water)
Tilt a wide tray or baking sheet slightly, crumple and re-flatten foil inside it to make low ridges, and pour a little water at different points. Watch which 'river' each pour joins. The ridges are basin divides: write two sentences on what a divide decides.

## Read a country by basins
On any map of Mexico that shows rivers, pick one river and shade — lightly, in pencil — the land you judge drains into it. Write under it: this is one basin; a country read by basins is read in drainage wholes, not single rivers.
