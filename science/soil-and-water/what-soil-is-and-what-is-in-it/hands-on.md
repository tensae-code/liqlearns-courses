# Hands-on — What soil is and what is in it

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The pit or the bank
With an adult's leave, dig one small hole or find a cut bank. Mark with sticks: where the dark soil starts, and where the roots end.

## Five-things hunt
In one handful of soil over white paper, hunt the five: grains (minerals), dark rotted bits (remains), a tiny creature or its hole (living things), dampness (water), and the spaces between crumbs (air).
