# Video script — What soil is and what is in it

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Soil** is the ground's top layer — as deep as **roots** reach.
Inside it: **minerals, tiny living things, remains, air and water**.
Black, red, grey, brown, white — every place's soil wears its own **colour**.
