# Lesson plan — What soil is and what is in it

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Soil and Water · **Lesson:** What soil is and what is in it
**Place in the curriculum:** Soil and Water — the title opens here: the ground itself, defined (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** soil is the layer of the ground from the surface down to where plant roots reach, and it holds minerals, tiny living things, plant and animal remains, air and water — its colour differs by place
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Natural Resources lessons — soil was named a resource there; here it is opened and looked inside. This lesson opens the Soil and Water title (CURRICULUM.md: the title opens with a later band — this is that band).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Natural Resources — Water, soil and air around us. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what soil is: the layer from the surface down to where plant roots reach.
2. List what soil holds: minerals, tiny living things, plant and animal remains, air and water.
3. Say soil's colour — black, red, grey, brown, white — differs by place and is one of its identifying marks.

## What you need
A place to dig a small hole or look at a bank where soil layers show; a handful of soil to examine.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls all ground 'soil' → the book's measure is roots: surface to root-depth is soil; the deep ground below is not.
- Thinks soil is only dirt/mineral → it holds living things and remains, air and water too — a mixture, alive.
