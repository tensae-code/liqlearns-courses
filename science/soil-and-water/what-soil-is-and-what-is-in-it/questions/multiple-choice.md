# What soil is and what is in it — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Soil is the layer of ground —**
a) from the surface down to where plant roots reach  b) all the way to the centre of the earth  c) only the top crumb
**Answer: a) from the surface down to where plant roots reach**

**2. Which of these is held in soil?**
a) tiny living things  b) stars  c) glass only
**Answer: a) tiny living things**

**3. Soil's colour —**
a) is the same everywhere  b) differs by place and identifies it  c) is always white
**Answer: b) differs by place and identifies it**

**4. Plant and animal remains in soil are —**
a) never there  b) part of what soil holds  c) poison to soil
**Answer: b) part of what soil holds**
**5. Soil is formed from weathered rock mixed with —**
a) plastic  b) decayed plant and animal remains  c) glass
**Answer: b) decayed plant and animal remains**

**6. How many ingredients make soil, as the book counts?**
a) three  b) five  c) nine
**Answer: b) five**

**7. River valleys hold —**
a) alluvial soil  b) no soil  c) only sand
**Answer: a) alluvial soil**

**8. Which is one of soil's five ingredients?**
a) gas  b) smoke  c) paint
**Answer: a) gas**
