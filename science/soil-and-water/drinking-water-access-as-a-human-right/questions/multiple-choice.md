# Drinking water — access as a human right — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about drinking water — access as a human right?
a) Access to safe drinking water is a human right — something every person is owed simply for being a person — and with the right comes a duty: water that is a right for everyone must not be wasted by anyone.
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Access to safe drinking water is a human right — something every person is owed simply for being a person — and with the right comes a duty: water that is a right for everyone must not be wasted by anyone.**

**2.** What does this lesson say about a right, not a luxury?

**Answer: b) Because no life is possible without water, access to drinking water is named a human right: every person, rich or poor, town or country, is owed enough safe water to drink and live.**
