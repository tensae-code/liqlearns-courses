# Drinking water — access as a human right — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Because no life is possible without water, access to drinking water is named a human right: every person, rich or poor, town or country, is owed enough safe water to drink and live.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The community's duty is the opposite of what this lesson teaches.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches this as stated.**

**3.** The right reaches a home through real work: sources protected, water made drinkable, pipes and cisterns maintained.
**Answer: True**

**4.** This lesson's topic plays no part in science at all.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
