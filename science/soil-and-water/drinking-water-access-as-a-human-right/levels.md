# Drinking water — access as a human right — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (access to safe drinking water is a human right — something every person is owed simply for being a person — and with the right comes a duty: water that is a right for everyone must not be wasted by anyone.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
