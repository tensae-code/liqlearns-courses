# The great lakes and the Nile — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The largest of the great lakes by area is —**
a) Tanganyika  b) Victoria  c) Malawi
**Answer: b) Victoria**

**2. The deepest great lake is —**
a) Victoria at 92 m  b) Malawi at 706 m  c) Tanganyika at 1,435 m
**Answer: c) Tanganyika at 1,435 m**

**3. The Nile's length is —**
a) 6,650 km  b) 1,700 km  c) 86 km
**Answer: a) 6,650 km**

**4. The Blue Nile rises —**
a) in Lake Victoria  b) at Gish Abbay in Ethiopia  c) in Sudan
**Answer: b) at Gish Abbay in Ethiopia**

**5. The Blue Nile carries what share of the Nile's water?**
a) 50%  b) 86%  c) 14%
**Answer: b) 86%**

**6. Uganda's named dam stands at —**
a) Koka  b) Owen Falls (Jinja)  c) Kariba
**Answer: b) Owen Falls (Jinja)**
