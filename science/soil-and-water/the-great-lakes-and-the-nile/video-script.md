# Video script — The great lakes and the Nile

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Victoria** — the widest. **Tanganyika** — the deepest.
Two births: the **Blue** from Ethiopia, the **White** from the great lake.
They meet, and one name runs to the sea: the **Nile**, the world's longest.
Ethiopia pours in **eighty-six of every hundred** — remember who the book says benefits.
