# Hands-on — The great lakes and the Nile

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-colour Nile
On the region map, draw the Blue Nile from Gish Abbay and the White Nile from Lake Victoria in two colours to their meeting at Sudan's capital, then one colour to the sea. Label the 86% where it belongs.

## Depth ladder
Build the depth ladder on the wall: 92 m (Victoria), 706 m (Malawi), 1,435 m (Tanganyika) as three paper strips to scale — the flip from the area order made visible.
