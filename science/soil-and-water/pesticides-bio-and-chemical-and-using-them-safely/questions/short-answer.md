# Pesticides — bio and chemical — and using them safely — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Insecticides: bio-insecticides made from plants insects dislike — the fermented jholmol of nettle, neem, timur and other strong-smelling leaves with cow urine and dung, sprayed diluted; ash dusted on fields; neem leaves stored with grain — against chemical insecticides made in factories (DDT, BHC, methoxychlor, aldrin, dieldrin, malathion)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science and Technology Grade 9 (Nepali, revised 2082)*, printed pp. 297–298.**
