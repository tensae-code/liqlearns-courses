# Video script — Water: why every living thing needs it

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Water** works all day: drink, cook, wash.
Inside you it **digests food** and carries waste out.
It grows plants, carries boats, raises fish — and at the **Hidase dam** it makes Ethiopia's **electricity**.
