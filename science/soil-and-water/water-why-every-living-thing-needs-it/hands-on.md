# Hands-on — Water: why every living thing needs it

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Follow the jerrycan
One day, one jerrycan: every time it is filled or emptied, write what the water did. The mind-map will fill itself in.

## Sweat and sense
After running or working, feel the sweat on your skin. That is the book's branch in your own body: water carrying waste out.
