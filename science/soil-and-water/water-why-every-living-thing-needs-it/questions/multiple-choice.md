# Water: why every living thing needs it — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Water carries waste out of the body in —**
a) sweat and urine  b) hair and nails  c) tears only
**Answer: a) sweat and urine**

**2. The Hidase (GERD) dam uses water to make —**
a) bricks  b) electricity  c) cloth
**Answer: b) electricity**

**3. Which is a use of water beyond the body?**
a) fish breeding  b) dreaming  c) thinking
**Answer: a) fish breeding**

**4. Water helps the body by —**
a) digesting food  b) growing hair overnight  c) making bones of stone
**Answer: a) digesting food**
