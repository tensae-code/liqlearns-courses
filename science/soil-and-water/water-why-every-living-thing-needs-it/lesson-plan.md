# Lesson plan — Water: why every living thing needs it

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Soil and Water · **Lesson:** Water: why every living thing needs it
**Place in the curriculum:** Soil and Water — the second half of the title: the water resource, use by use (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** water serves everything alive and everything people build: drinking, cooking and cleanliness; digesting food and carrying waste out of the body; growing plants; making Ethiopia's electricity at the Hidase (GERD) dam; transport and fish breeding
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on What soil is — the ground first, then the water that runs over and through it.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Soil and Water — What soil is and what is in it. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List water's uses for the body: drinking, cooking, cleanliness, digesting food, carrying waste out in sweat and urine.
2. List water's uses beyond the body: growing plants, making electricity, transport, fish breeding.
3. Name the great dam: the Hidase (Grand Ethiopian Renaissance) dam makes Ethiopia's electricity from water.

## What you need
A jerrycan or bucket of water at home — every use in the lesson has passed through it.

## If the learner is stuck
- Stops at drinking → the mind-map has more branches: the body alone uses water four ways, and plants, power, transport and fish stand beyond it.
- Thinks dams only store water → the Hidase dam's named work in the book is making Ethiopia's electricity.
