# Water management — dams and distribution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** An embalse is a…
a) river  b) reservoir of stored water  c) tap  d) pump  
**Answer: b) reservoir of stored water**

**2.** Controlled discharge means…
a) flooding the valley  b) releasing water in measured amounts  c) closing the dam forever  d) boiling the water  
**Answer: b) releasing water in measured amounts**

**3.** Stored water moves through the network because…
a) it is pumped uphill  b) it is stored high and water moves down  c) it evaporates  d) people carry it  
**Answer: b) it is stored high and water moves down**
