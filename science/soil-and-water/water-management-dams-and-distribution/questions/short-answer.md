# Water management — dams and distribution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What two things does water management join together?
**Answer: Storing water (reservoirs behind dams, with controlled discharge) and sharing it out (distribution networks).**

**2.** Why are Venezuela's own dams named in the lesson?
**Answer: Because the water a country manages is its own: the named dams are the real stores the lesson's reservoirs stand for.**
