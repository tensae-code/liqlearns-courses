# Water management — dams and distribution

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's title — Water management — dams and distribution. Say out loud anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on: this is Venezuela's way in — its Grade 2 *Comunidad con Ciencia* teaches how a country manages its water: the great reservoirs behind named Venezuelan dams, and the networks that carry the water to the people.

## Sub lesson 1 — Reservoirs — water held behind dams

A **reservoir** — an *embalse* — is water held back and stored behind a **dam**. The book names Venezuela's own dams: the country's great stores of water, held ready instead of running away to the sea.

## Sub lesson 2 — Controlled discharge and distribution

Stored water is let out by **controlled discharge** — released in measured amounts, when it is needed — and carried away through **distribution networks**, the joined pipes and channels that share it out. The stored water sits **high**, and water moves **down**: height is what sends it through the network to the towns below.
