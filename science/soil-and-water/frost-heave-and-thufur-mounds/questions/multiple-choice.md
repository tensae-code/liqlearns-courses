# Frost heave and þúfur mounds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which sentence gives this lesson's first key idea?
a) Þúfur are the little frost-made mounds that cover Iceland's heath ground.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Þúfur are the little frost-made mounds that cover Iceland's heath ground.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson opens by asking the learner to…
a) Memorise the key ideas first  b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation  c) Skip to the questions  d) Copy the lesson out
**Answer: b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's second key idea?
a) Moist soil expands and lifts as it freezes — proved by putting moist soil in a freezer.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Moist soil expands and lifts as it freezes — proved by putting moist soil in a freezer.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's third key idea?
a) Something from a different lesson  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) That same lifting, winter after winter, raises the þúfur in the heath.  d) None of these
**Answer: c) That same lifting, winter after winter, raises the þúfur in the heath.**
