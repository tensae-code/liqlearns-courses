# Frost heave and þúfur mounds — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Þúfur are the little frost-made mounds that cover Iceland's heath ground.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Moist soil expands and lifts as it freezes — proved by putting moist soil in a freezer.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What does the freezer experiment prove about the heath's mounds?
**Answer:** That moist soil expands and lifts when it freezes — so frost itself can raise the ground into þúfur, no digging or piling needed.
