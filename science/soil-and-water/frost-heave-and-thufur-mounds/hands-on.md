# Hands-on — Frost heave and þúfur mounds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run the freezer proof
With an adult: fill a small open container with moist soil, mark the soil's level, freeze it overnight, and measure the new level in the morning. Record the lift — the guide's own experiment.

## Mound map
From a heath visit or a clear picture, sketch the þúfur pattern: mound after mound. Write under your sketch the one-sentence cause the lesson teaches.
