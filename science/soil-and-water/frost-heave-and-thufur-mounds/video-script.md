Today: **Frost heave and þúfur mounds** — Iceland's heath chapter, from the Líf á landi guide.
The heath's little mounds are þúfur — and frost made them.
The proof: moist soil in a freezer expands and lifts as it freezes. Winter after winter, that lifting raises the mounds.
Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
