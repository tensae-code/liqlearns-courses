# Storing water safely — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Water storage — the unit's closing step" teach?
**Answer:** **Water storage** closes the unit: water that has been found, drawn and made safe must be **stored** — kept — until it is used.

**2.** What does the part "Keeping stored water safe" teach?
**Answer:** Stored water must stay safe: the keeping is part of the storing — water kept badly loses the safety the unit's purification gave it, so the store is guarded like the source and the filter before it.
