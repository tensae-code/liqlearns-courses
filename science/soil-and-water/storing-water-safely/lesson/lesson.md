# Storing water safely

## Do it first
Before you read: water is drawn, carried home — and then what? Where does your home keep its water, and what keeps that stored water good? Say your answers. Then read on: this is Rwanda's way in — its Primary 5 water unit runs from the importance of water to its purification, and closes with storing it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Water storage — the unit's closing step

**Water storage** closes the unit: water that has been found, drawn and made safe must be **stored** — kept — until it is used. Storage is taught as its own topic, the last step of the water's road from source to mouth.

## Sub lesson 2 — Keeping stored water safe

Stored water must stay safe: the keeping is part of the storing — water kept badly loses the safety the unit's purification gave it, so the store is guarded like the source and the filter before it.
