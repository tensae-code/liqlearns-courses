# Hard and soft water — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Water that lathers easily with soap is called _____ water.
**Answer: soft**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Water picks up its _____ from the rocks and soil it passes through.
**Answer: hardness**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) _____ a water sample is one way to remove its hardness.
**Answer: Boiling**
