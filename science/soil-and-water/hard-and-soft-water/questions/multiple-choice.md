# Hard and soft water — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Hard water is water that…
a) Is frozen  b) Lathers poorly with soap and can leave scum  c) Is always dirty  d) Cannot be drunk by anyone
**Answer: b) Lathers poorly with soap and can leave scum**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Water becomes hard by…
a) Being left in the sun  b) Passing through rocks and soil and dissolving a little of them  c) Being boiled first  d) Standing in a plastic bucket
**Answer: b) Passing through rocks and soil and dissolving a little of them**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) One way to remove hardness from water is…
a) Shaking it  b) Boiling it  c) Adding soap to it  d) Keeping it in the dark
**Answer: b) Boiling it**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Which is an advantage of hard water, as Bhutan's book weighs it?
a) It uses no soap at all  b) It never furs a kettle  c) The dissolved minerals it carries are among the things living bodies use  d) It lathers better than soft water
**Answer: c) The dissolved minerals it carries are among the things living bodies use**
