# Hard and soft water — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How does the lather test tell hard water from soft water?
**Answer: With the same soap and the same shaking, soft water lathers freely at once; hard water gives a poor, short-lived lather, wastes soap and may form scum instead of bubbles.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How can you prove hardness has been removed from a water sample?
**Answer: Soften it — for example by boiling — let it cool, and run the lather test again: if it now lathers freely like soft water, the hardness is gone.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Give one disadvantage and one advantage of hard water.
**Answer: Disadvantage: it wastes soap, leaves scum, and can fur up kettles and pipes. Advantage: the dissolved minerals that make it hard are among the things living bodies use (and many prefer its taste).**
