Today: **Hard and soft water** — Bhutan's way: the soap tells you.

Two buckets of water can look exactly alike and wash completely differently. In soft water, soap lathers at once. In hard water, the lather barely comes — the water picked up hardness from the rocks and soil on its way, and it fights your soap and leaves scum. Boil it or soften it, and test again: the returning lather is the proof. And Bhutan's book weighs the hard water fairly — minerals our bodies can use on one side, wasted soap and furred kettles on the other.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
