# Hands-on — Hard and soft water

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The lather test
Half-fill two clear bottles with waters from different sources. Add the same small amount of soap solution to each, close them, and shake each the same number of times. Compare the lather: how much, and how long it lasts. The water with the poorer, shorter lather is the harder water. Label each bottle with its source and its result.

## Soften and retest
Take the harder of your two waters. Boil one cupful (with an adult), let it cool completely, and pour it into a clean bottle. Run the lather test on the boiled water beside the unboiled. Record whether the boiling softened it — the new lather is the verdict, in the lesson's own method.
