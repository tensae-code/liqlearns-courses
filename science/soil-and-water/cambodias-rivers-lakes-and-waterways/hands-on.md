# Hands-on — Cambodia's rivers, lakes and waterways

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the system
Draw a map of the water near where you live: where water gathers, which way it flows, where it rests. Join the parts with arrows, the book's way — one system.

## Water's journey
Follow one rain shower in words: where it lands, where it runs, which waterway it joins, where it rests. Write the journey in the system's order.
