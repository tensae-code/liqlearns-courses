# Video script — Cambodia's rivers, lakes and waterways

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Cambodia's rivers, lakes and waterways** — Cambodia's way, from its Grade 7 book *វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រ ថ្នាក់ទី៧ (Science, Grade 7)*.

Cambodia's Grade 7 book gives its own country a chapter of Earth science: the **waterway system** — the rivers and the lakes read together as one system, not as separate blue lines on a map. Water that falls on the land gathers, flows, and rests in the lakes; the rivers join the parts into a whole, and the whole is the lesson (Earth Ch. 3, Lesson 1).

This is **country-specific content**: the system studied is the one the pupils live in. The book follows it with the flooded forest and its problems (Lesson 2) — the waterways first, then the living land they flood. A learner who knows how their own rivers and lakes join up holds the key to the chapter that follows and to the water lessons of the earlier grades, now read on their own country.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
