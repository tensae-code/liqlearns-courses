# Cambodia's rivers, lakes and waterways — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Cambodia's Grade 7 book teaches its rivers and lakes as…
a) one waterway system  b) separate decorations  c) a list of names only  d) part of the Physics part
**Answer: a) one waterway system** — Earth Ch. 3, Lesson 1: the waterway system, rivers and lakes joined.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson is the first of the chapter on…
a) Cambodia's own Earth science — its waterways and flooded forest  b) the planets  c) electricity  d) the human body
**Answer: a) Cambodia's own Earth science — its waterways and flooded forest** — Earth Ch. 3 opens with the country's waterways, then the flooded forest.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Water on the land…
a) gathers, flows, and rests in the lakes  b) never moves  c) only evaporates  d) flows uphill
**Answer: a) gathers, flows, and rests in the lakes** — The system's movement: gather, flow, rest — joined by the rivers.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The lesson that follows this one in the book is…
a) the flooded forest and its problems  b) the Moon's phases  c) simple machines  d) food chains
**Answer: a) the flooded forest and its problems** — Earth Ch. 3, Lesson 2 — the living land the waterways flood.

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) This content is described in the filing as…
a) country-specific content  b) a translation  c) an exam sample  d) a teacher-only note
**Answer: a) country-specific content** — Filed exactly so: Cambodia's waterway system is country-specific content.

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The book behind the lesson is published by…
a) MoEYS, Cambodia's Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport  b) a private school  c) another country  d) no publisher is named
**Answer: a) MoEYS, Cambodia's Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport** — An official MoEYS student textbook (Sala 00004320).
