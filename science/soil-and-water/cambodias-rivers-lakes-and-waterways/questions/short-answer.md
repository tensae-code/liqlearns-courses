# Cambodia's rivers, lakes and waterways — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words, what a waterway system is in this lesson.
**Answer:** A waterway system is the rivers and lakes of a place read as one joined whole — water gathers on the land, flows through the rivers and rests in the lakes.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is this lesson called country-specific content?
**Answer:** Because the system studied is Cambodia's own — the rivers and lakes its pupils live among — taught in Cambodia's own book.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Cambodia's rivers and lakes form one joined waterway system → the system is the country's own geography taught as science, opening the chapter that continues with the flooded forest — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
