# Saving the soil — conservation methods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Terracing defeats erosion mainly by —
a) stopping rain falling  b) cutting a slope into flat steps where water rests and sinks in  c) making soil heavier  d) hiding the soil
**Answer: b) cutting a slope into flat steps where water rests and sinks in**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ploughing along a slope's level curves is called —
a) check damming  b) afforestation  c) contour ploughing  d) shelter belting
**Answer: c) contour ploughing** — each furrow slows water instead of channelling it.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A check dam is built —
a) on a roof  b) across a gully, to slow flood water and trap carried soil  c) around a well  d) in a classroom
**Answer: b) across a gully, to slow flood water and trap carried soil**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A shelter belt protects a field mainly against —
a) wind lifting and carrying the topsoil  b) earthquakes  c) frost only  d) insects
**Answer: a) wind lifting and carrying the topsoil** — lines of trees break the wind.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Leaving crop residue on a field helps because it —
a) poisons weeds only  b) covers and shields the soil and rots into its food  c) makes harvest faster  d) scares birds
**Answer: b) covers and shields the soil and rots into its food**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A gully is deepening on a farm. The first matched method to choose is —
a) a shelter belt  b) a check dam across the gully  c) contour ploughing on flat land  d) afforestation of a distant hill
**Answer: b) a check dam across the gully** — match the method to the cause and place.
