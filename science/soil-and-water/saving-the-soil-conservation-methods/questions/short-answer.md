# Saving the soil — conservation methods — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match: a steep cultivated slope losing soil every storm — name two methods and what each does there.
**Answer:** Terracing (steps hold water and soil) and contour ploughing (furrows along curves slow water); cover/afforestation also acceptable with the matching reason.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a shelter belt do, step by step?
**Answer:** Trees in lines break the wind's speed, so the wind cannot lift and carry topsoil away; the field inside is sheltered.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** State the toolkit's matching rule in one sentence.
**Answer:** First name the erosion cause, then choose the method built against that cause.
