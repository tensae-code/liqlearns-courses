# Saving the soil — conservation methods — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ploughing up and down a slope protects it better than contour ploughing.
**Answer: False** — Up-and-down furrows are channels that speed water; contour furrows slow it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Afforestation can defeat erosion on bare land.
**Answer: True** — Roots hold soil, leaves break rain's force, litter feeds soil.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Any conservation method works against any erosion cause.
**Answer: False** — The method must be matched to the cause — water, wind, bare rain or gully.
