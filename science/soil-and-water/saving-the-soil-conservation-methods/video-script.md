# Video script — Saving the soil — conservation methods

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Rain on a **bare** slope washes the soil away.
**Steps** hold it — that is a **terrace**.
**Curved furrows** slow the water — **contour ploughing**.
A small dam in a gully — a **check dam** — traps the soil.
**Stalks left standing** cover the ground.
**Trees planted** hold it, and a **shelter belt** breaks the wind.
Name the cause. Then choose its method.
