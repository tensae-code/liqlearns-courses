# Hands-on — Saving the soil — conservation methods

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Read a slope
Find a real slope, gully or field edge. Name the erosion cause working there — running water, wind, bare rain-beaten ground, a gully. Then choose TWO methods from the toolkit for that exact place and write why each fits the cause.

## Contour lines with a string
On any sloping ground, walk a level line with a companion: keep a stretched string level (or keep both ends at the same height by eye against the horizon) and mark the line with stones. That line is a contour — the line a wise plough follows.
