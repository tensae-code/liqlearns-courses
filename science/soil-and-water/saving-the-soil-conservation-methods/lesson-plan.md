# Lesson plan — Saving the soil — conservation methods

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Soil and Water · **Lesson:** Saving the soil — conservation methods
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Ecosystem and Conservation of Natural Resources, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** soil is saved by a farmer's toolkit — afforestation, leaving crop residue as cover, terracing, check dams, shelter belts and contour ploughing — and each method is matched to the erosion cause it defeats.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Soil — types, formation and loss (this title, Grade 8): erosion's natural and man-made causes are named; this lesson is the matched answer, method by method. Soil fertility, erosion and why soil matters (this title) weighed why the saving matters.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Holding the slope — terraces, contours, dams and cover** — terracing, contour ploughing, check dams and crop residue, matched to running water.
2. **Planting against loss — forests and shelter belts** — afforestation and shelter belts, matched to wind and to bare land.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Soil — types, formation and loss (Soil and Water) — recommended, never forced.
## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the six conservation methods.
2. Match each method to the erosion cause it defeats.
3. Choose methods for a given slope, gully or windy field and defend the choice.
## What you need
A slope, gully or field edge to read — or the picture in this lesson; sticks or stones to mark contour lines.
