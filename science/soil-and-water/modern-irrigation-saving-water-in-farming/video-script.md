Today: **Modern irrigation — saving water in farming** — Egypt's way, from its ministry books.

**Farming drinks water — and loses much of it.** **Irrigation** is bringing water to crops. Egypt's Primary 6 book arrives here through soil and environmental change: how soil forms, changes and is lost, and how it is protected. Water spread openly over a field is shared with the sun and the wind — much evaporates or runs past the roots before a plant can drink. In a dry land, every lost bucket is a loss the farm and the aquifer both feel; saving water in farming is protecting soil and water together.

**A new modern irrigation system — the project.** The interdisciplinary project is to design **a new modern irrigation system**: a system whose measure of success is how much of its water reaches the plants' roots. Designing it joins the unit's science — water movement, soil, evaporation under heat — to the farmer's need. As with every design project in these books, the design is a claim to be tested: how much water did the old way lose, how much does the new system save, and what proves it?
