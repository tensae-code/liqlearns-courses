# Hands-on — Modern irrigation — saving water in farming

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Where does the water go?
Water two identical pots of soil, one covered with dry leaves or cloth and one bare, with the same amount of water. After a sunny day, dig a finger into each and say which kept its water — and where the other pot's water went.

## Sketch your system
Draw a modern irrigation system for a small farm plot. Label how water travels, where it is delivered, and how you would prove it saves water.
