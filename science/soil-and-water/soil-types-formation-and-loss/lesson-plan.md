# Lesson plan — Soil — types, formation and loss

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Soil and Water · **Lesson:** Soil — types, formation and loss
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Ecosystem and Conservation of Natural Resources, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** soil is told into loam, clay and sand by particle size and feel, and holds water very differently (clay most, loam in between, sand almost none); soil forms slowly from parent rock by weathering under five factors — parent material, climate, organisms, relief and time — and is lost by erosion, whose causes are natural and man-made.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Loam, clay and sand — the squeeze test and Soil fertility, erosion and why soil matters (this title), where the kinds were felt in the hand and fertility was weighed; this Grade 8 lesson adds particle size, the five formation factors and the water-holding lab, keyed by the teacher guide (item 15, labelled in sources). The Biology/Agriculture soil lessons carry soil further at their own depth — cross-referenced, not copied.

## Sub lessons
1. **Three soils — particle size and the water test** — loam, clay and sand by feel and particle size; the water-holding lab and its keyed results.
2. **How soil forms — and how it is lost** — weathering of parent rock under five factors; erosion, natural and man-made.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Loam, clay and sand — the squeeze test (Soil and Water)
- Recommended first: What soil is and what is in it (Soil and Water) — recommended, never forced.
## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell loam, clay and sand apart by particle size and by water-holding.
2. Name the five factors that form soil, and say soil forms from parent rock by weathering.
3. Read the water-holding lab's result and split erosion's causes into natural and man-made.
## What you need
Three soil samples — sandy, clayey, loamy — three funnels or cut bottles, cotton, three cups, water.
