# Soil — types, formation and loss — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sand holds more water than clay.
**Answer: False** — Sand holds essentially none; clay holds the most.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Loam holds a moderate amount of water — between clay and sand.
**Answer: True** — The lab's keyed intermediate result.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Soil forms quickly — a deep soil can form in one rainy season.
**Answer: False** — Soil forms slowly under the five factors; time is one of them.
