# Soil — types, formation and loss — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The soil with the smallest particles is —
a) sand  b) loam  c) clay  d) gravel
**Answer: c) clay** — clay feels smooth and sticky.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the water-holding lab, the soil that holds the most water is —
a) sand  b) clay  c) loam  d) stones
**Answer: b) clay** — sand holds essentially none; loam is intermediate.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Loam is the most fertile soil because it —
a) is pure clay  b) holds nutrients, moisture and humus, drains well and is easy to till  c) never gets wet  d) contains no living things
**Answer: b) holds nutrients, moisture and humus, drains well and is easy to till**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Soil forms from parent rock by —
a) burning  b) weathering  c) freezing only  d) planting
**Answer: b) weathering** — under the five factors.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is NOT one of the five soil-forming factors? 
a) parent material  b) climate  c) the price of land  d) time
**Answer: c) the price of land** — the five: parent material, climate, organisms, relief, time.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Clearing trees and overgrazing are erosion causes of which kind? 
a) natural  b) man-made  c) neither  d) weather
**Answer: b) man-made** — heavy rain and wind are the natural kind.
