# Soil — types, formation and loss — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A soil feels gritty and water runs straight through it in the lab. Name it, and say why the water runs through.
**Answer:** Sand — its particles are the biggest, with wide spaces water passes through.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the five factors that form soil.
**Answer:** Parent material, climate, organisms, relief and time.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one natural and one man-made cause of erosion.
**Answer:** Natural: e.g. heavy rain / flood / wind on a steep slope. Man-made: e.g. clearing trees, overgrazing, leaving ploughed soil bare.
