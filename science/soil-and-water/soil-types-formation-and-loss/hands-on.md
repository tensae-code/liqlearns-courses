# Hands-on — Soil — types, formation and loss

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## The water-holding lab
Line three funnels (or cut bottles) with cotton, fill each with equal soil — clay, loam, sand — and set each over a cup. Pour the same measure of water gently onto each. Wait, then compare the cups and the wetness left in each soil. Write the result in the lab's order: which held most, which held least.

## Five-factor walk
Find one patch of soil outdoors. For each of the five formation factors, write one line of evidence you can actually see or know for that patch: its rock below, its weather, its plants and worms, its slope, and the time it must have taken.
