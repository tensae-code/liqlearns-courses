# Video script — Soil — types, formation and loss

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Rub the soil. **Gritty** — big pieces: **sand**.
**Smooth and sticky** — tiny pieces: **clay**.
In between, the farmer's friend: **loam**.
Pour water. Clay **holds** it. Sand lets it **run**. Loam holds **some**.
Soil is born slowly — rock, weather, living things, slope, **time**.
Rain and people can carry it away. That loss is **erosion**.
