# Loam, clay and sand — the squeeze test — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Loam (ለም አፈር) is a mix of —**
a) sand, clay and humus  b) stone and glass  c) water and air only
**Answer: a) sand, clay and humus**

**2. Clay soil (ሸክላ) squeezed in the hand —**
a) falls apart at once  b) forms a smooth ball  c) turns to water
**Answer: b) forms a smooth ball**

**3. Sandy soil (አሸዋማ) has —**
a) very fine smooth grains  b) big rough grains that will not hold together  c) no grains
**Answer: b) big rough grains that will not hold together**

**4. Which soil is the fertile one?**
a) loam  b) pure sand  c) pure clay
**Answer: a) loam**
