# Video script — Loam, clay and sand — the squeeze test

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three soils live in your hand's answer.
**Loam (ለም አፈር)** — holds, then half-crumbles: the fertile mix.
**Clay (ሸክላ)** — a smooth ball. **Sandy (አሸዋማ)** — falls apart. Squeeze, and the soil tells its name.
