# Hands-on — Loam, clay and sand — the squeeze test

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The field trip
The book's own: walk out with your teacher or family, collect local soils in three tins, bring them back, dampen, squeeze, compare, label.

## Grain rubbing
Rub each soil between finger and thumb: clay feels smooth, sand feels rough and big-grained, loam in between with dark humus. Feeling is data.
