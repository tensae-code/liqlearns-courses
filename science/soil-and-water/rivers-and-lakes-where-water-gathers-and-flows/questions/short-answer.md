# Rivers and lakes — where water gathers and flows — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The parts of a river — source, course, bed, mouth and banks.
**Answer:** A river is learned part by part: its **source**, where it begins; its **course**, the way it runs; its **bed**, the hollow it runs in; its **mouth**, where it ends by joining bigger water; and its **banks**, the land along its sides.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Lowland and mountain rivers.
**Answer:** Rivers are sorted by the land they cross: **lowland rivers** run through flat low ground, and **mountain rivers** run through the mountains.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Lakes and ponds — water that gathers.
**Answer:** Not all surface water runs: **lakes** and **ponds** are waters that gather and stand in one place.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The parts of a river — source, course, bed, mouth and banks → Lowland and mountain rivers → Lakes and ponds — water that gathers — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
