# Hands-on — Rivers and lakes — where water gathers and flows

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace a river, source to mouth (paper and pencil)
Draw one river you know — real or from a map. Mark and label: source, course, bed, mouth and banks. Under it write whether it is a lowland or a mountain river, and one lake or pond in the same region, if you know one.
