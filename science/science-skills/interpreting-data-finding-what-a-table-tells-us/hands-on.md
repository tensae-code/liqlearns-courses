# Hands-on — Interpreting data — finding what a table tells us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: skills focus "Interpreting Data": data gathered in an experiment is easier to read in a table or a drawing; the worked example is a table of the average air temperatures of Riyadh city for every month of the year Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
