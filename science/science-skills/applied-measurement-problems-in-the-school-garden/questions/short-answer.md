# Applied measurement problems in the school garden — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: measurement problems in the school agriculture project.
**Answer:** Seychelles' Unit 1 Project 1, "Using what we have learnt", sends pupils back into the school agriculture project with the measuring skills of the unit: finding the volume of a watering can, weighing a fruit without picking it, and working out how much water seed beds need.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the three garden problems this lesson sets.
**Answer:** Finding a watering can's volume, weighing a fruit without picking it, and watering seed beds with a measured amount.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Measurement problems in the school agriculture project → Solving them with what you have learnt — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
