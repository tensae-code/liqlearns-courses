# Applied measurement problems in the school garden — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A watering-can problem asks first for its…
a) volume  b) length  c) weight  d) temperature
**Answer: a) volume**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Weighing a fruit without picking it means…
a) supporting it on a scale while it still hangs  b) shaking it off the tree  c) guessing only  d) cutting it in half
**Answer: a) supporting it on a scale while it still hangs**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The right order in these problems is…
a) say what must be found, guess, measure, then use the number  b) measure, then decide what you measured  c) guess and never measure  d) copy a friend's number
**Answer: a) say what must be found, guess, measure, then use the number**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) If a small can 'holds' more than a big bucket, the measure is…
a) wrong — check it again  b) always right  c) a temperature  d) a length
**Answer: a) wrong — check it again**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Seed beds are watered with…
a) a measured amount of water  b) no water at all  c) boiling water  d) salt water only
**Answer: a) a measured amount of water**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Each problem starts by choosing…
a) the right measure and instrument  b) the biggest ruler  c) a thermometer only  d) the answer
**Answer: a) the right measure and instrument**
