# Hands-on — Applied measurement problems in the school garden

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Solve three garden problems
In a garden or yard: (1) find the volume of a watering can or bucket by filling it with a measured jug; (2) estimate, then measure, how much water one seed bed or plant pot needs; (3) weigh a fruit still on its plant by supporting it on a balance or spring scale without picking it. Record guess, measure and difference for each.
