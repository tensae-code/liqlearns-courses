# Video script — Debating change: new foods or old ways?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two speakers. One question: **must our food change?**
**The New One**: more mouths, less produced — bring in new crops.
**The Old One**: habits change slowly; raise production, keep the culture. Now the class debates — **with reasons**.
