# Hands-on — Debating change: new foods or old ways?

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The class split
The book's own activity: split the group, one side per speaker, and debate both questions formally — a speaker, a reply, a closing. Reasons are written down by a listener and counted at the end.

## Interview the old and the new
Ask an elder what foods have changed in their lifetime, and a young farmer what new crops they plant. Two real voices for the two printed speakers.
