# Debating change: new foods or old ways? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The New One argues that —**
a) nothing must change  b) new productive root crops and grains must join the old ones, because population grows while production falls  c) farming should stop
**Answer: b) new productive root crops and grains must join the old ones, because population grows while production falls**

**2. The Old One answers that —**
a) habits change slowly and good values may be lost; technology should raise production without changing the food culture  b) new crops are poison  c) nobody needs food
**Answer: a) habits change slowly and good values may be lost; technology should raise production without changing the food culture**

**3. In the class work, pupils —**
a) memorise the debate  b) split and debate both questions with reasons  c) copy the debate out
**Answer: b) split and debate both questions with reasons**

**4. The old standbys the New One names are —**
a) teff, wheat, barley and livestock  b) rice and aeroplanes  c) coffee and cars
**Answer: a) teff, wheat, barley and livestock**
