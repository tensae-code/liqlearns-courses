# Hands-on — Using the gas burner safely

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Practise the steps without a flame
With a teacher present and no gas: rehearse the lighting order on an unlit burner or a drawing of one, say each step aloud, set the air hole open and closed, and show how a test tube is held in its holder, angled away from everyone. Only a teacher lights a real burner.
