# Using the gas burner safely — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The burner's flame should be made about…
a) 5 cm high  b) 50 cm high  c) 1 mm high  d) any height at all
**Answer: a) 5 cm high**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The flame is changed by…
a) opening or closing the air hole  b) adding water  c) shaking the burner  d) covering it with paper
**Answer: a) opening or closing the air hole**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A test tube being heated is held with…
a) a holder  b) bare fingers  c) a paper towel  d) another test tube
**Answer: a) a holder**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A heating test tube must never be…
a) pointed at anyone  b) held over the flame  c) watched  d) labelled
**Answer: a) pointed at anyone**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The burner is lit…
a) step by step, in the taught order  b) as fast as possible  c) by a pupil alone without a teacher  d) with the air hole covered in paper
**Answer: a) step by step, in the taught order**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Heating carelessly is dangerous because…
a) boiling liquid can spit out of the tube  b) the flame is cold  c) glass cannot break  d) gas smells nice
**Answer: a) boiling liquid can spit out of the tube**
