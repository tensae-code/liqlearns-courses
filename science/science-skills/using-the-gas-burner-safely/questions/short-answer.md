# Using the gas burner safely — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: lighting the burner, step by step.
**Answer:** Seychelles' Unit 1 Topic 8 teaches the gas (Bunsen) burner as a set of safe steps: light the burner in the taught order, open the air hole, and make a flame about 5 cm high.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How high should the flame be made, and how is a test tube held?
**Answer:** About 5 cm high, with the air hole opened; the test tube is held with a holder and never pointed at anyone.
