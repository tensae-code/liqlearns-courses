# Using the gas burner safely

## Do it first
Before you read: say out loud what you already think this lesson's one thing is — using the gas burner safely. Then read on: this is Seychelles' way in — the National Institute of Pedagogy's Integrated Science unit booklets (Unit 1).

## Sub lesson 1 — Lighting the burner, step by step

Seychelles' Unit 1 Topic 8 teaches the gas (Bunsen) burner as a set of safe steps: light the burner in the taught order, open the air hole, and make a flame about 5 cm high. The size and kind of flame are controlled, not left to chance — the air hole open or closed changes the flame, and the pupil sets the flame the task needs.

## Sub lesson 2 — Heating safely with the flame

Heating is done with the same care: a test tube is held with a holder, never in bare fingers, and it is never pointed at anyone — what is inside can boil up and spit. The rules are part of the skill: a burner used step by step is a tool; a burner used carelessly is the laboratory's most common danger.
