# Temperature scales and converting between them — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Ice melts at 0 °C / 32 °F / 273 K and water boils at 100 °C / 212 °F / 373 K; the healthy body's 98.6 °F is 37 °C; and converting between Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin with the shared formula, worked step by step.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science and Technology Grade 7*, printed pp. 137–139.**
