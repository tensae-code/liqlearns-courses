# Video script — Measuring: temperature, thermometers and the Celsius scale

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Temperature** — the measure of hotness or coldness.
It **decides the state** — ice, water, steam.
The **thermometer** measures it.
World unit: **kelvin**. Every day: **degrees Celsius**.
Water freezes at **zero**, boils at **one hundred** — at sea level.
