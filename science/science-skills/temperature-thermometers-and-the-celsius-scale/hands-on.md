# Hands-on — Measuring: temperature, thermometers and the Celsius scale

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fixed points, handled
With an adult, read the thermometer in melting ice and in steam over boiling water. Your two readings should stand at the book's two fixed points, 0 °C and 100 °C.

## A day's temperature diary
Read the thermometer four times in one day and keep the diary in °C and, converted, in K. The diary is the weather lesson's raw material.
