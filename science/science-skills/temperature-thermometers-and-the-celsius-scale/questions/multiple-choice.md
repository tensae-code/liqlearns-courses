# Measuring: temperature, thermometers and the Celsius scale — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Temperature measures —**
a) length  b) hotness or coldness  c) mass
**Answer: b) hotness or coldness**

**2. Temperature is measured with a —**
a) balance  b) thermometer  c) cylinder
**Answer: b) thermometer**

**3. The world unit of temperature is the —**
a) kelvin  b) litre  c) gram
**Answer: a) kelvin**

**4. 0 °C equals —**
a) 100 K  b) 273 K  c) 0 K
**Answer: b) 273 K**

**5. At sea level, water boils at —**
a) 0 °C  b) 273 °C  c) 100 °C
**Answer: c) 100 °C**
