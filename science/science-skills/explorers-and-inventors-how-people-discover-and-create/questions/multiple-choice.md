# Explorers and inventors — how people discover and create — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An explorer is a person who…
a) Creates a new machine  b) Goes out to find what is not yet known  c) Only reads books  d) Stays at home
**Answer: b) Goes out to find what is not yet known**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) An expedition is…
a) A kind of invention  b) A journey made to discover, whose knowledge is brought back  c) A modern technology  d) A science test
**Answer: b) A journey made to discover, whose knowledge is brought back**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) An inventor…
a) Creates what did not exist before  b) Only travels  c) Only names places  d) Copies what others made, exactly
**Answer: a) Creates what did not exist before**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Moldova's Grade 4 lesson runs…
a) From technologies to animals  b) From the named explorers to Edison and Liv Arnesen, and on to modern technologies  c) From plants to explorers only  d) From Edison to the Moon only
**Answer: b) From the named explorers to Edison and Liv Arnesen, and on to modern technologies**
