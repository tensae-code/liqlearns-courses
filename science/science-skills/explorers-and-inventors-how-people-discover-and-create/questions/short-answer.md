# Explorers and inventors — how people discover and create — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the difference between exploring and the thing an explorer finds?
**Answer:** Exploring is discovering: what the explorer finds was already there — the expedition makes it known, and the knowledge is brought back for everyone else.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How do the explorer, the inventor and the scientist differ, in the lesson's frame?
**Answer:** The explorer adds knowledge of what is there, by expeditions; the inventor creates what did not exist before; the scientist finds out how things work.
