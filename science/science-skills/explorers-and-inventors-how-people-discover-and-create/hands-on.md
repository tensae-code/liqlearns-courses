# Hands-on — Explorers and inventors — how people discover and create

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Explore your own ground
Take one small place you are allowed to walk — a yard, a garden, a school corner — and explore it as an expedition of one: walk it slowly, note three things you had never noticed there before, and draw the place from your notes. That is the explorer's work at its smallest: go, look, bring knowledge back.

## Invent for a real need
Name one small trouble in your day. Sketch an invention that would solve it: draw it, label its parts, and write one sentence for what each part does. That is the inventor's work at its smallest: create what was not there.

## Discover or create?
Take the lesson's people and things — an expedition, an explorer, an inventor, a scientist, a modern technology — and sort each one under "discovers what is there" or "creates what was not there". For any one that will not sort cleanly, say why.
