The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Explorers and inventors — how people discover and create**.

A person can be an explorer: going out on an expedition, into the unknown, and bringing knowledge back. A person can be an inventor: creating something that did not exist before. And a scientist finds out how things work.
Moldova's Grade 4 book puts them in one lesson — Man, explorer and inventor — from the named explorers to Edison and Liv Arnesen, and on to the modern technologies we live with now. Discovering and creating are two ways people add to what the world knows and has.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
