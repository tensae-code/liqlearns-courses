# Video script — Presentations and spreadsheets — first steps

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Presentations and spreadsheets — first steps** — Nepal's way, from its Curriculum Development Centre books.

Multimedia and making slides in PowerPoint (printed pp. 44–47), and first spreadsheets in Excel: rows, columns and cells, autofill and sorting (printed pp. 47–51)

Spreadsheets at work: every value named by its row and column (C2, B7), AutoSum, formulas for adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing, filling a formula down a column, finding an average, and turning figures into bar charts and pie charts (same proposed lesson as in the Nepal Grade 6 section)

Multimedia first: text, sound, video and animation combined make things easier to understand, with the guide's table of multimedia software (teacher-guide evidence; same proposed lesson)

PowerPoint how-to in the guide: layouts and themes, adding slides, resizing a text box by its handles, inserting picture, audio and video from This Device, transitions such as Push, and running the show from the beginning with Esc to leave (teacher-guide evidence; same proposed lesson)

Excel how-to in the guide: workbook and worksheet, rows, columns and cell addresses, entering data, autofill (with Ctrl held for a number series), widening columns and wrap text, inserting and deleting rows and columns, merging cells, and sorting A–Z and Z–A, closing with a family spreadsheet sorted by name and by age (teacher-guide evidence; same proposed lesson)

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
