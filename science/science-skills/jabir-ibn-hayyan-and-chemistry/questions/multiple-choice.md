# Jabir ibn Hayyan and chemistry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Jabir ibn Hayyan is presented as a scholar of…
a) Astronomy  b) Chemistry  c) Music  d) Geography only
**Answer: b) Chemistry**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) His page sits inside the Grade 5 unit on…
a) Magnets  b) Elements and compounds  c) The weather  d) Simple machines
**Answer: b) Elements and compounds**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The page is filed under…
a) Jobs linked with science  b) Safety rules  c) The contents  d) A test
**Answer: a) Jobs linked with science**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The book's placing teaches that chemistry…
a) Has no people in it  b) Is a human work with named scholars  c) Was invented last year  d) Needs no study
**Answer: b) Is a human work with named scholars**
