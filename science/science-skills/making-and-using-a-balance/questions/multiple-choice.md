# Measuring: making and using a balance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The balance's pans are made from —**
a) shoe-polish tins  b) glasses  c) leaves
**Answer: a) shoe-polish tins**

**2. Before weighing, a fair balance must be —**
a) painted  b) level with both pans empty  c) wet
**Answer: b) level with both pans empty**

**3. In the bottle test, which side goes down?**
a) the air-filled bottle  b) the water-filled bottle  c) neither
**Answer: b) the water-filled bottle**

**4. The two bottles are —**
a) different sizes  b) the same kind and size  c) both full of water
**Answer: b) the same kind and size**

**5. The test teaches that mass counts —**
a) size  b) colour  c) the amount of matter
**Answer: c) the amount of matter**
