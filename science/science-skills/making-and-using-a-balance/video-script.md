# Video script — Measuring: making and using a balance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two shoe-polish tins. String. A wooden beam.
Hang the pans. Level it empty — a fair balance starts level.
A bottle of **water** against a bottle of **air**.
The water side goes **down**.
Same size — but more **matter**. That is mass.
