# Hands-on — Measuring: making and using a balance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The build, step by step
Groups build the balance from tins, string and wood, following the lesson. Every group levels its balance empty and proves the level with two equal stones.

## Feeling mass
Eyes closed, take a bottle in each hand — water-filled and air-filled. Say which is heavier before opening your eyes; then let the balance judge. Hands and instrument agree: that agreement is measurement.

## Estimate, then measure (Grade 7 growth)
Textbook, chalk stick, duster: the group writes its estimate for each BEFORE the balance is touched, then measures on the class beam balance (or the tin balance from this lesson, with known stone standards). Estimates and readings side by side — the gap between them is estimation skill, made visible. Finish with the puzzle: a single grain on the pan. What happens? What finer way can the group invent (weigh 100 grains together, then share)?
