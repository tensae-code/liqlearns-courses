# Hands-on — Measurement — scalars, vectors and prefixes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The two-walks test
In pairs on the compound: one partner gives size-only instructions, the other follows them literally and stops wherever they end. Swap, now with directions added. Compare endpoints — the vector wins.

## Staircase cards
Make ten prefix cards (name, symbol, power). Shuffle, race to lay the staircase femto → peta, then convert three quantities by moving a counter up and down the laid stair.
