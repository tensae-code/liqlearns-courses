# Measurement — scalars, vectors and prefixes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A scalar quantity needs —
a) magnitude and direction  b) magnitude only  c) a compass  d) no unit
**Answer: b) magnitude only** — size with unit completes it

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a vector?
a) mass  b) temperature  c) force  d) energy
**Answer: c) force** — force needs direction

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Displacement is a vector because —
a) it is long  b) it is unfinished without its direction  c) it has no unit  d) it is measured in metres
**Answer: b) it is unfinished without its direction** — "5 metres" alone does not fix where you end

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Kilo means —
a) 10  b) 100  c) 10³ (one thousand)  d) 10⁶
**Answer: c) 10³ (one thousand)** — 10³ g = 1 kg

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The prefix staircase runs from —
a) kilo to mega only  b) femto (10⁻¹⁵) to peta (10¹⁵)  c) milli to centi  d) gram to kilogram
**Answer: b) femto (10⁻¹⁵) to peta (10¹⁵)** — the full printed range

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** 2 kg equals —
a) 200 g  b) 2,000 g  c) 20 g  d) 10³ g exactly
**Answer: b) 2,000 g** — 2 × 10³ g
