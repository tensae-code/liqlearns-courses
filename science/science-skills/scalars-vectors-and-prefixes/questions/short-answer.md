# Measurement — scalars, vectors and prefixes — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the one-question test that separates scalars from vectors.
**Answer:** Is the quantity complete with size (magnitude) alone? If yes — scalar; if it also needs direction — vector.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write 10³ g in kilograms.
**Answer:** 1 kg.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which prefix stands at 10¹⁵?
**Answer:** Peta (P).
