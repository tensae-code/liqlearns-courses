# Lesson plan — Measurement — scalars, vectors and prefixes

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Science Skills · **Lesson:** Measurement — scalars, vectors and prefixes
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Basic Concepts of Science (Grade 7 General Science).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** some quantities need only a size (scalars), others need size and direction (vectors) — and SI prefixes shrink or stretch units by powers of ten, from femto to peta
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 7 quantities lesson (Science Skills — Measuring: units of mass, length and time, grown) — the learner knows quantities carry magnitude plus unit; now they are sorted, and their units scaled

## Sub lessons
1. **Scalars and vectors** — magnitude alone vs magnitude plus direction, with the completeness test
2. **SI prefixes: the staircase of ten** — femto to peta as powers of ten; kilo worked as 10³ g = 1 kg

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Science Skills — Measuring: units of mass, length and time. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sort quantities into scalars and vectors, with the test for each.
2. Read the prefix staircase from femto (10⁻¹⁵) to peta (10¹⁵).
3. Work the kilo example: 10³ g = 1 kg, both directions.
4. Say why displacement is a vector but distance-style quantities in the list are scalars.

## What you need
Paper and pencil; a corridor or compound for a direction walk.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls velocity a scalar because it has a speed number → speed alone is the size; velocity is unfinished until its direction is given.
- Reads prefixes as new units → a prefix is a multiplier worn by a unit: kilo- always means ×10³, on grams, metres or anything else.
