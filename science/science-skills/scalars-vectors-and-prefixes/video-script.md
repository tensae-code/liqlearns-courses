# Video script — Measurement — scalars, vectors and prefixes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some quantities are complete with a size.
**3 kilograms.** Nothing missing. That is a **scalar**: time, mass, volume, temperature, energy.

Some quantities are unfinished without a direction.
**5 metres** — which way? **5 metres east** — complete. That is a **vector**: displacement, velocity, force.

And units climb a staircase of tens: milli, **kilo**, mega — up to peta.
Down: micro, nano — down to femto.
One step to remember: **10³ grams = 1 kilogram**. Kilo means one thousand.
