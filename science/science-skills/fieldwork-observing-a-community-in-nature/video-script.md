# Video script — Fieldwork — observing a community in nature

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Fieldwork — observing a community in nature** — Bulgaria's way: prepare the study before you walk it.

First, choose your community and write your questions. Take a notebook and a magnifier. That preparation is the first half of the work.

Then go: observe, look closely at the small things, and record everything — the living things, and the traces people leave among them.

Now you. Name a community near you and one question you would take to it. Say it back — in your own words.
