# Hands-on — Fieldwork — observing a community in nature

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run a mini field study
Choose a small community near you — a garden corner, a tree, a pond edge. Write three questions before you go. Take a notebook and, if you have one, a magnifier. Spend twenty minutes observing and recording, human traces included.

## Report the notebook
Turn your field notebook page into a short report: the community, your questions, what you found for each, and one human trace you recorded.
