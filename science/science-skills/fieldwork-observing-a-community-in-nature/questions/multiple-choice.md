# Fieldwork — observing a community in nature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A field study begins with…
a) Walking out at once  b) Choosing a community and framing questions  c) Buying equipment  d) Drawing the animals from memory
**Answer: b) Choosing a community and framing questions**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The book's named kit is…
a) A net and a jar  b) A notebook and a magnifier  c) A camera and a map  d) A ruler and a thermometer
**Answer: b) A notebook and a magnifier**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) What else must the observers record besides the living things?
a) The weather only  b) The traces of human activity  c) The way home  d) Nothing else
**Answer: b) The traces of human activity**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The magnifier is for…
a) Writing notes  b) Looking closely at small things  c) Keeping dry  d) Measuring temperature
**Answer: b) Looking closely at small things**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) An excursion without questions is, in the book's teaching,…
a) The best kind  b) Only a walk  c) A test  d) Impossible
**Answer: b) Only a walk**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The result the observers bring home is…
a) The notebook record  b) A collected animal  c) A photograph only  d) Nothing
**Answer: a) The notebook record**
