# Fieldwork — observing a community in nature — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the two halves of the book's field study.
**Answer:** Preparation — choose the community, frame the questions, ready the notebook and magnifier; and carrying it out — observe, look closely, and record, including human traces.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is preparation called the first half of the fieldwork, not a delay?
**Answer:** Because the questions decide what will be looked for and recorded; without them nothing is studied, only walked past.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What are 'traces of human activity' doing in a nature study?
**Answer:** The community is recorded as it is — people pass and live in it, and their marks are part of its true state.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Your notebook comes home with three unanswered questions. Was the study wasted? Say why or why not, from the lesson.
**Answer:** No — the record itself is the result: what was found is recorded, and the unanswered questions are recorded as open, ready for the next study.
