# Being a good netizen and your online reputation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Netizenship (guide evidence): a netizen is a person who actively uses the internet, and netizenship is the set of good practices for using it legally, safely, ethically and responsibly — polite language, respect for all, no posting another person's messages, photos or videos without consent, no character assassination, no unauthorised use of others' intellectual property; the world joined by phone, internet and social media named a global village.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Shikshak Nirdeshika Vigyan Grade 10 (guide)*, printed p. 422.**
