# Hands-on — DNA as evidence — forensic identification

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Patterns that differ
Make two 'code strips' — two rows of coloured squares or letters, the same length, differing in a few places. Swap with a partner, then find a third strip that matches one of them exactly. That matching, pattern against pattern, is the shape of DNA identification.

## The evidence chain
Act out the chain with paper: a 'trace' card, a 'laboratory' step where its pattern is copied, and two 'person' cards with patterns. Match the trace to its person and write the one-sentence conclusion the evidence supports — and one thing it does not prove.
