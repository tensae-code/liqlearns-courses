# DNA as evidence — forensic identification — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The body's coded material is called _____.
**Answer: DNA**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Each person's DNA _____ differs from person to person.
**Answer: pattern**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Matching a trace's DNA to a person's is forensic _____.
**Answer: identification**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In the project reading, the DNA match identifies a _____.
