# DNA as evidence — forensic identification — multiple choice


**1.** (Sub lesson 1) DNA is…
a) A kind of bone  b) The body's coded material, carrying its instructions  c) A forensic tool only  d) The same in everyone
**Answer: b) The body's coded material, carrying its instructions**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Each person's DNA pattern…
a) Is identical to everyone else's  b) Differs from person to person  c) Changes every day  d) Cannot be read
**Answer: b) Differs from person to person**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) DNA can be used as evidence because…
a) It is visible to the eye  b) Its pattern differs between people  c) It is found only in books  d) It never appears in traces
**Answer: b) Its pattern differs between people**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Forensic identification works by…
a) Guessing who was present  b) Matching a trace's DNA pattern against a person's pattern  c) Asking witnesses only  d) Measuring height
**Answer: b) Matching a trace's DNA pattern against a person's pattern**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) In the book's project reading, the DNA match…
a) Identifies a murderer  b) Grows a plant  c) Dates a fossil  d) Measures pressure
**Answer: a) Identifies a murderer**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The book's reading is carried in this lesson as…
a) A full transcription  b) A plot summary only  c) A photograph  d) A question list
**Answer: b) A plot summary only**
