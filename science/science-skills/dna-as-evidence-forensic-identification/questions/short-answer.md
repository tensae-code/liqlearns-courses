# DNA as evidence — forensic identification — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does DNA's differing pattern make it useful as evidence?
**Answer: Because a pattern that differs between people can tell people apart: a trace's pattern can match one person and not another.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Describe the forensic matching method in order.
**Answer: Read the DNA pattern from a trace, compare it with a person's DNA pattern, and a match identifies whose trace it is.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What does the book's project reading show the method doing?
**Answer: Identifying a murderer — the DNA evidence, not a guess, names the person (carried here as a summary only).**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Where is DNA found, in the lesson's terms?
**Answer: Inside the body's cells — the long coded material that carries a living thing's instructions.**
