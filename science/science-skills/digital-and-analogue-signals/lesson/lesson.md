## Sub lesson 1 — What a signal is (guide evidence)

A physical quantity that changes with time; an analogue signal changes continuously and is drawn as a sine wave (sound, temperature, landline phones, analogue TV), a digital signal changes in steps and is drawn as a square wave with only two values, binary 1 and 0, built in class with a 6 V source, switch and bulb and graphed on graph paper; ADC turns analogue into digital and DAC turns digital back.
