# Digital and analogue signals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: A physical quantity that changes with time; an analogue signal changes continuously and is drawn as a sine wave (sound, temperature, landline phones, analogue TV), a digital signal changes in steps and is drawn as a square wave with only two values, binary 1 and 0, built in class with a 6 V source, switch and bulb and graphed on graph paper; ADC turns analogue into digital and DAC turns digital back.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Shikshak Nirdeshika Vigyan Grade 10 (guide)*, printed p. 402.**
