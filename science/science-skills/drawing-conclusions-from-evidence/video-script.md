The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Drawing conclusions from evidence** — Iraq's way: inferring in Grade 3, and the scientist's order in Grade 5.

To infer is to use relations — how things connect — to reach a judgement. The conclusion is reached, not guessed. And the Grade 5 book gives the order: I analyse data, and draw conclusions. Analyse first, conclude second.
A conclusion drawn before the data is analysed is the order reversed — and the books' order is the safe one.
