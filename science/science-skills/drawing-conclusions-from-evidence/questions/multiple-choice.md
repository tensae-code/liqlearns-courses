# Drawing conclusions from evidence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Inferring means…
a) Guessing quickly  b) Using relations to reach a judgement  c) Copying a friend's answer  d) Naming things in order
**Answer: b) Using relations to reach a judgement**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A conclusion differs from an opinion because a conclusion…
a) Is louder  b) Is reached from what is in front of you  c) Is always longer  d) Needs no evidence
**Answer: b) Is reached from what is in front of you**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The Grade 5 book's order is…
a) Conclude, then analyse  b) Analyse data, then draw conclusions  c) Guess, then check nothing  d) Draw, then forget
**Answer: b) Analyse data, then draw conclusions**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Data is…
a) What was observed and recorded  b) A kind of magnet  c) A conclusion already drawn  d) A picture frame
**Answer: a) What was observed and recorded**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A conclusion drawn before analysing the data is…
a) The safe order  b) The books' order  c) The order reversed  d) Impossible to write
**Answer: c) The order reversed**
