# Drawing conclusions from evidence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does inferring use to reach a judgement?
**Answer: Relations — how the things observed connect and hang together.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Say the scientist's sentence from the Grade 5 book.
**Answer: I analyse data and draw conclusions.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is a guess not a conclusion?
**Answer: A conclusion must be reached from evidence — relations followed, data analysed; a guess starts from nothing in front of you.**
