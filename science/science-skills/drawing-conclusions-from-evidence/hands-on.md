# Hands-on — Drawing conclusions from evidence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Relations first
Choose two plants, two animals or two materials you know. List the relations between them — same, different, bigger, feeds on, made of. From the list, reach one judgement and write the relation it stands on.

## Analyse, then conclude
Count something for a week — birds at a window, pages read, cups of water. Analyse the seven numbers, then draw one conclusion and write the numbers it stands on.
