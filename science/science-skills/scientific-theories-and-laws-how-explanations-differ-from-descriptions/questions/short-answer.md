# Scientific theories and laws — how explanations differ from descriptions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why is calling a theory 'just a guess' wrong, on this lesson?
**Answer: A theory is a tried explanation of a pattern noticed again and again in the natural world — it rests on tested pattern, not on a guess.**

**2.** What two questions does Sudan's unit assessment ask about theories and laws?
**Answer: Compare the two — theory against law — and: can a theory change?**
