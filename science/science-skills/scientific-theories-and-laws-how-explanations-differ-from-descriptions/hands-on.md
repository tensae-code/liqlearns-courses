# Hands-on — Scientific theories and laws — how explanations differ from descriptions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The assessment, done
Answer the unit's own assessment questions in writing: compare a scientific theory and a scientific law; can a theory change? Use only this lesson's definitions.

## Guess or theory?
Sort three statements — a guess with nothing behind it, a tried explanation of a repeated pattern, a description of what always happens — into guess, theory and law, and say what decided each sort.
