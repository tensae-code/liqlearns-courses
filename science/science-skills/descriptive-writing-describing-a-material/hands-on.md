# Hands-on — Descriptive writing — describing a material

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: scientific writing, "I describe matter": describe a slice of pizza to someone who has never seen it, through measurable observations; good descriptive writing uses words that tell the thing's appearance, sounds and smell, gives details that build a clear picture, and groups related details together Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
