# Descriptive writing — describing a material — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: I describe matter.
**Answer:** scientific writing, "I describe matter": describe a slice of pizza to someone who has never seen it, through measurable observations; good descriptive writing uses words that tell the thing's appearance, sounds and smell, gives details that build a clear picture, and groups related details together.
