# Descriptive writing — describing a material — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) I describe matter  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) I describe matter**

**2.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: descriptive writing — describing a material. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**3.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Saudi Arabia, Grade 3, Part 2 — *Science, Grade 3, Part 2*, printed pp. 84–85  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Saudi Arabia, Grade 3, Part 2 — *Science, Grade 3, Part 2*, printed pp. 84–85**
