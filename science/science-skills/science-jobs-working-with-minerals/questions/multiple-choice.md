# Science jobs — working with minerals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Iraq's Grade 3 book closes its minerals run with…
a) A test only  b) Jobs connected with minerals  c) A story  d) Nothing
**Answer: b) Jobs connected with minerals**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The book's order is…
a) Jobs first, science never  b) The science of the thing first, then the jobs it supports  c) Only jobs  d) Only properties
**Answer: b) The science of the thing first, then the jobs it supports**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The work around a mineral is decided by…
a) Its colour alone  b) Its properties and its uses  c) Its price tag only  d) Guessing
**Answer: b) Its properties and its uses**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Jobs linked with science are…
a) Outside the science book  b) Part of Iraq's science-book content  c) Only for adults to read  d) Maths content
**Answer: b) Part of Iraq's science-book content**
