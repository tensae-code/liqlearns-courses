## Sub lesson 1 — Jobs connected with minerals

Iraq's Grade 3 book teaches minerals — what a mineral is, its properties and its uses — and then closes the run with the people: **jobs connected with minerals**. The book's own order is the lesson: first the science of the thing, then the jobs the thing supports.

A mineral's properties and its uses decide the work around it: where minerals are got from, how they are judged by their properties, and how they are turned to their uses — each step is work, and the book files that work under jobs linked with science.
