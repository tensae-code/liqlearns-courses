# Hands-on — Science jobs — working with minerals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## From mineral to job
Pick a mineral you know — gold is the books' own example of a worked mineral. List its properties, its uses, and the work each use needs.

## Jobs wall
Draw a minerals wall: mineral in the middle, properties on one side, uses on the other, and the jobs underneath each use.
