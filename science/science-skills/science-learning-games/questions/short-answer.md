# Science learning games — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words, how a loop game works.
**Answer:** Each card carries an answer and the next question; the class plays the cards in chain and the loop must close through everyone — where it breaks is the missing knowledge.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the two families of games in the chapter and one game in each.
**Answer:** Recall-as-contest games — memory game, Jeopardy, last-winner; and chaining games — loop games and domino games.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Recall games — memory, Jeopardy, last-winner — make the class rehearse the science in order to play → loop and domino games chain the ideas, and a break in the chain names the missing link — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
