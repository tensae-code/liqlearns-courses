# Hands-on — Science learning games

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build a loop
Take one lesson you know. Write six cards: each card carries one answer and the next question. Shuffle, play the loop with a companion, and note where it breaks — that break is your revision list.

## Domino line
Write five science pairs (a thing and its fact) as domino ends. Join them into one line where matching ends touch. Say the whole line aloud as one story.
