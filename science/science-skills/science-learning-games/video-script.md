Today: **Science learning games** — Cambodia's way, from its cross-grade teacher guide book *ការបង្រៀនមុខវិជ្ជាវិទ្យាសាស្ត្រតាមបែបគោលវិធីសិស្សមជ្ឈមណ្ឌល, ផ្នែកទី 3 (Student-Centred Science Teaching, Part 3)*.

Part 3 of Cambodia's manual closes with **learning games** (Ch. 6). Its first games are recall played as contest: the **memory game**, matching what belongs together from memory; **Jeopardy**, answering in question-form against the clock of the game; and **last-winner** formats, where holding your knowledge longest wins. Each game makes the class rehearse the science in order to play — the playing is the revision.

The chapter's other family chains the ideas: **loop games**, where each card's answer opens the next card's question and the loop must close through the whole class, and **domino games**, where matching ends join idea to idea in a line. A loop that will not close, or a domino that will not match, shows the class exactly which link is missing — the game itself does the diagnosing. The same chapter puts the family to work on the rock cycle (Activity 4) and the water cycle (Activity 5).
