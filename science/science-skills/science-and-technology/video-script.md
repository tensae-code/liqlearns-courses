# Video script — Science and technology — how they differ and feed each other

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Science asks: **what is the world?**
Technology asks: **what can we build?**

Science explores. It discovers. It predicts.
Technology applies. It invents. It makes life simpler — and it can help, or harm.

They feed each other. An explanation makes an invention possible.
The invention raises new questions. Round and round.

The light bulb took many hands and many years.
In **1879**, Edison made it practical.
Understanding first — then building — then new understanding.
