# Science and technology — how they differ and feed each other — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Science and technology are exactly the same activity.
**Answer: False** — different jobs — exploring vs applying

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A new technology can raise new questions for science.
**Answer: True** — the reciprocal loop

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the guide's contrast, science changes only by addition while technology changes rapidly.
**Answer: True** — one of the eight points (teacher guide — evidence)
