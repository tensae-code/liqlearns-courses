# Science and technology — how they differ and feed each other — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is science said to aim at, besides discovery?
**Answer:** Prediction.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who made the light bulb practical, and in what year?
**Answer:** Edison, in 1879.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one community technology and one risk it carries.
**Answer:** Any honest pair, e.g. the motorbike — fast transport; road accidents.
