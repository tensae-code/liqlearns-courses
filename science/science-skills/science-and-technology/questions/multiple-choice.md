# Science and technology — how they differ and feed each other — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Science mainly —
a) invents devices  b) explores and discovers, aiming at prediction  c) sells products  d) avoids questions
**Answer: b) explores and discovers, aiming at prediction** — its product is knowledge

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Technology —
a) only asks questions  b) applies knowledge and invents  c) never harms  d) is the same as science
**Answer: b) applies knowledge and invents** — it puts knowledge to work

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Technology can help or harm because it —
a) is magic  b) touches society directly  c) is always old  d) hides its effects
**Answer: b) touches society directly** — named in the contrast

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Edison's part in the bulb story (1879) was to —
a) invent light  b) make the bulb practical after many others' improvements  c) ban the bulb  d) write its theory only
**Answer: b) make the bulb practical after many others' improvements** — many-handed improvement, made practical

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The loop runs —
a) invention → nothing  b) explanation → invention → new questions  c) questions are forbidden  d) science stops at invention
**Answer: b) explanation → invention → new questions** — each feeds the other

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A home survey entry is complete only with —
a) a price  b) the problem solved AND the risk carried  c) a photograph  d) the maker's name
**Answer: b) the problem solved AND the risk carried** — both columns required
