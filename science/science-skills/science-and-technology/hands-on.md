# Hands-on — Science and technology — how they differ and feed each other

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The technology survey
Walk home or the market street with the survey sheet. Five technologies minimum; no entry is complete without its risk. Compare sheets in class: which technology appeared most? Which risk was named most?

## Take one apart (in words)
Choose a simple tool — a lamp, a hand mill, a water pot. List: the knowledge inside it, the problem it solves, one harm it can do, one question it raises for science.
