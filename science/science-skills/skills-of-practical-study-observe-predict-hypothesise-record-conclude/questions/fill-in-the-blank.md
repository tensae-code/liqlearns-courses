# Skills of practical study — observe, predict, hypothesise, record, conclude — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) "If … then … because …" is the shape of a testable _____.
**Answer: hypothesis**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The variable you deliberately change is the _____ variable.
**Answer: independent**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A single reading is an anecdote; _____ readings are evidence.
**Answer: repeated**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Turning a results table into a pattern — often with a graph — is _____.
**Answer: analysis**
