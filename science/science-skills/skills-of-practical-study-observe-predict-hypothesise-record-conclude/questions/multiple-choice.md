# Skills of practical study — observe, predict, hypothesise, record, conclude — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A hypothesis becomes scientific when it…
a) Sounds clever  b) Is testable — an experiment could show it wrong  c) Is written in long words  d) Matches the textbook
**Answer: b) Is testable — an experiment could show it wrong**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Kuwait's lab activities ask you to predict…
a) After seeing the result  b) Before you begin the activity  c) Only if the teacher forgets  d) At the end of the course
**Answer: b) Before you begin the activity**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In a fair test, the number of variables deliberately changed is…
a) As many as possible  b) One — the independent variable  c) None  d) Three
**Answer: b) One — the independent variable**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Readings should be recorded…
a) From memory at the end  b) As they happen, including odd ones, in a table prepared beforehand  c) Only when they fit the prediction  d) On loose scraps
**Answer: b) As they happen, including odd ones, in a table prepared beforehand**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) An anomalous reading in your table should be…
a) Deleted quietly  b) Kept, noted, and discussed in the analysis  c) Changed to fit the line  d) Copied from a neighbour
**Answer: b) Kept, noted, and discussed in the analysis**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A good conclusion…
a) Claims more than was tested, to impress  b) Answers the hypothesis and stops where the evidence stops  c) Ignores the hypothesis  d) Repeats the method word for word
**Answer: b) Answers the hypothesis and stops where the evidence stops**
