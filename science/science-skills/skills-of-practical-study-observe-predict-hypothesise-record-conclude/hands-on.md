# Hands-on — Skills of practical study — observe, predict, hypothesise, record, conclude

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run the whole sequence in ten minutes
You need a metre ruler and a partner for the reaction-time practical: your partner drops the ruler without warning; you catch it and read where your fingers close — the distance fallen prices your reaction time. Before the first drop, write a prediction and a hypothesis. Record five drops in a prepared table, convert each distance with t = √(2d/g) (your teacher may supply the working), average them, and write a two-sentence conclusion that says only what your five readings support — including the reading you liked least.
