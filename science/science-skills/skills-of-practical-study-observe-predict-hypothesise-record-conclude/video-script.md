# Video script — Skills of practical study — observe, predict, hypothesise, record, conclude

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **practical study is a sequence, not a scramble**. Observe first. Predict, and sharpen it into a hypothesis an experiment could prove wrong. Design a fair test, build the table before you touch anything, and record every reading as it happens — repeats, anomalies and all. Then analyse, and conclude exactly as far as the evidence reaches, and not one step further.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
