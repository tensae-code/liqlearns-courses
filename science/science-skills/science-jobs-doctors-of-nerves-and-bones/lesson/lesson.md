## Sub lesson 1 — The nerve doctor and the bone doctor

Iraq's Grade 6 book teaches the body's systems — the nervous system, the skeletal system and the muscular system, each with its health — and then its reading names the people: **the nerve doctor and the bone doctor**.

Each system has its own doctor. The doctor of the nerves answers for the nervous system; the doctor of the bones answers for the skeleton. The reading's point is the match: the body is studied system by system, and its doctors specialise the same way.
