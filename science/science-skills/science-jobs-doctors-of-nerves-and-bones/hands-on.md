# Hands-on — Science jobs — doctors of nerves and bones

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Match the specialist
Make two cards: NERVE DOCTOR and BONE DOCTOR. Under each, list the system's parts you know from the body lessons, and one thing that can go wrong with it.

## Systems and their people
Draw the body in three bands — nerves, skeleton, muscles — and write beside the first two the doctor the reading names for each.
