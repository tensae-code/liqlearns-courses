# Video script — Science jobs — doctors of nerves and bones

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Science jobs — doctors of nerves and bones** — a reading in Iraq's Grade 6 book.

The book teaches the body's systems — nervous, skeletal, muscular — and then names the people: the nerve doctor and the bone doctor. Each system has its own doctor. The body is studied system by system, and its doctors specialise the same way.
