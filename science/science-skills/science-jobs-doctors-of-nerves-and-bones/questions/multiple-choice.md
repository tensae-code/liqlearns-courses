# Science jobs — doctors of nerves and bones — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Grade 6 reading names…
a) The eye doctor and the tooth doctor  b) The nerve doctor and the bone doctor  c) Only one doctor for everything  d) No doctors
**Answer: b) The nerve doctor and the bone doctor**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The nerve doctor answers for…
a) The skeleton  b) The nervous system  c) The muscles only  d) The skin
**Answer: b) The nervous system**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The bone doctor answers for…
a) The nerves  b) The blood  c) The skeleton  d) The lungs
**Answer: c) The skeleton**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The reading comes after lessons on…
a) Rocks and soil  b) The nervous, skeletal and muscular systems  c) Magnets  d) The weather
**Answer: b) The nervous, skeletal and muscular systems**
