# Making a wind vane and rain gauge — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. In the project, the instruments are built —**
a) by a shop  b) by the pupils with their teacher  c) by the health worker
**Answer: b) by the pupils with their teacher**

**2. Wind direction is recorded —**
a) once a year  b) daily at break time  c) only when it rains
**Answer: b) daily at break time**

**3. The class hears the records —**
a) weekly, in a report  b) never  c) at year's end only
**Answer: a) weekly, in a report**

**4. The rain gauge in the project is for —**
a) pointing at the wind  b) measuring rainfall  c) telling the time
**Answer: b) measuring rainfall**
