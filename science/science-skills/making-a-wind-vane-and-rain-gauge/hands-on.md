# Hands-on — Making a wind vane and rain gauge

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The build (with the teacher)
The project itself: build the vane and gauge with the class and teacher, fix them where wind and rain reach them freely, and open the record book the same day.

## The weekly report
Friday's five minutes: the week's wind directions read aloud from the record, the rainiest day named from the gauge. Reporting is the project's crown — never skip it.
