# Lesson plan — Making a wind vane and rain gauge

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Science Skills · **Lesson:** Making a wind vane and rain gauge
**Place in the curriculum:** Science Skills — the Grade 4 book's instrument project: build the measurers, then keep the records (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the project: build a wind vane and a rain gauge with your teacher, record the wind's direction daily at break time, and report weekly to the class
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Measuring temperature, rain and wind — the instruments were named there; here they are built by the pupils' own hands, the project's way.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Weather and Seasons — Measuring temperature, rain and wind. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say the project in full: build both instruments with the teacher; record wind direction daily at break time; report weekly to the class.
2. Say what each built instrument does: the vane shows direction, the gauge measures rainfall.
3. Keep a week's record and give the weekly report.

## What you need
Simple building materials chosen with the teacher; a notebook page ruled for daily records; the school yard or roof for the instruments' home.

## If the learner is stuck
- Treats the build as the whole project → the record and the report are two of its three movements; an unrecorded instrument is a toy, a recorded one is science.
- Records at random hours → the book fixes break time daily; same hour is what makes days comparable.
