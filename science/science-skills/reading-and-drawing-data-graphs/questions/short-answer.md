# Reading and drawing data graphs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is raw, unsorted data not a finding?
**Answer:** Because the answers are still a mixed pile in the order they arrived; only after sorting into groups and counts can a pattern be seen and pointed to.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What do you look for after sorting data?
**Answer:** Patterns — what is most common, what is rare, and whether one thing rises or falls as another does.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How do you test whether your graph works?
**Answer:** Read it back: if you can state the pattern from the graph alone, without the table, the graph is doing its job.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) What three things does the story-poster carry?
**Answer:** The question the study asked, what was found (the pattern in plain words), and the graphs that prove it.
