# Reading and drawing data graphs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Collected data must first be…
a) Thrown away  b) Sorted into groups and counted  c) Drawn as a poster  d) Memorised in order
**Answer: b) Sorted into groups and counted**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A finding is…
a) The first answer collected  b) A pattern you can point to in the sorted data  c) A guess  d) The longest answer
**Answer: b) A pattern you can point to in the sorted data**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A graph's job is to…
a) Hide the data  b) Make small differences look big  c) Show a pattern at a glance  d) Replace the question
**Answer: c) Show a pattern at a glance**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An honest graph must have…
a) No labels  b) A stretched scale  c) Labels and a true scale  d) Colours only
**Answer: c) Labels and a true scale**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The presentation poster tells…
a) Only the teacher's opinion  b) The story: question, finding, and the graphs that prove it  c) A joke  d) The class register
**Answer: b) The story: question, finding, and the graphs that prove it**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A good story-poster can be understood by…
a) Only its makers  b) Nobody  c) A reader who was never in the class  d) Only in Cook Islands Māori
**Answer: c) A reader who was never in the class**
