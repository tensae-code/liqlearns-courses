# Video script — Reading and drawing data graphs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Reading and drawing data graphs** — the Cook Islands way: process the data, then present your findings.

You collected answers. A pile of answers is not a finding yet. First, sort: group the answers, count them. Then look — where is the pattern? What is common? What is rare?
Now draw it. A good graph shows the pattern at a glance, with honest labels, so anyone can read it.
Last, make your poster. The poster tells the story: our question, what we found, and the graphs that prove it.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
