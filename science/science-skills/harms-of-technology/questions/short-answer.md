# Harms of technology — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Pollution from power and farming.
**Answer:** Technology has a negative side, and Bangladesh's Class 5 book names it plainly: coal power and farm chemicals pollute air, soil and water — the same tools that help can harm the ground they stand on..

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Weapon technology.
**Answer:** The darkest harm is deliberate: weapon technology — bombs, tanks and guns — built to destroy.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Addiction — the quiet harm.
**Answer:** TV and computer addiction steal exercise, play and free thinking.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Pollution from power and farming → Weapon technology → Addiction — the quiet harm — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
