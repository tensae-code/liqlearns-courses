# Harms of technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Pollution from power and farming  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Pollution from power and farming**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Weapon technology  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Weapon technology**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Addiction — the quiet harm  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Addiction — the quiet harm**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: the negative side of technology — coal power and farm chemicals polluting air, soil and water, weapon technology, and TV and computer addiction stealing exercise, play and free thinking — as Bangladesh's Class 5 book names it. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Bangladesh, Grade 5 — *Elementary Science Class 5*, printed p. 66  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Bangladesh, Grade 5 — *Elementary Science Class 5*, printed p. 66**
