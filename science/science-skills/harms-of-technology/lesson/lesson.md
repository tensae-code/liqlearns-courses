# Harms of technology

## Do it first
Before you read: say, in one sentence, what you think this lesson's one thing is, from its title alone — **Harms of technology**. Then read on: this is Bangladesh's way in — its book teaches this thing at the place cited in the sources, in the order below.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pollution from power and farming

Technology has a negative side, and Bangladesh's Class 5 book names it plainly: coal power and farm chemicals pollute air, soil and water — the same tools that help can harm the ground they stand on.

## Sub lesson 2 — Weapon technology

The darkest harm is deliberate: weapon technology — bombs, tanks and guns — built to destroy. The book places weapons in the same chapter as pollution and addiction, as technology turned against life.

## Sub lesson 3 — Addiction — the quiet harm

TV and computer addiction steal exercise, play and free thinking. Nothing is poisoned and nothing explodes; what is lost is time and movement, quietly, every day. The harm list ends at home, where the learner can act on it first.
