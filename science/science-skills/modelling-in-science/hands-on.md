# Hands-on — Modelling in science

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Judge a model
Take any model to hand — a globe, a toy, a map, a diagram. List three features it keeps from the real thing and three it drops. Say for what question it is a good model, and for what question it is not.

## Model your classroom
Make a scale sketch-plan of your classroom. Measure one real length and its plan length, and write the scale. Your plan is a model: say what it keeps and what it leaves out.
