Today: **Modelling in science** — Armenia's way: learning from stand-ins.

A model keeps the likeness of the real thing — in the features that matter, and drops the rest.

Science models what is too big, too small, too far or too dangerous to study directly.

And it tests ideas on the model first. Modelling is a way of knowing.

Now you. Name a model, and say what it keeps and what it drops. Say it back — in your own words.
