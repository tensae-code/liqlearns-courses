# Modelling in science — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A stand-in built to be studied in place of the real thing is a _____.
**Answer: model**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A model works by _____: likeness in the features that matter.
**Answer: similarity**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Using stand-ins to know things is _____.
**Answer: modelling**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Modelling is one of science's methods of _____.
**Answer: cognition**
