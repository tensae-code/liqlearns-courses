# Modelling in science — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is similarity, in a model?
**Answer:** The model's likeness to the original in the features that matter for the question at hand — knowingly unlike it in the rest.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the modeller's first skill?
**Answer:** Choosing what the model keeps and what it drops.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Give two cases where science studies a model instead of the real thing.
**Answer:** Any two of: too big, too small, too far, too slow, too dangerous to study directly.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is modelling a method of cognition and not a drawing exercise?
**Answer:** Because it is a way of knowing: ideas about the real thing are formed and tested on the stand-in.
