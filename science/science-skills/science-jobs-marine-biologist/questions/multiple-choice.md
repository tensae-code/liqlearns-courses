# Science jobs — the marine biologist — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A marine biologist is a scientist of…
a) The desert  b) The sea  c) The sky only  d) The mountains
**Answer: b) The sea**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Iraq's book says the marine biologist studies…
a) Rocks on land  b) The tiny living things in seas and oceans  c) Only sharks  d) Only boats
**Answer: b) The tiny living things in seas and oceans**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The book places this job as…
a) A maths exercise  b) An enrichment reading in the water unit  c) A spelling list  d) A song
**Answer: b) An enrichment reading in the water unit**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Tiny living things in the sea are…
a) Not worth studying  b) What the sea's life hangs on  c) Always dangerous  d) Not alive
**Answer: b) What the sea's life hangs on**
