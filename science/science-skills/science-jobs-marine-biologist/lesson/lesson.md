# Science jobs — the marine biologist

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's one thing — the marine biologist — the scientist who studies the tiny living things in seas and oceans, as Iraq's Grade 2 pupil's book presents the job in an enrichment reading. Say what you already know about it out loud. Then read on: this is Iraq's way in — its Ministry of Education *Science* books (the Primary Science Book Series) teach this thing at the places named in this lesson's sources.

## Sub lesson 1 — The marine biologist's work

Iraq's Grade 2 book closes its water-environment unit with a job: the **marine biologist**. A marine biologist is a scientist of the sea — and the book names the work exactly: the marine biologist **studies the tiny living things in seas and oceans**.

Tiny living things are easy to pass by, yet the sea's life hangs on them. Studying them is a real job, done by a named kind of scientist — that is the book's point in placing the job inside the science book.
