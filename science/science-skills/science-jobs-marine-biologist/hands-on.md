# Hands-on — Science jobs — the marine biologist

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tiny life, big sea
Draw the sea in two layers: the big animals everyone names, and the tiny living things beneath notice. Write the marine biologist's job beside the tiny layer.

## A scientist's question list
Write three questions a marine biologist could study about tiny sea life. Keep each question about the tiny living things themselves.
