# Drawing biological specimens — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A biological drawing shows…
a) A general plan, simplified  b) The one real specimen as it truly is  c) What the drawer wishes it looked like  d) Only the colours
**Answer: b) The one real specimen as it truly is**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Proportion in a drawing means…
a) Using bright colours  b) Drawing quickly  c) The parts in their true sizes against each other  d) Making every part the same size
**Answer: c) The parts in their true sizes against each other**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Magnification tells the reader…
a) Who drew the drawing  b) How much bigger or smaller the drawing is than the real specimen  c) The specimen's name in Latin  d) Where the specimen was found
**Answer: b) How much bigger or smaller the drawing is than the real specimen**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Label lines should be…
a) Curved and decorated  b) Freehand and wavy  c) Ruled — straight, joining the label to the exact part  d) Left out
**Answer: c) Ruled — straight, joining the label to the exact part**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A diagram is…
a) The same as a drawing  b) A general plan, simplified  c) Always wrong  d) A photograph
**Answer: b) A general plan, simplified**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Barbados's syllabus teaches drawing specimens in…
a) Its weather strand  b) Appendix B  c) Its farming strand  d) A song
**Answer: b) Appendix B**
