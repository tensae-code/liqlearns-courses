# Drawing biological specimens — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A biological _____ shows the one real specimen as it truly is.
**Answer: drawing**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) _____ keeps the parts in their true sizes against each other.
**Answer: Proportion**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) _____ states how much bigger or smaller a drawing is than the real specimen.
**Answer: Magnification**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Label lines are _____, not freehand.
**Answer: ruled**
