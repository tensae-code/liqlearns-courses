# Hands-on — Drawing biological specimens

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw from the real thing
Place a real leaf, shell or insect in front of you and draw only what is there — no parts added, none left out. Say, as you draw, which rule you are keeping: proportion, magnification or label lines.

## Rule the label lines
Use a ruler for every label line. Draw one label line freehand first, then the same line ruled, and compare: say why the syllabus asks for ruled lines.
