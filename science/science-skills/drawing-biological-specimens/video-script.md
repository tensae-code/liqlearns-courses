Today: **Drawing biological specimens** — Barbados's way: a drawing you can study from.

A biological drawing is not a diagram. It shows the one real specimen, as it truly is. Keep the parts in true proportion. Say the magnification, so the reader knows the real size. And rule your label lines — straight lines from each name to the exact part it names. Three rules, and your drawing becomes evidence someone else can study.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
