# Hands-on — Measuring time the old way — sundial, sand-glass, water clock

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build and prosecute the shadow clock
Set a stick upright in the school compound and mark its shadow through a school day, as the guide's activity sets. Invite others to read it. Then list, from your own marks, every way the clock could mislead — that list is the prosecution.

## The market measures walk
In groups, visit a nearby market or host a trader. Record each indigenous measure in live use — cubit, span, foot, pace or others — what it measures, and whether two traders' versions agree. Report back to class.
