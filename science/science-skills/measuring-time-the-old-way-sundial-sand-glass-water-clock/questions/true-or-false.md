# Measuring time the old way — sundial, sand-glass, water clock — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The sundial's twelve parts divide the whole 24-hour day.
**Answer: False** — they divide the daylight interval, sunrise to sunset

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The sand-glass came into being in the 14th century.
**Answer: True** — two conical glasses joined by a narrow neck

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The guide keys cubit, span, foot and pace as indigenous measures in use in Addis Ababa markets.
**Answer: True** — named in the key to Exercise 1.12 and Project 1.2's market visit
