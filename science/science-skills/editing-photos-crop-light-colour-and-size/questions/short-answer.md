# Editing photos — crop, light, colour and size — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Moving photos from a phone or camera to the computer; editing a photo — cropping away what is not wanted, adjusting light and colour, removing spots, and resizing in Paint to the exact size and format an online form demands; and preparing photos inside PowerPoint.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science and Technology Grade 7*, printed pp. 33–41.**
