# What science is — evidence, inference and tentative knowledge — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** You can observe the bottom face of a sealed cube without opening it.
**Answer: False** — it is hidden — you can only infer it

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Science uses one single method for every question.
**Answer: False** — it uses many methods

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Scientific knowledge rests on empirical evidence.
**Answer: True** — evidence from observation and measurement is its base
