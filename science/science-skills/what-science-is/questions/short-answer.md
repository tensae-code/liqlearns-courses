# What science is — evidence, inference and tentative knowledge — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two things must an inference be backed with, in the cube task?
**Answer:** Evidence — the observations it stands on (and the reasoning that joins them).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the characteristic: "knowledge that can change when new evidence comes."
**Answer:** Tentativeness (knowledge is tentative).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In what year was the telephone patented, and by whom?
**Answer:** 1876, by Alexander Graham Bell.
