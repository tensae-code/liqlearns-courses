# Video script — What science is — evidence, inference and tentative knowledge

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is your science course. Seven units. One way of working: try first — then learn the name.

Look. A sealed cube. You see three faces. The bottom face is hidden.
What is on it? You may not look. You must **reason**.
What your eyes report — that is **observation**.
What your mind concludes — that is **inference**.
A good inference stands on **evidence**.

Science is like this. Its knowledge rests on evidence.
It is **tentative** — new evidence can change it.
It has **laws** — patterns we see again and again.
It has **theories** — explanations that evidence supports.
It uses **many methods**. Not one. And people do it **together**.

Seven units wait for you: basic concepts, matter, elements and reactions,
the cell, living things, the Earth, force and energy.
Try first. Then learn the name. That is the course.
