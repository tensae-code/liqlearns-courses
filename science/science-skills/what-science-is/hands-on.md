# Hands-on — What science is — evidence, inference and tentative knowledge

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The cube in your group
Seal a box in class. Each group observes, infers the hidden face, and presents its evidence. Open it only after every group has spoken — then judge which inferences the evidence truly supported.

## Evidence walk
Walk the school compound. List five observations (seen, heard, measured) and five inferences (concluded). Read them to a partner; they challenge every inference: "What is your evidence?"
