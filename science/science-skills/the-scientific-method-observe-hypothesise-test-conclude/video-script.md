# Video script — The scientific method — observe, hypothesise, test, conclude

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

After the rain, the mud is full of worms and snails.
Where did they come from? Do not guess — **investigate**.

**Observe** first. Some observations tell kinds — red, solid, sour.
Some tell numbers — 36 grams, 115 degrees.
Turn what you saw into a **question**. Then a **hypothesis** — an answer that can be **tested**.
Test it fairly. Read the results as they are.
Wrong? Change the hypothesis and test again. That is not failure — that **is** the method.

Hold the pattern, and you may have a **law** — it says what happens.
Explain the pattern, and you have a **theory** — it says why.
