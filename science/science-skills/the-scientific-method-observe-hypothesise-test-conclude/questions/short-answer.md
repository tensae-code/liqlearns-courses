# The scientific method — observe, hypothesise, test, conclude — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give one quantitative observation, with its number and unit.
**Answer:** Any measured statement in numbers and units, e.g. the melting point of sulfur is 115.21 °C.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State a testable hypothesis for the question “Why does an iron nail rust?”
**Answer:** Any testable proposed answer, e.g. a nail rusts because it is exposed to air and water together.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the two-plants case, what was held the same for both plants?
**Answer:** The same type of soil and the same amount of water — sunlight was the thing allowed to differ.
