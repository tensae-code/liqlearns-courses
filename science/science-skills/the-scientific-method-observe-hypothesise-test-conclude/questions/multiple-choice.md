# The scientific method — observe, hypothesise, test, conclude — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** “A ripe strawberry is red” is —
a) a quantitative observation  b) a qualitative observation  c) a hypothesis  d) a law
**Answer: b) a qualitative observation** — it describes a property, with no number or unit

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** “100 g of water dissolves 36 g of salt” is —
a) qualitative  b) a theory  c) quantitative  d) a conclusion
**Answer: c) quantitative** — it is told in numbers and units

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A hypothesis must be —
a) already proven  b) testable  c) popular  d) written as a law
**Answer: b) testable** — a tentative explanation that an investigation can try

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Testing is best done —
a) with everything changing at once  b) under controlled conditions, a single variable changing  c) without collecting data  d) once only, quickly
**Answer: b) under controlled conditions, a single variable changing** — control is what lets the result speak about the hypothesis

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** If the result shows the hypothesis is wrong, the investigator —
a) hides the result  b) restates the hypothesis and runs the steps again  c) declares a law  d) stops investigating
**Answer: b) restates the hypothesis and runs the steps again** — the loop back is part of the method

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A law differs from a theory because a law —
a) explains why  b) describes what happens and lets us predict  c) is never tested  d) is a guess
**Answer: b) describes what happens and lets us predict** — a theory is the one that explains why
