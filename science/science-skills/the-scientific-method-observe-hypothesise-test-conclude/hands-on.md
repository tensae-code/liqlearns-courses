# Hands-on — The scientific method — observe, hypothesise, test, conclude

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The mud investigation
After rain (or watering a patch of earth), observe what appears on the surface for ten minutes. Write one qualitative and one quantitative observation, then the question they raise.

## Two-plants rerun
Grow two bean plants in the same soil with the same water, one in full sun and one in shade. Record leaf colour weekly for three weeks and conclude only from what you recorded.
