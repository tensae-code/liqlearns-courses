# Hands-on — Robots and artificial intelligence — an introduction

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Design your robot on paper
Draw a homework robot and label each part with the job it does. Draw a line around the jobs it cannot do, and write one beside the drawing.

## Hold the debate
In pairs, argue the book's question — what can computers and AI do, and what can they not do? Each side must point to the robot design as evidence at least twice.
