# Video script — Robots and artificial intelligence — an introduction

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Robots and artificial intelligence — an introduction** — Egypt's way, from its ministry books.

**Design a homework robot.** Egypt's Primary 6 book introduces robots through a story-project: **children design a homework robot**. Designing it forces the first real questions — what jobs would the robot do, what would it need to sense, and what instructions would it follow? A **robot** is a machine built to carry out tasks; what it can do is bounded by what it was designed and instructed to do. The design step teaches that boundary from the inside: every ability in the drawing was put there by a designer, on purpose.

**What computers and AI can and cannot do — the debate.** The story closes in a **debate: what computers and artificial intelligence can and cannot do**. Computers follow instructions at great speed and handle patterns and repetition tirelessly; what they cannot do is step outside what they were given — they do not understand a task the way the child who set it does. Egypt's own way is to end the project by arguing both sides from the design the learners just made: the robot they drew is the evidence for what machines can do, and its limits are drawn in the same picture.
