# Hands-on — Fair tests — variables and control groups

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The two-pot rerun
Plant two bean pots in the same soil, same light, same water — fertiliser in one only. Measure heights weekly for three weeks and compare against the control, not against hope.

## Rigged-test clinic
In pairs, each pupil writes a deliberately rigged test (two changes at once). Swap papers. The partner must find every unfairness and rewrite the test fair.
