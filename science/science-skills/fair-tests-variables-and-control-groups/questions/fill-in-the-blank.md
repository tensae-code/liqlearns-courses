# Fair tests — variables and control groups — fill in the blank

Fill in each blank with the right word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The variable you deliberately change is the ______ variable.
**Answer:** independent

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A group that receives no treatment, kept as the comparison baseline, is the ______ group.
**Answer:** control

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A test that changes only one factor at a time while keeping all other conditions the same is a ______ test.
**Answer:** fair
