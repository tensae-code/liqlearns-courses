# Finding and judging information on the internet — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: The Google search operators (site:, inurl:, exact-phrase quotes, OR, AND, the minus sign, filetype:, maps:, weather:); judging whether a source can be trusted (official institution sites and .edu, .gov and .org addresses, publication and revision dates, readers' comments); and copyright — Google Books material is read-only, PhET simulations are free, and borrowed material must be cited.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science and Technology Grade 9 (Nepali, revised 2082)*, printed pp. 219–220.**
