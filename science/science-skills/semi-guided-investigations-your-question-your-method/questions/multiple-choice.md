# Semi-guided investigations — your question, your method — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a semi-guided investigation —
a) the method is given and the question is yours  b) the question is given and the method is yours  c) both are given  d) neither is given
**Answer: b) the question is given and the method is yours** — that middle rung is what “semi-guided” means

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The null hypothesis in the bean case says —
a) sunlight helps beans  b) there will be no difference between the indoor and outdoor plants  c) the indoor plant will turn yellow  d) soil makes the difference
**Answer: b) there will be no difference between the indoor and outdoor plants** — null means no difference; the alternative expects the colour difference

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A plain coiled nail, with no current —
a) lifts many clips  b) deflects a compass  c) attracts no filings and deflects no compass  d) glows
**Answer: c) attracts no filings and deflects no compass** — that failed test is what forced Hypothesis 2

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Hypothesis 2, the one the evidence confirmed, is —
a) a longer nail is a magnet  b) a current-carrying coiled nail acts as a magnet  c) copper alone is magnetic  d) filings are magnetic by themselves
**Answer: b) a current-carrying coiled nail acts as a magnet** — the current was the missing ingredient

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In the starch test, the ethanol step —
a) adds starch  b) removes the leaf's colour  c) boils the iodine  d) feeds the leaf
**Answer: b) removes the leaf's colour** — the ethanol turns green as the leaf decolourises

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Iodine turning blue-black on the leaf means —
a) no starch  b) the leaf is dead  c) starch is present  d) the ethanol failed
**Answer: c) starch is present** — brown staying brown is the no-starch reading

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** A candle under a small cover goes out because —
a) the glass is cold  b) the air under the cover is used up  c) the wax melts  d) light is blocked
**Answer: b) the air under the cover is used up** — air is necessary for burning

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** With covers of different sizes, the fair prediction is —
a) all flames die at once  b) the biggest cover's flame lives longest  c) cover size cannot matter  d) the open candle dies first
**Answer: b) the biggest cover's flame lives longest** — more cover, more air — then the timings decide
