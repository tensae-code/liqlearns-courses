# Semi-guided investigations — your question, your method — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did the nail investigation need a second hypothesis?
**Answer:** The first hypothesis failed its test — a plain coiled nail attracted nothing — so the missing ingredient, the current, was built into a second hypothesis and tested.

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** What single conclusion do the candle timings support?
**Answer:** Air is necessary for burning: the covered flames die when the air under the cover is used up, bigger covers later, while the open candle burns on.
