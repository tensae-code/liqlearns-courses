# Semi-guided investigations — your question, your method — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the bean case, the two pots were given the same soil and the same care.
**Answer: True** — only the sunlight differed — that is what makes the comparison speak

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When Hypothesis 1 failed, the right move was to hide the result.
**Answer: False** — the right move was to frame and test Hypothesis 2

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The leaf is boiled in ethanol directly over the flame in the guide's procedure.
**Answer: False** — the burner is turned off and the ethanol tube stands in hot water — ethanol is flammable

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** A conclusion in these investigations is drawn from the timings and observations actually collected.
**Answer: True** — evidence first, conclusion after — never the guess dressed up
