FULL SCRIPT — the leaf starch test, step by step, as placed (teacher guide — evidence: the nine-step bench procedure).

---

A leaf makes food in sunlight. It stores the extra as **starch**.
Iodine is our witness. Where starch hides, iodine turns **blue-black**.

**Step 1.** Take a green leaf from a plant that has stood in sunlight for hours.
**Step 2.** Half-fill a beaker with water and bring it to the boil. Keep it boiling.
**Step 3.** With forceps, lay the leaf in the boiling water. Boil for **two minutes**. The leaf goes soft.
**Step 4.** Turn the burner **off**. Hot ethanol and naked flames must never meet.
**Step 5.** Put the boiled leaf in a tube of **90% ethanol**.
**Step 6.** Stand the tube in the hot water and boil for about **ten minutes**, until the leaf loses its colour. The ethanol turns green — the colour is leaving the leaf.
**Step 7.** Lift the leaf out gently and rinse it under a fine trickle of cold water. It goes soft again.
**Step 8.** Spread the leaf flat on a **white tile**.
**Step 9.** Add a few drops of **iodine solution** and watch.

Iodine stays **brown**? No starch in this leaf.
Iodine turns **blue-black**? Starch is there — the leaf was storing food.

That colour change is the conclusion, earned by nine careful steps.
