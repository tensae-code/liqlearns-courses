# Hands-on — Semi-guided investigations — your question, your method

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Electromagnet clinic
Build the coiled-nail electromagnet. Test it with the circuit open, then closed. Count lifted paper clips both ways and record the two counts side by side — the contrast is the evidence.

## Candle timings
One candle, three covers of different sizes, one timer. Predict the order before lighting; time each burn; write the conclusion the timings force, even if your prediction was wrong.
