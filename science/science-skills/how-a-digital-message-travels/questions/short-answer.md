# How a digital message travels — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: The parts of a digital communication system in order (guide evidence): source, input transducer, encoder, modulator, channel, demodulator, decoder, output transducer and output signal, taught by role-play with nine learners as the nine parts and by following a text from one mobile to another through the telecom towers; analogue against digital communication compared — coding possible only in digital, digital needing more bandwidth but suffering far less from outside disturbance.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Shikshak Nirdeshika Vigyan Grade 10 (guide)*, printed p. 408.**
