# Experiment 1: melting ice and boiling water — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. In Experiment 1, the ice is held —**
a) in bare hands over a fire  b) on a big spoon over a candle flame  c) in a glass of water
**Answer: b) on a big spoon over a candle flame**

**2. At sea level, ice melts at —**
a) 0 °C  b) 50 °C  c) 100 °C
**Answer: a) 0 °C**

**3. At sea level, water boils at —**
a) 0 °C  b) 37 °C  c) 100 °C
**Answer: c) 100 °C**

**4. The change in Experiment 1 is physical because —**
a) a new material is made  b) only density and volume changed, not what the material is  c) nothing changed at all
**Answer: b) only density and volume changed, not what the material is**
