# Hands-on — Experiment 1: melting ice and boiling water

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch the melt (adult-led)
The book's experiment with an adult in charge of the flame: ice, big spoon, cloth on the handle, thermometer readings written before and after. Pupils record; adults hold fire.

## Steam border watch
Over a cooking pot (adult's kitchen, safe distance), watch steam rise: water crossing the second border. Name the state on each side of the border.
