# Video script — Experiment 1: melting ice and boiling water

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

**Experiment 1**: ice on a big spoon, over a candle. Thermometer before — thermometer after.
Ice melts at **0 °C**. Water boils at **100 °C** — at sea level.
Only density and volume changed. The material is still water. A **physical** change — proved.
