# Video script — Making models to show how things work

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Making models to show how things work** — Nepal's way, from its Curriculum Development Centre books.

Making models to show what cannot be seen directly: collage models of photosynthesis, the atom and a food chain, the globe as a model of the Earth, and a working model of the lungs from balloons, a bottle and a Y-tube.

The guide's recipe for an ecosystem model: an aquatic ecosystem in a transparent vessel half-filled with water, sand, algae-bearing water, water plants, fish fry and a frog, and a land ecosystem planted in a flower pot with twigs, insects and model animals, both kept where sunlight falls (teacher-guide evidence; same proposed lesson as in the Nepal Grade 7 section)

Energy projects in the guide: build a working model of a pani ghatta water mill and explain the energy conversion when it is used, and build a roller-coaster track and describe how the toy train works (teacher-guide evidence; same proposed lesson)

A papier-mâché model of the Earth's inside: old newspaper cut fine, soaked, mashed wet and rolled into a ball, dried in the sun, cut open and painted — orange for the outer core, red for the inner core, yellow for the mantle, brown for the crust — with each layer's thickness, temperature, state and materials filled into a table (teacher-guide evidence; same proposed lesson)

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
