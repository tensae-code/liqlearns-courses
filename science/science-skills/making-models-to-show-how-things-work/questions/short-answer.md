# Making models to show how things work — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Making models to show what cannot be seen directly: collage models of photosynthesis, the atom and a food chain, the globe as a model of the Earth, and a working model of the lungs from balloons, a bottle and a Y-tube.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science and Technology Grade 7*, printed pp. 7–9.**
