# Explaining and arguing in science — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Scientific explanation skills are Chapter…
a) 2 of Part 1 of Cambodia's manual  b) 1 of Part 3  c) the whole of Part 2  d) an appendix
**Answer: a) 2 of Part 1 of Cambodia's manual** — Part 1, Ch. 2 (Activities 1–6) — between reading/writing and the scientific method.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) An explanation in science says…
a) what happened, and how and why  b) only what happened  c) only names  d) nothing aloud
**Answer: a) what happened, and how and why** — The middle skill: what, how and why together.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Agree/disagree circles make every learner…
a) take a position and give its reasons  b) stay silent  c) copy the teacher  d) leave the room
**Answer: a) take a position and give its reasons** — Position plus reasons — the manual's discussion training.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An odd-one-out task asks the learner to…
a) say which item does not belong and defend the choice  b) memorise a list  c) draw a circuit  d) measure a liquid
**Answer: a) say which item does not belong and defend the choice** — The defence of the choice is the argument practice.

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A thought experiment tests a case…
a) in the mind, before any apparatus  b) only outdoors  c) never  d) by examination
**Answer: a) in the mind, before any apparatus** — Tested in the mind first — one of Ch. 2's named tools.

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The manual is an official…
a) MoEYS teacher-training manual — guide evidence only  b) student textbook  c) private exam booklet  d) Grade 9 text
**Answer: a) MoEYS teacher-training manual — guide evidence only** — Department of Teacher Training, 2012 — cited as guide evidence, outside the acceptance count.
