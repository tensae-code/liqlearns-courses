# Lesson plan — Measuring: units of mass, length and time

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Science Skills · **Lesson:** Measuring: units of mass, length and time
**Place in the curriculum:** Science Skills — measuring, the carried Grade 4 set (CURRICULUM.md: the G4 "Measuring — …" placements land with Band C under Science Skills).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the standard units join by fixed relations — 1 kg = 1000 g; 1 m = 100 cm = 1000 mm; 1 km = 1000 m; 1 day = 24 hours, 1 hour = 60 minutes, 1 minute = 60 seconds — and measuring with them is a class craft: mass, height, and the time to walk 50 metres
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the two measuring lessons before it — old measures and the balance built; now the modern units and their relations.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Recite the relations: 1 kg = 1000 g; 1 m = 100 cm = 1000 mm; 1 km = 1000 m.
2. Recite the time relations: 1 day = 24 h; 1 h = 60 min; 1 min = 60 s.
3. Measure and compare, the book's class activity: mass, height, and the time to walk 50 m.

## What you need
A ruler or metre measure, a balance or scale, and a watch or clock; a 50-metre stretch marked outside.

## If the learner is stuck
- Counts a metre as 10 centimetres → say the family aloud: 1 m = 100 cm = 1000 mm; hundreds and thousands, never tens.
- Thinks time joins in tens like length → time's family is sixties and twenty-four: 60 seconds, 60 minutes, 24 hours.
