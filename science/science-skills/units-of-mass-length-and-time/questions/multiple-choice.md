# Measuring: units of mass, length and time — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. 1 kilogram equals —**
a) 100 g  b) 1000 g  c) 10 g
**Answer: b) 1000 g**

**2. 1 metre equals —**
a) 10 cm  b) 1000 cm  c) 100 cm
**Answer: c) 100 cm**

**3. 1 kilometre equals —**
a) 100 m  b) 1000 m  c) 60 m
**Answer: b) 1000 m**

**4. 1 hour equals —**
a) 24 min  b) 100 min  c) 60 min
**Answer: c) 60 min**

**5. The class activity times pupils walking —**
a) 50 m  b) 5 m  c) 1 km
**Answer: a) 50 m**

**6. A physical quantity carries —**
a) magnitude only  b) magnitude plus unit  c) a direction only
**Answer: b) magnitude plus unit**

**7. How many fundamental quantities are there?**
a) three  b) five  c) seven
**Answer: c) seven**

**8. Which of these is a DERIVED quantity?**
a) mass  b) force (the newton)  c) time
**Answer: b) force (the newton)**

**9. The SI unit of temperature is the —**
a) degree Celsius only  b) kelvin (K)  c) joule
**Answer: b) kelvin (K)**
