# Measuring: units of mass, length and time — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. 1 kilogram equals —**
a) 100 g  b) 1000 g  c) 10 g
**Answer: b) 1000 g**

**2. 1 metre equals —**
a) 10 cm  b) 1000 cm  c) 100 cm
**Answer: c) 100 cm**

**3. 1 kilometre equals —**
a) 100 m  b) 1000 m  c) 60 m
**Answer: b) 1000 m**

**4. 1 hour equals —**
a) 24 min  b) 100 min  c) 60 min
**Answer: c) 60 min**

**5. The class activity times pupils walking —**
a) 50 m  b) 5 m  c) 1 km
**Answer: a) 50 m**
