# Measuring: units of mass, length and time — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: recite all six relations (mass, length ×2, time ×3) and carry out the measure-and-compare activity in standard units.
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to sort or name, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.

Grade 7 growth pass mark (added 2026-11-12): name the seven fundamental quantities with their SI units, and derive area, volume, density and power units from fundamentals.
