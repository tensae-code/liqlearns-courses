# Hands-on — Measuring: units of mass, length and time

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The measure-and-compare day
Run the book's class activity in full: every pupil's mass and height measured and written, and every pupil timed over 50 m. The class table, in standard units, is the day's product.

## Unit ladders
Draw the three ladders — g→kg, mm→cm→m→km, s→min→h→day — on one sheet, with the joining numbers on the rungs. Climb each ladder aloud.
