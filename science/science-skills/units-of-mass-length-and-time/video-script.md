# Video script — Measuring: units of mass, length and time

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One kilogram — **one thousand grams**.
One metre — **one hundred centimetres, one thousand millimetres**.
One kilometre — **one thousand metres**.
One day — **twenty-four hours**. One hour — **sixty minutes**. One minute — **sixty seconds**.
Same units for everyone — so measuring can be **compared** fairly.

Seven quantities are measured **directly**: length, time, mass, temperature, current, amount, light.
Everything else is **built** from them — velocity, force, work.
Fundamental first. Derived after. That is the Grade 7 growth of this lesson.
