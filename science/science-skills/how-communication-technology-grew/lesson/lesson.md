# How communication technology grew

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The long road of messages

Communication travelled a long road: smoke signals and drums, then letters, then the telegraph (1832/1837), the telephone (1876), radio and TV, and at last the computer and the internet — each step carrying messages faster and further than the one before.

## Sub lesson 2 — The long road of recording

Recording grew alongside it: cave paintings, then writing and the printing press, then the camera, and then CD, DVD and memory card — each step keeping more information, smaller and longer.

## Sub lesson 3 — Two roads, one history

Bangladesh's Class 4 book tells the two roads together: carrying information further, and keeping information longer. Every new tool on either road changes what the other road can do.
