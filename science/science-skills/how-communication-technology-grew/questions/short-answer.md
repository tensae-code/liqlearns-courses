# How communication technology grew — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The long road of messages.
**Answer:** Communication travelled a long road: smoke signals and drums, then letters, then the telegraph (1832/1837), the telephone (1876), radio and TV, and at last the computer and the internet — each step carrying messages faster and further than the one before..

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The long road of recording.
**Answer:** Recording grew alongside it: cave paintings, then writing and the printing press, then the camera, and then CD, DVD and memory card — each step keeping more information, smaller and longer..

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Two roads, one history.
**Answer:** Bangladesh's Class 4 book tells the two roads together: carrying information further, and keeping information longer.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The long road of messages → The long road of recording → Two roads, one history — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
