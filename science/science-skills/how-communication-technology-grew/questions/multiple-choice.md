# How communication technology grew — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) The long road of messages  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Two roads, one history  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Two roads, one history**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: the long road of communication — smoke signals and drums to the computer and internet — told together with the long road of recording, from cave paintings to the memory card, as Bangladesh's Class 4 book tells it. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
