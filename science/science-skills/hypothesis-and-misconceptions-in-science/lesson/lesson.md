## Sub lesson 1 — What a hypothesis is

Papua New Guinea's Standards Based Grade 9 guide defines the **hypothesis** inside its Science as Inquiry strand: a proposed explanation, stated so that it can be tested — the idea an investigation is built to check. A hypothesis is not a guess thrown away; it is the sentence the experiment judges.

## Sub lesson 2 — What a misconception is

Against it, the guide sets the **misconception**: a wrong idea a learner already holds, which feels true but does not match the evidence. Misconceptions matter in science classes because a learner filters new teaching through them — naming them is the first step to correcting them.

## Sub lesson 3 — Comparing them — and their applications

The guide closes by **comparing** the two and their applications: a hypothesis is put forward deliberately to be tested; a misconception is already there, untested, and must be found before it can be replaced. Both are ideas about how things work — the difference is whether they have been, and will be, put to the test.
