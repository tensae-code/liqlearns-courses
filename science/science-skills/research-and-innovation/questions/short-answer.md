# Research and innovation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Research" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's Grade 5 guide teaches **research and innovation** as a named unit near the end of its book: research is the finding-out done in order, so its answers can be trusted.

**2.** What does the part "Innovation" teach?
**Answer:** Beside research stands **innovation**: making the new thing — or the new way — out of what research found.

**3.** What does the part "The pair at work" teach?
**Answer:** Research without innovation stops at knowing; innovation without research guesses.
