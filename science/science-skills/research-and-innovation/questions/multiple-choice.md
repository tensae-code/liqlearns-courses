# Research and innovation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) Innovation  b) The pair at work  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Research
**Answer: d) Research**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about finding out in order?
a) Research  b) Innovation  c) The pair at work
**Answer: a) Research**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about making the new from what was found?
a) Research  b) Innovation  c) The pair at work
**Answer: b) Innovation**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about knowing and making, together?
a) Research  b) Innovation  c) The pair at work
**Answer: c) The pair at work**
