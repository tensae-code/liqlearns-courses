## Sub lesson 2 — Innovation

Beside research stands **innovation**: making the new thing — or the new way — out of what research found. The pair is taught as one unit, because each feeds the other.

## Sub lesson 3 — The pair at work

Research without innovation stops at knowing; innovation without research guesses. Zimbabwe's own way is the single unit: the two are learned together, as one skill with two halves.

## The key ideas

- **Research** — finding out in order.
- **Innovation** — making the new from what was found.
- **The pair at work** — knowing and making, together.
