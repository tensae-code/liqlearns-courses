# Video script — Measurement — quantities, units, prefixes, accuracy and precision (Grade 8)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your span says the desk is four. Your friend's span says five.
Both of you measured honestly. The **measure itself** changed hands.

That is why science built the SI frame.
**Seven** fundamental quantities — no more. Everything else is **derived** from them.
Length in metres. Mass in **kilograms — kg**. Time in seconds. Temperature in kelvin.

Too big? Too small? A **prefix** stretches the unit by tens.
And 0.0012 seconds? Count the steps: one point two **thousandths** — **milliseconds**.

Last, judge every set of readings **twice**.
Close to the truth — that is **accuracy**. Close to each other — that is **precision**.
Twelve point one seven is not nine point eight one — precise, yes. Accurate, no.
