# Hands-on — Measurement — quantities, units, prefixes, accuracy and precision (Grade 8)

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Span against ruler
The whole class measures the same desk in spans, then in centimetres. Write both class sets on the board. Which set clusters? That clustering — or its absence — is reliability, seen.

## Conversion relay
Cards carry one measure each (0.6 km, 500 g, 30 min, 50 m, 0.25 kg, 0.0012 s). Each pupil converts one card aloud, naming the power of ten used, and passes the pack on.
