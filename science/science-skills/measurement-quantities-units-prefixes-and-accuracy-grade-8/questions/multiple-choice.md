# Measurement — quantities, units, prefixes, accuracy and precision (Grade 8) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The number of fundamental quantities is —
a) four  b) seven  c) ten  d) unlimited
**Answer: b) seven** — all other quantities are derived by combining them

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Density is —
a) fundamental  b) derived, with unit kg/m³  c) a prefix  d) an instrument
**Answer: b) derived, with unit kg/m³** — mass divided by volume — built from two fundamentals

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The SI symbol for the kilogram is —
a) m  b) k  c) kg  d) Kg
**Answer: c) kg** — m is the metre's symbol

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 0.0012 s equals —
a) 1.2 µs  b) 1.2 ms  c) 12 ms  d) 0.12 s
**Answer: b) 1.2 ms** — 0.0012 is 1.2 thousandths of a second — milliseconds

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** 0.6 km equals —
a) 60 m  b) 600 m and 60,000 cm  c) 6,000 cm only  d) 0.6 m
**Answer: b) 600 m and 60,000 cm** — kilo means a thousand: 0.6 × 1000 m, then × 100 for centimetres

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** One day, in seconds, is —
a) 1,440  b) 3,600  c) 86,400  d) 24,000
**Answer: c) 86,400** — 24 × 60 minutes = 1,440 minutes; × 60 = 86,400 seconds

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Readings averaging 12.17 m/s² against an accepted 9.81 m/s², sitting close together, are —
a) accurate and precise  b) precise but not accurate  c) accurate but not precise  d) neither
**Answer: b) precise but not accurate** — close to each other, far from the accepted value

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** The precise dart player is the one whose darts —
a) always hit the centre  b) land close to the same spot, which may or may not be the target  c) are newest  d) are thrown fastest
**Answer: b) land close to the same spot, which may or may not be the target** — precision is about the cluster, not the target
