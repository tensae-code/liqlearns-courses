# Measurement — quantities, units, prefixes, accuracy and precision (Grade 8) — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous measures are unreliable mainly because they vary from person to person.
**Answer: True** — availability and zero cost are their strengths; variation is the fault

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 0.5 hours is 1,800 seconds.
**Answer: True** — 0.5 × 60 minutes = 30 minutes; × 60 = 1,800 seconds

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A set of readings can be precise and still not accurate.
**Answer: True** — the 12.17 m/s² case is exactly that
