# Hands-on — Engineering design — bridges and loads

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build and load a model bridge
Build a small bridge from paper, sticks or string across two books. Add coins or stones one at a time and record how many it carries before it fails — and where it failed first.

## Redesign from the failure
Change one thing where your bridge failed — a support, a stronger deck — and test again. Say what changed and whether the bridge carried more.
