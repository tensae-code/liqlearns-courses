# Everyday technology around us

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's one thing — everyday technology around us — the technology in daily life and what it is used for, as Zimbabwe's ECD and Grade 2 guides teach it. Say what you already know about it out loud. Then read on: this is Zimbabwe's way in — its PlusOne Science and Technology guides teach this topic as a unit of their own, returning to it across the grades named in this lesson's sources.

## Sub lesson 1 — Everyday technology

Zimbabwe's ECD A and ECD B guides teach **everyday technology** as a named topic, and the Grade 2 guide returns to it as everyday technology and its uses: the technology around a child every day can be named and studied.

## Sub lesson 2 — What everyday technology is used for

The Grade 2 unit's own title carries the point: everyday technology **and its uses**. Each piece of technology is learned with the job it does in daily life.

## Sub lesson 3 — Noticing the technology around you

The topic's skill is noticing: a learner who can name the technology around them and say what each piece is for has begun the science-and-technology way of looking that Zimbabwe's whole subject builds.

## The key ideas

- **Everyday technology** — the technology of daily life, named.
- **What everyday technology is used for** — each technology learned with its use.
- **Noticing the technology around you** — noticing and naming the technology around you.
