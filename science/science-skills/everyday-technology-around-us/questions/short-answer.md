# Everyday technology around us — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Everyday technology" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's ECD A and ECD B guides teach **everyday technology** as a named topic, and the Grade 2 guide returns to it as everyday technology and its uses: the technology around a child every day can be named and studied.

**2.** What does the part "What everyday technology is used for" teach?
**Answer:** The Grade 2 unit's own title carries the point: everyday technology **and its uses**.

**3.** What does the part "Noticing the technology around you" teach?
**Answer:** The topic's skill is noticing: a learner who can name the technology around them and say what each piece is for has begun the science-and-technology way of looking that Zimbabwe's whole subject builds.
