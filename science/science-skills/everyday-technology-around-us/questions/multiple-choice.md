# Everyday technology around us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) What everyday technology is used for  b) Noticing the technology around you  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Everyday technology
**Answer: d) Everyday technology**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about the technology of daily life, named?
a) Everyday technology  b) What everyday technology is used for  c) Noticing the technology around you
**Answer: a) Everyday technology**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about each technology learned with its use?
a) Everyday technology  b) What everyday technology is used for  c) Noticing the technology around you
**Answer: b) What everyday technology is used for**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about noticing and naming the technology around you?
a) Everyday technology  b) What everyday technology is used for  c) Noticing the technology around you
**Answer: c) Noticing the technology around you**
