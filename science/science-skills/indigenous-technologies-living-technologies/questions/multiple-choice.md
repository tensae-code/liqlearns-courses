# Indigenous technologies — living technologies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Technology, as this lesson teaches it, is…
a) Only machines and screens  b) Knowledge applied to materials to make useful things  c) Only work done in factories  d) Anything new
**Answer: b) Knowledge applied to materials to make useful things**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Fibre technology is…
a) Taking fibres from the land and making them into useful things  b) Growing cotton only  c) Buying string  d) Cutting wood
**Answer: a) Taking fibres from the land and making them into useful things**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A woodwork maker must know…
a) Only the price of wood  b) How to paint  c) The wood's grain, strength and season  d) Nothing — wood shapes itself
**Answer: c) The wood's grain, strength and season**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Food preservation is…
a) Keeping food in a cupboard  b) Eating food quickly  c) Buying food in tins  d) Keeping food good beyond its season, by methods that defeat spoiling
**Answer: d) Keeping food good beyond its season, by methods that defeat spoiling**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A preservation method becomes trusted because…
a) It is old  b) It is tested over generations — methods that work are kept and taught  c) It is expensive  d) An elder said so once
**Answer: b) It is tested over generations — methods that work are kept and taught**

**6.** (Whole lesson) The guide calls these technologies "living" because…
a) They are made of living things only  b) They move  c) They are still practised and taught, carrying a people's knowledge in the making  d) They are new
**Answer: c) They are still practised and taught, carrying a people's knowledge in the making**
