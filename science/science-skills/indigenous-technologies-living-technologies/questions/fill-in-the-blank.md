# Indigenous technologies — living technologies — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) _____ technology takes fibres from the land and makes them into useful things.
**Answer: Fibre**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Fibres woven and made into cloth and garments are _____.
**Answer: textiles**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Food _____ keeps food good beyond its season.
**Answer: preservation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Together, the four are called _____ technologies.
**Answer: living**
