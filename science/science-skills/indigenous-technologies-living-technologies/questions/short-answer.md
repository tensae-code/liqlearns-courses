# Indigenous technologies — living technologies — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the three crafts of sub lesson 1 and the material each one works.
**Answer: Fibre technology — plant and animal fibres; textiles — fibres woven into cloth and garments; woodwork — wood shaped into tools, vessels and structures.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is each craft a science of its material?
**Answer: Because the maker must know what the material will do — where fibre is strong, how wood splits and holds — and that knowledge is tested every time the made thing is used.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What makes food preservation a technology and not just storage?
**Answer: Preservation is a method that defeats spoiling — each step does a job against a way food goes bad — and the methods were tested, kept and handed down over generations.**

**4.** (Whole lesson) What three things does the guide join inside living technologies?
**Answer: Land, resources and culture — the making carries a people's knowledge of all three at once.**
