# Hands-on — Indigenous technologies — living technologies

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The technology walk
Walk through your home and list every made thing you find that comes from fibre, textile work, woodwork, or food preparation and preservation. Beside each, write the material it is made from and the knowledge its maker needed.

## Preserve something
With an adult at home, preserve one food by a method your family knows — drying, salting, cooking into a preserve, or another handed-down way. Write the steps in order, and say what each step does to stop the food spoiling.

## Work a fibre
Take one natural fibre you can find — string, wool, plant fibre, or cloth thread — and make the smallest useful thing you can from it: a tied bundle, a braid, a wrapped handle. Write what the fibre let you do and where it fought back.
