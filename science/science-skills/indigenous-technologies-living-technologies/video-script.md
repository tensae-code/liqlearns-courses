# Video script — Indigenous technologies — living technologies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Indigenous technologies — living technologies** — Canada's way: technology that lives in people's hands.

Technology is not only machines. Take a fibre from the land and make it useful — that is fibre technology. Weave it into cloth — textiles. Shape wood into tools and vessels — woodwork. Each one is a science of its material, learned by doing and handed down.
And food: preparing it well, and preserving it so it keeps beyond its season — those are technologies too, and a community's life depends on them. Land, resources and culture, joined in the making. That is why the guide calls them living technologies.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
