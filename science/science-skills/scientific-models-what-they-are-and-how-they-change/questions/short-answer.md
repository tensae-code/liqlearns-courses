# Scientific models — what they are and how they change — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name two ways models save time, money and lives, from the book's examples.
**Answer: Crash-test dummies take the crash instead of a person; NASA's astronaut-training aircraft lets astronauts practise without the risk of space — and wind tunnels test designs before a real plane is built.**

**2.** Which everyday things does the book name as models that communicate ideas?
**Answer: Maps, drawings, recipes and the globe.**
