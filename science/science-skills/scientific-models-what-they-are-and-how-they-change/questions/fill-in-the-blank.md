# Scientific models — what they are and how they change — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** A model is a _______ of a thing or event that helps us understand the world.
**Answer: representation**

**2.** Models are not perfect, and they _______ when new information arrives.
**Answer: change**

**3.** Engineers test predictions in wind _______.
**Answer: tunnels**
