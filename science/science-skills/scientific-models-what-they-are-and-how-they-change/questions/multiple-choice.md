# Scientific models — what they are and how they change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The three kinds of model are…
a) big, medium, small  b) physical, computer and mental  c) old, new, future  d) drawn, spoken, sung  
**Answer: b) physical, computer and mental**

**2.** The more information a scientist gathers, the model becomes…
a) less accurate  b) more accurate  c) a law  d) useless  
**Answer: b) more accurate**

**3.** The solar-system model changed from…
a) Sun-centred to Earth-centred  b) Earth-centred to Sun-centred  c) flat to round  d) mental to physical  
**Answer: b) Earth-centred to Sun-centred**
