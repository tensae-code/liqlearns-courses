# Hands-on — Scientific models — what they are and how they change

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The sketch-artist test
Work in a pair: one describes a hidden object (the witness), the other draws (the artist). Show the object, add one more detail, and redraw. Record what the added information did to the model's accuracy.

## Sort the models
Sort six named models — globe, weather computer run, a mental picture of the water cycle, a wind-tunnel plane, a map, a crash-test dummy — into physical, computer and mental, and say for each what it is used for: communicating, testing, or saving time, money and lives.
