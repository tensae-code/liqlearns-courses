# Science and technology careers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Science and technology careers" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's ECD B guide already teaches **science and technology careers**, and the Grade 1 and Grade 2 guides carry a careers unit of the same name: the jobs are named from the earliest levels, not saved for the end of school.

**2.** What does the part "Career opportunities" teach?
**Answer:** Grades 3, 4, 5 and 6 each teach the unit as **career opportunities**: the opportunities science and technology open, returned to every year as the learner's science grows.

**3.** What does the part "Choosing a science path" teach?
**Answer:** A career named early can be aimed at.
