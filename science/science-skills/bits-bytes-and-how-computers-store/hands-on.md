# Hands-on — Bits, bytes and how computers store

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work through the lesson's parts
Take the lesson's parts one by one — Computer memory built from binary (guide evidence); One binary digit is a bit — and for each one, find or make one real example where you live: point at it, draw it, or act it out, and say in one sentence how it matches the lesson. Check your examples against the lesson.

## Draw the lesson
Draw the lesson's central object or set-up from the first sub lesson, label its parts, and write under the drawing the one thing this lesson teaches.
