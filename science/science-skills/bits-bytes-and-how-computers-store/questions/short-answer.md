# Bits, bytes and how computers store — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: One binary digit is a bit, four bits a nibble, eight bits a byte, and 1024 bytes make a kilobyte, then megabyte, gigabyte, terabyte and petabyte; learners compare the storage of their own and their parents' phones.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Shikshak Nirdeshika Vigyan Grade 10 (guide)*, printed p. 404.**
