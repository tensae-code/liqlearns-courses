# Video script — Science and industry — technology in daily life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Science and industry — technology in daily life** — Ghana's way of teaching where the made things around us come from.

Look around you. A phone, a bicycle, a tin of food, a bottle of medicine. None of these grew on a tree. People made them — and making them well takes science. That is science and industry.
Every technological product has a use. The first job is to name the product and say what it does for us.
Industry is science organised to make things — and to keep them. Food is part of it: foods are preserved — dried, smoked, salted, tinned, kept cold — so they last and stay safe to eat.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
