# Science and industry — technology in daily life — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A made thing with a use is called a technological _____.
**Answer: product**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the product, then say its _____ — the two travel together.
**Answer: use**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) _____ is science organised to make products — and to keep them.
**Answer: Industry**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Drying, smoking, salting, sealing and cooling are ways of _____ food.
**Answer: preserving**
