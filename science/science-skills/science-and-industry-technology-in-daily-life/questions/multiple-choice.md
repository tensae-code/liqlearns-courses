# Science and industry — technology in daily life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A technological product is…
a) Anything found in nature  b) A made thing with a use  c) Only a machine with an engine  d) A thing nobody uses
**Answer: b) A made thing with a use**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Ghana's Basic 2 curriculum files technological products together with…
a) Their prices  b) Their colours  c) Their uses  d) Their makers' names
**Answer: c) Their uses**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A product is understood when you can say…
a) Its use  b) Its weight  c) Where it was bought  d) Who owns it
**Answer: a) Its use**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Industry is…
a) Science forgotten  b) Making, organised — science put to work  c) Only farming  d) Buying finished goods
**Answer: b) Making, organised — science put to work**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is a way of preserving food?
a) Leaving it in the sun uncovered for flies  b) Drying it  c) Keeping it warm and wet  d) Opening the tin early
**Answer: b) Drying it**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) In Ghana's curriculum, "science and industry" is a sub-strand inside…
a) Strand 1 Diversity of Matter  b) Strand 4 Forces and Energy  c) Strand 5 Humans and the Environment  d) Strand 3 Systems
**Answer: c) Strand 5 Humans and the Environment**
