# Science and industry — technology in daily life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name three technological products in your home and give the use of each.
**Answer:** Any three made things, each paired with what it does for the people who use it — for example a bicycle (carries you), a lamp (gives light after dark), a bucket (carries and keeps water).

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does Ghana's curriculum teach products and their uses together?
**Answer:** Because a product is not understood until you can say its use — a thing whose use nobody can name is a thing nobody needs.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How are science and industry one thing, not two?
**Answer:** Making well takes knowledge — which material will hold, which will rust, how hot, how long, how to join and seal — and that knowledge is science, put to work in industry.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Name two ways foods are preserved, and say what preservation does.
**Answer:** Any two of drying, smoking, salting, sealing in tins, cooling. Preservation stops or slows food spoiling, keeping food safe until it is eaten.
