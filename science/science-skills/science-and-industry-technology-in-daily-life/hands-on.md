# Hands-on — Science and industry — technology in daily life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The product walk
Walk around your home or schoolyard and list ten technological products. Beside each one write its use — what it does for the people who use it. If you cannot say a product's use, that is the one to ask about.

## The preservation shelf
In your kitchen or a nearby shop, find three preserved foods. For each one, say how it is preserved — dried, smoked, salted, tinned or kept cold — and what would happen to that food without its preservation.
