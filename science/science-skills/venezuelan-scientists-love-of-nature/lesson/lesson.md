# Venezuelan scientists — love of nature

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's title — Venezuelan scientists — love of nature. Say out loud anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on: this is Venezuela's way in — its Grade 1 *Explorar* book closes with a reading about one of the country's own scientists: Francisco Tamayo, the botanist who explored and classified Venezuela's vegetation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Francisco Tamayo, botanist of Venezuela

The reading tells of **Francisco Tamayo**, a Venezuelan **botanist** — a scientist of plants. He **explored** the country's vegetation, travelling and observing the plants of Venezuela where they grow.

## Sub lesson 2 — Classifying plants, seeking medicines

Tamayo **classified** Venezuela's vegetation — sorting its plants into their kinds — and **sought medicines from plants**: the country's plants studied not only to be named, but to heal. His work joins love of nature to science: know the plants of your own land, and they can serve its people.
