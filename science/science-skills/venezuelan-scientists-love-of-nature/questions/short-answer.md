# Venezuelan scientists — love of nature — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What two things did Tamayo do with Venezuela's plants?

**2.** Why does the reading sit in a science book for Grade 1?
**Answer: It shows children a scientist of their own country whose work began with loving and knowing the nature around him.**
