# Venezuelan scientists — love of nature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Francisco Tamayo was a…
a) sailor  b) botanist  c) pilot  d) builder  
**Answer: b) botanist**

**2.** Tamayo explored and classified Venezuela's…
a) rivers  b) mountains  c) vegetation  d) stars  
**Answer: c) vegetation**

**3.** From plants, Tamayo also sought…
a) fuels  b) medicines  c) metals  d) dyes only  
**Answer: b) medicines**
