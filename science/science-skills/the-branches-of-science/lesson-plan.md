# Lesson plan — The branches of science

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Science Skills · **Lesson:** The branches of science
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Basic Concepts of Science (Grade 7 General Science).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** science splits into branches by what it studies: life science studies living things, physical science studies non-living things, earth science studies the Earth — and above them, natural science studies nature while social science studies people
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on What science is — the learner knows what science is; now its map is drawn

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sort a study into life, physical or earth science and say why.
2. Split physical science into physics and chemistry.
3. Place natural vs social science above the three branches (teacher guide — evidence).
4. Name the three border subjects and the two fields each one joins.

## What you need
Paper and pencil; a handful of study topics from home and school to sort.

## If the learner is stuck
- Sorts by where a thing is found, not what is studied → ask "What is being studied — a living thing, a non-living thing, or the Earth itself?"
- Puts chemistry inside life science because medicines heal people → chemistry studies the substances; life science studies the living body they act on.
