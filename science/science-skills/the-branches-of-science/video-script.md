# Video script — The branches of science

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Science is one house with many rooms.

**Life science** — the room of living things.
**Physical science** — the room of non-living things. Inside it, two doors: **physics** and **chemistry**.
**Earth science** — the room of the Earth itself: rocks, volcanoes, weather.

Above them all: **natural science** studies nature. **Social science** studies people.
And some subjects stand in the doorway between rooms — biochemistry, biophysics, physical chemistry.

Give me a study. I will tell you its room.
