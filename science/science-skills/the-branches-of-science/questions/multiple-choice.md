# The branches of science — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Life science studies —
a) rocks and volcanoes  b) living things  c) light and heat  d) acids and metals
**Answer: b) living things** — its subject is living things

**2.** Physical science splits into —
a) biology and geology  b) physics and chemistry  c) astronomy and medicine  d) earth and life
**Answer: b) physics and chemistry** — physics + chemistry

**3.** Why a metal spoon heats up in tea belongs to —
a) life science  b) earth science  c) physics  d) social science
**Answer: c) physics** — heat and matter — physics

**4.** Biochemistry is a border subject because it joins —
a) geology and astronomy  b) chemistry and living things  c) physics and rocks  d) medicine and trade
**Answer: b) chemistry and living things** — chemical processes in living organisms

**5.** Social science studies —
a) nature under natural laws  b) people and their interactions  c) only plants  d) only machines
**Answer: b) people and their interactions** — the guide's wider split

**6.** How volcanoes form belongs to —
a) life science  b) physical science  c) earth science  d) biochemistry
**Answer: c) earth science** — the Earth's structure and processes
