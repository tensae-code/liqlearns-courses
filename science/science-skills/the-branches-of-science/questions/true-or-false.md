# The branches of science — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** Earth science studies the Earth's structure, properties and processes.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Biophysics studies chemical processes in rocks.
**Answer: False** — biophysics studies physical processes in living things

**3.** Natural science and social science are the same division.
**Answer: False** — natural studies nature; social studies people
