# Hands-on — The branches of science

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The sorting game
Write twelve studies on slips — three per branch, three borderline. In pairs, sort them against the clock; a sort is only kept when the pair can say WHY in one sentence.

## Branch map on the wall
Draw the branching map big, as a class, and pin it up: every later unit of this course will be pinned onto its branch.
