# Hands-on — Laboratory safety — hazard signs and protective equipment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cover-and-read drill
Pairs with the signs grid: one covers the meanings, the other reads signs by sight against the clock. Ten signs, then swap. A sign missed twice is drawn by hand until it sticks.

## Dress the worker
Draw a bench worker. Your partner names dangers one by one — splash! flame! sharp edge! — and you add the guarding equipment to the drawing, saying its job and its limit each time.
