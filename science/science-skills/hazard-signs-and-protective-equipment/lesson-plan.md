# Lesson plan — Laboratory safety — hazard signs and protective equipment

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Science Skills · **Lesson:** Laboratory safety — hazard signs and protective equipment
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Basic Concepts of Science (Grade 7 General Science).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** ten hazard signs warn by sight — corrosive, flammable, toxic and the rest — and protective equipment (glasses, gloves, face shield, extinguisher) reduces harm without removing the hazard
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Laboratory safety — the ten rules: the learner keeps the rules; now they read the laboratory's warnings and wear its protection

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Science Skills — Laboratory safety — the ten rules. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Match the ten hazard signs to their meanings by sight.
2. Match protective equipment to its job: safety glasses, fire extinguisher, gloves, face shield.
3. State the limit of protection: PPE reduces harm; it does not remove the hazard.

## If the learner is stuck
- Memorises words without the sights → drill sign-first, meaning-second, until the picture alone triggers the meaning in under three seconds.
- Thinks gloves make a hazard safe → PPE reduces harm; the hazard remains exactly as dangerous.
