# Video script — Laboratory safety — hazard signs and protective equipment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ten signs. Learn them by sight.

**Corrosive** — it eats material and skin. **No open flames.**
**Electrical hazard.** **Oxidising agent** — it feeds fire.
**Flammable** — it catches fire. **Harmful irritant.**
**Poison** — toxic. **Flammable gas.** **Explosive.** **High voltage.**

And the protection: **glasses** for eyes, **gloves** for hands, a **face shield**, an **extinguisher** for a young fire.
Remember the limit: protection **reduces** harm. It does **not remove** the hazard.
