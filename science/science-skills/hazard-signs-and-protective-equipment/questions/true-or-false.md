# Laboratory safety — hazard signs and protective equipment — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** With gloves on, a corrosive bottle is no longer hazardous.
**Answer: False** — PPE reduces harm; the hazard remains

**2.** Hazard signs must be readable by sight, at a glance.
**Answer: True** — that is their purpose

**3.** A fire extinguisher is protective equipment matched in this lesson.
**Answer: True**
