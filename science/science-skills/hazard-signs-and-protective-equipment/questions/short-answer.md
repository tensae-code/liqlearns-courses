# Laboratory safety — hazard signs and protective equipment — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1.** Name any four of the ten hazard meanings.
**Answer:** Any four of: corrosive; no open flames; electrical hazard; oxidising agent; flammable/combustible; harmful irritant; poison/toxic; flammable gas; explosive; high voltage.

**2.** Which equipment fights a young fire?
**Answer:** The fire extinguisher.

**3.** Complete the limit: PPE reduces harm but does not ______.
**Answer:** Remove the hazard.
