# Laboratory safety — hazard signs and protective equipment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The skull-style sign means —
a) corrosive  b) poison / toxic  c) electrical hazard  d) no open flames
**Answer: b) poison / toxic**

**2.** An oxidising agent is dangerous because it —
a) feeds fire  b) freezes skin  c) blocks light  d) smells bad
**Answer: a) feeds fire** — it supplies oxygen to burning

**3.** Safety glasses guard —
a) the hands  b) the eyes  c) the whole face  d) the floor
**Answer: b) the eyes**

**4.** PPE reduces harm but does NOT —
a) fit anyone  b) remove the hazard  c) cost money  d) need cleaning
**Answer: b) remove the hazard** — the lesson's limit sentence

**5.** A face shield guards —
a) the hands  b) the whole face  c) the eyes only  d) the bench
**Answer: b) the whole face**

**6.** "Flammable gas" and "flammable/combustible" are —
a) the same sign twice  b) two of the ten signs  c) not in the ten  d) safe signs
**Answer: b) two of the ten signs** — both are listed separately
