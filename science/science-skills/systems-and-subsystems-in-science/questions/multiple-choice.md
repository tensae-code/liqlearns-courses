# Systems and subsystems in science — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A system is a set of…
a) unrelated objects  b) structures, cycles and processes bound together and acting on each other  c) guesses  d) laws only  
**Answer: b) structures, cycles and processes bound together and acting on each other**

**2.** Which is NOT one of the book's examples of a system?
a) The human body  b) The planets round the Sun  c) The energy carried in a lightning bolt  d) A single word in a dictionary  
**Answer: d) A single word in a dictionary**

**3.** Systems are built of…
a) nothing smaller  b) smaller systems  c) only descriptions  d) guesses  
**Answer: b) smaller systems**
