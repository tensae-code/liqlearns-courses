# Systems and subsystems in science — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Give the book's own example of a person inside a larger system.
**Answer: You are part of your school — and the body is a system inside a larger one.**

**2.** What do the parts of a system do?
**Answer: They interact — they are bound together and act on each other, each part's working touching the others.**
