# Hands-on — Systems and subsystems in science

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Nested systems drawing
Draw three nested boxes for one of the book's examples — for the human body: a smaller system inside the body, the body, and the larger system the body sits in. Label each box and one way its parts interact.

## Find the interactions
For the planets round the Sun, write two sentences on how the parts act on each other — what holds the system together as one system, not a pile of parts.
