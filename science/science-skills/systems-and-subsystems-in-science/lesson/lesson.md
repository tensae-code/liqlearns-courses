# Systems and subsystems in science

## Do it first
Is your body one thing, or many things working as one? And are you yourself part of something bigger? Say your answers out loud. Then read on: this is Sudan's way in — its Grade 8 Natural Sciences teaches systems in science inside its Nature of Science unit.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a system is

A **system** is a **set of structures, cycles and processes bound together and acting on each other**. The book's examples cross all of science: **the human body**, **the planets round the Sun**, and **the energy carried in a lightning bolt** — living, space and energy systems, all systems by the same definition.

## Sub lesson 2 — Parts interact — and systems sit inside systems

The **parts of a system interact** — each part's working touches the others. And **systems are built of smaller systems**: **you are part of your school**; **the body is a system inside a larger one**. Every system is at once a whole to its parts and a part of a bigger whole — its subsystems below it, and the larger system above it.
