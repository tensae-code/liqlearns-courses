# Hands-on — Science words — glossary of action verbs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Verb cards
Make seven cards, one for each verb in the guide's glossary: annotate, classify, compare, define, demonstrate, describe, observe. On the back of each card write, in your own words, the job that verb sets.

## Sort the questions
Write seven short science questions, each using a different verb from the cards (for example: "Define…", "Compare…", "Observe…"). Shuffle them, then sort each question under the card whose verb it uses, and say the job out loud before you answer any of them.

## Answer to the verb
Pick two questions with different verbs about the same thing — for example "Describe the leaf" and "Classify the leaf". Answer both, and check: did each answer do its own verb's job, and not the other's?
