# Science words — glossary of action verbs — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (The glossary and why it is there, The seven verbs at work), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 2 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — underlining the action verb in a question, saying what job it sets, and matching sample answers to the verb that asked for them.
