# Science words — glossary of action verbs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Where does the glossary of action verbs sit in Trinidad and Tobago's guide?
a) On the cover  b) At the closing end of the guide  c) In a different book  d) It is not in the guide
**Answer: b) At the closing end of the guide**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The glossary is there so learners know…
a) The examination dates  b) What each instruction word asks  c) The teachers' names  d) Which form they are in
**Answer: b) What each instruction word asks**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) "Classify" asks you to…
a) State an exact meaning  b) Sort things into groups by what they share  c) Look carefully  d) Add labels
**Answer: b) Sort things into groups by what they share**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) "Compare" asks you to…
a) Give one thing's features only  b) Show a thing working  c) Set things side by side — alike and different  d) Write the exact meaning
**Answer: c) Set things side by side — alike and different**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) "Observe" asks you to…
a) Look carefully and notice what is actually there  b) Sort into groups  c) Explain with labels  d) Recite a definition
**Answer: a) Look carefully and notice what is actually there**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The lesson's habit, in order, is…
a) Answer, then check the verb  b) Find the verb, say its job, then answer  c) Guess the job from the marks  d) Copy the glossary
**Answer: b) Find the verb, say its job, then answer**
