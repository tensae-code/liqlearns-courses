# Science words — glossary of action verbs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does the guide's closing glossary define, and why is it part of a science course?
**Answer:** It defines the curriculum's action verbs — the instruction words — because a learner can know the science and still miss a question if they do not know what the verb in it asks for.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name any four of the glossary's verbs and the job each one sets.
**Answer:** Any four of: annotate — add notes or labels that explain; classify — sort into groups by what they share; compare — set side by side, alike and different; define — state the exact meaning; demonstrate — show by doing or example; describe — give an account of a thing's features; observe — look carefully and notice what is actually there.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A question says "Describe the leaf" and another says "Classify the leaf." How do the right answers differ?
**Answer:** "Describe" wants an account of the leaf's features; "classify" wants the leaf sorted into a group by what it shares with others — different verbs, different jobs, so different answers.

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) What three steps does the lesson give for meeting a new question?
**Answer:** Find the verb, say its job in your own words, then answer — in that order.
