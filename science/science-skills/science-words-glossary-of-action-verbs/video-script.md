Today: **Science words — glossary of action verbs** — Trinidad and Tobago's way: end the guide by defining the words that give the instructions.

Every question tells you what to do with one verb. Trinidad and Tobago's Integrated Science guide closes with a glossary of those verbs: annotate, classify, compare, define, demonstrate, describe, observe.
Each verb is a different job. "Define" wants the exact meaning. "Compare" wants two things side by side, alike and different. "Observe" wants what you actually see — not what you think.
So before you answer, find the verb, and say its job. Then your answer does the job the question asked for.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
