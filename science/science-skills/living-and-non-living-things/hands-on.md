# Hands-on — Living and non-living things

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The characteristics checklist
Write the six characteristics as a pocket checklist. Carry it for a day; test ten things against it and log the verdicts.

## Hard-case court
In a group, prosecute the hardest case — 'the river is alive!' — and let the checklist judge. The loser must state the winning argument in full.

## Chick or seed watch
Where a hen sits or a seed sprouts near you, watch the making-young characteristic happen across days. Date your observations.
