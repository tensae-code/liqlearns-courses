# Living and non-living things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which of these is living?**
a) a stone  b) a tree  c) a chair
**Answer: b) a tree** — the tree grows, feeds, breathes and seeds.

**2. A river moves, but it is non-living because it does not —**
a) get wet  b) grow, feed or make young  c) flow downhill
**Answer: b) grow, feed or make young**

**3. Which of these is non-living?**
a) a seed  b) a cloud  c) a fish
**Answer: b) a cloud** — clouds move but do not grow, feed or make young.
