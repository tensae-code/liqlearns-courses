# Video script — Living and non-living things

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Does it grow? Does it feed? Does it breathe?
Does it make young of its kind?
Yes — **living**. No — **non-living**, however it moves.
The tree stands still and lives. The river runs and does not. Now sort the world.
