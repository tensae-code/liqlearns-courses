# Recording and editing audio and video — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Recording with Voice Recorder on computer and phone; cutting and joining audio with MP3 Cutter and Joiner and with the phone's Audio Editor and Music Editor, setting start and end points; audio file formats MP3, WAV, AAC and WMA; cutting and joining video in the computer's Video Editor and the phone's Video Trimmer, Merger and Joiner apps, finishing and exporting the edited clip.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Shikshak Nirdeshika Vigyan Grade 10 (guide)*, printed p. 430.**
