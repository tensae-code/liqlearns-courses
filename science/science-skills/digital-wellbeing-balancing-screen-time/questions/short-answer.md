# Digital wellbeing — balancing screen time — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Staying mentally, physically, socially and emotionally healthy by balancing online and offline time; the practices — set screen time, set age limits for social media, no gadgets at meals or before sleep, notifications off at the workplace; the apps named (Digital Wellbeing, Digital Detox, Forest, Google Family Link and others) and the iPhone's Screen Time with Downtime and App Limits; the teacher box adds the digital footprint — the traceable record of everything done online — and its lasting effect on reputation, credit rating, jobs and relationships, with employers checking social media before hiring.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Shikshak Nirdeshika Vigyan Grade 10 (guide)*, printed p. 426.**
