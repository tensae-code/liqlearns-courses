# NCSE assessment — how final marks are built — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State the three coursework shares, in form order.
**Answer:** Form 1 coursework counts 15%, Form 2 coursework counts 30% and Form 3 coursework counts 15% of the final mark.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) How much of the final mark is coursework in total, and how do you know?
**Answer:** 60% — the three shares add up: 15% + 30% + 15% = 60%.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What counts toward the rest of the final mark, and what does the final mark lead to?
**Answer:** Examinations count toward the rest of the final mark, and the built-up final mark leads to NCSE certification.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this design make Form 1 work matter, even though Form 1 is the first year?
**Answer:** Because Form 1 coursework is already 15% of the final mark — work done in the first form is part of the mark that leads to certification, not practice that is thrown away.
