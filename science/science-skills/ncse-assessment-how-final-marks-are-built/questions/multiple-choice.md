# NCSE assessment — how final marks are built — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Form 1 coursework counts what share of the final mark?
a) 30%  b) 15%  c) 40%  d) 60%
**Answer: b) 15%**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which form's coursework counts the biggest share?
a) Form 1  b) Form 3  c) Form 2  d) All equal
**Answer: c) Form 2**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Form 2 coursework counts…
a) 15%  b) 20%  c) 25%  d) 30%
**Answer: d) 30%**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The three coursework shares together make…
a) 50%  b) 60%  c) 75%  d) 100%
**Answer: b) 60%**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The rest of the final mark comes from…
a) Attendance  b) Examinations  c) Homework alone  d) Nothing — coursework is everything
**Answer: b) Examinations**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The built-up final mark leads to…
a) A class prize  b) Form 4 entry only  c) NCSE certification  d) A new textbook
**Answer: c) NCSE certification**
