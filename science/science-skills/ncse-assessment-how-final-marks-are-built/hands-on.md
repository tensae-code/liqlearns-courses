# Hands-on — NCSE assessment — how final marks are built

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the mark as a bar
Draw a bar 100 squares long (or 100 cm of string marked in tens). Colour 15 squares for Form 1 coursework, 30 for Form 2 coursework and 15 for Form 3 coursework. Label the uncoloured 40 squares: examinations. Check your bar: 15 + 30 + 15 = 60 coloured, 40 left for examinations.

## Say the shares
Cover the bar and recite the three shares in form order — Form 1: 15%, Form 2: 30%, Form 3: 15% — then uncover and check. Repeat until you can say them without the bar.

## Plan backwards
Write one thing you will do in each form that coursework can count — a project, a practical, a piece of written work — and beside it the share that form carries. This is the guide's design turned into your own plan.
