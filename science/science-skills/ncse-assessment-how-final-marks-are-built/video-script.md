Today: **NCSE assessment — how final marks are built** — Trinidad and Tobago's own way of building a final mark.

Your final mark is not one examination at the end. Coursework you do along the way counts, form by form: Form 1 coursework counts 15%, Form 2 coursework counts 30% — the biggest share — and Form 3 coursework counts 15%. Add them: 60% of the final mark is coursework.
The rest comes from examinations, and the built-up final mark leads to NCSE certification.
So work done in every form matters — not only the last examination.
