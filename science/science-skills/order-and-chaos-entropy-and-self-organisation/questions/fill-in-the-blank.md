# Order and chaos — entropy and self-organisation — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A process that cannot run back is _____.
**Answer: irreversible**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The measure of a system's disorder is _____.
**Answer: entropy**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In a closed system left to itself, entropy _____.
**Answer: grows**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Order arising by itself in an open system is _____.
**Answer: self-organisation**
