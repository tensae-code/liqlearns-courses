# Order and chaos — entropy and self-organisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A reversible process is one that…
a) Never happened  b) Can run back along its path and restore the start  c) Runs only forwards  d) Needs no energy
**Answer: b) Can run back along its path and restore the start**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Burning and mixing are examples of…
a) Reversible processes  b) Self-organisation  c) Irreversible processes  d) Measurement
**Answer: c) Irreversible processes**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Entropy measures…
a) Its temperature only  b) Its size  c) Its speed  d) A system's disorder
**Answer: d) A system's disorder**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In a closed system left to itself, entropy…
a) Grows — order spreads into disorder  b) Shrinks to zero  c) Stays frozen  d) Turns into mass
**Answer: a) Grows — order spreads into disorder**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Self-organisation happens in…
a) Closed boxes only  b) Open systems, with matter and energy flowing through  c) Empty space  d) Systems with no energy
**Answer: b) Open systems, with matter and energy flowing through**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Self-organisation's conditions, as the section closes, are…
a) Cold and darkness  b) Silence and rest  c) Openness and flow  d) Chance alone
**Answer: c) Openness and flow**
