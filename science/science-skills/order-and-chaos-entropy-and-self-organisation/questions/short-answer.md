# Order and chaos — entropy and self-organisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sort melting-and-refreezing and burning: which is reversible, and why?
**Answer:** Melting and refreezing can run back along the path, restoring the start — reversible; burning cannot be run back — irreversible.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State §7.2's claim about a closed system.
**Answer:** Left to itself, its entropy grows: orderliness spreads into chaos and does not gather itself back.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What is self-organisation?
**Answer:** The arising of ordered structures by themselves in an open system.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why is self-organisation not entropy's exception?
**Answer:** Because the order is built in an open system with energy flowing through — the wider whole pays the entropy price; the lesson calls order entropy's partner, not its exception.
