Today: **Order and chaos** — Armenia's way.

Some processes can run back. Most cannot — burning and mixing run one way.

Disorder has a measure: entropy. In a closed system, it grows.

But open a system to energy flowing through, and order can build itself — self-organisation.

Now you. Say what entropy measures, and what self-organisation needs. Say it back — in your own words.
