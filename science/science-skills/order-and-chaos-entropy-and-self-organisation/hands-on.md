# Hands-on — Order and chaos — entropy and self-organisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shuffle and sort
Shuffle a pack of cards and note the time order took to destroy; then sort it and note what the sorting cost you — effort in, order out. Say which process was reversible, and what your sorting needed from outside the pack.

## Mix and watch
Stir ink into water and watch: does it ever unmix while you watch? Write the one-way sentence the jar proves, in the lesson's words.
