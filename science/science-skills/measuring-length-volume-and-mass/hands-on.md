# Hands-on — Measuring length, volume and mass

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: "How is matter measured?": length with the ruler and the metre tape (the metre is 100 centimetres); the volume of liquids with graduated vessels in litres (the litre is 1000 millilitres); and the volume of a solid by the displacement method — the rise in the water level in a graduated cup Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
