# Measuring length, volume and mass — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: How is matter measured?.
**Answer:** "How is matter measured?": length with the ruler and the metre tape (the metre is 100 centimetres); the volume of liquids with graduated vessels in litres (the litre is 1000 millilitres); and the volume of a solid by the displacement method — the rise in the water level in a graduated cup.

**2.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** How is matter measured? — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
