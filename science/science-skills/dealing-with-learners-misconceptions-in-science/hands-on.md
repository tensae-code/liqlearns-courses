# Hands-on — Dealing with learners' misconceptions in science

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Surface one first idea
Ask someone at home a science question the lesson's way — for example, where a plant's food comes from — and write down their first idea exactly as they say it. Then say, out loud, how a teacher following Greece's guide would surface that idea and work on it rather than simply correcting it.

## Two columns: corrected, or worked on?
Draw two columns. In the first, write what happens when a wrong idea is only corrected ('No — the right answer is…'). In the second, write what happens when it is surfaced and worked on. Check both columns against the lesson.
