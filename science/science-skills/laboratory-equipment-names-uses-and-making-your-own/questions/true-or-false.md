# Laboratory equipment — names, uses, and making your own — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A magnifier and a microscope do the same job at the same power.
**Answer: False** — the microscope magnifies far beyond a hand lens

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Home-made apparatus follows the same safety rules as bought kit.
**Answer: True** — a home-made burner burns the same

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A funnel guides pouring and filtering.
**Answer: True**
