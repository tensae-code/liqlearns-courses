# Laboratory equipment — names, uses, and making your own — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Wire gauze is used to —
a) stir liquids  b) spread a flame's heat under a vessel  c) hold test tubes  d) magnify samples
**Answer: b) spread a flame's heat under a vessel**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A dropper's job is to —
a) heat small samples  b) add liquid drop by drop  c) weigh powders  d) hold tubes upright
**Answer: b) add liquid drop by drop**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** For evaporating a salty solution you must first —
a) choose kit, name hazards, state precautions  b) light the burner  c) taste the solution  d) pour it away
**Answer: a) choose kit, name hazards, state precautions** — the task's required order

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The test-tube holder exists because —
a) tubes are heavy  b) a heated tube must be gripped safely, not held bare  c) racks are expensive  d) tubes roll
**Answer: b) a heated tube must be gripped safely, not held bare**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The making project builds how many tools?
a) three  b) four  c) six  d) thirteen
**Answer: c) six** — holder, burner, rack, beaker, gauze, funnel

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The project's principle is that junior-school science —
a) needs only imported kit  b) does not need expensive apparatus  c) cannot be done locally  d) needs no safety rules
**Answer: b) does not need expensive apparatus** — right shapes, honestly made
