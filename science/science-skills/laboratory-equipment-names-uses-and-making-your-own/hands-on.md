# Hands-on — Laboratory equipment — names, uses, and making your own

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The making project
Groups build the six tools from local materials across the week. Each tool is presented with: what it is, what it replaces, one honest weakness against the bought version.

## Evaporation, cold rehearsal
Rehearse the evaporation set-up with cold water only: assemble the chosen kit, walk the steps aloud, name each hazard at the moment it would appear. No flame until the rehearsal is faultless.
