# Video script — Laboratory equipment — names, uses, and making your own

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Thirteen tools. Each has a job.

The **rack** holds tubes. The **holder** grips the hot one.
The **funnel** guides your pouring. The **gauze** spreads the flame.
**Tongs** reach what hands must not. The **dropper** counts in drops.
The **balance** weighs. The **microscope** makes the small big.

No kit at your school? **Make your own** — a holder, a burner, a rack, a beaker, a gauze, a funnel — from what your town already has.
Science does not need expensive tools. It needs the **right shapes**, honestly made.
