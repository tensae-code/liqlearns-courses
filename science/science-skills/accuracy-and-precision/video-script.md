# Video script — Measurement — accuracy and precision

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four groups boiled water. The true value: **100 degrees**.

Group A's numbers hug each other — 94, 95, 95, 94. Tight! But low.
Group C's numbers sit **on** 100.

Two words. **Accuracy**: close to the true value.
**Precision**: close to **each other**.

Group A is precise — and **not accurate**.
A tight set that misses the truth is warning you: something pulls **every** reading the same way.
Check the instrument. Check the method. That is the lesson.
