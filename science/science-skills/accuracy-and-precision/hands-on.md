# Hands-on — Measurement — accuracy and precision

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five throws
Throw five stones (or bottle caps) at a ground mark. Measure each landing's distance. Judge the set for accuracy (near the mark?) and precision (near each other?) — the two verdicts are written separately.

## The honest ruler test
Measure the same desk edge five times with the class ruler, writing every reading before looking at the last. Judge your own five: accurate against a second ruler? precise among themselves?
