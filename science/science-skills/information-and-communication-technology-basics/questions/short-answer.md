# Information and communication technology — the basics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: What ICT is.
**Answer:** Information and communication technology — ICT — is the set of tools we use to create, collect, analyse and exchange information.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Two halves — communication and recording.
**Answer:** Class 4 splits ICT into two halves: communication technology, which carries information between people, and recording technology, which keeps information so it can be used again.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Why sharing information matters.
**Answer:** Class 5 gives the reason ICT matters: shared information saves lives.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** What ICT is → Two halves — communication and recording → Why sharing information matters — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
