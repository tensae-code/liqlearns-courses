# Information and communication technology — the basics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) What ICT is  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) What ICT is**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Two halves — communication and recording  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Two halves — communication and recording**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Why sharing information matters  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Why sharing information matters**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: information and communication technology (ICT) — the tools to create, collect, analyse and exchange information — split into communication technology and recording technology, as Bangladesh's Classes 3, 4 and 5 teach it. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Bangladesh, Grade 3 — *Elementary Science Class 3*, printed p. 128  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Bangladesh, Grade 3 — *Elementary Science Class 3*, printed p. 128**
