# Venezuelan popular inventors — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** How does a popular inventor differ from a laboratory inventor?
**Answer: The popular inventor works from inside the community, with what is at hand, on the needs of everyday life around them.**

**2.** Why does the book place this reading among its complementary readings?
**Answer: To close the book with Venezuela's own inventors — science and invention shown as things the community itself does.**
