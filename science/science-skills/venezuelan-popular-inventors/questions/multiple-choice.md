# Venezuelan popular inventors — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A popular inventor invents from…
a) a great laboratory only  b) inside the community  c) another country  d) books alone  
**Answer: b) inside the community**

**2.** Community technology is technology…
a) imported whole  b) built by and for the community  c) only for factories  d) never used  
**Answer: b) built by and for the community**

**3.** The inventor begins by seeing…
a) a prize  b) a need in everyday life  c) a customer abroad  d) a machine  
**Answer: b) a need in everyday life**
