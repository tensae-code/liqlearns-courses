# Venezuelan popular inventors

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's title — Venezuelan popular inventors. Say out loud anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on: this is Venezuela's way in — its Grade 2 *Comunidad con Ciencia* closes with complementary readings about a Venezuelan popular inventor and the technology a community makes for itself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The popular inventor

The reading tells of a **Venezuelan popular inventor** — an inventor from the people, not from a great laboratory: someone who sees a need in everyday life and **invents** the thing that meets it, with what is at hand.

## Sub lesson 2 — Community technology

What such inventors make is **community technology**: technology built by and for the community, fitted to its own problems and materials. The reading's point stands beside the scientists' readings of this course: invention is not only done in institutes — it is done in neighbourhoods too.
