# Exercise-book sessions — learning by doing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: A book of sessions, not readings.
**Answer:** Bangladesh's Science Exercise Books are built differently from their Investigative Study companions: every unit is a set of sessions — things done, written and checked — not pages read.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Done, written, checked.
**Answer:** A session has three beats: the thing is done with the hands, then written down by the learner, then checked.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Bangladesh's own way at this level.
**Answer:** From Class 6, Bangladesh runs the two books as a pair: the Investigative Study to read and question, the Exercise Book to do.
