# Exercise-book sessions — learning by doing

## Sub lesson 1 — A book of sessions, not readings

Bangladesh's Science Exercise Books are built differently from their Investigative Study companions: every unit is a set of sessions — things done, written and checked — not pages read. The book's own form is its first lesson, at printed p. 1.

## Sub lesson 2 — Done, written, checked

A session has three beats: the thing is done with the hands, then written down by the learner, then checked. What is only read can be forgotten by evening; what is done, written and checked stays.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bangladesh's own way at this level

From Class 6, Bangladesh runs the two books as a pair: the Investigative Study to read and question, the Exercise Book to do. Learning by doing is the country's own way of teaching science at this level, and this lesson names it so learners know which book they are holding, and why.
