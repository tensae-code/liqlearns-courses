# Measuring: volume and displacement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Volume is —**
a) how heavy a body is  b) how much space a body takes  c) how hot a body is
**Answer: b) how much space a body takes**

**2. Volume is measured with —**
a) a balance  b) a graduated cylinder  c) a shadow
**Answer: b) a graduated cylinder**

**3. A body's volume equals —**
a) the water it pushes aside  b) its colour  c) its shadow's length
**Answer: a) the water it pushes aside**

**4. 1 litre equals —**
a) 100 cm³  b) 1000 cm³  c) 10 cm³
**Answer: b) 1000 cm³**

**5. 1 m³ equals —**
a) 1000 litres  b) 100 litres  c) 60 litres
**Answer: a) 1000 litres**
