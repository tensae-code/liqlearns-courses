# Hands-on — Measuring: volume and displacement

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Displacement, run properly
With a graduated cylinder or a jar you have marked yourself, measure the three stones by displacement and write each volume in cm³. Order the stones by your numbers.

## Overflow method
Fill a cup to the brim over a tray, lower a stone in gently, and collect the water that spills. The spilled water's volume is the stone's volume — displacement without a cylinder.
