# Video script — Measuring: volume and displacement

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fill the cylinder. Read the level.
Lower the stone. Read again.
The **rise** is the stone's **volume**.
One cubic metre — **one thousand litres**. One litre — **one thousand cubic centimetres**.
