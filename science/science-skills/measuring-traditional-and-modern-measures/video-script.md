# Video script — Measuring: traditional and modern measures

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Long ago, the body was the ruler.
**Kend, sinzir, gat** — the arm measures.
Footsteps and shoe-lengths for the road.
The **shadow** told the time. The cock crowed the dawn.
The **balance** weighed mass.
But my arm is not your arm — so modern instruments came, one measure for everyone.
