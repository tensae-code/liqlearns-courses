# Hands-on — Measuring: traditional and modern measures

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The class kend survey
Every pupil measures the teacher's desk in their own kend or sinzir. Write all the numbers on the board — the spread of numbers IS the lesson on why standard measures exist.

## Build a shadow day
Mark a shadow's end every hour of one school day. Join the marks: you have built the oldest clock, and it works.
