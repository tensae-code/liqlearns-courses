# Hands-on — Measuring: traditional and modern measures

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The class kend survey
Every pupil measures the teacher's desk in their own kend or sinzir. Write all the numbers on the board — the spread of numbers IS the lesson on why standard measures exist.

## Build a shadow day
Mark a shadow's end every hour of one school day. Join the marks: you have built the oldest clock, and it works.

## The three-measurer test (Grade 7 growth)
In groups: measure the blackboard, a book's thickness and the table — first in spans and cubits, then with ruler or tape — three measurers each, every result written. Compare the two spreads on the board: the body spread is the old fault, measured; the instrument spread is the standard, proved.
