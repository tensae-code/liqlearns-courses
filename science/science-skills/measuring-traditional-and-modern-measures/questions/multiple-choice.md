# Measuring: traditional and modern measures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which of these is an arm-measure?**
a) kend  b) litre  c) kelvin
**Answer: a) kend**

**2. Time was traditionally told by —**
a) a thermometer  b) the shadow and cock-crow  c) a ruler
**Answer: b) the shadow and cock-crow**

**3. Mass was measured with —**
a) a balance  b) a shadow  c) footsteps
**Answer: a) a balance**

**4. The deep fault of body measures is —**
a) they are slow  b) they differ from person to person  c) they are old
**Answer: b) they differ from person to person**

**5. Mass is —**
a) the size of a body  b) the amount of particles (matter) in a body  c) the colour of a body
**Answer: b) the amount of particles (matter) in a body**
