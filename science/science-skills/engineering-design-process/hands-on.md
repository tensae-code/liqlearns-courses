# Hands-on — Engineering design process

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run the loop on paper
Pick a small problem — keeping a drink cold, carrying books without a bag. Write its criteria and its constraints, sketch two different designs, and say how you would test each one and what result would make you modify it.

## Judge a version
Take any made thing at home. State the problem it solves, guess its criteria and constraints, and name one modification a test might have forced on its designer.
