# Hands-on — Measuring time — from shadows to clocks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The class sundial
Build one dial per group in different sunny spots. Next day, all groups read the time at the same moment and compare against the school clock — whose dial reads truest, and why?

## Pulse partners
Pairs: one counts pulses, one times. Swap roles, repeat at rest and after one minute of jumping. Record all four numbers; say what changed and what the timed interval proved.
