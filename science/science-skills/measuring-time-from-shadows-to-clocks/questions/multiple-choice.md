# Measuring time — from shadows to clocks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a traditional way of telling time?
a) the stopwatch  b) birdsong at dawn  c) the calendar  d) the speedometer
**Answer: b) birdsong at dawn** — named with shadows and sun position

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The SI unit of time is the —
a) hour  b) minute  c) second  d) day
**Answer: c) second** — s — the others are larger measures around it

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A sundial is built from —
a) a mirror and lens  b) an upright stick and hourly shadow marks  c) a pendulum  d) sand in glass
**Answer: b) an upright stick and hourly shadow marks** — stick + marks where the shadow falls

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The marks are placed where —
a) the sun stands  b) the stick's shadow tip falls at each hour  c) the builder likes  d) the wind points
**Answer: b) the stick's shadow tip falls at each hour** — shadow-tip positions, hour by hour

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Comparing sundial and clock shows the angles between hour marks are —
a) all exactly equal  b) not all equal, though the hours are  c) random  d) backwards
**Answer: b) not all equal, though the hours are** — the book's own comparison

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A sundial cannot work —
a) at noon  b) on a cloudy night  c) on Tuesdays  d) near the equator
**Answer: b) on a cloudy night** — no sun, no shadow, no reading
