# Measuring time — from shadows to clocks — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three traditional time-tellers.
**Answer:** Shadows, the sun's position, birdsong at dawn.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do you lay at the shadow tip each hour?
**Answer:** A rock or card, marked with the hour.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State one honest limit of the sundial.
**Answer:** Any one: it fails under cloud, at night, or its hour angles are unequal while hours are equal.
