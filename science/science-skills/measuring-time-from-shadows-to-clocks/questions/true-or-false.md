# Measuring time — from shadows to clocks — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Clocks and watches are the scientific way of measuring time.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Once marking starts, moving the stick does not matter.
**Answer: False** — a moved stick is a new dial — marks would lie

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A sundial works as well at night as by day.
**Answer: False** — it needs the sun
