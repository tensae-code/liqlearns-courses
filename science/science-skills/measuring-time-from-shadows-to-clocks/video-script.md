# Video script — Measuring time — from shadows to clocks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Build a clock from a stick and sunlight. Watch me, then build yours.

You need: one straight **stick**, stones or cards, a sunny patch, and a clock to check against.

Push the stick **upright** into the ground. Firm. It must not move again.
Morning: where the shadow's tip falls — lay a stone. Write the hour on it.
Every hour, the same. Stone. Hour. Stone. Hour.
By evening, your stones fan around the stick like numbers on a clock face.

Tomorrow, no clock. Read your stones.
The shadow has moved to the 10 stone? It is about ten.
Then check with a real clock. How close were you?

Look at your stones. The **gaps are not equal** — but the hours are.
That is a real discovery, and it is yours.
No sun, no time: clouds stop your clock. Say it honestly. That, too, is science.
