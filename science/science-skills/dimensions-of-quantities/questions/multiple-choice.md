# Dimensions of quantities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) A quantity has a dimension  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) A quantity has a dimension**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Working the dimension out from the unit  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Working the dimension out from the unit**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Derived quantities carry derived dimensions  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Derived quantities carry derived dimensions**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: the dimension of a quantity, worked out from its unit. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Indonesia, Grade 10 — *Ilmu Pengetahuan Alam untuk SMA Kelas X*, Bab 1, printed p. 7  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Indonesia, Grade 10 — *Ilmu Pengetahuan Alam untuk SMA Kelas X*, Bab 1, printed p. 7**
