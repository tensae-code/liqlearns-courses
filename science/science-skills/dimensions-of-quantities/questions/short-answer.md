# Dimensions of quantities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: A quantity has a dimension.
**Answer:** Every measured quantity — length, mass, time, and everything built from them — has a dimension: the kind of thing it is, underneath whatever unit it is measured in.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Working the dimension out from the unit.
**Answer:** The book's method is direct: the dimension of a quantity is worked out from its unit.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Derived quantities carry derived dimensions.
**Answer:** A derived quantity — one built from other quantities, as speed is built from length and time — carries a derived dimension, worked out the same way: from its unit, which is itself built from the units of the quantities underneath.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** A quantity has a dimension → Working the dimension out from the unit → Derived quantities carry derived dimensions — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
