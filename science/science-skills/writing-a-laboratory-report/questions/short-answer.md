# Writing a laboratory report — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Recite the seven parts in order.
**Answer:** Title (and date); Aim/Objective; Theory; Materials and chemicals used; Data/Observation; Result and Discussion; Conclusion.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which part answers the aim?
**Answer:** The Conclusion.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three things research ethics serves.
**Answer:** Knowledge, truth and the avoidance of error.
