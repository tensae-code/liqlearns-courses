# Writing a laboratory report — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many parts has the report format?
a) five  b) six  c) seven  d) nine
**Answer: c) seven** — Title through Conclusion

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The third part is —
a) Data  b) Theory  c) Conclusion  d) Materials
**Answer: b) Theory** — Title, Aim, Theory…

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** An experiment is defined as —
a) any classroom game  b) a planned activity using equipment, apparatus and chemicals  c) a guess written down  d) the last step of the method
**Answer: b) a planned activity using equipment, apparatus and chemicals** — the guide's definition

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Observation is —
a) the final conclusion  b) the first step of the scientific method  c) an untested theory  d) a kind of apparatus
**Answer: b) the first step of the scientific method**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A hypothesis is —
a) a proven law  b) a new, untested theory  c) the report's title  d) a protected participant
**Answer: b) a new, untested theory**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Research ethics in the write-up requires —
a) hiding weak data  b) protecting vulnerable participants and serving truth  c) naming no one ever  d) skipping the conclusion
**Answer: b) protecting vulnerable participants and serving truth** — keyed with the format
