# Hands-on — Writing a laboratory report

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The repeat test
Exchange reports with another group. Each group attempts to repeat the other's experiment from the paper alone (cold rehearsal, no chemicals). Every place the paper fails the repeaters is marked and fixed.

## Margin labels
Take a past exercise-book experiment and rule a margin down its page. Label which of the seven parts each paragraph is — and name the parts that are missing entirely.
