# Video script — Writing a laboratory report

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

You did the experiment. Now write it so anyone can check it. Seven parts. Always in this order.

One — **Title**, and the date.
Two — **Aim**. What did you set out to find?
Three — **Theory**. The science behind it.
Four — **Materials and chemicals used**. Everything. Nothing hidden.
Five — **Data and Observation**. What you saw and measured — exactly as it came.
Six — **Result and Discussion**. What the data shows. Read it honestly.
Seven — **Conclusion**. The aim — answered.

Protect the people in your work. Report the data you truly got.
Seven parts, honest throughout. That is a laboratory report.
