# Standards and quality marks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Criteria and standards for products.
**Answer:** A **standard** is an agreed set of **criteria** a product must meet — what it must do, what it must be made of, how safe it must be. Standards turn 'good' from an opinion into a check: a product is measured against the criteria, and passes or fails on evidence.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Quality marks — SLS and ISO.
**Answer:** A **quality mark** is the sign a product earns when it has been checked against a standard: Sri Lanka's own **SLS mark** at home, and the international **ISO** marks abroad. The manual teaches learners to read the marks on what they buy — the mark is the standard made visible on the packet.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Criteria and standards for products → Quality marks — SLS and ISO — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
