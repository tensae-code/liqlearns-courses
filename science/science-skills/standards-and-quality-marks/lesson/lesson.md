## Sub lesson 1 — Criteria and standards for products

A **standard** is an agreed set of **criteria** a product must meet — what it must do, what it must be made of, how safe it must be. Standards turn 'good' from an opinion into a check: a product is measured against the criteria, and passes or fails on evidence.

## Sub lesson 2 — Quality marks — SLS and ISO

A **quality mark** is the sign a product earns when it has been checked against a standard: Sri Lanka's own **SLS mark** at home, and the international **ISO** marks abroad. The manual teaches learners to read the marks on what they buy — the mark is the standard made visible on the packet.
