Today: **Standards and quality marks** — Sri Lanka's way, from the books cited in the sources.

A standard is an agreed set of criteria a product must meet — what it must do, what it must be made of, how safe it must be.

A quality mark is the sign a product earns when it has been checked against a standard: Sri Lanka's own SLS mark at home, and the international ISO marks abroad.
