Today: **Everyday technology — design and first aid**.

Pakistan (Punjab) ends every science book the same way — with making. Paper bags and cards, clay solids with wheels and gears, a first-aid box and a digital thermometer, a solar oven and a mirror solar cooker, drip against sprinkler, and bioplastic from milk and vinegar. Technology is science put to work by your own hands.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
