# Hands-on — Everyday technology — design and first aid

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make and test
Choose the paper bag or the clay solid. Make it, use it for its real job, and write one change that would make it better — design, build, test, improve.

## Compare two ways
Write the drip-versus-sprinkler comparison as a two-column table for a dry garden you know: where the water goes, what is wasted, what you would build first — and why.
