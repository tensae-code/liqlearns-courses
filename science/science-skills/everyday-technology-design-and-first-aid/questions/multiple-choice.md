# Everyday technology — design and first aid — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does Pakistan (Punjab)'s Grade 4 book teach in its closing Technology in Everyday Life chapter?
a) Only reading about machines  b) Making things — paper bags and cards, clay models — and health tools like the first-aid box  c) Memorising inventors' names  d) Copying diagrams  
**Answer: b) Making things — paper bags and cards, clay models — and health tools like the first-aid box**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A solar oven and a concave-mirror solar cooker both…
a) Burn gas  b) Use electricity  c) Convert solar energy into heat for cooking  d) Store water  
**Answer: c) Convert solar energy into heat for cooking**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Grade 7 compares which two ways of watering plants?
a) Drip and sprinkler irrigation  b) Flood and rain only  c) Bucket and hose only  d) River and well  
**Answer: a) Drip and sprinkler irrigation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Bioplastic in Grade 8 is made from…
a) Sand and water  b) Paper and clay  c) Milk and vinegar (with a corn-starch procedure also given)  d) Metal and wood  
**Answer: d) Milk and vinegar (with a corn-starch procedure also given)**
