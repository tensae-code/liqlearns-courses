# Everyday technology — design and first aid — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why is technology taught as making, in these books?
**Answer: Because the closing unit turns science into things learners design, build and test — paper and clay work, cookers, sprinklers and new materials — technology is science put to work.**

**2.** Name two health tools from the Grade 4 chapter and what each is for.
**Answer: The first-aid box (holding what is needed for first help after an injury) and the digital thermometer (measuring body temperature) — blood-pressure checks and phones/tablets as tools are also named in the chapter.**
