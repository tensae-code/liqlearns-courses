# Video script — Doing science honestly — ethics and Ethiopian scientists

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Five rules keep science honest.
Report your data **honestly** — the numbers you got are the numbers you write.
Analyse **carefully**. Give **credit** where it is due.
Read results **without bias**. And **warn the community** when your work shows a risk.

Now meet two of Ethiopia's own.
**Kitaw Ejigu** built mechanisms for space flight — patented work, GPS, flight simulation.
**Gebisa Ejeta** bred sorghum that survives drought and pests, and feeds people better.

Different fields. The same five rules. That is doing science honestly.
