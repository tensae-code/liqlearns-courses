# Hands-on — Doing science honestly — ethics and Ethiopian scientists

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The honest rerun
Repeat any small class measurement. This time, report the first numbers exactly as they came — then check them carefully, and write what changed in the analysis, not in the data.

## Credit map
Take any invention or cure used at home. List everyone who contributed to it that you can find — maker, teacher, elder, book. That list is proper credit, practised.

## The ethics audit (Grade 8)
Take any investigation your class has run this term. Audit it in writing against the five Grade 8 ethics — honesty, transparency, objectivity, safety, no harm — one line each: kept, or breached, with the evidence. Where animals or people were involved, add the justification line: why was the subject work defensible?
