# Doing science honestly — ethics and Ethiopian scientists — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Copying a friend's results into your report breaks first of all —
a) careful analysis  b) honesty in reporting data  c) proper credit only  d) nothing
**Answer: b) honesty in reporting data** — the data reported would not be yours

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Warning the community of a risk your work reveals is —
a) optional kindness  b) an ethical duty of science  c) the teacher's job only  d) bad for science
**Answer: b) an ethical duty of science** — the fifth discipline

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Gebisa Ejeta is known for —
a) GPS systems  b) drought- and pest-resistant sorghum  c) solar fuels  d) the endod fruit
**Answer: b) drought- and pest-resistant sorghum** — sorghum breeding

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Kitaw Ejigu worked on —
a) sorghum  b) aerospace mechanisms, GPS and flight simulation  c) bilharzia  d) physiology
**Answer: b) aerospace mechanisms, GPS and flight simulation** — NASA-patented space work

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Aklilu Lemma's contribution concerns —
a) ceramics  b) solar fuels  c) endod fruit against bilharzia  d) fair AI
**Answer: c) endod fruit against bilharzia** — endod as a bilharzia preventive

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Rediet Abebe co-founded —
a) NASA  b) Black in AI  c) a sorghum institute  d) a ceramics lab
**Answer: b) Black in AI** — mechanism design for social good; Black in AI
