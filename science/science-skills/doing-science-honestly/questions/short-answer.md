# Doing science honestly — ethics and Ethiopian scientists — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List any three of the five ethical disciplines.
**Answer:** Any three of: honesty in reporting data; careful analysis; proper credit; unbiased interpretation; warning the community of risks.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which scientist's work concerns the values and hazards of medicinal plants?
**Answer:** Alemtsehay Mekonnen.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What plant product did Aklilu Lemma link to bilharzia prevention?
**Answer:** The endod fruit.
