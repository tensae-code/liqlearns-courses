Today: **Putting things in order — sequence** — Iraq's way: a first science skill, taught from Grade 1.

To order is to decide what comes first, what comes second and what comes last — how things follow one another. The Grade 2 book orders cubes by size: every cube's place can be checked against its neighbours, and the rule must hold all along the line.
Classifying groups by likeness; ordering lines things up by sequence. Two skills, two different jobs.
