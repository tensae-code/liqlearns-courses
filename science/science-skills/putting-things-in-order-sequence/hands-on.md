# Hands-on — Putting things in order — sequence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The cube line
Take cubes, blocks or stones of different sizes. Order them by size in a line. Then check every pair of neighbours against the rule — fix any place where the rule breaks.

## A day's sequence
Draw three boxes: first, second, last. Draw one thing you did today in each box, in the order you did them.
