# Putting things in order — sequence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) To order things is to decide…
a) Which ones you like  b) What comes first, second and last  c) What they are made of  d) Who made them
**Answer: b) What comes first, second and last**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Ordering and sequencing is deciding…
a) How things follow one another  b) How much things cost  c) Where things are kept  d) When things break
**Answer: a) How things follow one another**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Iraq's Grade 2 book orders cubes by…
a) Colour  b) Weight  c) Size  d) Sound
**Answer: c) Size**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An order by size is finished when…
a) The biggest is hidden  b) The rule holds between every two neighbours  c) All the cubes are one colour  d) The line is very long
**Answer: b) The rule holds between every two neighbours**

**5.** (Sub lesson 1) Classifying differs from ordering because classifying…
a) Lines things up first to last  b) Puts similar things together into groups  c) Uses only cubes  d) Needs a magnet
**Answer: b) Puts similar things together into groups**
