Today: **Keeping a science journal** — Papua New Guinea's way, from its Department of Education science books (see lesson/sources.md for the exact books and pages).

Papua New Guinea's Standards Based Grade 9 guide opens its Science as Inquiry strand, inside Scientific Tools and Technology, with the **science journal**: the book in which science work is written down as it is done — observations, measurements, questions and results, kept in order. The journal is named a scientific tool, on the same list as the instruments.

The guide's second step is use: entries are made **as the work happens**, dated and in order, so the journal can be read back to check what was done and what was found. A journal written afterwards from memory is not the tool — the writing is part of the investigation itself.
