# Keeping a science journal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) What a science journal is  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) What a science journal is**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) How to use a science journal  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) How to use a science journal**

**3.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: the science journal — what it is and how a scientist uses it. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**4.** (Whole lesson) The books behind this lesson are…
a) Papua New Guinea's science teacher guides (Department of Education, Papua New Guinea) — guide evidence  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A student book only
**Answer: a) Papua New Guinea's science teacher guides (Department of Education, Papua New Guinea) — guide evidence**
