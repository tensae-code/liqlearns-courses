# Keeping a science journal — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: What a science journal is.
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's Standards Based Grade 9 guide opens its Science as Inquiry strand, inside Scientific Tools and Technology, with the **science journal**: the book in which science work is written down as it is done — observations, measurements, questions and results, kept in order. The journal is named a scientific tool, on the same list as the instruments.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: How to use a science journal.
**Answer:** The guide's second step is use: entries are made **as the work happens**, dated and in order, so the journal can be read back to check what was done and what was found. A journal written afterwards from memory is not the tool — the writing is part of the investigation itself.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** What a science journal is → How to use a science journal — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
