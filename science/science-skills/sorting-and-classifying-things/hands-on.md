# Hands-on — Sorting and classifying things

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort a drawer
Empty one drawer or box of mixed things. Decide your likeness first — what the things are made of, what they are for, or their state. Sort everything into groups by it, and name each group out loud.

## The magnet test
With a magnet, test ten materials and classify them into two groups: attracted and not attracted. Write the two lists. If you have no magnet, classify the same ten by their state instead.
