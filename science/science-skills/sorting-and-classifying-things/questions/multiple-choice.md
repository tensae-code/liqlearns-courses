# Sorting and classifying things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) To classify things is to…
a) Throw the mixed ones away  b) Gather them and put similar ones together into groups  c) Count them one by one  d) Give each one a new name
**Answer: b) Gather them and put similar ones together into groups**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A group in classifying is made by…
a) The colour of the box  b) Who owns the things  c) A likeness the things in it share  d) The order you found them
**Answer: c) A likeness the things in it share**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Iraq's Grade 1 book gives classifying with this example…
a) Similar animals in one group  b) Rivers in one group  c) Magnets in one group  d) Seeds in one group
**Answer: a) Similar animals in one group**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Classifying materials by whether a magnet attracts them gives…
a) Three groups  b) One group  c) Two groups — attracted and not attracted  d) No groups
**Answer: c) Two groups — attracted and not attracted**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Classifying materials by their state sorts them into…
a) Hard and soft  b) Solids, liquids and gases  c) Big and small  d) Mine and yours
**Answer: b) Solids, liquids and gases**
