# Sorting and classifying things — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In your own words, what does a classifier do first, and what next?
**Answer: First gather the things, then put the similar ones together into groups — the Grade 3 book's own order.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why must the magnet test be run honestly when classifying materials?
**Answer: Because the test decides the group: each material lands in the group the test gives it — attracted or not attracted — not in the group someone guesses.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name two ways Iraq's books classify materials.
**Answer: By whether a magnet attracts them (two groups), and by their state (solid, liquid, gas).**
