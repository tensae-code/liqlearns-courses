# Video script — Sorting and classifying things

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Sorting and classifying things** — Iraq's way: a first science skill, taught from Grade 1.

To classify is to gather things and put similar ones together. Similar animals go in one group — that is the Grade 1 book's own example. A group is made by a likeness the things in it share.
And a test can do the sorting. Iraq's Grade 2 book classifies materials by whether a magnet attracts them — two groups — and by their state. Run the test honestly, and each thing lands in its group.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
