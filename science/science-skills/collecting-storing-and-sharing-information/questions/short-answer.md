# Collecting, storing and sharing information — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Using information wisely — four steps.
**Answer:** Class 4's Information Collection Sheet activity teaches four steps: decide what you need, find sources, collect and record it, and organise and share it clearly.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Collecting through the internet.
**Answer:** Class 5 takes collecting online, in steps of its own: a search engine — Google, Yahoo, or Bangladesh's own Pipilika — keywords typed, results scanned, and keywords refined until the right pages stand out..

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Storing and exchanging.
**Answer:** Collected information must be kept and passed on: stored in a notebook, on a pen drive, CD, DVD or memory card; exchanged by telephone, letters, camera, email, SMS and social networking.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Using information wisely — four steps → Collecting through the internet → Storing and exchanging — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
