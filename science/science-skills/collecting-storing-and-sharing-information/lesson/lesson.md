# Collecting, storing and sharing information

## Do it first
Before you read: say, in one sentence, what you think this lesson's one thing is, from its title alone — **Collecting, storing and sharing information**. Then read on: this is Bangladesh's way in — its book teaches this thing at the place cited in the sources, in the order below.

## Sub lesson 1 — Using information wisely — four steps

Class 4's Information Collection Sheet activity teaches four steps: decide what you need, find sources, collect and record it, and organise and share it clearly. Information work starts before anything is collected — with deciding what is needed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Collecting through the internet

Class 5 takes collecting online, in steps of its own: a search engine — Google, Yahoo, or Bangladesh's own Pipilika — keywords typed, results scanned, and keywords refined until the right pages stand out.

## Sub lesson 3 — Storing and exchanging

Collected information must be kept and passed on: stored in a notebook, on a pen drive, CD, DVD or memory card; exchanged by telephone, letters, camera, email, SMS and social networking. A find that is not stored and shared clearly is a find lost.
