# Hands-on — Learning with analogies and models

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make an analogy
Take one hard idea from any science lesson. Build an analogy for it: 'this is like …, because …'. Write where the likeness holds and where it stops — the manual's reflection step.

## Make a model
Build a simple model of something too big, too small or too far to handle — from paper, clay or found things. Label what each part stands for, and one way the model is unlike the real thing.
