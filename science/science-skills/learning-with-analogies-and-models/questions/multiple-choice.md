# Learning with analogies and models — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Analogies and models are Chapter…
a) 5 of Part 3 of Cambodia's manual  b) 2 of Part 1  c) 4 of Part 2  d) an appendix
**Answer: a) 5 of Part 3 of Cambodia's manual** — Part 3, Ch. 5 (Activities 1–5), before the learning-games chapter.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) An analogy teaches by…
a) setting the new thing beside a known one  b) repeating the new thing louder  c) hiding the new thing  d) examining only
**Answer: a) setting the new thing beside a known one** — This is like that, in the ways that matter.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) FAR in the chapter stands for…
a) Focus-Action-Reflection  b) Fast And Right  c) Find And Repeat  d) Form And Rule
**Answer: a) Focus-Action-Reflection** — Focus-Action-Reflection — the staged way of working an analogy through.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Role play in the chapter means…
a) learners acting a process out with their own bodies  b) teachers only acting  c) reading aloud  d) a school play
**Answer: a) learners acting a process out with their own bodies** — Role play sits with the models — the body as the stand-in.

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The chapter ends, for both analogies and models, with…
a) learner-made ones  b) a test  c) the teacher's examples only  d) nothing
**Answer: a) learner-made ones** — Learner-made analogies and models — the making shows what the learner holds.

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The manual is cited here as…
a) teacher-guide evidence only, outside the acceptance count  b) a student textbook  c) a pupil reader  d) an exam paper
**Answer: a) teacher-guide evidence only, outside the acceptance count** — MoEYS Department of Teacher Training manual — guide evidence exactly as filed.
