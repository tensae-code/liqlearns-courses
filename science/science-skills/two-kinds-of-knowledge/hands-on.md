# Hands-on — Two kinds of knowledge — conventional science and indigenous knowledge

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The elder interview
Interview a parent or elder with three questions: What do you know how to do that you learned by watching? Who taught you? How do you know it works? Write the answers as a catalogue entry.

## Terrace and weave hunt
Find one example each of terracing, food drying and weaving near home or school. For each: name it locally, say its benefit, say one weakness.
