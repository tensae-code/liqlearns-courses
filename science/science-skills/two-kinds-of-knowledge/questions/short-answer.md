# Two kinds of knowledge — conventional science and indigenous knowledge — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the second story, who prepares the leaf medicine?
**Answer:** An experienced elder.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give two of the guide's worked applications of indigenous science.
**Answer:** Any two of: food preservation, drug extraction from medicinal plants, water purification, environmental conservation.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How is indigenous knowledge passed on?
**Answer:** By word of mouth — orally, inside the culture.
