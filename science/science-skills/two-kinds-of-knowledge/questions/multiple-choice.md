# Two kinds of knowledge — conventional science and indigenous knowledge — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Conventional knowledge is kept mainly by —
a) word of mouth only  b) systematic documentation, and it is formally taught  c) secrecy  d) songs alone
**Answer: b) systematic documentation, and it is formally taught** — written, checkable, taught in schools

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The hospital route in the story runs on —
a) indigenous knowledge  b) conventional knowledge  c) guesswork  d) no knowledge
**Answer: b) conventional knowledge** — diagnosis and named medicine

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Indigenous knowledge is built by —
a) one experiment  b) experience over long time  c) foreign textbooks  d)  examinations
**Answer: b) experience over long time** — generations of experience in one place

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is an Ethiopian example of indigenous knowledge?
a) terracing and crop rotation  b) satellite weather maps  c) factory medicine  d) printed timetables
**Answer: a) terracing and crop rotation** — named in the lesson's list

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The catalogue project asks for each item's —
a) price only  b) local name, benefit and weakness  c) owner's age  d) foreign translation
**Answer: b) local name, benefit and weakness** — the project's three fields

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** Conventional knowledge is tentative. Indigenous knowledge is best described as —
a) always wrong  b) long-term wisdom embedded in a culture  c) written in laboratories  d) taught only in universities
**Answer: b) long-term wisdom embedded in a culture** — experience-built, culture-embedded wisdom
