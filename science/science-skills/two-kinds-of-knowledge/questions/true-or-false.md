# Two kinds of knowledge — conventional science and indigenous knowledge — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Indigenous knowledge is usually written down in textbooks.
**Answer: False** — it is unwritten and oral

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Conventional knowledge can be corrected when evidence changes.
**Answer: True** — it is tentative

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Traditional water conservation is an example of indigenous knowledge.
**Answer: True** — named in the lesson
