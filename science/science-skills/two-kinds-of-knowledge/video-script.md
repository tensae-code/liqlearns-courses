# Video script — Two kinds of knowledge — conventional science and indigenous knowledge

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two children. One sickness — malaria.
One goes to the hospital. Tests. A name for the sickness. Medicine. She recovers.
One goes to an elder. Leaves, prepared with long experience. He is relieved.

Two kinds of knowledge.
**Conventional** knowledge: tested, written down, taught in school — and always ready to be corrected.
**Indigenous** knowledge: built by experience over generations, carried by word of mouth, woven into the culture.

Terracing. Weaving. Drying food. Healing plants. Saving water.
Old knowledge. Real knowledge. Kept differently.

Your project: ask at home. Write it down — its local name, its benefit, its weakness.
