# Laboratory safety — the ten rules — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1.** Name the four kinds of dangerous materials laboratories hold.
**Answer:** Hazardous, poisonous, flammable and explosive materials.

**2.** What is the first task the book sets before the rules?
**Answer:** Mapping your own room's safety equipment on a floor plan.

**3.** State rule 8 in your own words.
**Answer:** Use waste containers properly — laboratory waste goes in its proper container.
