# Laboratory safety — the ten rules — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Most laboratory accidents come from —
a) the materials alone  b) carelessness, impatience and unauthorised experiments  c) bad luck  d) old buildings
**Answer: b) carelessness, impatience and unauthorised experiments** — the three named roots

**2.** A splashed eye must be —
a) covered and left  b) flooded with water at once  c) rubbed dry  d) shown to a friend first
**Answer: b) flooded with water at once** — rule 2 — at once

**3.** Rule 7 forbids —
a) pouring acid into water under instruction  b) pouring water into concentrated acid  c) using water at all  d) cleaning spills
**Answer: b) pouring water into concentrated acid** — water into acid — never

**4.** A test-tube mouth must never be pointed —
a) at the bench  b) at anyone  c) at the floor  d) at the window
**Answer: b) at anyone** — rule 6

**5.** Every accident, even a small one, must be —
a) hidden  b) reported at once  c) written next week  d) cleaned silently
**Answer: b) reported at once** — rule 9

**6.** Eating and drinking in the laboratory are —
a) allowed at breaks  b) forbidden by the rules  c) the teacher's choice daily  d) fine if careful
**Answer: b) forbidden by the rules** — rule 5
