# Lesson plan — Laboratory safety — the ten rules

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Science Skills · **Lesson:** Laboratory safety — the ten rules
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Basic Concepts of Science (Grade 7 General Science).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** laboratories hold hazardous, poisonous, flammable and explosive materials, and most accidents come from carelessness, impatience and unauthorised experiments — the ten rules are the guard against all three
**This lesson's own level:** it stands before all Grade 7 practical work — the learner is about to meet the laboratory's equipment and experiments, and safety comes first

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name what laboratories hold: hazardous, poisonous, flammable, explosive materials.
2. Recite the ten rules and the accident each one prevents.
3. State the three roots of most accidents: carelessness, impatience, unauthorised experiments.
4. Map a room's safety equipment on a floor plan, as the book's first task sets.

## What you need
Paper and pencil; a floor plan sketch of the classroom or laboratory.

## If the learner is stuck
- Treats the rules as decoration → each rule is tied to a named material class (poisonous, flammable, explosive) and a named accident root; ask "which rule guards this bench, right now?"
- Forgets rule 2's speed → a splashed eye is flooded AT ONCE; seconds are the rule.
