# Video script — Laboratory safety — the ten rules

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Welcome to the laboratory. Before you touch anything — the rules. Walk with me, bench by bench.

One: **no unauthorised experiments. Never work alone.**
Two: something splashes your eye — **flood it with water, at once**.
Three: **tie back** long hair and loose clothing. Four: wear **proper clothing**.
Five: **no eating, no drinking** here. Ever.
Six: a test-tube mouth **never points at anyone**.
Seven: **never pour water into concentrated acid**.
Eight: waste goes in the **waste containers** — properly.
Nine: **report every accident at once** — even a small one.
Ten: **clean spills immediately**.

Most accidents come from carelessness, impatience, and experiments nobody authorised.
You now know the ten guards. Work safely.
