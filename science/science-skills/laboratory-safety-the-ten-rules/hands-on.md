# Hands-on — Laboratory safety — the ten rules

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The safety walk
Walk the laboratory or classroom with the floor plan. Mark water, exits, waste containers, and where protective equipment lives. Present the plan; the class lists what the room still lacks.

## Rule drill
Cards with the ten rules shuffled. Teacher reads a danger scene; groups raise the guarding rule within five seconds. A missed rule is re-read aloud by the whole class.
