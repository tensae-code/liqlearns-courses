# The LiqLearns Dictionary — decided design, FUTURE update

**His design (2026-10-27, courses chat):** we will make our own
dictionary. Learners translate words into their own mother tongue;
there is a list on the side — scroll the list, search for words.
It is a **future project, not a right-now project** — he will start
it soon, so that learners have something to work with for the
dictionary part. When it ships, it ships the way his updates ship:
named in the update list — "we created an update for the
dictionary," listed with the other updates by name.

## What it is
- Our own dictionary and translation tool, built inside LiqLearns —
  not an outside service, not a lesson.
- A word list on the side of the learning screen.

## How the learner uses it
1. Scroll the side list, or search for the word they want.
2. Pick a country (its language) — the word is shown as it is called
   in that country's tongue, in that language's own letters.
3. It works for a word, and for a whole sentence too: put in a
   sentence and see what the sentence means in the chosen country —
   like a translation tool, but ours.

## The entries (his example)
Everything is kept in a document: English on one side, and for each
country the word in that language's own letters, plus how to write
and say it in English letters.

- English: **hello / hi**
- Amharic: **ሰላም** — written in English letters **selam**
  (S-E-L-A-M): that is how an Amharic speaker says hello.
- A learner searches "selam" or "hello," reads the meaning, and
  reads it in their own country's language — or any country they
  choose.

## Why ours, not Google Translate (his reason)
Google Translate covers only some amount of languages and its
translations carry misalignments — words that come out wrong. Ours
is built country by country, in each language's own letters, so a
search lands on the real word as that country writes and says it.

## How it stands with the courses
- English Level 8 (Using the Dictionary) teaches the dictionary
  skill: how words are ordered, how an entry works, how to choose
  the meaning that fits a sentence.
- This dictionary is the tool that skill is practised on inside
  LiqLearns once the future update lands.

## Build notes (ours, not his words)
- The document of entries is the heart of it: per word — the English
  word, its meaning, and per country the word in its own letters
  with its English-letters spelling. Sentence translation grows from
  the same checked stock, not from a machine's guess.
- Where a country has more than one mother tongue, the learner will
  need to pick their language, not only their country — a build
  detail to settle when the tool is built.
