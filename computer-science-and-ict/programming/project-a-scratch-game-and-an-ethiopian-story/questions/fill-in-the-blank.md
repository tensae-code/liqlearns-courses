# Project: a Scratch game and an Ethiopian story — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is the model game: a bowl moving horizontally to collect falling apples. (Name the term.)
**Answer: Catch**

**2.** _____ is Ethiopian touristic and cultural stories, and corrective stories for younger children — with characters, scenes and dialogues, using wait and broadcast blocks. (Name the term.)
**Answer: storytelling in Scratch**

**3.** _____ is a written report: the problem understood, the design or storyboard, division of work, the stages and scripts, screenshots, and the group's reflection. (Name the term.)
**Answer: the project report (teacher guide)**
