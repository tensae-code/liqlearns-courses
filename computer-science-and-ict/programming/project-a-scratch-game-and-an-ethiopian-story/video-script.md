# Video script — Project: a Scratch game and an Ethiopian story

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **Project: a Scratch game and an Ethiopian story**.

**a game's parts** — actors / sprites, a script of actions, and stages that change as needed.

**Catch** — the model game: a bowl moving horizontally to collect falling apples.

**storytelling in Scratch** — Ethiopian touristic and cultural stories, and corrective stories for younger children — with characters, scenes and dialogues, using wait and broadcast blocks.

**the project report (teacher guide)** — a written report: the problem understood, the design or storyboard, division of work, the stages and scripts, screenshots, and the group's reflection.
