# Hands-on — Arithmetic Operations and Assigning Results to Variables

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four blocks, four answers
Build one program that adds, subtracts, multiplies and divides the same two numbers, printing each result. Check all four by hand.

## Keep the total
Make a program that adds three values into one variable step by step and prints the variable at the end. Predict the print before you run.
