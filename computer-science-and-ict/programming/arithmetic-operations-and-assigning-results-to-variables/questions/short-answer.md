# Arithmetic Operations and Assigning Results to Variables — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the four arithmetic operations in blocks?**
**Answer: Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does an arithmetic block take and produce?**
**Answer: Two values in; their result out**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four results.**
**Answer: The sum, the difference, the product, the quotient**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a bigger calculation built?**
**Answer: Blocks feeding blocks, step by step**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to a result that is not kept?**
**Answer: It is lost**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a result kept?**
**Answer: Assign it to a variable**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why keep a total or a count in a variable?**
**Answer: For later blocks to use — to drive by, or to test**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where are results shown as the program runs?**
**Answer: On the Print console**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What should you check the printed results against?**
**Answer: Your own head — your own arithmetic**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which program is this lesson about?**
**Answer: VEXcode VR**
