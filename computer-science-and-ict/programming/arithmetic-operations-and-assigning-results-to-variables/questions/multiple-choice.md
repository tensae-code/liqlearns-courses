# Arithmetic Operations and Assigning Results to Variables — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the four arithmetic operations in blocks?**
a) Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division  b) Blocks feeding blocks, step by step  c) Assign it to a variable  d) On the Print console
**Answer: a) Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does an arithmetic block take and produce?**
a) It is lost  b) Two values in; their result out  c) For later blocks to use — to drive by, or to test  d) Your own head — your own arithmetic
**Answer: b) Two values in; their result out**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four results.**
a) Assign it to a variable  b) On the Print console  c) The sum, the difference, the product, the quotient  d) VEXcode VR
**Answer: c) The sum, the difference, the product, the quotient**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a bigger calculation built?**
a) For later blocks to use — to drive by, or to test  b) Your own head — your own arithmetic  c) Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division  d) Blocks feeding blocks, step by step
**Answer: d) Blocks feeding blocks, step by step**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to a result that is not kept?**
a) It is lost  b) On the Print console  c) VEXcode VR  d) Two values in; their result out
**Answer: a) It is lost**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a result kept?**
a) Your own head — your own arithmetic  b) Assign it to a variable  c) Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division  d) The sum, the difference, the product, the quotient
**Answer: b) Assign it to a variable**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why keep a total or a count in a variable?**
a) VEXcode VR  b) Two values in; their result out  c) For later blocks to use — to drive by, or to test  d) Blocks feeding blocks, step by step
**Answer: c) For later blocks to use — to drive by, or to test**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where are results shown as the program runs?**
a) Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division  b) The sum, the difference, the product, the quotient  c) It is lost  d) On the Print console
**Answer: d) On the Print console**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What should you check the printed results against?**
a) Your own head — your own arithmetic  b) Two values in; their result out  c) Blocks feeding blocks, step by step  d) Assign it to a variable
**Answer: a) Your own head — your own arithmetic**
