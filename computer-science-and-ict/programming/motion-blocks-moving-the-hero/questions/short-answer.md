# Motion Blocks — Moving the Hero — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Explain, in your own words: say what a motion block does.**
**Answer:** The learner's own explanation, true to the lesson: Motion blocks are the blocks that move the hero: each block is one movement, and snapping motion blocks together builds the hero's whole path.

**2. A classmate gets this lesson's work wrong. What is the most likely fault, and how is it fixed?**
**Answer:** Hero stops short or goes too far → count the blocks and check the number in the move block: the hero does exactly what the blocks say, no more.
