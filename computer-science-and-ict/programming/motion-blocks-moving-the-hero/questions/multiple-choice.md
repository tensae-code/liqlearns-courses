# Motion Blocks — Moving the Hero — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What does a motion block do?**
a) It gives the hero one movement to do  b) It changes the hero's colour  c) It deletes the hero
**Answer: a) It gives the hero one movement to do**

**2. How does the hero follow a row of motion blocks?**
a) From the last block to the first  b) In a random order  c) From the first block to the last, in order
**Answer: c) From the first block to the last, in order**

**3. What should you do before running a row of motion blocks?**
a) Delete the first block  b) Read them and say where the hero will end  c) Close your eyes
**Answer: b) Read them and say where the hero will end**

**4. A longer move in a block makes the hero —**
a) go further  b) turn invisible  c) stop forever
**Answer: a) go further**
