# Recording and Editing Sounds in Scratch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What records a new sound?**
a) The microphone  b) As a clip in the sprite’s sounds  c) The part of the take to keep  d) An mp3 found on the internet
**Answer: a) The microphone**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is a recorded take kept?**
a) Selecting, cutting and deleting parts  b) As a clip in the sprite’s sounds  c) Parts that went wrong  d) Through uploading into the project
**Answer: b) As a clip in the sprite’s sounds**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What three edits does the sound editor teach?**
a) The part of the take to keep  b) An mp3 found on the internet  c) Selecting, cutting and deleting parts  d) The national anthem
**Answer: c) Selecting, cutting and deleting parts**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is selected in the editor?**
a) Parts that went wrong  b) Through uploading into the project  c) A sprite’s sound  d) The part of the take to keep
**Answer: d) The part of the take to keep**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is deleted?**
a) Parts that went wrong  b) An mp3 found on the internet  c) The national anthem  d) Like any clip, with the sound blocks
**Answer: a) Parts that went wrong**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What outside sound file can be uploaded?**
a) Through uploading into the project  b) An mp3 found on the internet  c) A sprite’s sound  d) The microphone
**Answer: b) An mp3 found on the internet**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does an uploaded sound enter?**
a) The national anthem  b) Like any clip, with the sound blocks  c) Through uploading into the project  d) As a clip in the sprite’s sounds
**Answer: c) Through uploading into the project**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the lesson’s project sound?**
a) A sprite’s sound  b) The microphone  c) Selecting, cutting and deleting parts  d) The national anthem
**Answer: d) The national anthem**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Whose sound does the anthem become?**
a) A sprite’s sound  b) Like any clip, with the sound blocks  c) As a clip in the sprite’s sounds  d) The part of the take to keep
**Answer: a) A sprite’s sound**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does the program play the anthem?**
a) The microphone  b) Like any clip, with the sound blocks  c) Selecting, cutting and deleting parts  d) Parts that went wrong
**Answer: b) Like any clip, with the sound blocks**
