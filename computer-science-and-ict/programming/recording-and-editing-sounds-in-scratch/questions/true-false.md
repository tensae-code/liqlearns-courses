# Recording and Editing Sounds in Scratch — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What records a new sound?" is: The microphone**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where is a recorded take kept?" is: The part of the take to keep**
**Answer: False** — As a clip in the sprite’s sounds

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What three edits does the sound editor teach?" is: Selecting, cutting and deleting parts**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is selected in the editor?" is: An mp3 found on the internet**
**Answer: False** — The part of the take to keep

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is deleted?" is: Parts that went wrong**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What outside sound file can be uploaded?" is: The national anthem**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How does an uploaded sound enter?" is: Through uploading into the project**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the lesson’s project sound?" is: Like any clip, with the sound blocks**
**Answer: False** — The national anthem
