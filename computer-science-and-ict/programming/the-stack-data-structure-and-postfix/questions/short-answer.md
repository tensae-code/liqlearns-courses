# The Stack Data Structure — and Postfix — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a stack?**
**Answer: A data structure keeping values in a pile with one open end — the top**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do push and pop do?**
**Answer: Push puts a value on top; pop takes the top value off**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is LIFO?**
**Answer: Last in, first out — the stack's law**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name two everyday stacks.**
**Answer: A pile of plates, and an editor's undo history**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the stack's power, exactly?**
**Answer: Its restriction — only the top may be touched**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is postfix notation?**
**Answer: The operator follows its values — 3 4 + instead of 3 + 4**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does a stack evaluate postfix?**
**Answer: Push numbers; at an operator, pop two values, apply it, push the result**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What remains on the stack at the end?**
**Answer: The single answer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does postfix not need?**
**Answer: Brackets or precedence rules — the stack's order does the grammar**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does the queue differ?**
**Answer: First in, first out — Grade 10 teaches choosing between stack and queue by the problem's shape**
