# Hands-on — The Stack Data Structure — and Postfix

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Evaluate by stack** — Evaluate 5 3 + 2 * and 4 2 5 * + step by step, drawing the stack after every token. Then write one postfix expression of your own and evaluate it.

## Task two
**Stack or queue?** — For each job — undo history, a printer's waiting jobs, a browser's back button, a ticket line — choose stack or queue and give the LIFO/FIFO reason.
