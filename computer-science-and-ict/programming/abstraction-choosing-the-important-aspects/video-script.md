# Video script — Abstraction — Choosing the Important Aspects

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every real situation carries **more detail than any decision needs**: the popia stall's decision about how much filling to make needs eater numbers and spoon size — not the colour of the stall cloth. Holding all details at once makes the problem unsolvable by clutter. **Abstraction** is the deliberate act of **choosing the important aspects** for the purpose at hand and setting the rest aside. The choice is purpose-shaped: the same situation gives different important aspects to a different decision.
Once the important aspects are chosen, the decision is made **on those aspects alone** — that is what makes the problem small enough to solve. Malaysia's discipline is two-sided: an aspect is kept only if changing it would **change the decision**, and the dropped aspects are **named as dropped**, so a wrong outcome can be traced to a wrong choice of aspects and the choice repaired. Abstraction is not ignoring the world; it is choosing, on record, which parts of it this decision will stand on.
