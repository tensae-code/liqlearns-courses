# Hands-on — Abstraction — Choosing the Important Aspects

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Choose the aspects', 'Decision: how many chairs to set out for a school concert. List ten details of the situation, keep the ones that pass the change-the-decision test, and name the dropped ones.')

## Task two
('Repair a choice', 'A stall ran out of filling early. Its aspects were eater numbers and spoon size. Say which dropped aspect likely mattered, and remake the choice with it kept.')
