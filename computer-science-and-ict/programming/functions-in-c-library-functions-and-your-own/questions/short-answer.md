# Functions in C — Library Functions and Your Own — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name two library functions.**
**Answer: sqrt and pow (also printf, scanf)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What header borrows the mathematics toolbox?**
**Answer: <math.h>**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What three parts has a function call?**
**Answer: The name, the arguments, and the returned value used**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) In `area = sqrt(x);` what replaces the call?**
**Answer: Its answer — the returned value**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Who wrote the library functions?**
**Answer: The language's makers — they are ready-made**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three parts of a user-defined function.**
**Answer: Prototype, definition and call**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a prototype declare?**
**Answer: The name, what it takes, and what it returns**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What ends a function's body of work?**
**Answer: return**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the function's economy?**
**Answer: Write once, use everywhere, fix in one place**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What divides a program, in the lesson's image?**
**Answer: Functions, as chapters divide a book — by their jobs**
