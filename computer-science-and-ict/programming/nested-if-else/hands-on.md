# Hands-on — Nested if-else

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the layers
Write a nested if-else for: if a number is positive, say whether it is large (over 100) or small; otherwise say not positive. Indent it so the pairing is unmistakable.

## Trace three runs
Trace your program (or the book's pattern) for inputs 150, 40 and −5: list each condition tested, its result, and the final message.
