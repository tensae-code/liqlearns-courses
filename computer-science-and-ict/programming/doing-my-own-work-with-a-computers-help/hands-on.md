# Hands-on — Doing My Own Work — With a Computer's Help

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**List your jobs** — Write the jobs of one of your days, in order. Star the parts that are hard, slow, or easy to get wrong.

## Task two
**One job, helped** — Pick one job and do it with a computer's help. Show a friend the before and after, and say which part the computer carried.
