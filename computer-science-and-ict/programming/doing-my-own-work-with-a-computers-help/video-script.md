# Video script — Doing My Own Work — With a Computer's Help

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Grade 3's informatics ends with a project about **the pupil's own work**: the jobs of a school day — learning lessons, doing homework, remembering, drawing, playing, helping at home. The first step is listing them honestly: what do I do, in what order, and which parts are **hard, slow, or easy to get wrong**? Vietnam's first book ends here on purpose: before the computer is a school subject, it is a helper standing beside a real child's real work.
The project's judgement: for each job, **can a computer help — and with which part?** It can hold and search information, show and play, practise with you, and keep your work safe; it cannot understand for you, decide for you, or do the learning itself. Each pupil chooses one job, works it **with the computer's help**, and shows the class the before and after. The rule the project plants for every later year: the work stays mine — the computer carries the parts it is good at.
