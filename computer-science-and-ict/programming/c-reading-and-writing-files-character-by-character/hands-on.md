# Hands-on — C Reading and Writing Files Character by Character

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Chapter 14 §14.5 Closing a File (fclose) and §14.6 Reading and Writing Characters to a File (getc and putc; Example 2: copy afile.txt to bfile.txt character by character) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach c reading and writing files character by character to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
