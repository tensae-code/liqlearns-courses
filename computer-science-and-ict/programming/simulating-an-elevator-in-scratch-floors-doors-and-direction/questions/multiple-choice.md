# Simulating an Elevator in Scratch — Floors, Doors and Direction — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do the floor-number sprites carry?**
a) Several costumes — one per floor number  b) They glide open and closed  c) The lift’s travel direction  d) Current floor, new floor and direction
**Answer: a) Several costumes — one per floor number**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do the door sprites do?**
a) Open and close buttons  b) They glide open and closed  c) The lift shaft  d) Repeat-until and wait-until, with AND/OR
**Answer: b) They glide open and closed**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What buttons does the lift have?**
a) The lift’s travel direction  b) Current floor, new floor and direction  c) Open and close buttons  d) Floor by floor
**Answer: c) Open and close buttons**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do the direction arrows show?**
a) The lift shaft  b) Repeat-until and wait-until, with AND/OR  c) Only after the lift arrives  d) The lift’s travel direction
**Answer: d) The lift’s travel direction**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the stage become?**
a) The lift shaft  b) Current floor, new floor and direction  c) Floor by floor  d) The real machine’s order
**Answer: a) The lift shaft**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What three variables steer the lift?**
a) Repeat-until and wait-until, with AND/OR  b) Current floor, new floor and direction  c) Only after the lift arrives  d) Several costumes — one per floor number
**Answer: b) Current floor, new floor and direction**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What logic blocks move the lift?**
a) Floor by floor  b) The real machine’s order  c) Repeat-until and wait-until, with AND/OR  d) They glide open and closed
**Answer: c) Repeat-until and wait-until, with AND/OR**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does the lift travel?**
a) Only after the lift arrives  b) Several costumes — one per floor number  c) Open and close buttons  d) Floor by floor
**Answer: d) Floor by floor**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When do the doors glide?**
a) Only after the lift arrives  b) The real machine’s order  c) They glide open and closed  d) The lift’s travel direction
**Answer: a) Only after the lift arrives**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must a simulation obey?**
a) Several costumes — one per floor number  b) The real machine’s order  c) Open and close buttons  d) The lift shaft
