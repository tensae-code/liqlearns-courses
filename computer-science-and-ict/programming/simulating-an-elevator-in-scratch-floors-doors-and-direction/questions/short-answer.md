# Simulating an Elevator in Scratch — Floors, Doors and Direction — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do the floor-number sprites carry?**
**Answer: Several costumes — one per floor number**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do the door sprites do?**
**Answer: They glide open and closed**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What buttons does the lift have?**
**Answer: Open and close buttons**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do the direction arrows show?**
**Answer: The lift’s travel direction**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the stage become?**
**Answer: The lift shaft**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What three variables steer the lift?**
**Answer: Current floor, new floor and direction**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What logic blocks move the lift?**
**Answer: Repeat-until and wait-until, with AND/OR**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does the lift travel?**
**Answer: Floor by floor**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When do the doors glide?**
**Answer: Only after the lift arrives**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must a simulation obey?**
**Answer: The real machine’s order**
