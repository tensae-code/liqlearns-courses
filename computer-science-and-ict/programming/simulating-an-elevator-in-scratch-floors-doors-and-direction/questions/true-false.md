# Simulating an Elevator in Scratch — Floors, Doors and Direction — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do the floor-number sprites carry?" is: Several costumes — one per floor number**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do the door sprites do?" is: The lift’s travel direction**
**Answer: False** — They glide open and closed

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What buttons does the lift have?" is: Open and close buttons**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do the direction arrows show?" is: Current floor, new floor and direction**
**Answer: False** — The lift’s travel direction

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the stage become?" is: The lift shaft**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What three variables steer the lift?" is: Floor by floor**
**Answer: False** — Current floor, new floor and direction

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What logic blocks move the lift?" is: Repeat-until and wait-until, with AND/OR**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How does the lift travel?" is: The real machine’s order**
**Answer: False** — Floor by floor

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When do the doors glide?" is: Only after the lift arrives**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What must a simulation obey?" is: They glide open and closed**
