# Hands-on — Simulating an Elevator in Scratch — Floors, Doors and Direction

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the shaft
Make the floor-number sprite with its costumes and the door sprites, and glide the doors open and closed on their buttons.

## Ride two floors
Set current floor 1 and new floor 3; move the lift floor by floor with repeat-until, stop, then open the doors.
