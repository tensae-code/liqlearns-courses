# Descriptive Naming of Sprites, Costumes and Backdrops — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. Automatic names like Sprite7 describe a sprite's job.**
**Answer: False** — They describe only creation order — nothing about the thing.

**2. A script switching to 'Maze 2' is easier to follow than one switching to 'backdrop2'.**
**Answer: True** — That readability is the whole point.

**3. Naming is a finishing extra with no effect on teamwork.**
**Answer: False** — Partners and teachers read your project through its names.
