# Descriptive Naming of Sprites, Costumes and Backdrops — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. A project has sprites Hero, Sprite2 and Sprite3, where Sprite2 is the enemy and Sprite3 the goal. Fix its naming and say why each new name helps.**
**Answer:** A sound answer renames by job (e.g. Enemy/Chaser and Goal) so scripts and discussions name things unmistakably, and notes Hero already follows the practice.

**2. Why does the guide teach naming to beginners at all, when the computer does not care?**
**Answer:** A sound answer: programs are read by people — partners, teachers, and the maker later; descriptive names keep the project's meaning visible and its code readable, which is a core programming practice, not decoration.
