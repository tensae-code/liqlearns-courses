# Hands-on — C While and Do While Loops

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Chapter 12 §12.1 Overview (iteration; the three C loops) and §12.2 while Statement (the loop control variable; flowchart; Example 1: print digits 1 to 10) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach c while and do while loops to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
