# Quantities in an Algorithm — Name, Value and Type — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the three faces of a quantity." is: Name, value and type**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a quantity's name?" is: What it holds at this moment — which may change as the algorithm runs**
**Answer: False** — The word the algorithm calls it by

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is its value?" is: What it holds at this moment — which may change as the algorithm runs**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does its type decide?" is: Calling a quantity never named; trusting a value never set; working a text as a number**
**Answer: False** — What operations make sense on it

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the three classic breakages." is: Calling a quantity never named; trusting a value never set; working a text as a number**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the book's example?" is: A circle; its radius is the rope**
**Answer: False** — A tethered horse's grazing area

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What shape is the grazing ground, and what is its radius?" is: A circle; its radius is the rope**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "In what three tellings is the problem worked?" is: Rope, radius and area**
**Answer: False** — In words, as a model, and in Turtle Art

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which quantities survive all three tellings?" is: Rope, radius and area**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the horse teach, in the lesson's phrase?" is: Name, value and type**
**Answer: False** — An algorithm's quantities survive every change of clothing
