# Quantities in an Algorithm — Name, Value and Type — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three faces of a quantity.**
**Answer: Name, value and type**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a quantity's name?**
**Answer: The word the algorithm calls it by**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is its value?**
**Answer: What it holds at this moment — which may change as the algorithm runs**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does its type decide?**
**Answer: What operations make sense on it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three classic breakages.**
**Answer: Calling a quantity never named; trusting a value never set; working a text as a number**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's example?**
**Answer: A tethered horse's grazing area**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What shape is the grazing ground, and what is its radius?**
**Answer: A circle; its radius is the rope**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) In what three tellings is the problem worked?**
**Answer: In words, as a model, and in Turtle Art**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which quantities survive all three tellings?**
**Answer: Rope, radius and area**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the horse teach, in the lesson's phrase?**
**Answer: An algorithm's quantities survive every change of clothing**
