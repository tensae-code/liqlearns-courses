# Other Data Structures — Lists, Trees and Graphs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a list?**
**Answer: Values joined in a chain, each carrying the way to the next**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What can a list do that a fixed array cannot?**
**Answer: Grow and shrink while the program runs — insert and remove links cheaply**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the array's strength against it?**
**Answer: Reaching any place at once by index**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must the learner name before choosing?**
**Answer: Which strength the job needs**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches them in the supplementary material?**
**Answer: Unit 1 — Other data structures: lists, trees, graphs**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a tree?**
**Answer: A branching chain — one root, children below, no loops back**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give a tree's everyday shape.**
**Answer: Folders inside folders (or a family's descent)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is a graph?**
**Answer: Nodes joined by edges in any pattern, loops allowed**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Give a graph's everyday shape.**
**Answer: A road map (or a network, or friendships)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does the supplementary material teach them?**
**Answer: At abstract level, with diagrams**
