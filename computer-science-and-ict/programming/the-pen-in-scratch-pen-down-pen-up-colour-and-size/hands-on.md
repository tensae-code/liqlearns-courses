# Hands-on — The Pen in Scratch — Pen Down, Pen Up, Colour and Size

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw and lift
Draw a house outline with the pen, lifting the pen between the walls and the roof so no stray lines join them.

## Write on the stage
Build the free-writing pencil: centre at the tip, pen down while the mouse is pressed, and a clear sprite to wipe the stage.
