# The Pen in Scratch — Pen Down, Pen Up, Colour and Size — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does pen down do?**
a) It puts the pen on the stage so movement draws  b) It lifts the pen so movement is invisible  c) More than 200 shades  d) Colour and size (and shade, light or dark)
**Answer: a) It puts the pen on the stage so movement draws**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does pen up do?**
a) It wipes the drawing away  b) It lifts the pen so movement is invisible  c) By number, or by touching a colour on screen  d) Ornaments like the decorations of Al-Aqsa Mosque
**Answer: b) It lifts the pen so movement is invisible**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does clear do?**
a) More than 200 shades  b) Colour and size (and shade, light or dark)  c) It wipes the drawing away  d) Stairs and a house
**Answer: c) It wipes the drawing away**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Across how many shades can Grade 6 choose the pen colour?**
a) By number, or by touching a colour on screen  b) Ornaments like the decorations of Al-Aqsa Mosque  c) At its tip  d) More than 200 shades
**Answer: d) More than 200 shades**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In Grade 8, how else can the colour be set?**
a) By number, or by touching a colour on screen  b) Colour and size (and shade, light or dark)  c) Stairs and a house  d) While the mouse is pressed
**Answer: a) By number, or by touching a colour on screen**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What two pen settings do the blocks set?**
a) Ornaments like the decorations of Al-Aqsa Mosque  b) Colour and size (and shade, light or dark)  c) At its tip  d) It puts the pen on the stage so movement draws
**Answer: b) Colour and size (and shade, light or dark)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What ornaments open the Grade 7 pen unit?**
a) Stairs and a house  b) While the mouse is pressed  c) Ornaments like the decorations of Al-Aqsa Mosque  d) It lifts the pen so movement is invisible
**Answer: c) Ornaments like the decorations of Al-Aqsa Mosque**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does Grade 8 draw with the pen?**
a) At its tip  b) It puts the pen on the stage so movement draws  c) It wipes the drawing away  d) Stairs and a house
**Answer: d) Stairs and a house**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In free writing, where is the pencil sprite’s centre set?**
a) At its tip  b) While the mouse is pressed  c) It lifts the pen so movement is invisible  d) More than 200 shades
**Answer: a) At its tip**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is the pen down in free writing?**
a) It puts the pen on the stage so movement draws  b) While the mouse is pressed  c) It wipes the drawing away  d) By number, or by touching a colour on screen
**Answer: b) While the mouse is pressed**
