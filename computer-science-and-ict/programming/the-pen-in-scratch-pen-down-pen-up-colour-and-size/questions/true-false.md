# The Pen in Scratch — Pen Down, Pen Up, Colour and Size — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does pen down do?" is: It puts the pen on the stage so movement draws**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does pen up do?" is: More than 200 shades**
**Answer: False** — It lifts the pen so movement is invisible

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does clear do?" is: It wipes the drawing away**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Across how many shades can Grade 6 choose the pen colour?" is: Colour and size (and shade, light or dark)**
**Answer: False** — More than 200 shades

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "In Grade 8, how else can the colour be set?" is: By number, or by touching a colour on screen**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What two pen settings do the blocks set?" is: Stairs and a house**
**Answer: False** — Colour and size (and shade, light or dark)

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What ornaments open the Grade 7 pen unit?" is: Ornaments like the decorations of Al-Aqsa Mosque**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does Grade 8 draw with the pen?" is: While the mouse is pressed**
**Answer: False** — Stairs and a house

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "In free writing, where is the pencil sprite’s centre set?" is: At its tip**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is the pen down in free writing?" is: It lifts the pen so movement is invisible**
**Answer: False** — While the mouse is pressed
