# Hands-on - Type conversion - when Python does it, when you must

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Convert the cards

Value cards in two colours - numbers as digits on blue cards, the same numbers as words-in-quotes on green 'string' cards. Adding two blue cards works. Blue + green 'clashes' - the pair must first swap the green card for its blue twin (the explicit conversion, acted out) before the sum may be written.

## Loss watch

With counters: an int pile of 3 and a float pile of 2.5 (five half-counters). Combine them. Ask: if the answer had to be a whole-number pile, what would be lost? That loss is exactly why Python promotes the result to float.
