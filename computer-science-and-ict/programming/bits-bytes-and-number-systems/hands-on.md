# Hands-on — Bits, Bytes and Number Systems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Count to 31 on one hand
Each finger is one bit: down is 0, up is 1, thumb worth 1, then 2, 4, 8, 16 to the little finger. Count from 0 upward, saying the binary pattern and the decimal value together. Feel why 31 is all five fingers up — and why a sixth finger would double your range.

## How big is your world?
Find three sizes you own: a photo's size, a song's size, your phone's storage. Write each one in bytes (use ×1,024 per step, or ×1,000 as a rough step and say it is rough). Then say how many bits that is. The numbers are huge — that hugeness, built from two signs, is the lesson.
