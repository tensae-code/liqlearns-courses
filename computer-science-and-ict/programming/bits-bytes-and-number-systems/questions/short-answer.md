# Bits, Bytes and Number Systems — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why does a computer use bits at all — why not store letters and pictures directly?**
**Answer: The machine's physical parts have two stable states (switch on/off, magnetised this way or that). Bits match that physics exactly, so everything — letters, pictures, sound — is first coded into patterns of the two signs the hardware can actually hold.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A photo is about 3 MB. Roughly how many photos fit in 12 GB? Show the staircase thinking.**
**Answer: 12 GB is 12 × 1,024 MB = 12,288 MB. At about 3 MB each, that is roughly 4,000 photos (12,288 ÷ 3 ≈ 4,096). Rough is fine — the staircase step is what matters.**
