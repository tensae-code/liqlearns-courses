# Hands-on — C Integer Types and Their Ranges

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Chapter 9 §9.5 Data Types and §9.5.1 Data Types for Integers (int, short, long; signed and unsigned; bytes and ranges table) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach c integer types and their ranges to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
