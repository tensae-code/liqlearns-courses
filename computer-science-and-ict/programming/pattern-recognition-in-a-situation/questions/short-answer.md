# Pattern Recognition in a Situation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does pattern recognition mean?**
**Answer: Examining a situation for repeating shapes and similarities**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Malay term Malaysia uses?**
**Answer: Pengecaman corak**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) In the popia example, what repeats?**
**Answer: The same wrapping motions, with a different spoonful of filling each time**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a named pattern tested?**
**Answer: Against every case you have — a pattern that fails one case is only a guess**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What must you do after naming what repeats?**
**Answer: Test the name against every case**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the pay of a found pattern?**
**Answer: The repeated part is solved once and applied to every case it covers**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What else can a pattern do?**
**Answer: Predict — equal repeats let you work out totals before counting**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must be named besides what repeats?**
**Answer: What changes between cases**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why must the changing part be named?**
**Answer: It is where the one solution must be fed different values**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Ten wraps take how long, if each wrap takes the same time?**
**Answer: Ten times one wrap's time**
