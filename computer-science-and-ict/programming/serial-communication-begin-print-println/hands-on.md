# Hands-on — Serial Communication — begin, print, println

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Open and report** — Write the setup() and loop() lines for a board that reports a light sensor's value every second, one reading per line, at the standing speed.

## Task two
**Read the voice** — A Serial Monitor shows: 512, 515, 1023, 1023, 30. Write what the board is probably sensing, which lines accuse a fault, and what you would print next to check.
