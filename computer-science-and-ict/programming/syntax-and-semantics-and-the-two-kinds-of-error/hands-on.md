# Hands-on - Syntax and semantics - and the two kinds of error

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Broken or wrong?

Cards carry six short instructions - three broken in their building (words missing, order scrambled) and three perfectly built but wrong in meaning (salt for sugar, left for right, add for subtract). Sort them into two piles and name each pile with this lesson's two error names.

## The tasting test

Pairs swap tiny paper 'programs' for making tea. Each pair first checks the building (is every step complete and in a possible order?), then 'tastes' the result by acting it out - and reports any quiet wrongness they had to catch by watching.
