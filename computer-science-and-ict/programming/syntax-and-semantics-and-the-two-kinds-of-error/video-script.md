# Video script - Syntax and semantics - and the two kinds of error

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
A short video script for this lesson; its placement names no full-script need.

---

Every language has rules. That is syntax.
Grammar for programs. Follow it strictly.

And every program has meaning. That is semantics.
What the parts DO.

Break a rule? Syntax error.
The translator stops you and tells you.

But a program can follow every rule...
run happily...
and still give the WRONG answer.
That is a logic error.
Nobody announces it. Only you can catch it - by watching.
