# Hands-on — Simple Data and Structured Data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Map the data** — For a school register (names, marks per subject, attendance flags, a class photo file), decide simple vs structured for each datum and name the structure that fits.

## Task two
**Whole and parts** — Take a string and an array example and list three operations done on the whole and three done on a part, for each.
