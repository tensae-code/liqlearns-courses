# Hands-on — C Strings as Arrays of Characters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Chapter 14 §14.7 String Handling (arrays — contiguous memory, subscripts; §14.7.1 declaring and initializing string variables; the '\0' null terminator; "I love Pakistan" in a char[16]) and §14.7.2 String Assignment (why = fails for strings; strcpy from string.h) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach c strings as arrays of characters to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
