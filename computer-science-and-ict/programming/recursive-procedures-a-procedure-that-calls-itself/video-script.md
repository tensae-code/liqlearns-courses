# Video script — Recursive procedures — a procedure that calls itself

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A procedure can **call itself**. That is **recursive**.

Think of a morning: get up. Clean up. Dress. Eat. Brush teeth. Go to school.
Steps that make something happen — a procedure from life.

Now the book's star: **repeat 100 [fd 100 rt 144]**.
Watch it draw — line after line, a many-pointed star.

But look closely at the code. Does **star** call **star**?
No. **REPEAT** does the turning.
Recursive means the name calls itself. This one repeats. Know the difference.
