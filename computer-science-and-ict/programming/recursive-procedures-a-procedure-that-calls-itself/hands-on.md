# Hands-on — Recursive procedures — a procedure that calls itself

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Morning procedure
Write your own waking procedure as a LOGO-style list: TO MORNING, the steps in order, END. Read only the name to a partner; they act the steps as you reveal them one by one.

## Spot the self-call
On paper, write two tiny procedures: one whose body contains its own name, one whose body contains only REPEAT. Swap with a partner — each must point to the recursive one and prove it from the code, not the title.
