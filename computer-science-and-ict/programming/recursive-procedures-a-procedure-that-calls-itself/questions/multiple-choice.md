# Recursive procedures — a procedure that calls itself — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A procedure is recursive when it —
a) is very long  b) invokes a copy of itself  c) uses REPEAT only  d) draws a star
**Answer: b) invokes a copy of itself** — a call of the procedure within the procedure.

**2.** The book grounds procedures first in —
a) cooking recipes from a textbook  b) the everyday steps followed on waking  c) driving rules  d) sports drills
**Answer: b) the everyday steps followed on waking** — get out of bed, clean up, dress, eat breakfast, brush teeth, go to school.

**3.** In the star procedure, the turning is done by —
a) the procedure calling itself  b) REPEAT 100 [fd 100 rt 144]  c) the HOME command  d) a variable
**Answer: b) REPEAT 100 [fd 100 rt 144].**

**4.** The test for a truly recursive procedure is —
a) its output is a star  b) its own name appears inside its body, calling itself  c) it has more than five lines  d) it uses the word REPEAT
**Answer: b) its own name appears inside its body, calling itself.**

**5.** The star procedure's output is —
a) a square  b) a circle  c) a many-pointed star  d) a house
**Answer: c) a many-pointed star.**
