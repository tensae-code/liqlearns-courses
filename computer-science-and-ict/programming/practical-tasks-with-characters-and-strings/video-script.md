# Video script — Practical Tasks with Characters and Strings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The earlier sections gave the pieces: **char** and codes, the **string** type, and the string **procedures and functions**.
A **practical task** joins them into one job with an input and a required result — count the letters in a text, build initials from a full name, check how a word is made, rearrange parts of a sentence.
A program that works on one easy text may still be wrong.
**Testing** means running it on chosen cases: an ordinary case, a short case (one word, one character), and an awkward case (an empty text, extra spaces, a piece that is not found).
