# Scratch AI Extensions — Text to Speech and Translate — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a Scratch extension?**
**Answer: An added family of blocks giving Scratch a special power**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Text to Speech do?**
**Answer: Takes written text and speaks it aloud**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What can be chosen for the spoken voice?**
**Answer: Voices and languages**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does speech change a Scratch story?**
**Answer: Narration and dialogue are performed, not read in bubbles**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does Translate do?**
**Answer: Turns text from one language into another inside the program**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Give a Translate example.**
**Answer: A sprite greets in Arabic and answers in English**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does joining the Scratch platform give the pupil?**
**Answer: Their own account, where projects live and are shared**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is packaging a project as an executable?**
**Answer: Making a file that runs outside Scratch**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why are these called Advanced Skills in the book?**
**Answer: They extend Scratch beyond its built-in block families**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 6 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 1 — Advanced Skills**
