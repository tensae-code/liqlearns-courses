# Iterative Development of Software — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is iterative development?**
**Answer: Building software in rounds, each a working version improved by the last**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does each round produce?**
**Answer: A small, incomplete, but real working version**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What shapes the next round?**
**Answer: Feedback from testing and using the version**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Against what approach does it stand?**
**Answer: The single-pass plan — everything designed, built, then discovered wrong at once**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When are repairs cheapest?**
**Answer: Spread across the building, not at its end**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the heartbeat loop?**
**Answer: Build → test → improve**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three decisions a round can end with.**
**Answer: Keep, fix, or drop a feature**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must a round end in?**
**Answer: A working version**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is 'nearly working' called?**
**Answer: Where projects go to hide**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is feedback collected?**
**Answer: On purpose — players observed, faults written**
