# Hands-on — Iterative Development of Software

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Two rounds** — Build a small Scratch game or program in two planned rounds: write round one's scope, collect feedback on purpose, and let it change round two's plan.

## Task two
**Round report** — Write the round report: what feedback arrived, what it decided (keep/fix/drop), and what round three would do.
