# Hands-on — if…else — one action or the other

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — every activity is done for real, in IDLE or on paper.

## Coin-road game
Draw a fork on paper. A partner calls numbers; you move a coin down the true road (even) or the false road (odd) and say the printed message for each.

## Pass/fail cards
Make PASS and FAIL cards. A partner calls marks; hold up the card the if…else program would print. Mark 50 is PASS — the test is 50 or more.
