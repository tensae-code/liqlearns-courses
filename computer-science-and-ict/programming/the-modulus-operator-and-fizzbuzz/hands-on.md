# Hands-on — The Modulus Operator — and FizzBuzz

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Write FizzBuzz** — Write FizzBuzz for 1 to 100 in C or Python. Then deliberately test the 3-case first and watch 15 break — and explain the theft.

## Task two
**Modulus errands** — Write three one-line modulus tests: even or odd; a leap-year component; the weekday 100 days from a Tuesday.
