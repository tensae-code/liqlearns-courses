# Video script — How Data Is Encoded — Text, Sound and Images

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The computer stores only numbers. So everything needs a code.
Text: every letter has a number. A is 65 in ASCII.
Sound: measure the wave, thousands of times a second. Store the numbers.
Pictures: cut them into tiny dots — pixels. Store each dot's colour.
More detail means a bigger file. That is the trade.
One idea, three codes.
