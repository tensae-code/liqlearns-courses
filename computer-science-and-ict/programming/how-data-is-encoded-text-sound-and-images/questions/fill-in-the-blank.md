# How Data Is Encoded — Text, Sound and Images — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In ASCII, capital A has the code number ____.**
**Answer: 65** — B is 66; the digits and letters each have their numbers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Sound is encoded by ____ — measuring the wave thousands of times per second.**
**Answer: sampling** — Sampling cuts sound through time.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The tiny dots an image is split into are called ____.**
**Answer: pixels** — Pixels cut an image through space.
