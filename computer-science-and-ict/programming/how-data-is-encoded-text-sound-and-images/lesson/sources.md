**Acceptance count: taught in 5 countries (China, Greece, Moldova, Armenia, Pakistan).**

Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (China, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked for the slug and for near matches before building. It sits in the Programming title (programming/), following the book's own order.

China's own way: in Compulsory 1, encoding is not a footnote in a hardware chapter — it sits in the opening chapter on data, between digitisation and compression, so the student meets data's three great codes (text, sound, image) as one idea with three faces.

The two Teacher's Guides in China's set (to Compulsory 1 and to Compulsory 2) are record-only and are cited nowhere in this course: their library records are swapped against the student books (mismatched pairings) and both PDFs are partial (front portions only).

Greece's own way (Grade 7, Pliroforiki A' Gymnasiou, 2024): the new Gymnasium informatics course is built around algorithms from the first page (a Python binary example in Unit 1), then hardware/software, the internet with a full copyright/Creative Commons/AI-copyright sequence, cybersecurity with cryptography, word processing, algorithmics, Scratch storytelling/chatbot projects, and a long society unit (robots, drones, autonomous cars, e-government, AI in education/medicine).

Greece's own way (Grade 11 EPAL, Basika Themata Pliroforikis — student notes): the EPAL (vocational) informatics-sector notes go far deeper into digital technology than the general track — full data representation (number systems and base conversion, character encoding, compression, integer representation and overflow), image/sound/video representation and editing, animation, VR/AR, 3D printing and scanning, then the electronics ladder (Boolean algebra, logic gates, combinational and sequential circuits, adders, flip-flops, counters, A/D and D/A converters, integrated circuits and the CPU), ending with digital-technology applications sector by sector and ICT professions.

Moldova's own way (Grade 7, Informatică): lower-secondary Informatica is a real textbook discipline, not computer-skills practice — number systems, text/image/sound encoding and data types in Pascal sit alongside GUI literacy, and two full chapters are given to behaviour, law and information culture in virtual spaces.

Switzerland's own way (Grades 7–8, Décodage 7e–8e): at 7e–8e the Décodage course becomes a real computer-science course under the Plan d'études romand EN 22/23: Thymio VPL missions (including Morse communication and self-driving-car autonomy levels), a full Scratch discovery course (movement, shortening programs, sub-programs as own blocks, remixing), a Scratch video-game project run in “studios” with a cahier des charges, data work (Huffman-style compression via Zip la marmotte, binary/octal/hex/ASCII codings, file sizes), access security (passwords, file encryption, phishing, authentication), home networks at two levels (IP addresses; message routing, packets, TCP and encryption), and inquiries into sorting, sorting networks, Caesar decryption and parity-bit error detection. Médias/Usages covers social networks and digital identity/footprint, information sources, cyberbullying and the environmental cost of digital technology.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 6 G6 student): Same author pair as Grade 5, one year on: the § sequence moves from society/data-rights into number systems and computing systems, then a long spreadsheet-and-diagrams block, and ends with algorithms in flowcharts and a block-based programming language.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 7 G7 student): A new author team (Topchyan & Melkonyan, 2025) rebuilds the course around the "virtual world": data and encoding, networks, e-security and e-communication, then a full Python chapter — decisions, lists, loops, functions, objects and classes — taught as making the real world virtual.


Pakistan's own way (Grade 7 Computer Science 7): Grade 7 opens with emerging technologies and a deep ports-and-peripherals hardware treatment, then teaches email all the way to DKIM/SPF/DMARC authentication and POP3/IMAP/SMTP protocols; binary data representation (ASCII/Unicode, RGB colour, sound sampling) is taught inside the Programming unit, and entrepreneurship becomes business-plan craft — promotion, value proposition, quality assurance, SEO and platform business models, with Twitter and Snapchat origin stories.
