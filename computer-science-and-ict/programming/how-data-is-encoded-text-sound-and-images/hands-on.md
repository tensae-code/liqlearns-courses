# Hands-on — How Data Is Encoded — Text, Sound and Images

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Grid-picture encoding
On squared paper, draw a tiny picture (a heart, a house) in two colours. Under it, write its encoding: row by row, the colour number of each square. Swap encodings with a classmate and rebuild each other's pictures from numbers alone.

## Sample a wave
Draw a smooth wave on paper. Measure its height at every vertical line of a coarse grid and write the numbers; then join your numbers with straight lines on fresh paper. Repeat with a finer grid. Compare: which rebuilt wave is closer — and which list of numbers is longer?
