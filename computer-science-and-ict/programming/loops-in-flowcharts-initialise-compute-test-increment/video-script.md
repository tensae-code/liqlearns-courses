# Video script — Loops in flowcharts — initialise, compute, test, increment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A loop has four parts. Learn them on one problem.

The problem: add the numbers 1 to 50.

INITIALISATION: sum is zero. Counter is one.
COMPUTATION: add the counter to the sum.
INCREMENT: counter grows by one.
TEST: is the counter still at most 50?
Yes — go round again.
No — leave the loop.

Now the chart. Watch the flow.
Start. Sum zero. Counter one.
Into the diamond: at most 50?
Yes — down to the work: add the counter.
Counter grows. Back to the diamond.
Round and round — 1, 2, 3 — up to 50.
At 51 the diamond says no.
Out of the loop. Write the sum: 1275.

No test — no exit. The loop would never end.
Four parts. Always four.
