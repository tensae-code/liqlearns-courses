# Loops in flowcharts — initialise, compute, test, increment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The four parts of a loop are —
a) initialisation, computation, test, increment  b) input, output, save, print  c) start, middle, end, title  d) read, write, draw, erase
**Answer: a) initialisation, computation, test, increment** — all four, in the book's list.

**2.** The part that is the way out of the loop is the —
a) initialisation  b) computation  c) test  d) increment
**Answer: c) test** — without it the program loops endlessly.

**3.** In sum-to-50, initialisation sets —
a) sum to 50, counter to 0  b) sum to 0, counter to 1  c) sum to 1, counter to 50  d) both to 50
**Answer: b) sum to 0, counter to 1** — before the first turn.

**4.** The loop repeats while the counter is —
a) above 50  b) at most 50  c) exactly 50 only  d) negative
**Answer: b) at most 50** — the decision block's question.

**5.** The final sum of the first 50 natural numbers is —
a) 1225  b) 1275  c) 1325  d) 2500
**Answer: b) 1275** — 50 × 51 ÷ 2 — and the walk-through's board agrees.
