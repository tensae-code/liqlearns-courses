# Hands-on — Loops in flowcharts — initialise, compute, test, increment

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Fifty-step walk
Walk the loop physically: a pupil calls the counter 1, 2, 3…; a second keeps the running sum on the board; at 50 the class calls 'exit!' and the sum is read out. (The final sum is 1275 — check the board.)

## Part hunt
On a printed looping flowchart, pupils label I, C, T, Inc on the four parts and defend each label in one sentence.
