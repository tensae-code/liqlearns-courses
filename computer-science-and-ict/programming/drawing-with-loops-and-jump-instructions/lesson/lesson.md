# Drawing with Loops — and Jump Instructions

## Do it first

The painter must draw a row of identical houses, and between the houses there must be empty space — no line dragged across the sky. Moldova's Grade 2 lesson gives the painter a loop for the houses and a new instruction for the gaps.

## Sub lesson 1 — Loops that draw — patterns from repetition

A drawing with a repeating part is a loop's natural work: draw one house, move, and **repeat** — the row of houses is the body of a loop with a count. Patterns everywhere in the book's drawings work this way: fence planks, window rows, roof tiles, dotted borders. The program stays short because the **pattern is written once**; the count decides how long the pattern runs. Change the count and the same program draws a longer fence — the loop separates **what repeats** from **how many times**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The jump instruction — moving without drawing

The **jump** is the instruction to **move without drawing** — the pen lifted, the painter carried to a new place, the paper left blank along the way. Without it, every move drags a line and separate drawings smear into one scribble. With it, a program can draw, jump, draw: two houses with sky between them, a dotted line (draw-jump-draw-jump in a loop), a face whose eyes do not join. Jump is the drawing executor's silence between words — the instruction that makes nothing, on purpose.

## Do It Yourself

1. Show it, don't just say it: A drawing with a repeating part — a row of houses, a fence, a pattern. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
2. Show it, don't just say it: The loop's count. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
3. Show it, don't just say it: The length of the pattern — the same program draws more or fewer repeats. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
4. Show it, don't just say it: What repeats, and how many times. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
