# Video script — Assignment and Output in Pascal

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Assignment** in Pascal is written with **:=** — `x := 5` puts 5 into the variable x, replacing what was there. The left side is always the variable that receives; the right side may be a number, another variable, or a whole expression, worked out first and then stored. Assignment is not equality: `x := x + 1` is a good instruction and a false equation.
**Output** is written with **write** and **writeln**: `writeln(x)` shows the value of x on the screen and moves to a new line. Text is shown in apostrophes — `writeln('Sum=', s)`. The book's next lesson is a repetition of this one: assignment and output are the pair every later program — branching, loops, files — is built from.
