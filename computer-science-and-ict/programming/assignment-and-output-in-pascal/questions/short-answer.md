# Assignment and Output in Pascal — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How is assignment written in Pascal?**
**Answer: With := **

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does x := 5 do?**
**Answer: Puts 5 into the variable x, replacing what was there**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which side receives the value?**
**Answer: The left side — the variable**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What may the right side be?**
**Answer: A number, another variable, or an expression**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why is x := x + 1 allowed?**
**Answer: Assignment is not equality — the right side is worked out, then stored**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which two words write output in Pascal?**
**Answer: write and writeln**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does writeln add after the output?**
**Answer: A move to a new line**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is text shown in an output statement?**
**Answer: In apostrophes**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does writeln(x) show?**
**Answer: The value of x**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's next lesson after this one?**
**Answer: A repetition of assignment and output**
