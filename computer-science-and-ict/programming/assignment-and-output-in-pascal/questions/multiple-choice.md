# Assignment and Output in Pascal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How is assignment written in Pascal?**
a) With :=   b) Puts 5 into the variable x, replacing what was there  c) The left side — the variable  d) A number, another variable, or an expression
**Answer: a) With := **

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does x := 5 do?**
a) The left side — the variable  b) Puts 5 into the variable x, replacing what was there  c) A number, another variable, or an expression  d) Assignment is not equality — the right side is worked out, then stored
**Answer: b) Puts 5 into the variable x, replacing what was there**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which side receives the value?**
a) A number, another variable, or an expression  b) Assignment is not equality — the right side is worked out, then stored  c) The left side — the variable  d) write and writeln
**Answer: c) The left side — the variable**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What may the right side be?**
a) Assignment is not equality — the right side is worked out, then stored  b) write and writeln  c) A move to a new line  d) A number, another variable, or an expression
**Answer: d) A number, another variable, or an expression**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why is x := x + 1 allowed?**
a) Assignment is not equality — the right side is worked out, then stored  b) write and writeln  c) A move to a new line  d) In apostrophes
**Answer: a) Assignment is not equality — the right side is worked out, then stored**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which two words write output in Pascal?**
a) A move to a new line  b) write and writeln  c) In apostrophes  d) The value of x
**Answer: b) write and writeln**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does writeln add after the output?**
a) In apostrophes  b) The value of x  c) A move to a new line  d) A repetition of assignment and output
**Answer: c) A move to a new line**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is text shown in an output statement?**
a) The value of x  b) A repetition of assignment and output  c) With :=   d) In apostrophes
**Answer: d) In apostrophes**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does writeln(x) show?**
a) The value of x  b) A repetition of assignment and output  c) With :=   d) Puts 5 into the variable x, replacing what was there
**Answer: a) The value of x**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's next lesson after this one?**
a) With :=   b) A repetition of assignment and output  c) Puts 5 into the variable x, replacing what was there  d) The left side — the variable
**Answer: b) A repetition of assignment and output**
