# Remixing Shared Projects — and the Scratch Backpack — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Explain remixing to a classmate who thinks it is cheating.**
**Answer:** A sound answer: remixing is Scratch's intended way of learning — you open a shared project, read how it works, change and build on it as your own version, and the original maker's credit stays attached; cheating would be passing an untouched copy off as your own.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) You write a perfect scoring script in today's project and need it next week. Describe the Backpack steps.**
**Answer:** A sound answer: drag the script stack into the Backpack in today's project; open next week's project; drag the script out of the Backpack into the right sprite.
