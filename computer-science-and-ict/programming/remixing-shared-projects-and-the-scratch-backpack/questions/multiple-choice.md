# Remixing Shared Projects — and the Scratch Backpack — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A remix is —**
a) An untouched copy submitted as yours  b) A shared project changed and built further as your own version  c) A deleted project
**Answer: b) A shared project changed and built further as your own version** — Remix = take, change, grow — with the original credited.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Sharing a project lets others —**
a) See, play and open it  b) Nothing  c) Only see its name
**Answer: a) See, play and open it** — Shared projects can be opened and learned from.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The Backpack carries —**
a) Only text  b) Blocks, sounds, sprites and costumes between projects  c) Backdrops only
**Answer: b) Blocks, sounds, sprites and costumes between projects** — Your pieces travel in the Backpack.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A carried piece appears in the new project when you —**
a) Wait  b) Drag it out of the Backpack into the project  c) Close the editor
**Answer: b) Drag it out of the Backpack into the project** — Drag in, open the other project, drag out.
