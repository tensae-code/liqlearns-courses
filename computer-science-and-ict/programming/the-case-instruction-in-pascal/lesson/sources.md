The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — programming/the-case-instruction-in-pascal). It sits in the Programming title (programming/), following the book's own order. Provenance scope note: every counted placement stocked here is from a Moldova student book (all seven books in this expansion are student books). Media slots in lesson/lesson.md are described slots only ([Picture — …]); no picture numbers are invented.

Moldova's own way (Grade 8, Informatică): Grade 8 pairs a full office-grade Word course (styles, templates, mail merge, formulas) with algorithmic thinking taught formally — executors, subalgorithms, counted/conditional loops, branching — then implemented instruction by instruction in Pascal, closing with image editing in Paint 3D and GIMP.

Moldova's own way (Grade 10, Informatică): the manual teaches Pascal and C++ side by side — each school chooses one or both — and upper-secondary programming is language-formal: BNF metalanguage and syntax diagrams, ordinal types, type conversions, every concept given in Pascal for teaching and in C++ for a technology career.


Kazakhstan's own way (Kadirkulov G8 Informatika, Lazarus edition): Grade 8 by the Almatykitap team measures information two ways (the probabilistic and alphabetic approaches, with Hartley-formula problems), works Excel hard (absolute/relative references, built-in functions, data analysis, applied problems), and teaches programming in the Lazarus IDE — case, for, nested, repeat-until and while operators, tracing, and procedures and functions — before a final part that runs the whole problem-solving cycle from statement to tested program.

Kazakhstan's own way (Salgaraeva G8 Informatika, 2018 edition): the Arman-PV Grade 8 book runs the shared national frame in 34 numbered paragraphs with a test after every part: two paragraphs split the processor from its characteristics, the Excel part goes furthest of the three publishers (data formats, text and logical functions, plotting and restyling function graphs), and the Lazarus part adds algorithm tracing and a practicum on typical programming errors.

Kazakhstan's own way (Mukhametzhanova G7 Informatika, updated edition): the updated-content revision of the Atamura Grade 7 book keeps the 2017 skeleton (units, archives, networks, Excel, practical programming) but replaces the general programming part with Python: working with files, branching, nested and compound conditions and organising a choice; an appendix teaches building electronic learning games with a game constructor (Editor and Class versions).

Kazakhstan's own way (Ataeva & Vyshkova G8 Informatics, NIS): the NIS Grade 8 course is the most distinctive of the wave: vector graphics flows into geographic information systems (planning an excursion, making maps, visualising data in GIS), spreadsheets are used to solve logical problems, loops are taught through mathematics, physics, business and multimedia problems, and the year ends with a full project cycle — six stages, presentation, documentation, defence — plus a mini-project on artificial intelligence.

Kazakhstan's own way (Zhunusov G7 Informatics, English-medium): the English-medium Grade 7 book teaches the updated national programme in English with trilingual terminology boxes — spreadsheets are Google Sheets (a "My to-do list" first spreadsheet), and practical programming is three worked games: a Quiz game, Rock Paper Scissors, a Guessing game and a Camel game, each split into algorithm-development and programming lessons.

Kazakhstan's own way (Mukametzhanova G8 «Информатика»): a «spiral» course (the preface says so): Grade 7's C++ integrated development environment is continued and deepened — classification of software, IDE components, switch, the three loop forms and algorithm tracing — alongside spreadsheet statistics and applied problem solving, and a full problem-solving cycle (define → algorithm → programme → test) as the closing section.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kadirkulov G7 «Информатика»): the Almatykitap Grade 7 course pairs every theory section with «Творческие задания» and test tasks, teaches spreadsheets up to conditional formatting and process modelling, and does its Python through files: reading/writing files and file functions come before branching, nested and compound conditions.

Kazakhstan's own way (Salgaraeva G8 «Информатика»): the Arman-PV Grade 8 book follows the same five-section state programme as the Atamura book (Book 11) but stretches it: a separate unit on processor characteristics, a practicum on modern cyber-threats, text and logical spreadsheet functions, plotting mathematical functions in a spreadsheet and changing the chart's properties, and a practicum on typical programming errors inside the C++ IDE sections.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kadirkulov G8 «Информатика» 2019 edition): the 2019 Almatykitap Grade 8 book splits "measuring information" into two named approaches — the probability approach and the alphabet approach — and its IDE section is a Pascal/Lazarus visual-component environment, ending in subroutines; the 2023 re-issue (152 pages, Book 21) is a different, shorter book.

Kazakhstan's own way (Zhunusov G7 «INFORMATICS»): the Astana-kitap Grade 7 English-medium book teaches spreadsheets in Google Sheets, files and archiving before networks, Python file work at the start of the programming chapter, and a practical chapter that builds three named games — a quiz, Rock Paper Scissors, a guessing game and a Camel game — each taken from problem statement to testing.

Kazakhstan's own way (Mukametzhanova G7 «Информатика» 2021 edition): the 2021 Atamura revision of the Grade 7 book drops the 2017 edition's 3D modelling and C++, and follows the renewed programme instead: Python from files and branching through nested and compound conditions and choice, ending in a practical programming section.

Kazakhstan's own way (Salgaraeva G7 «Информатика»): the Arman-PV Grade 7 book doubles almost every theory unit with a named practicum — units of information, user interface, tables, files, branching, nested and compound conditions each get their own practice unit — and its spreadsheet section adds building formulas for calculations in the sheet.

Kazakhstan's own way (Atayeva/Vyushkova/Zhurbenko G8 «Информатика» NIS): the NIS Grade 8 book is the widest of the set: a whole section on vector graphics and geographic information systems (planning an excursion, making maps, visualising data in GIS), octal and hexadecimal coding, loops applied separately to mathematics, physics, business and animation, strings and subroutines, and a nine-step project method ending in a mini-project on artificial intelligence.
