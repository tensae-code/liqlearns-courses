# The Case Instruction in Pascal — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What begins a case instruction?**
**Answer: A selector — an expression of ordinal type**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many times is the selector evaluated?**
**Answer: Once**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the else part do?**
**Answer: It catches every unlisted value**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three forms of labels.**
**Answer: Single values, lists (1, 3, 5:) and ranges (1..5:)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens with no else and an unlisted value?**
**Answer: Case does nothing and passes on**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does an if-chain test?**
**Answer: Conditions, one by one, each possibly different**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does case test?**
**Answer: One value against a set of labels**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is C++'s version of case called?**
**Answer: switch**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is switch's classic trap?**
**Answer: Forgetting break — execution falls through into the next branch**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is case the right chooser?**
**Answer: When one value chooses among labelled roads**
