# Hands-on — Signed Numbers — Using a Sign Bit

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Write the signed world** — Write −1 to −7 and +1 to +7 in 8-bit sign-and-magnitude. Circle the two zeros and write the comparison problem they cause.

## Task two
**Break the adder** — Add +5 and −3 with the ordinary binary adder on their sign-and-magnitude writings. Show the wrong result and find where the sign bit poisoned it.
