# Hands-on — Repeating Actions — Do It Again

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Mark the chorus
Take a long row of actions from an earlier puzzle. Circle each repeating group in one colour and write beside it how many times it repeats.

## Body repeats
Invent a clap-stamp pattern with a repeating part. A partner must find the repeating part and say how many times it comes — by watching, not by being told.
