# Operators — Arithmetic, Comparison, Boolean, Bitwise — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an operator?**
**Answer: A symbol that works on values**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the arithmetic family do?**
**Answer: Computes — + − * / and the remainder %**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the comparison family return?**
**Answer: TRUE or FALSE — each comparison asks a question**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the beginner's classic operator fault?**
**Answer: Writing = (assignment) where == (comparison) was meant**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the boolean family combine?**
**Answer: Tests — with && (AND), || (OR), ! (NOT)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the bitwise family work on?**
**Answer: The bits inside numbers, bit by bit**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the bitwise operators.**
**Answer: & | ^ ~ and the shifts << >>**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why does a board need bitwise work?**
**Answer: One number can carry eight yes/no answers at once — flags and pin states packed together**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do the four families divide their world?**
**Answer: By what they touch: values, tests, combinations of tests, and bits**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 12, Bab 3 G — operators in Arduino programs**
