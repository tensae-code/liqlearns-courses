# Hands-on — Nested Loops — a Repeat Inside a Repeat

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the hard house plan
Take the easy house plan and rebuild it with a repeat inside a repeat. Count how many times the inner blocks run in total.

## Draw an ornament
Draw a unit shape with an inner repeat, then repeat the unit with a turn until the ornament closes. Save it under its own name.
