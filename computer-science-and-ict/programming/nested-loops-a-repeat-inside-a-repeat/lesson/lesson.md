# Nested Loops — a Repeat Inside a Repeat

## Do it first

One repeat draws one side of an ornament. What draws the whole ornament — the side repeated, the whole figure repeated again? A repeat inside a repeat. Palestine teaches it building a Minecraft house in Grade 5, drawing ornaments in Grade 8, and reviewing it in Grade 9.

## Sub lesson 1 — A repeat inside a repeat — building the house

In Grade 5’s **Building the house**, plans come in easy, medium and hard — and the hard plan needs **a repeat inside a repeat**: the outer repeat runs the wall-building pattern, the inner repeat runs the small pattern inside it. The inner loop always finishes before the outer loop’s next turn.

## Sub lesson 2 — Nested repeats draw ornaments

Grades 8 and 9 draw **ornaments** the same way: a unit shape is drawn by the inner repeat, and the outer repeat turns and redraws the unit until the ornament closes — with the **colour changing inside the loop** in Grade 8, and in Grade 9 the **number of sides asked from the user** and stored as the answer. Each ornament file is saved under its own name.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: A nested loop is a repeat block inside another repeat block: the inner loop finishes its turns for every single turn of the outer loop, so small repeated patterns build big repeated patterns. Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.
