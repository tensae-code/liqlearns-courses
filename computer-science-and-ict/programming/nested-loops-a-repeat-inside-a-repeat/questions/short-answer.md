# Nested Loops — a Repeat Inside a Repeat — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a nested loop?**
**Answer: A repeat block inside another repeat block**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When does the inner loop finish?**
**Answer: Before the outer loop’s next turn**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What three plan levels does Building the house offer?**
**Answer: Easy, medium and hard**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which plan level needs the nested repeat?**
**Answer: The hard plan**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the outer repeat run in the house plan?**
**Answer: The wall-building pattern**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do Grades 8 and 9 draw with nested repeats?**
**Answer: Ornaments**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What changes inside the loop in the Grade 8 ornament?**
**Answer: The colour**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) In Grade 9, where does the number of sides come from?**
**Answer: It is asked from the user and stored as the answer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is each ornament file kept?**
**Answer: Saved under its own name**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What builds the big pattern in a nested loop?**
**Answer: The small repeated pattern, repeated**
