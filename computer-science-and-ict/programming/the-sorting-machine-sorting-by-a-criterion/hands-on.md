# Hands-on — The Sorting Machine — Sorting by a Criterion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Build a two-entry sorter** — Build a physical sorter for classroom objects by one criterion (rolls / does not roll, say) with chutes and one if-gate. Test it with ten objects; repair every mis-sort by naming the gate's failure.

## Task two
**Draw the machine as rules** — Draw your machine from above. Beside every gate, write its Si… alors… rule. Count the ifs — then say what a 4-entry version would add.
