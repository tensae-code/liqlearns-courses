# The Sorting Machine — Sorting by a Criterion — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the sorting machine sort by?**
**Answer: A criterion — size, shape, colour or material**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When was the decision made?**
**Answer: At building time, by the builders**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What steers the object on its way?**
**Answer: The object's own properties, through the arranged paths**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is sorting, in the machine's lesson?**
**Answer: A rule embodied in a path**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the machine in French.**
**Answer: La machine à trier**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is each gate, as a rule?**
**Answer: A Si… alors… — an if… then…**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do more entries (2, 4, 6) give?**
**Answer: Finer sorting — more classes at the bottom**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does each added gate add?**
**Answer: Another if**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is every sorting program, in this image?**
**Answer: This machine, flattened into text**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is the conditional met here?**
**Answer: Inside a physical machine, years before code**
