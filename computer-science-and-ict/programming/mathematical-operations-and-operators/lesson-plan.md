## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The operations and their operators** — Programs do **mathematical operations** — addition, subtraction, multiplication and division — and each operation is written with its **operator**, the sign that orders it, such as + and −. Grade 8's book adds the **relational operations** beside them: comparing two values to ask which is bigger, smaller, or whether they are equal.
2. **Unary and binary operations** — An operation can work on **two values** or on **one**. A **binary operation** takes two values, like adding two numbers. A **unary operation** works on a single value, like changing a number's sign. Naming which kind an operation is tells you how many values it needs before it can run.
