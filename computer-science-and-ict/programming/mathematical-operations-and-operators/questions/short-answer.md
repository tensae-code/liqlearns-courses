# Mathematical Operations and Operators — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What four operations does this lesson start with?**
**Answer: Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is an operator?**
**Answer: The sign that orders an operation**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What sign orders addition?**
**Answer: The + sign**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What kind of operations does Grade 8's book add?**
**Answer: Relational operations**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do relational operations do?**
**Answer: Compare two values — bigger, smaller or equal**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How many values does a binary operation take?**
**Answer: Two values**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How many values does a unary operation work on?**
**Answer: One value**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is an example of a unary operation?**
**Answer: Changing a number's sign**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does naming an operation unary or binary tell you?**
**Answer: How many values it needs before it can run**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) In which grades does Iraq teach these operations?**
**Answer: Grade 7 and Grade 8**
