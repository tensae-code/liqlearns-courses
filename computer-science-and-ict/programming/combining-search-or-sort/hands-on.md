# Hands-on — Combining Search or Sort

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Read five jobs', 'Classify each job as search, sort, or both, with the question quoted as evidence: (1) Is Aina in the club list? (2) Print the race results fastest-first. (3) A shop must answer stock questions all day from a price-ordered shelf list. (4) Put the library's new books in title order. (5) Find the third-cheapest item.')

## Task two
('Find the inner job', 'Take bubble sort and a smallest-first sort. For each, write where a search happens inside it, and what that inner search costs per pass.')
