# Combining Search or Sort — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Off what is the search-or-sort choice read?**
**Answer: The job's question**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A job asking whether or where a value is, is what kind?**
**Answer: A search job**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A job asking for the list itself in order, is what kind?**
**Answer: A sort job — a ranking, a timetable, a price line**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A job asking repeated where-questions and an ordered list is what?**
**Answer: Both**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does Malaysia's discipline refuse?**
**Answer: The unneeded half — no sorting for one where-question, no searching when an ordered list is owed**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does searching live inside sorting?**
**Answer: Sorts search as they work — for out-of-order pairs, or for the smallest remaining value**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is bucket scattering, seen this way?**
**Answer: A search-by-size that half-sorts as it places**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does recognising the inner job explain?**
**Answer: The outer job's cost**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) A sort built on repeated searching takes what kind of time?**
**Answer: Repeated-search time**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the chapter's final skill?**
**Answer: Naming the jobs inside the job before choosing the algorithm**
