# Combining Search or Sort — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Off what is the search-or-sort choice read?**
a) The job's question  b) A search job  c) A sort job — a ranking, a timetable, a price line  d) Both
**Answer: a) The job's question**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A job asking whether or where a value is, is what kind?**
a) A sort job — a ranking, a timetable, a price line  b) A search job  c) Both  d) The unneeded half — no sorting for one where-question, no searching when an ordered list is owed
**Answer: b) A search job**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A job asking for the list itself in order, is what kind?**
a) Both  b) The unneeded half — no sorting for one where-question, no searching when an ordered list is owed  c) A sort job — a ranking, a timetable, a price line  d) Sorts search as they work — for out-of-order pairs, or for the smallest remaining value
**Answer: c) A sort job — a ranking, a timetable, a price line**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A job asking repeated where-questions and an ordered list is what?**
a) The unneeded half — no sorting for one where-question, no searching when an ordered list is owed  b) Sorts search as they work — for out-of-order pairs, or for the smallest remaining value  c) A search-by-size that half-sorts as it places  d) Both
**Answer: d) Both**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does Malaysia's discipline refuse?**
a) The unneeded half — no sorting for one where-question, no searching when an ordered list is owed  b) Sorts search as they work — for out-of-order pairs, or for the smallest remaining value  c) A search-by-size that half-sorts as it places  d) The outer job's cost
**Answer: a) The unneeded half — no sorting for one where-question, no searching when an ordered list is owed**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does searching live inside sorting?**
a) A search-by-size that half-sorts as it places  b) Sorts search as they work — for out-of-order pairs, or for the smallest remaining value  c) The outer job's cost  d) Repeated-search time
**Answer: b) Sorts search as they work — for out-of-order pairs, or for the smallest remaining value**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is bucket scattering, seen this way?**
a) The outer job's cost  b) Repeated-search time  c) A search-by-size that half-sorts as it places  d) Naming the jobs inside the job before choosing the algorithm
**Answer: c) A search-by-size that half-sorts as it places**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does recognising the inner job explain?**
a) Repeated-search time  b) Naming the jobs inside the job before choosing the algorithm  c) The job's question  d) The outer job's cost
**Answer: d) The outer job's cost**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) A sort built on repeated searching takes what kind of time?**
a) Repeated-search time  b) Naming the jobs inside the job before choosing the algorithm  c) The job's question  d) A search job
**Answer: a) Repeated-search time**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the chapter's final skill?**
a) The job's question  b) Naming the jobs inside the job before choosing the algorithm  c) A search job  d) A sort job — a ranking, a timetable, a price line
