# Hands-on — Working with Data in Scratch

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make a score
In Scratch (or on paper with blocks drawn), build a score that starts at zero and changes by one each time a sprite is clicked, shown on the stage.

## Decide with data
Add a condition that ends or changes the project when the score reaches a number you choose, and trace two runs with different clicking speeds.
