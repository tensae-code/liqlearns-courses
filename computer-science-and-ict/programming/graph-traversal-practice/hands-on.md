# Hands-on — Graph Traversal Practice — Depth and Breadth Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Both walks, one graph** — Draw a graph of seven vertices. Run DFS and BFS from the same start, listing both orders side by side, and circle where they first differ.

## Task two
**Choose first** — Write three graph questions of your own. For each, name DFS or BFS first and your reason — then run the walk you chose.
