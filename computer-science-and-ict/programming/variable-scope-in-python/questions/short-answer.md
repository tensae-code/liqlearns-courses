# Variable Scope in Python — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a local variable?**
**Answer: One assigned inside a function — visible only there, gone at return**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why can two functions share the name total in peace?**
**Answer: Each has its own local total**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When may a function read an outside name?**
**Answer: When it does not assign to it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does assigning do to a name inside a function?**
**Answer: Makes it local throughout the function**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What error names the trap?**
**Answer: 'Referenced before assignment'**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a global variable?**
**Answer: A name assigned at the program's top level, visible everywhere**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What declaration writes a global from inside?**
**Answer: global**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why are globals warned against?**
**Answer: Any function may change what every function sees**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the craft's preference?**
**Answer: Pass values in as parameters, out as returns; keep names local**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is scope, in the closing phrase?**
**Answer: The function's privacy, and the program's sanity**
