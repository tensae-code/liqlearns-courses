# Hands-on — Characters and Strings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Kind the values** — List ten values from a school app (name, mark, class code, date, comment). Name each one's kind — number, character or string — and defend the two hardest choices.

## Task two
**Row work** — Take the string 'INFORMATIKA'. Write its length, its first and last characters, and the two strings made by cutting it after the fifth character and joining them back with a hyphen.
