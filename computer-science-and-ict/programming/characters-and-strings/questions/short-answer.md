# Characters and Strings — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a character?**
**Answer: One symbol — a letter, digit, space or punctuation mark**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is a character stored?**
**Answer: As a code number from the agreed character set**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A character can be counted with because of what?**
**Answer: Because it is a code number underneath**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a string?**
**Answer: A sequence of characters kept as one value**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What three properties follow from the row?**
**Answer: Length, order, and parts that can be cut out and joined**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book place characters after arrays?**
**Answer: A string is the array's closest relative — characters in a row**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What operations is every text program built from?**
**Answer: Cutting out parts and joining them (and searching and comparing)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is a value's kind named?**
**Answer: In the design — before the program is written, not after it breaks**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which values belong in strings?**
**Answer: Text that must be searched, compared or taken apart**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 11, Bab III C.2 — characters and strings**
