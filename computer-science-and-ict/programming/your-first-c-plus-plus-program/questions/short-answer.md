# Your First C++ Program — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does #include <iostream> do?**
**Answer: Brings in the input–output tools the program needs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a C++ program's running start?**
**Answer: In main() — the function the running starts from**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does cout do?**
**Answer: Sends output to the screen — prints what it is given**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What ends a C++ statement?**
**Answer: A semicolon**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where do main's statements live?**
**Answer: Inside its braces**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three steps of the making.**
**Answer: Write, compile, run**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does compiling do?**
**Answer: Translates the whole program into machine code**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When do errors appear, and where?**
**Answer: At compiling — reported at exact lines, to be fixed and compiled again**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What happens at running?**
**Answer: The compiled program executes and cout's line appears**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which worked programs follow in Fiji's chapter?**
**Answer: Pay calculation, grade from mark, and triangle and circle areas — with Fijian student names**
