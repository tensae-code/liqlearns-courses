# Hands-on — What Programming Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Program a person
Write instructions for making a cup of tea as a program: numbered, complete, in order. Give them to a partner who must follow them literally and flag every gap or vague step.

## Problem first
Pick a small problem (for example, find the largest of three numbers). Write the problem in one sentence, then the solution steps — no code. That second list is your algorithm.
