# Hands-on — The Goto Instruction — and Why It Is Avoided

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Untangle it** — Rewrite a goto-written fragment (print 1..5 with a backward jump) using for, and the same fragment's early exit using a while condition.

## Task two
**Verdict writing** — Write six lines defending the rule 'write without goto' to a sceptic, using the spaghetti argument and one honest exception.
