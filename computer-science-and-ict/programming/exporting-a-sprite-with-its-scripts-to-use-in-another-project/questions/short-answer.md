# Exporting a Sprite with Its Scripts to Use in Another Project — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) From which menu is a sprite saved?**
**Answer: Its right-click menu**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the sprite saved to?**
**Answer: A file**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What travels inside the file?**
**Answer: All the sprite’s properties and scripts**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name two things packed besides the blocks.**
**Answer: Its costumes and its sounds**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to the sprite on the stage when it is saved?**
**Answer: It stays where it was — the file is a copy**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does the sprite enter the other program?**
**Answer: The file is loaded**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What arrives with the sprite?**
**Answer: Its looks, properties and programming, whole**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Must the sprite be rebuilt in the new project?**
**Answer: No**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the lesson’s rule?**
**Answer: Build well once, carry it anywhere**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is copied by this lesson’s method?**
**Answer: A sprite with its programming, to another sprite/project**
