# Systems Phase 4 — System Development — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does system development do?" is: Carries out the design — creates the files or database and writes the programs**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What guides every part built?" is: The builder asks how to make a piece work; the design must already have answered what piece and why**
**Answer: False** — The design — each part does exactly its designed job

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why does Fiji keep building separate from designing?" is: The builder asks how to make a piece work; the design must already have answered what piece and why**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a project pay if the builder must also decide?" is: One part at a time**
**Answer: False** — It pays for design twice

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "In what order is the system built?" is: One part at a time**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is each part tested?" is: The whole system together — data in at the real inputs, out at the real reports**
**Answer: False** — As it is made — with real and awkward data, against its design

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is tested when the parts stand?" is: The whole system together — data in at the real inputs, out at the real reports**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is a system truly developed?" is: The evidence, not the effort**
**Answer: False** — When it has been shown to do what its requirements said

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What ends the phase?" is: The evidence, not the effort**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which earlier phase's promises does testing check?" is: Carries out the design — creates the files or database and writes the programs**
**Answer: False** — Analysis's — the requirements
