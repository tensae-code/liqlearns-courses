# Systems Phase 4 — System Development — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does system development do?**
**Answer: Carries out the design — creates the files or database and writes the programs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What guides every part built?**
**Answer: The design — each part does exactly its designed job**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Fiji keep building separate from designing?**
**Answer: The builder asks how to make a piece work; the design must already have answered what piece and why**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a project pay if the builder must also decide?**
**Answer: It pays for design twice**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In what order is the system built?**
**Answer: One part at a time**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When is each part tested?**
**Answer: As it is made — with real and awkward data, against its design**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is tested when the parts stand?**
**Answer: The whole system together — data in at the real inputs, out at the real reports**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is a system truly developed?**
**Answer: When it has been shown to do what its requirements said**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What ends the phase?**
**Answer: The evidence, not the effort**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which earlier phase's promises does testing check?**
**Answer: Analysis's — the requirements**
