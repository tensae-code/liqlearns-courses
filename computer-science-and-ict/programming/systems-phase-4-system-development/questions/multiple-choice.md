# Systems Phase 4 — System Development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does system development do?**
a) Carries out the design — creates the files or database and writes the programs  b) The design — each part does exactly its designed job  c) The builder asks how to make a piece work; the design must already have answered what piece and why  d) It pays for design twice
**Answer: a) Carries out the design — creates the files or database and writes the programs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What guides every part built?**
a) The builder asks how to make a piece work; the design must already have answered what piece and why  b) The design — each part does exactly its designed job  c) It pays for design twice  d) One part at a time
**Answer: b) The design — each part does exactly its designed job**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Fiji keep building separate from designing?**
a) It pays for design twice  b) One part at a time  c) The builder asks how to make a piece work; the design must already have answered what piece and why  d) As it is made — with real and awkward data, against its design
**Answer: c) The builder asks how to make a piece work; the design must already have answered what piece and why**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a project pay if the builder must also decide?**
a) One part at a time  b) As it is made — with real and awkward data, against its design  c) The whole system together — data in at the real inputs, out at the real reports  d) It pays for design twice
**Answer: d) It pays for design twice**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In what order is the system built?**
a) One part at a time  b) As it is made — with real and awkward data, against its design  c) The whole system together — data in at the real inputs, out at the real reports  d) When it has been shown to do what its requirements said
**Answer: a) One part at a time**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When is each part tested?**
a) The whole system together — data in at the real inputs, out at the real reports  b) As it is made — with real and awkward data, against its design  c) When it has been shown to do what its requirements said  d) The evidence, not the effort
**Answer: b) As it is made — with real and awkward data, against its design**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is tested when the parts stand?**
a) When it has been shown to do what its requirements said  b) The evidence, not the effort  c) The whole system together — data in at the real inputs, out at the real reports  d) Analysis's — the requirements
**Answer: c) The whole system together — data in at the real inputs, out at the real reports**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is a system truly developed?**
a) The evidence, not the effort  b) Analysis's — the requirements  c) Carries out the design — creates the files or database and writes the programs  d) When it has been shown to do what its requirements said
**Answer: d) When it has been shown to do what its requirements said**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What ends the phase?**
a) The evidence, not the effort  b) Analysis's — the requirements  c) Carries out the design — creates the files or database and writes the programs  d) The design — each part does exactly its designed job
**Answer: a) The evidence, not the effort**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which earlier phase's promises does testing check?**
a) Carries out the design — creates the files or database and writes the programs  b) Analysis's — the requirements  c) The design — each part does exactly its designed job  d) The builder asks how to make a piece work; the design must already have answered what piece and why
