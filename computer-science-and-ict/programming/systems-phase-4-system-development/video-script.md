# Video script — Systems Phase 4 — System Development

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**System development** carries out the design: the **files or database are created** as designed, and the **programs are written** — in the languages and tools the design chose — one part at a time, each part doing exactly its designed job. Fiji keeps building separate from designing for a reason its earlier phases taught: the builder's questions are *how do I make this piece work*, and the design must already have answered *what piece and why* — a builder who must also decide is a project paying for design twice.
Building includes its own proving. Each part is **tested as it is made** — fed real and awkward data, checked against its design — and when the parts stand, the **whole system is tested together**: data flowing in at the real inputs and out at the real reports. Fiji's testing rule is unglamorous and absolute: a system is not developed when it is *written*, but when it has been *shown* to do what its requirements said — the evidence, not the effort, ends the phase.
