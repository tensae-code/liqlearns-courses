# The Concept of an Action in a Program — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an action in a program?**
**Answer: One elementary operation the executor performs while the program runs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How do actions differ from the program's text?**
**Answer: The text stands still; actions happen in time, one after another**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do actions change as they go?**
**Answer: The program's state — the values in memory**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name three actions from the lesson.**
**Answer: Taking a value, computing, storing (also: showing, choosing)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does it mean to understand a program?**
**Answer: To follow its actions — what happens first, what changes, what happens next**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is an elementary action?**
**Answer: One command done at once by the executor — like adding two numbers**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a composed action?**
**Answer: A named block that unfolds into many elementary actions when executed**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Can one deed be both elementary and composed?**
**Answer: Yes — elementary to the caller, composed inside (print the table: one action above, a hundred below)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is programming, in this section's phrase?**
**Answer: Largely the art of composing — building big actions from small ones**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the state an action acts on?**
**Answer: The values held in memory at that moment**
