# Project — Building a Python Digital Product as a Team — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a digital product?**
**Answer: A finished piece of software made for users — serving a purpose beyond the maker's mark-sheet**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How big is the project team?**
**Answer: 4–5 members**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is the team's work organised?**
**Answer: Named roles — each part (plan, code, testing, presenting) has an owner**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why teams, in the book's teaching?**
**Answer: Real products are not built alone — the course refuses to pretend otherwise**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the six project steps, in order.**
**Answer: Idea, work environment (PyCharm), flowchart, code, testing, sharing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is spent at the flowchart step?**
**Answer: The year's flowchart drawing — the plan drawn before code**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is testing, here?**
**Answer: The debugger's discipline turned on the team's own work**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to a product that skips the flowchart? Skips testing?**
**Answer: It codes its confusions; it ships them**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the product's final step?**
**Answer: Sharing — handing it to its users**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book sets this project?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 10 Information Technology Term 1, Unit 3 — Digital Products**
