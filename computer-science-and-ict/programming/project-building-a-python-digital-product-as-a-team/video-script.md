# Video script — Project — Building a Python Digital Product as a Team

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **digital product** is a finished piece of software made **for users** — it solves a problem or serves a purpose beyond its maker's mark-sheet. The project is worked by a **team of 4–5 members with named roles**: the work is divided so each part — the plan, the code, the testing, the presenting — has an owner, and the team is itself part of the teaching: real products are not built alone, and Kuwait's first Python year refuses to pretend otherwise.
The build runs the course's own craft in order: the **idea** (the product's purpose, in a sentence a user would want), the **work environment** set up in **PyCharm**, the **flowchart** (the plan drawn before code — the year's drawing lessons spent), the **code** itself, **testing** (the debugger's discipline turned on the team's own work), and **sharing** — the product handed to its users. Six steps, and the sequence is the quality system: a product that skips the flowchart codes its confusions; one that skips testing ships them.
