# Hands-on — Finding the Best Strategy — One That Always Works

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Strategy trials** — Define your team's movement-saving strategy as written rules. Run it against five provided burrow maps; log movements per map against the naive count.

## Task two
**Adversarial burrow** — Design the nastiest legal burrow you can, aimed at another team's strategy. Run theirs on it. If it breaks, both teams co-author the improved strategy.
