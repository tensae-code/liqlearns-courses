# Hands-on — Loops with Arrays Reading Summing Combining

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 4 §4.2.5 Loops and Arrays (read 10 values into an array; store the first five multiples of 23; print an array; sum of elements) and §4.2.6 solved example (add two arrays element by element) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach loops with arrays reading summing combining to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
