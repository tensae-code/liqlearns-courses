# Realistic Character Motion in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What makes motion look real?**
**Answer: Speed that changes — start slow, hurry, settle**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the speed kept?**
**Answer: In a variable, changed a little each step**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is acceleration here?**
**Answer: Adding a little to the speed each step while a key is held**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when the key is released?**
**Answer: The speed is subtracted from gradually — slowing to rest**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 8 Pliroforiki B, Unit 5**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What grows each step as a character falls?**
**Answer: Its downward speed — gravity adds and adds**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must stop the growth?**
**Answer: A landing check**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What else must answer the movement?**
**Answer: Costume and direction — face the way you travel**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) State the lesson's rule.**
**Answer: Believable motion is ordinary motion blocks plus variables for the physics the eye expects**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Through the floor is the fault of what missing piece?**
**Answer: The landing check**
