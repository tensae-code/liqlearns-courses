# Hands-on — Realistic Character Motion in Scratch

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Build the ease', 'Write (on paper or in Scratch) the speed-variable loop that accelerates a sprite while a key is held and eases it to rest on release.')

## Task two
('Land the jump', 'Plan a jump: the upward start, gravity's addition each step, and the landing check — blocks named in order.')
