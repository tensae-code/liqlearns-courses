# Hands-on — Nested Selection Statements

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Nest a school rule
Write the rule “if school is open, then if it is raining take the bus, else walk” as nested selections. Trace it for a rainy school day and a dry one.

## Draw the paths
Draw the branching paths of your nested rule as a diagram. Count the paths and name what happens at the end of each.
