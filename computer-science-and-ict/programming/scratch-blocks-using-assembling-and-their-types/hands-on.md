# Hands-on — Scratch Blocks — Using, Assembling and Their Types

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Grammar sort** — Take twelve blocks (real or drawn) and sort them by type, naming the shape-evidence for each.

## Task two
**Predict, then run** — Read an unseen short script, write its predicted story from the block types, then run it and score your prediction.
