# Scratch Blocks — Using, Assembling and Their Types — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How are blocks assembled?**
**Answer: Dragged from the palette and snapped together in the script area**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What declares where a block can join?**
**Answer: Its shape**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) In what order does a script run?**
**Answer: From its top — its event — downward**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are the shapes, in the lesson's phrase?**
**Answer: The syntax, worn visibly**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a script tested and taken apart?**
**Answer: Clicked to run; dragged apart by its lower half**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the six types.**
**Answer: Event (hat), command (stack), reporter, boolean reporter, C blocks, cap blocks**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do reporter blocks do?**
**Answer: Hand back values; fit inside others**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do C blocks do?**
**Answer: Wrap other blocks inside them — loops and conditions**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do cap blocks do?**
**Answer: End a script**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why learn the types?**
**Answer: Naming a block's type lets you predict a script's reading before running it**
