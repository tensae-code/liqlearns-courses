# Video script — Project — Navigation Systems with Graphs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A navigation app hides a graph. Junctions are nodes. Road pieces are edges.

Every edge gets a weight: how long, or how many minutes.

Ask for a route, and the app runs a shortest-path search — Dijkstra's.

Then it turns the path back into streets: turn left, turn right.

Traffic changes the weights. The route changes with them. The graph heart stays the same.
