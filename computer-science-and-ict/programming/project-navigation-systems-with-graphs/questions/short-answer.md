# Project — Navigation Systems with Graphs — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. Your project's fastest route sends walkers along a road with no pavement. What do you change — the algorithm or the model — and how?**
**Answer: The model. The algorithm is exact on the graph given; the graph must carry reality better — e.g. forbid that edge for walkers, or weight it with a heavy penalty — and then re-run the same search.**

**2. Name two jobs a real navigation system does beyond finding the shortest path.**
**Answer: Any two of: geocoding the typed address to a start node; turning the path into turn-by-turn instructions; re-routing when the driver leaves the route or traffic re-weights edges; answering on a graph of millions of edges in under a second.**
