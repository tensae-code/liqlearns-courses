# Hands-on — Project — Navigation Systems with Graphs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build your navi
On a real map of your area, mark the junctions, draw the graph, and weight the edges with walking minutes (estimate them honestly — walk one edge with a timer if you can). Answer the route from home to school with Dijkstra, then compare with the route you actually take. Write down any difference and what it says about your model.

## Rush hour
Pick two edges on your graph and double their time-weights — a jam. Re-run the search for the same request. If the best route changes, your model has just done what a real navigation system does every few minutes.
