# Hands-on — Analytic Solutions and Simulation Solutions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Two roads** — For a falling ball and for a school canteen's queue, choose analytic or simulation and defend each choice by existence of a formula and the question asked.

## Task two
**Run reading** — Given two simulation runs of the same epidemic model with different starts, write what may and may not be concluded, and what a third run would add.
