# Analytic Solutions and Simulation Solutions — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an analytic solution?**
**Answer: An exact formula derived by mathematics, giving the result from the data**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name its three virtues.**
**Answer: Exactness for every input, instant computation, and insight into dependencies**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give an example.**
**Answer: The quadratic formula (also: the compound-interest formula)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the analytic road's limit?**
**Answer: Existence — most real models have no formula**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a formula show that a run does not?**
**Answer: How the answer depends on each datum**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a simulation solution?**
**Answer: The computer plays the model step by step by its rules, and the answer is read from the play**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where does simulation win?**
**Answer: Where no formula exists — weather, epidemics, traffic behaviour**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is its price?**
**Answer: It is one run's story — sensitive to starting values; random models need repeated, averaged runs**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do the roads treat each other?**
**Answer: Where both exist, each checks the other**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) State the chapter's judgement.**
**Answer: Analytic where a formula lives; simulation where behaviour must be watched**
