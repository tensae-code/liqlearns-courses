# Analytic Solutions and Simulation Solutions — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an analytic solution?** → **An exact formula derived by mathematics, giving the result from the data**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name its three virtues.** → **Exactness for every input, instant computation, and insight into dependencies**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give an example.** → **The quadratic formula (also: the compound-interest formula)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the analytic road's limit?** → **Existence — most real models have no formula**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a formula show that a run does not?** → **How the answer depends on each datum**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a simulation solution?** → **The computer plays the model step by step by its rules, and the answer is read from the play**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where does simulation win?** → **Where no formula exists — weather, epidemics, traffic behaviour**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is its price?** → **It is one run's story — sensitive to starting values; random models need repeated, averaged runs**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do the roads treat each other?** → **Where both exist, each checks the other**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) State the chapter's judgement.** → **Analytic where a formula lives; simulation where behaviour must be watched**
