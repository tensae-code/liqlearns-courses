# Hands-on — LOGO conditionals — ifelse

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Human ifelse
Stand at a fork drawn on the floor. A partner holds two number cards. If they are equal, walk the TRUE path and say "It's TRUE"; else walk the FALSE path and say "It's FALSE". Swap roles; then play the no-else version — on false, freeze: nothing happens.

## Predict, then run
Write both of the book's examples in the MSW LOGO editor exactly as the lesson gives them. Before running each, write your predicted output. Run, and mark the prediction.
