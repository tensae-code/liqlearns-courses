Programs can **decide**.

**ifelse.** Three parts. Listen:
**Condition** — is it true?
**Action if true.**
**Action if false.**

Two variables. Both **23**. Are they equal? **True!** It prints: **It's TRUE**.
Change one to **25**. Equal now? **False.** It prints: **It's FALSE**.

No else part? Then false means — nothing happens.
Decide first. Then act.
