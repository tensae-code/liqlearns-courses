# Lesson plan — LOGO conditionals — ifelse

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Programming · **Lesson:** LOGO conditionals — ifelse
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 12 — Programming in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 8 book, Unit 6 — Logic Oriented Graphics Oriented (LOGO) Programming. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** conditional statements decide by a condition: ifelse [condition][action if true][action if false] — and without the else part, a false condition means nothing happens.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the variables lesson — the worked examples test two variables against each other, so :value reading must be sure.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the general form of ifelse and what each of its three parts does.
2. Say what happens when the else part is left out and the condition is false.
3. Work both of the book's examples — variables 23 and 23 (TRUE), 23 and 25 (FALSE) — and predict their printed outputs.

## What you need
A computer with MSW LOGO open; paper and a pencil.
