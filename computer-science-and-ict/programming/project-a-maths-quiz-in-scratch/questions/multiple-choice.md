# Project — A Maths Quiz in Scratch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is one round of the quiz?**
a) Ask, capture the answer, compare, and answer back  b) With operators — random numbers and an operation  c) With the same operators that built the question  d) A condition — if they match, or not
**Answer: a) Ask, capture the answer, compare, and answer back**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How are the quiz's questions built?**
a) With the same operators that built the question  b) With operators — random numbers and an operation  c) A condition — if they match, or not  d) The player is shown the true answer
**Answer: b) With operators — random numbers and an operation**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is the true answer worked out?**
a) A condition — if they match, or not  b) The player is shown the true answer  c) With the same operators that built the question  d) A score variable
**Answer: c) With the same operators that built the question**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What compares the player's answer with the true one?**
a) The player is shown the true answer  b) A score variable  c) Zero  d) A condition — if they match, or not
**Answer: d) A condition — if they match, or not**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when the answers do not match?**
a) The player is shown the true answer  b) A score variable  c) Zero  d) By one, for every right answer
**Answer: a) The player is shown the true answer**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What keeps the quiz's count?**
a) Zero  b) A score variable  c) By one, for every right answer  d) By a loop, or by re-asking
**Answer: b) A score variable**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the score set to at the start?**
a) By one, for every right answer  b) By a loop, or by re-asking  c) Zero  d) By reporting the player's score
**Answer: c) Zero**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When does the score change?**
a) By a loop, or by re-asking  b) By reporting the player's score  c) Ask, capture the answer, compare, and answer back  d) By one, for every right answer
**Answer: d) By one, for every right answer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do the rounds repeat?**
a) By a loop, or by re-asking  b) By reporting the player's score  c) Ask, capture the answer, compare, and answer back  d) With operators — random numbers and an operation
**Answer: a) By a loop, or by re-asking**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does the quiz end?**
a) Ask, capture the answer, compare, and answer back  b) By reporting the player's score  c) With operators — random numbers and an operation  d) With the same operators that built the question
**Answer: b) By reporting the player's score**
