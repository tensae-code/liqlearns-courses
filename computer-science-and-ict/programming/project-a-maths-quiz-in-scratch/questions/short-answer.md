# Project — A Maths Quiz in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is one round of the quiz?**
**Answer: Ask, capture the answer, compare, and answer back**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How are the quiz's questions built?**
**Answer: With operators — random numbers and an operation**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is the true answer worked out?**
**Answer: With the same operators that built the question**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What compares the player's answer with the true one?**
**Answer: A condition — if they match, or not**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when the answers do not match?**
**Answer: The player is shown the true answer**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What keeps the quiz's count?**
**Answer: A score variable**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the score set to at the start?**
**Answer: Zero**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When does the score change?**
**Answer: By one, for every right answer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do the rounds repeat?**
**Answer: By a loop, or by re-asking**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does the quiz end?**
**Answer: By reporting the player's score**
