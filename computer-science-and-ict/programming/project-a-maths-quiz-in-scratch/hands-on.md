# Hands-on — Project — A Maths Quiz in Scratch

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build one round
Build a single round: ask a multiplication question from two random numbers, capture the answer, and answer right or show the true answer. Test it with a right and a wrong answer.

## Make it a game
Add the score variable — zero at the start, +1 per right answer — and repeat for five questions. End by reporting the score. Give the game to a classmate to play, and watch where they hesitate.
