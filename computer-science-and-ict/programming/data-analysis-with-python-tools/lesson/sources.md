China's own way: in Compulsory 1, analysis is not left to a spreadsheet alone — students use Python tools on real collected-and-cleaned data from their own chapter project, so the pipeline from raw collection to computed finding is one continuous piece of work.

The two Teacher's Guides in China's set (to Compulsory 1 and to Compulsory 2) are record-only and are cited nowhere in this course: their library records are swapped against the student books (mismatched pairings) and both PDFs are partial (front portions only).

Egypt's own way (Grade 8, Term 2 student book (Arabic)): Term 2 teaches AI from the neuron up — artificial neuron, its role in AI — then Python for data analysis and even encryption with XOR, a depth most countries save for secondary school.
