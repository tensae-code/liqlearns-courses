# Data Analysis with Python Tools — short answer

Answer each question in a few sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A shopkeeper says 'sales are bad lately'. Turn it into two analysable questions and say what grouping each needs.**
**Answer:** For example: 'Which product group's sales fell most this quarter versus last?' — grouping by product group, comparing periods. And 'Is the fall in all shops or one?' — grouping by shop. Each turns a mood into a question a table can answer.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Two students analyse the same clean table; one groups marks by subject, the other by month. Both computations are correct, yet their findings differ. Explain.**
**Answer:** Because grouping decides what can be seen: by subject reveals which subject fell; by month reveals when the fall happened. Neither is wrong — they answer different questions. Analysis findings are always answers to the question actually asked, which is why choosing the question is the human heart of the work.
