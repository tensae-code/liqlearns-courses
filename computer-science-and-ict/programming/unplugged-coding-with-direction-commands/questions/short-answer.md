# Unplugged Coding With Direction Commands — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a program, at this level?**
**Answer: Instructions followed in order, one step at a time**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does the character follow instructions?**
**Answer: Exactly as said — no guessing, no kindness**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When the character goes wrong, where is the fault?**
**Answer: In the instructions, never in the character**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is this lesson's first form of debugging?**
**Answer: Finding the fault in the instructions**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where must the character cross?**
**Answer: A grid or a maze**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the direction commands.**
**Answer: Forward (a number of steps), turn left, turn right**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a route, in this language?**
**Answer: A sequence of direction commands**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is guiding with words, in the lesson's phrase?**
**Answer: Writing a program in speech**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the guide's fallback without computers?**
**Answer: Print the pictures and do the direction work on paper**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the goal — the thinking or the device?**
**Answer: The thinking, not the device**
