# Hands-on — Binary Search Trees

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Build by hand** — Insert 50, 30, 70, 20, 40, 60, 80 into an empty BST, drawing each step. Then search for 60 and for 65, naming every node you visit.

## Task two
**Delete carefully** — From your tree, delete a leaf, then a node with one child, then the root. After each deletion, check the rule still holds at every node.
