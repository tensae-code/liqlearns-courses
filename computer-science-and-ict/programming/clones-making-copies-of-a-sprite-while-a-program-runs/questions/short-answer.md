# Clones — Making Copies of a Sprite While a Program Runs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a clone?**
**Answer: A copy a sprite makes of itself while the program runs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What block makes a clone?**
**Answer: “Create a copy of myself”**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When is a clone made?**
**Answer: While the program runs, not in the editor**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Whose scripts make the copies?**
**Answer: The original sprite’s**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the copies go?**
**Answer: Where the program sends them**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the lesson’s build?**
**Answer: The fruit seller**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do the clone blocks show in the build?**
**Answer: Rows of fruit**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is the stall stocked?**
**Answer: By a loop of copying and placing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How many fruit sprites start the stall?**
**Answer: One sprite, cloned**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What prices the cloned stall?**
**Answer: The shop’s variables (the lesson beside this one)**
