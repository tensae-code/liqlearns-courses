# Clones — Making Copies of a Sprite While a Program Runs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a clone?**
a) A copy a sprite makes of itself while the program runs  b) “Create a copy of myself”  c) The original sprite’s  d) The fruit seller
**Answer: a) A copy a sprite makes of itself while the program runs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What block makes a clone?**
a) While the program runs, not in the editor  b) “Create a copy of myself”  c) Where the program sends them  d) Rows of fruit
**Answer: b) “Create a copy of myself”**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When is a clone made?**
a) The original sprite’s  b) The fruit seller  c) While the program runs, not in the editor  d) By a loop of copying and placing
**Answer: c) While the program runs, not in the editor**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Whose scripts make the copies?**
a) Where the program sends them  b) Rows of fruit  c) One sprite, cloned  d) The original sprite’s
**Answer: d) The original sprite’s**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the copies go?**
a) Where the program sends them  b) The fruit seller  c) By a loop of copying and placing  d) The shop’s variables (the lesson beside this one)
**Answer: a) Where the program sends them**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the lesson’s build?**
a) Rows of fruit  b) The fruit seller  c) One sprite, cloned  d) A copy a sprite makes of itself while the program runs
**Answer: b) The fruit seller**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do the clone blocks show in the build?**
a) By a loop of copying and placing  b) The shop’s variables (the lesson beside this one)  c) Rows of fruit  d) “Create a copy of myself”
**Answer: c) Rows of fruit**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is the stall stocked?**
a) One sprite, cloned  b) A copy a sprite makes of itself while the program runs  c) While the program runs, not in the editor  d) By a loop of copying and placing
**Answer: d) By a loop of copying and placing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How many fruit sprites start the stall?**
a) One sprite, cloned  b) The shop’s variables (the lesson beside this one)  c) “Create a copy of myself”  d) The original sprite’s
**Answer: a) One sprite, cloned**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What prices the cloned stall?**
a) A copy a sprite makes of itself while the program runs  b) The shop’s variables (the lesson beside this one)  c) While the program runs, not in the editor  d) Where the program sends them
