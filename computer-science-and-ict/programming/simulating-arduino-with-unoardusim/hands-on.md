# Hands-on — Simulating Arduino with UnoArduSim

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**First light, simulated** — Write the plan for the standing exercise: which device you add, which pin it joins, and the sketch (setup and loop) that lights it — ready to type into UnoArduSim.

## Task two
**Break it safely** — List three faults you would deliberately make in the simulator (wrong pin, missing semicolon, LED never turned off) and what each teaches when run.
