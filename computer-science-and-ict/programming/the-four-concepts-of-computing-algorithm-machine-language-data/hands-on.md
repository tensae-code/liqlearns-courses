# Hands-on — The Four Concepts of Computing — Algorithm, Machine, Language, Data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Name the four** — Take five course activities (a Blue-Bot route, a Scratch story, a sorting game…). For each, point and name: the data, the algorithm, the language, the machine.

## Task two
**Break one leg** — Choose one concept and remove it from a working scene (no language — how does the plan reach the machine?). Present the collapse in three sentences.
