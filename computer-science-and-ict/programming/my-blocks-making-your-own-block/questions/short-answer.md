# My Blocks — Making Your Own Block — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a My Block?**
**Answer: Your own block, made from a stack you repeat**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When do you make one?**
**Answer: When the same stack is needed again and again**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a My Block created?**
**Answer: By naming it for what it does, then filling its definition**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do you drop in instead of rebuilding the stack?**
**Answer: Your one My Block**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a fix inside the definition do?**
**Answer: Fixes every use of the block**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What pattern does the guide define first?**
**Answer: The zigzag — the stairs pattern**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the repeated pattern live?**
**Answer: Inside the block's definition, once**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the second defined block draw?**
**Answer: A flower petal**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How does one petal become a flower?**
**Answer: By using the block turn after turn**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which program is this lesson about?**
