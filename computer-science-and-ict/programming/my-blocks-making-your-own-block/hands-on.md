# Hands-on — My Blocks — Making Your Own Block

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make the stairs block
Build the zigzag stairs pattern as a My Block, then climb three stairs using only your block. Open the definition and improve one step — watch all stairs improve.

## Petal by petal
Define a petal block and use it with turns to grow a flower. Count how many times your block ran.
