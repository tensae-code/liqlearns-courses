# Hands-on — SDLC Models — Waterfall, Spiral and Agile

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Choose the gait** — For three projects (a tax system, a start-up's app, a space probe's control) choose a model and defend it on the three grounds.

## Task two
**Betrayal test** — Take the Waterfall project and change one requirement mid-build; write what each model would do next.
