# SDLC Models — Waterfall, Spiral and Agile — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does Waterfall walk the stages?**
a) Once, in order, each finished before the next begins  b) When requirements are fixed and known  c) Repeatedly — each loop planning, weighing risks, building, evaluating  d) When risks are large and the destination is learned by travelling
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When is Waterfall right?**
a) When requirements are fixed and known  b) Repeatedly — each loop planning, weighing risks, building, evaluating  c) When risks are large and the destination is learned by travelling  d) Risks
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does Spiral walk?**
