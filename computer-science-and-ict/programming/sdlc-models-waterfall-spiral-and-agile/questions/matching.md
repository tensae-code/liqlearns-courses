# SDLC Models — Waterfall, Spiral and Agile — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does Waterfall walk the stages?** → **Once, in order, each finished before the next begins**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When is Waterfall right?** → **When requirements are fixed and known**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does Spiral walk?** → **Repeatedly — each loop planning, weighing risks, building, evaluating**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When is Spiral right?** → **When risks are large and the destination is learned by travelling**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does each spiral loop weigh especially?** → **Risks**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a sprint?** → **A short iteration producing a small working part**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name Agile's three characteristics.** → **Change welcomed, working software over documents, people over rigid process**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How often does the customer react in Agile?** → **Every few weeks, each round**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name the chooser's three grounds.** → **How fixed the requirements, how costly a wrong turn, how present the customer**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is a model, in the closing phrase?** → **A bet about the future of the project**
