# Hands-on — Mid-Level and High-Level Languages — and Famous Examples

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Place the languages** — Put six languages you know on the ladder — machine, assembly, mid, high, fourth generation — with the clue that placed each.

## Task two
**Profile a language** — Choose one language not profiled in the lesson. Write its three-line profile in the book's manner: parentage, strength, home ground.
