# A Scrolling News Ticker in Scratch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the ticker’s moving part?**
a) A headline sprite  b) In the painter  c) It grows  d) The Ministry’s decision to teach programming from Grade 5 to Grade 9
**Answer: a) A headline sprite**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the Arabic headline written?**
a) It scrolls across the screen  b) In the painter  c) The bulletin’s opening signal  d) The Ministry’s own
**Answer: b) In the painter**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the headline sprite do?**
a) It grows  b) The Ministry’s decision to teach programming from Grade 5 to Grade 9  c) It scrolls across the screen  d) A presenter sprite and a recorded voice
**Answer: c) It scrolls across the screen**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to it as the bulletin starts?**
a) The bulletin’s opening signal  b) The Ministry’s own  c) It reads the bulletin  d) It grows
**Answer: d) It grows**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the size change?**
a) The bulletin’s opening signal  b) The Ministry’s decision to teach programming from Grade 5 to Grade 9  c) A presenter sprite and a recorded voice  d) The news arriving
**Answer: a) The bulletin’s opening signal**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book’s example breaking news?**
a) The Ministry’s own  b) The Ministry’s decision to teach programming from Grade 5 to Grade 9  c) It reads the bulletin  d) A headline sprite
**Answer: b) The Ministry’s decision to teach programming from Grade 5 to Grade 9**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Whose decision is the headline?**
a) A presenter sprite and a recorded voice  b) The news arriving  c) The Ministry’s own  d) In the painter
**Answer: c) The Ministry’s own**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What two things does the project add?**
a) It reads the bulletin  b) A headline sprite  c) It scrolls across the screen  d) A presenter sprite and a recorded voice
**Answer: d) A presenter sprite and a recorded voice**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the recorded voice do?**
a) It reads the bulletin  b) The news arriving  c) In the painter  d) It grows
**Answer: a) It reads the bulletin**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the scroll show?**
a) A headline sprite  b) The news arriving  c) It scrolls across the screen  d) The bulletin’s opening signal
**Answer: b) The news arriving**
