# Hands-on — Objects and Classes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Plan a class** — Design a class for a school thing (Book, Bicycle, CanteenOrder): list four attributes and three methods. Then write out two objects of it with different attribute values.

## Task two
**Object walk** — Take a program or app you know. Name three objects in it, their classes, and one property and one method of each.
