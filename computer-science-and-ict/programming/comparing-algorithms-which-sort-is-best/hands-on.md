# Hands-on — Comparing Algorithms — Which Sort Is Best?

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Three-method trials** — Sort piles of 8, 32 and 100 numbered cards by bubble, merge and quick rules (teams as living algorithms). Count comparisons per method per size; table and crown by size.

## Task two
**Write the bilan** — Write the class bilan in the inquiry's shape: methods, trials, counts, verdict per situation — one paragraph, no adjectives without a number behind them.
