# Hands-on — What Programming Is — Giving Instructions to a Computer

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Be the baker

Write instructions for making a drink or a sandwich in numbered steps. Give them to a partner who must follow them exactly and say nothing. Note every place the result went wrong, and fix the instruction that caused it.

## Name the five words

Open any Scratch project or picture of one. Point to and name: one sprite, one block (command), one script, and say what program the whole project is.
