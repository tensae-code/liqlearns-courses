# Video script — What Programming Is — Giving Instructions to a Computer


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


Programming is giving instructions to a computer.

The computer does exactly what you say. Nothing more.

Like a baker. The recipe must say every step.

Blocks are commands. Joined blocks are a script.

The script tells the sprite what to do.
