# Hands-on — An Algorithm Is a Plan of Steps — Named for al-Khwarizmi

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name your plan
Take a puzzle you already solved and write its algorithm in words, one line per block, beside the program.

## Shorten the lava crossing
Write the lava-crossing algorithm, count its steps, then find a shorter one that still crosses safely.
