# Hands-on — The Real Data Type in Pascal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Approximation hunt** — On paper or a machine, add 0.1 ten times and compare with 1.0; write two sentences explaining the gap by approximation.

## Task two
**Form drill** — Write 45 000, 0.00045 and 6.02×10²³ in exponent form, and 3.2E−2 and 1.5E4 in fixed form.
