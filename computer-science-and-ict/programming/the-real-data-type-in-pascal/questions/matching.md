# The Real Data Type in Pascal — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the real type hold?** → **Numbers with fractional parts — measurements, fractions, averages**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are real's two written forms?** → **Fixed form (2.75) and exponent form (2.75E3 = 2750)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a real variable declared?** → **var x: real;**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is a real held exactly?** → **No — approximately; the type stores a very close neighbour of the true value**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the consequences of approximation return?** → **In Grade 12's lessons on calculation errors**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does 7 / 2 give for reals?** → **3.5 — division that keeps the fraction**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name three standard functions serving reals.** → **abs, sqr and sqrt (also: sin, cos)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is testing reals for exact equality unsafe?** → **Because reals are held approximately — 0.1 + 0.2 may not sit exactly on 0.3**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How should reals be compared?** → **By closeness, not by identity**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of result do sqrt and sin answer?** → **A real result**
