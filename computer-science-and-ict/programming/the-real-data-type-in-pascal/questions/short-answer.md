# The Real Data Type in Pascal — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the real type hold?**
**Answer: Numbers with fractional parts — measurements, fractions, averages**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are real's two written forms?**
**Answer: Fixed form (2.75) and exponent form (2.75E3 = 2750)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a real variable declared?**
**Answer: var x: real;**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is a real held exactly?**
**Answer: No — approximately; the type stores a very close neighbour of the true value**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the consequences of approximation return?**
**Answer: In Grade 12's lessons on calculation errors**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does 7 / 2 give for reals?**
**Answer: 3.5 — division that keeps the fraction**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name three standard functions serving reals.**
**Answer: abs, sqr and sqrt (also: sin, cos)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is testing reals for exact equality unsafe?**
**Answer: Because reals are held approximately — 0.1 + 0.2 may not sit exactly on 0.3**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How should reals be compared?**
**Answer: By closeness, not by identity**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of result do sqrt and sin answer?**
**Answer: A real result**
