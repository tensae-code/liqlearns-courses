# Testing an Algorithm — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does testing an algorithm mean?**
**Answer: Running it step by step on chosen values and comparing with the known right result**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is an algorithm tested against?**
**Answer: The result you already know is right**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a failed test point?**
**Answer: To the step where the trace leaves the right path**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When is testing done?**
**Answer: Before any computer sees the algorithm**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do you test with?**
**Answer: Pencil, paper and patience — a step-by-step trace**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What three kinds of test values should be chosen?**
**Answer: An ordinary value, the edges of the range, and a special or refused value**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are the edges of a range?**
**Answer: The smallest and the largest values allowed**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does each written test record?**
**Answer: Input, expected result, the algorithm's result, and the verdict**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why are ordinary values alone not enough?**
**Answer: The algorithm may still break at the edges**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where do faults like to hide?**
**Answer: At the edges of the range**
