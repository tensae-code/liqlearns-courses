# Hands-on — I Know and I Can — Final Review

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Prove the year
Work one task from each strand of the year in order. Tick each only when done alone and checked; list any gap by name.

## Say the summary
Write five lines: "This year I learned…" — one line per strand, in your own words, each backed by a task you can still do.
