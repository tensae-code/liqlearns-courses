# I Know and I Can — Final Review — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A final review covers —**
a) the whole course so far  b) only the last lesson  c) nothing already taught
**Answer: a) the whole course so far**

**2. Both books' closing reviews stock one lesson because —**
a) they have the same page number  b) reviews need no tasks  c) the thing is the same: a final honest proving of the course
**Answer: c) the thing is the same: a final honest proving of the course**

**3. A gap found in the final review should be —**
a) ticked anyway  b) named and practised  c) hidden
**Answer: b) named and practised**

**4. The Grade 4 closing review comes before —**
a) its game and journey projects  b) the first Scratch lesson  c) the types of information lesson
**Answer: a) its game and journey projects**
