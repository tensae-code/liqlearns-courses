# Hands-on — AND, OR, NOT — Joining Statements

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Both, one, or flip
Your teacher says joined statements about the room, some with AND, some with OR, some with NOT. For each, check the parts, then say if the whole statement is true or false — and which word decided it.

## Build your own
Make one true AND statement, one true OR statement and one NOT statement about today. A friend checks each part and judges the whole.
