# Lesson plan — AND, OR, NOT — Joining Statements

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Programming · **Lesson:** AND, OR, NOT — Joining Statements
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Turkmenistan (2026-11-14) — in the title Programming on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Turkmenistan's *Basics of Informatics, Grade 1*, because no existing lesson teaches this thing as its own lesson.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The little words AND, OR and NOT join and change statements: AND needs both parts true, OR needs at least one part true, and NOT flips a statement into its opposite — 'it is raining AND it is cold', 'tea OR water', 'NOT today'.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on True and False Judgements — every part joined here is a statement that can be true or false — and on the small joining words a child already uses in speech.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **AND and OR — joining two statements** — AND joins two statements and needs both of them true — one false part spoils the whole; OR joins two statements and is happy with at least one true part — one true part saves the whole.
2. **NOT — the opposite statement** — NOT stands in front of a statement and flips it: if the statement is true, NOT makes it false; if it is false, NOT makes it true — 'the door is open' becomes 'the door is NOT open'.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: True and False Judgements, in Programming. Recommended, never forced — a learner may land on this lesson directly, and it stands alone.
- Turkmenistan's own way is kept: in Turkmenistan's Grade 1 book these come in Chapter VI, Logic, as statements with the words И, ИЛИ, НЕ (AND, OR, NOT) — logic's joining words are learned in plain speech, years before any code.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Join two statements with AND and say when the whole is true: only when both parts are true.
2. Join two statements with OR and say when the whole is true: when at least one part is true.
3. Use NOT to flip a statement into its opposite, and say how its truth changes.

## What you need
Nothing but sentences — and a window or door to check the lesson's weather and door statements against.

## If the learner is stuck
Thinks AND is happy with one true part → that is OR's rule; give the carrying test: a bag with two handles is carried safely only if both hold — AND needs both. Forgets the flip with NOT → say the plain statement first, judge it, then say it with NOT and judge it again out loud; the two answers must be opposites.
