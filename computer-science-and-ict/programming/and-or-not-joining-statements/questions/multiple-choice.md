# AND, OR, NOT — Joining Statements — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) 'It is raining AND the wind is blowing' is true only when —**
a) the rain alone is true  b) both parts are true  c) neither part is true
**Answer: b) both parts are true** — AND is strict: one false part spoils the whole statement.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) 'I will drink tea OR water' is false only when —**
a) there is tea  b) there is water  c) there is no tea and no water
**Answer: c) there is no tea and no water** — OR needs at least one true part; it fails only when both fail.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) 'The door is NOT open' is true when —**
a) the door is open  b) the door is shut  c) there is no door at all
**Answer: b) the door is shut** — NOT flips the statement: if 'the door is open' is false, its NOT form is true.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Which word works on ONE statement and flips it?**
a) AND  b) OR  c) NOT
**Answer: c) NOT** — AND and OR join two statements; NOT changes one statement into its opposite.
