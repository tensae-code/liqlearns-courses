# AND, OR, NOT — Joining Statements — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) If one part of an AND statement is false, the whole statement can still be true.**
**Answer: False** — AND needs both parts true; one false part spoils the whole.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) An OR statement is true when at least one part is true.**
**Answer: True** — One true part is enough for OR.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) If 'it is raining' is false, then 'it is NOT raining' is true.**
**Answer: True** — NOT flips false into true.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) NOT joins two statements together.**
**Answer: False** — NOT works on one statement and flips it; AND and OR do the joining.
