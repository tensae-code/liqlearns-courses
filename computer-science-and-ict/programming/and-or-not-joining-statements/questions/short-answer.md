# AND, OR, NOT — Joining Statements — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Make an AND statement about the weather today, and say how you would check if it is true.**
**Answer:** Any two weather statements joined by AND (e.g. 'It is sunny AND it is warm'); checking means looking: both parts must be true for the whole to be true.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Your friend says: 'We can play outside OR read inside.' When is this statement false?**
**Answer:** Only when both parts fail — when playing outside cannot happen and reading inside cannot happen either.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A statement is true. You put NOT in it. What happens, and can you give an example?**
**Answer:** It becomes false — NOT flips truth into its opposite: e.g. 'the sun is shining' (true) becomes 'the sun is NOT shining' (false).
