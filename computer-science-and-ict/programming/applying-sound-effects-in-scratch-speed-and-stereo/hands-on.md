# Hands-on — Applying Sound Effects in Scratch — Speed and Stereo

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two voices, one recording
Take one recorded word. Play it plain, then with the pitch raised, then lowered. Write which playing sounds youngest and which oldest, and the numbers you used.

## Cross the stage
Make a sound cross: set the pan to the left, play, move it step by step to the right, playing as it goes. Follow a sprite crossing the stage with your eyes closed — can a partner follow it by ear?
