# Applying Sound Effects in Scratch — Speed and Stereo — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the pitch effect change?**
**Answer: How a sound plays — its speed and height**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when the pitch effect is raised?**
**Answer: The sound plays faster and higher**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when it is lowered?**
**Answer: The sound plays slower and lower**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is the effect set?**
**Answer: By number — and it holds until changed again**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Does the effect change the recording itself?**
**Answer: No — it is applied while the sound plays**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the pan effect move?**
**Answer: The sound between the left and the right side**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a middle pan setting do?**
**Answer: It plays the sound from both sides together**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can pan make a project do?**
**Answer: Place sounds in space — footsteps crossing with a character**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How are effects cleared?**
**Answer: With the clear-sound-effects step**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why clear effects?**
**Answer: So the sprite returns to its natural voice**
