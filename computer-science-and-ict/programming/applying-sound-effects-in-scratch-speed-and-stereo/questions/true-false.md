# Applying Sound Effects in Scratch — Speed and Stereo — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the pitch effect change?" is: How a sound plays — its speed and height**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What happens when the pitch effect is raised?" is: The sound between the left and the right side**
**Answer: False** — The sound plays faster and higher

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What happens when it is lowered?" is: The sound plays slower and lower**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is the effect set?" is: Place sounds in space — footsteps crossing with a character**
**Answer: False** — By number — and it holds until changed again

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Does the effect change the recording itself?" is: No — it is applied while the sound plays**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the pan effect move?" is: So the sprite returns to its natural voice**
**Answer: False** — The sound between the left and the right side

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does a middle pan setting do?" is: It plays the sound from both sides together**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What can pan make a project do?" is: The sound plays faster and higher**
**Answer: False** — Place sounds in space — footsteps crossing with a character

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How are effects cleared?" is: With the clear-sound-effects step**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why clear effects?" is: By number — and it holds until changed again**
**Answer: False** — So the sprite returns to its natural voice
