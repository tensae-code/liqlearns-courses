# Applying Sound Effects in Scratch — Speed and Stereo — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the pitch effect change?**
a) How a sound plays — its speed and height  b) The sound plays faster and higher  c) The sound plays slower and lower  d) By number — and it holds until changed again
**Answer: a) How a sound plays — its speed and height**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when the pitch effect is raised?**
a) The sound plays slower and lower  b) The sound plays faster and higher  c) By number — and it holds until changed again  d) No — it is applied while the sound plays
**Answer: b) The sound plays faster and higher**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when it is lowered?**
a) By number — and it holds until changed again  b) No — it is applied while the sound plays  c) The sound plays slower and lower  d) The sound between the left and the right side
**Answer: c) The sound plays slower and lower**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is the effect set?**
a) No — it is applied while the sound plays  b) The sound between the left and the right side  c) It plays the sound from both sides together  d) By number — and it holds until changed again
**Answer: d) By number — and it holds until changed again**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Does the effect change the recording itself?**
a) No — it is applied while the sound plays  b) The sound between the left and the right side  c) It plays the sound from both sides together  d) Place sounds in space — footsteps crossing with a character
**Answer: a) No — it is applied while the sound plays**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the pan effect move?**
a) It plays the sound from both sides together  b) The sound between the left and the right side  c) Place sounds in space — footsteps crossing with a character  d) With the clear-sound-effects step
**Answer: b) The sound between the left and the right side**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a middle pan setting do?**
a) Place sounds in space — footsteps crossing with a character  b) With the clear-sound-effects step  c) It plays the sound from both sides together  d) So the sprite returns to its natural voice
**Answer: c) It plays the sound from both sides together**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can pan make a project do?**
a) With the clear-sound-effects step  b) So the sprite returns to its natural voice  c) How a sound plays — its speed and height  d) Place sounds in space — footsteps crossing with a character
**Answer: d) Place sounds in space — footsteps crossing with a character**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How are effects cleared?**
a) With the clear-sound-effects step  b) So the sprite returns to its natural voice  c) How a sound plays — its speed and height  d) The sound plays faster and higher
**Answer: a) With the clear-sound-effects step**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why clear effects?**
a) How a sound plays — its speed and height  b) So the sprite returns to its natural voice  c) The sound plays faster and higher  d) The sound plays slower and lower
**Answer: b) So the sprite returns to its natural voice**
