# Algorithms and programs — the average of ten numbers — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is a sequence of instructions written using a programming language, executed by a computer. (Name the term.)
**Answer: a program**

**2.** _____ is the computer's own special language — computers do not understand human languages. (Name the term.)
**Answer: a programming language**

**3.** _____ is what must happen to an algorithm before a computer can execute it: it must become a program. (Name the term.)
**Answer: transforming**
