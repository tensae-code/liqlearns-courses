This is **Algorithms and programs — the average of ten numbers**.

**the average algorithm** — get the 10 numbers; get SUM by adding them; get AVERAGE by dividing SUM by 10; report AVERAGE.

**a program** — a sequence of instructions written using a programming language, executed by a computer.

**a programming language** — the computer's own special language — computers do not understand human languages.

**transforming** — what must happen to an algorithm before a computer can execute it: it must become a program.
