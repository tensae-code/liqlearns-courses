# Separators in Pascal and C++ — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do separators do?**
**Answer: They divide program text into its units — words, lists, groups, instructions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What separates words?**
**Answer: Blanks and line breaks**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the semicolon's job in Pascal?**
**Answer: It separates instructions from each other**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the semicolon's job in C++?**
**Answer: It terminates — ends — each instruction**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Does indentation change a program?**
**Answer: No — whitespace beyond separation is free; it is written for people**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Pascal's last instruction before end need no semicolon?**
**Answer: Because the semicolon separates instructions — there is nothing after it to separate from**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a missing semicolon in C++?**
**Answer: An immediate error — the instruction is unterminated**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is an extra semicolon in C++?**
**Answer: An empty statement — with the same traps as Pascal's empty instruction**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) State the emblematic difference in one line.**
**Answer: Pascal's semicolon separates; C++'s terminates**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the chapter conclude from separators?**
**Answer: That syntax is real — the same text, cut differently, means differently**
