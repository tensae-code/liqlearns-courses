# Hands-on — Separators in Pascal and C++

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Cut the stream** — Take a program printed without line breaks and restore its separators and layout in both languages, marking every separator's job.

## Task two
**Error planting** — Write the same five-instruction fragment with one separator error for Pascal and one for C++, and write the compiler's complaint for each.
