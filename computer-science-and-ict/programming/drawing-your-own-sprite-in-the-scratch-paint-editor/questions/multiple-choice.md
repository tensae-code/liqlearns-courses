# Drawing Your Own Sprite in the Scratch Paint Editor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where is a new sprite drawn?**
a) In the paint editor  b) A traffic-light sprite  c) Red, orange and green  d) It turns the drawing to face the other way
**Answer: a) In the paint editor**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the book’s worked drawing?**
a) Three costumes  b) A traffic-light sprite  c) One drawing, in three costumes  d) A library costume
**Answer: b) A traffic-light sprite**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Into how many costumes is the traffic light duplicated?**
a) Red, orange and green  b) It turns the drawing to face the other way  c) Three costumes  d) A new costume
**Answer: c) Three costumes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What colours are the three costumes coloured?**
a) One drawing, in three costumes  b) A library costume  c) By message  d) Red, orange and green
**Answer: d) Red, orange and green**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How many drawings does the traffic light need?**
a) One drawing, in three costumes  b) It turns the drawing to face the other way  c) A new costume  d) Yes — exactly like them
**Answer: a) One drawing, in three costumes**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does flipping do?**
a) A library costume  b) It turns the drawing to face the other way  c) By message  d) In the paint editor
**Answer: b) It turns the drawing to face the other way**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What can be edited instead of starting from nothing?**
a) A new costume  b) Yes — exactly like them  c) A library costume  d) A traffic-light sprite
**Answer: c) A library costume**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can be added to a sprite that has costumes?**
a) By message  b) In the paint editor  c) Three costumes  d) A new costume
**Answer: d) A new costume**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do the traffic light’s sprites communicate?**
a) By message  b) Yes — exactly like them  c) A traffic-light sprite  d) Red, orange and green
**Answer: a) By message**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Do drawn sprites join the program like library sprites?**
a) In the paint editor  b) Yes — exactly like them  c) Three costumes  d) One drawing, in three costumes
**Answer: b) Yes — exactly like them**
