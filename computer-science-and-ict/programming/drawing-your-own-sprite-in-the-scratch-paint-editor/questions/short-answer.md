# Drawing Your Own Sprite in the Scratch Paint Editor — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where is a new sprite drawn?**
**Answer: In the paint editor**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the book’s worked drawing?**
**Answer: A traffic-light sprite**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Into how many costumes is the traffic light duplicated?**
**Answer: Three costumes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What colours are the three costumes coloured?**
**Answer: Red, orange and green**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How many drawings does the traffic light need?**
**Answer: One drawing, in three costumes**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does flipping do?**
**Answer: It turns the drawing to face the other way**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What can be edited instead of starting from nothing?**
**Answer: A library costume**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can be added to a sprite that has costumes?**
**Answer: A new costume**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do the traffic light’s sprites communicate?**
**Answer: By message**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Do drawn sprites join the program like library sprites?**
**Answer: Yes — exactly like them**
