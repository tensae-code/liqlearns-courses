# The Pascal Alphabet and Symbols — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What letters does Pascal's alphabet use?**
**Answer: The Latin letters A–Z and a–z**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three special symbols of Pascal.**
**Answer: +, := and ; (any three special symbols)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens if a program uses a sign outside the alphabet?**
**Answer: The compiler stops at once**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) From what are Pascal's service words built?**
**Answer: From the alphabet's letters, kept whole**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name two service words.**
**Answer: program and begin (or end)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is an identifier?**
**Answer: A name the programmer invents**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must an identifier begin with?**
**Answer: A letter**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is 1sum not allowed?**
**Answer: It begins with a digit**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What may an identifier not be?**
**Answer: A service word**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the alphabet lesson stand first?**
**Answer: Every later rule is written in these letters and names**
