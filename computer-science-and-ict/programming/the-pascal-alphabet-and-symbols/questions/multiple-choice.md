# The Pascal Alphabet and Symbols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What letters does Pascal's alphabet use?**
a) The Latin letters A–Z and a–z  b) +, := and ; (any three special symbols)  c) The compiler stops at once  d) From the alphabet's letters, kept whole
**Answer: a) The Latin letters A–Z and a–z**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three special symbols of Pascal.**
a) The compiler stops at once  b) +, := and ; (any three special symbols)  c) From the alphabet's letters, kept whole  d) program and begin (or end)
**Answer: b) +, := and ; (any three special symbols)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens if a program uses a sign outside the alphabet?**
a) From the alphabet's letters, kept whole  b) program and begin (or end)  c) The compiler stops at once  d) A name the programmer invents
**Answer: c) The compiler stops at once**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) From what are Pascal's service words built?**
a) program and begin (or end)  b) A name the programmer invents  c) A letter  d) From the alphabet's letters, kept whole
**Answer: d) From the alphabet's letters, kept whole**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name two service words.**
a) program and begin (or end)  b) A name the programmer invents  c) A letter  d) It begins with a digit
**Answer: a) program and begin (or end)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is an identifier?**
a) A letter  b) A name the programmer invents  c) It begins with a digit  d) A service word
**Answer: b) A name the programmer invents**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must an identifier begin with?**
a) It begins with a digit  b) A service word  c) A letter  d) Every later rule is written in these letters and names
**Answer: c) A letter**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is 1sum not allowed?**
a) A service word  b) Every later rule is written in these letters and names  c) The Latin letters A–Z and a–z  d) It begins with a digit
**Answer: d) It begins with a digit**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What may an identifier not be?**
a) A service word  b) Every later rule is written in these letters and names  c) The Latin letters A–Z and a–z  d) +, := and ; (any three special symbols)
**Answer: a) A service word**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the alphabet lesson stand first?**
a) The Latin letters A–Z and a–z  b) Every later rule is written in these letters and names  c) +, := and ; (any three special symbols)  d) The compiler stops at once
**Answer: b) Every later rule is written in these letters and names**
