# Lesson plan — Sensing Touching in Scratch — When Sprites Meet

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Programming · **Lesson:** Sensing Touching in Scratch — When Sprites Meet
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Palestine (2026-11-14) — in the title Programming on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Palestine's Grade 8 Programming student book, because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Sensing blocks test touching — the mouse pointer, another sprite, or a colour; wait-until holds a script until its sensing test comes true; and layers (go to front/back) decide what shows in front when sprites meet.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Touching — pointer, sprite, colour** — A **touching** test asks whether the sprite is touching the **mouse pointer**, **another sprite**, or a **colour** on the stage. Inside a game these tests are the referee: the monkey has the banana exactly when touching says so — not when the player thinks so.
2. **Wait-until and layers** — **Wait-until** holds everything until its test comes true — the script’s way of standing at the meeting point. When sprites meet, **layers** decide the picture: **go to front** brings a sprite over the others, **go to back** sends it behind.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
