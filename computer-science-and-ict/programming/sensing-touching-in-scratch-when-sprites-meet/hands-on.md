# Hands-on — Sensing Touching in Scratch — When Sprites Meet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Referee a meeting
Build two sprites and a program that says “caught” only when touching reports the meeting. Move them apart and together to test it.

## Wait at the meeting point
Use wait-until so a sprite waits at a colour line, then moves on the instant it touches the colour.
