# Sensing Touching in Scratch — When Sprites Meet — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a touching test ask?**
**Answer: Whether the sprite is touching something**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three things can touching test?**
**Answer: The mouse pointer, another sprite, or a colour**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who is the referee in a sensing game?**
**Answer: The touching test**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When does the monkey have the banana?**
**Answer: Exactly when touching says so**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is the player’s opinion the test?**
**Answer: No — the sensing block is**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does wait-until do?**
**Answer: It holds the script until its test comes true**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is wait-until’s way of working?**
**Answer: Standing at the meeting point until the event**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do layers decide when sprites meet?**
**Answer: What shows in front**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does go to front do?**
**Answer: It brings the sprite over the others**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does go to back do?**
**Answer: It sends the sprite behind the others**
