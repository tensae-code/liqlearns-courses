# Hands-on — Finding a Path with Motion Blocks

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Spell with a path
Draw a path that follows the letters of a short word. Write the motion blocks that would walk it, then swap with a partner and check each other's spelling-path.

## Dice path game
Roll a dice to choose how many moves you may use. Build a path to the goal in exactly that many motion blocks — or say why it cannot be done.
