# Finding a Path with Motion Blocks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the first thing to do before building a path?**
a) Plan the whole route  b) Snap blocks at random  c) Run the hero at once
**Answer: a) Plan the whole route**

**2. In "Spell a word", the hero's path —**
a) avoids all letters  b) erases the word  c) follows the letters of a word
**Answer: c) follows the letters of a word**

**3. When the hero misses the goal, what do you change?**
a) The screen  b) The path  c) The goal
**Answer: b) The path**

**4. In the dice game, the dice helps you —**
a) choose moves to judge and order  b) skip planning  c) delete the maze
**Answer: a) choose moves to judge and order**
