# Hands-on — For Loop Programs Counting Multiples Evens and Primes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 4 §4.2.4 Solved Examples (count multiples in a range; even numbers between two limits; is it prime; all primes from 2 to 100) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach for loop programs counting multiples evens and primes to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
