# Hands-on — The Structure of a Pascal and C++ Program

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Anatomy labelling** — Take one full program in each language and label every structural part with its name and job, in the margins.

## Task two
**Skeleton writing** — Write the empty skeleton of a program that will need two constants, three types, five variables and two subprograms — in both languages, nothing filled in.
