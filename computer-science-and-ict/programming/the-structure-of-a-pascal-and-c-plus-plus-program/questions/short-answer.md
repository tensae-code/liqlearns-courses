# The Structure of a Pascal and C++ Program — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What opens a Pascal program?**
**Answer: The heading — program Name;**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What sections form the declaration part?**
**Answer: label, const, type and var — then the subprogram declarations**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the main body?**
**Answer: The instructions between begin and the final end.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) May parts appear out of order?**
**Answer: No — declarations stand before use, always**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a subprogram's shape like?**
**Answer: A small program in itself — its own heading, declarations and body**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What comes first in a C++ program?**
**Answer: Preprocessor directives — #include for the libraries**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is main in C++?**
**Answer: The function where execution begins — one function among the program's set**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do the two anatomies differ in image?**
**Answer: Pascal is one document in fixed order; C++ is a workshop of named tools with main as the door**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What law do both anatomies serve?**
**Answer: Everything used is declared, and execution starts at a known place**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Does C++ have a var section?**
**Answer: No — declarations stand with their functions and blocks**
