# Hands-on — Approximate Numbers — Absolute and Relative Error

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Error accounts** — For a = 3.14159 standing for π, and a ruler reading of 24.6 cm ± 0.05 cm, compute absolute and relative errors (use π's true value to 7 digits).

## Task two
**Quality court** — Judge three measurements by relative error: 1000 m ± 1 m; 10 m ± 1 cm; 5 mm ± 0.5 mm — best to worst, with the percentages shown.
