# Solving Problems by Enumeration — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Enumeration can miss a solution that lies inside its candidate list.**
**Answer: False** — Every candidate is tested — that is the guarantee.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Conditions from the problem can shrink the candidate list before testing begins.**
**Answer: True** — A known digit or a halving condition saves real time.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Enumeration is always the fastest method.**
**Answer: False** — It is certain, not fast — a huge list can take longer than a lifetime.
