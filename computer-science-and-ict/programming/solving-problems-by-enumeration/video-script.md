# Video script — Solving Problems by Enumeration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

No formula? Then list everything — and test it all.
Generate every candidate, in order. No gaps. No repeats.
Test each one against all the conditions.
Keep the ones that pass. You cannot miss a solution.
But count the list first. Lists can explode.
Small list: enumerate. Huge list: think again.
