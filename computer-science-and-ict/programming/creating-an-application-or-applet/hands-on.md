# Hands-on — Creating an Application or Applet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Two deliveries', 'Take a small program you have written (an average-marks calculator). State what must be added or changed to deliver it as an application, and what its delivery as an applet would need instead.')

## Task two
('Choose the shape', 'For each, choose application or applet and defend it: (a) the school office's marks system on office PCs; (b) a tiny temperature converter embedded in the school website's science page; (c) a library system run overnight on one machine.')
