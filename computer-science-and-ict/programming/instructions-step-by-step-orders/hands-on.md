# Hands-on — Instructions — Step-by-Step Orders

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Instruction test** — Give a partner three instructions to draw a house; if the drawing goes wrong, find which instruction failed the clear-exact-doable test and fix it.

## Task two
**Forms hunt** — Collect one instruction in each form — a picture sequence, a written order, a sign, a sound signal — and say who its executor is.
