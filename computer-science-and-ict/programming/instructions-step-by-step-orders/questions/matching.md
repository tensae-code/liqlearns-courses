# Instructions — Step-by-Step Orders — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an instruction?** → **An order that tells an executor exactly one thing to do**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three tests must a good instruction pass?** → **It must be clear, exact and doable**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does 'bring me something nice' fail?** → **It is not clear or exact — the executor cannot know what is wanted**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Give a passing instruction from the lesson.** → **'Bring me the blue book from the table'**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are instructions the bricks of?** → **Every algorithm and every program**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) In what forms can instructions come?** → **Pictures, spoken words, written words, signs and arrows, and sounds**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a whistle in a game?** → **An instruction to stop**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Do instructions live only in pictures?** → **No — pictures are only one of their forms**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What stays the same in every form?** → **One order, for one executor, to be carried out exactly**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who or what carries out an instruction?** → **Its executor**
