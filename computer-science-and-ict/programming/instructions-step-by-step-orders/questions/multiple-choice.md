# Instructions — Step-by-Step Orders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an instruction?**
a) An order that tells an executor exactly one thing to do  b) It must be clear, exact and doable  c) It is not clear or exact — the executor cannot know what is wanted  d) 'Bring me the blue book from the table'
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three tests must a good instruction pass?**
a) It must be clear, exact and doable  b) It is not clear or exact — the executor cannot know what is wanted  c) 'Bring me the blue book from the table'  d) Every algorithm and every program
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does 'bring me something nice' fail?**
a) It is not clear or exact — the executor cannot know what is wanted  b) 'Bring me the blue book from the table'  c) Every algorithm and every program  d) Pictures, spoken words, written words, signs and arrows, and sounds
**Answer: a**
