# Bubble Sort — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does bubble sort compare?**
**Answer: Neighbouring pairs, from the start of the list**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When are two neighbours swapped?**
**Answer: When they are out of order — the left bigger than the right, for ascending order**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does one pass guarantee?**
**Answer: The biggest unsorted value has reached the end**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is it called bubble sort?**
**Answer: The biggest value rises step by step to the end, like a bubble**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Over what part do later passes run?**
**Answer: The still-unsorted front — the sorted tail is left alone**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why do the passes get shorter?**
**Answer: Each pass parks one more biggest value at the end**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does the sort know it is finished?**
**Answer: A whole pass makes no swaps**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the pseudocode's shape?**
**Answer: An outer loop for passes, an inner loop for comparisons, and a swapped flag**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Is bubble sort fast?**
**Answer: No — simple and slow**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is its teaching worth?**
**Answer: Every swap can be watched and counted**
