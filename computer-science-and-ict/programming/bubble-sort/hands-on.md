# Hands-on — Bubble Sort

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Bubble by hand', 'Bubble-sort 5, 1, 4, 2 with cards, writing the list after every swap and circling the end of each pass. Count the passes and the swaps.')

## Task two
('Catch the clean pass', 'Bubble-sort 2, 3, 4, 5, 1 fully, then bubble-sort 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. Say which sort ended by the clean-pass rule and how you know.')
