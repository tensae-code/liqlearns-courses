# Hands-on — The Bisection Method

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Corner a root** — Run four bisection steps by hand on x² − 2 over [1, 2], tabulating bracket, midpoint, f(midpoint) and kept half.

## Task two
**Step budget** — Compute the steps needed to locate a root in [2, 3] to within 10⁻⁴, and the final bracket's width and reportable error.
