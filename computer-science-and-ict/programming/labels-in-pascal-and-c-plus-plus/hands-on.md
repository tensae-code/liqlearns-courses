# Hands-on — Labels in Pascal and C++

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Label spotting** — In a printed Pascal program, find the label declaration and every labelled instruction, and match each label to its goto.

## Task two
**Structure it** — Take a small program that uses goto with a label to repeat three prints, and rewrite it with a for loop, labels removed.
