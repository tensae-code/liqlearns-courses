# Programming a Tic-Tac-Toe Game in Python — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How is the board held?**
**Answer: Nine cells in a list — empty, X or O**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the program's five workings.**
**Answer: Display, take a move, place the mark, test the end, change the turn**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which moves are refused?**
**Answer: Occupied cells and moves outside the board**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What lines win?**
**Answer: Rows, columns and diagonals**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a full board with no line?**
**Answer: A draw**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What chooses the computer's move?**
**Answer: Adversarial search — minimax**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) In the search, who is MAX and MIN?**
**Answer: The computer is MAX; the player is MIN**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why can this tree be searched whole?**
**Answer: Tic-tac-toe is small enough**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What claim can the pupil verify by playing?**
**Answer: The minimax opponent never loses**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Jordan choose this game?**
**Answer: The search theory becomes an opponent testable by your own hand**
