# What a problem is — and what computers cannot settle — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is droughts and floods — hunger, destroyed infrastructure, dried lakes, wildfires, disease moving into the highlands. (Name the term.)
**Answer: climate change effects**

**2.** _____ is globally the main killer of 15–29-year-olds, with causes from poor roads to vehicles' age and condition. (Name the term.)
**Answer: road accidents**

**3.** _____ is individuals, local government, religious and community leaders — not a computer alone. (Name the term.)
**Answer: who settles big problems**
