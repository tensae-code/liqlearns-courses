# Hands-on — What a problem is — and what computers cannot settle

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Crossing audit
At a real crossing near school (with a teacher), check: sidewalk? crosswalk? view? List what a walker must do there, rule by rule.

## Problem panel
In groups, take one of the three problems and present: its harms, its causes, and who must act — people first.
