# Video script — What a problem is — and what computers cannot settle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Walk on the sidewalk, where there is one.
Where there is none: face the traffic. Keep far from it.
Cross at the crossing. Look every way — cars turn, too.
No crossing? Stand where the light is good and you can see. Wait for a safe gap.
Watch driveways. Watch cars backing up.
Rules keep walkers alive. Follow them.
