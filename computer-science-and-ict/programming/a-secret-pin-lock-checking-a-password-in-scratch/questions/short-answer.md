# A Secret PIN Lock — Checking a Password in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why do phones use PINs and passwords?**
**Answer: For privacy and security**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many button sprites form the keypad?**
**Answer: Ten**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How are the ten buttons made?**
**Answer: By duplicating one drawn button**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to the copied buttons?**
**Answer: They are renumbered**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is the entered number built?**
**Answer: Digit by digit, with join**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What block joins the digits?**
**Answer: Join**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where is the saved PIN kept?**
**Answer: In a hidden variable**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the PIN variable hidden?**
**Answer: So the secret is not shown on the stage**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When does the check run?**
**Answer: Only when the entered length equals the PIN length**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Are half-typed numbers judged?**
**Answer: No — never**
