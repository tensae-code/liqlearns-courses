# Video script — A Secret PIN Lock — Checking a Password in Scratch

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The lesson opens with the reason: phones use PINs and passwords for **privacy and security**. The lock’s face is a **keypad of ten button sprites made by duplicating** — one digit button drawn, then copied and renumbered until all ten stand in their grid.
Each press adds its digit: the **entered number is built digit by digit with join**. A **hidden variable keeps the saved PIN**, and the program’s care is in its timing — **the check runs only when the entered length equals the PIN length**, so half-typed numbers are never judged.
