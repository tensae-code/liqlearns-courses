# Hands-on — The Addition Algorithm — Carrying, Step by Step

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Robot addition** — One pupil is the “adder” who may only follow the written algorithm card, aloud, step by step, for 587 + 688. The class audits: any step taken that the card does not name gets added to the card.

## Task two
**Draw the logigramme** — Draw addition's flowchart with loop and carry-decision diamond. Test it by “running” 999 + 1 with a finger on the chart.
