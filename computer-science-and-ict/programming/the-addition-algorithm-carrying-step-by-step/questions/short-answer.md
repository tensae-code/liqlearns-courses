**1. (Sub lesson 1) In what order are columns worked?**
**Answer: From the right**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is added in each column?**
**Answer: The digits plus any carry**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is written down?**
**Answer: The units digit of the column result**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What travels to the next column?**
**Answer: The tens digit — the carry**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What can a final carry become?**
**Answer: A new first digit of the answer**

**Answer: Is the column sum ten or more?**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the carry, in program terms?**
**Answer: A variable carried through the iterations**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two flow shapes does addition reveal?**
