It sits in the Programming title (programming/), following the book's own order.

China's own way: Compulsory 1 uses the vivid phrase 'dirty data' (脏数据) and gives its cleaning a full section of the data-processing chapter — school students are taught that real data arrives dirty and that cleaning it is skilled, honest work, not a chore before the real work.

The two Teacher's Guides in China's set (to Compulsory 1 and to Compulsory 2) are record-only and are cited nowhere in this course: their library records are swapped against the student books (mismatched pairings) and both PDFs are partial (front portions only).
