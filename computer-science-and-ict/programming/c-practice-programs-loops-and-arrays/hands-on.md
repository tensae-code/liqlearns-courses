# Hands-on — C Practice Programs Loops and Arrays

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 4 Programming Practice (star and letter patterns; swap first and last elements; GCD by the Euclidean method; first/last element of a 7-element array; count elements greater than 10) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach c practice programs loops and arrays to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
