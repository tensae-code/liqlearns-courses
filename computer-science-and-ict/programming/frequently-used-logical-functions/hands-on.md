# Hands-on — Frequently Used Logical Functions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Table building** — Write the truth tables of NAND, NOR, XOR and XNOR from their definitions, and mark the one row where XOR differs from OR.

## Task two
**Universality proof** — Build NOT, AND and OR using only NAND gates in diagrams, and verify the AND construction by truth table.
