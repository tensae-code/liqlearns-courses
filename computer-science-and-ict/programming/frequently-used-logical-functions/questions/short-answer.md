# Frequently Used Logical Functions — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is NAND?**
**Answer: NOT-AND — 0 only when both inputs are 1**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is NOR?**
**Answer: NOT-OR — 1 only when both inputs are 0**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does 'universal' mean for NAND and NOR?**
**Answer: Each alone can build NOT, AND and OR — whole circuits from one gate kind**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is NAND's truth table written?**
**Answer: Take AND's table and flip every output**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why are real chips often NAND chips?**
**Answer: One universal gate kind simplifies manufacture**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When does XOR give 1?**
**Answer: When the inputs differ**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is XNOR?**
**Answer: XOR negated — 1 when the inputs agree**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does XOR work inside arithmetic?**
**Answer: As the sum bit of an adder (carry aside)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is XNOR used for?**
**Answer: Testing equality of bits**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is any of the four proved?**
**Answer: By its four-row truth table**
