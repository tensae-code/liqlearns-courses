# Algorithm Efficiency and Optimisation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is efficiency judged by?**
**Answer: How the work grows as the input grows**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why not judge by a stopwatch?**
**Answer: It measures one machine on one day, not the algorithm**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to n² work when n doubles?**
**Answer: It quadruples**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What decides survival on big data?**
**Answer: The growth shape, not the constant**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) About how much work is a simple pass over n items?**
**Answer: About n operations**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name two kinds of avoidable work.**
**Answer: Recalculating inside a loop and needless extra passes (any two from the lesson)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where should a repeated calculation move?**
**Answer: Once, before the loop**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does sorted data allow in searching?**
**Answer: Halving the search space each step**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is optimised first?**
**Answer: The growth — then the details**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must an optimisation never sacrifice?**
**Answer: Correctness (and readability)**
