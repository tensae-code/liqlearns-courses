# Numbers in Pascal and C++ — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How is an integer constant written?**
**Answer: Digits with an optional sign — 42, −7, +1000**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why is 1 000 wrong as one constant?**
**Answer: Spaces may not interrupt a constant — it reads as two**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) In C++, what do a leading 0 and 0x mean?**
**Answer: Octal and hexadecimal — 017 = 15, 0x1F = 31**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Does Pascal write integers in other bases?**
**Answer: No — decimal only, in this course's writing**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a constant's form announce?**
**Answer: Its reading — base and kind — before its value**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What makes a constant real?**
**Answer: A fractional part (point) or an exponent (E form)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does 2.5E3 mean?**
**Answer: 2.5 times ten to the power 3 — 2500**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is 3,14 an error?**
**Answer: The decimal separator is a point in both languages, never a comma**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What decides a numeric constant's type?**
**Answer: Its writing — point or E makes it real; plain digits make it integer**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Write 0.0012 in exponent form.**
**Answer: 1.2E−3 (also written 1.2E-3)**
