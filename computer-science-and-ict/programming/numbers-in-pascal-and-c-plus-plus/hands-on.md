# Hands-on — Numbers in Pascal and C++

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Constant court** — Judge ten written forms (1 000; 3,5; 0xFF; 017; 1E2; .5; 5.; −0) for Pascal and for C++: legal or not, type, and value.

## Task two
**Base reading** — Compute 0x2A, 052 and 2.75E2 in decimal, and write 255 in C++ hexadecimal form.
