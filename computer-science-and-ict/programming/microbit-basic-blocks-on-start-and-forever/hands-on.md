# Hands-on — micro:bit Basic Blocks — On Start and Forever

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('First sentence, heartbeat', 'Write a program whose On start shows your name once and whose Forever beats a heart shape on and off. Label each frame.')

## Task two
('Read a frame', 'Given a partner's program, point to what runs once and what runs for ever, and say how you know.')
