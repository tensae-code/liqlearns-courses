# Hands-on — Programming languages — and why LOGO

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Blind drawing
Repeat the opening game, but agree a tiny strict language first: only the words forward, back, left, right, and numbers. Draw the square again with only those. Compare the two attempts — the strict language is slower to speak and impossible to misunderstand. That trade is programming.

## Language sorting
Sort these into natural or programming, and defend each: Amharic, LOGO, English, Python, the language of a recipe book, Java.
