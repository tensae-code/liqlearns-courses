# Video script — Programming languages — and why LOGO

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

People speak natural languages. Loose. Full of guessing.
Computers need the opposite. Exact.

A programming language instructs a computer.
C-plus-plus. Python. Java. And LOGO.

LOGO was made for children. Easy. Graphical.
A turtle draws. Big pictures — little code.
Fun. Logic. Skill. That is why we learn it.
