# String Functions in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a string?**
**Answer: A piece of text — letters, spaces and marks held as one value**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the join operator do?**
**Answer: It glues two strings into one string**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is join 'Thim' and 'phu'?**
**Answer: Thimphu**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does nesting joins build?**
**Answer: A whole sentence, built piece by piece from joined results**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is often spoken by a say block?**
**Answer: A joined string — such as a greeting joined with a typed name**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Letter (n) of a string report?**
**Answer: Its nth character**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is letter 1 of 'Bhutan'?**
**Answer: B**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does Length Of report?**
**Answer: How many characters the string holds, spaces counted**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name a real tool these two build with ask and variables.**
**Answer: Counting the letters in a typed name, or checking an answer letter by letter (any one named)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which block family do string functions belong to?**
**Answer: The operator blocks**
