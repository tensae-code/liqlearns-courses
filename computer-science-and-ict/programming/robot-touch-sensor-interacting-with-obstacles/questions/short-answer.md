# Robot Touch Sensor — Interacting with Obstacles — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the Touch sensor do?**
**Answer: A front button pressed when the robot meets an obstacle — the program can ask: pressed or not**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What becomes writable with touch?**
**Answer: 'Drive until you feel it' — the first sensing loop**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does the wall announce itself?**
**Answer: By being touched**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does the parking mission stop the robot?**
**Answer: By evidence — touch at the bay's end — not by a guessed distance**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is an obstacle meeting in 'returns home'?**
**Answer: Information — a landmark the program uses to find its way back**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which robot carries the sensor here?**
**Answer: The SPIKE robot**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) In which simulation do the missions run?**
**Answer: The RCJ Rescue simulation in Open Roberta Lab**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of input is touch — planned or sensed?**
**Answer: Sensed — it comes from the world, not the plan**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name this unit's two missions.**
**Answer: Parking, and robot-returns-home**
