Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the number-conversion project do?**
a) Takes a number in one system and returns it in another  b) Decimal to binary, binary to hexadecimal, and their reverses (among the three systems)  c) A place to type the number, a choice of systems (from, to), a convert action, and a place the answer appears  d) An invalid entry — a 'binary' number containing a 2 is not binary

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the VB.NET form hold?**
