# Building a Number Converter in VB.NET — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the number-conversion project do?**
**Answer: Takes a number in one system and returns it in another**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the conversions it covers**
**Answer: Decimal to binary, binary to hexadecimal, and their reverses (among the three systems)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the VB.NET form hold?**
**Answer: A place to type the number, a choice of systems (from, to), a convert action, and a place the answer appears**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must the converter refuse?**
**Answer: An invalid entry — a 'binary' number containing a 2 is not binary**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How does a program convert decimal to another base?**
**Answer: Repeated division, remainders read back**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does it convert another base to decimal?**
**Answer: Place values multiplied and added**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How do binary and hexadecimal trade?**
**Answer: By groups of four bits**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Against what is the finished converter tested?**
**Answer: Hand-worked conversions**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what second language is the project rebuilt?**
**Answer: PHP**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is a converter that cannot be checked?**
**Answer: A guess with a form around it**
