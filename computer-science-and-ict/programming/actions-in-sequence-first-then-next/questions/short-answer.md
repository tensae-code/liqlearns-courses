# Actions in Sequence — First, Then, Next — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Give the sequence for brushing your teeth in four actions.**
**Answer:** For example: first take the brush, then put on toothpaste, then brush, last rinse (any true order with order words).

**2. Why does order matter in a sequence?**
**Answer:** Because many actions depend on the one before; the wrong order or a missing step breaks the result.
