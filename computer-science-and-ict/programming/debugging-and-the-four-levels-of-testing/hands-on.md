# Hands-on — Debugging and the Four Levels of Testing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 1 §1.7 Software Debugging and Testing (§1.7.1 debugging — debuggers, print statements, code reviews; §1.7.2 testing — unit, integration, system, acceptance/UAT) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach debugging and the four levels of testing to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
