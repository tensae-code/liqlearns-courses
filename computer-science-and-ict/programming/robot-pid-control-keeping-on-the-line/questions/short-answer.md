# Robot PID Control — Keeping on the Line — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does PID stand for?**
**Answer: Proportional, Integral, Derivative**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Proportional correct by?**
**Answer: The error now**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does Integral correct by?**
**Answer: The accumulated error over time — ending a long lean**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does Derivative correct by?**
**Answer: The error's rate of change — meeting a fast-growing error early**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three tenses of error.**
**Answer: Now, accumulated, and coming**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the error on the line?**
**Answer: The light reading's distance from the line's edge value**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does I fix that P tolerates?**
**Answer: The stubborn offset of a curved or tilted run**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does D damp?**
**Answer: The zigzag caused by eager correction**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is tuning three constants the point of Grade 9?**
**Answer: The robot is kept by the same controller family real machines use — genuine control theory**
