# Hands-on — The History of C and What a Program Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Chapter 8 §8.1 Overview (a computer program; the history of C — Dennis Ritchie 1972, from B by Ken Thompson, K&R C, ANSI C; C for UNIX system programs) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach the history of c and what a program is to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
