# Modelling Exponential Spread

## Do it first

One person tells two, each of them tells two more. Grade 9's modelling lesson shows why that innocent sentence outruns every hall, school and city — and how a program lets you watch it happen before it does.

## Sub lesson 1 — Exponential growth — multiplying by the same factor

A spread is **exponential** when each step **multiplies** the count by the same factor: 1, 2, 4, 8… when each holder passes it to **two** new people; the count after n steps is the starting count times the factor n times over. Its character is the lesson's shock: the growth looks sleepy for many steps and then stands enormous almost at once — the second half of the chessboard — because **the new spreaders are made by the spreading itself**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modelling it — a program that watches the spread

A **model** of the spread is a program holding the model's levers — **start count, factor per step, steps** — and **computing the table** the hand cannot extend: thirty steps, fifty steps, the step where the count passes the school, the city, the country. Changing a lever and re-running is the modelling craft: what if each tells **three**? what if only **half** pass it on? The lesson's point is civic as much as mathematical — exponential spread is how messages and infections truly move, and the person who has run the model is not surprised by the curve.

## Do It Yourself

1. Show it, don't just say it: Each step multiplies the count by the same factor. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
2. Show it, don't just say it: Two — each holder passes it to two new people. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
3. Show it, don't just say it: The new spreaders are made by the spreading itself. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
4. Show it, don't just say it: The starting count times the factor, n times over. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
