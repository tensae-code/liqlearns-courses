# Modelling Exponential Spread — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a spread exponential?**
**Answer: Each step multiplies the count by the same factor**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In the tells-two model, what is the factor?**
**Answer: Two — each holder passes it to two new people**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does it explode late?**
**Answer: The new spreaders are made by the spreading itself**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the count after n steps?**
**Answer: The starting count times the factor, n times over**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 9 Pliroforiki C, Unit 7**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the model's three levers.**
**Answer: Start count, factor per step, steps**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the program give the hand cannot?**
**Answer: The long table — thirty, fifty steps, the step it passes the school and the city**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the modelling craft?**
**Answer: Changing a lever and re-running — what if three? what if half pass it on?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name two things that truly spread this way.**
**Answer: Messages and infections**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who is not surprised by the curve?**
**Answer: The person who has run the model**
