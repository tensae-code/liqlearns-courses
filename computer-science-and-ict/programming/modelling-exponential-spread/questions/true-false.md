# Modelling Exponential Spread — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Each step multiplies the count by the same factor**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The new spreaders are made by the spreading itself** — as the answer to: In the tells-two model, what is the factor?
**Answer: False — Two — each holder passes it to two new people**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The new spreaders are made by the spreading itself**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Greece Grade 9 Pliroforiki C, Unit 7** — as the answer to: What is the count after n steps?
**Answer: False — The starting count times the factor, n times over**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Greece Grade 9 Pliroforiki C, Unit 7**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The long table — thirty, fifty steps, the step it passes the school and the city** — as the answer to: Name the model's three levers.

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The long table — thirty, fifty steps, the step it passes the school and the city**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Messages and infections** — as the answer to: What is the modelling craft?

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Messages and infections**
