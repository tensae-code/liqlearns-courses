# HTML Frames — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a frameset document hold?**
**Answer: No page content — a plan dividing the window into a grid**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do cols and rows declare?**
**Answer: The grid's columns by width, or its rows by height**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How can a division's size be given?**
**Answer: In pixels, percents, or a star share of what remains**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What fills each cell of the grid?**
**Answer: A FRAME element naming the page to show there**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How many pages does the server send for one framed window?**
**Answer: Several pages plus the frameset plan**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What problem did frames solve?**
**Answer: A menu frame could stay fixed while the content frame changed**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name a cost of frames.**
**Answer: One address for many pages — bookmarking and printing an inner page fight the design**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What replaced frames in later practice?**
**Answer: Page layouts (with styles)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book still teach frames?**
**Answer: To understand how the early web was assembled and read old sites without mystery**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which language and book teach this?**
**Answer: HTML, in Mongolia's Grade 11 IT book**
