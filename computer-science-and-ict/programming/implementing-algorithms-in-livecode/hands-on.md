# Hands-on — Implementing Algorithms in LiveCode

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Implement one algorithm', 'Take a short algorithm you know (average of three marks): implement it in LiveCode shape — name the card, the fields, the button and its script in near-English lines.')

## Task two
('Test against the hand answer', "Work your algorithm by hand for two sets of numbers, then trace your LiveCode design over the same numbers: the program must agree with the hand answers, step for step.")
