# Implementing Algorithms in LiveCode — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a LiveCode program built as?**
**Answer: A stack of cards carrying controls**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two controls.**
**Answer: Buttons and fields**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does every control hold?**
**Answer: A script — code answering its events**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is LiveCode's language like?**
**Answer: Near-English commands**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What stands behind the cards?**
**Answer: The database created earlier in SQLite**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does implementing mean?**
**Answer: Translating an algorithm's steps into the tool's objects and scripts**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does an input step become?**
**Answer: A field**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What becomes of a decision step?**
**Answer: A script that tests**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Against what is the program tested?**
**Answer: Cases whose answers are known from the algorithm worked by hand**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is debugged first when answers differ?**
**Answer: The implementation, not the algorithm**
