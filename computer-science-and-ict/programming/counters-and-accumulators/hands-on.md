# Hands-on — Counters and Accumulators

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Count and total by hand', 'Run a counter and an accumulator on paper through a list of ten marks: count them with the counter, total them with the accumulator, then give the average — showing every update, one line per value.')

## Task two
('Which one failed?', "A program reports an average of 0 for real marks: say which of the pair most likely was never started, and what its starting value should have been.")
