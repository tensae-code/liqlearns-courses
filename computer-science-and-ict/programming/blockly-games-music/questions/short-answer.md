# Blockly Games — Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is Blockly Games?**
**Answer: A set of programming games in the browser, programmed with blocks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the Music activity make?**
**Answer: Programs you hear — blocks name notes, and running the program plays them**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book place Music after Puzzle and Maze?**
**Answer: Music exposes program structure to the ear — a wrong block is a wrong sound, heard at once**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a melody, in program terms?**
**Answer: A sequence — note after note, the sequence structure**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) A repeated phrase in music is what, in programming?**
**Answer: A loop with a tune inside it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) A returning chorus is what?**
**Answer: A block reused**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Note lengths and rests are the program's what?**
**Answer: Its data — timing**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is a loop that runs once too many detected here?**
**Answer: It is heard, not read**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do the three Blockly worlds prove?**
**Answer: The structures — sequence, repetition, choice — are the subject; the worlds are costumes**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 8, Bab 3 D — exploring Blockly Games Music**
