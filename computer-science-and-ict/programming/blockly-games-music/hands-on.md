# Hands-on — Blockly Games — Music

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Compose a loop** — Plan a short tune on paper: a two-bar phrase, repeated, with a different ending the second time. Write it as blocks-in-words — sequence, loop count, and the changed ending.

## Task two
**Hear the bug** — A classmate's tune plan repeats its phrase three times instead of two and drops the final rest. Write what a listener hears, and the one-block fix.
