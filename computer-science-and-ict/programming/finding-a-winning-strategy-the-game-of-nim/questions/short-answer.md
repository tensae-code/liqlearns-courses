# Finding a Winning Strategy — The Game of Nim — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) From where does the analysis start?**
**Answer: From the end — backwards**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why is 4 a losing position (take 1–3, last wins)?**
**Answer: Whatever you take, the opponent takes the rest**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which positions are losing in this Nim?**
**Answer: Every multiple of 4**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does the opponent answer a take of k?**
**Answer: By taking 4−k, restoring the multiple of 4**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Into what do positions sort?**
**Answer: Winning and losing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a winning strategy, as conduct?**
**Answer: Always leave a losing position; from anywhere else, move to one**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Nim return in Grade 8?**
**Answer: As a video-game project in the studios, in expert mode**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is a solved game?**
**Answer: One understood completely — not just played better**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the solving method?**
**Answer: Work backwards from the end**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Whose algorithm is the unbeatable opponent?**
**Answer: The winning strategy, coded**
