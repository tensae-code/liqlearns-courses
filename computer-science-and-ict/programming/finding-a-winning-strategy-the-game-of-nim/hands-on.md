# Hands-on — Finding a Winning Strategy — The Game of Nim

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Solve your Nim** — With 15 matches and takes of 1–3, map winning/losing positions to 15 on paper, then play as the perfect strategist against three challengers. Keep score of the strategy, not the player.

## Task two
**Change the game** — Now taking the last match LOSES. Re-solve by backward analysis. Present the new losing positions and the first move from 15.
