# Prototyping and Rapid Application Development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a prototype?**
a) A quick, rough, working version of a system, built so users can try it and react  b) Users who cannot describe what they want can say it at once from a prototype  c) The version is shown, corrected and shown again  d) The clearest possible requirements statement for a full build

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why build one?**
a) The version is shown, corrected and shown again  b) Users who cannot describe what they want can say it at once from a prototype  c) The clearest possible requirements statement for a full build  d) Reaction is easier than imagination
**Answer: b) Users who cannot describe what they want can say it at once from a prototype**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens in prototype rounds?**
