# Prototyping and Rapid Application Development — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a prototype?**
**Answer: A quick, rough, working version of a system, built so users can try it and react**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why build one?**
**Answer: Users who cannot describe what they want can say it at once from a prototype**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens in prototype rounds?**
**Answer: The version is shown, corrected and shown again**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What else can a prototype become?**
**Answer: The clearest possible requirements statement for a full build**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is Fiji's insight about prototyping?**
**Answer: Reaction is easier than imagination**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is RAD?**
**Answer: Rapid Application Development — building the whole system by short prototype cycles with users**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Who works together in RAD?**
**Answer: Users and builders, in short cycles**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What are RAD's gains?**
**Answer: Speed and fit — a system its users helped shape**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is RAD's risk?**
**Answer: Wandering — a loosely run build ends half-documented and hard to maintain**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When does Fiji choose the full life cycle instead?**
**Answer: For systems that must be right — where being right matters more than learning fast**
