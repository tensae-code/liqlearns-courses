# Turtle Graphics in Small Basic

## Do it first

The GraphicsWindow draws by coordinates; the Turtle draws by walking. Mongolia's Grade 11 book teaches both: a small turtle on the canvas, carrying a pen, whose path is the picture. This lesson walks with it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Turtle's four acts — move, turn, pen up, pen down

The **Turtle** is a drawing agent: it stands on the canvas facing a direction, and obeys four first commands. **Move** walks it forward a given length. **Turn** rotates it by a given angle. **PenDown** puts its pen to the canvas so the walk draws; **PenUp** lifts the pen so the turtle can travel without marking. Every turtle picture is a journey told in these acts: move and turn, pen down for the drawing parts, pen up for the travelling parts.

In your mind's eye: the Turtle's four acts — move, turn, pen up, pen down.

## Sub lesson 2 — Polygons, spirals and the ulzii knot

Repetition turns the walk into geometry: move, turn 90, four times — a square; the same with other angles — **polygons** of any side count, the turn angle being 360 divided by the sides. Grow the step each round and the polygon winds into a **spiral**. The book's crown is Mongolian: the **ulzii**, the endless knot, drawn by a planned turtle path — the nation's symbol of eternity, produced by the same four acts as the square. Turtle graphics teach the deepest habit in the book's programming: think like the agent — stand where it stands, face where it faces.
