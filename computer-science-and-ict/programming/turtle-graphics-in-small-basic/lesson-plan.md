## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Turtle's four acts — move, turn, pen up, pen down** — The **Turtle** is a drawing agent: it stands on the canvas facing a direction, and obeys four first commands. **Move** walks it forward a given length. **Turn** rotates it by a given angle. **PenDown** puts its pen to the canvas so the walk draws; **PenUp** lifts the pen so the turtle can travel without marking. Every turtle picture is a journey told in these acts: move and turn, pen down for the drawing parts, pen up for the travelling parts.
2. **Polygons, spirals and the ulzii knot** — Repetition turns the walk into geometry: move, turn 90, four times — a square; the same with other angles — **polygons** of any side count, the turn angle being 360 divided by the sides. Grow the step each round and the polygon winds into a **spiral**. The book's crown is Mongolian: the **ulzii**, the endless knot, drawn by a planned turtle path — the nation's symbol of eternity, produced by the same four acts as the square. Turtle graphics teach the deepest habit in the book's programming: think like the agent — stand where it stands, face where it faces.
