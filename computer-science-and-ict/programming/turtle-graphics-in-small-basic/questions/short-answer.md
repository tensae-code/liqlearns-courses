# Turtle Graphics in Small Basic — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Turtle?**
**Answer: A drawing agent that walks on the canvas carrying a pen**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name its four first commands.**
**Answer: Move, Turn, PenUp and PenDown**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does PenUp do?**
**Answer: Lifts the pen so the turtle travels without marking**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is every turtle picture, in the lesson's phrase?**
**Answer: A journey told in moves, turns and pen acts**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a square drawn?**
**Answer: Move, turn 90 — four times**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the turn angle for a polygon?**
**Answer: 360 divided by the number of sides**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is a spiral made?**
**Answer: Grow the step each round of a polygon walk**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the ulzii?**
**Answer: The endless knot — Mongolia's symbol of eternity**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) By what acts is the ulzii drawn?**
**Answer: The same four — move, turn, pen up, pen down**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What habit does turtle graphics teach?**
**Answer: Think like the agent — stand where it stands, face where it faces**
