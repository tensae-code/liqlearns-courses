# Turtle Graphics in Small Basic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Turtle?**
a) A drawing agent that walks on the canvas carrying a pen  b) Move, Turn, PenUp and PenDown  c) Lifts the pen so the turtle travels without marking  d) A journey told in moves, turns and pen acts
**Answer: a) A drawing agent that walks on the canvas carrying a pen**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name its four first commands.**
a) Lifts the pen so the turtle travels without marking  b) Move, Turn, PenUp and PenDown  c) A journey told in moves, turns and pen acts  d) Move, turn 90 — four times
**Answer: b) Move, Turn, PenUp and PenDown**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does PenUp do?**
a) A journey told in moves, turns and pen acts  b) Move, turn 90 — four times  c) Lifts the pen so the turtle travels without marking  d) 360 divided by the number of sides
**Answer: c) Lifts the pen so the turtle travels without marking**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is every turtle picture, in the lesson's phrase?**
a) Move, turn 90 — four times  b) 360 divided by the number of sides  c) Grow the step each round of a polygon walk  d) A journey told in moves, turns and pen acts
**Answer: d) A journey told in moves, turns and pen acts**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a square drawn?**
a) Move, turn 90 — four times  b) 360 divided by the number of sides  c) Grow the step each round of a polygon walk  d) The endless knot — Mongolia's symbol of eternity
**Answer: a) Move, turn 90 — four times**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the turn angle for a polygon?**
a) Grow the step each round of a polygon walk  b) 360 divided by the number of sides  c) The endless knot — Mongolia's symbol of eternity  d) The same four — move, turn, pen up, pen down
**Answer: b) 360 divided by the number of sides**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is a spiral made?**
a) The endless knot — Mongolia's symbol of eternity  b) The same four — move, turn, pen up, pen down  c) Grow the step each round of a polygon walk  d) Think like the agent — stand where it stands, face where it faces
**Answer: c) Grow the step each round of a polygon walk**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the ulzii?**
a) The same four — move, turn, pen up, pen down  b) Think like the agent — stand where it stands, face where it faces  c) A drawing agent that walks on the canvas carrying a pen  d) The endless knot — Mongolia's symbol of eternity
**Answer: d) The endless knot — Mongolia's symbol of eternity**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) By what acts is the ulzii drawn?**
a) The same four — move, turn, pen up, pen down  b) Think like the agent — stand where it stands, face where it faces  c) A drawing agent that walks on the canvas carrying a pen  d) Move, Turn, PenUp and PenDown
**Answer: a) The same four — move, turn, pen up, pen down**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What habit does turtle graphics teach?**
a) A drawing agent that walks on the canvas carrying a pen  b) Think like the agent — stand where it stands, face where it faces  c) Move, Turn, PenUp and PenDown  d) Lifts the pen so the turtle travels without marking
