# Say and Think Blocks — Sprite Speech — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A say block shows the sprite's words in —**
a) a speech bubble  b) a thought cloud  c) the backdrop
**Answer: a) a speech bubble**

**2. A think block is right for —**
a) shouting across the stage  b) deleting text  c) silent thoughts only the audience reads
**Answer: c) silent thoughts only the audience reads**

**3. The words inside a say block —**
a) come only from the library  b) are typed and can be changed by you  c) can never be changed
**Answer: b) are typed and can be changed by you**

**4. Choosing say or think is —**
a) a storytelling decision  b) a colour decision  c) a saving decision
**Answer: a) a storytelling decision**
