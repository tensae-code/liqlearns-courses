# Say and Think Blocks — Sprite Speech — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Explain, in your own words: make a sprite say a typed sentence in a speech bubble.**
**Answer:** The learner's own explanation, true to the lesson: Say blocks give a sprite words in a speech bubble, think blocks give it silent thoughts, and typed text goes inside the block: the hero speaks, thinks, and takes in text you type.

**2. A classmate gets this lesson's work wrong. What is the most likely fault, and how is it fixed?**
**Answer:** Words appear in the wrong bubble → speech and thought use different blocks: check the bubble's shape in the block you picked — round-edged for speech, cloud-like for thought.
