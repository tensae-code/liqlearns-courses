# Hands-on — Variations of the Rectangle Method

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Average and bound** — For ∫₀¹ x² dx with n = 4, compute left and right sums, their average, the bracket and the average's error against 1/3.

## Task two
**Pace reading** — Compute the midpoint sum for the same integral at n = 2 and n = 4, estimate the error's shrink factor, and predict n = 8's error.
