This lesson: **Loops — and the for loop over a sequence**.

First, do it. Run the opening program. Watch what it prints.

Then the idea, in order: A loop repeats a statement or group of statements — a fixed number of times, or until a condition is satisfied. Syntax: for, a temporary variable, in, a sequence, a colon, an indented block. Python's two loops: for and while.

Now you. Say which loop problems in your day are fixed-count and which are until-a-condition.

Say it to your companion: Tell your companion what a loop is, name Python's two loops, and walk the for skeleton part by part.
