# Hands-on — Loops — and the for loop over a sequence

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — every activity is done for real, in IDLE or on paper.

## Human loop
Line up five objects. Walk along them, touching each and saying its name — you are the temporary variable; the line is the sequence. Then do it as a fixed-count loop: exactly three touches.

## Loop spotter
Find three loops in real life (washing dishes, climbing stairs, dealing cards). For each, name what repeats, and whether it is fixed-count or until-a-condition.
