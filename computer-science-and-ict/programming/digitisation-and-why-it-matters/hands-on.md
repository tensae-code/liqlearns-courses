# Hands-on — Digitisation and Why It Matters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Digitise by hand
Take a simple drawing — a house outline on grid paper. Write down, square by square, a number for each square's colour (0 empty, 1 line, 2 roof). Hand only your numbers to a classmate: if they can redraw your house from the numbers, you have digitised it.

## Four powers hunt
Find one real example of each power in your week: something stored that would have faded, a perfect copy, a file sent far away, data a program processed (a search, a chart). Write one line each.
