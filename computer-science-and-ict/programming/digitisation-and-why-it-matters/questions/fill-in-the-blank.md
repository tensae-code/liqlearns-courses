# Digitisation and Why It Matters — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A computer holds everything, in the end, as two digits: 0 and ____.**
**Answer: 1** — Binary data is built from 0 and 1.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The ____ of a photocopy blurs, but a digital copy is exact.**
**Answer: copy** — Each generation of an analogue copy loses a little; digital copies do not.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Digitisation is the ____ through which the real world enters the computer.**
**Answer: doorway** — Algorithms, processing and AI all work on the far side of that door.
