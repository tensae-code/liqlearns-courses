# Digitisation and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer each question in a few sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A library keeps its only copy of an old book on paper. Give two reasons digitising it matters, in your own words.**
**Answer:** First, preservation and copying: the paper will fade and can burn, but the digital copy can be stored safely and copied exactly forever. Second, use: once digitised, the text can be searched, sent to readers anywhere, and processed by programs — the book becomes data the whole world can work with.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Explain to a younger student what happens when a song is digitised.**
**Answer:** The song is a smooth wave of sound. The recorder measures the wave's height again and again, thousands of times a second, and writes each measurement as binary numbers. The file of numbers is the digitised song — the player turns the numbers back into sound.
