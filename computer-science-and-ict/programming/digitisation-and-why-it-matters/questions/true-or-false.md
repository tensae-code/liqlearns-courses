# Digitisation and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A digital copy is slightly worse than the one before it, like a photocopy of a photocopy.**
**Answer: False** — Digital copies are exact — the numbers copy perfectly.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A computer holds sound as a smooth wave.**
**Answer: False** — It holds numbers measured from the wave — binary data.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Digitised data can be searched and sorted by programs in ways paper records cannot match.**
**Answer: True** — Processing is one of the four powers.
