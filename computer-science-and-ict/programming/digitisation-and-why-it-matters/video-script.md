# Video script — Digitisation and Why It Matters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The world is smooth. A voice rises and falls. Light shades slowly.
A computer cannot hold smooth. It holds numbers — 0 and 1.
Digitisation is the translation. The world becomes data.
Then the magic: data never fades. Copies are perfect.
Data flies anywhere. Programs can work on it.
First the world becomes data. Then everything else can happen.
