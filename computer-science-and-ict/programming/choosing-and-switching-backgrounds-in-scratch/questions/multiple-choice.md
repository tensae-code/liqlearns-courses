# Choosing and Switching Backgrounds in Scratch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where are backgrounds inserted from?**
a) The library  b) The stage the action happens on  c) Switching the background  d) It bounces
**Answer: a) The library**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the background, in the book’s words?**
a) The sprite and the story  b) The stage the action happens on  c) Wait blocks  d) Left-right rotation style
**Answer: b) The stage the action happens on**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must the background match?**
a) Switching the background  b) It bounces  c) The sprite and the story  d) No — it belongs to the stage
**Answer: c) The sprite and the story**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What carries the travelling parrot’s journey?**
a) Wait blocks  b) Left-right rotation style  c) A storytelling choice  d) Switching the background
**Answer: d) Switching the background**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What blocks pace the journey?**
a) Wait blocks  b) It bounces  c) No — it belongs to the stage  d) The stage switches its background
**Answer: a) Wait blocks**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to the parrot at the stage edge?**
a) Left-right rotation style  b) It bounces  c) A storytelling choice  d) The library
**Answer: b) It bounces**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What rotation style keeps the parrot upright?**
a) No — it belongs to the stage  b) The stage switches its background  c) Left-right rotation style  d) The stage the action happens on
**Answer: c) Left-right rotation style**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Is the background part of the sprite?**
a) A storytelling choice  b) The library  c) The sprite and the story  d) No — it belongs to the stage
**Answer: d) No — it belongs to the stage**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of choice is choosing a background?**
a) A storytelling choice  b) The stage switches its background  c) The stage the action happens on  d) Switching the background
**Answer: a) A storytelling choice**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does the action move from place to place?**
a) The library  b) The stage switches its background  c) The sprite and the story  d) Wait blocks
**Answer: b) The stage switches its background**
