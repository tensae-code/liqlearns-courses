# Video script — Choosing and Switching Backgrounds in Scratch

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Backgrounds are **inserted from the library**: the background is **the stage the action happens on**, so the choice is a storytelling choice — the book teaches **matching the background to the sprite**, a place that fits the character and the moment.
The travelling parrot carries the second half: the project **switches background** as the parrot travels, **wait blocks** pace the journey, and the parrot’s flight is finished with **bounce at the edge** and the **left-right rotation style** so it turns without flipping upside down.
