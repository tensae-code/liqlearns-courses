# Robotics — Champions Challenge

## Do it first

Every robotics unit in Kuwait's Digital Intelligence series ends the same way: a Champions Challenge. No new blocks, no guided steps — a free challenge in which the unit's whole teaching must be spent at once. This lesson gathers the four books' challenges into one: what a Champions Challenge is, and what each year's arena demands.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a Champions Challenge is

The **Champions Challenge** is the unit's **final free-space project**: a set arena and goal, and the pupil's own program — built from **everything learned** in the unit — to meet it. Its rules are the series' project rules at their strictest: no worked example to copy, because the challenge is precisely the part no one can do for you. Passing a unit's lessons shows the blocks were understood; the Challenge is where they must become judgement.

## Sub lesson 2 — The four arenas — Grades 6 to 9

Grade 6 closes in free space: a robot project using the whole virtual-robot unit. Grade 7's challenge is a **maze**, run on the Ultrasonic sensor with a barrier variable. Grade 8's is **line-following** — a program that follows the white path. Grade 9's is the **Sumo Battle Arena**, a wrestling robot fighting with its Colour and Ultrasonic sensors. The arenas grow as the sensors grow: each year's Challenge is passable only with that year's new senses — the series' way of making revision unavoidable and victory honest.
