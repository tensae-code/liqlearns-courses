# Robotics — Champions Challenge — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a Champions Challenge?**
**Answer: The robotics unit's final free project, using everything the unit taught**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In which grades is it set?**
**Answer: Grades 6, 7, 8 and 9 — at the end of every robotics unit**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is given, and what is not?**
**Answer: An arena and a goal are given; no worked example to copy**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the Challenge prove that lessons cannot?**
**Answer: That the blocks have become judgement**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is Grade 7's arena?**
**Answer: A maze, run on the Ultrasonic sensor with a barrier variable**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is Grade 8's arena?**
**Answer: Line-following — following the white path**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is Grade 9's arena?**
**Answer: The Sumo Battle Arena**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which sensors fight in the Sumo arena?**
**Answer: Colour and Ultrasonic**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does each year's Challenge need that year's senses?**
**Answer: So revision is unavoidable and victory honest**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which books set these challenges?**
**Answer: Kuwait Digital Intelligence Grades 6–9, Term 1, Unit 3 of each**
