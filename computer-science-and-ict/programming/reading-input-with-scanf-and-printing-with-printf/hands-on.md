# Hands-on — Reading Input With scanf and Printing With printf

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**The worked program** — Write the Celsius-to-Fahrenheit program complete: prompt, scanf, compute, printf. Test it on 0, 100 and −40 (where the scales meet).

## Task two
**Courtesy pass** — Take a bare program that prints three numbers. Rework its printf until a stranger understands every number without seeing the code.
