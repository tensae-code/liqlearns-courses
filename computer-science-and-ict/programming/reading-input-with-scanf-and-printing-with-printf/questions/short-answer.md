# Reading Input With scanf and Printing With printf — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does printf's format string carry?**
**Answer: Words, format specifiers and escape sequences**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does \n do?**
**Answer: Starts a new line**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does \t do?**
**Answer: Moves to a tab stop**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How do values match specifiers?**
**Answer: In order, one per specifier**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Whose courtesy is the output's form?**
**Answer: The programmer's**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does scanf need that printf does not?**
**Answer: The variable's address — the &**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why the address?**
**Answer: scanf must put the value somewhere**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the beginner's rite of passage?**
**Answer: Forgetting the &**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) State the Celsius-to-Fahrenheit formula.**
**Answer: F = (C × 9/5) + 32**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What template is the worked program?**
**Answer: Input, process, output — in C**
