# Hands-on — Evaluating Expressions — and Operator Precedence in Pascal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Evaluation ladder** — Evaluate 10 − 2 * 3, (10 − 2) * 3, 17 div 5 + 17 mod 5 * 2 and sqrt(16) + 2 * 3 step by step, naming the rule used at each step.

## Task two
**Bracket the meaning** — Write expressions for (a+b)/(c+d), a + b/c + d as printed in a textbook formula, and x² + y² using sqr — then evaluate all three for sample values.
