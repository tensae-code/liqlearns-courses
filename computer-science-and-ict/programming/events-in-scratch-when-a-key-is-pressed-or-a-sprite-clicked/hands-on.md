# Hands-on — Events in Scratch — When a Key Is Pressed or a Sprite Clicked

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Drive it
Build a sprite driven by all four arrow keys, one event script per key, and test every direction.

## Answer a click
Add a when-sprite-clicked script that makes the sprite say or do something, and explain why it needs the event block on top.
