# Systems Phase 3 — System Design — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does system design turn requirements into?**
**Answer: A complete plan of the new system**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the inside work design?**
**Answer: The data — files or tables, fields and codes — and the processes that work on it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What becomes of 'find stock in under a minute' at Viti?**
**Answer: An actual stock file with an actual item code — the promise given a mechanism**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why are design mistakes cheap?**
**Answer: Found here, they are still pencil mistakes**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does design answer?**
**Answer: How — how the system will do what analysis required**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the outside work design?**
**Answer: Inputs — entry forms and screens — and outputs — the screens and reports each user receives**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How should entry be laid out?**
**Answer: So that entry is hard to get wrong**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must outputs carry?**
**Answer: Exactly the information of the user's level — no more, no less**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How does the phase end?**
**Answer: The whole design checked against the requirements, one by one**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does design give Phase 4's builders?**
**Answer: A blueprint — so they never guess what they are building**
