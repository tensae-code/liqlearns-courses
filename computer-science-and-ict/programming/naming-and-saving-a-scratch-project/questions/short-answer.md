# Naming and Saving a Scratch Project — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does saving a project do?**
**Answer: Writes it to a file on the computer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where are projects kept?**
**Answer: In their own folder — one home for all Scratch work**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why keep projects in their own folder?**
**Answer: So they never scatter among pictures and documents**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When do you save?**
**Answer: When the project is made, and again as it grows**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the saved file called in this lesson?**
**Answer: The project's memory**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of name does a project take?**
**Answer: A name that describes it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are the guide's two example names?**
**Answer: Space and Football**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a describing name let future-you do?**
**Answer: Pick the project from the folder without opening everything**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the whole system, in one line?**
**Answer: A describing name plus its own folder**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to unsaved work?**
**Answer: It is lost**
