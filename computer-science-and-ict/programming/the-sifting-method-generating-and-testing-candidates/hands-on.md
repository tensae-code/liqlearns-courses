# Hands-on — The Sifting Method — Generating and Testing Candidates

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Sift a small space** — List all three-digit numbers with distinct digits summing to 10, by a written generate-and-test plan, then count how many candidates were tested.

## Task two
**Prune it** — Redesign the same task with partial pruning (fix the first digit, test the sum's possibility) and count the candidates saved.
