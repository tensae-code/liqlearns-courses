# The Sifting Method — Generating and Testing Candidates — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are sifting's two halves?**
**Answer: Generate every candidate; test each against all the conditions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is sifting's guarantee?**
**Answer: Total — if a solution exists among the candidates, sifting finds it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why can nothing be missed?**
**Answer: Because nothing was skipped — the generation is exhaustive**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Give a textbook example.**
**Answer: Numbers with given digits and properties (also: arrangements under constraints, triples satisfying an equation)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a candidate?**
**Answer: One complete possible answer the problem's space allows**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is sifting's price?**
**Answer: The size of its candidate space — factorial/exponential growth**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is pruning?**
**Answer: Testing partial candidates early and abandoning a failing family at once**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What method grows from pruning?**
**Answer: Backtracking — generating with early rejection**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is sifting's second office?**
**Answer: The measure — clever methods must match its answers on small cases while beating its time**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is sifting called the examiner that never lies?**
**Answer: Its answers are exhaustive — completeness needs no trust**
