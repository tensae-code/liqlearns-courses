# The Sifting Method — Generating and Testing Candidates — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are sifting's two halves?** → **Generate every candidate; test each against all the conditions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is sifting's guarantee?** → **Total — if a solution exists among the candidates, sifting finds it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why can nothing be missed?** → **Because nothing was skipped — the generation is exhaustive**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Give a textbook example.** → **Numbers with given digits and properties (also: arrangements under constraints, triples satisfying an equation)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a candidate?** → **One complete possible answer the problem's space allows**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is sifting's price?** → **The size of its candidate space — factorial/exponential growth**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is pruning?** → **Testing partial candidates early and abandoning a failing family at once**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What method grows from pruning?** → **Backtracking — generating with early rejection**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is sifting's second office?** → **The measure — clever methods must match its answers on small cases while beating its time**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is sifting called the examiner that never lies?** → **Its answers are exhaustive — completeness needs no trust**
