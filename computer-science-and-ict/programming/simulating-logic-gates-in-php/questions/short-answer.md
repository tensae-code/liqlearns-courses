# Simulating Logic Gates in PHP — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How is the PHP simulation marked in Egypt's book?**
**Answer: As enrichment — the stretch version of the VB.NET project**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the inputs in the PHP version?**
**Answer: A form — the visitor chooses the values and submits**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the PHP logic run?**
**Answer: On the server, which generates the output page**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the lamp in the PHP version?**
**Answer: A drawn or written state in the returned HTML page**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the big mechanical difference from VB.NET?**
**Answer: The round trip — inputs are sent away and a rebuilt page returns, instead of instant reaction on one machine**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does not change between the two builds?**
**Answer: The truth tables and the gates' logic**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What changes around the logic?**
**Answer: Controls become form fields, reaction becomes request and response, the lamp becomes generated HTML**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What transferable thing does the double build teach?**
**Answer: The gate itself — learned separately from the perishable tool**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which Grade 11 rhythm does the PHP simulation reuse?**
**Answer: The dynamic-page rhythm — request, processing, generated page**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) For whom are the PHP builds intended?**
**Answer: Those ready to watch the logic survive a change of world — enrichment, beyond the core builds**
