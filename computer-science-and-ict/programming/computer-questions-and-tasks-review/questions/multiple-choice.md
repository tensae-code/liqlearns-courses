# Computer — Questions and Tasks Review — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which task belongs to this review?**
a) Formatting a short text in WordPad  b) Sorting information by senses  c) Grouping toys by colour
**Answer: a) Formatting a short text in WordPad** — WordPad formatting was a Unit 4 lesson.

**2. A program opens on the desktop in —**
a) a window  b) the mouse  c) a rule
**Answer: a) a window** — Each program's work happens in its window.

**3. In Calculator, before a new calculation you —**
a) clear the old one  b) add more numbers  c) close the school
**Answer: a) clear the old one** — Clearing stops old numbers mixing in.

**4. Text inside a Paint picture is added with —**
a) the text tool  b) the calculator  c) the title bar
**Answer: a) the text tool** — The letter-marked tool, then click and type.
