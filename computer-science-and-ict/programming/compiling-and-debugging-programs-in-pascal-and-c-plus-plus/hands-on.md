# Hands-on — Compiling and Debugging Programs in Pascal and C++

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Compiler reading** — Take three real compiler error messages (from the annexes) and translate each into a plain sentence: line, fault, and the fix.

## Task two
**Bug hunt plan** — For a program that prints the average of marks but answers 0 for every class, write the debugging plan: smallest failing input, where you would breakpoint, what value you expect at each step.
