# Hands-on — Algorithms in Everyday Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Write the morning algorithm** — Write your getting-ready algorithm in numbered steps. Give it to a partner to follow literally — they may do ONLY what is written. Fix every step where they get stuck.

## Task two
**Spot three** — Find three algorithms in one day at home (cooking, cleaning, a game). For each, name its steps' order rule and its stopping rule.
