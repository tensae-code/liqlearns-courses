# Algorithms in Everyday Life — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an algorithm?**
**Answer: A clear, ordered set of steps that finishes a task**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why is a recipe an algorithm?**
**Answer: It gives ordered steps that finish a task — making the dish**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give an everyday order that must not be swapped.**
**Answer: Socks before shoes — some steps only work in their order**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Does an algorithm need a computer?**
**Answer: No — people follow and invent algorithms every day**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In Armenia's Grade 5 book, where does this lesson sit?**
**Answer: Right after the concept of an algorithm (§19, after §18)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What makes an everyday algorithm followable?**
**Answer: Each step is one clear action, in working order, that another person can follow without asking questions**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must every algorithm do at the end?**
**Answer: End — it must not loop forever**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do you test an everyday algorithm?**
**Answer: Follow it literally, exactly as written, and find the step where the follower gets stuck**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a stopping rule?**
**Answer: The condition that ends a repeated step — for example 'stir until the sugar has dissolved'**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do you fix when a follower gets stuck?**
**Answer: The unclear or missing step — not the follower**
