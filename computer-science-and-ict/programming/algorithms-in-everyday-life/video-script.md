# Video script — Algorithms in Everyday Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An **algorithm** is a clear, ordered set of steps that finishes a task. A **recipe** is an algorithm for cooking; a **route** to school is an algorithm for travelling; getting dressed in the right order (socks before shoes) is an algorithm too. Armenia's Grade 5 book teaches 'Algorithms in everyday life' as its own lesson (§19) right after the concept of an algorithm: the point is that the steps were always there — the lesson names them.
An everyday algorithm is good when another person can follow it **without asking questions**: each step is one clear action, the steps are in working order, and the algorithm **ends** — it does not loop forever (stir the tea, taste, add sugar, taste… needs a stopping rule). Testing an everyday algorithm means following it literally, exactly as written, and fixing the step where the literal follower gets stuck.
