# Project — Invent Your Own Minecraft Coding Puzzles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the final Grade 5 lesson ask pupils to do?**
a) Invent new missions and puzzles  b) Everything learned in the book  c) On squared paper  d) They are exchanged with a classmate
**Answer: a) Invent new missions and puzzles**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must the invented puzzles use?**
a) It can be solved and its goal is clear  b) Everything learned in the book  c) Commands, sequences, repeat blocks, nested loops and conditionals  d) A classmate
**Answer: b) Everything learned in the book**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What makes an invented puzzle fair?**
a) On squared paper  b) They are exchanged with a classmate  c) It can be solved and its goal is clear  d) The success screen
**Answer: c) It can be solved and its goal is clear**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does an invented puzzle’s plan fit first?**
a) Commands, sequences, repeat blocks, nested loops and conditionals  b) A classmate  c) The flaw in the puzzle  d) On squared paper
**Answer: d) On squared paper**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What tools can an invented puzzle use?**
a) Commands, sequences, repeat blocks, nested loops and conditionals  b) They are exchanged with a classmate  c) The success screen  d) Thinking about the solver and the clarity of the rules
**Answer: a) Commands, sequences, repeat blocks, nested loops and conditionals**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to the invented puzzles next?**
a) A classmate  b) They are exchanged with a classmate  c) The flaw in the puzzle  d) Invent new missions and puzzles
**Answer: b) They are exchanged with a classmate**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Who solves your invented task?**
a) The success screen  b) Thinking about the solver and the clarity of the rules  c) A classmate  d) Everything learned in the book
**Answer: c) A classmate**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the solver meet if the puzzle is sound?**
a) The flaw in the puzzle  b) Invent new missions and puzzles  c) It can be solved and its goal is clear  d) The success screen
**Answer: d) The success screen**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What can the solver find if the puzzle is flawed?**
a) The flaw in the puzzle  b) Thinking about the solver and the clarity of the rules  c) Everything learned in the book  d) On squared paper
**Answer: a) The flaw in the puzzle**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does inventing teach that solving alone cannot?**
a) Invent new missions and puzzles  b) Thinking about the solver and the clarity of the rules  c) It can be solved and its goal is clear  d) Commands, sequences, repeat blocks, nested loops and conditionals
**Answer: b) Thinking about the solver and the clarity of the rules**
