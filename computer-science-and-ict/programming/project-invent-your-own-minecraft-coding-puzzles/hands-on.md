# Hands-on — Project — Invent Your Own Minecraft Coding Puzzles

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Invent a mission
Invent a Minecraft mission using at least one loop and one conditional. Plan it on squared paper and test that it can be solved.

## Solve a classmate’s puzzle
Exchange your puzzle with a classmate, solve theirs, and write one sentence on what made it clear or unclear.
