# Hands-on — Practical C Programs Pay Distance and Banknotes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Do the book's steps** — Work through Practical Answers 1–4 — employee ID and salary; fuel consumption and distance between two points; breaking an amount into the smallest number of bank notes exactly as South Sudan's book orders it, on a real computer if one is available; otherwise write the steps in order and check them against the book's order.

## Task two
**Show it to someone** — Take Doing it — Practical Answers 1–4 — employee ID and salary; fuel consumption and distance between two points; breaking an amount into the smallest number of bank notes in practice and show a partner what it does: do it once slowly, naming each step, then let the partner do it while you only watch and correct.
