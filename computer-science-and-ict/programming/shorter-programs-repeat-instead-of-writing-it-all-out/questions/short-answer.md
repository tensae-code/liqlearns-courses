# Shorter Programs — Repeat Instead of Writing It All Out — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What begs to fold?**
**Answer: A passage that repeats the same blocks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three steps fold it?**
**Answer: Find the repeated group, count it, lift it into a repeat block with its count**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Is the folded program different?**
**Answer: No — the behaviour is exactly the same**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is shortening, in one word?**
**Answer: Refactoring in miniature**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which two environments teach it?**
**Answer: ScratchJr (3e–4e) and Scratch (Grade 7)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three gains of shorter programs.**
**Answer: Read at a glance; repaired in one place; extended by changing a number**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When does folding not pay?**
**Answer: When the ‘repetition’ is not real — different blocks in similar shapes**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What eye is trained here?**
**Answer: Spot repetition, verify sameness, fold**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What later tools does that eye find?**
**Answer: Loops, sub-programs and patterns everywhere**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is a repair cheaper after folding?**
**Answer: Fix the group once, not twelve times**
