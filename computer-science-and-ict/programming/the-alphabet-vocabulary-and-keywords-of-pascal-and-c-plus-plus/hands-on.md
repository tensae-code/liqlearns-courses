# Hands-on — The Alphabet, Vocabulary and Keywords of Pascal and C++

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Alphabet audit** — Take a page of Pascal and a page of C++ and list every special symbol used, marking those only one language has.

## Task two
**Keyword hunt** — In a given program text, underline keywords in one colour and programmer identifiers in another, in both languages.
