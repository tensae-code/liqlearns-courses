# The Alphabet, Vocabulary and Keywords of Pascal and C++ — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a language's alphabet?**
**Answer: The set of characters its texts may use — letters, digits and special symbols**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name symbols C++ has that Pascal lacks.**
**Answer: #, %, &, |, ~ — for C++'s own operations**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What divides symbols into words?**
**Answer: Separators and blanks**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) May a character outside the alphabet appear?**
**Answer: No — it is the compiler's first and simplest error**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which alphabet do the two languages mostly share?**
**Answer: The Latin letters, the digits, and the common special symbols**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are keywords?**
**Answer: Reserved words with a fixed meaning in the language, unusable as identifiers**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name three Pascal keywords.**
**Answer: program, begin, end (also: var, if, while)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name three C++ keywords.**
**Answer: int, return, if (also: while, class)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How many keywords has a language?**
**Answer: Dozens, not thousands**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the pair's first practical difference?**
**Answer: Case — Pascal ignores it in keywords; C++ distinguishes Main from main**
