# Hands-on — First Sequences — Putting Steps in Order

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Act the sequence
Write three steps for a classmate to act out. They must do them exactly in order, no more and no less. Then swap two steps and watch what changes.

## Steps to a goal
Draw a small grid path from a start to a star. Write the sequence of moves under it, then trade with a partner and follow theirs exactly.
