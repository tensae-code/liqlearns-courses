# First Sequences — Putting Steps in Order — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Explain, in your own words: build a sequence of steps that moves a character to a goal.**
**Answer:** The learner's own explanation, true to the lesson: A sequence is actions in an order: do the steps one after another, in the order they are written, and the order itself decides what happens.

**2. A classmate gets this lesson's work wrong. What is the most likely fault, and how is it fixed?**
**Answer:** Character ends in the wrong place → do not add steps first: act the sequence out with your own body, one step at a time, and find the step that is out of order.
