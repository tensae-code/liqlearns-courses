# Hands-on — Initiating Pascal and C++ — Two Languages, One Course

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Side-by-side reading** — Take the book's first-program pair and draw matching boxes around corresponding parts in both texts, labelling each part's job.

## Task two
**Translation drill** — Translate a five-instruction Pascal fragment into C++ and one back, keeping a two-column glossary of the words that changed.
