# Initiating Pascal and C++ — Two Languages, One Course — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which language is the teaching language, and why?**
**Answer: Pascal — designed for learning, readable, strict about structure**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which is the technology language?**
**Answer: C++ — the language of real systems and careers**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How are they taught?**
**Answer: In parallel — each concept given in both languages in the same section**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the parallel teaching prove?**
**Answer: That the concept is one — syntax is only its clothing**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What may a school choose?**
**Answer: One language or both — the book supports either**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name Pascal's frame for a program.**
**Answer: program, declarations, begin…end. — closing with a full stop**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name C++'s frame.**
**Answer: #include, main, braces { }, semicolons, return 0**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the pupil's first skill in the dual course?**
**Answer: Reading across — recognising the same parts under both spellings**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does not change between the two first programs?**
**Answer: Their parts — input, processing, output**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is every later chapter written?**
**Answer: In the pair's double column — both languages throughout**
