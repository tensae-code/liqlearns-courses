# Hands-on — Programming Number Sequences — Arithmetic, Geometric and Fibonacci

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Generate both ways
Generate ten terms of an arithmetic sequence (start 3, difference 4) and a geometric one (start 3, ratio 2) into lists, and check them by hand.

## Fibonacci to twenty
Generate the Fibonacci sequence until a term passes 100, and verify each term against the two before it.
