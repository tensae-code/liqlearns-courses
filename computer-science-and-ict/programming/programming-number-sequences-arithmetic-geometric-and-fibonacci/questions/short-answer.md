# Programming Number Sequences — Arithmetic, Geometric and Fibonacci — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does an arithmetic sequence grow?**
**Answer: By adding the same difference each step**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does a geometric sequence grow?**
**Answer: By multiplying by the same ratio**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What generates the sequences?**
**Answer: A loop with the rule inside it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where are the terms generated into?**
**Answer: A list**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Does the programmer type the terms?**
**Answer: No — the loop generates them**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the Fibonacci rule?**
**Answer: Each term is the sum of the two before it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are the first six Fibonacci terms?**
**Answer: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) From whom did Fibonacci learn his mathematics?**
**Answer: From Arab scholars**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What did Fibonacci carry to Europe?**
**Answer: The sequence, and much else**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What differs between the three sequences?**
**Answer: The generating rule**
