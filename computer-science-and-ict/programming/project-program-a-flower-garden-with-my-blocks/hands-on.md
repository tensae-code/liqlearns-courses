# Hands-on — Project — Program a Flower Garden with My Blocks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Plant the garden
As a group, define the petal block with a size input and plant at least four flowers of two sizes.

## Fix it once
Deliberately plant with a fault in the petal definition, find it, and fix it in the definition only. Count how many flowers the one fix repaired.
