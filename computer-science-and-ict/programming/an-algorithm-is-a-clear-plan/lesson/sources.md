It is one whole lesson with no sub lessons — a small Grade 2 lesson running straight through.

Azerbaijan's own way: informatics starts two full years before touching a computer — objects and their features, information in everyday life, and algorithms as daily-life sequences and true/false judgements; the computer itself, WordPad, Paint and Calculator arrive only in the final unit. This lesson keeps that order and that Grade 2 voice.


Uzbekistan's own way (Grade 9): grade 9 is the capstone — computer graphics, Access, Excel, then a nine-lesson algorithms block and a 28-lesson Turbo Pascal 7.0 course that runs from the alphabet of the language to files, procedures and graphics mode, closing with the Internet. Every chapter ends with a dedicated repetition lesson, placed here to that chapter's main lesson.
