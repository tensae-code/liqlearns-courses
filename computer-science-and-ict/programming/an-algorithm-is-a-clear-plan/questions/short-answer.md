# An Algorithm Is a Clear Plan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Turn this wish into the first two steps of an algorithm: 'make a sandwich'.**
**Answer:** For example: 1. Take two slices of bread. 2. Put cheese on one slice (any clear first steps in that spirit).

**2. What makes a step unclear?**
