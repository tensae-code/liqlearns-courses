# An Algorithm Is a Clear Plan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. An algorithm is —**
a) a clear plan of steps in order  b) a drawing  c) a riddle
**Answer: a) a clear plan of steps in order** — Steps that finish a task.

**2. Which step is clear?**
a) Add some water  b) Add one cup of water  c) Add water somehow
**Answer: b) Add one cup of water** — A clear step needs no guessing.

**3. A recipe is an example of —**
a) a carrier  b) an algorithm  c) a sense
**Answer: b) an algorithm** — A recipe is a plan of steps for a dish.

**4. A good algorithm must —**
a) never end  b) have every needed step and end  c) have one step only
**Answer: b) have every needed step and end** — Complete and ending: the task gets finished.
