# ScratchJr — Making a Simple Game — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a game?**
**Answer: A play-world with a point — an aim, outcomes that can go well or badly, and a player whose actions matter**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does a game differ from a performed story?**
**Answer: In a game the player acts and the world answers**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What three marks must a game have?**
**Answer: A player, choices, and a point (aim)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four planning parts.**
**Answer: Idea, backgrounds, sprites, blocks**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the idea state?**
**Answer: What the player tries to do**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do the blocks decide in the plan?**
**Answer: How each part moves, reacts and scores winning or losing**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why are Grade 4 games kept simple?**
**Answer: A finished small game teaches more than an abandoned grand one**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What sprites does a simple game need named?**
**Answer: The player character, the goal, and the dangers**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is the game planned — before or after blocks?**
**Answer: Before — the plan comes first**
