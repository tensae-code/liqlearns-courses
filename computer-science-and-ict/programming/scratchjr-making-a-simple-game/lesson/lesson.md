# ScratchJr — Making a Simple Game

## Do it first

After the block lessons comes the first whole making. Kuwait's fifth ScratchJr lesson asks what a game is, then has pupils plan and create simple games of their own. This lesson is the planning as much as the building.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a game is

A **game** is a play-world with a **point**: something to aim at, something that can go well or badly, and a player whose actions matter. It differs from a story a sprite performs alone: in a game, the **player** acts — tapping, choosing, timing — and the world answers. Kuwait asks the question first so pupils stop building animations and calling them games: no player, no choices, no point — no game.

In your mind's eye: what a game is.

## Sub lesson 2 — Planning a game — idea, backgrounds, sprites, blocks

A simple game is planned in four parts before blocks are touched: the **idea** (what the player tries to do), the **backgrounds** (the pages the game moves through), the **sprites** (the player character, the goal, the dangers), and the **blocks** (how each part moves, reacts and scores the moment of winning or losing). Grade 4's games are simple on purpose — one clear aim, few sprites, blocks already learned — because a finished small game teaches more than an abandoned grand one.

In your mind's eye: planning a game — idea, backgrounds, sprites, blocks.

## Do It Yourself

1. Show it, don't just say it: A play-world with a point — an aim, outcomes that can go well or badly, and a player whose actions matter. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
2. Show it, don't just say it: In a game the player acts and the world answers. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
3. Show it, don't just say it: A player, choices, and a point (aim). Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
4. Show it, don't just say it: Idea, backgrounds, sprites, blocks. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
