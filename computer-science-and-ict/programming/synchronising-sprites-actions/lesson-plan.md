# Lesson plan — Synchronising Sprites' Actions

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Programming · **Lesson:** Synchronising Sprites' Actions
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Bulgaria (2026-11-14) — in the title Programming on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Bulgaria's *Компютърно моделиране* (Computer Modelling), Grade 4, because no existing lesson teaches this thing as its own lesson at this level.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Synchronising means sprites act together in time: messages and waits line their actions up, so one sprite starts exactly when another finishes — no gaps, no pile-ups.
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 4 simple — it stands on the child's own everyday world and on the lessons named below; every new word is built inside the lesson from things the child can do at once.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## What the learner can do when it ends

## If the learner is stuck
Actions overlap or gap → ask "what is the signal, and who waits for it?" A pile-up means a missing signal; a gap means a wait doing a signal's job badly.
