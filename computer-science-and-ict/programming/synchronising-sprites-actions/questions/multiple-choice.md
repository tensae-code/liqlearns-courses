# Synchronising Sprites' Actions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Synchronising sprites means —**
a) lining their actions up in one shared time  b) giving them the same costume  c) deleting one sprite
**Answer: a) lining their actions up in one shared time**

**2. A message in Scratch works as —**
a) a backdrop  b) a sound effect  c) a signal: broadcast by one sprite, received by another
**Answer: c) a signal: broadcast by one sprite, received by another**

**3. A message is surer than a guessed wait because —**
a) messages are louder  b) the next sprite starts on the event itself  c) waits are forbidden
**Answer: b) the next sprite starts on the event itself**

**4. Actions overlapping when they should follow each other means —**
a) a missing signal — a pile-up  b) perfect synchronising  c) a costume fault
**Answer: a) a missing signal — a pile-up**
