# Mini Project — Program Development — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the mini project take as its brief?**
**Answer: A situasi — a small problem situation from school or home life**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the phases it must pass through.**
**Answer: Problem analysis, algorithm design, coding, testing, and documentation**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the project's rule?**
**Answer: Completeness over size — no phase is skipped**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is the problem kept small?**
**Answer: So no phase needs skipping — a skipped phase is the fault the project exposes**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Give an example situation Malaysia's kind of brief uses.**
**Answer: A club's attendance (also: a canteen queue, a library's loans)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which technique does the analysis report?**
**Answer: Decomposition — the problem broken into its parts**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which techniques does the design report?**
**Answer: Pattern recognition and abstraction**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which technique does the algorithm report?**
**Answer: Sequencing — the steps in order**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is handed over at the end?**
**Answer: The program plus its report**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the report, in Malaysia's point?**
**Answer: Evidence that the program was developed, not merely typed**
