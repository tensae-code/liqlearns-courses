# Linear Search — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does linear search move?**
**Answer: In order, one item at a time, comparing each with the target**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When does the hunt stop?**
**Answer: The moment an item matches — or the list ends**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the answer if the end is reached with no match?**
**Answer: Not found**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Must the list be in order?**
**Answer: No — linear search works on any list**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the worst case?**
**Answer: Every item is checked — the target last, or absent**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What two values does the pseudocode track?**
**Answer: Position (starting at 1) and found (starting false)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are the loop's two exits?**
**Answer: Found is true, or position passes the list's end**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the decision diamond in the flowchart?**
**Answer: The comparison — item equals target?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) A search that can only exit on success is what?**
**Answer: A trap, not an algorithm**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What position does the first item have in Malaysia's pseudocode?**
**Answer: Position 1**
