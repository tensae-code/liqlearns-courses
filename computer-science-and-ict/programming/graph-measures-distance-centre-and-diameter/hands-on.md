# Hands-on — Graph Measures — Distance, Centre and Diameter

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Settle the argument
Using the seven-pupil graph from Do it first, compute every node’s eccentricity (waves from each). List the centre nodes, the radius and the diameter, each with the journey that proves it. Write the verdict on the B-versus-F argument in two sentences.

## Your own small world
Draw the graph of a real circle you belong to — a team, a class group — using initials only, at least ten nodes. Estimate its diameter by testing the pairs that look farthest apart. Then add one well-connected "hub" person and say what happens to the diameter, and why hubs shrink worlds.
