# Graph Measures — Distance, Centre and Diameter — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) The smallest eccentricity in a graph is called the ____.**
**Answer: radius — And the node (or nodes) holding it form the centre.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Milgram’s experiment suggested about ____ degrees of separation between strangers.**
**Answer: six — The famous "six degrees of separation".**
