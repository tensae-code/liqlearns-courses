# Graph Measures — Distance, Centre and Diameter — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) On the Do it first graph, G’s only friend is F, and F’s eccentricity is 3. What is G’s eccentricity, and why?**
**Answer: 4. Every journey from G begins with the edge to F, adding 1 to F’s distances; G’s farthest nodes are those at distance 3 from F (such as A or E), so G’s greatest distance is 3 + 1 = 4.**
