# Graph Measures — Distance, Centre and Diameter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The distance between two nodes in a graph is —**
a) The number of nodes  b) The length of the longest path  c) The length of a shortest path between them  d) Always 1
**Answer: c) The length of a shortest path between them — Counted in edges.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A node’s eccentricity is —**
a) Its number of edges  b) Its greatest distance to any other node  c) Its smallest distance  d) Its age
**Answer: b) Its greatest distance to any other node — How far it sits from the far end of its world.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The diameter of a graph is —**
a) The smallest eccentricity  b) The number of edges  c) The largest distance between any two nodes  d) The centre node
**Answer: c) The largest distance between any two nodes — The graph’s longest shortest journey.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Facebook’s measured average distance between users was about —**
a) 47.4  b) 4.74  c) 474  d) 0.474
**Answer: b) 4.74 — Even smaller than Milgram’s famous six degrees.**
