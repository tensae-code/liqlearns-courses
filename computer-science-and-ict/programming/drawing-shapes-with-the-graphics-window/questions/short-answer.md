# Drawing Shapes with the Graphics Window — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four Draw operations.**
**Answer: DrawLine, DrawRectangle, DrawTriangle, DrawEllipse**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does DrawTriangle need?**
**Answer: Three given points to join**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Inside what is an ellipse drawn?**
**Answer: Inside a given rectangle's frame**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a Fill operation do?**
**Answer: Paints the shape's inside solid**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do PenColor and PenWidth rule?**
**Answer: The outlines — their colour and thickness**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does BrushColor rule?**
**Answer: The fills**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When must pen and brush be set?**
**Answer: Before the operation they should dress**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Does the window remember the settings?**
**Answer: Yes — until they are changed**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a drawing program, in the lesson's phrase?**
**Answer: A list of shapes, each named, each placed by numbers**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's drawing order?**
**Answer: Plan shapes, set pen and brush, draw outlines, fill insides**
