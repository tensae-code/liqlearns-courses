# Drawing Shapes with the Graphics Window — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four Draw operations.**
a) DrawLine, DrawRectangle, DrawTriangle, DrawEllipse  b) Three given points to join  c) Inside a given rectangle's frame  d) Paints the shape's inside solid
**Answer: a) DrawLine, DrawRectangle, DrawTriangle, DrawEllipse**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does DrawTriangle need?**
a) Inside a given rectangle's frame  b) Three given points to join  c) Paints the shape's inside solid  d) The outlines — their colour and thickness
**Answer: b) Three given points to join**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Inside what is an ellipse drawn?**
a) Paints the shape's inside solid  b) The outlines — their colour and thickness  c) Inside a given rectangle's frame  d) The fills
**Answer: c) Inside a given rectangle's frame**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a Fill operation do?**
a) The outlines — their colour and thickness  b) The fills  c) Before the operation they should dress  d) Paints the shape's inside solid
**Answer: d) Paints the shape's inside solid**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do PenColor and PenWidth rule?**
a) The outlines — their colour and thickness  b) The fills  c) Before the operation they should dress  d) Yes — until they are changed
**Answer: a) The outlines — their colour and thickness**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does BrushColor rule?**
a) Before the operation they should dress  b) The fills  c) Yes — until they are changed  d) A list of shapes, each named, each placed by numbers
**Answer: b) The fills**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When must pen and brush be set?**
a) Yes — until they are changed  b) A list of shapes, each named, each placed by numbers  c) Before the operation they should dress  d) Plan shapes, set pen and brush, draw outlines, fill insides
**Answer: c) Before the operation they should dress**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Does the window remember the settings?**
a) A list of shapes, each named, each placed by numbers  b) Plan shapes, set pen and brush, draw outlines, fill insides  c) DrawLine, DrawRectangle, DrawTriangle, DrawEllipse  d) Yes — until they are changed
**Answer: d) Yes — until they are changed**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a drawing program, in the lesson's phrase?**
a) A list of shapes, each named, each placed by numbers  b) Plan shapes, set pen and brush, draw outlines, fill insides  c) DrawLine, DrawRectangle, DrawTriangle, DrawEllipse  d) Three given points to join
**Answer: a) A list of shapes, each named, each placed by numbers**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's drawing order?**
a) DrawLine, DrawRectangle, DrawTriangle, DrawEllipse  b) Plan shapes, set pen and brush, draw outlines, fill insides  c) Three given points to join  d) Inside a given rectangle's frame
**Answer: b) Plan shapes, set pen and brush, draw outlines, fill insides**
