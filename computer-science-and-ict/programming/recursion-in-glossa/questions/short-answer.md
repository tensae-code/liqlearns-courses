# Recursion in ΓΛΩΣΣΑ — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is recursion in ΓΛΩΣΣΑ?**
**Answer: A procedure or function that calls itself**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is each call?**
**Answer: A fresh run with its own parameters and local names, stacked on the unfinished calls**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must every recursive subprogram have?**
**Answer: A stopping case — a call that answers without calling again**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must every self-call do?**
**Answer: Move closer to the stopping case**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens without it?**
**Answer: The calls never end and the program fails**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does the book trace recursion?**
**Answer: On paper, call by call — calls going down, answers coming up**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the factorial's stopping case?**
**Answer: n=1 (or 0) answers 1 without calling again**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does unwinding mean?**
**Answer: The stacked calls each finish with the answer handed back, up to the first call**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name another recursion the book traces.**
**Answer: Greatest common divisor (or Fibonacci numbers)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 12 Anaptyxi Efarmogon, Chapter 10**
