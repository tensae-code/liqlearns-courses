# Using the cmath Library — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What makes cmath functions available?**
**Answer: #include <cmath> at the top**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is sqrt(81)?**
**Answer: 9**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where can a function call's value be used?**
**Answer: Stored, printed, or inside a bigger expression**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What kind of values do cmath functions use?**
**Answer: Decimal (double) values**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is sqrt a function you write yourself?**
**Answer: No — it comes from the library**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does pow(x, y) compute?**
**Answer: x raised to the power y**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do floor and ceil do?**
**Answer: Round down and round up**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does round do?**
**Answer: Rounds to the nearest whole value**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what unit do sin and cos take their angle?**
**Answer: Radians**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why prefer the library over hand-writing these?**
**Answer: Tested once by its makers, reused by everyone**
