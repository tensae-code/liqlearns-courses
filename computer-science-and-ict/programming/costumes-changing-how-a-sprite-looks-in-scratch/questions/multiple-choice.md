# Costumes — Changing How a Sprite Looks in Scratch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a costume?**
a) One of a sprite’s looks  b) It steps to the following look  c) Avery’s  d) It picks exactly the look the moment needs
**Answer: a) One of a sprite’s looks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the next-costume block do?**
a) Repeating movement while changing costume  b) It steps to the following look  c) Yes — the same character in different poses  d) Repeat-forever
**Answer: b) It steps to the following look**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What makes walking look real?**
a) Avery’s  b) It picks exactly the look the moment needs  c) Repeating movement while changing costume  d) Its timing — the waits between switches
**Answer: c) Repeating movement while changing costume**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Whose walk is Grade 7’s example?**
a) Yes — the same character in different poses  b) Repeat-forever  c) A character that walks, flaps or dances  d) Avery’s
**Answer: d) Avery’s**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Can a sprite hold more than one costume?**
a) Yes — the same character in different poses  b) It picks exactly the look the moment needs  c) Its timing — the waits between switches  d) No — they change how the sprite looks
**Answer: a) Yes — the same character in different poses**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does switch to a named costume do?**
a) Repeat-forever  b) It picks exactly the look the moment needs  c) A character that walks, flaps or dances  d) One of a sprite’s looks
**Answer: b) It picks exactly the look the moment needs**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What loop runs an endless animation?**
a) Its timing — the waits between switches  b) No — they change how the sprite looks  c) Repeat-forever  d) It steps to the following look
**Answer: c) Repeat-forever**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What gives the animation its life, besides the costumes?**
a) A character that walks, flaps or dances  b) One of a sprite’s looks  c) Repeating movement while changing costume  d) Its timing — the waits between switches
**Answer: d) Its timing — the waits between switches**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What can repeat-forever costume switching make?**
a) A character that walks, flaps or dances  b) No — they change how the sprite looks  c) It steps to the following look  d) Avery’s
**Answer: a) A character that walks, flaps or dances**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Do costumes change the sprite’s scripts?**
a) One of a sprite’s looks  b) No — they change how the sprite looks  c) Repeating movement while changing costume  d) Yes — the same character in different poses
**Answer: b) No — they change how the sprite looks**
