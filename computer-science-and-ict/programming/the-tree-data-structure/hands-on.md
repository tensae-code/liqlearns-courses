# Hands-on — The Tree Data Structure

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Draw your folders** — Draw the folder tree of a real device or account to depth three. Label the root, two leaves, one subtree, and write the tree's height.

## Task two
**Hierarchy or not?** — Take three datasets (a school's staff, a shop's stock by shelf, a bus route's stops). Decide which are truly trees and defend the one that is not.
