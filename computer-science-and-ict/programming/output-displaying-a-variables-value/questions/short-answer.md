# Output — Displaying a Variable's Value — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does cout send items to?**
**Answer: The screen**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do quotes make cout print?**
**Answer: The text exactly as written**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does cout print for a variable without quotes?**
**Answer: Its current value**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the classic first output bug?**
**Answer: Quoting a variable's name, printing the word instead of the value**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What links items in one cout statement?**
**Answer: The << operator, chained in order**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does endl do?**
**Answer: Ends the line — the next output starts a new line**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What else makes a new line?**
**Answer: The '\n' character in the text**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why separate printed values?**
**Answer: So results do not run together unreadably**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a result table in output terms?**
**Answer: Disciplined layout — label, value, new line, repeated**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the book place output?**
**Answer: Its own section, after assigning values and before expressions**
