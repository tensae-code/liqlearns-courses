Growth, 2026-11-12 (Ethiopia Wave 3): Sub lesson 4 was added to this lesson, not built as a duplicate — the Grade 8 book works the same Editor route in greater depth, as one full worked example. The Grade 8 book's §6.3 misspells SQUARE as "SQAURE" (printed p. 94); this lesson keeps the correct spelling throughout. The teacher guide's Unit 6 answer key (Grade 8 guide, printed p. 55) was used as the verified-answer check for the Grade 8 question items.

The teacher guide's Activity 6.3 key for the 10-by-5 rectangle is wrong as printed (it repeats a single forward step with a 5-unit turn and no right angles, so it would not draw the rectangle), and its review 'triangle' key uses a 20-degree turn — both flagged in the placements. The lesson builds the rectangle and triangle from correct right-angle / 120-degree turns and records the faulty keys here instead of teaching them.
