**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Ethiopia).**

The teacher guide's Activity 6.3 key for the 10-by-5 rectangle is wrong as printed (it repeats a single forward step with a 5-unit turn and no right angles, so it would not draw the rectangle), and its review 'triangle' key uses a 20-degree turn — both flagged in the placements. The lesson builds the rectangle and triangle from correct right-angle / 120-degree turns and records the faulty keys here instead of teaching them.
