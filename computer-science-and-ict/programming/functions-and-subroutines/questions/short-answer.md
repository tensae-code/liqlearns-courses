# Functions and Subroutines — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a function?**
**Answer: A named piece of program that does one job, callable from many places**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What happens at a call?**
**Answer: The program jumps to the function's steps, works them, and returns**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What may a call carry back?**
**Answer: A result, if the job produces one**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does a function appear in Scratch?**
**Answer: As a custom block — defined once, then used as built-in**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the main program read as, with functions?**
**Answer: A story of named jobs**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the two types by their giving.**
**Answer: Value-returning functions; act-doing subroutines (procedures)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give a subroutine example.**
**Answer: Draw the house; play the fanfare**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What are parameters?**
**Answer: Values handed in at the call**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do parameters make one definition do?**
**Answer: Serve many cases**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is a call, in the closing line?**
**Answer: How the program stays readable while doing much**
