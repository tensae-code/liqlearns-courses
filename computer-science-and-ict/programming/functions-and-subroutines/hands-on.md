# Hands-on — Functions and Subroutines

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Name the jobs** — Take a repeated Scratch trick (drawing a shape in many places) and define it as a custom block with a size parameter; use it three times.

## Task two
**Sort the types** — Sort six jobs into value-returning and act-doing, naming each one's parameters and result (or none).
