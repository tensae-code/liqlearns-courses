# Virtual Robot — Display Text and Sound — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the robot show text?**
**Answer: On its controller's small display**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two things display text can show.**
**Answer: Its mission step and a measured number (also its name, a warning)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What else is display text, for the programmer?**
**Answer: A debugging window — what the robot thinks it is doing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do sound blocks give the robot?**
**Answer: Sound effects — tones and sounds at chosen moments**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Give two moments that suit sound.**
**Answer: A start beep and a finish signal (also an alarm at an obstacle)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When is sound chosen over display?**
**Answer: When something must be noticed without looking**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When is display chosen over sound?**
**Answer: When something must be read**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the unit's arc, in the lesson's phrase?**
**Answer: From movement to expression — a machine that goes becomes a machine that communicates**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Are expression blocks decoration?**
**Answer: No — they are engineered communication**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 6 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 3 — From Movement to Expression**
