## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Showing text on the display** — The robot controller carries a small **display**, and blocks write **text onto it**: the robot can show its name, its mission step, a number it has measured, or a warning — the program's state made visible to the humans watching. Display text is the debugging window as well as the face: while a mission runs, what the display says tells the programmer what the robot thinks it is doing.
2. **Giving the robot sound** — **Sound** blocks give the robot a voice of effects: tones and sounds played at the program's chosen moments — a beep at a mission's start, a signal at its finish, an alarm at an obstacle. Expression is engineered, not decorated: display for what must be **read**, sound for what must be **noticed** without looking. Movement, then expression — the unit's arc from a machine that goes to a machine that communicates.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
