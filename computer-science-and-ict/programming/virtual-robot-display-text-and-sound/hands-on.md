# Hands-on — Virtual Robot — Display Text and Sound

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Say it on the display', 'Program (or plan) a mission that shows its step on the display at each stage. List the texts in order.')

## Task two
('Beep with meaning', 'Add two sounds to a mission — start and finish. Write why sound, not display, fits each moment.')
