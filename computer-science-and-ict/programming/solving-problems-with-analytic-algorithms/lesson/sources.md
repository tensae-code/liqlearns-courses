Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (China, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked for the slug and for near matches before building. It sits in the Programming title (programming/), following the book's own order.

China's own way: Compulsory 1 pairs its two great problem-solving methods as neighbours — analytic algorithms here, enumeration next — so the student learns to ask of every problem: is there a formula hiding in it, or must I search?

The two Teacher's Guides in China's set (to Compulsory 1 and to Compulsory 2) are record-only and are cited nowhere in this course: their library records are swapped against the student books (mismatched pairings) and both PDFs are partial (front portions only).
