# Solving Problems with Analytic Algorithms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) An analytic algorithm solves a problem by —**
a) trying every possible answer  b) finding the relationship in the problem and calculating from its formula  c) asking the teacher  d) drawing the problem
**Answer: b) finding the relationship in the problem and calculating from its formula** — The thinking happens once, up front.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The first move of the analytic method is to —**
a) compute  b) derive  c) model the problem — name the quantities and how they relate  d) check
**Answer: c) model the problem — name the quantities and how they relate** — Model, then derive, then compute, then check.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) For the taxi (start 10, price 2 per km), the fare for 7 km by the formula F = S + P × D is —**
a) 17  b) 24  c) 70  d) 12
**Answer: b) 24** — F = 10 + 2 × 7 = 24.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The check move tests the formula on —**
a) the hardest case  b) an easy case that can be verified by hand  c) no case at all  d) another student's formula
**Answer: b) an easy case that can be verified by hand** — D = 0 must give exactly the starting price.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) If the easy-case check fails, the fault is most likely in —**
a) the arithmetic alone  b) the model — a quantity misread or a part forgotten  c) the pencil  d) the units only
**Answer: b) the model — a quantity misread or a part forgotten** — Re-read the problem before re-adding.
