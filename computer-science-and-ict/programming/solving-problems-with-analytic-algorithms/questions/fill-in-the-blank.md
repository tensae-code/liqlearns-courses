# Solving Problems with Analytic Algorithms — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The analytic method's four moves are: model, ____, compute, check.**
**Answer: derive** — Derive = turn the relation into a formula.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In F = S + P × D, the letter D stands for the ____ travelled.**
**Answer: distance** — S is the start price, P the price per kilometre.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) An analytic algorithm replaces searching with ____.**
**Answer: knowing** — The relationship, once found, answers directly.
