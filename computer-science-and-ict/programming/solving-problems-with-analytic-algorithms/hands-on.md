# Hands-on — Solving Problems with Analytic Algorithms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Formula hunt
Find three formula-shaped problems in your maths book or daily life (a phone-credit balance, the cost of n notebooks, the area of the classroom floor). For each, write the model in words, then the formula in letters.

## Break it on purpose
Take your taxi formula and delete the starting price. Run the check with D = 0. Write down what the wrong answer tells you, and which move — model or arithmetic — was at fault.
