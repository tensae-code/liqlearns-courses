# Video script — Project — an Integrated Scratch Product: Map of Kuwait's Governorates

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The **product** is an **interactive map of Kuwait's governorates** built in Scratch: the country's regions drawn as the stage, each **governorate** a place the user can explore — clicked, named, and told about by the program. Integrated means nothing is decorative: sensing answers the user's clicks, lists hold the governorates' facts, broadcasts move the tour from place to place, and the year's variables keep its state.
An integrated product is built as products are: the **map first** (the stage and its governorate sprites), then the **interaction** (what happens when each place is chosen), then the **information** (each place's facts, held in the program's lists and spoken or shown on demand). The project closes the Scratch year by refusing smallness: not a drill but a thing a Kuwaiti pupil could show a visitor and say — this is my country, and my program knows it.
