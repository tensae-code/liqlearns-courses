# Project — an Integrated Scratch Product: Map of Kuwait's Governorates — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the project product?**
**Answer: An interactive map of Kuwait's governorates built in Scratch**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What can the user do with each governorate?**
**Answer: Click it, see it named, and be told about it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does 'integrated' mean here?**
**Answer: Nothing is decorative — every block family of the year is spent on the one product**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which families serve the map? Name two.**
**Answer: Sensing (clicks) and lists (facts) — also broadcasting, variables**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is built first?**
**Answer: The map — the stage and its governorate sprites**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What comes after the map?**
**Answer: The interaction, then the information**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where are the governorates' facts held?**
**Answer: In the program's lists**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does the project close the Scratch year?**
**Answer: By refusing smallness — a product, not a drill**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who is the product's imagined audience?**
**Answer: A visitor — 'this is my country, and my program knows it'**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book sets this project?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 6 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 2 Digital Products**
