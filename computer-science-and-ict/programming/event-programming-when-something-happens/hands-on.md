# Hands-on — Event Programming — When Something Happens

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Reflex table** — For Thymio (or a classroom robot), write a reflex table of five events with their reactions. Program or act out each; a partner springs the events in random order.

## Task two
**Same job, two kinds** — Specify one job — ‘cross the mat and greet a visitor’ — twice: as a pure sequence and as events-plus-sequence. Present both; the class judges which survives a moved chair.
