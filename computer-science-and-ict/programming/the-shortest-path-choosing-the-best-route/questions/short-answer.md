# The Shortest Path — Choosing the Best Route — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Two routes lead to the library: one around the square, one through the lane. How do you choose the shortest?**
**Answer:** Judge or count the travelling along both routes from the same start to the same finish, and pick the one with the least.

**2. Why must both routes end at the same finish when we compare?**
**Answer:** Otherwise the comparison is unfair — a route to a nearer place would 'win' without being a route to our finish at all.
