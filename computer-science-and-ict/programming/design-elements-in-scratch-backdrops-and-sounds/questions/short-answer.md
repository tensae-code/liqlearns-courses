# Design Elements in Scratch — Backdrops and Sounds — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a backdrop?**
**Answer: The stage's background picture**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name four ways to get a backdrop.**
**Answer: Library, paint it, upload, camera**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How are many backdrops used?**
**Answer: Switched between them by block or event**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must read against the backdrop?**
**Answer: The sprite's colours**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a backdrop switch called?**
**Answer: The cheapest scene-change in all of theatre**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where do sounds live in a project?**
**Answer: Per sprite and for the stage**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name three ways to get a sound.**
**Answer: Library, record it, upload it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can blocks do with sound?**
**Answer: Play, loop, stop, and set its volume**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Give sound-as-information examples.**
**Answer: The confirming click; the warning alarm**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why are the two taught together?**
**Answer: A project is judged by eye and ear together**
