# Hands-on — Design Elements in Scratch — Backdrops and Sounds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Dress a scene** — Build a two-scene Scratch moment: backdrop switch with a sound change, and write the design reason for each element.

## Task two
**Design court** — Review a partner's project on design alone: backdrops and sounds — what serves the story, what fights it?
