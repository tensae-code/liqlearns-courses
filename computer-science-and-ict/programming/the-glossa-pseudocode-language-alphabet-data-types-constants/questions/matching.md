Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is ΓΛΩΣΣΑ?** → **Greece's school pseudocode language, in which the AEPP course and its exams write algorithms**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a ΓΛΩΣΣΑ program's fixed frame?** → **ΠΡΟΓΡΑΜΜΑ with its name, declarations, then commands between ΑΡΧΗ and ΤΕΛΟΣ_ΠΡΟΓΡΑΜΜΑΤΟΣ**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the language's alphabet?** → **Greek and Latin letters, the digits, and the special symbols of its commands and operators**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How may an identifier be built?** → **From letters, digits and the underscore, beginning with a letter**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are the two logical values?** → **ΑΛΗΘΗΣ (true) and ΨΕΥΔΗΣ (false)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is a constant?** → **A value named once in the declarations and never changed in the run**
