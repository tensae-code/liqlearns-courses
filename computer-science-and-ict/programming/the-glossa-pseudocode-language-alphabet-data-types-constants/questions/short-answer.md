# The ΓΛΩΣΣΑ Pseudocode Language — Alphabet, Data Types, Constants — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is ΓΛΩΣΣΑ?**
**Answer: Greece's school pseudocode language, in which the AEPP course and its exams write algorithms**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a ΓΛΩΣΣΑ program's fixed frame?**
**Answer: ΠΡΟΓΡΑΜΜΑ with its name, declarations, then commands between ΑΡΧΗ and ΤΕΛΟΣ_ΠΡΟΓΡΑΜΜΑΤΟΣ**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the language's alphabet?**
**Answer: Greek and Latin letters, the digits, and the special symbols of its commands and operators**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How may an identifier be built?**
**Answer: From letters, digits and the underscore, beginning with a letter**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which books teach it here?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 12 Anaptyxi Efarmogon Ch.7, its study guide Ch.1 and its workbook Ch.7**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name ΓΛΩΣΣΑ's four data types.**
**Answer: ΑΚΕΡΑΙΕΣ (integer), ΠΡΑΓΜΑΤΙΚΕΣ (real), ΧΑΡΑΚΤΗΡΕΣ (character), ΛΟΓΙΚΕΣ (logical)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are the two logical values?**
**Answer: ΑΛΗΘΗΣ (true) and ΨΕΥΔΗΣ (false)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is a constant?**
**Answer: A value named once in the declarations and never changed in the run**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a variable?**
**Answer: A named place whose value commands may change**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why declare every name with its type?**
**Answer: So the reader always knows what kind of thing every name holds**
