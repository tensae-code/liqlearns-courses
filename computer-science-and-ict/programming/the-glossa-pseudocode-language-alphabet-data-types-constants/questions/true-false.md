Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**Answer: False — ΠΡΟΓΡΑΜΜΑ with its name, declarations, then commands between ΑΡΧΗ and ΤΕΛΟΣ_ΠΡΟΓΡΑΜΜΑΤΟΣ**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Greek and Latin letters, the digits, and the special symbols of its commands and operators**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Greece Grade 12 Anaptyxi Efarmogon Ch.7, its study guide Ch.1 and its workbook Ch.7** — as the answer to: How may an identifier be built?
**Answer: False — From letters, digits and the underscore, beginning with a letter**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Greece Grade 12 Anaptyxi Efarmogon Ch.7, its study guide Ch.1 and its workbook Ch.7**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: ΑΛΗΘΗΣ (true) and ΨΕΥΔΗΣ (false)** — as the answer to: Name ΓΛΩΣΣΑ's four data types.
**Answer: False — ΑΚΕΡΑΙΕΣ (integer), ΠΡΑΓΜΑΤΙΚΕΣ (real), ΧΑΡΑΚΤΗΡΕΣ (character), ΛΟΓΙΚΕΣ (logical)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: ΑΛΗΘΗΣ (true) and ΨΕΥΔΗΣ (false)**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A named place whose value commands may change** — as the answer to: What is a constant?
**Answer: False — A value named once in the declarations and never changed in the run**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A named place whose value commands may change**
