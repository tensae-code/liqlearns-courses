## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The data boxes — TextBox, PictureBox, RichTextBox, ImageList** — The **data boxes** are the form's holding places. The **TextBox** holds the user's typed line — the program's ear, already met, now in its family. The **PictureBox** holds a **picture** on the form. The **RichTextBox** holds **rich text** — formatted writing, saved and loaded as **RTF files** — the box for documents, not lines. The **ImageList** holds a **collection of images** off-stage, a store the other controls draw their pictures from. Four boxes, four appetites: a line, a picture, a document, a store.
2. **Loading and saving with Open and the Common Dialog** — Boxes are fed from files through the **Common Dialog**: its **Open** form lets the user **choose a file** in the familiar window — the picture for the PictureBox, the RTF for the RichTextBox — and its saving form writes the box's content **back to a file** the user names. Kuwait teaches the dialog beside the boxes because a data box without files is a bowl never filled: the program's data lives on disk, and the dialog is the door it passes through, chosen by the user's own hand.
