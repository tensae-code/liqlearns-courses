# Video script — Visual Basic — Data Box Controls

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The **data boxes** are the form's holding places. The **TextBox** holds the user's typed line — the program's ear, already met, now in its family. The **PictureBox** holds a **picture** on the form. The **RichTextBox** holds **rich text** — formatted writing, saved and loaded as **RTF files** — the box for documents, not lines. The **ImageList** holds a **collection of images** off-stage, a store the other controls draw their pictures from. Four boxes, four appetites: a line, a picture, a document, a store.
Boxes are fed from files through the **Common Dialog**: its **Open** form lets the user **choose a file** in the familiar window — the picture for the PictureBox, the RTF for the RichTextBox — and its saving form writes the box's content **back to a file** the user names. Kuwait teaches the dialog beside the boxes because a data box without files is a bowl never filled: the program's data lives on disk, and the dialog is the door it passes through, chosen by the user's own hand.
