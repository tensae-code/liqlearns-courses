# Hands-on — Adding Sounds to a Scratch Project

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Compare the two plays
Play the same clip with play-sound and with play-until-done, with a say block after each. Watch which one waits.

## Three sources
Give one sprite three sounds — one from the library, one recording brought in, one in your own voice — and play each in turn.
