# Adding Sounds to a Scratch Project — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does play-sound do?**
a) It starts the clip and the script runs on  b) It holds the script until the clip finishes  c) With loops and waits  d) About 240 sounds
**Answer: a) It starts the clip and the script runs on**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does play-sound until done do?**
a) Its own — each sprite has its own sound  b) It holds the script until the clip finishes  c) Play-sound against play-until-done  d) The library, external recordings, and your own voice
**Answer: b) It holds the script until the clip finishes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Whose sounds does a sprite play?**
a) With loops and waits  b) About 240 sounds  c) Its own — each sprite has its own sound  d) When only your voice says it right
**Answer: c) Its own — each sprite has its own sound**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How can sounds be repeated?**
a) Play-sound against play-until-done  b) The library, external recordings, and your own voice  c) Yes — sounds from different sprites can play together  d) With loops and waits
**Answer: d) With loops and waits**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What difference does Grade 6 make pupils compare?**
a) Play-sound against play-until-done  b) About 240 sounds  c) When only your voice says it right  d) The making of the project
**Answer: a) Play-sound against play-until-done**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How many sounds does Grade 8 count in the library?**
a) The library, external recordings, and your own voice  b) About 240 sounds  c) Yes — sounds from different sprites can play together  d) It starts the clip and the script runs on
**Answer: b) About 240 sounds**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are the three sources of sounds?**
a) When only your voice says it right  b) The making of the project  c) The library, external recordings, and your own voice  d) It holds the script until the clip finishes
**Answer: c) The library, external recordings, and your own voice**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is your own voice the right source?**
a) Yes — sounds from different sprites can play together  b) It starts the clip and the script runs on  c) Its own — each sprite has its own sound  d) When only your voice says it right
**Answer: d) When only your voice says it right**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Can two sprites’ sounds overlap?**
a) Yes — sounds from different sprites can play together  b) The making of the project  c) It holds the script until the clip finishes  d) With loops and waits
**Answer: a) Yes — sounds from different sprites can play together**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is choosing the sound’s source part of?**
a) It starts the clip and the script runs on  b) The making of the project  c) Its own — each sprite has its own sound  d) Play-sound against play-until-done
