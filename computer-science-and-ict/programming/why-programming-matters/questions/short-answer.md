**Answer: Because the modern world runs on programs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name four systems that run on programs.**
**Answer: The bank's accounts, the school's results, the phone's messages and the hospital's records**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who wrote each of those programs?**
**Answer: A programmer who first understood the task**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a person who cannot program, in the book's image?**
**Answer: A passenger in the systems**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What thinking does programming teach?**
**Answer: Solving problems in exact steps**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does programming turn the computer into?**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is programming in the world of work?**
