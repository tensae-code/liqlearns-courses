# Hands-on — Developing a Program Step by Step

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Climb the staircase', 'Take the problem 'work out the average of five marks'. Write the understanding (inputs, output), the plan as pseudocode, then build it in Scratch or Python and test it with marks whose average you know.')

## Task two
('Break and repair', 'Swap programs with a partner. Test theirs with two cases you worked out by hand; report any fault by its step — where in their staircase did it enter?')
