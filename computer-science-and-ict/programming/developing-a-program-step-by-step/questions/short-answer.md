# Developing a Program Step by Step — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the development steps, in order?**
**Answer: Understand the problem, plan the steps, build the program, test it, fix what the tests expose**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must be understood first?**
**Answer: What the program must do — what goes in and what must come out**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) In what forms is the plan written?**
**Answer: Pseudocode or flowchart — the algorithm**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why build from a plan?**
**Answer: A planned program can be checked against its plan; a guessed one only against another guess**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When is the program built?**
**Answer: After understanding and planning — third, not first**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a program tested?**
**Answer: Run with known inputs whose right outputs are worked out by hand, and compare**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a mismatch in testing?**
**Answer: A fault found — located and fixed, not a failure of the whole work**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) After a fix, which tests are run again?**
**Answer: All of them — including ones that passed, because a fix can break a neighbour**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When does development end?**
**Answer: When the tests pass and the program does the understood problem's job**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does skipping steps cause?**
**Answer: Programs that fail — the staircase exists because each step feeds the next**
