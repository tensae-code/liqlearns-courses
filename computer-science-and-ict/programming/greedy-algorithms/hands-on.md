# Hands-on — Greedy Algorithms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Run greedy by hand** — With coins 1, 5, 10, 25, make 63 greedily, writing each choice. Then try coins 1, 3, 4 for the amounts 5, 6 and 7, and mark where hunger loses.

## Task two
**Test a problem** — Take 'pack the most activities into one day'. State the greedy rule (earliest finish first), run it on six invented activities, and argue whether a local choice could block the best day.
