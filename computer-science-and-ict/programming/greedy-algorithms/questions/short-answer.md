# Greedy Algorithms — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a greedy algorithm do at each step?**
**Answer: Takes the locally best choice — and never looks back or reconsiders**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three 'best now' examples does the lesson name?**
**Answer: The largest coin, the earliest-finishing activity, the nearest city**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does greedy not need, where it works?**
**Answer: No table and no backtracking — just a rule for 'best now'**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is its school example?**
**Answer: Coin change — always take the largest coin that fits**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is greedy's risk?**
**Answer: The best now can poison the whole**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Give the failing coin example.**
**Answer: Coins 1, 3, 4 making 6: greedy takes 4+1+1 (three coins); 3+3 needs two**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the greedy test?**
**Answer: Asking whether a locally best choice can ever block a globally best answer**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) For which problems is greedy a proof, not a gamble?**
**Answer: Problems built so a local best can never close a needed door**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Between which strategies does Grade 11 place greedy?**
**Answer: Between recursion and dynamic programming**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 11, Bab III B.2 — greedy algorithms**
