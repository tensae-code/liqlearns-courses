# Video script — More Controls in Visual Basic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The choosing family gives the user ready-made answers. The **check box** switches a thing **on or off**, several able to be on together. The **option (radio) button** picks **exactly one** from a group — choosing one switches the others off. The **list box** shows a **list to pick from**, and its partner the **combo box** lets the user pick from a list *or type their own*. Fiji's teaching point is fitness: the control is chosen to match the shape of the choice the program must ask.
The second family works on what the window shows and does. The **picture box** holds an image on the form; the **image control** shows one more lightly. The **timer** makes things happen **by the clock**, without the user touching anything — the control behind animation and automatic updates. **Scroll bars** let a value or a view be **slid** through a range. With these, Fiji's windows stop being posters: they respond, move and keep time — the full surface on which the course's hands-on examples are built.
