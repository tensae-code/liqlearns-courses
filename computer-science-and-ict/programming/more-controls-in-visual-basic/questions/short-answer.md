# More Controls in Visual Basic — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a check box do?**
**Answer: Switches a thing on or off — several can be on together**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does an option (radio) button do?**
**Answer: Picks exactly one from a group — choosing one switches the others off**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a list box offer?**
**Answer: A list to pick from**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does a combo box differ?**
**Answer: The user can pick from the list or type their own entry**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is Fiji's teaching point about controls?**
**Answer: Fitness — the control is chosen to match the shape of the choice**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a picture box hold?**
**Answer: An image on the form**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the timer do?**
**Answer: Makes things happen by the clock, without the user touching anything**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the timer behind?**
**Answer: Animation and automatic updates**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do scroll bars do?**
**Answer: Let a value or a view be slid through a range**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do the second family of controls give a window?**
**Answer: Response, movement and time-keeping — it stops being a poster**
