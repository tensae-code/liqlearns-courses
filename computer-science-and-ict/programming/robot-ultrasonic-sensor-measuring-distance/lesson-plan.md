## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Sound and time — the echo that measures** — The **Ultrasonic sensor** sends out a pulse of sound too high to hear and **times its echo's** return from the nearest surface. Distance is the time halved and paced by sound's speed: a quick echo, a near wall; a slow echo, open space. The robot gains a number, not a bump — it can stop **before** the obstacle, keep a chosen distance from a wall, or report how wide a gateway is, all without touching anything.
2. **A tour of Kuwait's landmarks, by distance** — The book spends the new sense on home ground: a **robot tour of Kuwait landmarks** — the virtual robot travelling between the country's famous places in the simulation, its distances to each landmark's walls and gates measured ultrasonically as it goes. The lesson's pattern is the series' pattern: a real physical principle (echo timing), a sensor that embodies it, and a mission set in Kuwait itself.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
