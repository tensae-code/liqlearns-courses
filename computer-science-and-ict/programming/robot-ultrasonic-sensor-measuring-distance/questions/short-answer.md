# Robot Ultrasonic Sensor — Measuring Distance — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the Ultrasonic sensor send and receive?**
**Answer: A pulse of sound too high to hear, and its echo's return**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is distance found?**
**Answer: The echo's time, halved and paced by sound's speed**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a quick echo mean?**
**Answer: A near surface**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the robot gain over touch?**
**Answer: A distance number — it can act before reaching the obstacle**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name two uses of a distance number.**
**Answer: Stopping before an obstacle, and keeping a chosen distance from a wall (also measuring a gateway)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What mission does the book set with it?**
**Answer: A robot tour of Kuwait landmarks**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is measured on the tour?**
**Answer: Distances to the landmarks' walls and gates**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the pattern of the series' sensor lessons.**
**Answer: A physical principle, a sensor that embodies it, and a mission set in Kuwait**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Does the Ultrasonic sensor need contact?**
**Answer: No — it measures without touching anything**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 7 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 3**
