# Hands-on — Numeracy in Programming

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Estimate, then compute** — Write five quantities a school program might produce. For each, write your estimate first, then the true figure (count or measure it), and note the gap.

## Task two
**Sanity hunt** — A report says: 34 pupils, average mark 104, total fees less than one pupil's fee, −3 absences. Find all four faults and name the check that catches each.
