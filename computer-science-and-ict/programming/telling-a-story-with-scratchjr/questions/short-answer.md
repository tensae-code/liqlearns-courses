# Telling a Story With ScratchJr — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is written first?**
**Answer: The story — before the program**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which story does the séance write?**
**Answer: The story of the emotions — a character whose feelings change across scenes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the draft's parts.**
**Answer: Characters, places, the event that turns the feeling, the ending's feeling**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a clear story give every block?**
**Answer: A job**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a vague story make of programming?**
**Answer: Wandering**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What three beats does a staged feeling have?**
**Answer: It arrives, it shows, it passes or changes**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does staging feelings teach?**
**Answer: Sequence with meaning — scene order as heart order**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens in the next séance?**
**Answer: The written scenes become ScratchJr programs, scene by scene**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What may not be invented at the tablet?**
**Answer: Anything the page has not already earned**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name two feelings the story might turn between.**
**Answer: Joy to fear (also: anger, sadness, calm)**
