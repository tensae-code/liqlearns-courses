# Telling a Story With ScratchJr — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The story — before the program**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The story — before the program** — as the answer to: Which story does the séance write?
**Answer: False — The story of the emotions — a character whose feelings change across scenes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Characters, places, the event that turns the feeling, the ending's feeling**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Characters, places, the event that turns the feeling, the ending's feeling** — as the answer to: What does a clear story give every block?
**Answer: False — A job**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Wandering**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Wandering** — as the answer to: What three beats does a staged feeling have?
**Answer: False — It arrives, it shows, it passes or changes**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Sequence with meaning — scene order as heart order**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Sequence with meaning — scene order as heart order** — as the answer to: What happens in the next séance?
**Answer: False — The written scenes become ScratchJr programs, scene by scene**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Anything the page has not already earned**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Anything the page has not already earned** — as the answer to: Name two feelings the story might turn between.
**Answer: False — Joy to fear (also: anger, sadness, calm)**
