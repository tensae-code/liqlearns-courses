# Video script — Telling a Story With ScratchJr

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The séance is named **writing (écriture)** — of the story, before the program: the **story of the emotions** follows a character whose feelings change across scenes. Pupils draft it as writers: characters, places, the event that turns joy to fear (or anger, or sadness), and the ending's feeling. A clear story gives every later block a job; a vague story makes programming into wandering — the code can only stage what the story has decided.
Emotions are this story's events: a feeling **arrives** (something happens), **shows** (face, words, movement), and **passes or changes** (the next scene). Staging them teaches sequence with meaning — the order of scenes is the order of the heart — and prepares the next séance, where the written scenes become ScratchJr programs scene by scene, nothing invented at the tablet that the page has not already earned.
