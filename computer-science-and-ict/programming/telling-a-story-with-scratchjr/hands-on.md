# Hands-on — Telling a Story With ScratchJr

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Draft the emotion story** — In pairs, draft a three-scene emotion story on the story sheet: character, place, turning event, ending feeling. Read it aloud — listeners must name the feeling of each scene without being told.

## Task two
**Storyboard it** — Turn the draft into a three-panel storyboard: picture, caption, the exact words the character says. This storyboard is next séance's program plan.
