# Hands-on — An Algorithm for Grammar Rules — The Plural of Nouns

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Run the algorithm** — Given the class logigramme, process twelve nouns by finger-tracing the chart, writing each plural and the path taken. Any noun that falls through extends the chart — with its branch labelled.

## Task two
**Exception hunt** — Hunt five plural exceptions beyond the chart's knowledge (dictionaries allowed). For each, state whether it needs a new branch or a correction to an old one — then amend the chart.
