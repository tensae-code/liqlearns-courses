# An Algorithm for Grammar Rules — The Plural of Nouns — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the general plural rule?**
**Answer: Add -s**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do most -al nouns become?**
**Answer: -aux (cheval → chevaux)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name an -al exception.**
**Answer: Bal → bals**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which endings take -x?**
**Answer: -eau, -eu, -au (and seven -ou families)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name three of the -ou seven.**
**Answer: Bijou, caillou, chou (also: genou, hibou, joujou, pou)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must every exception have?**
**Answer: Its own labelled branch**
