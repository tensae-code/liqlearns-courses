# Hands-on — switch — Multiple-Way Selection

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Menu with switch
Write a switch that reads a choice 1–3 (print a triangle, square or circle's name) with breaks and a default scolding invalid choices. Trace choices 2 and 7.

## Fall-through autopsy
Take a switch with the break missing after case 1. For input 1, list every line that prints. Then insert the break and list again.
