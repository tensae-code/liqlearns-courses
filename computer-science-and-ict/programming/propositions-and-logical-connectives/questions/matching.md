# Propositions and Logical Connectives — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a proposition?** → **A statement that is true or false**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is not a proposition: 'the lab has twenty computers', 'open the lab'?** → **'Open the lab' — a command cannot be judged true or false**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does every proposition carry?** → **A truth value**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is negation?** → **The connective that flips a proposition's value — NOT p**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When is a conjunction true?** → **Only when both joined parts are true**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When is a disjunction true?** → **When at least one joined part is true**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When is an implication false?** → **Only when the first part is true and the second false**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does biimplication say?** → **The two propositions stand or fall together**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name the connectives in the book's order.** → **Negation, conjunction, disjunction, implication (and biimplication)**
