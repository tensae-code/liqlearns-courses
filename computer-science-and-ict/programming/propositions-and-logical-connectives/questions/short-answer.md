# Propositions and Logical Connectives — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a proposition?**
**Answer: A statement that is true or false**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is not a proposition: 'the lab has twenty computers', 'open the lab'?**
**Answer: 'Open the lab' — a command cannot be judged true or false**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does every proposition carry?**
**Answer: A truth value**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is negation?**
**Answer: The connective that flips a proposition's value — NOT p**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When is a conjunction true?**
**Answer: Only when both joined parts are true**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When is a disjunction true?**
**Answer: When at least one joined part is true**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When is an implication false?**
**Answer: Only when the first part is true and the second false**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does biimplication say?**
**Answer: The two propositions stand or fall together**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name the connectives in the book's order.**
**Answer: Negation, conjunction, disjunction, implication (and biimplication)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 10 SMK Semester 1, Bab 1 A–B — propositions and connectives**
