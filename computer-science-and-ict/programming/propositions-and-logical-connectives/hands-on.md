# Hands-on — Propositions and Logical Connectives

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Judge and join** — Write five statements from school life; mark which are propositions. Join two with each connective and compute the joined truth values from the parts.

## Task two
**Truth table** — Build the full truth table for p AND q, p OR q, and IF p THEN q. Mark the single row where implication is false and explain it in one sentence.
