# Hands-on — Nested Loops — Working Through a 2D Array

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace the table
For a 2-by-3 array, write every (row, column) visit in order as the nested loops run. Count them and check against rows times columns.

## Pages and chapters
Take a book of 3 chapters with 5 pages each. Write the two loops of reading it — outer for chapters, inner for pages — and count the page-turns.
