# Nested Loops — Working Through a 2D Array — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a nested loop?**
**Answer: A loop placed inside another loop**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How far does the inner loop run?**
**Answer: All the way through, for every single turn of the outer loop**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) In which unit does the book teach nested loops?**
**Answer: The arrays unit**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why can one loop alone not work through a table?**
**Answer: A table has rows and columns — two rounds of repeating**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the outer loop take on a 2D array?**
**Answer: The rows**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the inner loop take?**
**Answer: The columns**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When does the outer loop move to the next row?**
**Answer: After the inner loop has visited every column in the current row**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do you count the innermost visits?**
**Answer: Rows times columns**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) For a 3-row, 4-column table, how many innermost visits are there?**
**Answer: 12**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which grade's book teaches nested loops on arrays?**
**Answer: Grade 11**
