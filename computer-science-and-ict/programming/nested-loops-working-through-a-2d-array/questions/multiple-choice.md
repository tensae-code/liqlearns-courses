# Nested Loops — Working Through a 2D Array — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a nested loop?**
a) A loop placed inside another loop  b) All the way through, for every single turn of the outer loop  c) A table has rows and columns — two rounds of repeating  d) The columns
**Answer: a) A loop placed inside another loop**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How far does the inner loop run?**
a) The arrays unit  b) All the way through, for every single turn of the outer loop  c) The rows  d) After the inner loop has visited every column in the current row
**Answer: b) All the way through, for every single turn of the outer loop**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) In which unit does the book teach nested loops?**
a) A table has rows and columns — two rounds of repeating  b) The columns  c) The arrays unit  d) Rows times columns
**Answer: c) The arrays unit**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why can one loop alone not work through a table?**
a) The rows  b) After the inner loop has visited every column in the current row  c) 12  d) A table has rows and columns — two rounds of repeating
**Answer: d) A table has rows and columns — two rounds of repeating**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the outer loop take on a 2D array?**
a) The rows  b) The columns  c) Rows times columns  d) Grade 11
**Answer: a) The rows**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the inner loop take?**
a) After the inner loop has visited every column in the current row  b) The columns  c) 12  d) A loop placed inside another loop
**Answer: b) The columns**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When does the outer loop move to the next row?**
a) Rows times columns  b) Grade 11  c) After the inner loop has visited every column in the current row  d) All the way through, for every single turn of the outer loop
**Answer: c) After the inner loop has visited every column in the current row**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do you count the innermost visits?**
a) 12  b) A loop placed inside another loop  c) The arrays unit  d) Rows times columns
**Answer: d) Rows times columns**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) For a 3-row, 4-column table, how many innermost visits are there?**
a) 12  b) Grade 11  c) All the way through, for every single turn of the outer loop  d) A table has rows and columns — two rounds of repeating
**Answer: a) 12**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which grade's book teaches nested loops on arrays?**
a) A loop placed inside another loop  b) Grade 11  c) The arrays unit  d) The rows
**Answer: b) Grade 11**
