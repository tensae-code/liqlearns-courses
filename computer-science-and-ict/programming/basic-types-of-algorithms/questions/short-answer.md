# Basic Types of Algorithms — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the basic types by method.**
**Answer: Sequential, iterative (repetitive), recursive, and selection/branching algorithms**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does an iterative algorithm do?**
**Answer: Loops until a condition releases it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a recursive algorithm do?**
**Answer: Calls itself on a smaller piece**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the chapter's first analysis skill?**
**Answer: Naming the type of a printed algorithm**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 11 Eisagogi Epistimis H/Y, Ch.2.2**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the types by task.**
**Answer: Search, sort, calculate, and decide algorithms**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a decide algorithm answer?**
**Answer: Yes or no about its input**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do real programs use the types?**
**Answer: Several woven together — a calculation inside a loop inside a search**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do the types give the reader?**
**Answer: The words to describe an algorithm's working and task**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Method or task: 'recursive' is which?**
**Answer: Method**
