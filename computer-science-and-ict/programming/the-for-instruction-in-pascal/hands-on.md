# Hands-on — The For Instruction in Pascal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**For traces** — Trace for i := 3 to 6 and for i := 6 downto 3 printing i and i*i; list i's values and the printed lines.

## Task two
**Sum by for** — Write the for program that sums the first n integers and the one that prints the multiplication table of 7, each traced for a small n.
