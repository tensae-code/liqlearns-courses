# A Bank Account Program — Deposits, Withdrawals and a Statement List — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What four variables run the account?**
**Answer: Amount, deposit, balance and choice**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a withdrawal check first?**
**Answer: That the balance covers it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the program answer if it does not?**
**Answer: “Insufficient balance”**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Does the money move when the check fails?**
**Answer: No — it pays nothing**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When does the check happen?**
**Answer: Before the money moves**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where is every operation recorded?**
**Answer: In a statement list**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How are withdrawals recorded?**
**Answer: As negative amounts**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How are deposits recorded?**
**Answer: As positive amounts**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the statement list read as?**
**Answer: The account’s history in order**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is shown on request?**
**Answer: An account statement**
