# A Bank Account Program — Deposits, Withdrawals and a Statement List — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What four variables run the account?**
a) Amount, deposit, balance and choice  b) That the balance covers it  c) No — it pays nothing  d) In a statement list
**Answer: a) Amount, deposit, balance and choice**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a withdrawal check first?**
a) “Insufficient balance”  b) That the balance covers it  c) Before the money moves  d) As negative amounts
**Answer: b) That the balance covers it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the program answer if it does not?**
a) No — it pays nothing  b) In a statement list  c) “Insufficient balance”  d) As positive amounts
**Answer: c) “Insufficient balance”**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Does the money move when the check fails?**
a) Before the money moves  b) As negative amounts  c) The account’s history in order  d) No — it pays nothing
**Answer: d) No — it pays nothing**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When does the check happen?**
a) Before the money moves  b) In a statement list  c) As positive amounts  d) An account statement
**Answer: a) Before the money moves**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where is every operation recorded?**
a) As negative amounts  b) In a statement list  c) The account’s history in order  d) Amount, deposit, balance and choice
**Answer: b) In a statement list**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How are withdrawals recorded?**
a) As positive amounts  b) An account statement  c) As negative amounts  d) That the balance covers it
**Answer: c) As negative amounts**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How are deposits recorded?**
a) The account’s history in order  b) Amount, deposit, balance and choice  c) “Insufficient balance”  d) As positive amounts
**Answer: d) As positive amounts**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the statement list read as?**
a) The account’s history in order  b) An account statement  c) That the balance covers it  d) No — it pays nothing
**Answer: a) The account’s history in order**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is shown on request?**
a) Amount, deposit, balance and choice  b) An account statement  c) “Insufficient balance”  d) Before the money moves
**Answer: b) An account statement**
