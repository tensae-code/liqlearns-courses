# Project — Don't Touch! A Ball Game Avoiding Barriers in Scratch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which pieces does the game build first?**
a) A ball, barriers across the field, and a goal  b) The player  c) At the ball's start position, reset by the starting event  d) Because the game's rule is sensed through them
**Answer: a) A ball, barriers across the field, and a goal**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who steers the ball?**
a) At the ball's start position, reset by the starting event  b) The player  c) Because the game's rule is sensed through them  d) Reach the goal without touching a barrier
**Answer: b) The player**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does every attempt begin?**
a) Because the game's rule is sensed through them  b) Reach the goal without touching a barrier  c) At the ball's start position, reset by the starting event  d) A touching test
**Answer: c) At the ball's start position, reset by the starting event**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why keep the barriers one recognisable kind?**
a) Reach the goal without touching a barrier  b) A touching test  c) The run ends — back to the start, or the game is lost  d) Because the game's rule is sensed through them
**Answer: d) Because the game's rule is sensed through them**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What must the ball do to win a run?**
a) Reach the goal without touching a barrier  b) A touching test  c) The run ends — back to the start, or the game is lost  d) Reaching the goal
**Answer: a) Reach the goal without touching a barrier**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What watches constantly while the ball is steered?**
a) The run ends — back to the start, or the game is lost  b) A touching test  c) Reaching the goal  d) Waits and loops
**Answer: b) A touching test**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when the ball touches a barrier?**
a) Reaching the goal  b) Waits and loops  c) The run ends — back to the start, or the game is lost  d) Fair to win with care, impossible through a barrier, restartable at once
**Answer: c) The run ends — back to the start, or the game is lost**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What wins the game?**
a) Waits and loops  b) Fair to win with care, impossible through a barrier, restartable at once  c) A ball, barriers across the field, and a goal  d) Reaching the goal
**Answer: d) Reaching the goal**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What keeps steering smooth and the test running?**
a) Waits and loops  b) Fair to win with care, impossible through a barrier, restartable at once  c) A ball, barriers across the field, and a goal  d) The player
**Answer: a) Waits and loops**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the project's check?**
a) A ball, barriers across the field, and a goal  b) Fair to win with care, impossible through a barrier, restartable at once  c) The player  d) At the ball's start position, reset by the starting event
**Answer: b) Fair to win with care, impossible through a barrier, restartable at once**
