# Project — Don't Touch! A Ball Game Avoiding Barriers in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which pieces does the game build first?**
**Answer: A ball, barriers across the field, and a goal**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who steers the ball?**
**Answer: The player**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does every attempt begin?**
**Answer: At the ball's start position, reset by the starting event**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why keep the barriers one recognisable kind?**
**Answer: Because the game's rule is sensed through them**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What must the ball do to win a run?**
**Answer: Reach the goal without touching a barrier**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What watches constantly while the ball is steered?**
**Answer: A touching test**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when the ball touches a barrier?**
**Answer: The run ends — back to the start, or the game is lost**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What wins the game?**
**Answer: Reaching the goal**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What keeps steering smooth and the test running?**
**Answer: Waits and loops**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the project's check?**
**Answer: Fair to win with care, impossible through a barrier, restartable at once**
