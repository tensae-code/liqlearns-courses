# Project — Don't Touch! A Ball Game Avoiding Barriers in Scratch

## Do it first

Grade 5's game project has one rule, in its name: don't touch. A ball must cross a field of barriers, steered by the player — and sensing a touch is what makes touching cost the game.

## Sub lesson 1 — Building the field — ball, barriers, goal

The project builds the game's pieces first: a **ball sprite** the player will steer, **barriers** drawn or placed across the field — walls, blocks, a maze of obstacles — and a **goal** the ball must reach. The ball starts at its **start position** every game, reset by the starting event, so every attempt begins from the same field. The barriers' colour or shape is kept **one recognisable kind**, because the game's rule will be sensed through it.

## Sub lesson 2 — The rule sensed — touching ends the run

The game runs on **sensing**: the ball is steered — by keys, or following the pointer — while a **touching test watches constantly**: **if the ball touches a barrier, the run ends** — back to the start, or the game declares itself lost — and **reaching the goal wins**. Waits and loops keep the steering smooth and the test always running; the conditions are the game's whole law. The check is a played game: fair to win with care, impossible to win by crossing a barrier, and restartable at once.
