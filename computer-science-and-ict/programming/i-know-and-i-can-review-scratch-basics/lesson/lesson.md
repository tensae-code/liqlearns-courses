# I Know and I Can — Review: Scratch Basics

## Do it first

Open an old Scratch task from each kind: move the hero, draw with the pen, place a sprite. Do each alone. Any task that stops you names the lesson to revisit.

## I Know and I Can — Review: Scratch Basics

The second **"Знам и мога" (I know and I can)** check comes after Scratch has begun. It proves the Scratch basics as one set: finding your way in the **Scratch environment**, writing the first script that **moves the hero** and says hello, using **loops in Scratch**, **turning and drawing with the pen**, and **positioning and moving on the stage** — including the labyrinth work from the loops lesson. As in every mastery check in this book, the tasks are done alone and checked against what the script actually does; anything not proved is named and practised again before the book moves on to stage, sprites and sound.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: This "Знам и мога" mastery check proves the first Scratch skills together: the environment, moving the hero, loops, turning and drawing with the pen, and positioning on the stage. Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.

## Do It Yourself

1. Show it, don't just say it: move the hero with a first script, without help. Do this with a real example from your own work or your own school day, and write or say how you know it is right.
2. Show it, don't just say it: draw a shape with turning and the pen, and place a sprite exactly. Do this with a real example from your own work or your own school day, and write or say how you know it is right.
3. Show it, don't just say it: name which Scratch basic is not proved yet and practise it. Do this with a real example from your own work or your own school day, and write or say how you know it is right.
