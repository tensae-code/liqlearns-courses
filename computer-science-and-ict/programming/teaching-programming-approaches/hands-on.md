# Hands-on — Teaching Programming Approaches

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Do the book's steps** — Work through Teaching programming — methodical/algorithmic-oriented, data-oriented, specification-oriented, problem type-oriented, language-oriented, instruction-oriented, mathematics-oriented and hardware-oriented approaches exactly as South Sudan's book orders it, on a real computer if one is available; otherwise write the steps in order and check them against the book's order.

## Task two
**Show it to someone** — Take Doing it — Teaching programming — methodical/algorithmic-oriented, data-oriented, specification-oriented, problem type-oriented, language-oriented, instruction-oriented, mathematics-oriented and hardware-oriented approaches in practice and show a partner what it does: do it once slowly, naming each step, then let the partner do it while you only watch and correct.
