# Hiding and Showing Sprites — and the Wait Until Block — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Design a sprite's entrance: it must appear only when the player reaches the door. Write the block order.**
**Answer:** A sound answer: hide at the start; wait until the touching/condition for the door is true; then show, followed by the sprite's action or speech.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A classmate says their sprite vanished from the project completely. What do you check first?**
**Answer:** A sound answer: whether a hide block ran — select the sprite in the sprite list; if it is there but invisible, run show.
