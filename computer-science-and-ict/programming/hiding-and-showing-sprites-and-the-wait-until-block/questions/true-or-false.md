# Hiding and Showing Sprites — and the Wait Until Block — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A hidden sprite keeps its scripts.**
**Answer: True** — Only its visibility changes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Wait until repeats a block many times.**
**Answer: False** — It pauses once, until the condition is true; repeating is a loop's job.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Wait until can wait for a key press.**
**Answer: True** — Any condition can be waited for.
