# Hiding and Showing Sprites — and the Wait Until Block — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Hide makes a sprite —**
a) Deleted forever  b) Invisible but still in the project  c) Bigger
**Answer: b) Invisible but still in the project** — Hidden is not deleted; show brings it back.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A character for a later scene waits at the start by being —**
a) Hidden  b) Deleted  c) Copied
**Answer: a) Hidden** — Hide keeps it ready but off stage.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Wait until makes the script —**
a) Run backwards  b) Pause until its condition is true  c) Repeat forever
**Answer: b) Pause until its condition is true** — It stands still until the condition turns true.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) After the wait-until condition becomes true, the script —**
a) Stops forever  b) Continues with the next block  c) Restarts
**Answer: b) Continues with the next block** — It continues from where it waited.
