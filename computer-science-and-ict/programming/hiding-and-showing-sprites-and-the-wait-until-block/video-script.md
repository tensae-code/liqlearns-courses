# Video script — Hiding and Showing Sprites — and the Wait Until Block


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


Hide: the sprite leaves the stage.

Show: it comes back. Nothing was deleted.

Wait until: the script stands still.

When the condition turns true — it goes on.
