# Lesson plan — Hiding and Showing Sprites — and the Wait Until Block

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Programming · **Lesson:** Hiding and Showing Sprites — and the Wait Until Block
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Finland (2026-11-14) — in the title Programming on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Finland's *Ensiaskeleet ohjelmointiin* (student book, Oppilaan kirja, Versio 2.0), because no existing lesson teaches this thing as its own lesson.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Sprites can vanish and return with hide and show, and a script can pause until something becomes true with wait until — together they let a project keep characters off stage until their moment, then bring them in exactly when a condition is met.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Scratch scripts and events; hide, show and wait until are each built inside the lesson and then combined.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Hide and show** — Hide and show a sprite at the right moments.
2. **Wait until — the script stands still** — Pause a script with wait until a condition is true.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Scratch — Stage, Sprite, Palette, Script in Programming. Recommended, never forced — a learner may land on this lesson directly, and it stands alone.
- Finland's own way is kept: this placement sits in Finland's two-part Scratch path — Part 1 moves towards the interactive-story project and Part 2 towards the self-built maze game, in the Luetaan (Let's read) then Ohjelmoidaan (Let's program) rhythm, with pair work, reflection and peer showing built in.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Hide a sprite and show it again with blocks.
2. Say what wait until does to a script.
3. Combine hide/show with wait until so a sprite appears exactly when a condition becomes true.

## What you need
A computer with Scratch.

## If the learner is stuck
Sprite has 'disappeared forever' → it is hidden, not deleted; add show and run it. Script seems stuck → it is waiting: check whether its wait-until condition can ever become true in this project.
