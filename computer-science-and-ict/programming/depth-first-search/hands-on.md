# Hands-on — Depth-First Search

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Trace the plunge** — On a small graph of six vertices, run DFS by hand from a chosen start. List the visit order and draw the stack at its deepest moment.

## Task two
**DFS or BFS?** — For each job — 'is this town reachable?', 'what is the shortest road?' — choose DFS or BFS and give the depth/breadth reason.
