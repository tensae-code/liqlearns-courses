# Nested Loops — a Loop Inside a Loop — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a nested loop?**
**Answer: One loop placed inside another loop's body**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) State the governing law.**
**Answer: The inner loop runs to completion for every single turn of the outer loop**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A 3-turn outer around a 4-turn inner runs the innermost body how many times?**
**Answer: 12 times — 3 multiplied by 4**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the two family forms taught.**
**Answer: While inside while, and for inside for**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which form is the grid's natural shape?**
**Answer: For inside for — rows by the outer, columns by the inner**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How are nested loops read?**
**Answer: By tracing both counters — the outer's turn, then the inner's whole run beneath it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the classic trace?**
**Answer: The multiplication table**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the shape of job a nested loop answers.**
**Answer: 'For each row, for each thing in the row' — timetables, seating plans, star patterns**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does this lesson sit?**
**Answer: It closes Grade 10 Term 1's loops lesson (Lesson 5)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
