# I Know and I Can — Review: Branching — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A branching task is proved only when —**
a) both its yes and no runs work  b) its yes run works  c) it is written neatly
**Answer: a) both its yes and no runs work**

**2. This review's theme is —**
a) a football match  b) a lost teddy bear  c) a journey through Bulgaria
**Answer: c) a journey through Bulgaria**

**3. The branching block itself was taught in —**
a) the Blue-Bot lessons  b) Урок 7, stocked in the loops and conditions lesson  c) the sound gallery
**Answer: b) Урок 7, stocked in the loops and conditions lesson**

**4. A failing no-run means —**
a) that way is the gap to practise  b) the whole review is failed forever  c) branches need no no-way
**Answer: a) that way is the gap to practise**
