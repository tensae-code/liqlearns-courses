# Hands-on — Pattern Recognition in Search and Sort

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Name the pattern', 'Watch (or read) traces of the four methods with their names hidden. For each, write the repeating pattern in the check–next style and name the method it must be.')

## Task two
('Predict the work', 'A list of 500 ordered names will be searched 100 times. Predict, by pattern, the work of linear searching every time against sorting once (already ordered — so binary searching). Defend the choice by the patterns.')
