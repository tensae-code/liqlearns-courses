# Pattern Recognition in Search and Sort — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is every method in the chapter, seen by pattern recognition?**
**Answer: A pattern repeated**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What pattern does linear search repeat?**
**Answer: Check – next**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What pattern does binary search repeat?**
**Answer: Check the middle – halve**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What pattern does bubble sort repeat?**
**Answer: Compare neighbours – maybe swap, pass within pass**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does recognising a pattern let you say?**
**Answer: What happens next, and why — at any step**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a surprising step in a trace mean?**
**Answer: The pattern was misrecognised, or the algorithm has a fault**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Against what is a trace checked?**
**Answer: Against the pattern**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do patterns compare the methods?**
**Answer: By the work each pattern predicts — growing, collapsing, or repeating passes**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is sorting once worth it, by pattern?**
**Answer: When halving (binary search) will be used often**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does pattern recognition turn four methods into?**
**Answer: One understood family**
