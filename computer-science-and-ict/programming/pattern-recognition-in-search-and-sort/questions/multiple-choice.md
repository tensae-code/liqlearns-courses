# Pattern Recognition in Search and Sort — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is every method in the chapter, seen by pattern recognition?**
a) A pattern repeated  b) Check – next  c) Check the middle – halve  d) Compare neighbours – maybe swap, pass within pass
**Answer: a) A pattern repeated**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What pattern does linear search repeat?**
a) Check the middle – halve  b) Check – next  c) Compare neighbours – maybe swap, pass within pass  d) What happens next, and why — at any step
**Answer: b) Check – next**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What pattern does binary search repeat?**
a) Compare neighbours – maybe swap, pass within pass  b) What happens next, and why — at any step  c) Check the middle – halve  d) The pattern was misrecognised, or the algorithm has a fault
**Answer: c) Check the middle – halve**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What pattern does bubble sort repeat?**
a) What happens next, and why — at any step  b) The pattern was misrecognised, or the algorithm has a fault  c) Against the pattern  d) Compare neighbours – maybe swap, pass within pass
**Answer: d) Compare neighbours – maybe swap, pass within pass**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does recognising a pattern let you say?**
