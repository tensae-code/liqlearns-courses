# Coordinates on the Scratch Stage — Go To, Glide and Speed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the stage, in this lesson?**
a) A grid with numbered places  b) From −240 to 240  c) At the centre of the stage  d) It puts the sprite at the given position at once
**Answer: a) A grid with numbered places**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does x run from and to?**
a) From −180 to 180  b) From −240 to 240  c) Shapes  d) It travels to the position in seconds
**Answer: b) From −240 to 240**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does y run from and to?**
a) At the centre of the stage  b) It puts the sprite at the given position at once  c) From −180 to 180  d) A slower move
**Answer: c) From −180 to 180**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the origin?**
a) Shapes  b) It travels to the position in seconds  c) Where the sprite is  d) At the centre of the stage
**Answer: d) At the centre of the stage**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What can be drawn from corner coordinates?**
a) Shapes  b) It puts the sprite at the given position at once  c) A slower move  d) Its x and its y
**Answer: a) Shapes**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does go to do?**
a) It travels to the position in seconds  b) It puts the sprite at the given position at once  c) Where the sprite is  d) A grid with numbered places
**Answer: b) It puts the sprite at the given position at once**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does glide do?**
a) A slower move  b) Its x and its y  c) It travels to the position in seconds  d) From −240 to 240
**Answer: c) It travels to the position in seconds**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a longer glide mean?**
a) Where the sprite is  b) A grid with numbered places  c) From −180 to 180  d) A slower move
**Answer: d) A slower move**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do the x/y sensing blocks report?**
a) Where the sprite is  b) Its x and its y  c) From −240 to 240  d) At the centre of the stage
**Answer: a) Where the sprite is**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two numbers name every place on the stage?**
a) A grid with numbered places  b) Its x and its y  c) From −180 to 180  d) Shapes
