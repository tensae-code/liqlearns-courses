# Hands-on — Coordinates on the Scratch Stage — Go To, Glide and Speed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Corners by numbers
Write the four corner coordinates of a rectangle, then draw it by sending the pen sprite go-to from corner to corner.

## Slow and fast glides
Glide the same sprite to the same place in 1 second and in 5 seconds. Say which was slower and why the numbers prove it.
