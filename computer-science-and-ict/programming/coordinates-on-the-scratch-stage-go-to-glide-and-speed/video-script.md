# Video script — Coordinates on the Scratch Stage — Go To, Glide and Speed

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The stage is a **grid**: **x from −240 to 240** left-to-right, **y from −180 to 180** bottom-to-top, and the **origin** — (0, 0) — at the centre. Every place on the stage has a pair of numbers, and shapes can be drawn from their **corner coordinates**.
**Go to** puts the sprite at the given position at once; **glide** travels there **in seconds** — and the book’s rule of speed is plain: a **longer glide is a slower** move. The **x/y sensing blocks** report where the sprite is, so a program can ask the stage where its sprite stands.
