# Robot Colour Sensor and Line Following — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the colour sensor do?**
**Answer: Shines light at the surface and reads what returns**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does colour mode report?**
**Answer: The name of the colour it sees**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does reflected-light mode report?**
**Answer: How much light bounces back, as a number**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which surface reflects more — white or black?**
**Answer: White — bright; black returns faintly**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a line, to this sensor?**
**Answer: A place where the reflected number changes**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does line-following steer?**
**Answer: Keeps the reflected number at the line's border, correcting drift both ways**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How often are corrections made?**
**Answer: Constantly — a hundred small corrections a minute**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What mission setting does Kuwait use?**
**Answer: A robot tour of Kuwait City, the line as its road**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Is line-following one decision or many?**
**Answer: Many — a stream of small corrections**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 8 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 3**
