# Robot Colour Sensor and Line Following

## Do it first

Grade 8's robot learns to see — in the limited, exact way robots do: by colour and reflected light. This lesson is the colour sensor, its modes, and the oldest robot mission of all: following a line.

## Sub lesson 1 — The colour sensor — colour and reflected light

The **colour sensor** shines light at the surface beneath it and reads what returns. In **colour mode** it names the colour it sees; in **reflected-light mode** it reports how much of its light bounces back — bright from a white surface, faint from a black one — as a number. That number is the mission's gold: a line is nothing but a place where the reflected number changes, and a sensor that reports it can find edges no eye needs to see.

In your mind's eye: the colour sensor — colour and reflected light.

## Sub lesson 2 — First line-following missions — and a tour of Kuwait City

**Line-following** is the art of staying on that edge: read the reflected number, and steer so the number stays at the line's border — drift toward white, turn back; drift toward black, turn back — a hundred small corrections a minute. Kuwait's first missions run the follower on a **tour of Kuwait City** in the simulation: the line is the road, the city is home, and the robot that holds its line holds its route through both.

In your mind's eye: first line-following missions — and a tour of Kuwait City.
