# Video script — Structuring Information with Models

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Information thrown together in a heap cannot be searched or checked.
**Structuring** arranges it by a chosen order, and the arrangement itself becomes an information model of the material.
Some information is not a line or a level but a web: towns joined by roads, people joined by friendships, pages joined by links.
Its structure is a **graph** — points joined by lines — and a graph model answers connection questions a table cannot: is there a path, which point connects to most others, what is the shortest route.
