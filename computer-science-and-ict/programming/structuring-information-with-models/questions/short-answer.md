# Structuring Information with Models — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does structuring information mean?**
**Answer: Arranging it by a chosen order so the order can be used**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a list structure?**
**Answer: Items following one after another in a line**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a table structure?**
**Answer: Items in rows and their features in columns**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a tree structure?**
**Answer: Items hanging in levels under bigger items, found by a path from the top**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a graph structure?**
**Answer: Points joined by lines, modelling connections**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What questions does a graph answer well?**
**Answer: Connection questions — paths, most-connected points, shortest routes**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Is the arrangement itself a model?**
**Answer: Yes — structuring is an information model of the material**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is a finished structure checked?**
**Answer: Against its job — can you find, compare, add and follow what the purpose needs**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Give an example of a tree in computing.**
**Answer: Folders inside folders**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What makes one structure right for a purpose?**
**Answer: It answers the purpose's questions with the least hunting**
