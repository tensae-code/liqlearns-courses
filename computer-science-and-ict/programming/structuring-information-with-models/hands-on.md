# Hands-on — Structuring Information with Models

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Same facts, three structures
Take the classes, teachers and rooms of your school. Arrange them as a list, as a table, and as a tree. For each structure, write one question it answers easily and one it answers badly.

## Draw the connections
Draw your neighbourhood as a graph — places as points, roads as lines. Use it to answer: which place connects to most others, and what is the shortest route between two chosen places?
