# One-Dimensional Arrays in ΓΛΩΣΣΑ — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a one-dimensional array?**
**Answer: A row of places of one type under one name**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is it declared in ΓΛΩΣΣΑ?**
**Answer: ΠΙΝΑΚΑΣ[size] — the name, then the count in brackets**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is one place reached?**
**Answer: By its index — Π[1] the first place, Π[i] the place i names**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the index matter?**
**Answer: A ΓΙΑ loop counting the index can visit every place with the same commands**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the loop that walks an array.**
**Answer: ΓΙΑ…ΑΠΟ…ΜΕΧΡΙ (the for loop)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When should an array be used?**
**Answer: When many values of one kind must all be kept for later commands**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the standard first patterns.**
**Answer: Input in a loop, output of the whole array, sum and count over its places**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the workbook mark?**
**Answer: Which worked examples and activities are medium and which advanced**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which books teach it here?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 12 Anaptyxi Efarmogon Ch.9, its study guide Ch.4 and its workbook Ch.9**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What sits beside the one-dimensional array in the study guide?**
**Answer: Input into two-dimensional arrays**
