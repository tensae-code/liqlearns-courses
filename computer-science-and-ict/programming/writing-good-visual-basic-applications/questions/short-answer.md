# Writing Good Visual Basic Applications — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Who are a good application's two judges?**
**Answer: Its user, and the next programmer who reads its code**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does clear mean at the surface?**
**Answer: Every control labelled so its job is plain**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does predictable mean?**
**Answer: Buttons do what their labels promise, in the places users expect**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does kind mean?**
**Answer: It asks only for what it needs, warns before anything is lost, and reports in the user's words**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Whose experience is Fiji's standard?**
**Answer: The user's — not the programmer's pride**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What names must good code carry?**
**Answer: Meaningful names for controls and variables**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where do comments belong?**
**Answer: Where a choice needs explaining**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What layout does good code keep?**
**Answer: Orderly — one job per procedure**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does good layout help with faults?**
**Answer: A fault can be found in the part that does that job**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is goodness applied?**
**Answer: As a habit from the first form — not a finish applied at the end**
