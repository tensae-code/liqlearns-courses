# Hands-on — break and continue inside while

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — every activity is done for real, in IDLE or on paper.

## Count-out game
Count aloud 1 to 9 as a group loop. At 5 the leader shouts BREAK — everyone stops (first program). Restart: at 5 the leader shouts CONTINUE — that number is whispered/skipped and counting carries on (second program).

## Trace tables
Draw a trace table — turn, count, printed? — for both programs. Nine rows for the second; mark the escaped row.
