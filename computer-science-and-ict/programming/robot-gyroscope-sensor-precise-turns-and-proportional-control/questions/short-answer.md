# Robot Gyroscope Sensor — Precise Turns and Proportional Control — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the gyroscope measure?**
**Answer: The robot's rotation — degrees turned from its start**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does a gyro turn end?**
**Answer: The program turns until the gyro reads the target angle, then stops**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is that better than timing a turn?**
**Answer: 90 means 90 on any floor, with any battery — the turn is measured, not hoped**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What second gift does the gyro give?**
**Answer: The robot's heading at every moment**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the error in straight driving?**
**Answer: How far the heading is from the target heading**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does proportional control do with the error?**
**Answer: Corrects in proportion to it — big error, strong correction; small error, gentle**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is Kp?**
**Answer: The constant that sets the proportion of correction**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens if Kp is too small? Too large?**
**Answer: Too small: the robot wanders. Too large: it twitches**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is tuning?**
**Answer: Trying, watching and adjusting Kp — the engineer's real work**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 8 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 3**
