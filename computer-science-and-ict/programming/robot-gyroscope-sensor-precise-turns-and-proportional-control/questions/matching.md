# Robot Gyroscope Sensor — Precise Turns and Proportional Control — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the gyroscope measure?** → **The robot's rotation — degrees turned from its start**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does a gyro turn end?** → **The program turns until the gyro reads the target angle, then stops**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is that better than timing a turn?** → **90 means 90 on any floor, with any battery — the turn is measured, not hoped**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What second gift does the gyro give?** → **The robot's heading at every moment**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the error in straight driving?** → **How far the heading is from the target heading**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does proportional control do with the error?** → **Corrects in proportion to it — big error, strong correction; small error, gentle**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is Kp?** → **The constant that sets the proportion of correction**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens if Kp is too small? Too large?** → **Too small: the robot wanders. Too large: it twitches**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is tuning?** → **Trying, watching and adjusting Kp — the engineer's real work**
