# Video script — The ScratchJr Screen — Stage, Characters and Blocks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The screen's heart is the **stage**: where characters act, on the chosen background, with the **pages** (scenes) of the project beside it. Around it sit the project's furniture: the **character list** (who is in this scene), the **background** chooser, and the buttons that run, present and save. Knowing the stage's geography first means every later instruction — “add a character”, “change the scene” — has a place to land.
Below the stage lie the **block trays**, sorted by colour and job, and the **script area**, where the selected character's blocks are snapped into scripts. The screen's logic is a sentence: choose **who** (character), choose **what** (blocks), build **where** (script area), watch **on the stage**. The séance's exit test is pure geography: given any element, point to it — a pupil who can find everything can be taught anything on this screen.
