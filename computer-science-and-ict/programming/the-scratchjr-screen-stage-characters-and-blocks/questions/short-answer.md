# The ScratchJr Screen — Stage, Characters and Blocks — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the screen's heart?**
**Answer: The stage — where characters act**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What sits beside the stage?**
**Answer: The pages (scenes) of the project**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the character list show?**
**Answer: Who is in this scene**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name three furniture buttons.**
**Answer: Run, present, save (answers vary by version)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why learn the geography first?**
**Answer: So every later instruction has a place to land**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where do the blocks live?**
**Answer: In the block trays, sorted by colour and job**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where are scripts built?**
**Answer: In the script area, for the selected character**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) State the screen's sentence.**
**Answer: Choose who, choose what, build in the script area, watch on the stage**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the séance's exit test?**
**Answer: Point to any named element on the screen**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a pupil who can find everything gain?**
**Answer: They can be taught anything on this screen**
