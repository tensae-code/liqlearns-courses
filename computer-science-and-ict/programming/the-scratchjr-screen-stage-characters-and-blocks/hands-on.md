# Hands-on — The ScratchJr Screen — Stage, Characters and Blocks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Screen treasure hunt** — Teacher calls elements (“the script area!”, “page two!”, “the motion tray!”) — pupils point on projected screens or tablets. Ten finds, no misses, wins.

## Task two
**Draw the screen from memory** — After one minute's study, close the app and draw the ScratchJr screen with every zone labelled. Reopen and correct your map in another colour.
