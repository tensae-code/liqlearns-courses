# Drawing Geometric Figures in PascalABC — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does GraphABC open?" is: A graphics window — a drawing surface for the program.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does every point on the surface have?" is: No — the program states the numbers and the figure appears where they put it.**
**Answer: False** — Coordinates — distance across and distance down from the corner.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is a line drawn?" is: Between two points named by their coordinates.**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is a circle specified?" is: They dress a figure — set its outline and inside colour.**
**Answer: False** — By its centre and its radius.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is a rectangle specified?" is: By its opposite corners.**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Is anything dragged with a mouse?" is: The screen shows at once whether the program and its geometry are right.**
**Answer: False** — No — the program states the numbers and the figure appears where they put it.

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How are complex figures built?" is: As a sequence of simple figure commands in a chosen order.**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do colour and fill commands do?" is: Coordinates — distance across and distance down from the corner.**
**Answer: False** — They dress a figure — set its outline and inside colour.

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is a drawing moved or resized?" is: By changing the numbers and re-running the program.**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why does the Grade 11 programming part open with drawing?" is: By its centre and its radius.**
**Answer: False** — The screen shows at once whether the program and its geometry are right.
