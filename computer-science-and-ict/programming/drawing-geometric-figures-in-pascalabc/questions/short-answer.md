# Drawing Geometric Figures in PascalABC — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does GraphABC open?**
**Answer: A graphics window — a drawing surface for the program**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does every point on the surface have?**
**Answer: Coordinates — distance across and distance down from the corner**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a line drawn?**
**Answer: Between two points named by their coordinates**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a circle specified?**
**Answer: By its centre and its radius**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a rectangle specified?**
**Answer: By its opposite corners**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Is anything dragged with a mouse?**
**Answer: No — the program states the numbers and the figure appears where they put it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How are complex figures built?**
**Answer: As a sequence of simple figure commands in a chosen order**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do colour and fill commands do?**
**Answer: They dress a figure — set its outline and inside colour**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is a drawing moved or resized?**
**Answer: By changing the numbers and re-running the program**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the Grade 11 programming part open with drawing?**
**Answer: The screen shows at once whether the program and its geometry are right**
