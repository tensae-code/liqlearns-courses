# Hands-on — Drawing Geometric Figures in PascalABC

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw a house
Write a GraphABC program that draws a house from figures: a rectangle body, a triangular roof (lines), a rectangle door and a circle window. Run it and check every figure sits where your numbers intended.

## Move it by numbers
Change only the coordinate numbers in your program to move the whole house across the window, then change the radius and say exactly which lines of code controlled each change.
