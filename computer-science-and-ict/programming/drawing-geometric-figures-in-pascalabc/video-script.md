# Video script — Drawing Geometric Figures in PascalABC

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

GraphABC opens a **graphics window**: a drawing surface where every point has **coordinates** — its distance across and its distance down from the corner — and every drawing command names the points it needs by their coordinates.
A **line** is drawn between two points; a **rectangle** by its opposite corners; a **circle** by its centre and radius.
Figures are **built**: a house is a rectangle, a triangle roof and a circle window, drawn by a sequence of commands in an order the programmer chooses.
Colour and fill commands dress a figure, and a drawing program can be re-run with changed numbers to move or resize the whole construction — the figure is the numbers, so changing the numbers rebuilds it.
