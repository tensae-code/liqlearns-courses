# Logic of Programming — Foundations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is programming logic?**
a) The clear, ordered thinking a program is built from  b) States it exactly, splits it into exact steps, orders the steps  c) Gaps and maybe-steps  d) The steps themselves
**Answer: a) The clear, ordered thinking a program is built from**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three things does it do to a problem?**
a) Gaps and maybe-steps  b) States it exactly, splits it into exact steps, orders the steps  c) The steps themselves  d) A flowchart, pseudocode, or any language
**Answer: b) States it exactly, splits it into exact steps, orders the steps**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a logical solution have none of?**
a) The steps themselves  b) A flowchart, pseudocode, or any language  c) Gaps and maybe-steps  d) Order, exactness and completeness
**Answer: c) Gaps and maybe-steps**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What decides every case in a logical solution?**
a) A flowchart, pseudocode, or any language  b) Order, exactness and completeness  c) A step that uses a result comes after the step that makes it  d) The steps themselves
**Answer: d) The steps themselves**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In what forms is the same logic later written?**
a) A flowchart, pseudocode, or any language  b) Order, exactness and completeness  c) A step that uses a result comes after the step that makes it  d) One action the computer can do, nothing left to guess
**Answer: a) A flowchart, pseudocode, or any language**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three habits of logic at work.**
a) A step that uses a result comes after the step that makes it  b) Order, exactness and completeness  c) One action the computer can do, nothing left to guess  d) All the problem's cases, including the awkward ones
**Answer: b) Order, exactness and completeness**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the order rule?**
a) One action the computer can do, nothing left to guess  b) All the problem's cases, including the awkward ones  c) A step that uses a result comes after the step that makes it  d) Data and types, then variables, expressions and counters
**Answer: c) A step that uses a result comes after the step that makes it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does exactness demand of each step?**
a) All the problem's cases, including the awkward ones  b) Data and types, then variables, expressions and counters  c) The clear, ordered thinking a program is built from  d) One action the computer can do, nothing left to guess
**Answer: d) One action the computer can do, nothing left to guess**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does completeness cover?**
a) All the problem's cases, including the awkward ones  b) Data and types, then variables, expressions and counters  c) The clear, ordered thinking a program is built from  d) States it exactly, splits it into exact steps, orders the steps
**Answer: a) All the problem's cases, including the awkward ones**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What comes after logic in Cuba's chapter?**
a) The clear, ordered thinking a program is built from  b) Data and types, then variables, expressions and counters  c) States it exactly, splits it into exact steps, orders the steps  d) Gaps and maybe-steps
