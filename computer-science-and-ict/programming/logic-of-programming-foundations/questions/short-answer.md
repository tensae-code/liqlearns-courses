# Logic of Programming — Foundations — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is programming logic?**
**Answer: The clear, ordered thinking a program is built from**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three things does it do to a problem?**
**Answer: States it exactly, splits it into exact steps, orders the steps**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a logical solution have none of?**
**Answer: Gaps and maybe-steps**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What decides every case in a logical solution?**
**Answer: The steps themselves**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In what forms is the same logic later written?**
**Answer: A flowchart, pseudocode, or any language**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three habits of logic at work.**
**Answer: Order, exactness and completeness**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the order rule?**
**Answer: A step that uses a result comes after the step that makes it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does exactness demand of each step?**
**Answer: One action the computer can do, nothing left to guess**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does completeness cover?**
**Answer: All the problem's cases, including the awkward ones**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What comes after logic in Cuba's chapter?**
**Answer: Data and types, then variables, expressions and counters**
