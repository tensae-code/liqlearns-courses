# Hands-on — Setting Conditions with Operators on Variables

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 4, setting a condition using mathematical operators on a variable (random-numbers product script; nested join blocks) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach setting conditions with operators on variables to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
