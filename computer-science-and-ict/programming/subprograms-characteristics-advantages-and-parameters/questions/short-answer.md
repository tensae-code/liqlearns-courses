# Subprograms — Characteristics, Advantages and Parameters — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a subprogram?**
**Answer: A named, self-standing piece of a program that does one job and is called where needed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name its characteristics.**
**Answer: Own name and local names; written once, called many times; control goes and returns; meets the rest only through parameters/return**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does control return after a call?**
**Answer: To the place after the call**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A subprogram is a sealed room with what?**
**Answer: A serving hatch — its parameters**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 12 Anaptyxi Efarmogon, Chapter 10**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Count the advantages.**
**Answer: Shorter reading, separate testing, reuse, team work**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is reuse?**
**Answer: A proved piece serves many programs**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do parameters make possible between people?**
**Answer: Team work — different hands writing caller and piece apart**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are parameters, written down?**
**Answer: The list of values the subprogram needs from its caller, declared in its heading**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where do caller and piece meet?**
**Answer: Only at the call**
