# Subprograms — Characteristics, Advantages and Parameters — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a subprogram?** → **A named, self-standing piece of a program that does one job and is called where needed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name its characteristics.** → **Own name and local names; written once, called many times; control goes and returns; meets the rest only through parameters/return**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does control return after a call?** → **To the place after the call**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A subprogram is a sealed room with what?** → **A serving hatch — its parameters**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches it?** → **Greece Grade 12 Anaptyxi Efarmogon, Chapter 10**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Count the advantages.** → **Shorter reading, separate testing, reuse, team work**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is reuse?** → **A proved piece serves many programs**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do parameters make possible between people?** → **Team work — different hands writing caller and piece apart**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are parameters, written down?** → **The list of values the subprogram needs from its caller, declared in its heading**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where do caller and piece meet?** → **Only at the call**
