# Subprograms — Characteristics, Advantages and Parameters — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A named, self-standing piece of a program that does one job and is called where needed**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: To the place after the call** — as the answer to: Name its characteristics.
**Answer: False — Own name and local names; written once, called many times; control goes and returns; meets the rest only through parameters/return**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: To the place after the call**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Greece Grade 12 Anaptyxi Efarmogon, Chapter 10** — as the answer to: A subprogram is a sealed room with what?
**Answer: False — A serving hatch — its parameters**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Greece Grade 12 Anaptyxi Efarmogon, Chapter 10**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A proved piece serves many programs** — as the answer to: Count the advantages.
**Answer: False — Shorter reading, separate testing, reuse, team work**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A proved piece serves many programs**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The list of values the subprogram needs from its caller, declared in its heading** — as the answer to: What do parameters make possible between people?
**Answer: False — Team work — different hands writing caller and piece apart**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The list of values the subprogram needs from its caller, declared in its heading**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A named, self-standing piece of a program that does one job and is called where needed** — as the answer to: Where do caller and piece meet?
