# Hands-on — Using a Standard Library

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Shop the shelves', 'For each job, name the library member you would call and what you hand it and receive: a random whole number for a dice game; today's date printed on a page; the larger of two marks; 2 to the power 10.')

## Task two
('Read the label', 'A pupil expects Math.round behaviour from Math.floor and gets totals one less than expected. Explain the label difference (floor, round) and correct the program's calls.')
