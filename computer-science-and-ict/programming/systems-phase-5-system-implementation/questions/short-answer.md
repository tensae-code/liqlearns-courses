# Systems Phase 5 — System Implementation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does system implementation do?**
**Answer: Installs the developed system in the organisation's real work**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is implementation's heart?**
**Answer: The changeover — old way handing over to new**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to the old system's data?**
**Answer: It is converted and carried into the new files**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three changeover strategies.**
**Answer: Direct, parallel, and phased or pilot**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which is safest, and what is its cost?**
**Answer: Parallel — both systems run together until the new is trusted; safest but dearest**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When are users trained?**
**Answer: Before the switch — and helped through the first days**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What documents must users be given?**
**Answer: Documents that say how the new way runs**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What are most changeover 'system failures'?**
**Answer: People meeting an unpractised way of working on a busy morning**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When does the phase end?**
**Answer: When the new way is simply the way — old system retired, data safe, users at home**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Fiji count training inside the phase?**
**Answer: Because a system is not implemented until its people can work it**
