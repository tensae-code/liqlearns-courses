# Systems Phase 5 — System Implementation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does system implementation do?**
a) Installs the developed system in the organisation's real work  b) The changeover — old way handing over to new  c) It is converted and carried into the new files  d) Direct, parallel, and phased or pilot
**Answer: a) Installs the developed system in the organisation's real work**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is implementation's heart?**
a) It is converted and carried into the new files  b) The changeover — old way handing over to new  c) Direct, parallel, and phased or pilot  d) Parallel — both systems run together until the new is trusted; safest but dearest
**Answer: b) The changeover — old way handing over to new**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to the old system's data?**
a) Direct, parallel, and phased or pilot  b) Parallel — both systems run together until the new is trusted; safest but dearest  c) It is converted and carried into the new files  d) Before the switch — and helped through the first days
**Answer: c) It is converted and carried into the new files**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three changeover strategies.**
a) Parallel — both systems run together until the new is trusted; safest but dearest  b) Before the switch — and helped through the first days  c) Documents that say how the new way runs  d) Direct, parallel, and phased or pilot
**Answer: d) Direct, parallel, and phased or pilot**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which is safest, and what is its cost?**
a) Parallel — both systems run together until the new is trusted; safest but dearest  b) Before the switch — and helped through the first days  c) Documents that say how the new way runs  d) People meeting an unpractised way of working on a busy morning
**Answer: a) Parallel — both systems run together until the new is trusted; safest but dearest**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When are users trained?**
a) Documents that say how the new way runs  b) Before the switch — and helped through the first days  c) People meeting an unpractised way of working on a busy morning  d) When the new way is simply the way — old system retired, data safe, users at home
**Answer: b) Before the switch — and helped through the first days**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What documents must users be given?**
a) People meeting an unpractised way of working on a busy morning  b) When the new way is simply the way — old system retired, data safe, users at home  c) Documents that say how the new way runs  d) Because a system is not implemented until its people can work it
**Answer: c) Documents that say how the new way runs**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What are most changeover 'system failures'?**
a) When the new way is simply the way — old system retired, data safe, users at home  b) Because a system is not implemented until its people can work it  c) Installs the developed system in the organisation's real work  d) People meeting an unpractised way of working on a busy morning

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When does the phase end?**
a) When the new way is simply the way — old system retired, data safe, users at home  b) Because a system is not implemented until its people can work it  c) Installs the developed system in the organisation's real work  d) The changeover — old way handing over to new
