# Hands-on — From User Requirements to Technical Specification

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Translate three
Users say: 'easy to use', 'never lose my work', 'works on my old phone'. Translate each into one exact, measurable specification line.

## Spot the gap
Your companion writes a five-line specification. You interview 'the user' (your companion in role) and find one requirement missing from it.
