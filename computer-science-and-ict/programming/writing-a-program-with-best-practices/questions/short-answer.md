# Writing a Program with Best Practices — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the first family of best practices.**
**Answer: Meaningful names, comments, and named constants**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What should a name do?**
**Answer: Say what the thing is for — totalMark, not x2**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What should comments explain?**
**Answer: Why a part exists and what a non-obvious step does — not what the code plainly says**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why are constants named?**
**Answer: So no unexplained numbers wander the code**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Who is a program's next reader, often?**
**Answer: Its writer, months later, when something must change**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the second family?**
**Answer: Shape — layout and structure**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What should indentation mirror?**
**Answer: Nesting — so bodies and branches are seen at a glance**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does 'one structure per job' forbid?**
**Answer: Jumps that tangle the flow — structures stay in their standard shapes**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What order does development follow?**
**Answer: Algorithm first, code translated from it, tests planned and run**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is Malaysia's production standard?**
**Answer: The program passes its tests and can be handed to another programmer without apology or tour**
