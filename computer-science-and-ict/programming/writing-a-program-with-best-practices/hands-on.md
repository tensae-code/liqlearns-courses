# Hands-on — Writing a Program with Best Practices

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Rehabilitate a program', 'You are given a working but unreadable program (names a, b, c; no comments; flat indentation; a bare 50 in three places). Rewrite it to Malaysia's best practices without changing what it does, and list each practice you applied.')

## Task two
('Review a partner's', 'Review a partner's recent program against the two families — names/comments/constants, then indentation/structure/order. Give two strengths and two repairs, each tied to a named practice.')
