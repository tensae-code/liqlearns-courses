# Scratch — Changing Direction and Duplicating a Script — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What decides where a move takes the sprite?**
**Answer: The way the sprite faces — its direction**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two ways direction is controlled in this lesson.**
**Answer: Direction blocks, and the keyboard arrows**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is direction taught separately from motion?**
**Answer: Journeys blamed on the move block are usually lost at the facing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does duplicating do?**
**Answer: Copies a whole script at once — the stack and every block in it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where can a duplicated script go?**
**Answer: Again on this sprite, or onto another**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is done after duplicating?**
**Answer: Adjust what differs between the copies**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What travels with a duplicated script?**
**Answer: Its mistakes too**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must be done to the original first?**
**Answer: Test it before copying**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which Motion lesson is this?**
**Answer: Motion Blocks (2)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 5 Digital Explorer Term 1, Unit 2 Lesson 3**
