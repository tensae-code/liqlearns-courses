# Hands-on — Scratch — Changing Direction and Duplicating a Script

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Steer it', 'Make the arrow keys steer a sprite around a square path. Write which key does which facing.')

## Task two
('Build once, copy once', 'Build a short behaviour, duplicate it onto a second sprite, and list what you changed in the copy — and what you checked in the original first.')
