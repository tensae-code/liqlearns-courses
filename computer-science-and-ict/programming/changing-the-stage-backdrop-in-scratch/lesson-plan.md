## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Choosing a backdrop from the library** — The **backdrop** is the picture that fills the **stage** behind the sprites. Scratch keeps a **library** of backdrops, arranged **by category** — outdoors, indoors, space and more. Pick the category that fits your story, choose its backdrop, and the stage changes at once.
2. **Drawing one, uploading one — and changing the sprite** — A backdrop can also be **drawn new** in the paint editor, or **uploaded** from a picture file on the computer. The **sprite** changes the same three ways — library, drawn, uploaded — so both the actor and its stage can be entirely your own.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
