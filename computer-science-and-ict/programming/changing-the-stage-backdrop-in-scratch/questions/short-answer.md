# Changing the Stage Backdrop in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a backdrop?**
**Answer: The picture that fills the stage behind the sprites**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is the library arranged?**
**Answer: By category — outdoors, indoors, space and more**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when you choose a library backdrop?**
**Answer: The stage changes at once**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are the three ways to get a backdrop?**
**Answer: Choose from the library, draw a new one, upload a picture**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where is a new backdrop drawn?**
**Answer: In the paint editor**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is an uploaded backdrop made from?**
**Answer: A picture file on the computer**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What else changes the same three ways?**
**Answer: The sprite**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What two things can be entirely your own?**
**Answer: The actor (sprite) and its stage (backdrop)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What should the category fit?**
**Answer: Your story**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What stands behind the action in a story?**
**Answer: The backdrop**
