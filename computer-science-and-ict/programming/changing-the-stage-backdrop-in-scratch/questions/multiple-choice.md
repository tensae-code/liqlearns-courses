# Changing the Stage Backdrop in Scratch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a backdrop?**
a) The picture that fills the stage behind the sprites  b) Choose from the library, draw a new one, upload a picture  c) A picture file on the computer  d) The actor (sprite) and its stage (backdrop)
**Answer: a) The picture that fills the stage behind the sprites**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is the library arranged?**
a) In the paint editor  b) By category — outdoors, indoors, space and more  c) The sprite  d) Your story
**Answer: b) By category — outdoors, indoors, space and more**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when you choose a library backdrop?**
a) A picture file on the computer  b) The actor (sprite) and its stage (backdrop)  c) The stage changes at once  d) The backdrop
**Answer: c) The stage changes at once**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are the three ways to get a backdrop?**
a) The sprite  b) Your story  c) The picture that fills the stage behind the sprites  d) Choose from the library, draw a new one, upload a picture
**Answer: d) Choose from the library, draw a new one, upload a picture**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where is a new backdrop drawn?**
a) In the paint editor  b) The actor (sprite) and its stage (backdrop)  c) The backdrop  d) By category — outdoors, indoors, space and more
**Answer: a) In the paint editor**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is an uploaded backdrop made from?**
a) Your story  b) A picture file on the computer  c) The picture that fills the stage behind the sprites  d) The stage changes at once
**Answer: b) A picture file on the computer**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What else changes the same three ways?**
a) The backdrop  b) By category — outdoors, indoors, space and more  c) The sprite  d) Choose from the library, draw a new one, upload a picture
**Answer: c) The sprite**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What two things can be entirely your own?**
a) The picture that fills the stage behind the sprites  b) The stage changes at once  c) In the paint editor  d) The actor (sprite) and its stage (backdrop)
**Answer: d) The actor (sprite) and its stage (backdrop)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What should the category fit?**
a) Your story  b) By category — outdoors, indoors, space and more  c) Choose from the library, draw a new one, upload a picture  d) A picture file on the computer
**Answer: a) Your story**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What stands behind the action in a story?**
a) The stage changes at once  b) The backdrop  c) In the paint editor  d) The sprite
**Answer: b) The backdrop**
