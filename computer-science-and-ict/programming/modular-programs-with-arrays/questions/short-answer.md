# Modular Programs with Arrays — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a modular program divide its work among?**
**Answer: Subprograms — each doing one job well**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the natural modules around an array?**
**Answer: Fill it, process it, print it — the array's career**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is each module tested?**
**Answer: On its own, with the array handed to it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why must a fill module not also average?**
**Answer: A fault would have two homes and a test could not tell them apart**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the process module work out?**
**Answer: A total, an average, a highest — workings on the array**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the main program shrink to?**
**Answer: A conductor — declare the array, call the modules in order**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is passed to each module?**
**Answer: The array, and its size**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do worked-out values reach the conductor?**
**Answer: Returned, or left in shared results as the design states**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Change how printing looks — what is opened?**
**Answer: Only the print module**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) In Malaysia's image, what are array, modules and main program?**
**Answer: The shared stage, the actors, and the order of the play**
