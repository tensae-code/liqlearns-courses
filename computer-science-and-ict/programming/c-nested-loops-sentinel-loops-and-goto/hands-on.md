# Hands-on — C Nested Loops Sentinel Loops and Goto

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Chapter 12 §12.4 Nested Loop (Example 3: the asterisk triangle — a while inside a for), sentinel-controlled loops (Example 4: average marks of a class until a negative number; type casting with (float)) and §12.5 goto Statement (Example 5: retry until a positive number for the square root) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach c nested loops sentinel loops and goto to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
