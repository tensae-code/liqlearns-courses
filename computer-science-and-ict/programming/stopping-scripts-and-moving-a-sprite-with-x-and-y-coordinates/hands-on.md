# Hands-on — Stopping Scripts — and Moving a Sprite with X and Y Coordinates

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The right stop

Build a sprite with two forever scripts. End one with stop this script from a third action, prove the other still runs, then use stop all and prove nothing runs.

## Coordinate steps

Send a sprite to a start place by its coordinates, then move it 50 right, 30 up, 50 left and 30 down by changing x and y. Say the four changes before you run them.
