# Stopping Scripts — and Moving a Sprite with X and Y Coordinates — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) At game over, music (in another sprite) and all play must end. Which stop, and why not stop this script?**
**Answer:** A sound answer: stop all, because the ending must reach every sprite and script; stop this script would leave the music and other scripts running.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Write the coordinate changes that move a sprite left then up.**
**Answer:** A sound answer: make x smaller (left), then make y bigger (up) — by the amounts wanted.
