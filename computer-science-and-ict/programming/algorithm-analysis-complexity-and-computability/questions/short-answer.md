# Algorithm Analysis — Complexity and Computability — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does algorithm analysis study?**
**Answer: An algorithm's time and memory needs as the problem's size grows — without running it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is complexity?**
**Answer: How the needed steps grow with the input**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the exploding growth called?**
**Answer: Exponential — doubling with every added value**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) By what should an algorithm be chosen?**
**Answer: By its growth, not by its first small test**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 11 Eisagogi Epistimis H/Y, Ch.2.2**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is computability?**
**Answer: The theory of what can be computed at all**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the chapter's hard lesson?**
**Answer: Some clear problems have no algorithm that always ends with the right answer**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does intractable mean here?**
**Answer: Computable in principle, but the exact cost outgrows any real machine**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does knowing the boundary tell the programmer?**
**Answer: Which problems to solve exactly, which to approximate, which to stop attacking head-on**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) The theory of computation sits inside which science division?**
**Answer: Theoretical computer science**
