# Video script — Putting a Video into Scratch by Converting It to GIF

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The route starts with respect and brevity: a **short clip of 20–30 seconds, downloaded respecting the owner’s rights**, then **converted to GIF with an online converter**. The GIF is a strip of still pictures — which is exactly what Scratch costumes are.
**GIF has no sound**, so the clip’s **sound is recorded separately and added** as the project’s own sound. Inside Scratch, the **GIF’s costumes stepped through in a loop make the motion** — the video plays because the costumes change fast enough to fool the eye, with the added sound beside them.
