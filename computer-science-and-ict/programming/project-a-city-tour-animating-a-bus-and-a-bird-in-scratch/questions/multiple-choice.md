# Project — A City Tour: Animating a Bus and a Bird in Scratch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What stage does the project build first?**
a) A city backdrop — streets, buildings, sky  b) A bus on the road and a bird in the sky  c) The bus where its route begins; the bird where its flight begins  d) Its costumes alternate to flap its wings

a) The bus where its route begins; the bird where its flight begins  b) A bus on the road and a bird in the sky  c) Its costumes alternate to flap its wings  d) The bus's look

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does each traveller start?**
