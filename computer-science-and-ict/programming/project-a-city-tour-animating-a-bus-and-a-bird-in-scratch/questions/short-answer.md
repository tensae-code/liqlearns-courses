# Project — A City Tour: Animating a Bus and a Bird in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What stage does the project build first?**
**Answer: A city backdrop — streets, buildings, sky**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which two sprites travel in it?**
**Answer: A bus on the road and a bird in the sky**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does each traveller start?**
**Answer: The bus where its route begins; the bird where its flight begins**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does the bird's flight show in its costumes?**
**Answer: Its costumes alternate to flap its wings**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What stays steady as the city passes?**
**Answer: The bus's look**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What starts the tour running?**
**Answer: Its events — the green-flag start**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does the bus travel its route?**
**Answer: Moving, pointing and turning at the city's corners**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does the bird travel?**
**Answer: Flying across, flapping its costumes**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do sounds do in the scene?**
**Answer: The bus's engine or bell and the bird's call ride with it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the project's check?**
**Answer: A watcher sees one living tour, not two exercises**
