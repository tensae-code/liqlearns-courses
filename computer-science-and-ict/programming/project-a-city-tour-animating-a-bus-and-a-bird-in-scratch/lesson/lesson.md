# Project — A City Tour: Animating a Bus and a Bird in Scratch

## Do it first

Grade 4's first project animates a city: a bus tours its streets below while a bird crosses its sky. Motion, looks, sound and events — the year's block families — work together in one living scene.

## Sub lesson 1 — Building the city — backdrops and travellers

The project builds its stage first: a **city backdrop** — streets, buildings, sky — with a **bus sprite** on its road and a **bird sprite** in its sky. Each traveller is personalised and placed at its start: the bus where its route begins, the bird where its flight begins. Costumes do the quiet work of animation here: the bird's **costumes alternate** to flap its wings, and the bus's look stays steady as the city passes it by.

## Sub lesson 2 — Running the tour — motion, sound and events together

The tour runs when its **events** start it: the **bus moves along its route** — moving, pointing and turning at the city's corners — while the **bird flies across**, flapping. **Sounds** ride with the scene: the bus's engine or bell, the bird's call, set at volumes that let both be heard. The project's check is the scene alive: a watcher sees a tour, not two exercises — road and sky moving together, each traveller in its own element, from the flag to the route's end.
