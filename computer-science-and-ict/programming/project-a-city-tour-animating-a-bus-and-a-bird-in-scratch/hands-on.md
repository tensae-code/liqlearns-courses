# Hands-on — Project — A City Tour: Animating a Bus and a Bird in Scratch

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Plan the route
Draw a simple city backdrop on paper: one road with two corners, buildings, sky. Draw the bus's route and the bird's flight path on it, and mark each start.

## Build the tour
Build the scene in Scratch and run it from the flag: bus on its route with turns, bird flapping across, one sound for each. If one traveller finishes long before the other, adjust a wait or a speed and run it again.
