# Programming a Robot From a Distance — Bugs and Telegraphy — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is télégraphie in this séance?**
**Answer: Commanding an automaton from a distance, by sent messages**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What can you not do at a distance?**
**Answer: Point, or correct mid-run**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must travel inside the message?**
**Answer: The whole intention**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three arrival questions.**
**Answer: What did I send? What did it do? Where do they differ?**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What two topics does the séance pair?**
**Answer: Bug and telegraphy**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where can a distant fault live?**
**Answer: In the writing, the sending, the reading, or the machine**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must be kept, as evidence?**
**Answer: The sent program — your copy**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How many things change per new message?**
**Answer: One**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What should each reply report?**
**Answer: Position and facing**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the message log, for distant programmers?**
**Answer: The only eyes you have**
