# Composing Music with Note Blocks in Scratch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does set instrument choose?**
a) The voice the notes play in  b) A chosen note for a chosen length  c) Notes and rests together — the rhythm  d) Looping it
**Answer: a) The voice the notes play in**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does play note for beats sound?**
a) Silence for its beats  b) A chosen note for a chosen length  c) Set instrument, play note, rest  d) The pattern
**Answer: b) A chosen note for a chosen length**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a rest hold?**
a) Notes and rests together — the rhythm  b) Looping it  c) Silence for its beats  d) With small changes
**Answer: c) Silence for its beats**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a melody, beyond the notes?**
a) Set instrument, play note, rest  b) The pattern  c) Programming with the ears  d) Notes and rests together — the rhythm
**Answer: d) Notes and rests together — the rhythm**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What three blocks open the composing lesson?**
a) Set instrument, play note, rest  b) Looping it  c) With small changes  d) The moment the program runs
**Answer: a) Set instrument, play note, rest**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What turns a short sequence into a tune?**
a) The pattern  b) Looping it  c) Programming with the ears  d) The voice the notes play in
**Answer: b) Looping it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the listener recognise when a tune loops?**
a) With small changes  b) The moment the program runs  c) The pattern  d) A chosen note for a chosen length
**Answer: c) The pattern**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How can a tune grow between repeats?**
a) Programming with the ears  b) The voice the notes play in  c) Silence for its beats  d) With small changes
**Answer: d) With small changes**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is composing, in this lesson’s words?**
a) Programming with the ears  b) The moment the program runs  c) A chosen note for a chosen length  d) Notes and rests together — the rhythm
**Answer: a) Programming with the ears**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is every composing block heard?**
a) The voice the notes play in  b) The moment the program runs  c) Silence for its beats  d) Set instrument, play note, rest
**Answer: b) The moment the program runs**
