# Signing Up and Logging In — Names and Passwords in Lists — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where are subscriber names kept?**
**Answer: In one list**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where are their passwords kept?**
**Answer: In a second list, at the same positions**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does “same positions” mean?**
**Answer: Position 4’s password belongs to position 4’s name**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to a name already taken?**
**Answer: It is refused**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why must a taken name be refused?**
**Answer: Because one position must mean one person**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does login find first?**
**Answer: The name’s position in the names list**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does login check next?**
**Answer: The password stored at that position**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What changes by condition as you log in?**
**Answer: The background**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do three failed attempts do in the project?**
**Answer: They delete the name and force a new registration**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of system is this lesson’s build?**
**Answer: A library sign-up and login system**
