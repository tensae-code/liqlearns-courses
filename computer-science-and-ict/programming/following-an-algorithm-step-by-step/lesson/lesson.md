# Following an Algorithm Step by Step

## Do it first

Your friend wrote a plan. Now you follow it: step 1 — do it. Step 2 — do it. No skipping, no swapping. This lesson teaches executing: carrying an algorithm out, step by step.

## Following an Algorithm Step by Step

To **execute** an algorithm is to **carry it out**: follow its steps **one at a time, in order**, and do each step fully before moving to the next. The person (or later, the machine) who follows the steps is the **executor**.

An executor does exactly what the step says — no more, no less, and no guessing. If a step says 'draw a square', the executor draws a square, not a house. If the steps are followed exactly and the result is wrong, the fault is in the **plan**, not in the executor — the algorithm must be fixed.

Executing well means three habits: read the whole plan once before starting, tick each step as you finish it, and stop at once if a step is unclear — an unclear step is reported, not guessed. Step by step, in order, to the end: that is execution.
