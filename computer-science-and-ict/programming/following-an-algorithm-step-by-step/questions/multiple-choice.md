# Following an Algorithm Step by Step — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Executing an algorithm means —**
a) writing it  b) carrying out its steps one at a time, in order  c) guessing its steps
**Answer: b) carrying out its steps one at a time, in order** — The executor follows the plan to the end.

**2. The one who follows the steps is called the —**
a) author  b) executor  c) receiver
**Answer: b) executor** — Person or machine — the follower executes.

**3. If exact following gives a wrong result, the fault is in —**
a) the executor  b) the plan  c) the paper
**Answer: b) the plan** — The algorithm must be fixed, not the careful executor.

**4. When a step is unclear, the executor should —**
a) guess quietly  b) skip it  c) stop and report it
**Answer: c) stop and report it** — Unclear steps are reported, not guessed.
