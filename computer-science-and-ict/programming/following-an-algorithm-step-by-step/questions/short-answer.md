# Following an Algorithm Step by Step — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Name the three good habits of executing.**
**Answer:** Read the whole plan first; tick each step as you finish it; stop and report an unclear step instead of guessing.

**2. Two executors follow the same drawing algorithm and get different drawings. What likely happened?**
**Answer:** At least one did not follow the steps exactly — a step was skipped, swapped or guessed.
