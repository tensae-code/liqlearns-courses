# Following an Algorithm Step by Step — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. An executor may swap steps if it seems quicker.**
**Answer: False** — Steps are followed in their order.

**2. Reading the whole plan before starting is a good executing habit.**
**Answer: True** — Know the whole plan, then tick step by step.

**3. An executor does exactly what the step says — no more, no less.**
**Answer: True** — Exact following is what makes results match.
