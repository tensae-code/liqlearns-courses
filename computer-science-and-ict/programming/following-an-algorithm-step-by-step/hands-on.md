# Hands-on — Following an Algorithm Step by Step

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Be the executor
Follow a 5-step drawing algorithm exactly — each step fully, ticked, in order. Compare your drawing with a friend's: exact executors get the same drawing.

## Find the fault
Your teacher gives an algorithm with one wrong step. Execute it exactly, show the wrong result, and name the step that must be fixed.
