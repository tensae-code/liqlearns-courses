# Hands-on — ScratchJr — First Block Programs on a Tablet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**First program** — On a tablet, program scene 1 of your emotion story: one trigger, at least three action blocks. Run it for a partner — they read back what the program did, block by block.

## Task two
**Grow a page** — Add the second scene as a new page with its own background and script. Present the two-page story; the class names where the plan (storyboard) and the program part ways, if anywhere.
