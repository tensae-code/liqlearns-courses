# End-of-Year Project — Picnic Story and Animation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What three skills does the picnic project join?**
a) Typing a story in Notepad, drawing in Scratch and animating in Scratch  b) In Notepad, the plain text program  c) So the words come first, with no formatting to hide behind  d) Who went, where the picnic was and what happened
**Answer: a) Typing a story in Notepad, drawing in Scratch and animating in Scratch**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the picnic story typed?**
a) So the words come first, with no formatting to hide behind  b) In Notepad, the plain text program  c) Who went, where the picnic was and what happened  d) Saved as a file with a clear name in the class folder
**Answer: b) In Notepad, the plain text program**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why Notepad for the story?**
a) Who went, where the picnic was and what happened  b) Saved as a file with a clear name in the class folder  c) So the words come first, with no formatting to hide behind  d) A backdrop for the picnic place
