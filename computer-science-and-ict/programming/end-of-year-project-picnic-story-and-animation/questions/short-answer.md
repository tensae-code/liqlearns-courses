# End-of-Year Project — Picnic Story and Animation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What three skills does the picnic project join?**
**Answer: Typing a story in Notepad, drawing in Scratch and animating in Scratch**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the picnic story typed?**
**Answer: In Notepad, the plain text program**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why Notepad for the story?**
**Answer: So the words come first, with no formatting to hide behind**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is planned before typing?**
**Answer: Who went, where the picnic was and what happened**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is the finished story kept?**
**Answer: Saved as a file with a clear name in the class folder**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the Scratch part begin with?**
**Answer: A backdrop for the picnic place**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Who and what appear as sprites?**
**Answer: The people and things in the story**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which blocks make the scene move?**
**Answer: Motion and looks blocks**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is the project finished?**
**Answer: It is shown to the class — story file and animation together**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the project prove?**
**Answer: That the year's separate skills work together in one finished piece**
