# Hands-on — End-of-Year Project — Picnic Story and Animation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Write the story', 'Plan a short picnic story — who, where, what happened — type it in Notepad and save it with a clear name.')

## Task two
('Animate one scene', 'Build the picnic backdrop and one sprite in Scratch and animate one moment of your story with motion blocks.')
