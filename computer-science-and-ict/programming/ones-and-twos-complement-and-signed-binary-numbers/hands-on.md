# Hands-on — Ones and Twos Complement and Signed Binary Numbers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Flip and add
Draw an 8-box register. Write +6, flip every bit for the ones complement, then add 1 for the twos complement. Repeat for +11 and +20, checking each pair of rows adds back to zero when summed.

## Subtract by adding
Compute 15 − 9 and 8 − 13 in 8-bit twos complement on paper. For the negative answer, flip and add 1 to read its size, and state the final answers in decimal.
