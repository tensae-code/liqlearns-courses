# Ones and Twos Complement and Signed Binary Numbers — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) To subtract B from A, the computer adds the ____ complement of B to A.**
**Answer: twos — A + (twos complement of B) is A − B, done by the ordinary adder.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) An 8-bit signed number in twos complement runs from ____ to +127.**
**Answer: −128 — Seven bits of size plus the sign bit give one more negative value than positive.**
