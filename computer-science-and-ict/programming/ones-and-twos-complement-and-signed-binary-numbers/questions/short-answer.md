# Ones and Twos Complement and Signed Binary Numbers — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Work out 12 − 7 in 8-bit binary using twos complement, showing each step.**
**Answer: 7 is 00000111; flip the bits: 11111000; add 1: 11111001 (that is −7). 12 is 00001100. Add: 00001100 + 11111001 = 1 00000101. Discard the carry beyond 8 bits: 00000101 = 5. Correct: 12 − 7 = 5.**
