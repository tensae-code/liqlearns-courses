# Video script — Ones and Twos Complement and Signed Binary Numbers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A machine has no minus sign. The first bit is the sign: 0 plus, 1 minus.

Flip every bit: ones complement. Flip and add 1: twos complement.

Now subtraction is just addition. Add the twos complement, drop the extra carry — done.
