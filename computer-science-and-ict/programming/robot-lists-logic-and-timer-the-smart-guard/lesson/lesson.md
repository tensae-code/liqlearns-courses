# Robot Lists, Logic and Timer — the Smart Guard

## Do it first

The last robot of Kuwait's school strand stands guard. Its program needs memory (lists), judgement (logic) and time (a timer) together. This lesson is those three tools and the guard they build.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lists and logic — the guard's memory and judgement

Open Roberta Lab programs can keep **lists**: a **number list** (distances measured on patrol) or a **colour list** (the colours a guard route passes), built, added to, and **searched** — is this colour one of the allowed ones? is this distance in the recorded set? Over the lists sit the **logical operations** — AND, OR, NOT — combining the senses' answers into one decision: sound the alarm when (a stranger's colour appears) AND (patrol time says the gate should be shut).

In your mind's eye: lists and logic — the guard's memory and judgement.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Timer — and the Smart Guard

The **Timer** gives the program the time: how long since the patrol began, whether a pause has lasted its seconds — a guard's rounds are timed work. The **Smart Guard Robot** is the build that spends all three: it patrols with its lists recording the route, its logic deciding what counts as wrong, and its timer pacing the rounds and the alarms. Memory, judgement and time — the series' robot graduates from following lines to keeping watch.
