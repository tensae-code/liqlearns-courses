# Conditional Statements in PHP — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does an if statement do in PHP?**
**Answer: Runs its block only when its condition is true**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do else and elseif add?**
**Answer: Else offers the other road; elseif chains further choices**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the dictionary page's three decision branches**
**Answer: Empty term — refuse with a message; term already exists — say so; otherwise store it and confirm**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must the branches together do?**
**Answer: Cover the world the page will actually meet**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three daily families of web conditions**
**Answer: Presence, equality and comparison, and permission**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the permission condition protect on Add_Term?**
**Answer: It separates the page's public face from its working face — only the authorised user may add**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What fault does `=` for `==` cause?**
**Answer: Assigning instead of comparing — the condition stops testing and starts setting**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when branches are in the wrong order?**
**Answer: The general case swallows the specific one before it can be reached**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What arrives on the first day, in the book's warning?**
**Answer: The case nobody wrote a branch for**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Egypt teach PHP conditionals inside the project?**
**Answer: A conditional is only understood when something real depends on the branch taken**
