# Hands-on — Loops with the Robot Artist

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Letter by loop
On squared paper, write the strokes for a letter as a loop: the actions inside, and the count outside. Trade letters with a partner and draw from their loop only.

## Count the repeats
A partner runs a loop drawing slowly. Tap once per repeat and call out the total when it stops — then check the loop's number.
