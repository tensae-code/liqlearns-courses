# Hands-on — Robot Course Driving Project

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Plan a course** — Design a small test course on paper with four stages. For each, write the movement or sense demanded and the commands you will use.

## Task two
**Tune a run** — Run your robot (or a simulation of one) on a course. Keep a tuning log: each run, the failure, the one change, the result.
