# Algorithms for Artificial Intelligence — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an AI algorithm, in the course's own shape?**
**Answer: Inputs, steps and outputs — a procedure, like every algorithm**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How do AI algorithms differ from ordinary ones?**
**Answer: They handle cases nobody enumerated, following a pattern from examples instead of a rule for every case**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do AI algorithms work?**
**Answer: Where the rules cannot all be written down — recognising, predicting, choosing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a search algorithm do?**
**Answer: Finds a path or a best move by trying possibilities in an order**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a prediction algorithm do?**
**Answer: Applies a fitted pattern to new data**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a learning algorithm do?**
**Answer: Adjusts its own pattern from results, improving as examples accumulate**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does search fail?**
**Answer: It explodes when the possibilities multiply**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does prediction fail?**
**Answer: It inherits its data's blind spots**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is learning only as honest as?**
**Answer: The results it is fed**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Egypt teach AI algorithms inside the Python unit?**
**Answer: The pupil is about to write algorithms and should know which kind AI's are**
