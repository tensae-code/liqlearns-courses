# Arduino C Program Structure — setup() and loop() — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an Arduino program called?**
**Answer: A sketch — written in the C language**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two functions does every sketch have?**
**Answer: setup() and loop()**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How often does setup() run?**
**Answer: Once, when the program starts**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What belongs in setup()?**
**Answer: One-time preparation — pin modes, starting serial, preparing the parts**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How often does loop() run?**
**Answer: Forever — when it finishes, the board calls it again, until power ends**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does void name?**
**Answer: A function that returns nothing**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does a statement end in Arduino C?**
**Answer: With a semicolon**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where do a function's statements live?**
**Answer: Inside curly braces — its block**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the standing beginner's fault?**
**Answer: A one-time act written in loop(), or a behaviour trapped in setup()**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is any sketch read at a glance?**
**Answer: By its rooms — setup for once, loop for always**
