# Records in Pascal and C++ — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a record?**
**Answer: A structured type whose named fields may be of different types**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is a field reached?**
**Answer: Through the record variable with a dot — p.mark**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Write the lesson's Pascal example type.**
**Answer: type TPupil = record name: string; mark: integer; paid: boolean; end;**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is C++'s record called?**
**Answer: struct**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does assigning one record to another do?**
**Answer: Copies every field at once**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a class of pupils, as data?**
**Answer: An array of records**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give a working pattern from the lesson.**
**Answer: Loop the array, test a field, gather by another — names of pupils with mark ≥ 9 and paid = true**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) model a date as a record.**
**Answer: Three fields — day, month, year — of fitting subrange types**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are fields, in the lesson's phrase?**
**Answer: The datum's anatomy, written down**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where do records meet files?**
**Answer: A library (or register) kept as a file of records**
