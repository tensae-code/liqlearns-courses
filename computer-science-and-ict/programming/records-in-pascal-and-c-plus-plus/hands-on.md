# Hands-on — Records in Pascal and C++

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Anatomy design** — Define a record for a book (title, author, year, pages, on loan) in both languages, declare two variables, and fill one's fields.

## Task two
**Class table** — Sketch the array-of-records for a class and write the loop that prints pupils with unpaid fees, field tests shown.
