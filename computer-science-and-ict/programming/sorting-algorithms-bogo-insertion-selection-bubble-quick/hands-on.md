# Hands-on — Sorting Algorithms — Bogo, Insertion, Selection, Bubble, Quick

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Human sorts** — The class sorts itself by height five ways — once per algorithm, with the method's rule announced and obeyed strictly. Time or count comparisons for each; crown methods by situation, not by loyalty.

## Task two
**Race the network** — Draw a 4-input sorting network from the annexes. Feed it all the class's test quadruples (worst case included) and prove — or repair — that it always sorts.
