# Hands-on — JSON and XML — Exchanging Data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The crew in space (connection permitting)
Write the program: fetch the ISS crew JSON from api.open-notify.org with urllib, parse it with json, and print how many people are in space and each name with their craft. Run it again tomorrow — the answer may have changed; that is what "live data" means.

## The film catalogue (offline)
Open films.xml in a text editor first and draw its tree on paper. Then parse it with ElementTree and print every film’s title and year in a loop. Check the program’s list against your drawing, element by element.
