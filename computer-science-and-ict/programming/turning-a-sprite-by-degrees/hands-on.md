# Hands-on — Turning a Sprite by Degrees

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Landmarks
Make the sprite turn a quarter, a half and a full turn in three scripts. After each, say which way it faces and why.

## The exact cat
Copy the guide's action: turn the cat 40° clockwise with the words "I am turning". Run it nine times in a loop of turns — where does the cat face at the end?
