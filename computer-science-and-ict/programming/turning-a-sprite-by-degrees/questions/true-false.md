# Turning a Sprite by Degrees — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "In what are turns measured?" is: Degrees**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How many degrees make a full turn?" is: 90°**
**Answer: False** — 360°

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a quarter turn?" is: 90°**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a half turn, and what does the sprite face?" is: The sprite's direction — it faces as it started**
**Answer: False** — 180° — the way it came from

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a full turn leave unchanged?" is: The sprite's direction — it faces as it started**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the turn block take?" is: To the right**
**Answer: False** — The number of degrees you type

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which way is clockwise?" is: To the right**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "By how much does the guide's cat turn?" is: I am turning**
**Answer: False** — 40° clockwise

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the cat say as it turns?" is: I am turning**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why is a number better than "a little"?" is: Degrees**
**Answer: False** — The turn is precise and can be repeated exactly
