# Turning a Sprite by Degrees — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In what are turns measured?**
a) Degrees  b) 180° — the way it came from  c) The number of degrees you type  d) 40° clockwise
**Answer: a) Degrees**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many degrees make a full turn?**
a) The sprite's direction — it faces as it started  b) 360°  c) To the right  d) I am turning
**Answer: b) 360°**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a quarter turn?**
a) The number of degrees you type  b) 40° clockwise  c) 90°  d) The turn is precise and can be repeated exactly
**Answer: c) 90°**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a half turn, and what does the sprite face?**
a) To the right  b) I am turning  c) Degrees  d) 180° — the way it came from
**Answer: d) 180° — the way it came from**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a full turn leave unchanged?**
a) The sprite's direction — it faces as it started  b) 40° clockwise  c) The turn is precise and can be repeated exactly  d) 360°
**Answer: a) The sprite's direction — it faces as it started**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the turn block take?**
a) I am turning  b) The number of degrees you type  c) Degrees  d) 90°
**Answer: b) The number of degrees you type**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which way is clockwise?**
a) The turn is precise and can be repeated exactly  b) 360°  c) To the right  d) 180° — the way it came from
**Answer: c) To the right**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) By how much does the guide's cat turn?**
a) Degrees  b) 90°  c) The sprite's direction — it faces as it started  d) 40° clockwise
**Answer: d) 40° clockwise**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the cat say as it turns?**
a) I am turning  b) 360°  c) 180° — the way it came from  d) The number of degrees you type
**Answer: a) I am turning**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a number better than "a little"?**
a) 90°  b) The turn is precise and can be repeated exactly  c) The sprite's direction — it faces as it started  d) To the right
**Answer: b) The turn is precise and can be repeated exactly**
