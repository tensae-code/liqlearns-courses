# Sumo Robot — Strategies to Stay in the Ring — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a SUMO robot's purpose?**
**Answer: To push its opponent out of the ring — and never cross the edge itself**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two body qualities win first?**
**Answer: Weight (low and spread) and balance (heavy parts centred)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is body taught before program?**
**Answer: No logic rescues a robot that falls over**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which sensor watches for the ring's edge?**
**Answer: Colour — the edge line on the floor**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which sensor finds the opponent?**
**Answer: Ultrasonic**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which sensor reports contact?**
**Answer: Touch**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the logic's first priority?**
**Answer: If the edge is near, escape it first**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is edge-escape first?**
**Answer: A robot that pushes its opponent out and follows has lost**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What follows edge-safety in the priorities?**
**Answer: Seek, charge, and push while the edge is far**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 9 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 3**
