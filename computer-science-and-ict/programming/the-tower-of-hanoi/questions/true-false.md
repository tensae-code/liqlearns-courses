# The Tower of Hanoi — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Three pegs, and a tower of disks of decreasing size**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Three pegs, and a tower of disks of decreasing size** — as the answer to: What is the task?
**Answer: False — Move the whole tower to another peg**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: One at a time**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: One at a time** — as the answer to: What is the one rule?
**Answer: False — Never place a larger disk on a smaller one**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Its solution is the cleanest first sight of a problem containing itself**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Its solution is the cleanest first sight of a problem containing itself** — as the answer to: What are the solution's three moves?
**Answer: False — Move the n−1 tower aside, move the biggest disk, move the n−1 tower on top**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: n = 1 — move the single disk**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: n = 1 — move the single disk** — as the answer to: How many moves do n disks take?
**Answer: False — 2ⁿ − 1**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: 7**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: 7** — as the answer to: What recurrence gives the cost?
**Answer: False — T(n) = 2T(n−1) + 1**
