# The Tower of Hanoi — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How many pegs and disks has the puzzle?**
**Answer: Three pegs, and a tower of disks of decreasing size**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the task?**
**Answer: Move the whole tower to another peg**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How many disks move at once?**
**Answer: One at a time**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the one rule?**
**Answer: Never place a larger disk on a smaller one**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the recursion strand teach it?**
**Answer: Its solution is the cleanest first sight of a problem containing itself**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are the solution's three moves?**
**Answer: Move the n−1 tower aside, move the biggest disk, move the n−1 tower on top**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the base case?**
**Answer: n = 1 — move the single disk**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How many moves do n disks take?**
**Answer: 2ⁿ − 1**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How many moves do 3 disks take?**
**Answer: 7**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What recurrence gives the cost?**
**Answer: T(n) = 2T(n−1) + 1**
