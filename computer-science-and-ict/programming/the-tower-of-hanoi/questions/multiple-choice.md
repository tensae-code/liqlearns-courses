# The Tower of Hanoi — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How many pegs and disks has the puzzle?**
a) Three pegs, and a tower of disks of decreasing size  b) Move the whole tower to another peg  c) One at a time  d) Never place a larger disk on a smaller one
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the task?**
a) Move the whole tower to another peg  b) One at a time  c) Never place a larger disk on a smaller one  d) Its solution is the cleanest first sight of a problem containing itself
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How many disks move at once?**
a) One at a time  b) Never place a larger disk on a smaller one  c) Its solution is the cleanest first sight of a problem containing itself  d) Move the n−1 tower aside, move the biggest disk, move the n−1 tower on top
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the one rule?**
a) Never place a larger disk on a smaller one  b) Its solution is the cleanest first sight of a problem containing itself  c) Move the n−1 tower aside, move the biggest disk, move the n−1 tower on top  d) n = 1 — move the single disk
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the recursion strand teach it?**
a) Its solution is the cleanest first sight of a problem containing itself  b) Move the n−1 tower aside, move the biggest disk, move the n−1 tower on top  c) n = 1 — move the single disk  d) 2ⁿ − 1
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are the solution's three moves?**
a) Move the n−1 tower aside, move the biggest disk, move the n−1 tower on top  b) n = 1 — move the single disk  c) 2ⁿ − 1  d) 7
**Answer: a**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the base case?**
a) n = 1 — move the single disk  b) 2ⁿ − 1  c) 7  d) T(n) = 2T(n−1) + 1
**Answer: a**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How many moves do n disks take?**
a) 2ⁿ − 1  b) 7  c) T(n) = 2T(n−1) + 1  d) Three pegs, and a tower of disks of decreasing size

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How many moves do 3 disks take?**
a) 7  b) T(n) = 2T(n−1) + 1  c) Three pegs, and a tower of disks of decreasing size  d) Move the whole tower to another peg

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What recurrence gives the cost?**
