# Hands-on — The Tower of Hanoi

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Move it** — With coins or books of three sizes, solve the 3-disk tower in 7 moves. Then try 4 disks and count — did you need 15?

## Task two
**Write the procedure** — Write move(n, from, to, spare) in pseudocode: its base case and its three steps, in order.
