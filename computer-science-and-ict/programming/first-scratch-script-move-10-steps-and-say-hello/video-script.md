# Video script — First Scratch script — move 10 steps and say hello

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **First Scratch script — move 10 steps and say hello**.

**the event block** — 'when flag clicked' from Events — dragged into the script area to start the script.

**move 10 steps** — the Motion block attached under the event block — each click moves the cat 10 more steps right.

**the Looks block** — the palette where the speech block is found, dragged in so the sprite says hello.

**the green flag** — clicked on the window or the script, it runs the script.

Now you try. Say each word. Say what it means.
