# First Scratch script — move 10 steps and say hello — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** _____ is 'when flag clicked' from Events — dragged into the script area to start the script. (Name the term.)
**Answer: the event block**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ is the Motion block attached under the event block — each click moves the cat 10 more steps right. (Name the term.)
**Answer: move 10 steps**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** _____ is the palette where the speech block is found, dragged in so the sprite says hello. (Name the term.)
**Answer: the Looks block**
