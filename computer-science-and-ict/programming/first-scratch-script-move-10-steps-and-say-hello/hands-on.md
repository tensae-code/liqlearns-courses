# Hands-on — First Scratch script — move 10 steps and say hello

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Paper blocks
Block cards on paper: assemble the script in order, then 'run' it by moving a drawn cat 10 steps per flag tap.

## Change one thing
Modify the script with one chosen Motion or Looks block. Say exactly what changed when it ran — the next lesson's opening exercise.
