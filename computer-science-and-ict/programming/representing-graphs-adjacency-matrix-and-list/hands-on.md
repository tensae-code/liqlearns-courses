# Hands-on — Representing Graphs — Adjacency Matrix and List

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Both ways** — Draw a graph of five vertices and six edges. Write its adjacency matrix and its adjacency list, and count the cells/entries each one uses.

## Task two
**Choose the representation** — A road network has 10,000 towns and 15,000 roads. Which representation, and why? What about a friendship graph where you mostly ask 'are these two friends?'
