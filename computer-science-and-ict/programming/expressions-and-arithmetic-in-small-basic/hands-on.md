# Hands-on — Expressions and Arithmetic in Small Basic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Read and work', 'Write a program that reads a rectangle's two sides with ReadNumber and prints its area and perimeter. Run it twice with different sides.')

## Task two
('Precedence traps', 'Predict, then run: 2 + 3 * 4, (2 + 3) * 4, 10 − 6 / 2. Write the rule that explains each result.')
