# Expressions and Arithmetic in Small Basic — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an expression?**
**Answer: A written working-out the machine evaluates to a single result**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does ReadNumber do?**
**Answer: Pauses, takes a typed number from the user, and hands it to a variable**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the rhythm of a numeric program?**
**Answer: Read the quantities, work the expression, keep the result**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the basic arithmetic operators.**
**Answer: +, −, * and /**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Are operators worked strictly left to right?**
**Answer: No — by operator precedence**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which operators are worked first?**
**Answer: Multiplication and division, before addition and subtraction**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What stands above all in precedence?**
**Answer: Parentheses — the bracketed part is worked first**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is 2 + 3 * 4, and why?**
**Answer: 14 — multiplication is worked before addition**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do you force the answer 20?**
**Answer: Write the brackets: (2 + 3) * 4**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where do remainder and powers live in Small Basic?**
**Answer: Through the Math object**
