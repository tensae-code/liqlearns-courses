# Hands-on — C Strings in Text Files Fputs Fgets and Appending

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Chapter 14 §14.8 String Handling in Text Files (fputs and fgets; Example 3: write friends' names and phone numbers to Contacts.txt with gets and strlen and "!" as the field separator; Example 4: read and display the contacts in columns with gotoxy; Example 5: append records in mode "a") and §14.9 Formatted I/O (fprintf and fscanf; Example 6: the contacts program rewritten with fprintf) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach c strings in text files fputs fgets and appending to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
