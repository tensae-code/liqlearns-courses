# Hands-on — Building a Calculator Programme

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Build it** — Write the calculator programme in Python: two numbers, an operation chosen by symbol, the four operations, and honest refusals for the two dangers.

## Task two
**Break it kindly** — Exchange calculators with another team. Run their edge cases — zero division, wrong symbol, text instead of a number — and report behaviour against their design.
