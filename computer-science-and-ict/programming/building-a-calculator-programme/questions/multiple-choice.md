# Building a Calculator Programme — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What three things does the calculator programme do?**
a) Asks, computes and prints — input, process, output  b) Two numbers and an operation  c) An unknown operation, and division by zero  d) It misleads with authority
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What inputs does it ask for?**
a) Two numbers and an operation  b) An unknown operation, and division by zero  c) It misleads with authority  d) The input-process-output shape, grown to a tool

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What two design dangers must be decided in advance?**
