# Hands-on — Combining Functions and Arrays

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Crew plan
Plan a marks program as a crew: fillMarks, average, countAbove, printMarks. Give each function's full signature (array plus count) and say what main does.

## Copy or not?
A function doubles every element of the array it is handed. After it returns, are the caller's elements doubled? Answer yes or no with the lesson's reason — then contrast with an ordinary int parameter.
