# Hands-on — A Project Brief — Cahier des Charges for a Program

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Write the brief** — For your studio's game, write a one-page cahier des charges with all five contents. Another studio reads it and must be able to state your game, players and ‘done’ without asking you anything.

## Task two
**Judge by the brief** — At delivery, judge a rival studio's game strictly against its brief: list met charges, broken charges, silent points. No ‘fun’ verdicts allowed without a charge behind them.
