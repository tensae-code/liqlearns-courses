# A Project Brief — Cahier des Charges for a Program — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a cahier des charges?**
**Answer: The written brief a project is built against**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name its five contents.**
**Answer: Product, players, required behaviours, limits, acceptance**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is acceptance?**
**Answer: How ‘done’ will be judged**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why write it?**
**Answer: So studios argue once, on paper, instead of at delivery**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which fiche carries the brief?**
**Answer: Scénario 7, Fiche 4**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How are scope questions answered mid-build?**
**Answer: By reading the brief, not by enthusiasm**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Into what are playtest findings sorted?**
**Answer: ‘Brief broken’ (must fix) and ‘brief silent’ (choice or written amendment)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Against what is the delivered game judged?**
**Answer: The delivered brief**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two verdicts does the scenario distinguish?**
**Answer: ‘I like it’ against ‘it meets its charges’**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must a brief amendment be?**
**Answer: Written and agreed**
