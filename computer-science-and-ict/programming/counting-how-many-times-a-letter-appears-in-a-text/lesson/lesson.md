# Counting How Many Times a Letter Appears in a Text

## Do it first

Open the sentence — “The Dome of the Rock stands in Jerusalem” — and count one letter through it. By hand it is slow and easy to miss. Grade 9’s last lesson gives the job to a counter loop, and the same counting opens doors: vowels in English, the @ in an email, a whole word in a text.

## Sub lesson 1 — Letter by letter — position, length, counter

Three tools walk the text: **letter number of** reads the letter at one position, **length of** gives how many letters the text holds, and a **counter loop** steps position by position, **counting the matches** — each time the letter at the position is the letter sought, the counter grows by one.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: A letter is counted by walking the text: letter-number-of reads one letter’s position at a time, length-of gives the text’s end, and a counter loop counts the matches — the worked example counts a letter in “The Dome of the Rock stands in Jerusalem”. Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.
