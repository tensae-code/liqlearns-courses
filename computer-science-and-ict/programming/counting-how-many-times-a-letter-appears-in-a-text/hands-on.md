# Hands-on — Counting How Many Times a Letter Appears in a Text

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Count by program, check by hand
Count one letter in the Dome of the Rock sentence with the counter loop, then count by hand and compare.

## Vowels and @
Count the vowels in an English sentence of your own, then check an email address for its @ with the same walking loop.
