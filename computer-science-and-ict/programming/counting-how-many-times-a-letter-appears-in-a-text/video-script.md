# Video script — Counting How Many Times a Letter Appears in a Text

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three tools walk the text: **letter number of** reads the letter at one position, **length of** gives how many letters the text holds, and a **counter loop** steps position by position, **counting the matches** — each time the letter at the position is the letter sought, the counter grows by one.
The worked example counts a letter in the sentence **“The Dome of the Rock stands in Jerusalem”**. The projects widen the same walk: **counting the vowel letters in an English sentence**, **checking an email address contains @**, and **finding a whole word** in a text.
