# Binary Numbers — Representation and Use — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which digits does binary use?**
**Answer: 0 and 1 only**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is one binary digit called?**
**Answer: A bit**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are the first place values from the right?**
**Answer: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is 1011 in decimal?**
**Answer: 11 — 8 + 2 + 1**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How many bits make a byte?**
**Answer: 8 bits**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why do computers use binary?**
**Answer: Their circuits have two states — on or off**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What else is stored as bit patterns besides numbers?**
**Answer: Letters, pictures and sound**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is binary's drawback when written out?**
**Answer: The numbers get long**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do programmers make machine values easier to read?**
**Answer: By grouping bits, for example in hexadecimal**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a 1 in a place mean?**
**Answer: That place's value is counted in the total**
