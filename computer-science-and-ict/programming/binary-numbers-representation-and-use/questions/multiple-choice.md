# Binary Numbers — Representation and Use — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which digits does binary use?**
a) 0 and 1 only  b) A bit  c) 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32  d) 11 — 8 + 2 + 1
**Answer: a) 0 and 1 only**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is one binary digit called?**
a) 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32  b) A bit  c) 11 — 8 + 2 + 1  d) 8 bits
**Answer: b) A bit**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are the first place values from the right?**
a) 11 — 8 + 2 + 1  b) 8 bits  c) 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32  d) Their circuits have two states — on or off
**Answer: c) 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is 1011 in decimal?**
a) 8 bits  b) Their circuits have two states — on or off  c) Letters, pictures and sound  d) 11 — 8 + 2 + 1
**Answer: d) 11 — 8 + 2 + 1**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How many bits make a byte?**
a) 8 bits  b) Their circuits have two states — on or off  c) Letters, pictures and sound  d) The numbers get long
**Answer: a) 8 bits**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why do computers use binary?**
a) Letters, pictures and sound  b) Their circuits have two states — on or off  c) The numbers get long  d) By grouping bits, for example in hexadecimal
**Answer: b) Their circuits have two states — on or off**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What else is stored as bit patterns besides numbers?**
a) The numbers get long  b) By grouping bits, for example in hexadecimal  c) Letters, pictures and sound  d) That place's value is counted in the total
**Answer: c) Letters, pictures and sound**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is binary's drawback when written out?**
a) By grouping bits, for example in hexadecimal  b) That place's value is counted in the total  c) 0 and 1 only  d) The numbers get long
**Answer: d) The numbers get long**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do programmers make machine values easier to read?**
a) By grouping bits, for example in hexadecimal  b) That place's value is counted in the total  c) 0 and 1 only  d) A bit
**Answer: a) By grouping bits, for example in hexadecimal**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a 1 in a place mean?**
a) 0 and 1 only  b) That place's value is counted in the total  c) A bit  d) 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32
**Answer: b) That place's value is counted in the total**
