# Debugging — Finding and Fixing the Wrong Instruction — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is debugging?** → **Finding and fixing the wrong instruction in a program**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is the wrong instruction found?** → **Run the program slowly, watching each step, until the mouse goes wrong**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three kinds of wrongness.** → **Wrong direction, wrong count (too many or too few), wrong place in the row**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do you watch while running slowly?** → **Each step, against what the plan intended**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is a failed run the end of the work?** → **No — it is the start of debugging**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a step too many fixed?** → **It is removed**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is a wrong-direction arrow fixed?** → **It is replaced with the right one**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How are steps in the wrong place fixed?** → **They are reordered — swapped into their right places**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must happen after every fix?** → **Run the program again, from the start, all the way**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is a fix proved?** → **When the mouse reaches the cheese by the corrected program**
