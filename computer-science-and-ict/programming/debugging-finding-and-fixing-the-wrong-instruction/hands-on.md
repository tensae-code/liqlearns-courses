# Hands-on — Debugging — Finding and Fixing the Wrong Instruction

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Planted bug** — Take a working maze program and secretly change one arrow; a partner debugs it by the slow-run method and names the fault's kind.

## Task two
**Three faults drill** — Write three short programs, each with a different fault — direction, count, order — and fix each with its matching fix, running after each.
