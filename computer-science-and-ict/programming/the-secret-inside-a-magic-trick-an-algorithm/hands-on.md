# Hands-on — The Secret Inside a Magic Trick — An Algorithm

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Expose a trick** — Learn one provided card trick's method. Perform it once for effect, then again slowly with the algorithm narrated step by step. The audience maps narration to effect.

## Task two
**Chart the magic** — Draw the trick's logigramme with branches for the spectator's choices. Verify: from every branch, does the chart still reach the single reveal?
