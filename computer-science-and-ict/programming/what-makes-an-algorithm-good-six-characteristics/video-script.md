# Video script — What makes an algorithm good — six characteristics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Not every list of steps is an algorithm.

Six tests it must pass.
Clear — one meaning only.
Inputs — stated exactly.
Outputs — stated exactly.
Finite — it ENDS. No endless loops.
Feasible — possible here, today.
Independent — any language can carry it.

Easy to read. Step by step.
But slow to write —
and loops are hard to draw.
