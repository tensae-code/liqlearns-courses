# What makes an algorithm good — six characteristics — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1.** The characteristic that forbids infinite loops is ______.
**Answer: finiteness**

**2.** An algorithm that is simple and practical on available resources is ______.
**Answer: feasible**

**3.** Writing an algorithm takes a long ______ — one of its disadvantages.
**Answer: time**
