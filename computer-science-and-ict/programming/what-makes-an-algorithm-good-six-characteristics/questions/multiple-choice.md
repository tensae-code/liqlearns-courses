# What makes an algorithm good — six characteristics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many characteristics must an algorithm have?
a) four  b) five  c) six  d) eight
**Answer: c) six** — clear, defined inputs, defined outputs, finite, feasible, language-independent.

**2.** An algorithm that loops forever fails —
a) feasibility  b) finiteness  c) clarity  d) language-independence
**Answer: b) finiteness** — finite means it ends.

**3.** 'Each step has only one meaning' is the characteristic called —
a) feasible  b) finite  c) clear and unambiguous  d) well-defined output
**Answer: c) clear and unambiguous** — no step may be read two ways.

**4.** An algorithm needing technology that does not exist yet fails —
a) feasibility  b) finiteness  c) clarity  d) defined inputs
**Answer: a) feasibility** — feasible = practical on available resources.

**5.** One disadvantage of algorithms is —
a) they are too short  b) branching and looping are hard to show in one  c) they only work in one language  d) they cannot break problems down
**Answer: b) branching and looping are hard to show in one** — with writing taking a long time.
