# Hands-on — What makes an algorithm good — six characteristics

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Six-test inspection
Take any recipe at home. Walk it through the six tests on paper, marking pass or fail with a reason for each.

## Break it, fix it
Pairs: one pupil writes instructions that fail exactly one characteristic (secretly chosen). The other inspects, names the failed test, and repairs the set.
