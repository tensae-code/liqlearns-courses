# Hands-on — Comments in C — Single-Line and Multi-Line

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Comment a stranger's program** — Take an uncommented C program. Add a multi-line header and // notes that say why — then delete every note that merely repeats its line.

## Task two
**Comment out to debug** — In a faulty program, silence one block at a time with /* */ until the fault isolates itself. Record what each silence taught.
