# Hands-on — Declaring, Initialising and Assigning Variables

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Write the biography', 'For a counter of goals in a match program, write its declaration, its initialisation, and two assignments (a goal scored; a reset for the second half). Add a constant GOALS_TO_WIN = 3 declared with final.')

## Task two
('Diagnose three messages', 'Three programs fail with: cannot find symbol; variable might not have been initialised; cannot assign a value to final variable. For each, name the missing or forbidden act and write the one-line repair.')
