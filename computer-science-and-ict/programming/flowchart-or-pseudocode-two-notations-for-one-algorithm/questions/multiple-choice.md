# Flowchart or pseudocode — two notations for one algorithm — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is a flowchart?
a) an algorithm written in special shapes for different actions, with lines and arrows showing sequence and relationships  b) a simple, concise sequence of English-like instructions  c) visually appealing and can be easier to understand  d) closer to program instructions
**Answer: a) an algorithm written in special shapes for different actions, with lines and arrows showing sequence and relationships**

**2.** What is pseudocode?
a) an algorithm written in special shapes for different actions, with lines and arrows showing sequence and relationships  b) visually appealing and can be easier to understand  c) closer to program instructions  d) a simple, concise sequence of English-like instructions
**Answer: d) a simple, concise sequence of English-like instructions**

**3.** What is flowchart's strength?
a) a simple, concise sequence of English-like instructions  b) closer to program instructions  c) visually appealing and can be easier to understand  d) an algorithm written in special shapes for different actions, with lines and arrows showing sequence and relationships
**Answer: c) visually appealing and can be easier to understand**

**4.** What is pseudocode's strength?
a) visually appealing and can be easier to understand  b) closer to program instructions  c) an algorithm written in special shapes for different actions, with lines and arrows showing sequence and relationships  d) a simple, concise sequence of English-like instructions
**Answer: b) closer to program instructions**

**5.** Converting a flowchart into pseudocode is possible because —
a) both are pictures  b) symbols and structures are standardised  c) computers require it  d) they are the same thing
**Answer: b) symbols and structures are standardised**

**6.** Which notation can be drawn on paper with standard symbols?
a) pseudocode only  b) the flowchart  c) neither  d) only programs
**Answer: b) the flowchart**
