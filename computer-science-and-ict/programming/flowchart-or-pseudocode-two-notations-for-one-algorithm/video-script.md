This is **Flowchart or pseudocode — two notations for one algorithm**.

**a flowchart** — an algorithm written in special shapes for different actions, with lines and arrows showing sequence and relationships.

**pseudocode** — a simple, concise sequence of English-like instructions.

**flowchart's strength** — visually appealing and can be easier to understand.

**pseudocode's strength** — closer to program instructions.
