# Video script — Project — Little Red Riding Hood and the Wolf: A Scratch Story

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The tale is built as scenes in order, each with its backdrop and the sprites that scene needs: Little Red Riding Hood setting out, the wood where she meets the wolf, and the grandmother's house where the story ends. Sprites are added and personalised for each role, and the stage changes backdrop as the story travels, so the watcher follows the journey by its places, as a picture book turns its pages.
In each scene the sprites move and meet as the tale requires: Riding Hood travels the wood path, the wolf appears and the meeting is played, and the story reaches the house for its ending. The year's sounds can give the scenes their voices and warnings. The project's check is the tale in order: a watcher sees the journey, the meeting and the ending, in the story's own sequence, without the maker explaining which part is which.
