# Virtual Robot — Smart Motion and Repeat — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does the virtual robot steer without a steering wheel?**
**Answer: By its two drive motors — different speeds bend the path**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What makes it go straight?**
**Answer: Both motors at the same speed**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What bends the path?**
**Answer: Driving one motor slower than the other**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does it spin on the spot?**
**Answer: Stop one motor and drive the other**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a corner's sharpness, to the programmer?**
**Answer: The pair of motor speeds**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the Repeat block do?**
**Answer: Runs the blocks inside it a counted number of times**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Write a square as smart repetition.**
**Answer: One side's motion, inside Repeat 4**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is repetition 'smart'?**
**Answer: Shorter to write, and mending the inside once mends every repeat**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two teachings does the lesson pair?**
**Answer: True steering, and writing motion briefly with Repeat**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 6 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 3 — Secrets of Smart Motion**
