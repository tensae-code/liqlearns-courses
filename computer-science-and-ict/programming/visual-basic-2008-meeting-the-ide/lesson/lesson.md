# Visual Basic 2008 — Meeting the IDE

## Do it first

Every craft begins by meeting its workbench. Kuwait's VB course gives the environment its own chapter: the program started, a project opened, and each window named before a single control is placed. This lesson is that meeting.

## Sub lesson 1 — Visual Basic 2008 — what it is, and its advantages

**Visual Basic 2008 Express Edition** is Microsoft's environment for building **Windows programs visually**: the form is drawn, the controls are placed, and event code is written in Basic — the course's language since its first concepts chapter. Its **advantages**, as the book presents them, are the school-programmer's: a program's look is built by **placing**, not by describing; results are **run and seen** at once; and the language's statements stay close to the English of the problems they solve.

In your mind's eye: visual Basic 2008 — what it is, and its advantages.

## Sub lesson 2 — Starting, opening a project, and the windows

The working acts are the chapter's craft: **start** Visual Basic; **open a project** — a new one begun or an existing one brought back, the project being the whole application's kept form; and read the screen's **windows**: the form designer where the program is drawn, the toolbox of controls, the solution's file list, and the **Properties window** — the panel that shows and edits the **selected object's properties**, the place the next chapter's property craft will live. A programmer who can name the windows can follow every instruction the book gives after this chapter.

In your mind's eye: starting, opening a project, and the windows.
