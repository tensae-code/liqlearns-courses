# Variables in Scratch — Creating, Setting, Changing and Asking — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a variable hold?**
**Answer: One value — a number or text — until changed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the variable blocks the book counts?**
**Answer: Create, set, change by, show and hide**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What value does a variable keep?**
**Answer: The last value set, until it is set again**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What three kinds of value can be assigned?**
**Answer: Constants, expressions, and other variables**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does ask-and-answer take from the keyboard?**
**Answer: The user’s typed answer, as the value**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What two programs does Grade 8 run on ask-and-answer?**
**Answer: Square area and rectangle perimeter**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What can be done to a variable besides setting it?**
**Answer: It can be deleted and renamed**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) In the fruit shop, what does each price and quantity get?**
**Answer: Its own variable**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is the grand total built?**
**Answer: Totals multiplied out and added together**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What makes the shop program readable?**
**Answer: Meaningful variable names**
