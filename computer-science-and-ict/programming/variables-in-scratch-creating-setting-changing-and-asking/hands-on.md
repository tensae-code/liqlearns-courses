# Hands-on — Variables in Scratch — Creating, Setting, Changing and Asking

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Area and perimeter
Build the ask-and-answer programs: square area and rectangle perimeter, each asking for its lengths.

## Run the shop
Give three fruits a price-per-kilo variable and a quantity variable each, and build the grand total. Name every variable meaningfully.
