# Video script — Modelling Networks with NetworkX

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

You measured a graph by hand. Waves, distances, centre, diameter. Now let the machine do it — on graphs with millions of nodes.

Python's library is networkx. Add the nodes. Add the edges.

Choose carefully: friendship is mutual — an undirected graph. Following is one-way — a directed graph. The relation decides.

Then ask: nx.center. nx.radius. nx.diameter. The numbers of the earlier lesson, in one call each.

But first, build the small graph you measured by hand. Check the machine against yourself. Then trust it with the vast ones.
