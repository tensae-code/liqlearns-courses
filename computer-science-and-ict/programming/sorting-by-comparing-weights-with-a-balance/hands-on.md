# Hands-on — Sorting by Comparing Weights With a Balance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Sort the parcels** — With five similar objects of hidden different weights and a balance (or a “balance judge” with a secret weight list), sort them fully. Log every comparison; report your count.

## Task two
**Cheapest sorter contest** — Teams sort the same set with their own planned strategy. Fewest comparisons with a correct order wins — but a wrong order disqualifies, however cheap.
