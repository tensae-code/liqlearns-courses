Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Which of two objects is heavier — or that they are equal** — as the answer to: What is kept as objects are weighed?

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: If A > B and B > C, then A > C — no weighing needed**
