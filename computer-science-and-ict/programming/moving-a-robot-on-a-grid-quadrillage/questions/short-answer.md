# Moving a Robot on a Grid — Quadrillage — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is every place on a quadrillage?**
**Answer: A square**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In what unit are movements counted?**
**Answer: Squares — the robot's own steps**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the two things to know before planning.**
**Answer: Where the robot is, and which way it faces**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When is a route correct?**
**Answer: On the grid, before it is run on the mat**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What geography does the robot know?**
**Answer: None beyond the grid**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a turn change?**
**Answer: Facing, not place**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the beginners' classic grid bug?**
**Answer: Planning turns as moves, or forgetting forward changes meaning after a turn**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How many counts does a grid program keep?**
**Answer: Two — squares and facings**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the robot pivot?**
**Answer: On its square, in place**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must be tracked as carefully as position?**
**Answer: Facing**
