# Hands-on — Moving a Robot on a Grid — Quadrillage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Grid routes** — On a printed quadrillage, write three routes (start, facing, target) as step lists. A partner traces each with a counter and verifies arrival and facing.

## Task two
**Facing tracker** — Call a route aloud step by step; the class tracks a paper robot's facing with a finger after every instruction. Anyone whose facing drifts names the step that lost it.
