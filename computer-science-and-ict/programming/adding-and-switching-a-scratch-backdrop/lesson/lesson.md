# Adding and Switching a Scratch Backdrop

## Do it first

Your sprite stands on a white, empty stage. The story says the sprite is in a forest, then at the beach. The sprite has not moved rooms — the stage must change its background. That background has a name in Scratch, and this lesson is how you give the stage one.

## Adding and switching a backdrop

A **backdrop** is the picture the stage wears behind the sprites. Choose a new backdrop and Scratch opens the **backdrop library** — places and scenes ready to use — just as the sprite library holds characters. Pick one and the stage wears it at once. The likeness to sprites helps: a sprite's looks are its costumes, the stage's looks are its backdrops, and both are edited in the paint editor. The difference: a backdrop belongs to the stage, not to any sprite. A project can hold **several backdrops** — one per place in the story — and switch the stage from one to the next when the story moves to a new place. That switching is how a Scratch story travels without the sprite ever leaving the screen.
