# Adding and Switching a Scratch Backdrop — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A backdrop is —**
a) The picture the stage wears behind the sprites  b) A sound  c) A kind of block
**Answer: a) The picture the stage wears behind the sprites** — The backdrop is the stage's picture.

**2. Backdrops are chosen from the —**
a) Sound library  b) Backdrop library  c) Sprite list
**Answer: b) Backdrop library** — Places and scenes wait in the backdrop library.

**3. A backdrop belongs to —**
a) One sprite  b) The stage  c) The green flag
**Answer: b) The stage** — Backdrops belong to the stage, not a sprite.

**4. A project can hold —**
a) Only one backdrop ever  b) Several backdrops and switch between them  c) Backdrops only in sounds
**Answer: b) Several backdrops and switch between them** — Several backdrops, switched as the story moves.
