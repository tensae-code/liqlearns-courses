# Adding and Switching a Scratch Backdrop — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. How is a backdrop like a costume, and how is it different?**
**Answer:** A sound answer: both are painted looks chosen and edited the same way; a costume belongs to a sprite, a backdrop belongs to the stage.

**2. Your story has three places. Describe your backdrop plan.**
**Answer:** A sound answer: one backdrop per place held in the same project, with the stage switched to the next backdrop at each moment the story moves.
