# Video script — Plotting Graphs and Diagrams in PascalABC

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

To **plot a graph**, the program needs axes first: lines for the horizontal and vertical axes, with a chosen **scale** — how many window points stand for one unit.
Then it works the function like a table: take an x value, calculate its y, turn the pair into window coordinates, and mark the point; repeat in small steps and join the points, and the **curve** appears.
A **diagram** shows amounts, not a function: the program reads the data values and draws each as a bar or sector of the height or size the value deserves, in the same scale across the whole diagram — the comparison is the message.
Both graph and diagram share one discipline: the picture must be **honest to the numbers** — axes and scales stated, steps even, heights proportional — because a plotted picture persuades faster than a table, and a wrong scale persuades wrongly.
