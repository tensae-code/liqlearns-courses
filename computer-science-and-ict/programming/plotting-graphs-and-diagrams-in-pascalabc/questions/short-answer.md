# Plotting Graphs and Diagrams in PascalABC — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the program draw first for a graph?**
**Answer: The axes, with a chosen scale**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a scale here?**
**Answer: How many window points stand for one unit**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a curve produced?**
**Answer: By calculating y for step-by-step x values, marking each point, and joining the points**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a diagram?**
**Answer: A picture showing amounts — bars or sectors sized by their values**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What must be the same across a whole diagram?**
**Answer: The scale**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the shared discipline of graphs and diagrams?**
**Answer: Honesty to the numbers — stated axes and scales, even steps, proportional heights**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why does honesty matter so much in a plotted picture?**
**Answer: A picture persuades faster than a table, so a wrong scale persuades wrongly**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What two things does the program turn a value pair into?**
**Answer: Window coordinates for the point to be marked**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Can the same program plot with another scale?**
**Answer: Yes — change the scale and re-run**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is a graph's picture a claim about?**
**Answer: The function and its values — the programmer answers for it**
