# The Systems Life Cycle — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the systems life cycle?**
**Answer: The ordered life of an information system from first idea to old age, in six phases**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the six phases in order.**
**Answer: Preliminary investigation; systems analysis; system design; system development; system implementation; system maintenance**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What question does Phase 1 answer?**
**Answer: Is there a problem, and is a new system worth it?**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What question does Phase 2 answer?**
**Answer: Exactly what must the new system do?**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What question does Phase 3 answer?**
**Answer: How will it do it?**

**6. (Sub lesson 1) What do Phases 4 and 5 do?**
**Answer: Phase 4 builds the system; Phase 5 puts it to work**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does Phase 6 do?**
**Answer: Keeps the system true as the world changes**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is it a cycle, not a line?**
**Answer: Maintenance's changes — and the problems it cannot fix — start a new investigation; the wheel turns again**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which case carries the journey in Fiji's book?**
**Answer: The Viti Group of Companies — one furniture company's system**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What alternatives stand at the cycle's side?**
**Answer: Prototyping and rapid application development**
