# Hands-on — The Char Type in Pascal and C++

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Code work** — Using an ASCII table, give the codes of 'A', 'a', '0' and space, then compute ord('Z') − ord('A') and say what it counts.

## Task two
**Digit reading** — Write the steps (in either language) that read a two-digit number typed as two characters and form its numeric value.
