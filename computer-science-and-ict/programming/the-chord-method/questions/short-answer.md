# The Chord Method — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the chord?**
**Answer: The straight segment joining (a, f(a)) and (b, f(b))**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the method take from the chord?**
**Answer: Its crossing with the x-axis — the next estimate c**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which end is replaced each step?**
**Answer: The end whose f has the same sign as f(c) — so the bracket keeps opposite signs**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do the values add beyond signs?**
**Answer: They accuse the nearness — the root sits nearer the end whose |f| is smaller**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) State the midpoint–chord contrast in the lesson's phrase.**
**Answer: The midpoint was democracy; the chord is cross-examination**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When is the chord method fast?**
**Answer: When the function is nearly straight across the bracket**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What goes wrong under strong curvature?**
**Answer: One bracket end sticks — narrowing proceeds from one side only**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Is certainty surrendered then?**
**Answer: No — the bracket keeps opposite signs throughout**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) State the stopping rule.**
**Answer: When the bracket, or the step between successive c's, falls under the demanded precision**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Against what is the chord method measured?**
**Answer: Against bisection — faster when the slope is honest, never less certain**
