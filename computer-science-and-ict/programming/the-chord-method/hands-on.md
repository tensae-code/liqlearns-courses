# Hands-on — The Chord Method

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Chord steps** — Run three chord-method steps on x² − 2 over [1, 2] with full arithmetic, and compare the bracket with bisection's after three steps.

## Task two
**Sticking case** — Sketch a curving function on [0, 3] with a root near 2.8 and explain which end will stick, and why the method still ends right.
