# Hands-on — Arithmetic and Logical Operations — the At School Project

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Compute and decide
Build a small school quiz: three questions, an arithmetic total, and a branch that says pass or try again by the total. Check the total by hand first.

## Check both halves
Deliberately break the deciding half only. Show that the computing still checks out — then fix the decision and prove the whole.
