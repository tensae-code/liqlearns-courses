# Arithmetic and Logical Operations — the At School Project — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. In the At School project, arithmetic operations —**
a) compute scores, counts and totals  b) choose backdrops  c) record sounds
**Answer: a) compute scores, counts and totals**

**2. Logical operations in the project —**
a) draw costumes  b) move the stage  c) decide, through comparisons and branches
**Answer: c) decide, through comparisons and branches**

**3. The two halves should be checked —**
a) only at the very end  b) separately, before trusting the whole  c) never
**Answer: b) separately, before trusting the whole**

**4. The project's theme is —**
a) school  b) the seaside  c) a football stadium
**Answer: a) school**
