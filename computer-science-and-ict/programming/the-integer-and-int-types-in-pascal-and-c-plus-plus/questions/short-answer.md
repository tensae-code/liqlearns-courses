# The Integer and int Types in Pascal and C++ — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name Pascal's whole-number family members.**
**Answer: shortint, byte, integer, longint**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name C++'s.**
**Answer: short, int, long**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does each size fix?**
**Answer: A range — 2ⁿ patterns**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does an unsigned twin spend its patterns on?**
**Answer: 0 and positives only — doubling the top at the price of negatives**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is chosen when a whole-number type is chosen?**
**Answer: Range and sign, together**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are Pascal's three division answers?**
**Answer: / gives a real; div the whole quotient; mod the remainder**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is 7 / 2 in C++ integer arithmetic?**
**Answer: 3 — the whole quotient, fraction discarded**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is C++'s % ?**
**Answer: Its mod — the remainder (7 % 2 = 1)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What fixes what an operator does?**
**Answer: The language and the operands' types together — never the symbol alone**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which is the course's recurring lesson from this section?**
**Answer: Syntax is clothing; the concept — and its contract — is what must be known**
