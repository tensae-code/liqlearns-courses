# Procedures and Functions — Actual and Formal Parameters — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a procedure do?**
**Answer: Does a job — called as a command in its own line; results return through its parameters**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a function do?**
**Answer: Answers one value — called inside an expression, where a value could stand**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a procedure called in ΓΛΩΣΣΑ?**
**Answer: ΚΑΛΕΣΕ — as a command in its own line**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does a procedure hand results back?**
**Answer: Through its parameters**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How does the study guide teach the pair?**
**Answer: Worked side by side on the same jobs**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are formal parameters?**
**Answer: The names in the subprogram's heading for the values it will receive**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are actual parameters?**
**Answer: The caller's variables or values supplied in the call, matched in order**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must match?**
**Answer: The counts and the types of actuals and formals**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is a call traced?**
**Answer: Formals bound to actuals one by one, by hand**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which books teach it here?**
**Answer: Anaptyxi Efarmogon Ch.10, its study guide Ch.5 and its workbook Ch.10**
