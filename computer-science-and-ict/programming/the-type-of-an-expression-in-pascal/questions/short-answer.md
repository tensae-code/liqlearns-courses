# The Type of an Expression in Pascal — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What determines an expression's type?**
**Answer: Its operands and operators together**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What type is 7 * 3?**
**Answer: Integer — integer arithmetic with * stays integer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What type is 7 / 3, and 7 div 3?**
**Answer: 7 / 3 is real; 7 div 3 is integer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does one real operand do to an arithmetic expression?**
**Answer: It makes the whole result real**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is an expression's type written beside it?**
**Answer: No — it is derived from the types of its parts**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What type does a comparison give?**
**Answer: Boolean — whatever the compared operands' type**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do logical operators take and give?**
**Answer: They take booleans and give booleans**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is assigning a real expression to an integer variable an error?**
**Answer: A fraction cannot honestly enter a whole-number box**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Is assigning integer to real allowed?**
**Answer: Yes — whole numbers are reals with empty fractions**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) State the section's discipline in one line.**
**Answer: Know the expression's type before you assign it**
