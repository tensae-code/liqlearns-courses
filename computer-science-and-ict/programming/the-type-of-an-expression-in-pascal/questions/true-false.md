# The Type of an Expression in Pascal — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Its operands and operators together**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Its operands and operators together** — as the answer to: What type is 7 * 3?
**Answer: False — Integer — integer arithmetic with * stays integer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: 7 / 3 is real; 7 div 3 is integer**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: 7 / 3 is real; 7 div 3 is integer** — as the answer to: What does one real operand do to an arithmetic expression?
**Answer: False — It makes the whole result real**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: No — it is derived from the types of its parts**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: No — it is derived from the types of its parts** — as the answer to: What type does a comparison give?
**Answer: False — Boolean — whatever the compared operands' type**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: They take booleans and give booleans**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: They take booleans and give booleans** — as the answer to: Why is assigning a real expression to an integer variable an error?

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Yes — whole numbers are reals with empty fractions**
