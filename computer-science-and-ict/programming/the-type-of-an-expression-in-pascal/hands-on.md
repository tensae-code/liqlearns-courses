# Hands-on — The Type of an Expression in Pascal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Type the expressions** — Give the type of: 5 + 2 * 3; 5 / 2; 5 div 2; x > 4 (x real); sqr(3) + 1; not (a and b) — with the rule for each.

## Task two
**Guard check** — Decide which assignments are legal — i: integer; r: real; b: boolean — for i := 7/2; r := 7 div 2; b := 7 > 2; i := 7.0; and defend each verdict.
