Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Kazakhstan, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked for the slug and the live lesson content was checked for near matches before building. It sits in the Programming title (programming/), following the book's own order. Provenance scope note: the placements stocked here are from Kazakhstan's okulyk.kz (OKULYK) textbooks, recommended by the Ministry of Education and Science of the Republic of Kazakhstan — the Digital Literacy and Informatika lines of the Almatykitap, Arman-PV and Atamura teams and the Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools (NIS) books, all student books except the two Kadirkulov teacher guides named in the entries below, whose placements are stocked exactly as filed. Many books exist in parallel language editions (Kazakh / Russian / Uzbek / Uyghur / English) of the same textbook: each distinct book is taught once, and every distinct edition is named and cited in the book entry. Kazakhstan's own ways are kept: one textbook line in up to five parallel language editions, the okulyk.kz digital library as the books' home, Scratch from Grade 1 and robotics strands (LEGO WeDo 2.0 and Mindstorms EV3) from Grade 1–2, and every keyword given in Kazakh–Russian–English in the early grades. Media slots in lesson/lesson.md are described slots only ([Picture — …]); no picture numbers are invented.


Kazakhstan's own way (Sagimbaeva G1 «Цифровая грамотность»): two cartoon guides (Arman and Saule, themselves first-graders) lead the year; lessons are told as a journey, QR codes open lesson videos, and from lesson 10 the class builds and programmes LEGO-style robots (windmill model, labyrinth runs).

Kazakhstan's own way (Kobdikova G1 «Цифрлық сауаттылық»): the Arman-PV Grade 1 book teaches programming through Scratch from §6 (creating a project, the «Қош келдің, Наурыз!» project, saving/opening a project, a «Музыкалық аспаптар» project) where the Almatykitap book uses an unnamed game environment, and its safety section adds a lesson on unwanted contacts online.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kadirkulov G6 «Информатика»): the Almatykitap Grade 6 book follows the same state programme as the Arman-PV book (Book 24) — ergonomics, wireless networks, footnotes and hyperlinks, copyright, contents, report — but swaps vector graphics for a whole 3D-printing section in a SketchUp-style editor (cone, cylinder and sphere models, print setup) and swaps the Scratch game for a first Python course: IDE, alphabet and syntax, data types, arithmetic expressions, number input and output, linear algorithms.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kadirkulov G7 «Информатика» 2019 edition): the 2019 Almatykitap Grade 7 book is not the 2023 Python book (Book 15): after measurement, networks and spreadsheets it teaches programming through the Lazarus project interface — interface elements, data types, branching, nested and compound conditions — and ends with 3D modelling of objects and events (house, car, table, chair, window, glass as the final tasks).

Kazakhstan's own way (Mukametzhanova G7 «Информатика» 2017 edition): the Atamura Grade 7 book runs the same state programme as the Almatykitap one (Book 27) but adds a unit on converting between units of information, teaches the programming in C++, and likewise ends in 3D modelling of objects and events.

Kazakhstan's own way (Mukametzhanova G5 «Информатика» 2017 edition): the 2017 Atamura Grade 5 book opens with computer safety and a mini-project «Открытия, изменившие мир» (Discoveries that changed the world), teaches algorithms as following commands and escaping labyrinths (real and virtual), and the first program in Scratch with "graphics come alive".

Kazakhstan's own way (Salgaraeva/Kopeeva G6 «Информатика»): this Arman-PV Grade 6 book (a third distinct Grade 6 book in this half) opens with an explicit discussion of internet addiction, and its text-document section treats hyperlinks, copyright and plagiarism, the table of contents and the school report (реферат) as project work — the document-skills tail of the Grade 6 programme.

Kazakhstan's own way (Mukametzhanova G6 2020 «Информатика»): the 2020 Atamura Grade 6 revision drops the spreadsheet tail of its 2018 edition and follows the new programme instead — ergonomics, computer history, wireless networks, 3D printing, Python and a text-document section (instructions and links, hyperlinks, table of contents, report) — in four compact sections.
