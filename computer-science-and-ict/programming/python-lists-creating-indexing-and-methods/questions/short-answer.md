# Python Lists — Creating, Indexing and Methods — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a list?**
**Answer: Many values in one variable, in order, written in square brackets**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) From what number do indexes count?**
**Answer: From 0**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does marks[1] reach in [18, 15, 20]?**
**Answer: The second item — 15**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does len() answer?**
**Answer: How many items the list holds**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Are lists mutable?**
**Answer: Yes — they can change after creation**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which method adds at the end?**
**Answer: append**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which methods take items out?**
**Answer: remove and pop**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do sort and reverse do?**
**Answer: Order the items; turn the list around**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Does a method make a copy of the list?**
**Answer: No — the same list is changed, unless the program asks for a copy**
