# Drawing Figures with Pascal Graphics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which operator lights one point?**
a) PutPixel  b) Two points — the segment's ends  c) By centre and radius  d) Chooses the drawing colour
**Answer: a) PutPixel**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Line take?**
a) By centre and radius  b) Two points — the segment's ends  c) Chooses the drawing colour  d) By changing the numbers — coordinates and sizes
**Answer: b) Two points — the segment's ends**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a circle described to Circle?**
a) Chooses the drawing colour  b) By changing the numbers — coordinates and sizes  c) By centre and radius  d) A rectangle, a triangle roof and a circle sun
**Answer: c) By centre and radius**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does SetColor do?**
a) By changing the numbers — coordinates and sizes  b) A rectangle, a triangle roof and a circle sun  c) A repetition lesson  d) Chooses the drawing colour
**Answer: d) Chooses the drawing colour**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a drawn figure changed?**
a) By changing the numbers — coordinates and sizes  b) A rectangle, a triangle roof and a circle sun  c) A repetition lesson  d) The pupil now makes the screen show values, branches and loops
**Answer: a) By changing the numbers — coordinates and sizes**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a house, in graphics operators?**
a) A repetition lesson  b) A rectangle, a triangle roof and a circle sun  c) The pupil now makes the screen show values, branches and loops  d) Big figures first, then the small ones
**Answer: b) A rectangle, a triangle roof and a circle sun**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What closes this lesson in the book?**
a) The pupil now makes the screen show values, branches and loops  b) Big figures first, then the small ones  c) A repetition lesson  d) The Graph unit
**Answer: c) A repetition lesson**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why are graphics called the course's reward?**
a) Big figures first, then the small ones  b) The Graph unit  c) PutPixel  d) The pupil now makes the screen show values, branches and loops
**Answer: d) The pupil now makes the screen show values, branches and loops**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what order should a picture be composed?**
a) Big figures first, then the small ones  b) The Graph unit  c) PutPixel  d) Two points — the segment's ends
**Answer: a) Big figures first, then the small ones**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What unit holds the drawing operators?**
a) PutPixel  b) The Graph unit  c) Two points — the segment's ends  d) By centre and radius
**Answer: b) The Graph unit**
