# Drawing Figures with Pascal Graphics — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which operator lights one point?**
**Answer: PutPixel**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Line take?**
**Answer: Two points — the segment's ends**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a circle described to Circle?**
**Answer: By centre and radius**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does SetColor do?**
**Answer: Chooses the drawing colour**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a drawn figure changed?**
**Answer: By changing the numbers — coordinates and sizes**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a house, in graphics operators?**
**Answer: A rectangle, a triangle roof and a circle sun**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What closes this lesson in the book?**
**Answer: A repetition lesson**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why are graphics called the course's reward?**
**Answer: The pupil now makes the screen show values, branches and loops**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what order should a picture be composed?**
**Answer: Big figures first, then the small ones**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What unit holds the drawing operators?**
**Answer: The Graph unit**
