# Hands-on — Drawing Figures with Pascal Graphics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Draw a house in code', 'Write Pascal graphics code for a house: rectangle walls, a line roof, a circle sun — list each operator and its numbers first on paper.')

## Task two
('Change by numbers', 'Take a drawn figure and make it twice as wide and move it right, changing only numbers; write what you changed.')
