# Sensing the Mouse's X and Y Position in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do mouse x and mouse y report?**
**Answer: The pointer's position on the stage's grid**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are mouse x's edge values?**
**Answer: −240 at the left edge, 240 at the right**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are mouse y's edge values?**
**Answer: −180 at the bottom, 180 at the top**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where is 0, 0?**
**Answer: At the centre of the stage**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When do the values change?**
**Answer: As the mouse moves — live, with no click needed**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What can a sprite do with mouse x alone?**
**Answer: Go to the pointer's x while keeping its own height**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does a program test a region?**
**Answer: With conditions on the values — x greater than, y between**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does sensing let the program ask?**
**Answer: Where the user's hand is**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the whole stage become with these conditions?**
**Answer: A control the user already knows how to use**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does a drawing use the reporters?**
**Answer: It follows the pointer's numbers, not the pointer itself**
