# Sensing the Mouse's X and Y Position in Scratch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do mouse x and mouse y report?**
a) The pointer's position on the stage's grid  b) −240 at the left edge, 240 at the right  c) −180 at the bottom, 180 at the top  d) At the centre of the stage
**Answer: a) The pointer's position on the stage's grid**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are mouse x's edge values?**
a) −180 at the bottom, 180 at the top  b) −240 at the left edge, 240 at the right  c) At the centre of the stage  d) As the mouse moves — live, with no click needed
**Answer: b) −240 at the left edge, 240 at the right**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are mouse y's edge values?**
a) At the centre of the stage  b) As the mouse moves — live, with no click needed  c) −180 at the bottom, 180 at the top  d) Go to the pointer's x while keeping its own height
**Answer: c) −180 at the bottom, 180 at the top**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where is 0, 0?**
