# Hands-on — Sensing the Mouse's X and Y Position in Scratch

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read the pointer
Show the mouse x and mouse y monitors on the stage. Move the pointer to each corner and to the centre, and write the five pairs of numbers. Check them against the grid's edges.

## Answer the pointer
Program a sprite that always moves to the pointer's x but stays at its own height, sliding along the stage's floor as you move the mouse. Add a condition: when mouse y is above 100, the sprite says “up high!”
