# Hands-on — Writing Linear Programs in Pascal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Compose a converter', 'Write a complete linear Pascal program that reads a length in metres and prints it in centimetres and millimetres, with prompts.')

## Task two
('Read and repair', 'Take a linear program with its statements shuffled; put them in working order and say what breaks if output comes before input.')
