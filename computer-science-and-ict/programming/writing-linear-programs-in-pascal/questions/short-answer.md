# Writing Linear Programs in Pascal — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a linear program?**
**Answer: A program whose statements run in order, once each — no branches, no loops**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are its three beats?**
**Answer: Input, compute, output**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which operators carry the three beats?**
**Answer: readln, assignment, writeln**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Give the lesson's running task.**
**Answer: Read a rectangle's sides; compute and print area and perimeter**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What frame does the program fill?**
**Answer: Program name, declarations, begin…end**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What four choices does composing make?**
**Answer: Variables, formulas, prompts, output order**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's method, in order?**
**Answer: Say the task, name the data, write the steps, then the code**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a linear program called complete?**
**Answer: Everything in it was taught and nothing is decoration**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What road is the method a small copy of?**
**Answer: The problem-solving road — the stages of solving a task on a computer**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must every input have before it?**
**Answer: A prompt**
