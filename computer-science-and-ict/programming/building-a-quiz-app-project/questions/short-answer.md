# Building a Quiz App — Project — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are a quiz app's four working parts?**
**Answer: Questions with answers, showing and answering, checking, and a score**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which layer does the asking, checking and scoring?**
**Answer: JavaScript — HTML gives the page, CSS its face**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does generative AI help in the build?**
**Answer: Drafting code and questions — every suggestion tested before it is trusted**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the right build order?**
**Answer: One question working end to end first, then the set, then the score**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why one question first?**
**Answer: A quiz that asks one question perfectly is a foundation; one that half-asks ten is a ruin**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens in the review section?**
**Answer: The makers run their own app as its harshest user, hunting faults**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two score faults the review hunts**
**Answer: Skipped questions counted wrongly, double answers, empty sends (any two)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must every question be?**
**Answer: Fair, unambiguous, and actually about what it claims to test**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is the app done?**
**Answer: When its makers can explain every part and defend every question — not merely when it runs**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What rhythm does the chapter carry into the block-breaker game?**
**Answer: Build, then review — the same two halves**
