# Generalisation — Common Features of Problems — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does generalisation look for first?**
**Answer: The common features of problems — the same structure under different surfaces**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Malay phrase Malaysia uses?**
**Answer: Ciri-ciri kesamaan — the features of sameness**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What structure do popia and spring rolls share?**
**Answer: Fill – wrap – seal**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is the comparison done exactly?**
**Answer: List each problem's parts, line the lists up, and mark parts that match in role**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the family's skeleton?**
**Answer: The parts that match across all the problems**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Against what is the widened solution written?**
**Answer: Against the skeleton — with differing parts left as values to be supplied**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does one wrapping method need each time?**
**Answer: A filling and a skin, supplied as values**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How far may you generalise?**
**Answer: Only as far as the common features truly run**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) A food that is poured, not wrapped — is it in the wrapping family?**
**Answer: No — it breaks the skeleton**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What happens if a non-member is forced into the family?**
**Answer: The solution breaks**
