# Hands-on — The while loop — and the increment that ends it

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — every activity is done for real, in IDLE or on paper.

## Gate keeper
A partner is the gate: they hold a card 'count < 5'. You call your count aloud, step forward only while the gate says true, adding 1 each step — and freeze the moment it says false.

## Two roads
Solve 'print 0 to 4' twice — once with while, once with for/range. Lay both programs side by side and circle what is the same (output) and what differs (shape).
