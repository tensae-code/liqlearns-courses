# Built-In Functions in BASIC — SQR, SIN, LEFT$ and LEN — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a built-in function?**
**Answer: A named piece of work the language itself performs — you give a value, it hands back a result**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why are they called reserved?**
**Answer: Their names belong to the language — they are the language's own words**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does SQR(81) return?**
**Answer: 9 — the square root of 81**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does SIN give?**
**Answer: A number's (an angle's) sine**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name three more mathematical built-ins the book's family holds.**
**Answer: Cosine, tangent and absolute value (with whole part)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does LEN("KHARTOUM") return?**
**Answer: 8 — the count of the string's characters**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does LEFT$("KHARTOUM", 4) return?**
**Answer: "KHAR" — the first four characters**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What marks a built-in function in both families?**
**Answer: A reserved name, a value in brackets, a single result out**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a string?**
**Answer: Text held in a string variable**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the next lesson turn around?**
**Answer: Functions the programmer defines for herself**
