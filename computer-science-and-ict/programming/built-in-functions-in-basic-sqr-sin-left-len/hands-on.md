# Hands-on — Built-In Functions in BASIC — SQR, SIN, LEFT$ and LEN

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Call the toolbox
Work out by hand what SQR(49), LEN("SUDAN") and LEFT$("BASIC", 3) each return, then say which family each belongs to.

## Choose the function
For three jobs — a text's length, a square root, the first two letters of a name — name the built-in function for each.
