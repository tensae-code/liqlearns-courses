# Nested Loops — and Converting Between Loop Structures — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a nested loop?**
**Answer: A loop inside a loop — the inner runs to its end for every step of the outer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Give its standing pattern.**
**Answer: Tables — a ΓΙΑ for rows holding a ΓΙΑ for columns**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does the min/max pattern start?**
**Answer: The champion is set to the first value, then every later value challenges it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the pattern's classic fault?**
**Answer: Seeding the champion with 0 instead of the first value**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which book teaches it?**
**Answer: The Grade 12 Anaptyxi study guide, Chapter 3**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name ΓΛΩΣΣΑ's three loop structures.**
**Answer: ΟΣΟ…ΕΠΑΝΑΛΑΒΕ, ΑΡΧΗ_ΕΠΑΝΑΛΗΨΗΣ…ΜΕΧΡΙΣ_ΟΤΟΥ, ΓΙΑ…ΑΠΟ…ΜΕΧΡΙ**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) A ΓΙΑ converted to ΟΣΟ needs what handled openly?**
**Answer: Its counter — start, step and end by hand**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which structure always runs at least once?**
**Answer: ΑΡΧΗ_ΕΠΑΝΑΛΗΨΗΣ…ΜΕΧΡΙΣ_ΟΤΟΥ (repeat-until)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which may run never?**
**Answer: ΟΣΟ…ΕΠΑΝΑΛΑΒΕ (while)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the guide drill conversions?**
**Answer: Exams ask them directly, and translation proves mastery of repetition**
