# Hands-on — Iterative or Recursive — Choosing the Approach

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Twin writing** — Write the power function (xⁿ) both ways, count each version's operations and memory for n = 1000, and give the verdict with reasons.

## Task two
**Depth audit** — For three problems (a file-folder tree walk, summing a million marks, the Towers of Hanoi) choose iterative or recursive and defend each by depth, overlap and clarity.
