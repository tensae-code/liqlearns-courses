# Hands-on — The Integer Data Type in Pascal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Range test** — Write the largest and smallest classic integers, then work out on paper what your system does with 32767 + 1 and explain it by the range.

## Task two
**Div/mod drill** — Express 137 minutes as hours and minutes, and 500 coins of 25 as notes and change — once in words, once as div and mod expressions.
