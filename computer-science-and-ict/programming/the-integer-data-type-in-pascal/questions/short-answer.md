# The Integer Data Type in Pascal — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the integer type hold?**
**Answer: Whole numbers — counts, ages, scores — with no fractional part**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the classic Pascal integer range?**
**Answer: −32768 to 32767**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What sets the range?**
**Answer: The memory the type occupies**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is an integer variable declared?**
**Answer: var n: integer;**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Can the type quietly stretch to hold a bigger value?**
**Answer: No — it wraps or errors; it never stretches**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does / give on two integers?**
**Answer: A real result**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do div and mod give for 7 and 2?**
**Answer: div gives 3 (the whole quotient); mod gives 1 (the remainder)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is div for, in daily terms?**
**Answer: Counting whole groups — notes from coins, hours from minutes**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is mod for?**
**Answer: Finding what is left over — the change, the minutes remaining**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why choose the integer type?**
**Answer: For exact, whole, remainder-aware arithmetic**
