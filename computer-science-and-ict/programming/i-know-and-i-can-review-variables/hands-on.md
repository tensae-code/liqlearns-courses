# Hands-on — I Know and I Can — Review: Variables

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Rebuild the balloon
Rebuild the pop-the-balloon score from memory: variable made, +1 per pop, score shown. A partner pops and checks the score counts honestly.

## Name the gap
Any review task that stops you becomes your gap line; practise that part, then prove the whole task again.
