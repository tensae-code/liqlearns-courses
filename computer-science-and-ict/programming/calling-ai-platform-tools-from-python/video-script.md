# Video script — Calling AI Platform Tools from Python

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

You cannot build a giant AI alone. You do not need to.
Platforms offer trained tools — through a door called an interface.
Your program sends a request. The tool does the clever work.
The response comes back — and your program uses it.
Carry messages to WeChat, call the tool, reply: a chatbot.
Small code, borrowed intelligence, composed by you.
