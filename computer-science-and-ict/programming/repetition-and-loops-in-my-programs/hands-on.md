# Hands-on — Repetition and Loops in My Programs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Compress the corridor** — Write a maze program in full, then rewrite it with loops wherever instructions repeat; count how many arrows the loop form saves.

## Task two
**Loop walk** — Walk a square path twice — once following twelve written instructions, once following a loop card 'forward, turn — 4 times' — and say which was surer.
