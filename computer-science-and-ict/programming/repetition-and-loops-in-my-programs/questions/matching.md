# Repetition and Loops in My Programs — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a repetition?** → **The same instruction carried out again and again**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Give an example from the lesson.** → **Forward six times across a corridor (also: four turns around a square)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is writing every repetition out in full bad?** → **Programs become long, slow to write and easy to mistype**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where do repetitions appear in the world?** → **Fence posts, stairs, song choruses — tasks that repeat**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the book's answer to long repetitions?** → **Count them and write the instruction once with its count**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a loop?** → **An instruction written once, with the number of times it must repeat**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the body of a loop?** → **The instruction or block that repeats inside it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the executor do inside a loop?** → **Runs the body, counts, and repeats until the count is reached**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when the count is reached?** → **The executor moves to what follows the loop**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why do loops make repetition exact?** → **The executor counts — it never loses count the way a writer mistypes**
