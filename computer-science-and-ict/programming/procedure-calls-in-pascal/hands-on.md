# Hands-on — Procedure Calls in Pascal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Call reading** — Take a Pascal program with three procedures and list every call: the procedure, its actual parameters, and where control returns after each.

## Task two
**Refactor by call** — Take a program that prints a table row three times inline; extract the row-printing into a procedure and replace the copies with calls.
