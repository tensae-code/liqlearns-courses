# Procedure Calls in Pascal — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How is a procedure call written?**
**Answer: The procedure's name followed by its actual parameters in brackets**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when a call executes?**
**Answer: The values are handed over, the body runs, and control returns to the instruction after the call**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Is writeln(x) a procedure call?**
**Answer: Yes — a call to the built-in writing procedure**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How many instructions is a call in the calling program?**
**Answer: One — however many the called body holds**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are the values handed over called?**
**Answer: Actual parameters**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What lets one procedure body serve many data?**
**Answer: Different actual parameters at each call**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the main program become with calls?**
**Answer: A readable plan of named deeds — read, compute, print**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) A fault inside a procedure is fixed where?**
**Answer: Once, in its body — repairing every call at a stroke**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which later grade builds the full theory?**
**Answer: Grade 12 — subprograms, scope and side effects**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name the hinge of structured programming in the lesson's phrase.**
**Answer: The procedure call**
