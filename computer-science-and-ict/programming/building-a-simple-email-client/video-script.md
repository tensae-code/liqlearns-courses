# Video script — Building a Simple Email Client

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your mail app is just a program doing errands.
To send, it talks SMTP to a sending server.
The mail waits in your friend's mailbox on their server.
To collect, a client asks with POP3 or IMAP.
In Python: smtplib sends. poplib fetches.
You can build that client yourself.
