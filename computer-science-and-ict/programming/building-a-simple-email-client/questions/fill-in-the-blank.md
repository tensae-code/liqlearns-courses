# Building a Simple Email Client — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The protocol for sending mail is ____.**
**Answer: SMTP** — SMTP = Simple Mail Transfer Protocol.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Python's ____ module fetches mail over POP3.**
**Answer: poplib** — poplib for receiving; smtplib for sending.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A message waits in the receiver's ____ on the mail server until it is collected.**
**Answer: mailbox** — The mailbox is why a switched-off computer loses nothing.
