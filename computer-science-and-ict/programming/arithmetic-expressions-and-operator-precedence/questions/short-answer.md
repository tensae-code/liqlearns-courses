# Arithmetic Expressions and Operator Precedence — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an arithmetic expression?**
**Answer: A piece of a program that works out a number**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What joins the values in an expression?**
**Answer: Operators**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when an expression is evaluated?**
**Answer: It is replaced by the single number it works out to**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the rest of the program use?**
**Answer: The number the expression worked out to**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Give the example expression the book's teaching uses.**
**Answer: 2 + 3 × 4**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does precedence fix?**
**Answer: Which operation is done first**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which operations come before addition and subtraction?**
**Answer: Multiplication and division**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does grouping change the order?**
**Answer: The part inside brackets is worked out first**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is 2 + 3 × 4?**
**Answer: 14**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is (2 + 3) × 4, and what made the difference?**
**Answer: 20 — the brackets**
