# Arithmetic Expressions and Operator Precedence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an arithmetic expression?**
a) A piece of a program that works out a number  b) Operators  c) The number the expression worked out to  d) Which operation is done first
**Answer: a) A piece of a program that works out a number**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What joins the values in an expression?**
a) It is replaced by the single number it works out to  b) Operators  c) 2 + 3 × 4  d) Multiplication and division
**Answer: b) Operators**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when an expression is evaluated?**
a) The number the expression worked out to  b) Which operation is done first  c) It is replaced by the single number it works out to  d) The part inside brackets is worked out first
**Answer: c) It is replaced by the single number it works out to**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the rest of the program use?**
a) 2 + 3 × 4  b) Multiplication and division  c) 14  d) The number the expression worked out to
**Answer: d) The number the expression worked out to**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Give the example expression the book's teaching uses.**
a) 2 + 3 × 4  b) Which operation is done first  c) The part inside brackets is worked out first  d) 20 — the brackets
**Answer: a) 2 + 3 × 4**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does precedence fix?**
a) Multiplication and division  b) Which operation is done first  c) 14  d) A piece of a program that works out a number
**Answer: b) Which operation is done first**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which operations come before addition and subtraction?**
a) The part inside brackets is worked out first  b) 20 — the brackets  c) Multiplication and division  d) Operators
**Answer: c) Multiplication and division**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does grouping change the order?**
a) 14  b) A piece of a program that works out a number  c) It is replaced by the single number it works out to  d) The part inside brackets is worked out first
**Answer: d) The part inside brackets is worked out first**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is 2 + 3 × 4?**
a) 14  b) 20 — the brackets  c) Operators  d) The number the expression worked out to
**Answer: a) 14**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is (2 + 3) × 4, and what made the difference?**
a) A piece of a program that works out a number  b) 20 — the brackets  c) It is replaced by the single number it works out to  d) 2 + 3 × 4
**Answer: b) 20 — the brackets**
