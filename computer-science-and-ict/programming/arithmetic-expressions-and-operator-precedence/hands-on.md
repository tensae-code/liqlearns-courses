# Hands-on — Arithmetic Expressions and Operator Precedence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two answers, one expression
Write 5 + 2 × 3. Work it out by precedence, then add brackets to make a different answer, and explain both to a companion.

## Bracket hunt
Take a long expression from a companion. Put brackets in two different places and show the two different values it evaluates to.
