# Hands-on — Controlling a Sound's Volume in Scratch

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Whisper and shout
Give one sprite a sound. Play it at 100%, then set the volume to 30% and play it again. Say what changed and which block did it.

## Fade the music
Start a music sound and fade it: change the volume down in steps inside a repeat, until it reaches 0%. Then bring it back up. Write the steps you used.
