# Controlling a Sound's Volume in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is volume?**
**Answer: How loud a sound plays**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is Scratch's volume given?**
**Answer: As a percentage — 100% is full, 0% is silence**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Whose setting is the volume?**
**Answer: The sprite whose sounds are playing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Can two sprites have different volumes?**
**Answer: Yes — one loud, another quiet, in the same project**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When is the volume set?**
**Answer: Before the moment it must serve**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does set volume do?**
**Answer: It puts the loudness at an exact percentage**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does change volume do?**
**Answer: It raises or lowers the loudness by an amount**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do you fade a sound out?**
**Answer: Lower the volume step by step until it is gone**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which sounds do the volume blocks affect?**
**Answer: The sounds the sprite plays after them**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which older pair do set and change volume match?**
**Answer: The set and change pair met with variables**
