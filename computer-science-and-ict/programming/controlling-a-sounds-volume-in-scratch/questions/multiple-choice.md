# Controlling a Sound's Volume in Scratch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is volume?**
a) How loud a sound plays  b) As a percentage — 100% is full, 0% is silence  c) The sprite whose sounds are playing  d) Yes — one loud, another quiet, in the same project
**Answer: a) How loud a sound plays**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is Scratch's volume given?**
a) The sprite whose sounds are playing  b) As a percentage — 100% is full, 0% is silence  c) Yes — one loud, another quiet, in the same project  d) Before the moment it must serve
**Answer: b) As a percentage — 100% is full, 0% is silence**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Whose setting is the volume?**
a) Yes — one loud, another quiet, in the same project  b) Before the moment it must serve  c) The sprite whose sounds are playing  d) It puts the loudness at an exact percentage
**Answer: c) The sprite whose sounds are playing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Can two sprites have different volumes?**
a) Before the moment it must serve  b) It puts the loudness at an exact percentage  c) It raises or lowers the loudness by an amount  d) Yes — one loud, another quiet, in the same project
**Answer: d) Yes — one loud, another quiet, in the same project**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When is the volume set?**
a) Before the moment it must serve  b) It puts the loudness at an exact percentage  c) It raises or lowers the loudness by an amount  d) Lower the volume step by step until it is gone
**Answer: a) Before the moment it must serve**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does set volume do?**
a) It raises or lowers the loudness by an amount  b) It puts the loudness at an exact percentage  c) Lower the volume step by step until it is gone  d) The sounds the sprite plays after them
**Answer: b) It puts the loudness at an exact percentage**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does change volume do?**
a) Lower the volume step by step until it is gone  b) The sounds the sprite plays after them  c) It raises or lowers the loudness by an amount  d) The set and change pair met with variables
**Answer: c) It raises or lowers the loudness by an amount**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do you fade a sound out?**
a) The sounds the sprite plays after them  b) The set and change pair met with variables  c) How loud a sound plays  d) Lower the volume step by step until it is gone
**Answer: d) Lower the volume step by step until it is gone**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which sounds do the volume blocks affect?**
a) The sounds the sprite plays after them  b) The set and change pair met with variables  c) How loud a sound plays  d) As a percentage — 100% is full, 0% is silence
**Answer: a) The sounds the sprite plays after them**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which older pair do set and change volume match?**
a) How loud a sound plays  b) The set and change pair met with variables  c) As a percentage — 100% is full, 0% is silence  d) The sprite whose sounds are playing
**Answer: b) The set and change pair met with variables**
