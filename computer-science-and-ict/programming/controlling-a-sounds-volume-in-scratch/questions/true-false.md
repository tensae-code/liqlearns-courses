# Controlling a Sound's Volume in Scratch — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is volume?" is: How loud a sound plays**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is Scratch's volume given?" is: It puts the loudness at an exact percentage**
**Answer: False** — As a percentage — 100% is full, 0% is silence

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Whose setting is the volume?" is: The sprite whose sounds are playing**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Can two sprites have different volumes?" is: Lower the volume step by step until it is gone**
**Answer: False** — Yes — one loud, another quiet, in the same project

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "When is the volume set?" is: Before the moment it must serve**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does set volume do?" is: The set and change pair met with variables**
**Answer: False** — It puts the loudness at an exact percentage

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does change volume do?" is: It raises or lowers the loudness by an amount**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How do you fade a sound out?" is: As a percentage — 100% is full, 0% is silence**
**Answer: False** — Lower the volume step by step until it is gone

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which sounds do the volume blocks affect?" is: The sounds the sprite plays after them**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which older pair do set and change volume match?" is: Yes — one loud, another quiet, in the same project**
**Answer: False** — The set and change pair met with variables
