# Hands-on — Controlling a Robot Arm with an HMI

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Choreograph a grip on paper
The arm must pick a cup from a table. Write the sequence as a table: step, joint, direction of movement, what the human checks before the next step. At least six steps, ending with the lift. Mark the step where a mistake would knock the cup over — that is where the interface earns its keep.

## Drive the arm (if the kit is available)
With the Braccio and the Python interface, reproduce your paper sequence at the real arm, one joint at a time, returning every joint to its start angle afterwards. Then record the angle set of the successful grip; replaying it is your first step from driving to automating.
