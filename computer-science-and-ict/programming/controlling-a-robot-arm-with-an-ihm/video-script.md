# Video script — Controlling a Robot Arm with an HMI

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A robot arm is a chain of joints. At each joint, a servomotor: it turns to the angle it is ordered to, and holds it.

Base. Shoulder. Elbow. Wrist. Gripper. Six angles make a grip.

The Arduino is the brain in the middle. It takes commands and drives the motors in sequence, so the arm moves as one creature.

And the human? The human drives through an interface — a Python window, sliders for the joints, commands travelling down the USB cable. The arm has no eyes. The human is its eyes, and its judgement.

Mouse, window, cable, board, motor, joint. That is the whole road of one command.
