# Controlling a Robot Arm with an HMI — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A servomotor’s special ability is to —**
a) Spin as fast as possible  b) Turn to a commanded angle and hold it  c) Generate electricity  d) See objects
**Answer: b) Turn to a commanded angle and hold it — That is what makes it the muscle of a robot joint.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In this build, the Arduino board’s job is to —**
a) Display the window  b) Receive commands and drive the servos in sequence  c) Charge the battery  d) Replace the human
**Answer: b) Receive commands and drive the servos in sequence — It is the embedded brain between the interface and the motors.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The HMI in the book’s build is —**
a) The gripper  b) A Python (Tkinter) window sending commands over the serial link  c) The USB cable itself  d) A camera
**Answer: b) A Python (Tkinter) window sending commands over the serial link — Sliders and buttons for each joint, driven by the human.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the human still inside this control loop?**
a) The arm is decorative  b) The arm has no eyes — the human sees, judges and corrects through the interface  c) The servos need winding  d) The Arduino refuses commands
**Answer: b) The arm has no eyes — the human sees, judges and corrects through the interface — Judgement lives in the HMI half of the system.**
