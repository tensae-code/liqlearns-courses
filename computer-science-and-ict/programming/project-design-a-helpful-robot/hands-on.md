# Hands-on — Project — Design a Helpful Robot

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('My helper, designed', 'Design your helpful robot on one page: the help in one sentence, then its sensing, its doing, and its success sign.')

## Task two
('Four stages, one robot', 'Take a partner's robot design and lay it out in the four stages. Name the stage that was hardest to fill, and why.')
