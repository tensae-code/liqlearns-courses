# Project — Design a Helpful Robot — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the project called?**
**Answer: 'I am the little programmer' — Project 1 of Grade 5 Term 1**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must each pupil design?**
**Answer: A robot that helps them**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must be named first?**
**Answer: The help needed — a real task in the pupil's own day**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What three design questions follow?**
**Answer: What it must sense, what it must do, and how you would know it helped**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why choose a particular help?**
**Answer: A robot designed for everyone helps no one in particular**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four stages the design is worked through.**
**Answer: Goal, smart plan, flowchart, code instructions**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the project prove about the four stages?**
**Answer: They are a way of working, not a page in the book**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Is the robot bought or designed?**
**Answer: Designed — an invention on paper and in plan**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which unit's lessons feed this project?**
**Answer: Unit 1 The Digital World / The Little Programmer lessons**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book sets this project?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 5 Digital Explorer Term 1, Project 1**
