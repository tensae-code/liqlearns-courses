# Hands-on — Separating the Solutions of Algebraic and Transcendent Equations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Bracket hunt** — Tabulate f(x) = x³ − x − 2 at integer steps from −2 to 3, mark the sign change, and argue the bracket holds one root.

## Task two
**Blind-spot case** — Sketch a function with two roots inside [0, 3] and no sign change at the ends; explain the finer tabulation that separates them.
