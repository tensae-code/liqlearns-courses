# Hands-on — Implementing and Improving Algorithms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Implement and track** — Implement a paper algorithm as a program or precise procedure, then track both side by side on three inputs.

## Task two
**Improve and prove** — Take a wasteful algorithm (searching a whole list for a name, checking every pair) and improve it; prove the improvement with counts and tests.
