# Video script — Flowgorithm — your first flowchart

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **Flowgorithm — your first flowchart**.

**the opening screen** — Main (start) and End already created, with an arrow connector between them.

**inserting a symbol** — right-click the arrow connector to open the symbol pop-up, then choose the symbol — it is inserted between start and end.

**the Output symbol's box** — 'Enter an Expression Below' — where 'Welcome!' is typed after double-clicking the symbol.

**running** — clicking the Run icon on the toolbar to see the output.

Now you try. Say each word. Say what it means.
