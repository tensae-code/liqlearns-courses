# Hands-on — Queues and Waiting Lines

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Time a real queue', 'Observe one real queue for fifteen minutes: count arrivals and services, note the longest wait, and say where its anatomy strained.')

## Task two
('Run the change', "On paper, simulate your observed queue with one added server for the same arrivals and compare the waiting line minute by minute.")
