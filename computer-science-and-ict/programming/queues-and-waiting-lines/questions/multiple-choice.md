# Queues and Waiting Lines — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are a queue's three parts?**
a) Arrivals, the queue itself, and service  b) First in, first out  c) Waiting time, queue length, and how busy the servers stay  d) Arrivals bunch irregularly, so lines build and clear slowly even when averages look safe
**Answer: a) Arrivals, the queue itself, and service**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What order does a queue usually serve in?**
a) Waiting time, queue length, and how busy the servers stay  b) First in, first out  c) Arrivals bunch irregularly, so lines build and clear slowly even when averages look safe  d) What the manager can change — servers, service speed, appointments spreading arrivals
**Answer: b) First in, first out**
