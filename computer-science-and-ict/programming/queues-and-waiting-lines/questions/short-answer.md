# Queues and Waiting Lines — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are a queue's three parts?**
**Answer: Arrivals, the queue itself, and service**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What order does a queue usually serve in?**
**Answer: First in, first out**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three numbers that matter in a queue**
**Answer: Waiting time, queue length, and how busy the servers stay**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the average alone never tell a queue's story?**
**Answer: Arrivals bunch irregularly, so lines build and clear slowly even when averages look safe**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a queue simulation vary?**
**Answer: What the manager can change — servers, service speed, appointments spreading arrivals**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to waiting time near capacity?**
**Answer: It does not grow gently — it explodes**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Compare 95% busy with 80% busy**
**Answer: The 95% system can hold queues many times longer**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why are queues simulation's home ground?**
**Answer: A real queue cannot be experimented on without experimenting on real people**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What order do arrivals run in a simulation?**
**Answer: Minute by minute, or arrival by arrival**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Egypt place queues?**
**Answer: As the closing lesson of the Grade 10 simulation chapter, after modelling and simulations**
