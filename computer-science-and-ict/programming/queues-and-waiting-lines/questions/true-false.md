# Queues and Waiting Lines — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are a queue's three parts?" is: Arrivals, the queue itself, and service**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What order does a queue usually serve in?" is: Waiting time, queue length, and how busy the servers stay**
**Answer: False** — First in, first out

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the three numbers that matter in a queue?" is: Waiting time, queue length, and how busy the servers stay**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why does the average alone never tell a queue's story?" is: What the manager can change — servers, service speed, appointments spreading arrivals**
**Answer: False** — Arrivals bunch irregularly, so lines build and clear slowly even when averages look safe

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a queue simulation vary?" is: What the manager can change — servers, service speed, appointments spreading arrivals**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens to waiting time near capacity?" is: The 95% system can hold queues many times longer**
**Answer: False** — It does not grow gently — it explodes

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Compare 95% busy with 80% busy?" is: The 95% system can hold queues many times longer**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why are queues simulation's home ground?" is: Minute by minute, or arrival by arrival**
**Answer: False** — A real queue cannot be experimented on without experimenting on real people

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What order do arrivals run in a simulation?" is: Minute by minute, or arrival by arrival**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does Egypt place queues?" is: Arrivals, the queue itself, and service**
**Answer: False** — As the closing lesson of the Grade 10 simulation chapter, after modelling and simulations
