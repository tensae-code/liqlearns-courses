# An Alarm Clock in Scratch — Using the Current Time — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do the current-time blocks give?" is: Year, month, date, day of week, hour, minute and second**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are the alarm’s three buttons?" is: A custom block**
**Answer: False** — Set alarm, Stop and Snooze

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What switches the ringing off?" is: A yes/no variable**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What turns the weekday number into its name?" is: As lines with their centre at one end**
**Answer: False** — A custom block

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How long does snooze silence the alarm?" is: Ten minutes**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How are the clock’s hands drawn?" is: Minutes × 6 degrees**
**Answer: False** — As lines with their centre at one end

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "By what formula is the seconds hand pointed?" is: Seconds × 6 degrees**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "By what formula is the minutes hand pointed?" is: Times such as prayer times**
**Answer: False** — Minutes × 6 degrees

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "By what formula is the hours hand pointed?" is: Hours × 30 + minutes × 0.5 degrees**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What set times does the project’s alarm ring at?" is: Set alarm, Stop and Snooze**
**Answer: False** — Times such as prayer times
