# An Alarm Clock in Scratch — Using the Current Time — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do the current-time blocks give?**
**Answer: Year, month, date, day of week, hour, minute and second**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the alarm’s three buttons?**
**Answer: Set alarm, Stop and Snooze**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What switches the ringing off?**
**Answer: A yes/no variable**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What turns the weekday number into its name?**
**Answer: A custom block**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How long does snooze silence the alarm?**
**Answer: Ten minutes**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How are the clock’s hands drawn?**
**Answer: As lines with their centre at one end**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) By what formula is the seconds hand pointed?**
**Answer: Seconds × 6 degrees**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) By what formula is the minutes hand pointed?**
**Answer: Minutes × 6 degrees**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) By what formula is the hours hand pointed?**
**Answer: Hours × 30 + minutes × 0.5 degrees**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What set times does the project’s alarm ring at?**
**Answer: Times such as prayer times**
