# An Alarm Clock in Scratch — Using the Current Time — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do the current-time blocks give?**
a) Year, month, date, day of week, hour, minute and second  b) Set alarm, Stop and Snooze  c) A custom block  d) As lines with their centre at one end
**Answer: a) Year, month, date, day of week, hour, minute and second**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the alarm’s three buttons?**
a) A yes/no variable  b) Set alarm, Stop and Snooze  c) Ten minutes  d) Seconds × 6 degrees
**Answer: b) Set alarm, Stop and Snooze**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What switches the ringing off?**
a) A custom block  b) As lines with their centre at one end  c) A yes/no variable  d) Minutes × 6 degrees
**Answer: c) A yes/no variable**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What turns the weekday number into its name?**
a) Ten minutes  b) Seconds × 6 degrees  c) Hours × 30 + minutes × 0.5 degrees  d) A custom block
**Answer: d) A custom block**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How long does snooze silence the alarm?**
a) Ten minutes  b) As lines with their centre at one end  c) Minutes × 6 degrees  d) Times such as prayer times
**Answer: a) Ten minutes**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How are the clock’s hands drawn?**
a) Seconds × 6 degrees  b) As lines with their centre at one end  c) Hours × 30 + minutes × 0.5 degrees  d) Year, month, date, day of week, hour, minute and second
**Answer: b) As lines with their centre at one end**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) By what formula is the seconds hand pointed?**
a) Minutes × 6 degrees  b) Times such as prayer times  c) Seconds × 6 degrees  d) Set alarm, Stop and Snooze
**Answer: c) Seconds × 6 degrees**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) By what formula is the minutes hand pointed?**
a) Hours × 30 + minutes × 0.5 degrees  b) Year, month, date, day of week, hour, minute and second  c) A yes/no variable  d) Minutes × 6 degrees
**Answer: d) Minutes × 6 degrees**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) By what formula is the hours hand pointed?**
a) Hours × 30 + minutes × 0.5 degrees  b) Times such as prayer times  c) Set alarm, Stop and Snooze  d) A custom block
**Answer: a) Hours × 30 + minutes × 0.5 degrees**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What set times does the project’s alarm ring at?**
a) Year, month, date, day of week, hour, minute and second  b) Times such as prayer times  c) A yes/no variable  d) Ten minutes
**Answer: b) Times such as prayer times**
