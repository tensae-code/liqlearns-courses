# Hands-on — Arithmetic Blocks and Random Numbers

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Compute, then use
Build a script that adds two numbers and makes the sprite move by the answer. Predict the move before running, then check.

## Chance in range
Set a random block from 1 to 6 as a dice. Run it ten times, tally the results, and confirm every answer fell inside the range.
