**Book 2:** *Secondary School Teacher's Guide — Information and Communication Technology* (teacher guide) — Trinidad and Tobago, Ministry of Education, Curriculum Development Division, Republic of Trinidad and Tobago, 2014. Forms 1-3. No ISBN printed. 65 PDF pp. Read in full. Only its three sample lesson plans carry placeable ICT content; the rest is general pedagogy and assessment. Stocked as evidence. Trinidad and Tobago teaches ICT as a spiral NCSE subject over Forms 1–3 — the same six modules return each year at increasing complexity — and the guide's sample lessons are constructivist and project-based: this programming lesson starts from folding a paper item and writing instructions another pair must follow and peer-check.
