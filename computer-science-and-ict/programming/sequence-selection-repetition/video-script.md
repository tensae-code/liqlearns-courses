The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **Sequence, selection, repetition**.

**sequence** — execution top-down in the order the instructions are placed.

**selection** — execution or a jump depending on a decision's outcome — the condition kind of flow.

**repetition** — execution repeated — a fixed number of times, or until a condition occurs.

**the promotion example** — a Grade 9 average under 50% blocks Grade 10 — a selection.
