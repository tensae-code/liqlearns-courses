# Sequence, selection, repetition — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is sequence?
a) execution top-down in the order the instructions are placed  b) execution or a jump depending on a decision's outcome — the condition kind of flow  c) execution repeated — a fixed number of times, or until a condition occurs  d) a Grade 9 average under 50% blocks Grade 10 — a selection
**Answer: a) execution top-down in the order the instructions are placed**

**2.** What is selection?
a) execution top-down in the order the instructions are placed  b) execution repeated — a fixed number of times, or until a condition occurs  c) a Grade 9 average under 50% blocks Grade 10 — a selection  d) execution or a jump depending on a decision's outcome — the condition kind of flow
**Answer: d) execution or a jump depending on a decision's outcome — the condition kind of flow**

**3.** What is repetition?
a) execution or a jump depending on a decision's outcome — the condition kind of flow  b) a Grade 9 average under 50% blocks Grade 10 — a selection  c) execution repeated — a fixed number of times, or until a condition occurs  d) execution top-down in the order the instructions are placed
**Answer: c) execution repeated — a fixed number of times, or until a condition occurs**

**4.** What is the promotion example?
a) execution repeated — a fixed number of times, or until a condition occurs  b) a Grade 9 average under 50% blocks Grade 10 — a selection  c) execution top-down in the order the instructions are placed  d) execution or a jump depending on a decision's outcome — the condition kind of flow
**Answer: b) a Grade 9 average under 50% blocks Grade 10 — a selection**

**5.** Adding a list of 100 numbers is best done with —
a) sequence only  b) repetition  c) one terminator  d) pseudocode only
**Answer: b) repetition**

**6.** The umbrella question — open it outside, not inside — is —
a) repetition  b) selection  c) sequence  d) input
**Answer: b) selection**
