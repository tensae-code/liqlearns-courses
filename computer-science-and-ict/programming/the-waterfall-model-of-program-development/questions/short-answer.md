# The Waterfall Model of Program Development — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the seven stages in order.**
**Answer: Analysis, design, coding, testing, debugging, implementation, maintenance**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does analysis settle?**
**Answer: The exact problem, and what counts as its answer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is designed before any code?**
**Answer: The algorithm, the data and the solution's shape**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the difference between testing and debugging?**
**Answer: Testing shows a fault's presence; debugging finds its home**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is implementation?**
**Answer: Putting the program to work in its real place with real users**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is maintenance?**
**Answer: Keeping the program true as the world changes**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which stage is longest in a program's life?**
**Answer: Maintenance**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the waterfall's character?**
**Answer: Each stage completed and checked before the next begins**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does order matter economically?**
**Answer: A fault found one stage down costs ten times more**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does the waterfall relate to the models lesson?**
**Answer: It is the one-direction model, named and staged**
