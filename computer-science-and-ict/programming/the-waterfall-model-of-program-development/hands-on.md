# Hands-on — The Waterfall Model of Program Development

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Waterfall a real build** — Take one program you have built. Write its biography as the seven stages — what actually happened at each, and what the waterfall would have ordered differently.

## Task two
**The expensive fault** — Invent one fault at analysis and trace its cost at every later stage. Present the bill the waterfall's order would have avoided.
