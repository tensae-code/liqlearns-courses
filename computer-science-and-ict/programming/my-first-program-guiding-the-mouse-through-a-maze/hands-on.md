# Hands-on — My First Program — Guiding the Mouse Through a Maze

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Paper maze** — Draw a 5×5 maze, write its arrow program, and walk a counter through it exactly as written; mark where any wrong arrow sends you.

## Task two
**Stage swap** — Make a maze for a partner and keep its solution program hidden; your partner writes their own program — compare the two roads.
