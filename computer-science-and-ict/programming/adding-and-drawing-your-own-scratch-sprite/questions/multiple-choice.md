# Adding and Drawing Your Own Scratch Sprite — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Ready-made characters in Scratch are found in the —**
a) Library  b) Sound tab  c) Backdrop list
**Answer: a) Library** — The sprite library holds ready sprites.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The tool that fills a closed area with colour is —**
a) Eraser  b) Fill  c) Select
**Answer: b) Fill** — Fill paints an area with colour.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) To bend a shape by moving its points, use —**
a) Reshape  b) Text  c) Brush
**Answer: a) Reshape** — Reshape bends a shape by its points.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A library sprite's look —**
a) Can never change  b) Can be edited in the paint editor  c) Changes by itself
**Answer: b) Can be edited in the paint editor** — Open its costume and edit it with the paint tools.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) A completely new character that exists nowhere in the library is made by —**
a) Waiting  b) Drawing it in the paint editor  c) Clicking the flag
**Answer: b) Drawing it in the paint editor** — Draw your own sprite from nothing.
