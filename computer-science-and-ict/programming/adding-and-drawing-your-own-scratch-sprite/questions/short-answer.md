# Adding and Drawing Your Own Scratch Sprite — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Name three paint tools and say what each did in a sprite you drew or edited.**
**Answer:** A sound answer names any three of select, reshape, brush, eraser, fill, text, line, circle, rectangle, each matched to its real job in the drawing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Your story needs a purple fish and the library fish is blue. What are your two choices?**
**Answer:** A sound answer: use the library fish and edit its costume's colour in the paint editor, or draw a purple fish of your own from nothing.
