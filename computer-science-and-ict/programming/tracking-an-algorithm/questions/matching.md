# Tracking an Algorithm — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is tracking an algorithm?** → **Working through it step by step with chosen values, recording the state**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a tracking table?** → **A row per step, a column per variable**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does the tracker play the computer?** → **Honestly — no skipping, every step's arithmetic done and written**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is tracking used to check?** → **Algorithms, before a computer is touched**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where is a fault cornered?** → **The row where the table first goes wrong**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a loop tracked?** → **A row per turn, until the condition fails**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What two values does the lesson watch in a loop?** → **The counter and the accumulator**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How are conditions tracked?** → **By marking the branch taken at each meeting**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the finished loop table show?** → **The loop's whole story at a glance**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How are writing and tracking one craft?** → **The writer composes steps the tracker can follow; the tracker reads steps the writer answers for**
