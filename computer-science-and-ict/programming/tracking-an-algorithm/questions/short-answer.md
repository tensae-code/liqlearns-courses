# Tracking an Algorithm — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is tracking an algorithm?**
**Answer: Working through it step by step with chosen values, recording the state**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a tracking table?**
**Answer: A row per step, a column per variable**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does the tracker play the computer?**
**Answer: Honestly — no skipping, every step's arithmetic done and written**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is tracking used to check?**
**Answer: Algorithms, before a computer is touched**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where is a fault cornered?**
**Answer: The row where the table first goes wrong**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a loop tracked?**
**Answer: A row per turn, until the condition fails**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What two values does the lesson watch in a loop?**
**Answer: The counter and the accumulator**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How are conditions tracked?**
**Answer: By marking the branch taken at each meeting**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the finished loop table show?**
**Answer: The loop's whole story at a glance**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How are writing and tracking one craft?**
**Answer: The writer composes steps the tracker can follow; the tracker reads steps the writer answers for**
