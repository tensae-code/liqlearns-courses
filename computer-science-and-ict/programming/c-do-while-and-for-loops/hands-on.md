# Hands-on — C Do While and for Loops

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Chapter 12 §12.3 do-while Loop (test at the end, body runs at least once, ends with a semicolon; Example 2: force a valid telephone-line state w or d; while vs do-while) and the for loop (initialization, test condition, increment/decrement; which parts may be omitted) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach c do while and for loops to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
