# Character Arrays and Searching — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What ends a character array's text?" is: The null terminator, '\0'**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "In char word[10] = "Skopje", where does the terminator sit?" is: Yes — they take elements of their own**
**Answer: False** — Element 6 — after the six letters

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the difference between capacity and length?" is: Capacity is the declared size; length counts up to the terminator**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is a text's length measured?" is: At the terminator, with not-found**
**Answer: False** — By walking until the terminator

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Are spaces characters in the array?" is: Yes — they take elements of their own**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When does a search stop with success?" is: No — different characters, different codes**
**Answer: False** — On a match — reporting the position

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When does it stop with failure?" is: At the terminator, with not-found**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How does counting occurrences differ from searching?" is: The null terminator, '\0'**
**Answer: False** — It counts and continues instead of stopping at the first match

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Are 'A' and 'a' the same character?" is: No — different characters, different codes**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What must a search state about case?" is: Capacity is the declared size; length counts up to the terminator**
**Answer: False** — Which case it means
