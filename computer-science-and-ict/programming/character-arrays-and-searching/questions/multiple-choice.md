# Character Arrays and Searching — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What ends a character array's text?**
a) The null terminator, '\0'  b) Element 6 — after the six letters  c) Capacity is the declared size; length counts up to the terminator  d) By walking until the terminator
**Answer: a) The null terminator, '\0'**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In char word[10] = "Skopje", where does the terminator sit?**
a) Capacity is the declared size; length counts up to the terminator  b) Element 6 — after the six letters  c) By walking until the terminator  d) Yes — they take elements of their own
**Answer: b) Element 6 — after the six letters**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the difference between capacity and length?**
a) By walking until the terminator  b) Yes — they take elements of their own  c) Capacity is the declared size; length counts up to the terminator  d) On a match — reporting the position
**Answer: c) Capacity is the declared size; length counts up to the terminator**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a text's length measured?**
a) Yes — they take elements of their own  b) On a match — reporting the position  c) At the terminator, with not-found  d) By walking until the terminator
**Answer: d) By walking until the terminator**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Are spaces characters in the array?**
a) Yes — they take elements of their own  b) On a match — reporting the position  c) At the terminator, with not-found  d) It counts and continues instead of stopping at the first match
**Answer: a) Yes — they take elements of their own**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When does a search stop with success?**
a) At the terminator, with not-found  b) On a match — reporting the position  c) It counts and continues instead of stopping at the first match  d) No — different characters, different codes
**Answer: b) On a match — reporting the position**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When does it stop with failure?**
a) It counts and continues instead of stopping at the first match  b) No — different characters, different codes  c) At the terminator, with not-found  d) Which case it means
**Answer: c) At the terminator, with not-found**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does counting occurrences differ from searching?**
a) No — different characters, different codes  b) Which case it means  c) The null terminator, '\0'  d) It counts and continues instead of stopping at the first match
**Answer: d) It counts and continues instead of stopping at the first match**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Are 'A' and 'a' the same character?**
a) No — different characters, different codes  b) Which case it means  c) The null terminator, '\0'  d) Element 6 — after the six letters
**Answer: a) No — different characters, different codes**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must a search state about case?**
a) The null terminator, '\0'  b) Which case it means  c) Element 6 — after the six letters  d) Capacity is the declared size; length counts up to the terminator
**Answer: b) Which case it means**
