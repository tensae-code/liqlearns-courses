# Hands-on — Character Arrays and Searching

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace the hunt
For char t[] = "Bitola", hand-trace a search for 'o' and then for 'x': list each compared element and where each hunt stops, and why.

## Count them
Write (code or exact steps) a routine counting how many times 'a' appears in a character array. Test it mentally on "Skopje" and on "Vardar".
