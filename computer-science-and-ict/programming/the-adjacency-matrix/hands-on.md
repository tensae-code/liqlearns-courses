# Hands-on — The Adjacency Matrix

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Graph to table, table to graph
Draw a five-node graph of your own, then write its adjacency matrix. Cover the drawing, hand the matrix to a partner, and have them redraw the graph from the table alone. Compare — any difference is a wrong cell, and you can point to it.

## Degree from rows
Using the lesson's matrix, write each node's degree as its row sum. Then count the degrees on the drawing. The two lists must match exactly — the matrix knows the graph.
