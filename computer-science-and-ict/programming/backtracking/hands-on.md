# Hands-on — Backtracking

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Trace the pruning** — For the 4-queens problem, trace the search by hand: place queens row by row, and mark every point where a partial build fails and the search backtracks.

## Task two
**Skeleton first** — Write the backtracking skeleton in pseudocode — place, check, recurse, remove — then say what pieces, choices and conditions a maze-escape problem would give it.
