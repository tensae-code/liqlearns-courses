# Hands-on — Scratch Movement Blocks — Move, Turn and Point

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Walk a square', 'Build a script that walks the sprite in a square using only move and turn blocks. Read it first and say the turns' total before running it.')

## Task two
('Glide home', "Point the sprite toward a corner of the stage and glide it there in two seconds; then glide it back in four. Say which journey was slower and why.")
