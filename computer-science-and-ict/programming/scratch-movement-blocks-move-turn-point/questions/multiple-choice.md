# Scratch Movement Blocks — Move, Turn and Point — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the move block do?**
a) Steps the sprite forward by the number in the block  b) The direction it faces  c) The direction the sprite faces, by degrees  d) A bigger move number
**Answer: a) Steps the sprite forward by the number in the block**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In which direction does move carry the sprite?**
a) The direction the sprite faces, by degrees  b) The direction it faces  c) A bigger move number  d) Add up its steps and turns and say where the sprite will end
**Answer: b) The direction it faces**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the turn block change?**
a) A bigger move number  b) Add up its steps and turns and say where the sprite will end  c) The direction the sprite faces, by degrees  d) Sets the sprite's direction at once
**Answer: c) The direction the sprite faces, by degrees**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a longer walk?**
a) Add up its steps and turns and say where the sprite will end  b) Sets the sprite's direction at once  c) So the move goes the chosen way  d) A bigger move number
**Answer: d) A bigger move number**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What should you do before running a movement script?**
a) Add up its steps and turns and say where the sprite will end  b) Sets the sprite's direction at once  c) So the move goes the chosen way  d) Moves the sprite smoothly to a place over a number of seconds
**Answer: a) Add up its steps and turns and say where the sprite will end**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does point do?**
a) So the move goes the chosen way  b) Sets the sprite's direction at once  c) Moves the sprite smoothly to a place over a number of seconds  d) A slower journey
**Answer: b) Sets the sprite's direction at once**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why is direction set before a move?**
a) Moves the sprite smoothly to a place over a number of seconds  b) A slower journey  c) So the move goes the chosen way  d) Face the right way first, then travel
**Answer: c) So the move goes the chosen way**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does glide do?**
a) A slower journey  b) Face the right way first, then travel  c) Steps the sprite forward by the number in the block  d) Moves the sprite smoothly to a place over a number of seconds
**Answer: d) Moves the sprite smoothly to a place over a number of seconds**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does a longer glide time mean?**
a) A slower journey  b) Face the right way first, then travel  c) Steps the sprite forward by the number in the block  d) The direction it faces
**Answer: a) A slower journey**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the order this lesson teaches?**
a) Steps the sprite forward by the number in the block  b) Face the right way first, then travel  c) The direction it faces  d) The direction the sprite faces, by degrees
**Answer: b) Face the right way first, then travel**
