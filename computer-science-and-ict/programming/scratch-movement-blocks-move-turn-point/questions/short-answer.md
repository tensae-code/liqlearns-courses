# Scratch Movement Blocks — Move, Turn and Point — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the move block do?**
**Answer: Steps the sprite forward by the number in the block**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In which direction does move carry the sprite?**
**Answer: The direction it faces**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the turn block change?**
**Answer: The direction the sprite faces, by degrees**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a longer walk?**
**Answer: A bigger move number**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What should you do before running a movement script?**
**Answer: Add up its steps and turns and say where the sprite will end**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does point do?**
**Answer: Sets the sprite's direction at once**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why is direction set before a move?**
**Answer: So the move goes the chosen way**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does glide do?**
**Answer: Moves the sprite smoothly to a place over a number of seconds**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does a longer glide time mean?**
**Answer: A slower journey**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the order this lesson teaches?**
**Answer: Face the right way first, then travel**
