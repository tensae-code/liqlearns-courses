# Video script — Project Work — Graphs, Coding, Networks and AI

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A project makes a thing. First, one goal sentence you can check.

Break the goal into steps. Every step gets an owner. Milestones get dates.

Keep a short log. At the end, test the product against the goal — honestly.

The menu: a route-finder from graphs. A cipher tool from coding. A network map. A small trained AI.

The crown piece is a social bot: it reads a message, matches a pattern, answers in character.

And when no pattern fits, you see the border of rules — from inside your own build.
