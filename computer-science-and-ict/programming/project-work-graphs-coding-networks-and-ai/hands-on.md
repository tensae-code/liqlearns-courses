# Hands-on — Project Work — Graphs, Coding, Networks and AI

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Plan it in twenty minutes
In a team, choose one menu build and produce the one-page plan: goal sentence, steps with owners, three milestones with dates, and the final test the product must pass. Swap plans with another team and attack theirs: find one step with no owner, or one goal that cannot be checked. Fix yours from their attack before any building starts.

## Bot in a box
Build the social bot on paper: write six pattern cards (patterns on the left, answers on the right) plus one fallback card. A partner sends ten written messages, one at a time; you answer only with cards, and log every message that forced the fallback. Present the log as your result: it is a measured map of what rules cannot foresee.
