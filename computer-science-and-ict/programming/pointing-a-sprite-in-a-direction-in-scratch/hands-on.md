# Hands-on — Pointing a Sprite in a Direction in Scratch

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Compass faces
In Scratch, point a sprite to 90, 0, 180 and −90 in turn. After each, say in words which way it faces, before you run the next.

## Direct a journey
Program a sprite to cross the stage in three legs: point right and move, point down and move, point left and move. Read your directions aloud as the sprite obeys them.
