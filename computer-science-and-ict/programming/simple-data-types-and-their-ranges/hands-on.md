# Hands-on — Simple Data Types and Their Ranges

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Edge table** — For your language, find the ranges of its whole-number types (sizes in bits, smallest, largest). Present them as a table sorted by size.

## Task two
**Break it on purpose** — In a program, set a small integer variable to its type's largest value and add 1. Record exactly what happens, then change the type and repeat. Write the lesson in two sentences.
