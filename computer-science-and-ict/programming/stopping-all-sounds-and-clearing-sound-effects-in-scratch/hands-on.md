# Hands-on — Stopping All Sounds and Clearing Sound Effects in Scratch

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Silence the scene
Start two sprites playing sounds at once. Run stop all sounds from one sprite's script. Say whose sounds stopped, and what that proves about the block.

## Reset a voice
Give a sprite a sound with a strong pitch effect. Play it, clear the effects, and play it again. Then write the two reset blocks in the order a scene change should use them.
