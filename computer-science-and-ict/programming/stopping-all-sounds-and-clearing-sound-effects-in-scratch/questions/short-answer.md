# Stopping All Sounds and Clearing Sound Effects in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does stop all sounds silence?**
**Answer: Every sound playing in the project at that moment**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Whose sounds does it stop?**
**Answer: All sprites' sounds — not only the running sprite's**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When is it used?**
**Answer: At a scene's or a game's end, so the next part starts quiet**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Does it stop sounds started after it?**
**Answer: No — later sounds play normally**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which sounds is it most needed for?**
**Answer: Long sounds and music loops that would play over everything**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does clear sound effects remove?**
**Answer: The pitch and pan effects a sprite's sounds are carrying**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How do its sounds play after clearing?**
**Answer: Plain again, as recorded**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do stop and clear differ?**
**Answer: Stop ends what is sounding now; clear changes future playing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the order for a clean scene change?**
**Answer: Clear the effects, stop the sounds**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the new scene begin with after both?**
**Answer: A clean stage and natural voices**
