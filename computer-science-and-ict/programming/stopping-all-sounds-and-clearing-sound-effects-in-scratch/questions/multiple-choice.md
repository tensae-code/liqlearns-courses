# Stopping All Sounds and Clearing Sound Effects in Scratch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does stop all sounds silence?**
a) Every sound playing in the project at that moment  b) All sprites' sounds — not only the running sprite's  c) At a scene's or a game's end, so the next part starts quiet  d) No — later sounds play normally
**Answer: a) Every sound playing in the project at that moment**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Whose sounds does it stop?**
a) At a scene's or a game's end, so the next part starts quiet  b) All sprites' sounds — not only the running sprite's  c) No — later sounds play normally  d) Long sounds and music loops that would play over everything
**Answer: b) All sprites' sounds — not only the running sprite's**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When is it used?**
a) No — later sounds play normally  b) Long sounds and music loops that would play over everything  c) At a scene's or a game's end, so the next part starts quiet  d) The pitch and pan effects a sprite's sounds are carrying
**Answer: c) At a scene's or a game's end, so the next part starts quiet**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Does it stop sounds started after it?**
a) Long sounds and music loops that would play over everything  b) The pitch and pan effects a sprite's sounds are carrying  c) Plain again, as recorded  d) No — later sounds play normally
**Answer: d) No — later sounds play normally**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which sounds is it most needed for?**
a) Long sounds and music loops that would play over everything  b) The pitch and pan effects a sprite's sounds are carrying  c) Plain again, as recorded  d) Stop ends what is sounding now; clear changes future playing
**Answer: a) Long sounds and music loops that would play over everything**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does clear sound effects remove?**
a) Plain again, as recorded  b) The pitch and pan effects a sprite's sounds are carrying  c) Stop ends what is sounding now; clear changes future playing  d) Clear the effects, stop the sounds
**Answer: b) The pitch and pan effects a sprite's sounds are carrying**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How do its sounds play after clearing?**
a) Stop ends what is sounding now; clear changes future playing  b) Clear the effects, stop the sounds  c) Plain again, as recorded  d) A clean stage and natural voices
**Answer: c) Plain again, as recorded**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do stop and clear differ?**
a) Clear the effects, stop the sounds  b) A clean stage and natural voices  c) Every sound playing in the project at that moment  d) Stop ends what is sounding now; clear changes future playing
**Answer: d) Stop ends what is sounding now; clear changes future playing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the order for a clean scene change?**
a) Clear the effects, stop the sounds  b) A clean stage and natural voices  c) Every sound playing in the project at that moment  d) All sprites' sounds — not only the running sprite's
**Answer: a) Clear the effects, stop the sounds**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the new scene begin with after both?**
a) Every sound playing in the project at that moment  b) A clean stage and natural voices  c) All sprites' sounds — not only the running sprite's  d) At a scene's or a game's end, so the next part starts quiet
**Answer: b) A clean stage and natural voices**
