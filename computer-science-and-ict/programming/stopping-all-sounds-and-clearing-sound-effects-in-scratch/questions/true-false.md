# Stopping All Sounds and Clearing Sound Effects in Scratch — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does stop all sounds silence?" is: Every sound playing in the project at that moment**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Whose sounds does it stop?" is: The pitch and pan effects a sprite's sounds are carrying**
**Answer: False** — All sprites' sounds — not only the running sprite's

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "When is it used?" is: At a scene's or a game's end, so the next part starts quiet**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Does it stop sounds started after it?" is: Stop ends what is sounding now; clear changes future playing**
**Answer: False** — No — later sounds play normally

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which sounds is it most needed for?" is: Long sounds and music loops that would play over everything**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does clear sound effects remove?" is: A clean stage and natural voices**
**Answer: False** — The pitch and pan effects a sprite's sounds are carrying

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How do its sounds play after clearing?" is: Plain again, as recorded**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How do stop and clear differ?" is: All sprites' sounds — not only the running sprite's**
**Answer: False** — Stop ends what is sounding now; clear changes future playing

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the order for a clean scene change?" is: Clear the effects, stop the sounds**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the new scene begin with after both?" is: No — later sounds play normally**
**Answer: False** — A clean stage and natural voices
