# Video script — Stopping All Sounds and Clearing Sound Effects in Scratch

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The stop all sounds block silences every sound playing in the project at that moment — not only the sprite whose script runs it. It is the sound of a scene ending: when a story changes place or a game ends, one block clears the air so the next scene starts quiet. Sounds started after the block play normally — the block stops what is playing; it does not forbid what comes next. Long sounds and music loops, which would otherwise play over everything, are exactly what it is for.
Clear sound effects removes the effects a sprite's sounds are carrying — the pitch and pan settings of the earlier séance — so its sounds play plain again, as recorded. Stopping and clearing are different resets: stop ends what is sounding now; clear changes how future sounds will play. A well-built scene change uses both in order: clear the old scene's effects, stop its sounds, and the new scene begins with a clean stage and natural voices.
