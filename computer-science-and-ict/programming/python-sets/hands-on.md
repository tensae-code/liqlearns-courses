# Hands-on — Python Sets

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Deduplicate** — Take a list with repeats (a class's club choices) and use a set to list each choice once; say what order information you lost.

## Task two
**Venn in code** — Two sets: pupils in the chess club and in the science club. Write the four operations' results in words before running or imagining them.
