# Numbered Lines in BASIC — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What begins every line of a BASIC program?**
**Answer: A line number**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In what order does the computer run the lines?**
**Answer: In number order — not in typing order**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a line number, in the lesson's word?**
**Answer: The line's address**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do numbers make possible later in BASIC?**
**Answer: Branching and loops — sending the program back to a line by its number**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why number in steps of ten?**
**Answer: To leave gaps so new lines can be inserted without renumbering everything**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where does an inserted line between 10 and 20 go?**
**Answer: At 15**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What has BASIC grown in since 1964?**
**Answer: Versions, each adding powers to the standard language**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What shape does every version keep?**
**Answer: The line-numbered shape**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does Sudan's book teach first — a version or the standard?**
**Answer: Standard BASIC**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the program's true order?**
**Answer: The numbers, not the typing**
