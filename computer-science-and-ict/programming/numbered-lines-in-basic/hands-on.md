# Hands-on — Numbered Lines in BASIC

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Insert the forgotten line
Given lines 10, 20 and 30, insert a new instruction between 10 and 20 without changing any existing number.

## Restore the order
Take a shuffled set of numbered BASIC lines, arrange them as the computer will run them, and read the program aloud in that order.
