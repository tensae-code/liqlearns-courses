The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Five problems. Solved in full. Branch by branch — the quadratic first in detail.

Read a, b, c.
First — always first — the discriminant.
D equals b squared, minus 4 a c.

D above zero?
Two real roots, different.
Use the quadratic formula. Twice.

D equal to zero?
The roots are equal. One value, twice.

D below zero?
No real roots by this route. Stop.

The discriminant decides BEFORE the formula.
That is the whole shape of the solution.

And the other four wait in the lesson:
a triangle measured two ways,
the greatest of three,
a prime found by counting divisors,
and RESULT equals U plus V squared, over W.
