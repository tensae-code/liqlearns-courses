# Hands-on — Writing algorithms — worked problems

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Divisor counting race
Pairs take N = 13, 15, 17, 21. Run the keyed loop on paper, tallying exact divisors. First pair with all four verdicts (prime / not prime) correct wins.

## Discriminant sort
Cards carry (a, b, c) triples. Pupils compute D for each and sort the cards into three piles: two roots, equal roots, none — then check two piles by the quadratic formula.
