# Writing algorithms — worked problems — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1.** Area from base and height: area = ______ × base × height.
**Answer: ½ (one half)**

**2.** The ______ tells the nature of a quadratic's roots.
**Answer: discriminant**

**3.** Roots for D > 0 are found by the quadratic ______.
**Answer: formula**
