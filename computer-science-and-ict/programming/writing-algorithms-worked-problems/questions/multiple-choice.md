# Writing algorithms — worked problems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The semi-perimeter s of a triangle is —
a) side1 + side2 + side3  b) (side1 + side2 + side3) / 2  c) side1 × side2 × side3  d) (side1 + side2) / 3
**Answer: b) (side1 + side2 + side3) / 2** — half the perimeter.

**2.** In the greatest-of-three solution, comparisons move the bigger value into —
a) a variable called largest  b) the output at once  c) a flowchart symbol  d) the count
**Answer: a) a variable called largest** — chained comparison into largest.

**3.** In the prime check, a final count of zero means —
a) the number is not prime  b) the number is prime  c) the loop failed  d) the number is even
**Answer: b) the number is prime** — no exact divisors found from 2 to N.

**4.** The discriminant is —
a) b² + 4ac  b) b² − 4ac  c) 4ac − b²  d) a² − 4bc
**Answer: b) b² − 4ac** — it tells the roots' nature.

**5.** Discriminant equal to zero gives —
a) two different roots  b) the equal-roots case  c) no roots  d) three roots
**Answer: b) the equal-roots case** — one value, twice.

**6.** RESULT = (U + V²) / W is computed in the order —
a) divide first, then square  b) square V, add U, then divide by W  c) add W, then square  d) square U first
**Answer: b) square V, add U, then divide by W** — brackets and powers before division.
