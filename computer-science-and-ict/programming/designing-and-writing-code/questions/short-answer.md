# Designing and Writing Code — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does design decide?**
**Answer: The solution's shape — data and structures, the algorithm's steps, the division into subprograms**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why design before writing?**
**Answer: A fault found in design costs an eraser; after writing, a rewrite**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) In what forms is the algorithm designed?**
**Answer: Pseudocode or flowchart**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is each stage's product?**
**Answer: The next stage's orders**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 11 Eisagogi Epistimis H/Y, Ch.2.3**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is writing code?**
**Answer: Translating the design into a chosen language's exact syntax**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What two virtues must the writer hold together?**
**Answer: Fidelity (the code does the design) and clarity (names, layout, comments)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Who is the code's next reader, often?**
**Answer: The writer, later**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) A syntax breach is caught when?**
**Answer: By the translator — compiler or interpreter — before the program runs**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Design stands between which stages?**
**Answer: Analysis before it, writing code after it**
