# if…elif…else — a chain of choices — fill in the blank

Fill in the blank with the right word or symbol.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The keyword that adds an alternative is ____.
**Answer: elif**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tests in a chain are evaluated one after ____.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Age 80 lands in the ____ block.
