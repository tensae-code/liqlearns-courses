# Hands-on — if…elif…else — a chain of choices

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — every activity is done for real, in IDLE or on paper.

## Staircase walk
Draw the marks staircase on the floor or a big sheet. A partner calls marks; you step down rung by rung, saying each test aloud, until you stop on the rung that catches it.

## Chain builder
On cards, write five grade words. Order the test cards so the chain works — then swap the order of two cards, run it in your head, and say what breaks.
