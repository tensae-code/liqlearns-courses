# Executing a Program Step by Step — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does executing mean?**
**Answer: Carrying out the instructions in order**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does each instruction change?**
**Answer: The state — where things are, what values hold**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does the séance execute?**
**Answer: Together: read one instruction, perform it, state the new state aloud**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What two habits are banned?**
**Answer: Skipping and assuming**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Whose discipline is being practised?**
**Answer: The machine's own, by people**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does hand execution show?**
**Answer: What the program actually says — not what its writer meant**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where do bugs live?**
**Answer: In the gap between what was said and what was meant**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can a hand-executing class do before running?**
**Answer: Predict the program's behaviour**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is a machine surprise investigated?**
**Answer: Replay the execution to find the parting step**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) State the séance's order.**
**Answer: Prediction first, machine second**
