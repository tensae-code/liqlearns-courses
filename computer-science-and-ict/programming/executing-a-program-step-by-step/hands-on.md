# Hands-on — Executing a Program Step by Step

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Be the machine** — Execute a provided ten-instruction program as a class with assigned voices (reader, doers, state-speaker). No instruction may be read until the previous state is spoken.

## Task two
**Predict and compare** — For a new program, each pair writes the predicted final state, then executes by hand, then (if a machine version exists) runs it. Score predictions; locate every parting step.
