# ScratchJr Enrichment — Four Extra Builds — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is enrichment in this unit?**
**Answer: Four extra builds for pupils who finish, stretching the unit's blocks into new scenes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four enrichment builds.**
**Answer: The space robot on the moon, the butterfly, a beautiful day, and the exploring space robot**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first truth of the space builds?**
**Answer: New scenery plus old blocks is a new program**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does the exploring robot differ from the first moon build?**
**Answer: It explores — it journeys through its scene rather than posing in it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What movement does the butterfly demand?**
**Answer: Wandering flight, not straight lines**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is 'a beautiful day' built for?**
**Answer: Its mood — a scene that feels beautiful**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does enrichment trust the pupil with?**
**Answer: Their taste — the blocks are given, the beauty is theirs**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Does enrichment repeat the unit's exercises?**
**Answer: No — it stretches the same blocks into new scenes**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which sprites people the moon builds?**
**Answer: A space robot**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book sets these builds?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 4 Digital Explorer Term 1, Unit 2 Lesson 7 (Enrichment)**
