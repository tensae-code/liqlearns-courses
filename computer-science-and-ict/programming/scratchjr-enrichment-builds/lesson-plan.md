## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The space builds — a robot on the moon, and the exploring space robot** — Two builds leave Earth. The **space robot on the moon** places a robot sprite in a moon scene — new background, new character, the same blocks put to a strange world. The **exploring space robot** extends it: the robot **explores** — moving through its scene as an explorer does, a journey rather than a pose. Both teach the enrichment lesson's first truth: new scenery plus old blocks is a new program.
2. **The butterfly and a beautiful day** — The other two builds stay home and slow down. The **butterfly** is movement made graceful — a creature sprite crossing its scene as butterflies do, by wandering flight, not straight lines. **A beautiful day** is a scene built for its mood: background, weather-bright sprites, and small actions that make the day feel beautiful. Enrichment ends the unit by trusting the pupil's taste: the blocks are given; the beauty is theirs.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
