# Hands-on — Computer Modelling and Simulation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## What-if by hand and by machine
Take a simple model (a ball's fall, a shop's day). Change one starting value at a time and record what the model answers. Say which changes you could never test on the real thing, and why.

## Check the model
Find one place where a familiar simulation (a weather forecast, a game world) differs from reality. Name the feature the model probably left out, and what that does to its answers.
