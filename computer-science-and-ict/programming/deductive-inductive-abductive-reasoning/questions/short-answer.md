# Deductive, Inductive and Abductive Reasoning — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does deductive reasoning move from, and to?**
**Answer: From a rule to a case**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How strong is a deductive conclusion?**
**Answer: Certain — if the rule is true and the case falls under it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does inductive reasoning move from, and to?**
**Answer: From cases to a rule**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How strong is an inductive conclusion?**
**Answer: Likely, never certain**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does abductive reasoning move from, and to?**
**Answer: From a result to its most likely cause — the best explanation**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Whose reasoning is abduction?**
**Answer: The diagnoser's — technician, doctor, debugger**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why is abduction the weakest in certainty?**
**Answer: Another cause may explain the same result**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is abduction's strength?**
**Answer: It proposes what to check first**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Deduction proves; induction does what?**
**Answer: Discovers**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 10 SMK Semester 1, Bab 1 C — deductive, inductive, abductive**
