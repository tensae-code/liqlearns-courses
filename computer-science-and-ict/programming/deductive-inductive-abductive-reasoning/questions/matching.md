# Deductive, Inductive and Abductive Reasoning — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does deductive reasoning move from, and to?** → **From a rule to a case**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How strong is a deductive conclusion?** → **Certain — if the rule is true and the case falls under it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does inductive reasoning move from, and to?** → **From cases to a rule**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How strong is an inductive conclusion?** → **Likely, never certain**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does abductive reasoning move from, and to?** → **From a result to its most likely cause — the best explanation**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Whose reasoning is abduction?** → **The diagnoser's — technician, doctor, debugger**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why is abduction the weakest in certainty?** → **Another cause may explain the same result**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is abduction's strength?** → **It proposes what to check first**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Deduction proves; induction does what?** → **Discovers**
