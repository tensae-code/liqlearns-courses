# Hands-on — Deductive, Inductive and Abductive Reasoning

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Name the reasoning** — Write six short arguments from school life, two of each kind. Exchange with a companion: name each other's kinds and mark the one whose conclusion outruns its reasoning.

## Task two
**Abduct, then verify** — A program that worked yesterday fails today. Write three abductive explanations in order of likelihood, and the check that would verify or kill each.
