# Planning and Documenting a Programming Project — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How many times does Kuwait's series teach this project method?**
**Answer: In every year from Grade 6 to Grade 12 — eight placements across the series**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must a project name first?**
**Answer: Its goal — the product to be made, in words a stranger could judge**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What else is named at the start?**
**Answer: The skill areas the project will use**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is the work shared?**
**Answer: Team roles and tasks are divided by name, so each part has an owner**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Are teamwork skills hoped for or taught?**
**Answer: Taught — as part of the method**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the weekly plan show?**
**Answer: The timetable of stages — what will exist by when**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why time a project in stages?**
**Answer: So lateness is visible while it can still be repaired**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is documentation?**
**Answer: A scientific record of what was made and how, with references**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which reference style do the senior grades use?**
**Answer: APA style**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) State the method in four verbs.**
**Answer: Plan the work, share the work, time the work, document the work**
