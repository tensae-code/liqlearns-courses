# Hands-on — A Multi-Scene ScratchJr Project — The School Plan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Build the school** — In teams, plan and build a multi-scene ScratchJr school: at least four scenes, one travelling character, one micro-story per scene. Present the plan beside the project.

## Task two
**Visitor walk** — Swap projects. Visitors walk the other team's school and afterwards draw its plan from memory. Compare with the builders' plan — every missing room is a finding.
