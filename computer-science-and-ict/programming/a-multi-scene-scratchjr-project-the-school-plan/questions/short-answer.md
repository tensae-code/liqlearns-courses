# A Multi-Scene ScratchJr Project — The School Plan — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the extension's project?**
**Answer: The school plan — le plan de l'école**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does each place become?**
**Answer: A page (scene) with its own background, characters and scripts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the project's craft?**
**Answer: Coherence — the same character recognisable in every scene**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What should moving between scenes feel like?**
**Answer: Walking the school, not changing the channel**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What grows, against the first projects?**
**Answer: Several scenes and several characters in one project**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is drawn first?**
**Answer: The plan — a school map ticking each room's scene, cast and micro-story**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the finished project tested?**
**Answer: Walked by visitors who should never get lost**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a lost visitor locate?**
**Answer: Whether the plan or the scene failed**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does scale teach?**
**Answer: Planning**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does planning make survivable?**
**Answer: Scale**
