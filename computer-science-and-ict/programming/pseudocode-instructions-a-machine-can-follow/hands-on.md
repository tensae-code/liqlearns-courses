# Hands-on — Pseudocode — Instructions a Machine Can Follow

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Write for the robot** — Write pseudocode for one everyday task — sharpening a pencil, crossing the classroom — in steps a literal reader cannot misread.

## Task two
**Run it literally** — Exchange pseudocode. Execute your partner's text with robot literalness, failing honestly at every gap; return it marked at the exact failing words.
