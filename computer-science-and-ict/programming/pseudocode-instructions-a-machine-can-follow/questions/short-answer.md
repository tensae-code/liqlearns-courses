# Pseudocode — Instructions a Machine Can Follow — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is pseudocode?**
**Answer: An algorithm in short, ordered, unambiguous instructions in ordinary words**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must every step name?**
**Answer: One action**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must every decision name?**
**Answer: Its condition**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is it called pseudo?**
**Answer: It is not code a computer runs directly**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is pseudocode for, before real language?**
**Answer: Writing the programme's thinking where humans can check it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does the robot reader execute pseudocode?**
**Answer: Literally — exactly and only what is written**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a missing step, in the robot test?**
**Answer: A fall**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is an endless instruction?**
**Answer: A robot walking into the wall forever**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where do the writers look after a failure?**
**Answer: For the exact word that failed**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is pseudocode that survives a literal reader ready for?**
**Answer: Becoming a real program**
