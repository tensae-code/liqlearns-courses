# Hands-on — Inserting Elements and Computations in Arrays

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fill seven of a hundred
Sketch a program declaring room for 100 numbers that reads exactly 7, then prints their sum and average. Mark every place the number 7 (not 100) must appear.

## Tail trap
A program fills 5 elements of int a[10] and averages by dividing the sum of all 10 elements by 10. Explain both faults and write the corrected computation.
