# Hands-on — Sets and Venn Diagrams in Computing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Draw the class** — Survey your class for two clubs or subjects. Draw the Venn diagram with real counts in every region, and write the union and intersection as sets.

## Task two
**Three circles** — Add a third set to your diagram. Label all eight regions in words ('in A and C but not B'), and find one pupil or invent one for the emptiest region.
