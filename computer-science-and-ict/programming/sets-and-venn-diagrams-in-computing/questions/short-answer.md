# Sets and Venn Diagrams in Computing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a set?**
**Answer: A collection of distinct things — its members — where order does not matter and nothing appears twice**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Indonesian word?**
**Answer: Himpunan**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two sets a program thinks in.**
**Answer: A search result, and a list with duplicates removed**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a set written when listed?**
**Answer: Its members inside braces**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a Venn diagram draw sets as?**
**Answer: Overlapping circles**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the intersection?**
**Answer: The overlap — members in both sets**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the union?**
**Answer: The whole of both circles — members in either set**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the difference of two sets?**
**Answer: The part of one circle outside the other — members in the first but not the second**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How many regions do three overlapping circles make?**
**Answer: Eight**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) AND and OR in programs are which set operations?**
**Answer: Intersection and union — wearing program clothes**
