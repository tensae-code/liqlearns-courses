# Sets and Venn Diagrams in Computing — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A collection of distinct things — its members — where order does not matter and nothing appears twice**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A collection of distinct things — its members — where order does not matter and nothing appears twice** — as the answer to: What is the Indonesian word?
**Answer: False — Himpunan**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A search result, and a list with duplicates removed**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A search result, and a list with duplicates removed** — as the answer to: How is a set written when listed?
**Answer: False — Its members inside braces**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Overlapping circles**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Overlapping circles** — as the answer to: What is the intersection?
**Answer: False — The overlap — members in both sets**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The whole of both circles — members in either set**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The whole of both circles — members in either set** — as the answer to: What is the difference of two sets?
**Answer: False — The part of one circle outside the other — members in the first but not the second**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Eight**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Eight** — as the answer to: AND and OR in programs are which set operations?
**Answer: False — Intersection and union — wearing program clothes**
