# Video script — A Photo Editor in Scratch — Graphic Effects and Slider Variables

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The editor’s controls are **button sprites**: **Rotate**, **Zoom In**, **Zoom Out** and **Reset**, each a sprite whose script changes the photo when clicked. A program’s controls can be sprites themselves — the interface is built from the same pieces as the picture it controls.
Under the buttons, **variables hold the graphic effects** — **brightness, colour, fisheye, pixelate, mosaic, ghost** — and each variable is **changed by a slider on the stage**: drag the slider and the effect deepens or fades at once. The slider is a variable you can pull with a finger.
