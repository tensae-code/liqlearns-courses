# A Photo Editor in Scratch — Graphic Effects and Slider Variables — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the editor’s controls made of?**
**Answer: Button sprites**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the four buttons?**
**Answer: Rotate, Zoom In, Zoom Out and Reset**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When does a button act?**
**Answer: When it is clicked**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are the buttons’ scripts part of?**
**Answer: The same sprite pieces as the picture they control**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is this program, as a kind of thing?**
**Answer: A tool — a photo-improvement program**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What hold the graphic effects?**
**Answer: Variables**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the six effects the book lists.**
**Answer: Brightness, colour, fisheye, pixelate, mosaic and ghost**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How are the effect variables changed?**
**Answer: By sliders on the stage**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when a slider is dragged?**
**Answer: The effect deepens or fades at once**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is a slider, in this lesson’s words?**
**Answer: A variable you can pull with a finger**
