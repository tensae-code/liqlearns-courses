# A Photo Editor in Scratch — Graphic Effects and Slider Variables — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the editor’s controls made of?**
a) Button sprites  b) Rotate, Zoom In, Zoom Out and Reset  c) The same sprite pieces as the picture they control  d) Variables
**Answer: a) Button sprites**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the four buttons?**
a) When it is clicked  b) Rotate, Zoom In, Zoom Out and Reset  c) A tool — a photo-improvement program  d) Brightness, colour, fisheye, pixelate, mosaic and ghost
**Answer: b) Rotate, Zoom In, Zoom Out and Reset**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When does a button act?**
a) The same sprite pieces as the picture they control  b) Variables  c) When it is clicked  d) By sliders on the stage
**Answer: c) When it is clicked**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are the buttons’ scripts part of?**
a) A tool — a photo-improvement program  b) Brightness, colour, fisheye, pixelate, mosaic and ghost  c) The effect deepens or fades at once  d) The same sprite pieces as the picture they control
**Answer: d) The same sprite pieces as the picture they control**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is this program, as a kind of thing?**
a) A tool — a photo-improvement program  b) Variables  c) By sliders on the stage  d) A variable you can pull with a finger
**Answer: a) A tool — a photo-improvement program**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What hold the graphic effects?**
a) Brightness, colour, fisheye, pixelate, mosaic and ghost  b) Variables  c) The effect deepens or fades at once  d) Button sprites
**Answer: b) Variables**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the six effects the book lists.**
a) By sliders on the stage  b) A variable you can pull with a finger  c) Brightness, colour, fisheye, pixelate, mosaic and ghost  d) Rotate, Zoom In, Zoom Out and Reset
**Answer: c) Brightness, colour, fisheye, pixelate, mosaic and ghost**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How are the effect variables changed?**
a) The effect deepens or fades at once  b) Button sprites  c) When it is clicked  d) By sliders on the stage
**Answer: d) By sliders on the stage**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when a slider is dragged?**
a) The effect deepens or fades at once  b) A variable you can pull with a finger  c) Rotate, Zoom In, Zoom Out and Reset  d) The same sprite pieces as the picture they control
**Answer: a) The effect deepens or fades at once**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is a slider, in this lesson’s words?**
a) Button sprites  b) A variable you can pull with a finger  c) When it is clicked  d) A tool — a photo-improvement program
**Answer: b) A variable you can pull with a finger**
