# A Photo Editor in Scratch — Graphic Effects and Slider Variables — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are the editor’s controls made of?" is: Button sprites**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are the four buttons?" is: The same sprite pieces as the picture they control**
**Answer: False** — Rotate, Zoom In, Zoom Out and Reset

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "When does a button act?" is: When it is clicked**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are the buttons’ scripts part of?" is: Variables**
**Answer: False** — The same sprite pieces as the picture they control

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is this program, as a kind of thing?" is: A tool — a photo-improvement program**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What hold the graphic effects?" is: By sliders on the stage**
**Answer: False** — Variables

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name the six effects the book lists." is: Brightness, colour, fisheye, pixelate, mosaic and ghost**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How are the effect variables changed?" is: A variable you can pull with a finger**
**Answer: False** — By sliders on the stage

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens when a slider is dragged?" is: The effect deepens or fades at once**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is a slider, in this lesson’s words?" is: Rotate, Zoom In, Zoom Out and Reset**
**Answer: False** — A variable you can pull with a finger
