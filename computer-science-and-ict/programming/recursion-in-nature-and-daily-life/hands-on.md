# Hands-on — Recursion in Nature and Daily Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Recursion hunt** — Photograph or sketch three recursive patterns near you (a tree, a cauliflower or fern, nesting containers, a staircase's shadow). For each, mark the smaller copy and the base case.

## Task two
**Tell it recursively** — Write the instructions for opening a set of nested boxes as a recursive rule: 'to open box n…' Include the base case. Then write the same for a story-inside-a-story you know.
