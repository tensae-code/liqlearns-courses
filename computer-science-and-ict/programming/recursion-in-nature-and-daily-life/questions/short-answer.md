# Recursion in Nature and Daily Life — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is recursion in the world?**
**Answer: The pattern that contains a smaller copy of itself**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Give two natural examples.**
**Answer: A tree (each branch splits like a small tree) and a river system (each tributary is a small river system)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a family tree recursive?**
**Answer: Each person's ancestors form the same shape as the whole tree**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Armenia Grade 11 teach recursion's idea?**
**Answer: Lesson 11 — 'The idea of recursion. Recursion in nature'**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does Grade 10 add on recursion?**
**Answer: Everyday examples of recursion (Lesson 27), beside the mathematical treatment**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the base case in the nesting dolls?**
**Answer: The innermost doll — solid, holding nothing smaller**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to a recursive program with no base case?**
**Answer: It runs forever — it never bottoms out**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the two code rules this lesson makes unforgettable.**
**Answer: Smaller copy, and base case**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does a family tree bottom out?**
**Answer: At people whose parents the tree does not record**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why teach nature's recursion before code's?**
**Answer: The world's examples make the pattern — and its stopping rule — visible and unforgettable**
