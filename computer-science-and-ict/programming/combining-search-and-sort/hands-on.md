# Hands-on — Combining Search and Sort

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Build the combination', 'Write the combined pseudocode (bubble sort, then binary search) for a list of names. Trace it on 5 names with 2 targets and record the total comparisons.')

## Task two
('Judge three cases', 'For each, choose combined or linear-only and defend by counting questions: (a) find one name in a 1,000-name register, once; (b) a library system answering 200 lookups a day; (c) check whether one mark exists in today's 30 test scores.')
