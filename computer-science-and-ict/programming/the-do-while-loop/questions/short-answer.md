# The do-while Loop — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) When is the condition tested in do-while?**
**Answer: After the body has run**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many times does the body always run?**
**Answer: At least once**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What ends the loop?**
**Answer: The first test that comes out false**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What punctuation follows the while in do-while?**
**Answer: A semicolon**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How does while differ here?**
**Answer: A while loop may run zero times**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the classic use of do-while?**
**Answer: Asking for input and validating the answer**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) In the validation pattern, when does the loop repeat?**
**Answer: While the answer is invalid**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does the book tell you to trace its tasks?**
**Answer: Write the body's runs in a row and mark each test result**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must change inside the body for the loop to end?**
**Answer: Something the condition depends on**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which loop fits when work must precede any test?**
**Answer: do-while**
