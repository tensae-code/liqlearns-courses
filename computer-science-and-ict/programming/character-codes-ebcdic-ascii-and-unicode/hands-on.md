# Hands-on — Character Codes — EBCDIC, ASCII and Unicode

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Code a word three ways** — Write your first name's first letter in ASCII (if it fits), then find its Unicode number, then write the UTF-8 byte count. Note which codes could not hold it if it were a Bangla letter.

## Task two
**Read the Bangla table** — From the printed Bangla Unicode table, find five letters and write their code numbers in order — then arrange the five by number and say what ordering gives you.
