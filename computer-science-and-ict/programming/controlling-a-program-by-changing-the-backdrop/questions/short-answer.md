# Controlling a Program by Changing the Backdrop — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Compare starting a scene with the green flag and with a backdrop switch.**
**Answer:** A sound answer: the flag starts scripts once, at the project's start; the backdrop switch starts a scene's scripts at the moment the story reaches that scene, which can be long after the start.

**2. Your beach sprite appears while the story is still in the forest. What is the likely fault?**
**Answer:** A sound answer: its script is started by the flag or by the forest backdrop event instead of waiting on when-backdrop-switches-to the beach backdrop.
