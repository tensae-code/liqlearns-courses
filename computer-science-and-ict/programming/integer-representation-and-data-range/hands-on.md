# Hands-on — Integer Representation and Data Range

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Range table
Write the unsigned and signed ranges for 4, 8 and 16 bits. Show the pattern working for 4 bits by listing every value.

## Overflow hunt
In a signed byte, work out what 100 + 50 becomes and explain the wrap in one sentence. Then say which everyday count could overflow a signed byte in real life.
