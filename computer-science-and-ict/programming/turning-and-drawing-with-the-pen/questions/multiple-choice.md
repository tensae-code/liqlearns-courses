# Turning and Drawing with the Pen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A quarter-turn is —**
a) 90 degrees  b) 10 degrees  c) 360 degrees
**Answer: a) 90 degrees**

**2. What does pen down do?**
a) Lifts the pen so no line is drawn  b) Deletes the drawing  c) Starts the sprite leaving a line as it moves
**Answer: c) Starts the sprite leaving a line as it moves**

**3. To draw a square, the sprite needs —**
a) no turns at all  b) four equal sides and four 90-degree turns  c) two turns only
**Answer: b) four equal sides and four 90-degree turns**

**4. Turning and pen drawing in Scratch grow out of —**
a) the robot artist's move-and-turn strokes  b) the sound gallery  c) the backdrop library
**Answer: a) the robot artist's move-and-turn strokes**
