# Turning and Drawing with the Pen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Explain, in your own words: turn a sprite by a chosen number of degrees.**
**Answer:** The learner's own explanation, true to the lesson: In Scratch, a sprite turns by degrees and draws with the pen: put the pen down, move and turn, and the sprite's path becomes a drawing.

**2. A classmate gets this lesson's work wrong. What is the most likely fault, and how is it fixed?**
**Answer:** Shape will not close → count the turns: a square needs four equal sides and four 90-degree turns; a shape that misses its start has a wrong side or a wrong turn, never both first.
