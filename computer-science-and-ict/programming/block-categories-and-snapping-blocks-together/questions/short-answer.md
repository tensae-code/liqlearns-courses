# Block Categories and Snapping Blocks Together — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How are blocks sorted in the palette?**
**Answer: Into categories by colour**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a category?**
**Answer: A family of blocks with one kind of job**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do Motion blocks do?**
**Answer: Move the sprite**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do Looks blocks do?**
**Answer: Change how the sprite appears and speaks**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do Sound blocks do?**
**Answer: Play sounds**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How do you find a block?**
**Answer: Name its job first, then go to its colour**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How are blocks joined?**
**Answer: Snapped together — each under the one before it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) In what order does a script run?**
**Answer: From the top to the bottom**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What starts every script?**
**Answer: An Events block**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to a stack with no Events block at its head?**
**Answer: It never knows when to run**
