# Block Categories and Snapping Blocks Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How are blocks sorted in the palette?**
a) Into categories by colour  b) Change how the sprite appears and speaks  c) Name its job first, then go to its colour  d) From the top to the bottom
**Answer: a) Into categories by colour**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a category?**
a) Play sounds  b) A family of blocks with one kind of job  c) Snapped together — each under the one before it  d) An Events block
**Answer: b) A family of blocks with one kind of job**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do Motion blocks do?**
a) Name its job first, then go to its colour  b) From the top to the bottom  c) Move the sprite  d) It never knows when to run
**Answer: c) Move the sprite**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do Looks blocks do?**
a) Snapped together — each under the one before it  b) An Events block  c) Into categories by colour  d) Change how the sprite appears and speaks
**Answer: d) Change how the sprite appears and speaks**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do Sound blocks do?**
a) Play sounds  b) From the top to the bottom  c) It never knows when to run  d) A family of blocks with one kind of job
**Answer: a) Play sounds**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How do you find a block?**
a) An Events block  b) Name its job first, then go to its colour  c) Into categories by colour  d) Move the sprite
**Answer: b) Name its job first, then go to its colour**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How are blocks joined?**
a) It never knows when to run  b) A family of blocks with one kind of job  c) Snapped together — each under the one before it  d) Change how the sprite appears and speaks
**Answer: c) Snapped together — each under the one before it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) In what order does a script run?**
a) Into categories by colour  b) Move the sprite  c) Play sounds  d) From the top to the bottom
**Answer: d) From the top to the bottom**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What starts every script?**
a) An Events block  b) A family of blocks with one kind of job  c) Change how the sprite appears and speaks  d) Name its job first, then go to its colour
**Answer: a) An Events block**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to a stack with no Events block at its head?**
a) Move the sprite  b) It never knows when to run  c) Play sounds  d) Snapped together — each under the one before it
**Answer: b) It never knows when to run**
