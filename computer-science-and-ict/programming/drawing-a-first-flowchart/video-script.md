This is **Drawing a first flowchart**.

**the garden policy** — schools with a garden have it cultivated by students and teachers as Nature study; a new garden is prepared with parents' participation.

**the decision diamond's question** — 'Has the school a botanical garden?' — with Yes and No arrows to the two process boxes.

**the speed-check bands** — 40 km/h and under is reasonable speed; 40–60 is medium — be careful; over 60 is excessive speed, high risk.

**pair work in flowchart lessons** — draw individually, then discuss in pairs, swapping who draws and who argues for each symbol (teacher guide).
