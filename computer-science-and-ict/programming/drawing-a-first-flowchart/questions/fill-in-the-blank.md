# Drawing a first flowchart — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** _____ is schools with a garden have it cultivated by students and teachers as Nature study; a new garden is prepared with parents' participation. (Name the term.)
**Answer: the garden policy**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ is 'Has the school a botanical garden?' — with Yes and No arrows to the two process boxes. (Name the term.)
**Answer: the decision diamond's question**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** _____ is 40 km/h and under is reasonable speed; 40–60 is medium — be careful; over 60 is excessive speed, high risk. (Name the term.)
**Answer: the speed-check bands**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The sign test's question, in the diamond, is: x ______ than zero?
**Answer: less**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Displaying C is the chart's ______ step.
**Answer: output**
