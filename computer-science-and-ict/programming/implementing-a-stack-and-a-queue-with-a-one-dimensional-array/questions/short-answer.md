# Implementing a Stack and a Queue with a One-Dimensional Array — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What holds the pile?**
**Answer: A one-dimensional array of fixed size**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the top pointer remember?**
**Answer: The place of the last value pushed**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are push's steps?**
**Answer: Check not full, move top up one, store the value there**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are pop's steps?**
**Answer: Check not empty, take the value at top, move top down one**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the two edge faults.**
**Answer: Overflow (pushing onto a full stack) and underflow (popping an empty one)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the queue need two pointers?**
**Answer: Front where values leave, rear where values join — two moving ends**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens as the queue works?**
**Answer: The line walks along the array**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How are full and empty tested?**
**Answer: By the pointers' positions**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How does the unit teach the building?**
**Answer: Worked implementation examples, step by step, pointer by pointer**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 12 Pliroforiki supplementary material, Unit 1**
