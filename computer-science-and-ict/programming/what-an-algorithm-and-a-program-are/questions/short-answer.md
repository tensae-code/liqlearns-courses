# What an Algorithm and a Program Are — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an algorithm?**
**Answer: A plan of instructions in the right order for finishing a task**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the book's example?**
**Answer: Tying a shoe — cross, loop, pass around, pull tight**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens if the steps are done out of order?**
**Answer: The knot fails — the task is not finished**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What three marks must an algorithm have?**
**Answer: It must be complete, in order, and finished**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name an algorithm of daily life.**
**Answer: A recipe (also: a morning routine, the way home)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a program?**
**Answer: An algorithm written down for an executor to follow**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's program example?**
**Answer: The arrow plan that guides a character to the treasure**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Who writes a program, and who follows it?**
**Answer: The writer orders it; the executor follows it exactly**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the executor do with a wrong plan?**
**Answer: It still follows it — the fault is in the plan**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is the treasure plan written?**
**Answer: As arrows on the map — forward, forward, turn, forward**
