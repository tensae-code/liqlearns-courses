# Lesson plan — Flowgorithm practice — input, variables, decisions

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Programming · **Lesson:** Flowgorithm practice — input, variables, decisions
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Fundamentals of Programming (CURRICULUM.md) (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Flowgorithm practice builds: joined strings with &, outputs on separate lines, declared variables receiving keyboard input, the pseudocode viewer, areas with ^ — and a wage decision with tax.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course — the learner knows what a computer, a network and the Internet are. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Strings and output lines** — joining with & and a deliberate space; three Output symbols printing three countries on three lines.
2. **Input, variables and pseudocode** — declare variables before input, prompt first, watch Variables and Consoles while stepping; read pseudocode in the Source Code Viewer.
3. **Areas and the wage decision** — powers with ^ for circle and triangle areas; the wage decision — pay over 5,000 birr taxed at 5% on 50% of the total.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Join strings with & and control the space.
2. Declare, prompt and input correctly, and watch a stepped run.
3. Compute areas with ^ and build the wage decision.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Where the lesson is a doing-lesson, a school computer if one is available — paper works too.
