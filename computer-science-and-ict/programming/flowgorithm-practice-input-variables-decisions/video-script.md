This is **Flowgorithm practice — input, variables, decisions**.

**declaring variables** — naming where keyboard data will be placed — always before input; several variables can be declared in one symbol, separated by commas.

**the & operator** — the string connector joining text — no space appears until a space is put at the start of the second quoted text.

**powers in Flowgorithm** — written with ^ — 6^2 — so a circle's area for radius 6 is 3.142 * 6^2.

**the wage tax rule** — pay over 5,000 birr is taxed at 5% on 50% of the total pay; net pay = total − tax.
