# Working with Raster Images in PascalABC — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a raster image?**
**Answer: A picture made of a grid of coloured points — pixels**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does a raster image get into the program?**
**Answer: It is loaded from a picture file, whole, as one object**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does it differ from a drawn figure?**
**Answer: A figure is built from drawing commands; a raster image is loaded ready-made**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is animation, in this section?**
**Answer: Movement built from still steps, redrawn quickly at new coordinates**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are the steps of each animation move?**
**Answer: Draw the image, pause, clean its old place, draw it at the next coordinates**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What decides direction and speed?**
**Answer: The step pattern — how far coordinates change each step, and the pause length**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What causes a trail behind a moving image?**
**Answer: Not cleaning the image's old position**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What causes jerky motion?**
**Answer: Steps that jump too far at a time**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a loaded image's size on the window?**
**Answer: Its size in pixels**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where are motion faults fixed?**
**Answer: In the step pattern, not in the picture**
