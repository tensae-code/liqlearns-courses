# Working with Raster Images in PascalABC — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a raster image?**
a) A picture made of a grid of coloured points — pixels  b) Movement built from still steps, redrawn quickly at new coordinates  c) The step pattern — how far coordinates change each step, and the pause length  d) Steps that jump too far at a time
**Answer: a) A picture made of a grid of coloured points — pixels**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does a raster image get into the program?**
a) Draw the image, pause, clean its old place, draw it at the next coordinates  b) It is loaded from a picture file, whole, as one object  c) Not cleaning the image's old position  d) Its size in pixels
**Answer: b) It is loaded from a picture file, whole, as one object**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does it differ from a drawn figure?**
a) The step pattern — how far coordinates change each step, and the pause length  b) Steps that jump too far at a time  c) A figure is built from drawing commands; a raster image is loaded ready-made  d) In the step pattern, not in the picture
**Answer: c) A figure is built from drawing commands; a raster image is loaded ready-made**
