# Working with Raster Images in PascalABC — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a raster image?" is: A picture made of a grid of coloured points — pixels.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How does a raster image get into the program?" is: The step pattern — how far coordinates change each step, and the pause length.**
**Answer: False** — It is loaded from a picture file, whole, as one object.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How does it differ from a drawn figure?" is: A figure is built from drawing commands; a raster image is loaded ready-made.**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is animation, in this section?" is: Steps that jump too far at a time.**
**Answer: False** — Movement built from still steps, redrawn quickly at new coordinates.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are the steps of each animation move?" is: Draw the image, pause, clean its old place, draw it at the next coordinates.**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What decides direction and speed?" is: In the step pattern, not in the picture.**
**Answer: False** — The step pattern — how far coordinates change each step, and the pause length.

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What causes a trail behind a moving image?" is: Not cleaning the image's old position.**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What causes jerky motion?" is: It is loaded from a picture file, whole, as one object.**
**Answer: False** — Steps that jump too far at a time.

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is a loaded image's size on the window?" is: Its size in pixels.**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where are motion faults fixed?" is: Movement built from still steps, redrawn quickly at new coordinates.**
**Answer: False** — In the step pattern, not in the picture.
