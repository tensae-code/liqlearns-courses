# Hands-on — Working with Raster Images in PascalABC

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Walk a picture
Write a program that loads a raster image and moves it across the graphics window in steps, cleaning its old place each time. Run it slowly first, then with smaller pauses.

## Fix the trail
Deliberately remove the cleaning step and watch the trail the image leaves. Restore it, then make the steps smaller and say what changed in the motion's smoothness and speed.
