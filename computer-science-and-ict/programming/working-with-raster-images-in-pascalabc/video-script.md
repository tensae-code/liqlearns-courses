# Video script — Working with Raster Images in PascalABC

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **raster image** is a picture made of a grid of coloured points (**pixels**) — the kind of picture a photograph or a saved drawing is.
Unlike a figure the program draws from commands, a raster image is **loaded from a picture file** and placed on the graphics window at chosen coordinates, whole, as one object.
**Animation** here is movement built from still steps: draw the image, wait a blink, clear or cover its old place, and draw it at the next coordinates — repeated quickly, the eye joins the steps into **motion**.
Direction and speed are just the step pattern: how far the coordinates change each step, and how long the pause is.
