# Strings — Working with Text Values — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a string to a program?**
a) A row of characters  b) Cuts out a part of the string by its start and length  c) Everything from a position to the row's end  d) Glues (joins) them into one longer row
**Answer: a) A row of characters**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does GetSubText do?**
a) Everything from a position to the row's end  b) Cuts out a part of the string by its start and length  c) Glues (joins) them into one longer row  d) Cutting (GetSubText) and joining (+)
**Answer: b) Cuts out a part of the string by its start and length**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does GetSubTextToEnd take?**
a) Glues (joins) them into one longer row  b) Cutting (GetSubText) and joining (+)  c) Everything from a position to the row's end  d) Fold the whole row to upper or lower case
**Answer: c) Everything from a position to the row's end**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does + do to strings?**
a) Cutting (GetSubText) and joining (+)  b) Fold the whole row to upper or lower case  c) It makes differently-cased texts comparable  d) Glues (joins) them into one longer row
**Answer: d) Glues (joins) them into one longer row**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are the string's scissors and glue?**
a) Cutting (GetSubText) and joining (+)  b) Fold the whole row to upper or lower case  c) It makes differently-cased texts comparable  d) The row's length in characters
**Answer: a) Cutting (GetSubText) and joining (+)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do the Convert operations do?**
a) It makes differently-cased texts comparable  b) Fold the whole row to upper or lower case  c) The row's length in characters  d) GetIndexOf says where a part stands; IsSubText asks only whether it stands there at all
**Answer: b) Fold the whole row to upper or lower case**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why does case folding matter?**
a) The row's length in characters  b) GetIndexOf says where a part stands; IsSubText asks only whether it stands there at all  c) It makes differently-cased texts comparable  d) The row's two doors — its beginning and its end
**Answer: c) It makes differently-cased texts comparable**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does GetLength return?**
a) GetIndexOf says where a part stands; IsSubText asks only whether it stands there at all  b) The row's two doors — its beginning and its end  c) A row of characters  d) The row's length in characters
**Answer: d) The row's length in characters**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Distinguish GetIndexOf from IsSubText.**
a) GetIndexOf says where a part stands; IsSubText asks only whether it stands there at all  b) The row's two doors — its beginning and its end  c) A row of characters  d) Cuts out a part of the string by its start and length
**Answer: a) GetIndexOf says where a part stands; IsSubText asks only whether it stands there at all**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do StartsWith and EndsWith test?**
