# Strings — Working with Text Values — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a string to a program?" is: A row of characters**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does GetSubText do?" is: Everything from a position to the row's end**
**Answer: False** — Cuts out a part of the string by its start and length

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does GetSubTextToEnd take?" is: Everything from a position to the row's end**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does + do to strings?" is: Cutting (GetSubText) and joining (+)**
**Answer: False** — Glues (joins) them into one longer row

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are the string's scissors and glue?" is: Cutting (GetSubText) and joining (+)**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do the Convert operations do?" is: It makes differently-cased texts comparable**
**Answer: False** — Fold the whole row to upper or lower case

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why does case folding matter?" is: It makes differently-cased texts comparable**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does GetLength return?" is: GetIndexOf says where a part stands; IsSubText asks only whether it stands there at all**
**Answer: False** — The row's length in characters

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Distinguish GetIndexOf from IsSubText." is: GetIndexOf says where a part stands; IsSubText asks only whether it stands there at all**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do StartsWith and EndsWith test?" is: A row of characters**
**Answer: False** — The row's two doors — its beginning and its end
