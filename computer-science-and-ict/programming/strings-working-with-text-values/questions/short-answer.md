# Strings — Working with Text Values — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a string to a program?**
**Answer: A row of characters**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does GetSubText do?**
**Answer: Cuts out a part of the string by its start and length**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does GetSubTextToEnd take?**
**Answer: Everything from a position to the row's end**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does + do to strings?**
**Answer: Glues (joins) them into one longer row**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are the string's scissors and glue?**
**Answer: Cutting (GetSubText) and joining (+)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do the Convert operations do?**
**Answer: Fold the whole row to upper or lower case**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why does case folding matter?**
**Answer: It makes differently-cased texts comparable**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does GetLength return?**
**Answer: The row's length in characters**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Distinguish GetIndexOf from IsSubText.**
**Answer: GetIndexOf says where a part stands; IsSubText asks only whether it stands there at all**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do StartsWith and EndsWith test?**
**Answer: The row's two doors — its beginning and its end**
