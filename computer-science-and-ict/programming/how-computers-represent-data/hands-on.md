# Hands-on — How Computers Represent Data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One byte, three meanings
Take the byte 01000001. Find what it means as a binary number and as a character code. Write both answers and the scheme that produced each.

## Count the bytes
Estimate the bytes in: your name in a text file, a one-page document, a phone photo. Check real file sizes on a computer and compare with your estimates.
