# Hands-on - High-level languages - compilers and interpreters

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Book or live voice?

One pupil reads a three-sentence story in a language the class does not know. First round - the compiler way: a translator writes out the whole translation, then it is read. Second round - the interpreter way: sentence, translation, sentence, translation. Which felt faster to START hearing? Which finished the whole story as one piece?

## Language sort

Cards carry the seven language names. Sort them into two piles - compiler languages and interpreter languages - then turn the cards over: the pile names are written on the backs as the check.
