# Hands-on — Data Compression — Making Files Smaller

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Compress a sentence by hand
Write a sentence with a repeated phrase (for example a playground chant) in full. Now rewrite it using a pattern-and-count code of your own invention. Swap with a classmate: can they restore your exact original sentence? If yes, your code was lossless.

## Spot the repetition
Take any photo on a phone and zoom into the sky or a wall. Write down why that region compresses so well, and predict: will a photo of a crowded market compress as well? Say why.
