# Video script — Data Compression — Making Files Smaller

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Files are full of repetition. The same blue, again and again.
Compression writes the pattern once — and counts the repeats.
Lossless loses nothing. The original comes back, bit for bit.
Lossy throws away what eyes and ears barely miss.
Photos and music shrink a lot. But what is lost is lost forever.
Choose by one question: may anything be lost?
