# Data Compression — Making Files Smaller — short answer

Answer each question in a few sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A school must email two things: the examination questions document, and photos of the sports day. Say which compression each should get, and why.**
**Answer:** The examination document must be lossless (for example ZIP): every character matters and the original must come back exactly. The sports-day photos can be lossy (JPEG): small colour losses pass unnoticed, and the files become small enough to email easily.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Explain why 'lossy is smaller, so lossy is always better' is wrong.**
**Answer:** Because the smallness is bought with permanent loss. For eyes-and-ears files the loss may be invisible — but for text, programs and data, the discarded detail can be the part that matters, and once thrown away it can never be recovered.
