# Hands-on — Collecting Data — Sensors, Crawlers and Surveys

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Route chooser
For each question, choose the route and defend it in one line: the river's level every hour for a year; the price of one phone in fifty online shops; why parents chose this school; last term's marks for every class.

## Crawler's eye
Open (or imagine) a shop web page listing products. Write down: what repeats on the page, what exactly the crawler should extract from each repeat, and what the resulting table's columns would be. That written plan is what BeautifulSoup would be told to find.
