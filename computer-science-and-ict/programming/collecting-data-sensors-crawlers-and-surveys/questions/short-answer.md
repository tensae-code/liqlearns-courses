# Collecting Data — Sensors, Crawlers and Surveys — short answer

Answer each question in a few sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A town wants to know whether its market is getting more expensive. Plan the collection: which routes, collecting what?**
**Answer:** Prices live on shop and market listings, so: a crawler (or record collection) gathering the prices of a fixed basket of goods from the same sellers each week, plus existing records for past prices. A survey adds what prices cannot say — how households are coping — so both routes together answer the question.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the collection plan have to be made from the question, not from the tool you happen to like?**
**Answer:** Because each route gathers a different kind of data from a different home: sensors from the physical world, crawlers from pages, surveys from people. Starting from the tool collects whatever that tool can reach — which may entirely miss the data the question actually needs.
