# Collecting Data — Sensors, Crawlers and Surveys — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A sensor collects data by —**
a) asking people questions  b) measuring a physical quantity and recording it automatically  c) crawling web pages  d) copying registers
**Answer: b) measuring a physical quantity and recording it automatically** — Temperature, light, motion, water level — measured and stored without a person present.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Before collecting, the plan must fix —**
a) the chart colours  b) what to collect, how often, and in what units  c) the report title  d) the file names
**Answer: b) what to collect, how often, and in what units** — Data gathered without a plan misses the column analysis later needs.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A web crawler is —**
a) a slow browser  b) a program that requests pages, extracts what matches, stores it, and moves on  c) a kind of sensor  d) a survey form
**Answer: b) a program that requests pages, extracts what matches, stores it, and moves on** — Page by page, faster than any clipboard.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) BeautifulSoup's job in the crawler is to —**
a) send emails  b) parse a page's HTML into parts the program can find and extract  c) draw charts  d) clean dirty data
**Answer: b) parse a page's HTML into parts the program can find and extract** — It turns the page into a structure the crawler can search.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) A good survey question —**
a) is long and impressive  b) can be answered in one tap and means the same thing to everyone  c) has no fixed answers at all  d) asks three things at once
**Answer: b) can be answered in one tap and means the same thing to everyone** — Ambiguity at collection becomes noise no cleaning can remove.
