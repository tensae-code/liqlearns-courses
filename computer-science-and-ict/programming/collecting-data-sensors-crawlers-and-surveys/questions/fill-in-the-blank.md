# Collecting Data — Sensors, Crawlers and Surveys — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Devices that measure the physical world and record it automatically are ____.**
**Answer: sensors** — Weather stations, step counters, river gauges.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A program that gathers data from web pages is a web ____.**
**Answer: crawler** — It requests, extracts, stores, moves on.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Data that lives in people's heads is collected with ____ and surveys.**
**Answer: questionnaires** — Carefully worded, one meaning, quick to answer.
