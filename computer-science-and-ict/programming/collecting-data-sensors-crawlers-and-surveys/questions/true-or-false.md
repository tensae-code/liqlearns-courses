# Collecting Data — Sensors, Crawlers and Surveys — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A river gauge is a sensor-based collection.**
**Answer: True** — It measures water level and records it automatically.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A crawler may take anything from any site, however it likes.**
**Answer: False** — Collection must respect the site's rules and the data's owners.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A badly worded survey question can be fully repaired at the cleaning stage.**
**Answer: False** — Noise collected stays noise — cleaning cannot recover what answers meant.
