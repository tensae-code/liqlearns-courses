# Traversing a Binary Search Tree — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a traversal?**
**Answer: Visiting every node of a tree in a fixed, recursive order**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the inorder order?**
**Answer: Left subtree, root, right subtree**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the preorder order?**
**Answer: Root first, then left subtree, then right subtree**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the postorder order?**
**Answer: Left subtree, right subtree, root last**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What single thing differs between the three walks?**
**Answer: Where the visit sits — before, between, or after the subtree walks**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is special about inorder on a BST?**
**Answer: It visits the values in sorted order**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why does inorder sort?**
**Answer: Left subtrees hold smaller values, right subtrees larger, and inorder reads left–root–right**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is preorder good for?**
**Answer: Copying the tree — the root arrives before its subtrees**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is postorder good for?**
**Answer: Evaluating expressions, and deleting safely — children before their parent**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two jobs does the book say the BST joins?**
**Answer: Sorting and searching**
