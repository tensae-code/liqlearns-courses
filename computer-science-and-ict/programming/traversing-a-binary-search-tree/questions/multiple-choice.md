# Traversing a Binary Search Tree — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a traversal?**
a) Visiting every node of a tree in a fixed, recursive order  b) Left subtree, root, right subtree  c) Root first, then left subtree, then right subtree  d) Left subtree, right subtree, root last
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the inorder order?**
a) Left subtree, root, right subtree  b) Root first, then left subtree, then right subtree  c) Left subtree, right subtree, root last  d) Where the visit sits — before, between, or after the subtree walks
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the preorder order?**
a) Root first, then left subtree, then right subtree  b) Left subtree, right subtree, root last  c) Where the visit sits — before, between, or after the subtree walks  d) It visits the values in sorted order
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the postorder order?**
a) Left subtree, right subtree, root last  b) Where the visit sits — before, between, or after the subtree walks  c) It visits the values in sorted order  d) Left subtrees hold smaller values, right subtrees larger, and inorder reads left–root–right
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What single thing differs between the three walks?**
a) Where the visit sits — before, between, or after the subtree walks  b) It visits the values in sorted order  c) Left subtrees hold smaller values, right subtrees larger, and inorder reads left–root–right  d) Copying the tree — the root arrives before its subtrees
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is special about inorder on a BST?**
a) It visits the values in sorted order  b) Left subtrees hold smaller values, right subtrees larger, and inorder reads left–root–right  c) Copying the tree — the root arrives before its subtrees  d) Evaluating expressions, and deleting safely — children before their parent
**Answer: a**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why does inorder sort?**
a) Left subtrees hold smaller values, right subtrees larger, and inorder reads left–root–right  b) Copying the tree — the root arrives before its subtrees  c) Evaluating expressions, and deleting safely — children before their parent  d) Sorting and searching
**Answer: a**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is preorder good for?**
a) Copying the tree — the root arrives before its subtrees  b) Evaluating expressions, and deleting safely — children before their parent  c) Sorting and searching  d) Visiting every node of a tree in a fixed, recursive order
**Answer: a**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is postorder good for?**
a) Evaluating expressions, and deleting safely — children before their parent  b) Sorting and searching  c) Visiting every node of a tree in a fixed, recursive order  d) Left subtree, root, right subtree
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two jobs does the book say the BST joins?**
a) Sorting and searching  b) Visiting every node of a tree in a fixed, recursive order  c) Left subtree, root, right subtree  d) Root first, then left subtree, then right subtree
**Answer: a**
