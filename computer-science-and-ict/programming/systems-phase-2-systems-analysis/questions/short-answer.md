# Systems Phase 2 — Systems Analysis — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does systems analysis study?**
**Answer: The current system in detail — how work actually flows, what data is captured, where it hurts**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the fact-finding tools.**
**Answer: Interviews, written questionnaires, observation of the work, and studying documents and records**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Fiji's analyst sit with the stock clerks?**
**Answer: Because the system on paper and the system at 4pm on a Friday are often two different systems**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the analyst follow at Viti?**
**Answer: A stock card through its whole life**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What question does analysis answer?**
**Answer: What — exactly what the new system must do**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are requirements?**
**Answer: The exact statement of what the new system must do — data kept, processes carried, outputs produced, limits respected**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give Fiji's example requirement.**
**Answer: Any clerk must find a stock figure in under a minute**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What belongs to design, not analysis?**
**Answer: How the requirement is achieved — which program or screen does it**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why fix requirements in this phase?**
**Answer: A wrong requirement costs a sentence here; in development it costs the build**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does analysis hand to design?**
**Answer: The requirements — the complete 'what'**
