# Video script — Systems Phase 2 — Systems Analysis

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Systems analysis** studies the **current system** in detail: how work actually flows, what data is captured, where it hurts. Its tools are **fact-finding** — interviewing the people who do the work, questioning them in writing, watching the work happen, and reading the present system's documents and records. Fiji's analyst does not guess from an office: at Viti the analysis sits with the stock clerks and follows a stock card through its whole life, because the system that exists on paper and the system that exists at 4pm on a Friday are often two different systems.
Analysis ends by writing the **requirements**: the exact statement of what the new system **must do** — the data it must keep, the processes it must carry, the outputs it must produce, and the limits it must respect. Requirements answer *what*, never *how*: 'any clerk must find a stock figure in under a minute' is a requirement; which program or screen achieves it belongs to design. Fiji's discipline is the phase's gift to every later phase — a wrong requirement fixed here costs a sentence; fixed in development, it costs the build.
