# Hands-on — Programming the Thymio Robot With VPL

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**First pairs** — Program Thymio (or its simulator/cards) with two event–action pairs: approach-avoid and button-celebrate. Test on the floor; repair one pair that misfires and name the event that fooled it.

## Task two
**Mission: Morse messenger** — Program Thymio to signal a simplified Morse letter by light when its button is pressed. A partner robot (or pupil) reads the flashes and names the letter.
