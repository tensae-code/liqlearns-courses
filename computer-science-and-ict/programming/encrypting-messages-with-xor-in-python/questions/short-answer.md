# Encrypting Messages with XOR in Python — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does XOR answer?**
**Answer: 1 when the two bits differ, 0 when they are the same**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is 1 XOR 1?**
**Answer: 0 — the bits are the same**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when you XOR with the same key twice?**
**Answer: The original returns — each bit is flipped, then flipped back**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are the encryption and decryption formulas?**
**Answer: Ciphertext = plaintext XOR key; plaintext = ciphertext XOR key**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why is there no separate decryption method to invent?**
**Answer: The same operation undoes itself — the same line of code decrypts**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the program do to each character?**
**Answer: Takes it in a loop, turns it into its code number, XORs it with the key**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is gathered into the ciphertext?**
**Answer: The XORed results — as numbers, or turned back into characters**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the XOR cipher weak for real secrets?**
**Answer: A single short key repeats its pattern across a long message, and patterns are what codebreakers eat**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two things must the pupil be able to say?**
**Answer: How it works, and why the real world needs more**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does this lesson sit in Egypt's book?**
**Answer: The final lesson of Grade 8 Term 2**
