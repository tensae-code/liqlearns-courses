# Hands-on — Lists Stacks and Queues LIFO and FIFO

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 4 §4.1 Primitive Computational Structures: §4.1.1 Lists in Python (creation, properties, insert/delete/search operations, applications — party-planning lists), §4.1.2 Stacks (LIFO; push and pop; a stack of books in code) and §4.1.3 Queues (FIFO; enqueue and dequeue; the bank queue; Python's queue module) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.
