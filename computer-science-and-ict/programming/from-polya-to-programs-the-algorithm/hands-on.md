# Hands-on — From Polya to programs — the algorithm

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Human robot
One pupil is the 'computer': they follow instructions literally and only literally. The class directs them across the room. Every misunderstanding is an ambiguity to fix.

## Algorithm or not?
Sort instruction sets into algorithm / not-an-algorithm, naming the failed mark for each rejection.


## Recipe test
Write your family's doro wot (or any dish) as numbered steps. Give it to a partner who marks any step that could be read two ways — each mark is an ambiguity to repair until the recipe is an algorithm.
