# Hands-on — Python Arithmetic Operations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Predict, run, compare** — Write ten expressions mixing the four operations and variables. Predict each result and its type before running; score your predictions.

## Task two
**Hire the mathematics** — Write a small program that takes a bill's items and prints the total, the average item, and the change from 1000 taka — arithmetic doing real work.
