Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the limit live?**
**Answer: In the tool, not in the language**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What kind of Arabic content needs the workaround?**
**Answer: Headlines, names and titles**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the Arabic text written instead?**
**Answer: In MS Paint**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the written text saved as?**
**Answer: A picture file**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which picture formats does the book name?**
**Answer: JPEG, PNG and GIF**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does the picture enter Scratch?**
**Answer: It is uploaded**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can the uploaded picture work as?**
**Answer: A sprite or a costume**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the lesson’s project?**
**Answer: Your full name, each word a different colour**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How many steps does the workaround take?**
