# Hands-on — GW BASIC Sample Programs Seven Worked Programs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 5 §5.2.3 Sample Programs (sum and average of five numbers; product of five numbers; larger of two numbers; area of a rectangle; area of a circle; Celsius to Fahrenheit; Fahrenheit to Celsius) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach gw basic sample programs seven worked programs to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
