# Positioning and Moving on the Stage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Explain, in your own words: put a sprite at an exact chosen place on the stage.**
**Answer:** The learner's own explanation, true to the lesson: Every place on the Scratch stage has a position: a sprite can be put at an exact place, and moved from place to place on purpose — not just wandered forward.

**2. A classmate gets this lesson's work wrong. What is the most likely fault, and how is it fixed?**
**Answer:** Sprite starts in the wrong place → the scene forgot to place it: add the placing step at the very start of the script, before any moving.
