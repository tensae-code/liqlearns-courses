# Positioning and Moving on the Stage — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. Put a sprite at an exact chosen place on the stage.**
**Answer: True** — This is one of the lesson's own outcomes.

**2. It does not matter where a sprite starts, because a scene always sorts itself out.**
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite of this.

**3. Move a sprite between two named places in a planned way.**
**Answer: True** — This is one of the lesson's own outcomes.
