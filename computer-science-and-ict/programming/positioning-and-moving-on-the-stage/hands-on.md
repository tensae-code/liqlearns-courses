# Hands-on — Positioning and Moving on the Stage

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Place, then move
Place a sprite at a spot you choose, then move it to a second named spot. Run the script twice: the sprite must start at the first spot both times.

## Name the place
A partner names a place on the stage — top left, centre, bottom right. Place the sprite exactly there and say how you knew it was right.
