# Lesson plan — Personalising a Sprite — Size, Position and Colour

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Programming · **Lesson:** Personalising a Sprite — Size, Position and Colour
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Morocco (2026-11-14) — in the title Programming on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Morocco's Grade 3 “J'excelle en informatique” teacher guide, because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson. This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only (Morocco), named in lesson/sources.md, counted nowhere.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Every sprite carries settings its maker can change. A sprite's colours are changed where its look is made: in the paint editor, the learner recolours parts of the costume — choosing a new colour and applying it to a part — or adjusts the drawing itself, so the character wears the colours its maker chose, not only the library's.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

1. **Do it first** — Two learners add the same library sprite, and their projects already differ — because each sprite can be made your own. Grade 3's personalising séance changes the sprite itself: how big it is, where it stands, and the colours it wears. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
