# Personalising a Sprite — Size, Position and Colour — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What can be changed about a sprite itself?**
**Answer: Its size, its position, and its colours**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the size setting do?**
**Answer: It makes the sprite bigger or smaller**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why make one sprite big and another small?**
**Answer: A near character large, a far one small**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a sprite's position changed?**
**Answer: By dragging it to its place, or by its position settings**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do size and position changes not change?**
**Answer: What the sprite is — only its fit to the scene**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where are a sprite's colours changed?**
**Answer: In the paint editor**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is a part recoloured?**
**Answer: Choose a new colour and apply it to the part**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Whose colours does a personalised sprite wear?**
**Answer: The colours its maker chose, not only the library's**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is personalising finished?**
**Answer: When size, place and colours match the story's character**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must the learner be able to say at the end?**
**Answer: Which setting made each difference**
