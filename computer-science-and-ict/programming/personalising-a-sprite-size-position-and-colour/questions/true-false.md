# Personalising a Sprite — Size, Position and Colour — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What can be changed about a sprite itself?" is: Its size, its position, and its colours**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the size setting do?" is: In the paint editor**
**Answer: False** — It makes the sprite bigger or smaller

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why make one sprite big and another small?" is: A near character large, a far one small**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is a sprite's position changed?" is: The colours its maker chose, not only the library's**
**Answer: False** — By dragging it to its place, or by its position settings

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do size and position changes not change?" is: What the sprite is — only its fit to the scene**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where are a sprite's colours changed?" is: Which setting made each difference**
**Answer: False** — In the paint editor

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is a part recoloured?" is: Choose a new colour and apply it to the part**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Whose colours does a personalised sprite wear?" is: It makes the sprite bigger or smaller**
**Answer: False** — The colours its maker chose, not only the library's

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is personalising finished?" is: When size, place and colours match the story's character**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What must the learner be able to say at the end?" is: By dragging it to its place, or by its position settings**
**Answer: False** — Which setting made each difference
