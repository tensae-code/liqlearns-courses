# Personalising a Sprite — Size, Position and Colour — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What can be changed about a sprite itself?**
a) Its size, its position, and its colours  b) It makes the sprite bigger or smaller  c) A near character large, a far one small  d) By dragging it to its place, or by its position settings
**Answer: a) Its size, its position, and its colours**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the size setting do?**
a) A near character large, a far one small  b) It makes the sprite bigger or smaller  c) By dragging it to its place, or by its position settings  d) What the sprite is — only its fit to the scene
**Answer: b) It makes the sprite bigger or smaller**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why make one sprite big and another small?**
a) By dragging it to its place, or by its position settings  b) What the sprite is — only its fit to the scene  c) A near character large, a far one small  d) In the paint editor
**Answer: c) A near character large, a far one small**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a sprite's position changed?**
a) What the sprite is — only its fit to the scene  b) In the paint editor  c) Choose a new colour and apply it to the part  d) By dragging it to its place, or by its position settings
**Answer: d) By dragging it to its place, or by its position settings**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do size and position changes not change?**
a) What the sprite is — only its fit to the scene  b) In the paint editor  c) Choose a new colour and apply it to the part  d) The colours its maker chose, not only the library's
**Answer: a) What the sprite is — only its fit to the scene**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where are a sprite's colours changed?**
a) Choose a new colour and apply it to the part  b) In the paint editor  c) The colours its maker chose, not only the library's  d) When size, place and colours match the story's character
**Answer: b) In the paint editor**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is a part recoloured?**
a) The colours its maker chose, not only the library's  b) When size, place and colours match the story's character  c) Choose a new colour and apply it to the part  d) Which setting made each difference
**Answer: c) Choose a new colour and apply it to the part**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Whose colours does a personalised sprite wear?**
a) When size, place and colours match the story's character  b) Which setting made each difference  c) Its size, its position, and its colours  d) The colours its maker chose, not only the library's
**Answer: d) The colours its maker chose, not only the library's**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is personalising finished?**
a) When size, place and colours match the story's character  b) Which setting made each difference  c) Its size, its position, and its colours  d) It makes the sprite bigger or smaller
**Answer: a) When size, place and colours match the story's character**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must the learner be able to say at the end?**
a) Its size, its position, and its colours  b) Which setting made each difference  c) It makes the sprite bigger or smaller  d) A near character large, a far one small
**Answer: b) Which setting made each difference**
