# Hands-on — Personalising a Sprite — Size, Position and Colour

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make it yours
Add a library sprite. Change its size so it fits a backdrop you choose, drag it to its starting place, and recolour one part of its costume in the paint editor. List the three changes you made.

## Near and far
Add the same sprite twice. Make one big and low on the stage, the other small and high, so they read as near and far characters of one scene. Say which setting did the work.
