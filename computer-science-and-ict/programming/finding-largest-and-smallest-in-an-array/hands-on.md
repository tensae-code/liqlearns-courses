# Hands-on — Finding the Largest and Smallest in an Array

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Champion trace
Hand-trace max and imax over {3, 7, 2, 7, 5}: write both after every element, and give the final report exactly as the lesson phrases it.

## Both crowns
Write the paired walk that finds max and min in one pass. State both starting values and both comparisons, then test mentally on {−3, −8, −1}.
