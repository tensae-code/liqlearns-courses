# Finding the Largest and Smallest in an Array — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the walk keep?**
**Answer: The best-so-far — the largest seen yet**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does max start from?**
**Answer: The first element, a[0]**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When is max replaced?**
**Answer: When an element beats it — a[i] > max**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why not start max at 0?**
**Answer: All-negative arrays would break it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) After the walk, what does max hold?**
**Answer: The largest value in the array**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What changes for the smallest?**
**Answer: The comparison flips to <**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What extra variable reports the position?**
**Answer: imax, updated together with max**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a position report look like?**
**Answer: Largest is 9, at index 4**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which position wins a tie?**
**Answer: The first — strict comparison replaces only on a beating**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Does the walk change the array?**
**Answer: No — it only reads it**
