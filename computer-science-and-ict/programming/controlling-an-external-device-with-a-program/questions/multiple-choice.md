# Controlling an External Device with a Program — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an external device?**
a) A device outside the computer box, connected to it  b) Through the connection — its port or interface  c) The connection  d) The program's instructions
**Answer: a) A device outside the computer box, connected to it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does a program reach an external device?**
a) The connection  b) Through the connection — its port or interface  c) The program's instructions  d) One real connection from set-up to working
**Answer: b) Through the connection — its port or interface**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first check when a device does not respond?**
a) The program's instructions  b) One real connection from set-up to working  c) The connection  d) The device's actions — on/off, or movement
**Answer: c) The connection**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What tells an external device what to do?**
a) One real connection from set-up to working  b) The device's actions — on/off, or movement  c) To find faults where they happen  d) The program's instructions
**Answer: d) The program's instructions**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the case study follow?**
a) One real connection from set-up to working  b) The device's actions — on/off, or movement  c) To find faults where they happen  d) The program, or the connection
**Answer: a) One real connection from set-up to working**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What can a control program switch or move?**
a) To find faults where they happen  b) The device's actions — on/off, or movement  c) The program, or the connection  d) A motor-driven device
**Answer: b) The device's actions — on/off, or movement**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why test a control program in small steps?**
a) The program, or the connection  b) A motor-driven device  c) To find faults where they happen  d) The person in charge of the case study
**Answer: c) To find faults where they happen**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is adjusted after testing?**
a) A motor-driven device  b) The person in charge of the case study  c) A device outside the computer box, connected to it  d) The program, or the connection
**Answer: d) The program, or the connection**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name a device that can be controlled this way.**
a) A motor-driven device  b) The person in charge of the case study  c) A device outside the computer box, connected to it  d) Through the connection — its port or interface
**Answer: a) A motor-driven device**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who checks safety before the case study runs?**
a) A device outside the computer box, connected to it  b) The person in charge of the case study  c) Through the connection — its port or interface  d) The connection
**Answer: b) The person in charge of the case study**
