# Video script — Controlling an External Device with a Program

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An **external device** is a device **outside the computer box, connected to it**. The program reaches it **through the connection — its port or interface**. The case study follows **one real connection from set-up to working**, and its first check, when the device does not respond, is always the connection.
The device does what **the program's instructions** tell it — switching a device such as a **motor-driven device** on and off, or moving it. The control is **tested in small steps** so faults are found where they happen, and after testing, the program — or the connection — is adjusted. Before running the case, the person in charge checks safety.
