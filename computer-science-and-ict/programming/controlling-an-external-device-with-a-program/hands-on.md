# Hands-on — Controlling an External Device with a Program

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace the path
Draw the path: program, connection, device. Under each, write the one fault that could stop control there.

## Small-step plan
Write a control plan for switching a light on and off: step 1 connects, step 2 sends one instruction, step 3 tests. Say what you check at each step.
