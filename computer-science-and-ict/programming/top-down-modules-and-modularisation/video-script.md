The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **Top-down — modules and modularisation**.

**top-down solving** — starting with the most general modules and working toward the specific — decomposing a big problem into smaller sub-problems.

**modularisation** — dividing a problem into modules, each solved individually, then integrated into the complete solution.

**a module** — a sub-problem solved individually as one part of a larger solution.

**CalculateWage** — the subroutine module computing one net pay, called ten times by the main module in the wage example.
