# Top-down — modules and modularisation — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is dividing a problem into modules, each solved individually, then integrated into the complete solution. (Name the term.)
**Answer: modularisation**

**2.** _____ is a sub-problem solved individually as one part of a larger solution. (Name the term.)
**Answer: a module**

**3.** _____ is the subroutine module computing one net pay, called ten times by the main module in the wage example. (Name the term.)
**Answer: CalculateWage**
