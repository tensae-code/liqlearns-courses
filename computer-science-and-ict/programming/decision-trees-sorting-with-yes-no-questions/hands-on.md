# Hands-on — Decision Trees — Sorting With Yes–No Questions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Grow the house tree** — With twelve picture-house cards, build a decision tree on the board: choose each question by its split, draw branches, test by identifying three houses from the tree alone.

## Task two
**Twenty questions, engineered** — Play identification with a secret object, but the class must first agree the question order by splitting power. Count questions used against a free-play round.
