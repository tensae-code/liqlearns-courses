# Decision Trees — Sorting With Yes–No Questions — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a decision tree?**
**Answer: Sorting/identifying by a chain of yes–no questions, each answer choosing the next branch**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does each question do to the candidates?**
**Answer: Splits the remaining pile in two**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What sits at a leaf?**
**Answer: One name — one identified thing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a question powerful?**
**Answer: It splits the pile usefully — near half and half**
