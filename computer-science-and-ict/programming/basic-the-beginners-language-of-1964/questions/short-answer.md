# BASIC — the Beginners' Language of 1964 — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does BASIC stand for?**
**Answer: Beginner's All-purpose Symbolic Instruction Code**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When was BASIC designed?**
**Answer: 1964**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who was BASIC designed for?**
**Answer: Beginners — students who were not specialists**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are BASIC's commands like?**
**Answer: Short, English-like words**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How does BASIC run?**
**Answer: Through an interpreter**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the interpreter suit beginners?**
**Answer: The learner runs the program at once and sees results and errors immediately**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name four things this book builds inside BASIC.**
**Answer: Numbered lines, variables, input and output, and branching (also loops, subroutines and functions)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which language carries Sudan's Grade 11 programming course?**
**Answer: BASIC**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What comes first in the book's order?**
**Answer: BASIC met as history and as tool**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Can a learner run a first program in a first sitting?**
**Answer: Yes — that was the design's purpose**
