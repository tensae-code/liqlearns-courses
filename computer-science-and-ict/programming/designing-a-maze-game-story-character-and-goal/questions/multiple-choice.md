# Designing a Maze Game — Story, Character and Goal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The maze project begins with —**
a) Random walls  b) Planning the story  c) The final exam
**Answer: b) Planning the story** — Story first; it decides the rest.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A maze character should be drawn —**
a) As large as the stage  b) Small, so it fits the passages  c) In the wall colour
**Answer: b) Small, so it fits the passages** — Small — passages are narrow.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Zooming in while drawing helps by —**
a) Making the sprite bigger in the game  b) Keeping small work neat and edges clean  c) Adding sound
**Answer: b) Keeping small work neat and edges clean** — Zoom is a drawing view, for neat small work.

**4. (Sub lesson 3) The goal must be —**
a) Hidden  b) Clear at a glance to a new player  c) The same colour as the walls
**Answer: b) Clear at a glance to a new player** — A new player must see where to get to.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) The story answers —**
a) Who travels, why, and what they must reach  b) Nothing  c) Only the game's name
**Answer: a) Who travels, why, and what they must reach** — Those three answers drive the design.
