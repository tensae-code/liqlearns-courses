# Designing a Maze Game — Story, Character and Goal — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Write the three story questions for a maze game and answer them for a game of your own.**
**Answer:** A sound answer gives who travels, why/for what reason, and what they must reach, with answers that fit each other and could really be drawn and built.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the character drawn small and zoomed-in, in that order of ideas?**
**Answer:** A sound answer: small because maze passages are narrow; drawn zoomed-in because zooming is only the working view that keeps the small drawing neat — the character stays small on the stage.
