# Polya's four steps — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** _____ is finding what is asked, what is known and not known, and what type of answer is required. (Name the term.)
**Answer: understanding the problem**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ is choosing a strategy before calculating. (Name the term.)
**Answer: making a plan**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** _____ is using the chosen strategy to work the problem. (Name the term.)
**Answer: executing the plan**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** _____ is checking the answer, preferably by a different method. (Name the term.)
**Answer: reviewing**
