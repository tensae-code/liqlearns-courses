# Polya's four steps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is understanding the problem?
a) choosing a strategy — a list, a picture, elimination, a formula, guess and check, or a simpler related problem  b) using the chosen strategy — and returning to step 2 for another if it fails  c) checking the answer by a different method and evaluating where else the method could be used  d) finding what is asked, what is known and not known, and what type of answer is required
**Answer: d) finding what is asked, what is known and not known, and what type of answer is required**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a strategy named for the plan step?
a) checking the answer by a different method  b) guessing and checking  c) looking up what type of answer is required  d) asking whether the problem was seen before
**Answer: b) guessing and checking** — it is one of the six plan strategies: list, picture, eliminate, formula, guess and check, simpler related problem.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the execute step, if the chosen strategy itself fails, you —
a) stop and leave the problem  b) return to step 2 and choose another strategy  c) check the answer by a different method  d) start a new problem from step 1 of a different method
**Answer: b) return to step 2 and choose another strategy** — the steps are moved back and forth through, not walked once.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What is reviewing?
a) checking the answer, preferably by a different method  b) finding what is asked, what is known and not known, and what type of answer is required  c) choosing a strategy — a list, a picture, elimination, a formula, guess and check, or a simpler related problem  d) using the chosen strategy — and returning to step 2 for another if it fails
**Answer: a) checking the answer, preferably by a different method**
