# Video script — Polya's four steps

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **Polya's four steps**.

**understanding the problem** — finding what is asked, what is known and not known, and what type of answer is required.

**making a plan** — choosing a strategy — a list, a picture, elimination, a formula, guess and check, or a simpler related problem.

**executing the plan** — using the chosen strategy — and returning to step 2 for another if it fails.

**review and extend** — checking the answer by a different method and evaluating where else the method could be used.

Now you try. Say each word. Say what it means.
