# Hands-on — Dynamic Programming

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Count the waste** — For the staircase problem (climb 1 or 2 steps; count ways to reach step n), list the subproblems for n=6 and count how many times a plain recursion solves 'ways to reach step 2'. Then build the table instead.

## Task two
**Name the pieces** — Take a problem of shortest routes or cheapest shopping across days. Write its subproblem in one sentence, its smallest case, and its joining rule.
