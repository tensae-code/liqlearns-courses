# Hands-on — ScratchJr Climate Sketches — Programming a Message

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Make a climate saynète** — In groups, script and program a 30-second ScratchJr climate sketch ending in one act the viewer can do. Show it; the audience states your sentence.

## Task two
**Footprint to scene** — Take three footprint facts from the scenario's first séance. Turn each into a one-line scene idea. The class votes which would stage best — and says why.
