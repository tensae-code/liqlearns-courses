# Video script — ScratchJr Climate Sketches — Programming a Message

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **saynète** is a tiny scene with a point. The scenario's sketches — “Agir pour le climat” (Act for the climate) — join two crafts already learned: ScratchJr programming, and message design (a situation, a turn, a sentence that stays, as in the prevention video's family). Climate content supplies the matter: the carbon footprint defined in the scenario's first séance becomes scenes — a light left on, a journey chosen, a tap dripping — each ending in the act that answers it.
Programming a sketch spends blocks on **timing and emphasis**: characters enter when needed, speech appears line by line, a scene holds a beat longer on the message's key moment. Groups then perform-exchange: sketches are shown, and the audience must state each sketch's sentence-that-stays. If they cannot, the sketch — however charming — has not yet done its job, and its makers revise the staging, not the audience.
