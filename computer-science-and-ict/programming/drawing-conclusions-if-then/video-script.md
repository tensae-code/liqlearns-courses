# Video script — Drawing Conclusions — If… Then…

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

If this happens, then that follows.

If it rains, then the ground gets wet.

The if part comes first.

The then part follows it.

A picture can speak in judgements.

Look. Say what it shows.

Then draw your conclusion.

If… then…. In that order.
