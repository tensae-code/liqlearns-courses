# Hands-on — Drawing Conclusions — If… Then…

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Finish the conclusion
Your teacher says if parts: 'If the sun shines all day…' 'If we plant the seed…' Finish each with a then part that truly follows — and say why it follows.

## Read a picture
Choose a picture. Name three things it shows. Turn one into a judgement. Then draw one if… then… conclusion from the picture, if part first.
