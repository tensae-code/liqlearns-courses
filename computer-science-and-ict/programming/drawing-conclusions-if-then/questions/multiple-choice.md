# Drawing Conclusions — If… Then… — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In a conclusion, which part comes first?**
a) The then part  b) The if part  c) It does not matter
**Answer: b) The if part** — The if part is what happens first; the then part follows from it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) 'If you water the plant, then…' — the ending that follows is —**
a) the plant grows  b) the plant turns into a stone  c) nothing can follow
**Answer: a) the plant grows** — A conclusion's then part must truly follow from its if part.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Looking at a picture, what is the first step?**
a) Guess a conclusion  b) Name what the picture shows  c) Close the book
**Answer: b) Name what the picture shows** — Look first, compose the judgement from what is shown, then conclude.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Why is 'if the ground is wet, then it rained' a weak conclusion?**
a) It is too long  b) Wet ground can come from poured water, not only rain  c) Ground is never wet
**Answer: b) Wet ground can come from poured water, not only rain** — The then part must truly follow — here it may not, so the conclusion is not trusted.
