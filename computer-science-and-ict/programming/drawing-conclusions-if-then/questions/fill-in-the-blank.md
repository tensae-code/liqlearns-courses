# Drawing Conclusions — If… Then… — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A conclusion has the shape: ____ … then ….**
**Answer: if**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) From a picture you first ____ what it shows, then compose a judgement.**
**Answer: name**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The then part must truly ____ from the if part.**
**Answer: follow**
