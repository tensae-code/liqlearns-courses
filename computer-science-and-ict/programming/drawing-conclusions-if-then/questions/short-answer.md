# Drawing Conclusions — If… Then… — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Finish this conclusion and say why your ending follows: 'If the bell rings, then…'**
**Answer:** '…then the lesson ends' (or the class's true routine) — it follows because the bell is the signal that ends the lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A picture shows a child with a coat on, and snow outside. Compose one judgement from the picture, then one conclusion.**
**Answer:** Judgement: e.g. 'Snow is falling outside' or 'The child is wearing a coat' (checked against the picture). Conclusion: e.g. 'If the child goes out in the snow, then the coat keeps the child warm.'

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Why must a conclusion's parts stay in their order?**
**Answer:** Because the then part follows from the if part in time and in reason; swapped, the claim can become false — wet ground does not prove rain, since water can be poured.
