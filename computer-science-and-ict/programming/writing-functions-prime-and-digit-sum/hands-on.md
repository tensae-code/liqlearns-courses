# Hands-on — Writing Functions Prime and Digit Sum

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 5 Programming Time (prime() returns 1 or 0; digitsSum() sums 0..n) and Programming Practice (write square(x), power(x, y) and a factorial function) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach writing functions prime and digit sum to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
