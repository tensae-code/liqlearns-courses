# Hands-on — Breadth-First Search

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Human queue
Six people stand as nodes in the yard, holding string edges. One is the start. Run the search in bodies: the visited line is a real queue on the ground — no one jumps the line. Call out each layer as it forms.

## Handshake distance
Pick two people in your school who do not know each other. Using 'knows' as the edge, find the shortest chain of people joining them — you have just run breadth-first search on a real graph. Write the chain and its length.
