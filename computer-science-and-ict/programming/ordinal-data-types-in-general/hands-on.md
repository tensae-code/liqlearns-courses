# Hands-on — Ordinal Data Types in General

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Family census** — Place ten types (integer, real, char, boolean, a month enumeration, 1..12, a string, longint, a colour enumeration, double) into ordinal / not ordinal, with the reason for the two hardest.

## Task two
**Tool matching** — For each of ord, succ, pred, a for loop and a case selector, name one legal and one illegal argument type and defend both.
