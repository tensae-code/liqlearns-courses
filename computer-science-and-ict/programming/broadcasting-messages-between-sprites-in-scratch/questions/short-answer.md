# Broadcasting Messages Between Sprites in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. A game's 'caught' moment must start three things on three sprites. Show how messages do it in one move.**
**Answer:** A sound answer: the catching script broadcasts 'caught'; each of the three sprites carries a when-I-receive-'caught' script doing its part — all three wake on the one broadcast.

**2. Give one risk of running a whole game by messages.**
**Answer:** A sound answer, e.g.: the structure becomes invisible — signals instead of visible scene order — so name mix-ups wake the wrong scripts or none, and tangles are harder to trace than backdrop switches.
