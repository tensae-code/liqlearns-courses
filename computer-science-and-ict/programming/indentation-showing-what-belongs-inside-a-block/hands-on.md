# Hands-on — Indentation — Showing What Belongs Inside a Block

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Indent the slab** — Take a flush-left program (provided) with a nested if inside a loop. Rewrite it indented, one step per level. Swap with a partner: can they name each block's body from your stairs alone?

## Task two
**Break and repair** — Receive a program with lying indentation (steps that contradict the blocks). Find every lie by executing the program mentally, then rewrite it honest.
