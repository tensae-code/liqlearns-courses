# Indentation — Showing What Belongs Inside a Block — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is indentation?**
**Answer: Starting the lines inside a block further right, one step per nesting level**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three insides does the lesson name?**
**Answer: An if's body, a loop's inside, a sub-program's steps**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the indented program show?**
**Answer: Its skeleton — what each block governs**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What can you see without reading a word?**
**Answer: What the condition governs and where the loop's body ends**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which scénario teaches it?**
**Answer: Square CT Scénario 5, Séance 2 (Grade 6)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does indentation mirror?**
**Answer: The program's structure itself**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens with scrambled indentation?**
**Answer: Readers misjudge the program's behaviour**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Is indentation decoration?**
**Answer: No — it is meaning, kept honest**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How often are programs read against written?**
**Answer: Read a hundred times for every writing**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) In which representations does the séance work?**
**Answer: Block representations and logigrammes (flowcharts)**
