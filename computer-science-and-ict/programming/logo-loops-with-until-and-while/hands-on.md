# Hands-on — LOGO loops with until and while

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Be the loop
Stand and count as the loop: a partner reads the until program line by line; you say each printed number and step forward, and freeze the moment the condition turns true. Then run the while program from 5 the same way — and freeze before 10.

## Trace on paper
Make a trace table for the until example: one row per round — count before, printed, count after. Five rows. The table is the loop slowed down until it can be seen.
