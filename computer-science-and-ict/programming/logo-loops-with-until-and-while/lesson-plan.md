# Lesson plan — LOGO loops with until and while

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Programming · **Lesson:** LOGO loops with until and while
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 12 — Programming in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 8 book, Unit 6 — Logic Oriented Graphics Oriented (LOGO) Programming. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** until and while repeat an action under a condition — until [condition][action] runs until the condition turns true; while [condition][action] runs while the condition stays true — and a loop's true output is found by following the condition honestly.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the conditionals lesson — a loop is a conditional that is asked again after every round.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the forms of until and while and how their conditions differ (until stops when true; while runs while true).
2. Work the until example — make "count 1, print and add 1 until :count > 5 — and give its output.
3. Work the while example from 5 with count < 10 and give its true output (5–9), saying why 10 is not printed.

## What you need
A computer with MSW LOGO open; paper and a pencil.

1. **Do it first** — the learner counts aloud from 1, adding one each time, and stops exactly when the count passes 5 — saying the rule that stopped them, before until and while are named (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Reads the while example's output as 'up to 10' → follow the condition: while count < 10 lets 9 print, then count becomes 10, the condition is false, and the loop stops before 10 is printed.
