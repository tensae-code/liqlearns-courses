# Video script — LOGO loops with until and while

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A loop is a condition asked **again and again**.

**until.** Count with me: make count **1**.
Print. Add one. Print. Add one.
**1 — 2 — 3 — 4 — 5.** Count passed 5? **Stop.** That is until: repeat **until** the condition turns true.

**while.** Start at **5**. While count is **below 10**, print and add.
**5 — 6 — 7 — 8 — 9.** Now count is 10. Below 10? **No. Stop.**
Ten is never printed. The condition guards the door.
