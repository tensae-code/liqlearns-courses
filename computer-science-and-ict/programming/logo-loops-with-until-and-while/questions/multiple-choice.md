# LOGO loops with until and while — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** until repeats an action —
a) forever  b) until its condition becomes true  c) exactly ten times  d) while its condition is true
**Answer: b) until its condition becomes true.**

**2.** The book's until example prints —
a) 0 1 2 3 4  b) 1 2 3 4 5  c) 1 2 3 4 5 6  d) 5 4 3 2 1
**Answer: b) 1 2 3 4 5** — it stops when the count passes 5.

**3.** In the until example, the action holds two commands. They are —
a) print, then clear  b) print :count, then make "count :count + 1  c) home, then print  d) make, then stop
**Answer: b) print :count, then make "count :count + 1.**

**4.** while repeats an action —
a) while its condition stays true  b) until its condition is true  c) once only  d) backwards
**Answer: a) while its condition stays true.**

**5.** The while example starting at 5 with count < 10 truly prints —
a) 5 6 7 8 9 10  b) 5 6 7 8 9  c) 10 only  d) 5 5 5 5 5
**Answer: b) 5 6 7 8 9** — at 10 the condition is false and the loop stops before printing.
