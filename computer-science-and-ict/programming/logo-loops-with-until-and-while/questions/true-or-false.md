# LOGO loops with until and while — true or false

Say true or false for each one.

**1.** In until [:count > 5], the loop stops when the count becomes greater than 5.
**Answer: True.**

**2.** The while example prints 10 as its last number.
**Answer: False** — the condition count < 10 is checked before each round; at 10 it is false, so 9 is the last number printed.

**3.** A loop is a condition asked again after every round.
**Answer: True.**
