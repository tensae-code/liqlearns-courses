# LOGO loops with until and while — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** The teacher guide keys the closing activity's until loop — starting at 12, stepping by 2, until the count passes 20. Give its output.
**Answer: 12 14 16 18 20 — each round prints, then adds 2, and the loop stops when the count passes 20. (Keyed in the guide's Unit 6 answer key.)**

**2.** Why does the book's claim that the while example outputs 5–10 fail its own condition?
**Answer: The guard is count < 10, checked before each round. When the count reaches 10 the guard is false, so that round never runs and 10 is never printed.**
