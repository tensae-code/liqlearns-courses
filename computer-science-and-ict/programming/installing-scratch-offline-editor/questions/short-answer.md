# Installing the Scratch Offline Editor — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is installed so Scratch works without internet?**
**Answer: The Scratch Offline Editor**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must be installed with the editor?**
**Answer: Adobe AIR**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the editor need Adobe AIR?**
**Answer: It needs it in order to run**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How often is the install done?**
**Answer: Once**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is not needed to start working offline?**
**Answer: No browser, connection or account**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which grades’ books repeat the install?**
**Answer: Grades 6, 7, 8 and 9**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the offline editor to the course?**
**Answer: Its working home**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do lessons and projects assume?**
**Answer: That Scratch is on the machine**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does a class lose if its internet fails?**
**Answer: Nothing — the editor and saved projects are already there**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the installed Scratch open from?**
**Answer: The computer, like any other program**
