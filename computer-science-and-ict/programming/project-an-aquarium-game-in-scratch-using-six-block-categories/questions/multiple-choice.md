# Project — An Aquarium Game in Scratch Using Six Block Categories — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the project begin?**
a) With Grade 4's aquarium — an underwater scene of swimming fish  b) A player action, a win or loss, and fish behaviour to answer  c) The player's action, the fish's answers, and the winning moment  d) A picture
**Answer: a) With Grade 4's aquarium — an underwater scene of swimming fish**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three things turn the scene into a game?**
a) The player's action, the fish's answers, and the winning moment  b) A player action, a win or loss, and fish behaviour to answer  c) A picture  d) The tank's life — its swimming scene
**Answer: b) A player action, a win or loss, and fish behaviour to answer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the planning name first?**
a) A picture  b) The tank's life — its swimming scene  c) The player's action, the fish's answers, and the winning moment  d) Motion, Looks, Sound, Events, Control and Sensing
**Answer: c) The player's action, the fish's answers, and the winning moment**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a scene with fish, before the game layer?**
a) The tank's life — its swimming scene  b) Motion, Looks, Sound, Events, Control and Sensing  c) The scoring  d) A picture
**Answer: d) A picture**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What must the game layer not lose?**
a) The tank's life — its swimming scene  b) Motion, Looks, Sound, Events, Control and Sensing  c) The scoring  d) Events
**Answer: a) The tank's life — its swimming scene**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the six categories the game must use.**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do Operators and Variables add where the game counts?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is the breadth checked?**
