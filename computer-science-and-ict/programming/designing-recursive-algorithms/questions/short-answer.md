# Designing Recursive Algorithms — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What two parts must a recursive design name?**
**Answer: The base case and the recurrence**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the base case?**
**Answer: The smallest version of the problem, answered directly with no further call**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the recurrence?**
**Answer: The restating of the problem as smaller copies of itself, plus the work of joining their answers**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens without a base case?**
**Answer: The calls never stop — the descent has no floor**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does naming the two parts give you?**
**Answer: The recursive function almost writes itself**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What duty does the recurrence carry?**
**Answer: Every smaller copy must be closer to the base case**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which book example joins two smaller copies?**
**Answer: Fibonacci — each term from the two before it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which part is written first?**
**Answer: The base case**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must the designer argue at the end?**
**Answer: The shrinking — why the copies approach the base case**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What are the checklist's four items?**
**Answer: Name the size, write the base case, write the recurrence, argue the shrinking**
