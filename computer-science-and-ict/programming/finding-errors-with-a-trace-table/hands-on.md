# Hands-on — Finding Errors with a Trace Table

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 1 §1.6 Identification and Correction of Errors and §1.6.1 Trace Table (the volleyball-heights logical error) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach finding errors with a trace table to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
