# Hands-on — Arguments Parameters and Pass by Value

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 5, function arguments and parameters (the fun(x, y) pass-by-value example; declaring a function before or after main) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach arguments parameters and pass by value to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
