# Hands-on — The Scratch Screen — Stage, Sprites and Blocks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Interface orienteering** — On a projected Scratch screen, pupils direct a “driving” teacher by interface names only (“select the second sprite; open its costumes”). Wrong names = wrong clicks, kindly shown.

## Task two
**Map the bench** — Draw the Scratch interface from memory with eight labelled zones. Swap maps; the partner must find three named things on your map in under ten seconds each.
