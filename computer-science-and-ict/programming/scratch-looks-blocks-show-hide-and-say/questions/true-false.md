# Scratch Looks Blocks — Show, Hide and Say — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the say block put over the sprite?" is: A speech bubble with the words written in it**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Who hears what a sprite says?" is: The sprite's thoughts, in a thought bubble**
**Answer: False** — Everyone on stage

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the think block show?" is: The sprite's thoughts, in a thought bubble**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Who does not hear a thought?" is: Who knows what — the scene, or only the audience**
**Answer: False** — The other characters

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the choice between say and think?" is: Who knows what — the scene, or only the audience**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does hide do to a sprite?" is: The show block**
**Answer: False** — Takes it off the stage, still in the project with its scripts

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What brings a hidden sprite back?" is: The show block**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does switch costume change?" is: The stage behind the sprite**
**Answer: False** — The look the sprite wears

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does switch backdrop change?" is: The stage behind the sprite**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name the looks family in Cuba's order." is: A speech bubble with the words written in it**
**Answer: False** — Say, think, show, hide, costume, backdrop, size
