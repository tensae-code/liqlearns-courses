# Scratch Looks Blocks — Show, Hide and Say — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the say block put over the sprite?**
**Answer: A speech bubble with the words written in it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who hears what a sprite says?**
**Answer: Everyone on stage**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the think block show?**
**Answer: The sprite's thoughts, in a thought bubble**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Who does not hear a thought?**
**Answer: The other characters**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the choice between say and think?**
**Answer: Who knows what — the scene, or only the audience**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does hide do to a sprite?**
**Answer: Takes it off the stage, still in the project with its scripts**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What brings a hidden sprite back?**
**Answer: The show block**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does switch costume change?**
**Answer: The look the sprite wears**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does switch backdrop change?**
**Answer: The stage behind the sprite**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name the looks family in Cuba's order.**
**Answer: Say, think, show, hide, costume, backdrop, size**
