# Scratch Looks Blocks — Show, Hide and Say — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the say block put over the sprite?**
a) A speech bubble with the words written in it  b) Everyone on stage  c) The sprite's thoughts, in a thought bubble  d) The other characters
**Answer: a) A speech bubble with the words written in it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who hears what a sprite says?**
a) The sprite's thoughts, in a thought bubble  b) Everyone on stage  c) The other characters  d) Who knows what — the scene, or only the audience
**Answer: b) Everyone on stage**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the think block show?**
a) The other characters  b) Who knows what — the scene, or only the audience  c) The sprite's thoughts, in a thought bubble  d) Takes it off the stage, still in the project with its scripts
**Answer: c) The sprite's thoughts, in a thought bubble**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Who does not hear a thought?**
a) Who knows what — the scene, or only the audience  b) Takes it off the stage, still in the project with its scripts  c) The show block  d) The other characters
**Answer: d) The other characters**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the choice between say and think?**
a) Who knows what — the scene, or only the audience  b) Takes it off the stage, still in the project with its scripts  c) The show block  d) The look the sprite wears
**Answer: a) Who knows what — the scene, or only the audience**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does hide do to a sprite?**
a) The show block  b) Takes it off the stage, still in the project with its scripts  c) The look the sprite wears  d) The stage behind the sprite
**Answer: b) Takes it off the stage, still in the project with its scripts**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What brings a hidden sprite back?**
a) The look the sprite wears  b) The stage behind the sprite  c) The show block  d) Say, think, show, hide, costume, backdrop, size
**Answer: c) The show block**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does switch costume change?**
a) The stage behind the sprite  b) Say, think, show, hide, costume, backdrop, size  c) A speech bubble with the words written in it  d) The look the sprite wears
**Answer: d) The look the sprite wears**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does switch backdrop change?**
a) The stage behind the sprite  b) Say, think, show, hide, costume, backdrop, size  c) A speech bubble with the words written in it  d) Everyone on stage
**Answer: a) The stage behind the sprite**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name the looks family in Cuba's order.**
a) A speech bubble with the words written in it  b) Say, think, show, hide, costume, backdrop, size  c) Everyone on stage  d) The sprite's thoughts, in a thought bubble
**Answer: b) Say, think, show, hide, costume, backdrop, size**
