# Hands-on — Scratch Looks Blocks — Show, Hide and Say

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Enter on cue', 'Hide a sprite at the start of a script, make it appear with show at its moment, and give it one said line and one thought line. Say why each line is said or thought.')

## Task two
('One sprite, three looks', "Switch a sprite's costume and size and the stage's backdrop in one short scene, and name the block that made each change.")
