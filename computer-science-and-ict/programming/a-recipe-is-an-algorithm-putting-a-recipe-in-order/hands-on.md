# Hands-on — A Recipe Is an Algorithm — Putting a Recipe in Order

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Algorithmise a recipe** — Take a real family recipe in sentence form. Rewrite it as numbered instructions, one action per line. Swap with a partner: they “cook” it mentally and mark every step that cannot be done as written.

## Task two
**Find the loops** — In three recipes (cards provided), underline every loop in one colour and every if in another. Present the loopiest recipe to the class.
