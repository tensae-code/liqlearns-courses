# Hands-on — Dynamic Data Structures in Pascal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
** Pointer drawing** — Draw memory for: two integers by var, one by new, a pointer reassigned — marking what becomes unreachable (leaked) at the reassignment.

## Task two
**List walk** — Sketch the node chain for the list 4 → 7 → 9 → nil, then the pointer changes that insert 5 after 4 and remove 7.
