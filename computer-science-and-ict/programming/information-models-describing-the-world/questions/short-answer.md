# Information Models — Describing the World — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a model?**
**Answer: A stand-in for a real thing, made to be studied instead of it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is an information model?**
**Answer: A stand-in made of information, keeping only the features that matter for the purpose**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Can the same original have many information models?**
**Answer: Yes — different purposes keep different features**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a verbal model do?**
**Answer: Describes the thing in words**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a formula model do?**
**Answer: Describes with mathematical formulas, so values can be calculated**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a video/pictorial model keep?**
**Answer: How the thing looks, in images or film**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a scheme model show?**
**Answer: The structure — parts and connections — while dropping appearance**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is the model's form chosen?**
**Answer: By the purpose it must serve**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Give an example of an information model.**
**Answer: A timetable, a map, or a formula**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a model leave out?**
**Answer: The features that do not matter for its purpose**
