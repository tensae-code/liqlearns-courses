# Information Models — Describing the World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a model?**
a) A stand-in for a real thing, made to be studied instead of it  b) Describes the thing in words  c) How the thing looks, in images or film  d) By the purpose it must serve
**Answer: a) A stand-in for a real thing, made to be studied instead of it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is an information model?**
a) Describes with mathematical formulas, so values can be calculated  b) A stand-in made of information, keeping only the features that matter for the purpose  c) The structure — parts and connections — while dropping appearance  d) A timetable, a map, or a formula
**Answer: b) A stand-in made of information, keeping only the features that matter for the purpose**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Can the same original have many information models?**
a) How the thing looks, in images or film  b) By the purpose it must serve  c) Yes — different purposes keep different features  d) The features that do not matter for its purpose
**Answer: c) Yes — different purposes keep different features**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a verbal model do?**
a) The structure — parts and connections — while dropping appearance  b) A timetable, a map, or a formula  c) A stand-in for a real thing, made to be studied instead of it  d) Describes the thing in words
**Answer: d) Describes the thing in words**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a formula model do?**
a) Describes with mathematical formulas, so values can be calculated  b) By the purpose it must serve  c) The features that do not matter for its purpose  d) A stand-in made of information, keeping only the features that matter for the purpose
**Answer: a) Describes with mathematical formulas, so values can be calculated**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is the model's form chosen?**
