# Hands-on — Information Models — Describing the World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One thing, three models
Choose one thing (your school day, a bicycle, the water cycle). Make three information models of it: a verbal one in three sentences, a scheme with boxes and arrows, and say what a formula model of it would calculate. Note what each model keeps and what it leaves out.

## Purpose picks the model
For each purpose — calculating a journey's time, showing a new pupil where the classrooms are, describing what your school looks like — name the model form you would use and why.
