# Video script — Information Models — Describing the World

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **model** is a stand-in for a real thing, made to be studied instead of it.
An **information model** is a stand-in made of information: it keeps the features of the original that matter for the maker's **purpose** and leaves the rest out.
The book names the forms by how the information is carried.
A **verbal model** describes in words.
