# List Operations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What two things does a list keep?**
a) Its values in order, one after another — with no fixed length  b) Appends a value at the end  c) Opens a place in the middle and shifts the rest along  d) Takes a value out and closes the company up
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does add do?**
a) Opens a place in the middle and shifts the rest along  b) Appends a value at the end  c) Takes a value out and closes the company up  d) Greece Grade 8 Pliroforiki B, Unit 6
**Answer: b**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does insert do?**
a) Takes a value out and closes the company up  b) Greece Grade 8 Pliroforiki B, Unit 6  c) Opens a place in the middle and shifts the rest along  d) By its position (index) — first, third, last
**Answer: c**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does remove do?**
a) Greece Grade 8 Pliroforiki B, Unit 6  b) By its position (index) — first, third, last  c) A loop with a counter visiting every element once  d) Takes a value out and closes the company up
**Answer: d**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches it?**
a) Greece Grade 8 Pliroforiki B, Unit 6  b) By its position (index) — first, third, last  c) A loop with a counter visiting every element once  d) Printing, summing and searching the list
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a value reached?**
a) A loop with a counter visiting every element once  b) By its position (index) — first, third, last  c) Printing, summing and searching the list  d) Reaches any place at once but cannot grow
**Answer: b**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is traversal?**
a) Printing, summing and searching the list  b) Reaches any place at once but cannot grow  c) A loop with a counter visiting every element once  d) Grows freely but is walked step by step
**Answer: c**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name traversal's standing jobs.**
a) Reaches any place at once but cannot grow  b) Grows freely but is walked step by step  c) Its values in order, one after another — with no fixed length  d) Printing, summing and searching the list
**Answer: d**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the array's side of the trade?**
a) Reaches any place at once but cannot grow  b) Grows freely but is walked step by step  c) Its values in order, one after another — with no fixed length  d) Appends a value at the end
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the list's side?**
a) Its values in order, one after another — with no fixed length  b) Grows freely but is walked step by step  c) Appends a value at the end  d) Opens a place in the middle and shifts the rest along
