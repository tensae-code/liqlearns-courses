# List Operations — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What two things does a list keep?**
**Answer: Its values in order, one after another — with no fixed length**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does add do?**
**Answer: Appends a value at the end**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does insert do?**
**Answer: Opens a place in the middle and shifts the rest along**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does remove do?**
**Answer: Takes a value out and closes the company up**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 8 Pliroforiki B, Unit 6**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a value reached?**
**Answer: By its position (index) — first, third, last**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is traversal?**
**Answer: A loop with a counter visiting every element once**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name traversal's standing jobs.**
**Answer: Printing, summing and searching the list**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the array's side of the trade?**
**Answer: Reaches any place at once but cannot grow**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the list's side?**
**Answer: Grows freely but is walked step by step**
