# Hands-on — Lists in Scratch — Storing Many Values in Order

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Renumber it
Build a names list, delete its second item, and read the new numbers aloud to prove deleting renumbers the rest.

## Two-list budget
Track five expenses on two parallel lists (kind, price), total them with a counter loop, and compute the savings against an income.
