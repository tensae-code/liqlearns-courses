# Hands-on — Logical Expressions and Truth Tables

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the AND table
Write the truth table for “mark above 50 AND attended”. Fill all four rows and circle the only row where the whole expression is true.

## NOT and OR
Take one condition from your school day. Write its NOT, then join it with a second condition using OR, and fill that truth table too.
