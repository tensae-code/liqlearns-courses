# Logical Expressions and Truth Tables — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a logical expression join?**
**Answer: Conditions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) With what are conditions joined?**
**Answer: Logical words — AND, OR and NOT**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What two values can a condition take?**
**Answer: True or false**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When is an AND expression true?**
**Answer: When both sides are true**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When is an OR expression true?**
**Answer: When at least one side is true**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does NOT do?**
**Answer: It turns true into false and false into true**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a truth table list?**
**Answer: Every possible combination of truth values and the result for each**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How many rows does a truth table for two conditions have?**
**Answer: Four rows**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four rows for two conditions.**
**Answer: True/true, true/false, false/true, false/false**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does filling a truth table prove?**
**Answer: Exactly when the expression is true**
