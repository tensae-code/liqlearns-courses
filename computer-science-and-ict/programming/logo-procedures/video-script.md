The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Give the set a name. One word: SQUARE.

Built-in words are primitives. FORWARD is one.
Your words are procedures you teach Logo.

Rule: define first, use after.
Or Logo answers: not in my vocabulary.
