# Lesson plan — LOGO loops with do.until and do.while — predicting outputs

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Programming · **Lesson:** LOGO loops with do.until and do.while — predicting outputs
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 12 — Programming in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 8 book, Unit 6 — Logic Oriented Graphics Oriented (LOGO) Programming. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** do.until and do.while are until and while with the order swapped — action first, condition second — and the real skill of this lesson: predicting a program's output before it runs.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the until and while lesson — the swapped forms mean nothing until the plain forms are sure.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State how do.until and do.while differ from until and while — the action and the condition are reversed.
2. Work both counting examples: do.until printing 1 to 10, do.while printing 1 to 9.
3. Predict the outputs of the unit's four closing programs — the ifelse test, the until step-2 loop, the do.until step-5 loop, and the ring of 20 squares.

## What you need
A computer with MSW LOGO open; paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Treats do.while as identical in output to do.until → the guards differ (passes 10 against below 10): the same start prints 1–10 under one and 1–9 under the other. The guard, not the shape, decides the last number.
