# Hands-on — LOGO loops with do.until and do.while — predicting outputs

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Predict the four
The unit's closing activity, done properly: for each of the four programs — the ifelse test on 50 and 51, the until loop stepping by 2 from 12, the do.until loop stepping by 5 from 1, and SQUARE repeated 20 times with a turn — write the predicted output on paper first. Then run each in MSW LOGO and mark the prediction. A wrong prediction is the lesson working, not failing.

## Swap the order
Take the until example from the last lesson and rewrite it as do.until with the same guard. Before running, say whether the output changes and why. Run both and compare.
