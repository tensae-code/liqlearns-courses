# LOGO loops with do.until and do.while — predicting outputs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** do.until differs from until in that —
a) it uses no condition  b) the action and the condition are reversed — action first, condition second  c) it never stops  d) it only draws
**Answer: b) the action and the condition are reversed — action first, condition second.**

**2.** The do.until worked example prints —
a) 1 to 9  b) 0 to 10  c) 1 up to 10  d) 10 down to 1
**Answer: c) 1 up to 10** — before the count passes 10.

**3.** The do.while worked example prints —
a) 1 to 9  b) 1 to 10  c) 1 to 100  d) only odd numbers
**Answer: a) 1 to 9** — while the count stays below 10.

**4.** The closing activity's fourth program — SQUARE repeated 20 times with a turn — draws —
a) one big square  b) a straight line  c) a ring of squares  d) a star
**Answer: c) a ring of squares.**

**5.** The do.until loop stepping by 5 from 1 prints —
a) 1 6 11  b) 1 5 10  c) 5 10 15  d) 1 2 3 4 5
**Answer: a) 1 6 11** (keyed in the teacher guide's Unit 6 answer key).
