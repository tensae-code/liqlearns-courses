# Video script — LOGO loops with do.until and do.while — predicting outputs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Same loops — **order swapped**.

**until** asks first: condition, then action.
**do.until** acts first: action, then condition.

Count with **do.until**: print, add one — stop when the count **passes 10**.
**1 to 10.** All printed.
Count with **do.while**: print while **below 10**.
**1 to 9.** Ten never comes.

And the last picture of the unit: **SQUARE**, twenty times, turning each time —
a **ring of squares**. Predict first. Then run.
