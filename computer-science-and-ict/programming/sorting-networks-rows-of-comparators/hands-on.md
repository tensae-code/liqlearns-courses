# Hands-on — Sorting Networks — Rows of Comparators

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Build for four** — Design a sorting network for 4 wires (hint: five comparators suffice). Test it on all the class's shuffled quadruples, including sorted and reverse-sorted.

## Task two
**Sabotage and repair** — Swap networks with another team, each secretly removing or moving one comparator. Find the counterexample, trace it, repair, and prove the fix.
