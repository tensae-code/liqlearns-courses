# Sorting Networks — Rows of Comparators — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a sorting network?**
**Answer: A fixed pattern of comparators that sorts any input**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does one comparator do?**
**Answer: Joins two wires and leaves the smaller value on the agreed wire**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What can fire in parallel?**
**Answer: Comparators on separate wires — a whole row at once**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a network need none of?**
**Answer: Decisions, memory, control — the pattern is the program**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a network discovered in the inquiry?**
**Answer: Place comparators, test with shuffled inputs, watch order emerge**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a panne here?**
**Answer: A fault — a comparator missing, misplaced or wired wrong**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does a faulty network behave?**
**Answer: It fails on some inputs and passes others**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What starts a repair?**
**Answer: Finding a failing input — a counterexample**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is traced, wire by wire?**
**Answer: That input's journey through the network**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is a network correct?**
**Answer: When no input can embarrass it**
