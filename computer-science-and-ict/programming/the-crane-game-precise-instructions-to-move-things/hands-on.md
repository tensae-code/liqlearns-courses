# Hands-on — The Crane Game — Precise Instructions to Move Things

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Play the crane** — In pairs with three objects to move into a box. Operator instructs, crane obeys literally (vague = freeze). Swap roles after each object; count instructions per successful move.

## Task two
**Rewrite the failures** — Collect the class's five vaguest instructions of the day on the board. Rewrite each into a precise one, naming what was added.
