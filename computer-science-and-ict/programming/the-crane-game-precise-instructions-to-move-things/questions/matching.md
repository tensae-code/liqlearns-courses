# The Crane Game — Precise Instructions to Move Things — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Who are the game's two roles?** → **The crane (who obeys) and the operator (who instructs)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the machine's first law here?** → **A machine does only what it is told**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Does the crane guess what you meant?** → **No — it obeys literally**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What plays the crane's jib and claw?** → **The pupil's arm and hand**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Who does all the seeing and thinking?** → **The operator, through the instructions**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What fixes “go down”?** → **An amount — “down ten centimetres”**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What three things does a precise instruction name?** → **Direction, amount, order**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is a failure repaired?** → **Name its vagueness and rewrite the instruction**
