# The Crane Game — Precise Instructions to Move Things — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Who are the game's two roles?**
**Answer: The crane (who obeys) and the operator (who instructs)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the machine's first law here?**
**Answer: A machine does only what it is told**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Does the crane guess what you meant?**
**Answer: No — it obeys literally**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What plays the crane's jib and claw?**
**Answer: The pupil's arm and hand**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Who does all the seeing and thinking?**
**Answer: The operator, through the instructions**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What fixes “go down”?**
**Answer: An amount — “down ten centimetres”**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What three things does a precise instruction name?**
**Answer: Direction, amount, order**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is a failure repaired?**
**Answer: Name its vagueness and rewrite the instruction**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the repair loop, before the word exists?**
**Answer: Debugging**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) In a failure, what was wrong — crane or program?**
**Answer: The program — never the crane**
