# Hands-on — Converting Between Binary, Octal, Hexadecimal and Decimal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**The full map** — Convert 45.625 into binary, octal and hexadecimal by the lesson's methods, then convert each back to prove it.

## Task two
**The endless fraction** — Convert 0.1 to binary for eight digits. Write the precision you kept and the error you accepted, in one honest sentence.
