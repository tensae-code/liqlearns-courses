# Converting Between Binary, Octal, Hexadecimal and Decimal — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the two master methods.**
**Answer: Place-value expansion into decimal, and repeated division out of it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the power-of-two shortcut?**
**Answer: Grouping bits — threes for octal, fours for hexadecimal**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is octal reached from hexadecimal?**
**Answer: Through binary — expand to bits, regroup**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the proof habit?**
**Answer: Convert back and land on the number you left**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How many pairs of bases does this lesson cover?**
**Answer: Every pair among the four systems**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How do fractions convert?**
**Answer: Repeated multiplication by the new base, taking whole parts as digits**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is 0.625 in binary?**
**Answer: 0.101**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to 0.1 decimal in binary?**
**Answer: It becomes an endless pattern**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must the honest converter state?**
**Answer: That it does not finish, and the precision kept**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) In what order are fraction digits taken?**
**Answer: In the order the whole parts appear — first is nearest the point**
