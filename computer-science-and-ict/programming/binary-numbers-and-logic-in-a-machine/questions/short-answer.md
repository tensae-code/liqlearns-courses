# Binary Numbers and Logic in a Machine — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are a machine's two states?**
**Answer: On and off**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What digits does binary use?**
**Answer: 1 for on and 0 for off**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What becomes of every value inside the machine?**
**Answer: Patterns of the two states**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do the two values become for the programmer?**
**Answer: True and false — yes and no**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Grade 8 meet binary?**
**Answer: Inside programming, where it will be used**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What three logic words combine inputs?**
**Answer: AND, OR and NOT**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the combination decide?**
**Answer: The output**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Give the lesson's machine example.**
**Answer: A lock sensing 'door closed AND code correct' before unlocking**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is programming, in this lesson's shape?**
**Answer: Inputs, logic and output — deciding written in words**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which later lessons formalise the logic?**
**Answer: The logic gates and Boolean lessons**
