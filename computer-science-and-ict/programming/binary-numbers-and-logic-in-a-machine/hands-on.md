# Hands-on — Binary Numbers and Logic in a Machine

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Two-state census** — Find five machines around you with on/off parts. For each, name what its 1 and 0 mean.

## Task two
**Decide like a machine** — Write the logic of one everyday machine — a lift, a washing machine, a phone lock — as inputs combined by AND, OR, NOT deciding one output.
