# ΓΛΩΣΣΑ — Operators, Functions, Expressions and Assignment — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name ΓΛΩΣΣΑ's arithmetic operators.**
**Answer: + − * /, DIV (integer division), MOD (remainder) and ^ (power)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is an expression?**
**Answer: Values, variables and operators joined into one value to be worked out**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What decides an expression's meaning?**
**Answer: The order of working — brackets, powers, ×/÷, then +/−**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the comparison operators.**
**Answer: =, <>, <, >, <=, >=**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the logical operators.**
**Answer: ΚΑΙ (and), Η (or), ΟΧΙ (not)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three ΓΛΩΣΣΑ functions.**
**Answer: Α_Τ (absolute value), Τ_Ρ (square root), Α_Μ (integer part) — any three from the lesson**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a function do?**
**Answer: Takes a value in and hands a value back**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the assignment command?**
**Answer: name ← expression — work the expression out and store the answer in the name**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why an arrow and not = ?**
**Answer: The arrow points at the place that receives; = stays the sign of comparison**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 12 Anaptyxi Efarmogon, Chapter 7**
