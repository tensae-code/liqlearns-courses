# What Programming Is — Concepts and the Parts of a Language — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a program?**
**Answer: A set of instructions, written to solve a problem or do a task, that the computer carries out in order**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who is the programmer?**
**Answer: The person who writes the program — understanding the problem, planning the steps and giving them to the machine**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is programming, in the book's wide sense?**
**Answer: The whole craft — the thinking the code carries, not only the typing of code**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What three parts make a programming language?**
**Answer: Its words, its symbols and its rules**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are a language's words?**
**Answer: The reserved commands the language allows**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are its symbols?**
**Answer: The signs for operations and values**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do its rules fix?**
**Answer: How words and symbols may be joined into instructions the computer understands**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when the rules are broken?**
**Answer: The instruction is refused — as a sentence with broken grammar fails between people**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book open with concepts?**
**Answer: Because the whole Grade 9 book is programming — the concepts carry every later lesson**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which later language is examined against these parts?**
**Answer: Every one — including Scratch's blocks**
