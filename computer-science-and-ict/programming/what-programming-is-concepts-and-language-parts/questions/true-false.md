# What Programming Is — Concepts and the Parts of a Language — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a program?" is: A set of instructions, written to solve a problem or do a task, that the computer carries out in order**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Who is the programmer?" is: Its words, its symbols and its rules**
**Answer: False** — The person who writes the program — understanding the problem, planning the steps and giving them to the machine

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is programming, in the book's wide sense?" is: The whole craft — the thinking the code carries, not only the typing of code**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What three parts make a programming language?" is: The signs for operations and values**
**Answer: False** — Its words, its symbols and its rules

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are a language's words?" is: The reserved commands the language allows**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What are its symbols?" is: The instruction is refused — as a sentence with broken grammar fails between people**
**Answer: False** — The signs for operations and values

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do its rules fix?" is: How words and symbols may be joined into instructions the computer understands**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens when the rules are broken?" is: Every one — including Scratch's blocks**
**Answer: False** — The instruction is refused — as a sentence with broken grammar fails between people

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why does the book open with concepts?" is: Because the whole Grade 9 book is programming — the concepts carry every later lesson**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which later language is examined against these parts?" is: The person who writes the program — understanding the problem, planning the steps and giving them to the machine**
**Answer: False** — Every one — including Scratch's blocks
