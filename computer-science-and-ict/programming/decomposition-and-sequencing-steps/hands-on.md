# Hands-on — Decomposition and Sequencing Steps

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Decompose a school task', 'Take 'run a class drink stall for a day'. Decompose it into at least five parts, and for each part write its input and its output.')

## Task two
('Sequence the stall', 'Take your five parts, number them in running order, and beside each step write what must already be done before it.')
