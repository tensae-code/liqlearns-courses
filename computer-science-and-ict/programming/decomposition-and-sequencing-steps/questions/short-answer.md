# Decomposition and Sequencing Steps — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does decomposition mean?**
**Answer: Breaking a problem into smaller parts that can each be solved on their own**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What local example does Malaysia's Grade 7 book use?**
**Answer: Making popia basah — a filled, wrapped food**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When is a part small enough?**
**Answer: When you can say exactly what goes into it and what comes out of it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must the parts together still do?**
**Answer: Cover the whole problem — no part may be lost**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Malay term Malaysia uses for decomposition?**
**Answer: Leraian**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does sequencing mean?**
**Answer: Writing the steps in the strict order they must run**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why must the filling exist before wrapping?**
**Answer: Some steps must finish before others can start**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What two checks does each step's place need?**
**Answer: What must already be done before it, and what it makes possible next**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what order does Malaysia teach the pair?**
**Answer: Decompose first, then sequence**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is an unordered pile of good parts?**
**Answer: Still not a solution**
