# Decomposition and Sequencing Steps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does decomposition mean?**
a) Breaking a problem into smaller parts that can each be solved on their own  b) Making popia basah — a filled, wrapped food  c) When you can say exactly what goes into it and what comes out of it  d) Cover the whole problem — no part may be lost
**Answer: a) Breaking a problem into smaller parts that can each be solved on their own**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What local example does Malaysia's Grade 7 book use?**
a) When you can say exactly what goes into it and what comes out of it  b) Making popia basah — a filled, wrapped food  c) Cover the whole problem — no part may be lost  d) Leraian
**Answer: b) Making popia basah — a filled, wrapped food**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When is a part small enough?**
a) Cover the whole problem — no part may be lost  b) Leraian  c) When you can say exactly what goes into it and what comes out of it  d) Writing the steps in the strict order they must run
**Answer: c) When you can say exactly what goes into it and what comes out of it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must the parts together still do?**
a) Leraian  b) Writing the steps in the strict order they must run  c) Some steps must finish before others can start  d) Cover the whole problem — no part may be lost
**Answer: d) Cover the whole problem — no part may be lost**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Malay term Malaysia uses for decomposition?**
a) Leraian  b) Writing the steps in the strict order they must run  c) Some steps must finish before others can start  d) What must already be done before it, and what it makes possible next
**Answer: a) Leraian**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does sequencing mean?**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why must the filling exist before wrapping?**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What two checks does each step's place need?**
a) Decompose first, then sequence  b) Still not a solution  c) Breaking a problem into smaller parts that can each be solved on their own  d) What must already be done before it, and what it makes possible next

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what order does Malaysia teach the pair?**
