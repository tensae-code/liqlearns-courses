# Standard Functions and Expressions in Pascal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name three standard functions.**
a) abs, sqr and sqrt (any three)  b) Gives a value without its sign  c) 3  d) Turn real numbers into integers
**Answer: a) abs, sqr and sqrt (any three)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does abs do?**
a) 3  b) Gives a value without its sign  c) Turn real numbers into integers  d) By name, with the value in brackets
**Answer: b) Gives a value without its sign**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is sqrt(9)?**
a) Turn real numbers into integers  b) By name, with the value in brackets  c) 3  d) Operations — + - * / and div and mod
**Answer: c) 3**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do trunc and round do?**
a) By name, with the value in brackets  b) Operations — + - * / and div and mod  c) Brackets  d) Turn real numbers into integers
**Answer: d) Turn real numbers into integers**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a standard function called?**
a) By name, with the value in brackets  b) Operations — + - * / and div and mod  c) Brackets  d) Integer division and its remainder
**Answer: a) By name, with the value in brackets**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What joins values in an expression?**
a) Brackets  b) Operations — + - * / and div and mod  c) Integer division and its remainder  d) As a division in brackets, written flat
**Answer: b) Operations — + - * / and div and mod**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is worked out first?**
a) Integer division and its remainder  b) As a division in brackets, written flat  c) Brackets  d) Its result
**Answer: c) Brackets**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do div and mod serve?**
a) As a division in brackets, written flat  b) Its result  c) abs, sqr and sqrt (any three)  d) Integer division and its remainder
**Answer: d) Integer division and its remainder**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is a school fraction written in Pascal?**
a) As a division in brackets, written flat  b) Its result  c) abs, sqr and sqrt (any three)  d) Gives a value without its sign
**Answer: a) As a division in brackets, written flat**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What takes an expression's place once worked out?**
a) abs, sqr and sqrt (any three)  b) Its result  c) Gives a value without its sign  d) 3
**Answer: b) Its result**
