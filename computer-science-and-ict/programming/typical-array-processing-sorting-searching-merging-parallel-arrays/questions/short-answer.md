# Typical Array Processing — Sorting, Searching, Merging, Parallel Arrays — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does sorting do to an array?**
**Answer: Puts its elements in order in place**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does searching answer?**
**Answer: The element's position — or that it is absent**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does merging require of its two arrays?**
**Answer: Both already in order**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does merging proceed?**
**Answer: Repeatedly take the smaller of the two front values into the new array**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What must merging never do?**
**Answer: Join and re-sort — wasting the order both arrays brought**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are parallel arrays?**
**Answer: Arrays whose same index describes one thing — names in one, marks in the other**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must every operation do to them?**
**Answer: Move the companions together**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the classic parallel-array fault?**
**Answer: Sorting one array without swapping the other — the pair starts lying**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How does the guide teach them?**
**Answer: Solved and unsolved exercises in Chapter 4**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Same index, one what?**
**Answer: One thing described together**
