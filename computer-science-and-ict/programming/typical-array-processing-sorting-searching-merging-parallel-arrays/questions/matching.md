# Typical Array Processing — Sorting, Searching, Merging, Parallel Arrays — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does sorting do to an array?** → **Puts its elements in order in place**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does searching answer?** → **The element's position — or that it is absent**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does merging require of its two arrays?** → **Both already in order**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does merging proceed?** → **Repeatedly take the smaller of the two front values into the new array**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What must merging never do?** → **Join and re-sort — wasting the order both arrays brought**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are parallel arrays?** → **Arrays whose same index describes one thing — names in one, marks in the other**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must every operation do to them?** → **Move the companions together**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the classic parallel-array fault?** → **Sorting one array without swapping the other — the pair starts lying**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How does the guide teach them?** → **Solved and unsolved exercises in Chapter 4**
