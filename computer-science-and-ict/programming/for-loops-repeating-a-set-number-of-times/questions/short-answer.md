# For Loops — Repeating a Set Number of Times (Visual Basic) — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What kind of loop is For…Next?**
**Answer: A definite loop — repeating a set number of times**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What governs it?**
**Answer: A counter — with a written start, end and step**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does Next do?**
**Answer: Returns control to the top with the counter stepped onward**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When does the loop finish?**
**Answer: When the counter passes the end**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why can a For loop be 'counted by reading'?**
**Answer: Start, end and step are all written in the code**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Give a definite job example.**
**Answer: Print the table of 7 (visit 30 pupils; deal 52 cards)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What else can the block use the counter as?**
**Answer: A value — the multiplier, the turn's number: each turn knows which turn it is**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Kuwait open the loops book here?**
**Answer: Definiteness is traceable on paper, turn by turn — the safe first country**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which book and chapter teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 12 Term 2, Book 1 (Loops), Chapter 1 — Definite Loops**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 12 Information Technology Term 2, Book 1 Chapter 1**
