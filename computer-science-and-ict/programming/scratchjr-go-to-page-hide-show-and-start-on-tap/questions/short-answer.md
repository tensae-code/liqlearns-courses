# ScratchJr — Go to Page, Hide–Show and Start on Tap — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does Go to Page do?**
**Answer: Moves the story to another page (scene) of the project**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a page in ScratchJr?**
**Answer: One scene or room of the story**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do Hide and Show do?**
**Answer: Make a sprite invisible, then visible again**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is a hidden sprite gone from the project?**
**Answer: No — present but invisible**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name a story use of Hide–Show.**
**Answer: A surprise, an entrance or a disappearance (any one)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Start on Tap do?**
**Answer: Starts a script when the reader taps that sprite**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What changes when stories use tap?**
**Answer: The story is handled, not only watched**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Give an example of a tap trigger.**
**Answer: Tap the door and it opens (any sensible example)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which three powers does this lesson teach?**
**Answer: Go to Page, Hide–Show, and Start on Tap**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 4 Digital Explorer Term 1, Unit 2 Lesson 3**
