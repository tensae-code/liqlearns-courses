# Hands-on — Project — A Voyage in Space: An Animated Scratch Story

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Route map
Draw the voyage as a route of three scenes with their backdrops. Under each scene write: the sprites in it, and the one thing that happens.

## Build the voyage
Build the story scene by scene in Scratch, switching backdrops as the rocket travels. Run it for a partner who has not seen your plan: ask them to retell the journey.
