# Video script — Project — A Voyage in Space: An Animated Scratch Story

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The project plans the story as a journey in scenes: a launch or departure, the travelling through space, and an arrival — each scene with its backdrop (Earth, the stars, the destination) and its travellers, a rocket or traveller sprite above all. The plan names what changes at each scene turn: which backdrop, which sprites appear, and what the watcher must understand has happened. A voyage story is its route: if the scenes do not move the journey forward, the plan is fixed before any code.
The animation runs on the taught kit together: motion and waits pace the travelling, backdrop switches carry the journey from place to place, say blocks and sounds voice the travellers and the space around them, and conditions and sensing let the story answer its moments — a star touched, a signal received. The project's check is the voyage watched whole: a watcher travels from departure to arrival, in order, and can say afterwards where the story went and what happened on the way.
