# Lesson plan — Project — A Voyage in Space: An Animated Scratch Story

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Programming · **Lesson:** Project — A Voyage in Space: An Animated Scratch Story
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Morocco (2026-11-14) — in the title Programming on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Morocco's Grade 5 “J'excelle en informatique” teacher guide, because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson. This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only (Morocco), named in lesson/sources.md, counted nowhere.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The project plans the story as a journey in scenes: a launch or departure, the travelling through space, and an arrival — each scene with its backdrop (Earth, the stars, the destination) and its travellers, a rocket or traveller sprite above all. The animation runs on the taught kit together: motion and waits pace the travelling, backdrop switches carry the journey from place to place, say blocks and sounds voice the travellers and the space around them, and conditions and sensing let the story answer its moments — a star touched, a signal received.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.
