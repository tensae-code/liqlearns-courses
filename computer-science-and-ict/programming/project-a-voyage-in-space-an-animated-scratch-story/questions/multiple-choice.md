# Project — A Voyage in Space: An Animated Scratch Story — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How is the voyage planned?**
a) As a journey in scenes — departure, travelling, arrival  b) Its backdrop and its sprites  c) The rocket or traveller sprite  d) The backdrop, the appearing sprites, and what has happened
**Answer: a) As a journey in scenes — departure, travelling, arrival**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does each scene carry?**
a) The rocket or traveller sprite  b) Its backdrop and its sprites  c) The backdrop, the appearing sprites, and what has happened  d) Its route — the scenes must move the journey forward
**Answer: b) Its backdrop and its sprites**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which sprite travels the whole story?**
a) The backdrop, the appearing sprites, and what has happened  b) Its route — the scenes must move the journey forward  c) The rocket or traveller sprite  d) Motion and waits
**Answer: c) The rocket or traveller sprite**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the plan name at each scene turn?**

**Answer: d) The backdrop, the appearing sprites, and what has happened**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a voyage story, in the plan's test?**

**Answer: a) Its route — the scenes must move the journey forward**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What paces the travelling?**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What carries the journey between places?**

a) Conditions and sensing  b) The watcher can say where the story went and what happened  c) As a journey in scenes — departure, travelling, arrival  d) Its backdrop and its sprites

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the project's check?**
