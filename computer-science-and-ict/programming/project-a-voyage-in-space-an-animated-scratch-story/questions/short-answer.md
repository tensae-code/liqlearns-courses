# Project — A Voyage in Space: An Animated Scratch Story — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How is the voyage planned?**
**Answer: As a journey in scenes — departure, travelling, arrival**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does each scene carry?**
**Answer: Its backdrop and its sprites**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which sprite travels the whole story?**
**Answer: The rocket or traveller sprite**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the plan name at each scene turn?**
**Answer: The backdrop, the appearing sprites, and what has happened**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a voyage story, in the plan's test?**
**Answer: Its route — the scenes must move the journey forward**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What paces the travelling?**
**Answer: Motion and waits**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What carries the journey between places?**
**Answer: Backdrop switches**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What voices the travellers?**
**Answer: Say blocks and sounds**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What lets the story answer its moments?**
**Answer: Conditions and sensing**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the project's check?**
**Answer: The watcher can say where the story went and what happened**
