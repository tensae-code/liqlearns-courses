# Video script — Scratch loops and conditions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **Scratch loops and conditions**.

**the stage's coordinates** — X runs −240 to 240 and Y runs −180 to 180 — past the edge, the sprite leaves the visible stage.

**the worked if…else** — if the sprite touches the rightmost edge, set its x position to −100; otherwise move 10 steps right.

**keyboard control** — built inside a forever loop from 'when key … pressed' sensing blocks for the four arrow keys, each moving the sprite 10 steps.

**repeat until** — a loop that does not know its count in advance — the condition can even be fed by input while the program runs.

Now you try. Say each word. Say what it means.
