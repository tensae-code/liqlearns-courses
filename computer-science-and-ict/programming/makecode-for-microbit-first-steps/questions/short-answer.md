# MakeCode for micro:bit — First Steps — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the micro:bit?**
**Answer: A small programmable board — a computer whose inputs and outputs are visible and touchable**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three of its features a program can use.**
**Answer: The LED grid, the buttons, and its sensors (any three)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the LED grid do?**
**Answer: Displays pictures and text**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do its sensors feel?**
**Answer: Movement, direction, light and temperature (any of these)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does MakeCode open?**
**Answer: In the browser**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How are MakeCode programs built?**
**Answer: By dragging blocks from the block families together**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the simulator?**
**Answer: A picture-board on screen that runs the program as the real board would**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why test on the simulator first?**
**Answer: So work is checked before it is downloaded to the board**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How are projects kept?**
**Answer: Saved and opened by name**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 5 Digital Explorer Term 2, Unit 2 Lesson 7**
