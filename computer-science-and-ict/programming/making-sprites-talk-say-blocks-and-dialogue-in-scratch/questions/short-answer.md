# Making Sprites Talk — Say Blocks and Dialogue in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the say block do?**
**Answer: It puts words in a speech bubble above the sprite**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does say for seconds add?**
**Answer: It holds the line for a set time**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is Grade 7’s lion’s line?**
**Answer: “I am the lion”**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is long speech spaced?**
**Answer: So it can be read**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Arabic appear reversed in Scratch?**
**Answer: Because Scratch writes left-to-right**
