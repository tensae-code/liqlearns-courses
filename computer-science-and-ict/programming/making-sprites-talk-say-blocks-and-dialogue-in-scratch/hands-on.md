# Hands-on — Making Sprites Talk — Say Blocks and Dialogue in Scratch

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it like the lion
Make a sprite say “I am the lion” with say-for-seconds, and space a long line so it can be read.

## Run a dialogue by broadcast
Script a two-sprite dialogue where broadcasts cue each turn and each sprite’s line plays or shows on its cue.
