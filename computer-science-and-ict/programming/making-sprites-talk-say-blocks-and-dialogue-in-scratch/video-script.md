# Video script — Making Sprites Talk — Say Blocks and Dialogue in Scratch

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The **say block** gives a sprite words in a bubble — Grade 7’s lion announces “I am the lion” — and **say for seconds** holds the line for a set time. Long speech is **spaced** so it can be read. Grade 6 keeps the local note: **Arabic appears reversed in Scratch**, because Scratch writes left-to-right.
A conversation is timed turns: **say-for-seconds plus waits** so one speaks while the other is silent and speakers do not overlap — the Abby and Maya conversation, the homeland dialogue project. Grade 9 runs the turns by **broadcasts**: in the race of the conceited one and the persistent one, each sprite’s **recorded line plays on its cue**.
