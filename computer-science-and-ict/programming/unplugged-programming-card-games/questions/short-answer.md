# Unplugged Programming — Card Games — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does unplugged mean here?**
**Answer: Teaching computing with no computer — the ideas carried by bodies, paper and cards**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two card kinds does the game use?**
**Answer: Character cards (the figures who act) and command cards (the instructions they obey)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who executes the program in the game?**
**Answer: A player, playing the character — exactly, step by step, as a machine would**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What may the executing player not do?**
**Answer: Guess what was meant — only do what was written**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to a command that can be read two ways?**
**Answer: It will be — read the other way, honestly and literally**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What can intention not patch?**
**Answer: A missing step**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book place the game beside Scratch?**
**Answer: The pupil who has been the computer meets the real machine already knowing what it asks**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Being debugged by friends teaches what?**
**Answer: Exactness — faster than an error message**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 7, Bab 1 B.2a — unplugged programming card games**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What carries the ideas in unplugged work?**
**Answer: Bodies, paper and cards — nothing about a screen can hide them**
