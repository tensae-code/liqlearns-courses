This lesson: **Counting with range() — start, stop-before, step**.

First, do it. Run the opening program. Watch what it prints.

Then the idea, in order: One, two or three parameters: range(5) gives 0–4; range(2, 5) gives 2–4; range(1, 10, 2) gives 1, 3, 5, 7, 9. The end is always excluded. Anything typed arrives as a string; int() converts it to a number range() can use. Counting ranges in practice: 51 to 70; steps of 10.

Now you. Write the output of range(5), range(2, 5) and range(1, 10, 2) from memory, then check with list().

Say it to your companion: Explain stop-before to your companion with the GIF: the counter climbs and halts before the end number — then say why counting TO a typed number needs n + 1.
