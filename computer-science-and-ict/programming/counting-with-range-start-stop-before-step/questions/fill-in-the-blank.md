# Counting with range() — start, stop-before, step — fill in the blank

Fill in the blank with the right word or symbol.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** range() stops ____ the specified end.
**Answer: before**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The third parameter of range is the ____.
**Answer: step**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Typed input must be cast with ____ before range can use it as a number.
**Answer: int()**
