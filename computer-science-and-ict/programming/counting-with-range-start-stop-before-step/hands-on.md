# Hands-on — Counting with range() — start, stop-before, step

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — every activity is done for real, in IDLE or on paper.

## Number-line walk
Chalk a number line 0–6. Walk it as range(5): stand on 0,1,2,3,4 — and refuse to step on 5, saying 'stop-before' aloud.

## Target practice
A partner names a target list (51 to 70; tens to 100). You write the range() call that makes exactly that list, then check with list() in the shell.
