# Video script — LOGO drawing commands — fd, bk, rt, lt, cs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four commands move the turtle.
Fd: forward. Steps.
Bk: back. Steps.
Rt: right. Lt: left. Angles.

Fd ten. Rt ninety. Watch it draw.
Wrong drawing? Cs. Clear screen. Home again.

Predict on paper. Then run.
That is how programmers think.
