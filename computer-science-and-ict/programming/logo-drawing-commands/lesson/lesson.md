# LOGO drawing commands — fd, bk, rt, lt, cs

## Do it first

Pencil ready. A turtle stands at the centre of a page, facing up. It obeys: forward 10, turn right 90, forward 10. Draw the path you predict — every corner of it. By the end of this lesson you will run those commands for real and find out if you were right.

## Sub lesson 1 — The four commands

The turtle obeys four basic drawing commands. **Forward** and **backward** — abbreviated **fd** and **bk** — each takes **one argument: a number of steps**. **Right** and **left** — **rt** and **lt** — each takes **an angle**. The turtle's **home is the centre of the screen**. The book's examples: **fd 10** goes forward 10 steps; **rt 90** turns right 90 degrees; **lt 90** turns left 90 degrees; **bk 10** goes back 10 steps. And the eraser: **cs** (clear screen) **erases all drawings and returns the turtle to the centre**. Watch the GIF: fd, then rt — the turtle draws as it goes.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chaining commands — the guide's worked sequence (teacher guide — evidence)

Commands chain: the turtle does them in the order you give them. The teacher guide works one sequence step by step: **move twenty steps forward, turn fifty degrees left, move twenty steps forward again** — and it pairs the screen work with **drawing the result on paper first**. Predict, then run: the paper prediction is what teaches; the screen only marks it. Try the bent path in the second GIF — fd 20, lt 50, fd 20 — on paper before you watch it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Run fd 10, rt 90, lt 90, bk 10 — the book's examples — one at a time.
2. Draw a path of your own with four commands, predicting it on paper first.
3. Clear the screen with cs and say everything cs did.
