# LOGO drawing commands — fd, bk, rt, lt, cs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** fd 10 means —
a) turn forward 10 degrees  b) go forward 10 steps  c) draw 10 squares  d) go back 10 steps
**Answer: b) go forward 10 steps** — forward, a number of steps.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** rt 90 means —
a) move right 90 steps  b) turn right 90 degrees  c) repeat 90 times  d) go home
**Answer: b) turn right 90 degrees** — turns take angles.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** bk is short for —
a) break  b) backward  c) book  d) backspace
**Answer: b) backward** — backward — bk 10 goes back 10 steps.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** cs does what?
a) saves the drawing  b) erases all drawings and returns the turtle to the centre  c) closes Logo  d) centres the text
**Answer: b) erases all drawings and returns the turtle to the centre** — clear screen — clean slate, home position.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The turtle's home is —
a) a corner  b) the centre of the screen  c) the Commander window  d) the desktop
**Answer: b) the centre of the screen** — home is the centre.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's sequence fd 20, lt 50, fd 20 draws —
a) a square  b) a bent path of two segments with a 50-degree turn  c) a circle  d) a straight line
**Answer: b) a bent path of two segments with a 50-degree turn** — two segments, one left bend.
