# Counting Elements in an Array — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the counter start at?**
**Answer: Zero**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When does the counter rise?**
**Answer: Exactly when an element passes the test**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the walk store?**
**Answer: Nothing — its product is the single count**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the test for even elements?**
**Answer: a[i] % 2 == 0**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What must the test match?**
**Answer: The question's exact words, edges included**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Does 'above 10' include 10?**
**Answer: No — 'at least 10' includes it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of off-by-one hides in counting tasks?**
**Answer: One in the condition, not the loop**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is above-the-average a two-pass question?**
**Answer: The average must exist before elements can be compared with it**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the first pass compute there?**
**Answer: The total (and so the average)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Does counting change the array?**
**Answer: No**
