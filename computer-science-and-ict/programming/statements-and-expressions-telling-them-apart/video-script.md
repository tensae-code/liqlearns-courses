# Video script - Statements and expressions - telling them apart

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
A short video script for this lesson; its placement names no full-script need.

---

Every line of Python is one of two things.

A statement is an instruction. It DOES something.
x = 5 - stores. Silently.
print() - displays.
(If, while, for - those wait for Grade 12.)

An expression is worth something.
The interpreter evaluates it to a value.
A literal. A variable. Or a combination: x + 2.

And remember the two rooms:
In the SHELL, an expression shows its value at once.
In the EDITOR, it shows nothing - unless print() shows it.

Statement or expression? Ask: does it give an order - or is it worth a value?
