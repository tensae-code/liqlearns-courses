# Hands-on - Statements and expressions - telling them apart

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Line sort

Line cards from the unit's own programs - assignments, print lines, literals, variables, combinations - are sorted into STATEMENT and EXPRESSION trays. The checker uses the lesson's two questions: does it give an order? Is it worth a value?

## The silent script

On paper, write a three-line script: an assignment, a bare expression, a print() of the same expression. A partner predicts - before 'running' it on paper - exactly what will display, and why the middle line stays silent.
