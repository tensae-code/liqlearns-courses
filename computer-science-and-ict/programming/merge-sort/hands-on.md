# Hands-on — Merge Sort

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Merge by hand** — Sort [8, 3, 7, 1, 5] by merge sort on paper: draw the splitting tree, then every merge, front items compared.

## Task two
**Count the levels** — For a list of 16 items, how many splitting levels are there? How many items are merged at each level in total?
