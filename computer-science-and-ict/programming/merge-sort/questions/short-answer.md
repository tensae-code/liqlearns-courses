# Merge Sort — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are merge sort's two phases?**
**Answer: Split the list down to single items, then merge the pieces back in order**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When does splitting stop?**
**Answer: When every piece holds one item**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is a one-item list already sorted?**
**Answer: A single item has nothing out of order**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How much comparing happens during splitting?**
**Answer: None — splitting is pure division**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which technique is merge sort the showpiece of?**
**Answer: Divide and conquer**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does a merge proceed?**
**Answer: Repeatedly take the smaller of the two pieces' front items**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when one piece empties?**
**Answer: The other piece's remaining items follow in order**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the joined list sorted?**
**Answer: Each half was sorted, and the merge never takes a larger front while a smaller one waits**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How much work does merge sort do?**
**Answer: About n log n — log n levels of splitting, everything merged once per level**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must the two pieces be before merging?**
**Answer: Each already sorted**
