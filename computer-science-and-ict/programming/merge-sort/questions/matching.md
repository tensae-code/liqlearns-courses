# Merge Sort — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are merge sort's two phases?** → **Split the list down to single items, then merge the pieces back in order**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When does splitting stop?** → **When every piece holds one item**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is a one-item list already sorted?** → **A single item has nothing out of order**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How much comparing happens during splitting?** → **None — splitting is pure division**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which technique is merge sort the showpiece of?** → **Divide and conquer**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does a merge proceed?** → **Repeatedly take the smaller of the two pieces' front items**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when one piece empties?** → **The other piece's remaining items follow in order**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the joined list sorted?** → **Each half was sorted, and the merge never takes a larger front while a smaller one waits**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How much work does merge sort do?** → **About n log n — log n levels of splitting, everything merged once per level**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must the two pieces be before merging?** → **Each already sorted**
