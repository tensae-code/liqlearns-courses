# Graphical Programming Elements — Menus and Dialog Boxes — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a menu?**
**Answer: The application's organised offer of its commands**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a menu bar hold?**
**Answer: Named groups (File, Edit, Help), each opening a list of items**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does choosing an item do?**
**Answer: Fires its command**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a menu's honesty?**
**Answer: Completeness with order — everything grouped as users think of the work**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 12 Anaptyxi Efarmogon, Chapter 11**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a dialog box?**
**Answer: A small window that interrupts for one exchange — it asks or it tells**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name a telling dialog.**
**Answer: The error message (or the finished notice)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does the exchange end?**
**Answer: Its buttons — OK, Cancel and their kin — give the answer the application waits for**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What else does the chapter teach?**
**Answer: Applications communicating with other applications**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Applications here are neighbours, not what?**
**Answer: Not islands — one workshop**
