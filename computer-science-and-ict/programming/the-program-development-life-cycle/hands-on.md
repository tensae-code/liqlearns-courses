# Hands-on — The Program Development Life Cycle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run the first stages
Pick a real repeated task at home or school. Write its problem definition (inputs, outputs, three limits or special cases) and design its algorithm as a flowchart of at least six symbols. Swap with a partner: can they follow your design without you explaining?

## Test like a tester
Take any small program or a partner's algorithm. Prepare three test cases — one normal, one extreme, one invalid — run them, and write down what each test exposed and what debugging change it demands.
