# Comments in BASIC — REM — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does REM stand for?**
**Answer: Remark**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a REM line?**
**Answer: A comment — a note to the program's human readers**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the computer do with REM lines?**
**Answer: Ignores them completely — they change, print and compute nothing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Who are comments for?**
**Answer: The people — the programmer later, or another programmer reading the program**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book give comments their own section?**
**Answer: Because a program nobody can read is a program nobody can fix**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What should a good comment say?**
**Answer: Why — not a repetition of what the line does**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where do comments belong?**
**Answer: At the head of the program and before any part whose purpose is not obvious**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What goes in the head comment?**
**Answer: The program's name, its job and its writer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the rule of proportion?**
**Answer: Comment the thinking, not every line**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a program drowned in remarks do?**
**Answer: It hides its own code**
