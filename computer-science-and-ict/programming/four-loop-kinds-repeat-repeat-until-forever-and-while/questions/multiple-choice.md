# Four Loop Kinds — Repeat, Repeat Until, Forever and While — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How many loop kinds does VEXcode VR have in this lesson?**
a) Four — repeat, repeat until, forever and while  b) When its condition becomes true  c) Never on its own — it is stopped from outside  d) A counter variable counting the turns
**Answer: a) Four — repeat, repeat until, forever and while**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When does repeat stop?**
a) An if  b) After a counted number of times  c) The moment its condition turns false  d) A square and an octagon
**Answer: b) After a counted number of times**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When is repeat the right loop?**
a) Never on its own — it is stopped from outside  b) A counter variable counting the turns  c) When you know the count in advance  d) Eight
**Answer: c) When you know the count in advance**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When does repeat until stop?**
a) The moment its condition turns false  b) A square and an octagon  c) Four — repeat, repeat until, forever and while  d) When its condition becomes true
**Answer: d) When its condition becomes true**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What can sit inside a loop to let each turn decide?**
a) An if  b) A counter variable counting the turns  c) Eight  d) After a counted number of times
**Answer: a) An if**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When does forever stop?**
a) A square and an octagon  b) Never on its own — it is stopped from outside  c) Four — repeat, repeat until, forever and while  d) When you know the count in advance
**Answer: b) Never on its own — it is stopped from outside**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When does while stop?**
a) Eight  b) After a counted number of times  c) The moment its condition turns false  d) When its condition becomes true
**Answer: c) The moment its condition turns false**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can end a loop from inside?**
a) Four — repeat, repeat until, forever and while  b) When you know the count in advance  c) An if  d) A counter variable counting the turns
**Answer: d) A counter variable counting the turns**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the robot draw on the Art Canvas with these loops?**
a) A square and an octagon  b) After a counted number of times  c) When its condition becomes true  d) Never on its own — it is stopped from outside
**Answer: a) A square and an octagon**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How many sides does the counted octagon repeat draw?**
a) When you know the count in advance  b) Eight  c) An if  d) The moment its condition turns false
**Answer: b) Eight**
