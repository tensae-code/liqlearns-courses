# Four Loop Kinds — Repeat, Repeat Until, Forever and While — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How many loop kinds does VEXcode VR have in this lesson?**
**Answer: Four — repeat, repeat until, forever and while**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When does repeat stop?**
**Answer: After a counted number of times**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When is repeat the right loop?**
**Answer: When you know the count in advance**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When does repeat until stop?**
**Answer: When its condition becomes true**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What can sit inside a loop to let each turn decide?**
**Answer: An if**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When does forever stop?**
**Answer: Never on its own — it is stopped from outside**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When does while stop?**
**Answer: The moment its condition turns false**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can end a loop from inside?**
**Answer: A counter variable counting the turns**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the robot draw on the Art Canvas with these loops?**
**Answer: A square and an octagon**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How many sides does the counted octagon repeat draw?**
**Answer: Eight**
