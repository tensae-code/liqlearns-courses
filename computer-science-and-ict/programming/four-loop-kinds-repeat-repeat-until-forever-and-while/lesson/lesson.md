# Four Loop Kinds — Repeat, Repeat Until, Forever and While

## Do it first

One loop is not enough for every job. VEXcode VR keeps four — in this lesson you learn which job each one is for. (teacher guide — evidence)

## Sub lesson 1 — Repeat, and repeat until

**Repeat** runs its blocks a **counted number of times** — use it when you know the count in advance, as a square knows its four sides. **Repeat until** runs **until a condition becomes true**, checking as it goes — use it when the work must continue until something happens, however long that takes. An **if inside a loop** lets each turn decide something for itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — Forever, and while — and shapes on the Art Canvas

**Forever** loops with **no stop of its own** — for a robot that must keep responding until the program is stopped from outside. **While** runs **as long as a condition stays true** and stops the moment it turns false. A **counter variable** can end a loop from inside: count the turns, and let the count decide. With these loops the robot draws on the **Art Canvas** — a **square** by a counted repeat, an **octagon** by counting eight.
