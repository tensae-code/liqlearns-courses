# Hands-on — Four Loop Kinds — Repeat, Repeat Until, Forever and While

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four loops, four runs
Write the same short drive four ways — repeat, repeat until, forever (then stop it), while. Say what stopped each run.

## Draw the shapes
On the Art Canvas, draw a square with a counted repeat and an octagon with a counter variable. If a shape does not close, fix the count or the turn, not the pen.
