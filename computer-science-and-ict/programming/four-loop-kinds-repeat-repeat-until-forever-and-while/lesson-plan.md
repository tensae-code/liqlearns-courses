**The one thing this lesson teaches:** VEXcode VR has four loops, one for each job: repeat runs a counted number of times, repeat until runs until a condition becomes true, forever never stops on its own, and while runs as long as a condition stays true — with an if inside a loop and a counter variable, they draw exact shapes like a square and an octagon on the Art Canvas.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Repeat, and repeat until** — **Repeat** runs its blocks a **counted number of times** — use it when you know the count in advance, as a square knows its four sides. **Repeat until** runs **until a condition becomes true**, checking as it goes — use it when the work must continue until something happens, however long that takes. An **if inside a loop** lets each turn decide something for itself.
2. **Forever, and while — and shapes on the Art Canvas** — **Forever** loops with **no stop of its own** — for a robot that must keep responding until the program is stopped from outside. **While** runs **as long as a condition stays true** and stops the moment it turns false. A **counter variable** can end a loop from inside: count the turns, and let the count decide. With these loops the robot draws on the **Art Canvas** — a **square** by a counted repeat, an **octagon** by counting eight.
