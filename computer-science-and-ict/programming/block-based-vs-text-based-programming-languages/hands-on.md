# Hands-on — Block-Based and Text-Based Programming Languages

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Three clothings** — Take one algorithm (making tea, or a mark grade) and write it in pseudocode, flowchart shapes, and one block or text form.

## Task two
**Face audit** — List three things blocks make easier and three things text makes possible, each with an example.
