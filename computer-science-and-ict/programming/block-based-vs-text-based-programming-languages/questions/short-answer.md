# Block-Based and Text-Based Programming Languages — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How do block languages represent commands?**
**Answer: As shapes that snap together — only fitting joins possible**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What error can blocks not make?**
**Answer: Syntax errors cannot be typed**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the palette show?**
**Answer: Every command the language has**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What lives in text languages?**
**Answer: The whole literature of programming — libraries, idioms, tools**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is identical between the faces?**
**Answer: The concepts — sequence, selection, repetition**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What sits beneath both faces?**
**Answer: The algorithm**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is pseudocode?**
**Answer: Structured plain language for an algorithm's steps**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a flowchart draw?**
**Answer: Shapes for steps, decisions and flow**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's order?**
**Answer: Design in pseudocode/flowchart first, then dress in blocks or text**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who owns the algorithm?**
**Answer: The pupil who can move it between the three clothings**
