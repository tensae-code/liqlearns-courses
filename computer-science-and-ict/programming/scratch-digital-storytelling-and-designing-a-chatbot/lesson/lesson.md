# Scratch — Digital Storytelling and Designing a Chatbot

## Do it first

Grade 7's Scratch unit ends by making things with an audience: a story told in scenes on the Stage, and a small machine that talks back. Both are built from the same blocks — sequence, looks, sound and choice.

## Sub lesson 1 — Digital storytelling — scenes on the Stage

A **digital story** in Scratch is told as **scenes**: backdrops that change place and time, sprites whose **costumes, movements and say blocks** carry the action and the words, and a **sequence** the author has ordered so a watcher can follow who, where and what next. The unit teaches the storyteller's discipline inside the tool: plan the scenes first (a storyboard of the tale), then build scene by scene, testing the telling on a watcher who has not heard it before.

## Sub lesson 2 — Designing a chatbot — a sprite that answers

A **chatbot** in Scratch is a sprite that **asks, listens and answers by rule**: it uses **ask-and-wait** for the visitor's words, **sensing** of the answer, and **selection** to answer differently by what was said — greeting by name, answering the questions it knows, and replying honestly to the ones it does not. Designing it is the unit's small lesson in all conversational machines: the bot knows only the answers its maker wrote, which is why its honesty about the rest must also be written by its maker.

## Do It Yourself

1. Show it, don't just say it: As scenes — backdrops for place and time, sprites' costumes, movements and say blocks for action and words. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
2. Show it, don't just say it: The scenes — a storyboard of the tale. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
3. Show it, don't just say it: Scene by scene, in the story's order. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
4. Show it, don't just say it: On a watcher who has not heard it before. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
