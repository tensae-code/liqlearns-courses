## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Digital storytelling — scenes on the Stage** — A **digital story** in Scratch is told as **scenes**: backdrops that change place and time, sprites whose **costumes, movements and say blocks** carry the action and the words, and a **sequence** the author has ordered so a watcher can follow who, where and what next. The unit teaches the storyteller's discipline inside the tool: plan the scenes first (a storyboard of the tale), then build scene by scene, testing the telling on a watcher who has not heard it before.
2. **Designing a chatbot — a sprite that answers** — A **chatbot** in Scratch is a sprite that **asks, listens and answers by rule**: it uses **ask-and-wait** for the visitor's words, **sensing** of the answer, and **selection** to answer differently by what was said — greeting by name, answering the questions it knows, and replying honestly to the ones it does not. Designing it is the unit's small lesson in all conversational machines: the bot knows only the answers its maker wrote, which is why its honesty about the rest must also be written by its maker.
