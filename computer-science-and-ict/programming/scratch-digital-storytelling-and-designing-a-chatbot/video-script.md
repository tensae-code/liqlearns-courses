# Video script — Scratch — Digital Storytelling and Designing a Chatbot

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **digital story** in Scratch is told as **scenes**: backdrops that change place and time, sprites whose **costumes, movements and say blocks** carry the action and the words, and a **sequence** the author has ordered so a watcher can follow who, where and what next. The unit teaches the storyteller's discipline inside the tool: plan the scenes first (a storyboard of the tale), then build scene by scene, testing the telling on a watcher who has not heard it before.
A **chatbot** in Scratch is a sprite that **asks, listens and answers by rule**: it uses **ask-and-wait** for the visitor's words, **sensing** of the answer, and **selection** to answer differently by what was said — greeting by name, answering the questions it knows, and replying honestly to the ones it does not. Designing it is the unit's small lesson in all conversational machines: the bot knows only the answers its maker wrote, which is why its honesty about the rest must also be written by its maker.
