# Changing and Deleting a Scratch Sprite — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How is a sprite changed?**
**Answer: By choosing another sprite from the sprite library**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a newly chosen sprite appear?**
**Answer: On the stage and in the sprite list**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a new sprite start with?**
**Answer: Its own empty script area**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What order do the book's steps follow?**
**Answer: Open the library, choose the sprite, check the stage**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When might the old sprite be kept?**
**Answer: When another scene still needs it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a sprite deleted?**
**Answer: Select it in the sprite list and use its delete command**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What leaves the project when a sprite is deleted?**
**Answer: The sprite with its scripts and costumes**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What should you do instead of deleting a sprite needed later?**
**Answer: Hide it**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What check comes before deleting?**
**Answer: That no other part of the story depends on its scripts**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who is like a stage manager in a Scratch project?**
**Answer: The maker, casting and removing the actors — the sprites**
