# Changing and Deleting a Scratch Sprite — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How is a sprite changed?**
a) By choosing another sprite from the sprite library  b) On the stage and in the sprite list  c) Its own empty script area  d) Open the library, choose the sprite, check the stage
**Answer: a) By choosing another sprite from the sprite library**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a newly chosen sprite appear?**
a) Its own empty script area  b) On the stage and in the sprite list  c) Open the library, choose the sprite, check the stage  d) When another scene still needs it
**Answer: b) On the stage and in the sprite list**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a new sprite start with?**
a) Open the library, choose the sprite, check the stage  b) When another scene still needs it  c) Its own empty script area  d) Select it in the sprite list and use its delete command
**Answer: c) Its own empty script area**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What order do the book's steps follow?**
a) When another scene still needs it  b) Select it in the sprite list and use its delete command  c) The sprite with its scripts and costumes  d) Open the library, choose the sprite, check the stage
**Answer: d) Open the library, choose the sprite, check the stage**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When might the old sprite be kept?**
a) When another scene still needs it  b) Select it in the sprite list and use its delete command  c) The sprite with its scripts and costumes  d) Hide it
**Answer: a) When another scene still needs it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a sprite deleted?**
a) The sprite with its scripts and costumes  b) Select it in the sprite list and use its delete command  c) Hide it  d) That no other part of the story depends on its scripts
**Answer: b) Select it in the sprite list and use its delete command**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What leaves the project when a sprite is deleted?**
a) Hide it  b) That no other part of the story depends on its scripts  c) The sprite with its scripts and costumes  d) The maker, casting and removing the actors — the sprites
**Answer: c) The sprite with its scripts and costumes**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What should you do instead of deleting a sprite needed later?**
a) That no other part of the story depends on its scripts  b) The maker, casting and removing the actors — the sprites  c) By choosing another sprite from the sprite library  d) Hide it
**Answer: d) Hide it**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What check comes before deleting?**
a) That no other part of the story depends on its scripts  b) The maker, casting and removing the actors — the sprites  c) By choosing another sprite from the sprite library  d) On the stage and in the sprite list
**Answer: a) That no other part of the story depends on its scripts**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who is like a stage manager in a Scratch project?**
a) By choosing another sprite from the sprite library  b) The maker, casting and removing the actors — the sprites  c) On the stage and in the sprite list  d) Its own empty script area
**Answer: b) The maker, casting and removing the actors — the sprites**
