# The Rectangle Method for the Approximate Definite Integral — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The area under the curve over [a, b]**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The area under the curve over [a, b]** — as the answer to: What are the method's cuts?
**Answer: False — n equal subintervals of width h = (b − a) / n**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The sum of the rectangles' areas — Σ f(xᵢ)·h**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The sum of the rectangles' areas — Σ f(xᵢ)·h** — as the answer to: What happens as n grows?
**Answer: False — h shrinks, the staircase hugs the curve, the sum approaches the true area**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Method error — shrinking under refinement**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Method error — shrinking under refinement** — as the answer to: Name the three forms.
**Answer: False — Left, right and midpoint rectangles — by where the height is taken**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Under-shoot it (right rectangles overshoot it)**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Under-shoot it (right rectangles overshoot it)** — as the answer to: Why is the midpoint form usually most accurate?
**Answer: False — Its errors from the two sides of the curve partly cancel**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A bracket around the truth — and their difference bounds the error**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A bracket around the truth — and their difference bounds the error** — as the answer to: Write the sum's shape for left rectangles.
**Answer: False — h · (f(x₀) + f(x₁) + … + f(xₙ₋₁))**
