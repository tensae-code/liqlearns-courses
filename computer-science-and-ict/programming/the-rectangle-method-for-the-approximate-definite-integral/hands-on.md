# Hands-on — The Rectangle Method for the Approximate Definite Integral

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Staircase sum** — Approximate ∫₀¹ x² dx (true value 1/3) with n = 4 left, right and midpoint rectangles, full table shown, and rank the three errors.

## Task two
**Bracket the truth** — For ∫₁² (1/x) dx with n = 5, argue from monotonicity which sum over- and which under-estimates, before computing either.
