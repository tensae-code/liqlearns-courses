# Hands-on — A Dialogue Between Sprites

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Dolphin talk
Write a four-line conversation between two sprites. Build it with timed say blocks and waits, run it, and read along aloud — every line must be readable before the next starts.

## Break and fix
Deliberately shorten one wait until the dialogue breaks. Show a partner the fault, then fix it and prove the conversation runs clean again.
