# Video script — ScratchJr — Sound and Message Blocks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

ScratchJr's sound blocks make a sprite **make a noise at the moment its script reaches the block**: the ready-made **Pop** for a quick effect, and **recorded sound** — the pupil's own voice or a real-world sound, captured with the device — for everything a library pop cannot say. Sound is timed by its place in the script, like every block: the pop lands where the block stands.
A **message** is ScratchJr's signal between sprites: **Send Message** sends a coloured signal out, and **Start on Message** starts the script of any sprite waiting for that same colour. One sprite finishing its line can wake the next speaker — dialogue and turn-taking without the reader touching anything. Messages must match by colour: a script waiting on red hears nothing sent on blue.
