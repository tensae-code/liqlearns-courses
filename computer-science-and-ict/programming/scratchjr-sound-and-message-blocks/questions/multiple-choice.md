# ScratchJr — Sound and Message Blocks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name ScratchJr's two sound kinds in this lesson.**
a) The Pop block and recorded sound  b) The pupil's own voice or a real sound, captured with the device  c) At the moment the script reaches its block  d) A coloured signal sent between sprites
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is recorded sound?**
a) At the moment the script reaches its block  b) The pupil's own voice or a real sound, captured with the device  c) A coloured signal sent between sprites  d) Sends a coloured signal out to other sprites
**Answer: b**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a sound land in time?**
a) A coloured signal sent between sprites  b) Sends a coloured signal out to other sprites  c) At the moment the script reaches its block  d) Starts the script of a sprite waiting for that message colour
**Answer: c**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a message in ScratchJr?**
a) Sends a coloured signal out to other sprites  b) Starts the script of a sprite waiting for that message colour  c) Dialogue and turn-taking — one sprite waking the next speaker  d) A coloured signal sent between sprites
**Answer: d**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does Send Message do?**
a) Sends a coloured signal out to other sprites  b) Starts the script of a sprite waiting for that message colour  c) Dialogue and turn-taking — one sprite waking the next speaker  d) Yes — by colour; red sent is not heard by a script waiting on blue
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Start on Message do?**
a) Dialogue and turn-taking — one sprite waking the next speaker  b) Starts the script of a sprite waiting for that message colour  c) Yes — by colour; red sent is not heard by a script waiting on blue  d) No — the sprites set each other going
**Answer: b**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is messages' best story use?**
a) Yes — by colour; red sent is not heard by a script waiting on blue  b) No — the sprites set each other going  c) Dialogue and turn-taking — one sprite waking the next speaker  d) Kuwait Grade 4 Digital Explorer Term 1, Unit 2 Lesson 4
**Answer: c**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Do messages need to match?**
a) No — the sprites set each other going  b) Kuwait Grade 4 Digital Explorer Term 1, Unit 2 Lesson 4  c) The Pop block and recorded sound  d) Yes — by colour; red sent is not heard by a script waiting on blue
**Answer: d**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who acts in a message exchange — the reader?**
a) No — the sprites set each other going  b) Kuwait Grade 4 Digital Explorer Term 1, Unit 2 Lesson 4  c) The Pop block and recorded sound  d) The pupil's own voice or a real sound, captured with the device
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
a) The Pop block and recorded sound  b) Kuwait Grade 4 Digital Explorer Term 1, Unit 2 Lesson 4  c) The pupil's own voice or a real sound, captured with the device  d) At the moment the script reaches its block
**Answer: b**
