# ScratchJr — Sound and Message Blocks — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name ScratchJr's two sound kinds in this lesson.**
**Answer: The Pop block and recorded sound**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is recorded sound?**
**Answer: The pupil's own voice or a real sound, captured with the device**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a sound land in time?**
**Answer: At the moment the script reaches its block**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a message in ScratchJr?**
**Answer: A coloured signal sent between sprites**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does Send Message do?**
**Answer: Sends a coloured signal out to other sprites**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Start on Message do?**
**Answer: Starts the script of a sprite waiting for that message colour**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is messages' best story use?**
**Answer: Dialogue and turn-taking — one sprite waking the next speaker**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Do messages need to match?**
**Answer: Yes — by colour; red sent is not heard by a script waiting on blue**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who acts in a message exchange — the reader?**
**Answer: No — the sprites set each other going**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 4 Digital Explorer Term 1, Unit 2 Lesson 4**
