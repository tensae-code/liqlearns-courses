# Hands-on — Dijkstra's Algorithm — the Shortest Path

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run the post office
Draw a weighted graph of six places near your school, with walking minutes as weights. Run Dijkstra from school by hand, table and all. Then walk (or map) the route your parents-chain gives to the farthest place and check the minutes feel right.

## Break it with a negative edge
Take the lesson's graph and change one edge weight to −2. Run the algorithm again and find a settled answer that a later route beats. You have found, yourself, why the condition exists.
