# Recursion and Recursive Functions in Python — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a recursive function call?**
**Answer: Itself, on a smaller piece of the problem**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the two parts of every working recursion.**
**Answer: The base case and the recursive case**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the base case?**
**Answer: The smallest piece, answered directly, where calls stop**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Give factorial's two parts.**
**Answer: n! = n × (n−1)!; base 0! = 1**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why are definition and program the same shape?**
**Answer: Recursion defines a thing by a smaller piece of itself**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where do waiting calls wait?**
**Answer: On the call-stack**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is unwinding?**
**Answer: The base case answers, and each waiting call completes in turn**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What fault gives endless calls?**
**Answer: No base case, or one never reached**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the second fault?**
**Answer: A recursive step that does not shrink the problem**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) On what data does recursion do its finest work?**
**Answer: Trees and nested lists — smaller-piece-shaped by nature**
