# Recursion and Recursive Functions in Python — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a recursive function call?** → **Itself, on a smaller piece of the problem**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the two parts of every working recursion.** → **The base case and the recursive case**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the base case?** → **The smallest piece, answered directly, where calls stop**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Give factorial's two parts.** → **n! = n × (n−1)!; base 0! = 1**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why are definition and program the same shape?** → **Recursion defines a thing by a smaller piece of itself**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where do waiting calls wait?** → **On the call-stack**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is unwinding?** → **The base case answers, and each waiting call completes in turn**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What fault gives endless calls?** → **No base case, or one never reached**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the second fault?** → **A recursive step that does not shrink the problem**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) On what data does recursion do its finest work?** → **Trees and nested lists — smaller-piece-shaped by nature**
