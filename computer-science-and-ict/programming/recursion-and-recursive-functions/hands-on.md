# Hands-on — Recursion and Recursive Functions in Python

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Trace the stack** — Trace factorial(4) as a stack drawing: every call, its wait, and the unwinding values.

## Task two
**Guard the fault** — Take a recursive function with its base case removed; write what happens, then restore the guard and prove termination in two sentences.
