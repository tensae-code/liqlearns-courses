# Hands-on — Scratch Block Categories Block by Block

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 5, the block category tables (Motion, Looks, Sound, Sensing, Operators, Control, Events, Variables) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach scratch block categories block by block to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
