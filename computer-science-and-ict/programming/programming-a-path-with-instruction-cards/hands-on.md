# Hands-on — Programming a Path With Instruction Cards

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Program the path** — On a gridded board, lay a card program for the given start and target. Partner runs it exactly. Score: arrival, in how many repairs?

## Task two
**Trap setter** — Add one trap square to the board and swap programs with another pair: their program must route around your trap. Repair until it does.
