# Programming a Path With Instruction Cards — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the program, with cards?**
**Answer: A physical row of instruction cards**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When is the route planned?**
**Answer: Before anything moves — the whole route in cards**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do you do before running?**
**Answer: Predict the arrival**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a surprise during the run mean?**
**Answer: A card is wrong, missing, or out of order**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the character called in French?**
**Answer: Le lutin**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the practice loop.**
**Answer: Plan, run, compare, repair**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) In whose steps does the program count?**
**Answer: The board's steps**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Whose left does the character turn to?**
**Answer: Its own left — not yours**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What grows after each repaired path?**
**Answer: The ambition of the next path**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two traps does practice add?**
**Answer: Further routes, with turns and marked traps (answers vary)**
