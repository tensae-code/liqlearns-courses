# Hands-on — Graphs — Nodes and Edges

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Six coins, one web
Lay six coins on the table as nodes. Use string or pencils as edges to build the friendship graph from the lesson. Then slide the coins into a line without breaking any edge. Say out loud what stayed the same — that sameness is the graph.

## Degree hunt
Draw your own graph of your class or family (eight nodes is plenty). Write each node's degree beside it. Find the hub and find any island. If there is an island, say what one new edge would connect it.
