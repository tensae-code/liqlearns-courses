# Hands-on — Field Width and Formatting Numbers in Printf

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Chapter 10 §10.1.3 Field-width Specifier (formatting integers — %4d right-justified; formatting floating point numbers — %m.nf, rounding) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach field width and formatting numbers in printf to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
