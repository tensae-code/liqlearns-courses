# Hands-on — Variables in LOGO — MAKE, "name and :value

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Jar variables
Take three jars or boxes. Label them with names — size, count, name. Put a number card in each. A partner gives commands in words: 'MAKE size 45' (swap the card), 'PRINT :size' (read the card aloud), 'PRINT size' (read the label aloud — the word, not the number). The two readings side by side teach the colon.

## Predict the print
On paper, work the book's set before touching LOGO: make "size 60, then print :size/2, print :size + 2, print :size - 2. Write your three predictions, then run them in MSW LOGO and mark yourself.
