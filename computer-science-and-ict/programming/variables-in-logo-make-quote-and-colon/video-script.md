# Video script — Variables in LOGO — MAKE, "name and :value

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A variable is a **container with a name**. Watch me fill one.

Type: **make "size 60**.
The quote says: this is the name — do not work it out. Just write on the container: **size**. Inside goes **60**.

Now read it back. Type: **print :size**.
See the colon? The colon means: open the container, take the value. Out comes **60**.

Try the value at work: **print :size / 2** — thirty.
**print :size + 2** — sixty-two. **print :size - 2** — fifty-eight.

Words fit too: **make "name "Hello** — then **print :name** — and out comes: **Hello**.

Quote to write in. Colon to read out. That is a variable.
