# Variables in LOGO — MAKE, "name and :value — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or mark.

**1.** To read a variable's value, write a ______ in front of its name.
**Answer: colon (:).**

**2.** ______ "name "Hello, then print :name, outputs Hello.
**Answer: make.**

**3.** The word Hello is ______ so LOGO takes it as a string, not something to evaluate.
**Answer: quoted.**
