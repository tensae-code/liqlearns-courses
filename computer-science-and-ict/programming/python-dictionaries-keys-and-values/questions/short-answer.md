# Python Dictionaries — Keys and Values — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a dictionary keep?**
**Answer: Pairs — a key and its value, in curly brackets**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is a value reached?**
**Answer: By its key — student["grade"]**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does that differ from a list?**
**Answer: By name, not by position**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a pair added?**
**Answer: Assigning to a new key**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a value changed?**
**Answer: Assigning to its existing key**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name two ways pairs are removed.**
**Answer: del and pop**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What three questions can a dictionary answer about itself?**
**Answer: Its keys, its values, and whether a key is present**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when an absent key is asked for?**
**Answer: An error is raised**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do careful programs do first?**
**Answer: Test that the key is present before asking**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 10 Information Technology Term 2 (Book L1), Unit 1 — Dictionaries**
