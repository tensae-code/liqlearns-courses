# Hands-on — Two Ways to Move a Sprite X Y Method and Step Method

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 4, Allow the user to move a sprite using arrow keys: X–Y Method and Step Method (and Loop Method) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach two ways to move a sprite x y method and step method to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
