# The Boolean Data Type in Pascal — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What values can a boolean hold?**
**Answer: Only true and false**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Whom is the type named for?**
**Answer: George Boole**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How are booleans most often born?**
**Answer: From comparisons — n > 5 works out to true or false**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a boolean variable declared?**
**Answer: var ok: boolean;**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is boolean's job among the types?**
**Answer: It decides — if and loop conditions are made of it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When does and give true?**
**Answer: Only when both sides are true**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When does or give true?**
**Answer: When at least one side is true**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does not do?**
**Answer: It flips the value — not true is false**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Give a compound boolean from the lesson.**
**Answer: age >= 18 and hasTicket**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How many cases has a two-input truth table?**
**Answer: Four — true/true, true/false, false/true, false/false**
