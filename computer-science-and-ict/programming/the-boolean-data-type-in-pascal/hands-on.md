# Hands-on — The Boolean Data Type in Pascal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Truth-table proof** — Build the four-row truth tables for and and or from the definitions, then prove not (a and b) differs from (not a) and (not b) on one row.

## Task two
**Condition writing** — Write boolean expressions for: a pupil may enter (over 12 and accompanied, or over 16); a shop is open (weekday and not holiday).
