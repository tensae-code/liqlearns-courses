# Hands-on — Search and Sort Algorithms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Characterise by hand', 'Search a shuffled list of 12 name cards for one name, one by one, counting your checks. Then sort the cards and search again by halving. Record both counts and say what the sort bought.')

## Task two
('Lose nothing', 'Sort 8 numbered cards by any method you invent. Before and after, count the cards and add their values — both must match. Write the two checks as the sort's contract.')
