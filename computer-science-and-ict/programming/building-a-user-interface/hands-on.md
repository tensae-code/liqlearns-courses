# Hands-on — Building a User Interface

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Furnish a window', 'Build a window with a button and two text boxes (name and age) that greets the named person on the click. Write the handler first on paper, then in code.')

## Task two
('Solve in a window', 'Build the quadratic solver: three coefficient boxes, one button, the answer shown in the window. Test it on an equation with two known roots and one with none.')
