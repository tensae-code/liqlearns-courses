# Mathematical Functions in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the mathematical function block?**
**Answer: A single operator block: a number goes in, a dropdown chooses the function, and one answer is reported**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does abs return?**
**Answer: The value without its sign — its absolute value**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do floor and ceiling do?**
**Answer: Round a number down and up to a whole number**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does sqrt return?**
**Answer: The number's square root**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which trigonometric functions does the block offer?**
**Answer: Sin, cos and tan**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where can a reporter block like this one sit?**
**Answer: Inside a say block, a set-variable block, or a bigger calculation**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does sqrt build in a game?**
**Answer: The true distance between sprites, from two squared distances**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does abs measure between two scores?**
**Answer: How far apart they are, whichever is bigger**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does floor do for a game timer?**
**Answer: Turns a decimal timer into whole seconds for the display**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) With which lesson does this one form the operators' advanced family?**
**Answer: String Functions in Scratch — text in one, number in the other**
