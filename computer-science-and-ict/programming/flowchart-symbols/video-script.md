# Video script — Flowchart symbols

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **Flowchart symbols**.

**the terminator** — the symbol marking the starting or ending point, holding the word Start or End.

**the decision diamond** — the branching point — different decisions emerge from different points of the diamond.

**the input/output symbol** — a parallelogram — material or information entering or leaving the system.

**the connector** — a small circle — the flow continues at a matching symbol with the same letter.
