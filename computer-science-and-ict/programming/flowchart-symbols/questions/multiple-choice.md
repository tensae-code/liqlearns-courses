# Flowchart symbols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the terminator?
a) the symbol marking the starting or ending point, holding the word Start or End  b) the branching point — different decisions emerge from different points of the diamond  c) a parallelogram — material or information entering or leaving the system  d) a small circle — the flow continues at a matching symbol with the same letter
**Answer: a) the symbol marking the starting or ending point, holding the word Start or End**

**2.** What is the decision diamond?
a) the symbol marking the starting or ending point, holding the word Start or End  b) a parallelogram — material or information entering or leaving the system  c) a small circle — the flow continues at a matching symbol with the same letter  d) the branching point — different decisions emerge from different points of the diamond
**Answer: d) the branching point — different decisions emerge from different points of the diamond**

**3.** What is the input/output symbol?
a) the branching point — different decisions emerge from different points of the diamond  b) a small circle — the flow continues at a matching symbol with the same letter  c) a parallelogram — material or information entering or leaving the system  d) the symbol marking the starting or ending point, holding the word Start or End
**Answer: c) a parallelogram — material or information entering or leaving the system**

**4.** What is the connector?
a) a parallelogram — material or information entering or leaving the system  b) a small circle — the flow continues at a matching symbol with the same letter  c) the symbol marking the starting or ending point, holding the word Start or End  d) the branching point — different decisions emerge from different points of the diamond
**Answer: b) a small circle — the flow continues at a matching symbol with the same letter**

**5.** A whole sub-process inside a larger flowchart is drawn as —
a) a terminator  b) a process rectangle or subroutine symbol  c) a flow line  d) a connector
**Answer: b) a process rectangle or subroutine symbol**

**6.** Arrows showing sequence and direction are —
a) connectors  b) flow lines  c) terminators  d) parallelograms
**Answer: b) flow lines**

**7.** Algorithms need flowcharts and pseudocode because —
a) natural language is too imprecise for programming  b) computers like pictures  c) books require them  d) they are shorter to print
**Answer: a) natural language is too imprecise for programming** — precision is the reason.

**8.** In a flowchart, arrows show —
a) decoration  b) the direction of information and control flow  c) the author's name  d) page numbers
**Answer: b) the direction of information and control flow** — between the boxes.

**9.** The symbol set pictured at Grade 10 includes —
a) predefined process  b) a printer symbol  c) a battery symbol  d) a sound symbol
**Answer: a) predefined process** — with terminator, input, process, decision, output and flow line.
