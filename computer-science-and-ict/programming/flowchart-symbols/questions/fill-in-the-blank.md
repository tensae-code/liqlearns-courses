# Flowchart symbols — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is the branching point — different decisions emerge from different points of the diamond. (Name the term.)
**Answer: the decision diamond**

**2.** _____ is a parallelogram — material or information entering or leaving the system. (Name the term.)
**Answer: the input/output symbol**

**3.** _____ is a small circle — the flow continues at a matching symbol with the same letter. (Name the term.)
**Answer: the connector**
