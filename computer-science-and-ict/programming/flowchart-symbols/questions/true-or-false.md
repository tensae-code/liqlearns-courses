# Flowchart symbols — tf

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** True or false: the terminator — the symbol marking the starting or ending point, holding the word Start or End.
**Answer: True**

**2.** True or false: the decision diamond — the branching point — different decisions emerge from different points of the diamond.
**Answer: True**

**3.** True or false: the input/output symbol is the opposite: it has nothing to do with a parallelogram — material or information entering or leaving the system.
**Answer: False** — the input/output symbol is about a parallelogram — material or information entering or leaving the system.
