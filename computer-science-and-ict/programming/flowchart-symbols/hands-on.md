# Hands-on — Flowchart symbols

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Symbol cards
Make seven cards, symbol on the front, job on the back. Drill a partner until no card is missed.

## Read a chart
Take any flowchart in this unit and name every symbol in it, in flow order.


## Symbol spotting
Draw the pictured symbol set from memory, then check against Picture 15724. Beside each symbol, write one step from making tea that belongs in it.
