# Hands-on — Detecting Errors in Pseudocode and Flowcharts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Trace the trap', 'Trace this plan with score = 40: IF score ≥ 50 THEN print Pass ENDIF; print Fail. Say what the trace exposes and fix the flowchart so each outcome prints once.')

## Task two
('Plant and hunt', 'Draw a flowchart with exactly one planted logic fault (a loop without an exit, or a missing No path). Swap with a partner: each traces the other's and names the fault and its fix.')
