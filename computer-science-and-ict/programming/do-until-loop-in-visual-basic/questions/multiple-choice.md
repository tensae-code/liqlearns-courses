# DO UNTIL Loop in Visual Basic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) When does a DO..UNTIL loop repeat?**
a) While its condition is false  b) The moment its condition becomes true  c) At the top, before each run of the block  d) The block never runs
**Answer: a) While its condition is false**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When does it stop?**
a) At the top, before each run of the block  b) The moment its condition becomes true  c) The block never runs  d) By naming its stopping truth — 'this repeats until …'
**Answer: b) The moment its condition becomes true**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the test stand?**
a) The block never runs  b) By naming its stopping truth — 'this repeats until …'  c) At the top, before each run of the block  d) Its condition is true, and stops when it turns false
**Answer: c) At the top, before each run of the block**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens if the condition is already true at the start?**
a) By naming its stopping truth — 'this repeats until …'  b) Its condition is true, and stops when it turns false  c) By negating the condition — While count <= 10 is Until count > 10  d) The block never runs
**Answer: d) The block never runs**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How do you read an UNTIL loop?**
a) By naming its stopping truth — 'this repeats until …'  b) Its condition is true, and stops when it turns false  c) By negating the condition — While count <= 10 is Until count > 10  d) When the loop hunts until a finishing state — until found, until the total reaches the target
**Answer: a) By naming its stopping truth — 'this repeats until …'**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) State the mirror: DO..WHILE repeats while…**
a) By negating the condition — While count <= 10 is Until count > 10  b) Its condition is true, and stops when it turns false  c) When the loop hunts until a finishing state — until found, until the total reaches the target  d) FOR..NEXT, DO..WHILE, DO..UNTIL
**Answer: b) Its condition is true, and stops when it turns false**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How can the same loop be written in the other form?**
a) When the loop hunts until a finishing state — until found, until the total reaches the target  b) FOR..NEXT, DO..WHILE, DO..UNTIL  c) By negating the condition — While count <= 10 is Until count > 10  d) Do Until condition … Loop
**Answer: c) By negating the condition — While count <= 10 is Until count > 10**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When does UNTIL read more naturally?**
a) FOR..NEXT, DO..WHILE, DO..UNTIL  b) Do Until condition … Loop  c) While its condition is false  d) When the loop hunts until a finishing state — until found, until the total reaches the target
**Answer: d) When the loop hunts until a finishing state — until found, until the total reaches the target**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name Libya's three repetition shapes, in the book's order.**
a) FOR..NEXT, DO..WHILE, DO..UNTIL  b) Do Until condition … Loop  c) While its condition is false  d) The moment its condition becomes true
**Answer: a) FOR..NEXT, DO..WHILE, DO..UNTIL**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Write the shape of a DO..UNTIL loop.**
a) While its condition is false  b) Do Until condition … Loop  c) The moment its condition becomes true  d) At the top, before each run of the block
**Answer: b) Do Until condition … Loop**
