# DO UNTIL Loop in Visual Basic — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) When does a DO..UNTIL loop repeat?**
**Answer: While its condition is false**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When does it stop?**
**Answer: The moment its condition becomes true**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the test stand?**
**Answer: At the top, before each run of the block**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens if the condition is already true at the start?**
**Answer: The block never runs**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How do you read an UNTIL loop?**
**Answer: By naming its stopping truth — 'this repeats until …'**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) State the mirror: DO..WHILE repeats while…**
**Answer: Its condition is true, and stops when it turns false**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How can the same loop be written in the other form?**
**Answer: By negating the condition — While count <= 10 is Until count > 10**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When does UNTIL read more naturally?**
**Answer: When the loop hunts until a finishing state — until found, until the total reaches the target**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name Libya's three repetition shapes, in the book's order.**
**Answer: FOR..NEXT, DO..WHILE, DO..UNTIL**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Write the shape of a DO..UNTIL loop.**
**Answer: Do Until condition … Loop**
