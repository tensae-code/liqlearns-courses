## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **DO..UNTIL — repeat until the condition turns true** — A **DO..UNTIL** loop repeats its block **while its condition is false** and stops **the moment the condition becomes true**: `Do Until count > 10 … Loop`. The test stands at the **top**, before each run of the block, so if the condition is already true at the start, the block **never runs**. Read an UNTIL loop by naming its stopping truth: 'this repeats until the total reaches 100' — the sentence the loop is built from.
2. **UNTIL against WHILE — the mirror pair** — **DO..WHILE** repeats while its condition is **true** and stops when it turns **false**; **DO..UNTIL** repeats while its condition is **false** and stops when it turns **true**. The same loop can be written either way by **negating the condition** — While count <= 10 is Until count > 10. Choose the form whose condition reads as the natural sentence for the job: a loop that hunts **until found** reads in UNTIL; a loop that works **while work remains** reads in WHILE. Libya closes its repetition teaching with UNTIL, after FOR..NEXT and DO..WHILE, so all three shapes stand together.
