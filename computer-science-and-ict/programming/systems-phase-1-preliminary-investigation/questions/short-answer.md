# Systems Phase 1 — Preliminary Investigation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the preliminary investigation?**
**Answer: A short first look at the present system and its troubles, before any promise is made**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is its first question?**
**Answer: What is the problem — stated plainly, in the users' own experience**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give Fiji's example of a plain problem statement.**
**Answer: 'Stock figures are three weeks late' — not 'we need a computer'**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is its second question?**
**Answer: Is a new system feasible — buildable and runnable with the money, people, skills and time available?**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is its third question?**
**Answer: Is it worth it — would the gains outweigh the whole cost?**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Phase 1 end with?**
**Answer: A decision and a report — go ahead, change the idea, or stop**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why is stopping a success of this phase?**
**Answer: It saves an organisation from building the wrong system**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a 'go' hand to analysis?**
**Answer: The problem stated, the present system sketched, and the limits written down**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Is Phase 1 a full study?**
**Answer: No — deliberately a look, not a study**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does Phase 1 do for Viti's complaint?**
**Answer: Turns 'the stock cards are always wrong' into a bounded question the cycle can work on**
