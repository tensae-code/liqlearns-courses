# ScratchJr — First Steps and the Wait Block — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the stage in ScratchJr?**
**Answer: The screen where the story happens**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a sprite?**
**Answer: A character the program moves**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are blocks?**
**Answer: Snap-together commands each sprite obeys**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a block read?**
**Answer: By its colour family, joined left to right into the sprite's script**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the unit review the basics first?**
**Answer: Every later ScratchJr lesson speaks this language without stopping**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the Wait block do?**
**Answer: Makes a sprite pause for the count written in it, before its next block**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does waiting give a story?**
**Answer: Timing — sprites act in turn instead of in a tangle**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Give an example of Wait at work.**
**Answer: A sprite crosses, waits, then speaks (any sensible example)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Is a pause an accident in the program?**
**Answer: No — the pause is part of the program**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 4 Digital Explorer Term 1, Unit 2 Lesson 1**
