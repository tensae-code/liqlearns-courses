# Hands-on — The Real and float Types in Pascal and C++

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Precision probe** — Write 1/3, 2/3 and their sum in float-style (7 digits) and double-style (15 digits) arithmetic on paper and compare the sums with 1.

## Task two
**Type choice** — For five data (a temperature, a national debt, a pupil's height in metres, π to school accuracy, a game score with decimals) choose real/float or double and defend each.
