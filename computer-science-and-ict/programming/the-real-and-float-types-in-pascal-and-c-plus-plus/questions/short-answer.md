# The Real and float Types in Pascal and C++ — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are a stored real's two parts?**
**Answer: The mantissa (significant digits) and the exponent (the power placing the point)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which types are the school sizes?**
**Answer: Pascal's real and C++'s float**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does C++'s double add?**
**Answer: Roughly twice the mantissa bits — double precision: more exact digits**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do more exponent bits buy?**
**Answer: Range — larger and smaller magnitudes**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is true of any stored real size?**
**Answer: Infinitely many true values share each stored neighbour — storage is approximate**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How should reals be compared?**
**Answer: By a small difference — never by exact equality**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is accumulation?**
**Answer: The drift of error across many small additions**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why format real output?**
**Answer: Digits beyond the type's precision are noise the type never stored**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When does double earn its cost?**
**Answer: Money and long computations — where the wider mantissa argues**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which later lessons grow from this section?**
**Answer: Grade 12's chapters on calculation errors**
