# Hands-on — BNF Metalanguage — Describing a Language's Syntax

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Unfold a name** — From the <identifier> production, unfold three legal identifiers step by step, showing each replacement.

## Task two
**Write a production** — Write BNF productions for <unsigned-integer> and for a Pascal-style <sign>, then unfold 407 and −25 from them.
