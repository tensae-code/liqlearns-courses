# Hands-on — C Floating Point Types and Char

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Chapter 9 §9.5.2 Data Types for Floating Point Numbers (float, double, long double; mantissa and exponent; precision table; cancellation error, arithmetic underflow and overflow) and §9.5.3 Data Type for Characters — char (characters stored as ASCII codes; adding '2' and '6' gives 104) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach c floating point types and char to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
