# Evaluating a Program — Simplicity, Flexibility, Reliability, Speed — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is simplicity in a program?**
**Answer: A solution no more tangled than its problem — clear structure, no needless cleverness**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is flexibility?**
**Answer: Changes can be made in one place — constants and structures built for change**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give a change life surely asks.**
**Answer: A new limit, a new case, a new tax rate (any one)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How do the two criteria relate?**
**Answer: The simple program is usually the one that bends without breaking**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches the criteria?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 12 Anaptyxi Efarmogon, Chapter 14**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is reliability?**
**Answer: The right answer under the conditions the program will truly meet**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Under which conditions only is reliability NOT proved?**
**Answer: The demonstration's kind ones**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is speed judged by?**
**Answer: Its algorithm's growth against the time its job allows**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How many criteria judge the finished program?**
**Answer: Four — simplicity, flexibility, reliability, speed**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) A program failing one criterion is what?**
**Answer: A draft with a name to fix**
