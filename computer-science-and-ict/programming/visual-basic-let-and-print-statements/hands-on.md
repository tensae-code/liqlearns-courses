# Hands-on — Visual Basic — LET and PRINT Statements

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Assign and show', 'Write LET lines putting your name, your age and a total into three variables, then PRINT each one. Predict every output before running.')

## Task two
('Spot the error', 'Write three broken LET lines — variable on the right, assignment to a constant, equality used as assignment — and write what is wrong with each.')
