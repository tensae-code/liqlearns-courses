# Binary Search — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does binary search demand of the list?**
a) It must already be in order  b) The middle item  c) The right half — whole  d) When the field shrinks to nothing
**Answer: a) It must already be in order**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is checked first?**
a) The right half — whole  b) The middle item  c) When the field shrinks to nothing  d) About 10 — each check halves the field
**Answer: b) The middle item**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The target is smaller than the middle. What is discarded?**
a) When the field shrinks to nothing  b) About 10 — each check halves the field  c) The right half — whole  d) low and high — the ends of the surviving field
**Answer: c) The right half — whole**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When does the hunt answer 'not found'?**
a) About 10 — each check halves the field  b) low and high — the ends of the surviving field  c) (low + high) ÷ 2, as a whole number  d) When the field shrinks to nothing
**Answer: d) When the field shrinks to nothing**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) About how many checks does 1,000 items need at most?**
a) About 10 — each check halves the field  b) low and high — the ends of the surviving field  c) (low + high) ÷ 2, as a whole number  d) high = middle − 1
**Answer: a) About 10 — each check halves the field**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What two values track the field?**
a) (low + high) ÷ 2, as a whole number  b) low and high — the ends of the surviving field  c) high = middle − 1  d) low = middle + 1
**Answer: b) low and high — the ends of the surviving field**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is middle worked out?**
a) high = middle − 1  b) low = middle + 1  c) (low + high) ÷ 2, as a whole number  d) It can throw away the half that holds the target — the speed is bought by the order it demands
**Answer: c) (low + high) ÷ 2, as a whole number**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Target smaller: how does high move?**
a) low = middle + 1  b) It can throw away the half that holds the target — the speed is bought by the order it demands  c) It must already be in order  d) high = middle − 1
**Answer: d) high = middle − 1**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Target larger: how does low move?**
a) low = middle + 1  b) It can throw away the half that holds the target — the speed is bought by the order it demands  c) It must already be in order  d) The middle item
**Answer: a) low = middle + 1**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What happens on an unordered list?**
a) It must already be in order  b) It can throw away the half that holds the target — the speed is bought by the order it demands  c) The middle item  d) The right half — whole
**Answer: b) It can throw away the half that holds the target — the speed is bought by the order it demands**
