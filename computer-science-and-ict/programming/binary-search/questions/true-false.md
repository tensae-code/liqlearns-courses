# Binary Search — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does binary search demand of the list?" is: It must already be in order**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is checked first?" is: The right half — whole**
**Answer: False** — The middle item

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "The target is smaller than the middle. What is discarded?" is: The right half — whole**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "When does the hunt answer 'not found'?" is: About 10 — each check halves the field**
**Answer: False** — When the field shrinks to nothing

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "About how many checks does 1,000 items need at most?" is: About 10 — each check halves the field**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What two values track the field?" is: (low + high) ÷ 2, as a whole number**
**Answer: False** — low and high — the ends of the surviving field

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is middle worked out?" is: (low + high) ÷ 2, as a whole number**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Target smaller: how does high move?" is: low = middle + 1**
**Answer: False** — high = middle − 1

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Target larger: how does low move?" is: low = middle + 1**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens on an unordered list?" is: It must already be in order**
**Answer: False** — It can throw away the half that holds the target — the speed is bought by the order it demands
