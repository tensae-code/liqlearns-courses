# Hands-on — Binary Search

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Halve it', 'Binary-search the ordered list 3, 7, 9, 14, 21, 25, 30 for 21, writing low, middle and high at every step. Then count linear search's checks for the same target.')

## Task two
('Break it on purpose', 'Run binary search's steps on the unordered list 14, 3, 25, 7, 30, 9, 21 for target 7. Record the step where the target's half is thrown away.')
