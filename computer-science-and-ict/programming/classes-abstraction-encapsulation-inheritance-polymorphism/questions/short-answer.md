# Classes — Abstraction, Encapsulation, Inheritance, Polymorphism — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is abstraction?**
**Answer: Keeping only the properties and actions the problem needs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Give abstraction at work.**
**Answer: A pupil-class holds name and marks, not shoe size**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is encapsulation?**
**Answer: Sealing the data inside the object, reached only through its own methods**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What rule can encapsulation guard?**
**Answer: A mark can never exceed 100 — it cannot be bypassed from outside**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches them?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 12 supplementary material, Unit 4**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is inheritance?**
**Answer: A descendant class receiving an ancestor's properties and methods, adding or sharpening its own**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does inheritance save?**
**Answer: Rewriting — one design, a family of refinements**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is polymorphism?**
**Answer: One message calling each class's own behaviour**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Give the 'draw yourself' example.**
**Answer: It draws a circle in the circle class and a square in the square class, from one message**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does the unit show the family?**
**Answer: Class diagrams — ancestor above descendant**
