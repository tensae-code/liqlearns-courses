Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Rewriting — one design, a family of refinements** — as the answer to: What is inheritance?

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Rewriting — one design, a family of refinements**
