# The Boolean and bool Types in Pascal and C++ — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What values do boolean and bool hold?**
**Answer: true and false — in both languages**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are booleans mainly born from?**
**Answer: Comparisons**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is C++'s sign for comparison-equality?**
**Answer: == — double; single = is assignment (the most punished confusion)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Map the logical operations across.**
**Answer: Pascal and/or/not = C++ &&, ||, !**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the course's refrain here?**
**Answer: Same algebra, two notations**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) In C++, what counts as true in a condition?**
**Answer: Any non-zero value; 0 counts as false**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does if (x = 5) do in C++?**
**Answer: It assigns 5 to x and reads as true — a bug hiding in plain sight**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does Pascal require of a condition?**
**Answer: It must be boolean — the mixture is forbidden**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the section's rule?**
**Answer: Write booleans as booleans in both languages**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Is the loose reading a style to adopt?**
**Answer: No — a trap to recognise in others' code**
