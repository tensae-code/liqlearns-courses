# Hands-on — The With Instruction for Records in Pascal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Expansion** — Expand a given with block into full prefixed assignments, line by line, proving the shorthand changes nothing.

## Task two
**Trap construction** — Write a fragment where a global 'total' and a record field 'total' collide inside with, say what the fragment actually does, and repair it.
