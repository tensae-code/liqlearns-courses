# Hands-on — Declaring Variables in Pascal and C++

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Census reading** — Take a Pascal program and list its var section as a census table: name, type, first assigned where.

## Task two
**Placement rewrite** — Rewrite a small C++ program's scattered declarations into one gathered block at the top — and note what the for-loop declaration refuses to move, and why.
