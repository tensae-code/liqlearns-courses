# True and False Judgements — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A sentence that says something that can be checked is called a ____.**
**Answer: judgement**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A judgement that matches the world is ____.**
**Answer: true**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A judgement that does not match the world is ____.**
**Answer: false**
