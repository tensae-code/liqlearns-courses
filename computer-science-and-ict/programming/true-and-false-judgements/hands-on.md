# Hands-on — True and False Judgements

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## True or false walk
Your teacher reads judgements about the room: 'There are four walls.' 'The door is made of water.' For each one, look first — then hold up a hand for true, or shake your head for false.

## Make one of each
Make one true judgement about something in the room, and one false judgement. A friend must check both against the world and name which is which.
