# Video script — True and False Judgements

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A judgement is a sentence that says something.

The sun is shining. A cat has four legs.

Some judgements are true.

They match the world.

Some judgements are false.

They do not match the world.

Do not guess. Look and check.

Then say: true — or false.
