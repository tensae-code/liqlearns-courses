# Hands-on — Binary Trees

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Draw and count** — Draw a binary tree with seven nodes, two of them leaves' parents with only one child. Mark every leaf and every empty subtree.

## Task two
**Array it** — Write your tree into an array by the 2i / 2i+1 rule. Which indexes stay empty, and what does that tell you about its shape?
