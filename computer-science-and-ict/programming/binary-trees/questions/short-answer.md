# Binary Trees — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How many children may a node of a binary tree have?**
**Answer: At most two**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the two child places called?**
**Answer: Left child and right child**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Is a node with only a left child the same as one with only a right child?**
**Answer: No — the places have names, so the trees differ**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a leaf?**
**Answer: A node with no children**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is an empty subtree?**
**Answer: A child place left empty — it still counts in the structure**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a node store in the linked representation?**
**Answer: Its value, and references to its left and right children**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) In the array representation, where do node i's children sit?**
**Answer: At indexes 2i and 2i + 1, with the root at index 1**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) For which trees is the array way neat?**
**Answer: Complete trees — it wastes space on crooked ones**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a tree's height?**
**Answer: The longest path from the root to a leaf**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does height matter?**
**Answer: It decides how much work a walk down the tree can cost**
