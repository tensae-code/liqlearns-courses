# Moving Enemies — and a Chasing Enemy — in a Maze Game — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Explain how the way the player must play changes from the patrol to the chaser.**
**Answer:** A sound answer: against the patrol you learn the route and time your crossing; against the random enemy you watch the whole maze; against the chaser you cannot settle anywhere — you must keep moving because it keeps coming at you.

**2. Why introduce the enemies in the book's order rather than starting with the chaser?**
**Answer:** A sound answer: the order is a difficulty ladder — players learn to read movement against the predictable patrol first, and only then face randomness and hunting.
