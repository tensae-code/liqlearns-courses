# Moving Enemies — and a Chasing Enemy — in a Maze Game — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A patrol enemy moves —**
a) Between two points, back and forth  b) Only once  c) Towards the player always
**Answer: a) Between two points, back and forth** — A fixed path, beaten by timing.

**2. A random enemy is hard because —**
a) It is invisible  b) There is no pattern to learn  c) It moves the walls
**Answer: b) There is no pattern to learn** — Unpredictable places demand watching everywhere.

**3. The smart enemy —**
a) Sleeps  b) Chases the player sprite  c) Guards only the goal
**Answer: b) Chases the player sprite** — It steers toward the player again and again.

**4. The finished game ends when —**
a) An enemy touches the player (or the player touches a wall)  b) The music ends  c) A minute passes unused
**Answer: a) An enemy touches the player (or the player touches a wall)** — Enemy and wall touches end the game.
