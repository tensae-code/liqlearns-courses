# Operator Precedence and Associativity in Python — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does precedence rank?**
**Answer: The operators — who is worked out first**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Give the top of the ladder.**
**Answer: Brackets, then powers, then × and ÷, then + and −**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is 2 + 3 * 4 equal to 14?**
**Answer: Multiplication outranks addition**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is an expression worked in reading order?**
**Answer: No — by precedence**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the standing advice?**
**Answer: Use brackets when order matters to a reader**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What settles equal precedence?**
**Answer: Associativity — the direction of working**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which way do most operators associate?**
**Answer: Left to right**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is 10 - 4 - 3, and why?**
**Answer: 3 — (10−4)−3 by left associativity**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which operator associates right to left?**
**Answer: The power operator (and assignment)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is 2 ** 3 ** 2?**
**Answer: 512 — 2^(3^2), not 64**
