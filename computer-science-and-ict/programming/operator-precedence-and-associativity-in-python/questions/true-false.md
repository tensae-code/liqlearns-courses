# Operator Precedence and Associativity in Python — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The operators — who is worked out first**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The operators — who is worked out first** — as the answer to: Give the top of the ladder.
**Answer: False — Brackets, then powers, then × and ÷, then + and −**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Multiplication outranks addition**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Multiplication outranks addition** — as the answer to: Is an expression worked in reading order?
**Answer: False — No — by precedence**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Use brackets when order matters to a reader**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Use brackets when order matters to a reader** — as the answer to: What settles equal precedence?
**Answer: False — Associativity — the direction of working**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Left to right**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Left to right** — as the answer to: What is 10 - 4 - 3, and why?
**Answer: False — 3 — (10−4)−3 by left associativity**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The power operator (and assignment)**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The power operator (and assignment)** — as the answer to: What is 2 ** 3 ** 2?
**Answer: False — 512 — 2^(3^2), not 64**
