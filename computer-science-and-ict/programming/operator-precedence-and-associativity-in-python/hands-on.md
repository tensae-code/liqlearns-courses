# Hands-on — Operator Precedence and Associativity in Python

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Evaluate aloud** — Evaluate five mixed expressions step by step, naming the rule that fires at each step.

## Task two
**Bracket kindness** — Take three expressions that are correct but hard to read; add brackets that change nothing but the reader's certainty, and prove the values are unchanged.
