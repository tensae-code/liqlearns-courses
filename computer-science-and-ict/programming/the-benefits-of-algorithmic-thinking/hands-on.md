# Hands-on — The Benefits of Algorithmic Thinking

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 4 §4.4.1 Benefits of Algorithmic Thinking (decomposition, abstraction and generalisation, pattern recognition, algorithmic design, confidence in decision making) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach the benefits of algorithmic thinking to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
