# Hands-on — The General Process of Data Processing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map a real journey
Take the Grade 9 results question (or your class's sports-day scores). Write the five stages as five boxes and write, in each, the actual thing that would happen for your question — real sources, real mess, real chart, real decision.

## Break the chain
For the same question, write one sentence for each stage: 'If this stage were done badly, the damage later would be…' Keep the five sentences — they are the chain of dependencies in your own words.
