# The General Process of Data Processing — short answer

Answer each question in a few sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Walk the question 'Why did Grade 9 results fall this year?' through all five stages in five sentences.**
**Answer:** Purpose: the head teacher needs the causes, so marks by subject, class and term are the data wanted. Collect: gather the marks from registers and the clerk's records for this year and last. Organise and clean: enter them in one table, fix format differences, fill or flag missing marks. Analyse and present: compare averages by subject and term, chart the falls. Apply and report: the report states where results fell and the likely causes, and the school acts on it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A team skips cleaning and goes straight from collection to analysis. Predict the failure in two sentences.**
**Answer:** The analysis will be built on duplicates, mixed formats and missing entries, so its totals and averages will be wrong in unknown places. Worse, the wrongness will be invisible: the charts will look exact while quietly misreporting the school.
