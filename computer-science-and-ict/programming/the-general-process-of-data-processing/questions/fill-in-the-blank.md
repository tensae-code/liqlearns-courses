# The General Process of Data Processing — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Before collection begins, the ____ decides what data is worth having.**
**Answer: purpose (the question)** — No question, no sensible collection.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Data that has been organised and cleaned is called ____ data.**
**Answer: ready** — Ready = complete enough, consistent, in a workable shape.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The process is a chain of ____: each stage depends on the one before.**
**Answer: dependencies** — Damage travels down the chain.
