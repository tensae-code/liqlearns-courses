# Hands-on — Compound Instructions — begin and end in Pascal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Border drawing** — Take three Pascal fragments with ifs and loops and draw the border of every governed instruction; state what runs unconditionally in each.

## Task two
**Wrap the work** — Rewrite a loop whose body must read two values, double both and print both — first wrongly without compound, then rightly with begin…end.
