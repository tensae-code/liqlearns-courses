# Natural and Artificial Languages — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a natural language?**
**Answer: A language that grew with its people — like Greek or English**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name its three marks.**
**Answer: Enormous vocabulary, grammar full of history, ambiguity everywhere**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What carries a natural sentence's sense?**
**Answer: Context, tone and shared life**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why can't a computer simply be told in one?**
**Answer: An ambiguous instruction cannot be obeyed — guessing is not obeying**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 12 Anaptyxi Efarmogon, Chapter 6**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is an artificial language?**
**Answer: Built on purpose, with a small fixed vocabulary and exact rules**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two more artificial languages besides programming languages.**
**Answer: Mathematics' notation and the sign systems of science**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does every artificial sentence mean?**
**Answer: One thing — checkable by its rules alone**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What property lets a machine execute it?**
**Answer: Exactness — translation and execution without judgement**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) ΓΛΩΣΣΑ stands on which side?**
**Answer: Artificial — built for the classroom and the exam**
