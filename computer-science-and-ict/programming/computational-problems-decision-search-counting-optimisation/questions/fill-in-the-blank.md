# Computational problems — decision, search, counting, optimisation — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is a problem whose answer is Yes or No. (Name the term.)
**Answer: a decision problem**

**2.** _____ is a problem whose answer is the number of solutions to a search problem. (Name the term.)
**Answer: a counting problem**

**3.** _____ is a problem whose solution is the best possible — such as the fastest route. (Name the term.)
**Answer: an optimisation problem**
