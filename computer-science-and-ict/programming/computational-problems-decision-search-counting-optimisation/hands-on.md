# Hands-on — Computational problems — decision, search, counting, optimisation

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Type hunt
Write eight questions on cards. Sort them into the four types, then swap piles with another group and check their sorting.

## Make it computational
Take a vague question ('a good football team?') and rewrite it until its inputs and correct answer are well-defined — or show why it cannot be.
