# Bucket Sort — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a bucket?**
a) A range that the values are scattered into — such as 0–9, 10–19 for marks  b) By its own size — 37 goes in the 30s bucket  c) No — the value's size names its bucket  d) When ranges are known in advance and values spread across them
**Answer: a) A range that the values are scattered into — such as 0–9, 10–19 for marks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does a value find its bucket?**
a) No — the value's size names its bucket  b) By its own size — 37 goes in the 30s bucket  c) When ranges are known in advance and values spread across them  d) Ages, marks, prices — values with a known range
**Answer: b) By its own size — 37 goes in the 30s bucket**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Do values get compared during scattering?**
a) When ranges are known in advance and values spread across them  b) Ages, marks, prices — values with a known range  c) No — the value's size names its bucket  d) Its few values are put in order — by a simple sort
**Answer: c) No — the value's size names its bucket**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When does scattering work well?**
a) Ages, marks, prices — values with a known range  b) Its few values are put in order — by a simple sort  c) The buckets are small  d) When ranges are known in advance and values spread across them
**Answer: d) When ranges are known in advance and values spread across them**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name data that fits bucket sort.**
a) Ages, marks, prices — values with a known range  b) Its few values are put in order — by a simple sort  c) The buckets are small  d) Gather the buckets in bucket order, one after another
**Answer: a) Ages, marks, prices — values with a known range**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens inside each bucket?**
a) The buckets are small  b) Its few values are put in order — by a simple sort  c) Gather the buckets in bucket order, one after another  d) Bubble sort compares neighbours; bucket sort places values by size
**Answer: b) Its few values are put in order — by a simple sort**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why is even bubble sort quick inside a bucket?**
a) Gather the buckets in bucket order, one after another  b) Bubble sort compares neighbours; bucket sort places values by size  c) The buckets are small  d) A list with no known range
**Answer: c) The buckets are small**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is the sorted list produced?**
a) Bubble sort compares neighbours; bucket sort places values by size  b) A list with no known range  c) A range that the values are scattered into — such as 0–9, 10–19 for marks  d) Gather the buckets in bucket order, one after another
**Answer: d) Gather the buckets in bucket order, one after another**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the contrast with bubble sort?**
a) Bubble sort compares neighbours; bucket sort places values by size  b) A list with no known range  c) A range that the values are scattered into — such as 0–9, 10–19 for marks  d) By its own size — 37 goes in the 30s bucket
**Answer: a) Bubble sort compares neighbours; bucket sort places values by size**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What data does bucket sort not suit?**
a) A range that the values are scattered into — such as 0–9, 10–19 for marks  b) A list with no known range  c) By its own size — 37 goes in the 30s bucket  d) No — the value's size names its bucket
**Answer: b) A list with no known range**
