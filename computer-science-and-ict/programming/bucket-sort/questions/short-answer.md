# Bucket Sort — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a bucket?**
**Answer: A range that the values are scattered into — such as 0–9, 10–19 for marks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does a value find its bucket?**
**Answer: By its own size — 37 goes in the 30s bucket**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Do values get compared during scattering?**
**Answer: No — the value's size names its bucket**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When does scattering work well?**
**Answer: When ranges are known in advance and values spread across them**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name data that fits bucket sort.**
**Answer: Ages, marks, prices — values with a known range**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens inside each bucket?**
**Answer: Its few values are put in order — by a simple sort**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why is even bubble sort quick inside a bucket?**
**Answer: The buckets are small**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is the sorted list produced?**
**Answer: Gather the buckets in bucket order, one after another**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the contrast with bubble sort?**
**Answer: Bubble sort compares neighbours; bucket sort places values by size**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What data does bucket sort not suit?**
**Answer: A list with no known range**
