# Hands-on — Bucket Sort

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Bucket the marks', 'Bucket-sort these marks: 42, 8, 77, 35, 91, 35, 60, 17, 88, 54. Draw the ten buckets, scatter, order each bucket, and gather the sorted list.')

## Task two
('Choose the sort', 'For each list, choose bubble or bucket and defend it: (a) 200 exam marks 0–100; (b) 6 phone numbers with no known range; (c) the ages of everyone in school.')
