# Computational thinking — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is critical thinking, creativity, collaboration, communication — the learning skills. (Name the term.)
**Answer: the four Cs**

**2.** _____ is information literacy, media literacy, technology literacy — the literacy skills. (Name the term.)
**Answer: IMT**

**3.** _____ is flexibility, leadership, initiative, productivity, social skills — the life skills. (Name the term.)
**Answer: FLIPS**
