# Measuring and Comparing Strings — strlen and strcmp — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does strlen return?** → **The count of characters before the null**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does strlen("Bangla") return?** → **6**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does strcmp return for equal strings?** → **0**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a negative strcmp result mean?** → **The first string comes first in dictionary order**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does a == b lie for strings?** → **It compares addresses, not contents**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does strcat do?** → **Appends one string onto another's end**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must the destination have before strcat?** → **Room for the grown length plus the terminator**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does strcpy copy?** → **The string, terminator included**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which header holds the four functions?** → **<string.h>**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Whose job is checking the rooms suffice?** → **The caller's — never the function's**
