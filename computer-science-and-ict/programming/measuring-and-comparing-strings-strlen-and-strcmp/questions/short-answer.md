# Measuring and Comparing Strings — strlen and strcmp — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does strlen return?**
**Answer: The count of characters before the null**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does strlen("Bangla") return?**
**Answer: 6**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does strcmp return for equal strings?**
**Answer: 0**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a negative strcmp result mean?**
**Answer: The first string comes first in dictionary order**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does a == b lie for strings?**
**Answer: It compares addresses, not contents**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does strcat do?**
**Answer: Appends one string onto another's end**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must the destination have before strcat?**
**Answer: Room for the grown length plus the terminator**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does strcpy copy?**
**Answer: The string, terminator included**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which header holds the four functions?**
**Answer: <string.h>**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Whose job is checking the rooms suffice?**
**Answer: The caller's — never the function's**
