# Hands-on — Measuring and Comparing Strings — strlen and strcmp

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Measure and compare** — Write a C program that reads two words and prints the longer one's length and which comes first in dictionary order — strlen and strcmp doing the work.

## Task two
**Join with room** — Declare a destination with room to spare, strcpy a first name into it, strcat a surname, and print the whole — stating the room arithmetic that made it safe.
