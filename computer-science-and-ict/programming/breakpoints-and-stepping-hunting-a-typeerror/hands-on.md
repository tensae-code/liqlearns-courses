# Hands-on — Breakpoints and stepping — hunting a TypeError

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — every activity is done for real, in IDLE or on paper.

## Breakpoint drill
In IDLE, set breakpoints on three different lines and clear two of them. Record the exact clicks for set and for clear.

## Full hunt
Run the complete division hunt with a partner reading the steps aloud: breakpoint on the third print, Go, 50, 0, Step — stop when the error message appears and both of you read it aloud.
