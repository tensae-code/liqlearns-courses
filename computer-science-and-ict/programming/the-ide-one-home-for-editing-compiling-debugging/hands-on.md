# Hands-on — The IDE — one home for editing, compiling, debugging

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Three desks, one desk
Set three corners of the room as editor-desk, compiler-desk, debugger-desk and walk a 'program' between them; then do the same work seated at one 'IDE desk'. Feel the difference the definition names.

## Window hunt
On any school computer with an IDE (or a printed screenshot), find and label: editor, run/build control, debug control.
