# Hands-on — Binary Arithmetic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Columns by hand
Work these fully on paper, then check each in decimal: (a) 1101 + 1011; (b) 1111 + 0001 — watch the carry travel through every column; (c) the two's complement of 0110 in 4 bits, checking that the two add to 0000.

## All four operations
Take 12 and 3. Write both in binary. (a) Subtract 3 from 12 using only addition and a complement — show the discarded carry. (b) Multiply them by shift-and-add, showing both partial products. (c) Divide 12 by 3 by the compare-and-subtract method, writing each quotient bit as you earn it. All three answers must agree with decimal 9, 36 and 4.

## In the simulator
If your school uses TINA or Circuit Wizard, find the adder circuit (the next lesson builds it) or a calculator block. Feed it your three worked examples from the first activity as binary inputs and read the outputs in binary — the circuit and your pencil must agree bit for bit, carries included.
