# Binary Arithmetic — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In a binary column, 1 + 1 = 0 with a ____ of 1.**
**Answer: carry** — The overflow moves one column left as a carried 1.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Two's complement is formed by inverting the bits and adding ____.**
**Answer: 1** — Invert, then add 1 — both steps, in that order.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A − B is computed by an adder as A plus the ____ complement of B.**
**Answer: two's** — Two's complement is the machine's form of the negative number.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) In shift-and-add multiplication, a partial product is made for each ____ bit of the multiplier.**
**Answer: 1** — A 0 bit contributes a row of zeros (skipped); a 1 bit contributes a shifted copy of the number.
