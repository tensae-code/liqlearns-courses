# Hands-on — What Makes a Good Program — Structure

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Judge two programs** — Take two solutions to one problem (yours and a partner's). Score both against the four tests with evidence quoted from the code.

## Task two
**Hunt the hang** — Write a loop that almost ends. Find the input that starves its change, then repair the loop so the second test passes.
