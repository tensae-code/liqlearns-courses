# Hands-on — Writing algorithms — add, decide, loop

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Human loop
Ten pupils hold number cards. One pupil is the counter, one keeps the running sum on the board. Walk model 3 exactly: take, add, increase, check — aloud at every step.

## Branching doorway
Pupils walk through a doorway holding number cards from −5 to 5. The 'algorithm' pupil calls negative, positive or zero by the book's tests as each passes.
