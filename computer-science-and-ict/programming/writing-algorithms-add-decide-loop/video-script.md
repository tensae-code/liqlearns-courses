# Video script — Writing algorithms — add, decide, loop

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three models. One method.

ADD: take, take, add, display.
DECIDE: below zero — negative.
Above zero — positive. Otherwise — zero.
LOOP: sum starts at zero. i starts at one.
Take a number. Add it. i grows.
Check — still under the limit? Loop back.
Finish. Output the sum.
