# Systems Phase 6 — System Maintenance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is system maintenance?**
a) All the work done on a system after implementation — keeping it true, useful and alive  b) Fixes faults the running system reveals  c) A report that totals wrong, or a crash on the last day of the month  d) Adjusts the system to a changed world — a new tax rate, a new demanded form
**Answer: a) All the work done on a system after implementation — keeping it true, useful and alive**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does corrective maintenance do?**
a) A report that totals wrong, or a crash on the last day of the month  b) Fixes faults the running system reveals  c) Adjusts the system to a changed world — a new tax rate, a new demanded form  d) Improves what already works — a faster search, a clearer report
**Answer: b) Fixes faults the running system reveals**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give a corrective example.**
a) Adjusts the system to a changed world — a new tax rate, a new demanded form  b) Improves what already works — a faster search, a clearer report  c) A report that totals wrong, or a crash on the last day of the month  d) Living with the system teaches them what it should have been
**Answer: c) A report that totals wrong, or a crash on the last day of the month**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does adaptive maintenance do?**
a) Improves what already works — a faster search, a clearer report  b) Living with the system teaches them what it should have been  c) Maintenance — weeks of investigation against years of keeping  d) Adjusts the system to a changed world — a new tax rate, a new demanded form
**Answer: d) Adjusts the system to a changed world — a new tax rate, a new demanded form**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does perfective maintenance do?**
a) Improves what already works — a faster search, a clearer report  b) Living with the system teaches them what it should have been  c) Maintenance — weeks of investigation against years of keeping  d) In maintenance, not in its building
**Answer: a) Improves what already works — a faster search, a clearer report**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why do users ask for improvements late?**
a) Maintenance — weeks of investigation against years of keeping  b) Living with the system teaches them what it should have been  c) In maintenance, not in its building  d) A log of what it cannot fix and what the organisation newly needs
**Answer: b) Living with the system teaches them what it should have been**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which phase is the longest?**
a) In maintenance, not in its building  b) A log of what it cannot fix and what the organisation newly needs  c) Maintenance — weeks of investigation against years of keeping  d) That log becomes the complaint opening a new preliminary investigation — for the replacement system
**Answer: c) Maintenance — weeks of investigation against years of keeping**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where is most of a system's whole cost spent?**
a) A log of what it cannot fix and what the organisation newly needs  b) That log becomes the complaint opening a new preliminary investigation — for the replacement system  c) All the work done on a system after implementation — keeping it true, useful and alive  d) In maintenance, not in its building

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does maintenance keep for the future?**
a) A log of what it cannot fix and what the organisation newly needs  b) That log becomes the complaint opening a new preliminary investigation — for the replacement system  c) All the work done on a system after implementation — keeping it true, useful and alive  d) Fixes faults the running system reveals
