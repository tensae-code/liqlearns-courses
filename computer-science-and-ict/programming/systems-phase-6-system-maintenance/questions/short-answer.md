# Systems Phase 6 — System Maintenance — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is system maintenance?**
**Answer: All the work done on a system after implementation — keeping it true, useful and alive**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does corrective maintenance do?**
**Answer: Fixes faults the running system reveals**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give a corrective example.**
**Answer: A report that totals wrong, or a crash on the last day of the month**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does adaptive maintenance do?**
**Answer: Adjusts the system to a changed world — a new tax rate, a new demanded form**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does perfective maintenance do?**
**Answer: Improves what already works — a faster search, a clearer report**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why do users ask for improvements late?**
**Answer: Living with the system teaches them what it should have been**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which phase is the longest?**
**Answer: Maintenance — weeks of investigation against years of keeping**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where is most of a system's whole cost spent?**
**Answer: In maintenance, not in its building**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does maintenance keep for the future?**
**Answer: A log of what it cannot fix and what the organisation newly needs**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does the wheel turn again?**
**Answer: That log becomes the complaint opening a new preliminary investigation — for the replacement system**
