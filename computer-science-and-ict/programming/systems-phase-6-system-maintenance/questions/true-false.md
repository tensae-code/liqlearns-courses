# Systems Phase 6 — System Maintenance — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is system maintenance?" is: All the work done on a system after implementation — keeping it true, useful and alive**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does corrective maintenance do?" is: A report that totals wrong, or a crash on the last day of the month**
**Answer: False** — Fixes faults the running system reveals

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Give a corrective example." is: A report that totals wrong, or a crash on the last day of the month**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does adaptive maintenance do?" is: Improves what already works — a faster search, a clearer report**
**Answer: False** — Adjusts the system to a changed world — a new tax rate, a new demanded form

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does perfective maintenance do?" is: Improves what already works — a faster search, a clearer report**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why do users ask for improvements late?" is: Maintenance — weeks of investigation against years of keeping**
**Answer: False** — Living with the system teaches them what it should have been

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which phase is the longest?" is: Maintenance — weeks of investigation against years of keeping**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where is most of a system's whole cost spent?" is: A log of what it cannot fix and what the organisation newly needs**
**Answer: False** — In maintenance, not in its building

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does maintenance keep for the future?" is: A log of what it cannot fix and what the organisation newly needs**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How does the wheel turn again?" is: All the work done on a system after implementation — keeping it true, useful and alive**
**Answer: False** — That log becomes the complaint opening a new preliminary investigation — for the replacement system
