# Hands-on — Compiler Directives in Pascal and C++

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Directive reading** — Find three directives in book programs (one Pascal switch, #include, #define) and state for each: who obeys it, and what changes without it.

## Task two
**Switch story** — Explain in five lines what {$R+} would have caught in the Grade 7 subrange lesson's calendar example, and why a finished program might still switch it off.
