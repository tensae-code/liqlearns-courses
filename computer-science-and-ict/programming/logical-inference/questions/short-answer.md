# Logical Inference — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is inference?**
**Answer: Drawing a conclusion that the given propositions force**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the starting propositions called?**
**Answer: Premises — accepted as true for the argument**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) By what is an inference judged?**
**Answer: Its shape — not whether its story sounds true**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When is an inference valid?**
**Answer: When true premises cannot leave the conclusion false**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) An invalid inference can still sound what?**
**Answer: Believable — believability is not validity**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Give the invalid shape from the lesson.**
**Answer: From 'some pupils passed' and 'Dedi is a pupil', Dedi's passing does not follow**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How are arguments tested in the chapter?**
**Answer: By replacing content with letters and watching the shape alone**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do the connectives have to do with inference?**
**Answer: Connectives build propositions; inference is what propositions are for**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does inference lead, in computing?**
**Answer: To expert systems and program logic**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 10 SMK Semester 1, Bab 1 D — logical inference (logika inferensi)**
