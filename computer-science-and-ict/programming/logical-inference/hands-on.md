# Hands-on — Logical Inference

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Shape test** — Write four short arguments. Replace their content with letters, and mark each valid or invalid by shape alone — then check your marks against the stories.

## Task two
**Find the hole** — Take a persuasive paragraph (an advertisement, a speech). Extract one argument, write its premises and conclusion, and test whether the conclusion truly follows.
