# Computational Thinking — Designing a Project — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a project design start with?**
**Answer: A problem worth solving, stated in one sentence**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does decomposition do to a project?**
**Answer: It breaks it into parts small enough to build one at a time**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does pattern recognition save?**
**Answer: Repeated parts — one solution can serve every place the pattern appears**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does abstraction decide for each part?**
**Answer: What the part must show to others, and what it may hide inside**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In Armenia's Grade 5 book, what comes first — the design or the building?**
**Answer: The design — computational thinking is used to design the project before building**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What should the written design list?**
**Answer: The parts in build order, what each part needs from the others, and how the result will be checked**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Against what is the finished project checked?**
**Answer: Against the problem it set out to solve**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When should parts be tested?**
**Answer: As they are built — not only at the end**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does a good design say about team work?**
**Answer: Who does what, and what 'finished' looks like for each part**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why write the design down at all?**
**Answer: So the plan can be shared, checked and followed — not held in one head**
