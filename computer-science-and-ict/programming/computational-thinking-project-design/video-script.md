# Video script — Computational Thinking — Designing a Project

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **project design** starts with a problem worth solving, stated in one sentence. **Decomposition** breaks the project into parts small enough to build one at a time; **pattern recognition** finds parts that repeat, so one solution serves many places; **abstraction** decides what each part must show and what it may hide. Armenia's Grade 5 book gives computational thinking a two-lesson project of its own (§13–14): the thinking is the design, and the design comes first.
The design is written down as an **algorithm for the project**: the parts in build order, what each part needs from the others, and how the finished project will be **checked** against the problem it set out to solve. A good design names who does what in a team, what 'finished' looks like for each part, and the order that lets parts be tested as they are built — not only at the end.
