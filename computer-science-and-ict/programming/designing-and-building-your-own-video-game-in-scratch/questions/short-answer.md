# Designing and Building Your Own Video Game in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How is the class organised?**
**Answer: As studios — small game companies with a product to ship**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the apprenticeship game?**
**Answer: The Number Mystery (jeu du Nombre Mystère)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does it play?**
**Answer: The program holds a secret number; the player guesses; it answers higher/lower until the find**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the first steps.**
**Answer: State the rule in one sentence; list what the program must remember; sketch the play**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What must the program remember?**
**Answer: The secret, the guesses, the count**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the pipeline's stages.**
**Answer: Algorithm in natural language, Scratch program, written brief, playtesting, revision**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which game runs in expert mode in the studios?**
**Answer: Nim**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is a game finished?**
**Answer: When players say it works**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What paves the road from idea to players?**
**Answer: Documents, tests and repairs**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of lesson is the project's?**
**Answer: Industrial, not just technical**
