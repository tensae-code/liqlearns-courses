# Hands-on — Designing and Building Your Own Video Game in Scratch

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Build the Mystery Number** — In studios, build the Nombre Mystère in Scratch from the template: rule sentence, remembered things, algorithm, program. Another studio playtests it blind.

## Task two
**Ship your own** — Design your studio's own small game on the same pipeline. Deliverables: brief, program, and a playtest report signed by a rival studio.
