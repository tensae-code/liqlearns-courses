# Hands-on — What a Computer Program Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Spot the programs** — List five programs that ran in your morning. For one of them, write the problem it solves and two of its steps.

## Task two
**From problem to program** — Take 'make a cup of tea for a guest'. Write the algorithm in numbered steps, then say what would change to make it a program a computer could follow.
