# What a Computer Program Is — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a computer program?**
**Answer: An algorithm written in a programming language for the computer to follow**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three links form the chain?**
**Answer: A problem asks, an algorithm plans, a program writes the steps for the computer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) In what language is a program written?**
**Answer: A programming language — one the computer can be made to follow exactly**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the computer contribute of its own?**
**Answer: No understanding — it follows the program exactly**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does this lesson sit in Grade 6's book?**
**Answer: It closes the algorithms theme — after algorithms and control structures**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name two programs outside a desk computer.**
**Answer: A washing machine's cycles and a phone's alarm (any embedded or app programs)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the human's share?**
**Answer: Choose the problem, design the algorithm, write and test the program**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a wrong program worse than none?**
**Answer: The computer follows the wrong steps faithfully, exactly and tirelessly**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two qualities does the machine contribute?**
**Answer: Exactness and tirelessness**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What stays ours, at every program size?**
**Answer: The thinking**
