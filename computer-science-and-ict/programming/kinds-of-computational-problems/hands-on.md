# Hands-on — Kinds of Computational Problems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 3 §3.1 Understanding Computational Problems (input–process–output) and §3.1.1 Characterizing Computational Problems (decision, search, optimization and counting problems; well-defined vs ill-defined — "reduce poverty in Pakistan" as an ill-defined problem) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach kinds of computational problems to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
