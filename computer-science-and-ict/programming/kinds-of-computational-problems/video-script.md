# Video script — Kinds of Computational Problems

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This part of Pakistan (Punjab)'s book teaches **Understanding Computational Problems** — covering: Unit 3 §3.1 Understanding Computational Problems (input–process–output) and §3.1.1 Characterizing Computational Problems (decision, search, optimization and counting problems; well-defined vs ill-defined — "reduce poverty in Pakistan" as an ill-defined problem). The book teaches this as its own named step, in the book's own order, as a skill to be shown — not only named. The learner follows the book's own steps for this part, in order, and practises until the steps can be done without the book open.
**Doing it — Kinds of Computational Problems in practice** — the learner now works the lesson's own thing with a real example of their own, following the book's own steps in order, until the steps can be done without the book open.
