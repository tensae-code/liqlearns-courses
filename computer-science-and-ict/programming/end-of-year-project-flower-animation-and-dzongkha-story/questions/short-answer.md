# End-of-Year Project — Flower Animation and Dzongkha Story — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What three things make up the Class V end-of-year piece?**
**Answer: A Scratch flower animation, a Dzongkha story in Word and the year's folder sent by LAN messenger**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which block family draws the flower?**
**Answer: The pen blocks**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do the pen blocks draw as the sprite moves?**
**Answer: The stem and the petals**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which blocks change the flower as it opens?**
**Answer: The looks blocks**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which blocks repeat the drawing until the flower is complete?**
**Answer: The control blocks**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a flower an honest test of the pen?**
**Answer: Every petal is a planned path drawn by chosen blocks**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) In what language is the year's story typed?**
**Answer: Dzongkha**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What software and fonts does the story need?**
**Answer: Dzongkha keyboard software and Dzongkha fonts**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where is the story saved?**
**Answer: In the pupil's own folder**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is the folder handed to the teacher?**
**Answer: As a LAN-messenger attachment**
