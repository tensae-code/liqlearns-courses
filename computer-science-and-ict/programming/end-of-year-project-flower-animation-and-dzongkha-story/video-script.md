# Video script — End-of-Year Project — Flower Animation and Dzongkha Story

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The **flower animation** is built in Scratch from the year's block families working together: the **pen** draws the stem and petals as the sprite moves, **looks** blocks change the flower's appearance as it opens, and **control** blocks repeat the drawing until the flower is complete. A flower is the honest test of the pen: every petal is a planned path, drawn by blocks the pupil chose.
Beside the animation, the year's story is typed **in Dzongkha in Word** — the national language typed with Dzongkha keyboard software and fonts, saved into the pupil's folder. The folder itself is the third skill: the year's work gathered, named and **sent to the teacher as a LAN-messenger attachment** — flower, story and folder arriving together as one end-of-year piece.
