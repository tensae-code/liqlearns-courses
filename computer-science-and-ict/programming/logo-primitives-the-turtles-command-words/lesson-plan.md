# Lesson plan — LOGO primitives — the turtle's command words

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Programming · **Lesson:** LOGO primitives — the turtle's command words
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 12 — Programming in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 8 book, Unit 6 — Logic Oriented Graphics Oriented (LOGO) Programming. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the full table of LOGO primitives — the command words the MSW LOGO turtle obeys — and what each one does.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 7 LOGO lessons — the learner has met fd, bk, rt, lt and cs; this lesson grows the vocabulary to the book's full Grade 8 table.

The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: LOGO drawing commands — fd, bk, rt, lt, cs. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a primitive is and where commands are entered.
2. Give the meaning of all eighteen primitives in the book's table.
3. Use PU and PD to move the turtle without drawing, then draw again.
4. Use SUM, DIFFERENCE, PRODUCT, SQRT and EXP to make LOGO calculate.

## What you need
A computer with MSW LOGO (version 6.5b, as the book uses) open; paper and a pencil.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner writes every LOGO command word they already know from Grade 7, then counts them — the book's Grade 8 table is longer (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the parts below, in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 12310]: a picture of the turtle at the centre of a screen ringed by its command words — the student reads each word and says what the turtle does.
   - ADD GIF [GIF 12311]: a picture of the turtle obeying FD, RT, PU, PD in turn — moving, turning, lifting its pen, drawing again — the student names each primitive as it acts.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the eighteen primitives with their meanings — the student repeats each pair aloud.
3. **Say it to your companion** — explain the lesson back in your own words (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson retold in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, fill-in-the-blank — served at the learner's level (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes CS and CT → CS clears the drawing and sends the turtle home; CT clears the text — the commands in the recall list box. Screen against text keeps them apart.
- Thinks PU moves the turtle home → PU only lifts the pen; HOME is the word that brings the turtle home.
