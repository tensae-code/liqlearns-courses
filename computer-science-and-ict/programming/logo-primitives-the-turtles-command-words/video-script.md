# Video script — LOGO primitives — the turtle's command words

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The turtle obeys **words**. Those words are **primitives**.

Move: **FD** forward. **BK** back. **RT** right. **LT** left.
Pen: **PU** — pen up, no line. **PD** — pen down, draw again.
Screen: **CS** clears the drawing. **CT** clears the text. **HOME** sends the turtle home.
Hide and show: **HT**. **ST**.
Calculate: **SUM** adds. **DIFFERENCE** subtracts. **PRODUCT** multiplies. **SQRT** — square root. **EXP** — powers.
And **PRINT** writes.

Eighteen words. One turtle. All obeyed.
