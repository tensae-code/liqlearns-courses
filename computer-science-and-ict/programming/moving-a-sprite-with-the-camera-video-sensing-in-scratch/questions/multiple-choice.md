# Moving a Sprite with the Camera — Video Sensing in Scratch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does turning the video on bring in?**
a) The camera’s picture  b) Video motion and direction  c) How strongly the camera picture shows through  d) Ayman’s
**Answer: a) The camera’s picture**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two things does video sensing read on a sprite?**
a) How much movement the camera sees there  b) Video motion and direction  c) Which way the movement goes  d) The camera’s motion
**Answer: b) Video motion and direction**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Motion over the sprite means what?**
a) How strongly the camera picture shows through  b) Ayman’s  c) How much movement the camera sees there  d) A drawn goal
**Answer: c) How much movement the camera sees there**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does video transparency set?**
a) Which way the movement goes  b) The camera’s motion  c) The player’s real movement in the room  d) How strongly the camera picture shows through
**Answer: d) How strongly the camera picture shows through**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Direction tells the program what?**
a) Which way the movement goes  b) Ayman’s  c) A drawn goal  d) Clapping and a new backdrop
**Answer: a) Which way the movement goes**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Whose football game is the lesson’s?**
a) The camera’s motion  b) Ayman’s  c) The player’s real movement in the room  d) The camera’s picture
**Answer: b) Ayman’s**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the ball follow?**
a) A drawn goal  b) Clapping and a new backdrop  c) The camera’s motion  d) Video motion and direction
**Answer: c) The camera’s motion**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Towards what is the ball driven?**
a) The player’s real movement in the room  b) The camera’s picture  c) How much movement the camera sees there  d) A drawn goal
**Answer: d) A drawn goal**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What drives the game?**
a) The player’s real movement in the room  b) Clapping and a new backdrop  c) Video motion and direction  d) How strongly the camera picture shows through
**Answer: a) The player’s real movement in the room**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does scoring bring?**
a) The camera’s picture  b) Clapping and a new backdrop  c) How much movement the camera sees there  d) Which way the movement goes
**Answer: b) Clapping and a new backdrop**
