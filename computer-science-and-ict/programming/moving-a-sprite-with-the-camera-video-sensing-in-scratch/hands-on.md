# Hands-on — Moving a Sprite with the Camera — Video Sensing in Scratch

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sense a wave
Turn the video on, show video motion as a monitor, and wave over a sprite to watch the number answer your hand.

## Play Ayman’s game
Draw a goal and build the ball that follows the camera’s motion; score once and trigger the clapping and new backdrop.
