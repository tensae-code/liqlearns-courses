# Arithmetic Operations and Expressions in C++ — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the % operator give?**
**Answer: The remainder after division**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is 17 % 5?**
**Answer: 2**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is 17 / 5 between integers in C++?**
**Answer: 3 — the fraction is dropped**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which operators are named in this sub lesson?**
**Answer: +, −, *, / and %**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When does / divide fully?**
**Answer: When the numbers are decimal numbers**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is evaluated first in an expression?**
**Answer: Brackets**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which rank comes next after brackets?**
**Answer: *, / and %**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is 2 + 3 * 4?**
**Answer: 14**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is (2 + 3) * 4?**
**Answer: 20 — the brackets reorder it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does an assignment store from an expression?**
**Answer: Its single final value**
