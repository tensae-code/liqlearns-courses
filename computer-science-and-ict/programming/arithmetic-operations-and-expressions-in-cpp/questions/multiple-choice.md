# Arithmetic Operations and Expressions in C++ — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the % operator give?**
a) The remainder after division  b) 2  c) 3 — the fraction is dropped  d) +, −, *, / and %
**Answer: a) The remainder after division**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is 17 % 5?**
a) 3 — the fraction is dropped  b) 2  c) +, −, *, / and %  d) When the numbers are decimal numbers
**Answer: b) 2**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is 17 / 5 between integers in C++?**
a) +, −, *, / and %  b) When the numbers are decimal numbers  c) 3 — the fraction is dropped  d) Brackets
**Answer: c) 3 — the fraction is dropped**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which operators are named in this sub lesson?**
a) When the numbers are decimal numbers  b) Brackets  c) *, / and %  d) +, −, *, / and %
**Answer: d) +, −, *, / and %**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When does / divide fully?**
a) When the numbers are decimal numbers  b) Brackets  c) *, / and %  d) 14
**Answer: a) When the numbers are decimal numbers**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is evaluated first in an expression?**
a) *, / and %  b) Brackets  c) 14  d) 20 — the brackets reorder it
**Answer: b) Brackets**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which rank comes next after brackets?**
a) 14  b) 20 — the brackets reorder it  c) *, / and %  d) Its single final value
**Answer: c) *, / and %**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is 2 + 3 * 4?**
a) 20 — the brackets reorder it  b) Its single final value  c) The remainder after division  d) 14
**Answer: d) 14**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is (2 + 3) * 4?**
a) 20 — the brackets reorder it  b) Its single final value  c) The remainder after division  d) 2
**Answer: a) 20 — the brackets reorder it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does an assignment store from an expression?**
a) The remainder after division  b) Its single final value  c) 2  d) 3 — the fraction is dropped
**Answer: b) Its single final value**
