# Hands-on — Displaying Alphanumeric Information — write and writeln in Pascal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Table face** — Write the writeln calls that print a three-row multiplication table with values aligned in width-4 fields.

## Task two
**Receipt** — Format a shop receipt: three items with names, prices at width 8 with 2 decimals, and a labelled total line.
