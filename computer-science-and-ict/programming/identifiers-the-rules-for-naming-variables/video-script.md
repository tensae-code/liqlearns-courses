# Video script - Identifiers - the rules for naming variables

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
A short video script for this lesson; its placement names no full-script need.

---

Every variable needs a name. The name is an identifier.
And names have rules.

Rule one: start with a letter - or an underscore.
Never a digit. 2age? Not allowed.

Rule two: after that, letters, numbers, underscores.
No stars. No symbols. total with an &? Not allowed.

Rule three: never a keyword.
Keywords are the language's own reserved words.

Rule four: respect case.
x and X are DIFFERENT names.

And one style rule: choose names that describe.
 radius - not r-thing. Programs people can read.
