# Input, process, output — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is the working the computer does on the input. (Name the term.)
**Answer: process**

**2.** _____ is what the computer produces — images, text and sound. (Name the term.)
**Answer: output**

**3.** _____ is the single-CPU information-processing model framing all the unit's problem solving. (Name the term.)
**Answer: the IPO model**

**4.** In the book's worked thread, A + B is stored into ______.
**Answer: C**

**5.** Step 4 expresses the algorithm in precise ______.
**Answer: notation**
