# Hands-on — Input, process, output

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Three-label walk
Call out everyday machine moments; pupils hold up I, P or O cards for each. Speed rounds at the end.

## Draw the model
Draw the IPO model for a machine of your choice, with real examples in each box.


## Everyday algorithm
Do the book's opening activity for real: write the steps of making a sandwich (or cooking shiro wot) as numbered instructions, then swap with a partner who follows them literally — every gap they find is a step you must repair.
