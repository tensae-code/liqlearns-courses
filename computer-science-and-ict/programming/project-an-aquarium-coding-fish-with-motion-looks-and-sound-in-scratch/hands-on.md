# Hands-on — Project — An Aquarium: Coding Fish with Motion, Looks and Sound in Scratch

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stock your tank
Build the aquarium scene: underwater backdrop, three fish of different colours and sizes, placed spread through the water. Say what you personalised on each fish.

## Set them swimming
Give each fish its swimming script at its own speed, turning at the tank's ends. Add one quiet sound. Watch the whole tank for a minute: what would you adjust?
