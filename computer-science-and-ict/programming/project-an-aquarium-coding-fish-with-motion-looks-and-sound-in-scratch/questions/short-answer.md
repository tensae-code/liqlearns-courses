# Project — An Aquarium: Coding Fish with Motion, Looks and Sound in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What backdrop does the project set?**
**Answer: An underwater scene — water, sand, plants**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How do the fish differ from each other?**
**Answer: In colour, size and starting place**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where is each fish placed?**
**Answer: Inside the water, spread through the tank**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When is the tank stocked?**
**Answer: Before anything swims**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why personalise every fish?**
**Answer: So the tank holds a community, not copies**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does a fish swim back at the tank's end?**
**Answer: It turns, and swims the other way**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do looks do for the fish?**
**Answer: Costume changes or effects give them life**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What sounds belong in the scene?**
**Answer: Bubbles, water, or gentle music, kept quiet**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do the fish's speeds compare?**
**Answer: Different speeds and heights, like a real tank**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the project's check?**
**Answer: Continuous, varied swimming a watcher enjoys untouched**
