# Video script — Project — An Aquarium: Coding Fish with Motion, Looks and Sound in Scratch

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The project sets its scene with an underwater backdrop — water, sand, plants — and several fish sprites, each added and personalised: different colours, sizes and starting places, so the tank holds a community, not copies. Each fish is placed inside the water, spread through the tank, before anything swims: the stocking is a design act, and a crowded corner or an empty half is fixed now, not after the running begins.
Each fish swims by its scripts: motion carries it across the tank and turns it at the ends so it swims back, looks work — costume changes or effects — give it life, and the scene's sounds — bubbles, water, a gentle music — play at a volume that sits under the scene. Fish move at different speeds and heights, as real tank fish do. The check is the tank watched whole: continuous, varied swimming that a watcher can enjoy without touching anything.
