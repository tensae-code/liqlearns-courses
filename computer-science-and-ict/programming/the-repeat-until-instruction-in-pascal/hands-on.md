# Hands-on — The Repeat-Until Instruction in Pascal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Valid input loop** — Write a repeat loop that asks for a mark until a value between 1 and 10 is given, and trace it with inputs 15, −2, 7.

## Task two
**Loop conversion** — Convert a given while loop into repeat…until (negating its condition) and a given repeat into while with a priming first run; say when conversion is unsafe.
