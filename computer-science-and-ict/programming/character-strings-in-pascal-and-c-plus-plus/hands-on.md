# Hands-on — Character Strings in Pascal and C++

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**String anatomy** — For the string 'Informatică' (as written in each language's marks): give its length, its first and last characters, and the substring of its first four characters.

## Task two
**Join and order** — Concatenate three fragments into a sentence, then order the words 'ana', 'Ana', 'banana' by string comparison and explain the order by codes.
