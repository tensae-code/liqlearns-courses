# Procedures and Functions in Pascal — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a procedure?**
**Answer: A named block of statements, written once and called by name**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are parameters?**
**Answer: Values handed in for this call**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where is a procedure written?**
**Answer: Above the main program**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a procedure return to the caller?**
**Answer: Control — it hands back where to continue**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) With what are procedures taught in the book?**
**Answer: Together with functions, in one lesson**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a function return?**
**Answer: A value**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where is a function used?**
**Answer: Inside an expression**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When do you choose a function over a procedure?**
**Answer: When the block must give back one value**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When do you choose a procedure?**
**Answer: When the block does a job — draw, print, sort**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do both keep short?**
**Answer: The main program**
