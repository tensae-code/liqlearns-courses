# Hands-on — Remixing — Changing a Program Someone Else Wrote

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Remix a stranger** — Exchange working Scratch programs with another pair (no explanations!). Read, map, then remix exactly one behaviour. Present: original behaviour, your change, your retest evidence.

## Task two
**Credit line** — Write the credit line for your remix as it should appear in the project notes: original author, original behaviour, your change. The class checks three remixes for honest lines.
