# Remixing — Changing a Program Someone Else Wrote — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the séance's title, in English?**
**Answer: It wasn't me who made it, but I can modify it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does remixing start with?**
**Answer: Reading — running the program and predicting each script's job**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name three parts pupils learn to name.**
**Answer: Initialisation, movement, scoring (answers vary by program)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is an unmapped change?**
**Answer: Vandalism with good intentions**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a mapped change?**
**Answer: Engineering**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How many behaviours per remix step?**
**Answer: One**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do you find before changing?**
**Answer: The smallest set of blocks that owns the behaviour**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is retested afterwards?**
**Answer: Everything — the new behaviour and every kept old one**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How does credit travel?**
**Answer: A remix names its original — ‘after X's program’**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What culture is Scratch built on?**
**Answer: Remix culture — changing and sharing, with authors named**
