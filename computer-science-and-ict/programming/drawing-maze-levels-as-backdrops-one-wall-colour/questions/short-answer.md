# Drawing Maze Levels as Backdrops — One Wall Colour — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Explain to a partner why the wall-colour rule is about programming, not about looks.**
**Answer:** A sound answer: the character's script tests touching one colour to detect walls; extra wall colours would escape the test, so the rule keeps the game's sense of touch complete.

**2. Your partner's player walks through the top wall of Maze 2 only. Name the two likely faults.**
**Answer:** A sound answer: that stretch is painted in a different colour from the wall colour, or there is a gap in the wall there — repaint in the wall colour and close any gap.
