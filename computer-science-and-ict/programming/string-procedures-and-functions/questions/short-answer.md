# String Procedures and Functions — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does processing a string mean?**
**Answer: Changing it, or learning something about it — its length, a part of it, where a piece occurs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a function hand back here?**
**Answer: An answer — for example a length, a copied part, or a found position**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a procedure do here?**
**Answer: A job on the variable itself, such as inserting or deleting a piece, so the variable holds the changed text**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must you know before calling a string tool?**
**Answer: What it needs, and what it gives back or changes**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name an edge case to check.**
**Answer: A position beyond the string's length, a searched piece that is not there, or an empty string**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does joining strings do?**
**Answer: Builds longer text from shorter pieces, end to end**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are most real text tasks, in this section's view?**
**Answer: A chain of small tools — find, copy out, join, delete**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does a searched piece's answer come as?**
**Answer: The position where the piece begins**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Does deleting a piece change the original variable?**
**Answer: Yes — the procedure changes the variable itself**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why use ready tools instead of rebuilding them?**
**Answer: They are tested and do each standard job exactly**
