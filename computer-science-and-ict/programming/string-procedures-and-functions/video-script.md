# Video script — String Procedures and Functions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Processing** a string variable means changing it or learning something about it: how long it is, what part of it says, where a piece occurs.
PascalABC provides ready **procedures and functions** for these standard jobs.
The working method is the same for every tool: know what the tool needs (the string, and usually a position or a piece of text), know what it gives back or changes, and check the edges — a position beyond the string's length, a searched piece that is not there, an empty string.
Joining strings end to end builds longer text from shorter pieces, and most real text tasks are a chain of these small tools: find a piece, copy it out, join it to something else, delete what is not needed.
