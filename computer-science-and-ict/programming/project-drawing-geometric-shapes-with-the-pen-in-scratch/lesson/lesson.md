# Project — Drawing Geometric Shapes with the Pen in Scratch

## Do it first

The Pen extension's project draws the shapes of the geometry lesson: squares, triangles and their family, drawn by a sprite that walks their edges. Loops do the drawing's heavy work — one shape is a pattern of moves and turns.

## Sub lesson 1 — A shape is moves and turns — the square and the triangle

With the **pen down**, a sprite **draws as it moves**: a **square** is **move, turn 90°, four times**; a **triangle** is **move, turn 120°, three times** — the turning angle found from the shape's corners, the count from its sides. The maker tests each shape alone first, pen down at a clear place, and reads failures geometrically: a shape that does not close has the wrong turn or the wrong count, never a mystery. Erasing the pen's trails clears the stage between shapes.

## Sub lesson 2 — Loops draw them — and shapes combine

Each shape collapses into a **repeat**: the square is **repeat 4 [move, turn 90°]**, and changing the **count and the turn** draws the whole family — pentagon, hexagon — from one script shape. Pen **colour and size** personalise the drawing, and shapes **combine into figures**: a house is a square with a triangle on top, drawn by running the two shapes' scripts in their places. The project's check is a page of named shapes, each drawn by its own loop, that another learner can reproduce from the counts and turns alone.
