# Project — Drawing Geometric Shapes with the Pen in Scratch — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How does the pen draw a shape?" is: Pen down, the sprite draws as it moves along the edges**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a square, as moves and turns?" is: Repeat 4: move, turn 90°**
**Answer: False** — Move, turn 90° — four times

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a triangle, as moves and turns?" is: Move, turn 120° — three times**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where do the turn and the count come from?" is: From the same script shape, with their own counts and turns**
**Answer: False** — The turn from the shape's corners, the count from its sides

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does an unclosed shape mean?" is: The wrong turn or the wrong count — a findable fault**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How does a repeat draw a square?" is: Named shapes another learner can reproduce from counts and turns alone**
**Answer: False** — Repeat 4: move, turn 90°

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What two numbers draw the whole shape family?" is: The repeat count and the turn**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How are a pentagon and hexagon drawn?" is: Move, turn 90° — four times**
**Answer: False** — From the same script shape, with their own counts and turns

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is a house drawn?" is: A square with a triangle on top — the two shapes in their places**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the project's check?" is: The turn from the shape's corners, the count from its sides**
**Answer: False** — Named shapes another learner can reproduce from counts and turns alone
