# Project — Drawing Geometric Shapes with the Pen in Scratch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does the pen draw a shape?**
a) Pen down, the sprite draws as it moves along the edges  b) Move, turn 90° — four times  c) Move, turn 120° — three times  d) The turn from the shape's corners, the count from its sides
**Answer: a) Pen down, the sprite draws as it moves along the edges**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a square, as moves and turns?**
a) Move, turn 120° — three times  b) Move, turn 90° — four times  c) The turn from the shape's corners, the count from its sides  d) The wrong turn or the wrong count — a findable fault
**Answer: b) Move, turn 90° — four times**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a triangle, as moves and turns?**
a) The turn from the shape's corners, the count from its sides  b) The wrong turn or the wrong count — a findable fault  c) Move, turn 120° — three times  d) Repeat 4: move, turn 90°
**Answer: c) Move, turn 120° — three times**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the turn and the count come from?**
a) The wrong turn or the wrong count — a findable fault  b) Repeat 4: move, turn 90°  c) The repeat count and the turn  d) The turn from the shape's corners, the count from its sides
**Answer: d) The turn from the shape's corners, the count from its sides**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does an unclosed shape mean?**
a) The wrong turn or the wrong count — a findable fault  b) Repeat 4: move, turn 90°  c) The repeat count and the turn  d) From the same script shape, with their own counts and turns
**Answer: a) The wrong turn or the wrong count — a findable fault**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does a repeat draw a square?**
a) The repeat count and the turn  b) Repeat 4: move, turn 90°  c) From the same script shape, with their own counts and turns  d) A square with a triangle on top — the two shapes in their places
**Answer: b) Repeat 4: move, turn 90°**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What two numbers draw the whole shape family?**
a) From the same script shape, with their own counts and turns  b) A square with a triangle on top — the two shapes in their places  c) The repeat count and the turn  d) Named shapes another learner can reproduce from counts and turns alone
**Answer: c) The repeat count and the turn**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How are a pentagon and hexagon drawn?**
a) A square with a triangle on top — the two shapes in their places  b) Named shapes another learner can reproduce from counts and turns alone  c) Pen down, the sprite draws as it moves along the edges  d) From the same script shape, with their own counts and turns
**Answer: d) From the same script shape, with their own counts and turns**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is a house drawn?**
a) A square with a triangle on top — the two shapes in their places  b) Named shapes another learner can reproduce from counts and turns alone  c) Pen down, the sprite draws as it moves along the edges  d) Move, turn 90° — four times
**Answer: a) A square with a triangle on top — the two shapes in their places**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the project's check?**
