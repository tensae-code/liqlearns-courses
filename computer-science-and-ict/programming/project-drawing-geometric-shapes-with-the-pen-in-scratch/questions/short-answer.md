# Project — Drawing Geometric Shapes with the Pen in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does the pen draw a shape?**
**Answer: Pen down, the sprite draws as it moves along the edges**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a square, as moves and turns?**
**Answer: Move, turn 90° — four times**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a triangle, as moves and turns?**
**Answer: Move, turn 120° — three times**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the turn and the count come from?**
**Answer: The turn from the shape's corners, the count from its sides**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does an unclosed shape mean?**
**Answer: The wrong turn or the wrong count — a findable fault**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does a repeat draw a square?**
**Answer: Repeat 4: move, turn 90°**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What two numbers draw the whole shape family?**
**Answer: The repeat count and the turn**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How are a pentagon and hexagon drawn?**
**Answer: From the same script shape, with their own counts and turns**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is a house drawn?**
**Answer: A square with a triangle on top — the two shapes in their places**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the project's check?**
**Answer: Named shapes another learner can reproduce from counts and turns alone**
