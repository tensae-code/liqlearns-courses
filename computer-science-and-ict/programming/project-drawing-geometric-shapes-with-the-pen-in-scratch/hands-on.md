# Hands-on — Project — Drawing Geometric Shapes with the Pen in Scratch

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One shape, one loop
Draw a square with a repeat of 4, then change only the count and the turn to draw a triangle and a hexagon. Write each shape's pair of numbers beside it.

## Draw the house
Combine your shapes into a house: square body, triangle roof, pen colour changed for the roof. Clear the stage and redraw it in one run from the flag.
