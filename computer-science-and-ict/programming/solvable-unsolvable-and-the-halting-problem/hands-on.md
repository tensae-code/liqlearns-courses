# Hands-on — Solvable Unsolvable and the Halting Problem

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 3 §3.3 Problem Solvability and Complexity: §3.3.1 Solvable vs Unsolvable Problems (the Halting Problem, Alan Turing) and §3.3.2 Tractable vs Intractable Problems (the Traveling Salesman Problem) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach solvable unsolvable and the halting problem to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
