## Sub lesson 1 — The problem — queens that must not meet
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Any two queens sharing a row, a column, or a diagonal; N = 2 and N = 3; 92; Every part of backtracking is visible in it — choices, conditions, stepping back; Her row, her column, and both diagonals.

## Sub lesson 2 — The solution — one queen per row, checked at once
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Place one queen per row, row by row — rows then can never clash; The column and both diagonals; Backtrack — move the previous row's queen to her next column and descend again; When a full board stands — every row holding a safe queen; When row one's columns are exhausted.
