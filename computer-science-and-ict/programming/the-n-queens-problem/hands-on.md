# Hands-on — The N-Queens Problem

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Solve N=4** — Solve the 4-queens problem by hand, row by row. Write down every backtrack you are forced to make.

## Task two
**Count the checks** — For N=4, at each placement you attempt, list which checks (column, diagonals) passed or failed. Which check fails most often?
