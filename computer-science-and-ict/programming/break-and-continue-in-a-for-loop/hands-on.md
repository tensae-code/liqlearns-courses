# Hands-on — break and continue — leave early, or skip one

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — every activity is done for real, in IDLE or on paper.

## Track game
Lay a track of five cards. Walk it: at the break card, step OFF the track and stop. Walk again: at the continue card, HOP over it without touching and keep walking.

## Predict pairs
With a partner, write break and continue versions of one list program each. Swap, predict outputs, then run to check.
