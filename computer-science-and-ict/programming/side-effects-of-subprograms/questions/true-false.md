# Side Effects of Subprograms — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A change a subprogram makes outside its contract — to globals, files, the screen — beyond its return and agreed parameters**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A change a subprogram makes outside its contract — to globals, files, the screen — beyond its return and agreed parameters** — as the answer to: Why is the offence subtle?
**Answer: False — The heading promises a clean exchange — the extra change is invisible at the call**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Global variables, files, and the screen (also: devices)**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Global variables, files, and the screen (also: devices)** — as the answer to: Can two identical-looking calls differ in hidden work?
**Answer: False — Yes — exactly that invisibility is the danger**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Its parameters in, its returned value out**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Its parameters in, its returned value out** — as the answer to: What is the first move of the discipline?
**Answer: False — Name it — effect-work belongs in procedures whose purpose is the effect**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Passing what is needed as parameters instead of reaching for globals**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Passing what is needed as parameters instead of reaching for globals** — as the answer to: What is required of functions?
**Answer: False — To compute from parameters alone — safe to call twice, or never, inside any expression**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: An expression's parts may be evaluated in any order, or optimised — effects would fire unpredictably**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: An expression's parts may be evaluated in any order, or optimised — effects would fire unpredictably** — as the answer to: What keeps programs readable, in the chapter's standard?
**Answer: False — Effects staying visible at their calls**
