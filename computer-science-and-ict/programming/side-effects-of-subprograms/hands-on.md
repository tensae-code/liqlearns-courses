# Hands-on — Side Effects of Subprograms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Effect audit** — Take a program with three subprograms and list every effect of each: contracted (parameters, return) and side (globals, output); mark the moonlighting functions.

## Task two
**Repair by contract** — Rewrite a function that secretly increments a global counter into a procedure with the counter as a variable parameter, and list which call sites changed meaning.
