# Video script — Programming Tasks from School Subjects

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Each task in this practicum starts in **another subject**: a physics relationship to calculate, a geography quantity to compare, a biology or ecology count to model, an astronomy cycle to work out.
The informatics part is the translation: the subject's rule becomes **formulas and steps** a program can run, its data becomes the program's input, and its expected results become the check.
The solving order repeats the Grade 10 practicum's: understand the subject task first (what is asked, what rule governs it), plan the program, write it, and **test it on the subject's known cases** — values whose answers the subject itself supplies, in its textbook or from the teacher.
A program that calculates a physics task wrong is not a programming success with a physics error; it is a wrong answer, and the fault can sit in either subject's part — the rule as translated, or the code as written — so both are checked.
