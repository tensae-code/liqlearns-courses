# Programming Tasks from School Subjects — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which subjects supply this practicum's tasks?**
**Answer: Astronomy, geography, biology and ecology, and physics**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the informatics part of each task?**
**Answer: Translation — the subject's rule becomes formulas and steps a program can run**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What becomes the program's input?**
**Answer: The subject task's data**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do the subject's expected results become?**
**Answer: The check the program is tested against**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the solving order?**
**Answer: Understand the subject task, plan the program, write it, test it on known cases**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where do the known test answers come from?**
**Answer: The subject itself — its textbook or the teacher**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) If a physics answer is wrong, where can the fault sit?**
**Answer: In the rule as translated, or in the code as written**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why are science subjects chosen?**
**Answer: Their school material is already rules and numbers**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does this practicum use from earlier in the course?**
**Answer: PascalABC work, including the graphics work of § 7**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the practicum's claim?**
**Answer: Programming is a tool the other subjects can use**
