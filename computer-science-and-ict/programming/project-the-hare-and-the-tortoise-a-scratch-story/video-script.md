# Video script — Project — The Hare and the Tortoise: A Scratch Story

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The project assembles the story's pieces first: a hare sprite and a tortoise sprite, added and personalised, standing at the start of the race on a backdrop that shows the race's place — a path through fields or a wood. Further backdrops carry the story's places as it moves: where the hare stops, and the finish where the tortoise arrives. Each sprite is placed at its starting position before anything moves, so every run of the story begins the same race.
The story runs on its scripts: the hare moves fast and then stops — it rests, as in the tale — while the tortoise moves slowly and does not stop, step after step, until it reaches the finish first. Sounds from the year's sound work can mark the start and the finish. The project's check is the story itself: a watcher who does not know the code must be able to tell the tale from watching — fast start, rest, steady tortoise, surprise finish.
