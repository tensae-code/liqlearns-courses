# Project — The Hare and the Tortoise: A Scratch Story — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which two sprites does the project need?**
a) A hare sprite and a tortoise sprite  b) At the start of the race, on the race's backdrop  c) The story's later places — where the hare stops, and the finish  d) Before anything moves, before the story runs
**Answer: a) A hare sprite and a tortoise sprite**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the sprites stand at the start?**
a) The story's later places — where the hare stops, and the finish  b) At the start of the race, on the race's backdrop  c) Before anything moves, before the story runs  d) So every run begins the same race
**Answer: b) At the start of the race, on the race's backdrop**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do the further backdrops carry?**
a) Before anything moves, before the story runs  b) So every run begins the same race  c) The story's later places — where the hare stops, and the finish  d) Fast — and then it stops and rests
**Answer: c) The story's later places — where the hare stops, and the finish**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When are the sprites placed at their starts?**
