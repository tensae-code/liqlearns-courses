# Project — The Hare and the Tortoise: A Scratch Story — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which two sprites does the project need?" is: A hare sprite and a tortoise sprite**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where do the sprites stand at the start?" is: Fast — and then it stops and rests**
**Answer: False** — At the start of the race, on the race's backdrop

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do the further backdrops carry?" is: The story's later places — where the hare stops, and the finish**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "When are the sprites placed at their starts?" is: The tortoise**
**Answer: False** — Before anything moves, before the story runs

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why place the sprites before running?" is: So every run begins the same race**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How does the hare move in the project?" is: A watcher can tell the tale just from watching it run**
**Answer: False** — Fast — and then it stops and rests

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How does the tortoise move?" is: Slowly, and it does not stop**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Who reaches the finish first?" is: At the start of the race, on the race's backdrop**
**Answer: False** — The tortoise

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What can sounds mark in the story?" is: The start and the finish**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the project's check?" is: Before anything moves, before the story runs**
