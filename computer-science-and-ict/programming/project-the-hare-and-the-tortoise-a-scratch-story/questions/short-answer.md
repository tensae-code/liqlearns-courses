# Project — The Hare and the Tortoise: A Scratch Story — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which two sprites does the project need?**
**Answer: A hare sprite and a tortoise sprite**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the sprites stand at the start?**
**Answer: At the start of the race, on the race's backdrop**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do the further backdrops carry?**
**Answer: The story's later places — where the hare stops, and the finish**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When are the sprites placed at their starts?**
**Answer: Before anything moves, before the story runs**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why place the sprites before running?**
**Answer: So every run begins the same race**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does the hare move in the project?**
**Answer: Fast — and then it stops and rests**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does the tortoise move?**
**Answer: Slowly, and it does not stop**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Who reaches the finish first?**
**Answer: The tortoise**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What can sounds mark in the story?**
**Answer: The start and the finish**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the project's check?**
**Answer: A watcher can tell the tale just from watching it run**
