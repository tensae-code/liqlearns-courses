# Hands-on — Project — The Hare and the Tortoise: A Scratch Story

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Storyboard the race
Draw the project's three moments as three boxes: the start, the hare resting, the finish. Under each, write which sprites and backdrop it needs.

## Build and run it
Build the race in Scratch: two sprites, the race backdrop, the hare fast then resting, the tortoise slow and steady. Run it for a partner: can they retell the story from the running project alone?
