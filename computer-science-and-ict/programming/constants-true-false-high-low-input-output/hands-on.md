# Hands-on — Constants — TRUE/FALSE, HIGH/LOW, INPUT/OUTPUT

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Translate the sketch** — Take a sketch written with bare numbers (1/0 for levels, 0/1 for modes). Rewrite it with TRUE/FALSE, HIGH/LOW, INPUT/OUTPUT and say what the rewrite buys a reader.

## Task two
**Name the constant** — Write six one-line situations (a pressed button's reading, a lit LED's level, a sensor pin's mode…). For each, write the exact constant a program would use.
