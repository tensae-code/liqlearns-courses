# Hands-on — Models of the Software Development Process

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Order the stages
Write the stages on slips: requirements, design, build, test, deliver, maintain. Order them one-direction; then show where a repeating model would loop back.

## Pick a model
Two projects: a one-week class game, a hospital records system. Choose a model for each and defend the choice by risk.
