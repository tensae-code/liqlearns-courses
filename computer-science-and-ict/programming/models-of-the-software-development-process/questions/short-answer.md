# Models of the Software Development Process — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A process model organises what?**
**Answer: The stages of developing software**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first stage in most models?**
**Answer: Requirements and planning**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Testing comes when, in a simple model?**
**Answer: After building**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is maintenance?**
**Answer: Keeping software working after delivery**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Process models differ in what?**
**Answer: The order and repetition of the stages**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) A one-direction model moves how?**
**Answer: Stage by stage without going back**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) A repeating model allows what?**
**Answer: Returning to earlier stages**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Choosing a process model depends on what?**
**Answer: The project and its risks**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Following a model helps a team how?**
**Answer: Everyone works in an order everyone knows**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) The guide's lesson compares what?**
**Answer: Different types of software development process**
