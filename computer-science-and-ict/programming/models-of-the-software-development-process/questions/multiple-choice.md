# Models of the Software Development Process — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A process model organises what?**
a) The stages of developing software  b) Requirements and planning  c) After building  d) Keeping software working after delivery
**Answer: a) The stages of developing software**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first stage in most models?**
a) After building  b) Requirements and planning  c) Keeping software working after delivery  d) The order and repetition of the stages
**Answer: b) Requirements and planning**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Testing comes when, in a simple model?**
a) Keeping software working after delivery  b) The order and repetition of the stages  c) After building  d) Stage by stage without going back
**Answer: c) After building**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is maintenance?**
a) The order and repetition of the stages  b) Stage by stage without going back  c) Returning to earlier stages  d) Keeping software working after delivery
**Answer: d) Keeping software working after delivery**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Process models differ in what?**
a) The order and repetition of the stages  b) Stage by stage without going back  c) Returning to earlier stages  d) The project and its risks
**Answer: a) The order and repetition of the stages**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) A one-direction model moves how?**
a) Returning to earlier stages  b) Stage by stage without going back  c) The project and its risks  d) Everyone works in an order everyone knows
**Answer: b) Stage by stage without going back**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) A repeating model allows what?**
a) The project and its risks  b) Everyone works in an order everyone knows  c) Returning to earlier stages  d) Different types of software development process
**Answer: c) Returning to earlier stages**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Choosing a process model depends on what?**
a) Everyone works in an order everyone knows  b) Different types of software development process  c) The stages of developing software  d) The project and its risks
**Answer: d) The project and its risks**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Following a model helps a team how?**
a) Everyone works in an order everyone knows  b) Different types of software development process  c) The stages of developing software  d) Requirements and planning
**Answer: a) Everyone works in an order everyone knows**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) The guide's lesson compares what?**
a) The stages of developing software  b) Different types of software development process  c) Requirements and planning  d) After building
**Answer: b) Different types of software development process**
