# Video script — Models of the Software Development Process

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **process model** organises **the stages of developing software**. In most models the first stage is **requirements and planning**; **testing follows building** in a simple model and runs throughout in others; and **maintenance — keeping software working after delivery** — closes the process. The stages themselves are the shared skeleton.
Models differ in **the order and repetition of the stages**. A **one-direction model** moves stage by stage without going back. A **repeating model** allows returning to earlier stages. A model is chosen by the project and its risks, and following one helps a team work in an order everyone knows. The guide's lesson compares different types of software development process.
