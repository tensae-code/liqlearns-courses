# Making Your Own Blocks in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is defining your own block?**
**Answer: Naming a stack and keeping it as a block**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which two blocks does the book define?**
**Answer: “Square” and “sum”**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do the defined blocks take?**
**Answer: Number inputs**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does an input let one block do?**
**Answer: Draw any size, or add any pair**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is your block used once defined?**
**Answer: Like any built-in block**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the triangle block called?**
**Answer: “Draw the triangle”**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What inputs does the triangle block take?**
**Answer: The two sides**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the triangle block draw?**
**Answer: Any right-angled triangle the program names**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the Pythagoras check happen?**
**Answer: In the if-statement lesson’s program**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the drawing live?**
**Answer: Inside the custom block**
