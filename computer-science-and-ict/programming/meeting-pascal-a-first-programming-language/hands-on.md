# Hands-on — Meeting Pascal — a First Programming Language

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Shape spotting** — Take any Pascal program text from the book and mark its four parts — program line, declarations, begin…end body, final full stop — in four colours.

## Task two
**First program** — Write the greeting program by hand in full shape, then change only the greeting text and say which parts of the shape did not change.
