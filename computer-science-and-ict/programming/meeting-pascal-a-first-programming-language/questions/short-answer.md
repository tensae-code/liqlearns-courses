# Meeting Pascal — a First Programming Language — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is Pascal?**
**Answer: A programming language designed for teaching programming properly**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who designed it, and when?**
**Answer: Niklaus Wirth, around 1970**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What habits does its strictness train?**
**Answer: Precision — clear structure and checked types, no vague shortcuts**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How long does Moldova's course stay with Pascal?**
**Answer: From Grade 7 through Grade 12 (later beside C++)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a Pascal program, in one phrase?**
**Answer: A text in the language's exact vocabulary and grammar that the computer can execute**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What word opens a Pascal program?**
**Answer: program — followed by the program's name**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What comes after the opening, before the instructions?**
**Answer: The declarations — the data the program will use**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What separates the instructions?**
**Answer: Semicolons**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How does a program close?**
**Answer: With end followed by a full stop**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Does a tiny program wear the whole shape?**
**Answer: Yes — declarations, then a begin…end body, however small the program**
