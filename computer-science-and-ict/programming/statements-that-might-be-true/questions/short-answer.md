# Statements That Might Be True — Uncertain Statements — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Give one uncertain statement and say what would settle it.**
**Answer:** For example: 'There is a stone in the box' — opening the box settles it (any might-be statement with its settling check).

**2. Why must we not call a 'might be' statement true too soon?**
**Answer:** Because it may turn out false; careful thinking waits until it is known.
