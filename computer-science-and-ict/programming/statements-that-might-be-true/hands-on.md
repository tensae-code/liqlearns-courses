# Hands-on — Statements That Might Be True — Uncertain Statements

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Sort the statements
Sort eight statement cards into three piles: true, false, uncertain. For each uncertain one, say what would settle it.

## Box check
Guess about a hidden object: 'There is a red pencil in the box.' Say why it is uncertain — then open the box and say what it became.
