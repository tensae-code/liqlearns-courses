# Hands-on — The MSW Logo screen — graphics window and Commander

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Screen tour
With Logo open, point to each element as a partner calls its name. Seven elements, then swap. Finish by pointing to where a typed command goes and where its record appears.

## Turtle watch
Move nothing yet: find the turtle at the centre of the graphics screen and describe exactly where it sits and which way its point aims. In the next lesson it starts to move.

## If it is hard
The guide's differentiation instruction for LOGO work is carried into this course's doing-lessons: while observing, the teacher encourages pupils who lag behind and gives support to those with special needs — ask, and expect that help.
