The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — programming/algorithms-and-their-executors). It sits in the Programming title (programming/), following the book's own order. Provenance scope note: every counted placement stocked here is from a Moldova student book (all seven books in this expansion are student books). Media slots in lesson/lesson.md are described slots only ([Picture — …]); no picture numbers are invented.

Moldova's own way (Grade 8, Informatică): Grade 8 pairs a full office-grade Word course (styles, templates, mail merge, formulas) with algorithmic thinking taught formally — executors, subalgorithms, counted/conditional loops, branching — then implemented instruction by instruction in Pascal, closing with image editing in Paint 3D and GIMP.

Moldova's own way (Grade 10, Informatică): the manual teaches Pascal and C++ side by side — each school chooses one or both — and upper-secondary programming is language-formal: BNF metalanguage and syntax diagrams, ordinal types, type conversions, every concept given in Pascal for teaching and in C++ for a technology career.


Kazakhstan's own way (Kopeeva G5 Informatika): Grade 5 Informatika (the older, pre-"Digital Literacy" subject name) is built around Scratch as a "game programming environment": a whole chapter runs from a first program through blocks, animation graphics, sound effects and dialogue to linear/branching/cyclic commands; earlier chapters pair Internet safety with data protection, and the year ends with print-ready project work — a Word newspaper page with WordArt and columns, presented as slides.

Kazakhstan's own way (Mukhametzhanova G5 Informatika, 2017 edition): the 2017 Atamura Grade 5 book follows the older national programme shared with the Arman-PV book of the same year: computer safety, information and raster graphics, algorithms through labyrinths (real and virtual), a first Scratch program and "living graphics", and project work ending in a print-ready document; a full Scratch block dictionary is printed as an appendix (printed pp. 119–127). The book was written with Microsoft "Partnership in Education" material.

Kazakhstan's own way (Sagimbaeva G1 «Цифровая грамотность»): two cartoon guides (Arman and Saule, themselves first-graders) lead the year; lessons are told as a journey, QR codes open lesson videos, and from lesson 10 the class builds and programmes LEGO-style robots (windmill model, labyrinth runs).

Kazakhstan's own way (Grade 1 teacher guide «Цифровая грамотность. Методическое руководство» 2023 (Russian + Kazakh editions)): the 2023 guide restructures Grade 1 against the 2021 student book (Book 5): robotics disappears, replaced by a drawing section («Мой первый рисунок», taught in Tux Paint in the Kazakh plans) and a separate algorithms section (concept of algorithm, executors and their command systems, kinds of algorithms) before Scratch programming.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kopeeva G5 «Информатика»): the year runs safety → internet safety → information and its processing → a long Scratch chapter, and it closes not with a test but with two production projects: a printed newspaper page made in a word processor (page setup, columns, WordArt, page numbers) and a defended Scratch project published to the Scratch community site.
