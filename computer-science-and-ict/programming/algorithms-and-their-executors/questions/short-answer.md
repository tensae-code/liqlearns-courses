# Algorithms and Their Executors — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an executor?**
**Answer: The one who carries out an algorithm's instructions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is an executor's command system?**
**Answer: The fixed set of commands it understands**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the books' two teaching executors.**
**Answer: The Kangaroo and the Ant**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is an instruction outside the executor's set?**
**Answer: Meaningless to it — the algorithm fails at that line**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name two other executors from the lesson.**
**Answer: A person following a recipe, and a computer running a program**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is manual control?**
**Answer: A human gives each command one by one, watching each result**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is program control?**
**Answer: The whole sequence is written in advance and the executor runs it alone**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which is faster and tireless?**
**Answer: Program control**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is program control's price?**
**Answer: Everything must be thought of beforehand — it is only as right as the written sequence**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does programming exist, in the lesson's terms?**
**Answer: It frees the human from commanding, step by step**
