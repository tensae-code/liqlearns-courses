# Scrolling Backgrounds That Wrap Around in Scratch — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is split into two sprites?" is: A scene wider than the stage**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How are the two pieces laid?" is: Left**
**Answer: False** — Edge to edge

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How much of the scene does the stage show?" is: One scene’s width of it**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which way do both sprites glide?" is: Its whole width gone off the left**
**Answer: False** — Left

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "When does a sprite jump back?" is: When its position passes a limit**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the limit moment?" is: It glides through again**
**Answer: False** — Its whole width gone off the left

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does it jump back to?" is: The right, behind its partner**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does it do after jumping back?" is: It never ends**
**Answer: False** — It glides through again

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do the two sprites do forever?" is: They leapfrog**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the result for the background?" is: Edge to edge**
**Answer: False** — It never ends
