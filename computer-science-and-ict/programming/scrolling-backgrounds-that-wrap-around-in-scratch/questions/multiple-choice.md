# Scrolling Backgrounds That Wrap Around in Scratch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is split into two sprites?**
a) A scene wider than the stage  b) Edge to edge  c) Left  d) Its whole width gone off the left
**Answer: a) A scene wider than the stage**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How are the two pieces laid?**
a) One scene’s width of it  b) Edge to edge  c) When its position passes a limit  d) The right, behind its partner
**Answer: b) Edge to edge**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How much of the scene does the stage show?**
a) Left  b) Its whole width gone off the left  c) One scene’s width of it  d) It glides through again
**Answer: c) One scene’s width of it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which way do both sprites glide?**
a) When its position passes a limit  b) The right, behind its partner  c) They leapfrog  d) Left
**Answer: d) Left**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When does a sprite jump back?**
a) When its position passes a limit  b) Its whole width gone off the left  c) It glides through again  d) It never ends
**Answer: a) When its position passes a limit**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the limit moment?**
a) The right, behind its partner  b) Its whole width gone off the left  c) They leapfrog  d) A scene wider than the stage
**Answer: b) Its whole width gone off the left**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where does it jump back to?**
a) It glides through again  b) It never ends  c) The right, behind its partner  d) Edge to edge
**Answer: c) The right, behind its partner**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does it do after jumping back?**
a) They leapfrog  b) A scene wider than the stage  c) One scene’s width of it  d) It glides through again
**Answer: d) It glides through again**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do the two sprites do forever?**
a) They leapfrog  b) It never ends  c) Edge to edge  d) Left
**Answer: a) They leapfrog**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the result for the background?**
a) A scene wider than the stage  b) It never ends  c) One scene’s width of it  d) When its position passes a limit
**Answer: b) It never ends**
