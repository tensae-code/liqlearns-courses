# Hands-on — Scrolling Backgrounds That Wrap Around in Scratch

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cut a scene in two
Take or draw a wide scene, split it into two sprites, and lay them edge to edge with no visible join.

## Leapfrog forever
Glide both sprites left with the jump-back rule, and run it for a full minute to prove the background never ends.
