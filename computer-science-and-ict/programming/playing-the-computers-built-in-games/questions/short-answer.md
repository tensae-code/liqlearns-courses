# Playing the Computer's Built-in Games — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are a computer's built-in games?**
**Answer: Games installed with the system itself, needing no disc or download**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is a built-in game opened?**
**Answer: From the Start menu or the games folder, by name**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Class IV meet games before Scratch?**
**Answer: A game is a program the pupil can already run and answer back**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What kinds of built-in games are common?**
**Answer: Card games, puzzle games and board games**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the program do when you play?**
**Answer: It answers every move you make**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where can a player read how a game works?**
**Answer: In the game's rules or help shown on the screen**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What tells the player how they did?**
**Answer: The score, or reaching the finish**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What real skills does playing practise?**
**Answer: Exact mouse and keyboard control, reading instructions and planning moves**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What care rules hold while playing?**
**Answer: Sit well, rest your eyes and stop at the agreed time**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How long should a game be played for?**
**Answer: For a set time agreed before starting**
