# Hands-on — Playing the Computer's Built-in Games

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Open and name', 'Open one built-in game, read its name and its rules page, and tell your companion what the player must do to win.')

## Task two
('Play with a plan', 'Play one short game and write the three moves you planned ahead — and whether the plan worked.')
