# Sources of Calculation Errors — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is model error?** → **The gap from the model's simplification — exact for the model, off for the world**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is input-data error?** → **The measurements' own errors, inherited by the whole computation**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Can more arithmetic wash out input error?** → **No — it is irreducible at the desk**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How are the first two sources shrunk?** → **Only by a better model or better measuring**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which sources are born before the machine?** → **Model error and input-data error**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is method error?** → **The approximation built into the numerical method — shrinking as the method refines**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is rounding error?** → **The machine's per-operation rounding, accumulating across billions of operations**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is rounding error reduced?** → **Wider types, and arranging operations kindly (smalls before larges)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is method error reduced, and at what price?** → **By refining — more steps, narrower intervals — at the price of more work**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) State the chapter's bookkeeping rule.** → **Name every answer's error sources before trusting its digits**
