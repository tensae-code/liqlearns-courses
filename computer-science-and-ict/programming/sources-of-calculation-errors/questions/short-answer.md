# Sources of Calculation Errors — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is model error?**
**Answer: The gap from the model's simplification — exact for the model, off for the world**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is input-data error?**
**Answer: The measurements' own errors, inherited by the whole computation**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Can more arithmetic wash out input error?**
**Answer: No — it is irreducible at the desk**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How are the first two sources shrunk?**
**Answer: Only by a better model or better measuring**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which sources are born before the machine?**
**Answer: Model error and input-data error**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is method error?**
**Answer: The approximation built into the numerical method — shrinking as the method refines**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is rounding error?**
**Answer: The machine's per-operation rounding, accumulating across billions of operations**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is rounding error reduced?**
**Answer: Wider types, and arranging operations kindly (smalls before larges)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is method error reduced, and at what price?**
**Answer: By refining — more steps, narrower intervals — at the price of more work**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) State the chapter's bookkeeping rule.**
**Answer: Name every answer's error sources before trusting its digits**
