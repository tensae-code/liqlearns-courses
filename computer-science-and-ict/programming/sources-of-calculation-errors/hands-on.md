# Hands-on — Sources of Calculation Errors

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Inquest** — A weather model predicts 21.4° and the day reaches 23°: distribute the gap among the four sources, arguing which could and could not have contributed.

## Task two
**Budget writing** — For a rectangle-method integral with measured bounds, write the error budget: one line per source, each marked reducible or not, and how.
