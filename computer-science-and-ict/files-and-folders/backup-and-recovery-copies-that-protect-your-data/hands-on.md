# Hands-on — Backup and recovery — copies that protect your data

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — on a real computer where the lesson needs one.

## The two lists
On a real computer you use, list the files whose loss would hurt most. Beside each, write where a copy could live. Star the ones with no possible home — those need a drive or a cloud account first.

## Danger drill
A partner calls one of the four dangers. You say, in one sentence, what happens to the original and what the backup does about it. All four dangers, both roles.
