# Video script — Backup and recovery — copies that protect your data

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your work matters. Keep a copy of it — somewhere else.

That copy is a **backup**: photos, records, assignments,
kept on a separate drive, or in the cloud.

Failure? Corruption? A virus? A mistaken delete?
The original may fall. The copy stands.

And **restoring** brings the copy back,
so work goes on from a safe point.

Now you. What will you back up? Where will it live?
