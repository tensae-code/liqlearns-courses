# Backup and recovery — copies that protect your data — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1.** A backup must be kept on a ______ system or medium from the original.
**Answer: separate**

**2.** Restoring lets work continue from an earlier, ______ point.
**Answer: safe**

**3.** Hardware or software ______, corruption, virus attack and accidental deletion are the named dangers.
**Answer: failure**
