# Backup and recovery — copies that protect your data — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** Photos, records, assignments and credentials are examples of what matters most in a backup.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Corruption is one of the dangers a backup protects against.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Restoring happens before any loss, in quiet times.
**Answer: False — restoring is spent after loss; backup is made in quiet times**
