# Video script — Import and export — data in, data out

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Data travels two ways.

**Import** — bringing information IN to a program.
A CSV file comes into the spreadsheet.

**Export** — sending information OUT into a file.
The spreadsheet goes out as CSV again.

Export lives in the **File** menu. You choose the format.
**.txt … .bmp … .jpg … .gif.**

Now you. In, or out? Import, or export? Say it.
