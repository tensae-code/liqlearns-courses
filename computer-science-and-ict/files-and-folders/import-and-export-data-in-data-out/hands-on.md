# Hands-on — Import and export — data in, data out

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — on a real computer where the lesson needs one.

## CSV round trip
Write a three-line CSV on paper — name,mark with commas. Type it into a spreadsheet by importing or by hand, change a mark, then write out by hand the CSV the export would produce. Compare with a real export if a computer is near.

## Direction calling
A partner names eight data journeys ('photos from a phone into the computer', 'a sheet saved out as .txt'…). You call 'import' or 'export' for each, from the program's point of view they name.
