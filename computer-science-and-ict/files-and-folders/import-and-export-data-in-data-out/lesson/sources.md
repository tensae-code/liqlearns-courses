Cuba's own way (Grade 5): The Grade 5 programme is built around extracting information from Cuban educational software, encyclopedias and EcuRed into word-processor documents, then Scratch — drawing/animation first (Unit 3), control/movement/appearance blocks in Unit 4. (teacher programme — evidence)

Cuba's own way (Grade 5): Same Grade 5 content as the Programa, recast as per-unit teaching tables with hour allocations; Unit 4 is renamed “Aprendiendo y creando” and taught purely as Scratch control, movement and appearance blocks. (teacher guide — evidence)

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 6 G6 teacher): The guide reorganises the student book's 24 §s into 13 standard themes (6-1 to 6-13), each with lesson topic, outcome codes, cross-cutting competencies and practical work; it closes with a thematic plan, a worked sample lesson plan (§3) and slide-presentation assessment rubrics.

Indonesia's own way (Indonesia Grade 10 G10 Informatika SMK Sem1): The SMK Semester 1 book teaches the same Informatika elements with a logic-first opening (propositions and inference), applied office integration, hands-on network-device configuration, and an OS-installation practical.

South Sudan's own way (Grade 10 S2 Student): Year 2 moves from single-computer skills to connected and analytical work — networks taught through intranet file-sharing and a full Microsoft Outlook unit (contacts, calendar, meetings, task lists), then a deep Excel unit that goes well beyond basics into lookup/financial/text functions, inventories, correlation and regression, what-if analysis with Solver, and workbook protection; social media is taught platform by platform with its ethical and legal issues.

South Sudan's own way (Grade 12 S4 Teacher): The guide mirrors the student book's three units with teaching sequences, observation/conversation assessment points and worked projects — designing a database for a local medical practice, asking how books are managed in a school library, and a capstone project to design the school's own website, judged on navigation and usability. (teacher guide — evidence)
