# Video script — Common file extensions — the full table

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ten endings. Ten kinds of file.

**.txt** — simple text. **.pdf** — a portable document.
**.ppt** — a presentation. **.mdb** — a database.
**.zip** — squeezed small. **.exe** — a program.
**.mp3** and **.wav** — sound.

And remember: DOS allows three letters. Windows, three or four.
Unix-based systems — as long as you like.

Now you. Extension? Kind of file? Say both.
