# Lesson plan — Hard disk, optical drive and flash drive

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Files and Folders · **Lesson:** Hard disk, optical drive and flash drive
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Organization of Files — in the title Files and Folders on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Three kinds of drive: the hard disk drive (usually C:) — the main, quick-access store; the CD/DVD optical drive — for discs that play music and films and store files; and the small, portable USB flash drive for storage, backup and carrying files.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Drives — where files live: the learner knows what a drive is; now the three kinds they will actually meet.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the hard disk drive's usual letter and job: C:, the main, quick-access store for data and programs.
2. Say what the CD/DVD (optical) drive does: plays music and films, stores files on discs — often D: or G:.
3. Say what the USB flash drive is for: storage, backup and carrying files between devices.
4. Split drives into fixed (inside the computer) and removable (portable, plugged in externally).

## If the learner is stuck
Gives the flash drive letter C: → C: is the hard disk, the main store; a flash drive takes a letter when plugged in, and it is the removable one. Mixes the optical drive with the disc → the drive is the machine part; the CD/DVD is the disc it plays and stores on.
