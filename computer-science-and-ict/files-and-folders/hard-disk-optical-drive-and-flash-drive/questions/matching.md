# Hard disk, optical drive and flash drive — matching

Match each item on the left with the right item on the right.

**1. Match the drive with its job.
1. Hard disk — a) plays music and films from discs
2. Optical drive — b) main, quick-access store for data and programs
3. Flash drive — c) storage, backup, carrying files between devices
**Answer: 1—b, 2—a, 3—c**
