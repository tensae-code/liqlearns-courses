# Hard disk, optical drive and flash drive — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The hard disk drive is usually —**
a) C:  b) G:  c) F:
**Answer: a) C:** — the main, quick-access store.

**2. The optical drive is used with —**
a) flash sticks  b) CDs and DVDs  c) printers
**Answer: b) CDs and DVDs** — it plays music and films and stores files on discs.

**3. A USB flash drive plugs into —**
a) the monitor  b) a USB port  c) the optical drive
**Answer: b) a USB port**

**4. Which drive is removable and portable?**
a) the hard disk  b) the USB flash drive  c) neither
**Answer: b) the USB flash drive**
