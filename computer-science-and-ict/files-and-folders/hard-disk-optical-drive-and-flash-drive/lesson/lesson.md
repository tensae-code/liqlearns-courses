# Hard disk, optical drive and flash drive

## Do it first

Your files live on drives. But drives come in kinds: one never leaves the computer, one reads shining discs, one fits in a pocket. Before they are named, list every device you know that can hold a file or carry one to another computer. Then meet the book's three kinds, and sort your list into them.

## Kinds of drive

**The hard disk drive** — usually **C:** — is the **main, quick-access store** for data and programs. It is where the operating system, your programs and your saved work live day to day.

**The CD/DVD drive** — the **optical** drive, often **D:** or **G:** — **plays music and films** and **stores files on discs**.

**The USB flash drive** — the **memory stick** — is the **small portable device that plugs into a USB port**, used for **storage, backup and carrying files between devices**.

[Picture 12552 — the three drives side by side with their jobs captioned]

And one split runs across all drives: **fixed drives** live **inside** the computer; **removable** drives are **portable** and **plugged in externally**. The hard disk is fixed. The flash drive is removable. The disc in an optical drive is carried in and out, though the drive itself sits in the machine.
