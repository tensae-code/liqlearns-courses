# Video script — Hard disk, optical drive and flash drive

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three kinds of **drive**.

The **hard disk** — usually **C:**.
The main store. Quick to reach. Data and programs live here.

The **optical drive** — CD and DVD, often D: or G:.
It plays music and films. It stores files on discs.

The **flash drive** — the memory stick.
Small. Portable. Plugs into USB.
Storage. Backup. Carrying files.

Fixed inside — or removable in your pocket. Know your drives.
