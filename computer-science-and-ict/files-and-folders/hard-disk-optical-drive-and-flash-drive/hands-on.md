# Hands-on — Hard disk, optical drive and flash drive

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Hold the kinds
If a flash drive and a disc are at hand, hold each and give its kind, its job and whether it is fixed or removable. Then point at the computer and name the drive you cannot hold — the fixed hard disk inside.

## Carry test
Copy a small practice file onto a flash drive, carry it to another computer (or another port), and open it there. Say which kind of drive made the journey possible, and why the hard disk could not.
