# Video script — Making a picture in Paint

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A file can be a **picture**.

Programs for pictures: **Paint**. Paint 3D. GIMP. Photoshop.
Paint is already in Windows.

**Start**. Open **Paint**.
Draw your own image.
Fill it with colour.
**Save it.**

Now your drawing is a file — like any other.
