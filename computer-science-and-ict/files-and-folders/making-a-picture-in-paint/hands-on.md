# Hands-on — Making a picture in Paint

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Draw and save for real
Do the practical: open Paint, draw a picture of something Ethiopian — a jebena, a mesob, the flag — fill it with colour, and save it into your folder. Find it afterwards in File Explorer.

## Four programs hunt
Write the four program names on cards: Paint, Paint 3D, GIMP, Adobe Photoshop. Mark the card of the one built into Windows. Say what all four can do: view, edit and create images.
