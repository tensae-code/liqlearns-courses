# Making a picture in Paint

## Do it first

Documents hold words. Text files hold plain words. What holds a drawing? Try now: find any program on this computer that lets you draw a shape and fill it with colour. However far you get, this lesson walks the book's own route — with the program Windows itself carries.

## Making a picture in Paint

Images can be viewed, edited and created in programs such as **Microsoft Paint**, **Paint 3D**, **GIMP** and **Adobe Photoshop**. **Paint** is Windows' built-in one — it is already on the computer, waiting.

The practical, in the book's order:

1. Click **Start** and open **Paint**.
2. **Create your own image** — draw it yourself.
3. **Save it.** The drawing is now an image file, like any other file in this unit.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the four image programs the book names, and which one is built into Windows. Then tell the practical's steps in order: Start, open Paint, create, save.

## Do It Yourself

1. Open Paint. Draw a simple picture of your own — a house, a flag, a coffee cup.
2. Fill one part of your drawing with colour.
3. Save the picture into a folder and say its full name, extension included.
