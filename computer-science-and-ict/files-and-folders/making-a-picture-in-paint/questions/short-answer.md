# Making a picture in Paint — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. Name two image programs besides Paint from the book's list.**
**Answer: Any two of: Paint 3D, GIMP, Adobe Photoshop.**

**2. Why does the practical end with saving?**
**Answer: Saving turns the drawing into an image file that is kept on the computer; unsaved, it is not yet a file.**
