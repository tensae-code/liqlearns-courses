# Video script — File Organisation Methods — Sequential, Serial, Indexed and Random

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Where a record sits decides how fast you reach it.

Serial: as they arrive. Sequential: sorted, read in order. Indexed: sorted, with a map for jumping.

Random: the key itself calculates the address. One jump — that is what a cash machine uses.
