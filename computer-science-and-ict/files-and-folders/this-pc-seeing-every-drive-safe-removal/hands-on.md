# Hands-on — This PC — seeing every drive; safe removal

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The guide's model copy off a stick (teacher guide — evidence)
Follow the guide's keyed Practical Exercise 1.6: copy files off a USB flash drive through This PC using the Home tab's 'Copy to' button, into Documents (teacher guide — evidence). Eject the stick safely when the copy is done.

## Eject drill
Insert a flash drive, open one file from it, close the file, then eject by the book's order and remove it. Repeat once more, saying each step aloud as you do it.
