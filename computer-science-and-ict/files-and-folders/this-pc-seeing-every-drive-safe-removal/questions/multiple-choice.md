# This PC — seeing every drive; safe removal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. All mounted drives are viewed by choosing —**
a) This PC in the left pane  b) the Recycle Bin  c) Quick Access only
**Answer: a) This PC in the left pane** — the drives appear on the right.

**2. Before removing a flash drive you must first —**
a) rename it  b) right-click it and choose Disconnect/Eject  c) delete its files
**Answer: b) right-click it and choose Disconnect/Eject**

**3. Pulling a flash drive straight out can —**
a) charge it  b) damage the files on it  c) rename it
**Answer: b) damage the files on it**

**4. A flash drive can also be renamed from —**
a) the same right-click menu  b) the Search box  c) another computer
**Answer: a) the same right-click menu**
