# Video script — This PC — seeing every drive; safe removal

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

See **every drive** at once.

**Windows key + E.** File Explorer.
Click **This PC**, left side.
The drives stand on the right.

Finished with a flash drive? Do **not** just pull it.
**Right-click** the drive. **Disconnect / Eject**.
Wait for the safe moment. **Then** pull.

Eject first — or the files can be damaged.
