# Lesson plan — Why we organise files into folders

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Files and Folders · **Lesson:** Why we organise files into folders
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Organization of Files — in the title Files and Folders on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Folders keep related files organised and easy to locate — without them everything sits in one place and every file would need its own unique name.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on What a file is and Folders — containers for files: the learner knows both things, and now learns why the container matters.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say why folders matter: related files stay organised and easy to locate.
2. Say what goes wrong with no folders: everything in one place, and every file needing a unique name.
3. Explain how the same filename — My-letter.doc — can live in two different folders.

## What you need
A computer desktop to look at, tidy or cluttered. Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
Thinks folders are decoration → set the search test: one named file, cluttered desktop against foldered desktop. Thinks two files can never share a name → the rule is per place: unique inside its folder, free across folders.
