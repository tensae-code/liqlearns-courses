# Hands-on — Why we organise files into folders

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The spill test
Write twenty file names on slips and spill them on a table. Time how long it takes a partner to find one named slip. Then sort the slips into three labelled envelopes and time the same search. Compare the two times aloud.

## Same name, two homes
Make two paper folders. In each, put a slip named My-letter.doc — one a letter home, one a letter to a school. Show a partner both and explain why the computer allows it.
