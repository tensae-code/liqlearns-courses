# Video script — Why we organise files into folders

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

No folders? Everything in one place.
Hundreds of files. One long spill.
Find one letter in that. Hard.

Folders fix it.
Related files sit together.
You go straight to the right folder.

And here is the trick.
Without folders, every name must be different.
With folders, **My-letter.doc** can live in two folders at once.
The name only has to be new inside its own folder.

Organised. Easy to find. That is why folders matter.
