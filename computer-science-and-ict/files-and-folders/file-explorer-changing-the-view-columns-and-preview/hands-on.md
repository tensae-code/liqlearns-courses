# Hands-on — File Explorer — changing the view, columns and preview

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## View tour
Take one real folder through all eight views, hovering to preview each. For three of the views, say one job that view is best for — pictures in Extra-large icons, sizes and dates in Details, a long name list in List.

## Preview without opening
With the Preview pane on, sort a folder's files by what is inside them — read five files through the pane and never open one. Say what the pane showed for each.
