# Hands-on — File conversion — changing a file's format

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — on a real computer where the lesson needs one.

## The Dam paragraph conversion
Open Word, write one page about the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam, save it under the name Ethiopia, then convert it to PDF (teacher guide — evidence: the guide's own practical). Open both files and compare them.

## Zoom test
Open any photograph and zoom to the largest size. Look for the squares of the pixels. Then look at a drawn shape or logo and zoom again — smooth or squares? Say which kind each one is.
