# Video script — File conversion — changing a file's format

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Same words. New wrapping. That is **conversion**.

A Word file will not work in your program?
Convert it. **.doc** in — **.pdf** out.

A converter works in three steps.
**Upload** the file. **Choose** the format. **Convert**.

And pictures come in two builds.
**Vector** — shapes, smooth at any size.
**Bitmap** — pixels, blocky when you zoom.

Now you. Upload. Choose. Convert.
