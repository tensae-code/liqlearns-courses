# Video script — The Navigation pane — expanding the folder tree

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Left side of the window: the **Navigation pane**. The folder tree.
Sections: **Quick Access**. OneDrive. **This PC**. Network.

Read the triangles.
No triangle: no subfolders.
Triangle pointing right: subfolders **hidden**.
Triangle pointing down: subfolders **shown**.
Click — it flips.

A folder you use every day? **Right-click — Pin to Quick access.**
Now it is always at hand.
