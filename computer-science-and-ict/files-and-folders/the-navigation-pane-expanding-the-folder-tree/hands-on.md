# Hands-on — The Navigation pane — expanding the folder tree

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Tree survey
Draw your own folder tree on paper — three main branches, subfolders under two of them. Mark each branch with the right triangle sign: right-pointing where subfolders are hidden in your drawing, down-pointing where you drew them out.

## Pin and prove
Pin the folder you use most to Quick access. Close File Explorer, reopen it, and reach the folder in one click from Quick access. Show a partner the whole proof.
