# Hands-on — File Explorer — naming its parts

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Label the window
Draw a big empty window on paper. Draw the nine parts in their places and label each one. Swap drawings with a partner and check each other's labels against a real File Explorer window.

## Part hunt
A partner calls a job — 'sort the list', 'find a file', 'go back'. You point at the part that does it, on screen or on your drawing. Ten jobs, then swap.
