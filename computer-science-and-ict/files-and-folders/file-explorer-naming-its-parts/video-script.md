# Video script — File Explorer — naming its parts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This window is **File Explorer**.
It has nine parts. Learn their names.

Left: the **Navigation pane**, with Quick Access.
Then: **Back and Forward** buttons.
Across the top: the **Ribbon**.
The **Address bar** — it shows where you are.
**Column headings** — click one, the list sorts.
The **Search box** — type a name, find a file.
The big middle: the **file and folder listing**.
The side pane: **Preview and Details**.
At the foot: the **Status bar**.

Nine parts. Now you can name the window.
