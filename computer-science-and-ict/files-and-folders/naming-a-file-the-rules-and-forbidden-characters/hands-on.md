# Hands-on — Naming a file — the rules and the forbidden characters

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — on a real computer where the lesson needs one.

## Name court
Write ten filenames on cards — five valid, five carrying one forbidden character each. A partner judges each card and must point at the offending character. Shuffle and swap.

## Rename for real
On a real computer, find three badly named files ('new', 'work2', 'stuff'). Propose a better, valid name for each — contents reflected, nothing forbidden — and rename one of them.
