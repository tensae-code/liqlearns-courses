# Video script — Naming a file — the rules and the forbidden characters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A good name says what is inside.
**assignment.doc. memo.xls. report.pdf.** All valid.

Letters, numbers, spaces — allowed.
Some special characters — allowed.

But these are **forbidden**: star, bar, question mark,
slash, equals, quotes — and their row of friends.

**io*asst.doc**? Invalid. The star must go.

Now you. Judge the name. Fix the name.
