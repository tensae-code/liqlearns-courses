# Naming a file — the rules and the forbidden characters — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1.** The name first? name.xls is invalid because ______ is forbidden.
**Answer: ? (the question mark)**

**2.** Letters, ______ and spaces are allowed in a filename.
**Answer: numbers (0–9)**

**3.** A good filename ______ its contents.
**Answer: reflects**
