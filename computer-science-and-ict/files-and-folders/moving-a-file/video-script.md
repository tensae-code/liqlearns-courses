# Video script — Moving a file

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Moving** keeps one copy. Only one.

Way one: **cut and paste**.
Cut — right-click, Cut. Or Home — Cut. Or **Ctrl+X**.
The file waits on the clipboard.
Open the new folder. **Paste — Ctrl+V**. Done. The old place is empty.

Way two: **drag and drop**.
Both windows open. Drag with the **right** button.
Let go — choose **Move here**.

Way three: **Move to Folder**.
Home — Move to Folder. Browse. Click **Move**.

Three ways. One copy. That is moving.
