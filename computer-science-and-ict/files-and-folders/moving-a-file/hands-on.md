# Hands-on — Moving a file

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The guide's model move (teacher guide — evidence)
Finish the guide's model run: in Test2 write the school's name into testfile2.txt, then cut-and-paste testfile2 into Test1, and rename it testfile3.txt there (teacher guide — evidence). Check both folders when you finish.

## Three ways, three files
Move three practice files, one by each method. After each move, verify with your eyes: present in the new folder, absent from the old.
