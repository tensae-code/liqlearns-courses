# Finding and Saving Pictures from the Internet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the picture search filtered by?**
a) Type, colour and transparency  b) Still or animated  c) A see-through background where one is needed  d) “Save image as”
**Answer: a) Type, colour and transparency**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two types does the type filter offer?**
a) So the picture found is the picture needed  b) Still or animated  c) Filtering — picking your picture from every picture  d) The computer’s files
**Answer: b) Still or animated**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why filter the search?**
a) A see-through background where one is needed  b) “Save image as”  c) So the picture found is the picture needed  d) As a sprite with many costumes
**Answer: c) So the picture found is the picture needed**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a transparent background give a project?**
a) Filtering — picking your picture from every picture  b) The computer’s files  c) One frame of the animation  d) A see-through background where one is needed
**Answer: d) A see-through background where one is needed**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the skill of the lesson?**
a) Filtering — picking your picture from every picture  b) “Save image as”  c) As a sprite with many costumes  d) A plane, on a runway
**Answer: a) Filtering — picking your picture from every picture**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What command saves the found picture?**
a) The computer’s files  b) “Save image as”  c) One frame of the animation  d) Type, colour and transparency
**Answer: b) “Save image as”**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where is the picture saved to?**
a) As a sprite with many costumes  b) A plane, on a runway  c) The computer’s files  d) Still or animated
**Answer: c) The computer’s files**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does an animated GIF arrive in Scratch?**
a) One frame of the animation  b) Type, colour and transparency  c) So the picture found is the picture needed  d) As a sprite with many costumes
**Answer: d) As a sprite with many costumes**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is one GIF costume?**
a) One frame of the animation  b) A plane, on a runway  c) Still or animated  d) A see-through background where one is needed
**Answer: a) One frame of the animation**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the project land, and on what?**
a) Type, colour and transparency  b) A plane, on a runway  c) So the picture found is the picture needed  d) Filtering — picking your picture from every picture
**Answer: b) A plane, on a runway**
