# Finding and Saving Pictures from the Internet — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the picture search filtered by?**
**Answer: Type, colour and transparency**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two types does the type filter offer?**
**Answer: Still or animated**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why filter the search?**
**Answer: So the picture found is the picture needed**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a transparent background give a project?**
**Answer: A see-through background where one is needed**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the skill of the lesson?**
**Answer: Filtering — picking your picture from every picture**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What command saves the found picture?**
**Answer: “Save image as”**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where is the picture saved to?**
**Answer: The computer’s files**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does an animated GIF arrive in Scratch?**
**Answer: As a sprite with many costumes**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is one GIF costume?**
**Answer: One frame of the animation**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the project land, and on what?**
**Answer: A plane, on a runway**
