# Hands-on — Creating and saving a document file

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Make the real document
Do the practical for real: open WordPad, MS Word or LibreOffice Writer, title the document Ethiopian Cultural Foods, and type a paragraph from what you know and what you can ask at home. Save it with Save As into your own folder.

## Save or Save As cards
Write six cards: 'brand-new file', 'same file, new name', 'one line changed in a saved file', 'same file, different folder', 'first saving ever', 'daily update'. Sort the cards into two piles — Save and Save As — and say the rule that sorted them.
