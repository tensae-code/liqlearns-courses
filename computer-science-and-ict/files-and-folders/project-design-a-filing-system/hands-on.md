# Hands-on — Project: design a filing system

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Case 1 on paper, Case 2 for real
Draw Case 1 — the record officer's drive — as a visual tool with its folders and contents listed. Then draw, standardise and really build Case 2 — your own Grade 9 subjects — in File Explorer.

## Show it
Present the built system to your teacher or to the class: open This PC, walk the tree, open two folders and show the right files inside, and read your naming standard aloud.
