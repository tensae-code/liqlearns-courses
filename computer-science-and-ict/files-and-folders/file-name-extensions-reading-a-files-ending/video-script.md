# Video script — File name extensions — reading a file's ending

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at a file's name. Do not open it yet.

See the ending? The letters after the last full stop?
That ending is the **extension**. It tells you the kind of file.

**photo.jpg** — a picture. **letter.docx** — a Word document.
**song.mp3** — music. **marks.xlsx** — a spreadsheet.

And when you double-click, the computer reads that ending first —
and picks the right program for you.

Now you. Read the ending. Name the file.
