# Video script — Navigating with the Address bar

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The **Address bar** shows your whole road — the **path** of the folder you are in.

Click a name in the bar: you jump there.
**Back** and **Forward**: folders you already visited.

See the small arrow beside a name?
Click it. It turns down. The folder's subfolders appear.
Click one — you are inside it.

No opening folder after folder. Travel by the bar.
