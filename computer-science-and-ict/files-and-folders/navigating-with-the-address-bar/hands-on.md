# Hands-on — Navigating with the Address bar

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Count the clicks
Travel from a deep subfolder to the top folder twice: once by closing and reopening folders, once by the Address bar. Count clicks both ways and say the saving aloud.

## Path dictation
A partner reads a path aloud — drive, folder, subfolder — while you travel by the Address bar to land in exactly that folder. Three paths, then swap.
