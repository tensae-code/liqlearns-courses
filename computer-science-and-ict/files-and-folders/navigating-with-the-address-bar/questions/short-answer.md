# Navigating with the Address bar — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. Give two ways to return to a folder you visited before.**
**Answer: Click its name in the Address bar, or use the Back/Forward buttons.**

**2. Why is the Address bar faster than opening folders one by one?**
**Answer: The whole path is shown in one line — you click the folder you want, or open a drop-down and jump straight into a subfolder.**
