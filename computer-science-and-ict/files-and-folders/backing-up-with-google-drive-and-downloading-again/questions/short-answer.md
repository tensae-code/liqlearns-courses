# Backing Up with Google Drive — and Downloading Again — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is Google Drive, in this lesson?**
**Answer: Google's online storage, where backup copies are kept away from the computer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does uploading mean here?**
**Answer: Copying files from the computer to Google Drive over the internet**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to the originals when you upload?**
**Answer: They stay on the computer — Drive holds a copy**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why watch an upload until it finishes?**
**Answer: A half-uploaded backup is a backup of half the work**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does Drive show as the check a file arrived?**
**Answer: Each file listed with its name and size**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is downloading?**
**Answer: Bringing a file back from Drive to the computer**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where can the return journey start from?**
**Answer: Anywhere you can sign in to the account**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the downloaded file become?**
**Answer: A working file again on the computer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does Class VII practise in the same sitting?**
**Answer: Uploading, then downloading again and opening the returned file**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why practise the return at once?**
**Answer: To have proof, not hope, that the backup works**
