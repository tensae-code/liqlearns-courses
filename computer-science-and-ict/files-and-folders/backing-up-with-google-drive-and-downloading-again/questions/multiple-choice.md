# Backing Up with Google Drive — and Downloading Again — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is Google Drive, in this lesson?**
a) Google's online storage, where backup copies are kept away from the computer  b) Copying files from the computer to Google Drive over the internet  c) They stay on the computer — Drive holds a copy  d) A half-uploaded backup is a backup of half the work
**Answer: a) Google's online storage, where backup copies are kept away from the computer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does uploading mean here?**
a) They stay on the computer — Drive holds a copy  b) Copying files from the computer to Google Drive over the internet  c) A half-uploaded backup is a backup of half the work  d) Each file listed with its name and size
**Answer: b) Copying files from the computer to Google Drive over the internet**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to the originals when you upload?**
a) A half-uploaded backup is a backup of half the work  b) Each file listed with its name and size  c) They stay on the computer — Drive holds a copy  d) Bringing a file back from Drive to the computer
**Answer: c) They stay on the computer — Drive holds a copy**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why watch an upload until it finishes?**
