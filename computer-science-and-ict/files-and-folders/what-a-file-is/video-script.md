# Video script — What a file is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Six units. One road.

First: **Organization of Files** — your files, kept tidy.
Second: **Computer Network** — computers joined together.
Third: **Application Software** — Word, Excel, PowerPoint.
Fourth: **Image Processing and Multimedia** — pictures and sound.
Fifth: **Information and Computer Security** — keep it safe.
Sixth: **Fundamentals of Programming** — flowcharts, and your first scripts.

Every unit starts the same way: a brainstorm — what do you already know?
Then you practise. Then you review.

We start with files. A file is data, stored as one unit, under one name.
Look at its icon. The icon tells you its kind.

That is the road. Walk it with me.
