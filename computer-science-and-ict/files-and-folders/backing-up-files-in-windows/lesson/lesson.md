# Backing up files in Windows

## Do it first

You know why a copy kept apart protects your work. Now take one. If a Windows computer and an external drive are near you, do each step on the real machine as you read it — in this order, no step skipped.

## The steps, in order

[Picture 14112 — the backup strip]

[GIF 14123 — the backup click path]

1. **Plug the external storage device in** to the computer.
2. **Open Start and choose Settings** — or type "settings" in the search box.
3. **Choose Update and Security.**
4. **Click Backup** in the left column.
5. **Select the removable backup drive.**

That is the whole route. When the drive is selected, the files are copied to it. Unplug nothing until the copying is done, and afterwards open the external drive and **look**: your files should be standing there, on the separate drive, where a failure of the computer cannot reach them.

## Say it to your companion

Walk your companion through the five steps from memory — plug in, Settings, Update and Security, Backup, select the drive. They stop you the moment a step is missed or out of order.

## Do It Yourself

1. Take a real backup of one folder that matters, using the five steps.
2. Open the external drive afterwards and write down what you see there — the proof the copy exists.
3. Write the five steps on paper from memory. Check them against the picture strip.
