# Lesson plan — Backing up files in Windows

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Files and Folders · **Lesson:** Backing up files in Windows
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Organization of Files (Grade 10 depth) — in the title Files and Folders on the ICT course ladder (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a backup is taken in Windows, using an external storage device, in this order: plug the device in; open Start and choose Settings; choose Update and Security; click Backup in the left column; select the removable backup drive — and the files are copied to it.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Backup and recovery — copies that protect your data: the learner knows why; this doing-lesson walks the click path on a real machine, in the book's step order exactly.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Take a backup in Windows in the book's order, without missing a step.
2. Say where the copy ends up, and why the drive must be removable and separate.

## What you need
A Windows computer and an external storage device (external drive or USB drive).

## If the learner is stuck
- hunts for Backup on the main Settings page → Backup sits inside Update and Security, in its left column.
- picks the computer's own disk as the backup drive → the drive must be the removable external one; a backup sharing the machine's fate protects nothing (previous lesson).
