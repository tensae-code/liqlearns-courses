# Video script — Backing up files in Windows

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Time to take a real **backup**. Follow me, step by step.

One — **plug in** the external drive.
Two — open **Start**. Choose **Settings**.
Three — choose **Update and Security**.
Four — click **Backup**, in the left column.
Five — **select the removable backup drive**.

Now the files copy across. Wait until it finishes.
Open the drive. See your files? They are safe — kept apart.

Now you. Five steps. In order. Take your backup.
