# Hands-on — Data Exchange Formats

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**One table, two formats** — Write a 3-person class table (name, age, town) first as CSV, then as JSON. Say what each version makes easy and what it makes hard.

## Task two
**Tag it** — Write the same three people in XML with <person>, <name>, <age> and <town> tags. Check every opening tag has its closing tag.
