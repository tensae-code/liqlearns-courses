# Video script — Compressing and uncompressing files — zipping

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Too big to send? **Zip it.**

Compression shrinks the size — and keeps the same data.
Less disk space. Faster to travel.

Right-click the folder. **Send to.**
**Compressed (zipped) folder.** Done — same name, same place, smaller.

To open it back up: right-click — **Extract All**.
Or open it and drag one file out.

Now you. Zip it. Unzip it. Nothing lost.
