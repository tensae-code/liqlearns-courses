# Video script — Creating a new folder

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Make a **folder**. Five steps.

One: go where it should live.
Two: open the **Home** menu.
Three: click **New folder**.
Four: type its name.
Five: click outside. Done.

Place. Make. Name. Click away.
