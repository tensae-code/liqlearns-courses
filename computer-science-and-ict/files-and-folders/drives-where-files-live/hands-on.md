# Hands-on — Drives — where files live

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Two definitions on cards
Write 'drive' on one card: hardware that stores and retrieves. Write 'driver' on another: software that lets a device talk to the operating system. A partner holds up a case — 'the C: disk', 'the printer's helper program' — and you raise the right card.

## Load and save trace
Open a word processor and follow the book's example with your finger in the air: the program loads from the drive; you type; you save back to the drive. Say each leg of the journey aloud.

## Letter census (Grade 10 depth)
Open File Explorer (right-click Start, or This PC, or Windows key + E). List every drive letter present and what each drive is. Plug in a USB drive: which letter did it take? Say why.
