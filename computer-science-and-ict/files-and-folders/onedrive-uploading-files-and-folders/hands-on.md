# Hands-on — OneDrive — Uploading Files and Folders

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Up it goes', 'Upload one file and one folder to OneDrive (or walk the steps on paper). For each, write where it went and how you checked it arrived whole.')

## Task two
('Promise or copy?', 'Describe a half-finished upload a pupil might trust too early. Write the check that would have caught it.')
