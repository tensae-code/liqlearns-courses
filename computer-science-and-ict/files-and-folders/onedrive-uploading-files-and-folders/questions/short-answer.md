# OneDrive — Uploading Files and Folders — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is OneDrive?**
**Answer: Microsoft's cloud storage**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where do Kuwait's pupils meet OneDrive?**
**Answer: Inside Microsoft Teams**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does an upload do?**
**Answer: Copies a file or folder from your device up to the cloud storage**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Does uploading remove the original from the device?**
**Answer: No — the original stays; the cloud holds a copy**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) What can be uploaded in one move?**
**Answer: A single file, or a whole folder with everything inside it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When may an upload be trusted?**
**Answer: When it has finished**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a half-uploaded folder?**
**Answer: A promise, not a copy**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the closing check?**
**Answer: Open the cloud copy and see the work there, whole**

**9. (Sub lesson 1) From where is the cloud copy reachable?**
**Answer: Anywhere, any time, from any signed-in device**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 4 Digital Explorer Term 2, Unit 1 Lesson 3 — The Digital Cloud (2)**
