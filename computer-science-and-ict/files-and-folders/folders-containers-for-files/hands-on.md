# Hands-on — Folders — containers for files

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Dossier to folder
Take a real folder or envelope and put three papers about one subject inside it. That is a folder. Now put a smaller envelope, with one paper, inside the big one. That is a subfolder. Say the pair aloud: folder, subfolder.

## Sort the shelf
Write ten file names on slips of paper — letters, songs, photos, marks. Group the slips that belong together and put each group in its own envelope. Label each envelope. You have built folders with your hands.
