# Video script — Folders — containers for files

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

See this cover? A dossier.
Inside it: papers that belong together.

A computer **folder** is the same idea.
It holds files that belong together.

A folder inside a folder? A **subfolder**.

Remember the difference.
A **file** is the thing itself — the letter, the song.
A **folder** only holds things.
Files carry endings — .docx, .mp3.
Folders do not.

Paper can tear. Paper gets lost.
Files on a computer are easy to keep — and easy to carry.
