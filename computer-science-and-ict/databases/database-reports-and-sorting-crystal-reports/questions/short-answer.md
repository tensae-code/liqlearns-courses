# Database Reports and Sorting — Crystal Reports — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is Crystal Reports?**
**Answer: Visual Basic 2008's report builder — printed reports from the database**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a report lay out?**
**Answer: Title, headings, records in a designed order, totals where amounts run**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who is a report for?**
**Answer: Readers who never open the program**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Does a report hold its own data?**
**Answer: No — printing it tomorrow shows tomorrow's data in the same arrangement**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does sorting decide?**
**Answer: The order records are presented — by the field the reader thinks in**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Give two sorting examples.**
**Answer: Names alphabetical for a directory; dates in order for a log (also amounts largest-first)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does an unsorted report make its reader do?**
**Answer: Hunt**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Kuwait place this teaching?**
**Answer: Book 3, The Projects**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the report the program's public face?**
**Answer: It is the part the world actually receives**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 12 Information Technology Term 2, Book 3 Projects**
