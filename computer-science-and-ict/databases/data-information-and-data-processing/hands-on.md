# Hands-on — Data Information and Data Processing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Chapter 1 §1.1 Overview (data vs information; data operations — capturing, manipulation: classifying, calculations, sorting, summarizing; managing output — storing/retrieval, communication/reproduction) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach data information and data processing to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
