# Hands-on — Collecting and Preparing Data Surveys Cleaning Missing Values

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 5 §5.2 Data Collection and Preparation (§5.2.1 methods — surveys (an Islamabad grocery store), observations, experiments; §5.2.2 data preparation; §5.2.3 cleaning and transformation; §5.2.3.3 handling missing data — imputation, flagging, removal; class activity: survey classmates' homework hours and analyse them) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach collecting and preparing data surveys cleaning missing values to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
