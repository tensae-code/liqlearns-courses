# Forms for Entering and Viewing Data — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a form present?**
a) One record at a time, with labelled fields  b) No — it writes to the same table  c) Entering a new record  d) It follows the order a person thinks in
**Answer: a) One record at a time, with labelled fields**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Does a form store its own data?**
a) Entering a new record  b) No — it writes to the same table  c) It follows the order a person thinks in  d) A view and doorway onto the table
**Answer: b) No — it writes to the same table**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the blank position at the end allow?**
a) It follows the order a person thinks in  b) A view and doorway onto the table  c) Entering a new record  d) Types, rules and choice lists guide what can be entered
**Answer: c) Entering a new record**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How can a form's field order differ from the table's?**
a) A view and doorway onto the table  b) Types, rules and choice lists guide what can be entered  c) To stop spelling variants and invalid entries  d) It follows the order a person thinks in
**Answer: d) It follows the order a person thinks in**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a form, in the lesson's phrase?**
a) A view and doorway onto the table  b) Types, rules and choice lists guide what can be entered  c) To stop spelling variants and invalid entries  d) Refuse to be skipped
**Answer: a) A view and doorway onto the table**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How do forms protect data quality?**
a) To stop spelling variants and invalid entries  b) Types, rules and choice lists guide what can be entered  c) Refuse to be skipped  d) Forms are safer and friendlier for entry
**Answer: b) Types, rules and choice lists guide what can be entered**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why offer choices instead of free typing?**
a) Refuse to be skipped  b) Forms are safer and friendlier for entry  c) To stop spelling variants and invalid entries  d) Move from record to record
**Answer: c) To stop spelling variants and invalid entries**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can a required field do?**
a) Forms are safer and friendlier for entry  b) Move from record to record  c) One record at a time, with labelled fields  d) Refuse to be skipped
**Answer: d) Refuse to be skipped**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why are databases filled through forms, not grids?**
a) Forms are safer and friendlier for entry  b) Move from record to record  c) One record at a time, with labelled fields  d) No — it writes to the same table
**Answer: a) Forms are safer and friendlier for entry**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do navigation buttons do?**
a) One record at a time, with labelled fields  b) Move from record to record  c) No — it writes to the same table  d) Entering a new record
**Answer: b) Move from record to record**
