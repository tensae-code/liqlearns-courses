# Forms for Entering and Viewing Data — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a form present?" is: One record at a time, with labelled fields**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Does a form store its own data?" is: A view and doorway onto the table**
**Answer: False** — No — it writes to the same table

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the blank position at the end allow?" is: Entering a new record**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How can a form's field order differ from the table's?" is: To stop spelling variants and invalid entries**
**Answer: False** — It follows the order a person thinks in

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a form, in the lesson's phrase?" is: A view and doorway onto the table**
