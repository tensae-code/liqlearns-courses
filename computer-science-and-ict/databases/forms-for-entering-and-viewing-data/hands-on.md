# Hands-on — Forms for Entering and Viewing Data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Form on paper
Take a small table (students: name, class, mark). Draw its form: labels, boxes, order, navigation. Mark which fields you would give choice lists and say why.

## Grid versus form
Enter three new records on your paper form, then copy them into the table grid. Write two sentences on what the form made easier or safer.
