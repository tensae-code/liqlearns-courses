# Hands-on — Null — When a Value Is Missing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Blank audit** — Take a class register table design. Mark each field null-allowed or never-null, with one sentence of world-honesty for each decision.

## Task two
**Zero or null?** — For five blanks — no phone, unpaid fee, unmarked exam, no middle name, unknown birth date — decide zero, empty or null, and defend each.
