# Hands-on — Data Entry Forms in Access

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the counter
Create a form for an existing Access table with all its fields labelled and in a sensible order. Enter three records through the form, then open the table and confirm the records landed there.

## Walk the records
Using only the form, move through the records you entered, correct one field in an earlier record, and say why the form made the correction safer than editing the grid would have.
