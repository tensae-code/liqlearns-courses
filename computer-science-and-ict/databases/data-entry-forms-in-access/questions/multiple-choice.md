# Data Entry Forms in Access — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a form in Access?**
a) A layout showing one record at a time in labelled fields, for entering and reading data  b) Choosing the table and arranging its fields — which appear, in what order, with what labels  c) With the navigation controls, moving record by record  d) Makes correct entry the easy path
**Answer: a) A layout showing one record at a time in labelled fields, for entering and reading data**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is wrong with entering straight into a table?**
a) Moving to a new blank record and typing each field  b) The bare grid makes it easy to type into the wrong row or miss a field  c) People who use the database without needing to see a table  d) After linking tables — before queries
**Answer: b) The bare grid makes it easy to type into the wrong row or miss a field**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Does a form hold its own data?**
a) With the navigation controls, moving record by record  b) Makes correct entry the easy path  c) No — everything typed into it lands in the underlying table  d) Yes — navigate to it and edit its fields
**Answer: c) No — everything typed into it lands in the underlying table**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does creating a form involve?**
