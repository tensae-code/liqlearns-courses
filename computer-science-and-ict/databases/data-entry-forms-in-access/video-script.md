# Video script — Data Entry Forms in Access

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Entering data straight into a **table** means working in a grid of rows and columns where it is easy to type into the wrong row or miss a field.
A **form** presents **one record at a time**: each field with its label, in a chosen order and layout, with room for the person entering to see the whole record before saving it.
**Creating** a form means choosing the table it serves and arranging its fields: which fields appear, in what order, with what labels — by the program's form-building help or by placing fields by hand.
**Filling** it is the daily work: move to a new blank record, type each field, and move through records with the navigation controls to view or correct earlier ones.
