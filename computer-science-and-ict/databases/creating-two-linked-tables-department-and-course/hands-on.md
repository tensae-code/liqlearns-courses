# Hands-on — Creating two linked tables — DEPARTMENT and COURSE

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Build the pair
In Access SQL View, run both CREATE statements in the lesson's order. Open each table's design and confirm the keys.

## Break the match on purpose
Copy the COURSE statement with C_Department as CHAR(15). Predict what happens, then fix it back to CHAR(10) and say why the fix works.
