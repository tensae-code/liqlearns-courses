# Storage Architectures — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the simplest architecture?**
**Answer: Storage attached to the machine that uses it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Its flaw is what?**
**Answer: Stranded — others cannot reach the same data**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is network-attached storage?**
**Answer: Shelves on the network as a named place any authorised machine may reach**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does it kill?**
**Answer: Copies scattered per desk that disagree**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 11 Eisagogi Epistimis H/Y, Ch.3.2**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a storage area network?**
**Answer: Storage as its own dedicated network behind the servers, shared as pools at machine-room speed**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What can grow without servers stopping?**
**Answer: The storage pools**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Judge by need: the office's sharing takes which?**
**Answer: Network-attached storage**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) The institution whose data is its life takes which?**
**Answer: A storage area network**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What stands on these arrangements next?**
**Answer: The DBMS of the following sections**
