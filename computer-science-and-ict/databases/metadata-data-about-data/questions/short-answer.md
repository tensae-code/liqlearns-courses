Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) A school library has 5,000 books and no catalogue. Explain, using metadata, what the catalogue changes.**
**Answer: Without a catalogue, finding a book means opening books one by one. The catalogue is metadata — one record per book with title, author, year, subject — so questions are answered by filtering and sorting the records, and the right book is found in seconds.**
