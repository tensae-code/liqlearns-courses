# Video script — Metadata — Data about Data

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Metadata is data about data.

Take a book. Its story is the content. But there is a card about it: title, author, year, language, size. That card is metadata.

Some of it people write. Some of it machines write by themselves — like the record a camera hides inside a photo.

Why does it matter? Because nobody searches the ocean. They search the chart. Filters read the cards: only French, only 2020. Sorting reads the cards.

And an item with a missing or wrong card is lost in plain sight. It exists — and nobody can find it.
