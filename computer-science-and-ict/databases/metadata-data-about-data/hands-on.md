# Hands-on — Metadata — Data about Data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Write the cards
Choose five items in the room — books, bags, a phone. For each, write a metadata card with at least six entries, marking each D (descriptive) or A (administrative), and M (machine-written) or H (human-written). Then answer, from the cards alone: which item is the newest? Which are in French? Which is the largest?

## The lost item
Take one card away and shuffle. Now ask the same three questions. Write one sentence about what the missing card did to the collection — this is why databases guard metadata as carefully as content.
