# Hands-on — INSERT — adding a record to a table

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Fill the pair
Insert two DEPARTMENT records (form 2) and three COURSE records (form 1) into the standing tables. View the tables after each insert.

## Diagnosis drill (teacher guide — evidence)
Write three broken inserts on purpose — keyword with no space, counts mismatched, field names as values. Swap and repair with a partner.
