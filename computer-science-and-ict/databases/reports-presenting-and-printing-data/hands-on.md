# Hands-on — Reports — Presenting and Printing Data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Design a report
From a small table of library loans (borrower class, book, date), design a report grouped by class with a loan count per class and a grand total. Sketch the page layout with title and headings.

## Query or report?
For five needs (find one record; print the term's marks for parents; check today's entries; a grouped sales summary for a meeting; fix a wrong mark), say query, form or report — and why.
