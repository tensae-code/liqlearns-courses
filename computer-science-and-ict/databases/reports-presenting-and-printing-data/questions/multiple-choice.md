# Reports — Presenting and Printing Data — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a report's source?**
a) A table or a query  b) Title, headings, structure and finish  c) No — for viewing and printing  d) Its document layout — header and footer areas
**Answer: a) A table or a query**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a report add that a grid lacks?**
a) No — for viewing and printing  b) Title, headings, structure and finish  c) Its document layout — header and footer areas  d) People who read the data without touching the database
**Answer: b) Title, headings, structure and finish**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Are reports for editing data?**
a) Its document layout — header and footer areas  b) People who read the data without touching the database  c) No — for viewing and printing  d) Records gathered under group headings
**Answer: c) No — for viewing and printing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What carries page numbers and titles in a report?**
a) People who read the data without touching the database  b) Records gathered under group headings  c) A subtotal per group and a grand total  d) Its document layout — header and footer areas
**Answer: d) Its document layout — header and footer areas**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Who are reports made for?**
a) People who read the data without touching the database  b) Records gathered under group headings  c) A subtotal per group and a grand total  d) The pivot table idea
**Answer: a) People who read the data without touching the database**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is grouping in a report?**
a) A subtotal per group and a grand total  b) Records gathered under group headings  c) The pivot table idea  d) The design choices — groups, totals, page fit
**Answer: b) Records gathered under group headings**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What totals can a grouped report show?**
a) The pivot table idea  b) The design choices — groups, totals, page fit  c) A subtotal per group and a grand total  d) The preview of the pages
**Answer: c) A subtotal per group and a grand total**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the printed cousin of grouping with totals?**
a) The design choices — groups, totals, page fit  b) The preview of the pages  c) A table or a query  d) The pivot table idea
**Answer: d) The pivot table idea**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the report-maker's real work?**
a) The design choices — groups, totals, page fit  b) The preview of the pages  c) A table or a query  d) Title, headings, structure and finish
**Answer: a) The design choices — groups, totals, page fit**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What should you check before printing?**
a) A table or a query  b) The preview of the pages  c) Title, headings, structure and finish  d) No — for viewing and printing
**Answer: b) The preview of the pages**
