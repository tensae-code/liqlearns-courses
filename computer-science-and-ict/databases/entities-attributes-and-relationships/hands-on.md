# Hands-on — Entities, attributes and relationships

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Model your school bag
Pick three entities in your school life — pupil, book, teacher. For each, write four attributes. Then write one relationship sentence for a pair of them, with the verb underlined.

## Instance hunt
Take the entity “city”. Write down five instances — five real named cities. Then take “student” and write the attributes that tell one instance from another in your class.
