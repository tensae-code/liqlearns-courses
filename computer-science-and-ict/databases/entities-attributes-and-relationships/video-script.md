# Video script — Entities, attributes and relationships

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script — the placements name no full video for this lesson.

---

Before you build a database, you draw its plan. The plan is called a data model.

In the plan there are things. A customer. An account. A book. Things data is kept about — entities.

Each thing has properties. A customer has an ID, a name, an address — attributes.

And things connect. A customer *has* an account. The verb *has* is the relationship.

Thing. Properties. Connection. Entity, attribute, relationship.
