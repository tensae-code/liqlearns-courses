# Entities, attributes and relationships — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the entities a bank keeps data about, from the book.
**Answer:** Customers and accounts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is an instance? Give the book's kind of example.
**Answer:** A single occurrence of an entity — for example, one named city.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write a relationship sentence joining *borrower* and *book*, and name its verb.
**Answer:** “A borrower borrows a book” — the verb is *borrows*.
