# Hands-on — Reports from a Database in Access

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Report for a reader
Create a report from a table or query you already have: a title, the fields a reader needs, records grouped in a way the reader thinks in, and a total if amounts are involved. Print or preview it.

## Read it as the reader
Hand your report (or its preview) to a partner who has not seen the database. Ask them one question answerable from the report and one that is not; note what the arrangement helped and what it hid.
