# Video script — Reports from a Database in Access

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Tables hold data, queries find it, forms enter it — a **report** presents it.
A report takes records (from a table, or better, from a **query** that already chose them) and lays them out as a **document**: a title, headings, the records in a chosen order, often **grouped** — all the members of one class together, all the sales of one month together — with group and final **totals** where amounts are involved.
**Creating** one means choosing the source records, the fields to show, the grouping and order, and the layout — usually starting from the program's report-building help and then adjusting.
A report is a **view, not a copy**: it holds no data of its own, so printing it again tomorrow shows tomorrow's data in the same arrangement.
