# Data Integrity, Consistency and Redundancy — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does data integrity mean?**
**Answer: The data is correct and complete — true values entered under rules**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Give a rule integrity is kept by.**
**Answer: A mark between 0 and 100 (also: a date that exists)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does data consistency mean?**
**Answer: The database never disagrees with itself — the same fact reads the same everywhere**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does integrity guard, and what does consistency guard?**
**Answer: Integrity guards each value; consistency guards the agreement between values**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the pair Malaysia teaches together here.**
**Answer: Integrity and consistency — with redundancy, the three chapter-opening words**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is redundancy?**
**Answer: Storing the same fact in more than one place**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is redundancy's deeper harm?**
**Answer: The copies can drift apart — update one, miss another, and consistency breaks**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is Malaysia's design answer?**
**Answer: Store each fact once, in its own table, and refer to it by a key**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Is all copying always forbidden?**
**Answer: No — sometimes accepted knowingly for speed, as a chosen trade**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must copying never be?**
**Answer: An accident of design**
