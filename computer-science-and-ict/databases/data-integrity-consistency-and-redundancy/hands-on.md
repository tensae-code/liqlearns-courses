# Hands-on — Data Integrity, Consistency and Redundancy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Find the drift', 'A library file keeps each borrower's address in the borrower table and again on every loan record. A borrower moves and 2 of 5 records are updated. State which of the three words names each fault in this story, and redesign so the address is stored once.')

## Task two
('Write the rules', 'For a marks database, write three integrity rules (value ranges or required fields) and one consistency rule that two tables must agree on.')
