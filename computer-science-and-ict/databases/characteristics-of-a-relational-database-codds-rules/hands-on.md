# Hands-on — Characteristics of a Relational Database — Codd's Rules

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Hold a system to the rules** — Take one database program you know. Test it against five of Codd's heads from the lesson and report pass or fail for each, with evidence.

## Task two
**The catalogue test** — In SQLite, find the catalogue table that describes your tables, and query it with SQL — proving the rule on a real system.
