# Functional Dependency — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does A → B mean?**
a) B functionally depends on A — each value of A is paired with exactly one value of B  b) Knowing A settles B  c) No — two pupils can share a name; dependency is one-directional  d) The meaning — it must hold always, not just in the records present
**Answer: a) B functionally depends on A — each value of A is paired with exactly one value of B**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In plain words, what does dependency say?**
a) No — two pupils can share a name; dependency is one-directional  b) Knowing A settles B  c) The meaning — it must hold always, not just in the records present  d) ISBN → BookTitle — an ISBN settles a book's title
**Answer: b) Knowing A settles B**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) PupilID → PupilName holds. Does PupilName → PupilID?**
a) The meaning — it must hold always, not just in the records present  b) ISBN → BookTitle — an ISBN settles a book's title  c) No — two pupils can share a name; dependency is one-directional  d) By the meaning test: 'if I know this, do I always know that?'
**Answer: c) No — two pupils can share a name; dependency is one-directional**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is dependency about today's records or the data's meaning?**
