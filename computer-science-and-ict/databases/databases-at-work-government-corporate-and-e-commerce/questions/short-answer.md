# Databases at Work — Government, Corporate and E-Commerce — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name Bangladesh's three government examples.**
**Answer: National ID, passports and education board results**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why must a government record be exact?**
**Answer: A wrong record is a wronged citizen**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How are government databases shared?**
**Answer: Between offices, under permission**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How long are they kept?**
**Answer: For lifetimes**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is stretched from the classroom model?**
**Answer: The same tables and keys — to a hundred million rows**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is an ERP system?**
**Answer: One database under a company's purchasing, stock, payroll and sales**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) In e-commerce, what is the catalogue?**
**Answer: A table**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the order, in database terms?**
**Answer: A record that must never be lost**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the customer's history to the shop?**
**Answer: Its memory of them**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does not differ between the three workplaces?**
**Answer: The kind — rows, keys, queries, and keeping them true**
