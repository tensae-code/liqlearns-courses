# Hands-on — Databases at Work — Government, Corporate and E-Commerce

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Find the tables** — Take one online purchase you know. List the tables it must have touched — catalogue, stock, order, customer — and the key joining them.

## Task two
**A citizen's record** — Design the record one education board keeps for one candidate: fields, key, who may see it, who may change it.
