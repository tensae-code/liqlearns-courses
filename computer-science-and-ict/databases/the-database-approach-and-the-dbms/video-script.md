# Video script — The database approach and the DBMS

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script — the placements name no full video for this lesson.

---

Under the old way, every office kept its own files.

Here is the new way. One database. All the related data, together, in one place.

Above it sits the DBMS — the software that minds the database. It adds. It changes. It deletes. It fetches.

A program asks a question — a query. The DBMS finds the matching data and sends it back.

One store. Shared. Not copied again and again. And only the people allowed in can change it.
