# Hands-on — The database approach and the DBMS

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Draw the stack
On paper, draw the three layers — applications and users, DBMS, database — and draw one query going down and its answer coming back up. Label each layer with what it does.

## One copy or many?
Take the two disagreeing employee cards from the earlier lesson's hands-on. Now write the record once, on one card marked “the database”, and let both departments read that same card. Say what problem has disappeared, and what new question appears: who is allowed to change the card?

## Grade 12 — sort the sentences
Write six sentences on slips — three describing a database (the stored data, the schema, the tables of records) and three describing a DBMS (the software, Access/MySQL/Oracle, the tool that maintains). Sort them under two headings and read each heading's definition aloud.
