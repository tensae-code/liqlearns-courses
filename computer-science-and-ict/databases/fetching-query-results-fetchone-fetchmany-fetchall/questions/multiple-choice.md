# Fetching Query Results — fetchone, fetchmany, fetchall — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where do a SELECT's results wait?**
a) On the cursor — the query's answer, held by the database side  b) Moves rows from the cursor into the program  c) Not the program's — the query was asked but nothing was heard  d) The next one row
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does fetching do?**
a) Not the program's — the query was asked but nothing was heard  b) Moves rows from the cursor into the program  c) The next one row  d) For questions with a single answer
**Answer: b**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Until a row is fetched, whose is it?**
a) The next one row  b) For questions with a single answer  c) Not the program's — the query was asked but nothing was heard  d) The next counted rows — answers taken in mouthfuls
**Answer: c**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does fetchone take?**
a) For questions with a single answer  b) The next counted rows — answers taken in mouthfuls  c) Every remaining row at once, as a list  d) The next one row
**Answer: d**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When is fetchone right?**
a) For questions with a single answer  b) The next counted rows — answers taken in mouthfuls  c) Every remaining row at once, as a list  d) On a very large store — a million rows held at once
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does fetchmany take?**
a) Every remaining row at once, as a list  b) The next counted rows — answers taken in mouthfuls  c) On a very large store — a million rows held at once  d) Choosing how the program will hold the answer
**Answer: b**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does fetchall take?**
a) On a very large store — a million rows held at once  b) Choosing how the program will hold the answer  c) Every remaining row at once, as a list  d) Kuwait Grade 11 Information Technology Term 1, Unit 1 Lesson 5
**Answer: c**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is fetchall dangerous?**
a) Choosing how the program will hold the answer  b) Kuwait Grade 11 Information Technology Term 1, Unit 1 Lesson 5  c) On the cursor — the query's answer, held by the database side  d) On a very large store — a million rows held at once
**Answer: d**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is choosing the fetch, really?**
a) Choosing how the program will hold the answer  b) Kuwait Grade 11 Information Technology Term 1, Unit 1 Lesson 5  c) On the cursor — the query's answer, held by the database side  d) Moves rows from the cursor into the program
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
a) On the cursor — the query's answer, held by the database side  b) Kuwait Grade 11 Information Technology Term 1, Unit 1 Lesson 5  c) Moves rows from the cursor into the program  d) Not the program's — the query was asked but nothing was heard
