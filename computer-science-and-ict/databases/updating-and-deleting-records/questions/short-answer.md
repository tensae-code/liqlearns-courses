# Updating and Deleting Records — from the Program — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What two great acts does this chapter teach?**
**Answer: Adding new records and modifying existing ones from the program**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is prepared on the form for an addition?**
**Answer: The boxes are emptied for the new record's fresh values**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the order of a safe addition?**
**Answer: Prepare, check values complete and of the right kind, then save**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a half-typed record saved early?**
**Answer: A broken row everyone after must step around**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How does modifying begin?**
**Answer: Find and stand on the record (in the grid)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens on saving a modification?**
**Answer: The store's row is rewritten with the new values**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does deleting do?**
**Answer: Removes the current record from the table altogether**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why are changes confirmed with the record in view?**
**Answer: A saved change and a deletion are facts — irreversible**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Book 3 Chapter 3 — Adding and Editing Data**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 12 Information Technology Term 2, Book 3 Chapter 3**
