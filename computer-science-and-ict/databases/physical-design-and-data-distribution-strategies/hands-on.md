# Hands-on — Physical Design and Data Distribution Strategies

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Chapter 3 §3.3.2 Physical Database Design (data volume and usage analysis; distribution strategies — centralized, partitioned, replicated, hybrid; file organization; indexes; integrity constraints) and §3.4 Implementation (NADRA and CRICKINFO as distributed-database examples) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach physical design and data distribution strategies to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
