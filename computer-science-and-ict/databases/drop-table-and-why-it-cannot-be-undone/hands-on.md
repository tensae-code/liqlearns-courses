# Hands-on — DROP TABLE — and why it cannot be undone

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Build one to drop (teacher guide — evidence)
Create a School table, add one practice record, view it — then DROP TABLE School. Open the database window and confirm: the table is gone, record and all.

## The pause drill
Write the three pre-DROP questions on a card and keep it with your SQL notes. Read them aloud before every DROP in this course.
