# DROP TABLE — and why it cannot be undone — short answer

Answer in a word, a line of SQL, or one short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the statement that drops COURSE.
**Answer:** DROP TABLE COURSE;

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In one sentence, state the book's caution.
**Answer:** Delete a table that holds records and you cannot get it back.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three pre-DROP questions.
**Answer:** Does it hold records? Are they needed anywhere else? Is this truly the table I mean?
