# A Data Analysis Project — Indonesian Deforestation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the case study's subject?**
**Answer: Deforestation — the loss of Indonesia's forests, studied as data**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does preparation fix first?**
**Answer: The question the data must answer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What three questions shape the study?**
**Answer: Where forest is being lost, how fast, and what changes with it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must the data be, before use?**
**Answer: Open, trusted and legal — and judged fit before any chart is drawn**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does a case study use a real national problem?**
**Answer: The analysis stages are walked on ground that matters; a careless reading has a real cost**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is observation, in the project?**
**Answer: Exploring the prepared data — comparing regions and years, noting what the data cannot say**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is visualisation of the problem?**
**Answer: Charts and maps chosen so the pattern can be seen at a glance**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How can a deforestation chart mislead honestly-looking?**
**Answer: By its scale and its chosen years — it can accuse or excuse**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the project's discipline in choosing a view?**
**Answer: Show the data, not the view that wins the argument**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is published beside the picture?**
**Answer: The data's limits**
