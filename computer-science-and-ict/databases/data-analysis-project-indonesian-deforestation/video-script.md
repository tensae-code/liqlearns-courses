# Video script — A Data Analysis Project — Indonesian Deforestation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Grade 11 data chapter ends in a **case study** (studi kasus): the loss of Indonesia's forests — **deforestation** — studied as data. The project opens with **preparation**: fixing the question the data must answer (where is forest being lost, how fast, and what changes with it), finding data that is open, trusted and legal, and judging it fit before a single chart is drawn. A case study is the analysis stages walked on ground that matters: the numbers are about the learners' own country, and a careless reading has a real cost.
The project's working stages are **observation** — exploring the prepared data, comparing regions and years, noting what the data cannot say — and **visualisation of the problem**: charts and maps chosen so the pattern can be seen at a glance and argued about honestly. A deforestation chart can accuse or excuse depending on its scale and years; the project's discipline is to choose the view that shows the data, not the view that wins the argument, and to publish the limits beside the picture.
