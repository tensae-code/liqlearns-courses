# Database Security — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why does a database need special protection?**
**Answer: It concentrates an organisation's memory in one place**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is access control?**
**Answer: Controlling who may get in and what they may do — by accounts, passwords and permissions**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does every user have their own account?**
**Answer: So the system knows who is asking**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do permissions set?**
**Answer: What that user may see, add, change or delete**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is Fiji's rule for permissions?**
**Answer: Need — the powers the job needs, and no more**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a backup?**
**Answer: A copy of the database kept apart from the original**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Against what losses does a backup guard?**
**Answer: Fire, theft or a failed disk taking the only copy**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is accuracy part of security?**
**Answer: Wrong data entered carelessly harms like wrong data entered in malice**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What trail must changes leave?**
**Answer: A record of who changed what, and when**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is Fiji's complete picture?**
**Answer: Locks on the door, copies in another house, and a written trail inside**
