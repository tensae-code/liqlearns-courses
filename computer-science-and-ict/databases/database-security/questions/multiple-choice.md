# Database Security — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why does a database need special protection?**
a) It concentrates an organisation's memory in one place  b) Controlling who may get in and what they may do — by accounts, passwords and permissions  c) So the system knows who is asking  d) What that user may see, add, change or delete
**Answer: a) It concentrates an organisation's memory in one place**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is access control?**
a) So the system knows who is asking  b) Controlling who may get in and what they may do — by accounts, passwords and permissions  c) What that user may see, add, change or delete  d) Need — the powers the job needs, and no more
**Answer: b) Controlling who may get in and what they may do — by accounts, passwords and permissions**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does every user have their own account?**
a) What that user may see, add, change or delete  b) Need — the powers the job needs, and no more  c) So the system knows who is asking  d) A copy of the database kept apart from the original
**Answer: c) So the system knows who is asking**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do permissions set?**
a) Need — the powers the job needs, and no more  b) A copy of the database kept apart from the original  c) Fire, theft or a failed disk taking the only copy  d) What that user may see, add, change or delete
**Answer: d) What that user may see, add, change or delete**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is Fiji's rule for permissions?**
a) Need — the powers the job needs, and no more  b) A copy of the database kept apart from the original  c) Fire, theft or a failed disk taking the only copy  d) Wrong data entered carelessly harms like wrong data entered in malice
**Answer: a) Need — the powers the job needs, and no more**
