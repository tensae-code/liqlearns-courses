# Video script — One-to-one, one-to-many, many-to-many

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script — the placements name no full video for this lesson.

---

A country. Its capital city. One, and one. One-to-one.

A department. Its students. One department — many students. One-to-many.

An employee works on many projects. A project has many employees. Many, and many. Many-to-many.

Always count both ways. The two counts name the type.

Grade 12: relationships by hand.

Database Tools — Relationships. Add your tables. Close the list.

Take the primary key. Drag it onto the foreign key. Let go.

A line appears: 1 on one side — infinity on the many side. The relationship is built.
