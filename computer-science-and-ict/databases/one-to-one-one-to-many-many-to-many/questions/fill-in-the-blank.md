# One-to-one, one-to-many, many-to-many — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** One department, many students: this relationship is one-to-__________.
**Answer: many**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** When both counts are “many”, the relationship is __________-to-many.
**Answer: many**
