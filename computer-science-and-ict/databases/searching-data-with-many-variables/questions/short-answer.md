# Searching Data with Many Variables — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is searching with many variables?**
**Answer: Finding records by combining several conditions at once**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When is a record found under AND?**
**Answer: Only if it satisfies every condition**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does OR do to a search?**
**Answer: Widens it — a record satisfying any joined condition is found**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the data chapter open with this?**
**Answer: Cutting a dataset to exactly the question's records is the first move of all data work**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does an oversized catch mean?**
**Answer: A condition is missing or loose**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does an empty catch usually mean?**
**Answer: A condition is wrong — not that the world is empty**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is a search narrowed and widened?**
**Answer: Add a condition to narrow; drop one to widen**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must stay beside the search?**
**Answer: The question — so the catch can be judged against it**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) A search is a sentence in what?**
**Answer: The data's language**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 10, Bab 1 A — searching with many variables**
