# Hands-on — Searching Data with Many Variables

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Write the sentence** — Take a class list on paper. Write three real questions needing two or three variables each, state the conditions, and find each catch by hand.

## Task two
**Narrow and widen** — Start from a search that catches everyone. Add conditions one at a time, recording the catch size at each step, until exactly the records your question wants remain.
