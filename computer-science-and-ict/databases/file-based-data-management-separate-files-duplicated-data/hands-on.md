# Hands-on — File-based data management — separate files, duplicated data

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Two copies on paper
Write the same employee record — name, address, pay — on two separate cards, one marked HR, one marked Finance. Change one detail on the HR card only. Show a partner the two cards and ask: which card is right? Talk about why the cards themselves cannot answer.

## Find the duplication at school or home
List three facts that are written down in more than one place — a phone number in a notebook and in a phone, a name on two school lists. For each one, say what would happen if only one copy were updated.
