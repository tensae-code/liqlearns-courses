# Displaying Database Data in a Grid — DataGridView — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the DataGridView?**
**Answer: The form's grid control for showing a table's data**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How are records and fields shown in it?**
**Answer: Records as rows, fields as columns**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Through what is the grid bound to its table?**
**Answer: The Data Sources (via the connection and BindingSource)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the grid let the user see?**
**Answer: The whole store — what records exist, their order and values**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four record movements.**
**Answer: Next, previous, first, last**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the current record?**
**Answer: The record the form's other controls show and edit**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What three acts start from standing on a record?**
**Answer: Edit it, print it, delete it (any three sensible acts)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is movement taught as its own skill?**
**Answer: Every database program lives at find-the-record; the grid is where finding happens**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Book 3 Chapter 2 — Displaying Data**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 12 Information Technology Term 2, Book 3 Chapter 2**
