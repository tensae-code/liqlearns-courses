The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script — the placements name no full video for this lesson.

SQL. One language. Every relational database speaks it.

Three families. DDL builds the tables: CREATE — ALTER — DROP.

DML changes the data: INSERT — UPDATE — DELETE.

DQL asks: SELECT. It changes nothing. It only shows you what is there.

Build. Change. Ask. That is SQL.
