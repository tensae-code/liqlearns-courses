# Hands-on — SQL — DDL, DML and DQL

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The family sort
On paper, write the seven commands of this title on seven slips. Sort them into three piles — DDL, DML, DQL — and say each pile's job out loud.

## One job each
For a library database, invent one real job for each family and write it in plain words: a table to build, a record to change, a question to ask.
