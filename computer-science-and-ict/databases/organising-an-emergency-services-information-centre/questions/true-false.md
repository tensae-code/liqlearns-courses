# Organising an Emergency Services Information Centre — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Fire, police and ambulance (also medical help, disaster lines)**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Fire, police and ambulance (also medical help, disaster lines)** — as the answer to: What three things must a caller say?
**Answer: False — Who you are, where you are, and what has happened**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Wrong-service calls waste the right service's minutes**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Wrong-service calls waste the right service's minutes** — as the answer to: What two jobs does this part teach?
**Answer: False — Choosing the service and dialling it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Follow the service's instructions and keep the way clear**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Follow the service's instructions and keep the way clear** — as the answer to: What must be done to the numbers before publishing them?
**Answer: False — Verified**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Grouped by trouble, numbers large and plain**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Grouped by trouble, numbers large and plain** — as the answer to: Where do copies belong?
**Answer: False — Where people actually are — classroom, office, gate**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A database before the word is taught — facts checked, structured, retrievable**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A database before the word is taught — facts checked, structured, retrievable** — as the answer to: When is the centre finished?
**Answer: False — When a timed drill with a stranger succeeds**
