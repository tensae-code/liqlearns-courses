# Organising an Emergency Services Information Centre — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name three emergency services.**
**Answer: Fire, police and ambulance (also medical help, disaster lines)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three things must a caller say?**
**Answer: Who you are, where you are, and what has happened**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why must the service be chosen before the call?**
**Answer: Wrong-service calls waste the right service's minutes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What two jobs does this part teach?**
**Answer: Choosing the service and dialling it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do you do while help travels?**
**Answer: Follow the service's instructions and keep the way clear**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What must be done to the numbers before publishing them?**
**Answer: Verified**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How are services arranged in the centre?**
**Answer: Grouped by trouble, numbers large and plain**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where do copies belong?**
**Answer: Where people actually are — classroom, office, gate**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is an information centre, in the lesson's words?**
**Answer: A database before the word is taught — facts checked, structured, retrievable**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is the centre finished?**
**Answer: When a timed drill with a stranger succeeds**
