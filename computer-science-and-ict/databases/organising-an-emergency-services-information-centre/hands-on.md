# Hands-on — Organising an Emergency Services Information Centre

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Build the centre** — Collect and verify your local emergency numbers, then build the centre for your classroom: grouped by trouble, large print, tested by drill.

## Task two
**The thirty-second test** — Time three strangers finding the ambulance number in your centre. Fix whatever slowed them, and test again.
