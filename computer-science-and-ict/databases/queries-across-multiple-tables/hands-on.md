## The six-step database project (teacher guide — evidence) — project checklist
Work the whole title as one project, in this order:
1. **Choose a database problem from your own surroundings** (a shop's stock, a team's fixtures, a clinic's visits).
2. **Identify the entities (tables) and their potential attributes.**
3. **Identify the relationships between the entities.**
4. **Implement the tables in MS Access** (CREATE TABLE, with keys and references).
5. **Insert at least three records into every table created.**
6. **Modify at least one table by adding a column** (ALTER TABLE), **and write queries to select records** from your database.
A finished project can show all six steps, in order, with the SQL kept.
