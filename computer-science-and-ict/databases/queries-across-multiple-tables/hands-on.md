# Hands-on — Queries across multiple tables

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The book's example, for real
Build the three-table query from the lesson: add Student, Grade and Teacher; drag each join; pick name, subject, grade and teacher name; save it with a describing name; view the results and read one combined row aloud.

## Above 85
Do the book's practice: a query across Student and Grade for students scoring above 85 in all subjects — you will need the joins from this lesson and a criterion from the lesson before it. Predict the survivors on paper first, then check.
