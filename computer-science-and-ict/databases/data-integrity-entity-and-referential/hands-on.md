# Hands-on — Data Integrity Entity and Referential

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Chapter 4 §4.1 Overview: §4.1.1 Data Integrity (entity integrity — no null primary key; referential integrity — foreign key matches or is null) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach data integrity entity and referential to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
