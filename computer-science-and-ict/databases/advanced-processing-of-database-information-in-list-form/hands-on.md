# Hands-on — Advanced Processing of Database Information in List Form

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Craft chain** — Take a 15-row list of pupils (class, name, mark) and apply, on paper: sort by class then name; filter mark ≥ 9; subtotal the counts per class.

## Task two
**Border verdict** — For four jobs (a term report for parents; correcting a pupil's name everywhere; a one-off chart of marks by subject; next term's live register) rule list or database, with one line each.
