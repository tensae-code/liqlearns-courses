# Advanced Processing of Database Information in List Form — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a list, in this elective's sense?**
**Answer: Data extracted into rows and columns — the query's answer as a worksheet table**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does list form gain?**
**Answer: It travels — print, mail, archive — and submits to spreadsheet crafts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does list form lose?**
**Answer: The source's relationships, rules and live updating**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does truth live?**
**Answer: In the database — a list is its dated photograph**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a list's header row?**
**Answer: The row naming its columns (fields)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is multi-level sorting?**
**Answer: Ordering by one column, ties by the next (town, then name)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does filtering do?**
**Answer: Shows chosen slices without deleting the rest**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What are subtotals in a list?**
**Answer: Group sums inserted at each change of the grouping column**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does a pivot-style summary cross?**
**Answer: Rows by one field, columns by another, values aggregated in the cells**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) State the honesty rule.**
**Answer: Work on a copy, keep the source untouched, label every summary with its extraction**
