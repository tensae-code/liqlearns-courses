# Video script — Advanced Processing of Database Information in List Form

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **list** here is data **extracted into rows and columns** — the query's answer become a worksheet table: one row per record, one column per field, a header row naming them. In list form the data can travel (printed, mailed, archived) and submits to the spreadsheet's crafts, but it is **cut from its source**: relationships, rules and live updating stay behind in the database. The elective's first skill is recognising the moment: analysis and presentation live well in lists; **truth lives in the database**, and lists are its dated photographs.
The advanced crafts chain. **Multi-level sorting** orders by one column, ties by another (town, then name). **Filtering** shows chosen slices without deleting (this year, this class). **Subtotals** insert group sums into the sorted list at each change of the grouping column — the list echoing the totals query. **Pivot-style summaries** cross-tabulate: rows by one field, columns by another, values aggregated in the cells (marks: classes × subjects). Each craft's honesty rule is the elective's last word: **work on a copy, keep the source list untouched, and label every summary with the extraction it came from**.
