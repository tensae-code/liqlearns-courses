# Hands-on — Mapping an ERD to a Relational Schema

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Map it', 'ERD: Pupil (ID, name, phone numbers) takes many Loans; Book (ISBN, title) is in many Loans; a Loan has a date. Map the ERD to a full schema, listing each table's columns and keys, and trace every relationship.')

## Task two
('Find the unmapped', 'A schema is given beside its ERD, with one relationship carried nowhere and one multivalued attribute forced into a column. Find both faults and repair the schema.')
