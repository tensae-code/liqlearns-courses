# Hands-on — Indexing — Making Searches Fast

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Race the scan** — Shuffle a pack of 100 named cards. Time finding one name by scanning, then by a sorted index list you make first. Record both times.

## Task two
**Choose the indexes** — For a school library table, choose three fields to index and two to leave, with the search each index serves.
