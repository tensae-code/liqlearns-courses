# Hands-on — UPDATE — change rows, and never forget WHERE

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The raise, for real
In the standing Teacher table, run the below-10,000 raise. View the table before and after; count the changed rows and check the untouched ones.

## The accident, safely (teacher guide — evidence)
On a *practice copy* of a Student table, run a no-WHERE UPDATE setting every grade level at once. See why the same statement on the real table would be a disaster.
