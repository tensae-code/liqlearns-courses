# Hands-on — Adding a foreign key with ALTER

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Link them late
Create DEPARTMENT and a COURSE whose C_Department is a plain column. Add the foreign key with ALTER, then open the Relationships window and see your link drawn.

## Two links (teacher guide — evidence)
With Student, Teacher and Grade standing, write the ALTERs that link Grade to both — first as two separate statements.
