# Data Governance and Access Control — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is data governance?** → **The rule-layer above the tools — who owns data, who decides its uses, how long it is kept, who answers for harm**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does governance protect data from?** → **Confusion, excess and neglect inside the house — tools protect it from attackers**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is database hardening?** → **Closing the database's unneeded doors (penguatan database)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are the chapter's named parts?** → **Database hardening, database security, network security, and the governance of access**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does access governance decide?** → **For each person and each body of data: may they enter, and to do what — read, add, change, delete**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is least privilege?** → **No one holds powers their work does not need**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why named accounts?** → **Access belongs to a person — never to a shared secret**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is review of permissions?** → **Re-judging permissions as jobs change, and removing them when jobs end**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Governance is written down or what?** → **Or it does not exist**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?** → **Grade 12, Bab 4 I — data governance (tata kelola data)**
