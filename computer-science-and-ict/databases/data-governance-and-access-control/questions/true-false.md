# Data Governance and Access Control — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The rule-layer above the tools — who owns data, who decides its uses, how long it is kept, who answers for harm**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The rule-layer above the tools — who owns data, who decides its uses, how long it is kept, who answers for harm** — as the answer to: What does governance protect data from?
**Answer: False — Confusion, excess and neglect inside the house — tools protect it from attackers**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Closing the database's unneeded doors (penguatan database)**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Closing the database's unneeded doors (penguatan database)** — as the answer to: What are the chapter's named parts?
**Answer: False — Database hardening, database security, network security, and the governance of access**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: For each person and each body of data: may they enter, and to do what — read, add, change, delete**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: For each person and each body of data: may they enter, and to do what — read, add, change, delete** — as the answer to: What is least privilege?
**Answer: False — No one holds powers their work does not need**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Access belongs to a person — never to a shared secret**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Access belongs to a person — never to a shared secret** — as the answer to: What is review of permissions?
**Answer: False — Re-judging permissions as jobs change, and removing them when jobs end**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Or it does not exist**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Or it does not exist** — as the answer to: Where does Indonesia teach this?
**Answer: False — Grade 12, Bab 4 I — data governance (tata kelola data)**
