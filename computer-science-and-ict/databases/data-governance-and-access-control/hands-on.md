# Hands-on — Data Governance and Access Control

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Govern this data** — Take a school body of data (marks, library loans). Draft its governance in five lines: owner, who decides uses, keeping time, who answers for harm, and the access table by role.

## Task two
**Least-privilege audit** — List the accounts and powers in a system you know. Mark every power its holder's work does not need, and write the review that would remove it safely.
