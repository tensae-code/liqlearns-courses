## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Governance — the rules above the tools** — **Data governance** (tata kelola data) is the rule-layer above every tool: who owns a body of data, who may decide its uses, how long it is kept, and who answers when it is harmed. Indonesia's Grade 12 chapter teaches it after cyber security on purpose — tools protect data from attackers; governance protects it from confusion, excess and neglect inside the house. Its named parts: **database hardening** (penguatan database — closing the database's unneeded doors), **database security**, **network security**, and the governance of access itself.
2. **Access control — the governed door** — **Access governance** decides, for each person and each body of data: may they enter, and to do what — read, add, change, delete? Its working rules are the profession's: **least privilege** (no one holds powers their work does not need), **named accounts** (access belongs to a person, never a shared secret), and **review** (permissions are re-judged as jobs change, and removed when they end). Governance is written down or it does not exist: the chapter's learner leaves able to draft the rules, not only work the locks.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
