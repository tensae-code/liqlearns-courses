# Hands-on — Macros and a Switchboard Menu

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Wire three buttons', 'For a library database, specify three macros in words (trigger button, actions in order): Open New Loan, Print Overdue List, Close System. State which forms, queries or reports each action needs to exist first.')

## Task two
('Design the menu', 'Draw a switchboard for a school marks system with no more than six buttons, labelled in a teacher's words. Under each, write the macro it runs.')
