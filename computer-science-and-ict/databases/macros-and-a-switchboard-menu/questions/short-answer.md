# Macros and a Switchboard Menu — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a macro?**
**Answer: A saved list of actions that runs as one**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name actions a macro can run.**
**Answer: Open a form, run a query, print a report, close a window**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a macro built?**
**Answer: By choosing actions from a list — no code is written**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) To what is a macro attached?**
**Answer: To an event — most often a button's click**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do macros wire together?**
**Answer: The database's separate pieces, into behaviour a user can trigger**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a switchboard?**
**Answer: A special form that works as the system's main menu**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When does the switchboard open?**
**Answer: When the database opens**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do its buttons offer?**
**Answer: The system's whole work, in the user's own words**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the switchboard hide?**
**Answer: The workshop — tables and design views**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is Malaysia's project judged finished?**
**Answer: When a new user can do the system's jobs from the switchboard alone**
