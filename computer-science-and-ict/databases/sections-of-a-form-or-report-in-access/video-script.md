# Video script — Sections of a Form or Report in Access

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A form or report in Access is built in horizontal bands called sections — headers, the Detail section that repeats for every record, and footers — and each section has its own job and its own height The learner opens a form or report in Design view, names each section, and places controls in the section where they belong — titles in headers, record fields in Detail, totals in footers Because a control only behaves as expected in the right section: a field in Detail repeats per record, a field in a header appears once.
