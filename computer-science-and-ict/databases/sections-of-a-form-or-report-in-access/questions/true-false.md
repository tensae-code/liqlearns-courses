# Sections of a Form or Report in Access — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A form or report in Access is built in horizontal bands called sections — headers, the Detail section that repeats for every record, and footers — and each section has its own job and its own height**
**Answer: True**

**2. **Answer: False — The learner opens a form or report in Design view, names each section, and places controls in the section where they belong — titles in headers, record fields in Detail, totals in footers**
