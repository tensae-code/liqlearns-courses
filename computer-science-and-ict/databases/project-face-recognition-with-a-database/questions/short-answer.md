# Project — Face Recognition with a Database — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the difference between detection and recognition?**
**Answer: Detection finds that a face is present and where; recognition says whose it is**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What detector does the project use?**
**Answer: OpenCV's Haar cascade**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where are seen faces logged?**
**Answer: In an SQLite database — a face_logs / attendance table**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does a database beat a text file here?**
**Answer: The log can be queried, counted and kept honestly**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the log record?**
**Answer: Who was seen, and when**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does Term 2 grow the project?**
**Answer: An employees table linked to a face_log table, with queries and decision reports**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What library prints the PDF reports?**
**Answer: ReportLab**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What questions do the reports answer? Give one.**
**Answer: Who was late how often (or: which door saw whom)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the product, in the project's own lesson?**
**Answer: The integration — a system whose seeing is remembered, questioned and reported**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which books build this project?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 11 Information Technology Terms 1 and 2, Unit 2 of each**
