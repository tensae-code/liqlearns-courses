# Collecting Data by Web Scraping with Python — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is web scraping?**
**Answer: Collecting data by program — requesting pages and extracting their data into a table**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two acts does a scraper perform?**
**Answer: It requests a page, then extracts the wanted pieces from the page's structure**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the harvest become?**
**Answer: Rows of a table**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A scraper is only as honest as what?**
**Answer: Its pattern — it collects what the pattern says, including the page's errors**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the book's Python run?**
**Answer: In Google Colab — in the browser, with nothing installed**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Beautiful Soup do?**
**Answer: Parses the HTML and finds elements by their tags and classes**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does pandas hold the harvest as?**
**Answer: A DataFrame — rows and columns that can be cleaned, filtered and counted**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does Plotly do?**
**Answer: Turns the table into charts**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What limits what may be scraped?**
**Answer: The site's terms and the law of data — scrape only what may be taken**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two records must travel with a scraped table?**
**Answer: Where it came from — and validation before analysis**
