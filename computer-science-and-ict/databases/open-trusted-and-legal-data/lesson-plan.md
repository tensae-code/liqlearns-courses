## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Open — data that may be taken** — **Open data** (data terbuka) is published for use: reachable by anyone, in forms a program can read, under terms that allow reuse. Indonesia's Grade 11 data chapter teaches openness as the first test because the analysis project that follows must stand on data the class may actually take — a beautiful table behind a locked door or a 'no reuse' notice is, for the analyst, not data at all. Openness is a property of the terms, not of the topic: the same figures are open or closed by how they are published.
2. **Trusted and legal — data that may be believed and used** — Open is not enough. **Trusted** (terpercaya) asks whether the data deserves belief — a known maker, a stated method, figures that survive the validation tests. **Legal** (legal) asks whether using it is lawful: collected and shared within the law, respecting personal data and others' rights in their work. The three tests together are the chapter's gate: data for a decision or a publication must be open to take, trusted to stand on, and legal to use — failing any one, it waits outside.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
