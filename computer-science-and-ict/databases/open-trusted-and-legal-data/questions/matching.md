# Open, Trusted and Legal Data — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the three tests?** → **Open, trusted and legal**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is open data?** → **Data published for use — reachable by anyone, machine-readable, under terms allowing reuse**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Openness is a property of what?** → **The terms of publication — not of the topic**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the chapter teach openness first?** → **The analysis project must stand on data the class may actually take**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is trusted data?** → **Data that deserves belief — known maker, stated method, figures that survive validation**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does legal ask?** → **Whether using the data is lawful — respecting the law, personal data and others' rights**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must data for a decision pass?** → **All three tests — open to take, trusted to stand on, legal to use**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to data failing any test?** → **It waits outside — it is not used**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?** → **Grade 11, Bab II B — open, trusted and legal data**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) A locked or no-reuse table is, for the analyst, what?** → **Not data at all**
