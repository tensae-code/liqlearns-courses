# Video script — Open, Trusted and Legal Data

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Open data** (data terbuka) is published for use: reachable by anyone, in forms a program can read, under terms that allow reuse. Indonesia's Grade 11 data chapter teaches openness as the first test because the analysis project that follows must stand on data the class may actually take — a beautiful table behind a locked door or a 'no reuse' notice is, for the analyst, not data at all. Openness is a property of the terms, not of the topic: the same figures are open or closed by how they are published.
Open is not enough. **Trusted** (terpercaya) asks whether the data deserves belief — a known maker, a stated method, figures that survive the validation tests. **Legal** (legal) asks whether using it is lawful: collected and shared within the law, respecting personal data and others' rights in their work. The three tests together are the chapter's gate: data for a decision or a publication must be open to take, trusted to stand on, and legal to use — failing any one, it waits outside.
