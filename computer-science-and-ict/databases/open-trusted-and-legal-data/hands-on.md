# Hands-on — Open, Trusted and Legal Data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Run the three tests** — Find one public dataset (a statistics page, a timetable). Judge it against open, trusted and legal, with one line of evidence per test.

## Task two
**Fix a failing dataset** — Describe a dataset that fails exactly one test. Write what would have to change — terms, method note, or permission — for it to pass all three.
