# Hands-on — DBMS Objectives Advantages Disadvantages and Features

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Chapter 1 §1.4 Database Management System (§1.4.1 objectives — shareability, availability, evolvability, integrity; §1.4.2 advantages — data independence, complex relationships, security, backup/recovery; §1.4.3 disadvantages; §1.4.4 features — data dictionary, utilities, the SQL query language, report generator, access security, backup and recovery) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach dbms objectives advantages disadvantages and features to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
