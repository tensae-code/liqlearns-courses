# Hands-on — Organising Data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Organise a heap** — Take a messy list (a club's members scribbled in a notebook) and organise it into a table: records, fields, shapes, key.

## Task two
**Field court** — For a school library table, argue for cutting two proposed fields and keeping one contested one.
