# Organising Data — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is organising data?**
**Answer: Arranging data by a chosen structure — same kind, same place, same shape**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In a table, what are rows and columns?**
**Answer: Rows are records; columns are fields**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does each cell hold?**
**Answer: One value of its column's kind**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What can organised data do that unorganised cannot?**
**Answer: Be searched, sorted, counted and compared**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How can unorganised data be used?**
**Answer: Only read, slowly, by a human, once**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four organising decisions.**
**Answer: What is a record, which fields, what each field holds, what identifies each record**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why only needed fields?**
**Answer: Every extra field is collecting and guarding forever**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must a field's shape fix?**
**Answer: Its type and allowed shape — dates one way, names one way**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What keeps two same-named pupils distinct?**
**Answer: The identifying key**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is a database, in the closing definition?**
**Answer: Organised data with these decisions enforced by the machine**
