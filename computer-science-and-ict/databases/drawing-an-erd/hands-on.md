# Hands-on — Drawing an ERD

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Draw it big
On a full sheet, draw the student–borrows–book ERD as large as you can. Count your shapes out loud as you finish: rectangles, ellipses, labelled lines.

## The teacher–schedule practice
Do the book's practice task: draw a teacher–schedule ERD. Start with the entities, give each its attributes, then join them with a named line. Say the relationship's verb out loud before you write it.

## Solve a case in the guide's order
Take a small shop: customers buy products and each purchase is an order. On paper, work in the guide's order — entities first, attributes second, relationships last — and write the three relationship rules under your diagram.
