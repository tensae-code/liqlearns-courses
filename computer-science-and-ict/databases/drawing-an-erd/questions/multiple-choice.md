# Drawing an ERD — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In an ERD, an entity is drawn as a —
a) ellipse  b) rectangle  c) circle
**Answer: b)** — a rectangle, with the entity name inside.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An attribute is drawn as a —
a) rectangle  b) line  c) ellipse
**Answer: c)** — an ellipse (oval), with the attribute name inside.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the worked example, the relationship between student and book is named —
a) has  b) borrows  c) teaches
**Answer: b)** — *borrows*, written on the joining line.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How many ellipses does the worked example carry in total?
a) 4  b) 3  c) 7
**Answer: c)** — four on student, three on book.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In the solved case (teacher evidence), a customer makes —
a) exactly one order  b) one or many orders  c) no orders
**Answer: b)** — one or many orders.
