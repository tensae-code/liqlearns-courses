# Drawing an ERD — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The relationship name is written on the line that joins the entities.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's practice task after the worked example is a teacher–schedule ERD.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the solved case, a product must always belong to an order. (teacher evidence)
**Answer: False** — a product may have no order, or belong to only one order.
