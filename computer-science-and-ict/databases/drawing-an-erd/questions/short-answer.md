# Drawing an ERD — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the ERD's three components and the shape of each.
**Answer:** Entities — rectangles; attributes — ellipses; relationships — lines joining entities, named on the line.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the book entity's attributes in the worked example.
**Answer:** Book ID, title, publication year.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In what order does the case walkthrough solve an ERD? (teacher evidence)
**Answer:** Entities first, then attributes, then relationships.
