# Designing a Relational Database — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does design start?**
**Answer: From the need — what the database must hold and what questions it must answer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are entities?**
**Answer: The kinds of thing the database is about — Pupil, Book, Loan**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are attributes?**
**Answer: The facts kept about an entity — a pupil's name, class, date of birth**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When does an attribute belong in the design?**
**Answer: Only if the need asks for it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What should every table and column trace back to?**
**Answer: A question the database must answer**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a primary key?**
**Answer: The attribute (or added ID) that picks out one record from all others**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What else is stated before tables are drawn?**
**Answer: The relationships between entities, with their cardinality**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How are tables then drawn?**
**Answer: One per entity, attributes as columns, keys in place**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What carries the relationships in the tables?**
**Answer: Foreign keys**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What are tables drawn before entities and keys are understood?**
**Answer: A guess the data will later pay for**
