# Expressions in Access — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an Access expression?**
**Answer: A formula that works out a value from the record's data**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How are field names written in an expression?**
**Answer: In square brackets — [Pret], [Cantitate]**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does & do between texts?**
**Answer: It joins them — concatenation**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name three built-in functions of the lesson.**
**Answer: Sum, Avg, IIf (also: Date, Year)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When is an expression evaluated?**
**Answer: Per record — each row's fields feed its own result**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where can an expression be the test?**
**Answer: In a query's criteria row**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a calculated field?**
**Answer: An expression standing as a new column the table never stored — Total: [Pret]*[Cantitate]**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does IIf do?**
**Answer: It chooses between two answers by a condition**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does an expression display on paper?**
**Answer: In a form or report control**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How many grammars rule the homes?**
**Answer: One — the same expression grammar everywhere in Access**
