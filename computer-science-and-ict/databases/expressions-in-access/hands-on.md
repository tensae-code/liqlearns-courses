# Hands-on — Expressions in Access

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Expression writing** — Write Access expressions for: a 10% discount price; a pupil's age from [DataNasterii]; a full name from [Nume] and [Prenume]; a 'promoted'/'repeat' label by IIf on [Media] >= 5.

## Task two
**Home placing** — For one expression each, state whether it belongs in a criteria row, a calculated field or a report control — and what changes when its home changes.
