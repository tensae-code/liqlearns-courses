# Aggregate Functions — Count and Average with GROUP BY — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does an aggregate function return?**
**Answer: One value computed from many rows**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does COUNT return? AVG?**
**Answer: COUNT: how many rows. AVG: their mean (average)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give the course's two aggregate examples.**
**Answer: Counting students in each class; landmarks' average entry price by region**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does GROUP BY do?**
**Answer: Splits the table into groups sharing a value, one aggregate per group**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How many counts does 'COUNT per class' return?**
**Answer: One per class — not one overall**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Read a grouped query in two beats — which?**
**Answer: The grouping names the crowds; the aggregate asks each crowd its question**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Is the answer a row of the table?**
**Answer: No — a fact about the table (or about each group)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which column groups the landmarks?**
**Answer: Region**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which aggregate suits a price's typical value?**
**Answer: AVG — the average**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 11 Information Technology Term 2, Unit 1 Lesson 4**
