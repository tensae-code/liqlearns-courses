# Hands-on — Working in the Datasheet Edit Find Replace

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Chapter 6 §6.4 Modifying a Table (adding, editing and deleting records; inserting and deleting fields; resizing rows and columns; freezing and hiding columns; Find; Replace; spell check and AutoCorrect) and §6.5 Print a Datasheet Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach working in the datasheet edit find replace to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
