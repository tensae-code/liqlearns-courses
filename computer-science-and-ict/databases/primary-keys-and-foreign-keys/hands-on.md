# Hands-on — Primary keys and foreign keys

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The shared-name test
Write five invented student rows, with two pairs sharing a name. Show that name cannot identify a record, and ID can. Mark the primary key on your table.

## Trace a loan
Draw Student, Book and Book_Loan small on one page. Enter one loan row, then draw lines from its two foreign keys to the rows they match. Say each line's sentence out loud: “this loan's student is…; this loan's book is…”

## Grade 12 — the five-table map
Draw the five school tables as five boxes. Write Sid inside Students marked PK, and inside MarkList marked FK. Add one mark row per student for one student with three subjects — count Sid's appearances on each side and say the rule each count proves.
