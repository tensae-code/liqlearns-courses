# Primary keys and foreign keys — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two students may share the same student ID, as long as their names differ.
**Answer: False** — no two students can share an ID; that is what makes it a primary key.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tables are navigated and joined through foreign keys.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** One loan row can be followed through its foreign keys to exactly one student row and one book row.
**Answer: True** — each foreign key matches one primary-key value.
