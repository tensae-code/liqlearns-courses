# Hands-on — Filtering by date, AND, OR and BETWEEN

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The four hunts
On the standing Sales table (or a drawn ten-row list), run the lesson's four filters. Record the count each returns and compare with your paper prediction.

## Boundary drill
Write the filters for: sales in March only; sales before March; sales from March onward. Check the 1 March and 31 March rows land where you intended.
