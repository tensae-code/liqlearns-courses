# Filtering by date, AND, OR and BETWEEN — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** '>' and '>=' on a date boundary return identical results.
**Answer: False** — they differ by the boundary day's records.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Adding an AND criterion can increase the number of matching rows.
**Answer: False** — AND can only keep or shrink the catch.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** OR accepts a row that meets either criterion.
**Answer: True** — that is OR's rule.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** BETWEEN excludes its endpoints, like a strict pair of inequalities.
**Answer: False** — BETWEEN includes both endpoints.
