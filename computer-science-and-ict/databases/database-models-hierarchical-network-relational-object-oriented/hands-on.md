# Hands-on — Database Models — Hierarchical, Network, Relational, Object-Oriented

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Model the same school', 'Sketch the same facts (a school, its classes, its pupils, each pupil in one class but joining many clubs) in the hierarchical way and in the relational way. Mark where the clubs break the tree.')

## Task two
('Choose the model', 'For each system, choose a model and defend it: (a) a bank's accounts and customers; (b) a game whose characters are objects with behaviours; (c) a parts catalogue where each part belongs to exactly one assembly.')
