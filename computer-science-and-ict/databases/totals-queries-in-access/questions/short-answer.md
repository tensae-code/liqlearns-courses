# Totals Queries in Access — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What switches a query into a totals query?**
**Answer: The Totals button — adding the Total row to the grid**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Group By do?**
**Answer: It divides the records into groups — one group per value**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the five aggregate functions of the lesson.**
**Answer: Sum, Avg, Count, Min and Max**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What shape has a totals query's result?**
**Answer: One row per group**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to a grouped field's rows?**
**Answer: They collapse to the aggregate values per group**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a Where criterion filter?**
**Answer: Records — before grouping**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a criterion under an aggregate column filter?**
**Answer: Groups — after summing**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the grid need the Where marker?**
**Answer: To tell a before-grouping field (not shown) from the grouping and aggregate columns**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) State the classic error.**
**Answer: Confusing before-grouping filtering with after-grouping filtering**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Give the lesson's two examples.**
**Answer: Only this year's sales summed (before); only towns with Sum > 10 000 shown (after)**
