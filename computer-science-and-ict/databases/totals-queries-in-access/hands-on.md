# Hands-on — Totals Queries in Access

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Totals build** — Design the totals query over a Sales table that shows, per town, Count of sales and Sum of amounts, only for the current year, and keep only towns above a stated total.

## Task two
**Moment naming** — Take three criteria from given totals queries and state for each: before or after grouping, and what changes if its moment is swapped.
