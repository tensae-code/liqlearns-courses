# Video script — The Data Hierarchy — Character, Field, Record, Table, Database

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Data climbs a ladder. One letter is a character. Characters make a field — one fact.

Fields about one person make a record. Records make a table. Related tables together make the database.

Keep one fact in one field, and the whole ladder stays searchable.
