# Hands-on — The Data Hierarchy — Character, Field, Record, Table, Database

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Label a real record
Take one real card or form. Write out one character, three fields with their field names, and describe the full record in one sentence. Then say what the table holding many such records would be called.

## Design the top rung
For a small library, list three tables its database needs. For each table, list four fields and say what one record in it is. Check: does every field hold exactly one fact?
