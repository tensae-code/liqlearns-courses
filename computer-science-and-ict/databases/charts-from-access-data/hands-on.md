# Hands-on — Charts from Access Data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Chart a summary', 'From a small class list, summarise members per group on paper, then draw the column chart that presents it, titled and labelled.')

## Task two
('Fit the type', 'For three questions about one dataset, choose column, line or pie for each and defend the choice.')
