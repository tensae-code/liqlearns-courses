# Charts from Access Data — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a chart from Access data draw?**
a) A table's or query's records, without leaving the database  b) Members per class, or sales per month  c) The comparison at a glance  d) A query that has already summarised the records
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Give two summaries a chart might draw.**
a) The comparison at a glance  b) Members per class, or sales per month  c) A query that has already summarised the records  d) Columns (or bars)
**Answer: b**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a reader gain from the chart?**
a) A query that has already summarised the records  b) Columns (or bars)  c) The comparison at a glance  d) A line
**Answer: c**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) What is the best source for a chart?**
a) Columns (or bars)  b) A line  c) A pie  d) A query that has already summarised the records
**Answer: d**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Which chart type compares groups?**
a) Columns (or bars)  b) A line  c) A pie  d) Labels and a title
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which type shows change over time?**
a) A pie  b) A line  c) Labels and a title  d) A chart of raw unsummarised records is noise
**Answer: b**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which type shows parts of one whole?**
a) Labels and a title  b) A chart of raw unsummarised records is noise  c) A pie  d) Laos Grade 12 ICT, Chapter 3 §IV.2
**Answer: c**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must a chart carry to read honestly?**
a) A chart of raw unsummarised records is noise  b) Laos Grade 12 ICT, Chapter 3 §IV.2  c) A table's or query's records, without leaving the database  d) Labels and a title
**Answer: d**

**9. (Sub lesson 1) Why must the summary come before the drawing?**
a) A chart of raw unsummarised records is noise  b) Laos Grade 12 ICT, Chapter 3 §IV.2  c) A table's or query's records, without leaving the database  d) Members per class, or sales per month
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
a) A table's or query's records, without leaving the database  b) Laos Grade 12 ICT, Chapter 3 §IV.2  c) Members per class, or sales per month  d) The comparison at a glance
