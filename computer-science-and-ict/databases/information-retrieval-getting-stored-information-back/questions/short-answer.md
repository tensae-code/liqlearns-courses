# Information Retrieval — Getting Stored Information Back — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is information retrieval?**
**Answer: Getting stored information back out of a system to answer a need**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why does retrieval work at all?**
**Answer: Because storage was organised — records in fields, in a known order or index**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What would the system have to do without organisation?**
**Answer: Read everything to find anything**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does Fiji pair storage and retrieval?**
**Answer: Stored in one section, retrieved in the next — a store you cannot retrieve from is a hole, not a database**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Give a retrieval in plain words.**
**Answer: A name looked up; a record found; a list drawn**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a retrieval asked?**
**Answer: As a request in the system's terms — the field, the value, the conditions**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are retrieval's two quality tests?**
**Answer: It returns everything that matches, and only what matches**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Give Fiji's school example.**
**Answer: All Year 11 pupils in Suva — every such pupil, and no Year 12 visitor**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In three words, what is good retrieval?**
**Answer: Fast, complete and exact**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Whose promise does retrieval keep?**
**Answer: Storage's — the promise that what was put in can be got back**
