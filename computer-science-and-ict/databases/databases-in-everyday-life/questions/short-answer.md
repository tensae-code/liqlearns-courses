# Databases in Everyday Life — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What database does the school office keep?**
**Answer: Its pupils — names, classes, fees and marks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What question does the library database answer?**
**Answer: Do we have this book, and who has it?**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the shop's database hold?**
**Answer: Stock and prices, used at the till**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the bank's database hold?**
**Answer: Accounts and their balances**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is Fiji's point in these examples?**
**Answer: Recognition — each answers questions from stored records, which is a database's job**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What one shape sits under all the examples?**
**Answer: Records of one kind of thing, each record in fields, searched to answer questions**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What fields does a book record in a library carry?**
**Answer: Its title, author and borrower details — the fields every book record shares**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Give a question a pupil database answers in seconds.**
**Answer: Is this pupil in Year 8?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name two more everyday databases beyond Fiji's four.**
**Answer: An airline booking system and a hospital file system (or a phone contact list)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why learn the shape, not only the examples?**
**Answer: So a new system is recognised as the same kind of thing on first meeting**
