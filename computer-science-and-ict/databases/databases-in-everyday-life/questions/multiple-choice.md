# Databases in Everyday Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What database does the school office keep?**
a) Its pupils — names, classes, fees and marks  b) Do we have this book, and who has it?  c) Stock and prices, used at the till  d) Accounts and their balances
**Answer: a) Its pupils — names, classes, fees and marks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What question does the library database answer?**
a) Stock and prices, used at the till  b) Do we have this book, and who has it?  c) Accounts and their balances  d) Recognition — each answers questions from stored records, which is a database's job
**Answer: b) Do we have this book, and who has it?**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the shop's database hold?**
a) Accounts and their balances  b) Recognition — each answers questions from stored records, which is a database's job  c) Stock and prices, used at the till  d) Records of one kind of thing, each record in fields, searched to answer questions
**Answer: c) Stock and prices, used at the till**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the bank's database hold?**
a) Recognition — each answers questions from stored records, which is a database's job  b) Records of one kind of thing, each record in fields, searched to answer questions  c) Its title, author and borrower details — the fields every book record shares  d) Accounts and their balances
**Answer: d) Accounts and their balances**
