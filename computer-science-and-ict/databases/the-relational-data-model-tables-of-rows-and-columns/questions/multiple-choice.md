# The relational data model — tables of rows and columns — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the relational model, a two-dimensional table is called a —
a) relation  b) query  c) form
**Answer: a)** — a relation.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A row of a relation is also called a —
a) field  b) tuple  c) key
**Answer: b)** — a record, also called a tuple.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A column of a relation is an attribute, also called a —
a) tuple  b) instance  c) field
**Answer: c)** — a field.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The worked student relation's columns are —
a) ID, name, sex, grade, address  b) title, author, year  c) city, country, population
**Answer: a)** — the student relation's five attributes.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In the Country–City join (teacher evidence), the two tables are joined through —
a) their colour  b) the linking (shared) attribute  c) the row count
**Answer: b)** — the shared attribute is the bridge.
