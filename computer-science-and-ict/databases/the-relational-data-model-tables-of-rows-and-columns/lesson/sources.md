**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Ethiopia, Nepal, Belarus, North Macedonia).**

Belarus's own way (Grade 11): grade 11 is a web-and-data year — hand-written HTML in Notepad, then the same site rebuilt in FrontPage, a long PascalABC graphics practicum borrowed from other school subjects, and a full Access database course (tables, links, forms, queries, sorting, reports).

Honesty: Sub lesson 3 stands on teacher-guide evidence only (the guide's worked Activity 3.5 answer) and is labelled as such; Sub lessons 1–2 are pupil-taught, so the lesson counts as taught in 1 country. The teacher placement summarises the nine-row joined table and its populations without reproducing the figures row by row, so this lesson teaches the join — the linking attribute, the field count, the Kenyan cities, the nine cities across the four named countries — and does not quote population figures it was not given. No student-book error touches these pages.
