# Hands-on — The relational data model — tables of rows and columns

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Build the student relation on paper
Draw the Student relation with its five columns and enter four invented students. Circle one record, box one field, and label both with both of their names.

## Spot the shared attribute
Draw a small Book table (book ID, title) and a Loan table (loan number, book ID, student ID). Find the attribute that stands in both tables and draw a bridge line between its two columns.
