# Hands-on — Forms of Data Representation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**One data, three forms** — Take your class's marks or heights. Represent them as a table, a bar chart, and one summary number. Under each, write what it shows well and what it drops.

## Task two
**Form detective** — Find three data representations in a newspaper or textbook (a table, a chart, a picture). For each, name the question it answers best and one thing it hides.
