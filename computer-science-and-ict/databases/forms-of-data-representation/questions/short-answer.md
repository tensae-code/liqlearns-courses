# Forms of Data Representation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the six forms of data representation.**
**Answer: Text, numbers, tables, diagrams/charts, images, and sound**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which form lets amounts be calculated with?**
**Answer: Numbers**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which form organises data in rows and columns?**
**Answer: The table**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which form shows patterns and comparisons at a glance?**
**Answer: Diagrams and charts**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How does Armenia's Grade 5 guide name §23?**
**Answer: 'Forms of representing data' — the student book itself presents data by means of tables**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a bar chart invite that a table does not?**
**Answer: Comparison at a glance — the table invites lookup instead**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a single average hide?**
**Answer: Every individual value behind the verdict**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Match the form: the question is 'compare the classes'.**
**Answer: A chart (bar chart) — comparison is its invitation**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name one honesty rule for diagrams.**
**Answer: Axes start at zero, and labels say what the numbers are**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must you name about your chosen form?**
**Answer: What it drops — every form leaves something out**
