# Connecting a Program to a Database — DataSet and BindingSource — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Through what does a VB program work on a database?**
**Answer: A connection — its opened line to the database file**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which database does the book work on?**
**Answer: Db_inf**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a DataSet?**
**Answer: The database's tables copied into the program's memory**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is the DataSet the store itself?**
**Answer: No — it is the program's working copy; the real database waits until saving back**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What stands between the DataSet and the form's controls?**
**Answer: The BindingSource**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does binding mean here?**
**Answer: Controls show the DataSet's rows / current record, moving together as the record moves**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the BindingSource know when the user edits?**
**Answer: What must be saved back**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three named things of the connection.**
**Answer: The connection, the DataSet, and the BindingSource**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does editing happen — on the database or the copy?**
**Answer: On the working copy, until it is saved back**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 12 Information Technology Term 2, Book 3 Chapter 1**
