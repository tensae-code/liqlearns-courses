# Documenting Database Work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does documentation record?**
a) The build, in words and diagrams  b) The need; the design (entities, ERD, tables, keys); the objects built and what each does; the tests and their results  c) As the work goes — not reconstructed afterwards  d) A story — it will disagree with the database
**Answer: a) The build, in words and diagrams**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four parts Malaysia's document holds.**
a) As the work goes — not reconstructed afterwards  b) The need; the design (entities, ERD, tables, keys); the objects built and what each does; the tests and their results  c) A story — it will disagree with the database  d) The checker, the user, and the future maker
**Answer: b) The need; the design (entities, ERD, tables, keys); the objects built and what each does; the tests and their results**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When is it written?**
