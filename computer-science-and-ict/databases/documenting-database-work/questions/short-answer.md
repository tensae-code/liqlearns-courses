# Documenting Database Work — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does documentation record?**
**Answer: The build, in words and diagrams**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four parts Malaysia's document holds.**
**Answer: The need; the design (entities, ERD, tables, keys); the objects built and what each does; the tests and their results**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When is it written?**
**Answer: As the work goes — not reconstructed afterwards**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a document written from memory at the end?**
**Answer: A story — it will disagree with the database**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the document's three readers.**
**Answer: The checker, the user, and the future maker**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the checker do with it?**
**Answer: Verifies the work against the need without rebuilding it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the future maker need it for?**
**Answer: To change the database without breaking it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is Malaysia's practical standard?**
**Answer: A competent stranger can say what every object is for and where a named fact lives**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Is documentation decoration?**
**Answer: No — it makes the work outlast its maker's memory**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What objects must each be explained?**
**Answer: Tables, forms, queries, reports and macros — with what each one does**
