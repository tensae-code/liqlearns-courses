# Hands-on — Documenting Database Work

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Document a build', 'Take a database you have built (or the loans design from the design lesson) and write its documentation: need, design summary, object list with purposes, and two tests with results.')

## Task two
('Stranger test', 'Swap documentation with a partner. Using only their document, answer: what is this database for, where does a borrower's address live, and which object prints the overdue list? Score their document by your answers.')
