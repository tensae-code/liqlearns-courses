# Database Uses and Issues — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name five uses Fiji's senior course points to." is: Stock and sales; pupils and results; bank accounts; hospital patients; airline bookings**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are the database's three gifts?" is: Its daily work is the database being used — not a cupboard it merely has**
**Answer: False** — The record kept once, found fast, and shared by everyone whose job needs it

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How does Fiji describe an organisation's relation to its database?" is: Its daily work is the database being used — not a cupboard it merely has**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "At Viti, what runs on the database?" is: Everyone whose job needs it — office, teachers, principal**
**Answer: False** — Stock and sales

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Who shares one record at school?" is: Everyone whose job needs it — office, teachers, principal**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the privacy issue?" is: A wrong record, shared fast, misleads everyone at once — errors become efficient too**
**Answer: False** — One shared store means one breach reaches everything; records about people demand access rules

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the accuracy issue?" is: A wrong record, shared fast, misleads everyone at once — errors become efficient too**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the dependence issue?" is: Backup, access rules, and checked data**
**Answer: False** — When the system stops, work that forgot how to proceed without it stops too

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What three governings does Fiji name as the price?" is: Backup, access rules, and checked data**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Are those governings extras?" is: Stock and sales; pupils and results; bank accounts; hospital patients; airline bookings**
**Answer: False** — No — they are the price of the database's gifts
