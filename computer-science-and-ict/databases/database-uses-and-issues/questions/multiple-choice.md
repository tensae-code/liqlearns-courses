# Database Uses and Issues — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name five uses Fiji's senior course points to.**
a) Stock and sales; pupils and results; bank accounts; hospital patients; airline bookings  b) The record kept once, found fast, and shared by everyone whose job needs it  c) Its daily work is the database being used — not a cupboard it merely has  d) Stock and sales

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the database's three gifts?**
a) Its daily work is the database being used — not a cupboard it merely has  b) The record kept once, found fast, and shared by everyone whose job needs it  c) Stock and sales  d) Everyone whose job needs it — office, teachers, principal
