# Database Uses and Issues — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name five uses Fiji's senior course points to.**
**Answer: Stock and sales; pupils and results; bank accounts; hospital patients; airline bookings**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the database's three gifts?**
**Answer: The record kept once, found fast, and shared by everyone whose job needs it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does Fiji describe an organisation's relation to its database?**
**Answer: Its daily work is the database being used — not a cupboard it merely has**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) At Viti, what runs on the database?**
**Answer: Stock and sales**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Who shares one record at school?**
**Answer: Everyone whose job needs it — office, teachers, principal**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the privacy issue?**
**Answer: One shared store means one breach reaches everything; records about people demand access rules**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the accuracy issue?**
**Answer: A wrong record, shared fast, misleads everyone at once — errors become efficient too**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the dependence issue?**
**Answer: When the system stops, work that forgot how to proceed without it stops too**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What three governings does Fiji name as the price?**
**Answer: Backup, access rules, and checked data**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Are those governings extras?**
**Answer: No — they are the price of the database's gifts**
