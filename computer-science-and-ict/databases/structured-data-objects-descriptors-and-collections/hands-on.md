# Hands-on — Structured Data — Objects, Descriptors and Collections

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Describe and gather
Choose a kind of object everyone in the room has — bags, phones, exercise books. Agree on five descriptors that fit them all. Describe four real examples with values, then draw the collection as a table. Read one row aloud as a sentence: "This bag is…" — if the sentence makes sense, your table is right.

## The purpose test
Describe the same book twice: once for a library, once for a bookshop. Which descriptors appear in both lists, which only in one? Write one sentence on what that teaches about choosing descriptors.
