# Structured Data — Objects, Descriptors and Collections — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A school wants data about its students for a sports day. Name four descriptors it should collect, and one it should not need.**
**Answer: For example height, age (or class), chosen sport, and a contact for the day — descriptors the sports day actually uses. It does not need, for example, each student’s home address or grades: the purpose does not call for them.**
