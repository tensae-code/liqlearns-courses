# Integrated Library Management Systems — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is integrated Library Management Systems?**
**Answer: An integrated library management system is one database-driven system that runs a whole library — catalogue, members, borrowing and returning, and fines — with every part sharing the same records**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the key work in integrated Library Management Systems?**
**Answer: The learner traces a book through the system: catalogued once, borrowed against a member record, returned, and reported on — seeing how one shared database replaces separate paper registers**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does South Sudan teach integrated Library Management Systems?**
**Answer: In Secondary ICT, South Sudan Grade 12 S4 Student, printed p. 40 — Integrated Library Management Systems**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does integrated Library Management Systems matter?**
**Answer: Because a library's work is exactly the work databases do best, and integration means an item's story is written once and read everywhere**

**Answer: The book's own order for integrated Library Management Systems — and how to show it in practice, not only name it**
