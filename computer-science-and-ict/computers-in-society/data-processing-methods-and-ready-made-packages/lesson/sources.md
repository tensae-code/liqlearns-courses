Sudan's own way (Grade 11): a university-style secondary text (single named author, Dr Awad Haj Ali of the University of Gezira) that teaches systems work before code — file types and file security in a floppy-disk/UPS world, a complete spare-parts-factory system as the running example, the algorithm named for al-Khwarizmi, desk-checking with real sales data, and then a full BASIC course (numbered lines, READ/DATA, GOSUB, DEF FN) with payroll and mean/variance programs — data processing taught as a school subject in its own right.

Laos's own way (Grade 6 ICT student book): a theory-only foundation year — no hands-on computer practicals at all; ICT is introduced through Lao daily-life examples (TV remote, household appliances) and every chapter opens with objectives, guiding questions and a discussion activity (ກິດຈະກໍາ) before the exposition. The RIES ICT line runs ມ.1–ມ.7 (Grades 6–12).

Laos's own way (Grade 12 ICT student book): the final year is a modern, applied course — problem-solving method, a long English-titled internet-safety sequence (Internet Safety, Email Tips for Scams and Spam, Smart Social Networking, Cyber harassment/Stalking/Addiction), data summaries and PivotTables for decision-making, copyright and Creative Commons licensing, a full PowerPoint course, and choosing technology for your future occupation.
