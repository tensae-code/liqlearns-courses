# Data Processing Methods and Ready-Made Packages — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does Sudan's book name the computer at its opening?**
**Answer: A data-processing machine**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a data-processing machine do?**
**Answer: Takes data in, works on it by set rules, and gives out information**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is batch processing?**
**Answer: Data gathered first and processed together**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is processing at once?**
**Answer: Each transaction processed as it arrives**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the two older ways behind both.**
**Answer: Processing by hand, and processing by machines that are not computers**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which job suits a batch?**
**Answer: A monthly payroll**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which job cannot wait for a batch?**
**Answer: A bank balance**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is a ready-made package?**
**Answer: Software written once and sold ready to many users**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What stands against the package?**
**Answer: A specially written program, built for one organisation's exact needs**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the package's trade-off?**
**Answer: Cheaper and instant, but it fits its buyer only as well as it fits everyone**
