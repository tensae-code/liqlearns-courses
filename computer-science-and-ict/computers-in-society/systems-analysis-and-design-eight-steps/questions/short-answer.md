# Systems Analysis and Design — the Eight Steps — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How many steps does Sudan's book count for building a computerised system?**
**Answer: Eight**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why are the steps climbed in order?**
**Answer: Each step answers the question the next one needs**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does skipping a step do?**
**Answer: It moves the step's cost into the running system, where mistakes are dearer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is systems analysis?**
**Answer: Studying the present system — its data, people and faults — and fixing what the new system must do**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is systems design?**
**Answer: Drawing the new system before it is built — its files, inputs, processing and outputs**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which asks what and which answers how?**
**Answer: Analysis asks what; design answers how**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do the builders work from?**
**Answer: The design — a plan, not guesses**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's worked example after this lesson?**
**Answer: A complete information system for a spare-parts factory**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the road end?**
**Answer: With the new system running — built, tested and handed over**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the road begin?**
**Answer: With the first look at the present system**
