# What a Model Is — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A ____ is a made thing that stands for a real thing.**
**Answer: model**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A model is smaller and ____ than the real thing, so hands and eyes can learn from it.**
**Answer: simpler**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A small made copy of Turkmen national dress is a model of ____.**
**Answer: clothing**
