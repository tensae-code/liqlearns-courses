# What a Model Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A model car can drive a child home.**
**Answer: False** — A model shows the real thing; it does not do the real thing's job.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A model leaves out what you do not need and keeps what you are learning.**
**Answer: True** — Smaller and simpler is what makes a model easy to learn from.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A drawing can be a model.**
**Answer: True** — A flat picture that shows a thing's shape is one kind of model.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A model must always be something you can hold.**
**Answer: False** — Some models are flat — pictures and drawings — and they still stand for the real thing.
