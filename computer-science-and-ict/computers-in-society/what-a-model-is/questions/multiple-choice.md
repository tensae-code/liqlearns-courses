# What a Model Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a model?**
a) The real thing itself  b) A made thing that stands for a real thing and shows what it is like  c) A kind of computer
**Answer: b) A made thing that stands for a real thing and shows what it is like** — That is the model's job: to stand for the real thing and show it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why is a model useful?**
a) It is smaller, simpler and safe to hold and turn over  b) It can do the real thing's job  c) It is always made of gold
**Answer: a) It is smaller, simpler and safe to hold and turn over** — A model car cannot carry you — its job is to show, not to do.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is a model?**
a) A real boat on the water  b) A drawing of a boat  c) The sea
**Answer: b) A drawing of a boat** — A flat picture that shows a thing's shape is one kind of model.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A model of Turkmen clothing shows —**
a) only its price  b) its shape, colours and patterns  c) nothing at all
**Answer: b) its shape, colours and patterns** — The made copy shows the dress closely, without the real dress being worn.
