# What a Model Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A model car and a real car: name two ways they are the same, and two ways they are different.**
**Answer:** Same: shape, wheels, windows (any two). Different: size; the model can be held; the model cannot carry people or drive (any two).

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name three models you have seen, and say what real thing each one stands for.**
**Answer:** Any three made or flat copies — e.g. a toy car (a car), a doll (a person or baby), a drawing of a house (a house), a model of Turkmen clothing (the national dress) — each standing for its real thing.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Why might a teacher show a class a model instead of the real thing?**
**Answer:** Because the model is small, safe and easy to hold, turn over and look at from all sides — and it can be passed around or remade, which the real thing often cannot.
