# Video script — What a Model Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A model stands for a real thing.

A model car is small. You can hold it.

It shows the shape of a real car.

It cannot drive. It shows. It does not do.

A drawing can be a model too.

And a small copy of Turkmen dress is a model.

It shows the shape, the colours, the patterns.

A model helps you learn the real thing.
