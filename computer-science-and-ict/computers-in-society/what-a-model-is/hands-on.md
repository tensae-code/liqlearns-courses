# Hands-on — What a Model Is

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Model or real?
Your teacher names things: a toy car, a real car, a drawing of a house, a house, a doll, a baby. For each one, say: model, or real thing — and say why.

## What did it keep?
Look at a toy or a picture in the room. Name three things it kept from the real thing, and one thing it left out.
