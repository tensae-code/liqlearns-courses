Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Turkmenistan, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked first: no lesson in Computers in Society or elsewhere teaches what a model is. The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — computers-in-society/what-a-model-is). It sits in the Computers in Society title (computers-in-society/), where the placements file placed it.

Turkmenistan's own way: modelling is taught in Grade 1 as its own chapter (Chapter IV, Modelling), with paper and made things, not software — and the book's own examples of models include models of Turkmen national dress, so the idea is learned from the child's own culture first. This lesson keeps those examples.
