## Sub lesson 1 — A model stands for a real thing
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A model copies the look of a real thing in a way you can hold, look at from all sides and learn from — it is not the real thing, and that is exactly why it is useful.
## Sub lesson 2 — Models around us — including clothes
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Models come in kinds: a small copy you can hold, a picture or drawing that shows the thing flat, and a made copy of a real object's dress and shape — the book's own examples include models of Turkmen clothing.
