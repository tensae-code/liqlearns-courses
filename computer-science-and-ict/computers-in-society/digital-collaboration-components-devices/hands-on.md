# Hands-on — Digital collaboration components — devices are the connection

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Device census of a team
Before a group task, census the team: device per person, battery state, data access, one fallback each. File the census at the top of the group document — the lesson, applied.

## Three doors test
Open the same shared document on a phone, a tablet and a laptop (borrow what you lack). Note what each door does well and badly for editing, commenting and presenting.
