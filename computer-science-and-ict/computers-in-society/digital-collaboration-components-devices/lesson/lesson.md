# Digital collaboration components — devices are the connection

## Do it first

The team is ready. The tools are chosen. Then one member's phone battery dies, another has no laptop, and the plan stops. This short lesson is about the component everyone forgets to plan — until it stops them.

## The third component

**Devices are the third component** of digital collaboration — people, tools, and now the hardware in the hand. They are **often treated as an afterthought**, planned last or not at all.

But **smartphones, tablets and laptops are the physical media through which people actually connect** — no device, no connection, whatever the people and tools are ready to do. So **they belong in any collaboration plan**, from the first page, not as a rescue at the end.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the three components in order, then describe a collaboration that failed at the device — and the plan that would have saved it.

## Do It Yourself

1. For your next group task, write the device line of the plan: who connects by what, whose battery or data is the weak point, the fallback.
2. List what each of the three device kinds does best in a collaboration — phone, tablet, laptop — one honest line each.
