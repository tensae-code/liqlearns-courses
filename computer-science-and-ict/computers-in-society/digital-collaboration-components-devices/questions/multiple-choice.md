# Digital collaboration components — devices are the connection — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The three components of digital collaboration are —
a) people, tools, devices  b) chairs, tables, rooms  c) apps, games, videos  d) teachers, pupils, parents
**Answer: a) people, tools, devices** — the guide's review answers confirm the same three (teacher guide — evidence).

**2.** Devices in collaboration are often treated as —
a) the first concern  b) an afterthought  c) a luxury  d) a problem
**Answer: b) an afterthought** — the book's warning.

**3.** The named device kinds are —
a) radios and televisions  b) smartphones, tablets and laptops  c) printers only  d) servers only
**Answer: b) smartphones, tablets and laptops** — the three doors.

**4.** Devices matter because they are —
a) decoration  b) the physical media through which people actually connect  c) the most expensive part  d) owned by schools
**Answer: b) the physical media through which people actually connect** — connection runs through them.

**5.** A collaboration plan should include devices —
a) at the end if time allows  b) from the start, as a component  c) never  d) only for teachers
**Answer: b) from the start, as a component** — planned, not rescued.
