# Digital collaboration components — devices are the connection — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** If the people and tools are ready, the devices can be assumed.
**Answer: False** — no device, no connection.

**2.** The guide's review answers confirm people, tools and devices as the three components.
**Answer: True** — teacher guide — evidence.

**3.** A phone counts as a collaboration device.
**Answer: True** — smartphones head the list.
