# Digital collaboration components — devices are the connection — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Name the three components and the three device kinds.
**Answer:** Components: people, tools, devices. Devices: smartphones, tablets and laptops.

**2.** Why do devices belong in the plan from the start?
**Answer:** Because they are the physical media through which people actually connect — a member without a working device cannot join, whatever else is ready.
