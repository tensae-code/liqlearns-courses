# Hands-on — ICT in Daily Life and at Work

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Map your day', 'List your day's ICT touches and sort them under banking, government, industry, medicine and education (or 'other'). Add the ones you never noticed before this lesson.')

## Task two
('Judge like a citizen', 'Take one online claim you met recently. Work the citizen's qualities on it: find its source, judge it, and say what you would pass on.')
