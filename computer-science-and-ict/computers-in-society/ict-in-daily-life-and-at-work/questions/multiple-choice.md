# ICT in Daily Life and at Work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the five fields where the lesson places ICT.**
a) Banking, government, industry, medicine and education  b) Keeps, moves and checks money electronically  c) Delivers services and records through systems, not queues  d) Carries records, images and consultations by networks
**Answer: a) Banking, government, industry, medicine and education**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does ICT do in banking?**
a) Delivers services and records through systems, not queues  b) Keeps, moves and checks money electronically  c) Carries records, images and consultations by networks  d) To make the pupil's own day visible as a system
**Answer: b) Keeps, moves and checks money electronically**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does it do in government?**
