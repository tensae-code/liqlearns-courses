# Hands-on — Lateral Reading — Checking Whether Content Is True

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Read laterally** — Take a claim page you have never judged. Spend no more than two minutes on the page itself; spend ten on what other sources say about the claim and the maker. Write your verdict and your bearings.

## Task two
**Triangulate** — Choose one contested claim. Find three independent sources' positions, note where they agree and split, and write the honest one-sentence verdict a careful reader should carry away.
