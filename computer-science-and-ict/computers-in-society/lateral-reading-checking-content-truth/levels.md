## Sub lesson 1 — Reading laterally — leave the page to judge the page
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Judging a page by stepping sideways from it — seeing what other sources say about the claim and its maker; Reading the page itself more deeply — and being persuaded by its own polish; It is advertising; independent sources' accounts are evidence; From 'does this look right?' to 'who else says so?'; Fixing a claim by bearings from more than one independent point, as a position is fixed.
