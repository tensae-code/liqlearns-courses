# Lateral Reading — Checking Whether Content Is True — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Judging a page by stepping sideways from it — seeing what other sources say about the claim and its maker**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Judging a page by stepping sideways from it — seeing what other sources say about the claim and its maker** — as the answer to: What is vertical reading, its opposite?
**Answer: False — Reading the page itself more deeply — and being persuaded by its own polish**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It is advertising; independent sources' accounts are evidence**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It is advertising; independent sources' accounts are evidence** — as the answer to: How does the reader's question change?
**Answer: False — From 'does this look right?' to 'who else says so?'**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Fixing a claim by bearings from more than one independent point, as a position is fixed**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Fixing a claim by bearings from more than one independent point, as a position is fixed** — as the answer to: What must the sources in triangulation not do?
**Answer: False — Copy each other — they must be independent**
