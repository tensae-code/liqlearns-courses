# Managing an Informatics Project — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a project?**
**Answer: A bounded piece of work with a goal**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is project management?**
**Answer: What keeps the bound real — the work planned, tracked and delivered as intended**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are the plan's four questions?**
**Answer: What will be made, who does each part, when each part lands, and with what resources**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must the scope include?**
**Answer: What will be made — and what is NOT included**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why name roles instead of assuming them?**
**Answer: Unnamed work is undone work — a part with no owner has no finisher**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are milestones?**
**Answer: The schedule's fixed points by which progress is judged**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name three standing risks the book's habit names early.**
**Answer: A missing device, one person who alone knows a part, and data that has not arrived**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is a change to scope handled honestly?**
**Answer: As a change to time or resources — recorded and agreed, never silently absorbed**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are the project's documents?**
**Answer: Its memory and its proof**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 10 SMK Semester 2, Bab 3 A — informatics project management**
