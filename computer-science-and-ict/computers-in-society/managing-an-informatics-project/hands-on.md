# Hands-on — Managing an Informatics Project

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Plan a real project** — Take a small real computing job (a class database, a school web page). Write its plan: scope with exclusions, named roles, three milestones, and the resources list.

## Task two
**Risk register** — For the same project, list three risks with the early sign of each and the response you would take. Share it with a companion and add the risk they see that you missed.
