# E-learning — learning through digital devices — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five components of e-learning.
**Answer: The internet; computers or phones; digital learning resources; content developers (teachers, graphic editors); learners.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two benefits and two drawbacks of e-learning.
**Answer: Benefits (any two): any time, own pace, resources from many schools, rich multimedia, no travel/accommodation costs. Drawbacks (any two): physical isolation, weak monitoring, technology costs shutting out low-income learners.**
