# E-learning — learning through digital devices — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** In e-learning, teachers can monitor progress only weakly.
**Answer: True** — a named drawback.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** E-learning always costs more than classroom learning because of travel.
**Answer: False** — it saves travel and accommodation costs; technology cost is the drawback.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Learners are themselves a component of e-learning.
**Answer: True** — the fifth component.
