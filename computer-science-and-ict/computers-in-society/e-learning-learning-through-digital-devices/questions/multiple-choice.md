# E-learning — learning through digital devices — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** E-learning is —
a) learning only in classrooms  b) learning delivered through digital devices  c) learning from printed books only  d) learning by radio only
**Answer: b) learning delivered through digital devices** — also called online, virtual, distributed or computer-based learning.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a component of e-learning?
a) content developers such as teachers and graphic editors  b) school buses  c) paper registers  d) uniforms
**Answer: a) content developers such as teachers and graphic editors** — with internet, devices, resources and learners.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Online learning' is e-learning that —
a) uses no devices  b) runs over the internet  c) is always free  d) needs a classroom
**Answer: b) runs over the internet** — the book's naming rule.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a benefit of e-learning?
a) learn at your own pace  b) physical isolation  c) weak monitoring  d) high travel costs
**Answer: a) learn at your own pace** — the drawbacks sit in the other options.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide warns that e-learning — (teacher guide — evidence)
a) is forced on everyone  b) runs on personal commitment — nobody forces you  c) needs no effort  d) ends quickly
**Answer: b) runs on personal commitment — nobody forces you** — the freedom and the weakness are one door.
