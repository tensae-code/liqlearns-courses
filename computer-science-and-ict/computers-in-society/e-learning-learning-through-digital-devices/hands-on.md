# Hands-on — E-learning — learning through digital devices

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Component map
Draw an e-lesson you know as five labelled parts — internet, device, resources, developers, learners. Put a real name or example in each box. A missing box means a missing component; say what breaks.

## Commitment contract
Write a one-week e-learning plan for yourself: topic, time each day, how you will check your own progress (since a teacher can monitor only weakly). Sign it. Review it on day seven.
