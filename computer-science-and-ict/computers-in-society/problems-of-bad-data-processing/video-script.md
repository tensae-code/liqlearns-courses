# Video script — Problems of Bad Data Processing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Data processing fails at the stages it runs through. **Collection** faults: asking the wrong people, recording carelessly, losing part of the returns. **Keeping** faults: duplicates, contradictions, files nobody can find or read. **Processing** faults: the wrong tool for the data, a formula trusted without checking, results read as saying more than they do. Indonesia's Grade 9 chapter names these as the basic problems of data processing (permasalahan dasar pemrosesan data) because each stage's small fault becomes the next stage's false fact.
The costs are real: decisions taken on wrong numbers, money and time spent twice, and — the slowest to heal — **trust** lost in every figure the office publishes afterwards. The prevention is the processing discipline the rest of the course teaches: validate at entry, keep one clean copy of the truth, check a result against a second road before publishing, and match the tool to the data's size and kind. Bad processing is rarely bad luck; it is skipped care, and its bill always arrives.
