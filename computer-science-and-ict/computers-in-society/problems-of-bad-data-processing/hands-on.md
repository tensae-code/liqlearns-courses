# Hands-on — Problems of Bad Data Processing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Fault hunt** — Take a small processed result you know (a class average, a club total). Walk it back through the three stages and list every place a fault could have entered; say which you can rule out and how.

## Task two
**Design the care** — For a data job your school repeats (attendance, marks, fees), write the four cares into a one-page processing rule the office could actually follow.
