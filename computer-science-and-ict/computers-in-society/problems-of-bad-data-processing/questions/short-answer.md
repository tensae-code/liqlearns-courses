# Problems of Bad Data Processing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does data processing fail?**
**Answer: At every stage it runs through — collection, keeping and processing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two collection faults.**
**Answer: Asking the wrong people, recording carelessly, losing part of the returns**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two keeping faults.**
**Answer: Duplicates and contradictions; files nobody can find or read**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name two processing faults.**
**Answer: The wrong tool for the data, and a formula trusted without checking (also: results read as saying more than they do)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why name the faults stage by stage?**
**Answer: Each stage's small fault becomes the next stage's false fact**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What three costs does bad processing bring?**
**Answer: Decisions on wrong numbers, money and time spent twice, and trust lost**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which cost is slowest to heal?**
**Answer: Trust in every figure published afterwards**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What four cares prevent it?**
**Answer: Validate at entry, keep one clean copy of the truth, check results by a second road, and match the tool to the data**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Bad processing is rarely what?**
**Answer: Bad luck — it is skipped care**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 9, Bab III A.1 — basic problems of data processing**
