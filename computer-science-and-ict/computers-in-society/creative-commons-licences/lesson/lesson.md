# Creative Commons Licences

## Do it first

Copyright keeps every work closed unless its maker opens it. Class VIII teaches the opening: Creative Commons licences — the maker's own public permission, written in advance, saying what others may do with a work and on what terms.

## Sub lesson 1 — The licences — permission granted in advance

A **Creative Commons (CC) licence** is a standard permission a creator attaches to a work — a picture, a song, a text — so anyone may use it **as the licence says**, without asking each time. The licence is built from conditions: **attribution** (credit the maker) always; and, as the maker chooses, no commercial use, no changed versions, or share-alike — copies and adaptations must carry the same licence.

In your mind's eye: the licences — permission granted in advance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Using CC work — credit and obey the terms

To use a CC work lawfully, the user **reads its licence, follows its conditions, and gives the credit it asks** — the maker's name and the licence named with the work. CC is how the internet's honest sharing works: Bhutan's pupils, who learn citation and copyright in the same unit, meet CC as the bridge — work shared on purpose, used with gratitude, never mistaken for ownerless.
