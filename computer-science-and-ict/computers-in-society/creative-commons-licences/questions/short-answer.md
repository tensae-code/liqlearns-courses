# Creative Commons Licences — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a Creative Commons licence?**
**Answer: A standard public permission a creator attaches to a work, saying what others may do with it and on what terms**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does CC differ from ordinary copyright?**
**Answer: Copyright keeps a work closed unless the maker opens it; a CC licence opens it in advance**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Must a user ask the maker each time to use a CC work?**
**Answer: No — the licence is the permission, given in advance**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which condition does every CC licence carry?**
**Answer: Attribution — credit the maker**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the other conditions a maker may choose.**
**Answer: No commercial use, no changed versions, or share-alike (any of these)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does share-alike require?**
**Answer: Copies and adaptations must carry the same licence**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What three things must a CC user do?**
**Answer: Read the licence, follow its conditions, and give the credit it asks**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What credit is given?**
**Answer: The maker's name, with the licence named beside the work**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a CC work not ownerless?**
**Answer: It is shared on purpose, on terms — the maker still owns it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) In which unit does Bhutan teach CC?**
**Answer: In the information-ethics unit, with copyright and software piracy**
