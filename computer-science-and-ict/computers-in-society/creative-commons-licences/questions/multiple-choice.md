# Creative Commons Licences — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a Creative Commons licence?**
a) A standard public permission a creator attaches to a work, saying what others may do with it and on what terms  b) Copyright keeps a work closed unless the maker opens it; a CC licence opens it in advance  c) No — the licence is the permission, given in advance  d) Attribution — credit the maker
**Answer: a) A standard public permission a creator attaches to a work, saying what others may do with it and on what terms**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does CC differ from ordinary copyright?**
a) No — the licence is the permission, given in advance  b) Copyright keeps a work closed unless the maker opens it; a CC licence opens it in advance  c) Attribution — credit the maker  d) No commercial use, no changed versions, or share-alike (any of these)
**Answer: b) Copyright keeps a work closed unless the maker opens it; a CC licence opens it in advance**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Must a user ask the maker each time to use a CC work?**
a) Attribution — credit the maker  b) No commercial use, no changed versions, or share-alike (any of these)  c) No — the licence is the permission, given in advance  d) Copies and adaptations must carry the same licence
**Answer: c) No — the licence is the permission, given in advance**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which condition does every CC licence carry?**
a) No commercial use, no changed versions, or share-alike (any of these)  b) Copies and adaptations must carry the same licence  c) Read the licence, follow its conditions, and give the credit it asks  d) Attribution — credit the maker
**Answer: d) Attribution — credit the maker**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the other conditions a maker may choose.**
a) No commercial use, no changed versions, or share-alike (any of these)  b) Copies and adaptations must carry the same licence  c) Read the licence, follow its conditions, and give the credit it asks  d) The maker's name, with the licence named beside the work
**Answer: a) No commercial use, no changed versions, or share-alike (any of these)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does share-alike require?**
a) Read the licence, follow its conditions, and give the credit it asks  b) Copies and adaptations must carry the same licence  c) The maker's name, with the licence named beside the work  d) It is shared on purpose, on terms — the maker still owns it
**Answer: b) Copies and adaptations must carry the same licence**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What three things must a CC user do?**
a) The maker's name, with the licence named beside the work  b) It is shared on purpose, on terms — the maker still owns it  c) Read the licence, follow its conditions, and give the credit it asks  d) In the information-ethics unit, with copyright and software piracy
**Answer: c) Read the licence, follow its conditions, and give the credit it asks**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What credit is given?**
a) It is shared on purpose, on terms — the maker still owns it  b) In the information-ethics unit, with copyright and software piracy  c) A standard public permission a creator attaches to a work, saying what others may do with it and on what terms  d) The maker's name, with the licence named beside the work
**Answer: d) The maker's name, with the licence named beside the work**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a CC work not ownerless?**
a) It is shared on purpose, on terms — the maker still owns it  b) In the information-ethics unit, with copyright and software piracy  c) A standard public permission a creator attaches to a work, saying what others may do with it and on what terms  d) Copyright keeps a work closed unless the maker opens it; a CC licence opens it in advance
