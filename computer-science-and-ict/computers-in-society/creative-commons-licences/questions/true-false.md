# Creative Commons Licences — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a Creative Commons licence?" is: A standard public permission a creator attaches to a work, saying what others may do with it and on what terms**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How does CC differ from ordinary copyright?" is: No commercial use, no changed versions, or share-alike (any of these)**
**Answer: False** — Copyright keeps a work closed unless the maker opens it; a CC licence opens it in advance

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Must a user ask the maker each time to use a CC work?" is: No — the licence is the permission, given in advance**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which condition does every CC licence carry?" is: Read the licence, follow its conditions, and give the credit it asks**
**Answer: False** — Attribution — credit the maker

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the other conditions a maker may choose." is: No commercial use, no changed versions, or share-alike (any of these)**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does share-alike require?" is: It is shared on purpose, on terms — the maker still owns it**
**Answer: False** — Copies and adaptations must carry the same licence

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What three things must a CC user do?" is: Read the licence, follow its conditions, and give the credit it asks**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What credit is given?" is: A standard public permission a creator attaches to a work, saying what others may do with it and on what terms**
**Answer: False** — The maker's name, with the licence named beside the work

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why is a CC work not ownerless?" is: It is shared on purpose, on terms — the maker still owns it**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "In which unit does Bhutan teach CC?" is: No — the licence is the permission, given in advance**
**Answer: False** — In the information-ethics unit, with copyright and software piracy
