# Creative Commons Licences — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right. Write the letter beside each number.

1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a Creative Commons licence?
2. (Sub lesson 1) How does CC differ from ordinary copyright?
3. (Sub lesson 1) Must a user ask the maker each time to use a CC work?

5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the other conditions a maker may choose.

7. (Sub lesson 2) What three things must a CC user do?
8. (Sub lesson 2) What credit is given?
9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a CC work not ownerless?
10. (Sub lesson 2) In which unit does Bhutan teach CC?

a) Attribution — credit the maker
b) A standard public permission a creator attaches to a work, saying what others may do with it and on what terms
c) No commercial use, no changed versions, or share-alike (any of these)
d) Copyright keeps a work closed unless the maker opens it; a CC licence opens it in advance
e) Copies and adaptations must carry the same licence
f) No — the licence is the permission, given in advance
g) The maker's name, with the licence named beside the work
h) In the information-ethics unit, with copyright and software piracy
i) Read the licence, follow its conditions, and give the credit it asks
j) It is shared on purpose, on terms — the maker still owns it

**Answer key:** 1–b, 2–d, 3–f, 4–a, 5–c, 6–e, 7–i, 8–g, 9–j, 10–h
