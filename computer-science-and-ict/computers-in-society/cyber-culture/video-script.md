# Video script — Cyber Culture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Cyber culture** is the **culture that grows among people who live part of their lives online**: its own **etiquette** for greeting, disagreeing and apologising; its own **language** — abbreviations, symbols and pictures that carry tone where faces cannot; and its own habits of meeting, in **social networks** where friends, families and strangers gather. Fiji teaches it as culture, not as rules pasted on: a newcomer online, like a newcomer anywhere, must learn how the place speaks before they can be understood in it.
Cyber culture **changes the people in it**. It widens the world — a pupil in Fiji talks daily with people their grandparents could never have reached — and it thins some things: talk without faces loses patience and care, and the crowd's habits can pull a person's manners toward the loudest voices. Fiji's Year 12 reader is asked to see both: to join the culture **knowingly** — using its reach, keeping their own standards — rather than being quietly reshaped by it.
