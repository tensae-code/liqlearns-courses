# Cyber Culture — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is cyber culture?" is: The culture that grows among people who live part of their lives online**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are its three named parts?" is: Abbreviations, symbols and pictures**
**Answer: False** — Its etiquette, its language, and its habits of meeting in social networks

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What carries tone online where faces cannot?" is: Abbreviations, symbols and pictures**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why must a newcomer learn the culture?" is: In social networks — friends, families and strangers together**
**Answer: False** — Like a newcomer anywhere, they must learn how the place speaks to be understood in it

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where do people gather in cyber culture?" is: In social networks — friends, families and strangers together**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How does cyber culture widen the world?" is: Patience and care — talk without faces loses them**
**Answer: False** — A pupil in Fiji talks daily with people earlier generations could never have reached

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does it thin?" is: Patience and care — talk without faces loses them**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What pull does the crowd exert?" is: Knowingly — using its reach while keeping your own standards**
**Answer: False** — A person's manners can be pulled toward the loudest voices

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How does Fiji say to join the culture?" is: Knowingly — using its reach while keeping your own standards**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the alternative to joining knowingly?" is: The culture that grows among people who live part of their lives online**
**Answer: False** — Being quietly reshaped by it
