# Cyber Culture — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is cyber culture?**
**Answer: The culture that grows among people who live part of their lives online**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are its three named parts?**
**Answer: Its etiquette, its language, and its habits of meeting in social networks**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What carries tone online where faces cannot?**
**Answer: Abbreviations, symbols and pictures**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why must a newcomer learn the culture?**
**Answer: Like a newcomer anywhere, they must learn how the place speaks to be understood in it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where do people gather in cyber culture?**
**Answer: In social networks — friends, families and strangers together**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does cyber culture widen the world?**
**Answer: A pupil in Fiji talks daily with people earlier generations could never have reached**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does it thin?**
**Answer: Patience and care — talk without faces loses them**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What pull does the crowd exert?**
**Answer: A person's manners can be pulled toward the loudest voices**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How does Fiji say to join the culture?**
**Answer: Knowingly — using its reach while keeping your own standards**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the alternative to joining knowingly?**
**Answer: Being quietly reshaped by it**
