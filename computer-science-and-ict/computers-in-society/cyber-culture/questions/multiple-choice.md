# Cyber Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is cyber culture?**
a) The culture that grows among people who live part of their lives online  b) Its etiquette, its language, and its habits of meeting in social networks  c) Abbreviations, symbols and pictures  d) Like a newcomer anywhere, they must learn how the place speaks to be understood in it
**Answer: a) The culture that grows among people who live part of their lives online**
