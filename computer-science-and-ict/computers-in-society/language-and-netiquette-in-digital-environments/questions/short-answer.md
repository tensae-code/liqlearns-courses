# Language and Netiquette in Digital Environments — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Your friend writes to the class teacher in slang and abbreviations, and the teacher cannot follow it. Explain the two language faults.**
**Answer:** The language is not clear for its reader — heavy abbreviations and slang confuse someone who does not share them — and it is not right for the place: a teacher is a formal place that asks for full, careful, respectful language.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name three netiquette rules and say what each one stops.**
**Answer:** Any three, correctly explained — e.g. be polite and kind (stops insults); do not shout in capitals or flood (stops buried, angry-feeling groups); respect privacy (stops other people's photos, numbers and private messages being shared without permission); respect work and time (stops copying work and off-topic repeats); think before sending; put mistakes right.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Why do the manners of face-to-face life still matter in a digital environment?**
**Answer:** Because the words are still spoken to real people — and online they stay and travel further than spoken words, so politeness, privacy and care matter at least as much, not less.
