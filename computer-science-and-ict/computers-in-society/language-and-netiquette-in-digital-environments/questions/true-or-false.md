# Language and Netiquette in Digital Environments — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A message you delete is gone forever and can never be shown to anyone.**
**Answer: False** — It can be copied, saved or forwarded before you delete it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The same loose language is right for every digital place, from a friend's chat to a teacher's email.**
**Answer: False** — The place decides the language; formal places ask for full, careful words.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Flooding a group with message after message is good netiquette.**
**Answer: False** — Flooding buries everyone else's words; it breaks netiquette.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) If your message hurt someone, good netiquette includes saying so and apologising.**
**Answer: True** — Putting a mistake right is part of the manners of digital places.
