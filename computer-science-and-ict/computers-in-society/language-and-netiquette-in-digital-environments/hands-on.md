# Hands-on — Language and Netiquette in Digital Environments

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Fix the message

Write a bad online message on paper — unclear, in capital letters, rude, and off the group's topic. Now rewrite it three ways until it is clear, polite and right for its place. Read both versions aloud and say what changed.

## Manners for our group

Write the netiquette rules for a class group you belong to, in your own words — at least six rules, covering politeness, shouting and flooding, privacy, other people's work, and putting mistakes right. For each rule, write one short example of breaking it and one of keeping it.
