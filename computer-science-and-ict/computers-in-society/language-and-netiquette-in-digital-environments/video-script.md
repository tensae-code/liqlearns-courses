# Video script — Language and Netiquette in Digital Environments

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your words travel.

A message stays. It can be copied. It can go far.

No face. No voice. The words must be clear alone.

Write for the place. A teacher is not a close friend.

Now the manners. Netiquette.

Be kind. Do not shout in big letters.

Do not flood. Respect what is private.

Think before you send. Fix a mistake when you make one.
