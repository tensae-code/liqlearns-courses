# Computers in Military Systems — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What carries a military system's information?**
**Answer: Computers — its communications, sensing and calculation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do military communications networks do?**
**Answer: Link commanders and units so orders and reports move in seconds, protected by encryption**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do navigation systems fix?**
**Answer: Position exactly, from satellites, in any weather**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do radar and surveillance systems do?**
**Answer: Watch continuously, sort real threats from noise, and build the commander's picture**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why are these systems built to keep working when parts fail?**
**Answer: Because the cost of slowness or error is measured differently here**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is military logistics?**
**Answer: Tracking supplies, vehicles, fuel and spare parts across a whole country**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Whose civilian twin is logistics?**
**Answer: A supermarket's stock database — at national scale and under deadline**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do training simulators let crews do?**
**Answer: Practise dangerous work safely, failing on a screen instead of in the air**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does guided equipment calculate continuously?**
**Answer: How to hit what it is aimed at — aircraft systems, air defence, precision-guided weapons**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the topic's lesson for every field?**
**Answer: Wherever information must move fast and be right, computers will be found**
