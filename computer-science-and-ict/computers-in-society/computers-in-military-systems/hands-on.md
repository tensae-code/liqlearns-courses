# Hands-on — Computers in Military Systems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the systems
List the military computer uses from this lesson. Beside each, write its civilian twin — the same computer work in ordinary life (navigation and a delivery driver's map, logistics and a supermarket's stock). Say what is genuinely different about the military version.

## The simulator argument
Write a short paragraph arguing for or against this claim: 'A simulator can teach a pilot everything.' Use the lesson — what simulators practise safely, and what only the real thing can teach — and have a partner answer your paragraph from the other side.
