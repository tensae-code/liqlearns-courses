# Hands-on — Digital collaboration components — people first

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Duplication hunt
Re-run a finished group task on paper: lay out who did what, and circle every overlap. Rewrite the arrangement so each circled item happens once. The rewrite is the deliverable.

## Workflow in miniature
Two people, one shared list, different rooms: build a study timetable together using only a workflow you agreed first — turns, signals, a done-check. Judge the workflow, not the timetable.
