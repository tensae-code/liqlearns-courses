# Digital collaboration components — people first — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first component of digital collaboration is —
a) devices  b) people  c) software licences  d) offices
**Answer: b) people** — the heart of the system.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** People are kept in touch by —
a) luck  b) workflows and established practices  c) shouting  d) deadlines only
**Answer: b) workflows and established practices** — the carrying structure.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Remote working happens — the book names.
a) only in war  b) by schedule preference or under restrictions such as COVID-19  c) never  d) only abroad
**Answer: b) by schedule preference or under restrictions such as COVID-19** — the two named causes.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Proper arrangement and communication remove —
a) all deadlines  b) unnecessary and duplicated work  c) meetings  d) documents
**Answer: b) unnecessary and duplicated work** — the named waste.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A paragraph written three times by three people is a failure of —
a) typing  b) arrangement between people  c) the internet  d) spelling
**Answer: b) arrangement between people** — social before technical.
