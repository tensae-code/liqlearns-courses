---

**Software piracy** is the **unauthorised copying, distribution or use of software**: installing a program on more machines than its licence allows, downloading a paid program from a pirate source instead of buying it, or sharing copies with friends. The copy works — that is what makes piracy tempting and what makes it theft of a special kind: the maker loses the sale each copy replaces, though the original was never touched.
Software is protected by **copyright**: its maker holds the right to be paid for copies, and the **law** treats pirated software as stolen property. Bhutan's unit places piracy between copyright and Creative Commons for a reason: the honest roads stand on both sides of it — pay for the licence, use free software, or use work shared under an open licence. Piracy is choosing none of them.
