# Software Piracy — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is software piracy?**
**Answer: The unauthorised copying, distribution or use of software**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two forms of piracy from the lesson.**
**Answer: Installing on more machines than the licence allows, and downloading a paid program from a pirate source (any two named forms)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is piracy tempting?**
**Answer: The copy works, though no licence was paid for**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the maker lose in piracy?**
**Answer: The sale each pirate copy replaces**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What protects software in law?**
**Answer: Copyright — the maker's right to be paid for copies**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does the law treat pirated software?**
**Answer: As stolen property**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are the honest alternatives to piracy?**
**Answer: Pay for the licence, use free software, or use openly licensed work**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Between which two lessons does Bhutan place piracy?**
**Answer: Between copyright and Creative Commons**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Is sharing a copy with a friend piracy?**
**Answer: Yes — distributing a copy without the licence is piracy**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Whose work is a program?**
**Answer: Its maker's — software is work, and work is owned**
