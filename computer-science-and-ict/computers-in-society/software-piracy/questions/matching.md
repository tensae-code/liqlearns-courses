10. (Sub lesson 2) Whose work is a program?

a) The sale each pirate copy replaces
b) The unauthorised copying, distribution or use of software
c) Copyright — the maker's right to be paid for copies
d) Installing on more machines than the licence allows, and downloading a paid program from a pirate source (any two named forms)
e) As stolen property
f) The copy works, though no licence was paid for
g) Between copyright and Creative Commons
h) Its maker's — software is work, and work is owned
i) Pay for the licence, use free software, or use openly licensed work
j) Yes — distributing a copy without the licence is piracy
