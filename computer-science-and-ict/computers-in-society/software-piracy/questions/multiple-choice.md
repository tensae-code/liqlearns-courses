# Software Piracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is software piracy?**
a) The unauthorised copying, distribution or use of software  b) Installing on more machines than the licence allows, and downloading a paid program from a pirate source (any two named forms)  c) The copy works, though no licence was paid for  d) The sale each pirate copy replaces
**Answer: a) The unauthorised copying, distribution or use of software**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two forms of piracy from the lesson.**
a) The copy works, though no licence was paid for  b) Installing on more machines than the licence allows, and downloading a paid program from a pirate source (any two named forms)  c) The sale each pirate copy replaces  d) Copyright — the maker's right to be paid for copies
**Answer: b) Installing on more machines than the licence allows, and downloading a paid program from a pirate source (any two named forms)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is piracy tempting?**
a) The sale each pirate copy replaces  b) Copyright — the maker's right to be paid for copies  c) The copy works, though no licence was paid for  d) As stolen property
**Answer: c) The copy works, though no licence was paid for**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the maker lose in piracy?**
a) Copyright — the maker's right to be paid for copies  b) As stolen property  c) Pay for the licence, use free software, or use openly licensed work  d) The sale each pirate copy replaces
**Answer: d) The sale each pirate copy replaces**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What protects software in law?**
a) Copyright — the maker's right to be paid for copies  b) As stolen property  c) Pay for the licence, use free software, or use openly licensed work  d) Between copyright and Creative Commons
**Answer: a) Copyright — the maker's right to be paid for copies**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does the law treat pirated software?**
a) Pay for the licence, use free software, or use openly licensed work  b) As stolen property  c) Between copyright and Creative Commons  d) Yes — distributing a copy without the licence is piracy
**Answer: b) As stolen property**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are the honest alternatives to piracy?**
a) Between copyright and Creative Commons  b) Yes — distributing a copy without the licence is piracy  c) Pay for the licence, use free software, or use openly licensed work  d) Its maker's — software is work, and work is owned
**Answer: c) Pay for the licence, use free software, or use openly licensed work**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Between which two lessons does Bhutan place piracy?**
a) Yes — distributing a copy without the licence is piracy  b) Its maker's — software is work, and work is owned  c) The unauthorised copying, distribution or use of software  d) Between copyright and Creative Commons
**Answer: d) Between copyright and Creative Commons**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Is sharing a copy with a friend piracy?**
a) Yes — distributing a copy without the licence is piracy  b) Its maker's — software is work, and work is owned  c) The unauthorised copying, distribution or use of software  d) Installing on more machines than the licence allows, and downloading a paid program from a pirate source (any two named forms)
**Answer: a) Yes — distributing a copy without the licence is piracy**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Whose work is a program?**
a) The unauthorised copying, distribution or use of software  b) Its maker's — software is work, and work is owned  c) Installing on more machines than the licence allows, and downloading a paid program from a pirate source (any two named forms)  d) The copy works, though no licence was paid for
**Answer: b) Its maker's — software is work, and work is owned**
