# Classification of Social Media — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is classification of social media?**
**Answer: Social media can be classified — sorted into kinds by what they mainly do: sharing with friends, publishing media, discussing, reviewing, or connecting for work**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the key work in classification of social media?**
**Answer: The learner takes the common platforms and sorts each into its kind, giving the reason — what the platform is mainly used for decides its class**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does South Sudan teach classification of social media?**
**Answer: In Secondary ICT, South Sudan Grade 10 S2 Student, printed p. 112 — Classification of social media**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does classification of social media matter?**
**Answer: Because a classification turns a crowd of brand names into a small map a learner can reason about — new platforms can be placed on it as they appear**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What should a learner remember about classification of social media?**
**Answer: The book's own order for classification of social media — and how to show it in practice, not only name it**
