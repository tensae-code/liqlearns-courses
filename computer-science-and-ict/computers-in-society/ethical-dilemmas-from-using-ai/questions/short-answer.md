# Ethical Dilemmas from Using AI — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) State the learner's dilemma.**
**Answer: AI help and harm in one act — the essay exists, the learning does not**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the certainty dilemma.**
**Answer: An answer that sounds certain and is wrong — trust or check?**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the authorship dilemma.**
**Answer: Work presented as one's own that a machine largely made**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) By what questions is a dilemma judged?**
**Answer: Who gains, who is harmed, what is hidden, what would I defend if known?**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 9 Pliroforiki C, Unit 6**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) State the safety-privacy dilemma.**
**Answer: Systems that watch to protect also follow everyone**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the explanation dilemma.**
**Answer: Decisions by systems no one fully explains — the loan refused by a score**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is bias here?**
**Answer: The machine repeating its teachers' unfairness, at scale**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who answers for an AI's harm?**
**Answer: A person or organisation behind the deployment — never 'the AI did it'**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When must they be named?**
**Answer: Before the harm, not after**
