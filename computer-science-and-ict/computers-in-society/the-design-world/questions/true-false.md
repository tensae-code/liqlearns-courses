# The Design World — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "The design world includes what?" is: All the designed things around us**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Who works in the design world?" is: Useful and suited to its users**
**Answer: False** — Designers and technologists

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Design starts from what?" is: Human needs and problems**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "A designed product must be what?" is: Any everyday made thing, such as a chair, a phone or a school bag**
**Answer: False** — Useful and suited to its users

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Design technology studies what?" is: How design changes and serves society**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Give an example of a designed thing you use daily." is: Users, materials and the environment**
**Answer: False** — Any everyday made thing, such as a chair, a phone or a school bag

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Design affects how we do what?" is: Live, work and travel**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Good design considers what together?" is: Design Technology**
**Answer: False** — Users, materials and the environment

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Poor design leads to what?" is: Products that fail their users**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "The design-world lesson opens which topic?" is: Designers and technologists**
**Answer: False** — Design Technology
