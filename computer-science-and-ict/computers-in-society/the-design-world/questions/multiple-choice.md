# The Design World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The design world includes what?**
a) All the designed things around us  b) Designers and technologists  c) Human needs and problems  d) Useful and suited to its users
**Answer: a) All the designed things around us**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who works in the design world?**
a) Human needs and problems  b) Designers and technologists  c) Useful and suited to its users  d) How design changes and serves society
**Answer: b) Designers and technologists**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Design starts from what?**
a) Useful and suited to its users  b) How design changes and serves society  c) Human needs and problems  d) Any everyday made thing, such as a chair, a phone or a school bag
**Answer: c) Human needs and problems**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A designed product must be what?**
a) How design changes and serves society  b) Any everyday made thing, such as a chair, a phone or a school bag  c) Live, work and travel  d) Useful and suited to its users
**Answer: d) Useful and suited to its users**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Design technology studies what?**
a) How design changes and serves society  b) Any everyday made thing, such as a chair, a phone or a school bag  c) Live, work and travel  d) Users, materials and the environment
**Answer: a) How design changes and serves society**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Give an example of a designed thing you use daily.**
a) Live, work and travel  b) Any everyday made thing, such as a chair, a phone or a school bag  c) Users, materials and the environment  d) Products that fail their users
**Answer: b) Any everyday made thing, such as a chair, a phone or a school bag**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Design affects how we do what?**
a) Users, materials and the environment  b) Products that fail their users  c) Live, work and travel  d) Design Technology
**Answer: c) Live, work and travel**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Good design considers what together?**
a) Products that fail their users  b) Design Technology  c) All the designed things around us  d) Users, materials and the environment
**Answer: d) Users, materials and the environment**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Poor design leads to what?**
a) Products that fail their users  b) Design Technology  c) All the designed things around us  d) Designers and technologists
**Answer: a) Products that fail their users**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) The design-world lesson opens which topic?**
a) All the designed things around us  b) Design Technology  c) Designers and technologists  d) Human needs and problems
**Answer: b) Design Technology**
