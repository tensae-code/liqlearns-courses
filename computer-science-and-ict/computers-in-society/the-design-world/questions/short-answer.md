# The Design World — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The design world includes what?**
**Answer: All the designed things around us**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who works in the design world?**
**Answer: Designers and technologists**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Design starts from what?**
**Answer: Human needs and problems**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A designed product must be what?**
**Answer: Useful and suited to its users**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Design technology studies what?**
**Answer: How design changes and serves society**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Give an example of a designed thing you use daily.**
**Answer: Any everyday made thing, such as a chair, a phone or a school bag**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Design affects how we do what?**
**Answer: Live, work and travel**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Good design considers what together?**
**Answer: Users, materials and the environment**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Poor design leads to what?**
**Answer: Products that fail their users**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) The design-world lesson opens which topic?**
**Answer: Design Technology**
