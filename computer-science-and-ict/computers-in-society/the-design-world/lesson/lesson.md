# The Design World

## Do it first

Look around: almost everything you touch was designed by someone. Papua New Guinea's Grade 12 Design Technology topic opens by naming that fact — the design world. This lesson walks into it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Design is everywhere

The **design world** includes **all the designed things around us**. It is the world of **designers and technologists**, and every design in it starts from **human needs and problems**. A designed product must be **useful and suited to its users**.

[Picture — Design is everywhere]

## Sub lesson 2 — Roles of design technology

**Design technology** studies **how design changes and serves society**. Design affects how we **live, work and travel**, and good design considers **users, materials and the environment** together. Poor design leads to products that fail their users. The design-world lesson opens the guide's Design Technology topic.
