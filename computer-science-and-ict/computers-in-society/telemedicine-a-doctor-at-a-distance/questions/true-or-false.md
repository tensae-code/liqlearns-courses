# Telemedicine — a doctor at a distance — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** In telemedicine the patient requests an online visit, consults a doctor and pays online.
**Answer: True** — the book's visit order.

**2.** Telemedicine is only for emergencies in big cities.
**Answer: False** — it began with remote patients and scarce staff, and is now a mainstream convenience too.
