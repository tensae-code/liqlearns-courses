# Telemedicine — a doctor at a distance — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1.** Name three advantages of telemedicine.
**Answer: It crosses geography; cuts travel and lodging costs; brings specialists to remote patients. (Guide adds: more frequent contact; easier follow-up and monitoring.)**

**2.** State telemedicine's limit in the book's words, shortly.
**Answer: Rural areas may not have the training and equipment it needs.**
