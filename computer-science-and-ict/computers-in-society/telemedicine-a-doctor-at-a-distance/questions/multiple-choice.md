# Telemedicine — a doctor at a distance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Telemedicine is —
a) medicine sold online only  b) caring for patients remotely when caregiver and patient are not in the same place  c) a hospital building  d) self-treatment with no doctor
**Answer: b) caring for patients remotely when caregiver and patient are not in the same place** — the book's definition.

**2.** Telemedicine was first made for —
a) city shoppers  b) patients far from health facilities or where medical staff are scarce  c) gamers  d) bankers
**Answer: b) patients far from health facilities or where medical staff are scarce** — distance and scarcity came first.

**3.** At home, patients can measure and track —
a) temperature, glucose and blood pressure  b) rainfall only  c) prices  d) marks
**Answer: a) temperature, glucose and blood pressure** — the book's three readings.

**4.** A limit of telemedicine is —
a) it crosses geography  b) the training and equipment rural areas may not have  c) it cuts travel costs  d) it brings specialists closer
**Answer: b) the training and equipment rural areas may not have** — the advantage options are the other list.
