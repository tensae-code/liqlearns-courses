# Hands-on — Telemedicine — a doctor at a distance

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Readings diary
For three days, if a thermometer or any home measure is available, record one reading morning and evening in a diary table — the same table a patient would send a doctor. No device? Record pulse by counting, and note the limit honestly.

## Visit script
Write the script of a remote visit: the patient's request, three questions the doctor asks, the readings sent, the advice given. Read it aloud in pairs — patient in one room corner, doctor in the other.
