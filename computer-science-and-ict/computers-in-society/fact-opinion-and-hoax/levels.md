# Fact, Opinion and Hoax — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Fact and opinion — the checkable and the judged
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A statement that can be checked against the world; A person's judgement — what they think, prefer or conclude; A fact is settled by checking; an opinion can be wise or foolish but checking alone does not settle it; Facts kept checkable, opinions owned — 'I think' is the honest uniform of an opinion; A statement made to deceive, built to be believed and forwarded.

## Sub lesson 2 — Hoax — the lie built to travel
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: No accountable maker, no inspectable evidence, and pressure to share before thinking; Fact — sweetened with emotion and aimed at a crowd; Pause, and name what kind of statement it is; Checking it against a source whose business is truth — and not forwarding it; Grade 7, Bab 3 H — fact, opinion and hoax.
