# Fact, Opinion and Hoax — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A statement that can be checked against the world**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A statement that can be checked against the world** — as the answer to: What is an opinion?
**Answer: False — A person's judgement — what they think, prefer or conclude**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A fact is settled by checking; an opinion can be wise or foolish but checking alone does not settle it**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A fact is settled by checking; an opinion can be wise or foolish but checking alone does not settle it** — as the answer to: How does honest writing handle the pair?
**Answer: False — Facts kept checkable, opinions owned — 'I think' is the honest uniform of an opinion**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A statement made to deceive, built to be believed and forwarded**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A statement made to deceive, built to be believed and forwarded** — as the answer to: What three marks does a hoax show?
**Answer: False — No accountable maker, no inspectable evidence, and pressure to share before thinking**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Fact — sweetened with emotion and aimed at a crowd**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Fact — sweetened with emotion and aimed at a crowd** — as the answer to: What is the first move of the defence?
**Answer: False — Pause, and name what kind of statement it is**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Checking it against a source whose business is truth — and not forwarding it**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Checking it against a source whose business is truth — and not forwarding it** — as the answer to: Where does Indonesia teach this?
**Answer: False — Grade 7, Bab 3 H — fact, opinion and hoax**
