# Fact, Opinion and Hoax — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a fact?**
**Answer: A statement that can be checked against the world**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is an opinion?**
**Answer: A person's judgement — what they think, prefer or conclude**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a fact settled, and an opinion not?**
**Answer: A fact is settled by checking; an opinion can be wise or foolish but checking alone does not settle it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does honest writing handle the pair?**
**Answer: Facts kept checkable, opinions owned — 'I think' is the honest uniform of an opinion**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a hoax?**
**Answer: A statement made to deceive, built to be believed and forwarded**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What three marks does a hoax show?**
**Answer: No accountable maker, no inspectable evidence, and pressure to share before thinking**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a hoax dressed as?**
**Answer: Fact — sweetened with emotion and aimed at a crowd**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the first move of the defence?**
**Answer: Pause, and name what kind of statement it is**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What kills a hoax's chain?**
**Answer: Checking it against a source whose business is truth — and not forwarding it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 7, Bab 3 H — fact, opinion and hoax**
