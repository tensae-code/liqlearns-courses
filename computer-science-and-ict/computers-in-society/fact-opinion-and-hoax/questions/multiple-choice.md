# Fact, Opinion and Hoax — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a fact?**
a) A statement that can be checked against the world  b) A person's judgement — what they think, prefer or conclude  c) A fact is settled by checking; an opinion can be wise or foolish but checking alone does not settle it  d) Facts kept checkable, opinions owned — 'I think' is the honest uniform of an opinion
