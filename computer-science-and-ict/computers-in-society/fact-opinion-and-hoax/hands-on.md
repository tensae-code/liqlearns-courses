# Hands-on — Fact, Opinion and Hoax

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Sort the statements** — Collect six statements from one news page or feed. Label each fact, opinion or suspected hoax, and write the check you would run on each.

## Task two
**Break a chain** — Describe a hoax you have received (without forwarding it). Name its three marks in it, and write the message you would send back to the person who sent it to you.
