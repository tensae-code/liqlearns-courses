# The National ID project and Digital Ethiopia 2025

## Do it first

Everything the last two lessons said about one person's identity — the image, the verification, the doors it opens — now imagine it built for an entire country at once. That building project has a name, and a strategy behind it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The project and its promise

At national level **Ethiopia is building a robust, reliable digital identification for all Ethiopians and legal residents** through the **National ID project**.

Robust and reliable are the load-bearing words: an identification the whole country's services can stand on, for everyone entitled to it — not a pilot for a few.

## Sub lesson 2 — Part of Digital Ethiopia 2025

The project **started as part of the Digital Ethiopia 2025 strategy** — the country's own plan for its digital build-out, with identity as one of its foundations.

The guide (teacher guide — evidence) widens the frame: digital identity is **a catalyst for trade, governance, social protection, financial inclusion, domestic resource mobilisation, security and human rights**, because a renewable digital identity lets a person **choose which credentials to give each provider** and **builds trust between the parties** — and **Ethiopia's National ID project is named as the local attempt to win those benefits**.
