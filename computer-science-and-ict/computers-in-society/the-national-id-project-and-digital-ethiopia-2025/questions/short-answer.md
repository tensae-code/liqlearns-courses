# The National ID project and Digital Ethiopia 2025 — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the National ID project's promise in one sentence.
**Answer:** Ethiopia is building a robust, reliable digital identification for all Ethiopians and legal residents.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four goods the guide says digital identity catalyses.
**Answer:** Any four of: trade, governance, social protection, financial inclusion, domestic resource mobilisation, security, human rights (teacher guide — evidence).
