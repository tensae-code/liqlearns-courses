# The National ID project and Digital Ethiopia 2025 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The National ID project is building digital identification for —
a) only students  b) all Ethiopians and legal residents  c) only officials  d) only travellers
**Answer: b) all Ethiopians and legal residents** — the project's people.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The identification is described as —
a) temporary and partial  b) robust and reliable  c) secret  d) optional for services
**Answer: b) robust and reliable** — the book's two words.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The project is the book's example of digital identity management at —
a) family scale  b) country scale  c) school scale  d) company scale
**Answer: b) country scale** — one system, a whole population.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The project started as part of —
a) a bank merger  b) the Digital Ethiopia 2025 strategy  c) a school reform  d) a trade fair
**Answer: b) the Digital Ethiopia 2025 strategy** — the named strategy.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the guide's frame, a digital identity builds — between person and provider.
a) suspicion  b) trust, by letting the person choose which credentials to give  c) distance  d) fees
**Answer: b) trust, by letting the person choose which credentials to give** — the mechanism named (teacher guide — evidence).
