# Hands-on — The National ID project and Digital Ethiopia 2025

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Foundation sketch
Draw the strategy as a building: Digital Ethiopia 2025 as the plan, the National ID as one foundation stone, and the services (finance, health, education, mobile) as floors that need it. Label what cracks if the foundation is weak.

## Trust interview
Ask two adults what would make them trust a national digital ID with their credentials. Map their answers onto the guide's mechanism: choice of credentials, trust between parties.
