# Inclusive Technology — Technology for All Learners — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Inclusion — who is shut out, and by what
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Technology designed so nobody is shut out by a barrier they did not choose; In the design, not in the learner; Video with no captions; Language (no version the learner reads), means (a cheap phone, a slow connection), and input (no mouse possible); Grade 11, Theme 4 — Technologies ensuring inclusion.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ensuring inclusion — the maker's toolkit
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Captions and transcripts; keyboard-only operation; scalable text and high contrast (also screen-reader-friendly structure, plain/multi-language versions, low-end modes); Retrofitting inclusion is costlier and weaker — inclusion is a design decision, not a patch; A person: can this learner, with their body, language and phone, finish the lesson?; They help learners in noise, learners of the language, and anyone searching the text; Design that every learner can actually use — inclusion is part of done.
