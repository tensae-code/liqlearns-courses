# Video script — Inclusive Technology — Technology for All Learners

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Inclusive technology** is technology designed so nobody is shut out by a barrier they did not choose: the learner who cannot see the screen, cannot hear the audio, cannot use a mouse, reads another language, or owns only a cheap phone on a slow connection. The barriers live in the **design**, not in the learner: text with no spoken form shuts out the blind learner; video with no captions shuts out the deaf learner. Armenia's Grade 11 book gives inclusion its own theme (Theme 4, 'Technologies ensuring inclusion').
Inclusion is ensured by building the second path in from the start: **captions and transcripts** for audio and video, **text-to-speech and screen readers** welcomed by clean structure, **keyboard-only operation**, **scalable text and high-contrast** display, **plain-language and multi-language** versions, and **low-end modes** that work on cheap devices. The test of inclusion is not a checklist but a person: can *this* learner, with *their* body, language and phone, finish the lesson? Design that passes that test is simply design finished.
