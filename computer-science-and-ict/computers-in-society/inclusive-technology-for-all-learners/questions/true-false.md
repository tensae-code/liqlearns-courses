# Inclusive Technology — Technology for All Learners — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Technology designed so nobody is shut out by a barrier they did not choose**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Technology designed so nobody is shut out by a barrier they did not choose** — as the answer to: Where do the barriers live — the learner or the design?
**Answer: False — In the design, not in the learner**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Video with no captions**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Video with no captions** — as the answer to: Name three more barriers beyond body.
**Answer: False — Language (no version the learner reads), means (a cheap phone, a slow connection), and input (no mouse possible)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Grade 11, Theme 4 — Technologies ensuring inclusion**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Grade 11, Theme 4 — Technologies ensuring inclusion** — as the answer to: Name three tools from the inclusion toolkit.
**Answer: False — Captions and transcripts; keyboard-only operation; scalable text and high contrast (also screen-reader-friendly structure, plain/multi-language versions, low-end modes)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Retrofitting inclusion is costlier and weaker — inclusion is a design decision, not a patch**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Retrofitting inclusion is costlier and weaker — inclusion is a design decision, not a patch** — as the answer to: What is the real test of inclusion?
**Answer: False — A person: can this learner, with their body, language and phone, finish the lesson?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: They help learners in noise, learners of the language, and anyone searching the text**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: They help learners in noise, learners of the language, and anyone searching the text** — as the answer to: What does 'design finished' mean here?
**Answer: False — Design that every learner can actually use — inclusion is part of done**
