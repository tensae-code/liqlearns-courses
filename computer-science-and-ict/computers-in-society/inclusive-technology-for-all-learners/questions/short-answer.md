# Inclusive Technology — Technology for All Learners — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is inclusive technology?**
**Answer: Technology designed so nobody is shut out by a barrier they did not choose**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the barriers live — the learner or the design?**
**Answer: In the design, not in the learner**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What shuts out a deaf learner from a video lesson?**
**Answer: Video with no captions**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name three more barriers beyond body.**
**Answer: Language (no version the learner reads), means (a cheap phone, a slow connection), and input (no mouse possible)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Armenia teach inclusion?**
**Answer: Grade 11, Theme 4 — Technologies ensuring inclusion**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three tools from the inclusion toolkit.**
**Answer: Captions and transcripts; keyboard-only operation; scalable text and high contrast (also screen-reader-friendly structure, plain/multi-language versions, low-end modes)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why must the second path be built from the start?**
**Answer: Retrofitting inclusion is costlier and weaker — inclusion is a design decision, not a patch**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the real test of inclusion?**
**Answer: A person: can this learner, with their body, language and phone, finish the lesson?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do captions help beyond deaf learners?**
**Answer: They help learners in noise, learners of the language, and anyone searching the text**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does 'design finished' mean here?**
**Answer: Design that every learner can actually use — inclusion is part of done**
