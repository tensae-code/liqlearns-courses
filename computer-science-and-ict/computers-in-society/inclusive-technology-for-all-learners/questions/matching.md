# Inclusive Technology — Technology for All Learners — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is inclusive technology?** → **Technology designed so nobody is shut out by a barrier they did not choose**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the barriers live — the learner or the design?** → **In the design, not in the learner**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What shuts out a deaf learner from a video lesson?** → **Video with no captions**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name three more barriers beyond body.** → **Language (no version the learner reads), means (a cheap phone, a slow connection), and input (no mouse possible)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Armenia teach inclusion?** → **Grade 11, Theme 4 — Technologies ensuring inclusion**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three tools from the inclusion toolkit.** → **Captions and transcripts; keyboard-only operation; scalable text and high contrast (also screen-reader-friendly structure, plain/multi-language versions, low-end modes)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why must the second path be built from the start?** → **Retrofitting inclusion is costlier and weaker — inclusion is a design decision, not a patch**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the real test of inclusion?** → **A person: can this learner, with their body, language and phone, finish the lesson?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do captions help beyond deaf learners?** → **They help learners in noise, learners of the language, and anyone searching the text**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does 'design finished' mean here?** → **Design that every learner can actually use — inclusion is part of done**
