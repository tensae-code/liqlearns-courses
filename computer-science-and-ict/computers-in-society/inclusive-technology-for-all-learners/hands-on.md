# Hands-on — Inclusive Technology — Technology for All Learners

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Barrier hunt** — Take one lesson page or app you use. List every barrier you can find for: a blind learner, a deaf learner, a learner with no mouse, a learner with a cheap phone. Rank them by how fully they shut the learner out.

## Task two
**Second path** — Fix the worst barrier from your hunt in one artefact: add captions/transcript, a keyboard path, or a low-end version. Test it the way the shut-out learner would.
