# Telegram — messaging over Wi-Fi and mobile internet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Telegram is —
a) a video-sharing website  b) an online messaging application  c) a spreadsheet  d) a web browser
**Answer: b) an online messaging application**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Telegram messages travel over —
a) Wi-Fi or mobile internet  b) printed post  c) radio only  d) cable television
**Answer: a) Wi-Fi or mobile internet**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these can Telegram send?
a) location information and documents  b) printed books  c) computer hardware  d) electricity
**Answer: a) location information and documents**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Telegram can show —
a) the weather  b) whether your communication partner is online or not  c) your marks  d) the time in London only
**Answer: b) whether your communication partner is online or not**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The first step in creating an account is —
a) start chatting  b) install the Telegram application  c) send a photo  d) add a picture
**Answer: b) install the Telegram application**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The account is made with —
a) an email address only  b) your phone number, then a username and a picture  c) a password from the teacher  d) a bank card
**Answer: b) your phone number, then a username and a picture**
