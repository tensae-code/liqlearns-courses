# Hands-on — Telegram — messaging over Wi-Fi and mobile internet

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Seven-kind send drill
On paper chat screens, 'send' one of each kind in turn — draw the photo, the document, the location pin. Label each with its kind. Then check a partner's set against the seven.

## Step-order cards
Write the six steps on cards, shuffle, rebuild the order, and have a partner check against the lesson. Creating the account on a real phone, in the real order, is how this skill is assessed.
