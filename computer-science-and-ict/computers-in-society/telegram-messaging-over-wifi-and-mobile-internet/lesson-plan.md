# Lesson plan — Telegram — messaging over Wi-Fi and mobile internet

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** Telegram — messaging over Wi-Fi and mobile internet
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 14 — Computers in Society of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 4 — Internet. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Telegram is a messaging application that sends texts, photos, videos, audio files, location, contacts and documents to your contacts over Wi-Fi or mobile internet — and shows whether your partner is online; an account is made by installing the app, entering your phone number, and adding a username and picture.
**This lesson's own level:** it is a doing-lesson standing beside the Facebook lesson: same title, second platform — the learner compares as they go.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What Telegram sends** — an online messaging application over Wi-Fi or mobile internet sending texts, photos, videos, audio files, location, contacts and documents, and showing whether the partner is online
2. **Creating a Telegram account — the steps in order** — install the app, enter the phone number, add username and picture, find a friend with Telegram, start chatting, send a photo, video, document, contact or location

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Social networks — connecting in virtual communities. Recommended, never forced. A learner can land here directly; the lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what Telegram is and the seven kinds of thing it sends.
2. Say how messages travel: over Wi-Fi or mobile internet — and that the partner's online state shows.
3. Create an account by the six steps in their exact order.

## What you need
A phone with the Telegram application for the real account; paper and a pencil for rehearsal.
