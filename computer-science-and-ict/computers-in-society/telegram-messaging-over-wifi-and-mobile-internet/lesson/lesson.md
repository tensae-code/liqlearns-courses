# Telegram — messaging over Wi-Fi and mobile internet

## Do it first

You want to send a friend three things at once: a photo from yesterday, a document for school, and the place where you are standing. One app in this lesson carries all three — and tells you whether your friend is there to receive them. Name what you would look for on its screen.

## Sub lesson 1 — What Telegram sends

**Telegram** is an online messaging application. It sends messages to your contacts over **Wi-Fi or mobile internet**: **texts, photos, videos, audio files, location information, contacts and documents**. It can also **show whether your communication partner is online or not**. Watch the GIF: the message leaves, and the partner's 'online' mark appears.

## Sub lesson 2 — Creating a Telegram account — the steps in order

Create the account in this order:

1. **Install the Telegram application.**
2. **Enter your phone number.**
3. **Add your username and a picture.**
4. **Find a friend with Telegram.**
5. **Start chatting.**
6. **Send a photo, video, document, contact or your current location.**
