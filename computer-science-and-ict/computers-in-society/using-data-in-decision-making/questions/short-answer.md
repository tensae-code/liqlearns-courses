# Using Data in Decision Making — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name data's four services to a decision.**
**Answer: Describes the present, compares options, predicts, checks afterwards**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In what must options be compared?**
**Answer: The same measures**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the afterwards check ask?**
**Answer: Did the chosen option do what was promised?**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a decision with data guaranteed?**
**Answer: Examinable — not guaranteed right**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What can be done to examinable reasons?**
**Answer: Shown, challenged and improved**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four limits.**
**Answer: Past-bound, measures only the measurable, can be wrong or gamed, never makes the decision**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What escapes the library counter?**
**Answer: Its quiet value**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Who makes the decision, finally?**
**Answer: People, by their values**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does data say, and people choose?**
**Answer: Data says costs and earnings; people choose what matters**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the mature user hold?**
**Answer: Both halves — deciding with data, knowing where it stopped**
