The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **scientific method of inquiry** is a disciplined way of asking: start from an **observation** and a sharp **question**; propose a **hypothesis** — an answer stated so it could prove wrong; design a **test** (experiment or observation plan) whose result can support or refute it; and record **evidence** honestly, including the results you did not hope for. Armenia's Grade 10 book closes with scientific methods (Lesson 34, Theme 24) — the computing course handing its learners the general method of finding out.
A test is fair when it changes **one variable at a time** and holds the rest still (controls), and when it can be **repeated** by others with the same result. Science then shares: methods and data are published so others can **review, repeat and challenge** — peer review is organised doubt. In computing the same method runs A/B tests, benchmarks and debugging itself: a bug report is a hypothesis ('it fails when…') waiting for its test.
