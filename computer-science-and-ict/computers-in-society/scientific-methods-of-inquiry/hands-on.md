# Hands-on — Scientific Methods of Inquiry

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**One-variable test** — Ask a computing question (does this program sort faster with 100 or 1,000 items? does this page load faster with pictures off?). State a falsifiable hypothesis, then test changing one variable at a time.

## Task two
**Write the bug hypothesis** — Take a program fault you know. Write it as a scientific hypothesis ('it fails when…'), the test that could refute it, and the evidence you would record.
