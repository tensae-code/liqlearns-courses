## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What terms and conditions are** — **Terms and conditions** are the rules a work's owner sets for its use: who may use it, for what, for how long, at what price or free, and what may never be done with it. They turn the owner's rights — the copyright of the companion lessons — into a usable agreement: the user who accepts the terms gains the use; the user who breaks them loses it, and the law stands behind the sheet. A work with no terms is not free for all — the owner's full rights apply by default.
2. **Making the sheet — the class project** — The Grade 7 session has each team **make a terms and conditions sheet** for a work of theirs: name the work and its owner; state what use is **allowed** (see, copy for study, perform, build on); state what is **forbidden** (selling it, claiming it, changing it without credit); state the **price or freedom** and the **time** it lasts; and end with what happens when the terms are broken. Writing both columns — allowed and forbidden — is the whole craft: a sheet that only forbids teaches nothing about the use it exists to grant.

Writing the sheet teaches what reading never does: every line is a decision about what you allow. (lesson/lesson.md).
