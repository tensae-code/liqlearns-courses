# Terms and Conditions for Using a Work — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What terms and conditions are
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The rules a work's owner sets for its use; Who may use it, for what, and at what price (also how long, and what is forbidden); The use of the work; The use is lost, and the law stands behind the sheet; No — the owner's full rights apply by default.

## Sub lesson 2 — Making the sheet — the class project
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The work and its owner; Copying for study and performing (also seeing, building on); Selling it and claiming it as yours (also uncredited changing); The price or freedom, and how long the terms last; A sheet that only forbids teaches nothing about the use it grants.
