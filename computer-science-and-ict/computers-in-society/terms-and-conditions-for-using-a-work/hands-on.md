# Hands-on — Terms and Conditions for Using a Work

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Write the sheet** — Choose a class work — a poster, song or program. Write its full terms sheet: owner, allowed, forbidden, price or freedom, time, and the breaking clause.

## Task two
**Read one like a writer** — Take a real app's terms summary. Find its allowed and forbidden columns in the legal words, and one line you would rewrite for your own sheet.
