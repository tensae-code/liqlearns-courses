# Terms and Conditions for Using a Work — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are terms and conditions?** → **The rules a work's owner sets for its use**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three things terms can settle.** → **Who may use it, for what, and at what price (also how long, and what is forbidden)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a user gain by accepting terms?** → **The use of the work**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when terms are broken?** → **The use is lost, and the law stands behind the sheet**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is a work with no terms free for all?** → **No — the owner's full rights apply by default**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What must a terms sheet name first?** → **The work and its owner**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two allowed uses a sheet might grant.** → **Copying for study and performing (also seeing, building on)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two forbidden uses.** → **Selling it and claiming it as yours (also uncredited changing)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two money-and-time facts must the sheet state?** → **The price or freedom, and how long the terms last**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why must the sheet have an 'allowed' column?** → **A sheet that only forbids teaches nothing about the use it grants**
