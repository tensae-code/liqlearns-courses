# Terms and Conditions for Using a Work — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are terms and conditions?**
**Answer: The rules a work's owner sets for its use**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three things terms can settle.**
**Answer: Who may use it, for what, and at what price (also how long, and what is forbidden)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a user gain by accepting terms?**
**Answer: The use of the work**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when terms are broken?**
**Answer: The use is lost, and the law stands behind the sheet**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is a work with no terms free for all?**
**Answer: No — the owner's full rights apply by default**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What must a terms sheet name first?**
**Answer: The work and its owner**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two allowed uses a sheet might grant.**
**Answer: Copying for study and performing (also seeing, building on)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two forbidden uses.**
**Answer: Selling it and claiming it as yours (also uncredited changing)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two money-and-time facts must the sheet state?**
**Answer: The price or freedom, and how long the terms last**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why must the sheet have an 'allowed' column?**
**Answer: A sheet that only forbids teaches nothing about the use it grants**
