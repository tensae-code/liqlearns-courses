# Terms and Conditions for Using a Work — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The rules a work's owner sets for its use**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The rules a work's owner sets for its use** — as the answer to: Name three things terms can settle.
**Answer: False — Who may use it, for what, and at what price (also how long, and what is forbidden)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The use of the work**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The use of the work** — as the answer to: What happens when terms are broken?
**Answer: False — The use is lost, and the law stands behind the sheet**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: No — the owner's full rights apply by default**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: No — the owner's full rights apply by default** — as the answer to: What must a terms sheet name first?
**Answer: False — The work and its owner**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Copying for study and performing (also seeing, building on)**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Copying for study and performing (also seeing, building on)** — as the answer to: Name two forbidden uses.
**Answer: False — Selling it and claiming it as yours (also uncredited changing)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The price or freedom, and how long the terms last**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The price or freedom, and how long the terms last** — as the answer to: Why must the sheet have an 'allowed' column?
**Answer: False — A sheet that only forbids teaches nothing about the use it grants**
