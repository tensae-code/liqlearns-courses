# Terms and Conditions for Using a Work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are terms and conditions?**
a) The rules a work's owner sets for its use  b) Who may use it, for what, and at what price (also how long, and what is forbidden)  c) The use of the work  d) The use is lost, and the law stands behind the sheet
**Answer: a**

a) Who may use it, for what, and at what price (also how long, and what is forbidden)  b) The use of the work  c) The use is lost, and the law stands behind the sheet  d) No — the owner's full rights apply by default
