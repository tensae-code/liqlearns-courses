# Giving Peer Feedback on a Programming Project — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The form asks, for each member, first for —**
a) Two things they did well  b) Their marks  c) Their age
**Answer: a) Two things they did well** — Two specific things done well.

**2. The less-well part must also carry —**
a) A punishment  b) What could be done differently  c) Nothing else
**Answer: b) What could be done differently** — A difficulty plus a different way for next time.

**3. The third move names each member's —**
a) Mistakes again  b) Coding strengths  c) Favourite colour
**Answer: b) Coding strengths** — The particular thing they are good at in the group's coding.

**4. Good feedback in this lesson is —**
a) General and vague  b) Specific, about the work, and kind  c) Only positive, always
**Answer: b) Specific, about the work, and kind** — Specific enough to act on, aimed at the work.
