# Giving Peer Feedback on a Programming Project — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Write a model less-well item for a member who often talked over others' ideas.**
**Answer:** A sound answer names the difficulty in work terms (ideas were cut off before the group could test them) and pairs it with a different way (each idea gets a hearing and a quick test before deciding), without attacking the person.

**2. Why does the form ask strengths separately, when done-well is also positive?**
**Answer:** A sound answer: done-well items are moments in this project; a named coding strength is the person's standing ability — knowing it tells the member, and the group, what to build on in the next project.
