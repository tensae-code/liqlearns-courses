# Hands-on — Business Money Revenue Profit Budgets Loans

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 9 §9.5 Financial Concepts for Business (§9.5.1 revenue and profit; expenses and budgeting — a Rawalpindi bakery's monthly budget; §9.5.2 investment and savings — gold as saving in Pakistan; §9.5.3 loans and interest) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
