# Prototyping Screen Designs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a screen prototype?**
**Answer: A working rough version of the designed screens**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) With what is it built in Malaysia's course?**
**Answer: Java in NetBeans**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is deliberately left out?**
**Answer: The database, the full workings, and the polish**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must the prototype keep?**
**Answer: Everything the interaction depends on — layout, order, wording, what happens on each click**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the prototype exist to test?**
**Answer: The interaction**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why is roughness a virtue?**
**Answer: Built fast, it can be changed in minutes when evaluation condemns a screen**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When is the prototype made?**
**Answer: Before building the system behind the screens**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Moving a button costs a drag on a prototype, and what in a finished system?**
**Answer: A rebuild**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Into what is the prototype thrown?**
**Answer: The loop — users try it, findings land, it changes, users try again**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is a prototype nobody may criticise?**
**Answer: A finished system wearing a false name**
