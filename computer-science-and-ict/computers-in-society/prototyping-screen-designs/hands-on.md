# Hands-on — Prototyping Screen Designs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Prototype two screens', 'For the library self-checkout brief, build (on paper as a click-through, or in NetBeans if available) prototypes of the welcome screen and the borrow screen. List what you deliberately left out.')

## Task two
('Price a change', 'Evaluation finds the 'Borrow' button is mistaken for 'Return'. State the change on your prototype and its cost; then state what the same change would touch in a finished system (database? reports? training?) and its cost there.')
