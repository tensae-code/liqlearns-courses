# Citing Sources — the International Standard — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Why cite — the reader must be able to find it
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A promise that an idea, figure or sentence came from a work the reader can find; It credits the maker, lets the reader check the claim at its source, and separates your words from borrowed ones; Plagiarism — presenting borrowed work as your own; Author, title, where it was published, and when; So any reader in any country can follow the citation home.

Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: In the Tools menu; Its kind — book, website, journal — and its facts; The citations in the text and the bibliography, in the chosen style; Cite everything borrowed, cite it where it is used, and never list a work that was not opened; Grade 11, Bab I E — enrichment, citing with the international standard.
