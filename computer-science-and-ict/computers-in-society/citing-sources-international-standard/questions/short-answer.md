# Citing Sources — the International Standard — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a citation?**
**Answer: A promise that an idea, figure or sentence came from a work the reader can find**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three jobs does citing do?**
**Answer: It credits the maker, lets the reader check the claim at its source, and separates your words from borrowed ones**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which line does citing keep you from crossing?**
**Answer: Plagiarism — presenting borrowed work as your own**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What facts does the international standard name, in order?**
**Answer: Author, title, where it was published, and when**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why one agreed shape everywhere?**
**Answer: So any reader in any country can follow the citation home**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where is the citation menu in Google Docs?**
**Answer: In the Tools menu**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is chosen and filled when adding a source?**
**Answer: Its kind — book, website, journal — and its facts**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the tool build from the added sources?**
**Answer: The citations in the text and the bibliography, in the chosen style**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What duties stay with the writer, not the tool?**
**Answer: Cite everything borrowed, cite it where it is used, and never list a work that was not opened**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 11, Bab I E — enrichment, citing with the international standard**
