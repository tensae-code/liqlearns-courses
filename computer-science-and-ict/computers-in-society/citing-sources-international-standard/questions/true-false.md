# Citing Sources — the International Standard — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A promise that an idea, figure or sentence came from a work the reader can find**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A promise that an idea, figure or sentence came from a work the reader can find** — as the answer to: What three jobs does citing do?
**Answer: False — It credits the maker, lets the reader check the claim at its source, and separates your words from borrowed ones**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Plagiarism — presenting borrowed work as your own**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Plagiarism — presenting borrowed work as your own** — as the answer to: What facts does the international standard name, in order?
**Answer: False — Author, title, where it was published, and when**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: So any reader in any country can follow the citation home**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: So any reader in any country can follow the citation home** — as the answer to: Where is the citation menu in Google Docs?
**Answer: False — In the Tools menu**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Its kind — book, website, journal — and its facts**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Its kind — book, website, journal — and its facts** — as the answer to: What does the tool build from the added sources?
**Answer: False — The citations in the text and the bibliography, in the chosen style**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Cite everything borrowed, cite it where it is used, and never list a work that was not opened**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Cite everything borrowed, cite it where it is used, and never list a work that was not opened** — as the answer to: Where does Indonesia teach this?
**Answer: False — Grade 11, Bab I E — enrichment, citing with the international standard**
