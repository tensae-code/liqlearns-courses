# Citing Sources — the International Standard — matching

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a citation?** → **A promise that an idea, figure or sentence came from a work the reader can find**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three jobs does citing do?** → **It credits the maker, lets the reader check the claim at its source, and separates your words from borrowed ones**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which line does citing keep you from crossing?** → **Plagiarism — presenting borrowed work as your own**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What facts does the international standard name, in order?** → **Author, title, where it was published, and when**
