# Hands-on — Citing Sources — the International Standard

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Cite it properly** — Take a piece of your own writing that used one book and one website. Add both sources through Google Docs' citation menu (or by hand in the standard order) and produce the bibliography.

## Task two
**Spot the gap** — Find a paragraph online that states a striking figure with no citation. Write what a reader would need to check it, and what the missing citation should have contained.
