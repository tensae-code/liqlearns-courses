# Lesson plan — IT for entrepreneurship — starting an IT-based business

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** IT for entrepreneurship — starting an IT-based business
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 14 — Computers in Society of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 11, Unit 1 — Information Systems and Its Applications. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an IT-enabled business runs on IT as its platform, needs little starting capital, and opens youth employment — as RIDE did in Ethiopia, and as a tutoring service or a phone catalogue still can.
**This lesson's own level:** it closes Unit 1 of this band: it stands on every application lesson before it (recommended first, never forced) — each application was a business waiting to be started.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Entrepreneurship and the IT-enabled business** — what entrepreneurship and an entrepreneur are; the IT-enabled business kinds; why little capital can be enough; why it matters for youth.
2. **RIDE — and starting where you stand (teacher guide — evidence)** — Samrawit Fikru's RIDE from SMS service to taxi-hailing platform; the guide's contrast of shop-rent business with internet business, and its two starter ideas.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the lesson named in this plan's level line, inside this same title. Recommended, never forced. A learner can land here directly; the lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define entrepreneurship and the entrepreneur.
2. Name the IT-enabled business kinds and the three youth-employment reasons.
3. Tell the RIDE story and work the guide's start-up contrast with its two starter ideas (teacher guide — evidence).

## If the learner is stuck
Thinks a business must start with a shop → the IT-enabled business runs on IT as its platform; an idea and a laptop can be enough, and RIDE began as an SMS service. Thinks the entrepreneur risks nothing → the entrepreneur carries most of the risk and reward; that carrying is the job.
