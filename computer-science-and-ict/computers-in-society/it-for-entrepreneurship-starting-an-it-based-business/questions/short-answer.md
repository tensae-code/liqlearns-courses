# IT for entrepreneurship — starting an IT-based business — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three reasons IT entrepreneurship matters for youth employment.
**Answer: More jobs need IT skills; IT is changing how young entrepreneurs trade; online learning for job seekers is exploding.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Contrast the traditional and internet business starts (teacher guide — evidence).
**Answer: Traditional: rent a shop, hire staff, heavy starting cost. Internet: flexible, cheap and worldwide from day one — no shop rent, no salesperson.**
