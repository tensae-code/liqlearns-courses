# IT for entrepreneurship — starting an IT-based business — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Online selling and app development are IT-enabled business kinds.
**Answer: True** — with online training, data entry and internet cafés.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** RIDE began as a large internet company with many offices.
**Answer: False** — it grew from an SMS service.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A traditional business start carries heavy cost — shop rent and staff (teacher guide — evidence).
**Answer: True** — the guide's contrast with the internet start.
