# IT for entrepreneurship — starting an IT-based business — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Entrepreneurship is —
a) working for a salary only  b) designing, launching and running a new business  c) buying shares only  d) a school subject
**Answer: b) designing, launching and running a new business** — the book's definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The entrepreneur is seen as —
a) an employee  b) an innovator  c) a customer  d) an inspector
**Answer: b) an innovator** — carrying most of the risk and reward.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** An IT-enabled business needs —
a) a large factory  b) little starting capital — an idea and a laptop can be enough  c) a rented mall shop  d) ten staff first
**Answer: b) little starting capital — an idea and a laptop can be enough** — IT is its platform.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** RIDE was founded by —
a) Samrawit Fikru  b) an unnamed company  c) a ministry  d) a bank
**Answer: a) Samrawit Fikru** — grown from an SMS service.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's two starter ideas are — (teacher guide — evidence)
a) a taxi fleet and a hotel  b) an online tutoring service and a product catalogue on Facebook  c) a farm and a shop  d) a café and a garage
**Answer: b) an online tutoring service and a product catalogue on Facebook** — both start without premises.
