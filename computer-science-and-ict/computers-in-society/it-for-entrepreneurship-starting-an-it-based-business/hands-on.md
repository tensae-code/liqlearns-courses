# Hands-on — IT for entrepreneurship — starting an IT-based business

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Catalogue on paper
Design a one-page product catalogue for a real or imagined product, as it would appear on a phone: photo space, price, how to order, how it is delivered. Show it to someone and take their first question as your first fix (teacher guide — evidence).

## Tutoring service plan
Plan an online tutoring service: subject, learners, how lessons travel (which application from this unit?), price, and the first three learners you would invite (teacher guide — evidence).
