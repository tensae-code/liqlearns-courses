# Stereotypes in the Media — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a stereotype?**
**Answer: A ready-made picture of a group — “all X are like this”**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a prejudice?**
**Answer: A judgement made before knowing — the stereotype turned into a verdict on a real person**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How are stereotypes unpicked?**
**Answer: By counter-examples and closer looks**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How are prejudices unpicked?**
**Answer: By meeting the actual person or fact before judging**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the livret insist on the difference?**
**Answer: Because the fight differs for each**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why do media repeat stereotypes?**
**Answer: Because they are shortcuts**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do gender stereotypes quietly teach?**
**Answer: Who may do what**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the scenario's craft?**
**Answer: Counting — tallying who does what**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does counting reveal?**
**Answer: That the pattern is a choice someone made, not just how things are**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Give a media stereotype example.**
**Answer: The advertisement's mother always cooking (also: the game's hero always a boy)**
