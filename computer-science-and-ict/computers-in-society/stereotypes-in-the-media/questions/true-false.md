# Stereotypes in the Media — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A ready-made picture of a group — “all X are like this”**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A ready-made picture of a group — “all X are like this”** — as the answer to: What is a prejudice?
**Answer: False — A judgement made before knowing — the stereotype turned into a verdict on a real person**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: By counter-examples and closer looks**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: By counter-examples and closer looks** — as the answer to: How are prejudices unpicked?
**Answer: False — By meeting the actual person or fact before judging**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Because the fight differs for each**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Because the fight differs for each** — as the answer to: Why do media repeat stereotypes?
**Answer: False — Because they are shortcuts**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Who may do what**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Who may do what** — as the answer to: What is the scenario's craft?
**Answer: False — Counting — tallying who does what**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: That the pattern is a choice someone made, not just how things are**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: That the pattern is a choice someone made, not just how things are** — as the answer to: Give a media stereotype example.
**Answer: False — The advertisement's mother always cooking (also: the game's hero always a boy)**
