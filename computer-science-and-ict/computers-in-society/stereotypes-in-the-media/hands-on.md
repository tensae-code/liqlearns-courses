# Hands-on — Stereotypes in the Media

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Count the screen** — Watch one advertisement break or one cartoon episode with a tally sheet: who cooks, who fixes, who leads, who is rescued? Present the counts.

## Task two
**Break the picture** — Take one stereotyped scene and rewrite or redraw it with the roles exchanged. What changes? What feels strange — and why?
