# Hands-on — Writing a Business Plan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 9 §9.2 Creating a Business Plan (§9.2.1 what a business plan is; §9.2.2 key parts — executive summary, business description, market analysis, products or services, marketing and sales strategy, financial plan; §9.2.2.1 plan-creation software; §9.2.2.2 collaborative tools; class activity: write and present a business plan) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach writing a business plan to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
