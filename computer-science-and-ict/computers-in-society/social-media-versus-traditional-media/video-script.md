# Video script — Social Media versus Traditional Media

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Social media and traditional media — newspapers, radio, television — differ in who speaks, how fast, and who answers: traditional media broadcasts from few to many, social media lets everyone publish and reply The learner compares the two on the book's points — the speaker, the audience, the speed, the cost, the answer-back — and judges real examples against them Because learners live in both media worlds at once, and the difference explains both the power and the dangers of the newer one.
