# Management Levels in an Organisation — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the three management levels." is: Operational (lower), tactical (middle) and strategic (top)**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the operational level run?" is: A department or a season — this quarter's production, this term's budget**
**Answer: False** — The day's work — today's orders made, today's workers present

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the tactical level run?" is: A department or a season — this quarter's production, this term's budget**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the strategic level decide?" is: Time and scope — the higher the level, the longer the time and wider the ground of its decisions**
**Answer: False** — The organisation's direction — new products, new buildings, new markets

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What grows as you climb the ladder?" is: Time and scope — the higher the level, the longer the time and wider the ground of its decisions**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What information does the operational level eat?" is: Summaries over weeks and months — totals, comparisons, trends**
**Answer: False** — Detail, now — every order, every absence

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the tactical level eat?" is: Summaries over weeks and months — totals, comparisons, trends**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the strategic level eat?" is: Because the level decides the information diet a system must serve**
**Answer: False** — The widest summaries and the outside world — years of results, competitors, the economy

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why does the book teach levels before systems?" is: Because the level decides the information diet a system must serve**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How can a system fail both ends?" is: Operational (lower), tactical (middle) and strategic (top)**
**Answer: False** — By feeding a director daily detail, or a supervisor yearly summaries
