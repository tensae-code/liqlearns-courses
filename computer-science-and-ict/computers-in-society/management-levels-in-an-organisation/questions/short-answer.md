# Management Levels in an Organisation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three management levels.**
**Answer: Operational (lower), tactical (middle) and strategic (top)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the operational level run?**
**Answer: The day's work — today's orders made, today's workers present**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the tactical level run?**
**Answer: A department or a season — this quarter's production, this term's budget**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the strategic level decide?**
**Answer: The organisation's direction — new products, new buildings, new markets**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What grows as you climb the ladder?**
**Answer: Time and scope — the higher the level, the longer the time and wider the ground of its decisions**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What information does the operational level eat?**
**Answer: Detail, now — every order, every absence**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the tactical level eat?**
**Answer: Summaries over weeks and months — totals, comparisons, trends**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the strategic level eat?**
**Answer: The widest summaries and the outside world — years of results, competitors, the economy**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book teach levels before systems?**
**Answer: Because the level decides the information diet a system must serve**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How can a system fail both ends?**
**Answer: By feeding a director daily detail, or a supervisor yearly summaries**
