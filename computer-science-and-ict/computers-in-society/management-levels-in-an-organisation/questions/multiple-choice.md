# Management Levels in an Organisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three management levels.**
a) Operational (lower), tactical (middle) and strategic (top)  b) The day's work — today's orders made, today's workers present  c) A department or a season — this quarter's production, this term's budget  d) The organisation's direction — new products, new buildings, new markets
**Answer: a) Operational (lower), tactical (middle) and strategic (top)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the operational level run?**
a) A department or a season — this quarter's production, this term's budget  b) The day's work — today's orders made, today's workers present  c) The organisation's direction — new products, new buildings, new markets  d) Time and scope — the higher the level, the longer the time and wider the ground of its decisions
**Answer: b) The day's work — today's orders made, today's workers present**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the tactical level run?**
a) The organisation's direction — new products, new buildings, new markets  b) Time and scope — the higher the level, the longer the time and wider the ground of its decisions  c) A department or a season — this quarter's production, this term's budget  d) Detail, now — every order, every absence
**Answer: c) A department or a season — this quarter's production, this term's budget**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the strategic level decide?**
a) Time and scope — the higher the level, the longer the time and wider the ground of its decisions  b) Detail, now — every order, every absence  c) Summaries over weeks and months — totals, comparisons, trends  d) The organisation's direction — new products, new buildings, new markets
**Answer: d) The organisation's direction — new products, new buildings, new markets**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What grows as you climb the ladder?**
a) Time and scope — the higher the level, the longer the time and wider the ground of its decisions  b) Detail, now — every order, every absence  c) Summaries over weeks and months — totals, comparisons, trends  d) The widest summaries and the outside world — years of results, competitors, the economy
**Answer: a) Time and scope — the higher the level, the longer the time and wider the ground of its decisions**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What information does the operational level eat?**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the tactical level eat?**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the strategic level eat?**
