# E-commerce — buying and selling online — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put the flow in order: delivery, order, payment processing, merchant receives.
**Answer: Buyer orders; merchant receives the order; payment is processed through the bank; goods are delivered.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four advantages of e-commerce.
**Answer: Cutting out intermediaries; convenience; stronger buyer bargaining power; global reach.**
