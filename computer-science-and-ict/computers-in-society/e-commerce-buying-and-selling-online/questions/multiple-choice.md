# E-commerce — buying and selling online — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In e-commerce, after the merchant receives the order —
a) the goods fly out at once  b) payment is processed through the bank  c) the buyer travels to the shop  d) the order is printed
**Answer: b) payment is processed through the bank** — payment comes before delivery.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The four actors in e-commerce are —
a) buyers, sellers' cousins, drivers, bankers  b) merchants, buyers, banks and transporters  c) teachers, pupils, banks, farmers  d) buyers, printers, banks, pilots
**Answer: b) merchants, buyers, banks and transporters** — the book's four.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The buyer orders —
a) by letter  b) on a website by phone or computer  c) in person only  d) by shouting
**Answer: b) on a website by phone or computer** — the flow's first step.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopian e-commerce grows partly because sellers —
a) must rent big shops  b) need no physical shop  c) pay no attention to prices  d) sell only locally
**Answer: b) need no physical shop** — with buyers browsing and comparing prices.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Global reach comes paired with —
a) global competition  b) free delivery  c) no intermediaries  d) local monopoly
**Answer: a) global competition** — the book holds the two together.
