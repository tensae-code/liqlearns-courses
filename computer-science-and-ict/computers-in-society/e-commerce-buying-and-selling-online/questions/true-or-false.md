# E-commerce — buying and selling online — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Transporters are actors in e-commerce.
**Answer: True** — they carry the goods to the door.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** E-commerce cuts out intermediaries.
**Answer: True** — a named advantage.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book says Ethiopia's supporting infrastructure for e-commerce is fully mature.
**Answer: False** — it is still young, the book notes.
