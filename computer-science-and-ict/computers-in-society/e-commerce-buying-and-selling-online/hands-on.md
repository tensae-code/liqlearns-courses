# Hands-on — E-commerce — buying and selling online

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Order journey map
Draw the four actors as four stations and one order's journey as arrows between them. Add, at each arrow, what passes: the order, the payment, the goods.

## Compare and bargain
Choose one item. Find its price in three places — two online, one in a shop you know. Write the three prices and what the comparison gives you as a buyer.
