## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Up and down — the vertical flow** — **Downward** flow carries the decisions: plans, targets and instructions travel from the top level to the middle, and from the middle to the floor — each level turning the direction it receives into the orders the level below can act on. **Upward** flow carries the results: the floor's detail is gathered, summarised by the middle, and summarised again for the top — Viti's chairs made today becoming a line in a quarterly report becoming a fact in a director's decision. Fiji's symmetry is exact: orders go down whole and are divided; results come up divided and are joined.
2. **Across — the horizontal flow, and the informal channels** — Between functions at the same level runs the **horizontal** flow: production tells marketing what can be promised; accounting tells both what the numbers allow. Without it, each function would decide from its own picture alone. Beside the formal flows runs the **informal** one — talk, messages, what people hear — faster than every pipe and not always true, which Fiji does not dismiss: a wise manager knows the informal flow exists, listens to it, and never mistakes it for the record.
