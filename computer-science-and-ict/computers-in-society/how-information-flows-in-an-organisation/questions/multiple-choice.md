# How Information Flows in an Organisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does downward flow carry?**
a) The decisions — plans, targets and instructions  b) Turns it into orders the level below can act on  c) The results — detail gathered and summarised, level by level  d) Chairs made today → a line in a quarterly report → a fact in a director's decision

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does each level do to the direction it receives?**
