# How Information Flows in an Organisation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does downward flow carry?**
**Answer: The decisions — plans, targets and instructions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does each level do to the direction it receives?**
**Answer: Turns it into orders the level below can act on**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does upward flow carry?**
**Answer: The results — detail gathered and summarised, level by level**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Trace one fact upward at Viti.**
**Answer: Chairs made today → a line in a quarterly report → a fact in a director's decision**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is Fiji's symmetry of the vertical flow?**
**Answer: Orders go down whole and are divided; results come up divided and are joined**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does horizontal flow connect?**
**Answer: Functions at the same level — production, marketing, accounting telling each other what is true**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why is horizontal flow needed?**
**Answer: Without it each function decides from its own picture alone**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the informal flow?**
**Answer: Talk and messages — faster than every pipe, and not always true**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How should a wise manager treat the informal flow?**
**Answer: Listen to it, but never mistake it for the record**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is this lesson's governing image?**
**Answer: Information moving like water in a building — up, down and across, by pipes someone built**
