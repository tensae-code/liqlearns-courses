# Video script — How Information Flows in an Organisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Downward** flow carries the decisions: plans, targets and instructions travel from the top level to the middle, and from the middle to the floor — each level turning the direction it receives into the orders the level below can act on. **Upward** flow carries the results: the floor's detail is gathered, summarised by the middle, and summarised again for the top — Viti's chairs made today becoming a line in a quarterly report becoming a fact in a director's decision. Fiji's symmetry is exact: orders go down whole and are divided; results come up divided and are joined.
Between functions at the same level runs the **horizontal** flow: production tells marketing what can be promised; accounting tells both what the numbers allow. Without it, each function would decide from its own picture alone. Beside the formal flows runs the **informal** one — talk, messages, what people hear — faster than every pipe and not always true, which Fiji does not dismiss: a wise manager knows the informal flow exists, listens to it, and never mistakes it for the record.
