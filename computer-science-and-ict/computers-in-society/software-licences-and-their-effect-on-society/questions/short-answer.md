# Software Licences and Their Effect on Society — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a software licence?**
**Answer: The maker's permission, written in advance — who may use, copy, change and pass on the software, and at what price**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do proprietary licences keep?**
**Answer: The code closed, and copying forbidden**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do free/open-source licences grant?**
**Answer: Use, study, change and sharing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is copyleft's condition?**
**Answer: Sharing must keep the same freedoms — derivatives stay free**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How does Armenia's Grade 11 book frame licences?**
**Answer: By their influence on society (Lesson 3, Theme 3) — not only what one user may do**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How do licences affect learning?**
**Answer: Open code can be studied by anyone; closed code teaches use without understanding**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is vendor dependence?**
**Answer: Being locked into one maker's prices and formats by strict terms and closed file shapes**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How can free licences help poor schools?**
**Answer: They put real tools in them at no per-seat price**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What project does Armenia's Grade 11 guide set on this theme?**
**Answer: The impact of software licences on technological development**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a licence choice a society-level choice?**
**Answer: Its effects — on learning, building, dependence and price — land on everyone, one program at a time**
