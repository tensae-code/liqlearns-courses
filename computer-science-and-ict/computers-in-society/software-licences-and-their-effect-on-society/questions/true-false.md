# Software Licences and Their Effect on Society — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The maker's permission, written in advance — who may use, copy, change and pass on the software, and at what price**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The maker's permission, written in advance — who may use, copy, change and pass on the software, and at what price** — as the answer to: What do proprietary licences keep?
**Answer: False — The code closed, and copying forbidden**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Use, study, change and sharing**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Use, study, change and sharing** — as the answer to: What is copyleft's condition?
**Answer: False — Sharing must keep the same freedoms — derivatives stay free**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: By their influence on society (Lesson 3, Theme 3) — not only what one user may do**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: By their influence on society (Lesson 3, Theme 3) — not only what one user may do** — as the answer to: How do licences affect learning?
**Answer: False — Open code can be studied by anyone; closed code teaches use without understanding**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Being locked into one maker's prices and formats by strict terms and closed file shapes**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Being locked into one maker's prices and formats by strict terms and closed file shapes** — as the answer to: How can free licences help poor schools?
**Answer: False — They put real tools in them at no per-seat price**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The impact of software licences on technological development**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The impact of software licences on technological development** — as the answer to: Why is a licence choice a society-level choice?
**Answer: False — Its effects — on learning, building, dependence and price — land on everyone, one program at a time**
