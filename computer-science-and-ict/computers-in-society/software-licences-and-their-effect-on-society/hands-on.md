# Hands-on — Software Licences and Their Effect on Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Licence census** — List the software on one school computer. For each, find its licence kind (proprietary, free/open, trial). Total what the proprietary set would cost per seat.

## Task two
**Project brief** — Draft the plan for Armenia's project question: how would you measure a licence's impact on technological development in your town? Name your evidence and your comparison.
