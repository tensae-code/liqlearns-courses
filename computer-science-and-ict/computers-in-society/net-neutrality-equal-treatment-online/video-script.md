# Video script — Net Neutrality — Equal Treatment Online

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

All your internet traffic passes through one company: your provider.

Net neutrality is a simple rule for that company. Carry everything the same way.

Do not block a lawful site. Do not slow one service down on purpose. Do not sell a fast lane to the rich and leave the rest in the slow lane.

Why? So that a small site made by a student reaches you on the same terms as a giant's site. You choose what succeeds — not the provider.

Providers answer: we built the network, and the heaviest services should help pay. That is the argument. Now you know both sides.
