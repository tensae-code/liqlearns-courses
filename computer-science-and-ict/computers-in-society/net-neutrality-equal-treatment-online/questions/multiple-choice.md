# Net Neutrality — Equal Treatment Online — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Net neutrality requires an internet provider to —**
a) Give free internet to schools  b) Treat all traffic equally  c) Own no websites  d) Block harmful sites
**Answer: b) Treat all traffic equally — Whatever the sender or the content, the provider carries it on the same terms.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these does net neutrality forbid?**
a) Charging users a monthly fee  b) Selling paid priority — a fast lane for sites that pay  c) Repairing broken cables  d) Offering faster home connections for a higher price
**Answer: b) Selling paid priority — a fast lane for sites that pay — Priority sold to sites is exactly what the rule bans; the speed of your own connection package is a different matter.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The strongest argument for net neutrality is —**
a) Providers earn too much  b) Anyone can publish and reach the public on the same terms as a giant  c) Video uses too much data  d) Governments should run the network
**Answer: b) Anyone can publish and reach the public on the same terms as a giant — Equal terms keep the choice with users, not providers.**
