# Net Neutrality — Equal Treatment Online — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) State the providers' argument against net neutrality, fairly, in one or two sentences.**
**Answer: Providers say they built and maintain the network, that a few heavy services flood it with traffic, and that those services should help pay for it — for example through priority charges — instead of all costs falling on ordinary subscribers.**
