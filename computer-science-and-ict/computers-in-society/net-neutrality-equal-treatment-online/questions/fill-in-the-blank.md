# Net Neutrality — Equal Treatment Online — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Deliberately slowing down some services while others run fast is called ____.**
**Answer: throttling — Throttling is one of the three practices net neutrality forbids.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Under neutrality, what decides what succeeds online is the ____, by visiting what they choose.**
**Answer: users — The choice stays with the people at the end of the line.**
