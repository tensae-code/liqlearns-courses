# Net Neutrality — Equal Treatment Online — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Under net neutrality, a provider may slow down a rival's video service so its own video service looks better.**
**Answer: False — Deliberately slowing a service is throttling, one of the three forbidden practices.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Net neutrality means nobody pays anything for internet access.**
**Answer: False — Users still pay for their connection; the rule governs how traffic is treated, not whether access is charged for.**
