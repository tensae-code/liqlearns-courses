# Hands-on — Net Neutrality — Equal Treatment Online

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two speeds thought experiment
Write the school-site story from Do it first as a short news item of five lines, first as it would read under net neutrality, then as it would read without it. Under each version, list who gains and who loses.

## Find your provider's promises
If you have internet access, find your own provider's public description of its service. Does it say anything about equal treatment, traffic management or priority? Write down one sentence you find and say which side of the lesson's argument it belongs to. If you have no access, do this from any provider advertisement you have seen.
