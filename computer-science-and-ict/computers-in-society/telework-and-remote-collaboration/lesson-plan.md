## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Telework — the work travels, not the worker** — **Telework (τηλεργασία)** is work done **away from the employer's place**, the work travelling over networks instead of the worker travelling over roads: from home, from another town, from another country. Its shape is set by its tools — the connection, the shared systems of the office reached remotely, the communication channels — and its gains are the chapter's first list: journeys unspent, places unbound (work for a distant employer without moving house), hours shaped around life instead of around the road.
2. **Remote collaboration — together, in two kinds of time** — Working apart is only half; the chapter's second half is **collaborating** apart, in its two kinds of time. **Synchronous** collaboration happens **together in the moment** — the video meeting, the shared screen, the live-edited document. **Asynchronous** collaboration happens **in turns** — messages, comments and shared files each answered when its reader arrives, the work kept in the shared place between turns. Telework stands or falls on choosing rightly between them: what needs a meeting's speed, and what a turn's thoughtfulness — and on the discipline that replaces the corridor: everything agreed is **written where all can find it**.
