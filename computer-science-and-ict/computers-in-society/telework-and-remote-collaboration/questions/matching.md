# Telework and Remote Collaboration — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is telework?** → **Work done away from the employer's place — the work travels over networks, not the worker over roads**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What sets its shape?** → **Its tools — connection, remotely reached office systems, communication channels**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two gains.** → **Journeys unspent; work for a distant employer without moving house (or hours shaped around life)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) From where may it be done?** → **Home, another town, another country**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches it?** → **Greece Grade 10 Efarmoges Pliroforikis, Chapter 14**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the two kinds of collaboration time.** → **Synchronous (together in the moment) and asynchronous (in turns)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give a synchronous example.** → **Video meeting (or shared screen, or live-edited document)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Give an asynchronous example.** → **Messages, comments, shared files answered in turns**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What replaces the corridor?** → **Everything agreed is written where all can find it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Choosing rightly between the kinds decides what?** → **Whether telework stands or falls**
