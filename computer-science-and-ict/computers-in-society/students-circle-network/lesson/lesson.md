# Students Circle Network

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Students circle network — an OpenCourseWare social network

Students Circle Network is a social network built for education — an OpenCourseWare network where students and teachers hold profiles, exchange messages, join study groups and follow courses The learner meets the network and its features: profiles, private messages, chat, aggregated courses, study groups and social games, and says what each feature is for Because it shows social networking turned to study: the same tools as the big platforms, pointed at courses and learning.
