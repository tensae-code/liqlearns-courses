# Hands-on — Students Circle Network

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Do the book's steps** — Work through Students circle network — an OpenCourseWare social network exactly as South Sudan's book orders it, on a real computer if one is available; otherwise write the steps in order and check them against the book's order.

## Task two
**Show it to someone** — Take Features — profiles, private messages, chat, aggregated courses, study groups and social games and show a partner what it does: do it once slowly, naming each step, then let the partner do it while you only watch and correct.
