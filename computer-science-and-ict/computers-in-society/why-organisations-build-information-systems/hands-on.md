# Hands-on — Why Organisations Build Information Systems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Diagnose a need', 'Choose one organisation you know. Write its internal pain in three named symptoms, and the external pressure on it, and conclude: is a new system necessary, or merely fashionable?')

## Task two
('Both at once', 'Take the university-entrance registration. Argue its need twice — once from inside the office, once from outside society — and mark the stronger argument.')
