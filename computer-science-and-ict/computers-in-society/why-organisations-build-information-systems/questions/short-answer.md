# Why Organisations Build Information Systems — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) From where is a system's need first felt?**
**Answer: Inside the organisation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the internal symptoms.**
**Answer: Records outgrowing the ledger, queues outgrowing clerks, multiplying errors, late information (any three)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) In what does internal need speak?**
**Answer: Pain — the present way costs more than its replacement**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the school's internal strain in the book.**
**Answer: Pupil records and timetables straining paper**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the two external driver families.**
**Answer: Social drivers and technological drivers**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do social drivers consist of?**
**Answer: Citizens and customers expecting electronic service as the norm**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do technological drivers consist of?**
**Answer: New capabilities making the previously impossible system merely overdue**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the book's two state-scale examples.**
**Answer: Citizen registration and university-entrance-exam registration**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is Mongolia's rule for reading any system?**
**Answer: Ask whose pain built it — inside, outside, or both at once**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which lesson follows this one in the book?**
**Answer: The system development life cycle — how the needed system is built**
