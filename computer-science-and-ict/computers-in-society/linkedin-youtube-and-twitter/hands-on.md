# Hands-on — LinkedIn, YouTube and Twitter

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Platform match
Write twelve activity cards — six from the Do It Yourself sort and six of your own. Deal them out and sort them under three platform headings, saying each platform's job aloud as you place its cards.

## Count to 140
Take a long message and shrink it, draft by draft, until it fits 140 characters without losing its news. Count every draft. That shrinking is micro-blogging.
