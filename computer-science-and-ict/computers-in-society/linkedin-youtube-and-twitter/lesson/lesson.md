# LinkedIn, YouTube and Twitter

## Do it first

Three people speak. One is looking for work and professional contacts. One has filmed something inspiring and wants the world to watch it. One wants to post a very short message and follow the latest news. Three platforms, one for each. Match them by guesswork — then check the matches.

## Three platforms, three jobs

The three panels show the three jobs side by side — work, video, short messages.

[IMAGE 11736 — LinkedIn, YouTube and Twitter in three panels]

## LinkedIn — work and professional networking

**LinkedIn** is an **online business and employment service**, used **mainly for professional networking** — the platform of jobs and work connections.

## YouTube — videos to watch and share

**YouTube** is a **video-sharing website** on which users **upload, view and share videos** that are **informative and inspirational to others**.

## Twitter — micro-blogging in 140 characters

**Twitter** is a **micro-blogging service** — **broadcasting short messages to other subscribers**. It lets people **post and exchange text messages limited to 140 characters** through computers or mobile devices — mainly to **post, and to get and follow the latest information that interests them**.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the three platforms and each one's job, and Twitter's character limit.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the three platforms and their jobs from memory.
2. Write one message of at most 140 characters that you could post, and count its characters.
3. Sort six activities — find a job contact, watch a lesson video, post breaking news, upload your film, follow a professional, share an inspirational video — to their platforms.
