# Lesson plan — LinkedIn, YouTube and Twitter

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** LinkedIn, YouTube and Twitter
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 14 — Computers in Society of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 4 — Internet. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** three named platforms, three different jobs: LinkedIn for professional networking and employment, YouTube for uploading, viewing and sharing videos, and Twitter for micro-blogging — short messages of up to 140 characters to post and follow the latest information.
**This lesson's own level:** it completes the platform set of this band: the learner has met social networks in general, Facebook and Telegram in depth; these three are taught by their one distinguishing job each.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Social networks — connecting in virtual communities. Recommended, never forced. A learner can land here directly; the lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say LinkedIn's job: business and employment, mainly professional networking.
2. Say YouTube's job: uploading, viewing and sharing informative and inspirational videos.
3. Say Twitter's job: micro-blogging, messages limited to 140 characters, posting and following the latest information.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes YouTube and Twitter → ask what travels: whole videos (YouTube) or short text messages of 140 characters (Twitter).
- Thinks LinkedIn is for games and friends → LinkedIn's field is business and employment — professional networking.
