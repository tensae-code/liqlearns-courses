# LinkedIn, YouTube and Twitter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** LinkedIn is mainly used for —
a) professional networking  b) watching videos  c) short messages  d) sending files by FTP
**Answer: a) professional networking**

**2.** LinkedIn is an online service for —
a) games  b) business and employment  c) photo printing  d) weather
**Answer: b) business and employment**

**3.** On YouTube, users —
a) upload, view and share videos  b) only write text  c) make phone calls  d) edit spreadsheets
**Answer: a) upload, view and share videos**

**4.** The videos on YouTube are described as —
a) secret and private  b) informative and inspirational to others  c) silent only  d) advertisements only
**Answer: b) informative and inspirational to others**

**5.** Twitter is a —
a) video service  b) micro-blogging service  c) file protocol  d) browser
**Answer: b) micro-blogging service**

**6.** Twitter messages are limited to —
a) 10 characters  b) 140 characters  c) 1,000 characters  d) no limit
**Answer: b) 140 characters**
