# Video script — Electronic Services — E-Government, E-Learning and E-Banking

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An **electronic service** is a service delivered through ICT instead of a counter. Mongolia's emblem is the **ТҮЦ service kiosk** — the state's self-service machine standing in public places, giving citizens certificates and services without the office queue; behind it stands the Grade 12 book's **ХУР state information-exchange system**, joining the state's own databases so one office can serve what another holds. **E-government** is the whole pattern: the state met through systems — and e-services stand or fall on the citizen's trust that the system is the state, not a stranger wearing its name.
**E-learning** delivers teaching through networks, and its pocket form, **m-learning**, through the phone. **E-commerce** buys and sells electronically, in kinds the book names by their parties: **B2C** (business to citizen — the online bookshop, bookstore.mn, is the book's own example), **B2B** (business to business), **G2B** (government to business) and **C2C** (citizen to citizen). **Internet banking** moves money through the bank's electronic doors. Grade 12 widens the same family to society itself: e-education, e-agriculture with IoT chips on livestock, e-health telemedicine, and GIS — one family, growing with the pupil, from the kiosk of Grade 9 to the systems of Grade 12.
