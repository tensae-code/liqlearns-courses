# Electronic Services — E-Government, E-Learning and E-Banking — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an electronic service?**
**Answer: A service delivered through ICT instead of a counter**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the ТҮЦ?**
**Answer: Mongolia's state self-service kiosk, giving certificates and services in public places**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the ХУР system?**
**Answer: The state information-exchange system joining the state's databases**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) On what do e-services stand or fall?**
**Answer: The citizen's trust that the system is truly the state**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is m-learning?**
**Answer: E-learning delivered through the phone**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four e-commerce kinds.**
**Answer: B2C, B2B, G2B and C2C**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's B2C example?**
**Answer: The online bookshop — bookstore.mn**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does internet banking do?**
**Answer: Moves money through the bank's electronic doors**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name Grade 12's widenings of the family.**
**Answer: E-education, e-agriculture (IoT chips on livestock), e-health telemedicine and GIS (any three)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is this lesson Mongolia's own way?**
**Answer: It teaches the e-services as one family through Mongolia's own systems, in Grade 9 and Grade 12 together**
