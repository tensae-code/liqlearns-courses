# Video script — Advantages and Limitations of Information Systems

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Information systems are fast. They do not get tired.
They store mountains of data in a tiny space.
They share the same facts with everyone at once.
But they cost money. And when they break, work can stop.
They hold private data — and private data can leak.
Strong and weak. A wise user knows both sides.
