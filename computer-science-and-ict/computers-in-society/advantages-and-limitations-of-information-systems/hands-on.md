# Hands-on — Advantages and Limitations of Information Systems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-column audit
Pick a system you use (mobile money, a school register, a ticketing app). Draw two columns and write at least three true entries in each: advantages it gives you, limitations it shows you.

## Breakdown day
Write a short paragraph: the system you chose stops for one whole day. What work stops with it? What could still be done by hand? That paragraph is the limitation of dependence, in your own words.
