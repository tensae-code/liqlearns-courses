# Advantages and Limitations of Information Systems — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A system can ____ enormous amounts of data in a tiny space and find any item instantly.**
**Answer: store** — Storage is one of the named advantages.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Systems create ____: when they fail, work that used to continue by hand may stop.**
**Answer: dependence** — Power, network or system failure stops the work.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) An ____ user can enter wrong data, and the system will spread the error faster.**
**Answer: untrained** — Systems demand trained people.
