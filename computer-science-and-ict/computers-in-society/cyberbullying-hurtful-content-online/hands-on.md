# Hands-on — Cyberbullying — hurtful content and exposed private information

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Spot the form
Write six one-line cases on cards — mix both forms with two harmless cases. A partner sorts them and must name the form or say why a case is not cyberbullying.

## The bystander's move
Practise the three moves of a witness: do not forward, save the evidence, stand with the person. Write them on a card and keep it in your phone case or notebook.
