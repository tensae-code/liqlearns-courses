# Cyberbullying — hurtful content and exposed private information — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the two forms of cyberbullying.
**Answer:** Spreading hurtful or malicious content about someone online; and revealing their sensitive or private information to embarrass or humiliate them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the places the book says cyberbullying happens.
**Answer:** Social media, forums and games, and by text and messaging apps.
