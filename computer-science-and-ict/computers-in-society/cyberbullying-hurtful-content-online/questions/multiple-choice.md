# Cyberbullying — hurtful content and exposed private information — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cyberbullying includes —
a) only jokes between friends  b) sending, posting or spreading hurtful or malicious content about someone online  c) losing a game  d) slow replies
**Answer: b) sending, posting or spreading hurtful or malicious content about someone online** — the first form of the definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The second form of cyberbullying is —
a) winning too often  b) revealing someone's private information to embarrass or humiliate them  c) using caps lock  d) posting photos of food
**Answer: b) revealing someone's private information to embarrass or humiliate them** — exposure as the weapon.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Content meant to wound is described as —
a) unfavourable, hurtful or malicious  b) long and detailed  c) always false  d) always anonymous
**Answer: a) unfavourable, hurtful or malicious** — the book's three adjectives.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Cyberbullying happens —
a) only at school  b) on social media, forums and games, and by text and messaging apps  c) only on television  d) only by email
**Answer: b) on social media, forums and games, and by text and messaging apps** — the named places.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The book names as a serious form to be addressed —
a) online gender-based violence, harassment and sexual abuse  b) late homework  c) loud music  d) spoilers
**Answer: a) online gender-based violence, harassment and sexual abuse** — named with full weight.
