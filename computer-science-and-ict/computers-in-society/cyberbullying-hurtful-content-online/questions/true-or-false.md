# Cyberbullying — hurtful content and exposed private information — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Revealing private information to shame someone is cyberbullying even if no cruel words are written.
**Answer: True** — the second form stands alone.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cyberbullying can only happen on social media.
**Answer: False** — forums, games, texts and messaging apps are named too.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Online gender-based violence is treated in the book as a serious form of cyberbullying.
**Answer: True** — named as a form to be addressed.
