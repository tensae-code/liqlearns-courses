# Cyberbullying — hurtful content and exposed private information

## Do it first

A cruel message costs the sender ten seconds. It can cost the person who receives it a night's sleep, a school term, a sense of safety. This lesson defines the act exactly — both of its forms — and names where it happens.

## Sub lesson 1 — What cyberbullying is — both forms

**Cyberbullying is sending, posting or spreading unfavourable, hurtful or malicious content about someone online** — the first form: content aimed to wound.

The second form cuts differently: **revealing someone's sensitive or private information to embarrass or humiliate them**. Nothing cruel needs to be written; exposing what was private does the harm.

## Sub lesson 2 — Where it happens — and its serious form

It happens **on social media, forums and games, and by text and messaging apps** — everywhere people gather online, which is why no single platform rule ends it.

And the book names **online gender-based violence, harassment and sexual abuse** as **a serious form of it to be addressed** — not a side note, but part of the definition's weight.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the definition in your own words, both forms, then name the places it happens and the serious form.
