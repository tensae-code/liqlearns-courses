# Hands-on — Evaluating Interactive Products

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Evaluate a product', 'Evaluate a school-used app or website on the three measures: set two real tasks, attempt them as a new user would, and record effectiveness, efficiency and satisfaction evidence separately.')

## Task two
('Sort the findings', 'Six findings are given (a task abandoned; a task done in twice the steps expected; users smiling but entering wrong dates; and three more your teacher adds). Sort each under its measure and say what repair each calls for.')
