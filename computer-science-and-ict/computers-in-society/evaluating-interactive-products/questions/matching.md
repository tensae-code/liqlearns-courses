Match each question on the left with its answer on the right. Write the letter beside each number.

9. (Sub lesson 2) A pleasant but error-strewn product fails which?
10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the evaluation's product?

a) With users, or as users — walking the tasks in the users' shoes
b) Effectiveness, efficiency, satisfaction
c) Judging how well its meeting with users works — whether intended users can do their tasks
d) Can users complete the task, correctly?
e) What happens at the meeting — tasks finished or abandoned, errors, time, reported experience
f) How using it feels — confidence or frustration, willingness to return
g) Effectiveness
h) What completing it costs — time, steps, effort
i) A judgement with evidence, each finding named by its measure
j) Efficiency — it has effectiveness without efficiency
