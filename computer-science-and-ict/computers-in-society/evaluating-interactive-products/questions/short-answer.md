# Evaluating Interactive Products — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is evaluation of an interactive product?**
**Answer: Judging how well its meeting with users works — whether intended users can do their tasks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is evaluation done?**
**Answer: With users, or as users — walking the tasks in the users' shoes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is its evidence?**
**Answer: What happens at the meeting — tasks finished or abandoned, errors, time, reported experience**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three measures.**
**Answer: Effectiveness, efficiency, satisfaction**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does effectiveness ask?**
**Answer: Can users complete the task, correctly?**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does efficiency ask?**
**Answer: What completing it costs — time, steps, effort**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does satisfaction ask?**
**Answer: How using it feels — confidence or frustration, willingness to return**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) A product correct but exhausting fails which measure?**
**Answer: Efficiency — it has effectiveness without efficiency**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) A pleasant but error-strewn product fails which?**
**Answer: Effectiveness**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the evaluation's product?**
**Answer: A judgement with evidence, each finding named by its measure**
