# Evaluating Interactive Products — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is evaluation of an interactive product?" is: Judging how well its meeting with users works — whether intended users can do their tasks**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is evaluation done?" is: What happens at the meeting — tasks finished or abandoned, errors, time, reported experience**
**Answer: False** — With users, or as users — walking the tasks in the users' shoes

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is its evidence?" is: What happens at the meeting — tasks finished or abandoned, errors, time, reported experience**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the three measures." is: Can users complete the task, correctly?**
**Answer: False** — Effectiveness, efficiency, satisfaction

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does effectiveness ask?" is: Can users complete the task, correctly?**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does efficiency ask?" is: How using it feels — confidence or frustration, willingness to return**
**Answer: False** — What completing it costs — time, steps, effort

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does satisfaction ask?" is: How using it feels — confidence or frustration, willingness to return**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "A product correct but exhausting fails which measure?" is: Effectiveness**
**Answer: False** — Efficiency — it has effectiveness without efficiency

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "A pleasant but error-strewn product fails which?" is: Effectiveness**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the evaluation's product?" is: Judging how well its meeting with users works — whether intended users can do their tasks**
**Answer: False** — A judgement with evidence, each finding named by its measure
