# Evaluating Interactive Products — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Evaluation — judging the meeting
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Judging how well its meeting with users works — whether intended users can do their tasks; With users, or as users — walking the tasks in the users' shoes; What happens at the meeting — tasks finished or abandoned, errors, time, reported experience; Effectiveness, efficiency, satisfaction; Can users complete the task, correctly?.

## Sub lesson 2 — The three measures
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: What completing it costs — time, steps, effort; How using it feels — confidence or frustration, willingness to return; Efficiency — it has effectiveness without efficiency; Effectiveness; A judgement with evidence, each finding named by its measure.
