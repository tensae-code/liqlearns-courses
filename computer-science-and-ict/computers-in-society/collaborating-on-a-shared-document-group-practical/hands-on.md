# Hands-on — Collaborating on a shared document — group practical

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The practical itself
Run the lesson's practical end to end as your hands-on: group, summary, document, link, meeting, live edit. Keep the document — it is the artefact that proves the lesson happened.

## The second pass
A week later, reconvene the same group on the same document. Fix what your class report criticised. Compare versions — the version history is your group's collaboration, made visible.
