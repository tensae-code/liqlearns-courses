# Collaborating on a shared document — group practical — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the practical's steps in order up to the meeting.
**Answer:** Form a group of five and name it; write the half-page summary; create it as an online document named with the group name; send the link to all members; choose a screen-sharing meeting tool.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What three things should the closing discussion listen for?
**Answer:** A change impossible on paper; a disagreement the screen-share settled; a moment the technology got in the way.
