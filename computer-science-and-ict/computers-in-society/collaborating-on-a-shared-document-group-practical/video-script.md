# Video script — Collaborating on a shared document — group practical

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. (FULL script — the placement names a full video need for this lesson.)

---

Your group has a job. Five people. One document. Let us do it in order.

First — form your group of five. Give it a name. A real name; the document will carry it.

Second — together, write a half-page summary: the benefits of digital collaboration tools for online learning and group work. Short. Half a page. Every member contributes.

Third — create it in cloud word-processing software, as an online document, named with your group's name.

Fourth — send the link to all members, by email or social media. Everyone opens it. Everyone can edit.

Fifth — choose your meeting tool: Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, or another with screen sharing. One member generates the meeting link. Another joins first. Then everyone.

Now the heart of it: debate your summary on the call. Review it line by line. And change the document by screen sharing — from wherever each of you is — while the debate is live.

When the document settles, discuss: how did these tools improve learning? How did they improve teamwork? What got in the way?

Last — share the experience with the class. What you learned, and the challenges. Both. That report is part of the work.

Five people. One document. Edited together, live. Begin.
