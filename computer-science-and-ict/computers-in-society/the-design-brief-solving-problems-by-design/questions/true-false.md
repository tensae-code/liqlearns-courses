# The Design Brief — Solving Problems by Design — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a design brief?" is: A written statement of a problem to be solved by designing something**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Who is a design brief written for?" is: Time, materials and cost limits**
**Answer: False** — The person or group who needs the problem solved

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What must a design brief name about the finished thing?" is: What the finished thing must do**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What limits belong in a design brief?" is: Research the problem and the needs**
**Answer: False** — Time, materials and cost limits

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Is a design brief the design itself?" is: No — it states the problem; the design answers it**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the first stage after reading the brief?" is: After a first version is made**
**Answer: False** — Research the problem and the needs

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What comes after sketching ideas?" is: Choose the best idea against the brief**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is a design tested?" is: A real brief carried through to a finished product**
**Answer: False** — After a first version is made

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does testing a design lead to?" is: Improvements to the design**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does a case study of a design brief show?" is: The person or group who needs the problem solved**
**Answer: False** — A real brief carried through to a finished product
