# The Design Brief — Solving Problems by Design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a design brief?**
a) A written statement of a problem to be solved by designing something  b) The person or group who needs the problem solved  c) What the finished thing must do  d) Time, materials and cost limits
**Answer: a) A written statement of a problem to be solved by designing something**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who is a design brief written for?**
a) What the finished thing must do  b) The person or group who needs the problem solved  c) Time, materials and cost limits  d) No — it states the problem; the design answers it
**Answer: b) The person or group who needs the problem solved**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must a design brief name about the finished thing?**
a) Time, materials and cost limits  b) No — it states the problem; the design answers it  c) What the finished thing must do  d) Research the problem and the needs
**Answer: c) What the finished thing must do**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What limits belong in a design brief?**
a) No — it states the problem; the design answers it  b) Research the problem and the needs  c) Choose the best idea against the brief  d) Time, materials and cost limits
**Answer: d) Time, materials and cost limits**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is a design brief the design itself?**
a) No — it states the problem; the design answers it  b) Research the problem and the needs  c) Choose the best idea against the brief  d) After a first version is made
**Answer: a) No — it states the problem; the design answers it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the first stage after reading the brief?**
a) Choose the best idea against the brief  b) Research the problem and the needs  c) After a first version is made  d) Improvements to the design
**Answer: b) Research the problem and the needs**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What comes after sketching ideas?**
a) After a first version is made  b) Improvements to the design  c) Choose the best idea against the brief  d) A real brief carried through to a finished product
**Answer: c) Choose the best idea against the brief**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is a design tested?**
a) Improvements to the design  b) A real brief carried through to a finished product  c) A written statement of a problem to be solved by designing something  d) After a first version is made
**Answer: d) After a first version is made**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does testing a design lead to?**
a) Improvements to the design  b) A real brief carried through to a finished product  c) A written statement of a problem to be solved by designing something  d) The person or group who needs the problem solved
**Answer: a) Improvements to the design**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a case study of a design brief show?**
a) A written statement of a problem to be solved by designing something  b) A real brief carried through to a finished product  c) The person or group who needs the problem solved  d) What the finished thing must do
**Answer: b) A real brief carried through to a finished product**
