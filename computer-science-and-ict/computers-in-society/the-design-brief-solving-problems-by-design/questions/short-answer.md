# The Design Brief — Solving Problems by Design — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a design brief?**
**Answer: A written statement of a problem to be solved by designing something**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who is a design brief written for?**
**Answer: The person or group who needs the problem solved**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must a design brief name about the finished thing?**
**Answer: What the finished thing must do**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What limits belong in a design brief?**
**Answer: Time, materials and cost limits**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is a design brief the design itself?**
**Answer: No — it states the problem; the design answers it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the first stage after reading the brief?**
**Answer: Research the problem and the needs**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What comes after sketching ideas?**
**Answer: Choose the best idea against the brief**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is a design tested?**
**Answer: After a first version is made**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does testing a design lead to?**
**Answer: Improvements to the design**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a case study of a design brief show?**
**Answer: A real brief carried through to a finished product**
