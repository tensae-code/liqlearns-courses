# Hands-on — The Design Brief — Solving Problems by Design

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Write a one-page brief
Choose a problem in your school — a bag that breaks, a dark storeroom. Write its design brief: who needs it, what the solution must do, and the time, material and cost limits.

## Walk a case through the stages
Take the brief you wrote and list, stage by stage, what research, sketches, choice, making and testing would look like for it.
