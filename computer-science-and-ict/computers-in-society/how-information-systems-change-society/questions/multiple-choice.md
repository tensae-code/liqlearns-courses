# How information systems change society — gains and problems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a social gain the book names?
a) access that narrows the digital divide  b) more cyberbullying  c) plagiarism  d) e-waste
**Answer: a) access that narrows the digital divide** — a gain, not a problem.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** AI and augmented reality appear in the book as —
a) a social gain in public life  b) a crime  c) a health harm only  d) a kind of e-waste
**Answer: a) a social gain in public life** — named on the gains side.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Digital platforms, social media and games serve —
a) only harm  b) business and entertainment  c) only schools  d) only government
**Answer: b) business and entertainment** — the third gain family.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The unit's method with social problems is to —
a) ban the technology  b) reduce the harm while keeping the benefits  c) ignore them  d) deny the benefits
**Answer: b) reduce the harm while keeping the benefits** — the pairing the book teaches.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the guide's frame, unmanaged time online harms school results because it —
a) breaks devices  b) replaces reading and course resources  c) costs money  d) annoys teachers
**Answer: b) replaces reading and course resources** — the mechanism (teacher guide — evidence).
