# How information systems change society — gains and problems — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three families of social gains.
**Answer:** Access narrowing the digital divide; AI and augmented reality in public life; digital platforms, social media and games for business and entertainment.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why are more hours online associated with more cyberbullying?
**Answer:** Teenagers follow different social standards online, where anonymity and freedom from penalty make them more aggressive or judgemental (teacher guide — evidence).
