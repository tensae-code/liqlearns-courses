# Hands-on — How information systems change society — gains and problems

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## One advance, two sides
Pick one advance (mobile money, group chats, video lessons). Interview two people at home: one gain it brought them, one problem. Write both, side by side, without editing either away.

## Campaign sketch
Design a one-poster awareness campaign for one problem from this lesson, in the guide's spirit: the poster must protect the benefit while fighting the harm.
