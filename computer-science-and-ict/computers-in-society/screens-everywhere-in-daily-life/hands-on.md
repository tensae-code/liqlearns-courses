# Hands-on — Screens Everywhere in Daily Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Screen safari** — Walk one route — classroom to gate, or kitchen to door — and list every screen, with its job. Compare lists: who found the hidden ones?

## Task two
**One place, all its screens** — Draw one room. Mark every screen in it with a star and write its job beside it.
