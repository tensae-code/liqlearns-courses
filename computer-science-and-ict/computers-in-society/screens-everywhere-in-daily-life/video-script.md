# Video script — Screens Everywhere in Daily Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some screens announce themselves: television, tablet, phone, computer. Others hide inside things: the washing machine's little display, the oven clock, the bus-stop board, the ticket machine, the car's dashboard. The Décodage scenario sends the youngest pupils to find screens **in the places of life (les lieux de vie)** — because you cannot think about your screen life until you can see how surrounded by screens it already is.
Each found screen gets one question: **what is it for?** Some show (a departure board), some let you choose (a ticket machine), some watch and measure (a thermostat display), some entertain. Sorting screens by their job turns a vague feeling — “screens everywhere” — into knowledge: this place has these screens, doing these jobs, and I use them in these ways.
