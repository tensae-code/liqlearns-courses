# Screens Everywhere in Daily Life — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name a screen that hides inside a thing.**
**Answer: The washing machine's display (also: oven clock, dashboard)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the scenario send pupils hunting?**
**Answer: Into the places of life — home, street, shop, school**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why find the screens first?**
**Answer: You cannot think about your screen life until you see how surrounded it is**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name two screens that announce themselves.**
**Answer: Television and phone (also: tablet, computer)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a bus-stop board?**
**Answer: A screen that shows information in a public place**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What question does each found screen get?**
**Answer: What is it for?**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a departure board do?**
**Answer: It shows information**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a ticket machine let you do?**
**Answer: Choose (and buy)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What turns the vague feeling into knowledge?**
**Answer: Sorting the screens by their jobs**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four jobs named in the lesson.**
**Answer: Show, let you choose, watch and measure, entertain**
