# Transparency Guidelines for a Citizen Service — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Its facts are public before anyone asks**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Its facts are public before anyone asks** — as the answer to: Name three facts a transparent service publishes.
**Answer: False — Its cost, its waiting time and its needed papers (also what it gives, who decides)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Power over citizens**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Power over citizens** — as the answer to: How does digital technology serve transparency?
**Answer: False — The same facts reach everyone at once and cannot be quietly changed**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The citizens**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The citizens** — as the answer to: What is accountability?
**Answer: False — Transparency with a name attached — every decision has a decider**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A published charter of fees and times, and a receipt with a complaint number (also recorded deciders, open review)**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A published charter of fees and times, and a receipt with a complaint number (also recorded deciders, open review)** — as the answer to: What road must every complaint have?
**Answer: False — A road and a deadline**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A service binds itself before it is tested**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A service binds itself before it is tested** — as the answer to: What does every taka need?
**Answer: False — A record**
