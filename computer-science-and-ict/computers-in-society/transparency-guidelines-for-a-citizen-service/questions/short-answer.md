# Transparency Guidelines for a Citizen Service — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does transparency mean for a service?**
**Answer: Its facts are public before anyone asks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three facts a transparent service publishes.**
**Answer: Its cost, its waiting time and its needed papers (also what it gives, who decides)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are hidden facts, in the lesson's words?**
**Answer: Power over citizens**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does digital technology serve transparency?**
**Answer: The same facts reach everyone at once and cannot be quietly changed**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Who gains power from published facts?**
**Answer: The citizens**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is accountability?**
**Answer: Transparency with a name attached — every decision has a decider**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two things the class guidelines require.**
**Answer: A published charter of fees and times, and a receipt with a complaint number (also recorded deciders, open review)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What road must every complaint have?**
**Answer: A road and a deadline**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why must guidelines be written in advance?**
**Answer: A service binds itself before it is tested**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does every taka need?**
**Answer: A record**
