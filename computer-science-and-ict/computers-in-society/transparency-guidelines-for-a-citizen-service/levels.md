# Transparency Guidelines for a Citizen Service — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Transparency — the service in the open
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Its facts are public before anyone asks; Its cost, its waiting time and its needed papers (also what it gives, who decides); Power over citizens; The same facts reach everyone at once and cannot be quietly changed; The citizens.

## Sub lesson 2 — Accountability — and the guidelines that bind them
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Transparency with a name attached — every decision has a decider; A published charter of fees and times, and a receipt with a complaint number (also recorded deciders, open review); A road and a deadline; A service binds itself before it is tested; A record.
