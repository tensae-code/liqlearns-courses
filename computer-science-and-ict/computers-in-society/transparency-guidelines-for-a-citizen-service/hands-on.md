# Hands-on — Transparency Guidelines for a Citizen Service

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Write the guidelines** — Choose one citizen service. Write its transparency guidelines: the charter to publish, the records to keep, the complaint road and its deadline.

## Task two
**Audit a service** — Take one real service's public information. Score it against your guidelines — which facts are published, which are hidden — and name the first fix.
