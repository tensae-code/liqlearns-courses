# The Regional Diversity Card — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the regional diversity card profile?**
**Answer: A region, through its people's differences**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three things the research looks for.**
**Answer: Languages, occupations and festivals (also foods, dress, beliefs, crafts)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three source kinds.**
**Answer: Elders and family, books, and the internet**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a one-website profile called in this lesson?**
**Answer: A rumour with headings**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Whose mouths give knowledge of a region?**
**Answer: Its own people's**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the card's second half about?**
**Answer: How technology is changing the region**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two changes technology brings a region.**
**Answer: Phones in the market and machines in the field (also new jobs, pressured crafts)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What two halves does the finished card hold?**
**Answer: What the region is, and what it is becoming**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How are the cards finally used?**
**Answer: Presented side by side to teach the country's diversity**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who describes each region?**
**Answer: Its own children**
