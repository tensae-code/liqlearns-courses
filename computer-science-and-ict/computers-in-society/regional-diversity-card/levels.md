# The Regional Diversity Card — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The beauty of diversity — researching a region
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A region, through its people's differences; Languages, occupations and festivals (also foods, dress, beliefs, crafts); Elders and family, books, and the internet; A rumour with headings; Its own people's.

## Sub lesson 2 — Change of technology — the region in motion
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: How technology is changing the region; Phones in the market and machines in the field (also new jobs, pressured crafts); What the region is, and what it is becoming; Presented side by side to teach the country's diversity; Its own children.
