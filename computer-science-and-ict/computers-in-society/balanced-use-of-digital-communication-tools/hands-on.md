# Hands-on — Balanced Use of Digital Communication Tools

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Balance audit** — Audit your last day's tool use: errands served, hours taken, one limit you will set — in writing.

## Task two
**Pause practice** — Write the message you would have sent in anger (unsent), then its morning-after version; name what the night changed.
