# Balanced Use of Digital Communication Tools — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Distance abolished, speed and cheapness, communities of interest, a record of what was said**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Distance abolished, speed and cheapness, communities of interest, a record of what was said** — as the answer to: Name five negatives.
**Answer: False — Time past intention, misunderstanding without faces, exposure, permanent-availability pressure, strangers and harms**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The screenshot**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The screenshot** — as the answer to: What two lazy verdicts are refused?
**Answer: False — 'Tools are bad' and 'more is better'**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Per tool, per person, per hour**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Per tool, per person, per hour** — as the answer to: Name four irresponsible-use risks.
**Answer: False — Unsendable angry words, private shared publicly, rumours forwarded, hours eating sleep/study/family**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Purpose before opening, limits kept, the pause before sending, presence**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Purpose before opening, limits kept, the pause before sending, presence** — as the answer to: What does the angry message do?
**Answer: False — Sleeps a night**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The person in the room outranks the person on the screen**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The person in the room outranks the person on the screen** — as the answer to: What are tools to be kept as?
**Answer: False — Servants of the life, not its landlords**
