# Balanced Use of Digital Communication Tools — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name four positives.**
**Answer: Distance abolished, speed and cheapness, communities of interest, a record of what was said**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name five negatives.**
**Answer: Time past intention, misunderstanding without faces, exposure, permanent-availability pressure, strangers and harms**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What outlives the chat?**
**Answer: The screenshot**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What two lazy verdicts are refused?**
**Answer: 'Tools are bad' and 'more is better'**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Per what is balance remade?**
**Answer: Per tool, per person, per hour**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name four irresponsible-use risks.**
**Answer: Unsendable angry words, private shared publicly, rumours forwarded, hours eating sleep/study/family**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the balanced craft's four habits.**
**Answer: Purpose before opening, limits kept, the pause before sending, presence**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the angry message do?**
**Answer: Sleeps a night**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who outranks whom in presence?**
**Answer: The person in the room outranks the person on the screen**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What are tools to be kept as?**
**Answer: Servants of the life, not its landlords**
