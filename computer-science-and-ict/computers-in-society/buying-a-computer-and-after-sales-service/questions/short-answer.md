# Buying a Computer and After-Sales Service — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What must a buyer know first before choosing a computer?**
**Answer: What the computer is needed for — the user's needs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is compared against those needs?**
**Answer: The prepared specification — processor, hard disk, monitor and peripherals — and the price**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does price alone not decide the purchase?**
**Answer: A cheap computer that does not fit the user's needs is not a saving — it is the wrong computer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Who is the specification prepared for?**
**Answer: A real user with real work the computer must do**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is checked about the seller before buying?**
**Answer: The after-sales service they give after the sale**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is after-sales service?**
**Answer: The help given after you buy — warranty, repairs and service**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a warranty or guarantee promise?**
**Answer: That faults within a set time will be repaired or put right by the seller**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why does after-sales service count in the buying decision?**
**Answer: A computer that cannot be repaired nearby costs its owner time and money for years**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is after-sales service used?**
**Answer: After the purchase, when the computer needs repair or service**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two halves make a wise purchase?**
**Answer: A specification that fits the user, and service that stands behind the machine after the sale**
