# Buying a Computer and After-Sales Service — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What must a buyer know first before choosing a computer?" is: What the computer is needed for — the user's needs**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is compared against those needs?" is: A cheap computer that does not fit the user's needs is not a saving — it is the wrong computer**
**Answer: False** — The prepared specification — processor, hard disk, monitor and peripherals — and the price

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why does price alone not decide the purchase?" is: A cheap computer that does not fit the user's needs is not a saving — it is the wrong computer**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Who is the specification prepared for?" is: The after-sales service they give after the sale**
**Answer: False** — A real user with real work the computer must do

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is checked about the seller before buying?" is: The after-sales service they give after the sale**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is after-sales service?" is: That faults within a set time will be repaired or put right by the seller**
**Answer: False** — The help given after you buy — warranty, repairs and service

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does a warranty or guarantee promise?" is: That faults within a set time will be repaired or put right by the seller**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why does after-sales service count in the buying decision?" is: After the purchase, when the computer needs repair or service**
**Answer: False** — A computer that cannot be repaired nearby costs its owner time and money for years

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is after-sales service used?" is: After the purchase, when the computer needs repair or service**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What two halves make a wise purchase?" is: What the computer is needed for — the user's needs**
**Answer: False** — A specification that fits the user, and service that stands behind the machine after the sale
