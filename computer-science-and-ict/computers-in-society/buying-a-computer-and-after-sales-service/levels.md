# Buying a Computer and After-Sales Service — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Buying considerations — needs first, then the machine
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: What the computer is needed for — the user's needs; The prepared specification — processor, hard disk, monitor and peripherals — and the price; A cheap computer that does not fit the user's needs is not a saving — it is the wrong computer; A real user with real work the computer must do; The after-sales service they give after the sale.

## Sub lesson 2 — After-sales service — the help that comes after the sale
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The help given after you buy — warranty, repairs and service; That faults within a set time will be repaired or put right by the seller; A computer that cannot be repaired nearby costs its owner time and money for years; After the purchase, when the computer needs repair or service; A specification that fits the user, and service that stands behind the machine after the sale.
