# Hands-on — Buying a Computer and After-Sales Service

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Prepare a real purchase', 'Choose a real buyer — a student, a school office, a small shop. Write their needs, prepare the specification that fits, and list what you would ask the seller about warranty and repairs before paying.')

## Task two
('Compare two sellers', 'Take two sellers offering the same computer at nearly the same price. Compare their after-sales service — warranty time, repair place, service — and write which buy is wiser and why.')
