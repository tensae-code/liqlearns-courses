# Hands-on — Women and Gender in the History of Computing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Rewrite the picture** — Find a picture or page that shows “people who work with computers”. Describe who is shown and who is missing. Redraw or rewrite it so the true history shows.

## Task two
**Three names, three deeds** — Learn one deed each of Lovelace, Hopper and the ENIAC programmers, and teach them to a partner in one minute.
