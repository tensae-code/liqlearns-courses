# Screens and socialising — the family and café problem — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Being in the same room means a group is socialising.
**Answer: False** — the café picture shows presence without company.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Digital well-being requires giving up all devices.
**Answer: False** — it restores the device's place; it does not ban it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Socialising is the glue that holds a group together.
**Answer: True** — the book's starting claim.
