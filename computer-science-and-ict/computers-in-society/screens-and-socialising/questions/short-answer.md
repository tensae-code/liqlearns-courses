# Screens and socialising — the family and café problem — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Describe the book's café scene in one sentence.
**Answer:** Friends sit together in a café, each staring at a touchscreen instead of talking to one another.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the book's remedy for the socialisation gap.
**Answer:** Digital well-being: let technology improve life, not distract from it and from other people.
