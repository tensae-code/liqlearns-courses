# Screens and socialising — the family and café problem — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What holds a family or friend group together?
a) screens  b) socialising — talking and being together  c) silence  d) separate rooms
**Answer: b) socialising — talking and being together** — the glue the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Homes full of devices can —
a) guarantee conversation  b) hollow out socialising  c) remove all problems  d) teach manners
**Answer: b) hollow out socialising** — the harm stated plainly.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's café picture, the friends are —
a) debating  b) each staring at a touchscreen instead of talking  c) playing cards  d) reading newspapers
**Answer: b) each staring at a touchscreen instead of talking** — the book's own image.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Digital well-being means letting technology —
a) rule the home  b) improve life, not distract from it and from other people  c) replace friends  d) run all night
**Answer: b) improve life, not distract from it and from other people** — the remedy in the book's words.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The target of well-being is —
a) the device itself  b) distraction from life and people  c) café owners  d) homework
**Answer: b) distraction from life and people** — the device keeps its place; distraction is fought.
