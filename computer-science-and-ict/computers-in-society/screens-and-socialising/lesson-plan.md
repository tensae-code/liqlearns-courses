# Lesson plan — Screens and socialising — the family and café problem

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** Screens and socialising — the family and café problem
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 14 — Computers in Society of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 12, Unit 1 — Information Systems and Their Applications. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** homes full of devices can hollow out the socialising that holds families and friends together; the remedy is digital well-being — technology improving life, not distracting from people.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the society lesson just before it (recommended first, never forced) and needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The café problem** — socialising holds a group together; device-filled homes can hollow it out.
2. **Digital well-being — the remedy** — let technology improve life, not distract from it and from other people.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
