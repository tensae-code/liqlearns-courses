# Hands-on — Screens and socialising — the family and café problem

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The one-table count
At one family meal, quietly count glances at screens (tally marks on paper under the table are fine). Afterwards, share the number — not as blame, but as the café picture made real.

## Phones in the middle
Try the stack: at one gathering, all phones go face-down in the middle of the table; the first person to pick theirs up washes the cups. Note what the talking does.
