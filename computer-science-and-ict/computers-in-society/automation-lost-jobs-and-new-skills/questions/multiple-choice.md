# Automation, lost jobs and new skills — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Machines take over first the jobs built on —
a) routine tasks  b) leadership  c) invention  d) teaching
**Answer: a) routine tasks** — routine is what automation is good at.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which job does the book name as taken over?
a) cashier  b) surgeon  c) judge  d) poet
**Answer: a) cashier** — with data-entry clerks and factory workers.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Data-entry clerks and factory workers share with cashiers —
a) uniforms  b) routine, predictable work  c) high pay  d) no tools
**Answer: b) routine, predictable work** — the pattern in the named jobs.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Over time, automation creates —
a) no jobs  b) about as many jobs as it destroys  c) only factory jobs  d) jobs only abroad
**Answer: b) about as many jobs as it destroys** — the book's long-run balance.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Most at risk from automation are —
a) the highly educated  b) people with low education  c) children  d) farmers only
**Answer: b) people with low education** — the future market needs skilled professionals.
