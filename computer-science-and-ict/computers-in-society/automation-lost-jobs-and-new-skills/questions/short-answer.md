# Automation, lost jobs and new skills — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three jobs the book says automated systems can take over.
**Answer:** Cashiers, data-entry clerks and factory workers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why are people with low education most at risk?
**Answer:** Because the future market needs skilled professionals, and the skills needed keep changing.
