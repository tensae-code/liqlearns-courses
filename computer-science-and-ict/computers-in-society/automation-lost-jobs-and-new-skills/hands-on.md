# Hands-on — Automation, lost jobs and new skills

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Two columns for real
In a group of three (the guide's own grouping), list jobs in your area ended by technology and jobs created by it. Present both columns and the balance you found — not the one you expected.

## Skill ladder
Pick one endangered job and one created job. Write the skill ladder between them: what a person in the first would learn, step by step, to reach the second.
