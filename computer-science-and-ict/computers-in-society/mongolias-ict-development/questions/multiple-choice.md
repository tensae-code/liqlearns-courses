# Mongolia's ICT Development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which policy steers Mongolia's ICT development?**
a) The State ICT Policy 2017–2025  b) 17 January 1996, with about 500 users  c) 2.6 million  d) 36,760 km, reaching 299 soums in 21 aimags
**Answer: a) The State ICT Policy 2017–2025**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When did Mongolia's internet service begin, and with how many users?**
a) 2.6 million  b) 17 January 1996, with about 500 users  c) 36,760 km, reaching 299 soums in 21 aimags  d) VSAT satellite links and triple-play IPTV
**Answer: b) 17 January 1996, with about 500 users**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How many users by 2017?**
a) 36,760 km, reaching 299 soums in 21 aimags  b) VSAT satellite links and triple-play IPTV  c) 2.6 million  d) Television, internet and phone in one service
