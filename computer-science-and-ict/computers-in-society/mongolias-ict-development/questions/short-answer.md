# Mongolia's ICT Development — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which policy steers Mongolia's ICT development?**
**Answer: The State ICT Policy 2017–2025**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When did Mongolia's internet service begin, and with how many users?**
**Answer: 17 January 1996, with about 500 users**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How many users by 2017?**
**Answer: 2.6 million**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How much fibre, reaching how far?**
**Answer: 36,760 km, reaching 299 soums in 21 aimags**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What carries service where fibre cannot?**
**Answer: VSAT satellite links and triple-play IPTV**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is triple-play?**
**Answer: Television, internet and phone in one service**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is Mongolsat-1?**
**Answer: Mongolia's own satellite, launched 2017**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the digital divide?**
**Answer: The gap between the connected and the unconnected — city against far soum**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the divide's shadow in statistics?**
**Answer: Mongolia's falling place in the world's index**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the account given to the pupils?**
**Answer: Because it is unfinished — closing the divide is their generation's homework**
