# Writing Instructions for a Public Service — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What must happen before instructions are written?**
**Answer: The service's real steps must be identified**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three things a step's research must capture.**
**Answer: Its papers, its order and its office (also fees, hours)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why walk the service or question a user?**
**Answer: Instructions from imagination fail at unchecked steps**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What evidence does each step need?**
**Answer: Real names, real hours, real amounts**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the service journey end, in the research?**
**Answer: At the step where the citizen actually receives the service**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How are handbook steps written?**
**Answer: Numbered, in order, one action each**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where do warnings belong?**
**Answer: Where the mistakes happen**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What closes the handbook?**
**Answer: A checklist to run before leaving home**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who tests a handbook?**
**Answer: A stranger who has never used the service**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When the stranger stumbles, what is at fault?**
**Answer: The handbook's step — the rewrite fixes the step, not the reader**
