# Writing Instructions for a Public Service — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What must happen before instructions are written?** → **The service's real steps must be identified**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three things a step's research must capture.** → **Its papers, its order and its office (also fees, hours)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why walk the service or question a user?** → **Instructions from imagination fail at unchecked steps**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What evidence does each step need?** → **Real names, real hours, real amounts**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the service journey end, in the research?** → **At the step where the citizen actually receives the service**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How are handbook steps written?** → **Numbered, in order, one action each**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where do warnings belong?** → **Where the mistakes happen**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What closes the handbook?** → **A checklist to run before leaving home**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who tests a handbook?** → **A stranger who has never used the service**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When the stranger stumbles, what is at fault?** → **The handbook's step — the rewrite fixes the step, not the reader**
