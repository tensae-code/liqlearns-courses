# Writing Instructions for a Public Service — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The service's real steps must be identified**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The service's real steps must be identified** — as the answer to: Name three things a step's research must capture.
**Answer: False — Its papers, its order and its office (also fees, hours)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Instructions from imagination fail at unchecked steps**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Instructions from imagination fail at unchecked steps** — as the answer to: What evidence does each step need?
**Answer: False — Real names, real hours, real amounts**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: At the step where the citizen actually receives the service**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: At the step where the citizen actually receives the service** — as the answer to: How are handbook steps written?
**Answer: False — Numbered, in order, one action each**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Where the mistakes happen**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Where the mistakes happen** — as the answer to: What closes the handbook?
**Answer: False — A checklist to run before leaving home**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A stranger who has never used the service**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A stranger who has never used the service** — as the answer to: When the stranger stumbles, what is at fault?
**Answer: False — The handbook's step — the rewrite fixes the step, not the reader**
