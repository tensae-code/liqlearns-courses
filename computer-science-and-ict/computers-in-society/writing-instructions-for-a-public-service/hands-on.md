# Hands-on — Writing Instructions for a Public Service

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Write the handbook** — Choose one real public service. Research its steps and write the handbook: numbered steps, papers, fees, warnings, and the leaving-home checklist.

## Task two
**Stranger-test it** — Give your handbook to someone outside your team. Watch in silence, note every stumble, and rewrite those steps.
