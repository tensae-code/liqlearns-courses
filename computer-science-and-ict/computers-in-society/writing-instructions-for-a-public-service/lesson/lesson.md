# Writing Instructions for a Public Service

## Do it first

A service can exist and still be unusable — if nobody can find its steps. The citizen queues at the wrong window, lacks one paper, returns tomorrow. Grade 7's project answers that failure with a writer's tool: instructions so clear that a stranger succeeds the first time.

## Sub lesson 1 — Finding the steps — identify before writing

Good instructions begin as research: **identify the steps** of the service as they truly are — where it starts, what papers and fees it needs, which office or screen does what, in what order, and where it ends. The Grade 7 teams walk the service themselves or question someone who has, because instructions written from imagination fail at exactly the step nobody checked. Every step gets its evidence: the form's real name, the office's real hours, the fee's real amount.

## Sub lesson 2 — The handbook — steps a stranger can follow

Then the steps become a **handbook**: numbered, in order, each step one action with its place and its needed papers named; warnings placed where the mistakes happen ('bring the photocopy — the office has no copier'); and a final checklist to run before leaving home. The test of the handbook is a stranger: hand it to someone who has never used the service and watch silently where they stumble — the stumble is the handbook's fault, and the rewrite fixes the step, not the reader.

## Do It Yourself

1. Show it, don't just say it: The service's real steps must be identified. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
2. Show it, don't just say it: Its papers, its order and its office (also fees, hours). Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
3. Show it, don't just say it: Instructions from imagination fail at unchecked steps. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
4. Show it, don't just say it: Real names, real hours, real amounts. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
