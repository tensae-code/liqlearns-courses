# Writing Instructions for a Public Service — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Finding the steps — identify before writing
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The service's real steps must be identified; Its papers, its order and its office (also fees, hours); Instructions from imagination fail at unchecked steps; Real names, real hours, real amounts; At the step where the citizen actually receives the service.

## Sub lesson 2 — The handbook — steps a stranger can follow
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Numbered, in order, one action each; Where the mistakes happen; A checklist to run before leaving home; A stranger who has never used the service; The handbook's step — the rewrite fixes the step, not the reader.
