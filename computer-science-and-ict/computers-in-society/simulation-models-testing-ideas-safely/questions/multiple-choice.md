# Simulation Models — Testing Ideas Safely — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a simulation model copy?**
a) A real system or process  b) To test ideas without risk to the real thing  c) The real system it copies  d) Before building or changing the real system
**Answer: a) A real system or process**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the purpose of simulation?**
a) The real system it copies  b) To test ideas without risk to the real thing  c) Before building or changing the real system  d) Less — and it is safer
**Answer: b) To test ideas without risk to the real thing**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A simulation model is simpler than what?**
a) Before building or changing the real system  b) Less — and it is safer  c) The real system it copies  d) The inputs and conditions
**Answer: c) The real system it copies**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When are simulations used?**
a) Less — and it is safer  b) The inputs and conditions  c) The outcomes (results)  d) Before building or changing the real system
**Answer: d) Before building or changing the real system**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Testing ideas in a model costs what, compared with the real thing?**
a) Less — and it is safer  b) The inputs and conditions  c) The outcomes (results)  d) The design or idea — not the real system
**Answer: a) Less — and it is safer**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do you change in a simulation?**
a) The outcomes (results)  b) The inputs and conditions  c) The design or idea — not the real system  d) Because mistakes there harm no one
**Answer: b) The inputs and conditions**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do you watch in a simulation?**
a) The design or idea — not the real system  b) Because mistakes there harm no one  c) The outcomes (results)  d) The real system it represents
**Answer: c) The outcomes (results)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) If simulation outcomes are bad, what do you change?**
a) Because mistakes there harm no one  b) The real system it represents  c) A real system or process  d) The design or idea — not the real system
**Answer: d) The design or idea — not the real system**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why do pilots train in simulators?**
a) Because mistakes there harm no one  b) The real system it represents  c) A real system or process  d) To test ideas without risk to the real thing
**Answer: a) Because mistakes there harm no one**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) A model must be checked against what?**
a) A real system or process  b) The real system it represents  c) To test ideas without risk to the real thing  d) The real system it copies
**Answer: b) The real system it represents**
