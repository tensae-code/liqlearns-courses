## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What a simulation model is for** — A **simulation model** copies **a real system or process** in a simpler form. Its purpose is **to test ideas without risk to the real thing**: a simulation is used **before building or changing the real system**, and testing in a model **costs less and is safer** than testing the real thing — this is why pilots train in simulators, where mistakes harm no one.
2. **Using a model to try ideas** — To use a simulation you **change the inputs and conditions** and **watch the outcomes**. If the outcomes are bad, you change **the design or idea — not the real system**. A model earns its trust by being **checked against the real system it represents**.
