# Video script — Simulation Models — Testing Ideas Safely

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **simulation model** copies **a real system or process** in a simpler form. Its purpose is **to test ideas without risk to the real thing**: a simulation is used **before building or changing the real system**, and testing in a model **costs less and is safer** than testing the real thing — this is why pilots train in simulators, where mistakes harm no one.
To use a simulation you **change the inputs and conditions** and **watch the outcomes**. If the outcomes are bad, you change **the design or idea — not the real system**. A model earns its trust by being **checked against the real system it represents**.
