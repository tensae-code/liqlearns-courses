# Hands-on — Simulation Models — Testing Ideas Safely

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Paper simulation
Model a shop queue on paper: one server, customers arriving each minute. Run ten minutes twice — once with one server, once with two — and compare queue lengths.

## Safe to fail
Name three real systems where testing the real thing is dangerous or costly. For each, say what the simulation copy would be.
