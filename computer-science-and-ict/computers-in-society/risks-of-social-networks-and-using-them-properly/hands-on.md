# Hands-on — Risks of social networks — and using them properly

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Risk sort
Write the eight risks on cards. A partner reads out scenes — 'a stranger asks where you live', 'the same hurtful joke every day for a week' — and you hold up the matching risk card and say its full name.

## The family question
Do the closing activity for real: ask at home how much the family uses social networks and for what purpose. Bring the purposes back and sort them beside the benefits lesson's five.
