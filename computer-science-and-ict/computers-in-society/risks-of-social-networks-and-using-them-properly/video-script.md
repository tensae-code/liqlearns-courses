# Video script — Risks of social networks — and using them properly

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Used badly, the internet harms.

Bad content you see. Bad content you send.
Your number, your birthday, your place — told to strangers.
Bullying on a screen. Adverts without end.
Your information stolen. Lies that look like news.

Eight risks. Learn their names.
Then use it properly.
