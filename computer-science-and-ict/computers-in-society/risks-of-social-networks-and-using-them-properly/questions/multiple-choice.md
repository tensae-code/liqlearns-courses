# Risks of social networks — and using them properly — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Cyberbullying is —
a) one accidental rude word  b) using digital technology to deliberately and repeatedly hurt someone  c) losing a game  d) a slow connection
**Answer: b) using digital technology to deliberately and repeatedly hurt someone**

**2.** Which personal information is named as risky to share with strangers?
a) phone numbers, date of birth, location  b) favourite colour  c) a book's title  d) the weather
**Answer: a) phone numbers, date of birth, location**

**3.** Inappropriate content a user might be exposed to includes —
a) school notices  b) mean, aggressive or sexual comments or images  c) timetables  d) maps
**Answer: b) mean, aggressive or sexual comments or images**

**4.** Uploading inappropriate content means —
a) uploading photos or videos of yourself or others that should not be shared  b) uploading homework  c) uploading nothing  d) uploading slowly
**Answer: a) uploading photos or videos of yourself or others that should not be shared**

**5.** Too much targeted _______ is one of the risks.
a) homework  b) advertising and marketing  c) reading  d) exercise
**Answer: b) advertising and marketing**
