# Lesson plan — Risks of social networks — and using them properly

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** Risks of social networks — and using them properly
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 14 — Computers in Society of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 4 — Internet. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** used improperly, social networks carry eight named risks — from inappropriate content seen and uploaded, through sharing personal information with strangers and cyberbullying, to stolen information and misleading content — and knowing them by name is how a user stays on the proper side.
**This lesson's own level:** it closes this band's social-network set: it stands on the benefits lesson before it, whose condition — used properly — this lesson defines from the other side.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Benefits of social networks. Recommended, never forced. A learner can land here directly; the lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the eight risks of improper use.
2. Define cyberbullying in the book's words.
3. State which personal information is named as dangerous to share with strangers: phone numbers, date of birth, location.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner meets three danger scenes — a stranger asking, a repeated hurtful message, an invented story — and names as many more risks as they can before the list of eight is given (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the parts of the lesson, in order (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Counts fewer than eight → recount by groups: content seen (1), content uploaded (2), information shared and stolen (3, 7), people hurting people (4, 5), adverts (6), false content (8).
- Thinks cyberbullying is one rude message → the definition carries two extra words: deliberately and repeatedly.
