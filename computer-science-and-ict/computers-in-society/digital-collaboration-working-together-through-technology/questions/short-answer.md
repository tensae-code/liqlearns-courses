# Digital collaboration — working together through technology — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five ingredients the definition gathers.
**Answer:** Tools, apps, software, procedures and processes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What can a teacher do with collaboration as a way of working?
**Answer:** Give a more dynamic experience, modify classes to meet each pupil's needs, and let pupils work at their own speed and interests while engaging classmates (teacher guide — evidence).
