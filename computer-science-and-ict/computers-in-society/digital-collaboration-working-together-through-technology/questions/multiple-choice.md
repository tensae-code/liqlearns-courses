# Digital collaboration — working together through technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Digital collaboration is —
a) owning software  b) working with other people and getting things done using digital devices and technology  c) emailing files  d) video games
**Answer: b) working with other people and getting things done using digital devices and technology** — the definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** It improves — across an organisation.
a) communication, document management and information flow  b) only speed  c) only storage  d) only printing
**Answer: a) communication, document management and information flow** — the three named improvements.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The internet lets people collaborate —
a) only in one room  b) in real time across time zones and geography  c) only by post  d) only at night
**Answer: b) in real time across time zones and geography** — distance removed.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's framing: digital collaboration is —
a) a set of tools  b) a way of working or learning  c) a brand  d) a room layout
**Answer: b) a way of working or learning** — not the tools (teacher guide — evidence).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Used well, it lets pupils —
a) avoid classmates  b) work at their own speed and engage with classmates at the same time  c) skip all tasks  d) work only alone
**Answer: b) work at their own speed and engage with classmates at the same time** — the guide's pair (teacher guide — evidence).
