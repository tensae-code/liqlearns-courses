# Hands-on — Digital collaboration — working together through technology

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Same page, three places
With two partners in different places (or different rooms), open one shared document and each add a paragraph in turn, live. Note the moment all three see the same page — that moment is the definition, experienced.

## Working agreement
Write your group's agreement before its next task — roles, document home, decision rule, meeting rhythm — and judge afterwards whether the agreement, not the app, was what worked.
