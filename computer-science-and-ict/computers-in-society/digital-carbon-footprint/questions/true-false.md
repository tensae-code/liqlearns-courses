# The Digital Carbon Footprint — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The total greenhouse-gas emissions an activity causes, in CO₂-equivalent**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The total greenhouse-gas emissions an activity causes, in CO₂-equivalent** — as the answer to: Name the digital footprint's three homes.
**Answer: False — Devices, networks, data centres**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Its making**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Its making** — as the answer to: What is the scale honesty?
**Answer: False — Tiny × billions × always-on is a country's power station**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Other people's very material buildings**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Other people's very material buildings** — as the answer to: Name the life's four counting stages.
**Answer: False — Making, using, storing forever, throwing away**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Electricity × the grid's dirtiness × hours**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Electricity × the grid's dirtiness × hours** — as the answer to: What does storing forever keep warm?
**Answer: False — The never-deleted file, copied in three buildings**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Keep devices longer, delete/unsubscribe, stream at screen size, choose renewable-powered services (or recycle)**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Keep devices longer, delete/unsubscribe, stream at screen size, choose renewable-powered services (or recycle)** — as the answer to: Why is the footprint inside green computing?
**Answer: False — A footprint is a principle with a number on it — numbers can be improved**
