# Hands-on — The Digital Carbon Footprint

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Count a day** — Estimate your digital footprint for one day in rough parts — device making-share, use, storage — and name the biggest part.

## Task two
**Shrink plan** — Write a five-line shrink plan for your household's digital life, each line tied to a counting stage.
