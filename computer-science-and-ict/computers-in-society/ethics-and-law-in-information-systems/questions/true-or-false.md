# Ethics and law in information systems — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Medicine, education, government and business already have formal ethical and legal oversight.
**Answer: True** — the book names those settled areas.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Because banks encrypt what travels, a public computer is completely safe for banking.
**Answer: False** — encryption protects the journey; recovered login details are the risk (teacher guide — evidence).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Sanctions for unethical behaviour can reach decades in prison and fines of hundreds of thousands of birr.
**Answer: True** — the guide's heaviest end of the scale (teacher guide — evidence).
