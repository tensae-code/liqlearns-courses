# Ethics and law in information systems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethics is —
a) deciding right from wrong  b) a computer program  c) a password rule  d) a kind of software
**Answer: a) deciding right from wrong** — the book's definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do information systems raise unsettled ethical questions?
a) they never change  b) they change so fast that no settled standard matches their problems  c) nobody uses them  d) they are all illegal
**Answer: b) they change so fast that no settled standard matches their problems** — speed is the reason the standard lags.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Banking on a public computer is risky mainly because —
a) banks never encrypt  b) login details left behind can be recovered and used later  c) public computers are slow  d) the screen is big
**Answer: b) login details left behind can be recovered and used later** — the guide's key point (teacher guide — evidence).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The banks' stated problem is that —
a) users are not always vigilant about their own information  b) encryption is too cheap  c) branches are too far  d) phones are too small
**Answer: a) users are not always vigilant about their own information** — the guide names the user, not the bank (teacher guide — evidence).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** An organisation whose people act unethically risks —
a) only praise  b) credibility, morale and productivity  c) nothing at all  d) free advertising
**Answer: b) credibility, morale and productivity** — the guide's graded costs (teacher guide — evidence).
