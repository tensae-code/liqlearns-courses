# Video script — Ethics and law in information systems

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. (Short script — the placement names no full video need for this lesson, so the script is short.)

---

Ethics is deciding right from wrong.

Old fields have settled rules. Computer systems change too fast.

So we learn the questions directly: privacy. Security. Gathering. Ownership.

And breaking ethics has a price — for you, for your organisation, under the law.
