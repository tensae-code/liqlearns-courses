# Hands-on — Ethics and law in information systems

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Judge it twice
Take three real online actions (your own or ones you have seen). For each, write two lines: the ethics line (right or wrong, and why) and the law line (is there a rule? what sanction?). Keep the two judgements separate — that separation is the skill.

## Public-device audit
List every shared or public device you touch in a week. Beside each, write what private thing must never be done on it, and what you would do instead.
