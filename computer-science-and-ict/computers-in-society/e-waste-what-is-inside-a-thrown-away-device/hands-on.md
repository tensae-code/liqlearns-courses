# Hands-on — E-waste — what is inside a thrown-away device

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Dead-device census
Count every dead electronic item in your home and write its proper route: collection point, repair, safe disposal. The census sheet, signed by a family member, is the work.

## Ambassador survey
Walk your area with the guide's list — solar, wind, biogas — and record real examples with their owners' permission. Present the survey as the club's first act.
