# E-waste — what is inside a thrown-away device — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A thrown-away device disappears once it is buried.
**Answer: False** — its toxic materials leak into ground and water.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** E-waste management is only the manufacturer's responsibility.
**Answer: False** — it is the user's responsibility too.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide calls discarded electronics hazardous waste.
**Answer: True** — the guide's category (teacher guide — evidence).
