# E-waste — what is inside a thrown-away device — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three toxic materials and one place each can reach.
**Answer:** Cadmium, lead and mercury; they can leak into the ground and contaminate drinking water, food plants and animals.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the guide ask pupils to become for their environment?
**Answer:** Guardians and ambassadors — forming or joining a club or initiative for environmental protection and e-waste management (teacher guide — evidence).
