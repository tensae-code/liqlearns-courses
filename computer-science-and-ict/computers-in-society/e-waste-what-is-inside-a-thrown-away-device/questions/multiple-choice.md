# E-waste — what is inside a thrown-away device — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which toxic materials does the book name in devices?
a) cadmium, lead and mercury  b) gold and silver  c) plastic and glass only  d) iron and wood
**Answer: a) cadmium, lead and mercury** — the three named metals.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Leaked materials can contaminate —
a) only air  b) drinking water, food plants and animals  c) nothing  d) only roads
**Answer: b) drinking water, food plants and animals** — the named destinations.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The materials are a problem because they are —
a) too light  b) non-biodegradable, heavy-metal and toxic  c) too valuable  d) invisible
**Answer: b) non-biodegradable, heavy-metal and toxic** — the book's three properties.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** E-waste management includes —
a) burning everything  b) gathering systematically, recovering and recycling safely, disposing to minimise damage  c) burying deeper  d) exporting the problem
**Answer: b) gathering systematically, recovering and recycling safely, disposing to minimise damage** — the three-part answer.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide sends groups to survey green technologies such as —
a) coal and diesel  b) solar, wind and biogas  c) plastic and nylon  d) none exist
**Answer: b) solar, wind and biogas** — the named families (teacher guide — evidence).
