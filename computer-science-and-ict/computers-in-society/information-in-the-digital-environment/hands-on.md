# Hands-on — Information in the Digital Environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Paper against digital** — Take one message — a class announcement. Write how it would travel on paper, then how it travels digitally, and name one gain and one risk of the digital way.

## Task two
**Judge before you share** — Find one striking claim online. Run the four checks on it and write your verdict: share, check more, or do not share — with your reason.
