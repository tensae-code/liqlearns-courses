# Information in the Digital Environment — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Digital information — copies without loss, travels without delay
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Information held and shared as digital data — on computers, phones and the Internet; Exact — the thousandth copy is identical to the first, and copying costs almost nothing; Almost instantly, to anywhere in the world; A long time — a post or file can still be found years later; Because every judgement about digital information starts from how it behaves.

## Sub lesson 2 — The same properties cut both ways — judge before you trust
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Because digital information copies exactly and travels instantly, before anyone checks it; Because digital information lasts — it can be found years later; It can be edited or faked without leaving an obvious mark; Judge information before trusting it or sharing it; Who made it, whether it is current, whether another source agrees, and whether it appeals to emotion or to thought.
