# Information in the Digital Environment — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Information held and shared as digital data — on computers, phones and the Internet**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Information held and shared as digital data — on computers, phones and the Internet** — as the answer to: How good is a digital copy?
**Answer: False — Exact — the thousandth copy is identical to the first, and copying costs almost nothing**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Almost instantly, to anywhere in the world**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Almost instantly, to anywhere in the world** — as the answer to: How long does digital information last?
**Answer: False — A long time — a post or file can still be found years later**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Because every judgement about digital information starts from how it behaves**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Because every judgement about digital information starts from how it behaves** — as the answer to: Why can a false story spread so far?
**Answer: False — Because digital information copies exactly and travels instantly, before anyone checks it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Because digital information lasts — it can be found years later**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Because digital information lasts — it can be found years later** — as the answer to: What quiet change can digital information suffer?
**Answer: False — It can be edited or faked without leaving an obvious mark**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Judge information before trusting it or sharing it**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Judge information before trusting it or sharing it** — as the answer to: What four checks judge a piece of information?
**Answer: False — Who made it, whether it is current, whether another source agrees, and whether it appeals to emotion or to thought**
