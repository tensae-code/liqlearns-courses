# Information in the Digital Environment — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is information in the digital environment?** → **Information held and shared as digital data — on computers, phones and the Internet**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How good is a digital copy?** → **Exact — the thousandth copy is identical to the first, and copying costs almost nothing**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How fast does digital information travel?** → **Almost instantly, to anywhere in the world**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How long does digital information last?** → **A long time — a post or file can still be found years later**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book teach these properties first?** → **Because every judgement about digital information starts from how it behaves**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why can a false story spread so far?** → **Because digital information copies exactly and travels instantly, before anyone checks it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why can a careless post follow its writer?** → **Because digital information lasts — it can be found years later**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What quiet change can digital information suffer?** → **It can be edited or faked without leaving an obvious mark**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the digital citizen's habit?** → **Judge information before trusting it or sharing it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What four checks judge a piece of information?** → **Who made it, whether it is current, whether another source agrees, and whether it appeals to emotion or to thought**
