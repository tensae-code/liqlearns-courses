# Marketing Your Talents — an Introduction to Marketing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is this lesson an introduction to?**
**Answer: Marketing — through marketing your own talents**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does marketing begin, before any advertisement?**
**Answer: With a talent shaped into a product**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a product, in the lesson's sense?**
**Answer: A defined offer a person can choose — with a name, a form and a price or exchange in view**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do Kuwait's Grade 7 pupils market?**
**Answer: The designs the year has taught them to make**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why must the product be real first?**
**Answer: Marketing a thing that does not exist is only noise**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does an advertisement carry, and to whom?**
**Answer: The product, to the people it is for**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two things an advertisement says.**
**Answer: What the product is and what it does for them (also: why this maker's version)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where must the telling be placed?**
**Answer: In the places the right people actually look**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What rule keeps the telling honest?**
**Answer: An advertisement promises what the product keeps**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which of the year's skills give the product its face?**
**Answer: The poster and publication design lessons**
