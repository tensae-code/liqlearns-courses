# Hands-on — Mobile Phones in Schools

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Phone with a job', 'Choose one school task — revising vocabulary. Write how a phone does it better than paper alone, in three steps, and one rule that keeps the phone on the task.')

## Task two
('Reach map', 'Draw your school's reach: three people a phone connects in one school day (learner, teacher, parent) and the message each sends. Mark which message could not travel without the phone.')
