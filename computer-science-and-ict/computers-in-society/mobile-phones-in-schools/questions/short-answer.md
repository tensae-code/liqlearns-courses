# Mobile Phones in Schools — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is this lesson built from?**
**Answer: The additional content of Rwanda's S1 teacher guide**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name one learning use of a phone in school.**
**Answer: Looking up information beyond the textbook**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which built-in phone tools does the guide name?**
**Answer: The calculator and the dictionary**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What can a learner photograph to study later?**
**Answer: A diagram or a note**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why do phones matter to a school with few computers?**
**Answer: They are the largest computer room the school already has**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the second advantage the guide names?**
**Answer: Reach — contact between learners, teachers and parents**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name one message a school phone carries.**
**Answer: A timetable change**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How quickly can a parent be reached by phone?**
**Answer: The same day**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What travels home with the learner?**
**Answer: The lesson material, recorded or photographed**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the real advantage, in the lesson's words?**
**Answer: The connection the phone keeps open**
