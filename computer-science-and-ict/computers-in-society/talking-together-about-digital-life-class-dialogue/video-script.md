# Video script — Talking Together About Digital Life — Class Dialogue

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Digital life raises questions no exercise can settle alone: May I share this photo? Why did that video upset me? Who may see what I post? The Décodage guides give these questions a standing place — a **class dialogue** (discussion philosophique) where pupils bring real moments, listen to each other, and think together, with the teacher keeping the talk safe and fair. The point is not one right answer; it is learning to reason about digital life out loud, together.
A good dialogue has a shape: one question at a time; speak from your own experience; listen fully before answering; disagree with the idea, never attack the person; and let the question grow — a good talk ends with better questions than it started with. Image rights and shocking content return often in these talks, because they are the moments young pupils actually meet: a photo of someone else, a picture that frightens, a share that cannot be taken back.
