# Talking Together About Digital Life — Class Dialogue — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the class dialogue?**
**Answer: A philosophical class discussion of digital-life questions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name a question the dialogue takes up.**
**Answer: May I share this photo? (also: why did that video upset me?)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who keeps the talk safe and fair?**
**Answer: The teacher**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the point of the dialogue?**
**Answer: Learning to reason about digital life out loud, together — not one right answer**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Décodage place the dialogue?**
**Answer: As a standing Référence practice across the cycle**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How many questions at a time?**
**Answer: One**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How do you disagree in the dialogue?**
**Answer: With the idea, never attacking the person**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a good talk end with?**
**Answer: Better questions than it started with**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which two topics return often?**
**Answer: Image rights and shocking content**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What should you speak from?**
**Answer: Your own experience**
