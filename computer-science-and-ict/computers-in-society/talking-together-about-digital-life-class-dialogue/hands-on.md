# Hands-on — Talking Together About Digital Life — Class Dialogue

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Hold a ten-minute dialogue** — Choose one question — “May I share a photo of someone else?” Sit in a circle, one question only, teacher keeping turns. End by naming the best new question the talk produced.

## Task two
**Question box** — Keep a class box for digital-life questions that come up during the week. Open it in the next dialogue and choose one together.
