# Patents — an exclusive right for an invention — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A patent is —
a) a shop sign  b) an exclusive right granted for an invention  c) a secret recipe  d) a song's protection
**Answer: b) an exclusive right granted for an invention** — the definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In exchange for the exclusive right, the inventor —
a) hides everything  b) has the technical information published in the patent document  c) pays the public  d) gives up ownership
**Answer: b) has the technical information published in the patent document** — publication is the other side of the bargain.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** While a patent lasts, others may not —
a) read about it  b) make, use or offer the product for sale  c) invent other things  d) study science
**Answer: b) make, use or offer the product for sale** — the three barred acts.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Ethiopia, patent applications are processed —
a) only abroad  b) through the Intellectual Property Office, online at eipo.gov.et  c) at any police station  d) by email to a friend
**Answer: b) through the Intellectual Property Office, online at eipo.gov.et** — the named route.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's distinction: a trademark protects —
a) inventions  b) the words, symbols and logos identifying where goods come from  c) recipes  d) customer lists
**Answer: b) the words, symbols and logos identifying where goods come from** — source-identification is the trademark's work (teacher guide — evidence).
