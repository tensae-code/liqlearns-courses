# Patents — an exclusive right for an invention — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the patent bargain in one sentence.
**Answer:** The owner gets an exclusive right — deciding how or whether others use the invention — and in exchange the technical information is published in the patent document.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the Ethiopian office and its portal.
**Answer:** The Ethiopian Intellectual Property Office; applications can be processed online at eipo.gov.et.
