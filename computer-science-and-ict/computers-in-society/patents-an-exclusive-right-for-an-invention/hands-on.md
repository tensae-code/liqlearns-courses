# Hands-on — Patents — an exclusive right for an invention

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Bargain debate
In pairs: one argues the inventor's side (five years of work deserve exclusivity), one argues the public's side (knowledge should be free). The patent document is the compromise — end by stating what each side gets in it.

## Standards check
Take three software ideas. For each, write whether it could meet an authority's standards for a patent and why — the guide's 'Koket's software' exercise, done with your own cases.
