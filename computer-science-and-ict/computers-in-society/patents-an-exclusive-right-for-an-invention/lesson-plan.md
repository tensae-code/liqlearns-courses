# Lesson plan — Patents — an exclusive right for an invention

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** Patents — an exclusive right for an invention
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 14 — Computers in Society of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 12, Unit 1 — Information Systems and Their Applications. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a patent is an exclusive right granted for an invention — the owner decides how, or whether, others may use it, and in exchange the technical information is published; in Ethiopia applications go to the Ethiopian Intellectual Property Office, online at eipo.gov.et.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the intellectual-property lesson (recommended first, never forced) — the second of the four types, in the book's order.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The patent bargain** — exclusive right; owner decides use; technical information published in exchange; others may not make, use or offer for sale.
2. **Applying in Ethiopia — and the guide's distinction (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Ethiopian Intellectual Property Office and its portal; the guide names the Ethiopian Intellectual Property Authority and keys patent vs trademark.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the patent bargain, both sides.
2. Name what others may not do while a patent lasts.
3. Apply in Ethiopia: the office, the portal, and the guide's distinction.
