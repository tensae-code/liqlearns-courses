Tanzania's own way: a concept-first TIE book (2005 ICS syllabus) — a full chapter on data, information dissemination and communication media comes before any hardware, so dissemination is taught as part of what information does in society; the year closes with a practical "Computer handling" chapter (utilities, backup, security) rather than applications software. This lesson keeps that order.
