# Information Dissemination — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Information kept in one place, where nobody can reach it, is already disseminated.**
**Answer: False** — Dissemination is the travelling: the information must reach the many people who need it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Sending one message to one friend is dissemination.**
**Answer: False** — That is just sending; dissemination spreads one source's information to many receivers.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Mobile phones let receivers answer back at once.**
**Answer: True** — Two-way answering is one of the things the modern channels add.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The older ways — word of mouth, printed notices, letters — no longer work at all.**
**Answer: False** — They still work; they are simply slower, and each copy reaches only so far.
