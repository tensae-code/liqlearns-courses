# Information Dissemination — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Spreading information out so it reaches many people is called ____.**
**Answer: dissemination**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In dissemination, the way the information travels is called the ____.**
**Answer: channel**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Radio and television ____ one message to a whole country at the same moment.**
**Answer: broadcast**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A notice posted once on the ____ can be read anywhere in the world.**
**Answer: internet**
