# Information Dissemination — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A school prints its examination timetable but keeps every copy in the office. Has dissemination happened? Say why.**
**Answer:** No. The information never travelled from its source to its receivers — dissemination is the spreading itself, not the printing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name two modern ways of disseminating information and one strength of each.**
**Answer:** Any two of: radio and television — reach a whole country at the same moment; mobile phones and messaging — seconds-fast, and receivers answer back; the internet (websites, email, social media) — one posting readable worldwide, copyable and passable onward.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A storm warning must reach every village tonight. Which kind of channel do you choose first, and which of the three tests decides it?**
**Answer:** A broadcast channel (radio/television), or phone messaging where sets are few — the deciding test is speed joined to reach: fast and far, before tomorrow, matters more than answers coming back.
