# Information Dissemination — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Information dissemination means —**
a) Keeping information in one safe place  b) Spreading information out so it reaches many people  c) Deleting old information
**Answer: b) Spreading information out so it reaches many people** — One source, many receivers, on purpose.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The three parts of dissemination are —**
a) Source, channel, receivers  b) Paper, pen, ink  c) Sender, letter, stamp only
**Answer: a) Source, channel, receivers** — The one who sends, the way it travels, and the many people at the far end.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is dissemination?**
a) Telling one friend a secret  b) A health office telling a whole region the vaccination day  c) Writing a note to yourself
**Answer: b) A health office telling a whole region the vaccination day** — One source spreading to many receivers who need it.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Which channel reaches a whole country at the same moment, without the receivers asking first?**
a) A letter  b) Word of mouth  c) Radio and television broadcast
**Answer: c) Radio and television broadcast** — Broadcasting sends one message to everyone listening or watching at once.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) A notice posted on a website can —**
a) Be read in only one room  b) Be read anywhere in the world, copied and passed on  c) Never be copied
**Answer: b) Be read anywhere in the world, copied and passed on** — That reach is what the internet adds.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The three tests for choosing a channel are —**
a) Cheap, pretty, short  b) Fast, far, and whether receivers can answer back  c) Old, new, newest
**Answer: b) Fast, far, and whether receivers can answer back** — Judge the channel by the message's needs.
