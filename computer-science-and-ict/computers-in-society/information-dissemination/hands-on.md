# Hands-on — Information Dissemination

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Trace one announcement
Take one real announcement from your school or your street this week. Write down its source, its channel and its receivers. Count roughly how many people it reached, and guess how long it took. Then say: would a different channel have done the job better? Why?

## Spread it three ways
Take one short notice — for example, "Sports day moves to Friday." Write out how you would disseminate it three different ways: one broadcast way, one phone way and one internet way. For each one, write its speed, its reach, and whether the receivers can answer back.
