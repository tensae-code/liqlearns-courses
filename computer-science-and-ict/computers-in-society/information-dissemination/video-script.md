# Video script — Information Dissemination

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Information in one place helps one person.

Dissemination spreads it to many people.

One source. One channel. Many receivers.

Radio and television reach a whole country at once.

Phones carry messages in seconds — and answers come back.

The internet carries one notice around the world.

Fast. Far. With an answer.

That is dissemination today.
