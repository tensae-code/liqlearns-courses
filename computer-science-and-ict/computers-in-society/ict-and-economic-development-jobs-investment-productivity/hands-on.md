# Hands-on — ICT and Economic Development — Jobs, Investment, Productivity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Levers in your town** — List five local businesses. For each, name the ICT lever it already uses — jobs, sales reach, productivity — and one lever it has not pulled yet.

## Task two
**The honest ledger** — Draw the environment ledger for one service going digital: resources saved on one side, energy and devices spent on the other.
