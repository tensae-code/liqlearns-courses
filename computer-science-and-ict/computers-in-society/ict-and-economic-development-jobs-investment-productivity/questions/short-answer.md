# ICT and Economic Development — Jobs, Investment, Productivity — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the levers ICT pushes in this lesson.**
**Answer: Jobs, investment, productivity and communication**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two occupations that exist only because ICT does.**
**Answer: Programmers and network engineers (also support workers, freelancers)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does investment follow connections?**
**Answer: Companies build where communication is fast and reliable**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which Bangladeshi programmes pull the investment lever deliberately?**
**Answer: Hi-tech parks and Startup Bangladesh**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does every connected business employ people for?**
**Answer: Running its systems**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is productivity?**
**Answer: Output per worker**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give one productivity example.**
**Answer: A farmer with the market price before selling**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What shrinks as communication gets cheaper?**
**Answer: The cost of every agreement, order and delivery**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name the environment's credit side.**
**Answer: Paperless work and meetings replacing travel**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name the environment's debit side.**
**Answer: Energy spent by devices and data centres**
