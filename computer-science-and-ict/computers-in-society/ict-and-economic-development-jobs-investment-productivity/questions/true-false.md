# ICT and Economic Development — Jobs, Investment, Productivity — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Jobs, investment, productivity and communication**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Jobs, investment, productivity and communication** — as the answer to: Name two occupations that exist only because ICT does.
**Answer: False — Programmers and network engineers (also support workers, freelancers)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Companies build where communication is fast and reliable**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Companies build where communication is fast and reliable** — as the answer to: Which Bangladeshi programmes pull the investment lever deliberately?
**Answer: False — Hi-tech parks and Startup Bangladesh**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Running its systems**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Running its systems** — as the answer to: What is productivity?
**Answer: False — Output per worker**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A farmer with the market price before selling**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A farmer with the market price before selling** — as the answer to: What shrinks as communication gets cheaper?
**Answer: False — The cost of every agreement, order and delivery**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Paperless work and meetings replacing travel**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Paperless work and meetings replacing travel** — as the answer to: Name the environment's debit side.
**Answer: False — Energy spent by devices and data centres**
