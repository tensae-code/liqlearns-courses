# Unethical Use of Information and Technology — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does this lesson catalogue?" is: The unethical uses of information and computer technology**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name a misuse of information itself." is: Copying another's work and claiming it as your own**
**Answer: False** — Opening, reading or changing information that is not yours to touch

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is passing off another's copied work called in plain words?" is: Copying another's work and claiming it as your own**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Is spreading information you know is false unethical use?" is: Its owner never agreed to that purpose**
**Answer: False** — Yes — it is a misuse of information

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is wrong with using information for another purpose?" is: Its owner never agreed to that purpose**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name a misuse of the technology itself." is: Programs planted or passed on to damage or spy — a misuse of technology**
**Answer: False** — Getting into accounts, files or systems without permission

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What are harmful programs in this catalogue?" is: Programs planted or passed on to damage or spy — a misuse of technology**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name two more technology misuses." is: The person the information belongs to**
**Answer: False** — Using a computer to steal, cheat or harass; wasting or damaging shared machines and networks

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Who is harmed by misuse of information?" is: The person the information belongs to**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why does Fiji teach the catalogue by name?" is: The unethical uses of information and computer technology**
**Answer: False** — A learner who can name a misuse on sight has the first defence against doing it
