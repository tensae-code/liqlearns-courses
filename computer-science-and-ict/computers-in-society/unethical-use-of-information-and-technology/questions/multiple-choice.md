# Unethical Use of Information and Technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does this lesson catalogue?**
a) The unethical uses of information and computer technology  b) Opening, reading or changing information that is not yours to touch  c) Copying another's work and claiming it as your own  d) Yes — it is a misuse of information
**Answer: a) The unethical uses of information and computer technology**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name a misuse of information itself.**
a) Copying another's work and claiming it as your own  b) Opening, reading or changing information that is not yours to touch  c) Yes — it is a misuse of information  d) Its owner never agreed to that purpose
**Answer: b) Opening, reading or changing information that is not yours to touch**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is passing off another's copied work called in plain words?**
a) Yes — it is a misuse of information  b) Its owner never agreed to that purpose  c) Copying another's work and claiming it as your own  d) Getting into accounts, files or systems without permission
**Answer: c) Copying another's work and claiming it as your own**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is spreading information you know is false unethical use?**
a) Its owner never agreed to that purpose  b) Getting into accounts, files or systems without permission  c) Programs planted or passed on to damage or spy — a misuse of technology  d) Yes — it is a misuse of information
**Answer: d) Yes — it is a misuse of information**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is wrong with using information for another purpose?**
a) Its owner never agreed to that purpose  b) Getting into accounts, files or systems without permission  c) Programs planted or passed on to damage or spy — a misuse of technology  d) Using a computer to steal, cheat or harass; wasting or damaging shared machines and networks
**Answer: a) Its owner never agreed to that purpose**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name a misuse of the technology itself.**
a) Programs planted or passed on to damage or spy — a misuse of technology  b) Getting into accounts, files or systems without permission  c) Using a computer to steal, cheat or harass; wasting or damaging shared machines and networks  d) The person the information belongs to
**Answer: b) Getting into accounts, files or systems without permission**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are harmful programs in this catalogue?**
a) Using a computer to steal, cheat or harass; wasting or damaging shared machines and networks  b) The person the information belongs to  c) Programs planted or passed on to damage or spy — a misuse of technology  d) A learner who can name a misuse on sight has the first defence against doing it
**Answer: c) Programs planted or passed on to damage or spy — a misuse of technology**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two more technology misuses.**
a) The person the information belongs to  b) A learner who can name a misuse on sight has the first defence against doing it  c) The unethical uses of information and computer technology  d) Using a computer to steal, cheat or harass; wasting or damaging shared machines and networks
**Answer: d) Using a computer to steal, cheat or harass; wasting or damaging shared machines and networks**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who is harmed by misuse of information?**
a) The person the information belongs to  b) A learner who can name a misuse on sight has the first defence against doing it  c) The unethical uses of information and computer technology  d) Opening, reading or changing information that is not yours to touch
**Answer: a) The person the information belongs to**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Fiji teach the catalogue by name?**
