# Unethical Use of Information and Technology — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does this lesson catalogue?**
**Answer: The unethical uses of information and computer technology**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name a misuse of information itself.**
**Answer: Opening, reading or changing information that is not yours to touch**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is passing off another's copied work called in plain words?**
**Answer: Copying another's work and claiming it as your own**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is spreading information you know is false unethical use?**
**Answer: Yes — it is a misuse of information**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is wrong with using information for another purpose?**
**Answer: Its owner never agreed to that purpose**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name a misuse of the technology itself.**
**Answer: Getting into accounts, files or systems without permission**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are harmful programs in this catalogue?**
**Answer: Programs planted or passed on to damage or spy — a misuse of technology**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two more technology misuses.**
**Answer: Using a computer to steal, cheat or harass; wasting or damaging shared machines and networks**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who is harmed by misuse of information?**
**Answer: The person the information belongs to**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Fiji teach the catalogue by name?**
**Answer: A learner who can name a misuse on sight has the first defence against doing it**
