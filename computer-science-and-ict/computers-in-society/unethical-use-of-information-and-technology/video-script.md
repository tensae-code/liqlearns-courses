# Video script — Unethical Use of Information and Technology

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Unethical use begins with **information itself**: opening, reading or changing information that is **not yours to touch**; **copying** another's work, words or programs and passing them off as your own; **spreading information you know is false**; and using information gathered for one purpose for **another purpose its owner never agreed to**. Fiji's order matters — these are wrongs done with data, before any machine is damaged: the harm is to the person the information belongs to.
The second half of the catalogue is misuse of the **technology**: getting into accounts, files or systems **without permission**; planting or passing on **harmful programs**; using a computer to **steal, cheat or harass**; and using machines and networks in ways that **waste or damage** what a whole class or office shares. Fiji teaches the catalogue beside the ethics definition for a reason: a learner who can *name* a misuse when they see it — in a story, in a friend's plan, in their own temptation — has the first defence against doing it.
