# Data, information, knowledge, wisdom — the pyramid — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** At the base of the pyramid sits —
a) wisdom  b) knowledge  c) data  d) information
**Answer: c) data** — data is the wide base.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A digit string becomes information when —
a) it is printed  b) it is known to be a person's phone number  c) it is longer  d) it is memorised
**Answer: b) it is known to be a person's phone number** — context is what lifts data to information.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Knowledge, in the pyramid, is —
a) raw observations  b) understanding of patterns and rules drawn from information  c) any digit string  d) a chart
**Answer: b) understanding of patterns and rules drawn from information** — it guides future action.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** From data to wisdom, volume — (teacher guide — evidence)
a) grows  b) stays equal  c) shrinks from large to small  d) vanishes
**Answer: c) shrinks from large to small** — large at the base, small at the tip.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Wisdom's source is — (teacher guide — evidence)
a) observations  b) data alone  c) the application of knowledge  d) guessing
**Answer: c) the application of knowledge** — the chart's source row.
