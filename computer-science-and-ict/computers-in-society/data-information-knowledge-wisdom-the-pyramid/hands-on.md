# Hands-on — Data, information, knowledge, wisdom — the pyramid

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Climb one fact
On four stacked paper strips, write one school fact at each level — a mark (data), the class average (information), the pattern in your marks (knowledge), the study decision it should drive (wisdom). Pin the strips as a pyramid.

## Chart detective
Copy the five-row comparison chart. A partner names a layer; you read its five cells aloud. Swap until both can read any layer's row from memory (teacher guide — evidence).
