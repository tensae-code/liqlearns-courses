# Lesson plan — Facebook — profiles and staying connected; creating an account

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** Facebook — profiles and staying connected; creating an account
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 14 — Computers in Society of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 4 — Internet. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Facebook is a social network where users create profiles, upload photos and videos, send messages and stay connected — and an account is created by signing up with name, email or phone, password, birth date and gender, then confirming the email or number.
**This lesson's own level:** it is a doing-lesson standing on the social-networks lesson before it: the learner knows what a social network is; this lesson opens one account on one named platform, in the exact step order.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What Facebook is** — a popular social network website: create profiles, upload photos and videos, send messages, stay connected with friends, family and colleagues
2. **Creating a Facebook account — the steps in order** — go to facebook.com, click Create New Account, enter name, email or phone, password, birth date and gender, click Sign Up, confirm the email or number — then log in at the Log in pane

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Social networks — connecting in virtual communities. Recommended, never forced. A learner can land here directly; the lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what Facebook is and its four user actions: profiles, photos and videos, messages, staying connected.
2. Create an account by the five steps in their exact order.
3. Log in with email or phone number and password at the Log in pane.

## What you need
A computer or phone with internet for the real sign-up; paper and a pencil for rehearsal.

## If the learner is stuck
- Forgets to confirm → signing up is not finished until the email or mobile phone number is confirmed; an unconfirmed account is an unfinished account.
- Enters the password at sign-up but cannot log in → the Log in pane takes the same email or phone number and the same password you signed up with.
