# Facebook — profiles and staying connected; creating an account

## Do it first

On paper, design the front page of YOU: your name, one photo, a way for friends to write to you. That page has a name on the platform in this lesson — and a five-part road to creating it. Guess the road before you read it.

## Sub lesson 1 — What Facebook is

**Facebook** is a popular social network website. It lets users **create profiles, upload photos and videos, send messages, and stay connected with friends, family and colleagues**.

[IMAGE 11733 — a Facebook profile page]

## Sub lesson 2 — Creating a Facebook account — the steps in order

Create the account in this order — the steps are not shuffled or merged:

1. **Go to facebook.com and click Create New Account.**
2. **Enter your name, email or mobile phone number, password, date of birth and gender.**
3. **Click Sign Up.**
4. **Confirm the email or mobile phone number to finish creating the account.**
5. **Then go to facebook.com, enter the email or phone number and password, and click the Log in pane** — you are in.
