# Hands-on — Facebook — profiles and staying connected; creating an account

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Paper profile
Build your profile on paper first: name, a drawn photo space, three friends or family you would connect with, and one message you would send. Everything on the paper profile is something the real profile holds.

## Step-order cards
Write the five steps on cards, shuffle, rebuild the order, and have a partner check against the lesson — creating the account in the laboratory, in the real order, is how this skill is assessed.
