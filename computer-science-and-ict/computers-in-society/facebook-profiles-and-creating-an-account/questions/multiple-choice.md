# Facebook — profiles and staying connected; creating an account — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Facebook lets users —
a) only read news  b) create profiles, upload photos and videos, send messages and stay connected  c) only buy computers  d) write spreadsheets
**Answer: b) create profiles, upload photos and videos, send messages and stay connected**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Users stay connected on Facebook with —
a) friends, family and colleagues  b) only strangers  c) only customers  d) nobody
**Answer: a) friends, family and colleagues**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The first step in creating an account is —
a) click Sign Up  b) go to facebook.com and click Create New Account  c) confirm the email  d) click Log in
**Answer: b) go to facebook.com and click Create New Account**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** At sign-up you enter —
a) name, email or mobile phone number, password, date of birth and gender  b) only a username  c) your school marks  d) a bank account
**Answer: a) name, email or mobile phone number, password, date of birth and gender**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** To finish creating the account you must —
a) upload ten photos  b) confirm the email or mobile phone number  c) send a message  d) log out
**Answer: b) confirm the email or mobile phone number**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** At the Log in pane you enter —
a) a new password  b) the email or phone number and the password, then click Log in  c) your birth date only  d) a friend's name
**Answer: b) the email or phone number and the password, then click Log in**
