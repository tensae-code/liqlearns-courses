# Facebook — profiles and staying connected; creating an account — ordering

Put the steps in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Put the steps in order: click Sign Up — click Create New Account — confirm the email or phone number — enter name, email or phone, password, birth date and gender.
**Answer: click Create New Account → enter name, email or phone, password, birth date and gender → click Sign Up → confirm the email or phone number**
