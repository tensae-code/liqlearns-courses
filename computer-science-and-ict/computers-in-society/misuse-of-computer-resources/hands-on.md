# Hands-on — Misuse of Computer Resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 5 §5.9 Misuse of Computer Resources (unauthorized access, improper use, illegal use, interfering with others, altering system files) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach misuse of computer resources to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
