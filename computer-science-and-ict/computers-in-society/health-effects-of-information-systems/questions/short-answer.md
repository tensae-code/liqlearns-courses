# Health effects of information systems — benefits and harms — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four harms of unmanaged use.
**Answer:** Any four of: sleeping disorders, lost productivity, loss of attention, stress, musculoskeletal pain, vision problems and eye strain, headaches, obesity from long sitting.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Walk the guide's health chain in order.
**Answer:** Excessive internet use → high psychological arousal → lack of sleep, long fasting and limited physical activity → depression, poor family connections and anxiety (teacher guide — evidence).
