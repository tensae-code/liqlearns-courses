# Health effects of information systems — benefits and harms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Health information systems generate —
a) rumours  b) high-quality data quickly for planning, review and monitoring  c) only bills  d) appointments only
**Answer: b) high-quality data quickly for planning, review and monitoring** — the systems' gift.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mobile access can —
a) close clinics  b) improve care, empower patients with personalised messages and optimise resources  c) replace all doctors  d) raise prices
**Answer: b) improve care, empower patients with personalised messages and optimise resources** — the three mobile gains.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Planning, review and _______ are what the fast data serves.
a) monitoring  b) advertising  c) gaming  d) printing
**Answer: a) monitoring** — the third use.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a harm of unmanaged use?
a) sleeping disorders  b) better posture  c) sharper eyes  d) more exercise
**Answer: a) sleeping disorders** — head of the harm list.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Musculoskeletal pain from device use can strike —
a) only fingers  b) back, neck, chest, arms, shoulders and feet  c) only toes  d) nowhere
**Answer: b) back, neck, chest, arms, shoulders and feet** — the book's full map of it.
