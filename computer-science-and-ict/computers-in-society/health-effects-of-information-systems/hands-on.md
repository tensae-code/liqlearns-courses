# Hands-on — Health effects of information systems — benefits and harms

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Body audit
Keep a three-day end-of-day audit: eyes, neck, back, sleep. On day three, match each sore item to its habit. The audit makes the harm list personal and checkable.

## Proper-use charter
Write your charter of proper use — posture, breaks, distance, last screen — and post it where you sit. A charter kept beats a symptom list memorised.
