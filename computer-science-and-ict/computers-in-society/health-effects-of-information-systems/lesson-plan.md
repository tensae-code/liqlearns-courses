# Lesson plan — Health effects of information systems — benefits and harms

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** Health effects of information systems — benefits and harms
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 14 — Computers in Society of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 12, Unit 1 — Information Systems and Their Applications. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** health information systems produce high-quality data fast and mobile access can improve care — while unmanaged use brings sleep disorders, lost productivity, stress, musculoskeletal pain, vision problems, headaches and obesity; the guide spells out the addiction chain.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the environment lessons just before it (recommended first, never forced) and needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What health information systems give** — high-quality data quickly for planning, review, monitoring; mobile access improving care, empowering patients, optimising resources.
2. **What unmanaged use costs — and the guide's chain (teacher guide — evidence)** — sleep disorders, lost productivity, lost attention, stress; musculoskeletal pain, vision problems, headaches, obesity; the guide's arousal chain and wider list.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the lesson named in this plan's level line, inside this same title. Recommended, never forced. A learner can land here directly; the lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name what health information systems give.
2. List the harms of unmanaged use.
3. Walk the guide's chain from excessive use to depression and anxiety.
