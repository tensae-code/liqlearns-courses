# Copyright — automatic protection for original works — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Copyright protects —
a) inventions  b) original works of authorship  c) shop signs only  d) land
**Answer: b) original works of authorship** — expression, not invention.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which are named copyright works?
a) literary works, music and art  b) bridges and roads  c) recipes only  d) laws
**Answer: a) literary works, music and art** — the classic three.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Nowadays copyright also reaches —
a) computer software and architecture  b) weather  c) traffic  d) minerals
**Answer: a) computer software and architecture** — the modern additions; GUIs can be registered too.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Copyright protection begins —
a) after paying a fee  b) automatically on creation  c) after ten years  d) only in court
**Answer: b) automatically on creation** — no application stands in the way.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Registration is needed before —
a) creating a work  b) suing over an infringement  c) reading a book  d) singing a song
**Answer: b) suing over an infringement** — the courtroom condition.
