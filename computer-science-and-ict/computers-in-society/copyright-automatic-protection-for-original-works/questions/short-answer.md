# Copyright — automatic protection for original works — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does copyright differ from a patent in what it protects?
**Answer:** Copyright protects original works of authorship — literary works, music, art, software, architecture — while a patent protects inventions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the two halves of the protection rule.
**Answer:** Protection is automatic on creation; registration is needed before suing over an infringement.
