# Copyright — automatic protection for original works

## Do it first

The moment you finish a song, a story, a program — before you file anything, pay anything, tell anyone — the law already counts it as yours. This lesson is about that instant protection, and the one step it does not include.

## Sub lesson 1 — What copyright protects — and how it differs from a patent

**Copyright is different from a patent: it protects original works of authorship — literary works, music and art**.

Nowadays it reaches further: **computer software and architecture**, and **graphical user interfaces can be registered too**. The work's form is expression — the written, composed, drawn or coded thing itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — Automatic — but registration before suing

**Protection is automatic on creation** — no application, no waiting, no fee stands between finishing a work and owning its copyright.

But the courtroom has a door of its own: **registration is needed before suing over an infringement**. Automatic to own; registered to enforce in court. Both halves matter.
