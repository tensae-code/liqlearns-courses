# Hands-on — Copyright — automatic protection for original works

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Creation stamp
Finish one small work this week — a verse, a drawing, a tiny program. Write under it: the date of creation and 'copyright mine from this moment'. Then write the extra step you would take before ever suing over it.

## Spot the shield
From magazines, packages and screens at home, collect six marks and works. Sort: which shield protects each — and for the copyright ones, say when protection began.
