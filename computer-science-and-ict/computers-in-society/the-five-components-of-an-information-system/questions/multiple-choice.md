# The five components of an information system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list gives the five components?
a) hardware, software, data/information, people, procedure  b) screens, cables, mice, keyboards, printers  c) teachers, pupils, parents, guards, cooks  d) input, output, storage, processing, deleting
**Answer: a) hardware, software, data/information, people, procedure** — the book's five.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Data/information is called the organisation's —
a) cheapest part  b) critical asset  c) oldest machine  d) extra copy
**Answer: b) critical asset** — captured from customers and suppliers, stored in a database.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Procedure says —
a) how fast the network is  b) what data is captured, who may see it and who may change it  c) which colours to print  d) when school closes
**Answer: b) what data is captured, who may see it and who may change it** — the step-by-step policies.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is information the school generates, in the guide's case? (teacher guide — evidence)
a) a pupil's address  b) pupil-to-classroom ratios  c) a teacher's name  d) an exam mark
**Answer: b) pupil-to-classroom ratios** — ratios are generated from captured data.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A pupil-to-classroom ratio above the Ministry standard drives the decision to — (teacher guide — evidence)
a) close the school  b) build more classrooms  c) send pupils home  d) buy computers
**Answer: b) build more classrooms** — the guide's own decision example.
