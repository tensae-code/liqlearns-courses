# Hands-on — The five components of an information system

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Component census
Walk your school (or picture it) and census the five components: list the hardware you can touch, the software you can name, the data captured this week, the people who run it, and one procedure in force. Five lists, one system.

## Ratio decision
Count pupils and classrooms in one grade. Work the pupil-to-classroom ratio. Write the decision the guide's example would drive if the ratio beat the standard (teacher guide — evidence).
