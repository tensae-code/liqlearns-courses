# Video script — The five components of an information system

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. (Full script — the placement names a full script for this lesson.)

---

This is our school — as an information system.

At the gate, data flows in: names, ages, marks, attendance.

Inside, the five components work: hardware, software, data, people, procedure.

Out come reports: teachers by qualification. Pupils per classroom. Absence summaries.

Now watch one register. A pupil is absent, again and again.

The summary grows. The school sees it — and acts: a visit home, support arranged.

Data in. Information out. A decision made. That is the system, end to end.
