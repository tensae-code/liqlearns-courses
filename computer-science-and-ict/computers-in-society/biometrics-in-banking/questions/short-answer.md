# Biometrics in Banking — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is biometrics?**
**Answer: Identifying a person by a measurable part of their body or behaviour**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name one biometric a bank can use.**
**Answer: A fingerprint**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When does the bank record the biometric?**
**Answer: Once, at the start**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens at each later visit?**
**Answer: The live sample is compared with the record**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is checked in biometrics, in the lesson's words?**
**Answer: Something you are, not something you carry or remember**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why do banks add biometrics?**
**Answer: Because a biometric is hard to lend, steal or forget**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name one fraud biometrics fights.**
**Answer: The lent card (or the watched PIN, or the forged signature)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does one touch prove at the counter?**
**Answer: The customer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Beside what is biometrics taught in Rwanda's unit?**
**Answer: Mobile banking's risks (teacher guide — evidence)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does biometrics replace the hunt for?**
**Answer: Documents proving identity**
