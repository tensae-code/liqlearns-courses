# Biometrics in Banking — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is biometrics?**
a) Identifying a person by a measurable part of their body or behaviour  b) A fingerprint  c) Once, at the start  d) The live sample is compared with the record
**Answer: a) Identifying a person by a measurable part of their body or behaviour**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name one biometric a bank can use.**
a) Once, at the start  b) A fingerprint  c) The live sample is compared with the record  d) Something you are, not something you carry or remember
**Answer: b) A fingerprint**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When does the bank record the biometric?**
a) The live sample is compared with the record  b) Something you are, not something you carry or remember  c) Once, at the start  d) Identifying a person by a measurable part of their body or behaviour
**Answer: c) Once, at the start**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens at each later visit?**
a) Something you are, not something you carry or remember  b) Identifying a person by a measurable part of their body or behaviour  c) A fingerprint  d) The live sample is compared with the record
**Answer: d) The live sample is compared with the record**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is checked in biometrics, in the lesson's words?**
a) Something you are, not something you carry or remember  b) Identifying a person by a measurable part of their body or behaviour  c) A fingerprint  d) Once, at the start
