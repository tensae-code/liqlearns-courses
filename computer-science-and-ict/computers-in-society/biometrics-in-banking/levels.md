# Biometrics in Banking — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What biometrics is
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Identifying a person by a measurable part of their body or behaviour; A fingerprint; Once, at the start; The live sample is compared with the record; Something you are, not something you carry or remember.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why banks add it
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Because a biometric is hard to lend, steal or forget; The lent card (or the watched PIN, or the forged signature); The customer; Mobile banking's risks (teacher guide — evidence); Documents proving identity.
