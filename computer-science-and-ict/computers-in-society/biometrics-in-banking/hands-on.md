# Hands-on — Biometrics in Banking

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Three keys compared', 'Make a three-row table: card, PIN, fingerprint. For each write: can it be lent? can it be forgotten? can it be stolen? Use the table to say why a bank might ask for two together.')

## Task two
('Enrol a finger', 'Write the enrolment steps for a bank fingerprint system in order — record, store, compare, match — as instructions for a new bank clerk, one line per step.')
