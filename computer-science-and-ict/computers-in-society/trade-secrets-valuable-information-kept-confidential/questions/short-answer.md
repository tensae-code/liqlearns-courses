# Trade secrets — valuable information kept confidential — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the book's trade-secret examples.
**Answer:** Customer lists, source code, food and drink recipes (Pepsi, Coca-Cola, Sprite), and software processes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What three acts violate a trade secret?
**Answer:** Taking, using or disclosing it, contrary to honest commercial practice.
