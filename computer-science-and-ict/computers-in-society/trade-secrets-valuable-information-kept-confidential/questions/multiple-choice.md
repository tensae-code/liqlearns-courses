# Trade secrets — valuable information kept confidential — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A trade secret is IP in —
a) published inventions  b) confidential information that may be sold or licensed  c) shop signs  d) songs
**Answer: b) confidential information that may be sold or licensed** — the definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a named trade-secret example?
a) customer lists  b) a flag  c) a road sign  d) a school bell
**Answer: a) customer lists** — with source code, recipes and software processes.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Pepsi, Coca-Cola and Sprite illustrate —
a) patents  b) food and drink recipes as trade secrets  c) trademarks only  d) copyright
**Answer: b) food and drink recipes as trade secrets** — the book's drink examples.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A violation is taking, using or disclosing a trade secret —
a) with permission  b) contrary to honest commercial practice  c) after fifty years  d) in a library
**Answer: b) contrary to honest commercial practice** — the standard named.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Such an act is called —
a) fair play  b) an unfair practice and a violation  c) advertising  d) research
**Answer: b) an unfair practice and a violation** — the book's verdict.
