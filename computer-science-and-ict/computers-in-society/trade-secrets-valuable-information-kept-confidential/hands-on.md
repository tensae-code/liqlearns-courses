# Hands-on — Trade secrets — valuable information kept confidential

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Secret or not?
Sort ten business assets on cards: secret, or not a trade secret? The recipe, the shop's public menu, the customer list, the advertised price, the source code. Defend each sort by one test: is it confidential, and valuable because it is?

## Guard plan
For an invented bakery, write the guard plan for its recipe: who knows it, where it is written, what a new worker signs. Secrecy is a practice, not a wish.
