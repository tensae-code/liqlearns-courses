# Running an Information Verification Campaign — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a campaign, in this lesson?**
**Answer: Organised persuasion for a good habit**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which claims does the campaign choose?**
**Answer: The ones its community actually forwards**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How are the checks divided?**
**Answer: Among members — different eyes for source, impartiality and agreement**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does each public verdict state?**
**Answer: What was claimed, what the checks found, and what is true**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why verify in the open?**
**Answer: To show verification as something ordinary people do**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the after-campaign review report?**
**Answer: What was done, which claims were settled, and what the community answered**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What else does the review ask?**
**Answer: What the team would run differently**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the campaign's real product?**
**Answer: A standing checking habit in the community**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does a checker stay on duty after the campaign?**
**Answer: In every group chat the class belongs to**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does an unreviewed campaign teach?**
**Answer: Only half its lesson**
