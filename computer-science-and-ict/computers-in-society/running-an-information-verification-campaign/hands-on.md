# Hands-on — Running an Information Verification Campaign

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Plan the campaign** — In a team, list three claims your community forwards, assign the three checks per claim to named members, and set the public verdict format.

## Task two
**Write the review** — After any class verification activity, write the after-action review: done, settled, answered, and one thing to run differently next time.
