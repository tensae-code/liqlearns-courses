# Hands-on — Information Technology Laws and Regulations — the UU ITE

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Article or not?** — Write six short online acts (invented, no real names). Decide for each: does it break Article 27, 28 or 29, or is it lawful — and give your reason from the article's words.

## Task two
**Teach the law** — Prepare a one-page notice for your school's computer room explaining the UU ITE in plain words, with the three articles and one example each.
