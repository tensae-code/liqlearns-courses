# Information Technology Laws and Regulations — the UU ITE — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the UU ITE?** → **Indonesia's Electronic Information and Transactions Law — the statute for electronic information and transactions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why does such a law exist?** → **When a country's life moves online, the law must follow it there and make electronic acts legally real**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which grades teach it?** → **Grade 11 (the regulation chapter) and Grade 12 (inside social impact)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does Article 27 forbid?** → **Distributing content that violates decency, gambles, insults or defames, or extorts**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What else stands in Article 27's family?** → **Threats used in extortion — taking by menace**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Article 28 forbid?** → **Spreading false news that harms consumers, and content meant to raise hatred or hostility against groups**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is berita bohong?** → **False news — literally 'lying news'**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does Article 29 forbid?** → **Sending threats of violence or menace aimed at a person**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What pattern stands under the articles?** → **The law forbids acts, not the network — the same acts ethics forbids, with the state's penalties behind them**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must a citizen online know?** → **The law of the place they are standing in — online, they are standing in it**
