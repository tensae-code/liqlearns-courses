# Information Technology Laws and Regulations — the UU ITE — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Indonesia's Electronic Information and Transactions Law — the statute for electronic information and transactions**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Indonesia's Electronic Information and Transactions Law — the statute for electronic information and transactions** — as the answer to: Why does such a law exist?
**Answer: False — When a country's life moves online, the law must follow it there and make electronic acts legally real**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Grade 11 (the regulation chapter) and Grade 12 (inside social impact)**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Grade 11 (the regulation chapter) and Grade 12 (inside social impact)** — as the answer to: What does Article 27 forbid?
**Answer: False — Distributing content that violates decency, gambles, insults or defames, or extorts**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Threats used in extortion — taking by menace**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Threats used in extortion — taking by menace** — as the answer to: What does Article 28 forbid?
**Answer: False — Spreading false news that harms consumers, and content meant to raise hatred or hostility against groups**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: False news — literally 'lying news'**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: False news — literally 'lying news'** — as the answer to: What does Article 29 forbid?
**Answer: False — Sending threats of violence or menace aimed at a person**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The law forbids acts, not the network — the same acts ethics forbids, with the state's penalties behind them**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The law forbids acts, not the network — the same acts ethics forbids, with the state's penalties behind them** — as the answer to: What must a citizen online know?
**Answer: False — The law of the place they are standing in — online, they are standing in it**
