# Information Technology Laws and Regulations — the UU ITE — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the UU ITE?**
**Answer: Indonesia's Electronic Information and Transactions Law — the statute for electronic information and transactions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why does such a law exist?**
**Answer: When a country's life moves online, the law must follow it there and make electronic acts legally real**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which grades teach it?**
**Answer: Grade 11 (the regulation chapter) and Grade 12 (inside social impact)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does Article 27 forbid?**
**Answer: Distributing content that violates decency, gambles, insults or defames, or extorts**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What else stands in Article 27's family?**
**Answer: Threats used in extortion — taking by menace**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Article 28 forbid?**
**Answer: Spreading false news that harms consumers, and content meant to raise hatred or hostility against groups**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is berita bohong?**
**Answer: False news — literally 'lying news'**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does Article 29 forbid?**
**Answer: Sending threats of violence or menace aimed at a person**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What pattern stands under the articles?**
**Answer: The law forbids acts, not the network — the same acts ethics forbids, with the state's penalties behind them**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must a citizen online know?**
**Answer: The law of the place they are standing in — online, they are standing in it**
