# Hands-on — Pakistani Digital Entrepreneurs and Their Startups

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 7, Examples of digital/traditional entrepreneurship (Investors Lounge, Zero Makeup, PayPal, Foodpanda and EatOye, Shopsy.pk) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach pakistani digital entrepreneurs and their startups to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
