# Creating Content for Social Media — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The maker's forms — video, photo, writing
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Choosing the form that fits the message — video, photo, blog or microblog; A phone screen — and first seconds that work even with the sound off; One clear subject, chosen and framed on purpose; The blog develops an idea; the microblog must land in a few lines; The writing that makes a post work — first line, promised value, and a close that says what to do next.

## Sub lesson 2 — Copywriting — words that earn their place
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Stop the thumb — honestly; Content made for a known audience, true to what it shows, posted where its form belongs — the opposite of noise; Credit for others' work, and care with other people's images (also: honesty about what is advertisement); Grade 12, Bab 5 A.6 — creative use of social media; The value the first line promised.
