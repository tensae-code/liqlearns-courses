# Creating Content for Social Media

## Do it first

Creating content for social media — making video, photo and written posts (blog and microblogging, with copywriting) that are made on purpose for the platform's people

## Sub lesson 1 — The maker's forms — video, photo, writing

Social media content is made, not spilled. Indonesia's Grade 12 book teaches creative use of social media through its forms: **video** (short, made for sound-off first seconds and a phone screen), **photo** (one clear subject, chosen and framed on purpose), and **writing** — the **blog** post that develops an idea and the **microblog** post that must land in a few lines. Each form has its own craft, and the maker's first skill is choosing the form that fits the message instead of forcing every message into one.

In your mind's eye: the maker's forms — video, photo, writing.

## Sub lesson 2 — Copywriting — words that earn their place

**Copywriting** is the writing that makes a post work: a first line that stops the thumb honestly, a body that gives the value it promised, and a close that tells the reader what to do next. Creative use, in the book's sense, is the opposite of noise: content made for a known audience, true to what it shows, and posted where its form belongs. The maker also owns the duties of the platform — credit for others' work, care with other people's images, and honesty about what is advertisement and what is not.

In your mind's eye: copywriting — words that earn their place.
