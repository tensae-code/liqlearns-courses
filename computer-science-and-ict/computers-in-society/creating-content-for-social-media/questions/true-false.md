# Creating Content for Social Media — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Choosing the form that fits the message — video, photo, blog or microblog**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Choosing the form that fits the message — video, photo, blog or microblog** — as the answer to: What is video on social media made for?
**Answer: False — A phone screen — and first seconds that work even with the sound off**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: One clear subject, chosen and framed on purpose**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: One clear subject, chosen and framed on purpose** — as the answer to: How do a blog post and a microblog post differ?
**Answer: False — The blog develops an idea; the microblog must land in a few lines**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The writing that makes a post work — first line, promised value, and a close that says what to do next**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The writing that makes a post work — first line, promised value, and a close that says what to do next** — as the answer to: What must the first line of a post do?
**Answer: False — Stop the thumb — honestly**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Content made for a known audience, true to what it shows, posted where its form belongs — the opposite of noise**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Content made for a known audience, true to what it shows, posted where its form belongs — the opposite of noise** — as the answer to: Name two duties the maker owns on the platform.
**Answer: False — Credit for others' work, and care with other people's images (also: honesty about what is advertisement)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Grade 12, Bab 5 A.6 — creative use of social media**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Grade 12, Bab 5 A.6 — creative use of social media** — as the answer to: What does the body of a post owe the reader?
**Answer: False — The value the first line promised**
