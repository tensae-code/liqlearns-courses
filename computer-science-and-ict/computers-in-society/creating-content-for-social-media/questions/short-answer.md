# Creating Content for Social Media — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the maker's first skill?**
**Answer: Choosing the form that fits the message — video, photo, blog or microblog**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is video on social media made for?**
**Answer: A phone screen — and first seconds that work even with the sound off**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a photo post work?**
**Answer: One clear subject, chosen and framed on purpose**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How do a blog post and a microblog post differ?**
**Answer: The blog develops an idea; the microblog must land in a few lines**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is copywriting?**
**Answer: The writing that makes a post work — first line, promised value, and a close that says what to do next**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What must the first line of a post do?**
**Answer: Stop the thumb — honestly**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is creative use, in the book's sense?**
**Answer: Content made for a known audience, true to what it shows, posted where its form belongs — the opposite of noise**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two duties the maker owns on the platform.**
**Answer: Credit for others' work, and care with other people's images (also: honesty about what is advertisement)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 12, Bab 5 A.6 — creative use of social media**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the body of a post owe the reader?**
**Answer: The value the first line promised**
