# Creating Content for Social Media — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the maker's first skill?** → **Choosing the form that fits the message — video, photo, blog or microblog**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is video on social media made for?** → **A phone screen — and first seconds that work even with the sound off**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a photo post work?** → **One clear subject, chosen and framed on purpose**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How do a blog post and a microblog post differ?** → **The blog develops an idea; the microblog must land in a few lines**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is copywriting?** → **The writing that makes a post work — first line, promised value, and a close that says what to do next**
