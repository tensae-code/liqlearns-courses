# Hands-on — Creating Content for Social Media

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**One message, three forms** — Take one true message about your school or street. Draft it as a photo post, a microblog post and a short video plan — then say which form fits it best and why.

## Task two
**Rewrite the copy** — Find a weak post (your own or a public one). Rewrite its first line and its close using the copywriting pattern, and note what changed.
