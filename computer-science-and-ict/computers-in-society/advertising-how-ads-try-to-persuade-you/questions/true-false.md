# Advertising — How Ads Try to Persuade You — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Persuade — make you want, choose or remember**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Persuade — make you want, choose or remember** — as the answer to: Who pays for an advertisement?
**Answer: False — Someone who wants to change what you do**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: So it sticks in memory**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: So it sticks in memory** — as the answer to: How is the product usually shown?
**Answer: False — Larger and brighter than life**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Recognising its intention**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Recognising its intention** — as the answer to: Name the five tools.
**Answer: False — Promise, belonging, repetition, image over words, hiding the ad**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Repeats the message until it feels like your own idea**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Repeats the message until it feels like your own idea** — as the answer to: Where can an ad hide?
**Answer: False — Inside a game, a video, or a favourite person's recommendation**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A feeling pictured**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A feeling pictured** — as the answer to: What does naming a tool do?
**Answer: False — It returns the choice to you**
