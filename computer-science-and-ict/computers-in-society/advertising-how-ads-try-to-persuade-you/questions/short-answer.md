# Advertising — How Ads Try to Persuade You — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an advertisement made to do?**
**Answer: Persuade — make you want, choose or remember**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who pays for an advertisement?**
**Answer: Someone who wants to change what you do**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is a slogan short?**
**Answer: So it sticks in memory**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is the product usually shown?**
**Answer: Larger and brighter than life**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does recognising an ad start with?**
**Answer: Recognising its intention**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the five tools.**
**Answer: Promise, belonging, repetition, image over words, hiding the ad**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does repetition do?**
**Answer: Repeats the message until it feels like your own idea**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where can an ad hide?**
**Answer: Inside a game, a video, or a favourite person's recommendation**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What beats a fact stated, in ads?**
**Answer: A feeling pictured**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does naming a tool do?**
**Answer: It returns the choice to you**
