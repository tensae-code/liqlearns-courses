# Hands-on — Advertising — How Ads Try to Persuade You

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Dissect an ad** — Take one real advertisement. Label its promise, its belonging trick, its slogan, its pictured feeling. Present the dissection to the class.

## Task two
**Spot the hidden ad** — Hunt one video, game or post that carries a hidden advertisement. Show the class the exact moment the ad begins.
