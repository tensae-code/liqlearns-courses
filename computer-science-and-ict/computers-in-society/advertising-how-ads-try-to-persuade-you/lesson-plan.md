## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What an advertisement is for** — An **advertisement (une publicité)** is a message made to persuade: to make you want, choose or remember a product, a place, a behaviour. It is not information that happens to be pretty — every part is chosen for effect: the smiling people, the music, the slogan short enough to stick, the product shown larger and brighter than life. Recognising an ad means first recognising its **intention**: someone paid for this message to change what I do.
2. **The persuader's tools** — The parcours names the tools: **promise** (this will make you happy, strong, loved), **belonging** (everyone like you has one), **repetition** (the same message until it feels like your own idea), **image over words** (a feeling pictured beats a fact stated), and **hiding the ad** (inside a game, a video, a favourite person's recommendation). Naming a tool while it is used on you does not ruin the ad — it returns the choice to you.

2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
