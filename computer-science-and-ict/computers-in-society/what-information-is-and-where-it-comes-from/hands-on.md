# Hands-on — What Information Is — and Where It Comes From

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Trace it to the source** — Take three items from any feed. For each, dig until you can name the true source — person, organisation or instrument. If you cannot, label it sourceless.

## Task two
**Information or not?** — Sort ten statements: information / opinion / decoration / noise. For each information, write how it could in principle be checked.
