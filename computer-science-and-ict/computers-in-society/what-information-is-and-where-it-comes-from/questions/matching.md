# What Information Is — and Where It Comes From — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does information do?** → **It reduces your uncertainty about the world**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three forms information takes.** → **A fact, an event, a measurement**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must information be, in principle?** → **Checkable — able to be verified**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is everything on a screen information?** → **No — decoration, opinion and noise travel the same wires**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When does a fact inform twice?** → **Never — the same fact told twice informs once**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a source?** → **The person, organisation, instrument or document information comes from**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a measurement's source?** → **The instrument and who read it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is a report's source?** → **The reporter and who sent them**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does “it says online” name?** → **No source at all**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) State the fiche's rule.** → **No source, no trust**
