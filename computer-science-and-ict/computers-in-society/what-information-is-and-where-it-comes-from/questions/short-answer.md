# What Information Is — and Where It Comes From — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does information do?**
**Answer: It reduces your uncertainty about the world**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three forms information takes.**
**Answer: A fact, an event, a measurement**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must information be, in principle?**
**Answer: Checkable — able to be verified**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is everything on a screen information?**
**Answer: No — decoration, opinion and noise travel the same wires**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When does a fact inform twice?**
**Answer: Never — the same fact told twice informs once**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a source?**
**Answer: The person, organisation, instrument or document information comes from**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a measurement's source?**
**Answer: The instrument and who read it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is a report's source?**
**Answer: The reporter and who sent them**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does “it says online” name?**
**Answer: No source at all**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) State the fiche's rule.**
**Answer: No source, no trust**
