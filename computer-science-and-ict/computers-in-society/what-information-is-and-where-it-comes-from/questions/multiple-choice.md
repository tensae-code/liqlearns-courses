# What Information Is — and Where It Comes From — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does information do?**
a) It reduces your uncertainty about the world  b) A fact, an event, a measurement  c) Checkable — able to be verified  d) No — decoration, opinion and noise travel the same wires
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three forms information takes.**
a) A fact, an event, a measurement  b) Checkable — able to be verified  c) No — decoration, opinion and noise travel the same wires  d) Never — the same fact told twice informs once
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must information be, in principle?**
a) Checkable — able to be verified  b) No — decoration, opinion and noise travel the same wires  c) Never — the same fact told twice informs once  d) The person, organisation, instrument or document information comes from
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is everything on a screen information?**
