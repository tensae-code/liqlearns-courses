# Hands-on — How Online Media Treat Information Differently

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Same event, two front pages** — Open two online news front pages covering the same event. Compare order, headlines, updates. Where do they differ — and what steered each?

## Task two
**Follow the corrections** — Track one developing story for a day, noting each version's time. What changed between versions? What did the first version get wrong?
