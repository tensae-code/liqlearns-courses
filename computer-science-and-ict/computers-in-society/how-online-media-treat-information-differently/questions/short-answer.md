# How Online Media Treat Information Differently — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How do online media correct?**
**Answer: In place — the story itself is changed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the online reader often meet?**
**Answer: Unfinished stories — speed bought with certainty**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do links do?**
**Answer: Carry the reader outward to sources and sideways to related pieces**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What joins the text online?**
**Answer: Multimedia — video, sound, live data**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the fixed edition become online?**
**Answer: A flowing stream**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Who chooses what the online reader sees?**
**Answer: Both — the reader through links and search, and the medium through rankings and feeds**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What shapes a feed's order?**
**Answer: What the reader clicked before**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do comments and shares make readers?**
**Answer: Distributors of the story**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the scenario's double vision?**
**Answer: Read the story, and read the machinery that brought it to you**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name two steering tools of online media.**
**Answer: Rankings and recommendations (also: feeds)**
