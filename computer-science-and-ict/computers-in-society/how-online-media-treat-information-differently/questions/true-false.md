# How Online Media Treat Information Differently — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: In place — the story itself is changed**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: In place — the story itself is changed** — as the answer to: What does the online reader often meet?
**Answer: False — Unfinished stories — speed bought with certainty**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Carry the reader outward to sources and sideways to related pieces**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Carry the reader outward to sources and sideways to related pieces** — as the answer to: What joins the text online?
**Answer: False — Multimedia — video, sound, live data**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A flowing stream**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A flowing stream** — as the answer to: Who chooses what the online reader sees?
**Answer: False — Both — the reader through links and search, and the medium through rankings and feeds**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: What the reader clicked before**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: What the reader clicked before** — as the answer to: What do comments and shares make readers?
**Answer: False — Distributors of the story**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Read the story, and read the machinery that brought it to you**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Read the story, and read the machinery that brought it to you** — as the answer to: Name two steering tools of online media.
**Answer: False — Rankings and recommendations (also: feeds)**
