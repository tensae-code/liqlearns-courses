# Evaluating Data and Its Sources — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is validation of data?**
**Answer: Testing whether data is accurate, complete and fit to use, before analysis stands on it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the four plain tests of data?**
**Answer: Accurate, complete, current and consistent**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does accurate mean here?**
**Answer: The data matches the real thing it counts or describes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does completeness matter?**
**Answer: The missing rows may be the story**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why can old data mislead?**
**Answer: The world it describes has changed — it misleads politely**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to data that fails validation?**
**Answer: It is repaired, replaced or refused — not argued with**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why must it not be argued with?**
**Answer: Every later step multiplies its faults**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What four questions evaluate a source?**
**Answer: Who collected it, by what method, for what purpose, and with what care**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the source part of the data's meaning?**
**Answer: The same-looking figure from a careful count and from an interested party is not the same data**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 10, Bab 1 D — evaluation of data and its sources**
