# Hands-on — Evaluating Data and Its Sources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Validate a dataset** — Take a small dataset you can reach (a class list, a price list, a results table). Run the four tests on it and write what you would repair, replace or refuse.

## Task two
**Two sources, one figure** — Find one figure published by two different sources. Compare who collected it and how, and write which you would use and why.
