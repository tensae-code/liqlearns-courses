# Social networks — connecting in virtual communities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Social networks are —
a) means of interaction among people who create, share and exchange information and ideas in virtual communities  b) a kind of computer cable  c) only video games  d) printed newspapers
**Answer: a) means of interaction among people who create, share and exchange information and ideas in virtual communities**

**2.** Social networking uses internet-based sites to stay connected with —
a) only strangers  b) friends, family, colleagues, customers or clients  c) nobody  d) only teachers
**Answer: b) friends, family, colleagues, customers or clients**

**3.** Social networking can serve —
a) a social purpose only  b) a business purpose only  c) social, business and educational purposes  d) no purpose
**Answer: c) social, business and educational purposes**

**4.** Which of these is a named social network platform?
a) Telegram  b) a spreadsheet  c) a printer  d) FTP
**Answer: a) Telegram**

**5.** A virtual community is —
a) a real village square  b) a community people form and meet in online  c) a computer shop  d) a password
**Answer: b) a community people form and meet in online**
