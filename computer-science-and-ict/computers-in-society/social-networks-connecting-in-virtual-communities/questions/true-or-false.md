# Social networks — connecting in virtual communities — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** People on social networks only receive information; they never create or share it.
**Answer: False — they create, share and exchange information and ideas.**

**2.** Skype, YouTube and Instagram are among the named platforms.
**Answer: True — all three are named.**

**3.** A social network used improperly carries risks.
**Answer: True — the warning is given with the definition, and the risks lesson takes it up.**
