# Hands-on — Social networks — connecting in virtual communities

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Map your communities
Draw yourself in the middle of a page and your virtual communities around you — family group, class group, friends. Draw a line for each and label what is created, shared or exchanged along it.

## Purpose sort
Take the seven named platforms on seven cards. A partner names a job — 'tell my family in another town', 'learn a skill', 'reach customers' — and you hold up every platform that fits, saying social, business or educational for each.
