# Lesson plan — Social networks — connecting in virtual communities

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** Social networks — connecting in virtual communities
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 14 — Computers in Society of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 4 — Internet. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** social networks are means of interaction among people who create, share and exchange information and ideas in virtual communities — used to stay connected with friends, family, colleagues, customers and clients, for social, business and educational purposes.
**This lesson's own level:** it opens the Computers in Society title for this band: it stands on the learner's own everyday experience of messaging and groups online, and on nothing else in this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define a social network and a virtual community.
2. Say who social networking keeps you connected with.
3. Name the three purposes: social, business, educational — and the seven named platforms.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a social network is one app → the apps are platforms; the network is the people interacting — creating, sharing and exchanging — through them.
- Confuses purpose with platform → ask what the connection is FOR: friendship (social), work and customers (business), learning (educational).
