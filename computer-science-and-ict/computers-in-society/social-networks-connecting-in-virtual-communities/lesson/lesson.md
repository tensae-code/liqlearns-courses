# Social networks — connecting in virtual communities

## Do it first

Think of the last time you exchanged a message, a photo or an idea with someone you were not standing next to. Where did that exchange live? Who else could see it? You were inside this lesson's idea. Now give it its name.

## What a social network is

**Social networks** are **means of interaction among people** in which they **create, share and exchange information and ideas in virtual communities and networks**. **Social networking** uses internet-based sites to **stay connected with friends, family, colleagues, customers or clients**. It can serve a **social purpose**, a **business purpose** and an **educational purpose**.

## The named platforms — and a warning held for later

The platforms named are **Facebook, Telegram, Skype, YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn and Twitter**. Hold one warning in mind from the start: a social network **used improperly carries risks** — the risks have their own lesson at the end of this set, after the benefits.

## Tip — from us, not from the books

**Tip — from us, not from the books:** when you join any new platform, spend your first visit just looking: find where messages live, where your profile lives, and who can see what you post — before you post anything yourself.
