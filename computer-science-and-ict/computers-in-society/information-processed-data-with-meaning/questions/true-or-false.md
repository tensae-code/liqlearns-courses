# From data to information — processed data with meaning — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Information can be shown as a table, a line graph, a bar graph or a formula.
**Answer: True** — all four forms are named in the book.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Loose daily sales figures on their own show a clear trend.
**Answer: False** — grouped by week they show rising or falling; alone they say little.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Davenport & Prusak definition, knowledge lives only in minds, never in organisational routines (teacher guide — evidence).
**Answer: False** — it is embedded in routines and norms as well as in minds.
