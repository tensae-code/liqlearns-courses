# From data to information — processed data with meaning — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A week's grouped sales rise from 200 to 260. What information does that give that the loose daily figures did not?
**Answer: That sales are rising — a trend the loose figures did not show.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the Davenport & Prusak definition of knowledge in your own short form (teacher guide — evidence).
**Answer: Experience, values, context, expert insight and intuition mixed together, held in minds and in organisational routines and norms.**
