# Hands-on — From data to information — processed data with meaning

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Group it yourself
Count something for five days at home. Write the five bare numbers. Then group them, total them, average them — the ladder's first rungs — and write the one sentence of information they now give you.

## Two ways to show it
Turn your grouped numbers into a table, then into a bar graph on paper. Show both to someone and ask which tells the story faster. That is why information's form matters.
