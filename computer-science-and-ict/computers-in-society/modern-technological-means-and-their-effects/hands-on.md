# Hands-on — Modern Technological Means and Their Effects

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Inventory a day** — List the modern means you used before noon; name each by the doing it enabled, not the gadget.

## Task two
**Weigh one means** — Take one means and write its effects in four boxes: individual gain, individual harm, society gain, society harm — with evidence.
