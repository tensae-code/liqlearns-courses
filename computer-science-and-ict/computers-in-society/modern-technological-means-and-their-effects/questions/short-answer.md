# Modern Technological Means and Their Effects — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four families of means.**
**Answer: Communication, information, work and money tools, and smart devices**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) By what is a means named?**
**Answer: What it lets a person do that could not be done, or not so easily, before**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why name by doing, not by gadget?**
**Answer: So the list stays honest when the gadgets change**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Give a money-tool example.**
**Answer: Payments without cash**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is an information tool?**
**Answer: Search, media and learning tools**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name four individual effects.**
**Answer: Time and attention reshaped, skills gained and rusted, health helped and harmed, privacy thinned**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name four society effects.**
**Answer: Work and shopping moved, communities formed and fractured, information fast and false, the connection gap**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the connection gap become?**
**Answer: A gap in everything else**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the weighing two-handed?**
**Answer: A pupil who can only praise or only blame has not learned to weigh**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the next lesson take alone?**
**Answer: The risks**
