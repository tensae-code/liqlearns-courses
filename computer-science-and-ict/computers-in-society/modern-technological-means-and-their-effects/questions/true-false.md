# Modern Technological Means and Their Effects — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Communication, information, work and money tools, and smart devices**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Communication, information, work and money tools, and smart devices** — as the answer to: By what is a means named?
**Answer: False — What it lets a person do that could not be done, or not so easily, before**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: So the list stays honest when the gadgets change**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: So the list stays honest when the gadgets change** — as the answer to: Give a money-tool example.
**Answer: False — Payments without cash**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Search, media and learning tools**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Search, media and learning tools** — as the answer to: Name four individual effects.
**Answer: False — Time and attention reshaped, skills gained and rusted, health helped and harmed, privacy thinned**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Work and shopping moved, communities formed and fractured, information fast and false, the connection gap**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Work and shopping moved, communities formed and fractured, information fast and false, the connection gap** — as the answer to: What does the connection gap become?
**Answer: False — A gap in everything else**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A pupil who can only praise or only blame has not learned to weigh**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A pupil who can only praise or only blame has not learned to weigh** — as the answer to: What does the next lesson take alone?
**Answer: False — The risks**
