Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which institution closes Sudan's tour of computers at work?**
a) The police  b) Who a person is and what documents they hold  c) The national number  d) The computer's records

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why hold the records on computers?**
