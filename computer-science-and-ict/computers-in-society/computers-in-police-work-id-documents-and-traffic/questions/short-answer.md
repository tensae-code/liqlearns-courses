# Computers in Police Work — ID Documents and Traffic — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which institution closes Sudan's tour of computers at work?**
**Answer: The police**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What records do police institutions keep?**
**Answer: Who a person is and what documents they hold**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why hold the records on computers?**
**Answer: So they can be searched in seconds and shared between offices**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What identifies every citizen in the book's account?**
**Answer: The national number**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three ID documents computers issue.**
**Answer: The national number, the passport and the national card**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where is each document checked against?**
**Answer: The computer's records**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What road work do computers serve?**
**Answer: Traffic control — vehicle and licence records and watching traffic**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is a document, in this lesson's pattern?**
**Answer: The printout of a record the computer keeps**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which other institutions did the unit visit first?**
**Answer: Banks and schools**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How long did a paper record take to find, against the computer's seconds?**
**Answer: A day**
