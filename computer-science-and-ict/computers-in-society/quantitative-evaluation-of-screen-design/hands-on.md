# Hands-on — Quantitative Evaluation of Screen Design

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Read a report', 'A prototype trial reports: Task 1 success 9/10, mean 40s (aim 60s); Task 2 success 4/10, mean 2:40 (aim 60s); satisfaction 4.1/5. Write the discussion: what passed, what failed, what is repaired first, and one limit of these numbers.')

## Task two
('Design the counting', 'Plan a quantitative trial for your screen prototype: name the users, two tasks with aims, the four counts you will take, and the questionnaire line you will use for satisfaction.')
