# Quantitative Evaluation of Screen Design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a quantitative evaluation measure with?**
a) Counts — numbers  b) Task completion (success rate), time taken, errors made, satisfaction scores  c) By questionnaire — such as an average out of 5  d) Its task and its users named
**Answer: a) Counts — numbers**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four counted things.**
a) By questionnaire — such as an average out of 5  b) Task completion (success rate), time taken, errors made, satisfaction scores  c) Its task and its users named  d) A number about nothing
**Answer: b) Task completion (success rate), time taken, errors made, satisfaction scores**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How are satisfaction scores gathered?**
a) Its task and its users named  b) A number about nothing  c) By questionnaire — such as an average out of 5  d) The design's aim for that task
**Answer: c) By questionnaire — such as an average out of 5**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must every reported number carry?**
a) A number about nothing  b) The design's aim for that task  c) Failure — however pleasant the screens felt  d) Its task and its users named
**Answer: d) Its task and its users named**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) A success rate with no task named is what?**
a) A number about nothing  b) The design's aim for that task  c) Failure — however pleasant the screens felt  d) Lowest success first — repaired first
**Answer: a) A number about nothing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Against what is each number set in discussion?**
a) Failure — however pleasant the screens felt  b) The design's aim for that task  c) Lowest success first — repaired first  d) Indications, not proofs
**Answer: b) The design's aim for that task**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) A 2:40 average against an under-a-minute aim means what?**
a) Lowest success first — repaired first  b) Indications, not proofs  c) Failure — however pleasant the screens felt  d) Decisions — which screens change, in what order, and what is measured again
**Answer: c) Failure — however pleasant the screens felt**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How are failures ranked?**
a) Indications, not proofs  b) Decisions — which screens change, in what order, and what is measured again  c) Counts — numbers  d) Lowest success first — repaired first
**Answer: d) Lowest success first — repaired first**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are a small trial's figures?**
a) Indications, not proofs  b) Decisions — which screens change, in what order, and what is measured again  c) Counts — numbers  d) Task completion (success rate), time taken, errors made, satisfaction scores
**Answer: a) Indications, not proofs**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the evaluation end in?**
a) Counts — numbers  b) Decisions — which screens change, in what order, and what is measured again  c) Task completion (success rate), time taken, errors made, satisfaction scores  d) By questionnaire — such as an average out of 5
**Answer: b) Decisions — which screens change, in what order, and what is measured again**
