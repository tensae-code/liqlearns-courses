# Quantitative Evaluation of Screen Design — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a quantitative evaluation measure with?**
**Answer: Counts — numbers**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four counted things.**
**Answer: Task completion (success rate), time taken, errors made, satisfaction scores**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How are satisfaction scores gathered?**
**Answer: By questionnaire — such as an average out of 5**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must every reported number carry?**
**Answer: Its task and its users named**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) A success rate with no task named is what?**
**Answer: A number about nothing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Against what is each number set in discussion?**
**Answer: The design's aim for that task**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) A 2:40 average against an under-a-minute aim means what?**
**Answer: Failure — however pleasant the screens felt**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How are failures ranked?**
**Answer: Lowest success first — repaired first**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are a small trial's figures?**
**Answer: Indications, not proofs**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the evaluation end in?**
**Answer: Decisions — which screens change, in what order, and what is measured again**
