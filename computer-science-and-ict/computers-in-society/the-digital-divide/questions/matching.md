# The Digital Divide — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the digital divide?** → **The gap between people who can use digital technology and people who cannot**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the divide's first level?** → **Access — a device, a connection, electricity, and prices a household can pay**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two lines the divide runs along.** → **Between countries, and between city and village (also between rich and poor households, and inside one classroom)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Armenia teach the digital divide?** → **Grade 10, Theme 6 — inside the senior book's society sequence**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does electricity belong in the access list?** → **Without power there is no device and no connection — access fails before it starts**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the divide's second level?** → **Skills and use — searching, judging and creating, versus only consuming**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the third level?** → **Outcomes — who turns access into education, work and income**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Give one closing measure for the access level.** → **Public access points and affordable connections**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Give one closing measure for the skills level.** → **Digital-skills teaching for all ages**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How should services be designed to not widen the divide?** → **For low-end devices and slow connections, so nobody is locked out by the shape of their phone**
