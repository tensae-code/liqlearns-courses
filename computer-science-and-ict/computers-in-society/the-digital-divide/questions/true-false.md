# The Digital Divide — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The gap between people who can use digital technology and people who cannot**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The gap between people who can use digital technology and people who cannot** — as the answer to: What is the divide's first level?
**Answer: False — Access — a device, a connection, electricity, and prices a household can pay**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Between countries, and between city and village (also between rich and poor households, and inside one classroom)**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Between countries, and between city and village (also between rich and poor households, and inside one classroom)** — as the answer to: Where does Armenia teach the digital divide?
**Answer: False — Grade 10, Theme 6 — inside the senior book's society sequence**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Without power there is no device and no connection — access fails before it starts**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Without power there is no device and no connection — access fails before it starts** — as the answer to: What is the divide's second level?
**Answer: False — Skills and use — searching, judging and creating, versus only consuming**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Outcomes — who turns access into education, work and income**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Outcomes — who turns access into education, work and income** — as the answer to: Give one closing measure for the access level.
**Answer: False — Public access points and affordable connections**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Digital-skills teaching for all ages**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Digital-skills teaching for all ages** — as the answer to: How should services be designed to not widen the divide?
**Answer: False — For low-end devices and slow connections, so nobody is locked out by the shape of their phone**
