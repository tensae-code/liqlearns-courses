# Hands-on — The Digital Divide

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Map the divide nearby** — For your own school area, list the divide's three levels with one local example of each (a place without connection, a skills gap, an outcome gap).

## Task two
**Design for the far side** — Take an online service you use. Rewrite its first screen's requirements so it works on a cheap phone with a slow connection. List what you changed and what you refused to change.
