# Video script — The Digital Divide

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The **digital divide** is the gap between people who can use digital technology and people who cannot. Its first level is **access**: a device, a connection, electricity, and prices a household can pay. The divide runs between countries, between city and village, between rich and poor households — and inside one classroom, between the learner with a quiet connected home and the learner without. Armenia's senior-school book gives the divide its own lesson (Grade 10, Theme 6) inside its society sequence.
Access alone does not close the divide. The second level is **skills and use**: two people with the same connection differ if one can search, judge and create while the other can only consume. The third level is **outcomes**: who turns access into education, work and income. Closing the gap works at all three levels — public access points and affordable connections, digital-skills teaching for all ages, and services designed for low-end devices and slow connections, so nobody is locked out by the shape of their phone.
