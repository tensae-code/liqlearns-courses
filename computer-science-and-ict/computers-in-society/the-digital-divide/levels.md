# The Digital Divide — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The divide — access first
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The gap between people who can use digital technology and people who cannot; Access — a device, a connection, electricity, and prices a household can pay; Between countries, and between city and village (also between rich and poor households, and inside one classroom); Grade 10, Theme 6 — inside the senior book's society sequence; Without power there is no device and no connection — access fails before it starts.

## Sub lesson 2 — The deeper levels — skills, use and outcomes; closing the gap
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Skills and use — searching, judging and creating, versus only consuming; Outcomes — who turns access into education, work and income; Public access points and affordable connections; Digital-skills teaching for all ages; For low-end devices and slow connections, so nobody is locked out by the shape of their phone.
