# The Modern Office — Remote Work and Paperless Files — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: An office whose documents are born digital — written, filed and found by search**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: An office whose documents are born digital — written, filed and found by search** — as the answer to: Name two gifts of digital files.
**Answer: False — They can be in two places at once and are found by search (also copied without fading)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: One drive is one point of failure**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: One drive is one point of failure** — as the answer to: What office work does automation remove first?
**Answer: False — The slowest, most repetitive work — filing, copying, totalling**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: By search, not by drawer**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: By search, not by drawer** — as the answer to: What is remote work?
**Answer: False — Office work done from anywhere, not from one shared room**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Its members span time zones and hand work on**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Its members span time zones and hand work on** — as the answer to: Which jobs does office automation cost?
**Answer: False — Jobs made of copying, filing and totalling**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Judgement and communication (also care of the systems)**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Judgement and communication (also care of the systems)** — as the answer to: Does automation end office work?
**Answer: False — No — it changes which work people do**
