# The Modern Office — Remote Work and Paperless Files — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the paperless office?**
**Answer: An office whose documents are born digital — written, filed and found by search**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two gifts of digital files.**
**Answer: They can be in two places at once and are found by search (also copied without fading)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does a digital archive still need a backup?**
**Answer: One drive is one point of failure**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What office work does automation remove first?**
**Answer: The slowest, most repetitive work — filing, copying, totalling**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a digital file found?**
**Answer: By search, not by drawer**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is remote work?**
**Answer: Office work done from anywhere, not from one shared room**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How can a remote team work around the clock?**
**Answer: Its members span time zones and hand work on**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which jobs does office automation cost?**
**Answer: Jobs made of copying, filing and totalling**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name two skills the modern office asks for instead.**
**Answer: Judgement and communication (also care of the systems)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Does automation end office work?**
**Answer: No — it changes which work people do**
