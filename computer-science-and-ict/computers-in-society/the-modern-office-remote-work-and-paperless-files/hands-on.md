# Hands-on — The Modern Office — Remote Work and Paperless Files

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Drawer against search** — Time two hunts for the same fact: once in paper (a book's index) and once by digital search. Record both times and what made the difference.

## Task two
**The job that changed** — Interview or imagine one office worker. List which of their tasks a computer now does, and which tasks it cannot take. Present both lists.
