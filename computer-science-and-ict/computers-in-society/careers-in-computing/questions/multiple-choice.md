# Careers in Computing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does computing work first divide?**
a) Into those who build systems and those who run them  b) Writes the software  c) Studies an organisation's needs and designs the system that meets them  d) Keeps the networks working
**Answer: a) Into those who build systems and those who run them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a programmer do?**
a) Studies an organisation's needs and designs the system that meets them  b) Writes the software  c) Keeps the networks working  d) Guards the data
**Answer: b) Writes the software**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a systems analyst do?**
a) Keeps the networks working  b) Guards the data  c) Studies an organisation's needs and designs the system that meets them  d) Turns the world's paper into the system's records
**Answer: c) Studies an organisation's needs and designs the system that meets them**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a network administrator do?**
a) Guards the data  b) Turns the world's paper into the system's records  c) Make new users capable  d) Keeps the networks working
**Answer: d) Keeps the networks working**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a database administrator do?**
a) Guards the data  b) Turns the world's paper into the system's records  c) Make new users capable  d) Plans and answers for the whole system
**Answer: a) Guards the data**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a data entry operator do?**
a) Make new users capable  b) Turns the world's paper into the system's records  c) Plans and answers for the whole system  d) Installs, maintains and repairs the machines
**Answer: b) Turns the world's paper into the system's records**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do IT teachers and trainers do?**
a) Plans and answers for the whole system  b) Installs, maintains and repairs the machines  c) Make new users capable  d) By the subjects and skills of this course, deepened in the career's direction
**Answer: c) Make new users capable**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a systems manager do?**
a) Installs, maintains and repairs the machines  b) By the subjects and skills of this course, deepened in the career's direction  c) Into those who build systems and those who run them  d) Plans and answers for the whole system
**Answer: d) Plans and answers for the whole system**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does a technician do?**
a) Installs, maintains and repairs the machines  b) By the subjects and skills of this course, deepened in the career's direction  c) Into those who build systems and those who run them  d) Writes the software
**Answer: a) Installs, maintains and repairs the machines**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is each career entered?**
a) Into those who build systems and those who run them  b) By the subjects and skills of this course, deepened in the career's direction  c) Writes the software  d) Studies an organisation's needs and designs the system that meets them
**Answer: b) By the subjects and skills of this course, deepened in the career's direction**
