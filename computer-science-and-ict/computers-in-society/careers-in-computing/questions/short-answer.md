# Careers in Computing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does computing work first divide?**
**Answer: Into those who build systems and those who run them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a programmer do?**
**Answer: Writes the software**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a systems analyst do?**
**Answer: Studies an organisation's needs and designs the system that meets them**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a network administrator do?**
**Answer: Keeps the networks working**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a database administrator do?**
**Answer: Guards the data**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a data entry operator do?**
**Answer: Turns the world's paper into the system's records**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do IT teachers and trainers do?**
**Answer: Make new users capable**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a systems manager do?**
**Answer: Plans and answers for the whole system**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does a technician do?**
**Answer: Installs, maintains and repairs the machines**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is each career entered?**
**Answer: By the subjects and skills of this course, deepened in the career's direction**
