# Video script — Careers in Computing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Computing work divides first into those who **build** systems and those who **run** them. The builders: the **programmer**, who writes the software; the **systems analyst**, who studies an organisation's needs and designs the system that meets them; the **web designer/developer**, who builds the web's pages and sites. The runners: the **network administrator**, who keeps the networks working; the **database administrator**, who guards the data; and the **technician**, who installs, maintains and repairs the machines themselves.
A second family keeps people and systems together: the **data entry operator**, who turns the world's paper into the system's records; the **IT teacher and trainer**, who make new users capable; and the **systems manager**, who plans and answers for the whole. Fiji's careers lesson ends where it should — with the road: each career is entered by the subjects and skills this very course teaches, deepened in the direction the career needs — code for the programmer, organisations for the analyst, machines for the technician.
