# Video script — Fair Use, Public Domain and Creative Commons Licences

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Fair use** (the book's lawful-use exceptions) lets a protected work be used **without permission for limited honest purposes** — quotation in study and criticism, classroom use, news — judged by how much is taken, why, and whether the owner's market is harmed. The **public domain** holds works **no longer protected** — their term ended, or they were never protected (laws, facts) — which **anyone may use wholly and freely**. The two differ in kind: fair use borrows a living work carefully; the public domain owns the dead work's freedom completely.
A **Creative Commons licence** is a creator's **standing permission, written in advance**: the work stays the creator's, but the licence states what all may do with it — the family of conditions the licences lessons teach (credit the creator — BY; no commercial use — NC; no changed versions — ND; share under the same licence — SA) combined into the licence the work carries. Greece's unit teaches all three doors in one section because a citizen meets them in one act: meeting a work online and asking, rightly, 'what may I do with this?' — and the answer is one of wall, door one, door two, or door three.
