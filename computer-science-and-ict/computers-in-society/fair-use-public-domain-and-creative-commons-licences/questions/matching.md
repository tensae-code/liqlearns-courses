# Fair Use, Public Domain and Creative Commons Licences — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is fair use?** → **Using a protected work without permission for limited honest purposes, judged by amount, purpose and harm to the owner's market**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name an honest purpose.** → **Quotation in study or criticism (or classroom use, or news)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the public domain?** → **Works no longer protected — anyone may use them wholly and freely**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does a work enter it?** → **Its term ended, or it was never protected (laws, facts)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How do the two differ?** → **Fair use borrows a living work carefully; the public domain is complete freedom**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a Creative Commons licence?** → **The creator's standing permission, written in advance, stating what all may do with the work**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the condition letters.** → **BY credit, NC no commercial use, ND no derivatives, SA share-alike**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Does the work stop being the creator's?** → **No — the licence opens use, not ownership**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) The citizen's one question is what?** → **What may I do with this work?**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) The four answers are what?** → **The wall (ask), fair use, public domain, Creative Commons**
