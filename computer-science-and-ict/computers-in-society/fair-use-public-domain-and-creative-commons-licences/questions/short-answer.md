# Fair Use, Public Domain and Creative Commons Licences — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is fair use?**
**Answer: Using a protected work without permission for limited honest purposes, judged by amount, purpose and harm to the owner's market**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name an honest purpose.**
**Answer: Quotation in study or criticism (or classroom use, or news)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the public domain?**
**Answer: Works no longer protected — anyone may use them wholly and freely**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does a work enter it?**
**Answer: Its term ended, or it was never protected (laws, facts)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How do the two differ?**
**Answer: Fair use borrows a living work carefully; the public domain is complete freedom**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a Creative Commons licence?**
**Answer: The creator's standing permission, written in advance, stating what all may do with the work**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the condition letters.**
**Answer: BY credit, NC no commercial use, ND no derivatives, SA share-alike**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Does the work stop being the creator's?**
**Answer: No — the licence opens use, not ownership**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) The citizen's one question is what?**
**Answer: What may I do with this work?**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) The four answers are what?**
**Answer: The wall (ask), fair use, public domain, Creative Commons**
