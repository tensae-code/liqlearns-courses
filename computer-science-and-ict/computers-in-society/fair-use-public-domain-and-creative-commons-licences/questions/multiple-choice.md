# Fair Use, Public Domain and Creative Commons Licences — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is fair use?**
a) Using a protected work without permission for limited honest purposes, judged by amount, purpose and harm to the owner's market  b) Quotation in study or criticism (or classroom use, or news)  c) Works no longer protected — anyone may use them wholly and freely  d) Its term ended, or it was never protected (laws, facts)
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name an honest purpose.**
a) Works no longer protected — anyone may use them wholly and freely  b) Quotation in study or criticism (or classroom use, or news)  c) Its term ended, or it was never protected (laws, facts)  d) Fair use borrows a living work carefully; the public domain is complete freedom
**Answer: b**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the public domain?**
a) Its term ended, or it was never protected (laws, facts)  b) Fair use borrows a living work carefully; the public domain is complete freedom  c) Works no longer protected — anyone may use them wholly and freely  d) The creator's standing permission, written in advance, stating what all may do with the work
**Answer: c**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does a work enter it?**
a) Fair use borrows a living work carefully; the public domain is complete freedom  b) The creator's standing permission, written in advance, stating what all may do with the work  c) BY credit, NC no commercial use, ND no derivatives, SA share-alike  d) Its term ended, or it was never protected (laws, facts)
**Answer: d**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How do the two differ?**
a) Fair use borrows a living work carefully; the public domain is complete freedom  b) The creator's standing permission, written in advance, stating what all may do with the work  c) BY credit, NC no commercial use, ND no derivatives, SA share-alike  d) No — the licence opens use, not ownership
