# Hands-on — Fair Use, Public Domain and Creative Commons Licences

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Judge the use', 'Take five printed uses of found works and judge each: wall, fair use, public domain or CC — with the reason.')

## Task two
('Licence your work', 'Choose a CC licence for a photo you took, naming its letters, and write the notice that travels with it.')
