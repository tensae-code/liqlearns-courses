# Hands-on — Digital Devices That Protect Us — Alarms and Locks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Guardians at home** — Find the protective devices in your home — alarm, lock, smoke detector — and for each say what it watches or what proof it asks for.

## Task two
**Right proof game** — Make a paper lock with a secret three-number code; a partner tries wrong codes and then the right one — say why only one opens it.
