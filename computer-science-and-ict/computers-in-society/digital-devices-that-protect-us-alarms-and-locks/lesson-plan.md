## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Alarms — devices that watch and warn** — An **alarm** is a digital device that **watches and warns**. A car alarm feels a door opened without its key and answers with sound and light; a home alarm watches doors and windows and calls the owners. The alarm does not sleep: watching is its whole work, day and night, and it acts the moment its rule is broken — a door opened, a window forced, smoke smelled by a smoke alarm. Protection by alarm is protection by **a rule watched without tiring**.
2. **Locks — devices that open only for their owner** — A **digital lock** keeps a thing closed until the right person comes. The car's lock opens for its own key or code and for no other; a bicycle lock with a code opens only for the one who knows the numbers; a home's digital lock may ask for a code, a card or a fingerprint. The lock's rule is the opposite of the alarm's: the alarm watches for the **wrong event**, the lock waits for the **right proof** — and together they guard the things a family owns.
