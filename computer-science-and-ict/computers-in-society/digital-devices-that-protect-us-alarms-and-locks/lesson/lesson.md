# Digital Devices That Protect Us — Alarms and Locks

## Do it first

Not every digital device entertains or washes. Moldova's Grade 1 book gives a whole lesson to the guardians: the devices that stand watch over cars, bicycles and homes, and call out when something is wrong.

## Sub lesson 1 — Alarms — devices that watch and warn

An **alarm** is a digital device that **watches and warns**. A car alarm feels a door opened without its key and answers with sound and light; a home alarm watches doors and windows and calls the owners. The alarm does not sleep: watching is its whole work, day and night, and it acts the moment its rule is broken — a door opened, a window forced, smoke smelled by a smoke alarm. Protection by alarm is protection by **a rule watched without tiring**.

In your mind's eye: alarms — devices that watch and warn.

## Sub lesson 2 — Locks — devices that open only for their owner

A **digital lock** keeps a thing closed until the right person comes. The car's lock opens for its own key or code and for no other; a bicycle lock with a code opens only for the one who knows the numbers; a home's digital lock may ask for a code, a card or a fingerprint. The lock's rule is the opposite of the alarm's: the alarm watches for the **wrong event**, the lock waits for the **right proof** — and together they guard the things a family owns.

In your mind's eye: locks — devices that open only for their owner.

## Do It Yourself

1. Show it, don't just say it: To watch and to warn when its rule is broken. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
2. Show it, don't just say it: A door opened without its key. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
3. Show it, don't just say it: The moment its rule is broken — a door opened, a window forced, smoke detected. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
