# Digital Devices That Protect Us — Alarms and Locks — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: To watch and to warn when its rule is broken**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: To watch and to warn when its rule is broken** — as the answer to: What does a car alarm react to?
**Answer: False — A door opened without its key**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The moment its rule is broken — a door opened, a window forced, smoke detected**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The moment its rule is broken — a door opened, a window forced, smoke detected** — as the answer to: Does an alarm sleep?
**Answer: False — No — watching is its whole work, day and night**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It warns with sound and calls the owners**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: It warns with sound and calls the owners** — as the answer to: What does a digital lock wait for?
**Answer: False — The right proof — its own key, code, card or fingerprint**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Knowing the code's numbers**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Knowing the code's numbers** — as the answer to: How is a lock's rule different from an alarm's?
**Answer: False — The alarm watches for the wrong event; the lock waits for the right proof**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A code and a fingerprint (also: a card)**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A code and a fingerprint (also: a card)** — as the answer to: What do alarms and locks do together?
**Answer: False — They guard the things a family owns — cars, bicycles and homes**
