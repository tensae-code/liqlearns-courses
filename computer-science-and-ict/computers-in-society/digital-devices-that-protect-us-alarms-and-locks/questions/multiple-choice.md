# Digital Devices That Protect Us — Alarms and Locks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an alarm's job?**
a) To watch and to warn when its rule is broken  b) A door opened without its key  c) The moment its rule is broken — a door opened, a window forced, smoke detected  d) No — watching is its whole work, day and night
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a car alarm react to?**
a) A door opened without its key  b) The moment its rule is broken — a door opened, a window forced, smoke detected  c) No — watching is its whole work, day and night  d) It warns with sound and calls the owners
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When does an alarm act?**
a) The moment its rule is broken — a door opened, a window forced, smoke detected  b) No — watching is its whole work, day and night  c) It warns with sound and calls the owners  d) The right proof — its own key, code, card or fingerprint
**Answer: a**
