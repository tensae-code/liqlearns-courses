# Digital Devices That Protect Us — Alarms and Locks — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an alarm's job?** → **To watch and to warn when its rule is broken**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a car alarm react to?** → **A door opened without its key**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When does an alarm act?** → **The moment its rule is broken — a door opened, a window forced, smoke detected**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Does an alarm sleep?** → **No — watching is its whole work, day and night**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a home alarm do when it is triggered?** → **It warns with sound and calls the owners**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a digital lock wait for?** → **The right proof — its own key, code, card or fingerprint**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What opens a code bicycle lock?** → **Knowing the code's numbers**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is a lock's rule different from an alarm's?** → **The alarm watches for the wrong event; the lock waits for the right proof**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name two proofs a home digital lock may ask for.** → **A code and a fingerprint (also: a card)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do alarms and locks do together?** → **They guard the things a family owns — cars, bicycles and homes**
