# Hands-on — E-banking — services, benefits and challenges

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Signal or paper audit
For one week, note every money movement you see at home. Sort into two columns: paper (cash, cheque) and signal (phone, ATM, card). At week's end, say which column is growing, and why.

## Include one person
Choose the challenge of digital literacy. Write a five-step picture guide that would let a grandparent check a balance by phone. Test it on someone patient; fix the step they stumble on.
