# E-banking — services, benefits and challenges — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** E-banking can exclude people who are not digitally literate.
**Answer: True** — one of the four challenges.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** E-banking raises banks' operating costs.
**Answer: False** — cheaper operations for banks is a named benefit.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Credit, debit and smart cards are among e-banking's named services.
**Answer: True** — the book names all three.
