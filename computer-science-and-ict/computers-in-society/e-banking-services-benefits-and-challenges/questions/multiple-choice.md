# E-banking — services, benefits and challenges — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** E-banking moves money by —
a) paper forms only  b) electronic signals instead of cash, cheques and paper  c) carrier pigeon  d) hand delivery
**Answer: b) electronic signals instead of cash, cheques and paper** — the book's defining contrast.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a named e-banking service?
a) electronic funds transfer  b) paper cheques  c) cash bundles  d) passbooks only
**Answer: a) electronic funds transfer** — with cards, internet banking, ATMs and mobile banking.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Mobile banking, internet banking and _______ carry e-banking.
a) ATMs  b) branches  c) queues  d) letters
**Answer: a) ATMs** — the three channels named.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a benefit of e-banking?
a) cash at any time  b) longer queues  c) higher bank costs  d) paper receipts only
**Answer: a) cash at any time** — with cheaper operations, payments from anywhere, easy checks, instant messages.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a challenge of e-banking?
a) digital theft  b) instant messages  c) easy balance checks  d) payments from anywhere
**Answer: a) digital theft** — theft heads the challenges list.
