# E-banking — services, benefits and challenges — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four challenges of e-banking.
**Answer: Digital theft; exclusion of people who are not digitally literate; missing IT infrastructure in remote areas; the cost of devices for low-income citizens.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three benefits of e-banking.
**Answer: Any three of: cheaper operations for banks; cash at any time; payments from anywhere; easy balance checks; instant transaction messages.**
