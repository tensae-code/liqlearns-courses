# Mindfulness in the Digital World — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is mindfulness in the digital world?**
**Answer: Attention paid on purpose — noticing what you are doing with a device while you do it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does most digital harm enter, by this lesson?**
**Answer: Unattended — the automatic unlock, the endless scroll, the reply sent in heat**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What three questions form the breath of noticing?**
**Answer: What am I opening, why now, and what will it cost the thing I was doing?**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is presence?**
**Answer: One task while it lasts, one person while they speak, rest that is actually rest**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name two humble tools of presence.**
**Answer: Notifications silenced by decision, and the phone in another room for meals and conversation**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What pause protects a post?**
**Answer: Imagining the future reader before posting**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Attention is what, to every platform?**
**Answer: The one resource they all compete for**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Who spends attention as their own?**
**Answer: The mindful user**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 9, Bab IV E.2 — mindfulness in the digital world**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Mindfulness is taught as what — a mood or a skill?**
**Answer: A skill of the digital citizen**
