# Hands-on — Mindfulness in the Digital World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Catch three automatics** — For one day, note three times you unlocked or scrolled without deciding to. For each, write the noticing question you could have asked, and what it would have saved.

## Task two
**One present hour** — Choose one hour with people (a meal, a visit). Put the phone in another room and silence notifications. Afterwards, write what was different — in the conversation and in you.
