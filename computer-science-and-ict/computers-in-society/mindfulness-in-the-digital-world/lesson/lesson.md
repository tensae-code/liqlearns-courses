# Mindfulness in the Digital World

## Do it first

Mindfulness in the digital world — attending on purpose to your own digital acts: noticing before clicking, choosing where attention goes, and being present in the life the screen interrupts

## Sub lesson 1 — Attention is the battleground — noticing first

**Mindfulness** (kesadaran penuh) in the digital world is attention paid **on purpose**: noticing what you are doing with a device while you do it. Indonesia's Grade 9 digital-behaviour chapter sets it between behaviour and health because most digital harm enters unattended — the automatic unlock, the scroll that eats an hour, the reply sent in heat. The mindful user inserts one breath of noticing into the chain: what am I opening, why now, and what will it cost the thing I was doing? The question is small; asked at the right second, it is the whole defence.

## Sub lesson 2 — Presence — one thing, one person, one moment

Mindfulness practised becomes **presence**: one task while the task lasts, one person while the person is speaking, and rest that is actually rest. Its tools are humble — notifications silenced by decision, the phone in another room for the meal and the conversation, a pause before posting in which the future reader is imagined. Grade 9 teaches it as a skill of the digital citizen, not a mood: attention is the one resource every platform competes for, and the mindful user is the one who spends it as their own.
