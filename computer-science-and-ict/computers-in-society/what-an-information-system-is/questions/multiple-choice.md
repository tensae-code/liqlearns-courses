# What an information system is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** An information system is —
a) one fast computer  b) a set of interrelated components working together to collect, process, store and share information  c) a filing cabinet only  d) a single report
**Answer: b) a set of interrelated components working together to collect, process, store and share information** — the book's definition.

**2.** A school information system turns marks into —
a) posters  b) averages and rankings  c) timetables only  d) uniforms
**Answer: b) averages and rankings** — quiz, assignment and exam marks become averages and rankings.

**3.** Managers use system reports to —
a) price a product  b) paint offices  c) hire buses  d) plant crops
**Answer: a) price a product** — the book's workplace example.
