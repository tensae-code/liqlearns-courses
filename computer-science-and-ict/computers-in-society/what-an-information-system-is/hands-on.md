# Hands-on — What an information system is

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Follow a report
Choose one report you have seen — a school report card, a shop receipt summary. Trace it backwards: what was collected, where was it stored, what processing made it, who received it. Draw the four steps.

## System hunt
List three places in your town where the four verbs happen together. For each, name the components you can see and the one you cannot.
