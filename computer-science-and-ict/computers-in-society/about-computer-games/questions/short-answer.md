# About Computer Games — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a computer game?**
**Answer: A program made for playing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does the player act in a game?**
**Answer: Through the keyboard, mouse or joystick**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does the game answer the player?**
**Answer: At once — the score changes, the figure moves, the level ends**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the games lesson sit in Uzbekistan's Grade 5 book?**
**Answer: Near the end of the year, after Paint**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What must a pupil do before playing, in the book's order?**
**Answer: Learn to name what a game is**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name two kinds of computer games.**
**Answer: Races and adventures; puzzles and logic games (any two kinds)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What can a game train?**
**Answer: Speed, memory, attention and planning**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What care does a player keep?**
**Answer: Sits well, rests the eyes, and stops at the agreed time**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In the book's view, what is the computer first?**
**Answer: A tool first and a toy second**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does a pupil play a game 'as a lesson'?**
**Answer: By naming its kind and what it trains**
