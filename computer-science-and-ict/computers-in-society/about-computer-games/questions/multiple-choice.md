# About Computer Games — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a computer game?**
a) A program made for playing  b) Through the keyboard, mouse or joystick  c) At once — the score changes, the figure moves, the level ends  d) Near the end of the year, after Paint
**Answer: a) A program made for playing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does the player act in a game?**
a) At once — the score changes, the figure moves, the level ends  b) Through the keyboard, mouse or joystick  c) Near the end of the year, after Paint  d) Learn to name what a game is
**Answer: b) Through the keyboard, mouse or joystick**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does the game answer the player?**
a) Near the end of the year, after Paint  b) Learn to name what a game is  c) At once — the score changes, the figure moves, the level ends  d) Races and adventures; puzzles and logic games (any two kinds)
**Answer: c) At once — the score changes, the figure moves, the level ends**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the games lesson sit in Uzbekistan's Grade 5 book?**
a) Learn to name what a game is  b) Races and adventures; puzzles and logic games (any two kinds)  c) Speed, memory, attention and planning  d) Near the end of the year, after Paint
**Answer: d) Near the end of the year, after Paint**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What must a pupil do before playing, in the book's order?**
a) Learn to name what a game is  b) Races and adventures; puzzles and logic games (any two kinds)  c) Speed, memory, attention and planning  d) Sits well, rests the eyes, and stops at the agreed time
**Answer: a) Learn to name what a game is**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name two kinds of computer games.**
a) Speed, memory, attention and planning  b) Races and adventures; puzzles and logic games (any two kinds)  c) Sits well, rests the eyes, and stops at the agreed time  d) A tool first and a toy second
**Answer: b) Races and adventures; puzzles and logic games (any two kinds)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What can a game train?**
a) Sits well, rests the eyes, and stops at the agreed time  b) A tool first and a toy second  c) Speed, memory, attention and planning  d) By naming its kind and what it trains

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What care does a player keep?**
a) A tool first and a toy second  b) By naming its kind and what it trains  c) A program made for playing  d) Sits well, rests the eyes, and stops at the agreed time
**Answer: d) Sits well, rests the eyes, and stops at the agreed time**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In the book's view, what is the computer first?**
a) A tool first and a toy second  b) By naming its kind and what it trains  c) A program made for playing  d) Through the keyboard, mouse or joystick
**Answer: a) A tool first and a toy second**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does a pupil play a game 'as a lesson'?**
