# ICT in STEM Learning and Careers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four STEM fields and say what each one does.**
**Answer:** Science asks questions about the world and tests them; Technology is the tools and methods people make and use; Engineering designs and builds things that work; Mathematics is numbers, patterns, measurement and step-by-step reasoning.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Give two ways ICT helps you learn a science topic, and say what each does.**
**Answer:** Any two of: finding information beyond the classroom; recording observations and measurements in a table; turning numbers into a chart that shows a pattern; modelling/testing an idea safely in a simulation; programming step-by-step instructions; sharing work with teachers, classmates or experts far away.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Name one STEM career and say what the computer does in that job.**
**Answer:** Any one, correctly matched — e.g. an engineer designs and tests models on screen before building; a scientist records results, works on data and shares findings; a programmer writes software; a health worker keeps and shares patient information.
