# ICT in STEM Learning and Careers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does STEM stand for?**
a) Sports, Television, English and Music  b) Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics  c) Science, Tools, Electricity and Machines
**Answer: b) Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics — The four fields are named in full.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which field designs and builds things that work, like roads and machines?**
a) Science  b) Mathematics  c) Engineering
**Answer: c) Engineering — Designing and building is engineering's part.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why are the four STEM fields taught together?**
a) Because they are used together on real problems  b) Because there are only four teachers  c) Because they are all easy
**Answer: a) Because they are used together on real problems — A clean-water project needs all four at once.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A learner puts experiment measurements into a table and turns them into a chart. Which ICT job is that?**
a) Modelling  b) Recording and working on data  c) Sharing
**Answer: b) Recording and working on data — The chart shows a pattern in the recorded numbers.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) What does a simulation let a STEM learner do?**
a) Skip the lesson  b) Copy a friend's answer  c) Try an idea safely on a screen and watch what happens
**Answer: c) Try an idea safely on a screen and watch what happens — Change a value, watch, try again.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Programming helps STEM learning because it teaches —**
a) Exact step-by-step instructions and thinking  b) How to type fast only  c) How to play games
**Answer: a) Exact step-by-step instructions and thinking — The same careful thinking mathematics and engineering need.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3) An engineer tests a design on a computer before anything is built. That ICT job is —**
a) Finding  b) Modelling and testing  c) Sharing
**Answer: b) Modelling and testing — The design is tried on screen first.**

**8. (Sub lesson 3) Which of these is a STEM career that uses ICT every day?**
a) A worker who never touches information  b) None of them  c) A scientist recording and sharing results on a computer
**Answer: c) A scientist recording and sharing results on a computer — Recording, working on data and sharing are ICT work.**
