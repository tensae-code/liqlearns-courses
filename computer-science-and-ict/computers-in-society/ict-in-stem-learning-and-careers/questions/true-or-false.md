# ICT in STEM Learning and Careers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) STEM is one new school subject that replaces science and mathematics.**
**Answer: False** — STEM is a name for four fields working together; science and mathematics stay on the timetable.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A chart can show a pattern in measurements that the eye would miss in a list of numbers.**
**Answer: True** — Turning recorded numbers into a chart is one of ICT's STEM jobs.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ICT replaces thinking in STEM learning.**
**Answer: False** — ICT gives thinking better tools; the learner still asks, tests and decides.

**4. (Sub lesson 3) STEM careers use the same ICT tools as STEM learning — finding, recording, modelling and sharing.**
**Answer: True** — That is the lesson's pattern, from scientist to health worker.
