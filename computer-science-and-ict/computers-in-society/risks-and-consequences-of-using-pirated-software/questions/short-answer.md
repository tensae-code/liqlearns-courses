# Risks and Consequences of Using Pirated Software — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What can a pirate copy carry into a machine?**
**Answer: Malware — viruses and spyware packed with the program**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why are pirate sources careless carriers?**
**Answer: They have no reputation to protect**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who installs the malware in a pirated program?**
**Answer: The user, by their own hand, when they install it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why do a pirated program's holes stay open?**
**Answer: It cannot be updated**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What support stands behind pirated software?**
**Answer: None — no maker's help, manual or support**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What law does using pirated software break?**
**Answer: Copyright law**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What legal consequences can reach the user?**
**Answer: Fines and legal action, and removal of the software**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Who else can pirated software bring blame on?**
**Answer: The whole school or institution the user belongs to**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what sense was the copy never free?**
**Answer: It is paid for in risk — to the machine, the work and the user**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What are the unit's three costs of piracy?**
**Answer: It steals from the maker, endangers the user and breaks the law**
