# Risks and Consequences of Using Pirated Software — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What can a pirate copy carry into a machine?" is: Malware — viruses and spyware packed with the program**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why are pirate sources careless carriers?" is: None — no maker's help, manual or support**
**Answer: False** — They have no reputation to protect

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Who installs the malware in a pirated program?" is: The user, by their own hand, when they install it**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why do a pirated program's holes stay open?" is: Fines and legal action, and removal of the software**
**Answer: False** — It cannot be updated

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What support stands behind pirated software?" is: None — no maker's help, manual or support**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What law does using pirated software break?" is: It is paid for in risk — to the machine, the work and the user**
**Answer: False** — Copyright law

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What legal consequences can reach the user?" is: Fines and legal action, and removal of the software**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Who else can pirated software bring blame on?" is: Malware — viruses and spyware packed with the program**
**Answer: False** — The whole school or institution the user belongs to

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "In what sense was the copy never free?" is: It is paid for in risk — to the machine, the work and the user**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What are the unit's three costs of piracy?" is: The user, by their own hand, when they install it**
**Answer: False** — It steals from the maker, endangers the user and breaks the law
