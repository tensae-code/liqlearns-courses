# Risks and Consequences of Using Pirated Software — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What can a pirate copy carry into a machine?**
a) Malware — viruses and spyware packed with the program  b) They have no reputation to protect  c) The user, by their own hand, when they install it  d) It cannot be updated
**Answer: a) Malware — viruses and spyware packed with the program**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why are pirate sources careless carriers?**
a) The user, by their own hand, when they install it  b) They have no reputation to protect  c) It cannot be updated  d) None — no maker's help, manual or support
**Answer: b) They have no reputation to protect**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who installs the malware in a pirated program?**
a) It cannot be updated  b) None — no maker's help, manual or support  c) The user, by their own hand, when they install it  d) Copyright law
**Answer: c) The user, by their own hand, when they install it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why do a pirated program's holes stay open?**
a) None — no maker's help, manual or support  b) Copyright law  c) Fines and legal action, and removal of the software  d) It cannot be updated
**Answer: d) It cannot be updated**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What support stands behind pirated software?**
a) None — no maker's help, manual or support  b) Copyright law  c) Fines and legal action, and removal of the software  d) The whole school or institution the user belongs to
**Answer: a) None — no maker's help, manual or support**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What law does using pirated software break?**
a) Fines and legal action, and removal of the software  b) Copyright law  c) The whole school or institution the user belongs to  d) It is paid for in risk — to the machine, the work and the user
**Answer: b) Copyright law**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What legal consequences can reach the user?**
a) The whole school or institution the user belongs to  b) It is paid for in risk — to the machine, the work and the user  c) Fines and legal action, and removal of the software  d) It steals from the maker, endangers the user and breaks the law
**Answer: c) Fines and legal action, and removal of the software**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Who else can pirated software bring blame on?**
a) It is paid for in risk — to the machine, the work and the user  b) It steals from the maker, endangers the user and breaks the law  c) Malware — viruses and spyware packed with the program  d) The whole school or institution the user belongs to
**Answer: d) The whole school or institution the user belongs to**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what sense was the copy never free?**
a) It is paid for in risk — to the machine, the work and the user  b) It steals from the maker, endangers the user and breaks the law  c) Malware — viruses and spyware packed with the program  d) They have no reputation to protect
**Answer: a) It is paid for in risk — to the machine, the work and the user**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What are the unit's three costs of piracy?**
a) Malware — viruses and spyware packed with the program  b) It steals from the maker, endangers the user and breaks the law  c) They have no reputation to protect  d) The user, by their own hand, when they install it
**Answer: b) It steals from the maker, endangers the user and breaks the law**
