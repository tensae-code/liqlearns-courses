# Hands-on — Checking Whether Information Is Credible

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Three-mark check** — Take one claim you met online this week. Score it against the three marks — trust, evidence, qualifications — and write your verdict: believable, not yet believable, or false, with your reasons.

## Task two
**One medium, one trick** — Find an image or short video shared with a dramatic caption. Check it against its medium's trick (old/crop/edit for images; cut/order/place for video) and report what you found.
