# Checking Whether Information Is Credible — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It can be believed because it shows its grounds**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It can be believed because it shows its grounds** — as the answer to: What are the three marks of a credible source?
**Answer: False — Trustworthiness, inspectable evidence, and the author's qualifications**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: One that is known and accountable, with a record to lose**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: One that is known and accountable, with a record to lose** — as the answer to: What verdict fits a claim with none of the three marks?
**Answer: False — Not yet believable — which is safer than calling it false**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Kredibilitas sumber informasi — the credibility of information sources**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Kredibilitas sumber informasi — the credibility of information sources** — as the answer to: What is checked in a text source?
**Answer: False — Who wrote it and what they cite**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Whether it is the scene it claims — or an old, cropped or edited picture with a new caption**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Whether it is the scene it claims — or an old, cropped or edited picture with a new caption** — as the answer to: What is checked in a video?
**Answer: False — Whether the clip is whole, in its real order and place**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Looking a claim up in a source whose business is checking claims**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Looking a claim up in a source whose business is checking claims** — as the answer to: Credibility is a judgement of what kind?
**Answer: False — A judgement with reasons — not a feeling about a page's polish**
