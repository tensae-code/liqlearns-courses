# Checking Whether Information Is Credible — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does credible mean for a source?** → **It can be believed because it shows its grounds**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the three marks of a credible source?** → **Trustworthiness, inspectable evidence, and the author's qualifications**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a trustworthy source?** → **One that is known and accountable, with a record to lose**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What verdict fits a claim with none of the three marks?** → **Not yet believable — which is safer than calling it false**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Indonesian phrase for this check?** → **Kredibilitas sumber informasi — the credibility of information sources**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is checked in a text source?** → **Who wrote it and what they cite**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is checked in an image?** → **Whether it is the scene it claims — or an old, cropped or edited picture with a new caption**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is checked in a video?** → **Whether the clip is whole, in its real order and place**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a fact-check?** → **Looking a claim up in a source whose business is checking claims**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Credibility is a judgement of what kind?** → **A judgement with reasons — not a feeling about a page's polish**
