# Checking Whether Information Is Credible — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does credible mean for a source?**
a) It can be believed because it shows its grounds  b) Trustworthiness, inspectable evidence, and the author's qualifications  c) One that is known and accountable, with a record to lose  d) Not yet believable — which is safer than calling it false
**Answer: a**
