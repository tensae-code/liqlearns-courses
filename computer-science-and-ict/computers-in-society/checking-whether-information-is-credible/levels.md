# Checking Whether Information Is Credible — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The marks of a credible source — trust, evidence, qualifications
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: It can be believed because it shows its grounds; Trustworthiness, inspectable evidence, and the author's qualifications; One that is known and accountable, with a record to lose; Not yet believable — which is safer than calling it false; Kredibilitas sumber informasi — the credibility of information sources.

## Sub lesson 2 — Credibility by medium — text, image, video — and the fact-check
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Who wrote it and what they cite; Whether it is the scene it claims — or an old, cropped or edited picture with a new caption; Whether the clip is whole, in its real order and place; Looking a claim up in a source whose business is checking claims; A judgement with reasons — not a feeling about a page's polish.
