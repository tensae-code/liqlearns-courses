# Security breaches, liability and reporting them — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A security breach exposes an organisation to —
a) praise  b) liability  c) nothing  d) free storage
**Answer: b) liability** — answerability is the consequence.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Stolen personal details can be harvested for —
a) gardening  b) identity theft  c) faster internet  d) backups
**Answer: b) identity theft** — the harvest the book names.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which channels add further liability of their own?
a) blogs and instant messaging  b) only letters  c) only radio  d) none
**Answer: a) blogs and instant messaging** — liability spreads beyond databases.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A breach on your own systems must be reported —
a) next year  b) promptly, to the concerned unit or the responsible government body  c) only to friends  d) never
**Answer: b) promptly, to the concerned unit or the responsible government body** — promptness and destination are both in the rule.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** INSA's job, as the guide names it, is to —
a) sell phones  b) protect Ethiopia's information and information infrastructure  c) run schools  d) print passports
**Answer: b) protect Ethiopia's information and information infrastructure** — the guide's naming (teacher guide — evidence).
