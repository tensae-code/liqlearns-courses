# Security breaches, liability and reporting them — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two harms follow a breach, for the organisation and for the person?
**Answer:** The organisation is exposed to liability; the person's stolen details can be harvested for identity theft.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one argument for and one against government monitoring of citizens' digital data.
**Answer:** For: national security and catching unlawful activity. Against: citizens' privacy, and platform cooperation that is not clear from privacy policies (teacher guide — evidence).
