# Hands-on — Security breaches, liability and reporting them

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Breach drill on paper
Invent a small breach (a class list taken from a teacher's laptop). Write the report: what, when, whose data, who is told first, what happens next. Time yourself — promptness is part of the exercise.

## Two-chair debate
Two chairs, two positions. Sit in one chair and argue FOR monitoring for national security for one minute; move chairs and argue AGAINST for one minute. Note which argument survived the swap.
