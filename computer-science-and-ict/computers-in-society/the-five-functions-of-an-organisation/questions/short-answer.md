# The Five Functions of an Organisation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which organisation does Fiji build the chapter around?**
**Answer: The Viti Group of Companies, a Fiji furniture manufacturer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does production do?**
**Answer: Makes the thing — at Viti, furniture from timber**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does marketing do?**
**Answer: Finds and keeps the buyers — knowing what they want and telling them what is made, at what price**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does accounting do?**
**Answer: Counts the money — what came in, went out, is owed and owned**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does accounting matter?**
**Answer: So the organisation always knows whether it is winning or losing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does human resources care for?**
**Answer: The people — hiring, training, paying, keeping them safe and able**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does research and development look at?**
**Answer: Tomorrow — better designs, materials and ways of making**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why must research exist?**
**Answer: So the organisation is not standing still while the world moves**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are information systems built for?**
**Answer: These five functions**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does each function ask the system for?**
**Answer: Different information — each function has its own needs**
