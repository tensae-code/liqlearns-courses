# The Five Functions of an Organisation — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which organisation does Fiji build the chapter around?" is: The Viti Group of Companies, a Fiji furniture manufacturer**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does production do?" is: Finds and keeps the buyers — knowing what they want and telling them what is made, at what price**
**Answer: False** — Makes the thing — at Viti, furniture from timber

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does marketing do?" is: Finds and keeps the buyers — knowing what they want and telling them what is made, at what price**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does accounting do?" is: So the organisation always knows whether it is winning or losing**
**Answer: False** — Counts the money — what came in, went out, is owed and owned

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why does accounting matter?" is: So the organisation always knows whether it is winning or losing**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does human resources care for?" is: Tomorrow — better designs, materials and ways of making**
**Answer: False** — The people — hiring, training, paying, keeping them safe and able

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does research and development look at?" is: Tomorrow — better designs, materials and ways of making**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why must research exist?" is: These five functions**
**Answer: False** — So the organisation is not standing still while the world moves

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What are information systems built for?" is: These five functions**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does each function ask the system for?" is: The Viti Group of Companies, a Fiji furniture manufacturer**
**Answer: False** — Different information — each function has its own needs
