# Video script — The Five Functions of an Organisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three functions face outward and inward at once. **Production** makes the thing — at Viti, furniture: timber in, tables and chairs out. **Marketing** finds and keeps the buyers — knowing what customers want, telling them what is made, at what price. **Accounting** counts the money — what came in, what went out, what is owed and owned — so the organisation always knows whether it is winning or losing. Fiji teaches the five at a furniture maker deliberately: every function can be *seen* working on the factory floor and in its office.
The other two functions serve the organisation itself. **Human resources** cares for the people — hiring, training, paying, keeping them safe and able. **Research and development** looks at tomorrow — better designs, better materials, better ways of making — so the organisation is not standing still while the world moves. Fiji's five together are the map on which the rest of the senior book is drawn: information systems are built *for these functions*, and each function asks the system for different information.
