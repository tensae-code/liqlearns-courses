# IT Careers and Certifications — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Is informatics one job?**
**Answer: No — a field of professions: building, keeping alive, guarding, and making useful**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four families of informatics work.**
**Answer: Builders (programmers, analysts), keepers (administrators, technicians), guardians (security specialists), and people-facing roles (support, data, media)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) In which chapter do the Grade 10 books teach careers?**
**Answer: The digital-culture chapter (budaya digital)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the SMK book add?**
**Answer: The practitioner's view — professions by their daily work and place in a company**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a certification?**
**Answer: An examination by a recognised body that a person can do a stated skill to a stated standard**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why does a certificate persuade a stranger?**
**Answer: It is proof that travels — like a citation, it can be trusted without knowing the person**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What stands beside careers in the book's chapter?**
**Answer: The legal side of informatics — copyright and the profession's rules**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What three marks make a professional, in the book's pairing?**
**Answer: Skilled, certified where the field asks it, and bound by the field's law and ethics**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why do certifications expire?**
**Answer: The field they certify keeps moving**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Certification is the skill's what?**
**Answer: Its passport — not the skill itself**
