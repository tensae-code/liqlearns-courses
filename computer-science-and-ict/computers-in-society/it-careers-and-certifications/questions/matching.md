**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a certification?** → **An examination by a recognised body that a person can do a stated skill to a stated standard**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why does a certificate persuade a stranger?** → **It is proof that travels — like a citation, it can be trusted without knowing the person**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What stands beside careers in the book's chapter?** → **The legal side of informatics — copyright and the profession's rules**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What three marks make a professional, in the book's pairing?** → **Skilled, certified where the field asks it, and bound by the field's law and ethics**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why do certifications expire?** → **The field they certify keeps moving**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Certification is the skill's what?** → **Its passport — not the skill itself**
