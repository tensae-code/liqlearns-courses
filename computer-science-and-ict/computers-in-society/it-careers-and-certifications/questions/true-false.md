# IT Careers and Certifications — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: No — a field of professions: building, keeping alive, guarding, and making useful**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: No — a field of professions: building, keeping alive, guarding, and making useful** — as the answer to: Name the four families of informatics work.
**Answer: False — Builders (programmers, analysts), keepers (administrators, technicians), guardians (security specialists), and people-facing roles (support, data, media)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The digital-culture chapter (budaya digital)**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The digital-culture chapter (budaya digital)** — as the answer to: What does the SMK book add?
**Answer: False — The practitioner's view — professions by their daily work and place in a company**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: An examination by a recognised body that a person can do a stated skill to a stated standard**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: An examination by a recognised body that a person can do a stated skill to a stated standard** — as the answer to: Why does a certificate persuade a stranger?
**Answer: False — It is proof that travels — like a citation, it can be trusted without knowing the person**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The legal side of informatics — copyright and the profession's rules**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The legal side of informatics — copyright and the profession's rules** — as the answer to: What three marks make a professional, in the book's pairing?
**Answer: False — Skilled, certified where the field asks it, and bound by the field's law and ethics**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The field they certify keeps moving**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The field they certify keeps moving** — as the answer to: Certification is the skill's what?
**Answer: False — Its passport — not the skill itself**
