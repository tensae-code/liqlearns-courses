# Hands-on — IT Careers and Certifications

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Profile a profession** — Choose one informatics profession. Write its daily work, the skills it needs, one certification that fits it, and one legal or ethical rule that binds it.

## Task two
**Plan a certificate path** — For a skill you are learning now, find one real certification ladder in it (first certificate, then the next). Write what each step examines and when you could honestly sit the first.
