# Interaction Design Process — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) With what does the process open?**
a) Understanding — the users, their tasks, and the setting  b) The interaction — screens, controls, order of steps, words  c) A named user and task  d) Skipped the first step
**Answer: a) Understanding — the users, their tasks, and the setting**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is designed on that ground?**
a) A named user and task  b) The interaction — screens, controls, order of steps, words  c) Skipped the first step  d) A rough, cheap, testable version of the interaction
**Answer: b) The interaction — screens, controls, order of steps, words**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) To what must each design choice be answerable?**
a) Skipped the first step  b) A rough, cheap, testable version of the interaction  c) A named user and task  d) The chapter's three measures — with users
**Answer: c) A named user and task**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A design that cannot say whose task a screen serves has done what?**
a) A rough, cheap, testable version of the interaction  b) The chapter's three measures — with users  c) Refinement — the design changes where the evidence says it fails  d) Skipped the first step
**Answer: d) Skipped the first step**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a prototype?**
a) A rough, cheap, testable version of the interaction  b) The chapter's three measures — with users  c) Refinement — the design changes where the evidence says it fails  d) Yes — prototype and evaluate again, until the measures pass
**Answer: a) A rough, cheap, testable version of the interaction**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) On what is the prototype evaluated?**
a) Refinement — the design changes where the evidence says it fails  b) The chapter's three measures — with users  c) Yes — prototype and evaluate again, until the measures pass  d) Its judges are users — and users must be met
**Answer: b) The chapter's three measures — with users**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do the findings drive?**
a) Yes — prototype and evaluate again, until the measures pass  b) Its judges are users — and users must be met  c) Refinement — the design changes where the evidence says it fails  d) The one the loop releases — not the first one the designer loved
**Answer: c) Refinement — the design changes where the evidence says it fails**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Does the loop repeat?**
a) Its judges are users — and users must be met  b) The one the loop releases — not the first one the designer loved  c) Understanding — the users, their tasks, and the setting  d) Yes — prototype and evaluate again, until the measures pass
**Answer: d) Yes — prototype and evaluate again, until the measures pass**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why can interaction not be got right by thinking alone?**
a) Its judges are users — and users must be met  b) The one the loop releases — not the first one the designer loved  c) Understanding — the users, their tasks, and the setting  d) The interaction — screens, controls, order of steps, words
**Answer: a) Its judges are users — and users must be met**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which design is the final one?**
a) Understanding — the users, their tasks, and the setting  b) The one the loop releases — not the first one the designer loved  c) The interaction — screens, controls, order of steps, words  d) A named user and task
**Answer: b) The one the loop releases — not the first one the designer loved**
