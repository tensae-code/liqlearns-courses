# Interaction Design Process — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) With what does the process open?**
**Answer: Understanding — the users, their tasks, and the setting**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is designed on that ground?**
**Answer: The interaction — screens, controls, order of steps, words**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) To what must each design choice be answerable?**
**Answer: A named user and task**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A design that cannot say whose task a screen serves has done what?**
**Answer: Skipped the first step**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a prototype?**
**Answer: A rough, cheap, testable version of the interaction**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) On what is the prototype evaluated?**
**Answer: The chapter's three measures — with users**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do the findings drive?**
**Answer: Refinement — the design changes where the evidence says it fails**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Does the loop repeat?**
**Answer: Yes — prototype and evaluate again, until the measures pass**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why can interaction not be got right by thinking alone?**
**Answer: Its judges are users — and users must be met**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which design is the final one?**
**Answer: The one the loop releases — not the first one the designer loved**
