# Hands-on — Spotting a News Item — and Checking It

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**News or not?** — Take ten real items (headlines/screenshots). Sort them: news / opinion / advertising / rumour / old. Defend each sort in one sentence.

## Task two
**Check one item** — Choose one news item and run the four first checks on it. Report: passed or failed, and the evidence.
