# Spotting a News Item — and Checking It — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Something that happened, recently, to someone, somewhere**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Something that happened, recently, to someone, somewhere** — as the answer to: What three things can a news item say?
**Answer: False — Who reports it, when, and where from**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Opinion, advertising, rumour (also: entertainment, old news dressed as new)**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Opinion, advertising, rumour (also: entertainment, old news dressed as new)** — as the answer to: What is the first media skill?
**Answer: False — Spotting — naming what kind of thing you are looking at**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Before believing or sharing**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Before believing or sharing** — as the answer to: What do you check about the source?
**Answer: False — Whether it is a real newsroom or a copy of one**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: That the story is current, not an old one recirculating**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: That the story is current, not an old one recirculating** — as the answer to: How do other sources help?
**Answer: False — If they report the same event in their own words, it gains weight**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: That the item may not deliver what it claims**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: That the item may not deliver what it claims** — as the answer to: What is an item that passes worth?
**Answer: False — Reading closely**
