# Spotting a News Item — and Checking It — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a news item report?**
**Answer: Something that happened, recently, to someone, somewhere**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three things can a news item say?**
**Answer: Who reports it, when, and where from**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name three things that are not news.**
**Answer: Opinion, advertising, rumour (also: entertainment, old news dressed as new)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first media skill?**
**Answer: Spotting — naming what kind of thing you are looking at**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When do you name the kind?**
**Answer: Before believing or sharing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do you check about the source?**
**Answer: Whether it is a real newsroom or a copy of one**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do you check about the date?**
**Answer: That the story is current, not an old one recirculating**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do other sources help?**
**Answer: If they report the same event in their own words, it gains weight**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does an over-promising headline warn of?**
**Answer: That the item may not deliver what it claims**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is an item that passes worth?**
**Answer: Reading closely**
