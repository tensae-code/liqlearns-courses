# Spotting a News Item — and Checking It — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a news item report?** → **Something that happened, recently, to someone, somewhere**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three things can a news item say?** → **Who reports it, when, and where from**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name three things that are not news.** → **Opinion, advertising, rumour (also: entertainment, old news dressed as new)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first media skill?** → **Spotting — naming what kind of thing you are looking at**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When do you name the kind?** → **Before believing or sharing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do you check about the source?** → **Whether it is a real newsroom or a copy of one**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do you check about the date?** → **That the story is current, not an old one recirculating**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do other sources help?** → **If they report the same event in their own words, it gains weight**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does an over-promising headline warn of?** → **That the item may not deliver what it claims**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is an item that passes worth?** → **Reading closely**
