# Spotting a News Item — and Checking It — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What makes a thing news
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Something that happened, recently, to someone, somewhere; Who reports it, when, and where from; Opinion, advertising, rumour (also: entertainment, old news dressed as new); Spotting — naming what kind of thing you are looking at; Before believing or sharing.

## Sub lesson 2 — The first checks
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Whether it is a real newsroom or a copy of one; That the story is current, not an old one recirculating; If they report the same event in their own words, it gains weight; That the item may not deliver what it claims; Reading closely.
