# Video script — Spotting a News Item — and Checking It

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **news item (une information d'actualité)** reports something that happened, recently, to someone, somewhere — and it can say who reports it, when, and where from. Around it swims everything else: opinion, advertising, entertainment, rumour, old news dressed as new. Spotting news is the first media skill: before believing or sharing, name what kind of thing you are looking at.
A spotted news item gets first checks, fast: Is the source a real newsroom or a copy of one? Is there a date — and is it today's story or an old one recirculating? Do other sources report the same event, in their own words? Does the headline promise more than the text delivers? An item that passes is worth reading closely; one that fails has told you what it is.
