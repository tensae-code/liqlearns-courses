# Media Literacy and Types of Media — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are expression media?**
a) The forms a message is shaped in — words, pictures, sound, video  b) The ways a message travels — network, broadcast, carried print  c) The ways a message is kept — files, discs, archives  d) Each family can fail and mislead in its own way, so the reader checks the right one
**Answer: a) The forms a message is shaped in — words, pictures, sound, video**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are transmission media?**
a) The ways a message is kept — files, discs, archives  b) The ways a message travels — network, broadcast, carried print  c) Each family can fail and mislead in its own way, so the reader checks the right one  d) All three — expressed, transmitted and recorded
**Answer: b) The ways a message travels — network, broadcast, carried print**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are recording media?**
