# Media Literacy and Types of Media — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are expression media?**
**Answer: The forms a message is shaped in — words, pictures, sound, video**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are transmission media?**
**Answer: The ways a message travels — network, broadcast, carried print**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are recording media?**
**Answer: The ways a message is kept — files, discs, archives**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why sort media into families?**
**Answer: Each family can fail and mislead in its own way, so the reader checks the right one**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How many families does one message usually use?**
**Answer: All three — expressed, transmitted and recorded**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is media literacy?**
**Answer: Judging what media bring — who made it, why, what was left out, and whether it can be checked**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the literate reader remember about every message?**
**Answer: That it was made — every choice in it was decided by someone**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What question tests an expression choice?**
**Answer: What would the same event look like expressed differently?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Egypt place media literacy in the Grade 10 book?**
**Answer: In the opening section, with data, information and knowledge — before ethics and security**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does media literacy come first?**
**Answer: Later lessons assume it — information cannot be protected or used honestly if messages cannot be judged**
