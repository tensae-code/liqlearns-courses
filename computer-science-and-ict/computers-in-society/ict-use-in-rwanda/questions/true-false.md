# ICT Use in Rwanda — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does Rwanda's S1 book teach first about ICT use?" is: How ICT is used in Rwanda itself, in daily life**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name one Rwandan daily-life use of ICT from the book." is: How ICT is used in Rwanda itself, in daily life**
**Answer: False** — Phones carrying money and messages

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do computers keep in Rwandan schools and offices?" is: School and office records**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What carries news and learning in the book's account?" is: How ICT is used in Rwanda itself, in daily life**
**Answer: False** — Radio, television and the internet

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why does the book start with Rwanda's own ICT use?" is: So the learner recognises the ICT already around them**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name one service field in Rwanda's ICT survey." is: Keeping patient records and linking clinics**
**Answer: False** — Mobile money and banking

**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How do farmers use ICT in Rwanda's book?" is: Mobile money and banking**
