# ICT Use in Rwanda — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does Rwanda's S1 book teach first about ICT use?**
**Answer: How ICT is used in Rwanda itself, in daily life**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name one Rwandan daily-life use of ICT from the book.**
**Answer: Phones carrying money and messages**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do computers keep in Rwandan schools and offices?**
**Answer: School and office records**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What carries news and learning in the book's account?**
**Answer: Radio, television and the internet**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book start with Rwanda's own ICT use?**
**Answer: So the learner recognises the ICT already around them**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name one service field in Rwanda's ICT survey.**
**Answer: Mobile money and banking**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do health services use ICT for in the book's account?**
**Answer: Keeping patient records and linking clinics**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do farmers use ICT in Rwanda's book?**
**Answer: Getting prices and weather by phone**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How does government reach citizens in the book's account?**
**Answer: Through computer systems instead of paper queues**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What pattern do all the service fields share?**
**Answer: Information travels by signal instead of by foot and paper**
