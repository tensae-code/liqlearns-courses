# ICT Use in Rwanda — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — ICT in Rwandan daily life
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: How ICT is used in Rwanda itself, in daily life; Phones carrying money and messages; School and office records; Radio, television and the internet; So the learner recognises the ICT already around them.

## Sub lesson 2 — ICT in services — money, health, farming and government
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Mobile money and banking; Keeping patient records and linking clinics; Getting prices and weather by phone; Through computer systems instead of paper queues; Information travels by signal instead of by foot and paper.
