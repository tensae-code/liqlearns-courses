# Hands-on — ICT Use in Rwanda

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('ICT around me', 'List five ICT uses you met today in Rwanda — at home, at school, on the road. For each, write what job it did and what did that job before ICT.')

## Task two
('One service, two ways', 'Pick one service from the lesson — money, health, farming or government. Write its story twice: the paper way and the ICT way, with time and cost for each.')
