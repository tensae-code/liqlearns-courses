# Video script — Puzzle and Word-Building Games

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In **Puzzle**, a finished picture is cut into pieces, the pieces are mixed, and the player puts each piece back in its place. The game trains the eye: look at the whole, remember colours and edges, and test a piece before fixing it. Uzbekistan's Grade 5 book teaches it by name — the same careful looking the pupil used in Paint, now turned into play.
In the **word-building game** («Построение слова»), the player is given letters and must build a word from them — the longer and truer the word, the better the score. It is the keyboard trainer turned into a contest: letters you can find and spell become points. Together the two games end the Grade 5 book's games lessons: one for the eye, one for letters.
