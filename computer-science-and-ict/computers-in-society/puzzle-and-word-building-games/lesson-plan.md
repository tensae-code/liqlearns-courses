## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Puzzle game — a picture from pieces** — In **Puzzle**, a finished picture is cut into pieces, the pieces are mixed, and the player puts each piece back in its place. The game trains the eye: look at the whole, remember colours and edges, and test a piece before fixing it. Uzbekistan's Grade 5 book teaches it by name — the same careful looking the pupil used in Paint, now turned into play.
2. **Building a word from letters** — In the **word-building game** («Построение слова»), the player is given letters and must build a word from them — the longer and truer the word, the better the score. It is the keyboard trainer turned into a contest: letters you can find and spell become points. Together the two games end the Grade 5 book's games lessons: one for the eye, one for letters.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
