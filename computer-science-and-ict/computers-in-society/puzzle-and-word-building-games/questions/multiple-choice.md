# Puzzle and Word-Building Games — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is cut into pieces in Puzzle?**
a) A finished picture  b) Puts each piece back in its place  c) The eye — looking at the whole, colours and edges  d) Test it
**Answer: a) A finished picture**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the player do with the pieces?**
a) The eye — looking at the whole, colours and edges  b) Puts each piece back in its place  c) Test it  d) Paint
**Answer: b) Puts each piece back in its place**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does Puzzle train?**
a) Test it  b) Paint  c) The eye — looking at the whole, colours and edges  d) Letters
**Answer: c) The eye — looking at the whole, colours and edges**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What should a player do before fixing a piece?**
a) Paint  b) Letters  c) A word  d) Test it
**Answer: d) Test it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which earlier tool used the same careful looking?**
a) Paint  b) Letters  c) A word  d) A longer and truer word
**Answer: a) Paint**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the player given in the word-building game?**
a) A word  b) Letters  c) A longer and truer word  d) The keyboard trainer
**Answer: b) Letters**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must the player build from the letters?**
a) A longer and truer word  b) The keyboard trainer  c) A word  d) The word-building game
**Answer: c) A word**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What makes the score better?**
a) The keyboard trainer  b) The word-building game  c) A finished picture  d) A longer and truer word
**Answer: d) A longer and truer word**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What earlier practice does the word game turn into a contest?**
a) The keyboard trainer  b) The word-building game  c) A finished picture  d) Puts each piece back in its place
**Answer: a) The keyboard trainer**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which of the two games is for letters?**
a) A finished picture  b) The word-building game  c) Puts each piece back in its place  d) The eye — looking at the whole, colours and edges
**Answer: b) The word-building game**
