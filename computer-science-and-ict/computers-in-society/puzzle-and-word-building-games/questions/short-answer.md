# Puzzle and Word-Building Games — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is cut into pieces in Puzzle?**
**Answer: A finished picture**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the player do with the pieces?**
**Answer: Puts each piece back in its place**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does Puzzle train?**
**Answer: The eye — looking at the whole, colours and edges**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What should a player do before fixing a piece?**
**Answer: Test it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which earlier tool used the same careful looking?**
**Answer: Paint**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the player given in the word-building game?**
**Answer: Letters**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must the player build from the letters?**
**Answer: A word**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What makes the score better?**
**Answer: A longer and truer word**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What earlier practice does the word game turn into a contest?**
**Answer: The keyboard trainer**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which of the two games is for letters?**
**Answer: The word-building game**
