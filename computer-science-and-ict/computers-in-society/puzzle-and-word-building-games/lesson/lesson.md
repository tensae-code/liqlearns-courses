# Puzzle and Word-Building Games

## Do it first

Two games close Uzbekistan's Grade 5 year: one builds a picture from pieces, the other builds a word from letters. Both train the same eye that informatics has trained all year.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Puzzle game — a picture from pieces

In **Puzzle**, a finished picture is cut into pieces, the pieces are mixed, and the player puts each piece back in its place. The game trains the eye: look at the whole, remember colours and edges, and test a piece before fixing it. Uzbekistan's Grade 5 book teaches it by name — the same careful looking the pupil used in Paint, now turned into play.

## Sub lesson 2 — Building a word from letters

In the **word-building game** («Построение слова»), the player is given letters and must build a word from them — the longer and truer the word, the better the score. It is the keyboard trainer turned into a contest: letters you can find and spell become points. Together the two games end the Grade 5 book's games lessons: one for the eye, one for letters.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: In **Puzzle**, a finished picture is cut into pieces, the pieces are mixed, and the player puts each piece back in its place. In the **word-building game** («Построение слова»), the player is given letters and must build a word from them — the longer and truer the word, the better the score. Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.

## Do It Yourself

1. Show it, don't just say it: A finished picture. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
2. Show it, don't just say it: Puts each piece back in its place. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
3. Show it, don't just say it: Letters. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
4. Show it, don't just say it: A word. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
