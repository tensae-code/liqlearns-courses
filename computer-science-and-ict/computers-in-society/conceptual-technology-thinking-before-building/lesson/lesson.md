# Conceptual Technology — Thinking Before Building

## Do it first

Before a thing is designed in detail or built, it exists as an idea. Papua New Guinea's Grade 12 conceptual-technology lessons live in that stage on purpose. This lesson thinks there.

## Sub lesson 1 — Thinking in concepts before building

**Conceptual technology** deals with **ideas and plans before making**. A **concept** is **an idea of the thing to be made**, and it can be shown as a **sketch or a model**. Thinking first exists to **solve problems before spending on building**: building without a concept risks wasted work and a poor product.

[Picture — Thinking in concepts before building]

## Sub lesson 2 — Concepts become designs

The concept stage comes **before making and building**. Designers and technologists test a concept by **thinking and modelling it against the brief**; if the concept fails, the idea is changed — cheaply, before building. The concept is the link between the problem and the design that follows.
