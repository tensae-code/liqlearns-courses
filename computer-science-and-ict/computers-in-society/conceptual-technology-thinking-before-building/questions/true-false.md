# Conceptual Technology — Thinking Before Building — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Conceptual technology deals with what?" is: Ideas and plans before making**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a concept?" is: A sketch or a model**
**Answer: False** — An idea of the thing to be made

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why think in concepts before building?" is: To solve problems before spending on building**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "A concept can be shown as what?" is: Making and building**
**Answer: False** — A sketch or a model

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Building without a concept risks what?" is: Wasted work and a poor product**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "The concept stage comes before what?" is: By thinking and modelling it against the brief**
**Answer: False** — Making and building

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Who works with concepts?" is: Designers and technologists**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is a concept tested?" is: The design that follows**
**Answer: False** — By thinking and modelling it against the brief

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "If a concept fails, what is changed?" is: The idea — cheaply, before building**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "The concept links the problem to what?" is: An idea of the thing to be made**
**Answer: False** — The design that follows
