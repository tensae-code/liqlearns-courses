# Conceptual Technology — Thinking Before Building — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Conceptual technology deals with what?**
**Answer: Ideas and plans before making**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a concept?**
**Answer: An idea of the thing to be made**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why think in concepts before building?**
**Answer: To solve problems before spending on building**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A concept can be shown as what?**
**Answer: A sketch or a model**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Building without a concept risks what?**
**Answer: Wasted work and a poor product**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The concept stage comes before what?**
**Answer: Making and building**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Who works with concepts?**
**Answer: Designers and technologists**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is a concept tested?**
**Answer: By thinking and modelling it against the brief**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) If a concept fails, what is changed?**
**Answer: The idea — cheaply, before building**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) The concept links the problem to what?**
**Answer: The design that follows**
