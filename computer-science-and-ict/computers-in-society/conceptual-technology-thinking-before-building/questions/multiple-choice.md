# Conceptual Technology — Thinking Before Building — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Conceptual technology deals with what?**
a) Ideas and plans before making  b) An idea of the thing to be made  c) To solve problems before spending on building  d) A sketch or a model
**Answer: a) Ideas and plans before making**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a concept?**
a) To solve problems before spending on building  b) An idea of the thing to be made  c) A sketch or a model  d) Wasted work and a poor product
**Answer: b) An idea of the thing to be made**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why think in concepts before building?**
a) A sketch or a model  b) Wasted work and a poor product  c) To solve problems before spending on building  d) Making and building
**Answer: c) To solve problems before spending on building**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A concept can be shown as what?**
a) Wasted work and a poor product  b) Making and building  c) Designers and technologists  d) A sketch or a model
**Answer: d) A sketch or a model**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Building without a concept risks what?**
a) Wasted work and a poor product  b) Making and building  c) Designers and technologists  d) By thinking and modelling it against the brief
**Answer: a) Wasted work and a poor product**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The concept stage comes before what?**
a) Designers and technologists  b) Making and building  c) By thinking and modelling it against the brief  d) The idea — cheaply, before building
**Answer: b) Making and building**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Who works with concepts?**
a) By thinking and modelling it against the brief  b) The idea — cheaply, before building  c) Designers and technologists  d) The design that follows
**Answer: c) Designers and technologists**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is a concept tested?**
a) The idea — cheaply, before building  b) The design that follows  c) Ideas and plans before making  d) By thinking and modelling it against the brief
**Answer: d) By thinking and modelling it against the brief**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) If a concept fails, what is changed?**
a) The idea — cheaply, before building  b) The design that follows  c) Ideas and plans before making  d) An idea of the thing to be made
**Answer: a) The idea — cheaply, before building**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) The concept links the problem to what?**
a) Ideas and plans before making  b) The design that follows  c) An idea of the thing to be made  d) To solve problems before spending on building
**Answer: b) The design that follows**
