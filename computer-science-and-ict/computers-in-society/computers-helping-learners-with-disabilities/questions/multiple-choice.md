# Computers Helping Learners with Disabilities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a Braille keyboard do?**
a) Lets a blind learner type and read by touch — the screen's words turned into touch  b) Turn the screen's print into speech for a learner who cannot see it  c) Magnification — print made large enough to read  d) It shows what others hear — captions, written instructions, visual signals
**Answer: a) Lets a blind learner type and read by touch — the screen's words turned into touch**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do screen-reading programs do?**
a) Magnification — print made large enough to read  b) Turn the screen's print into speech for a learner who cannot see it  c) It shows what others hear — captions, written instructions, visual signals  d) The Braille keyboard
**Answer: b) Turn the screen's print into speech for a learner who cannot see it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What helps a learner who sees a little?**
a) It shows what others hear — captions, written instructions, visual signals  b) The Braille keyboard  c) Magnification — print made large enough to read  d) Lessons at their own pace, repeatable without embarrassment
**Answer: c) Magnification — print made large enough to read**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does the computer help a learner who hears little?**
