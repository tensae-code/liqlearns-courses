# Computers Helping Learners with Disabilities — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a Braille keyboard do?" is: Lets a blind learner type and read by touch — the screen's words turned into touch**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do screen-reading programs do?" is: Magnification — print made large enough to read**
**Answer: False** — Turn the screen's print into speech for a learner who cannot see it

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What helps a learner who sees a little?" is: Magnification — print made large enough to read**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How does the computer help a learner who hears little?" is: The Braille keyboard**
**Answer: False** — It shows what others hear — captions, written instructions, visual signals

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which device does Fiji name itself?" is: The Braille keyboard**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is a tutorial program's gift to a learner?" is: Patient at the tenth try as at the first**
**Answer: False** — Lessons at their own pace, repeatable without embarrassment

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is the machine described as a teacher?" is: Patient at the tenth try as at the first**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How can a learner who cannot write by hand use a computer?" is: No — they are how the same lesson reaches every learner**
**Answer: False** — By dictation, or by switch or touch input

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Are these helps favours?" is: No — they are how the same lesson reaches every learner**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the helped learner doing?" is: Lets a blind learner type and read by touch — the screen's words turned into touch**
**Answer: False** — The same work as everyone else
