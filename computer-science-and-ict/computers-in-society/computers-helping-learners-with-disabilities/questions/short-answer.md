# Computers Helping Learners with Disabilities — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a Braille keyboard do?**
**Answer: Lets a blind learner type and read by touch — the screen's words turned into touch**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do screen-reading programs do?**
**Answer: Turn the screen's print into speech for a learner who cannot see it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What helps a learner who sees a little?**
**Answer: Magnification — print made large enough to read**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does the computer help a learner who hears little?**
**Answer: It shows what others hear — captions, written instructions, visual signals**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which device does Fiji name itself?**
**Answer: The Braille keyboard**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a tutorial program's gift to a learner?**
**Answer: Lessons at their own pace, repeatable without embarrassment**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the machine described as a teacher?**
**Answer: Patient at the tenth try as at the first**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How can a learner who cannot write by hand use a computer?**
**Answer: By dictation, or by switch or touch input**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Are these helps favours?**
**Answer: No — they are how the same lesson reaches every learner**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the helped learner doing?**
**Answer: The same work as everyone else**
