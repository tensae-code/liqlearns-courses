# From an Idea to a Digital Project — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does every digital project begin?**
**Answer: As an idea — something to show, teach, celebrate or solve**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three questions make an idea buildable?**
**Answer: What exactly it will be, who it is for, and what parts it will be made of, in what order**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the project's parts come from?**
**Answer: The unit's own lessons — data, presentation, website — chosen and ordered for this idea**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What separates a project from a pile of beginnings?**
**Answer: A plan written down**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the craft in the building stage?**
**Answer: The joining — parts agreeing in data, names, colours and words so the audience meets one thing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the road end?**
**Answer: In showing — presenting the project to its real audience**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do the audience's questions reveal?**
**Answer: What the maker could not see**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the five steps of Egypt's Grade 5 project road**
**Answer: Idea, plan, parts, joining, showing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which later projects walk this same road?**
**Answer: Every bigger project in the course, up to the secondary programming projects**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What grade and unit does this lesson close?**
**Answer: Grade 5, the 'My Digital Project' unit (Unit 4)**
