# From an Idea to a Digital Project — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does every digital project begin?" is: As an idea — something to show, teach, celebrate or solve**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What three questions make an idea buildable?" is: The unit's own lessons — data, presentation, website — chosen and ordered for this idea**
**Answer: False** — What exactly it will be, who it is for, and what parts it will be made of, in what order

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where do the project's parts come from?" is: The unit's own lessons — data, presentation, website — chosen and ordered for this idea**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What separates a project from a pile of beginnings?" is: The joining — parts agreeing in data, names, colours and words so the audience meets one thing**
**Answer: False** — A plan written down

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the craft in the building stage?" is: The joining — parts agreeing in data, names, colours and words so the audience meets one thing**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does the road end?" is: What the maker could not see**
**Answer: False** — In showing — presenting the project to its real audience

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do the audience's questions reveal?" is: What the maker could not see**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which later projects walk this same road?" is: Every bigger project in the course, up to the secondary programming projects**
