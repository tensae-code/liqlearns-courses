# Video script — Managing Information — Collecting, Storing, Retrieving

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Managing information** begins with **collecting**: gathering the data an organisation or a person needs, from its sources, completely and accurately — what is not collected cannot be used later. Then **storing**: keeping what was collected in an ordered place — files, records, a database — where it stays safe and findable. Collecting without storing scatters; storing without order buries. The book's pair is deliberate: get it, then keep it where a hand can reach it again.
Stored data becomes useful by **processing** — sorting, calculating, summarising — and by **presenting**: showing the result in the form its user needs. And the test of the whole cycle is **retrieving**: asking for a piece of stored information and getting it back, quickly and exactly. A store that cannot be searched has failed at its job even if nothing was ever lost from it. Laos teaches managing as one cycle because each act earns its meaning from the next: we collect in order to retrieve.
