# Managing Information — Collecting, Storing, Retrieving — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Collecting, storing, processing, presenting and retrieving information**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Keeping collected information in an ordered, safe, findable place** — as the answer to: What is collecting?
**Answer: False — Gathering the data needed, completely and accurately**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Keeping collected information in an ordered, safe, findable place**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Showing the result in the form its user needs** — as the answer to: What does processing do to stored data?
**Answer: False — Sorts, calculates and summarises it**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Showing the result in the form its user needs**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: If what is stored cannot be found and retrieved** — as the answer to: What is retrieving?
**Answer: False — Asking for stored information and getting it back quickly and exactly**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: If what is stored cannot be found and retrieved**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 6 ICT, Chapter 2 §8** — as the answer to: Why is managing taught as one cycle?
**Answer: False — Each act earns its meaning from the next**

**9. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Laos Grade 6 ICT, Chapter 2 §8**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Collecting, storing, processing, presenting and retrieving information** — as the answer to: What happens to information that is never collected?
**Answer: False — It cannot be used later at all**
