# Managing Information — Collecting, Storing, Retrieving — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What cycle does this lesson teach?**
**Answer: Collecting, storing, processing, presenting and retrieving information**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is collecting?**
**Answer: Gathering the data needed, completely and accurately**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is storing?**
**Answer: Keeping collected information in an ordered, safe, findable place**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) What does processing do to stored data?**
**Answer: Sorts, calculates and summarises it**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) What is presenting?**
**Answer: Showing the result in the form its user needs**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is retrieving?**
**Answer: Asking for stored information and getting it back quickly and exactly**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why can a store fail even if nothing is lost from it?**
**Answer: If what is stored cannot be found and retrieved**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is managing taught as one cycle?**
**Answer: Each act earns its meaning from the next**

**9. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 6 ICT, Chapter 2 §8**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to information that is never collected?**
**Answer: It cannot be used later at all**
