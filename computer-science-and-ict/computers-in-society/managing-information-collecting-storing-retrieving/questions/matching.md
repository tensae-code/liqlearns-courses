# Managing Information — Collecting, Storing, Retrieving — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What cycle does this lesson teach?** → **Collecting, storing, processing, presenting and retrieving information**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is collecting?** → **Gathering the data needed, completely and accurately**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is storing?** → **Keeping collected information in an ordered, safe, findable place**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) What does processing do to stored data?** → **Sorts, calculates and summarises it**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) What is presenting?** → **Showing the result in the form its user needs**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is retrieving?** → **Asking for stored information and getting it back quickly and exactly**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why can a store fail even if nothing is lost from it?** → **If what is stored cannot be found and retrieved**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is managing taught as one cycle?** → **Each act earns its meaning from the next**

**9. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches this?** → **Laos Grade 6 ICT, Chapter 2 §8**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to information that is never collected?** → **It cannot be used later at all**
