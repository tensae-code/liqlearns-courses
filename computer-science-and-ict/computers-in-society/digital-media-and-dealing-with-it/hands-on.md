# Hands-on — Digital Media — and Dealing With It

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Media diary** — Keep a one-hour media diary: each item's kind, its mark at work, and your dealing habit used or missed.

## Task two
**Shaping autopsy** — Take one viral item and write what its headline, crop and counts are doing to the reader's feeling.
