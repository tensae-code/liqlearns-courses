# Digital Media — and Dealing With It — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the kinds of digital media.**
**Answer: Websites and articles, video and audio, social posts, images, games, interactive pieces**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name digital media's four marks.**
**Answer: Instant, interactive, measurable, endless**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does measurable do to making?**
**Answer: Every view is counted — and the counting shapes what gets made**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why inventory by kind and mark?**
**Answer: Dealing begins with knowing what kind of thing is in front of you**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What has digital media no last one of?**
**Answer: Page — it is endless**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four wise habits.**
**Answer: Check the source, check the claim, notice the shaping, govern your own part**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What two source questions are asked?**
**Answer: Who made this, and what do they want?**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name three shaping tools to notice.**
**Answer: The headline, the crop, the count**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do when you spread a thing?**
**Answer: Endorse it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is digital media not, in the closing line?**
**Answer: Weather — it is made by people, and can be answered by people**
