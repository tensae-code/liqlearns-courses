## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The means of digital media** — **Digital media** is content made, carried and consumed digitally: **websites and articles, video and audio, social posts, images, games and interactive pieces**. Its marks against older media: it is **instant** (published in a second), **interactive** (the audience answers, remakes, spreads), **measurable** (every view counted — and the counting shapes what gets made), and **endless** (no last page). Jordan's inventory is by kind and by mark, because dealing with media begins with knowing what kind of thing is in front of you.
2. **Dealing with it — the wise user's crafts** — Dealing wisely is the media unit's craft, in four habits: **check the source** (who made this, and what do they want?), **check the claim** (who else says it — the verification lessons' skill), **notice the shaping** (what the headline, the crop and the count are doing to your feeling), and **govern your own part** (what you spread, you endorse; what you watch by the hour, you chose by the minute). The book's closing line is the citizen's: digital media is not weather — it is made by people, and can be answered by people.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of the means of digital media as the lesson describes it — the student studies the picture and names what each part shows.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of dealing with it — the wise user's crafts in action — the student watches once, then says what changed and why.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key names and definitions aloud, in the order the lesson gives them — the student listens once through, then reads the same lines aloud along with the voice. (Audio is a described slot in this build; no audio file is made.)
