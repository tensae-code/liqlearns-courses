# Green Computing in Jordan — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name Jordan's four ground facts.**
**Answer: Abundant sun, imported fuel's cost, water scarcity, a young multiplying-device population**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is solar energy in Jordan?**
**Answer: A national resource, not a metaphor**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does efficiency save at national scale?**
**Answer: Imported fuel's cost**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What Jordanian price do cooling and washing carry?**
**Answer: Water's price — scarcity**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What can be a necessity here but a preference elsewhere?**
**Answer: Green practice**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the five practices gathered.**
**Answer: Solar power, efficient devices/settings, e-services replacing paper and journeys, e-waste recycling/disposal, awareness**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why is e-government a green policy?**
**Answer: It replaces paper and journeys**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the curriculum itself, in this list?**
**Answer: A national instrument of awareness**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a country's green computing, in the closing phrase?**
**Answer: Its principles translated into its own ground**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who can translate anywhere?**
**Answer: The pupil who can translate for Jordan**
