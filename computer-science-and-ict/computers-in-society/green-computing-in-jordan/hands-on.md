# Hands-on — Green Computing in Jordan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Translate a principle** — Take three worldwide green-computing principles and write their Jordanian form, with the ground fact that shapes each.

## Task two
**School audit, Jordan edition** — Audit your school for energy, paper-journeys and e-waste; propose three fixes that spend Jordan's advantages (sun first).
