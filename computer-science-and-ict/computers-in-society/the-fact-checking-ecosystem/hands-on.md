# Hands-on — The Fact-Checking Ecosystem

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Use the ecosystem** — Take a circulating claim. Search for a published check of it; if one exists, write its verdict and the evidence it shows. If none exists, write the claim's exact form and mark it 'not yet checked'.

## Task two
**Run a check** — Choose a small checkable claim from school life. Run the three steps yourself — claim fixed, evidence gathered, verdict with evidence — in half a page.
