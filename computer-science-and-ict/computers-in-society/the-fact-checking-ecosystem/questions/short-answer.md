# The Fact-Checking Ecosystem — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the fact-checking ecosystem?**
**Answer: The people, organisations and habits whose work is checking claims**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name its four kinds of member.**
**Answer: Fact-checking organisations, newsroom verification desks, platforms and community groups, and checking readers**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who is the ecosystem's last mile?**
**Answer: Ordinary readers — their habits carry the check to where the claim travelled**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What decides a claim's danger?**
**Answer: How fast a check can reach the people the claim reached**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are the three steps of a professional check?**
**Answer: Fix the claim exactly, gather the evidence, publish a verdict with the evidence shown**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What verdicts does a checker publish?**
**Answer: True, false, misleading, unproven — with evidence**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why show the evidence with the verdict?**
**Answer: So the reader can re-walk the road and judge the check itself**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What should a reader prefer?**
**Answer: Checks that show their evidence**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) 'Not yet checked' means what for a reader?**
**Answer: A reason to wait before sharing**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 10, Bab 1 C — the fact-checking ecosystem**
