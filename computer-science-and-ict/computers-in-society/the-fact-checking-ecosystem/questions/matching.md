# The Fact-Checking Ecosystem — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the fact-checking ecosystem?** → **The people, organisations and habits whose work is checking claims**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name its four kinds of member.** → **Fact-checking organisations, newsroom verification desks, platforms and community groups, and checking readers**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who is the ecosystem's last mile?** → **Ordinary readers — their habits carry the check to where the claim travelled**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What decides a claim's danger?** → **How fast a check can reach the people the claim reached**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are the three steps of a professional check?** → **Fix the claim exactly, gather the evidence, publish a verdict with the evidence shown**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What verdicts does a checker publish?** → **True, false, misleading, unproven — with evidence**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why show the evidence with the verdict?** → **So the reader can re-walk the road and judge the check itself**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What should a reader prefer?** → **Checks that show their evidence**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) 'Not yet checked' means what for a reader?** → **A reason to wait before sharing**
