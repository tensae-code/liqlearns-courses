# Video script — The Fact-Checking Ecosystem

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

No single reader can check everything; societies grow an **ecosystem** (ekosistem periksa fakta) for it. Its members: **fact-checking organisations** that take public claims one by one and publish verdicts with evidence; **newsrooms** with verification desks; **platforms and community groups** that flag and forward checks; and the checking habits of ordinary readers, who are the ecosystem's last mile. Indonesia's Grade 10 chapter teaches the ecosystem as a system — each member weak alone, the web of them strong — because a claim's danger is decided by how fast a check can reach the people the claim reached.
A professional check runs in order: fix the **claim** exactly (who said what, where), gather the **evidence** (the original source, the data, the maker asked to answer), and publish a **verdict** with the evidence shown — true, false, misleading, unproven — so the reader can re-walk the road. The reader's skill is using the ecosystem well: search the claim before sharing it, prefer checks that show their evidence, and notice when no check exists yet — 'not yet checked' is itself a reason to wait.
