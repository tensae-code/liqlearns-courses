## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Posts — the team's written conversation** — **Posts** are the team's shared written conversation: the teacher's announcements, questions asked where everyone can learn from the answer, and pupils' replies. A post is public inside the team — written for the whole class to read, in the class's respectful language — which is why Kuwait teaches posts as a school behaviour, not only a button: what you post in the team, the team keeps.
2. **Files — what the teacher adds to the team** — The team's **files** are its shelves: documents, worksheets and materials the **teacher adds** for the class to open, read and use. The pupil's skills are finding a file by its name and place, opening the right one (not yesterday's version), and knowing that team files belong to the class — you take your copy or read them in place; you do not tidy the teacher's shelves away.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
