# Microsoft Teams — Team Posts and Class Files

## Do it first

A class talks, and a class keeps things. Kuwait's fifth Teams lesson teaches the team's two commons: its posts — the written conversation of the class — and its files, the materials the teacher places there for everyone. This lesson is how a pupil takes part in both.

## Sub lesson 1 — Posts — the team's written conversation

**Posts** are the team's shared written conversation: the teacher's announcements, questions asked where everyone can learn from the answer, and pupils' replies. A post is public inside the team — written for the whole class to read, in the class's respectful language — which is why Kuwait teaches posts as a school behaviour, not only a button: what you post in the team, the team keeps.

## Sub lesson 2 — Files — what the teacher adds to the team

The team's **files** are its shelves: documents, worksheets and materials the **teacher adds** for the class to open, read and use. The pupil's skills are finding a file by its name and place, opening the right one (not yesterday's version), and knowing that team files belong to the class — you take your copy or read them in place; you do not tidy the teacher's shelves away.
