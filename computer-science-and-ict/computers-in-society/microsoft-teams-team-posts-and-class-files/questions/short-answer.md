# Microsoft Teams — Team Posts and Class Files — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are posts in a team?**
**Answer: The team's shared written conversation — announcements, questions and replies**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who can read a post?**
**Answer: The whole team — a post is public inside the team**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Kuwait teach posts as a behaviour?**
**Answer: Because what you post in the team, the team keeps — it must be written respectfully**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) What are a team's files?**
**Answer: Its shelves — documents and materials the teacher adds for the class**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Name the pupil's file skills.**
**Answer: Find a file by name and place, and open the right one**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the right version matter?**
**Answer: Yesterday's version may not be the material the teacher means now**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Who do team files belong to?**
**Answer: The class — the pupil reads or copies; the shelves are the teacher's**

**8. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a question asked as a post help more than a private one?**
**Answer: Everyone in the team can learn from its answer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which two commons does this lesson teach?**
**Answer: Posts (the conversation) and files (the shelves)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this lesson?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 4 Digital Explorer Term 1, Teams Lesson 5**
