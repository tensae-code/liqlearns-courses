# Bangladesh's Cyber Laws — the ICT Act and the Digital Security Act — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which law was Bangladesh's first for the digital world?**
**Answer: The ICT Act 2006**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What legal recognition did the ICT Act give?**
**Answer: To electronic records and signatures**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two crimes the ICT Act made punishable.**
**Answer: Hacking and data theft (also damaging data, harmful electronic publishing)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does every later cyber law build on?**
**Answer: The ICT Act's foundation**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Who judges a digital crime?**
**Answer: A real court**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the 2018 law.**
**Answer: The Digital Security Act 2018**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two things the 2018 Act covers.**
**Answer: Digital fraud and identity theft (also defamation, harmful content)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which 2012 Act stands beside it?**
**Answer: The Pornography Control Act 2012**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What agency matches the 2018 Act?**
**Answer: The Digital Security Agency**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the citizen's lesson?**
**Answer: Online is not outside the law**
