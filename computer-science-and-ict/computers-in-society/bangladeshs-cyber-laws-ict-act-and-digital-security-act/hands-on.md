# Hands-on — Bangladesh's Cyber Laws — the ICT Act and the Digital Security Act

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Which law, which act?** — Take five digital wrongs from the course's ethics lessons. For each, name the Bangladeshi law it falls under and what the law protects.

## Task two
**A citizen's card** — Design a one-page card for your school: the three laws, one line each on what they forbid, and where a victim reports.
