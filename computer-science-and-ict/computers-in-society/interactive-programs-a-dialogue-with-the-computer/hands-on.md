# Hands-on — Interactive Programs — a Dialogue with the Computer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spot the dialogue
List every program you used this week. For each, write the turns of its dialogue: what it asked, what you answered, what it did because of it. Mark any program that was not interactive at all, and say what it was instead.

## Design a dialogue
On paper, design a small interactive program — a quiz or a helper. Write its first three questions, two answers a user might give to each, and what the program does for each answer. Trade with a partner and walk their dialogue, hunting for an answer they did not plan for.
