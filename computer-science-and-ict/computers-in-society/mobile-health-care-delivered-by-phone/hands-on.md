# Hands-on — Mobile health — care delivered by phone

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The Ethiopia assessment
Do the guide's own task: research and list the medical apps and phone health services that actually exist in Ethiopia — a clinic's SMS reminders, a consultation app, a records system. One line of evidence per item; unverified claims stay off the list.

## One visit, two routes
Take a real clinic visit (yours or a family member's, told with permission). Split it on paper: what had to happen in the room, and what the guide's tools could have carried by phone. The split is the lesson's map.
