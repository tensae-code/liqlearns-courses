# Mobile health — care delivered by phone — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mobile health is —
a) a phone brand  b) using technological tools to track health, collect health data and deliver care remotely  c) a hospital building  d) a kind of medicine
**Answer: b) using technological tools to track health, collect health data and deliver care remotely** — the guide's definition (teacher guide — evidence).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Health programmes by phone can be run —
a) only inside clinics  b) outside the clinic, by nurse leaders, doctors and patients  c) only abroad  d) only by software firms
**Answer: b) outside the clinic, by nurse leaders, doctors and patients** — the guide's carriers (teacher guide — evidence).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's content comes from —
a) the student book  b) the teacher guide alone, as labelled evidence  c) a website  d) the internet generally
**Answer: b) the teacher guide alone, as labelled evidence** — honesty about its source.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Medical apps help professionals with —
a) only games  b) health records, patient communication, consultation and monitoring  c) only billing  d) only transport
**Answer: b) health records, patient communication, consultation and monitoring** — from the guide's list of eight (teacher guide — evidence).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide sends pupils to assess —
a) phone prices  b) which medical apps and services actually exist in Ethiopia  c) hospital buildings  d) radio health shows
**Answer: b) which medical apps and services actually exist in Ethiopia** — its own research question (teacher guide — evidence).
