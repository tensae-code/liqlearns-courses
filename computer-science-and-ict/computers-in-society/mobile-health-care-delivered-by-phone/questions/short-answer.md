# Mobile health — care delivered by phone — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define mobile health.
**Answer:** Using technological tools to track health, collect health data and deliver care remotely (teacher guide — evidence).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name five of the guide's eight professional uses of medical apps.
**Answer:** Any five of: managing information and time; keeping and accessing health records; communicating with patients; consulting other professionals; gathering references; managing and monitoring patients; making clinical decisions; learning and practising medicine (teacher guide — evidence).
