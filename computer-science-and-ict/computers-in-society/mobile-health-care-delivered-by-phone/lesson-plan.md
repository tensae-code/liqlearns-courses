# Lesson plan — Mobile health — care delivered by phone

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** Mobile health — care delivered by phone
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 14 — Computers in Society of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 12, Unit 1 — Information Systems and Their Applications. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** mobile health (mHealth) uses technological tools to track health, collect health data and deliver care remotely — medical apps now help professionals manage information and time, records, communication, consultation, monitoring, decisions and learning.
