# Hands-on — Respecting Diversity in a Digital World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Grow the tree** — Make your diversity card and add it to a class tree (wall or digital). Write one sentence on what the finished tree shows that no single card could.

## Task two
**Respect rewrite** — Take three mocking comments (invented). Rewrite each as curiosity or respect, keeping the same topic.
