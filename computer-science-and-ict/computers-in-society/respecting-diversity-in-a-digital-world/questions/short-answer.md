# Respecting Diversity in a Digital World — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is hung on the diversity tree?**
**Answer: Each learner's diversity card**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three differences a card can carry.**
**Answer: Language, birthplace and festivals (also family, foods, skills, fingerprint, voice)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do the cards show together?**
**Answer: The class's true shape — differences sharing one root**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is diversity, in the lesson's first teaching?**
**Answer: Not a problem — what the class is made of**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the tree's shared root?**
**Answer: The class itself**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does the digital world widen the class?**
**Answer: Every screen holds people more different than anyone in your street**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the tree's rule online?**
**Answer: Difference is met with curiosity and respect, never mockery**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two places the rule applies.**
**Answer: Comments and group chats (also forwards)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What can travel as fast as mockery?**
**Answer: Respect**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who chooses what rides on your name online?**
**Answer: You do**
