# Lesson plan — Respecting Diversity in a Digital World

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** Respecting Diversity in a Digital World
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Bangladesh (2026-11-14) — in the title Computers in Society on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Bangladesh's NCTB student books (Bangladesh Grade 6 Digital Technology), because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree and live lesson content were checked for the slug and for near matches before building.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** respecting diversity in a digital world: meeting classmates' and families' differences — told by fingerprint and voice, language, place and custom — and carrying respect for them into digital life, through Bangladesh's Grade 6 diversity tree (Grade 6 Digital Technology, Learning Experience 9).
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The diversity tree — differences made visible** — Each learner adds their **diversity card** to the class **tree**: the differences that make them themselves — the fingerprint and voice of the companion identity lesson, family, language, birthplace, foods, festivals, skills. Hung together, the cards show the class its true shape: not one kind of person repeated, but a tree of differences that share one root — the class itself. The lesson's first teaching is simple sight: diversity is not a problem the class has; it is what the class is made of.
2. **Respect online — the tree's rule carried out** — The digital world widens the class to the world: every screen holds people more different from you than anyone in your street. The tree's rule travels: **difference is met with curiosity and respect, never mockery** — in comments, in group chats, in what we forward about other people's customs, languages and beliefs. The same technology that carries mockery fastest can carry respect just as far, and the Grade 6 grower of the tree is the citizen who chooses which one rides on their name.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — No two fingerprints in your class match. No two voices, no two families, no two home languages in the story of one classroom. Grade 6's final experience builds a tree of those differences — and this lesson asks what the tree teaches the digital world its growers are entering. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of the diversity tree — differences made visible as the lesson describes it — the student studies the picture and names what each part shows.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of respect online — the tree's rule carried out in action — the student watches once, then says what changed and why.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key names and definitions aloud, in the order the lesson gives them — the student listens once through, then reads the same lines aloud along with the voice. (Audio is a described slot in this build; no audio file is made.)
3. **Say it to your companion** — the companion block in lesson/lesson.md.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-false, matching, short-answer — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.
