# Using Information to Solve Problems — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a problem, in information terms?**
**Answer: A gap — some things are known, others are needed, and the answer sits across the gap**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first step?**
**Answer: State the problem exactly, in your own words**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What question comes before solving?**
**Answer: What information is missing — what must be found out first?**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does naming the missing information matter?**
**Answer: It gives the search a target — it is half the solution**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a solver who cannot name the missing information show?**
**Answer: That they do not yet understand the problem**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are the four steps, in order?**
**Answer: Find the information, judge it, use it, then solve and check**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where can needed information be found?**
**Answer: In books, on the Internet, by asking people, and by measuring**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What three judgements are made on found information?**
**Answer: Is it trustworthy, is it current, and is it really about this problem?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is checked at the end?**
**Answer: That the answer really answers the problem that was stated**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which parts stay human work when a computer helps?**
**Answer: The judging of the information and the using of it**
