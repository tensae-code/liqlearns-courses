# Hands-on — ICS and Its Relationship with Other Subjects

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The two-arrows map
Draw a circle in the middle of your page and write ICS inside it. Around it, write the subjects on your own timetable. For each subject, draw two arrows: one pointing into ICS, labelled with what ICS takes from that subject, and one pointing out of ICS, labelled with what that subject uses a computer for. Fill in at least four subjects.

## One school day, many meetings
Write down your school day, lesson by lesson. Mark every place where a computer could help that lesson — calculating, storing, drawing, finding, sharing — and every place where that lesson gives something back to ICS. Count the meetings you found.
