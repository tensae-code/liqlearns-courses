# ICS and Its Relationship with Other Subjects — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ICS stands alone, with nothing to do with the other school subjects.**
**Answer: False** — ICS is tied to the other subjects in both directions.

**2. Everything a computer stores, shows and sends is written in a language.**
**Answer: True** — Words come from the languages, and information is carried in words.

**3. Business Studies can use computers to keep records of money, stock and sales.**
**Answer: True** — Record-keeping is one of the things other subjects take from ICS.

**4. The other subjects give things to ICS, but ICS gives nothing back to them.**
**Answer: False** — They use its tools in return: calculating, mapping, recording, drawing and sharing.
