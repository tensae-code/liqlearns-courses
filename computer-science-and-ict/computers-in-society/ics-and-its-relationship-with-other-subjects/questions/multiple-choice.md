# ICS and Its Relationship with Other Subjects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What does ICS stand for?**
a) International Computer School  b) Information and Computer Studies  c) Internet and Communication Systems
**Answer: b) Information and Computer Studies** — That is the subject's full name.

**2. The relationship between ICS and the other subjects runs —**
a) One way only: ICS helps them  b) One way only: they help ICS  c) Both ways
**Answer: c) Both ways** — ICS takes ideas and tools from the other subjects, and they use its tools in return.

**3. Which subject does ICS take numbers, calculation and step-by-step thinking from?**
a) Art  b) Mathematics  c) Geography
**Answer: b) Mathematics** — A computer works with numbers, and numbers come from Mathematics.

**4. What does Geography use computers for?**
a) Drawing maps and storing facts about places  b) Cooking food  c) Playing football
**Answer: a) Drawing maps and storing facts about places** — Maps and place data are Geography's computer work.

**5. From Science, ICS takes —**
a) Its songs  b) The habit of testing: try, observe, and check the result  c) Its maps
**Answer: b) The habit of testing: try, observe, and check the result** — Testing before trusting is Science's gift to ICS.
