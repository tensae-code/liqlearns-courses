# ICS and Its Relationship with Other Subjects — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. ICS stands for Information and ____ Studies.**
**Answer: Computer**

**2. A computer works with numbers, and numbers come from ____.**
**Answer: Mathematics**

**3. The relationship between ICS and the other subjects runs in ____ directions.**
**Answer: two / both** — ICS takes from them, and they take its tools in return.
