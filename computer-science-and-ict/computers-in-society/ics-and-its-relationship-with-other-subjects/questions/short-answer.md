# ICS and Its Relationship with Other Subjects — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Name two things ICS takes from other subjects, and say which subject each comes from.**
**Answer:** Any two of: numbers, calculation and step-by-step thinking from Mathematics; the habit of testing — try, observe, check — from Science; words themselves from the languages, since everything a computer stores, shows and sends is written and read.

**2. Your teacher keeps the class marks on a computer. Which relationship direction is that, and why?**
**Answer:** The return direction — another subject (or the school's own work) using an ICS tool, the computer, to store and work with its information.

**3. Why does the Form One book teach this relationship before any hardware?**
**Answer:** Because when the learner knows where ICS sits among the subjects they already know, every later lesson has somewhere to stand — the new subject is tied to familiar ground first.
