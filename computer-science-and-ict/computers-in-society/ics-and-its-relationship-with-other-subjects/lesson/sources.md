Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Tanzania, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked first: Computers in Society holds lessons on information systems, e-services and digital life, but none teaches how the school subject ICS relates to the other school subjects. The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — computers-in-society/ics-and-its-relationship-with-other-subjects). It sits in the Computers in Society title (computers-in-society/). It is one whole lesson with no sub lessons — a short, single-skill lesson (the 2026-11-09 ruling): the two directions of the relationship are one idea learned together, not sequential parts.

Tanzania's own way: a concept-first TIE book (2005 ICS syllabus) — the Form One book opens with the concept of Information and Computer Studies and, inside that first chapter and before any hardware, places ICS among the learner's other subjects; a full chapter on data, information dissemination and communication media also comes before any hardware, and the year closes with a practical "Computer handling" chapter (utilities, backup, security) rather than applications software. This lesson keeps that order.
