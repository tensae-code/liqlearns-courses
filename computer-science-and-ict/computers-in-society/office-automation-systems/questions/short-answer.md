# Office Automation Systems — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an Office Automation System?**
**Answer: The technologies that automate an office's routine work**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What routine work does it automate?**
**Answer: Producing documents, communicating, filing and finding**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does it free office workers to do?**
**Answer: Spend their time on judgement instead of copying and carrying**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the OAS heart — one program or a joining?**
**Answer: A joining of many tools reusing the same information**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Name four tools in the OAS toolbox.**
**Answer: Word processing, email, spreadsheets/databases and desktop publishing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do scheduling and filing tools carry?**
**Answer: The office's appointments and archives**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which levels does an OAS serve?**
**Answer: Every level at once — clerk and director**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is information typed only once in an OAS?**
**Answer: It is reused in the letter, the file and the message**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 10 ICT, Chapter 1 §11**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) Where does this system sit in the chapter?**
**Answer: Among the information-system types — TPS, MIS, DSS, EIS, expert and office systems**
