# Moodle as a Teleformación Platform — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is teleformación?**
a) Distance learning carried by a platform  b) On the platform  c) Teaches, assigns and answers  d) A course with a home, open at all hours
**Answer: a) Distance learning carried by a platform**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where do a teleformación course's materials live?**
a) Teaches, assigns and answers  b) On the platform  c) A course with a home, open at all hours  d) As preparation for university study
**Answer: b) On the platform**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the teacher do through the platform?**
a) A course with a home, open at all hours  b) As preparation for university study  c) Teaches, assigns and answers  d) Resources and activities
**Answer: c) Teaches, assigns and answers**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a teleformación course, in one image?**
a) As preparation for university study  b) Resources and activities  c) Tasks to hand in and forums (or quizzes)  d) A course with a home, open at all hours
**Answer: d) A course with a home, open at all hours**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Cuba teach it in Grade 12?**
a) As preparation for university study  b) Resources and activities  c) Tasks to hand in and forums (or quizzes)  d) They are enrolled on it
**Answer: a) As preparation for university study**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What two kinds of things fill a Moodle course's sections?**
a) Tasks to hand in and forums (or quizzes)  b) Resources and activities  c) They are enrolled on it  d) On the platform
**Answer: b) Resources and activities**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two Moodle activities.**
a) They are enrolled on it  b) On the platform  c) Tasks to hand in and forums (or quizzes)  d) The free platform its teleformación runs on
**Answer: c) Tasks to hand in and forums (or quizzes)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do pupils join a Moodle course?**
a) On the platform  b) The free platform its teleformación runs on  c) Distance learning carried by a platform  d) They are enrolled on it
**Answer: d) They are enrolled on it**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where do pupils see their grades?**
a) On the platform  b) The free platform its teleformación runs on  c) Distance learning carried by a platform  d) On the platform
**Answer: a) On the platform**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of platform is Moodle, as Cuba names it?**
a) Distance learning carried by a platform  b) The free platform its teleformación runs on  c) On the platform  d) Teaches, assigns and answers
**Answer: b) The free platform its teleformación runs on**
