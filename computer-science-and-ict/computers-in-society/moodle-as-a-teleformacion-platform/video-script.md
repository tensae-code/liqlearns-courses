# Video script — Moodle as a Teleformación Platform

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Teleformación** — distance learning through a platform — moves the course's shared life **online**: the materials live on the platform, the pupils work from wherever they are, and the teacher teaches, assigns and answers **through the same place**. It is not a pile of files sent out; it is a **course with a home**, open at all hours, that keeps the class together while its members are apart. Cuba introduces it as the way pre-university prepares for university study.
**Moodle** organises a course into **sections** holding **resources** — the materials to read, watch and download — and **activities** — the work to do: tasks to hand in, forums to discuss in, quizzes to answer. Pupils are **enrolled** on their courses, hand work in through the platform, and see their **grades** there; the teacher sees every hand-in and mark in one place. Cuba names Moodle plainly — *La plataforma Moodle* — the free platform its teleformación runs on.
