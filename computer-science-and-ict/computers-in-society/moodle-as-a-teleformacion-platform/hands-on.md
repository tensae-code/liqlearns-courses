# Hands-on — Moodle as a Teleformación Platform

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Map a Moodle course', 'Draw the home page of a Moodle course for a subject you study: its sections, one resource and two activities per section, and say where grades would appear.')

## Task two
('Room and platform', "Take one week of a real course: say what the shared room gives that the platform cannot, and what the platform gives that the room cannot. One honest item each way is enough.")
