# Information Culture — Norms, Rules and Principles Online — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The part of general culture governing a person's life with information — knowledge, skills, and habits and values**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The part of general culture governing a person's life with information — knowledge, skills, and habits and values** — as the answer to: Name its three parts.
**Answer: False — Knowledge of information, skills of seeking/judging/using it, and habits and values**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Accuracy and honesty (also: respect for others' work)**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Accuracy and honesty (also: respect for others' work)** — as the answer to: Does a person with information culture merely use the virtual space?
**Answer: False — No — they know its rules of the road and keep them unobserved too**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: As the crown of the course — its final chapter**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: As the crown of the course — its final chapter** — as the answer to: State the first norm.
**Answer: False — Judge before you trust — a source is checked, not believed at sight**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Credit — the author named and the licence kept**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Credit — the author named and the licence kept** — as the answer to: Why is forwarding a falsehood your responsibility?
**Answer: False — What you share becomes partly yours — falsehood forwarded is falsehood spread**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Yes — they do not dissolve in virtual space**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Yes — they do not dissolve in virtual space** — as the answer to: How should private information be guarded?
**Answer: False — As you would a house key — your own and others'**
