# Information Culture — Norms, Rules and Principles Online — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is information culture?**
**Answer: The part of general culture governing a person's life with information — knowledge, skills, and habits and values**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name its three parts.**
**Answer: Knowledge of information, skills of seeking/judging/using it, and habits and values**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two values the lesson names.**
**Answer: Accuracy and honesty (also: respect for others' work)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Does a person with information culture merely use the virtual space?**
**Answer: No — they know its rules of the road and keep them unobserved too**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the book place this topic?**
**Answer: As the crown of the course — its final chapter**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) State the first norm.**
**Answer: Judge before you trust — a source is checked, not believed at sight**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must happen before using another's work?**
**Answer: Credit — the author named and the licence kept**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is forwarding a falsehood your responsibility?**
**Answer: What you share becomes partly yours — falsehood forwarded is falsehood spread**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Do real-world politeness and law apply online?**
**Answer: Yes — they do not dissolve in virtual space**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How should private information be guarded?**
**Answer: As you would a house key — your own and others'**
