# Hands-on — Information Culture — Norms, Rules and Principles Online

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Norm audit** — Take one day of your own online life and mark each act against the five norms; write the one norm you broke most and its fix.

## Task two
**Culture card** — Design a one-page 'rules of the road' card for a younger pupil, turning the five norms into five short do/don't lines.
