**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** Applying the Technological Method to a Real Problem

**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The technological method in practice** — **Practical application** means **using the method on a real problem**. The technological method used in the guide has **seven methods (steps)**. It begins by **identifying and stating the problem**, and a brief is written before designing the solution.
2. **Carrying a real problem through** — The application continues: **designing and planning, then making**, keeping **records that show decisions and results**, and finally **evaluation — after making, against the brief**. If evaluation fails, the method **loops back to improve the design**. The method ends when the solution works and has been evaluated.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
