# Hands-on — Applying the Technological Method to a Real Problem

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run the seven
Pick a real broken or weak product near you. Write the seven steps of the method as seven lines, each with the action you would actually take.

## Evaluation day
Take a finished school project. Evaluate it against its brief in writing: three passes, one fail, and the loop-back improvement for the fail.
