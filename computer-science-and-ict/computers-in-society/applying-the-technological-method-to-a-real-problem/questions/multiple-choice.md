# Applying the Technological Method to a Real Problem — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does practical application mean?**
a) Using the method on a real problem  b) Identify and state the problem  c) Seven  d) Designing the solution
**Answer: a) Using the method on a real problem**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first step with a real problem?**
a) Seven  b) Identify and state the problem  c) Designing the solution  d) After making, against the brief
**Answer: b) Identify and state the problem**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The technological method in the guide has how many steps?**
a) Designing the solution  b) After making, against the brief  c) Seven  d) A broken or needs-improving product around you
**Answer: c) Seven**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A brief is written before what?**
a) After making, against the brief  b) A broken or needs-improving product around you  c) Designing and planning  d) Designing the solution
**Answer: d) Designing the solution**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When does evaluation happen?**
a) After making, against the brief  b) A broken or needs-improving product around you  c) Designing and planning  d) Loops back to improve the design
**Answer: a) After making, against the brief**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Give an example of a real problem for the method.**
a) Designing and planning  b) A broken or needs-improving product around you  c) Loops back to improve the design  d) Decisions and results
**Answer: b) A broken or needs-improving product around you**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Making a solution follows which stage?**
a) Loops back to improve the design  b) Decisions and results  c) Designing and planning  d) When the solution works and is evaluated
**Answer: c) Designing and planning**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) If evaluation fails, what does the method do?**
a) Decisions and results  b) When the solution works and is evaluated  c) Using the method on a real problem  d) Loops back to improve the design
**Answer: d) Loops back to improve the design**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do records during an application show?**
a) Decisions and results  b) When the solution works and is evaluated  c) Using the method on a real problem  d) Identify and state the problem
**Answer: a) Decisions and results**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When does the technological method end?**
a) Using the method on a real problem  b) When the solution works and is evaluated  c) Identify and state the problem  d) Seven
**Answer: b) When the solution works and is evaluated**
