# Employee Monitoring — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: An organisation watching and recording workers' use of its systems**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Protecting the network and keeping work time for work** — as the answer to: What may be recorded?
**Answer: False — Sites visited, mail sent, sometimes keys pressed or calls made**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Protecting the network and keeping work time for work**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Being secret, purposeless, or wider than its purpose needs** — as the answer to: What tools does monitoring use?
**Answer: False — Logs, filters and reports on the organisation's machines**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Being secret, purposeless, or wider than its purpose needs**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Only what the stated purpose requires** — as the answer to: What should workers be told?
**Answer: False — What is monitored, and why**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Only what the stated purpose requires**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 10 ICT, Chapter 3 §4.5** — as the answer to: What may gathered material be used for?
**Answer: False — Only the purpose it was gathered for**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 10 ICT, Chapter 3 §4.5**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: An organisation watching and recording workers' use of its systems** — as the answer to: Beside which topic is it taught?
**Answer: False — Privacy law — in the book's privacy sequence**
