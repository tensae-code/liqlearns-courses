# Employee Monitoring — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is employee monitoring?**
a) An organisation watching and recording workers' use of its systems  b) Sites visited, mail sent, sometimes keys pressed or calls made  c) Protecting the network and keeping work time for work  d) Logs, filters and reports on the organisation's machines
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What may be recorded?**
a) Protecting the network and keeping work time for work  b) Sites visited, mail sent, sometimes keys pressed or calls made  c) Logs, filters and reports on the organisation's machines  d) Being secret, purposeless, or wider than its purpose needs
**Answer: b**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two honest reasons for monitoring.**
a) Logs, filters and reports on the organisation's machines  b) Being secret, purposeless, or wider than its purpose needs  c) Protecting the network and keeping work time for work  d) What is monitored, and why
**Answer: c**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What tools does monitoring use?**
a) Being secret, purposeless, or wider than its purpose needs  b) What is monitored, and why  c) Only what the stated purpose requires  d) Logs, filters and reports on the organisation's machines
**Answer: d**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) What makes monitoring cross the privacy line?**
