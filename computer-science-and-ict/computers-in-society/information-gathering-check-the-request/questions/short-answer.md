# Information gathering — check the request before you answer it — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is information gathering disputed?
**Answer:** Because what may be gathered, and why, has no single settled answer.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What should a user examine before answering a request for credentials?
**Answer:** The intention behind the request — why this information is being asked for.
