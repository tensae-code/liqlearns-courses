# Hands-on — Information gathering — check the request before you answer it

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Request autopsy
Take one real sign-up form or app permission screen. List every item it asks for. Mark each: needed for the service, or not explained. The unexplained items are your finding.

## The pause drill
With a partner, role-play: one reads a request aloud ('to continue, enter your password'), the other must ask 'why do you need it?' before any answer. Swap five times.
