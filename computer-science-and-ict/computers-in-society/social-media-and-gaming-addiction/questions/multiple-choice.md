# Social media and gaming addiction — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The addiction is driven by —
a) boredom only  b) an uncontrollable urge to log on  c) teachers  d) slow devices
**Answer: b) an uncontrollable urge to log on** — the engine of the pattern.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The measure of the addiction is —
a) how fun it is  b) so much time and effort that other important parts of life are damaged  c) the price of the phone  d) the number of friends
**Answer: b) so much time and effort that other important parts of life are damaged** — displacement is the test.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Being _______ with social media and games is part of the definition.
a) overly concerned  b) slightly aware  c) paid to play  d) forced
**Answer: a) overly concerned** — the definition's first phrase.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Research links intensive use to harm to —
a) eyes only  b) young people's mental health  c) batteries  d) prices
**Answer: b) young people's mental health** — the researched harm.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A pupil's use connects directly to —
a) family relationships, learning, future goals and mental health  b) only grades  c) only sleep  d) only friends
**Answer: a) family relationships, learning, future goals and mental health** — the four named stakes.
