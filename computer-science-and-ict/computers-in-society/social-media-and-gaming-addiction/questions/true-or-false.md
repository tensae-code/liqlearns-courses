# Social media and gaming addiction — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Playing a game you enjoy is, by itself, addiction.
**Answer: False** — the test is the uncontrollable urge and the damage done.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Watching your own use is part of what the book teaches.
**Answer: True** — pupils are told to watch it, with the stakes named.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Intensive use has no researched link to mental-health harm.
**Answer: False** — research links the two.
