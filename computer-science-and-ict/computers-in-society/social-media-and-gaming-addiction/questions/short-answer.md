# Social media and gaming addiction — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the three parts of the addiction test.
**Answer:** Being overly concerned with social media and games; an uncontrollable urge to log on; so much time and effort that other important parts of life are damaged.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four things a pupil's use connects to.
**Answer:** Family relationships, learning, future goals and mental health.
