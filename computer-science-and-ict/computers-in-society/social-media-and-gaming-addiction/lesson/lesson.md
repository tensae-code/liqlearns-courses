# Social media and gaming addiction

## Do it first

How long was your longest session this week — honestly? And what did it push aside? This lesson gives the pattern a precise name, so it can be seen while it is still small.

## Sub lesson 1 — What the addiction is

This addiction is **being overly concerned with social media and games, driven by an uncontrollable urge to log on**.

The measure is the damage: giving them **so much time and effort that other important parts of life are damaged**. Enjoyment is not the test — displacement is.

## Sub lesson 2 — The harm — and watching your own use

**Research links intensive use to harm to young people's mental health.** The pull is engineered to feel light; the cost lands heavily.

So pupils are told to **watch their own use**, because it connects directly to **family relationships, learning, future goals and mental health**. Four things worth more than a feed — named so the trade can be seen.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the test for addiction, then apply it aloud to one habit — yours or an invented one — and give the verdict with reasons.

## Do It Yourself

1. For three days, note every session: start, end, what it displaced. Total the time; name the displaced things.
2. Set one boundary (a time, a place, or an app order — school first) and keep it for the three days. Write what changed.
