# Hands-on — Social media and gaming addiction

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The displaced-things diary
Three days, every session logged with what it pushed aside. The log is the lesson: addiction becomes visible as a list of displaced things, not as a feeling.

## Boundary week
Choose one boundary and one person to tell. Keep it a week; the telling is part of the keeping. Report honestly at the end — kept, broken, or changed — and what each day cost.
