# Microsoft Teams — the School Learning Platform — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The national school learning platform — where the class meets online**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Because the platform is where the class meets — a pupil who cannot open it is locked out** — as the answer to: Where is Teams installed from?
**Answer: False — The device's app store, by its real name and maker**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Because the platform is where the class meets — a pupil who cannot open it is locked out**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Your teams, classes and assignments — the ones your account belongs to** — as the answer to: What account does a Kuwaiti pupil sign in with?
**Answer: False — The school account given by the Ministry (moe.edu.kw)**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Your teams, classes and assignments — the ones your account belongs to**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: From school and from home** — as the answer to: What is a digital learning platform?
**Answer: False — Lessons, meetings, files and homework gathered in one signed-in place**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: From school and from home**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The 'Digital Classroom' front section of Kuwait's Digital Explorer books** — as the answer to: What must be done once after installing?
**Answer: False — Open the program once so it is ready for use**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The 'Digital Classroom' front section of Kuwait's Digital Explorer books**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The national school learning platform — where the class meets online** — as the answer to: What comes after signing in, in the book's order?
**Answer: False — Settings, then teams and assignments, then meetings and team posts**
