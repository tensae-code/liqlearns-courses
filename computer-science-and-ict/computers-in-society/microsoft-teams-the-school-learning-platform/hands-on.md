# Hands-on — Microsoft Teams — the School Learning Platform

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Install and enter', 'Install Teams (or find it already installed), open it, and sign in with your school account. Write the three steps in order.')

## Task two
('Whose classroom is it?', 'Sign in and list the teams your account belongs to. Say in one sentence why another pupil's account would show different teams.')
