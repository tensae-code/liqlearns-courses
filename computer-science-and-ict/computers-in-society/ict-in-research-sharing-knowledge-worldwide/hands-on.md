# Hands-on — ICT in Research — Sharing Knowledge Worldwide

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Simulate on paper** — Choose one thing that is expensive to test for real — a bridge load, a flood, a new medicine. Write what a simulation of it must model, and one way its result could be checked against reality.

## Task two
**Judge two sources** — Find two online sources about one discovery. Say which deserves more trust and name the checks you used.
