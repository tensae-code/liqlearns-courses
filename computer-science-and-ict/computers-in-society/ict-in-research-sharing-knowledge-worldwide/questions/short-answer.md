# ICT in Research — Sharing Knowledge Worldwide — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does research begin with, in this lesson?**
**Answer: Data — measurements and records**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a simulation?**
**Answer: A model of the real thing run inside a computer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why simulate before building?**
**Answer: So ideas can fail cheaply and safely first**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a simulation honest or dishonest?**
**Answer: The model inside it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How are simulations checked?**
**Answer: Against the real world, whenever possible**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is an e-journal?**
**Answer: A research journal published and read electronically**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do shared databases let research teams do?**
**Answer: Work on the same data from different continents**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What are open patent databases for?**
**Answer: Checking whether an idea is already claimed**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What skill grows as sources flood in?**
**Answer: Judging which sources deserve trust**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How fast does ICT share a new finding?**
**Answer: At once, to every connected desk**
