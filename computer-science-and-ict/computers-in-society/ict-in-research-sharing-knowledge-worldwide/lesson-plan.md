# Lesson plan — ICT in Research — Sharing Knowledge Worldwide

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** ICT in Research — Sharing Knowledge Worldwide
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Bangladesh (2026-11-14) — in the title Computers in Society on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). **The one thing this lesson teaches:** ICT in research: how computers serve the search for new knowledge — holding data, running simulations, and opening the world's journals and patent records to any researcher (Bangladesh HSC ICT, Chapter 1, §1.1.5).
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Data and simulation — the laboratory inside the computer** — Research begins with **data** — measurements and records too many for any notebook — and computers hold, sort and analyse them. Then comes **simulation**: a model of the real thing — a bridge, a drug, a storm, a market — run inside the computer so ideas can be tested, and fail, cheaply and safely before anyone builds or risks the real one. A simulation is only as honest as the model inside it, which is why researchers check its results against the real world whenever they can.
2. **E-journals and open records — the world's library open** — Research is shared, and ICT shares it at village speed: **e-journals** put new findings on every connected desk at once; shared databases let teams on different continents work on the same data; and **open patent databases** let an inventor check whether an idea is already claimed before spending a year on it. The researcher's skill grows with the flood: not finding *a* source, but judging which sources deserve trust — the verification skill this course teaches in its own lessons.
