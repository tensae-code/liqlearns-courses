# Hands-on — Organising an Online Fair

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Write the constitution** — In a team, write your online fair's full guidelines: purpose, exhibitors, exhibits, judging, conduct, and the weak-connection plan.

## Task two
**Run a mini-fair** — Hold a three-exhibit online fair for another class. Keep the records and write the closing review.
