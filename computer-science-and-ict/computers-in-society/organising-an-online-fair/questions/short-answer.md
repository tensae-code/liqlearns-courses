# Organising an Online Fair — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an online fair?**
**Answer: A fair of exhibits and visitors that happens on screens**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must be written before the first stall?**
**Answer: The guidelines — the fair's constitution**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name three things the guidelines settle.**
**Answer: Who may exhibit, what exhibits contain, and how work is judged (also visitor movement, conduct)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name one digital rule the guidelines must carry.**
**Answer: Working links (also honest authorship, safe conduct, a weak-connection plan)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which earlier lessons stand behind honest authorship?**
**Answer: The plagiarism lessons**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name two things that happen at the fair itself.**
**Answer: Presentations at set times and judging by the published rules**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What records do organisers keep?**
**Answer: Who exhibited, who visited, what was judged best**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does the fair close?**
**Answer: With a review, like the campaign**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the online fair's gift?**
**Answer: Reach — visitors far beyond the school gate**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two questions does the review ask?**
**Answer: What reach gave that a hall could not — and what the hall still does better**
