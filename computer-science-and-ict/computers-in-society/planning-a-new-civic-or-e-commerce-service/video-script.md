# Video script — Planning a New Civic or E-Commerce Service

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A new service stands on **prerequisites**: a real need it answers, the people it serves, the offices, devices and connections it requires, the staff and their skills, and the money to run after the launch day. The **service-delivery plan** then walks one citizen through it: where they apply, what happens to the application at each desk or screen, what they receive, and how long each step may take. A plan that cannot walk a citizen from door to done is a wish, not a plan.
The plan must then persuade: the **proposal** argues the service's case — the need, the plan, the cost, the benefit — to the people who can approve and fund it. And the **planning poster** compresses the whole design onto one public sheet: the service, its users, its steps, its needs. The poster is the plan's honesty test — what cannot be drawn clearly has not been thought clearly — and the class presents and defends it, question by question, as a real service team must.
