# Hands-on — Planning a New Civic or E-Commerce Service

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Plan the service** — In a team, choose a missing local service. Write its prerequisites and walk one citizen through its delivery plan, timing every step.

## Task two
**Poster and proposal** — Make the planning poster and a one-page proposal. Present both; record the three hardest questions and your answers.
