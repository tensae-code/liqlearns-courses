# Planning a New Civic or E-Commerce Service — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Prerequisites and the delivery plan
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A real need, its people and its required offices/devices (also staff skills, running money); One citizen's whole journey, door to done; What the application meets at each desk or screen, and how long it may take; A wish, not a plan; The money to run after launch day.

## Sub lesson 2 — The proposal and the planning poster
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The need, the plan, the cost and the benefit; The people who can approve and fund the service; The whole design — service, users, steps, needs — onto one sheet; What cannot be drawn clearly has not been thought clearly; Presented and answered question by question.
