# Planning a New Civic or E-Commerce Service — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A real need, its people and its required offices/devices (also staff skills, running money)**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A real need, its people and its required offices/devices (also staff skills, running money)** — as the answer to: What does the delivery plan walk through?
**Answer: False — One citizen's whole journey, door to done**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: What the application meets at each desk or screen, and how long it may take**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: What the application meets at each desk or screen, and how long it may take** — as the answer to: What is a plan that cannot walk a citizen?
**Answer: False — A wish, not a plan**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The money to run after launch day**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The money to run after launch day** — as the answer to: What does the proposal argue?
**Answer: False — The need, the plan, the cost and the benefit**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The people who can approve and fund the service**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The people who can approve and fund the service** — as the answer to: What does the planning poster compress?
**Answer: False — The whole design — service, users, steps, needs — onto one sheet**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: What cannot be drawn clearly has not been thought clearly**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: What cannot be drawn clearly has not been thought clearly** — as the answer to: How is the poster defended?
**Answer: False — Presented and answered question by question**
