# Planning a New Civic or E-Commerce Service — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name three prerequisites of a new service.**
**Answer: A real need, its people and its required offices/devices (also staff skills, running money)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the delivery plan walk through?**
**Answer: One citizen's whole journey, door to done**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens at each step of the delivery plan?**
**Answer: What the application meets at each desk or screen, and how long it may take**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a plan that cannot walk a citizen?**
**Answer: A wish, not a plan**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What money is easiest to forget?**
**Answer: The money to run after launch day**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the proposal argue?**
**Answer: The need, the plan, the cost and the benefit**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Who is the proposal for?**
**Answer: The people who can approve and fund the service**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the planning poster compress?**
**Answer: The whole design — service, users, steps, needs — onto one sheet**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the poster an honesty test?**
**Answer: What cannot be drawn clearly has not been thought clearly**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is the poster defended?**
**Answer: Presented and answered question by question**
