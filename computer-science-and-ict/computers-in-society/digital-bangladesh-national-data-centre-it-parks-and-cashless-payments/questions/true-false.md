# Digital Bangladesh — National Data Centre, IT Parks and Cashless Payments — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Government data and services, on home soil**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Government data and services, on home soil** — as the answer to: What are hi-tech parks for?
**Answer: False — Gathering IT companies with connections and power**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Young technology firms**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Young technology firms** — as the answer to: What do Made-in-Bangladesh laptops prove?
**Answer: False — The country assembles its own computers**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The country's data lives under its own keeping**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The country's data lives under its own keeping** — as the answer to: What is Cashless Bangladesh built on?
**Answer: False — Bangladesh Bank's QR-based payments**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Scan a code; money moves account to account**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Scan a code; money moves account to account** — as the answer to: Name two gifts of cashless payment.
**Answer: False — A record that is harder to steal, and lower handling cost than cash**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Sellers like the tea stall and the rickshaw**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Sellers like the tea stall and the rickshaw** — as the answer to: Name two e-services riding on the foundations.
**Answer: False — E-agriculture and e-governance (also e-services generally)**
