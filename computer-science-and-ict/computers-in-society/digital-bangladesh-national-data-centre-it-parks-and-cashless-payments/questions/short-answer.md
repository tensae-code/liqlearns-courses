# Digital Bangladesh — National Data Centre, IT Parks and Cashless Payments — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the National Data Centre hold?**
**Answer: Government data and services, on home soil**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are hi-tech parks for?**
**Answer: Gathering IT companies with connections and power**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does Startup Bangladesh back?**
**Answer: Young technology firms**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do Made-in-Bangladesh laptops prove?**
**Answer: The country assembles its own computers**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does home-soil data matter?**
**Answer: The country's data lives under its own keeping**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is Cashless Bangladesh built on?**
**Answer: Bangladesh Bank's QR-based payments**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does a QR payment work?**
**Answer: Scan a code; money moves account to account**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two gifts of cashless payment.**
**Answer: A record that is harder to steal, and lower handling cost than cash**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which small sellers does it bring into the recorded economy?**
**Answer: Sellers like the tea stall and the rickshaw**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name two e-services riding on the foundations.**
**Answer: E-agriculture and e-governance (also e-services generally)**
