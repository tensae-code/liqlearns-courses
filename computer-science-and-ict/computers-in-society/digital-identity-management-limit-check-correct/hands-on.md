# Hands-on — Digital identity management — limit, check and correct your data

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The one-account clean-up
Choose one account and do the full pass with the lesson open: limit, check, correct, de-duplicate — then run the guide's six tips down the same account. Write what changed; the list is the evidence.

## Teach the tips
Turn the guide's six tips into a one-page guide for a grandparent or a younger sibling, in their language register. Test it on a real person and fix the tip they misunderstand.
