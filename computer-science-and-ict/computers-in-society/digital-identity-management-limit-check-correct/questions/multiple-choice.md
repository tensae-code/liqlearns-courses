# Digital identity management — limit, check and correct your data — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Managing a digital identity includes —
a) giving out more data  b) limiting the data a person gives out  c) never checking records  d) keeping duplicates
**Answer: b) limiting the data a person gives out** — the first verb.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Incorrect personal data should be —
a) ignored  b) corrected  c) shared  d) sold
**Answer: b) corrected** — correction is a named action.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Redundant data should be —
a) multiplied  b) avoided  c) printed  d) hidden
**Answer: b) avoided** — duplicates are waste and risk.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Identity can be verified by —
a) guessing  b) database checks, document verification and biometric verification  c) asking friends  d) lottery
**Answer: b) database checks, document verification and biometric verification** — the named routes.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide warns care with disclosing —
a) your favourite colour  b) birthdate, age or location  c) your school name only  d) nothing
**Answer: b) birthdate, age or location** — data-mining firms and identity thieves use them (teacher guide — evidence).
