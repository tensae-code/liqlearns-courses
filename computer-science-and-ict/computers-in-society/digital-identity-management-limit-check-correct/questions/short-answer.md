# Digital identity management — limit, check and correct your data — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the management actions the book names.
**Answer:** Limit the data given out; avoid excessive storage; check what data is stored about you; correct incorrect personal data; avoid redundant data.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give three of the guide's safety tips.
**Answer:** Any three of: learn each platform's privacy options and check settings regularly; guard birthdate, age and location; use third-party programs with caution; restrict what a stranger-friend can see; extra care after stalking, harassment or abuse; log out of accounts no longer needed (teacher guide — evidence).
