# Hands-on — Verifying Information Before You Share It

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**The two-minute drill** — Take one forwarded message. Run the three checks with a timer running, using one technology tool. Record each check's result and your final decision: share, hold or correct.

## Task two
**Correct the chain** — Write the correction message you would send back for one false claim — polite, sourced, and short enough to be forwarded itself.
