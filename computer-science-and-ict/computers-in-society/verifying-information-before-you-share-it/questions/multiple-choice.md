# Verifying Information Before You Share It — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the three first verification questions?**
a) Source, impartiality and agreement  b) Who first said this, and would they know  c) Whether more than one side is shown  d) Independent sources saying the same in their own words
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the source check ask?**

**Answer: a**
