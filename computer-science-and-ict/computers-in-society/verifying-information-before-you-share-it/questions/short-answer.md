# Verifying Information Before You Share It — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the three first verification questions?**
**Answer: Source, impartiality and agreement**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the source check ask?**
**Answer: Who first said this, and would they know**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the impartiality check look for?**
**Answer: Whether more than one side is shown**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the agreement check need?**
**Answer: Independent sources saying the same in their own words**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What has a claim earned when it passes all three?**
**Answer: Forwarding**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does reverse image search show?**
**Answer: Whether a photo is old or from another place**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What kills most recycled scares?**
**Answer: Checking the original date and context**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three share decisions.**
**Answer: Share, hold, or correct**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is holding an unchecked message?**
**Answer: A kindness — it stops the chain**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where should a correction be sent?**
**Answer: Back along the chain it arrived by**
