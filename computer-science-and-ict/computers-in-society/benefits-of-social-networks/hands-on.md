# Hands-on — Benefits of social networks

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Benefit diary
For one day, note every time you or your family use the internet well. In the evening, sort the notes under the five benefits. Which benefit fills first? Which is empty — and what proper use would fill it?

## Teach the five
Teach the five benefits to someone at home, with one example each from your family's own use. Teaching them is the assessment the guide names: listing the benefits, from understanding.
