# Benefits of social networks

## Do it first

List everything good that came to you through a screen in the last month: a fact you learned, a person you reached, something you made, a game you enjoyed. Keep the list — the lesson's benefits will be checked against it, item by item.

## What proper use brings

**Used properly**, the internet — and its social networks — brings real benefits. **Children and adults** use it to **search for information**; to **communicate and express ideas, and share information and experiences**; to **interact socially with friends and family — including teachers**; to **innovate, create and share content**; and to **play and be entertained — games, movies, music and books**.

[IMAGE 11737 — five benefits around one pupil]

## The word that carries it all — properly

Every benefit in this lesson hangs on two words: **used properly**. The same networks, used improperly, carry the risks of the next lesson. Proper use is not a different internet — it is the same internet, used well.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the five benefits, with one example of each from your own life.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the five benefits from memory.
2. For each benefit, write one real example — yours or your family's.
3. Write which benefit a class group chat with your teacher belongs to, and why.
