# Lesson plan — Benefits of social networks

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** Benefits of social networks
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 14 — Computers in Society of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 4 — Internet. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** used properly, social networks and the internet let children and adults search for information, communicate and share ideas and experiences, interact socially — with teachers among others — innovate, create and share content, and play and be entertained.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the platform lessons of this band: the learner knows what the networks are; this lesson gathers what good use of them brings.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Social networks — connecting in virtual communities. Recommended, never forced. A learner can land here directly; the lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the five benefits: searching, communicating and sharing, social interaction, creating, play and entertainment.
2. Give a real example of each benefit.
3. State the condition the benefits hang on: used properly.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Lists only entertainment → run the five in order: search, communicate and share, interact, create, be entertained — entertainment is one of five, not the whole.
- Forgets the condition → every benefit sentence in this lesson begins, in meaning, with 'used properly'.
