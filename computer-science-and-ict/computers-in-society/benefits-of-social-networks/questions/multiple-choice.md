# Benefits of social networks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The benefits in this lesson come when the internet is —
a) used properly  b) used all night  c) used secretly  d) never used
**Answer: a) used properly**

**2.** Which of these is a named benefit?
a) searching for information  b) hiding from family  c) arguing with strangers  d) skipping school
**Answer: a) searching for information**

**3.** Children and adults use the internet to communicate and —
a) waste money  b) express ideas and share information and experiences  c) avoid all people  d) break rules
**Answer: b) express ideas and share information and experiences**

**4.** Social interaction online includes interacting with —
a) teachers as well as friends and family  b) nobody real  c) only celebrities  d) only businesses
**Answer: a) teachers as well as friends and family**

**5.** Play and entertainment online includes —
a) only homework  b) games, movies, music and books  c) only adverts  d) nothing enjoyable
**Answer: b) games, movies, music and books**
