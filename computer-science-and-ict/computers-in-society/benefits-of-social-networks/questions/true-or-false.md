# Benefits of social networks — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** Innovating, creating and sharing content is one of the benefits.
**Answer: True — innovate, create and share content is named.**

**2.** The benefits belong to adults only, not children.
**Answer: False — children and adults are both named.**

**3.** The same networks can carry risks when used improperly.
**Answer: True — proper and improper use are the two sides of this set of lessons.**
