# A Cross-Field Computing Project — the Smart Waste Project — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a cross-field project?**
**Answer: A project that takes a problem from another field and answers it with computing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is Indonesia's Grade 12 project theme?**
**Answer: Waste (sampah)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three example systems the book's groups build.**
**Answer: A smart trash bin on Arduino, a waste-bank application, and a compost application**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a waste bank?**
**Answer: A scheme where sorted waste is weighed, recorded and exchanged**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the smart trash bin use?**
**Answer: Arduino, with sensors that sense and respond**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What records the work as it happens?**
**Answer: The group journal**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the journal protect the report from?**
**Answer: Being written from forgetting — the journal is the project's memory**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What three products close the project?**
**Answer: A report, a presentation, and peer assessment**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does peer assessment judge?**
**Answer: Each member's contribution to the group**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is a computing system truly finished?**
**Answer: When other people can understand it, use it, and judge whether it works**
