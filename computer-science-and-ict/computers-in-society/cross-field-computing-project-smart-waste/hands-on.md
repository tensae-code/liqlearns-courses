# Hands-on — A Cross-Field Computing Project — the Smart Waste Project

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Design your own cross-field project** — Choose a real problem from another field in your school (water, queues, lost property). Sketch a computing system for it: what it senses, what it computes, what it shows — and name the journal entries you would keep in week one.

## Task two
**Judge a project** — Take any finished group project you know. Assess it as the book does: from its journal (or its absence), its report, and a presentation — and give each member's contribution one honest line.
