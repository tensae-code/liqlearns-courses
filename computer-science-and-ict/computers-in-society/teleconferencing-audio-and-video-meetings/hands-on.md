# Hands-on — Teleconferencing — audio and video meetings

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Two meetings audit
Recall one voice-only call and one video meeting you have joined or seen. Score each: could you tell who was speaking? Could you read faces? Write which jobs each kind suits.

## Assignment by conference
With classmates, plan one group assignment to be finished entirely by teleconference: roles, times, audio or video for each session, and how the final work is gathered (teacher guide — evidence).
