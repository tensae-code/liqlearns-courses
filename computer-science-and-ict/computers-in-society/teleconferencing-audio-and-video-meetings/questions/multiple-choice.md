# Teleconferencing — audio and video meetings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A teleconference joins —
a) one person alone  b) two or more people in different places electronically  c) only computers with no people  d) a crowd in one hall
**Answer: b) two or more people in different places electronically** — the book's definition.

**2.** Audio conferencing is —
a) video with sound off  b) voice-only, over telephone lines  c) silent texting  d) recorded radio
**Answer: b) voice-only, over telephone lines** — the conference call.

**3.** Video conferencing adds —
a) live images, so participants see facial expressions and appearance  b) printed photos  c) bigger rooms  d) travel
**Answer: a) live images, so participants see facial expressions and appearance** — close to a face-to-face meeting.

**4.** Named video-conferencing software includes —
a) Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams  b) a calculator  c) a photo editor  d) a music player
**Answer: a) Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams** — the book's three names.
