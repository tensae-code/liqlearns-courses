# Teleconferencing — audio and video meetings — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1.** Name the four benefits of teleconferencing.
**Answer: Saved time and travel; joining from home or office; lower hosting costs; room for more participants.**

**2.** What school use does the guide add for teleconferencing? (teacher guide — evidence)
**Answer: Pupils can finish group assignments together without meeting outside school hours.**
