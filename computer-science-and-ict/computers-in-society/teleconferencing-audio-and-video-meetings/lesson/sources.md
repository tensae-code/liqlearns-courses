**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Ethiopia, Mozambique, Belarus, Egypt).**

Belarus's own way (Grade 10): the communications chapter teaches teleconferencing through the 2010-era NetMeeting program, then Skype, then blogs, forums, chats and social networks — the period tools are the lesson's content, kept as filed.

Book errors touching Unit 1, taught corrected and named here: the student book's figure numbering jumps from Figure 1.10 (e-banking) to Figure 1.13 (e-learning) — Figures 1.11 and 1.12 never appear; printed p. 19 prints \u201cFigureure 1.17\u201d for Figure 1.17; and \u201cstatment\u201d is misspelled in review headings throughout the book. The lessons teach from the pictured content and the correct spellings, and name the errors here rather than copying them.

Egypt's own way (Grade 7, Term 1 student book): One chapter ("Digital Programs and Applications") of 10 self-assessed lessons, each opening with "I can…" objectives and closing with Learn-by-doing; national platforms are the examples throughout — Digital Egypt platform, InstaPay, IDSC — and a separate Arabic activity booklet mirrors every lesson.

Egypt's own way (Grade 7, Term 1 workbook): A printed activity booklet is issued alongside the student book; the student book directs pupils to it by activity number after each sub-topic. The workbook is counted as its own book and mirrors the student book lesson by lesson (Lessons 1–9; no activity set for the Lesson 10 project).
