# Hands-on — Intellectual property — owning a creative work

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Three shields sorting
Deal twelve cards — creations and business assets — and sort each under copyright, patent, trademark or trade secret. Defend every placement with the table's rows: what it protects, what it requires.

## Proclamation card
Write Proclamation No. 320/2003 and its definition on a card in your own handwriting. Keep it at the front of this unit's notes — the anchor for the next four lessons.
