# Intellectual property — owning a creative work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Intellectual property rights are —
a) rights over physical land  b) intangible rights of ownership over an asset such as a software program  c) a tax  d) a password
**Answer: b) intangible rights of ownership over an asset such as a software program** — intangible — held by law, not by hand.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia's definition is quoted from Proclamation No. —
a) 320/2003  b) 100/1995  c) 720/2011  d) 55/2000
**Answer: a) 320/2003** — the Intellectual Property Office Establishment Proclamation.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The proclamation defines IP as a legal right over —
a) imported goods  b) a creative work of the human intellect  c) government files  d) land titles
**Answer: b) a creative work of the human intellect** — the quoted words.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which set is the book's four types for software and systems?
a) trade secrets, patents, copyrights, trademarks  b) taxes, licences, permits, fines  c) files, folders, disks, drives  d) names, dates, places, prices
**Answer: a) trade secrets, patents, copyrights, trademarks** — the four members of the family.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the guide's table, a patent protects —
a) original works of authorship  b) inventions such as processes, machines and manufactures  c) shop signs  d) secret recipes
**Answer: b) inventions such as processes, machines and manufactures** — the table's patent row (teacher guide — evidence).
