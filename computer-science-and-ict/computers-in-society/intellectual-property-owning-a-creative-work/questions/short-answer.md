# Intellectual property — owning a creative work — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Quote the proclamation's definition of intellectual property.
**Answer:** A legal right over a creative work of the human intellect.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Compare patent and trademark in one sentence each, from the guide's table.
**Answer:** A patent protects inventions and grants the right to prevent others making, selling, using or importing them; a trademark protects a word, phrase, symbol or design identifying a source and grants use of the mark plus stopping confusingly similar marks (teacher guide — evidence).
