# Hands-on — Blogs, Forums and Chats

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the talk
Take three needs: publishing your travel journal, getting help with a rare homework question for future pupils too, and arranging tonight's game with friends now. Match each need to blog, forum or chat, and say why in one sentence each.

## Read a thread
Open a forum topic (or a printed example). Find the opening question, the best answer, and one reply that adds nothing. Say what the forum's sorted, lasting shape did for this question that a chat could not.
