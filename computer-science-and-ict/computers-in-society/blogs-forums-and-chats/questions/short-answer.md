# Blogs, Forums and Chats — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a blog?**
**Answer: One author's page of dated entries, newest first, with readers' comments under each entry**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who leads a blog?**
**Answer: The author — the talk hangs on the author's entries**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a forum?**
**Answer: A lasting discussion where members open topics and others reply, sorted and searchable**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a forum's strength?**
**Answer: A question asked once keeps its answers where the next asker can find them**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a chat?**
**Answer: Written conversation in real time, flowing past like speech**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How do the three forms divide the jobs?**
**Answer: Blog — publish and gather comments; forum — ask, answer and keep; chat — talk now**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Are these forms private writing?**
**Answer: No — they are public or semi-public, and messages can be copied and kept**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the care rule for all three?**
**Answer: Write as if the whole world may read it**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are forum replies grouped under?**
**Answer: Topics, also called threads**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where do a blog's readers answer?**
**Answer: In comments under each entry**
