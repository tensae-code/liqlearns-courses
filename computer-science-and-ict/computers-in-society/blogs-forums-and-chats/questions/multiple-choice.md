# Blogs, Forums and Chats — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a blog?**
a) One author's page of dated entries, newest first, with readers' comments under each entry  b) A question asked once keeps its answers where the next asker can find them  c) Blog — publish and gather comments; forum — ask, answer and keep; chat — talk now  d) Write as if the whole world may read it
**Answer: a) One author's page of dated entries, newest first, with readers' comments under each entry**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who leads a blog?**
a) Written conversation in real time, flowing past like speech  b) The author — the talk hangs on the author's entries  c) No — they are public or semi-public, and messages can be copied and kept  d) Topics, also called threads
**Answer: b) The author — the talk hangs on the author's entries**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a forum?**
a) Blog — publish and gather comments; forum — ask, answer and keep; chat — talk now  b) Write as if the whole world may read it  c) A lasting discussion where members open topics and others reply, sorted and searchable  d) In comments under each entry
**Answer: c) A lasting discussion where members open topics and others reply, sorted and searchable**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a forum's strength?**
a) No — they are public or semi-public, and messages can be copied and kept  b) Topics, also called threads  c) One author's page of dated entries, newest first, with readers' comments under each entry  d) A question asked once keeps its answers where the next asker can find them
