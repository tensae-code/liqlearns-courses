# Video script — Blogs, Forums and Chats

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **blog** is an author's own page of dated entries — newest first — where the author publishes and readers answer in **comments** under each entry; the author's voice leads and the talk hangs on the entries.
A **forum** belongs to no single author: members open **topics** (threads), others reply under them, and the discussion stays, sorted and searchable, for later readers.
A **chat** is written conversation in **real time**: participants are present together, messages appear as they are sent, and the talk flows past like speech — quick, informal, and soon gone or buried.
The three forms divide the jobs of written community: publish and gather comments — a blog; ask, answer and keep — a forum; talk now — a chat.
