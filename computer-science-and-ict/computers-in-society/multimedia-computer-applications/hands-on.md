# Hands-on — Multimedia Computer Applications

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Composition plan** — Plan a one-minute multimedia explainer of one school topic: which medium does each part, and why that medium?

## Task two
**Weight audit** — Take one project folder; weigh its media, name the compression choices, and refit one file to a smaller promise without losing its job.
