# Multimedia Computer Applications — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the five tool families.**
**Answer: Graphics/photo editors, audio/music tools, video editors, animation tools, players and presentation forms**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the family's common craft?**
**Answer: Composition — media timed and layered, each doing its best**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give composition's example trio.**
**Answer: The diagram explains, the voice warms, the animation shows movement**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name three delivery forms.**
**Answer: Educational packages, exhibitions, games (or encyclopedias, advertisements)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What can no still do?**
**Answer: Show the movement — animation's job**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why is compression the family's daily bread?**
**Answer: Media files are heavy — video outweighs shelves of text**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is quality treated?**
**Answer: Chosen — resolution and sampling fit the purpose**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the billboard-and-thumbnail point.**
**Answer: Different sizes are different promises**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two things does every medium bring?**
**Answer: Tools to learn, and creators' rights to respect**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the tour close here?**
**Answer: Multimedia is where pupils meet computing as makers**
