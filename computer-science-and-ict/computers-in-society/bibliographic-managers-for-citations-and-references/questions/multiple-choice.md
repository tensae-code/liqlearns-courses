# Bibliographic Managers for Citations and References — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a citation do?**
a) Points to the source an idea or fact came from  b) In the reference list at the end  c) Author, title, year, publisher or journal  d) Details — a mistyped year, an unlisted source, a changing style
**Answer: a) Points to the source an idea or fact came from**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is every source given in full?**
a) Author, title, year, publisher or journal  b) In the reference list at the end  c) Details — a mistyped year, an unlisted source, a changing style  d) A tool that keeps the record once and reuses it exactly
**Answer: b) In the reference list at the end**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four parts of a full reference.**
a) Details — a mistyped year, an unlisted source, a changing style  b) A tool that keeps the record once and reuses it exactly  c) Author, title, year, publisher or journal  d) A library of your sources, each with all its parts
**Answer: c) Author, title, year, publisher or journal**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What drifts when citations are done by hand?**
a) A tool that keeps the record once and reuses it exactly  b) A library of your sources, each with all its parts  c) Once  d) Details — a mistyped year, an unlisted source, a changing style
**Answer: d) Details — a mistyped year, an unlisted source, a changing style**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the real need?**
a) A tool that keeps the record once and reuses it exactly  b) A library of your sources, each with all its parts  c) Once  d) The whole reference list, from the citations actually used
**Answer: a) A tool that keeps the record once and reuses it exactly**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a bibliographic manager keep?**
a) Once  b) A library of your sources, each with all its parts  c) The whole reference list, from the citations actually used  d) Nothing cited missing, nothing listed unused
**Answer: b) A library of your sources, each with all its parts**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How many times is a source entered?**
a) The whole reference list, from the citations actually used  b) Nothing cited missing, nothing listed unused  c) Once  d) The manager re-forms every citation to match
**Answer: c) Once**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the manager build at the end?**
a) Nothing cited missing, nothing listed unused  b) The manager re-forms every citation to match  c) Points to the source an idea or fact came from  d) The whole reference list, from the citations actually used
**Answer: d) The whole reference list, from the citations actually used**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two faults can the built list not have?**
a) Nothing cited missing, nothing listed unused  b) The manager re-forms every citation to match  c) Points to the source an idea or fact came from  d) In the reference list at the end
**Answer: a) Nothing cited missing, nothing listed unused**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when the style changes?**
a) Points to the source an idea or fact came from  b) The manager re-forms every citation to match  c) In the reference list at the end  d) Author, title, year, publisher or journal
**Answer: b) The manager re-forms every citation to match**
