# Bibliographic Managers for Citations and References — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a citation do?**
**Answer: Points to the source an idea or fact came from**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is every source given in full?**
**Answer: In the reference list at the end**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four parts of a full reference.**
**Answer: Author, title, year, publisher or journal**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What drifts when citations are done by hand?**
**Answer: Details — a mistyped year, an unlisted source, a changing style**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the real need?**
**Answer: A tool that keeps the record once and reuses it exactly**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a bibliographic manager keep?**
**Answer: A library of your sources, each with all its parts**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How many times is a source entered?**
**Answer: Once**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the manager build at the end?**
**Answer: The whole reference list, from the citations actually used**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two faults can the built list not have?**
**Answer: Nothing cited missing, nothing listed unused**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when the style changes?**
**Answer: The manager re-forms every citation to match**
