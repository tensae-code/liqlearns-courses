# Bibliographic Managers for Citations and References — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a citation do?" is: Points to the source an idea or fact came from**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where is every source given in full?" is: Author, title, year, publisher or journal**
**Answer: False** — In the reference list at the end

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the four parts of a full reference." is: Author, title, year, publisher or journal**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What drifts when citations are done by hand?" is: A tool that keeps the record once and reuses it exactly**
**Answer: False** — Details — a mistyped year, an unlisted source, a changing style

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the real need?" is: A tool that keeps the record once and reuses it exactly**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does a bibliographic manager keep?" is: Once**
**Answer: False** — A library of your sources, each with all its parts

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How many times is a source entered?" is: Once**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the manager build at the end?" is: Nothing cited missing, nothing listed unused**
**Answer: False** — The whole reference list, from the citations actually used

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What two faults can the built list not have?" is: Nothing cited missing, nothing listed unused**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens when the style changes?" is: Points to the source an idea or fact came from**
**Answer: False** — The manager re-forms every citation to match
