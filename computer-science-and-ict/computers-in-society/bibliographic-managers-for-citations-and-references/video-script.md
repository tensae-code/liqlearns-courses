# Video script — Bibliographic Managers for Citations and References

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Scientific work stands on other people's work, and honesty names it: a **citation** inside the text points to the source an idea or fact came from, and the **reference list** at the end gives every source in full — **author, title, year, publisher or journal**. Done by hand across a long work, citations drift: a year mistyped, a source used and never listed, a style that changes halfway. The need is not care — it is a tool that keeps the record once and reuses it exactly.
A **bibliographic manager** keeps a **library of your sources**, each entered or captured once with all its parts. While you write, it **inserts the citation** at the point you choose, numbered or named as the required **style** demands; at the end it **builds the whole reference list** from the citations actually used — nothing cited missing, nothing listed unused. Change the style and the manager **re-forms every citation** to match. Cuba closes its school ICT with this tool because university work begins with it.
