# Hands-on — Bibliographic Managers for Citations and References

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('A five-source library', 'Open a bibliographic manager (or its paper shape): enter five real sources with author, title, year and publisher, then produce a reference list from three of them — only the cited three may appear.')

## Task two
('Catch the drift', "Take a hand-made reference list: check each entry against its source for a mistyped year, a missing part or a changed style, and say which fault a manager would have prevented.")
