## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Teams — the class's rooms on the platform** — A **team** is the platform's room for a class or group: its members are the pupils and teacher of that class, and everything the class shares gathers there. A pupil may belong to several teams — one per subject — and moving between them is moving between classrooms. Kuwait teaches the team first because every other Teams skill hangs on it: assignments, meetings, posts and files all live inside a team.
2. **Assignments — view, solve, submit** — An **assignment** arrives inside its team with the teacher's instructions and its due moment. The pupil's three moves are the lesson: **view** the assignment and read what is actually asked; **solve** it — do the work, in the file or form the assignment carries; and **submit** it, handing the finished work back through the platform before the due moment. Submitting is a real act, not a hope: until it is done, the teacher has nothing.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
