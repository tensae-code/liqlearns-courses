# Video script — Microsoft Teams — Teams and Assignments

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **team** is the platform's room for a class or group: its members are the pupils and teacher of that class, and everything the class shares gathers there. A pupil may belong to several teams — one per subject — and moving between them is moving between classrooms. Kuwait teaches the team first because every other Teams skill hangs on it: assignments, meetings, posts and files all live inside a team.
An **assignment** arrives inside its team with the teacher's instructions and its due moment. The pupil's three moves are the lesson: **view** the assignment and read what is actually asked; **solve** it — do the work, in the file or form the assignment carries; and **submit** it, handing the finished work back through the platform before the due moment. Submitting is a real act, not a hope: until it is done, the teacher has nothing.
