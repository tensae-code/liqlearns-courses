# Microsoft Teams — Teams and Assignments — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a team on the platform?**
**Answer: The room for a class or group — its members, and everything the class shares**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Can a pupil belong to more than one team?**
**Answer: Yes — often one per subject**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do assignments, meetings, posts and files live?**
**Answer: Inside a team**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) What are the pupil's three moves with an assignment?**
**Answer: View it, solve it, and submit it**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) What must be read first when viewing?**
**Answer: The teacher's instructions — what is actually asked**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does submitting do?**
**Answer: Hands the finished work back to the teacher through the platform**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Is work finished but not submitted handed in?**
**Answer: No — until it is submitted, the teacher has nothing**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What marks an assignment's deadline on the platform?**
**Answer: Its due moment, shown with the assignment**

**9. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Kuwait teach teams before assignments?**
**Answer: Because assignments live inside teams — the room comes before the work in it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which lesson number is this in the book's Teams section?**
**Answer: Lesson 3 — teams and assignments**
