# Lesson plan — Microsoft Teams — Project

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** Microsoft Teams — Project
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Kuwait (2026-11-14) — in the title Computers in Society on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Kuwait's student books (Ministry of Education, State of Kuwait), because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree and live lesson content were checked for the slug and for near matches before building.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Kuwait's closing Teams project — using the whole platform at once: team, assignments, meeting, posts and files — in one practical piece of work
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The project — the platform used whole** — The **project** gathers the section's skills into one piece of work done **on the platform itself**: work inside a team, handle an assignment from viewing to submitting, take part in a meeting, and use posts and files as the class's conversation and shelves. Nothing in the project is new — that is its point. It proves the section's lessons were skills, not viewings, by demanding them together without a lesson title to hint which comes next.
2. **Working the project — in the platform's own order** — The work follows the platform's own order: **enter** signed in as yourself; **find** your team and read its posts; **take** the assignment and its files; **do** the work and, where the project asks, meet and discuss; **submit** and leave the team's conversation with a closing post. Each stage is checked against the project brief before the next begins — the same discipline Kuwait's project lessons teach in every subject.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. A practical project using the whole platform at once, at the end of the Teams section

4. That the section's lessons were skills, not viewings

## What you need
Paper and a pencil, and a computer with the program this lesson uses, if available.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — The Teams section ends the way Kuwait's units end: with a project. After the platform, its settings, its teams and assignments, its meetings and its posts and files, the pupil must now use them together, on purpose, in one practical project. This lesson is that closing work. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of the project — the platform used whole as the lesson describes it — the student studies the picture and names what each part shows.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of working the project — in the platform's own order in action — the student watches once, then says what changed and why.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key names and definitions aloud, in the order the lesson gives them — the student listens once through, then reads the same lines aloud along with the voice. (Audio is a described slot in this build; no audio file is made.)
3. **Say it to your companion** — the companion block in lesson/lesson.md.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-false, matching, short-answer — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.

## If the learner is stuck
