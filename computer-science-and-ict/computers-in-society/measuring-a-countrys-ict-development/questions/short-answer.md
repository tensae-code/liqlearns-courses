# Measuring a Country's ICT Development — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Whose index is the IDI?**
**Answer: The ITU's — the ICT Development Index**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the IDI's three baskets.**
**Answer: Access, use and skills**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the access basket measure?**
**Answer: Whether people can reach ICT — subscriptions, coverage, bandwidth, equipped households**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the use basket measure?**
**Answer: Whether people actually use it — users and broadband**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What stands for capability in the skills basket?**
**Answer: Schooling and literacy measures**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which countries topped the index in the book's 2017 snapshot?**
**Answer: Iceland, South Korea and Switzerland**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How many internet users did the world have?**
**Answer: 4.156 billion**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why are the baskets the real reading, not the one number?**
**Answer: Countries with the same index can be strong in opposite baskets, needing opposite remedies**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is every rank, in the lesson's phrase?**
**Answer: A year-stamped photograph, not a destiny**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) On whom does the next lesson turn the instrument?**
**Answer: Mongolia itself**
