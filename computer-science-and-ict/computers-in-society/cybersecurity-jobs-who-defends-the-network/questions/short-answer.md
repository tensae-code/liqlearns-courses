# Cybersecurity Jobs — Who Defends the Network — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is this lesson about?**
**Answer: The jobs of cyber security — who defends the network**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two defending jobs besides the responder.**
**Answer: The security analyst and the security engineer (testers also named)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does an analyst watch?**
**Answer: The network's traffic and logs, for signs of attack**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do engineers build and maintain?**
**Answer: The defences — firewalls, accounts, updates**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a tester do?**
**Answer: Attacks their own organisation's systems, with permission, to find holes first**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Who is the incident responder?**
**Answer: The person who leads the answer when an attack is real and running**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two things the responder does during an incident.**
**Answer: Contains the damage and drives the attacker out (also: confirms, restores)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the responder do after the incident?**
**Answer: Writes what happened, so it cannot happen the same way twice**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two qualities does the responder's job ask for?**
**Answer: Technical depth and calm under pressure**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Kuwait teach these jobs?**
**Answer: Grade 10 Information Technology Term 1, Unit 1 Theme 5**
