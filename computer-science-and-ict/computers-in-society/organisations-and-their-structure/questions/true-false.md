# Organisations and Their Structure — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A group of people joined to reach shared goals**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A system is built for a particular organisation and its goals** — as the answer to: Give two examples of organisations.
**Answer: False — A school and a factory (or a ministry, a shop)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A system is built for a particular organisation and its goals**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Levels — a top that directs, a middle that manages, a floor that works** — as the answer to: What two ways does structure arrange an organisation?
**Answer: False — Vertically by levels and horizontally by functions**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Levels — a top that directs, a middle that manages, a floor that works**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Functions or departments side by side — production, sales, accounts, personnel** — as the answer to: What travels down and up the levels?
**Answer: False — Authority steps down; reports step up**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Functions or departments side by side — production, sales, accounts, personnel**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 10 ICT, Chapter 1 §1–2** — as the answer to: What is the organisation's chart?
**Answer: False — The drawing of levels and departments together**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 10 ICT, Chapter 1 §1–2**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A group of people joined to reach shared goals** — as the answer to: What must a system serve to succeed?
**Answer: False — This organisation, with these goals**
