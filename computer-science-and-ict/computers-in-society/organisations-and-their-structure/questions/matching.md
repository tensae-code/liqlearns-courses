# Organisations and Their Structure — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an organisation?** → **A group of people joined to reach shared goals**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Give two examples of organisations.** → **A school and a factory (or a ministry, a shop)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the systems chapter begin with organisations?** → **A system is built for a particular organisation and its goals**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What two ways does structure arrange an organisation?** → **Vertically by levels and horizontally by functions**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) What does the vertical structure arrange?** → **Levels — a top that directs, a middle that manages, a floor that works**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What travels down and up the levels?** → **Authority steps down; reports step up**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the horizontal structure divide?** → **Functions or departments side by side — production, sales, accounts, personnel**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the organisation's chart?** → **The drawing of levels and departments together**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?** → **Laos Grade 10 ICT, Chapter 1 §1–2**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) What must a system serve to succeed?** → **This organisation, with these goals**
