# Organisations and Their Structure — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an organisation?**
a) A group of people joined to reach shared goals  b) A school and a factory (or a ministry, a shop)  c) A system is built for a particular organisation and its goals  d) Vertically by levels and horizontally by functions
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Give two examples of organisations.**
a) A system is built for a particular organisation and its goals  b) A school and a factory (or a ministry, a shop)  c) Vertically by levels and horizontally by functions  d) Levels — a top that directs, a middle that manages, a floor that works
