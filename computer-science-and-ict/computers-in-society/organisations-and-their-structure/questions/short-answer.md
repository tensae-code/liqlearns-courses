# Organisations and Their Structure — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an organisation?**
**Answer: A group of people joined to reach shared goals**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Give two examples of organisations.**
**Answer: A school and a factory (or a ministry, a shop)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the systems chapter begin with organisations?**
**Answer: A system is built for a particular organisation and its goals**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What two ways does structure arrange an organisation?**
**Answer: Vertically by levels and horizontally by functions**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) What does the vertical structure arrange?**
**Answer: Levels — a top that directs, a middle that manages, a floor that works**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What travels down and up the levels?**
**Answer: Authority steps down; reports step up**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the horizontal structure divide?**
**Answer: Functions or departments side by side — production, sales, accounts, personnel**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the organisation's chart?**
**Answer: The drawing of levels and departments together**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 10 ICT, Chapter 1 §1–2**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) What must a system serve to succeed?**
**Answer: This organisation, with these goals**
