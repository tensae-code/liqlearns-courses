# Hands-on — Ethics of Publishing and Communicating Online

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Judge three posts
Take three example posts. For each, say which ethical rule it keeps or breaks — truth, source, permission, respect — and rewrite the breaking one rightly.

## The reread rule
Write a reply to a message that made you angry. Wait, reread it as its receiver, and improve it until it says the same thing with respect.
