# Ethics of Publishing and Communicating Online — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What can happen to what you publish online?**
**Answer: It can be seen, copied and kept by people you will never meet**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first rule of publishing online?**
**Answer: Publish only what is true, and check before you share**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How do you use another person's words or pictures rightly?**
**Answer: Name the source — never present them as your own**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) May you publish someone's private photos if you hold them?**
**Answer: No — not without their permission**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why are the rules of online publishing strict?**
**Answer: Because a post can spread and be kept far beyond its first audience**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What respect is online communication owed?**
**Answer: The same respect as speech face to face**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How should you disagree online?**
**Answer: With the idea — never by attacking the person**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must you never forward?**
**Answer: A message that mocks, threatens or shames someone**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who is behind every account?**
**Answer: A real person, who can be hurt**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the ethical move in a heated conversation?**
**Answer: Slow down, reread before sending, or leave the exchange**
