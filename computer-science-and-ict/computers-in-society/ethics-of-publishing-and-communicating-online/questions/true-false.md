# Ethics of Publishing and Communicating Online — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What can happen to what you publish online?" is: It can be seen, copied and kept by people you will never meet**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the first rule of publishing online?" is: Name the source — never present them as your own**
**Answer: False** — Publish only what is true, and check before you share

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How do you use another person's words or pictures rightly?" is: Name the source — never present them as your own**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "May you publish someone's private photos if you hold them?" is: Because a post can spread and be kept far beyond its first audience**
**Answer: False** — No — not without their permission

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why are the rules of online publishing strict?" is: Because a post can spread and be kept far beyond its first audience**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What respect is online communication owed?" is: With the idea — never by attacking the person**
**Answer: False** — The same respect as speech face to face

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How should you disagree online?" is: With the idea — never by attacking the person**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What must you never forward?" is: A real person, who can be hurt**
**Answer: False** — A message that mocks, threatens or shames someone

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Who is behind every account?" is: A real person, who can be hurt**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the ethical move in a heated conversation?" is: It can be seen, copied and kept by people you will never meet**
**Answer: False** — Slow down, reread before sending, or leave the exchange
