# Ethics of Publishing and Communicating Online — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What can happen to what you publish online?**
a) It can be seen, copied and kept by people you will never meet  b) Publish only what is true, and check before you share  c) Because a post can spread and be kept far beyond its first audience  d) A message that mocks, threatens or shames someone
**Answer: a) It can be seen, copied and kept by people you will never meet**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first rule of publishing online?**
a) Name the source — never present them as your own  b) Publish only what is true, and check before you share  c) The same respect as speech face to face  d) A real person, who can be hurt
**Answer: b) Publish only what is true, and check before you share**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How do you use another person's words or pictures rightly?**
a) No — not without their permission  b) With the idea — never by attacking the person  c) Name the source — never present them as your own  d) Slow down, reread before sending, or leave the exchange
**Answer: c) Name the source — never present them as your own**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) May you publish someone's private photos if you hold them?**
a) Because a post can spread and be kept far beyond its first audience  b) A message that mocks, threatens or shames someone  c) It can be seen, copied and kept by people you will never meet  d) No — not without their permission
**Answer: d) No — not without their permission**
