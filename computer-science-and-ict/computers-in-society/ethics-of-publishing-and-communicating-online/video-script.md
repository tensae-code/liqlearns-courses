# Video script — Ethics of Publishing and Communicating Online

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What you **publish** online — a post, a photo, a comment — can be seen, copied and kept by people you will never meet, so the rules are strict: publish only what is **true**, check before you share, and never present another person's words or pictures as your own — name the source instead. A person's private information and private photos are not yours to publish, even if you hold them; publishing them without permission is a wrong against that person.
Online **communication** — messages, comments, discussions — is owed the same respect as speech face to face: address people politely, disagree with ideas without attacking the person, and never forward a message that mocks, threatens or shames someone. Behind every account is a real person who can be hurt. When a conversation turns heated, the ethical move is to slow down, reread before sending, or leave the exchange rather than wound with words.
