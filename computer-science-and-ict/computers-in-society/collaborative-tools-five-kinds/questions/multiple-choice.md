# Collaborative tools — communication, files, projects, charts and notes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A tool's effectiveness is decided by —
a) its fame  b) features, price, policies and ease of use  c) its colour  d) its age
**Answer: b) features, price, policies and ease of use** — the four tests.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book sorts collaborative tools into — kinds.
a) three  b) five  c) seven  d) ten
**Answer: b) five** — the five trays.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A tool the team cannot operate loses because — counts among the tests.
a) price  b) ease of use  c) policies  d) features
**Answer: b) ease of use** — power unused is not effectiveness.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Zoom, Slack and Google Meet are —
a) note-taking tools  b) communication tools  c) chart tools  d) file stores
**Answer: b) communication tools** — the first tray.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Google Drive, Dropbox and SharePoint belong to —
a) documentation and file-sharing tools  b) project management  c) data visualisation  d) note-taking
**Answer: a) documentation and file-sharing tools** — the second tray.
