# Hands-on — Collaborative tools — communication, files, projects, charts and notes

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Tray audit
List every tool your class or group actually uses, and sort them onto the five trays. Photograph or copy the sorted list — gaps and pile-ups are the finding.

## Four-test trial
Pick one free tool from a tray your group lacks. Trial it on a small real task and score it on the four tests. Present the scores, not an advertisement.
