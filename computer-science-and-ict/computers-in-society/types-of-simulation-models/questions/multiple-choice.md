# Types of Simulation Models — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In a simulation, what is an entity?**
a) A thing that moves through or sits in the system  b) What happens to entities over time  c) The customers  d) Being served at the counter
**Answer: a) A thing that moves through or sits in the system**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In a simulation, what is a process?**
a) The customers  b) What happens to entities over time  c) Being served at the counter  d) Processes
**Answer: b) What happens to entities over time**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give the entities in a shop simulation.**
a) Being served at the counter  b) Processes  c) The customers  d) A real, scaled copy you can touch
**Answer: c) The customers**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Give a process in a shop simulation.**
a) Processes  b) A real, scaled copy you can touch  c) A program that imitates the system  d) Being served at the counter
**Answer: d) Being served at the counter**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) A simulation model represents entities and what else?**
a) Processes  b) A real, scaled copy you can touch  c) A program that imitates the system  d) How they represent the system
**Answer: a) Processes**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a physical model?**
a) A program that imitates the system  b) A real, scaled copy you can touch  c) How they represent the system  d) Different systems need different kinds of models
**Answer: b) A real, scaled copy you can touch**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a computer model?**
a) How they represent the system  b) Different systems need different kinds of models  c) A program that imitates the system  d) What must be tested
**Answer: c) A program that imitates the system**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Models are grouped by what?**
a) Different systems need different kinds of models  b) What must be tested  c) A thing that moves through or sits in the system  d) How they represent the system
**Answer: d) How they represent the system**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why are there different types of simulation models?**
a) Different systems need different kinds of models  b) What must be tested  c) A thing that moves through or sits in the system  d) What happens to entities over time
**Answer: a) Different systems need different kinds of models**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Choosing a model type depends on what?**
a) A thing that moves through or sits in the system  b) What must be tested  c) What happens to entities over time  d) The customers
**Answer: b) What must be tested**
