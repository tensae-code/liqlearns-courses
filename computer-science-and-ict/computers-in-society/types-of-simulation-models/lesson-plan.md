## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Models of entities and processes** — Inside a simulation, an **entity** is **a thing that moves through or sits in the system** — the customers in a shop simulation. A **process** is **what happens to entities over time** — those customers being served at the counter. A simulation model represents the entities and the processes together.
2. **Different types of models** — Models are grouped by **how they represent the system**. A **physical model** is a real, scaled copy you can touch. A **computer model** is a program that imitates the system. Different systems need different kinds of models, so the type is chosen by **what must be tested**.
