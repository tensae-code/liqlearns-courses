# Plagiarism — other people's work is not yours — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Plagiarism is —
a) quoting with credit  b) presenting work or ideas from another source as your own without full acknowledgement  c) reading widely  d) using a library
**Answer: b) presenting work or ideas from another source as your own without full acknowledgement** — the definition, complete.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Even with the author's consent, unacknowledged presentation is —
a) fine  b) still plagiarism  c) required  d) a reward
**Answer: b) still plagiarism** — acknowledgement is part of the honesty.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Plagiarism can cost —
a) nothing  b) credibility, a school place or a job  c) only marks  d) only time
**Answer: b) credibility, a school place or a job** — the book's named costs.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The pupil's rule is to —
a) copy carefully  b) produce original work, use sources as references, and credit authors  c) avoid all sources  d) hide sources
**Answer: b) produce original work, use sources as references, and credit authors** — the three-part rule.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The last of the guide's five steps is to —
a) submit fast  b) run a plagiarism checker before submitting  c) delete the references  d) ask a friend to sign
**Answer: b) run a plagiarism checker before submitting** — the method's final step (teacher guide — evidence).
