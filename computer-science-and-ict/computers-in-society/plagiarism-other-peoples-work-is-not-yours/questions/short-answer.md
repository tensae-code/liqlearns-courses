# Plagiarism — other people's work is not yours — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does consent not settle plagiarism?
**Answer:** Because plagiarism is presenting another's work as your own without full acknowledgement — with or without the author's consent.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Walk the guide's five steps in order.
**Answer:** Note the sources you consult; paraphrase or quote; credit the author in an in-text citation; list it in the references; run a checker before submitting (teacher guide — evidence).
