# Hands-on — Plagiarism — other people's work is not yours

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Fix a paragraph
Take a paragraph of your own schoolwork. Apply the five steps to it in order, on paper: source list, paraphrase marks, in-text credit, reference list, and a final originality read-through. The repaired paragraph is the product.

## Skills self-check
From the guide's 21st-century skills list, pick your strongest and your weakest. Write one line of evidence for each — a real thing you did. That honesty is information literacy in action.
