# Hands-on — Misinformation — How False Information Spreads

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Sort the varieties** — Collect four wrong claims (from memory or examples your teacher gives). Label each mistake, rumour, propaganda or fake news, and write the cure each one needs.

## Task two
**Break a chain** — Write a forwarding chain of a rumour through six people. Mark the exact person and moment where one verification question would have stopped it.
