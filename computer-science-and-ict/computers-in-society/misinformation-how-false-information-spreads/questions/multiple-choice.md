# Misinformation — How False Information Spreads — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four varieties of wrong information in this lesson.**
a) Mistake, rumour, propaganda and fake news  b) An unchecked story passed on because it might be true  c) True parts chosen and false parts added, to push a side  d) A story invented whole and dressed as reporting
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a rumour?**
