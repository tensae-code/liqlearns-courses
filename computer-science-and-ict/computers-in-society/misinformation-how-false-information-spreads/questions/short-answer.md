# Misinformation — How False Information Spreads — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four varieties of wrong information in this lesson.**
**Answer: Mistake, rumour, propaganda and fake news**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a rumour?**
**Answer: An unchecked story passed on because it might be true**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is propaganda made?**
**Answer: True parts chosen and false parts added, to push a side**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is fake news?**
**Answer: A story invented whole and dressed as reporting**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does an honest mistake need?**
**Answer: A correction**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three engines of spread.**
**Answer: Speed, trust and feeling**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why do we believe forwarded falsehoods?**
**Answer: They arrive from our own trusted people**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why does truth travel slower?**
**Answer: It must be checked first**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's answer to misinformation?**
**Answer: A population of checkers — receivers who verify before forwarding**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a verifying receiver become, in the lesson's image?**
**Answer: A wall the falsehood cannot pass**
