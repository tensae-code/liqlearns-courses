# Smartphone Apps in Daily Life — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an app?**
**Answer: An application — a program made for one kind of job**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a smartphone?**
**Answer: A phone that is also a computer, running programs far beyond calls**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do new apps come from?**
**Answer: An app store**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the phone jobs that are apps from the start.**
**Answer: Calling, the camera and the clock**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which apps carry money jobs?**
**Answer: Banking and paying apps**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which apps replace the map?**
**Answer: Maps and travel apps**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name four institutions or tools shrunk into apps.**
**Answer: The bank branch, the map, the library and the post office**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Sudan's book end the unit with smartphones?**
**Answer: Because it is the computer its pupils already hold every day**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What stands behind every tap?**
**Answer: A program somebody wrote**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the lesson's habit?**
**Answer: To name the app behind each job of the day**
