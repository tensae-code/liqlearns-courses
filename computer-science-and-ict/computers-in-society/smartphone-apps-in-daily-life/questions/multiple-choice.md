# Smartphone Apps in Daily Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an app?**
a) An application — a program made for one kind of job  b) A phone that is also a computer, running programs far beyond calls  c) Calling, the camera and the clock  d) Maps and travel apps
**Answer: a) An application — a program made for one kind of job**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a smartphone?**
a) An app store  b) A phone that is also a computer, running programs far beyond calls  c) Banking and paying apps  d) The bank branch, the map, the library and the post office
**Answer: b) A phone that is also a computer, running programs far beyond calls**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do new apps come from?**
