Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (China, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked for the slug and for near matches before building. It sits in the Computers in Society title (computers-in-society/), following the book's own order.

China's own way: each chapter of Compulsory 2 is driven by a themed project — here a ticketing system and the design of a 'smart teaching building' carry the development process, so the four stages are learned on a system the student follows end to end, not as a bare list.

The two Teacher's Guides in China's set (to Compulsory 1 and to Compulsory 2) are record-only and are cited nowhere in this course: their library records are swapped against the student books (mismatched pairings) and both PDFs are partial (front portions only).

Papua New Guinea's own way (Grade 12): the final year is design-and-build — simulation models, a seven-method technological problem-solving process, biotechnology with a DNA-technology case study, CAD, the software development process, IT career pathways, and GUI work with client-side scripting and interactive menus.
