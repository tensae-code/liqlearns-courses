# Hands-on — Developing an Information System — the Four Stages

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four questions drill
Take a system you know — a library catalogue, a bus ticketing app. Write the one big question each of the four stages would ask about it.

## Stage sorting
Write eight short actions (for example 'ask the head teacher what reports she needs', 'draw the login screen', 'train the clerks', 'check whether the school can afford servers') and sort each into its stage.
