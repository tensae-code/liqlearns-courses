# Hands-on — The Global Village — One Connected World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Map your village** — List five services you used this week that reached beyond your town. For each, name the place it came from and the connection that carried it.

## Task two
**Village problem, village cure** — Take one village problem from the lesson — a rumour, a distant failure, a culture clash. Write how the same connections that cause it can also cure it.
