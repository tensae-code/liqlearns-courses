# The Global Village — One Connected World — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Who named the connected world a global village?**
**Answer: Marshall McLuhan**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does instant worldwide communication do to distance?**
**Answer: It collapses it — the world behaves like one village**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two components of the global village.**
**Answer: Communication networks and the services that ride on them**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is the global village a place or an effect of connections?**
**Answer: An effect — what worldwide connections do to distance**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How fast does news travel in the global village?**
**Answer: To everyone at once, the same day**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three parts of life remade in the village.**
**Answer: Communication, employment and education (also medicine, business, news)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does employment change in the village?**
**Answer: Work crosses borders without the worker moving**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name one problem the village shares with a real village.**
**Answer: Rumours outrun corrections**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Bangladesh's book teach the village first?**
**Answer: Because every later chapter is one of its streets**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must each culture decide in the village?**
**Answer: What of itself to keep**
