# Video script — The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

AI is moving into work, services and daily life.
It takes the routine tasks first — and never gets tired.
It helps the doctor, the teacher, the farmer.
But jobs can vanish. Biased data can judge unfairly.
Private data feeds it. And someone must answer for its decisions.
Use it where it helps. Weigh both sides. Stay human.
