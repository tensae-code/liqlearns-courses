# The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Society — short answer

Answer each question in a few sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Give one true benefit story and one true risk story about AI in a hospital, each in one or two sentences.**
**Answer:** Benefit: AI assists radiologists by flagging shadows on scans tirelessly, so a small clinic gains expert-level attention for every patient. Risk: if the system was trained mostly on scans from one kind of patient, it may miss disease in others — bias — and when a patient is harmed, the hospital, not 'the AI', must answer.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A factory owner says: 'AI will answer all customer questions; I can dismiss the whole desk, and if customers are harmed by wrong answers, that is the AI's fault.' Answer him using this lesson.**
**Answer:** The first half may be partly true — question-answering is routine and pattern-shaped, so AI will take much of it; but dismissing the whole desk ignores the new work of checking, training and handling what the AI cannot, and the people displaced. The second half is simply wrong: responsibility cannot be handed to a machine — if the answers harm customers, the firm that deployed the system answers for it.
