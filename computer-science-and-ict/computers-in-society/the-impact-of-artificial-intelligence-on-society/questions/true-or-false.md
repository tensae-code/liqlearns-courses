# The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Society — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) AI creates new kinds of jobs as well as displacing others.**
**Answer: True** — Building, training, checking and working alongside AI are growing tasks.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Intelligent services need no personal data to work.**
**Answer: False** — Privacy is a named risk precisely because such services feed on personal data.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The impact of AI is a subject only for programmers.**
**Answer: False** — The book closes by teaching every citizen to weigh it.
