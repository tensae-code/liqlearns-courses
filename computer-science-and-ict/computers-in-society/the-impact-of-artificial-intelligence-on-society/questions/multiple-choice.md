# The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) AI takes which tasks first?**
a) Tasks needing human judgement most  b) Routine, pattern-shaped, data-rich tasks  c) Tasks with no data at all  d) Only physical tasks
**Answer: b) Routine, pattern-shaped, data-rich tasks** — The ten-thousandth scan gets the same attention as the first.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In medicine, the pattern the book shows is AI —**
a) replacing all doctors  b) assisting professionals — doing the tireless part while people decide  c) refusing to work with scans  d) charging patients
**Answer: b) assisting professionals — doing the tireless part while people decide** — Assistance, with judgement staying human.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A system trained on biased data will tend to —**
a) correct the bias by itself  b) repeat the bias, at scale  c) refuse to answer  d) become fairer over time
**Answer: b) repeat the bias, at scale** — Fairness is one of the named risks.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The responsibility question asks —**
a) whether AI is clever  b) who answers when an AI-assisted decision harms someone — and the answer must be a human or organisation  c) how fast the system is  d) what the system cost
**Answer: b) who answers when an AI-assisted decision harms someone — and the answer must be a human or organisation** — 'The AI decided' is never an acceptable final answer.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) The balanced position toward AI is —**
a) worship it — it is always right  b) fear it — refuse all of it  c) use it where it genuinely helps, keep humans deciding where humans must answer, and weigh both sides  d) let only programmers judge it
**Answer: c) use it where it genuinely helps, keep humans deciding where humans must answer, and weigh both sides** — Neither worship nor fear — judgement.
