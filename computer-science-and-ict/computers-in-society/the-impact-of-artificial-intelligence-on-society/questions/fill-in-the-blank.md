# The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Society — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) AI reads the ten-thousandth scan with the same ____ as the first.**
**Answer: attention** — Tirelessness is its great working gift.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A system trained on ____ data repeats unfairness at scale.**
**Answer: biased** — Bias in, bias out — multiplied.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) When an AI-assisted decision causes harm, a ____ must always be answerable.**
**Answer: human (or an organisation)** — Responsibility cannot be delegated to the machine.
