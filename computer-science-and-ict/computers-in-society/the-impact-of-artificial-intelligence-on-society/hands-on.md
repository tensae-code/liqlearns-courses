# Hands-on — The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One street, twice
Choose one place you know — a clinic, a market, your school. Write two short paragraphs: the tasks there today, and the same place with AI helpers inside it in ten years. Mark each changed task: taken over, assisted, or new.

## Weigh one application
Pick one real AI application (a translation app, a marking assistant, a recommendation feed). Fill the balance: three true benefits, three true risks, and your one-sentence judgement. Be ready to defend the judgement with the pans, not with slogans.
