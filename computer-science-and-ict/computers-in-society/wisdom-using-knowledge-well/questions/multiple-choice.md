# Wisdom — using knowledge well — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Wisdom is —
a) stored facts  b) the ability to make wise decisions and sound judgements using knowledge  c) a fast computer  d) a long book
**Answer: b) the ability to make wise decisions and sound judgements using knowledge** — the book's definition.

**2.** Wisdom is the _______ level of human understanding.
a) lowest  b) middle  c) highest  d) first
**Answer: c) highest** — it sits at the top of the climb.

**3.** Wisdom is linked to —
a) good judgement and living well  b) speed  c) memorising  d) machines
**Answer: a) good judgement and living well** — the book links it to both.
