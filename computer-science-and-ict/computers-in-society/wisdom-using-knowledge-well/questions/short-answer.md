# Wisdom — using knowledge well — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1.** Give an example where two people hold the same knowledge but only one acts wisely.
**Answer: Any sound example, e.g. two drivers on a wet road — one slows early, the other speeds and skids.**

**2.** How is wisdom different from knowledge?
**Answer: Knowledge is understanding that can act; wisdom is using that knowledge to make wise decisions and sound judgements.**
