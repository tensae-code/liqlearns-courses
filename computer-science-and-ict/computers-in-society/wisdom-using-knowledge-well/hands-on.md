# Hands-on — Wisdom — using knowledge well

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The pause practice
For one day, before any choice that matters, stop for ten seconds and say what you know and what the wise course is. Write the three choices where the pause changed what you did.

## Ask and record
Interview someone older about one wise decision. Write it in two lines: the knowledge they held, and the judgement they made. Keep the two lines separate — that separation is the lesson.
