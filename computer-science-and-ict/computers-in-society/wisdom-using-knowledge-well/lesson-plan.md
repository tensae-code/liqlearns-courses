# Lesson plan — Wisdom — using knowledge well

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** Wisdom — using knowledge well
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 14 — Computers in Society of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 11, Unit 1 — Information Systems and Its Applications. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** wisdom is the ability to make wise decisions and sound judgements using your knowledge — the highest level of human understanding.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Knowledge lesson just before it (recommended first, never forced). It is a short, single-idea lesson.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what wisdom is: the ability to make wise decisions and sound judgements using knowledge.
2. Say where wisdom sits: the highest level of human understanding.
3. Give a daily-life example where equal knowledge leads to a wise and an unwise choice.
