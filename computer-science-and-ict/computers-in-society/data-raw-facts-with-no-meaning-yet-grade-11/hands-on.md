# Hands-on — Data — raw facts with no meaning yet (Grade 11)

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Three forms, one fact
Take one fact about yourself — your age. Write it as a number. Write your name as text. Draw or show a picture of your street. Hold the three up: one person, three forms of data. Say which form suits which fact, and why.

## The bare-fact test
Write a single number on a card and show it to someone at home. Ask: what does it mean? Write down their guesses. Then tell them what it counts. That moment — from guessing to knowing — is the whole road of this unit in miniature.
