# Data — raw facts with no meaning yet (Grade 11) — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A bare figure like 17 tells you by itself what it counts.
**Answer: False** — nothing in the figure says whether it is an age, a price or a mark.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The best form of data depends on the kind of data.
**Answer: True** — age suits numbers, a name suits text.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The teacher guide budgets 105 periods for the Grade 11 year (teacher guide — evidence).
**Answer: True** — Unit 1 takes 12 of the 105.
