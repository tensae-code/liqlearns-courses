# Data — raw facts with no meaning yet (Grade 11) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Data is —
a) raw facts with no meaning on their own  b) a finished report  c) a wise decision  d) a computer program
**Answer: a) raw facts with no meaning on their own** — data is the raw material, before processing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which form suits a person's age best?
a) an image  b) a number  c) a song  d) a paragraph
**Answer: b) a number** — age is counted, so numbers suit it.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Satellite imagery has made which form of data common?
a) text  b) numbers  c) images  d) sound
**Answer: c) images** — the book names satellite imagery for image data.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How many units does the Grade 11 course have?
a) four  b) five  c) six  d) seven
**Answer: c) six** — six units, from information systems to programming.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The last unit of the course is —
a) web development  b) database management  c) fundamentals of programming  d) emerging technologies
**Answer: c) fundamentals of programming** — the road ends with a first Python program.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Every unit opens with —
a) an exam  b) brainstorming from daily life  c) a video  d) copying notes
**Answer: b) brainstorming from daily life** — the brainstorm-first rhythm is the book's own way.
