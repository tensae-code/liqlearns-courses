# Data — raw facts with no meaning yet (Grade 11) — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does a bare word or figure mean nothing on its own?
**Answer: Because it means nothing until it is related to other data — nothing in it says what it refers to.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two inclusion instructions that repeat in the guide (teacher guide — evidence).
**Answer: Face the class while lecturing, in case of pupils with hearing difficulties; and use appropriate audio to accommodate visually impaired pupils.**
