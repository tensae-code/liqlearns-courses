# Video script — Data — raw facts with no meaning yet (Grade 11)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. (Full script — the placement names a full script for this lesson.)

---

Six units. One road.

First: information systems — data, information, knowledge, wisdom, and the systems people use every day.

Second: emerging technologies — new tools changing work and life.

Third: database management — keeping data in one shared store.

Fourth: web development — building pages of your own.

Fifth: hardware troubleshooting — finding faults and preventing them.

Sixth: fundamentals of programming — ending with your first Python program.

Every unit starts the same way: brainstorming, from your own daily life. Then sections, activities, a summary, key terms, and a review.

Start where the road starts: with data — raw facts, waiting for meaning.
