# Hands-on — The Business Plan and Its Seven Key Sections

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 6 §6.1 Business plan and §6.1.1 Define business plan and its components (executive summary, business description, market strategies, competitor analysis, design and development plan, operations and management, financial documents) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach the business plan and its seven key sections to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
