# Hands-on — Knowledge — information put into action

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Write it or ride it
Pick a task in your home. Write the instructions for it as fully as you can — that is the explicit part. Then do the task and note every judgement your hands made that the writing missed. That missed part is tacit.

## Learn by the four ways
Learn one small new thing four ways in one week: read about it, do it, discuss it, observe someone doing it. Note which way turned information into knowledge fastest for you.
