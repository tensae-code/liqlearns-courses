# Knowledge — information put into action — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Knowledge is the personal capability to think and act to solve a problem or finish a task.
**Answer: True** — the book's own closing definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tacit knowledge is the smaller of the two kinds (teacher guide — evidence).
**Answer: False** — the guide calls it the larger, unshareable kind.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Driving a car well is the guide's example of explicit knowledge (teacher guide — evidence).
**Answer: False** — driving well is its tacit example.
