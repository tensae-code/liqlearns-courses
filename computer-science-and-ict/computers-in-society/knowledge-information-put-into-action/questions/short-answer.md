# Knowledge — information put into action — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four ways knowledge is built.
**Answer: Reading, doing activities, discussing and observing.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why can tacit knowledge not simply be sent as a document? (teacher guide — evidence)
**Answer: Because it lives in the knower and is tied to skill gained in action; it can only be passed on by working together.**
