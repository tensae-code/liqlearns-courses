# Video script — Knowledge — information put into action

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. (Short script — the placement names no full video need for this lesson, so the script is short.)

---

I can tell you how to ride a bicycle.

That telling is information.

Now ride it. That riding is knowledge.

Knowledge is understanding you can act on — and it includes skill.
