# Hands-on — Market Research and the Business Pitch

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 9 §9.3 Collecting Market Insights (§9.3.1 market research techniques — qualitative and quantitative, customer surveys and focus groups; §9.3.2 analyzing market data — trends, segmentation, competitor and predictive analysis; §9.3.3 the business pitch; §9.3.4 steps to pitching your idea; §9.3.5 importance of pitching) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach market research and the business pitch to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
