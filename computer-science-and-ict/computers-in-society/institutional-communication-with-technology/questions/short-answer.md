# Institutional Communication With Technology — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an institution, in this lesson?**
**Answer: An organised body like a school, office or ministry**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three things official communication carries.**
**Answer: Notices, orders and reports (also records)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do shared documents give an institution?**
**Answer: One true version**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When is a digital message official?**
**Answer: It comes through the institution's channel, from a named sender, and is kept as a record**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What travels with the new tools?**
**Answer: The institution's rules and formality**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name two things institutions tell the public.**
**Answer: Announcements and service information (also answers, emergency warnings)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book place this in the Asia-Pacific experience?**
**Answer: Institutions across the region communicate the same way**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must be published before rumours?**
**Answer: The facts**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What single thing must the public be able to check?**
**Answer: One official source**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must the public be able to do, besides receive?**
**Answer: Answer back — and be answered**
