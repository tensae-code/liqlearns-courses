# Hands-on — Institutional Communication With Technology

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Official or not?** — Collect five messages claiming to speak for your school. Judge each by the lesson's three tests — channel, named sender, record — and report the verdicts.

## Task two
**Write the announcement** — Draft one institutional announcement two ways: the paper notice and the digital message. Keep the facts identical; note what each road gains and loses.
