# Video script — Institutional Communication With Technology

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An **institution** — a school, an office, a ministry — runs on official communication: notices, orders, reports and records moving between its parts. Technology gives that movement new roads: email and messaging for speed, shared documents for one true version, databases and websites for records anyone authorised can reach. The institution's rules travel with the tools: a message is **official** when it comes through the institution's channel, from its named sender, and is kept as a record — the same formality the paper world kept, at digital speed.
Outward, the institution speaks to its citizens and customers: announcements, service information, answers to questions, warnings in emergencies. The Grade 8 experience places this in the Asia-Pacific region's shared life — institutions across countries communicating the same way, so a citizen moving between them already knows the roads. Good institutional communication is the transparency lesson practised daily: facts published before rumours, one official source to check, and a way for the public to answer back and be answered.
