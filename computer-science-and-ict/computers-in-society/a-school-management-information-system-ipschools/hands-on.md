# Hands-on — A School Management Information System — ipSchools

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace one mark
Follow one pupil's mark on paper: written by a teacher, entered into ipSchools, and printed in the results table. Say who touches it at each step.

## Enter once, report many
List three different reports one set of school data could produce — for a teacher, for the head teacher, for a parent.
