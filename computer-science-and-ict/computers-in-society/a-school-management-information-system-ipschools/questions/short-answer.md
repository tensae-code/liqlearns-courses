# A School Management Information System — ipSchools — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is ipSchools?**
**Answer: A school management information system — a networked system that runs a school's working data**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What data does it hold?**
**Answer: Pupils' records, classes, attendance and marks**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the system replace?**
**Answer: Passing paper from office to office**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Whose real school appears in the book's example?**
**Answer: A Bakht Al-Ruda school**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What report does the book work through?**
**Answer: A school results table with every pupil's marks**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How many times is the data entered?**
**Answer: Once — then reported many ways**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name three people the reports serve.**
**Answer: The administration, teachers and parents**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is ipSchools the unit's model system?**
**Answer: It joins computers, a network and shared data to do one institution's whole job**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of system is it, in the course's words?**
**Answer: A management information system**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the pattern of every such system?**
**Answer: Enter data once; produce the reports each user needs**
