## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What ipSchools is** — Sudan's Grade 6 book shows a networked system at work in a real school: **ipSchools**, a **school management information system**. It holds the school's working data in one system — pupils' records, classes, attendance and marks — and the school's people use it over the network instead of passing paper from office to office. It is the book's model of everything the unit has taught: computers, a network and shared data, doing one institution's whole job.
2. **The system's reports** — A management system earns its keep in its **reports**: the book works through ipSchools reports, among them a real **school results table** from a Bakht Al-Ruda school — every pupil's marks gathered by the system into the table the school prints and posts. Data entered once, in classrooms and offices, comes out as the lists the administration, teachers and parents each need. That is the pattern of every management information system: enter once, report many ways.
