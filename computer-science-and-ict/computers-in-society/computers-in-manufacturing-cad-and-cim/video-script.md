# Video script — Computers in Manufacturing — CAD and CIM

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**CAD — computer-aided design** — is **drawing and designing a product on a computer** instead of on paper. The designer draws the part once, then **turns it, zooms it and changes it** on screen; a mistake costs an edit, not a new model. The finished design is exact numbers, ready to hand to the machines that will make the thing. Rwanda's guide files CAD under the application areas of computers (teacher guide — evidence).
**CIM — computer-integrated manufacturing** — goes one step further: the **whole factory is linked by computer**, from the CAD design to the **machines that cut, weld and assemble**, to the **stock counts** of parts and finished goods. The design file itself can drive the machine, so what is made matches what was drawn. CAD designs the thing; CIM organises **making it, start to finish**.
