# Computers in Manufacturing — CAD and CIM — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does CAD stand for?**
a) Computer-aided design  b) Drawing and designing a product on a computer  c) Turn it, zoom it or change it on screen  d) An edit, not a new model
**Answer: a) Computer-aided design**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is CAD?**
a) Turn it, zoom it or change it on screen  b) Drawing and designing a product on a computer  c) An edit, not a new model  d) Under the application areas of computers (teacher guide — evidence)
**Answer: b) Drawing and designing a product on a computer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing a designer can do to a CAD drawing.**
a) An edit, not a new model  b) Under the application areas of computers (teacher guide — evidence)  c) Turn it, zoom it or change it on screen  d) Computer-aided design
**Answer: c) Turn it, zoom it or change it on screen**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a mistake cost in CAD?**
a) Under the application areas of computers (teacher guide — evidence)  b) Computer-aided design  c) Drawing and designing a product on a computer  d) An edit, not a new model
**Answer: d) An edit, not a new model**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Rwanda's guide file CAD?**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does CIM stand for?**
a) The whole factory — design, machines and stock counts  b) Computer-integrated manufacturing  c) The design file itself  d) Because the design file drives the machine
