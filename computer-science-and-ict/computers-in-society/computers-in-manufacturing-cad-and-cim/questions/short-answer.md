# Computers in Manufacturing — CAD and CIM — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does CAD stand for?**
**Answer: Computer-aided design**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is CAD?**
**Answer: Drawing and designing a product on a computer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing a designer can do to a CAD drawing.**
**Answer: Turn it, zoom it or change it on screen**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a mistake cost in CAD?**
**Answer: An edit, not a new model**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Rwanda's guide file CAD?**
**Answer: Under the application areas of computers (teacher guide — evidence)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does CIM stand for?**
**Answer: Computer-integrated manufacturing**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is linked in CIM?**
**Answer: The whole factory — design, machines and stock counts**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can drive the machine in CIM?**
**Answer: The design file itself**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the product match the drawing in CIM?**
**Answer: Because the design file drives the machine**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the difference between CAD and CIM?**
**Answer: CAD designs the thing; CIM organises making it start to finish**
