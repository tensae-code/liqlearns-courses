Provenance scope note: the placements stocked here are from Kazakhstan's okulyk.kz (OKULYK) textbooks, recommended by the Ministry of Education and Science of the Republic of Kazakhstan — the Digital Literacy and Informatika lines of the Almatykitap, Arman-PV and Atamura teams and the Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools (NIS) books, all student books except the two Kadirkulov teacher guides named in the entries below, whose placements are stocked exactly as filed. Many books exist in parallel language editions (Kazakh / Russian / Uzbek / Uyghur / English) of the same textbook: each distinct book is taught once, and every distinct edition is named and cited in the book entry. Kazakhstan's own ways are kept: one textbook line in up to five parallel language editions, the okulyk.kz digital library as the books' home, Scratch from Grade 1 and robotics strands (LEGO WeDo 2.0 and Mindstorms EV3) from Grade 1–2, and every keyword given in Kazakh–Russian–English in the early grades. Media slots in lesson/lesson.md are described slots only ([Picture — …]); no picture numbers are invented.


Kazakhstan's own way (Kopeeva G5 Informatika): Grade 5 Informatika (the older, pre-"Digital Literacy" subject name) is built around Scratch as a "game programming environment": a whole chapter runs from a first program through blocks, animation graphics, sound effects and dialogue to linear/branching/cyclic commands; earlier chapters pair Internet safety with data protection, and the year ends with print-ready project work — a Word newspaper page with WordArt and columns, presented as slides.

Kazakhstan's own way (Mukhametzhanova G5 Informatika, 2017 edition): the 2017 Atamura Grade 5 book follows the older national programme shared with the Arman-PV book of the same year: computer safety, information and raster graphics, algorithms through labyrinths (real and virtual), a first Scratch program and "living graphics", and project work ending in a print-ready document; a full Scratch block dictionary is printed as an appendix (printed pp. 119–127). The book was written with Microsoft "Partnership in Education" material.

Kazakhstan's own way (Mukametzhanova G5 «Информатика» 2017 edition): the 2017 Atamura Grade 5 book opens with computer safety and a mini-project «Открытия, изменившие мир» (Discoveries that changed the world), teaches algorithms as following commands and escaping labyrinths (real and virtual), and the first program in Scratch with "graphics come alive".
