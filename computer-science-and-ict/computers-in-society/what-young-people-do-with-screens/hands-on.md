# Hands-on — What Young People Do With Screens

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Class practices survey** — In teams, write five practice questions, survey the class anonymously, tally by family, and chart the result. Present one surprise from your data.

## Task two
**My screen day, sorted** — Keep one day's screen log with times. Sort every entry into the five families and total each. Which family owns your day?
