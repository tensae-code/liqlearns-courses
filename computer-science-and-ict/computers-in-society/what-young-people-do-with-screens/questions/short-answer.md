# What Young People Do With Screens — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the five practice families.**
**Answer: Learning, communicating, playing, watching, creating**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why is “screen time” alone a weak measure?**
**Answer: The same hour can be homework, friendship, rest or making**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which practice includes messages and calls?**
**Answer: Communicating**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What question comes before “how much”?**
**Answer: “Doing what?”**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the scenario place this survey?**
**Answer: Scénario 10, Grades 5–6, first séance**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do pupils become in this scenario?**
**Answer: Surveyors of their own generation**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What should survey questions ask about?**
**Answer: Practices, not judgements**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When should answers be anonymous?**
**Answer: Where truth needs it**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must the report follow?**
**Answer: What the tally says, even when it surprises**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is presented at the end?**
**Answer: What young people around you actually do with screens**
