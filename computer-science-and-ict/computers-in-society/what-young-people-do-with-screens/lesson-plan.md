## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **One screen, many practices** — “The screen” is not one activity. The scenario sorts young people's practices into families: **learning** (lessons, research, tutorials), **communicating** (messages, calls, shared games with friends), **playing**, **watching** (video, streams), and **creating** (drawing, music, video, code). The same hour of “screen time” can be homework, friendship, rest or making — judging the hour without its practice judges nothing.
2. **Surveying honestly** — The scenario turns pupils into surveyors of their own generation: design a few questions, ask the class, tally the practices, and present what young people around you actually do. Honest surveying has rules: ask about practices, not judgements; let answers be anonymous where truth needs it; and report what the tally says — even when it surprises the adults, or you.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — “How much screen time?” is the question adults ask. This scenario asks the question before it: “Doing what?” List yesterday's screen minutes by what you were actually doing. The list is the lesson's raw material. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of one screen, many practices as the lesson describes it — the student studies the picture and names what each part shows.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of surveying honestly in action — the student watches once, then says what changed and why.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key names and definitions aloud, in the order the lesson gives them — the student listens once through, then reads the same lines aloud along with the voice. (Audio is a described slot in this build; no audio file is made.)
3. **Say it to your companion** — the companion block in lesson/lesson.md.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-false, matching, short-answer — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.
