# Printing Responsibly — Only Print What You Need — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does printing spend?**
**Answer: Paper, ink and energy**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first responsible-printing question?**
**Answer: Do I need this on paper at all?**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the second question?**
**Answer: Do I need all of it, or only part?**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When is the pause taken?**
**Answer: Before printing, every time**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is the habit taught in Décodage?**
**Answer: As a class habit in the Parcours, not as a scolding**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How can pages be saved when printing?**
**Answer: Print only the pages that matter, and use both sides when allowed**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why check the preview?**
**Answer: So a mistake is not printed twice**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How can one copy serve several readers?**
**Answer: Share the printed copy between readers instead of one each**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does a page thrown away unread prove?**
**Answer: That it was never needed at all**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name the habit in one phrase.**
**Answer: Only print what you need**
