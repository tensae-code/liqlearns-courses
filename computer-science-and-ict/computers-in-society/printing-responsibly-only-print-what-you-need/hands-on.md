# Hands-on — Printing Responsibly — Only Print What You Need

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**The class print audit** — For one day, keep every printed page the class produces. At the end, sort them: needed / could have stayed on screen / printed by mistake. Count each pile.

## Task two
**Preview before paper** — Next time you print, open the preview first and cut the job to the pages you truly need. Say how many pages you saved.
