# Cybercrime — and defending your country's digital resources — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Violating intellectual property with a computer can be cybercrime.
**Answer: True** — it is one of the named ends.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Defending digital resources is only the government's job.
**Answer: False** — pupils prepare as mindful users; the effort is collaborative.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** National cyber-attack reports are the guide's research source for attack types and defences.
**Answer: True** — from the designated offices (teacher guide — evidence).
