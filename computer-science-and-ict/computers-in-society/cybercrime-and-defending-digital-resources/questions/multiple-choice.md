# Cybercrime — and defending your country's digital resources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cybercrime is —
a) any computer use  b) using a computer to further illegal ends  c) playing games at night  d) slow internet
**Answer: b) using a computer to further illegal ends** — the definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a named end of cybercrime?
a) fraud  b) homework  c) shopping  d) photography
**Answer: a) fraud** — with identity theft and violations of privacy and IP.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Cybercrime has grown because —
a) computers became central to education, commerce, entertainment and government  b) computers became rarer  c) laws vanished  d) schools closed
**Answer: a) computers became central to education, commerce, entertainment and government** — crime follows centrality.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** INSA reports that attacks on Ethiopia are —
a) finished  b) increasing  c) imaginary  d) only historical
**Answer: b) increasing** — the reported trend.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A cyber warrior is —
a) a game character  b) a trained information-security professional who manages networks and systems  c) a hacker for hire  d) a soldier with a laptop brand
**Answer: b) a trained information-security professional who manages networks and systems** — the book's career name.
