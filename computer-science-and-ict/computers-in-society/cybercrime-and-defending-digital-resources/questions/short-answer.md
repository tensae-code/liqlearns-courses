# Cybercrime — and defending your country's digital resources — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the illegal ends the book lists for cybercrime.
**Answer:** Fraud, stealing identities, and violating privacy and intellectual property.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are pupils to prepare as, and what does INSA report?
**Answer:** Mindful users protecting personal, organisational and national digital resources; INSA reports attacks on Ethiopia are increasing.
