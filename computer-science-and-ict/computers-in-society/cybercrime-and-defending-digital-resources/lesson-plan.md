# Lesson plan — Cybercrime — and defending your country's digital resources

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** Cybercrime — and defending your country's digital resources
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 14 — Computers in Society of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 12, Unit 1 — Information Systems and Their Applications. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** cybercrime is using a computer to further illegal ends — fraud, stealing identities, violating privacy and intellectual property; INSA reports attacks on Ethiopia increasing, and pupils prepare as mindful users — some toward becoming cyber warriors.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the security and plagiarism lessons just before it (recommended first, never forced) and needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What cybercrime is** — a computer used to further illegal ends: fraud, identity theft, violating privacy and intellectual property; grown with computers' centrality.
2. **Ethiopia's attacks — and the cyber warrior** — INSA reports increasing attacks; collaborative effort; mindful users; the cyber-warrior path.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson

1. Define cybercrime and name its ends.
2. Say what INSA reports about attacks on Ethiopia.
3. Describe the cyber warrior and the pupil's preparation.
