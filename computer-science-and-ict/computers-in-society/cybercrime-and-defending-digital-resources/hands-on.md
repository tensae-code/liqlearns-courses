# Hands-on — Cybercrime — and defending your country's digital resources

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Mindful-user audit
Take one device you use. Walk it as a defender: passwords, updates, what you would never open, what you would report and to whom. Write the audit as five lines a younger pupil could follow.

## Career sketch
From the book's description, sketch the cyber warrior's week: what systems, what attacks watched for, what reports read. One page, drawn from the guide's research route.
