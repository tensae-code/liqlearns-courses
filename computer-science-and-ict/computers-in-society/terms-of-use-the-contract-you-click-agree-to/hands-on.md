# Hands-on — Terms of Use the Contract You Click Agree to

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 7 §7.1 Understanding Terms of Use (§7.1.1 importance; §7.1.2 common clauses — user obligations, limitations of liability, privacy and data use, intellectual property rights, termination; §7.1.3 legal ramifications; §7.1.4 ethical considerations; §7.1.5 personal rights Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach terms of use the contract you click agree to to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
