# Using Technology Ethically — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the three habits for information?**
a) Respect ownership, be honest, and keep confidences  b) Permission, following the rules for copying, and crediting what you use  c) Correct what you shared  d) Information given privately stays private
**Answer: a) Respect ownership, be honest, and keep confidences**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does respecting ownership require when you use another's work?**
a) Correct what you shared  b) Permission, following the rules for copying, and crediting what you use  c) Information given privately stays private  d) In the classroom's own information life — essays, searches and shared files
**Answer: b) Permission, following the rules for copying, and crediting what you use**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does honesty require when shared information proves false?**
a) Information given privately stays private  b) In the classroom's own information life — essays, searches and shared files  c) Correct what you shared  d) Care, fairness and repair
**Answer: c) Correct what you shared**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does keeping confidences mean?**
a) In the classroom's own information life — essays, searches and shared files  b) Care, fairness and repair  c) Leave them — and shared accounts and networks — as you found them, or better  d) Information given privately stays private
**Answer: d) Information given privately stays private**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Fiji apply these rules first?**
a) In the classroom's own information life — essays, searches and shared files  b) Care, fairness and repair  c) Leave them — and shared accounts and networks — as you found them, or better  d) Locking others out of what the class shares, and using the machine for an unfair edge in your own work
**Answer: a) In the classroom's own information life — essays, searches and shared files**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are the three rules for machines and people?**
a) Leave them — and shared accounts and networks — as you found them, or better  b) Care, fairness and repair  c) Locking others out of what the class shares, and using the machine for an unfair edge in your own work  d) When your use harms someone, say so and put it right
**Answer: b) Care, fairness and repair**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does care mean for shared computers?**
a) Locking others out of what the class shares, and using the machine for an unfair edge in your own work  b) When your use harms someone, say so and put it right  c) Leave them — and shared accounts and networks — as you found them, or better  d) A person whose information and machine habits others never have to guard against
**Answer: c) Leave them — and shared accounts and networks — as you found them, or better**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does fairness forbid?**
a) When your use harms someone, say so and put it right  b) A person whose information and machine habits others never have to guard against  c) Respect ownership, be honest, and keep confidences  d) Locking others out of what the class shares, and using the machine for an unfair edge in your own work
**Answer: d) Locking others out of what the class shares, and using the machine for an unfair edge in your own work**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does repair require?**
a) When your use harms someone, say so and put it right  b) A person whose information and machine habits others never have to guard against  c) Respect ownership, be honest, and keep confidences  d) Permission, following the rules for copying, and crediting what you use
**Answer: a) When your use harms someone, say so and put it right**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is Fiji's closing picture of the ethical user?**
a) Respect ownership, be honest, and keep confidences  b) A person whose information and machine habits others never have to guard against  c) Permission, following the rules for copying, and crediting what you use  d) Correct what you shared
