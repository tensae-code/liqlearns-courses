# Using Technology Ethically — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the three habits for information?**
**Answer: Respect ownership, be honest, and keep confidences**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does respecting ownership require when you use another's work?**
**Answer: Permission, following the rules for copying, and crediting what you use**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does honesty require when shared information proves false?**
**Answer: Correct what you shared**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does keeping confidences mean?**
**Answer: Information given privately stays private**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Fiji apply these rules first?**
**Answer: In the classroom's own information life — essays, searches and shared files**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are the three rules for machines and people?**
**Answer: Care, fairness and repair**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does care mean for shared computers?**
**Answer: Leave them — and shared accounts and networks — as you found them, or better**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does fairness forbid?**
**Answer: Locking others out of what the class shares, and using the machine for an unfair edge in your own work**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does repair require?**
**Answer: When your use harms someone, say so and put it right**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is Fiji's closing picture of the ethical user?**
**Answer: A person whose information and machine habits others never have to guard against**
