# Using Technology Ethically — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are the three habits for information?" is: Respect ownership, be honest, and keep confidences**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does respecting ownership require when you use another's work?" is: Correct what you shared**
**Answer: False** — Permission, following the rules for copying, and crediting what you use

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does honesty require when shared information proves false?" is: Correct what you shared**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does keeping confidences mean?" is: In the classroom's own information life — essays, searches and shared files**
**Answer: False** — Information given privately stays private

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does Fiji apply these rules first?" is: In the classroom's own information life — essays, searches and shared files**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What are the three rules for machines and people?" is: Leave them — and shared accounts and networks — as you found them, or better**
**Answer: False** — Care, fairness and repair

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does care mean for shared computers?" is: Leave them — and shared accounts and networks — as you found them, or better**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does fairness forbid?" is: When your use harms someone, say so and put it right**
**Answer: False** — Locking others out of what the class shares, and using the machine for an unfair edge in your own work

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does repair require?" is: When your use harms someone, say so and put it right**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is Fiji's closing picture of the ethical user?" is: Respect ownership, be honest, and keep confidences**
**Answer: False** — A person whose information and machine habits others never have to guard against
