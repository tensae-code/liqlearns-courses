# Reading Keys for Digital Technology — Modularity, Delegation, Responsibility — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does modularity mean?** → **A technology is built from smaller parts that can be combined, replaced and reused**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a technology's socio-technical environment?** → **The people, habits, rules and other machines around it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two modules of a phone.** → **Camera and screen (also: battery, programs, network)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the Décodage guides put the reading keys?** → **In the Introduction of every cycle's guide**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why name a tool's parts and surroundings?** → **Because a tool can only be understood that way**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is delegation?** → **Handing work over to the machine**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Does delegating erase responsibility?** → **No — the person who delegates still answers for the result**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does pluralism say?** → **There is never only one tool, one answer or one way — several ways exist and can be compared**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who answers when a delegated tool goes wrong?** → **The person who delegated and should have checked**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Give an example of delegation.** → **Letting the map navigate (also: an alarm remembering, a spell-checker watching)**
