# Reading Keys for Digital Technology — Modularity, Delegation, Responsibility — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does modularity mean?**
**Answer: A technology is built from smaller parts that can be combined, replaced and reused**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a technology's socio-technical environment?**
**Answer: The people, habits, rules and other machines around it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two modules of a phone.**
**Answer: Camera and screen (also: battery, programs, network)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the Décodage guides put the reading keys?**
**Answer: In the Introduction of every cycle's guide**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why name a tool's parts and surroundings?**
**Answer: Because a tool can only be understood that way**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is delegation?**
**Answer: Handing work over to the machine**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Does delegating erase responsibility?**
**Answer: No — the person who delegates still answers for the result**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does pluralism say?**
**Answer: There is never only one tool, one answer or one way — several ways exist and can be compared**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who answers when a delegated tool goes wrong?**
**Answer: The person who delegated and should have checked**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Give an example of delegation.**
**Answer: Letting the map navigate (also: an alarm remembering, a spell-checker watching)**
