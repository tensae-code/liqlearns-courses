# Hands-on — Reading Keys for Digital Technology — Modularity, Delegation, Responsibility

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Take a tool apart on paper** — Choose one classroom device. List its modules, its environment, what it does for you by delegation, and who is responsible for it.

## Task two
**One tool, three ways** — Name one job — remembering a date — and three different tools or ways to do it. Say which you would choose and why: pluralism at work.
