## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Modularity and the socio-technical environment** — Every digital technology is **modular**: it is built from smaller parts that can be combined, replaced and reused — a phone is camera, screen, battery, programs, network, each a module. And no technology lives alone: it sits in a **socio-technical environment** — the people, habits, rules and other machines around it. The Décodage guides open every cycle with these reading keys, because a tool can only be understood by naming its parts and its surroundings.
2. **Delegation, responsibility and pluralism** — **Delegation** is handing work to the machine: the alarm remembers, the map navigates, the spell-checker watches. But delegating does not erase **responsibility** — the person who delegates still answers for the result, so the guides ask who decided, who checked, who is accountable. **Pluralism** completes the keys: there is never only one tool, one answer or one way of living with technology — several ways exist, and they can be compared and chosen between.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Pick any digital tool you used today — a phone, a bus card, a game. Before reading on, ask: what smaller parts is it made of, what work have you handed over to it, and who answers if it goes wrong? Those three questions are this lesson. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of modularity and the socio-technical environment as the lesson describes it — the student studies the picture and names what each part shows.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of delegation, responsibility and pluralism in action — the student watches once, then says what changed and why.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key names and definitions aloud, in the order the lesson gives them — the student listens once through, then reads the same lines aloud along with the voice. (Audio is a described slot in this build; no audio file is made.)
3. **Say it to your companion** — the companion block in lesson/lesson.md.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-false, matching, short-answer — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.
