# Hands-on — Raising a national voice online — the GERD campaign

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Anatomy of a hashtag
Take the GERD movement as your specimen: write its anatomy on one page — trigger (filling), message, tag, platforms, audiences (inward and outward). The anatomy is a reusable campaign skeleton.

## Your agenda, four do's
Plan a small campaign for a school or neighbourhood agenda and test every planned post against the four do's before anything is published. The checklist, signed by the group, is the work.
