# Raising a national voice online — the GERD campaign

## Do it first

One hashtag. Four platforms. A country's citizens, at home and abroad, speaking to the world at the same moment about the same dam. The last lesson defined the good digital citizen; this one shows millions of them at once.

## Sub lesson 1 — The campaign

In **2021** Ethiopians united behind the **Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam** in the **"#It_is_my_dam" social-media movement across Twitter, Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn**.

Its two purposes ran together: **expressing support for the national project** and **creating awareness of its purpose for the global community during dam filling** — citizens speaking inward to each other and outward to the world at once.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why the book teaches it here

The book presents the movement **as citizens curating a positive, effective digital footprint on a national agenda** — the previous lesson's definition, at national scale.

Read it against the four do's: a community made better, voices heard by the global community, one message carried respectfully and consistently. The GERD campaign is the unit's closing proof that digital citizenship is action, not only care.

## Say it to your companion
