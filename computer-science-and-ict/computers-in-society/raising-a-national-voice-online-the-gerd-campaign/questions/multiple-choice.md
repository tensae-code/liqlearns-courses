# Raising a national voice online — the GERD campaign — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The GERD social-media movement happened in —
a) 2015  b) 2021  c) 2024  d) 2010
**Answer: b) 2021** — the book's year.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The movement's hashtag was —
a) #MyRiver  b) #It_is_my_dam  c) #EthiopiaRises  d) #BlueNile
**Answer: b) #It_is_my_dam** — the named tag.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The platforms named are —
a) only Twitter  b) Twitter, Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn  c) only Telegram  d) radio and television
**Answer: b) Twitter, Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn** — the four named platforms.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The campaign's purposes were —
a) selling electricity  b) expressing support for the national project and creating global awareness during dam filling  c) tourism only  d) recruitment
**Answer: b) expressing support for the national project and creating global awareness during dam filling** — inward support, outward awareness.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The book presents the movement as citizens curating —
a) a protest  b) a positive, effective digital footprint on a national agenda  c) an advertisement  d) a news report
**Answer: b) a positive, effective digital footprint on a national agenda** — the citizenship frame.
