# Digital Traces — What You Leave Behind Online — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name a deliberate trace.**
**Answer: A posted photo (also: a signed comment, a filled profile)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name a recorded trace.**
**Answer: Connection times (also: reported places, lingered pages, typed searches)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do the traces together form?**
**Answer: Your digital footprint (empreinte numérique)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Who can read your footprint?**
**Answer: Anyone who can access it — including people you will never meet**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When does Décodage first teach traces?**
**Answer: In the Parcours du partage, Grades 3–4**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Can a footprint be switched off?**
**Answer: No — but it can be walked carefully**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two careful-walking habits.**
**Answer: Share less by default; keep private things off public ground**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What multiplies traces?**
**Answer: Hyperconnected life across every device and account**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) To whom does your footprint speak?**
**Answer: A stranger, an employer, a future self**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What should you check about a service?**
**Answer: Who it shares your data with**
