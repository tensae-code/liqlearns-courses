# Hands-on — Digital Traces — What You Leave Behind Online

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Map your morning footprint** — List everything a person did online before school. Beside each act, write the trace it left and who could read it.

## Task two
**The stranger test** — Write the description of a person using only their (imaginary) footprint: posts, times, places, likes. Swap descriptions — how much of a person is in a trail?
