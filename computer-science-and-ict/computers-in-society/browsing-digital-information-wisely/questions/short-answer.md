# Browsing Digital Information Wisely — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What comes before the first click in wise browsing?**
**Answer: Naming what you are looking for — the purpose decides which sources can answer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which source fits a rule or regulation?**
**Answer: An official site — the body that made the rule**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which source fits a product's own specifications?**
**Answer: The maker's own page**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Indonesian book's phrase for this lesson's skill?**
**Answer: Menjelajah informasi digital dengan bijak — exploring digital information wisely**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does purpose matter at the search box?**
**Answer: The search box opens onto every kind of page at once; purpose tells which doorway fits**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What three questions does the wise browser ask of a page?**
**Answer: Who made it, what they gain if it is believed or shared, and what evidence it offers**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What should be checked beside the maker's gain?**
**Answer: The page's date and the evidence it offers — and a comparison with another source**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What are the two quiet costs of browsing?**
**Answer: The trail you leave and the time you spend**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does wisdom do with a page that does not serve the purpose?**
**Answer: Closes it — and keeps what does serve in a place it can be found again**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this lesson?**
**Answer: Grade 11, Bab I A.2 — exploring digital information wisely**
