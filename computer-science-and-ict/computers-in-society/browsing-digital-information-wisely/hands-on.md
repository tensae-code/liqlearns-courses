# Hands-on — Browsing Digital Information Wisely

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Purpose-first search** — Write one real question you need answered. Name the kind of source that fits it, find that source, and write one sentence on why it fits better than the first result the search offered.

## Task two
**Read a page's intentions** — Open a page you use often. List: who made it, what it wants from you, its date, and one claim it makes — then find a second source for that claim and say whether they agree.
