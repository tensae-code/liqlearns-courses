# Hands-on — Screens in the City — Devices in Public Places

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Read your city** — From photos or a walk, list the public screens on one street or in one station. For each: what does it show or let you do?

## Task two
**Queue craft** — Role-play a ticket machine with a queue behind you: read first, choose, shield your code, take everything, leave it ready. The queue judges.
