# Screens in the City — Devices in Public Places — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Departure boards, ticket machines, cash machines (also: parking machines, information points)**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Departure boards, ticket machines, cash machines (also: parking machines, information points)** — as the answer to: How are public screens used?
**Answer: False — Standing up, quickly, among strangers**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Readable at a glance**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Readable at a glance** — as the answer to: Who do public screens belong to?
**Answer: False — Everyone and no one — they are shared**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: With pictures and walks to read the pupils' own city**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: With pictures and walks to read the pupils' own city** — as the answer to: What do you do before touching a public screen?
**Answer: False — Read the whole screen first**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Private entries like codes, from other eyes**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Private entries like codes, from other eyes** — as the answer to: What three things do you take away?
**Answer: False — Your ticket, card and change**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Ready for the next person**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Ready for the next person** — as the answer to: Why does care count double in public?
**Answer: False — Because the machine serves a queue of strangers**
