# Screens in the City — Devices in Public Places — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name three city screens.**
**Answer: Departure boards, ticket machines, cash machines (also: parking machines, information points)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How are public screens used?**
**Answer: Standing up, quickly, among strangers**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must a public screen be?**
**Answer: Readable at a glance**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Who do public screens belong to?**
**Answer: Everyone and no one — they are shared**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How does Décodage teach this scenario?**
**Answer: With pictures and walks to read the pupils' own city**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do before touching a public screen?**
**Answer: Read the whole screen first**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must you shield?**
**Answer: Private entries like codes, from other eyes**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What three things do you take away?**
**Answer: Your ticket, card and change**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do you leave the device?**
**Answer: Ready for the next person**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does care count double in public?**
**Answer: Because the machine serves a queue of strangers**
