# Screens in the City — Devices in Public Places — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name three city screens.** → **Departure boards, ticket machines, cash machines (also: parking machines, information points)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How are public screens used?** → **Standing up, quickly, among strangers**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must a public screen be?** → **Readable at a glance**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Who do public screens belong to?** → **Everyone and no one — they are shared**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How does Décodage teach this scenario?** → **With pictures and walks to read the pupils' own city**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do before touching a public screen?** → **Read the whole screen first**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must you shield?** → **Private entries like codes, from other eyes**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What three things do you take away?** → **Your ticket, card and change**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do you leave the device?** → **Ready for the next person**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does care count double in public?** → **Because the machine serves a queue of strangers**
