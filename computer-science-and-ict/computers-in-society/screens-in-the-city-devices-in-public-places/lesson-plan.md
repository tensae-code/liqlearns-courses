## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The city's screens** — A city runs on public screens: departure and arrival boards, ticket and parking machines, cash machines, advertising panels, information points, the display at the bus stop counting minutes. Unlike home screens, these belong to everyone and no one: they are **used standing up, quickly, among strangers**, and they must be readable at a glance. The Décodage scenario takes pupils out — in pictures and on walks — to read their own city.
2. **Using a public device well** — Using a city device well is a small craft: read the whole screen before touching; choose deliberately (the mouse or finger selects — a choice made is an action done); shield private entries like codes from other eyes; take your ticket, card and change; and leave the device ready for the next person. The same care the course teaches for classroom machines applies doubled in public, where the machine serves a queue.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Picture a station or a main street. The city is talking to you through screens: times, prices, directions, warnings. Before reading on, name three city screens you have actually used or read this month. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of the city's screens as the lesson describes it — the student studies the picture and names what each part shows.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of using a public device well in action — the student watches once, then says what changed and why.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key names and definitions aloud, in the order the lesson gives them — the student listens once through, then reads the same lines aloud along with the voice. (Audio is a described slot in this build; no audio file is made.)
3. **Say it to your companion** — the companion block in lesson/lesson.md.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-false, matching, short-answer — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
