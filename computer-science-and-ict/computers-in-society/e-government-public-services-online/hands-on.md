# Hands-on — E-government — public services online

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Design a portal home page
On paper, draw a portal's first screen: the services as labelled doors — business registration, vehicle registry, tax, birth certificates, ID cards. A partner must find two services in under ten seconds; if not, redesign.

## Queue versus phone
Time a pretend queue: five people, one counter, two minutes each. Then 'pay' the same five bills by phone in seconds. Write the two totals — that difference is the efficiency claim, felt.
