# E-government — public services online — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four municipal services the book says can move online.
**Answer: Any four of: business registration, vehicle registry, tax payment, birth certificates, ID cards.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give both sides of the mobile electricity-bill example (teacher guide — evidence).
**Answer: Citizen side: no queueing. Corporation side: automatic data capture and instant management reports.**
