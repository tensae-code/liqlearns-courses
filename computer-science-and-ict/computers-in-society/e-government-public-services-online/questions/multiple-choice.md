# E-government — public services online — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** E-services are —
a) services for computers only  b) public services delivered through technology  c) private games  d) bank loans
**Answer: b) public services delivered through technology** — the book's definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A portal —
a) is one office building  b) gathers an organisation's services into one web interface  c) prints certificates  d) is a phone brand
**Answer: b) gathers an organisation's services into one web interface** — one web door, many services.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's portal example is —
a) the Ministry of Health portal  b) a shopping site  c) a games portal  d) a weather page
**Answer: a) the Ministry of Health portal** — Ethiopia's Ministry of Health.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the guide's electricity example, the citizen gains — (teacher guide — evidence)
a) a longer queue  b) no queueing  c) a paper bill only  d) a free phone
**Answer: b) no queueing** — the bill is paid by mobile, with no queue.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is in the guide's list of reachable citizen services? (teacher guide — evidence)
a) trade-licence renewal  b) buying a car  c) booking a holiday  d) school admission abroad
**Answer: a) trade-licence renewal** — with phone bill payment, birth certificates and tax payment.
