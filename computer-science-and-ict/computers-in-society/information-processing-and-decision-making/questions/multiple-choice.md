# Information Processing and Decision Making — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a decision?**
a) A choice between actions  b) The information under it  c) Wrong choices  d) Late decisions
**Answer: a) A choice between actions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does every real decision depend on?**
a) Wrong choices  b) The information under it  c) Late decisions  d) Summarised, compared, and on time
**Answer: b) The information under it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do wrong numbers make?**
a) Late decisions  b) Summarised, compared, and on time  c) Wrong choices  d) It selects, it shapes, and it arrives in time
**Answer: c) Wrong choices**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does late information make?**
a) Summarised, compared, and on time  b) It selects, it shapes, and it arrives in time  c) Taking from all the data the part this decision turns on  d) Late decisions
**Answer: d) Late decisions**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In what form must a decider receive information?**
a) Summarised, compared, and on time  b) It selects, it shapes, and it arrives in time  c) Taking from all the data the part this decision turns on  d) Totals, averages, comparisons and trends
**Answer: a) Summarised, compared, and on time**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are processing's three services to a decision?**
a) Taking from all the data the part this decision turns on  b) It selects, it shapes, and it arrives in time  c) Totals, averages, comparisons and trends  d) History, not help
**Answer: b) It selects, it shapes, and it arrives in time**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does selecting mean?**
a) Totals, averages, comparisons and trends  b) History, not help  c) Taking from all the data the part this decision turns on  d) A principal deciding class sizes from enrolment summaries (or a shopkeeper deciding orders from sales totals)
**Answer: c) Taking from all the data the part this decision turns on**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What shapes does shaping produce?**
a) History, not help  b) A principal deciding class sizes from enrolment summaries (or a shopkeeper deciding orders from sales totals)  c) A choice between actions  d) Totals, averages, comparisons and trends
**Answer: d) Totals, averages, comparisons and trends**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is information that arrives after the decision?**
a) History, not help  b) A principal deciding class sizes from enrolment summaries (or a shopkeeper deciding orders from sales totals)  c) A choice between actions  d) The information under it
**Answer: a) History, not help**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Give Fiji's everyday example.**
a) A choice between actions  b) A principal deciding class sizes from enrolment summaries (or a shopkeeper deciding orders from sales totals)  c) The information under it  d) Wrong choices
**Answer: b) A principal deciding class sizes from enrolment summaries (or a shopkeeper deciding orders from sales totals)**
