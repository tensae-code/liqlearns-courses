# Information Processing and Decision Making — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a decision?" is: A choice between actions**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does every real decision depend on?" is: Wrong choices**
**Answer: False** — The information under it

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do wrong numbers make?" is: Wrong choices**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does late information make?" is: Summarised, compared, and on time**
**Answer: False** — Late decisions

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "In what form must a decider receive information?" is: Summarised, compared, and on time**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What are processing's three services to a decision?" is: Taking from all the data the part this decision turns on**
**Answer: False** — It selects, it shapes, and it arrives in time

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does selecting mean?" is: Taking from all the data the part this decision turns on**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What shapes does shaping produce?" is: History, not help**
**Answer: False** — Totals, averages, comparisons and trends

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is information that arrives after the decision?" is: History, not help**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Give Fiji's everyday example." is: A choice between actions**
**Answer: False** — A principal deciding class sizes from enrolment summaries (or a shopkeeper deciding orders from sales totals)
