# Information Processing and Decision Making — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a decision?**
**Answer: A choice between actions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does every real decision depend on?**
**Answer: The information under it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do wrong numbers make?**
**Answer: Wrong choices**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does late information make?**
**Answer: Late decisions**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In what form must a decider receive information?**
**Answer: Summarised, compared, and on time**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are processing's three services to a decision?**
**Answer: It selects, it shapes, and it arrives in time**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does selecting mean?**
**Answer: Taking from all the data the part this decision turns on**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What shapes does shaping produce?**
**Answer: Totals, averages, comparisons and trends**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is information that arrives after the decision?**
**Answer: History, not help**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Give Fiji's everyday example.**
**Answer: A principal deciding class sizes from enrolment summaries (or a shopkeeper deciding orders from sales totals)**
