# Risks of Modern Technological Means

## Do it first

The last lesson weighed; this one stares at the heavy side of the scale. The risks of modern technology, named plainly — because unnamed risks cannot be guarded.

## Sub lesson 1 — Risks to the individual

For the **individual**, Jordan names: **addiction and stolen time** (designs that hunt attention), **health harms** (eyes, sleep, posture, the sitting life), **privacy and safety losses** (data taken, strangers reaching, fraud), **mental harms** (comparison, bullying, the fear of missing out), and **skill decay** (the map unread, the sum uncalculated, the memory outsourced and gone). Each risk has the same anatomy: a real benefit, overused or misused or turned against its user.

## Sub lesson 2 — Risks to society

For **society**: **misinformation at speed** (lies outpacing corrections), **division** (groups sealed in their own feeds), **job displacement** (means that do work people lived by), **dependence** (a society that cannot function when its systems stop — and stops often), and the **widening gap** (the connected gaining on the unconnected in every field at once). The lesson closes with the guarding principle the whole Grade 9 unit repeats: risks are answered by **knowledge, habits and laws together** — never by fear alone, and never by pretending the scale's other side is empty.
