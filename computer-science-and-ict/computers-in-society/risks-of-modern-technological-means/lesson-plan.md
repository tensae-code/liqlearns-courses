**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** Risks of Modern Technological Means
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Jordan (2026-11-14) — in the title Computers in Society on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Jordan's NCCD Digital Skills books (Jordan Grade 9 G9T1 Student), because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree and live lesson content were checked for the slug and for near matches before building.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the risks of modern technological means for the individual and society (Jordan Grade 9).
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Risks to the individual** — For the **individual**, Jordan names: **addiction and stolen time** (designs that hunt attention), **health harms** (eyes, sleep, posture, the sitting life), **privacy and safety losses** (data taken, strangers reaching, fraud), **mental harms** (comparison, bullying, the fear of missing out), and **skill decay** (the map unread, the sum uncalculated, the memory outsourced and gone). Each risk has the same anatomy: a real benefit, overused or misused or turned against its user.
2. **Risks to society** — For **society**: **misinformation at speed** (lies outpacing corrections), **division** (groups sealed in their own feeds), **job displacement** (means that do work people lived by), **dependence** (a society that cannot function when its systems stop — and stops often), and the **widening gap** (the connected gaining on the unconnected in every field at once). The lesson closes with the guarding principle the whole Grade 9 unit repeats: risks are answered by **knowledge, habits and laws together** — never by fear alone, and never by pretending the scale's other side is empty.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — The last lesson weighed; this one stares at the heavy side of the scale. The risks of modern technology, named plainly — because unnamed risks cannot be guarded. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of risks to the individual as the lesson describes it — the student studies the picture and names what each part shows.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of risks to society in action — the student watches once, then says what changed and why.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key names and definitions aloud, in the order the lesson gives them — the student listens once through, then reads the same lines aloud along with the voice. (Audio is a described slot in this build; no audio file is made.)
3. **Say it to your companion** — the companion block in lesson/lesson.md.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-false, matching, short-answer — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.
