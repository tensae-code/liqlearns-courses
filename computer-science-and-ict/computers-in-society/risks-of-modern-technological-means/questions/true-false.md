# Risks of Modern Technological Means — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Addiction/stolen time, health harms, privacy and safety losses, mental harms, skill decay**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Addiction/stolen time, health harms, privacy and safety losses, mental harms, skill decay** — as the answer to: What anatomy does every risk share?
**Answer: False — A real benefit, overused, misused or turned against its user**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Eyes, sleep, posture (the sitting life)**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Eyes, sleep, posture (the sitting life)** — as the answer to: Name two mental harms.
**Answer: False — Comparison and bullying (or fear of missing out)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The map unread, the sum uncalculated, the memory outsourced**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The map unread, the sum uncalculated, the memory outsourced** — as the answer to: Name the five society risks.
**Answer: False — Misinformation at speed, division, job displacement, dependence, the widening gap**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A society that cannot function when its systems stop**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A society that cannot function when its systems stop** — as the answer to: How do lies harm at speed?
**Answer: False — They outpace corrections**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Knowledge, habits and laws together**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Knowledge, habits and laws together** — as the answer to: By what are they never answered?
**Answer: False — Fear alone — or pretending the other side is empty**
