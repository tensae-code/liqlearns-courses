# Hands-on — Risks of Modern Technological Means

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Risk anatomy** — Take two risks and write each one's anatomy: the benefit, and the overuse/misuse/turning that makes it a risk.

## Task two
**Guard plan** — For your own technology use, write a guard plan answering three named risks — one knowledge, one habit, one rule.
