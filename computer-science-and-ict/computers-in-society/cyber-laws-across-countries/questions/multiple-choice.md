# Cyber Laws Across Countries — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a cyber law?**
a) A country's law for acts done with computers and networks  b) Computer misuse, data protection, electronic transactions, online copyright, harmful content  c) Each country, its own  d) The Computer Crimes Act and the Personal Data Protection Act
**Answer: a) A country's law for acts done with computers and networks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name areas cyber laws cover.**
a) Each country, its own  b) Computer misuse, data protection, electronic transactions, online copyright, harmful content  c) The Computer Crimes Act and the Personal Data Protection Act  d) No — laws, plural, each stopping at its own border
**Answer: b) Computer misuse, data protection, electronic transactions, online copyright, harmful content**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who makes cyber laws?**
a) The Computer Crimes Act and the Personal Data Protection Act  b) No — laws, plural, each stopping at its own border  c) Each country, its own  d) Lawful in one country and an offence in another
**Answer: c) Each country, its own**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name two Malaysian Acts the chapter names.**
a) No — laws, plural, each stopping at its own border  b) Lawful in one country and an offence in another  c) Several at once — writer, server and reader in different countries  d) The Computer Crimes Act and the Personal Data Protection Act
**Answer: d) The Computer Crimes Act and the Personal Data Protection Act**
