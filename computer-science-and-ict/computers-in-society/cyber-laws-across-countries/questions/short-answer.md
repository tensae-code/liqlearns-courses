# Cyber Laws Across Countries — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a cyber law?**
**Answer: A country's law for acts done with computers and networks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name areas cyber laws cover.**
**Answer: Computer misuse, data protection, electronic transactions, online copyright, harmful content**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who makes cyber laws?**
**Answer: Each country, its own**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name two Malaysian Acts the chapter names.**
**Answer: The Computer Crimes Act and the Personal Data Protection Act**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is there a single world cyber law?**
**Answer: No — laws, plural, each stopping at its own border**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What can the same online act be, across countries?**
**Answer: Lawful in one country and an offence in another**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How many countries can one network act touch?**
**Answer: Several at once — writer, server and reader in different countries**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is Malaysia's practical caution?**
**Answer: Find out whose law you stand under before acting**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where laws conflict, what should you obey?**
**Answer: The stricter law**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is law, in the chapter's closing line?**
**Answer: Ethics with a border and a penalty**
