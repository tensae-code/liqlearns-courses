# Cyber Laws Across Countries — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a cyber law?" is: A country's law for acts done with computers and networks**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name areas cyber laws cover." is: Each country, its own**
**Answer: False** — Computer misuse, data protection, electronic transactions, online copyright, harmful content

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Who makes cyber laws?" is: Each country, its own**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name two Malaysian Acts the chapter names." is: No — laws, plural, each stopping at its own border**
**Answer: False** — The Computer Crimes Act and the Personal Data Protection Act

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Is there a single world cyber law?" is: No — laws, plural, each stopping at its own border**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What can the same online act be, across countries?" is: Several at once — writer, server and reader in different countries**
**Answer: False** — Lawful in one country and an offence in another

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How many countries can one network act touch?" is: Several at once — writer, server and reader in different countries**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is Malaysia's practical caution?" is: The stricter law**
**Answer: False** — Find out whose law you stand under before acting

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where laws conflict, what should you obey?" is: The stricter law**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is law, in the chapter's closing line?" is: A country's law for acts done with computers and networks**
**Answer: False** — Ethics with a border and a penalty
