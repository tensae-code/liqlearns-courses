# Hands-on — Cyber Laws Across Countries

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Whose law?', 'For each act, name whose laws could judge it and what you would check first: (a) a pupil in Malaysia posts a photo taken in Singapore, stored on a US server; (b) a shop in Malaysia sells downloads to buyers in three countries.')

## Task two
('Compare two Acts', 'Take one area (breaking into a system) and compare Malaysia's Act with one other country's equivalent your teacher names: scope, one difference in definition, and the penalty each states.')
