# How Information Becomes News — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does news begin as?**
**Answer: Information — an event someone witnessed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do reporters gather?**
**Answer: Who, what, where, when and why**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a news agency do?**
**Answer: Collects reports from many places and shares them with many newsrooms**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why do a hundred papers tell one event in similar words?**
**Answer: They share agency reports**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is gatekeeping?**
**Answer: Editors choosing what becomes news**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three roads news is published on.**
**Answer: Print, broadcast and online**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What can online stories do that print cannot?**
**Answer: Be updated as facts arrive**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is online speed's danger?**
**Answer: Errors reach millions before fact-checking catches them**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does fact-checking stand on the road?**
**Answer: Between gathering and publishing — and after, as corrections**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the reader's defence?**
**Answer: Verify before you share**
