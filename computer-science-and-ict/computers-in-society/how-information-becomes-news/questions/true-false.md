# How Information Becomes News — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Information — an event someone witnessed**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Information — an event someone witnessed** — as the answer to: What do reporters gather?
**Answer: False — Who, what, where, when and why**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Collects reports from many places and shares them with many newsrooms**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Collects reports from many places and shares them with many newsrooms** — as the answer to: Why do a hundred papers tell one event in similar words?
**Answer: False — They share agency reports**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Editors choosing what becomes news**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Editors choosing what becomes news** — as the answer to: Name three roads news is published on.
**Answer: False — Print, broadcast and online**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Be updated as facts arrive**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Be updated as facts arrive** — as the answer to: What is online speed's danger?
**Answer: False — Errors reach millions before fact-checking catches them**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Between gathering and publishing — and after, as corrections**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Between gathering and publishing — and after, as corrections** — as the answer to: What is the reader's defence?
**Answer: False — Verify before you share**
