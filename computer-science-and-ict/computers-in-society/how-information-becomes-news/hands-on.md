# Hands-on — How Information Becomes News

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Trace one story** — Pick one news story and find it in two outlets. Mark what is agency-shared (the same in both) and what each outlet added or chose.

## Task two
**Walk an event to news** — Invent a school event. Write its journey: witness, reporter, editor's choice, headline, one online update — and the fact-check at each step.
