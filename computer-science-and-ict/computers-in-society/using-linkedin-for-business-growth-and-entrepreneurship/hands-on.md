# Hands-on — Using LinkedIn for Business Growth and Entrepreneurship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Do the book's steps** — Work through Exercise — how LinkedIn can promote business growth and entrepreneurship in South Sudan exactly as South Sudan's book orders it, on a real computer if one is available; otherwise write the steps in order and check them against the book's order.

## Task two
**Show it to someone** — Take Doing it — Exercise — how LinkedIn can promote business growth and entrepreneurship in South Sudan in practice and show a partner what it does: do it once slowly, naming each step, then let the partner do it while you only watch and correct.
