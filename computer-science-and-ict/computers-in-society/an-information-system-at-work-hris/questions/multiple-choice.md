# An information system at work — HRIS — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An HRIS stores —
a) only pay slips  b) employee data: recruitment, demographics, performance, training, pay and benefits  c) product prices  d) school marks
**Answer: b) employee data: recruitment, demographics, performance, training, pay and benefits** — the five stored kinds.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Recruitment data includes —
a) CVs and candidate numbers  b) bus routes  c) exam questions  d) weather
**Answer: a) CVs and candidate numbers** — the book's recruitment pair.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** HRIS data is held —
a) in one notebook  b) on servers and networked computers  c) on paper only  d) in workers' pockets
**Answer: b) on servers and networked computers** — reachable by every office that needs it.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Time and attendance software ties —
a) pay to hours worked  b) training to holidays  c) jobs to birthdays  d) reports to colours
**Answer: a) pay to hours worked** — pay follows the hours recorded.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Performance management software provides evidence for —
a) only ending employment  b) keeping, promoting, moving or ending employment  c) choosing uniforms  d) setting prices
**Answer: b) keeping, promoting, moving or ending employment** — all four decisions are named.
