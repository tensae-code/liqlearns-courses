# An information system at work — HRIS

## Do it first

An office hires you. Somewhere, your CV, your age, your training and your pay must live — findable in seconds, years later, without a paper mountain. This lesson opens the system that holds a whole workforce.

## Sub lesson 1 — What an HRIS stores

A **Human Resource Information System (HRIS)** stores **employee data**:

- **Recruitment** data — **CVs and candidate numbers**.
- **Demographic details**.
- **Performance records**.
- **Training** records.
- **Pay and benefits**.

The data sits **on servers and networked computers**, where every office that needs it can reach the one true copy.

## Sub lesson 2 — The HRIS software at work

Matching **software** works on that data in four kinds:

- **Recruitment** software — **posting jobs and sorting applications**.
- **Time and attendance** — **pay tied to hours worked**.
- **Performance management** — **evidence for keeping, promoting, moving or ending employment**.
- **Training and development** — planning and recording how staff grow.

The book closes the section with a turn: information systems are now **strategic assets**, **not just report machines** — organisations compete with them, not merely file with them.
