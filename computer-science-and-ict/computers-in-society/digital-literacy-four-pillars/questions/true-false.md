# Digital Literacy — the Four Pillars — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Indonesia's Kominfo — the national digital-literacy framework**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Indonesia's Kominfo — the national digital-literacy framework** — as the answer to: What are the four pillars?
**Answer: False — Digital Skills, Digital Culture, Digital Ethics, Digital Safety**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The ability to use devices, applications and networks**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The ability to use devices, applications and networks** — as the answer to: What is Digital Culture?
**Answer: False — Belonging well in the digital world as a citizen, in the spirit of Pancasila and Bhinneka Tunggal Ika**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A fast user with no direction**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A fast user with no direction** — as the answer to: What are the four habits of Digital Ethics?
**Answer: False — Awareness, integrity, responsibility and virtue**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Being the same person online as off**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Being the same person online as off** — as the answer to: What is responsibility, in the pillar's sense?
**Answer: False — Owning the effects of your acts**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Protection — guarding accounts, data and self against the digital world's harms**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Protection — guarding accounts, data and self against the digital world's harms** — as the answer to: What makes a person digitally literate, in this framework?
**Answer: False — Standing on all four pillars — using, belonging, judging and being safe**
