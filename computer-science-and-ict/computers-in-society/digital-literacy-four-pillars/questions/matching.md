# Digital Literacy — the Four Pillars — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Whose framework are the four pillars?** → **Indonesia's Kominfo — the national digital-literacy framework**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the four pillars?** → **Digital Skills, Digital Culture, Digital Ethics, Digital Safety**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is Digital Skills?** → **The ability to use devices, applications and networks**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is Digital Culture?** → **Belonging well in the digital world as a citizen, in the spirit of Pancasila and Bhinneka Tunggal Ika**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do skills without culture make?** → **A fast user with no direction**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are the four habits of Digital Ethics?** → **Awareness, integrity, responsibility and virtue**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is integrity, in the pillar's sense?** → **Being the same person online as off**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is responsibility, in the pillar's sense?** → **Owning the effects of your acts**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is Digital Safety?** → **Protection — guarding accounts, data and self against the digital world's harms**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What makes a person digitally literate, in this framework?** → **Standing on all four pillars — using, belonging, judging and being safe**
