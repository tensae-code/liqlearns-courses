# Digital Literacy — the Four Pillars — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Whose framework are the four pillars?**
**Answer: Indonesia's Kominfo — the national digital-literacy framework**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the four pillars?**
**Answer: Digital Skills, Digital Culture, Digital Ethics, Digital Safety**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is Digital Skills?**
**Answer: The ability to use devices, applications and networks**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is Digital Culture?**
**Answer: Belonging well in the digital world as a citizen, in the spirit of Pancasila and Bhinneka Tunggal Ika**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do skills without culture make?**
**Answer: A fast user with no direction**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are the four habits of Digital Ethics?**
**Answer: Awareness, integrity, responsibility and virtue**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is integrity, in the pillar's sense?**
**Answer: Being the same person online as off**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is responsibility, in the pillar's sense?**
**Answer: Owning the effects of your acts**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is Digital Safety?**
**Answer: Protection — guarding accounts, data and self against the digital world's harms**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What makes a person digitally literate, in this framework?**
**Answer: Standing on all four pillars — using, belonging, judging and being safe**
