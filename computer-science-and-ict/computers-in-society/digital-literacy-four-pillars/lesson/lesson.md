# Digital Literacy — the Four Pillars

## Do it first

Digital literacy's four pillars — Indonesia's Kominfo framework taught in Grade 12: Digital Skills, Digital Culture, Digital Ethics and Digital Safety, the four supports of a capable digital life.

## Sub lesson 1 — Skills and culture — using, and belonging

Indonesia's national digital-literacy framework (Kominfo) stands on four pillars. **Digital Skills** is the ability to use the devices, applications and networks — the hands of digital life. **Digital Culture** is belonging well in the digital world: taking part in its communities and shared works in the spirit of the nation's own values — Pancasila and Bhinneka Tunggal Ika — so the online world is lived as a citizen, not only used as a consumer. Skills without culture make a fast user; culture gives the speed a direction.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethics and safety — judging, and staying whole

**Digital Ethics** is judgement inside the digital world, and the book names its four habits: **awareness** (kesadaran — noticing what you do), **integrity** (integritas — being the same person online as off), **responsibility** (tanggung jawab — owning your acts' effects) and **virtue** (kebajikan — doing good, not only avoiding harm). **Digital Safety** is protection: guarding accounts, data and self against the digital world's harms. The four pillars hold one roof — a person who can use, belongs, judges and is safe is digitally literate; any pillar missing leaves the roof tilted.
