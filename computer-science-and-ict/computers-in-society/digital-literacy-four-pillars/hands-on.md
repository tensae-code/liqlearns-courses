# Hands-on — Digital Literacy — the Four Pillars

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Pillar audit** — Take your own last week online. Give one real example of each pillar done well or done badly, and name the weakest pillar in your own practice.

## Task two
**Teach a pillar** — Choose one pillar and prepare a two-minute explanation of it for a younger pupil, with one example from school life for each of its parts.
