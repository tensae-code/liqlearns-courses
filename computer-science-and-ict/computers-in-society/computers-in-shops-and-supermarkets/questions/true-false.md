# Computers in Shops and Supermarkets — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the point of sale (POS)?" is: The place in a shop where goods are paid for**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How does the till computer know an item?" is: In the shop's database of products and prices**
**Answer: False** — From its barcode or code, looked up in the shop's database

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does the computer find an item's price?" is: In the shop's database of products and prices**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the computer print at the end of a sale?" is: So the money in the till can be checked against the computer's record**
**Answer: False** — A receipt for the customer

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why is each sale recorded as it happens?" is: So the money in the till can be checked against the computer's record**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens to an item's stock count when it is sold?" is: Every product, its price, and how many are left**
**Answer: False** — It goes down by the number sold

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What three things does the shop database hold?" is: Every product, its price, and how many are left**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What can the shopkeeper ask the database?" is: One database, so a price changed once is right at every till**
**Answer: False** — What is running low, what sold today, and what to reorder

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "In a supermarket, what do all the tills share?" is: One database, so a price changed once is right at every till**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What has the computer joined into one system?" is: The place in a shop where goods are paid for**
**Answer: False** — The till, the shelves and the storeroom
