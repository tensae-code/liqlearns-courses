# Computers in Shops and Supermarkets — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the point of sale (POS)?**
a) The place in a shop where goods are paid for  b) From its barcode or code, looked up in the shop's database  c) In the shop's database of products and prices  d) A receipt for the customer
**Answer: a) The place in a shop where goods are paid for**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does the till computer know an item?**
a) In the shop's database of products and prices  b) From its barcode or code, looked up in the shop's database  c) A receipt for the customer  d) So the money in the till can be checked against the computer's record
**Answer: b) From its barcode or code, looked up in the shop's database**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the computer find an item's price?**
a) A receipt for the customer  b) So the money in the till can be checked against the computer's record  c) In the shop's database of products and prices  d) It goes down by the number sold
**Answer: c) In the shop's database of products and prices**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the computer print at the end of a sale?**
a) So the money in the till can be checked against the computer's record  b) It goes down by the number sold  c) Every product, its price, and how many are left  d) A receipt for the customer
**Answer: d) A receipt for the customer**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why is each sale recorded as it happens?**
a) So the money in the till can be checked against the computer's record  b) It goes down by the number sold  c) Every product, its price, and how many are left  d) What is running low, what sold today, and what to reorder
**Answer: a) So the money in the till can be checked against the computer's record**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to an item's stock count when it is sold?**
a) Every product, its price, and how many are left  b) It goes down by the number sold  c) What is running low, what sold today, and what to reorder  d) One database, so a price changed once is right at every till
**Answer: b) It goes down by the number sold**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What three things does the shop database hold?**
a) What is running low, what sold today, and what to reorder  b) One database, so a price changed once is right at every till  c) Every product, its price, and how many are left  d) The till, the shelves and the storeroom
**Answer: c) Every product, its price, and how many are left**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can the shopkeeper ask the database?**
a) One database, so a price changed once is right at every till  b) The till, the shelves and the storeroom  c) The place in a shop where goods are paid for  d) What is running low, what sold today, and what to reorder
**Answer: d) What is running low, what sold today, and what to reorder**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In a supermarket, what do all the tills share?**
a) One database, so a price changed once is right at every till  b) The till, the shelves and the storeroom  c) The place in a shop where goods are paid for  d) From its barcode or code, looked up in the shop's database
**Answer: a) One database, so a price changed once is right at every till**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What has the computer joined into one system?**
a) The place in a shop where goods are paid for  b) The till, the shelves and the storeroom  c) From its barcode or code, looked up in the shop's database  d) In the shop's database of products and prices
**Answer: b) The till, the shelves and the storeroom**
