# Computers in Shops and Supermarkets — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the point of sale (POS)?**
**Answer: The place in a shop where goods are paid for**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does the till computer know an item?**
**Answer: From its barcode or code, looked up in the shop's database**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the computer find an item's price?**
**Answer: In the shop's database of products and prices**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the computer print at the end of a sale?**
**Answer: A receipt for the customer**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why is each sale recorded as it happens?**
**Answer: So the money in the till can be checked against the computer's record**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to an item's stock count when it is sold?**
**Answer: It goes down by the number sold**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What three things does the shop database hold?**
**Answer: Every product, its price, and how many are left**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can the shopkeeper ask the database?**
**Answer: What is running low, what sold today, and what to reorder**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In a supermarket, what do all the tills share?**
**Answer: One database, so a price changed once is right at every till**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What has the computer joined into one system?**
**Answer: The till, the shelves and the storeroom**
