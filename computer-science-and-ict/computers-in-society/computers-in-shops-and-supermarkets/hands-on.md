# Hands-on — Computers in Shops and Supermarkets

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work the till on paper
Write a bill for three items with prices. Play cashier and computer in turns: one reads out items, the other finds each price from your written price list (your database), adds the total and works out the change from a banknote.

## Count the stock
List ten items in a pretend shop with a starting stock for each. Sell some, on paper, and update every stock count. Then answer from your list alone: what is running low, and what must be reordered?
