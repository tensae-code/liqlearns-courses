# Hands-on — Green technology and paperless systems

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Paper audit
One week, two columns: pages printed, documents sent electronically. Weigh the paper pile at week's end (a kitchen scale works). Report both numbers and the weight.

## Green hunt
Find and photograph (or draw) three green technologies in your area. For each, write the impact it monitors or reduces — judged by the book's test, not by its colour.
