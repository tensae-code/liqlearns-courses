# Green technology and paperless systems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Green technology is technology that —
a) is painted green  b) monitors or reduces environmental impact  c) is the newest  d) is the cheapest
**Answer: b) monitors or reduces environmental impact** — the functional test.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a named green-technology example?
a) solar panels  b) diesel generators  c) coal stoves  d) plastic bags
**Answer: a) solar panels** — with electric vehicles, smart meters, natural gas, wind turbines.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A smart meter earns its green place by —
a) looking modern  b) monitoring energy use so impact can be reduced  c) being expensive  d) replacing teachers
**Answer: b) monitoring energy use so impact can be reduced** — monitor or reduce — the test again.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Paperless communication cuts —
a) internet use  b) tree-felling for paper  c) reading  d) meetings
**Answer: b) tree-felling for paper** — the document travels; the tree stands.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Virtual collaboration saves the carbon emissions of —
a) heating  b) travelling to meet  c) printing  d) lighting
**Answer: b) travelling to meet** — the meeting happens; the flight does not.
