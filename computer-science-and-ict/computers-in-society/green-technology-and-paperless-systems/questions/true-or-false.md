# Green technology and paperless systems — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Natural gas and wind turbines are both in the book's green examples.
**Answer: True** — the named list.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Paperless working means no documents are produced.
**Answer: False** — documents are produced electronically — email, portals, applications.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Experts sharing research virtually emit the same travel carbon as flying to meet.
**Answer: False** — the emissions of travelling are avoided.
