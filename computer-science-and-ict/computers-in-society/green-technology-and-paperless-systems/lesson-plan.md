# Lesson plan — Green technology and paperless systems

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** Green technology and paperless systems
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 14 — Computers in Society of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 12, Unit 1 — Information Systems and Their Applications. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the response to technology's environmental cost is green technology — solar, electric vehicles, smart meters, wind — plus paperless communication that cuts tree-felling, and virtual collaboration that cuts travel carbon.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the e-waste lesson just before it (recommended first, never forced): that lesson named the harm; this one names the response.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Green technology — the named examples** — technology that monitors or reduces environmental impact: solar panels, electric vehicles, smart meters, natural gas, wind turbines.
2. **Paperless working and the virtual laboratory** — electronic communication cuts tree-felling for paper; worldwide virtual collaboration shares research without travel emissions.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define green technology and name its examples.
2. Say what paperless communication saves.
3. Say what virtual collaboration saves.
