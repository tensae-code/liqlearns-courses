# Mobile Banking — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is mobile banking?**
**Answer: Using a mobile phone to work with your bank account**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the phone become in mobile banking?**
**Answer: The bank's counter**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does a customer reach mobile banking?**
**Answer: Through a bank app or a dialled code menu**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does mobile banking work?**
**Answer: Anywhere the phone network reaches**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Between which two topics does Rwanda's S2 book place mobile banking?**
**Answer: E-commerce and the ATM**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name one everyday mobile banking job.**
**Answer: Checking a balance**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What record does mobile banking give?**
**Answer: A message recording every movement of money**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must be guarded as the account's key?**
**Answer: The PIN**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who holds the account, in the lesson's warning?**
**Answer: Whoever holds the phone and knows the PIN**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What fraud does Rwanda's guide teach beside mobile banking?**
**Answer: Smishing — fraud by text message (teacher guide — evidence)**
