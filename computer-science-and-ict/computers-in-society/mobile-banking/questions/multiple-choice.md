# Mobile Banking — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is mobile banking?**
a) Using a mobile phone to work with your bank account  b) The bank's counter  c) Through a bank app or a dialled code menu  d) Anywhere the phone network reaches
**Answer: a) Using a mobile phone to work with your bank account**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the phone become in mobile banking?**
a) Through a bank app or a dialled code menu  b) The bank's counter  c) Anywhere the phone network reaches  d) E-commerce and the ATM
**Answer: b) The bank's counter**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does a customer reach mobile banking?**
a) Anywhere the phone network reaches  b) E-commerce and the ATM  c) Through a bank app or a dialled code menu  d) Using a mobile phone to work with your bank account
**Answer: c) Through a bank app or a dialled code menu**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does mobile banking work?**
a) E-commerce and the ATM  b) Using a mobile phone to work with your bank account  c) The bank's counter  d) Anywhere the phone network reaches
**Answer: d) Anywhere the phone network reaches**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Between which two topics does Rwanda's S2 book place mobile banking?**
a) E-commerce and the ATM  b) Using a mobile phone to work with your bank account  c) The bank's counter  d) Through a bank app or a dialled code menu
**Answer: a) E-commerce and the ATM**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name one everyday mobile banking job.**
