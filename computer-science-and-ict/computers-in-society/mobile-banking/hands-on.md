# Hands-on — Mobile Banking

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Counter in the pocket', 'Write the steps of one mobile banking job — checking a balance — as numbered instructions a grandparent could follow, including where the PIN is entered and why it is never shared.')

## Task two
('Branch vs phone', 'Take one payment your family makes. Write its branch story and its mobile banking story side by side: time, travel, cost, and the record each leaves.')
