# Hands-on — Talking with Skype

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Set up the conversation
On a practice machine, open Skype (or a present-day program of the same kind), sign in, and add a classmate by their program name. Send a written message first, then start a voice call, then add video.

## Name or number?
Write two short lists: what a telephone call needs and uses, and what a Skype call needs and uses. Circle the differences — network, address, and what can travel besides voice.
