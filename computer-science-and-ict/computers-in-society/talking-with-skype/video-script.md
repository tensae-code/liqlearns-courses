# Video script — Talking with Skype

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Skype** is a communication program: it carries a conversation over the **Internet** instead of over the telephone network.
With a microphone you hold a **voice call**; with a webcam added, a **video call**, where the speakers see each other; and written **messages** can be sent in the same program, to a person who will read them when they come to their computer.
The working order is: install and open the program, register or sign in with your name, find and add the people you will talk to, then choose the form — message, voice or video — and start.
Sound and picture quality depend on the connection: a slow connection breaks the voice and freezes the picture, which is why the book pairs Skype with the chapter's other Internet-communication tools and treats a clear connection as part of the skill.
