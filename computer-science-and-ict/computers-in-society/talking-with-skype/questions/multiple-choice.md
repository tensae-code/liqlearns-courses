# Talking with Skype — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is Skype?**
a) A program for communicating over the Internet  b) A webcam — the speakers see each other  c) Open the program, sign in, add the people, choose the form, and start  d) It breaks the voice and freezes the picture
**Answer: a) A program for communicating over the Internet**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three forms of conversation does Skype carry?**
a) By the names they registered in the program  b) Voice calls, video calls and written messages  c) The speed of the Internet connection  d) No — it is a claim, not a proof
**Answer: b) Voice calls, video calls and written messages**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is needed for a voice call?**
a) Open the program, sign in, add the people, choose the form, and start  b) It breaks the voice and freezes the picture  c) A microphone (and the connection to the other person's computer)  d) The chapter's other Internet-communication tools, including NetMeeting
**Answer: c) A microphone (and the connection to the other person's computer)**
