# Talking with Skype — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is Skype?" is: A program for communicating over the Internet.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What three forms of conversation does Skype carry?" is: Open the program, sign in, add the people, choose the form, and start.**
**Answer: False** — Voice calls, video calls and written messages.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is needed for a voice call?" is: A microphone (and the connection to the other person's computer).**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a video call add?" is: It breaks the voice and freezes the picture.**
**Answer: False** — A webcam — the speakers see each other.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How are people reached in Skype?" is: By the names they registered in the program.**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the working order for a Skype conversation?" is: The chapter's other Internet-communication tools, including NetMeeting.**
**Answer: False** — Open the program, sign in, add the people, choose the form, and start.

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does sound and picture quality depend on?" is: The speed of the Internet connection.**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does a slow connection do to a call?" is: Voice calls, video calls and written messages.**
**Answer: False** — It breaks the voice and freezes the picture.

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Is a name in the program a proof of who someone is?" is: No — it is a claim, not a proof.**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which chapter tools is Skype taught beside?" is: A webcam — the speakers see each other.**
**Answer: False** — The chapter's other Internet-communication tools, including NetMeeting.
