# Talking with Skype — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is Skype?**
**Answer: A program for communicating over the Internet**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three forms of conversation does Skype carry?**
**Answer: Voice calls, video calls and written messages**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is needed for a voice call?**
**Answer: A microphone (and the connection to the other person's computer)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a video call add?**
**Answer: A webcam — the speakers see each other**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How are people reached in Skype?**
**Answer: By the names they registered in the program**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the working order for a Skype conversation?**
**Answer: Open the program, sign in, add the people, choose the form, and start**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does sound and picture quality depend on?**
**Answer: The speed of the Internet connection**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a slow connection do to a call?**
**Answer: It breaks the voice and freezes the picture**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Is a name in the program a proof of who someone is?**
**Answer: No — it is a claim, not a proof**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter tools is Skype taught beside?**
**Answer: The chapter's other Internet-communication tools, including NetMeeting**
