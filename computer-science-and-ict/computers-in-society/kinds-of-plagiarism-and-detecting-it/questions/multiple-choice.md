# Kinds of Plagiarism — and Detecting It — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How many kinds of plagiarism does the book count?**
a) Seven  b) Stitching sentences from several sources into a 'new' text  c) Submitting another's whole work, and copying large parts without credit  d) Yes — presenting it as new
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is patchwork plagiarism?**
a) Stitching sentences from several sources into a 'new' text  b) Submitting another's whole work, and copying large parts without credit  c) Yes — presenting it as new  d) The reader is lied to about whose work it is
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the brazen end of the family.**
a) Submitting another's whole work, and copying large parts without credit  b) Yes — presenting it as new  c) The reader is lied to about whose work it is  d) It compares a text against vast libraries and reports matching passages
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Can reusing your own old work be plagiarism?**
a) Yes — presenting it as new  b) The reader is lied to about whose work it is  c) It compares a text against vast libraries and reports matching passages  d) It steals credit and hollows the plagiarist's learning (also poisons trust)
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the shared wrong in all seven kinds?**

**Answer: a**
