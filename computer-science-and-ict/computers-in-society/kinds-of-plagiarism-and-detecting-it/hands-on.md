# Hands-on — Kinds of Plagiarism — and Detecting It

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Spot the kind** — Your teacher gives five short passages with their sources. Name the kind of plagiarism in each — or clear it as honest use, saying why.

## Task two
**Make it honest** — Take one patchwork paragraph and rewrite it properly: your own words, one marked quotation, credits where used.
