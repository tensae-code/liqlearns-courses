# Hands-on — Assessing the Quality of Digital Artefacts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Assess a class artefact** — Take a presentation or spreadsheet your class made. Score it on purpose, correctness, clarity and craft, with one sentence of evidence for each score.

## Task two
**Improve one thing** — Make the single improvement your assessment named first. Show before and after, and say which check it raises.
