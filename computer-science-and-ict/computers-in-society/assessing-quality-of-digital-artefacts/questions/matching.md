# Assessing the Quality of Digital Artefacts — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a digital artefact?** → **Anything made with a computer — a document, spreadsheet, program, presentation or website**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Against what is an artefact's quality judged first?** → **Its purpose — the job it was made for, for the people it was made for**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A beautiful artefact that fails its purpose — high or low quality?** → **Low quality — beauty does not rescue a failed purpose**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A plain artefact that serves its purpose well?** → **High quality**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In which Armenia book does this lesson appear?** → **Grade 6, §8 — Assessing the quality of computational works**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three working checks after purpose.** → **Correctness, clarity and craft**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the correctness check ask?** → **Whether the content and calculations are right**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the clarity check ask?** → **Whether a user can understand and use the artefact without help**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How does an assessment finish?** → **With improvement — the two weakest points and the change that would raise each**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why name exactly what to improve?** → **An assessment that cannot say what to improve has not finished its job**
