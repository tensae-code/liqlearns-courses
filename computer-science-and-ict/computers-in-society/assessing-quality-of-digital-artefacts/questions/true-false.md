# Assessing the Quality of Digital Artefacts — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Anything made with a computer — a document, spreadsheet, program, presentation or website**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Anything made with a computer — a document, spreadsheet, program, presentation or website** — as the answer to: Against what is an artefact's quality judged first?
**Answer: False — Its purpose — the job it was made for, for the people it was made for**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Low quality — beauty does not rescue a failed purpose**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Low quality — beauty does not rescue a failed purpose** — as the answer to: A plain artefact that serves its purpose well?
**Answer: False — High quality**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Grade 6, §8 — Assessing the quality of computational works**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Grade 6, §8 — Assessing the quality of computational works** — as the answer to: Name the three working checks after purpose.
**Answer: False — Correctness, clarity and craft**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Whether the content and calculations are right**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Whether the content and calculations are right** — as the answer to: What does the clarity check ask?
**Answer: False — Whether a user can understand and use the artefact without help**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: With improvement — the two weakest points and the change that would raise each**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: With improvement — the two weakest points and the change that would raise each** — as the answer to: Why name exactly what to improve?
**Answer: False — An assessment that cannot say what to improve has not finished its job**
