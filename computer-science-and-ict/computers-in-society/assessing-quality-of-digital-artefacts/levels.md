# Assessing the Quality of Digital Artefacts — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What quality means for a digital artefact — purpose first
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Anything made with a computer — a document, spreadsheet, program, presentation or website; Its purpose — the job it was made for, for the people it was made for; Low quality — beauty does not rescue a failed purpose; High quality; Grade 6, §8 — Assessing the quality of computational works.

## Sub lesson 2 — The checks — correctness, clarity, craft and improvement
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Correctness, clarity and craft; Whether the content and calculations are right; Whether a user can understand and use the artefact without help; With improvement — the two weakest points and the change that would raise each; An assessment that cannot say what to improve has not finished its job.
