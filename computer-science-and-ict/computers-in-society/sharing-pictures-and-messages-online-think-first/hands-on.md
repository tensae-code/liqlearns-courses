# Hands-on — Sharing Pictures and Messages Online — Think First

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Three questions drill** — The teacher shows ten sharing situations (photos, messages, forwards). For each, the class asks the three questions aloud and votes: share or keep.

## Task two
**The untakeable share** — Share a paper “picture” in class and let it be copied hand to hand for one minute. Try to collect every copy back. Count what is missing — that is the lesson.
