# Sharing Pictures and Messages Online — Think First

## Do it first

Your finger is over the share button. The picture is funny, the moment is now. Stop the film right there — this lesson lives in the three seconds before the tap.

## Sub lesson 1 — A share cannot be taken back

Once a picture or message is shared, it can be copied, saved and sent on by anyone who receives it — **sharing gives the thing away**, and taking it back is never guaranteed. The Parcours du partage teaches this before any rule: what leaves your hands lives by other people's choices. That is why the thinking must happen before the share, while the choice is still yours.

In your mind's eye: a share cannot be taken back.

## Sub lesson 2 — Three questions before sharing

The pause has three questions. **Whose is it?** A picture of someone else needs their agreement — the right to one's image (droit à l'image) — and a picture someone sent you is not yours to pass on. **Who will see it?** Not only the friend you send it to — everyone it can travel to. **Will it still be fine tomorrow?** For the person in the picture, and for you. Three yeses, then share; any doubt, keep it.

In your mind's eye: three questions before sharing.
