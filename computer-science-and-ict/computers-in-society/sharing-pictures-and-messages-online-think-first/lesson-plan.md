## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **A share cannot be taken back** — Once a picture or message is shared, it can be copied, saved and sent on by anyone who receives it — **sharing gives the thing away**, and taking it back is never guaranteed. The Parcours du partage teaches this before any rule: what leaves your hands lives by other people's choices. That is why the thinking must happen before the share, while the choice is still yours.
2. **Three questions before sharing** — The pause has three questions. **Whose is it?** A picture of someone else needs their agreement — the right to one's image (droit à l'image) — and a picture someone sent you is not yours to pass on. **Who will see it?** Not only the friend you send it to — everyone it can travel to. **Will it still be fine tomorrow?** For the person in the picture, and for you. Three yeses, then share; any doubt, keep it.
