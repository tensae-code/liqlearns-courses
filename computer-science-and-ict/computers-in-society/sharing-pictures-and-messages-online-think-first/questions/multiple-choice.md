# Sharing Pictures and Messages Online — Think First — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What happens once a picture is shared?**
a) It can be copied, saved and sent on by anyone who receives it  b) It gives the thing away  c) Before the share, while the choice is still yours  d) Never
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does sharing do, in one phrase?**
a) It gives the thing away  b) Before the share, while the choice is still yours  c) Never  d) The Parcours du partage (sharing)
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When must the thinking happen?**
a) Before the share, while the choice is still yours  b) Never  c) The Parcours du partage (sharing)  d) The right to one's image (droit à l'image)
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is taking a share back guaranteed?**
a) Never  b) The Parcours du partage (sharing)  c) The right to one's image (droit à l'image)  d) Not only the friend sent to — everyone it can travel to
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which parcours teaches this?**
a) The Parcours du partage (sharing)  b) The right to one's image (droit à l'image)  c) Not only the friend sent to — everyone it can travel to  d) Will it still be fine tomorrow — for them and for me?
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What right protects a person in a picture?**
a) The right to one's image (droit à l'image)  b) Not only the friend sent to — everyone it can travel to  c) Will it still be fine tomorrow — for them and for me?  d) Theirs — the person in the picture
**Answer: a**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Who will see a shared picture?**
a) Not only the friend sent to — everyone it can travel to  b) Will it still be fine tomorrow — for them and for me?  c) Theirs — the person in the picture  d) Keep it — do not share
**Answer: a**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the third question?**
a) Will it still be fine tomorrow — for them and for me?  b) Theirs — the person in the picture  c) Keep it — do not share  d) It can be copied, saved and sent on by anyone who receives it
**Answer: a**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Whose agreement does a picture of someone else need?**
a) Theirs — the person in the picture  b) Keep it — do not share  c) It can be copied, saved and sent on by anyone who receives it  d) It gives the thing away
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do when in doubt?**
a) Keep it — do not share  b) It can be copied, saved and sent on by anyone who receives it  c) It gives the thing away  d) Before the share, while the choice is still yours
**Answer: a**
