# Sharing Pictures and Messages Online — Think First — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What happens once a picture is shared?** → **It can be copied, saved and sent on by anyone who receives it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does sharing do, in one phrase?** → **It gives the thing away**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When must the thinking happen?** → **Before the share, while the choice is still yours**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is taking a share back guaranteed?** → **Never**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which parcours teaches this?** → **The Parcours du partage (sharing)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What right protects a person in a picture?** → **The right to one's image (droit à l'image)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Who will see a shared picture?** → **Not only the friend sent to — everyone it can travel to**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the third question?** → **Will it still be fine tomorrow — for them and for me?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Whose agreement does a picture of someone else need?** → **Theirs — the person in the picture**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do when in doubt?** → **Keep it — do not share**
