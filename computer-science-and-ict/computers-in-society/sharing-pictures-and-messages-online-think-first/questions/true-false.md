# Sharing Pictures and Messages Online — Think First — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It can be copied, saved and sent on by anyone who receives it**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It can be copied, saved and sent on by anyone who receives it** — as the answer to: What does sharing do, in one phrase?
**Answer: False — It gives the thing away**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Before the share, while the choice is still yours**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Before the share, while the choice is still yours** — as the answer to: Is taking a share back guaranteed?
**Answer: False — Never**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The Parcours du partage (sharing)**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The Parcours du partage (sharing)** — as the answer to: What right protects a person in a picture?
**Answer: False — The right to one's image (droit à l'image)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Not only the friend sent to — everyone it can travel to**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Not only the friend sent to — everyone it can travel to** — as the answer to: What is the third question?
**Answer: False — Will it still be fine tomorrow — for them and for me?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Theirs — the person in the picture**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Theirs — the person in the picture** — as the answer to: What do you do when in doubt?
**Answer: False — Keep it — do not share**
