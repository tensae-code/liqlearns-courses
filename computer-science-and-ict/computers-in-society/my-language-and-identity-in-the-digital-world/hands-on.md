# Hands-on — My Language and Identity in the Digital World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Say it properly', 'Take a short digital message you have written (or invent one) and rewrite it in fully correct Arabic. List what you fixed.')

## Task two
('Language map', 'List five places your language appears in your digital day. For one of them, write what would be lost if the language were not there.')
