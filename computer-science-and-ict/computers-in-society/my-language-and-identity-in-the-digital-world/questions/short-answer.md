# My Language and Identity in the Digital World — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is this lesson about?**
**Answer: Pride in my language and my identity in the digital world**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is identity?**
**Answer: Who you are and where you belong**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What place does Arabic hold for Kuwait's pupils?**
**Answer: It is a language of identity — of home, country and history**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Arabic belong, in the lesson's teaching?**
**Answer: In the digital world at full strength — messages, posts and schoolwork**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) How is pride in the language practised?**
**Answer: By writing correct Arabic in digital communication**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does correct Arabic in a message mean?**
**Answer: Real words written properly, in sentences that say what they mean**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Is careful writing old-fashioned?**
**Answer: No — and sloppy writing is not modern**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does every message in Arabic do to the language?**
**Answer: It carries Arabic a little further into the digital world**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) As what does the quality of a pupil's writing carry the language?**
**Answer: Exactly as well — or as badly — as it is written**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit and lesson is this in the book?**
**Answer: Unit 1 The Digital World, Lesson 1, of Kuwait Grade 4 Digital Explorer Term 2**
