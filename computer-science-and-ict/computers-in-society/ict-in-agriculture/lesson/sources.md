Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Mozambique, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked first: Computers in Society holds lessons on ICT in learning, health, banking and commerce, but none teaches ICT applied to agriculture. The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — computers-in-society/ict-in-agriculture). It sits in the Computers in Society title (computers-in-society/). It has three sub lessons: the farmer's ICT tools first, then ICT for growing (crops, animals and weather), then ICT for selling, buying and records — three distinct sequential parts following the farming year, each opening by proving the one before.

Mozambique's own way: a self-study distance-learning module (each lesson states objectives and a minimum study time, with a preparation test and answer key at the end) that runs from ICT concepts and device care through networks, digital citizenship and cybersecurity to ICT applied in teaching, agriculture and health. This lesson keeps that shape — it is a self-study lesson from a distance module, written to be learned alone with stated objectives, and it sits in the module's order as the placements record gives it.
