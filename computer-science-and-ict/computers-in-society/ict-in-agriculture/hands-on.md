# Hands-on — ICT in Agriculture

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Walk one farming year

Draw the farming year as a line: preparing, planting, growing, harvest, selling. At each step, write one ICT tool and the information it brings at that step. Count how many steps ICT touches.

## The price check

A farmer is offered a low price at the farm gate for a sack of produce. Write out, step by step, how the farmer uses ICT before answering: who they contact, what price they check, and how they decide. Then write the same story with no ICT, and compare the endings.
