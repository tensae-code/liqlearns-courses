# Video script — ICT in Agriculture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The farmer has questions.

When will the rain come? What is eating the leaves? What price in town?

ICT brings the answers to the farm.

A phone. A radio. The internet.

The forecast before planting. Advice while the crop grows.

The price before selling. The records kept straight.

The farmer still farms. Now the farmer decides on information, not on a guess.
