# ICT in Agriculture — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ICT does not do the farming; it brings ____ and connection to the farm.**
**Answer: information**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A ____ received by phone or radio helps a farmer choose when to plant.**
**Answer: weather forecast / forecast**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Before selling, a farmer can check the market ____ in town by phone or internet.**
**Answer: price**
