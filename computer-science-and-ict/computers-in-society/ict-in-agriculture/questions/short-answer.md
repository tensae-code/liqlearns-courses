# ICT in Agriculture — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name three ICT tools a farmer can use and say what each one brings.**
**Answer:** Any three, correctly matched — e.g. the mobile phone brings calls and messages to buyers, officers and other farmers (and the internet on a smartphone); radio brings farming programmes, weather and market news to many at once; computers and the internet bring farming information, pictures of pests and diseases, instructions and contact with distant experts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Walk through one pest problem on a farm and show, step by step, where ICT improves it.**
**Answer:** A sound walk-through: the farmer sees insects or marks on leaves; describes or photographs them by phone; sends the question to an agricultural officer, expert or farming source; receives identification and treatment advice the same day; treats the crop early instead of losing it while waiting for help to travel.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Why does this lesson say a farmer deciding on information decides better than a farmer deciding on a guess?**
**Answer:** Because each big decision of the farming year — when to plant, how to treat a pest or a sick animal, where and when to sell — turns out better when it rests on a forecast, advice, a checked price or straight records than when it rests on guessing, and ICT is what brings that information to the farm in time.
