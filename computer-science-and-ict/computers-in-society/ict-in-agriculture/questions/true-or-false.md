# ICT in Agriculture — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ICT tools plant the seeds and do the farming themselves.**
**Answer: False** — ICT brings information and connection; the farmer still farms.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A forecast can help a farmer plant into the right rain instead of guessing.**
**Answer: True** — Choosing when to plant is the forecast's farming value.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) If help is far away, ICT cannot help with a pest attack at all.**
**Answer: False** — A description or photograph can carry the question to an expert and bring advice back the same day.

**4. (Sub lesson 3) A good harvest sold at the first low price offered can still be a poor year.**
**Answer: True** — That is why price information matters before selling.
