# Hands-on — Privacy — your data spread across systems

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Four doors map
Draw your four doors: the systems holding your data. For each door draw its lock and write who holds a key besides you. A door with no lock gets fixed first.

## The lab rule
In a pair, draft a two-line privacy rule for your school lab — one line for what may be checked, one for what stays private. Read it aloud and defend both lines.
