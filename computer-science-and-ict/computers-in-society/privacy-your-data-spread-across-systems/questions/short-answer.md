# Privacy — your data spread across systems — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three kinds of account the book says unauthorised people can reach.
**Answer:** Mail, social and bank accounts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why can a privacy violation become a legal matter?
**Answer:** Because looking at private information is not only wrong morally — the law also protects it, so the act can be pursued legally.
