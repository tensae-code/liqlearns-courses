# Privacy — your data spread across systems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Personal data in digital systems is —
a) kept in one drawer  b) spread across many systems  c) never stored  d) always public
**Answer: b) spread across many systems** — the book's starting fact.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Personal data should sit behind —
a) a shared password  b) personal passwords  c) no lock at all  d) a friend's password
**Answer: b) personal passwords** — each person's own lock.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which accounts does the book name as reachable by unauthorised people?
a) mail, social and bank accounts  b) only library cards  c) only games  d) none
**Answer: a) mail, social and bank accounts** — the three named kinds.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Looking at someone's private information is —
a) fine if nothing is taken  b) wrong in itself, and can become a legal matter  c) a good habit  d) required
**Answer: b) wrong in itself, and can become a legal matter** — the looking is the violation.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** An employer checking staff computer use must —
a) read everything  b) do it without damaging individual privacy  c) share what it finds  d) never check anything
**Answer: b) do it without damaging individual privacy** — the limit binds employers too.
