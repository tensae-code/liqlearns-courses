# Privacy — your data spread across systems — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A password guarantees nobody unauthorised can ever reach an account.
**Answer: False** — accounts can still be reached by unauthorised people.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Privacy must be defended, not assumed.
**Answer: True** — the spread of data makes defence necessary.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** If nothing is stolen, looking at private information does no wrong.
**Answer: False** — looking is wrong in itself.
