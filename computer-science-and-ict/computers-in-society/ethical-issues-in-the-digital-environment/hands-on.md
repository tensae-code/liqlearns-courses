# Hands-on — Ethical Issues in the Digital Environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 5 §5.2 Ethics in Digital Environment (misuse of personal information, misinformation and deep fakes, lack of oversight) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach ethical issues in the digital environment to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
