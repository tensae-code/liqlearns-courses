# Digital identity — your information that exists online — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A digital identity covers only individuals, never organisations or devices.
**Answer: False** — all three are in the definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A trusted, secured digital identity is presented as a right for everyone.
**Answer: True** — the book's claim.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Digital identity has no connection to health or education services.
**Answer: False** — both are named as services it unlocks.
