# Digital identity — your information that exists online — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A digital identity is —
a) one password  b) all the information about a person, organisation or device that exists online  c) a photograph only  d) a SIM card
**Answer: b) all the information about a person, organisation or device that exists online** — the full definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Collected together, the information forms —
a) a bill  b) a digital image of the person  c) a game profile  d) nothing
**Answer: b) a digital image of the person** — the assembled picture.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Businesses use the digital image to —
a) identify clients  b) decorate websites  c) slow services  d) replace staff
**Answer: a) identify clients** — recognition is its use.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Everyone should have a right to a digital identity that is —
a) public and open  b) trusted and secured  c) sold freely  d) anonymous always
**Answer: b) trusted and secured** — the book's two adjectives.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The identity is the key to —
a) only games  b) financial services, mobile connectivity, health and education  c) only travel  d) only shopping
**Answer: b) financial services, mobile connectivity, health and education** — the named fundamental services.
