# Digital identity — your information that exists online

## Do it first

Search your own name in your mind: a school record here, a bank account there, a profile, a SIM registration, a photo someone else posted. Collected together, those pieces are a person — this lesson names that collected person, and what it unlocks.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a digital identity is

A **digital identity is all the information about an individual, organisation or electronic device that exists online**.

Collected together, that information **forms a digital image of the person that businesses can use to identify clients** — the online pieces assembled into someone a system can recognise.

[Picture 29730 — a person's silhouette built out of small online data tiles, unlocking service doors]

## Sub lesson 2 — A right — and a key to services

**Everyone should have a right to a trusted, secured digital identity** — trusted, so others can rely on it; secured, so it cannot be taken over.

The right matters because the identity is **the key to fundamental services — financial services, mobile connectivity, health and education among them**. No key, and the doors stay shut.
