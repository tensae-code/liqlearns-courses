# Hands-on — Digital identity — your information that exists online

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Assemble the image
On one page, draw your digital image as tiles: one tile per system, one fact per tile. Then stand back — that page is what a business sees when it 'identifies' you.

## Key and doors
Draw four doors — finance, mobile, health, education. For each, write what a person without a digital identity cannot do at that door. The drawing is the argument for the right.
