# Hands-on — The Information Society — What It Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three ages walk
Walk through your home or street and write one thing that belongs to each age: something of the land (agricultural), something made in a factory (industrial), and something that is pure information work (information age — a price checked online, a mobile-money transfer).

## Feature hunt
For each basic feature of an information society, find one real example within a day of your life and write it down in one line.
