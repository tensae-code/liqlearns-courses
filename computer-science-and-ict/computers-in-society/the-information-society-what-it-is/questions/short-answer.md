# The Information Society — What It Is — short answer

Answer each question in a few sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in two or three sentences, how the centre of society changed from the agricultural age to today.**
**Answer:** First most people worked the land and food was the key resource; then factories and energy took the centre in the industrial age; now creating, processing and sharing information is a central activity, and information itself is a key resource.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name two basic features of an information society and give one example of each from your own town.**
**Answer:** Any two of: information as a key resource (a shop lives by its online prices and orders); information technology running through everything (school results sent by phone); growing information work (a mobile-money agent); information flowing fast and far (a lesson watched from another country).
