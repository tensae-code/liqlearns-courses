# The Information Society — What It Is — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In an agricultural society, the key resource was the ____.**
**Answer: land** — Land and what it grows stood at the centre.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) An information society treats ____ itself as a key resource.**
**Answer: information** — The resource changed: land, then energy and goods, then information.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) In an information society, information can cross the world in ____.**
**Answer: seconds** — Networks carry it fast and far.
