# The Information Society — What It Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Put the three ages in the order they came.**
a) Industrial, agricultural, information  b) Agricultural, industrial, information  c) Information, agricultural, industrial  d) Agricultural, information, industrial
**Answer: b) Agricultural, industrial, information** — First the land, then factories, then information at the centre.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In an industrial society, the key resources were —**
a) land and crops  b) energy and manufactured goods  c) data and networks  d) letters and messengers
**Answer: b) energy and manufactured goods** — Factories ran on energy and produced goods.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) An information society is a society where —**
a) nobody farms or makes things any more  b) creating, processing and sharing information is a central activity  c) computers are forbidden in schools  d) information is kept secret
**Answer: b) creating, processing and sharing information is a central activity** — That is the book's definition.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is a basic feature of an information society?**
a) Information travels slowly  b) Distance matters more than ever  c) Information is treated as a key resource  d) Most jobs are in factories
**Answer: c) Information is treated as a key resource** — Information stands where land and energy once stood.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Information work growing means —**
a) more paper files  b) more jobs about data and knowledge  c) fewer networks  d) slower messages
**Answer: b) more jobs about data and knowledge** — Information itself becomes the work.
