# The Information Society — What It Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The information society grew out of the industrial society.**
**Answer: True** — Computers and networks spread inside the industrial world first.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In an information society, distance stops mattering completely for everything.**
**Answer: False** — Distance matters less for information, not for everything — goods still travel by road.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A mobile-money transfer is information work.**
**Answer: True** — No physical thing moves; information about money moves.
