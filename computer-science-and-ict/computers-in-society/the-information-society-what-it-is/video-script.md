# Video script — The Information Society — What It Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Long ago, most people worked the land.
Then factories came. Making things was the centre.
Now something new is at the centre: information.
People collect it. They process it. They send it everywhere.
Information is a treasure, like land once was.
Networks join the school, the shop, the hospital, the home.
That is an information society.
