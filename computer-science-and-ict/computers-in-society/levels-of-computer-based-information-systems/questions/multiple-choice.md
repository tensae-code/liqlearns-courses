# Levels of Computer-Based Information Systems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does TPS stand for, and whom does it serve?**
a) Transaction Processing System — it serves the operational level  b) The day's transactions — sales, wages, orders — one by one, completely and accurately  c) Its memory of what actually happened  d) Takes the TPS's records and produces summaries and reports for managers

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a TPS record?**

**Answer: b) The day's transactions — sales, wages, orders — one by one, completely and accurately**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the TPS to the organisation?**
