# Levels of Computer-Based Information Systems — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does TPS stand for, and whom does it serve?**
**Answer: Transaction Processing System — it serves the operational level**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a TPS record?**
**Answer: The day's transactions — sales, wages, orders — one by one, completely and accurately**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the TPS to the organisation?**
**Answer: Its memory of what actually happened**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does an MIS do?**
**Answer: Takes the TPS's records and produces summaries and reports for managers**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is Fiji's food-chain point?**
**Answer: An MIS can only summarise what the TPS faithfully kept**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a DSS let a manager do?**
**Answer: Try 'what if' — work on the data with models before the real decision is spent**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Whom does a DSS serve?**
**Answer: Managers facing choices**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does ESS stand for, and whom does it serve?**
**Answer: Executive Support System — it serves the top level, the directors**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What view does an ESS present?**
**Answer: The widest view, gathered from inside and outside the organisation**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How many system levels for how many management levels?**
**Answer: Four system levels — for the three management levels and the operational floor**
