# Levels of Computer-Based Information Systems — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does TPS stand for, and whom does it serve?" is: Transaction Processing System — it serves the operational level**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a TPS record?" is: Its memory of what actually happened**
**Answer: False** — The day's transactions — sales, wages, orders — one by one, completely and accurately

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the TPS to the organisation?" is: Its memory of what actually happened**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does an MIS do?" is: An MIS can only summarise what the TPS faithfully kept**
**Answer: False** — Takes the TPS's records and produces summaries and reports for managers

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is Fiji's food-chain point?" is: An MIS can only summarise what the TPS faithfully kept**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does a DSS let a manager do?" is: Managers facing choices**
**Answer: False** — Try 'what if' — work on the data with models before the real decision is spent

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Whom does a DSS serve?" is: Managers facing choices**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does ESS stand for, and whom does it serve?" is: The widest view, gathered from inside and outside the organisation**
**Answer: False** — Executive Support System — it serves the top level, the directors

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What view does an ESS present?" is: The widest view, gathered from inside and outside the organisation**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How many system levels for how many management levels?" is: Transaction Processing System — it serves the operational level**
**Answer: False** — Four system levels — for the three management levels and the operational floor
