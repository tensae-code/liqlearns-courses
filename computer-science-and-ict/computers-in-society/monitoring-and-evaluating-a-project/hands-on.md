# Hands-on — Monitoring and Evaluating a Project

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Monitor a live project** — Take a project running now (yours or a group's). Write this week's monitoring note: milestone due, its true state, spending against plan, and one fault found small.

## Task two
**Evaluate a finished one** — Choose a finished project. Write its evaluation in four lines: delivered scope, time and cost against plan, whether users are served, and the one change the next plan must make.
