# Monitoring and Evaluating a Project — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is monitoring?** → **Watching a running project against its plan while there is still time to fix it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three comparisons does monitoring make?** → **Progress against milestones, spending against planned resources, and faults found while small**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A plan nobody monitors is what?** → **A wish**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What rhythm does monitoring keep?** → **Regular, short and recorded**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is monitoring's honesty rule?** → **Report the milestone as it is, not as it was hoped**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does evaluation judge?** → **Whether the project delivered its scope, at the planned time and cost, and whether the result works for its users**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is evaluation's second half?** → **Looking backward for the future — what went well, where the plan was wrong, what the next plan must change**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Monitoring and evaluation are one habit at two moments — which?** → **Attention during, judgement after**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What makes both possible instead of argued from memory?** → **The project's documents, kept from the first day**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?** → **Grade 10 SMK Semester 2, Bab 3 B — monitoring and evaluation of projects**
