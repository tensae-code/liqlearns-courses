# Monitoring and Evaluating a Project — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is monitoring?**
**Answer: Watching a running project against its plan while there is still time to fix it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three comparisons does monitoring make?**
**Answer: Progress against milestones, spending against planned resources, and faults found while small**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A plan nobody monitors is what?**
**Answer: A wish**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What rhythm does monitoring keep?**
**Answer: Regular, short and recorded**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is monitoring's honesty rule?**
**Answer: Report the milestone as it is, not as it was hoped**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does evaluation judge?**
**Answer: Whether the project delivered its scope, at the planned time and cost, and whether the result works for its users**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is evaluation's second half?**
**Answer: Looking backward for the future — what went well, where the plan was wrong, what the next plan must change**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Monitoring and evaluation are one habit at two moments — which?**
**Answer: Attention during, judgement after**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What makes both possible instead of argued from memory?**
**Answer: The project's documents, kept from the first day**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 10 SMK Semester 2, Bab 3 B — monitoring and evaluation of projects**
