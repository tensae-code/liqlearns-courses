# Video script — Monitoring and Evaluating a Project

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Monitoring** is the plan watched while the project runs: progress compared with the schedule's milestones, spending compared with the resources planned, and faults found while they are still small enough to fix. The SMK chapter pairs it with management on purpose — a plan nobody monitors is a wish. Monitoring has a rhythm (regular, short, recorded) and an honesty rule: report the milestone as it is, not as it was hoped, because a late truth costs days and a hidden one costs the project.
**Evaluation** judges the finished project: did it deliver the scope, at the planned time and cost, and does the delivered thing actually work for its users? Its second half looks backward for the future's sake — what the team did well, where the plan was wrong, and what the next project's plan must change. Monitoring and evaluation are one habit at two moments: attention during, judgement after — and the project's documents, kept from the first day, are what make both possible instead of argued from memory.
