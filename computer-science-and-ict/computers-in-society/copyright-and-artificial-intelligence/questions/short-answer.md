# Copyright and Artificial Intelligence — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Copyright was built for whom?**
**Answer: Human creators**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who holds the strongest claim to an AI-made work?**
**Answer: The user who directed it — chose, prompted, selected, arranged**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The AI's maker is likened to whom?**
**Answer: A camera-maker — who does not own the photographs**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A work with no human hand may be what?**
**Answer: Authorless — outside protection, free for all**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why teach it as open?**
**Answer: Laws and courts are deciding it now**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are AI systems trained on?**
**Answer: Enormous collections of existing works — protected pictures and texts**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) State the two positions on training.**
**Answer: Fair use (learning by looking) against copying at scale without consent or payment**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the two questions for any AI work.**
**Answer: Whose is the output? What was taken for the input?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is digital citizenship here?**
**Answer: The habit of asking both questions**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which unit teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 7 Pliroforiki A, Unit 5**
