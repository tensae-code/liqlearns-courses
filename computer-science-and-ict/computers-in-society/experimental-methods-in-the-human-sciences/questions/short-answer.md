# Experimental Methods in the Human Sciences — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the experiment's two variables?**
**Answer: The independent variable, changed deliberately; the dependent variable, measured**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a control group?**
**Answer: A group receiving everything except the change — so differences can be credited to the variable**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the instruments of the lesson.**
**Answer: Questionnaires, observation and tests**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why hold other conditions equal?**
**Answer: So the result's difference is not credited to the day, the teacher or chance**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How do samples serve?**
**Answer: They stand for populations, by the care statistics demands**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name two ethical limits.**
**Answer: Informed consent with the right to withdraw; protection of persons' private data (also: bounded deception, no harm)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does the computer serve these experiments?**
**Answer: Processing — coding questionnaires, computing tables and averages, drawing comparisons**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which course crafts does it reuse?**
**Answer: The database and list-processing crafts of the Grade 12 course**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the elective's real syllabus, in its last phrase?**
**Answer: Method includes how people may be treated**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Can a participant be kept against their will for completeness?**
**Answer: No — withdrawal is a right, and the design must survive it**
