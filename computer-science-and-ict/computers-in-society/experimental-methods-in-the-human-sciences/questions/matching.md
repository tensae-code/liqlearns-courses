# Experimental Methods in the Human Sciences — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the experiment's two variables?** → **The independent variable, changed deliberately; the dependent variable, measured**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a control group?** → **A group receiving everything except the change — so differences can be credited to the variable**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the instruments of the lesson.** → **Questionnaires, observation and tests**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why hold other conditions equal?** → **So the result's difference is not credited to the day, the teacher or chance**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How do samples serve?** → **They stand for populations, by the care statistics demands**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name two ethical limits.** → **Informed consent with the right to withdraw; protection of persons' private data (also: bounded deception, no harm)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does the computer serve these experiments?** → **Processing — coding questionnaires, computing tables and averages, drawing comparisons**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which course crafts does it reuse?** → **The database and list-processing crafts of the Grade 12 course**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the elective's real syllabus, in its last phrase?** → **Method includes how people may be treated**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Can a participant be kept against their will for completeness?** → **No — withdrawal is a right, and the design must survive it**
