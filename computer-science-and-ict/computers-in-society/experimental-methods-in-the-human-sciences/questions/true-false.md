# Experimental Methods in the Human Sciences — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The independent variable, changed deliberately; the dependent variable, measured**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The independent variable, changed deliberately; the dependent variable, measured** — as the answer to: What is a control group?
**Answer: False — A group receiving everything except the change — so differences can be credited to the variable**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Questionnaires, observation and tests**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Questionnaires, observation and tests** — as the answer to: Why hold other conditions equal?
**Answer: False — So the result's difference is not credited to the day, the teacher or chance**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: They stand for populations, by the care statistics demands**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: They stand for populations, by the care statistics demands** — as the answer to: Name two ethical limits.
**Answer: False — Informed consent with the right to withdraw; protection of persons' private data (also: bounded deception, no harm)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Processing — coding questionnaires, computing tables and averages, drawing comparisons**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Processing — coding questionnaires, computing tables and averages, drawing comparisons** — as the answer to: Which course crafts does it reuse?
**Answer: False — The database and list-processing crafts of the Grade 12 course**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Method includes how people may be treated**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Method includes how people may be treated** — as the answer to: Can a participant be kept against their will for completeness?
**Answer: False — No — withdrawal is a right, and the design must survive it**
