# Hands-on — Experimental Methods in the Human Sciences

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Design an experiment** — Design a fair test of 'morning revision beats evening revision' for a class: variables, control group, instruments, consent — one page.

## Task two
**Ethics court** — Take a proposed study (secretly timing pupils' cafeteria choices for a sociology poster) and rule on consent, privacy and publication, naming the repairs that would make it lawful.
