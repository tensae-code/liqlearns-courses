# Video script — Computers in Everyday Life — Education, Health, Banks and Services

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In **education**, the computer teaches: lessons, pictures and exercises reach the learner on its screen, and the continuing-education learner practises on it step by step. In **hospitals and health**, the computer keeps each patient's record, helps the staff find it quickly, and supports the machines that examine and treat. In both fields the computer's gifts are the same: it stores a great deal, finds it fast, and does not forget.
In **banks**, the computer keeps accounts and moves money safely and quickly. **Government services** run on computers, so a citizen's request is recorded and answered in order. **Aviation** uses computers to book seats and guide flights; **communications** — calls and messages — travel through them; and **entertainment** — games, films and music — is made and played on them. Everyday life without the computer would be slower in every one of these places.
