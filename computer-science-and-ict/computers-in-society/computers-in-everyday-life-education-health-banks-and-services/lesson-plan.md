# Lesson plan — Computers in Everyday Life — Education, Health, Banks and Services

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Computers in Society · **Lesson:** Computers in Everyday Life — Education, Health, Banks and Services
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Saudi Arabia (2026-11-14) — in the title Computers in Society on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Saudi Arabia's Grade 3 student book, because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The computer is not only a school machine: the same machine teaches lessons in education, keeps patient records in hospitals, moves money in banks, delivers government services, guides aviation, carries communications and entertains — one tool woven through everyday life.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the Saudi book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The computer in learning and health** — In **education**, the computer teaches: lessons, pictures and exercises reach the learner on its screen, and the continuing-education learner practises on it step by step. In **hospitals and health**, the computer keeps each patient's record, helps the staff find it quickly, and supports the machines that examine and treat. In both fields the computer's gifts are the same: it stores a great deal, finds it fast, and does not forget.
2. **The computer in money, services and leisure** — In **banks**, the computer keeps accounts and moves money safely and quickly. **Government services** run on computers, so a citizen's request is recorded and answered in order. **Aviation** uses computers to book seats and guide flights; **communications** — calls and messages — travel through them; and **entertainment** — games, films and music — is made and played on them. Everyday life without the computer would be slower in every one of these places.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Think of your day yesterday. A computer probably touched it more than once — in a lesson, a payment, a message. In this lesson you name where the computer works in everyday life. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of the computer in learning and health as the lesson describes it — the student studies the picture and names what each part shows.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of the computer in money, services and leisure in action — the student watches once, then says what changed and why.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key names and definitions aloud, in the order the lesson gives them — the student listens once through, then reads the same lines aloud along with the voice. (Audio is a described slot in this build; no audio file is made.)
3. **Say it to your companion** — the companion block in lesson/lesson.md.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-false, matching, short-answer — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
