# Hands-on — Computers in Everyday Life — Education, Health, Banks and Services

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map your day
List yesterday's day in order. Mark every place a computer touched it — a lesson, a payment, a message — and name the field for each mark.

## One field deeply
Choose one field — education, health, banks, services, aviation, communications or entertainment. Write three sentences on what the computer does there and what would slow down without it.
