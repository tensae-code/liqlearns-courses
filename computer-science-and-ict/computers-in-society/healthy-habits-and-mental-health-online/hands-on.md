# Hands-on — Healthy Habits and Mental Health Online

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**A week's pattern** — For three days, note your sleep, movement and longest screen stretch. Write the one habit change that would most support your own mental health, and try it for the next three days.

## Task two
**Design the break** — Plan a deliberate break you would actually keep: when it starts, where the device goes, what fills the time instead. Share the plan with a companion and report back whether it held.
