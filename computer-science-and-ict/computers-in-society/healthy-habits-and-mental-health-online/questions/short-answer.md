# Healthy Habits and Mental Health Online — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is pola hidup sehat?**
**Answer: A healthy pattern of life — taught here as the support of mental health in the digital world**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which claims come first, before any app?**
**Answer: The body's — sleep, movement, food, daylight, rest for eyes and posture**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book teach health before willpower?**
**Answer: A tired, sleepless mind meets digital pressures with its weakest defences**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is balance, in this lesson?**
**Answer: Keeping face-to-face life so real company is never wholly replaced by online life**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a deliberate break?**
**Answer: Putting the device down by decision — not only when the battery ends**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How should a low feeling from a feed be treated?**
**Answer: As information, not weakness**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What habit guards all the others?**
**Answer: Telling a person — family, friend, teacher — when the digital world is making life heavier**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the phone charge, by this lesson's rule?**
**Answer: Outside the night's reach**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Mental health online is not what?**
**Answer: Not a setting in an app — a pattern of life, kept daily**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 9, Bab IV E.3 — healthy living patterns supporting mental health in the digital world**
