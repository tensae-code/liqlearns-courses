# Healthy Habits and Mental Health Online

## Do it first

Healthy habits and mental health online — a pattern of life (pola hidup sehat) that supports mental health in the digital world: sleep, movement, face-to-face life and deliberate rest from screens

## Sub lesson 1 — The body first — sleep, movement, eyes

Indonesia's Grade 9 digital-behaviour chapter closes with the pattern of life itself: **pola hidup sehat** — healthy habits — as the support of mental health in the digital world. The body's claims come first and are not negotiable by any app: **sleep** enough (the phone charges outside the night's reach), **movement** every day, food and daylight, and rest for eyes and posture that hours of screen bend. A tired, sleepless mind meets the digital world's pressures with its weakest defences — which is why the book teaches health before willpower.

In your mind's eye: the body first — sleep, movement, eyes.

## Sub lesson 2 — The mind's habits — balance, company, and asking for help

On the body's foundation the mind's habits stand: **balance** between online and face-to-face life, so real company is never wholly replaced; **deliberate breaks**, where the device is put down by decision, not only by battery; noticing one's own state — the flatness, envy or restlessness a feed can grow — and treating it as information, not weakness. And the habit that guards all the others: **telling a person** — family, friend, teacher — when the digital world is making life heavier. Mental health online is not a setting in an app; it is a pattern of life, kept daily.
