# Video script — Building Models from Geometric Shapes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at a boat. See its shapes.

A long body. A triangle sail. A round sun.

Circle. Square. Triangle. Rectangle.

These shapes are your building pieces.

First lay them out. Look. No glue yet.

Then stick them down. That is appliqué.

Many hands can build one model together.
