# Building Models from Geometric Shapes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Look at a house. Which shape is each part: the walls, the roof, a round window?**
**Answer:** Walls — a square or rectangle; roof — a triangle; a round window — a circle.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why do we lay the shapes out before gluing them?**
**Answer:** So we can look at the whole model and move anything that is wrong while moving is still easy — before the glue holds it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) In group work on the boat, what does each child do?**
**Answer:** Each child brings a shape and places one part of the boat; the group lays it out, looks together, and sticks it down as one model.
