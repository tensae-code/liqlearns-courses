# Building Models from Geometric Shapes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which shape makes a boat's sail?**
a) A circle  b) A triangle  c) A square
**Answer: b) A triangle** — A sail is the pointy, three-sided part — a triangle.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A car's wheels are which shape?**
a) Circles  b) Triangles  c) Squares
**Answer: a) Circles** — Wheels are round — circles.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do before you glue the shapes down?**
a) Close your eyes  b) Lay them out and look at the whole model  c) Throw them away
**Answer: b) Lay them out and look at the whole model** — Lay, look, then stick — moving is free before the glue.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Building a picture-model from cut shapes stuck down is called —**
a) appliqué  b) singing  c) counting
**Answer: a) appliqué** — Appliqué is building with cut shapes laid and glued into a picture-model.
