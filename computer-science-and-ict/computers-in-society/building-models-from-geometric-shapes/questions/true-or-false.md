# Building Models from Geometric Shapes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A house's roof can be made with a triangle.**
**Answer: True** — A roof is the pointy top part — a triangle fits it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) You should glue every shape down before you look at the model.**
**Answer: False** — Lay out and look first; only then stick them down.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) In the book's group work, a whole class builds one boat model together.**
**Answer: True** — Each child brings a shape and places a part; together they make one model.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Only one shape can be used in a model.**
**Answer: False** — A model joins different shapes — each shape makes a different part.
