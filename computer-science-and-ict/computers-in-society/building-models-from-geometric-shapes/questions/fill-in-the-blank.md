# Building Models from Geometric Shapes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The four building shapes are the circle, the square, the ____ and the rectangle.**
**Answer: triangle**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Lay the shapes out and ____ before you glue them.**
**Answer: look**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A picture-model built from cut shapes stuck down is called ____.**
**Answer: appliqué**
