# Hands-on — Building Models from Geometric Shapes

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Shape hunt
Look at three things in the room. For each, name the shapes you can see in it, part by part.

## Build the boat
With your group, build the boat model: choose who brings each shape, lay the whole boat out first, look together, then stick it down. Say each shape's name as it is placed.
