# Building Models from Geometric Shapes — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The shapes are the parts
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Every part of the thing you want to build matches a flat shape: a circle for a wheel or the sun, a triangle for a sail or a roof, a square or rectangle for a body or a wall — first look at the thing, then choose its shapes.
## Sub lesson 2 — Joining the shapes — appliqué
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Cut or take the shapes in the colours you chose, lay them where the parts go, look once before you glue, then stick them down as appliqué — and in the book's group work, a whole class joins its shapes into one boat model.
