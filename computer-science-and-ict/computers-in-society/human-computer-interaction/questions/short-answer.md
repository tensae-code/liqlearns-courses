# Human-Computer Interaction — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is HCI?**
**Answer: The study and design of how people and computers meet**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Through what does the meeting happen?**
**Answer: Screens, controls and responses**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Does every program have a meeting point?**
**Answer: Yes — designed or not**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) HCI draws on knowing what, besides machines?**
**Answer: People — how they see, remember, and err**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the question HCI asks of any meeting point?**
**Answer: Whether it was designed for the person, or merely happened**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why is HCI needed, in one line?**
**Answer: The meeting decides whether the computer's power reaches the person**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to a confusing system?**
**Answer: It is unused or misused — its workings locked behind a door users cannot open**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Whose limits are the real limits?**
**Answer: The users' — their time, training and patience**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Is designing interaction decoration?**
**Answer: No — it is half the work, the half the user actually meets**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who is blamed for a badly designed system's errors, wrongly?**
**Answer: The users, who were never given a fair chance**
