# Hands-on — Outsourcing and Freelancing — Working for the World From Home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Read a portal like a worker** — Open a job portal named in the lesson. Find one job you could nearly do today and list the skills its posting asks for that you still need.

## Task two
**Price the downside** — Write a one-month plan for a freelancer with no work in week two: what is saved in good weeks, and what work-hunting happens every week, not only in empty ones.
