# Video script — Outsourcing and Freelancing — Working for the World From Home

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Outsourcing** is work a company gives to people outside itself — often in another country — instead of hiring staff to do it in-house: design, programming, data entry, customer support. Companies outsource to find skills and lower costs; workers gain access to the whole world's employers. The meeting place is the **online job portal** — Bangladesh's book names its own, bdjobs and chakri.com, beside the international freelance marketplaces where clients post jobs and workers bid for them with profiles and past work as their shop window.
A **freelancer** works job by job for many clients, with no single employer: free to choose work and hours, and free to go unpaid between jobs. The book is honest about the downsides: income is **irregular**, there is **no job security** or paid leave, payment can arrive late or not at all, and the worker competes with the whole world on price. The skills that protect a freelancer are the village's skills — a strong profile, kept promises, clear communication, and work good enough that clients return.
