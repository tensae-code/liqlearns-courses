**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why do companies outsource?** → **To find skills and lower costs**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a freelancer compete on, against the whole world?** → **Price — and quality and reliability**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name two things that protect a freelancer.** → **A strong profile and kept promises (also clear communication)**
