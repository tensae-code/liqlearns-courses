# Outsourcing and Freelancing — Working for the World From Home — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is outsourcing?**
**Answer: Work a company gives to people outside itself, often in another country**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why do companies outsource?**
**Answer: To find skills and lower costs**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do outsourced jobs and workers meet?**
**Answer: On online job portals and freelance marketplaces**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the two Bangladeshi job portals the book names.**
**Answer: bdjobs and chakri.com**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a freelancer's shop window?**
**Answer: Their profile and past work**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a freelancer?**
**Answer: A worker who does job-by-job work for many clients, with no single employer**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two downsides of freelancing.**
**Answer: Irregular income and no job security (also late payment, world-wide price competition)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a freelancer compete on, against the whole world?**
**Answer: Price — and quality and reliability**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name two things that protect a freelancer.**
**Answer: A strong profile and kept promises (also clear communication)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Does a freelancer get paid leave from a client?**
**Answer: No — there is no employer to grant it**
