# Outsourcing and Freelancing — Working for the World From Home — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Work a company gives to people outside itself, often in another country**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Work a company gives to people outside itself, often in another country** — as the answer to: Why do companies outsource?
**Answer: False — To find skills and lower costs**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: On online job portals and freelance marketplaces**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: On online job portals and freelance marketplaces** — as the answer to: Name the two Bangladeshi job portals the book names.
**Answer: False — bdjobs and chakri.com**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Their profile and past work**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Their profile and past work** — as the answer to: What is a freelancer?
**Answer: False — A worker who does job-by-job work for many clients, with no single employer**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Irregular income and no job security (also late payment, world-wide price competition)**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Irregular income and no job security (also late payment, world-wide price competition)** — as the answer to: What does a freelancer compete on, against the whole world?
**Answer: False — Price — and quality and reliability**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A strong profile and kept promises (also clear communication)**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A strong profile and kept promises (also clear communication)** — as the answer to: Does a freelancer get paid leave from a client?
**Answer: False — No — there is no employer to grant it**
