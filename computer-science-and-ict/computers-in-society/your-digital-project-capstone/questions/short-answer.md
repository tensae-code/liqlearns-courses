# Your Digital Project — Capstone — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a capstone project?**
**Answer: One finished piece of work that proves the whole term's skills together**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does project planning start from?**
**Answer: A single clear goal — what the project will show or do, and who it is for**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the project's parts come from?**
**Answer: From the term's lessons — database, documents, cloud, meetings — chosen for the goal**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the plan name?**
**Answer: The parts, the order of making them, and how each part is known to be finished**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What mirrors Egypt's Grade 7 lessons in print?**
**Answer: A separate Arabic activity booklet with an activity set for each lesson**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When is each part of the project checked?**
**Answer: As it is finished — not only at the end**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why check early?**
**Answer: A fault found early is cheap; a fault found at presentation is not**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens at the presentation?**
**Answer: The pupil shows what the project does and answers questions about the choices made**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the project's real subject, beyond the tools?**
**Answer: The pupil's own judgement — choosing tools for a purpose**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does Egypt's Grade 7 book close its lessons?**
**Answer: With self-assessment — each lesson opens with 'I can…' objectives and closes with Learn-by-doing**
