# Hands-on — Trademarks — the sign that tells enterprises apart

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Mark hunt
Walk one street or market (or one screen of apps) and log ten trademarks: sign, enterprise, goods. Star the Ethiopian ones. The log shows how much of shopping runs on these signs.

## Design and defend
Design a mark for a real or invented local business. Present it to a partner whose job is to argue it is confusingly similar to an existing mark. Improve it until the argument fails.
