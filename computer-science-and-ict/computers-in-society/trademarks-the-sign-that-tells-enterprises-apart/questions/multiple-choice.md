# Trademarks — the sign that tells enterprises apart — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A trademark is —
a) an invention grant  b) a sign that distinguishes one enterprise's goods or services from another's  c) a secret  d) a tax stamp
**Answer: b) a sign that distinguishes one enterprise's goods or services from another's** — the definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which set is the book's examples?
a) Meta, RIDE and Microsoft  b) only local shops  c) only banks  d) no examples given
**Answer: a) Meta, RIDE and Microsoft** — the recognisable three.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopian companies such as Ride —
a) hold no trademarks  b) hold registered trademarks  c) are forbidden marks  d) are patents
**Answer: b) hold registered trademarks** — the home example.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Trademark rights come from —
a) registration only  b) use  c) inheritance only  d) the government
**Answer: b) use** — unlike patents and copyrights.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Trademark rights can be held —
a) for one year  b) indefinitely  c) for a school term  d) never
**Answer: b) indefinitely** — no fixed term while in use.
