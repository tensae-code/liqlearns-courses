# Trademarks — the sign that tells enterprises apart — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Registration is legally required before any trademark right exists.
**Answer: False** — rights come from use; registration adds advantages.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A trademark answers the buyer's question: whose goods are these?
**Answer: True** — distinguishing sources is its work.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A patent and a trademark last the same way.
**Answer: False** — trademark rights can be held indefinitely; patents are term rights.
