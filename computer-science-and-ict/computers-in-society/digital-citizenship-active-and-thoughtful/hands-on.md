# Hands-on — Digital citizenship — active and thoughtful, not just careful

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Campaign in miniature
Run the guide's task small: pick the issue, draft the message, make one real post or poster, and show it to ten people. Record their first question — that question is your campaign's first feedback.

## Footprint audit
List your last twenty public online acts. Mark each: builds connection, neutral, or careless. Rewrite the worst one as the citizen of this lesson would have posted it.
