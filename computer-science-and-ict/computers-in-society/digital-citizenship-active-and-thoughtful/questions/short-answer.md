# Digital citizenship — active and thoughtful, not just careful — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Recite the book's four do's.
**Answer:** Make your community better with technology; engage respectfully with people of different beliefs; make your voice heard by public leaders and the global community; determine the validity of online sources.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the guide's positive definition of the good digital citizen.
**Answer:** Someone who uses technology successfully to interact with others, participate in society, project a positive image and consume digital information safely and responsibly — creating a positive and useful digital footprint (teacher guide — evidence).
