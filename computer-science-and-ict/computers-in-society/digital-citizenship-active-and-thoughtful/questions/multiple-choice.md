# Digital citizenship — active and thoughtful, not just careful — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Digital citizenship is —
a) owning a computer  b) the responsible use of technology to engage with society  c) a passport type  d) an internet package
**Answer: b) the responsible use of technology to engage with society** — the definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Good citizenship engages —
a) positively, critically and competently  b) loudly and angrily  c) rarely  d) only for profit
**Answer: a) positively, critically and competently** — the three adverbs.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Poor citizenship is shown by —
a) checking sources  b) online bullying, careless social-media use and ignorance of internet safety  c) helping community  d) respect
**Answer: b) online bullying, careless social-media use and ignorance of internet safety** — the contrast set.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is one of the book's four do's?
a) hide your views  b) determine the validity of online sources  c) post first, think later  d) avoid public leaders
**Answer: b) determine the validity of online sources** — verification as a civic act.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's competition is the —
a) Spelling Bee  b) Digital Citizenship Challenge Ambassador  c) Young Coders Cup  d) Debate Shield
**Answer: b) Digital Citizenship Challenge Ambassador** — the guide's motivational device (teacher guide — evidence).
