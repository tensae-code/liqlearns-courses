# Video script — Digital citizenship — active and thoughtful, not just careful

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. (FULL script — the placement names a full video need for this lesson.)

---

A citizen is not just careful. A citizen acts.

Pick your issue first: something pressing in your own community. A heritage site nobody visits. A practice that should end. A national agenda that needs voices.

Say it in one sentence — the message a stranger can repeat.

Choose your tools from what is actually available: the social platforms your community really uses, the formats they really open.

Make the first piece: a poster, a short post, a photo with the story under it. Positive image. True information. Respectful of every belief it passes.

Share it to the wider community — regional first, then global. Watch what returns. Answer with the same respect you posted with.

That is the campaign. And if your school runs it — the Digital Citizenship Challenge Ambassador is watching for exactly this.
