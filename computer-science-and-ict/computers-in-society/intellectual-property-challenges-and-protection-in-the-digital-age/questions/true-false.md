# Intellectual Property — Challenges and Protection in the Digital Age — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Copies are perfect, instant and free to make**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Copies are perfect, instant and free to make** — as the answer to: Name four challenge forms.
**Answer: False — Copied without loss, spread in seconds, altered, hard to trace**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: No broken lock**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: No broken lock** — as the answer to: What do creators lose?
**Answer: False — Income and control**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The incentive that pays for creating**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The incentive that pays for creating** — as the answer to: Name the three protection families.
**Answer: False — Law, technology, practice**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Watermarks/fingerprints, DRM/access controls, platform copy detection**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Watermarks/fingerprints, DRM/access controls, platform copy detection** — as the answer to: What is the user's half of practice?
**Answer: False — Crediting, licensing and paying**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Law needs technology's enforcement; technology needs law's backing; both need practice's respect**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Law needs technology's enforcement; technology needs law's backing; both need practice's respect** — as the answer to: What is a protection answering no named challenge?
**Answer: False — Decoration**
