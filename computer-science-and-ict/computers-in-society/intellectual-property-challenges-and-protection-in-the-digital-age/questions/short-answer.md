# Intellectual Property — Challenges and Protection in the Digital Age — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What three facts challenge IP digitally?**
**Answer: Copies are perfect, instant and free to make**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name four challenge forms.**
**Answer: Copied without loss, spread in seconds, altered, hard to trace**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What shows no trace in a quiet copy?**
**Answer: No broken lock**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do creators lose?**
**Answer: Income and control**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does society lose?**
**Answer: The incentive that pays for creating**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three protection families.**
**Answer: Law, technology, practice**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name three technological protections.**
**Answer: Watermarks/fingerprints, DRM/access controls, platform copy detection**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the user's half of practice?**
**Answer: Crediting, licensing and paying**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does no family suffice alone?**
**Answer: Law needs technology's enforcement; technology needs law's backing; both need practice's respect**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is a protection answering no named challenge?**
**Answer: Decoration**
