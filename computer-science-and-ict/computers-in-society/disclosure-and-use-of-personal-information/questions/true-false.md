# Disclosure and Use of Personal Information — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is 'use' of personal information?" is: Working with it inside the organisation that holds it**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the standing rule for use?" is: In a record reused for something its owner never agreed to**
**Answer: False** — Use stays inside the purpose the information was given for

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does most quiet harm happen?" is: In a record reused for something its owner never agreed to**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name two duties of a holder of personal information?" is: Letting personal information leave the holder — to another organisation, person or the public**
**Answer: False** — Keep it accurate, and keep it only as long as the purpose needs (also: tell the person what is held and why)

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is disclosure?" is: Letting personal information leave the holder — to another organisation, person or the public**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name the three bases for disclosure?" is: As small as the basis allows — the needed facts to the needed receiver, and no more**
**Answer: False** — The owner's consent, a legal duty, or a genuine emergency protecting life

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How big should a disclosure be?" is: As small as the basis allows — the needed facts to the needed receiver, and no more**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What duties does the receiver of disclosed information inherit?" is: Because at disclosure the person loses control of the information**
**Answer: False** — Use it only for the disclosed purpose and protect it the same way

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why does disclosure need a basis when use does not?" is: Because at disclosure the person loses control of the information**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which Egyptian platform does the book's teaching sit beside?" is: Working with it inside the organisation that holds it**
**Answer: False** — The Digital Egypt platform — citizens hand data to state digital services
