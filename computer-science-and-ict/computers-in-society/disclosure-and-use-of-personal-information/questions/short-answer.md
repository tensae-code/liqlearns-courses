# Disclosure and Use of Personal Information — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is 'use' of personal information?**
**Answer: Working with it inside the organisation that holds it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the standing rule for use?**
**Answer: Use stays inside the purpose the information was given for**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does most quiet harm happen?**
**Answer: In a record reused for something its owner never agreed to**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name two duties of a holder of personal information**
**Answer: Keep it accurate, and keep it only as long as the purpose needs (also: tell the person what is held and why)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is disclosure?**
**Answer: Letting personal information leave the holder — to another organisation, person or the public**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three bases for disclosure**
**Answer: The owner's consent, a legal duty, or a genuine emergency protecting life**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How big should a disclosure be?**
**Answer: As small as the basis allows — the needed facts to the needed receiver, and no more**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What duties does the receiver of disclosed information inherit?**
**Answer: Use it only for the disclosed purpose and protect it the same way**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does disclosure need a basis when use does not?**
**Answer: Because at disclosure the person loses control of the information**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which Egyptian platform does the book's teaching sit beside?**
**Answer: The Digital Egypt platform — citizens hand data to state digital services**
