# Hands-on — Digital ownership — ask the creator first

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Permission practice
Choose a real photo or text online you would like in a project. Write the actual request: who you are, what you want to use, where it will appear, and the credit line you will give. Send it or keep it ready — the writing is the practice.

## Owner for a day
Publish nothing; instead, mark three of your own digital works (a photo, an essay, a recording) with your name and a line saying how others may use them. Feel the owner's side of the rule.
