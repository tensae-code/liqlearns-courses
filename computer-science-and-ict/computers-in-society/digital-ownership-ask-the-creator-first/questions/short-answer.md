# Digital ownership — ask the creator first — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the three verbs in the definition of digital ownership.
**Answer:** Access, use and protect.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three things permission does.
**Answer:** It credits the source, spreads knowledge properly, and protects the user from the legal results of breaking ownership rights.
