# Digital ownership — ask the creator first — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Digital ownership is being able to —
a) access, use and protect data and work that is yours  b) copy anything  c) hide your files  d) sell any data
**Answer: a) access, use and protect data and work that is yours** — the three verbs of the definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Digital ownership covers —
a) only paper documents  b) data, information and knowledge about yourself or things you own  c) only money  d) only passwords
**Answer: b) data, information and knowledge about yourself or things you own** — the definition's own scope.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Ownership is hard to control online because —
a) files are heavy  b) digital work copies and pastes so easily  c) creators hide  d) screens are small
**Answer: b) digital work copies and pastes so easily** — ease of copying is the pressure.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Using someone else's digital work without the creator's permission is —
a) clever  b) wrong  c) required  d) impossible
**Answer: b) wrong** — the book's plain verdict.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Permission protects the user from —
a) all homework  b) the legal results of breaking ownership rights  c) learning  d) the internet
**Answer: b) the legal results of breaking ownership rights** — one of permission's three works.
