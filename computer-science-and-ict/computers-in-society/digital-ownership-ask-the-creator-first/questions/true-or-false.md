# Digital ownership — ask the creator first — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** If a work is easy to copy, it has no owner.
**Answer: False** — copying is easy; ownership remains.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Permission credits the source and spreads knowledge properly.
**Answer: True** — two of the three things permission does.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Asking permission is only politeness, with no legal effect.
**Answer: False** — it protects the user from the legal results of breaking ownership rights.
