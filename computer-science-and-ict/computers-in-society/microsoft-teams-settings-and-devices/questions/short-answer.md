# Microsoft Teams — Settings and Devices — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does this lesson teach?**
**Answer: Teams' settings on the computer program and on the smart-device app**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two things computer settings control.**
**Answer: Notifications and which microphone, speaker and camera Teams uses (any two)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How should a setting be explored?**
**Answer: Change one thing, see what it does, and know how to change it back**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the profile setting decide?**
**Answer: What classmates see of you on the platform**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Are the teams on the phone different from the computer's?**
**Answer: No — the same account and the same teams on both**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is different on each device?**
**Answer: Each device's own settings and permissions**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Does changing a phone setting rewrite the computer's?**
**Answer: No — each device's settings are its own**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which permissions does the app ask for?**
**Answer: The device's camera and microphone**

**9. (Sub lesson 1) Why do meeting devices matter?**
**Answer: A meeting fails if Teams is listening to the wrong microphone or camera**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book's Teams section teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 4 Digital Explorer Term 1, Teams Lesson 2**
