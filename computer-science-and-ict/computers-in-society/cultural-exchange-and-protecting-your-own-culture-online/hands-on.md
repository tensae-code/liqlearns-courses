# Hands-on — Cultural Exchange — and Protecting Your Own Culture Online

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Fill your stall** — Choose one piece of your own local culture — a song, recipe, craft or story. Plan its online form: what is recorded, in what language, and who it is for.

## Task two
**Two-way audit** — List the last ten cultural things you watched or heard online, with their countries. Count how many are your own culture's, and write one sentence on the balance.
