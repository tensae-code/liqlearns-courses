# Cultural Exchange — and Protecting Your Own Culture Online — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Cultures meeting and borrowing — music, food, ideas crossing borders in seconds**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Cultures meeting and borrowing — music, food, ideas crossing borders in seconds** — as the answer to: Name two gifts of exchange.
**Answer: False — A weaver finds buyers abroad; a festival finds an audience of millions**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The ones with the biggest speakers**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The ones with the biggest speakers** — as the answer to: How do people meet cultures now, differently from before?
**Answer: False — Directly, through screens, not through travellers' tales**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: No — it runs through connections**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: No — it runs through connections** — as the answer to: What is the named risk of one-way exchange?
**Answer: False — Knowing another culture better than your own**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Filling your own stall — making and sharing your own culture**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Filling your own stall — making and sharing your own culture** — as the answer to: Is protection closing the gate?
**Answer: False — No — it is making the exchange run both ways**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: By being practised, recorded and passed on**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: By being practised, recorded and passed on** — as the answer to: Name one thing of your own culture that belongs online.
**Answer: False — Its language, music, stories or crafts (any one, named concretely)**
