# Cultural Exchange — and Protecting Your Own Culture Online — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is cultural exchange through ICT?**
**Answer: Cultures meeting and borrowing — music, food, ideas crossing borders in seconds**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two gifts of exchange.**
**Answer: A weaver finds buyers abroad; a festival finds an audience of millions**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) In the market image, whose stalls are heard farthest?**
**Answer: The ones with the biggest speakers**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How do people meet cultures now, differently from before?**
**Answer: Directly, through screens, not through travellers' tales**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Does exchange need travel?**
**Answer: No — it runs through connections**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the named risk of one-way exchange?**
**Answer: Knowing another culture better than your own**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is protecting a culture, in this lesson?**
**Answer: Filling your own stall — making and sharing your own culture**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Is protection closing the gate?**
**Answer: No — it is making the exchange run both ways**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How does a culture survive online?**
**Answer: By being practised, recorded and passed on**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name one thing of your own culture that belongs online.**
**Answer: Its language, music, stories or crafts (any one, named concretely)**
