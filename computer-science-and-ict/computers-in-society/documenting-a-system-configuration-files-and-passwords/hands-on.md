# Hands-on — Documenting a System — Configuration Files and Passwords

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Document your machine
Write the configuration record for one computer you use: its name, three settings, one share, and who has permission for what. Mark where a password would be kept protected.

## The missing page
Your companion removes one item from your record. Act out (in words) the fault-fixing delay it causes, then restore the page.
