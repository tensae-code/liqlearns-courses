# Project — Making a Small Book in Word — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Grade 7 Word unit organised as?**
**Answer: One long project — a printed booklet**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the booklets' subject?**
**Answer: "Усаа хайрлая" — Cherish the Water**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three opening decisions.**
**Answer: Purpose, audience and structure**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the audience decide?**
**Answer: The reading level and interests that shape every page**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is sketched in the structure?**
**Answer: The booklet's parts in order — cover, contents, sections**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why is gathered information checked at gathering time?**
**Answer: A printed book cannot be corrected after the run**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is each gathered piece marked with?**
**Answer: The section it belongs to**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens if a book is begun by typing?**
**Answer: It is rewritten all the way through**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name the stages that follow planning in the unit.**
**Answer: Type it into Word, set its pages, dress it with pictures and tables, print it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of lesson is this, in the course's terms?**
**Answer: A project-planning lesson — the opening of a book-length project**
