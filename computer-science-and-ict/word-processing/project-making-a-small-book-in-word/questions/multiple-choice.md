# Project — Making a Small Book in Word — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Grade 7 Word unit organised as?**
a) One long project — a printed booklet  b) "Усаа хайрлая" — Cherish the Water  c) Purpose, audience and structure  d) The reading level and interests that shape every page
**Answer: a) One long project — a printed booklet**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the booklets' subject?**
a) Purpose, audience and structure  b) "Усаа хайрлая" — Cherish the Water  c) The reading level and interests that shape every page  d) The booklet's parts in order — cover, contents, sections
**Answer: b) "Усаа хайрлая" — Cherish the Water**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three opening decisions.**
a) The reading level and interests that shape every page  b) The booklet's parts in order — cover, contents, sections  c) Purpose, audience and structure  d) A printed book cannot be corrected after the run
**Answer: c) Purpose, audience and structure**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the audience decide?**
a) The booklet's parts in order — cover, contents, sections  b) A printed book cannot be corrected after the run  c) The section it belongs to  d) The reading level and interests that shape every page
**Answer: d) The reading level and interests that shape every page**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is sketched in the structure?**
a) The booklet's parts in order — cover, contents, sections  b) A printed book cannot be corrected after the run  c) The section it belongs to  d) It is rewritten all the way through
**Answer: a) The booklet's parts in order — cover, contents, sections**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is each gathered piece marked with?**
a) It is rewritten all the way through  b) Type it into Word, set its pages, dress it with pictures and tables, print it  c) The section it belongs to  d) A project-planning lesson — the opening of a book-length project

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens if a book is begun by typing?**
a) Type it into Word, set its pages, dress it with pictures and tables, print it  b) A project-planning lesson — the opening of a book-length project  c) One long project — a printed booklet  d) It is rewritten all the way through

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name the stages that follow planning in the unit.**
