# Hands-on — Project — Making a Small Book in Word

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Plan the booklet', 'In a team, fix your own small book's purpose, audience and structure on one sheet. Only when all three are written may anyone open Word.')

## Task two
('Gather for one section', 'Gather the information for one section of your team's book: at least three facts and one picture, each checked and marked with its section.')
