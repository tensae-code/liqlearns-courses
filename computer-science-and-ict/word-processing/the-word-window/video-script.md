# Video script — The Word window — naming its parts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Open Word. Look at the window.
Top: the title bar. The tabs. Save, Undo, Repeat — always in reach.

Middle: the Ribbon, full of buttons. The rulers. The big white document area.
Bottom: the status bar. The views. The zoom.

And the File tab — Save, Open, Print.
Now you can name every part.
