# Hands-on — The Word window — naming its parts

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Pointing quiz
In pairs: one names a part, the other points to it on the real window. Ten parts, then swap. Misses go on a list to re-check against the picture.

## Draw the window
Draw the Word window from memory on paper, label every part you can, then compare with the real window and add what you missed in another colour.
