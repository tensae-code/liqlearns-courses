# The Word window — naming its parts — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ______ bar at the top centre carries the program and document titles.
**Answer: title**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Buttons grouped in tabs by task form the ______.
**Answer: Ribbon**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The ______ bar shows document information and the insertion-point location.
**Answer: status**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** More commands are added to the Quick Access Toolbar from its ______-down.
**Answer: pull**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The ______ Slide Control changes how big the page looks.
**Answer: Zoom**
