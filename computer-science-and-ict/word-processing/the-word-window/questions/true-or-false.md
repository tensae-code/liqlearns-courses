# The Word window — naming its parts — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Quick Access Toolbar holds Save, Undo and Repeat by default.
**Answer: True** — the book names those three.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Your text and pictures go in the document area.
**Answer: True** — the big white area is for content.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The status bar is at the top of the window.
**Answer: False** — it is the bottom bar, with document information.
