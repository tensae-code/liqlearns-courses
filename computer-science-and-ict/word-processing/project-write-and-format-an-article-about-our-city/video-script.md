# Video script — Project — Write and Format an Article About Our City

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The **team** writes an **article about our city** in **Word**: agree the parts first — what our city is, its places, its life — then draft each part with the unit's typing and editing skills, in Arabic or English with the language switch where needed. Every member writes; the team reads the whole aloud before formatting begins.
Then the article is **finished as a document**: a **title** at its head, **symbols and bullets to mark its parts**, and the unit's **formatting** — capitals where English needs them, bold and colour where emphasis belongs, alignment that suits each part. The test of the project: a reader can see the article's structure before reading a word.
