# Project — Write and Format an Article About Our City

## Do it first

Your city, your words, your team's article. Everything from the unit — typing, editing, formatting, lists — lands on this one page. (teacher guide — evidence; PARTIAL — Term 1 only)

## Sub lesson 1 — Writing the article as a team

The **team** writes an **article about our city** in **Word**: agree the parts first — what our city is, its places, its life — then draft each part with the unit's typing and editing skills, in Arabic or English with the language switch where needed. Every member writes; the team reads the whole aloud before formatting begins.

[Picture — writing the article as a team]

## Sub lesson 2 — Applying everything — title, marks, formatting

Then the article is **finished as a document**: a **title** at its head, **symbols and bullets to mark its parts**, and the unit's **formatting** — capitals where English needs them, bold and colour where emphasis belongs, alignment that suits each part. The test of the project: a reader can see the article's structure before reading a word.

[Picture — applying everything — title, marks, formatting]

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: The unit's project: in a team, write an article about your city in Word and apply everything the unit taught — a title, symbols and bullets to mark its parts, and formatting throughout — until the article reads and looks like a finished piece. Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.
