# Project — Write and Format an Article About Our City — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the team write?**
**Answer: An article about our city**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In what program?**
**Answer: Word**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is agreed before drafting?**
**Answer: The article's parts — what the city is, its places, its life**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What skills draft each part?**
**Answer: The unit's typing and editing skills**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Who writes?**
**Answer: Every member of the team**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the team do before formatting?**
**Answer: Reads the whole article aloud**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What stands at the article's head?**
**Answer: A title**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What marks the article's parts?**
**Answer: Symbols and bullets**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name two formatting jobs in the finishing.**
**Answer: Capitals where English needs them; bold and colour for emphasis (and alignment that suits each part)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the project's test?**
**Answer: A reader can see the article's structure before reading a word**
