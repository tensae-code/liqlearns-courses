# Hands-on — Project — Write and Format an Article About Our City

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draft as a team
Agree your city's three parts, draft one each, then read the joined article aloud as a team and fix what the ear catches.

## Finish the document
Give the article its title, mark its parts with bullets or symbols, and apply the team's formatting. Print or show it and ask a reader to name its structure at a glance.
