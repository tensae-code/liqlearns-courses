# Hands-on — Comments and Reviewing a Document

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Comment a classmate's page', 'Exchange documents: put at least three comments on your partner's text — one question, one correction, one suggestion — without changing a word of the text itself.')

## Task two
('Answer every comment', "Take a document commented by someone else: answer each comment by a change or a reply, until none is open, and say which comment improved the document most.")
