# Comments and Reviewing a Document — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a comment?**
**Answer: A note attached to a place in a document**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is a comment shown?**
**Answer: Beside the text, in the margin or a note area**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a comment do to the text itself?**
**Answer: Nothing — it asks, doubts or proposes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Who decides after a comment?**
**Answer: The writer**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Whose comments does a document carry?**
**Answer: A second reader's — a classmate's or teacher's**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is reviewing?**
**Answer: Reading a document as its reader will and commenting wherever it is unclear, wrong or weak**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the writer do with each comment?**
**Answer: Answers it — changing the text or replying**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is reviewing done?**
**Answer: When no open comment remains**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Cuba place commenting in the chapter?**
**Answer: Right before printing**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is review the last work?**
**Answer: Because after it the page leaves the screen**
