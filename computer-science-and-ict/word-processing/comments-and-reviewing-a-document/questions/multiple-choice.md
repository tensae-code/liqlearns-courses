# Comments and Reviewing a Document — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a comment?**
a) A note attached to a place in a document  b) Beside the text, in the margin or a note area  c) Nothing — it asks, doubts or proposes  d) The writer
**Answer: a) A note attached to a place in a document**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is a comment shown?**
a) Nothing — it asks, doubts or proposes  b) Beside the text, in the margin or a note area  c) The writer  d) A second reader's — a classmate's or teacher's
**Answer: b) Beside the text, in the margin or a note area**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a comment do to the text itself?**
a) The writer  b) A second reader's — a classmate's or teacher's  c) Nothing — it asks, doubts or proposes  d) Reading a document as its reader will and commenting wherever it is unclear, wrong or weak
**Answer: c) Nothing — it asks, doubts or proposes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Who decides after a comment?**
a) A second reader's — a classmate's or teacher's  b) Reading a document as its reader will and commenting wherever it is unclear, wrong or weak  c) Answers it — changing the text or replying  d) The writer
**Answer: d) The writer**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Whose comments does a document carry?**
a) A second reader's — a classmate's or teacher's  b) Reading a document as its reader will and commenting wherever it is unclear, wrong or weak  c) Answers it — changing the text or replying  d) When no open comment remains
**Answer: a) A second reader's — a classmate's or teacher's**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is reviewing?**
a) Answers it — changing the text or replying  b) Reading a document as its reader will and commenting wherever it is unclear, wrong or weak  c) When no open comment remains  d) Right before printing
**Answer: b) Reading a document as its reader will and commenting wherever it is unclear, wrong or weak**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the writer do with each comment?**
a) When no open comment remains  b) Right before printing  c) Answers it — changing the text or replying  d) Because after it the page leaves the screen
**Answer: c) Answers it — changing the text or replying**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is reviewing done?**
a) Right before printing  b) Because after it the page leaves the screen  c) A note attached to a place in a document  d) When no open comment remains
**Answer: d) When no open comment remains**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Cuba place commenting in the chapter?**
a) Right before printing  b) Because after it the page leaves the screen  c) A note attached to a place in a document  d) Beside the text, in the margin or a note area
**Answer: a) Right before printing**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is review the last work?**
a) A note attached to a place in a document  b) Because after it the page leaves the screen  c) Beside the text, in the margin or a note area  d) Nothing — it asks, doubts or proposes
**Answer: b) Because after it the page leaves the screen**
