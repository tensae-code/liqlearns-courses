# Footnotes and Endnotes — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does a footnote stand?" is: At the foot of the page where its mark appears**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where are endnotes gathered?" is: Only the small raised mark**
**Answer: False** — At the document's end (or a section's)

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the text itself carry?" is: Only the small raised mark**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the writer's choice between the houses?" is: Word — automatically**
**Answer: False** — A courtesy decision — how far the reader should travel for the aside

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Who numbers the notes?" is: Word — automatically**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens when a note is deleted?" is: Its note travels and takes its new number**
**Answer: False** — The remaining notes renumber

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens when a paragraph moves?" is: Its note travels and takes its new number**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why is hand numbering hopeless?" is: Naming sources where they are used**
**Answer: False** — No hand-kept list survives editing

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What duty do the pupils use notes for?" is: Naming sources where they are used**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which strand teaches this?" is: At the foot of the page where its mark appears**
**Answer: False** — Mongolia's Grade 9 Word strand
