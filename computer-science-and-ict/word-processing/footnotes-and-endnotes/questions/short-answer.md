# Footnotes and Endnotes — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a footnote stand?**
**Answer: At the foot of the page where its mark appears**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where are endnotes gathered?**
**Answer: At the document's end (or a section's)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the text itself carry?**
**Answer: Only the small raised mark**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the writer's choice between the houses?**
**Answer: A courtesy decision — how far the reader should travel for the aside**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Who numbers the notes?**
**Answer: Word — automatically**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when a note is deleted?**
**Answer: The remaining notes renumber**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when a paragraph moves?**
**Answer: Its note travels and takes its new number**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is hand numbering hopeless?**
**Answer: No hand-kept list survives editing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What duty do the pupils use notes for?**
**Answer: Naming sources where they are used**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which strand teaches this?**
**Answer: Mongolia's Grade 9 Word strand**
