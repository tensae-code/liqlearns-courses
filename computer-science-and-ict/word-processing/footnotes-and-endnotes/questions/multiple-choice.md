# Footnotes and Endnotes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a footnote stand?**
a) At the foot of the page where its mark appears  b) At the document's end (or a section's)  c) Only the small raised mark  d) A courtesy decision — how far the reader should travel for the aside
**Answer: a) At the foot of the page where its mark appears**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where are endnotes gathered?**
a) Only the small raised mark  b) At the document's end (or a section's)  c) A courtesy decision — how far the reader should travel for the aside  d) Word — automatically
**Answer: b) At the document's end (or a section's)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the text itself carry?**
a) A courtesy decision — how far the reader should travel for the aside  b) Word — automatically  c) Only the small raised mark  d) The remaining notes renumber
**Answer: c) Only the small raised mark**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the writer's choice between the houses?**
a) Word — automatically  b) The remaining notes renumber  c) Its note travels and takes its new number  d) A courtesy decision — how far the reader should travel for the aside
**Answer: d) A courtesy decision — how far the reader should travel for the aside**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Who numbers the notes?**
a) Word — automatically  b) The remaining notes renumber  c) Its note travels and takes its new number  d) No hand-kept list survives editing
**Answer: a) Word — automatically**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when a note is deleted?**
a) Its note travels and takes its new number  b) The remaining notes renumber  c) No hand-kept list survives editing  d) Naming sources where they are used
**Answer: b) The remaining notes renumber**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when a paragraph moves?**
a) No hand-kept list survives editing  b) Naming sources where they are used  c) Its note travels and takes its new number  d) Mongolia's Grade 9 Word strand
**Answer: c) Its note travels and takes its new number**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is hand numbering hopeless?**
a) Naming sources where they are used  b) Mongolia's Grade 9 Word strand  c) At the foot of the page where its mark appears  d) No hand-kept list survives editing
**Answer: d) No hand-kept list survives editing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What duty do the pupils use notes for?**
a) Naming sources where they are used  b) Mongolia's Grade 9 Word strand  c) At the foot of the page where its mark appears  d) At the document's end (or a section's)
**Answer: a) Naming sources where they are used**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which strand teaches this?**
a) At the foot of the page where its mark appears  b) Mongolia's Grade 9 Word strand  c) At the document's end (or a section's)  d) Only the small raised mark
**Answer: b) Mongolia's Grade 9 Word strand**
