# Hands-on — Footnotes and Endnotes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Two houses', 'In one document, add a footnote explaining a term and an endnote giving a source. Say why each note lives in its house.')

## Task two
('Break the count', 'Add three footnotes, delete the middle one, and move the first paragraph to the end. Record the numbers before and after each act.')
