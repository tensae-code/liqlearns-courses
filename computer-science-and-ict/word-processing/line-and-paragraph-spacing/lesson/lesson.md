# Line and paragraph spacing

## Do it first

Open the two paragraphs from a paper on Dagu communication in north-eastern Ethiopia. The lines sit close. By the end of this lesson you will spread them to double spacing — and know exactly what Word's spacing was before you touched it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Line spacing

Word's defaults are stated first: **1.15 line spacing** and **8 points after paragraphs**. To change line spacing on the worked Dagu text: **select** the text; go **Home → Line and Paragraph Spacing**; **choose a value** — the example chooses **2.0**, and the lines spread to double spacing.

## Sub lesson 2 — Paragraph spacing

Paragraph spacing is worked from the **same menu**: **Add Space Before Paragraph** and **Remove Space After Paragraph** open or close the gap around the selected paragraph. For exact control, **Line Spacing Options** opens the **Paragraph dialog**, where the before and after amounts are set precisely — the same dialog the indentation lessons opened by the launcher.
