# Hands-on — Line and paragraph spacing

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Default detective

Open a fresh document, type two paragraphs, and check them against the stated defaults: lines at 1.15, 8 points after each paragraph. Write down how you verified each number.

## Readable page

Take a dense page of your own writing. Set its line spacing to 1.5 and add space before each paragraph. Read both versions aloud to a partner and let them judge which reads easier.
