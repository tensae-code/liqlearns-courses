# Hands-on — Printing a Document and Booklets

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Impose by hand', 'Take four small sheets. Number and fold them into a booklet, then unfold and record which page numbers share each face. Check your map against the rule in this lesson.')

## Task two
('Print the booklet', 'Set a short document to booklet printing and print it double-sided. Fold the run, and check every page stands in reading order.')
