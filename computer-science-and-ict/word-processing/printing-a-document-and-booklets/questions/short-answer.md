# Printing a Document and Booklets — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the settings a print run is ordered by.**
**Answer: Printer, pages, copies and the paper agreement (any three)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must the document's page setup match?**
**Answer: The sheets actually loaded in the printer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When are the booklet's print decisions made?**
**Answer: At the plan, not at the printer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is imposition?**
**Answer: Arranging pages on sheets so the folded sheets read in order**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In booklet printing, do pages print in reading order?**
**Answer: No — first and last share a sheet's face, and so on inward**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What two acts turn sheets into the booklet after printing?**
**Answer: Folding and fixing them together**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which setting does the arranging?**
**Answer: The word processor's booklet setting**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the finished booklet prove?**
**Answer: The whole Word unit — a document planned as a book, printed as a book**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which project does this lesson finish?**
**Answer: Mongolia's Grade 7 'Cherish the Water' booklet project**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does this lesson differ from the course's general printing lesson?**
**Answer: It carries booklet imposition — the folded-book half — as its own thing**
