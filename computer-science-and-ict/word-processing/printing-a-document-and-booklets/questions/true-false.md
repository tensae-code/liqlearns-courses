# Printing a Document and Booklets — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the settings a print run is ordered by." is: Printer, pages, copies and the paper agreement (any three)**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What must the document's page setup match?" is: At the plan, not at the printer**
**Answer: False** — The sheets actually loaded in the printer

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "When are the booklet's print decisions made?" is: At the plan, not at the printer**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is imposition?" is: No — first and last share a sheet's face, and so on inward**
**Answer: False** — Arranging pages on sheets so the folded sheets read in order

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "In booklet printing, do pages print in reading order?" is: No — first and last share a sheet's face, and so on inward**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What two acts turn sheets into the booklet after printing?" is: The word processor's booklet setting**
**Answer: False** — Folding and fixing them together

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which setting does the arranging?" is: The word processor's booklet setting**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the finished booklet prove?" is: Mongolia's Grade 7 'Cherish the Water' booklet project**
**Answer: False** — The whole Word unit — a document planned as a book, printed as a book

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which project does this lesson finish?" is: Mongolia's Grade 7 'Cherish the Water' booklet project**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How does this lesson differ from the course's general printing lesson?" is: Printer, pages, copies and the paper agreement (any three)**
**Answer: False** — It carries booklet imposition — the folded-book half — as its own thing
