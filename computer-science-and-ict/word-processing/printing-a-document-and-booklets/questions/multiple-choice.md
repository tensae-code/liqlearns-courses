# Printing a Document and Booklets — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the settings a print run is ordered by.**
a) Printer, pages, copies and the paper agreement (any three)  b) The sheets actually loaded in the printer  c) At the plan, not at the printer  d) Arranging pages on sheets so the folded sheets read in order
**Answer: a) Printer, pages, copies and the paper agreement (any three)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must the document's page setup match?**
a) At the plan, not at the printer  b) The sheets actually loaded in the printer  c) Arranging pages on sheets so the folded sheets read in order  d) No — first and last share a sheet's face, and so on inward
**Answer: b) The sheets actually loaded in the printer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When are the booklet's print decisions made?**
a) Arranging pages on sheets so the folded sheets read in order  b) No — first and last share a sheet's face, and so on inward  c) At the plan, not at the printer  d) Folding and fixing them together
**Answer: c) At the plan, not at the printer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is imposition?**
a) No — first and last share a sheet's face, and so on inward  b) Folding and fixing them together  c) The word processor's booklet setting  d) Arranging pages on sheets so the folded sheets read in order
**Answer: d) Arranging pages on sheets so the folded sheets read in order**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In booklet printing, do pages print in reading order?**
a) No — first and last share a sheet's face, and so on inward  b) Folding and fixing them together  c) The word processor's booklet setting  d) The whole Word unit — a document planned as a book, printed as a book
**Answer: a) No — first and last share a sheet's face, and so on inward**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What two acts turn sheets into the booklet after printing?**
a) The word processor's booklet setting  b) Folding and fixing them together  c) The whole Word unit — a document planned as a book, printed as a book  d) Mongolia's Grade 7 'Cherish the Water' booklet project
**Answer: b) Folding and fixing them together**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which setting does the arranging?**
a) The whole Word unit — a document planned as a book, printed as a book  b) Mongolia's Grade 7 'Cherish the Water' booklet project  c) The word processor's booklet setting  d) It carries booklet imposition — the folded-book half — as its own thing
**Answer: c) The word processor's booklet setting**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the finished booklet prove?**
a) Mongolia's Grade 7 'Cherish the Water' booklet project  b) It carries booklet imposition — the folded-book half — as its own thing  c) Printer, pages, copies and the paper agreement (any three)  d) The whole Word unit — a document planned as a book, printed as a book
