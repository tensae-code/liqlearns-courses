Mongolia's own way (Grade 7): the whole applications half is one long project — research and produce a printed booklet "Усаа хайрлая" (Cherish the Water) in Word, a Paint.NET layered picture, and a class PowerPoint; image work uses the free Paint.NET (downloaded from getpaint.net) rather than MS Paint; the e-communication chapter teaches Yahoo mail and Yahoo Messenger, Mongolian history of the örtöö relay post and the @ sign, and makes correct Latin transliteration of Mongolian Cyrillic (standard MNS 5217:2003) an ethics topic in its own right.


Kazakhstan's own way (Kopeeva G5 «Информатика»): the year runs safety → internet safety → information and its processing → a long Scratch chapter, and it closes not with a test but with two production projects: a printed newspaper page made in a word processor (page setup, columns, WordArt, page numbers) and a defended Scratch project published to the Scratch community site.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kopeeva G3 «Ақпараттық-коммуникациялық технологиялар»): Grade 3 ICT is a tools year organised by cross-cutting themes («Менің мектебім», «Сәулет», «Өнер»…): text, then sound, then computer pictures in a graphics editor, then a mixed document that combines them, then internet safety/search and presentations — with a practicum closing almost every section.

Kazakhstan's own way (Mukametzhanova G5 «Информатика» 2017 edition): the 2017 Atamura Grade 5 book opens with computer safety and a mini-project «Открытия, изменившие мир» (Discoveries that changed the world), teaches algorithms as following commands and escaping labyrinths (real and virtual), and the first program in Scratch with "graphics come alive".

Kazakhstan's own way (Kobdikova G5 «Информатика»): the Arman-PV Grade 5 book is the practicum twin of the same programme as Books 18 and 35 — nearly every unit comes in a theory + practice pair, and it adds a block the others lack: sharing files (placing, changing and downloading shared files), setting a password on documents, and organising a document effectively for print, taught as "mini-project works".
