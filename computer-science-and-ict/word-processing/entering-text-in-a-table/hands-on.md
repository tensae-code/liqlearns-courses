# Hands-on — Entering Text in a Table

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Fill a grid', 'Enter a real 3×3 record (names and marks) into a table by the type–Tab rhythm, in reading order.')

## Task two
('Grow and shrink', 'Deliberately Tab in the last cell, note the new row, remove it with Undo, and write what happened.')
