# Entering Text in a Table — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How is text entered in a table?**
**Answer: Cell by cell — click or arrive in a cell and type**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when text outgrows a cell's width?**
**Answer: It wraps inside the cell and the row's height grows**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a cell, for writing?**
**Answer: A small page of its own — the document's rules apply inside it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book enter text after merging?**
**Answer: Typing into a grid that will still change shape wastes the typing**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Which key moves to the next cell?**
**Answer: Tab**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) In which order does Tab travel?**
**Answer: Across the row, then down to the next row's start**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does Tab do in the last cell?**
**Answer: Creates a new row**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is an accidental Tab-row removed?**
**Answer: By Undo**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What rhythm does the book teach?**
**Answer: Type, Tab, type, Tab — filling in reading order**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 9 ICT, Chapter 4 §4.3**
