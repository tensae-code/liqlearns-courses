## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Capitals in English — Shift for one, Caps Lock for many** — Hold **Shift** while pressing a letter and that one letter comes out **capital** — for the **first letter of a sentence**, and for **names and countries**, which always begin with a capital in English. Press **Caps Lock** and every letter is capital until you press it again — right for a whole word or line of capitals, wrong for ordinary sentences.
2. **Shift in Arabic — the hamza and punctuation** — In Arabic typing, **Shift** reaches the forms printed above the keys — above all the **hamza** form **أ** (alif with hamza above), typed with Shift where the plain key gives the bare letter. Shift likewise types the **full stop and the comma** where they sit as upper forms — the punctuation that ends and divides Arabic sentences.
