# Hands-on — Capital Letters — Shift and Caps Lock — and Typing the Hamza

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One or all
Type a sentence with a name and a country in it, using Shift for each capital. Then type one whole word in capitals with Caps Lock — and remember to unlock it.

## Hamza practice
In Arabic, type three words containing أ using Shift, then end the line with a full stop typed with Shift.
