# Video script — Capital Letters — Shift and Caps Lock — and Typing the Hamza

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Hold **Shift** while pressing a letter and that one letter comes out **capital** — for the **first letter of a sentence**, and for **names and countries**, which always begin with a capital in English. Press **Caps Lock** and every letter is capital until you press it again — right for a whole word or line of capitals, wrong for ordinary sentences.
In Arabic typing, **Shift** reaches the forms printed above the keys — above all the **hamza** form **أ** (alif with hamza above), typed with Shift where the plain key gives the bare letter. Shift likewise types the **full stop and the comma** where they sit as upper forms — the punctuation that ends and divides Arabic sentences.
