# Capital Letters — Shift and Caps Lock — and Typing the Hamza — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does holding Shift do to one letter?" is: Makes that one letter capital**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name three places English needs a capital." is: Makes every letter capital until it is pressed again**
**Answer: False** — Names, countries, the first letter of a sentence

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does Caps Lock do?" is: Makes every letter capital until it is pressed again**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "When is Caps Lock right?" is: For ordinary sentences**
**Answer: False** — For a whole word or line of capitals

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "When is Caps Lock wrong?" is: For ordinary sentences**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does Shift reach in Arabic typing?" is: Alif with hamza above — أ**
**Answer: False** — The forms printed above the keys

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What hamza form does Shift type?" is: Alif with hamza above — أ**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What two punctuation marks does Shift type in this lesson?" is: Ends it**
**Answer: False** — The full stop and the comma

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the full stop do to a sentence?" is: Ends it**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the comma do?" is: Makes that one letter capital**
**Answer: False** — Divides a sentence
