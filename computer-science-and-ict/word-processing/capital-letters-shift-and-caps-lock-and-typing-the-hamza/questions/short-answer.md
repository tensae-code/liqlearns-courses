# Capital Letters — Shift and Caps Lock — and Typing the Hamza — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does holding Shift do to one letter?**
**Answer: Makes that one letter capital**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three places English needs a capital.**
**Answer: Names, countries, the first letter of a sentence**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does Caps Lock do?**
**Answer: Makes every letter capital until it is pressed again**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When is Caps Lock right?**
**Answer: For a whole word or line of capitals**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When is Caps Lock wrong?**
**Answer: For ordinary sentences**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Shift reach in Arabic typing?**
**Answer: The forms printed above the keys**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What hamza form does Shift type?**
**Answer: Alif with hamza above — أ**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What two punctuation marks does Shift type in this lesson?**
**Answer: The full stop and the comma**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the full stop do to a sentence?**
**Answer: Ends it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the comma do?**
**Answer: Divides a sentence**
