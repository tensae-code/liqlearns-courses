# Capital Letters — Shift and Caps Lock — and Typing the Hamza — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does holding Shift do to one letter?**
a) Makes that one letter capital  b) For a whole word or line of capitals  c) The forms printed above the keys  d) The full stop and the comma
**Answer: a) Makes that one letter capital**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three places English needs a capital.**
a) For ordinary sentences  b) Names, countries, the first letter of a sentence  c) Alif with hamza above — أ  d) Ends it
**Answer: b) Names, countries, the first letter of a sentence**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does Caps Lock do?**
a) The forms printed above the keys  b) The full stop and the comma  c) Makes every letter capital until it is pressed again  d) Divides a sentence
**Answer: c) Makes every letter capital until it is pressed again**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When is Caps Lock right?**
a) Alif with hamza above — أ  b) Ends it  c) Makes that one letter capital  d) For a whole word or line of capitals
**Answer: d) For a whole word or line of capitals**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When is Caps Lock wrong?**
a) For ordinary sentences  b) The full stop and the comma  c) Divides a sentence  d) Names, countries, the first letter of a sentence
**Answer: a) For ordinary sentences**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Shift reach in Arabic typing?**
a) Ends it  b) The forms printed above the keys  c) Makes that one letter capital  d) Makes every letter capital until it is pressed again
**Answer: b) The forms printed above the keys**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What hamza form does Shift type?**
a) Divides a sentence  b) Names, countries, the first letter of a sentence  c) Alif with hamza above — أ  d) For a whole word or line of capitals
**Answer: c) Alif with hamza above — أ**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What two punctuation marks does Shift type in this lesson?**
a) Makes that one letter capital  b) Makes every letter capital until it is pressed again  c) For ordinary sentences  d) The full stop and the comma
**Answer: d) The full stop and the comma**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the full stop do to a sentence?**
a) Ends it  b) Names, countries, the first letter of a sentence  c) For a whole word or line of capitals  d) The forms printed above the keys
**Answer: a) Ends it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the comma do?**
a) Makes every letter capital until it is pressed again  b) Divides a sentence  c) For ordinary sentences  d) Alif with hamza above — أ
**Answer: b) Divides a sentence**
