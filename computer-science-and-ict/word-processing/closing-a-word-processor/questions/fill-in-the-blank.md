# Closing a word processor — and first writing practice — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Close button is at the top-______ of the title bar.
**Answer: right**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Save the about-yourself text on the ______ under your first name.
**Answer: desktop**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Open the File menu and choose ______ to close Word the second way.
