# Hands-on — Closing a word processor — and first writing practice

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The full round
Do the complete round without help: open by any route, write, save by Save As, close by File → Exit. Then repeat, closing by the X. A partner ticks each stage.

## Discussion circle
In a small group, argue both sides for two minutes each: first that paper writing is better, then that word processing is better. Use your own experience from the practical as evidence.

## If it is hard
The guide directs the teacher to assist pupils while they write in Word, save the document they created, and close it properly — ask for that help freely during the practical; it is part of the lesson, not a failure in it.
