# Making a Document Cover Page — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a built-in cover page provide?**
a) A ready first page — title, author, date and decoration in a designed arrangement  b) The document's own facts  c) Its styles and colours follow the work's theme  d) Decorative lettering — filled, outlined and shaped
**Answer: a) A ready first page — title, author, date and decoration in a designed arrangement**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is filled into the template's fields?**
a) Its styles and colours follow the work's theme  b) The document's own facts  c) Decorative lettering — filled, outlined and shaped  d) Shapes laid as its ground
**Answer: b) The document's own facts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does an inserted cover join the document?**
