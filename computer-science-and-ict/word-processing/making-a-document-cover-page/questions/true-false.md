# Making a Document Cover Page — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a built-in cover page provide?" is: A ready first page — title, author, date and decoration in a designed arrangement**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is filled into the template's fields?" is: Its styles and colours follow the work's theme**
**Answer: False** — The document's own facts

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How does an inserted cover join the document?" is: Its styles and colours follow the work's theme**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is WordArt?" is: Shapes laid as its ground**
**Answer: False** — Decorative lettering — filled, outlined and shaped

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What carries a designed cover's structure?" is: Shapes laid as its ground**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name the design rules for a cover." is: When the work must look finished quickly and borrowing serves**
**Answer: False** — One strong idea; the title unmissable; nothing the document cannot honour inside

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is the template the professional act?" is: When the work must look finished quickly and borrowing serves**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When do you design your own?" is: The face a received document is met at**
**Answer: False** — When the work deserves its own face

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is a cover, in the lesson's opening phrase?" is: The face a received document is met at**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which pupils design covers in the book?" is: A ready first page — title, author, date and decoration in a designed arrangement**
**Answer: False** — Mongolia's Grade 9 pupils, for their own projects
