# Making a Document Cover Page — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a built-in cover page provide?**
**Answer: A ready first page — title, author, date and decoration in a designed arrangement**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is filled into the template's fields?**
**Answer: The document's own facts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does an inserted cover join the document?**
**Answer: Its styles and colours follow the work's theme**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is WordArt?**
**Answer: Decorative lettering — filled, outlined and shaped**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What carries a designed cover's structure?**
**Answer: Shapes laid as its ground**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the design rules for a cover.**
**Answer: One strong idea; the title unmissable; nothing the document cannot honour inside**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When is the template the professional act?**
**Answer: When the work must look finished quickly and borrowing serves**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When do you design your own?**
**Answer: When the work deserves its own face**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a cover, in the lesson's opening phrase?**
**Answer: The face a received document is met at**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which pupils design covers in the book?**
**Answer: Mongolia's Grade 9 pupils, for their own projects**
