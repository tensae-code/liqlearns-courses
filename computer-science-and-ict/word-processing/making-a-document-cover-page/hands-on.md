# Hands-on — Making a Document Cover Page

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Borrow a face', 'Add a built-in cover to a finished document and fill every field with the work's true facts. Check its colours against the document's theme.')

## Task two
('Design a face', 'Design a cover for the same document with WordArt and shapes only. Lay both covers side by side and defend which the work should wear.')
