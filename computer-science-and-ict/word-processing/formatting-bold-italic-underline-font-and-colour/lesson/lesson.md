# Formatting — bold, italic, underline, font and colour

## Do it first

Look at two words: one plain, one dark and heavy. The heavy one pulls your eye first. Writers control that pull. This lesson gives you the controls — and at the end you will use them on a real paragraph.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bold, italic and underline

Select the text first — formatting lands where text is selected. Then:

- **Bold** — the characters become **darker and heavier** than normal text. They are designed heavier, not thickened on the fly.
- **Italic** — a font style that **slopes the letters evenly to the right**, like handwriting leaning forward.
- **Underline** — a line **runs under the words**, holding the reader's eye on them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Font, size and colour

Word processors offer various **default font types** — the letter-shapes the text is drawn in — and various **font sizes**. Size is increased or decreased by **selecting the size number**: a bigger number, bigger letters.

**Text colour** is used to **emphasise particular text**. To change it, select a colour from the **colour options**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Writing a paragraph — the history of Ethiopian Airlines

Now use it all. The activity: **write a paragraph on the history of Ethiopian Airlines in the word processor and show it to the teacher.**

[Picture 11332 — from propeller planes to jets at Bole]

Write from these facts. The airline was founded on **30 December 1945** and began operating on **8 April 1946**, with a weekly Addis Ababa–Cairo service flown by **five Douglas DC-3 propeller aircraft**. Its main hub is **Bole International Airport**. Over some 75 years it became one of Africa's leading carriers, noted for efficiency and for turning a profit in almost all its years, and a major Ethiopian industry.

Make the airline's name bold in your paragraph. Underline the two dates. Then read your paragraph back and check every fact stands as given here.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what bold, italic and underline each do, how size is changed, and what text colour is for.
