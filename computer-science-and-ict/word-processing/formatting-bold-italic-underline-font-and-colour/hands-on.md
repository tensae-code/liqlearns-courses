# Hands-on — Formatting — bold, italic, underline, font and colour

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Six ways wall

Type one sentence about your school. Copy it five times and give each copy one format: bold, italic, underlined, a new font, a bigger size, a colour. Print or show the page: six lines, one sentence, six looks.

## The airline paragraph

Write the history-of-Ethiopian-Airlines paragraph from Sub lesson 3 in the word processor. Bold the airline's name, underline the founding and first-operating dates, and give the title a colour and a bigger size. Show it to your teacher — that showing is part of the task.
