# Formatting — bold, italic, underline, font and colour — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bold characters are —
a) lighter and thinner  b) darker and heavier than normal  c) sloped to the right  d) underlined
**Answer: b) darker and heavier than normal** — designed heavier, not thickened on the fly.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Italic is a font style that —
a) runs a line under words  b) slopes the letters evenly to the right  c) makes letters bigger  d) changes the colour
**Answer: b) slopes the letters evenly to the right.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Underline —
a) crosses the words out  b) runs a line under the words  c) doubles the words  d) leans the words left
**Answer: b) runs a line under the words.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Font size is increased or decreased by —
a) typing the word bigger  b) pressing Enter  c) selecting the size number  d) changing the colour
**Answer: c) selecting the size number** — a bigger number, bigger letters.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Text colour is used to —
a) save the document  b) emphasise particular text  c) count words  d) open a new document
**Answer: b) emphasise particular text** — chosen from the colour options.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopian Airlines began operating on —
a) 30 December 1945  b) 8 April 1946  c) 8 April 1945  d) 30 December 1946
**Answer: b) 8 April 1946** — it was founded on 30 December 1945 and began operating the next April.
