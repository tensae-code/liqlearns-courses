# Formatting — bold, italic, underline, font and colour — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bold text is made by thickening normal letters on the fly as you look at them.
**Answer: False** — bold characters are designed heavier than normal text.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Word processors offer various default font types and font sizes.
**Answer: True** — the writer chooses among them.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The airline's first service was a weekly Addis Ababa–Cairo flight.
**Answer: True** — flown by five Douglas DC-3 propeller aircraft.
