# Lesson plan — Formatting — bold, italic, underline, font and colour

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Word Processing · **Lesson:** Formatting — bold, italic, underline, font and colour
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 5 — Word Processing in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 8 book, Unit 3 — Computer Software. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** formatting changes how text looks: bold makes it darker and heavier, italic slopes it to the right, underline runs a line under it, and font, size and colour can each be chosen.
**This lesson's own level:** it is a doing-lesson. It stands on typing text — formatting is done to text that already stands on the page.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bold, italic and underline** — bold characters are darker and heavier than normal; italic slopes the letters evenly to the right; underline runs a line under the words
2. **Font, size and colour** — word processors offer various default font types and sizes — size grows or shrinks by selecting the size number — and text colour, chosen from the colour options, emphasises particular text
3. **Writing a paragraph — the history of Ethiopian Airlines** — the activity: write the paragraph in the word processor and show it to the teacher; a model of the paragraph, from the teacher guide, gives the facts to write from

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Typing text — the blinking insertion point, and Copy and paste. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Make text bold, italic and underlined, and say what each one does to the letters.
2. Change a text's font type and font size, bigger and smaller.
3. Change text colour from the colour options to emphasise particular text.
4. Write a paragraph in the word processor and present it, formatted.

## What you need
A computer with a word processor.

## If the learner is stuck
- Formats before selecting → formatting lands on selected text; select the words first, then click the format.
- Thinks bold is made by pressing harder or typing twice → bold characters are designed heavier and darker than normal text; one click on selected text does it.
