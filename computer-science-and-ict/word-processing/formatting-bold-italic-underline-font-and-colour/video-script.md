# Video script — Formatting — bold, italic, underline, font and colour

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Select the words first. Formatting lands on what is selected.

Bold — darker, heavier. It shouts.
Italic — the letters lean to the right.
Underline — a line runs underneath.

A new font changes the letter-shapes.
A bigger number makes bigger letters.
Colour makes the important words stand out.

Now write the paragraph. Make it stand up.
