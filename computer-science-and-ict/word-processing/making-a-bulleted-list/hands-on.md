# Hands-on — Making a bulleted list

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Principles poster

Build the 12 Principles of Business Ethics as a bulleted list with a style you previewed and chose on purpose. Then make a second copy with a coloured symbol bullet. Show both and say which you would put on a wall.

## Gap control

In one list, use the ruler's indent markers to widen and narrow the gap between bullet and text. Write down which marker changed the gap.
