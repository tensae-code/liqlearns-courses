# Video script — Making a bulleted list

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Lines sit flat. Give them bullets.
Select the text. Home. The arrow beside Bullets.
Hover — the page previews the style.
Click. A bulleted list stands.
Enter: a new bulleted line. Enter twice: back to normal.
Deeper: Define New Bullet. A symbol. A colour.
Even a picture can be a bullet.
