# Lesson plan — Making a bulleted list

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Word Processing · **Lesson:** Making a bulleted list
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 5 — Word Processing in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 9 book, Unit 3 — Application Software. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** select text and give it bullets from the Bullets drop-down — hovering previews each style live — and, going further, define your own bullet: a symbol, in a chosen colour, or a picture.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Paragraph group map — bullets are the group's first list command, and the custom-bullet depth comes from the teacher guide's extra practical.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Making the list** — select the text, Home → the drop-down arrow beside Bullets, hover styles for a live preview, click the chosen style; removing bullets, Enter's behaviour, and the ruler's list indents
2. **Custom bullets — symbols and colours (teacher guide — evidence)** — the guide's extra practical: Define New Bullet — a symbol bullet from Wingdings or Symbol, a coloured bullet through Font → Font Color, and a picture as a bullet

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Paragraph group — a map of its buttons.  Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Turn selected text into a bulleted list, previewing styles live before choosing.
2. Remove bullets, and use Enter and Enter-twice correctly inside a list.
3. Define a custom bullet: a symbol from Wingdings or Symbol, coloured through Font Color; or a picture.

## What you need
A computer with Word and the ethics principles typed as plain lines.

## If the learner is stuck
- Bullets landed on one line only → select the whole text first; the command works on the selection.
- Stuck inside the list at the end → press Enter twice to return to normal formatting.
- Cannot find Symbol fonts → in Define New Bullet, click Symbol; Wingdings and Symbol are the recommended fonts.
