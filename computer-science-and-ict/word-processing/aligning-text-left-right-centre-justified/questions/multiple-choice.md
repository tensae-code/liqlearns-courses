# Aligning text — left, right, centre, justified — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Left alignment lines text evenly at —
a) both margins  b) the left margin, with a jagged right edge  c) the right margin  d) the centre
**Answer: b) the left margin, with a jagged right edge.**

**2.** Centre alignment is pressed with —
a) Ctrl+L  b) Ctrl+R  c) Ctrl+E  d) Ctrl+J
**Answer: c) Ctrl+E.**

**3.** Justified alignment reaches both margins by adjusting —
a) the font size  b) the spaces between words  c) the page size  d) the margins themselves
**Answer: b) the spaces between words.**

**4.** To change a paragraph's alignment, the cursor must be —
a) at the document's start  b) anywhere in the paragraph  c) in the header  d) nowhere — select all first
**Answer: b) anywhere in the paragraph.**

**5.** Centre alignment suits —
a) long paragraphs  b) tables only  c) titles and headings  d) footnotes
**Answer: c) titles and headings.**

**6.** Right alignment lines up —
a) the first letter of each line at the left  b) the last character of each line at the right margin  c) nothing  d) the page numbers
**Answer: b) the last character of each line at the right margin.**
