# Hands-on — Aligning text — left, right, centre, justified

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Four corners

Take one paragraph. Print or show it four times, once in each alignment, labelled with the shortcut that made it. Under each, write one line: where the even edge is.

## Title treatment

Give a page a centred title over a justified body paragraph. Then re-do the title right-aligned. Say which pairing you would hand to a teacher, and why.
