# Video script — Aligning text — left, right, centre, justified

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cursor in the paragraph. One click aligns it.
Left: even left, jagged right. The common one.
Right: the mirror picture.
Centre: equal space both sides. For titles.
Justified: stretched to both margins.
Four edges. Four shortcuts.
