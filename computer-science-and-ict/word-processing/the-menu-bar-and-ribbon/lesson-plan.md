## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The menu bar — commands in words** — The **menu bar** is the row of words near the top of the Word window — File, Home, Insert, Layout and their companions. Clicking a word opens its **menu**, a list of commands in that family: File holds opening, saving and printing, while the other menus hold the commands for working on the document itself. The menu bar is the map of everything Word can do, written out in words.
2. **The ribbon — commands as buttons** — Below the menus sits the **ribbon**: a wide band of buttons organised into **tabs** such as Home, Insert and Design, and inside each tab into named **groups** such as Font and Paragraph. The Home tab carries the everyday commands — typeface, size, bold, alignment — while other tabs appear for special jobs. Pointing at a button without clicking shows its name and job in a small tip.
