# The Menu Bar and Ribbon — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the menu bar?**
a) The row of words near the top of the Word window  b) Its menu opens — a list of commands in that family  c) Everything Word can do, written out in words  d) The Home tab
**Answer: a) The row of words near the top of the Word window**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when you click a word on the menu bar?**
a) The File menu  b) Its menu opens — a list of commands in that family  c) A wide band of buttons below the menus  d) In the Font and Paragraph groups on the Home tab
**Answer: b) Its menu opens — a list of commands in that family**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which menu holds opening, saving and printing?**
a) Commands for working on the document itself  b) Into tabs, and inside each tab into named groups  c) The File menu  d) Point at it and read the small tip that appears
**Answer: c) The File menu**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do the other menus hold?**
a) Everything Word can do, written out in words  b) The Home tab  c) The row of words near the top of the Word window  d) Commands for working on the document itself
**Answer: d) Commands for working on the document itself**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the menu bar a map of?**
a) Everything Word can do, written out in words  b) A wide band of buttons below the menus  c) In the Font and Paragraph groups on the Home tab  d) Its menu opens — a list of commands in that family
**Answer: a) Everything Word can do, written out in words**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the ribbon?**
a) Into tabs, and inside each tab into named groups  b) A wide band of buttons below the menus  c) Point at it and read the small tip that appears  d) The File menu
**Answer: b) A wide band of buttons below the menus**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the ribbon organised?**
a) The Home tab  b) The row of words near the top of the Word window  c) Into tabs, and inside each tab into named groups  d) Commands for working on the document itself
**Answer: c) Into tabs, and inside each tab into named groups**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which tab carries the everyday commands?**
a) In the Font and Paragraph groups on the Home tab  b) Its menu opens — a list of commands in that family  c) Everything Word can do, written out in words  d) The Home tab
**Answer: d) The Home tab**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where do bold and alignment live?**
a) In the Font and Paragraph groups on the Home tab  b) Point at it and read the small tip that appears  c) The File menu  d) A wide band of buttons below the menus
**Answer: a) In the Font and Paragraph groups on the Home tab**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How can you learn a button's job without clicking?**
a) The row of words near the top of the Word window  b) Point at it and read the small tip that appears  c) Commands for working on the document itself  d) Into tabs, and inside each tab into named groups
