# The Menu Bar and Ribbon — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the menu bar?" is: The row of words near the top of the Word window**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What happens when you click a word on the menu bar?" is: The File menu**
**Answer: False** — Its menu opens — a list of commands in that family

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which menu holds opening, saving and printing?" is: The File menu**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do the other menus hold?" is: Everything Word can do, written out in words**
**Answer: False** — Commands for working on the document itself

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the menu bar a map of?" is: Everything Word can do, written out in words**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the ribbon?" is: Into tabs, and inside each tab into named groups**
**Answer: False** — A wide band of buttons below the menus

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is the ribbon organised?" is: Into tabs, and inside each tab into named groups**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which tab carries the everyday commands?" is: In the Font and Paragraph groups on the Home tab**
**Answer: False** — The Home tab

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where do bold and alignment live?" is: In the Font and Paragraph groups on the Home tab**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How can you learn a button's job without clicking?" is: The row of words near the top of the Word window**
**Answer: False** — Point at it and read the small tip that appears
