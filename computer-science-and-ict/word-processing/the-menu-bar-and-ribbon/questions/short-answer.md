# The Menu Bar and Ribbon — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the menu bar?**
**Answer: The row of words near the top of the Word window**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when you click a word on the menu bar?**
**Answer: Its menu opens — a list of commands in that family**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which menu holds opening, saving and printing?**
**Answer: The File menu**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do the other menus hold?**
**Answer: Commands for working on the document itself**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the menu bar a map of?**
**Answer: Everything Word can do, written out in words**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the ribbon?**
**Answer: A wide band of buttons below the menus**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the ribbon organised?**
**Answer: Into tabs, and inside each tab into named groups**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which tab carries the everyday commands?**
**Answer: The Home tab**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where do bold and alignment live?**
**Answer: In the Font and Paragraph groups on the Home tab**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How can you learn a button's job without clicking?**
**Answer: Point at it and read the small tip that appears**
