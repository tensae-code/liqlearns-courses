# Hands-on — The Layout Tab — Margins and Page Setup

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Set up a real page
Take a document and set its margins, size and orientation for printing on your school's paper. Use Print Preview to check the text sits well before anything is printed.

## Tall or wide
Put the same wide table on a portrait page and a landscape page. Say which fits better and why, using the words margin and orientation.
