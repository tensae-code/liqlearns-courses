# The Layout Tab — Margins and Page Setup — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the Layout tab hold?**
**Answer: Page setup**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is page setup?**
**Answer: How the document sits on its paper**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two things page setup decides.**
**Answer: The paper's size and the page orientation**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is portrait orientation?**
**Answer: The page stands tall**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is landscape orientation?**
**Answer: The page lies wide**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are margins?**
**Answer: The blank borders between the text and the paper's edges**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How much of the document do margin settings cover?**
**Answer: The whole document at once**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What paragraph setting does the Layout tab also carry?**
**Answer: Paragraph spacing — the space before and after paragraphs**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When should page setup be decided?**
**Answer: Before printing, not after**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which grade's book teaches the Layout tab?**
**Answer: Grade 8**
