# The Layout Tab — Margins and Page Setup — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the Layout tab hold?**
a) Page setup  b) How the document sits on its paper  c) The page stands tall  d) The blank borders between the text and the paper's edges
**Answer: a) Page setup**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is page setup?**
a) The paper's size and the page orientation  b) How the document sits on its paper  c) The page lies wide  d) The whole document at once
**Answer: b) How the document sits on its paper**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two things page setup decides.**
a) The page stands tall  b) The blank borders between the text and the paper's edges  c) The paper's size and the page orientation  d) Paragraph spacing — the space before and after paragraphs
**Answer: c) The paper's size and the page orientation**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is portrait orientation?**
a) The page lies wide  b) The whole document at once  c) Before printing, not after  d) The page stands tall
**Answer: d) The page stands tall**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is landscape orientation?**
a) The page lies wide  b) The blank borders between the text and the paper's edges  c) Paragraph spacing — the space before and after paragraphs  d) Grade 8
**Answer: a) The page lies wide**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are margins?**
a) The whole document at once  b) The blank borders between the text and the paper's edges  c) Before printing, not after  d) Page setup
**Answer: b) The blank borders between the text and the paper's edges**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How much of the document do margin settings cover?**
a) Paragraph spacing — the space before and after paragraphs  b) Grade 8  c) The whole document at once  d) How the document sits on its paper
**Answer: c) The whole document at once**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What paragraph setting does the Layout tab also carry?**
a) Before printing, not after  b) Page setup  c) The paper's size and the page orientation  d) Paragraph spacing — the space before and after paragraphs
**Answer: d) Paragraph spacing — the space before and after paragraphs**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When should page setup be decided?**
a) Before printing, not after  b) Grade 8  c) How the document sits on its paper  d) The page stands tall
**Answer: a) Before printing, not after**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which grade's book teaches the Layout tab?**
a) Page setup  b) Grade 8  c) The paper's size and the page orientation  d) The page lies wide
**Answer: b) Grade 8**
