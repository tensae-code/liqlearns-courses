# Inserting Charts and Graphs in a Document — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a chart built from?**
**Answer: Numbers — a table or typed values**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which chart compares?**
**Answer: Columns**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which chart shows change over time?**
**Answer: A line**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which chart shows parts of a whole?**
**Answer: A circle (pie) chart**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when a value is corrected?**
**Answer: The drawing corrects itself**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where must a chart sit?**
**Answer: Beside the paragraph that speaks of it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must accompany a chart?**
**Answer: A caption naming what it shows**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where do charts sit in the Grade 8 document chapter?**
**Answer: Last — after pictures, tables and shapes**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a chart the most demanding object?**
**Answer: Data, drawing and text must agree on one page**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) At what size must a chart read?**
**Answer: At the page's size**
