# Inserting Charts and Graphs in a Document — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a chart built from?**
a) Numbers — a table or typed values  b) Columns  c) A line  d) A circle (pie) chart
**Answer: a) Numbers — a table or typed values**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which chart compares?**
a) A line  b) Columns  c) A circle (pie) chart  d) The drawing corrects itself
**Answer: b) Columns**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which chart shows change over time?**
a) A circle (pie) chart  b) The drawing corrects itself  c) A line  d) Beside the paragraph that speaks of it
**Answer: c) A line**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which chart shows parts of a whole?**
a) The drawing corrects itself  b) Beside the paragraph that speaks of it  c) A caption naming what it shows  d) A circle (pie) chart
**Answer: d) A circle (pie) chart**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when a value is corrected?**
a) The drawing corrects itself  b) Beside the paragraph that speaks of it  c) A caption naming what it shows  d) Last — after pictures, tables and shapes
**Answer: a) The drawing corrects itself**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where must a chart sit?**
a) A caption naming what it shows  b) Beside the paragraph that speaks of it  c) Last — after pictures, tables and shapes  d) Data, drawing and text must agree on one page
**Answer: b) Beside the paragraph that speaks of it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must accompany a chart?**
a) Last — after pictures, tables and shapes  b) Data, drawing and text must agree on one page  c) A caption naming what it shows  d) At the page's size
**Answer: c) A caption naming what it shows**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where do charts sit in the Grade 8 document chapter?**
a) Data, drawing and text must agree on one page  b) At the page's size  c) Numbers — a table or typed values  d) Last — after pictures, tables and shapes
**Answer: d) Last — after pictures, tables and shapes**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a chart the most demanding object?**
a) Data, drawing and text must agree on one page  b) At the page's size  c) Numbers — a table or typed values  d) Columns
**Answer: a) Data, drawing and text must agree on one page**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) At what size must a chart read?**
a) Numbers — a table or typed values  b) At the page's size  c) Columns  d) A line
**Answer: b) At the page's size**
