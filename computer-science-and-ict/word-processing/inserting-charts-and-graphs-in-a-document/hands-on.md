# Hands-on — Inserting Charts and Graphs in a Document

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Chart a table', "Take a small table of numbers and insert it as two different charts; say which kind serves the paragraph's claim better, and why.")

## Task two
('Caption and place', 'Place a chart beside its paragraph with a caption, then move a value in the table and verify the drawing follows.')
