# Inserting Charts and Graphs in a Document

## Do it first

A table of numbers persuades slowly; the same numbers as a chart persuade at a glance. Grade 8's Word course closes its document lessons with inserting diagrams and graphs.

## Sub lesson 1 — From table to chart

A **chart** (диаграмма) in a document is built from numbers: select the table or type the values, choose the chart kind, and Word draws it — columns for comparing, a line for change over time, a circle for parts of a whole. The chart is tied to its numbers: correct a value and the drawing corrects itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — Placing a chart in the text

An inserted chart must be **placed like evidence**: beside the paragraph that speaks of it, with a caption naming what it shows, small enough to read at the page's size. Grade 8 puts charts last in its document chapter — after pictures, tables and shapes — as the document's most demanding object: data, drawing and text agreeing on one page.
