# Lesson plan — Indentation — moving a paragraph in from the margin

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Word Processing · **Lesson:** Indentation — moving a paragraph in from the margin
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 5 — Word Processing in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 9 book, Unit 3 — Application Software. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a paragraph is a collection of words describing one idea, and indentation is how far that paragraph is moved away from the left or right margin — done four ways: the Tab key, the Ruler, the Indent commands and the Paragraph dialog box.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Paragraph group map — indentation is the group's first skill taken up in depth, and the next four lessons teach its four methods one by one.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a paragraph is: a collection of words describing one idea or point.
2. Say what indentation is: how far a paragraph is moved from the left or right margin.
3. Name the four indentation methods in the book's order.

## What you need

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks indentation is the gap between paragraphs → no: it is the distance from the side margins; the gap between paragraphs is spacing, a later lesson.
- Cannot spot it in a book → look at where each paragraph's first line starts against the left margin.
