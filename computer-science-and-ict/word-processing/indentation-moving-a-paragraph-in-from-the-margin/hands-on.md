# Hands-on — Indentation — moving a paragraph in from the margin

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Margin hunt

Open three different textbooks. In each, find one flush paragraph and one indented paragraph. Mark the gaps with a finger and say which margin each one is measured from.

## Four doors

Write the four method names on four cards in the book's order. Shuffle, re-order them from memory, and check against the lesson.
