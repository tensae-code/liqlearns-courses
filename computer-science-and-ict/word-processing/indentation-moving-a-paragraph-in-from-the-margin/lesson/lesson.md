# Indentation — moving a paragraph in from the margin

## Do it first

Take any of your own textbooks and flip through it. Some paragraphs start flush with the margin; some start further in. That starting distance has a name. This lesson gives the name, and names the four ways of making it.

## What a paragraph is; what indentation is

A **paragraph** is a collection of words or numbers describing **one idea or point**. **Indentation** is how far the paragraph is moved **away from the left or right margin**. Look again at your textbooks: every place a paragraph sits in from the margin, that distance is indentation, put there on purpose.

## The four methods, announced

Indentation is made four ways, and the next four lessons teach them one by one, in this order: the **Tab Key method**, the **Ruler method**, the **Indent Command method** and the **Paragraph Dialog Box method**. Same result — a paragraph moved in — four different hands doing it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what a paragraph is, what indentation measures, and the four methods in the book's order.

## Do It Yourself

1. Find one indented paragraph in any textbook you own and point to the gap.
2. Say the four indentation methods in order, from memory.
3. Write, in one sentence, what indentation is.
