# Indentation — moving a paragraph in from the margin — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** A paragraph can be made of numbers as well as words.
**Answer: True — words or numbers describing one idea or point.**

**2.** Indentation moves a paragraph away from the margin.
**Answer: True.**

**3.** The book teaches only one way to indent.
**Answer: False — four methods are taught.**
