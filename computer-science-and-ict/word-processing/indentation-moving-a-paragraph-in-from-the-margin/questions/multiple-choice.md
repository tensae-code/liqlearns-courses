# Indentation — moving a paragraph in from the margin — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A paragraph is —
a) one word  b) a collection of words describing one idea or point  c) a whole chapter  d) a page number
**Answer: b) a collection of words describing one idea or point.**

**2.** Indentation is —
a) the size of the letters  b) how far a paragraph is moved away from the left or right margin  c) the gap between pages  d) a kind of bullet
**Answer: b) how far a paragraph is moved away from the left or right margin.**

**3.** How many indentation methods are taught?
a) Two  b) Three  c) Four  d) Six
**Answer: c) Four — Tab key, Ruler, Indent commands, Paragraph dialog box.**

**4.** The first method taught is —
a) the Ruler method  b) the Tab Key method  c) the Dialog Box method  d) the Sort method
**Answer: b) the Tab Key method.**

**5.** Indentation is measured from —
a) the top of the page  b) the left or right margin  c) the header  d) the next paragraph
**Answer: b) the left or right margin.**

**6.** The last of the four methods is —
a) the Tab Key method  b) the Ruler method  c) the Indent Command method  d) the Paragraph Dialog Box method
**Answer: d) the Paragraph Dialog Box method.**
