# Video script — Writing a Business Letter

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A business letter is work on paper.

Top: your name and address. The date. The receiver's name and address.

One line: the subject.

Formal greeting.

First paragraph: say why you write. At once. No waiting.

Next: the facts, in order. What. How much. When. The price.

Last paragraph: what happens next. What you ask. What you will do.

Formal closing. Sign it. Your full name. Your position.

Exact. Short. Respectful.

Check it like the office will: Can they file it? Understand it? Act on it?

Then send it.
