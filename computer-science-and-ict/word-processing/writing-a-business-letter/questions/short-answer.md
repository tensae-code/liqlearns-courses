# Writing a Business Letter — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Name the parts of a business letter in order.**
**Answer:** The sender's name and address; the date; the receiver's name and address; the subject line; a formal greeting; a first paragraph stating the purpose; body paragraphs with the facts in order; a closing paragraph saying what happens next; a formal closing; the signature with the writer's name and position.

**2. What are the three checks of the receiving office's way, before a business letter is sent?**
**Answer:** Could a stranger file it (names, addresses, date and subject all present)? Could they understand it (purpose first, facts in order)? Could they act on it (the next step stated, the sender signed and named)?

**3. Why must a business letter's facts be in the order of the job rather than the order they come to mind?**
**Answer:** Because the receiver acts on the letter in the order of the job — what, how much, when, at what terms — and that order lets them check and carry out the business without hunting.
