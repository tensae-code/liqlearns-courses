# Writing a Business Letter — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. An error in a number in a business letter can become an error in goods, money or time.**
**Answer: True** — The letter is acted on, so its facts must be exact.

**2. A business letter may be kept and read by many people over years.**
**Answer: True** — That is one reason it stays formal and respectful.

**3. The subject line of a business letter is optional decoration.**
**Answer: False** — It names the business and lets the letter be filed and found.

**4. Warm familiarity with the receiver makes a business letter stronger.**
**Answer: False** — A business letter is respectful and formal; familiarity is the personal letter's voice.
