# Writing a Business Letter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A business letter is —**
a) work written down between people and organisations doing business  b) a friendly letter to a relative  c) a note you keep for yourself only
**Answer: a) work written down between people and organisations doing business**

**2. The first paragraph of a business letter must —**
a) tell the writer's life story  b) state the letter's purpose at once  c) save the purpose for the end
**Answer: b) state the letter's purpose at once** — The business reader decides from it what the letter needs.

**3. The body paragraphs of a business letter carry —**
a) the business facts in the order of the job: what, how much, when, at what terms  b) facts in any order they come to mind  c) jokes to warm the reader
**Answer: a) the business facts in the order of the job**

**4. The closing paragraph of a business letter —**
a) fades out politely  b) repeats the greeting  c) says what happens next or what is asked, and by when
**Answer: c) says what happens next or what is asked**

**5. Under the signature of a business letter stand —**
a) the writer's full name and position  b) only a first name  c) nothing at all
**Answer: a) the writer's full name and position** — The office must know who stands behind the letter.

**6. The three disciplines of a business letter are —**
a) long, funny and personal  b) exact, brief and respectful  c) fast, loud and repeated
**Answer: b) exact, brief and respectful**
