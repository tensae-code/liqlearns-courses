Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Cambodia, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked first: Word Processing's lessons teach the Writer/Word program's operations, and Writing Personal and Official Letters (built in this same Cambodia merge, from the book's Chapter 2 Lesson 1) teaches those two letter kinds; the business letter is the book's own separate session (Chapter 2 Lesson 2 Session 2) and is built as its own lesson at the slug the placements file named (NEW LESSON NEEDED — word-processing/writing-a-business-letter). It sits in the Word Processing title (word-processing/).

Cambodia's own way: ICT is taught through Khmer-language office life — personal, official and business letters, job applications and Cornell note-taking — in OpenOffice Writer, with a dedicated session on typing Khmer script, and the book closes with STEM and e-learning rather than programming. The applications work in Cambodia's book is office correspondence first: recruitment, job applications and business letters in Khmer working life.


Kazakhstan's own way (Vyushkova/Zhurbenko/Orazbekov G7 «Информатика» (NIS student book, Russian edition)): the NIS Grade 7 book is organised around data: measuring and representing information (including the von Neumann architecture), business documents, spreadsheets that grow into databases (error checking, sorting/filtering, protecting data from change), raster graphics and logo design, a whole modelling section (modelling games, school life and social problems, forecasting from data), networks and information systems, and linear-structure programming that ends with building your own calculator.
