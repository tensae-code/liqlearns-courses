# Hands-on — Writing a Business Letter

The lesson with your hands, in a word processor where one is available.

## Lay out the frame
Open a blank page and build a business letter's frame first: sender's name and address, date, receiver's name and address, subject line, and labelled spaces for greeting, first paragraph, body, closing paragraph, formal closing, signature, name and position. Save the frame — a frame you trust makes every later letter faster.

## One real letter
Choose one real piece of business — order goods, ask a price, apply for work — and write the full letter in your frame. Keep the facts exact: names, amounts, dates. Keep it to one page if you can.

## The stranger test
Swap letters with a partner. Your partner reads yours as the receiving office: they write on the back — where they would file it, what it asks, and the next step and its date, all from the page alone. Anything they had to guess is what you rewrite.
