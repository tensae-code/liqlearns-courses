# Hands-on — Making a School Bulletin — and an Online Portfolio

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Launch the bulletin** — Produce and launch one bulletin issue for your school: masthead, sections, six short pieces — with the next issue's date printed in it.

## Task two
**Plant the portfolio** — Start your online portfolio: five pieces of your best work, captioned by the lesson's three questions, and the link shared with one real reader.
