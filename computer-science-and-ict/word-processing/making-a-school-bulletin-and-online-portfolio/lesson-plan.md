## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The bulletin — created and launched** — A **bulletin** is a publication of short, current pieces — notices, results, short reports, announcements — appearing regularly. The Grade 9 teams **create** theirs with the word-processing and design skills of the course: a name and a masthead, sections, short pieces edited tight (a bulletin is read standing up), and then the **launch** — the first issue put before its real readers with the next issue's promise attached. A launch is a commitment device: after it, the bulletin owes its school a rhythm, and keeping rhythm is the difference between a publication and an event.
2. **The online portfolio — your work, standing ready** — The **online portfolio** is the learner's own publication of one: a kept collection of their **best work** — documents, designs, programs, certificates — organised, captioned (what it is, when, what it proves) and shareable by a link. Its purposes are the working world's: the application, the interview, the client who asks 'show me'. The portfolio's discipline is selection — everything is not a portfolio; the best, honestly captioned, is — and maintenance: a portfolio is a garden, weeded and planted each term, or it becomes evidence of a learner who stopped.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
