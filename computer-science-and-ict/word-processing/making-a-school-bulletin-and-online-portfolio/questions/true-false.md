# Making a School Bulletin — and an Online Portfolio — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A regular publication of short, current pieces**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A regular publication of short, current pieces** — as the answer to: Name three bulletin contents.
**Answer: False — Notices, results and short reports (also announcements)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Very — a bulletin is read standing up**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Very — a bulletin is read standing up** — as the answer to: What is a launch?
**Answer: False — The first issue before real readers, with the next promise attached**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Rhythm — appearing on schedule**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Rhythm — appearing on schedule** — as the answer to: What is an online portfolio?
**Answer: False — A kept online collection of your best work, shareable by a link**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: What it is, when, and what it proves**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: What it is, when, and what it proves** — as the answer to: Name two purposes of a portfolio.
**Answer: False — Applications and interviews (also clients asking 'show me')**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Selection — the best, honestly captioned**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Selection — the best, honestly captioned** — as the answer to: What is a neglected portfolio evidence of?
**Answer: False — A learner who stopped**
