# Making a School Bulletin — and an Online Portfolio — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a bulletin?**
**Answer: A regular publication of short, current pieces**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three bulletin contents.**
**Answer: Notices, results and short reports (also announcements)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How tight is bulletin writing, and why?**
**Answer: Very — a bulletin is read standing up**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a launch?**
**Answer: The first issue before real readers, with the next promise attached**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What separates a publication from an event?**
**Answer: Rhythm — appearing on schedule**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is an online portfolio?**
**Answer: A kept online collection of your best work, shareable by a link**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What three things does a caption say?**
**Answer: What it is, when, and what it proves**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two purposes of a portfolio.**
**Answer: Applications and interviews (also clients asking 'show me')**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the portfolio's discipline?**
**Answer: Selection — the best, honestly captioned**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is a neglected portfolio evidence of?**
**Answer: A learner who stopped**
