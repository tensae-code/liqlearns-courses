# Hands-on — The Design Tab — Themes and Document Formatting

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One document, three themes
Take a one-page document and apply three different themes in turn. For each, note the colours and fonts that changed — without touching any text yourself.

## Match the piece
Style one heading by hand to match a theme's look, then apply the theme and compare: which route kept the whole document agreeing?
