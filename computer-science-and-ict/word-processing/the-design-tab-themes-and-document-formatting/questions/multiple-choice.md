# The Design Tab — Themes and Document Formatting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where is a document's overall look set?**
a) On the Design tab  b) A ready-made matching set of colours, fonts and effects  c) Headings, text and tables  d) The whole document changes with it
**Answer: a) On the Design tab**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a theme?**
a) The whole document, in one choice  b) A ready-made matching set of colours, fonts and effects  c) Colours and fonts  d) The page background and borders
**Answer: b) A ready-made matching set of colours, fonts and effects**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How much of the document does a theme apply to?**
a) Headings, text and tables  b) The whole document changes with it  c) The whole document, in one choice  d) It formats the whole document at once, not one piece at a time
**Answer: c) The whole document, in one choice**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What agrees with each other under a theme?**
a) Colours and fonts  b) The page background and borders  c) Tab by tab — Home, Insert, Design, Layout  d) Headings, text and tables
**Answer: d) Headings, text and tables**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name two things a theme sets.**
a) Colours and fonts  b) The whole document changes with it  c) It formats the whole document at once, not one piece at a time  d) Grade 8
**Answer: a) Colours and fonts**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when you change the theme?**
a) The page background and borders  b) The whole document changes with it  c) Tab by tab — Home, Insert, Design, Layout  d) On the Design tab
**Answer: b) The whole document changes with it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What page-level things are set from the Design tab?**
a) It formats the whole document at once, not one piece at a time  b) Grade 8  c) The page background and borders  d) A ready-made matching set of colours, fonts and effects
**Answer: c) The page background and borders**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is the Design tab different from the Home tab?**
a) Tab by tab — Home, Insert, Design, Layout  b) On the Design tab  c) The whole document, in one choice  d) It formats the whole document at once, not one piece at a time
**Answer: d) It formats the whole document at once, not one piece at a time**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In which order does Iraq's book teach the tabs?**
a) Tab by tab — Home, Insert, Design, Layout  b) Grade 8  c) A ready-made matching set of colours, fonts and effects  d) Headings, text and tables
**Answer: a) Tab by tab — Home, Insert, Design, Layout**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which grade's book teaches the Design tab?**
a) On the Design tab  b) Grade 8  c) The whole document, in one choice  d) Colours and fonts
**Answer: b) Grade 8**
