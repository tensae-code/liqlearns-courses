# The Design Tab — Themes and Document Formatting — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where is a document's overall look set?**
**Answer: On the Design tab**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a theme?**
**Answer: A ready-made matching set of colours, fonts and effects**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How much of the document does a theme apply to?**
**Answer: The whole document, in one choice**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What agrees with each other under a theme?**
**Answer: Headings, text and tables**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name two things a theme sets.**
**Answer: Colours and fonts**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when you change the theme?**
**Answer: The whole document changes with it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What page-level things are set from the Design tab?**
**Answer: The page background and borders**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is the Design tab different from the Home tab?**
**Answer: It formats the whole document at once, not one piece at a time**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In which order does Iraq's book teach the tabs?**
**Answer: Tab by tab — Home, Insert, Design, Layout**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which grade's book teaches the Design tab?**
**Answer: Grade 8**
