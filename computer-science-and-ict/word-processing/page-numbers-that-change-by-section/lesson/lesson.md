# Page numbers that change by section

## Do it first

Open any real book, research paper or project proposal. Before the main text, its first pages are often numbered i, ii, iii — and the cover carries no number at all. Then the body starts again at 1. One document, three numberings. Two tricks make it happen; this lesson teaches both.

## Sub lesson 1 — The two tricks and the breaks

The document has three parts: a **cover page with no page number**, **acknowledgements and contents in Roman numerals (i, ii, iii)**, and the **main body in Arabic from 1**. The two tricks are stated first: a **Section Break – Next Page must stand between each pair of sections**, and **each section's footer must be unlinked from the one before**. Begin: turn on **Show/Hide** so the breaks are visible; put the **cursor at the end of a section** and insert the break from **Page Layout → Breaks → Next Page**.

[Picture 13333 — one document in three bands]

## Sub lesson 2 — Unlinking the footers

Go to the **next section's footer**. On the **Header & Footer Tools Design tab**, the **Link to Previous** button stands highlighted — **click it to unlink** this footer from the section before. **Repeat for the third section**: break first, then unlink. When every section stands alone, turn **Show/Hide off**.

[GIF 13334 — the footer unlinked, the numbering changing]

## Sub lesson 3 — Numbering each section

Now **insert each section's numbers**, using **Page Number → Format Page Numbers** to choose the **format and starting number** for each: the cover takes **no number**; the front pages take **Roman numerals**; the body takes **Arabic, starting at 1**. Because each footer was unlinked, each section keeps its own system.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the document's three parts and their numberings, the two tricks, and the order of the whole procedure.

## Do It Yourself

1. Build a three-section document and number it: cover bare, contents ii-iii, body from 1.
2. Say the two tricks from memory before you start.
3. Show a partner the two section breaks with Show/Hide on.
