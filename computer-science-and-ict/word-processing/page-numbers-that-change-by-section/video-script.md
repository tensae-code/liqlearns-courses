# Video script — Page numbers that change by section

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One document. Three numberings.
Cover: no number. Front pages: i, ii, iii.
Body: 1, 2, 3 — starting again.
Trick one: a section break between the parts.
Page Layout. Breaks. Next Page.
Trick two: unlink the footer.
Link to Previous stands lit. Click it. Unlinked.
Again for the third part: break, then unlink.
Now number each part its own way.
Format Page Numbers: Roman here, Arabic from 1 there.
Three systems. One document.
