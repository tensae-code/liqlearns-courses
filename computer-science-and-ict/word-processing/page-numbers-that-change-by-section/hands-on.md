# Hands-on — Page numbers that change by section

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## A real front matter

Take a report you have written. Give it a cover, a contents page, and its body as three sections: no number, Roman, Arabic from 1. Verify each page's number against the plan.

## Break audit

In pairs: one builds the three-section document; the other audits it with Show/Hide on — two breaks found, three footers checked for linking, three numberings read aloud.
