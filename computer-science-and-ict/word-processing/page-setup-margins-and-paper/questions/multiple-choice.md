# Page Setup — Margins and Paper — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are margins?**
a) The blank spaces between the text and the four edges of the paper  b) Top, bottom, left and right  c) Leave a bigger blank border and fit fewer words on the page  d) The text crowds the edge and the printer can cut it off
**Answer: a) The blank spaces between the text and the four edges of the paper**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the four margins called?**
a) Leave a bigger blank border and fit fewer words on the page  b) Top, bottom, left and right  c) The text crowds the edge and the printer can cut it off  d) So the whole work looks even, page after page
**Answer: b) Top, bottom, left and right**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do wider margins do to a page?**
a) The text crowds the edge and the printer can cut it off  b) So the whole work looks even, page after page  c) Leave a bigger blank border and fit fewer words on the page  d) A4
**Answer: c) Leave a bigger blank border and fit fewer words on the page**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the risk of very narrow margins?**
a) So the whole work looks even, page after page  b) A4  c) The paper standing tall — for letters and essays  d) The text crowds the edge and the printer can cut it off
**Answer: d) The text crowds the edge and the printer can cut it off**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why keep the same margins through a document?**
a) So the whole work looks even, page after page  b) A4  c) The paper standing tall — for letters and essays  d) The paper lying wide — for wide tables and pictures
**Answer: a) So the whole work looks even, page after page**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What paper size is the school standard?**
a) The paper standing tall — for letters and essays  b) A4  c) The paper lying wide — for wide tables and pictures  d) The real paper in the printer
**Answer: b) A4**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is portrait direction?**
a) The paper lying wide — for wide tables and pictures  b) The real paper in the printer  c) The paper standing tall — for letters and essays  d) Before finishing the document — and check print preview before printing
**Answer: c) The paper standing tall — for letters and essays**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is landscape direction?**
a) The real paper in the printer  b) Before finishing the document — and check print preview before printing  c) The blank spaces between the text and the four edges of the paper  d) The paper lying wide — for wide tables and pictures
**Answer: d) The paper lying wide — for wide tables and pictures**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must the page setup match?**
a) The real paper in the printer  b) Before finishing the document — and check print preview before printing  c) The blank spaces between the text and the four edges of the paper  d) Top, bottom, left and right
**Answer: a) The real paper in the printer**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When should you set the page up, and what do you check before printing?**
a) The blank spaces between the text and the four edges of the paper  b) Before finishing the document — and check print preview before printing  c) Top, bottom, left and right  d) Leave a bigger blank border and fit fewer words on the page
**Answer: b) Before finishing the document — and check print preview before printing**
