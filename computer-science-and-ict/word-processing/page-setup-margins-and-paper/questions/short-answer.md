# Page Setup — Margins and Paper — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are margins?**
**Answer: The blank spaces between the text and the four edges of the paper**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the four margins called?**
**Answer: Top, bottom, left and right**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do wider margins do to a page?**
**Answer: Leave a bigger blank border and fit fewer words on the page**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the risk of very narrow margins?**
**Answer: The text crowds the edge and the printer can cut it off**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why keep the same margins through a document?**
**Answer: So the whole work looks even, page after page**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What paper size is the school standard?**
**Answer: A4**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is portrait direction?**
**Answer: The paper standing tall — for letters and essays**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is landscape direction?**
**Answer: The paper lying wide — for wide tables and pictures**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must the page setup match?**
**Answer: The real paper in the printer**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When should you set the page up, and what do you check before printing?**
**Answer: Before finishing the document — and check print preview before printing**
