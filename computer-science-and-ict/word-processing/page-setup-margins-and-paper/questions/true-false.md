# Page Setup — Margins and Paper — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are margins?" is: The blank spaces between the text and the four edges of the paper**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are the four margins called?" is: Leave a bigger blank border and fit fewer words on the page**
**Answer: False** — Top, bottom, left and right

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do wider margins do to a page?" is: Leave a bigger blank border and fit fewer words on the page**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the risk of very narrow margins?" is: So the whole work looks even, page after page**
**Answer: False** — The text crowds the edge and the printer can cut it off

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why keep the same margins through a document?" is: So the whole work looks even, page after page**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What paper size is the school standard?" is: The paper standing tall — for letters and essays**
**Answer: False** — A4

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is portrait direction?" is: The paper standing tall — for letters and essays**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is landscape direction?" is: The real paper in the printer**
**Answer: False** — The paper lying wide — for wide tables and pictures

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What must the page setup match?" is: The real paper in the printer**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When should you set the page up, and what do you check before printing?" is: The blank spaces between the text and the four edges of the paper**
**Answer: False** — Before finishing the document — and check print preview before printing
