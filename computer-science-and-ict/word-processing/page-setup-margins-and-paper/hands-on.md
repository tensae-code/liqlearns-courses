# Hands-on — Page Setup — Margins and Paper

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Measure a margin
Print or open a finished page and measure its four margins with a ruler. Write the four numbers and say which margin is widest.

## Set up a page
In a word processor, set A4 paper, portrait direction, and margins you choose. Then turn the same page to landscape and say what changed and which direction suits a wide table better.
