# Video script — Adding page numbers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Do not type page numbers. Insert them.
Insert. Page Number.
Top? Bottom? Margins? Choose a style.
Numbers appear — every page at once.
Esc locks them in.

---

(Grade 10 depth.)

Now the finishing touches. Open Eagle again.
Page numbers on — with a header and a footer.
But the first page is a cover. Hide its number.
Page one: no number. The rest: numbered.
Formats are many, and numbering can restart where a document changes.
Save. That is a finished document.
