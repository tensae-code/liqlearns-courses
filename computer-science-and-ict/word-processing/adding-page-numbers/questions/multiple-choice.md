# Adding page numbers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Page numbers are inserted from —
a) Home → Number  b) Insert → Page Number  c) View → Number  d) the ruler
**Answer: b) Insert → Page Number.**

**2.** The menu offers Top of Page, Bottom of Page and —
a) Middle of Page  b) Page Margins  c) Inside the Text  d) The Title Bar
**Answer: b) Page Margins.**

**3.** The book's example chooses —
a) Top of Page  b) Page Margins  c) Bottom of Page  d) no style
**Answer: c) Bottom of Page.**

**4.** After choosing a style, the numbering —
a) appears on one page  b) appears on every page  c) must be typed  d) prints only
**Answer: b) appears on every page.**

**5.** The header/footer is locked by pressing —
a) Enter  b) Esc  c) Tab  d) Ctrl+N
**Answer: b) Esc.**

**6.** A locked header/footer is unlocked by —
a) restarting Word  b) double-clicking it  c) deleting it  d) saving
**Answer: b) double-clicking it.**
