# Adding page numbers — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner inserts page numbers in a chosen place and style, locks them with Esc, and unlocks them by double-clicking. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners of the same skill — more examples to sort, longer sequences to put in order, trickier cases of the same idea — never by a new topic.

## Grade 10 depth — formats, restarting, and the first page
Added at Grade 10 (no sub lessons — this lesson stays one whole lesson): the learner works the Eagle practical — reopen the file, add page numbers with header and footer, hide the number on the first page, save — and states that numbers come in many formats and can be restarted where pages number differently.
