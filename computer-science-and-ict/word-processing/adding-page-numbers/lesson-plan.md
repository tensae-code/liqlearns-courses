# Lesson plan — Adding page numbers

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Word Processing · **Lesson:** Adding page numbers
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 5 — Word Processing in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 9 book, Unit 3 — Application Software. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** page numbers are inserted once — Insert → Page Number, then a place (Top of Page, Bottom of Page, Page Margins) and a style — and they appear on every page, locked in with Esc.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the page-break lessons — breaks control where pages start; numbers name them, and they live in the header and footer this title teaches next.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Removing a page break.  Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Insert page numbers by Insert → Page Number, choosing place and style.
2. Name the three places: Top of Page, Bottom of Page, Page Margins.
3. Lock the header/footer with Esc and unlock it by double-clicking.

## What you need
A computer with Word and a document of several pages.

## If the learner is stuck
- Numbers on one page only → page numbers are inserted once for the whole document; if only one page shows them, re-insert from Insert → Page Number.
- Cannot edit the numbers afterwards → double-click the header/footer to unlock it; Esc locks it again.
