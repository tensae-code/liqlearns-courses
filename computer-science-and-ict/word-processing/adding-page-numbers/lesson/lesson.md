# Adding page numbers

## Do it first

Take a three-page document with no numbers. Type a 1 at the foot of page one, a 2 on page two, a 3 on page three. Now insert a paragraph on page one — your hand-typed numbers are wrong or adrift. Real page numbers are not typed. This lesson inserts them the way that survives editing.

## Inserting, in the book's order

Page numbers **identify the pages**; they are usually placed in the **header, footer or side margins**, come in **many formats**, and numbering can be **restarted** where pages must be numbered differently. In the book's order: 1. Make a **multi-page document**. 2. **Insert → Page Number**. 3. Open **Top of Page, Bottom of Page or Page Margins** and **choose a style** — the example chooses **Bottom of Page**. 4. The numbering **appears** — on every page at once. 5. Press **Esc** to lock the header/footer; **double-click it later** to unlock and edit.
