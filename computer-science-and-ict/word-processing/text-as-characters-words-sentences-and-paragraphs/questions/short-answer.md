# Text as Characters, Words, Sentences and Paragraphs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the smallest piece of text?**
**Answer: The character — one letter, digit, sign or space**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What joins to make a word?**
**Answer: Characters — the runs of characters between spaces**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does the computer hold text?**
**Answer: As a counted line of characters it can copy, search, count and change**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a sentence?**
**Answer: A complete statement closed by its ending marks**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a paragraph?**
**Answer: A block of text carrying one stretch of thought, separated by new lines**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four levels of text in order.**
**Answer: Character, word, sentence, paragraph**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which level does character formatting act on?**
**Answer: Characters**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which level does alignment act on?**
**Answer: Paragraphs**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why learn the levels before the tools?**
**Answer: So you know what a tool will change before you touch it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which language's text does the book work in?**
**Answer: Mongolian, in Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book**
