# Text as Characters, Words, Sentences and Paragraphs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the smallest piece of text?**
a) The character — one letter, digit, sign or space  b) Characters — the runs of characters between spaces  c) As a counted line of characters it can copy, search, count and change  d) A complete statement closed by its ending marks
**Answer: a) The character — one letter, digit, sign or space**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What joins to make a word?**
a) As a counted line of characters it can copy, search, count and change  b) Characters — the runs of characters between spaces  c) A complete statement closed by its ending marks  d) A block of text carrying one stretch of thought, separated by new lines
**Answer: b) Characters — the runs of characters between spaces**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does the computer hold text?**
a) A complete statement closed by its ending marks  b) A block of text carrying one stretch of thought, separated by new lines  c) As a counted line of characters it can copy, search, count and change  d) Character, word, sentence, paragraph
**Answer: c) As a counted line of characters it can copy, search, count and change**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a sentence?**
a) A block of text carrying one stretch of thought, separated by new lines  b) Character, word, sentence, paragraph  c) Characters  d) A complete statement closed by its ending marks
**Answer: d) A complete statement closed by its ending marks**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a paragraph?**
a) A block of text carrying one stretch of thought, separated by new lines  b) Character, word, sentence, paragraph  c) Characters  d) Paragraphs
**Answer: a) A block of text carrying one stretch of thought, separated by new lines**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four levels of text in order.**
a) Characters  b) Character, word, sentence, paragraph  c) Paragraphs  d) So you know what a tool will change before you touch it
