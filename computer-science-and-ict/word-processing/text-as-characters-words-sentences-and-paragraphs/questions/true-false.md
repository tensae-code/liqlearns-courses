# Text as Characters, Words, Sentences and Paragraphs — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the smallest piece of text?" is: The character — one letter, digit, sign or space**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What joins to make a word?" is: As a counted line of characters it can copy, search, count and change**
**Answer: False** — Characters — the runs of characters between spaces

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How does the computer hold text?" is: As a counted line of characters it can copy, search, count and change**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a sentence?" is: A block of text carrying one stretch of thought, separated by new lines**
**Answer: False** — A complete statement closed by its ending marks

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a paragraph?" is: A block of text carrying one stretch of thought, separated by new lines**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name the four levels of text in order." is: Characters**
**Answer: False** — Character, word, sentence, paragraph

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which level does character formatting act on?" is: Characters**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which level does alignment act on?" is: So you know what a tool will change before you touch it**
**Answer: False** — Paragraphs

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why learn the levels before the tools?" is: So you know what a tool will change before you touch it**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which language's text does the book work in?" is: The character — one letter, digit, sign or space**
**Answer: False** — Mongolian, in Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book
