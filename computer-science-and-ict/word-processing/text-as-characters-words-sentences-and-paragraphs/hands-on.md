# Hands-on — Text as Characters, Words, Sentences and Paragraphs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Count the levels', 'Take one paragraph of your textbook. Count its sentences, words and characters. Say which count was hardest, and why.')

## Task two
('Work at one level', 'In a word processor, change one character, then one word, then one paragraph. After each, say which level you worked at and what changed.')
