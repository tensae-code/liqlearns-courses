# The Insert Menu — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does everything on the Insert menu enter?" is: At the insertion point**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What keeps page numbers right as a document grows?" is: Today's date, typed perfectly by the computer**
**Answer: False** — Word itself — Page Numbers are inserted, not typed

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does Date and Time insert?" is: Today's date, typed perfectly by the computer**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is Symbol for?" is: Ends the page where you are and starts a new one**
**Answer: False** — The characters the keyboard does not have

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does Break do?" is: Ends the page where you are and starts a new one**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why is a break better than empty Enter presses?" is: The Clip Art collection or a file**
**Answer: False** — Empty Enters shift as you edit; a break holds the new page's start

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What two sources can Picture insert from?" is: The Clip Art collection or a file**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What other whole things can Insert bring in?" is: Typing is for words you compose; inserting is for things that already exist, made correctly elsewhere**
**Answer: False** — A text box, and another document's text as a file

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the one idea behind the Insert menu?" is: Typing is for words you compose; inserting is for things that already exist, made correctly elsewhere**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "In which grades do Afghanistan's books teach this menu?" is: At the insertion point**
**Answer: False** — Grade 10 and again in Grade 11
