# The Insert Menu — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does everything on the Insert menu enter?**
**Answer: At the insertion point**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What keeps page numbers right as a document grows?**
**Answer: Word itself — Page Numbers are inserted, not typed**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does Date and Time insert?**
**Answer: Today's date, typed perfectly by the computer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is Symbol for?**
**Answer: The characters the keyboard does not have**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does Break do?**
**Answer: Ends the page where you are and starts a new one**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a break better than empty Enter presses?**
**Answer: Empty Enters shift as you edit; a break holds the new page's start**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What two sources can Picture insert from?**
**Answer: The Clip Art collection or a file**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What other whole things can Insert bring in?**
**Answer: A text box, and another document's text as a file**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the one idea behind the Insert menu?**
**Answer: Typing is for words you compose; inserting is for things that already exist, made correctly elsewhere**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) In which grades do Afghanistan's books teach this menu?**
**Answer: Grade 10 and again in Grade 11**
