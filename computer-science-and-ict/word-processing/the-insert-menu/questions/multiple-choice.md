# The Insert Menu — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does everything on the Insert menu enter?**
a) At the insertion point  b) Word itself — Page Numbers are inserted, not typed  c) Today's date, typed perfectly by the computer  d) The characters the keyboard does not have
**Answer: a) At the insertion point**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What keeps page numbers right as a document grows?**
a) Today's date, typed perfectly by the computer  b) Word itself — Page Numbers are inserted, not typed  c) The characters the keyboard does not have  d) Ends the page where you are and starts a new one
**Answer: b) Word itself — Page Numbers are inserted, not typed**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does Date and Time insert?**
a) The characters the keyboard does not have  b) Ends the page where you are and starts a new one  c) Today's date, typed perfectly by the computer  d) Empty Enters shift as you edit; a break holds the new page's start
**Answer: c) Today's date, typed perfectly by the computer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is Symbol for?**
a) Ends the page where you are and starts a new one  b) Empty Enters shift as you edit; a break holds the new page's start  c) The Clip Art collection or a file  d) The characters the keyboard does not have
**Answer: d) The characters the keyboard does not have**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does Break do?**
a) Ends the page where you are and starts a new one  b) Empty Enters shift as you edit; a break holds the new page's start  c) The Clip Art collection or a file  d) A text box, and another document's text as a file
**Answer: a) Ends the page where you are and starts a new one**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a break better than empty Enter presses?**
a) The Clip Art collection or a file  b) Empty Enters shift as you edit; a break holds the new page's start  c) A text box, and another document's text as a file  d) Typing is for words you compose; inserting is for things that already exist, made correctly elsewhere
**Answer: b) Empty Enters shift as you edit; a break holds the new page's start**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What two sources can Picture insert from?**
a) A text box, and another document's text as a file  b) Typing is for words you compose; inserting is for things that already exist, made correctly elsewhere  c) The Clip Art collection or a file  d) Grade 10 and again in Grade 11
**Answer: c) The Clip Art collection or a file**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What other whole things can Insert bring in?**
a) Typing is for words you compose; inserting is for things that already exist, made correctly elsewhere  b) Grade 10 and again in Grade 11  c) At the insertion point  d) A text box, and another document's text as a file
**Answer: d) A text box, and another document's text as a file**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the one idea behind the Insert menu?**
a) Typing is for words you compose; inserting is for things that already exist, made correctly elsewhere  b) Grade 10 and again in Grade 11  c) At the insertion point  d) Word itself — Page Numbers are inserted, not typed
**Answer: a) Typing is for words you compose; inserting is for things that already exist, made correctly elsewhere**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) In which grades do Afghanistan's books teach this menu?**
a) At the insertion point  b) Grade 10 and again in Grade 11  c) Word itself — Page Numbers are inserted, not typed  d) Today's date, typed perfectly by the computer
**Answer: b) Grade 10 and again in Grade 11**
