# Hands-on — The Insert Menu

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Insert, don't type
Make a two-page document by inserting a page break (no empty Enters). Add page numbers from the Insert menu, insert today's date at the top, and insert one picture. For each, say why inserting beat typing it.

## Break it properly
Type a heading at the bottom of a page. First push it to the next page with empty Enters; then undo that and use a page break instead. Add a paragraph above and watch which method keeps the heading at the page top.
