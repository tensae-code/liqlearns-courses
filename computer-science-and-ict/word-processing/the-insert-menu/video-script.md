# Video script — The Insert Menu

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Everything on the **Insert menu** enters the document at the **insertion point**, whole: you choose the thing, and Word places it for you, correctly made.
**Page Numbers** numbers the pages — Word keeps the numbers right even as the document grows.
**Date and Time** inserts today's date, typed perfectly by the computer.
**Symbol** gives the characters the keyboard does not have.
**Break** ends the page where you are and starts a new one, so a chapter can begin at a page top without a row of empty Enter presses that will shift as you edit.
**Picture** inserts an image — from the Clip Art collection or from a file — where the insertion point stands; the picture then lives in the document, moving with the text around it.
The menu also inserts things like a **text box** and whole **files** — another document's text brought into this one.
The thinking behind the menu is one idea: typing is for words you compose; inserting is for things that already exist, made correctly elsewhere — numbers, dates, symbols, pictures.
Both Afghanistan books teach this menu as its own lesson, in Grade 10 and again in Grade 11, because a document that only holds typed text is only half a document.
