# Hands-on — Writing in WordPad

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Write a real note
In WordPad, write a short real note — to a teacher, a shop, or your family. Make the heading bigger and bold, save the note with a proper name, close WordPad, and open your note again to prove it was kept.

## Simple or Word?
List five writing jobs. For each, say whether WordPad can finish it or it needs Word, and name the missing thing — spell-checker, tables, page numbers — when WordPad falls short.
