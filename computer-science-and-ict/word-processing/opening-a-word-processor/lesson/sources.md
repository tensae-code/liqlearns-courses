Mauritania's own way (Grade 7): Informatics is taught for the first time in 1AS as thirty 30-minute teacher fiches moving hardware → information systems (illustrated with Mauritanian systems Bankily, Masrvi, Houwiyeti and the Ministry's Sirage platform) → a long Microsoft Word sequence → a full unit on internet ethics before browsers and search are even introduced.

Saudi Arabia's own way (Grade 4, Digital Skills): the primary Digital Skills course (Binary Logic) is built as three units per term with the same guide pattern as the intermediate books (outcomes, important points, steps, answers, unit project, "Test Yourself"), files on the Ain platform, and a strong Arabic/English bilingual-typing strand — pupils switch the keyboard between Arabic and English with Alt+Shift and learn text direction, hamza and Arabic punctuation on the keyboard alongside Word skills; Scratch is introduced in Term 1 through algorithms from daily life. This citation is PARTIAL — Term 1 only. (teacher guide — evidence)

Iraq's own way (Grade 8): the same four-unit spiral one year on — the hardware unit becomes “the computer platform” and opens with choosing a computer's specifications before buying; Word is taught tab by tab (Home, Insert, Design, Layout); the algorithms unit advances to selection statements and logic truth tables, still landing in Scratch; the internet unit ends with writing a report from web research.

Cuba's own way (Grade 2): Play-based (lúdico) introduction through the graficador (Paint-type drawing program) and educational software; correct workstation posture/ergonomics is taught explicitly in Grade 2, and files/folders are introduced intuitively through saving and opening drawings in a class-group folder. (teacher guide — evidence)

Cuba's own way (Grade 5): The Grade 5 programme is built around extracting information from Cuban educational software, encyclopedias and EcuRed into word-processor documents, then Scratch — drawing/animation first (Unit 3), control/movement/appearance blocks in Unit 4. (teacher programme — evidence)

Bhutan's own way (Class V, Literacy with ICT student book): Word is taught together with typing in Dzongkha, the national language (Dzongkha keyboard software and fonts); communication is taught on the school LAN with Qualia LAN Messenger (chat, file sharing and broadcast with no Internet connection), and the end-of-year folder is sent to the teacher as a LAN-messenger attachment.

Laos's own way (Grade 9 ICT student book): the first fully practical year — a screenshot-led Windows XP, Word and Excel course where every numbered topic is a click-by-click procedure (the Excel half even teaches the Chart Wizard and worksheet Page Setup with its Orientation/Paper Size/Page Numbering table in English). Windows XP-era office teaching runs through the middle years.

Jordan's own way (Jordan Grade 3 G3 Workbook): The Office ladder continues — Word carries the early blocks, PowerPoint the middle blocks (e.g. multiplication facts), and Excel appears in the geometry-and-measurement block, with Save As taught as an explicit step.

Jordan's own way (Jordan Grade 3 G3 Teacher): Guide plans mirror the workbook's Office ladder — Word cards and writing first (Block 1 product: an ID card for a living thing with a drawing of its life cycle), then PowerPoint, then Excel.

Jordan's own way (Jordan Grade 6 G6 Workbook): Grade 6 adds real Windows skills inside the activity blocks — creating a blank Word document, capturing the active window with Alt+Print Screen into the clipboard and Paint, and pixel art built in Excel with controlled dimensions.

Jordan's own way (Jordan Grade 6 G6 Teacher): Guide plans mirror the workbook blocks with learning products, digital tools and assessment appendices, as in the other early-grades guides.

Vietnam's own way (Grade 4 Tin học 4): Grade 4 is where the visual programming language first appears (its preface announces it), software legality is taught as a child's rule — only use software when you have permission — and the applied theme offers alternative Bài 12A (multimedia tools) or 12B (typing-trainer software).
