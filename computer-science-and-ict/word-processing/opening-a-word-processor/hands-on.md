# Hands-on — Opening a word processor

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Three ways race
With a partner and one computer each (or taking turns on one): open Word by route 1, close it, open by route 2, close it, open by route 3. Your partner checks each route against the book's order and ticks it off.

## Teach the route
Teach route 1 to someone who has never opened Word, saying each click before they make it. Teaching the order fixes it in your own memory.

## If it is hard
While you practise, a teacher or partner watches the doing itself — opening is assessed by observation, the way the teacher guide directs for the whole Word practical. If a click goes wrong, go back one step, not to the beginning.

## Name watcher

Open the word processor and read the new document's default name aloud. Open a second new document without saving the first, and read its name. Write both names down and the rule you see: DOCUMENT, then the number rises.
