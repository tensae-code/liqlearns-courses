# Video script — Opening a word processor

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three ways to open Word.

Way one. Start. All Programs. Microsoft Office. Microsoft Word 2016.
Way two. Search. Type: word. Choose Microsoft Word 2016.
Way three. Double-click the Word shortcut on the desktop.

Every way ends the same.
A blank document. Ready for your words.

One more thing. Click — and a blank window stands open.
That blank window is your new document.
Its first name is not yours yet: DOCUMENT, and a number.
Save it, and it can open again — even on another computer.

Newer Word opens two ways.
Way one: Start. The Word tile. Blank document.
Way two: Start. Type: Word. Click the icon.
Both end at the same blank page.
