# Indent with the Increase/Decrease buttons

## Do it first

Select a whole paragraph in Word. On the Home tab, find Increase Indent and click it. Click again. Again. The paragraph is walking in, step by equal step. This lesson names the step's size and the way back.

## The clicks and their measure

For several lines or a whole paragraph: **select the text, then Home → Increase Indent or Decrease Indent**. Each click adjusts the indent by **half an inch** — in with Increase, back out with Decrease, click by click, always the same step.

## Custom amounts, and the definitions

For an exact amount, the **Layout tab's Indent boxes** take the number you type. The definitions are pinned down: **left indention** is the distance from the **left margin**; **right indention** is the distance from the **right margin** — increase or decrease either, by the boxes.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the button method, its measure per click, and where custom amounts are set.

## Do It Yourself

1. Step a paragraph in three clicks, then back out three clicks.
2. Say the size of one click's step, from memory.
3. Set a custom left indent on the Layout tab and say which margin it is measured from.
