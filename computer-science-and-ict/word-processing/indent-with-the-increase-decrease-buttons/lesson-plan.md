# Lesson plan — Indent with the Increase/Decrease buttons

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Word Processing · **Lesson:** Indent with the Increase/Decrease buttons
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 5 — Word Processing in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 9 book, Unit 3 — Application Software. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** select the text and click Increase Indent or Decrease Indent on the Home tab — each click moves the paragraph by half an inch; custom amounts are set on the Layout tab.
**This lesson's own level:** it is the third of the four indentation methods — the button method, for several lines or a whole paragraph at once.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Indent with the Ruler.  Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Indent selected text with Increase Indent and back out with Decrease Indent.
2. Say each click's measure: half an inch.
3. Set a custom indent amount in the Layout tab's Indent boxes, and define left and right indention.

## What you need
A computer with Word and a document of several paragraphs.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner selects a paragraph and clicks Increase Indent three times before the half-inch rule is stated (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the parts below, in order (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- One line moved instead of the paragraph → select the text first; the buttons work on the selection.
- Overshot the indent → Decrease Indent steps back out by the same half inch per click.
