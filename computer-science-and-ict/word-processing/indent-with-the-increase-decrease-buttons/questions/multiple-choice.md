# Indent with the Increase/Decrease buttons — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Each click of Increase Indent adjusts the indent by —
a) one inch  b) half an inch  c) two inches  d) a random amount
**Answer: b) half an inch.**

**2.** The Indent commands sit on the —
a) View tab  b) Insert tab  c) Home tab  d) File tab
**Answer: c) Home tab.**

**3.** Custom indent amounts are typed on the —
a) Layout tab, in the Indent boxes  b) status bar  c) ruler only  d) title bar
**Answer: a) Layout tab, in the Indent boxes.**

**4.** Left indention is the distance from —
a) the top margin  b) the left margin  c) the next paragraph  d) the page number
**Answer: b) the left margin.**

**5.** To step a paragraph back out you click —
a) Increase Indent  b) Decrease Indent  c) Sort  d) Shading
**Answer: b) Decrease Indent.**

**6.** Before clicking the Indent commands you must —
a) print  b) save  c) select the text  d) close the ruler
**Answer: c) select the text.**
