# Hands-on — Indent with the Increase/Decrease buttons

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Count the inches

Indent a paragraph with clicks only, counting aloud: half, one, one and a half. Then measure with the ruler showing and check your count against it.

## Exact by box

Set a paragraph's left indent to an exact amount in the Layout tab's Indent boxes. Then reach the same place from the margin using clicks only, and compare.
