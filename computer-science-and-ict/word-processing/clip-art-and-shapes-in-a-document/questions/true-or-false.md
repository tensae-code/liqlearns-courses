# Clip art and shapes in a document — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Online Pictures results can be filtered to clip art only.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Lock Drawing Mode is cancelled by Esc or a right-click on blank space.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Shift makes a shape bigger.
**Answer: False** — Shift keeps it perfect — a true circle or square.
