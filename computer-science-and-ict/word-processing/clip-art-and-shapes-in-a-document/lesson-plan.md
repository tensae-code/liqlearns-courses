# Lesson plan — Clip art and shapes in a document

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Word Processing · **Lesson:** Clip art and shapes in a document
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Application Software, Grade 10 depth, in the Word Processing title of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md). The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** clip art is found and inserted ready-made, and shapes are drag-drawn in the document — with Shift held for a perfect shape, and Lock Drawing Mode for drawing the same shape again and again.
**This lesson's own level:** it is a doing-lesson. It stands on Inserting pictures and screenshots; this one adds found imagery and drawn shapes.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## If the learner is stuck
- The shape comes out stretched → hold Shift while dragging for the perfect version.
- The same shape keeps drawing when you are done → press Esc, or right-click on blank space, to leave Lock Drawing Mode.
