# Video script — Clip art and shapes in a document

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Need balloons? Flowers? You do not draw those. Clip art is ready-made.
Insert. Clip Art. Search. Double-click. Done.
Or Online Pictures, filtered to clip art.
Shapes are yours to draw. Insert. Shapes. Pick one. Drag it to size.
Hold Shift — perfect! A true circle.
Many the same? Right-click the shape: Lock Drawing Mode.
Draw, draw, draw. Esc stops it.
