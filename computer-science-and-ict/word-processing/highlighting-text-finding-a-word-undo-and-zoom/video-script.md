# Video script — Highlighting Text, Finding a Word, Undo and Zoom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Highlighting** paints a colour behind selected text so it stands out — a mark for the reader, not a change to the words. Where the keyboard has no key, **symbols are inserted** from the program's symbol list. **Find** searches the whole document for a **word** and takes you to it — no reading page by page to hunt one word.
The **Undo button** takes back the **last action** — a deletion, a format, a paste — restoring what was there before; it is the safe net under all editing. The **zoom slider** changes the **size of the page on screen** — slide up to read small text closely, down to see the whole page — while the document itself, and its print, stay exactly as they were.
