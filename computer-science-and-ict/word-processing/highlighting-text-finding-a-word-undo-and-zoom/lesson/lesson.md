# Highlighting Text, Finding a Word, Undo and Zoom

## Do it first

A long document, a word to find, a mistake to take back, a page too small to read. Four tools solve all four problems. (teacher guide — evidence; PARTIAL — Term 1 only)

## Sub lesson 1 — Highlight, insert a symbol, find a word

**Highlighting** paints a colour behind selected text so it stands out — a mark for the reader, not a change to the words. Where the keyboard has no key, **symbols are inserted** from the program's symbol list. **Find** searches the whole document for a **word** and takes you to it — no reading page by page to hunt one word.

## Sub lesson 2 — Undo — and the zoom slider

The **Undo button** takes back the **last action** — a deletion, a format, a paste — restoring what was there before; it is the safe net under all editing. The **zoom slider** changes the **size of the page on screen** — slide up to read small text closely, down to see the whole page — while the document itself, and its print, stay exactly as they were.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: Four everyday tools in one lesson: highlight marks text with colour, symbols can be inserted where the keyboard has no key for them, Find locates any word in the document, Undo takes back the last action, and the zoom slider changes how big the page looks without changing the document. Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.
