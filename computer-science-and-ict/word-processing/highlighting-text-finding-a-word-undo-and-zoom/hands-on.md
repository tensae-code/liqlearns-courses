# Hands-on — Highlighting Text, Finding a Word, Undo and Zoom

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find and mark
In a long document, use Find to locate a named word, then highlight its sentence. Say which tool found and which marked.

## Undo and zoom
Delete a word, undo the deletion, then slide the zoom up to read closely and down to see the whole page. Check the printed page size never changed.
