# Highlighting Text, Finding a Word, Undo and Zoom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does highlighting do?**
a) Paints a colour behind selected text so it stands out  b) Searches the whole document for a word and takes you to it  c) The last action  d) The safe net under all editing
**Answer: a) Paints a colour behind selected text so it stands out**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Is highlighting a change to the words?**
a) Reading page by page to hunt one word  b) No — a mark for the reader  c) A deletion, a format, a paste  d) How big the page looks on screen
**Answer: b) No — a mark for the reader**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do symbols come from when the keyboard has no key?**
a) The last action  b) The safe net under all editing  c) The program's symbol list, by inserting  d) No — both stay exactly as they were
**Answer: c) The program's symbol list, by inserting**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does Find do?**
a) A deletion, a format, a paste  b) How big the page looks on screen  c) Paints a colour behind selected text so it stands out  d) Searches the whole document for a word and takes you to it
**Answer: d) Searches the whole document for a word and takes you to it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does Find save you from?**
a) Reading page by page to hunt one word  b) The safe net under all editing  c) No — both stay exactly as they were  d) No — a mark for the reader
**Answer: a) Reading page by page to hunt one word**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Undo take back?**
a) How big the page looks on screen  b) The last action  c) Paints a colour behind selected text so it stands out  d) The program's symbol list, by inserting
**Answer: b) The last action**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name three actions Undo can take back.**
a) No — both stay exactly as they were  b) No — a mark for the reader  c) A deletion, a format, a paste  d) Searches the whole document for a word and takes you to it
**Answer: c) A deletion, a format, a paste**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is Undo called in this lesson?**
a) Paints a colour behind selected text so it stands out  b) The program's symbol list, by inserting  c) Reading page by page to hunt one word  d) The safe net under all editing
**Answer: d) The safe net under all editing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the zoom slider change?**
a) How big the page looks on screen  b) No — a mark for the reader  c) Searches the whole document for a word and takes you to it  d) The last action
**Answer: a) How big the page looks on screen**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Does zoom change the document or its print?**
a) The program's symbol list, by inserting  b) No — both stay exactly as they were  c) Reading page by page to hunt one word  d) A deletion, a format, a paste
**Answer: b) No — both stay exactly as they were**
