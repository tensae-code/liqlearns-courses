# Highlighting Text, Finding a Word, Undo and Zoom — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does highlighting do?**
**Answer: Paints a colour behind selected text so it stands out**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Is highlighting a change to the words?**
**Answer: No — a mark for the reader**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do symbols come from when the keyboard has no key?**
**Answer: The program's symbol list, by inserting**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does Find do?**
**Answer: Searches the whole document for a word and takes you to it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does Find save you from?**
**Answer: Reading page by page to hunt one word**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Undo take back?**
**Answer: The last action**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name three actions Undo can take back.**
**Answer: A deletion, a format, a paste**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is Undo called in this lesson?**
**Answer: The safe net under all editing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the zoom slider change?**
**Answer: How big the page looks on screen**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Does zoom change the document or its print?**
**Answer: No — both stay exactly as they were**
