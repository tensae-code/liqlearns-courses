# Highlighting Text, Finding a Word, Undo and Zoom — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does highlighting do?" is: Paints a colour behind selected text so it stands out**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Is highlighting a change to the words?" is: The program's symbol list, by inserting**
**Answer: False** — No — a mark for the reader

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where do symbols come from when the keyboard has no key?" is: The program's symbol list, by inserting**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does Find do?" is: Reading page by page to hunt one word**
**Answer: False** — Searches the whole document for a word and takes you to it

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does Find save you from?" is: Reading page by page to hunt one word**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does Undo take back?" is: A deletion, a format, a paste**
**Answer: False** — The last action

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name three actions Undo can take back." is: A deletion, a format, a paste**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is Undo called in this lesson?" is: How big the page looks on screen**
**Answer: False** — The safe net under all editing

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the zoom slider change?" is: How big the page looks on screen**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Does zoom change the document or its print?" is: Paints a colour behind selected text so it stands out**
**Answer: False** — No — both stay exactly as they were
