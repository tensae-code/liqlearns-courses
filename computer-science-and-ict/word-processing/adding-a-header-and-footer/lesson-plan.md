# Lesson plan — Adding a header and footer

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Word Processing · **Lesson:** Adding a header and footer
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 5 — Word Processing in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 9 book, Unit 3 — Application Software. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a header repeats at the top of every page and a footer at the bottom — double-click the margin to open one, type once, and Close Header and Footer (or Esc) to set it on all pages.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the page-numbers lesson — numbers were the header and footer's first tenants; now the learner writes in them freely.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Adding page numbers.  Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a header and a footer are and where each sits.
2. Name what they carry: page numbers, dates, the author's name, footnotes.
3. Open a header by double-clicking the top margin, type, and close it onto all pages.

## What you need
A computer with Word and a document of several pages.

1. **Do it first** — the learner types a paper's title at the top of three pages by hand, then deletes two, before the header is opened (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Typed the title on one page only → that was body text, not a header; double-click the top margin and type inside the header area.
- Header will not close → Close Header and Footer, or press Esc.
