# Hands-on — Adding a header and footer

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## One typing, all pages

Take a four-page document. Put the paper's title and date in the header with one typing, close it, and verify page by page. Then change the title once in the header and verify the change everywhere.

## Header and footer pair

Give a document both: a title header and a footer with your name and a page number. Print or present it and let a partner find both without being told where they are.
