# Adding a header and footer

## Do it first

Take a three-page paper. Type its title at the top of page one. Now page two needs it too. And page three. Three typings for one title — or one typing in the right place. This lesson shows the right place.

## What headers and footers carry

A **header** sits at the **top of every page**; a **footer** at the **bottom**. They carry **page numbers, dates, the author's name and footnotes**, and their text **repeats on each page** — helping long documents stay organised and readable.

## Adding your own, in the book's order

The worked example puts a paper's **title and date in the header**: 1. **Double-click the top margin** to open the header. 2. **Type the information.** 3. **Close Header and Footer** — or press **Esc**. The header now appears on **all pages**. The footer is worked the same way at the bottom margin.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what a header and a footer are, what they carry, and the worked example's steps in order.

## Do It Yourself

1. Put a paper's title and date in its header by double-clicking the top margin.
2. Add your name in the footer of the same document.
3. Close with Esc and flip through: count the pages carrying your header.
