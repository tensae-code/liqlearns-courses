**Acceptance count: taught in 6 countries (Ethiopia, Sudan, Bhutan, Rwanda, Vietnam, Pakistan).**

Mauritania's own way (Grade 7): Informatics is taught for the first time in 1AS as thirty 30-minute teacher fiches moving hardware → information systems (illustrated with Mauritanian systems Bankily, Masrvi, Houwiyeti and the Ministry's Sirage platform) → a long Microsoft Word sequence → a full unit on internet ethics before browsers and search are even introduced.

Sudan's own way (Grade 5): a short, highly practical Arabic primary series that revises the previous grade first, then works entirely inside Windows and its built-in apps (Calculator, Paint) before reaching Word — every lesson ends with hands-on activities and each unit with an assessment.

Bhutan's own way (Class VI, Literacy with ICT student book): School computing runs on Google's G Suite for Education — email is Gmail on the school account, collaboration is Google Classroom, and the end-of-year food-chain project is agreed in Classroom, built in Word, password-protected, cited, filed in folders and reported to the teacher by email.

Rwanda's own way (S1 ICT Student Book, KLB): competency-based units each opening with a Key Unit Competency and keywords; a full GIS/ArcGIS unit and a Scratch music-making unit sit inside the first secondary year, and ICT use is grounded in Rwandan daily life.

Vietnam's own way (Grade 8 Tin học 8): Grade 8 opens with a history of calculating tools (abacus, Pascal's Pascaline, computer generations) and its applied theme is a genuine fork — track a does advanced Word/PowerPoint, track b learns photo editing in GIMP; programming is Scratch again, but now with data representation and a named debugging lesson (Gỡ lỗi).


Pakistan's own way (Grade 7 Computer Science 7): Grade 7 opens with emerging technologies and a deep ports-and-peripherals hardware treatment, then teaches email all the way to DKIM/SPF/DMARC authentication and POP3/IMAP/SMTP protocols; binary data representation (ASCII/Unicode, RGB colour, sound sampling) is taught inside the Programming unit, and entrepreneurship becomes business-plan craft — promotion, value proposition, quality assurance, SEO and platform business models, with Twitter and Snapchat origin stories.
