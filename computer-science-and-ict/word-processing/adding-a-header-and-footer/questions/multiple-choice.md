# Adding a header and footer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A header sits —
a) at the bottom of every page  b) at the top of every page  c) on the first page only  d) in the margin notes
**Answer: b) at the top of every page.**

**2.** A footer sits —
a) at the bottom of every page  b) beside the title  c) in the header  d) on the cover only
**Answer: a) at the bottom of every page.**

**3.** Headers and footers carry —
a) only pictures  b) page numbers, dates, the author's name and footnotes  c) the whole text  d) nothing
**Answer: b) page numbers, dates, the author's name and footnotes.**

**4.** The header is opened by —
a) clicking the ruler  b) double-clicking the top margin  c) pressing Print  d) opening the File tab
**Answer: b) double-clicking the top margin.**

**5.** The worked example puts in the header —
a) a table and a chart  b) a paper's title and date  c) the whole paper  d) a photograph
**Answer: b) a paper's title and date.**

**6.** The header is closed by —
a) Close Header and Footer, or Esc  b) deleting the text  c) saving only  d) restarting Word
**Answer: a) Close Header and Footer, or Esc.**
