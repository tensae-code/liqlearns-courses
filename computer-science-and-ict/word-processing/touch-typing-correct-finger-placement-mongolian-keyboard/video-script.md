# Video script — Touch Typing — Correct Finger Placement on the Mongolian Keyboard

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Touch typing begins with **correct finger placement**: the fingers rest on the **home row** of the **Mongolian Cyrillic layout** — the middle letter row where each finger has its own starting keys and returns after every stroke. The thumbs rest by the space bar. Placement comes before speed on purpose: a finger that starts from home reaches its keys by the shortest road, and the eyes stay on the text, not hunting the keyboard — that is what 'touch' typing means.
The book builds the skill **row by row**: the home row first, then the upper row, then the lower, each row drilled until its letters are found without looking, with Mongolian words and sentences as the practice text. Mongolia's own tutor programs carry the drilling — **MonTT** and **Fast type** — setting the exercises and watching accuracy. The order never changes: placement first, accuracy second, speed last — speed is the reward of correct fingers, never its substitute.
