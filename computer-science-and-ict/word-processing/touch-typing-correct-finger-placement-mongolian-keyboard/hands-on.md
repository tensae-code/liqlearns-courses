# Hands-on — Touch Typing — Correct Finger Placement on the Mongolian Keyboard

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Find home blind', 'Place your fingers on the Mongolian home row, close your eyes, and name each key under each finger. Open your eyes and check. Repeat until no check is needed.')

## Task two
('One row at a time', 'In MonTT or Fast type (or your tutor), drill the home row only until you finish a line with no look and no error. Only then add the next row.')
