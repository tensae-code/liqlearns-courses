# Touch Typing — Correct Finger Placement on the Mongolian Keyboard — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which keyboard layout does this lesson teach?**
**Answer: The Mongolian Cyrillic layout**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the fingers rest?**
**Answer: On the home row — the middle letter row**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does each finger have on the home row?**
**Answer: Its own starting keys, returned to after every stroke**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does placement come before speed?**
**Answer: A finger starting from home reaches its keys by the shortest road, and the eyes stay on the text**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does 'touch' typing mean?**
**Answer: Typing by finger placement without looking at the keyboard**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) In what order are the rows learned?**
**Answer: Home row first, then the upper row, then the lower**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the practice text made of?**
**Answer: Mongolian words and sentences**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name Mongolia's tutor programs for typing.**
**Answer: MonTT and Fast type**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's order of progress?**
**Answer: Placement first, accuracy second, speed last**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is this lesson Mongolia's own way?**
**Answer: It teaches typing on the Mongolian Cyrillic layout with Mongolian drills and tutor programs**
