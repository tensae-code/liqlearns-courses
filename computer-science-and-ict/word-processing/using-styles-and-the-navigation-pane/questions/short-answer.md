# Using Styles and the Navigation Pane — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a style?**
**Answer: A named bundle of formatting, applied in one click**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do Heading 1 and Heading 2 styles create?**
**Answer: The document's outline — chapters and sections the program can see**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What project does the lesson open?**
**Answer: A travel handbook — the Applied specialist book's continuous project**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why are styles and the pane taught together?**
**Answer: The pane works on the outline the heading styles create**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is an outline, here?**
**Answer: The document's structure of headings, in their levels**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the navigation pane?**
**Answer: The panel showing the document's outline beside the text**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What three things does it let you do?**
**Answer: Jump by headings, search the text, and reshape the document**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when a heading is dragged in the pane?**
**Answer: Its whole section moves with it**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does dragging replace?**
**Answer: Cutting and pasting pages of text**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What keeps the pane a true map?**
**Answer: Heading styles used for real headings only**
