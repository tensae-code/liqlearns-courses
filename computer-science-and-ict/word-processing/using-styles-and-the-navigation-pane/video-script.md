# Video script — Using Styles and the Navigation Pane

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The specialist project — a travel handbook — begins with **outlining and formatting with advanced tools**, and the first tool is the **style**: a named bundle of formatting applied in one click. Its advanced use is structural: paragraphs styled **Heading 1, Heading 2** stop being merely bigger text and become the document's **outline** — chapters and sections the word processor itself can see. Everything the navigation pane does next is done on this outline, which is why the book teaches styles and the pane in one lesson.
The **navigation pane** is the panel that shows the outline beside the text. In it you **jump** — click a heading and the document moves there, however long it is; you **search** the document's text from its search box; and you **reshape** — **drag a heading in the pane and its whole section moves with it**, pages of text reordered without a single cut and paste. The handbook discipline closes the lesson: keep headings honest (a heading style for real headings only), and the pane stays a true map.
