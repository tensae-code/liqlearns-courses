# Hands-on — Using Styles and the Navigation Pane

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Outline a document** — Take a document of several pages. Style its real headings Heading 1/2, open the navigation pane, and jump between sections by clicking.

## Task two
**Reshape by dragging** — In the pane, drag one section to a new place in the document. Check that its whole text moved and the document still reads in order.
