# End-of-Year Project — Food Chain Document — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the food-chain document present?**
a) A food chain — who eats whom  b) In Google Classroom  c) A table  d) Pictures
**Answer: a) A food chain — who eats whom**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the project agreed before building?**
a) A table  b) In Google Classroom  c) Pictures  d) The title and the page numbers
**Answer: b) In Google Classroom**

**Answer: c) A table**
