# Hands-on — End-of-Year Project — Food Chain Document

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Build the document', 'Make a one-page food-chain document: a table for the chain, one picture, a header with the title and a footer with the page number.')

## Task two
('Report it properly', 'Protect your document with a password, file it in your folder, and write the short email that reports it to your teacher.')
