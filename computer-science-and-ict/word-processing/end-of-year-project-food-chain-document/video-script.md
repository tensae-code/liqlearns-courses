# Video script — End-of-Year Project — Food Chain Document

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The **food-chain document** presents a food chain — who eats whom — in Word: the chain shown in a **table**, **pictures** inserted for the living things, a **header and footer** carrying the title and page numbers, and the online sources **cited** at the end. Every part is a chapter of the year used once, for real: tables, images, headers, protection and citation in a single finished document.
The project is **agreed in Google Classroom** first — the group settles its chain and its parts where the class collaborates. The finished document is **password-protected**, saved into the group's **folders**, and **reported to the teacher by email** — the report saying what was made and where it is filed. The project is the year's proof: Word, files, internet and collaboration in one hand-in.
