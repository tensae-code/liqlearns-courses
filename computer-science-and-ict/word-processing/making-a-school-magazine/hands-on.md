# Hands-on — Making a School Magazine

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Publish one issue** — In teams, produce one issue of a school magazine: three reports, credited borrowings only, a cover and contents — printed or digital.

## Task two
**The credit audit** — Exchange magazines. Audit every borrowed item for permission and credit, and every source for its naming; report the verdicts as editors.
