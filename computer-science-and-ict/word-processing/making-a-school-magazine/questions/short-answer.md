# Making a School Magazine — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What two crafts does the magazine unite?**
**Answer: Writing reports, and producing a publication**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does each team do first?**
**Answer: Reports — observes, interviews, drafts, revises**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How are others' photographs and texts used?**
**Answer: With permission and credit**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where are sources named?**
**Answer: Where they are used**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does making a magazine teach copyright well?**
**Answer: It is copyright practised, with the school watching**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three production jobs of the word processor here.**
**Answer: Headings, pictures placed and captioned, pages ordered (also cover design)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four publishing roles.**
**Answer: Writers, editors, designers and the final proof reader**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do editors check?**
**Answer: Facts and credit**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is the magazine delivered?**
**Answer: Printed or shared digitally, for real readers**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the finished magazine evidence of?**
**Answer: Skills, law and teamwork, bound together**
