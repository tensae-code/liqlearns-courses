# Making a School Magazine — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Writing reports, and producing a publication**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Writing reports, and producing a publication** — as the answer to: What does each team do first?
**Answer: False — Reports — observes, interviews, drafts, revises**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: With permission and credit**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: With permission and credit** — as the answer to: Where are sources named?
**Answer: False — Where they are used**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It is copyright practised, with the school watching**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: It is copyright practised, with the school watching** — as the answer to: Name three production jobs of the word processor here.

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Writers, editors, designers and the final proof reader**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Writers, editors, designers and the final proof reader** — as the answer to: What do editors check?
**Answer: False — Facts and credit**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Printed or shared digitally, for real readers**
**Answer: True**
