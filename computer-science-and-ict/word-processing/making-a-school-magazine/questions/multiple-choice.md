# Making a School Magazine — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What two crafts does the magazine unite?**
a) Writing reports, and producing a publication  b) Reports — observes, interviews, drafts, revises  c) With permission and credit  d) Where they are used
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does each team do first?**
a) Reports — observes, interviews, drafts, revises  b) With permission and credit  c) Where they are used  d) It is copyright practised, with the school watching
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How are others' photographs and texts used?**
a) With permission and credit  b) Where they are used  c) It is copyright practised, with the school watching  d) Headings, pictures placed and captioned, pages ordered (also cover design)
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where are sources named?**
a) Where they are used  b) It is copyright practised, with the school watching  c) Headings, pictures placed and captioned, pages ordered (also cover design)  d) Writers, editors, designers and the final proof reader
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does making a magazine teach copyright well?**
a) It is copyright practised, with the school watching  b) Headings, pictures placed and captioned, pages ordered (also cover design)  c) Writers, editors, designers and the final proof reader  d) Facts and credit
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three production jobs of the word processor here.**
a) Headings, pictures placed and captioned, pages ordered (also cover design)  b) Writers, editors, designers and the final proof reader  c) Facts and credit  d) Printed or shared digitally, for real readers

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do editors check?**
