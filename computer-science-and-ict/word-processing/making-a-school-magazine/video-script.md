# Video script — Making a School Magazine

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The magazine begins as **writing**: each team **reports** — observes, interviews, drafts, revises — on the school's own life. And because the same experience teaches intellectual property (the magazine is where the IP sessions live), every borrowed thing is handled by the law just learned: photographs and texts of others used with **permission and credit**, the sources named where they are used. A school magazine that credits honestly teaches copyright better than a chapter of rules — it is copyright, practised with the whole school watching.
Then **production**: the reports are typed and formatted in the word processor — headings, columns of text, pictures placed and captioned, the cover designed, pages ordered into a contents list — and the magazine is printed or shared digitally for its real readers. The roles are a publisher's: writers, editors who check facts and credit, designers who keep the pages consistent, and a final reader who hunts errors in the proof. The finished magazine on the school shelf is the experience's evidence: skills, law and teamwork, bound together.
