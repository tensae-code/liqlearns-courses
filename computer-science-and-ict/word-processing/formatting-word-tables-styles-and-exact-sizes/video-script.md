# Video script — Formatting Word tables — styles and exact sizes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your table is plain. Dress it.
Click inside the table. Design tab. Table Styles.
Plain, grid, list — pick one. The exercise picks a grid accent style.
Save it: Clients Table 5.
Now the sizes. Drag the markers on the ruler — quick.
Or type it: Layout tab, Cell Size — a column at 1.1 inches.
Or Table Properties — a row at exactly 0.5 inches.
Style for the look. Numbers for the measure.
