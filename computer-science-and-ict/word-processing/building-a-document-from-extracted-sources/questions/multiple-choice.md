# Building a Document from Extracted Sources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the information come from?**
a) Educational software, encyclopedias and EcuRed  b) Cuba's own encyclopedia  c) Selecting the needed part of a source and copying it out  d) A text, an image or a symbol
**Answer: a) Educational software, encyclopedias and EcuRed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is EcuRed?**
a) Selecting the needed part of a source and copying it out  b) Cuba's own encyclopedia  c) A text, an image or a symbol  d) Take what the document needs and keep the source's meaning whole
**Answer: b) Cuba's own encyclopedia**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does extracting mean here?**
a) A text, an image or a symbol  b) Take what the document needs and keep the source's meaning whole  c) Selecting the needed part of a source and copying it out  d) In the word processor
**Answer: c) Selecting the needed part of a source and copying it out**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What can be extracted?**
a) Take what the document needs and keep the source's meaning whole  b) In the word processor  c) In an order that makes sense  d) A text, an image or a symbol
**Answer: d) A text, an image or a symbol**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the choosing rule of extraction?**
a) Take what the document needs and keep the source's meaning whole  b) In the word processor  c) In an order that makes sense  d) A title, links between the parts and a closing
**Answer: a) Take what the document needs and keep the source's meaning whole**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where are extracted parts assembled?**
a) In an order that makes sense  b) In the word processor  c) A title, links between the parts and a closing  d) A new document built from sources
**Answer: b) In the word processor**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) In what order are the parts placed?**
a) A title, links between the parts and a closing  b) A new document built from sources  c) In an order that makes sense  d) Creating documents with graphic and textual information
**Answer: c) In an order that makes sense**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the pupil add of their own?**
a) A new document built from sources  b) Creating documents with graphic and textual information  c) Educational software, encyclopedias and EcuRed  d) A title, links between the parts and a closing
**Answer: d) A title, links between the parts and a closing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the finished thing?**
a) A new document built from sources  b) Creating documents with graphic and textual information  c) Educational software, encyclopedias and EcuRed  d) Cuba's own encyclopedia
**Answer: a) A new document built from sources**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the Grade 6 book call this work?**
a) Educational software, encyclopedias and EcuRed  b) Creating documents with graphic and textual information  c) Cuba's own encyclopedia  d) Selecting the needed part of a source and copying it out
