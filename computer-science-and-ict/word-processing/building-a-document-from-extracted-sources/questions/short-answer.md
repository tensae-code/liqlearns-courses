# Building a Document from Extracted Sources — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the information come from?**
**Answer: Educational software, encyclopedias and EcuRed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is EcuRed?**
**Answer: Cuba's own encyclopedia**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does extracting mean here?**
**Answer: Selecting the needed part of a source and copying it out**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What can be extracted?**
**Answer: A text, an image or a symbol**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the choosing rule of extraction?**
**Answer: Take what the document needs and keep the source's meaning whole**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where are extracted parts assembled?**
**Answer: In the word processor**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) In what order are the parts placed?**
**Answer: In an order that makes sense**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the pupil add of their own?**
**Answer: A title, links between the parts and a closing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the finished thing?**
**Answer: A new document built from sources**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the Grade 6 book call this work?**
**Answer: Creating documents with graphic and textual information**
