# Hands-on — Building a Document from Extracted Sources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Extract and build', 'Choose a small topic: find it in an encyclopedia or educational program, extract one text and one image, and build a one-page document with your own title and closing sentence.')

## Task two
('Source check', "Take a document built from sources: for each part, say which source it was extracted from and why that part was chosen — extraction is choosing, so every part must have a reason.")
