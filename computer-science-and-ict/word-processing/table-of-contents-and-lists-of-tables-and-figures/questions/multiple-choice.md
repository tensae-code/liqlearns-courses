# Table of Contents and Lists of Tables and Figures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a table of contents built from?**
a) Heading styles  b) Heading 1  c) The headings in order with their page numbers  d) Update Table rebuilds it
**Answer: a) Heading styles**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which style marks a chapter title?**
a) The headings in order with their page numbers  b) Heading 1  c) Update Table rebuilds it  d) As one field, never line by line
**Answer: b) Heading 1**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the list gather?**
a) Update Table rebuilds it  b) As one field, never line by line  c) The headings in order with their page numbers  d) Heading styles
**Answer: c) The headings in order with their page numbers**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is the table kept right after edits?**
a) As one field, never line by line  b) Heading styles  c) Heading 1  d) Update Table rebuilds it
**Answer: d) Update Table rebuilds it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a table of contents deleted?**
a) As one field, never line by line  b) Heading styles  c) Heading 1  d) The headings in order with their page numbers
**Answer: a) As one field, never line by line**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a caption?**
a) Word's caption tool (Insert Caption)  b) The numbered label under a table or figure  c) It renumbers itself  d) The list of tables and the list of figures
**Answer: b) The numbered label under a table or figure**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What tool inserts a caption?**
a) It renumbers itself  b) The list of tables and the list of figures  c) Word's caption tool (Insert Caption)  d) At the front, beside the contents
**Answer: c) Word's caption tool (Insert Caption)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to numbering when a table is added?**
a) The list of tables and the list of figures  b) At the front, beside the contents  c) The numbered label under a table or figure  d) It renumbers itself
**Answer: d) It renumbers itself**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two lists are built from captions?**
a) The list of tables and the list of figures  b) At the front, beside the contents  c) The numbered label under a table or figure  d) Word's caption tool (Insert Caption)
**Answer: a) The list of tables and the list of figures**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where do the lists stand in the document?**
a) The numbered label under a table or figure  b) At the front, beside the contents  c) Word's caption tool (Insert Caption)  d) It renumbers itself
**Answer: b) At the front, beside the contents**
