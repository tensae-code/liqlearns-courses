# Table of Contents and Lists of Tables and Figures — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a table of contents built from?**
**Answer: Heading styles**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which style marks a chapter title?**
**Answer: Heading 1**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the list gather?**
**Answer: The headings in order with their page numbers**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is the table kept right after edits?**
**Answer: Update Table rebuilds it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a table of contents deleted?**
**Answer: As one field, never line by line**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a caption?**
**Answer: The numbered label under a table or figure**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What tool inserts a caption?**
**Answer: Word's caption tool (Insert Caption)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to numbering when a table is added?**
**Answer: It renumbers itself**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two lists are built from captions?**
**Answer: The list of tables and the list of figures**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where do the lists stand in the document?**
**Answer: At the front, beside the contents**
