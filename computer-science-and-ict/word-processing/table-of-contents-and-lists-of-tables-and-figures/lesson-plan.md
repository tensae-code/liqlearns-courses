## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The table of contents — built from heading styles** — Word builds a **table of contents** from **heading styles**: mark chapter titles **Heading 1**, sections **Heading 2**, and the list gathers them **in order with their page numbers**. It is **automatic** — after edits, **Update Table** rebuilds it; it can be **customised** (levels shown, leader dots) and **deleted** as one field, never line by line. Rwanda's S3 unit opens here because every later list in the unit is the same idea wearing another name: mark the thing in the text, and let Word keep the list.
2. **Lists of tables and figures — built from captions** — A **caption** is the numbered label under a table or figure — *Table 1*, *Figure 2* — inserted with Word's caption tool so the numbering **renumbers itself** when a table is added or removed. From the captions Word builds the **list of tables** and the **list of figures** (Insert Table of Figures): every caption with its page, gathered at the front of the document beside the contents. Rwanda's practical documents — a GS Bumbogo meeting file among them — carry all three lists, because a reader of a long document should never hunt.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
