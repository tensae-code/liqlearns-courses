# Hands-on — Table of Contents and Lists of Tables and Figures

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Mark and gather', 'Take a two-page document of yours. Style its headings, insert a table of contents, then change one heading and update the table. Write what updated and what you did not have to retype.')

## Task two
('Caption drill', 'Give captions to three tables or figures in a document (real or on paper), then write out by hand the list of tables Word would build — captions in order with the pages you placed them on.')
