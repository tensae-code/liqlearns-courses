# Video script — Table of Contents and Lists of Tables and Figures

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Word builds a **table of contents** from **heading styles**: mark chapter titles **Heading 1**, sections **Heading 2**, and the list gathers them **in order with their page numbers**. It is **automatic** — after edits, **Update Table** rebuilds it; it can be **customised** (levels shown, leader dots) and **deleted** as one field, never line by line. Rwanda's S3 unit opens here because every later list in the unit is the same idea wearing another name: mark the thing in the text, and let Word keep the list.
A **caption** is the numbered label under a table or figure — *Table 1*, *Figure 2* — inserted with Word's caption tool so the numbering **renumbers itself** when a table is added or removed. From the captions Word builds the **list of tables** and the **list of figures** (Insert Table of Figures): every caption with its page, gathered at the front of the document beside the contents. Rwanda's practical documents — a GS Bumbogo meeting file among them — carry all three lists, because a reader of a long document should never hunt.
