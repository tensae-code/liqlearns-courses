# Intermediate Word Features — WordArt and Wrapping Text — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is WordArt?**
**Answer: A short piece of text turned into a styled object — shaped, coloured, outlined or shadowed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is a WordArt object treated on the page?**
**Answer: Like a picture — inserted, moved and resized as an object**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is WordArt for?**
**Answer: One short line — a title or heading that carries the page's name**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why must WordArt never be used for a whole paragraph?**
**Answer: Decorated text cannot be read at length — decoration serves the title and leaves the text plain**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How do you edit WordArt words?**
**Answer: By reopening the WordArt object's text**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is text wrapping?**
**Answer: The decision about how text flows around a picture placed in the document**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does square wrapping do?**
**Answer: The text flows around the picture's box, leaving it a clear space**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does tight wrapping do?**
**Answer: The text hugs the picture's own shape**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does 'in line with text' mean?**
**Answer: The picture sits in the text line and moves along like one big character**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Fiji teach these as intermediate features?**
**Answer: Because the learner must arrange objects on a laid-out page, not only type**
