# Removing a page break

## Do it first

Open a document that holds a manual page break. Try to find the break. You cannot see it — breaks are invisible by default, and an invisible thing cannot be deleted. This lesson makes it visible, then removes it in two moves.

## Show it, then delete it

In the book's order: 1. Show the break with **Home → Show/Hide** — the dotted **Page Break** line appears. 2. Place the **insertion point at the beginning** of that dotted line. 3. Press the **Delete key** — the break is removed and the **text flows back** to where it would naturally sit.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the three moves in order, and why the first move is showing the break.

## Do It Yourself

1. Remove a manual break from a document and watch the text flow back.
2. Insert the break again, show it, and remove it a second time without looking at the lesson.
3. Say what the dotted line is called.
