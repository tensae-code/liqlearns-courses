# Removing a page break — ordering

Put each set in the right order.

**1.** The removal in order: press Delete / show the break with Show/Hide / place the insertion point at the line's beginning.
**Answer: show the break with Show/Hide → place the insertion point at the line's beginning → press Delete.**

**2.** Seeing it: open the Home tab / click Show/Hide / see the dotted line.
**Answer: open the Home tab → click Show/Hide → see the dotted line.**
