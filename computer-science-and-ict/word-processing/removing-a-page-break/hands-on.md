# Hands-on — Removing a page break

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Hide and seek

In pairs: one inserts a break somewhere in a shared document and turns Show/Hide off. The other must find it — Show/Hide on — and remove it. Swap roles and time each other.

## Flow-back check

Note the first words of the page after a break. Remove the break and write down where those words sit now. That movement is the text flowing back.
