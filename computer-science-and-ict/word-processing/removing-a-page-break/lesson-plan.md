# Lesson plan — Removing a page break

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Word Processing · **Lesson:** Removing a page break
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 5 — Word Processing in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 9 book, Unit 3 — Application Software. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a manual page break is removed by first showing it (Home → Show/Hide), placing the insertion point at the beginning of its dotted Page Break line, and pressing Delete — the text flows back.
**This lesson's own level:** it is the twin of the inserting lesson — every break placed can be taken out again, by the same dotted line it arrived on.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Show a hidden page break with Home → Show/Hide.
2. Place the insertion point at the beginning of the dotted Page Break line.
3. Press Delete and watch the text flow back.

## What you need
A computer with Word and a document holding a manual page break.

## If the learner is stuck
- Cannot see the break → breaks are invisible by default; Home → Show/Hide makes the dotted line appear.
- Deleted a letter instead of the break → the insertion point must stand at the very beginning of the dotted Page Break line; undo and place it exactly there.
