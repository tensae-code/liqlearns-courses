# Basic Document Settings — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What three page settings open the lesson?**
**Answer: Size, margins and orientation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are margins?**
**Answer: The blank frame on the page's four sides**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are the two orientations?**
**Answer: Portrait (tall) and landscape (wide)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why are page settings set first?**
**Answer: Every later judgement depends on them**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In which two Uzbekistan books does this lesson appear?**
**Answer: The Grade 6 and Grade 8 Informatika books**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three text parameters.**
**Answer: Font, font size and line spacing (or alignment)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do defaults set at the start keep in one dress?**
**Answer: The whole document**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do mid-way changes make?**
**Answer: Patches**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the lesson's discipline?**
**Answer: Decide the parameters once, at the beginning**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What should carry the rest of the work?**
**Answer: The writing, not the settings**
