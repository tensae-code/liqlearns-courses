# Basic Document Settings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What three page settings open the lesson?**
a) Size, margins and orientation  b) The blank frame on the page's four sides  c) Portrait (tall) and landscape (wide)  d) Every later judgement depends on them
**Answer: a) Size, margins and orientation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are margins?**
a) Portrait (tall) and landscape (wide)  b) The blank frame on the page's four sides  c) Every later judgement depends on them  d) The Grade 6 and Grade 8 Informatika books
**Answer: b) The blank frame on the page's four sides**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are the two orientations?**
a) Every later judgement depends on them  b) The Grade 6 and Grade 8 Informatika books  c) Portrait (tall) and landscape (wide)  d) Font, font size and line spacing (or alignment)
**Answer: c) Portrait (tall) and landscape (wide)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why are page settings set first?**
a) The Grade 6 and Grade 8 Informatika books  b) Font, font size and line spacing (or alignment)  c) The whole document  d) Every later judgement depends on them
**Answer: d) Every later judgement depends on them**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In which two Uzbekistan books does this lesson appear?**
a) The Grade 6 and Grade 8 Informatika books  b) Font, font size and line spacing (or alignment)  c) The whole document  d) Patches
**Answer: a) The Grade 6 and Grade 8 Informatika books**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three text parameters.**
a) The whole document  b) Font, font size and line spacing (or alignment)  c) Patches  d) Decide the parameters once, at the beginning
**Answer: b) Font, font size and line spacing (or alignment)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do defaults set at the start keep in one dress?**
a) Patches  b) Decide the parameters once, at the beginning  c) The whole document  d) The writing, not the settings
**Answer: c) The whole document**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do mid-way changes make?**
a) Decide the parameters once, at the beginning  b) The writing, not the settings  c) Size, margins and orientation  d) Patches
**Answer: d) Patches**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the lesson's discipline?**
a) Decide the parameters once, at the beginning  b) The writing, not the settings  c) Size, margins and orientation  d) The blank frame on the page's four sides
**Answer: a) Decide the parameters once, at the beginning**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What should carry the rest of the work?**
a) Size, margins and orientation  b) The writing, not the settings  c) The blank frame on the page's four sides  d) Portrait (tall) and landscape (wide)
**Answer: b) The writing, not the settings**
