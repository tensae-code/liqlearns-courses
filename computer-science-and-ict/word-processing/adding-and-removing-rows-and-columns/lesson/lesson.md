# Adding and removing rows and columns

## Do it first

Your 5×2 table needs a third row in the middle and one column fewer. Do not rebuild it — tables grow and shrink from inside. Add and remove, in place.

## Sub lesson 1 — Adding — the insert control

Insertion has a dedicated on-table tool:

1. **Hover at the left or top border**, between two rows or between two columns.
2. The **insert control** appears — **grey until focused, blue when live**.
3. Click its **+** — a **row is added below**, or a **column to the right**.

No selection is needed first: the control knows where it sits.

## Sub lesson 2 — Deleting — and resizing the whole table

Deletion works two ways:

1. **The ribbon's Delete tool** — put the insertion point in the row or column, then use Delete.
2. **The right-click menu** — select the row or column, right-click, and choose **Delete Rows** or **Delete Columns**.

Each way was demonstrated in the book, then **undone** — practise the same way. And the **whole table** is resized by dragging the **resize handle at its bottom-right corner**.

## Do It Yourself

1. Hover at a table's left border and add a row below with the insert control.
2. Delete a column with the right-click Delete Columns, then undo it.
3. Drag the whole table bigger by its bottom-right resize handle.
