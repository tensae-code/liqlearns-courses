# Video script — Adding and removing rows and columns

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your table needs one more row — in the middle.
Hover on the left border, between two rows.
A small control appears. Grey… now blue. It is live.
Click the plus. A new row, right there. No selecting first.
Too many columns? Click inside one. Delete — on the ribbon.
Or select it, right-click, Delete Columns. Gone.
Whole table too small? Drag the little handle at its bottom-right corner.
