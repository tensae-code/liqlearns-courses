# Adding and removing rows and columns — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The insert control is live when it turns —
a) red  b) blue  c) green  d) black
**Answer: b) blue** — grey until focused, blue when live.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Clicking + at the left border adds —
a) a row above  b) a row below  c) a column  d) a page
**Answer: b) a row below**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Before clicking +, you must first —
a) select the whole table  b) select the row  c) nothing — no selection is needed  d) save
**Answer: c) nothing — no selection is needed**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Delete Columns is found —
a) only on the Home tab  b) in the right-click menu after selecting the column  c) in Quick Tables  d) under Insert
**Answer: b) in the right-click menu after selecting the column**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The whole table is resized by dragging —
a) any cell  b) the resize handle at its bottom-right corner  c) the ruler  d) the title bar
**Answer: b) the resize handle at its bottom-right corner**
