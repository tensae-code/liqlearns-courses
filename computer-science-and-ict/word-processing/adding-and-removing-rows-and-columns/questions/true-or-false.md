# Adding and removing rows and columns — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The insert control appears at the left or top border, between two rows or columns.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Deleting a row can only be done from the ribbon.
**Answer: False** — the right-click menu also deletes rows and columns.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the book's practice, each deletion was demonstrated and then undone.
**Answer: True**
