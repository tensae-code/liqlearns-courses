# Hands-on — Saving a word document

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Save it three places
Save the same short document three times by Save As: once into Documents through This PC, once onto the Desktop through Browse, once under a new name. Open each one back up to prove it survived.

## Name keeper
Before saving, write the name you will give the file and the place you will keep it, on paper. Then save. Checking plan against result is the habit that prevents lost work.

## If it is hard
Saving is assessed by watching the doing, as the teacher guide directs for the Word practical: a teacher or partner watches you save, open the file back, and close — the opened-back file is the proof.
