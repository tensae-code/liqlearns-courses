# Video script — Saving a word document

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

You typed your work. Now keep it.

File. Save As.
Choose the place: a drive — or This PC, Documents — or Browse, anywhere, even the Desktop.
Type the name. Word adds dot-docx by itself.

Saved. Your work survives.
Switch off safely now.

Now the same saving, in its five steps — follow along.

One. Click the File tab.
Two. Click Save As.
Three. Choose where it lives: Documents.
Four. Give it a file name.
Five. Click Save.

Saved — in five steps, in order, every time.
