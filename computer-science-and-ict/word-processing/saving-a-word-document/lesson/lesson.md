# Saving a word document

## Do it first

Type two lines about yourself in Word. Now — the computer will be switched off soon. If you do nothing, your lines vanish. Saving is how work survives. Do not close anything yet: this lesson walks the book's saving order, step by step.

## Sub lesson 1 — Save As — the first save

For a first save, in the book's order: click **File**, then choose **Save As**. Save As is the route for a document that does not have a name or a home yet — it asks both questions: where, and under what name. Follow the GIF through the dialogue.

## Sub lesson 2 — Choosing the place and the name

Choose the **location or drive** for the file. Or go through **This PC → Documents** to keep it with your documents. Or click **Browse** to pick any location you like — including the **Desktop**. Type the document's name — and know this: **Word automatically adds the .docx suffix** to the name. You type the name; Word finishes it.
