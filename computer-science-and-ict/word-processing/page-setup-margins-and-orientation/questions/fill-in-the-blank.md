# Page Setup — Margins and Orientation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The wide page shape, wider than it is tall, is called ____.**
**Answer: landscape**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The four margins of a page are top, bottom, left and ____.**
**Answer: right**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Text in ____ reads like a newspaper: down one strip, then the top of the next.**
**Answer: columns**
