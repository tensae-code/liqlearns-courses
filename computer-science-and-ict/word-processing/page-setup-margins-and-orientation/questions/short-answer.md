# Page Setup — Margins and Orientation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A learner shrinks a wide table again and again to fit a portrait page. Give the page-setup answer.**
**Answer:** Turn the page to landscape: the wide shape fits the wide table at full size, instead of the table being shrunk to fit the wrong-shaped page.

**2. (Sub lesson 3) Why are orientation, margins and columns taught as one lesson rather than three?**
**Answer:** Because they are one decision about the same page: orientation gives the page its shape, margins frame the text area inside that shape, and columns divide that text area — each setting changes what the others mean.
