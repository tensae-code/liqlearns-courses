# Page Setup — Margins and Orientation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Portrait orientation means the page is —**
a) Wider than it is tall  b) Taller than it is wide  c) Square
**Answer: b) Taller than it is wide** — Portrait is the tall, upright shape of letters and essays.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A wide table fits best on a —**
a) Portrait page  b) Landscape page  c) Page with no margins
**Answer: b) Landscape page** — The wide shape fits wide work at full size.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Margins are —**
a) The blank borders between the page edge and the text  b) Lines drawn under headings  c) Extra pages
**Answer: a) The blank borders between the page edge and the text** — Top, bottom, left and right; no text goes in them.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Narrower margins make the same words take —**
a) More pages  b) Fewer pages  c) The same pages, always
**Answer: b) Fewer pages** — The text gets more room on each page, until it crowds the edge.

**5. (Sub lesson 3) In columns, after the bottom of the first column you continue at —**
a) The bottom of the next column  b) The top of the next column  c) The next page
**Answer: b) The top of the next column** — Reading runs down a column, then across to the next column's top.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) Columns suit —**
a) Newsletters and leaflets  b) A formal letter  c) Every document
**Answer: a) Newsletters and leaflets** — Short lines are quicker to read; a letter stays in one full-width block.
