# WordArt — Decorative Text — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is WordArt?" is: Decorative text — writing turned into a styled object**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name three things a WordArt style can do." is: Like a picture — inserted, moved and resized**
**Answer: False** — Shape, colour and outline the text (or shadow it)

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is WordArt handled in the document?" is: Like a picture — inserted, moved and resized**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is its wording changed?" is: Between tables and formulas**
**Answer: False** — By reopening its text for editing

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does WordArt sit in the Grade 6 course?" is: Between tables and formulas**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is WordArt for?" is: One short line — never whole paragraphs**
**Answer: False** — Titles and posters

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How much text belongs in WordArt?" is: One short line — never whole paragraphs**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the taste rule?" is: The document's text**
**Answer: False** — If a reader must work to read it, it has failed

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What stays plain?" is: The document's text**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do pupils practise WordArt on?" is: Decorative text — writing turned into a styled object**
**Answer: False** — The cover of their own document from earlier lessons
