# WordArt — Decorative Text — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is WordArt?**
**Answer: Decorative text — writing turned into a styled object**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three things a WordArt style can do.**
**Answer: Shape, colour and outline the text (or shadow it)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is WordArt handled in the document?**
**Answer: Like a picture — inserted, moved and resized**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is its wording changed?**
**Answer: By reopening its text for editing**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does WordArt sit in the Grade 6 course?**
**Answer: Between tables and formulas**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is WordArt for?**
**Answer: Titles and posters**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How much text belongs in WordArt?**
**Answer: One short line — never whole paragraphs**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the taste rule?**
**Answer: If a reader must work to read it, it has failed**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What stays plain?**
**Answer: The document's text**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do pupils practise WordArt on?**
**Answer: The cover of their own document from earlier lessons**
