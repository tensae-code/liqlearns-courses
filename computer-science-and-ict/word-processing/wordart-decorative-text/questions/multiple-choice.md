# WordArt — Decorative Text — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is WordArt?**
a) Decorative text — writing turned into a styled object  b) Shape, colour and outline the text (or shadow it)  c) Like a picture — inserted, moved and resized  d) By reopening its text for editing
**Answer: a) Decorative text — writing turned into a styled object**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three things a WordArt style can do.**
a) Like a picture — inserted, moved and resized  b) Shape, colour and outline the text (or shadow it)  c) By reopening its text for editing  d) Between tables and formulas
**Answer: b) Shape, colour and outline the text (or shadow it)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is WordArt handled in the document?**
a) By reopening its text for editing  b) Between tables and formulas  c) Like a picture — inserted, moved and resized  d) Titles and posters
**Answer: c) Like a picture — inserted, moved and resized**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is its wording changed?**
a) Between tables and formulas  b) Titles and posters  c) One short line — never whole paragraphs  d) By reopening its text for editing
**Answer: d) By reopening its text for editing**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does WordArt sit in the Grade 6 course?**
a) Between tables and formulas  b) Titles and posters  c) One short line — never whole paragraphs  d) If a reader must work to read it, it has failed
**Answer: a) Between tables and formulas**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is WordArt for?**
a) One short line — never whole paragraphs  b) Titles and posters  c) If a reader must work to read it, it has failed  d) The document's text
**Answer: b) Titles and posters**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How much text belongs in WordArt?**
a) If a reader must work to read it, it has failed  b) The document's text  c) One short line — never whole paragraphs  d) The cover of their own document from earlier lessons
**Answer: c) One short line — never whole paragraphs**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the taste rule?**
a) The document's text  b) The cover of their own document from earlier lessons  c) Decorative text — writing turned into a styled object  d) If a reader must work to read it, it has failed
**Answer: d) If a reader must work to read it, it has failed**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What stays plain?**
a) The document's text  b) The cover of their own document from earlier lessons  c) Decorative text — writing turned into a styled object  d) Shape, colour and outline the text (or shadow it)
**Answer: a) The document's text**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do pupils practise WordArt on?**
a) Decorative text — writing turned into a styled object  b) The cover of their own document from earlier lessons  c) Shape, colour and outline the text (or shadow it)  d) Like a picture — inserted, moved and resized
**Answer: b) The cover of their own document from earlier lessons**
