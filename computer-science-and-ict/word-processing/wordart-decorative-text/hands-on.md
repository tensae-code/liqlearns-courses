# Hands-on — WordArt — Decorative Text

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Make a cover title', 'Give a document you have written a WordArt cover title: one line, one style, sized to the page — then say why the style fits the document.')

## Task two
('Judge two titles', 'Compare two decorative titles for the same poster: which reads at a glance, and what exactly makes the other fail?')
