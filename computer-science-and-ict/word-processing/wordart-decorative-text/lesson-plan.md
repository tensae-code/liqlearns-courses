## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What WordArt is** — **WordArt** is Word's decorative text: a short piece of writing turned into an **object** with a chosen style — shaped, coloured, outlined, shadowed. It is inserted like a picture, moved and resized like a picture, and edited by reopening its text. The Grade 6 book gives WordArt a full lesson between tables and formulas: decoration is a real document skill, with its own tool.
2. **When decoration serves the document** — WordArt serves **titles and posters**: one short line, large, saying the document's name — never whole paragraphs, which become unreadable in decoration. The lesson's taste rule: if a reader must work to read it, it has failed; decoration carries the title and leaves the text plain. Grade 6 pupils practise on the cover of their own document from the course's earlier lessons.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
