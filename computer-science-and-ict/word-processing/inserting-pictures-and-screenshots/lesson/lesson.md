# Inserting pictures and screenshots

## Do it first

Your Green Legacy page is words only. It needs the photograph — and your other document needs exactly one piece of a photograph that is open in another window. Insert the first; clip the second.

## Sub lesson 1 — Inserting a picture

Illustrations — **pictures, shapes, SmartArt, charts and screenshots** — can be resized, cropped, rotated, captioned, recoloured, given styles and artistic effects, and repositioned. An inserted picture becomes an **embedded object**: part of the document, not linked to its file.

Insert one, in the book's order:

1. **Prepare and format the heading text** first.
2. **Insert tab → Pictures**, in the **Illustrations group**.
3. **Find the image** in the lesson folder.
4. Click **Insert** — the picture lands **at the cursor**, and the **Picture Tools Format tab** appears.
5. Save the worked document under its name: **Green Legacy**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Screenshots and screen clipping

Screenshots work in place:

1. **Insert → Screenshot** — thumbnails of the **open windows** are shown.
2. Insert a whole window by picking its thumbnail — **or** choose **Screen Clipping**.
3. With Screen Clipping, **drag a rectangle** over the other window.
4. Release — **exactly that piece** is pasted into your document.

The worked example clips a photograph into an **Athlete Haile G/Silase** document.
