# Lesson plan — Inserting pictures and screenshots

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Word Processing · **Lesson:** Inserting pictures and screenshots
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Application Software, Grade 10 depth, in the Word Processing title of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md). The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a picture is inserted from the Illustrations group and becomes an embedded part of the document, and a screenshot can be clipped as an exact rectangle from another window.
**This lesson's own level:** it is a doing-lesson. It stands on the Word lessons; the worked documents are the Green Legacy page and the Athlete Haile G/Silase page, kept as the book gives them.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Inserting a picture** — pass mark: insert a picture by the book's route and explain embedded.
2. **Screenshots and screen clipping** — pass mark: insert a whole-window screenshot and clip an exact rectangle.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Formatting Word tables — styles and exact sizes (the chained file this unit keeps reopening). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Insert a picture by Insert → Pictures and say where it lands.
2. Say that an inserted picture is embedded — part of the document, not linked.
3. Name what can be done to an illustration: resize, crop, rotate, caption, recolour, style, effects, reposition.
4. Insert a whole window screenshot and clip an exact rectangle with Screen Clipping.

## What you need
A computer with Word, a picture file in a lesson folder, and a second window open.

## If the learner is stuck
- The Format tab is missing → the picture is not selected; click the picture once.
- The clipping took the whole screen → choose Screen Clipping (not a window thumbnail) and drag a smaller rectangle.
