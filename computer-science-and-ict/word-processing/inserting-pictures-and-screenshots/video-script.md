The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Words alone? Add the picture.
First, the heading — ready and formatted.
Insert tab. Pictures. Find the image in the folder. Insert.
There it is — at the cursor. And see: it lives inside the document now. Embedded, not linked.
Save it: Green Legacy.
Need only a piece of another window?
Insert. Screenshot. Screen Clipping.
Drag a rectangle over exactly the piece you want. Let go.
