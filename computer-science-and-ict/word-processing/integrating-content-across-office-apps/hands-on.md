# Hands-on — Integrating Content Across Office Apps

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Three joinings** — Take one small table of figures. Produce a report page three ways — pasted, embedded, linked (or describe each exactly if a tool is missing) — then change one figure at the source and record what each report shows.

## Task two
**Choose the strength** — For three real documents (a final exam report, a monthly fees summary, a one-off poster with a chart), choose the joining for their figures and defend each choice.
