# Integrating Content Across Office Apps — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Joining content between the word processor, spreadsheet and presentation so one body of work lives in several documents**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Joining content between the word processor, spreadsheet and presentation so one body of work lives in several documents** — as the answer to: Where does real work's content start?
**Answer: False — Figures in the spreadsheet, writing in the word processor, the talk in the presentation**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The numbers are computed once and shown everywhere**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The numbers are computed once and shown everywhere** — as the answer to: What is paste, as a joining?
**Answer: False — A copy carried across — simple, and dead: it never learns the original changed**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A working object of the other app placed inside the document — still editable as its own kind**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A working object of the other app placed inside the document — still editable as its own kind** — as the answer to: What is linking?
**Answer: False — Showing the original through a window — the document displays the source's current values**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The link breaks when files move**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The link breaks when files move** — as the answer to: One correction in a linked sheet does what?
**Answer: False — Heals every document that links it**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Monthly links its figures; a final report may freeze (paste or embed) them**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Monthly links its figures; a final report may freeze (paste or embed) them** — as the answer to: Where does Indonesia teach this?
**Answer: False — Grade 9 Bab III B.3 and Grade 10 SMK Bab 2 D — integration of office application content**
