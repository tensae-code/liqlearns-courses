# Video script — Integrating Content Across Office Apps

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Real work does not live in one application: the figures are computed in the **spreadsheet**, the report is written in the **word processor**, the talk is given from the **presentation**. **Integration** (integrasi konten) is the craft of joining them — a chart carried from the sheet into the report, a table from the document into the slides — so the numbers are computed once and shown everywhere. Indonesia teaches it in both senior tracks (Grade 9's productivity chapter, the SMK book's office chapter) because it is where office skill stops being three subjects and becomes one workmanship.
Content crosses between apps in three strengths. **Paste** carries a copy — simple, and dead: it never learns the original changed. **Embedding** places a working object of the other app inside the document — the chart in the report can still be edited as a chart. **Linking** shows the original through a window: the document displays the sheet's own current values, so one correction in the sheet heals every report that links it — at the price of the link breaking when files move. The integrator's skill is choosing the strength each joining deserves: a final report may freeze its figures; a monthly one must link them.
