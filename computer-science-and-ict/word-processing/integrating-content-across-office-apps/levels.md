# Integrating Content Across Office Apps — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — One work, several apps — why integrate
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Joining content between the word processor, spreadsheet and presentation so one body of work lives in several documents; Figures in the spreadsheet, writing in the word processor, the talk in the presentation; The numbers are computed once and shown everywhere; A copy carried across — simple, and dead: it never learns the original changed; A working object of the other app placed inside the document — still editable as its own kind.

Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files.
