# Video script — Table of Contents and Indexes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

If headings carry **heading styles**, Word can **insert a table of contents** that lists them in order with **page numbers**, indented by level. It is a field, not typed text: after edits, **Update Table** rebuilds it — new headings appear, page numbers correct themselves. A hand-typed contents cannot do that, which is why the book teaches styles first.
An **index** starts by **marking entries**: select a word, mark it (with its main entry wording), and Word plants a hidden mark. Repeat for the words a reader would look up, then **insert the index** where it should stand — usually at the end — and Word gathers every marked word, alphabetises it and lists its pages. Update it the same way after changes.
