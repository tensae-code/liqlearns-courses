# Hands-on — Table of Contents and Indexes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Contents in one click
Take a document with styled headings (or style one quickly). Insert a table of contents, add a new heading in the middle, then update the table and confirm the new heading and correct pages appear.

## Mark five words
In a short document, mark five words a reader might look up, insert an index at the end, and check each word's pages by finding them yourself.
