# Table of Contents and Indexes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a table of contents list?**
a) The document's styled headings with page numbers  b) Heading styles  c) No — it is a generated field  d) Rebuilds the contents after edits
**Answer: a) The document's styled headings with page numbers**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must headings carry for contents to work?**
a) No — it is a generated field  b) Heading styles  c) Rebuilds the contents after edits  d) It cannot update itself when the document changes
**Answer: b) Heading styles**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Is a contents table typed text?**
a) Rebuilds the contents after edits  b) It cannot update itself when the document changes  c) No — it is a generated field  d) An alphabetical list of chosen words with their pages
**Answer: c) No — it is a generated field**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does Update Table do?**
a) It cannot update itself when the document changes  b) An alphabetical list of chosen words with their pages  c) By marking the word in the document  d) Rebuilds the contents after edits
**Answer: d) Rebuilds the contents after edits**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why can't a hand-typed contents match it?**
a) It cannot update itself when the document changes  b) An alphabetical list of chosen words with their pages  c) By marking the word in the document  d) At the end of the document
**Answer: a) It cannot update itself when the document changes**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is an index?**
a) By marking the word in the document  b) An alphabetical list of chosen words with their pages  c) At the end of the document  d) Gathers, alphabetises and lists the marked words with pages
**Answer: b) An alphabetical list of chosen words with their pages**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does an index entry begin?**
a) At the end of the document  b) Gathers, alphabetises and lists the marked words with pages  c) By marking the word in the document  d) By updating it after changes, like the contents
**Answer: c) By marking the word in the document**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does an index usually stand?**
a) Gathers, alphabetises and lists the marked words with pages  b) By updating it after changes, like the contents  c) The document's styled headings with page numbers  d) At the end of the document
**Answer: d) At the end of the document**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does Word do when the index is inserted?**
a) Gathers, alphabetises and lists the marked words with pages  b) By updating it after changes, like the contents  c) The document's styled headings with page numbers  d) Heading styles
**Answer: a) Gathers, alphabetises and lists the marked words with pages**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is an index kept current?**
a) The document's styled headings with page numbers  b) By updating it after changes, like the contents  c) Heading styles  d) No — it is a generated field
**Answer: b) By updating it after changes, like the contents**
