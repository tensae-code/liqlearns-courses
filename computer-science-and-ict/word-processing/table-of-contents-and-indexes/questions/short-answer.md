# Table of Contents and Indexes — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a table of contents list?**
**Answer: The document's styled headings with page numbers**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must headings carry for contents to work?**
**Answer: Heading styles**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Is a contents table typed text?**
**Answer: No — it is a generated field**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does Update Table do?**
**Answer: Rebuilds the contents after edits**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why can't a hand-typed contents match it?**
**Answer: It cannot update itself when the document changes**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is an index?**
**Answer: An alphabetical list of chosen words with their pages**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does an index entry begin?**
**Answer: By marking the word in the document**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does an index usually stand?**
**Answer: At the end of the document**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does Word do when the index is inserted?**
**Answer: Gathers, alphabetises and lists the marked words with pages**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is an index kept current?**
**Answer: By updating it after changes, like the contents**
