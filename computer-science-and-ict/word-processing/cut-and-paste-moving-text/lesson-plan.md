# Lesson plan — Cut and paste — moving text

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Word Processing · **Lesson:** Cut and paste — moving text
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 5 — Word Processing in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 8 book, Unit 3 — Computer Software. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** cut and paste moves a portion of text from one place to another through the clipboard: select, cut, place the insertion point, paste.
**This lesson's own level:** it is a doing-lesson. It stands on copy and paste — cut is copy’s moving twin, and the difference between them is the whole point.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Cutting the text** — select the text; right-click and choose Cut — or click Cut in the Clipboard group of the Home tab; the text leaves its place and waits on the clipboard
2. **Pasting it in the new place** — place the insertion point where the text is to be inserted; click Paste in the Clipboard group of the Home tab — or right-click and choose Paste

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Copy and paste — Home tab and right-click ways. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Cut selected text by right-click, or by Cut in the Clipboard group of the Home tab.
2. Place the insertion point and paste the cut text there, by the Home tab or by right-click.
3. Say how cut differs from copy: cut moves the text; copy leaves the original standing.

## What you need
A computer with a word processor and a short paragraph typed in it — the practice text is a paragraph about Addis Ababa.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner looks at a paragraph standing in the wrong place in a document and says how they would move it — not copy it — to the right place (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Reaches for Copy when the text must move → copy leaves the original standing; if the text should stand in one place only, cut it.
- Cuts, then clicks around the page and loses the text → the cut text waits on the clipboard; place the insertion point deliberately, then paste.
