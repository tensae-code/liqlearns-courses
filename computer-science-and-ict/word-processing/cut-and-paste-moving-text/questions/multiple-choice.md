# Cut and paste — moving text — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first step of cut and paste is —
a) click Paste  b) select the text  c) place the insertion point  d) open the clipboard
**Answer: b) select the text** — cutting starts with selecting what will move.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cut can be given by —
a) right-click and choose Cut, or Cut in the Clipboard group of the Home tab  b) the File tab only  c) pressing Enter  d) double-clicking the text
**Answer: a) right-click and choose Cut, or Cut in the Clipboard group of the Home tab** — the book's two routes.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** After Cut, the selected text —
a) is deleted forever  b) vanishes from its place and waits on the clipboard  c) prints out  d) stays and a copy is made
**Answer: b) vanishes from its place and waits on the clipboard.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Before pasting, you —
a) select more text  b) save the document  c) place the insertion point where the text is to be inserted  d) cut again
**Answer: c) place the insertion point where the text is to be inserted** — the text lands at the point.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Paste can be given by —
a) Paste in the Clipboard group of the Home tab, or right-click and choose Paste  b) the Spacebar  c) the Save As dialog  d) closing the document
**Answer: a) Paste in the Clipboard group of the Home tab, or right-click and choose Paste.**
