# Hands-on — Cut and paste — moving text

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Move the paragraph

Type the paragraph about Addis Ababa at the top of a page, with two other paragraphs under it. Cut the Addis Ababa paragraph and paste it at the very end. Read the page top to bottom to prove it moved — it must appear once, at the end.

## Paper clipboard

Write three sentences on three slips of paper in the wrong order. Move the slips into the right order without rewriting any of them. You have done by hand what cut and paste does: the words themselves travelled.
