# Video script — Cut and paste — moving text

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This paragraph stands in the wrong place.
Do not copy it. Move it.

Select it. Right-click — Cut. Gone!
It waits on the clipboard.

Put the insertion point where it belongs.
Paste. There it stands — in one place only.

Copy makes a twin. Cut makes a move.
