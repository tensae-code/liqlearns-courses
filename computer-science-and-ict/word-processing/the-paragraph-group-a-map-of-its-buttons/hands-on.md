# Hands-on — The Paragraph group — a map of its buttons

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Map drill

In pairs: one reads a command name from the picture, the other points to the button on a real Word window and says its job. Work through all fourteen, then swap.

## Shortcut cards

Write the five shortcuts from this lesson on cards — Ctrl+L, Ctrl+E, Ctrl+R, Ctrl+J, Ctrl+* — and test each other until all five are instant.
