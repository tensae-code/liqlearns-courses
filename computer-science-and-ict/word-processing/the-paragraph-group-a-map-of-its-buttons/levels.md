# The Paragraph group — a map of its buttons — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner names every command in the Paragraph group with its job, and gives the four alignment shortcuts and the Show/Hide shortcut from memory. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners of the same skill — more examples to sort, longer sequences to put in order, trickier cases of the same idea — never by a new topic.
