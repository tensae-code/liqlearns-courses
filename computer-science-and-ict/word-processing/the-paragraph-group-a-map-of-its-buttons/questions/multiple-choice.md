# The Paragraph group — a map of its buttons — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The Paragraph group lives on the —
a) File tab  b) Home tab  c) View tab  d) status bar
**Answer: b) Home tab.**

**2.** Sort arranges selected text —
a) by colour  b) in ascending or descending order  c) by size only  d) randomly
**Answer: b) in ascending or descending order.**

**3.** Shading —
a) deletes a line  b) colours a line's background, margin to margin  c) prints a line  d) counts lines
**Answer: b) colours a line's background, margin to margin.**

**4.** Justify is pressed with —
a) Ctrl+L  b) Ctrl+E  c) Ctrl+R  d) Ctrl+J
**Answer: d) Ctrl+J.**

**5.** Show/Hide shows or hides —
a) the Ribbon  b) paragraph marks  c) page numbers  d) pictures
**Answer: b) paragraph marks.**

**6.** Centre alignment is pressed with —
a) Ctrl+E  b) Ctrl+L  c) Ctrl+J  d) Ctrl+*
**Answer: a) Ctrl+E.**
