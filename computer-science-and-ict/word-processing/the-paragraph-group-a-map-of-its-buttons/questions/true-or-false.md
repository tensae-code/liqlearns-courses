# The Paragraph group — a map of its buttons — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** The Multilevel List button gives a list levels.
**Answer: True — lines inside lines, at different levels.**

**2.** Decrease Indent moves a paragraph further in.
**Answer: False — Decrease Indent moves it out; Increase Indent moves it in.**

**3.** Show/Hide is pressed with Ctrl+*.
**Answer: True.**
