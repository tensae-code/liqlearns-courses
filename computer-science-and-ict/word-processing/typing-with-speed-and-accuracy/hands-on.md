# Hands-on — Typing with Speed and Accuracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One-minute count
Type a paragraph from the home keys for exactly one minute. Count the words — that is your wpm. Repeat after a week of practice and compare.

## Accuracy first
Type the same paragraph slowly, aiming for zero errors. Mark every error, work out your accuracy share, then type it again a little faster without losing accuracy.
