# Typing with Speed and Accuracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does wpm mean?**
a) Words per minute  b) 20 wpm  c) 30 wpm  d) 40 wpm
**Answer: a) Words per minute**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is Papua New Guinea's Grade 9 speed benchmark?**
a) 30 wpm  b) 20 wpm  c) 40 wpm  d) Regular practice from the home keys
**Answer: b) 20 wpm**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Grade 10 speed benchmark?**
a) 40 wpm  b) Regular practice from the home keys  c) 30 wpm  d) The share of the typing that is correct
**Answer: c) 30 wpm**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Grade 11 speed benchmark?**
a) Regular practice from the home keys  b) The share of the typing that is correct  c) 80% accuracy  d) 40 wpm
**Answer: d) 40 wpm**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does typing speed grow from?**
a) Regular practice from the home keys  b) The share of the typing that is correct  c) 80% accuracy  d) 85–100% accuracy
**Answer: a) Regular practice from the home keys**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is accuracy in typing?**
a) 80% accuracy  b) The share of the typing that is correct  c) 85–100% accuracy  d) Because fast typing full of errors is not good typing
**Answer: b) The share of the typing that is correct**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the Grade 9 accuracy benchmark?**
a) 85–100% accuracy  b) Because fast typing full of errors is not good typing  c) 80% accuracy  d) Correct it and keep the rhythm
**Answer: c) 80% accuracy**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What accuracy range does Grade 11 ask for?**
a) Because fast typing full of errors is not good typing  b) Correct it and keep the rhythm  c) Words per minute  d) 85–100% accuracy
**Answer: d) 85–100% accuracy**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why are speed and accuracy measured together?**
a) Because fast typing full of errors is not good typing  b) Correct it and keep the rhythm  c) Words per minute  d) 20 wpm
**Answer: a) Because fast typing full of errors is not good typing**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What should you do when you make an error in practice?**
a) Words per minute  b) Correct it and keep the rhythm  c) 20 wpm  d) 30 wpm
**Answer: b) Correct it and keep the rhythm**
