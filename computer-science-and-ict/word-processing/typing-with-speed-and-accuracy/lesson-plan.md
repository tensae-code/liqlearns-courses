## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Speed — words per minute** — Typing speed is counted in **words per minute (wpm)** — how many words are typed in one minute. Papua New Guinea's benchmarks rise by grade: **20 wpm in Grade 9**, **30 wpm in Grade 10** and **40 wpm in Grade 11**. Speed grows from **regular practice starting at the home keys**, not from hunting for keys faster.
2. **Accuracy — typing without errors** — **Accuracy** is the share of the typing that is **correct**. The benchmarks pair it with speed every time: **80% accuracy in Grade 9**, **75–90% in Grade 10** and **85–100% in Grade 11**. Both are measured together because fast typing full of errors is not good typing; when you make an error in practice, correct it and keep the rhythm.
