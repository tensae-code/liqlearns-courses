# Video script — Diagrams and Schemes in a Document

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **scheme** draws **how a thing is organised**: the whole at the top or the start, its **parts in boxes** under or after it, and **connecting lines or arrows** showing what belongs to what and what follows what. A scheme of a lesson shows its units and sub-units; a scheme of a story shows its parts in order. The scheme is not decoration — it is the structure itself, made visible, and it is read by following the connections, not line by line.
The word processor builds a scheme from its **drawing shapes**: choose a shape for each box, place the boxes in their levels, write the part's name inside each, and join them with **connector lines or arrows**. Order matters: draw the whole first, then its parts under it, then the connections — a box added before its place is known makes tangles. Cuba teaches schemes inside the word processor, not a drawing program, because the scheme belongs inside the document it explains.
