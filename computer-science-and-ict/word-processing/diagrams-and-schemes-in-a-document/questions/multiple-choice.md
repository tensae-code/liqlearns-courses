# Diagrams and Schemes in a Document — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a scheme show?**
a) How a thing is organised — its structure  b) Boxes  c) What belongs to what and what follows what  d) By following the connections, not line by line
**Answer: a) How a thing is organised — its structure**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What holds the parts in a scheme?**
a) What belongs to what and what follows what  b) Boxes  c) By following the connections, not line by line  d) Its units and sub-units, drawn
**Answer: b) Boxes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do the lines or arrows show?**
a) By following the connections, not line by line  b) Its units and sub-units, drawn  c) What belongs to what and what follows what  d) Its drawing shapes
**Answer: c) What belongs to what and what follows what**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a scheme read?**
a) Its units and sub-units, drawn  b) Its drawing shapes  c) Inside its box  d) By following the connections, not line by line
**Answer: d) By following the connections, not line by line**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a scheme of a lesson?**
a) Its units and sub-units, drawn  b) Its drawing shapes  c) Inside its box  d) Connector lines or arrows
**Answer: a) Its units and sub-units, drawn**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are a scheme's boxes made from in the word processor?**
a) Inside its box  b) Its drawing shapes  c) Connector lines or arrows  d) The whole first, then its parts, then the connections
**Answer: b) Its drawing shapes**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where does each part's name go?**
a) Connector lines or arrows  b) The whole first, then its parts, then the connections  c) Inside its box  d) Because the scheme belongs inside the document it explains
**Answer: c) Inside its box**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What joins the boxes?**
a) The whole first, then its parts, then the connections  b) Because the scheme belongs inside the document it explains  c) How a thing is organised — its structure  d) Connector lines or arrows
**Answer: d) Connector lines or arrows**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what order is a scheme built?**
a) The whole first, then its parts, then the connections  b) Because the scheme belongs inside the document it explains  c) How a thing is organised — its structure  d) Boxes
**Answer: a) The whole first, then its parts, then the connections**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Cuba teach schemes in the word processor?**
a) How a thing is organised — its structure  b) Because the scheme belongs inside the document it explains  c) Boxes  d) What belongs to what and what follows what
**Answer: b) Because the scheme belongs inside the document it explains**
