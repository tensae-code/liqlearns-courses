# Diagrams and Schemes in a Document — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a scheme show?**
**Answer: How a thing is organised — its structure**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What holds the parts in a scheme?**
**Answer: Boxes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do the lines or arrows show?**
**Answer: What belongs to what and what follows what**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a scheme read?**
**Answer: By following the connections, not line by line**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a scheme of a lesson?**
**Answer: Its units and sub-units, drawn**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are a scheme's boxes made from in the word processor?**
**Answer: Its drawing shapes**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where does each part's name go?**
**Answer: Inside its box**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What joins the boxes?**
**Answer: Connector lines or arrows**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what order is a scheme built?**
**Answer: The whole first, then its parts, then the connections**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Cuba teach schemes in the word processor?**
**Answer: Because the scheme belongs inside the document it explains**
