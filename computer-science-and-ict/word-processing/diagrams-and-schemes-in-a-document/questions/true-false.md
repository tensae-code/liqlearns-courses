# Diagrams and Schemes in a Document — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a scheme show?" is: How a thing is organised — its structure**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What holds the parts in a scheme?" is: What belongs to what and what follows what**
**Answer: False** — Boxes

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do the lines or arrows show?" is: What belongs to what and what follows what**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is a scheme read?" is: Its units and sub-units, drawn**
**Answer: False** — By following the connections, not line by line

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a scheme of a lesson?" is: Its units and sub-units, drawn**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What are a scheme's boxes made from in the word processor?" is: Inside its box**
**Answer: False** — Its drawing shapes

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does each part's name go?" is: Inside its box**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What joins the boxes?" is: The whole first, then its parts, then the connections**
**Answer: False** — Connector lines or arrows

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "In what order is a scheme built?" is: The whole first, then its parts, then the connections**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why does Cuba teach schemes in the word processor?" is: How a thing is organised — its structure**
**Answer: False** — Because the scheme belongs inside the document it explains
