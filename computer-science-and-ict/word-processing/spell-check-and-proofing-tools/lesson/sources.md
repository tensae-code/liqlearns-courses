**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Uganda, Fiji, Sri Lanka, Rwanda).**

Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Uganda, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked first: Word Processing holds lessons on typing, formatting, saving and tables, but none teaches find, replace, spell check and proofreading as its own lesson. The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — word-processing/spell-check-and-proofing-tools). It sits in the Word Processing title (word-processing/). It has two sub lessons: locating and replacing text first, then the checkers and the final proofread — two distinct sequential parts of cleaning one document, each opening by proving the one before.

Uganda's own way: a slim activity-led prototype — every topic is an "Activity" learners do (assembling a real desktop, managing the Recycle Bin, word-processing a letter as the integration task), under Uganda's revised Lower Secondary Curriculum; the teacher's guide mirrors the learner's book chapter by chapter with teacher preparation, instructions and possible responses for every activity, plus chapter summaries — the facilitator model of the revised curriculum. This lesson keeps that shape: the learner does the thing with a real tool or document in front of them, and the guide's rows are stocked as labelled evidence only.

Fiji's own way (Years 7–8 Computer Education student book): A practical, exercise-driven primary book built around a class "students folder" of set files (work is saved as U<unit>-Ex<n>-<FirstName>); local Fijian names and schools throughout (e.g. Babasiga Secondary School), Internet searching is practised on the "Map of Fiji" with kids' search engines, and a full unit covers computers in Fijian society including Braille keyboards for blind learners. PARTIAL: the PDF as received has no cover, title page, contents or imprint — the PARTIAL label travels with every citation of this book.

Sri Lanka's own way (Grade 12 Science for Technology, Tamil): the A/L technology-stream ICT volume teaches every office skill twice — once in Microsoft Office 2010 and once in LibreOffice (Writer/Calc/Impress 4.1) — and closes with creating a real Gmail account, mailbox folders, attachments and password safety; hardware goes down to cache levels L1/L2/L3, ROM types and file systems (FAT/NTFS/ext4).

Rwanda's own way (S1 ICT Student Book, KLB): competency-based units each opening with a Key Unit Competency and keywords; a full GIS/ArcGIS unit and a Scratch music-making unit sit inside the first secondary year, and ICT use is grounded in Rwandan daily life.

Rwanda's own way (S1 ICT Teacher Guide, KLB): the guide mirrors the student units with content maps, additional teacher content per unit, SEN/multi-ability support, sample lesson plans (Appendix 1) and a glossary (Appendix 2); assessment is framed by Rwanda's competence-based curriculum.
