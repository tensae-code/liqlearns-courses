# Hands-on — Spell Check and Proofing Tools

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Find and fix it everywhere

In a practice document, find every appearance of one misspelled name. Decide: step-by-step replace or replace-all? Do the fix, then find the name again to prove no wrong spelling remains.

## Checker, then reader

Run the spell and grammar checker on a document and write down each flag you accepted and each you rejected, with why. Then proofread the same document slowly and list anything the checker missed.
