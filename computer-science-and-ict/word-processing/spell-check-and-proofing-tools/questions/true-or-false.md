# Spell Check and Proofing Tools — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Find can show every appearance of a word, one after another.**
**Answer: True** — It jumps from appearance to appearance, however long the document.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Replace-all is the safe choice whenever you are unsure.**
**Answer: False** — When unsure, replace step by step; replace-all is for when every appearance should change.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) If the spell checker flags a person's name, the name must be wrong.**
**Answer: False** — Its dictionary may not know the name; a flag is a question, not a verdict.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A grammar checker can flag a sentence where the wording breaks a rule it knows.**
**Answer: True** — Like spelling flags, its flags are questions for the writer to judge.
