# Spell Check and Proofing Tools — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A name is misspelled the same way in six places in a letter. Which replace do you use, and what do you do first?**
**Answer:** First use Find to look at the appearances and confirm every one is the same mistake; then replace-all is the right tool, because every appearance should change to the correct spelling.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the checker run not the finish of cleaning a document?**
**Answer:** Because the checkers only flag what they doubt: they pass correctly spelled wrong words, flag right words their dictionary does not know, and cannot judge meaning — so the writer must still proofread the document for sense.
