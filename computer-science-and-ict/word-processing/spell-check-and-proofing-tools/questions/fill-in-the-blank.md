# Spell Check and Proofing Tools — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) To locate a specific word in a long document, you use ____.**
**Answer: Find**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The checker's list of possible spellings for a flagged word is called its ____.**
**Answer: suggestions**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The writer's own final read of a document, for meaning, is called ____.**
**Answer: proofreading**
