# Hands-on — Inserting Objects — WordArt, Text Boxes, Charts, SmartArt and Drop Caps

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Five objects, one page', 'Build one leaflet page holding all five: a WordArt title, a text-box note, a small chart, a SmartArt cycle, and a drop-cap opening. Label each object with its office.')

## Task two
('Chart from data', 'Take a four-row data table of your own. Insert its chart, then change one number in the table and record what the chart does.')
