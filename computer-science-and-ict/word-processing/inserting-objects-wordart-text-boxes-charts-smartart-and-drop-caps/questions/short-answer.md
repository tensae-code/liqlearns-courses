# Inserting Objects — WordArt, Text Boxes, Charts, SmartArt and Drop Caps — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does WordArt turn a title into?**
**Answer: An object — letters filled, outlined, bent and shadowed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a text box free words from?**
**Answer: The paragraph column — boxed text placed anywhere**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do a leaflet's side-notes and captions live?**
**Answer: In text boxes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) From what is an inserted chart built?**
**Answer: Numbers the document owns**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the book's leaflet chart?**
**Answer: Air-pollution sources**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does SmartArt draw?**
**Answer: Relations — lists, cycles, hierarchies — as ready diagrams**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a drop cap?**
**Answer: The first letter enlarged to span lines, opening the text**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the toolkit's five offices.**
**Answer: Display, placement, evidence, structure and honour**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What law comes with the object's freedom?**
**Answer: The designer alone answers for where it stands**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which project uses all five?**
**Answer: Mongolia's Grade 8 Ulaanbaatar air-pollution leaflet**
