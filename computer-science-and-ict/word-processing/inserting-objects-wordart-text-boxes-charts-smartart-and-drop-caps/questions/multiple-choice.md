# Inserting Objects — WordArt, Text Boxes, Charts, SmartArt and Drop Caps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does WordArt turn a title into?**
a) An object — letters filled, outlined, bent and shadowed  b) The paragraph column — boxed text placed anywhere  c) In text boxes  d) Numbers the document owns
**Answer: a) An object — letters filled, outlined, bent and shadowed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a text box free words from?**
a) In text boxes  b) The paragraph column — boxed text placed anywhere  c) Numbers the document owns  d) Air-pollution sources
**Answer: b) The paragraph column — boxed text placed anywhere**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do a leaflet's side-notes and captions live?**
a) Numbers the document owns  b) Air-pollution sources  c) In text boxes  d) Relations — lists, cycles, hierarchies — as ready diagrams
**Answer: c) In text boxes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) From what is an inserted chart built?**
a) Air-pollution sources  b) Relations — lists, cycles, hierarchies — as ready diagrams  c) The first letter enlarged to span lines, opening the text  d) Numbers the document owns
**Answer: d) Numbers the document owns**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the book's leaflet chart?**
