# Hands-on — The Word Toolbars

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hover and name
In Word, rest the pointer on every button of the Standard toolbar and write down each tooltip name in order. Then do the same for the Formatting toolbar. Cover your list and test yourself from the pictures alone.

## Menu or button?
Take six commands — Save, Bold, Open, Underline, Print, Paste. For each, find it in its menu and as a toolbar button, and say which toolbar it lives on and why it belongs there.
