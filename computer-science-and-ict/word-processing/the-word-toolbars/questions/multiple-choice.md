# The Word Toolbars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a toolbar?**
a) A row of buttons, each a picture of the job it does  b) So the commonest commands are one click away, not buried in menus  c) The Standard toolbar and the Formatting toolbar  d) From the View menu
**Answer: a) A row of buttons, each a picture of the job it does**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why do toolbars exist?**
a) The Standard toolbar and the Formatting toolbar  b) So the commonest commands are one click away, not buried in menus  c) From the View menu  d) Rest the pointer on it until its tooltip label appears
**Answer: b) So the commonest commands are one click away, not buried in menus**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which two toolbars matter in the book's Word?**
a) From the View menu  b) Rest the pointer on it until its tooltip label appears  c) The Standard toolbar and the Formatting toolbar  d) File and editing commands — New, Open, Save, Print, Cut, Copy, Paste, Undo
**Answer: c) The Standard toolbar and the Formatting toolbar**
