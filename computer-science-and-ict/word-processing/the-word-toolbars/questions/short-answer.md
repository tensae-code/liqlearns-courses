# The Word Toolbars — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a toolbar?**
**Answer: A row of buttons, each a picture of the job it does**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why do toolbars exist?**
**Answer: So the commonest commands are one click away, not buried in menus**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which two toolbars matter in the book's Word?**
**Answer: The Standard toolbar and the Formatting toolbar**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a toolbar shown or hidden?**
**Answer: From the View menu**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How do you learn an unknown button's name?**
**Answer: Rest the pointer on it until its tooltip label appears**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the Standard toolbar carry?**
**Answer: File and editing commands — New, Open, Save, Print, Cut, Copy, Paste, Undo**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the Formatting toolbar carry?**
**Answer: Font, size, Bold, Italic, Underline, alignment, lists, borders and colours**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the split between the two toolbars?**
**Answer: Standard works on the document and your place in it; Formatting changes how selected text looks**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Are toolbar buttons different commands from the menus?**
**Answer: No — the same commands as the File and Edit menus, as buttons**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What should you never do with an unknown button?**
**Answer: Guess it — hover first and read its name**
