# Video script — The Word Toolbars

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **toolbar** is a row of **buttons**, each button a picture of the job it does.
Toolbars exist because the commonest commands deserve to be one click away, not buried in menus.
In the book's Word the two toolbars that matter are the **Standard toolbar** and the **Formatting toolbar**, sitting under the menu bar.
A toolbar can be shown or hidden from the View menu, and if you do not know a button, you rest the mouse pointer on it and a **tooltip** — a small label — appears with its name.
Never guess a button: hover first.
The **Standard toolbar** is the working toolbar: New, Open, Save, Print, Print Preview, Cut, Copy, Paste, Undo and the rest of the file-and-editing jobs — the same commands the File and Edit menus hold, as buttons.
The **Formatting toolbar** is the appearance toolbar: font name and size, Bold, Italic, Underline, alignment, bullets and numbering, borders and colours — everything that changes how the text looks.
The split to remember: Standard changes the document as a whole and your place in it; Formatting changes how the selected text looks.
