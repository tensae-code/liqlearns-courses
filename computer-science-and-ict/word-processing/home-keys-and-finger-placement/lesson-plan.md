## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The home keys** — The **home keys** are the middle row keys your fingers rest on: **A S D F** for the left hand and **J K L ;** for the right hand. The two **index (pointing) fingers** start on **F and J**, which carry small raised bumps so the fingers can find home **without looking**. The **thumbs rest on the space bar**.
2. **Which finger presses which keys** — **Each finger owns a column of keys**: the left little finger owns A, the right index finger owns the J column, and so on. After pressing a key away from home, the finger **returns to its home key** at once. Keeping the fingers on the home keys is what makes every key quick and accurate to reach.
