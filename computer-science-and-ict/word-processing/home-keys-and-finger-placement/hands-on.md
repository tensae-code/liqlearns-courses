# Hands-on — Home Keys and Finger Placement

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find home blind
Rest your fingers on the home row. Close your eyes, lift both hands, and put them back — find F and J by their bumps alone. Repeat five times.

## Columns drill
Say a key aloud (R, U, V, M). Your companion names the finger that owns its column, then presses it and returns to home.
