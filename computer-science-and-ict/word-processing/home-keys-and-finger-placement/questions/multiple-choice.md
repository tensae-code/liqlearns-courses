# Home Keys and Finger Placement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the home keys for the left hand?**
a) A S D F  b) J K L ;  c) On the space bar  d) The two index (pointing) fingers
**Answer: a) A S D F**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the home keys for the right hand?**
a) On the space bar  b) J K L ;  c) The two index (pointing) fingers  d) So fingers can find home without looking
**Answer: b) J K L ;**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the thumbs rest?**
a) The two index (pointing) fingers  b) So fingers can find home without looking  c) On the space bar  d) The left little finger
**Answer: c) On the space bar**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which fingers start on F and J?**
a) So fingers can find home without looking  b) The left little finger  c) The right index finger  d) The two index (pointing) fingers
**Answer: d) The two index (pointing) fingers**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why do F and J carry raised bumps?**
a) So fingers can find home without looking  b) The left little finger  c) The right index finger  d) Back to its home key
**Answer: a) So fingers can find home without looking**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which finger presses A?**
a) The right index finger  b) The left little finger  c) Back to its home key  d) A thumb
**Answer: b) The left little finger**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which finger owns the J column of keys?**
a) Back to its home key  b) A thumb  c) The right index finger  d) So every key is reached quickly and accurately
**Answer: c) The right index finger**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does a finger go after pressing a key away from home?**
a) A thumb  b) So every key is reached quickly and accurately  c) A S D F  d) Back to its home key
**Answer: d) Back to its home key**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which finger presses the space bar?**
a) A thumb  b) So every key is reached quickly and accurately  c) A S D F  d) J K L ;
**Answer: a) A thumb**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why keep the fingers on the home keys?**
a) A S D F  b) So every key is reached quickly and accurately  c) J K L ;  d) On the space bar
**Answer: b) So every key is reached quickly and accurately**
