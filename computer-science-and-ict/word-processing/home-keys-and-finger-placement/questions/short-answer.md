# Home Keys and Finger Placement — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the home keys for the left hand?**
**Answer: A S D F**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the home keys for the right hand?**
**Answer: J K L ;**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the thumbs rest?**
**Answer: On the space bar**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which fingers start on F and J?**
**Answer: The two index (pointing) fingers**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why do F and J carry raised bumps?**
**Answer: So fingers can find home without looking**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which finger presses A?**
**Answer: The left little finger**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which finger owns the J column of keys?**
**Answer: The right index finger**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does a finger go after pressing a key away from home?**
**Answer: Back to its home key**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which finger presses the space bar?**
**Answer: A thumb**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why keep the fingers on the home keys?**
**Answer: So every key is reached quickly and accurately**
