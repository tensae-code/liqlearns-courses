# Hands-on — Indent with the Ruler

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Marker trials

Take one paragraph. Drag each marker in turn and write down what moved. Reset between trials. By the third trial you should predict the result before you drag.

## Whole-document indent

Select a whole document with Ctrl+A and give every paragraph a first-line indent with one drag of the first-line marker. Count the paragraphs changed.
