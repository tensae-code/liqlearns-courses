# Indent with the Ruler

## Do it first

Open Word. If no ruler sits above your page, you will fix that in a moment. Look at the left end of the ruler space: small marker shapes wait there. This lesson names all three, then puts them to work.

## Show the ruler; the three markers

To show the ruler: **View tab, tick Ruler in the Show group**. Three markers sit at the ruler's left. The **First-line indent marker** moves **only the first line**. The **Hanging indent marker** moves **the rest** of the paragraph. The **Left indent marker** moves **both together**, indenting every line.

## The method

Select the paragraphs — **Ctrl+A selects a whole document**. Drag the chosen marker along the ruler. Release it — the paragraphs indent, each line following the marker that owns it. First-line marker: first lines move. Left indent marker: whole paragraphs move. Choose the marker by the result you want.
