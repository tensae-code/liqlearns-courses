# Video script — Indent with the Ruler

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

No ruler? View tab. Tick Ruler.
Three markers wait at the left.
Top triangle: first line only.
Bottom triangle: the rest.
Small box: both together.
Select the paragraphs. Drag. Release.
The lines follow the marker.
