# Hands-on — Borders and Shading for Text

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make a notice
Write a three-line notice in Word — a real one, for a class or a shop. Give its heading a bottom border only, and put the key sentence in a box with light shading. Print-preview it and judge: does the important part stand out, and is the shading light enough to read easily?

## Same words, two voices
Copy one plain paragraph twice. Leave the first untouched; give the second a border and shading. Show both to a partner without comment and ask which they read first, and which looks more important. Write one sentence on what the formatting did.
