# Borders and Shading for Text — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a border?**
**Answer: A line drawn around the outside of a paragraph, a piece of text, or a page**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where are borders set in the book's Word?**
**Answer: Format → Borders and Shading**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What border choices must be made?**
**Answer: Which sides, the line style, its width and colour, and text or paragraph**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is shading?**
**Answer: A colour filled in behind the text**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the dialog's preview give you?**
**Answer: The result, seen whole before you commit to it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What classic pair makes a notice unmissable?**
**Answer: A light shading with a border around it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What discipline do borders and shading demand?**
**Answer: Restraint**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when everything is highlighted?**
**Answer: Nothing is highlighted**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How should a border be used, like a voice?**
**Answer: Rarely, briefly, and only where it matters**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name a job for borders and shading.**
**Answer: Making a heading, notice, warning or key sentence stand out**
