# Borders and Shading for Text — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a border?**
a) A line drawn around the outside of a paragraph, a piece of text, or a page  b) Format → Borders and Shading  c) Which sides, the line style, its width and colour, and text or paragraph  d) A colour filled in behind the text
**Answer: a) A line drawn around the outside of a paragraph, a piece of text, or a page**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where are borders set in the book's Word?**
a) Which sides, the line style, its width and colour, and text or paragraph  b) Format → Borders and Shading  c) A colour filled in behind the text  d) The result, seen whole before you commit to it
**Answer: b) Format → Borders and Shading**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What border choices must be made?**
a) A colour filled in behind the text  b) The result, seen whole before you commit to it  c) Which sides, the line style, its width and colour, and text or paragraph  d) A light shading with a border around it
