# Finding Text with Search in Word — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does Find do?**
**Answer: Looks through the whole document for the word or phrase you type**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is Find opened from?**
**Answer: The Edit menu, or with Ctrl+F**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does Word show a found word?**
**Answer: It jumps to it and shows it selected in the text**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why can Word promise not to miss an occurrence?**
**Answer: Because it compares every character in the document against the search text**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does Find Next do?**
**Answer: Takes you to each further occurrence in turn**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How do you know you have seen them all?**
**Answer: Word reports that it has finished searching the document**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does Replace do?**
**Answer: Changes each found occurrence into corrected text**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does Replace All do?**
**Answer: Changes all occurrences at once, in one pass**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the care rule for Replace All?**
**Answer: Check a few replacements with your own eyes first**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why can Replace All go wrong?**
**Answer: Word replaces exactly what you typed, including anywhere you did not mean**
