# Finding Text with Search in Word — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does Find do?**
a) Looks through the whole document for the word or phrase you type  b) The Edit menu, or with Ctrl+F  c) It jumps to it and shows it selected in the text  d) Because it compares every character in the document against the search text
**Answer: a) Looks through the whole document for the word or phrase you type**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is Find opened from?**
a) It jumps to it and shows it selected in the text  b) The Edit menu, or with Ctrl+F  c) Because it compares every character in the document against the search text  d) Takes you to each further occurrence in turn
**Answer: b) The Edit menu, or with Ctrl+F**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does Word show a found word?**
a) Because it compares every character in the document against the search text  b) Takes you to each further occurrence in turn  c) It jumps to it and shows it selected in the text  d) Word reports that it has finished searching the document
**Answer: c) It jumps to it and shows it selected in the text**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why can Word promise not to miss an occurrence?**
a) Takes you to each further occurrence in turn  b) Word reports that it has finished searching the document  c) Changes each found occurrence into corrected text  d) Because it compares every character in the document against the search text
**Answer: d) Because it compares every character in the document against the search text**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does Find Next do?**
a) Takes you to each further occurrence in turn  b) Word reports that it has finished searching the document  c) Changes each found occurrence into corrected text  d) Changes all occurrences at once, in one pass
**Answer: a) Takes you to each further occurrence in turn**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How do you know you have seen them all?**
a) Changes each found occurrence into corrected text  b) Word reports that it has finished searching the document  c) Changes all occurrences at once, in one pass  d) Check a few replacements with your own eyes first
**Answer: b) Word reports that it has finished searching the document**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does Replace do?**
a) Changes all occurrences at once, in one pass  b) Check a few replacements with your own eyes first  c) Changes each found occurrence into corrected text  d) Word replaces exactly what you typed, including anywhere you did not mean
**Answer: c) Changes each found occurrence into corrected text**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does Replace All do?**
a) Check a few replacements with your own eyes first  b) Word replaces exactly what you typed, including anywhere you did not mean  c) Looks through the whole document for the word or phrase you type  d) Changes all occurrences at once, in one pass
**Answer: d) Changes all occurrences at once, in one pass**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the care rule for Replace All?**
a) Check a few replacements with your own eyes first  b) Word replaces exactly what you typed, including anywhere you did not mean  c) Looks through the whole document for the word or phrase you type  d) The Edit menu, or with Ctrl+F
**Answer: a) Check a few replacements with your own eyes first**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why can Replace All go wrong?**
a) Looks through the whole document for the word or phrase you type  b) Word replaces exactly what you typed, including anywhere you did not mean  c) The Edit menu, or with Ctrl+F  d) It jumps to it and shows it selected in the text
**Answer: b) Word replaces exactly what you typed, including anywhere you did not mean**
