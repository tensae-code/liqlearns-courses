# Hands-on — Finding Text with Search in Word

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Beat the search
Take a long document. Time a partner finding three occurrences of a chosen word by reading; then find the same word with Find and Find Next. Compare times — and say which method can promise it missed none.

## Find and fix
In a practice document with one word spelled wrong five times on purpose, use Find to visit each one, then Replace All to fix them. Search again for the wrong spelling to prove none are left.
