# Making a numbered list

## Do it first

Take the bulleted list from the last lesson. Turn it into a numbered list three times over — once counted 1, 2, 3, once lettered A, B, C, once in Roman numerals. Then answer this: what do you do when a list must start counting again from 1 in the middle of a document? This lesson holds the answer.

## Choosing the style

The same text is reformatted as a numbered list by choosing a **numbering style from the Numbering command's options** — **numbers, letters or Roman numerals** — with the same **live preview** the Bullets command gave: hover, watch the list change, click to choose.

[Picture 13320 — one list, numbered three ways]

## Restarting the count

Numbering can be **restarted**: **right-click the list and choose the restart option**. The rule holds for **numeric and alphabetical lists alike** — wherever a fresh count must begin, the restart begins it, and the lines after it count on from the new start.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion how a numbered list is made, the three style families, and how numbering is restarted.
