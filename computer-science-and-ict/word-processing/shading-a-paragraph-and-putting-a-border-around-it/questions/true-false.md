# Shading a Paragraph and Putting a Border Around It — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does shading fill?" is: The area behind a paragraph or piece of text, with colour**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What happens to the words themselves?" is: A light shade — dark shading swallows dark letters**
**Answer: False** — They stay the same — the ground under them changes

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What shade should be chosen, and why?" is: A light shade — dark shading swallows dark letters**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What happens if everything is shaded?" is: A frame of lines around a paragraph**
**Answer: False** — Nothing stands out

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a border?" is: A frame of lines around a paragraph**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Must a border be on all sides?" is: A paragraph that reads as a separate notice**
**Answer: False** — No — only where you choose

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do border and shading make together?" is: A paragraph that reads as a separate notice**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name three things set apart this way." is: A notice pinned on a board**
**Answer: False** — Warnings, rules and announcements

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the framed paragraph compared to?" is: A notice pinned on a board**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which paragraph should be dressed this way?" is: The area behind a paragraph or piece of text, with colour**
**Answer: False** — The one that must stand out
