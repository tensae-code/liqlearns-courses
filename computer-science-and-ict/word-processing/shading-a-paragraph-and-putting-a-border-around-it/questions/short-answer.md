# Shading a Paragraph and Putting a Border Around It — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does shading fill?**
**Answer: The area behind a paragraph or piece of text, with colour**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to the words themselves?**
**Answer: They stay the same — the ground under them changes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What shade should be chosen, and why?**
**Answer: A light shade — dark shading swallows dark letters**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens if everything is shaded?**
**Answer: Nothing stands out**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a border?**
**Answer: A frame of lines around a paragraph**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Must a border be on all sides?**
**Answer: No — only where you choose**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do border and shading make together?**
**Answer: A paragraph that reads as a separate notice**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name three things set apart this way.**
**Answer: Warnings, rules and announcements**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the framed paragraph compared to?**
**Answer: A notice pinned on a board**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which paragraph should be dressed this way?**
**Answer: The one that must stand out**
