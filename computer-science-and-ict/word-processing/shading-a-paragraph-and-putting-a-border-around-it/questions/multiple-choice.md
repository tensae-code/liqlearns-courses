# Shading a Paragraph and Putting a Border Around It — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does shading fill?**
a) The area behind a paragraph or piece of text, with colour  b) Nothing stands out  c) No — only where you choose  d) Warnings, rules and announcements
**Answer: a) The area behind a paragraph or piece of text, with colour**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to the words themselves?**
a) A frame of lines around a paragraph  b) They stay the same — the ground under them changes  c) A paragraph that reads as a separate notice  d) A notice pinned on a board
**Answer: b) They stay the same — the ground under them changes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What shade should be chosen, and why?**
a) No — only where you choose  b) Warnings, rules and announcements  c) A light shade — dark shading swallows dark letters  d) The one that must stand out
**Answer: c) A light shade — dark shading swallows dark letters**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens if everything is shaded?**
a) A paragraph that reads as a separate notice  b) A notice pinned on a board  c) The area behind a paragraph or piece of text, with colour  d) Nothing stands out
**Answer: d) Nothing stands out**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a border?**
a) A frame of lines around a paragraph  b) Warnings, rules and announcements  c) The one that must stand out  d) They stay the same — the ground under them changes
**Answer: a) A frame of lines around a paragraph**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Must a border be on all sides?**
a) A notice pinned on a board  b) No — only where you choose  c) The area behind a paragraph or piece of text, with colour  d) A light shade — dark shading swallows dark letters
**Answer: b) No — only where you choose**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do border and shading make together?**
a) The one that must stand out  b) They stay the same — the ground under them changes  c) A paragraph that reads as a separate notice  d) Nothing stands out
**Answer: c) A paragraph that reads as a separate notice**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name three things set apart this way.**
a) The area behind a paragraph or piece of text, with colour  b) A light shade — dark shading swallows dark letters  c) A frame of lines around a paragraph  d) Warnings, rules and announcements
**Answer: d) Warnings, rules and announcements**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the framed paragraph compared to?**
a) A notice pinned on a board  b) They stay the same — the ground under them changes  c) Nothing stands out  d) No — only where you choose
**Answer: a) A notice pinned on a board**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which paragraph should be dressed this way?**
a) A light shade — dark shading swallows dark letters  b) The one that must stand out  c) A frame of lines around a paragraph  d) A paragraph that reads as a separate notice
**Answer: b) The one that must stand out**
