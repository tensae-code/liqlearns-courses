# Formulas in Word — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How is a formula in Word made?**
**Answer: Assembled — choose the pattern and fill its boxes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three formula patterns.**
**Answer: Fraction, power and root (or sum)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Is a formula typed like a sentence?**
**Answer: No — it is built from parts**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does a finished formula behave?**
**Answer: As one object — movable and resizable as a whole**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where do formulas sit in the Grade 6 Word course?**
**Answer: After tables, the last new tool before printing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where can a formula sit?**
**Answer: On its own line, or inside the sentence that names it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must the text around a formula say?**
**Answer: What its letters mean**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the lesson practise on?**
**Answer: Real sums from mathematics and physics homework**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why set homework formulas in Word?**
**Answer: So they can be printed and handed in**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two qualities make a formula read well?**
**Answer: It is placed well and explained**
