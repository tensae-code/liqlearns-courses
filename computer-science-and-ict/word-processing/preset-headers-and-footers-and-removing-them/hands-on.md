# Hands-on — Preset headers and footers — and removing them

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Gallery tour

Open the header gallery and the footer gallery. For three presets, list the placeholder fields each carries. Choose the one you would use for a research paper and say why.

## The full specification

Work the closing practical as a checklist: print the nine points, tick each as you apply it, and have a partner audit the finished document point by point before you save it into the Word Project folder.
