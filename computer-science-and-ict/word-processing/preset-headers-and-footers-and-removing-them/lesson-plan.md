# Lesson plan — Preset headers and footers — and removing them

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Word Processing · **Lesson:** Preset headers and footers — and removing them
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 5 — Word Processing in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 9 book, Unit 3 — Application Software. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Word offers ready-made header and footer designs with placeholder fields to type over — and a header or footer can be edited by double-clicking it, or removed outright from its own menu.
**This lesson's own level:** it completes the header-and-footer skill — your own header last lesson, ready-made designs now — and its last sub lesson is the Word block's closing practical, where the whole block is used at once.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Choosing a preset** — Insert → Header or Footer, choose a preset design; its Content Control placeholder fields (title, author, date, page number) are clicked and typed over
2. **Editing and removing** — a closed header/footer is locked but still visible — double-click to unlock and edit; removal is Header → Remove Header (likewise Footer → Remove Footer)
3. **The closing practical — the whole Word block at once** — Practical Exercise 3.5: centre the titles, Roman-numeral the lists, 1.5 line spacing, a page break, justification, Arial 12 throughout, an Arabic page number, a title header, a "Prepared by Grade 9 Students" footer — saved into a new Word Project folder in Documents

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Choose a preset header or footer and type over its Content Control placeholder fields.
2. Unlock and edit a closed header/footer by double-clicking it.
3. Remove a header and a footer from their own menus.
4. Complete the closing practical to its full nine-point specification.

## If the learner is stuck
- Typed beside a placeholder instead of over it → click the placeholder field itself; your typing replaces its standing words.
- Header still visible after closing → closed headers stay visible but locked; that is normal — double-click to edit, or Remove Header to take it off.
