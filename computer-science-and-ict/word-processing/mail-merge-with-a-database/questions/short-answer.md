# Mail Merge with a Database — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What two parts does a merge have?**
**Answer: The main document and the data source**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What stands where personal details go?**
**Answer: Merge fields**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are the data source's rows?**
**Answer: The recipients — one record each**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are its columns in merge language?**
**Answer: Fields, matching the merge fields**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Can either part work alone?**
**Answer: No — the merge needs both**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What should you do before finishing the merge?**
**Answer: Preview a few records**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does finishing produce?**
**Answer: One personalised copy per record**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two merge outputs besides letters.**
**Answer: Labels and envelopes**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What breaks a field match?**
**Answer: A mis-typed field name — names must match exactly**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which two themes of the book meet in mail merge?**
**Answer: Word processing and databases**
