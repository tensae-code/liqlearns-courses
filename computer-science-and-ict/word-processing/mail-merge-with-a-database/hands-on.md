# Hands-on — Mail Merge with a Database

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Merge by hand
Write a three-line invitation with two merge fields. Make a four-record data table and produce the four finished invitations by hand, exactly as the merge would.

## Catch the fault
A merged letter prints «Town» as a blank for every recipient. List two likely causes from the lesson and how you would check each.
