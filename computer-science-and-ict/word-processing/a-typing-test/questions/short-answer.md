# A Typing Test — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In a typing test, what do you copy?**
**Answer: A given text**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is a typing test measured?**
**Answer: It is timed**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do your fingers start in a typing test?**
**Answer: On the home keys**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Should you look at the keys during a test?**
**Answer: No — eyes on the text**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What two scores does a typing test give?**
**Answer: Speed (wpm) and accuracy**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) A result of 30 wpm means what?**
**Answer: 30 words were typed in one minute**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Accuracy of 90% means what?**
**Answer: 90 of every 100 were correct**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do you compare your test result with?**
**Answer: The grade benchmark**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) If accuracy is low, what practice fixes it?**
**Answer: Correct finger placement and a steady rhythm**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Does one typing test settle your level?**
**Answer: No — tests are repeated to show progress**
