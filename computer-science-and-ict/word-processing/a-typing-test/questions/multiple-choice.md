# A Typing Test — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In a typing test, what do you copy?**
a) A given text  b) It is timed  c) On the home keys  d) No — eyes on the text
**Answer: a) A given text**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is a typing test measured?**
a) On the home keys  b) It is timed  c) No — eyes on the text  d) Speed (wpm) and accuracy
**Answer: b) It is timed**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do your fingers start in a typing test?**
a) No — eyes on the text  b) Speed (wpm) and accuracy  c) On the home keys  d) 30 words were typed in one minute
**Answer: c) On the home keys**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Should you look at the keys during a test?**
a) Speed (wpm) and accuracy  b) 30 words were typed in one minute  c) 90 of every 100 were correct  d) No — eyes on the text
**Answer: d) No — eyes on the text**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What two scores does a typing test give?**
a) Speed (wpm) and accuracy  b) 30 words were typed in one minute  c) 90 of every 100 were correct  d) The grade benchmark
**Answer: a) Speed (wpm) and accuracy**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) A result of 30 wpm means what?**
a) 90 of every 100 were correct  b) 30 words were typed in one minute  c) The grade benchmark  d) Correct finger placement and a steady rhythm
**Answer: b) 30 words were typed in one minute**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Accuracy of 90% means what?**
a) The grade benchmark  b) Correct finger placement and a steady rhythm  c) 90 of every 100 were correct  d) No — tests are repeated to show progress
**Answer: c) 90 of every 100 were correct**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do you compare your test result with?**
a) Correct finger placement and a steady rhythm  b) No — tests are repeated to show progress  c) A given text  d) The grade benchmark
**Answer: d) The grade benchmark**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) If accuracy is low, what practice fixes it?**
a) Correct finger placement and a steady rhythm  b) No — tests are repeated to show progress  c) A given text  d) It is timed
**Answer: a) Correct finger placement and a steady rhythm**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Does one typing test settle your level?**
a) A given text  b) No — tests are repeated to show progress  c) It is timed  d) On the home keys
**Answer: b) No — tests are repeated to show progress**
