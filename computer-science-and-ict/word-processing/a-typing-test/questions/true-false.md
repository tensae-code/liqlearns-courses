# A Typing Test — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "In a typing test, what do you copy?" is: A given text**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is a typing test measured?" is: No — eyes on the text**
**Answer: False** — It is timed

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where do your fingers start in a typing test?" is: On the home keys**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Should you look at the keys during a test?" is: 30 words were typed in one minute**
**Answer: False** — No — eyes on the text

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What two scores does a typing test give?" is: Speed (wpm) and accuracy**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "A result of 30 wpm means what?" is: The grade benchmark**
**Answer: False** — 30 words were typed in one minute

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Accuracy of 90% means what?" is: 90 of every 100 were correct**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do you compare your test result with?" is: No — tests are repeated to show progress**
**Answer: False** — The grade benchmark

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "If accuracy is low, what practice fixes it?" is: Correct finger placement and a steady rhythm**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Does one typing test settle your level?" is: It is timed**
**Answer: False** — No — tests are repeated to show progress
