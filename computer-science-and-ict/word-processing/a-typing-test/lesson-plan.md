## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Taking a typing test** — In a typing test you **copy a given text**, starting with your fingers on the **home keys** and your **eyes on the text, not the keys**. The test is **timed**. A test always gives **two scores together: speed in words per minute, and accuracy**.
2. **Reading your test result** — A result of **30 wpm** means 30 words were typed in one minute; **90% accuracy** means 90 of every 100 were correct. You read your result **against the benchmark for your grade**. If accuracy is low, the practice to fix is **correct finger placement and a steady rhythm**. One test does not settle your level — tests are repeated to show progress.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
