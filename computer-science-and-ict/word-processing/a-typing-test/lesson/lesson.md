# A Typing Test

## Do it first

Twice in Papua New Guinea's guides the keyboarding unit ends the same way: a typing test. This lesson runs one properly — what you copy, how it is timed, and how to read the two scores it gives you.

## Sub lesson 1 — Taking a typing test

In a typing test you **copy a given text**, starting with your fingers on the **home keys** and your **eyes on the text, not the keys**. The test is **timed**. A test always gives **two scores together: speed in words per minute, and accuracy**.

[Picture — Taking a typing test]

## Sub lesson 2 — Reading your test result

A result of **30 wpm** means 30 words were typed in one minute; **90% accuracy** means 90 of every 100 were correct. You read your result **against the benchmark for your grade**. If accuracy is low, the practice to fix is **correct finger placement and a steady rhythm**. One test does not settle your level — tests are repeated to show progress.
