# Switching the Keyboard Language — Arabic and English — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What key pair switches the typing language?**
a) Alt+Shift  b) The language indicator on the taskbar  c) Left to right  d) To the correct side for the new language
**Answer: a) Alt+Shift**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which two languages does this lesson switch between?**
a) Right to left  b) Arabic and English  c) The text direction  d) Yes — each part in its own direction
**Answer: b) Arabic and English**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What changes on the keys when the language switches?**
a) Left to right  b) To the correct side for the new language  c) The letters the keys produce  d) Alt+Shift again
**Answer: c) The letters the keys produce**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where can you see which language is live?**
a) The text direction  b) Yes — each part in its own direction  c) Alt+Shift  d) The language indicator on the taskbar
**Answer: d) The language indicator on the taskbar**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which way does Arabic run?**
a) Right to left  b) To the correct side for the new language  c) Alt+Shift again  d) Arabic and English
**Answer: a) Right to left**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which way is English written?**
a) Yes — each part in its own direction  b) Left to right  c) Alt+Shift  d) The letters the keys produce
**Answer: b) Left to right**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What changes with the language?**
a) Alt+Shift again  b) Arabic and English  c) The text direction  d) The language indicator on the taskbar
**Answer: c) The text direction**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the writing point move when direction changes?**
a) Alt+Shift  b) The letters the keys produce  c) Right to left  d) To the correct side for the new language
**Answer: d) To the correct side for the new language**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Can one document hold both languages?**
a) Yes — each part in its own direction  b) Arabic and English  c) The language indicator on the taskbar  d) Left to right
**Answer: a) Yes — each part in its own direction**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do you press to switch back?**
a) The letters the keys produce  b) Alt+Shift again  c) Right to left  d) The text direction
**Answer: b) Alt+Shift again**
