# Hands-on — Switching the Keyboard Language — Arabic and English

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Switch and type
Type your name in Arabic, press Alt+Shift, and type it in English. Watch the indicator and the writing point each time you switch.

## Two directions, one line
Write one sentence in Arabic and one in English in the same document, each in its own direction. Read both aloud from their correct starting sides.
