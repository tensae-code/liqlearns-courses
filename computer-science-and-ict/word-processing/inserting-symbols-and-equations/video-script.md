# Video script — Inserting Symbols and Equations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The **Insert Symbol** gallery holds the characters the keyboard lacks: mathematical signs, arrows, currency marks, Greek letters, special punctuation. The act is threefold — open the gallery, find the sign (by its font's sets, where the special families live), and insert it as a true character of the text: once inserted, a symbol is text — it copies, formats and prints like any letter, because to the document it is one. The lesson's habit: a sign inserted is a sign owned; a sign drawn or pasted as a picture is a stranger in the text.
Formulas are not lines of text but **structures** — fractions with floors, roots with contents, powers raised — and Word builds them with the **Equation editor**: choose the structure (fraction, root, power, sum), then fill its boxes with numbers, letters and nested structures. Mongolia's pupils build the formulas of their other subjects — maths, physics and chemistry — as document objects that sit in the text, resize with its font, and can be re-opened and corrected. The distinction is the lesson: symbols are characters; equations are constructions.
