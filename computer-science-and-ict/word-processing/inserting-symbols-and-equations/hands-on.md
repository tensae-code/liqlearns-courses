# Hands-on — Inserting Symbols and Equations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Own the signs', 'Write a line needing five signs your keyboard lacks. Insert all five from the gallery, then copy the line and prove the signs travelled as text.')

## Task two
('Build the formula', 'Build the quadratic-formula or a chemistry equation in the Equation editor, set inside a sentence of explanation. Reopen it and change one value.')
