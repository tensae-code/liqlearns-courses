# Inserting Symbols and Equations — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the keyboard's missing signs live in Word?**
**Answer: In the Insert Symbol gallery**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name signs the gallery holds.**
**Answer: Mathematical signs, arrows, currency marks, Greek letters (any two)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Once inserted, what is a symbol to the document?**
**Answer: A true character of the text — it copies, formats and prints like a letter**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why must a formula not be typed as plain text?**
**Answer: A formula is a structure — fractions, roots and powers — not a line**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the Equation editor do?**
**Answer: Builds formulas from structures whose boxes are filled and nested**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which subjects' formulas does the book build?**
**Answer: Maths, physics and chemistry**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does an equation sit in the document?**
**Answer: As an object that resizes with the font and can be re-opened and corrected**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) State the lesson's distinction.**
**Answer: Symbols are characters; equations are constructions**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the habit for signs?**
**Answer: Insert them as characters, never paste them as pictures**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which project strand uses this?**
**Answer: Mongolia's Grade 9 Word strand — documents for the other school subjects**
