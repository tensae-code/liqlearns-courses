# Inserting Symbols and Equations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the keyboard's missing signs live in Word?**
a) In the Insert Symbol gallery  b) Mathematical signs, arrows, currency marks, Greek letters (any two)  c) A true character of the text — it copies, formats and prints like a letter  d) A formula is a structure — fractions, roots and powers — not a line
**Answer: a) In the Insert Symbol gallery**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name signs the gallery holds.**
a) A true character of the text — it copies, formats and prints like a letter  b) Mathematical signs, arrows, currency marks, Greek letters (any two)  c) A formula is a structure — fractions, roots and powers — not a line  d) Builds formulas from structures whose boxes are filled and nested
**Answer: b) Mathematical signs, arrows, currency marks, Greek letters (any two)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Once inserted, what is a symbol to the document?**
a) A formula is a structure — fractions, roots and powers — not a line  b) Builds formulas from structures whose boxes are filled and nested  c) A true character of the text — it copies, formats and prints like a letter  d) Maths, physics and chemistry
**Answer: c) A true character of the text — it copies, formats and prints like a letter**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why must a formula not be typed as plain text?**
