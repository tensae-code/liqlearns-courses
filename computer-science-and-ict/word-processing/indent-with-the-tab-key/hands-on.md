# Hands-on — Indent with the Tab key

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Two paragraphs

Type or open the two dam paragraphs. Indent each with the Tab key. Print or show the page: two first lines sitting in, the rest flush.

## Spot the jump

In pairs: one closes their eyes while the other indents one paragraph of a three-paragraph page. The first partner opens their eyes and points to the paragraph that changed.
