# Lesson plan — Indent with the Tab key

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Word Processing · **Lesson:** Indent with the Tab key
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 5 — Word Processing in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 9 book, Unit 3 — Application Software. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** put the cursor at the very start of a paragraph and press Tab — the paragraph's first line jumps in; that is the Tab Key method of indentation.
**This lesson's own level:** it is the first of the four indentation methods, and the quickest: one key, one paragraph.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Put the cursor at a paragraph's very start and indent it with one press of Tab.
2. Say what moved: the first line only.

## If the learner is stuck
- Pressed Tab in the middle of a line → the cursor must stand at the very start of the paragraph; in the middle, Tab just pushes the text along.
- Whole paragraph moved → that is a different method's work; Tab at the start moves the first line.
