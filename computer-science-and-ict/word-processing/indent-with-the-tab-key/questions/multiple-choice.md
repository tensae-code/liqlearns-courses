# Indent with the Tab key — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In the Tab Key method, the cursor goes —
a) at the end of the paragraph  b) at the very start of the paragraph  c) in the title  d) on the ruler
**Answer: b) at the very start of the paragraph.**

**2.** Pressing Tab at the start moves —
a) the whole paragraph  b) the first line only  c) the page  d) nothing
**Answer: b) the first line only.**

**3.** The worked text for this method is about —
a) a shopping list  b) the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam  c) a football match  d) the school timetable
**Answer: b) the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam.**

**4.** The dam stands on the —
a) White Nile  b) Blue Nile  c) Awash River  d) Lake Tana shore only
**Answer: b) Blue Nile — in Benishangul-Gumuz.**

**5.** The dam is owned by —
a) the city council  b) a private company abroad  c) Ethiopian Electric Power Corporation  d) the school
**Answer: c) Ethiopian Electric Power Corporation.**

**6.** How many presses of Tab indent one paragraph in this method?
a) One  b) Two  c) Five  d) Ten
**Answer: a) One.**
