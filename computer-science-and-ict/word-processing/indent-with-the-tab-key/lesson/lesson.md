# Indent with the Tab key

## Do it first

Open a document with two paragraphs about the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam — the dam on the Blue Nile in Benishangul-Gumuz, owned by Ethiopian Electric Power Corporation. Click at the very start of one paragraph. Press Tab. Watch what changed before this lesson names it.

## The method

For one paragraph, the whole method is this: **put the cursor at the very start of the paragraph and press Tab**. The first line jumps in from the margin. Nothing else moves.

## See what changed

Look carefully at the result: only the **first line** moved. The rest of the paragraph still starts at the margin. That is the Tab key's signature — a first-line indent, made with one press. Press it at the start of the second dam paragraph and give that paragraph the same start.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the Tab Key method in one breath: where the cursor goes, what you press, and what moves.

## Do It Yourself

1. Indent both dam paragraphs with the Tab key, one press each.
2. Say, from memory, which part of the paragraph the Tab key moves.
3. Remove one indent: click at the paragraph's start and press Backspace once — then put the indent back with Tab.
