# Merging Cells in a Word Table — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does merging do?** → **Joins two or more selected cells into one cell**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the joined cell span?** → **The space of all its parts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give a use for merging across the top.** → **A title cell stretched over the whole table**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Give a use for merging down a column.** → **One entry serving several rows**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the merged cells' contents go?** → **They gather into the single new cell**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What must selected cells be, to merge?** → **Together, in one block**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the command called?** → **Merge Cells (Table menu / right-click)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What reverses a merge?** → **Split Cells — dividing a cell back into rows and columns**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is a wrong merge fixed?** → **Split and re-merge — never by deleting the table**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?** → **Laos Grade 9 ICT, Chapter 4 §4.2**
