# Merging Cells in a Word Table — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does merging do?**
**Answer: Joins two or more selected cells into one cell**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the joined cell span?**
**Answer: The space of all its parts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give a use for merging across the top.**
**Answer: A title cell stretched over the whole table**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Give a use for merging down a column.**
**Answer: One entry serving several rows**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the merged cells' contents go?**
**Answer: They gather into the single new cell**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What must selected cells be, to merge?**
**Answer: Together, in one block**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the command called?**
**Answer: Merge Cells (Table menu / right-click)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What reverses a merge?**
**Answer: Split Cells — dividing a cell back into rows and columns**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is a wrong merge fixed?**
**Answer: Split and re-merge — never by deleting the table**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 9 ICT, Chapter 4 §4.2**
