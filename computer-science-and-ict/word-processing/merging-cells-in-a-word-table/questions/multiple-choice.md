# Merging Cells in a Word Table — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does merging do?**
a) Joins two or more selected cells into one cell  b) The space of all its parts  c) A title cell stretched over the whole table  d) One entry serving several rows
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the joined cell span?**
a) A title cell stretched over the whole table  b) The space of all its parts  c) One entry serving several rows  d) They gather into the single new cell
**Answer: b**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give a use for merging across the top.**
a) One entry serving several rows  b) They gather into the single new cell  c) A title cell stretched over the whole table  d) Together, in one block
**Answer: c**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Give a use for merging down a column.**
a) They gather into the single new cell  b) Together, in one block  c) Merge Cells (Table menu / right-click)  d) One entry serving several rows
**Answer: d**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the merged cells' contents go?**
a) They gather into the single new cell  b) Together, in one block  c) Merge Cells (Table menu / right-click)  d) Split Cells — dividing a cell back into rows and columns
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What must selected cells be, to merge?**
a) Merge Cells (Table menu / right-click)  b) Together, in one block  c) Split Cells — dividing a cell back into rows and columns  d) Split and re-merge — never by deleting the table
**Answer: b**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the command called?**
a) Split Cells — dividing a cell back into rows and columns  b) Split and re-merge — never by deleting the table  c) Merge Cells (Table menu / right-click)  d) Laos Grade 9 ICT, Chapter 4 §4.2
**Answer: c**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What reverses a merge?**
a) Split and re-merge — never by deleting the table  b) Laos Grade 9 ICT, Chapter 4 §4.2  c) Joins two or more selected cells into one cell  d) Split Cells — dividing a cell back into rows and columns
**Answer: d**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is a wrong merge fixed?**
a) Split and re-merge — never by deleting the table  b) Laos Grade 9 ICT, Chapter 4 §4.2  c) Joins two or more selected cells into one cell  d) The space of all its parts
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
a) Joins two or more selected cells into one cell  b) Laos Grade 9 ICT, Chapter 4 §4.2  c) The space of all its parts  d) A title cell stretched over the whole table
**Answer: b**
