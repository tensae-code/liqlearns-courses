# Merging Cells in a Word Table — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Joins two or more selected cells into one cell**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A title cell stretched over the whole table** — as the answer to: What does the joined cell span?
**Answer: False — The space of all its parts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A title cell stretched over the whole table**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: They gather into the single new cell** — as the answer to: Give a use for merging down a column.
**Answer: False — One entry serving several rows**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: They gather into the single new cell**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Merge Cells (Table menu / right-click)** — as the answer to: What must selected cells be, to merge?
**Answer: False — Together, in one block**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Merge Cells (Table menu / right-click)**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Split and re-merge — never by deleting the table** — as the answer to: What reverses a merge?
**Answer: False — Split Cells — dividing a cell back into rows and columns**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Split and re-merge — never by deleting the table**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Joins two or more selected cells into one cell** — as the answer to: Which chapter teaches this?
**Answer: False — Laos Grade 9 ICT, Chapter 4 §4.2**
