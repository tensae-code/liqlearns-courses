# Video script — Typing text — the blinking insertion point

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Open the word processor.
See the small line blinking? That is the insertion point.
It sits in the text area, under the ribbon.

Type. The words appear — right there, where it blinks.

Spacebar — a space. Enter — a new line. Tab — a jump.
Text lands where the point stands.
