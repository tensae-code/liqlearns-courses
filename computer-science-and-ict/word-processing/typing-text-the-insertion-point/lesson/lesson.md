# Typing text — the blinking insertion point

## Do it first

Open the word processor and look at the blank page before you type. One thing on it is moving — a small line, blinking. That blinking line is where everything you type is about to happen. Find it first.

## Typing text — in order

1. Click the **word processor icon** to open it.
2. The blinking cursor — the **insertion point** — sits in the **text area below the ribbon**.
3. As you type, the words appear on screen in the text area, right where the insertion point stands.
4. To move the insertion point, press the **Spacebar**, **Enter** or **Tab** keys — Spacebar moves it along by a space, Enter drops it to a new line, Tab jumps it forward.

The rule to carry away: text always lands where the insertion point is. Put the point where you want the words, then type.
