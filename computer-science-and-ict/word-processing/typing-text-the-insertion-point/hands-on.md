# Hands-on — Typing text — the blinking insertion point

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Follow the point

Type a short sentence slowly. After every word, stop and point at the insertion point with your finger and say: "the next word lands here." Your partner checks that the point is where you said.

## Three keys, three moves

Type your name. Press Spacebar once and type your school. Press Enter and type your class on the new line. Press Tab and type today's subject. Look at the page: three different moves, three different places — all done by moving one blinking point.
