# Editing Pictures and Shapes in a Document — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which tools dress a shape?**
**Answer: The Drawing Tools**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a shape's fill?**
**Answer: The colour or texture poured into it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What can a texture imitate?**
**Answer: A material — a patterned surface**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which tools dress a picture?**
**Answer: The Picture Tools**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does flipping do?**
**Answer: Mirrors the object left-right or top-bottom**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How can an object be rotated?**
**Answer: By its handle, or by exact degrees**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does editing points do?**
**Answer: Drags the outline's points one by one, bending the shape into a custom one**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What was ClipArt?**
**Answer: Word's ready-picture library of the book's era**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's working order for an object?**
**Answer: Insert, dress, reshape**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) In which project is this editing used?**
**Answer: Mongolia's Grade 7 'Cherish the Water' booklet**
