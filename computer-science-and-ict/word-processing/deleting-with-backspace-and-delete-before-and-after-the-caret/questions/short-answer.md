# Deleting with Backspace and Delete — Before and After the Caret — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the caret?**
**Answer: The blinking writing cursor — where letters land**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Backspace delete?**
**Answer: The letter before the caret**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to the caret with each Backspace?**
**Answer: It pulls back with the deletion**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which mistake is Backspace for?**
**Answer: The one just made, one step behind you**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does Delete delete?**
**Answer: The letter after the caret**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to the caret when Delete is pressed?**
**Answer: It stays where it is**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which mistake is Delete for?**
**Answer: The one ahead of you, in the middle of a word**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the Space bar add?**
**Answer: A space**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does deleting a space do to two words?**
**Answer: Joins them wrongly together**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How are wrongly joined words separated?**
**Answer: With the Space bar — a space put back**
