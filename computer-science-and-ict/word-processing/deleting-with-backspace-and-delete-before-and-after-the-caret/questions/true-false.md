# Deleting with Backspace and Delete — Before and After the Caret — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the caret?" is: The blinking writing cursor — where letters land**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does Backspace delete?" is: It pulls back with the deletion**
**Answer: False** — The letter before the caret

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What happens to the caret with each Backspace?" is: It pulls back with the deletion**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which mistake is Backspace for?" is: The letter after the caret**
**Answer: False** — The one just made, one step behind you

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does Delete delete?" is: The letter after the caret**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens to the caret when Delete is pressed?" is: The one ahead of you, in the middle of a word**
**Answer: False** — It stays where it is

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which mistake is Delete for?" is: The one ahead of you, in the middle of a word**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the Space bar add?" is: Joins them wrongly together**
**Answer: False** — A space

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does deleting a space do to two words?" is: Joins them wrongly together**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How are wrongly joined words separated?" is: The blinking writing cursor — where letters land**
**Answer: False** — With the Space bar — a space put back
