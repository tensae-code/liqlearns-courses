# Deleting with Backspace and Delete — Before and After the Caret — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the caret?**
a) The blinking writing cursor — where letters land  b) The one just made, one step behind you  c) It stays where it is  d) A space
**Answer: a) The blinking writing cursor — where letters land**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Backspace delete?**
a) The letter after the caret  b) The letter before the caret  c) The one ahead of you, in the middle of a word  d) Joins them wrongly together
**Answer: b) The letter before the caret**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to the caret with each Backspace?**
a) It stays where it is  b) A space  c) It pulls back with the deletion  d) With the Space bar — a space put back
**Answer: c) It pulls back with the deletion**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which mistake is Backspace for?**
a) The one ahead of you, in the middle of a word  b) Joins them wrongly together  c) The blinking writing cursor — where letters land  d) The one just made, one step behind you
**Answer: d) The one just made, one step behind you**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does Delete delete?**
a) The letter after the caret  b) A space  c) With the Space bar — a space put back  d) The letter before the caret
**Answer: a) The letter after the caret**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to the caret when Delete is pressed?**
a) Joins them wrongly together  b) It stays where it is  c) The blinking writing cursor — where letters land  d) It pulls back with the deletion
**Answer: b) It stays where it is**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which mistake is Delete for?**
a) With the Space bar — a space put back  b) The letter before the caret  c) The one ahead of you, in the middle of a word  d) The one just made, one step behind you
**Answer: c) The one ahead of you, in the middle of a word**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the Space bar add?**
a) The blinking writing cursor — where letters land  b) It pulls back with the deletion  c) The letter after the caret  d) A space
**Answer: d) A space**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does deleting a space do to two words?**
a) Joins them wrongly together  b) The letter before the caret  c) The one just made, one step behind you  d) It stays where it is
**Answer: a) Joins them wrongly together**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How are wrongly joined words separated?**
a) It pulls back with the deletion  b) With the Space bar — a space put back  c) The letter after the caret  d) The one ahead of you, in the middle of a word
**Answer: b) With the Space bar — a space put back**
