# Video script — Deleting with Backspace and Delete — Before and After the Caret

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The blinking **writing cursor — the caret** — marks where letters land. **Backspace deletes the letter before the caret**: each press removes one letter to the caret's left (in left-to-right text) and pulls the caret back with it. It is the key for the mistake you just made, one step behind you.
**Delete deletes the letter after the caret**: the caret stays where it is and the letter in front of it disappears — the key for the mistake ahead of you, when you have clicked back into the middle of a word. Beside them, the **Space bar adds a space** — deleting joins words wrongly together; the space bar is how they are separated again.
