# Deleting with Backspace and Delete — Before and After the Caret

## Do it first

A wrong letter sits in your word. Which key eats it — the one that deletes backwards or the one that deletes forwards? (teacher guide — evidence; PARTIAL — Term 1 only)

## Sub lesson 1 — Backspace — deleting before the caret

The blinking **writing cursor — the caret** — marks where letters land. **Backspace deletes the letter before the caret**: each press removes one letter to the caret's left (in left-to-right text) and pulls the caret back with it. It is the key for the mistake you just made, one step behind you.

[Picture — backspace — deleting before the caret]

## Sub lesson 2 — Delete — deleting after the caret; the Space bar

**Delete deletes the letter after the caret**: the caret stays where it is and the letter in front of it disappears — the key for the mistake ahead of you, when you have clicked back into the middle of a word. Beside them, the **Space bar adds a space** — deleting joins words wrongly together; the space bar is how they are separated again.

[Picture — delete — deleting after the caret; the space bar]

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: Two keys delete in two directions: Backspace deletes the letter before the writing cursor (the caret), Delete deletes the letter after it — and the Space bar does the opposite work, adding the space that keeps words apart. Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.
