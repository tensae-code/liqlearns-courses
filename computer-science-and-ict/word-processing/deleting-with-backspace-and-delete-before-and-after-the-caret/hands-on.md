# Hands-on — Deleting with Backspace and Delete — Before and After the Caret

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Both directions
Type a word with two planted mistakes — one behind the caret, one ahead. Fix the first with Backspace and the second with Delete, saying each key's direction aloud.

## Join and separate
Delete the space between two words, watch them join, then put the space back with the Space bar. Say what each key did.
