# Hands-on — Multilevel lists — outlines with levels

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Outline by hand first

Draw the computer-systems outline on paper as a tree — boxes inside boxes — then build it in Word with Tab. Compare the two: every box on paper should be a level on screen.

## Inventory build

List your classroom's inventory as a multilevel list: room → furniture → items on it. Three levels minimum, built with Tab and checked with Shift+Tab corrections.
