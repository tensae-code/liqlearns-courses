# Video script — Multilevel lists — outlines with levels

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A flat list says nothing.
Tab: the line drops a level.
Tab twice: two levels in.
Shift+Tab: back out a level.
CPU holds ALU, control unit, registers.
Now the list is an outline.
