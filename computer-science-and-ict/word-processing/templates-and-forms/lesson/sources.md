**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (North Macedonia, Mongolia, South Sudan).**

Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (North Macedonia, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked for the slug and for near matches before building. The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — word-processing/templates-and-forms). It sits in the Word Processing title (word-processing/), following the book's own order.

North Macedonia's own way (Year 1): five themes move from modern digital technology and online living into a full structured-programming course taught in C++ (with Scratch/C++/Java/Python/PHP/Lisp compared), then advanced Word work (styles, contents, templates) and spreadsheets up to pivot tables.

Mongolia's own way (Grade 8): Grade 8 is built around two long projects — a folded leaflet on Ulaanbaatar's air pollution (Word templates, styles, WordArt, charts and SmartArt) that then becomes a PowerPoint presentation with its own master slide and hyperlink buttons, and a Paint.NET collage of the five-snout livestock (таван хошуу мал) in a steppe landscape. Models are taught on Mongolian material (the livestock, Rutherford's atom, a virus-growth formula); spreadsheets open with the chessboard-and-wheat legend and count Mongolia's real 2014 livestock by aimag; social networks are taught on Facebook and Twitter ("жиргээ") with Mongolian examples.

South Sudan's own way (Grade 12 S4 Student): The final year is specialisation: a full databases unit in Microsoft Access with real SQL, forms and reports, normalisation and joins, ending in integrated library management systems; an advanced-formatting unit that goes from Word tables of contents, styles, track changes, templates and mail merge through PowerPoint multimedia to VBA macros in Excel; and a website-design unit built around HTML, CSS and Adobe Dreamweaver with FTP site management.

South Sudan's own way (Grade 12 S4 Teacher): The guide mirrors the student book's three units with teaching sequences, observation/conversation assessment points and worked projects — designing a database for a local medical practice, asking how books are managed in a school library, and a capstone project to design the school's own website, judged on navigation and usability.
