# Hands-on — Templates and Forms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Template of your own
Design a simple one-page letter or certificate layout, save it as a template, then create two documents from it with different content. Confirm the template itself did not change.

## Build a small form
Make a short enrolment-style form: fixed labels, one text field, one drop-down, one checkbox. Protect it so only the fields can be edited, then fill it in as a test.
