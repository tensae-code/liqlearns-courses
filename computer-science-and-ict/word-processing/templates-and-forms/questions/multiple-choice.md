# Templates and Forms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a template save?**
a) A document's skeleton — layout, styles and standard text  b) The skeleton is copied; the template stays untouched  c) Yes — save any designed document as a template  d) Margins and styles (any two from the lesson)

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when you create a document from a template?**
a) Yes — save any designed document as a template  b) The skeleton is copied; the template stays untouched  c) Margins and styles (any two from the lesson)  d) The pattern keeps every document identical and saves the design work
