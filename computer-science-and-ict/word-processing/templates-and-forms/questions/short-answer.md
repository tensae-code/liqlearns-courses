# Templates and Forms — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a template save?**
**Answer: A document's skeleton — layout, styles and standard text**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when you create a document from a template?**
**Answer: The skeleton is copied; the template stays untouched**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Can you make your own template?**
**Answer: Yes — save any designed document as a template**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name two things a skeleton can hold.**
**Answer: Margins and styles (any two from the lesson)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why not start from blank each time?**
**Answer: The pattern keeps every document identical and saves the design work**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a form in Word?**
**Answer: A document built to be filled in**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two content controls.**
**Answer: Text fields and drop-down lists (any two from the lesson)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does protection do in a form?**
**Answer: Locks everything except the fill-in fields**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why must a form resist its filler-in?**
**Answer: So it cannot be accidentally broken while completed**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who uses forms like these?**
**Answer: Schools, offices and clinics**
