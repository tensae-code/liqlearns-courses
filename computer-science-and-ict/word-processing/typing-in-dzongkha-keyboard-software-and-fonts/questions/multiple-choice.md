# Typing in Dzongkha — Keyboard Software and Fonts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What language is Bhutan's national language, typed in this lesson?**
a) Dzongkha  b) Dzongkha keyboard software and Dzongkha fonts  c) It maps the keys to Dzongkha characters  d) They draw the Dzongkha characters correctly on screen and on paper
**Answer: a) Dzongkha**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two things must a computer have to type Dzongkha?**
a) It maps the keys to Dzongkha characters  b) Dzongkha keyboard software and Dzongkha fonts  c) They draw the Dzongkha characters correctly on screen and on paper  d) Dzongkha as the typing language
**Answer: b) Dzongkha keyboard software and Dzongkha fonts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the keyboard software do?**
a) They draw the Dzongkha characters correctly on screen and on paper  b) Dzongkha as the typing language  c) It maps the keys to Dzongkha characters  d) Microsoft Word
**Answer: c) It maps the keys to Dzongkha characters**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do the fonts do?**
a) Dzongkha as the typing language  b) Microsoft Word  c) The Dzongkha font  d) They draw the Dzongkha characters correctly on screen and on paper
**Answer: d) They draw the Dzongkha characters correctly on screen and on paper**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the pupil select before typing Dzongkha?**
a) Dzongkha as the typing language  b) Microsoft Word  c) The Dzongkha font  d) That characters stack and join as Dzongkha writing requires
**Answer: a) Dzongkha as the typing language**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) In which program is the Dzongkha document made?**
a) The Dzongkha font  b) Microsoft Word  c) That characters stack and join as Dzongkha writing requires  d) A Dzongkha story typed in Word
**Answer: b) Microsoft Word**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is chosen inside Word before typing?**
a) That characters stack and join as Dzongkha writing requires  b) A Dzongkha story typed in Word  c) The Dzongkha font  d) Saved with a clear name and filed like every other piece of work
**Answer: c) The Dzongkha font**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What script care must the typist watch?**
a) A Dzongkha story typed in Word  b) Saved with a clear name and filed like every other piece of work  c) Dzongkha  d) That characters stack and join as Dzongkha writing requires
**Answer: d) That characters stack and join as Dzongkha writing requires**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does Class V produce at the end of the year?**
a) A Dzongkha story typed in Word  b) Saved with a clear name and filed like every other piece of work  c) Dzongkha  d) Dzongkha keyboard software and Dzongkha fonts
**Answer: a) A Dzongkha story typed in Word**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is the Dzongkha document kept?**
a) Dzongkha  b) Saved with a clear name and filed like every other piece of work  c) Dzongkha keyboard software and Dzongkha fonts  d) It maps the keys to Dzongkha characters
