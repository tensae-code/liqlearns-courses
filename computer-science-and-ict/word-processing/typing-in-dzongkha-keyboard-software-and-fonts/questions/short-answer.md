# Typing in Dzongkha — Keyboard Software and Fonts — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What language is Bhutan's national language, typed in this lesson?**
**Answer: Dzongkha**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two things must a computer have to type Dzongkha?**
**Answer: Dzongkha keyboard software and Dzongkha fonts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the keyboard software do?**
**Answer: It maps the keys to Dzongkha characters**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do the fonts do?**
**Answer: They draw the Dzongkha characters correctly on screen and on paper**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the pupil select before typing Dzongkha?**
**Answer: Dzongkha as the typing language**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) In which program is the Dzongkha document made?**
**Answer: Microsoft Word**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is chosen inside Word before typing?**
**Answer: The Dzongkha font**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What script care must the typist watch?**
**Answer: That characters stack and join as Dzongkha writing requires**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does Class V produce at the end of the year?**
**Answer: A Dzongkha story typed in Word**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is the Dzongkha document kept?**
**Answer: Saved with a clear name and filed like every other piece of work**
