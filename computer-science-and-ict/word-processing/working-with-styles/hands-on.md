# Hands-on — Working with Styles

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Style a page
Take a page with a title, two headings and body text. Style the title and headings from the gallery, body as Normal. Then modify Heading 1's colour and watch every heading change at once.

## Make your own
Format one paragraph exactly as you like it, save it as a new style with your own name, and apply it to two more paragraphs. Write what was in the bundle.
