# Working with Styles — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a style?" is: A named, saved bundle of formatting**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where do Word's ready styles live?" is: It knows it is a heading — contents and navigation can use it**
**Answer: False** — In the Styles gallery

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What lands when you apply a style?" is: The whole formatting bundle at once**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name two of Word's ready styles." is: From a hand-formatted paragraph, saved under a name**
**Answer: False** — Heading 1 and Normal (any two from the lesson)

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why does Word treat a styled heading specially?" is: It knows it is a heading — contents and navigation can use it**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens when you modify a style?" is: Contents are built on styled headings**
**Answer: False** — Every paragraph wearing it updates together

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is a new style created?" is: From a hand-formatted paragraph, saved under a name**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What can one style edit change?" is: A named, saved bundle of formatting**
**Answer: False** — The look of the whole document

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why does the book teach styles before contents?" is: Contents are built on styled headings**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the alternative styles replace?" is: The whole formatting bundle at once**
**Answer: False** — Formatting paragraph by paragraph by hand
