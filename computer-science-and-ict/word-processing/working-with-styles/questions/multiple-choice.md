# Working with Styles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a style?**
a) A named, saved bundle of formatting  b) In the Styles gallery  c) The whole formatting bundle at once  d) Heading 1 and Normal (any two from the lesson)
**Answer: a) A named, saved bundle of formatting**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where do Word's ready styles live?**
a) The whole formatting bundle at once  b) In the Styles gallery  c) Heading 1 and Normal (any two from the lesson)  d) It knows it is a heading — contents and navigation can use it
**Answer: b) In the Styles gallery**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What lands when you apply a style?**
a) Heading 1 and Normal (any two from the lesson)  b) It knows it is a heading — contents and navigation can use it  c) The whole formatting bundle at once  d) Every paragraph wearing it updates together
**Answer: c) The whole formatting bundle at once**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name two of Word's ready styles.**
a) It knows it is a heading — contents and navigation can use it  b) Every paragraph wearing it updates together  c) From a hand-formatted paragraph, saved under a name  d) Heading 1 and Normal (any two from the lesson)
**Answer: d) Heading 1 and Normal (any two from the lesson)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Word treat a styled heading specially?**
a) It knows it is a heading — contents and navigation can use it  b) Every paragraph wearing it updates together  c) From a hand-formatted paragraph, saved under a name  d) The look of the whole document
**Answer: a) It knows it is a heading — contents and navigation can use it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when you modify a style?**
a) From a hand-formatted paragraph, saved under a name  b) Every paragraph wearing it updates together  c) The look of the whole document  d) Contents are built on styled headings
**Answer: b) Every paragraph wearing it updates together**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is a new style created?**
a) The look of the whole document  b) Contents are built on styled headings  c) From a hand-formatted paragraph, saved under a name  d) Formatting paragraph by paragraph by hand
**Answer: c) From a hand-formatted paragraph, saved under a name**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can one style edit change?**
a) Contents are built on styled headings  b) Formatting paragraph by paragraph by hand  c) A named, saved bundle of formatting  d) The look of the whole document
**Answer: d) The look of the whole document**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book teach styles before contents?**
a) Contents are built on styled headings  b) Formatting paragraph by paragraph by hand  c) A named, saved bundle of formatting  d) In the Styles gallery
**Answer: a) Contents are built on styled headings**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the alternative styles replace?**
a) A named, saved bundle of formatting  b) Formatting paragraph by paragraph by hand  c) In the Styles gallery  d) The whole formatting bundle at once
**Answer: b) Formatting paragraph by paragraph by hand**
