# Working with Styles — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a style?**
**Answer: A named, saved bundle of formatting**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where do Word's ready styles live?**
**Answer: In the Styles gallery**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What lands when you apply a style?**
**Answer: The whole formatting bundle at once**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name two of Word's ready styles.**
**Answer: Heading 1 and Normal (any two from the lesson)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Word treat a styled heading specially?**
**Answer: It knows it is a heading — contents and navigation can use it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when you modify a style?**
**Answer: Every paragraph wearing it updates together**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is a new style created?**
**Answer: From a hand-formatted paragraph, saved under a name**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can one style edit change?**
**Answer: The look of the whole document**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book teach styles before contents?**
**Answer: Contents are built on styled headings**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the alternative styles replace?**
**Answer: Formatting paragraph by paragraph by hand**
