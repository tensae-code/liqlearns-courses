# The Edit Menu — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the Edit menu work on?**
a) Whatever is selected  b) Removes the selected text and holds it on the clipboard  c) Leaves the text where it is and holds a copy on the clipboard  d) Puts what the clipboard holds at the insertion point
**Answer: a) Whatever is selected**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Cut do?**
a) Leaves the text where it is and holds a copy on the clipboard  b) Removes the selected text and holds it on the clipboard  c) Puts what the clipboard holds at the insertion point  d) Cut moves; Copy duplicates
**Answer: b) Removes the selected text and holds it on the clipboard**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does Copy do?**
a) Puts what the clipboard holds at the insertion point  b) Cut moves; Copy duplicates  c) Leaves the text where it is and holds a copy on the clipboard  d) Takes back the last action
**Answer: c) Leaves the text where it is and holds a copy on the clipboard**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does Paste do?**
a) Cut moves; Copy duplicates  b) Takes back the last action  c) Selects the whole document at once  d) Puts what the clipboard holds at the insertion point
**Answer: d) Puts what the clipboard holds at the insertion point**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the one difference at the menu's heart?**
a) Cut moves; Copy duplicates  b) Takes back the last action  c) Selects the whole document at once  d) Removes the selection without holding it on the clipboard
**Answer: a) Cut moves; Copy duplicates**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Undo do?**
a) Selects the whole document at once  b) Takes back the last action  c) Removes the selection without holding it on the clipboard  d) Find and Replace
**Answer: b) Takes back the last action**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does Select All do?**
a) Removes the selection without holding it on the clipboard  b) Find and Replace  c) Selects the whole document at once  d) Text can be moved and fixed instead of retyped
**Answer: c) Selects the whole document at once**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does Clear do differently from Cut?**
a) Find and Replace  b) Text can be moved and fixed instead of retyped  c) Whatever is selected  d) Removes the selection without holding it on the clipboard
**Answer: d) Removes the selection without holding it on the clipboard**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which search commands live in the Edit menu?**
a) Find and Replace  b) Text can be moved and fixed instead of retyped  c) Whatever is selected  d) Removes the selected text and holds it on the clipboard
**Answer: a) Find and Replace**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is editing what makes Word better than a typewriter?**
a) Whatever is selected  b) Text can be moved and fixed instead of retyped  c) Removes the selected text and holds it on the clipboard  d) Leaves the text where it is and holds a copy on the clipboard
**Answer: b) Text can be moved and fixed instead of retyped**
