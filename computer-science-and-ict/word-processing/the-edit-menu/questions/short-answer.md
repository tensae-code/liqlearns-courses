# The Edit Menu — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the Edit menu work on?**
**Answer: Whatever is selected**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Cut do?**
**Answer: Removes the selected text and holds it on the clipboard**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does Copy do?**
**Answer: Leaves the text where it is and holds a copy on the clipboard**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does Paste do?**
**Answer: Puts what the clipboard holds at the insertion point**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the one difference at the menu's heart?**
**Answer: Cut moves; Copy duplicates**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Undo do?**
**Answer: Takes back the last action**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does Select All do?**
**Answer: Selects the whole document at once**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does Clear do differently from Cut?**
**Answer: Removes the selection without holding it on the clipboard**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which search commands live in the Edit menu?**
**Answer: Find and Replace**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is editing what makes Word better than a typewriter?**
**Answer: Text can be moved and fixed instead of retyped**
