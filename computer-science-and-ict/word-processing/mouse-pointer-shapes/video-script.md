# Video script — Mouse pointer shapes and what they mean

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

The mouse pointer speaks.
Arrow: click this. Timer: busy — wait.
Flashing line: your letters land here.
I-beam: the mouse is over text.

Four shapes. Four messages.
Now you can read them.
