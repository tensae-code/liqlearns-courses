# Mouse pointer shapes and what they mean

## Do it first

Open Word. Move the mouse very slowly — across the margin, over some text, to a button. The pointer is not always the same pointer: it keeps changing its shape. Each shape is a message. This lesson teaches you to read the messages.

## Four shapes, four messages

The **ordinary pointer** — the arrow — means: you can click this item; clicking chooses it. The **timer** means: the computer is busy — wait; clicking again will not hurry it. The **flashing insertion point** is the blinking line in your text: it shows where letters will be entered or deleted. The **I-beam** is the shape the mouse takes over text: it shows where the mouse is, and where the insertion point will jump when you click. Watch the GIF: one pointer, four shapes, four meanings.
