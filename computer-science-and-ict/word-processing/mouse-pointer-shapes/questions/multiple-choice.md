# Mouse pointer shapes and what they mean — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The ordinary arrow pointer means —
a) the computer is busy  b) you can click an item to choose it  c) text goes here  d) delete everything
**Answer: b) you can click an item to choose it** — the arrow clicks and chooses.

**2.** The timer shape means —
a) click faster  b) the computer is busy — wait  c) the mouse is broken  d) save now
**Answer: b) the computer is busy — wait** — timer = busy; wait.

**3.** Where will typed letters appear?
a) At the top of the screen  b) At the flashing insertion point  c) Under the mouse only  d) On the status bar
**Answer: b) At the flashing insertion point** — text is entered at the insertion point.

**4.** The I-beam shows —
a) a timer  b) where the mouse is over text  c) that Word is closing  d) the file name
**Answer: b) where the mouse is over text** — the I-beam marks the mouse over text.

**5.** Letters are deleted at —
a) the insertion point  b) the title bar  c) the ruler  d) the zoom control
**Answer: a) the insertion point** — entering and deleting both happen at the insertion point.

**6.** The pointer over text changes to —
a) a timer  b) an I-beam  c) a hand only  d) nothing
**Answer: b) an I-beam** — over text, the mouse becomes the I-beam.
