# Mouse pointer shapes and what they mean — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** Clicking again and again makes a busy computer hurry.
**Answer: False** — the timer means wait.

**2.** The insertion point flashes.
**Answer: True** — it is the flashing line in the text.

**3.** The I-beam and the insertion point are the same thing.
**Answer: False** — the I-beam is the mouse; the insertion point is in the text.
