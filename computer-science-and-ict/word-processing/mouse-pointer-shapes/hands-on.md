# Hands-on — Mouse pointer shapes and what they mean

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Shape hunt
Move the mouse around a Word window until you have seen all four shapes. Each time a new shape appears, call out its meaning before you click anything.

## Flashing line
Click in three different places in a line of text. Watch the insertion point jump to each click. That blinking line — not the mouse — is where your next letter lands.
