# Word processing — writing on a computer

## Do it first

Write one sentence by hand. Now change one word in the middle of it. On paper, you cross out, squeeze in, or start the page again. Keep that feeling — this lesson is about a tool where changing a word costs nothing.

## Writing on a computer

A word processor is a program that lets users **create, edit, format and save** documents. Into those documents you can also add **images, tables and charts**. Compared with pen-and-paper writing, word-processed text is more readable and clearer, with fewer errors — and errors are easy to correct: you change the word, not the page. The rest of this title is the doing of it: opening the program, knowing its window, saving your work, and closing properly.
