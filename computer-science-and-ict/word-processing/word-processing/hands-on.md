# Hands-on — Word processing — writing on a computer

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Paper vs screen
Write the same short paragraph twice: once by hand, once on a computer if you can. Deliberately include one wrong word in each. Fix the wrong word in both and time yourself. Write down which fix was easier and why.

## Spot the features
Find any printed document made on a computer — a worksheet, an exam, a letter. Point to one place where an image, a table or a chart has been added, and say what it helps the reader do.
