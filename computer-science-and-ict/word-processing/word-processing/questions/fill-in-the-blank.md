# Word processing — writing on a computer — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1.** A word processor lets you create, ______, format and save documents.
**Answer: edit**

**2.** You can add images, ______ and charts to a document.
**Answer: tables**

**3.** Word-processed text has fewer ______ than pen-and-paper writing.
**Answer: errors**
