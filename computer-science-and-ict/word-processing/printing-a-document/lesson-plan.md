**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Printing turns the document on the screen into paper, and doing it well means checking the preview and the settings first — the right pages, the right number of copies, the right printer — before any paper is used.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Before you print — preview and settings** — Open **Print** from the File menu and the **print preview** shows the document exactly as it will come out — page by page, margins and all. Here you choose the **printer**, the pages to print (all, the current page, or a range such as 2–4) and the **number of copies**. Checking the preview first catches the mistakes — a heading stranded on a new page, a picture cut in half — while they still cost nothing.
2. **Printing — and if something goes wrong** — Click **Print** and the printer takes the pages in order; a small printer icon and queue show the job waiting its turn. If nothing comes out, check the simple things first: is the printer on, does it have paper, is it the printer you chose? A job sent by mistake can be cancelled in the **print queue**. Never send the job again and again — each click adds another copy to the queue.
