Mauritania's own way (Grade 7): Informatics is taught for the first time in 1AS as thirty 30-minute teacher fiches moving hardware → information systems (illustrated with Mauritanian systems Bankily, Masrvi, Houwiyeti and the Ministry's Sirage platform) → a long Microsoft Word sequence → a full unit on internet ethics before browsers and search are even introduced.

Sudan's own way (Grade 5): a short, highly practical Arabic primary series that revises the previous grade first, then works entirely inside Windows and its built-in apps (Calculator, Paint) before reaching Word — every lesson ends with hands-on activities and each unit with an assessment.


Uzbekistan's own way (Grade 6): the whole of grade 6 is one application taught to mastery — MS Word from its interface to formulas and print — on the principle that one tool learned deeply beats many tools skimmed.

Cuba's own way (Grade 7): Secondary Informática opens by contrasting proprietary Windows with Cuba's free operating system Nova; programming continues the primary Scratch line, here centred on the Scratch pen tool for drawing geometric figures and the linear function; word-processor work ends by exporting documents to HTML for the web.

Sri Lanka's own way (Grade 12 Science for Technology, Tamil): the A/L technology-stream ICT volume teaches every office skill twice — once in Microsoft Office 2010 and once in LibreOffice (Writer/Calc/Impress 4.1) — and closes with creating a real Gmail account, mailbox folders, attachments and password safety; hardware goes down to cache levels L1/L2/L3, ROM types and file systems (FAT/NTFS/ext4).

Rwanda's own way (S3 ICT Learner's Book, Fountain): the same ten units as the S3 guide taught as long screenshot-by-screenshot practicals rooted in Rwandan school life (a GS Bumbogo parents'-meeting mail merge, an administration map of Rwanda built in ArcGIS, an environmental-protection presentation, 'Rwanda Nziza' hyperlink decks), closing with a complete Scratch platform game, Hard Journey.

Laos's own way (Grade 9 ICT student book): the first fully practical year — a screenshot-led Windows XP, Word and Excel course where every numbered topic is a click-by-click procedure (the Excel half even teaches the Chart Wizard and worksheet Page Setup with its Orientation/Paper Size/Page Numbering table in English). Windows XP-era office teaching runs through the middle years.

Indonesia's own way (Indonesia Grade 7 G7 Informatika teacher guide): Panduan Umum sets out capaian pembelajaran, pedagogy principles and assessment for Grade 7; each chapter guide presents the essential material, pre-learning assessment, a step-by-step guide to the student book, enrichment/remedial, answer keys and parent interaction. (teacher guide — evidence)
