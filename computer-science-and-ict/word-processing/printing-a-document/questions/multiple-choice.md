# Printing a Document — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the Print command?**
a) In the File menu  b) The document exactly as it will come out, page by page  c) Mistakes are caught while they still cost no paper  d) In the print queue
**Answer: a) In the File menu**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the print preview show?**
a) The printer, the pages, and the number of copies  b) The document exactly as it will come out, page by page  c) The printer icon and the print queue  d) In order, first page to last
**Answer: b) The document exactly as it will come out, page by page**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What three choices do you make before printing?**
a) Enter the range 2–4 in the pages box  b) The printer is on, it has paper, and it is the one you chose  c) The printer, the pages, and the number of copies  d) Each click adds another copy of the job to the queue
**Answer: c) The printer, the pages, and the number of copies**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How do you print only pages 2 to 4?**
a) Mistakes are caught while they still cost no paper  b) In the print queue  c) In the File menu  d) Enter the range 2–4 in the pages box
**Answer: d) Enter the range 2–4 in the pages box**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why check the preview first?**
a) Mistakes are caught while they still cost no paper  b) The printer icon and the print queue  c) In order, first page to last  d) The document exactly as it will come out, page by page
**Answer: a) Mistakes are caught while they still cost no paper**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What shows that a print job is waiting?**
a) The printer is on, it has paper, and it is the one you chose  b) The printer icon and the print queue  c) Each click adds another copy of the job to the queue  d) The printer, the pages, and the number of copies
**Answer: b) The printer icon and the print queue**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the first checks when nothing prints.**
a) In the print queue  b) In the File menu  c) The printer is on, it has paper, and it is the one you chose  d) Enter the range 2–4 in the pages box
**Answer: c) The printer is on, it has paper, and it is the one you chose**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where can a mistaken job be cancelled?**
a) In order, first page to last  b) The document exactly as it will come out, page by page  c) Mistakes are caught while they still cost no paper  d) In the print queue
**Answer: d) In the print queue**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what order do the pages come out?**
a) In order, first page to last  b) Each click adds another copy of the job to the queue  c) The printer, the pages, and the number of copies  d) The printer icon and the print queue
**Answer: a) In order, first page to last**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why must you not click Print again and again?**
a) In the File menu  b) Each click adds another copy of the job to the queue  c) Enter the range 2–4 in the pages box  d) The printer is on, it has paper, and it is the one you chose
**Answer: b) Each click adds another copy of the job to the queue**
