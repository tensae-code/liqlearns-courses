# Printing a Document — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the Print command?**
**Answer: In the File menu**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the print preview show?**
**Answer: The document exactly as it will come out, page by page**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What three choices do you make before printing?**
**Answer: The printer, the pages, and the number of copies**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How do you print only pages 2 to 4?**
**Answer: Enter the range 2–4 in the pages box**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why check the preview first?**
**Answer: Mistakes are caught while they still cost no paper**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What shows that a print job is waiting?**
**Answer: The printer icon and the print queue**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the first checks when nothing prints.**
**Answer: The printer is on, it has paper, and it is the one you chose**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where can a mistaken job be cancelled?**
**Answer: In the print queue**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what order do the pages come out?**
**Answer: In order, first page to last**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why must you not click Print again and again?**
**Answer: Each click adds another copy of the job to the queue**
