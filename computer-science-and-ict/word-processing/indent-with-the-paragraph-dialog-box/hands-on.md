# Hands-on — Indent with the Paragraph dialog box

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## One box, whole document

Take a document whose paragraphs are ragged — different alignments, different spacing. Select all, open the dialog, set one alignment and one line spacing, click OK. Compare before and after.

## Launcher hunt race

In pairs, starting from a closed Word: race to the open Paragraph dialog, naming each click aloud as you make it. The loser names the five steps from memory.
