# Lesson plan — Indent with the Paragraph dialog box

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Word Processing · **Lesson:** Indent with the Paragraph dialog box
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 5 — Word Processing in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 9 book, Unit 3 — Application Software. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** open the Paragraph dialog from the Home tab's launcher arrow, make the alignment and line-spacing adjustments inside it, and click OK — the dialog-box method sets a whole document's paragraphs at once.
**This lesson's own level:** it is the fourth and last of the four indentation methods — the one that sets everything from a single box.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Indent with the Increase/Decrease buttons.  Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Open the Paragraph dialog with the group's launcher arrow.
2. Adjust alignment and line spacing for all selected paragraphs in the dialog.
3. Click OK to save the settings and close the dialog.

## What you need
A computer with Word and a document of several paragraphs.

- Settings landed on one paragraph only → select all the paragraphs before opening the dialog.
