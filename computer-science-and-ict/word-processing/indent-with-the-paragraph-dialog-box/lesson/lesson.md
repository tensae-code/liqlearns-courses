# Indent with the Paragraph dialog box

## Do it first

Look at the Paragraph group on the Home tab. In its corner sits a tiny arrow, easy to miss. That arrow opens a box that holds this lesson: every paragraph setting in one place. Click it and look inside before the steps are given.

## The steps, in the book's order

1. Go to the **Home tab**. 2. **Select all the paragraphs.** 3. Click the Paragraph group's **dialog-box launcher** — the small corner arrow. 4. Make the **alignment and line-spacing adjustments** in the dialog. 5. Click **OK** to save and close.

[Picture 13313 — the launcher arrow and the open dialog]

## Why a dialog at all

The other three methods move one thing at a time — a first line, a marker, a half-inch step. The dialog gathers the settings together: alignment and line spacing adjusted side by side, applied to all the selected paragraphs with one OK. When many paragraphs must match exactly, this is the method that makes them match.

## Do It Yourself
