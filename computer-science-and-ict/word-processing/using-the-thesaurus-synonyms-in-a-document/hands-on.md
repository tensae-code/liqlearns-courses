# Hands-on — Using the Thesaurus — Synonyms in a Document

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Use synonyms in your text', 'Take a short paragraph of your own: find three repeated or plain words, look each up in the thesaurus, and replace them with synonyms that fit. Read the paragraph aloud both ways.')

## Task two
('Reject a synonym', "Find a word whose thesaurus offers a synonym that does NOT fit its sentence. Write both sentences and say in one line why the offered word fails.")
