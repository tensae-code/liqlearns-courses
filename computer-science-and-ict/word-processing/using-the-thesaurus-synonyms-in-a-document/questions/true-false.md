# Using the Thesaurus — Synonyms in a Document — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a synonym?" is: A word that means the same, or nearly the same, as another**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Give a pair of synonyms." is: Tires its reader**
**Answer: False** — Big and large (or begin and start)

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does repeating one word do to a text?" is: Tires its reader**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What must a chosen synonym do?" is: So documents built from sources are written in the pupil's own words**
**Answer: False** — Fit the sentence — its meaning there and the text's tone

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why does Cuba open word-processing with synonyms?" is: So documents built from sources are written in the pupil's own words**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is a thesaurus?" is: The word in your text**
**Answer: False** — A synonym finder built into the word processor

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is selected first?" is: The word in your text**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the thesaurus offer?" is: The chosen synonym, by the program or typed yourself**
**Answer: False** — Synonyms for the selected word

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What replaces the word?" is: The chosen synonym, by the program or typed yourself**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Who finally decides the word?" is: A word that means the same, or nearly the same, as another**
**Answer: False** — The writer, after reading the sentence whole again
