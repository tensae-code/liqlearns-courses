# Using the Thesaurus — Synonyms in a Document — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a synonym?**
**Answer: A word that means the same, or nearly the same, as another**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Give a pair of synonyms.**
**Answer: Big and large (or begin and start)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does repeating one word do to a text?**
**Answer: Tires its reader**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must a chosen synonym do?**
**Answer: Fit the sentence — its meaning there and the text's tone**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Cuba open word-processing with synonyms?**
**Answer: So documents built from sources are written in the pupil's own words**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a thesaurus?**
**Answer: A synonym finder built into the word processor**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is selected first?**
**Answer: The word in your text**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the thesaurus offer?**
**Answer: Synonyms for the selected word**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What replaces the word?**
**Answer: The chosen synonym, by the program or typed yourself**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who finally decides the word?**
**Answer: The writer, after reading the sentence whole again**
