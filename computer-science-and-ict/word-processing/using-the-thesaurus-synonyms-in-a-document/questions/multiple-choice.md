# Using the Thesaurus — Synonyms in a Document — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a synonym?**
a) A word that means the same, or nearly the same, as another  b) Big and large (or begin and start)  c) Tires its reader  d) Fit the sentence — its meaning there and the text's tone
**Answer: a) A word that means the same, or nearly the same, as another**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Give a pair of synonyms.**
a) Tires its reader  b) Big and large (or begin and start)  c) Fit the sentence — its meaning there and the text's tone  d) So documents built from sources are written in the pupil's own words
**Answer: b) Big and large (or begin and start)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does repeating one word do to a text?**
a) Fit the sentence — its meaning there and the text's tone  b) So documents built from sources are written in the pupil's own words  c) Tires its reader  d) A synonym finder built into the word processor
**Answer: c) Tires its reader**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must a chosen synonym do?**
a) So documents built from sources are written in the pupil's own words  b) A synonym finder built into the word processor  c) The word in your text  d) Fit the sentence — its meaning there and the text's tone
**Answer: d) Fit the sentence — its meaning there and the text's tone**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Cuba open word-processing with synonyms?**
a) So documents built from sources are written in the pupil's own words  b) A synonym finder built into the word processor  c) The word in your text  d) Synonyms for the selected word
**Answer: a) So documents built from sources are written in the pupil's own words**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a thesaurus?**
a) The word in your text  b) A synonym finder built into the word processor  c) Synonyms for the selected word  d) The chosen synonym, by the program or typed yourself
**Answer: b) A synonym finder built into the word processor**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is selected first?**
a) Synonyms for the selected word  b) The chosen synonym, by the program or typed yourself  c) The word in your text  d) The writer, after reading the sentence whole again
**Answer: c) The word in your text**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the thesaurus offer?**
a) The chosen synonym, by the program or typed yourself  b) The writer, after reading the sentence whole again  c) A word that means the same, or nearly the same, as another  d) Synonyms for the selected word
**Answer: d) Synonyms for the selected word**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What replaces the word?**
a) The chosen synonym, by the program or typed yourself  b) The writer, after reading the sentence whole again  c) A word that means the same, or nearly the same, as another  d) Big and large (or begin and start)
**Answer: a) The chosen synonym, by the program or typed yourself**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who finally decides the word?**
a) A word that means the same, or nearly the same, as another  b) The writer, after reading the sentence whole again  c) Big and large (or begin and start)  d) Tires its reader
**Answer: b) The writer, after reading the sentence whole again**
