# Video script — Typing Khmer Script

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Open a blank page.

First: switch the typing to Khmer script.

Test it. Press one key. Khmer script must appear.

Now type. Slowly. Watch the screen.

Read a short part of the copy. Type it. Check it.

Wrong? Fix it now, not later.

Part by part, the line is done.

Read it all back against the copy.

Right first. Fast later. Practice makes the hands fast.
