# Hands-on — Typing Khmer Script

The lesson with your hands, on a real computer.

## Switch and prove
Open a blank page in a text-editing program. Switch the typing to Khmer script. Press one key and look: write down what appeared. If it was not Khmer script, switch again and test again, until the proof character is Khmer.

## The practice line, in parts
Copy this line — the title line of Cambodia's Grade 10 ICT book — in short parts: read a part, type it, check it against the copy, correct it if needed, then continue.

បច្ចេកវិទ្យាព័ត៌មាន និងសារគមនាគមន៍ ថ្នាក់ទី ១០

## Two passes
Type the same line a second time on a new page. Compare the two finished pages against the copy and against each other. Write one line: what got easier, and where do your mistakes still gather?
