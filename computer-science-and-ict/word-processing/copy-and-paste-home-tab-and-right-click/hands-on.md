# Hands-on — Copy and paste — Home tab and right-click ways

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The twin paragraph

Type a two-line paragraph. Make it appear twice more — once by the Home tab way, once by the right-click way — until the page holds three identical paragraphs. Read all three aloud to check they truly match.

## Order cards

Write the four Home-tab steps on cards and the four right-click steps on cards of another colour. Shuffle one set, lay it in order, then the other. A partner checks each set against the lesson.
