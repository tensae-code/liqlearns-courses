**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Ethiopia, Afghanistan, Saudi Arabia, Cuba).**

Afghanistan's own way (Grade 10): a Windows XP-era office apprenticeship — one full chapter per application, taught menu by menu (File, Edit, Insert, Format), with the Dari keyboard language added as its own lesson; networks arrive only in the final chapter.

Saudi Arabia's own way (Grade 3, continuing education): a workbook-style book for the continuing-education (adult/lifelong learning) track — every one of the 16 lessons opens with "I want to learn" objectives and closes with written exercises, and everything is taught on an older Windows desktop (Start menu, "My Computer", Recycle Bin) with Notepad as the only application, worked through step-by-step practicals in the school lab.

Saudi Arabia's own way (Grade 4, Digital Skills): the primary Digital Skills course (Binary Logic) is built as three units per term with the same guide pattern as the intermediate books (outcomes, important points, steps, answers, unit project, "Test Yourself"), files on the Ain platform, and a strong Arabic/English bilingual-typing strand — pupils switch the keyboard between Arabic and English with Alt+Shift and learn text direction, hamza and Arabic punctuation on the keyboard alongside Word skills; Scratch is introduced in Term 1 through algorithms from daily life. This citation is PARTIAL — Term 1 only. (teacher guide — evidence)

Morocco's own way (Grade 2): Grade 2 goes online before it goes deep on the machine — browser and search first, then saving what the web gives (page, image, video), then creating in Paint and WordPad, with removable-disk file handling taught as a whole unit of its own. (teacher guide — evidence)

Cuba's own way (Grade 5): The Grade 5 programme is built around extracting information from Cuban educational software, encyclopedias and EcuRed into word-processor documents, then Scratch — drawing/animation first (Unit 3), control/movement/appearance blocks in Unit 4. (teacher programme — evidence)

Cuba's own way (Grade 5): Same Grade 5 content as the Programa, recast as per-unit teaching tables with hour allocations; Unit 4 is renamed “Aprendiendo y creando” and taught purely as Scratch control, movement and appearance blocks. (teacher guide — evidence)

Cuba's own way (Grade 5): Student text mirrors the programme in four chapters; word-processing is taught as a research workflow (synonyms, symbols, searching, copying texts and images from sources), and Scratch is introduced through its paint editor before movement/appearance/control blocks.
