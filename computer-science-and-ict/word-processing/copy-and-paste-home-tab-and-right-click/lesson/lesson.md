# Copy and paste — Home tab and right-click ways

## Do it first

You have typed a good paragraph. Now you need the same paragraph again, lower down the page. Typing it again would waste time and invite mistakes. Copy and paste does it in four moves. There are two ways to make those moves — learn both.

## Sub lesson 1 — Copy and paste by the Home tab

In order:

1. **Select** the text to copy.
2. On the **Home tab**, click **Copy**.
3. **Place the cursor** where the text should go.
4. Click **Paste** on the Home tab.

The selected text stays where it was — and a copy of it now stands at the cursor too.

## Sub lesson 2 — Copy and paste by right-click

In order:

1. **Select** the text.
2. Place the cursor over it and **right-click**; from the menu that appears, **left-click Copy**.
3. Move the cursor to the desired location and **right-click**.
4. From the menu, **left-click Paste**.

Same result as the Home tab way: the original stays, and a copy appears at the new place. The two ways differ only in where the Copy and Paste commands are clicked.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion both ways in order, and what is different between them — and what is the same.

## Do It Yourself

1. Type one sentence. Copy and paste it by the Home tab way so it appears twice.
2. Clear the page and do the same by the right-click way.
3. Write the four Home-tab steps from memory, numbered.
