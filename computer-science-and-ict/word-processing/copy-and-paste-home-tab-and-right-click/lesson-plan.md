# Lesson plan — Copy and paste — Home tab and right-click ways

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Word Processing · **Lesson:** Copy and paste — Home tab and right-click ways
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 5 — Word Processing in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 8 book, Unit 3 — Computer Software. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** copy and paste makes a second copy of selected text at the cursor — by the Home tab (select, Copy, place cursor, Paste) or by right-click (select, right-click Copy, right-click at the new place, Paste).
**This lesson's own level:** it is a doing-lesson. It stands on typing text — you must have text, and know the insertion point, before you can copy it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Copy and paste by the Home tab** — select the text; on the Home tab click Copy; place the cursor where the text should go; click Paste on the Home tab
2. **Copy and paste by right-click** — select the text; right-click on it and left-click Copy in the menu; right-click at the desired location and left-click Paste

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Typing text — the blinking insertion point. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Copy and paste by the Home tab, in order: select, Copy, place the cursor, Paste.
2. Copy and paste by right-click, in order: select, right-click Copy, right-click at the new place, Paste.
3. Say what copy leaves behind: the original text stays where it was.

## What you need
A computer with a word processor and a short paragraph typed in it.

## If the learner is stuck
- Pastes before copying → nothing was taken, so nothing can be pasted; the order is select, copy, place, paste.
- Forgets to place the cursor → the copy lands wherever the insertion point stands; place it first, then paste.
