# Copy and paste — Home tab and right-click ways — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After copy and paste, the original text is gone from its place.
**Answer: False** — copy leaves the original where it was; that is what makes it copy, not cut.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the right-click way, you left-click Copy in the menu that appears.
**Answer: True** — right-click opens the menu; left-click chooses from it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Home tab way and the right-click way give different results.
**Answer: False** — both leave the original and add a copy at the cursor; only the clicking differs.
