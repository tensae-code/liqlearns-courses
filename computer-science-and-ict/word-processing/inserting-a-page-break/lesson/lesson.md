# Inserting a page break

## Do it first

Type a paragraph low on a page, with its picture meant to sit under it on the next page. Push the paragraph down with Enter presses until it reaches the picture's page. Now delete one line earlier in the document — and watch your careful pushing fall apart. This lesson gives the tool that does not fall apart.

## Why a break, not Enter presses

A **page break** is where one page ends and a new page begins. Word breaks **automatically** when text reaches the bottom — but a **manual break** is needed when, for example, a paragraph describing a picture would be **split from the picture** on the next page. Pressing Enter repeatedly is **rejected**: later edits would shift everything. The Breaks menu also offers **Column** and **Text Wrapping** breaks beside the page break.

[Picture 13325 — split across pages, then together]

## Inserting, in the book's order

1. Place the **insertion point** where the break belongs. 2. **Insert tab → Page Break** — or press **Ctrl+Enter**. The text after the insertion point jumps to the next page, and it stays with its page whatever is edited before it.
