# Video script — Inserting a page break

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Pages end. Word breaks them by itself.
But a paragraph can split from its picture.
Enter, Enter, Enter? No — edits will break it.
Cursor where the break belongs.
Insert. Page Break. Or Ctrl+Enter.
The text jumps — and stays jumped.
