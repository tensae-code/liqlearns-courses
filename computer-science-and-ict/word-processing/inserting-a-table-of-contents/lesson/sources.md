Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Mauritania, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked for the slug and for near matches before building. The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — word-processing/inserting-a-table-of-contents). It stands on teacher-guide evidence only: Mauritania is named here and counted nowhere, and the lesson's countries list is empty.

Mauritania's own way (Grade 7): Informatics is taught for the first time in 1AS as thirty 30-minute teacher fiches moving hardware → information systems (illustrated with Mauritanian systems Bankily, Masrvi, Houwiyeti and the Ministry's Sirage platform) → a long Microsoft Word sequence → a full unit on internet ethics before browsers and search are even introduced.

Mongolia's own way (Grade 9): Grade 9 turns outward to society and real tools — the information society taught through Mongolia's own shift, open-source software through Soyombo Linux (Mongolia's own Linux) and Mongolian OpenOffice, copyright through Mongolia's 2006 copyright law; algorithms are drawn as flowcharts and run in Turtle Art; the long spreadsheet unit grades pupils with nested IFs, builds Fibonacci sequences, grows a 500,000-tögrög bank deposit, and charts the depth and area of Mongolia's great lakes (Khuvsgul, Uvs, Khar-Us); e-services are the real Mongolian ones — the ТҮЦ service kiosk, bookstore.mn and internet banking.

Indonesia's own way (Indonesia Grade 10 G10 Informatika SMK Sem1): The SMK Semester 1 book teaches the same Informatika elements with a logic-first opening (propositions and inference), applied office integration, hands-on network-device configuration, and an OS-installation practical.

Indonesia's own way (Indonesia Grade 10 G10 Informatika SMK teacher guide): Per-meeting teaching guides with activity steps, rubrics and remedial/enrichment for every SMK Grade 10 topic, from logic propositions and number conversion to a full Ubuntu installation demonstration and flowchart activity sets. (teacher guide — evidence)

Vietnam's own way (Grade 10 Chuyên đề Tin học 10 Ứng dụng): the whole Applied study-topics book is one continuous project — each class group role-plays a travel company advertising Vietnamese destinations at a "Ba miền" tourism fair, producing in turn a Word travel handbook, an Excel quotation program, and a PowerPoint media product.

South Sudan's own way (Grade 12 S4 Student): The final year is specialisation: a full databases unit in Microsoft Access with real SQL, forms and reports, normalisation and joins, ending in integrated library management systems; an advanced-formatting unit that goes from Word tables of contents, styles, track changes, templates and mail merge through PowerPoint multimedia to VBA macros in Excel; and a website-design unit built around HTML, CSS and Adobe Dreamweaver with FTP site management.
