# Inserting a Table of Contents — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does Word build a table of contents from?**
**Answer: Heading styles — Heading 1, Heading 2 and their levels**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Does big, bold text count as a heading for the table?**
**Answer: No — only text marked with a heading style**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which style marks a chapter title?**
**Answer: Heading 1**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which style marks a section inside a chapter?**
**Answer: Heading 2**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What second job do heading styles do?**
**Answer: They make headings look even through the whole document**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the table of contents usually stand?**
**Answer: On its own page, after the title page**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which tab holds the Table of Contents command?**
**Answer: The References tab**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does Word collect into the table?**
**Answer: Every styled heading, with its page number**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must you do before building the table?**
**Answer: Put the cursor where the table should stand**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do after headings or pages change?**
**Answer: Click Update Table — never retype the table by hand**
