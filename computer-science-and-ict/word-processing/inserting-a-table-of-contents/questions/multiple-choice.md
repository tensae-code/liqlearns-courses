# Inserting a Table of Contents — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does Word build a table of contents from?**
a) Heading styles — Heading 1, Heading 2 and their levels  b) No — only text marked with a heading style  c) They make headings look even through the whole document  d) Every styled heading, with its page number
**Answer: a) Heading styles — Heading 1, Heading 2 and their levels**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Does big, bold text count as a heading for the table?**
a) Heading 1  b) No — only text marked with a heading style  c) On its own page, after the title page  d) Put the cursor where the table should stand
**Answer: b) No — only text marked with a heading style**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which style marks a chapter title?**
a) Heading 2  b) The References tab  c) Heading 1  d) Click Update Table — never retype the table by hand
**Answer: c) Heading 1**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which style marks a section inside a chapter?**
a) They make headings look even through the whole document  b) Every styled heading, with its page number  c) Heading styles — Heading 1, Heading 2 and their levels  d) Heading 2
**Answer: d) Heading 2**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What second job do heading styles do?**
a) They make headings look even through the whole document  b) On its own page, after the title page  c) Put the cursor where the table should stand  d) No — only text marked with a heading style
**Answer: a) They make headings look even through the whole document**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the table of contents usually stand?**
a) The References tab  b) On its own page, after the title page  c) Click Update Table — never retype the table by hand  d) Heading 1
**Answer: b) On its own page, after the title page**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which tab holds the Table of Contents command?**
a) Every styled heading, with its page number  b) Heading styles — Heading 1, Heading 2 and their levels  c) The References tab  d) Heading 2
**Answer: c) The References tab**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does Word collect into the table?**
a) Put the cursor where the table should stand  b) No — only text marked with a heading style  c) They make headings look even through the whole document  d) Every styled heading, with its page number
**Answer: d) Every styled heading, with its page number**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must you do before building the table?**
a) Put the cursor where the table should stand  b) Click Update Table — never retype the table by hand  c) Heading 1  d) On its own page, after the title page
**Answer: a) Put the cursor where the table should stand**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do after headings or pages change?**
a) Heading styles — Heading 1, Heading 2 and their levels  b) Click Update Table — never retype the table by hand  c) Heading 2  d) The References tab
**Answer: b) Click Update Table — never retype the table by hand**
