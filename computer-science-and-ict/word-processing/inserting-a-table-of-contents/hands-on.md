# Hands-on — Inserting a Table of Contents

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Style, then build
Take a short multi-section document, style its headings, and build an automatic table of contents. Check every entry against the real heading and page.

## Break it and fix it
Change one heading's words and move a section to a new page. Then update the table and show that both changes appear without retyping.
