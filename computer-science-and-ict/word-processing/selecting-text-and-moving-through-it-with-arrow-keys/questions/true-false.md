# Selecting Text and Moving Through It with Arrow Keys — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do the arrow keys move?" is: The caret**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How far does one arrow press move?" is: A small text**
**Answer: False** — One letter sideways; one line up or down

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "For what size of text are arrow keys better?" is: A small text**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why are arrows better for a small text?" is: Marks a piece of text for a command to act on at once**
**Answer: False** — Every step is exact and nothing is skipped

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does selecting do?" is: Marks a piece of text for a command to act on at once**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What are the two ways to select?" is: A large text**
**Answer: False** — Drag the mouse; or hold Shift and extend with the keyboard

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "For what size of text is selecting better?" is: A large text**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does one marked sweep cover?" is: The caret**
**Answer: False** — What a hundred arrow steps would crawl through

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What always shows where you are in the text?" is: The caret**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Must text be chosen before it is copied or formatted?" is: The caret**
**Answer: False** — Yes — selecting comes first
