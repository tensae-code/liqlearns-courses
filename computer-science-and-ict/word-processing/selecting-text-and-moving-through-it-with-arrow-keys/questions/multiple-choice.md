# Selecting Text and Moving Through It with Arrow Keys — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do the arrow keys move?**
a) The caret  b) Every step is exact and nothing is skipped  c) Drag the mouse; or hold Shift and extend with the keyboard  d) What a hundred arrow steps would crawl through
**Answer: a) The caret**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How far does one arrow press move?**
a) Marks a piece of text for a command to act on at once  b) One letter sideways; one line up or down  c) A large text  d) The caret
**Answer: b) One letter sideways; one line up or down**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) For what size of text are arrow keys better?**
a) Drag the mouse; or hold Shift and extend with the keyboard  b) What a hundred arrow steps would crawl through  c) A small text  d) Yes — selecting comes first
**Answer: c) A small text**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why are arrows better for a small text?**
a) A large text  b) The caret  c) The caret  d) Every step is exact and nothing is skipped
**Answer: d) Every step is exact and nothing is skipped**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does selecting do?**
a) Marks a piece of text for a command to act on at once  b) What a hundred arrow steps would crawl through  c) Yes — selecting comes first  d) One letter sideways; one line up or down
**Answer: a) Marks a piece of text for a command to act on at once**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are the two ways to select?**
a) The caret  b) Drag the mouse; or hold Shift and extend with the keyboard  c) The caret  d) A small text
**Answer: b) Drag the mouse; or hold Shift and extend with the keyboard**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) For what size of text is selecting better?**
a) Yes — selecting comes first  b) One letter sideways; one line up or down  c) A large text  d) Every step is exact and nothing is skipped
**Answer: c) A large text**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does one marked sweep cover?**
a) The caret  b) A small text  c) Marks a piece of text for a command to act on at once  d) What a hundred arrow steps would crawl through
**Answer: d) What a hundred arrow steps would crawl through**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What always shows where you are in the text?**
a) The caret  b) One letter sideways; one line up or down  c) Every step is exact and nothing is skipped  d) Drag the mouse; or hold Shift and extend with the keyboard
**Answer: a) The caret**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Must text be chosen before it is copied or formatted?**
a) A small text  b) Yes — selecting comes first  c) Marks a piece of text for a command to act on at once  d) A large text
**Answer: b) Yes — selecting comes first**
