# Writing Personal and Official Letters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A personal letter is written to —**
a) an office you have never visited  b) someone you know — a friend or relative  c) a newspaper
**Answer: b) someone you know** — Its voice is warm because the reader knows you.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The head of a personal letter carries —**
a) your own address and the date  b) the receiver's office address and a subject line  c) a reference number
**Answer: a) your own address and the date**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The subject line of an official letter —**
a) repeats the greeting  b) names the letter's business in a few words, so the office knows what it is about and can file it  c) is the writer's signature
**Answer: b) names the letter's business in a few words**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The body of an official letter should —**
a) tell warm stories first  b) state the matter in order: what you write about, the facts, and exactly what you ask  c) avoid saying what you want
**Answer: b) state the matter in order**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Under the signature of an official letter stands —**
a) nothing  b) a friendly nickname  c) your full name — and your position, if you write in one
**Answer: c) your full name — and your position, if you write in one**

**6. (Sub lesson 1) The voice of a personal letter is —**
a) warm and ordinary, like you speaking to someone who knows you  b) formal and exact  c) the same as an official letter's
**Answer: a) warm and ordinary**
