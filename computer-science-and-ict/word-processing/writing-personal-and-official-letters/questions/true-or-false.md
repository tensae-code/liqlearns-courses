# Writing Personal and Official Letters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A personal letter needs a receiver's address block and a subject line at its head.**
**Answer: False** — Those belong to the official letter; a personal letter's head is your own address and the date.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) An official letter is filed by an office, so its date is part of its record.**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Feelings do the work of facts in a good official letter.**
**Answer: False** — The official letter rests on facts, stated clearly and in order.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A personal letter that does not sound like you, speaking to someone who knows you, is not yet a personal letter.**
**Answer: True**
