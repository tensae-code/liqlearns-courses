# Writing Personal and Official Letters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of a personal letter in order.**
**Answer:** Your own address and the date; a friendly greeting; the body in paragraphs (your news in your own voice); a friendly closing; your name.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name the parts of an official letter in order.**
**Answer:** The sender's address; the receiver's address; the date; a subject line naming the business; a formal greeting; body paragraphs stating the matter, the facts and what you ask; a formal closing; signature and full name (with position, if you write in one).

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Why must an official letter be more complete in its form than a personal letter?**
**Answer:** Because its reader is an office that does not know the writer: nothing except the page carries who the writer is, who it is for and what the matter is, so the form must carry all of it by itself.
