# Writing Personal and Official Letters — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) The few words before the body of an official letter that name its business are its ____ line.**
**Answer: subject**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A personal letter ends with a friendly closing and your ____.**
**Answer: name**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) An official letter's greeting and closing are ____, not friendly.**
**Answer: formal**
