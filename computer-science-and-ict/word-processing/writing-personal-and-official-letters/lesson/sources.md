The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — word-processing/writing-personal-and-official-letters). It sits in the Word Processing title (word-processing/).

Cambodia's own way: ICT is taught through Khmer-language office life — personal, official and business letters, job applications and Cornell note-taking — in OpenOffice Writer, with a dedicated session on typing Khmer script, and the book closes with STEM and e-learning rather than programming. Letter writing is where Cambodia's book turns ICT toward working life: Chapter 2 opens with personal and official letters before the Writer sessions that produce them.

Indonesia's own way (Indonesia Grade 7 G7 Informatika student): Kurikulum Merdeka Informatika built on four elements (Berpikir Komputasional, Literasi Digital, Analisis Data, Algoritma dan Pemrograman), mixing plugged and unplugged activities with coded activity IDs (e.g. VII-BK, VII-LD) and QR-linked resources; Grade 7 covers only BK + Literasi Digital + data handling.
