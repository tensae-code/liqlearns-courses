# Video script — Writing Personal and Official Letters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two letters. Two readers.

A letter to a friend: your address. The date. A warm hello.

Then your news, in your own voice. A warm goodbye. Your name.

A letter to an office is different. The office does not know you.

Your address. The office's address. The date.

One line: the subject. It says what the letter is about.

A formal greeting. Clear paragraphs: the facts, and what you ask.

A formal closing. Your signature. Your full name.

Friend — warm. Office — formal and complete. Choose the right form.
