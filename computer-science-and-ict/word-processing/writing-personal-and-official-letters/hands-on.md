# Hands-on — Writing Personal and Official Letters

The lesson with your hands, in a word processor where one is available.

## Build both skeletons
Open a blank page. First, lay out a personal letter with no body yet — only its parts in order, labelled with a line of space where the body will go. Save it. On a new page, lay out an official letter the same way: both addresses, date, subject line, and spaces for greeting, body, closing, signature and name. Compare the two skeletons side by side and list every difference.

## Fill one of each
Write the personal letter to a real person you know, about real news. Then write the official letter about a real matter to a real office. Keep each to one page.

## The subject test
Cover an official letter's body and read only its subject line aloud to a partner. The partner must say what the letter is about and what it probably asks. If they cannot, rewrite the subject line until they can.
