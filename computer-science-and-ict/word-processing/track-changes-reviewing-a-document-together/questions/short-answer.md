# Track Changes — Reviewing a Document Together — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does Track Changes do?**
**Answer: It marks every edit on the document instead of silently replacing text**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What kinds of change are marked?**
**Answer: Insertions, deletions and formatting changes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How are changes shown?**
**Answer: In colour, with the changer's name — underlined, struck through or in the margin**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a tracked document carry with it?**
**Answer: Its own history of changes as it is reviewed**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is tracking like commenting?**
**Answer: A reviewer improves or questions the document without overwriting its author**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Who decides the fate of each tracked change?**
**Answer: The document's author**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does accepting a change do?**
**Answer: The change becomes plain text in the document**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does rejecting a change do?**
**Answer: The original words stand**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How else can changes be accepted?**
**Answer: All together at once, when the review is trusted**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does Bhutan's Class VII use it at year's end?**
**Answer: Peers review the end-of-year presentation with tracked changes and comments sent by email**
