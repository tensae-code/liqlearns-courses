# Video script — Track Changes — Reviewing a Document Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

With **Track Changes** switched on, Word stops silently replacing text: every **insertion, deletion and formatting change** is marked in colour, with the changer's name, in the margin or underlined and struck through in the text. The document carries its own history as it is reviewed — a classmate or teacher can improve a draft without overwriting its author, exactly as comments question a document without changing it.
The tracked document returns to its **author**, who reads each change and decides: **accept** it and the change becomes plain text; **reject** it and the original words stand. Changes can be accepted one by one or all together when the review is trusted. Bhutan's Class VII year ends with this in real use: the end-of-year presentation is reviewed by peers through tracked changes and comments sent by email.
