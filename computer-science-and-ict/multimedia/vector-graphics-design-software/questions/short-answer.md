# Vector Graphics Design Software — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a bitmap picture?**
**Answer: A grid of coloured dots — pixels**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when a bitmap is enlarged?**
**Answer: It tears into visible squares — it loses quality**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a vector picture?**
**Answer: A description — shapes, lines and curves held as objects with positions and properties**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when a vector picture is enlarged?**
**Answer: Nothing is lost — it scales cleanly, from a stamp to a banner**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which software does Vietnam teach?**
**Answer: Inkscape — free, open-source vector design software**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is everything in a vector editor?**
**Answer: An object — a shape, a path, or a piece of text**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What can be done to an object?**
**Answer: Selected, moved, resized, rotated, recoloured and layered**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What stays true of an object after drawing?**
**Answer: It stays editable — its fill and outline can be changed later**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is designing, in this software?**
**Answer: Arranging objects into the finished product**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What product does the specialist book design?**
**Answer: A decorative postcard, for a national tourism-design contest**
