# Hands-on — Vector Graphics Design Software

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Same picture, two kinds** — Draw a simple star as a bitmap in a paint program and as a vector object in Inkscape. Enlarge both four times and compare.

## Task two
**Postcard start** — Begin a postcard design in Inkscape: a background shape, one drawn object, and its title text — each on its own, editable.
