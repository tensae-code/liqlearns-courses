# Hands-on — Removing hot spots with the Clone Stamp

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Sample and paint on paper
Draw a car with a white shiny patch. On a separate strip, copy the car's clean colour pattern. Hold the strip beside the patch ('Alt-click' — your sample), then colour over the patch matching the sample. Only the light patch changes — Darken mode by hand.

## Flash-fault clinic
Collect or describe three damaged photos — one dark, one red-eyed, one with a hot spot. For each, write the fix from this title: Exposure on a duplicate, the Red Eye Tool, or the Clone Stamp in Darken mode. Save the plan in an 'Image Processing Practice' folder, as the book's practical directs.
