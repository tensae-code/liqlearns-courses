# Removing hot spots with the Clone Stamp — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Give the hot-spot steps in order.
**Answer:** Open the photo; duplicate the layer and work on the copy; select the Clone Stamp Tool; set Blend Mode to Darken; choose a soft-edged brush of 40–50; Alt-click a clean area to sample; paint over the hot spot.

**2.** Why does Darken mode suit hot spots?
**Answer:** In Darken mode only pixels lighter than the sample change — and hot spots are light pixels, so the shine darkens while the rest is left alone.
