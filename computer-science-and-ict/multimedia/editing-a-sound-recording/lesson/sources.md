Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Kazakhstan, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked for the slug and the live lesson content was checked for near matches before building. It sits in the Multimedia title (multimedia/), following the book's own order. Provenance scope note: the placements stocked here are from Kazakhstan's okulyk.kz (OKULYK) textbooks, recommended by the Ministry of Education and Science of the Republic of Kazakhstan — the Digital Literacy and Informatika lines of the Almatykitap, Arman-PV and Atamura teams and the Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools (NIS) books, all student books except the two Kadirkulov teacher guides named in the entries below, whose placements are stocked exactly as filed. Many books exist in parallel language editions (Kazakh / Russian / Uzbek / Uyghur / English) of the same textbook: each distinct book is taught once, and every distinct edition is named and cited in the book entry. Kazakhstan's own ways are kept: one textbook line in up to five parallel language editions, the okulyk.kz digital library as the books' home, Scratch from Grade 1 and robotics strands (LEGO WeDo 2.0 and Mindstorms EV3) from Grade 1–2, and every keyword given in Kazakh–Russian–English in the early grades. Media slots in lesson/lesson.md are described slots only ([Picture — …]); no picture numbers are invented.


Kazakhstan's own way (Kobdikova G2 Digital Literacy): the same six-part national Grade 2 frame as the Almatykitap book, but the tools differ: the "word by word" part builds a spoken-text dialogue between two Scratch characters, sound is recorded and trimmed in the Windows Voice Recorder app (not WavePad), media players AIMP and Windows Media Player are named, the keyboard trainer is the online typingstudy.com Kazakh course, the text editor is WordPad, and the Dancing Robot dances to the Kazakh folk melody "Kamajai" loaded through the EV3 Sound Editor.

Kazakhstan's own way (Isabaeva G2 Digital Literacy): a third Grade 2 course on the same six-part national frame, from the Kokzhiek/Atamura side: the keyboard trainer is RapidTyping 5 with full finger-placement rules, sound is edited in Wave Editor (wave-editor.com) by selecting waveform segments, voice recording also uses a smartphone dictaphone, and the final part has no text-editor lesson — the project algorithm itself is the writing task. Unnumbered end-of-part project and summary pages (printed pp. 27, 50, 84, 99, 114, 136–137) are not placed separately.

Kazakhstan's own way (Isabaeva G2 «Цифровая грамотность»): six sections each built round a cross-cutting theme («Всё обо мне», «Моя семья и друзья», «Моя школа»/«Мой родной край», «В здоровом теле — здоровый дух», «Традиции и фольклор», «Окружающая среда»/«Путешествие»); every section ends in a project, and the year ends with a build-and-programme «Танцующий робот» (Dancing Robot) project in Scratch + LEGO Mindstorms EV3 (the book's own resource list names scratch.mit.edu and the Mindstorms EV3 software).

Kazakhstan's own way (Kobdikova G2 «Цифрлиқ саватлиқ»): a second, parallel Grade 2 textbook from the Arman-PV team follows the same state programme as Book 2 but re-orders it (browser search before files, two sound-editing lessons, robotics taught as «Робототехника: сенсорлар») and writes project ideas in a text editor before building them.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kopeeva G3 «Ақпараттық-коммуникациялық технологиялар»): Grade 3 ICT is a tools year organised by cross-cutting themes («Менің мектебім», «Сәулет», «Өнер»…): text, then sound, then computer pictures in a graphics editor, then a mixed document that combines them, then internet safety/search and presentations — with a practicum closing almost every section.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kadirkulov G3 «Ақпараттық-коммуникациялық технологиялар»): the older Almatykitap Grade 3 ICT book (2018, a different book from the 2021 Digital Literacy, Book 23) is organised by cross-curricular themes — Living Nature, What is Good and What is Bad, Time, Architecture, Art, Outstanding People, Water is the Source of Life, Culture of Rest — and teaches sound (playback, effects, recording, processing) and the graphics editor inside them, ending every theme with a defended mini-project.
