# Mixing Audio Tracks in Audacity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a mix?**
a) What all the tracks sound like playing together at once  b) On its own track  c) By moving the track or its clips along the timeline  d) Balance
**Answer: a) What all the tracks sound like playing together at once**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does each sound sit in Audacity?**
a) By moving the track or its clips along the timeline  b) On its own track  c) Balance  d) Each track's volume is set against the others
**Answer: b) On its own track**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How are a track's parts placed in time?**
a) Balance  b) Each track's volume is set against the others  c) By moving the track or its clips along the timeline  d) Low — speech stays clear above it
**Answer: c) By moving the track or its clips along the timeline**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is mixing, in one word?**
a) Each track's volume is set against the others  b) Low — speech stays clear above it  c) Silences a track without deleting it  d) Balance
**Answer: d) Balance**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is balance set?**
a) Each track's volume is set against the others  b) Low — speech stays clear above it  c) Silences a track without deleting it  d) Plays one track alone so it can be checked
**Answer: a) Each track's volume is set against the others**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where does music sit under speech?**
a) Silences a track without deleting it  b) Low — speech stays clear above it  c) Plays one track alone so it can be checked  d) Listens to the whole, adjusts one track at a time, and listens again
**Answer: b) Low — speech stays clear above it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does Mute do?**
a) Plays one track alone so it can be checked  b) Listens to the whole, adjusts one track at a time, and listens again  c) Silences a track without deleting it  d) When every part can be heard as intended and nothing fights anything else
**Answer: c) Silences a track without deleting it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does Solo do?**
a) Listens to the whole, adjusts one track at a time, and listens again  b) When every part can be heard as intended and nothing fights anything else  c) What all the tracks sound like playing together at once  d) Plays one track alone so it can be checked
**Answer: d) Plays one track alone so it can be checked**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How does the mixer work?**
a) Listens to the whole, adjusts one track at a time, and listens again  b) When every part can be heard as intended and nothing fights anything else  c) What all the tracks sound like playing together at once  d) On its own track
**Answer: a) Listens to the whole, adjusts one track at a time, and listens again**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is a mix finished?**
a) What all the tracks sound like playing together at once  b) When every part can be heard as intended and nothing fights anything else  c) On its own track  d) By moving the track or its clips along the timeline
**Answer: b) When every part can be heard as intended and nothing fights anything else**
