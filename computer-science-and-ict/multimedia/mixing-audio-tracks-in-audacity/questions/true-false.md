# Mixing Audio Tracks in Audacity — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a mix?" is: What all the tracks sound like playing together at once**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does each sound sit in Audacity?" is: Each track's volume is set against the others**
**Answer: False** — On its own track

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How are a track's parts placed in time?" is: By moving the track or its clips along the timeline**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is mixing, in one word?" is: Silences a track without deleting it**
**Answer: False** — Balance

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is balance set?" is: Each track's volume is set against the others**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does music sit under speech?" is: Listens to the whole, adjusts one track at a time, and listens again**
**Answer: False** — Low — speech stays clear above it

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does Mute do?" is: Silences a track without deleting it**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does Solo do?" is: What all the tracks sound like playing together at once**
**Answer: False** — Plays one track alone so it can be checked

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How does the mixer work?" is: Listens to the whole, adjusts one track at a time, and listens again**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is a mix finished?" is: By moving the track or its clips along the timeline**
**Answer: False** — When every part can be heard as intended and nothing fights anything else
