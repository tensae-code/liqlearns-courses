# Mixing Audio Tracks in Audacity — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a mix?**
**Answer: What all the tracks sound like playing together at once**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does each sound sit in Audacity?**
**Answer: On its own track**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How are a track's parts placed in time?**
**Answer: By moving the track or its clips along the timeline**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is mixing, in one word?**
**Answer: Balance**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is balance set?**
**Answer: Each track's volume is set against the others**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where does music sit under speech?**
**Answer: Low — speech stays clear above it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does Mute do?**
**Answer: Silences a track without deleting it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does Solo do?**
**Answer: Plays one track alone so it can be checked**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How does the mixer work?**
**Answer: Listens to the whole, adjusts one track at a time, and listens again**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is a mix finished?**
**Answer: When every part can be heard as intended and nothing fights anything else**
