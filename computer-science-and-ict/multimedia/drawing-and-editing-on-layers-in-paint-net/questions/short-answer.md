# Drawing and Editing on Layers in Paint.NET — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is chosen before the tool in layered drawing?**
**Answer: The layer the part belongs on**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why draw each part on its own layer?**
**Answer: A mistake costs one sheet, not the whole picture**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does duplicating a layer give?**
**Answer: The same part twice — a row of trees from one tree**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Can a whole layer be rotated?**
**Answer: Yes — the sheet turns under its drawing**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What question replaces 'how do I fix this mark?'**
**Answer: 'Which layer is the mark on?'**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's finding tool for pictures?**
**Answer: Google image search**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are the steps of using a found picture?**
**Answer: Search, judge the results, save it into the work**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What duties come with a found picture?**
**Answer: Name its source and respect its maker's rights**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why are finding and copyright taught together?**
**Answer: So the skill and its ethics arrive as one habit**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which editor is this drawing done in?**
**Answer: Paint.NET**
