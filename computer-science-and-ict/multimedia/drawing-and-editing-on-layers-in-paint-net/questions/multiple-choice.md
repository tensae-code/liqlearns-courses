# Drawing and Editing on Layers in Paint.NET — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is chosen before the tool in layered drawing?**
a) The layer the part belongs on  b) A mistake costs one sheet, not the whole picture  c) The same part twice — a row of trees from one tree  d) Yes — the sheet turns under its drawing
