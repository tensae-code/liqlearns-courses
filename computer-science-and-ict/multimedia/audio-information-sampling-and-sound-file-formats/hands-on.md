# Hands-on — Audio Information — Sampling and Sound File Formats

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Sample a wave', 'Draw a smooth wave. Sample it at 5 instants, then at 20, keeping the numbers. Rebuild both waves from the numbers and compare them with the original.')

## Task two
('Name what is kept', 'You are given three files of the same song with wildly different sizes. Argue which is which format family (master, MP3, MIDI) and what evidence decides it.')
