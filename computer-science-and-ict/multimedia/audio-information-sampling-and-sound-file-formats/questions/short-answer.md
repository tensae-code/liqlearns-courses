# Audio Information — Sampling and Sound File Formats — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is sound in the air, and what can the computer hold?**
**Answer: A continuous wave; the computer holds only numbers**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is sampling?**
**Answer: Measuring the wave at regular instants and keeping each measurement as a number**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do the stored numbers rebuild at playback?**
**Answer: A wave near enough to the original for the ear**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does sampling more finely cost?**
**Answer: A larger file**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do WAV and AIF hold?**
**Answer: The samples themselves, full and uncompressed**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does MP3 hold?**
**Answer: A compressed account — samples squeezed by discarding what the ear least misses**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does MID hold?**
**Answer: No sound — instructions: notes, instruments, durations**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) From what is MIDI sound made at playing?**
**Answer: Rebuilt from the instructions**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) State the formats' one question.**
**Answer: What exactly did you keep — the wave, a squeeze of it, or the recipe?**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which formats are the masters?**
**Answer: WAV and AIF — large and true**
