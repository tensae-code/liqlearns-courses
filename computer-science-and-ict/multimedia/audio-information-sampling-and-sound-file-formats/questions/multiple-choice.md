# Audio Information — Sampling and Sound File Formats — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is sound in the air, and what can the computer hold?**
a) A continuous wave; the computer holds only numbers  b) Measuring the wave at regular instants and keeping each measurement as a number  c) A wave near enough to the original for the ear  d) A larger file
**Answer: a) A continuous wave; the computer holds only numbers**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is sampling?**
a) A wave near enough to the original for the ear  b) Measuring the wave at regular instants and keeping each measurement as a number  c) A larger file  d) The samples themselves, full and uncompressed
**Answer: b) Measuring the wave at regular instants and keeping each measurement as a number**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do the stored numbers rebuild at playback?**
a) A larger file  b) The samples themselves, full and uncompressed  c) A wave near enough to the original for the ear  d) A compressed account — samples squeezed by discarding what the ear least misses
**Answer: c) A wave near enough to the original for the ear**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does sampling more finely cost?**
a) The samples themselves, full and uncompressed  b) A compressed account — samples squeezed by discarding what the ear least misses  c) No sound — instructions: notes, instruments, durations  d) A larger file
**Answer: d) A larger file**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do WAV and AIF hold?**
a) The samples themselves, full and uncompressed  b) A compressed account — samples squeezed by discarding what the ear least misses  c) No sound — instructions: notes, instruments, durations  d) Rebuilt from the instructions
**Answer: a) The samples themselves, full and uncompressed**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does MP3 hold?**
a) No sound — instructions: notes, instruments, durations  b) A compressed account — samples squeezed by discarding what the ear least misses  c) Rebuilt from the instructions  d) What exactly did you keep — the wave, a squeeze of it, or the recipe?
**Answer: b) A compressed account — samples squeezed by discarding what the ear least misses**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does MID hold?**
a) Rebuilt from the instructions  b) What exactly did you keep — the wave, a squeeze of it, or the recipe?  c) No sound — instructions: notes, instruments, durations  d) WAV and AIF — large and true
**Answer: c) No sound — instructions: notes, instruments, durations**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) From what is MIDI sound made at playing?**
a) What exactly did you keep — the wave, a squeeze of it, or the recipe?  b) WAV and AIF — large and true  c) A continuous wave; the computer holds only numbers  d) Rebuilt from the instructions
**Answer: d) Rebuilt from the instructions**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) State the formats' one question.**
a) What exactly did you keep — the wave, a squeeze of it, or the recipe?  b) WAV and AIF — large and true  c) A continuous wave; the computer holds only numbers  d) Measuring the wave at regular instants and keeping each measurement as a number
**Answer: a) What exactly did you keep — the wave, a squeeze of it, or the recipe?**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which formats are the masters?**
a) A continuous wave; the computer holds only numbers  b) WAV and AIF — large and true  c) Measuring the wave at regular instants and keeping each measurement as a number  d) A wave near enough to the original for the ear
**Answer: b) WAV and AIF — large and true**
