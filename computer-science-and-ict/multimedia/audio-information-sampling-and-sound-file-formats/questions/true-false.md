# Audio Information — Sampling and Sound File Formats — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is sound in the air, and what can the computer hold?" is: A continuous wave; the computer holds only numbers**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is sampling?" is: A wave near enough to the original for the ear**
**Answer: False** — Measuring the wave at regular instants and keeping each measurement as a number

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do the stored numbers rebuild at playback?" is: A wave near enough to the original for the ear**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does sampling more finely cost?" is: The samples themselves, full and uncompressed**
**Answer: False** — A larger file

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do WAV and AIF hold?" is: The samples themselves, full and uncompressed**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does MP3 hold?" is: No sound — instructions: notes, instruments, durations**
**Answer: False** — A compressed account — samples squeezed by discarding what the ear least misses

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does MID hold?" is: No sound — instructions: notes, instruments, durations**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "From what is MIDI sound made at playing?" is: What exactly did you keep — the wave, a squeeze of it, or the recipe?**
**Answer: False** — Rebuilt from the instructions

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "State the formats' one question." is: What exactly did you keep — the wave, a squeeze of it, or the recipe?**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which formats are the masters?" is: A continuous wave; the computer holds only numbers**
**Answer: False** — WAV and AIF — large and true
