# Designing Characters for Animation — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What must an animated character's design survive?** → **Being drawn again, in any pose, hundreds of times**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a character built from?** → **Simple shapes — circles and ovals**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the silhouette test?** → **Cover the character in black — if it is still recognisably itself, the design works**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a big head read as?** → **Young and comic**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does design start?** → **On paper, before any film is made**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a model sheet?** → **The fixed record of the character — front and side views, expressions, and colours**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How are expressions kept consistent?** → **They are drawn with the same shapes as the character itself**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How many colours does a character get?** → **Few — and constant through the film**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is personality shown?** → **It is drawn — posture, eye size, a signature item of clothing**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What keeps a character the same through a whole film?** → **Every scene is drawn from the model sheet**
