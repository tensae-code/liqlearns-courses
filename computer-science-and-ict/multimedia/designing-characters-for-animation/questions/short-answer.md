# Designing Characters for Animation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What must an animated character's design survive?**
**Answer: Being drawn again, in any pose, hundreds of times**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a character built from?**
**Answer: Simple shapes — circles and ovals**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the silhouette test?**
**Answer: Cover the character in black — if it is still recognisably itself, the design works**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a big head read as?**
**Answer: Young and comic**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does design start?**
**Answer: On paper, before any film is made**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a model sheet?**
**Answer: The fixed record of the character — front and side views, expressions, and colours**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How are expressions kept consistent?**
**Answer: They are drawn with the same shapes as the character itself**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How many colours does a character get?**
**Answer: Few — and constant through the film**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is personality shown?**
**Answer: It is drawn — posture, eye size, a signature item of clothing**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What keeps a character the same through a whole film?**
**Answer: Every scene is drawn from the model sheet**
