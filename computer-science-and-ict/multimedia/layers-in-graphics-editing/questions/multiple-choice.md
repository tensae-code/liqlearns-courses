# Layers in Graphics Editing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a layer?**
a) A transparent sheet holding part of the picture  b) The stack seen from above, through all sheets  c) Covers what is below  d) Shows or hides that layer
**Answer: a) A transparent sheet holding part of the picture**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the visible picture?**
a) Covers what is below  b) The stack seen from above, through all sheets  c) Shows or hides that layer  d) The picture — upper paint covers lower
**Answer: b) The stack seen from above, through all sheets**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the top layer do where its paint is opaque?**
a) Shows or hides that layer  b) The picture — upper paint covers lower  c) Covers what is below  d) One element per layer, named
**Answer: c) Covers what is below**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the eye toggle do?**
a) The picture — upper paint covers lower  b) One element per layer, named  c) Editing while the wrong layer is active  d) Shows or hides that layer
**Answer: d) Shows or hides that layer**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What changes when the stack is reordered?**
a) The picture — upper paint covers lower  b) One element per layer, named  c) Editing while the wrong layer is active  d) A whole layer
**Answer: a) The picture — upper paint covers lower**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the working habit for elements?**
a) Editing while the wrong layer is active  b) One element per layer, named  c) A whole layer  d) Merges the stack into one layer
**Answer: b) One element per layer, named**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What causes most wrong-thing-changed accidents?**
a) A whole layer  b) Merges the stack into one layer  c) Editing while the wrong layer is active  d) Last, on a copy — it cannot be undone into parts
**Answer: c) Editing while the wrong layer is active**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does opacity fade?**
a) Merges the stack into one layer  b) Last, on a copy — it cannot be undone into parts  c) A transparent sheet holding part of the picture  d) A whole layer
**Answer: d) A whole layer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does flattening do?**
a) Merges the stack into one layer  b) Last, on a copy — it cannot be undone into parts  c) A transparent sheet holding part of the picture  d) The stack seen from above, through all sheets
**Answer: a) Merges the stack into one layer**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When and how should flattening happen?**
a) A transparent sheet holding part of the picture  b) Last, on a copy — it cannot be undone into parts  c) The stack seen from above, through all sheets  d) Covers what is below
**Answer: b) Last, on a copy — it cannot be undone into parts**
