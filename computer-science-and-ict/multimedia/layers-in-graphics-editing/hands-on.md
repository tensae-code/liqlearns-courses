# Hands-on — Layers in Graphics Editing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stack sketch
Plan a poster as a layer stack of five layers. List them bottom to top, say what each holds, and predict what hiding the middle layer would change.

## Accident report
A student erases part of a photo while trying to fix the text. Explain in two sentences which layer mistake happened and the habit that prevents it.
