# Hands-on — Multimedia — many forms, one presentation

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Form-spotting walk
Look at a newspaper page, listen to a radio announcement (or imagine one), and look at a phone screen. For each, list the content forms it carries — then say which one is multimedia and why.

## Plan a one-screen presentation
On paper, design one screen about your school that uses all five forms: write the text, describe the audio, sketch the image, say what moves (animation) and what the video shows.


## The impact test (Grade 10)
Take one fact you must remember. Present it three ways on paper: words alone; words plus a drawing; words, drawing, a sound you make, and a moving mime. Test a partner after each version: which presentation did they remember best after five minutes? That difference is retention — the grown lesson's claim, tested by your own hands.
