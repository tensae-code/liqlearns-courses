The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Multimedia — many forms, one presentation**.

Remember the one thing: multimedia combines different content forms — text, audio, image, animation and video — into a single interactive presentation.

Watch the lesson's pictures. Say each key word after me. Then do the Do It Yourself at the end of the lesson — with your own hands.

---

(Grade 10 opening, added 2026-11-12.) Five forms — text, sound, video, graphics, animation — combined on purpose. Together they multiply the message. Together they help you remember. Wherever a person meets electronic information — multimedia belongs.
