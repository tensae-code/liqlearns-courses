## Do it first

## Many forms, one presentation

**Multimedia** combines different content forms — **text, audio, image, animation and video** — into a single interactive presentation.

More fully, multimedia is the computer-based field of presenting, storing, transmitting and combining those forms, with links and tools that let the user **navigate, interact, create and communicate**.

[Picture 20506 — five forms merging into one screen]

Its history in short: newspapers were the first mass medium, carrying text, graphics and images. Then radio came for audio, and television for video broadcasting. Multimedia is the vehicle that carries information from source to destination — all the forms together.
