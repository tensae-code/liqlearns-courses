# Multimedia — many forms, one presentation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words that complete the sentence.

**1.** The five forms are text, audio, image, ____ and video.
**Answer: animation**

**2.** Multimedia is the ____ that carries information from source to destination.
**Answer: vehicle**

**3.** Television was the mass medium for ____ broadcasting.
**Answer: video**
