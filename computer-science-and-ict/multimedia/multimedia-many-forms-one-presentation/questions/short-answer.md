# Multimedia — many forms, one presentation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Define multimedia in one sentence.
**Answer:** Multimedia combines different content forms — text, audio, image, animation and video — into a single interactive presentation.

**2.** Give the short history of the media before multimedia.
**Answer:** Newspapers were the first mass medium (text, graphics, images); then radio for audio; then television for video broadcasting.

**3.** Why do combined media multiply a message's impact?
**Answer:** Because each form carries the message a different way at once — the learner sees, hears and follows together, so the message lands harder and is retained better.

**4.** Name the road the Grade 10 unit walks from this opening, in a few words.
**Answer:** Components and requirements, formats, production and authoring, editing (text, images, sound, movies), transitions and trimming, animation by keyframes and motion guides, and multimedia against hypermedia.
