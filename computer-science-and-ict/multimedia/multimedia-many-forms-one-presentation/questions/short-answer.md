# Multimedia — many forms, one presentation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Define multimedia in one sentence.
**Answer:** Multimedia combines different content forms — text, audio, image, animation and video — into a single interactive presentation.

**2.** Give the short history of the media before multimedia.
**Answer:** Newspapers were the first mass medium (text, graphics, images); then radio for audio; then television for video broadcasting.
