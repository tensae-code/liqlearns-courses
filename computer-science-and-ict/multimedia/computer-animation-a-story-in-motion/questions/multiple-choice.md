# Computer Animation — a Story in Motion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Computer animation is —**
a) a story made to move as a row of ordered moments  b) a single still picture  c) a typed document
**Answer: a) a story made to move as a row of ordered moments**

**2. The book's animation story is about —**
a) a football match  b) a robot artist  c) a lost teddy bear
**Answer: c) a lost teddy bear**

**3. Each moment in an animation should change —**
a) not at all  b) a little from the one before  c) completely, at random
**Answer: b) a little from the one before**

**4. A confusing jump in a story means —**
a) a moment is missing or changed too much  b) the stage is too small  c) there are too many costumes
**Answer: a) a moment is missing or changed too much**
