# Computer Animation — a Story in Motion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Explain, in your own words: say what computer animation is, in your own words.**
**Answer:** The learner's own explanation, true to the lesson: Computer animation makes a story move: a row of scenes and costume changes, each a little different, played in order — as in the book's story "The lost teddy bear".

**2. A classmate gets this lesson's work wrong. What is the most likely fault, and how is it fixed?**
**Answer:** Story jumps and confuses → a moment changed too much or is missing: add the in-between moment, small enough that the eye can follow it.
