**Acceptance count: taught in 6 countries (Ethiopia, Fiji, Bhutan, Sri Lanka, Armenia, Vietnam).**

Nothing in this lesson claims a page the placements do not give; the lesson's content is the placements' content, re-expressed for the learner, with the book's own step orders, examples and Ethiopian practicals kept. Procedures are taught exactly as the book teaches them — Photoshop first, with the GIMP alternative where the book notes it.

Fiji's own way (Year 11 Computer Education student book): The first locally written text of its kind (the book's Preface): written by a panel of Computer Studies officers and University of the South Pacific lecturers for the new Year 11 Computer Education syllabus trialled in 2014; each chapter uses "Discovery Learning" prompts, and the database chapter is a full Access 2007 practical building a Student database in a "Lab Chapter 3" folder.

Bhutan's own way (Class VII, Literacy with ICT student book): Social media is taught hands-on — learners create a Facebook account, post with audience settings, and follow netiquette and an Acceptable Use Policy; backup is taught to Google Drive, and the end-of-year experience is shared on Facebook.

Sri Lanka's own way (Grade 11, Tamil): a full Visual Basic 6.0 programming course inside the O/L book (IDE, controls, data types, Select Case, arrays), an SDLC chapter, Photoshop-based multimedia, hand-coded HTML with Sri Lankan examples (the Sinharaja rainforest page, ucsc.cmb.ac.lk addresses, sltnet.lk domains), and sector chapters (agriculture, industry, government, commerce) before malware and health.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 6 G6 student): Same author pair as Grade 5, one year on: the § sequence moves from society/data-rights into number systems and computing systems, then a long spreadsheet-and-diagrams block, and ends with algorithms in flowcharts and a block-based programming language.

Vietnam's own way (Grade 8 Tin học 8): Grade 8 opens with a history of calculating tools (abacus, Pascal's Pascaline, computer generations) and its applied theme is a genuine fork — track a does advanced Word/PowerPoint, track b learns photo editing in GIMP; programming is Scratch again, but now with data representation and a named debugging lesson (Gỡ lỗi).

Vietnam's own way (Grade 11 Tin học 11 Tin học ứng dụng): Bài 1–16 are the same common core as the Computer Science orientation book (spot-checked page-identical, e.g. Bài 1); the Applied-only second half is hands-on database administration in Microsoft Access (through backup), then photo editing in GIMP and film-making/animation.
