# Image processing software — meeting Photoshop

## Do it first

Before reading on, name every program you have ever used to change a picture — fix a dark photo, cut something out, make a card. This lesson puts them on one shelf and picks the one you will learn deeply.

## The shelf of programs — and the one in front

**Image processing software** organises, retouches and edits images from CDs/DVDs, scans or cameras. It can crop out unwanted things — the book's examples are fixing a red-eye effect, and an image with one person's head put on another's body.

The software can be commercial or free. Named programs: **Photoshop, MS Paint, GIMP, Paint 3D, Photo Styler, Photo Editor and Photo Explosion**.

[Picture 20509 — the shelf of programs, Photoshop forward]

**Photoshop** is a general-purpose program for **raster** (pixel-based) images and **vector graphics** — among the most popular image programs today. It is used by photographers, graphic designers, video-game artists, advertising and meme designers, and it can build website layouts, posters, business cards and greeting cards.

Through this title, Photoshop is taught first; where the book notes the GIMP way of doing the same job, the GIMP way is given with it.
