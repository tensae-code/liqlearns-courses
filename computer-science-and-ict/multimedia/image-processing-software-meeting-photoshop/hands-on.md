# Hands-on — Image processing software — meeting Photoshop

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Red-eye detective
Find a photo taken with flash where someone's eyes look red (or imagine one clearly). Write what image processing software would do to it — this is the red-eye example the book opens with; you will fix it for real in the red-eye lesson.

## Who uses Photoshop?
Match the users: photographer, graphic designer, video-game artist, advertising designer, meme designer. For each, write one thing they would make with Photoshop.
