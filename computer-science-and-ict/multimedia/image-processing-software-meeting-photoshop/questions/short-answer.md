# Image processing software — meeting Photoshop — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Name four programs that process images.
**Answer:** Any four of: Photoshop, MS Paint, GIMP, Paint 3D, Photo Styler, Photo Editor, Photo Explosion.

**2.** Who uses Photoshop? Name three kinds of user.
**Answer:** Any three of: photographers, graphic designers, video-game artists, advertising designers, meme designers.
