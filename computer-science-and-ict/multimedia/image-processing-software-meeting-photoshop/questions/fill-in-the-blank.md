# Image processing software — meeting Photoshop — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words that complete the sentence.

**1.** Photoshop works with ____ images — images based on pixels.
**Answer: raster**

**2.** The free alternative named again and again in this unit is ____.
**Answer: GIMP**

**3.** Software that fixes and improves images is said to ____ them.
**Answer: retouch**
