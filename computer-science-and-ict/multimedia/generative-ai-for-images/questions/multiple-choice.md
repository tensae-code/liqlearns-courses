# Generative AI for Images — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a generative image model produce from a prompt?**
a) A new image matching the description  b) Huge collections of images paired with text  c) No — it is new; no such photo existed  d) Subject, style, colours and composition
**Answer: a) A new image matching the description**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What was the model trained on?**
a) No — it is new; no such photo existed  b) Huge collections of images paired with text  c) Subject, style, colours and composition  d) How descriptions and pictures correspond
**Answer: b) Huge collections of images paired with text**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Is a generated image fetched from somewhere?**
a) Subject, style, colours and composition  b) How descriptions and pictures correspond  c) No — it is new; no such photo existed  d) Precision — subject, style, setting and details
**Answer: c) No — it is new; no such photo existed**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What can a prompt steer?**
a) How descriptions and pictures correspond  b) Precision — subject, style, setting and details  c) Generate, judge, sharpen the prompt, repeat  d) Subject, style, colours and composition
**Answer: d) Subject, style, colours and composition**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does training teach the model?**
