# Generative AI for Images — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a generative image model produce from a prompt?**
**Answer: A new image matching the description**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What was the model trained on?**
**Answer: Huge collections of images paired with text**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Is a generated image fetched from somewhere?**
**Answer: No — it is new; no such photo existed**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What can a prompt steer?**
**Answer: Subject, style, colours and composition**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does training teach the model?**
**Answer: How descriptions and pictures correspond**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What makes a good prompt?**
**Answer: Precision — subject, style, setting and details**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is iteration here?**
**Answer: Generate, judge, sharpen the prompt, repeat**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two things generators often fumble.**
**Answer: Hands and written text (factual details also counts)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must machine-made work be given?**
**Answer: Honest credit — it is not a photograph of reality**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Whose rights must be respected?**
**Answer: Copyright holders and people's likenesses**
