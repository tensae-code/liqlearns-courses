# Hands-on — Generative AI for Images

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Prompt ladder
Write a vague prompt for a school poster image, generate or imagine its result, then sharpen it twice with added detail. Keep all three prompts and note what each addition should change.

## Judge the picture
Take any AI-looking image (from the web or a classmate's generation). List three things to check before trusting or using it, from the lesson, and your verdict on each.
