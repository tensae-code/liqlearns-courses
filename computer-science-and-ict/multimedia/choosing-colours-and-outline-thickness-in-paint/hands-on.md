# Hands-on — Choosing Colours and Outline Thickness in Paint

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Same shape, two ways
Draw a simple shape twice in Paint. Give the first a thin outline in one colour, the second a thick outline in another. Say what changed and which you prefer.

## Personalise a drawing
Open a drawing from the earlier séances. Change the colours of two parts and the thickness of one outline to make it your own. Save it with your name in its file name.
