# Choosing Colours and Outline Thickness in Paint — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where are Paint's colours offered?" is: In a palette of ready colours**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is a colour chosen?" is: How wide the tool's stroke is**
**Answer: False** — By clicking it in the palette

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the chosen colour become?" is: The colour the next drawing will use**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "When must a colour be chosen?" is: It makes the main shapes stand out bold**
**Answer: False** — Before drawing the part it colours

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does personalise mean in this lesson?" is: Making the drawing your own by your colour choices**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the thickness choice set?" is: Thick outlines for main shapes, thin lines for details**
**Answer: False** — How wide the tool's stroke is

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is a thin outline suited to?" is: Small details and writing**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does a thick outline do?" is: By clicking it in the palette**
**Answer: False** — It makes the main shapes stand out bold

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is thickness chosen?" is: First — before drawing with it**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How can one drawing use both thicknesses?" is: Before drawing the part it colours**
**Answer: False** — Thick outlines for main shapes, thin lines for details
