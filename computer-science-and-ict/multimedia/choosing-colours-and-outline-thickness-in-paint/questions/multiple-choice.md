# Choosing Colours and Outline Thickness in Paint — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where are Paint's colours offered?**
a) In a palette of ready colours  b) By clicking it in the palette  c) The colour the next drawing will use  d) Before drawing the part it colours
**Answer: a) In a palette of ready colours**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is a colour chosen?**
a) The colour the next drawing will use  b) By clicking it in the palette  c) Before drawing the part it colours  d) Making the drawing your own by your colour choices
**Answer: b) By clicking it in the palette**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the chosen colour become?**
a) Before drawing the part it colours  b) Making the drawing your own by your colour choices  c) The colour the next drawing will use  d) How wide the tool's stroke is
**Answer: c) The colour the next drawing will use**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When must a colour be chosen?**
a) Making the drawing your own by your colour choices  b) How wide the tool's stroke is  c) Small details and writing  d) Before drawing the part it colours
**Answer: d) Before drawing the part it colours**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does personalise mean in this lesson?**
a) Making the drawing your own by your colour choices  b) How wide the tool's stroke is  c) Small details and writing  d) It makes the main shapes stand out bold
**Answer: a) Making the drawing your own by your colour choices**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the thickness choice set?**
a) Small details and writing  b) How wide the tool's stroke is  c) It makes the main shapes stand out bold  d) First — before drawing with it
**Answer: b) How wide the tool's stroke is**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a thin outline suited to?**
a) It makes the main shapes stand out bold  b) First — before drawing with it  c) Small details and writing  d) Thick outlines for main shapes, thin lines for details
**Answer: c) Small details and writing**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a thick outline do?**
a) First — before drawing with it  b) Thick outlines for main shapes, thin lines for details  c) In a palette of ready colours  d) It makes the main shapes stand out bold
**Answer: d) It makes the main shapes stand out bold**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is thickness chosen?**
a) First — before drawing with it  b) Thick outlines for main shapes, thin lines for details  c) In a palette of ready colours  d) By clicking it in the palette
**Answer: a) First — before drawing with it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How can one drawing use both thicknesses?**
a) In a palette of ready colours  b) Thick outlines for main shapes, thin lines for details  c) By clicking it in the palette  d) The colour the next drawing will use
**Answer: b) Thick outlines for main shapes, thin lines for details**
