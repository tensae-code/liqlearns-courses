# Choosing Colours and Outline Thickness in Paint — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where are Paint's colours offered?**
**Answer: In a palette of ready colours**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is a colour chosen?**
**Answer: By clicking it in the palette**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the chosen colour become?**
**Answer: The colour the next drawing will use**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When must a colour be chosen?**
**Answer: Before drawing the part it colours**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does personalise mean in this lesson?**
**Answer: Making the drawing your own by your colour choices**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the thickness choice set?**
**Answer: How wide the tool's stroke is**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a thin outline suited to?**
**Answer: Small details and writing**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a thick outline do?**
**Answer: It makes the main shapes stand out bold**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is thickness chosen?**
**Answer: First — before drawing with it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How can one drawing use both thicknesses?**
**Answer: Thick outlines for main shapes, thin lines for details**
