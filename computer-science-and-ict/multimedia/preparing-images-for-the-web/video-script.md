# Video script — Preparing Images for the Web

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A camera photograph is made to be printed and kept; a web page needs it **no bigger than the place it fills**.
Preparation starts with **size**: shrink the image's dimensions to the display size — a picture shown small but stored huge wastes loading time on detail nobody sees.
The saving **format** is the second decision.
Photographs, with their millions of shades, belong in a format made for photographs, which squeezes them by dropping detail the eye barely misses.
