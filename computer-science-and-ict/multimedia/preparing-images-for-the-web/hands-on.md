# Hands-on — Preparing Images for the Web

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shrink to the slot
Take one camera photograph. Measure the slot it must fill on a page, resize a copy to that size, and compare the two files' weights. Say what was lost and whether the eye can see it at display size.

## Pick the format
Save the same photograph and the same flat-colour logo in two formats each. Compare weights and zoom into the edges; write which format suits each kind of image and why.
