# Learning a Craft From a Tutorial Video — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The video is the teacher — watch in steps, not all at once
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A tutorial video — a maker showing how a real thing is made, step by step; In steps — watch one step, pause, and do that step yourself; Everything the maker lists — all materials and tools; Real physical products — a craft product, then a household item; Because the learning is in doing each step as it is shown.

## Sub lesson 2 — Hands follow eyes — checking, fixing, finishing
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Your piece against the video's piece — same fold, same join, same size; Rewind and watch that step again before going on; A small error carried forward becomes a big one; A finished, usable product; A video can teach your hands almost anything, if the hands work while the eyes watch.
