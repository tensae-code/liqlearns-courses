# Hands-on — Learning a Craft From a Tutorial Video

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Follow a maker** — Choose a short craft tutorial. Gather everything it lists, then make the product step by step, pausing the video for each stage.

## Task two
**Teach it back** — Show your finished product and name the step where you had to rewind. Say what the video showed that your first try missed.
