# Learning a Craft From a Tutorial Video — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A tutorial video — a maker showing how a real thing is made, step by step**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A tutorial video — a maker showing how a real thing is made, step by step** — as the answer to: How is the video watched?
**Answer: False — In steps — watch one step, pause, and do that step yourself**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Everything the maker lists — all materials and tools**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Everything the maker lists — all materials and tools** — as the answer to: What products does track B make?
**Answer: False — Real physical products — a craft product, then a household item**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Because the learning is in doing each step as it is shown**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Because the learning is in doing each step as it is shown** — as the answer to: What do you compare at every pause?
**Answer: False — Your piece against the video's piece — same fold, same join, same size**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Rewind and watch that step again before going on**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Rewind and watch that step again before going on** — as the answer to: Why fix errors early?
**Answer: False — A small error carried forward becomes a big one**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A finished, usable product**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A finished, usable product** — as the answer to: What is the track's real lesson beside the craft?
**Answer: False — A video can teach your hands almost anything, if the hands work while the eyes watch**
