# Learning a Craft From a Tutorial Video — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Who is the teacher in track B?**
**Answer: A tutorial video — a maker showing how a real thing is made, step by step**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is the video watched?**
**Answer: In steps — watch one step, pause, and do that step yourself**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is gathered before starting?**
**Answer: Everything the maker lists — all materials and tools**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What products does track B make?**
**Answer: Real physical products — a craft product, then a household item**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why not watch the whole video first like a story?**
**Answer: Because the learning is in doing each step as it is shown**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do you compare at every pause?**
**Answer: Your piece against the video's piece — same fold, same join, same size**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do where yours differs?**
**Answer: Rewind and watch that step again before going on**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why fix errors early?**
**Answer: A small error carried forward becomes a big one**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What stands at the end?**
**Answer: A finished, usable product**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the track's real lesson beside the craft?**
**Answer: A video can teach your hands almost anything, if the hands work while the eyes watch**
