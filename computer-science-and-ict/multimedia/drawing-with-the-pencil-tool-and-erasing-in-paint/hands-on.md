# Hands-on — Drawing with the Pencil Tool and Erasing in Paint

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Free-hand outline
In Paint, draw the outline of a house using only the pencil tool — roof, walls, door, one window. Keep the mouse slow and steady.

## Correct with the eraser
Swap drawings with your partner. Each of you rubs out exactly one thing in the other's drawing with the eraser, in short strokes, without touching the rest. Say what you removed and why.
