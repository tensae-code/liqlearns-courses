# Drawing with the Pencil Tool and Erasing in Paint — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the pencil tool draw?**
a) A thin free line that follows the mouse  b) Press the button, move the mouse, release  c) It makes any line at all — nothing corrects it into a ready shape  d) Wavy and zigzag lines, a signature, an outline
**Answer: a) A thin free line that follows the mouse**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is a pencil line made?**
a) It makes any line at all — nothing corrects it into a ready shape  b) Press the button, move the mouse, release  c) Wavy and zigzag lines, a signature, an outline  d) A slow, steady hand
**Answer: b) Press the button, move the mouse, release**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is the pencil unlike the shape tools?**
a) Wavy and zigzag lines, a signature, an outline  b) A slow, steady hand  c) It makes any line at all — nothing corrects it into a ready shape  d) It removes drawing where it passes
**Answer: c) It makes any line at all — nothing corrects it into a ready shape**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What can the pencil draw that shapes cannot?**
a) A slow, steady hand  b) It removes drawing where it passes  c) Drag it over the part to rub out  d) Wavy and zigzag lines, a signature, an outline
**Answer: d) Wavy and zigzag lines, a signature, an outline**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a surer pencil line?**
a) A slow, steady hand  b) It removes drawing where it passes  c) Drag it over the part to rub out  d) Small corrections — one wrong line, one stray mark
**Answer: a) A slow, steady hand**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the eraser do?**
a) Drag it over the part to rub out  b) It removes drawing where it passes  c) Small corrections — one wrong line, one stray mark  d) Because it removes good lines it crosses, too
**Answer: b) It removes drawing where it passes**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the eraser used?**
a) Small corrections — one wrong line, one stray mark  b) Because it removes good lines it crosses, too  c) Drag it over the part to rub out  d) Draw bravely, correct precisely
**Answer: c) Drag it over the part to rub out**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the eraser for?**
a) Because it removes good lines it crosses, too  b) Draw bravely, correct precisely  c) A thin free line that follows the mouse  d) Small corrections — one wrong line, one stray mark
**Answer: d) Small corrections — one wrong line, one stray mark**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why use the eraser in short, careful strokes?**
a) Because it removes good lines it crosses, too  b) Draw bravely, correct precisely  c) A thin free line that follows the mouse  d) Press the button, move the mouse, release
**Answer: a) Because it removes good lines it crosses, too**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How do the pencil and eraser work as a pair?**
a) A thin free line that follows the mouse  b) Draw bravely, correct precisely  c) Press the button, move the mouse, release  d) It makes any line at all — nothing corrects it into a ready shape
**Answer: b) Draw bravely, correct precisely**
