# Drawing with the Pencil Tool and Erasing in Paint — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the pencil tool draw?**
**Answer: A thin free line that follows the mouse**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is a pencil line made?**
**Answer: Press the button, move the mouse, release**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is the pencil unlike the shape tools?**
**Answer: It makes any line at all — nothing corrects it into a ready shape**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What can the pencil draw that shapes cannot?**
**Answer: Wavy and zigzag lines, a signature, an outline**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a surer pencil line?**
**Answer: A slow, steady hand**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the eraser do?**
**Answer: It removes drawing where it passes**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the eraser used?**
**Answer: Drag it over the part to rub out**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the eraser for?**
**Answer: Small corrections — one wrong line, one stray mark**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why use the eraser in short, careful strokes?**
**Answer: Because it removes good lines it crosses, too**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How do the pencil and eraser work as a pair?**
**Answer: Draw bravely, correct precisely**
