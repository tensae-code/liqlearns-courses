# Drawing with the Pencil Tool and Erasing in Paint — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the pencil tool draw?" is: A thin free line that follows the mouse**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is a pencil line made?" is: It removes drawing where it passes**
**Answer: False** — Press the button, move the mouse, release

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is the pencil unlike the shape tools?" is: It makes any line at all — nothing corrects it into a ready shape**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What can the pencil draw that shapes cannot?" is: Small corrections — one wrong line, one stray mark**
**Answer: False** — Wavy and zigzag lines, a signature, an outline

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What makes a surer pencil line?" is: A slow, steady hand**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the eraser do?" is: Draw bravely, correct precisely**
**Answer: False** — It removes drawing where it passes

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is the eraser used?" is: Drag it over the part to rub out**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the eraser for?" is: Press the button, move the mouse, release**
**Answer: False** — Small corrections — one wrong line, one stray mark

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why use the eraser in short, careful strokes?" is: Because it removes good lines it crosses, too**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How do the pencil and eraser work as a pair?" is: Wavy and zigzag lines, a signature, an outline**
**Answer: False** — Draw bravely, correct precisely
