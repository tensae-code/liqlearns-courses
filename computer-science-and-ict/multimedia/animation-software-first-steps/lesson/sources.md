Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked for the slug and the live lesson content was checked for near matches before building. The live animation lessons teach what animation is and keyframing inside other tools; meeting dedicated animation software itself — stage, timeline, frames, layers — as a first-steps lesson is Vietnam's specialist opening, taught by no live lesson. The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — multimedia/animation-software-first-steps). It sits in the Multimedia title (multimedia/), following the book's own order. Provenance scope note: the placements stocked here are from Vietnam's “Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống” (Connecting Knowledge with Life) Tin học books (Vietnamese) — one series, Grades 3–12, from Nhà xuất bản Giáo dục Việt Nam, splitting from Grade 10 into Computer Science (Khoa học máy tính) and Applied Informatics (Tin học ứng dụng) orientations, each with its own specialist-topic (Chuyên đề) volumes; the books are image-only PDFs read from rendered pages. Every placement stocked here is from a student book and counts toward the acceptance count; there are no teacher guides in this country. Kazakhstan's own way (Heathcote ICT Grade 10): an imported British ICT course (Express Publishing's Grammar Schools series) adapted for Kazakhstan — ten modules that include two topics the national books do not teach at all: expert and control systems, and developing a mobile app.
