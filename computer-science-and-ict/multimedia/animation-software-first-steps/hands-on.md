# Hands-on — Animation Software — First Steps

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Workplace tour** — Open an animation program. Name its stage, timeline and layers aloud, set the stage size, and add ten frames.

## Task two
**First motion** — Animate one simple thing — a bouncing ball — in about twelve frames. Play it, fix the worst frame, and play it again.
