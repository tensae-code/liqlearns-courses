# Animation Software — First Steps — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the stage?** → **The picture the audience sees — the film's canvas**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the timeline?** → **The film's length, cut into frames, under the stage**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is one frame?** → **One still picture in the row**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How do frames become motion?** → **Played in order at speed, the still pictures become movement**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are the first steps in the software?** → **Set the stage size, add frames, draw on a frame, and play the row**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why are layers used?** → **So the background and each character can change without redrawing the rest**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a first animation move?** → **One thing, simply — a bouncing ball, a blinking face**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What are the two ways to make the change?** → **Draw it frame by frame, or set key positions the software fills between**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the beginner's discipline?** → **Few frames, one moving thing, play it often**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why play often?** → **So no error grows too big to find**
