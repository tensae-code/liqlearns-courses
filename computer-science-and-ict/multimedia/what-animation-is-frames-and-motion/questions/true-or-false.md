# What Animation Is — Frames and Motion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In an animation, the drawn thing itself truly moves across the screen.**
**Answer: False** — Nothing moves; the pictures change, and the eye joins the fast row into movement.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A flip book is an animation.**
**Answer: True** — Its pages are frames, flipped fast enough to read as motion.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Raising the frame rate makes movement smoother.**
**Answer: True** — More frames per second means smaller steps between positions.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) In computer animation, the software can work out the in-between frames from the key frames the animator sets.**
**Answer: True** — The animator sets the key frames; the computer fills the steps between.
