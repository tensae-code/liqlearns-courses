# What Animation Is — Frames and Motion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Animation is —**
a) a photograph of a moving thing  b) movement made from a row of still pictures, each changed a little, shown fast  c) a sound effect added to a video
**Answer: b) movement made from a row of still pictures, each changed a little, shown fast** — Frames plus speed is the whole idea.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A frame is —**
a) the border around a screen  b) one single still picture in an animation's row  c) the machine that shows the animation
**Answer: b) one single still picture in an animation's row**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Neighbouring frames in an animation differ —**
a) completely — each is a new scene  b) by a little — the thing drawn a step further along  c) not at all
**Answer: b) by a little — the thing drawn a step further along** — Big jumps read as teleports, not movement.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Frames per second (fps) counts —**
a) how many frames pass in one second  b) how long one frame is in centimetres  c) how many colours a frame holds
**Answer: a) how many frames pass in one second**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) When the frame rate is too low, the movement looks —**
a) smoother  b) jerky, with gaps  c) brighter
**Answer: b) jerky, with gaps** — The eye starts separating the frames again.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Key frames are —**
a) the frames that hold the important positions, drawn first  b) the frames thrown away  c) frames shown only once a year
**Answer: a) the frames that hold the important positions, drawn first** — The in-between frames are then filled in between them.
