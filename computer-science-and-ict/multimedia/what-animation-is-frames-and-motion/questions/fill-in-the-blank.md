# What Animation Is — Frames and Motion — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) One single still picture in an animation's row is called a ____.**
**Answer: frame**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The speed of an animation is counted in frames per ____.**
**Answer: second**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The important positions, drawn first, are the ____ frames.**
**Answer: key**
