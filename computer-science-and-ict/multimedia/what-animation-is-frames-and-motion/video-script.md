# Video script — What Animation Is — Frames and Motion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nothing on the screen really moves.

One picture. Then the next picture — a little bit changed.

Show them slowly: flicker. Show them fast: movement!

Fast is counted in frames per second.

More frames, smoother movement.

That is animation: still pictures, standing in a row, shown fast.
