# Hands-on — Clip speed and duration — freeze frames

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Create, trim, re-time, freeze (the book's practical)
Take one clip for the class: trim it, change its speed once each way, then freeze one frame and insert the still at that frame's place. Present the before and after.

## Frame counting
Count the steps of a partner crossing the room at normal speed. Predict the step count visible at double speed (half the frames shown) and at half speed — then act both out and check.
