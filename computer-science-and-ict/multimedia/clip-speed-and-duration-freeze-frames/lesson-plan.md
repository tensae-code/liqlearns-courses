# Lesson plan — Clip speed and duration — freeze frames

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Multimedia · **Lesson:** Clip speed and duration — freeze frames
**Place in the curriculum:** Ethiopia Grade 10, Unit 4 — Image Processing and Multimedia (printed pp. 139–181).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** speed and duration are tied — speeding removes frames and shortens a clip, slowing repeats frames and lengthens it — duration runs from In point to Out point, and a freeze frame exports one frame as a still and plays it in place.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Trimming video clips: after the weak parts are cut, the kept parts can be re-timed — and one frame can be held.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Speed and duration — tied together** — speeding removes frames and shortens, audio pitch rises unless pitch is maintained; slowing repeats frames and lengthens; duration from In to Out; stills can take any duration.
2. **Freeze frames — holding one frame** — export one frame as a still; Freeze Frame in the Monitor panel makes the still, inserted at the frame's location so the video starts still, then moves.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Trimming video clips — smart trim. Recommended, never forced. A learner can land here directly; the lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain how speeding and slowing change frames, length and pitch.
2. Define duration and the two ways it is changed; state the still-image rule.
3. Export and insert a freeze frame by the book's sequence.
## What you need
A computer with Premiere Elements for the real doing; a room to walk across for the rehearsal.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks slowing adds new filmed frames → slowing repeats frames; nothing new was filmed. Speeding removes frames.
- Expects pitch to stay put at double speed → pitch rises with speed unless the maintain-pitch option is used.
