# Clip speed and duration — freeze frames — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Speeding a clip —
a) adds frames and lengthens it  b) removes frames and shortens it  c) changes nothing  d) deletes its audio
**Answer: b) removes frames and shortens it** — the tie, forward.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Slowing a clip —
a) removes frames  b) repeats frames and lengthens it  c) freezes it  d) exports it
**Answer: b) repeats frames and lengthens it** — the tie, backward.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** At higher speed, audio pitch —
a) falls  b) stays always the same  c) rises, unless the maintain-pitch option is used  d) disappears
**Answer: c) rises, unless the maintain-pitch option is used** — pitch follows speed by default.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Speed is changed —
a) from the Timeline only  b) from any panel  c) from the library  d) on paper
**Answer: a) from the Timeline only** — Timeline only.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Duration runs from —
a) first clip to last clip  b) In point to Out point  c) title to credits  d) import to export
**Answer: b) In point to Out point** — first frame to last frame.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** Still images, unlike video, can be set to —
a) no duration  b) any duration at all  c) one second only  d) the clip's duration
**Answer: b) any duration at all** — the still-image rule.

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** A freeze frame is made by pressing Freeze Frame in —
a) the Task panel  b) the Monitor panel  c) Color Swatches  d) the Timeline ruler
**Answer: b) the Monitor panel** — the Monitor panel.

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** The made still is inserted —
a) at the end of the film  b) at the original frame's location  c) in the library only  d) before the title
**Answer: b) at the original frame's location** — so the video starts still, then moves.
