# Video script — Clip speed and duration — freeze frames

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Speed, duration — and the freeze**.

Faster: frames removed, clip shorter, voice higher.
Slower: frames repeated, clip longer.
Duration: from In point to Out point.
And the freeze: one frame becomes a still. The film starts frozen — then moves!

Re-time it. Hold it. Your clip obeys.
