# Storyboarding — planning before making — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Filmmakers use storyboards to move ideas from mind to screen.
**Answer: True** — that is the book's picture of it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The outline comes before the script in the six steps.
**Answer: False** — script first (step 3), then outline (step 4), then flow chart (step 5).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A storyboard may include audio and text as well as sketches.
**Answer: True** — video, sketch, text, audio, photos and prototypes are all named.
