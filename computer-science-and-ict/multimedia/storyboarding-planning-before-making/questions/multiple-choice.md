# Storyboarding — planning before making — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A storyboard is —
a) the finished film  b) a graphic layout sequencing illustrations and images to tell a story visually  c) a list of actors  d) a sound recording
**Answer: b) a graphic layout sequencing illustrations and images to tell a story visually** — scene-by-scene visual planning.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Storyboards tell the story —
a) in one big picture  b) scene by scene  c) in words only  d) backwards
**Answer: b) scene by scene** — scene by scene.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A storyboard can be made —
a) only in expensive software  b) in software, or as a rough paper-and-pencil sketch  c) only by filmmakers  d) only on video
**Answer: b) in software, or as a rough paper-and-pencil sketch** — software or paper and pencil.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The FIRST planning step is —
a) develop the storyboard  b) define the goals and objectives of the proposed title  c) write the script  d) draw the flow chart
**Answer: b) define the goals and objectives of the proposed title** — goals and objectives first.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The storyboard itself is developed at step —
a) 2  b) 4  c) 6  d) 3
**Answer: c) 6** — the sixth and last step.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a named storyboarding tool?
a) Storyboarder  b) WordPad  c) Calculator  d) Paint only
**Answer: a) Storyboarder** — Storyboarder is named in the guide.

**7. (Sub lesson 1)** Storyboarding also delineates the program's —
a) budget  b) navigation scheme  c) cast  d) music
**Answer: b) navigation scheme** — how the user moves through the product.
