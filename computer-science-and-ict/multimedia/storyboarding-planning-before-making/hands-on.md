# Hands-on — Storyboarding — planning before making

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Paper storyboard
On A3 paper or a chart board, draw the alphabet-video storyboard: at least four scenes, each with a sketch, the letter it teaches, and a note of any sound or text. Present it to the class.

## Storyboard in slides
Build the same storyboard in PowerPoint — one slide per scene, with a picture placeholder and a caption. Compare: which version was easier to change?
