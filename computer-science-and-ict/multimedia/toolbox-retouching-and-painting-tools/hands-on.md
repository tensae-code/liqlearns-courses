# Hands-on — Toolbox — retouching and painting tools

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Tool-job matching cards
Write the tool names on cards and the jobs on other cards. Shuffle and match them: 'removes blemishes quickly' → Spot Healing Brush; 'samples colours' → Eyedropper; 'darkens areas' → Burn.

## Clone stamp on paper
Draw a wall with a stain on it. On a second sheet, draw the clean brick pattern. 'Sample' the clean pattern with your eye and copy it over the stain drawing — you have done the Clone Stamp's job by hand.
