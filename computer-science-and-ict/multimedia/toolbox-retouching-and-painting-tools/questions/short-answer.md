# Toolbox — retouching and painting tools — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Name the tools in the Spot Healing Brush family.
**Answer:** Healing Brush, Patch, Content-Aware Move and Red Eye (with the Spot Healing Brush itself).

**2.** What is the difference between the Brush and the Clone Stamp?
**Answer:** The Brush paints with a colour you choose; the Clone Stamp paints with pixels sampled from another area of the image.
