# Toolbox — retouching and painting tools

## Do it first

Before reading on, sort these jobs: remove a spot from a face, copy grass over a hole in a photo, paint a sunset, soften a sharp edge, lighten a dark corner. This lesson's tools do exactly those jobs — guess which tool fits which job as you read.

## Crop, slice and measurement

- **Crop family** — Crop cuts out unwanted areas; its family holds Perspective Crop, Slice and Slice Select.
- **Frame** — places an image into rectangular or elliptical shapes.
- **Eyedropper** — samples colours; increase Sample Size for a better average. Its family: 3D Material Eyedropper, Colour Sampler, Ruler, Note, Count.

## Retouching and painting

- **Spot Healing Brush** — removes blemishes quickly; family: Healing Brush, Patch, Content-Aware Move, Red Eye.
- **Brush** — the primary painting tool; family: Pencil, Colour Replacement, Mixer Brush.
- **Clone Stamp** — samples pixels from one area and paints them over another; family: Pattern Stamp.
- **History Brush** — restores part of an image to an earlier state; family: Art History Brush.
- **Eraser** — erases pixels permanently, or paints back an earlier state; family: Background Eraser, Magic Eraser.
- **Gradient** — gradual blends between colours; family: Paint Bucket, 3D Material Drop.
- **Blur** — softens; family: Sharpen, Smudge.
- **Dodge** — lightens; family: Burn (darkens), Sponge.
