# Hands-on — Colour Depth, Resolution and Image Quality

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Depth in your hands', 'Save the same image at 24-bit colour and reduced to 256 colours. Compare the two by eye and by file size.')

## Task two
('Resolution in your hands', 'Shrink an image to a quarter of its pixel dimensions, then print or view both at the same physical size. Describe what the finer grid kept that the coarser one lost.')
