# Colour Depth, Resolution and Image Quality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is colour depth?**
a) The number of bits per pixel — how many colours one pixel can choose from  b) 2 — black or white  c) About 16.7 million  d) Smoother shading and richer colour — higher quality
**Answer: a) The number of bits per pixel — how many colours one pixel can choose from**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many colours does 1 bit per pixel give?**
a) About 16.7 million  b) 2 — black or white  c) Smoother shading and richer colour — higher quality  d) Makes it bigger — every pixel carries more bits
**Answer: b) 2 — black or white**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How many colours do 24 bits give?**
a) Smoother shading and richer colour — higher quality  b) Makes it bigger — every pixel carries more bits  c) About 16.7 million  d) How many pixels the image has — the fineness of its grid
**Answer: c) About 16.7 million**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does more colour depth do to quality?**
a) Makes it bigger — every pixel carries more bits  b) How many pixels the image has — the fineness of its grid  c) Sharper edges and smaller detail  d) Smoother shading and richer colour — higher quality
