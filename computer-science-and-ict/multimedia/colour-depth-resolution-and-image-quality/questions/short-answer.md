# Colour Depth, Resolution and Image Quality — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is colour depth?**
**Answer: The number of bits per pixel — how many colours one pixel can choose from**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many colours does 1 bit per pixel give?**
**Answer: 2 — black or white**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How many colours do 24 bits give?**
**Answer: About 16.7 million**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does more colour depth do to quality?**
**Answer: Smoother shading and richer colour — higher quality**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does more colour depth do to file size?**
**Answer: Makes it bigger — every pixel carries more bits**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is resolution?**
**Answer: How many pixels the image has — the fineness of its grid**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a finer grid show?**
**Answer: Sharper edges and smaller detail**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do quality, colour depth, resolution and size relate?**
**Answer: They rise together**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which work pays for both numbers to be high?**
**Answer: Print and archive work**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Can screen images be lighter?**
**Answer: Yes — screens need less than print, so the balance can be lower**
