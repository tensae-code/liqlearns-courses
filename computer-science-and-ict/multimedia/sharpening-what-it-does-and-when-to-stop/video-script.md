Today: **Sharpening — what it does and when to stop**.

Remember the one thing: sharpening makes an image look crisp by enhancing the edges of objects — and the Unsharp Mask, the first of three methods, is steered by Amount, Radius (0.3–0.5 usual) and Threshold (keep at 0).
