# Sharpening — what it does and when to stop — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Sharpening brings out detail by raising contrast along edges.
**Answer: True** — the edge idea.

**2.** The Unsharp Mask's name comes from a blurred mask used to detect edges.
**Answer: True** — a blurred negative mask, subtracted to detect edges.

**3.** A larger Radius is always better.
**Answer: False** — 0.3–0.5 suits most images; more is only for high-resolution ones.
