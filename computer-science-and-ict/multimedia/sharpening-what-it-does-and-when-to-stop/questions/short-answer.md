# Sharpening — what it does and when to stop — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Name the three sharpening methods.
**Answer:** Unsharp Mask, Smart Sharpen and High Pass.

**2.** Explain the Unsharp Mask's name.
**Answer:** A blurred (unsharp) negative mask is made and subtracted to detect the edges; contrast is then raised along those edges.
