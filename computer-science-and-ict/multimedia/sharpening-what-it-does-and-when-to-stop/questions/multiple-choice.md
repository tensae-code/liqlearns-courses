# Sharpening — what it does and when to stop — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Sharpening works by —
a) adding pixels  b) enhancing the edges of objects — increasing contrast along edges  c) brightening everything  d) cropping the edges
**Answer: b) enhancing the edges of objects — increasing contrast along edges** — contrast along edges.

**2.** Too much sharpness —
a) always helps  b) makes an image worse and loses detail  c) adds colour  d) does nothing
**Answer: b) makes an image worse and loses detail** — worse, with detail lost.

**3.** Which is NOT one of the three sharpening methods?
a) Unsharp Mask  b) Smart Sharpen  c) High Pass  d) Auto Colour
**Answer: d) Auto Colour** — Auto Colour is a colour tool.

**4.** Radius for most images is —
a) 0.3–0.5  b) 3–5  c) 10–15  d) 0
**Answer: a) 0.3–0.5** — 0.3–0.5; high-resolution 1–1.5.

**5.** Threshold should be kept at —
a) 100  b) 0, unless noise appears  c) 50  d) the same as Amount
**Answer: b) 0, unless noise appears** — 0 unless noise appears.

**6.** Amount controls —
a) the file size  b) how much sharpness  c) the radius  d) the colour
**Answer: b) how much sharpness** — how much sharpness.
