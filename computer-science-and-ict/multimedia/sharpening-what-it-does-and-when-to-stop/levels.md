# Sharpening — what it does and when to stop — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do and explain the lesson's one thing — sharpening makes an image look crisp by enhancing the edges of objects — and the Unsharp Mask, the first of three methods, is steered by Amount, Radius (0.3–0.5 usual) and Threshold (keep at 0). Practice deepens with more items from the question files and harder corners of the same skill (new images, new values, the same steps), never with new content the lesson does not teach.
