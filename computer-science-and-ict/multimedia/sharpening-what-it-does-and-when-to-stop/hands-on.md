# Hands-on — Sharpening — what it does and when to stop

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Edge tracing
Draw a soft, blurry-edged shape. Now trace its edges with a dark, firm line. The picture suddenly looks 'sharper' — you raised the contrast along its edges, which is all sharpening does.

## Stop-line judgement
Look at the lesson picture's three versions. Write the point where sharpening stops helping, in your own words — that judgement is the real skill of this lesson.
