# Hands-on — Cropping with the Marquee Tool

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Ants on paper
Draw a dashed rectangle around the part of a printed picture you would keep — dash, gap, dash, like marching ants. Then cut along the dashes with scissors: you have run Image → Crop by hand.

## Two crops compared
Crop the same drawn picture twice — once framing it with paper strips (the Crop Tool way) and once drawing a dashed marquee and cutting. Which was quicker? Which let you adjust more before cutting?
