# Cropping with the Marquee Tool — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Give the marquee crop steps in order.
**Answer:** Open the image; select the Rectangular Marquee Tool; click and drag a marquee around the area to keep; choose Image → Crop.

**2.** How does the marquee crop differ from the Crop Tool crop?
**Answer:** The Crop Tool adjusts a border with handles and confirms with Enter; the marquee crop selects with the Rectangular Marquee Tool and cuts at once with Image → Crop.
