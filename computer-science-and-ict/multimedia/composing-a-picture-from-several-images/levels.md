# Composing a Picture from Several Images — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Combining pictures — one world from many parts
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A new picture made by combining several pictures into one scene; Light from the same side, colours that sit together, sizes that fit their places; Drawing, selecting, copying and resizing; Near things drawn larger, far things smaller — perspective; Resizing.

## Sub lesson 2 — Near and far, and adding text
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Text — a title or a label; Where it finishes the scene without covering it; As one picture, not as the sum of its parts; A Mongolian scene — ger, horse, mountains and sky; The Paint (image-processing) unit of Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book.
