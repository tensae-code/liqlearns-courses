# Composing a Picture from Several Images — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a composition?**
a) A new picture made by combining several pictures into one scene  b) Light from the same side, colours that sit together, sizes that fit their places  c) Drawing, selecting, copying and resizing  d) Near things drawn larger, far things smaller — perspective
**Answer: a) A new picture made by combining several pictures into one scene**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must the combined parts agree on?**
a) Drawing, selecting, copying and resizing  b) Light from the same side, colours that sit together, sizes that fit their places  c) Near things drawn larger, far things smaller — perspective  d) Resizing
**Answer: b) Light from the same side, colours that sit together, sizes that fit their places**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which earlier skills does composition join?**
a) Near things drawn larger, far things smaller — perspective  b) Resizing  c) Drawing, selecting, copying and resizing  d) Text — a title or a label
**Answer: c) Drawing, selecting, copying and resizing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is depth shown in a composition?**
a) Resizing  b) Text — a title or a label  c) Where it finishes the scene without covering it  d) Near things drawn larger, far things smaller — perspective
**Answer: d) Near things drawn larger, far things smaller — perspective**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which tool builds the depth?**
a) Resizing  b) Text — a title or a label  c) Where it finishes the scene without covering it  d) As one picture, not as the sum of its parts
**Answer: a) Resizing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is added last to finish a composition?**
a) Where it finishes the scene without covering it  b) Text — a title or a label  c) As one picture, not as the sum of its parts  d) A Mongolian scene — ger, horse, mountains and sky
**Answer: b) Text — a title or a label**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where should the text be placed?**
a) As one picture, not as the sum of its parts  b) A Mongolian scene — ger, horse, mountains and sky  c) Where it finishes the scene without covering it  d) The Paint (image-processing) unit of Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book
**Answer: c) Where it finishes the scene without covering it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is a composition judged?**
a) A Mongolian scene — ger, horse, mountains and sky  b) The Paint (image-processing) unit of Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book  c) A new picture made by combining several pictures into one scene  d) As one picture, not as the sum of its parts
**Answer: d) As one picture, not as the sum of its parts**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Give the book's kind of scene for a composition.**
a) A Mongolian scene — ger, horse, mountains and sky  b) The Paint (image-processing) unit of Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book  c) A new picture made by combining several pictures into one scene  d) Light from the same side, colours that sit together, sizes that fit their places
**Answer: a) A Mongolian scene — ger, horse, mountains and sky**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which unit does this lesson close?**
a) A new picture made by combining several pictures into one scene  b) The Paint (image-processing) unit of Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book  c) Light from the same side, colours that sit together, sizes that fit their places  d) Drawing, selecting, copying and resizing
**Answer: b) The Paint (image-processing) unit of Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book**
