# Composing a Picture from Several Images — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a composition?**
**Answer: A new picture made by combining several pictures into one scene**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must the combined parts agree on?**
**Answer: Light from the same side, colours that sit together, sizes that fit their places**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which earlier skills does composition join?**
**Answer: Drawing, selecting, copying and resizing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is depth shown in a composition?**
**Answer: Near things drawn larger, far things smaller — perspective**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which tool builds the depth?**
**Answer: Resizing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is added last to finish a composition?**
**Answer: Text — a title or a label**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where should the text be placed?**
**Answer: Where it finishes the scene without covering it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is a composition judged?**
**Answer: As one picture, not as the sum of its parts**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Give the book's kind of scene for a composition.**
**Answer: A Mongolian scene — ger, horse, mountains and sky**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which unit does this lesson close?**
**Answer: The Paint (image-processing) unit of Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book**
