# Hands-on — Composing a Picture from Several Images

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Build the scene', 'Compose a steppe scene from at least four parts drawn or copied separately. Make the far mountains smaller than the near ger, and check the light falls one way.')

## Task two
('Title it', 'Add a title to your composition in the picture itself. Move it twice and keep the place that covers least and reads best. Say why you chose it.')
