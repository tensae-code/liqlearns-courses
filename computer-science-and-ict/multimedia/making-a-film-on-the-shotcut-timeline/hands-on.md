# Hands-on — Making a Film on the Shotcut Timeline

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the opening
Import three clips and add them to the Timeline in story order. Delete one shot that does not fit and say why.

## Lock, undo, save
Lock the finished track, make one deliberate change, undo it, then save the project, close Shotcut, and reopen the project to prove it survived.
