# Making a Film on the Shotcut Timeline — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does Shotcut's interface centre on?**
a) The Timeline — the tracks where the film is assembled  b) The order the story needs them  c) So later edits cannot disturb finished work  d) Save it
**Answer: a) The Timeline — the tracks where the film is assembled**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the preview show?**
a) Delete it from the Timeline  b) The film as it grows  c) Undo  d) Reopen it and carry on where you stopped
**Answer: b) The film as it grows**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first step of making the film?**
a) So later edits cannot disturb finished work  b) Save it  c) Importing the files — clips, pictures and sounds — into the project  d) The whole arrangement of the film
**Answer: c) Importing the files — clips, pictures and sounds — into the project**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) In what order are clips added to the Timeline?**
a) Undo  b) Reopen it and carry on where you stopped  c) The Timeline — the tracks where the film is assembled  d) The order the story needs them
**Answer: d) The order the story needs them**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do you do with a clip that does not fit?**
a) Delete it from the Timeline  b) Save it  c) The whole arrangement of the film  d) The film as it grows
**Answer: a) Delete it from the Timeline**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why lock a track?**
a) Reopen it and carry on where you stopped  b) So later edits cannot disturb finished work  c) The Timeline — the tracks where the film is assembled  d) Importing the files — clips, pictures and sounds — into the project
**Answer: b) So later edits cannot disturb finished work**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What steps back over a mistake?**
a) The whole arrangement of the film  b) The film as it grows  c) Undo  d) The order the story needs them
**Answer: c) Undo**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must you do to the project as you work?**
a) The Timeline — the tracks where the film is assembled  b) Importing the files — clips, pictures and sounds — into the project  c) Delete it from the Timeline  d) Save it
**Answer: d) Save it**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What can you do with a saved project next time?**
a) Reopen it and carry on where you stopped  b) The film as it grows  c) The order the story needs them  d) So later edits cannot disturb finished work
**Answer: a) Reopen it and carry on where you stopped**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the project file remember?**
a) Importing the files — clips, pictures and sounds — into the project  b) The whole arrangement of the film  c) Delete it from the Timeline  d) Undo
**Answer: b) The whole arrangement of the film**
