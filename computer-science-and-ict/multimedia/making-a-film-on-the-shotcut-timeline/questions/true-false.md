# Making a Film on the Shotcut Timeline — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does Shotcut's interface centre on?" is: The Timeline — the tracks where the film is assembled**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the preview show?" is: Importing the files — clips, pictures and sounds — into the project**
**Answer: False** — The film as it grows

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the first step of making the film?" is: Importing the files — clips, pictures and sounds — into the project**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "In what order are clips added to the Timeline?" is: Delete it from the Timeline**
**Answer: False** — The order the story needs them

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do you do with a clip that does not fit?" is: Delete it from the Timeline**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why lock a track?" is: Undo**
**Answer: False** — So later edits cannot disturb finished work

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What steps back over a mistake?" is: Undo**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What must you do to the project as you work?" is: Reopen it and carry on where you stopped**
**Answer: False** — Save it

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What can you do with a saved project next time?" is: Reopen it and carry on where you stopped**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the project file remember?" is: The Timeline — the tracks where the film is assembled**
**Answer: False** — The whole arrangement of the film
