# Making an Awareness Infographic — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an infographic?**
**Answer: Information presented graphically on one page**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What replaces paragraphs in an infographic?**
**Answer: Icons, simple charts and short labels**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How many messages may it carry?**
**Answer: One**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How are numbers best shown?**
**Answer: As pictures — bars, pies, coloured figures**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What order must the eye travel?**
**Answer: Title, danger, defence, action**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which crime should a team choose?**
**Answer: The one its audience actually meets**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where do the facts come from?**
**Answer: The unit's verified learning**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is a draft tested?**
**Answer: On a stranger from the target audience**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is the infographic finished?**
**Answer: When the stranger can repeat its message unaided**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the unit's final standard?**
**Answer: A page that changes what its reader does next**
