# Hands-on — Making an Awareness Infographic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Make the page** — Choose one cybercrime your schoolmates meet. Build its awareness infographic: one message, pictured numbers, the four-step eye path.

## Task two
**Stranger test** — Show your infographic to a stranger for twenty seconds, then hide it. Ask them to repeat the message; fix everything they lost.
