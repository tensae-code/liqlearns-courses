# Lesson plan — Importing digital movies — and controlling their sound

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Multimedia · **Lesson:** Importing digital movies — and controlling their sound
**Place in the curriculum:** Ethiopia Grade 10, Unit 4 — Image Processing and Multimedia (printed pp. 139–181).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a digital movie is imported like sound, its playback conditions are set from the audio properties — Fade In, Fade Out, Bypass, Balance, Time Duration, Toggle, Repeat, Clip Volume — and its sound is silenced by toggling the Volume effect off.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Importing sound: the movie arrives by the same Get Media route, and its sound is governed from the same properties idea.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Importing a digital movie** — digital video defined; the accepted formats; import by the Get Media route; Premiere work on movies named.
2. **Playback conditions — and silencing the sound** — the eight playback conditions from Show Properties, and silencing by toggling the Volume effect off.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Importing sound and changing its play speed. Recommended, never forced. A learner can land here directly; the lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define digital video and list its accepted formats.
2. Import a movie by the Get Media route.
3. Name the eight playback conditions and silence sound by the Volume toggle.
## What you need
A computer with Premiere Elements for the real doing; paper for the list work.
