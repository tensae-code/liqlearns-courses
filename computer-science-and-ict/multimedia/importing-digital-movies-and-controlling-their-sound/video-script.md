# Video script — Importing digital movies — and controlling their sound

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Movies in — and their sound under control**.

A digital movie records with a digital signal.
Bring it in like sound: Organize, Get Media, open it.
Right-click its audio — Show Properties.
Fade in. Fade out. Balance. Repeat. Volume.
Silence? Toggle Volume off. The sound stays — it just does not play.
