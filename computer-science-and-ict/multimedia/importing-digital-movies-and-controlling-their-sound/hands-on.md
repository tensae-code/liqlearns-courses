# Hands-on — Importing digital movies — and controlling their sound

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Mime the properties
A partner names a condition; you mime what the listener hears when it is applied. Eight conditions, eight mimes.

## Silence-and-restore
On a machine you may use, import a short clip, toggle its Volume off, play, toggle it back on, and write what changed and what did not.
