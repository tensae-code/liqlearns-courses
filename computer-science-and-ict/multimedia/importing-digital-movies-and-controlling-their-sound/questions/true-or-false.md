# Importing digital movies — and controlling their sound — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Fade In and Fade Out are playback conditions.
**Answer: True** — from the list of eight.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Toggling Volume off deletes the clip's sound file.
**Answer: False** — the sound stays stored; it does not play.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** MP3 and WAV are accepted formats for this work even though they are audio.
**Answer: True** — as listed in the book.
