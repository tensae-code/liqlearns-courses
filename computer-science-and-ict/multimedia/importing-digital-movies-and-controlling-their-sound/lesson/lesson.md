# Importing digital movies — and controlling their sound

## Do it first

Mime showing a friend a video on a phone: you turn the sound down, then off, then on again. Before reading on, say how many separate sound decisions you just made. This lesson names the full list a movie's sound obeys.

## Sub lesson 1 — Importing a digital movie

**Digital video** records with a **digital rather than analogue** signal. Premiere work on it includes **trimming, motion, audio enhancing and transitions**. The audio/video formats it accepts are listed as **MP3, MP4, AVI, MPG, WAV and WMV**.

A movie is **imported like sound**: **Organize → Get Media → Files and Folders → browse → Open**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Playback conditions — and silencing the sound

Playback conditions are set from the **audio properties**: **right-click the timeline audio → Show Properties**. The conditions are: **Fade In, Fade Out, Bypass, Balance, Time Duration, Toggle, Repeat** and **Clip Volume**.

Turning a sound **off** is done in that same properties dialogue by **toggling the Volume effect off** — the clip keeps its sound stored; it simply does not play it.
