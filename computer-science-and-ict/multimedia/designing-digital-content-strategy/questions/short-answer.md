# Designing Digital Content — Strategy — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a content strategy?**
**Answer: The plan a digital work is made from**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the strategy's order?**
**Answer: Audience, purpose, message, then form**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must be known about the audience?**
**Answer: Who exactly it is — their age, needs and habits**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What three changes can a purpose name?**
**Answer: That the audience know, feel, or do something**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the message?**
**Answer: The one thing the content must land**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When is form chosen?**
**Answer: Last — whichever form carries that message to that audience**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where does most failed content fail?**
**Answer: In the strategy — before any tool is opened**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What test of success does the plan name?**
**Answer: 'The viewer can now…' — stated in advance**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What judges the drafts?**
**Answer: The strategy — a piece serving another audience or message is a different work, not a success**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 9, Bab III C.1 — digital content design strategy**
