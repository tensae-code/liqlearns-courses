## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Strategy before making — audience and purpose** — A **content strategy** (strategi perancangan konten) is the plan a digital work is made from, and its order is the discipline: **audience** first (who exactly is this for — their age, needs and habits), **purpose** second (what should change for them — know, feel, or do), then the **message** (the one thing the content must land), and only then the **form** (video, image, text, sound — whichever carries that message to that audience). Indonesia's Grade 9 chapter teaches strategy before production because most failed content fails here, before any tool is opened.
2. **The plan on one page — and judging by it** — A strategy that fits on one page can guide every making decision after it: form chosen, tone set, length bounded, and the test of success named in advance ('the viewer can now…'). When drafts arrive, the strategy is the judge — a beautiful piece that serves another audience or lands another message is not a success but a different work. The chapter's order in the book is itself the strategy lesson: strategy (C.1) before AI tools (C.2), planning before production, in content as in programs.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
