# Hands-on — Designing Digital Content — Strategy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**One-page strategy** — Choose a real content job (recruit for a club, teach a safety rule). Write its one-page strategy: audience, purpose, message, form, and the 'viewer can now…' test.

## Task two
**Judge by the strategy** — Take a published piece of content. Reverse-engineer its strategy from the work itself, then judge: does the piece serve its own apparent strategy?
