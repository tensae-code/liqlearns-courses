**The one thing this lesson teaches:** creating an advertisement in groups (créer une publicité): planning and making a real ad — product, audience, promise, image and slogan — using the persuader's tools knowingly (Switzerland Grades 3–4 Décodage, Parcours de la pub).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Plan before making** — An advertisement is planned like any designed thing: **product** (what, exactly?), **audience** (whom — their age, their wishes), **promise** (the one feeling or gain you offer), and **action** (what the audience should do after seeing it). The parcours makes groups answer all four before touching materials, because an ad that cannot say its audience and promise in one sentence each will persuade no one, however pretty it is.
2. **Making it — and answering for it** — Making spends the persuader's tools on purpose: an image that pictures the promise, a slogan short enough to stick, the product shown at its best, repetition of the name. Then the parcours turns the makers back into readers: each group presents, and the class names the tools it can spot in the others' ads. Making an ad honestly — for a real thing, with tools openly named — is the strongest advertisement education there is.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — You know how ads work on you. Now the tools change hands: your group must sell something real — a school event, a fruit, a book. Before reading on, choose: who are you persuading, and what exactly do you want them to do? (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of plan before making as the lesson describes it — the student studies the picture and names what each part shows.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of making it — and answering for it in action — the student watches once, then says what changed and why.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key names and definitions aloud, in the order the lesson gives them — the student listens once through, then reads the same lines aloud along with the voice. (Audio is a described slot in this build; no audio file is made.)
3. **Say it to your companion** — the companion block in lesson/lesson.md.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-false, matching, short-answer — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.
