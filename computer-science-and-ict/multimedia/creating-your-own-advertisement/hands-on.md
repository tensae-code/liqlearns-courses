# Hands-on — Creating Your Own Advertisement

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Make the ad** — In groups, plan (four answers on paper) and create an advertisement — poster, audio spot or short video — for a real school thing. Present it; the class names your tools.

## Task two
**Peer audit** — Exchange ads between groups. Each group writes the other's four planning answers from the ad alone. Where the answers cannot be read off, the ad needs repair — say where.
