# Creating Your Own Advertisement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four planning answers.**
a) Product, audience, promise, action  b) The one feeling or gain you offer  c) Before touching materials  d) It persuades no one, however pretty
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the promise?**
a) The one feeling or gain you offer  b) Before touching materials  c) It persuades no one, however pretty  d) One sentence each
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When are the four answered?**
a) Before touching materials  b) It persuades no one, however pretty  c) One sentence each  d) An image picturing the promise; a short slogan; the product shown at its best (also: repetition of the name)
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to an ad that cannot state its audience and promise?**
a) It persuades no one, however pretty  b) One sentence each  c) An image picturing the promise; a short slogan; the product shown at its best (also: repetition of the name)  d) Names the tools it can spot in the other groups' ads
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In how many sentences must audience and promise each fit?**
a) One sentence each  b) An image picturing the promise; a short slogan; the product shown at its best (also: repetition of the name)  c) Names the tools it can spot in the other groups' ads  d) An honest one — for a real thing, with tools openly named
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three tools the makers spend.**

**Answer: a**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of ad does the parcours make?**
a) An honest one — for a real thing, with tools openly named  b) Makers who have used the tools can never unsee them  c) Short enough to stick  d) Product, audience, promise, action

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is making the strongest ad education?**
a) Makers who have used the tools can never unsee them  b) Short enough to stick  c) Product, audience, promise, action  d) The one feeling or gain you offer
