# Creating Your Own Advertisement — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four planning answers.**
**Answer: Product, audience, promise, action**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the promise?**
**Answer: The one feeling or gain you offer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When are the four answered?**
**Answer: Before touching materials**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to an ad that cannot state its audience and promise?**
**Answer: It persuades no one, however pretty**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In how many sentences must audience and promise each fit?**
**Answer: One sentence each**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three tools the makers spend.**
**Answer: An image picturing the promise; a short slogan; the product shown at its best (also: repetition of the name)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the class do at presentations?**
**Answer: Names the tools it can spot in the other groups' ads**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of ad does the parcours make?**
**Answer: An honest one — for a real thing, with tools openly named**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is making the strongest ad education?**
**Answer: Makers who have used the tools can never unsee them**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must the slogan be?**
**Answer: Short enough to stick**
