# Video script — Creating Your Own Advertisement

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An advertisement is planned like any designed thing: **product** (what, exactly?), **audience** (whom — their age, their wishes), **promise** (the one feeling or gain you offer), and **action** (what the audience should do after seeing it). The parcours makes groups answer all four before touching materials, because an ad that cannot say its audience and promise in one sentence each will persuade no one, however pretty it is.
Making spends the persuader's tools on purpose: an image that pictures the promise, a slogan short enough to stick, the product shown at its best, repetition of the name. Then the parcours turns the makers back into readers: each group presents, and the class names the tools it can spot in the others' ads. Making an ad honestly — for a real thing, with tools openly named — is the strongest advertisement education there is.
