# Design Foundations — Elements and Colour Systems — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is digital design?**
**Answer: Making visual things on the computer, on purpose — every element placed to serve what the design must say**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two principles of design.**
**Answer: Balance and contrast (also alignment, repetition, deliberate space)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does contrast do?**
**Answer: Makes what matters stand out**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why are principles taught before the tool?**
**Answer: Tools change; principles judge every tool's work**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the screen's colour system mix?**
**Answer: Light — red, green and blue added toward white**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does print's system mix?**
**Answer: Ink — cyan, magenta, yellow and black**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why can a screen colour print dull?**
**Answer: The systems reach the colour differently**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must the designer know to choose colours?**
**Answer: Which world the design will live in — screen or paper**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which year's opening is this?**
**Answer: Grade 7 — the producer year, 'I am a Producer'**
