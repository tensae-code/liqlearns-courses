# GIMP — Designing a Poster Publication — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a poster built as?**
**Answer: A stack of layers with jobs — background, images, drawn elements, text on top**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are duplicated layers for? Name two uses.**
**Answer: Repeating an element in a pattern; protecting a good version before experimenting**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a poster's layer panel, read top to bottom?**
**Answer: Its recipe**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is poster text formatted?**
**Answer: To be read at a distance and in an order — title largest, details smaller**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is formatting called in the lesson's image?**
**Answer: The poster's traffic control — it decides what is read first, second, never**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where must text sit against its background?**
**Answer: In chosen contrast to what lies under it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the GIMP basics course end with a poster?**
**Answer: It hangs in public and is judged by strangers — design stops being exercises**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which earlier crafts are gathered in it? Name two.**
**Answer: Blending/cutting images and layers (also drawing, formatted text)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of publication is the poster in the book?**
**Answer: A designed publication — the 'Designing Publications' lesson**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 7 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 1 — Designing Publications**
