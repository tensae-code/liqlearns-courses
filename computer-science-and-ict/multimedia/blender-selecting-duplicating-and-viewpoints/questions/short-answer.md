# Blender — Selecting, Duplicating and Viewpoints — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does selecting several objects allow?**
**Answer: Treating them as a company — moved, scaled or deleted together**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does duplicating do?**
**Answer: Copies a selected object whole — exactly equal, then placed**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the Rotate tool decide?**
**Answer: The object's aim — turning around its axis by a chosen amount**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the set build?**
**Answer: The lithium atom**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How are its electrons made?**
**Answer: One sphere, selected, duplicated and placed around the nucleus**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is Perspective view?**
**Answer: The eye's way — far things smaller, as a photograph sees**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is Orthographic view?**
**Answer: The draughtsman's — no shrinking with distance, measures true**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which view is for building by measure?**
**Answer: Orthographic**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is an object's origin point?**
**Answer: Its personal centre — the point it rotates and scales from**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 8 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 1 — Basic 3D Modeling 2**
