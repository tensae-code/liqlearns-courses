# Importing sound and changing its play speed — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** The audio must be dragged to the Timeline before Time Stretch can be used on it.
**Answer: True** — the taught order.

**2.** Importing sound deletes the original audio file.
**Answer: False** — it opens the file into the project; nothing is deleted.

**3.** A sound icon is itself the sound you hear.
**Answer: False** — it is the graphical stand-in for the audio file.
