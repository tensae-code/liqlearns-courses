# Lesson plan — Importing sound and changing its play speed

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Multimedia · **Lesson:** Importing sound and changing its play speed
**Place in the curriculum:** Ethiopia Grade 10, Unit 4 — Image Processing and Multimedia (printed pp. 139–181).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** sound enters a Premiere Elements project by Organize → Get Media → Files and Folders, and its play speed is changed on the Timeline through right-click → Time Stretch.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Video transitions: the Timeline that held clips now holds a waveform whose speed can be set.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Video transitions — placing and previewing them — the Timeline and its clips. Recommended, never forced. A learner can land here directly; the lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define sound icon.
2. Import sound by the Organize → Get Media route.
3. Change play speed through Time Stretch on the Timeline.
## What you need
A computer with Premiere Elements for the real doing; hands for the clapping rehearsal.

## If the learner is stuck
- Looks for Time Stretch before importing → nothing can be stretched that is not in the project; import first, drag to Timeline, then stretch.
- Right-clicks the project panel → the Time Stretch route starts on the waveform on the Timeline.
