# Video script — Importing sound and changing its play speed

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Sound in — and sound faster or slower**.

Organize. Get Media. Files and Folders. Your sound is in.
Drag it to the Timeline.
Right-click the waveform — Time Stretch.
Set the speed. OK.

Same sound. New speed. No re-recording.
