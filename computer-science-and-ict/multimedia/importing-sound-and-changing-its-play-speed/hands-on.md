# Hands-on — Importing sound and changing its play speed

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Clap the stretch
Clap a four-beat pattern while a partner calls percentages — 50%, 100%, 200%. Your hands perform what Time Stretch does to the waveform.

## Import rehearsal
On a real machine you may use, import one audio file by the Organize route and change its speed once in each direction, noting the setting you used.
