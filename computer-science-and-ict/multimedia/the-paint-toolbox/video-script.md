# Video script — The Paint Toolbox

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Paint's **toolbox** (панель инструментов) is the panel of drawing tools: pencil and brush for free lines, line and shapes for exact figures, the fill for pouring colour into a closed area, the eraser, the picker that takes a colour from the picture, and the magnifier. Grade 5's lesson is the panel itself and working with it — one lesson spent only on knowing where each tool lives.
Grade 9 meets the same panel as the **interface of the Paint graphics editor**: toolbox, palette of colours, drawing canvas and menus together. The older pupil is expected to name the parts and say what each is for — the interface is the editor's face, and every later graphics program keeps the same four parts under new drawings.
