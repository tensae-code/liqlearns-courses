# The Paint Toolbox — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Paint toolbox?**
**Answer: The panel of drawing tools**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which tools draw free lines?**
**Answer: Pencil and brush**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the fill do?**
**Answer: Pours colour into a closed area**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the picker do?**
**Answer: Takes a colour from the picture**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does Grade 5 spend the whole lesson on?**
**Answer: Knowing where each tool lives**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which four parts make the editor's interface?**
**Answer: Toolbox, colour palette, drawing canvas and menus**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the interface called in the Grade 9 lesson?**
**Answer: The face of the graphics editor**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must the Grade 9 pupil do with the parts?**
**Answer: Name them and say what each is for**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do later graphics programs keep?**
**Answer: The same four parts under new drawings**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches the toolbox first?**
**Answer: Uzbekistan's Grade 5 Informatika**
