# Hands-on — Downloading and Managing Images

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Save it properly
Download one image for a school topic: right source, Save image as, the right folder, a content name. Show the saved file in its folder.

## Folder rescue
Take a messy set of images (or a list of their names) and reorganise it: folders, new names, deletions — then explain your system in three sentences.
