# Text Inside a Picture in Paint — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. To put text in a Paint picture, first choose —**
a) the eraser  b) the text tool  c) the bucket
**Answer: b) the text tool** — The tool marked with a letter.

**2. After choosing the text tool, you —**
a) close Paint  b) click in the picture and type  c) print at once
**Answer: b) click in the picture and type** — Click the place, then type the words.

**3. Good words in a picture are —**
a) very long and covering the drawing  b) short and in a clear place  c) hidden
**Answer: b) short and in a clear place** — Readable at a glance, covering nothing important.

**4. Once you click away, placed text —**
a) vanishes  b) becomes part of the picture  c) turns into a drawing tool
**Answer: b) becomes part of the picture** — So think first, then type.
