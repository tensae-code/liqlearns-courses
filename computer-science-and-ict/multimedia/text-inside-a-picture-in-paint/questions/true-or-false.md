# Text Inside a Picture in Paint — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. A picture in Paint can carry words as well as a drawing.**
**Answer: True** — Names, captions and poster words are text in a picture.

**2. You should type your words before choosing any tool.**
**Answer: False** — Choose the text tool first, then click and type.

**3. Words can be placed under a drawing as its caption.**
**Answer: True** — Under the drawing is a clear, good place.
