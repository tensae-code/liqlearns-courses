# Gradients and Shadows That Make Depth in a Game — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Your partner's shadows look like grey strips glued on top of the maze. Give the two fixes.**
**Answer:** A sound answer: fill the shapes with a gradient that fades away from the wall, and send the shadow shapes behind the walls so only the cast part shows.

**2. Why does the book put this tip with the advanced ones, at the end of the project?**
**Answer:** A sound answer: it is pure finishing — it improves how the finished game looks and feels without touching how it plays, so it belongs after the game itself is built and working.
