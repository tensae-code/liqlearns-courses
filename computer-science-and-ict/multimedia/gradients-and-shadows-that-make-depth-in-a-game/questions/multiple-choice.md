# Gradients and Shadows That Make Depth in a Game — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A shadow shape should be filled with —**
a) A gradient, dark at the wall and fading out  b) The wall colour  c) Random colours
**Answer: a) A gradient, dark at the wall and fading out** — Real shadows soften at their edges — so does a gradient.

**2. The shadow must be sent —**
a) In front of the walls  b) Behind the walls  c) Into a sound
**Answer: b) Behind the walls** — Behind, so only the falling shadow shows.

**3. In front of the wall, the shadow looks like —**
a) Depth  b) Dirt covering the wall  c) A goal
**Answer: b) Dirt covering the wall** — Ordering is what makes the effect.

**4. This depth tip changes —**
a) How the game plays  b) How the game looks and feels  c) The rules of the maze
**Answer: b) How the game looks and feels** — Looks and feel only — a finishing touch.
