## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Reflection — the geometry of the mirror** — A **reflection** turns a picture into its **mirror image**: every point lands as far on the other side of an invisible mirror line as it began. Reflection is not rotation — a reflected face is not the face turned around; it is the face as a mirror shows it, left and right exchanged. Kuwait teaches it inside the design course because designers use mirrors constantly: a subject facing into the page, a pattern doubled into symmetry.
2. **The Flip tool — horizontal and vertical** — GIMP's **Flip tool** reflects the layer or selection in one click, in the two directions the book teaches: **horizontally** — mirrored across a vertical line, left and right exchanged — and **vertically** — mirrored across a horizontal line, top and bottom exchanged, as a scene reflects in still water. The craft is choosing the direction the design needs and noticing what reflection does to text and known faces: mirrored writing reads backwards, and the honest designer flips pictures, not words.
