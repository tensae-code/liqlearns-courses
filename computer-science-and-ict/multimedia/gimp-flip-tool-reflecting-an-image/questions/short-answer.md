# GIMP — Flip Tool: Reflecting an Image — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a reflection?**
**Answer: A picture turned into its mirror image — points landing as far the other side of a mirror line**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Is reflection the same as rotation?**
**Answer: No — a reflected face is the mirror's face, left and right exchanged**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two designer uses of reflection.**
**Answer: Facing a subject into the page; doubling a pattern into symmetry (any two)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which tool reflects in GIMP?**
**Answer: The Flip tool**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a horizontal flip exchange?**
**Answer: Left and right (mirrored across a vertical line)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a vertical flip exchange?**
**Answer: Top and bottom — as a scene reflects in water**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to writing in a flipped image?**
**Answer: It reads backwards**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the honest designer flip — pictures or words?**
**Answer: Pictures, not words**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is flipped — the whole image only?**
**Answer: A layer or a selection can be flipped**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 7 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 1 — Reflection**
