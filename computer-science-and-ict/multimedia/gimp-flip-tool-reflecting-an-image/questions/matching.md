**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a reflection?** → **A picture turned into its mirror image — points landing as far the other side of a mirror line**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Is reflection the same as rotation?** → **No — a reflected face is the mirror's face, left and right exchanged**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two designer uses of reflection.** → **Facing a subject into the page; doubling a pattern into symmetry (any two)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which tool reflects in GIMP?** → **The Flip tool**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a horizontal flip exchange?** → **Left and right (mirrored across a vertical line)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a vertical flip exchange?** → **Top and bottom — as a scene reflects in water**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to writing in a flipped image?** → **It reads backwards**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the honest designer flip — pictures or words?** → **Pictures, not words**
