# Hands-on — Pixels, Bitmaps and Colour Depth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Count a tiny bitmap', 'Draw a 4×4 picture in two colours on squared paper, then write its bitmap as sixteen 0s and 1s in order.')

## Task two
('Depth sums', 'A picture has 100 pixels. Work out its size in bits at 1 bit, 8 bits and 24 bits per pixel, and say the colours each depth allows.')
