# Pixels, Bitmaps and Colour Depth — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a bitmap picture?**
**Answer: A grid of pixels, with a number stored for each pixel's colour**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the file store for each pixel?**
**Answer: A number saying its colour**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What shows when you zoom far in?**
**Answer: The grid of pixels**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when a bitmap is printed big?**
**Answer: The picture softens**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does this lesson sit in the Grade 9 book?**
**Answer: After the graphics editor, inside working with graphic information**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is colour depth?**
**Answer: How many bits describe one pixel**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How many colours does 1 bit give?**
**Answer: Two — black and white**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How many colours do 24 bits give?**
**Answer: Millions — true colour**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does every added bit do to the colours?**
**Answer: Doubles them**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is choosing a depth a balance between?**
**Answer: Truth of colour and file size**
