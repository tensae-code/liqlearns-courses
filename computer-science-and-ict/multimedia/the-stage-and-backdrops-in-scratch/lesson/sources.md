Bulgaria's own way: "Computer modelling" in Grade 3 is an unplugged-to-screen progression — code.org Course 1 sequences and loops with a robot-artist first, Scratch only from lesson 11, and a "Знам и мога" (I know and I can) mastery check every few lessons; Grade 4 continues in Scratch (branching, operators, variables, synchronising sprites), then leaves the screen for the Blue-Bot bee robot, before two long project arcs (a computer game; a "Journey through Bulgaria" project). This lesson sits in the Grade 3 part of that way.

Morocco's own way (Grade 3): a full primary year given to one tool — Scratch — sequenced interface → sprites → stage/backdrops → sounds, each strand ending in a familiar story retold as a Scratch project (the hare and the tortoise; Little Red Riding Hood). (teacher guide — evidence)
