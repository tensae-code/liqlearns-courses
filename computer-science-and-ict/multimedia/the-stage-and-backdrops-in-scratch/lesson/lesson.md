# The Stage and Backdrops in Scratch

## Do it first

Imagine your sprite standing in an empty white room. Now the wall behind it becomes a forest, then a beach. The sprite did not move — its place changed. That place is the stage, and its pictures are backdrops.

## The Stage and Backdrops in Scratch

This lesson meets the **stage** properly for the first time: the stage is the fixed place of the project — the screen the sprites act on — and it has its own looks, called **backdrops (декори)**. A backdrop is chosen for the stage the way a costume is chosen for a sprite: from a library, or made in the editor. A project can hold more than one backdrop, and **changing the backdrop** moves the whole story to a new place without moving a single sprite. Bulgaria's lesson covers the stage itself and its backdrops together, broadly: what the stage is, where backdrops live, how to add one, and how a change of backdrop changes the scene. That breadth is why it is built as its own lesson, separate from Finland's narrower adding-and-switching-a-backdrop lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: The stage is the place where a Scratch project happens, and a backdrop is the picture it wears: choose and change backdrops, and the same sprites stand in a new place. Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.

## Do It Yourself

1. Show it, don't just say it: say what the stage is and what a backdrop is. Do this with a real example from your own work or your own school day, and write or say how you know it is right.
2. Show it, don't just say it: add a backdrop to the stage and change to another one. Do this with a real example from your own work or your own school day, and write or say how you know it is right.
3. Show it, don't just say it: say how changing the backdrop changes a scene without moving sprites. Do this with a real example from your own work or your own school day, and write or say how you know it is right.
