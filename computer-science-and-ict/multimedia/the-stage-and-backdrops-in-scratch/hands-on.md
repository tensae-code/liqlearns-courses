# Hands-on — The Stage and Backdrops in Scratch

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Three places, one sprite
Put one sprite on the stage. Give the stage three backdrops and change between them. Say the place aloud each time — the sprite never moves.

## Backdrop for a story
Pick a two-place story (home, then school). Choose the two backdrops in order and show the change to a partner, who must name both places.
