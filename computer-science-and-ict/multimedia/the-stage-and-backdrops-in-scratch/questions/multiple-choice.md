# The Stage and Backdrops in Scratch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The stage is —**
a) the fixed place where the project happens  b) a kind of sprite  c) a sound block
**Answer: a) the fixed place where the project happens**

**2. A backdrop is —**
a) a sprite's script  b) a pen colour  c) a picture the stage wears
**Answer: c) a picture the stage wears**

**3. Changing the backdrop —**
a) deletes the stage  b) moves the story to a new place without moving sprites  c) moves every sprite
**Answer: b) moves the story to a new place without moving sprites**

**4. A backdrop is to the stage as a costume is to —**
a) a sprite  b) a sound  c) a loop
**Answer: a) a sprite**
