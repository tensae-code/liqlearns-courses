# The Stage and Backdrops in Scratch — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. Say what the stage is and what a backdrop is.**
**Answer: True** — This is one of the lesson's own outcomes.

**2. To move a story to a new place, every sprite must be carried to a new stage.**
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite of this.

**3. Add a backdrop to the stage and change to another one.**
**Answer: True** — This is one of the lesson's own outcomes.
