# The Stage and Backdrops in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Explain, in your own words: say what the stage is and what a backdrop is.**
**Answer:** The learner's own explanation, true to the lesson: The stage is the place where a Scratch project happens, and a backdrop is the picture it wears: choose and change backdrops, and the same sprites stand in a new place.

**2. A classmate gets this lesson's work wrong. What is the most likely fault, and how is it fixed?**
**Answer:** Sprites look lost in the scene → the backdrop and the story disagree: choose the backdrop that matches the place the story names, then check each sprite against it.
