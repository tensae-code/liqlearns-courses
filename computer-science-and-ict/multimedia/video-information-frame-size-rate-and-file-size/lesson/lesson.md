# Video Information — Frame Size, Rate and File Size

## Do it first

Audio was sampled in time; video is sampled in time and space at once. Mongolia's Grade 10 video chapter teaches video as arithmetic a pupil can do: frames, their size, their rate — and the file size that falls out of them before a single frame is recorded.

## Sub lesson 1 — Frames — size and rate

A **video** is a row of still frames shown fast enough to move. Two numbers rule it. The **frame size** is one frame's dimensions in pixels — width by height — deciding the picture's detail and the weight of every single frame. The **frame rate** is how many frames pass each second, deciding the motion's smoothness. Together with the sound and the seconds, they are the whole video's recipe: hold either number up and the file grows; the maker's art is choosing how much of each the purpose truly needs.

## Sub lesson 2 — Working out the file size — and the formats

The **file size is calculated** before it is suffered: frames' pixel count, times the bytes each pixel takes, times the rate, times the seconds — the book works the arithmetic, and its answer explains why raw video is enormous and **compression** is not a luxury but the condition of video's existence. The compressed results travel in the book's **formats**: **AVI, MOV, WMV, RM and MPEG** — each a container and coding family with its own home ground. Mongolia's lesson is a maker's warning: the format is chosen at the start, because size, quality and company are all decided there.
