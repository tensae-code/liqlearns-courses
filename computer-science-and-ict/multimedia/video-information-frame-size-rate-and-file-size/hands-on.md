# Hands-on — Video Information — Frame Size, Rate and File Size

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Calculate before recording', 'Work out the uncompressed size of 10 seconds of video at a frame size and 25 frames per second your teacher sets. Then say what compression must achieve for a phone to hold it.')

## Task two
('Choose the recipe', 'For a 1-minute school announcement video, choose a frame size and rate and defend the pair against one bigger and one smaller offer.')
