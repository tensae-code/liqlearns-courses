# Project — Digital Photo Albums and Collections — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What makes an album more than a folder of photos?**
**Answer: A theme — a collection with an idea**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) State the project's working order.**
**Answer: Collect, select, process, order**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is done in processing?**
**Answer: The chapter's crafts — crop, correct, resize — in Paint 3D or GIMP**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does order matter?**
**Answer: So the album reads — opening, middle, close — not merely accumulates**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What belongs under each photograph?**
**Answer: A caption — a line saying what the eye might miss**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) In what forms can the album be presented?**
**Answer: A slideshow, a shared folder, or a printed selection**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How are formats chosen?**
**Answer: By use — quality for print, lighter files for sharing**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Whose photographs may be used?**
**Answer: Your own, or ones you may use — with authors named**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must never be processed directly?**
**Answer: The originals — processing works on copies**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What three things does the presented collection grade?**
**Answer: The chapter's skills, taste and honesty**
