# Project — Digital Photo Albums and Collections

## Do it first

A chapter of tools ends, as Moldova's book likes to end, in a product. The recommended project of the image chapter: an album — photographs not just kept, but chosen, processed and presented as a collection with an idea.

## Sub lesson 1 — The album as a work — choosing and ordering

A **digital photo album** is a **collection with an idea**: a theme (our town in autumn, the class year, water) chooses which photographs belong. The project work runs in the profession's order: **collect** more photographs than needed, **select** the strongest for the theme, **process** them with the chapter's tools (crop, correct, resize in Paint 3D or GIMP), and **order** them so the album reads — opening, middle, close — not merely accumulates. Captions belong to the work: a line per photograph, saying what the eye might miss.

## Sub lesson 2 — Presenting the collection — formats and care

The album is finished for an audience: **exported or presented** in the form the audience needs — a slideshow, a shared folder, a printed selection — with file formats chosen by use (quality for print, lighter for sharing). The project's care rules close the chapter's ethics: use **your own photographs or ones you may use**, name authors where photographs are others', and keep the **originals untouched** — processing works on copies. A collection presented is the chapter's skills, taste and honesty graded at once.
