# Hands-on — Project — Digital Photo Albums and Collections

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Album build** — Build a ten-photograph album on one theme: select from at least twenty, process three with the chapter's tools, order them, caption each, and present as a slideshow.

## Task two
**Curator's note** — Write the one-page note for your album: theme, why these photographs, what processing was done, and whose work is credited.
