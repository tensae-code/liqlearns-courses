# Meeting Audacity and Its Window — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is Audacity?" is: A free program for recording and editing audio**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What three things does Audacity do with sound?" is: One sound held in the track area, worked on as a single line of audio**
**Answer: False** — It records it, opens audio files, and works on their sound — cutting, changing and mixing

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why do schools use Audacity?" is: It costs nothing and runs on ordinary computers**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where are the play, stop and record buttons?" is: The seconds along the sound**
**Answer: False** — On the toolbar, among the transport buttons

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a track?" is: One sound held in the track area, worked on as a single line of audio**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is a waveform?" is: The timeline**
**Answer: False** — The picture of a sound — tall where it is loud, flat where it is silent

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the timeline count?" is: The seconds along the sound**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is a point in the sound found?" is: A free program for recording and editing audio**
**Answer: False** — By its time on the timeline

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What sits above the tracks?" is: The timeline**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is learned before any sound is touched?" is: It costs nothing and runs on ordinary computers**
**Answer: False** — The window — where every tool and part lives
