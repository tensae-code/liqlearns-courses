# Meeting Audacity and Its Window — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is Audacity?**
**Answer: A free program for recording and editing audio**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three things does Audacity do with sound?**
**Answer: It records it, opens audio files, and works on their sound — cutting, changing and mixing**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why do schools use Audacity?**
**Answer: It costs nothing and runs on ordinary computers**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where are the play, stop and record buttons?**
**Answer: On the toolbar, among the transport buttons**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a track?**
**Answer: One sound held in the track area, worked on as a single line of audio**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a waveform?**
**Answer: The picture of a sound — tall where it is loud, flat where it is silent**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the timeline count?**
**Answer: The seconds along the sound**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is a point in the sound found?**
**Answer: By its time on the timeline**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What sits above the tracks?**
**Answer: The timeline**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is learned before any sound is touched?**
**Answer: The window — where every tool and part lives**
