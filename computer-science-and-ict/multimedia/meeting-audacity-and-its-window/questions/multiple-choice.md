# Meeting Audacity and Its Window — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is Audacity?**
a) A free program for recording and editing audio  b) It records it, opens audio files, and works on their sound — cutting, changing and mixing  c) It costs nothing and runs on ordinary computers  d) On the toolbar, among the transport buttons
**Answer: a) A free program for recording and editing audio**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three things does Audacity do with sound?**
a) It costs nothing and runs on ordinary computers  b) It records it, opens audio files, and works on their sound — cutting, changing and mixing  c) On the toolbar, among the transport buttons  d) One sound held in the track area, worked on as a single line of audio
**Answer: b) It records it, opens audio files, and works on their sound — cutting, changing and mixing**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why do schools use Audacity?**
a) On the toolbar, among the transport buttons  b) One sound held in the track area, worked on as a single line of audio  c) It costs nothing and runs on ordinary computers  d) The picture of a sound — tall where it is loud, flat where it is silent
**Answer: c) It costs nothing and runs on ordinary computers**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where are the play, stop and record buttons?**
a) One sound held in the track area, worked on as a single line of audio  b) The picture of a sound — tall where it is loud, flat where it is silent  c) The seconds along the sound  d) On the toolbar, among the transport buttons
**Answer: d) On the toolbar, among the transport buttons**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a track?**
a) One sound held in the track area, worked on as a single line of audio  b) The picture of a sound — tall where it is loud, flat where it is silent  c) The seconds along the sound  d) By its time on the timeline
**Answer: a) One sound held in the track area, worked on as a single line of audio**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a waveform?**
a) The seconds along the sound  b) The picture of a sound — tall where it is loud, flat where it is silent  c) By its time on the timeline  d) The timeline
**Answer: b) The picture of a sound — tall where it is loud, flat where it is silent**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the timeline count?**
a) By its time on the timeline  b) The timeline  c) The seconds along the sound  d) The window — where every tool and part lives
**Answer: c) The seconds along the sound**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is a point in the sound found?**
a) The timeline  b) The window — where every tool and part lives  c) A free program for recording and editing audio  d) By its time on the timeline
**Answer: d) By its time on the timeline**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What sits above the tracks?**
a) The timeline  b) The window — where every tool and part lives  c) A free program for recording and editing audio  d) It records it, opens audio files, and works on their sound — cutting, changing and mixing

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is learned before any sound is touched?**
a) A free program for recording and editing audio  b) The window — where every tool and part lives  c) It records it, opens audio files, and works on their sound — cutting, changing and mixing  d) It costs nothing and runs on ordinary computers
