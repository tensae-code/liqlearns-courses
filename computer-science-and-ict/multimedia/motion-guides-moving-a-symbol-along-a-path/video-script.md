# Video script — Motion guides — moving a symbol along a path

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Motion guides**.

Your symbol sits on a layer called Graphic.
Right-click — Add Motion Guide. A new layer appears.
Draw the path — a circle.
Frame 60 — F5. Stretch the road.
Frame 1 — symbol at one end, bubble under the path.
Frame 60 — F6 — symbol at the other end.
Right-click between — Create Motion Tween.
Control Enter — watch it travel!
