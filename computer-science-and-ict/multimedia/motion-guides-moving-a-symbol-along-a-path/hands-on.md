# Hands-on — Motion guides — moving a symbol along a path

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Coin-and-circle rehearsal
Draw a circle, mark its two 'ends' (top and bottom of a diameter). Move a coin between them along the circle while reciting which Flash step your hands are performing at each moment.

## Path design
Draw two more paths a symbol could travel — a zig-zag and a wave. For each, write where frame 1 and frame 60 would sit.
