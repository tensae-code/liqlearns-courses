# Video Digitisation and Compression — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a video, digitally?" is: A fast sequence of still images — frames — shown quickly enough that the eye reads movement**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is each frame digitised?" is: How many frames one second of video holds**
**Answer: False** — Like a photograph — pixels with colour depth

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does frame rate decide?" is: How many frames one second of video holds**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What product gives a video's raw data rate?" is: Uncompressed video is so large that storage and transmission cannot carry it**
**Answer: False** — Resolution × colour depth × frame rate

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why is compression essential for video, not optional?" is: Uncompressed video is so large that storage and transmission cannot carry it**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does compression remove?" is: Store the changes — most of a frame is the same as the frame before**
**Answer: False** — What the viewer will not miss

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the video-only compression trick between frames?" is: Store the changes — most of a frame is the same as the frame before**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does lossy compression discard?" is: Softer — some detail is gone**
**Answer: False** — Fine detail the eye forgives

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How does a lossy stream look compared with the original?" is: Softer — some detail is gone**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "In what order does Egypt's book teach digitisation?" is: A fast sequence of still images — frames — shown quickly enough that the eye reads movement**
**Answer: False** — Sound, then images, then video with compression
