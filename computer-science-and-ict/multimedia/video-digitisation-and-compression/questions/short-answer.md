# Video Digitisation and Compression — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a video, digitally?**
**Answer: A fast sequence of still images — frames — shown quickly enough that the eye reads movement**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is each frame digitised?**
**Answer: Like a photograph — pixels with colour depth**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does frame rate decide?**
**Answer: How many frames one second of video holds**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What product gives a video's raw data rate?**
**Answer: Resolution × colour depth × frame rate**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why is compression essential for video, not optional?**
**Answer: Uncompressed video is so large that storage and transmission cannot carry it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does compression remove?**
**Answer: What the viewer will not miss**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the video-only compression trick between frames?**
**Answer: Store the changes — most of a frame is the same as the frame before**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does lossy compression discard?**
**Answer: Fine detail the eye forgives**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How does a lossy stream look compared with the original?**
**Answer: Softer — some detail is gone**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) In what order does Egypt's book teach digitisation?**
**Answer: Sound, then images, then video with compression**
