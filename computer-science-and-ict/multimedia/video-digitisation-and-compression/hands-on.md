# Hands-on — Video Digitisation and Compression

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Do the arithmetic', 'Work out the raw size of 10 seconds of video at a resolution and frame rate you choose, then say what compression ratio a real file of that clip implies.')

## Task two
('Spot the changes', "Watch a short clip and list what actually changes between frames in one busy second and one still second — and which second compresses better, and why.")
