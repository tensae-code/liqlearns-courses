# Editing an Image in Paint — Crop, Resize, Rotate and Zoom — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does every edit start with?**
**Answer: Selection — marking the part to work on**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What can a selection be copied into?**
**Answer: Many copies — one flower becomes a row**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does cropping do?**
**Answer: Cuts the picture down to the part that matters, throwing the edges away**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does resize do?**
**Answer: Makes the picture or selection bigger or smaller**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is skewing?**
**Answer: Slanting the picture**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does rotate do?**
**Answer: Turns the picture — sideways, upside down, or flipped**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does zoom change?**
**Answer: Your view of the picture, not the picture itself**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What appears when you zoom far in?**
**Answer: Pixels — the tiny single-colour squares the picture is made of**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name the editor's discipline in the book's order.**
**Answer: Take first, then change, then inspect**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which program is this editing taught in?**
**Answer: MS Paint, in Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book**
