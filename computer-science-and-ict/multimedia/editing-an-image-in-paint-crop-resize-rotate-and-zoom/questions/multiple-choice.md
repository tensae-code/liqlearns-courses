# Editing an Image in Paint — Crop, Resize, Rotate and Zoom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does every edit start with?**
a) Selection — marking the part to work on  b) Many copies — one flower becomes a row  c) Cuts the picture down to the part that matters, throwing the edges away  d) Makes the picture or selection bigger or smaller
**Answer: a) Selection — marking the part to work on**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What can a selection be copied into?**
a) Cuts the picture down to the part that matters, throwing the edges away  b) Many copies — one flower becomes a row  c) Makes the picture or selection bigger or smaller  d) Slanting the picture
**Answer: b) Many copies — one flower becomes a row**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does cropping do?**
a) Makes the picture or selection bigger or smaller  b) Slanting the picture  c) Cuts the picture down to the part that matters, throwing the edges away  d) Turns the picture — sideways, upside down, or flipped
**Answer: c) Cuts the picture down to the part that matters, throwing the edges away**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does resize do?**
a) Slanting the picture  b) Turns the picture — sideways, upside down, or flipped  c) Your view of the picture, not the picture itself  d) Makes the picture or selection bigger or smaller
**Answer: d) Makes the picture or selection bigger or smaller**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is skewing?**
a) Slanting the picture  b) Turns the picture — sideways, upside down, or flipped  c) Your view of the picture, not the picture itself  d) Pixels — the tiny single-colour squares the picture is made of
**Answer: a) Slanting the picture**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does rotate do?**
a) Your view of the picture, not the picture itself  b) Turns the picture — sideways, upside down, or flipped  c) Pixels — the tiny single-colour squares the picture is made of  d) Take first, then change, then inspect
**Answer: b) Turns the picture — sideways, upside down, or flipped**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does zoom change?**
a) Pixels — the tiny single-colour squares the picture is made of  b) Take first, then change, then inspect  c) Your view of the picture, not the picture itself  d) MS Paint, in Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book
**Answer: c) Your view of the picture, not the picture itself**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What appears when you zoom far in?**
a) Take first, then change, then inspect  b) MS Paint, in Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book  c) Selection — marking the part to work on  d) Pixels — the tiny single-colour squares the picture is made of
**Answer: d) Pixels — the tiny single-colour squares the picture is made of**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name the editor's discipline in the book's order.**
a) Take first, then change, then inspect  b) MS Paint, in Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book  c) Selection — marking the part to work on  d) Many copies — one flower becomes a row
**Answer: a) Take first, then change, then inspect**
