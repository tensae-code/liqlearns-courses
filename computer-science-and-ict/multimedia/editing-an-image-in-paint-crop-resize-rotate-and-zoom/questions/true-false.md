# Editing an Image in Paint — Crop, Resize, Rotate and Zoom — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does every edit start with?" is: Selection — marking the part to work on**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What can a selection be copied into?" is: Cuts the picture down to the part that matters, throwing the edges away**
**Answer: False** — Many copies — one flower becomes a row

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does cropping do?" is: Cuts the picture down to the part that matters, throwing the edges away**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does resize do?" is: Slanting the picture**
**Answer: False** — Makes the picture or selection bigger or smaller

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is skewing?" is: Slanting the picture**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does rotate do?" is: Your view of the picture, not the picture itself**
**Answer: False** — Turns the picture — sideways, upside down, or flipped

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does zoom change?" is: Your view of the picture, not the picture itself**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What appears when you zoom far in?" is: Take first, then change, then inspect**
**Answer: False** — Pixels — the tiny single-colour squares the picture is made of

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name the editor's discipline in the book's order." is: Take first, then change, then inspect**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which program is this editing taught in?" is: Selection — marking the part to work on**
**Answer: False** — MS Paint, in Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book
