# Hands-on — Editing an Image in Paint — Crop, Resize, Rotate and Zoom

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Copy and crop', 'Draw one small motif, select it and copy it into a row of five. Then crop the picture to just the row and save it under a new name.')

## Task two
('Pixel surgery', 'Zoom into a picture until pixels show. Change three single pixels to repair a broken line, zoom out, and say whether the repair can be seen.')
