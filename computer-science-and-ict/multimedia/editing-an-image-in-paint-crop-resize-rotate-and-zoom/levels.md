This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Select, copy and crop — taking the part you need
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Selection — marking the part to work on; Many copies — one flower becomes a row; Cuts the picture down to the part that matters, throwing the edges away; Makes the picture or selection bigger or smaller; Slanting the picture.
