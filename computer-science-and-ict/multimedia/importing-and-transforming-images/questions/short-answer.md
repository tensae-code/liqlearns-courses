# Importing and transforming images — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Give the Free Transform sequence in order.
**Answer:** Select the image, click Free Transform so a boundary box appears, choose the operation in the options panel, and the operation applies.

**2.** How do the selfie practical and this lesson join?
**Answer:** The practical captures an image and edits it — the importing and transforming skills of this lesson are the editing route it uses.
