# Importing and transforming images

## Do it first

Take one photo or drawing. Show with your hands: make it bigger, tilt it, stretch one corner. Before reading on, count how many different changes you mimed — the Free Transform Tool holds them all.

## Importing and transforming images

An image enters a project by **importing** — **File → Import → choose the image → Open** — or by **pasting**: copy it elsewhere, then **Edit → Paste / Ctrl+V** in the project.

Modifying uses the **Free Transform Tool**: **select the image**, click **Free Transform** so a **boundary box** appears, then choose the operation in the options panel — **Rotate and Skew, Scale, Distort** or **Envelope** — and the operation applies to the picture.
