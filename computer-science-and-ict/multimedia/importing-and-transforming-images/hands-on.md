# Hands-on — Importing and transforming images

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Capture and edit a selfie (the book's practical)
Capture an image of yourself or a school scene and edit it: import it, then apply two different transforms and save both versions with names that say what was done.

## Paper transform
Trace a photo's outline on paper four times; draw it rotated, scaled, distorted and enveloped by hand, labelling each with the operation the tool would use.
