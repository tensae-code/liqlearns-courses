# Vector and Raster Graphics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a raster image store?**
a) A colour for every pixel in a grid  b) The pixel count across and down  c) Pixelation — the squares show  d) Raster
**Answer: a) A colour for every pixel in a grid**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is resolution?**
a) Pixelation — the squares show  b) The pixel count across and down  c) Raster  d) JPG and PNG
**Answer: b) The pixel count across and down**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What shows when a raster is over-enlarged?**
a) Raster  b) JPG and PNG  c) Pixelation — the squares show  d) Instructions — shapes defined by rules
**Answer: c) Pixelation — the squares show**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which kind suits photographs?**
a) JPG and PNG  b) Instructions — shapes defined by rules  c) As a centre, a radius and a fill  d) Raster
**Answer: d) Raster**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name two raster formats.**
a) JPG and PNG  b) Instructions — shapes defined by rules  c) As a centre, a radius and a fill  d) The shapes are recomputed on every redraw
**Answer: a) JPG and PNG**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a vector image store?**
a) As a centre, a radius and a fill  b) Instructions — shapes defined by rules  c) The shapes are recomputed on every redraw  d) Vector
**Answer: b) Instructions — shapes defined by rules**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is a vector circle stored?**
a) The shapes are recomputed on every redraw  b) Vector  c) As a centre, a radius and a fill  d) Clean shapes, poorly suited to photographic subtlety
**Answer: c) As a centre, a radius and a fill**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why do vectors stay sharp at any size?**
a) Vector  b) Clean shapes, poorly suited to photographic subtlety  c) A colour for every pixel in a grid  d) The shapes are recomputed on every redraw
**Answer: d) The shapes are recomputed on every redraw**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which kind suits logos and diagrams?**
a) Vector  b) Clean shapes, poorly suited to photographic subtlety  c) A colour for every pixel in a grid  d) The pixel count across and down
**Answer: a) Vector**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the vector trade-off?**
a) A colour for every pixel in a grid  b) Clean shapes, poorly suited to photographic subtlety  c) The pixel count across and down  d) Pixelation — the squares show
**Answer: b) Clean shapes, poorly suited to photographic subtlety**
