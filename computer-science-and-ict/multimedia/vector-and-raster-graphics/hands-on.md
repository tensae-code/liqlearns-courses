# Hands-on — Vector and Raster Graphics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Zoom test
Open any photo and any logo or clip-art. Zoom both to 400% and describe what each does at the edges. Say which kind each is and the evidence.

## Choose the kind
For six jobs (school logo, class photo, map diagram, T-shirt print at two sizes, scanned drawing, website chart), choose raster or vector and give the deciding reason in one line each.
