# Hands-on — A Digital Classification Adventure — Project

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Plan the adventure** — Plan a classification adventure for one corner of the living world: stages, expected groups, tools, and the product's shape.

## Task two
**Sort and defend** — Classify eight creatures (real or pictured) into your groups; write the contested one and defend its place with characteristics.
