# Video script — A Digital Classification Adventure — Project

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Classification** is sorting living things into groups by their **shared characteristics** — the science the project practises. The **adventure** form stages it as a journey: stages of the plant and animal world, at each of which the pupil **observes, records and sorts** — building the classification as the journey's collection. The digital tools carry the collecting: researched facts, pictures gathered and captioned, and the growing classification presented in the project's slides and documents (the guide's earlier environments supply the tool skills — this project spends them together).
The project runs in the guide's project stages: **plan** (the journey's stages, the groups expected, the tools chosen), **journey and build** (each stage's creatures observed, classified and filed, with sources documented — the intellectual-property habit of the earlier environments riding along), and **present** (the classification adventure shown to its audience, the reasoning for contested sorts defended). Its assessment is the guide's project pattern: a product, a process and a presentation — the pupil is marked as a scientist is judged: by the sorting, the evidence and the telling.
