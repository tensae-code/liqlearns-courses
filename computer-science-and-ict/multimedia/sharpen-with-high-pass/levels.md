# Sharpen with High Pass — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do and explain the lesson's one thing — High Pass sharpening: convert the layer to a Smart Object, apply Filter → Other → High Pass at Radius about 2–5 until edges show on grey, then blend with Overlay or Soft Light and fine-tune with Opacity. Practice deepens with more items from the question files and harder corners of the same skill (new images, new values, the same steps), never with new content the lesson does not teach.
