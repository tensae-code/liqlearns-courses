# Hands-on — Sharpen with High Pass

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Edges on grey
Draw a scene. Now draw ONLY its edges on a grey sheet — outlines with nothing filled. Lay the grey edge sheet over the scene: the edges stand out harder. That is High Pass + blend, by hand.

## Mode tasting
Plan four versions of one photo — Overlay, Soft Light, Hard Light, Linear Light — and rank them strongest to subtlest from the lesson, before ever opening the program.
