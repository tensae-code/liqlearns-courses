# Sharpen with High Pass — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Give the High Pass steps in order.
**Answer:** Open the image; convert the background layer to a Smart Object; Filter → Other → High Pass; raise Radius from the lowest value to about 2–5 until edges highlight; open the filter's Blending Options and set Mode to Overlay or Soft Light; fine-tune with Opacity; click OK.

**2.** Name the candidate blend modes and say which two lead.
**Answer:** Overlay (stronger, higher contrast) and Soft Light (subtler) lead; Hard Light and Linear Light are also candidates.
