# Sharpen with High Pass

## Do it first

The strangest sharpener of the three turns your photo grey first. Before reading on, look at the lesson picture: if only the EDGES survive on the grey, what could blending that grey back into the photo possibly do?

## High Pass, step by step — the book's order

1. Open the image.
2. Convert the background layer to a **Smart Object** (Layers panel menu → Convert to Smart Object), so sharpening is applied to the smart object and the original stays unharmed.
3. **Filter → Other → High Pass** — the image turns grey.
4. Adjust **Radius** starting from the lowest value (solid grey, no edges) up to about **2–5**, so edges are highlighted.
5. Double-click the filter's Blending Options icon and change the blend **Mode** to a contrast-boosting mode — **Overlay** (stronger, higher contrast) or **Soft Light** (subtler); Hard Light and Linear Light are also candidates.
6. Fine-tune by lowering the filter's **Opacity**.
7. Click **OK**.
