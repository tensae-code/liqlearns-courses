# Blender — First Steps: 3D Design and the Viewport — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is 3D design?**
**Answer: Building objects with length, width and depth — models that can be turned, lit and printed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do 3D file formats divide into?**
**Answer: The project's own editable format, and exchange formats for other programs and printers**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the filing skill taught here?**
**Answer: Knowing which format a moment needs — keeping, sharing, printing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is Blender?**
**Answer: The free 3D program Kuwait's course is taught in**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the 3D Viewport?**
**Answer: The space where the model stands and is seen**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is the scene viewed?**
**Answer: From any side — orbit, pan and zoom (moving around it)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the Outliner?**
**Answer: The list of every object in the scene, by name — the scene's table of contents**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the first manual skill?**
**Answer: Handling an object — selecting it and moving it in space**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why before modelling?**
**Answer: You cannot build in a room you cannot walk around**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 8 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 1**
