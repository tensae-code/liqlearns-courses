# Adding Sound to a Video and Exporting the Film

## Do it first

The pictures tell the story, but nobody can hear it yet. In this lesson you give the film its sound — and export the finished film for sharing. (teacher guide — evidence)

## Sub lesson 1 — Adding and editing the sound

**Sound effects** and **music** are added to the video on their own audio tracks, placed where the pictures need them: an effect lands on its moment, music runs under a scene. The audio itself is then **edited**: **filters** shape the sound, and the **volume** is balanced so speech is heard clearly and music supports instead of drowning it.

[Picture — adding and editing the sound]

## Sub lesson 2 — Exporting the film

Editing works on a **project**; the audience needs a **film**. **Exporting** renders the whole project — pictures, text, transitions and sound together — into one finished file. The guide exports as **.mp4 for sharing**: a single file that plays on other computers and phones, ready to present and send.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: A silent film is finished by its sound: effects and music are added on their own tracks, the audio is edited with filters and its volume balanced — and then the whole project is exported as a single .mp4 file that can be shared and played anywhere. Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.
