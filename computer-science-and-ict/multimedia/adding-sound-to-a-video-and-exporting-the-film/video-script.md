# Video script — Adding Sound to a Video and Exporting the Film

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Sound effects** and **music** are added to the video on their own audio tracks, placed where the pictures need them: an effect lands on its moment, music runs under a scene. The audio itself is then **edited**: **filters** shape the sound, and the **volume** is balanced so speech is heard clearly and music supports instead of drowning it.
Editing works on a **project**; the audience needs a **film**. **Exporting** renders the whole project — pictures, text, transitions and sound together — into one finished file. The guide exports as **.mp4 for sharing**: a single file that plays on other computers and phones, ready to present and send.
