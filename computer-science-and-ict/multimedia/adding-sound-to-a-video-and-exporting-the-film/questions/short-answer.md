# Adding Sound to a Video and Exporting the Film — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How is a silent film finished?**
**Answer: By its sound — effects and music added and balanced**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where are effects and music added?**
**Answer: On their own audio tracks**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a sound effect land?**
**Answer: On its moment in the pictures**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What shapes the sound itself?**
**Answer: Audio filters**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What must the volume balance achieve?**
**Answer: Speech heard clearly; music supporting, not drowning**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the difference between a project and a film?**
**Answer: A project is what you edit; a film is what the audience plays**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does exporting do?**
**Answer: Renders the whole project into one finished file**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What goes into the exported file?**
**Answer: Pictures, text, transitions and sound together**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what format does the guide export for sharing?**
**Answer: .mp4**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why export as .mp4?**
**Answer: One file that plays on other computers and phones**
