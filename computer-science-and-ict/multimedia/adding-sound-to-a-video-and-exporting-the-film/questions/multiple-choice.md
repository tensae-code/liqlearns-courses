# Adding Sound to a Video and Exporting the Film — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How is a silent film finished?**
a) By its sound — effects and music added and balanced  b) Audio filters  c) A project is what you edit; a film is what the audience plays  d) Pictures, text, transitions and sound together
**Answer: a) By its sound — effects and music added and balanced**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where are effects and music added?**
a) Speech heard clearly; music supporting, not drowning  b) On their own audio tracks  c) Renders the whole project into one finished file  d) .mp4
**Answer: b) On their own audio tracks**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a sound effect land?**
a) A project is what you edit; a film is what the audience plays  b) Pictures, text, transitions and sound together  c) On its moment in the pictures  d) One file that plays on other computers and phones
**Answer: c) On its moment in the pictures**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What shapes the sound itself?**
a) Renders the whole project into one finished file  b) .mp4  c) By its sound — effects and music added and balanced  d) Audio filters
**Answer: d) Audio filters**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What must the volume balance achieve?**
a) Speech heard clearly; music supporting, not drowning  b) Pictures, text, transitions and sound together  c) One file that plays on other computers and phones  d) On their own audio tracks
**Answer: a) Speech heard clearly; music supporting, not drowning**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the difference between a project and a film?**
a) .mp4  b) A project is what you edit; a film is what the audience plays  c) By its sound — effects and music added and balanced  d) On its moment in the pictures
**Answer: b) A project is what you edit; a film is what the audience plays**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does exporting do?**
a) One file that plays on other computers and phones  b) On their own audio tracks  c) Renders the whole project into one finished file  d) Audio filters
**Answer: c) Renders the whole project into one finished file**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What goes into the exported file?**
a) By its sound — effects and music added and balanced  b) On its moment in the pictures  c) Speech heard clearly; music supporting, not drowning  d) Pictures, text, transitions and sound together
**Answer: d) Pictures, text, transitions and sound together**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what format does the guide export for sharing?**
a) .mp4  b) On their own audio tracks  c) Audio filters  d) A project is what you edit; a film is what the audience plays
**Answer: a) .mp4**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why export as .mp4?**
a) On its moment in the pictures  b) One file that plays on other computers and phones  c) Speech heard clearly; music supporting, not drowning  d) Renders the whole project into one finished file
**Answer: b) One file that plays on other computers and phones**
