# Lesson plan — Trimming video clips — smart trim

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Multimedia · **Lesson:** Trimming video clips — smart trim
**Place in the curriculum:** Ethiopia Grade 10, Unit 4 — Image Processing and Multimedia (printed pp. 139–181).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** editing a clip means previewing it, trimming away unnecessary material and revising its properties — and smart trim finds the blurred, shaky, low-quality and low-interest sections, manually by your options or automatically by the program's choices.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the movie and transitions lessons: clips on the Timeline are now judged, and their weak sections cut.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What trimming is** — preview, trim away the unnecessary, revise properties; clips editable in their original applications or inside Premiere Elements.
2. **Smart trim — manual and automatic** — the two ways smart trim finds trimmable sections, and where trimming can be done — Timeline, or one clip at a time from Sceneline.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Importing digital movies — and controlling their sound. Recommended, never forced. A learner can land here directly; the lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define editing a clip, with its three moves.
2. Tell manual from automatic smart trim, and who decides in each.
3. Name the sections aimed at and the two views trimming works from.
## What you need
A computer with Premiere Elements for the real doing; a story to retell for the rehearsal.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks automatic means unchangeable → in automatic smart trim the program proposes; you keep or delete its choices. The final word stays yours.
- Confuses trimming with the transitions lesson's frame note → both use the same truth: cutting a window is not destroying the original material.
