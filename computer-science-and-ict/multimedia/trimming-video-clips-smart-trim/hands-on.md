# Hands-on — Trimming video clips — smart trim

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Record and trim (the guide's school practical)
Record an educational event at school, or take a sample video: trim it, set its duration and freeze one frame — the practical exactly as the guide sets it.

## Story trim
Retell a familiar story in ten sentences, then trim it to six without losing the plot. Mark which sentences were 'shaky' and why.
