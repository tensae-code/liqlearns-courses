# Trimming video clips — smart trim — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Editing a clip means —
a) only renaming it  b) previewing it, trimming away unnecessary material and revising its properties  c) deleting the project  d) adding transitions only
**Answer: b) previewing it, trimming away unnecessary material and revising its properties** — the three moves taught.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Premiere Elements is aimed at sections that are —
a) blurred, shaky, low-quality and low-interest  b) loud and bright  c) very long only  d) silent
**Answer: a) blurred, shaky, low-quality and low-interest** — the four targets.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Clips made in other programs can be edited —
a) nowhere else  b) in their original applications or inside Premiere Elements  c) only on paper  d) only by re-recording
**Answer: b) in their original applications or inside Premiere Elements** — two homes for editing.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Manual smart trim is where —
a) the program decides everything  b) you set the options deciding what counts as trimmable  c) nothing is trimmed  d) the clip plays backwards
**Answer: b) you set the options deciding what counts as trimmable** — the default, user-set way.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In automatic smart trim —
a) you set every option  b) the program decides and you keep or delete its choices  c) trimming is forbidden  d) the clip is deleted
**Answer: b) the program decides and you keep or delete its choices** — the program proposes, you decide.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** From Sceneline view, trimming works on —
a) all clips at once  b) one clip at a time  c) no clips  d) audio only
**Answer: b) one clip at a time** — one at a time.
