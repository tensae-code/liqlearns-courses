Today: **Trimming — cutting the weak parts out**.

Preview the clip. Find what is blurred, shaky, dull.
Cut it away. Keep what matters.
Smart trim helps two ways: you set the rules — or the program proposes, and you decide.
Timeline — many clips. Sceneline — one at a time.

A trimmed film tells the same story — shorter and stronger.
