# Hands-on — Measuring Digital Image and Audio

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Measure three images', 'Find the pixel dimensions of three images (phone photos or downloaded pictures). Work out each one's total pixels and say which is largest — and whether largest also means clearest when printed.')

## Task two
('Measure a song', 'For one audio file, find its sample rate, bit depth and duration. Multiply out its data size in bits, then convert to megabytes.')
