# Measuring Digital Image and Audio — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a digital image made of?**
**Answer: A grid of pixels — single-colour dots**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How are an image's dimensions stated?**
**Answer: In pixels — width × height, such as 1920 × 1080**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does resolution count?**
**Answer: How finely pixels are packed — pixels per inch (ppi) when printed**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a megapixel?**
**Answer: A million pixels**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is an image's total pixel count found?**
**Answer: Width × height in pixels**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the sample rate count?**
**Answer: Samples per second — in hertz (Hz)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What sample rate does CD audio use?**
**Answer: 44,100 Hz**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does bit depth count?**
**Answer: The bits kept per sample**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What three factors give an audio file's data size?**
**Answer: Sample rate × bit depth × seconds**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Malaysia teach the units first?**
**Answer: Every later claim about quality and file size is built from them**
