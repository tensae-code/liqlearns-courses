# Blender — Textures and Lighting — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: An image wrapped onto an object's surface — the mesh wears the picture's detail**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It pinches at the poles, like every globe's map** — as the answer to: What does a plane become with a texture?
**Answer: False — A poster or a landscape skin (any sensible example)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It pinches at the poles, like every globe's map**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Yes — light objects, placed like other objects** — as the answer to: Name three texture-fitting cares.
**Answer: False — Right way up, right scale, seams where eyes forgive**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Yes — light objects, placed like other objects**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A key light to model the forms; softer light to lift shadows** — as the answer to: What do a light's kind and power decide?
**Answer: False — Kind: how the light spreads (point, sun, spot). Power: how strongly it burns**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A key light to model the forms; softer light to lift shadows**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Textures (surfaces) and lighting (revealing them)** — as the answer to: Can lighting alone change a model's worth?
**Answer: False — Yes — the same model reads cheap or precious by its lighting**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Textures (surfaces) and lighting (revealing them)**
**Answer: True**
