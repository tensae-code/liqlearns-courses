# Blender — Modifiers and Multiple Materials — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a modifier?**
**Answer: A standing instruction applied over the mesh — the mesh keeps its simple form; the effect can be changed or removed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Array do?**
**Answer: Repeats the object in a row or grid**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the garden fence, in modifier terms?**
**Answer: One post, arrayed**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does Mirror do?**
**Answer: Reflects the object across an axis — model half, both halves stay identical**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which set build uses Mirror?**
**Answer: The magnet**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Wireframe do?**
**Answer: Turns the solid's skin into its edges' lattice**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does one object wear several materials?**
**Answer: Parts of its mesh are assigned different materials**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Give the lesson's two-material example.**
**Answer: The magnet's two poles in two colours (or the post's wood and metal cap)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What did the first material lesson teach, by contrast?**
**Answer: One object, one material — surface as a single coat**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 8 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 1 — Modifiers**
