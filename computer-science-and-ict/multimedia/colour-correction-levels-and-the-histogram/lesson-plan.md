# Lesson plan — Colour correction — Levels and the histogram

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Multimedia · **Lesson:** Colour correction — Levels and the histogram
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Multimedia of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 9, Unit 4 §4.3 — Image Processing and Multimedia.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Levels corrects colour by adjusting shadows, highlights and midtones against the histogram: pull the white slider to the data's right edge, the black slider only if shadows are too bright, and compare by toggling the adjustment layer.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — what an image is, pixels, and the Photoshop basics as they come; a learner can land here directly and the lesson stands alone.

## If the learner is stuck
- Dragged the white slider past the data's edge → detail is lost — that is clipping. Stop the slider AT the histogram's right-most edge.
