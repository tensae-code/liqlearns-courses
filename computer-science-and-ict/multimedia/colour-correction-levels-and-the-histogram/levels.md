# Colour correction — Levels and the histogram — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do and explain the lesson's one thing — Levels corrects colour by adjusting shadows, highlights and midtones against the histogram: pull the white slider to the data's right edge, the black slider only if shadows are too bright, and compare by toggling the adjustment layer. Practice deepens with more items from the question files and harder corners of the same skill (new images, new values, the same steps), never with new content the lesson does not teach.
