# Colour correction — Levels and the histogram — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Give the Levels steps in order.
**Answer:** Open the photo; add a Levels adjustment layer from the Layers panel; read the histogram; drag the white slider left to the histogram's right-most edge; drag the black slider right only if shadows are too bright; optionally move the grey midtone slider; toggle the layer off to compare.

**2.** What are shadows, highlights and midtones?
**Answer:** Shadows are the darkest parts of an image, highlights the brightest, and midtones everything between them.
