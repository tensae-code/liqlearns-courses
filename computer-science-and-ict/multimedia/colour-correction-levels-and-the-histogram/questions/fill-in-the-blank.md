# Colour correction — Levels and the histogram — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words that complete the sentence.

**1.** The graph of tones in the Properties panel is the ____.
**Answer: histogram**

**2.** Dragging past the edge loses detail — that is ____.
**Answer: clipping**

**3.** The darkest parts of an image are its ____.
**Answer: shadows**
