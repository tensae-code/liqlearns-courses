# Hands-on — Colour correction — Levels and the histogram

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Draw the histogram
Take a photo you know is dark. Sketch its histogram: most bars on the left, a gap on the right. Mark with an arrow where the white slider must travel to. Now sketch the histogram after correction — data stretched to the edge.

## Toggle compare
On any photo app with an edit you can switch on and off, apply one correction and toggle it five times, saying each time which version keeps more detail. That toggling is step 7 — the comparison habit.
