# Editing Audio in Audacity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does every Audacity edit apply to?**
a) The selection — the part of the waveform marked by dragging across it  b) Removes the selected part and holds it for pasting  c) Removes it outright  d) It keeps the selection and removes everything outside it
**Answer: a) The selection — the part of the waveform marked by dragging across it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Cut do?**
a) Removes it outright  b) Removes the selected part and holds it for pasting  c) It keeps the selection and removes everything outside it  d) Undo — a cut can be tried and taken back
**Answer: b) Removes the selected part and holds it for pasting**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does Delete do to a selection?**
a) It keeps the selection and removes everything outside it  b) Undo — a cut can be tried and taken back  c) Removes it outright  d) By pasting, and by placing clips along the track in order
**Answer: c) Removes it outright**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does Trim keep and remove?**
a) Undo — a cut can be tried and taken back  b) By pasting, and by placing clips along the track in order  c) As deliberately as sound — shortened where it drags, left where it gives weight  d) It keeps the selection and removes everything outside it
**Answer: d) It keeps the selection and removes everything outside it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What stands behind every editing move?**
a) Undo — a cut can be tried and taken back  b) By pasting, and by placing clips along the track in order  c) As deliberately as sound — shortened where it drags, left where it gives weight  d) Introduction first, then the body, then the close
**Answer: a) Undo — a cut can be tried and taken back**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How are pieces joined?**
a) As deliberately as sound — shortened where it drags, left where it gives weight  b) By pasting, and by placing clips along the track in order  c) Introduction first, then the body, then the close  d) It is played through once, whole
**Answer: b) By pasting, and by placing clips along the track in order**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is silence treated in editing?**
a) Introduction first, then the body, then the close  b) It is played through once, whole  c) As deliberately as sound — shortened where it drags, left where it gives weight  d) When the piece sounds intended from end to end
**Answer: c) As deliberately as sound — shortened where it drags, left where it gives weight**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) In what order are clips arranged?**
a) It is played through once, whole  b) When the piece sounds intended from end to end  c) The selection — the part of the waveform marked by dragging across it  d) Introduction first, then the body, then the close
**Answer: d) Introduction first, then the body, then the close**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is done before the finished edit is saved?**
a) It is played through once, whole  b) When the piece sounds intended from end to end  c) The selection — the part of the waveform marked by dragging across it  d) Removes the selected part and holds it for pasting
**Answer: a) It is played through once, whole**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is editing finished?**
a) The selection — the part of the waveform marked by dragging across it  b) When the piece sounds intended from end to end  c) Removes the selected part and holds it for pasting  d) Removes it outright
**Answer: b) When the piece sounds intended from end to end**
