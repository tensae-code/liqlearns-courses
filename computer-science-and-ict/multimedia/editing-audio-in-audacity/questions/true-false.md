# Editing Audio in Audacity — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does every Audacity edit apply to?" is: The selection — the part of the waveform marked by dragging across it**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does Cut do?" is: Undo — a cut can be tried and taken back**
**Answer: False** — Removes the selected part and holds it for pasting

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does Delete do to a selection?" is: Removes it outright**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does Trim keep and remove?" is: As deliberately as sound — shortened where it drags, left where it gives weight**
**Answer: False** — It keeps the selection and removes everything outside it

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What stands behind every editing move?" is: Undo — a cut can be tried and taken back**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How are pieces joined?" is: It is played through once, whole**
**Answer: False** — By pasting, and by placing clips along the track in order

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is silence treated in editing?" is: As deliberately as sound — shortened where it drags, left where it gives weight**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "In what order are clips arranged?" is: The selection — the part of the waveform marked by dragging across it**
**Answer: False** — Introduction first, then the body, then the close

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is done before the finished edit is saved?" is: It is played through once, whole**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is editing finished?" is: Removes it outright**
**Answer: False** — When the piece sounds intended from end to end
