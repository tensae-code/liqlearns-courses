# Editing Audio in Audacity — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does every Audacity edit apply to?**
**Answer: The selection — the part of the waveform marked by dragging across it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Cut do?**
**Answer: Removes the selected part and holds it for pasting**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does Delete do to a selection?**
**Answer: Removes it outright**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does Trim keep and remove?**
**Answer: It keeps the selection and removes everything outside it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What stands behind every editing move?**
**Answer: Undo — a cut can be tried and taken back**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How are pieces joined?**
**Answer: By pasting, and by placing clips along the track in order**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is silence treated in editing?**
**Answer: As deliberately as sound — shortened where it drags, left where it gives weight**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) In what order are clips arranged?**
**Answer: Introduction first, then the body, then the close**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is done before the finished edit is saved?**
**Answer: It is played through once, whole**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is editing finished?**
**Answer: When the piece sounds intended from end to end**
