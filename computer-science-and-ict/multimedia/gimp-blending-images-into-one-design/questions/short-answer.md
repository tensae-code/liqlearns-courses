# GIMP — Blending Images into One Design — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is blending images?**
**Answer: Placing parts of several images into one design so they read as a single scene**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does each brought-in part sit?**
**Answer: On its own layer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the book's set example?**
**Answer: Balloons over Kuwait Towers**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a blend judged?**
**Answer: By its seams — if the eye finds the joins, it is not finished**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the Scale tool do?**
**Answer: Resizes a part to its true size in the scene**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What betrays a blend at once?**
**Answer: A part at the wrong scale — a balloon bigger than the Tower**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do perspective lines give?**
**Answer: The design's depth rules — things shrink as they recede**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Is blending pasting?**
**Answer: No — it is persuading several photographs they were always one**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name the second set activity.**
**Answer: The TV activity**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 7 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 1 — Blending Images**
