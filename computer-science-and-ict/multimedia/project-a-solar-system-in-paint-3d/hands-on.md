# Hands-on — Project a Solar System in Paint 3D

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 3 project: create a Solar System in Paint 3D with labelled planets Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach project a solar system in paint 3d to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
