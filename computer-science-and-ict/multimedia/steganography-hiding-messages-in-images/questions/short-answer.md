# Steganography — Hiding Messages in Images — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) A student hides a message in a photo, posts the photo, and the platform recompresses it as JPEG. What happens to the message, and why?**
**Answer: It is destroyed. JPEG compression recalculates and approximates the colour values to save space, so the least significant bits — where the message lives — are rewritten; the decoder then reads unrelated bits.**
