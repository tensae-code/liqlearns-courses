Today: **Digital images — made of pixels**.

Remember the one thing: a digital image is an image represented digitally, in combinations of bits called pixels — and a pixel is the smallest element of an image.

Watch the lesson's pictures. Say each key word after me. Then do the Do It Yourself at the end of the lesson — with your own hands.
