# Hands-on — Digital images — made of pixels

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Pixel drawing on squared paper
On squared (graph) paper, fill squares with three colours to draw a simple shape — a house, a letter, a flag. Each filled square is one pixel. Stand two metres away: the squares join into a picture.

## Logo detective
Find three logos around your school or street. For each one, sketch it and label the symbol or brand mark, the logotype, and the tagline if there is one.
