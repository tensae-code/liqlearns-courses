# Digital images — made of pixels — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What happens to a picture when it is digitised?
**Answer:** It is turned into binary digits (bits) — represented digitally as combinations of bits called pixels.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do images matter, in the guide's own motto?
**Answer:** A single still image can convey complex, multiple ideas more effectively than a verbal description.
