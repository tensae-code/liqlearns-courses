# Digital images — made of pixels — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words that complete the sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A digital image is made of small elements called ____.
**Answer: pixels**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The three basic colours are red, ____ and green.
**Answer: blue**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The name of a business written in letters inside its logo is its ____.
**Answer: logotype**
