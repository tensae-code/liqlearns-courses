# Digital images — made of pixels — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Scanning a photo into Photoshop digitises it.
**Answer: True** — scanning turns the picture into bits.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A logo is made of text only.
**Answer: False** — a logo is a symbol made of text AND images.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** One pixel can show many colours at once.
**Answer: False** — one pixel is one square of one colour.
