# Digital images — made of pixels — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A pixel is —
a) a whole photograph  b) the smallest element of an image  c) a kind of camera  d) a colour filter
**Answer: b) the smallest element of an image** — the smallest element.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A digital image is represented in combinations of bits called —
a) files  b) folders  c) pixels  d) layers
**Answer: c) pixels** — pixels.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Taking a picture with a digital camera —
a) prints it  b) digitises it  c) scans it  d) paints it
**Answer: b) digitises it** — the camera turns the picture into bits.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Every other colour is obtained by mixing which three basics?
a) red, yellow, blue  b) cyan, magenta, yellow  c) red, blue and green  d) black, white, grey
**Answer: c) red, blue and green** — red, blue and green — the teacher guide's concluding summary.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A logo is usually built from —
a) a photograph and a frame  b) a symbol or brand mark plus a logotype, often with a tagline  c) text only  d) a table and a chart
**Answer: b) a symbol or brand mark plus a logotype, often with a tagline** — mark + logotype (+ tagline).

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** A computer does everything in —
a) pixels only  b) binary digits (bits)  c) letters  d) colours
**Answer: b) binary digits (bits)** — series of 0s and 1s.
