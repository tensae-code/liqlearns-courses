# Drawing and filling a first Flash piece

## Do it first

On paper, draw a rectangle. Now colour it in without lifting your pen more than twice. Before reading on, say the order you worked in — outline first, or colour first? Flash works outline first, fill second, and this lesson is that order on screen.

## Draw, fill, save — in the book's order

A new project starts **File → New**. The first basic piece is built in the work area with the toolbox drawing tools — Line, Rectangle and Oval among them. In the book's order:

1. Click the **Rectangle Tool**.
2. **Draw the rectangle** in the work area.
3. Click the **Paint Bucket Tool** and pick a colour from **Color Swatches**.
4. **Click inside the rectangle** to fill it.

Saving is **File → Save** or **Ctrl+S**, under a suitable name. A piece that is not saved is a piece that can vanish — save the first piece the moment it is filled.

## Say it to your companion

Walk your companion through the four steps in order, naming the tool at each step. Then say the two saving routes.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the four steps in order from memory; check against the lesson and fix any slip.
2. Draw the same piece with the Oval Tool instead: which steps change, which stay the same?
3. Say why the fill must come after the outline, using the bucket's behaviour.
