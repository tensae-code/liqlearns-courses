# Hands-on — Drawing and filling a first Flash piece

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Paper Flash
Rule a 'work area' box on paper. Draw the rectangle with a ruler, then fill it with a crayon in one chosen colour — narrating each of the four steps aloud as you do it.

## Teach the order
Teach the four steps to a partner who must repeat them back in order before touching anything. You sign off only a perfect recital.
