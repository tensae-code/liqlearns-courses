# 2D and 3D Animation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does animation rest on?**
**Answer: The eye blending quick frames into motion — persistence of vision**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are key poses?**
**Answer: The main positions, with in-between frames filled by hand or tweening**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) 2D animation's world is what?**
**Answer: Flat — drawings of characters and backgrounds**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name 2D's homes.**
**Answer: Cartoons, explainer films, the moving web (any two)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 11 EPAL Basika Themata, Chapter 6**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does 3D differ at birth?**
**Answer: It builds its world — modelled objects with depth, surfaces and light — instead of drawing it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What moves in 3D?**
**Answer: The objects and a virtual camera**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does photorealistic mean here?**
**Answer: Frames the eye takes for film**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name 3D's two costs.**
**Answer: Modelling before any movement; computing time for every frame**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When choose 2D?**
**Answer: When the drawing's voice is the point; 3D when the world must be walked around**
