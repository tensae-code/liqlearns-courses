# Sharpen with Unsharp Mask

## Do it first

You know the three controls. Before reading on, say why this method — like the brightening lesson — begins by duplicating the layer, and why you would zoom right into the photo before judging sharpness.

## Unsharp Mask, step by step — the book's order

1. Open the photo and **duplicate the background layer** (right-click → Duplicate Layer), renaming the copy **Sharpen Layer**.
2. With that layer selected, **zoom to 100%**.
3. **Filter → Sharpen → Unsharp Mask**.
4. In the dialog, set the **Radius and Amount**, experimenting while watching the preview window.
5. Click the preview to **toggle before/after**, and drag inside it to inspect different parts (the main window previews too).
6. When satisfied, click **OK** and save.
