# Hands-on — Sharpen with Unsharp Mask

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Preview toggling
In any editor with a before/after preview, apply one effect and toggle it ten times, dragging the view to a detailed area — an eye, text, grass. Judge from the detail, as this lesson's preview step teaches.

## Sharpen Layer discipline
Plan a full sharpen on paper: write the layer name, the zoom, the menu path, and the Radius and Amount you would try first for a normal photo and for a high-resolution one.
