# Sharpen with Unsharp Mask — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Give the Unsharp Mask steps in order.
**Answer:** Open the photo; duplicate the background layer and rename the copy Sharpen Layer; select it and zoom to 100%; Filter → Sharpen → Unsharp Mask; set Radius and Amount while watching the preview, toggling and dragging to inspect; click OK and save.

**2.** Why duplicate the layer before sharpening?
**Answer:** So the sharpening is applied to a copy — the Sharpen Layer — in the same protective pattern as the other corrections in this unit.
