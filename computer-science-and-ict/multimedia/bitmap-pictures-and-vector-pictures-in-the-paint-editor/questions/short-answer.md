# Bitmap Pictures and Vector Pictures in the Paint Editor — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) A classmate converted their only copy of a character to bitmap and now wants its arm reshaped. Explain their position and the lesson in it.**
**Answer:** A sound answer: the shapes became dots, so reshape-point editing and clean sharpness are lost and cannot be converted back; keep a vector copy before converting.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When is grouping the right tool in a vector drawing?**
**Answer:** A sound answer: when several shapes form one piece — a face of eyes, nose and mouth — and must move, scale or be placed together as a unit, then ungrouped when parts need separate editing.
