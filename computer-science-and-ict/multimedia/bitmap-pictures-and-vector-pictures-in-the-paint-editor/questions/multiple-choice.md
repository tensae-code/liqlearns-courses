# Bitmap Pictures and Vector Pictures in the Paint Editor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A vector picture is made of —**
a) Coloured dots only  b) Shapes the editor remembers  c) Sounds
**Answer: b) Shapes the editor remembers** — Shapes — remembered and editable.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Reshape points let you —**
a) Bend and stretch a shape exactly  b) Play a sound  c) Name a sprite
**Answer: a) Bend and stretch a shape exactly** — Drag a point, the shape changes.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A bitmap picture is made of —**
a) Shapes  b) A grid of coloured dots (pixels)  c) Blocks
**Answer: b) A grid of coloured dots (pixels)** — Dots, not shapes.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Converting vector to bitmap loses —**
a) Nothing  b) The editable parts and the sharpness  c) The colours only
**Answer: b) The editable parts and the sharpness** — That loss does not reverse.
