# Video script — Bitmap Pictures and Vector Pictures in the Paint Editor


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


Vector: remembered shapes. Sharp at any size.

Points reshape them. Shapes can group.

Bitmap: a grid of dots. Enlarged, it goes blocky.

Converting loses the shapes. It does not come back.
