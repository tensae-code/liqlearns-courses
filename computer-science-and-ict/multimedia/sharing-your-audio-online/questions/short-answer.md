# Sharing Your Audio Online — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why must a finished piece be exported before sharing?**
**Answer: The Audacity project is a workbench, not a playable file — exporting renders the tracks into one audio file**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What format is the export commonly saved in?**
**Answer: MP3**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What can the exported file carry with it?**
**Answer: Its title and its maker**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is shared — the project file or the export?**
**Answer: The export, never the project file**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why do listeners need the export?**
**Answer: They need the sound, not the bench it was built on**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What goes with a shared piece?**
**Answer: A title and a sentence saying what the piece is**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where is audio shared online?**
**Answer: On a sound or video service, or where the class publishes its work**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What credit care goes with sharing?**
**Answer: Credit any music or voice that is not yours**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What travel warning applies to a published sound?**
**Answer: Like a published page, it can travel beyond its first audience**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Whose work should you share?**
**Answer: Your own**
