# Sharing Your Audio Online — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why must a finished piece be exported before sharing?" is: The Audacity project is a workbench, not a playable file — exporting renders the tracks into one audio file**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What format is the export commonly saved in?" is: They need the sound, not the bench it was built on**
**Answer: False** — MP3

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What can the exported file carry with it?" is: Its title and its maker**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is shared — the project file or the export?" is: On a sound or video service, or where the class publishes its work**
**Answer: False** — The export, never the project file

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why do listeners need the export?" is: They need the sound, not the bench it was built on**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What goes with a shared piece?" is: Like a published page, it can travel beyond its first audience**
**Answer: False** — A title and a sentence saying what the piece is

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where is audio shared online?" is: On a sound or video service, or where the class publishes its work**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What credit care goes with sharing?" is: The Audacity project is a workbench, not a playable file — exporting renders the tracks into one audio file**
**Answer: False** — Credit any music or voice that is not yours

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What travel warning applies to a published sound?" is: Like a published page, it can travel beyond its first audience**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Whose work should you share?" is: Its title and its maker**
**Answer: False** — Your own
