# Hands-on — Inserting and styling text in a multimedia project

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Half-page biography (the book's practical)
Write and edit a half-page biography of a person you admire, on paper or a computer: draft the text, then perform one before-styling and one after-styling pass and note what each changed.

## Route rehearsal
On a paper 'work area', act out all three entry routes with a partner calling the clicks; swap roles until both recitals are clean.
