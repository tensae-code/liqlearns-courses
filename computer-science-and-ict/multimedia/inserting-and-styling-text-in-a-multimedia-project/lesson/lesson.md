# Inserting and styling text in a multimedia project

## Do it first

On paper, draw an empty box and write a title inside it in your best lettering. Before reading on, say: did you choose the lettering style before you wrote, or after? Flash lets you do both — and the order changes how the styling step works.

## Sub lesson 1 — Getting text in — insert, import, paste

Editing is the creation and manipulation of digital audio-visual files — image, audio, video, animation, graphics. Its **display icons** — the icons that show images or graphics: symbols, tools, figures, text — are inserted alike whether single or combined.

Text work, first route — **inserting**: take the **Text Tool**, **drag a text field** in the work area, **type**, then **click away** to deactivate the field.

Second route — **importing**: **File → Import**, choose the file, **Open** — and the text lands in the work area. Or **paste** copied text with **Edit → Paste / Ctrl+V**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Styling text — before and after

**Styling before typing**: set the text **type, character position and spacing** in the **Properties panel before** drawing the field, so the style applies as you type. If the panel is hidden, **right-click the work area → Properties** brings it up.

**Editing and formatting afterwards**: click into the field, **select the text**, and set **font, alignment and colour** from the same panel.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: style before typing for a whole new field; style afterwards for fixing one word. Choosing the order first saves re-selecting everything later.
