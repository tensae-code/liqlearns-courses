# Colour correction — Curves — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Give the Curves steps in order.
**Answer:** Open the image; add a Curves adjustment layer; click the line to create points and drag them to shape the curve, corners left in place; delete points with Delete or by dragging them off; compare by hiding the layer; save.

**2.** How do you increase contrast with Curves?
**Answer:** Pull the curve down in the shadows (left) to darken them and up in the highlights (right) to brighten them.
