# Colour correction — Curves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Curves differs from Levels by giving —
a) fewer controls  b) more separate control over shadows, highlights and midtones  c) automatic fixes only  d) sharpening
**Answer: b) more separate control over shadows, highlights and midtones** — more separate control.

**2.** To INCREASE contrast, you pull the curve —
a) up in the shadows, down in the highlights  b) down in the shadows, up in the highlights  c) all up  d) all down
**Answer: b) down in the shadows, up in the highlights** — shadows down, highlights up — the S-curve.

**3.** Points on the curve are created by —
a) double-clicking the canvas  b) clicking the line in the Properties panel  c) pressing Ctrl  d) the Zoom Tool
**Answer: b) clicking the line in the Properties panel** — click the line.

**4.** A point is deleted by —
a) the Delete key, or dragging it off the line  b) saving  c) the Eraser  d) closing the panel
**Answer: a) the Delete key, or dragging it off the line** — Delete key or drag it off.

**5.** Beginners should leave —
a) all points alone  b) the two corner points in their corners  c) the layer hidden  d) the presets unused
**Answer: b) the two corner points in their corners** — corners stay in their corners.

**6.** The curve is reset with —
a) the Default preset  b) Ctrl+J  c) Image → Crop  d) the Hand Tool
**Answer: a) the Default preset** — Default.
