# Colour correction — Curves

## Do it first

Levels gave you three sliders. Before reading on, imagine instead a line you can bend at any point — push one part down, pull another up. What extra control would that give you over a photo's tones?

## Bending the line — the book's order

**Curves** is like Levels but gives more separate control over shadows, highlights and midtones — more powerful, so more care is needed.

To **increase contrast**: pull the curve **down in the shadows** (left) to darken them, and **up in the highlights** (right) to brighten them. Decreasing contrast does the reverse.

1. Open the image.
2. Add a **Curves adjustment layer**.
3. Click the line in the Properties panel to create points, and drag them to shape the curve — leave the two corner points in their corners until you are experienced.
4. Delete a point with the Delete key, or by dragging it off the line.
5. Compare by hiding the layer, then save.
