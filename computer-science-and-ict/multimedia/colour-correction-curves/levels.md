# Colour correction — Curves — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do and explain the lesson's one thing — Curves works like Levels but with more separate control: drag points on the line — down in the shadows and up in the highlights to raise contrast — leaving the corner points in place until experienced. Practice deepens with more items from the question files and harder corners of the same skill (new images, new values, the same steps), never with new content the lesson does not teach.
