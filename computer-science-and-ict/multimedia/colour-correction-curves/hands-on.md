# Hands-on — Colour correction — Curves

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Bend a wire
Take a straight piece of soft wire or string pinned at both corners of a drawn square. Bend its left part down and its right part up — an S. That shape, on the Curves graph, darkens shadows and brightens highlights: contrast.

## Preset first
In any photo editor with curve presets, apply two presets to the same photo and sketch each curve's shape. Say which raised contrast and which lowered it, from the shapes alone.
