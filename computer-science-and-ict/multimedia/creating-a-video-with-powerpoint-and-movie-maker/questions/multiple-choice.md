# Creating a Video with PowerPoint and Movie Maker — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does a presentation become a video?**
a) By exporting it  b) A narration  c) How long each slide stays  d) A presenter, or PowerPoint
**Answer: a) By exporting it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is recorded over the slides?**
a) How long each slide stays  b) A narration  c) A presenter, or PowerPoint  d) An object that can be sent, stored and replayed
**Answer: b) A narration**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do timings set?**
a) A presenter, or PowerPoint  b) An object that can be sent, stored and replayed  c) How long each slide stays  d) By exporting it
**Answer: c) How long each slide stays**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the exported video not need?**
a) An object that can be sent, stored and replayed  b) By exporting it  c) A narration  d) A presenter, or PowerPoint
**Answer: d) A presenter, or PowerPoint**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the slide show become on export?**
a) An object that can be sent, stored and replayed  b) By exporting it  c) A narration  d) How long each slide stays
**Answer: a) An object that can be sent, stored and replayed**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is added to a Movie Maker project?**
a) Cuts a clip's ends  b) Videos, photos and music  c) Divides a clip so its middle can be removed or moved  d) Fade and speed change
**Answer: b) Videos, photos and music**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does trim do?**
a) Divides a clip so its middle can be removed or moved  b) Fade and speed change  c) Cuts a clip's ends  d) Gather, arrange, trim, then polish
**Answer: c) Cuts a clip's ends**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does split do?**
a) Fade and speed change  b) Gather, arrange, trim, then polish  c) Videos, photos and music  d) Divides a clip so its middle can be removed or moved
**Answer: d) Divides a clip so its middle can be removed or moved**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name two more shaping tools from the lesson.**
a) Fade and speed change  b) Gather, arrange, trim, then polish  c) Videos, photos and music  d) Cuts a clip's ends
**Answer: a) Fade and speed change**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the editor's order in the book?**
a) Videos, photos and music  b) Gather, arrange, trim, then polish  c) Cuts a clip's ends  d) Divides a clip so its middle can be removed or moved
**Answer: b) Gather, arrange, trim, then polish**
