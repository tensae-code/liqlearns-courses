# Creating a Video with PowerPoint and Movie Maker — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does a presentation become a video?**
**Answer: By exporting it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is recorded over the slides?**
**Answer: A narration**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do timings set?**
**Answer: How long each slide stays**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the exported video not need?**
**Answer: A presenter, or PowerPoint**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the slide show become on export?**
**Answer: An object that can be sent, stored and replayed**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is added to a Movie Maker project?**
**Answer: Videos, photos and music**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does trim do?**
**Answer: Cuts a clip's ends**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does split do?**
**Answer: Divides a clip so its middle can be removed or moved**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name two more shaping tools from the lesson.**
**Answer: Fade and speed change**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the editor's order in the book?**
**Answer: Gather, arrange, trim, then polish**
