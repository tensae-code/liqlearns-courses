# Hands-on — Creating a Video with PowerPoint and Movie Maker

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Deck to video plan', 'Take a five-slide deck you know. Write its video plan: the narration line for each slide and the seconds it stays. Add them up — if the total is over two minutes, cut timings before you cut words.')

## Task two
('Paper edit', 'A clip runs: [0–10s good][10–25s shaky middle][25–40s good][40–50s too dark]. Write the Movie Maker edit list — splits, trims, one fade, one speed change — that saves the good parts, in order.')
