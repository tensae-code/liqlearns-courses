# Hands-on — Project — Making a Poster on Protecting Nature

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Plan on paper
In your group, plan the poster on paper: the one ask, a sketch of the picture, the exact words, and where each part goes. Show the plan to another group — can they read the message from the plan alone?

## Make and test it
Make the poster in Paint from your plan. Save it, then hold it up at the front of the class: read its words aloud from the back row. If they cannot be read, what must grow?
