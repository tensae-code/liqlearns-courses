# Project — Making a Poster on Protecting Nature

## Do it first

Grade 2 ends the year by making something for other people: a poster (affiche) that asks its readers to protect nature — built from the year's Paint and words skills, working as the guide's projects do, together.

## Sub lesson 1 — Planning the poster — message first

A **poster** shows **one message** that can be read at a glance. The project's message is **protecting nature**: the group chooses the one thing its poster asks for — keep water clean, care for trees, do not drop litter — and plans **a picture that shows it** and **few, big words that say it**. Planning comes before Paint: what the picture shows, what the words say, and where each goes on the page are decided first, so the making is execution, not searching.

## Sub lesson 2 — Making the poster in Paint

The poster is made with the year's tools together: **shapes and the pencil** draw the nature picture, the **fill and chosen colours** make it clear and bright, and **text added to the picture** carries the words — big enough to read across a classroom. The finished poster is **saved as a file** and **printed or shown** to its readers: a poster is finished only when someone else can read its message without its makers explaining it.
