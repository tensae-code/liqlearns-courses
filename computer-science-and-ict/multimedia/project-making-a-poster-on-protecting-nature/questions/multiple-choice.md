# Project — Making a Poster on Protecting Nature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a poster show?**
a) One message, readable at a glance  b) Protecting nature  c) A picture that shows it, and few big words that say it  d) What the picture shows, what the words say, and where each goes
**Answer: a) One message, readable at a glance**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is this project's message?**
a) A picture that shows it, and few big words that say it  b) Protecting nature  c) What the picture shows, what the words say, and where each goes  d) So the making is execution, not searching
**Answer: b) Protecting nature**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What two parts carry the message?**
a) What the picture shows, what the words say, and where each goes  b) So the making is execution, not searching  c) A picture that shows it, and few big words that say it  d) Shapes and the pencil, with fill and chosen colours
**Answer: c) A picture that shows it, and few big words that say it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is decided before opening Paint?**
a) So the making is execution, not searching  b) Shapes and the pencil, with fill and chosen colours  c) As text inside the Paint picture  d) What the picture shows, what the words say, and where each goes
**Answer: d) What the picture shows, what the words say, and where each goes**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why plan before making?**
a) So the making is execution, not searching  b) Shapes and the pencil, with fill and chosen colours  c) As text inside the Paint picture  d) Big enough to read across a classroom
**Answer: a) So the making is execution, not searching**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which tools make the poster's picture?**
a) As text inside the Paint picture  b) Shapes and the pencil, with fill and chosen colours  c) Big enough to read across a classroom  d) It is saved as a file, and printed or shown
**Answer: b) Shapes and the pencil, with fill and chosen colours**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the poster's text added?**
a) Big enough to read across a classroom  b) It is saved as a file, and printed or shown  c) As text inside the Paint picture  d) When a reader gets its message without the makers explaining
**Answer: c) As text inside the Paint picture**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How big must the poster's words be?**
a) It is saved as a file, and printed or shown  b) When a reader gets its message without the makers explaining  c) One message, readable at a glance  d) Big enough to read across a classroom
**Answer: d) Big enough to read across a classroom**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is done with the finished poster?**
a) It is saved as a file, and printed or shown  b) When a reader gets its message without the makers explaining  c) One message, readable at a glance  d) Protecting nature
**Answer: a) It is saved as a file, and printed or shown**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is a poster truly finished?**
a) One message, readable at a glance  b) When a reader gets its message without the makers explaining  c) Protecting nature  d) A picture that shows it, and few big words that say it
**Answer: b) When a reader gets its message without the makers explaining**
