# Project — Making a Poster on Protecting Nature — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a poster show?" is: One message, readable at a glance**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is this project's message?" is: Shapes and the pencil, with fill and chosen colours**
**Answer: False** — Protecting nature

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What two parts carry the message?" is: A picture that shows it, and few big words that say it**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is decided before opening Paint?" is: Big enough to read across a classroom**
**Answer: False** — What the picture shows, what the words say, and where each goes

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why plan before making?" is: So the making is execution, not searching**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which tools make the poster's picture?" is: When a reader gets its message without the makers explaining**
**Answer: False** — Shapes and the pencil, with fill and chosen colours

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is the poster's text added?" is: As text inside the Paint picture**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How big must the poster's words be?" is: Protecting nature**
**Answer: False** — Big enough to read across a classroom

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is done with the finished poster?" is: It is saved as a file, and printed or shown**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is a poster truly finished?" is: What the picture shows, what the words say, and where each goes**
**Answer: False** — When a reader gets its message without the makers explaining
