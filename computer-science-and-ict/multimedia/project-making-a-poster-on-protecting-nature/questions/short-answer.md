# Project — Making a Poster on Protecting Nature — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a poster show?**
**Answer: One message, readable at a glance**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is this project's message?**
**Answer: Protecting nature**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What two parts carry the message?**
**Answer: A picture that shows it, and few big words that say it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is decided before opening Paint?**
**Answer: What the picture shows, what the words say, and where each goes**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why plan before making?**
**Answer: So the making is execution, not searching**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which tools make the poster's picture?**
**Answer: Shapes and the pencil, with fill and chosen colours**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the poster's text added?**
**Answer: As text inside the Paint picture**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How big must the poster's words be?**
**Answer: Big enough to read across a classroom**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is done with the finished poster?**
**Answer: It is saved as a file, and printed or shown**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is a poster truly finished?**
**Answer: When a reader gets its message without the makers explaining**
