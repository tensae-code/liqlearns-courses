# GIMP — Drawing Regular Shapes: Selections and Paths — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does a selection become a shape?**
**Answer: A shaped selection, filled, is a shape**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which tool makes an exact ellipse or circle?**
**Answer: Ellipse Select**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does holding proportions guarantee?**
**Answer: A circle stays a circle at any size**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the selection, in the lesson's image?**
**Answer: A stencil the computer holds steady for you**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the Paths tool draw?**
**Answer: An outline of points and curves, placed by the maker**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What can a path then be?**
**Answer: Filled, stroked (outlined), or used as a selection**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which shapes belong to paths?**
**Answer: Shapes selections cannot name — a hill's curve, a flag's wave**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Are paths editable afterwards?**
**Answer: Yes — move a point and the curve follows**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do the two instruments divide the world?**
**Answer: Selections for named shapes; paths for shapes with intentions**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 7 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 1 — Regular Drawing**
