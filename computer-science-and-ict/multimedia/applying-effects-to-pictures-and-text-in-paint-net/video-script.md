# Video script — Applying Effects to Pictures and Text in Paint.NET

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Effects** are ready transformations applied to a layer or selection in one command. The book walks the menu by families: **artistic** effects restyle a picture toward painting and sketching; **distort** effects bend and twist its geometry; **noise** adds or removes the grain of specks; and the **fractal** renderings grow mathematical patterns of endless detail. An effect is judged by the picture's purpose: the same photograph can be evidence, illustration or artwork, and the effect — or its absence — is what decides which.
Text in Paint.NET is drawn on its own layer, so every picture effect is also a **text effect**: titles can be textured, shadowed and bent by the same menu — the booklet's display lettering is made this way. The lesson's set piece gathers the strand: **drawing a Mongol ger with effects** — the national dwelling built from the editor's shapes and then finished with its effects, sky, ground and ornament transformed by the menu the lesson teaches. Mongolia's sequence is the teaching: draw truly first, then let effects dress what drawing built.
