# Applying Effects to Pictures and Text in Paint.NET — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an effect?**
**Answer: A ready transformation applied to a layer or selection in one command**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four Effects families the book walks.**
**Answer: Artistic, distort, noise and fractals**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do artistic effects do?**
**Answer: Restyle a picture toward painting and sketching**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do distort effects do?**
**Answer: Bend and twist the picture's geometry**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does noise work on?**
**Answer: The grain of specks — adding or removing it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How do effects reach text?**
**Answer: Text is drawn on its own layer, so picture effects apply to it too**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the lesson's set piece?**
**Answer: Drawing a Mongol ger with effects**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) By what is an effect judged?**
**Answer: By the picture's purpose — evidence, illustration or artwork**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's sequence?**
**Answer: Draw truly first, then let effects dress what drawing built**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which editor's Effects menu is this?**
**Answer: Paint.NET's**
