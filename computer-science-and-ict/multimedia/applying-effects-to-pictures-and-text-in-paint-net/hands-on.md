# Hands-on — Applying Effects to Pictures and Text in Paint.NET

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('One picture, three fates', 'Apply an artistic, a distort and a noise effect to copies of one photograph on separate layers. Label each with its family and the purpose it would serve.')

## Task two
('The ger, dressed', 'Draw a simple ger, then finish sky and ground with effects of your choice. Write which families you used and what each did.')
