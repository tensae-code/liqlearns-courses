# Applying Audio Effects in Audacity — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where do Audacity's effects live?**
**Answer: In the Effect menu**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Amplify do?**
**Answer: Makes a selection louder or quieter**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do Fade In and Fade Out do?**
**Answer: Grow and shrink the sound at its ends, so it begins and ends gently**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name two character-changing effects from the lesson.**
**Answer: Change of speed or pitch, and echo (any two named)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does noise reduction clean?**
**Answer: A hiss or hum recorded with the voice**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Preview do?**
**Answer: Lets the editor hear the effect before committing to it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does an effect apply to?**
**Answer: The selection only**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the fixed order for using an effect?**
**Answer: Select, open the effect, preview, adjust, apply, then listen**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when the ear says no to an effect?**
**Answer: It is undone**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the taste rule for effects?**
**Answer: An effect serves the listening — decoration is not improvement**
