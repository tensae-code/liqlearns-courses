# Applying Audio Effects in Audacity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where do Audacity's effects live?**
a) In the Effect menu  b) Makes a selection louder or quieter  c) Grow and shrink the sound at its ends, so it begins and ends gently  d) Change of speed or pitch, and echo (any two named)
**Answer: a) In the Effect menu**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Amplify do?**
a) Grow and shrink the sound at its ends, so it begins and ends gently  b) Makes a selection louder or quieter  c) Change of speed or pitch, and echo (any two named)  d) A hiss or hum recorded with the voice
**Answer: b) Makes a selection louder or quieter**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do Fade In and Fade Out do?**
a) Change of speed or pitch, and echo (any two named)  b) A hiss or hum recorded with the voice  c) Grow and shrink the sound at its ends, so it begins and ends gently  d) Lets the editor hear the effect before committing to it
**Answer: c) Grow and shrink the sound at its ends, so it begins and ends gently**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name two character-changing effects from the lesson.**
a) A hiss or hum recorded with the voice  b) Lets the editor hear the effect before committing to it  c) The selection only  d) Change of speed or pitch, and echo (any two named)
**Answer: d) Change of speed or pitch, and echo (any two named)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does noise reduction clean?**
a) A hiss or hum recorded with the voice  b) Lets the editor hear the effect before committing to it  c) The selection only  d) Select, open the effect, preview, adjust, apply, then listen
**Answer: a) A hiss or hum recorded with the voice**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Preview do?**
a) The selection only  b) Lets the editor hear the effect before committing to it  c) Select, open the effect, preview, adjust, apply, then listen  d) It is undone
**Answer: b) Lets the editor hear the effect before committing to it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does an effect apply to?**
a) Select, open the effect, preview, adjust, apply, then listen  b) It is undone  c) The selection only  d) An effect serves the listening — decoration is not improvement
**Answer: c) The selection only**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the fixed order for using an effect?**
a) It is undone  b) An effect serves the listening — decoration is not improvement  c) In the Effect menu  d) Select, open the effect, preview, adjust, apply, then listen
**Answer: d) Select, open the effect, preview, adjust, apply, then listen**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when the ear says no to an effect?**
a) It is undone  b) An effect serves the listening — decoration is not improvement  c) In the Effect menu  d) Makes a selection louder or quieter
**Answer: a) It is undone**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the taste rule for effects?**
a) In the Effect menu  b) An effect serves the listening — decoration is not improvement  c) Makes a selection louder or quieter  d) Grow and shrink the sound at its ends, so it begins and ends gently
**Answer: b) An effect serves the listening — decoration is not improvement**
