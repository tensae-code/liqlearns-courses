# Applying Audio Effects in Audacity — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where do Audacity's effects live?" is: In the Effect menu**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does Amplify do?" is: A hiss or hum recorded with the voice**
**Answer: False** — Makes a selection louder or quieter

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do Fade In and Fade Out do?" is: Grow and shrink the sound at its ends, so it begins and ends gently**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name two character-changing effects from the lesson." is: The selection only**
**Answer: False** — Change of speed or pitch, and echo (any two named)

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does noise reduction clean?" is: A hiss or hum recorded with the voice**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does Preview do?" is: It is undone**
**Answer: False** — Lets the editor hear the effect before committing to it

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does an effect apply to?" is: The selection only**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the fixed order for using an effect?" is: In the Effect menu**
**Answer: False** — Select, open the effect, preview, adjust, apply, then listen

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens when the ear says no to an effect?" is: It is undone**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the taste rule for effects?" is: Grow and shrink the sound at its ends, so it begins and ends gently**
**Answer: False** — An effect serves the listening — decoration is not improvement
