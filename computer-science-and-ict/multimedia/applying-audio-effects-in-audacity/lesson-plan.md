## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The effects — amplify, fade, change and clean** — Audacity's **Effect menu** holds the sound-changers. **Amplify** makes a selection louder or quieter. **Fade in** and **fade out** grow and shrink the sound at its ends, so a piece begins and ends gently instead of starting with a bang. Other effects change the sound's character — its speed, its pitch, an echo — and **noise reduction** cleans a hiss or hum recorded with the voice. Every effect offers settings, and its **preview** lets the editor hear before committing.
2. **The editor's rule — select, preview, apply, listen** — An effect applies to the **selection** only, so the order is fixed: select the part, open the effect, **preview** it, adjust, then apply — and **listen** to the result in its place in the piece. Effects are undone when the ear says no. The lesson's taste rule: an effect serves the listening; a piece buried under effects has been decorated, not improved.
