# Applying Audio Effects in Audacity

## Do it first

A clean recording can still be the wrong shape: too quiet, too harsh, an echo where none belongs. Class VIII's effects lesson applies Audacity's effects — chosen changes to the sound itself — each on a selection, each judged by ear.

## Sub lesson 1 — The effects — amplify, fade, change and clean

Audacity's **Effect menu** holds the sound-changers. **Amplify** makes a selection louder or quieter. **Fade in** and **fade out** grow and shrink the sound at its ends, so a piece begins and ends gently instead of starting with a bang. Other effects change the sound's character — its speed, its pitch, an echo — and **noise reduction** cleans a hiss or hum recorded with the voice. Every effect offers settings, and its **preview** lets the editor hear before committing.

## Sub lesson 2 — The editor's rule — select, preview, apply, listen

An effect applies to the **selection** only, so the order is fixed: select the part, open the effect, **preview** it, adjust, then apply — and **listen** to the result in its place in the piece. Effects are undone when the ear says no. The lesson's taste rule: an effect serves the listening; a piece buried under effects has been decorated, not improved.
