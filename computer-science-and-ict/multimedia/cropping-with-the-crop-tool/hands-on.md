# Hands-on — Cropping with the Crop Tool

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Paper crop
Draw or print a picture with too much background. Lay four strips of paper over its edges to frame a tighter crop — move the strips until the subject is strong, then draw the frame line. That is the Crop Tool with paper.

## Before and after sketch
Sketch a wide scene, then sketch the same scene cropped two different ways. Under each, write what the crop focuses on.
