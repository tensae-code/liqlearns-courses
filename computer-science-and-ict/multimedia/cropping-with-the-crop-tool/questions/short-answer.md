# Cropping with the Crop Tool — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Give the four crop steps in order.
**Answer:** Launch Photoshop and open the image; select the Crop Tool; drag the edges and corner/side squares to resize the border; press Enter.

**2.** Why crop a photograph?
**Answer:** To create focus or strengthen composition by removing portions of the photo.
