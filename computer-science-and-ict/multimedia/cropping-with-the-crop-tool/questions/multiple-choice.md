# Cropping with the Crop Tool — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Cropping is done to —
a) make the file bigger  b) create focus or strengthen composition  c) add text  d) change colours
**Answer: b) create focus or strengthen composition** — focus and composition.

**2.** After selecting the Crop Tool, the whole image is —
a) deleted  b) selected automatically  c) blurred  d) rotated
**Answer: b) selected automatically** — selected automatically.

**3.** The border is resized by dragging —
a) the Menu Bar  b) the edges and the squares at the sides and corners  c) the Rulers  d) the Layer Palette
**Answer: b) the edges and the squares at the sides and corners** — edges and corner/side squares.

**4.** The cut is confirmed by pressing —
a) Esc  b) Enter  c) Tab  d) Shift
**Answer: b) Enter** — Enter.

**5.** The first step is —
a) press Enter  b) launch Photoshop and open the image  c) select the Crop Tool  d) drag the border
**Answer: b) launch Photoshop and open the image** — open the image first.
