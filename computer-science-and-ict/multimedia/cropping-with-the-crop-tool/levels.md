# Cropping with the Crop Tool — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do and explain the lesson's one thing — cropping removes portions of a photo to create focus or strengthen composition: open the image, select the Crop Tool, drag the border's edges and corner squares until satisfied, and press Enter. Practice deepens with more items from the question files and harder corners of the same skill (new images, new values, the same steps), never with new content the lesson does not teach.
