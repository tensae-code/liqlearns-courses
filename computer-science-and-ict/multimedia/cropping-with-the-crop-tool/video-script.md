Today: **Cropping with the Crop Tool**.

First, what we are doing: cropping removes portions of a photo to create focus or strengthen composition: open the image, select the Crop Tool, drag the border's edges and corner squares until satisfied, and press Enter.

Cropping removes portions of a photo to create focus or strengthen composition.
1. Launch Photoshop and open the image.
2. Select the Crop Tool — the whole image is selected automatically.
3. Drag the edges and the squares at the sides and corners to resize the border, until you are satisfied.
4. Press Enter — the outside is cut away.
