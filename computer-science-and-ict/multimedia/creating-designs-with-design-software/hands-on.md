# Hands-on — Creating Designs with Design Software

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Design a label
Use any drawing tool (or squared paper as 'software'): design a label for a jar, to a brief you write first — size, words, one picture. Check the result against the brief.

## Undo with intent
Make a design, then deliberately change one thing you dislike using undo and redo thinking: say aloud what changed and which version fits the brief better.
