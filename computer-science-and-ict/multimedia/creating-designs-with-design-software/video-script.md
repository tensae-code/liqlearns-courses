# Video script — Creating Designs with Design Software

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Design software** is software used to **create designs on a computer**. The design itself starts as **an idea — from the design brief** — and the software's tools let the designer **draw and change shapes and lines**. Finished designs are **saved as files**, for later use and sharing.
Good design work in software begins with **the brief or plan**. Measurements in the design must be **accurate**, the undo tool lets the designer **take back a change**, and before a design is final it is **checked against the brief**. The design file can then be sent to a printer, a maker, or a machine.
