# Creating Designs with Design Software — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is design software used for?**
a) To create designs on a computer  b) An idea — from the design brief  c) Draw and change shapes and lines  d) Files, for later use and sharing
**Answer: a) To create designs on a computer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A design in software starts as what?**
a) Draw and change shapes and lines  b) An idea — from the design brief  c) Files, for later use and sharing  d) Changes are quick and exact copies are easy
**Answer: b) An idea — from the design brief**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do design software tools let you do?**
a) Files, for later use and sharing  b) Changes are quick and exact copies are easy  c) Draw and change shapes and lines  d) The design brief
**Answer: c) Draw and change shapes and lines**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Designs made in software can be saved as what?**
a) Changes are quick and exact copies are easy  b) The design brief  c) Accurate  d) Files, for later use and sharing
**Answer: d) Files, for later use and sharing**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why design on a computer?**
a) Changes are quick and exact copies are easy  b) The design brief  c) Accurate  d) To a printer, a maker, or a machine
**Answer: a) Changes are quick and exact copies are easy**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Before it is final, a design is checked against what?**
a) Accurate  b) The design brief  c) To a printer, a maker, or a machine  d) Take back a change
**Answer: b) The design brief**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Measurements in a design must be what?**
a) To a printer, a maker, or a machine  b) Take back a change  c) Accurate  d) The design brief or plan
**Answer: c) Accurate**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) A design file can be sent where?**
a) Take back a change  b) The design brief or plan  c) To create designs on a computer  d) To a printer, a maker, or a machine
**Answer: d) To a printer, a maker, or a machine**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the undo tool let a designer do?**
a) Take back a change  b) The design brief or plan  c) To create designs on a computer  d) An idea — from the design brief
**Answer: a) Take back a change**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Good design software work begins with what?**
a) To create designs on a computer  b) The design brief or plan  c) An idea — from the design brief  d) Draw and change shapes and lines
**Answer: b) The design brief or plan**
