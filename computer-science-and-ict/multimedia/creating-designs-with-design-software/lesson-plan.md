## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Design software** — **Design software** is software used to **create designs on a computer**. The design itself starts as **an idea — from the design brief** — and the software's tools let the designer **draw and change shapes and lines**. Finished designs are **saved as files**, for later use and sharing.
2. **Creating a design** — Good design work in software begins with **the brief or plan**. Measurements in the design must be **accurate**, the undo tool lets the designer **take back a change**, and before a design is final it is **checked against the brief**. The design file can then be sent to a printer, a maker, or a machine.
