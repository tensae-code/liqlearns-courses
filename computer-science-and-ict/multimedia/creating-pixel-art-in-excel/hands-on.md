# Hands-on — Creating Pixel Art in Excel

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Plan and fill** — Plan a 12×12 pixel picture on paper (count it), then build it in Excel with square cells and fills; compare against the plan.

## Task two
**Resize by numbers** — Take your picture and state its dimensions in cells and centimetres; then make a version at half the cells across, saying what was lost.
