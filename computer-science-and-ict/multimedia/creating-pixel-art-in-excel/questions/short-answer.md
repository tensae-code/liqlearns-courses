# Creating Pixel Art in Excel — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a pixel?**
**Answer: A tiny coloured dot in a grid**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is Excel's grid made into a canvas?**
**Answer: Columns narrow and equal so cells are square, then filled with colour**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How many cells per pixel?**
**Answer: One**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is made before filling?**
**Answer: A plan — the drawing counted out on paper**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name three example pictures.**
**Answer: A flag, a flower, a character**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What sets the picture's size and fineness?**
**Answer: The number of cells across and down**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What sets its printed size?**
**Answer: The cell size**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What keeps parts in proportion?**
**Answer: The plan's counting**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must a pixel artist be able to say?**
**Answer: How wide the picture is — in cells and in centimetres**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the project's craft, in three acts?**
**Answer: Plan the grid, fill the grid, check against the plan**
