# Video transitions — placing and previewing them — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A transition —
a) deletes frames  b) phases one clip out and the next in  c) records sound  d) imports clips
**Answer: b) phases one clip out and the next in** — the definition taught.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A transition on the cut between two clips is called —
a) single-sided  b) double-sided  c) trimmed  d) repeated
**Answer: b) double-sided** — on the cut = double-sided.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A fade to black at one clip's end alone is —
a) double-sided  b) impossible  c) single-sided  d) a motion path
**Answer: c) single-sided** — one clip only.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Trimming a clip —
a) deletes the trimmed frames  b) never deletes frames; In and Out points frame a window  c) exports the frames  d) repeats the frames
**Answer: b) never deletes frames; In and Out points frame a window** — nothing is deleted.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Where trimmed frames do not exist, a transition uses —
a) black frames  b) repeated frames  c) the next clip only  d) no frames
**Answer: b) repeated frames** — repeating frames.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Selection of transitions is —
a) File → Import  b) Edit → Transitions  c) Control → Test Movie  d) Start → Programs
**Answer: b) Edit → Transitions** — the book's route.

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** The craft warning says add a transition —
a) on every cut  b) only where it makes sense, after editing and before the soundtrack  c) before editing  d) instead of a soundtrack
**Answer: b) only where it makes sense, after editing and before the soundtrack** — the warning in full.

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** Transition position types are —
a) Top, Middle, Bottom  b) Start, End, Centre  c) In, Out, Through  d) First, Second, Third
**Answer: b) Start, End, Centre** — Start, End, Centre.
