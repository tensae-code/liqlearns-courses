# Video transitions — placing and previewing them — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cross dissolve is an emphatic transition.
**Answer: False** — it is the subtle end; page turn and pinwheel are emphatic.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Transitions can be customised and previewed before applying.
**Answer: True** — as taught.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Motion paths can be drawn with Line, Oval, Rectangle and Lasso tools.
**Answer: True** — the four path tools.
