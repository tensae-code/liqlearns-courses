# Lesson plan — Video transitions — placing and previewing them

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Multimedia · **Lesson:** Video transitions — placing and previewing them
**Place in the curriculum:** Ethiopia Grade 10, Unit 4 — Image Processing and Multimedia (printed pp. 139–181).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a transition phases one clip out and the next in — normally double-sided on the cut, or single-sided at one clip's start or end — is customised and previewed before applying, and is added only where it makes sense.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the editing lessons before it in this title: clips must exist on a timeline before anything can phase between them. The worked program is Adobe Premiere Elements 9, as the book names it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Transitions and how they sit on clips** — what a transition is; double-sided on the cut vs single-sided at a start or end; where its frames come from — trimmed frames or repeated frames.
2. **Choosing and setting a transition** — Edit → Transitions; preview and customise before applying; the craft warning; transform properties and transition position types.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Importing and transforming images; the editing work of this unit so far. Recommended, never forced. A learner can land here directly; the lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define a transition and name three kinds, from subtle to emphatic.
2. Place transitions double-sided and single-sided, and explain their frames.
3. Choose, preview and customise a transition by the book's route, heeding the craft warning.
## What you need
A computer with Premiere Elements 9 for the real doing; paper timeline strips for the placing work.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks trimmed frames are deleted frames → trimming deletes nothing; In and Out points frame a window over the original clip, and the hidden frames feed the transition.
- Adds a transition everywhere → the craft warning is part of the skill: only where it makes sense.
