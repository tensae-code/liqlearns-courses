# Video script — Video transitions — placing and previewing them

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Video transitions**.

One clip fades out. The next fades in. That is a transition.
Soft — a cross dissolve. Bold — a page turn.
On the cut, it holds both clips. At the end alone — a fade to black.
Choose it. Preview it. Then apply it.
And remember the warning: only where it makes sense.
