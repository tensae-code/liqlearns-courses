# Hands-on — Video transitions — placing and previewing them

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Paper timeline
Cut two paper 'clips'. Lay them end to end, then overlap them slightly under a strip of tracing paper labelled 'dissolve'. Slide a third clip away and mark where a single-sided fade to black would sit.

## Transition audit
Watch any short video and log every transition: kind, double or single-sided, and whether it made sense there. Bring the log to class.
