# Hands-on — Information Design — Making Information Clear

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Redesign one page', 'Take one confusing real page — a timetable, bill or form — and redesign it on paper: reorder, group, emphasise, simplify. Keep every fact identical.')

## Task two
('Five-second test', "Show your redesign to someone new for five seconds and ask what the key fact was. If they miss it, redesign again.")
