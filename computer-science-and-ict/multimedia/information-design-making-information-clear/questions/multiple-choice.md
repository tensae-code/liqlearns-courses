# Information Design — Making Information Clear — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is information design?**
a) The deliberate arrangement of information so its reader can find, understand and use it  b) Order, grouping, emphasis and plain language  c) Yes — a badly designed timetable, form or chart can hide true facts  d) Digitising multiplies copies — a confusing original becomes a million confusing screens
**Answer: a) The deliberate arrangement of information so its reader can find, understand and use it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four plain tools of information design**
a) Yes — a badly designed timetable, form or chart can hide true facts  b) Order, grouping, emphasis and plain language  c) Digitising multiplies copies — a confusing original becomes a million confusing screens  d) The reader's task — what they must decide or do
**Answer: b) Order, grouping, emphasis and plain language**
