# Information Design — Making Information Clear — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is information design?**
**Answer: The deliberate arrangement of information so its reader can find, understand and use it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four plain tools of information design**
**Answer: Order, grouping, emphasis and plain language**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Can bad design make true information unusable?**
**Answer: Yes — a badly designed timetable, form or chart can hide true facts**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Egypt teach design inside the digitisation chapter?**
**Answer: Digitising multiplies copies — a confusing original becomes a million confusing screens**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does good design start from?**
**Answer: The reader's task — what they must decide or do**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the designer's first practical check?**
**Answer: Can a new reader find the key fact in seconds?**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What should emphasis and colour tell the truth about?**
**Answer: Importance — they must not merely decorate**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name one honesty fault in information design**
**Answer: Making a small number look big (or a guess look certain, or a choice look forced)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What should be removed from a design?**
**Answer: Anything the reader's task does not need**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does this lesson sit in Egypt's Grade 10 book?**
**Answer: At the close of the digitisation chapter, after sound, images and video**
