# Information Design — Making Information Clear — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is information design?" is: The deliberate arrangement of information so its reader can find, understand and use it**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the four plain tools of information design?" is: Yes — a badly designed timetable, form or chart can hide true facts**
**Answer: False** — Order, grouping, emphasis and plain language

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Can bad design make true information unusable?" is: Yes — a badly designed timetable, form or chart can hide true facts**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why does Egypt teach design inside the digitisation chapter?" is: The reader's task — what they must decide or do**
**Answer: False** — Digitising multiplies copies — a confusing original becomes a million confusing screens

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does good design start from?" is: The reader's task — what they must decide or do**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the designer's first practical check?" is: Importance — they must not merely decorate**
**Answer: False** — Can a new reader find the key fact in seconds?

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What should emphasis and colour tell the truth about?" is: Importance — they must not merely decorate**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name one honesty fault in information design?" is: Anything the reader's task does not need**
**Answer: False** — Making a small number look big (or a guess look certain, or a choice look forced)

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What should be removed from a design?" is: Anything the reader's task does not need**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does this lesson sit in Egypt's Grade 10 book?" is: The deliberate arrangement of information so its reader can find, understand and use it**
**Answer: False** — At the close of the digitisation chapter, after sound, images and video
