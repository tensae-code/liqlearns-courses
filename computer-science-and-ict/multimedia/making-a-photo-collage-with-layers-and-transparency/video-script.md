# Video script — Making a Photo Collage with Layers and Transparency

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **collage** pastes pictures together into one image, and in the layered editor each pasted part lands on **its own layer**. The **Magic Wand** selects by colour likeness — click a sky and the wand takes the sky — so a photographed animal can be lifted out of its background in one selection. **Transparency** then does the blending: a layer's opacity lowered lets it melt into what is beneath, edges and skies soften into each other, and five photographs stop looking pasted and start looking present. Cut cleanly, stack honestly, blend patiently — the collage's order.
Two knowledges keep the made scene true. **Perspective**: the pasted parts must obey one space — far animals smaller, all feet on one ground line, one light over all. And **colour choice** is done with instruments, not wishes: the **colour calculator** gives a colour its numbers — **RGB** for the screen, **CMYK** for print, its **hex code** for the web — and the **colour wheel** shows which colours marry and which fight. Mongolia's collage is judged as the composition lesson judged: one picture, one land, one light — the livestock standing in a steppe that never existed, believed because every craft in the lesson was obeyed.
