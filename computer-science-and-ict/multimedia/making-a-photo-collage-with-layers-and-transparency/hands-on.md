# Hands-on — Making a Photo Collage with Layers and Transparency

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Cut and stack', 'Lift one animal or object out of its photograph with the Magic Wand, place it on its own layer over a landscape, and set its size by the perspective rules.')

## Task two
('Blend and number', 'Blend your pasted part by transparency until a partner cannot find the seam at arm's length. Then record the RGB and hex values of the sky colour you matched.')
