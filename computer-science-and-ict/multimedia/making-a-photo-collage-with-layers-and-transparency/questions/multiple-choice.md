# Making a Photo Collage with Layers and Transparency — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a collage?**
a) Pictures pasted together into one image  b) On its own layer  c) Colour likeness — click a sky, take the sky  d) Lets it melt into what is beneath — edges and skies soften together

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does each pasted part land?**
a) Colour likeness — click a sky, take the sky  b) On its own layer  c) Lets it melt into what is beneath — edges and skies soften together  d) Cut cleanly, stack honestly, blend patiently
**Answer: b) On its own layer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the Magic Wand select by?**
