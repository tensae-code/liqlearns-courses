# Making a Photo Collage with Layers and Transparency — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a collage?" is: Pictures pasted together into one image**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does each pasted part land?" is: Colour likeness — click a sky, take the sky**
**Answer: False** — On its own layer

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the Magic Wand select by?" is: Colour likeness — click a sky, take the sky**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does lowering a layer's transparency do?" is: Cut cleanly, stack honestly, blend patiently**
**Answer: False** — Lets it melt into what is beneath — edges and skies soften together

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "State the collage's working order." is: Cut cleanly, stack honestly, blend patiently**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What must the pasted parts obey for perspective?" is: Its numbers — RGB, CMYK and its hex code**
**Answer: False** — One space — far smaller, one ground line, one light

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the colour calculator give a colour?" is: Its numbers — RGB, CMYK and its hex code**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which colour system serves the screen, and which print?" is: Which colours marry and which fight**
**Answer: False** — RGB for the screen; CMYK for print

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the colour wheel show?" is: Which colours marry and which fight**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the book's collage subject?" is: Pictures pasted together into one image**
**Answer: False** — The five-snout livestock in a steppe landscape
