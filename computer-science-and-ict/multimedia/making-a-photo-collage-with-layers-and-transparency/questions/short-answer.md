# Making a Photo Collage with Layers and Transparency — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a collage?**
**Answer: Pictures pasted together into one image**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does each pasted part land?**
**Answer: On its own layer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the Magic Wand select by?**
**Answer: Colour likeness — click a sky, take the sky**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does lowering a layer's transparency do?**
**Answer: Lets it melt into what is beneath — edges and skies soften together**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) State the collage's working order.**
**Answer: Cut cleanly, stack honestly, blend patiently**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What must the pasted parts obey for perspective?**
**Answer: One space — far smaller, one ground line, one light**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the colour calculator give a colour?**
**Answer: Its numbers — RGB, CMYK and its hex code**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which colour system serves the screen, and which print?**
**Answer: RGB for the screen; CMYK for print**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the colour wheel show?**
**Answer: Which colours marry and which fight**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's collage subject?**
**Answer: The five-snout livestock in a steppe landscape**
