# Meeting GIMP — a Raster Image Editor — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A free raster image editor — it edits images made of pixels**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A free raster image editor — it edits images made of pixels** — as the answer to: What does raster mean here?
**Answer: False — The image is made of pixels, changed dot by dot**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The toolbox, the canvas, and the panels (options, colours, layers)**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The toolbox, the canvas, and the panels (options, colours, layers)** — as the answer to: What rhythm does tool work follow?
**Answer: False — Choose the tool, set its options, apply it to the canvas**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: GNU Image Manipulation Program**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: GNU Image Manipulation Program** — as the answer to: What is a layer?
**Answer: False — One transparent sheet of the image's stack, holding part of the picture, edited without touching the others**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Cropping a photo, correcting colours, erasing/retouching a flaw (also: composing layers)**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Cropping a photo, correcting colours, erasing/retouching a flaw (also: composing layers)** — as the answer to: What is the difference between saving and exporting?
**Answer: False — Saving keeps the working format with layers; exporting flattens to PNG or JPEG for use**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Name your layers, and edit the right one**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Name your layers, and edit the right one** — as the answer to: Why keep text on its own layer?
**Answer: False — So it can be changed without touching the picture beneath**
