# Meeting GIMP — a Raster Image Editor — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is GIMP?** → **A free raster image editor — it edits images made of pixels**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does raster mean here?** → **The image is made of pixels, changed dot by dot**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the window's three gatherings.** → **The toolbox, the canvas, and the panels (options, colours, layers)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What rhythm does tool work follow?** → **Choose the tool, set its options, apply it to the canvas**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does GIMP stand for?** → **GNU Image Manipulation Program**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a layer?** → **One transparent sheet of the image's stack, holding part of the picture, edited without touching the others**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name three practical crafts from the section.** → **Cropping a photo, correcting colours, erasing/retouching a flaw (also: composing layers)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the difference between saving and exporting?** → **Saving keeps the working format with layers; exporting flattens to PNG or JPEG for use**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is layer discipline?** → **Name your layers, and edit the right one**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why keep text on its own layer?** → **So it can be changed without touching the picture beneath**
