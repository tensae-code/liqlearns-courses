# Meeting GIMP — a Raster Image Editor — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is GIMP?**
**Answer: A free raster image editor — it edits images made of pixels**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does raster mean here?**
**Answer: The image is made of pixels, changed dot by dot**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the window's three gatherings.**
**Answer: The toolbox, the canvas, and the panels (options, colours, layers)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What rhythm does tool work follow?**
**Answer: Choose the tool, set its options, apply it to the canvas**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does GIMP stand for?**
**Answer: GNU Image Manipulation Program**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a layer?**
**Answer: One transparent sheet of the image's stack, holding part of the picture, edited without touching the others**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name three practical crafts from the section.**
**Answer: Cropping a photo, correcting colours, erasing/retouching a flaw (also: composing layers)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the difference between saving and exporting?**
**Answer: Saving keeps the working format with layers; exporting flattens to PNG or JPEG for use**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is layer discipline?**
**Answer: Name your layers, and edit the right one**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why keep text on its own layer?**
**Answer: So it can be changed without touching the picture beneath**
