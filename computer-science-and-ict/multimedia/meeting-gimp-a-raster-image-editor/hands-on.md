# Hands-on — Meeting GIMP — a Raster Image Editor

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**First GIMP job** — Open a photo in GIMP (or plan it fully on paper): crop it, add a text layer with a title, and export it as PNG — keeping the layered original saved.

## Task two
**Layer sketch** — Draw the layer stack of a poster: background photo, a pasted object, a title — in order, naming each layer and one edit safe on it.
