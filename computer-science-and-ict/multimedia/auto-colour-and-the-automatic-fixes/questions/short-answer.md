# Auto Colour and the automatic fixes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** What are the automatic tools, and where are they?
**Answer:** One-command correction tools in the Image menu — Auto Colour is the pictured example — that improve an image instead of manual Levels, Curves or Saturation work.

**2.** When would you choose a manual correction over Auto Colour?
**Answer:** When the one command does not get it right — manual Levels/Curves give step-by-step control over shadows, highlights, midtones and vividness.
