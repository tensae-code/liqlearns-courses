# Auto Colour and the automatic fixes

## Do it first

You have corrected colour three ways by hand. Before reading on, say when you would rather let the program decide in one click — and what you give up when you do.

## One command instead of manual work

Some images need more specialised correction; several **automatic tools** are offered for them.

They are found in the **Image menu** — **Auto Colour** is the one pictured in the book, alongside the other auto adjustments there.

[Picture 20535 — the Image menu's auto options]

An automatic tool improves an image in **one command**, instead of the manual Levels, Curves or Saturation work of the last three lessons. The hand methods stay yours for when the one command does not get it right.
