# Project — Produce a Short Film in a Group

## Do it first

Everything in the unit comes together here: your group chooses a topic, researches it, films it, edits it — and presents it. Work as a production team. (teacher guide — evidence)

## Sub lesson 1 — Research the topic — and respect intellectual property

The group first **researches its topic on the web**, gathering facts worth filming. Everything the group borrows — text, pictures, music — belongs to someone: **intellectual property** must be **respected**, so the group uses what it may use, names its sources, and makes its own material wherever it can.

## Sub lesson 2 — Produce and present the film

Production follows the unit's road: plan (script, storyboard), shoot, build on the Timeline, add text, effects and sound, export. Then the group **presents the film** to its audience and can say how each part was made — the presentation is part of the project, not an extra.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: The unit's project: in a group, research a topic on the web, plan and produce a short film about it — respecting intellectual property in everything you use — and present the finished film. Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.
