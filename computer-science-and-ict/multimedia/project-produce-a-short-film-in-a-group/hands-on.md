# Hands-on — Project — Produce a Short Film in a Group

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Research sheet
As a group, fill one research sheet for your topic: five facts from the web, their sources, and the borrowed material you plan to use — each marked with how it respects its owner.

## Present the film
Export the group's film and present it. After the showing, each member names one part they made and one thing the group would improve.
