# Project — Produce a Short Film in a Group — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the group research its topic?**
**Answer: On the web**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the group gather in research?**
**Answer: Facts worth filming**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must be respected in everything borrowed?**
**Answer: Intellectual property**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does the group respect intellectual property?**
**Answer: Uses what it may use, names sources, makes its own material where it can**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the film's planning pair?**
**Answer: Script and storyboard**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the production road, in order?**
**Answer: Plan, shoot, build on the Timeline, add text, effects and sound, export**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the group do at the end?**
**Answer: Presents the finished film**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must the group be able to say at the presentation?**
**Answer: How each part of the film was made**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who owns borrowed text, pictures and music?**
**Answer: Someone — they are that person's property**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Is the presentation optional?**
**Answer: No — it is part of the project**
