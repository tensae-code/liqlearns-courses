# Project — Produce a Short Film in a Group — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does the group research its topic?" is: On the web**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the group gather in research?" is: Intellectual property**

**Answer: True**
