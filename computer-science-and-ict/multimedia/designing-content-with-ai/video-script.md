# Video script — Designing Content with AI

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Digital content — text, image, sound, video — can now be **drafted by an AI** from a description: the tool produces a first version at a speed no hand matches. Indonesia's Grade 9 teaching guide covers designing content with AI inside the productivity-and-content chapter, beside the strategy lesson: the AI enters as one more instrument in the content workshop, strongest at producing raw material — variants, drafts, first pictures — from which the designer chooses. (This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only.)
What the AI does not do is the design: **choosing** what the content is for and whether a draft serves it, **correcting** the tool's errors and flatness, and **owning** the result — the honesty duties of credit and truth do not move to the tool. A design made with AI is judged like any design: does it serve its audience and purpose, and is what it shows true? The guide's pairing is the lesson: strategy first, then tools — the designer who knows the strategy can use the AI; the one who only has the AI has drafts, not designs.
