**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Multimedia · **Lesson:** Designing Content with AI
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Indonesia (2026-11-14) — in the title Multimedia on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). **The one thing this lesson teaches:** designing content with AI — making and designing digital content with artificial intelligence as the tool: what the AI contributes, and what stays the designer's judgement (Indonesia Grade 9 teacher guide — evidence).
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The AI as a making tool** — Digital content — text, image, sound, video — can now be **drafted by an AI** from a description: the tool produces a first version at a speed no hand matches. Indonesia's Grade 9 teaching guide covers designing content with AI inside the productivity-and-content chapter, beside the strategy lesson: the AI enters as one more instrument in the content workshop, strongest at producing raw material — variants, drafts, first pictures — from which the designer chooses. (This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only.)
2. **The designer's part — choose, correct, own** — What the AI does not do is the design: **choosing** what the content is for and whether a draft serves it, **correcting** the tool's errors and flatness, and **owning** the result — the honesty duties of credit and truth do not move to the tool. A design made with AI is judged like any design: does it serve its audience and purpose, and is what it shows true? The guide's pairing is the lesson: strategy first, then tools — the designer who knows the strategy can use the AI; the one who only has the AI has drafts, not designs.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
