# Hands-on — Designing Content with AI

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Draft, then design** — Take one content job (a poster for a school event). Produce or imagine three AI drafts, then do the designer's three acts in writing: choose, correct (list the fixes), and own (the credit and truth note you would publish with it).

## Task two
**Judge an AI design** — Find a piece of AI-made content. Judge it as this lesson does — audience, purpose, truth — and write the two changes a designer, not a tool, should make.
