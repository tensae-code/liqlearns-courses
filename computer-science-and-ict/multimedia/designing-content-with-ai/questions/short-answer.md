# Designing Content with AI — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What can an AI draft from a description?**
**Answer: A first version of text, image, sound or video content**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the AI strongest, as a content tool?**
**Answer: Producing raw material — variants, drafts, first pictures — to choose from**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the AI not do?**
**Answer: The design — choosing, correcting and owning**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What three duties stay the designer's?**
**Answer: Choosing what the content is for, correcting the tool's errors, and owning the result**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which duties do not move to the tool?**
**Answer: The honesty duties of credit and truth**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is an AI-made design judged?**
**Answer: Like any design — does it serve its audience and purpose, and is what it shows true?**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must come before tools, in the guide's pairing?**
**Answer: Strategy**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) The designer with only the AI has what?**
**Answer: Drafts, not designs**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) This lesson stands on what evidence?**
**Answer: Teacher-guide evidence only (Indonesia Grade 9 guide)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where is this taught?**
**Answer: Grade 9 teacher guide, Bab III — making and designing content using AI**
