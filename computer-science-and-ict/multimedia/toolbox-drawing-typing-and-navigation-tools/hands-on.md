# Hands-on — Toolbox — drawing, typing and navigation tools

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Perfect square practice
On paper, draw a square freehand, then draw one using a ruler's corner as a guide. The ruler is your Shift key — the constraint that keeps the shape perfect.

## Zoom and pan on a phone
Open a photo on a phone. Pinch to zoom into one corner (the Zoom tool), then drag the image to see another corner (the Hand tool). Say which Photoshop tool each finger action copies.
