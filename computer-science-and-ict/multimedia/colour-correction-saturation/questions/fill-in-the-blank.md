# Colour correction — Saturation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words that complete the sentence.

**1.** Drag the ____ slider up or down in the Properties panel.
**Answer: Saturation**

**2.** Dull, ____ colours are made vivid by increasing saturation.
**Answer: muted**

**3.** Too much saturation makes colours look ____.
**Answer: unnatural**
