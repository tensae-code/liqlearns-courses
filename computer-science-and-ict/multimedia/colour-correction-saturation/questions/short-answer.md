# Colour correction — Saturation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Give the Saturation steps in order.
**Answer:** Open the photo; add a Hue/Saturation adjustment layer in the Layers panel; drag the Saturation slider in the Properties panel up or down; save.

**2.** What does the Saturation slider control, at its two extremes?
**Answer:** Colour vividness: up makes colours brighter and more vivid (too far = unnatural); all the way down removes colour completely, giving a black-and-white image.
