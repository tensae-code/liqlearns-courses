# Hands-on — Colour correction — Saturation

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Three versions
Take one colourful scene — real or drawn. Produce its three versions on paper: colour the same drawing vividly, naturally, and in greys only. Label each with its saturation: high, natural, removed.

## Find the natural point
On a phone photo editor, slide saturation up slowly on a portrait until the skin looks unnatural, then come back to where it looks true. Note the number where 'too much' begins.
