# Colour correction — Saturation

## Do it first

Picture a market photo: the fabrics and peppers should sing with colour, but in the photo they look grey and tired. Before reading on, guess which single slider wakes colours up — and what happens if you push it too far.

## The Saturation slider — the book's order

Dull, muted colours are made brighter and more vivid by **increasing saturation**. Decreasing saturation makes colours less vivid, and removing it completely gives a black-and-white (grey-scale) image.

1. Open the photo.
2. Add a **Hue/Saturation adjustment layer** in the Layers panel.
3. Drag the **Saturation slider** in the Properties panel up or down.
4. Save.
