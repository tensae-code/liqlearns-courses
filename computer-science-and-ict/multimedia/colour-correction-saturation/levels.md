# Colour correction — Saturation — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do and explain the lesson's one thing — saturation is colour vividness: add a Hue/Saturation adjustment layer and drag the slider up for vivid colours or down for muted ones — all the way down gives a black-and-white image, and too much looks unnatural. Practice deepens with more items from the question files and harder corners of the same skill (new images, new values, the same steps), never with new content the lesson does not teach.
