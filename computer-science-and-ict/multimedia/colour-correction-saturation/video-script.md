Today: **Colour correction — Saturation**.

Remember the one thing: saturation is colour vividness: add a Hue/Saturation adjustment layer and drag the slider up for vivid colours or down for muted ones — all the way down gives a black-and-white image, and too much looks unnatural.

Watch the lesson's pictures. Say each key word after me. Then do the Do It Yourself at the end of the lesson — with your own hands.
