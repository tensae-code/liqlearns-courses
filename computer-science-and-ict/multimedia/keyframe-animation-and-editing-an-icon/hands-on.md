# Hands-on — Keyframe animation — and editing an icon

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Four-frame flip book
On four slips, draw a figure at 0°, 90°, 180°, 270°. Staple one edge and flick: your paper keyframe animation. Then redraw with eight frames and feel the smoothness change.

## Property hunt
On any program you may use, select one object and list every property its panel shows. Sort your list against the lesson's seven: appearance, placement, size, duration, contents, sound, colour.
