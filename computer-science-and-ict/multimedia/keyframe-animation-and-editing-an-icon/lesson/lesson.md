# Keyframe animation — and editing an icon

## Do it first

Draw the same arrow four times on paper, turning it a quarter-turn each time: up, right, down, left. Flick the corners of the pages quickly. Before reading on — you have just built what this lesson builds in Flash.

## Sub lesson 1 — Animating by keyframes

Rotation animation is built **frame by frame**:

1. Click the **first frame** of the layer.
2. **Place the object** (or a clipart image).
3. Move to the **next frame** and **add a keyframe** — Flash duplicates the previous frame's contents.
4. **Turn the object slightly** — 90° to the right in the book's run.
5. **Repeat**: turn it face-down, then to 270° facing left.

Smaller angle steps give smoother fun. Press **Enter** from the first keyframe to play, and **Control → Test Movie (Ctrl+Enter)** previews the test movie.

## Sub lesson 2 — Editing an icon

An **icon** is any graphical, textual, audio or video element met through the GUI. To edit one: **select it**, read its properties in the **Properties panel** — appearance, placement, size, duration, contents, sound, colour — **change** them, then **run or pause** to judge, and **re-edit until satisfied**. Editing is a loop, not a single click: change, watch, change again.

## Say it to your companion

Talk your companion through one full turn: place, keyframe, turn — saying the angle at each keyframe. Then describe the icon-editing loop in one sentence.
