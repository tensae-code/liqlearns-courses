**Acceptance count: taught in 6 countries (Ethiopia, Finland, Mongolia, Armenia, Vietnam, Kazakhstan).**

Nothing in this lesson claims a page the placements do not give; the lesson's content is the placements' content, re-expressed for the learner, with the book's own step orders, examples and practicals kept exactly as printed.

Mongolia's own way (Grade 10): Grade 10 is the "citizen" book — ICT ethics, law and intellectual property first, taught with Mongolian digital content as the examples (the Nogun comic, the Chinggis Khaan animation, GoGo Kids, Maamuu and YoLo; the CRC's black-list.mn), then a distinctive "package information" chapter — collecting, storing, merging and presenting information as one whole, worked through a 24-slide presentation on the sights of Bayankhongor aimag — multimedia production with free tools (Pencil2D, Pivot Animator, Unfreez, Audacity), and a full MS Access database chapter built around a Mongolian phone-book database and the state's unified registration system.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 4 G4 student): Grade 4 (Stepanyan, Amirkhanyan, Matinyan) opens with e-security and health (recognising device malfunctions) and what to tell about yourself online, then purposeful documents (paragraph formats, inserted pictures, tables), slide presentations, e-mail, files, solving technical problems, ways of picturing data, and a Scratch strand of story animation, conditions, loops and variables.

Vietnam's own way (Grade 11 Tin học 11 Tin học ứng dụng): Bài 1–16 are the same common core as the Computer Science orientation book (spot-checked page-identical, e.g. Bài 1); the Applied-only second half is hands-on database administration in Microsoft Access (through backup), then photo editing in GIMP and film-making/animation.

Vietnam's own way (Grade 11 Chuyên đề Tin học 11 Ứng dụng): a media-production volume in three crafts — vector decoration design in Inkscape (a postcard for a national tourism-design contest), cartoon-film making, and animated-GIF editing in a photo editor, layers first.


Kazakhstan's own way (Vyushkova/Zhurbenko/Orazbekov G7 «Информатика» (NIS student book, Russian edition)): the NIS Grade 7 book is organised around data: measuring and representing information (including the von Neumann architecture), business documents, spreadsheets that grow into databases (error checking, sorting/filtering, protecting data from change), raster graphics and logo design, a whole modelling section (modelling games, school life and social problems, forecasting from data), networks and information systems, and linear-structure programming that ends with building your own calculator.
