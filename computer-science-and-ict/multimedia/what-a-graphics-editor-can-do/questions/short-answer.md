# What a Graphics Editor Can Do — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are a graphics editor's three tasks?**
**Answer: Create, edit and prepare pictures**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two editing jobs.**
**Answer: Fix and crop (or recolour, combine)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does preparing a picture include?**
**Answer: Sizing it and saving it in the needed form**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) In the book's order, what comes before the interface?**
**Answer: The tasks and possibilities**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why state the tasks first?**
**Answer: So the pupil knows the job before the tools**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name two possibilities beyond drawing.**
**Answer: Work with photographs; join pieces into one picture (or add text)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where can a finished picture be prepared for?**
**Answer: Print or screen**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When does a possibility count as learned?**
**Answer: When the pupil can say which task it serves and try it**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What can be added to an image?**
**Answer: Text**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this lesson?**
**Answer: Uzbekistan's Grade 9 Basics of Informatics**
