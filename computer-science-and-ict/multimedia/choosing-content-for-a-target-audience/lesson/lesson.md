# Choosing Content for a Target Audience

## Do it first

A birthday message for your grandmother and one for your best friend may carry the same love — and not one same word, picture or song. The difference is not the feeling; it is the audience. Grade 6's digital-gifts project begins here: before any making, name who it is for.

## Sub lesson 1 — Content and its target group

**Digital content** comes in forms — **text, image, video, music** and their mixes (the multimedia companion lesson) — and every piece of content has a **target group**: the people it is made for. A target group is named by real traits: age, language, place, what they know, what they love, what they can use. 'Everyone' is not a target group; a gift 'for everyone' fits no one in particular, and its maker chooses blindly.

## Sub lesson 2 — Planning the gift for the named audience

With the audience named, the **planning** follows: choose the form they receive best (a song for the friend who never reads, large print for the grandfather), the language they dream in, the length their patience holds, and the examples from *their* world, not the maker's. The Grade 6 teams plan a **digital gift** for a named person or group and defend each choice by the audience: not 'I like video' but 'she watches video with her daughter, so video'. The audience is the plan's ruler — every later choice in the making lessons is measured against it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Show it, don't just say it: Text, image, video and music. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
2. Show it, don't just say it: The people a piece of content is made for. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
3. Show it, don't just say it: Age, language and place (also knowledge, loves, abilities). Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
4. Show it, don't just say it: A gift for everyone fits no one in particular. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
