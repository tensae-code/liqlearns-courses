# Hands-on — Choosing Content for a Target Audience

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Name them first** — Choose one message and two real audiences. Write each audience's traits, then the different content plan each one demands.

## Task two
**Defend by the audience** — Plan a digital gift for one named person. List five choices and defend each in one sentence that starts with the audience, not with you.
