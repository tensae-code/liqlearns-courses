# Choosing Content for a Target Audience — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four content forms.**
**Answer: Text, image, video and music**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a target group?**
**Answer: The people a piece of content is made for**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name three traits that name a target group.**
**Answer: Age, language and place (also knowledge, loves, abilities)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is 'everyone' not a target group?**
**Answer: A gift for everyone fits no one in particular**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a maker do without a named audience?**
**Answer: Chooses blindly**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three choices the audience decides.**
**Answer: The form, the language and the length (also the examples)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the Grade 6 product?**
**Answer: A digital gift for a named person or group**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How must each choice be defended?**
**Answer: By the audience — 'she watches video', not 'I like video'**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the audience, in the plan?**
**Answer: The plan's ruler — every choice is measured against it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Whose world should the examples come from?**
**Answer: The audience's, not the maker's**
