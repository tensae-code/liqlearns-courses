# Choosing Content for a Target Audience — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four content forms.** → **Text, image, video and music**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a target group?** → **The people a piece of content is made for**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name three traits that name a target group.** → **Age, language and place (also knowledge, loves, abilities)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is 'everyone' not a target group?** → **A gift for everyone fits no one in particular**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a maker do without a named audience?** → **Chooses blindly**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three choices the audience decides.** → **The form, the language and the length (also the examples)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the Grade 6 product?** → **A digital gift for a named person or group**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How must each choice be defended?** → **By the audience — 'she watches video', not 'I like video'**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the audience, in the plan?** → **The plan's ruler — every choice is measured against it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Whose world should the examples come from?** → **The audience's, not the maker's**
