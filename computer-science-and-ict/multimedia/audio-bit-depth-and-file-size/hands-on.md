# Hands-on — Audio Bit Depth and File Size

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Work the sum', 'A 30-second mono clip is recorded at 22,050 Hz and 8 bits. Work out its size; then work out the same clip at 16 bits and say the difference.')

## Task two
('Hear the depth', 'Record the same spoken sentence at the lowest and highest bit depth your app allows. Compare the sound and the two file sizes.')
