# Audio Bit Depth and File Size — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a sample?**
**Answer: A measurement of the sound wave, taken thousands of times a second**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is bit depth?**
**Answer: How many bits each sample is kept in**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How many levels do 8 bits give?**
**Answer: 256 possible levels for the wave's height**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How many levels do 16 bits give?**
**Answer: 65,536 — CD quality**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does greater bit depth buy?**
**Answer: A more exact trace of the wave — cleaner sound, less hiss**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the uncompressed audio size formula?**
**Answer: Sample rate × bit depth × channels × seconds**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does doubling bit depth do to the file?**
**Answer: Doubles it, for the same recording**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) About how big is one minute of CD audio?**
**Answer: About 10 MB**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is bit depth set, in Malaysia's teaching?**
**Answer: By purpose — low for voice notes, deep for music worth keeping**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Is bit depth an add-on or a multiplier in the sum?**
**Answer: A multiplier**
