# Audio Bit Depth and File Size — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a sample?**
a) A measurement of the sound wave, taken thousands of times a second  b) How many bits each sample is kept in  c) 256 possible levels for the wave's height  d) 65,536 — CD quality
**Answer: a) A measurement of the sound wave, taken thousands of times a second**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is bit depth?**
a) 256 possible levels for the wave's height  b) How many bits each sample is kept in  c) 65,536 — CD quality  d) A more exact trace of the wave — cleaner sound, less hiss
**Answer: b) How many bits each sample is kept in**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How many levels do 8 bits give?**
a) 65,536 — CD quality  b) A more exact trace of the wave — cleaner sound, less hiss  c) 256 possible levels for the wave's height  d) Sample rate × bit depth × channels × seconds
**Answer: c) 256 possible levels for the wave's height**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How many levels do 16 bits give?**
a) A more exact trace of the wave — cleaner sound, less hiss  b) Sample rate × bit depth × channels × seconds  c) Doubles it, for the same recording  d) 65,536 — CD quality
**Answer: d) 65,536 — CD quality**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does greater bit depth buy?**
a) A more exact trace of the wave — cleaner sound, less hiss  b) Sample rate × bit depth × channels × seconds  c) Doubles it, for the same recording  d) About 10 MB
**Answer: a) A more exact trace of the wave — cleaner sound, less hiss**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the uncompressed audio size formula?**
a) Doubles it, for the same recording  b) Sample rate × bit depth × channels × seconds  c) About 10 MB  d) By purpose — low for voice notes, deep for music worth keeping
**Answer: b) Sample rate × bit depth × channels × seconds**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does doubling bit depth do to the file?**
a) About 10 MB  b) By purpose — low for voice notes, deep for music worth keeping  c) Doubles it, for the same recording  d) A multiplier
**Answer: c) Doubles it, for the same recording**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) About how big is one minute of CD audio?**
a) By purpose — low for voice notes, deep for music worth keeping  b) A multiplier  c) A measurement of the sound wave, taken thousands of times a second  d) About 10 MB
**Answer: d) About 10 MB**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is bit depth set, in Malaysia's teaching?**
a) By purpose — low for voice notes, deep for music worth keeping  b) A multiplier  c) A measurement of the sound wave, taken thousands of times a second  d) How many bits each sample is kept in
**Answer: a) By purpose — low for voice notes, deep for music worth keeping**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Is bit depth an add-on or a multiplier in the sum?**
a) A measurement of the sound wave, taken thousands of times a second  b) A multiplier  c) How many bits each sample is kept in  d) 256 possible levels for the wave's height
**Answer: b) A multiplier**
