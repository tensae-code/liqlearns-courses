# Hands-on — The Photoshop window — naming its parts

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Label the window
On a printed or drawn Photoshop window with no labels, write in A–K and the name of each part. Swap with a partner and check each other's against the lesson picture.

## Layer stack with paper
Stack three sheets — one drawing each of a sky, a house, a tree — so the top sheet hides parts of those below. That is the Layer Palette's rule: the top layer shows above those below it. Reorder the sheets and watch the picture change.
