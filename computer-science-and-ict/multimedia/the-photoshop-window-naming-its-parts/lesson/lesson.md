# The Photoshop window — naming its parts

## Do it first

Before reading on, look at the picture of the Photoshop window in this lesson and guess what each lettered part is for — especially the tall strip on the left and the stack on the right. Then read the table and check your guesses.

## Anatomy A–K

The main interface is tabled, A to K:

- **A — Menu Bar** (top): the menus of the whole program.
- **B — Tool Options Bar**: options attached to the selected tool — a brush shows size, softness and more.
- **C — Document Window / tab**: the document's name, its current scaling and its colour space.
- **D — Toolbar / Palette** (left): the main editing tools.
- **E — Document Details**: the compressed and real size of the document.
- **F — Active Image**: the large working area — your canvas.
- **G — Rulers**: they measure position; guidelines are dragged from them.
- **H — Search and Share**.
- **I — Palettes**: interchangeable panels, reached from the Window menu.
- **J — Layer Palette**: the layer stack — the top layer shows above those below it.
- **K — Layer Options**: effects, adjustments and styles for the active layer.
