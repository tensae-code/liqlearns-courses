# The Photoshop window — naming its parts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** What does the Tool Options Bar do?
**Answer:** It shows the options attached to the tool currently selected — for a brush, its size, softness and more.

**2.** Name the parts J and K and say how they differ.
**Answer:** J is the Layer Palette — the stack of layers, top above below. K is Layer Options — effects, adjustments and styles for the active layer.
