# The Photoshop window — naming its parts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** The top layer in the stack shows above the layers below it.
**Answer: True** — the Layer Palette rule.

**2.** The Toolbar is on the right side of the window.
**Answer: False** — the Toolbar/Palette is on the LEFT.

**3.** The Document tab shows the document's name, scaling and colour space.
**Answer: True** — part C.
