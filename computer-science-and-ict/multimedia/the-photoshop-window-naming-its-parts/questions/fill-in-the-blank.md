# The Photoshop window — naming its parts — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words that complete the sentence.

**1.** Part A, at the very top, is the ____ Bar.
**Answer: Menu**

**2.** Effects, adjustments and styles for the active layer are the Layer ____.
**Answer: Options**

**3.** The ____ measure position, and guidelines are dragged from them.
**Answer: Rulers**
