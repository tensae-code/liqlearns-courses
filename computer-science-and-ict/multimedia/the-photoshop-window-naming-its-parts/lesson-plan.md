# Lesson plan — The Photoshop window — naming its parts

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Multimedia · **Lesson:** The Photoshop window — naming its parts
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Multimedia of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 9, Unit 4 §4.3 — Image Processing and Multimedia.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Photoshop's main interface runs A to K — Menu Bar, Tool Options Bar, Document Window, Toolbar, Document Details, Active Image, Rulers, Search and Share, Palettes, Layer Palette and Layer Options.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — what an image is, pixels, and the Photoshop basics as they come; a learner can land here directly and the lesson stands alone.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.
