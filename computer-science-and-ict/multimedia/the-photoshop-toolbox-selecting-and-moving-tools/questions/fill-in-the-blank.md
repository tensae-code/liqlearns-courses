# The Photoshop Toolbox — selecting and moving tools — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words that complete the sentence.

**1.** Freehand selections are made with the ____.
**Answer: Lasso**

**2.** The ____ tools make rectangular and elliptical selections.
**Answer: Marquee**

**3.** The double ____ at the top of the Toolbox switches it between one and two columns.
**Answer: arrow**
