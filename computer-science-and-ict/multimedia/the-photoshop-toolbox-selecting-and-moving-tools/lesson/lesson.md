# The Photoshop Toolbox — selecting and moving tools

## Do it first

Before reading on: in any app you know, how do you choose one part of a picture — a face, a corner, an object — to work on alone? Photoshop's answer is a family of selection tools. Guess which tool you would use for a rectangle, and which for a wavy outline.

## Finding tools — arrows, families, hover help

The left **Toolbox** is single-column by default; the double arrow at its top expands it to two shorter columns and back.

A small arrow at a tool icon's bottom-right — or right-clicking the tool — reveals its **hidden tool family**. Hovering over a tool shows a short description and a usage video.

## The moving and selection family

- **Move** — moves layers, selections and guides; its family includes the Artboard tool.
- **Marquee tools** — Rectangular, Elliptical, Single row and Single column selections.
- **Lasso** — freehand selections; its family holds the Polygonal Lasso and the Magnetic Lasso — three lasso tools in all.
- **Object Selection** — draw a rough selection around an object and the tool finds it; its family includes Quick Selection and the Magic Wand.
