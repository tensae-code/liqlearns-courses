# Hands-on — The Photoshop Toolbox — selecting and moving tools

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Family chart
Draw the Toolbox's selection family as a chart on paper: four group boxes (Move, Marquee, Lasso, Object Selection), with each group's hidden tools written inside.

## Paper marquee and lasso
On a printed picture, draw a rectangle around one object (marquee), an ellipse around another, and a freehand wavy line around a third (lasso). Label which tool each outline belongs to.
