# GIMP — Removing an Image Background — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does removing a background do?**
**Answer: Selects the subject exactly and deletes or masks away everything around it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which tools do the cutting?**
**Answer: Selection tools for clean edges; the Paths tool for edges a marquee cannot follow**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where is a cut judged?**
**Answer: At its boundary — its worst centimetre**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What betrays a cut-out?**
**Answer: A halo of the old background clinging to an edge**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does merging into a new scene mean?**
**Answer: Placing the freed subject, on its layer, over a new background so it belongs there**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What else must agree for the merge?**
**Answer: Scale — and light and ground**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Is the cut-out the finished product?**
**Answer: No — a cut-out that never lands anywhere is only an exercise**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which earlier lesson's craft does merging reuse?**
**Answer: Blending — true size in the scene**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is removing taught after blending?**
**Answer: Pupils who have blended know what a good cut must survive**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 7 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 1 — Removing an Image Background**
