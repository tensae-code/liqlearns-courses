**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is GIMP in the book?**
**Answer: The image manipulation program**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two things done to the image in GIMP.**
**Answer: Cropping and resizing**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What else is adjusted in GIMP?**
**Answer: Brightness and colour**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How does the worked image leave GIMP?**
**Answer: It is exported in the format its use demands**
