# Hands-on — Creating Digital Media — Capture and GIMP

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Capture three', 'Capture one photo three ways — too far, too dark, and right. Write what you changed each time (distance, light, steadiness) and which fault editing could not have fixed.')

## Task two
('Voice note production', 'Record a 20-second audio file with a voice recorder: a title, one fact, a closing. Listen back and log two capture faults (noise, distance) and how a second take fixes them.')
