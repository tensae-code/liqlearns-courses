# Hands-on — Multimedia libraries — create, open, add, use

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Class permanent library
As a class, agree five symbols every project needs (school badge, title banner, three buttons). Draw them, place them in one file, and write the file's name and folder on the board — your permanent library.

## Route cards
Make four cards — Create, Open, Add, Use. A partner names a card; you recite its route. Swap until both are clean.
