# Brightening a dark photo — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do and explain the lesson's one thing — an underexposed indoor photo is brightened by duplicating its layer and applying Image → Adjustments → Exposure to the copy — duplicating again until satisfied (in GIMP: Adjust Colour Levels under Colours → Levels). Practice deepens with more items from the question files and harder corners of the same skill (new images, new values, the same steps), never with new content the lesson does not teach.
