# Hands-on — Brightening a dark photo

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Layer copies with tracing paper
Draw a dark scene on paper. Lay tracing paper (or a clear sheet) over it and trace the scene lighter; add a second traced layer lighter still. Each sheet is a duplicated layer — the dark original underneath never changes.

## Too dark / just right
Take one photo (or describe one) that is underexposed. Write how many Exposure duplications you would try, and how you would know when to stop.
