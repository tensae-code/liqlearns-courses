# Brightening a dark photo — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Give the brightening steps in order.
**Answer:** Open the photo; duplicate the layer (Ctrl+J) and name the copy Layer 1; with Layer 1 selected, Image → Adjustments → Exposure and choose the amount; keep duplicating Layer 1 until satisfied.

**2.** Why duplicate the layer before adjusting?
**Answer:** So the adjustment works on a copy (Layer 1) — the book's method builds the lightening up copy by copy until it is right.
