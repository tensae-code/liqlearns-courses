# Brightening a dark photo — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** The Exposure adjustment is applied to the duplicate layer, not the background.
**Answer: True** — Layer 1 is selected when Exposure is applied.

**2.** One duplication is always enough.
**Answer: False** — keep duplicating until satisfied.

**3.** Underexposed means the photo came out too dark.
**Answer: True** — too little light reached it.
