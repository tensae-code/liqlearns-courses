# Brightening a dark photo — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words that complete the sentence.

**1.** Right-click the layer and choose ____, or press Ctrl+J.
**Answer: Duplicate**

**2.** Apply Image → Adjustments → ____ to lighten the copy.
**Answer: Exposure**

**3.** A dark photo taken without flash is ____.
**Answer: underexposed**
