# Brightening a dark photo

## Do it first

An indoor photo taken without flash comes out dark — faces lost in shadow. Before reading on, say why working on a COPY of the photo's layer is safer than working on the original.

## Adding flash after the fact — the book's order

Indoor photos taken without flash come out **underexposed** and dark. The fix adds flash after the fact:

1. Open the photo.
2. **Layers → right-click the layer → Duplicate** (Ctrl+J), naming the copy **Layer 1**.
3. With Layer 1 selected: **Image → Adjustments → Exposure**, and choose the amount — the whole image lightens.
4. Keep duplicating Layer 1 until you are satisfied.

## The GIMP pointer

In GIMP: use **Adjust Colour Levels**, in the Levels submenu of the Colours menu.

## Say it to your companion

Walk your companion through the brightening steps, and tell them why the work happens on Layer 1, not the background.
