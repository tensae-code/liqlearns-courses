Today: **Brightening a dark photo**.

Remember the one thing: an underexposed indoor photo is brightened by duplicating its layer and applying Image → Adjustments → Exposure to the copy — duplicating again until satisfied (in GIMP: Adjust Colour Levels under Colours → Levels).

Watch the lesson's pictures. Say each key word after me. Then do the Do It Yourself at the end of the lesson — with your own hands.
