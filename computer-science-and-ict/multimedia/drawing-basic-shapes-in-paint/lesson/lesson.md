# Drawing Basic Shapes in Paint

## Do it first

Draw a house freehand. Now draw it again using only ready shapes — a rectangle for the walls, a triangle for the roof, a small rectangle for the door. In this lesson Paint's shapes do the second drawing for you.

## Sub lesson 1 — The shapes tools — line, oval, rectangle and more

Paint carries a **shapes** gallery: ready-made shapes you draw in one drag — a straight **line**, an **oval** (a circle when dragged evenly), a **rectangle**, a triangle and more. Pick the shape, then **drag** across the canvas: the shape grows from where you press to where you let go. A shape drawn this way is exact in a way a freehand drawing is not — straight edges stay straight and corners stay square — which is why Sudan's Grade 5 book gives shapes their own lesson after free drawing in Paint.

## Sub lesson 2 — Colouring a shape — and saving the drawing

A shape is finished with colour: an **outline** colour for its edge and a **fill** colour for its inside, both picked before or after drawing. Then the drawing is **saved** — given a name and kept as a file, like every file in this course — so it can be opened again, printed or put into a document. Draw, colour, save: the order of this lesson is the order of every Paint job after it.
