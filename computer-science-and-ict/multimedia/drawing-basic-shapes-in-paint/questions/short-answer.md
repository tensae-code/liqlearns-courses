# Drawing Basic Shapes in Paint — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where are Paint's ready shapes found?**
**Answer: In the shapes gallery**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three basic shapes Paint can draw.**
**Answer: A line, an oval and a rectangle**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a shape drawn?**
**Answer: Pick the shape, then drag across the canvas from press to release**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why draw with shapes instead of freehand?**
**Answer: The shape is exact — straight edges stay straight and corners stay square**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When does an oval become a circle?**
**Answer: When it is dragged evenly**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the outline of a shape?**
**Answer: The colour of its edge**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the fill of a shape?**
**Answer: The colour of its inside**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the order of a Paint job in this lesson?**
**Answer: Draw, colour, save**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why save the drawing?**
**Answer: So it can be opened again, printed or put into a document**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the saved drawing become?**
**Answer: A file with a name, kept like every other file**
