# Drawing Basic Shapes in Paint — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where are Paint's ready shapes found?**
a) In the shapes gallery  b) A line, an oval and a rectangle  c) The shape is exact — straight edges stay straight and corners stay square  d) The colour of its edge
**Answer: a) In the shapes gallery**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three basic shapes Paint can draw.**
a) Pick the shape, then drag across the canvas from press to release  b) A line, an oval and a rectangle  c) When it is dragged evenly  d) The colour of its inside
**Answer: b) A line, an oval and a rectangle**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a shape drawn?**
a) The shape is exact — straight edges stay straight and corners stay square  b) The colour of its edge  c) Pick the shape, then drag across the canvas from press to release  d) Draw, colour, save
**Answer: c) Pick the shape, then drag across the canvas from press to release**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why draw with shapes instead of freehand?**
a) When it is dragged evenly  b) The colour of its inside  c) So it can be opened again, printed or put into a document  d) The shape is exact — straight edges stay straight and corners stay square
**Answer: d) The shape is exact — straight edges stay straight and corners stay square**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When does an oval become a circle?**
a) When it is dragged evenly  b) The colour of its edge  c) Draw, colour, save  d) A file with a name, kept like every other file
**Answer: a) When it is dragged evenly**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the outline of a shape?**
a) The colour of its inside  b) The colour of its edge  c) So it can be opened again, printed or put into a document  d) In the shapes gallery
**Answer: b) The colour of its edge**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the fill of a shape?**
a) Draw, colour, save  b) A file with a name, kept like every other file  c) The colour of its inside  d) A line, an oval and a rectangle
**Answer: c) The colour of its inside**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the order of a Paint job in this lesson?**
a) So it can be opened again, printed or put into a document  b) In the shapes gallery  c) Pick the shape, then drag across the canvas from press to release  d) Draw, colour, save
**Answer: d) Draw, colour, save**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why save the drawing?**
a) So it can be opened again, printed or put into a document  b) A file with a name, kept like every other file  c) A line, an oval and a rectangle  d) The shape is exact — straight edges stay straight and corners stay square
**Answer: a) So it can be opened again, printed or put into a document**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the saved drawing become?**
a) In the shapes gallery  b) A file with a name, kept like every other file  c) Pick the shape, then drag across the canvas from press to release  d) When it is dragged evenly
**Answer: b) A file with a name, kept like every other file**
