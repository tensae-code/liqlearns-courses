# Making a Digital Product With Graphics Software — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a product different from a practice picture?**
**Answer: It is finished, and it has a purpose and a person it is for**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three things are planned first?**
**Answer: What the product says, who will see it, and a sketch of its layout**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are example products in track A?**
**Answer: A greeting card, a poster for the class wall**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How are the software's tools chosen?**
**Answer: To serve the plan — shapes, colours, text and pictures picked for the purpose**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In which software is the product made?**
**Answer: Graphics software — the graphics program met in the track's first lesson**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do as you build?**
**Answer: Save as you go**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What three questions check the whole product?**
**Answer: Is the main thing biggest, can the words be read, do the colours fight?**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is fixed first?**
**Answer: The worst fault**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is a product done?**
**Answer: When its person can receive it — saved in final form and presented**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the sketch do during production?**
**Answer: It guides the building, piece by piece**
