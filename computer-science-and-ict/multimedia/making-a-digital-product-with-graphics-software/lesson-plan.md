**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Vietnam (2026-11-14) — in the title Multimedia on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). **The one thing this lesson teaches:** making a digital product with graphics software — planning and producing a finished digital product (a card, a poster, a picture with a purpose) in a graphics program (Vietnam Grade 5, track A).
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **A product has a purpose — plan before you draw** — Track A of Vietnam's Grade 5 applied theme turns graphics software to a **product**: not a practice picture, but a finished thing with a **purpose and a person it is for** — a greeting card for a grandmother, a poster for the class wall. Planning comes first: **what** the product says, **who** will see it, and a rough **sketch** of its layout. The software's tools — shapes, colours, text, pictures — are then chosen to serve the plan, not wandered through.
2. **Make it, look at it, finish it** — Production follows the sketch: build the product piece by piece, **save as you go**, then stop and **look at the whole** — is the main thing biggest, can the words be read, do the colours fight? Fix the worst fault, then finish: save the product in its final form and **present it** — a product is not done when the maker stops, but when its person can receive it.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
