# Hands-on — Making a Digital Product With Graphics Software

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Plan a product** — Pick a real person and purpose. Sketch your product's layout on paper and label what each part says.

## Task two
**Make and present it** — Build the product in graphics software from your sketch. Check it with the three questions, fix the worst fault, save it finally, and present it to its person.
