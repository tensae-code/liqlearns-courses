# Multimedia and hypermedia — linear vs linked — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** Hypermedia material is linked together by a hypertext program.
**Answer: True** — as defined.

**2.** Computer animation is the digital successor to stop-motion and frame-by-frame 2D illustration.
**Answer: True** — the summary's line of descent.

**3.** A film watched start to finish is the best example of hypermedia.
**Answer: False** — that straight line is multimedia-shaped; the Web is the hypermedia example.
