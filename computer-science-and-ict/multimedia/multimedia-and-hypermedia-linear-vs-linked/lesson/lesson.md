## Multimedia and hypermedia — linear vs linked

**Multimedia** is the broad integration of **text, pictures, graphics, audio, animation and video**, processed digitally through computer software and hardware.

**Hypermedia** is its more diverse, **non-linear extension**: multimedia material **linked together by a hypertext program**, so the user moves **non-linearly** through it. The **World Wide Web** is the best example — no reader walks a website from first page to last; each follows the links that serve their purpose.
