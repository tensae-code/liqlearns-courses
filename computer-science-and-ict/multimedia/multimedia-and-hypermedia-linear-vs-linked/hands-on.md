# Hands-on — Multimedia and hypermedia — linear vs linked

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Draw both journeys
Draw one straight-line journey (a film of your school day) and one linked web (a website of your school) on facing pages. Label three links in the web and say where each takes the visitor.

## Link hunt
In any app or site you may use, follow three links from one starting page and map the web you walked. Compare maps with a partner: no two webs alike — that is non-linear.
