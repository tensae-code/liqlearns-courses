# Effects for Animated Images — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does an animated effect differ from a still one?** → **It changes the picture gradually, frame by frame**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a fade?** → **Opacity raised or lowered step by step, so a thing appears or vanishes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What two states are chosen first?** → **The first state and the last state of the effect**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How are the in-between frames built?** → **In equal steps between the first and last states**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the eye read the steps as?** → **One smooth change**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What sets an effect's timing?** → **The frame delays — many frames for a slow fade, few for a flash**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What purpose must an effect have?** → **A story purpose — marking the magic object, ending the scene**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why do five competing effects fail?** → **The eye stops believing any of them**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What beats five effects?** → **One effect, well placed**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a flash get?** → **Few frames — it is over quickly**
