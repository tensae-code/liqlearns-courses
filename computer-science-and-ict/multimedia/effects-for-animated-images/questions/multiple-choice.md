# Effects for Animated Images — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does an animated effect differ from a still one?**
a) It changes the picture gradually, frame by frame  b) Opacity raised or lowered step by step, so a thing appears or vanishes  c) The first state and the last state of the effect  d) In equal steps between the first and last states
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a fade?**
a) Opacity raised or lowered step by step, so a thing appears or vanishes  b) The first state and the last state of the effect  c) In equal steps between the first and last states  d) One smooth change
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What two states are chosen first?**
a) The first state and the last state of the effect  b) In equal steps between the first and last states  c) One smooth change  d) The frame delays — many frames for a slow fade, few for a flash
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How are the in-between frames built?**
a) In equal steps between the first and last states  b) One smooth change  c) The frame delays — many frames for a slow fade, few for a flash  d) A story purpose — marking the magic object, ending the scene
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the eye read the steps as?**
a) One smooth change  b) The frame delays — many frames for a slow fade, few for a flash  c) A story purpose — marking the magic object, ending the scene  d) The eye stops believing any of them
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What sets an effect's timing?**
a) The frame delays — many frames for a slow fade, few for a flash  b) A story purpose — marking the magic object, ending the scene  c) The eye stops believing any of them  d) One effect, well placed
**Answer: a**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What purpose must an effect have?**
a) A story purpose — marking the magic object, ending the scene  b) The eye stops believing any of them  c) One effect, well placed  d) Few frames — it is over quickly
**Answer: a**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why do five competing effects fail?**
a) The eye stops believing any of them  b) One effect, well placed  c) Few frames — it is over quickly  d) It changes the picture gradually, frame by frame
**Answer: a**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What beats five effects?**
a) One effect, well placed  b) Few frames — it is over quickly  c) It changes the picture gradually, frame by frame  d) Opacity raised or lowered step by step, so a thing appears or vanishes
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a flash get?**
a) Few frames — it is over quickly  b) It changes the picture gradually, frame by frame  c) Opacity raised or lowered step by step, so a thing appears or vanishes  d) The first state and the last state of the effect
**Answer: a**
