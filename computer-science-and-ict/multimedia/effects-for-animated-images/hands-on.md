# Hands-on — Effects for Animated Images

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Fade it** — Take a short GIF you have made. Add a fade-out over its last five frames, in equal opacity steps.

## Task two
**One effect only** — Make a tiny animation with exactly one effect — a glow on the object that matters. Show it and say what the effect tells the audience.
