# Recording and Playing Audio on a Smartphone or Tablet

## Do it first

The phone in your hand is also a sound machine. Grade 1's sound séance meets it properly: where its sound comes out, how it records your voice, and how it plays a sound back.

## Sub lesson 1 — The sound of the device — speaker and microphone

A smartphone or tablet **plays sound through its speaker** and **takes sound in through its microphone** — the same two directions of sound the class met on the computer, in one small device. Sounds the device plays include **music, voices in a video, and the sounds of its apps**. Before recording anything, the learner finds both parts: the speaker, where sound comes out, and the microphone, where the voice goes in — and learns to keep the microphone uncovered while recording.

## Sub lesson 2 — Recording your voice — and playing it back

With the device's **recording app**, the learner **records a short sound — their voice saying their name or a short sentence** — then **plays it back** and listens: the recording is kept on the device as a sound that can be played again. The round trip is the lesson: sound goes in through the microphone, is kept, and comes back out through the speaker when played. If the playback cannot be heard, the checks are the lesson's own: was the microphone covered, and is the volume up?

## Do It Yourself

1. Show it, don't just say it: Speaker out, microphone in. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
2. Show it, don't just say it: Record with the recording app. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
3. Show it, don't just say it: The recording is kept and replays. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
4. Show it, don't just say it: Check the microphone and the volume. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
