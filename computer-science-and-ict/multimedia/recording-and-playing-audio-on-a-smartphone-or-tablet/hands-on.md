# Hands-on — Recording and Playing Audio on a Smartphone or Tablet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Record and play back
On a smartphone or tablet, record yourself saying your name and your class. Play it back. Say which part of the device the sound went into, and which part it came out of.

## Find the sound parts
On a drawing of a phone, mark the speaker and the microphone. Under each, write one line: what goes through it, and in which direction.
