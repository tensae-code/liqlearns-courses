# Recording and Playing Audio on a Smartphone or Tablet — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a phone's sound come out?**
**Answer: Through its speaker**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does your voice go in when you record?**
**Answer: Through its microphone**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name three sounds a phone or tablet plays.**
**Answer: Music, voices in a video, and app sounds**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must you keep uncovered while recording?**
**Answer: The microphone**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is the phone like the computer in this lesson?**
**Answer: It has the same two directions of sound — in and out**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do you use to record on the phone or tablet?**
**Answer: The device's recording app**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What short sound does the learner record?**
**Answer: Their voice — their name or a short sentence**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to the recording after it is made?**
**Answer: It is kept on the device and can be played again**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are the two checks if playback cannot be heard?**
**Answer: The microphone was covered, or the volume is down**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the round trip of a recording?**
**Answer: In through the microphone, kept, out through the speaker**
