# Recording and Playing Audio on a Smartphone or Tablet — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does a phone's sound come out?" is: Through its speaker**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does your voice go in when you record?" is: The device's recording app**
**Answer: False** — Through its microphone

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name three sounds a phone or tablet plays.?" is: Music, voices in a video, and app sounds**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What must you keep uncovered while recording?" is: It is kept on the device and can be played again**
**Answer: False** — The microphone

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is the phone like the computer in this lesson?" is: It has the same two directions of sound — in and out**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do you use to record on the phone or tablet?" is: In through the microphone, kept, out through the speaker**
**Answer: False** — The device's recording app

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What short sound does the learner record?" is: Their voice — their name or a short sentence**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens to the recording after it is made?" is: Through its microphone**
**Answer: False** — It is kept on the device and can be played again

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What are the two checks if playback cannot be heard?" is: The microphone was covered, or the volume is down**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the round trip of a recording?" is: The microphone**
**Answer: False** — In through the microphone, kept, out through the speaker
