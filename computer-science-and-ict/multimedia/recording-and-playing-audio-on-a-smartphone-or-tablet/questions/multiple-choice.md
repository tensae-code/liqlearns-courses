# Recording and Playing Audio on a Smartphone or Tablet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a phone's sound come out?**
a) Through its speaker  b) Through its microphone  c) Music, voices in a video, and app sounds  d) The microphone
**Answer: a) Through its speaker**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does your voice go in when you record?**
a) Music, voices in a video, and app sounds  b) Through its microphone  c) The microphone  d) It has the same two directions of sound — in and out
**Answer: b) Through its microphone**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name three sounds a phone or tablet plays.**
a) The microphone  b) It has the same two directions of sound — in and out  c) Music, voices in a video, and app sounds  d) The device's recording app
**Answer: c) Music, voices in a video, and app sounds**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must you keep uncovered while recording?**
a) It has the same two directions of sound — in and out  b) The device's recording app  c) Their voice — their name or a short sentence  d) The microphone
**Answer: d) The microphone**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is the phone like the computer in this lesson?**
a) It has the same two directions of sound — in and out  b) The device's recording app  c) Their voice — their name or a short sentence  d) It is kept on the device and can be played again
**Answer: a) It has the same two directions of sound — in and out**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do you use to record on the phone or tablet?**
a) Their voice — their name or a short sentence  b) The device's recording app  c) It is kept on the device and can be played again  d) The microphone was covered, or the volume is down
**Answer: b) The device's recording app**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What short sound does the learner record?**
a) It is kept on the device and can be played again  b) The microphone was covered, or the volume is down  c) Their voice — their name or a short sentence  d) In through the microphone, kept, out through the speaker
**Answer: c) Their voice — their name or a short sentence**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to the recording after it is made?**
a) The microphone was covered, or the volume is down  b) In through the microphone, kept, out through the speaker  c) Through its speaker  d) It is kept on the device and can be played again
**Answer: d) It is kept on the device and can be played again**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are the two checks if playback cannot be heard?**
a) The microphone was covered, or the volume is down  b) In through the microphone, kept, out through the speaker  c) Through its speaker  d) Through its microphone
**Answer: a) The microphone was covered, or the volume is down**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the round trip of a recording?**
a) Through its speaker  b) In through the microphone, kept, out through the speaker  c) Through its microphone  d) Music, voices in a video, and app sounds
**Answer: b) In through the microphone, kept, out through the speaker**
