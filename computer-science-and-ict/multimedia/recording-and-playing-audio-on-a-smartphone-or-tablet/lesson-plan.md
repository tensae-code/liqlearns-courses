# Lesson plan — Recording and Playing Audio on a Smartphone or Tablet

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Multimedia · **Lesson:** Recording and Playing Audio on a Smartphone or Tablet
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Morocco (2026-11-14) — in the title Multimedia on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Morocco's Grade 1 “J'excelle en informatique” teacher guide, because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson. This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only (Morocco), named in lesson/sources.md, counted nowhere.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A smartphone or tablet plays sound through its speaker and takes sound in through its microphone — the same two directions of sound the class met on the computer, in one small device. With the device's recording app, the learner records a short sound — their voice saying their name or a short sentence — then plays it back and listens: the recording is kept on the device as a sound that can be played again.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The sound of the device — speaker and microphone** — A smartphone or tablet plays sound through its speaker and takes sound in through its microphone — the same two directions of sound the class met on the computer, in one small device.
2. **Recording your voice — and playing it back** — With the device's recording app, the learner records a short sound — their voice saying their name or a short sentence — then plays it back and listens: the recording is kept on the device as a sound that can be played again.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Speaker out, microphone in.
2. Record with the recording app.
3. The recording is kept and replays.
4. Check the microphone and the volume.
