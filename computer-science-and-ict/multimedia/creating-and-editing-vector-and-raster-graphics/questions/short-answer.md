# Creating and Editing Vector and Raster Graphics — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How are vector graphics created?**
**Answer: Drawing with shapes, lines and paths — each object named and kept**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What can be done to a vector object?**
**Answer: Selected, moved, reshaped, recoloured and scaled without loss**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is vector editing?**
**Answer: Re-deciding the objects' descriptions — drag a point, change a fill, group objects**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Vector is the graphics of what?**
**Answer: Logos, diagrams and type**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 11 EPAL Basika Themata, Chapter 4**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How do raster graphics arrive?**
**Answer: By capture — photograph, scan, screenshot**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name three raster edits.**
**Answer: Crop, correct brightness/contrast/colour, retouch (any three)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is resizing's one-way trade?**
**Answer: Fewer pixels, smaller file, less detail — detail cannot be restored**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When draw vector?**
**Answer: When it must scale and stay sharp**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When capture raster?**
**Answer: When it must show the real world**
