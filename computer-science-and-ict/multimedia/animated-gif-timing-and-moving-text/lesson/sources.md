Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked for the slug and the live lesson content was checked for near matches before building. The live keyframe-animation lesson teaches animating by keyframes and editing an icon; frame-delay timing of a GIF and animated text are taught by no live lesson — they are the craft of Vietnam's Grade 11 specialist GIF lessons. The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — multimedia/animated-gif-timing-and-moving-text). It sits in the Multimedia title (multimedia/), following the book's own order. Provenance scope note: the placements stocked here are from Vietnam's “Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống” (Connecting Knowledge with Life) Tin học books (Vietnamese) — one series, Grades 3–12, from Nhà xuất bản Giáo dục Việt Nam, splitting from Grade 10 into Computer Science (Khoa học máy tính) and Applied Informatics (Tin học ứng dụng) orientations, each with its own specialist-topic (Chuyên đề) volumes; the books are image-only PDFs read from rendered pages. Every placement stocked here is from a student book and counts toward the acceptance count; there are no teacher guides in this country. Media slots in lesson/lesson.md are described slots only ([Picture — …]); no picture numbers are invented.

Vietnam's own way (Grade 11 Chuyên đề Tin học 11 Ứng dụng): a media-production volume in three crafts — vector decoration design in Inkscape (a postcard for a national tourism-design contest), cartoon-film making, and animated-GIF editing in a photo editor, layers first.


Kazakhstan's own way (Vyshkova & Zhurbenko G7 Informatics, NIS): the Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools course is a different programme from the national one: business documents, databases inside the information-processing part, logo design and GIF animation in raster graphics, a whole chapter on modelling the world (including forecasting social problems from data), client–server networks, utilities, and linear-structure programming ending in building your own calculator. Lessons run two pages each with research-style tasks.

Kazakhstan's own way (Vyushkova/Zhurbenko/Orazbekov G7 «Информатика» (NIS student book, Russian edition)): the NIS Grade 7 book is organised around data: measuring and representing information (including the von Neumann architecture), business documents, spreadsheets that grow into databases (error checking, sorting/filtering, protecting data from change), raster graphics and logo design, a whole modelling section (modelling games, school life and social problems, forecasting from data), networks and information systems, and linear-structure programming that ends with building your own calculator.
