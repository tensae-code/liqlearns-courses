# Hands-on — Animated GIFs — Timing and Moving Text

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Retiming** — Take or make a short GIF. Set all delays equal, watch it; then give one frame a long hold and watch again. Say what the hold does.

## Task two
**Headline in motion** — Make a GIF where a one-word headline glides in from the left and then holds. Set the delays so the word can actually be read.
