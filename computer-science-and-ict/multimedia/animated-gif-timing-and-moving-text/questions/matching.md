# Animated GIFs — Timing and Moving Text — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an animated GIF, mechanically?** → **A row of still frames shown one after another**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What sets the speed of the motion?** → **Each frame's delay — the time that frame stays on screen**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do short delays make?** → **Fast, smooth motion**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why give one frame a long delay?** → **To hold the picture — letting the eye rest at a chosen moment**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is timing judged?** → **By watching the preview, then re-setting — not by numbers alone**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is moving text made?** → **The text layer is copied across frames, shifted a little in each frame**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What makes motion look smooth?** → **Small, equal steps between frames**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why do text frames get longer delays?** → **So the words stay on screen long enough to be read**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what directions can text travel?** → **Entering from an edge, crossing the picture, or rising — any steady path**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must the text's motion serve?** → **The message — motion that fights the message is cut**
