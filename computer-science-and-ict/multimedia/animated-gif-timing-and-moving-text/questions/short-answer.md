# Animated GIFs — Timing and Moving Text — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an animated GIF, mechanically?**
**Answer: A row of still frames shown one after another**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What sets the speed of the motion?**
**Answer: Each frame's delay — the time that frame stays on screen**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do short delays make?**
**Answer: Fast, smooth motion**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why give one frame a long delay?**
**Answer: To hold the picture — letting the eye rest at a chosen moment**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is timing judged?**
**Answer: By watching the preview, then re-setting — not by numbers alone**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is moving text made?**
**Answer: The text layer is copied across frames, shifted a little in each frame**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What makes motion look smooth?**
**Answer: Small, equal steps between frames**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why do text frames get longer delays?**
**Answer: So the words stay on screen long enough to be read**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what directions can text travel?**
**Answer: Entering from an edge, crossing the picture, or rising — any steady path**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must the text's motion serve?**
**Answer: The message — motion that fights the message is cut**
