# Animated GIFs — Timing and Moving Text — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an animated GIF, mechanically?**
a) A row of still frames shown one after another  b) Each frame's delay — the time that frame stays on screen  c) Fast, smooth motion  d) To hold the picture — letting the eye rest at a chosen moment
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What sets the speed of the motion?**
a) Each frame's delay — the time that frame stays on screen  b) Fast, smooth motion  c) To hold the picture — letting the eye rest at a chosen moment  d) By watching the preview, then re-setting — not by numbers alone
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do short delays make?**
a) Fast, smooth motion  b) To hold the picture — letting the eye rest at a chosen moment  c) By watching the preview, then re-setting — not by numbers alone  d) The text layer is copied across frames, shifted a little in each frame
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why give one frame a long delay?**
a) To hold the picture — letting the eye rest at a chosen moment  b) By watching the preview, then re-setting — not by numbers alone  c) The text layer is copied across frames, shifted a little in each frame  d) Small, equal steps between frames
