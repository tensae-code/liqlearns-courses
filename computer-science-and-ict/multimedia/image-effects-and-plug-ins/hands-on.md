# Hands-on — Image Effects and Plug-Ins

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Extend the editor', 'With your teacher, install one approved Paint.NET plug-in. Find its effect in the menu and apply it to a layer copy of a photo. Record the before and after.')

## Task two
('Make 3D text', 'Build a title in 3D text using the plug-in effects: depth, one light direction, one shadow. Place it over a photograph and judge it as a poster.')
