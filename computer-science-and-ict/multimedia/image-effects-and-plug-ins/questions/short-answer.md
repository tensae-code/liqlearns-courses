# Image Effects and Plug-Ins — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) On what does Grade 9 apply the Effects menu?**
**Answer: Ready photographs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is an effect on a photograph, ethically?**
**Answer: A claim about it — an undeclared effect on a documentary photo crosses the ethics line**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where are effects placed?**
**Answer: On their own layer, chosen and owned**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a plug-in?**
**Answer: An added module that gives the editor new powers**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a plug-in installed?**
**Answer: Its file is placed where the program reads extensions, and the program is restarted**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How do a plug-in's effects appear?**
**Answer: They join the menu as citizens**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's set project with plug-ins?**
**Answer: 3D text — letters given depth, light and shadow**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is an editor, in the lesson's closing phrase?**
**Answer: Not a fixed shelf but a growing bench**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who is responsible for the bench's tools?**
**Answer: The pupil who installed them**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which editor is extended this way?**
**Answer: Paint.NET**
