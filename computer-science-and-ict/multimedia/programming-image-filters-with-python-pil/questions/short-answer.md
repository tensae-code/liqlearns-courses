**1. (Sub lesson 2) Why must a filter be applied by a program rather than by hand for a real phone photo?**
**Answer: A phone photo holds millions of pixels — twelve million is ordinary — and the rule must be applied to every single one identically; by hand that would take years, while the program’s two loops do it in seconds without mistakes.**
