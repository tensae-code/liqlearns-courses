# Graphics Software and Its Uses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does graphics software create and edit?**
a) Images on a computer  b) Bitmaps — the picture as a grid of coloured dots  c) Vectors — shapes described by lines and curves  d) Painting/bitmap programs

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do painting programs work on?**
a) Vectors — shapes described by lines and curves  b) Bitmaps — the picture as a grid of coloured dots  c) Painting/bitmap programs  d) Drawing/vector programs

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do drawing programs work on?**
a) Painting/bitmap programs  b) Drawing/vector programs  c) Vectors — shapes described by lines and curves  d) Editing photographs, drawing illustrations, and designing posters and publications

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which kind suits photographs?**
