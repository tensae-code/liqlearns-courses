# Graphics Software and Its Uses — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does graphics software create and edit?**
**Answer: Images on a computer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do painting programs work on?**
**Answer: Bitmaps — the picture as a grid of coloured dots**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do drawing programs work on?**
**Answer: Vectors — shapes described by lines and curves**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which kind suits photographs?**
**Answer: Painting/bitmap programs**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which kind suits logos that must scale?**
**Answer: Drawing/vector programs**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three uses of graphics software.**
**Answer: Editing photographs, drawing illustrations, and designing posters and publications**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is CAD used for?**
**Answer: Technical drawing — buildings and parts that must be exact**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must be ready before web and print?**
**Answer: Images prepared for the web and for print**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the guide give graphics two activities?**
**Answer: Its product can be seen, judged and shown — proof of skill needing no translation**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which source teaches this lesson?**
