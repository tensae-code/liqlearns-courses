# Layers and Colour Channels in Image Editing — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Layers — the stack of transparent sheets
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A transparent sheet holding part of the picture, in a stack; The stack seen through, top to bottom; Shown or hidden, reordered, made partly transparent (also: edited, deleted); A layer's partial transparency — how much of what is below shows through; A mistake lives on one layer and dies with it, not with the whole picture.

## Sub lesson 2 — Colour channels — the image in its components
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: One colour component of the image kept separately as a grayscale map; Red, green and blue; As their sum — each pixel's colour from its three channel values; How much red each pixel carries — bright where the picture is reddish; Layers divide by place; channels divide by colour.
