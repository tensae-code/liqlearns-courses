# Hands-on — Layers and Colour Channels in Image Editing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Stack plan** — Plan a birthday card as a layer stack of five named layers in order, with one layer at reduced opacity and the reason why.

## Task two
**Channel reading** — Take a photo description (red car, green field, blue sky) and sketch which channel is bright/dark in each region.
