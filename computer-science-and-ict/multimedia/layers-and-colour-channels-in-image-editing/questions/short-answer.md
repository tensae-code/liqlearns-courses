# Layers and Colour Channels in Image Editing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a layer?**
**Answer: A transparent sheet holding part of the picture, in a stack**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is the visible image formed?**
**Answer: The stack seen through, top to bottom**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name three things that can be done to a layer alone.**
**Answer: Shown or hidden, reordered, made partly transparent (also: edited, deleted)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is opacity?**
**Answer: A layer's partial transparency — how much of what is below shows through**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why are layers the editor's safety?**
**Answer: A mistake lives on one layer and dies with it, not with the whole picture**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a colour channel?**
**Answer: One colour component of the image kept separately as a grayscale map**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which channels has the RGB model?**
**Answer: Red, green and blue**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is the colour picture formed from channels?**
**Answer: As their sum — each pixel's colour from its three channel values**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the red channel show?**
**Answer: How much red each pixel carries — bright where the picture is reddish**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How do layers and channels differ as cuts?**
**Answer: Layers divide by place; channels divide by colour**
