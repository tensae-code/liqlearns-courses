# Layers and Colour Channels in Image Editing — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A transparent sheet holding part of the picture, in a stack**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A transparent sheet holding part of the picture, in a stack** — as the answer to: How is the visible image formed?
**Answer: False — The stack seen through, top to bottom**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Shown or hidden, reordered, made partly transparent (also: edited, deleted)**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Shown or hidden, reordered, made partly transparent (also: edited, deleted)** — as the answer to: What is opacity?
**Answer: False — A layer's partial transparency — how much of what is below shows through**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A mistake lives on one layer and dies with it, not with the whole picture**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A mistake lives on one layer and dies with it, not with the whole picture** — as the answer to: What is a colour channel?
**Answer: False — One colour component of the image kept separately as a grayscale map**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Red, green and blue**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Red, green and blue** — as the answer to: How is the colour picture formed from channels?
**Answer: False — As their sum — each pixel's colour from its three channel values**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: How much red each pixel carries — bright where the picture is reddish**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: How much red each pixel carries — bright where the picture is reddish** — as the answer to: How do layers and channels differ as cuts?
**Answer: False — Layers divide by place; channels divide by colour**
