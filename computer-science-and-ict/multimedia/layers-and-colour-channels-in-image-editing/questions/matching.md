# Layers and Colour Channels in Image Editing — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a layer?** → **A transparent sheet holding part of the picture, in a stack**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is the visible image formed?** → **The stack seen through, top to bottom**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name three things that can be done to a layer alone.** → **Shown or hidden, reordered, made partly transparent (also: edited, deleted)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is opacity?** → **A layer's partial transparency — how much of what is below shows through**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why are layers the editor's safety?** → **A mistake lives on one layer and dies with it, not with the whole picture**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a colour channel?** → **One colour component of the image kept separately as a grayscale map**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which channels has the RGB model?** → **Red, green and blue**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is the colour picture formed from channels?** → **As their sum — each pixel's colour from its three channel values**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the red channel show?** → **How much red each pixel carries — bright where the picture is reddish**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How do layers and channels differ as cuts?** → **Layers divide by place; channels divide by colour**
