# Sprites and Costumes in Scratch — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. Say what a sprite is and what a costume is.**
**Answer: True** — This is one of the lesson's own outcomes.

**2. A sprite can have only one costume, so a new look always needs a new sprite.**
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite of this.

**3. Add a costume to a sprite and switch to it.**
**Answer: True** — This is one of the lesson's own outcomes.
