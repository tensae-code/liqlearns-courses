# Sprites and Costumes in Scratch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A costume is —**
a) one of a sprite's looks  b) a second stage  c) a sound file
**Answer: a) one of a sprite's looks**

**2. How many costumes can one sprite hold?**
a) Only one, ever  b) None  c) Several
**Answer: c) Several**

**3. Switching costumes fast, one after another, is the seed of —**
a) saving  b) animation  c) printing
**Answer: b) animation**

**4. Costumes belong to —**
a) one sprite each  b) the stage  c) the backdrop library only
**Answer: a) one sprite each**
