# Sprites and Costumes in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Explain, in your own words: say what a sprite is and what a costume is.**
**Answer:** The learner's own explanation, true to the lesson: A sprite is a character or object in Scratch, and a costume is one of its looks: one sprite can hold many costumes and change its look by switching costume.

**2. A classmate gets this lesson's work wrong. What is the most likely fault, and how is it fixed?**
**Answer:** Sprite will not change look → check you are switching the costume of the right sprite: costumes belong to one sprite each, and the stage cannot wear them.
