**1. (Sub lesson 1) A friend describes a colour as "a pale, light blue". Translate that description into HSL terms.**
**Answer: The hue is blue; the saturation is low (pale means much of the colour is faded towards grey); the lightness is high (light means near the white end of the scale).**
