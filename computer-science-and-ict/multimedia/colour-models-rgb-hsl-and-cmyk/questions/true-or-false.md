# Colour Models — RGB, HSL and CMYK — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In RGB, the colour white is all three channels at zero.**
**Answer: False — Zero is black; white is all three channels at full (255, 255, 255).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A vivid neon green on a screen may print duller because CMYK cannot reach every colour RGB can.**
**Answer: True — Light and ink build colour by opposite roads and reach different sets of colours.**
