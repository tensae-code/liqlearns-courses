# Colour Models — RGB, HSL and CMYK — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) HSL names a colour by hue, saturation and ____.**
**Answer: lightness — Lightness places the colour between black and white.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The three printing inks CMY are cyan, magenta and ____.**
**Answer: yellow — Together with black (K) they form the CMYK model.**
