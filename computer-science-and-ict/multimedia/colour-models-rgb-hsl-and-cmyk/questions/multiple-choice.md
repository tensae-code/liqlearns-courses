# Colour Models — RGB, HSL and CMYK — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In HSL, what does saturation describe?**
a) How light or dark the colour is  b) How pure or grey the colour is  c) Which lamp is brightest  d) How large the image is
**Answer: b) How pure or grey the colour is — Lowering saturation fades every hue towards grey.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Red and green light at full strength, added together, make —**
a) Blue  b) White  c) Yellow  d) Black
**Answer: c) Yellow — Screens add light: red + green = yellow, and all three at full make white.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Why do printers carry a separate black ink (K)?**
a) For decoration  b) Because cyan, magenta and yellow together give a muddy, costly black  c) Because black paper is used  d) To print faster text only
**Answer: b) Because cyan, magenta and yellow together give a muddy, costly black — A true black ink is sharper and cheaper.**
