# Hands-on — Colour Models — RGB, HSL and CMYK

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say one colour three ways
Pick three objects in the room with strong colours. For each, write its description in words (hue, vivid or pale, light or dark), then estimate its HSL numbers, then guess which RGB lamps dominate. If a colour picker is available, check one object’s colour from a photo of it and compare with your estimates.

## The print test
If a printer is available, print a small image with a very bright green or blue in it. Hold the print beside the screen. Write down which colours changed and explain, in CMYK terms, why those were the ones.
