# Video script — Colour Models — RGB, HSL and CMYK

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A screen makes colour with light. Red, green and blue. Add them together: full red and green make yellow. All three at full make white. That is RGB.

But people do not describe colours that way. We say: it is orange, it is vivid, it is fairly light. That is HSL. Hue: which colour. Saturation: how pure, or how grey. Lightness: how near to black or white.

A printer cannot shine. It lays ink on white paper, and the ink takes light away. Cyan, magenta, yellow — and black, because three inks alone make a muddy brown. That is CMYK.

Two roads to colour. That is why a colour can change when it leaves the screen and goes onto paper.
