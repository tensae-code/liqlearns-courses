# Hands-on — Applying Adjustments and Effects to an Image

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Correct the copy', 'Open a copy of a dull photo; adjust brightness, then contrast, a little at a time, and save the corrected version.')

## Task two
('One picture, two moods', 'Apply two different effects to copies of one corrected picture and write which job each version suits.')
