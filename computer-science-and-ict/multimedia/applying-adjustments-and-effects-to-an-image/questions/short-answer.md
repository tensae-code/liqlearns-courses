# Applying Adjustments and Effects to an Image — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do adjustments do to an image?**
**Answer: They correct what the camera got wrong — brightness, contrast and colour**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does brightness change?**
**Answer: The light of the whole picture, lifted or lowered**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does contrast deepen?**
**Answer: The difference between the picture's light and dark**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the working order for adjustments?**
**Answer: Correct first, on a copy, a little at a time, checking after each change**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does an adjustment pushed too far do?**
**Answer: It destroys the detail it meant to save**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are effects?**
**Answer: Deliberate changes of a picture's look and mood**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two effects taught in this lesson.**
**Answer: Black and white, and an aged or painted appearance (any two named effects)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is an effect chosen?**
**Answer: For the picture's job — a portrait, a poster or an illustration**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is an effect applied?**
**Answer: Once the picture is correct**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How do adjustment and effect differ in one line?**
**Answer: Adjustment repairs; effect dresses**
