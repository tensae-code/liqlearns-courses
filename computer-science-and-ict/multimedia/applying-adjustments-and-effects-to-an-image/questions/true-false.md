# Applying Adjustments and Effects to an Image — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do adjustments do to an image?" is: They correct what the camera got wrong — brightness, contrast and colour**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does brightness change?" is: It destroys the detail it meant to save**
**Answer: False** — The light of the whole picture, lifted or lowered

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does contrast deepen?" is: The difference between the picture's light and dark**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the working order for adjustments?" is: Black and white, and an aged or painted appearance (any two named effects)**
**Answer: False** — Correct first, on a copy, a little at a time, checking after each change

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does an adjustment pushed too far do?" is: It destroys the detail it meant to save**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What are effects?" is: Once the picture is correct**
**Answer: False** — Deliberate changes of a picture's look and mood

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name two effects taught in this lesson." is: Black and white, and an aged or painted appearance (any two named effects)**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is an effect chosen?" is: They correct what the camera got wrong — brightness, contrast and colour**
**Answer: False** — For the picture's job — a portrait, a poster or an illustration

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is an effect applied?" is: Once the picture is correct**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How do adjustment and effect differ in one line?" is: The difference between the picture's light and dark**
**Answer: False** — Adjustment repairs; effect dresses
