# Applying Adjustments and Effects to an Image — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do adjustments do to an image?**
a) They correct what the camera got wrong — brightness, contrast and colour  b) The light of the whole picture, lifted or lowered  c) The difference between the picture's light and dark  d) Correct first, on a copy, a little at a time, checking after each change
**Answer: a) They correct what the camera got wrong — brightness, contrast and colour**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does brightness change?**
a) The difference between the picture's light and dark  b) The light of the whole picture, lifted or lowered  c) Correct first, on a copy, a little at a time, checking after each change  d) It destroys the detail it meant to save
**Answer: b) The light of the whole picture, lifted or lowered**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does contrast deepen?**
a) Correct first, on a copy, a little at a time, checking after each change  b) It destroys the detail it meant to save  c) The difference between the picture's light and dark  d) Deliberate changes of a picture's look and mood
**Answer: c) The difference between the picture's light and dark**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the working order for adjustments?**
a) It destroys the detail it meant to save  b) Deliberate changes of a picture's look and mood  c) Black and white, and an aged or painted appearance (any two named effects)  d) Correct first, on a copy, a little at a time, checking after each change
**Answer: d) Correct first, on a copy, a little at a time, checking after each change**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does an adjustment pushed too far do?**
a) It destroys the detail it meant to save  b) Deliberate changes of a picture's look and mood  c) Black and white, and an aged or painted appearance (any two named effects)  d) For the picture's job — a portrait, a poster or an illustration
**Answer: a) It destroys the detail it meant to save**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are effects?**
a) Black and white, and an aged or painted appearance (any two named effects)  b) Deliberate changes of a picture's look and mood  c) For the picture's job — a portrait, a poster or an illustration  d) Once the picture is correct

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is an effect chosen?**
a) Once the picture is correct  b) Adjustment repairs; effect dresses  c) They correct what the camera got wrong — brightness, contrast and colour  d) For the picture's job — a portrait, a poster or an illustration
