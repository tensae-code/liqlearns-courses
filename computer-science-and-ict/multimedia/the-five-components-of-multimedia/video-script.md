# Video script — The five components of multimedia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **The five components**.

**Text** says it — headlines, captions, buttons.
**Graphics** show it — charts, photos, clipart.
**Audio** is heard — speech, music, sounds.
**Video** films the real — pictures played fast look alive. Big files!
**Animation** draws the moving — step by step, even what no camera can film.

Text says. Graphics show. Audio is heard. Video films. Animation draws. Five jobs — one presentation.
