# The five components of multimedia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Text in multimedia includes —
a) only novels  b) headlines, subtitles, captions, directions, menus and buttons  c) only file names  d) sounds and effects
**Answer: b) headlines, subtitles, captions, directions, menus and buttons** — the backbone list.

**2.** A graphic becomes hypermedia when —
a) it is very large  b) it is printed  c) the graphic itself links to further information  d) it has no colour
**Answer: c) the graphic itself links to further information** — the link is the difference.

**3.** Audio is captured by —
a) a scanner  b) a microphone  c) a printer  d) the Stage
**Answer: b) a microphone** — or played from synthesizer/keyboard via MIDI.

**4.** Video files are very large because —
a) they are text  b) photographic images are played at speed, many images per second  c) they have no sound  d) they are drawn
**Answer: b) photographic images are played at speed, many images per second** — many images make big files.

**5.** Animation is a strong teaching aid for —
a) spelling lists  b) techniques and complex procedures  c) file names  d) page numbers
**Answer: b) techniques and complex procedures** — it shows steps moving.
