# Hands-on — The five components of multimedia

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Component hunt
Find one real presentation — a lesson, a poster with a screen, a phone app. Tick off each component present and write its job in three words.

## Draw the dissected screen
On paper, draw one screen and draw lines out to five labelled boxes — text, graphics, audio, video, animation — one line per component you included.
