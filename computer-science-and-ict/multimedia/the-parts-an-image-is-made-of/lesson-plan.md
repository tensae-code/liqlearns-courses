## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Points, lines and areas** — An image is made of **parts**: single **points** (pixels), **lines** joining points, and filled **areas**. A drawing program builds every picture from these — a house is lines and areas, a face is areas and points of colour. Naming the parts is the first step of the Grade 9 graphics chapter's acquaintance lesson: you cannot edit what you cannot take apart in words.
2. **Foreground, background and colour** — An image also divides into **foreground** — the thing shown — and **background** — what stands behind it — and every part carries a **colour**. Editors keep these divisions real: select the foreground, change the background, recolour one area. The parts named in this lesson are the handles every later editing lesson grabs.
