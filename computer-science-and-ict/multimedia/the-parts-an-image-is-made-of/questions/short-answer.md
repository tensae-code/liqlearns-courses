# The Parts an Image Is Made Of — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What three parts is an image made of?**
**Answer: Points, lines and filled areas**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a single point in an image called?**
**Answer: A pixel**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a house, in parts?**
**Answer: Lines and areas**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why name the parts first?**
**Answer: You cannot edit what you cannot take apart in words**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does this lesson sit?**
**Answer: In the Grade 9 graphics chapter, before processing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What two regions does an image divide into?**
**Answer: Foreground and background**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What stands behind the thing shown?**
**Answer: The background**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does every part of an image carry?**
**Answer: A colour**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do editors let you do with the foreground?**
**Answer: Select it and change it separately**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What are the named parts, for later lessons?**
**Answer: The handles every editing lesson grabs**
