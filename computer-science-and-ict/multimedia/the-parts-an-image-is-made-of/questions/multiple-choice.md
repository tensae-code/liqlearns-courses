# The Parts an Image Is Made Of — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What three parts is an image made of?**
a) Points, lines and filled areas  b) A pixel  c) Lines and areas  d) You cannot edit what you cannot take apart in words
**Answer: a) Points, lines and filled areas**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a single point in an image called?**
a) Lines and areas  b) A pixel  c) You cannot edit what you cannot take apart in words  d) In the Grade 9 graphics chapter, before processing
**Answer: b) A pixel**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a house, in parts?**
a) You cannot edit what you cannot take apart in words  b) In the Grade 9 graphics chapter, before processing  c) Lines and areas  d) Foreground and background
**Answer: c) Lines and areas**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why name the parts first?**
