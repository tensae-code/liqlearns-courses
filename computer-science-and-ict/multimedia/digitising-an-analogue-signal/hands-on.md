# Hands-on — Digitising an Analogue Signal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sample by hand
Draw a smooth wave on squared paper, one second wide. Sample it every half-second and join the sample points with straight lines; then sample the same wave every tenth of a second and join again. Write two sentences: what did the sparse sampling invent or lose, and which copy would you trust?

## Count the copy
A voice note is recorded at 8 kHz with 8-bit depth, mono, for 30 seconds. Compute its size in bits and in bytes. Then compute the same 30 seconds at CD quality on one channel (44.1 kHz, 16 bits). Say in one line what the extra size buys.

## In the simulator or on a phone
If your phone or school software shows recording settings, find the sample rate and bit depth of a voice recording and a music file. Predict each file's size per minute from the formula before checking the file's real size in its properties — the two should be close; say what might explain any gap.
