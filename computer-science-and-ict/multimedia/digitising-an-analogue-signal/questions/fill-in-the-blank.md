# Digitising an Analogue Signal — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The number of samples taken per second is called the sampling ____.**
**Answer: rate** — Measured in hertz — samples per second.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) To avoid aliasing, sample at least ____ times the highest frequency present.**
**Answer: twice** — The sampling (Nyquist) theorem's rule.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Rounding each sample to the nearest fixed level is called ____.**
**Answer: quantisation** — The middle step of digitising.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) With a bit depth of n bits, the number of levels is ____.**
**Answer: 2ⁿ (two to the n)** — 8 bits → 256 levels; 16 bits → 65 536.
