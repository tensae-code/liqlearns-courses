## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lines and curves — drawing by hand, guided** — The **line** tool draws straight: press where the line starts, drag to where it ends, release — the program keeps the stroke true. The **curve** tool draws in two acts: first drag out a straight line, then pull it once or twice to bend it into the curve the picture needs. Lines and curves are the drawing's skeleton — Mongolia teaches them first because every later picture, from the ger to the flag's pattern, is planned in them.
2. **Shapes and fill — ready forms, poured colour** — **Shapes** are ready forms — rectangles, ellipses, polygons and the rest of Paint's shape set — drawn by dragging out their frame, each true every time. **Fill with colour** pours a chosen colour into a **closed area** with one click: click inside a shape and its inside takes the colour — and if the outline has a gap, the colour escapes through it, the classic Paint lesson. The four methods together — line, curve, shape, fill — are the whole drawing technology of the book's Paint unit.
