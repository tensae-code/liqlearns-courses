# Hands-on — Paint Drawing Tools — Lines, Curves, Shapes and Fill

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Four methods, one picture', 'Draw a ger in Paint using all four methods: lines for the ropes, a curve for the roof's slope, shapes for the body and door, fill for its colours. Label each method on your plan.')

## Task two
('The gap test', 'Draw a shape with a deliberate gap in its outline and fill it. Watch the escape, undo, close the gap, and fill again. Write the rule you learned.')
