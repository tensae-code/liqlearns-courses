# Paint Drawing Tools — Lines, Curves, Shapes and Fill — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four drawing methods in this lesson.**
**Answer: Line, curve, shapes and fill with colour**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does the line tool draw?**
**Answer: Press at the start, drag to the end, release — the program keeps it straight**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a curve drawn?**
**Answer: Drag out a straight line first, then pull it once or twice to bend it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are shapes in Paint?**
**Answer: Ready forms — rectangles, ellipses, polygons — drawn by dragging out their frame**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does fill with colour do?**
**Answer: Pours a chosen colour into a closed area with one click**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens if the outline has a gap?**
**Answer: The colour escapes through the gap**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why are lines and curves taught first?**
**Answer: They are the drawing's skeleton — every later picture is planned in them**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which program are these tools taught in?**
**Answer: MS Paint**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must an area be for fill to work cleanly?**
**Answer: Closed**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name a Mongolian picture the book builds with these tools.**
**Answer: The ger (any one of: the national flag's pattern)**
