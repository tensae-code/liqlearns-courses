# Paint Drawing Tools — Lines, Curves, Shapes and Fill — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the four drawing methods in this lesson." is: Line, curve, shapes and fill with colour**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How does the line tool draw?" is: Drag out a straight line first, then pull it once or twice to bend it**
**Answer: False** — Press at the start, drag to the end, release — the program keeps it straight

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is a curve drawn?" is: Drag out a straight line first, then pull it once or twice to bend it**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are shapes in Paint?" is: Pours a chosen colour into a closed area with one click**
**Answer: False** — Ready forms — rectangles, ellipses, polygons — drawn by dragging out their frame

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does fill with colour do?" is: Pours a chosen colour into a closed area with one click**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens if the outline has a gap?" is: They are the drawing's skeleton — every later picture is planned in them**
**Answer: False** — The colour escapes through the gap

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why are lines and curves taught first?" is: They are the drawing's skeleton — every later picture is planned in them**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which program are these tools taught in?" is: Closed**
**Answer: False** — MS Paint

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What must an area be for fill to work cleanly?" is: Closed**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name a Mongolian picture the book builds with these tools." is: Line, curve, shapes and fill with colour**
**Answer: False** — The ger (any one of: the national flag's pattern)
