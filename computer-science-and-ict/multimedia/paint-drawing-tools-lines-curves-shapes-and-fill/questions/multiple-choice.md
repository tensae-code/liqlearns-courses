# Paint Drawing Tools — Lines, Curves, Shapes and Fill — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four drawing methods in this lesson.**
a) Line, curve, shapes and fill with colour  b) Press at the start, drag to the end, release — the program keeps it straight  c) Drag out a straight line first, then pull it once or twice to bend it  d) Ready forms — rectangles, ellipses, polygons — drawn by dragging out their frame
**Answer: a) Line, curve, shapes and fill with colour**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does the line tool draw?**
a) Drag out a straight line first, then pull it once or twice to bend it  b) Press at the start, drag to the end, release — the program keeps it straight  c) Ready forms — rectangles, ellipses, polygons — drawn by dragging out their frame  d) Pours a chosen colour into a closed area with one click

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a curve drawn?**
