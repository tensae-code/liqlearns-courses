# Hands-on — Making a Prevention Video

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Make the ninety seconds** — In groups, plan (situation, turn, sentence) and produce a prevention video of at most ninety seconds on cyberbullying. Show it to another class.

## Task two
**Fresh-viewer test** — Show your video to viewers who know nothing of the plan. Ask them the three questions: what happened, what can be done, what line stayed? Repair what they missed.
