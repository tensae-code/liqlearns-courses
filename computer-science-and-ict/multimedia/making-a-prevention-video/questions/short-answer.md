# Making a Prevention Video — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a prevention video's job?**
**Answer: Make viewers recognise a danger and know the move that answers it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the plan's three parts.**
**Answer: The situation, the turn, and the sentence that stays**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a video with no turn teach?**
**Answer: Only fear**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) On what danger is the scenario's video made?**
**Answer: Cyberbullying (la cyberintimidation)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What serves what?**
**Answer: Camera work serves the message**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is checked against the plan?**
**Answer: Whether a fresh viewer catches the situation, the turn and the sentence**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the filming done?**
**Answer: Simply and clearly, edited to the essential**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Who is the video shown to?**
**Answer: Its real audience — the class, the school**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When does a prevention video succeed?**
**Answer: In the audience's memory a week later**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do the makers do after showing?**
**Answer: Answer the audience's questions**
