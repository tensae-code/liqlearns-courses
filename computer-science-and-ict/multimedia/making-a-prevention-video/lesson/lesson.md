# Making a Prevention Video

## Do it first

A warning that nobody watches protects nobody. Your class must make ninety seconds that pupils will actually watch to the end — about cyberbullying. Before reading on: what is the one sentence your video exists to plant?

## Sub lesson 1 — Message first

A **prevention video** is an audiovisual message with a protective job: make viewers recognise a danger and know the move that answers it. The scenario's prolongement plans it as message first: the **situation** (a cyberbullying scene viewers recognise), the **turn** (what the victim or a bystander can do), and the **sentence that stays** (the line the video exists to plant). Camera work serves these three; a video with no turn teaches only fear.

## Sub lesson 2 — Making and showing it

Making follows the class's audiovisual craft: script the scenes, film simply and clearly, edit to the essential, and check the result against the plan — does a fresh viewer catch the situation, the turn and the sentence? Showing is part of prevention: the video is presented to its real audience (the class, the school), and its makers answer questions. A prevention video succeeds in the audience's memory a week later, not in the filming.
