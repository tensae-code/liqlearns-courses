# EXIF Metadata — the Hidden Data in Photos — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) EXIF data is —**
a) A filter applied to photos  b) A written record stored inside a photo file  c) The name of a camera brand  d) A kind of lens
**Answer: b) A written record stored inside a photo file — Device, date, settings and often GPS coordinates, written at the moment of the shot.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is commonly found in an EXIF block?**
a) The photographer’s password  b) The date, time and device model  c) The names of people in the photo  d) The price of the camera
**Answer: b) The date, time and device model — Along with settings and, when location is on, GPS coordinates.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A photo’s EXIF GPS numbers give its position as —**
a) A street name  b) A postcode  c) Latitude and longitude  d) A compass direction
**Answer: c) Latitude and longitude — Placed on a map, they mark the exact spot of the shot.**
