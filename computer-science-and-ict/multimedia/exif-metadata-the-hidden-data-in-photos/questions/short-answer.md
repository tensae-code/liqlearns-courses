# EXIF Metadata — the Hidden Data in Photos — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) A student posts an original photo of her new puppy taken in her garden. What could a stranger learn from the file that the picture does not show?**
**Answer: From the EXIF block: the phone model, the exact date and time of the shot, and — if location was on — the GPS coordinates of the garden, which on a map reveal where she lives.**
