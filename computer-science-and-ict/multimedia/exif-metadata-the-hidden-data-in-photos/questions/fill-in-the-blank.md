# EXIF Metadata — the Hidden Data in Photos — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The hidden written record inside a photo file is called ____ data.**
**Answer: EXIF — Short for Exchangeable Image File Format.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) To share a photo without its place, use the share option that removes the ____.**
**Answer: location — Most phones offer to strip the location (the GPS part of EXIF) when sharing.**
