# Video script — EXIF Metadata — the Hidden Data in Photos

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every photo file hides a written record. It is called EXIF.

The camera writes it by itself, at the moment of the shot. Which device. What day, what minute. Which settings.

And very often: where. GPS numbers — latitude and longitude — of the exact place.

Anyone who gets your photo can read this record. They can put the numbers on a map and stand where you stood.

So before you send an original photo, check what it carries. And when the place is private, remove the location first.
