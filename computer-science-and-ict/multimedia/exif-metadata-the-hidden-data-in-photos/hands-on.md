# Hands-on — EXIF Metadata — the Hidden Data in Photos

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a real record (if a device is available)
Open a photo’s properties or information panel. Write down every EXIF item you can find: device, date, settings, and whether coordinates are present. If they are, convert them to decimal degrees and find the spot on a map. Was the phone right?

## The mystery photo
Your teacher gives you a photo (or its printed EXIF record) with the place removed from the picture itself. From the coordinates alone, name the town the photo was taken in. This is the SNT book’s own mystery-photo activity — feel how much a "hidden" block gives away.
