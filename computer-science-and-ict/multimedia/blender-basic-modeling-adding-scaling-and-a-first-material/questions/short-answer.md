**1. (Sub lesson 1) How is a Blender scene populated?**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What removes objects?**
**Answer: Deleting**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does scaling do?**
**Answer: Resizes an object along its measures**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are the book's set builds?**
**Answer: The table-tennis ball and racket**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does proportion mean in those builds?**
**Answer: The ball's small sphere against the racket's flattened shape, judged by real objects**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a material give an object?**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a grey solid, in the lesson's phrase?**
**Answer: A ghost of a thing**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) In what order do form and surface come?**
**Answer: Form first (add and scale), then surface (material)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How complex is the first material?**
