# A Sprite's Look and Computer Animation

## Do it first

Look at two drawings of the same cat: legs together, legs apart. Flick between them: the cat steps. Its look changed — and the change itself is the movement.

## A Sprite's Look and Computer Animation

Grade 4 returns to the sprite's **appearance (Външен вид на героя)** with new depth: the sprite's look is not just chosen, it is **made** — in Scratch's paint editor, drawing and editing the costume the sprite wears, changing shape, colour and detail until the look fits the story. Then the second half of the lesson joins look to motion: **computer animation (Компютърна анимация)** at the sprite level — a row of costumes, each changed a little, switched in order, so the sprite walks, flaps, blinks or dances by its own body, not by sliding across the stage. The craft is small honest changes: a costume that changes too much jumps; a row that is too short stutters. This builds directly on Grade 3's sprites-and-costumes and story-animation lessons, going deeper into the editor and the costume row.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: A sprite's look is made in the Scratch paint editor — drawn, coloured and changed costume by costume — and a row of looks, switched in order, is computer animation. Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.

## Do It Yourself

1. Show it, don't just say it: edit a sprite's look in the paint editor until it fits the story. Do this with a real example from your own work or your own school day, and write or say how you know it is right.
2. Show it, don't just say it: build a row of costumes, each changed a little. Do this with a real example from your own work or your own school day, and write or say how you know it is right.
3. Show it, don't just say it: switch the costume row in order to animate the sprite's own body. Do this with a real example from your own work or your own school day, and write or say how you know it is right.
