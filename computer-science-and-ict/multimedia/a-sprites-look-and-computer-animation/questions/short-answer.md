# A Sprite's Look and Computer Animation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Explain, in your own words: edit a sprite's look in the paint editor until it fits the story.**
**Answer:** The learner's own explanation, true to the lesson: A sprite's look is made in the Scratch paint editor — drawn, coloured and changed costume by costume — and a row of looks, switched in order, is computer animation.

**2. A classmate gets this lesson's work wrong. What is the most likely fault, and how is it fixed?**
**Answer:** Animation jumps or stutters → a costume changed too much, or the row is too short: add the in-between look, or slow the switching, until the eye reads it as one movement.
