# A Sprite's Look and Computer Animation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. Edit a sprite's look in the paint editor until it fits the story.**
**Answer: True** — This is one of the lesson's own outcomes.

**2. Animation at the sprite level means sliding the sprite across the stage without changing its look at all.**
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite of this.

**3. Build a row of costumes, each changed a little.**
**Answer: True** — This is one of the lesson's own outcomes.
