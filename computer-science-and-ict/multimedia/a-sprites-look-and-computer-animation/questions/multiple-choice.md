# A Sprite's Look and Computer Animation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A sprite's look is made and changed in —**
a) the paint editor  b) the sound gallery  c) the backdrop library
**Answer: a) the paint editor**

**2. Sprite-level animation switches —**
a) backdrops only  b) random costumes  c) a row of costumes in order
**Answer: c) a row of costumes in order**

**3. A costume that changes too much makes the animation —**
a) silent  b) jump  c) smoother
**Answer: b) jump**

**4. This lesson builds on Grade 3's —**
a) sprites and costumes and story animation  b) Blue-Bot lessons  c) typing lessons
**Answer: a) sprites and costumes and story animation**
