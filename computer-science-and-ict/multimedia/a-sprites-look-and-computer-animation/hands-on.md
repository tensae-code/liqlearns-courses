# Hands-on — A Sprite's Look and Computer Animation

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Make the look
Edit one sprite's costume in the paint editor: change its colours and one detail so it fits a story you name. Show before and after.

## Row of looks
Make a four-costume row for one movement — a step, a flap, a blink. Switch the row in a loop and tune it until it reads as one smooth movement.
