# Video script — Paint 3D — and Working with 3D Images

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Paint 3D** extends the familiar Paint canvas with a **3D library**: ready objects — cubes, spheres, animals, people — that are **placed, moved, resized and rotated in space**, viewed from any angle by turning them. A 3D object is not a picture of a thing but a model of it: it has a back the flat drawing never had, painted with the same brushes and colours, and it can stand **inside a 2D scene** — the application's mixed canvas is its signature: drawing and model on one page.
The practical section turns knowledge into a product: **compose a scene** — choose a background, place objects, set their sizes and depths so nearer objects overlap farther ones, paint surfaces, add 2D stickers and text — then **save and export** the work, including as a flat image for documents. The working order the book drills: plan the scene on paper, place big before small, turn objects to check them from two angles, and keep the file saved as you go — 3D scenes are heavier, and lost work costs more.
