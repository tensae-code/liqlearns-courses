# Hands-on — Paint 3D — and Working with 3D Images

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Scene build** — Compose a scene in Paint 3D (or on paper as a full plan): three library objects at three depths on a painted background, exported as a flat image.

## Task two
**Turn and compare** — Take one object and draw or describe it from three angles; say what the flat Paint of earlier lessons could never show.
