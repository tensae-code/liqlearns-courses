**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Multimedia · **Lesson:** Paint 3D — and Working with 3D Images
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Moldova (2026-11-14) — in the title Multimedia on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). **The one thing this lesson teaches:** Paint 3D: the drawing application that adds the third dimension — placing ready 3D objects, turning them in space, painting them — and the practical work of processing three-dimensional images (Moldova Grade 8, Informatică, §4.5–4.6).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Paint 3D — objects with depth** — **Paint 3D** extends the familiar Paint canvas with a **3D library**: ready objects — cubes, spheres, animals, people — that are **placed, moved, resized and rotated in space**, viewed from any angle by turning them. A 3D object is not a picture of a thing but a model of it: it has a back the flat drawing never had, painted with the same brushes and colours, and it can stand **inside a 2D scene** — the application's mixed canvas is its signature: drawing and model on one page.
2. **Practical work — processing 3D images** — The practical section turns knowledge into a product: **compose a scene** — choose a background, place objects, set their sizes and depths so nearer objects overlap farther ones, paint surfaces, add 2D stickers and text — then **save and export** the work, including as a flat image for documents. The working order the book drills: plan the scene on paper, place big before small, turn objects to check them from two angles, and keep the file saved as you go — 3D scenes are heavier, and lost work costs more.
