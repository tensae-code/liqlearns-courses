# Lesson plan — Taking a Photo and Filming a Video on a Smartphone

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Multimedia · **Lesson:** Taking a Photo and Filming a Video on a Smartphone
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Morocco (2026-11-14) — in the title Multimedia on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Morocco's Grade 1 “J'excelle en informatique” teacher guide, because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson. This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only (Morocco), named in lesson/sources.md, counted nowhere.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The smartphone or tablet's camera app shows on the screen what the camera sees; the learner points the device at the thing to photograph, holds it steady, and taps the photo button to keep the picture. In the camera app's video mode, recording starts with the record button and stops with the same button: everything the camera sees and hears between start and stop is kept as a video — a moving picture with sound.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Taking a photo with the camera** — The smartphone or tablet's camera app shows on the screen what the camera sees; the learner points the device at the thing to photograph, holds it steady, and taps the photo button to keep the picture.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Point, steady, tap.
2. A photo is one still moment.
3. Video keeps movement and sound.
4. Always play it back and check.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil, and a computer with the program this lesson uses, if available.
