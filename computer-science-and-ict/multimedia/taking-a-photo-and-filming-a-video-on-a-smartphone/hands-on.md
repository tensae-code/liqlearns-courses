# Hands-on — Taking a Photo and Filming a Video on a Smartphone

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Photo, then video
Choose one thing in the classroom. Take its photo. Then film it for a few seconds while something moves. Play both back and say, for each, what was kept.

## Check your shot
Take a photo of a classmate's drawing. Look at it together: is it straight, is the whole drawing in, is it clear? If not, say what you will change and take it again.
