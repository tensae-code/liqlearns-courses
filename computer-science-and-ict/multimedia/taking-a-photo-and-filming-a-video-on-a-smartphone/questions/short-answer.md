# Taking a Photo and Filming a Video on a Smartphone — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the camera app show on the screen?**
**Answer: What the camera sees**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is a photo taken?**
**Answer: Point the device, hold it steady, tap the photo button**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a photo?**
**Answer: A still image — one moment, kept**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do you do right after taking a photo?**
**Answer: Look at it to check it shows what you meant to keep**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When should a photo be taken again?**
**Answer: If it is blurred or the thing is missing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does video recording start and stop?**
**Answer: With the record button — the same button for both**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a video?**
**Answer: A moving picture with sound, kept between start and stop**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How must the device be held while filming?**
**Answer: Steady, and pointed at the action**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is filming finished?**
**Answer: By playing the video back and checking it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does a video differ from a photo?**
**Answer: A photo is one still moment; a video keeps movement and sound**
