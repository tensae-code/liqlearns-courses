# Taking a Photo and Filming a Video on a Smartphone — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the camera app show on the screen?**
a) What the camera sees  b) Point the device, hold it steady, tap the photo button  c) A still image — one moment, kept  d) Look at it to check it shows what you meant to keep
**Answer: a) What the camera sees**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is a photo taken?**
a) A still image — one moment, kept  b) Point the device, hold it steady, tap the photo button  c) Look at it to check it shows what you meant to keep  d) If it is blurred or the thing is missing
**Answer: b) Point the device, hold it steady, tap the photo button**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a photo?**
a) Look at it to check it shows what you meant to keep  b) If it is blurred or the thing is missing  c) A still image — one moment, kept  d) With the record button — the same button for both
**Answer: c) A still image — one moment, kept**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do you do right after taking a photo?**
a) If it is blurred or the thing is missing  b) With the record button — the same button for both  c) A moving picture with sound, kept between start and stop  d) Look at it to check it shows what you meant to keep
**Answer: d) Look at it to check it shows what you meant to keep**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When should a photo be taken again?**
a) If it is blurred or the thing is missing  b) With the record button — the same button for both  c) A moving picture with sound, kept between start and stop  d) Steady, and pointed at the action
**Answer: a) If it is blurred or the thing is missing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does video recording start and stop?**
a) A moving picture with sound, kept between start and stop  b) With the record button — the same button for both  c) Steady, and pointed at the action  d) By playing the video back and checking it
**Answer: b) With the record button — the same button for both**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a video?**
a) Steady, and pointed at the action  b) By playing the video back and checking it  c) A moving picture with sound, kept between start and stop  d) A photo is one still moment; a video keeps movement and sound
**Answer: c) A moving picture with sound, kept between start and stop**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How must the device be held while filming?**
a) By playing the video back and checking it  b) A photo is one still moment; a video keeps movement and sound  c) What the camera sees  d) Steady, and pointed at the action
**Answer: d) Steady, and pointed at the action**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is filming finished?**
a) By playing the video back and checking it  b) A photo is one still moment; a video keeps movement and sound  c) What the camera sees  d) Point the device, hold it steady, tap the photo button
**Answer: a) By playing the video back and checking it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does a video differ from a photo?**
a) What the camera sees  b) A photo is one still moment; a video keeps movement and sound  c) Point the device, hold it steady, tap the photo button  d) A still image — one moment, kept
**Answer: b) A photo is one still moment; a video keeps movement and sound**
