# Taking a Photo and Filming a Video on a Smartphone — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the camera app show on the screen?" is: What the camera sees**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is a photo taken?" is: With the record button — the same button for both**
**Answer: False** — Point the device, hold it steady, tap the photo button

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a photo?" is: A still image — one moment, kept**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do you do right after taking a photo?" is: Steady, and pointed at the action**
**Answer: False** — Look at it to check it shows what you meant to keep

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "When should a photo be taken again?" is: If it is blurred or the thing is missing**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How does video recording start and stop?" is: A photo is one still moment; a video keeps movement and sound**
**Answer: False** — With the record button — the same button for both

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is a video?" is: A moving picture with sound, kept between start and stop**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How must the device be held while filming?" is: Point the device, hold it steady, tap the photo button**
**Answer: False** — Steady, and pointed at the action

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is filming finished?" is: By playing the video back and checking it**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How does a video differ from a photo?" is: Look at it to check it shows what you meant to keep**
**Answer: False** — A photo is one still moment; a video keeps movement and sound
