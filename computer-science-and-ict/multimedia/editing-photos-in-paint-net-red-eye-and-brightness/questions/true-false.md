# Editing Photos in Paint.NET — Red Eye and Brightness — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What causes red eye?" is: The flash lighting the blood of the eye itself**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where is the red-eye correction applied?" is: Show — a repair that shows is a second fault**
**Answer: False** — At the fault's place — the eye's area

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What must a repair not do?" is: Show — a repair that shows is a second fault**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does brightness do?" is: Deepens or softens the distance between lights and darks**
**Answer: False** — Lifts or lowers the whole picture's light at once

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does contrast do?" is: Deepens or softens the distance between lights and darks**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does Levels remap?" is: Levels**
**Answer: False** — The picture's tones — where blacks begin, where whites peak, the balance between

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which instrument is finest?" is: Levels**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is judged before any correction?" is: The smallest one that reaches the fault**
**Answer: False** — The fault — too dark, too flat or too harsh

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which instrument should be chosen?" is: The smallest one that reaches the fault**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which pictures does the lesson work on?" is: The flash lighting the blood of the eye itself**
**Answer: False** — Ready photographs from the picture library, in Paint.NET
