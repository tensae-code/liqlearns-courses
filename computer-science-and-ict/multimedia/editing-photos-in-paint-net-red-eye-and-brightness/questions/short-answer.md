# Editing Photos in Paint.NET — Red Eye and Brightness — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What causes red eye?**
**Answer: The flash lighting the blood of the eye itself**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the red-eye correction applied?**
**Answer: At the fault's place — the eye's area**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must a repair not do?**
**Answer: Show — a repair that shows is a second fault**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does brightness do?**
**Answer: Lifts or lowers the whole picture's light at once**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does contrast do?**
**Answer: Deepens or softens the distance between lights and darks**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Levels remap?**
**Answer: The picture's tones — where blacks begin, where whites peak, the balance between**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which instrument is finest?**
**Answer: Levels**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is judged before any correction?**
**Answer: The fault — too dark, too flat or too harsh**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which instrument should be chosen?**
**Answer: The smallest one that reaches the fault**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which pictures does the lesson work on?**
**Answer: Ready photographs from the picture library, in Paint.NET**
