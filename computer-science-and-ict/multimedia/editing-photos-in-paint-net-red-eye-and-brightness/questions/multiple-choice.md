# Editing Photos in Paint.NET — Red Eye and Brightness — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What causes red eye?**
a) The flash lighting the blood of the eye itself  b) At the fault's place — the eye's area  c) Show — a repair that shows is a second fault  d) Lifts or lowers the whole picture's light at once
**Answer: a) The flash lighting the blood of the eye itself**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the red-eye correction applied?**
a) Show — a repair that shows is a second fault  b) At the fault's place — the eye's area  c) Lifts or lowers the whole picture's light at once  d) Deepens or softens the distance between lights and darks
**Answer: b) At the fault's place — the eye's area**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must a repair not do?**
a) Lifts or lowers the whole picture's light at once  b) Deepens or softens the distance between lights and darks  c) Show — a repair that shows is a second fault  d) The picture's tones — where blacks begin, where whites peak, the balance between
**Answer: c) Show — a repair that shows is a second fault**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does brightness do?**
a) Deepens or softens the distance between lights and darks  b) The picture's tones — where blacks begin, where whites peak, the balance between  c) Levels  d) Lifts or lowers the whole picture's light at once
**Answer: d) Lifts or lowers the whole picture's light at once**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does contrast do?**
a) Deepens or softens the distance between lights and darks  b) The picture's tones — where blacks begin, where whites peak, the balance between  c) Levels  d) The fault — too dark, too flat or too harsh
**Answer: a) Deepens or softens the distance between lights and darks**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Levels remap?**
a) Levels  b) The picture's tones — where blacks begin, where whites peak, the balance between  c) The fault — too dark, too flat or too harsh  d) The smallest one that reaches the fault
**Answer: b) The picture's tones — where blacks begin, where whites peak, the balance between**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which instrument is finest?**
a) The fault — too dark, too flat or too harsh  b) The smallest one that reaches the fault  c) Levels  d) Ready photographs from the picture library, in Paint.NET
**Answer: c) Levels**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is judged before any correction?**
a) The smallest one that reaches the fault  b) Ready photographs from the picture library, in Paint.NET  c) The flash lighting the blood of the eye itself  d) The fault — too dark, too flat or too harsh
**Answer: d) The fault — too dark, too flat or too harsh**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which instrument should be chosen?**
a) The smallest one that reaches the fault  b) Ready photographs from the picture library, in Paint.NET  c) The flash lighting the blood of the eye itself  d) At the fault's place — the eye's area
**Answer: a) The smallest one that reaches the fault**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which pictures does the lesson work on?**
a) The flash lighting the blood of the eye itself  b) Ready photographs from the picture library, in Paint.NET  c) At the fault's place — the eye's area  d) Show — a repair that shows is a second fault
**Answer: b) Ready photographs from the picture library, in Paint.NET**
