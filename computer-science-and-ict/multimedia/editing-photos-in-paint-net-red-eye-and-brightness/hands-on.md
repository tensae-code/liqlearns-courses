# Hands-on — Editing Photos in Paint.NET — Red Eye and Brightness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Repair the portrait', 'Take a flash portrait with red eye. Remove the fault on a layer copy, then compare at true size with the original and have a partner judge whether the repair shows.')

## Task two
('Three lights', 'Take one dull photograph. Correct copies three ways — brightness only, contrast only, Levels. Present the three and defend the smallest sufficient one.')
