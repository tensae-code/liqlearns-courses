# Video script — Maps with Layers — OpenStreetMap and uMap

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A paper map must choose. A digital map does not.

It is a stack of layers. At the bottom, a base: streets, or relief, or a satellite photo. Above it, sheets you can switch: cycle paths, bus lines, flood zones.

You choose what shows. The machine assembles your view, from a continent down to a doorstep.

And you can be an author too. OpenStreetMap is the world map drawn by its users. With uMap, you place your own points on it, draw your own lines, import a track file — and publish your map with a link.
