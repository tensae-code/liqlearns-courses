**1. (Sub lesson 1) A hiking club and a delivery company look at the same town data. Give one layer each would switch on that the other would switch off, and why.**
**Answer: The hiking club switches on relief contours and footpaths — they plan by terrain and trails — and switches off motorway and parking layers. The delivery company does the reverse: roads and parking matter for every stop; contours and footpaths only clutter the view.**
