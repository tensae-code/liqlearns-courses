# Maps with Layers — OpenStreetMap and uMap — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A digital map shows every layer at once, and the reader cannot change what is shown.**
**Answer: False — Choosing layers is the point: each reader assembles the view their purpose needs.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) With uMap, a user can publish a personal map — points, lines, imported tracks — over the OpenStreetMap base.**
**Answer: True — Points with descriptions, drawn lines and areas, and imported files, shared by a link.**
