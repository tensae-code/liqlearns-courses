# Maps with Layers — OpenStreetMap and uMap — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In a digital map, a layer (calque) is —**
a) A printed page  b) A transparent sheet of one kind of data, shown or hidden independently  c) A password  d) The map’s price
**Answer: b) A transparent sheet of one kind of data, shown or hidden independently — Layers stack over a base and the reader toggles them.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is a base layer?**
a) Cycle paths  b) Satellite photography of the ground  c) Flood zones  d) A user’s points
**Answer: b) Satellite photography of the ground — A base shows the ground itself; thematic layers ride above it.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) OpenStreetMap is —**
a) A satellite company  b) The free world map drawn and corrected by its users  c) A paper atlas  d) A GPS receiver
**Answer: b) The free world map drawn and corrected by its users — Free to use, which is why tools like uMap build on it.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) In the SNT book’s activity, the file imported into uMap to lay a flight over the map is —**
a) A CSV  b) A KML file  c) A JPEG  d) An MP3
**Answer: b) A KML file — The tracked aircraft’s route, imported as its own layer.**
