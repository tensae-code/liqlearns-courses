# Maps with Layers — OpenStreetMap and uMap — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) France’s national geographic institute, whose maps appear in Géoportail, is the ____.**
**Answer: IGN — Its drawn maps and aerial photos serve as base layers.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The French word the book uses for a map layer is ____.**
**Answer: calque — A transparent sheet laid over the others.**
