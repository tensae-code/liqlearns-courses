# Hands-on — Maps with Layers — OpenStreetMap and uMap

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Plan a layered map on paper
For a visitor arriving in your town for one day, design a map: name the base layer and four thematic layers, and for each say in one line why this visitor needs it. Then say which layer a delivery driver would add and which they would switch off.

## Build it (if a browser is available)
In uMap over OpenStreetMap, create a map of your school’s surroundings: place at least three points with real descriptions (entrance, sports ground, bus stop), draw one line (a walking route), and — if a KML or GPS track file is available — import it as its own layer. Share the link with a partner and have them find your route using only your map.
