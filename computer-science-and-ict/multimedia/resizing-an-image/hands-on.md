# Hands-on — Resizing an image

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Scale a drawing
Draw a small picture on squared paper, counting its squares (its size). Redraw it twice as big on a second sheet, square by square. You have resized by hand — the whole picture kept, the size changed.

## Print-size planning
List three photos you know and for each write: keep the size, shrink for a small print, or enlarge for a poster — and which of the book's three purposes that matches.
