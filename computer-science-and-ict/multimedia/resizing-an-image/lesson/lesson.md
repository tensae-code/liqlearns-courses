# Resizing an image

## Do it first

Cropping cuts a photo; resizing keeps the whole photo but changes how big it is. Before reading on, name three reasons you might need the same photo bigger or smaller without cutting anything away.

## Resize, step by step — the book's order

**Resizing (scaling)** an image serves three purposes: printing in standard photo sizes, preserving the quality of digital photos, and enlarging small images to poster size.

1. Open the image.
2. Go to the **Image** menu.
3. Select **Image Size**.
4. Choose the size wanted in the Image Size window.
5. Click **OK**.
6. Save the file.
