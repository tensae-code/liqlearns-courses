# Resizing an image — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Give the resize steps in order.
**Answer:** Open the image; go to the Image menu; select Image Size; choose the size in the window; click OK; save the file.

**2.** Name the three purposes of resizing.
**Answer:** Printing in standard photo sizes; preserving the quality of digital photos; enlarging small images to poster size.
