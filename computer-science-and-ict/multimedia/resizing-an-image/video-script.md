Today: **Resizing an image**.

Remember the one thing: resizing changes an image's size through Image → Image Size: choose the size in the window, click OK, and save — for standard print sizes, for photo quality, and for enlarging small images to posters.

Watch the lesson's pictures. Say each key word after me. Then do the Do It Yourself at the end of the lesson — with your own hands.
