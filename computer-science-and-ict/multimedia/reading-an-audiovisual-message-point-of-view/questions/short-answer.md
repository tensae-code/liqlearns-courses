# Reading an Audiovisual Message — Point of View — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four elements of an audiovisual message.**
**Answer: Image, sound, editing, and the text it carries**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What belongs to the image element?**
**Answer: Framing, angle, movement**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What belongs to sound?**
**Answer: Words, music, silence**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why dismantle a message element by element?**
**Answer: A message you can dismantle is a message you can judge**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is anything in a made message “just there”?**
**Answer: No — every element was placed**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is point of view?**
**Answer: Where the camera stands and as whom the narration speaks — the eyes lent to the viewer**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What moves when point of view changes?**
**Answer: Sympathies — who the audience blames**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) On what topic does the activity practise this?**
**Answer: Bullying between pupils**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the reader's standing question?**
**Answer: Whose eyes am I watching through — and how would this look from the other side?**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the camera's side decide in the bullying message?**
**Answer: Who the audience blames**
