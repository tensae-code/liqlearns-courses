# Reading an Audiovisual Message — Point of View — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Image, sound, editing, and the text it carries**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Image, sound, editing, and the text it carries** — as the answer to: What belongs to the image element?
**Answer: False — Framing, angle, movement**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Words, music, silence**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Words, music, silence** — as the answer to: Why dismantle a message element by element?
**Answer: False — A message you can dismantle is a message you can judge**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: No — every element was placed**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: No — every element was placed** — as the answer to: What is point of view?
**Answer: False — Where the camera stands and as whom the narration speaks — the eyes lent to the viewer**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Sympathies — who the audience blames**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Sympathies — who the audience blames** — as the answer to: On what topic does the activity practise this?
**Answer: False — Bullying between pupils**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Whose eyes am I watching through — and how would this look from the other side?**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Whose eyes am I watching through — and how would this look from the other side?** — as the answer to: What does the camera's side decide in the bullying message?
**Answer: False — Who the audience blames**
