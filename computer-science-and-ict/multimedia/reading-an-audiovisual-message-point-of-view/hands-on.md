# Hands-on — Reading an Audiovisual Message — Point of View

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Dismantle a clip** — Watch a one-minute clip with the sound off, then sound only, then whole. List what each element alone made you think and feel.

## Task two
**Refilm the quarrel** — Storyboard one short scene twice, from two opposite points of view (six frames each). Present both: where did sympathy move?
