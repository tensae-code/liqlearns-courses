# Hands-on — Sharpen with Smart Sharpen

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Noisy photo rescue plan
Describe or find a grainy, noisy photo. Write the sharpening plan: tool, starting Amount, starting Radius, and the reason this tool suits noise.

## Settings ladder
On paper, make a ladder of tries: Radius 1 at 150%, then Radius 2 at 150%, then adjust. Beside each rung write what you would look for in the preview before climbing.
