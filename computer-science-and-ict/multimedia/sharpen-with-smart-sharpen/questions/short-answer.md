# Sharpen with Smart Sharpen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Give Smart Sharpen's starting settings.
**Answer:** Amount 150%, Radius around 1 or 2 (try 1 first).

**2.** What is Smart Sharpen's advantage over Unsharp Mask?
**Answer:** Built-in noise reduction — it can reduce the noise that sharpening brings with it.
