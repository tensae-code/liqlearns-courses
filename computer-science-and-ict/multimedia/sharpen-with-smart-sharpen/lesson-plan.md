# Lesson plan — Sharpen with Smart Sharpen

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Multimedia · **Lesson:** Sharpen with Smart Sharpen
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Multimedia of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 9, Unit 4 §4.3 — Image Processing and Multimedia.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Smart Sharpen, from the Filter menu, starts at Amount 150% and Radius 1 or 2 — and its advantage over Unsharp Mask is built-in noise reduction.
