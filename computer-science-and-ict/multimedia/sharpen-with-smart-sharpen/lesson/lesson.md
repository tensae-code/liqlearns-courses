# Sharpen with Smart Sharpen

## Do it first

Two sharpeners sit in the same menu. Before reading on, guess what 'smart' could add to sharpening — what problem of sharpening could a smarter tool clean up as it works?

## Smart Sharpen — settings and its advantage

**Smart Sharpen** is reached from the **Filter menu**.

Start with the **Amount slider at 150%** and **Radius around 1 or 2** — try 1 first.

[Picture 20538 — the Smart Sharpen panel]

Its advantage over Unsharp Mask: **built-in noise reduction**, which can reduce the noise that sharpening brings with it.
