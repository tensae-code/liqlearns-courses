# Sharpen with Smart Sharpen — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do and explain the lesson's one thing — Smart Sharpen, from the Filter menu, starts at Amount 150% and Radius 1 or 2 — and its advantage over Unsharp Mask is built-in noise reduction. Practice deepens with more items from the question files and harder corners of the same skill (new images, new values, the same steps), never with new content the lesson does not teach.
