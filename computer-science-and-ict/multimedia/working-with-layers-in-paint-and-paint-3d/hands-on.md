# Hands-on — Working with Layers in Paint and Paint 3D

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Two-colour drill', 'On paper or in Paint: draw a simple house outline in one colour only. Then add all colour with a second colour, never touching the outline. Write one sentence on where the technique nearly failed.')

## Task two
('Stack the scene', 'Plan a Paint 3D scene — a market stall — as a stack list: background, three objects in depth order, one text. Beside each, write what you would do to it alone (move, turn, re-light) without touching the rest.')
