# Working with Layers in Paint and Paint 3D — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does classic MS Paint not have?**
a) A layer panel  b) Line art in one colour, the second colour worked under and around the lines  c) Like two layers on one canvas  d) The line colour
**Answer: a) A layer panel**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the two-colour line-art technique?**
a) Like two layers on one canvas  b) Line art in one colour, the second colour worked under and around the lines  c) The line colour  d) Before the brush moves
**Answer: b) Line art in one colour, the second colour worked under and around the lines**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How do lines and fills behave in the technique?**
a) The line colour  b) Before the brush moves  c) Like two layers on one canvas  d) A layer panel
**Answer: c) Like two layers on one canvas**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must stay untouched while colouring?**
a) Before the brush moves  b) A layer panel  c) Line art in one colour, the second colour worked under and around the lines  d) The line colour
**Answer: d) The line colour**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When must the layer decision be made?**
a) Before the brush moves  b) A layer panel  c) Line art in one colour, the second colour worked under and around the lines  d) Like two layers on one canvas
**Answer: a) Before the brush moves**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Paint 3D give that Paint does not?**
a) Placed, turned and layered in depth  b) Real stacking — objects layered in depth  c) The light and mood of the scene  d) Flat, or as a 3D object
**Answer: b) Real stacking — objects layered in depth**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name one thing that can be done with 3D objects.**
a) The light and mood of the scene  b) Flat, or as a 3D object  c) Placed, turned and layered in depth  d) So the learner feels the difference between simulating layers and having them
**Answer: c) Placed, turned and layered in depth**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do effects change in Paint 3D?**
a) Flat, or as a 3D object  b) So the learner feels the difference between simulating layers and having them  c) Real stacking — objects layered in depth  d) The light and mood of the scene
**Answer: d) The light and mood of the scene**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How can text stand in Paint 3D?**
a) Flat, or as a 3D object  b) So the learner feels the difference between simulating layers and having them  c) Real stacking — objects layered in depth  d) Placed, turned and layered in depth
**Answer: a) Flat, or as a 3D object**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book pair Paint with Paint 3D?**
a) Real stacking — objects layered in depth  b) So the learner feels the difference between simulating layers and having them  c) Placed, turned and layered in depth  d) The light and mood of the scene
