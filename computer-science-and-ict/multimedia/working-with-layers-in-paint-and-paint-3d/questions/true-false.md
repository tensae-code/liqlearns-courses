# Working with Layers in Paint and Paint 3D — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does classic MS Paint not have?" is: A layer panel**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the two-colour line-art technique?" is: A layer panel**
**Answer: False** — Line art in one colour, the second colour worked under and around the lines

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How do lines and fills behave in the technique?" is: Like two layers on one canvas**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What must stay untouched while colouring?" is: A layer panel**
**Answer: False** — The line colour

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "When must the layer decision be made?" is: Before the brush moves**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does Paint 3D give that Paint does not?" is: Placed, turned and layered in depth**
**Answer: False** — Real stacking — objects layered in depth

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name one thing that can be done with 3D objects." is: Placed, turned and layered in depth**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do effects change in Paint 3D?" is: Real stacking — objects layered in depth**
**Answer: False** — The light and mood of the scene

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How can text stand in Paint 3D?" is: Flat, or as a 3D object**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why does the book pair Paint with Paint 3D?" is: Real stacking — objects layered in depth**
**Answer: False** — So the learner feels the difference between simulating layers and having them
