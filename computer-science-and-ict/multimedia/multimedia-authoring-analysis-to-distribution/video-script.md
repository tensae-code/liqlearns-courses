# Video script — Multimedia authoring — analysis to distribution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **The five stages of authoring**.

One: **Analysis** — decide what to do, and what to use.
Two: **Design** — draw the storyboard.
Three: **Development** — build the prototype.
Four: **Evaluation** — test it, tune it, make it shine.
Five: **Distribution** — package it, hand it out.

Decide. Draw. Build. Test. Deliver. Walk all five — in order.
