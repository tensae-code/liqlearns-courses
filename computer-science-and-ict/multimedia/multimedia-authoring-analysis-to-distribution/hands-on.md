# Hands-on — Multimedia authoring — analysis to distribution

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Walk a school project through all five stages
Take one small project — a multimedia welcome presentation for your school. Write one card per stage: what happens, who does it, what it hands to the next stage. Lay the cards in order and read the road aloud.

## The guide's fuller road (teacher evidence)
Using the fuller breakdown in the sources, mark where planning-and-costing and testing-against-platforms sit inside the five taught stages.
