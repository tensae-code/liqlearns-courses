# Hands-on — Audio File Formats — Size and Quality

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Same song, three sizes', 'Export one short recording as WAV and as MP3 at two bit rates. Record the three sizes and listen for differences with headphones.')

## Task two
('Choose the rate', 'A school podcast episode is 20 minutes. Choose a publishing bit rate and defend it: state the size it gives and what quality it keeps.')
