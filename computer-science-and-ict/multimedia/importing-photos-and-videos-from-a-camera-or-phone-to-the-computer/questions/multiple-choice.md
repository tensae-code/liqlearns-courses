# Importing Photos and Videos from a Camera or Phone to the Computer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where are pictures and videos viewed on the computer in this lesson?**
a) In Microsoft Photos  b) The pictures and videos to import  c) When the media is stored in a named folder on the computer  d) So they can be found, used and kept safe
**Answer: a) In Microsoft Photos**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What can you do with a clip in Microsoft Photos?**
a) Wirelessly, over a shared connection  b) Play it — and move through a folder of shots  c) That the imported files open from their new home  d) Wired (with a cable) and wireless
**Answer: b) Play it — and move through a folder of shots**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first step of importing with a cable?**
a) When the media is stored in a named folder on the computer  b) So they can be found, used and kept safe  c) Connect the camera or phone and let the computer recognise it  d) The computer
**Answer: c) Connect the camera or phone and let the computer recognise it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do you choose before copying?**
a) That the imported files open from their new home  b) Wired (with a cable) and wireless  c) In Microsoft Photos  d) The pictures and videos to import
**Answer: d) The pictures and videos to import**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How else can media arrive without a cable?**
a) Wirelessly, over a shared connection  b) So they can be found, used and kept safe  c) The computer  d) Play it — and move through a folder of shots
**Answer: a) Wirelessly, over a shared connection**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When is importing finished?**
a) Wired (with a cable) and wireless  b) When the media is stored in a named folder on the computer  c) In Microsoft Photos  d) Connect the camera or phone and let the computer recognise it

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do you check before clearing the device copy?**
a) The computer  b) Play it — and move through a folder of shots  c) That the imported files open from their new home  d) The pictures and videos to import

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why store imports in a named folder?**
a) In Microsoft Photos  b) Connect the camera or phone and let the computer recognise it  c) Wirelessly, over a shared connection  d) So they can be found, used and kept safe
**Answer: d) So they can be found, used and kept safe**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are the two ways of importing named in this lesson?**
a) Wired (with a cable) and wireless  b) Play it — and move through a folder of shots  c) The pictures and videos to import  d) When the media is stored in a named folder on the computer

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do you copy the media onto?**
