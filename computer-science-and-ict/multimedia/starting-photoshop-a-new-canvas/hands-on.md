# Hands-on — Starting Photoshop — a new canvas

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Paper canvas
Cut a sheet of paper to a size you choose. Write on its back: its name, its width and height (measure them), its orientation, and pretend-resolution. You have done by hand what Create New does on screen.

## Open an existing image
On a real computer or phone, open an existing picture in any image app — the File-menu route of this lesson. Note how it differs from creating a new blank canvas.
