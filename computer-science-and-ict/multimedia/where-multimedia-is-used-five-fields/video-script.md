# Video script — Where multimedia is used — five fields

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Where multimedia is used**.

Five fields. School. Fun. Public places. Working together. Health.

At school, pictures and sound make ideas clear. You can learn with a teacher far away.
On the move, films and music travel with you.
In a station or a church, a big screen helps everyone.
Far apart, people meet by video — they talk, they share a screen.
In a hospital, doctors send pictures, learn, and practise surgery on a model — no patient at risk.

Same five forms. Five different jobs. Now name them.
