**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Ethiopia, Bhutan, Libya, Rwanda).**

Nothing in this lesson claims a page the placements do not give; the lesson's content is the placements' content, re-expressed for the learner, with the book's own step orders, examples and practicals kept exactly as printed. Book error taught corrected: printed p. 141 says “Figure 4.3 shows virtual meetings” — the virtual-meeting figure is 4.4; Figure 4.3 is entertainment. This lesson teaches from the pictured content (the meeting grid belongs with collaboration) and names the slip here. The teacher guide's Activity 4.1 key (printed pp. 73–75) is taught in the lesson's "Five sectors" section as labelled teacher-guide evidence — its own five sectors (classrooms, banks, health monitoring, business, virtual meetings), with the bank, business and real-time patient-monitoring detail in the lesson text; the student book's five fields remain the lesson's main set.

Bhutan's own way (Class VII, Literacy with ICT student book): Social media is taught hands-on — learners create a Facebook account, post with audience settings, and follow netiquette and an Acceptable Use Policy; backup is taught to Google Drive, and the end-of-year experience is shared on Facebook.

Libya's own way (Grade 12 Humanities, Information Technology student book): the Humanities final year pairs the social/legal side of ICT (data security, e-government, computer law and software licences) with a long multimedia-and-PowerPoint practical; the closing projects are Libyan — a deck about Libyan dishes and a deck about Libya's archaeological cities and landmarks.

Rwanda's own way (S3 ICT Learner's Book, Fountain): the same ten units as the S3 guide taught as long screenshot-by-screenshot practicals rooted in Rwandan school life (a GS Bumbogo parents'-meeting mail merge, an administration map of Rwanda built in ArcGIS, an environmental-protection presentation, 'Rwanda Nziza' hyperlink decks), closing with a complete Scratch platform game, Hard Journey.

Rwanda's own way (S3 ICT Teacher Guide, Fountain): the S3 guide (a different publisher from the KLB S1/S2 set) organises the year into four study areas — application software, GIS/spreadsheets/presentations, graphics and multimedia, networks and programming — ending in platform game programming in Scratch.
