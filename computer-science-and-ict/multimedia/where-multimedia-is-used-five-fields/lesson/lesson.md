# Where multimedia is used — five fields

## Do it first

Before reading on, think of the last week. Where did you meet a screen that mixed words, sound and moving pictures — at school, on a journey, at a clinic? List the places. This lesson sorts those meetings into five fields.

## Five fields, one combination

Multimedia is put to work wherever people meet electronic information. Five fields are walked through here: **education**, **entertainment**, **public places**, **collaboration** and **healthcare**. The same five media forms appear in each — what changes is the job they do.

In **education**, presentations and interactive graphics clarify ideas; group work and self-guided learning flourish. **Virtual learning** — teaching and learning online, with teacher and learners separated by place or time — becomes possible.

## Entertainment, public places and collaboration

**Entertainment** now travels: films, music and books can be enjoyed anywhere, on the move. In **public places**, stand-alone terminals and kiosks serve people in hotels, stations, malls, museums and libraries — and in churches, live video runs with song lyrics on large screens.

**Collaboration** links people across regions by chat, social networking and video. Its centrepiece is the **virtual meeting**: people sharing ideas by audio and video conferencing, screen sharing and webinars, as if they sat in one room.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: when you join a virtual meeting, test your microphone and camera before the meeting starts, not after it begins.

## Healthcare

In **healthcare**, staff store and send images for diagnosis, and multimedia carries medical education and training. **Telemedicine** is medical information exchanged electronically between locations to improve a patient's health. **Virtual surgery** lets surgeons practise operations on 3D models — with no patient and no cadaver — before the real operation.

The same combination that entertains on a bus here helps a doctor learn and a patient be treated from far away. That is why the field matters: the forms are the same, the stakes are not.

## Five sectors (teacher guide — evidence)

The same multimedia at work, told sector by sector:

- **Classrooms** — multimedia engages pupils who expect immediate information, and lets them work in groups, show knowledge in several ways and revise their own work.
- **Banks** — online and internet banking run on multimedia, and multimedia also teaches customers the bank's schemes.
- **Health** — patient and disease data are managed, and critical patients are monitored in real time: the screen alerts the doctor or nurse on duty when a condition changes.
- **Business** — training, marketing, advertising and product demos.
- **Virtual meetings** — real-time interaction over the internet with audio, video, chat and application sharing — and the wider virtual learning built on them.
