# Hands-on — Where multimedia is used — five fields

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Field walk
Walk through your school, home or street in your mind. Write down every screen or speaker you pass and sort each into one of the five fields. A screen that fits no field — say why.

## Design a kiosk
On paper, design a stand-alone kiosk for a bus station: what does it show, what sound does it make, what can a traveller touch or press? Label which media forms you used.
