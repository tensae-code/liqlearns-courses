# Where multimedia is used — five fields — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Give one way multimedia helps in education.
**Answer:** Presentations and interactive graphics clarify ideas; group work and self-guided learning flourish, and virtual learning becomes possible.

**2.** Why does virtual surgery use 3D models?
**Answer:** So surgeons can practise operations with no patient or cadaver at risk.

**3.** What does multimedia do in banks, in the guide's keyed answer?
**Answer:** Online and internet banking run on multimedia, and multimedia teaches customers the bank's schemes.

**4.** Name two things multimedia lets pupils do in classrooms, in the guide's keyed answer.
**Answer:** Any two of: work in groups, show knowledge in several ways, revise their own work (and it engages pupils who expect immediate information).
