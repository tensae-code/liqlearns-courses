# Where multimedia is used — five fields — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** Kiosks in hotels, stations and museums are multimedia in public places.
**Answer: True** — stand-alone terminals and kiosks.

**2.** Entertainment multimedia can only be enjoyed at home.
**Answer: False** — films, music and books anywhere, on the move.

**3.** In healthcare, staff store and send images for diagnosis.
**Answer: True** — one of the healthcare uses taught.
