# Where multimedia is used — five fields — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which list gives the five fields in this lesson?
a) education, entertainment, public places, collaboration, healthcare  b) education, farming, trade, sport, travel  c) text, audio, video, graphics, animation  d) school, home, office, market, field
**Answer: a) education, entertainment, public places, collaboration, healthcare** — the five fields of use — not the five media forms.

**2.** Virtual learning means —
a) watching any video  b) teaching and learning online with teacher and learners separated by place or time  c) learning alone from a book  d) a classroom with a big screen
**Answer: b) teaching and learning online with teacher and learners separated by place or time** — separation by place or time is the mark.

**3.** A virtual meeting mainly belongs to —
a) entertainment  b) healthcare  c) public places  d) collaboration
**Answer: d) collaboration** — people across regions working together.

**4.** Telemedicine is —
a) a television in a hospital  b) medical information exchanged electronically between locations to improve a patient's health  c) a robot doctor  d) practising surgery on a model
**Answer: b) medical information exchanged electronically between locations to improve a patient's health** — information exchanged to improve health.

**5.** Virtual surgery is practised on —
a) a real patient  b) a cadaver  c) 3D models  d) photographs only
**Answer: c) 3D models** — no patient and no cadaver.
