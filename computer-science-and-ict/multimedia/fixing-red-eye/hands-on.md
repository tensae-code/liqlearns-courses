# Hands-on — Fixing red eye

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Red-eye hunt
Look through family or school photos taken with flash indoors or at night. Find one with red pupils (or draw an eye with a red pupil). Mark the eye you would zoom to first.

## Paint it out on paper
Draw a large eye with a red pupil. With a dark pencil, 'paint' over the red in small strokes until it reads as a dark pupil — holding strokes over the red is what the Red Eye Tool does.
