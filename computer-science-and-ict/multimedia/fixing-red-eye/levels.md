# Fixing red eye — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do and explain the lesson's one thing — red eye — caused by the camera flash sitting right above the lens — is removed with the Red Eye Tool: zoom to the eye, then click on the red part and paint until the red disappears (in GIMP: select the iris, then Filters → Enhance → Red Eye Removal). Practice deepens with more items from the question files and harder corners of the same skill (new images, new values, the same steps), never with new content the lesson does not teach.
