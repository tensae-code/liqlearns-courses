Today: **Fixing red eye**.

Remember the one thing: red eye — caused by the camera flash sitting right above the lens — is removed with the Red Eye Tool: zoom to the eye, then click on the red part and paint until the red disappears (in GIMP: select the iris, then Filters → Enhance → Red Eye Removal).

Watch the lesson's pictures. Say each key word after me. Then do the Do It Yourself at the end of the lesson — with your own hands.
