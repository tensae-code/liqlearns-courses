# Fixing red eye

## Do it first

Look at any flash photo of people at night: the pupils glow red like a cat's. Before reading on, guess what causes it — the answer is in where the flash sits on the camera.

## The Photoshop route — the book's order

**Red eye** comes from the camera flash sitting right above the lens.

1. Open the photo.
2. With the **Zoom Tool**, drag a rectangle around the eye.
3. Make sure the default foreground/background colours are black and white.
4. Click and hold the Healing Brush button's small triangle and select the **Red Eye Tool**.
5. Click on the red part of the eye and paint, holding the mouse down — the red disappears.

## The GIMP alternative

In GIMP: select the boundary of the iris with the red pupil, then **Filters → Enhance → Red Eye Removal**.
