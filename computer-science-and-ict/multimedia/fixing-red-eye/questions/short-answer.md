# Fixing red eye — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Give the Photoshop red-eye steps in order.
**Answer:** Open the photo; drag a Zoom rectangle around the eye; make sure foreground/background are the default black and white; click and hold the Healing Brush triangle and select the Red Eye Tool; click on the red part and paint, holding the mouse down.

**2.** What causes red eye?
**Answer:** The camera flash sitting right above the lens.
