# Fixing red eye — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Red eye is caused by —
a) a dirty lens  b) the camera flash sitting right above the lens  c) low battery  d) a scratched photo
**Answer: b) the camera flash sitting right above the lens** — flash above the lens.

**2.** The Red Eye Tool is found by clicking and holding —
a) the Crop Tool  b) the Healing Brush button's small triangle  c) the Zoom Tool  d) the Menu Bar
**Answer: b) the Healing Brush button's small triangle** — the Healing Brush family.

**3.** Before painting, you zoom to the eye with —
a) the Hand Tool  b) the Zoom Tool, dragging a rectangle around the eye  c) the Move Tool  d) the Pen Tool
**Answer: b) the Zoom Tool, dragging a rectangle around the eye** — Zoom Tool rectangle.

**4.** While painting you —
a) tap once  b) click on the red part and paint, holding the mouse down  c) press Enter  d) drag the whole photo
**Answer: b) click on the red part and paint, holding the mouse down** — hold the mouse down and paint.

**5.** The GIMP route is —
a) Colours → Levels  b) Filters → Enhance → Red Eye Removal  c) Image → Crop  d) File → Export
**Answer: b) Filters → Enhance → Red Eye Removal** — Filters → Enhance → Red Eye Removal, after selecting the iris boundary.
