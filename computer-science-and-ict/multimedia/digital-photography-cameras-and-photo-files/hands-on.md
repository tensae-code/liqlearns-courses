# Hands-on — Digital Photography — Cameras and Photo Files

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
** Megapixel count** — From three cameras' resolutions (e.g. 6000×4000, 4000×3000, 1920×1080), compute each sensor's megapixels and rank the file sizes you expect.

## Task two
**Format decision** — For five jobs (a poster print, a class slideshow, an archive of originals, a web gallery, evidence photos) choose JPEG or RAW and defend each in one line.
