# Digital Photography — Cameras and Photo Files — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a digital camera make its picture on?**
**Answer: A sensor — a grid of light-sensitive elements, one per pixel**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a picture's resolution?**
**Answer: The grid's size — width × height in pixels, counted in megapixels**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does more resolution cost?**
**Answer: Bigger files (and it buys finer detail)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three controls over light.**
**Answer: The lens (gathering/focusing), the shutter time, and the sensitivity (ISO)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does higher ISO trade?**
**Answer: Brightness for noise — grain in the picture**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does JPEG do to a picture?**
**Answer: It compresses it — discarding fine detail — into a small, universal file**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does RAW keep?**
**Answer: The sensor's measurements nearly untouched — everything, for later developing**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which format is for sharing, which for working?**
**Answer: JPEG for sharing; RAW for working**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is EXIF metadata?**
**Answer: Data about the photo inside its file — camera, date, settings**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is chosen when a format is chosen?**
**Answer: What to keep — convenience, or everything**
