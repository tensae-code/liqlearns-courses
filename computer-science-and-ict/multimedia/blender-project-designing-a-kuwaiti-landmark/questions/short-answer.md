# Blender Project — Designing a Kuwaiti Landmark — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What landmark is modelled?**
**Answer: The Al-Shagaya renewable-energy station — Kuwait's solar and wind plant**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which modifier has its 'real home' in the panels?**
**Answer: Array**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two other unit crafts the model spends.**
**Answer: Mesh editing and materials/textures (also lighting, camera/render)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does 'integrated' mean here?**
**Answer: The whole unit spent at once, on the one product**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What closes the project?**
**Answer: An exhibition of the class's digital products**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does each maker present with their work?**
**Answer: Their choices — what was modelled, simplified, and what the render argues**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the products unit end in showing?**
**Answer: A product's last stage is its readers — private skills become public standards**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What subject does the landmark itself teach?**
**Answer: How Kuwait makes its power — renewable energy**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two units does this project sit across?**
**Answer: Unit 1's Blender course and Unit 2 Digital Products (the Projects Unit)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book sets this project?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 8 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 2**
