# Lesson plan — Blender Project — Designing a Kuwaiti Landmark

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Multimedia · **Lesson:** Blender Project — Designing a Kuwaiti Landmark
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Kuwait (2026-11-14) — in the title Multimedia on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Kuwait's student books (Ministry of Education, State of Kuwait), because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree and live lesson content were checked for the slug and for near matches before building.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Kuwait's integrated 3D project — modelling the Al-Shagaya renewable-energy station in Blender, and exhibiting the class's digital products
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The project — modelling Al-Shagaya** — The integrated project models the **Al-Shagaya renewable-energy station** — Kuwait's solar and wind plant — in Blender: its panels' arrays (the Array modifier's real home), its structures modelled and mesh-edited, materials and textures assigned, the scene lit and framed for its render. Integrated means the whole unit is spent at once, on a Kuwaiti subject: the pupil's country supplying both the landmark and the lesson — how a nation makes its power is Grade 8's final 3D subject.
2. **Exhibiting the class's digital products** — The project closes with an **exhibition of the class's digital products**: the models shown to an audience, each maker's choices — what was modelled, what was simplified, what the render argues — presented with the work. Kuwait's products unit always ends in showing, because a product's last stage is its readers: the exhibition is where the class's private skills become public standards, and where the next project quietly begins in a pupil who saw a better tower.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
