# Hands-on — Analysing a Film or Video in Class — A Method

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Run the method** — Take a three-minute film. Run all four steps as a class, keeping a visible “no opinions yet” rule during description. Time each step.

## Task two
**Settle it by rewatching** — During interpretation, when two pupils disagree about what happened, stop and rewatch the moment. Record how often the screen settled the argument.
