# Analysing a Film or Video in Class — A Method — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the method's order?**
**Answer: Watch whole, describe, analyse, interpret**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is banned during describing?**
**Answer: Opinions — only what is literally there**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does analysing examine?**
**Answer: How the elements work and what effects they build**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When may the class judge the film?**
**Answer: Last — at interpretation**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What survives argument?**
**Answer: Judgement built on description**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is the description built?**
**Answer: Together, on the board — any pupil may add or challenge**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What settles a memory argument?**
**Answer: A second viewing of the contested moment**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is the analysis shared out?**
**Answer: Elements assigned to groups**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must interpretation point back at?**
**Answer: Evidence on the screen**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the démarche make possible?**
**Answer: Any class analysing any film the same disciplined way**
