# Analysing a Film or Video in Class — A Method — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the method's order?** → **Watch whole, describe, analyse, interpret**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is banned during describing?** → **Opinions — only what is literally there**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does analysing examine?** → **How the elements work and what effects they build**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When may the class judge the film?** → **Last — at interpretation**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What survives argument?** → **Judgement built on description**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is the description built?** → **Together, on the board — any pupil may add or challenge**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What settles a memory argument?** → **A second viewing of the contested moment**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is the analysis shared out?** → **Elements assigned to groups**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must interpretation point back at?** → **Evidence on the screen**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the démarche make possible?** → **Any class analysing any film the same disciplined way**
