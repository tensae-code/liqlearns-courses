# GIMP — Meeting the Image Editor, and Layers — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is GIMP?**
**Answer: A free image editor — making and changing pictures, pixel by pixel and layer by layer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the screen's working parts.**
**Answer: The canvas, the toolbox, and the adjustment panels**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are preferences?**
**Answer: The program's own settings — how it behaves and shows itself**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why set preferences at the first meeting?**
**Answer: A producer's tool should be arranged before producing starts**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a layer?**
**Answer: A transparent sheet carrying part of the picture, stacked with others**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the eye see?**
**Answer: The stack as one image**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why do layers make design forgiving?**
**Answer: A part can be moved, hidden, edited or removed without touching other layers**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name three things layers might carry in one design.**
**Answer: Background, a person, and text (any sensible three)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which tool does Kuwait choose for design — and why does it matter that it is free?**
**Answer: GIMP — free software, so every pupil can hold the same professional tool**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 7 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 1**
