## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Meeting GIMP — screen and preferences** — **GIMP** is a free **image editor** — a program for making and changing pictures, pixel by pixel and layer by layer. Its **screen** gathers the working parts: the canvas where the image lives, the toolbox of instruments, and the panels where the current work is adjusted. Its **preferences** are the program's own settings — how it behaves and shows itself — and Kuwait has the pupil set them at the meeting, because a producer's tool should be arranged before the producing starts.
2. **Layers — the idea everything stands on** — A GIMP image is built in **layers**: transparent sheets stacked over each other, each carrying part of the picture — one the background, one a person, one the text — while the eye sees the stack as one image. Layers are why digital design is forgiving: a part can be moved, hidden, edited or removed **without touching the parts on other layers**. Every GIMP lesson after this one — blending, drawing, posters — is layers at work.
