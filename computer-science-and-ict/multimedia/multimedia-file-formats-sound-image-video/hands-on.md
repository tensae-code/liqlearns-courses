# Hands-on — Multimedia file formats — sound, image, video

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Shelf sort
Write the ten formats on ten slips. Shuffle, then sort them onto three drawn shelves — sound, image, video — against the clock. Repeat until the sort is clean twice in a row.

## Ending hunt
On a computer or phone you may use, find three real media files and read their endings. Place each on its shelf and write how you know.
