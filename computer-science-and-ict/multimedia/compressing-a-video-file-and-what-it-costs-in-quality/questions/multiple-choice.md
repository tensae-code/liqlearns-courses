# Compressing a Video File — and What It Costs in Quality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why is a video compressed?**
a) To make its file smaller — less storage, faster travel  b) The recipe that squeezes and un-squeezes the file  c) Quality — pictures soften and block, sound thins  d) Small enough to share, clear enough to watch
**Answer: a) To make its file smaller — less storage, faster travel**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does compression make a file smaller?**
a) A smaller file  b) By keeping less of the original picture and sound information  c) No — what is thrown away cannot come back  d) Its own codec on the computer
**Answer: b) By keeping less of the original picture and sound information**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the encoder throw away first?**
a) Quality — pictures soften and block, sound thins  b) Small enough to share, clear enough to watch  c) Detail the eye is least likely to miss  d) The file will not play until that codec is available
**Answer: c) Detail the eye is least likely to miss**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a codec?**
a) No — what is thrown away cannot come back  b) Its own codec on the computer  c) To make its file smaller — less storage, faster travel  d) The recipe that squeezes and un-squeezes the file
**Answer: d) The recipe that squeezes and un-squeezes the file**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does stronger compression always give?**
a) A smaller file  b) Small enough to share, clear enough to watch  c) The file will not play until that codec is available  d) By keeping less of the original picture and sound information
**Answer: a) A smaller file**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does strong compression cost?**
a) Its own codec on the computer  b) Quality — pictures soften and block, sound thins  c) To make its file smaller — less storage, faster travel  d) Detail the eye is least likely to miss
**Answer: b) Quality — pictures soften and block, sound thins**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Can thrown-away detail come back?**
a) The file will not play until that codec is available  b) By keeping less of the original picture and sound information  c) No — what is thrown away cannot come back  d) The recipe that squeezes and un-squeezes the file
**Answer: c) No — what is thrown away cannot come back**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What balance must the maker choose?**
a) To make its file smaller — less storage, faster travel  b) Detail the eye is least likely to miss  c) A smaller file  d) Small enough to share, clear enough to watch
**Answer: d) Small enough to share, clear enough to watch**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does a compressed file need in order to play?**
a) Its own codec on the computer  b) By keeping less of the original picture and sound information  c) The recipe that squeezes and un-squeezes the file  d) Quality — pictures soften and block, sound thins
**Answer: a) Its own codec on the computer**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What happens if the computer does not support the file's codec?**
a) Detail the eye is least likely to miss  b) The file will not play until that codec is available  c) A smaller file  d) No — what is thrown away cannot come back
**Answer: b) The file will not play until that codec is available**
