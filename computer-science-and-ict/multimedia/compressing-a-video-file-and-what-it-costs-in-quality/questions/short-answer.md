# Compressing a Video File — and What It Costs in Quality — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why is a video compressed?**
**Answer: To make its file smaller — less storage, faster travel**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does compression make a file smaller?**
**Answer: By keeping less of the original picture and sound information**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the encoder throw away first?**
**Answer: Detail the eye is least likely to miss**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a codec?**
**Answer: The recipe that squeezes and un-squeezes the file**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does stronger compression always give?**
**Answer: A smaller file**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does strong compression cost?**
**Answer: Quality — pictures soften and block, sound thins**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Can thrown-away detail come back?**
**Answer: No — what is thrown away cannot come back**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What balance must the maker choose?**
**Answer: Small enough to share, clear enough to watch**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does a compressed file need in order to play?**
**Answer: Its own codec on the computer**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What happens if the computer does not support the file's codec?**
**Answer: The file will not play until that codec is available**
