# Hands-on — Compressing a Video File — and What It Costs in Quality

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Squeeze and compare
Compress the same short clip twice — lightly and strongly. Compare the two file sizes, then watch both and name what quality the stronger one paid away.

## The codec test
Try to play a file in a format your player does not know. Write down exactly what happens, then find out which codec the file needs.
