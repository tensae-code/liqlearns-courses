# Multimedia production — the eight roles — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Which role would you give the job 'record and edit the narration'? Why?
**Answer:** The audio-video specialist — recording and editing narration, effects and music is that role's job.

**2.** Can one person hold two roles? What must not happen?
**Answer:** Yes, on a small project one person can hold two roles — but no role's work can be skipped.
