# Lesson plan — Multimedia production — the eight roles

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Multimedia · **Lesson:** Multimedia production — the eight roles
**Place in the curriculum:** Ethiopia Grade 10, Unit 4 — Image Processing and Multimedia (printed pp. 139–181).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** multimedia production tells a story by gathering text, photo and video elements and integrating them — work that usually takes a team of eight named roles.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on What multimedia is and The five components: production is where those components are gathered and integrated on purpose.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The five components of multimedia — what the team must gather. Recommended, never forced. A learner can land here directly; the lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define multimedia production and its two opening moves: gather, integrate.
2. Name the eight roles with each role's job.
3. Match any described job to its role.
## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.
