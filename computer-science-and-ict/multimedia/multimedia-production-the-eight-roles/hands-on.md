# Hands-on — Multimedia production — the eight roles

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Staff your project
Invent a two-minute presentation for your school. On paper, staff all eight roles from your class (names can repeat) and write each person's first task.

## Role cards
Make eight role cards. A partner reads a job description aloud from this lesson; you hold up the right card. Swap and repeat.
