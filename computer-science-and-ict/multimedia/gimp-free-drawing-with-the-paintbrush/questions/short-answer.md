# GIMP — Free Drawing with the Paintbrush — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the Paintbrush tool do?**
**Answer: Paints where the pointer moves — a stroke follows the hand**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three brush choices the painter makes.**
**Answer: Size, hardness and colour**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Is a paintbrush stroke plotted or free?**
**Answer: Free — the line is as the hand makes it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the set drawing activity?**
**Answer: The cat**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How are drawing and colouring arranged?**
**Answer: Drawing on one layer, colouring on another beneath it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why beneath, and why separate?**
**Answer: Colour can be corrected without smearing lines; lines mended without touching colour**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the structure let a beginner be?**
**Answer: Brave — every mark can be fixed in its own place**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the digital version of, in the lesson's image?**
**Answer: A light pencil and clean paint**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is drawing's first question, not the software's?**
**Answer: Whether the pupil will look at the subject and trust their stroke**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 7 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 1 — Free Drawing**
