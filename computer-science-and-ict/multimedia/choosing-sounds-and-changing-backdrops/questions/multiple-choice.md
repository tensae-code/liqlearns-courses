# Choosing Sounds and Changing Backdrops — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Choosing a sound well means picking the one that —**
a) fits the moment  b) is loudest  c) comes first in the gallery
**Answer: a) fits the moment**

**2. A backdrop should change —**
a) every second  b) never  c) when the story moves to a new place
**Answer: c) when the story moves to a new place**

**3. A sound should be tested —**
a) before the project exists  b) in the scene, not only in the gallery  c) only by its name
**Answer: b) in the scene, not only in the gallery**

**4. Rejecting a sound that fights the scene is —**
a) real multimedia judgement  b) wasted time  c) a block error
**Answer: a) real multimedia judgement**
