# Choosing Sounds and Changing Backdrops — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Explain, in your own words: choose a sound from the gallery that fits a scene and say why it fits.**
**Answer:** The learner's own explanation, true to the lesson: A scene is finished by its sound and its place: choose the sound that fits the moment from the gallery, and change the backdrop when the story moves — sound and place working together.

**2. A classmate gets this lesson's work wrong. What is the most likely fault, and how is it fixed?**
**Answer:** Scene feels wrong but blocks work → the fight is fit, not code: play the sound alone, look at the backdrop alone, and ask of each, "does this belong in this moment?"
