# Choosing Sounds and Changing Backdrops — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. Choose a sound from the gallery that fits a scene and say why it fits.**
**Answer: True** — This is one of the lesson's own outcomes.

**2. Any sound from the gallery is equally good for any moment in a scene.**
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite of this.

**3. Change the backdrop at the moment the story changes place.**
**Answer: True** — This is one of the lesson's own outcomes.
