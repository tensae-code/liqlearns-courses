# Hands-on — Choosing Sounds and Changing Backdrops

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Fit the sound
Take one scene moment. Try three sounds from the gallery, reject two with reasons, and keep the one that fits. A partner must guess your reasons.

## Right place, right moment
In a two-place story, set the backdrop change at the exact moment the story moves. Run it early and late once, then at the right moment, and feel the difference.
