# Hands-on — Editing Pictures with Filters — White Balance, Colour Grading and Sepia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Repair first
Take a photo with a yellow or blue cast. Repair it with the white-balance filter and compare before and after.

## One picture, three looks
Grade the repaired picture two ways — one warm, one cold — and make a third copy sepia. Name the mood of each.
