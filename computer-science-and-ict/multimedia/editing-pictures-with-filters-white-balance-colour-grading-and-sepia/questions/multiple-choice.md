# Editing Pictures with Filters — White Balance, Colour Grading and Sepia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What goes wrong when a camera records the wrong white balance?**
a) Whites come out yellow or blue and every colour shifts  b) Deliberately shifting colours and tones toward a chosen look  c) Washes it in warm browns — a modern photo with an old look  d) After the picture is correct
**Answer: a) Whites come out yellow or blue and every colour shifts**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the white-balance filter do?**
a) Warmer, colder, stronger or softer  b) It tells the editor what should be truly white, and the colours settle back to natural  c) A ready-made colour grading of one special kind  d) Its colours
**Answer: b) It tells the editor what should be truly white, and the colours settle back to natural**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which filter is the fixing filter, used first?**
a) Washes it in warm browns — a modern photo with an old look  b) After the picture is correct  c) The white-balance filter  d) No — each filter changes it in a different way
**Answer: c) The white-balance filter**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is colour grading?**
a) A ready-made colour grading of one special kind  b) Its colours  c) Whites come out yellow or blue and every colour shifts  d) Deliberately shifting colours and tones toward a chosen look
**Answer: d) Deliberately shifting colours and tones toward a chosen look**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What can a graded look be?**
a) Warmer, colder, stronger or softer  b) After the picture is correct  c) No — each filter changes it in a different way  d) It tells the editor what should be truly white, and the colours settle back to natural
**Answer: a) Warmer, colder, stronger or softer**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does sepia do to a picture?**
a) Its colours  b) Washes it in warm browns — a modern photo with an old look  c) Whites come out yellow or blue and every colour shifts  d) The white-balance filter
**Answer: b) Washes it in warm browns — a modern photo with an old look**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of filter is sepia?**
a) No — each filter changes it in a different way  b) It tells the editor what should be truly white, and the colours settle back to natural  c) A ready-made colour grading of one special kind  d) Deliberately shifting colours and tones toward a chosen look
**Answer: c) A ready-made colour grading of one special kind**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When are styling filters applied?**
a) Whites come out yellow or blue and every colour shifts  b) The white-balance filter  c) Warmer, colder, stronger or softer  d) After the picture is correct
**Answer: d) After the picture is correct**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What sets a film's mood, in this lesson?**
a) Its colours  b) It tells the editor what should be truly white, and the colours settle back to natural  c) Deliberately shifting colours and tones toward a chosen look  d) Washes it in warm browns — a modern photo with an old look
**Answer: a) Its colours**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Do filters change the same picture in the same way?**
a) The white-balance filter  b) No — each filter changes it in a different way  c) Warmer, colder, stronger or softer  d) A ready-made colour grading of one special kind
**Answer: b) No — each filter changes it in a different way**
