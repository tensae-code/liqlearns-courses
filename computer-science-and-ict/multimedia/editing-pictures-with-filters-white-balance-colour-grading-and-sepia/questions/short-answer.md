# Editing Pictures with Filters — White Balance, Colour Grading and Sepia — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What goes wrong when a camera records the wrong white balance?**
**Answer: Whites come out yellow or blue and every colour shifts**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the white-balance filter do?**
**Answer: It tells the editor what should be truly white, and the colours settle back to natural**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which filter is the fixing filter, used first?**
**Answer: The white-balance filter**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is colour grading?**
**Answer: Deliberately shifting colours and tones toward a chosen look**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What can a graded look be?**
**Answer: Warmer, colder, stronger or softer**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does sepia do to a picture?**
**Answer: Washes it in warm browns — a modern photo with an old look**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of filter is sepia?**
**Answer: A ready-made colour grading of one special kind**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When are styling filters applied?**
**Answer: After the picture is correct**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What sets a film's mood, in this lesson?**
**Answer: Its colours**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Do filters change the same picture in the same way?**
**Answer: No — each filter changes it in a different way**
