# Editing Pictures with Filters — White Balance, Colour Grading and Sepia — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What goes wrong when a camera records the wrong white balance?" is: Whites come out yellow or blue and every colour shifts**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the white-balance filter do?" is: The white-balance filter**
**Answer: False** — It tells the editor what should be truly white, and the colours settle back to natural

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which filter is the fixing filter, used first?" is: The white-balance filter**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is colour grading?" is: Warmer, colder, stronger or softer**
**Answer: False** — Deliberately shifting colours and tones toward a chosen look

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What can a graded look be?" is: Warmer, colder, stronger or softer**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does sepia do to a picture?" is: A ready-made colour grading of one special kind**
**Answer: False** — Washes it in warm browns — a modern photo with an old look

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What kind of filter is sepia?" is: A ready-made colour grading of one special kind**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When are styling filters applied?" is: Its colours**
**Answer: False** — After the picture is correct

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What sets a film's mood, in this lesson?" is: Its colours**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Do filters change the same picture in the same way?" is: Whites come out yellow or blue and every colour shifts**
**Answer: False** — No — each filter changes it in a different way
