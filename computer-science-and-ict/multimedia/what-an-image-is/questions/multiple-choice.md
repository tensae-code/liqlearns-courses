# What an image is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** An image is —
a) a sound recording  b) a visual representation of a person, animal or thing  c) a printed paragraph  d) a computer program
**Answer: b) a visual representation of a person, animal or thing** — that is the definition this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these is one of the four forms of an image?
a) a song  b) a photograph taken with a camera  c) a telephone call  d) a text message
**Answer: b) a photograph taken with a camera** — a camera photograph is the second form.

**3.** A picture turned into electronic form by an image scanner is —
a) a painting  b) a photograph  c) a scanned picture  d) a video
**Answer: c) a scanned picture** — scanning is the fourth form.

**4.** Which two forms live inside the computer?
a) painting and photograph  b) stored picture and scanned picture  c) painting and scanned picture  d) photograph and painting
**Answer: b) stored picture and scanned picture** — the stored and the scanned forms are electronic.

**5.** A picture created on a computer and kept as a file is —
a) a painting  b) a picture stored in electronic form  c) a photograph  d) a scanned picture
**Answer: b) a picture stored in electronic form** — created or copied and stored electronically.
