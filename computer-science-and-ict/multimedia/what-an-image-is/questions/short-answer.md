# What an image is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Name the four forms an image can take.
**Answer:** A painting; a photograph taken with a camera; a picture created or copied and stored in electronic form; a picture scanned through an image scanner.

**2.** Give one example of an image you saw today, and name its form.
**Answer:** Any real example with the right form named — e.g. a photo on a phone (stored picture), a drawing in a notebook (painting).
