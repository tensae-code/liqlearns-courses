# Hands-on — What an image is

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Four-form hunt
Walk around your classroom or home. Find one painting or drawing, one photograph, one picture stored on a phone or computer, and one printed picture that could be scanned. Show each one to a partner and name its form.

## Draw, then scan in your mind
Draw a small picture on paper. Now describe, step by step, what a scanner would do to turn your drawing into the fourth form — an electronic picture.
