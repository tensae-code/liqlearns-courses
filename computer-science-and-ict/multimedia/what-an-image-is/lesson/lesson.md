# What an image is

## Do it first

Look around you and point at four different pictures: one drawn or painted, one photograph, one picture on a phone or computer screen, and one picture in a printed book. Before reading on, say how each of the four was made.

## The four forms of an image

An image is a visual representation of a person, animal or thing. It reaches your eyes in four forms.

First, a **painting** — an image made by hand, with paint or pencil.

Second, a **photograph** — an image taken with a camera.

Third, a picture **created or copied and stored in electronic form** — made on a computer, or copied into one, and kept as a file.

Fourth, a picture **scanned through an image scanner** — a paper picture turned into an electronic one by the scanner.

Painting and photograph start in the real world. The stored picture and the scanned picture live inside the computer — and the rest of this title works with those.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the four forms an image can take, and give one example of each from your own home or school.

## Do It Yourself

1. Find one image of each of the four forms at home or at school and write down where you found it.
2. Say which two of the four forms live inside a computer, and which two start outside it.
