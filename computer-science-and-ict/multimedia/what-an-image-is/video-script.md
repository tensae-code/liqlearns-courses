Today: **What an image is**.

Remember the one thing: an image is a visual representation of a person, animal or thing — a painting, a camera photograph, a picture created or copied and stored in electronic form, or a picture scanned through an image scanner.

Watch the lesson's pictures. Say each key word after me. Then do the Do It Yourself at the end of the lesson — with your own hands.
