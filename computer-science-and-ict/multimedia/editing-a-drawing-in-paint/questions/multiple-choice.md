# Editing a Drawing in Paint — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What must you do to a part before editing it?**
a) Select it  b) Move, be copied, or be deleted  c) The rest of the drawing stays untouched  d) After the toolbox and the first actions in Paint
**Answer: a) Select it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three things a selected part can do.**
a) The rest of the drawing stays untouched  b) Move, be copied, or be deleted  c) After the toolbox and the first actions in Paint  d) The selection tool
**Answer: b) Move, be copied, or be deleted**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does selection protect?**
a) After the toolbox and the first actions in Paint  b) The selection tool  c) The rest of the drawing stays untouched  d) Flip or turn; stretch or shrink
**Answer: c) The rest of the drawing stays untouched**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does editing sit in the Grade 5 Paint sequence?**
a) The selection tool  b) Flip or turn; stretch or shrink  c) The background colour  d) After the toolbox and the first actions in Paint
**Answer: d) After the toolbox and the first actions in Paint**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which tool selects a part?**
a) The selection tool  b) Flip or turn; stretch or shrink  c) The background colour  d) Try the change on a copy or be ready to undo
**Answer: a) The selection tool**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name two changes of shape a selected part can take.**
a) The background colour  b) Flip or turn; stretch or shrink  c) Try the change on a copy or be ready to undo  d) One step at a time
**Answer: b) Flip or turn; stretch or shrink**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the eraser paint in?**
a) Try the change on a copy or be ready to undo  b) One step at a time  c) The background colour  d) Rubbing out small mistakes
**Answer: c) The background colour**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is editing's safety rule?**
a) One step at a time  b) Rubbing out small mistakes  c) Select it  d) Try the change on a copy or be ready to undo
**Answer: d) Try the change on a copy or be ready to undo**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How far back does undo reach in the book's Paint?**
a) One step at a time  b) Rubbing out small mistakes  c) Select it  d) Move, be copied, or be deleted
**Answer: a) One step at a time**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the eraser for?**
a) Select it  b) Rubbing out small mistakes  c) Move, be copied, or be deleted  d) The rest of the drawing stays untouched
**Answer: b) Rubbing out small mistakes**
