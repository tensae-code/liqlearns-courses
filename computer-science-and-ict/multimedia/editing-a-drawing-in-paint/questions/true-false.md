# Editing a Drawing in Paint — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What must you do to a part before editing it?" is: Select it**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name three things a selected part can do." is: The rest of the drawing stays untouched**
**Answer: False** — Move, be copied, or be deleted

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does selection protect?" is: The rest of the drawing stays untouched**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does editing sit in the Grade 5 Paint sequence?" is: The selection tool**
**Answer: False** — After the toolbox and the first actions in Paint

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which tool selects a part?" is: The selection tool**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name two changes of shape a selected part can take." is: The background colour**
**Answer: False** — Flip or turn; stretch or shrink

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the eraser paint in?" is: The background colour**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is editing's safety rule?" is: One step at a time**
**Answer: False** — Try the change on a copy or be ready to undo

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How far back does undo reach in the book's Paint?" is: One step at a time**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the eraser for?" is: Select it**
**Answer: False** — Rubbing out small mistakes
