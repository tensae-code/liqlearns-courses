# Editing a Drawing in Paint

## Do it first

Drawing is half the work; fixing is the other half. Uzbekistan's Grade 5 book gives editing its own Paint lesson: select, move, copy, turn and erase — changing a drawing without starting again.

## Sub lesson 1 — Selecting and moving a part

To edit, first **select** a part of the drawing with the selection tool — then the part can be **moved** to a new place, **copied** so it appears twice, or **deleted** without touching the rest. The Grade 5 book places editing after the toolbox and the first actions in Paint: you cannot sensibly edit what you cannot yet draw.

## Sub lesson 2 — Turning, stretching and the eraser

A selected part can also be **flipped or turned**, and **stretched or shrunk** to a new size; small mistakes are rubbed out with the **eraser**, which paints in the background colour. Editing's rule is the same as on paper, only kinder: try the change on a copy or be ready to undo — Paint forgives, but only one step back at a time in the book's version.
