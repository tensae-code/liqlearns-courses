## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Selecting and moving a part** — To edit, first **select** a part of the drawing with the selection tool — then the part can be **moved** to a new place, **copied** so it appears twice, or **deleted** without touching the rest. The Grade 5 book places editing after the toolbox and the first actions in Paint: you cannot sensibly edit what you cannot yet draw.
2. **Turning, stretching and the eraser** — A selected part can also be **flipped or turned**, and **stretched or shrunk** to a new size; small mistakes are rubbed out with the **eraser**, which paints in the background colour. Editing's rule is the same as on paper, only kinder: try the change on a copy or be ready to undo — Paint forgives, but only one step back at a time in the book's version.
