Nothing in this lesson claims a page the placements do not give; the lesson's content is the placements' content, re-expressed for the learner, with the book's own step orders, examples and Ethiopian practicals kept. Procedures are taught exactly as the book teaches them — Photoshop first, with the GIMP alternative where the book notes it.

Mauritania's own way (Grade 8): The second year is organised as five applied units of 35 thirty-minute fiches — image processing first (Paint/GIMP/Photoshop, bitmap vs vector), then internet ethics, a full Excel data-processing unit ending in a practical project, web source-checking and responsible downloading, and finally email — each unit closed by evaluation and support fiches with full answer keys at the end.

Vietnam's own way (Grade 8 Tin học 8): Grade 8 opens with a history of calculating tools (abacus, Pascal's Pascaline, computer generations) and its applied theme is a genuine fork — track a does advanced Word/PowerPoint, track b learns photo editing in GIMP; programming is Scratch again, but now with data representation and a named debugging lesson (Gỡ lỗi).

Vietnam's own way (Grade 10 Tin học 10): the Grade 10 book is one shared book for both orientations (ICT-coloured and CS-coloured lesson title bands tell each track its own lessons, per its preface); its data strand is unusually deep for school level — binary integers, logic data, sound and image data — and its programming strand is a complete first Python course from variables to functions, scope, error recognition and testing.

Vietnam's own way (Grade 11 Chuyên đề Tin học 11 Ứng dụng): a media-production volume in three crafts — vector decoration design in Inkscape (a postcard for a national tourism-design contest), cartoon-film making, and animated-GIF editing in a photo editor, layers first.
