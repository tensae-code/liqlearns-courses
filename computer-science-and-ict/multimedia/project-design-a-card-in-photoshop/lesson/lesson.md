# Project: design a card in Photoshop

## Do it first

You have the whole unit's tools now — canvas, toolbox, crops, corrections, sharpening. Before reading on, sketch on paper the card you would make for yourself: what picture, what logo, what words, laid out how?

## Sub lesson 1 — A business card for yourself

The Mini Project: design a **business card for yourself**, containing:

- your image,
- a business logo,
- the business name,
- contact information.

Build it on a new canvas (the Create New lesson), place the image (crop it to size first), and set the words with the Type tool.

[Picture 20541 — a finished business card]

## Sub lesson 2 — A promotional card for your place of birth

The review Project Work: design a **promotional card for your place of birth**, with an image of a known building, a common animal or a renowned person, plus brief text.

The section practicals belong with these projects: crop the Activity 4.1 images and compare the crop methods; correct colour problems and save the original and corrected versions together.

## Say it to your companion

Show your companion your business-card sketch and name its four required contents; then pitch your promotional card's image in one sentence.

## Do It Yourself

1. Make the business card: your image, a logo, the business name, contact information — laid out and saved.
2. Make the promotional card for your place of birth: one strong image (known building, common animal or renowned person) plus brief text.
