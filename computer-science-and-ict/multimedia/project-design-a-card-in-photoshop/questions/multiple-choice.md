# Project: design a card in Photoshop — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A business card in this project must contain —
a) a map  b) your image, a business logo, the business name and contact information  c) a price list  d) a full letter
**Answer: b) your image, a business logo, the business name and contact information** — the four contents.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The business card is designed for —
a) your teacher  b) yourself  c) a famous person  d) the school
**Answer: b) yourself** — for yourself — the Mini Project.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The promotional card promotes —
a) a business  b) your place of birth  c) a computer program  d) a football team
**Answer: b) your place of birth** — your place of birth.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The promotional card's image shows —
a) a known building, a common animal or a renowned person  b) a screenshot  c) a chart  d) a keyboard
**Answer: a) a known building, a common animal or a renowned person** — one of the three named subjects.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Besides the image, the promotional card carries —
a) a long essay  b) brief text  c) a song  d) page numbers
**Answer: b) brief text** — brief text.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** The section practicals paired with these projects include —
a) typing practice  b) cropping the Activity 4.1 images and comparing crop methods  c) making spreadsheets  d) drawing networks
**Answer: b) cropping the Activity 4.1 images and comparing crop methods** — crop comparison and colour correction, saved together.
