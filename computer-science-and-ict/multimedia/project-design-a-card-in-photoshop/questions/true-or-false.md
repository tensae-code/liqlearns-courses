# Project: design a card in Photoshop — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A business logo is one of the card's required contents.
**Answer: True** — image, logo, name, contacts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The promotional card's text should be long and detailed.
**Answer: False** — brief text.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Colour corrections in the practicals are saved with original and corrected versions together.
**Answer: True** — side by side, as with the flash-fault practical.
