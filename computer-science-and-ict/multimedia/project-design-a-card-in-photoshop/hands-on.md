# Hands-on — Project: design a card in Photoshop

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Card on paper first
Cut card-sized paper. Draw your business card completely by hand — image box, logo, name, contacts — before touching Photoshop. A planned card builds twice as fast.

## Promote your birthplace
Choose the one image that says your place of birth best — a known building, a common animal, a renowned person. Write the brief text that will sit with it: five to ten words only, as a real promotional card carries.
