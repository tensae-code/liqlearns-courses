# Multimedia hardware and software requirements — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Why does a multimedia machine need a sound card?
**Answer:** Because audio is one of the media types the system must generate and play; the sound card serves the audio component.

**2.** What makes virtual reality 'immersive'?
**Answer:** The user works inside a computer-generated 3D environment through headphones, goggles and data gloves, feeling inside the scene rather than in front of it.
