The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **What a multimedia computer needs**.

Six parts. A strong brain — the CPU. Memory — 1 GB of RAM. A good screen. A video card for moving pictures. A sound card for sound. A CD-ROM drive for the big files.

And five kinds of programs: one for words, one for pictures, one for animation, one for sound, one for video.

Every part serves a medium. Name the part — name its medium.
