# Hands-on — Multimedia hardware and software requirements

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Part-to-medium matching cards
Make twelve cards: six hardware parts, five software kinds, one virtual-reality set. Lay a component card (text, graphics, audio, video, animation) down and match every card that serves it.

## Check a real machine
On any school or home computer you may use, find its RAM size and say whether it meets the book's floor — and which parts you cannot check from outside, and why.
