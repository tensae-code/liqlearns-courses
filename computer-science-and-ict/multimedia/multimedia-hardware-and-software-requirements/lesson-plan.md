# Lesson plan — Multimedia hardware and software requirements

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Multimedia · **Lesson:** Multimedia hardware and software requirements
**Place in the curriculum:** Ethiopia Grade 10, Unit 4 — Image Processing and Multimedia (printed pp. 139–181).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a multimedia system needs a stated floor of hardware — Pentium-class CPU, 1 GB RAM, SVGA monitor, video card, sound card, CD-ROM drive — and five kinds of software.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The five components of multimedia: each requirement exists because one of the components must be generated, stored, shown or heard.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The five components of multimedia — the jobs each part must serve. Recommended, never forced. A learner can land here directly; the lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the hardware floor exactly as taught.
2. Match the five software kinds to their named programs.
3. Define 2D animation, 3D animation and virtual reality.
## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Swaps video card and sound card jobs → the card is named for the medium it serves: video card → moving pictures; sound card → audio.
- Treats the floor as today's shopping list → see the Tip: the floor is the book's stated minimum; the lasting skill is matching part to medium.
