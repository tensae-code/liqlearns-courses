# Blender — Editing a Mesh: Loop Cut and Extrude — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a mesh made of?**
**Answer: Vertices (points), edges (lines between them) and faces (the surfaces they bound)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Object Mode treat?**
**Answer: The object whole — moved, scaled, rotated in the scene**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does Edit Mode open?**
**Answer: The mesh itself — its parts selectable and movable**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the set build?**
**Answer: The water tower — an edited cylinder**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a Loop Cut do?**
**Answer: Slices a new ring of edges around the mesh**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Slide do to the cut?**
**Answer: Moves the cut along the surface, to where the design needs it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does Extrude do?**
**Answer: Pulls a face outward (or inward) into new solid**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Give an extrusion example.**
**Answer: The tank's top, a building's tower, a cup's handle (any one)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) State the two verbs of mesh modelling.**
**Answer: Cut to prepare, extrude to grow**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 8 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 1 — Editing a Mesh Object**
