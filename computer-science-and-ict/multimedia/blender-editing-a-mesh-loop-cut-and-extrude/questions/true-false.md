# Blender — Editing a Mesh: Loop Cut and Extrude — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Vertices (points), edges (lines between them) and faces (the surfaces they bound)**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The mesh itself — its parts selectable and movable** — as the answer to: What does Object Mode treat?
**Answer: False — The object whole — moved, scaled, rotated in the scene**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The mesh itself — its parts selectable and movable**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Slices a new ring of edges around the mesh** — as the answer to: What is the set build?
**Answer: False — The water tower — an edited cylinder**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Slices a new ring of edges around the mesh**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Pulls a face outward (or inward) into new solid** — as the answer to: What does Slide do to the cut?
**Answer: False — Moves the cut along the surface, to where the design needs it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Pulls a face outward (or inward) into new solid**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Cut to prepare, extrude to grow** — as the answer to: Give an extrusion example.
**Answer: False — The tank's top, a building's tower, a cup's handle (any one)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Cut to prepare, extrude to grow**
**Answer: True**
