# Video script — Blender — Editing a Mesh: Loop Cut and Extrude

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Blender works on a solid in two states. **Object Mode** treats the object whole — move, scale, rotate it in the scene. **Edit Mode** opens it: the object is a **mesh** of **vertices** (points), **edges** (lines between them) and **faces** (the surfaces they bound), each selectable and movable. Modelling is the second mode's craft: the water tower is a cylinder whose mesh has been edited — its tank widened, its legs drawn down — not a tower found in any menu.
The **Loop Cut** slices a new ring of edges **around** the mesh — and **Slide** moves the cut along the surface — giving the mesh a new place to bend or widen exactly where the design needs it: the tower's tank gets its rim from a cut. **Extrude** pulls a face **outward** (or inward) into new solid — the tank's top, a building's tower, a handle from a cup are extrusions. Cut to prepare, extrude to grow: the two verbs of mesh modelling in one lesson.
