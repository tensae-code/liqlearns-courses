# Vector Graphics Editing in Illustrator — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What holds a vector path?**
a) Nodes (anchor points) joined by segments  b) Bend the segment into a curve  c) Move its node  d) Pull the node's handle (control point)
**Answer: a) Nodes (anchor points) joined by segments**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do control points do?**
a) Move its node  b) Bend the segment into a curve  c) Pull the node's handle (control point)  d) It is re-described, not repainted
**Answer: b) Bend the segment into a curve**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How do you move a shape's corner?**
a) Pull the node's handle (control point)  b) It is re-described, not repainted  c) Move its node  d) Nodes (anchor points) joined by segments
**Answer: c) Move its node**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How do you bow a curve?**
a) It is re-described, not repainted  b) Nodes (anchor points) joined by segments  c) Bend the segment into a curve  d) Pull the node's handle (control point)
**Answer: d) Pull the node's handle (control point)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to the drawing when you edit nodes?**
a) It is re-described, not repainted  b) Nodes (anchor points) joined by segments  c) Bend the segment into a curve  d) Move its node
**Answer: a) It is re-described, not repainted**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a fill do?**
a) Flat colour and gradient (or pattern)  b) Colours the inside of a closed shape  c) Bend and shadow the whole object  d) Set inside it, flowing to its boundary, or along a path
**Answer: b) Colours the inside of a closed shape**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two kinds of fill.**
a) Bend and shadow the whole object  b) Set inside it, flowing to its boundary, or along a path  c) Flat colour and gradient (or pattern)  d) Shape first with nodes, then fill, then effects, then words
**Answer: c) Flat colour and gradient (or pattern)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do effects do to an object?**
a) Set inside it, flowing to its boundary, or along a path  b) Shape first with nodes, then fill, then effects, then words  c) Colours the inside of a closed shape  d) Bend and shadow the whole object
**Answer: d) Bend and shadow the whole object**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How can text sit in a shape?**
a) Set inside it, flowing to its boundary, or along a path  b) Shape first with nodes, then fill, then effects, then words  c) Colours the inside of a closed shape  d) Flat colour and gradient (or pattern)
**Answer: a) Set inside it, flowing to its boundary, or along a path**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's practical order?**
a) Colours the inside of a closed shape  b) Shape first with nodes, then fill, then effects, then words  c) Flat colour and gradient (or pattern)  d) Bend and shadow the whole object
**Answer: b) Shape first with nodes, then fill, then effects, then words**
