# Vector Graphics Editing in Illustrator — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What holds a vector path?" is: Nodes (anchor points) joined by segments**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do control points do?" is: Nodes (anchor points) joined by segments**
**Answer: False** — Bend the segment into a curve

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How do you move a shape's corner?" is: Move its node**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How do you bow a curve?" is: Nodes (anchor points) joined by segments**
**Answer: False** — Pull the node's handle (control point)

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What happens to the drawing when you edit nodes?" is: It is re-described, not repainted**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does a fill do?" is: Flat colour and gradient (or pattern)**
**Answer: False** — Colours the inside of a closed shape

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name two kinds of fill." is: Flat colour and gradient (or pattern)**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do effects do to an object?" is: Colours the inside of a closed shape**
**Answer: False** — Bend and shadow the whole object

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How can text sit in a shape?" is: Set inside it, flowing to its boundary, or along a path**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the book's practical order?" is: Colours the inside of a closed shape**
**Answer: False** — Shape first with nodes, then fill, then effects, then words
