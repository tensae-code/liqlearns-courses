# Vector Graphics Editing in Illustrator — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What holds a vector path?**
**Answer: Nodes (anchor points) joined by segments**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do control points do?**
**Answer: Bend the segment into a curve**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How do you move a shape's corner?**
**Answer: Move its node**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How do you bow a curve?**
**Answer: Pull the node's handle (control point)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to the drawing when you edit nodes?**
**Answer: It is re-described, not repainted**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a fill do?**
**Answer: Colours the inside of a closed shape**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two kinds of fill.**
**Answer: Flat colour and gradient (or pattern)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do effects do to an object?**
**Answer: Bend and shadow the whole object**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How can text sit in a shape?**
**Answer: Set inside it, flowing to its boundary, or along a path**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's practical order?**
**Answer: Shape first with nodes, then fill, then effects, then words**
