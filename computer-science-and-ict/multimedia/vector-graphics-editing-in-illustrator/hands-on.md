# Hands-on — Vector Graphics Editing in Illustrator

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Bend one curve', 'Draw one curve on paper and mark its two nodes and two handles. Redraw it three times, moving one handle each time, and write what changed in the curve each time.')

## Task two
('Shape, fill, words', 'Design a badge for a school club on paper in the book's order: draw the shape and its nodes, choose the fill, add one effect, then fit the club's name inside the shape. Label each stage.')
