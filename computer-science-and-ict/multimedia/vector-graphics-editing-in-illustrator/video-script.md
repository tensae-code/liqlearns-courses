# Video script — Vector Graphics Editing in Illustrator

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A vector path is held by **nodes (anchor points)** joined by segments. Each node carries **control points** — handles that bend the segment into a **curve**: move a node and the shape's corner moves; pull a handle and the curve bows. Editing a vector graphic *is* this work — choosing the node, moving it, and shaping its handles until the line says what you meant. Nothing is repainted; the drawing is **re-described**.
On the reshaped paths, Illustrator builds the finished graphic: a **fill** colours the inside of a closed shape — flat colour, gradient or pattern; **effects** bend and shadow the whole object; and **text can be set inside a shape**, flowing to its boundary, or along a path. Rwanda's S3 practical order is the professional one: **shape first with nodes, then fill, then effects, then words** — decoration never rescues a wrong shape.
