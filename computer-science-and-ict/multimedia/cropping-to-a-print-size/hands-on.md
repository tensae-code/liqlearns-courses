# Hands-on — Cropping to a print size

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Cardboard frame
Cut a frame from cardboard with an 8×10-proportioned hole (e.g. 8 cm × 10 cm). Move and scale your view of a picture through it — the shape never changes, only the size. That is the locked ratio.

## Two papers test
Draw one photo twice. Crop one copy freely into a long thin shape and one with an 8×10 frame. Which one fits the photo paper? Say why.
