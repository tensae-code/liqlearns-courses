# Cropping to a print size

## Do it first

You have a photo to print on 8×10 paper. If you crop freely, the print may not fit the paper. Before reading on, say how you could make sure the cropped piece matches the paper's shape before you cut.

## The locked-ratio crop, step by step

For printing on standard photo paper — the book's example is **8×10**:

1. Open the image and select the **Crop Tool**.
2. Enter the **Width and Height** values in the options bar.
3. Drag the cropping border — it stays **constrained to those proportions**: whatever its size, the area inside will fit the 8×10 paper.
4. Press **Enter** — or the confirm tick at the right of the options bar.
