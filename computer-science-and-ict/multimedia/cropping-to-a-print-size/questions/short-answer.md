# Cropping to a print size — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1.** Why lock the proportions when cropping for print?
**Answer:** So that whatever size the border is dragged to, the area inside fits the standard photo paper exactly.

**2.** Give the steps of the print-size crop in order.
**Answer:** Open the image; select the Crop Tool; enter Width and Height in the options bar; drag the border; press Enter or the confirm tick.
