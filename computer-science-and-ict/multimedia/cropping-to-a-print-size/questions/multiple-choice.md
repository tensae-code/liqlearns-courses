# Cropping to a print size — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The Width and Height are entered in —
a) the Layer Palette  b) the options bar  c) the File menu  d) the Rulers
**Answer: b) the options bar** — the Crop Tool's options bar.

**2.** With Width and Height set, the border —
a) changes shape freely  b) stays constrained to those proportions  c) cannot move  d) disappears
**Answer: b) stays constrained to those proportions** — locked proportions.

**3.** The book's example print size is —
a) 4×6  b) 8×10  c) A4  d) 5×7
**Answer: b) 8×10** — 8×10.

**4.** The crop can also be confirmed by —
a) the confirm tick at the right of the options bar  b) double-clicking  c) Esc  d) the Zoom tool
**Answer: a) the confirm tick at the right of the options bar** — Enter or the confirm tick.

**5.** This crop is for —
a) web banners  b) printing on standard photo paper  c) video  d) text documents
**Answer: b) printing on standard photo paper** — standard photo paper.
