Today: **Cropping to a print size**.

Remember the one thing: to crop for standard photo paper, enter the Width and Height in the Crop Tool's options bar — the border then stays locked to those proportions whatever size it is dragged to.

Watch the lesson's pictures. Say each key word after me. Then do the Do It Yourself at the end of the lesson — with your own hands.
