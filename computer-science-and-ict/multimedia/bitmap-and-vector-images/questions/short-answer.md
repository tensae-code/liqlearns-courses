# Bitmap and Vector Images — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a bitmap image built from?**
**Answer: Pixels — tiny coloured squares in a grid**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when a bitmap is stretched bigger?**
**Answer: The squares show and the picture turns blocky and blurred**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which bitmap format suits photographs?**
**Answer: JPEG**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which format suits sharp edges or transparency?**
**Answer: PNG**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is GIF used for?**
**Answer: Simple pictures and short animations**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a vector image store?**
**Answer: The rules for its shapes — lines, curves and fills**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why does a vector stay sharp at any size?**
**Answer: The computer redraws it perfectly from its rules at each size**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What kinds of pictures are drawn as vectors?**
**Answer: Logos, diagrams and lettering**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which editors work mainly with bitmaps?**
**Answer: GIMP and Photoshop**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Can a bitmap be turned truly back into a vector?**
**Answer: No — a vector can become a bitmap, but not the other way**
