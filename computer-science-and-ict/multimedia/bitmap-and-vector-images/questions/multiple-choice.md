# Bitmap and Vector Images — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a bitmap image built from?**
a) Pixels — tiny coloured squares in a grid  b) The squares show and the picture turns blocky and blurred  c) Simple pictures and short animations  d) Logos, diagrams and lettering
**Answer: a) Pixels — tiny coloured squares in a grid**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when a bitmap is stretched bigger?**
a) JPEG  b) The squares show and the picture turns blocky and blurred  c) The rules for its shapes — lines, curves and fills  d) GIMP and Photoshop
**Answer: b) The squares show and the picture turns blocky and blurred**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which bitmap format suits photographs?**
a) PNG  b) The computer redraws it perfectly from its rules at each size  c) JPEG  d) No — a vector can become a bitmap, but not the other way
**Answer: c) JPEG**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which format suits sharp edges or transparency?**
a) Simple pictures and short animations  b) Logos, diagrams and lettering  c) Pixels — tiny coloured squares in a grid  d) PNG
**Answer: d) PNG**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is GIF used for?**
a) Simple pictures and short animations  b) The rules for its shapes — lines, curves and fills  c) GIMP and Photoshop  d) The squares show and the picture turns blocky and blurred
**Answer: a) Simple pictures and short animations**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a vector image store?**
a) The computer redraws it perfectly from its rules at each size  b) The rules for its shapes — lines, curves and fills  c) No — a vector can become a bitmap, but not the other way  d) JPEG
**Answer: b) The rules for its shapes — lines, curves and fills**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why does a vector stay sharp at any size?**
a) Logos, diagrams and lettering  b) Pixels — tiny coloured squares in a grid  c) The computer redraws it perfectly from its rules at each size  d) PNG
**Answer: c) The computer redraws it perfectly from its rules at each size**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What kinds of pictures are drawn as vectors?**
a) GIMP and Photoshop  b) The squares show and the picture turns blocky and blurred  c) Simple pictures and short animations  d) Logos, diagrams and lettering
**Answer: d) Logos, diagrams and lettering**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which editors work mainly with bitmaps?**
a) GIMP and Photoshop  b) No — a vector can become a bitmap, but not the other way  c) JPEG  d) The rules for its shapes — lines, curves and fills
**Answer: a) GIMP and Photoshop**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Can a bitmap be turned truly back into a vector?**
a) Pixels — tiny coloured squares in a grid  b) No — a vector can become a bitmap, but not the other way  c) PNG  d) The computer redraws it perfectly from its rules at each size
**Answer: b) No — a vector can become a bitmap, but not the other way**
