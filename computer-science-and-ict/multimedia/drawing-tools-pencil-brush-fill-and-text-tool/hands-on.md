# Hands-on — Drawing Tools — Pencil, Brush, Fill and Text Tool

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Four tools, four marks', 'In the graficador or Paint, make one small drawing that uses all four tools: a pencil outline, brush colouring, a filled shape and a written name. Say which mark each tool made.')

## Task two
('The escaping fill', "Draw a shape with a small gap in its outline and fill it: watch the colour escape. Close the gap and fill again, and say in one sentence what the fill needs.")
