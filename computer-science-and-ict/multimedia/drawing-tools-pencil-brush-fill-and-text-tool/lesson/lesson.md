# Drawing Tools — Pencil, Brush, Fill and Text Tool

## Do it first

Cuba's youngest pupils meet the computer through drawing. Before letters and documents, the graficador's tools are taught one by one — what each tool is for, and the mark it makes. This lesson is those tools.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pencil and brush — the free-drawing tools

The **pencil** draws a **thin line that follows your hand exactly** — the tool for outlines, details and writing-like strokes. The **brush** paints a **wider, softer stroke**: it covers more with each pass and is the tool for colouring areas by hand and for bold marks. Both draw only while the mouse button is held, and both take the colour chosen before drawing. Cuba's Grade 2 guide introduces the graficador's tools as the child's first real work on the computer: choose the tool, choose the colour, then draw.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fill and text — colour poured, words placed

The **fill** (paint bucket) **pours colour into a closed area** with one click: click inside a shape and its whole inside takes the colour — and if the outline has a gap, the colour escapes through it. The **text tool** writes **words inside the drawing**: choose it, click where the words belong, and type — a name under a picture, a title on a poster. Cuba's guides name the text tool inside the same tool lesson as pencil and brush, because a finished school drawing carries both marks and words.

## Do It Yourself

1. Show it, don't just say it: A thin line that follows your hand exactly. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
2. Show it, don't just say it: Outlines, details and writing-like strokes. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
3. Show it, don't just say it: Pours colour into a closed area with one click. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
4. Show it, don't just say it: Closed, with no gap in its outline. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
