# Drawing Tools — Pencil, Brush, Fill and Text Tool — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the pencil draw?" is: A thin line that follows your hand exactly**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the pencil the tool for?" is: Wider and softer, covering more with each pass**
**Answer: False** — Outlines, details and writing-like strokes

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How does the brush's stroke differ from the pencil's?" is: Wider and softer, covering more with each pass**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "When do the pencil and brush draw?" is: The tool and the colour**
**Answer: False** — While the mouse button is held

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is chosen before drawing?" is: The tool and the colour**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the fill do?" is: Closed, with no gap in its outline**
**Answer: False** — Pours colour into a closed area with one click

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What must a shape be for the fill to stay inside?" is: Closed, with no gap in its outline**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the text tool do?" is: Choose it, click the place, and type**
**Answer: False** — Writes words inside the drawing

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What are the steps with the text tool?" is: Choose it, click the place, and type**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What can a finished drawing carry?" is: A thin line that follows your hand exactly**
**Answer: False** — Both marks and words
