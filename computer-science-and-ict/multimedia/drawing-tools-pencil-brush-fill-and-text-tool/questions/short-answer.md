# Drawing Tools — Pencil, Brush, Fill and Text Tool — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the pencil draw?**
**Answer: A thin line that follows your hand exactly**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the pencil the tool for?**
**Answer: Outlines, details and writing-like strokes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does the brush's stroke differ from the pencil's?**
**Answer: Wider and softer, covering more with each pass**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When do the pencil and brush draw?**
**Answer: While the mouse button is held**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is chosen before drawing?**
**Answer: The tool and the colour**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the fill do?**
**Answer: Pours colour into a closed area with one click**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must a shape be for the fill to stay inside?**
**Answer: Closed, with no gap in its outline**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the text tool do?**
**Answer: Writes words inside the drawing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are the steps with the text tool?**
**Answer: Choose it, click the place, and type**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What can a finished drawing carry?**
**Answer: Both marks and words**
