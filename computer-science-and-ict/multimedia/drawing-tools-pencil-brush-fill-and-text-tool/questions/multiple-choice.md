# Drawing Tools — Pencil, Brush, Fill and Text Tool — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the pencil draw?**
a) A thin line that follows your hand exactly  b) Outlines, details and writing-like strokes  c) Wider and softer, covering more with each pass  d) While the mouse button is held
**Answer: a) A thin line that follows your hand exactly**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the pencil the tool for?**
a) Wider and softer, covering more with each pass  b) Outlines, details and writing-like strokes  c) While the mouse button is held  d) The tool and the colour
**Answer: b) Outlines, details and writing-like strokes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does the brush's stroke differ from the pencil's?**
a) While the mouse button is held  b) The tool and the colour  c) Wider and softer, covering more with each pass  d) Pours colour into a closed area with one click
**Answer: c) Wider and softer, covering more with each pass**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When do the pencil and brush draw?**
a) The tool and the colour  b) Pours colour into a closed area with one click  c) Closed, with no gap in its outline  d) While the mouse button is held
**Answer: d) While the mouse button is held**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is chosen before drawing?**
a) The tool and the colour  b) Pours colour into a closed area with one click  c) Closed, with no gap in its outline  d) Writes words inside the drawing
**Answer: a) The tool and the colour**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the fill do?**
a) Closed, with no gap in its outline  b) Pours colour into a closed area with one click  c) Writes words inside the drawing  d) Choose it, click the place, and type
**Answer: b) Pours colour into a closed area with one click**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must a shape be for the fill to stay inside?**
a) Writes words inside the drawing  b) Choose it, click the place, and type  c) Closed, with no gap in its outline  d) Both marks and words
**Answer: c) Closed, with no gap in its outline**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the text tool do?**
a) Choose it, click the place, and type  b) Both marks and words  c) A thin line that follows your hand exactly  d) Writes words inside the drawing
**Answer: d) Writes words inside the drawing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are the steps with the text tool?**
a) Choose it, click the place, and type  b) Both marks and words  c) A thin line that follows your hand exactly  d) Outlines, details and writing-like strokes
**Answer: a) Choose it, click the place, and type**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What can a finished drawing carry?**
a) A thin line that follows your hand exactly  b) Both marks and words  c) Outlines, details and writing-like strokes  d) Wider and softer, covering more with each pass
**Answer: b) Both marks and words**
