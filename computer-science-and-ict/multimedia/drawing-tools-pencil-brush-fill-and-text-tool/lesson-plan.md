# Lesson plan — Drawing Tools — Pencil, Brush, Fill and Text Tool

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Multimedia · **Lesson:** Drawing Tools — Pencil, Brush, Fill and Text Tool
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Cuba (2026-11-14) — in the title Multimedia on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Cuba's Mi Mundo Digital / Informática student books and teacher-side books (Ministerio de Educación / Editorial Pueblo y Educación), because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The graficador's **drawing tools** each make one kind of mark: the **pencil** draws a thin free line, the **brush** paints a wider stroke, the **fill** pours colour into a closed area, and the **text tool** writes words into the drawing.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Pencil and brush — the free-drawing tools** — The **pencil** draws a **thin line that follows your hand exactly** — the tool for outlines, details and writing-like strokes. The **brush** paints a **wider, softer stroke**: it covers more with each pass and is the tool for colouring areas by hand and for bold marks. Both draw only while the mouse button is held, and both take the colour chosen before drawing. Cuba's Grade 2 guide introduces the graficador's tools as the child's first real work on the computer: choose the tool, choose the colour, then draw.
2. **Fill and text — colour poured, words placed** — The **fill** (paint bucket) **pours colour into a closed area** with one click: click inside a shape and its whole inside takes the colour — and if the outline has a gap, the colour escapes through it. The **text tool** writes **words inside the drawing**: choose it, click where the words belong, and type — a name under a picture, a title on a poster. Cuba's guides name the text tool inside the same tool lesson as pencil and brush, because a finished school drawing carries both marks and words.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. A thin line that follows your hand exactly.
2. Outlines, details and writing-like strokes.
3. Pours colour into a closed area with one click.
4. Closed, with no gap in its outline.
