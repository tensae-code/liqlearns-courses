# Blender — Camera, Text, and Packing the Design — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the scene's camera?**
**Answer: Its official eye — what the camera sees is what the render shows**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a render?**
**Answer: The finished picture of the framed scene**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do render size and quality set?**
**Answer: The picture's dimensions and its fineness**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why not always render biggest and finest?**
**Answer: They cost time — size and quality are chosen for the picture's destination**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is text added in Blender?**
**Answer: As a 3D object — letters with depth, material like any solid**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does packing do?**
**Answer: Gathers the design's textures and parts into the file, so it arrives whole**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is packing called in the lesson's image?**
**Answer: The designer's suitcase check**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What goes wrong unpacked?**
**Answer: The design points at files left behind on the first machine**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does a packed design go next, in the course?**
**Answer: To the 3D printing lesson — exported in a printable format**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 8 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 1 — Camera, Text, Pack Resources**
