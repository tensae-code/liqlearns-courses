# Hands-on — Image processing — capture, work, output

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Trace one real image
Choose a printed photo or a drawing. Follow it through the flow on paper: draw the scanner or camera that inputs it, name one change software could make to it, and draw where the output appears — printer or monitor.

## Environmental damage evidence
The book's practical: collect or describe three images from different sources that show environmental damage — a cut forest, a dried river, litter in a street. For each, write where it came from (its source) and what information it gives.
