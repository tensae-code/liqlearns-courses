## The three-stage flow

**Image processing** is a computational technique for analysing, enhancing, compressing, reconstructing and generally operating on an image. Its purpose: to get an **enhanced image**, or to **extract useful information** from it — improving pictorial information for human perception.

The flow runs in three stages:

1. **Input / import** — the image is captured by a scanner or a digital camera.
2. **Analysis and manipulation** — the image is worked on in specialised software.
3. **Output** — the result goes to a printer or a monitor.
