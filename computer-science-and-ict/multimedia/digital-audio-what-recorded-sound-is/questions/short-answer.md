# Digital Audio — What Recorded Sound Is — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is digital audio?**
**Answer: Sound recorded and stored as data in an audio file**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What turns sound into a signal the computer can take in?**
**Answer: A microphone**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the computer do with the signal?**
**Answer: It samples it into numbers it can keep**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does an audio file actually hold?**
**Answer: The counted pattern of the sound, not the sound itself**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the same file sound the same everywhere?**
**Answer: Every machine plays the same stored pattern**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why can an audio file be copied perfectly?**
**Answer: Because it is data — numbers, copied exactly**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are the two ways sound enters an editor?**
**Answer: It is recorded, or imported**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is done to audio in editing?**
**Answer: It is cut, joined and cleaned**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two things finish the unit's road?**
**Answer: Exporting and sharing the audio in a playable format**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which program does Class VIII use for the whole road?**
**Answer: Audacity**
