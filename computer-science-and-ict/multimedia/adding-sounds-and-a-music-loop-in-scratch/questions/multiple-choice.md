# Adding Sounds and a Music Loop in Scratch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Sounds for a project are chosen from the —**
a) Sound library  b) Backdrop library  c) Costume editor
**Answer: a) Sound library** — Sounds live in the sound library.

**2. Play sound until done makes the script —**
a) Run faster  b) Wait until the sound ends  c) Delete the sound
**Answer: b) Wait until the sound ends** — Until done means the script waits.

**3. A door creak that should ring out while the sprite keeps walking uses —**
a) Play sound  b) Play sound until done  c) Stop all sounds
**Answer: a) Play sound** — Play sound moves on at once.

**4. Looping background music is made with —**
a) One play block, once  b) Play sound until done inside a forever loop  c) A backdrop
**Answer: b) Play sound until done inside a forever loop** — Looping restarts the music when it ends.
