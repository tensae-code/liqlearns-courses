# Adding Sounds and a Music Loop in Scratch — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Your character's speech keeps being cut by its next move. What is wrong and what is the fix?**
**Answer:** A sound answer: play sound was used, so the script moved on during the speech; use play sound until done so the move waits for the speech to finish.

**2. Why does background music get its own script?**
**Answer:** A sound answer: so its forever loop runs in parallel alongside the action instead of trapping the action inside the music loop.
