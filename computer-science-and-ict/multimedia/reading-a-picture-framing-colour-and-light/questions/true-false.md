# Reading a Picture — Framing, Colour and Light — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The decision of what is inside the picture and what is cut away**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The decision of what is inside the picture and what is cut away** — as the answer to: What does close framing make you feel?
**Answer: False — You meet the subject's eyes and feel with it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The subject's smallness in a large world**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The subject's smallness in a large world** — as the answer to: Which parcours teaches picture reading?
**Answer: False — The Parcours des bestioles — the little creatures**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Built — by the choices of framing, colour and light**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Built — by the choices of framing, colour and light** — as the answer to: What do warm, saturated colours do?
**Answer: False — Excite and cheer**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Quieten or sadden**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Quieten or sadden** — as the answer to: What does soft light do?
**Answer: False — Soothes**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Name the choices — framing, colour, light**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Name the choices — framing, colour, light** — as the answer to: What two powers does naming the steering give?
**Answer: False — To use it, and to resist it**
