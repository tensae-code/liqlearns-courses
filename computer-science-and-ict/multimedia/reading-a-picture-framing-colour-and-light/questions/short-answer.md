# Reading a Picture — Framing, Colour and Light — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is framing (cadrage)?**
**Answer: The decision of what is inside the picture and what is cut away**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does close framing make you feel?**
**Answer: You meet the subject's eyes and feel with it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does wide framing make you feel?**
**Answer: The subject's smallness in a large world**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which parcours teaches picture reading?**
**Answer: The Parcours des bestioles — the little creatures**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is a picture's emotion found or built?**
**Answer: Built — by the choices of framing, colour and light**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do warm, saturated colours do?**
**Answer: Excite and cheer**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do cold, grey colours do?**
**Answer: Quieten or sadden**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does soft light do?**
**Answer: Soothes**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must a reader do before surrendering to the feeling?**
**Answer: Name the choices — framing, colour, light**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two powers does naming the steering give?**
**Answer: To use it, and to resist it**
