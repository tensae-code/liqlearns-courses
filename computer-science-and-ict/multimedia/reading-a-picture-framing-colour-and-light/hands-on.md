# Hands-on — Reading a Picture — Framing, Colour and Light

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Same animal, two feelings** — Find or draw two pictures of one animal that feel opposite. Label the framing, colour and light choices that make each feeling.

## Task two
**Reframe it** — Take one wide picture (printed) and crop it two ways with paper frames. Show the class: what did each crop make the picture say?
