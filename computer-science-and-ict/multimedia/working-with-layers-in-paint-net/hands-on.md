# Hands-on — Working with Layers in Paint.NET

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Open the stack', 'Install or open Paint.NET. Make a canvas, add two layers, and draw a different simple shape on each. Hide one layer and say what remains.')

## Task two
('Save both ways', 'Save your picture as .pdn and as .jpg. Close and reopen both. Write which one still has its layers, and why the other does not.')
