# Working with Layers in Paint.NET — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where is Paint.NET obtained?**
a) Downloaded free from getpaint.net  b) A canvas of a chosen size in pixels, with a resolution  c) A transparent sheet in the stack, holding part of the image  d) It is a stack of layers, not a single sheet
**Answer: a) Downloaded free from getpaint.net**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a new picture begin as?**
a) A transparent sheet in the stack, holding part of the image  b) A canvas of a chosen size in pixels, with a resolution  c) It is a stack of layers, not a single sheet  d) Drawn, hidden, reordered and erased
**Answer: b) A canvas of a chosen size in pixels, with a resolution**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a layer?**
a) It is a stack of layers, not a single sheet  b) Drawn, hidden, reordered and erased  c) A transparent sheet in the stack, holding part of the image  d) Every act done to the picture
**Answer: c) A transparent sheet in the stack, holding part of the image**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is Paint.NET's canvas unlike Paint's sheet?**
a) Drawn, hidden, reordered and erased  b) Every act done to the picture  c) Walks back through the History  d) It is a stack of layers, not a single sheet
**Answer: d) It is a stack of layers, not a single sheet**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What can be done to one layer without touching others?**
a) Drawn, hidden, reordered and erased  b) Every act done to the picture  c) Walks back through the History  d) The layers, for tomorrow's editing
**Answer: a) Drawn, hidden, reordered and erased**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the History window list?**
a) Walks back through the History  b) Every act done to the picture  c) The layers, for tomorrow's editing  d) Flattens the stack into one finished picture
**Answer: b) Every act done to the picture**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does Undo do?**
a) The layers, for tomorrow's editing  b) Flattens the stack into one finished picture  c) Walks back through the History  d) Keep the .pdn master, publish the .jpg
**Answer: c) Walks back through the History**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does saving as .pdn keep?**
a) Flattens the stack into one finished picture  b) Keep the .pdn master, publish the .jpg  c) Downloaded free from getpaint.net  d) The layers, for tomorrow's editing
**Answer: d) The layers, for tomorrow's editing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does saving as .jpg do?**
a) Flattens the stack into one finished picture  b) Keep the .pdn master, publish the .jpg  c) Downloaded free from getpaint.net  d) A canvas of a chosen size in pixels, with a resolution
**Answer: a) Flattens the stack into one finished picture**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) State the book's saving rule.**
a) Downloaded free from getpaint.net  b) Keep the .pdn master, publish the .jpg  c) A canvas of a chosen size in pixels, with a resolution  d) A transparent sheet in the stack, holding part of the image
**Answer: b) Keep the .pdn master, publish the .jpg**
