# Working with Layers in Paint.NET — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where is Paint.NET obtained?" is: Downloaded free from getpaint.net**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a new picture begin as?" is: A transparent sheet in the stack, holding part of the image**
**Answer: False** — A canvas of a chosen size in pixels, with a resolution

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a layer?" is: A transparent sheet in the stack, holding part of the image**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is Paint.NET's canvas unlike Paint's sheet?" is: Drawn, hidden, reordered and erased**
**Answer: False** — It is a stack of layers, not a single sheet

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What can be done to one layer without touching others?" is: Drawn, hidden, reordered and erased**
**Answer: True**

**Answer: True**
