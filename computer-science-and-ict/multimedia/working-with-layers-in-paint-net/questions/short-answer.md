# Working with Layers in Paint.NET — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where is Paint.NET obtained?**
**Answer: Downloaded free from getpaint.net**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a new picture begin as?**
**Answer: A canvas of a chosen size in pixels, with a resolution**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a layer?**
**Answer: A transparent sheet in the stack, holding part of the image**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is Paint.NET's canvas unlike Paint's sheet?**
**Answer: It is a stack of layers, not a single sheet**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What can be done to one layer without touching others?**
**Answer: Drawn, hidden, reordered and erased**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the History window list?**
**Answer: Every act done to the picture**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does Undo do?**
**Answer: Walks back through the History**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does saving as .pdn keep?**
**Answer: The layers, for tomorrow's editing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does saving as .jpg do?**
**Answer: Flattens the stack into one finished picture**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) State the book's saving rule.**
**Answer: Keep the .pdn master, publish the .jpg**
