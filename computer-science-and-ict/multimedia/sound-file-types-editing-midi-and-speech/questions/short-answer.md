# Sound — File Types, Editing, MIDI, and Speech — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do sound file types trade?**
**Answer: Size against quality — compressed types discard what the ear forgives**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three sound edits.**
**Answer: Cut and join, fade in/out, mix tracks, clean noise (any three)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the editor's timeline?**
**Answer: The sound's picture — the wave you see is what you cut**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Uncompressed sound keeps what?**
**Answer: Every sample**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 11 EPAL Basika Themata, Chapter 5**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is MIDI?**
**Answer: A standard language of musical instructions — note, force, length — not sound itself**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What can a MIDI performance be played by?**
**Answer: Any instrument that reads the instructions**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is speech synthesis (TTS)?**
**Answer: Turning written text into spoken voice**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is speech recognition (STT)?**
**Answer: Turning voice into text and commands**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does internet sound reach listeners in streaming?**
**Answer: Played as it arrives, not after a whole download**
