# Adding a Sound to a Scratch Script — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where do a project's sound clips live?**
a) In the Sounds panel (tab)  b) The sprite whose panel it is  c) Where the story needs the sound  d) Sound effects
**Answer: a) In the Sounds panel (tab)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does a new sound enter the project?**
a) Sound blocks in the script  b) By adding it in the Sounds panel  c) When it appears  d) How the clip sounds
**Answer: b) By adding it in the Sounds panel**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What sound does the guide add?**
a) Where the story needs the sound  b) Sound effects  c) The bird sound  d) Deliberately, on its moment — like every block
**Answer: c) The bird sound**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Whose panel do the clips belong to?**
a) When it appears  b) How the clip sounds  c) In the Sounds panel (tab)  d) The sprite whose panel it is
**Answer: d) The sprite whose panel it is**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What plays the clips?**
a) Sound blocks in the script  b) Sound effects  c) Deliberately, on its moment — like every block  d) By adding it in the Sounds panel
**Answer: a) Sound blocks in the script**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where does a sound block go?**
a) How the clip sounds  b) Where the story needs the sound  c) In the Sounds panel (tab)  d) The bird sound
**Answer: b) Where the story needs the sound**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When does the bird sing in the guide's example?**
a) Deliberately, on its moment — like every block  b) By adding it in the Sounds panel  c) When it appears  d) The sprite whose panel it is
**Answer: c) When it appears**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can be tried on a clip in the panel?**
a) In the Sounds panel (tab)  b) The bird sound  c) Sound blocks in the script  d) Sound effects
**Answer: d) Sound effects**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do effects change?**
a) How the clip sounds  b) By adding it in the Sounds panel  c) The sprite whose panel it is  d) Where the story needs the sound
**Answer: a) How the clip sounds**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is sound placed?**
a) The bird sound  b) Deliberately, on its moment — like every block  c) Sound blocks in the script  d) When it appears
**Answer: b) Deliberately, on its moment — like every block**
