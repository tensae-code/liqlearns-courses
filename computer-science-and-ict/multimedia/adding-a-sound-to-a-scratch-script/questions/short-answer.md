# Adding a Sound to a Scratch Script — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where do a project's sound clips live?**
**Answer: In the Sounds panel (tab)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does a new sound enter the project?**
**Answer: By adding it in the Sounds panel**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What sound does the guide add?**
**Answer: The bird sound**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Whose panel do the clips belong to?**
**Answer: The sprite whose panel it is**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What plays the clips?**
**Answer: Sound blocks in the script**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where does a sound block go?**
**Answer: Where the story needs the sound**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When does the bird sing in the guide's example?**
**Answer: When it appears**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can be tried on a clip in the panel?**
**Answer: Sound effects**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do effects change?**
**Answer: How the clip sounds**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is sound placed?**
**Answer: Deliberately, on its moment — like every block**
