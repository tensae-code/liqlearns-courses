# Adding a Sound to a Scratch Script — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where do a project's sound clips live?" is: In the Sounds panel (tab)**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How does a new sound enter the project?" is: The bird sound**
**Answer: False** — By adding it in the Sounds panel

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What sound does the guide add?" is: The bird sound**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Whose panel do the clips belong to?" is: Sound blocks in the script**
**Answer: False** — The sprite whose panel it is

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What plays the clips?" is: Sound blocks in the script**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does a sound block go?" is: When it appears**
**Answer: False** — Where the story needs the sound

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When does the bird sing in the guide's example?" is: When it appears**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What can be tried on a clip in the panel?" is: How the clip sounds**
**Answer: False** — Sound effects

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do effects change?" is: How the clip sounds**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is sound placed?" is: In the Sounds panel (tab)**
**Answer: False** — Deliberately, on its moment — like every block
