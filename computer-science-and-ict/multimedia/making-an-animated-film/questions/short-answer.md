# Making an Animated Film — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What names the scenes of the film?**
**Answer: The script and the storyboard**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What fixes how characters look in every scene?**
**Answer: The model sheets**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is each scene built?**
**Answer: Background layer, character layers, then the frame-by-frame changes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When is each scene played back?**
**Answer: At once, while fixing is cheap**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the production happen?**
**Answer: On the animation software's timeline**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What three kinds of sound are added?**
**Answer: Voices, music and effects**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is exporting?**
**Answer: Rendering the project as one video file any player can show**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the release?**
**Answer: Presenting the finished film to its audience**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why are export and release part of the lesson?**
**Answer: A film that cannot be shown is a workshop exercise, not a film**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Under what are sounds laid?**
**Answer: Under the scenes they belong to**
