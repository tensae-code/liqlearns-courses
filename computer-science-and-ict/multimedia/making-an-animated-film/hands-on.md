# Hands-on — Making an Animated Film

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Produce a scene** — From a storyboard you have, produce one complete scene: layers, animation, playback, fix. Keep it under twenty seconds.

## Task two
**Finish and release** — Add sound to a short animation, export it as a video file, and hold its release: show it to an audience and note one thing you would change.
