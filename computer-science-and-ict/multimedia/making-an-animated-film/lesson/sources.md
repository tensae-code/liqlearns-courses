Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked for the slug and the live lesson content was checked for near matches before building. The live animation lessons teach animation concepts and GIF craft; producing a complete animated film — scenes, sound, export and release — as one production practical is taught by no live lesson. The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — multimedia/making-an-animated-film). It sits in the Multimedia title (multimedia/), following the book's own order. Provenance scope note: the placements stocked here are from Vietnam's “Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống” (Connecting Knowledge with Life) Tin học books (Vietnamese) — one series, Grades 3–12, from Nhà xuất bản Giáo dục Việt Nam, splitting from Grade 10 into Computer Science (Khoa học máy tính) and Applied Informatics (Tin học ứng dụng) orientations, each with its own specialist-topic (Chuyên đề) volumes; the books are image-only PDFs read from rendered pages. Every placement stocked here is from a student book and counts toward the acceptance count; there are no teacher guides in this country. Media slots in lesson/lesson.md are described slots only ([Picture — …]); no picture numbers are invented.

Vietnam's own way (Grade 11 Tin học 11 Tin học ứng dụng): Bài 1–16 are the same common core as the Computer Science orientation book (spot-checked page-identical, e.g. Bài 1); the Applied-only second half is hands-on database administration in Microsoft Access (through backup), then photo editing in GIMP and film-making/animation.

Vietnam's own way (Grade 11 Chuyên đề Tin học 11 Ứng dụng): a media-production volume in three crafts — vector decoration design in Inkscape (a postcard for a national tourism-design contest), cartoon-film making, and animated-GIF editing in a photo editor, layers first.
