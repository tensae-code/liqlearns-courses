# Excel Text Functions — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does LEFT do?** → **Takes the first n characters of a text**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does FIND report?** → **The position of a piece inside a text (or an error if absent)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How do FIND and LEFT work together?** → **Find the separator, cut at it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does SUBSTITUTE swap?** → **Every occurrence of one piece for another**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are the three dissectors?** → **Position (FIND), cut (LEFT), replace (SUBSTITUTE)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does TRIM remove?** → **Extra spaces — leading, trailing and doubled**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why do doubled spaces matter?** → **They make the same city count as two**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do UPPER and LOWER force?** → **A text wholly into capitals or small letters**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does analysis need text functions?** → **Analysis dies of dirty text — these do the cleaning**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the kit's audit virtue?** → **Cleaning by formula is auditable by anyone**
