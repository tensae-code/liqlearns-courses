# Excel Text Functions — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does LEFT do?**
**Answer: Takes the first n characters of a text**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does FIND report?**
**Answer: The position of a piece inside a text (or an error if absent)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How do FIND and LEFT work together?**
**Answer: Find the separator, cut at it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does SUBSTITUTE swap?**
**Answer: Every occurrence of one piece for another**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are the three dissectors?**
**Answer: Position (FIND), cut (LEFT), replace (SUBSTITUTE)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does TRIM remove?**
**Answer: Extra spaces — leading, trailing and doubled**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why do doubled spaces matter?**
**Answer: They make the same city count as two**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do UPPER and LOWER force?**
**Answer: A text wholly into capitals or small letters**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does analysis need text functions?**
**Answer: Analysis dies of dirty text — these do the cleaning**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the kit's audit virtue?**
**Answer: Cleaning by formula is auditable by anyone**
