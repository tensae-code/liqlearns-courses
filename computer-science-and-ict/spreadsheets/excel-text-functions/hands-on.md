# Hands-on — Excel Text Functions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Clean a column** — Take a messy column (mixed case, stray spaces, a code prefix) and clean it with TRIM, UPPER/LOWER and LEFT+FIND formulas, one fault per column step.

## Task two
**Substitute census** — Use SUBSTITUTE to standardise three variant spellings in a list, and count before and after with a check.
