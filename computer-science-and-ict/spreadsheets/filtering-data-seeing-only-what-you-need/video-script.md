# Video script — Filtering data — seeing only what you need

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Too many rows? Filter.
Your sheet needs a header row first — a name on every column.
Data. Filter. An arrow appears in every header.
Click the arrow on your column.
Uncheck Select All. Check only what you want. OK.
The other rows hide. They are not deleted!
Filter again — and the whole list comes back.
