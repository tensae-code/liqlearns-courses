# VLOOKUP in Excel — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does VLOOKUP search?**
**Answer: The first column of a table, for the lookup value**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does it return?**
**Answer: The value in the stated column of the found row**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is VLOOKUP, in one phrase?**
**Answer: The spreadsheet's join — tables lending columns through a shared key**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the V stand for?**
**Answer: Vertical — the search runs down a column**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why do records need the vertical form?**
**Answer: Records live in rows**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does FALSE demand?**
**Answer: An exact match — or the honest #N/A**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When is TRUE (nearest) right?**
**Answer: For bands like tax bands**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where must lookup values live?**
**Answer: In the table's first column only**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do duplicates return?**
**Answer: The first match, silently**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is #N/A the analyst's friend?**
**Answer: It fails loudly; a silent lookup invents data**
