# Hands-on — VLOOKUP in Excel

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Join two tables** — Join a marks table to a names table by pupil number with VLOOKUP (exact), and handle the #N/A rows honestly in a note.

## Task two
**Fault hunt** — A lookup returns wrong names. List the four standing faults as suspects and the test that convicts each.
