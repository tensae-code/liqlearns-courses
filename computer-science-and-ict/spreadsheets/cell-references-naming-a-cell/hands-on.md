# Hands-on — Cell references

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Where there is no computer laboratory, do the screen work on a teacher's or your own phone, as the course's teaching pattern provides.

## Name that cell
On a paper grid, a partner points at a cell; you call its reference — letter, then number. Ten cells, then swap. Speed comes from always saying the letter first.

## Freeze and free
Build =40+10 in one cell and a reference version beside it. Change the source value and point at which answer moved and which stayed frozen. Say why, in one sentence.
