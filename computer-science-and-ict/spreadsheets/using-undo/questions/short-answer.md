# Using Undo in Excel — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does Undo reverse?**
**Answer: The last action taken**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Undo sit in the book's Excel?**
**Answer: The Edit menu, the toolbar's curved arrow, and Ctrl+Z**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book teach Undo between copying and deleting?**
**Answer: The learner meets the safety net before the riskiest work**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) In what order does Undo work?**
**Answer: Backwards, one action at a time — last done, first undone**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Can Undo skip a newer action to rescue an older one?**
**Answer: No — the stack unwinds strictly in reverse**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Redo do?**
**Answer: Re-applies an undone action, walking forwards again**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What ends the way back completely?**
**Answer: Closing the workbook**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name an act Undo cannot catch.**
**Answer: A deleted worksheet**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Is Undo a licence to work carelessly?**
**Answer: No — it is a safety net, not a licence**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 9 ICT, Chapter 5 §5.10**
