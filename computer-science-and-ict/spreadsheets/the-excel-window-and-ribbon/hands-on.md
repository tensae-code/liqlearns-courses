# Hands-on — The Excel Window and Ribbon

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the window from memory
Close Excel (or turn away) and draw its window: worksheet, ribbon, title. Open it again and correct your drawing — the gaps are what this lesson is for.

## Ribbon tour
Open the ribbon's tabs one by one and write, for each tab, one tool you recognise and what you think it does.
