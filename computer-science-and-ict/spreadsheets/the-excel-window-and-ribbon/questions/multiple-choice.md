# The Excel Window and Ribbon — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What fills the middle of the Excel window?**
a) The worksheet of rows and columns  b) The data  c) What a spreadsheet is  d) The strip of tabs and tools for working on the worksheet
**Answer: a) The worksheet of rows and columns**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What goes on the worksheet?**
a) The main interface of Microsoft Excel  b) The data  c) The ribbon  d) Formatting and inserting
**Answer: b) The data**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Excel window called in the book?**
a) What a spreadsheet is  b) The strip of tabs and tools for working on the worksheet  c) The main interface of Microsoft Excel  d) Laying out the page
**Answer: c) The main interface of Microsoft Excel**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the unit's first lesson teach, before this one?**
a) The ribbon  b) Formatting and inserting  c) So later lessons can simply say where a tool lives  d) What a spreadsheet is
**Answer: d) What a spreadsheet is**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What runs across the top of the window?**
a) The ribbon  b) The strip of tabs and tools for working on the worksheet  c) Laying out the page  d) Grade 11
**Answer: a) The ribbon**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the ribbon?**
a) Formatting and inserting  b) The strip of tabs and tools for working on the worksheet  c) So later lessons can simply say where a tool lives  d) The worksheet of rows and columns
**Answer: b) The strip of tabs and tools for working on the worksheet**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two kinds of work whose tools live on the ribbon.**
a) Laying out the page  b) Grade 11  c) Formatting and inserting  d) The data
**Answer: c) Formatting and inserting**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What third kind of work lives there, for a later lesson?**
a) So later lessons can simply say where a tool lives  b) The worksheet of rows and columns  c) The main interface of Microsoft Excel  d) Laying out the page
**Answer: d) Laying out the page**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the ribbon's layout learned once, early?**
a) So later lessons can simply say where a tool lives  b) Grade 11  c) The data  d) What a spreadsheet is
**Answer: a) So later lessons can simply say where a tool lives**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which grade's book teaches the Excel window?**
a) The worksheet of rows and columns  b) Grade 11  c) The main interface of Microsoft Excel  d) The ribbon
**Answer: b) Grade 11**
