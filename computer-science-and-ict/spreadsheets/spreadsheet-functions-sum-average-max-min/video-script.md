Four functions you know. Here is the fifth: COUNT.
COUNT does not add. It counts — how many cells hold numbers.
Five now: SUM adds. AVERAGE averages. COUNT counts. MAX the highest. MIN the lowest.

Type one yourself. Select the cell.
Equals. The name: AVERAGE. Open bracket. The range: D3 to D12. Close bracket. Enter.
There is the average.

Or let AutoSum help. Home tab, Editing group, AutoSum.
It inserts the function — and guesses the range.
Is the guess right? Correct it if not. THEN Enter.
Five functions, two routes, one shape: equals, name, arguments.
