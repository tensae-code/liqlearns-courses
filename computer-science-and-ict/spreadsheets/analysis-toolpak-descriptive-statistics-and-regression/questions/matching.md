# The Analysis ToolPak — Descriptive Statistics and Regression in Excel — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Analysis ToolPak?** → **Excel's built-in analysis add-in, switched on in add-ins settings**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name six outputs of Descriptive Statistics.** → **Mean, median, mode, standard deviation, range, count (or variance, min, max, sum)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) In what form does it return them?** → **One summary table, fit for a report**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is the ToolPak taught after the measures?** → **It is a calculator for ideas the pupil already owns**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is its output read?** → **With the ideas — never worshipped without them**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the Regression tool fit?** → **The line relating the explainer (x) to the explained (y)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is R-squared?** → **The share of y's variation the line accounts for**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does R-squared near 0 say?** → **The line doesn't carry the story**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name two judgements the tool cannot make.** → **Whether a line is the right shape; whether the relation is cause or companion**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who computes, and who judges?** → **The tool computes; the analyst judges**
