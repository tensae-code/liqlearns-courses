# The Analysis ToolPak — Descriptive Statistics and Regression in Excel — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Excel's built-in analysis add-in, switched on in add-ins settings**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Excel's built-in analysis add-in, switched on in add-ins settings** — as the answer to: Name six outputs of Descriptive Statistics.
**Answer: False — Mean, median, mode, standard deviation, range, count (or variance, min, max, sum)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: One summary table, fit for a report**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: One summary table, fit for a report** — as the answer to: Why is the ToolPak taught after the measures?
**Answer: False — It is a calculator for ideas the pupil already owns**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: With the ideas — never worshipped without them**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: With the ideas — never worshipped without them** — as the answer to: What does the Regression tool fit?
**Answer: False — The line relating the explainer (x) to the explained (y)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The share of y's variation the line accounts for**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The share of y's variation the line accounts for** — as the answer to: What does R-squared near 0 say?
**Answer: False — The line doesn't carry the story**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Whether a line is the right shape; whether the relation is cause or companion**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Whether a line is the right shape; whether the relation is cause or companion** — as the answer to: Who computes, and who judges?
**Answer: False — The tool computes; the analyst judges**
