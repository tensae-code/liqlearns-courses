# Hands-on — The Analysis ToolPak — Descriptive Statistics and Regression in Excel

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**One-dialog summary** — Run (or simulate by hand for a small set) the Descriptive Statistics tool on a real column and write its story in three sentences from the output table.

## Task two
**Judge a regression** — Given a regression output with R² = 0.21, write the honest report paragraph: the line, the fit, the doubts, the claim allowed.
