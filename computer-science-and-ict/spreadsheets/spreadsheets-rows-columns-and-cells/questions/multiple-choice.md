# Spreadsheets — data in rows, columns and cells — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A spreadsheet is an application used to —
a) draw pictures only  b) organise and calculate data in cells  c) play music  d) send email
**Answer: b) organise and calculate data in cells.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A spreadsheet data file is made up of —
a) pages and chapters  b) rows and columns  c) slides and notes  d) folders and files
**Answer: b) rows and columns.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Values in a spreadsheet are calculated with —
a) the keyboard alone  b) mathematical formulas in the cells  c) the printer  d) pictures
**Answer: b) mathematical formulas in the cells.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** When a spreadsheet opens, you see —
a) a blank white page only  b) its important buttons, rows, columns and headers  c) a slideshow  d) an email inbox
**Answer: b) its important buttons, rows, columns and headers.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Tables in rows and columns let data be —
a) hidden  b) printed only  c) analysed quickly and easily  d) deleted safely
**Answer: c) analysed quickly and easily.**
