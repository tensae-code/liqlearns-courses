# Spreadsheets — data in rows, columns and cells — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A ______ runs across; a column runs down.
**Answer: row**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Data lives in ______, the boxes of the grid.
**Answer: cells**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** On opening, a spreadsheet shows its buttons, rows, columns and ______.
**Answer: headers**
