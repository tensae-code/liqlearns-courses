# Video script — Spreadsheets — data in rows, columns and cells

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Data needs a home with order.

Rows run across. Columns run down.
Where they meet — a cell. Data lives in cells.

A spreadsheet holds the grid,
sorts it, arranges it,
and calculates in the cells with formulas.
