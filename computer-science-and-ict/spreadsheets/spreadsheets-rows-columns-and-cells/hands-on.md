# Hands-on — Spreadsheets — data in rows, columns and cells

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Paper spreadsheet

Draw a grid on paper: four rows, four columns. Write names down the first column and two subjects across the top; fill the cells with marks. You have built a spreadsheet table by hand — rows, columns and cells all in their places.

## Find the cell

With a partner at a real spreadsheet or your paper grid: your partner names a row and a column — "row 2, column 3" — and you put your finger on the one cell where they meet. Five rounds, then swap.
