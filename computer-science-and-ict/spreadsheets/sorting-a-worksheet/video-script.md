# Video script — Sorting a worksheet

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Names in any order? Sort them.
First decide: the WHOLE sheet — every row moves together, marks stay with their names.
Or only a range — the tables beside it stay untouched.
Decide first. Then: click a cell in the name column.
Data tab. A to Z — Ascending.
The whole sheet follows the names.
Check one pupil. Their marks are still theirs. That is a good sort.
