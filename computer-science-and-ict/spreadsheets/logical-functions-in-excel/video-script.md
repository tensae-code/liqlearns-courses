# Video script — Logical Functions in Excel

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **logical function** works on a **test**: it compares a value with a condition — is this mark at least the pass mark? — and returns **one answer if the test is true and another if it is false**. The test is written with the comparison signs the formulas lessons already met. With a logical function the sheet stops only calculating and starts **reporting a judgement** in words the reader understands, for every row at once.
A formula written once is rarely needed once. **Copy and paste** a formula to the neighbouring cells and Excel **adjusts its cell references** to each new row — the fill-handle lesson's rule, worked by hand. **Cut and paste** moves the formula itself to a new home. Libya pairs the two teachings in one section because they are one daily skill: write the deciding formula once, then spread it down the whole class list.
