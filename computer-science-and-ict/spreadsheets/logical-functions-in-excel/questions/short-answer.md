# Logical Functions in Excel — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a logical function do?**
**Answer: It tests a condition and answers differently according to the result**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two answers can it return?**
**Answer: One answer if the test is true and another if it is false**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give an example of a test.**
**Answer: Is this mark at least the pass mark?**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is the test written?**
**Answer: With comparison signs, comparing a value with a condition**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What changes when a sheet gains logical functions?**
**Answer: It stops only calculating and starts reporting a judgement**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when you copy a formula to neighbouring cells?**
**Answer: Excel adjusts its cell references to each new row**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does cut and paste do to a formula?**
**Answer: It moves the formula itself to a new home**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why write the formula only once?**
**Answer: Because copy and paste spread it, adjusted, down the whole list**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which earlier lesson's rule is copy-paste using?**
**Answer: The fill handle's rule — references adjust as a formula is copied**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name the two halves of this lesson.**
**Answer: Logical functions, and cut/copy/paste of a formula**
