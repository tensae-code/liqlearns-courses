---

A **range** (дыяпазон) is a rectangle of cells: a stretch of one row, one column, or a block of several rows and columns.
Its name joins the addresses of its **opposite corners** with a colon — the block from B2 to D5 is written B2:D5 — and that name can be used wherever a single cell address can, for example inside a formula that totals the whole block.
Working with ranges means doing once what would otherwise be done cell by cell.
**Select** the block by dragging from one corner to the opposite one; then the action applies to all of it: copy or move the block to a new place, fill a series across it, clear its contents, or format every cell in it at once.
