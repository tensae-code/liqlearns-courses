# Working with Cell Ranges — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a range?**
a) A rectangular block of cells  b) Yes — a range of one  c) Copying or moving it, filling a series across it, clearing or formatting it  d) No — it always describes the whole rectangle
**Answer: a) A rectangular block of cells**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is a range named?**
a) By dragging from one corner to the opposite one  b) By the addresses of its opposite corners, joined with a colon — for example B2:D5  c) Checking the selection — a range one row too tall carries the extra row into everything  d) A stretch of one row, one column, or a block of several rows and columns
**Answer: b) By the addresses of its opposite corners, joined with a colon — for example B2:D5**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Can a range name be used in a formula?**
a) Copying or moving it, filling a series across it, clearing or formatting it  b) No — it always describes the whole rectangle  c) Yes — wherever a single cell address can be used  d) To do once what would otherwise be done cell by cell
**Answer: c) Yes — wherever a single cell address can be used**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is a single cell a range?**
a) Checking the selection — a range one row too tall carries the extra row into everything  b) A stretch of one row, one column, or a block of several rows and columns  c) A rectangular block of cells  d) Yes — a range of one
**Answer: d) Yes — a range of one**
