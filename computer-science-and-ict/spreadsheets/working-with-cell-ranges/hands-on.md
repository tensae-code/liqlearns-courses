# Hands-on — Working with Cell Ranges

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name that block
On a practice sheet, select three different blocks by dragging and write each range's name from its corners. Then write a formula that totals one whole block using its range name.

## One action, whole block
Fill a range with a number series, format the whole range at once, then copy the block to a new place. Check the copy cell by cell against the original and say what a one-row-too-tall selection would have done.
