# Data Validation — Checking What Is Entered — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is data validation?**
**Answer: Setting the rules for what may be entered in a cell**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three kinds of rule.**
**Answer: A whole number in a range, a date in a period, a value from a list**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to a breaking entry?**
**Answer: It is refused, with a message saying why**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the advanced track open with validation?**
**Answer: A shared workbook is only as good as what others are allowed to type into it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a list rule give the typist?**
**Answer: A drop-down of the allowed values**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When does the input message appear?**
**Answer: When the cell is chosen — before typing**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What should a good error alert say?**
**Answer: What was wrong and what is allowed**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can validation not guarantee?**
**Answer: Truth — a permitted value can still be the wrong one**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which family of errors does validation kill?**
**Answer: The impossible entries — out-of-range, wrong-kind, not-in-list**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) On what can checking then spend itself?**
**Answer: The possible-but-wrong entries**
