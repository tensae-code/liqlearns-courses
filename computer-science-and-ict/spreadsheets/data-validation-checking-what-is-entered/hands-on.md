# Hands-on — Data Validation — Checking What Is Entered

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Lock a mark cell** — Set a cell to accept only whole numbers 0–100, with an input message and a clear error alert. Try to enter 150, 3.5 and 'ten' — record what happens.

## Task two
**A list rule** — Give a 'class' cell a drop-down list of three classes. Then say one wrong entry this rule cannot catch, and how you would catch it.
