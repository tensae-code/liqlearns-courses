# Scatter Plots and Correlation — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a scatter plot draw?" is: Two measures of the same cases — one point per case**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do points climbing bottom-left to top-right say?" is: No visible link between the measures**
**Answer: False** — The two measures rise together

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a shapeless cloud say?" is: No visible link between the measures**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Between what values does the correlation coefficient lie?" is: A strong link — both measures rising together**
**Answer: False** — −1 and +1

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a coefficient near +1 mean?" is: A strong link — both measures rising together**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does a coefficient near −1 mean?" is: No straight-line link worth the name**
**Answer: False** — A strong link — one measure falling as the other rises

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does a coefficient near 0 mean?" is: No straight-line link worth the name**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the standing warning about correlation?" is: A third thing may be moving them both**
**Answer: False** — Correlation is not causation

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why can two measures move together without causing each other?" is: A third thing may be moving them both**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is correlation honestly used for?" is: Two measures of the same cases — one point per case**
**Answer: False** — Telling the analyst where to look next — never finishing the investigation alone
