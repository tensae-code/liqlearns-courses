# Scatter Plots and Correlation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a scatter plot draw?**
**Answer: Two measures of the same cases — one point per case**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do points climbing bottom-left to top-right say?**
**Answer: The two measures rise together**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a shapeless cloud say?**
**Answer: No visible link between the measures**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Between what values does the correlation coefficient lie?**
**Answer: −1 and +1**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a coefficient near +1 mean?**
**Answer: A strong link — both measures rising together**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a coefficient near −1 mean?**
**Answer: A strong link — one measure falling as the other rises**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a coefficient near 0 mean?**
**Answer: No straight-line link worth the name**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the standing warning about correlation?**
**Answer: Correlation is not causation**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why can two measures move together without causing each other?**
**Answer: A third thing may be moving them both**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is correlation honestly used for?**
**Answer: Telling the analyst where to look next — never finishing the investigation alone**
