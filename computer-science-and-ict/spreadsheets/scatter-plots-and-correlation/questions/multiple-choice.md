# Scatter Plots and Correlation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a scatter plot draw?**
a) Two measures of the same cases — one point per case  b) The two measures rise together  c) No visible link between the measures  d) −1 and +1
**Answer: a) Two measures of the same cases — one point per case**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do points climbing bottom-left to top-right say?**
a) No visible link between the measures  b) The two measures rise together  c) −1 and +1  d) A strong link — both measures rising together
**Answer: b) The two measures rise together**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a shapeless cloud say?**
a) −1 and +1  b) A strong link — both measures rising together  c) No visible link between the measures  d) A strong link — one measure falling as the other rises
**Answer: c) No visible link between the measures**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Between what values does the correlation coefficient lie?**
a) A strong link — both measures rising together  b) A strong link — one measure falling as the other rises  c) No straight-line link worth the name  d) −1 and +1
**Answer: d) −1 and +1**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a coefficient near +1 mean?**
a) A strong link — both measures rising together  b) A strong link — one measure falling as the other rises  c) No straight-line link worth the name  d) Correlation is not causation
**Answer: a) A strong link — both measures rising together**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a coefficient near −1 mean?**
a) No straight-line link worth the name  b) A strong link — one measure falling as the other rises  c) Correlation is not causation  d) A third thing may be moving them both
**Answer: b) A strong link — one measure falling as the other rises**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a coefficient near 0 mean?**
a) Correlation is not causation  b) A third thing may be moving them both  c) No straight-line link worth the name  d) Telling the analyst where to look next — never finishing the investigation alone
**Answer: c) No straight-line link worth the name**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the standing warning about correlation?**
a) A third thing may be moving them both  b) Telling the analyst where to look next — never finishing the investigation alone  c) Two measures of the same cases — one point per case  d) Correlation is not causation
**Answer: d) Correlation is not causation**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why can two measures move together without causing each other?**
a) A third thing may be moving them both  b) Telling the analyst where to look next — never finishing the investigation alone  c) Two measures of the same cases — one point per case  d) The two measures rise together
**Answer: a) A third thing may be moving them both**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is correlation honestly used for?**
a) Two measures of the same cases — one point per case  b) Telling the analyst where to look next — never finishing the investigation alone  c) The two measures rise together  d) No visible link between the measures
**Answer: b) Telling the analyst where to look next — never finishing the investigation alone**
