# Hands-on — Scatter Plots and Correlation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Plot your own pair', 'Collect ten cases of two measures you can really gather — hours of sleep against a morning score — plot the scatter by hand and describe the cloud.')

## Task two
('Number it', "Enter the same table in a spreadsheet, draw the scatter chart, compute the correlation, and write one honest sentence about what it does and does not prove.")
