# Hands-on — Relative cell references

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Where there is no computer laboratory, do the screen work on a teacher's or your own phone, as the course's teaching pattern provides.

## The clicking build
Enter four marks for one student in B2:E2. Build the total in F2 by clicking only — no typing of references. Read the finished formula aloud from the formula bar.

## Typo trap
Deliberately type one reference wrong (B2 as B22). Watch the wrong total. Rebuild by clicking and say why clicking protects you.
