# Video script — Relative cell references

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This lesson's placements name a full walkthrough script, so this script walks every step.

---

Watch my screen. We do it together, step by step.

Five students, four marks each, and a Total column waiting empty. You could type twenty sums. Instead you will build ONE formula — by clicking — and let the sheet do the rest. Build it now, exactly in the order given.

A relative reference adjusts automatically when rows or columns are inserted or deleted, or when the formula is copied. Relative is the spreadsheet's default way of referring.

The mark sheet holds five students, with Individual Assignment, Mid Exam, Projects and Final Exam marks in columns B to E, and the total waiting in column F.

1. Click the total cell, F2.
2. Type =.
3. Click the first mark's cell, B2 (or type it).
4. Type +.
5. Click or type each next cell in turn — C2, then D2, then E2 — with a + between each.
6. Press Enter — the cell shows the total, and the formula bar shows the formula.

Clicking cells while building is recommended over typing: it is quicker and typo-proof. Case does not matter — type b4 and the program makes it B4.

Now you. Do it again, from the start, steps in order. Say each step out loud as you do it.
