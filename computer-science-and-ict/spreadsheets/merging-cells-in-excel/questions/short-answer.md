# Merging Cells in Excel — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does merging join in Excel?**
**Answer: A selected block of cells into one large cell**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Whose value survives a merge?**
**Answer: The block's top-left value**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the menu route in the book's Excel?**
**Answer: Format → Cells → Alignment → Merge cells**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the Merge and Center button add?**
**Answer: It joins and centres in one act**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What address does a merged cell keep?**
**Answer: Its top-left corner's address**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is merging's daily job?**
**Answer: Placing a title centred over a whole table**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the title made, in order?**
**Answer: Select across the table's width, merge, then type the title**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Is merging data work or formatting?**
**Answer: Formatting — the numbers below are untouched**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What can merged cells tangle?**
**Answer: Sorting and selecting — a partly merged column refuses tidy sorts**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 9 ICT, Chapter 6 §6.1**
