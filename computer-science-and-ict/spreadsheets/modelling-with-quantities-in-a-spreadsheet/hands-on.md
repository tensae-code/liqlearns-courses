# Hands-on — Modelling with Quantities in a Spreadsheet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Build the Heron model', 'Build the Heron template: three input cells for sides, a half-perimeter cell, an area cell. Test it on a 3-4-5 triangle whose area you know (6) — the model's first check.')

## Task two
('Pour new cases', 'Pour in three more triangles, one of them impossible (one side longer than the other two together). Record what the model does with the impossible case and what that teaches.')
