# Modelling with Quantities in a Spreadsheet — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the sheet's three citizens in a model.**
**Answer: Input cells, formula cells and answer cells**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What never changes in a model?**
**Answer: The formula cells — the case's law**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a new case answered?**
**Answer: By changing only the inputs**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is the template reusable?**
**Answer: Because the law and the data stopped sharing cells**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is Mongolia's test of a model?**
**Answer: Replacement — pour in further cases; the answers must stay true**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Heron's formula give?**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is worked first inside Heron's formula?**
**Answer: The half-perimeter**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How are its two formula cells related?**
**Answer: Chained — the second drinks from the first**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the spreadsheet end as, in this unit?**
**Answer: An instrument — a model**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is a model judged, by the course's standing phrase?**
