# Modelling with Quantities in a Spreadsheet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the sheet's three citizens in a model.**
a) Input cells, formula cells and answer cells  b) The formula cells — the case's law  c) By changing only the inputs  d) Because the law and the data stopped sharing cells
**Answer: a) Input cells, formula cells and answer cells**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What never changes in a model?**
a) By changing only the inputs  b) The formula cells — the case's law  c) Because the law and the data stopped sharing cells  d) Replacement — pour in further cases; the answers must stay true
**Answer: b) The formula cells — the case's law**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a new case answered?**
a) Because the law and the data stopped sharing cells  b) Replacement — pour in further cases; the answers must stay true  c) By changing only the inputs  d) A triangle's area from its three sides alone
**Answer: c) By changing only the inputs**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is the template reusable?**
a) Replacement — pour in further cases; the answers must stay true  b) A triangle's area from its three sides alone  c) The half-perimeter  d) Because the law and the data stopped sharing cells

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is Mongolia's test of a model?**
