# Video script — Types of Data — Text, Numbers, Formulas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Text** data is words and labels: names, headings, notes — anything the sheet describes rather than calculates with.
**Numeric** data is a number the sheet can calculate with: a quantity, a price, a mark.
A **formula** is an entry that tells the sheet to calculate: it begins with the equals sign and names cells and operations, and the cell shows the formula's **result**, not the formula itself.
Change a number the formula uses and the result changes with it — that is the whole point of a spreadsheet.
