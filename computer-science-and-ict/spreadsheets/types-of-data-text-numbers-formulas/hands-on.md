# Hands-on — Types of Data — Text, Numbers, Formulas

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the kinds
Make a small sheet with a text heading, three typed numbers, and a formula adding them. Click each cell in turn and say, from the formula bar, which type each cell holds.

## The moving answer
Change one of the typed numbers and watch the formula's result change. Then retype the result cell with the same number as plain data and change the input again — say what is different now, and why it matters.
