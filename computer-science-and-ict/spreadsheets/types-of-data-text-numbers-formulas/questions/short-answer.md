# Types of Data — Text, Numbers, Formulas — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the three types of data in a cell?**
**Answer: Text, a number, or a formula**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is text data for?**
**Answer: Words and labels — what the sheet describes rather than calculates with**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is numeric data?**
**Answer: A number the sheet can calculate with**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is the difference between text and numbers about looks?**
**Answer: No — it is about behaviour: what the program can do with the entry**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why can the sheet subtract one date from another?**
**Answer: Because dates and times count as numbers underneath**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does a formula entry begin?**
**Answer: With the equals sign**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a formula cell show?**
**Answer: The formula's result, not the formula itself**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to a result when an input changes?**
**Answer: The result changes with it**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How can you tell a formula cell from a typed number?**
**Answer: Select the cell and look in the formula bar**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What stays fixed until retyped?**
**Answer: A typed number**
