# Types of Data — Text, Numbers, Formulas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the three types of data in a cell?**
a) Text, a number, or a formula  b) No — it is about behaviour: what the program can do with the entry  c) With the equals sign  d) The result changes with it
**Answer: a) Text, a number, or a formula**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is text data for?**
a) Because dates and times count as numbers underneath  b) Words and labels — what the sheet describes rather than calculates with  c) The formula's result, not the formula itself  d) Select the cell and look in the formula bar
**Answer: b) Words and labels — what the sheet describes rather than calculates with**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is numeric data?**
a) With the equals sign  b) The result changes with it  c) A number the sheet can calculate with  d) A typed number
**Answer: c) A number the sheet can calculate with**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is the difference between text and numbers about looks?**
a) The formula's result, not the formula itself  b) Select the cell and look in the formula bar  c) Text, a number, or a formula  d) No — it is about behaviour: what the program can do with the entry
**Answer: d) No — it is about behaviour: what the program can do with the entry**
