# Spreadsheet Tasks from Other Subjects — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What kinds of tasks does this practicum use?**
**Answer: Calculation tasks from different subject areas**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many times is the subject's formula entered in the sheet?**
**Answer: Once — the sheet recalculates it for every row**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the learner's work shift to in a spreadsheet task?**
**Answer: Setting the task up correctly — data in columns, the formula translated exactly, results checked**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a spreadsheet result checked?**
**Answer: Against one hand-worked case**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a dependency studied?**
**Answer: By varying one quantity in steps, calculating the other, and drawing the result as a graph**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a graph show that a table hides?**
**Answer: Where growth is fast or slow, where it levels or turns**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the experiment discipline in these tasks?**
**Answer: Change one thing at a time and keep the steps even**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name a subject whose tasks appear here.**
**Answer: Physics (geography and economics tasks are also used)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How should the graph be read?**
**Answer: As a claim about the subject's rule, not just a picture of numbers**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which earlier spreadsheet skills does the practicum join?**
**Answer: Entering data, formulas, and charts**
