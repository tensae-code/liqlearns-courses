# Video script — Spreadsheet Tasks from Other Subjects

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Other subjects are full of repeated calculation: physics works values through a formula many times, geography compares quantities across places, household and economics tasks total and share amounts.
Done in a **spreadsheet**, the subject's formula is entered **once** as a sheet formula and the sheet recalculates it for every row of data — the learner's work shifts from arithmetic to setting the task up correctly: the right data in columns, the subject formula translated exactly, and the results checked against one hand-worked case.
A dependency — how one quantity answers to another — is studied by **varying** the first quantity in steps and letting the sheet calculate the second, then drawing the calculated table as a **graph**.
The graph shows what a table of numbers hides: where the growth is fast or slow, where it levels or turns.
