# Hands-on — Spreadsheet Tasks from Other Subjects

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One formula, twenty rows
Take a formula from another subject (for example distance from speed and time). Build a sheet that calculates it for twenty rows of data, and check the first row by hand.

## From table to graph
Vary one quantity in even steps, let the sheet calculate the dependent quantity, and draw the graph. Write two sentences the graph shows that the table alone did not.
