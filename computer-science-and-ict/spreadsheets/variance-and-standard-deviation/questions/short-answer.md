# Variance and Standard Deviation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What question do variance and standard deviation answer?**
**Answer: How far values scatter around their centre**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is variance?**
**Answer: The average of the squared distances of the values from the mean**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why are the distances squared?**
**Answer: So negatives cannot cancel positives, and big distances count heavily**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which spreadsheet function does Egypt's book use for variance?**
**Answer: VAR.P — over the whole group being described**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a variance of zero mean?**
**Answer: Every value equals the mean — no scatter at all**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is standard deviation?**
**Answer: The square root of the variance**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why take the square root?**
**Answer: To return the spread to the data's own units — marks, not marks squared**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is standard deviation read?**
**Answer: As the typical distance of a value from the mean**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Can two groups share an average yet differ in spread?**
**Answer: Yes — one bunched, one split — which is why the average alone misleads**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What fair comparison does the spread enable?**
**Answer: Judging whether a difference in averages matters, given how wide each group scatters**
