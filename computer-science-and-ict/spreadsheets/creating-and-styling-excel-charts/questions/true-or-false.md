# Creating and styling Excel charts — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Radar and surface are among the chart types the book names.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Quick Layout applies a ready-made arrangement in one pick.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A chart can be made without selecting any cells first.
**Answer: False** — the source cells are selected first.
