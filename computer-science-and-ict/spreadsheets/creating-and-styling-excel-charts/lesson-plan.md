# Lesson plan — Creating and styling Excel charts

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Spreadsheets · **Lesson:** Creating and styling Excel charts
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Application Software, Grade 10 depth, in the Spreadsheets title of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md). The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a chart is made from selected cells — titles and labels included — by Insert → a Chart command, and afterwards shaped on the Design tab with Add Chart Element, Quick Layout and Chart Styles.
**This lesson's own level:** it is a doing-lesson. It stands on the data lessons; the chart shows the data those lessons entered, sorted and filtered.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## If the learner is stuck
- The chart has no labels → the selection missed the column titles and row labels; delete the chart, select again including them, and re-insert.
- A title will not change → double-click the title's placeholder and type; a single click only selects it.
