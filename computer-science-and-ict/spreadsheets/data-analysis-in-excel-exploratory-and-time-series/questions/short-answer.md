# Data Analysis in Excel — Exploratory and Time-Series Analysis — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is exploratory data analysis?**
**Answer: The first meeting with a dataset — summaries, charts, splits, surprises**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three surprises EDA hunts.**
**Answer: The outlier, the gap, the group unlike its siblings**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does EDA earn?**
**Answer: The questions (and hypotheses) the later work tests**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does EDA not do?**
**Answer: Make claims**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the analyst in EDA compared to?**
**Answer: A detective on a first walk through the scene**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a time series?**
**Answer: A measure taken in time order**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name time's written parts.**
**Answer: Trend, seasonality, cycles and noise**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do moving averages do?**
**Answer: Smooth the noise so the trend shows**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the like-for-like rule?**
**Answer: Compare this June with last June, never with January**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What are the analyst's two first moves?**
**Answer: Look around (explore), then look along time**
