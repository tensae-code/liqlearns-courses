# Hands-on — Data Analysis in Excel — Exploratory and Time-Series Analysis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Explore first** — Take a new small dataset and do a pure EDA pass in Excel or on paper: summaries, one chart, one split — ending with three earned questions, no claims.

## Task two
**Read a series** — Take a 24-month series; plot it, smooth it with a moving average, and write its trend, season and one surprise in three lines.
