# Hands-on — Conditional Formatting — Colouring Cells by Their Values

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Above and below
Take a column of test scores. Apply above-average and below-average highlighting in two colours and check three cells by hand.

## Break it on purpose
Edit one value so it crosses the average. Watch its colour change with no formatting command from you — then write one sentence explaining why.
