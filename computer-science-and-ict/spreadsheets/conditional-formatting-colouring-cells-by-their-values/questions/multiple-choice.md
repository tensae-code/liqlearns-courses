# Conditional Formatting — Colouring Cells by Their Values — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does conditional formatting do?**
a) Colours cells by their values, by rule  b) Unique values and duplicate values  c) No — they change by themselves when values change  d) Its highlighting appears or disappears by itself
**Answer: a) Colours cells by their values, by rule**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when a cell meets the rule?**
a) A new formatting rule — condition plus the formatting it triggers  b) It is highlighted automatically  c) The values, not the colours  d) In the cells of the chosen range
**Answer: b) It is highlighted automatically**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two ready-made value rules.**
a) No — they change by themselves when values change  b) Its highlighting appears or disappears by itself  c) Above average and below average  d) The condition, and the formatting it triggers
**Answer: c) Above average and below average**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What two odd-one-out rules are named?**
a) The values, not the colours  b) In the cells of the chosen range  c) Colours cells by their values, by rule  d) Unique values and duplicate values
**Answer: d) Unique values and duplicate values**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do you build when no ready rule fits?**
a) A new formatting rule — condition plus the formatting it triggers  b) Its highlighting appears or disappears by itself  c) The condition, and the formatting it triggers  d) It is highlighted automatically
**Answer: a) A new formatting rule — condition plus the formatting it triggers**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Are the colours fixed once applied?**
a) In the cells of the chosen range  b) No — they change by themselves when values change  c) Colours cells by their values, by rule  d) Above average and below average
**Answer: b) No — they change by themselves when values change**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the rule watch?**
a) The condition, and the formatting it triggers  b) It is highlighted automatically  c) The values, not the colours  d) Unique values and duplicate values
**Answer: c) The values, not the colours**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when a value crosses the rule after an edit?**
a) Colours cells by their values, by rule  b) Above average and below average  c) A new formatting rule — condition plus the formatting it triggers  d) Its highlighting appears or disappears by itself
**Answer: d) Its highlighting appears or disappears by itself**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what does the highlighting live?**
a) In the cells of the chosen range  b) It is highlighted automatically  c) Unique values and duplicate values  d) No — they change by themselves when values change
**Answer: a) In the cells of the chosen range**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two things does a new rule state?**
a) Above average and below average  b) The condition, and the formatting it triggers  c) A new formatting rule — condition plus the formatting it triggers  d) The values, not the colours
**Answer: b) The condition, and the formatting it triggers**
