# Conditional Formatting — Colouring Cells by Their Values — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does conditional formatting do?**
**Answer: Colours cells by their values, by rule**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when a cell meets the rule?**
**Answer: It is highlighted automatically**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two ready-made value rules.**
**Answer: Above average and below average**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What two odd-one-out rules are named?**
**Answer: Unique values and duplicate values**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do you build when no ready rule fits?**
**Answer: A new formatting rule — condition plus the formatting it triggers**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Are the colours fixed once applied?**
**Answer: No — they change by themselves when values change**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the rule watch?**
**Answer: The values, not the colours**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when a value crosses the rule after an edit?**
**Answer: Its highlighting appears or disappears by itself**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what does the highlighting live?**
**Answer: In the cells of the chosen range**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two things does a new rule state?**
**Answer: The condition, and the formatting it triggers**
