# Subtotals and Grand Totals in a Worksheet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A subtotal is —**
a) The total of the whole worksheet  b) The total of one group of rows in a list  c) A kind of chart  d) The average of a column
**Answer: b) The total of one group of rows in a list — The total of one group of rows in a list — the grand total is the one that covers the whole list.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The grand total in a subtotalled list is worked out with —**
a) Plain SUM over every row including the subtotal rows  b) The SUBTOTAL function, which ignores other subtotal rows in its range  c) A calculator after printing  d) The AVERAGE function
**Answer: b) The SUBTOTAL function, which ignores other subtotal rows in its range — The SUBTOTAL function, which ignores other subtotal rows in its range — that is what stops the grand total double-counting.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Before inserting subtotals you must first —**
a) Print the worksheet  b) Delete the headings  c) Sort the list by the grouping column  d) Merge the cells
**Answer: c) Sort the list by the grouping column — Sort the list by the grouping column — a new group starts every time the value changes, so an unsorted list gives broken groups.**
