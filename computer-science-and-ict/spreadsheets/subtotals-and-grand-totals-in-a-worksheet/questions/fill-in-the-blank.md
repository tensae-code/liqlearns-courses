# Subtotals and Grand Totals in a Worksheet — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Outline level ____ shows only the subtotals and the grand total.**
**Answer: 2 — Level 1 shows the grand total alone; level 3 shows every row.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Excel's Subtotal tool is found on the ____ tab.**
**Answer: Data — The Data tab holds the data-management tools: sort, filter, validation and subtotals.**
