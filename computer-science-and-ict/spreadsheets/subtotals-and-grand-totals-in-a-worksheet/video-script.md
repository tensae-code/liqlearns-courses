# Video script — Subtotals and Grand Totals in a Worksheet

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A list can be added up in pieces. The piece total is a subtotal. The total of everything is the grand total.

Excel builds them for you. But sort first — group by group, or the subtotals lie.

Fold the list with the outline: grand total only, subtotals, or every row. Remove them, and your list is back, untouched.
