# Hands-on — Subtotals and Grand Totals in a Worksheet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the summary
Type a list of at least twelve rows: a grouping column (three classes, shops or months) and a number column, deliberately shuffled. Sort by the grouping column, insert subtotals, and write down each subtotal and the grand total. Check the grand total by adding the subtotals on paper.

## Fold it
Use the outline buttons: show level 1, then 2, then 3, sketching what is visible each time. Then remove all subtotals and confirm the original twelve rows are back, unchanged.
