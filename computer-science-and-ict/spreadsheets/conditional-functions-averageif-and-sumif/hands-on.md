# Hands-on — Conditional Functions — AVERAGEIF and SUMIF in Excel

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Ask conditionally** — From a marks table, answer three conditional questions with SUMIF and AVERAGEIF, reporting each condition with its number.

## Task two
**Bare-number repair** — Take a bare conditional claim ('average price: 40') and rewrite it honestly, naming the condition it must have used and how you'd verify it.
