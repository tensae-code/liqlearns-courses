# Conditional Functions — AVERAGEIF and SUMIF in Excel — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does SUMIF add?**
**Answer: Only values whose row meets the criteria**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name SUMIF's three parts.**
**Answer: Where to test, what to test for, what to gather**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give two criteria examples.**
**Answer: City is Amman; month is June**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What forms can a criteria take?**
**Answer: Equality, a comparison like '>100', or a pattern**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to excluded rows?**
**Answer: They simply do not enter the sum**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does AVERAGEIF compute?**
**Answer: The mean over only the rows meeting the criteria**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When must criteria be stated?**
**Answer: Before computing**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is a condition chosen after seeing results?**
**Answer: A story, not a statistic**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must be reported with a conditional number?**
**Answer: The condition itself**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book teach the pair inside analysis?**
**Answer: Most real questions are conditional**
