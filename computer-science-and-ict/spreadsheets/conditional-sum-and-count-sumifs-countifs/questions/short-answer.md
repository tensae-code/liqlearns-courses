# Conditional Sum and Count — SUMIFS and COUNTIFS — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does SUMIFS do?**
**Answer: Adds only the values whose rows meet every stated condition**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is SUMIFS' argument order?**
**Answer: The summed range first, then pairs of criteria range and criteria**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does that differ from SUMIF?**
**Answer: SUMIF puts its summed range last; SUMIFS puts it first**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does COUNTIFS do?**
**Answer: Counts the rows meeting every condition**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) The pair answers which two question shapes?**
**Answer: 'How much' (SUMIFS) and 'how many' (COUNTIFS)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Under how many conditions?**
**Answer: Any number — every condition must hold**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What stands beside them in the chapter?**
**Answer: The pivot table — the same summarising done by dragging**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What discipline travels with conditional numbers?**
**Answer: State the conditions before computing, and report them with the number**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) A conditional total quoted bare is what?**
**Answer: A whole truth cut in half**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 8, Bab 1 C — data summarising with SUMIFS and COUNTIFS**
