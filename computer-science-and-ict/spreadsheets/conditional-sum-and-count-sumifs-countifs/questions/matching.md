# Conditional Sum and Count — SUMIFS and COUNTIFS — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does SUMIFS do?** → **Adds only the values whose rows meet every stated condition**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is SUMIFS' argument order?** → **The summed range first, then pairs of criteria range and criteria**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does that differ from SUMIF?** → **SUMIF puts its summed range last; SUMIFS puts it first**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does COUNTIFS do?** → **Counts the rows meeting every condition**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) The pair answers which two question shapes?** → **'How much' (SUMIFS) and 'how many' (COUNTIFS)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Under how many conditions?** → **Any number — every condition must hold**
