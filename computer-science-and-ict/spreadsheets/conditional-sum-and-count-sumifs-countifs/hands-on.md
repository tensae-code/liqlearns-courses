# Hands-on — Conditional Sum and Count — SUMIFS and COUNTIFS

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Three conditions** — On a small invented sales table (month, shop, item, amount), write the SUMIFS that totals June's rice sales in the north shop, and the COUNTIFS that counts those rows.

## Task two
**Bare-number repair** — A report says 'sales: 4,200,000'. Write the three questions you must ask before the number means anything, then rewrite the report line properly with its conditions.
