# SmartArt Graphics in a Worksheet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does SmartArt build from your text?**
a) A diagram  b) The graphic  c) Every shape  d) Structure is automatic; your work is the words and the look
**Answer: a) A diagram**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three kinds of SmartArt graphic in this lesson.**
a) As one object — new colours and style for the whole graphic  b) A diagram (process/cycle/hierarchy), a visual list, captioned pictures  c) The arrangement  d) Inside the worksheet
**Answer: b) A diagram (process/cycle/hierarchy), a visual list, captioned pictures**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where can the text be typed?**
a) Every shape  b) Structure is automatic; your work is the words and the look  c) Into the shapes, or the text pane beside them  d) Its own shape
**Answer: c) Into the shapes, or the text pane beside them**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What arranges itself as you type?**
a) The arrangement  b) Inside the worksheet  c) A diagram  d) The graphic
**Answer: d) The graphic**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a SmartArt graphic restyled?**
a) As one object — new colours and style for the whole graphic  b) Structure is automatic; your work is the words and the look  c) Its own shape  d) A diagram (process/cycle/hierarchy), a visual list, captioned pictures
**Answer: a) As one object — new colours and style for the whole graphic**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What updates together when the style changes?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the SmartArt graphic live in this lesson?**
