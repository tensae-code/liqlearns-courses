# SmartArt Graphics in a Worksheet — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does SmartArt build from your text?" is: A diagram**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name three kinds of SmartArt graphic in this lesson." is: Into the shapes, or the text pane beside them**
**Answer: False** — A diagram (process/cycle/hierarchy), a visual list, captioned pictures

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where can the text be typed?" is: Into the shapes, or the text pane beside them**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What arranges itself as you type?" is: As one object — new colours and style for the whole graphic**
**Answer: False** — The graphic

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is a SmartArt graphic restyled?" is: As one object — new colours and style for the whole graphic**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What updates together when the style changes?" is: The arrangement**
**Answer: False** — Every shape

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What stays the same when colours change?" is: The arrangement**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is SmartArt's bargain?" is: Inside the worksheet**
**Answer: False** — Structure is automatic; your work is the words and the look

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does the SmartArt graphic live in this lesson?" is: Inside the worksheet**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does a visual list give each point?" is: A diagram**
**Answer: False** — Its own shape
