# SmartArt Graphics in a Worksheet — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does SmartArt build from your text?**
**Answer: A diagram**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three kinds of SmartArt graphic in this lesson.**
**Answer: A diagram (process/cycle/hierarchy), a visual list, captioned pictures**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where can the text be typed?**
**Answer: Into the shapes, or the text pane beside them**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What arranges itself as you type?**
**Answer: The graphic**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a SmartArt graphic restyled?**
**Answer: As one object — new colours and style for the whole graphic**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What updates together when the style changes?**
**Answer: Every shape**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What stays the same when colours change?**
**Answer: The arrangement**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is SmartArt's bargain?**
**Answer: Structure is automatic; your work is the words and the look**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the SmartArt graphic live in this lesson?**
**Answer: Inside the worksheet**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a visual list give each point?**
**Answer: Its own shape**
