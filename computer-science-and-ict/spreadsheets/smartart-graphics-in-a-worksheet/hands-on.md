# Hands-on — SmartArt Graphics in a Worksheet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Words into shapes
Write three steps of any process as a list, then insert a SmartArt process graphic and type the steps in. Compare: which is easier to grasp at a glance?

## One graphic, three looks
Change your graphic's colours twice and its style once. Note that the words and arrangement never moved.
