# SmartArt Graphics in a Worksheet

## Do it first

Some ideas are shapes, not sentences: steps, parts, a cycle. In this lesson you draw them with SmartArt, right inside the worksheet. (teacher guide — evidence)

## Sub lesson 1 — Inserting SmartArt — diagrams, lists and pictures

**SmartArt** builds a **diagram from your text**: choose a graphic that matches your idea — a process, a cycle, a hierarchy — or a **visual list** that gives each point its own shape, or a picture graphic where each item carries a **captioned picture**. Type the text into the shapes (or the text pane beside them) and the graphic arranges itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — Changing SmartArt colours and styles

The whole graphic is restyled as one object: change its **colours** to a new colour set and its **style** to a new effect, and every shape updates together — the diagram keeps its arrangement while its look changes. That is SmartArt's bargain: the structure is automatic, so your work is the words and the choice of look.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: SmartArt turns words into a diagram inside the worksheet: you insert a graphic — a diagram, a visual list, captioned pictures — type your text into its shapes, and restyle the whole graphic at once with new colours and styles. Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.

## Do It Yourself

1. Show it, don't just say it: A diagram. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
2. Show it, don't just say it: Into the shapes, or the text pane beside them. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
3. Show it, don't just say it: Every shape. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
4. Show it, don't just say it: Structure is automatic; your work is the words and the look. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
