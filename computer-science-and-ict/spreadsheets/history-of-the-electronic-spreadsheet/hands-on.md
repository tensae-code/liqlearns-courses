# Hands-on — History of the Electronic Spreadsheet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Recalculation by hand** — On paper, make a 3-row shopping table with a total. Change one price and redo every affected total by hand. Then do the same in a spreadsheet and change the price once. Time both.

## Task two
**Timeline** — Draw the spreadsheet timeline: paper ledgers, VisiCalc 1979, Lotus 1-2-3 1983, Excel 1985, Google Sheets 2006 — one line of 'what changed' under each.
