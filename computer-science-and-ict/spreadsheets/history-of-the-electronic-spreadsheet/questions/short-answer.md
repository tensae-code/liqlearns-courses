# History of the Electronic Spreadsheet — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What was the paper ledger's great weakness?**
**Answer: Change one number and every total depending on it had to be re-added by hand**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does an electronic spreadsheet do when a number changes?**
**Answer: It recalculates itself — every formula that depends on the number updates**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What was the first successful electronic spreadsheet?**
**Answer: VisiCalc, in 1979**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Who made VisiCalc?**
**Answer: Dan Bricklin and Bob Frankston**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why did businesses buy the Apple II?**
**Answer: To run VisiCalc — the program sold the computer**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What did Lotus 1-2-3 add in 1983?**
**Answer: It made the spreadsheet a business standard on the IBM PC, adding charts and database features**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How did Excel win?**
**Answer: With the graphical interface — clicking cells instead of memorising commands**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What did Google Sheets change in 2006?**
**Answer: It put the spreadsheet online in a browser, free — recalculation for anyone**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What was the winning feature all along?**
**Answer: Automatic recalculation — change a number and the sheet redoes the maths**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) In Armenia's Grade 6 book, what comes right after this history?**
**Answer: The features of using electronic spreadsheets (§11) — the sheet itself**
