# Forecasting and Trendlines in Excel — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does forecasting begin?**
**Answer: By seeing — a scatter plot of the values**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a trendline?**
**Answer: The line that best follows the points**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What can be displayed with a trendline?**
**Answer: Its equation**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is its fit read?**
**Answer: Points hugging the line support it; a cloud mocks it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What fiction does a season-blind trendline forecast?**
**Answer: A climb (or fall) that is really the season's rhythm**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does FORECAST.LINEAR return?**
**Answer: The line's value at a future x**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a forecast extend?**
**Answer: The past's pattern**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two world-changes that break it.**
**Answer: A competitor opens; a law changes (or a season turns)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is a forecast worth less?**
**Answer: The farther it reaches**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is a forecast honestly reported?**
**Answer: With its pattern and its doubts — 'on the trend, about X, if nothing changes'**
