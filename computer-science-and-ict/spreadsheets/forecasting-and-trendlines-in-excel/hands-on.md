# Hands-on — Forecasting and Trendlines in Excel

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Forecast a year** — From twelve months of (real or invented) canteen sales, draw the scatter, fit the trendline and forecast month thirteen — reported with pattern and doubts.

## Task two
**Break a forecast** — Write three events that would break your forecast and the sign in the data that each had arrived.
