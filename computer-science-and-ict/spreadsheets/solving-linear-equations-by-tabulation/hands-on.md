# Hands-on — Solving Linear Equations by Tabulation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Walk to the meeting', 'Tabulate 3x + 2 = 17 for x from 0 upward. Mark the meeting row and state the solution in the lesson's definition.')

## Task two
('Corner the fraction', 'Solve a fractional equation your teacher sets, first with step 1, then with a finer step near the crossing. Write both hunts and the cornered answer.')
