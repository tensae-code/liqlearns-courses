# Solving Linear Equations by Tabulation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What fills the unknown's column?**
**Answer: Growing values the unknown might take**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do the two side columns work out?**
**Answer: The equation's left side and right side at each value**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the meeting row?**
**Answer: The row where the two sides stand equal**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the solution?**
**Answer: The unknown's value in the meeting row**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What algebra does the method need?**
**Answer: Only substitution**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a solution, in the lesson's definition?**
**Answer: The value where the two sides agree**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Do fractional equations change the method?**
**Answer: No — the sheet substitutes and compares exactly as before**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the craftsman's choice in the hunt?**
**Answer: The step's size**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is a coarse crossing cornered?**
**Answer: Walk finer around the crossing**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What did the sequences lesson teach that this one aims?**
**Answer: The sheet carrying a rule forward — here aimed at a meeting**
