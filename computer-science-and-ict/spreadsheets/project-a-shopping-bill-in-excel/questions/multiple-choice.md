# Project: a shopping bill in Excel — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Ebsa shops at —
a) a supermarket chain  b) a local gulit market  c) an online shop  d) the school store
**Answer: b) a local gulit market**

**2.** The formula in E3 multiplies —
a) tax by total  b) price by quantity  c) quantity by tax  d) price by tax rate
**Answer: b) price by quantity**

**3.** The sales tax rate is —
a) 5%  b) 10%  c) 15%  d) 25%
**Answer: c) 15%** — Held in G1.

**4.** The tax is applied to every item in column F using —
a) a relative reference to G1  b) the absolute reference $G$1  c) a typed 15 in every row  d) the fill handle only
**Answer: b) the absolute reference $G$1** — $G$1 stays pinned for every row.

**5.** The grand total stands in —
a) E9  b) F9  c) G9  d) G1
**Answer: c) G9** — It sums all the net prices.

**6.** The net price in G3 is —
a) price − tax  b) total + tax  c) price × quantity  d) tax only
**Answer: b) total + tax**
