# Hands-on — Project: a shopping bill in Excel

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Where there is no computer laboratory, do the screen work on a teacher's or your own phone, as the course's teaching pattern provides.

## Your own gulit list
Replace Ebsa's six items with six things your household buys, with real quantities and prices you know. Rebuild the four formulas and find your own grand total.

## Rate change test
Change the tax in G1 from 15% to 10%. Before looking, say which columns will change and which will not — then check. That prediction is the absolute reference understood.
