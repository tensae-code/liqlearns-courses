# Hands-on — Removing Duplicate Data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Clean a sheet** — Take or invent a 15-row worksheet with three planted duplicates (one a same-name trap). Copy it, choose the identifying columns, remove the true duplicates, and report the rows removed with reasons.

## Task two
**Defend the catch** — Write the cleaning note for your sheet: the rule you used, the count before and after, and the sample you hand-checked.
