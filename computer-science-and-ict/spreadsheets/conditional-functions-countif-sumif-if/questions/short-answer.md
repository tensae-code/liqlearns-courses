# Conditional Functions — COUNTIF, SUMIF and IF — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does COUNTIF do?**
**Answer: Counts the cells in a range that meet one condition**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does SUMIF do?**
**Answer: Adds only the values whose row meets the condition**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the shared pattern?**
**Answer: Name the range, state the condition, gather only what passes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Give a COUNTIF question.**
**Answer: How many pupils scored above 80 (any one-condition counting question)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where do COUNTIFS and SUMIFS live?**
**Answer: In their own lesson in this course — the multi-condition cousins**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does IF return?**
**Answer: One of two answers, chosen by its test**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are IF's three parts?**
**Answer: The test, the value if true, and the value if false**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does IF(mark>=50, "Pass", "Fail") give for 72?**
**Answer: Pass**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do the three functions build in the specialist project?**
**Answer: A cost estimate — COUNTIF/SUMIF its numbers, IF its choices**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How exactly must a condition be stated?**
**Answer: Exactly — the function applies it literally to every row**
