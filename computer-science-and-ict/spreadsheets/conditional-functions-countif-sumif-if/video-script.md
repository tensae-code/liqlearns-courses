# Video script — Conditional Functions — COUNTIF, SUMIF and IF

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**COUNTIF(range, condition)** counts the cells in a range that **meet one condition** — how many pupils scored above 80, how many days it rained. **SUMIF(range, condition, sum_range)** adds — but only the values whose row **meets the condition**: the sales **where** the month is June. Vietnam's Grade 9 track gives each function its own lesson because the pattern is one and worth owning: **name the range, state the condition, gather only what passes**. The multi-condition cousins (COUNTIFS, SUMIFS) live in their own lesson in this course; here the single condition is mastered first.
**IF(test, value_if_true, value_if_false)** makes the sheet **decide**: it evaluates the test and returns one answer or the other — `IF(mark>=50, "Pass", "Fail")`. The Applied specialist book spends the three functions together in its quotation project: COUNTIF and SUMIF build the **estimate's numbers**, and IF turns conditions into choices the estimate acts on. The discipline across all three: a condition must be **stated exactly** — the function applies it literally, to every row, without mercy or common sense.
