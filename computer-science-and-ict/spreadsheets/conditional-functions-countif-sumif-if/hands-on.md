# Hands-on — Conditional Functions — COUNTIF, SUMIF and IF

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Count and add with conditions** — From a class mark list, use COUNTIF for 'how many passed' and SUMIF for 'the total marks of those who passed'. Check both by hand on five rows.

## Task two
**Make it decide** — Add an IF column that labels each mark Pass or Fail, then COUNTIF the labels. Your two answers must agree — if not, find the row that lies.
