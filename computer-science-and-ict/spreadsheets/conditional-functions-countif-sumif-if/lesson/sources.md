Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked for the slug and the live lesson content was checked for near matches before building. The live lessons split this family three ways — SUMIF inside an AVERAGEIF lesson, the multi-condition SUMIFS/COUNTIFS pair, and IF inside logical functions — and COUNTIF itself is taught by no live lesson; Vietnam's track teaches the three single-condition functions as one strand, lesson by lesson. The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — spreadsheets/conditional-functions-countif-sumif-if). It sits in the Spreadsheets title (spreadsheets/), following the book's own order. Provenance scope note: the placements stocked here are from Vietnam's “Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống” (Connecting Knowledge with Life) Tin học books (Vietnamese) — one series, Grades 3–12, from Nhà xuất bản Giáo dục Việt Nam, splitting from Grade 10 into Computer Science (Khoa học máy tính) and Applied Informatics (Tin học ứng dụng) orientations, each with its own specialist-topic (Chuyên đề) volumes; the books are image-only PDFs read from rendered pages. Every placement stocked here is from a student book and counts toward the acceptance count; there are no teacher guides in this country. Media slots in lesson/lesson.md are described slots only ([Picture — …]); no picture numbers are invented.

Vietnam's own way (Grade 9 Tin học 9): Grade 9 teaches simulation software (PhET, Geometer's Sketchpad) as a way to learn by experiment, and its applied fork is advanced Excel (data validation, COUNTIF/SUMIF/IF, a family-finance workbook project) versus a full video-production track in Video Editor from storyboard to export.

Vietnam's own way (Grade 10 Chuyên đề Tin học 10 Ứng dụng): the whole Applied study-topics book is one continuous project — each class group role-plays a travel company advertising Vietnamese destinations at a "Ba miền" tourism fair, producing in turn a Word travel handbook, an Excel quotation program, and a PowerPoint media product.


Kazakhstan's own way (Ataeva & Vyshkova G8 Informatics, NIS): the NIS Grade 8 course is the most distinctive of the wave: vector graphics flows into geographic information systems (planning an excursion, making maps, visualising data in GIS), spreadsheets are used to solve logical problems, loops are taught through mathematics, physics, business and multimedia problems, and the year ends with a full project cycle — six stages, presentation, documentation, defence — plus a mini-project on artificial intelligence.

Kazakhstan's own way (Vyushkova/Zhurbenko/Orazbekov G7 «Информатика» (NIS student book, Russian edition)): the NIS Grade 7 book is organised around data: measuring and representing information (including the von Neumann architecture), business documents, spreadsheets that grow into databases (error checking, sorting/filtering, protecting data from change), raster graphics and logo design, a whole modelling section (modelling games, school life and social problems, forecasting from data), networks and information systems, and linear-structure programming that ends with building your own calculator.

Kazakhstan's own way (Atayeva/Vyushkova/Zhurbenko G8 «Информатика» NIS): the NIS Grade 8 book is the widest of the set: a whole section on vector graphics and geographic information systems (planning an excursion, making maps, visualising data in GIS), octal and hexadecimal coding, loops applied separately to mathematics, physics, business and animation, strings and subroutines, and a nine-step project method ending in a mini-project on artificial intelligence.
