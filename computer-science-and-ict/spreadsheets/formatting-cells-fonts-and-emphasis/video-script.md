# Video script — Formatting cells — fonts and emphasis

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every cell starts the same: Calibri. Plain.
Select your title cell. Home tab.
Font dropdown — hover, and watch it preview live. Pick one.
Size dropdown — bigger. Or type your own size.
Font Color — pick a colour.
Bold: Ctrl+B. Italic: Ctrl+I. Underline: Ctrl+U.
Same words. Now they stand out.
