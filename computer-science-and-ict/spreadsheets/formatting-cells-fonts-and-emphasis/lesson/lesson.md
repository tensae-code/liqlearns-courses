# Formatting cells — fonts and emphasis

## Do it first

Every cell in your sheet wears the same plain dress. Make the title cell stand out — new font, bigger, coloured, bold — one change at a time, in the book's order.

## Font, size, colour and emphasis — in order

All cells start alike (**Calibri**); **font formatting distinguishes them**. The book works a **title cell** through each change:

1. **Select the cells**.
2. **Font** — Home tab, the **font dropdown → pick the font**; hovering gives a **live preview**.
3. **Size** — the **font-size dropdown → pick the size** (or the **Increase / Decrease Font Size** commands, or **type a custom size**).
4. **Colour** — the **Font Color dropdown → pick the colour**.
5. **Emphasis** — the **Bold, Italic and Underline** buttons, with keyboard equivalents **Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I and Ctrl+U**.

## Do It Yourself

1. Take a plain title cell and change its font, watching the live preview as you hover.
2. Set its size three ways: dropdown, Increase Font Size, and a typed custom size.
3. Colour it, then apply Bold, Italic and Underline from the keyboard.
