# Lesson plan — Formatting cells — fonts and emphasis

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Spreadsheets · **Lesson:** Formatting cells — fonts and emphasis
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Application Software, Grade 10 depth, in the Spreadsheets title of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md). The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** cells all start alike in Calibri; a title cell is distinguished by changing its font, its size, its colour, and its emphasis — Bold, Italic, Underline (Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, Ctrl+U).
**This lesson's own level:** it is a doing-lesson. It stands on entering data; this lesson changes how cells look, never what they hold.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say that all cells start in Calibri and formatting distinguishes them.
2. Change a font and a font size, including a typed custom size.
3. Change font colour from the Font Color dropdown.
4. Apply Bold, Italic and Underline by button and by Ctrl+B / Ctrl+I / Ctrl+U.

## What you need
A computer with Excel.

## If the learner is stuck
- No live preview → hover over the font name in the dropdown without clicking; the selected cell previews it.
- A shortcut did nothing → the cell was not selected first; select, then Ctrl+B / Ctrl+I / Ctrl+U.
