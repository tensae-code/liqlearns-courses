# Checkbox Forms in a Spreadsheet — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a form in a spreadsheet?**
**Answer: A sheet area with controls a person operates, not only cells to type in**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a checkbox?**
**Answer: A small box that is ticked or unticked with a click**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What form does the book build?**
**Answer: A customer form for a travel company's quotation program**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is ticked on the book's form?**
**Answer: What the trip includes — meals, guide, insurance**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the form to the working sheet?**
**Answer: Its friendly face**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a linked cell read?**
**Answer: TRUE when the box is ticked, FALSE when it is not**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How do formulas spend a checkbox's truth?**
**Answer: An IF adds a cost only when its box is ticked, for example**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What three rules make a form good?**
**Answer: Labels a stranger understands, boxes grouped by meaning, nothing required left ambiguous**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the form feed?**
**Answer: The quotation — the project's cost estimate**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the quotation only as honest as?**
**Answer: The form's ticks**
