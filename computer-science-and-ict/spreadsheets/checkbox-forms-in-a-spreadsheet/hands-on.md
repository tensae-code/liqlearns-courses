# Hands-on — Checkbox Forms in a Spreadsheet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Build a tick form** — Make a small form with four checkboxes — options for a school trip — each linked to a cell. Watch the TRUE/FALSE cells change as you tick.

## Task two
**Spend the ticks** — Add an IF formula per option that adds its price only when ticked, and a total. Test all unticked, all ticked, and one mixed case.
