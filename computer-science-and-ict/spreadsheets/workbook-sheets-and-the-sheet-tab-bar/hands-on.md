# Hands-on — Workbook Sheets and the Sheet Tab Bar

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build a term workbook
Make a workbook for a school term: rename the three opening sheets for three subjects, and add a fourth sheet for the summary. Enter one mark on each subject sheet. Click through the tabs and say, from the names alone, what each sheet holds.

## Navigate blind
A partner opens a workbook with several named sheets and reads you a tab name. You must reach that sheet and say what work lives on it. Swap roles. Note how much the names matter compared with Sheet1, Sheet2, Sheet3.
