# Workbook Sheets and the Sheet Tab Bar — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an Excel file called?**
a) A workbook  b) Several worksheets — a book of sheets  c) Three  d) Related tables together in one workbook, each on its own sheet
**Answer: a) A workbook**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a workbook hold?**
a) Three  b) Several worksheets — a book of sheets  c) Related tables together in one workbook, each on its own sheet  d) A learner who cannot move between sheets is locked out of most of every workbook
**Answer: b) Several worksheets — a book of sheets**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How many sheets does the book's Excel open with?**
a) Related tables together in one workbook, each on its own sheet  b) A learner who cannot move between sheets is locked out of most of every workbook  c) Three  d) At the bottom of the Excel window
**Answer: c) Three**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does Excel expect real work to be organised?**
a) A learner who cannot move between sheets is locked out of most of every workbook  b) At the bottom of the Excel window  c) By clicking its tab, which brings it to the front  d) Related tables together in one workbook, each on its own sheet
**Answer: d) Related tables together in one workbook, each on its own sheet**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book give the tab bar its own lesson?**
a) A learner who cannot move between sheets is locked out of most of every workbook  b) At the bottom of the Excel window  c) By clicking its tab, which brings it to the front  d) By double-clicking its tab and typing the new name
**Answer: a) A learner who cannot move between sheets is locked out of most of every workbook**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where is the sheet tab bar?**
a) By clicking its tab, which brings it to the front  b) At the bottom of the Excel window  c) By double-clicking its tab and typing the new name  d) With Insert → Worksheet
**Answer: b) At the bottom of the Excel window**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is a sheet selected?**
a) By double-clicking its tab and typing the new name  b) With Insert → Worksheet  c) By clicking its tab, which brings it to the front  d) Because a name like 'January' guides, and 'Sheet1' does not
**Answer: c) By clicking its tab, which brings it to the front**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is a sheet renamed?**
a) With Insert → Worksheet  b) Because a name like 'January' guides, and 'Sheet1' does not  c) A workbook  d) By double-clicking its tab and typing the new name
**Answer: d) By double-clicking its tab and typing the new name**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is a new sheet added?**
a) With Insert → Worksheet  b) Because a name like 'January' guides, and 'Sheet1' does not  c) A workbook  d) Several worksheets — a book of sheets
**Answer: a) With Insert → Worksheet**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why rename Sheet1 to a real name?**
a) A workbook  b) Because a name like 'January' guides, and 'Sheet1' does not  c) Several worksheets — a book of sheets  d) Three
**Answer: b) Because a name like 'January' guides, and 'Sheet1' does not**
