# Workbook Sheets and the Sheet Tab Bar — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an Excel file called?**
**Answer: A workbook**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a workbook hold?**
**Answer: Several worksheets — a book of sheets**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How many sheets does the book's Excel open with?**
**Answer: Three**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does Excel expect real work to be organised?**
**Answer: Related tables together in one workbook, each on its own sheet**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book give the tab bar its own lesson?**
**Answer: A learner who cannot move between sheets is locked out of most of every workbook**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where is the sheet tab bar?**
**Answer: At the bottom of the Excel window**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is a sheet selected?**
**Answer: By clicking its tab, which brings it to the front**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is a sheet renamed?**
**Answer: By double-clicking its tab and typing the new name**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is a new sheet added?**
**Answer: With Insert → Worksheet**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why rename Sheet1 to a real name?**
**Answer: Because a name like 'January' guides, and 'Sheet1' does not**
