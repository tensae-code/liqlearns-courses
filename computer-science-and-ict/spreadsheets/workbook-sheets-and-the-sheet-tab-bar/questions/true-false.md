# Workbook Sheets and the Sheet Tab Bar — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is an Excel file called?" is: A workbook**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a workbook hold?" is: Three**
**Answer: False** — Several worksheets — a book of sheets

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How many sheets does the book's Excel open with?" is: Three**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How does Excel expect real work to be organised?" is: A learner who cannot move between sheets is locked out of most of every workbook**
**Answer: False** — Related tables together in one workbook, each on its own sheet

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why does the book give the tab bar its own lesson?" is: A learner who cannot move between sheets is locked out of most of every workbook**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where is the sheet tab bar?" is: By clicking its tab, which brings it to the front**
**Answer: False** — At the bottom of the Excel window

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is a sheet selected?" is: By clicking its tab, which brings it to the front**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is a sheet renamed?" is: With Insert → Worksheet**
**Answer: False** — By double-clicking its tab and typing the new name

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is a new sheet added?" is: With Insert → Worksheet**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why rename Sheet1 to a real name?" is: A workbook**
**Answer: False** — Because a name like 'January' guides, and 'Sheet1' does not
