# Statistical Hypothesis Testing — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a hypothesis test judge?** → **A claim about a population, using a sample**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the null hypothesis H0?** → **The claim of no effect and no difference — things as they were**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the alternative hypothesis H1?** → **The difference the researcher suspects**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What precise question does the test ask?** → **If H0 were true, how surprising would data like ours be?**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the strand close with this lesson?** → **Every earlier tool — sampling, centres, spread, correlation — is gathered into it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When is the significance level chosen?** → **Before the test — 0.05 is the common choice**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are the two possible verdicts?** → **Reject H0, or do not reject H0**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is H0 never 'proven'?** → **The data can only fail to overthrow it — absence of surprise is not proof**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are the two errors?** → **Rejecting a true H0 (false alarm), and keeping a false H0 (missed difference)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the spreadsheet contribute?** → **The test's arithmetic — the thinking stays the statistician's**
