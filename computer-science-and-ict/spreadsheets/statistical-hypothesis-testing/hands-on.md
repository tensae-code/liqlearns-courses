# Hands-on — Statistical Hypothesis Testing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**State the pair** — For the claim 'the new study method raised marks', state H0 and H1 exactly, and choose a significance level with a reason.

## Task two
**Judge a verdict** — A test at 0.05 does not reject H0. Write the verdict in honest words — and say which of the two errors might still have been made.
