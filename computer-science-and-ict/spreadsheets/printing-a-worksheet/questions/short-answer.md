# Printing a Worksheet — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where do you always start before printing?**
**Answer: In print preview**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does print preview show?**
**Answer: The worksheet exactly as it will land on paper, page by page**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do you fix a badly split table?**
**Answer: In page setup — before printing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What three things does page setup decide here?**
**Answer: Paper size, portrait or landscape direction, and margins**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a print area do?**
**Answer: Marks the chosen part of the sheet as the only part that prints**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What three choices do you make in the Print box?**
**Answer: The printer, the pages to print, and the number of copies**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What paper check do you make at the real printer?**
**Answer: That it holds the paper size and direction your setup promised**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do with the printed sheets first?**
**Answer: Read them like a stranger — checking for clipped columns or near-empty pages**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) A column is clipped on paper. What do you fix?**
**Answer: The setup — then print again, rather than accepting a bad sheet**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why not print first and check later?**
**Answer: Every bad sheet wastes paper — the preview shows the faults for free**
