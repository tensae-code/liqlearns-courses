# Printing a Worksheet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where do you always start before printing?**
a) In print preview  b) The worksheet exactly as it will land on paper, page by page  c) In page setup — before printing  d) Paper size, portrait or landscape direction, and margins
**Answer: a) In print preview**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does print preview show?**
a) In page setup — before printing  b) The worksheet exactly as it will land on paper, page by page  c) Paper size, portrait or landscape direction, and margins  d) Marks the chosen part of the sheet as the only part that prints
**Answer: b) The worksheet exactly as it will land on paper, page by page**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do you fix a badly split table?**
a) Paper size, portrait or landscape direction, and margins  b) Marks the chosen part of the sheet as the only part that prints  c) In page setup — before printing  d) The printer, the pages to print, and the number of copies
**Answer: c) In page setup — before printing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What three things does page setup decide here?**
a) Marks the chosen part of the sheet as the only part that prints  b) The printer, the pages to print, and the number of copies  c) That it holds the paper size and direction your setup promised  d) Paper size, portrait or landscape direction, and margins
**Answer: d) Paper size, portrait or landscape direction, and margins**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a print area do?**
a) Marks the chosen part of the sheet as the only part that prints  b) The printer, the pages to print, and the number of copies  c) That it holds the paper size and direction your setup promised  d) Read them like a stranger — checking for clipped columns or near-empty pages
**Answer: a) Marks the chosen part of the sheet as the only part that prints**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What three choices do you make in the Print box?**
a) That it holds the paper size and direction your setup promised  b) The printer, the pages to print, and the number of copies  c) Read them like a stranger — checking for clipped columns or near-empty pages  d) The setup — then print again, rather than accepting a bad sheet
**Answer: b) The printer, the pages to print, and the number of copies**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What paper check do you make at the real printer?**
a) Read them like a stranger — checking for clipped columns or near-empty pages  b) The setup — then print again, rather than accepting a bad sheet  c) That it holds the paper size and direction your setup promised  d) Every bad sheet wastes paper — the preview shows the faults for free
**Answer: c) That it holds the paper size and direction your setup promised**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do with the printed sheets first?**
a) The setup — then print again, rather than accepting a bad sheet  b) Every bad sheet wastes paper — the preview shows the faults for free  c) In print preview  d) Read them like a stranger — checking for clipped columns or near-empty pages
**Answer: d) Read them like a stranger — checking for clipped columns or near-empty pages**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) A column is clipped on paper. What do you fix?**
a) The setup — then print again, rather than accepting a bad sheet  b) Every bad sheet wastes paper — the preview shows the faults for free  c) In print preview  d) The worksheet exactly as it will land on paper, page by page
**Answer: a) The setup — then print again, rather than accepting a bad sheet**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why not print first and check later?**
a) In print preview  b) Every bad sheet wastes paper — the preview shows the faults for free  c) The worksheet exactly as it will land on paper, page by page  d) In page setup — before printing
**Answer: b) Every bad sheet wastes paper — the preview shows the faults for free**
