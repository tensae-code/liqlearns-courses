# Hands-on — Printing a Worksheet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Preview before paper
Open a worksheet and read it in print preview. Write down every fault you can see — spills, splits, stray pages — before a single sheet is printed.

## Print one perfect copy
Fix the faults in page setup, set the print area to your table, and print exactly one copy. Inspect it, and only when it is right, print the number of copies you actually need.
