# Video script — Printing a Worksheet

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Always start in print preview: the screen shows the worksheet exactly as it will land on paper, page by page. If the table is split badly, fix it in page setup before printing — paper size, portrait or landscape direction, and margins — and, for a wide table, the setting that fits it to one page wide. You can also mark a print area so only the chosen part of the sheet prints, not every stray cell around it.
When the preview is right, open Print and choose: the printer, the pages to print (all, or a range), and the number of copies. Check the real printer holds the paper your setup promised — size and direction — then print. Take the sheets and read them like a stranger: if a column is clipped or a page is nearly empty, the fault is in the setup, so fix the setup and print again rather than accepting a bad sheet.
