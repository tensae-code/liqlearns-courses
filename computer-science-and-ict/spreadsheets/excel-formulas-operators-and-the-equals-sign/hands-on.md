# Hands-on — Excel formulas — operators and the equals sign

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Where there is no computer laboratory, do the screen work on a teacher's or your own phone, as the course's teaching pattern provides.

## Operator hunt
Write the six operators on six cards. A partner calls a job — "multiply", "percent", "power" — and you hold up the right sign, then say one formula that uses it.

## Result or formula?
In a real sheet, enter =5+3*3/2-4 in A1. Select the cell and read the formula bar. Double-click the cell. Then switch Show Formulas on and off, saying each time what the cell is showing.
