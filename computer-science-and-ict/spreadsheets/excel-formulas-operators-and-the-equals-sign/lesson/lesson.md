# Excel formulas — operators and the equals sign

## Do it first

Type 5+3*3/2-4 into a cell as plain text. Nothing happens — it just sits there. Now type the same thing with an equals sign in front of it. The cell answers. That one sign is this lesson.

## Spreadsheet software and formulas

Spreadsheet software **organises, stores and analyses data in tables**. Excel is the example program. Its most powerful feature is **calculating with formulas** — add, subtract, multiply, divide, and more.

## The six operators

| Sign | Job |
|---|---|
| + | addition |
| − | subtraction |
| * | multiplication |
| / | division |
| % | percent |
| ^ | exponentiation (a power) |

Every formula **begins with =** so the program can tell it from data. Type the worked entry **=5+3*3/2-4** into cell A1 — typed without spaces — and press Enter: the cell shows the result.

## Seeing the formula behind the result

A cell shows the **result**. The formula itself is seen three ways: in the **formula bar** when the cell is selected; **in the cell** when you double-click it for editing; or **everywhere at once** — Formulas tab → **Show Formulas**. Click Show Formulas again to hide them. While formulas show, results do not.
