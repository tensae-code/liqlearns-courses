# Excel formulas — operators and the equals sign — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A cell shows the result, while the formula bar shows the formula.
**Answer: True**

**2.** While Show Formulas is on, the cells show their results.
**Answer: False — while formulas show, results do not.**

**3.** The worked entry =5+3*3/2-4 is typed without spaces into A1.
**Answer: True**

**4.** LibreOffice's spreadsheet program is called Excel.
**Answer: False — it is Calc; the guide's "LibreOffice Excel" is its slip.**
