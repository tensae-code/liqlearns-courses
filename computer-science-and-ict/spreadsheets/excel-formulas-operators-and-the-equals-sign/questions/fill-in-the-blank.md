# Excel formulas — operators and the equals sign — fill in the blank

Fill in the blank.

**1.** Every formula begins with the ___ sign.
**Answer: =**

**2.** Double-clicking a cell shows the formula ___ the cell, ready for editing.
**Answer: in / inside**

**3.** The % operator means ___.
**Answer: percent**

**4.** After parentheses come ______ in the order of operations.
**Answer: exponentials**

**5.** A simple formula has ______ operator(s).
**Answer: one**
