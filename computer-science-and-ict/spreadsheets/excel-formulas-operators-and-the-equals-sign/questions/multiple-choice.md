# Excel formulas — operators and the equals sign — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which sign must every formula begin with?
a) %  b) =  c) ^  d) +
**Answer: b) =** — Without =, the entry is data, not a formula.

**2.** The sign * means —
a) percent  b) division  c) multiplication  d) a power
**Answer: c) multiplication**

**3.** The sign ^ means —
a) percent  b) exponentiation — a power  c) subtraction  d) addition
**Answer: b) exponentiation — a power**

**4.** Where do you read a selected cell's formula?
a) the status bar  b) the formula bar  c) the title bar  d) the scroll bar
**Answer: b) the formula bar**

**5.** Show Formulas lives on —
a) the Home tab  b) the Formulas tab  c) the View tab only  d) the File tab
**Answer: b) the Formulas tab** — Clicking it again hides the formulas; while formulas show, results do not.

**6.** Spreadsheet software organises, stores and analyses —
a) pictures  b) data in tables  c) music  d) letters only
**Answer: b) data in tables**
