# Inserting Objects — Pictures and Shapes in Excel — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does inserting objects mean?**
**Answer: Bringing things that are not cell data into the worksheet**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the two main kinds of object inserted.**
**Answer: Pictures and shapes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does an inserted object sit?**
**Answer: On the sheet, over the grid**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What can be done with an inserted object?**
**Answer: It can be moved and sized where the worksheet needs it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What carries on underneath an object?**
**Answer: The cells and their values**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a picture show?**
**Answer: The real thing the numbers are about**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a shape do? Give one job.**
**Answer: It points, groups or explains**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two example shapes.**
**Answer: An arrow and a box (or a callout)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the book place this lesson in the Excel unit?**
**Answer: In the formatting part — after data is entered and formatted**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What comes after objects in the unit?**
**Answer: Laying out the worksheet for printing**
