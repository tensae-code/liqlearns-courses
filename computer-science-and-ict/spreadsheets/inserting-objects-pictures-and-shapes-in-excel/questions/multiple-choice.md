# Inserting Objects — Pictures and Shapes in Excel — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does inserting objects mean?**
a) Bringing things that are not cell data into the worksheet  b) Pictures and shapes  c) It can be moved and sized where the worksheet needs it  d) The real thing the numbers are about
**Answer: a) Bringing things that are not cell data into the worksheet**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the two main kinds of object inserted.**
a) On the sheet, over the grid  b) Pictures and shapes  c) The cells and their values  d) It points, groups or explains
**Answer: b) Pictures and shapes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does an inserted object sit?**
a) It can be moved and sized where the worksheet needs it  b) The real thing the numbers are about  c) On the sheet, over the grid  d) An arrow and a box (or a callout)
**Answer: c) On the sheet, over the grid**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What can be done with an inserted object?**
a) The cells and their values  b) It points, groups or explains  c) In the formatting part — after data is entered and formatted  d) It can be moved and sized where the worksheet needs it
**Answer: d) It can be moved and sized where the worksheet needs it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What carries on underneath an object?**
a) The cells and their values  b) The real thing the numbers are about  c) An arrow and a box (or a callout)  d) Laying out the worksheet for printing
**Answer: a) The cells and their values**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a picture show?**
a) It points, groups or explains  b) The real thing the numbers are about  c) In the formatting part — after data is entered and formatted  d) Bringing things that are not cell data into the worksheet
**Answer: b) The real thing the numbers are about**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a shape do? Give one job.**
a) An arrow and a box (or a callout)  b) Laying out the worksheet for printing  c) It points, groups or explains  d) Pictures and shapes
**Answer: c) It points, groups or explains**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two example shapes.**
a) In the formatting part — after data is entered and formatted  b) Bringing things that are not cell data into the worksheet  c) On the sheet, over the grid  d) An arrow and a box (or a callout)
**Answer: d) An arrow and a box (or a callout)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the book place this lesson in the Excel unit?**
a) In the formatting part — after data is entered and formatted  b) Laying out the worksheet for printing  c) Pictures and shapes  d) It can be moved and sized where the worksheet needs it
**Answer: a) In the formatting part — after data is entered and formatted**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What comes after objects in the unit?**
a) Bringing things that are not cell data into the worksheet  b) Laying out the worksheet for printing  c) On the sheet, over the grid  d) The cells and their values
**Answer: b) Laying out the worksheet for printing**
