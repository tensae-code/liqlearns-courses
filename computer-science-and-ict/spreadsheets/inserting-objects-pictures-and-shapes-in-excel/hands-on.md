# Hands-on — Inserting Objects — Pictures and Shapes in Excel

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Finish a sheet with objects
Take a plain data worksheet. Insert one picture the numbers are about and one shape that points at the most important figure. Say why each earned its place.

## Move without breaking
Move and resize your inserted picture twice. Check the cells underneath still hold their values — the object rides on the sheet, not in it.
