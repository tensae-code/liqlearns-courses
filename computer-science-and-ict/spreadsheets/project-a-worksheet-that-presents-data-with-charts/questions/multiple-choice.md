# Project — a Worksheet That Presents Data with Charts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the group choose first?**
a) A topic  b) Its honesty or its errors  c) The right kind for each question the topic raises  d) From the data to the chart to the conclusion
**Answer: a) A topic**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What fills the worksheet?**
a) Charts built from the worksheet  b) The topic's real data  c) So an audience reads it at a glance  d) A group
**Answer: b) The topic's real data**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a good foundation worksheet?**
a) The right kind for each question the topic raises  b) From the data to the chart to the conclusion  c) Tidy rows and columns, clear labels, checkable values  d) The data, the chart and the conclusion — all three
**Answer: c) Tidy rows and columns, clear labels, checkable values**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does every chart inherit from the worksheet?**
a) So an audience reads it at a glance  b) A group  c) A topic  d) Its honesty or its errors
**Answer: d) Its honesty or its errors**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What presents the data?**
a) Charts built from the worksheet  b) From the data to the chart to the conclusion  c) The data, the chart and the conclusion — all three  d) The topic's real data
**Answer: a) Charts built from the worksheet**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is the chart kind chosen?**
a) A group  b) The right kind for each question the topic raises  c) A topic  d) Tidy rows and columns, clear labels, checkable values
**Answer: b) The right kind for each question the topic raises**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How must a chart be formatted?**
a) The data, the chart and the conclusion — all three  b) The topic's real data  c) So an audience reads it at a glance  d) Its honesty or its errors
**Answer: c) So an audience reads it at a glance**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What walk must the group be able to make?**
a) A topic  b) Tidy rows and columns, clear labels, checkable values  c) Charts built from the worksheet  d) From the data to the chart to the conclusion
**Answer: d) From the data to the chart to the conclusion**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who works on the project?**
a) A group  b) The topic's real data  c) Its honesty or its errors  d) The right kind for each question the topic raises
**Answer: a) A group**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must the group answer for?**
a) Tidy rows and columns, clear labels, checkable values  b) The data, the chart and the conclusion — all three  c) Charts built from the worksheet  d) So an audience reads it at a glance
