# Project — a Worksheet That Presents Data with Charts — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the group choose first?**
**Answer: A topic**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What fills the worksheet?**
**Answer: The topic's real data**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a good foundation worksheet?**
**Answer: Tidy rows and columns, clear labels, checkable values**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does every chart inherit from the worksheet?**
**Answer: Its honesty or its errors**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What presents the data?**
**Answer: Charts built from the worksheet**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is the chart kind chosen?**
**Answer: The right kind for each question the topic raises**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How must a chart be formatted?**
**Answer: So an audience reads it at a glance**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What walk must the group be able to make?**
**Answer: From the data to the chart to the conclusion**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who works on the project?**
**Answer: A group**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must the group answer for?**
**Answer: The data, the chart and the conclusion — all three**
