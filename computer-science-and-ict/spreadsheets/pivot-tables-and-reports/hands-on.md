# Hands-on — Pivot Tables and Reports

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## First pivot
Make a list of about twenty sales (product, month, amount). Build a pivot totalling amount by product, then repivot to product by month. Write the two questions your two reports answer.

## Change the summary
On the same list, change the Values summary from sum to count, then to average. Write one sentence on what each version reveals that the others hide.
