**Acceptance count: taught in 6 countries (North Macedonia, Laos, Jordan, Indonesia, Vietnam, Kazakhstan).**

Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (North Macedonia, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked for the slug and for near matches before building. The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — spreadsheets/pivot-tables-and-reports). It sits in the Spreadsheets title (spreadsheets/), following the book's own order.

North Macedonia's own way (Year 1): five themes move from modern digital technology and online living into a full structured-programming course taught in C++ (with Scratch/C++/Java/Python/PHP/Lisp compared), then advanced Word work (styles, contents, templates) and spreadsheets up to pivot tables.

Laos's own way (Grade 12 ICT student book): the final year is a modern, applied course — problem-solving method, a long English-titled internet-safety sequence (Internet Safety, Email Tips for Scams and Spam, Smart Social Networking, Cyber harassment/Stalking/Addiction), data summaries and PivotTables for decision-making, copyright and Creative Commons licensing, a full PowerPoint course, and choosing technology for your future occupation.

Jordan's own way (Jordan Grade 10 G10T1 Student): Grade 10 Term 1 is a data year: data and binary representation first, then big data and visualisation, then a long practical Excel lesson (text functions, AVERAGEIF/SUMIF, VLOOKUP, Pivot Tables, FORECAST and the Analysis ToolPak); the second unit builds web pages in HTML topic by topic.

Indonesia's own way (Indonesia Grade 8 G8 Informatika student): Grade 8 deepens the four elements: spreadsheet data analysis (lookup, pivot), computational thinking through functions, sets and number systems, and programming that moves from Scratch to Blockly Games, procedural blocks and a physical Ozobot robot.

Vietnam's own way (Grade 12 Chuyên đề Tin học 12 Ứng dụng): three workplace crafts — project management in the open-source GanttProject (schedule, people, budget, progress), installing/uninstalling software and an operating system with data protection, and a real statistics course inside the spreadsheet: probability and random sampling, measures of centre and spread, PivotTables, correlation and hypothesis testing.


Kazakhstan's own way (Shaniev G9 Informatics): the bilingual Grade 9 book (taught in English for the trilingual programme) opens with computer literacy including netiquette and pricing a computer configuration, teaches databases practically (design, build, sort/filter, pivot tables, charts, a "family shop" mini-project), then Python lists in depth — and finishes with a whole chapter of 2D game programming in PyGame.

Kazakhstan's own way (Yerzhanov G9 Informatics, English-medium): the English-medium twin of the Mektep Grade 9 book — the same four chapters (computer literacy with netiquette and pricing a computer, database basics with pivot tables and a "family shop" mini-project, Python lists, PyGame 2D games ending in a Program Arcade Game) taught in English with trilingual terminology.

Kazakhstan's own way (Shaniyev team G9 «INFORMATICS»): the Astana-kitap Grade 9 English-medium book is where Kazakhstan teaches databases properly — design, build, sort/filter, pivot tables and charts, ending in a "family shop" mini project — and then spends two chapters on Python lists (one- and two-dimensional, with search/swap/sort/insert/delete) and a full PyGame 2D games chapter.
