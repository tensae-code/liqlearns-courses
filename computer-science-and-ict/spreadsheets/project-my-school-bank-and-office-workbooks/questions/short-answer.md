# Project — My School, Bank and Office Workbooks — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How many workbooks does the project build?**
**Answer: Three — school, bank and office**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What file type are they?**
**Answer: .xlsx workbooks**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the school workbook keep?**
**Answer: School numbers — pupils, marks, attendance**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the bank workbook's heart?**
**Answer: The formula computing the running balance (deposits, withdrawals, balances)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the office workbook keep?**
**Answer: A workplace's lists and totals**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which earlier skills are demanded together? Name three.**
**Answer: Rows/columns/cells, formatting, formulas starting with = (also fill handle, saving/opening)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Is anything in the project new?**
**Answer: No — the journey's lessons spent together**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the project examine?**
**Answer: Whether the four 'My Special Table' lessons became skills**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How many finished workbooks are handed in?**
**Answer: One per world — three, presentable and reopenable**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book sets this project?**
