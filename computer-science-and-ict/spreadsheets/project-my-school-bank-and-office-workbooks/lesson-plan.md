# Lesson plan — Project — My School, Bank and Office Workbooks

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Spreadsheets · **Lesson:** Project — My School, Bank and Office Workbooks
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Kuwait (2026-11-14) — in the title Spreadsheets on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Kuwait's student books (Ministry of Education, State of Kuwait), because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree and live lesson content were checked for the slug and for near matches before building.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Kuwait's Excel project — building three real workbooks (.xlsx): the school workbook, the bank workbook and the office workbook
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Three workbooks, three worlds** — The project builds three **.xlsx workbooks**, one per world the pupil knows. The **school workbook** keeps school numbers — pupils, marks, attendance — in tables with the journey's formatting and formulas. The **bank workbook** keeps money's records — deposits, withdrawals, balances — where the formula that computes the running balance is the workbook's heart. The **office workbook** keeps a workplace's lists and totals. Three worlds, one craft: each is a workbook of rows and columns, formatted cells and working formulas, saved properly and reopened whole.
2. **Working the project — the journey's skills, together** — Nothing in the project is new: launching and keeping workbooks, rows columns and cells, formatting, formulas that start with =, and the fill handle are all demanded **together**, without a lesson title to say which comes next. That is the project's examination: the journey's four lessons ('My Special Table' 1–4) were skills, and the three workbooks are where the pupil proves they can be spent without instructions — one finished, presentable workbook per world.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
