# Moving Around a Workbook — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the active cell?**
**Answer: The cell with the thick border — the cell you are working in**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do the arrow keys do in a sheet?**
**Answer: Move the active cell one cell at a time**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does Ctrl+Home do?**
**Answer: Returns the active cell to A1, the start of the sheet**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does the Name Box move you?**
**Answer: Type a cell reference such as M50 and press Enter — the active cell jumps there**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the difference between scrolling and moving?**
**Answer: Scrolling changes what you see; moving changes which cell is active**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where are a workbook's sheets found?**
**Answer: Behind the sheet tabs at the bottom of the window**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How do you bring another sheet to the front?**
**Answer: Click its sheet tab**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What are the tab arrows for?**
**Answer: Scrolling the row of sheet tabs when there are too many to show**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are the two halves of moving around a workbook?**
**Answer: Tabs between sheets; keys and the Name Box inside a sheet**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Fiji teach moving before creating?**
**Answer: A learner who cannot travel cannot check, correct or find anything in a big workbook**
