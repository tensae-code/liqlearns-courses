# Regression Analysis and the Regression Line — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does regression fit?**
**Answer: A straight line through a scatter of points, summarising the relationship**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the line's slope say?**
**Answer: About how much y grows when x grows by one**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the least-squares rule?**
**Answer: Choose the line whose squared distances from points to line are smallest in total**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why squares again?**
**Answer: So big misses count heavily and no point pulls the line cheaply**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What two numbers does the line's equation give?**
**Answer: Its slope and its intercept**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the line let the analyst do?**
**Answer: Estimate y for an x the table never measured**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why are estimates inside the measured range safer?**
**Answer: They rest on evidence all around them**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is extrapolation?**
**Answer: Estimating beyond the measured range, assuming the climb continues unchanged**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the standing honesty about a fitted model?**
**Answer: It summarises the past it was fitted on; the future is allowed to differ**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does regression gather from the earlier analysis lessons?**
**Answer: Organised data, centre, spread and relationship — into one model**
