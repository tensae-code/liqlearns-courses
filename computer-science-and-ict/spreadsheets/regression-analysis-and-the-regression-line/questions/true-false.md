# Regression Analysis and the Regression Line — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does regression fit?" is: A straight line through a scatter of points, summarising the relationship**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the line's slope say?" is: Choose the line whose squared distances from points to line are smallest in total**
**Answer: False** — About how much y grows when x grows by one

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the least-squares rule?" is: Choose the line whose squared distances from points to line are smallest in total**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why squares again?" is: Its slope and its intercept**
**Answer: False** — So big misses count heavily and no point pulls the line cheaply

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What two numbers does the line's equation give?" is: Its slope and its intercept**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the line let the analyst do?" is: They rest on evidence all around them**
**Answer: False** — Estimate y for an x the table never measured

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why are estimates inside the measured range safer?" is: They rest on evidence all around them**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is extrapolation?" is: It summarises the past it was fitted on; the future is allowed to differ**
**Answer: False** — Estimating beyond the measured range, assuming the climb continues unchanged

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the standing honesty about a fitted model?" is: It summarises the past it was fitted on; the future is allowed to differ**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does regression gather from the earlier analysis lessons?" is: A straight line through a scatter of points, summarising the relationship**
**Answer: False** — Organised data, centre, spread and relationship — into one model
