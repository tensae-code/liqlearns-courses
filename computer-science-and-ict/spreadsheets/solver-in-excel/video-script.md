# Video script — Solver in Excel

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Solver is Excel's optimiser: it changes several input cells at once, within limits you set, to make a chosen result as large, as small, or exactly a value you name The learner sets the objective cell, the variable cells Solver may change, and the constraints they must obey, then runs Solver and reads its solution Because the best answer under limits — cheapest plan, biggest output — is rarely found by trying values one at a time.
