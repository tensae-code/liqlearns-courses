# Hands-on — Protecting Worksheet Data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lock the formulas
Build a small sheet with two input cells and one formula cell. Unlock the inputs, protect the sheet, and test: typing in inputs works, typing over the formula is refused.

## Password discipline
Protect a practice sheet with a password, write the password where you would keep it for real work, then unprotect. Write one sentence on where such passwords should live.
