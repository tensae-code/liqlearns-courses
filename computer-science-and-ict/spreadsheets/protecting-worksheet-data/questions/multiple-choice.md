# Protecting Worksheet Data — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the Locked setting do by itself?**
a) Nothing until the sheet is protected  b) Unlock the cells meant for typing  c) Sheet protection  d) The edit is refused
**Answer: a) Nothing until the sheet is protected**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the correct first step?**
a) Sheet protection  b) Unlock the cells meant for typing  c) The edit is refused  d) The ones unlocked beforehand
**Answer: b) Unlock the cells meant for typing**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is switched on second?**
a) The edit is refused  b) The ones unlocked beforehand  c) Sheet protection  d) Unprotecting the sheet
**Answer: c) Sheet protection**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when you type in a locked cell of a protected sheet?**
a) The ones unlocked beforehand  b) Unprotecting the sheet  c) You cannot unprotect your own sheet easily  d) The edit is refused
**Answer: d) The edit is refused**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which cells stay open on a protected sheet?**
a) The ones unlocked beforehand  b) Unprotecting the sheet  c) You cannot unprotect your own sheet easily  d) Accidents and casual edits
**Answer: a) The ones unlocked beforehand**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a protection password guard?**
a) You cannot unprotect your own sheet easily  b) Unprotecting the sheet  c) Accidents and casual edits  d) No
**Answer: b) Unprotecting the sheet**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the risk of a forgotten protection password?**
a) Accidents and casual edits  b) No  c) You cannot unprotect your own sheet easily  d) So its formulas and headings stay intact while others fill it in
**Answer: c) You cannot unprotect your own sheet easily**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does protection mainly guard against?**
a) No  b) So its formulas and headings stay intact while others fill it in  c) Nothing until the sheet is protected  d) Accidents and casual edits
**Answer: d) Accidents and casual edits**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Is sheet protection the same as file encryption?**
a) No  b) So its formulas and headings stay intact while others fill it in  c) Nothing until the sheet is protected  d) Unlock the cells meant for typing
**Answer: a) No**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does a shared input sheet need protection?**
a) Nothing until the sheet is protected  b) So its formulas and headings stay intact while others fill it in  c) Unlock the cells meant for typing  d) Sheet protection
**Answer: b) So its formulas and headings stay intact while others fill it in**
