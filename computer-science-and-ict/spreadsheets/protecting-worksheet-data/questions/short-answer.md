# Protecting Worksheet Data — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the Locked setting do by itself?**
**Answer: Nothing until the sheet is protected**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the correct first step?**
**Answer: Unlock the cells meant for typing**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is switched on second?**
**Answer: Sheet protection**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when you type in a locked cell of a protected sheet?**
**Answer: The edit is refused**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which cells stay open on a protected sheet?**
**Answer: The ones unlocked beforehand**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a protection password guard?**
**Answer: Unprotecting the sheet**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the risk of a forgotten protection password?**
**Answer: You cannot unprotect your own sheet easily**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does protection mainly guard against?**
**Answer: Accidents and casual edits**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Is sheet protection the same as file encryption?**
**Answer: No**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does a shared input sheet need protection?**
**Answer: So its formulas and headings stay intact while others fill it in**
