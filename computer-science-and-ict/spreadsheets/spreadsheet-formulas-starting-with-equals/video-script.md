# Video script — Spreadsheet formulas — starting with =

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two cells hold numbers. The third must answer by itself.

Select the cell.
Type the equal sign. Every formula begins with equals.
Click B2 — or type its address.
Enter the operator: minus.
Click B3 — or type its address.
Press Enter.

The result stands in the cell.
Change a number — the answer changes with it.
That is a formula.
