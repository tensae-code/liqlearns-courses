Sri Lanka's own way (Grade 9 Reading Book): it opens with preparing a computer specification for a real buyer (user needs, peripherals, processor/HDD/monitor/VGA specs, after-sales service); microcontrollers are taught with BBC micro:bit and Arduino and local sensor examples (solar lamp, washing machine, microwave).

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 9, Digital Technology): Grade 9 turns practical — a full Python course (Thonny IDE) inside the book, website building with WordPress and Google services for a citizen-service site, and hands-on networking between two school computers. The NCTB Digital Technology line runs in parallel English and Bangla editions; placements are cited from the English Version with the Bangla Version parallel spot-checked.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 5 G5 student (Antares)): One of two different Grade 5 books in the set (this one by Velijanyan & Ayvazyan, Antares); it runs as numbered § lessons mixing information theory, careful judgement of fake vs true information, and a long practical spine — text editor, spreadsheets, algorithms and variables — ending in teamwork principles.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 7 G7 teacher): SERIES MISMATCH NOTED — this guide (Velijanyan & Aslanyan) is the teacher guide for the OTHER Grade 7 series, whose student book (§1 Electronic security … §23 Computer hardware maintenance, Python-based) is NOT among the records; it does not match the Topchyan & Melkonyan Grade 7 student book filed as Book 12. The guide is complete in itself: 20 themes, a thematic plan, the student book's questions/tasks with answers for §1–§23, worked Python solutions, and assessment rubrics.

Vietnam's own way (Grade 7 Tin học 7): Grade 7 is the spreadsheet year (five Excel lessons in a row, in Windows/Excel/PowerPoint named openly) and its computer-science strand is pure algorithms with no code at all — sequential search, binary search and sorting acted out and flowcharted before any language appears.

South Sudan's own way (Grade 10 S2 Student): Year 2 moves from single-computer skills to connected and analytical work — networks taught through intranet file-sharing and a full Microsoft Outlook unit (contacts, calendar, meetings, task lists), then a deep Excel unit that goes well beyond basics into lookup/financial/text functions, inventories, correlation and regression, what-if analysis with Solver, and workbook protection; social media is taught platform by platform with its ethical and legal issues.


Pakistan's own way (Grade 8 Computer Education 8): the Grade 8 book is the older National-Curriculum-2006 Punjab book (not SNC): it teaches networks through physical media and cutting-edge wireless (cellular, satellite, GPS, Bluetooth), a whole unit of hands-on antivirus management (definitions, updates, an AVG whole-computer scan), Excel 2007 taught through three full worked reports (monthly expenses, salaries, school results), and programming in GW-BASIC with line numbers — the pre-SNC Punjab way into code.
