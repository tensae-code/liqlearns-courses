# Spreadsheet formulas — starting with = — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A formula is —
a) a picture in a cell  b) an expression that operates on values in a range of cells or a cell  c) a kind of chart  d) a saved file
**Answer: b) an expression that operates on values in a range of cells or a cell.**

**2.** Every formula begins with —
a) a plus sign  b) the word SUM  c) the equal sign (=)  d) a cell address
**Answer: c) the equal sign (=)** — formulas always begin with =.

**3.** The first step in building a formula is —
a) press Enter  b) select a cell  c) enter the operator  d) type the result
**Answer: b) select a cell** — the cell where the formula will stand.

**4.** After typing =, the next step is —
a) press Enter at once  b) close the spreadsheet  c) select a cell or type its address  d) print the sheet
**Answer: c) select a cell or type its address** — the first value the formula works on.

**5.** When you press Enter at the end, —
a) the sheet closes  b) the result of the calculation appears in the cell with the formula  c) the formula is deleted  d) a new sheet opens
**Answer: b) the result of the calculation appears in the cell with the formula.**
