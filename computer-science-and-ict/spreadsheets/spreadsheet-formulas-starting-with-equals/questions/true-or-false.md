# Spreadsheet formulas — starting with = — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** A formula can refer to the values in other cells.
**Answer: True** — it operates on values in a range of cells or a cell, by their addresses.

**2.** A formula begins with the operator.
**Answer: False** — it begins with the equal sign; the operator comes after the first cell address.

**3.** If a cell a formula refers to changes, the formula's result can change too.
**Answer: True** — the formula works on the cells' values, not on a frozen number.
