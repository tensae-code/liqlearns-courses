# Hands-on — Spreadsheet formulas — starting with =

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Build it for real

Open a spreadsheet. Put 9 in B2 and 4 in B3. In B4, build the formula by the six steps: select B4, type =, click B2, type −, click B3, press Enter. Check that B4 shows 5.

## Break it and mend it

Type the same thing without the equal sign and look: the cell shows text, not an answer. Now fix it by putting = first. Seeing the difference once fixes the rule forever: formulas always begin with =.
