# Quick Analysis and Recommended Charts in Excel

## Do it first

You have the numbers selected and thirty seconds. In this lesson Excel does the suggesting — through Quick Analysis and its recommended charts. (teacher guide — evidence)

## Sub lesson 1 — The Quick Analysis tool

Select a block of data and the **Quick Analysis** tool appears: one button opening a small gallery of instant help for exactly that selection — **formatting, charts, totals, tables** and sparklines. It exists for speed: the common analyses of a selection are offered before you go hunting through the ribbon.

[Picture — the quick analysis tool]

## Sub lesson 2 — Recommended charts

Inside Quick Analysis (and the Insert ribbon), **recommended charts** are the chart kinds **Excel suggests for your data**: it reads the selection — categories with values, values over time — and proposes the chart types that would show it honestly. You still choose; but the recommendation is a strong first answer, and previewing each suggestion against the data teaches which chart suits which data.
