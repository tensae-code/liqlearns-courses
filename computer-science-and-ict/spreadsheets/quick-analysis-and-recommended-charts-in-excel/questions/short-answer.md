# Quick Analysis and Recommended Charts in Excel — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) When does the Quick Analysis tool appear?**
**Answer: When you select a block of data**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Quick Analysis offer for the selection?**
**Answer: Formatting, charts, totals, tables and sparklines**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the tool exist?**
**Answer: For speed — common analyses offered in one place**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are recommended charts?**
**Answer: Chart kinds Excel picks to suit the selected data**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does Excel read before recommending?**
**Answer: The selection — categories with values, values over time**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Who still makes the final choice of chart?**
**Answer: You do**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does previewing each suggestion teach?**
**Answer: Which chart suits which data**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where else do recommended charts appear besides Quick Analysis?**
**Answer: On the Insert ribbon**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must a suggested chart show the data as?**
**Answer: Honestly — the type must fit the data**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is Quick Analysis a gallery of?**
**Answer: Instant help for exactly the selected data**
