# Video script — Inserting and Deleting Cells, Rows and Columns

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A table is finished. Now it must change.

Need room in the middle? Insert cells.

Excel asks: move the old cells right, or down? Pick one. Room appears. Nothing is lost.

Need a cell gone? Delete cells. The rest shift left, or up. No hole is left.

A whole row? Insert puts it above the row you chose.

A whole column? It comes in on the left.

Select three rows first, and three new rows come.

Remember: clearing empties a cell. Deleting removes it.
