# Inserting and Deleting Cells, Rows and Columns — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) When cells are deleted, the remaining cells can shift ____ or up to close the gap.**
**Answer: left**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A new row appears ____ the row you selected.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) To select a whole column before deleting it, click its ____ heading.**
