# Inserting and Deleting Cells, Rows and Columns — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Inserting cells overwrites and loses the cells that were in the way.**
**Answer: False** — The existing cells shift aside to make room; nothing is overwritten.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The Delete Cells dialog can shift the remaining cells up.**
**Answer: True** — Shift left and shift up are its two closing choices.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Right-clicking a row or column heading offers Insert and Delete.**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Deleting a row leaves an empty row behind inside the table.**
**Answer: False** — The rows below move up to close the space; no empty line is left.
