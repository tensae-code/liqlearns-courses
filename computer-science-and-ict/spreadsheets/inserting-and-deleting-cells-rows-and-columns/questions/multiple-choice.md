# Inserting and Deleting Cells, Rows and Columns — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) When you insert cells, the dialog asks —**
a) for a file name  b) which way the existing cells should shift — right or down  c) for the cell's colour
**Answer: b) which way the existing cells should shift — right or down**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) After cells are deleted, the worksheet —**
a) keeps an empty hole  b) closes the gap as the remaining cells shift left or up  c) deletes the whole file
**Answer: b) closes the gap as the remaining cells shift left or up**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Pressing the Delete key on the keyboard —**
a) removes the cell itself  b) deletes the whole row  c) only clears the cell's contents, leaving the empty cell in place
**Answer: c) only clears the cell's contents**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A new row is inserted —**
a) above the row you selected  b) below the row you selected  c) at the very bottom of the sheet only
**Answer: a) above the row you selected**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) A new column is inserted —**
a) to the right of the chosen column  b) to the left of the chosen column  c) in a new workbook
**Answer: b) to the left of the chosen column**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) You select three rows and then choose Insert. Excel inserts —**
a) one row  b) three rows  c) three columns
**Answer: b) three rows** — It inserts as many rows as were selected.
