# Inserting and Deleting Cells, Rows and Columns — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the difference between clearing a cell and deleting it?**
**Answer:** Clearing empties the cell's contents and the empty cell stays in place; deleting removes the cell itself, and its neighbours shift in to close the gap.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) You need a new column between the name column and the marks column. Where do you click first, and why?**
**Answer:** Select the marks column (or a cell in it), because a new column is inserted to the left of the column chosen — so it lands between names and marks.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Name the two routes to inserting a row.**
**Answer:** Home tab → Insert → Insert Sheet Rows, and right-clicking the row heading and choosing Insert.
