Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Eritrea, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked first: Spreadsheets holds lessons on rows, columns and cells themselves and on formatting, sorting and formulas, and Word Processing holds Adding and Removing Rows and Columns — but that lesson is about Word tables, not Excel cells, rows and columns; none teaches inserting and deleting cells, rows and columns in a worksheet. The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — spreadsheets/inserting-and-deleting-cells-rows-and-columns). It sits in the Spreadsheets title (spreadsheets/).

Eritrea's own way: this is the country's first middle-school ICT course, split explicitly into theory taught in class and hands-on lab work, and anchored to the software of its era — Windows 7, Microsoft Office Word 2007 and Excel 2007. This lesson teaches the skills as the book teaches them, in that era's software; the era anchoring is itself part of what the book records.

Laos's own way (Grade 9 ICT student book): the first fully practical year — a screenshot-led Windows XP, Word and Excel course where every numbered topic is a click-by-click procedure (the Excel half even teaches the Chart Wizard and worksheet Page Setup with its Orientation/Paper Size/Page Numbering table in English). Windows XP-era office teaching runs through the middle years.
