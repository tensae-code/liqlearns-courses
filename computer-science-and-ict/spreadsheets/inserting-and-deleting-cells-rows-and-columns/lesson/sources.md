The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — spreadsheets/inserting-and-deleting-cells-rows-and-columns). It sits in the Spreadsheets title (spreadsheets/).

Eritrea's own way: this is the country's first middle-school ICT course, split explicitly into theory taught in class and hands-on lab work, and anchored to the software of its era — Windows 7, Microsoft Office Word 2007 and Excel 2007. This lesson teaches the skills as the book teaches them, in that era's software; the era anchoring is itself part of what the book records.
