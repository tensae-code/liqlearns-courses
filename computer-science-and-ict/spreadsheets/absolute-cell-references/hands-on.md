# Hands-on — Absolute cell references — $B$9

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Where there is no computer laboratory, do the screen work on a teacher's or your own phone, as the course's teaching pattern provides.

## Pin and copy
Put a bonus of 5 in B9. Build =B2+C2+D2+E2+$B$9 in F2, copy down, then click F4 and read its formula aloud — point at the part that moved and the part that stayed.

## F4 drill
Type a reference in a formula, select it, and press F4. Watch the $ signs arrive. Press F4 again and watch them change. Say which shape pins both column and row.
