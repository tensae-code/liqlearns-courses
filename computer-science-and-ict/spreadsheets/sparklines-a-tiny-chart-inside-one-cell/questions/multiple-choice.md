# Sparklines — a Tiny Chart Inside One Cell — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a sparkline?**
a) A tiny chart that lives inside one cell  b) The shape of every row at a glance — rising, falling, steady  c) With its own colours and styles  d) So the eye lands on the extremes at once
**Answer: a) A tiny chart that lives inside one cell**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How much data does one sparkline draw?**
a) Select the data, insert the sparkline, name the cell that holds it  b) One row or one column  c) Its data points — highest, lowest, first, last, negative  d) Inside one cell of the worksheet
**Answer: b) One row or one column**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a sparkline not have?**
a) With its own colours and styles  b) So the eye lands on the extremes at once  c) Axes and labels  d) No — it shows the shape beside the numbers
**Answer: c) Axes and labels**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a column of sparklines let you read?**
a) Its data points — highest, lowest, first, last, negative  b) Inside one cell of the worksheet  c) A tiny chart that lives inside one cell  d) The shape of every row at a glance — rising, falling, steady
**Answer: d) The shape of every row at a glance — rising, falling, steady**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are the steps to create one?**
a) Select the data, insert the sparkline, name the cell that holds it  b) So the eye lands on the extremes at once  c) No — it shows the shape beside the numbers  d) One row or one column
**Answer: a) Select the data, insert the sparkline, name the cell that holds it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a sparkline formatted?**
a) Inside one cell of the worksheet  b) With its own colours and styles  c) A tiny chart that lives inside one cell  d) Axes and labels
**Answer: b) With its own colours and styles**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What can be marked on a sparkline?**
a) No — it shows the shape beside the numbers  b) One row or one column  c) Its data points — highest, lowest, first, last, negative  d) The shape of every row at a glance — rising, falling, steady
**Answer: c) Its data points — highest, lowest, first, last, negative**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why mark the highest and lowest points?**
a) A tiny chart that lives inside one cell  b) Axes and labels  c) Select the data, insert the sparkline, name the cell that holds it  d) So the eye lands on the extremes at once
**Answer: d) So the eye lands on the extremes at once**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the sparkline live?**
a) Inside one cell of the worksheet  b) One row or one column  c) The shape of every row at a glance — rising, falling, steady  d) With its own colours and styles
