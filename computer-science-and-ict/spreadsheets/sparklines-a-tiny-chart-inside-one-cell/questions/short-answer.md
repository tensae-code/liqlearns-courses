# Sparklines — a Tiny Chart Inside One Cell — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a sparkline?**
**Answer: A tiny chart that lives inside one cell**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How much data does one sparkline draw?**
**Answer: One row or one column**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a sparkline not have?**
**Answer: Axes and labels**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a column of sparklines let you read?**
**Answer: The shape of every row at a glance — rising, falling, steady**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are the steps to create one?**
**Answer: Select the data, insert the sparkline, name the cell that holds it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a sparkline formatted?**
**Answer: With its own colours and styles**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What can be marked on a sparkline?**
**Answer: Its data points — highest, lowest, first, last, negative**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why mark the highest and lowest points?**
**Answer: So the eye lands on the extremes at once**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the sparkline live?**
**Answer: Inside one cell of the worksheet**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Does a sparkline replace reading the numbers?**
**Answer: No — it shows the shape beside the numbers**
