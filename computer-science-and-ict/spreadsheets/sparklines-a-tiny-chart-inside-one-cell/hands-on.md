# Hands-on — Sparklines — a Tiny Chart Inside One Cell

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A column of shapes
Make a small table of five rows of numbers and put a sparkline beside each row. Read the five shapes aloud: rising, falling or steady.

## Mark the extremes
Format your sparklines in a new colour and mark the highest and lowest points. Check each mark against the real biggest and smallest numbers.
