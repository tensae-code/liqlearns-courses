# Video script — Worksheet Tabs — Adding, Renaming and Moving

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at the bottom of the Excel window.

Small labels: Sheet1, Sheet2, Sheet3. One label, one sheet.

Click a label. That sheet comes to the front.

Need a new sheet? Click the small sheet button. Or press Shift and F11.

Sheet1 says nothing. Double-click it. Type a real name: Marks. Press Enter.

Wrong order? Drag the tab to its new place.

Right-click a tab for the careful way: Move or Copy.
