# Worksheet Tabs — Adding, Renaming and Moving — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name two of the three ways to add a worksheet, and say where the new sheet appears for one of them.**
**Answer:** Any two of: the Insert Worksheet button beside the last tab (the new sheet appears after the others); Shift+F11 (before the current sheet); right-click a tab → Insert (before the tab right-clicked).

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why rename Sheet1 to a name like Marks?**
**Answer:** The tab's name should say what the sheet holds, so the right sheet can be found at a glance without opening each one.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) When is Move or Copy better than dragging a tab?**
**Answer:** For a long jump along the row, for making a copy, and for moving a sheet into another open workbook — the dialog places the sheet exactly, before the sheet you choose.
