# Worksheet Tabs — Adding, Renaming and Moving — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where are the worksheet tabs?**
a) Along the top of the window  b) Along the bottom of the window  c) Inside each cell
**Answer: b) Along the bottom of the window**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many worksheets does a new workbook start with in the book's Excel?**
a) One  b) Two  c) Three
**Answer: c) Three** — Sheet1, Sheet2 and Sheet3.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these adds a new worksheet?**
a) Shift+F11  b) Ctrl+S  c) Double-clicking a cell
**Answer: a) Shift+F11** — The Insert Worksheet button and right-click → Insert also work.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) To rename a tab, you —**
a) type over cell A1  b) double-click the tab, type the new name, press Enter  c) rename the file
**Answer: b) double-click the tab, type the new name, press Enter**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) When you drag a tab along the tab row, the small arrow shows —**
a) the sheet to delete  b) where the dragged sheet will land  c) the active cell
**Answer: b) where the dragged sheet will land**

**6. (Sub lesson 3) In Move or Copy, ticking Create a copy means —**
a) the sheet is deleted from its old place  b) a copy is made and the original stays  c) the workbook is saved twice
**Answer: b) a copy is made and the original stays** — Without the tick, the sheet moves.
