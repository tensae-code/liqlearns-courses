# Worksheet Tabs — Adding, Renaming and Moving — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Press ____ + F11 to insert a new worksheet before the current sheet.**
**Answer: Shift**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ____-click a tab to make its name editable, then type and press Enter.**
**Answer: Double**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Right-click a tab and choose Move or ____ to place a sheet exactly, or to copy it.**
**Answer: Copy**
