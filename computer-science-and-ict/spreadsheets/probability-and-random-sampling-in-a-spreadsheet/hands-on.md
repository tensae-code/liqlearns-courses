# Hands-on — Probability and Random Sampling in a Spreadsheet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Chances from counts** — Tally 30 throws of a die (real or simulated). Turn the frequencies into probabilities in the sheet and check they sum to 1.

## Task two
**Draw a fair sample** — Number a class list of 30. Use RANDBETWEEN to draw a sample of 6, re-drawing duplicates. Say why this sample is fair and a 'first six' sample is not.
