# Video script — Probability and Random Sampling in a Spreadsheet

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The specialist statistics strand opens where statistics opens: **probability** — the chance of an event, computed as **favourable outcomes ÷ all outcomes**, from data counted in the sheet. Frequencies gathered from a real set (how often each value occurs) turn into probabilities by division, and the sheet's sums check the law: all the probabilities of a complete set of outcomes add to **1**. Vietnam teaches it inside Excel because the counting is the spreadsheet's home ground.
A **random sample** is chosen so every member of the population has an **equal chance** — no favourites, no pattern. The sheet's tools: **RAND()** gives a random fraction between 0 and 1 (new every recalculation), **RANDBETWEEN(a, b)** a random whole number in a range. The sampling method: number the population, generate random numbers, take the members they point to. The book's warning rides along: a sample chosen by convenience — the first ten, the nearest ten — is not random, and conclusions from it lean.
