# Preparing a Worksheet for Printing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where is Page Setup found?**
**Answer: On the Page Layout tab**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Portrait or landscape for a wide worksheet?**
**Answer: Landscape**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are margins?**
**Answer: The white frame around the printed page**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does Fit to Page do?**
**Answer: Scales the sheet so all its columns print one page wide**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What can repeat on every printed page?**
**Answer: Chosen rows or columns — the headings of a long list**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the print preview show?**
**Answer: The worksheet exactly as paper will receive it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two checks in the preview.**
**Answer: The page count, and that no column is cut off at an edge**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can over-scaling do to the text?**
**Answer: Shrink it past reading size**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do you print only part of a sheet?**
**Answer: Set that part as the print area**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When do you finally click Print?**
**Answer: Only when the preview is right**
