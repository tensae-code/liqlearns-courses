# Getting Started with Excel Workbooks — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an Excel file called?**
**Answer: A workbook**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two parts of the Excel window explored first.**
**Answer: The ribbon of commands and the grid of rows and columns (also the sheet's name below)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why explore the window before using it?**
**Answer: Every later instruction names its parts — the pupil must be able to find them**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does saving do?**
**Answer: Writes the workbook to a chosen place and name — screen work becomes kept work**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does closing ask, if changes are unsaved?**
**Answer: Whether to keep them**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does opening do?**
**Answer: Brings a saved workbook back, exactly as it was left**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the journey this lesson opens called?**
**Answer: 'My Special Table' — Kuwait Grade 5's Excel journey**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) In what order are the file acts taught?**
**Answer: Saving, opening and closing — in the first lesson**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why must keeping come first?**
**Answer: The journey's later tables exist only if they can be kept and found again**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 5 Digital Explorer Term 2, Unit 2 Lesson 1**
