# Hands-on — Getting Started with Excel Workbooks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Open, look, keep', 'Launch Excel, name the window's three parts aloud, type one word in a cell, save the workbook with a name that says what it holds, and close it.')

## Task two
('Find it again', 'Open the workbook you saved and prove it is the same one: the word, the cell, the name. Then close it without changes.')
