# Resizing a Chart and Adding Another Data Series — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What appears on a chart's edges when you click it?**
a) Handles  b) Too small hides labels; too big crowds out the data  c) Select the chart and add the new data to its range  d) The chart updates automatically
**Answer: a) Handles**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a corner handle change?**
a) A new row or column of data added to the chart's picture  b) Height and width together  c) Its own colour and a legend entry  d) No — it redraws itself
**Answer: b) Height and width together**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does an edge handle change?**
a) Select the chart and add the new data to its range  b) The chart updates automatically  c) One direction only  d) A living object on the sheet
**Answer: c) One direction only**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does a chart's size matter?**
a) Its own colour and a legend entry  b) No — it redraws itself  c) Handles  d) Too small hides labels; too big crowds out the data
**Answer: d) Too small hides labels; too big crowds out the data**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is an extra data series?**
a) A new row or column of data added to the chart's picture  b) The chart updates automatically  c) A living object on the sheet  d) Height and width together
**Answer: a) A new row or column of data added to the chart's picture**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a series added?**
a) No — it redraws itself  b) Select the chart and add the new data to its range  c) Handles  d) One direction only
**Answer: b) Select the chart and add the new data to its range**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What comes with a new series in the plot?**
a) A living object on the sheet  b) Height and width together  c) Its own colour and a legend entry  d) Too small hides labels; too big crowds out the data
**Answer: c) Its own colour and a legend entry**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens after adding the series?**
a) Handles  b) One direction only  c) A new row or column of data added to the chart's picture  d) The chart updates automatically
**Answer: d) The chart updates automatically**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Must the chart be rebuilt by hand?**
a) No — it redraws itself  b) Height and width together  c) Too small hides labels; too big crowds out the data  d) Select the chart and add the new data to its range
**Answer: a) No — it redraws itself**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is a chart, in this lesson's words?**
a) One direction only  b) A living object on the sheet  c) A new row or column of data added to the chart's picture  d) Its own colour and a legend entry
**Answer: b) A living object on the sheet**
