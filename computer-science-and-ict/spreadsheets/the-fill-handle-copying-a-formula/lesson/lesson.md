# The fill handle — copying a formula

## Do it first

Your total formula stands in F2, and four empty total cells wait below it. Do not rebuild the formula four times. Find the tiny square at F2's corner, take hold of it, and pull. Watch what lands in every row.

## The fill handle

The **fill handle** is the **small square at the bottom-right corner** of the selected cell. It exists to repeat a calculation down a column.

## Copying with the fill handle — in order

1. **Select** the cell holding the formula.
2. **Drag the fill handle** over the cells to fill.
3. **Release** — the formula is copied into each cell.

Double-clicking the fill handle also fills. To edit afterwards, **double-click a cell** and change its formula there.

## What the copies say

Because new formulas use **relative references** by default, the copies adjust to their own rows: the copied formula in **F3 reads =B3+C3+D3+E3**, in **F4 =B4+…**, and so on down. Save the finished workbook as **"G9 IT Students' Mark Sheet"**.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: dragging the small square at a cell's bottom-right corner copies its formula down a column — and each copy adjusts its relative references to its own row. Use the lesson's own words for the key names.
