# The fill handle — copying a formula — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The fill handle is —
a) a button on the Home tab  b) the small square at the selected cell's bottom-right corner  c) the formula bar's handle  d) a kind of chart
**Answer: b) the small square at the selected cell's bottom-right corner**

**2.** To copy a formula down, you —
a) retype it in every row  b) drag the fill handle over the cells to fill, then release  c) press Ctrl+M  d) use Show Formulas
**Answer: b) drag the fill handle over the cells to fill, then release**

**3.** The copied formula in F3 reads —
a) =B2+C2+D2+E2  b) =B3+C3+D3+E3  c) =B9+C9+D9+E9  d) =F3+F3
**Answer: b) =B3+C3+D3+E3** — Relative references adjust to their own row.

**4.** Double-clicking the fill handle —
a) deletes the formula  b) also fills the cells below  c) opens the gallery  d) prints the sheet
**Answer: b) also fills the cells below**

**5.** To edit a cell's formula, you —
a) single-click it  b) double-click the cell  c) delete the sheet  d) press F4
**Answer: b) double-click the cell**
