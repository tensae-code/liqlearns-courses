# Lesson plan — The fill handle — copying a formula

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Spreadsheets · **Lesson:** The fill handle — copying a formula
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Spreadsheets in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 9 book, Unit 3 — Application Software. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** dragging the small square at a cell's bottom-right corner copies its formula down a column — and each copy adjusts its relative references to its own row.
**This lesson's own level:** it is a doing-lesson. It stands on Relative cell references.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Point to the fill handle: the small square at the bottom-right corner of the selected cell.
2. Copy a formula down by dragging the fill handle; fill by double-clicking it, too.
3. Read a copied formula and show how its references moved row by row (F3 reads =B3+C3+D3+E3).
4. Edit any cell's formula by double-clicking the cell.
## What you need
A computer with a spreadsheet program; the mark sheet from Relative cell references.
