# Hands-on — The fill handle — copying a formula

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Where there is no computer laboratory, do the screen work on a teacher's or your own phone, as the course's teaching pattern provides.

## Drag and read
Copy the F2 total down to F6 with the fill handle. Then click F3, F4, F5, F6 one by one and read each formula aloud — hear the row number climb.

## Double-click fill
Clear the totals and fill again by double-clicking the fill handle. Compare the two routes: which suited this column, and why?
