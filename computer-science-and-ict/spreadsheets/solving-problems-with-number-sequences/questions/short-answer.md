# Solving Problems with Number Sequences — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is solving by tabulating?**
**Answer: Turning a word problem into a table — quantities as columns, steps as rows**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the method's power?**
**Answer: Inspection — every intermediate value stands visible**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where is a wrong turn found?**
**Answer: At its row, not at the answer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a number sequence in the sheet?**
**Answer: A column where each term is built by a rule from the ones before**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What rule builds Fibonacci?**
**Answer: Each term is the sum of the two before it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Write Fibonacci's first six terms.**
**Answer: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the sequence built in the sheet?**
**Answer: By one formula copied down**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the fiftieth term cost the sheet?**
**Answer: Nothing — the rule is written once and repetition is free**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which maths does the book tabulate?**
**Answer: Word problems — growth, exchange, journeys**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the spreadsheet in this method?**
**Answer: The rough paper that checks itself**
