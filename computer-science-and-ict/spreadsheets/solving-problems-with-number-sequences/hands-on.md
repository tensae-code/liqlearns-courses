# Hands-on — Solving Problems with Number Sequences

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Tabulate a journey', 'Take a word problem about a journey with stops. Tabulate it: columns for the quantities, rows for the steps, formulas carrying it forward. Point at the row where the answer stands.')

## Task two
('Grow Fibonacci', 'Build Fibonacci down fifty rows with one copied formula. Then change the two starting values and describe the new family that grows.')
