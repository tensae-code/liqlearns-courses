# Formatting cells — alignment, borders, fill and number formats

## Do it first

Your title sits in the bottom-left corner of a tall cell, borderless, on white — and your 0.5 could be a half, a date or money. Position it, frame it, and tell Excel what kind of number it is.

## Sub lesson 1 — Alignment, borders and fill

**Alignment** repositions content inside the cell: **three horizontal alignments and three vertical alignments** on the Home tab. By nature, **text normally sits bottom-left, numbers bottom-right**.

- **Borders** come from the **Borders dropdown**, with **Draw Borders tools** for hand-drawn lines and colours.
- **Fill colour** from the **Fill Color dropdown** paints the cell's **background**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cell Styles and number formats

Rather than hand-formatting, ready-made **Cell Styles** apply professional **title and header formatting in one selection**.

**Number formatting tells Excel what kind of data it holds** — **dates, times, decimals, percentages, currency** — via the **Number Format dropdown**, so values display in their proper form: the same value can show as **0.5, 50%** or as **currency**.

The closing practical restyles a given range: **Tahoma, size 32, a named blue, bold/italic/underline, vertical alignment, and borders**.
