# Hands-on — Formatting cells — alignment, borders, fill and number formats

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Header row, two ways

Make a header row by hand — alignment, borders, fill — then make the same header with a Cell Style. Time both; say which you would teach first.

## One value, three faces

Put 0.5 in three cells and display them as a decimal, a percentage and currency. Label each with its format name.
