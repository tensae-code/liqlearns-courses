# Video script — Formatting cells — alignment, borders, fill and number formats

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Where does the word sit in its cell? Alignment decides.
Three across. Three up-and-down. Text sits bottom-left; numbers bottom-right — by nature.
Frame it: Borders. Or draw the lines yourself.
Paint behind it: Fill Color.
In a hurry? Cell Styles dress a title in one pick.
And tell Excel what your number IS.
0.5 — or 50% — or money. Number Format decides how it shows.
