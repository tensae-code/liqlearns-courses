# Entering and Editing Data in Cells — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the steps of entering data?**
**Answer: Select the cell, type, and confirm the entry**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Enter do after typing?**
**Answer: Confirms the entry and moves the selection down**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does an entry show while you type?**
**Answer: In the formula bar**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Can an unconfirmed entry be cancelled?**
**Answer: Yes — while typing, the entry is not yet in the cell**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when you type into an occupied cell and confirm?**
**Answer: The new entry replaces the old content completely**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is editing?**
**Answer: Changing an existing entry without retyping all of it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How do you open a cell for editing?**
**Answer: Double-click the cell, or work in the formula bar**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is editing the right tool?**
**Answer: For a long entry with one small fault, like one wrong reference or letter**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is finished work kept?**
**Answer: By saving the workbook**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to unsaved work when the program closes?**
**Answer: It is lost — it exists only while the program stays open**
