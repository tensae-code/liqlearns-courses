# Entering and Editing Data in Cells — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are the steps of entering data?" is: Select the cell, type, and confirm the entry.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does Enter do after typing?" is: Changing an existing entry without retyping all of it.**
**Answer: False** — Confirms the entry and moves the selection down.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does an entry show while you type?" is: In the formula bar.**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Can an unconfirmed entry be cancelled?" is: For a long entry with one small fault, like one wrong reference or letter.**
**Answer: False** — Yes — while typing, the entry is not yet in the cell.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What happens when you type into an occupied cell and confirm?" is: The new entry replaces the old content completely.**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is editing?" is: It is lost — it exists only while the program stays open.**
**Answer: False** — Changing an existing entry without retyping all of it.

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How do you open a cell for editing?" is: Double-click the cell, or work in the formula bar.**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is editing the right tool?" is: Confirms the entry and moves the selection down.**
**Answer: False** — For a long entry with one small fault, like one wrong reference or letter.

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is finished work kept?" is: By saving the workbook.**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens to unsaved work when the program closes?" is: Yes — while typing, the entry is not yet in the cell.**
**Answer: False** — It is lost — it exists only while the program stays open.
