# Entering and Editing Data in Cells — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the steps of entering data?**
a) Select the cell, type, and confirm the entry  b) Yes — while typing, the entry is not yet in the cell  c) Changing an existing entry without retyping all of it  d) For a long entry with one small fault, like one wrong reference or letter
**Answer: a) Select the cell, type, and confirm the entry**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Enter do after typing?**
a) The new entry replaces the old content completely  b) Confirms the entry and moves the selection down  c) Double-click the cell, or work in the formula bar  d) By saving the workbook
**Answer: b) Confirms the entry and moves the selection down**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does an entry show while you type?**
a) Changing an existing entry without retyping all of it  b) For a long entry with one small fault, like one wrong reference or letter  c) In the formula bar  d) It is lost — it exists only while the program stays open
**Answer: c) In the formula bar**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Can an unconfirmed entry be cancelled?**
a) Double-click the cell, or work in the formula bar  b) By saving the workbook  c) Select the cell, type, and confirm the entry  d) Yes — while typing, the entry is not yet in the cell
**Answer: d) Yes — while typing, the entry is not yet in the cell**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when you type into an occupied cell and confirm?**
a) The new entry replaces the old content completely  b) For a long entry with one small fault, like one wrong reference or letter  c) It is lost — it exists only while the program stays open  d) Confirms the entry and moves the selection down
**Answer: a) The new entry replaces the old content completely**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is editing?**
a) By saving the workbook  b) Changing an existing entry without retyping all of it  c) Select the cell, type, and confirm the entry  d) In the formula bar
**Answer: b) Changing an existing entry without retyping all of it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How do you open a cell for editing?**
a) It is lost — it exists only while the program stays open  b) Confirms the entry and moves the selection down  c) Double-click the cell, or work in the formula bar  d) Yes — while typing, the entry is not yet in the cell
**Answer: c) Double-click the cell, or work in the formula bar**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is editing the right tool?**
a) Select the cell, type, and confirm the entry  b) In the formula bar  c) The new entry replaces the old content completely  d) For a long entry with one small fault, like one wrong reference or letter
**Answer: d) For a long entry with one small fault, like one wrong reference or letter**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is finished work kept?**
a) By saving the workbook  b) Confirms the entry and moves the selection down  c) Yes — while typing, the entry is not yet in the cell  d) Changing an existing entry without retyping all of it
**Answer: a) By saving the workbook**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to unsaved work when the program closes?**
a) In the formula bar  b) It is lost — it exists only while the program stays open  c) The new entry replaces the old content completely  d) Double-click the cell, or work in the formula bar
**Answer: b) It is lost — it exists only while the program stays open**
