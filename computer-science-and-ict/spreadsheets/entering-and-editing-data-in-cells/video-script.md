# Video script — Entering and Editing Data in Cells

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

To **enter** data: select the cell, type the text, number or formula, and confirm — with Enter, which moves the selection down, or with another movement key, which confirms and moves that way.
While you type, the entry is not yet in the cell: it can be cancelled, and it shows in the **formula bar** as you work.
**Editing** changes an existing entry without retyping all of it: double-click the cell (or use the formula bar) and the entry opens for correction — move the cursor inside it, delete or insert characters, and confirm.
Editing is the right tool for a long formula with one wrong reference, or a label with one wrong letter.
