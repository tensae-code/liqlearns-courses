# Hands-on — Entering and Editing Data in Cells

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Enter and confirm
Enter a column of five numbers, confirming each with Enter, then a column of five labels. Cancel one entry halfway through typing and say at which moment an entry truly enters a cell.

## Edit, don't retype
Type a long label with a deliberate mistake, then edit it in place in the formula bar to fix one letter. Then save the workbook, close it, reopen it, and check the edited entry is there.
