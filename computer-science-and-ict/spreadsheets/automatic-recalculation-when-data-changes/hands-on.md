# Hands-on — Automatic Recalculation — When Data Changes, Results Change

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Watch it move', 'In a spreadsheet, build a three-cell chain (price, tax on it, total with tax), change the price, and write down everything that moved.')

## Task two
('Break the rule, see the cost', 'Type one derived value by hand in a copy of the sheet, change its source, and write what went stale and why.')
