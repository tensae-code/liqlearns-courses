# Automatic Recalculation — When Data Changes, Results Change — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is automatic recalculation?**
**Answer: When a value changes, every formula depending on it works itself out again at once**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What was the total, really?**
**Answer: A standing instruction, not a typed number**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Directly or how else may a formula depend?**
**Answer: Through other formulas, in a chain**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A sheet of typed totals is what?**
**Answer: A photograph**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) A sheet of formulas is what?**
**Answer: A living calculator that cannot go stale**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How far do recalculation chains run?**
**Answer: As far as the formulas reach**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must never be typed by hand?**
**Answer: A derived value — build it with a formula**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must the builder check?**
**Answer: The chain's sources — one wrong source poisons every result downstream**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Instantly and where?**
**Answer: Instantly and everywhere downstream**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which unit teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 8 Pliroforiki B, Unit 3**
