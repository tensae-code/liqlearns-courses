# Hands-on — Date and Time Functions in a Spreadsheet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Days remaining** — In a sheet, put today's date with TODAY(), a deadline a month ahead with DATE(), and a formula for the days remaining. Format the same date three ways.

## Task two
**Pull it apart** — Enter your birth date as a real date. Use YEAR, MONTH and DAY to display its parts, and compute your age in days by subtraction.
