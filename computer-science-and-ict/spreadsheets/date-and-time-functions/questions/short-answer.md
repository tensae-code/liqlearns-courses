# Date and Time Functions in a Spreadsheet — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does a spreadsheet store a date?**
**Answer: As a number — the count of days since its day zero**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is a time stored?**
**Answer: As a fraction of the day**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why can dates be subtracted?**
**Answer: Because they are numbers — deadline minus today gives days remaining**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a date's display format?**
**Answer: A costume — the number underneath does the arithmetic**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does sorting dates sort?**
**Answer: Their numbers**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do TODAY() and NOW() give?**
**Answer: The current date — and, for NOW(), the current date with the time**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which functions pull a date apart?**
**Answer: YEAR, MONTH and DAY**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which function builds a date from parts?**
**Answer: DATE(year, month, day)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is the span between two dates counted?**
**Answer: By subtraction, or with DATEDIF**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a date typed as text a dead value?**
**Answer: No date function can spend it — it is letters, not a number**
