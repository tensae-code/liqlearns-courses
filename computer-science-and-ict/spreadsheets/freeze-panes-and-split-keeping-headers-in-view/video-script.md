# Video script — Freeze panes and Split — keeping headers in view

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two hundred rows. Scroll down — where did the headings go?
Freeze them. Click the row BELOW the headings.
View. Freeze Panes. Freeze Panes.
See the grey line? Scroll now. The headings stand still. The rows move.
Unfreeze Panes brings it back.
Need two far parts at once? Select a cell. View. Split.
Two panes, each scrolling on its own. Split again removes it.
