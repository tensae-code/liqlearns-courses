# Attributes or elements? Carrying the same information two ways — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or code.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An attribute is a ___-value pair.
**Answer: key** — key-value pair is the term.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the paired example, the fact carried two ways is ___.
**Answer: gender** — gender is stored as attribute and as element.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** XML has ___ rule for when to use attributes versus elements.
**Answer: no** — no rule — the choice is the author's.
