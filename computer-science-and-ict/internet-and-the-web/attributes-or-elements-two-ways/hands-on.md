# Hands-on — Attributes or elements? Carrying the same information two ways

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Convert the record
Take a paper record of yours with two attributes. Rewrite it with those facts as child elements instead. Then convert a partner's elements back into attributes.

## Quote rescue
Write three attribute values that contain double quotes inside them. Rescue each one with single quotes, and read them aloud to check they still say the same thing.
