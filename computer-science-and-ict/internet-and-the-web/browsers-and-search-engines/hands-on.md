# Hands-on — Browsers and search engines

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Vehicle and guide
Act it out in pairs: one is the browser — they can walk the room (travel) but know nothing; the other is the search engine — they cannot move but can answer 'where is…?' questions from a list. Find three classroom objects by asking, then being carried by the answers.

## Ask and travel
On a real computer if you can: use a search engine to ask one question, then use the browser to travel to a result. Write down which tool did which part.
