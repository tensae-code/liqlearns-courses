# Video script — Browsers and search engines

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two tools help you on the Web.
The browser is your vehicle.
It locates pages. It shows pages. It moves you page to page.

The search engine is your guide.
It speeds up the search.
Google. Yahoo. Those are guides.

Ask the guide. Travel in the vehicle.
