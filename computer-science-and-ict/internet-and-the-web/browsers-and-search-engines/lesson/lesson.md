# Browsers and search engines

## Do it first

You need one fact: the height of a famous mountain. Describe, step by step, how you would find it on the internet. Somewhere in your steps there are two different tools doing two different jobs. This lesson names them.

## Two tools, two jobs

A **browser** is the software application for **exploring the internet** — locating and viewing web pages, and moving from page to page. It is the vehicle. A **search engine** is the internet tool that **speeds up searching** for information and resources. It is the guide who knows where things are. The book's examples of search engines are **Google** and **Yahoo**. You travel in the browser; you ask the search engine where to go.
