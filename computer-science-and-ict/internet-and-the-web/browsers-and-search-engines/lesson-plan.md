# Lesson plan — Browsers and search engines

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Internet and the Web · **Lesson:** Browsers and search engines
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 8 — Internet and the Web in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 7 book, Unit 4 — Internet. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a browser is the software for exploring the internet — locating and viewing pages and moving between them — while a search engine is the tool that speeds up finding information; Google and Yahoo are the book's examples.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Web lesson — pages must exist before the tools that find and show them make sense.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Internet, Web, website and web page.  Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define a browser and its three actions: locating, viewing, moving between pages.
2. Define a search engine: the tool that speeds searching.
3. Name Google and Yahoo as search engines.
4. Tell the two tools apart on any example.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil; a computer with a browser if you can practise.
