# Hands-on — Attaching Files to an Email

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Send something real
Compose an email to a partner or teacher with a real file attached — a document or picture you made. Check it is listed with its size before sending. At the other end, your receiver saves and opens it, and reports back exactly what arrived.

## The three cares check
Collect three attachment situations — a huge video, a file in a rare program, a surprise attachment from a stranger. Judge each against the lesson's three cares (size, form, safety) and write what you would do in each case, and why.
