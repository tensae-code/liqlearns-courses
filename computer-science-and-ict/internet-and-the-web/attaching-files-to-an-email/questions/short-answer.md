# Attaching Files to an Email — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an attachment?**
**Answer: A file sent together with an email**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which command attaches a file?**
**Answer: Attach — the paperclip — while composing the message**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What travels — the original or a copy?**
**Answer: A copy — the original stays on your computer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is shown when a file is attached?**
**Answer: Its name and size, listed with the message before sending**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book teach attachments last in its e-mail chapter?**
**Answer: Because a message carrying a file is the complete act — the one real work most often needs**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does a receiver spot an attachment?**
**Answer: By the message's paperclip**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the receiver do with an attachment?**
**Answer: Downloads (saves) it and opens it with a program that understands it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What are the three cares of attachments?**
**Answer: Size, form and safety**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why can a file in a rare program fail its receiver?**
**Answer: It is a letter in a language they cannot read — they have no program to open it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What should you do with an unexpected attachment?**
**Answer: Not open it — check with the sender first, as it may carry a virus**
