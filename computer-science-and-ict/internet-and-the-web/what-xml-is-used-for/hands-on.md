# Hands-on — What XML is used for

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## A record on paper
Write a vacation-book record on paper in nested tags. Swap with a partner and read each other's records aloud, tag by tag.

## Database or XML?
List five small collections (songs, scores, contacts, recipes, marks). For each, say: table-shaped, or not? Mark which ones suit XML storage and say why.
