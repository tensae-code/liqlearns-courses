# Video script — Designing a website — audience, navigation, consistency, simplicity, accessibility

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A website is a house for information.
Design it for the people who come.
Make the way in easy to find.
Keep every room looking the same family.
Keep it simple. Five colours is enough.
And open the door to everybody.
That is good design.
