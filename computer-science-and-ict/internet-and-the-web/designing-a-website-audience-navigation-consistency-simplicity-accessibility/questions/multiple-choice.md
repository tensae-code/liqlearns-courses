# Designing a website — audience, navigation, consistency, simplicity, accessibility — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Website design covers —**
a) the layout, navigation, appearance and graphics of a site  b) only the colours of a site  c) only the code of a site  d) only the server of a site
**Answer: a) the layout, navigation, appearance and graphics of a site** — design is the whole planned look and working of the site.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The aim of website design is a site that —**
a) uses the most graphics possible  b) appeals to its audience and makes information easy to find  c) hides its pages well  d) needs no navigation
**Answer: b) appeals to its audience and makes information easy to find** — appeal plus easy finding is the book's stated aim.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A children's education site and an adults' business site should —**
a) look exactly the same  b) look different, because their audiences differ  c) both use twenty colours  d) have no menu
**Answer: b) look different, because their audiences differ** — design for the target audience.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Logical navigation means —**
a) visitors find things fast instead of being confused away  b) every page links to every page  c) the menu is hidden  d) links open new windows
**Answer: a) visitors find things fast instead of being confused away** — navigation is the visitor's way through the site.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) A consistent look and feel keeps the same — across pages.**
a) backgrounds, colours and typefaces  b) prices  c) file sizes  d) passwords
**Answer: a) backgrounds, colours and typefaces** — consistency is about the repeated visual choices.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Keeping it simple means about — colours, plus or minus two.**
a) five  b) fifteen  c) fifty  d) two
**Answer: a) five** — about five colours, plus or minus two.
