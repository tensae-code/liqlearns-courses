# Designing a website — audience, navigation, consistency, simplicity, accessibility — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or code.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Website design aims to make information ___ to find.**
**Answer: easy** — appeal without easy finding is only half the aim.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The book's example of good navigation is the ___ site.**
**Answer: WHO** — the World Health Organization site.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Keep the look and ___ consistent across pages.**
**Answer: feel** — look and feel is the book's phrase.
