# Ordered lists in HTML — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or code.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) An ordered list is also called a ___ list.**
**Answer: numbered** — numbered = ordered.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ___ numerals are the default numbering.**
**Answer: Arabic** — 1, 2, 3.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Numbering style is changed by the ___ attribute.**
**Answer: type** — type carries the style.
