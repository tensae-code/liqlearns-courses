# Website Project — Requirements and Production Stages — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are requirements?**
**Answer: The project's contract — what the finished site must do and be, written before building**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are requirements used for after building?**
**Answer: Judging the result — checking the site against them one by one**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the dictionary site's three jobs**
**Answer: Visitors browse and search terms; an authorised user adds a term; every term shows its meaning and illustration**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a requirement real rather than a wish?**
**Answer: It is testable — it can be checked**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Egypt give requirements a whole unit?**
**Answer: Otherwise pupils build first and discover the contract afterwards, from the complaints**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the five production stages in order**
**Answer: Analysis, design, implementation, testing, and publishing and maintenance**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does each stage end in?**
**Answer: Something checkable — a product the next stage can build on**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why must pages wait for the database design?**
**Answer: The pages write into the database — its design must exist first**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do the stages help fault-finding?**
**Answer: A fault is traced to the stage that owns it — a wrong requirement is not fixed by patching code**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is the Grade 11 term itself built in these stages?**
**Answer: Unit 1 plans, Unit 2 specifies, Unit 3 produces**
