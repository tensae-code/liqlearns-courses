# Website Project — Requirements and Production Stages — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are requirements?" is: The project's contract — what the finished site must do and be, written before building**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are requirements used for after building?" is: Visitors browse and search terms; an authorised user adds a term; every term shows its meaning and illustration**
**Answer: False** — Judging the result — checking the site against them one by one

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the dictionary site's three jobs?" is: Visitors browse and search terms; an authorised user adds a term; every term shows its meaning and illustration**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What makes a requirement real rather than a wish?" is: Otherwise pupils build first and discover the contract afterwards, from the complaints**
**Answer: False** — It is testable — it can be checked

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why does Egypt give requirements a whole unit?" is: Otherwise pupils build first and discover the contract afterwards, from the complaints**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name the five production stages in order?" is: Something checkable — a product the next stage can build on**
**Answer: False** — Analysis, design, implementation, testing, and publishing and maintenance

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does each stage end in?" is: Something checkable — a product the next stage can build on**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why must pages wait for the database design?" is: A fault is traced to the stage that owns it — a wrong requirement is not fixed by patching code**
**Answer: False** — The pages write into the database — its design must exist first

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How do the stages help fault-finding?" is: A fault is traced to the stage that owns it — a wrong requirement is not fixed by patching code**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is the Grade 11 term itself built in these stages?" is: The project's contract — what the finished site must do and be, written before building**
**Answer: False** — Unit 1 plans, Unit 2 specifies, Unit 3 produces
