# Downloading Files Safely from the Internet — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) From where should you download?**
**Answer: The maker's own site, an official store, or a site your teacher names**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is wrong with a paid file offered free on a stranger's page?**
**Answer: It takes the owner's rights — and often hides harmful programs**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does downloading mean?**
**Answer: Bringing a file from the internet onto your own computer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does the real download button usually look?**
**Answer: Quiet — while fake buttons shout, flash and open adverts**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Whose rights does legal downloading respect?**
**Answer: The file owner's rights**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What should happen before a downloaded file is opened?**
**Answer: The antivirus scans it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which downloaded files need the most care?**
**Answer: Program files — they can change the computer itself**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where do downloads land, and how should they be kept?**
**Answer: In the Downloads folder, with clear names for the ones kept**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do with downloads you do not need?**
**Answer: Delete them, so the folder stays trustworthy**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What if a file behaves strangely after opening?**
**Answer: Stop, disconnect, and tell a teacher or another adult**
