## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Choosing a safe, legal source** — **Download** only from the source that has the right to give the file: the maker's own site, an official store, or a site your teacher names. A file offered free on a stranger's page when its maker sells it is both a legal wrong — it takes the owner's rights — and a common hiding place for harmful programs. Read the page before clicking: the real download button is usually quiet, while fake buttons shout, flash and open adverts.
2. **After the download — check before opening** — A downloaded file is a stranger at the door until it is checked: let the **antivirus** scan it before opening, and be most careful with program files, which can change the computer itself. Keep downloads in the **Downloads folder**, give kept files clear names, and delete what you do not need so the folder stays a place you can trust. If a file behaves strangely after opening, stop, disconnect and tell a teacher or another adult.
