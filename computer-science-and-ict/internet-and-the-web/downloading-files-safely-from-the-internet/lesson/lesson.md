# Downloading Files Safely from the Internet

## Do it first

Two Mauritanian guides teach downloading — in Grade 7 as its own fiche, and in Grade 8 as responsible and legal downloading. This lesson joins both into one safe routine.

## Sub lesson 1 — Choosing a safe, legal source

**Download** only from the source that has the right to give the file: the maker's own site, an official store, or a site your teacher names. A file offered free on a stranger's page when its maker sells it is both a legal wrong — it takes the owner's rights — and a common hiding place for harmful programs. Read the page before clicking: the real download button is usually quiet, while fake buttons shout, flash and open adverts.

## Sub lesson 2 — After the download — check before opening

A downloaded file is a stranger at the door until it is checked: let the **antivirus** scan it before opening, and be most careful with program files, which can change the computer itself. Keep downloads in the **Downloads folder**, give kept files clear names, and delete what you do not need so the folder stays a place you can trust. If a file behaves strangely after opening, stop, disconnect and tell a teacher or another adult.

[Picture — after the download]

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: Downloading brings a file from the internet onto your own computer, and doing it safely and legally means choosing a trustworthy source, respecting the owner's rights, and checking the file before it is opened. Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.

## Do It Yourself

1. Show it, don't just say it: The maker's own site, an official store, or a site your teacher names. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
2. Show it, don't just say it: Bringing a file from the internet onto your own computer. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
3. Show it, don't just say it: The antivirus scans it. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
4. Show it, don't just say it: In the Downloads folder, with clear names for the ones kept. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
