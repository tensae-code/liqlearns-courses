# Hands-on — Downloading Files Safely from the Internet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Source check
For three files you might download, name the one right source for each and say why the other sources you found should not be used.

## Downloads-folder routine
Write the five-step routine — download, scan, open, rename or delete — and use it on one real download, ticking each step.
