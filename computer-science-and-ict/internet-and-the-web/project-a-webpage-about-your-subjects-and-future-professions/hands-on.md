# Hands-on — Project a Webpage About Your Subjects and Future Professions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 5 Activity 5.7: webpage of your 9th-class subjects with a famous person's image and the professions each subject opens Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach project a webpage about your subjects and future professions to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
