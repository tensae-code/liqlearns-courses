# HTML links — linking to other pages — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or code.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Links are also called ___.**
**Answer: hyperlinks** — hyperlinks = links.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The text between the anchor's tags is the ___ part.**
**Answer: clickable** — the part the visitor clicks.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Same tab or new tab is decided by the ___ attribute.**
**Answer: target** — target aims the opening.
