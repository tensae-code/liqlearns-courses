# Creating Online Content

## Do it first

The web is not only for reading. This lesson learns to make for it — well.

## Sub lesson 1 — Plan before making — audience, purpose, form

**Online content** is anything published on the web: text, pictures, audio, video, pages. Good content starts with three decisions: the **audience** (who it is for), the **purpose** (what it should do — inform, teach, persuade, entertain), and the **form** that fits both (a post, a page, a short video, a gallery). Armenia's Grade 8 book teaches 'Creating online content' (§6) right after creating a blog — first one kind of making, then making in general.

## Sub lesson 2 — Publish responsibly — honesty, credit, safety

Publishing carries duties: **honesty** (publish what you know, mark what you guess), **credit** (name the makers of anything you reuse, and reuse only what you may), **safety** (no private data — yours or others' — no addresses, no school-and-home details of children), and **care after publishing**: read responses, correct mistakes visibly, and take down what proves harmful. The creator's name travels with the content — Armenia's guide treats creating online content as a standard theme of its own (8-5), not a free-time extra.
