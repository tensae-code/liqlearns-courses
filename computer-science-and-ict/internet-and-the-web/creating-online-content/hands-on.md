# Hands-on — Creating Online Content

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Content plan** — Plan one piece of online content for a real audience: audience, purpose, form, and the three duties checked one by one before it could be published.

## Task two
**Credit audit** — Take a page or post you have made (or one from the class blog). List everything in it you did not make, and write the credit line each one needs.
