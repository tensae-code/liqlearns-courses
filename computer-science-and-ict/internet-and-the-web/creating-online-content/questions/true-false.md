# Creating Online Content — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Anything published on the web — text, pictures, audio, video, pages**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Anything published on the web — text, pictures, audio, video, pages** — as the answer to: Name the three decisions good content starts with.
**Answer: False — Audience, purpose and form**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: What the content should do — inform, teach, persuade or entertain**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: What the content should do — inform, teach, persuade or entertain** — as the answer to: Why choose the form after audience and purpose?
**Answer: False — The form must fit both — a how-to for children and a report for officials should not look the same**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Grade 8, §6 — right after creating a blog (§5)**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Grade 8, §6 — right after creating a blog (§5)** — as the answer to: Name the creator's three duties before publishing.
**Answer: False — Honesty, credit and safety**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Name the makers of anything reused — and reuse only what you may**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Name the makers of anything reused — and reuse only what you may** — as the answer to: What must never be published?
**Answer: False — Private data — yours or others': addresses, and school-and-home details of children**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Read responses, correct mistakes visibly, and take down what proves harmful**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Read responses, correct mistakes visibly, and take down what proves harmful** — as the answer to: Why does the creator's name matter?
**Answer: False — It travels with the content — the creator stays responsible for it**
