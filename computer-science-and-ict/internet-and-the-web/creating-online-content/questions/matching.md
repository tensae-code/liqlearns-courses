# Creating Online Content — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is online content?** → **Anything published on the web — text, pictures, audio, video, pages**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three decisions good content starts with.** → **Audience, purpose and form**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does purpose decide?** → **What the content should do — inform, teach, persuade or entertain**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why choose the form after audience and purpose?** → **The form must fit both — a how-to for children and a report for officials should not look the same**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Armenia teach creating online content?** → **Grade 8, §6 — right after creating a blog (§5)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the creator's three duties before publishing.** → **Honesty, credit and safety**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the credit duty require?** → **Name the makers of anything reused — and reuse only what you may**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must never be published?** → **Private data — yours or others': addresses, and school-and-home details of children**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What care is owed after publishing?** → **Read responses, correct mistakes visibly, and take down what proves harmful**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the creator's name matter?** → **It travels with the content — the creator stays responsible for it**
