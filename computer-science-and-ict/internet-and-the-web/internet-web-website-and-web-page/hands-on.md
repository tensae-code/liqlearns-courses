# Hands-on — Internet, Web, website and web page

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Book model
Take a real book. Its cover is the home page; each leaf is a web page; the whole book is the website; the shelf it sits on with other books is the internet; reading your way through it is the Web. Explain the model to a partner, then let them test you on it.

## Reason hunt
Find three web pages or sites you know (or describe three from memory). For each, say which of the book's three reasons it exists for: sharing information or news, selling or advertising, expressing a view.
