# Bookmarking a Webpage for Later — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a bookmark?**
a) A saved signpost to a webpage's address, kept inside the browser  b) A favourite  c) While the page is open, by choosing Bookmark this page  d) Its address, not the page itself
**Answer: a) A saved signpost to a webpage's address, kept inside the browser**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a bookmark called in some browsers?**
a) While the page is open, by choosing Bookmark this page  b) A favourite  c) Its address, not the page itself  d) Because the bookmark leads to the living page at its address
**Answer: b) A favourite**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When is a bookmark made?**
a) Its address, not the page itself  b) Because the bookmark leads to the living page at its address  c) While the page is open, by choosing Bookmark this page  d) Short clear names and folders for kinds of pages
**Answer: c) While the page is open, by choosing Bookmark this page**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is saved in a bookmark — the page or its address?**
a) Because the bookmark leads to the living page at its address  b) Short clear names and folders for kinds of pages  c) Delete it, so the list stays honest  d) Its address, not the page itself
**Answer: d) Its address, not the page itself**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the returned page show updates?**
a) Because the bookmark leads to the living page at its address  b) Short clear names and folders for kinds of pages  c) Delete it, so the list stays honest  d) In the bookmarks list or on the bookmarks toolbar
**Answer: a) Because the bookmark leads to the living page at its address**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What makes bookmarks findable?**
a) Delete it, so the list stays honest  b) Short clear names and folders for kinds of pages  c) In the bookmarks list or on the bookmarks toolbar  d) A bookmark is a page you chose to keep; history is everything you visited
**Answer: b) Short clear names and folders for kinds of pages**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What should be done with a bookmark to a moved or closed page?**
a) In the bookmarks list or on the bookmarks toolbar  b) A bookmark is a page you chose to keep; history is everything you visited  c) Delete it, so the list stays honest  d) When a good page will be needed again later
**Answer: c) Delete it, so the list stays honest**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where do bookmarks live?**
a) A bookmark is a page you chose to keep; history is everything you visited  b) When a good page will be needed again later  c) A saved signpost to a webpage's address, kept inside the browser  d) In the bookmarks list or on the bookmarks toolbar
**Answer: d) In the bookmarks list or on the bookmarks toolbar**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How does a bookmark differ from history?**
a) A bookmark is a page you chose to keep; history is everything you visited  b) When a good page will be needed again later  c) A saved signpost to a webpage's address, kept inside the browser  d) A favourite
**Answer: a) A bookmark is a page you chose to keep; history is everything you visited**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When does a bookmark serve best?**
a) A saved signpost to a webpage's address, kept inside the browser  b) When a good page will be needed again later  c) A favourite  d) While the page is open, by choosing Bookmark this page
**Answer: b) When a good page will be needed again later**
