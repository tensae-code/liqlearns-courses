# Bookmarking a Webpage for Later — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a bookmark?**
**Answer: A saved signpost to a webpage's address, kept inside the browser**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a bookmark called in some browsers?**
**Answer: A favourite**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When is a bookmark made?**
**Answer: While the page is open, by choosing Bookmark this page**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is saved in a bookmark — the page or its address?**
**Answer: Its address, not the page itself**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the returned page show updates?**
**Answer: Because the bookmark leads to the living page at its address**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What makes bookmarks findable?**
**Answer: Short clear names and folders for kinds of pages**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What should be done with a bookmark to a moved or closed page?**
**Answer: Delete it, so the list stays honest**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where do bookmarks live?**
**Answer: In the bookmarks list or on the bookmarks toolbar**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How does a bookmark differ from history?**
**Answer: A bookmark is a page you chose to keep; history is everything you visited**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When does a bookmark serve best?**
**Answer: When a good page will be needed again later**
