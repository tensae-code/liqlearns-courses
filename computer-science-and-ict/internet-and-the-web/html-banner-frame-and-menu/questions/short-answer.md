# HTML Banner, Frame and Menu — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the banner stand?**
**Answer: Across the top of the page**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the banner carry?**
**Answer: The site's name, logo and colours — whose site this is**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is the banner used across pages?**
**Answer: The same on every page**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the frame arrangement do?**
**Answer: Divides the page into regions — top, side, main — each showing its own content**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why are standing parts kept separate from content?**
**Answer: So banner and menu stay standing while the main region changes**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the menu?**
**Answer: The standing list of links to the site's pages**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the menu's job?**
**Answer: Navigation — every main page one click away, from any page**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why should the menu look the same everywhere?**
**Answer: So visitors learn it once**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are the three parts together called in this lesson?**
**Answer: The site's furniture — banner names it, frame holds it, menu moves you through it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What changes from page to page — content or furniture?**
**Answer: Content changes; the furniture stands**
