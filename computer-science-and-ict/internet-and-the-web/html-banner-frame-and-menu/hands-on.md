# Hands-on — HTML Banner, Frame and Menu

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Draw the furniture', 'Sketch one page of a school site and label banner, frame regions and menu. Then sketch a second page of the same site and show what stayed standing.')

## Task two
('Build the three parts', 'Build a two-page site where both pages share the same banner and menu and differ only in the main region's content.')
