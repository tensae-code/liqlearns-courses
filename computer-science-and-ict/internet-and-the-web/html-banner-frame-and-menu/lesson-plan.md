## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The banner and the frame** — The **banner** is the strip across the **top** of the page: the site's name, logo and colours — the part that tells a visitor whose site this is, repeated the same on every page. The page's layout is held in a **frame** arrangement: the page is divided into regions (top, side, main) and each region shows its own content, so the banner and menu can stay standing while the main region changes. Malaysia's unit builds the frame arrangement with the page-structure tools its book teaches, keeping the standing parts separate from the changing content.
2. **The menu — the site's links, standing still** — The **menu** is the standing list of **links** to the site's pages — Home, About, Activities, Contact — placed where every page keeps it (often down the side or under the banner). Its job is navigation: from any page, every main page is one click away, and the menu looks the same everywhere so visitors learn it once. Together the three parts make the site's **furniture**: banner names it, frame holds it, menu moves you through it — content changes, furniture stands.
