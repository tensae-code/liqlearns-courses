# Video script — HTML Banner, Frame and Menu

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The **banner** is the strip across the **top** of the page: the site's name, logo and colours — the part that tells a visitor whose site this is, repeated the same on every page. The page's layout is held in a **frame** arrangement: the page is divided into regions (top, side, main) and each region shows its own content, so the banner and menu can stay standing while the main region changes. Malaysia's unit builds the frame arrangement with the page-structure tools its book teaches, keeping the standing parts separate from the changing content.
The **menu** is the standing list of **links** to the site's pages — Home, About, Activities, Contact — placed where every page keeps it (often down the side or under the banner). Its job is navigation: from any page, every main page is one click away, and the menu looks the same everywhere so visitors learn it once. Together the three parts make the site's **furniture**: banner names it, frame holds it, menu moves you through it — content changes, furniture stands.
