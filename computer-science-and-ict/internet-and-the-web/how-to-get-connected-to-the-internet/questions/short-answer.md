# How to Get Connected to the Internet — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Is a computer alone on the Internet?**
**Answer: No — a connection must join it to the network**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a modem do?**
**Answer: Translates between the computer's digital signals and the telephone line's signals**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does an ISP give you?**
**Answer: Entry to the Internet through its permanently connected machines, by subscription, with a username and password**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How many things does the book's connection take?**
**Answer: Four — device, modem, line, and an ISP account**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why is this lesson taught before the Web lessons?**
**Answer: Because browsing and e-mail all stand on this joining**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is set up on the computer?**
**Answer: The ISP's number and the account's username and password**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does 'online' mean here?**
**Answer: The modem has dialled, the ISP has checked the account, and the computer is connected**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the checking order when a connection fails?**
**Answer: Line plugged in and free, modem answering, username and password exactly right, account live**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why did the book's world connect, work, and disconnect?**
**Answer: Because dial-up connection time was paid for**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What has not changed about the joining?**
**Answer: Its four-part shape — device, carrier, line, account**
