# Hands-on — How to Get Connected to the Internet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four-thing audit
Choose any Internet connection you know — at home, school or a phone. Name its four things: device, carrier (the modem's modern heir), line or signal, and account/provider. For any one you cannot name, find out and complete the audit.

## Where does it fail?
A partner describes a failed connection in three clues, hiding which of the four things is at fault. Diagnose it aloud using the lesson's checking order, one question at a time, until only one of the four can be guilty.
