# Designing an HTML Page — and the dir Attribute — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) With what does page design begin?**
**Answer: Structure — skeleton, sections, headings, expected placement**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When do design choices sit?**
**Answer: Only on a sound structure**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a beautiful page with broken structure?**
**Answer: A decorated ruin**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What two lessons does this frame carry?**
**Answer: The formatting, images and tables lessons after it (the unit's later work)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In what order does the book teach?**
**Answer: Structure first, then design**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does dir set?**
**Answer: The reading direction of the page or an element**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Write dir for an Arabic page.**
**Answer: dir="rtl" on the <html> element**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name three things direction moves.**
**Answer: The text's start, alignment's home, the order of inline elements (and the reader's path)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is a mixed page designed?**
**Answer: Direction set per part — each language in its own direction**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is this first-class for Jordan's pupils?**
**Answer: They live in the Arabic–English mixture**
