# HTML Comments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does an HTML comment begin and end?**
a) It begins with <!-- and ends with -->  b) No — everything between the marks is ignored  c) No — it takes no space and shows nothing  d) Yes
**Answer: a) It begins with <!-- and ends with -->**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Does the browser show comments?**
a) No — it takes no space and shows nothing  b) No — everything between the marks is ignored  c) Yes  d) It swallows the rest of the page's code into the comment — the page breaks below it
**Answer: b) No — everything between the marks is ignored**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Can a comment be a heading or image on the page?**
a) Yes  b) It swallows the rest of the page's code into the comment — the page breaks below it  c) No — it takes no space and shows nothing  d) The people who maintain the code
**Answer: c) No — it takes no space and shows nothing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Can a comment cover several lines?**
a) It swallows the rest of the page's code into the comment — the page breaks below it  b) The people who maintain the code  c) Labelling parts of a page, and leaving reminders (also: explaining unusual code)  d) Yes
**Answer: d) Yes**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a missing closing mark do?**
a) It swallows the rest of the page's code into the comment — the page breaks below it  b) The people who maintain the code  c) Labelling parts of a page, and leaving reminders (also: explaining unusual code)  d) Wrap the block in comment marks — hidden without deleting, restored by removing the marks
**Answer: a) It swallows the rest of the page's code into the comment — the page breaks below it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Who are comments for?**
a) Labelling parts of a page, and leaving reminders (also: explaining unusual code)  b) The people who maintain the code  c) Wrap the block in comment marks — hidden without deleting, restored by removing the marks  d) Anyone who views the page's source
**Answer: b) The people who maintain the code**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give two uses Malaysia's unit names.**
a) Wrap the block in comment marks — hidden without deleting, restored by removing the marks  b) Anyone who views the page's source  c) Labelling parts of a page, and leaving reminders (also: explaining unusual code)  d) Passwords or private remarks
**Answer: c) Labelling parts of a page, and leaving reminders (also: explaining unusual code)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How can a comment switch code off?**
a) Anyone who views the page's source  b) Passwords or private remarks  c) It begins with <!-- and ends with -->  d) Wrap the block in comment marks — hidden without deleting, restored by removing the marks
**Answer: d) Wrap the block in comment marks — hidden without deleting, restored by removing the marks**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who can read a comment?**
a) Anyone who views the page's source  b) Passwords or private remarks  c) It begins with <!-- and ends with -->  d) No — everything between the marks is ignored
**Answer: a) Anyone who views the page's source**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must never go in a comment?**
a) It begins with <!-- and ends with -->  b) Passwords or private remarks  c) No — everything between the marks is ignored  d) No — it takes no space and shows nothing
**Answer: b) Passwords or private remarks**
