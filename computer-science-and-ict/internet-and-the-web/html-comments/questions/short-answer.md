# HTML Comments — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does an HTML comment begin and end?**
**Answer: It begins with <!-- and ends with -->**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Does the browser show comments?**
**Answer: No — everything between the marks is ignored**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Can a comment be a heading or image on the page?**
**Answer: No — it takes no space and shows nothing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Can a comment cover several lines?**
**Answer: Yes**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a missing closing mark do?**
**Answer: It swallows the rest of the page's code into the comment — the page breaks below it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Who are comments for?**
**Answer: The people who maintain the code**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give two uses Malaysia's unit names.**
**Answer: Labelling parts of a page, and leaving reminders (also: explaining unusual code)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How can a comment switch code off?**
**Answer: Wrap the block in comment marks — hidden without deleting, restored by removing the marks**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who can read a comment?**
**Answer: Anyone who views the page's source**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must never go in a comment?**
**Answer: Passwords or private remarks**
