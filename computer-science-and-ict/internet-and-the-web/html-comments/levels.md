## Sub lesson 1 — The comment's shape — <!-- and -->
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: It begins with <!-- and ends with -->; No — everything between the marks is ignored; No — it takes no space and shows nothing; Yes; It swallows the rest of the page's code into the comment — the page breaks below it.

## Sub lesson 2 — What comments are for
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The people who maintain the code; Labelling parts of a page, and leaving reminders (also: explaining unusual code); Wrap the block in comment marks — hidden without deleting, restored by removing the marks; Anyone who views the page's source; Passwords or private remarks.
