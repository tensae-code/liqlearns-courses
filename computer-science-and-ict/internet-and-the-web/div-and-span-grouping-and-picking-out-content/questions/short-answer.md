# Div and Span — Grouping and Picking Out Content — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do div and span say about meaning?**
**Answer: Nothing — they are content-free**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does div group?**
**Answer: Block-level content into one unit**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What gives a div its power?**
**Answer: A class or id attribute that CSS and scripts take hold of**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How are modern pages built, in the lesson's image?**
**Answer: As trees of divs — named regions**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Does a div display anything by itself?**
**Answer: No**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does span pick out?**
**Answer: A run of text inside a block**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Does span break the line?**
**Answer: No — the flow continues around it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can a classed span receive?**
**Answer: Colour, size or scripting, inside its sentence**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) State the pair's division.**
**Answer: Div for blocks, span for words inside blocks**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What breaks when a div is used where a span belongs?**
**Answer: The reader's line**
