# Hands-on — Div and Span — Grouping and Picking Out Content

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Region the page** — Take a plain article page. Wrap its regions in divs with class names — header, story, aside, footer — and label each region's job.

## Task two
**Pick out the words** — In one paragraph, span three kinds of runs — a name, a price, a foreign word — and style each kind differently with CSS.
