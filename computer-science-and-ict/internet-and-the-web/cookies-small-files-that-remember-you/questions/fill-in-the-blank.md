# Cookies — Small Files That Remember You — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. A cookie usually carries a long random session ____ by which the site recognises the browser.**
**Answer: ID**

**2. Cookies set by the site you are visiting are called ____-party cookies.**
**Answer: first**

**3. Following a person across sites to build a profile is called ____.**
**Answer: tracking**
