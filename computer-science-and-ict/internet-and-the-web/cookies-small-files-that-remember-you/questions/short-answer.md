# Cookies — Small Files That Remember You — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. Why can a website not simply remember you on its own server between visits?**
**Answer: Because between visits there is no connection, and many browsers may arrive claiming anything — the server needs something on the visitor's side to recognise. The cookie, stored by the browser and handed back each time, is that recognition.**

**2. A friend clicks 'accept all' on every consent box to make it disappear. Give the one-sentence case for pausing.**
**Answer: Accepting all lets third-party trackers assemble a cross-site profile of you — the box is the one moment the following is actually your choice, so it is worth the ten seconds to refuse what you never invited.**
