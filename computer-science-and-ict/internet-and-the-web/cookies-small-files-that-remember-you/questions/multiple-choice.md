# Cookies — Small Files That Remember You — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A cookie is —**
a) A small program  b) A small text file a site asks your browser to store and hand back  c) A virus  d) A password
**Answer: b) A small text file a site asks your browser to store and hand back** — A cookie cannot run or read files — it is a note, faithfully handed back.

**2. Where is a cookie stored?**
a) On the website's server only  b) In your browser, on your device  c) On paper  d) In the network cable
**Answer: b) In your browser, on your device** — The memory lives on the visitor's side — that is the whole design.

**3. A full basket after you return tomorrow is the work of —**
a) A third-party tracker  b) A first-party cookie remembering your session  c) The router  d) DNS
**Answer: b) A first-party cookie remembering your session** — First-party cookies do the remembering the visitor asked for.

**4. A third-party cookie typically belongs to —**
a) The site you are visiting  b) An advertiser or tracker whose page-piece loads inside many sites  c) Your browser's maker  d) Your school
**Answer: b) An advertiser or tracker whose page-piece loads inside many sites** — Seeing its piece load on site after site is how the trail is assembled.

**5. Tracking means —**
a) Fast loading  b) Following a person's movements across sites and joining them into a profile  c) Saving a file  d) Encrypting a page
**Answer: b) Following a person's movements across sites and joining them into a profile** — The profile is then used, typically, to choose ads.

**6. A session cookie differs from a persistent cookie in that it —**
a) Is bigger  b) Dies when the browser closes, while a persistent one carries an expiry date  c) Tracks more  d) Cannot be deleted
**Answer: b) Dies when the browser closes, while a persistent one carries an expiry date** — Lifetime is the difference; both are handed back while they live.
