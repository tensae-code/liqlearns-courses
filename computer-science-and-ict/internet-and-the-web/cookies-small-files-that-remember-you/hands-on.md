# Hands-on — Cookies — Small Files That Remember You

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Empty the jar
In your browser's settings, find the cookies stored for one site you use. Count them. Then clear that site's cookies, revisit the site, and write down three things that changed (sign-in, basket, settings). You have just seen the memory the cookie was carrying.

## Spot the third party
Open a news page and count the companies named in its cookie-consent box before pressing anything. Every name beyond the site itself is a would-be third party. Decide, name by name if the box allows, what you accept — and write down which choice you made and why.
