# Video script — Cookies — Small Files That Remember You

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A website forgets you the moment you leave. A cookie is its memory — on your side.

It is a small text file in your browser. The site writes it. Your browser hands it back.

Your basket, your sign-in, your language: that is the site's own cookie remembering you.

But other companies hide inside pages, too. Their cookies follow you from site to site.

That following is called tracking. The consent box is your chance to say no.

The files live in your browser — so the controls live there, too.
