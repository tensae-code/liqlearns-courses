# Hands-on — Online Research Kinds and Digital Literacy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 8 §8.1 Online Research and Digital Literacy (§8.1.1 importance of online research; §8.1.2 types — general, academic, market, fact-checking, health; §8.1.3 digital literacy; §8.1.4 key components — using technology, searching for information (Punjab farmers searching irrigation methods), evaluating sources) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach online research kinds and digital literacy to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
