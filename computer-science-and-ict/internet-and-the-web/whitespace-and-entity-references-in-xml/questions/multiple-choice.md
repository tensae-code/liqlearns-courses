# Whitespace and entity references in XML — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** XML treats multiple white spaces by —
a) collapsing them to one  b) keeping them — it does not truncate them  c) deleting them
**Answer: b) keeping them — it does not truncate them** — XML keeps all whitespace.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** HTML treats multiple white spaces by —
a) keeping them all  b) collapsing them to one  c) erroring
**Answer: b) collapsing them to one** — HTML collapses them to one — the opposite rule.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A < inside content is read by the parser as —
a) a space  b) the start of a new element  c) a comment
**Answer: b) the start of a new element** — that misreading is why the reference swap exists.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The strictly illegal characters are —
a) < and &  b) > and '  c) " and >
**Answer: a) < and &** — only < and & are strictly illegal; replacing > is good habit.
