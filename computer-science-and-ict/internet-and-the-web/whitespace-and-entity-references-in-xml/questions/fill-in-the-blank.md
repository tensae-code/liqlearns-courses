# Whitespace and entity references in XML — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or code.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The reference for less than is &___;
**Answer: lt** — &lt; is the less-than reference.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The reference &amp; stands for the ___.
**Answer: ampersand** — &amp; is the ampersand's reference.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The quotation mark's reference is &___;
**Answer: quot** — &quot; is the quotation mark's reference.
