# Video script — Whitespace and entity references in XML

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Type five, less-than, six — as content. Error!
The parser thinks a new element is starting.
Swap the sign for its reference: and-l-t-semicolon.
Now it reads as text. Fixed.
Five references are ready-made: less than, greater than,
ampersand, apostrophe, quotation mark.
Two are strictly illegal as themselves: less-than and ampersand.
And spaces? XML keeps them all. Every one.
