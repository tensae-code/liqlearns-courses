# HTML Headings and Paragraphs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How many heading levels does HTML give?**
**Answer: Six — h1 to h6**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which heading is biggest and most important?**
**Answer: h1 — usually the page's title**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are the parts of a heading element?**
**Answer: An opening tag, the heading text, and a closing tag**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How do browsers show headings?**
**Answer: Bold, sized by level, with space around them**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Are heading levels just sizes?**
**Answer: No — they are a rank, stepped down in order like chapters and sections**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which element wraps ordinary text?**
**Answer: The paragraph element — <p>…</p>**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Are line breaks typed in the HTML shown by the browser?**
**Answer: No — running spaces and breaks are gathered and the text flows to the window's width**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does HTML separate one thought from the next?**
**Answer: A new <p> element for each paragraph**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Write a level-2 heading element for the text Sports Day.**
**Answer: <h2>Sports Day</h2>**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What carries a page's actual reading?**
**Answer: The paragraphs, under their headings**
