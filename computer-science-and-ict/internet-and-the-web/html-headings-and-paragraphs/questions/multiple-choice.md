# HTML Headings and Paragraphs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How many heading levels does HTML give?**
a) Six — h1 to h6  b) h1 — usually the page's title  c) An opening tag, the heading text, and a closing tag  d) Bold, sized by level, with space around them
**Answer: a) Six — h1 to h6**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which heading is biggest and most important?**
a) An opening tag, the heading text, and a closing tag  b) h1 — usually the page's title  c) Bold, sized by level, with space around them  d) No — they are a rank, stepped down in order like chapters and sections
**Answer: b) h1 — usually the page's title**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are the parts of a heading element?**
a) Bold, sized by level, with space around them  b) No — they are a rank, stepped down in order like chapters and sections  c) An opening tag, the heading text, and a closing tag  d) The paragraph element — <p>…</p>
**Answer: c) An opening tag, the heading text, and a closing tag**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How do browsers show headings?**
a) No — they are a rank, stepped down in order like chapters and sections  b) The paragraph element — <p>…</p>  c) No — running spaces and breaks are gathered and the text flows to the window's width  d) Bold, sized by level, with space around them
**Answer: d) Bold, sized by level, with space around them**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Are heading levels just sizes?**
a) No — they are a rank, stepped down in order like chapters and sections  b) The paragraph element — <p>…</p>  c) No — running spaces and breaks are gathered and the text flows to the window's width  d) A new <p> element for each paragraph
**Answer: a) No — they are a rank, stepped down in order like chapters and sections**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which element wraps ordinary text?**
a) No — running spaces and breaks are gathered and the text flows to the window's width  b) The paragraph element — <p>…</p>  c) A new <p> element for each paragraph  d) <h2>Sports Day</h2>
**Answer: b) The paragraph element — <p>…</p>**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Are line breaks typed in the HTML shown by the browser?**
a) A new <p> element for each paragraph  b) <h2>Sports Day</h2>  c) No — running spaces and breaks are gathered and the text flows to the window's width  d) The paragraphs, under their headings
**Answer: c) No — running spaces and breaks are gathered and the text flows to the window's width**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does HTML separate one thought from the next?**
a) <h2>Sports Day</h2>  b) The paragraphs, under their headings  c) Six — h1 to h6  d) A new <p> element for each paragraph
**Answer: d) A new <p> element for each paragraph**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Write a level-2 heading element for the text Sports Day.**
a) <h2>Sports Day</h2>  b) The paragraphs, under their headings  c) Six — h1 to h6  d) h1 — usually the page's title
**Answer: a) <h2>Sports Day</h2>**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What carries a page's actual reading?**
a) Six — h1 to h6  b) The paragraphs, under their headings  c) h1 — usually the page's title  d) An opening tag, the heading text, and a closing tag
**Answer: b) The paragraphs, under their headings**
