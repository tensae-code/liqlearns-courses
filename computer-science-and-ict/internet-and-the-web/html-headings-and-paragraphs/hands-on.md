# Hands-on — HTML Headings and Paragraphs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Mark up a page', 'Take a short printed article. Write it as HTML: title in h1, section names in h2, and every block of text in <p> elements. Open it in a browser and check the structure.')

## Task two
('Fix the wall', 'You are given a page whose text is one bare block with Enter keys for breaks. Rewrite it with headings and paragraphs so a browser shows its structure.')
