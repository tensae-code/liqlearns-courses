# Hands-on — FTP — moving big files over the internet

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Sort the sending jobs
Write six sending jobs on cards — a short message, one huge video file, a folder of twenty documents, a one-line question, a very large photo set, a quick greeting. Sort them: email or FTP? Say the rule out loud for each FTP card.

## Draw the journey
Draw two computers in two towns and the large file travelling between them by FTP. Label the sending end 'upload' and the receiving end 'download'.
