Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does an HTML form do?** → **It collects a visitor's input and sends it to a server**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which attribute of <input> chooses the field kind?** → **The type attribute**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Checkbox or radio — which lets a visitor pick only one of a group?** → **Radio buttons — checkboxes allow ticking any number**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does every field need beside it?** → **A <label> — the visible words saying what the field asks, joined by matching for and id**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Armenia teach HTML forms?** → **Grade 9, §5 — lists and forms in a webpage**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a form sent?** → **By its submit control**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do action and method say?** → **Action says where the data goes; method says how (GET in the address, POST in the body)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the required attribute do?** → **The browser will not send the form until that field is filled**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is placeholder text for?** → **An example inside the field showing the expected kind of answer**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the politeness rule of forms?** → **Ask only what will actually be used — a form requests a stranger's data**
