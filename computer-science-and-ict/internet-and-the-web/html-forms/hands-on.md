# Hands-on — HTML Forms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Build a signup form** — Write an HTML form for a school club signup: name (text), class (radio group), interests (checkboxes), email, and a submit button — every field labelled, the needed ones required.

## Task two
**Form audit** — Find a form on a real website. List its field types, its required fields, and one thing it asks that it probably does not need.
