# Hands-on — The Structure of a Website — Homepage and Common Pages

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Draw the skeleton** — Choose a real website. Draw its structure map — homepage, sections, pages — and label which of the three types each part uses.

## Task two
**One job per page** — Take a cluttered homepage you know. Redesign it on paper: what stays, what moves to its own page, and the route to each.
