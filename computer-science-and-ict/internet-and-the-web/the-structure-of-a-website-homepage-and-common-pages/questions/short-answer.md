# The Structure of a Website — Homepage and Common Pages — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a homepage?**
**Answer: The site's door and map in one**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two things must a homepage do?**
**Answer: Name the site and route visitors onward**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two common pages.**
**Answer: About and contact (also section pages, help, search)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How many jobs should one page have?**
**Answer: One**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is structure, as an agreement?**
**Answer: Which job lives where, so visitors find things by memory**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three structure types.**
**Answer: Sequence, hierarchy and web**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where is a sequence structure right?**
**Answer: Step-by-step journeys — a lesson, a checkout**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which shape is commonest?**
**Answer: Hierarchy — the tree**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a web structure's risk?**
**Answer: Visitors get lost in free linking**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What should the structure choice follow?**
**Answer: The visitor's errand**
