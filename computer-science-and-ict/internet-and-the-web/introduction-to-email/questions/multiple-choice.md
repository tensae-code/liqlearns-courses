# Introduction to Email — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is email?**
a) A message sent from one computer to another over the internet — electronic mail  b) One person's mailbox  c) Files, as attachments  d) In the receiver's inbox
**Answer: a) A message sent from one computer to another over the internet — electronic mail**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does an email address name?**
a) Files, as attachments  b) One person's mailbox  c) In the receiver's inbox  d) It waits — writer and reader never need to be present together
**Answer: b) One person's mailbox**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What can email carry besides words?**
a) In the receiver's inbox  b) It waits — writer and reader never need to be present together  c) Files, as attachments  d) It is delivered in moments where a posted letter takes days
**Answer: c) Files, as attachments**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a received email wait?**
a) It waits — writer and reader never need to be present together  b) It is delivered in moments where a posted letter takes days  c) It can be reread, searched, forwarded and filed  d) In the receiver's inbox
**Answer: d) In the receiver's inbox**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why is email different from a phone call?**
a) It waits — writer and reader never need to be present together  b) It is delivered in moments where a posted letter takes days  c) It can be reread, searched, forwarded and filed  d) It is fast, written and kept
**Answer: a) It waits — writer and reader never need to be present together**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) In what way is email fast?**
a) It can be reread, searched, forwarded and filed  b) It is delivered in moments where a posted letter takes days  c) It is fast, written and kept  d) Gmail, on their G Suite for Education school account
**Answer: b) It is delivered in moments where a posted letter takes days**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) In what way is email kept?**
a) It is fast, written and kept  b) Gmail, on their G Suite for Education school account  c) It can be reread, searched, forwarded and filed  d) Sign into the account and work from the inbox
**Answer: c) It can be reread, searched, forwarded and filed**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why do schools and offices run on email?**
a) Gmail, on their G Suite for Education school account  b) Sign into the account and work from the inbox  c) A message sent from one computer to another over the internet — electronic mail  d) It is fast, written and kept
**Answer: d) It is fast, written and kept**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which email do Bhutan's Class VI pupils use?**
a) Gmail, on their G Suite for Education school account  b) Sign into the account and work from the inbox  c) A message sent from one computer to another over the internet — electronic mail  d) One person's mailbox
**Answer: a) Gmail, on their G Suite for Education school account**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do this chapter's next lessons do with email?**
a) A message sent from one computer to another over the internet — electronic mail  b) Sign into the account and work from the inbox  c) One person's mailbox  d) Files, as attachments
**Answer: b) Sign into the account and work from the inbox**
