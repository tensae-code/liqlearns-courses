# Introduction to Email — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is email?" is: A message sent from one computer to another over the internet — electronic mail**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does an email address name?" is: It waits — writer and reader never need to be present together**
**Answer: False** — One person's mailbox

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What can email carry besides words?" is: Files, as attachments**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does a received email wait?" is: It can be reread, searched, forwarded and filed**
**Answer: False** — In the receiver's inbox

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why is email different from a phone call?" is: It waits — writer and reader never need to be present together**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "In what way is email fast?" is: Gmail, on their G Suite for Education school account**
**Answer: False** — It is delivered in moments where a posted letter takes days

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "In what way is email kept?" is: It can be reread, searched, forwarded and filed**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why do schools and offices run on email?" is: A message sent from one computer to another over the internet — electronic mail**
**Answer: False** — It is fast, written and kept

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which email do Bhutan's Class VI pupils use?" is: Gmail, on their G Suite for Education school account**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do this chapter's next lessons do with email?" is: Files, as attachments**
**Answer: False** — Sign into the account and work from the inbox
