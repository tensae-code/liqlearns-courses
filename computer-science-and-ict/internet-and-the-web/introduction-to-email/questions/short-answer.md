# Introduction to Email — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is email?**
**Answer: A message sent from one computer to another over the internet — electronic mail**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does an email address name?**
**Answer: One person's mailbox**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What can email carry besides words?**
**Answer: Files, as attachments**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a received email wait?**
**Answer: In the receiver's inbox**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why is email different from a phone call?**
**Answer: It waits — writer and reader never need to be present together**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) In what way is email fast?**
**Answer: It is delivered in moments where a posted letter takes days**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) In what way is email kept?**
**Answer: It can be reread, searched, forwarded and filed**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why do schools and offices run on email?**
**Answer: It is fast, written and kept**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which email do Bhutan's Class VI pupils use?**
**Answer: Gmail, on their G Suite for Education school account**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do this chapter's next lessons do with email?**
**Answer: Sign into the account and work from the inbox**
