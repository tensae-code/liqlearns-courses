**1. (Sub lesson 1) When is information reliable?**
a) When it is true, current enough for the question, and from a source that knows and can be checked  b) A search rarely arrives in one step — the searcher compares at each stage, like a traveller checking signs  c) No — stale pages, dressed-up guesses and repeated opinion fail the same test  d) Who published it, and would they know — named, expert sources outrank unnamed copiers
