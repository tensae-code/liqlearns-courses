# Unit 4 in short — the internet

## Do it first

Close the other lessons in your mind and tell the unit back to yourself: what is the internet, what services ride on it, how does a page reach you, how does email work, and what do social networks give and threaten? Five answers. This lesson checks all five, in order.

## The unit in short

**The internet** is a **network of networks** — the largest computer network in the world. Its named **services** include the **World Wide Web, email, Telnet and FTP**. On the Web, **pages** join by **hyperlinks** into **websites**; every page has a **URL** of two parts — **protocol identifier and resource name**. Pages are **static** or **dynamic**; **FTP** moves large files; **HTTP** carries the request-and-response round trip between **client** and **server**. **Browsing** searches by **keywords**. **Email** is created, addressed as **username@domainname**, and worked through **inbox, message pane, compose pane and forward**. **Social networks** connect people in **virtual communities** — and, **used properly**, they bring the five benefits; **used improperly**, the eight risks.
