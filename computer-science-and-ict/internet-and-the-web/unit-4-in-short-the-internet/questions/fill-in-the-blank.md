# Unit 4 in short — the internet — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1.** Pages are viewed through a web _______.
**Answer: browser**

**2.** A page that does not change until updated is a _______ web page.
**Answer: static**

**3.** FTP stands for _______ Transfer Protocol.
**Answer: File**

**4.** WWW stands for World _______ Web.
**Answer: Wide**
