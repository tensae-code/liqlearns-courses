# Lesson plan — Unit 4 in short — the internet

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Internet and the Web · **Lesson:** Unit 4 in short — the internet
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 8 — Internet and the Web of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 4 — Internet. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Unit 4 in one pass: the internet is a network of networks offering services — WWW, email, Telnet and FTP — reached through browsers and protocols, worked through email, and extended into social networks whose benefits and risks depend on proper use.
**This lesson's own level:** it is the summary lesson of the Grade 8 internet unit: it stands on every internet and social-network lesson of this band and gathers them; a learner landing here directly gets the whole unit in short form, with each part pointing back to its own lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the whole Grade 8 internet set — Internet services; The Web; Static and dynamic pages; FTP; HTTP; Searching; the three email lessons; and the Computers in Society set. Recommended, never forced. A learner can land here directly; the lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell the whole unit in short, part by part.
2. Name the four internet services and the two parts of a URL.
3. Close the unit's review checks: web browser; protocol identifier and resource name; static web page; File Transfer Protocol; World Wide Web.
