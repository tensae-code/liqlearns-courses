# Video script — Project — Building a Website about AI Applications

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Explore AI Applications** is a **seven-file website**: its pages built in VS Code across the project — the home page and the application pages that survey where AI works — styled by the course's CSS, linked into one family, with images and tables in their HTML lessons' crafts. Seven files is the project's discipline: big enough that organisation matters (the project folder, consistent styles, working links everywhere), small enough to finish whole.
Among the seven stands the **contact form page**: the page where a visitor **writes to the site** — name, message, the form's fields and its sending. It changes the site's nature: every other page speaks; this one **listens**. Kuwait ends the hand-built site here, with hosting and FTP as the project's road out (taught beside it), because a site that can be visited and answered is the complete thing the year promised: designed, built, styled, linked, published, and open for reply.
