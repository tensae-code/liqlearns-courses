# Internet Services — Everyday Uses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an internet service?**
a) A job done for you over the network  b) Moves and checks money through the bank's site instead of its counter  c) Buys goods from online shops, chosen and paid for from home  d) Teaches over the network — lessons, exercises and courses
**Answer: a) A job done for you over the network**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does e-banking do?**
a) Buys goods from online shops, chosen and paid for from home  b) Moves and checks money through the bank's site instead of its counter  c) Teaches over the network — lessons, exercises and courses  d) A journey — the counter, the shop and the classroom come to the computer
**Answer: b) Moves and checks money through the bank's site instead of its counter**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does e-shopping do?**
