# Internet Services — Everyday Uses — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is an internet service?" is: A job done for you over the network**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does e-banking do?" is: Buys goods from online shops, chosen and paid for from home**
**Answer: False** — Moves and checks money through the bank's site instead of its counter

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does e-shopping do?" is: Buys goods from online shops, chosen and paid for from home**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does e-learning do?" is: A journey — the counter, the shop and the classroom come to the computer**
**Answer: False** — Teaches over the network — lessons, exercises and courses

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does each service replace?" is: A journey — the counter, the shop and the classroom come to the computer**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name the weather service the book uses." is: Find information — they return the pages that hold what you need**
**Answer: False** — Mongolia's tsag-agaar.mn

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do search services do?" is: Find information — they return the pages that hold what you need**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why is search called the door to the other services?" is: No — it is a market of services**
**Answer: False** — A service you cannot find is a service you cannot use

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Is the internet one thing?" is: No — it is a market of services**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Who is the skilled user, in the book's closing point?" is: A job done for you over the network**
**Answer: False** — The one who can name the right service for the job in hand
