# Internet Services — Everyday Uses — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an internet service?**
**Answer: A job done for you over the network**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does e-banking do?**
**Answer: Moves and checks money through the bank's site instead of its counter**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does e-shopping do?**
**Answer: Buys goods from online shops, chosen and paid for from home**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does e-learning do?**
**Answer: Teaches over the network — lessons, exercises and courses**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does each service replace?**
**Answer: A journey — the counter, the shop and the classroom come to the computer**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the weather service the book uses.**
**Answer: Mongolia's tsag-agaar.mn**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do search services do?**
**Answer: Find information — they return the pages that hold what you need**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is search called the door to the other services?**
**Answer: A service you cannot find is a service you cannot use**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Is the internet one thing?**
**Answer: No — it is a market of services**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who is the skilled user, in the book's closing point?**
**Answer: The one who can name the right service for the job in hand**
