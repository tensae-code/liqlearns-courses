# Hands-on — Internet Services — Everyday Uses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Name the service', 'For five jobs — check a bank balance, buy boots, learn a lesson, plan a trip by the weather, find a fact — name the internet service for each and say what journey it replaces.')

## Task two
('Visit a Mongolian service', 'Open a Mongolian service site your teacher allows (such as the weather service). Write what service it offers, who it serves, and one thing you found there.')
