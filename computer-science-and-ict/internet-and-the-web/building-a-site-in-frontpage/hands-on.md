# Hands-on — Building a Site in FrontPage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rebuild the site
Take a site you designed (or the class design). Create its skeleton in FrontPage — home page and planned pages, linked as designed — then fill one page completely with prepared text and a prepared image.

## Walk it as a visitor
Click through the whole site using only its links. List any page you cannot reach, any link that lands wrong, and fix them before calling the site developed.
