# Building a Site in FrontPage — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does FrontPage work on?**
**Answer: The whole site — its pages, folders and links together**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is a page edited in FrontPage?**
**Answer: Visually — text formatted, pictures and tables placed — while the editor writes the HTML underneath**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does FrontPage sit on the book's ladder?**
**Answer: In the middle — more control than Word conversion, less bare code than Notepad**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What becomes real first in developing a site?**
**Answer: The skeleton — home page and planned pages, linked as designed**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What fills the pages after the skeleton?**
**Answer: Prepared content — texts, prepared images, and tables where they help**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How are links checked?**
**Answer: By walking the site as a visitor, using only its links**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When is a developed site ready?**
**Answer: When it walks cleanly — ready for publication**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What design work does the build start from?**
**Answer: The course's opening design — purpose, audience, structure**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Can the HTML underneath be viewed in FrontPage?**
**Answer: Yes — it can also be viewed and touched directly**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is Belarus's deliberate pattern here?**
**Answer: The same site idea is carried from design, through hand-written Notepad HTML, and rebuilt in FrontPage**
