# Video script — Building a Site in FrontPage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**FrontPage** is a site **editor**: where Notepad works on one HTML document at a time, FrontPage works on the **whole site** — its window shows the pages, the folders holding them, and the links between them, alongside the page being edited.
The page itself is edited **visually**: text is typed and formatted, pictures and tables are placed, and hyperlinks are made by choosing their target — while the editor writes the HTML underneath, which can also be viewed and touched directly.
**Developing** the site (§ 5.4) is the build carried out in order: the design from the course's opening sections — purpose, audience, structure — becomes a real site skeleton first (the home page and the planned pages, linked as designed), then each page is filled with its prepared content — texts, the images prepared in § 4, tables where they help (§ 5.3) — and the links are checked by walking the site as a visitor.
Only a site that walks cleanly is ready for the next step, publication (§ 5.5).
