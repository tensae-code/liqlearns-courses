# Hands-on — Website Project — a School, Tourism or E-Library Site

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Build one of the three', 'Choose one project — school, tourism or e-library — plan its pages for its audience, build the site, and publish or present it as a finished whole.')

## Task two
('Judge by audience', 'Swap sites with a partner. Visit theirs as its audience — a parent, a tourist, a reader — and write what you could not find or reach.')
