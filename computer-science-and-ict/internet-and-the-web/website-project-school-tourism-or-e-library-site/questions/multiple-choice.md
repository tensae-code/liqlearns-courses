# Website Project — a School, Tourism or E-Library Site — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name Libya's three set website projects.**
a) A secondary-school website, a tourism-company website, and an electronic library  b) Choosing pages, order and content for the site's named audience — the project's first and largest work  c) Informing (school), persuading (tourism), providing (e-library)  d) Find and reach books
**Answer: a) A secondary-school website, a tourism-company website, and an electronic library**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is each project's audience work?**
a) Informing (school), persuading (tourism), providing (e-library)  b) Choosing pages, order and content for the site's named audience — the project's first and largest work  c) Find and reach books  d) Structure, formatting, links, lists and tables, then publishing (any four in order)
**Answer: b) Choosing pages, order and content for the site's named audience — the project's first and largest work**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How do the three projects' purposes differ?**
a) Find and reach books  b) Structure, formatting, links, lists and tables, then publishing (any four in order)  c) Informing (school), persuading (tourism), providing (e-library)  d) A registered domain, a chosen host, and the files uploaded by FTP
**Answer: c) Informing (school), persuading (tourism), providing (e-library)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must an e-library site let readers do?**
a) Structure, formatting, links, lists and tables, then publishing (any four in order)  b) A registered domain, a chosen host, and the files uploaded by FTP  c) As a whole: reach, unity of look, and the published site actually working  d) Find and reach books
**Answer: d) Find and reach books**
