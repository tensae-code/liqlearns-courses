# Video script — Web Utilities — Plug-ins, Filters and File Transfer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **plug-in** is a small program that **adds a capability to the browser** — playing a kind of video, showing a kind of document — that the browser lacks on its own; the page asks, the plug-in performs. A **filter** works the other way: it **screens what comes in** — blocking pages, adverts or material a school or parent has set aside — so the browser shows less, on purpose. Fiji teaches the pair as the browser's two directions of help: more power in, more protection around.
The third utility is the oldest: **file transfer** — moving whole files between computers over the network, the work the **FTP** way of the internet was built for. Uploading puts a file up; downloading brings one down; web pages themselves arrive by transfer, and the big files of school and office life — documents, pictures, programs — travel this way. Fiji's section gathers the three because together they are the web's working kit: the browser extended, the browser guarded, and the files carried.
