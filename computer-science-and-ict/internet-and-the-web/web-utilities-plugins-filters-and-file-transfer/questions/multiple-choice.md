# Web Utilities — Plug-ins, Filters and File Transfer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a plug-in?**
a) A small program that adds a capability to the browser  b) Playing a kind of video or showing a kind of document the browser cannot show alone  c) Screens what comes in — blocking pages, adverts or set-aside material  d) A school's or a parent's
**Answer: a) A small program that adds a capability to the browser**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Give a plug-in's job.**
a) Screens what comes in — blocking pages, adverts or set-aside material  b) Playing a kind of video or showing a kind of document the browser cannot show alone  c) A school's or a parent's  d) More power in (plug-ins), more protection around (filters)
**Answer: b) Playing a kind of video or showing a kind of document the browser cannot show alone**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a filter do?**
a) A school's or a parent's  b) More power in (plug-ins), more protection around (filters)  c) Screens what comes in — blocking pages, adverts or set-aside material  d) Moving whole files between computers over the network
**Answer: c) Screens what comes in — blocking pages, adverts or set-aside material**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Whose settings can a filter carry?**
a) More power in (plug-ins), more protection around (filters)  b) Moving whole files between computers over the network  c) File transfer  d) A school's or a parent's
**Answer: d) A school's or a parent's**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are the browser's two directions of help?**
a) More power in (plug-ins), more protection around (filters)  b) Moving whole files between computers over the network  c) File transfer  d) Putting a file up; bringing a file down
**Answer: a) More power in (plug-ins), more protection around (filters)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is file transfer?**
a) File transfer  b) Moving whole files between computers over the network  c) Putting a file up; bringing a file down  d) Yes — pages themselves arrive by transfer
**Answer: b) Moving whole files between computers over the network**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What was FTP built for?**
a) Putting a file up; bringing a file down  b) Yes — pages themselves arrive by transfer  c) File transfer  d) The browser extended, the browser guarded, and the files carried
**Answer: c) File transfer**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do uploading and downloading mean?**
a) Yes — pages themselves arrive by transfer  b) The browser extended, the browser guarded, and the files carried  c) A small program that adds a capability to the browser  d) Putting a file up; bringing a file down

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Do web pages use transfer?**
a) Yes — pages themselves arrive by transfer  b) The browser extended, the browser guarded, and the files carried  c) A small program that adds a capability to the browser  d) Playing a kind of video or showing a kind of document the browser cannot show alone
