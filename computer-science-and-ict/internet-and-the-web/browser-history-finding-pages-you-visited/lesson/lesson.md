# Browser History — Finding Pages You Visited

## Do it first

Yesterday you read the perfect page — and today you cannot remember its address, its name, or how you got there. The browser can: Class VII teaches browser history, the browser's diary of everywhere it has been.

## Sub lesson 1 — History — the browser's diary

**History** is the list the browser keeps of the **pages you visited**, in the order and on the days you visited them — today, yesterday, last week. Open History and the diary is there: titles and addresses, grouped by time, **searchable** by a word from the page's title. The page you lost is found by remembering when you saw it or a word it carried, not by wandering the web again.

## Sub lesson 2 — Clearing history — your diary, your choice

History is also **private information**: it shows what a person has been reading, so browsers let the user **clear** it — the last hour, a day, or everything. On a shared school computer, clearing history is courtesy and safety together. Class VII teaches history beside bookmarks: history keeps everything automatically; a bookmark keeps what you chose.
