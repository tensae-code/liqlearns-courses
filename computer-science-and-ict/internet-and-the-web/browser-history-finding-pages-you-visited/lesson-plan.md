## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **History — the browser's diary** — **History** is the list the browser keeps of the **pages you visited**, in the order and on the days you visited them — today, yesterday, last week. Open History and the diary is there: titles and addresses, grouped by time, **searchable** by a word from the page's title. The page you lost is found by remembering when you saw it or a word it carried, not by wandering the web again.
2. **Clearing history — your diary, your choice** — History is also **private information**: it shows what a person has been reading, so browsers let the user **clear** it — the last hour, a day, or everything. On a shared school computer, clearing history is courtesy and safety together. Class VII teaches history beside bookmarks: history keeps everything automatically; a bookmark keeps what you chose.
