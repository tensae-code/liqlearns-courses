# Browser History — Finding Pages You Visited — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is browser history?**
a) The list the browser keeps of the pages you visited, in time order  b) By time — today, yesterday, last week  c) By when you saw it, or by searching a word from its title  d) The page's title and address
**Answer: a) The list the browser keeps of the pages you visited, in time order**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is history grouped?**
a) By when you saw it, or by searching a word from its title  b) By time — today, yesterday, last week  c) The page's title and address  d) It shows what a person has been reading
**Answer: b) By time — today, yesterday, last week**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a lost page found in history?**
a) The page's title and address  b) It shows what a person has been reading  c) By when you saw it, or by searching a word from its title  d) Clear it — the last hour, a day or everything
**Answer: c) By when you saw it, or by searching a word from its title**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does history show for each visit?**
a) It shows what a person has been reading  b) Clear it — the last hour, a day or everything  c) As courtesy and safety together  d) The page's title and address
**Answer: d) The page's title and address**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why is history private information?**
a) It shows what a person has been reading  b) Clear it — the last hour, a day or everything  c) As courtesy and safety together  d) History keeps everything automatically; a bookmark keeps what you chose
**Answer: a) It shows what a person has been reading**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What can the user do with history?**
a) As courtesy and safety together  b) Clear it — the last hour, a day or everything  c) History keeps everything automatically; a bookmark keeps what you chose  d) No — it records visits automatically
**Answer: b) Clear it — the last hour, a day or everything**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why clear history on a shared school computer?**
a) History keeps everything automatically; a bookmark keeps what you chose  b) No — it records visits automatically  c) As courtesy and safety together  d) When you visited it and a word from its title
**Answer: c) As courtesy and safety together**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does history differ from a bookmark?**
a) No — it records visits automatically  b) When you visited it and a word from its title  c) The list the browser keeps of the pages you visited, in time order  d) History keeps everything automatically; a bookmark keeps what you chose
**Answer: d) History keeps everything automatically; a bookmark keeps what you chose**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Does the browser ask before recording a visit in history?**
a) No — it records visits automatically  b) When you visited it and a word from its title  c) The list the browser keeps of the pages you visited, in time order  d) By time — today, yesterday, last week
**Answer: a) No — it records visits automatically**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two things help you find a page in history?**
a) The list the browser keeps of the pages you visited, in time order  b) When you visited it and a word from its title  c) By time — today, yesterday, last week  d) By when you saw it, or by searching a word from its title
**Answer: b) When you visited it and a word from its title**
