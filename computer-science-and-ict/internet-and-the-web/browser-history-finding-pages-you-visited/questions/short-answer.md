# Browser History — Finding Pages You Visited — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is browser history?**
**Answer: The list the browser keeps of the pages you visited, in time order**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is history grouped?**
**Answer: By time — today, yesterday, last week**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a lost page found in history?**
**Answer: By when you saw it, or by searching a word from its title**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does history show for each visit?**
**Answer: The page's title and address**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why is history private information?**
**Answer: It shows what a person has been reading**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What can the user do with history?**
**Answer: Clear it — the last hour, a day or everything**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why clear history on a shared school computer?**
**Answer: As courtesy and safety together**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does history differ from a bookmark?**
**Answer: History keeps everything automatically; a bookmark keeps what you chose**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Does the browser ask before recording a visit in history?**
**Answer: No — it records visits automatically**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two things help you find a page in history?**
**Answer: When you visited it and a word from its title**
