# Browser History — Finding Pages You Visited — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is browser history?" is: The list the browser keeps of the pages you visited, in time order**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is history grouped?" is: It shows what a person has been reading**
**Answer: False** — By time — today, yesterday, last week

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is a lost page found in history?" is: By when you saw it, or by searching a word from its title**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does history show for each visit?" is: As courtesy and safety together**
**Answer: False** — The page's title and address

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why is history private information?" is: It shows what a person has been reading**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What can the user do with history?" is: No — it records visits automatically**
**Answer: False** — Clear it — the last hour, a day or everything

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why clear history on a shared school computer?" is: As courtesy and safety together**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How does history differ from a bookmark?" is: The list the browser keeps of the pages you visited, in time order**
**Answer: False** — History keeps everything automatically; a bookmark keeps what you chose

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Does the browser ask before recording a visit in history?" is: No — it records visits automatically**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What two things help you find a page in history?" is: By when you saw it, or by searching a word from its title**
**Answer: False** — When you visited it and a word from its title
