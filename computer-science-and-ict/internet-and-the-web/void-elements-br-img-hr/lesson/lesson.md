# Void elements — br, img, hr

## Do it first

Type a paragraph. Put a horizontal line above it and below it, and break its text in the middle so the second half starts on a new line. Three effects, and not one of them needed an end tag. This lesson names those three elements — the ones with nothing inside them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Elements with nothing inside

Most elements wrap content between a start tag and an end tag. A minority have **no content and no end tag** — they do their whole job in the one tag. They are called **void elements**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The three void elements at work

The three you meet here:

- The **line-break element** — starts a **new line**.
- The **image element** — **inserts an image**.
- The **horizontal-rule element** — draws a **horizontal line**.

The book's worked example sandwiches a paragraph between two horizontal lines and breaks its text mid-sentence, so you see both effects at once. Its practice builds a page of **three paragraphs separated by horizontal lines**. One guide point (teacher guide — evidence): line-break elements placed after images are what **stack the pictures vertically** on the page.
