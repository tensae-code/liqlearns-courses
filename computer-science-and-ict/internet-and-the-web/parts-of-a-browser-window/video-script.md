# Video script — Parts of a browser window

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A browser window has parts. Learn them.
Menu bar: the strip of menus.
Address bar: type the address. Click GO. Or press Enter.
Status bar: the bottom bar. Watch it while things download.

Open one. Point. Name them.
