# Parts of a browser window — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The address bar is also called the —
a) status bar  b) URL bar  c) menu bar  d) ruler
**Answer: b) URL bar** — address bar / URL bar — same bar.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** After typing an address, you —
a) close the browser  b) click GO or press Enter  c) type it again  d) wait an hour
**Answer: b) click GO or press Enter** — GO or Enter loads the page.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The status bar shows —
a) menus  b) messages such as download progress  c) your password  d) the address
**Answer: b) messages such as download progress** — bottom bar with progress messages.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which two elements does the teacher guide add?
a) Ribbon and ruler  b) Standard toolbar and workspace  c) Home page and web page  d) Zoom and Help
**Answer: b) Standard toolbar and workspace** — standard toolbar and workspace.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The menu bar is —
a) a horizontal strip of available menus  b) the bottom bar  c) where addresses are typed  d) a picture
**Answer: a) a horizontal strip of available menus** — the strip of menus.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Why might your browser look different from the book's picture?
a) The book is always wrong  b) Browser screens vary with the software installed  c) Browsers change daily  d) It cannot differ
**Answer: b) Browser screens vary with the software installed** — the guide's own warning.
