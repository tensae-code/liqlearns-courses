# Parts of a browser window — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Type a web address in the ______ bar.
**Answer: address**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The ______ bar shows download progress.
**Answer: status**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The ______ is the area where the page itself is shown.
**Answer: workspace**
