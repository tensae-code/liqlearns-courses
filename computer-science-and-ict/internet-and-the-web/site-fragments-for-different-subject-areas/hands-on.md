# Hands-on — Site Fragments for Different Subject Areas

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build a fragment
Choose a subject area and build a fragment of its site: at least two linked pages, with text and at least one prepared image fitted to the subject. Walk it as a visitor before finishing.

## Same skills, new subject
Take the fragment you built and sketch how it would change for a different subject area: structure, kinds of images, tables or none. Say which decisions stayed the same and which changed.
