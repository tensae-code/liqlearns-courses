# Video script — Site Fragments for Different Subject Areas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **fragment** is a working piece of a site: not the whole finished site, but a complete, linked part of one — a page or small group of pages that already does its job.
Building fragments is the practicum form of this course: design, page-making, prepared images and links are all exercised at once, on content that matters.
**Different subject areas** give different material, and the fragment must fit it: a literature fragment is mostly text and portrait, a geography fragment leans on maps and tables, a biology fragment on labelled photographs.
The maker's decisions repeat the course in miniature — who is this for, what structure serves it, which images must be prepared and how — but the answers change with the subject.
