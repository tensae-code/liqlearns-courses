# Site Fragments for Different Subject Areas — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a site fragment?**
**Answer: A working piece of a site — a complete, linked part that already does its job**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What skills does building a fragment exercise at once?**
**Answer: Design, page-making, prepared images and links**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a fragment judged?**
**Answer: Whole — it must open, read clearly, show its pictures, and link correctly**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Does a broken link fail a beautiful fragment?**
**Answer: Yes — the fragment is judged whole**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why do different subjects need different fragments?**
**Answer: Their material differs — text, maps, tables, or labelled photographs lead**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Give an example of a subject fit.**
**Answer: A geography fragment leans on maps and tables; a biology one on labelled photographs**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What decisions repeat in miniature?**
**Answer: Who it is for, what structure serves it, which images to prepare and how**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is this section's point?**
**Answer: The web skills are not tied to one example — they carry to new subjects**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Is a fragment the whole finished site?**
**Answer: No — a complete part of one**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does this section stand in the course?**
**Answer: At the end of the web part — the practicum that applies everything**
