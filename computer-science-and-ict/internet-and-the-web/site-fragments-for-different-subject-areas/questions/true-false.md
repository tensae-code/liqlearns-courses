# Site Fragments for Different Subject Areas — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a site fragment?" is: A working piece of a site — a complete, linked part that already does its job.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What skills does building a fragment exercise at once?" is: A geography fragment leans on maps and tables; a biology one on labelled photographs.**
**Answer: False** — Design, page-making, prepared images and links.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is a fragment judged?" is: Whole — it must open, read clearly, show its pictures, and link correctly.**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Does a broken link fail a beautiful fragment?" is: The web skills are not tied to one example — they carry to new subjects.**
**Answer: False** — Yes — the fragment is judged whole.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why do different subjects need different fragments?" is: Their material differs — text, maps, tables, or labelled photographs lead.**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Give an example of a subject fit." is: At the end of the web part — the practicum that applies everything.**
**Answer: False** — A geography fragment leans on maps and tables; a biology one on labelled photographs.

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What decisions repeat in miniature?" is: Who it is for, what structure serves it, which images to prepare and how.**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is this section's point?" is: Design, page-making, prepared images and links.**
**Answer: False** — The web skills are not tied to one example — they carry to new subjects.

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Is a fragment the whole finished site?" is: No — a complete part of one.**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does this section stand in the course?" is: Yes — the fragment is judged whole.**
**Answer: False** — At the end of the web part — the practicum that applies everything.
