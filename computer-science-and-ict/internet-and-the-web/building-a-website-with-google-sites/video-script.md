# Video script — Building a Website With Google Sites

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vietnam's Applied Grade 12 project builds a real website in **Google Sites**, and its first lesson is **preparation**: decide the site's **purpose and audience**, gather and write its **content** before touching the builder, and plan its pages. Then the build begins at the top: the **header** — the site's title, its banner image and its look — the part every page shares and every visitor sees first. Sites makes the mechanics easy on purpose, so the learner's work is the deciding: what this site is for, and what it says.
The build continues down the page: the **body**, where the content sections live — text, images and layout blocks arranged for reading — and the **footer**, carrying contact and closing information. A website also **collects**: the project adds **forms** (with Google Forms) so visitors can answer, register or ask. Last, the parts are joined: **links and the navigation bar** connect the pages into one site, and the finished site is checked as a visitor would meet it — every link followed, every page reachable.
