# Building a Website With Google Sites — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The site's purpose, its audience, its content, and its plan of pages**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The site's purpose, its audience, its content, and its plan of pages** — as the answer to: Which part is built first?
**Answer: False — The header — title, banner image and look, shared by every page**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: So the learner's work is the deciding — what the site is for and what it says**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: So the learner's work is the deciding — what the site is for and what it says** — as the answer to: What does every visitor see first?
**Answer: False — The header**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Google Sites**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Google Sites** — as the answer to: What lives in the body of the page?
**Answer: False — The content sections — text, images and layout blocks arranged for reading**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Contact and closing information**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Contact and closing information** — as the answer to: How does the site collect answers from visitors?
**Answer: False — With forms, added through Google Forms**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Links and the navigation bar**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Links and the navigation bar** — as the answer to: How is the finished site checked?
**Answer: False — As a visitor would meet it — every link followed, every page reachable**
