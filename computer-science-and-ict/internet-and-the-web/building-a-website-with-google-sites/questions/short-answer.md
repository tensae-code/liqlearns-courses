# Building a Website With Google Sites — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is prepared before building begins?**
**Answer: The site's purpose, its audience, its content, and its plan of pages**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which part is built first?**
**Answer: The header — title, banner image and look, shared by every page**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Google Sites make the mechanics easy?**
**Answer: So the learner's work is the deciding — what the site is for and what it says**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does every visitor see first?**
**Answer: The header**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In which tool is the site built?**
**Answer: Google Sites**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What lives in the body of the page?**
**Answer: The content sections — text, images and layout blocks arranged for reading**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the footer carry?**
**Answer: Contact and closing information**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does the site collect answers from visitors?**
**Answer: With forms, added through Google Forms**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What joins the pages into one site?**
**Answer: Links and the navigation bar**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How is the finished site checked?**
**Answer: As a visitor would meet it — every link followed, every page reachable**
