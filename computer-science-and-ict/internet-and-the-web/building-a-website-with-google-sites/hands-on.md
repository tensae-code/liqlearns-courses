# Hands-on — Building a Website With Google Sites

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Plan a real site** — Choose a purpose you care about — a class, a club, a family business. Write its audience, its page plan, and gather the content for its first page.

## Task two
**Build it** — In Google Sites, build the header, one body page and the footer; add a form; link the pages with a navigation bar. Then test it as a visitor.
