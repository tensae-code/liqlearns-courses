# Video script — Adding a video to a webpage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A page can hold a film.
The source attribute brings the file.
Width and height set its size.
One word — controls — with no value at all.
And the visitor can play, pause, and turn the sound.
