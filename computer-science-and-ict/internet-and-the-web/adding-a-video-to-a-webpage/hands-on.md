# Hands-on — Adding a video to a webpage

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. You need a computer with a text editor and a browser for the doing parts.

## The three experiments
Embed a video. (1) Halve its width and height. (2) Double them. (3) Remove controls and try to play it. Write what each experiment proved.

## Video beside image
Build a page holding one image and one video about the same subject, each brought in by its source attribute.
