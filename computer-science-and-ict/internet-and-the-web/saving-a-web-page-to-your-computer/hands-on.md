# Hands-on — Saving a Web Page to Your Computer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Save a page
Open a page your teacher chooses. Save it from the browser into your class folder, with a name that says what the page is.

## Open it offline
Close the browser. Find your saved page in its folder and open it. Check: are the words there? The pictures? Say what you would do if a picture were missing.
