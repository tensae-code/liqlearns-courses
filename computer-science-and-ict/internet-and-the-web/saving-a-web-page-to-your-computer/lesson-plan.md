# Lesson plan — Saving a Web Page to Your Computer

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Internet and the Web · **Lesson:** Saving a Web Page to Your Computer
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Morocco (2026-11-14) — in the title Internet and the Web on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Morocco's Grade 2 “J'excelle en informatique” teacher guide, because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson. This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only (Morocco), named in lesson/sources.md, counted nowhere.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** To save a web page is to keep a copy of the page — its words and pictures — as a file on your own computer. The saving is done from the browser, while the wanted page is open: the browser's save command keeps the page, and the learner gives the copy a name that says what the page is and puts it in the right folder — the file habits of the course apply to pages as to every file.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. A copy of the page as a file.
2. Save from the browser.
3. Name it for what it is.
4. Open it again to prove it.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — A web page lives on the internet — until you bring a copy home. Grade 2's séance teaches saving the whole page onto your own computer, so it is there when you need it, even with no connection. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of what saving a page keeps as the lesson describes it — the student studies the picture and names what each part shows.
