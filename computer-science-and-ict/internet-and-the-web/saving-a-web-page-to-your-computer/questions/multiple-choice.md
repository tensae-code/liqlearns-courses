# Saving a Web Page to Your Computer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does saving a web page keep?**
a) A copy of the page — its words and pictures — as a file  b) On your own computer, in your files  c) So it can be opened again, even with no connection  d) Borrowed — shown from far away, and gone when the browser closes
**Answer: a) A copy of the page — its words and pictures — as a file**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the saved copy kept?**
a) So it can be opened again, even with no connection  b) On your own computer, in your files  c) Borrowed — shown from far away, and gone when the browser closes  d) No — it keeps the page as it was on the day it was saved
**Answer: b) On your own computer, in your files**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why save a page at all?**
a) Borrowed — shown from far away, and gone when the browser closes  b) No — it keeps the page as it was on the day it was saved  c) So it can be opened again, even with no connection  d) From the browser, while the page is open
**Answer: c) So it can be opened again, even with no connection**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a page before it is saved?**
a) No — it keeps the page as it was on the day it was saved  b) From the browser, while the page is open  c) A name that says what the page is  d) Borrowed — shown from far away, and gone when the browser closes
**Answer: d) Borrowed — shown from far away, and gone when the browser closes**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Does a saved copy change when the real page changes?**
a) No — it keeps the page as it was on the day it was saved  b) From the browser, while the page is open  c) A name that says what the page is  d) In the right folder, like every file
**Answer: a) No — it keeps the page as it was on the day it was saved**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) From where is a page saved?**
a) A name that says what the page is  b) From the browser, while the page is open  c) In the right folder, like every file  d) Open the copy from the folder, without the internet
**Answer: b) From the browser, while the page is open**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What name should the saved copy get?**
a) In the right folder, like every file  b) Open the copy from the folder, without the internet  c) A name that says what the page is  d) When the copy cannot be found again
**Answer: c) A name that says what the page is**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where should the saved page be put?**
a) Open the copy from the folder, without the internet  b) When the copy cannot be found again  c) A copy of the page — its words and pictures — as a file  d) In the right folder, like every file
**Answer: d) In the right folder, like every file**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do you check the saving worked?**
a) Open the copy from the folder, without the internet  b) When the copy cannot be found again  c) A copy of the page — its words and pictures — as a file  d) On your own computer, in your files
**Answer: a) Open the copy from the folder, without the internet**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When was a page not saved properly?**
a) A copy of the page — its words and pictures — as a file  b) When the copy cannot be found again  c) On your own computer, in your files  d) So it can be opened again, even with no connection
**Answer: b) When the copy cannot be found again**
