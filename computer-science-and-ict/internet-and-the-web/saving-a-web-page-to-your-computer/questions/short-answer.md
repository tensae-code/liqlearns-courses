# Saving a Web Page to Your Computer — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does saving a web page keep?**
**Answer: A copy of the page — its words and pictures — as a file**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the saved copy kept?**
**Answer: On your own computer, in your files**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why save a page at all?**
**Answer: So it can be opened again, even with no connection**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a page before it is saved?**
**Answer: Borrowed — shown from far away, and gone when the browser closes**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Does a saved copy change when the real page changes?**
**Answer: No — it keeps the page as it was on the day it was saved**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) From where is a page saved?**
**Answer: From the browser, while the page is open**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What name should the saved copy get?**
**Answer: A name that says what the page is**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where should the saved page be put?**
**Answer: In the right folder, like every file**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do you check the saving worked?**
**Answer: Open the copy from the folder, without the internet**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When was a page not saved properly?**
**Answer: When the copy cannot be found again**
