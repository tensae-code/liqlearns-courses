# Creating a Blog — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Setting up — home, name, theme and the first settings
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A website of dated entries (posts) shown newest-first; The machinery — hosting and the software that runs the blog; A memorable name and address, a theme (its look), and its settings; Who may comment, and whether comments are moderated before they show; Grade 8, §5 — inside the block on making things online.

## Sub lesson 2 — Posting and keeping it alive — writing, tags, rhythm
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A clear title, a first line that earns the next, tags, and pictures where they help; They label posts so topics can be grouped and found; A rhythm — regular posts on a promise the writer can keep; Answer comments, fix errors openly, and never publish another's work as your own; The three kept promises — a blog lives by its rhythm.
