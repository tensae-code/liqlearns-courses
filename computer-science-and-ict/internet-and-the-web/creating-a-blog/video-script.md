# Video script — Creating a Blog

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **blog** is a website of dated entries (**posts**) shown newest-first. Creating one starts with choices: a **platform** (a hosted service that runs the machinery), a **name and address** a reader can remember, a **theme** (its look), and its **settings** — who may read, who may comment, and whether comments are moderated before they show. Armenia's Grade 8 book teaches 'Creating a blog' as a practical lesson (§5) inside its online-making block, between spreadsheets and online content.
A blog lives by its posts: each post gets a clear **title**, a first line that earns the next, **tags** (labels that group posts by topic), and pictures where they help. Keeping a blog alive is a **rhythm** — regular posts on a promise the writer can keep — plus care of readers: answer comments, fix errors openly, and never publish another person's work as your own. A blog with three kept promises beats a blog with thirty posts and silence.
