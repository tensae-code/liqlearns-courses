# Creating a Blog — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A website of dated entries (posts) shown newest-first**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A website of dated entries (posts) shown newest-first** — as the answer to: What does the platform provide?
**Answer: False — The machinery — hosting and the software that runs the blog**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A memorable name and address, a theme (its look), and its settings**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A memorable name and address, a theme (its look), and its settings** — as the answer to: What must comment settings decide?
**Answer: False — Who may comment, and whether comments are moderated before they show**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Grade 8, §5 — inside the block on making things online**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Grade 8, §5 — inside the block on making things online** — as the answer to: What four things does each post need?
**Answer: False — A clear title, a first line that earns the next, tags, and pictures where they help**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: They label posts so topics can be grouped and found**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: They label posts so topics can be grouped and found** — as the answer to: What keeps a blog alive?
**Answer: False — A rhythm — regular posts on a promise the writer can keep**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Answer comments, fix errors openly, and never publish another's work as your own**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Answer comments, fix errors openly, and never publish another's work as your own** — as the answer to: A blog with three kept promises or thirty posts then silence — which wins?
**Answer: False — The three kept promises — a blog lives by its rhythm**
