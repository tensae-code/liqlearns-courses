# Creating a Blog — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a blog?**
**Answer: A website of dated entries (posts) shown newest-first**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the platform provide?**
**Answer: The machinery — hosting and the software that runs the blog**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three start-up choices after the platform.**
**Answer: A memorable name and address, a theme (its look), and its settings**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must comment settings decide?**
**Answer: Who may comment, and whether comments are moderated before they show**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Armenia teach creating a blog?**
**Answer: Grade 8, §5 — inside the block on making things online**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What four things does each post need?**
**Answer: A clear title, a first line that earns the next, tags, and pictures where they help**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do tags do?**
**Answer: They label posts so topics can be grouped and found**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What keeps a blog alive?**
**Answer: A rhythm — regular posts on a promise the writer can keep**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How are readers cared for?**
**Answer: Answer comments, fix errors openly, and never publish another's work as your own**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) A blog with three kept promises or thirty posts then silence — which wins?**
**Answer: The three kept promises — a blog lives by its rhythm**
