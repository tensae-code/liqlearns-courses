# Blogs and Wikis — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a blog?**
**Answer: A website of dated entries by one author, newest first, with readers' comments**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which service does Fiji name for blogs?**
**Answer: Blogger**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does publishing a blog entry need?**
**Answer: An account and the entry — no printing house and no permission**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What leads in a blog?**
**Answer: The author's voice**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What gathers under a blog entry?**
**Answer: Readers' comments**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a wiki?**
**Answer: A website whose pages visitors may edit — written by its readers together**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is Fiji's great wiki example?**
**Answer: Wikipedia**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is MediaWiki?**
**Answer: The program Wikipedia runs on, free for any group to run its own wiki**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the blog–wiki contrast?**
**Answer: A blog gathers comments around one voice; a wiki merges many hands into one text**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why do wiki pages carry histories?**
**Answer: So readers can see who changed what — and check the page as travellers check a sign others wrote**
