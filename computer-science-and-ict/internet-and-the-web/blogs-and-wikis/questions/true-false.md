# Blogs and Wikis — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a blog?" is: A website of dated entries by one author, newest first, with readers' comments**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which service does Fiji name for blogs?" is: An account and the entry — no printing house and no permission**
**Answer: False** — Blogger

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does publishing a blog entry need?" is: An account and the entry — no printing house and no permission**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What leads in a blog?" is: Readers' comments**
**Answer: False** — The author's voice

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What gathers under a blog entry?" is: Readers' comments**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is a wiki?" is: Wikipedia**
**Answer: False** — A website whose pages visitors may edit — written by its readers together

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is Fiji's great wiki example?" is: Wikipedia**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is MediaWiki?" is: A blog gathers comments around one voice; a wiki merges many hands into one text**
**Answer: False** — The program Wikipedia runs on, free for any group to run its own wiki

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the blog–wiki contrast?" is: A blog gathers comments around one voice; a wiki merges many hands into one text**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why do wiki pages carry histories?" is: A website of dated entries by one author, newest first, with readers' comments**
**Answer: False** — So readers can see who changed what — and check the page as travellers check a sign others wrote
