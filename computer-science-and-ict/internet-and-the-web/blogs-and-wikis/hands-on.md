# Hands-on — Blogs and Wikis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Draft an entry', 'Write a blog entry for a school club on paper — title, date, entry, and the comment you hope for.')

## Task two
('Edit together', 'In a group, draft one wiki-style page about your school, pass it twice for edits, and keep the history of changes.')
