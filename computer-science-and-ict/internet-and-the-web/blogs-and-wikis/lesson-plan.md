## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Blogs — one voice, dated, answered** — A **blog** is a website of **dated entries by one author** (or a small team), newest first, where readers answer in **comments**. Fiji names **Blogger** as the standing service: open an account, write an entry, publish — no printing house, no permission. The blog's bargain is its shape: the author's voice leads, the entries pile into an archive, and anyone may read — so a blog is publishing with the gate removed, for a school club, a teacher, or a pupil with something to say.
2. **Wikis — pages everyone may edit** — A **wiki** is a website whose pages **visitors may edit** — not only read — so the text is **written by its readers together**. **Wikipedia** is the great example: an encyclopedia with no office of authors, corrected and grown by its users; **MediaWiki** is the program it runs on, free for any group to run its own wiki. Fiji's contrast is the lesson: a blog gathers *comments around* one voice; a wiki merges *many hands into* one text — which is why a wiki's pages carry histories, and why its readers check them the way travellers check a sign other travellers wrote.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Two web forms changed who gets to publish: the blog, where one voice speaks to the world, and the wiki, where the world writes together. Fiji's Year 12 web-technologies section names them together — Blogger, Wikipedia, MediaWiki. This lesson takes both. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of blogs — one voice, dated, answered as the lesson describes it — the student studies the picture and names what each part shows.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of wikis — pages everyone may edit in action — the student watches once, then says what changed and why.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key names and definitions aloud, in the order the lesson gives them — the student listens once through, then reads the same lines aloud along with the voice. (Audio is a described slot in this build; no audio file is made.)

   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-false, matching, short-answer — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
