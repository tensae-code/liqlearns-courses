# Hands-on — Designing an Application Framework (Wireframe)

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Draw the framework', 'For a school club site with Home, Activities, Gallery and Join pages, draw the four wireframes in one skeleton style and mark the shared furniture. Then draw every link as a join and check for orphans.')

## Task two
('Review a framework', 'A framework is given with one page promised in the brief but missing, one link landing nowhere, and one orphan page. Find all three faults and state the repairs on the drawing.')
