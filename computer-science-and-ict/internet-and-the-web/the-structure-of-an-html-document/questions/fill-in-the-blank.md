# The structure of an HTML document — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or code.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The ___ element wraps the whole page.**
**Answer: root** — root = the wrapping element.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Everything the browser displays sits in the ___ section.**
**Answer: body** — body shows; head tells about.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Only what sits inside ___ and body changes from page to page.**
**Answer: head** — head and body carry the changing contents.
