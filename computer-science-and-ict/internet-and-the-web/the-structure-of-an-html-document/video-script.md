# Video script — The structure of an HTML document

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every page has the same bones.
A declaration at the top.
One root around everything.
A head that talks about the page.
A body that shows the page.
Learn the bones once. Use them always.
