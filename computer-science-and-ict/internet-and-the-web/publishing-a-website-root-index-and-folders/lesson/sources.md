**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Ethiopia).**

The ethiopia.gov.et working is teacher evidence (guide, printed pp. 93–94): its address in full and short forms, root directory, sub-directories, media/image directory and home page file are each identified there, and pupils are then sent to other federal ministry sites (Health and Foreign Affairs named) and to find two web-hosting companies serving Ethiopia with their features. The guide files these answers as Activity 4.9 under its §4.3 while its exercises refer to Activities 4.11–4.14 — the numbering drift noted in the placements — so the evidence is filed by topic, not by the drifted numbers. The guide titles this unit "Web Development" where the student book says "Web Authoring"; the student book's name is used. No statement about the Web's invention date is made in this lesson, so the Grade 8 (1991) and Grade 11 (1989) books' differing statements, kept in their own lessons' provenance, are not merged here.
