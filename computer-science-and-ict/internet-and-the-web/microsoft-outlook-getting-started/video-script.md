# Video script — Microsoft Outlook — Getting Started

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Microsoft Outlook is Microsoft's program for electronic mail and personal organisation: one window holds mail, contacts, a calendar of events and meetings, and task lists The learner opens Outlook, finds its parts — mail, contacts, calendar, tasks — and learns what each part is for before using any of them Because Outlook gathers the whole working day — messages, people, appointments and jobs — into one program, and the parts only help once the learner knows which is which.
