# Accessing a website — address, keyword and ISP — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Type the site's ______ in the address bar and follow links.
**Answer: address**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ISP stands for Internet ______ Provider.
**Answer: Service**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Search by ______ when you know the topic but not the address.
**Answer: keyword**
