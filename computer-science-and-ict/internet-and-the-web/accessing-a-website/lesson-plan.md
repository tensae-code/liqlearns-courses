# Lesson plan — Accessing a website — address, keyword and ISP

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Internet and the Web · **Lesson:** Accessing a website — address, keyword and ISP
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 8 — Internet and the Web in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 7 book, Unit 4 — Internet. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a website is reached by typing its address and following links, or by searching a keyword — and connecting at all needs an Internet Service Provider; Ethiopia's example is Ethio-telecom.
**This lesson's own level:** it is a doing-lesson that gathers the whole Internet title: browser, address parts, and searching all come together in it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Two routes to a page** — by address — type it, follow links (the Ministry of Education worked example); by keyword — 'the Victory of Adwa', 'preventive mechanisms of Covid-19'
2. **The ISP — and judging research (teacher guide — evidence)** — connecting needs an Internet Service Provider (Ethio-telecom); the Abebe Bikila research task is judged on finding and reporting, with official sources named

3. **Searching the internet — keywords and results (Grade 8)** — browsing as searching, with Google and Yahoo; the five steps in order — engine, keywords, Enter, results, click or return (Grade 8 growth, Wave 3 band B2)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The parts of a web address, and Browsers and search engines.  Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Reach a site by typing its address and following links.
2. Reach information by keyword search.
3. Name what an ISP is and Ethiopia's example, Ethio-telecom.
4. Do the Bikila research properly: half a page or more, keywords listed, official sources preferred.
5. Search by the Grade 8 five steps, in order, and return to the results page for more options when the first click disappoints.

## What you need
A computer connected to the internet; paper for the research report.
