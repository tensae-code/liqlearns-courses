# Hands-on — Accessing a website — address, keyword and ISP

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Two-route run
Find the same fact twice: once starting from a known address and following links, once from a keyword search. Write down both journeys, step by step, and say which was faster and why.

## Bikila research
Do the book's research task as the guide judges it: find information about Abebe Bikila, write half a page or more, list your keywords at the end, and take your facts from official sources where you can.
