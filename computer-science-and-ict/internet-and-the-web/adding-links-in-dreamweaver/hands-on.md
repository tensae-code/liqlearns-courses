# Hands-on — Adding Links in Dreamweaver

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Do the book's steps** — Work through Adding Links in Dreamweaver exactly as South Sudan's book orders it, on a real computer if one is available; otherwise write the steps in order and check them against the book's order.

## Task two
