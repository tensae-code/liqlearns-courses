# Website Builders and Design Tools — Canva and Figma — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a website builder let you do?**
a) Build pages with ready tools and templates  b) Hand-written code  c) Its starting layout and design  d) Graphics and designs
**Answer: a) Build pages with ready tools and templates**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Building with a builder needs less of what?**
a) Its starting layout and design  b) Hand-written code  c) Graphics and designs  d) Designing interfaces and prototypes
**Answer: b) Hand-written code**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a template give a page?**
a) Graphics and designs  b) Designing interfaces and prototypes  c) Its starting layout and design  d) How the site will look and work before building
**Answer: c) Its starting layout and design**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Canva is used for what?**
a) Designing interfaces and prototypes  b) How the site will look and work before building  c) By the job they must do  d) Graphics and designs
**Answer: d) Graphics and designs**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Figma is used for what?**
a) Designing interfaces and prototypes  b) How the site will look and work before building  c) By the job they must do  d) Yes — builders include publishing
**Answer: a) Designing interfaces and prototypes**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a prototype show?**
a) By the job they must do  b) How the site will look and work before building  c) Yes — builders include publishing  d) No — planning comes first
**Answer: b) How the site will look and work before building**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How are design tools chosen?**
a) Yes — builders include publishing  b) No — planning comes first  c) By the job they must do  d) The building of the real page
**Answer: c) By the job they must do**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Can a builder site be published online?**
a) No — planning comes first  b) The building of the real page  c) Build pages with ready tools and templates  d) Yes — builders include publishing
**Answer: d) Yes — builders include publishing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Do design tools replace planning?**
a) No — planning comes first  b) The building of the real page  c) Build pages with ready tools and templates  d) Hand-written code
**Answer: a) No — planning comes first**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) A design made in Figma can guide what?**
a) Build pages with ready tools and templates  b) The building of the real page  c) Hand-written code  d) Its starting layout and design
**Answer: b) The building of the real page**
