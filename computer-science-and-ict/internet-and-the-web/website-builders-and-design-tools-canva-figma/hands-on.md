# Hands-on — Website Builders and Design Tools — Canva and Figma

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tool for the job
List four jobs: a poster, a page layout to show a client, a live club page, a logo sketch. Match each to builder, Canva or Figma, and defend the match.

## Prototype on paper
Prototype one page of a site in boxes on paper: where the heading, picture, menu and button go. Label which tool you would build each part in.
