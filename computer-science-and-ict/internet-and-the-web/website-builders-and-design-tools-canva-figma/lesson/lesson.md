# Website Builders and Design Tools — Canva and Figma

## Do it first

Not every page in Papua New Guinea's Grade 11 design unit is hand-coded. The guide also teaches the tools professionals reach for: builders that assemble pages, Canva for graphics, Figma for designing the page before it exists. This lesson separates their jobs.

## Sub lesson 1 — Website builders

A **website builder** lets you **build pages with ready tools and templates**, so a page needs **less hand-written code**. A template gives the page its **starting layout and design**, and builders include publishing — a builder site **can be published online** from the tool itself.

[Picture — Website builders]

## Sub lesson 2 — Canva and Figma as design tools

**Canva** is used for **graphics and designs**; **Figma** is used for **designing interfaces and prototypes**. A prototype shows **how the site will look and work before it is built**, and a design made in Figma can guide the building of the real page. The tools are chosen by the job they must do — and they do not replace planning, which still comes first.
