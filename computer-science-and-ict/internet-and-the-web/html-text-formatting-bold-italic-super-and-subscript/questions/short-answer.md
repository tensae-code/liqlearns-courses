# HTML Text Formatting — Bold, Italic, Superscript and Subscript — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which element makes text bold?**
**Answer: <b>**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is italic used for?**
**Answer: Titles and foreign words (emphasis that leans)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why should underline be used sparingly?**
**Answer: Readers now read underlines as links**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do <big> and <small> change?**
**Answer: Size, relative to neighbouring text**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What shape is every formatting element?**
**Answer: A container — opening tag, text, closing tag**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which element raises text?**
**Answer: <sup> — superscript**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Write the 2 of H2O correctly.**
**Answer: H<sub>2</sub>O**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two homes of superscript.**
**Answer: Formulas and ordinals like 10th (also footnotes)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What writing would be crippled without sub and sup?**
**Answer: Science writing — formulas and chemical names**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the family's rule of use?**
**Answer: Formatting serves meaning, never decoration everywhere**
