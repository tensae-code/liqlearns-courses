# Hands-on — HTML Text Formatting — Bold, Italic, Superscript and Subscript

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Format a fact sheet** — Write a one-page science fact sheet using all seven elements where they truly belong — a formula, a chemical name, a title, an aside.

## Task two
**Strip and restore** — Take a formatted page and remove all formatting tags. Read it flat, then restore only the formatting the meaning needs.
