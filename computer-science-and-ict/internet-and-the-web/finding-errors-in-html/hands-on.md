# Hands-on — Finding Errors in HTML

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Hunt three faults', 'Your teacher gives you a page with three planted faults — one unclosed tag, one wrong nesting, one missing quote. Find and fix each, re-opening the page after every fix.')

## Task two
('Write the checklist', 'Write the four-fault checklist on a card in your own words. Use it to check a partner's page and report which faults, if any, you found and where.')
