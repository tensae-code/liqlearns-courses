# Finding Errors in HTML — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the first common fault.**
**Answer: An unclosed tag — its effect runs to the page's end**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is wrong nesting?**
**Answer: Tags closed in the wrong order — </b> before </i> when <i> opened last**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a misspelled tag name do?**
**Answer: Nothing visible — the browser silently ignores it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What breaks an attribute?**
**Answer: Missing quotes around its value**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Do browsers usually report HTML errors?**
**Answer: No — they guess and carry on; the page is the evidence**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is finding a fault, in Malaysia's method?**
**Answer: A comparison — what the page shows against the code**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where is the fault usually found?**
**Answer: At or just before the place where the wrongness starts**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) A heading swallows the whole page. Which fault do you suspect?**
**Answer: Its unclosed tag**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How many faults should you fix at once?**
**Answer: One — then re-open the page, so each fix's effect is seen alone**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does fixing in batches hide?**
**Answer: Which fix actually worked**
