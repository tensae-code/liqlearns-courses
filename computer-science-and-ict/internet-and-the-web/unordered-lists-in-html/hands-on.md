# Hands-on — Unordered lists in HTML

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. You need a computer with a text editor and a browser for the doing parts.

## Climate actions, three bullets
Build the book's climate-change actions list three times: disc, square, circle. Say which you would choose for a school poster, and why.

## Fruits and vegetables
Following the guide's pairing: fruits letter-numbered on one side, vegetables square-bulleted on the other — two list kinds on one page.
