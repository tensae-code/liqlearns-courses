# Instant Messaging and Online Chat — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is instant messaging?" is: Written messages that arrive instantly, between people online at the same time**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is online chat?" is: Presence — the program shows who is available**
**Answer: False** — The same live talk in a shared room, often among several people

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do both rest on?" is: Presence — the program shows who is available**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How fast do lines appear?" is: Writing became a form of being together**
**Answer: False** — As they are typed — question, answer, joke, in seconds

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What did messaging change about writing to someone?" is: Writing became a form of being together**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does chat's speed make silence do?" is: It is informal — short lines and abbreviations, closer to speech**
**Answer: False** — Silence itself sends a message

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How does chat writing differ from letter writing?" is: It is informal — short lines and abbreviations, closer to speech**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Is live talk gone once sent?" is: Write every line as something that may outlive the moment**
**Answer: False** — No — the words are written and can be saved, copied and shown later

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is Fiji's user rule for live talk?" is: Write every line as something that may outlive the moment**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does Fiji place messaging in the book?" is: Written messages that arrive instantly, between people online at the same time**
**Answer: False** — In the communication chapter, beside email
