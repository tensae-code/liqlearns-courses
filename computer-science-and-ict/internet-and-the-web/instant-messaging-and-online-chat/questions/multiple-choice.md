# Instant Messaging and Online Chat — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is instant messaging?**
a) Written messages that arrive instantly, between people online at the same time  b) The same live talk in a shared room, often among several people  c) Presence — the program shows who is available  d) As they are typed — question, answer, joke, in seconds
**Answer: a) Written messages that arrive instantly, between people online at the same time**

a) Presence — the program shows who is available  b) The same live talk in a shared room, often among several people  c) As they are typed — question, answer, joke, in seconds  d) Writing became a form of being together
