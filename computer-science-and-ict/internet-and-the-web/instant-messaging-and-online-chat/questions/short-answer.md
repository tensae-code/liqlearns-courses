# Instant Messaging and Online Chat — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is instant messaging?**
**Answer: Written messages that arrive instantly, between people online at the same time**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is online chat?**
**Answer: The same live talk in a shared room, often among several people**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do both rest on?**
**Answer: Presence — the program shows who is available**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How fast do lines appear?**
**Answer: As they are typed — question, answer, joke, in seconds**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What did messaging change about writing to someone?**
**Answer: Writing became a form of being together**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does chat's speed make silence do?**
**Answer: Silence itself sends a message**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does chat writing differ from letter writing?**
**Answer: It is informal — short lines and abbreviations, closer to speech**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Is live talk gone once sent?**
**Answer: No — the words are written and can be saved, copied and shown later**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is Fiji's user rule for live talk?**
**Answer: Write every line as something that may outlive the moment**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Fiji place messaging in the book?**
**Answer: In the communication chapter, beside email**
