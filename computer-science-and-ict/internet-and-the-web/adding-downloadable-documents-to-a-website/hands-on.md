# Hands-on — Adding Downloadable Documents to a Website

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Do the book's steps** — Work through Add Downloadable Documents exactly as South Sudan's book orders it, on a real computer if one is available; otherwise write the steps in order and check them against the book's order.

## Task two
**Show it to someone** — Take Step 2: Place document within local site files and show a partner what it does: do it once slowly, naming each step, then let the partner do it while you only watch and correct.
