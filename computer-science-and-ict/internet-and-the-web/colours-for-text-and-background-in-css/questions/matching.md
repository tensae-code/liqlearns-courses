# Colours for Text and Background in CSS — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which property colours text?** → **color**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are three ways to name a colour in CSS?** → **By a colour name, by a hexadecimal value, or by rgb() values**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does rgb(255, 0, 0) give?** → **Pure red — full red light, no green, no blue**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do the three rgb numbers range over?** → **0 to 255 each — the amounts of red, green and blue light**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first way colour goes wrong?** → **Text that stops being readable against its background**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which property colours the ground behind an element?** → **background-color**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the whole page's background set?** → **Through the body element's background-color**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must the text/background pair keep?** → **Contrast high enough to read comfortably, for every visitor**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How many colours should a page use?** → **Few — used consistently, each earning its place**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What loses, when decoration fights reading?** → **The decoration — readability wins**
