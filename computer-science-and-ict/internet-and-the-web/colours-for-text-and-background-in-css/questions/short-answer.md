# Colours for Text and Background in CSS — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which property colours text?**
**Answer: color**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are three ways to name a colour in CSS?**
**Answer: By a colour name, by a hexadecimal value, or by rgb() values**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does rgb(255, 0, 0) give?**
**Answer: Pure red — full red light, no green, no blue**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do the three rgb numbers range over?**
**Answer: 0 to 255 each — the amounts of red, green and blue light**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first way colour goes wrong?**
**Answer: Text that stops being readable against its background**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which property colours the ground behind an element?**
**Answer: background-color**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the whole page's background set?**
**Answer: Through the body element's background-color**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must the text/background pair keep?**
**Answer: Contrast high enough to read comfortably, for every visitor**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How many colours should a page use?**
**Answer: Few — used consistently, each earning its place**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What loses, when decoration fights reading?**
**Answer: The decoration — readability wins**
