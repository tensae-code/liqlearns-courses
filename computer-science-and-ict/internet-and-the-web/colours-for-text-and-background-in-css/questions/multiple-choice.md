# Colours for Text and Background in CSS — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which property colours text?**
a) color  b) By a colour name, by a hexadecimal value, or by rgb() values  c) Pure red — full red light, no green, no blue  d) 0 to 255 each — the amounts of red, green and blue light
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are three ways to name a colour in CSS?**
a) By a colour name, by a hexadecimal value, or by rgb() values  b) Pure red — full red light, no green, no blue  c) 0 to 255 each — the amounts of red, green and blue light  d) Text that stops being readable against its background
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does rgb(255, 0, 0) give?**
a) Pure red — full red light, no green, no blue  b) 0 to 255 each — the amounts of red, green and blue light  c) Text that stops being readable against its background  d) background-color
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do the three rgb numbers range over?**
a) 0 to 255 each — the amounts of red, green and blue light  b) Text that stops being readable against its background  c) background-color  d) Through the body element's background-color
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first way colour goes wrong?**
a) Text that stops being readable against its background  b) background-color  c) Through the body element's background-color  d) Contrast high enough to read comfortably, for every visitor
**Answer: a**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the whole page's background set?**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must the text/background pair keep?**
a) Contrast high enough to read comfortably, for every visitor  b) Few — used consistently, each earning its place  c) The decoration — readability wins  d) color

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How many colours should a page use?**
a) Few — used consistently, each earning its place  b) The decoration — readability wins  c) color  d) By a colour name, by a hexadecimal value, or by rgb() values

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What loses, when decoration fights reading?**
