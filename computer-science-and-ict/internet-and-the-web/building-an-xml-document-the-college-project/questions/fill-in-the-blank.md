# Building an XML document — the college project — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or code.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The file is saved under your own name with an ___ extension.
**Answer: .xml** — .xml is the project's save form.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A course carries its name, its code, its ___ hour, and its teacher.
**Answer: credit** — credit hour is the third course piece.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The three first-stage branches are empty ___ elements.
**Answer: Department** — Department sub-elements, three of them.
