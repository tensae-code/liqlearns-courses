# Hands-on — Building an XML document — the college project

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The staged build, on a real machine
Do the full project on a computer, saving a copy after every stage (stage1.xml, stage2.xml, …). If a later stage breaks, your saved stages show exactly where.

## Tree check
At the end, view the document in XML Notepad. Expand department by department — as the GIF grows it — and tick off each department's courses, teachers and students against your plan.
