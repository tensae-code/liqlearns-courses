# Video script — Search Strategy — Planning a Good Search

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **search strategy** starts away from the screen: state the **question** exactly — what do I need to know? — then choose **keywords**: the few words a page answering the question would surely contain. Too-wide words drown the searcher in useless pages; exact phrases and added words narrow it. The planner also decides where to search — a general search engine, or a known trusted site directly.
The results are **worked**, not just read: open the promising ones, judge each by its source and date, and **refine** — change the keywords and search again — when the first words missed. What is used is **recorded** for citation: the searcher who planned can say where every fact came from. Strategy is what turns Bhutan's later citation lesson from a rule into a habit.
