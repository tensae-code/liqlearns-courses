# Search Strategy — Planning a Good Search — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a search strategy?**
a) Planning a search before searching — the question, the keywords and how results will be judged  b) The exact question — what do I need to know  c) The few words a page answering the question would surely contain  d) A flood of useless pages
**Answer: a) Planning a search before searching — the question, the keywords and how results will be judged**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is stated first in a planned search?**
a) The few words a page answering the question would surely contain  b) The exact question — what do I need to know  c) A flood of useless pages  d) With exact phrases and added, more specific words
**Answer: b) The exact question — what do I need to know**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are keywords?**
a) A flood of useless pages  b) With exact phrases and added, more specific words  c) The few words a page answering the question would surely contain  d) Directly on a known trusted site
**Answer: c) The few words a page answering the question would surely contain**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do too-wide keywords cause?**
a) With exact phrases and added, more specific words  b) Directly on a known trusted site  c) Opened, judged by source and date, and the search refined if they miss  d) A flood of useless pages
**Answer: d) A flood of useless pages**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a search narrowed?**
a) With exact phrases and added, more specific words  b) Directly on a known trusted site  c) Opened, judged by source and date, and the search refined if they miss  d) Changing the keywords and searching again
**Answer: a) With exact phrases and added, more specific words**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where might a planner search besides a search engine?**
a) Opened, judged by source and date, and the search refined if they miss  b) Directly on a known trusted site  c) Changing the keywords and searching again  d) Where each fact came from, for citation
**Answer: b) Directly on a known trusted site**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How are results worked?**
a) Changing the keywords and searching again  b) Where each fact came from, for citation  c) Opened, judged by source and date, and the search refined if they miss  d) A habit, not only a rule
**Answer: c) Opened, judged by source and date, and the search refined if they miss**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does refine mean in searching?**
a) Where each fact came from, for citation  b) A habit, not only a rule  c) Planning a search before searching — the question, the keywords and how results will be judged  d) Changing the keywords and searching again
**Answer: d) Changing the keywords and searching again**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is recorded as the search goes on?**
a) Where each fact came from, for citation  b) A habit, not only a rule  c) Planning a search before searching — the question, the keywords and how results will be judged  d) The exact question — what do I need to know
**Answer: a) Where each fact came from, for citation**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does strategy turn citation into?**
a) Planning a search before searching — the question, the keywords and how results will be judged  b) A habit, not only a rule  c) The exact question — what do I need to know  d) The few words a page answering the question would surely contain
**Answer: b) A habit, not only a rule**
