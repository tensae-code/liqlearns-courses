# Search Strategy — Planning a Good Search — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a search strategy?**
**Answer: Planning a search before searching — the question, the keywords and how results will be judged**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is stated first in a planned search?**
**Answer: The exact question — what do I need to know**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are keywords?**
**Answer: The few words a page answering the question would surely contain**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do too-wide keywords cause?**
**Answer: A flood of useless pages**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a search narrowed?**
**Answer: With exact phrases and added, more specific words**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where might a planner search besides a search engine?**
**Answer: Directly on a known trusted site**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How are results worked?**
**Answer: Opened, judged by source and date, and the search refined if they miss**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does refine mean in searching?**
**Answer: Changing the keywords and searching again**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is recorded as the search goes on?**
**Answer: Where each fact came from, for citation**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does strategy turn citation into?**
**Answer: A habit, not only a rule**
