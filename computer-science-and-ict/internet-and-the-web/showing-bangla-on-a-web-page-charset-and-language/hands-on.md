# Hands-on — Showing Bangla on a Web Page — Charset and Language

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Fix the boxes** — Take a Bangla HTML page saved without declarations. Open it, see the failure, add charset and lang, and open it again.

## Task two
**Passport check** — Visit three Bangladeshi websites and view their source. Record each one's charset and lang declarations — or their absence.
