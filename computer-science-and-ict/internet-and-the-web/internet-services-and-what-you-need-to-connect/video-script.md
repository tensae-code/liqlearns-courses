# Video script — Internet Services — and What You Need to Connect

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The **internet** earns its place through its **services** — the things it does for its users: the **World Wide Web** of pages, **electronic mail**, searching for information, talking and sending files across the world, and the e-services of governments and banks. Its advantages follow from the joining itself: information from everywhere, communication that is fast and cheap, and services reached from home. Sudan's Grade 7 book opens its internet unit with this lesson — what the internet gives, before how it works.
Joining the internet takes a kit of things together: a **device** — a computer or phone; a **connection** to carry the signals — a line or wireless link with its modem or adaptor; an **account with a service provider** that lets your device onto the network; and a **browser** to use the web once you arrive. Miss one and the rest cannot help you: the checklist is the lesson, and it is also the first thing to check, in order, when a connection fails.
