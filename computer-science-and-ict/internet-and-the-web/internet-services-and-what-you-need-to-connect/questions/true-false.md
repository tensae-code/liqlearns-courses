# Internet Services — and What You Need to Connect — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name four internet services." is: The World Wide Web, electronic mail, searching for information, and talking and sending files across the world**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What advantages follow from the joining itself?" is: A device, a connection with its modem or adaptor, an account with a service provider, and a browser**
**Answer: False** — Information from everywhere, fast cheap communication, and services reached from home

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why does Sudan's book teach services before workings?" is: To show what the internet gives before how it works**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the four things needed to connect." is: The connection — a line or wireless link**
**Answer: False** — A device, a connection with its modem or adaptor, an account with a service provider, and a browser

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the service provider give you?" is: An account that lets your device onto the network**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What carries the signals?" is: The rest cannot help you — there is no connection**
**Answer: False** — The connection — a line or wireless link

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the browser for?" is: To use the web once you arrive**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens if one of the four is missing?" is: No — a computer or a phone**
**Answer: False** — The rest cannot help you — there is no connection

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the checklist's second job?" is: It is the first thing to check, in order, when a connection fails**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which device can join — only a computer?" is: Information from everywhere, fast cheap communication, and services reached from home**
**Answer: False** — No — a computer or a phone
