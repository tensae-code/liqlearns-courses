# Internet Services — and What You Need to Connect — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name four internet services.**
a) The World Wide Web, electronic mail, searching for information, and talking and sending files across the world  b) Information from everywhere, fast cheap communication, and services reached from home  c) A device, a connection with its modem or adaptor, an account with a service provider, and a browser  d) The connection — a line or wireless link
