# Internet Services — and What You Need to Connect — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name four internet services.**
**Answer: The World Wide Web, electronic mail, searching for information, and talking and sending files across the world**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What advantages follow from the joining itself?**
**Answer: Information from everywhere, fast cheap communication, and services reached from home**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Sudan's book teach services before workings?**
**Answer: To show what the internet gives before how it works**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four things needed to connect.**
**Answer: A device, a connection with its modem or adaptor, an account with a service provider, and a browser**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the service provider give you?**
**Answer: An account that lets your device onto the network**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What carries the signals?**
**Answer: The connection — a line or wireless link**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the browser for?**
**Answer: To use the web once you arrive**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens if one of the four is missing?**
**Answer: The rest cannot help you — there is no connection**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the checklist's second job?**
**Answer: It is the first thing to check, in order, when a connection fails**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which device can join — only a computer?**
**Answer: No — a computer or a phone**
