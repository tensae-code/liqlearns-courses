# Video script — XML — marking up what data is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

XML. The letters mean eXtensible Markup Language.
HTML makes a page LOOK right.
XML says what the data IS.
In XML, you invent the tags you need.
Title. Author. Region. City. Your tags, your structure.
XML does not change how a page looks.
It changes how a document is read, filed and stored.
