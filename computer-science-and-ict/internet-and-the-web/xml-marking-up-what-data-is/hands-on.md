# Hands-on — XML — marking up what data is

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Two versions of one fact
On paper, write one fact about your school twice: once made to look nice, once held in labelled tags you invent. Show a partner both and ask which one a computer could sort.

## Tag your area
Draw your area as nested boxes: the country, your region, your town. Turn the boxes into opening and closing tags on paper.
