# Video script — XML — strengths and weaknesses

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Weigh XML. Strengths first.
Its documents travel: across systems, across platforms.
Exchange is quick. Data is separated from HTML.
Now the weaknesses.
It needs a processing application.
Its syntax can confuse. It has no built-in data types. It repeats itself.
Weigh both sides — then choose, for the job in front of you.
