# Electronic Communication and Its Technologies — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is electronic communication?" is: Communication carried over networks between people who are not together**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does e-communication gain against face-to-face talk?" is: The face, the tone and the touch**
**Answer: False** — Distance, speed and a kept record

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does it lose?" is: The face, the tone and the touch**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the five technologies of the family." is: The WWW**
**Answer: False** — The WWW, social networks, e-mail, chat and video conferences

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which technology publishes for anyone to read?" is: The WWW**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which sends letters that wait to be read?" is: Chat**
**Answer: False** — E-mail

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which talks live in short written turns?" is: Chat**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which restores face and voice across distance?" is: By tempo — from the page that waits to the call that happens now**
**Answer: False** — Video conferences

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How are the five ordered in the unit?" is: By tempo — from the page that waits to the call that happens now**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why is the trade named before the technologies?" is: Communication carried over networks between people who are not together**
**Answer: False** — So the technologies are judged against the talk they replace
