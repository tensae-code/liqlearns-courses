# Electronic Communication and Its Technologies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is electronic communication?**
a) Communication carried over networks between people who are not together  b) Distance, speed and a kept record  c) The face, the tone and the touch  d) The WWW, social networks, e-mail, chat and video conferences
**Answer: a) Communication carried over networks between people who are not together**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does e-communication gain against face-to-face talk?**
a) The face, the tone and the touch  b) Distance, speed and a kept record  c) The WWW, social networks, e-mail, chat and video conferences  d) The WWW
**Answer: b) Distance, speed and a kept record**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does it lose?**
a) The WWW, social networks, e-mail, chat and video conferences  b) The WWW  c) The face, the tone and the touch  d) E-mail
**Answer: c) The face, the tone and the touch**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the five technologies of the family.**
a) The WWW  b) E-mail  c) Chat  d) The WWW, social networks, e-mail, chat and video conferences
**Answer: d) The WWW, social networks, e-mail, chat and video conferences**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which technology publishes for anyone to read?**
a) The WWW  b) E-mail  c) Chat  d) Video conferences
**Answer: a) The WWW**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which sends letters that wait to be read?**
a) Chat  b) E-mail  c) Video conferences  d) By tempo — from the page that waits to the call that happens now
**Answer: b) E-mail**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which talks live in short written turns?**
a) Video conferences  b) By tempo — from the page that waits to the call that happens now  c) Chat  d) So the technologies are judged against the talk they replace
**Answer: c) Chat**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which restores face and voice across distance?**
a) By tempo — from the page that waits to the call that happens now  b) So the technologies are judged against the talk they replace  c) Communication carried over networks between people who are not together  d) Video conferences
**Answer: d) Video conferences**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How are the five ordered in the unit?**
a) By tempo — from the page that waits to the call that happens now  b) So the technologies are judged against the talk they replace  c) Communication carried over networks between people who are not together  d) Distance, speed and a kept record
**Answer: a) By tempo — from the page that waits to the call that happens now**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the trade named before the technologies?**
a) Communication carried over networks between people who are not together  b) So the technologies are judged against the talk they replace  c) Distance, speed and a kept record  d) The face, the tone and the touch
**Answer: b) So the technologies are judged against the talk they replace**
