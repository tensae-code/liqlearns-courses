# Hands-on — Electronic Communication and Its Technologies

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Judge the trade', 'Take one message you sent this week. Write what e-communication gained for it and what face-to-face would have given instead.')

## Task two
('Five tempos', 'For five messages — an announcement, a letter to a friend, a quick question, a family meeting, a shared interest — choose the family's fitting technology and defend each choice.')
