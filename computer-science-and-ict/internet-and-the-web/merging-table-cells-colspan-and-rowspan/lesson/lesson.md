## Sub lesson 1 — Colspan and rowspan

When a layout needs a cell to **cover several columns or rows**, two attributes do the merging:

- **colspan** — the cell spans across **columns**.
- **rowspan** — the cell spans down **rows**.

The value says how many columns or rows the one cell covers. Watch the GIF: a header cell stretches to cover its neighbours as its span value is set.
