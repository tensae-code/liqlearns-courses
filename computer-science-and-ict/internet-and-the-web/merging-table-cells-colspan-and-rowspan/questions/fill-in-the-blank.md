# Merging table cells — colspan and rowspan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or code.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) To span columns, set ___; to span rows, set ___.**
**Answer: colspan; rowspan** — col across, row down.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Cases and Deaths each span the two columns ___ and New.**
**Answer: Total** — Total and New.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A two-level ___ is what the worked example builds.**
**Answer: header** — header across two levels.
