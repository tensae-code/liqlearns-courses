# Merging table cells — colspan and rowspan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Colspan merges a cell downwards through rows.**
**Answer: False** — colspan goes across columns; rowspan goes down.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The worked example's figures are WHO COVID-19 figures for Ethiopia.**
**Answer: True** — that is the book's example.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A spanning header cell replaces the cells beneath it in its row.**
**Answer: True** — one cell covers where two stood.
