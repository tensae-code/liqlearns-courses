# The CSS Box Model — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the box's four layers, inside out?**
a) Content, padding, border, margin  b) Inside the box — clear space between the content and the edge  c) Its width, its style and its colour  d) Outside the box — clear space between this box and its neighbours
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is padding?**
a) Inside the box — clear space between the content and the edge  b) Its width, its style and its colour  c) Outside the box — clear space between this box and its neighbours  d) The content, sized by width and height
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What three things describe a border?**
a) Its width, its style and its colour  b) Outside the box — clear space between this box and its neighbours  c) The content, sized by width and height  d) Width plus both paddings, both borders and both margins
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the margin?**
a) Outside the box — clear space between this box and its neighbours  b) The content, sized by width and height  c) Width plus both paddings, both borders and both margins  d) Their paddings and borders were forgotten in the sum
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is at the box's centre?**
a) The content, sized by width and height  b) Width plus both paddings, both borders and both margins  c) Their paddings and borders were forgotten in the sum  d) They collapse into one — the larger of the two
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is an element's true width computed?**
a) Width plus both paddings, both borders and both margins  b) Their paddings and borders were forgotten in the sum  c) They collapse into one — the larger of the two  d) Draw the boxes — the picture finds the forgotten layer
**Answer: a**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why do two 'fitting' boxes sometimes overflow?**
a) Their paddings and borders were forgotten in the sum  b) They collapse into one — the larger of the two  c) Draw the boxes — the picture finds the forgotten layer  d) The content and padding area — the background reaches to the border's inside edge
**Answer: a**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to adjoining vertical margins?**
a) They collapse into one — the larger of the two  b) Draw the boxes — the picture finds the forgotten layer  c) The content and padding area — the background reaches to the border's inside edge  d) Content, padding, border, margin
**Answer: a**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do when a layout breaks?**
a) Draw the boxes — the picture finds the forgotten layer  b) The content and padding area — the background reaches to the border's inside edge  c) Content, padding, border, margin  d) Inside the box — clear space between the content and the edge
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which layer takes the element's background colour?**
a) The content and padding area — the background reaches to the border's inside edge  b) Content, padding, border, margin  c) Inside the box — clear space between the content and the edge  d) Its width, its style and its colour
**Answer: a**
