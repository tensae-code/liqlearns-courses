# The CSS Box Model — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the box's four layers, inside out?** → **Content, padding, border, margin**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is padding?** → **Inside the box — clear space between the content and the edge**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What three things describe a border?** → **Its width, its style and its colour**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the margin?** → **Outside the box — clear space between this box and its neighbours**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is at the box's centre?** → **The content, sized by width and height**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is an element's true width computed?** → **Width plus both paddings, both borders and both margins**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why do two 'fitting' boxes sometimes overflow?** → **Their paddings and borders were forgotten in the sum**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to adjoining vertical margins?** → **They collapse into one — the larger of the two**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do when a layout breaks?** → **Draw the boxes — the picture finds the forgotten layer**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which layer takes the element's background colour?** → **The content and padding area — the background reaches to the border's inside edge**
