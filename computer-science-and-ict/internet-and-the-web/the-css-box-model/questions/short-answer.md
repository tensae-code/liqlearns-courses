# The CSS Box Model — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the box's four layers, inside out?**
**Answer: Content, padding, border, margin**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is padding?**
**Answer: Inside the box — clear space between the content and the edge**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What three things describe a border?**
**Answer: Its width, its style and its colour**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the margin?**
**Answer: Outside the box — clear space between this box and its neighbours**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is at the box's centre?**
**Answer: The content, sized by width and height**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is an element's true width computed?**
**Answer: Width plus both paddings, both borders and both margins**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why do two 'fitting' boxes sometimes overflow?**
**Answer: Their paddings and borders were forgotten in the sum**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to adjoining vertical margins?**
**Answer: They collapse into one — the larger of the two**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do when a layout breaks?**
**Answer: Draw the boxes — the picture finds the forgotten layer**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which layer takes the element's background colour?**
**Answer: The content and padding area — the background reaches to the border's inside edge**
