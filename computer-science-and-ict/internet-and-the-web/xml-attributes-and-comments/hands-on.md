# Hands-on — XML attributes and comments

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Add an attribute
Take a paper record you wrote earlier. Add one attribute that gives extra information about an element, with its value quoted. Read it aloud, naming the attribute and its value.

## Comment your work
Write two comment lines on a paper document — one saying what the document is, one saying who wrote it. Cross-check with a partner: could the parser ignore both and lose no data?
