# Planning a Website for Its Audience — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a website's intended audience?**
**Answer: The people the website is made for**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Planning a website comes before what?**
**Answer: Building the pages**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the audience affect in a website?**
**Answer: Content, words and design**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a website plan list?**
**Answer: The pages and what each one holds**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why plan a website's navigation?**
**Answer: So visitors find things easily**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the first planning question?**
**Answer: Who is this site for?**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give an audience for a school website.**
**Answer: Students, parents and teachers**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must match the audience in the site's text?**
**Answer: Reading level and language**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do planning sketches show?**
**Answer: How pages will look and link**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) A website plan is checked against what?**
**Answer: The audience's needs**
