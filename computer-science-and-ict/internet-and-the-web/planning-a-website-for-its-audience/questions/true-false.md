# Planning a Website for Its Audience — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a website's intended audience?" is: The people the website is made for**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Planning a website comes before what?" is: The pages and what each one holds**
**Answer: False** — Building the pages

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the audience affect in a website?" is: Content, words and design**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a website plan list?" is: Who is this site for?**
**Answer: False** — The pages and what each one holds

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why plan a website's navigation?" is: So visitors find things easily**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the first planning question?" is: Reading level and language**
**Answer: False** — Who is this site for?

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Give an audience for a school website." is: Students, parents and teachers**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What must match the audience in the site's text?" is: The audience's needs**
**Answer: False** — Reading level and language

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do planning sketches show?" is: How pages will look and link**
**Answer: True**

**Answer: False** — The audience's needs
