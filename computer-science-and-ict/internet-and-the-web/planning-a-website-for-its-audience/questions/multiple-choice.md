# Planning a Website for Its Audience — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a website's intended audience?**
a) The people the website is made for  b) Building the pages  c) Content, words and design  d) The pages and what each one holds
**Answer: a) The people the website is made for**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Planning a website comes before what?**
a) Content, words and design  b) Building the pages  c) The pages and what each one holds  d) So visitors find things easily
**Answer: b) Building the pages**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the audience affect in a website?**
a) The pages and what each one holds  b) So visitors find things easily  c) Content, words and design  d) Who is this site for?
**Answer: c) Content, words and design**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a website plan list?**
a) So visitors find things easily  b) Who is this site for?  c) Students, parents and teachers  d) The pages and what each one holds
**Answer: d) The pages and what each one holds**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why plan a website's navigation?**
a) So visitors find things easily  b) Who is this site for?  c) Students, parents and teachers  d) Reading level and language
**Answer: a) So visitors find things easily**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the first planning question?**
a) Students, parents and teachers  b) Who is this site for?  c) Reading level and language  d) How pages will look and link
**Answer: b) Who is this site for?**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give an audience for a school website.**
a) Reading level and language  b) How pages will look and link  c) Students, parents and teachers  d) The audience's needs
**Answer: c) Students, parents and teachers**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must match the audience in the site's text?**
a) How pages will look and link  b) The audience's needs  c) The people the website is made for  d) Reading level and language
**Answer: d) Reading level and language**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do planning sketches show?**
a) How pages will look and link  b) The audience's needs  c) The people the website is made for  d) Building the pages
**Answer: a) How pages will look and link**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) A website plan is checked against what?**
a) The people the website is made for  b) The audience's needs  c) Building the pages  d) Content, words and design
**Answer: b) The audience's needs**
