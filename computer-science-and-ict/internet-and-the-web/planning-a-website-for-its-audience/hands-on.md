# Hands-on — Planning a Website for Its Audience

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Audience first
Choose a real audience (market sellers, a youth group, new parents). Write their five needs from a website before you name a single page.

## Page plan
From those needs, list the site's pages, one line each: page name, what it holds, and the link path a visitor follows to it.
