# Planning a Website for Its Audience

## Do it first

Before Papua New Guinea's Grade 11 learners build a single page, the guide makes them answer one question: who is this for? This lesson plans a website the way the guide does — audience first, pages second.

## Sub lesson 1 — Knowing the audience

The **intended audience** is **the people the website is made for** — a school site is made for students, parents and teachers. The audience decides **content, words and design**: the reading level and language must match the audience, and every plan is checked against the audience's needs. Planning always comes before building the pages.

## Sub lesson 2 — Planning pages and content

The plan **lists the pages and what each one holds**, sketches **how the pages will look and link**, and plans the **navigation so visitors find things easily**. Only when the audience and the page plan agree does building begin.
