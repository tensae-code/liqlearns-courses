# Video script — The internet — a network of networks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One computer alone can do little.
Join computers: a network. They can talk.

Join networks to networks.
Homes. Schools. Offices. Governments.
That is the internet. A network of networks.

Information travels the whole world.
