# Hands-on — The internet — a network of networks

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Net of nets
In a class or family group, each small group joins hands inside itself — a network. Then groups link to one other group with one joined pair of hands — a network of networks. Pass a written message from the far end to the far end and count the hops.

## Where it reaches
List five places you know that are connected to the internet, and one thing shared from each. Mark which are private, which public.
