# Lesson plan — The internet — a network of networks

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Internet and the Web · **Lesson:** The internet — a network of networks
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 8 — Internet and the Web in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 7 book, Unit 4 — Internet. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the internet is a network of networks — private, public and government networks joined so computers everywhere can share and reach information — and, in its Grade 8 part, the four services that ride on it: WWW, e-mail, Telnet and FTP.
**This lesson's own level:** it opens the Internet title — it stands on the learner's everyday experience of phones and messages, and on nothing else in this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Networks joining networks** — a computer network is computers connected to communicate; the internet joins private, public and government networks into a network of networks (Grade 7 part)
2. **Internet services — WWW, email, Telnet, FTP (Grade 8)** — the services that facilitate access to internet resources, and the modem that joins a computer to the internet (Grade 8 growth, Wave 3 band B2)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: any lesson so far — this title opens fresh.  Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define a computer network.
2. Define the internet as a network of networks.
3. Name the kinds of network joined in it: private, public, government.
4. Say what the joined computers can do: share and access information globally.
5. Name the four internet services: WWW, e-mail, Telnet, FTP.
6. Say what a modem joins a computer to: the internet.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.
