# The internet — a network of networks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The internet is —
a) one big computer  b) a network of networks  c) a single website  d) a kind of cable
**Answer: b) a network of networks** — networks joined together.

**2.** A computer network is —
a) computers connected so they can communicate  b) one computer alone  c) a printer  d) a password
**Answer: a) computers connected so they can communicate** — connected computers communicating.

**3.** Which networks join in the internet?
a) Only school networks  b) Private, public and government networks  c) Only government networks  d) Only home networks
**Answer: b) Private, public and government networks** — all three kinds are named.

**4.** Through the internet, computers can —
a) share and access information globally  b) only play games  c) never share  d) work without power
**Answer: a) share and access information globally** — sharing and accessing information, worldwide.

**5.** A school lab's joined computers are an example of —
a) a computer network  b) the whole internet  c) a website  d) a browser
**Answer: a) a computer network** — a network — one of the many joined into the internet.

**6.** 'Globally' means —
a) in one room  b) all over the world  c) very slowly  d) in secret
**Answer: b) all over the world** — across the whole world.
