# The internet — a network of networks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The internet is —
a) one big computer  b) a network of networks  c) a single website  d) a kind of cable
**Answer: b) a network of networks** — networks joined together.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A computer network is —
a) computers connected so they can communicate  b) one computer alone  c) a printer  d) a password
**Answer: a) computers connected so they can communicate** — connected computers communicating.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which networks join in the internet?
a) Only school networks  b) Private, public and government networks  c) Only government networks  d) Only home networks
**Answer: b) Private, public and government networks** — all three kinds are named.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Through the internet, computers can —
a) share and access information globally  b) only play games  c) never share  d) work without power
**Answer: a) share and access information globally** — sharing and accessing information, worldwide.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** A school lab's joined computers are an example of —
a) a computer network  b) the whole internet  c) a website  d) a browser
**Answer: a) a computer network** — a network — one of the many joined into the internet.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Globally' means —
a) in one room  b) all over the world  c) very slowly  d) in secret
**Answer: b) all over the world** — across the whole world.

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** A computer network is —
a) two or more computer systems connected together  b) one computer switched off  c) a single web page  d) a printer cable alone
**Answer: a) two or more computer systems connected together — the Grade 8 book's own wording.**

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** The internet connects —
a) one school only  b) millions of computers, as the largest computer network in the world  c) two computers only  d) no computers
**Answer: b) millions of computers, as the largest computer network in the world**

**9. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a named internet service?
a) Telnet  b) a joystick  c) a spreadsheet  d) a monitor
**Answer: a) Telnet — WWW, e-mail, Telnet and FTP are the named services.**

**10. (Sub lesson 2)** Internet services —
a) block access to resources  b) facilitate access to internet resources  c) work only on paper  d) replace the network
**Answer: b) facilitate access to internet resources**
