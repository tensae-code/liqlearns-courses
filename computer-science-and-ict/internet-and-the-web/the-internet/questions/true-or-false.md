# The internet — a network of networks — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The internet is a single computer in one country.
**Answer: False** — it is networks of many kinds, joined worldwide.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A network lets one computer communicate with another.
**Answer: True** — that is the definition.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Government networks are part of the internet.
**Answer: True** — private, public and government networks together.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The internet is a worldwide global system of interconnected computer networks.
**Answer: True — worldwide, global, interconnected — the definition.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A modem joins a computer to the intranet.
**Answer: False — a modem joins a computer to the internet; 'intranet' is the glossary's faulty wording, flagged in the sources.**
