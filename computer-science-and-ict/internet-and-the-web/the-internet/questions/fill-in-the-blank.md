# The internet — a network of networks — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The internet is a network of ______.
**Answer: networks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Computers on the internet share and access information ______.
**Answer: globally**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A group of connected computers is a computer ______.
**Answer: network**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** WWW, e-mail, Telnet and FTP are internet _______.
**Answer: services**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The internet is the _______ computer network in the world.
**Answer: largest**
