# The internet — a network of networks

## Do it first

Draw three computers in three different places — a home, a school, a government office. Draw lines showing them talking to each other. You have just drawn this lesson's idea. Now find out its name.

## Networks joining networks

A **computer network** is a group of computers connected so that one computer can communicate with another. Now join networks to networks: home networks, school networks, office networks — **private, public and government networks together**. That joining is the **internet**: a collection of computers connected to **share and access information globally** — all over the world. Watch the GIF: small nets, joining, lights of information travelling between them. That is why the internet is called a network of networks.
