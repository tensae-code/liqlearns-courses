# Email Etiquette — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is email etiquette?" is: The manners of email — a clear subject, a proper greeting, clear writing and care with replies and attachments**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What should a subject line do?" is: Shouting**
**Answer: False** — Say what the mail is about

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What opens and closes a polite message?" is: A greeting that names the reader and a signature that names the writer**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How should the body be written?" is: Only when everyone on the message truly needs the answer**
**Answer: False** — Short and clear — one matter per message where possible, in plain words

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does writing in capital letters read as?" is: Shouting**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens to a message written in anger?" is: Announced in the message and kept to a size the receiver can take**
**Answer: False** — It is written twice — drafted, cooled down, and only the calm version sent

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is reply-all right?" is: Only when everyone on the message truly needs the answer**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How should replies be timed?" is: The manners of email — a clear subject, a proper greeting, clear writing and care with replies and attachments**
**Answer: False** — Answered in reasonable time

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What must attachments be?" is: Announced in the message and kept to a size the receiver can take**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the standing check before Send?" is: A greeting that names the reader and a signature that names the writer**
**Answer: False** — The right address, the right words and the right file attached
