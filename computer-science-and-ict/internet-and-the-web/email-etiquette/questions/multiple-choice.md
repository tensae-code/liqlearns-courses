# Email Etiquette — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is email etiquette?**
a) The manners of email — a clear subject, a proper greeting, clear writing and care with replies and attachments  b) Say what the mail is about  c) A greeting that names the reader and a signature that names the writer  d) Short and clear — one matter per message where possible, in plain words
**Answer: a) The manners of email — a clear subject, a proper greeting, clear writing and care with replies and attachments**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What should a subject line do?**
a) A greeting that names the reader and a signature that names the writer  b) Say what the mail is about  c) Short and clear — one matter per message where possible, in plain words  d) Shouting
**Answer: b) Say what the mail is about**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What opens and closes a polite message?**
a) Short and clear — one matter per message where possible, in plain words  b) Shouting  c) A greeting that names the reader and a signature that names the writer  d) It is written twice — drafted, cooled down, and only the calm version sent
**Answer: c) A greeting that names the reader and a signature that names the writer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How should the body be written?**
a) Shouting  b) It is written twice — drafted, cooled down, and only the calm version sent  c) Only when everyone on the message truly needs the answer  d) Short and clear — one matter per message where possible, in plain words
**Answer: d) Short and clear — one matter per message where possible, in plain words**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does writing in capital letters read as?**
a) Shouting  b) It is written twice — drafted, cooled down, and only the calm version sent  c) Only when everyone on the message truly needs the answer  d) Answered in reasonable time
**Answer: a) Shouting**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to a message written in anger?**
a) Only when everyone on the message truly needs the answer  b) It is written twice — drafted, cooled down, and only the calm version sent  c) Answered in reasonable time  d) Announced in the message and kept to a size the receiver can take
**Answer: b) It is written twice — drafted, cooled down, and only the calm version sent**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When is reply-all right?**
a) Answered in reasonable time  b) Announced in the message and kept to a size the receiver can take  c) Only when everyone on the message truly needs the answer  d) The right address, the right words and the right file attached
**Answer: c) Only when everyone on the message truly needs the answer**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How should replies be timed?**
a) Announced in the message and kept to a size the receiver can take  b) The right address, the right words and the right file attached  c) The manners of email — a clear subject, a proper greeting, clear writing and care with replies and attachments  d) Answered in reasonable time
**Answer: d) Answered in reasonable time**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must attachments be?**
a) Announced in the message and kept to a size the receiver can take  b) The right address, the right words and the right file attached  c) The manners of email — a clear subject, a proper greeting, clear writing and care with replies and attachments  d) Say what the mail is about
**Answer: a) Announced in the message and kept to a size the receiver can take**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the standing check before Send?**
a) The manners of email — a clear subject, a proper greeting, clear writing and care with replies and attachments  b) The right address, the right words and the right file attached  c) Say what the mail is about  d) A greeting that names the reader and a signature that names the writer
**Answer: b) The right address, the right words and the right file attached**
