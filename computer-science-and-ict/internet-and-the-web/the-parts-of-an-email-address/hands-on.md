# Hands-on — The parts of an email address

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Address bricks
Write 'username', '@' and 'domainname' on three cards. Build addresses by laying real names on the first and third cards — five addresses, each read aloud part by part.

## Spot the fault
A partner writes five addresses, two of them faulty (a space, a missing @). Find the faults and say what would happen to mail sent to each.
