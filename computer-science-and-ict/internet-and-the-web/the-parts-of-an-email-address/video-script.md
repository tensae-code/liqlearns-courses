# Video script — The parts of an email address

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every address has three parts.

Username — the name you choose.
At — the @ sign.
Domain — who hosts the account.

abdisa.aga — at — gmail.com.
Write it exactly. No spaces.
Big letters or small — the same account.
