# The parts of an email address — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The standard format of an email address is —
a) domainname@username  b) username@domainname  c) username.domainname  d) @username@domain
**Answer: b) username@domainname**

**2.** In abdisa.aga@gmail.com, the username is —
a) gmail.com  b) @  c) abdisa.aga  d) com
**Answer: c) abdisa.aga**

**3.** The domain name is —
a) the user's password  b) the email provider's domain — the website hosting the account  c) the message subject  d) the @ symbol
**Answer: b) the email provider's domain — the website hosting the account**

**4.** If an address is not entered exactly, the mail —
a) still always arrives  b) will not be delivered, or may go to the wrong person  c) is printed instead  d) waits a week
**Answer: b) will not be delivered, or may go to the wrong person**

**5.** Email addresses are —
a) case sensitive  b) not case sensitive  c) written with spaces  d) numbers only
**Answer: b) not case sensitive**
