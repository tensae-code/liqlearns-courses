# HTML Text Formatting — Font Size, Style and Colour — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does this lesson format?**
**Answer: Text in hand-written HTML — its size, styles and colours**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does font size show on a page?**
**Answer: Rank — headings larger than body or detail text**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the font styles the chapter teaches.**
**Answer: Bold and italic (either, for one)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does style show?**
**Answer: Emphasis — what the reader must not miss**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In which two places is colour set?**
**Answer: The page's background colour and the font colour of the text**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What rule must the colour pair obey?**
**Answer: They must stay readable together — dark on light or light on dark**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does 'combining font properties' mean?**
**Answer: Setting size, style and colour on the same piece of text at once**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is decoration-everything a fault?**
**Answer: Because size and style are signals; if everything shouts, nothing is emphasised**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Libya place this chapter?**
**Answer: Grade 11 Term 2, Chapter 3 — after HTML structure, before links**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does formatting become when properties are combined with a reason?**
**Answer: Design**
