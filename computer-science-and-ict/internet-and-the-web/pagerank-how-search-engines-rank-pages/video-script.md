---

How does a search engine decide which page comes first?

It looks at the links. A link is a vote: this page is worth visiting.

But a page that links to three pages gives only a third of its vote to each.

And a vote from an important page counts more than a vote from a page nobody reads.

Now imagine a surfer who clicks links at random, forever. Some pages they visit again and again. Others, almost never.

PageRank is that share of visits. The pages the surfer keeps coming back to are the pages the engine shows first.
