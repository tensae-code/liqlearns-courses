# PageRank — How Search Engines Rank Pages — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In the link graph, pages are the nodes and links are the ____.**
**Answer: arrows — Each link is drawn as an arrow from the linking page to the linked page.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The model that defines PageRank imagines a ____ surfer clicking links forever.**
**Answer: random — The random surfer clicks links at random and sometimes jumps to any page.**
