# PageRank — How Search Engines Rank Pages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In the link graph of the Web, what is a link from page X to page Y?**
a) A decoration  b) A vote by X for Y  c) A copy of Y inside X  d) An advertisement
**Answer: b) A vote by X for Y — A link says "this page is worth visiting", and Y collects the votes pointing to it.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Page X has a score of 0.30 and links to three pages. How much does each linked page receive from X in one round?**
a) 0.30  b) 0.90  c) 0.10  d) Nothing
**Answer: c) 0.10 — A page splits what it passes on evenly among its outgoing links: 0.30 ÷ 3 = 0.10.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) PageRank of a page is —**
a) Its size in kilobytes  b) The share of time a random surfer spends on it  c) The number of words it contains  d) Its age
**Answer: b) The share of time a random surfer spends on it — Pages the random walk keeps returning to are the important ones.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) When do the iteration rounds stop?**
a) After exactly three rounds  b) When a round no longer changes the scores  c) When one page reaches 1  d) Never
**Answer: b) When a round no longer changes the scores — The settled values are the PageRank scores.**
