Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why can one link from a famous news site’s front page be worth more than fifty links from tiny unknown pages?**
**Answer: Because votes are weighted by the voter’s own importance — the famous page passes on a share of its large score, while each tiny page passes on a share of almost nothing.**
