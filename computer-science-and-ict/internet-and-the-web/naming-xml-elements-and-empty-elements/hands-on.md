# Hands-on — Naming XML elements — and empty elements

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Name court
Deal out name cards — legal and illegal mixed. Each player holds up a name; the others judge it and name the rule. Keep score of correct rules, not just verdicts.

## Bookstore on paper
Copy the bookstore record onto paper and colour its parts: text-holding elements one colour, element-holding another, the attribute a third.
