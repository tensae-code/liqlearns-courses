# Video script — The parts of a web address

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An address has four parts.
Http — the protocol. How pages travel.
Www. Then the name: google. Then the ending: dot-com.

The ending tells the type.
Dot-gov: government. Dot-edu: education. Dot-et: Ethiopia.

Read moe dot gov dot et.
The Ministry of Education. Government. Ethiopia.
