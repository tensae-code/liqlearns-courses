# Hands-on — The parts of a web address

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Address surgery
Write five addresses (from books, posters, or memory). Under each, draw the four-part split with a different colour per part, and write what the extension says about the site.

## Extension detective
Collect addresses ending in .et, .gov, .edu and .org. For each, say — before visiting — what kind of owner you expect, then check if you can.
