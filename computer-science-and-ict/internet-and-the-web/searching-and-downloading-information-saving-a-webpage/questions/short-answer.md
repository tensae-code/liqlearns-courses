# Searching and Downloading Information — Saving a Webpage — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does Fiji practise searching on?**
**Answer: The Map of Fiji, with search engines made for children**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first step of a search?**
**Answer: Turn the need into search words**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does extracting mean?**
**Answer: Picking out the facts, words or pictures the task needs, and leaving the rest**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is extracting a reading skill?**
**Answer: The page is not copied whole — the searcher takes the part that answers the question**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What must a searcher be able to say about an extracted part?**
**Answer: Why that part answers the question**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is downloading?**
**Answer: Bringing a copy from the web onto your own computer**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why save a webpage?**
**Answer: So it can be opened again without the internet**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is a single picture from a page saved?**
**Answer: By its own save command, into your folder**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is printing the right download?**
**Answer: When paper is what the task needs**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must work built from downloaded material do?**
**Answer: Acknowledge its sources — name where each fact and picture came from**
