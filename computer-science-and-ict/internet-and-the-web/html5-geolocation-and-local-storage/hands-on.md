# Hands-on — HTML5 Geolocation and Local Storage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Permission map
List three sites or apps that ask for your location. For each, write what it uses the position for and whether you allow it — and what the page loses when you refuse.

## Drawers design
Design the local storage for a small game page: list four key–value pairs it should remember between visits, with an example value for each, and say which one belongs in session storage instead.
