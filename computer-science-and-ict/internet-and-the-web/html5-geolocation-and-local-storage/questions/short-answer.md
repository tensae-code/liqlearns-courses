# HTML5 Geolocation and Local Storage — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does geolocation give a page?**
**Answer: The device's position — latitude and longitude**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must happen before a page gets the position?**
**Answer: The browser asks the user for permission**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What signals can position come from?**
**Answer: GPS, Wi-Fi or network signals**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens if the user refuses?**
**Answer: The page gets no position**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name two uses of geolocation.**
**Answer: Maps and local search (delivery estimates also counts)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What shape does local storage keep data in?**
**Answer: Key–value pairs of text**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Does local storage survive closing the browser?**
**Answer: Yes — it is still there on return**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is session storage's difference?**
**Answer: It forgets when the tab closes**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name two things kept in local storage.**
**Answer: Settings and preferences (drafts also counts)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Can one site read another site's local storage?**
**Answer: No — storage is per-site**
