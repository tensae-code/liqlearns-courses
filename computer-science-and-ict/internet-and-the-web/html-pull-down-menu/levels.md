## Sub lesson 2 — When a pull-down is the right control
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A choice from a fixed list — states, months, class names; Misspellings and wrong entries — the visitor can only pick what is offered; When the list is very long, or the answer might not be on the list; The answers — clean and comparable; Match the control to the answer: fixed and short — pull-down; open or long — another control.
