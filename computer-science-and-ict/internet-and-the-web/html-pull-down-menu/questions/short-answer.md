# HTML Pull-Down Menu — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which element makes a pull-down menu?**
**Answer: The <select> element**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which element makes each choice inside it?**
**Answer: The <option> element**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does the browser show the menu at rest?**
**Answer: Folded — only the current choice visible, with a small arrow beside it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when it is clicked?**
**Answer: The full list opens; clicking a choice folds it again with that choice showing**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) As what is the choice sent when the form is submitted?**
**Answer: As the menu's name — its name attribute**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When does a pull-down suit well?**
**Answer: A choice from a fixed list — states, months, class names**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does it prevent?**
**Answer: Misspellings and wrong entries — the visitor can only pick what is offered**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When does it suit badly?**
**Answer: When the list is very long, or the answer might not be on the list**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What arrives cleaner with a pull-down?**
**Answer: The answers — clean and comparable**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is Malaysia's teaching point?**
**Answer: Match the control to the answer: fixed and short — pull-down; open or long — another control**
