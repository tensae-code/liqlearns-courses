# Hands-on — HTML Pull-Down Menu

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Build a state menu', 'Build a form with a pull-down menu of Malaysian states. Test it: open the list, pick one, and say what the browser shows at each step.')

## Task two
('Choose the control', 'For each question — your state, your birth month, your favourite food, your school name from a list of 300 — choose pull-down or text box and give the reason.')
