# Hands-on — Planning and Designing a Website

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Plan before code', 'Plan a small school site on paper: audience, purpose, a structure diagram naming every page, and one page layout. Do not write any HTML yet.')

## Task two
('Choose the build', 'For your planned site, choose CMS, hand-coded HTML or a design program. Defend the choice against the other two in three sentences.')
