# Planning and Designing a Website — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What two questions open a website plan?**
**Answer: Who is the audience, and what is the purpose?**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four site structures.**
**Answer: Linear, hierarchical, cycle and web**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which structure hangs pages in a tree from the home page?**
**Answer: Hierarchical**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What promise does a structure make to the visitor?**
**Answer: At any page, the way onward and the way home are guessable from the shape**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a page layout fix?**
**Answer: Where the title, menu, text and pictures stand — the same across the site**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three ways of building a site.**
**Answer: A CMS, hand-coded HTML, and design programs**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is hosting?**
**Answer: Putting the site on a server so visitors can reach it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is tested before opening?**
**Answer: Every page and every link**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's working order?**
**Answer: Plan, structure, build, host, test**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name a real site the book's pupils plan.**
**Answer: The "Our School" site (any one of: the Animals of the Mongolian Red Book page)**
