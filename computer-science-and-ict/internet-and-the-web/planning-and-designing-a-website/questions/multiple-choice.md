# Planning and Designing a Website — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What two questions open a website plan?**
a) Who is the audience, and what is the purpose?  b) Linear, hierarchical, cycle and web  c) Hierarchical  d) At any page, the way onward and the way home are guessable from the shape
**Answer: a) Who is the audience, and what is the purpose?**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four site structures.**
a) Hierarchical  b) Linear, hierarchical, cycle and web  c) At any page, the way onward and the way home are guessable from the shape  d) Where the title, menu, text and pictures stand — the same across the site
**Answer: b) Linear, hierarchical, cycle and web**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which structure hangs pages in a tree from the home page?**
a) At any page, the way onward and the way home are guessable from the shape  b) Where the title, menu, text and pictures stand — the same across the site  c) Hierarchical  d) A CMS, hand-coded HTML, and design programs
**Answer: c) Hierarchical**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What promise does a structure make to the visitor?**
a) Where the title, menu, text and pictures stand — the same across the site  b) A CMS, hand-coded HTML, and design programs  c) Putting the site on a server so visitors can reach it  d) At any page, the way onward and the way home are guessable from the shape
**Answer: d) At any page, the way onward and the way home are guessable from the shape**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a page layout fix?**
a) Where the title, menu, text and pictures stand — the same across the site  b) A CMS, hand-coded HTML, and design programs  c) Putting the site on a server so visitors can reach it  d) Every page and every link
**Answer: a) Where the title, menu, text and pictures stand — the same across the site**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three ways of building a site.**
a) Putting the site on a server so visitors can reach it  b) A CMS, hand-coded HTML, and design programs  c) Every page and every link  d) Plan, structure, build, host, test
**Answer: b) A CMS, hand-coded HTML, and design programs**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is hosting?**
a) Every page and every link  b) Plan, structure, build, host, test  c) Putting the site on a server so visitors can reach it  d) The "Our School" site (any one of: the Animals of the Mongolian Red Book page)
**Answer: c) Putting the site on a server so visitors can reach it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is tested before opening?**
a) Plan, structure, build, host, test  b) The "Our School" site (any one of: the Animals of the Mongolian Red Book page)  c) Who is the audience, and what is the purpose?  d) Every page and every link
**Answer: d) Every page and every link**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's working order?**
a) Plan, structure, build, host, test  b) The "Our School" site (any one of: the Animals of the Mongolian Red Book page)  c) Who is the audience, and what is the purpose?  d) Linear, hierarchical, cycle and web
**Answer: a) Plan, structure, build, host, test**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name a real site the book's pupils plan.**
a) Who is the audience, and what is the purpose?  b) The "Our School" site (any one of: the Animals of the Mongolian Red Book page)  c) Linear, hierarchical, cycle and web  d) Hierarchical
