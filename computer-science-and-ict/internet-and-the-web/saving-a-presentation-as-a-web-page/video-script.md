# Video script — Saving a Presentation as a Web Page

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The job of this section is **publication**: a presentation normally needs its own program and its audience in the room, but saved **as a web page** it becomes a small site — the slides as pages, in the talk's order, that any **browser** can open.
The viewer's part is simple: open the first page and move through the slides with the links the saving provides.
What carries over is the slides' **content and look**: text, pictures and order.
What does not fully carry is the live performance — the speaker's words, the timing, and effects that depended on the program running.
