# Hands-on — HTTPS and SSL — how a site keeps visitors' information safe

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The padlock hunt
Browse sites you know — school, bank, shop, ministry. Keep a tally: locked, or not secure? Never type private information while hunting; you are only reading address bars.

## Teach the check
Teach a partner the check in under a minute: where to look, what the lock means, what 'Not secure' warns. Then watch them do it alone on a new site.
