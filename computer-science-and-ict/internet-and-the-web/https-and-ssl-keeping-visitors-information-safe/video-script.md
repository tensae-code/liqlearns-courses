# Video script — HTTPS and SSL — how a site keeps visitors' information safe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Private information is travelling? Stop. Check.
The site needs HTTPS. Not HTTP. HTTPS.
HTTPS is the secure transfer protocol.
An SSL certificate enables it.
SSL encrypts the information in transit —
between the website and its database. Others cannot read it.
Your check: look at the address bar.
A locked padlock: secure. 'Not secure': walk away.
