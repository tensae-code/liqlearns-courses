# HTTPS and SSL — how a site keeps visitors' information safe — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or code.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The secure transfer protocol is ___.
**Answer: HTTPS** — HTTPS, not HTTP.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** SSL provides the secure connection and ___ information in transit.
**Answer: encrypts** — encrypts is SSL's action.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An insecure site is marked "Not ___".
**Answer: secure** — "Not secure" is the warning wording.
