# Hands-on — Chatting and Sharing Files on a LAN Messenger

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Chat on the LAN', 'Send a chat message to a classmate on the school LAN messenger and answer the reply you receive.')

## Task two
('Share the file', 'Send one of your files to your teacher or a classmate as a LAN-messenger attachment, and confirm it arrived.')
