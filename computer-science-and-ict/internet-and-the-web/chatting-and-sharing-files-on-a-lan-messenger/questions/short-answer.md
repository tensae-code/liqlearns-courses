# Chatting and Sharing Files on a LAN Messenger — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a LAN messenger?**
**Answer: A messaging program that works on the school's local area network only**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is not needed for a LAN messenger to work?**
**Answer: An internet connection**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which LAN messenger does Bhutan's Class V book use?**
**Answer: Qualia LAN Messenger**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How do you start a chat in it?**
**Answer: Choose a signed-in person from the list and write to them**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where do LAN messages travel?**
**Answer: Only inside the school's own network**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What else can the messenger send besides chat?**
**Answer: Files — documents and pictures as attachments**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a broadcast message?**
**Answer: One message sent to everyone signed in at once**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Who uses broadcasts in the classroom?**
**Answer: The teacher, to reach the whole class**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is Class V's end-of-year folder handed in?**
**Answer: Sent to the teacher as a LAN-messenger attachment**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is LAN sharing simpler than a drive here?**
**Answer: The file travels directly between the school's computers**
