# Chatting and Sharing Files on a LAN Messenger

## Do it first

Bhutan's Class V classrooms talk without the internet. Their school network carries a LAN messenger — Qualia LAN Messenger — where pupils chat, share files and hear broadcasts inside the school, with no internet connection at all.

## Sub lesson 1 — The LAN messenger — talk inside the school network

A **LAN messenger** is a messaging program that works on the school's **local area network** only: the computers of the school talk to each other directly, so **no internet connection is needed**. In **Qualia LAN Messenger**, each signed-in computer appears in a list; choose a person and **chat** — written messages that arrive at once, like online chat, but travelling only inside the school's own wires and air.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sharing files and broadcasts — and the year's folder

The messenger also **shares files**: a document or picture is sent to a classmate or the teacher as an attachment on the LAN, faster and simpler than carrying it on a drive. A **broadcast** sends one message to everyone signed in at once — the teacher's way to reach the whole class. Class V's end-of-year folder is sent to the teacher exactly this way: the year's work, handed in as a LAN-messenger attachment.
