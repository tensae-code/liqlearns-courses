# Video script — Favourites and Saving a Web Page

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The **favourites list** keeps a page's **address** under a name you choose, so one click returns to the living page. **Saving a web page** keeps a **copy** of the page itself on your own computer, to open with no connection — the copy stays as it was on saving day. The two are not rivals: favourites for pages you revisit, saving for pages you must hold. The section also meets the **image toolbar**, the small bar that appears over a picture on a page, with its own save and print actions.
The section closes with three settings of daily use. **Printing** puts the page on paper. Choosing the **home page** decides which page the browser opens with — the page you start from every time. And **Arabic in the browser** makes the browser show Arabic pages correctly, the setting a Libyan learner needs before any Arabic site can be read at all. Keeping, printing, starting, reading: the browser made fully the learner's own.
