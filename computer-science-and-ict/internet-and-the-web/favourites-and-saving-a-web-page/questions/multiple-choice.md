# Favourites and Saving a Web Page — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the favourites list keep?**
a) A page's address, under a name you choose  b) A copy of the page itself, on your own computer  c) Saving — the saved copy opens offline  d) For a page you revisit, because it returns to the living, updated page
**Answer: a) A page's address, under a name you choose**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does saving a web page keep?**
a) Saving — the saved copy opens offline  b) A copy of the page itself, on your own computer  c) For a page you revisit, because it returns to the living, updated page  d) The small bar that appears over a picture on a page, with save and print actions
**Answer: b) A copy of the page itself, on your own computer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which keeping works with no connection?**
a) For a page you revisit, because it returns to the living, updated page  b) The small bar that appears over a picture on a page, with save and print actions  c) Saving — the saved copy opens offline  d) Puts the page on paper
**Answer: c) Saving — the saved copy opens offline**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When is a favourite better than a saved copy?**
a) The small bar that appears over a picture on a page, with save and print actions  b) Puts the page on paper  c) Which page the browser opens with, every time  d) For a page you revisit, because it returns to the living, updated page
**Answer: d) For a page you revisit, because it returns to the living, updated page**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the image toolbar?**
a) The small bar that appears over a picture on a page, with save and print actions  b) Puts the page on paper  c) Which page the browser opens with, every time  d) So Arabic pages show correctly and can be read at all
**Answer: a) The small bar that appears over a picture on a page, with save and print actions**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does printing do?**
a) Which page the browser opens with, every time  b) Puts the page on paper  c) So Arabic pages show correctly and can be read at all  d) Favourites (the address) and saving (the copy)
**Answer: b) Puts the page on paper**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does choosing the home page decide?**
a) So Arabic pages show correctly and can be read at all  b) Favourites (the address) and saving (the copy)  c) Which page the browser opens with, every time  d) Libya's Grade 9 book, in its Internet Explorer chapter
**Answer: c) Which page the browser opens with, every time**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book teach Arabic in the browser?**
a) Favourites (the address) and saving (the copy)  b) Libya's Grade 9 book, in its Internet Explorer chapter  c) A page's address, under a name you choose  d) So Arabic pages show correctly and can be read at all
**Answer: d) So Arabic pages show correctly and can be read at all**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name the two ways of keeping a page.**
a) Favourites (the address) and saving (the copy)  b) Libya's Grade 9 book, in its Internet Explorer chapter  c) A page's address, under a name you choose  d) A copy of the page itself, on your own computer

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Whose browser chapter teaches this set?**
