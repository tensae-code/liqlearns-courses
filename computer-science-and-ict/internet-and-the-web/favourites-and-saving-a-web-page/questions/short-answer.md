# Favourites and Saving a Web Page — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the favourites list keep?**
**Answer: A page's address, under a name you choose**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does saving a web page keep?**
**Answer: A copy of the page itself, on your own computer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which keeping works with no connection?**
**Answer: Saving — the saved copy opens offline**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When is a favourite better than a saved copy?**
**Answer: For a page you revisit, because it returns to the living, updated page**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the image toolbar?**
**Answer: The small bar that appears over a picture on a page, with save and print actions**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does printing do?**
**Answer: Puts the page on paper**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does choosing the home page decide?**
**Answer: Which page the browser opens with, every time**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book teach Arabic in the browser?**
**Answer: So Arabic pages show correctly and can be read at all**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name the two ways of keeping a page.**
**Answer: Favourites (the address) and saving (the copy)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Whose browser chapter teaches this set?**
**Answer: Libya's Grade 9 book, in its Internet Explorer chapter**
