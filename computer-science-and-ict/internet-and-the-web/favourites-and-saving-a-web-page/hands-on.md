# Hands-on — Favourites and Saving a Web Page

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Keep it both ways', 'Find a useful page. Add it to favourites, then save a copy to your computer. Open the saved copy with the connection off.')

## Task two
('Make it yours', 'Set the browser's home page to your school's page or a search page, and print one page you saved.')
