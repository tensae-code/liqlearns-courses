# Hands-on — Responsive Design and Flexbox

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make boxes flex
In an editor, write a page with a container holding four coloured boxes. Make the container a flex container. Give the boxes different grow values and open the page: who took the space? Then allow wrapping and drag the browser window narrow — watch the boxes flow. Write down the exact moment the first box wrapped.

## One breakpoint
Add a media query at 600px that stacks the boxes in a column and makes the text larger. Resize the window across 600px slowly and note the exact switch. Change the breakpoint to 800px and say what changed for a tablet-sized window.
