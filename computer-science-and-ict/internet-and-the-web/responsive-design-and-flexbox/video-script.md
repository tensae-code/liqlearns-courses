# Video script — Responsive Design and Flexbox

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One page must fit every screen. A wide monitor. A small phone.

Flexbox helps. Put boxes in a flex container, and they line up by themselves. They stretch to fill free space. They shrink when space runs out. And when they no longer fit side by side, they wrap onto the next line, like words in a paragraph.

Media queries help too. The page asks: how wide is this screen? If it is narrow, different rules apply. Three boxes across become one column. One file, two layouts.

That is responsive design: one page that responds to the screen it meets.
