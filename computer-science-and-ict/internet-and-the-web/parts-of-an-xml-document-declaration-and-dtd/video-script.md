# Video script — Parts of an XML document — declaration and DTD

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Open a full XML document. Look at the top.
First line: the declaration. Version. Encoding.
Next: DOCTYPE. It describes the structure.
Inside it, element declarations.
people holds person. person holds name.
name holds first and last.
first and last hold text. That text is PCDATA.
The top of the document is its rule sheet.
