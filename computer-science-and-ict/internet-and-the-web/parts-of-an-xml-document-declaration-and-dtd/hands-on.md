# Hands-on — Parts of an XML document — declaration and DTD

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Label the top
Copy the athletes document top onto paper. Draw a bracket beside each part and label it: declaration, DOCTYPE, internal DTD.

## Declare your own
Invent a small structure — a class holding pupils, a pupil holding a name. Write its internal element declarations on paper, ending the name at #PCDATA.
