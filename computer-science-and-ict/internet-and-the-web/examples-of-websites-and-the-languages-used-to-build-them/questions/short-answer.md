# Examples of Websites — and the Languages Used to Build Them — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the six kinds of websites.**
**Answer: Informational, commercial, educational, communication/social, entertainment, service**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) By what are websites sorted?**
**Answer: Their main work**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the work decide the design?**
**Answer: Each kind's design serves its work — telling, selling, teaching…**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Give a service-site example.**
**Answer: Banking or booking sites**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Do real sites mix kinds?**
**Answer: Yes — most do; the sort is by main work**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the building languages and their layers.**
**Answer: HTML — structure/content; CSS — presentation; JavaScript — behaviour; server-side — dynamic pages and data**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does JavaScript add?**
**Answer: Behaviour — response, movement, checking**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do server-side languages do?**
**Answer: Build pages on request and hold the data**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What can the pupil now say of a page?**
**Answer: Which language is speaking — plain, styled or alive**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is this lesson's gift?**
**Answer: The map of the web's languages**
