# Building My School Website — the Digital Project — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What audiences does a school website serve?**
**Answer: Pupils, parents, teachers and visitors**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does planning start from?**
**Answer: The audiences' questions — dates, place, news — that the site must answer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the plan list?**
**Answer: The pages needed, the order a visitor meets them, and what each page must hold**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which design principles does the project apply?**
**Answer: Clear navigation, consistency, simplicity and accessibility**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Egypt make the project the pupil's own school?**
**Answer: A real audience disciplines every choice — faults become visible to their maker**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) In what format is each page built?**
**Answer: The HTML structure the unit taught — headings, text, pictures and links in their places**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the finished project judged?**
**Answer: As a whole — reachability in a few clicks, one-site consistency, correct opening on real devices**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the unit's final lesson do with this project?**
**Answer: Re-does it with AI help**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why build by hand first?**
**Answer: A pupil who has built unaided can judge what the machine later offers**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does this project sit in Egypt's Grade 8 book?**
**Answer: Lesson 3 of the website unit, after design principles and web page format**
