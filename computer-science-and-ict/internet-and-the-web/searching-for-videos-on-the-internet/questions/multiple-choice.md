# Searching for Videos on the Internet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a video search start?**
a) In the browser, at a search engine or a video site  b) Words that name the video wanted  c) As pictures and titles of videos  d) Read the titles and look at the pictures
**Answer: a) In the browser, at a search engine or a video site**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do you type to search for a video?**
a) As pictures and titles of videos  b) Words that name the video wanted  c) Read the titles and look at the pictures  d) It plays in the browser, and you watch it
**Answer: b) Words that name the video wanted**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How do video results arrive?**
a) Read the titles and look at the pictures  b) It plays in the browser, and you watch it  c) As pictures and titles of videos  d) Search again, and change the search words to steer the results
**Answer: c) As pictures and titles of videos**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do you do before choosing a result?**
a) It plays in the browser, and you watch it  b) Search again, and change the search words to steer the results  c) It brings different videos back  d) Read the titles and look at the pictures
**Answer: d) Read the titles and look at the pictures**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when you open the chosen video?**
a) It plays in the browser, and you watch it  b) Search again, and change the search words to steer the results  c) It brings different videos back  d) Saving it onto the computer
**Answer: a) It plays in the browser, and you watch it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How do you find other videos on a subject?**
a) It brings different videos back  b) Search again, and change the search words to steer the results  c) Saving it onto the computer  d) So it can be watched again without searching again
**Answer: b) Search again, and change the search words to steer the results**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does changing a search word do?**
a) Saving it onto the computer  b) So it can be watched again without searching again  c) It brings different videos back  d) Searching again with new words, and saving what is worth keeping
**Answer: c) It brings different videos back**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does downloading a video mean?**
a) So it can be watched again without searching again  b) Searching again with new words, and saving what is worth keeping  c) In the browser, at a search engine or a video site  d) Saving it onto the computer
**Answer: d) Saving it onto the computer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why download a video you found?**
a) So it can be watched again without searching again  b) Searching again with new words, and saving what is worth keeping  c) In the browser, at a search engine or a video site  d) Words that name the video wanted
**Answer: a) So it can be watched again without searching again**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What turns one search into a way of gathering?**
a) In the browser, at a search engine or a video site  b) Searching again with new words, and saving what is worth keeping  c) Words that name the video wanted  d) As pictures and titles of videos
**Answer: b) Searching again with new words, and saving what is worth keeping**
