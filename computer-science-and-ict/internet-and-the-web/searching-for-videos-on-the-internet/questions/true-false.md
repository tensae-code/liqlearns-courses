# Searching for Videos on the Internet — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does a video search start?" is: In the browser, at a search engine or a video site**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do you type to search for a video?" is: Search again, and change the search words to steer the results**
**Answer: False** — Words that name the video wanted

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How do video results arrive?" is: As pictures and titles of videos**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do you do before choosing a result?" is: Saving it onto the computer**
**Answer: False** — Read the titles and look at the pictures

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What happens when you open the chosen video?" is: It plays in the browser, and you watch it**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How do you find other videos on a subject?" is: Searching again with new words, and saving what is worth keeping**
**Answer: False** — Search again, and change the search words to steer the results

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does changing a search word do?" is: It brings different videos back**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does downloading a video mean?" is: Words that name the video wanted**
**Answer: False** — Saving it onto the computer

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why download a video you found?" is: So it can be watched again without searching again**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What turns one search into a way of gathering?" is: Read the titles and look at the pictures**
**Answer: False** — Searching again with new words, and saving what is worth keeping
