# Searching for Videos on the Internet — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a video search start?**
**Answer: In the browser, at a search engine or a video site**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do you type to search for a video?**
**Answer: Words that name the video wanted**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How do video results arrive?**
**Answer: As pictures and titles of videos**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do you do before choosing a result?**
**Answer: Read the titles and look at the pictures**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when you open the chosen video?**
**Answer: It plays in the browser, and you watch it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How do you find other videos on a subject?**
**Answer: Search again, and change the search words to steer the results**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does changing a search word do?**
**Answer: It brings different videos back**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does downloading a video mean?**
**Answer: Saving it onto the computer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why download a video you found?**
**Answer: So it can be watched again without searching again**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What turns one search into a way of gathering?**
**Answer: Searching again with new words, and saving what is worth keeping**
