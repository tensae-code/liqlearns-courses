# Lesson plan — Searching for Videos on the Internet

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Internet and the Web · **Lesson:** Searching for Videos on the Internet
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Morocco (2026-11-14) — in the title Internet and the Web on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Morocco's Grade 2 “J'excelle en informatique” teacher guide, because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson. This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only (Morocco), named in lesson/sources.md, counted nowhere.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A video search starts like every search: the learner opens the browser, goes to a search engine or a video site, and types words that name the video wanted — the subject, and the word video where it helps. One found video leads to more: the guide's second séance searches for other videos on the same or a new subject, changing the search words to steer what comes back — a different word brings different videos.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

2. **Searching for other videos — and bringing one home** — One found video leads to more: the guide's second séance searches for other videos on the same or a new subject, changing the search words to steer what comes back — a different word brings different videos.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Search words name the video.
2. Read titles before choosing.
3. New words, new videos.
4. Download to watch again.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil, and a computer with the program this lesson uses, if available.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — You have searched the internet for words and for pictures. Some things are better watched than read. Grade 2 teaches the third search: finding videos — and finding more of them. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of searching for a video as the lesson describes it — the student studies the picture and names what each part shows.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of searching for other videos — and bringing one home in action — the student watches once, then says what changed and why.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key names and definitions aloud, in the order the lesson gives them — the student listens once through, then reads the same lines aloud along with the voice. (Audio is a described slot in this build; no audio file is made.)
3. **Say it to your companion** — the companion block in lesson/lesson.md.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-false, matching, short-answer — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.
