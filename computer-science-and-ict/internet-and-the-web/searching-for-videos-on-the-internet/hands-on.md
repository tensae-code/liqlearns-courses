# Hands-on — Searching for Videos on the Internet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Search, watch, search again
Search for a video about an animal. Watch it. Then change one search word and search again: write down how the results changed.

## Bring one home
With your teacher, choose one video from your search and download it to the computer. Find it afterwards and play it without using the browser search.
