# Bots on the Web — Proving You Are Human — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a web bot?**
**Answer: A program that visits pages automatically**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do good bots do?**
**Answer: Crawl the web so search engines can build their indexes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two things bad bots do.**
**Answer: Scrape addresses; test stolen passwords (also: flood forms, hunt weaknesses)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What sentence does the activity keep?**
**Answer: There are not only humans on the web**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What must every site decide, visit by visit?**
**Answer: Who — or what — is knocking**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a humanity test?**
**Answer: A task easy for a person and hard for a program — distorted letters, pictures to pick**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why do sites use them?**
**Answer: To check a person, not a password-testing bot, is asking to enter**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the activity meet the tests?**
**Answer: When connecting to a protected site**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the annoyance of the test, in this lesson?**
**Answer: The receipt that the border is guarded**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Are the tests hard for programs forever?**
**Answer: For now — programs keep improving**
