# Hands-on — Bots on the Web — Proving You Are Human

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Bot or not?** — List ten things a visitor might do on a school website. For each, decide: person, bot, or either? Defend the “either”s.

## Task two
**Design a humanity test** — Invent a test a young child could pass and a simple program could not. Swap tests with another pair and try to think like a bot against theirs.
