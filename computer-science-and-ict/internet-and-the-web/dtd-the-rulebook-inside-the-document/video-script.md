# Video script — DTD — the rulebook inside the document

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every document needs its rules written down.
That rulebook is the DTD.
It defines the structure. The legal elements. The legal attributes.
An application checks the data against it. Valid, or not.
Inside the file, it sits wrapped in DOCTYPE.
album holds: singer, name, release, language. In that order.
Each one holds text. PCDATA.
Read the rules line by line — then check the document.
