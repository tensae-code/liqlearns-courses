# Hands-on — DTD — the rulebook inside the document

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Check the album
On paper, write three album documents: one correct, one with a part missing, one with parts out of order. Check each against the DTD and stamp it valid or invalid, naming the rule.

## Rulebook for a game
Write a DTD for a football match record — teams, score, scorers in order. Swap rulebooks with a partner and write a document that passes theirs.
