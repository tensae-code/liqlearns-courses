# Hands-on — Building, Updating and Backing Up Web Data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Write the update', 'Stored guestbook lines are name|school|message. A pupil changes school. Write the ordered steps (or PHP) by which the program updates exactly her record, stating where her key comes from and what happens to every other line.')

## Task two
('Plan the backup', 'For the school's sign-up file, write the backup plan: what is copied, how often, where apart it is kept, and the monthly test you will run. Defend the rhythm in two sentences.')
