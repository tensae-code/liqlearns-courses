# Video script — Managing Contacts in Outlook

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Contacts in Outlook are the address book of the program: each contact holds a person's name, email address and details, so mail and meetings can find them by name The learner creates contacts, fills their details, finds a contact again, and keeps the list in order Because every other Outlook skill — writing mail, inviting people to meetings — depends on the contacts being there and correct.
