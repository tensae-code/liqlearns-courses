# Domain extensions — .com, .edu, .gov, .org, .mil, .net — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or code.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Commercial sites end in .___
**Answer: com** — .com is commercial.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Government sites end in .___
**Answer: gov** — .gov is government.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Network is .___; military is .mil.
**Answer: net** — .net completes the pair.
