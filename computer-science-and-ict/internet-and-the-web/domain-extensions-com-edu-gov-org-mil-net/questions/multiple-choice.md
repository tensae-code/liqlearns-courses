# Domain extensions — .com, .edu, .gov, .org, .mil, .net — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The extension at the end of an address is its —
a) root directory  b) top-level domain  c) home page
**Answer: b) top-level domain** — top-level domain is the ending's name.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** .edu belongs to —
a) commercial sites  b) educational institutions  c) the military
**Answer: b) educational institutions** — .edu is education's extension.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A non-profit organisation's extension is —
a) .org  b) .com  c) .mil
**Answer: a) .org** — .org is for non-profit organisations.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** .mil tells you the site is —
a) a shop  b) military  c) a network
**Answer: b) military** — .mil is the military extension.
