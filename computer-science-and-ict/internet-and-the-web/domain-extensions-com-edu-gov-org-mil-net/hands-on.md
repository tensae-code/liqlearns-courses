# Hands-on — Domain extensions — .com, .edu, .gov, .org, .mil, .net

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Six doors
Draw six doors on a big sheet, one per extension. Under each, draw or write its kind of owner. Visitors (partners) knock with an address — open the right door.

## Ending hunt
List every website address you can remember. Sort the list by extension. Which kinds do you actually visit? Which kind is missing from your list?
