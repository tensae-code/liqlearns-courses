# Video script — Domain extensions — .com, .edu, .gov, .org, .mil, .net

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The end of an address talks.
That ending is the top-level domain.
.com — commercial. .edu — education.
.gov — government. .org — non-profit.
.mil — military. .net — network.
Six endings. Six kinds of owner.
Read the ending first — it tells you whose door you are at.
