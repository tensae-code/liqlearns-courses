# Sign-Up and Login with Server Scripting — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does sign-up collect, check, and store?**
**Answer: The details on a form; validated on the server; the account stored as a record**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three sign-up validations.**
**Answer: Presence; well-formed fields; the name not already taken**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is 'name taken' checked?**
**Answer: By querying the store**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Sign-up's two answers are what?**
**Answer: An account made — or the field-by-field reasons it was not**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Sign-up is the unit's whole craft in what one act?**
**Answer: A form, a gate, a stored line**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does login check?**
**Answer: The sent name and password against the stored record**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) On a match, what happens?**
**Answer: The visitor is let in as that user — pages built for them**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) On a mismatch, what does the message say?**
**Answer: 'Name or password not recognised' — not which part failed**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why not say which part failed?**
**Answer: A courtesy to the honest and a wall to the guesser**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What caution travels with this school model?**
**Answer: Real systems store passwords protected, never readable — plain text files are a model of the shape**
