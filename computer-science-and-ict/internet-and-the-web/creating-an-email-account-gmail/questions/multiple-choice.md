# Creating an email account — Gmail sign-up — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Email is —
a) slow and costly  b) an efficient, inexpensive, real-time way of distributing information  c) only for businesses  d) a kind of web page
**Answer: b) an efficient, inexpensive, real-time way of distributing information**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which three services are pictured as offering free email accounts?
a) Google, Yahoo, Telegram  b) Yahoo, Outlook and Gmail  c) Gmail, Facebook, YouTube  d) Outlook, Twitter, Skype
**Answer: b) Yahoo, Outlook and Gmail**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Step 2 of the sign-up is —
a) enter a password  b) click Create account  c) tap Verify  d) click 'I agree'
**Answer: b) click Create account**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The password must have —
a) exactly 4 letters  b) 8 or more characters mixing letters, numbers and symbols  c) only numbers  d) your birth date
**Answer: b) 8 or more characters mixing letters, numbers and symbols**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The account is verified by —
a) a phone call to the school  b) a verification code sent by text message to your phone number  c) a letter in the post  d) a teacher's signature
**Answer: b) a verification code sent by text message to your phone number**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The last step is —
a) choose a username  b) enter date of birth  c) read the terms of service and privacy policy and click 'I agree'  d) click Create account
**Answer: c) read the terms of service and privacy policy and click 'I agree'**
