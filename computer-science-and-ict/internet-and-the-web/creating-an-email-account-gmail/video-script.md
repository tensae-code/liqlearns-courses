# Video script — Creating an email account — Gmail sign-up

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Let us make an email account. Gmail. Step by step.

First — open the Google account creation page.
Click: Create account.
Write your first name. Write your last name.
Choose a username. That name is yours.

Now a password. Eight characters — or more.
Mix letters. Mix numbers. Mix symbols.
Type it. Type it again — the same.
Click Next.

Write your phone number.
A code comes by text message.
Type the code. Tap Verify.
Write your date of birth.
Choose gender. Click Next.

Now read. Terms of service. Privacy policy.
Read first — then click: I agree.

Done. Your account is made.
Name. Birth date. Gender. Location. And your chosen name.
That is all the sign-up asked for.
