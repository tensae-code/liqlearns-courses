# Lesson plan — Creating an email account — Gmail sign-up

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Internet and the Web · **Lesson:** Creating an email account — Gmail sign-up
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 8 — Internet and the Web of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 4 — Internet. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an email account is created by following the sign-up steps in order — name, username, password, verification, birth date, gender, and agreeing to the terms — and email itself is an efficient, inexpensive, real-time way of sending and receiving messages over the internet.
**This lesson's own level:** it is a doing-lesson: it stands on the internet-services idea and needs nothing else — a learner landing here directly can follow the twelve steps as they stand.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Steps 1–4 — open the form and name yourself** — visit the creation page, click Create account, enter first and last name, choose a username
2. **Steps 5–9 — password and verification** — enter and confirm a password of 8+ characters mixing letters, numbers and symbols, click Next, enter a phone number, enter the texted code and tap Verify, enter date of birth
3. **Steps 10–12 — finish and agree** — choose gender, click Next, read the terms and privacy policy and click 'I agree'; the basic information list

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Internet services — WWW, email, Telnet, FTP (the grown The internet lesson). Recommended, never forced. A learner can land here directly; the lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what email is and name three services offering free accounts: Yahoo, Outlook, Gmail.
2. Follow the twelve Gmail sign-up steps in their exact order.
3. State the password rule: 8 or more characters mixing letters, numbers and symbols.
4. Name the basic information the sign-up needs.

## What you need
A computer or phone with internet for the real sign-up; paper and a pencil for the paper rehearsal. A phone number that can receive a text message, for the verification step.

## If the learner is stuck
- Username already taken → the username must be your own chosen name for the address; choose another and continue from that step — do not restart the form.
- Password rejected → count the characters (8 or more) and check all three kinds are there: letters, numbers and symbols.
- No verification code arrives → check the phone number was entered correctly; the code comes by text message to that number.
