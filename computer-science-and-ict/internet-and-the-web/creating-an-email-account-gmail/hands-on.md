# Hands-on — Creating an email account — Gmail sign-up

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Paper rehearsal
On paper, draw the sign-up form as empty boxes in the twelve steps' order. Fill each box with practice information — an invented name, an invented username, a practice password that follows the rule. Then read your boxes aloud in order: that reading is the sign-up, rehearsed.

## Step-order cards
Write the twelve steps on twelve cards, shuffle them, and rebuild the order without looking. A partner checks against the lesson. Creating the account in the laboratory, in the real order, is how this lesson is assessed — by doing.
