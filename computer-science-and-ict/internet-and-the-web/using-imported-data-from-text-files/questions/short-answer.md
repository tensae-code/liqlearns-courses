# Using Imported Data from Text Files — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does reading reverse?**
**Answer: Writing — open, read line by line, split each line into fields**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Splitting is the inverse of what?**
**Answer: The joining that stored the record**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must be trimmed from each line, and why?**
**Answer: The newline — or it rides into the last field and poisons comparisons**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) From what is the reader written?**
**Answer: From the writer's format agreement — same separator, same field order**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) A format change must change what?**
**Answer: Both writer and reader — or neither**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three uses of imported records.**
**Answer: Listing on a page; searching; counting and totalling (also: answering a login)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is 'imported data' in this unit?**
**Answer: Data made elsewhere, taken in and used as the program's own**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Data can be made by whom?**
**Answer: An earlier visit, another program, or a person typing the file**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the test of an import?**
**Answer: A round trip — three records in, the same three out, field for field**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) A login check is a use of what?**
**Answer: The stored sign-up records, read back**
