# Using Imported Data from Text Files — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does reading reverse?**
a) Writing — open, read line by line, split each line into fields  b) The joining that stored the record  c) The newline — or it rides into the last field and poisons comparisons  d) From the writer's format agreement — same separator, same field order
**Answer: a) Writing — open, read line by line, split each line into fields**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Splitting is the inverse of what?**
a) The newline — or it rides into the last field and poisons comparisons  b) The joining that stored the record  c) From the writer's format agreement — same separator, same field order  d) Both writer and reader — or neither
**Answer: b) The joining that stored the record**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must be trimmed from each line, and why?**
a) From the writer's format agreement — same separator, same field order  b) Both writer and reader — or neither  c) The newline — or it rides into the last field and poisons comparisons  d) Listing on a page; searching; counting and totalling (also: answering a login)
**Answer: c) The newline — or it rides into the last field and poisons comparisons**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) From what is the reader written?**
a) Both writer and reader — or neither  b) Listing on a page; searching; counting and totalling (also: answering a login)  c) Data made elsewhere, taken in and used as the program's own  d) From the writer's format agreement — same separator, same field order
**Answer: d) From the writer's format agreement — same separator, same field order**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) A format change must change what?**
a) Both writer and reader — or neither  b) Listing on a page; searching; counting and totalling (also: answering a login)  c) Data made elsewhere, taken in and used as the program's own  d) An earlier visit, another program, or a person typing the file
**Answer: a) Both writer and reader — or neither**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three uses of imported records.**
a) Data made elsewhere, taken in and used as the program's own  b) Listing on a page; searching; counting and totalling (also: answering a login)  c) An earlier visit, another program, or a person typing the file  d) A round trip — three records in, the same three out, field for field
**Answer: b) Listing on a page; searching; counting and totalling (also: answering a login)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is 'imported data' in this unit?**
a) An earlier visit, another program, or a person typing the file  b) A round trip — three records in, the same three out, field for field  c) Data made elsewhere, taken in and used as the program's own  d) The stored sign-up records, read back
**Answer: c) Data made elsewhere, taken in and used as the program's own**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Data can be made by whom?**
a) A round trip — three records in, the same three out, field for field  b) The stored sign-up records, read back  c) Writing — open, read line by line, split each line into fields  d) An earlier visit, another program, or a person typing the file
**Answer: d) An earlier visit, another program, or a person typing the file**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the test of an import?**
a) A round trip — three records in, the same three out, field for field  b) The stored sign-up records, read back  c) Writing — open, read line by line, split each line into fields  d) The joining that stored the record
**Answer: a) A round trip — three records in, the same three out, field for field**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) A login check is a use of what?**
a) Writing — open, read line by line, split each line into fields  b) The stored sign-up records, read back  c) The joining that stored the record  d) The newline — or it rides into the last field and poisons comparisons
**Answer: b) The stored sign-up records, read back**
