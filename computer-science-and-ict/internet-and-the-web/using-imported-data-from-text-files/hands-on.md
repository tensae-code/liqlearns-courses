# Hands-on — Using Imported Data from Text Files

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Write the reader', 'Stored lines are name|school|message. Write, in ordered steps or PHP, the reader that turns the file into records and prints a numbered list of names and schools only.')

## Task two
('Round-trip test', 'Store three records by the agreed format (one name containing a space, one message containing a comma). Import them with your reader and show the fields. If your separator was the comma, state what broke and the repair.')
