# Forms of Online Communication — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four forms Fiji sorts.**
a) Email; instant messaging and chat; voice and video calls; social networking and forums  b) Letters that wait in an inbox until they are read  c) Written talk that happens now, line by line  d) Speech and faces, carried by the network
**Answer: a) Email; instant messaging and chat; voice and video calls; social networking and forums**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is email's shape?**
a) Written talk that happens now, line by line  b) Letters that wait in an inbox until they are read  c) Speech and faces, carried by the network  d) Now or later; one person or many; writing, voice or face
**Answer: b) Letters that wait in an inbox until they are read**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is chat's shape?**
a) Speech and faces, carried by the network  b) Now or later; one person or many; writing, voice or face  c) Written talk that happens now, line by line  d) In email
**Answer: c) Written talk that happens now, line by line**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do voice and video calls carry?**
a) Now or later; one person or many; writing, voice or face  b) In email  c) In instant messaging  d) Speech and faces, carried by the network
**Answer: d) Speech and faces, carried by the network**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are Fiji's three sorting questions for a form?**
a) Now or later; one person or many; writing, voice or face  b) In email  c) In instant messaging  d) In a video call
**Answer: a) Now or later; one person or many; writing, voice or face**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where does a message that must be kept and reread belong?**
a) In instant messaging  b) In email  c) In a video call  d) They wait unread in the inbox
**Answer: b) In email**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where does a quick question between friends belong?**
a) In a video call  b) They wait unread in the inbox  c) In instant messaging  d) They vanish in the flow of talk
**Answer: c) In instant messaging**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does a family talk across the sea belong?**
a) They wait unread in the inbox  b) They vanish in the flow of talk  c) Email; instant messaging and chat; voice and video calls; social networking and forums  d) In a video call
**Answer: d) In a video call**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the cost of urgent words sent by email?**
a) They wait unread in the inbox  b) They vanish in the flow of talk  c) Email; instant messaging and chat; voice and video calls; social networking and forums  d) Letters that wait in an inbox until they are read
**Answer: a) They wait unread in the inbox**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the cost of official words sent by chat?**
a) Email; instant messaging and chat; voice and video calls; social networking and forums  b) They vanish in the flow of talk  c) Letters that wait in an inbox until they are read  d) Written talk that happens now, line by line
**Answer: b) They vanish in the flow of talk**
