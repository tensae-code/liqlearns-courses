# Forms of Online Communication — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the four forms Fiji sorts." is: Email; instant messaging and chat; voice and video calls; social networking and forums**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is email's shape?" is: Written talk that happens now, line by line**
**Answer: False** — Letters that wait in an inbox until they are read

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is chat's shape?" is: Written talk that happens now, line by line**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do voice and video calls carry?" is: Now or later; one person or many; writing, voice or face**
**Answer: False** — Speech and faces, carried by the network

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are Fiji's three sorting questions for a form?" is: Now or later; one person or many; writing, voice or face**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does a message that must be kept and reread belong?" is: In instant messaging**
**Answer: False** — In email

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does a quick question between friends belong?" is: In instant messaging**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does a family talk across the sea belong?" is: They wait unread in the inbox**
**Answer: False** — In a video call

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the cost of urgent words sent by email?" is: They wait unread in the inbox**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the cost of official words sent by chat?" is: Email; instant messaging and chat; voice and video calls; social networking and forums**
**Answer: False** — They vanish in the flow of talk
