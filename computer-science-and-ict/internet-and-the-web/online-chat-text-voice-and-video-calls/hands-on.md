# Hands-on — Online Chat — Text, Voice and Video Calls

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Three modes, one message', 'Deliver the same invitation by text chat, voice and video with a partner. After each, note what the mode added and what it took.')

## Task two
('The kept line', 'Read back a chat you had. Mark one line that reads worse kept than it felt live, and write the rule it teaches you.')
