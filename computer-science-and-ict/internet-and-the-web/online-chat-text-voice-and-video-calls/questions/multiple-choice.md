# Online Chat — Text, Voice and Video Calls — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is text chat?**
a) Live written conversation in short, immediately sent turns  b) It is brief, quick and informal — but it is kept  c) Yahoo Messenger  d) Write nothing you would not want read back later
**Answer: a) Live written conversation in short, immediately sent turns**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does chat writing differ from letter writing?**
a) Yahoo Messenger  b) It is brief, quick and informal — but it is kept  c) Write nothing you would not want read back later  d) Tone, pace and feeling
**Answer: b) It is brief, quick and informal — but it is kept**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What messenger does the book teach through?**
a) Write nothing you would not want read back later  b) Tone, pace and feeling  c) Yahoo Messenger  d) The face — the far speaker seen as well as heard
**Answer: c) Yahoo Messenger**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first rule of the live line?**
a) Tone, pace and feeling  b) The face — the far speaker seen as well as heard  c) More connection and more exposure  d) Write nothing you would not want read back later
**Answer: d) Write nothing you would not want read back later**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does voice conversation return to the talk?**
a) Tone, pace and feeling  b) The face — the far speaker seen as well as heard  c) More connection and more exposure  d) To leave a quick word
**Answer: a) Tone, pace and feeling**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does video add?**
a) More connection and more exposure  b) The face — the far speaker seen as well as heard  c) To leave a quick word  d) To truly meet
**Answer: b) The face — the far speaker seen as well as heard**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does each rise in mode cost?**
a) To leave a quick word  b) To truly meet  c) More connection and more exposure  d) The moment — never merely the newest
**Answer: c) More connection and more exposure**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is text the right mode?**
a) To truly meet  b) The moment — never merely the newest  c) Live written conversation in short, immediately sent turns  d) To leave a quick word
**Answer: d) To leave a quick word**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is video the right mode?**
a) To truly meet  b) The moment — never merely the newest  c) Live written conversation in short, immediately sent turns  d) It is brief, quick and informal — but it is kept
**Answer: a) To truly meet**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must the mode be matched to?**
a) Live written conversation in short, immediately sent turns  b) The moment — never merely the newest  c) It is brief, quick and informal — but it is kept  d) Yahoo Messenger
