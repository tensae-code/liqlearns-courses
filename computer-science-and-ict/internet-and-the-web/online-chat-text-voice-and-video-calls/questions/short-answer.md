# Online Chat — Text, Voice and Video Calls — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is text chat?**
**Answer: Live written conversation in short, immediately sent turns**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does chat writing differ from letter writing?**
**Answer: It is brief, quick and informal — but it is kept**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What messenger does the book teach through?**
**Answer: Yahoo Messenger**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first rule of the live line?**
**Answer: Write nothing you would not want read back later**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does voice conversation return to the talk?**
**Answer: Tone, pace and feeling**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does video add?**
**Answer: The face — the far speaker seen as well as heard**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does each rise in mode cost?**
**Answer: More connection and more exposure**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is text the right mode?**
**Answer: To leave a quick word**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is video the right mode?**
**Answer: To truly meet**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must the mode be matched to?**
**Answer: The moment — never merely the newest**
