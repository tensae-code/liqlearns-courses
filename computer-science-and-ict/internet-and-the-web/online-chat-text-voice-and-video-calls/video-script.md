# Video script — Online Chat — Text, Voice and Video Calls

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Text chat** is live written conversation: short turns, sent the moment they are typed, answered in the same minute. Its writing is its own dialect — brief, quick, forgiving of form — but it is **kept**: unlike spoken words, chat lines remain on the screen and in the record. Mongolia teaches the mode with its tool of the era, **Yahoo Messenger**: add your people, see who is present, and talk. The first rule rides with it: write nothing in the live line that you would not want read back later.
The same conversation can rise in modes. **Voice conversation** sends the talk itself — tone, pace and feeling return, the written record goes. **Video conversation** adds the face: the far speaker is seen as well as heard, the closest the network comes to the room. Each rise costs more connection and more exposure, and Mongolia's pupils learn the choosing as well as the using: text to leave a quick word, voice to truly talk, video to truly meet — the mode matched to the moment, never merely the newest.
