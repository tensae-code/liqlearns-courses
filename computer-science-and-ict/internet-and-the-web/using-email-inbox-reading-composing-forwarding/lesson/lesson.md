# Using email — inbox, reading, composing, forwarding

## Do it first

Your mailbox is open and three messages have arrived. One you must read carefully, one you must answer with a photo attached, and one you must pass on to a friend. Before any names are given: say what you would click, in what order, for each of the three jobs.

## Sub lesson 1 — The inbox and the message pane

In the **inbox**, emails received from others are **viewed and managed**. Each one is listed by **sender name, subject and date received**. **Select an email and it opens in the message pane**, where it can be **read** — and **answered with different commands**.

[IMAGE 11730 — a mailbox — inbox, message, compose, forward]

## Sub lesson 2 — The compose pane — writing and attaching

Click **Compose** and the **compose pane** opens. Watch it open in the GIF. In the compose pane, in order: **fill in the recipient's email address**; **fill in the subject**; **write the message**; and, if you need to, **upload files — such as photos and documents — as attachments**.

[GIF 11731 — Compose clicked, the pane opening]

## Sub lesson 3 — Forward — passing a message on

**Forward** shares an email you received by **sending it on to another person**. Read first, then forward: the message that arrived to you leaves from you to the next person, whole.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the four moves — inbox, message pane, compose pane, forward — and what is done in each.
