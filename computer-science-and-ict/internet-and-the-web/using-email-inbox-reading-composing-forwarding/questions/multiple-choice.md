# Using email — inbox, reading, composing, forwarding — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the inbox, each email is listed by —
a) sender name, subject and date received  b) colour and size  c) password and username  d) price
**Answer: a) sender name, subject and date received**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Selecting an email in the inbox —
a) deletes it  b) opens it in the message pane, where it can be read and answered  c) forwards it at once  d) prints it
**Answer: b) opens it in the message pane, where it can be read and answered**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Clicking Compose opens —
a) the inbox  b) the compose pane  c) the address book  d) a new account
**Answer: b) the compose pane**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the compose pane you fill in, first —
a) the attachment  b) the recipient's email address and the subject, then write the message  c) only emojis  d) the date
**Answer: b) the recipient's email address and the subject, then write the message**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Files such as photos and documents are added to a message as —
a) subjects  b) attachments  c) passwords  d) domains
**Answer: b) attachments**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Forward means —
a) writing a brand-new message  b) sharing an email you received by sending it on to another person  c) deleting an email  d) reading an email twice
**Answer: b) sharing an email you received by sending it on to another person**
