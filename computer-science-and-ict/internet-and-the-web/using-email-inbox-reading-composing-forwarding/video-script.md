# Video script — Using email — inbox, reading, composing, forwarding

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your mailbox has four moves.

Inbox — your letters that arrived.
Sender. Subject. Date.
Tap one. It opens. Read it.

Compose — a new letter.
Address. Subject. Your words.
Add a photo if you need to.

Forward — pass a letter on.
Four moves. That is email at work.
