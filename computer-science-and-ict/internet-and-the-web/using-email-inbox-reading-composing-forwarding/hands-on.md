# Hands-on — Using email — inbox, reading, composing, forwarding

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The classmate activity
On real accounts if you can: send a message to a classmate, read the new messages in your inbox, and forward an email to a classmate — the three jobs of the activity, done for real. No computer? Rehearse all three on a paper mailbox your partner manages.

## Paper mailbox
Draw a mailbox on a big sheet: an inbox list on the left, a message pane and a compose pane on the right. A partner 'delivers' paper messages into the inbox; you list them by sender, subject and date, open one, compose one answer with a paperclip 'attachment', and forward one on.
