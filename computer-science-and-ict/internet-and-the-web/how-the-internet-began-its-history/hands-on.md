# Hands-on — How the Internet Began — Its History

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Network timeline** — Place on one timeline: ARPANET 1969, TCP/IP 1983, the Web at CERN, the first browser, the smartphone. Each card gets one sentence: what changed?

## Task two
**Packets by hand** — Pass a long message across the class as five numbered paper packets taking different routes (different rows). Reassemble at the front — in order. Name the trick you just performed.
