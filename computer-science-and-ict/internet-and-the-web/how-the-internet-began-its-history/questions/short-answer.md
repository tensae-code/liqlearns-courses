# How the Internet Began — Its History — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) When did the first research network begin?**
**Answer: 1969 (ARPANET)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What did it link?**
**Answer: A few American universities and laboratories**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is packet switching?**
**Answer: Messages chopped into pieces that travel independently and are reassembled on arrival**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What lets different machines understand each other?**
**Answer: A shared protocol**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why was the network built?**
**Answer: So scarce computers and researchers could share work across distance**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does “internet” literally join?**
**Answer: Networks — an inter-network of networks**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When were TCP/IP standardised?**
**Answer: 1983**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Who invented the Web, and where?**
**Answer: Tim Berners-Lee, at CERN**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When did the network open beyond research?**
**Answer: The 1990s**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How should pupils see the internet after this history?**
**Answer: As a made, recent, human thing — built by agreement**
