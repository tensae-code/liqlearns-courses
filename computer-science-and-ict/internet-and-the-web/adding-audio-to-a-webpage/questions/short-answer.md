# Adding Audio to a Webpage — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which element adds audio?**
**Answer: The <audio> element**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does src name?**
**Answer: The sound file**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does controls give the visitor?**
**Answer: The browser's play, pause and volume bar**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is Jordan's rule?**
**Answer: A page's sound is the visitor's to command**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is ambushing autoplay called?**
**Answer: A fault, not a feature**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why offer more than one source?**
**Answer: Browsers speak different formats**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is offered if no format plays?**
**Answer: A fallback message**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What alternative serves those who cannot hear?**
**Answer: A transcript or text alternative**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Against what is a file's weight judged?**
**Answer: Its purpose**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What pair does this lesson complete?**
**Answer: Video for the eye's motion, audio for the ear**
