# Hands-on — Adding Audio to a Webpage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Add sound honestly** — Write the HTML for a page carrying a short audio clip: controls on, two sources, a fallback line and a transcript note.

## Task two
**Fit court** — Judge three proposed page sounds (autoplay anthem, a poet reading her poem, background music under text) against the lesson's fit rules.
