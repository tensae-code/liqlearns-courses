# Writing a Report from Web Research — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "With what does the report begin?" is: Research**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What tools does the research use?" is: Raw material — notes of the facts found**
**Answer: False** — Browsers and search engines

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "From how many pages should information be found?" is: More than one page**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the gathered information, before the report is written?" is: Arranging what was found into a written report of your own**
**Answer: False** — Raw material — notes of the facts found

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What should the notes be kept with?" is: The pages they came from**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does preparing the report mean?" is: The information**
**Answer: False** — Arranging what was found into a written report of your own

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What must the finished report do?" is: Stand by itself**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What did the web supply?" is: The internet unit**
**Answer: False** — The information

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the writer's own work?" is: The report — its order, its wording, its sense**
**Answer: True**
