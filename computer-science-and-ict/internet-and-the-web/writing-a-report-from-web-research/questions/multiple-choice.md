# Writing a Report from Web Research — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) With what does the report begin?**
a) Research  b) Browsers and search engines  c) Raw material — notes of the facts found  d) Arranging what was found into a written report of your own
**Answer: a) Research**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What tools does the research use?**
a) More than one page  b) Browsers and search engines  c) The pages they came from  d) Stand by itself
**Answer: b) Browsers and search engines**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) From how many pages should information be found?**
a) Raw material — notes of the facts found  b) Arranging what was found into a written report of your own  c) More than one page  d) The information
**Answer: c) More than one page**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the gathered information, before the report is written?**
a) The pages they came from  b) Stand by itself  c) The report — its order, its wording, its sense  d) Raw material — notes of the facts found
**Answer: d) Raw material — notes of the facts found**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What should the notes be kept with?**
a) The pages they came from  b) Arranging what was found into a written report of your own  c) The information  d) The internet unit
**Answer: a) The pages they came from**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does preparing the report mean?**
a) Stand by itself  b) Arranging what was found into a written report of your own  c) The report — its order, its wording, its sense  d) Research
**Answer: b) Arranging what was found into a written report of your own**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must the finished report do?**
a) The information  b) The internet unit  c) Stand by itself  d) Browsers and search engines
**Answer: c) Stand by itself**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What did the web supply?**
a) The report — its order, its wording, its sense  b) Research  c) More than one page  d) The information
**Answer: d) The information**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the writer's own work?**
a) The report — its order, its wording, its sense  b) The internet unit  c) Browsers and search engines  d) Raw material — notes of the facts found
**Answer: a) The report — its order, its wording, its sense**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which unit does this lesson close in Grade 8's book?**
a) Research  b) The internet unit  c) More than one page  d) The pages they came from
**Answer: b) The internet unit**
