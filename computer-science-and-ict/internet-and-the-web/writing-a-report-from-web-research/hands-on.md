# Hands-on — Writing a Report from Web Research

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Research one topic
Choose a small topic. Find facts about it on at least two different pages, keep notes with each page named, and list what the two pages agree on.

## Arrange before writing
Take your notes and number them in the order your report will use them. Write the report's opening sentence — then check it says what the topic is.
