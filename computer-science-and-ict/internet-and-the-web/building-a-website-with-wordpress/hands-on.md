# Hands-on — Building a Website With WordPress

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Build the service site** — On a practice WordPress install, build the citizen-service site: theme, three standing pages, one news post, and a two-click menu.

## Task two
**Citizen check** — Test another team's site on a phone as a first-time citizen. Report every failure to find a fee, a step or a number.
