# Building a Website With WordPress — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is WordPress?**
**Answer: Open-source website software worked through a browser admin panel**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does open-source mean here?**
**Answer: Free to use and modify**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a theme control?**
**Answer: The site's look**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are pages for, against posts?**
**Answer: Pages hold standing content; posts carry news**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What must menus let a citizen do?**
**Answer: Find any page in two clicks**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name two Google services the book adds.**
**Answer: A map and a form (also sharing and search tools)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the site checked before launch?**
**Answer: As a citizen would — on a phone, on a slow connection**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What two facts must the checker hunt?**
**Answer: The fee and the phone number**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is the site announced?**
**Answer: Only after what fails is fixed**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is a public-service website, in the lesson's words?**
**Answer: A promise**
