# Nesting HTML Elements Correctly — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is nesting?**
**Answer: Placing an element inside another element**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the inside and outside elements called?**
**Answer: Child and parent**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the browser build from nesting?**
**Answer: The page's tree of boxes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What later skills walk this tree?**
**Answer: CSS styling and scripting**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the tree, for the document?**
**Answer: Its skeleton**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) State the mirror rule.**
**Answer: Elements close in the reverse order they opened**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which is correct: <p><b>x</p></b> or <p><b>x</b></p>?**
**Answer: <p><b>x</b></p>**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the browser do with overlapping tags?**
**Answer: It guesses where boxes end**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a guessed structure dangerous?**
**Answer: The guess may differ between browsers**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What writing habit prevents wrong nesting?**
**Answer: Write the closing tag with the opening one, then fill the middle**
