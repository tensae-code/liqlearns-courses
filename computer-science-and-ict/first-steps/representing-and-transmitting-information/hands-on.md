# Hands-on — Representing and Transmitting Information

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Road map** — For a phone call, a letter, and a streamed song, fill the six-part model (source, code, channel, noise, decode, destination) for each.

## Task two
**Noise story** — Take a message sent through a noisy channel game (whisper chain) and name, from the model, exactly where its damage entered and one coding defence.
