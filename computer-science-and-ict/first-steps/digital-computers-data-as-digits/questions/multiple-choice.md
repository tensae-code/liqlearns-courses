# Digital computers — data as digits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A digital computer works on —
a) Continuous data  b) Discrete values  c) Dial readings  d) Mercury levels
**Answer: b) Discrete values** — Digital computers work on discrete values.

**2.** A digital computer takes raw data in as —
a) Pictures only  b) Digits  c) Sound waves  d) Heat
**Answer: b) Digits** — It takes raw data in as digits.

**3.** A digital computer processes data with —
a) Programs stored in its memory  b) A dial  c) Mercury  d) Hand cranks
**Answer: a) Programs stored in its memory** — It processes data with programs stored in its memory.

**4.** Compared with analog, digital memory is — (teacher guide — evidence)
a) Very low and limited  b) Very large  c) Nonexistent  d) The same
**Answer: b) Very large** — The guide's table: analog memory is very low and limited; digital memory is very large.

**5.** A digital computer performs —
a) Only printing  b) Calculations and logical operations at high speed  c) Only games  d) No operations
**Answer: b) Calculations and logical operations at high speed** — It performs calculations and logical operations at high speed.
