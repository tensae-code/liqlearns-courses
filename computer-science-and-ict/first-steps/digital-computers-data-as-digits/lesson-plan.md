# Lesson plan — Digital computers — data as digits

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Digital computers — data as digits
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 1 — First Steps of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 1 — Basics of Computer.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a digital computer works on discrete values: data goes in as digits, programs stored in memory process it, and calculations and logical operations come out at high speed.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the analog lesson: analog flows smoothly; digital works in separate, countable values — digits.
