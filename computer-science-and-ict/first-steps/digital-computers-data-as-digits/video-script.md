**Digital computers.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: a digital computer works on discrete values: data goes in as digits, programs stored in memory process it, and calculations and logical operations come out at high speed.

**Data as digits.** A digital computer works on discrete values — separate values that can be counted.
**What it does with the digits.** A digital computer produces output from the processed digits, performing calculations and logical operations at high speed.

Now you: Draw numbers entering a computer and a result coming out, labelled 'digital'.
