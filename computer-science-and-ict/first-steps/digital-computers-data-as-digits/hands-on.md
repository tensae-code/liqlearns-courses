# Hands-on — Digital computers — data as digits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Steps and glide
Repeat the movement game from the analog lesson, but now lead it: glide for analog, click in steps for digital. A partner must name the type from the movement alone.

## Digit hunt
List five digital devices in your home or school and for each say what goes in as digits and what comes out.
