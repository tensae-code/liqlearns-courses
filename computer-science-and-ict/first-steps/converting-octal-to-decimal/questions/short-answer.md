# Converting Octal to Decimal — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the octal place values, from the right?**
**Answer: 1, 8, 64, 512 — powers of eight**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do you do with each digit?**
**Answer: Multiply it by the place value it stands in**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) For 47₈, what is the 4 worth?**
**Answer: 4×8 = 32 — it stands in the eights place**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do you do with the products?**
**Answer: Add them all — the sum is the decimal answer**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is 47₈ in decimal?**
**Answer: 39 — 32 + 7**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is 125₈ in decimal?**
**Answer: 85 — 64 + 16 + 5**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is 10₈ in decimal?**
**Answer: 8 — 1×8 + 0×1**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How can the forward method check this one?**
**Answer: Convert the decimal answer back by dividing by 8 — the starting octal number must return**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is 777₈ in decimal?**
**Answer: 511 — 7×64 + 7×8 + 7×1**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Does the order of multiplying matter?**
**Answer: No — biggest place first or smallest first, the products are the same**
