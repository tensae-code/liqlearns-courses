# Second generation — transistors — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The second generation replaced vacuum tubes with —
a) Integrated circuits  b) Transistors  c) Microprocessors  d) Lasers
**Answer: b) Transistors** — Second-generation computers used transistors.

**2.** A transistor is a device that controls —
a) The flow of electricity in electronic equipment  b) The flow of water  c) The speed of cars  d) The growth of plants
**Answer: a) The flow of electricity in electronic equipment** — A transistor controls the flow of electricity in electronic equipment.

**3.** The transistor was invented in —
a) 1940  b) 1947  c) 1956  d) 1971
**Answer: b) 1947** — Invented in 1947, widely used in computers from the late 1950s.

**4.** The second generation ran from —
a) 1940–1956  b) 1964–1971  c) 1956–1963  d) 1971–today
**Answer: c) 1956–1963** — Second generation: 1956–1963.
