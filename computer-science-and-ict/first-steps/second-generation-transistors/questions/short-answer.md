# Second generation — transistors — short answer

Answer each question in a sentence or two.

**1.** What job does a transistor do?
**Answer: It controls the flow of electricity in electronic equipment.**

**2.** Why did it take years between the transistor's invention and its wide use in computers?
**Answer: It was invented in 1947 but only widely used in computers from the late 1950s — new technology takes time to be built into machines in large numbers.**
