# Second generation — transistors

## Do it first

Hold two fingers a centimetre apart. The device in this lesson is small enough to sit between them — yet it does the same job as a whole glass tube. Guess: what happens to a computer's size and heat when thousands of tubes become thousands of these?

## The transistor

The second generation replaced tubes with the transistor: a device that controls the flow of electricity in electronic equipment. One transistor does the controlling work of one vacuum tube, in a fraction of the space.

[Picture 10903 — second generation]

## Invented 1947, used from the late 1950s

The transistor was invented in 1947, but it was only widely used in computers from the late 1950s. The second generation runs from 1956 to 1963 — the years when transistors, not tubes, were the heart of new computers.
