# Hands-on — Second generation — transistors

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Big tube, small transistor
Draw a big glass tube and a tiny transistor side by side at real-ish sizes. Under each, write the generation it belongs to and its years.

## Count the saving
If one tube takes the space of a matchbox and one transistor the space of a pea, talk with a partner about what happens to a machine with ten thousand of each.
