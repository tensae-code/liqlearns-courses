# Hands-on — Detecting Errors in Transmitted Data — The Parity Bit

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Flip and catch** — In pairs, send 7-bit letters with an even-parity eighth bit on paper. The “wire” (a third pupil) secretly flips one bit in half the letters. Receiver: catch the corrupted ones by counting.

## Task two
**Beat the parity** — Find a two-bit flip that parity cannot catch. Show both flips and the unchanged count — then say what a stronger check would need.
