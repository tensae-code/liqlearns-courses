# A Short History of Computing — From Automata to Microcomputers — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an automaton?**
**Answer: A machine that follows a built-in pattern of actions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What steered Jacquard's loom?**
**Answer: Punched cards**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who planned the Analytical Engine?**
**Answer: Babbage**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Who was the first programmer?**
**Answer: Ada Lovelace, in the 1840s**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What idea does computing begin as?**
**Answer: Instructions stored and followed by a machine**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What filled rooms in the first electronic generation?**
**Answer: Electronic computers computing with valves**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three component generations in order.**
**Answer: Valves, then transistors, then integrated circuits**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What did the microcomputer put on one chip?**
**Answer: A whole processor**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When did home microcomputers arrive?**
**Answer: The 1970s**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the history's shape?**
**Answer: One idea, shrinking — until the computer disappears into everything**
