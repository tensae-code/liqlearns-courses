# Hands-on — A Short History of Computing — From Automata to Microcomputers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Timeline wall** — Make a class timeline: automaton, punched cards, Lovelace, electronic computer, transistor, microcomputer, network, smartphone. Each pupil owns one card and must defend its place.

## Task two
**Shrink ray** — Draw the same computer at three moments: room-sized, desk-sized, pocket-sized. Label what shrank and what grew (speed, users, price).
