# Hands-on — Number Systems Through History

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Count like an ancient', 'Count 37 sheep three ways: tally marks, stones in groups, and written digits. Say which way is fastest to check, and why.')

## Task two
('Zero at work', 'Write 5, 50, 500 and 5000. For each, say aloud what the zero or zeros are doing.')
