# Number Systems Through History — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is this lesson's history about?**
**Answer: How counting began among the ancient nations, and how the discovery of zero changed number systems**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How did early people count before written signs?**
**Answer: With fingers, stones, knots and tally marks**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Did all ancient nations use the same number signs?**
**Answer: No — each nation built its own signs and groupings**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What limit did the early systems share?**
**Answer: Big numbers were heavy to write and arithmetic was hard**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Libya teach the history before the systems?**
**Answer: So number systems are seen as inventions made to solve a real problem**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is zero as a sign?**
**Answer: A sign for nothing — none of these**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does zero do as a place holder?**
**Answer: It lets a digit's place fix its value, as in 5, 50 and 500**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What did zero make possible?**
**Answer: Writing any number with few digits, and arithmetic by written rules**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which later system stands at the end of this road?**
**Answer: The binary system the computer uses**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Is zero only a sign for nothing?**
**Answer: No — it is also a place holder, and that second job is the powerful one**
