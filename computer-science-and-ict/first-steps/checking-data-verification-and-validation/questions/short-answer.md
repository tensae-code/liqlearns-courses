# Checking Data — Verification and Validation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What two checks does this lesson teach?**
**Answer: Verification and validation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does verification check?**
**Answer: That data was transferred correctly from its source**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name verification's two classic methods.**
**Answer: Double entry and proofreading against the original**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What error does verification catch?**
**Answer: The copying slip — a dropped digit or a misspelled name**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does validation check data against?**
**Answer: Rules about what could be correct**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a range check ask?**
**Answer: Whether a number sits inside its allowed span**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give an example of a format check.**
**Answer: Checking a value has the right shape, like a date or a code**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why can validation not catch every wrong value?**
**Answer: A wrong value can still pass every rule**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why are the two checks taught together?**
**Answer: One guards the copying, the other guards the sense**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 12 ICT, Chapter 1 §I.2**
