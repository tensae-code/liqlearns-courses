# Hands-on — Sending Information — Sender, Message and Receiver

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Play Pixel paravent** — In pairs across a book-screen: sender describes a 5×5 black-and-white picture with “black/white” and positions only. Compare the rebuilt picture with the original.

## Task two
**Break the language** — Replay with the two sides secretly using different agreements for the same words. Watch the message fail — then name what was missing.
