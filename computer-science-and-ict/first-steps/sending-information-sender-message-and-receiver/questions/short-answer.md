# Sending Information — Sender, Message and Receiver — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three parts of a transmission.**
**Answer: Sender, message, receiver**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do the French words émetteur and récepteur mean?**
**Answer: Sender and receiver**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What stands between the two pupils in Pixel paravent?**
**Answer: A screen (paravent)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must the receiver do?**
**Answer: Rebuild the picture square by square, unseen**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What may the sender send?**
**Answer: Only the agreed signals**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What must both sides share in advance?**
**Answer: A language — the agreed code**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are signals without the agreement?**
**Answer: Noise**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the sender's order tell the receiver?**
**Answer: In what order the squares are read**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the deep lesson for computing?**
