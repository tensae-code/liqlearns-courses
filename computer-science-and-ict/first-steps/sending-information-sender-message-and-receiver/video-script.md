# Video script — Sending Information — Sender, Message and Receiver

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every transmission has the same skeleton: a **sender (émetteur)** who has something to pass on, a **message** that travels, and a **receiver (récepteur)** who must rebuild the meaning at the other end. Pixel paravent plays it with a screen (paravent) between two pupils: the sender sees a black-and-white picture and may only send agreed signals; the receiver, who cannot see the picture, must rebuild it square by square.
The game only works because both sides share a **language in advance**: which signal means black, which means white, in what order the squares are read. Without the agreement, the same signals are noise. That is the deep lesson the activity carries into all computing: machines, like pupils, can only exchange information through a code both sides hold before the first signal is sent.
