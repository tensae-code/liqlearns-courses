# Hands-on — Octal and Decimal Numbers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Two systems, one set of marks', 'Write 17, 25 and 63. Read each first as decimal, then as octal, working out the octal values by place value. Say which readings were possible and why.')

## Task two
('Digit detective', 'Write five numbers, some possible in octal and some not. Swap with a partner: each marks which could be octal and gives the reason.')
