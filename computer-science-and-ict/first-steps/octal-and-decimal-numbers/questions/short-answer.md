# Octal and Decimal Numbers — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the base of the octal system?**
**Answer: 8 — octal counts in eights**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which digits does octal use?**
**Answer: Only 0 to 7 — 8 and 9 never appear**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are the first octal place values, from the right?**
**Answer: 1, 8, 64, 512 — powers of eight**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is octal 25 in decimal?**
**Answer: 21 — 2×8 + 5×1**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Malaysia teach octal beside decimal first?**
**Answer: So the difference between the systems is stated plainly before conversions begin**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the base of the decimal system?**
**Answer: 10 — decimal counts in tens**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are the first decimal place values, from the right?**
**Answer: 1, 10, 100, 1000 — powers of ten**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Can the marks 25 belong to both systems?**
**Answer: Yes — 25 is 25 in decimal but 21 when read as octal**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How does Malaysia mark which system a number is in?**
**Answer: With a small subscript base — 25₈ against 25₁₀**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) A number contains the digit 8. Can it be octal?**
**Answer: No — octal has no digit 8**
