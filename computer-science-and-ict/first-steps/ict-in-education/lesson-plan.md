# Lesson plan — ICT in education

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** ICT in education
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Introduction to Information & Communication Technology — in the title First Steps on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** ICT improves and widens education — Ethiopian radio and television school programmes and plasma channels, computers, tablets, displays and interactive boards — so learning can happen anytime and anywhere, and a learner with a sight problem can access information by listening.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on ICT in daily life — communication: the same daily-life walk, now stopped at the school gate.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name Ethiopia's broadcast examples: radio and television school programmes and plasma channels.
2. Name the classroom devices the book lists: computers, tablets, displays, interactive electronic boards.
3. Say the two widenings: learning anytime and anywhere; audio access for a learner with a sight problem.
