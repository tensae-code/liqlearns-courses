# ICT in education — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Ethiopia's broadcast school examples in the book are —**
a) radio and television school programmes and plasma channels  b) only printed books  c) only letters
**Answer: a) radio and television school programmes and plasma channels**

**2. Classroom ICT the book names includes —**
a) computers, tablets, displays and interactive electronic boards  b) only chalk  c) only exercise books
**Answer: a) computers, tablets, displays and interactive electronic boards**

**3. Because lessons ride on devices, learning can happen —**
a) only at 8 in the morning  b) anytime and anywhere  c) only in the capital
**Answer: b) anytime and anywhere**

**4. A learner with a sight problem can access information by —**
a) listening to audio  b) reading smaller print  c) waiting
**Answer: a) listening to audio**

**5. ICT in education does what to education?**
a) Narrows it  b) Improves and widens it  c) Ends it
**Answer: b) Improves and widens it** — The book's own words.
