# Hands-on — ICT in education

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Learn by listening
Close your eyes and listen to a short lesson, a news item or a teaching programme on radio or a phone. Afterwards, write three things you learned — proof that listening carries a lesson.

## School without walls
Write a timetable for learning anytime and anywhere: one thing you could learn at home in the evening, one on a journey, one at the weekend — and the ICT that would carry each.
