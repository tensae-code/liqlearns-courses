# Hands-on — Number Systems — Decimal and Binary

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Break a number open
Write 2,058 and break it open by place value: each digit times its place. Then say what the same digits would mean if the places doubled instead of multiplying by ten.

## Count in binary
Count from 1 to 10 in binary on paper, writing the places 8-4-2-1 over each number. Then write your age in binary and check it by adding the places back up.
