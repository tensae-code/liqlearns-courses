# Number Systems — Decimal and Binary — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a number system?**
a) A way of writing numbers from a small set of digits by place value  b) Ten digits — 0 to 9  c) A digit's worth depends on where it stands  d) 1, 10 and 100 — each ten times the one before
**Answer: a) A way of writing numbers from a small set of digits by place value**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many digits does the decimal system use, and which?**
a) A digit's worth depends on where it stands  b) Ten digits — 0 to 9  c) 1, 10 and 100 — each ten times the one before  d) Ten
**Answer: b) Ten digits — 0 to 9**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is place value?**
a) 1, 10 and 100 — each ten times the one before  b) Ten  c) A digit's worth depends on where it stands  d) Two digits — 0 and 1
**Answer: c) A digit's worth depends on where it stands**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are the first three decimal places worth, from the right?**
a) Ten  b) Two digits — 0 and 1  c) 1, 2, 4 and 8 — each double the one before  d) 1, 10 and 100 — each ten times the one before
**Answer: d) 1, 10 and 100 — each ten times the one before**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the base of the decimal system?**
a) Ten  b) Two digits — 0 and 1  c) 1, 2, 4 and 8 — each double the one before  d) 11 — 8 + 0 + 2 + 1
**Answer: a) Ten**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How many digits does binary use, and which?**
a) 1, 2, 4 and 8 — each double the one before  b) Two digits — 0 and 1  c) 11 — 8 + 0 + 2 + 1  d) Its circuits have two easy states — on and off — which 1 and 0 match
**Answer: b) Two digits — 0 and 1**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are the first four binary places worth, from the right?**
a) 11 — 8 + 0 + 2 + 1  b) Its circuits have two easy states — on and off — which 1 and 0 match  c) 1, 2, 4 and 8 — each double the one before  d) The same: place value. Different: the base — ten against two
**Answer: c) 1, 2, 4 and 8 — each double the one before**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is binary 1011 in decimal?**
a) Its circuits have two easy states — on and off — which 1 and 0 match  b) The same: place value. Different: the base — ten against two  c) A way of writing numbers from a small set of digits by place value  d) 11 — 8 + 0 + 2 + 1
**Answer: d) 11 — 8 + 0 + 2 + 1**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the computer use binary?**
a) Its circuits have two easy states — on and off — which 1 and 0 match  b) The same: place value. Different: the base — ten against two  c) A way of writing numbers from a small set of digits by place value  d) Ten digits — 0 to 9
**Answer: a) Its circuits have two easy states — on and off — which 1 and 0 match**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the same in decimal and binary, and what differs?**
a) A way of writing numbers from a small set of digits by place value  b) The same: place value. Different: the base — ten against two  c) Ten digits — 0 to 9  d) A digit's worth depends on where it stands
**Answer: b) The same: place value. Different: the base — ten against two**
