# Number Systems — Decimal and Binary — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a number system?**
**Answer: A way of writing numbers from a small set of digits by place value**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many digits does the decimal system use, and which?**
**Answer: Ten digits — 0 to 9**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is place value?**
**Answer: A digit's worth depends on where it stands**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are the first three decimal places worth, from the right?**
**Answer: 1, 10 and 100 — each ten times the one before**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the base of the decimal system?**
**Answer: Ten**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How many digits does binary use, and which?**
**Answer: Two digits — 0 and 1**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are the first four binary places worth, from the right?**
**Answer: 1, 2, 4 and 8 — each double the one before**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is binary 1011 in decimal?**
**Answer: 11 — 8 + 0 + 2 + 1**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the computer use binary?**
**Answer: Its circuits have two easy states — on and off — which 1 and 0 match**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the same in decimal and binary, and what differs?**
**Answer: The same: place value. Different: the base — ten against two**
