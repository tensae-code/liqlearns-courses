# Information — Questions and Tasks Review — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which task belongs to this review?**
a) Sorting information into see, hear and read  b) Formatting text in WordPad  c) Naming computer parts
**Answer: a) Sorting information into see, hear and read** — Types of information was Unit 2's first lesson.

**2. A photograph is —**
a) a sense  b) a carrier  c) a type of sound
**Answer: b) a carrier** — Photographs carry the look of a moment.

**3. Presenting one fact as a drawing and as words shows —**
a) two different facts  b) the same information in different forms  c) a mistake
**Answer: b) the same information in different forms** — The form changes; the information stays.

**4. If a review task goes wrong, you should —**
a) visit the lesson it checks again  b) ignore it  c) start the whole course again
**Answer: a) visit the lesson it checks again** — The review is a map back to the lesson.
