# Information — Questions and Tasks Review — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Name the five senses that receive information.**
**Answer:** Sight (eyes), hearing (ears), smell (nose), taste (tongue) and touch (skin).

**2. Say one thing this review checks from the storing lesson.**
**Answer:** Memory stores inside a person; carriers such as paper, books and photographs store outside (either point is right).
