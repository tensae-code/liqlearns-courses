# Hands-on — How ICT Developed Over Time

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the timeline
Draw a timeline of ICT: carried messages, print, telegraph and telephone, radio and television, computers, networks and phones. Put one real example from your own family's life on the stage it belongs to.

## Then and now
Take one job — sending news to a relative in another town — and write how it was done in your grandparents' time and how it is done now. Name the ICT stage each way belongs to.
