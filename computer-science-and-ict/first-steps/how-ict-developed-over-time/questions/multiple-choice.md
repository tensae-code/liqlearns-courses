# How ICT Developed Over Time — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How did information travel for most of history?**
a) Only as fast as a person could carry it — speech, letters, print  b) The speed — telegraph and telephone sent messages along wires almost instantly  c) Voice and pictures carried through the air to many people at once  d) Work on information themselves — they only carried messages
**Answer: a) Only as fast as a person could carry it — speech, letters, print**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What did electricity change?**
a) Voice and pictures carried through the air to many people at once  b) The speed — telegraph and telephone sent messages along wires almost instantly  c) Work on information themselves — they only carried messages  d) Print — information carried on paper
**Answer: b) The speed — telegraph and telephone sent messages along wires almost instantly**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What did radio and television add?**
a) Work on information themselves — they only carried messages  b) Print — information carried on paper  c) Voice and pictures carried through the air to many people at once  d) A machine that stores and processes information, not only carries it
**Answer: c) Voice and pictures carried through the air to many people at once**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What could those early machines not do?**
a) Print — information carried on paper  b) A machine that stores and processes information, not only carries it  c) They shrank from room-sized to desk, laptop and phone — faster and cheaper  d) Work on information themselves — they only carried messages
**Answer: d) Work on information themselves — they only carried messages**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are books and newspapers in this story?**
a) Print — information carried on paper  b) A machine that stores and processes information, not only carries it  c) They shrank from room-sized to desk, laptop and phone — faster and cheaper  d) Networks and the Internet connecting the computers

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What did the computer add to ICT?**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the last stage of the development?**
a) Processing and communicating happen in the same device  b) Each stage faster, and reaching further, than the one before  c) Only as fast as a person could carry it — speech, letters, print  d) Networks and the Internet connecting the computers

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What happens in today's pocket-sized device?**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the direction of the whole development?**
a) Only as fast as a person could carry it — speech, letters, print  b) Each stage faster, and reaching further, than the one before  c) The speed — telegraph and telephone sent messages along wires almost instantly  d) Voice and pictures carried through the air to many people at once
