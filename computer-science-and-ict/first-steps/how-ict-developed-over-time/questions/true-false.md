# How ICT Developed Over Time — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How did information travel for most of history?" is: Only as fast as a person could carry it — speech, letters, print**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What did electricity change?" is: Voice and pictures carried through the air to many people at once**
**Answer: False** — The speed — telegraph and telephone sent messages along wires almost instantly

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What did radio and television add?" is: Voice and pictures carried through the air to many people at once**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What could those early machines not do?" is: Print — information carried on paper**
**Answer: False** — Work on information themselves — they only carried messages

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are books and newspapers in this story?" is: Print — information carried on paper**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What did the computer add to ICT?" is: They shrank from room-sized to desk, laptop and phone — faster and cheaper**
**Answer: False** — A machine that stores and processes information, not only carries it

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How did computers themselves change?" is: They shrank from room-sized to desk, laptop and phone — faster and cheaper**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the last stage of the development?" is: Processing and communicating happen in the same device**
**Answer: False** — Networks and the Internet connecting the computers

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens in today's pocket-sized device?" is: Processing and communicating happen in the same device**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the direction of the whole development?" is: Only as fast as a person could carry it — speech, letters, print**
**Answer: False** — Each stage faster, and reaching further, than the one before
