# How ICT Developed Over Time — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How did information travel for most of history?**
**Answer: Only as fast as a person could carry it — speech, letters, print**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What did electricity change?**
**Answer: The speed — telegraph and telephone sent messages along wires almost instantly**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What did radio and television add?**
**Answer: Voice and pictures carried through the air to many people at once**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What could those early machines not do?**
**Answer: Work on information themselves — they only carried messages**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are books and newspapers in this story?**
**Answer: Print — information carried on paper**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What did the computer add to ICT?**
**Answer: A machine that stores and processes information, not only carries it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How did computers themselves change?**
**Answer: They shrank from room-sized to desk, laptop and phone — faster and cheaper**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the last stage of the development?**
**Answer: Networks and the Internet connecting the computers**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What happens in today's pocket-sized device?**
**Answer: Processing and communicating happen in the same device**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the direction of the whole development?**
**Answer: Each stage faster, and reaching further, than the one before**
