# Hands-on — Forms of Representing Information

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Sort the forms', 'Collect five pieces of information around you — a notice, a price, a photo, a song, a clip. Name the form of each and say how you took it in.')

## Task two
('Make it mixed', 'Take one fact and present it three ways: as text, as a picture and as a number. Then join two forms into one mixed presentation.')
