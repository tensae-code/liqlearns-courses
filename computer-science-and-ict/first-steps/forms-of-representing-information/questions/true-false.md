# Forms of Representing Information — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does it mean to represent information?" is: To give it a shape the senses can take in**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the five forms of representing information." is: Picture**
**Answer: False** — Text, number, picture, sound and video

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which form is a photograph?" is: Picture**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which form is a price or a mark?" is: Sound**
**Answer: False** — Number

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which form carries speech and music?" is: Sound**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is a mixed form?" is: A web page joining text, pictures and sound (any one of: a film with titles)**
**Answer: False** — Several forms combined in one whole

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Give an example of a mixed form." is: A web page joining text, pictures and sound (any one of: a film with titles)**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does each form in a mixture do?" is: It can hold and join every form in one machine**
**Answer: False** — The job it does best — the picture shows, the text explains, the number measures

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the computer's power over forms?" is: It can hold and join every form in one machine**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which book opens with these forms?" is: To give it a shape the senses can take in**
**Answer: False** — Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book, in its first unit
