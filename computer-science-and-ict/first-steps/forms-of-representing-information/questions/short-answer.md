# Forms of Representing Information — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does it mean to represent information?**
**Answer: To give it a shape the senses can take in**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the five forms of representing information.**
**Answer: Text, number, picture, sound and video**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which form is a photograph?**
**Answer: Picture**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which form is a price or a mark?**
**Answer: Number**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which form carries speech and music?**
**Answer: Sound**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a mixed form?**
**Answer: Several forms combined in one whole**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give an example of a mixed form.**
**Answer: A web page joining text, pictures and sound (any one of: a film with titles)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does each form in a mixture do?**
**Answer: The job it does best — the picture shows, the text explains, the number measures**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the computer's power over forms?**
**Answer: It can hold and join every form in one machine**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book opens with these forms?**
**Answer: Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book, in its first unit**
