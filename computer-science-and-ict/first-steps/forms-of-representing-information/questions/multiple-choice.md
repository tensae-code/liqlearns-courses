# Forms of Representing Information — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does it mean to represent information?**
a) To give it a shape the senses can take in  b) Text, number, picture, sound and video  c) Picture  d) Number
**Answer: a) To give it a shape the senses can take in**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the five forms of representing information.**
a) Picture  b) Text, number, picture, sound and video  c) Number  d) Sound
**Answer: b) Text, number, picture, sound and video**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which form is a photograph?**
a) Number  b) Sound  c) Picture  d) Several forms combined in one whole
**Answer: c) Picture**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which form is a price or a mark?**
a) Sound  b) Several forms combined in one whole  c) A web page joining text, pictures and sound (any one of: a film with titles)  d) Number
**Answer: d) Number**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which form carries speech and music?**
a) Sound  b) Several forms combined in one whole  c) A web page joining text, pictures and sound (any one of: a film with titles)  d) The job it does best — the picture shows, the text explains, the number measures
**Answer: a) Sound**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a mixed form?**
a) A web page joining text, pictures and sound (any one of: a film with titles)  b) Several forms combined in one whole  c) The job it does best — the picture shows, the text explains, the number measures  d) It can hold and join every form in one machine
**Answer: b) Several forms combined in one whole**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give an example of a mixed form.**
a) The job it does best — the picture shows, the text explains, the number measures  b) It can hold and join every form in one machine  c) A web page joining text, pictures and sound (any one of: a film with titles)  d) Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book, in its first unit
**Answer: c) A web page joining text, pictures and sound (any one of: a film with titles)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does each form in a mixture do?**
a) It can hold and join every form in one machine  b) Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book, in its first unit  c) To give it a shape the senses can take in  d) The job it does best — the picture shows, the text explains, the number measures
**Answer: d) The job it does best — the picture shows, the text explains, the number measures**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the computer's power over forms?**
a) It can hold and join every form in one machine  b) Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book, in its first unit  c) To give it a shape the senses can take in  d) Text, number, picture, sound and video
**Answer: a) It can hold and join every form in one machine**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book opens with these forms?**
a) To give it a shape the senses can take in  b) Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book, in its first unit  c) Text, number, picture, sound and video  d) Picture
**Answer: b) Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book, in its first unit**
