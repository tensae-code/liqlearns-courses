# Objects — Questions and Tasks Review — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Say one thing this review checks from each of the unit's five lessons.**
**Answer:** Naming objects; similar features; grouping by a feature; distinguishing features; riddles (one check per lesson).

**2. What do you do after a review task goes wrong?**
**Answer:** Go back to the lesson that task checks, practise it, and try the task again.
