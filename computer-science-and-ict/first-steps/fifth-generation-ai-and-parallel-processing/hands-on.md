# Hands-on — Fifth generation — AI and parallel processing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Parallel hands
With three friends, each write one line of a four-line message at the same time, then read them together. Compare with one person writing all four lines alone. That is parallel against serial working.

## Spot the AI
List machines or phone features around you that seem to learn, play games, or understand speech. Beside each, write which fifth-generation application it matches.
