# Hands-on — Representing Information in a Computer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Represent three things
Take a letter, a number and a small picture. For each one, say in one sentence how it must be represented before the computer can hold it.

## On or off
Draw a row of eight boxes. Fill some and leave others empty to make your own two-state pattern, then swap patterns with a companion and read theirs aloud as on/off.
