# Video script — Representing Information in a Computer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before a computer can store or process anything, the information must be **represented** — turned into a form the machine can hold. Letters, numbers, pictures and sounds are all changed into **data** made of simple signals the computer's circuits can keep and move. That is why the same computer can hold a letter, a photo and a song: inside, all of them are represented the same way.
The computer's circuits work with **two states** — a signal is present or absent, on or off. Every piece of information is represented by **patterns of these two states**. The next lesson names the two-state digits themselves: the **binary numbers** that are the computer's own language.
