# Early Counting Devices — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first counting device?**
**Answer: The fingers — ten of them, always carried**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why does counting often go in tens?**
**Answer: Because people first counted on ten fingers**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What did stones and pebbles do?**
**Answer: One stone stood for each thing counted**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the idea all these tools share?**
**Answer: Let one thing stand for another, and keep the stand-ins in order**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What did a notch in a stick do?**
**Answer: It kept the count after the things counted had gone**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What did knots in a rope record?**
**Answer: One knot for each item counted**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why do these devices open the computer history?**
**Answer: Storing a count is the first step toward a computer's memory**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can fingers and loose stones not do well?**
**Answer: Remember a count for a long time**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 6 ICT, Chapter 3 §1**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four early devices in the book's order.**
**Answer: Fingers, stones, sticks and knotted rope**
