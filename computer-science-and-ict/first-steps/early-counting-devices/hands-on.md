# Hands-on — Early Counting Devices

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Count like the ancients', 'Count a real pile of things three ways — fingers, stones, notches on paper — and say which record lasts.')

## Task two
('Knot a record', 'Tie (or draw) a knotted-rope record of something in your day and label what each knot stands for.')
