# Check Digits — The ISBN Control Number — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a check digit?**
**Answer: A final digit calculated from all the digits before it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What weights does ISBN-10 use?**
**Answer: Each digit multiplied by its position weight**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Against what is the remainder taken in ISBN-10?**
**Answer: 11**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What letter can a check digit be?**
**Answer: X, standing for 10**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when a digit is typed wrong?**
**Answer: The calculation no longer agrees with the check digit**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three places check digits guard.**
**Answer: Bank accounts, barcodes, card numbers (also: identity numbers)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When is the arithmetic spent?**
**Answer: At issue time — once**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does it buy?**
**Answer: Error detection forever, at every till and terminal**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Whose family is the check digit in?**
**Answer: Parity's — spare data watching the rest**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the check digit, in one word?**
**Answer: A witness**
