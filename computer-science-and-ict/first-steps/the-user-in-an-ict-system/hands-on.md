# Hands-on — The user in an ICT system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The people behind one computer
Pick the computer or phone your family uses most. Write down every human job that keeps it working: who charges it, who buys the data, who repairs it, who decides the rules for using it.

## Success or failure
Tell a true story (yours or one you have seen) where an ICT system failed because of a person — a wrong number typed, a password shared, a machine never cleaned. Say which human job went wrong.
