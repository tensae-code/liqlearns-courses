# Glossary of Informatics Terms — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a glossary?**
**Answer: The book's own dictionary — its words in alphabetical order with short explanations**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How are glossary words ordered?**
**Answer: Alphabetically**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two words the Grade 5 glossary explains.**
**Answer: System unit and desktop (any two of the year's new words)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why not search the whole book for a word?**
**Answer: The glossary gives the explanation in one short line**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the glossary printed in the Grade 5 book?**
**Answer: After the last lesson, at printed p. 67**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three moments to use a glossary.**
**Answer: Before a lesson, during it, and after it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How do you test yourself with a glossary?**
**Answer: Cover the explanation and say it from the word alone**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is a word learned?**
**Answer: When you can explain it in your own words**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the glossary's line, if not the goal?**
**Answer: The check**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do the moment a word stops you?**
**Answer: Look it up in the glossary at once**
