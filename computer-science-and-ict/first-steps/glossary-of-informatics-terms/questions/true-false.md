# Glossary of Informatics Terms — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a glossary?" is: The book's own dictionary — its words in alphabetical order with short explanations**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How are glossary words ordered?" is: System unit and desktop (any two of the year's new words)**
**Answer: False** — Alphabetically

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name two words the Grade 5 glossary explains." is: System unit and desktop (any two of the year's new words)**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why not search the whole book for a word?" is: After the last lesson, at printed p. 67**
**Answer: False** — The glossary gives the explanation in one short line

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where is the glossary printed in the Grade 5 book?" is: After the last lesson, at printed p. 67**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name the three moments to use a glossary." is: Cover the explanation and say it from the word alone**
**Answer: False** — Before a lesson, during it, and after it

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How do you test yourself with a glossary?" is: Cover the explanation and say it from the word alone**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is a word learned?" is: The check**
**Answer: False** — When you can explain it in your own words

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the glossary's line, if not the goal?" is: The check**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do you do the moment a word stops you?" is: The book's own dictionary — its words in alphabetical order with short explanations**
**Answer: False** — Look it up in the glossary at once
