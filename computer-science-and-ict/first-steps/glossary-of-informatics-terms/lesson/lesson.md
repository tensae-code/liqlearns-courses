# Glossary of Informatics Terms

## Do it first

At the back of Uzbekistan's Grade 5 book sits a glossary — every new word of the year, explained in one short line. This lesson teaches how to use it, and why it is there.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a glossary is

A **glossary** is the book's own dictionary: an alphabetical list of the subject's words, each with a short explanation in plain words. Informatics brings many new words in one year — system unit, desktop, file, program — and the glossary is where a pupil checks a word without searching the whole book. Uzbekistan's Grade 5 book prints its glossary after the last lesson, at printed p. 67.

## Sub lesson 2 — Using the glossary well

Use the glossary three ways: before a lesson, to meet its new words; during a lesson, the moment a word stops you; and after, to test yourself — cover the explanation and say it from the word alone. A word you can explain in your own words is learned; the glossary's line is the check, not the goal.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: A **glossary** is the book's own dictionary: an alphabetical list of the subject's words, each with a short explanation in plain words. Use the glossary three ways: before a lesson, to meet its new words; during a lesson, the moment a word stops you; and after, to test yourself — cover the explanation and say it from the word alone. Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.
