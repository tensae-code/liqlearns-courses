# Video script — Characteristics of computers — speed, accuracy, diligence, versatility, reliability, automatic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Characteristics of computers.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: the first six characteristics of a computer: speed, accuracy, diligence, versatility, reliability, and working automatically once set going.

**Speed and accuracy.** Speed: a computer works at millions of instructions per second, and its operations are timed in microseconds and nanoseconds — millionths and billionths of a second.
**Diligence, versatility, reliability.** Diligence: a computer never gets tired — no fatigue, no loss of concentration, at hour one or hour ten.
**Automatic.** Automatic: once a task is set going, a computer performs it without manual intervention — nobody has to push each step along.

Now you: Draw six small panels, one symbol per characteristic: a lightning flash for speed, a target for accuracy, and so on.
