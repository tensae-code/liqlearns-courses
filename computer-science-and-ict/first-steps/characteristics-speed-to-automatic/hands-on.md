# Hands-on — Characteristics of computers — speed, accuracy, diligence, versatility, reliability, automatic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tireless test
Add a column of twenty numbers. Time yourself, check your mistakes, rest, and do it again when tired. Compare with what diligence means for a machine that never tires.

## Same data, same answer
Ask a calculator the same sum five times across a day. Same result every time — that is reliability, felt in your own hands.
