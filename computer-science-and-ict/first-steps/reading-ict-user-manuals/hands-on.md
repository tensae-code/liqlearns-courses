# Hands-on — Reading ICT User Manuals

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Use a real manual

Take the manual of a tool in your home or school. Ask one real question about that tool. Find, through the contents list, the page that answers it, and write the answer in your own words — with the page you found it on.

## Follow it exactly

Choose one short job in the manual. Do it, step by step in the manual's order, ticking each step as you finish it. At the end, write whether the job worked — and if it did not, which step was missed.
