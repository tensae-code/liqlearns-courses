It is one whole lesson with no sub lessons — a short, single-skill lesson: finding and following a manual is one skill, practised whole.

Uganda's own way: a slim activity-led prototype — every topic is an "Activity" learners do (assembling a real desktop, managing the Recycle Bin, word-processing a letter as the integration task), under Uganda's revised Lower Secondary Curriculum. This lesson keeps that shape: the learner does the thing with a real tool or document in front of them.
