# Reading ICT User Manuals — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. The instruction book that comes with an ICT tool is called its user ____.**
**Answer: manual**

**2. At the front of a manual, the ____ list shows its chapters and headings.**
**Answer: contents**

**3. The commonest reason a job done from a manual fails is ____ a step.**
**Answer: skipping**
