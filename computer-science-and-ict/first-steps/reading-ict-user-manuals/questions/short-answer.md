# Reading ICT User Manuals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. A printer will not take paper and nobody is free to help. Show, step by step, how its manual helps you.**
**Answer:** A sound answer: name the question (paper feeding), find the place through the contents under loading paper or trouble-shooting, follow the steps in order, obey any warning about hands inside the machine, and check whether the job worked.

**2. Why does this lesson say a learner who can read manuals 'owns every new tool that arrives'?**
**Answer:** Because every new tool arrives with its own instruction book: the learner who can find and follow it can set the tool up and use it without depending on someone else knowing it first.
