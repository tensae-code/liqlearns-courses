# Hands-on — Digital Devices — and the Commands They Follow

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Device hunt** — Walk through your home or classroom and list every digital device you find; for each one, say one command it follows.

## Task two
**Be the processor** — A partner gives you simple commands — stand, turn, take two steps. Carry out only the commands you understand exactly, and say what happens to an unclear one.
