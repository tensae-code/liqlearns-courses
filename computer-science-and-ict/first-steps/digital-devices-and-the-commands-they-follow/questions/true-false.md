# Digital Devices — and the Commands They Follow — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A machine that works with information and follows commands**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A machine that works with information and follows commands** — as the answer to: Name two digital devices from the lesson.
**Answer: False — The television and the washing machine (also: the phone, the tablet)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It gives no commands and takes none — it does not work with information**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It gives no commands and takes none — it does not work with information** — as the answer to: What does a digital device do with information?
**Answer: False — It receives it, works on it, and answers — with a picture, a sound or an action**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Ana and Ștefan**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Ana and Ștefan** — as the answer to: What is the processor?
**Answer: False — The working heart inside a digital device that receives commands and executes them**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The processor receives the command and sets the music going**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The processor receives the command and sets the music going** — as the answer to: What does a washing machine's processor do?
**Answer: False — It counts the minutes and turns the drum through the programme**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: It is not carried out at all**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: It is not carried out at all** — as the answer to: Do devices guess what a command means?
**Answer: False — No — they do exactly what they are told, nothing guessed**
