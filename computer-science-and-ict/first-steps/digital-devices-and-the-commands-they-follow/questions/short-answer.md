# Digital Devices — and the Commands They Follow — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a digital device?**
**Answer: A machine that works with information and follows commands**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two digital devices from the lesson.**
**Answer: The television and the washing machine (also: the phone, the tablet)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is a chair not a digital device?**
**Answer: It gives no commands and takes none — it does not work with information**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a digital device do with information?**
**Answer: It receives it, works on it, and answers — with a picture, a sound or an action**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Who are the book's two guides?**
**Answer: Ana and Ștefan**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the processor?**
**Answer: The working heart inside a digital device that receives commands and executes them**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when you press 'play'?**
**Answer: The processor receives the command and sets the music going**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a washing machine's processor do?**
**Answer: It counts the minutes and turns the drum through the programme**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to a command the processor does not know?**
**Answer: It is not carried out at all**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Do devices guess what a command means?**
**Answer: No — they do exactly what they are told, nothing guessed**
