# Video script — Digital Devices — and the Commands They Follow

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **digital device** is a machine that works with information and follows commands: the television shows the channel you choose, the washing machine washes the programme you set, the phone rings, photographs and plays. Ana and Ștefan, the book's two guides, sort the devices of a home into digital and not-digital: a chair gives no commands and takes none; a tablet does both. The mark of a digital device is that it **receives information, works on it, and answers** — with a picture, a sound or an action.
Inside every digital device sits a **processor** — its working heart. The processor **receives a command and executes it**: you press 'play' and the processor sets the music going; the washing machine's processor counts the minutes and turns the drum. A command the processor does not know is not carried out at all — devices do exactly what they are told, nothing more and nothing guessed. That is why the order and the clarity of commands matter, in this book's later lessons and in every program after.
