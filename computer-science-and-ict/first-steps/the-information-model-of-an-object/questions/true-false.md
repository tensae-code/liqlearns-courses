# The Information Model of an Object — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is an object's information model?" is: The organised information that stands for the object**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What form does the book choose for it?" is: The object's name; its properties**
**Answer: False** — A table of properties

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What stands at the table's head, and what fills its rows?" is: The object's name; its properties**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why is the table a model?" is: Be compared, sorted and studied at a desk**
**Answer: False** — It keeps what the purpose needs and drops the rest

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What can a table do that the living animal cannot?" is: Be compared, sorted and studied at a desk**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What are the five-snout livestock?" is: The tables can be laid side by side and read against each other**
**Answer: False** — Horse, camel, cow, sheep and goat

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does modelling all five in one form allow?" is: The tables can be laid side by side and read against each other**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name questions the five tables answer together." is: Cat, bird, human and snakes**
**Answer: False** — Which gives milk, which carries, which survives the cold (any two)

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name the book's four opening examples." is: Cat, bird, human and snakes**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the table's power, in the closing phrase?" is: The organised information that stands for the object**
**Answer: False** — One form makes unlike things comparable
