# The Information Model of an Object — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an object's information model?**
a) The organised information that stands for the object  b) A table of properties  c) The object's name; its properties  d) It keeps what the purpose needs and drops the rest
**Answer: a) The organised information that stands for the object**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What form does the book choose for it?**
a) The object's name; its properties  b) A table of properties  c) It keeps what the purpose needs and drops the rest  d) Be compared, sorted and studied at a desk
**Answer: b) A table of properties**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What stands at the table's head, and what fills its rows?**
a) It keeps what the purpose needs and drops the rest  b) Be compared, sorted and studied at a desk  c) The object's name; its properties  d) Horse, camel, cow, sheep and goat
**Answer: c) The object's name; its properties**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is the table a model?**
a) Be compared, sorted and studied at a desk  b) Horse, camel, cow, sheep and goat  c) The tables can be laid side by side and read against each other  d) It keeps what the purpose needs and drops the rest
**Answer: d) It keeps what the purpose needs and drops the rest**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What can a table do that the living animal cannot?**
a) Be compared, sorted and studied at a desk  b) Horse, camel, cow, sheep and goat  c) The tables can be laid side by side and read against each other  d) Which gives milk, which carries, which survives the cold (any two)
**Answer: a) Be compared, sorted and studied at a desk**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are the five-snout livestock?**
a) The tables can be laid side by side and read against each other  b) Horse, camel, cow, sheep and goat  c) Which gives milk, which carries, which survives the cold (any two)  d) Cat, bird, human and snakes

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does modelling all five in one form allow?**
a) Which gives milk, which carries, which survives the cold (any two)  b) Cat, bird, human and snakes  c) The tables can be laid side by side and read against each other  d) One form makes unlike things comparable

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name questions the five tables answer together.**
a) Cat, bird, human and snakes  b) One form makes unlike things comparable  c) The organised information that stands for the object  d) Which gives milk, which carries, which survives the cold (any two)
**Answer: d) Which gives milk, which carries, which survives the cold (any two)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name the book's four opening examples.**
a) Cat, bird, human and snakes  b) One form makes unlike things comparable  c) The organised information that stands for the object  d) A table of properties
**Answer: a) Cat, bird, human and snakes**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the table's power, in the closing phrase?**
