# Video script — The Information Model of an Object

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An object's **information model** is the organised information that stands for it. The book's chosen form is the **table of properties**: the object's name at the head, its properties in the rows — for the **cat**: its size, coat, voice, habits; for the **bird**: wing, flight, song; for the **human**: height, language, work; for the **snakes**: length, venom, movement. The table is a model because it keeps what the purpose needs and drops the rest: the living animal stays outside; its table can be compared, sorted and studied at a desk.
The lesson's own modelling is Mongolia's wealth: the **five-snout livestock** (таван хошуу мал) — horse, camel, cow, sheep, goat — each modelled in its property table, so the five tables can be laid side by side and read against each other: which gives milk, which carries, which survives the dzud's cold. One form for every object makes unlike things comparable — that is the table's power, and the reason the whole modelling strand of the Mongolian books keeps returning to it.
