# Hands-on — The Information Model of an Object

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Model an animal', 'Build a property table for an animal you know well: at least six properties, each with the value it takes for your animal.')

## Task two
('Five tables, one question', 'Using the five-snout tables, answer in writing: which animal would you choose for milk in winter, and which for a long journey? Defend both from the tables alone.')
