# Hands-on — Adding and Subtracting in Binary

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Add and check
Add 110 + 101 in binary in columns, marking every carry. Then turn both numbers and your answer into decimal and check the sum.

## Subtract and check
Subtract 1010 − 110 in binary, marking the borrow. Check in decimal, then undo it: add your answer back to 110 in binary and see whether you return to 1010.
