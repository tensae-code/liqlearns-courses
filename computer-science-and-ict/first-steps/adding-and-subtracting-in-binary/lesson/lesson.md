# Adding and Subtracting in Binary

## Do it first

You can already add in decimal by columns, carrying when a column overflows. Binary asks for the same skill with only 0 and 1. In this lesson you add and subtract in binary, column by column.

## Sub lesson 1 — Adding in binary — carry the two

Add column by column from the right, exactly as in decimal. The only sums are: 0 + 0 = **0**, 0 + 1 = **1**, and 1 + 1 = **10 in binary** — write **0** and **carry 1** into the next column, just as 5 + 5 carries in decimal. Where a carry arrives, add three digits: 1 + 1 + 1 = **11 in binary** — write 1, carry 1. Example: 101 + 11 = 1000 (5 + 3 = 8 — check it in decimal).

## Sub lesson 2 — Subtracting in binary — borrow a two

Subtract column by column from the right: 0 − 0 = **0**, 1 − 0 = **1**, 1 − 1 = **0**. The hard case is 0 − 1: **borrow** from the next column — the borrowed 1 is worth **2** in your column (a whole place), so 2 − 1 = **1**, and the lending column drops by one. Example: 101 − 11 = 10 (5 − 3 = 2). Again the decimal check proves the binary work.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: Binary arithmetic follows the same column rules as decimal, with a smaller digit set: adding works with carries when 1 + 1 makes 10 in binary, and subtracting works with borrows when a 0 must take from the column beside it. Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.

## Do It Yourself

1. Show it, don't just say it: At the right-hand column, as in decimal. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
2. Show it, don't just say it: 10 — write 0 and carry 1. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
3. Show it, don't just say it: 0. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
4. Show it, don't just say it: Borrow from the next column. Do this with a real example of your own and write or say how you know it is right.
