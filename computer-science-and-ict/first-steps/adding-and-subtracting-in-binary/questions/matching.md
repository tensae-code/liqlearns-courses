9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a borrowed 1 worth in your column?
10. (Sub lesson 2) What is 101 − 11 in binary — and its decimal check?

a) 0
b) 0 − 1 — there is nothing to take from
c) Borrow from the next column
d) 2 — one whole place
e) 10 — because 5 − 3 = 2
f) At the right-hand column, as in decimal
g) 1
h) 10 — write 0 and carry 1
i) 11 in binary — write 1 and carry 1
