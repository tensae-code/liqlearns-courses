# Adding and Subtracting in Binary — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where do you start a binary addition?**
a) At the right-hand column, as in decimal  b) 1  c) 10 — write 0 and carry 1  d) 11 in binary — write 1 and carry 1
**Answer: a) At the right-hand column, as in decimal**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is 0 + 1 in binary?**
a) 10 — write 0 and carry 1  b) 1  c) 11 in binary — write 1 and carry 1  d) 1000 — because 5 + 3 = 8
**Answer: b) 1**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is 1 + 1 in binary?**
a) 11 in binary — write 1 and carry 1  b) 1000 — because 5 + 3 = 8  c) 10 — write 0 and carry 1  d) 0
**Answer: c) 10 — write 0 and carry 1**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is 1 + 1 + 1, when a carry arrives?**
a) 1000 — because 5 + 3 = 8  b) 0  c) 0 − 1 — there is nothing to take from  d) 11 in binary — write 1 and carry 1
**Answer: d) 11 in binary — write 1 and carry 1**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is 101 + 11 in binary — and its decimal check?**
a) 1000 — because 5 + 3 = 8  b) 0  c) 0 − 1 — there is nothing to take from  d) Borrow from the next column
**Answer: a) 1000 — because 5 + 3 = 8**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is 1 − 1 in binary?**
a) 0 − 1 — there is nothing to take from  b) 0  c) Borrow from the next column  d) 2 — one whole place
**Answer: b) 0**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the hard case in binary subtraction?**
a) Borrow from the next column  b) 2 — one whole place  c) 0 − 1 — there is nothing to take from  d) 10 — because 5 − 3 = 2
**Answer: c) 0 − 1 — there is nothing to take from**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do for 0 − 1?**
a) 2 — one whole place  b) 10 — because 5 − 3 = 2  c) At the right-hand column, as in decimal  d) Borrow from the next column
**Answer: d) Borrow from the next column**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a borrowed 1 worth in your column?**
a) 2 — one whole place  b) 10 — because 5 − 3 = 2  c) At the right-hand column, as in decimal  d) 1
**Answer: a) 2 — one whole place**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is 101 − 11 in binary — and its decimal check?**
a) At the right-hand column, as in decimal  b) 10 — because 5 − 3 = 2  c) 1  d) 10 — write 0 and carry 1
**Answer: b) 10 — because 5 − 3 = 2**
