# Hands-on — Kinds of Data — Numbers, Letters, Pictures

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort real data
Collect ten real items — marks, names, prices, photos, sentences — and sort them into three piles: numbers, letters, pictures. Say the rule you used for any item that was hard to place.

## One record, three kinds
Draw one student record card holding a name, a mark and a photo space. Label each part with its kind of data and say what the computer can do with each part.
