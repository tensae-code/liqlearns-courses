# Kinds of Data — Numbers, Letters, Pictures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the three kinds of data in the book's first classification?**
a) Numbers, letters, and pictures  b) Data made of numbers — a mark, a price, a count  c) Calculate with it  d) Data made of letters and words — a name, an address, a sentence
**Answer: a) Numbers, letters, and pictures**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is numeric data?**
a) Calculate with it  b) Data made of numbers — a mark, a price, a count  c) Data made of letters and words — a name, an address, a sentence  d) Because a number stored as letters cannot be calculated with
**Answer: b) Data made of numbers — a mark, a price, a count**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What can the computer do with numeric data?**
a) Data made of letters and words — a name, an address, a sentence  b) Because a number stored as letters cannot be calculated with  c) Calculate with it  d) Photographs, drawings and scanned pages — data you take in by seeing
**Answer: c) Calculate with it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is alphabetic data?**
a) Because a number stored as letters cannot be calculated with  b) Photographs, drawings and scanned pages — data you take in by seeing  c) By seeing, not by reading symbols  d) Data made of letters and words — a name, an address, a sentence
**Answer: d) Data made of letters and words — a name, an address, a sentence**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why must a number not be stored as letters?**
a) Because a number stored as letters cannot be calculated with  b) Photographs, drawings and scanned pages — data you take in by seeing  c) By seeing, not by reading symbols  d) Far more storage
**Answer: a) Because a number stored as letters cannot be calculated with**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is picture data?**
a) By seeing, not by reading symbols  b) Photographs, drawings and scanned pages — data you take in by seeing  c) Far more storage  d) On the screen, or in print
**Answer: b) Photographs, drawings and scanned pages — data you take in by seeing**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is a picture taken in differently from a name?**
a) Far more storage  b) On the screen, or in print  c) By seeing, not by reading symbols  d) Sound and moving pictures
**Answer: c) By seeing, not by reading symbols**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How much storage does a picture take compared with a name?**
a) On the screen, or in print  b) Sound and moving pictures  c) Numbers, letters, and pictures  d) Far more storage
**Answer: d) Far more storage**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the computer show picture data back?**
a) On the screen, or in print  b) Sound and moving pictures  c) Numbers, letters, and pictures  d) Data made of numbers — a mark, a price, a count
**Answer: a) On the screen, or in print**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What further kinds stand beside still pictures?**
a) Numbers, letters, and pictures  b) Sound and moving pictures  c) Data made of numbers — a mark, a price, a count  d) Calculate with it
**Answer: b) Sound and moving pictures**
