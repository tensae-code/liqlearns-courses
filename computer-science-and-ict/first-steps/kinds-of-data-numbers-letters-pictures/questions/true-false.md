# Kinds of Data — Numbers, Letters, Pictures — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are the three kinds of data in the book's first classification?" is: Numbers, letters, and pictures**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is numeric data?" is: Calculate with it**
**Answer: False** — Data made of numbers — a mark, a price, a count

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What can the computer do with numeric data?" is: Calculate with it**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is alphabetic data?" is: Because a number stored as letters cannot be calculated with**
**Answer: False** — Data made of letters and words — a name, an address, a sentence

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why must a number not be stored as letters?" is: Because a number stored as letters cannot be calculated with**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is picture data?" is: By seeing, not by reading symbols**
**Answer: False** — Photographs, drawings and scanned pages — data you take in by seeing

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is a picture taken in differently from a name?" is: By seeing, not by reading symbols**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How much storage does a picture take compared with a name?" is: On the screen, or in print**
**Answer: False** — Far more storage

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does the computer show picture data back?" is: On the screen, or in print**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What further kinds stand beside still pictures?" is: Numbers, letters, and pictures**
**Answer: False** — Sound and moving pictures
