# Converting Decimal to Octal — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do you divide by, again and again?**
**Answer: By 8 — the octal base**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do you write down at each division?**
**Answer: The remainder — always a digit from 0 to 7**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When do the divisions stop?**
**Answer: When the answer reaches 0**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) For 25, what are the divisions?**
**Answer: 25 ÷ 8 = 3 rem 1; then 3 ÷ 8 = 0 rem 3**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In what order are the remainders read?**
**Answer: From last to first — upwards**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is 25 in octal?**
**Answer: 31₈**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which remainder becomes the first octal digit?**
**Answer: The last remainder found — the biggest place**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do you check the answer?**
**Answer: By place value — 31₈ = 3×8 + 1 = 25**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is 10 in octal?**
**Answer: 12₈ — 10 ÷ 8 = 1 rem 2, then 1 ÷ 8 = 0 rem 1, read upwards**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a failed check mean?**
**Answer: A division or the reading direction went wrong — work it again**
