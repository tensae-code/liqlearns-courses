# Converting Decimal to Octal — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right. Write the letter beside each number.

1. (Sub lesson 1) What do you divide by, again and again?
2. (Sub lesson 1) What do you write down at each division?
3. (Sub lesson 1) When do the divisions stop?
4. (Sub lesson 1) For 25, what are the divisions?
5. (Sub lesson 1) In what order are the remainders read?
6. (Sub lesson 2) What is 25 in octal?
7. (Sub lesson 2) Which remainder becomes the first octal digit?
8. (Sub lesson 2) How do you check the answer?
9. (Sub lesson 2) What is 10 in octal?
10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a failed check mean?

a) The remainder — always a digit from 0 to 7
b) 25 ÷ 8 = 3 rem 1; then 3 ÷ 8 = 0 rem 3
c) By 8 — the octal base
d) From last to first — upwards
e) When the answer reaches 0
f) The last remainder found — the biggest place
g) 12₈ — 10 ÷ 8 = 1 rem 2, then 1 ÷ 8 = 0 rem 1, read upwards
h) 31₈
i) A division or the reading direction went wrong — work it again
j) By place value — 31₈ = 3×8 + 1 = 25
