Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Kazakhstan, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked for the slug and the live lesson content was checked for near matches before building. It sits in the First Steps title (first-steps/), following the book's own order. Provenance scope note: the placements stocked here are from Kazakhstan's okulyk.kz (OKULYK) textbooks, recommended by the Ministry of Education and Science of the Republic of Kazakhstan — the Digital Literacy and Informatika lines of the Almatykitap, Arman-PV and Atamura teams and the Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools (NIS) books, all student books except the two Kadirkulov teacher guides named in the entries below, whose placements are stocked exactly as filed. Many books exist in parallel language editions (Kazakh / Russian / Uzbek / Uyghur / English) of the same textbook: each distinct book is taught once, and every distinct edition is named and cited in the book entry. Kazakhstan's own ways are kept: one textbook line in up to five parallel language editions, the okulyk.kz digital library as the books' home, Scratch from Grade 1 and robotics strands (LEGO WeDo 2.0 and Mindstorms EV3) from Grade 1–2, and every keyword given in Kazakh–Russian–English in the early grades. Media slots in lesson/lesson.md are described slots only ([Picture — …]); no picture numbers are invented.


Kazakhstan's own way (Kopeeva G5 Informatika): Grade 5 Informatika (the older, pre-"Digital Literacy" subject name) is built around Scratch as a "game programming environment": a whole chapter runs from a first program through blocks, animation graphics, sound effects and dialogue to linear/branching/cyclic commands; earlier chapters pair Internet safety with data protection, and the year ends with print-ready project work — a Word newspaper page with WordArt and columns, presented as slides.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kopeeva G3 ICT): the older Grade 3 "ICT" subject (not Digital Literacy): no programming or robotics at all — the year is computer basics, WordPad-style text work, sound recording, a long Paint strand (tools, shapes, repeating patterns, palette, printing, mixed text-and-picture documents), Internet safety and search, and a full presentation strand ending with sound inside slides.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kadirkulov G5 Informatika): Grade 5 Informatika by the Almatykitap team drops Scratch entirely: information theory first (transmission, encryption, binary, graphics in binary code), then raster work in Paint against vector work with curves, and half the year is EV3 robotics beyond simple movement — the gyroscopic sensor, line following and robo-sumo competitions.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kadirkulov G3 ICT): the Almatykitap team's Grade 3 ICT runs the same eight cross-cutting themes as the Arman-PV book (Living nature; What is good, what is bad; Time; Architecture; Art; Outstanding personalities; Water — source of life; Culture of rest. Holidays) but as 34 short lessons with named mini-project defences ("My idol"); the glossary names Tux Paint as the children's raster editor and WordPad as the text editor. The mixed review lesson 21 ("Creative tasks: searching, typing, drawing", printed p. 53) is not placed.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kobdikova G5 Informatika): the Arman-PV Grade 5 book teaches the same five national sections as the other two publishers, but every second paragraph is a practicum or a "creating a project" lesson, and the safety section ends in cloud skills — placing, changing and downloading shared-access files — plus mini-projects on setting passwords on documents and preparing a document for print. The vector editor is Inkscape (named in the glossary), the raster editor Paint.

Kazakhstan's own way (Mukhametzhanova G5 Informatika, 2020 edition): the Atamura team's 2020 Grade 5 book follows the same five national sections in numbered-paragraph form (1.1–5.5) with end-of-section final tasks; its graphics strand works in Paint for raster and Inkscape for vector (canvas size, SVG, Bezier pen), and the introduction also promises a first taste of 3D technologies (3D model, 3D editor, 3D printing) inside the graphics work.

Kazakhstan's own way (Mukhametzhanova G5 Informatika, 2017 edition): the 2017 Atamura Grade 5 book follows the older national programme shared with the Arman-PV book of the same year: computer safety, information and raster graphics, algorithms through labyrinths (real and virtual), a first Scratch program and "living graphics", and project work ending in a print-ready document; a full Scratch block dictionary is printed as an appendix (printed pp. 119–127). The book was written with Microsoft "Partnership in Education" material.

Kazakhstan's own way (Vyshkova & Zhurbenko G7 Informatics, NIS): the Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools course is a different programme from the national one: business documents, databases inside the information-processing part, logo design and GIF animation in raster graphics, a whole chapter on modelling the world (including forecasting social problems from data), client–server networks, utilities, and linear-structure programming ending in building your own calculator. Lessons run two pages each with research-style tasks.

Kazakhstan's own way (Shaniyev team G9 «INFORMATICS»): the Astana-kitap Grade 9 English-medium book is where Kazakhstan teaches databases properly — design, build, sort/filter, pivot tables and charts, ending in a "family shop" mini project — and then spends two chapters on Python lists (one- and two-dimensional, with search/swap/sort/insert/delete) and a full PyGame 2D games chapter.

Kazakhstan's own way (Mukametzhanova G5 «Информатика» 2017 edition): the 2017 Atamura Grade 5 book opens with computer safety and a mini-project «Открытия, изменившие мир» (Discoveries that changed the world), teaches algorithms as following commands and escaping labyrinths (real and virtual), and the first program in Scratch with "graphics come alive".

Kazakhstan's own way (Mukametzhanova G5 «Информатика» 2020 edition): the 2020 Atamura revision of the Grade 5 book is rebuilt on the renewed programme — the same five sections as the Almatykitap book (Book 18): presenting information with encryption and binary, raster and vector graphics with curves, robotics with the gyroscope sensor and turns, robot competitions (line following and robo-sumo), and computer safety moved to the end.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kobdikova G5 «Информатика»): the Arman-PV Grade 5 book is the practicum twin of the same programme as Books 18 and 35 — nearly every unit comes in a theory + practice pair, and it adds a block the others lack: sharing files (placing, changing and downloading shared files), setting a password on documents, and organising a document effectively for print, taught as "mini-project works".

Kazakhstan's own way (Mukametzhanova G9 «Информатика»): the Atamura Grade 9 book works with Google services for collaborative documents, builds the database inside a spreadsheet, and its database section separates searching methods from sorting and filtering before the Python arrays and PyGame chapters.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kolyeva G9 «Информатика»): the Mektep Grade 9 book is the compact version of the state programme — five chapters only, with the "choosing a computer" chapter separating configuration, software choice and the final choice itself, and the PyGame chapter compressed into three double units.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kadirkulov G9 «Информатика»): the Almatykitap Grade 9 book gives Google Drive collaborative editing its own unit, splits sorting from filtering and adds an advanced-filter unit in the database section, separates array input/output and the "main parameters" of a two-dimensional array into their own units, and ends the PyGame chapter with sprite collision detection and a football game.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kadirkulov G3 «Ақпараттық-коммуникациялық технологиялар»): the older Almatykitap Grade 3 ICT book (2018, a different book from the 2021 Digital Literacy, Book 23) is organised by cross-curricular themes — Living Nature, What is Good and What is Bad, Time, Architecture, Art, Outstanding People, Water is the Source of Life, Culture of Rest — and teaches sound (playback, effects, recording, processing) and the graphics editor inside them, ending every theme with a defended mini-project.
