# Hands-on — Hybrid computers — analog and digital together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pump watch
If you pass a petrol station, watch a pump display while fuel flows (from a safe place, with an adult). The litres climb smoothly, the price counts exactly — name the analog part and the digital part.

## Hybrid or not?
Cards: speedometer alone, petrol pump, phone, hospital monitor that turns body signals into numbers, mercury thermometer. Sort into hybrid and not-hybrid, and defend each.
