# Hybrid computers — analog and digital together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A hybrid computer has characteristics of —
a) Only analog  b) Only digital  c) Both analog and digital  d) Neither
**Answer: c) Both analog and digital** — A hybrid has characteristics of both analog and digital computers.

**2.** A hybrid is —
a) Slow like analog, rough like digital  b) Fast like analog, accurate like digital  c) Only a printer  d) A type of keyboard
**Answer: b) Fast like analog, accurate like digital** — Fast like analog, accurate like digital.

**3.** Before processing, a hybrid converts —
a) Digital signals into analog  b) Analog signals into digital form  c) Text into pictures  d) Sound into light
**Answer: b) Analog signals into digital form** — It converts analog signals into digital form before processing.

**4.** The book's hybrid example is —
a) A word processor  b) The petrol-pump processor  c) A school roster  d) A game
**Answer: b) The petrol-pump processor** — The petrol-pump processor turns fuel-flow measurements into quantity and price.

**5.** Hybrids are also used in —
a) Aeroplanes, hospitals and scientific work  b) Only kitchens  c) Only farms  d) Only games
**Answer: a) Aeroplanes, hospitals and scientific work** — Hybrids are used in aeroplanes, hospitals and scientific work.
