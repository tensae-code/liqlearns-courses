**Hybrid computers.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: a hybrid computer joins both worlds: fast like analog, accurate like digital, converting analog signals into digital form before processing — as the petrol pump does with fuel flow, quantity and price.

**Both characteristics in one machine.** A hybrid computer has characteristics of both analog and digital computers: it is fast like analog and accurate like digital, and it is able to process both continuous and discrete data.
**The petrol-pump example.** The book's example is the petrol-pump processor: it turns fuel-flow measurements — smooth, continuous — into quantity and price — exact digital numbers.

Now you: Draw a petrol pump with two displays — litres and price — rising together.
