# Hybrid computers — analog and digital together

## Do it first

At a petrol station, fuel flows smoothly — that is continuous. The pump's display shows litres and price in exact numbers — that is discrete. Before reading on, say how one machine can be both.

## Both characteristics in one machine

A hybrid computer has characteristics of both analog and digital computers: it is fast like analog and accurate like digital, and it is able to process both continuous and discrete data. Before processing, it converts analog signals into digital form.

## The petrol-pump example

The book's example is the petrol-pump processor: it turns fuel-flow measurements — smooth, continuous — into quantity and price — exact digital numbers. Watch the GIF: as the fuel flows, the litres display and the price display rise together. Hybrids are also used in aeroplanes, hospitals and scientific work.
