# Hands-on — Output — showing the result

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three-way output
Take any result you produced in the earlier lessons and present it three ways: spoken, shown, and written out on paper. A partner names the output device each time.

## Silent computer
Mime using a computer with the screen covered and sound off. Your partner explains what is missing and why no work can be judged without output.
