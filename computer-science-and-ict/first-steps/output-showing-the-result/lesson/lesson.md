# Output — showing the result

## Do it first

The roster is totalled and ranked inside the computer, but the screen is off and no printer is connected. Does anyone know who came top? Say what is missing — that missing step is this lesson.

## Output — the result reaches the user

Output is showing the result of processing to the user. A result locked inside the machine helps nobody; output is the moment the user sees it, hears it or holds it.

[Picture 10910 — output]

## Three ways out — screen, sound, paper

The result can come through a monitor (seen on screen), a speaker (heard as sound), or a printer (held as a printed page). The book's example: the processed roster, printed on paper — the same roster from the processing lesson, now in the teacher's hand.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what output is, and the three devices named for it. Which one gives a result you can hold?
