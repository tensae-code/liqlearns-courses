# Technology — tools people make to solve problems

## Do it first

Name a problem at home that used to be solved with hands alone — washing clothes, cooking, carrying water, sending word to a far village. Now name the tool or machine that solves it today. You have just defined this lesson's word. Read on and check your definition against the book's.

## Technology — tools people make to solve problems

Technology is the set of tools and machines humans create to solve problems. Notice the shape of the definition: a problem comes first, and people make something to solve it.

Technology is found everywhere people have problems to solve: at home, in industry, in business and in medicine. 

[Picture 10110 — a phone and a TV labelled as technology at home]

 The book's everyday examples are the mobile phone — solving the problem of talking across distance — and the television — solving the problem of seeing and hearing events far away.

So technology is wider than computers. A tool made by people, to solve a problem: that is the whole test.
