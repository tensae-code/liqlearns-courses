# Hands-on — Technology — tools people make to solve problems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Problem first
Write four problems — one at home, one in a workshop, one in a shop, one at a clinic. Beside each, write the tool or machine people made to solve it.

## Not technology?
Make a list of five things in your home that are NOT technology (a stone, rain, a tree). For each, say which part of the test it fails: made by people? made to solve a problem?
