# Lesson plan — Technology — tools people make to solve problems

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Technology — tools people make to solve problems
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Introduction to Information & Communication Technology — in the title First Steps on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Technology is the set of tools and machines humans create to solve problems — found at home, in industry, in business and in medicine — with the mobile phone and the television as the book's everyday examples.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on The five elements of an ICT system: technology is the fifth element, taken here in its own lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what technology is: tools and machines humans create to solve problems.
2. Name the four places the book finds technology: home, industry, business, medicine.
3. Test any object with the book's definition: was it made by people, to solve a problem?

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Name a problem at home that used to be solved with hands alone — washing clothes, cooking, carrying water, sending word to a far village. Now name the tool or machine that solves it today. You have just defined this lesson's word. Read on and check your definition against the book's. (lesson/lesson.md).
