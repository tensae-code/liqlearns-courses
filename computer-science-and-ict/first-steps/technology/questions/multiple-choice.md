# Technology — tools people make to solve problems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Technology is —**
a) anything old  b) the set of tools and machines humans create to solve problems  c) only computers
**Answer: b) the set of tools and machines humans create to solve problems**

**2. The book's everyday examples of technology at home are —**
a) the mobile phone and the television  b) a broom and a pot only  c) rain and sunlight
**Answer: a) the mobile phone and the television**

**3. Technology is found in —**
a) the home only  b) home, industry, business and medicine  c) schools only
**Answer: b) home, industry, business and medicine**

**4. In the book's definition, what comes first?**
a) The machine  b) The problem it solves  c) The shop that sells it
**Answer: b) The problem it solves** — People create tools to solve problems — problem first.

**5. Which passes the book's test for technology?**
a) A river  b) A tool people made to solve a problem  c) A mountain
**Answer: b) A tool people made to solve a problem**
