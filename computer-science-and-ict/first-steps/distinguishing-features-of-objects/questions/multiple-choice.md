# Distinguishing Features of Objects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A distinguishing feature is —**
a) a feature every object has  b) the feature that makes one object different from another  c) the name of an object
**Answer: b) the feature that makes one object different from another** — It tells one object apart from the others.

**2. Two yellow pencils: one sharp, one blunt. The distinguishing feature of the first is —**
a) yellow  b) sharp  c) long
**Answer: b) sharp** — Both are yellow and long; only one is sharp.

**3. To pick out one exact object, look for —**
a) its distinguishing feature  b) any feature  c) its group
**Answer: a) its distinguishing feature** — The feature the others do not have.

**4. 'The cup with the chip' names a cup by —**
a) its colour  b) its distinguishing feature  c) its price
**Answer: b) its distinguishing feature** — The chip is what tells that cup apart.
