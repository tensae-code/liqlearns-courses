# Distinguishing Features of Objects — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. The feature that makes one object different is its ____ feature.**
**Answer: distinguishing**

**2. Two pencils can be alike, but one is sharp and the other is ____.**
**Answer: blunt**

**3. To ask for one exact object, name its ____ feature.**
**Answer: distinguishing**
