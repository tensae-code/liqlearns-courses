# Hands-on — From data to information

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Title it three ways
Write a list: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12. Give it three different fair titles. (Even numbers is one. Find two more the numbers truly fit.)

## Conclusion practice
Collect data for one day: the times the bus or the family leaves, arrives, eats. At the end of the day, interpret it: write one conclusion the data supports, in one sentence.
