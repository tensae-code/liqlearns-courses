# From data to information — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Information is —**
a) raw data  b) processed data — data put into context and given meaning  c) a computer
**Answer: b) processed data — data put into context and given meaning**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Interpreting data means —**
a) copying it out neatly  b) reviewing it through a process to reach a conclusion  c) deleting it
**Answer: b) reviewing it through a process to reach a conclusion**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) In the book's example, the numbers in the list turned out to be —**
a) prices  b) ages, ranging 14 to 20  c) house numbers
**Answer: b) ages, ranging 14 to 20**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) What changed when the list became information?**
a) The names in the list  b) The numbers in the list  c) The context around the list
**Answer: c) The context around the list** — Nothing in the list changed.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) A list titled "even numbers" for 2, 4, 6, 8 is —**
a) always wrong  b) a fair title the data truly fits  c) not information
**Answer: b) a fair title the data truly fits** — Many fair titles can be acceptable.
