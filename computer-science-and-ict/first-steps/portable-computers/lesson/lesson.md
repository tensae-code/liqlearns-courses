# Portable computers — laptop, notebook, tablet, smartphone

## Do it first

Four devices lie on a table: one folds open like a book, one is the size of a school exercise book, one is a flat screen you touch, one fits a pocket and makes calls. Before reading on, put them in size order, biggest first.

## Portable — small enough to carry

Portable PCs are small enough to carry in a handbag and use while travelling. They are still microcomputers — the same type as the desktop — made to move with their owner.

[Picture 10914 — portable computers]

## The four portables

A laptop has the same power as a desktop in a carryable size. A notebook is smaller still — the size of a notebook — but with desktop power. A tablet is a portable computer worked by touching its screen with a finger instead of a mouse, touchpad or keyboard, and it is bigger than a smartphone. A smartphone combines a mobile telephone and computing functions in one unit.
