# Video script — Portable computers — laptop, notebook, tablet, smartphone

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Portable computers.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: portable computers are microcomputers you can carry: the laptop, the smaller notebook, the touch-worked tablet, and the smartphone that joins a telephone and a computer in one unit.

**Portable — small enough to carry.** Portable PCs are small enough to carry in a handbag and use while travelling.
**The four portables.** A laptop has the same power as a desktop in a carryable size.

Now you: Draw the four portables in a size line-up, laptop down to smartphone, and label each.
