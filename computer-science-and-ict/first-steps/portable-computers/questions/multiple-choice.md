# Portable computers — laptop, notebook, tablet, smartphone — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Portable PCs are —
a) Fixed to one room  b) Small enough to carry in a handbag and use while travelling  c) Only for banks  d) Worked only standing up
**Answer: b) Small enough to carry in a handbag and use while travelling** — Portables are small enough to carry in a handbag and use while travelling.

**2.** Which portable has the same power as a desktop in a carryable size?
a) The tablet  b) The smartphone  c) The laptop  d) The calculator
**Answer: c) The laptop** — Laptops have the same power as a desktop in a carryable size.

**3.** A notebook computer is —
a) Bigger than a laptop  b) The size of a notebook, with desktop power  c) A paper book  d) A tablet with no screen
**Answer: b) The size of a notebook, with desktop power** — Notebooks are smaller still — the size of a notebook — with desktop power.

**4.** A tablet is worked by —
a) A mouse only  b) Touching its screen with a finger  c) Voice only  d) A light pen only
**Answer: b) Touching its screen with a finger** — A tablet is worked by touching its screen with a finger instead of mouse, touchpad or keyboard.

**5.** A smartphone combines —
a) A camera and a radio only  b) A mobile telephone and computing functions in one unit  c) A printer and a scanner  d) Two telephones
**Answer: b) A mobile telephone and computing functions in one unit** — A smartphone combines a mobile telephone and computing functions in one unit.
