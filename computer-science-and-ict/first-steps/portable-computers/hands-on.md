# Hands-on — Portable computers — laptop, notebook, tablet, smartphone

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Handbag test
Take a bag and test which real devices at home would fit and could be used on a journey. The ones that pass are portable — sort them into laptop, notebook, tablet, smartphone.

## Touch or keys?
For one hour, notice every device you see being used. Tally: worked by touch, by keys, or by both. Tablets and smartphones lead the touch column.
