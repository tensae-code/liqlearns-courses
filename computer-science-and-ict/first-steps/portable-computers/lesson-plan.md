# Lesson plan — Portable computers — laptop, notebook, tablet, smartphone

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Portable computers — laptop, notebook, tablet, smartphone
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 1 — First Steps of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 1 — Basics of Computer.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** portable computers are microcomputers you can carry: the laptop, the smaller notebook, the touch-worked tablet, and the smartphone that joins a telephone and a computer in one unit.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the microcomputer lesson: same family, smaller and carryable.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A real computer, phone or the devices named in the lesson help where the hands-on activities call for them, but every activity has a paper way through.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes notebook and laptop → the notebook is smaller — the size of a notebook — with the same desktop-level power claim.
- Thinks a tablet is worked with a mouse → a tablet is worked by touching its screen with a finger.
