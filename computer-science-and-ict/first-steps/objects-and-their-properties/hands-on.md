# Hands-on — Objects and Their Properties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Describe an object', 'Choose one object in the room. List six of its properties and the value each takes. Swap lists with a partner — can they name your object from the properties alone?')

## Task two
('Sort the class', 'Take eight objects and classify them two ways: by one property, then by another. Say which sorting tells you more, and why.')
