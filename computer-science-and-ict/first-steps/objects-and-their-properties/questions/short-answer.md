# Objects and Their Properties — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an object?**
**Answer: Any thing around us that can be named and pointed to**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are an object's properties?**
**Answer: The features by which it can be described and measured — its parameters**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give two properties of a book.**
**Answer: Size and number of pages (any two of: colour, weight)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How are two objects of the same kind told apart?**
**Answer: By the values their properties take**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is an object's environment?**
**Answer: The surroundings it belongs to and acts in**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is classification?**
**Answer: Putting objects with matching key properties into one class**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why classify objects?**
**Answer: So information about them can be stored, compared and reasoned with**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name an object the Mongolian book teaches through.**
**Answer: The Mazaalai (Gobi bear) (any one of: the ger, the national flag)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does describing an object mean?**
**Answer: Listing its properties and their values**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does this lesson stand in the book?**
**Answer: In the first unit of Mongolia's Grade 6 IT book, before the computer is introduced**
