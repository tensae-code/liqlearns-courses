# Objects and Their Properties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an object?**
a) Any thing around us that can be named and pointed to  b) The features by which it can be described and measured — its parameters  c) Size and number of pages (any two of: colour, weight)  d) By the values their properties take

a) Size and number of pages (any two of: colour, weight)  b) The features by which it can be described and measured — its parameters  c) By the values their properties take  d) The surroundings it belongs to and acts in
