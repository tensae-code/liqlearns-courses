# End-of-Year Review Tasks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are review tasks?**
a) Mixed exercises drawn from every topic of the year  b) The right tool themselves  c) The Grade 5 and Grade 6 Informatika books  d) Sixteen lessons (the review is the seventeenth)
**Answer: a) Mixed exercises drawn from every topic of the year**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must the pupil choose in a review task?**
a) The Grade 5 and Grade 6 Informatika books  b) The right tool themselves  c) Sixteen lessons (the review is the seventeenth)  d) So the pupil practises choosing, not only repeating
**Answer: b) The right tool themselves**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which two Uzbekistan books close with review tasks?**
a) Sixteen lessons (the review is the seventeenth)  b) So the pupil practises choosing, not only repeating  c) The Grade 5 and Grade 6 Informatika books  d) Every task you cannot start
**Answer: c) The Grade 5 and Grade 6 Informatika books**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How many lessons precede them in each book?**
a) So the pupil practises choosing, not only repeating  b) Every task you cannot start  c) The lesson to revisit  d) Sixteen lessons (the review is the seventeenth)
**Answer: d) Sixteen lessons (the review is the seventeenth)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why are the tasks mixed, not by topic?**
a) So the pupil practises choosing, not only repeating  b) Every task you cannot start  c) The lesson to revisit  d) Do the marked task again
**Answer: a) So the pupil practises choosing, not only repeating**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What should you mark while working a review?**
a) The lesson to revisit  b) Every task you cannot start  c) Do the marked task again  d) It is a map of the whole year
**Answer: b) Every task you cannot start**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a mark name?**
a) Do the marked task again  b) It is a map of the whole year  c) The lesson to revisit  d) The short list of what still needs work
**Answer: c) The lesson to revisit**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) After revisiting, what do you do?**
a) It is a map of the whole year  b) The short list of what still needs work  c) Mixed exercises drawn from every topic of the year  d) Do the marked task again
**Answer: d) Do the marked task again**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the review placed last in the book?**
a) It is a map of the whole year  b) The short list of what still needs work  c) Mixed exercises drawn from every topic of the year  d) The right tool themselves
**Answer: a) It is a map of the whole year**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the marked list for?**
a) Mixed exercises drawn from every topic of the year  b) The short list of what still needs work  c) The right tool themselves  d) The Grade 5 and Grade 6 Informatika books
**Answer: b) The short list of what still needs work**
