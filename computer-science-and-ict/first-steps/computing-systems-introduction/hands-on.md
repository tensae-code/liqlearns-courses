# Hands-on — Computing Systems — an Introduction

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Map a school system** — Choose one computing system in your school (the office computer, the library list). Name its hardware, software, data and people, and draw its input-process-store-output cycle.

## Task two
**Find the join** — For your mapped system, name one join between stages and one thing that could go wrong there.
