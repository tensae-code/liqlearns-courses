# Computing Systems — an Introduction — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four parts of a computing system.**
**Answer: Hardware, software, data and people**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the hardware part?**
**Answer: The physical devices — the parts you can touch**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the software part?**
**Answer: The programs that run on the hardware**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is hardware without software idle?**
**Answer: Nothing tells it what to do — there is no job running**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Armenia teach computing systems?**
**Answer: Grade 6, §5 — after number systems, before files and their types**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four stages of a computing system's work.**
**Answer: Input, processing, storage and output**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does storage add to the cycle?**
**Answer: Data and results are kept for later, not lost when processing ends**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do computing systems connect?**
**Answer: One system's output becomes another system's input**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where do most system failures live?**
**Answer: At the joins — where one stage hands over to the next**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does processing do?**
**Answer: It transforms the input data into results**
