# Hands-on — Storage — keeping data, and the four functions together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Keep it safe
Take the roster from the earlier lessons. Decide where it would be stored at school and why — inside the computer or on something removable. Defend your choice to a partner.

## Cycle in the wild
Watch any one complete job on a real device — sending a message, taking a photo. Stop the action at each of the four functions and name them in order.
