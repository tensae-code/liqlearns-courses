# Lesson plan — Storage — keeping data, and the four functions together

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Storage — keeping data, and the four functions together
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 1 — First Steps of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 1 — Basics of Computer.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** storage keeps data on internal or external storage for later — and with it, the four functions stand together as one cycle: input, process, output, storage.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the three function lessons before it: input, processing and output are known; storage completes the set.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds, looping.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks storage is the same as memory in your head → storage is a place in or attached to the machine: hard drive, flash drive, CD.
- Loses the cycle order → data travels input → process → output → storage, then round again when it is used.
