**Storage.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: storage keeps data on internal or external storage for later — and with it, the four functions stand together as one cycle: input, process, output, storage.

**Storage — keeping data.** Storage is keeping data on the computer's internal or external storage — a hard drive inside the machine, or a flash drive or compact disk outside it.
**The four functions together.** Now the whole cycle stands together: data is put in (input), worked on (process), shown as a result (output), and kept for later (storage).

Now you: Draw the four-function cycle as a circle and put one example on each step.
