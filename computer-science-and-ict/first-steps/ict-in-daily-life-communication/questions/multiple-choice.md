# ICT in daily life — communication — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Parents once sent messages by —**
a) SMS  b) letter  c) social media
**Answer: b) letter** — The book contrasts the letters parents once wrote with today's ways.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Today's communication ICT in the book includes —**
a) SMS, email and social media  b) only radio  c) only letters
**Answer: a) SMS, email and social media**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Radio and television are —**
a) not ICT  b) also counted as communication ICT  c) only entertainment
**Answer: b) also counted as communication ICT**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ICT is met —**
a) only in computer rooms  b) at home, school, work, in the street and in public places  c) only at school
**Answer: b) at home, school, work, in the street and in public places**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) In health, the guide's example is —**
a) patient records kept in a computer database, easy to retrieve  b) a letter to the clinic  c) a radio song
**Answer: a) patient records kept in a computer database, easy to retrieve** — Teacher guide key, printed pp. 14–15.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) A farmer can receive market prices by —**
a) a short SMS  b) waiting for a letter  c) a television drama
**Answer: a) a short SMS** — The guide adds weather forecasts reaching farmers too.
