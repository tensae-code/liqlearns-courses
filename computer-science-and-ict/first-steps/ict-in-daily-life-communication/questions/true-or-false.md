# ICT in daily life — communication — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) An email arrives faster than a posted letter.**
**Answer: True** — That instant arrival is the book's contrast.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Television is not counted as communication ICT.**
**Answer: False** — Radio and television are also counted.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Weather forecasts reaching farmers are an agriculture use of ICT.**
**Answer: True** — Guide key.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Patient records in a database are harder to retrieve than paper files.**
**Answer: False** — The guide's point is they can be retrieved easily.
