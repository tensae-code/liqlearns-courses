# Hands-on — ICT in daily life — communication

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ask the older way
Ask a parent or grandparent how they sent a message to a far place when they were young. Write their way and today's way side by side, with the waiting time of each.

## Sector diary
For one day, note every time your family uses ICT for health, farming or travel — a clinic visit, a price check, a bus or flight time. Write the sector beside each.
