# Lesson plan — ICT in daily life — communication

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** ICT in daily life — communication
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Introduction to Information & Communication Technology — in the title First Steps on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** ICT is met at home, school, work, in the street and in public places — and for communication it has replaced the letters parents once wrote with SMS, email and social media, with radio and television also counted as communication ICT.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on What ICT is and Communication in an ICT system: now the ideas are walked through real daily life.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **From letters to SMS, email and social media** — The book's contrast: the letters parents once wrote against today's SMS, email and social media, with radio and television also counted as communication ICT; ICT met at home, school, work, in the street and in public places.
2. **ICT in health, agriculture and transport (teacher guide — evidence)** — Three daily-life sectors the student book sets aside and the teacher guide's key works through: patient records in a database, market prices and weather by SMS for farmers, and travel information.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the lessons before this one in First Steps on the ladder. Recommended, never forced — a learner may land on this lesson directly, and it stands alone.
- The book's own rhythm is kept: this lesson opens with a brainstorm — the learner first says what they already know — before any definition is given.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name where ICT is met: home, school, work, the street, public places.
2. Tell the book's contrast: letters then; SMS, email and social media now; radio and television counted too.
3. Give the guide's worked examples for health (records in a database), agriculture (price SMS, weather forecasts) and transport (travel information).

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.
