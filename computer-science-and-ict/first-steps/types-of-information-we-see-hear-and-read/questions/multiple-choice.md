# Types of Information — We See, Hear and Read — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A drawing gives information we —**
a) hear  b) see  c) taste
**Answer: b) see** — Pictures reach us through the eyes.

**2. The school bell gives information we —**
a) read  b) hear  c) smell
**Answer: b) hear** — A bell is a sound.

**3. Words in a book give information we —**
a) read  b) hear only  c) touch
**Answer: a) read** — Written words and numbers are read.

**4. Information is —**
a) only on computers  b) what we learn about the world around us  c) only pictures
**Answer: b) what we learn about the world around us** — It comes in types: seen, heard and read.
