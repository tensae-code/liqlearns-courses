# Types of Information — We See, Hear and Read — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Name one example of each type of information.**
**Answer:** Seen: a picture/drawing; heard: a song/bell; read: words/numbers in a book (answers in the same spirit are right).

**2. How do you decide the type of a piece of information?**
**Answer:** By how it reaches us — as a picture to see, a sound to hear, or writing to read.
