# Lesson plan — Types of Information — We See, Hear and Read

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Types of Information — We See, Hear and Read
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Azerbaijan (2026-11-14) — in the title First Steps on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Azerbaijan's *İnformatika 2, Grade 2*, because no existing lesson teaches this thing as its own lesson at this level.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Information comes in types: some we see (pictures and shapes), some we hear (sounds and speech), and some we read (words and numbers).
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 2 simple — it stands on the child's own everyday world and on the lessons named below; every new word is built inside the lesson from things the child can do at once.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Objects lessons in First Steps. Recommended, never forced — a learner may land on this lesson directly, and it stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what information is, in your own words.
2. Sort examples into information we see, information we hear and information we read.
3. Name the type of a new example and say how it reaches us.

## What you need
A picture, something that makes a sound, and a page with words. No computer is needed.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Look at a picture: that information came through your eyes. Listen to the bell: that came through your ears. Read one word on the board: that came as writing. This lesson sorts information into its types. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a picture of three panels: an eye looking at a drawing, an ear beside a bell, and an open book with words — the student the student names the type of information in each panel: seen, heard or read.

## If the learner is stuck
Sorts by the object instead of by how it reaches us → ask 'Does it reach your eyes as a picture, your ears as a sound, or your eyes as writing to read?' Mixes seeing and reading → a picture is seen at once; words must be read letter by letter.
