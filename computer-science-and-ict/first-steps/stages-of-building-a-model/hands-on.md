# Hands-on — Stages of Building a Model

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Stage the build', 'Choose an object and write its modelling plan stage by stage, ending with the test you will apply. Build to the plan and record any repair.')

## Task two
('Fork the kind', 'Take one object and specify both a material and an information model of it. Say what each copies and what each is for.')
