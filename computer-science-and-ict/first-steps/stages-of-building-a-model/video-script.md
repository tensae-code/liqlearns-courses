# Video script — Stages of Building a Model

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Modelling is a **process** with stages. **Meet the object and the purpose**: what must the model do? **Study the object**: gather what is known of it. **Choose the properties** the purpose needs and drop the rest — the stage where models are won and lost. **Choose the kind and the form**: material or informational, and its shape. **Build the model**, then **test it against the object** and repair where it fails. No stage may be skipped for free: the model built without a purpose serves none, and the model never tested is a guess with good handwriting.
The process forks at its kind. A **material model** is built in matter — the clay ger, the demonstration skeleton — copying form. An **information model** is built in information — the property table, the formula, the drawing — copying knowledge. Both serve the one master: **a model is for studying, predicting and presenting** what cannot be handled directly — too big, too small, too far, too dangerous, or not yet built. Mongolia's strand keeps the two kinds in one lesson because the stages before the fork are one craft.
