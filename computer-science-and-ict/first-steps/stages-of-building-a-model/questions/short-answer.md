# Stages of Building a Model — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the stages of building a model.**
**Answer: Meet object and purpose; study the object; choose properties; choose kind and form; build; test and repair**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) At which stage are models won and lost?**
**Answer: Choosing the properties the purpose needs**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a model built without a purpose?**
**Answer: One that serves none**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a model never tested?**
**Answer: A guess with good handwriting**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a material model copy?**
**Answer: Form — it is built in matter**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does an information model copy?**
**Answer: Knowledge — built in information**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a model for?**
**Answer: Studying, predicting and presenting what cannot be handled directly**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why can some things only be modelled?**
**Answer: They are too big, too small, too far, too dangerous, or not yet built**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where do the two kinds fork?**
**Answer: At the choice of kind — the stages before are one craft**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which books carry this modelling strand?**
**Answer: Mongolia's IT books, Grades 6 to 9**
