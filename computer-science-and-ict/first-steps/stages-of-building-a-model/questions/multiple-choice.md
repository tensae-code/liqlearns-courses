# Stages of Building a Model — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the stages of building a model.**
a) Meet object and purpose; study the object; choose properties; choose kind and form; build; test and repair  b) Choosing the properties the purpose needs  c) One that serves none  d) A guess with good handwriting
**Answer: a) Meet object and purpose; study the object; choose properties; choose kind and form; build; test and repair**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) At which stage are models won and lost?**
a) One that serves none  b) Choosing the properties the purpose needs  c) A guess with good handwriting  d) Form — it is built in matter
**Answer: b) Choosing the properties the purpose needs**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a model built without a purpose?**
a) A guess with good handwriting  b) Form — it is built in matter  c) One that serves none  d) Knowledge — built in information
**Answer: c) One that serves none**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a model never tested?**
a) Form — it is built in matter  b) Knowledge — built in information  c) Studying, predicting and presenting what cannot be handled directly  d) A guess with good handwriting
**Answer: d) A guess with good handwriting**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a material model copy?**
a) Form — it is built in matter  b) Knowledge — built in information  c) Studying, predicting and presenting what cannot be handled directly  d) They are too big, too small, too far, too dangerous, or not yet built
**Answer: a) Form — it is built in matter**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does an information model copy?**
a) Studying, predicting and presenting what cannot be handled directly  b) Knowledge — built in information  c) They are too big, too small, too far, too dangerous, or not yet built  d) At the choice of kind — the stages before are one craft
**Answer: b) Knowledge — built in information**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a model for?**
a) They are too big, too small, too far, too dangerous, or not yet built  b) At the choice of kind — the stages before are one craft  c) Studying, predicting and presenting what cannot be handled directly  d) Mongolia's IT books, Grades 6 to 9
**Answer: c) Studying, predicting and presenting what cannot be handled directly**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why can some things only be modelled?**
a) At the choice of kind — the stages before are one craft  b) Mongolia's IT books, Grades 6 to 9  c) Meet object and purpose; study the object; choose properties; choose kind and form; build; test and repair  d) They are too big, too small, too far, too dangerous, or not yet built
**Answer: d) They are too big, too small, too far, too dangerous, or not yet built**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where do the two kinds fork?**
a) At the choice of kind — the stages before are one craft  b) Mongolia's IT books, Grades 6 to 9  c) Meet object and purpose; study the object; choose properties; choose kind and form; build; test and repair  d) Choosing the properties the purpose needs
**Answer: a) At the choice of kind — the stages before are one craft**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which books carry this modelling strand?**
a) Meet object and purpose; study the object; choose properties; choose kind and form; build; test and repair  b) Mongolia's IT books, Grades 6 to 9  c) Choosing the properties the purpose needs  d) One that serves none
