**Acceptance count: taught in 5 countries (Ethiopia, Tanzania, Mozambique, Yemen, Uzbekistan).**

Yemen's own way (Grade 10): a skills ladder — every unit alternates short teaching topics with a named تدريب (guided training) and نشاط (activity), and Windows itself is taught as a school subject for a full unit before any application is opened.

The student book's text carries stray character runs typeset into it — runs of the letter k on printed pp. 2, 4 and 5 fall inside this lesson's pages; they are typesetting faults, carry no meaning, and are not taught. The teacher guide has the same fault family: a run of 'h' characters after the generations table (guide printed p. 4), also not taught. The guide's Unit 5 period figures contradict each other (its overview says 'seven (5) periods' while its sections add to 6); the road-map part of this lesson therefore names the guide's stated unit totals only, and files the Unit 5 contradiction as an open discrepancy, not a settled number. Nothing in this lesson claims a page the placements do not give; the lesson's content is the placements' content, re-expressed for the learner, with the book's own examples kept.

The Grade 8 teacher guide's Unit 1 answer key (printed p. 9) was used as the verified-answer check for this lesson's Grade 8 questions (teacher guide — evidence).

Papua New Guinea's own way (Grade 9): one TIA teacher guide carries two ICT subjects side by side — Communication Technology (networks, ergonomics, safety, societal impact) and Computer Technology (architecture, software) — organised by content standards and numbered benchmarks (9.4.x / 9.5.x), with the design brief used as the problem-solving method for both a product and a simple network, and typing assessed at 20 wpm with 80% accuracy. (teacher guide — evidence, PARTIAL)


Uzbekistan's own way (Grade 8): grade 8 is theory-first — number systems, coding and Boolean logic before the machine's own story — then a full operating-system chapter and a five-lesson Word course; safety rules return as a closing appendix.

Cuba's own way (Grade 2): Play-based (lúdico) introduction through the graficador (Paint-type drawing program) and educational software; correct workstation posture/ergonomics is taught explicitly in Grade 2, and files/folders are introduced intuitively through saving and opening drawings in a class-group folder. (teacher guide — evidence)
