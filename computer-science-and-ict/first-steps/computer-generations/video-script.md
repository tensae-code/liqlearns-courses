# Video script — Computer generations — five periods of change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Grade 8 Information Technology.

Six units. First: **Basics of Computer** — how computers developed, and what they are.
Second: **Computer Hardware** — the parts you can touch, in depth.
Third: **Computer Software** — and the office programs: word processor, spreadsheet, presentations.
Fourth: **Internet** — services, browsing, email, and social networks.
Fifth: **Computer Ergonomics** — looking after your body at a computer.
Sixth: **LOGO Programming** — programs that decide.

Grade 8 builds on Grade 7. Every unit starts the same way: **brainstorm first** — what do you already know? Then the definitions.

This first lesson: **a generation is a period of change.** Five generations. Tubes. Transistors. Chips. Microprocessors. Machines that learn.
