# Computer generations — five periods of change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What makes a new computer generation begin?
a) A new colour of computer case  b) A major technological change in how computers operate  c) A new school year  d) A cheaper price
**Answer: b) A major technological change in how computers operate** — A generation is a period marked by a major technological change.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What did the word 'generation' first distinguish?
a) Software programs  b) Games  c) Hardware technologies  d) Websites
**Answer: c) Hardware technologies** — It first distinguished hardware technologies; now it covers hardware and software together.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How many computer generations are there?
a) Three  b) Four  c) Five  d) Six
**Answer: c) Five** — There are five generations.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which technology belongs to the second generation?
a) Vacuum tubes  b) Transistors  c) Artificial intelligence  d) Microprocessors
**Answer: b) Transistors** — Second generation (1956–1963) used transistors.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which technology belongs to the fourth generation?
a) Transistors  b) Integrated circuits  c) Vacuum tubes  d) Very large scale integrated circuits — the microprocessor
**Answer: d) Very large scale integrated circuits — the microprocessor** — Fourth generation (1971–present) uses VLSI circuits, the microprocessor.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** What defines the fifth generation?
a) Artificial intelligence  b) Vacuum tubes  c) The personal computer  d) Magnetic tape
**Answer: a) Artificial intelligence** — The fifth generation is defined by artificial intelligence.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** What comes first in every unit of this course, before any definition?
a) A test  b) A brainstorming activity linking to what you already know  c) The glossary  d) Copying notes
**Answer: b) A brainstorming activity linking to what you already know** — Every unit opens with outcomes, an overview, then a brainstorm before definitions.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the last unit of the Grade 8 course?
a) Internet  b) Computer Hardware  c) LOGO Programming  d) Computer Ergonomics
**Answer: c) LOGO Programming** — Unit 6 is LOGO Programming.
