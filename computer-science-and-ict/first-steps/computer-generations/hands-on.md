# Hands-on — Computer generations — five periods of change

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the road map
On a big sheet, draw six boxes for the six units of this course, from 'how computers developed' to 'LOGO programs that decide'. Put the sheet where you will see it as you work through the course.

## Generation hunt at home
Find the oldest and newest computing devices in your home — a phone, a radio with a computer inside, a calculator. Guess each one's generation from what is inside it, and say your reason out loud.
