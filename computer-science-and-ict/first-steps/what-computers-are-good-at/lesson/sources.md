**Acceptance count: taught in 5 countries (Ethiopia, Tanzania, Bhutan, Sri Lanka, Vietnam).**

The student text on printed p. 11 shows the characteristics in a figure; the names speed, storage and diligence are the ones its own Unit 1 review works with (speed named in the review question, storage and diligence as its alternatives) — the lesson is built on exactly those, and claims no further figure detail.

Bhutan's own way (Class IV, Literacy with ICT student book): Competency-based "Literacy with ICT" (not procedure drill): every chapter opens with learning objectives, mixes off-machine "Try This" with "Try This On Machine", and closes with Now You Know / Check Your Progress / Explore Further; content is localised (Bhutanese names, kuenselonline, education.gov.bt, Bhutan's 1999 Internet start) and one class per week across the year.

Sri Lanka's own way (Grade 6 Reading Book): a story/dialogue-led "Reading Book" with a talking computer character; every chapter cross-refers to numbered Workbook activities; local sectors (school office, ATM, ICU, factories, agriculture) and the 3R e-waste method are taught from Grade 6.

Vietnam's own way (Grade 5 Tin học 5): Grade 5 splits its applied theme into two alternative tracks — track A makes digital products with graphics software, track B follows tutorial videos to make physical craft products — and its programming theme is taught in a visual block language as pure structures first (sequence, loop, branch), then variables and expressions, ending with turning a written script/storyboard into a program.
