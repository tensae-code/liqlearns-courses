# What computers are good at — and the harm of overuse — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Speed, as a computer characteristic, means —**
a) how heavy it is  b) how fast it performs a task  c) how much it costs
**Answer: b) how fast it performs a task** — The review's own wording.

**2. Diligence in a computer means —**
a) it works without tiring or losing concentration  b) it is expensive  c) it is small
**Answer: a) it works without tiring or losing concentration**

**3. Storage as a characteristic is about —**
a) how much the computer can keep  b) its colour  c) its sound
**Answer: a) how much the computer can keep**

**4. The book warns that overuse brings —**
a) faster typing  b) muscle and joint pain, overuse injuries of arms, wrist and hand, and eyestrain  c) better eyesight
**Answer: b) muscle and joint pain, overuse injuries of arms, wrist and hand, and eyestrain**

**5. Who is especially at risk of eyestrain, says the book?**
a) Children who game too much  b) Teachers  c) Farmers
**Answer: a) Children who game too much**
