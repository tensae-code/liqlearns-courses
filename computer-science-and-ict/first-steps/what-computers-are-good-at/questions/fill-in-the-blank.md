# What computers are good at — and the harm of overuse — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. How fast a computer performs a task is its ____.**
**Answer: speed**

**2. Pain in muscles and ____ is one harm of overuse.**
**Answer: joints**

**3. The characteristic of working without tiring is called ____.**
**Answer: diligence**
