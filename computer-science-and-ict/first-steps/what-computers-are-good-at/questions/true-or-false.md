# What computers are good at — and the harm of overuse — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. A computer loses concentration after long work, like a person.**
**Answer: False** — Diligence means it does not tire that way — the person does.

**2. Overuse injuries can affect the wrist and hand.**
**Answer: True**

**3. Eyestrain is one of the harms the book names.**
**Answer: True**

**4. Wrong use of a computer can harm the user's body.**
**Answer: True** — The book warns plainly about overuse and wrong use.
