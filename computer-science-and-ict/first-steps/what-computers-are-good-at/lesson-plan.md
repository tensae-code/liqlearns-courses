# Lesson plan — What computers are good at — and the harm of overuse

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** What computers are good at — and the harm of overuse
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Introduction to Information & Communication Technology — in the title First Steps on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The computer's characteristics — speed, storage and diligence among them — make it powerful, and the book warns plainly that overuse and wrong use bring muscle and joint pain, overuse injuries of arms, wrist and hand, and eyestrain.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on What a computer is: the learner knows the machine's four functions; now its character — and its cost to the body when it is overused.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name characteristics from the book's figure and review: speed, storage, diligence.
2. Say what speed means here: how fast the computer performs a task.
3. Name the harms of overuse: muscle and joint pain, overuse injuries of arms, wrist and hand, and eyestrain — especially for children who game too much.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.
