# Video script — What computers are good at — and the harm of overuse

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Fast. Roomy. Tireless.

That is the computer: speed, storage, diligence.

But your eyes tire. Your wrists ache.

The machine does not rest — so you must.
