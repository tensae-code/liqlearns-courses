# Hands-on — What computers are good at — and the harm of overuse

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Speed, honestly measured
Time yourself copying a page by hand. Then imagine typing and printing it. Write both times and the difference — that gap is the computer's speed, felt in your own hand.

## Rest rules
Write three rest rules for your own screen time — when you will stand, stretch your wrists, and rest your eyes — based on the harms the book names. Keep the rules where you use the computer.
