# Video script — Processing — the computer works on the data

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Processing.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: processing is doing operations on the entered data — adding, changing, totalling, averaging, ranking.

**Processing is the work done on data.** Processing is doing operations on the entered data.
**The book's example — the class roster.** When preparing a roster, the computer takes students' marks and works out totals, averages, and who came top.

Now you: Take two numbers and write down every operation a computer would do to find their product and their average.
