# Lesson plan — Processing — the computer works on the data

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Processing — the computer works on the data
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 1 — First Steps of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 1 — Basics of Computer.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** processing is doing operations on the entered data — adding, changing, totalling, averaging, ranking.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the input lesson: data has been inserted; now the computer works on it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio.
