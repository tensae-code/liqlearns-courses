# Hands-on — Processing — the computer works on the data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Be the processor
Take the paper from the input lesson (two numbers). Do the processing yourself, slowly, writing each operation. You have just done by hand what the computer does in a blink.

## Roster by hand
With a partner, take five invented students' marks, total them, average them, and rank them. Label each step 'processing'.
