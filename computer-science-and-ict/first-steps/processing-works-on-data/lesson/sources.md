Nothing in this lesson claims a page the placements do not give; the lesson's content is the placements' content, re-expressed for the learner, with the book's own examples kept.

Laos's own way (Grade 6 ICT student book): a theory-only foundation year — no hands-on computer practicals at all; ICT is introduced through Lao daily-life examples (TV remote, household appliances) and every chapter opens with objectives, guiding questions and a discussion activity (ກິດຈະກໍາ) before the exposition. The RIES ICT line runs ມ.1–ມ.7 (Grades 6–12).

Vietnam's own way (Grade 3 Tin học 3): Informatics starts in Grade 3 not with the machine but with information in daily life — an alarm clock ringing is "information" that leads to a decision — and only then meets the computer; problem-solving (Chủ đề 6) is taught unplugged as ordering the steps of everyday jobs, with and without conditions, before any code exists.

Vietnam's own way (Grade 6 Tin học 6): the first lower-secondary book names its software openly (Google Chrome, MindMaple Lite, Microsoft Word in the how-to-use page) and its three characters each stand for one strand of the subject — digital literacy, ICT, and computer science; information safety gets a full lesson before any applied software work.
