# General-purpose computers — built for many jobs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A general-purpose computer is designed to —
a) Solve a single problem  b) Solve a variety of problems  c) Print only  d) Count only
**Answer: b) Solve a variety of problems** — It is designed to solve a variety of problems.

**2.** A general-purpose computer changes jobs by —
a) Changing its case  b) Running programs for different tasks  c) Being switched off  d) Changing its screen
**Answer: b) Running programs for different tasks** — It runs programs or sets of instructions for different tasks.

**3.** Which of these is a general-purpose example?
a) A pocket calculator  b) A ticket machine  c) A microcomputer  d) A traffic light
**Answer: c) A microcomputer** — Microcomputers, minicomputers and supercomputers are the examples.

**4.** General-purpose computers are described as —
a) Fixed and narrow  b) Flexible and versatile  c) Slow and hot  d) Single and fitted
**Answer: b) Flexible and versatile** — Running different programs makes them flexible and versatile.
