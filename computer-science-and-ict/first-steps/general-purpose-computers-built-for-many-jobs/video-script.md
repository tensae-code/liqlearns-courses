# Video script — General-purpose computers — built for many jobs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**General-purpose computers.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: a general-purpose computer is designed to solve a variety of problems, running different programs for different tasks — which makes it flexible and versatile.

**Designed for a variety of problems.** A general-purpose computer is designed to solve a variety of problems.
**The examples.** Microcomputers, minicomputers and supercomputers are the book's examples of general-purpose computers.

Now you: Draw one computer three times: doing schoolwork, accounts, and a game.
