# Hands-on — General-purpose computers — built for many jobs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Program swap
Act it out: one partner is the computer, the other hands over 'program cards' — write a letter, do sums, play a song. The computer changes behaviour with each card. That change is the general-purpose idea.

## Sort the street
Reuse your one-job list from the special-purpose lesson. Walk the same street and list the many-job machines. Compare the two lists with a partner.
