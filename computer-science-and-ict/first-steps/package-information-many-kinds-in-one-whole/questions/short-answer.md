# Package Information — Many Kinds in One Whole — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is package information?**
**Answer: Many kinds of information gathered into one whole about one subject**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the kinds a package gathers.**
**Answer: Text, numbers, pictures, sound and video (recordings)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What binds the kinds together?**
**Answer: The subject they all serve**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How do the kinds treat each other in a package?**
**Answer: They complete and check each other**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Give an instance of one kind checking another.**
**Answer: The table corrects the memory the text relied on (any one of: the caption explains the photograph)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How must a kind earn its place?**
**Answer: By adding what the others cannot say**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must the reader who takes the package receive?**
**Answer: The subject entire, not whichever kind was nearest**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What chapters follow the package's introduction?**
**Answer: How it is processed, stored, and merged with other information**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Whose own concept is 'package information'?**
**Answer: Mongolia's — its Grade 10 book's own chapter**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does the package relate to Grade 6's forms lesson?**
**Answer: Grade 6 listed the forms one by one; Grade 10 gathers them into one whole**
