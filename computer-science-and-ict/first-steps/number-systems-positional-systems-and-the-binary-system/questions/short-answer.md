# Number Systems — Positional Systems and the Binary System — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a system positional?**
**Answer: A digit's value depends on its position in the number**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a system's base?**
**Answer: Its number of different digits — and the weight each position step multiplies by**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is decimal's base and digits?**
**Answer: Base 10, digits 0–9**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) In 222, what are the three digits worth?**
**Answer: Two hundred, twenty and two**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a positional number's value computed?**
**Answer: As the sum of its digits times their place weights**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are binary's base and digits?**
**Answer: Base 2, digits 0 and 1**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are binary's first place values?**
**Answer: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 — doubling each step**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is 101 in binary, in decimal?**
**Answer: 5 (4 + 0 + 1)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the computer use binary?**
**Answer: Its parts have two stable states, and one digit matches one state**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is one binary digit called?**
**Answer: A bit**
