# Hands-on — Number Systems — Positional Systems and the Binary System

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Place-value build** — Write 101, 1101 and 11111 in binary and compute each one's decimal value by place weights; check by counting objects.

## Task two
**Two-state hunt** — Find three two-state things at home (switch, lamp, door) and write the day's states of one of them as a row of bits.
