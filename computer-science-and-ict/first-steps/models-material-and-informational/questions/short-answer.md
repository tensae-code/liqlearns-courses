# Models — Material and Informational — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a model?**
**Answer: A stand-in made to study an object instead of the object itself**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a model keep and leave out?**
**Answer: It keeps the features that matter for the purpose and leaves the rest out**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name a material model from the book.**
**Answer: The paper origami ger (any one of: a globe, a toy car)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a material model made of?**
**Answer: Physical material copying the object's shape and size**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is an informational model made of?**
**Answer: Information — words, tables, drawings, formulas**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which kind does the computer work with?**
**Answer: Informational models only**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a model's simplicity its use?**
**Answer: What can be held, turned and tested teaches what the real thing cannot**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Give an informational model of a bear.**
**Answer: A description or table of its properties (any one of: a drawing)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Can one object have several models?**
**Answer: Yes — different purposes keep different features**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is every computer file, in this lesson's terms?**
**Answer: An informational model — information standing in for a thing**
