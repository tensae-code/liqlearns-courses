# Hands-on — Models — Material and Informational

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Two models of one thing', 'Choose an object. Make its material model from paper or clay, then its informational model as a table of properties. Say what each model keeps and drops.')

## Task two
('Judge the model', 'Take a map of your area. List three things it models truly and three it leaves out. Say for what purpose it is still a good model.')
