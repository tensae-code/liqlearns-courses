**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do the most frequent symbols get?**

**Answer: Because frequent symbols dominate the text**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a symbol's code in the tree?**
**Answer: The path from the root to its leaf**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where do frequent symbols sit?**
**Answer: Near the root — short paths**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does each branch stand for?**
**Answer: One bit — left for 0, right for 1 (say)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why can the bit stream be decoded in exactly one way?**
**Answer: No symbol sits on another symbol's path**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What sits at a leaf?**
