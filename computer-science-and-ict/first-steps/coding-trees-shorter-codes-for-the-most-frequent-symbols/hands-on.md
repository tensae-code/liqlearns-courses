# Hands-on — Coding Trees — Shorter Codes for the Most Frequent Symbols

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Build a tree for a sentence** — Take the sentence “ZIP LA MARMOTTE” (ignore spaces). Count letter frequencies, sketch a tree giving the top two letters the shortest paths, and write the sentence's total code length against a fixed 4-bit code.

## Task two
**Decode by walking** — A partner writes a short message with your tree's codes, no spaces between letters. Decode it by walking the tree from the root at every bit.
