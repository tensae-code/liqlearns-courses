# Hands-on — Information Models — and How to Build One

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Build one whole', 'Build an information model of a bicycle trip to school: purpose, three properties, a form, and one check against a real trip. Present it as one page.')

## Task two
('Drop a property', 'Take the car model and remove speed. List three questions the model can no longer answer. Then remove colour from a fuller model and list what is lost — compare.')
