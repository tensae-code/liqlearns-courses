## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Building a model — the steps** — An **information model** of an object is **built**, not found, and the building has an order. **Name the purpose**: what the model must answer decides everything after. **Choose the properties** that matter for that purpose, and leave the rest out — a model of a car for journey time needs speed and distance, not colour. **Fix the form**: words, a table, a formula or a drawing, as the chosen properties ask. **Check the model** against the object: where it disagrees, the model — not the world — is repaired.
2. **The moving car — a physics model worked whole** — The book's worked example is physics: the **moving car**. Its model keeps position, time and speed, joined by the relation between them; with the model, journeys are calculated instead of travelled — how far in an hour, how long for the steppe road to the aimag centre. Every step of the method shows in the example: purpose (predict the journey), properties (speed, distance, time), form (a formula), check (against a real timed trip). Mongolia's point closes the Grade 9 opening unit: a model is an instrument — built for a purpose, judged by its work.
