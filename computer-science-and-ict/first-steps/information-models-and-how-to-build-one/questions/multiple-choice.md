# Information Models — and How to Build One — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What decides everything in building a model?**
a) The purpose — what the model must answer  b) Name the purpose; choose the properties; fix the form; check against the object  c) They are left out  d) The model
**Answer: a) The purpose — what the model must answer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the building steps in order.**
a) They are left out  b) Name the purpose; choose the properties; fix the form; check against the object  c) The model  d) Position (distance), time and speed
**Answer: b) Name the purpose; choose the properties; fix the form; check against the object**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to properties that do not matter for the purpose?**
a) The model  b) Position (distance), time and speed  c) They are left out  d) A formula joining speed, distance and time
**Answer: c) They are left out**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where a model and the world disagree, what is repaired?**
a) Position (distance), time and speed  b) A formula joining speed, distance and time  c) Journeys can be calculated — how far, how long  d) The model
**Answer: d) The model**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What properties does the moving-car model keep?**
a) Position (distance), time and speed  b) A formula joining speed, distance and time  c) Journeys can be calculated — how far, how long  d) Against a real timed trip
**Answer: a) Position (distance), time and speed**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What form does the car model take?**
a) Journeys can be calculated — how far, how long  b) A formula joining speed, distance and time  c) Against a real timed trip  d) An instrument — built for a purpose, judged by its work
**Answer: b) A formula joining speed, distance and time**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What can be done with the model instead of travelling?**
a) Against a real timed trip  b) An instrument — built for a purpose, judged by its work  c) Journeys can be calculated — how far, how long  d) Grade 9, in Mongolia's IT book

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is the example checked?**
a) An instrument — built for a purpose, judged by its work  b) Grade 9, in Mongolia's IT book  c) The purpose — what the model must answer  d) Against a real timed trip
**Answer: d) Against a real timed trip**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a model, in the lesson's closing phrase?**
a) An instrument — built for a purpose, judged by its work  b) Grade 9, in Mongolia's IT book  c) The purpose — what the model must answer  d) Name the purpose; choose the properties; fix the form; check against the object
