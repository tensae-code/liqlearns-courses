# Information Models — and How to Build One — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What decides everything in building a model?**
**Answer: The purpose — what the model must answer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the building steps in order.**
**Answer: Name the purpose; choose the properties; fix the form; check against the object**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to properties that do not matter for the purpose?**
**Answer: They are left out**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where a model and the world disagree, what is repaired?**
**Answer: The model**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What properties does the moving-car model keep?**
**Answer: Position (distance), time and speed**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What form does the car model take?**
**Answer: A formula joining speed, distance and time**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What can be done with the model instead of travelling?**
**Answer: Journeys can be calculated — how far, how long**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is the example checked?**
**Answer: Against a real timed trip**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a model, in the lesson's closing phrase?**
**Answer: An instrument — built for a purpose, judged by its work**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) In which grade does the model return as a craft?**
**Answer: Grade 9, in Mongolia's IT book**
