# Hands-on — Types of computer by size — microcomputers and desktops

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Desk survey
Measure a desk at home or school. A microcomputer fits on it — that is the test of the type. Note what else must share the desk: keyboard, monitor, case.

## Forms in the wild
On a walk past offices and shops, spot desktops and tally them: separate-case against all-in-one. Report the count to a partner.
