**Types of computer by size.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: computers are classified by size, by method of operation and by purpose — and by size the first and most widely used type is the microcomputer, the personal computer that fits on a desk.

**Three ways of classifying.** Computers are classified in three ways: by size, storage and speed; by method of operation; and by purpose.
**The microcomputer and the two desktop forms.** The microcomputer is the small, most widely used computer, fitting on a desk — the personal computer.

Now you: Draw the two desktop forms side by side and label each part.
