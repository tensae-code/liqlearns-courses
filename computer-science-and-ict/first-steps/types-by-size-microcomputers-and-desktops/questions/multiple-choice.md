# Types of computer by size — microcomputers and desktops — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Computers are classified by —
a) Colour, price and brand  b) Size (with storage and speed), method of operation, and purpose  c) Country only  d) Age of the user
**Answer: b) Size (with storage and speed), method of operation, and purpose** — The three classifications: by size, storage and speed; by method of operation; by purpose.

**2.** How many types are there by size?
a) Two  b) Three  c) Four  d) Five
**Answer: c) Four** — By size there are four types, starting with the microcomputer.

**3.** The microcomputer is —
a) The biggest computer  b) The small, most widely used computer that fits on a desk  c) A computer only for banks  d) A computer with no screen
**Answer: b) The small, most widely used computer that fits on a desk** — The microcomputer is the small, most widely used computer — the personal computer.

**4.** An all-in-one desktop is one where —
a) Everything is wireless  b) The system unit and monitor are integrated in one device  c) There is no keyboard  d) Two people share one screen
**Answer: b) The system unit and monitor are integrated in one device** — In an all-in-one, system unit and monitor are integrated in one device.

**5.** In the separate-case desktop form, the monitor often sits —
a) Under the desk  b) On top of the case  c) In another room  d) Inside the keyboard
**Answer: b) On top of the case** — Separate case on the desk, keyboard in front, monitor often on top.
