# Types of computer by size — microcomputers and desktops

## Do it first

Look at the computers around your life: a phone, a school computer, the big machine behind a bank counter. Are they all the same kind of thing? Before reading on, say how you would sort computers into kinds.

## Three ways of classifying

Computers are classified in three ways: by size, storage and speed; by method of operation; and by purpose. This lesson and the next three take classification by size, which has four types. Size here goes together with storage and speed — a bigger type holds more and works faster.

## The microcomputer and the two desktop forms

The microcomputer is the small, most widely used computer, fitting on a desk — the personal computer. Desktops come in two forms: a separate case on the desk, with the keyboard in front of it and the monitor often on top; and the all-in-one, where the system unit and monitor are integrated in one device.
