# Hands-on — Embedded Computers in Everyday Devices

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Find the hidden computers', 'Walk through your home or school and list five devices. For each one, write whether it holds an embedded computer and what job that computer controls.')

## Task two
('Explain it to a younger pupil', 'Pick one device — a washing machine, a phone, a car or a television — and explain in your own words where its embedded computer is and what would stop working without it.')
