# Hands-on — Converting Decimal Numbers to Binary

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Convert by hand
Convert 25 to binary by the divide-by-two method, writing every division. Then check your answer by place value before you look at anyone else's.

## Convert back and forth
Take binary 10110, turn it into decimal by place value, then convert that decimal number back by dividing. You should arrive at 10110 exactly — if not, find the step that drifted.
