# Converting Decimal Numbers to Binary — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do you divide by, again and again?**
**Answer: By two**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do you write down at each division?**
**Answer: The remainder — always 0 or 1**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When do you stop dividing?**
**Answer: When the answer reaches 0**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are the divisions for 13?**
**Answer: 13 ÷ 2 = 6 rem 1; 6 ÷ 2 = 3 rem 0; 3 ÷ 2 = 1 rem 1; 1 ÷ 2 = 0 rem 1**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Can a remainder ever be 2?**
**Answer: No — a remainder on dividing by 2 is always 0 or 1**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) In which order do you read the remainders?**
**Answer: From last to first — upwards**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is 13 in binary?**
**Answer: 1101**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the first remainder you found end up?**
**Answer: As the last digit — the ones place of the binary number**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do you check a conversion?**
**Answer: By place value — 1101 = 8 + 4 + 0 + 1 = 13**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a failed check tell you?**
**Answer: A division or the reading direction went wrong — do the method again**
