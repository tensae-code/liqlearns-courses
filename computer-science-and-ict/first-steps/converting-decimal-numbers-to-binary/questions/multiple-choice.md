# Converting Decimal Numbers to Binary — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do you divide by, again and again?**
a) By two  b) The remainder — always 0 or 1  c) When the answer reaches 0  d) 13 ÷ 2 = 6 rem 1; 6 ÷ 2 = 3 rem 0; 3 ÷ 2 = 1 rem 1; 1 ÷ 2 = 0 rem 1
**Answer: a) By two**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do you write down at each division?**
a) When the answer reaches 0  b) The remainder — always 0 or 1  c) 13 ÷ 2 = 6 rem 1; 6 ÷ 2 = 3 rem 0; 3 ÷ 2 = 1 rem 1; 1 ÷ 2 = 0 rem 1  d) No — a remainder on dividing by 2 is always 0 or 1
**Answer: b) The remainder — always 0 or 1**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When do you stop dividing?**
a) 13 ÷ 2 = 6 rem 1; 6 ÷ 2 = 3 rem 0; 3 ÷ 2 = 1 rem 1; 1 ÷ 2 = 0 rem 1  b) No — a remainder on dividing by 2 is always 0 or 1  c) When the answer reaches 0  d) From last to first — upwards
**Answer: c) When the answer reaches 0**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are the divisions for 13?**
a) No — a remainder on dividing by 2 is always 0 or 1  b) From last to first — upwards  c) 1101  d) 13 ÷ 2 = 6 rem 1; 6 ÷ 2 = 3 rem 0; 3 ÷ 2 = 1 rem 1; 1 ÷ 2 = 0 rem 1
**Answer: d) 13 ÷ 2 = 6 rem 1; 6 ÷ 2 = 3 rem 0; 3 ÷ 2 = 1 rem 1; 1 ÷ 2 = 0 rem 1**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Can a remainder ever be 2?**
a) No — a remainder on dividing by 2 is always 0 or 1  b) From last to first — upwards  c) 1101  d) As the last digit — the ones place of the binary number
**Answer: a) No — a remainder on dividing by 2 is always 0 or 1**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) In which order do you read the remainders?**
a) 1101  b) From last to first — upwards  c) As the last digit — the ones place of the binary number  d) By place value — 1101 = 8 + 4 + 0 + 1 = 13
**Answer: b) From last to first — upwards**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is 13 in binary?**
a) As the last digit — the ones place of the binary number  b) By place value — 1101 = 8 + 4 + 0 + 1 = 13  c) 1101  d) A division or the reading direction went wrong — do the method again
**Answer: c) 1101**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the first remainder you found end up?**
a) By place value — 1101 = 8 + 4 + 0 + 1 = 13  b) A division or the reading direction went wrong — do the method again  c) By two  d) As the last digit — the ones place of the binary number
**Answer: d) As the last digit — the ones place of the binary number**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do you check a conversion?**
a) By place value — 1101 = 8 + 4 + 0 + 1 = 13  b) A division or the reading direction went wrong — do the method again  c) By two  d) The remainder — always 0 or 1
**Answer: a) By place value — 1101 = 8 + 4 + 0 + 1 = 13**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a failed check tell you?**
a) By two  b) A division or the reading direction went wrong — do the method again  c) The remainder — always 0 or 1  d) When the answer reaches 0
**Answer: b) A division or the reading direction went wrong — do the method again**
