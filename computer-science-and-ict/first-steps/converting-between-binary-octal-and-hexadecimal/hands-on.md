# Hands-on — Converting Between Binary, Octal and Hexadecimal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Grouping drill** — Convert 11010110₂ to octal and to hexadecimal by grouping, then check both by decimal conversion.

## Task two
**Broker work** — Convert 725₈ to binary and on to hexadecimal, and AF3₁₆ back to binary and octal, showing each grouping.
