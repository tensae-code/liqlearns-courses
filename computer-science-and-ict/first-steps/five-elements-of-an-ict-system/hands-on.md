# Hands-on — The five elements of an ICT system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five cards
Make five cards, one per element, each with its name and a drawing of its job. Mix them, then lay them back in the book's order from memory.

## Spot the five
Take one real moment — a parent checking a price by SMS. Write down where each of the five elements appears in that one moment.
