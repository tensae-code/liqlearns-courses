# Video script — The five elements of an ICT system

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Every ICT system has five parts.

Data. Information. Communication. User. Technology.

Five parts, working together. Take one away, and the system stops.
