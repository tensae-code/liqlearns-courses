# The five elements of an ICT system — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) The five elements in order: data, ____, communication, user, technology.**
**Answer: information**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The ____ is the person who accesses and uses the ICT system.**
**Answer: user**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Parts that work ____ make a system.**
**Answer: together**
