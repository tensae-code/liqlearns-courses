# Presenting Information in Different Forms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Presenting information means —**
a) hiding it  b) showing it to someone else  c) forgetting it
**Answer: b) showing it to someone else** — A presenter shows information in a form.

**2. Which is NOT a form in this lesson?**
a) Speech  b) A picture  c) Sleeping
**Answer: c) Sleeping** — The forms are speech, writing, picture and number.

**3. 'Five apples' drawn as five apples and written as 5 — the information is —**
a) different  b) the same  c) lost
**Answer: b) the same** — Only the form changed.

**4. For a listener who cannot read yet, the best form is often —**
a) small writing  b) a picture or speech  c) numbers only
**Answer: b) a picture or speech** — Choose the form that helps the listener most.
