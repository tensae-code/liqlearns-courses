# Presenting Information in Different Forms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. Changing the form changes the information.**
**Answer: False** — Five apples stay five apples in every form.

**2. Information can be presented as a picture.**
**Answer: True** — Drawing is one of the forms.

**3. Writing is a good form for someone who must remember information later.**
**Answer: True** — Writing stays; speech is gone once spoken.
