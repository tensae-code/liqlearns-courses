# Presenting Information in Different Forms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Present 'four books on the desk' in three forms.**
**Answer:** Spoken aloud; written as words; drawn as four books (or written as the number 4) — any three forms, same information.

**2. Why does a presenter choose a form carefully?**
**Answer:** Because the form must help the listener — a picture for one who cannot read, writing for one who must remember.
