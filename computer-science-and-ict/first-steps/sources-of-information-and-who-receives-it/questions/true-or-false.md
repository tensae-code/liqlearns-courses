# Sources of Information and Who Receives It — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A road sign can be a source of information.**
**Answer: True** — A sign gives information without speaking.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A receiver is always a machine.**
**Answer: False** — A receiver is the one who takes information in — very often a person.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The same person can be a source at one moment and a receiver at the next.**
**Answer: True** — Listening makes you a receiver; speaking makes you a source.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) When a radio plays, nobody is a receiver if nobody listens.**
**Answer: True** — A receiver is the one who takes the information in — if no one hears it, no one receives it.
