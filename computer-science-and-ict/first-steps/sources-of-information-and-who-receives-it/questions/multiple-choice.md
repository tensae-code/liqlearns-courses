# Sources of Information and Who Receives It — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does information start?**
a) With the receiver  b) With the source  c) With nobody
**Answer: b) With the source** — A source is the one who gives the information — that is where it starts.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Your teacher is speaking and you are listening. The teacher is —**
a) the receiver  b) the source  c) neither
**Answer: b) the source** — The teacher gives the information; the listening pupils receive it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) When you read a book, you are —**
a) the source  b) the receiver  c) the book
**Answer: b) the receiver** — The book is the source; the reader takes the information in.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) You tell a friend a story. Now you are —**
a) only a receiver  b) a source, and your friend is the receiver  c) nobody
**Answer: b) a source, and your friend is the receiver** — Speaking makes you the source; listening makes your friend the receiver.
