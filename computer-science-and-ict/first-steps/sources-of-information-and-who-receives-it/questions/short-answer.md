# Sources of Information and Who Receives It — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name three sources of information you can see or hear today.**
**Answer:** Any three of: a teacher, a book, a radio, a television, a road sign, a person speaking, a picture or notice — each is a place information starts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A friend tells you a joke. Who is the source, and who is the receiver?**
**Answer:** The friend telling is the source; you, listening, are the receiver.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) How can one child be both a source and a receiver in one minute?**
**Answer:** By listening first (receiver) and then speaking (source) — the job changes with what the child is doing, not with who the child is.
