# Sources of Information and Who Receives It — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The one who gives information is called its ____.**
**Answer: source**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The one who takes information in is called the ____.**
**Answer: receiver**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) When you listen, you are a receiver; when you ____, you are a source.**
**Answer: speak**
