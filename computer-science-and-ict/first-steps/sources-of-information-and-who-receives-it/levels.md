# Sources of Information and Who Receives It — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The source — where information comes from
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A source gives information: a teacher telling, a book showing words and pictures, a radio playing, a sign on the road. If you can point to where the information started, you have found its source.
## Sub lesson 2 — The receiver — who takes the information in
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A receiver takes information in: the pupils listening, the reader, the person who hears the radio. The same person swaps jobs all day — receiving when listening, a source when speaking.
