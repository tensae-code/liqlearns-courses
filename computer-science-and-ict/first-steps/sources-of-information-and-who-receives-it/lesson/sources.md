The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — first-steps/sources-of-information-and-who-receives-it). It sits in the First Steps title (first-steps/).

Turkmenistan's own way: Informatics begins at age six with no machine time assumed — in Chapter III, Information around us, information is taught through sources and receivers in the child's own world (a voice, a book, a sign) before it is ever taught as something a computer handles. This lesson keeps that order.
