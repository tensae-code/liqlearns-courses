# Hands-on — Sources of Information and Who Receives It

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Find the two ends
Look around the room. Name three pieces of information you can see or hear right now. For each one, point to its source, then point to yourself: you are the receiver.

## Swap the jobs
With a friend: one speaks, one listens. Name who is the source and who is the receiver. Then swap, and name them again.
