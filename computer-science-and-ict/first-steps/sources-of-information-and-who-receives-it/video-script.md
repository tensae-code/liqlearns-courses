# Video script — Sources of Information and Who Receives It

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Information comes from somewhere.

And it goes to someone.

The one who gives it is the source.

A teacher. A book. A radio. A sign.

The one who takes it in is the receiver.

That is you, when you listen and read.

When you speak, you are the source.

When you listen, you are the receiver.

You swap jobs all day long.
