# Hands-on — Representing Information with Numbers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Write it in numbers', 'Write three facts — your age, whether it rained today, and where you sit in class — using numbers only, and explain each writing.')

## Task two
('Count a message', 'Count the symbols in a one-line message, then say how the count changes when the message doubles.')
