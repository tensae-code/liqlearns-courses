# Representing Information with Numbers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a computer store, at bottom?**
a) Only numbers  b) 1 or 0  c) By two numbers  d) Between conversions and coding
**Answer: a) Only numbers**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How can yes or no be written as numbers?**
a) By two numbers  b) 1 or 0  c) Between conversions and coding  d) That numbers can stand for things
**Answer: b) 1 or 0**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a place on a grid written?**
a) Between conversions and coding  b) That numbers can stand for things  c) By two numbers  d) Measure the amount of information
**Answer: c) By two numbers**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does this lesson sit in the Grade 8 book?**
a) That numbers can stand for things  b) Measure the amount of information  c) In the message's digits and symbols  d) Between conversions and coding
**Answer: d) Between conversions and coding**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What must a pupil learn before codes?**
a) That numbers can stand for things  b) Measure the amount of information  c) In the message's digits and symbols  d) Volume of information and transfer speed
**Answer: a) That numbers can stand for things**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does representing with numbers let us do?**
a) In the message's digits and symbols  b) Measure the amount of information  c) Volume of information and transfer speed  d) Representation
**Answer: b) Measure the amount of information**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) In what is the amount counted?**
a) Volume of information and transfer speed  b) Representation  c) In the message's digits and symbols  d) Numbers
**Answer: c) In the message's digits and symbols**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which two ideas follow this lesson in the book?**
a) Representation  b) Numbers  c) Only numbers  d) Volume of information and transfer speed
**Answer: d) Volume of information and transfer speed**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which comes first — representation, measurement or code?**
a) Representation  b) Numbers  c) Only numbers  d) 1 or 0
**Answer: a) Representation**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a bigger store hold more of?**
a) Only numbers  b) Numbers  c) 1 or 0  d) By two numbers
**Answer: b) Numbers**
