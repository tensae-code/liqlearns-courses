# Fourth generation — microprocessors and the PC — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** VLSI stands for —
a) Very Large Scale Integrated (circuits)  b) Very Light Small Instrument  c) Video Language System International  d) Virtual Learning Software Inside
**Answer: a) Very Large Scale Integrated (circuits)** — VLSI = very large scale integrated circuits.

**2.** A VLSI microchip holds —
a) One transistor  b) Ten transistors  c) Hundreds of thousands of transistors  d) No transistors
**Answer: c) Hundreds of thousands of transistors** — A VLSI chip holds hundreds of thousands of transistors.

**3.** The fourth generation is built around —
a) The vacuum tube  b) The microprocessor  c) Magnetic tape  d) The typewriter
**Answer: b) The microprocessor** — The personal computer revolution was built around the microprocessor.

**4.** What did the fourth generation bring about?
a) The PC revolution — personal computers  b) The first computer  c) The internet café only  d) Handwriting
**Answer: a) The PC revolution — personal computers** — Small, affordable computers brought the personal computer (PC) revolution.

**5.** The fourth generation began in —
a) 1956  b) 1964  c) 1971  d) 2000
**Answer: c) 1971** — Fourth generation: 1971–present.
