**Fourth generation.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: the fourth generation (1971–present) packs hundreds of thousands of transistors onto one microchip — the microprocessor — and that is what brought the personal computer.

**VLSI — very large scale integration.** The fourth generation uses very large scale integrated (VLSI) circuits: a microchip holding hundreds of thousands of transistors.
**The microprocessor and the PC revolution.** The fourth generation is built around the microprocessor — the chip that does the computer's processing.

Now you: Draw a microprocessor resting in an open palm, beside a desktop PC.
