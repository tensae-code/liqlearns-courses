# Hands-on — Fourth generation — microprocessors and the PC

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Palm power
Trace your hand on paper and draw a small square in the palm labelled 'microprocessor — hundreds of thousands of transistors'. Beside it draw the room-sized machine of the first generation. Compare the two drawings.

## PC spotter
Walk through your home or school and count every device that exists because computers became personal: phones, laptops, tills, ATMs. List them.
