# Fourth generation — microprocessors and the PC

## Do it first

## VLSI — very large scale integration

The fourth generation uses very large scale integrated (VLSI) circuits: a microchip holding hundreds of thousands of transistors. With so much on one chip, computers became more powerful, compact, reliable and affordable — small enough, in time, to hold in the palm of the hand.

## The microprocessor and the PC revolution

The fourth generation is built around the microprocessor — the chip that does the computer's processing. Because computers became small and affordable, they stopped belonging only to big organisations: the personal computer (PC) revolution put a computer on one person's desk. This generation began in 1971 and is still running today.
