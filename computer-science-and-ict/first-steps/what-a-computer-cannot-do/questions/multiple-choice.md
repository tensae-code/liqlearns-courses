# What a computer cannot do — no IQ, no feelings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The power of remembering means —
a) The computer forgets quickly  b) Any amount of information can be stored and recalled for as long as required  c) The computer dreams  d) Only one page can be kept
**Answer: b) Any amount of information can be stored and recalled for as long as required** — Any amount of information can be stored and recalled for as long as required.

**2.** A computer has no IQ, which means —
a) It is slow  b) It cannot do any work without instructions from the user  c) It cannot store data  d) It cannot show pictures
**Answer: b) It cannot do any work without instructions from the user** — No IQ: no work without instructions from the user.

**3.** Which of these does a computer NOT have?
a) Storage  b) Speed  c) Feelings and emotions of its own  d) Accuracy
**Answer: c) Feelings and emotions of its own** — A computer has no feeling: no emotions, taste, knowledge or experience of its own.

**4.** A large amount of data can be held —
a) Only on paper  b) In the computer's in-built memory  c) Nowhere  d) Only for one second
**Answer: b) In the computer's in-built memory** — Storage capacity: a large amount of data in its in-built memory.
