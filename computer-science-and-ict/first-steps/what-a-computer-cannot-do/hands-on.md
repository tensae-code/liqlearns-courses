# Hands-on — What a computer cannot do — no IQ, no feelings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Instruction test
Give a partner robot-orders for making tea, word by word, exactly. If a step is missing, the partner stops dead. That stop is 'no IQ' — no instruction, no work.

## Memory contest
Read a list of twenty words once to a partner and test recall after a minute. Then type the list into a phone or write it in a notebook file. Which 'memory' recalled all twenty perfectly, and for how long?
