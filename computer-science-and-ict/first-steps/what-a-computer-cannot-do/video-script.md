# Video script — What a computer cannot do — no IQ, no feelings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**What a computer cannot do.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: a computer remembers powerfully and stores hugely — but it has no IQ of its own and no feelings: without instructions from the user, it can do no work at all.

**The power of remembering.** A computer has the power of remembering: any amount of information can be stored in it and recalled for as long as required.
**No IQ, no feelings.** But a computer has no IQ: it cannot do any work without instructions from the user.

Now you: Draw a computer beside a huge memory cabinet, with a crossed-out brain and a crossed-out heart.
