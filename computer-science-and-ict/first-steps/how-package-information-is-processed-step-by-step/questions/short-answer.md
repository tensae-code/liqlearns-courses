# How Package Information Is Processed, Step by Step — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) By what is package information processed?**
**Answer: By a method — a fixed order of named steps**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many steps has the book's method?**
**Answer: Thirteen**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) As what is the method drawn?**
**Answer: A flowchart — steps as blocks, order as flow**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What runs from the method's start to its end?**
**Answer: Taking the package in, working its kinds, presenting the finished whole**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why do named steps matter?**
**Answer: The method can be repeated by another worker and repaired at its failing step**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to unmethodical work when it fails?**
**Answer: It can only be redone entire**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the worked example?**
**Answer: A household budget**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the budget package's kinds.**
**Answer: Incomes and prices as numbers, lists as text, the picture of need**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why a household example?**
**Answer: The citizen book proves its method on the citizen's own information first**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the method's whole promise?**
**Answer: A result that can be defended step by step**
