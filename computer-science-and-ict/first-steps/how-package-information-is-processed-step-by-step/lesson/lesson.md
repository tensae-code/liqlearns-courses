# How Package Information Is Processed, Step by Step

## Do it first

A package gathered must be worked. Mongolia's processing chapter gives the working a method with numbered steps — thirteen of them, drawn as a flowchart so the method itself is a model — and proves the method on the most citizenly example there is: a household budget.

## Sub lesson 1 — The 13-step method — a flowchart for working a package

Package information is processed by a **method**: a fixed order of steps running from taking the package in — gathering and checking its kinds — through ordering, working and joining them, to presenting the finished whole. The book draws the method as a **flowchart**: the steps as blocks, their order as the flow, so the method can be followed, taught and checked like any algorithm. Thirteen steps is the book's count, and the count matters less than the discipline: a method with named steps can be repeated by another worker and repaired at its failing step, where unmethodical work can only be redone entire.

In your mind's eye: the 13-step method — a flowchart for working a package.

## Sub lesson 2 — The household budget — the method at home

The worked example is a **household budget**: the family's information package — incomes and prices as numbers, lists as text, the week's picture of need — is taken through the steps: gathered, checked, ordered by the method, worked into the budget's judgements, and presented as the month's plan. Mongolia's choice is deliberate: the citizen book's method is proved on the citizen's own information, in the home, before it is trusted anywhere larger. Process the package by steps, and the result can be defended step by step — the method's whole promise.
