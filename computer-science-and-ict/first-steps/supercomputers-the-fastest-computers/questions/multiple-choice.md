# Supercomputers — the fastest computers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A supercomputer processes —
a) Millions of instructions per second  b) Trillions of instructions per second  c) Hundreds of instructions per second  d) One instruction per second
**Answer: b) Trillions of instructions per second** — Supercomputers process trillions of instructions per second.

**2.** Which of these uses a supercomputer?
a) A national weather forecasting agency  b) A pupil writing homework  c) A shop till  d) A pocket calculator
**Answer: a) A national weather forecasting agency** — National weather forecasting agencies are named supercomputer users.

**3.** The supercomputer is —
a) The slowest type  b) The fastest and most powerful type  c) The smallest type  d) A type of tablet
**Answer: b) The fastest and most powerful type** — It is the fastest and most powerful type of computer.

**4.** Which list puts the size ladder in order?
a) Super, mainframe, micro, mini  b) Micro, mini, mainframe, supercomputer  c) Mini, micro, super, mainframe  d) Mainframe, micro, mini, super
**Answer: b) Micro, mini, mainframe, supercomputer** — By size: microcomputer, minicomputer, mainframe, supercomputer.
