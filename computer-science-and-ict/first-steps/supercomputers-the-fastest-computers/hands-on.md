# Hands-on — Supercomputers — the fastest computers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trillion counting
Try to count aloud for ten seconds and note your number. A supercomputer does trillions of instructions in that same second — talk with a partner about the gap between human counting and the machine.

## Job sorting
On cards, write six jobs: class roster, national weather forecast, shop accounts, aircraft design, a game, defence calculations. Sort them: supercomputer jobs and not. Defend each sort.
