# Supercomputers — the fastest computers

## Do it first

A weather office must work out tomorrow's rain for a whole country before tonight. Millions of instructions per second is not enough. Before reading on, guess how many instructions per second the fastest machines do — thousands, millions, or trillions?

## The top of the ladder

The supercomputer is the fastest and most powerful type of computer. Where the mainframe processes millions of instructions per second, the supercomputer processes trillions of instructions per second.

## Who uses supercomputers

Its users are research organisations, military defence systems, national weather forecasting agencies, large corporations and aircraft manufacturers — the jobs too big and too urgent for any other machine: predicting weather, designing aircraft, defending a country, and the largest research questions.
