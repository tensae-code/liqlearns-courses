# Hands-on — Data — raw facts with no meaning yet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make meaningless data
Write five numbers and three names on a slip, with no title and no explanation. Give it to someone at home. Ask them what it means. Their puzzlement is the lesson: data alone has no meaning.

## Data around you
In one hour, note five pieces of data you meet: a price, a time, a house number, a name called out, a bell ringing. Write what form each one is — number, text, symbol, speech or image.
