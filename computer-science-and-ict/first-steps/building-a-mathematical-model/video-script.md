# Video script — Building a Mathematical Model

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **mathematical model** describes its object with mathematics — quantities and the relations between them. Its simplest face is spatial: the **seating map**, a grid where each seat is a coordinate pair, the classroom modelled so any pupil can be found by numbers. Its pictorial face is the **function's graph**: the relation between two quantities drawn as a line, so the eye reads at once what the formula says slowly — growth, steadiness, fall. Map and graph teach the same truth: mathematics models by turning 'where' and 'how much' into number and relation.
The model's working face is the formula that **computes**: the book's example is **virus growth**, V = V₀(1+d)ⁿ — the count V after n steps, from a starting count V₀ growing by a rate d each step. Pour in a start and a rate, and the model answers for step 10, step 100 — futures no observation could wait for. Here the strand's arc completes: the property table described, the stages disciplined, and the mathematical model **predicts** — the strongest thing a model can do, and the reason the computer, the tireless calculator of such formulas, waits in the next lessons.
