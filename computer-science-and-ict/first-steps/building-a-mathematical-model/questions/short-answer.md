# Building a Mathematical Model — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a mathematical model describe its object with?**
**Answer: Quantities and the relations between them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a seating map as a model?**
**Answer: A grid where each seat is a coordinate pair, found by numbers**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a function's graph show?**
**Answer: The relation between two quantities, read by the eye at once**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) State the virus-growth model.**
**Answer: V = V₀(1+d)ⁿ**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do V₀, d and n stand for?**
**Answer: The starting count, the growth rate per step, and the number of steps**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What can the growth model answer that observation cannot wait for?**
**Answer: The count at step 10, step 100 — futures**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the strongest thing a model can do?**
**Answer: Predict**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which machine waits to calculate such formulas?**
**Answer: The computer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name the mathematical model's three faces in the lesson.**
**Answer: Spatial (map), pictorial (graph), and working (formula)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does mathematics model, in the lesson's phrase?**
**Answer: By turning 'where' and 'how much' into number and relation**
