# Building a Mathematical Model — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a mathematical model describe its object with?**
a) Quantities and the relations between them  b) A grid where each seat is a coordinate pair, found by numbers  c) The relation between two quantities, read by the eye at once  d) V = V₀(1+d)ⁿ
