# Hands-on — Building a Mathematical Model

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Map the room', 'Draw your classroom's seating map with coordinates. Give the coordinates of three pupils and find one pupil from coordinates alone.')

## Task two
('Run the growth', 'A virus count starts at 100 and grows by rate 0.5 each step. Compute the count at steps 1, 2, 3 and 10 from the model, and say what pours in and what reads off.')
