# Hands-on — Binary — and How Integers Are Stored

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Count and convert** — Write the decimal numbers 5, 13 and 26 in binary, then read 1101 and 10010 back into decimal. Show the place values you used.

## Task two
**Overflow by hand** — With only 4 bits for unsigned integers, add 9 + 8 on paper in binary. What goes wrong, and what is it called?
