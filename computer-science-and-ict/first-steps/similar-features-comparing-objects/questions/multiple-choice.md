# Similar Features — Comparing Objects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A feature of an object can be —**
a) its colour  b) its name day  c) a song
**Answer: a) its colour** — Colour, shape and size are features.

**2. A red ball and a red apple share which similar feature?**
a) They are both books  b) They are both red and round  c) They can fly
**Answer: b) They are both red and round** — Red is a shared colour; round is a shared shape.

**3. To compare objects well, look at —**
a) everything at once  b) one feature at a time  c) only the name
**Answer: b) one feature at a time** — Colour, then shape, then size, one by one.

**4. Two pencils are similar because both are —**
a) long and thin, for writing  b) made of water  c) alive
**Answer: a) long and thin, for writing** — Those are features the two pencils share.
