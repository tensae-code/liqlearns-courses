# What ICT is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How many units does the Grade 7 Information Technology course have?**
a) Four  b) Five  c) Six  d) Eight
**Answer: c) Six** — The road map shows six units, from what ICT is to LOGO programming.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does every unit in this course start with?**
a) A test  b) A brainstorm that links to what you already know  c) A definition to copy  d) A video
**Answer: b) A brainstorm that links to what you already know** — The book's rhythm is brainstorm-first, before any definition is given.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the last unit of the course?**
a) The internet  b) LOGO programming  c) Hardware  d) Safety
**Answer: b) LOGO programming** — LOGO programming is Unit 6, the end of the road map.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Which list is all ICT?**
a) Phone, television, computer, tablet, camera  b) Table, chair, book, pencil  c) Phone, table, television, shoe
**Answer: a) Phone, television, computer, tablet, camera** — ICT is the communication technologies together.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) ICT joins computer networks with —**
a) water pipes  b) telephone and audio-visual networks  c) roads and railways
**Answer: b) telephone and audio-visual networks** — The book says ICT joins telephone and audio-visual networks with computer networks.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which of these can people do with information through ICT?**
a) Only watch it  b) Access, retrieve, store, transmit and change it  c) Print it on paper only
**Answer: b) Access, retrieve, store, transmit and change it** — Those five verbs are the book's own list.
