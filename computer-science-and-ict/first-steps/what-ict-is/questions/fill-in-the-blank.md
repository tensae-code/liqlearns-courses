# What ICT is — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The course road map ends with a first ____ program.**
**Answer: LOGO** — Unit 6 is LOGO programming.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ICT stands for Information and ____ Technology.**
**Answer: Communication**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A tablet that stores a photo is doing the ____ part of ICT's work.**
**Answer: store / storing** — One of the five verbs: access, retrieve, store, transmit, change.
