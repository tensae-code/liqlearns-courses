# Video script — What ICT is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This course has six steps.

First: what ICT is. Then hardware. Then software.

Then the internet. Then safety. Last: your first program.

Every step starts with a brainstorm — what do you already know?

ICT is all communication technologies together.

Phones. Television. Computers. Tablets. Cameras. The internet.

Joined together, they let us access, store, send and change information.
