# Lesson plan — What ICT is

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** What ICT is
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Introduction to Information & Communication Technology — in the title First Steps on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** ICT is all the communication technologies together — internet, mobile phones, television, computers, tablets and cameras — joining telephone and audio-visual networks with computer networks so people can access, retrieve, store, transmit and change information in digital form.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on nothing but the learner's own daily world — the phones, television and computers they already see at home and at school. It is the first lesson of the course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The road map of this course, and its brainstorm-first rhythm** — The six units of the Grade 7 course in order — what ICT is, hardware, software, the internet, safety and security, and a first LOGO program — and the rhythm every unit follows: first a brainstorm that links to what you already know, then the new idea.
2. **What ICT is** — ICT as all communication technologies together, joining telephone and audio-visual networks with computer networks, so people can access, retrieve, store, transmit and change information in digital form.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the lessons before this one in First Steps on the ladder. Recommended, never forced — a learner may land on this lesson directly, and it stands alone.
- The book's own rhythm is kept: this lesson opens with a brainstorm — the learner first says what they already know — before any definition is given.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say the six units of the course in order, and say what a brainstorm-first opening does.
2. Say what ICT stands for, and name the technologies the book counts as ICT.
3. Name the two kinds of networks ICT joins together.
4. Name the five things people can do with information through ICT: access, retrieve, store, transmit and change it.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — your own home and street are the examples.

## If the learner is stuck
Thinks ICT is one machine → it is a family: many technologies together, joined by networks. Names only computers → ask what carries a voice (telephone network) and what carries a picture (audio-visual network) — ICT joins those to computer networks too.
