# Hands-on — What ICT is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The brainstorm list
Before reading the explanation again, write down everything you already knew about phones, television and computers — three lines minimum. This is the brainstorm habit: every unit in this course starts from what you already know.

## ICT hunt at home
Walk through your home. Write the name of every ICT thing you find — phone, television, radio, tablet, camera — and beside each, one thing it does: sends a message, shows a film, stores a photo.

## Five verbs
For one device on your list, write one example of each verb: access, retrieve, store, transmit, change. Example for a phone: store a photo, transmit a message, change a contact's name.
