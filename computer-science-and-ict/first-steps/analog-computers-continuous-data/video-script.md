**Analog computers.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: an analog computer processes continuous data — speed, temperature, pressure, current — taken straight from a measuring device, and gives its answer as a reading on a dial or scale.

**Continuous data.** By method of operation there are three types of computer.
**Answers on a dial.** An analog computer gives its output as a reading on a dial or scale — like the sweeping needle in the GIF.

Now you: Draw a speedometer with its needle sweeping, and label it 'analog — continuous'.
