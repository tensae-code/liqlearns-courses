# Lesson plan — Analog computers — continuous data

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Analog computers — continuous data
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 1 — First Steps of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 1 — Basics of Computer.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an analog computer processes continuous data — speed, temperature, pressure, current — taken straight from a measuring device, and gives its answer as a reading on a dial or scale.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the classification lessons: after size comes classification by method of operation, which has three types — analog, digital, hybrid.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Watch a car's speedometer as it speeds up: the needle glides smoothly, never jumping in steps. Now watch a digital clock: the numbers hop from one value to the next. Before reading on, say which movement is 'continuous' and which is in separate steps. (lesson/lesson.md).
