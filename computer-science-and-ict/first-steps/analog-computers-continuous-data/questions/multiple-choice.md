# Analog computers — continuous data — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** An analog computer processes —
a) Continuous data  b) Only text  c) Discrete values only  d) Printed pages
**Answer: a) Continuous data** — Analog computers process continuous data.

**2.** Which of these is continuous data?
a) A count of pupils: 1, 2, 3  b) Temperature rising smoothly  c) Letters of the alphabet  d) A list of names
**Answer: b) Temperature rising smoothly** — Speed, temperature, pressure and current flow continuously.

**3.** An analog computer takes data —
a) Only from a keyboard  b) Straight from a measuring device, without converting it into numbers and codes first  c) From the internet  d) From a printer
**Answer: b) Straight from a measuring device, without converting it into numbers and codes first** — It takes data straight from a measuring device without first converting it into numbers and codes.

**4.** Analog output is given as —
a) A printed report  b) A reading on a dial or scale  c) A sound  d) A saved file
**Answer: b) A reading on a dial or scale** — Its output is a reading on a dial or scale.

**5.** An everyday analog example is —
a) A word processor  b) A speedometer  c) A spreadsheet  d) An email
**Answer: b) A speedometer** — The speedometer and the mercury thermometer are the everyday examples.
