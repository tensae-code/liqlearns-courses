# Hands-on — Analog computers — continuous data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Smooth or steps?
Act out a needle gliding smoothly, then a counter clicking in steps. A partner names each movement: continuous (analog) or separate values (digital). Five rounds.

## Dial reading walk
Find dials and scales around you — a thermometer, a weighing scale, a speedometer in a parked car. Read each one aloud; every reading came straight from a measuring device.
