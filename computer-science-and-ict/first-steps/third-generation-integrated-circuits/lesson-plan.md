# Lesson plan — Third generation — integrated circuits

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Third generation — integrated circuits
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 1 — First Steps of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 1 — Basics of Computer.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the third generation (1964–1971) came with the integrated circuit — many small interconnected components on one small chip of semiconductor material.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the second-generation lesson: transistors were small, but they still sat one by one on boards. Putting many of them together on one chip is the next change.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks 'integrated' means the chip is glued on top → integrated means many components joined into one system on the chip itself.
- Forgets the material → the chip is made from semiconductor material.
