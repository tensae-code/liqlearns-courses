# Hands-on — Third generation — integrated circuits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One chip, many parts
On a small square of paper, draw as many tiny joined shapes as you can fit — circles, lines, dots. That crowd on one small square is the idea of an integrated circuit.

## Shrink it
Draw the same simple circuit three ways: big separate parts, smaller parts, then all on one chip. Label the generations 1, 2 and 3 under them.
