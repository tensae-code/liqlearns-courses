**Third generation.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: the third generation (1964–1971) came with the integrated circuit — many small interconnected components on one small chip of semiconductor material.

**The integrated circuit.** The third generation came with the integrated circuit: a small device made from semiconductor material.
**Why it mattered.** Putting many components on one chip made computers smaller, cheaper and more reliable than machines built from separate transistors.

Now you: Draw one chip with many tiny connected parts inside it, and label it 'integrated circuit'.
