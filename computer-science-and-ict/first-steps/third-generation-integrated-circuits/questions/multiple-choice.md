# Third generation — integrated circuits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The third generation came with —
a) Vacuum tubes  b) The integrated circuit  c) Artificial intelligence  d) The mouse
**Answer: b) The integrated circuit** — Third generation (1964–1971): integrated circuits.

**2.** An integrated circuit is —
a) One big glass tube  b) Many small interconnected components on one chip  c) A type of printer  d) A computer game
**Answer: b) Many small interconnected components on one chip** — It is an integrated system of many small interconnected components on one chip.

**3.** An integrated circuit is made from —
a) Wood  b) Glass only  c) Semiconductor material  d) Paper
**Answer: c) Semiconductor material** — The chip is made from semiconductor material.

**4.** The third generation ran from —
a) 1956–1963  b) 1964–1971  c) 1971–today  d) 1940–1956
**Answer: b) 1964–1971** — Third generation: 1964–1971.
