## The integrated circuit

The third generation came with the integrated circuit: a small device made from semiconductor material. It is an integrated system — many small interconnected components joined together on one chip, instead of separate parts wired one by one.

[Picture 10904 — third generation]

## Why it mattered

Putting many components on one chip made computers smaller, cheaper and more reliable than machines built from separate transistors. The third generation runs from 1964 to 1971.
