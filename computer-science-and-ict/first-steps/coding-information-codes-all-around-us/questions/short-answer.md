# Coding Information — Codes All Around Us — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a code?**
**Answer: A system of signs with agreed meanings, representing information in another form**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does the alphabet-position code work?**
**Answer: Each letter is replaced by its number in the alphabet**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does Morse code turn letters into?**
**Answer: Short and long signals — dots and dashes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) By what can Morse travel?**
**Answer: Light, sound or wire**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does music notation code?**
**Answer: Sounds — fixing pitch and length so a song can be stored and read back**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do a road sign's shape and colour announce?**
**Answer: The kind of message — warning, forbidding, directing — before the symbol is read**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a pictogram?**
**Answer: A picture-code — a small standard figure understood at a glance**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why do pictograms work across languages?**
**Answer: They are read by the picture, not by words**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When does a code work at all?**
**Answer: Only when sender and receiver share it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is coding, in the lesson's closing phrase?**
**Answer: Agreement, written in signs**
