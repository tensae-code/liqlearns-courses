# Coding Information — Codes All Around Us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a code?**
a) A system of signs with agreed meanings, representing information in another form  b) Each letter is replaced by its number in the alphabet  c) Short and long signals — dots and dashes  d) Light, sound or wire
**Answer: a) A system of signs with agreed meanings, representing information in another form**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does the alphabet-position code work?**
a) Short and long signals — dots and dashes  b) Each letter is replaced by its number in the alphabet  c) Light, sound or wire  d) Sounds — fixing pitch and length so a song can be stored and read back
**Answer: b) Each letter is replaced by its number in the alphabet**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does Morse code turn letters into?**
a) Light, sound or wire  b) Sounds — fixing pitch and length so a song can be stored and read back  c) Short and long signals — dots and dashes  d) The kind of message — warning, forbidding, directing — before the symbol is read
**Answer: c) Short and long signals — dots and dashes**
