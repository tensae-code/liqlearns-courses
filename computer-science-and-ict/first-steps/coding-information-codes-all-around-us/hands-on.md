# Hands-on — Coding Information — Codes All Around Us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Send it three ways', 'Write your name in the alphabet-position code, in Morse, and as a pictogram message of your own design. Swap with a partner and decode each other's.')

## Task two
('Read the street', 'Sketch three road signs or public pictograms from your area. For each, write what it codes and how its shape or colour helps.')
