# Video script — Getting Information Through the Senses

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

You take in the world with your senses.

Eyes take in colour and shape.

Ears take in sound.

The nose takes in smells.

The tongue takes in tastes.

Skin takes in touch: hot, cold, soft, hard.

Five senses.

Five ways to receive information.
