Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Turkmenistan, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked first: no lesson in First Steps or elsewhere teaches receiving information through the sense organs. The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — first-steps/getting-information-through-the-senses). It sits in the First Steps title (first-steps/). It is one whole lesson with no sub lessons — a short, single-skill lesson (the 2026-11-09 ruling): the five senses are one list learned together, not sequential parts.

Turkmenistan's own way: Informatics begins at age six with no machine time assumed — in Chapter III, Information around us, the ways of receiving information are taught through the five senses and the sense organs, so a child meets 'receiving' in their own body before meeting it in any device. This lesson keeps that order.
