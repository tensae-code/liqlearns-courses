# Getting Information Through the Senses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which sense takes in colour and shape?**
a) The ears  b) The eyes  c) The skin
**Answer: b) The eyes** — Eyes take in colours, shapes, sizes and light.

**2. You hear a bell ringing. Which sense received that information?**
a) Smell  b) Taste  c) Hearing, through the ears
**Answer: c) Hearing, through the ears** — Ears take in sounds.

**3. With your skin you can feel —**
a) hot and cold, soft and hard  b) colours  c) songs
**Answer: a) hot and cold, soft and hard** — Skin takes in touch.

**4. How many senses does this lesson name?**
a) Three  b) Five  c) Ten
**Answer: b) Five** — Eyes, ears, nose, tongue and skin — five senses.
