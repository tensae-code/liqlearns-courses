# Getting Information Through the Senses — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. The nose takes in tastes.**
**Answer: False** — The nose takes in smells; the tongue takes in tastes.

**2. You can receive information with your eyes closed.**
**Answer: True** — Ears, nose, tongue and skin keep receiving — hearing a bell needs no eyes.

**3. The tongue can tell sweet from sour.**
**Answer: True** — Tastes — sweet, sour, salty — are the tongue's information.

**4. Only computers can receive information.**
**Answer: False** — Your five senses receive information all day long.
