# Getting Information Through the Senses — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Name the five senses and the organ for each.**
**Answer:** Sight through the eyes, hearing through the ears, smell through the nose, taste through the tongue, and touch through the skin.

**2. A friend is baking bread in the next room. You have not seen it. Which senses could tell you, and how?**
**Answer:** The nose (the smell of baking bread) and the ears (sounds from the kitchen); taste only once a piece is given to you.

**3. Why does this lesson call each sense organ a receiver?**
**Answer:** Because each one takes information in — colour, sound, smell, taste or touch — from the world around the child.
