# Hands-on — Getting Information Through the Senses

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Five senses walk
Walk slowly around the room with your teacher. Stop five times. Each time, use one sense and say what information it gave you: I see…, I hear…, I smell…, I taste… (only what your teacher gives you), I feel….

## Which sense?
Your teacher names a thing: a bell ringing, a red ball, a warm cup, a sweet, a flower. For each one, point to the sense organ that receives it.
