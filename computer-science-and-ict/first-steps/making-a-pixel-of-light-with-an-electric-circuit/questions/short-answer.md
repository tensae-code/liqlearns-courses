# Making a Pixel of Light With an Electric Circuit — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a circuit?**
**Answer: A closed loop the current can travel**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the circuit's parts in order.**
**Answer: Battery, wire, lamp, wire back to the battery**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens if the loop breaks anywhere?**
**Answer: The lamp dies**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are the French words for battery and lamp?**
**Answer: Pile and ampoule**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What discipline do the first builds teach?**
**Answer: Finding the break when the light refuses**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is one lit lamp, in screen language?**
**Answer: A pixel — one point of light, on or off**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What two states does a pixel have?**
**Answer: On and off**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is a screen, in this lesson's words?**
**Answer: Millions of switchable light points drawing pictures together**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does every pixel obey?**
**Answer: The same simple loop — a whole circuit**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why start with one lamp?**
**Answer: Because every screen is this build multiplied**
