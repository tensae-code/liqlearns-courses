# Hands-on — Making a Pixel of Light With an Electric Circuit

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Build the loop** — With a battery, two wires and a lamp (or the class kit), make the lamp light. Then break and remake the loop at each point, naming what you broke.

## Task two
**From one to many** — Draw a 4×4 grid of “lamps”. Colour the lit ones to draw a letter. Beside it, write how many separate circuits a real 4×4 light board would need.
