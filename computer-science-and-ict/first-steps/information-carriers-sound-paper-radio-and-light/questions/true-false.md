# Information Carriers — Sound, Paper, Radio and Light — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A sender, a carrier and a receiver**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A sender, a carrier and a receiver** — as the answer to: What is a carrier?
**Answer: False — The physical thing that bears information between sender and receiver**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Sound, paper, radio waves and light**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Sound, paper, radio waves and light** — as the answer to: What changes when the carrier changes?
**Answer: False — The journey — speed, distance, how long the information survives**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The information itself**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The information itself** — as the answer to: Which carrier keeps information longest?
**Answer: False — Paper — for centuries, with no power**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Radio waves and light**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Radio waves and light** — as the answer to: What is sound's weakness as a carrier?
**Answer: False — It is short-ranged and gone in a moment**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Electric signals in wires and light in fibres**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Electric signals in wires and light in fibres** — as the answer to: Why do broadcasts use radio waves?
**Answer: False — They cross countries fast and reach many receivers at once**
