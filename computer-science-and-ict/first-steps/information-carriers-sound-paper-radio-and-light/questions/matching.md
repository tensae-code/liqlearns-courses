# Information Carriers — Sound, Paper, Radio and Light — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the three parts of informing?** → **A sender, a carrier and a receiver**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a carrier?** → **The physical thing that bears information between sender and receiver**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four carriers of the lesson.** → **Sound, paper, radio waves and light**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What changes when the carrier changes?** → **The journey — speed, distance, how long the information survives**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What can stay the same when the carrier changes?** → **The information itself**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which carrier keeps information longest?** → **Paper — for centuries, with no power**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which carriers travel at the speed of light?** → **Radio waves and light**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is sound's weakness as a carrier?** → **It is short-ranged and gone in a moment**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What carries information inside computers?** → **Electric signals in wires and light in fibres**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why do broadcasts use radio waves?** → **They cross countries fast and reach many receivers at once**
