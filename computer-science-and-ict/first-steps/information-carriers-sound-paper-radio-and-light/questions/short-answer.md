# Information Carriers — Sound, Paper, Radio and Light — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the three parts of informing?**
**Answer: A sender, a carrier and a receiver**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a carrier?**
**Answer: The physical thing that bears information between sender and receiver**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four carriers of the lesson.**
**Answer: Sound, paper, radio waves and light**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What changes when the carrier changes?**
**Answer: The journey — speed, distance, how long the information survives**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What can stay the same when the carrier changes?**
**Answer: The information itself**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which carrier keeps information longest?**
**Answer: Paper — for centuries, with no power**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which carriers travel at the speed of light?**
**Answer: Radio waves and light**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is sound's weakness as a carrier?**
**Answer: It is short-ranged and gone in a moment**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What carries information inside computers?**
**Answer: Electric signals in wires and light in fibres**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why do broadcasts use radio waves?**
**Answer: They cross countries fast and reach many receivers at once**
