# Hands-on — Information Carriers — Sound, Paper, Radio and Light

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Carrier sort** — Take five messages (a shout, a letter, a radio news item, a lighthouse flash, a text message) and name sender, carrier and receiver for each.

## Task two
**Same message, three carriers** — Carry the message 'come at five' by voice, on paper and by a torch signal; write one strength and one weakness you felt in each.
