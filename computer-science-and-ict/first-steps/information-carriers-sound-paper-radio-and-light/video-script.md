# Video script — Information Carriers — Sound, Paper, Radio and Light

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every act of informing has three parts: a **sender** who gives the information, a **receiver** who takes it, and a **carrier (purtător)** — the physical thing that bears it between them. Your voice rides on **sound**; a letter's words ride on **paper**; a broadcast rides on **radio waves**; a signal lamp's message rides on **light**. Change the carrier and the journey changes — its speed, its distance, how long the information survives — while the information itself can stay exactly the same.
Carriers are chosen by their strengths. **Sound** is instant but short-ranged and gone in a moment. **Paper** is slow to travel but keeps information for centuries and needs no power. **Radio waves** cross countries at the speed of light and reach many receivers at once, but fade into the air. **Light** — in lamps, fibres and signals — is fastest of all and can be aimed exactly. The computer's carriers join this family: electric signals in wires and light in fibres are the carriers of the digital lessons ahead.
