# Hands-on — ICT in e-commerce — and ICT in big issues

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace a purchase
Describe one real or imagined internet purchase step by step: choosing, paying, receiving. Mark the exact step where funds move and the step where data moves.

## Group discussion, honestly
In a group of two to four, take one big issue from the lesson. Each person gives one ICT role for it. Write all the answers down — varied answers are accepted on these questions, as the guide instructs.
