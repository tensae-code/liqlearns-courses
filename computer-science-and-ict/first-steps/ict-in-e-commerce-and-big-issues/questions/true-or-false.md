# ICT in e-commerce — and ICT in big issues — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Transferring funds through the internet is part of e-commerce.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A buyer in e-commerce must always visit a physical shop.**
**Answer: False** — The basket and payment live in the phone.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The guide tells teachers to accept varied group answers on the big-issues discussion.**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Equal access to information for men and women is framed as women's empowerment in the guide's key.**
**Answer: True**
