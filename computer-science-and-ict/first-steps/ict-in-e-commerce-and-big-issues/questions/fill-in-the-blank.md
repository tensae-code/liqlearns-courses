# ICT in e-commerce — and ICT in big issues — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) E-commerce is buying and selling goods and ____ through the internet.**
**Answer: services**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Trusted ____ information is ICT's role for COVID-19 and HIV/AIDS.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Weather ____ is ICT's role for climate change and agriculture.**
