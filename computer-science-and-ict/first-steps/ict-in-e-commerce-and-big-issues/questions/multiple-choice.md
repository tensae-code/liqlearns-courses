# ICT in e-commerce — and ICT in big issues — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) E-commerce is —**
a) shopping only in a market  b) buying and selling goods and services through the internet  c) sending letters
**Answer: b) buying and selling goods and services through the internet** — It also covers transferring funds and data through the internet.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In e-commerce, what travels through the internet?**
a) Only the goods  b) Funds and data  c) Only advertisements
**Answer: b) Funds and data**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The 'e' in e-commerce points to —**
a) electronic — through the internet  b) expensive  c) everyday
**Answer: a) electronic — through the internet**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Against child trafficking, the guide keys ICT's role as —**
a) awareness-raising, reaching stakeholders through channels such as social media  b) selling tickets  c) games
**Answer: a) awareness-raising, reaching stakeholders through channels such as social media** — Guide printed p. 15.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) For COVID-19 and HIV/AIDS, ICT carries —**
a) trusted prevention information from bodies such as the Ministry of Health  b) rumours  c) only music
**Answer: a) trusted prevention information from bodies such as the Ministry of Health** — Guide key.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) For climate change and agriculture, the keyed ICT role is —**
a) weather forecasting  b) online shopping  c) video calls
**Answer: a) weather forecasting** — Guide key.
