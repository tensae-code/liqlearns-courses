# Lesson plan — ICT in e-commerce — and ICT in big issues

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** ICT in e-commerce — and ICT in big issues
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Introduction to Information & Communication Technology — in the title First Steps on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** E-commerce is buying and selling goods and services, or transferring funds and data, through the internet — and ICT also has a role in the big issues groups discuss: child trafficking, COVID-19, HIV/AIDS, and climate change and agriculture.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on ICT in entertainment: the last stop on the daily-life walk is the market — and the world's hard problems.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

2. Give the guide's keyed ICT roles: awareness against trafficking; trusted prevention information for COVID-19 and HIV/AIDS; weather forecasting for climate and agriculture.
3. Say why discussion answers on big issues can fairly vary.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Imagine buying something without walking to a shop: you choose it on a phone, money moves without any hand touching it, and the thing comes to you. That is the first half of this lesson. The second half asks a harder question: what can ICT do about the biggest problems a country faces? (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 10115]: a picture of a phone shopping basket with a payment arrow — the student the student traces the purchase: basket, payment arrow, and what actually travels (funds and data).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 10116]: a picture of a phone carrying a health-ministry prevention message — the student the student says who sent the message, who it protects, and why it must be trusted information.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key names and definitions aloud, in the order the lesson gives them — the student listens once through, then reads the same lines aloud along with the voice. (Audio is a described slot in this build; no audio file is made.)
