# Positional and Non-Positional Number Systems — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In a positional system, what does a digit's value depend on?**
**Answer: Its position**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In 222, what do the three twos stand for?**
**Answer: Two hundreds, two tens and two ones**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two positional systems.**
**Answer: Decimal and binary**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is each place's worth fixed?**
**Answer: The base times the place before it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why can positional systems write any number with few digits?**
**Answer: Because place multiplies a digit's value**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) In a non-positional system, what does a sign's value depend on?**
**Answer: Nothing — it keeps the same value wherever it stands**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is VIII in Roman writing?**
**Answer: 8 — five plus one plus one plus one**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Give an example of a non-positional system.**
**Answer: Roman numerals**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why are non-positional systems hard for arithmetic?**
**Answer: Big numbers grow heavy and rules do not carry them**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What base is the computer's positional system?**
**Answer: Base two**
