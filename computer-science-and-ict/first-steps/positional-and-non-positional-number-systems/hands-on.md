# Hands-on — Positional and Non-Positional Number Systems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Sort the systems', 'Write decimal, binary and Roman in two columns — positional and non-positional — and one number written in each.')

## Task two
('Same digits, two readings', 'Write 11 in decimal and in binary thinking: say what the left 1 is worth in each, and why.')
