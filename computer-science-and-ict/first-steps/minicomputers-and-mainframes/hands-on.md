# Hands-on — Minicomputers and mainframes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Many users at once
In a group, five people ask one 'computer' (a partner with paper) for answers at the same time, while the partner handles every request without stopping. Talk about what the partner needed: speed and the ability to serve many at once — the mainframe idea.

## Middle machine hunt
Discuss with a partner: what kind of office in your town might need more than a desktop but less than a mainframe? Defend the minicomputer as its answer.
