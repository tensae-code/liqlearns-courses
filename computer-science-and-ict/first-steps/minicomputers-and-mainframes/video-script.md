**Minicomputers and mainframes.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: above the microcomputer sit the minicomputer — a small general-purpose machine — and the mainframe: a large, powerful machine serving many users at once at millions of instructions per second.

**The minicomputer — the middle machine.** A minicomputer sits between the microcomputer and the mainframe: larger and more powerful than most microcomputers, smaller and less powerful than a mainframe.
**The mainframe — many users at once.** Mainframes are large, powerful machines.

Now you: Draw the size ladder with micro, mini and mainframe, and one user example at the mainframe step.
