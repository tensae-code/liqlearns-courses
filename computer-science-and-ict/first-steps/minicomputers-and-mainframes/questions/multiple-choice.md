# Minicomputers and mainframes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A minicomputer sits —
a) Below the microcomputer  b) Between the microcomputer and the mainframe  c) Above the supercomputer  d) Inside a smartphone
**Answer: b) Between the microcomputer and the mainframe** — The minicomputer sits between micro and mainframe.

**2.** A mainframe processes data at —
a) Ten instructions per second  b) Millions of instructions per second  c) One instruction per minute  d) No instructions at all
**Answer: b) Millions of instructions per second** — Mainframes process data at millions of instructions per second.

**3.** Which of these is a typical mainframe user?
a) A single pupil at home  b) A bank  c) A street vendor with one phone  d) A pocket calculator
**Answer: b) A bank** — Banks, insurance companies, manufacturers and airlines are the typical users.

**4.** A minicomputer is —
a) Less powerful than most microcomputers  b) Larger and more powerful than most microcomputers, smaller than a mainframe  c) The fastest computer of all  d) A type of phone
**Answer: b) Larger and more powerful than most microcomputers, smaller than a mainframe** — It is larger and more powerful than most micros, smaller and less powerful than a mainframe.
