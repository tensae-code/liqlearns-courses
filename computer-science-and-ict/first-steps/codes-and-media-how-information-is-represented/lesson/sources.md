Cambodia's own way: ICT is taught through Khmer-language office life — personal, official and business letters, job applications and Cornell note-taking — in OpenOffice Writer, with a dedicated session on typing Khmer script, and the book closes with STEM and e-learning rather than programming. This lesson keeps Cambodia's order: context, codes and media are met in Chapter 1 Lesson 3, opening the lesson that then turns to text-editing programs and typing Khmer script.
