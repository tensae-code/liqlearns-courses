# Codes and Media — How Information Is Represented — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Give two codes you read every day, and what their signs stand for.**
**Answer:** For example: letters (signs standing for sounds and words of a language) and digits (signs standing for numbers) — or traffic-light colours (red for stop, green for go).

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A message is shown as a picture sign. Name its code and its medium.**
**Answer:** The code is the picture-sign system (the drawn signs and what they stand for); the medium is the surface carrying it — the board, paper or screen it is shown on.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The same announcement must reach a person who is far away and a person who must keep proof of it. What two representations fit, and why?**
**Answer:** For the distant person, a sent message or spoken announcement that travels (sound or a phone message); for proof, written text, because it can be kept and checked later. The context — distance, and keeping proof — chooses the forms.
