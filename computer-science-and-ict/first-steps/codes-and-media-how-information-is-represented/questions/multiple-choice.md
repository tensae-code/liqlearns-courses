# Codes and Media — How Information Is Represented — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A code is —**
a) a system of signs whose meanings are shared, that information is put into  b) the paper a message is printed on  c) a secret only one person may ever know
**Answer: a) a system of signs whose meanings are shared** — Sender and receiver both read the signs the same way.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The context of a message is —**
a) its font  b) the whole situation around it: who sends, who must receive, where and why  c) its price
**Answer: b) the whole situation around it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A code fails when —**
a) the signs are coloured  b) the receiver does not share the code  c) the message is short
**Answer: b) the receiver does not share the code** — The signs arrive, but the information does not.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A medium is —**
a) the carrier the coded information rides on, such as paper, sound or a screen  b) the meaning of a sign  c) the sender of a message
**Answer: a) the carrier the coded information rides on**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) A warning that must be read from far away is best represented as —**
a) small written words  b) a large sign with a picture code  c) a whispered message
**Answer: b) a large sign with a picture code** — The context — distance — chooses the form.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) News for a person who cannot see the screen should ride in —**
a) a silent picture  b) sound  c) smaller text
**Answer: b) sound**
