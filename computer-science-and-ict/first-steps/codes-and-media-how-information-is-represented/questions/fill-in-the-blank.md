# Codes and Media — How Information Is Represented — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) At a traffic light, the colour red is a sign in a code: it stands for ____.**
**Answer: stop**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The carrier that coded information rides on — paper, sound, a screen — is called a ____.**
**Answer: medium**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who sends a message, who must receive it, where they are and why — together these make the message's ____.**
**Answer: context**
