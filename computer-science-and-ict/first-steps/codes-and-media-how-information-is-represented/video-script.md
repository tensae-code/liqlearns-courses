# Video script — Codes and Media — How Information Is Represented

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A message does not travel naked.

First, it goes into a code. A code is signs with shared meanings.

Letters are a code. Digits are a code. Red means stop. Green means go.

Then the signs ride on a medium. Paper. A picture. Sound. A screen.

Same message, many forms. A notice. A voice. A picture sign.

Who is it for? Where are they? Why do they need it?

That is the context. The context picks the right code and the right medium.
