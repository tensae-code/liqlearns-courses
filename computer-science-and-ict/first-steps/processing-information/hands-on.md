# Hands-on — Processing Information

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Process and prove', 'Take five classmates' heights. Process them into an average and a tallest/shortest statement. Then check by a second method and record any correction.')

## Task two
('Catch the error', 'Your teacher gives you a short processed result with one planted error. Find it by checking against the source, correct it, and write how the check caught it.')
