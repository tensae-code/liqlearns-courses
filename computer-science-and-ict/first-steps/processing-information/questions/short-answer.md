# Processing Information — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is processing information?**
**Answer: Working on received information to produce new information from it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does processed information differ from a copy?**
**Answer: The output says something the input did not say alone**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give an example of processing.**
**Answer: Numbers gathered becoming a total (any one of: sounds heard becoming words understood)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What errors can processed information carry?**
**Answer: A misheard word, a wrong total, a hasty conclusion (any one)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is checking?**
**Answer: Comparing the result against the source, a second method, or plain sense**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What follows a failed check?**
**Answer: Correcting what the check condemns**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does processing without checking manufacture?**
**Answer: Confident mistakes**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why must checking happen before storing?**
**Answer: Storing will faithfully keep whatever processing hands it — errors included**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Does processing need a computer?**
**Answer: No — the definition names the act, not the tool**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What are the information road's stations around this lesson?**
**Answer: Receiving before it, storing after it**
