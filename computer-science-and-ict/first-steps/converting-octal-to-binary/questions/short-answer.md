# Converting Octal to Binary — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How many binary digits is each octal digit worth?**
**Answer: Exactly three**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why three?**
**Answer: Three bits make the same eight values, 000 to 111, as one octal digit, 0 to 7**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is octal digit 5 in binary?**
**Answer: 101**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is octal digit 2 in binary?**
**Answer: 010 — the leading zero is kept**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why are leading zeros in a group kept?**
**Answer: They hold the places of the digits beside the group**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is 47₈ in binary?**
**Answer: 100111₂ — 4→100, 7→111**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is 10₈ in binary?**
**Answer: 001000₂ — written in full as 1000₂**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) In what order are the groups written?**
**Answer: Side by side, in the same order as the octal digits**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is 25₈ in binary?**
**Answer: 010101₂ — 2→010, 5→101**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How can you check a conversion?**
**Answer: By value — both numbers must name the same amount**
