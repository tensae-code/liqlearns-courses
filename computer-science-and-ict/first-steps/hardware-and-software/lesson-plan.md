# Lesson plan — Hardware and software — the two parts of a computer system

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Hardware and software — the two parts of a computer system
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Introduction to Information & Communication Technology — in the title First Steps on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A computer system has two parts — hardware and software — like a body and a mind: this lesson is the frame the next two units fill in.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on What a computer is and its characteristics: the whole Unit 1 road now arrives at the doorway of Units 2 and 3.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the two parts of a computer system: hardware and software.
2. Say which unit teaches each: hardware in Unit 2, software in Unit 3.
3. Say why neither part alone is a working system.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.
