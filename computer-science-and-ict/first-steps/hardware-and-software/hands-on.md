# Hands-on — Hardware and software — the two parts of a computer system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Touch test
List six things about a computer. Mark each T (you can touch it) or N (you cannot). The T list is heading for hardware; the N list for software.

## Body and mind
In your own words, write the book's comparison: what is the 'body' of a computer system, what is its 'mind', and why does it need both?
