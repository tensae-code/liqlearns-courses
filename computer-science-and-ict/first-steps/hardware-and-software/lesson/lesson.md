# Hardware and software — the two parts of a computer system

## Do it first

Touch the screen in front of you — or picture touching it. Now try to touch the program running on it. You cannot. Yet both are real parts of the same system. This short lesson gives the two parts their names, so the next two units have their frame.

## Hardware and software — the two parts of a computer system

Like a body and a mind, a computer system has two parts. 

[Picture 10120 — a computer split into a solid "hardware" half and a glowing "software" half]



**Hardware** is the part you can see and touch — the subject of the next unit, Unit 2. **Software** is the instructions that make the hardware work — the subject of Unit 3.

Neither half is a computer system alone. Hardware without software sits silent; software without hardware has nothing to run on. This lesson only builds the frame — two names, two next units — and the detail fills in from here.

## Say it to your companion

Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.
