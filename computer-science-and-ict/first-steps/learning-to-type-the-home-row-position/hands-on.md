# Hands-on — Learning to Type the Home Row Position

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 3 §3.8 Using Rapid Typing Tutor; §3.8.2 Home Row position and finger placement Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach learning to type the home row position to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
