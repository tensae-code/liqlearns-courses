# Hands-on — The Value of Information — Timely, Complete and True

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Fail it three ways', 'Take one piece of school information and write three versions of it: one late, one partial, one false. For each, name the quality failed and the harm it would do.')

## Task two
('Test before passing', 'Before you next pass on a fact, run the three questions in writing. Keep the note: in time? whole? true? — and what you checked for each.')
