# The Value of Information — Timely, Complete and True — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three qualities of valuable information.**
a) Timely, complete and true  b) History, not help  c) The decision it was meant to serve to be guessed at  d) It invites confidence it has not earned
**Answer: a) Timely, complete and true**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is information that arrives after its moment?**
a) The decision it was meant to serve to be guessed at  b) History, not help  c) It invites confidence it has not earned  d) Matching the world — real numbers, the real state
**Answer: b) History, not help**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does incomplete information force?**
a) It invites confidence it has not earned  b) Matching the world — real numbers, the real state  c) The decision it was meant to serve to be guessed at  d) None sends you to look; false sends you onward, wrongly armed
**Answer: c) The decision it was meant to serve to be guessed at**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is partial information dangerous?**
a) Matching the world — real numbers, the real state  b) None sends you to look; false sends you onward, wrongly armed  c) Is it in time? Is it whole? Is it true?  d) It invites confidence it has not earned
**Answer: d) It invites confidence it has not earned**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the quality of truth?**
a) Matching the world — real numbers, the real state  b) None sends you to look; false sends you onward, wrongly armed  c) Is it in time? Is it whole? Is it true?  d) Before information is used or passed on
**Answer: a) Matching the world — real numbers, the real state**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why is false information worse than none?**
a) Is it in time? Is it whole? Is it true?  b) None sends you to look; false sends you onward, wrongly armed  c) Before information is used or passed on  d) The property tables of the earlier grades
**Answer: b) None sends you to look; false sends you onward, wrongly armed**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) State the citizen's three questions.**
a) Before information is used or passed on  b) The property tables of the earlier grades  c) Is it in time? Is it whole? Is it true?  d) Mongolia's Grade 10 IT book
**Answer: c) Is it in time? Is it whole? Is it true?**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When are the three questions asked?**
a) The property tables of the earlier grades  b) Mongolia's Grade 10 IT book  c) Timely, complete and true  d) Before information is used or passed on
**Answer: d) Before information is used or passed on**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which earlier work quietly required the same three?**
a) The property tables of the earlier grades  b) Mongolia's Grade 10 IT book  c) Timely, complete and true  d) History, not help
**Answer: a) The property tables of the earlier grades**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book names the triad?**
a) Timely, complete and true  b) Mongolia's Grade 10 IT book  c) History, not help  d) The decision it was meant to serve to be guessed at
**Answer: b) Mongolia's Grade 10 IT book**
