# The Value of Information — Timely, Complete and True — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three qualities of valuable information.**
**Answer: Timely, complete and true**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is information that arrives after its moment?**
**Answer: History, not help**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does incomplete information force?**
**Answer: The decision it was meant to serve to be guessed at**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is partial information dangerous?**
**Answer: It invites confidence it has not earned**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the quality of truth?**
**Answer: Matching the world — real numbers, the real state**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why is false information worse than none?**
**Answer: None sends you to look; false sends you onward, wrongly armed**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) State the citizen's three questions.**
**Answer: Is it in time? Is it whole? Is it true?**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When are the three questions asked?**
**Answer: Before information is used or passed on**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which earlier work quietly required the same three?**
**Answer: The property tables of the earlier grades**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book names the triad?**
**Answer: Mongolia's Grade 10 IT book**
