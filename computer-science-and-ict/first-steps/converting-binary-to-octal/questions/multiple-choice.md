# Converting Binary to Octal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How are the bits grouped?**
a) In threes  b) From the right — the places run from the right  c) Grouping from the left would misplace every value  d) It still counts — think of it as filled with leading zeros
**Answer: a) In threes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) From which end does grouping start?**
a) Grouping from the left would misplace every value  b) From the right — the places run from the right  c) It still counts — think of it as filled with leading zeros  d) 5
**Answer: b) From the right — the places run from the right**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why must grouping start there?**
a) It still counts — think of it as filled with leading zeros  b) 5  c) Grouping from the left would misplace every value  d) 47₈ — groups 100 and 111
**Answer: c) Grouping from the left would misplace every value**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What if the last group has fewer than three bits?**
a) 5  b) 47₈ — groups 100 and 111  c) 52₈ — groups 101 and 010  d) It still counts — think of it as filled with leading zeros
**Answer: d) It still counts — think of it as filled with leading zeros**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the group 101 read as?**
a) 5  b) 47₈ — groups 100 and 111  c) 52₈ — groups 101 and 010  d) 7₈ — one full group
**Answer: a) 5**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is 100111₂ in octal?**
a) 52₈ — groups 101 and 010  b) 47₈ — groups 100 and 111  c) 7₈ — one full group  d) They are inverses — spreading digits into bits, and gathering bits into digits
**Answer: b) 47₈ — groups 100 and 111**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is 101010₂ in octal?**
a) 7₈ — one full group  b) They are inverses — spreading digits into bits, and gathering bits into digits  c) 52₈ — groups 101 and 010  d) 155₈ — groups 1, 101, 101 read as 001, 101, 101
**Answer: c) 52₈ — groups 101 and 010**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is 111₂ in octal?**
a) They are inverses — spreading digits into bits, and gathering bits into digits  b) 155₈ — groups 1, 101, 101 read as 001, 101, 101  c) In threes  d) 7₈ — one full group
**Answer: d) 7₈ — one full group**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How are the two octal–binary roads related?**
a) They are inverses — spreading digits into bits, and gathering bits into digits  b) 155₈ — groups 1, 101, 101 read as 001, 101, 101  c) In threes  d) From the right — the places run from the right
**Answer: a) They are inverses — spreading digits into bits, and gathering bits into digits**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is 1101101₂ in octal?**
a) In threes  b) 155₈ — groups 1, 101, 101 read as 001, 101, 101  c) From the right — the places run from the right  d) Grouping from the left would misplace every value
**Answer: b) 155₈ — groups 1, 101, 101 read as 001, 101, 101**
