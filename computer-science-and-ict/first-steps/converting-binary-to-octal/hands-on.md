# Hands-on — Converting Binary to Octal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Gather in threes', 'Convert 101101₂, 111111₂ and 1000001₂ to octal, marking the groups with pencil lines first.')

## Task two
('Round trip', 'Take 346₈, spread it into binary, then gather your binary back into octal. Say what must happen if both roads were worked right.')
