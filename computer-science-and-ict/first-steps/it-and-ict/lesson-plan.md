# Lesson plan — IT and ICT — telling them apart

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** IT and ICT — telling them apart
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Introduction to Information & Communication Technology — in the title First Steps on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** IT is the broader computing-data concept — the industry and process of creating, maintaining and using computer software, hardware and networks to manage information — while ICT is the communication-related technologies.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the lesson before it, What ICT is — the learner already knows ICT as the communication technologies joined together.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what IT is: creating, maintaining and using software, hardware and networks to manage information.
2. Say the book's difference in your own words: IT is the broader computing-data concept; ICT is the communication-related technologies.
3. Sort examples: a technician repairing a school computer (IT work); a message travelling between two phones (ICT at work).

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Say the two short names out loud: IT. ICT. One letter is different. In your notebook, write one sentence about computers working with information, and one sentence about people sending messages to each other. This lesson teaches you which name belongs to which sentence. (lesson/lesson.md).
