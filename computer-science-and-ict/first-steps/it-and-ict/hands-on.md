# Hands-on — IT and ICT — telling them apart

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Own words
Write the difference between IT and ICT in your own words — two sentences, no copying. Read them to someone at home and ask: does that make sense?

## Sort the scenes
Write six scenes on slips of paper: fixing a computer, writing a program, sending an SMS, a video call, keeping a school's records on a computer, a radio broadcast. Sort the slips into IT, ICT, or both — and be ready to say why.
