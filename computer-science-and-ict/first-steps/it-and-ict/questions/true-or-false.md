# IT and ICT — telling them apart — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ICT is the broader concept and IT is only about communication.**
**Answer: False** — It is the other way round: IT is the broader computing-data concept; ICT is the communication-related technologies.

**2. IT and ICT overlap — one device can be both at once.**
**Answer: True** — A phone sending a message is both a computer working with data and a communication technology.

**3. Copying the book's definition word for word is the best way to show you understand it.**
**Answer: False** — The guide's marking rule: pupils must give the difference in their own words.

**4. Software, hardware and networks are all part of what IT uses to manage information.**
**Answer: True**
