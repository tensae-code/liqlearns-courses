# IT and ICT — telling them apart — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. IT manages ____ using software, hardware and networks.**
**Answer: information**

**2. The C in ICT stands for ____.**
**Answer: Communication**

**3. Fixing and maintaining a school computer is ____ work; a message travelling between phones is ____ at work.**
**Answer: IT; ICT**
