# IT and ICT — telling them apart — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. IT is the industry and process of —**
a) growing crops  b) creating, maintaining and using computer software, hardware and networks to manage information  c) building roads
**Answer: b) creating, maintaining and using computer software, hardware and networks to manage information** — That is the book's definition of IT.

**2. The book says the difference plainly: IT is —**
a) the communication technologies only  b) the broader computing-data concept  c) a kind of telephone
**Answer: b) the broader computing-data concept**

**3. The extra letter in ICT stands for —**
a) Computer  b) Communication  c) Control
**Answer: b) Communication** — C for Communication.

**4. A technician who maintains the school's computers is doing —**
a) IT work  b) farming  c) transport work
**Answer: a) IT work** — Maintaining computers to manage information is IT.

**5. A voice message travelling from one phone to another is —**
a) ICT at work  b) not technology  c) a printed letter
**Answer: a) ICT at work** — Communication-related technology is ICT.
