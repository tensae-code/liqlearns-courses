# Video script — IT and ICT — telling them apart

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

IT. ICT. One letter apart.

IT is computers working with information — making them, mending them, using them.

ICT is the communication side — messages, voices and pictures travelling.

The C is for Communication.
