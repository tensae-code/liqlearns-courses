# Computer Science — Theoretical and Applied — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does theoretical CS ask?**
**Answer: What a problem and an algorithm are, which problems can be solved at all, and what a solution costs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are its tools?**
**Answer: Definitions and proofs, not devices**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name a theoretical topic in this book.**
**Answer: Complexity and computability (or algorithms, data structures, paradigms)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit of the book is this science?**
**Answer: Unit 2 — the theoretical unit**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which book teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 11 Eisagogi Epistimis H/Y, Unit 1**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does applied CS build?**
**Answer: Working systems — operating systems, databases, information systems, networks, applications**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are its questions?**
**Answer: Does it work, is it usable, does it stay correct and safe under real hands and real data?**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which unit of the book is applied?**
**Answer: Unit 3**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does theory tell practice?**
**Answer: What is possible and what it costs**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does practice give theory?**
**Answer: Its problems**
