# First generation — vacuum tubes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The first generation of computers used —
a) Transistors  b) Vacuum tubes  c) Microprocessors  d) Integrated circuits
**Answer: b) Vacuum tubes** — First-generation circuits were built from vacuum tubes.

**2.** A vacuum tube controls the flow of —
a) Water  b) Air  c) Electrons in a vacuum  d) Paper
**Answer: c) Electrons in a vacuum** — A vacuum tube controls the flow of electrons in a vacuum.

**3.** The first generation ran from —
a) 1956–1963  b) 1940–1956  c) 1964–1971  d) 1971–today
**Answer: b) 1940–1956** — First generation: 1940–1956.

**4.** Which of these describes first-generation computers?
a) Small and cheap  b) Carried in a pocket  c) Large, expensive, using much electricity and giving off heat  d) Silent and cold
**Answer: c) Large, expensive, using much electricity and giving off heat** — They were large, very expensive, used a lot of electricity and gave off a lot of heat.

**5.** Why did first-generation computer rooms need cooling?
a) The tubes gave off a lot of heat  b) The computers were made of ice  c) People worked better cold  d) The tubes were frozen
**Answer: a) The tubes gave off a lot of heat** — Thousands of tubes gave off a lot of heat.
