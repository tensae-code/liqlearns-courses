**First generation.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: the first computers (1940–1956) used vacuum tubes for their circuits — and were large, very expensive, power-hungry and hot.

**Vacuum tubes.** The first computers used vacuum tubes for their circuits.
**What the first computers were like.** Because they were built from so many tubes, first-generation computers were large — they filled rooms.

Now you: Draw a vacuum tube and label what it controls: the flow of electrons in a vacuum.
