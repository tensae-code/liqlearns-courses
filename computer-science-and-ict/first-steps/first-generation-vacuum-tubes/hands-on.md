# Hands-on — First generation — vacuum tubes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Heat hunt
Feel how warm a working phone or lamp gets after an hour. Now imagine thousands of glowing tubes in one room. Talk about why heat was a real problem for the first computers.

## Room-sized computer
Measure your classroom or a room at home with steps. The first computers filled rooms like this — pace out where the machine would stand and where the people would work.
