# Lesson plan — First generation — vacuum tubes

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** First generation — vacuum tubes
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 1 — First Steps of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 1 — Basics of Computer.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the first computers (1940–1956) used vacuum tubes for their circuits — and were large, very expensive, power-hungry and hot.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the generations lesson: a generation begins with a major technological change. This is the first of the five.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a vacuum tube is a pipe for water → it is a glass device controlling electrons — electricity's tiny carriers — in an empty space.
- Forgets the years → first generation: 1940–1956, the tube years.
