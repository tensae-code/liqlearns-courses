# First generation — vacuum tubes

## Do it first

## Vacuum tubes

The first computers used vacuum tubes for their circuits. A vacuum tube is a device that controls the flow of electrons in a vacuum — an empty space with no air in it. Every calculation the machine made passed through tubes like these.

## What the first computers were like

Because they were built from so many tubes, first-generation computers were large — they filled rooms. They were very expensive. They used a lot of electricity, and they gave off a lot of heat, so the rooms had to be kept cool. This is the starting point of the whole story: computing began big, costly and hot.
