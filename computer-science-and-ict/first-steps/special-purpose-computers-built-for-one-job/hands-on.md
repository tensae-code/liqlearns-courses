# Hands-on — Special-purpose computers — built for one job

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One-job hunt
Walk your street or school and list machines that do exactly one job. For each, say the job in three words or fewer. If a machine does many jobs, it does not go on the list.

## Fit the parts
For a ticket machine, discuss with a partner: which parts does it need for its one job (screen, buttons, printer) and which parts of a full computer would be wasted in it?
