# Video script — Special-purpose computers — built for one job

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Special-purpose computers.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: a special-purpose computer is designed to solve a single problem, its components and functions uniquely fitted to that one application.

**Designed for a single problem.** By purpose, there are two kinds of computer.
**The book's five examples.** The public telephone box; road traffic control and safety systems; ticket machines in shops and supermarkets; pocket calculators; and counters.

Now you: Draw the five examples in a row, each doing its one job, and label the job under each.
