# Special-purpose computers — built for one job

## Do it first

A pocket calculator adds, subtracts, multiplies, divides — and does nothing else, ever. Your phone does a thousand things. Before reading on, say which of the two was built for one job, and what that job is.

## Designed for a single problem

By purpose, there are two kinds of computer. A special-purpose computer is designed to solve a single problem. Its components and functions are uniquely fitted to that one application — everything in it serves the one job, and it does nothing else.

## The book's five examples

The public telephone box; road traffic control and safety systems; ticket machines in shops and supermarkets; pocket calculators; and counters. Each one exists for its single task, and does that task only.
