# Special-purpose computers — built for one job — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A special-purpose computer is designed to —
a) Solve a single problem  b) Do every job  c) Play all games  d) Replace all computers
**Answer: a) Solve a single problem** — It is designed to solve a single problem, fitted to that one application.

**2.** Which of these is a special-purpose computer?
a) A laptop  b) A pocket calculator  c) A smartphone  d) A school desktop
**Answer: b) A pocket calculator** — Pocket calculators are among the book's examples.

**3.** Ticket machines in supermarkets are —
a) General-purpose  b) Special-purpose  c) Supercomputers  d) Analog only
**Answer: b) Special-purpose** — Ticket machines do one job — they are special-purpose.

**4.** Its components and functions are —
a) Random  b) Uniquely fitted to the one application  c) Copied from games  d) Left empty
**Answer: b) Uniquely fitted to the one application** — Its components and functions are uniquely fitted to that one application.
