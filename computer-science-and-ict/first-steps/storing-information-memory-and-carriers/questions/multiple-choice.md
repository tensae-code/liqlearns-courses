# Storing Information — Memory and Carriers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Storing information means —**
a) forgetting it  b) keeping it to use again later  c) saying it once
**Answer: b) keeping it to use again later** — A store keeps information safe for later.

**2. Your name and your way home are stored in your —**
a) shoe  b) memory  c) book only
**Answer: b) memory** — Memory is the store inside a person.

**3. A book is a —**
a) carrier of information  b) kind of memory  c) sound
**Answer: a) carrier of information** — Books carry written words and pictures.

**4. A photograph carries —**
a) the look of a moment  b) a song  c) a taste
**Answer: a) the look of a moment** — A photo keeps how a moment looked.
