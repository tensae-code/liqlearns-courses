Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Azerbaijan, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson at this Grade 2 level — the live tree was checked first, including the overlap watches named in the merger brief. The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — first-steps/storing-information-memory-and-carriers). It sits in the First Steps title (first-steps/), where the placements file placed it. It is one whole lesson with no sub lessons — a small Grade 2 lesson running straight through.

Azerbaijan's own way: informatics starts two full years before touching a computer — objects and their features, information in everyday life, and algorithms as daily-life sequences and true/false judgements; the computer itself, WordPad, Paint and Calculator arrive only in the final unit. This lesson keeps that order and that Grade 2 voice.

Mongolia's own way (Grade 7): the whole applications half is one long project — research and produce a printed booklet "Усаа хайрлая" (Cherish the Water) in Word, a Paint.NET layered picture, and a class PowerPoint; image work uses the free Paint.NET (downloaded from getpaint.net) rather than MS Paint; the e-communication chapter teaches Yahoo mail and Yahoo Messenger, Mongolian history of the örtöö relay post and the @ sign, and makes correct Latin transliteration of Mongolian Cyrillic (standard MNS 5217:2003) an ethics topic in its own right.
