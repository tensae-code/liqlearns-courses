# Hands-on — Binary Numbers — the Language of Computers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Write your age in bits
Using 0 for off and 1 for on, work out a pattern of bits for a small number your companion chooses, then check each other's patterns.

## Signal or no signal
Tap a rhythm of taps and pauses on the desk. Let your companion write 1 for each tap and 0 for each pause — you have sent data as two-state signals.
