# Binary Numbers — the Language of Computers — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "As what does data travel inside the computer?" is: Electrical signals**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How many states does an electrical signal inside the computer have?" is: Because the machine itself works in two states**
**Answer: False** — Two states

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the smallest piece of data called?" is: A bit**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why are the computer's numbers built from two states?" is: 0 or 1**
**Answer: False** — Because the machine itself works in two states

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are the two states of a signal?" is: There or not there — on or off**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What two values can a bit take?" is: Letters, numbers, pictures and sounds**
**Answer: False** — 0 or 1

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does 0 stand for, and what does 1 stand for?" is: 0 for off, 1 for on**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do long patterns of bits represent?" is: Binary numbers**
**Answer: False** — Letters, numbers, pictures and sounds

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What are binary numbers called?" is: The language of computers**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "In what is everything the computer holds written?" is: Two states**
**Answer: False** — Binary numbers
