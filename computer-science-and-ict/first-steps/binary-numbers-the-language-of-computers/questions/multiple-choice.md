# Binary Numbers — the Language of Computers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) As what does data travel inside the computer?**
a) Electrical signals  b) Two states  c) Because the machine itself works in two states  d) 0 or 1
**Answer: a) Electrical signals**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many states does an electrical signal inside the computer have?**
a) A bit  b) Two states  c) There or not there — on or off  d) 0 for off, 1 for on
**Answer: b) Two states**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the smallest piece of data called?**
a) Because the machine itself works in two states  b) 0 or 1  c) A bit  d) Letters, numbers, pictures and sounds
**Answer: c) A bit**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why are the computer's numbers built from two states?**
a) There or not there — on or off  b) 0 for off, 1 for on  c) The language of computers  d) Because the machine itself works in two states
**Answer: d) Because the machine itself works in two states**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are the two states of a signal?**
a) There or not there — on or off  b) 0 or 1  c) Letters, numbers, pictures and sounds  d) Binary numbers
**Answer: a) There or not there — on or off**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What two values can a bit take?**
a) 0 for off, 1 for on  b) 0 or 1  c) The language of computers  d) Electrical signals
**Answer: b) 0 or 1**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does 0 stand for, and what does 1 stand for?**
a) Letters, numbers, pictures and sounds  b) Binary numbers  c) 0 for off, 1 for on  d) Two states
**Answer: c) 0 for off, 1 for on**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do long patterns of bits represent?**
a) The language of computers  b) Electrical signals  c) A bit  d) Letters, numbers, pictures and sounds
**Answer: d) Letters, numbers, pictures and sounds**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are binary numbers called?**
a) The language of computers  b) Binary numbers  c) Two states  d) Because the machine itself works in two states
**Answer: a) The language of computers**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) In what is everything the computer holds written?**
a) Electrical signals  b) Binary numbers  c) A bit  d) There or not there — on or off
**Answer: b) Binary numbers**
