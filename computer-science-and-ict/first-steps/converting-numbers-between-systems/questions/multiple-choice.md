# Converting Numbers Between Systems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What stays the same when a number is converted?**
a) Its value  b) Each digit times its place value, then the parts are added  c) 5  d) The base raised to the digit's position
**Answer: a) Its value**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is a number carried into decimal?**
a) 5  b) Each digit times its place value, then the parts are added  c) The base raised to the digit's position  d) As its own lesson, after the number systems themselves
**Answer: b) Each digit times its place value, then the parts are added**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is binary 101 in decimal?**
a) The base raised to the digit's position  b) As its own lesson, after the number systems themselves  c) 5  d) Divide by the new base again and again and keep the remainders
**Answer: c) 5**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a digit's place value built from?**
a) As its own lesson, after the number systems themselves  b) Divide by the new base again and again and keep the remainders  c) In reverse — last first  d) The base raised to the digit's position
**Answer: d) The base raised to the digit's position**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does conversion sit in Uzbekistan's Grade 8 book?**
a) As its own lesson, after the number systems themselves  b) Divide by the new base again and again and keep the remainders  c) In reverse — last first  d) 1101
**Answer: a) As its own lesson, after the number systems themselves**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a decimal number carried into another system?**
a) In reverse — last first  b) Divide by the new base again and again and keep the remainders  c) 1101  d) By converting back
**Answer: b) Divide by the new base again and again and keep the remainders**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) In what order are the remainders read?**
a) 1101  b) By converting back  c) In reverse — last first  d) The remainder
**Answer: c) In reverse — last first**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is 13 in binary?**
a) By converting back  b) The remainder  c) Its value  d) 1101
**Answer: d) 1101**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do you check a conversion?**
a) By converting back  b) The remainder  c) Its value  d) Each digit times its place value, then the parts are added
**Answer: a) By converting back**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is kept at every division?**
a) Its value  b) The remainder  c) Each digit times its place value, then the parts are added  d) 5
**Answer: b) The remainder**
