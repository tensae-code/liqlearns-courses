# Hands-on — Controlling Digital Devices — On-Off Buttons and Remotes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Button survey** — Find the on/off button on three different devices and draw its sign; say what each device does when it wakes.

## Task two
**Remote practice** — Use a television remote: change the channel with the numbers, raise and lower the volume, then switch off — naming each command before you press it.
