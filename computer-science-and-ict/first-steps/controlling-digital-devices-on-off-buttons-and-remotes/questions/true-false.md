# Controlling Digital Devices — On-Off Buttons and Remotes — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It starts the device with one press and stops it with another**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It starts the device with one press and stops it with another** — as the answer to: What sign does the on/off button carry?
**Answer: False — A circle with a line through its top**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: No — switching on comes before every other command**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: No — switching on comes before every other command** — as the answer to: How is a piece of work with a device properly ended?
**Answer: False — By switching the device off**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Yes — it is known on devices all over the world**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Yes — it is known on devices all over the world** — as the answer to: What is a remote control?
**Answer: False — A hand-held controller that commands a device from a distance**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Through the air, in a straight line to the device**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Through the air, in a straight line to the device** — as the answer to: What do the number buttons do?
**Answer: False — They choose the channel**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Because the command travels in a straight line and can be blocked**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Because the command travels in a straight line and can be blocked** — as the answer to: Name two devices commanded by remotes in the lesson.
**Answer: False — The television and the air conditioner (also: a toy car)**
