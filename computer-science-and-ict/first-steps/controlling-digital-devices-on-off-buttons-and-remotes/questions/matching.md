# Controlling Digital Devices — On-Off Buttons and Remotes — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the on/off button do?** → **It starts the device with one press and stops it with another**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What sign does the on/off button carry?** → **A circle with a line through its top**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Can a switched-off device take commands?** → **No — switching on comes before every other command**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a piece of work with a device properly ended?** → **By switching the device off**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is the on/off sign the same everywhere?** → **Yes — it is known on devices all over the world**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a remote control?** → **A hand-held controller that commands a device from a distance**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does the command travel?** → **Through the air, in a straight line to the device**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do the number buttons do?** → **They choose the channel**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why must the remote point at the device?** → **Because the command travels in a straight line and can be blocked**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name two devices commanded by remotes in the lesson.** → **The television and the air conditioner (also: a toy car)**
