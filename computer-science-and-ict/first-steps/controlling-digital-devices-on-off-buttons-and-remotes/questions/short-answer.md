# Controlling Digital Devices — On-Off Buttons and Remotes — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the on/off button do?**
**Answer: It starts the device with one press and stops it with another**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What sign does the on/off button carry?**
**Answer: A circle with a line through its top**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Can a switched-off device take commands?**
**Answer: No — switching on comes before every other command**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a piece of work with a device properly ended?**
**Answer: By switching the device off**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is the on/off sign the same everywhere?**
**Answer: Yes — it is known on devices all over the world**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a remote control?**
**Answer: A hand-held controller that commands a device from a distance**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does the command travel?**
**Answer: Through the air, in a straight line to the device**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do the number buttons do?**
**Answer: They choose the channel**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why must the remote point at the device?**
**Answer: Because the command travels in a straight line and can be blocked**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name two devices commanded by remotes in the lesson.**
**Answer: The television and the air conditioner (also: a toy car)**
