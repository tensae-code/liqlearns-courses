# Video script — Typing on the AZERTY Keyboard

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at the top row of letters.

A. Z. E. R. T. Y.

That is the name: AZERTY.

A and Z changed places with Q and W.

For a number, hold Shift.

Now rest your fingers on the middle row.

Each finger has its own keys. Thumbs on the space bar.

Type slowly. Eyes on the words, not on your hands.

Slow and right first. Fast comes later.
