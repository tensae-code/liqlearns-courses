# Hands-on — Typing on the AZERTY Keyboard

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Draw the map
On paper, draw the three letter rows of the AZERTY keyboard from memory. Check your drawing against a real keyboard or the picture in the lesson, fix what was wrong, and draw it again.

## Home row drill
Rest your fingers on the home row. Type the home-row letters in order, slowly, ten times, eyes on this page. Then type them in reverse order ten times.

## One line, twice
Copy one short line from a book. Count your errors. Copy the same line again and try to make fewer errors, at the same slow speed.
