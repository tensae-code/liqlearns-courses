# Typing on the AZERTY Keyboard — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) AZERTY and QWERTY put every letter in the same place.**
**Answer: False** — A, Z, Q, W and M sit in different places.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Pressing a number-row key on its own on AZERTY gives an accented letter or a symbol.**
**Answer: True** — The figures on that row need Shift.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Good typing means looking at your hands to find each key.**
**Answer: False** — Good typing keeps the eyes on the copy; each finger knows its own keys.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Speed should come before accuracy when learning to type.**
**Answer: False** — Accuracy first; speed follows practice.
