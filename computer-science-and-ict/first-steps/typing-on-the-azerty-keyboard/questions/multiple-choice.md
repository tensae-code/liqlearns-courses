# Typing on the AZERTY Keyboard — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why is this keyboard called AZERTY?**
a) It was invented by a person named Azerty  b) Its top letter row begins A Z E R T Y  c) It has keys from A to Z and Y
**Answer: b) Its top letter row begins A Z E R T Y** — A keyboard is named by the first letters of its top letter row.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) On AZERTY, where Q sits on a QWERTY keyboard you find —**
a) A  b) Z  c) M
**Answer: a) A** — A and Z trade places with Q and W compared with QWERTY.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) To type a figure on the AZERTY number row, you —**
a) press Caps Lock  b) hold Shift and press the key  c) press the key twice
**Answer: b) hold Shift and press the key** — Unshifted, the number row gives accented letters and symbols.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Where do your fingers rest when you type?**
a) On the top row  b) On the home row  c) On the space bar
**Answer: b) On the home row** — Each finger owns its keys from its home position; the thumbs rest on the space bar.
