# Typing on the AZERTY Keyboard — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name two ways the AZERTY layout differs from QWERTY.**
**Answer:** Any two of: A sits where Q sits on QWERTY; Z sits where W sits; M sits at the end of the home row; the number row gives accents and symbols unshifted and figures with Shift.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Your typing has many errors. What does the lesson tell you to do, and why?**
**Answer:** Slow down and type letter by letter with the right finger for each key — accuracy first teaches the fingers the map; speed follows practice.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What does each finger do in good typing?**
**Answer:** Each finger rests on its home-row key, owns the keys around it, reaches for them and returns home; the thumbs work the space bar.
