# Typing on the AZERTY Keyboard — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The first six letters of the AZERTY top row are A, Z, E, R, T and ____.**
**Answer: Y**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) To type a figure on the number row, hold ____ while you press the key.**
**Answer: Shift**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Your fingers rest on the ____ row, and your thumbs rest on the space bar.**
**Answer: home**
