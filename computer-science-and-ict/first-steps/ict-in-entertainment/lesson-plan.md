# Lesson plan — ICT in entertainment

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** ICT in entertainment
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Introduction to Information & Communication Technology — in the title First Steps on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Entertainment and leisure come through ICT at home — films and music from television, and games played over the internet in free time.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on ICT in education: the daily-life walk continues, from the classroom to the home's free time.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the book's entertainment examples: films and music from television; games played over the internet.
2. Say where this entertainment happens: at home, in free time.
3. Say that the same ICT carries work, school and play — the purpose changes, not the technology.

## What you need
Nothing but your own memory of your free time.
