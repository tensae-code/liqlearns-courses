# Hands-on — ICT in entertainment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Free-time tally
For three days, note your ICT entertainment: what it was (film, music, game), and how long it lasted. At the end, say honestly whether the balance felt right.

## Same device, three jobs
Pick one device at home. Write one work job, one school job and one entertainment job it does in a single day.
