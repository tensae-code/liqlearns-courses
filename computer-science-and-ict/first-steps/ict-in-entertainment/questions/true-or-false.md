# ICT in entertainment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. Films at home can come through television as ICT.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Music is one of the book's entertainment examples.**
**Answer: True**

**3. Internet games are played in free time, says the book.**
**Answer: True**

**4. Entertainment use is not counted as ICT in daily life.**
**Answer: False** — The book counts it as one of the daily-life areas.
