# ICT in entertainment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The book's entertainment examples are —**
a) films and music from television, and games over the internet  b) only board games  c) only reading
**Answer: a) films and music from television, and games over the internet**

**2. Games in the book are played —**
a) only on paper  b) over the internet  c) only at school
**Answer: b) over the internet**

**3. This entertainment happens —**
a) at home, in free time  b) only in offices  c) only in class
**Answer: a) at home, in free time**

**4. The technology that carries entertainment is —**
a) different from the ICT of work and school  b) the same networks and devices  c) not ICT at all
**Answer: b) the same networks and devices**

**5. Too much gaming is linked in the book to —**
a) better eyesight  b) eyestrain and sore wrists  c) faster typing only
**Answer: b) eyestrain and sore wrists** — The harm is taught in the characteristics lesson, printed p. 11.
