# Hands-on — What a computer is — input, process, store, output

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four-step diary
Take three computer tasks from your day. For each, write the four steps: what went in, what processing happened, what was stored, what came out.

## Act the cycle
With three partners, stand as Input, Processing and Storage, with the fourth as Output. Pass a written note along the line: the note is read in, changed (capital letters), kept in a pocket, then read aloud. You have acted the computer's four functions.
