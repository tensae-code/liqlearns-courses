# Hands-on — The four functions of a computer — input

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Function spotting
Use a phone or watch someone use one for two minutes. Every time data goes IN, say 'input'. Count how many inputs you spot.

## Numbers in, product out — set up
Write two numbers on paper. Say: 'These are my input.' Keep the paper — the next lessons process, output and store it.
