# Communication in an ICT system

## Do it first

Whisper a sentence to the person next to you — or imagine doing it. The sentence existed in one place, then in another. Nothing was carried in your hands. This lesson is about that travelling, inside ICT systems.

## Communication in an ICT system

Communication is passing information from one place to another. It happens in more ways than a phone call: feeding information into a computer is communication; reading information out of a system is communication; and a message passing between two people is communication. 

[Picture 10109 — two computers exchanging a message through a communication device]



Watch a message make the journey. 

[GIF 10108 — a message travelling from one computer to the other]

 It leaves one computer, travels, and arrives at the other. That travel is only possible because of communication devices — the devices that let computers and ICT systems talk to each other. Without them, every computer would be an island: full of information nobody else could reach.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: Communication is passing information from one place to another — feeding information to a computer, reading it from a system, or a message passing between two people — and communication devices are what let computers and ICT systems talk to each other. Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.

## Do It Yourself

1. Show it, don't just say it: say what communication is: passing information from one place to another. Do this with a real example from your own home, school or street, and write or say how you know it is right.
2. Show it, don't just say it: name three happenings that count as communication: feeding information in, reading it out, a message between two people. Do this with a real example from your own home, school or street, and write or say how you know it is right.
3. Show it, don't just say it: say what communication devices do: they let computers and ICT systems talk to each other. Do this with a real example from your own home, school or street, and write or say how you know it is right.
