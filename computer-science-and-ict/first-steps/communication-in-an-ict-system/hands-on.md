# Hands-on — Communication in an ICT system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three kinds in one day
Keep a tally for one day. Every time you feed information into a device, read information out of one, or send a message to a person, make a mark in that column. Which column filled fastest?

## Island computer
Imagine the school computer with its communication devices removed. Write three things it could still do and three things it could no longer do.
