# Lesson plan — Communication in an ICT system

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Communication in an ICT system
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Introduction to Information & Communication Technology — in the title First Steps on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Communication is passing information from one place to another — feeding information to a computer, reading it from a system, or a message passing between two people — and communication devices are what let computers and ICT systems talk to each other.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on The five elements of an ICT system: communication is the third element, taken here in its own lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what communication is: passing information from one place to another.
2. Name three happenings that count as communication: feeding information in, reading it out, a message between two people.
3. Say what communication devices do: they let computers and ICT systems talk to each other.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.
