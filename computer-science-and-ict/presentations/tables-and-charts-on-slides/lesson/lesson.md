# Tables and charts on slides

## Do it first

Some ideas are rows and columns. Some are shapes that rise and fall. A slide can hold both — a table for the exact numbers, a chart for the pattern. Build one of each now.

## Inserting a table — in order

1. Open the table route from the content placeholder or the Insert tab.
2. In the **Insert Table dialog**, set the **columns and rows**.
3. Click **OK** — the table stands on the slide.

Design and layout are then edited on the **Table Tools contextual tab**.

## Inserting a chart — in order

1. Open the chart route.
2. **Choose the chart type** and click **OK**.
3. Click the chart on the slide to activate **Chart Tools** — for **editing the data** and **changing layouts and styles**.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: a table is born by naming its columns and rows in the Insert Table dialog; a chart by choosing its type — then Table Tools and Chart Tools appear for editing. Use the lesson's own words for the key names.
