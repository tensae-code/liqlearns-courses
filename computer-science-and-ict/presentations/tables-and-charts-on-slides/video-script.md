# Video script — Tables and charts on slides

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One thing today: a table is born by naming its columns and rows in the Insert Table dialog; a chart by choosing its type — then Table Tools and Chart Tools appear for editing.

Do it first, in the lesson's order. Say each step out loud as you do it.

Then do it again — this time without looking.
