# Hands-on — Tables and charts on slides

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Where there is no computer laboratory, do the screen work on a teacher's or your own phone, as the course's teaching pattern provides.

## Numbers two ways
Put four market prices in a table on one slide, then chart the same four prices on the next. Present both: which slide answers "how much is the sugar?" and which answers "what is rising?"

## Tool-tab spotting
Click the table, then click outside it, then click it again. Watch Table Tools appear and vanish. Do the same with the chart and Chart Tools. The tools follow the thing you touch.
