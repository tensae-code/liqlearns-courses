# Hands-on — SmartArt, pictures and video on slides

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Where there is no computer laboratory, do the screen work on a teacher's or your own phone, as the course's teaching pattern provides.

## List to SmartArt
Write your school's leadership as a plain list, then rebuild it as SmartArt. Stand back: which version could a visitor understand in five seconds?

## Embed or link?
Find one short video. Decide for a real deck: embed it, or link it? Write your reason in one line — file size and whether the deck must travel decide it.
