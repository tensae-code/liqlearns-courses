# Hands-on — PowerPoint — making a slideshow; inserting slides

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Paper slideshow

Cut four paper slides. Put one idea of a short talk on each — a title, a point with a drawing, a second point, an ending. Hold them up in order for a partner: you have run a slide-show system by hand.

## Grow the presentation

In PowerPoint, start a presentation and insert slides until it has five, choosing a different layout for at least two of them. On each slide, write what that slide is for. Five slides, five jobs.
