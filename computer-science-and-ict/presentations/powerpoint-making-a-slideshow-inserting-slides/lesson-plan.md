# Lesson plan — PowerPoint — making a slideshow; inserting slides

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Presentations · **Lesson:** PowerPoint — making a slideshow; inserting slides
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 7 — Presentations in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 8 book, Unit 3 — Computer Software. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** PowerPoint creates a slideshow addressing a topic, with three functions — an editor for inserting and formatting text, a method for inserting and manipulating graphic images, and a slide-show system to display the content — and a presentation that begins with one slide can take in as many more as needed, from a variety of layouts.
**This lesson's own level:** it opens the Presentations title and is a doing-lesson. It stands on application software — PowerPoint is the presentation kind.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What PowerPoint is** — it creates a slideshow addressing a topic — sometimes simply called a presentation or slide — with three major functions: an editor for inserting and formatting text, a method for inserting and manipulating graphic images, and a slide-show system to display the content
2. **Inserting slides** — a presentation begins with one slide; as many slides as needed can be inserted, from a variety of layouts

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Application software — programs for particular jobs. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what PowerPoint creates and what a presentation is also called.
2. Name PowerPoint's three major functions.
3. Insert more slides into a presentation, choosing from a variety of layouts.

## What you need
A computer with PowerPoint — this lesson is learned by doing it.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a presentation is one fixed slide → it begins with one slide; as many as needed can be inserted.
- Confuses the three functions → editor (text), images (graphics), slide-show system (display): words, pictures, showing.
