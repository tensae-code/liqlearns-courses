# PowerPoint — making a slideshow; inserting slides — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A slideshow made in PowerPoint is sometimes simply called a presentation.
**Answer: True** — presentation, or slide, are its other names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** PowerPoint cannot work with graphic images.
**Answer: False** — inserting and manipulating graphic images is its second major function.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Once a presentation is started, no more slides can be added.
**Answer: False** — as many slides as needed can be inserted.
