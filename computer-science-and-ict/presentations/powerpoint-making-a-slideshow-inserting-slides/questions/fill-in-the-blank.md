# PowerPoint — making a slideshow; inserting slides — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** PowerPoint's first function is an ______ for inserting and formatting text.
**Answer: editor**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Each inserted slide can be taken from a variety of ______.
**Answer: layouts**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A presentation begins with ______ slide.
