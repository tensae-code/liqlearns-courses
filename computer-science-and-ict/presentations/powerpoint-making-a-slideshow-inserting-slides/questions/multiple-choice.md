# PowerPoint — making a slideshow; inserting slides — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** PowerPoint creates —
a) a spreadsheet  b) a slideshow addressing a topic  c) an email account  d) a database
**Answer: b) a slideshow addressing a topic** — also simply called a presentation, or a slide.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The function for inserting and formatting text is —
a) the editor  b) the slide-show system  c) the layout  d) the printer
**Answer: a) the editor** — one of PowerPoint's three major functions.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The slide-show system is for —
a) typing text  b) drawing images  c) displaying the content  d) saving files
**Answer: c) displaying the content** — the third function.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A presentation begins with —
a) ten slides  b) one slide  c) no slides  d) a printed handout
**Answer: b) one slide** — more are inserted as needed.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** New slides can be inserted —
a) only one more  b) as many as needed, from a variety of layouts  c) only at the end of the year  d) by the teacher only
**Answer: b) as many as needed, from a variety of layouts.**
