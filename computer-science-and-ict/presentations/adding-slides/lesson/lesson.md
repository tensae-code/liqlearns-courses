# Adding slides

## Do it first

Your blank presentation shows one slide with two empty boxes inviting you: "Click to Add Title", "Click to Add Subtitle". Click the first and type your presentation's title. Then your talk needs a second page — find the button that adds slides, and notice it has TWO halves that do different things.

## The title slide

A new presentation opens with a **Title Slide** carrying two placeholders: **"Click to Add Title"** and **"Click to Add Subtitle"**. **Click inside a placeholder and type** — the invitation words are replaced by yours.

## The two-part New Slide button

New slides come from the **Home tab's New Slide button**, which has **two parts**:

- The **top part** inserts a slide with the **default layout at once**.
- The **bottom part** opens the **layout gallery** to choose from.

The keyboard shortcut is **Ctrl+M**.
