# Hands-on — Adding slides

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Where there is no computer laboratory, do the screen work on a teacher's or your own phone, as the course's teaching pattern provides.

## Title it
Start blank, type a real title and subtitle for a talk you could give. Then add three slides: one with the button's top half, one with Ctrl+M, one choosing from the gallery.

## Half-button test
Ask a partner which half of New Slide you pressed — top or bottom — from what happened on screen. Swap roles. The gallery appearing is the bottom half's signature.
