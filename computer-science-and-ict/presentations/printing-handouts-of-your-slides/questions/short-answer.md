# Printing Handouts of Your Slides — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a handout?**
**Answer: The printed form of a presentation — slides set several to a page**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many slides can a handout page hold?**
**Answer: One, two, three, four, six or nine**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is three-per-page the classroom favourite?**
**Answer: It prints ruled lines beside each slide for the listener's notes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a handout chosen for?**
**Answer: The audience's job — following the talk, taking notes, keeping a copy**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is a handout just the slides printed?**
**Answer: No — its layout is designed for the reader, not only printed from the deck**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is chosen first in the print settings?**
**Answer: Handouts, instead of full-page slides**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What order choice do multi-slide pages offer?**
**Answer: Across then down, or down then across**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is checked in the preview?**
**Answer: That slides are big enough to read and the header and footer are right**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why print one test page first?**
**Answer: To catch mistakes before a whole class set spends paper and ink**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does Fiji close its presentation unit with?**
**Answer: Printing the handouts**
