# Printing Handouts of Your Slides — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a handout?**
a) The printed form of a presentation — slides set several to a page  b) One, two, three, four, six or nine  c) It prints ruled lines beside each slide for the listener's notes  d) The audience's job — following the talk, taking notes, keeping a copy
**Answer: a) The printed form of a presentation — slides set several to a page**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many slides can a handout page hold?**
a) It prints ruled lines beside each slide for the listener's notes  b) One, two, three, four, six or nine  c) The audience's job — following the talk, taking notes, keeping a copy  d) No — its layout is designed for the reader, not only printed from the deck
**Answer: b) One, two, three, four, six or nine**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is three-per-page the classroom favourite?**
a) The audience's job — following the talk, taking notes, keeping a copy  b) No — its layout is designed for the reader, not only printed from the deck  c) It prints ruled lines beside each slide for the listener's notes  d) Handouts, instead of full-page slides
**Answer: c) It prints ruled lines beside each slide for the listener's notes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a handout chosen for?**
a) No — its layout is designed for the reader, not only printed from the deck  b) Handouts, instead of full-page slides  c) Across then down, or down then across  d) The audience's job — following the talk, taking notes, keeping a copy
**Answer: d) The audience's job — following the talk, taking notes, keeping a copy**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is a handout just the slides printed?**
a) No — its layout is designed for the reader, not only printed from the deck  b) Handouts, instead of full-page slides  c) Across then down, or down then across  d) That slides are big enough to read and the header and footer are right
**Answer: a) No — its layout is designed for the reader, not only printed from the deck**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is chosen first in the print settings?**
a) Across then down, or down then across  b) Handouts, instead of full-page slides  c) That slides are big enough to read and the header and footer are right  d) To catch mistakes before a whole class set spends paper and ink

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What order choice do multi-slide pages offer?**
a) That slides are big enough to read and the header and footer are right  b) To catch mistakes before a whole class set spends paper and ink  c) Across then down, or down then across  d) Printing the handouts

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is checked in the preview?**
a) To catch mistakes before a whole class set spends paper and ink  b) Printing the handouts  c) The printed form of a presentation — slides set several to a page  d) That slides are big enough to read and the header and footer are right
