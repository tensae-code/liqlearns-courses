# Hands-on — Printing Handouts of Your Slides

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

('Two layouts', 'Print-preview one deck as three-per-page and as six-per-page handouts; say which suits a note-taking class and why.')

## Task two
('The test page', 'Print one real handout page, check it against the preview, and list anything you would change.')
