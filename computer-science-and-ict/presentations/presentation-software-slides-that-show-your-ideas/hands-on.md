# Hands-on — Presentation software — slides that show your ideas

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Where there is no computer laboratory, do the screen work on a teacher's or your own phone, as the course's teaching pattern provides.

## Slide spotting
Watch any presentation — in class or on a screen — and tally what each slide holds: text only, text and picture, a movie. Which mix held your attention, and why?

## One idea, one slide
Take one idea you know well. On paper, sketch the single slide that would show it: title, one picture, three short lines. Freely arranged — your arrangement is the work.
