# Presentation software — slides that show your ideas

## Do it first

You must explain your idea to a room of people. One page at a time, big enough for everyone to see, with words and pictures together — that is a slide. This lesson names the software that makes slides, and what a slide can carry.

## What presentation software is

**Presentation software displays slides during a presentation.** Three programs are named: **MS PowerPoint**, **Google Slides** and **LibreOffice Impress**.

[Picture 13710 — a speaker beside a screen of slides, one slide enlarged]

## Slides

A **presentation is a set of individual pages — slides**. Each slide can hold **text, graphics, sound, movies and other objects**, **freely arranged** — you place things where they serve the idea best.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: presentation software shows ideas as a set of slides — each slide a page that can hold text, graphics, sound, movies and other objects, freely arranged. Use the lesson's own words for the key names.
