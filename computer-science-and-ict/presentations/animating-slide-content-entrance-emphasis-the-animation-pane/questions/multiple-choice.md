# Animating slide content — entrance, emphasis, the Animation Pane — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Animations reveal a slide's items —
a) all at once  b) one at a time  c) backwards  d) in print only
**Answer: b) one at a time** — so the audience cannot read ahead.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Hovering over an animation in the list —
a) applies it  b) previews it  c) deletes it  d) prints it
**Answer: b) previews it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The worked example picks —
a) Fade  b) Spin  c) Bounce  d) Zoom
**Answer: a) Fade**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Pane sequence, the title's entrance is moved —
a) after the picture  b) before the list  c) to another slide  d) off the slide
**Answer: b) before the list**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The second animation added is —
a) an exit  b) an emphasis animation  c) a transition  d) a sound
**Answer: b) an emphasis animation**
