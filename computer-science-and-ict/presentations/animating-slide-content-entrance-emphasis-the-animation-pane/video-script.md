# Video script — Animating slide content — entrance, emphasis, the Animation Pane

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your audience is reading ahead of you. Stop them.
Animations tab. Select the bullet points.
Hover — each animation previews. Open the full list with More. Pick Fade.
Now the points come one at a time, when YOU click.
The Animation Pane runs the order: the title enters first.
An emphasis second. The picture follows.
Play From checks it. F5 runs it.
And not too many — busy slides distract.
