# Animating slide content — entrance, emphasis, the Animation Pane

## Do it first

Your audience reads the whole slide while you are still on its first point. Hide the points, then reveal them one at a time as you speak.

## Sub lesson 1 — Animating the bullets — the worked route

Animations **reveal a slide's items one at a time so the audience cannot read ahead**. On a bulleted slide, in the book's order:

1. **Animations tab**.
2. **Select the bullet points**.
3. **Hover animations to preview** them.
4. Open the **full list with More** and **pick one** — **Fade** is the worked example.
5. **Effect Options** adjusts it.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Animation Pane — ordering the sequence

The advanced route opens the **Animation Pane**:

1. An **entrance animation** is added to the **title** and **moved before the list** with the timing controls.
2. An **emphasis animation** is added **second**.
3. The **picture is animated to follow**.
4. **Play From / Preview** checks the sequence — then save, and run **from the first slide with F5**.

The same restraint rule applies as for transitions: **too many different animations distract**, and **flashy ones should be avoided for accessibility**.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion why items are revealed one at a time, the worked Fade route, and the Pane's order: title's entrance, emphasis, picture.

## Do It Yourself

1. Select a slide's bullets, preview animations by hovering, and apply Fade from the full list.
2. Open the Animation Pane; add an entrance animation to the title and move it before the list.
3. Add an emphasis animation second and animate the picture to follow.
4. Preview with Play From, then run the deck from the first slide with F5.
