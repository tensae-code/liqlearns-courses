# Hands-on — Slide layouts

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Where there is no computer laboratory, do the screen work on a teacher's or your own phone, as the course's teaching pattern provides.

## Six arrangements
Open the layout gallery and sketch all six arrangements on paper, labelling where title, text and picture sit in each. Keep the sketches as your choosing map.

## Rearrange rescue
Crowd a slide on purpose — title, long text, picture squeezed together. Rescue it by changing only its layout, twice, and say which layout served the content best.
