# Slide layouts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A layout is —
a) a theme  b) the arrangement of a slide's items  c) a background colour  d) a kind of chart
**Answer: b) the arrangement of a slide's items** — Title, graphics, text boxes.

**2.** A new slide's layout is chosen from —
a) the Status bar  b) the gallery under the New Slide button's bottom half  c) the File tab  d) Notes Page
**Answer: b) the gallery under the New Slide button's bottom half**

**3.** To change an existing slide's layout: Home → —
a) Themes  b) Layout, in the Slides group  c) Background  d) Bullets
**Answer: b) Layout, in the Slides group** — First select the slide in the Thumbnail pane.

**4.** Which can be slide content?
a) text and tables  b) charts and graphics  c) pictures, clip art and videos  d) all of these
**Answer: d) all of these**
