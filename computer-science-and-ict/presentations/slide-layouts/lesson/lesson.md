# Slide layouts

## Do it first

Your slide holds a title, a block of text and a picture — all crowded in the wrong places. You do not drag them one by one. You change the slide's LAYOUT, and everything rearranges at once. Try it now.

## What a layout is

A **layout is the arrangement of a slide's items** — title, graphics, text boxes. A new slide's layout is **chosen from the gallery** under the New Slide button's bottom half; each layout there is **captioned by type**. Content on a slide may be **text, tables, charts, graphics, pictures, clip art or videos** — the layout decides where each kind sits.

## Changing a slide's layout later — in order

1. **Select the slide** in the Thumbnail pane.
2. Go to **Home → Layout** in the Slides group.
3. **Click a new layout** — the slide's contents rearrange into it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: a layout is the arrangement of a slide's items; choose it from the gallery when a slide is born, or change it later with Home → Layout. Use the lesson's own words for the key names.
