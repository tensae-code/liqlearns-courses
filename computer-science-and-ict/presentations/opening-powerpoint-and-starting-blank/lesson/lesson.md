# Opening PowerPoint and starting blank

## Do it first

PowerPoint is open and you must begin. Everything starts behind one tab at the top-left: File. Open it now and find the three doors — one for starting, one for finding, one for returning.

## The File tab's three choices

After launching PowerPoint, the **File tab (top-left)** offers the three common choices:

- **New** — a **blank presentation**, or one from a **template or theme**.
- **Open** — navigate to an **existing file** to view or modify it.
- **Recent** — your **most recently created presentations, with their locations**.

[Picture 13711 — the File menu with New, Open and Recent marked]

## Starting blank — in order

1. Select **New**.
2. Choose **Blank presentation**.
3. Click **Create** — a blank presentation opens, ready for its first slide.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: from the File tab: New starts a blank presentation or one from a template or theme, Open finds an existing file, Recent lists your latest presentations with their places. Use the lesson's own words for the key names.
