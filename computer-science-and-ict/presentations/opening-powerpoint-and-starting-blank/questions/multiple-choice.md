# Opening PowerPoint and starting blank — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The File tab sits at the —
a) bottom-right  b) top-left  c) centre  d) end of the Ribbon
**Answer: b) top-left**

**2.** To start a blank presentation you choose —
a) Open  b) Recent  c) New  d) Print
**Answer: c) New**

**3.** Recent shows —
a) templates  b) your most recently created presentations, with their locations  c) the Recycle Bin  d) help pages
**Answer: b) your most recently created presentations, with their locations**

**4.** The last click in starting blank is —
a) Save  b) Create  c) Print  d) Close
**Answer: b) Create** — New → Blank presentation → Create.

**5.** Open is for —
a) starting from a template  b) navigating to an existing file to view or modify  c) seeing recent files  d) closing PowerPoint
**Answer: b) navigating to an existing file to view or modify**
