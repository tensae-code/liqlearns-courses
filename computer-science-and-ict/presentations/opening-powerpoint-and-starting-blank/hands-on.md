# Hands-on — Opening PowerPoint and starting blank

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Where there is no computer laboratory, do the screen work on a teacher's or your own phone, as the course's teaching pattern provides.

## Three doors
Open the File tab three times: once to start blank, once to browse with Open (then cancel), once to read the Recent list and name where each recent file lives.

## Template look
Under New, open the templates and themes without choosing one. Name two you would use — one for a school report, one for a celebration — and say what decided you.
