# Adding content to a slide

## Do it first

Your slide's big empty box is not empty — it holds six small icons in its middle, each one a door. One door makes a table, one a chart, one brings a picture. Learn the six doors now.

## Text first

**Text is the default content** — a **bulleted list**: **click and type**. Every other kind of content is added on top of that simple start.

[Picture 13720 — a content placeholder with its six icons labelled]

## The six standard elements

Other content is added by **clicking an icon in the content placeholder**; each icon opens **its dialog or the Drawing Tools contextual tab**. The same options also live on the **Insert tab**. The six standard graphical elements are:

1. **Table**
2. **Chart**
3. **SmartArt**
4. **Pictures**
5. **Online Pictures**
6. **Video**

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: text is the default content — click and type; the other content comes from the six icons in the content placeholder: Table, Chart, SmartArt, Pictures, Online Pictures, Video. Use the lesson's own words for the key names.

## Do It Yourself

2. Teach the doing to someone else, steps in order, while they follow on a real computer or phone.
3. Write the key steps from memory, numbered, in order.
