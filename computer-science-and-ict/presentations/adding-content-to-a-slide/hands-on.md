# Hands-on — Adding content to a slide

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Where there is no computer laboratory, do the screen work on a teacher's or your own phone, as the course's teaching pattern provides.

## Six doors tour
On one slide, open each of the six icons' doors in turn — look at what each asks you, then cancel. Name from memory which door you would use for a price list, a rainfall graph, a family tree.

## Words to picture
Write a three-line bulleted list, then replace its middle line with the content it describes — a small table or picture — using the placeholder icons.
