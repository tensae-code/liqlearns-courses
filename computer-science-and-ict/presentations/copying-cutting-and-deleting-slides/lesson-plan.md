# Lesson plan — Copying, cutting and deleting slides

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Presentations · **Lesson:** Copying, cutting and deleting slides
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 7 — Presentations in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 8 book, Unit 3 — Computer Software. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** slides are rearranged the document way: copy and paste duplicates a slide, cut and paste moves it, and Delete removes it by right-click.
**This lesson's own level:** it is a doing-lesson. It stands on PowerPoint — making a slideshow; inserting slides, and on the document lessons’ copy, cut and paste, now done to whole slides.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Copy and paste a slide** — click the slide and select Copy from the Home tab, then right-click and select Paste — the slide gains a twin
2. **Cut and paste a slide** — select the slide to cut; right-click on the slide; click Cut; then click Paste to paste it — the slide moves
3. **Delete a slide — and the closing presentation** — right-click the slide and choose Delete; the closing activity presents a village tale with its topic bolded, italicised and underlined, page numbers inserted, a colour of choice used, and the file saved under your own name

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: PowerPoint — making a slideshow; inserting slides, and Cut and paste — moving text. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Copy and paste a slide: click the slide, Copy from the Home tab, right-click and Paste.
2. Cut and paste a slide in order: select it, right-click, Cut, then Paste.
3. Delete a slide by right-click and the Delete option.
4. Prepare the closing presentation: a village tale with its topic bolded, italicised and underlined, page numbers inserted, a colour of your choice, saved under your own name.

## What you need
A computer with PowerPoint and a short presentation open in it.

## If the learner is stuck
- Copies when the slide should move → copy leaves the slide standing and adds a twin; to move it, cut and paste.
- Hunts for Delete on the ribbon → in this lesson's route, Delete is chosen by right-clicking the slide itself.
