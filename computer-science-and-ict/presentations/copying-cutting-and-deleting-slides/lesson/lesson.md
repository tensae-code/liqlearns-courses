# Copying, cutting and deleting slides

## Do it first

Your presentation has grown — and now it needs surgery. One slide is so good it should appear twice. One stands in the wrong place. One should not be there at all. Three different jobs: copy, cut, delete. Do them in this lesson's order.

## Sub lesson 1 — Copy and paste a slide

To duplicate a slide: **click the slide** and select **Copy** from the **Home tab**; then **right-click** and select **Paste**. The slide now stands twice — the original and its twin, exactly as copy works on text.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cut and paste a slide

To move a slide, in order:

1. **Select** the slide to cut.
2. **Right-click** on the slide.
3. Click the **Cut** option.
4. Then click **Paste** to paste it in its new place.

The slide leaves its old place and stands in the new one — moved, not copied.

## Sub lesson 3 — Delete a slide — and the closing presentation

To remove a slide: **right-click the slide** and choose the **Delete** option. The slide is gone from the strip.

Now the closing activity, which uses everything in this unit's practicals: **write a tale from your village and present it in PowerPoint** — the topic **bolded, italicised and underlined**, **page numbers inserted**, a **colour of your choice** used, and the file **saved under your own name**.

## Do It Yourself

1. In a practice presentation, duplicate one slide, move one slide, and delete one slide.
2. Write the cut-and-paste steps from memory, numbered.
3. Build the village-tale presentation with all five requirements, and save it under your own name.
