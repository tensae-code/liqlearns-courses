# Video script — Copying, cutting and deleting slides

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A slide can travel like text does.

Copy: click the slide. Home tab — Copy. Right-click — Paste. Now it stands twice.
Cut: select the slide. Right-click — Cut. Paste it where it belongs. It moved.
Delete: right-click the slide. Delete. Gone.

Copy makes a twin. Cut makes a move. Delete makes room.
