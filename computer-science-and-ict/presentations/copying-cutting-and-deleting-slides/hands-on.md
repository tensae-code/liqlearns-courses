# Hands-on — Copying, cutting and deleting slides

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Rearrange a real strip

Build a five-slide practice presentation, one big number per slide (1 to 5). Duplicate slide 2, move slide 5 to the front by cut and paste, and delete the duplicate's extra copy. Read the strip aloud in its new order.

## Your biography presentation

Prepare a short PowerPoint presentation about your own biography — three or four slides: who you are, your school, your village or town, what you want to learn. Format the topic bold, present it to a partner, and take their feedback on the content and on the slides themselves.
