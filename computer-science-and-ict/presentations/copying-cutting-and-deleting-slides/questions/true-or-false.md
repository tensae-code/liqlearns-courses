# Copying, cutting and deleting slides — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Copy and paste leaves the original slide standing and adds a twin.
**Answer: True** — as with text, copy duplicates.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cut and paste a slide, and the slide stands in both the old and the new place.
**Answer: False** — cut moves it; it stands in the new place only.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the closing activity, the file is saved under your own name.
**Answer: True** — one of the activity's five requirements.
