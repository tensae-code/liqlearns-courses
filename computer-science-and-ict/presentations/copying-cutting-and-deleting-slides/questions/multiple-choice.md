# Copying, cutting and deleting slides — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** To copy a slide, you click the slide and select Copy from —
a) the File tab  b) the Home tab  c) the slide show  d) the desktop
**Answer: b) the Home tab** — then right-click and select Paste.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** After copying, the slide is pasted by —
a) pressing Enter  b) double-clicking it  c) right-clicking and selecting Paste  d) saving the file
**Answer: c) right-clicking and selecting Paste.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The first step in cutting a slide is —
a) click Paste  b) select the slide to cut  c) right-click the ribbon  d) delete the slide
**Answer: b) select the slide to cut.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In cut and paste, after right-clicking the selected slide, you click —
a) Delete  b) Copy  c) Cut  d) Save
**Answer: c) Cut** — then click Paste to paste it in its new place.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A slide is deleted by —
a) right-clicking the slide and choosing Delete  b) closing PowerPoint  c) cutting it twice  d) dragging it off the screen
**Answer: a) right-clicking the slide and choosing Delete.**
