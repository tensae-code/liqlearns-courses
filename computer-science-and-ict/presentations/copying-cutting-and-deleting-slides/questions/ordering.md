# Copying, cutting and deleting slides — ordering

Put each set in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Cut and paste a slide: click Paste / select the slide to cut / click the Cut option / right-click on the slide.
**Answer: select the slide to cut → right-click on the slide → click the Cut option → click Paste.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The closing presentation's checks: topic bolded, italicised and underlined / page numbers inserted / a colour of your choice used / the file saved under your own name — all four are required; name the one that comes last in the book's list.
**Answer: the file saved under your own name — the book lists it last, after the bold/italic/underline topic, the page numbers and the colour.**
