# Hands-on — Themes and readable slide design

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Where there is no computer laboratory, do the screen work on a teacher's or your own phone, as the course's teaching pattern provides.

## Before and after
Build three plain slides. Apply a theme and stand back: read the smallest text from two metres away. If it fails, the theme or the sizes fail the rules — fix the sizes first.

## Rule audit
Find any presentation and audit it against the six rules, one tick or cross each. Name the single change that would help it most.
