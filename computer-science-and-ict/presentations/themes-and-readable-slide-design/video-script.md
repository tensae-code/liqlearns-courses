# Video script — Themes and readable slide design

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One thing today: one click on a theme's thumbnail restyles the whole deck — and the design rules keep it readable: contrast, calm backgrounds, sans-serif fonts, big titles, at most two fonts.

Do it first, in the lesson's order. Say each step out loud as you do it.

Then do it again — this time without looking.
