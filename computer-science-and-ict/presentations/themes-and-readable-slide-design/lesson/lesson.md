# Themes and readable slide design

## Do it first

Your slides are plain — black words on white, slide after slide. One click can dress the whole deck at once: colours, fonts, backgrounds, all agreeing. Make the click — then learn the rules that keep a dressed deck READABLE.

## Applying a theme — in order

A **theme** adds a **design and colour scheme** to the whole presentation.

1. Go to the **Design tab**.
2. **Click a built-in theme's thumbnail**.

The **scroll bar and down-arrow** beside the thumbnails offer more; still more come from **Microsoft Office Online**. One click restyles **every slide at once**.

## The design rules

- Use **colour carefully** — to **enhance, not detract**.
- Keep **strong contrast** between background and text: a **light background with dark text, or the reverse**.
- **Avoid busy backgrounds** and **primary colours**.
- Use **sans-serif fonts** — **Arial, Calibri, Helvetica**.
- Sizes: **titles 44–60 points**, **sub-headings 32–40**, **body text 18–32**.
- **No more than two fonts** in one deck.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: one click on a theme's thumbnail restyles the whole deck — and the design rules keep it readable: contrast, calm backgrounds, sans-serif fonts, big titles, at most two fonts. Use the lesson's own words for the key names.

2. Teach the doing to someone else, steps in order, while they follow on a real computer or phone.
