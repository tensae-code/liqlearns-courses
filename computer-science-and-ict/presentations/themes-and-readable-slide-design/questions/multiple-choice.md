# Themes and readable slide design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A theme is applied from —
a) the Home tab  b) the Design tab  c) the File tab  d) the View tab
**Answer: b) the Design tab** — Click a built-in theme's thumbnail.

**2.** Titles should be —
a) 10–18 points  b) 18–32 points  c) 32–40 points  d) 44–60 points
**Answer: d) 44–60 points** — Sub-headings 32–40; body text 18–32.

**3.** Body text should be —
a) 44–60 points  b) 18–32 points  c) 8–12 points  d) 60–72 points
**Answer: b) 18–32 points**

**4.** The contrast rule says —
a) dark text on a dark background  b) strong contrast — light background with dark text, or the reverse  c) no contrast at all  d) primary colours everywhere
**Answer: b) strong contrast — light background with dark text, or the reverse**

**5.** Use at most — fonts in one deck
a) one  b) two  c) four  d) six
**Answer: b) two**

**6.** Fonts named as suitable are —
a) serif fonts like Times  b) sans-serif fonts — Arial, Calibri, Helvetica  c) handwriting fonts  d) decorative fonts
**Answer: b) sans-serif fonts — Arial, Calibri, Helvetica**
