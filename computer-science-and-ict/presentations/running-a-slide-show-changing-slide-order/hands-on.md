# Hands-on — Running a slide show; changing slide order

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Where there is no computer laboratory, do the screen work on a teacher's or your own phone, as the course's teaching pattern provides.

## The two-slide swap
In Slide Sorter, drag your last slide to second place. Run the show from the beginning and check the new order tells the story better. If not, drag it back — order is cheap to change.

## Presenter run
Rehearse the whole show aloud once, advancing with the arrows, and exit with Esc. Note the one slide where you stumbled — that slide, not your voice, usually needs the fix.
