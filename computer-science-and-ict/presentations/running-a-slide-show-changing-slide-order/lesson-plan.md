# Lesson plan — Running a slide show; changing slide order

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Presentations · **Lesson:** Running a slide show; changing slide order
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Presentations in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 9 book, Unit 3 — Application Software. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Slide Show tab runs the show from the beginning or the current slide and rehearses timings; slide order changes by dragging a slide in Slide Sorter — a line shows where it will land.
**This lesson's own level:** it is a doing-lesson. It stands on The four PowerPoint views.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The four PowerPoint views. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Run a show from the beginning, and from the current slide, from the Slide Show tab.
2. Use Set Up Slide Show to control how the show is presented, and rehearse timings.
3. Reorder slides: click and hold a slide in Slide Sorter (or the Thumbnail pane), drag it to its new place — the line shows where it will land — and release.
## What you need
A computer with PowerPoint; a finished presentation.
