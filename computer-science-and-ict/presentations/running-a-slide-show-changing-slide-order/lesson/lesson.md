# Running a slide show; changing slide order

## Do it first

Your talk is built — but slide four should come second, and the show must start on time. Two last skills: move the slide without rebuilding it, and run the show like a presenter.

## The Slide Show tab

The **Slide Show tab** **runs the show from the beginning or from the current slide**, **controls how it is presented**, and **rehearses timings** through **Set Up Slide Show**.

## Changing slide order — in order

Slide order is changed in **Slide Sorter view** (or the **Thumbnail pane**):

1. **Click and hold** the slide.
2. **Drag it** to its new place — **a line shows where it will land**.
3. **Release** — the slide stands in its new place, and the others close around it.
