# Creating a slide master

## Do it first

Your school logo must sit in the same place on every one of forty slides. Do not paste it forty times — put it once, on the master, and watch every slide take it.

## Creating a slide master — in order

A **slide master is the top slide of the hierarchy** storing a presentation's **theme and layouts — background, colour, fonts, effects, placeholder sizes and positions** — so **one edit governs every slide**: the way to put a **school logo in the same place throughout**.

In the book's order:

1. **View tab → Slide Master**.
2. **Select the master slide at the top of the thumbnail pane**.
3. **Master Layout** on the **Slide Master tab**.
4. **Make the updates** — theme, background, fonts, the logo.
5. **Delete unwanted built-in layouts**: right-click them in the pane → **Delete Layout**.
6. Set **Slide Orientation** — portrait or landscape — for all slides.
7. **File → Save As**, name it, and save with type **PowerPoint Template**.

The practical builds a master carrying a **school logo or picture, background style 6 and a named theme**.

## Do It Yourself

1. Open View → Slide Master and select the master at the top of the pane.
2. Put a school logo on the master and check it appears on the layouts beneath.
3. Delete one unwanted built-in layout.
4. Set the orientation, then Save As a PowerPoint Template.
5. Build the practical master: logo or picture, background style 6, a named theme.
