# Lesson plan — Creating a slide master

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Presentations · **Lesson:** Creating a slide master
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Application Software, Grade 10 depth, in the Presentations title of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md). The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the slide master is the top slide of the hierarchy — one edit to its theme, background, fonts and logo governs every slide — and it is saved as a PowerPoint Template.
**This lesson's own level:** it is a doing-lesson. It stands on the slide and theme lessons; the master is where a whole deck's sameness is kept.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Animating slide content — entrance, emphasis, the Animation Pane. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a slide master is and what it stores.
2. Open Slide Master view and work on the top master slide.
3. Delete unwanted layouts and set orientation for all slides.
4. Save the master as a PowerPoint Template.

## What you need
A computer with PowerPoint and a school logo or picture file.

## If the learner is stuck
- Slides did not take the change → you edited a layout, not the master; select the top slide in the thumbnail pane.
- The save offered only Presentation → in Save As, set the type to PowerPoint Template before saving.
