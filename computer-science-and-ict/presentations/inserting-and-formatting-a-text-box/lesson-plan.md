## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Inserting a text box — text where you put it** — A **text box** is a box the maker **inserts and draws** on the slide — at the chosen place, of the chosen size — and types into: a caption under a picture, a label on a diagram, a title where no title placeholder stands. Unlike the slide's built-in placeholders, the text box belongs entirely to its maker: it goes where the design needs words, and it can be moved, resized and removed as one object.
2. **Formatting the box and its text — and WordArt** — The box is formatted as an **object** (its fill, its outline, its position) and its **text is formatted inside it** (the font, size, colour and alignment of the words) — two dressings, kept distinct. The lesson's flourish is **WordArt**: text given a ready **style** — shaped, coloured and shadowed display lettering — for the words that must be seen before they are read. The judgement taught with the tool: WordArt for the few display words; plain, readable text for everything the audience must actually read.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
