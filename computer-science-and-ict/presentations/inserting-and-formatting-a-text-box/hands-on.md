# Hands-on — Inserting and Formatting a Text Box

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Place your words', 'On one slide, add three text boxes — a title in WordArt, a caption, and a label. List each box's job.')

## Task two
('Two dressings', 'Format one text box twice: once as an object only, once as text only. Say what each dressing changed.')
