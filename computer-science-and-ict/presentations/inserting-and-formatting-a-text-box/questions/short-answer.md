# Inserting and Formatting a Text Box — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a text box?**
**Answer: A box the maker inserts and draws on a slide, at the chosen place and size, and types into**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two uses of a text box.**
**Answer: A caption under a picture; a label on a diagram (also a title where no placeholder stands)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does it differ from built-in placeholders?**
**Answer: It belongs entirely to its maker — placed, moved, resized and removed as one object**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What two dressings does formatting give?**
**Answer: The box as an object (fill, outline, position), and the text inside it (font, size, colour, alignment)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is WordArt?**
**Answer: Text given a ready style — shaped, coloured, shadowed display lettering**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which words suit WordArt?**
**Answer: The few display words that must be seen before they are read**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which words should stay plain?**
**Answer: Everything the audience must actually read**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is this lesson in the book's journey?**
**Answer: 'The Exciting Presentation' (2) — the second PowerPoint lesson**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who is 'you' in the slide's other lessons — what journey follows?**
**Answer: Inserting pictures, audio, and slide design follow in lessons 3–5**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 4 Digital Explorer Term 2, Unit 2 Lesson 2**
