## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Inserting audio — sound placed on a slide** — **Inserting audio material** puts a sound **onto a slide**: the sound belongs to that slide and **plays when the slide is shown** (or when its icon is clicked, as the presenter chooses). A sound is placed like a picture — chosen, inserted, positioned — but judged by ear: does it **belong** to this slide's point, does it start at the **right moment**, and is it **short** enough not to swallow the presenter's words?
2. **Recording in PowerPoint, and ready-made clips** — PowerPoint can also **record sound itself**: speaking into the computer's microphone, the presenter records a **narration or comment for a slide** from inside the program — no other tool needed. And a **ready-made sound clip** — a sound file already made, from a library or an earlier recording — is inserted the same way as any audio. Libya's three moves make one skill set: **bring** a sound, **make** a sound, **use** a ready sound — every way a slide gains a voice.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
