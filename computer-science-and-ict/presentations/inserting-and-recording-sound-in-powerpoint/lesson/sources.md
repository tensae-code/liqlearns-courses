Libya's own way (Grade 12 Humanities, Information Technology student book): the Humanities final year pairs the social/legal side of ICT (data security, e-government, computer law and software licences) with a long multimedia-and-PowerPoint practical; the closing projects are Libyan — a deck about Libyan dishes and a deck about Libya's archaeological cities and landmarks.

Kuwait's own way (Grade 4 Digital Explorer Term 2, printed title — records name it 'Our Digital World'): the Digital World unit mixes identity (pride in Arabic language and identity online), cloud storage on the national platform (OneDrive inside Teams), and a three-lesson AI arc (data → training a machine → AI decisions) before the tool unit; PowerPoint is taught as a five-step 'Exciting Presentation' journey ending in presentations about Kuwait itself.


Kazakhstan's own way (Kopeeva G3 ICT): the older Grade 3 "ICT" subject (not Digital Literacy): no programming or robotics at all — the year is computer basics, WordPad-style text work, sound recording, a long Paint strand (tools, shapes, repeating patterns, palette, printing, mixed text-and-picture documents), Internet safety and search, and a full presentation strand ending with sound inside slides.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kobdikova G4 ICT): Grade 4 ICT deepens every primary strand at once: Scratch grows variables, logical and comparison operators on the way to "My game"; EV3 robotics is taught through its sensors (colour, ultrasonic) towards labyrinth and kegeling; video is a real subject in the NCH VideoPad editor; and the final part pairs e-mail file exchange with password reliability and "computers of the future". Augmented-reality markers (the AR+ app from arman-pv.kz) animate pictures through a phone.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kadirkulov G3 ICT): the Almatykitap team's Grade 3 ICT runs the same eight cross-cutting themes as the Arman-PV book (Living nature; What is good, what is bad; Time; Architecture; Art; Outstanding personalities; Water — source of life; Culture of rest. Holidays) but as 34 short lessons with named mini-project defences ("My idol"); the glossary names Tux Paint as the children's raster editor and WordPad as the text editor. The mixed review lesson 21 ("Creative tasks: searching, typing, drawing", printed p. 53) is not placed.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kopeeva G3 «Ақпараттық-коммуникациялық технологиялар»): Grade 3 ICT is a tools year organised by cross-cutting themes («Менің мектебім», «Сәулет», «Өнер»…): text, then sound, then computer pictures in a graphics editor, then a mixed document that combines them, then internet safety/search and presentations — with a practicum closing almost every section.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kadirkulov G3 «Ақпараттық-коммуникациялық технологиялар»): the older Almatykitap Grade 3 ICT book (2018, a different book from the 2021 Digital Literacy, Book 23) is organised by cross-curricular themes — Living Nature, What is Good and What is Bad, Time, Architecture, Art, Outstanding People, Water is the Source of Life, Culture of Rest — and teaches sound (playback, effects, recording, processing) and the graphics editor inside them, ending every theme with a defended mini-project.
