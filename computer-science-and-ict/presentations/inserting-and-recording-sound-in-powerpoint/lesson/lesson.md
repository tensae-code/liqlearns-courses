# Inserting and Recording Sound in PowerPoint

## Do it first

A slide can be heard as well as seen. Libya's Grade 12 multimedia presentations chapter gives sound its own teaching, in three moves. This lesson works through the three in the book's order.

## Sub lesson 1 — Inserting audio — sound placed on a slide

**Inserting audio material** puts a sound **onto a slide**: the sound belongs to that slide and **plays when the slide is shown** (or when its icon is clicked, as the presenter chooses). A sound is placed like a picture — chosen, inserted, positioned — but judged by ear: does it **belong** to this slide's point, does it start at the **right moment**, and is it **short** enough not to swallow the presenter's words?

## Sub lesson 2 — Recording in PowerPoint, and ready-made clips

PowerPoint can also **record sound itself**: speaking into the computer's microphone, the presenter records a **narration or comment for a slide** from inside the program — no other tool needed. And a **ready-made sound clip** — a sound file already made, from a library or an earlier recording — is inserted the same way as any audio. Libya's three moves make one skill set: **bring** a sound, **make** a sound, **use** a ready sound — every way a slide gains a voice.
