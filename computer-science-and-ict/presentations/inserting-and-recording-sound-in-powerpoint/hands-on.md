# Hands-on — Inserting and Recording Sound in PowerPoint

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Give a slide a voice', 'Take one slide and record a 20-second narration for it inside PowerPoint. Play the show and judge: does the sound belong, and is it clear?')

## Task two
('Clip it', 'Insert a ready-made sound clip on a title slide. Set when it plays, then present the slide and say why that moment is right.')
