# Inserting and Recording Sound in PowerPoint — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What three moves does this lesson teach?**
**Answer: Inserting audio material, recording sound in PowerPoint, and inserting a ready-made sound clip**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does an inserted sound live?**
**Answer: On the slide it was inserted into; it plays when that slide is shown or its icon is clicked**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a sound judged, unlike a picture?**
**Answer: By ear — it must belong to the slide's point, start at the right moment and not swallow the presenter's words**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What can PowerPoint record?**
**Answer: Sound itself — narration or a comment for a slide, spoken into the microphone**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is another program needed to record in PowerPoint?**
**Answer: No — the recording is done from inside PowerPoint**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a ready-made sound clip?**
**Answer: A sound file already made — from a library or an earlier recording**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is a ready-made clip added?**
**Answer: Inserted the same way as any audio material**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Say the three moves as one skill set.**
**Answer: Bring a sound, make a sound, use a ready sound**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Libya's Grade 12 Humanities book, Chapter 5 — using multimedia in producing presentations**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must a sound never do?**
**Answer: Swallow the presenter's words — it serves the slide's point**
