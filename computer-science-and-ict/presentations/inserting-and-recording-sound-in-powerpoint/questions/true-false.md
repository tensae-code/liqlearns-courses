# Inserting and Recording Sound in PowerPoint — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What three moves does this lesson teach?" is: Inserting audio material, recording sound in PowerPoint, and inserting a ready-made sound clip**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does an inserted sound live?" is: By ear — it must belong to the slide's point, start at the right moment and not swallow the presenter's words**
**Answer: False** — On the slide it was inserted into; it plays when that slide is shown or its icon is clicked

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is a sound judged, unlike a picture?" is: By ear — it must belong to the slide's point, start at the right moment and not swallow the presenter's words**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What can PowerPoint record?" is: No — the recording is done from inside PowerPoint**
**Answer: False** — Sound itself — narration or a comment for a slide, spoken into the microphone

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Is another program needed to record in PowerPoint?" is: No — the recording is done from inside PowerPoint**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is a ready-made sound clip?" is: Inserted the same way as any audio material**
**Answer: False** — A sound file already made — from a library or an earlier recording

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is a ready-made clip added?" is: Inserted the same way as any audio material**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Say the three moves as one skill set." is: Libya's Grade 12 Humanities book, Chapter 5 — using multimedia in producing presentations**
**Answer: False** — Bring a sound, make a sound, use a ready sound

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which chapter teaches this?" is: Libya's Grade 12 Humanities book, Chapter 5 — using multimedia in producing presentations**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What must a sound never do?" is: Inserting audio material, recording sound in PowerPoint, and inserting a ready-made sound clip**
**Answer: False** — Swallow the presenter's words — it serves the slide's point
