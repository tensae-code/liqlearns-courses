# Building an Interactive Game With Hyperlinks — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is each slide in the game?**
**Answer: A place or a question**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are hyperlinks in the game?**
**Answer: The doors — chosen answers jump to answering slides**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is drawn first?**
**Answer: The game's map — slides as boxes, links as arrows**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to a player in an unmapped game?**
**Answer: They are stranded in rooms with no doors**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In which subject environment is the project set?**
**Answer: Maths — patterns and equations**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a hyperlink placed?**
**Answer: Select the object, insert the link, choose the slide**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What slide must every path reach?**
**Answer: A home slide**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What should wrong answers do?**
**Answer: Teach — a hint, not a dead end**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is the game tested?**
**Answer: As players play it — clicking everything, in the wrong order**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where do an interactive game's faults live?**
**Answer: In its links — only clicking finds them**
