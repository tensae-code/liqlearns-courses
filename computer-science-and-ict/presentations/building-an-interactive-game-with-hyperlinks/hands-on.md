# Hands-on — Building an Interactive Game With Hyperlinks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Map a game** — Draw the map of a five-slide quiz game (start, two questions with right/wrong paths, a win slide, a home) as boxes and arrows.

## Task two
**Build and break** — Build a small linked game in PowerPoint, then test it by clicking wrongly and out of order; fix every stranding you find.
