# Video script — Slide transitions — and using one throughout

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Between two slides, there is a jump. A transition softens it.
Normal view. First slide selected.
Transitions tab. Choose an effect. A star appears beside the slide.
Effect Options — its direction. Duration — longer is slower.
A sound? A camera shutter, if you must.
Ctrl+S saves. F5 runs the show. Esc stops it.
But remember the rule: mixed transitions distract.
None — Apply to All. Choose ONE. Apply to All. Calm, all the way through.
