# Hands-on — Slide transitions — and using one throughout

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Calm deck, loud deck

Make two copies of a deck: one with a different transition on every slide, one with a single transition throughout. Show both to a partner and let them judge which they could watch for an hour.

## Timed show

Set every slide to advance automatically after set seconds and run the deck hands-free.
