# Slide transitions — and using one throughout — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A transition acts —
a) on items within a slide  b) between slides  c) on the printer  d) on the file name
**Answer: b) between slides** — animations act within a slide.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A chosen transition is marked by —
a) a star beside the slide miniature  b) a red border  c) a sound  d) a new slide
**Answer: a) a star beside the slide miniature**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** To slow a transition, lengthen its —
a) Sound  b) Duration  c) Advance  d) Layout
**Answer: b) Duration**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The craft rule begins by removing all transitions with —
a) Esc  b) None → Apply to All  c) Delete  d) F5
**Answer: b) None → Apply to All**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Mixed transitions are ruled against because they —
a) slow the computer  b) distract and harm accessibility  c) cost money  d) change the text
**Answer: b) distract and harm accessibility**
