# Lesson plan — Graphic Organisers — Mind Maps, Timelines and Org Charts

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Presentations · **Lesson:** Graphic Organisers — Mind Maps, Timelines and Org Charts
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Cuba (2026-11-14) — in the title Presentations on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Cuba's Mi Mundo Digital / Informática student books and teacher-side books (Ministerio de Educación / Editorial Pueblo y Educación), because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A **graphic organiser** is a drawing that organises ideas so their relations are seen at once; its common kinds are the **mind map** (ideas branching from a centre), the **timeline** (events in time order) and the **org chart** (who stands above and below whom).
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Graphic organisers — relations made visible** — A list hides relations; a drawing shows them. A **graphic organiser** arranges ideas **in space** — centre and branches, before and after, above and below — so the reader sees **how the parts connect** in one look. The organiser is chosen by the relation it must show: branching ideas, time order, or rank. Cuba teaches organisers inside the presentations book because slides are where organised ideas are shown to an audience.
2. **Mind maps, timelines and org charts** — A **mind map** grows from a **centre**: the main idea in the middle, its parts on branches, their details on smaller branches. A **timeline** lays **events along a line in time order** — first to last, each at its date. An **org chart** stacks **boxes by rank**: who directs at the top, who answers to whom below. One content, three drawings: the same school can be a mind map of its activities, a timeline of its year, and an org chart of its staff.
