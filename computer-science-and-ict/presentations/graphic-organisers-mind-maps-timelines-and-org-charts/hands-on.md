# Hands-on — Graphic Organisers — Mind Maps, Timelines and Org Charts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Three organisers, one topic', 'Take one topic and draw it all three ways: a mind map, a timeline and an org chart (or the nearest each can honestly come). Say which drawing shows the topic best and why.')

## Task two
('Organiser on a slide', "Put one graphic organiser on a presentation slide in place of a bulleted list of the same content, and say what the audience gains.")
