# Graphic Organisers — Mind Maps, Timelines and Org Charts — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a graphic organiser do?**
**Answer: Arranges ideas in space so their relations are seen at once**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a list hide that a drawing shows?**
**Answer: The relations between the parts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) By what is the organiser chosen?**
**Answer: By the relation it must show**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three relations named in this lesson.**
**Answer: Branching ideas, time order and rank**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Cuba teach organisers?**
**Answer: Inside the presentations book**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where does a mind map start?**
**Answer: At a centre, with the main idea in the middle**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What lies along a timeline?**
**Answer: Events in time order, each at its date**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does an org chart stack?**
**Answer: Boxes by rank — who directs and who answers to whom**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What grows on a mind map's smaller branches?**
**Answer: The details of each part**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What can the same school be drawn as?**
**Answer: A mind map of activities, a timeline of its year and an org chart of its staff**
