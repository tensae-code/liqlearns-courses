# Graphic Organisers — Mind Maps, Timelines and Org Charts — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a graphic organiser do?" is: Arranges ideas in space so their relations are seen at once**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a list hide that a drawing shows?" is: By the relation it must show**
**Answer: False** — The relations between the parts

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "By what is the organiser chosen?" is: By the relation it must show**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the three relations named in this lesson." is: Inside the presentations book**
**Answer: False** — Branching ideas, time order and rank

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does Cuba teach organisers?" is: Inside the presentations book**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does a mind map start?" is: Events in time order, each at its date**
**Answer: False** — At a centre, with the main idea in the middle

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What lies along a timeline?" is: Events in time order, each at its date**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does an org chart stack?" is: The details of each part**
**Answer: False** — Boxes by rank — who directs and who answers to whom

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What grows on a mind map's smaller branches?" is: The details of each part**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What can the same school be drawn as?" is: Arranges ideas in space so their relations are seen at once**
**Answer: False** — A mind map of activities, a timeline of its year and an org chart of its staff
