# Graphic Organisers — Mind Maps, Timelines and Org Charts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a graphic organiser do?**
a) Arranges ideas in space so their relations are seen at once  b) The relations between the parts  c) By the relation it must show  d) Branching ideas, time order and rank
**Answer: a) Arranges ideas in space so their relations are seen at once**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a list hide that a drawing shows?**
a) By the relation it must show  b) The relations between the parts  c) Branching ideas, time order and rank  d) Inside the presentations book
**Answer: b) The relations between the parts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) By what is the organiser chosen?**
a) Branching ideas, time order and rank  b) Inside the presentations book  c) By the relation it must show  d) At a centre, with the main idea in the middle
**Answer: c) By the relation it must show**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three relations named in this lesson.**
a) Inside the presentations book  b) At a centre, with the main idea in the middle  c) Events in time order, each at its date  d) Branching ideas, time order and rank
**Answer: d) Branching ideas, time order and rank**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does Cuba teach organisers?**
a) Inside the presentations book  b) At a centre, with the main idea in the middle  c) Events in time order, each at its date  d) Boxes by rank — who directs and who answers to whom
**Answer: a) Inside the presentations book**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where does a mind map start?**
a) Events in time order, each at its date  b) At a centre, with the main idea in the middle  c) Boxes by rank — who directs and who answers to whom  d) The details of each part
**Answer: b) At a centre, with the main idea in the middle**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What lies along a timeline?**
a) Boxes by rank — who directs and who answers to whom  b) The details of each part  c) Events in time order, each at its date  d) A mind map of activities, a timeline of its year and an org chart of its staff
**Answer: c) Events in time order, each at its date**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does an org chart stack?**
a) The details of each part  b) A mind map of activities, a timeline of its year and an org chart of its staff  c) Arranges ideas in space so their relations are seen at once  d) Boxes by rank — who directs and who answers to whom
**Answer: d) Boxes by rank — who directs and who answers to whom**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What grows on a mind map's smaller branches?**
a) The details of each part  b) A mind map of activities, a timeline of its year and an org chart of its staff  c) Arranges ideas in space so their relations are seen at once  d) The relations between the parts
**Answer: a) The details of each part**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What can the same school be drawn as?**
a) Arranges ideas in space so their relations are seen at once  b) A mind map of activities, a timeline of its year and an org chart of its staff  c) The relations between the parts  d) By the relation it must show
