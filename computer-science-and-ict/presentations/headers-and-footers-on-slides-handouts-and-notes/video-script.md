# Video script — Headers and Footers on Slides, Handouts and Notes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **header** prints at the **top** of a page and a **footer** at the **bottom**, the same on every page. In PowerPoint the Header and Footer setting carries three different things: on **slides** it can add the **date and time**, the **slide number** and a **footer text**; on **notes and handouts** it adds a true header as well. Fiji teaches them together because the dialog is one — but what appears, and where, depends on which carrier you choose.
The working order is: open the **Header and Footer** dialog, choose the **Slides** tab or the **Notes and Handouts** tab, tick what the set needs — date (fixed, or updating automatically), slide or page number, footer words such as the talk's title or the school's name — and **Apply to All** so every page carries it. Then **preview the print**: headers and footers are print furniture, and the preview is where a wrong date or a missing number is caught before paper is spent.
