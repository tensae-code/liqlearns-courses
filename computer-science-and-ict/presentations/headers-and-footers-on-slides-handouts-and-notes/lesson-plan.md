**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Headers and footers — one setting, three carriers** — A **header** prints at the **top** of a page and a **footer** at the **bottom**, the same on every page. In PowerPoint the Header and Footer setting carries three different things: on **slides** it can add the **date and time**, the **slide number** and a **footer text**; on **notes and handouts** it adds a true header as well. Fiji teaches them together because the dialog is one — but what appears, and where, depends on which carrier you choose.
2. **Setting them — and checking the print** — The working order is: open the **Header and Footer** dialog, choose the **Slides** tab or the **Notes and Handouts** tab, tick what the set needs — date (fixed, or updating automatically), slide or page number, footer words such as the talk's title or the school's name — and **Apply to All** so every page carries it. Then **preview the print**: headers and footers are print furniture, and the preview is where a wrong date or a missing number is caught before paper is spent.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
