# Headers and Footers on Slides, Handouts and Notes — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a header?**
**Answer: Text that prints at the top of every page**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a footer?**
**Answer: Text that prints at the bottom of every page**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What three things can a slide footer carry?**
**Answer: The date and time, the slide number, and a footer text**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which carriers does the PowerPoint setting serve?**
**Answer: Slides, and notes and handouts**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What extra does the notes-and-handouts carrier have?**
**Answer: A true header at the top of the page as well as the footer**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the first step in setting them?**
**Answer: Open the Header and Footer dialog and choose the Slides tab or the Notes and Handouts tab**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What choice does the date offer?**
**Answer: A fixed date, or one that updates automatically**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why choose Apply to All?**
**Answer: So every slide or page in the set carries the same header and footer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What should footer words usually be?**
**Answer: The talk's title or the school's name — what identifies the printed set**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where is a wrong date caught before printing?**
**Answer: In the print preview**
