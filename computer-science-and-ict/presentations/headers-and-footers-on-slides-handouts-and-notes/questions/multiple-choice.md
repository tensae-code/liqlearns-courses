# Headers and Footers on Slides, Handouts and Notes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a header?**
a) Text that prints at the top of every page  b) Text that prints at the bottom of every page  c) The date and time, the slide number, and a footer text  d) Slides, and notes and handouts
**Answer: a) Text that prints at the top of every page**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a footer?**
a) The date and time, the slide number, and a footer text  b) Text that prints at the bottom of every page  c) Slides, and notes and handouts  d) A true header at the top of the page as well as the footer
**Answer: b) Text that prints at the bottom of every page**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What three things can a slide footer carry?**
a) Slides, and notes and handouts  b) A true header at the top of the page as well as the footer  c) The date and time, the slide number, and a footer text  d) Open the Header and Footer dialog and choose the Slides tab or the Notes and Handouts tab
**Answer: c) The date and time, the slide number, and a footer text**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which carriers does the PowerPoint setting serve?**
a) A true header at the top of the page as well as the footer  b) Open the Header and Footer dialog and choose the Slides tab or the Notes and Handouts tab  c) A fixed date, or one that updates automatically  d) Slides, and notes and handouts
**Answer: d) Slides, and notes and handouts**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What extra does the notes-and-handouts carrier have?**
a) A true header at the top of the page as well as the footer  b) Open the Header and Footer dialog and choose the Slides tab or the Notes and Handouts tab  c) A fixed date, or one that updates automatically  d) So every slide or page in the set carries the same header and footer
**Answer: a) A true header at the top of the page as well as the footer**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the first step in setting them?**
a) A fixed date, or one that updates automatically  b) Open the Header and Footer dialog and choose the Slides tab or the Notes and Handouts tab  c) So every slide or page in the set carries the same header and footer  d) The talk's title or the school's name — what identifies the printed set
**Answer: b) Open the Header and Footer dialog and choose the Slides tab or the Notes and Handouts tab**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What choice does the date offer?**
a) So every slide or page in the set carries the same header and footer  b) The talk's title or the school's name — what identifies the printed set  c) A fixed date, or one that updates automatically  d) In the print preview
**Answer: c) A fixed date, or one that updates automatically**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why choose Apply to All?**
a) The talk's title or the school's name — what identifies the printed set  b) In the print preview  c) Text that prints at the top of every page  d) So every slide or page in the set carries the same header and footer
**Answer: d) So every slide or page in the set carries the same header and footer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What should footer words usually be?**
a) The talk's title or the school's name — what identifies the printed set  b) In the print preview  c) Text that prints at the top of every page  d) Text that prints at the bottom of every page
**Answer: a) The talk's title or the school's name — what identifies the printed set**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where is a wrong date caught before printing?**
a) Text that prints at the top of every page  b) In the print preview  c) Text that prints at the bottom of every page  d) The date and time, the slide number, and a footer text
**Answer: b) In the print preview**
