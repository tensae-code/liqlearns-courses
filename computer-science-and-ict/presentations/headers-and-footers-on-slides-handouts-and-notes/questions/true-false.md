# Headers and Footers on Slides, Handouts and Notes — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a header?" is: Text that prints at the top of every page**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a footer?" is: The date and time, the slide number, and a footer text**
**Answer: False** — Text that prints at the bottom of every page

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What three things can a slide footer carry?" is: The date and time, the slide number, and a footer text**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which carriers does the PowerPoint setting serve?" is: A true header at the top of the page as well as the footer**
**Answer: False** — Slides, and notes and handouts

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What extra does the notes-and-handouts carrier have?" is: A true header at the top of the page as well as the footer**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the first step in setting them?" is: A fixed date, or one that updates automatically**
**Answer: False** — Open the Header and Footer dialog and choose the Slides tab or the Notes and Handouts tab

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What choice does the date offer?" is: A fixed date, or one that updates automatically**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why choose Apply to All?" is: The talk's title or the school's name — what identifies the printed set**
**Answer: False** — So every slide or page in the set carries the same header and footer

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What should footer words usually be?" is: The talk's title or the school's name — what identifies the printed set**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where is a wrong date caught before printing?" is: Text that prints at the top of every page**
**Answer: False** — In the print preview
