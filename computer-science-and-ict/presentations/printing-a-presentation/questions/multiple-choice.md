# Printing a Presentation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What choice begins printing a presentation?**
a) The form — full-page slides, handouts, or notes pages  b) A record of each slide, large  c) Several slides to a page, for the audience to keep  d) The speaker's notes under each slide
**Answer: a) The form — full-page slides, handouts, or notes pages**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are full-page slides printed for?**
a) Several slides to a page, for the audience to keep  b) A record of each slide, large  c) The speaker's notes under each slide  d) The printer, which slides to print, and the number of copies
**Answer: b) A record of each slide, large**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are handouts?**
a) The speaker's notes under each slide  b) The printer, which slides to print, and the number of copies  c) Several slides to a page, for the audience to keep  d) The pages exactly as they will come out
**Answer: c) Several slides to a page, for the audience to keep**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do notes pages carry?**
a) The printer, which slides to print, and the number of copies  b) The pages exactly as they will come out  c) A wrong layout, wrong order, or text cut or too small to read  d) The speaker's notes under each slide
**Answer: d) The speaker's notes under each slide**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What else does the Print command choose?**
a) The printer, which slides to print, and the number of copies  b) The pages exactly as they will come out  c) A wrong layout, wrong order, or text cut or too small to read  d) Preview, fix, then print
**Answer: a) The printer, which slides to print, and the number of copies**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Print Preview show?**
a) A wrong layout, wrong order, or text cut or too small to read  b) The pages exactly as they will come out  c) Preview, fix, then print  d) So a mistake costs one sheet, not a whole class set
**Answer: b) The pages exactly as they will come out**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What can the preview reveal?**
a) Preview, fix, then print  b) So a mistake costs one sheet, not a whole class set  c) A wrong layout, wrong order, or text cut or too small to read  d) The audience — slides live on screen, paper travels
**Answer: c) A wrong layout, wrong order, or text cut or too small to read**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the order taught for printing?**
a) So a mistake costs one sheet, not a whole class set  b) The audience — slides live on screen, paper travels  c) The form — full-page slides, handouts, or notes pages  d) Preview, fix, then print
**Answer: d) Preview, fix, then print**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why print one test copy first?**
a) So a mistake costs one sheet, not a whole class set  b) The audience — slides live on screen, paper travels  c) The form — full-page slides, handouts, or notes pages  d) A record of each slide, large
**Answer: a) So a mistake costs one sheet, not a whole class set**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who goes home with the printed presentation?**
a) The form — full-page slides, handouts, or notes pages  b) The audience — slides live on screen, paper travels  c) A record of each slide, large  d) Several slides to a page, for the audience to keep
**Answer: b) The audience — slides live on screen, paper travels**
