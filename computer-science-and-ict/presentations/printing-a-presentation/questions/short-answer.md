# Printing a Presentation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What choice begins printing a presentation?**
**Answer: The form — full-page slides, handouts, or notes pages**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are full-page slides printed for?**
**Answer: A record of each slide, large**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are handouts?**
**Answer: Several slides to a page, for the audience to keep**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do notes pages carry?**
**Answer: The speaker's notes under each slide**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What else does the Print command choose?**
**Answer: The printer, which slides to print, and the number of copies**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Print Preview show?**
**Answer: The pages exactly as they will come out**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What can the preview reveal?**
**Answer: A wrong layout, wrong order, or text cut or too small to read**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the order taught for printing?**
**Answer: Preview, fix, then print**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why print one test copy first?**
**Answer: So a mistake costs one sheet, not a whole class set**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who goes home with the printed presentation?**
**Answer: The audience — slides live on screen, paper travels**
