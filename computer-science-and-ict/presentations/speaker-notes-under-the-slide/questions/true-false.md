# Speaker Notes Under the Slide — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are speaker notes?" is: The presenter's private text kept under each slide**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Who never sees the notes on the show screen?" is: Below the slide**
**Answer: False** — The audience

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where is the notes area in Normal view?" is: Below the slide**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What belongs in notes?" is: A slide is a poster — the words read and the words spoken are two different texts**
**Answer: False** — The fuller explanation, exact figures, the story for a picture, reminders

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why are slides not a script?" is: A slide is a poster — the words read and the words spoken are two different texts**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What can the presenter print to present from?" is: What you will say, in the order you will say it — short to glance at, complete enough to find a lost place**
**Answer: False** — A notes page — slide small at the top, its notes beneath

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How should notes be written?" is: What you will say, in the order you will say it — short to glance at, complete enough to find a lost place**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do notes hand over when someone else presents?" is: Cover the slides — the notes alone should still make the presentation make sense**
**Answer: False** — Not just the pictures, but the talk

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the lesson's test for notes?" is: Cover the slides — the notes alone should still make the presentation make sense**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which grade's book teaches this, and to finish what?" is: The presenter's private text kept under each slide**
**Answer: False** — Grade 12, finishing the PowerPoint course
