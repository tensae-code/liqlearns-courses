# Speaker Notes Under the Slide — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are speaker notes?**
**Answer: The presenter's private text kept under each slide**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who never sees the notes on the show screen?**
**Answer: The audience**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the notes area in Normal view?**
**Answer: Below the slide**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What belongs in notes?**
**Answer: The fuller explanation, exact figures, the story for a picture, reminders**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why are slides not a script?**
**Answer: A slide is a poster — the words read and the words spoken are two different texts**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What can the presenter print to present from?**
**Answer: A notes page — slide small at the top, its notes beneath**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How should notes be written?**
**Answer: What you will say, in the order you will say it — short to glance at, complete enough to find a lost place**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do notes hand over when someone else presents?**
**Answer: Not just the pictures, but the talk**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the lesson's test for notes?**
**Answer: Cover the slides — the notes alone should still make the presentation make sense**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which grade's book teaches this, and to finish what?**
**Answer: Grade 12, finishing the PowerPoint course**
