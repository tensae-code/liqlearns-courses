# Speaker Notes Under the Slide — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are speaker notes?**
a) The presenter's private text kept under each slide  b) The audience  c) Below the slide  d) The fuller explanation, exact figures, the story for a picture, reminders
**Answer: a) The presenter's private text kept under each slide**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who never sees the notes on the show screen?**
a) Below the slide  b) The audience  c) The fuller explanation, exact figures, the story for a picture, reminders  d) A slide is a poster — the words read and the words spoken are two different texts
**Answer: b) The audience**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the notes area in Normal view?**
a) The fuller explanation, exact figures, the story for a picture, reminders  b) A slide is a poster — the words read and the words spoken are two different texts  c) Below the slide  d) A notes page — slide small at the top, its notes beneath
**Answer: c) Below the slide**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What belongs in notes?**
a) A slide is a poster — the words read and the words spoken are two different texts  b) A notes page — slide small at the top, its notes beneath  c) What you will say, in the order you will say it — short to glance at, complete enough to find a lost place  d) The fuller explanation, exact figures, the story for a picture, reminders
**Answer: d) The fuller explanation, exact figures, the story for a picture, reminders**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why are slides not a script?**

**Answer: a) A slide is a poster — the words read and the words spoken are two different texts**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What can the presenter print to present from?**
a) What you will say, in the order you will say it — short to glance at, complete enough to find a lost place  b) A notes page — slide small at the top, its notes beneath  c) Not just the pictures, but the talk  d) Cover the slides — the notes alone should still make the presentation make sense

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How should notes be written?**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do notes hand over when someone else presents?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the lesson's test for notes?**
