# Hands-on — Speaker Notes Under the Slide

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Write the second text
Take a presentation of three slides (your own or one from class). Write real speaker notes for each — the explanation, one exact figure, and one reminder. Then present to a partner from the notes alone, glancing at the slides only to change them.

## The cover test
Swap notes pages with a partner. Each of you, given only the other's notes (slides covered), must say what the presentation is about, slide by slide. Mark every place the notes failed the test, and fix your own.
