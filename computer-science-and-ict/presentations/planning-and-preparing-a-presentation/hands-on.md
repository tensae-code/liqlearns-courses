# Hands-on — Planning and Preparing a Presentation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Prepare, don't open', 'Prepare a five-slide talk fully on paper: aim sentence, bounded topic, material list, five slide sketches. Only then open the program.')

## Task two
('Rehearse and repair', 'Rehearse your talk aloud with a timer and one listener. Record two stumbles, repair them in the plan, and rehearse those slides again.')
