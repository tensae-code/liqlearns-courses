# Planning and Preparing a Presentation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the five stages in order.**
a) Fix the aim; choose the topic; gather material; plan the slides; rehearse  b) What must the listeners leave able to do or ready to decide?  c) More than the talk needs, so the plan can choose  d) Narrows it
**Answer: a) Fix the aim; choose the topic; gather material; plan the slides; rehearse**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What question fixes the aim?**
a) More than the talk needs, so the plan can choose  b) What must the listeners leave able to do or ready to decide?  c) Narrows it  d) One idea per slide, in the talk's order
**Answer: b) What must the listeners leave able to do or ready to decide?**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How much material is gathered?**
a) Narrows it  b) One idea per slide, in the talk's order  c) More than the talk needs, so the plan can choose  d) On paper — title, picture or chart, few words — before the program opens
**Answer: c) More than the talk needs, so the plan can choose**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does each stage do to the next?**
a) One idea per slide, in the talk's order  b) On paper — title, picture or chart, few words — before the program opens  c) The talk is spoken aloud, timed, and its stumbles repaired in the plan  d) Narrows it
**Answer: d) Narrows it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the slide plan assign?**
a) One idea per slide, in the talk's order  b) On paper — title, picture or chart, few words — before the program opens  c) The talk is spoken aloud, timed, and its stumbles repaired in the plan  d) Where the audience will ask
**Answer: a) One idea per slide, in the talk's order**

a) The talk is spoken aloud, timed, and its stumbles repaired in the plan  b) On paper — title, picture or chart, few words — before the program opens  c) Where the audience will ask  d) A prepared act, not a file

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens in rehearsal?**
a) Where the audience will ask  b) A prepared act, not a file  c) The talk is spoken aloud, timed, and its stumbles repaired in the plan  d) The air-pollution presentation built from the leaflet project

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does rehearsal reveal?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a presentation, in the book's judgement?**
