# Planning and Preparing a Presentation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the five stages in order.**
**Answer: Fix the aim; choose the topic; gather material; plan the slides; rehearse**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What question fixes the aim?**
**Answer: What must the listeners leave able to do or ready to decide?**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How much material is gathered?**
**Answer: More than the talk needs, so the plan can choose**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does each stage do to the next?**
**Answer: Narrows it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the slide plan assign?**
**Answer: One idea per slide, in the talk's order**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a slide first sketched?**
**Answer: On paper — title, picture or chart, few words — before the program opens**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens in rehearsal?**
**Answer: The talk is spoken aloud, timed, and its stumbles repaired in the plan**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does rehearsal reveal?**
**Answer: Where the audience will ask**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a presentation, in the book's judgement?**
**Answer: A prepared act, not a file**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which Grade 8 project is prepared this way?**
**Answer: The air-pollution presentation built from the leaflet project**
