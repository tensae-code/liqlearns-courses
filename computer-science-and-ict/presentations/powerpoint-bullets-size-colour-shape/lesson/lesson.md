# PowerPoint bullets — size, colour, shape

## Do it first

Your list's bullets are small black dots, all alike. On a slide, the bullet itself can speak: bigger, coloured, shaped like an arrow pointing at each idea. Open the bullet's own dialog and restyle the list.

## Bullets and Numbering

Inside a text box, the **Bullets button's drop-down** in the **Paragraph group** opens **Bullets and Numbering**. There you can change:

- the bullet's **size relative to the text**,
- its **colour**,
- its **shape** — a **picture or a character** can serve as the bullet.

[Picture 13719 — a slide list restyled with large coloured arrow bullets]

## Line spacing

The spacing between paragraphs or list items is adjusted through the **Line Spacing button's Line Spacing Options**, which opens the **Indents and Spacing dialog**.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: in Bullets and Numbering a bullet's size against the text, its colour, and its shape — picture or character — can all be changed; line spacing is set in the Indents and Spacing dialog. Use the lesson's own words for the key names.
