# Hands-on — PowerPoint bullets — size, colour, shape

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Where there is no computer laboratory, do the screen work on a teacher's or your own phone, as the course's teaching pattern provides.

## Arrow list
Restyle a four-line list with arrow bullets at 120% size in a colour drawn from your theme. Read it from two metres: do the arrows guide the eye or shout over the words? Adjust once.

## Spacing fix
Take a cramped list and open Line Spacing Options. Widen the spacing until each line breathes, then tighten it too far on purpose — feel where readable lives between the two.
