# Hands-on — Slide backgrounds

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Where there is no computer laboratory, do the screen work on a teacher's or your own phone, as the course's teaching pattern provides.

## One bold, rest calm
Give your title slide a strong background with Close, leaving the rest plain. Then undo the experiment with Apply to All on a calm fill, and say when each scope is right.

## Gradient watch
Apply a gradient fill and check the design rules from the themes lesson: is the contrast still strong at BOTH ends of the gradient? Move the text or change the fill until it is.
