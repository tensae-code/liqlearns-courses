# Lesson plan — Slide backgrounds

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Presentations · **Lesson:** Slide backgrounds
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Presentations in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 9 book, Unit 3 — Application Software. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Format Background fills selected slides — or the whole deck with Apply to All — with a colour, gradient, transparency, texture or picture; Ctrl-click picks several slides at once.
**This lesson's own level:** it is a doing-lesson. It stands on Themes and readable slide design.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Themes and readable slide design. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Open Format Background and name its fills: colours, gradients, transparencies, textures, pictures.
2. Apply a background to the selected slide with Close.
3. Apply it to the whole presentation with Apply to All.
4. Select several slides to design together: click one in the Thumbnail pane, then Ctrl-click the others.
## What you need
A computer with PowerPoint; a presentation with several slides.
