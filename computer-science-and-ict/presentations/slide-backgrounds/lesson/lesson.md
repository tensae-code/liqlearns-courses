# Slide backgrounds

## Do it first

A theme dresses everything the same way. But what if only SOME slides need a new background — the title slide bold, the rest calm? Select your slides first, then paint only them.

## Format Background

Without a theme, **background styles can be added directly**. **Format Background** allows **fill colours, gradients, transparencies, textures or pictures**. Finish with **Close** to apply to the **selected slide**, or **Apply to All** for the **whole presentation**.

[Picture 13718 — the Format Background panel with its fill choices]

## Designing selected slides only

Designs can target **selected slides only**:

1. **Click one slide** in the Thumbnail pane.
2. **Ctrl-click the others** you want with it.
3. Apply the background — only the selected slides change.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: Format Background fills selected slides — or the whole deck with Apply to All — with a colour, gradient, transparency, texture or picture; Ctrl-click picks several slides at once. Use the lesson's own words for the key names.
