The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

One thing today: Format Background fills selected slides — or the whole deck with Apply to All — with a colour, gradient, transparency, texture or picture; Ctrl-click picks several slides at once.

Do it first, in the lesson's order. Say each step out loud as you do it.
