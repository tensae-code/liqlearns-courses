# Hands-on — Project: build and present a slide deck

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Where there is no computer laboratory, do the screen work on a teacher's or your own phone, as the course's teaching pattern provides.

## Specification check
Before presenting, check the tourism deck against its four specification lines one by one — layout, theme, background style, SmartArt conversion. A specification is a promise; check it like one.

## Source line
Add a final slide acknowledging every source your group used — book, website, photograph. Presenting others' work without its sources named is the one fault this project will not forgive.
