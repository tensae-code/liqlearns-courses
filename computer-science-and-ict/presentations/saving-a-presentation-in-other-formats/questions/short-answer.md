# Saving a Presentation in Other Formats — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why save a presentation in other formats?**
**Answer: Because its own format opens only in its own program — other readers need forms they can open**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which command saves in a new format?**
**Answer: Save As**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a PDF saving give the reader?**
**Answer: A document any computer can read and print, with the slides fixed as designed**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does saving as pictures give?**
**Answer: Each slide as an image that can be placed in other work**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does saving as a show do?**
**Answer: The file opens straight into the slide show**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Who should receive a PDF?**
**Answer: A reader who must only read the deck**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Who should receive a slide as a picture?**
**Answer: Someone who needs the slide inside other work**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Who should receive the show format?**
**Answer: An audience, with no editing tools in the way**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is always kept besides the copies sent out?**
**Answer: The original editable deck — the master**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why keep the master?**
**Answer: So the deck can still be changed for next time**
