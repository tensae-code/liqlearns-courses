# Hands-on — Saving a Presentation in Other Formats

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Three forms', 'Save one short deck in three formats — PDF, picture and show — and open each to check it.')

## Task two
('Match the reader', 'Write which format you would send to: a teacher who prints, a friend designing a poster, and an audience — with a reason each.')
