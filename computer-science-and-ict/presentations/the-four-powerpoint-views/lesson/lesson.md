# The four PowerPoint views

## Do it first

The same deck looks four different ways, and each way is for a different job: making, sorting, preparing to speak, showing. Switch through all four now and feel the job change each time.

## Normal view

**Normal view** shows **one slide as it will appear**, plus **thumbnails and an outline** for organising, with **speaker notes added at the bottom**. It is the **creating and editing view**.

## Slide Sorter view

**Slide Sorter** shows **all slides as thumbnails** — for **reordering by drag-and-drop**, **setting timings**, and **hiding slides**. A hidden slide **stays in the file but is not shown**.

## Notes Page and Reading/Slide Show view

**Notes Page** gives **each slide a page of speaker notes, printable for the presenter**. **Reading/Slide Show view** displays the slides **as the audience sees them** — **arrows advance**, and **Esc returns to Normal**.

## Reaching the views

Views are reached from the **status bar buttons** or the **View tab**. (Teacher guide — evidence: the guide's Unit 3 review answer prints this view's name as "Slide shorter view" — its misprint for **Slide Sorter view**; guide Unit 3 answer keys, printed pp. 56–58.)

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: Normal for creating, Slide Sorter for ordering, Notes Page for the presenter's pages, Reading/Slide Show for the audience — reached from the status bar or the View tab. Use the lesson's own words for the key names.
