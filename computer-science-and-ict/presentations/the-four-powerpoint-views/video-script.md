# Video script — The four PowerPoint views

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One thing today: Normal for creating, Slide Sorter for ordering, Notes Page for the presenter's pages, Reading/Slide Show for the audience — reached from the status bar or the View tab.

Do it first, in the lesson's order. Say each step out loud as you do it.

Then do it again — this time without looking.
