# Interactive Slides — Triggers and Action Buttons — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an action button?**
**Answer: A shape on a slide that does something when clicked during the show**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What can a click do?**
**Answer: Jump to another slide, return home, open a link, or play a sound**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which shapes can carry an action?**
**Answer: The ready-made action shapes — and any picture or shape**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does interactivity turn a presentation into?**
**Answer: A stand the visitor drives, not a lecture**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When does the action fire?**
**Answer: When the button is clicked while the show runs**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a trigger?**
**Answer: An animation set to start when a chosen object is clicked**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the book build with triggers?**
**Answer: An interactive game on slides — attracting customers at the tourism fair**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What happens on a wrong choice in the game?**
**Answer: It shakes or vanishes; the right one celebrates**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is a trigger set?**
**Answer: Add the animation, then set it to start on click of the chosen object**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must the audience always know?**
**Answer: What can be clicked — unseen interactivity is only decoration**
