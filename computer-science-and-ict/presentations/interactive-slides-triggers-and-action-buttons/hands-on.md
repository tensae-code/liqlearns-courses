# Hands-on — Interactive Slides — Triggers and Action Buttons

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**A three-slide stand** — Build three slides joined by action buttons: home, forward and back. Test the whole loop in the show.

## Task two
**One-question game** — Make one quiz slide with a trigger: the answer appears only when the question is clicked; a wrong choice shakes, the right one celebrates.
