# Microsoft Publisher — Making Posters and Cards — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is Microsoft Publisher for?**
a) Making publications — posters, cards, invitations and booklets  b) Posters and business cards  c) Its main interface and the File menu  d) The Home and Insert tabs
**Answer: a) Making publications — posters, cards, invitations and booklets**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two things Publisher makes.**
a) Invitations and booklets  b) Posters and business cards  c) Tab by tab, like PowerPoint before it  d) A school-festival publicity leaflet
**Answer: b) Posters and business cards**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two more things Publisher makes.**
a) Its main interface and the File menu  b) The Home and Insert tabs  c) Invitations and booklets  d) The everyday text and object tools
**Answer: c) Invitations and booklets**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What opens the Publisher unit?**
a) Tab by tab, like PowerPoint before it  b) A school-festival publicity leaflet  c) Pictures and extra parts  d) Its main interface and the File menu
**Answer: d) Its main interface and the File menu**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In what way is Publisher taught?**
a) Tab by tab, like PowerPoint before it  b) The Home and Insert tabs  c) The everyday text and object tools  d) Printing and handing out
**Answer: a) Tab by tab, like PowerPoint before it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which tabs does the second Publisher lesson work?**
a) A school-festival publicity leaflet  b) The Home and Insert tabs  c) Pictures and extra parts  d) Making publications — posters, cards, invitations and booklets
**Answer: b) The Home and Insert tabs**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What real job does the second lesson design?**
a) The everyday text and object tools  b) Printing and handing out  c) A school-festival publicity leaflet  d) Posters and business cards
**Answer: c) A school-festival publicity leaflet**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the Home tab hold?**
a) Pictures and extra parts  b) Making publications — posters, cards, invitations and booklets  c) Invitations and booklets  d) The everyday text and object tools
**Answer: d) The everyday text and object tools**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does Insert bring into the leaflet?**
a) Pictures and extra parts  b) Printing and handing out  c) Posters and business cards  d) Its main interface and the File menu
**Answer: a) Pictures and extra parts**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the leaflet ready for at the end?**
a) Making publications — posters, cards, invitations and booklets  b) Printing and handing out  c) Invitations and booklets  d) Tab by tab, like PowerPoint before it
**Answer: b) Printing and handing out**
