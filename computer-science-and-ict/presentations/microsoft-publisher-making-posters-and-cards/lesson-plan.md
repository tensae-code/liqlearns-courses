# Lesson plan — Microsoft Publisher — Making Posters and Cards

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Presentations · **Lesson:** Microsoft Publisher — Making Posters and Cards
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Iraq (2026-11-14) — in the title Presentations on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Iraq's Grade 10 Computer student book, because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Microsoft Publisher is the program the book teaches for publications: posters, business cards, invitations and booklets — pages designed first and read second. The second lesson works the Home and Insert tabs on a real job: designing a school-festival publicity leaflet.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Microsoft Publisher and what it makes** — **Microsoft Publisher** is the program the book teaches for **publications**: **posters, business cards, invitations and booklets** — pages designed first and read second. Its **main interface and File menu** open the unit, in the same tab-by-tab way the book taught PowerPoint just before it.
2. **The Home and Insert tabs — a festival leaflet** — The second lesson works the **Home and Insert tabs** on a real job: **designing a school-festival publicity leaflet**. Home holds the everyday text and object tools; Insert brings in what the leaflet needs — pictures and extra parts — and together they take the leaflet from a blank publication to one ready to print and hand out.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
