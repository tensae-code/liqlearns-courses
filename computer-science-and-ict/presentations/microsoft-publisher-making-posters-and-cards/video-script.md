# Video script — Microsoft Publisher — Making Posters and Cards

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Microsoft Publisher** is the program the book teaches for **publications**: **posters, business cards, invitations and booklets** — pages designed first and read second. Its **main interface and File menu** open the unit, in the same tab-by-tab way the book taught PowerPoint just before it.
The second lesson works the **Home and Insert tabs** on a real job: **designing a school-festival publicity leaflet**. Home holds the everyday text and object tools; Insert brings in what the leaflet needs — pictures and extra parts — and together they take the leaflet from a blank publication to one ready to print and hand out.
