# Hands-on — Microsoft Publisher — Making Posters and Cards

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Design the leaflet
Plan a school-festival leaflet on paper: name the headline, the picture and the three facts it must carry. Then say which tab you would use for each part in Publisher.

## Poster or booklet
Take one school message. Decide whether it belongs on a poster, a card, an invitation or a booklet — and defend the choice by who will hold it.
