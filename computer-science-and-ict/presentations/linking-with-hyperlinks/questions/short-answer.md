# Linking with Hyperlinks — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a hyperlink on a slide?**
**Answer: Text or a picture that, clicked during the show, jumps to a target**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three targets a link can jump to.**
**Answer: Another slide, another file, or a web page**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does linking turn a deck into?**
**Answer: A small network instead of a single road**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When do slide hyperlinks work?**
**Answer: While the show is running, not while editing**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a link made, in order?**
**Answer: Select the text or picture, give Insert Hyperlink, choose the target**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What choices does the target step offer?**
**Answer: Place in this document, existing file, or web address**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a link, in the book's discipline?**
**Answer: A promise — its words must say where it goes**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When must links be tested?**
**Answer: In the show, before the audience arrives**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does a link to a file depend on?**
**Answer: That file staying in its place — and the connection, for a web link**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 12 ICT, Chapter 5 §V**
