# Hands-on — Secure Data Transfer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Guard a journey** — Plan the sending of exam results from a school to a ministry: the five means applied, in order, with the danger each answers.

## Task two
**Spot the careless sending** — Given four sending stories, find the unguarded one(s) and write the repair for each.
