# Secure Data Transfer — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does data cross in transfer?**
**Answer: Networks it does not own — cables, airwaves, others' machines**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the road's three dangers.**
**Answer: Copied by watchers, changed, delayed and lost**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the arithmetic argument?**
**Answer: Years of careful storage lost in three seconds of careless sending**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is protection that ends at the door?**
**Answer: Protection unfinished**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When is data most exposed?**
**Answer: While it moves**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the five securing means.**
**Answer: Encryption in transit, integrity checks, verified endpoints, authorised channels, minimal sending**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do hashes and signatures detect?**
**Answer: A changed message**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do certificates verify?**
**Answer: The receiver is truly the receiver**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What should travel, by minimal sending?**
**Answer: Only the data the journey needs**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name the trilogy's three postures.**
**Answer: Data stored, data used, data moving**
