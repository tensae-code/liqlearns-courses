# Video script — Choosing your CIA priorities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **Choosing your CIA priorities**.

**CIA priorities** — the focus an organisation chooses among confidentiality, integrity and availability, because all three at once can be costly.

**a school's lean** — trustworthy knowledge resources open to the wider student community — integrity and availability first.

**a military lean** — confidentiality and integrity first.

**examination results** — the school case to judge: who may see them, and who may change them.

Now you try. Say each word. Say what it means.
