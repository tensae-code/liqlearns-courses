# Choosing your CIA priorities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is CIA priorities?
a) the focus an organisation chooses among confidentiality, integrity and availability, because all three at once can be costly  b) trustworthy knowledge resources open to the wider student community — integrity and availability first  c) confidentiality and integrity first  d) the school case to judge: who may see them, and who may change them

a) the focus an organisation chooses among confidentiality, integrity and availability, because all three at once can be costly  b) confidentiality and integrity first  c) the school case to judge: who may see them, and who may change them  d) trustworthy knowledge resources open to the wider student community — integrity and availability first

**3.** What is a military lean?
a) trustworthy knowledge resources open to the wider student community — integrity and availability first  b) the school case to judge: who may see them, and who may change them  c) confidentiality and integrity first  d) the focus an organisation chooses among confidentiality, integrity and availability, because all three at once can be costly

**4.** What is examination results?
a) confidentiality and integrity first  b) the school case to judge: who may see them, and who may change them  c) the focus an organisation chooses among confidentiality, integrity and availability, because all three at once can be costly  d) trustworthy knowledge resources open to the wider student community — integrity and availability first

**5.** Why do organisations choose CIA focuses?

**6.** Open, trustworthy resources for all students is the lean of —
