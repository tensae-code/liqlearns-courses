# Choosing your CIA priorities — tf

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** True or false: CIA priorities — the focus an organisation chooses among confidentiality, integrity and availability, because all three at once can be costly.
**Answer: True**

**2.** True or false: a school's lean — trustworthy knowledge resources open to the wider student community — integrity and availability first.
**Answer: True**

**3.** True or false: a military lean is the opposite: it has nothing to do with confidentiality and integrity first.
**Answer: False** — a military lean is about confidentiality and integrity first.
