# Lesson plan — Antivirus software — scan, detect, remove

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Safety, Security and Care · **Lesson:** Antivirus software — scan, detect, remove
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Information and Computer Security (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** antivirus software protects computers from malware — viruses, worms, spyware, and also botnets, rootkits and keyloggers — by functioning to scan, detect and remove viruses from the computer.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the threats lesson of this unit — the learner knows the malware family the antivirus hunts. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Security threats (the Grade 10 growth of Malware — Trojans, viruses, worms, spyware, ransomware). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what antivirus software is designed to do.
2. Name the three jobs in order: scan, detect, remove.
3. Name the extended malware list: botnets, rootkits and keyloggers alongside viruses, worms and spyware.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes detect and remove → detect is the flag, remove is the taking-away; the GIF shows them in that order.
- Thinks antivirus is only about viruses → the fuller list is six kinds; the name is older than the list.
