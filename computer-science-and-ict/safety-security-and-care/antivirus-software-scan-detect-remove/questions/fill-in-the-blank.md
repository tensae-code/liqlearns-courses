# Antivirus software — scan, detect, remove — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1.** The three jobs: scan, ______, remove.
**Answer: detect**

**2.** A ______ secretly records what keys are pressed.
**Answer: keylogger**

**3.** Antivirus software is designed to protect computers from ______.
**Answer: malware**
