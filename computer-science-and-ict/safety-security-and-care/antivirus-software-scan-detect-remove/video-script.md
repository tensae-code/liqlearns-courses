# Video script — Antivirus software — scan, detect, remove

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One program. Three jobs.

SCAN — sweep every file.
DETECT — that one is infected. Flag it.
REMOVE — take it away. Clean.

Viruses. Worms. Spyware.
Botnets. Rootkits. Keyloggers.
The antivirus guards against them all.
