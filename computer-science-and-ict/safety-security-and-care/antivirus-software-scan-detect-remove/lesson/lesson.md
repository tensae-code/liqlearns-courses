## What antivirus software is for

**Antivirus software** is designed to **protect computers from malware** — **viruses, worms and spyware**, and in the fuller list **botnets, rootkits and keyloggers** too. Several example products are pictured in the book as the section's figures — different names, one job.

## The three jobs, in order

The software functions in three moves:

1. **Scan** — sweep the computer's files, looking through them for malware.
2. **Detect** — when a virus or other malware is found, flag it: this file is infected.
3. **Remove** — take the virus away from the computer, so the machine is clean again.

Scan, detect, remove — the order never changes: you cannot remove what you have not detected, and you cannot detect what you have not scanned.
