**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Ethiopia, Afghanistan, Saudi Arabia, Iraq).**

Afghanistan's own way (Grade 12): the final year turns outward — computers in society (e-commerce, communications, even military systems), system software and virus protection, then Excel for the first time and a closing block on the Web, e-mail and browsers (taught through Internet Explorer).

Mauritania's own way (Grade 8): The second year is organised as five applied units of 35 thirty-minute fiches — image processing first (Paint/GIMP/Photoshop, bitmap vs vector), then internet ethics, a full Excel data-processing unit ending in a practical project, web source-checking and responsible downloading, and finally email — each unit closed by evaluation and support fiches with full answer keys at the end.

Saudi Arabia's own way (Grade 3, continuing education): a workbook-style book for the continuing-education (adult/lifelong learning) track — every one of the 16 lessons opens with "I want to learn" objectives and closes with written exercises, and everything is taught on an older Windows desktop (Start menu, "My Computer", Recycle Bin) with Notepad as the only application, worked through step-by-step practicals in the school lab.

Iraq's own way (Grade 10): the preparatory stage turns practical and examinable — a whole unit on computer maintenance (preventive vs corrective, hardware and software, hard-disk management with defragmentation, ScanDisk, partitions and FAT32 vs NTFS), then PowerPoint and Microsoft Publisher taught tab by tab, looping control structures in Scratch, and a final unit pairing HTML web-page building with information security.
