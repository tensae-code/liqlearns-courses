# Hands-on — Privacy and security — rights vs locks

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Rights or locks?
Write six cards — three about rights (see, correct, erase your data) and three about measures (firewall, antivirus, password). A partner reads each aloud; you call 'rights' or 'locks' and defend the call.

## One account, two questions
Pick one account you use. Write two questions about it: one privacy question (what does it hold about me, and can I correct it?) and one security question (what stops a stranger getting in?).
