# Lesson plan — Privacy and security — rights vs locks

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Safety, Security and Care · **Lesson:** Privacy and security — rights vs locks
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Information and Computer Security (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** privacy safeguards people's right to manage their own data; security guards against leaks — privacy is about rights over personal information, security is the measures that stop third parties reaching it unauthorised.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 10 opening lesson of this unit — the learner knows what information security defends. It needs nothing else.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Information security and computer security (the Grade 10 growth in this title). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what privacy safeguards: the right to manage your own data.
2. Say what security guards against: leaks from malware and breaches.
3. Give the definitions of data privacy and data security.
4. Sort new cases into privacy (rights) and security (measures).

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Uses the two words as one → go back to the two questions: rights (privacy) or measures (security)?
- Thinks privacy is 'having secrets' → privacy here is a right you can act on: access, correct, erase.
