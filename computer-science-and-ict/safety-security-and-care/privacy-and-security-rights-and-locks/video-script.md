# Video script — Privacy and security — rights vs locks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two doors. Two different jobs.

Door one: PRIVACY.
Your data. Your rights.
See what they hold about you.
Fix what is wrong. Erase errors.

Door two: SECURITY.
Keep the attackers out.
Measures. Locks. Guards.
No stranger reaches your data.

Rights — privacy.
Locks — security.
Now you can tell them apart.
