# Privacy and security — rights vs locks — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1.** Privacy lets a person ______ what firms hold about them.
**Answer: access**

**2.** Security is measured by how well it stops ______ parties reaching data.
**Answer: unauthorised (third)**

**3.** Correcting or ______ errors in your data is a privacy right.
**Answer: erasing**
