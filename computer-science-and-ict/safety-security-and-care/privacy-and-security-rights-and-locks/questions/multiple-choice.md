# Privacy and security — rights vs locks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Privacy safeguards —
a) people's right to manage their own data  b) the speed of a network  c) the price of computers  d) the size of files
**Answer: a) people's right to manage their own data** — access, correct, erase — the rights.

**2.** Security guards against —
a) homework  b) leaks from malware and breaches  c) rain  d) slow typing
**Answer: b) leaks from malware and breaches** — security is the guarding side.

**3.** Data privacy is —
a) the proper handling, processing, storage and use of personal information, and the individual's rights in it  b) a kind of password  c) a faster computer  d) a printed book
**Answer: a) the proper handling, processing, storage and use of personal information, and the individual's rights in it** — the book's definition.

**4.** Data security is —
a) the right to erase errors  b) the policies, methods and means that secure personal data  c) owning a computer  d) a school rule
**Answer: b) the policies, methods and means that secure personal data** — measures, not rights.

**5.** Asking a firm to correct wrong data it holds about you is a matter of —
a) security  b) privacy  c) neither  d) hardware
**Answer: b) privacy** — correcting errors is one of the named rights.
