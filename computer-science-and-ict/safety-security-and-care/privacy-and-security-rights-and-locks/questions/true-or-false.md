# Privacy and security — rights vs locks — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** Privacy is about who may access personal information.
**Answer: True** — acquired, used, maintained — and who may access it.

**2.** Data privacy and data security are exactly the same thing.
**Answer: False** — rights vs measures — the lesson's whole point.

**3.** A firewall is a security measure.
**Answer: True** — it stops unauthorised third parties reaching data.
