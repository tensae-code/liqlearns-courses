# Encryption — the Caesar and Rail Fence Ciphers — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Readable data; its encrypted unreadable form**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Readable data; its encrypted unreadable form** — as the answer to: What two things does encryption use?
**Answer: False — A method and, usually, a key**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Substitution replaces letters; transposition rearranges their order**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Substitution replaces letters; transposition rearranges their order** — as the answer to: To which families do Caesar and Rail Fence belong?
**Answer: False — Substitution; transposition**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: These two ideas industrialised**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: These two ideas industrialised** — as the answer to: Encrypt HELLO with Caesar key 3.
**Answer: False — KHOOR**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A tiny key space — 25 shifts to try**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A tiny key space — 25 shifts to try** — as the answer to: How does Rail Fence write its message?
**Answer: False — In a zigzag across rails, read off row by row**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Hides the order; keeps the letters themselves**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Hides the order; keeps the letters themselves** — as the answer to: What must real systems hide?
**Answer: False — Both the letters' identities and their order**
