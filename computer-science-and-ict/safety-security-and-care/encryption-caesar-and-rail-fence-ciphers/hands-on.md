# Hands-on — Encryption — the Caesar and Rail Fence Ciphers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work both ciphers** — Encrypt a six-word message by Caesar (your key) and by Rail Fence (two rails); swap with a partner and decrypt both.

## Task two
**Break the Caesar** — Given a Caesar ciphertext, break it by trying shifts; record how many you tried and what clue (a one-letter word, a common letter) confirmed the break.
