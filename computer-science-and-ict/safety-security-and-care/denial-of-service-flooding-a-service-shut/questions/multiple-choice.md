# Denial of Service — flooding a service shut — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is Denial of Service (DoS)?
a) an attack that floods a target with requests so regular users cannot connect  b) sending so many requests that the service cannot answer real users in time  c) a DoS could stop a bank's ATM and CBE Birr services  d) customers disappointed and the bank's reputation damaged, though nothing is stolen
**Answer: a) an attack that floods a target with requests so regular users cannot connect**

**2.** What is flooding?
a) an attack that floods a target with requests so regular users cannot connect  b) a DoS could stop a bank's ATM and CBE Birr services  c) customers disappointed and the bank's reputation damaged, though nothing is stolen  d) sending so many requests that the service cannot answer real users in time
**Answer: d) sending so many requests that the service cannot answer real users in time**

**3.** What is the bank example?
a) sending so many requests that the service cannot answer real users in time  b) customers disappointed and the bank's reputation damaged, though nothing is stolen  c) a DoS could stop a bank's ATM and CBE Birr services  d) an attack that floods a target with requests so regular users cannot connect
**Answer: c) a DoS could stop a bank's ATM and CBE Birr services**

**4.** What is the damage of DoS?
a) a DoS could stop a bank's ATM and CBE Birr services  b) customers disappointed and the bank's reputation damaged, though nothing is stolen  c) an attack that floods a target with requests so regular users cannot connect  d) sending so many requests that the service cannot answer real users in time
**Answer: b) customers disappointed and the bank's reputation damaged, though nothing is stolen**

**5.** In a DoS attack, what is taken from the target?
a) its money  b) its data  c) nothing — the service is denied, not stolen  d) its computers
**Answer: c) nothing — the service is denied, not stolen**

**6.** DoS stands for —
a) Disk operating System  b) Denial of Service  c) Data over Server  d) Delete or Save
**Answer: b) Denial of Service**
