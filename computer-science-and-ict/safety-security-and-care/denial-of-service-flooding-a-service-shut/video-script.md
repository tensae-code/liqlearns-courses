The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **Denial of Service — flooding a service shut**.

**Denial of Service (DoS)** — an attack that floods a target with requests so regular users cannot connect.

**flooding** — sending so many requests that the service cannot answer real users in time.

**the bank example** — a DoS could stop a bank's ATM and CBE Birr services.

**the damage of DoS** — customers disappointed and the bank's reputation damaged, though nothing is stolen.
