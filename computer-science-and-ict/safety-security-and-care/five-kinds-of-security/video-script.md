The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This is **Five kinds of security**.

**information security** — securing information from unauthorised access, modification and deletion.

**application security** — building security features into applications against cyber threats.

**network security** — securing the software and hardware technologies of a network.

**cyber security** — protecting computer systems that communicate over computer networks.
