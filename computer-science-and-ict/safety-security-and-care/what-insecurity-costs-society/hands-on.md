# Hands-on — What insecurity costs — impact on society

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The one-week blackout
Imagine the school network is down for one week. Walk through a school day hour by hour and write what cannot be done at each hour — lessons, records, payments, messages home.

## Cost hunt
Take the five costs. For each one, write one real example from a school or a home: a thing lost, changed, stolen or unreachable.
