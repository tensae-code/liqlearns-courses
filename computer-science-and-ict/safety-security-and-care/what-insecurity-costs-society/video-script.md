# Video script — What insecurity costs — impact on society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A school goes quiet.

The network is down. Files will not open.
Nobody can sign in.

That is the cost of insecurity.
Hardware hurt. Software hurt.
Private information — read by strangers.
Data changed — no longer trusted.
The connection itself — gone.

Risks come two ways.
On purpose — an attack.
By mistake — a careless click.
Both open the same door.

And breaking security is punishable.
Protect the school's computers. It is worth doing.
