# What insecurity costs — impact on society — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** Unintentional misuse can open the same door as an attack.
**Answer: True** — both kinds of misuse are named as risk sources.

**2.** Unsecured school computers can become targets for spam and malware attack.
**Answer: True** — the teacher guide's keyed consequences.

**3.** If data is altered or deleted, the school can still always reach its teaching materials.
**Answer: False** — the guide keys data left altered, deleted or inaccessible — teaching materials included.

**4.** Losing availability means the network itself cannot be reached.
**Answer: True** — availability is the fifth cost.
