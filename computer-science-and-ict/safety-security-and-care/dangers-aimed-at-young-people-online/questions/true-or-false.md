# Dangers aimed at young people online — tf

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** True or false: cyberbullying — ridiculing or humiliating young people online — sharing negative, harmful, false or mean content about them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** True or false: cyber predators — people who exploit younger people online, often pretending to be someone else to gain trust.
**Answer: True**

**3.** True or false: personally identifiable information is the opposite: it has nothing to do with information such as where you live or study, posted publicly when it should stay private.
**Answer: False** — personally identifiable information is about information such as where you live or study, posted publicly when it should stay private.
