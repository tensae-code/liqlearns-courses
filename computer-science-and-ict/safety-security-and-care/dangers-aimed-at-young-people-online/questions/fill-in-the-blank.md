# Dangers aimed at young people online — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is people who exploit younger people online, often pretending to be someone else to gain trust. (Name the term.)
**Answer: cyber predators**

**2.** _____ is information such as where you live or study, posted publicly when it should stay private. (Name the term.)
**Answer: personally identifiable information**

**3.** _____ is the danger of young people sharing identifiable information because they do not yet understand social boundaries. (Name the term.)
**Answer: posting private information**
