# Dangers aimed at young people online — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is cyberbullying?
a) ridiculing or humiliating young people online — sharing negative, harmful, false or mean content about them  b) people who exploit younger people online, often pretending to be someone else to gain trust  c) information such as where you live or study, posted publicly when it should stay private  d) the danger of young people sharing identifiable information because they do not yet understand social boundaries
**Answer: a) ridiculing or humiliating young people online — sharing negative, harmful, false or mean content about them**

**2.** What is cyber predators?
a) ridiculing or humiliating young people online — sharing negative, harmful, false or mean content about them  b) information such as where you live or study, posted publicly when it should stay private  c) the danger of young people sharing identifiable information because they do not yet understand social boundaries  d) people who exploit younger people online, often pretending to be someone else to gain trust
**Answer: d) people who exploit younger people online, often pretending to be someone else to gain trust**

**3.** What is personally identifiable information?
a) people who exploit younger people online, often pretending to be someone else to gain trust  b) the danger of young people sharing identifiable information because they do not yet understand social boundaries  c) information such as where you live or study, posted publicly when it should stay private  d) ridiculing or humiliating young people online — sharing negative, harmful, false or mean content about them
**Answer: c) information such as where you live or study, posted publicly when it should stay private**

**4.** What is posting private information?
a) information such as where you live or study, posted publicly when it should stay private  b) the danger of young people sharing identifiable information because they do not yet understand social boundaries  c) ridiculing or humiliating young people online — sharing negative, harmful, false or mean content about them  d) people who exploit younger people online, often pretending to be someone else to gain trust
**Answer: b) the danger of young people sharing identifiable information because they do not yet understand social boundaries**

**5.** Sharing someone's private information to humiliate them is —
a) a joke, always  b) cyberbullying, and can be criminal  c) phishing  d) a network fault
**Answer: b) cyberbullying, and can be criminal**

**6.** A predator's first tool is —
a) a virus  b) pretending to be someone else to gain trust  c) a rainbow table  d) flooding
**Answer: b) pretending to be someone else to gain trust**
