# Video script — Dangers aimed at young people online

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **Dangers aimed at young people online**.

**cyberbullying** — ridiculing or humiliating young people online — sharing negative, harmful, false or mean content about them.

**cyber predators** — people who exploit younger people online, often pretending to be someone else to gain trust.

**personally identifiable information** — information such as where you live or study, posted publicly when it should stay private.

**posting private information** — the danger of young people sharing identifiable information because they do not yet understand social boundaries.

Now you try. Say each word. Say what it means.
