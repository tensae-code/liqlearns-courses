# Hands-on — Breaking a Code With Frequency Analysis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Break the class cipher** — The teacher encrypts a paragraph with a secret substitution. In teams, tally symbol frequencies on paper, propose the top three matches, and crack it. First team to read a full sentence aloud wins.

## Task two
**Count your language** — Take one page of any book and tally its letters for ten minutes as a class (split the page). Compare your ranking with the published habits of the language.
