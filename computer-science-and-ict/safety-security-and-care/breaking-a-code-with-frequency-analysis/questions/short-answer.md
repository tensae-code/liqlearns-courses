**Answer: The language's most frequent letter, wearing a mask**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does frequency analysis count first?**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Against what is the ranking matched?**
**Answer: The language's known letter habits**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What must a one-letter English word be?**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a doubled symbol narrow?**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What confirms or corrects guesses?**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a habit-preserving cipher carry?**
