# Phishing — fake messages from trusted names — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is a trusted name the message pretends to be — a bank or known service. (Name the term.)
**Answer: a reputable source**

**2.** _____ is sensitive data such as credit-card and login information. (Name the term.)
**Answer: what phishing steals**

**3.** _____ is learning to recognise it. (Name the term.)
**Answer: the defence against phishing**
