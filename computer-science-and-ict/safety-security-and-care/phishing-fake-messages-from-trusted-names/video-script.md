# Video script — Phishing — fake messages from trusted names

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **Phishing — fake messages from trusted names**.

**phishing** — fraudulent communications appearing to come from a reputable source, sent to steal data or install malware.

**a reputable source** — a trusted name the message pretends to be — a bank or known service.

**what phishing steals** — sensitive data such as credit-card and login information.

**the defence against phishing** — learning to recognise it.
