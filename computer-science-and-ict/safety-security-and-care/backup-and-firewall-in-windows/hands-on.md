# Hands-on — Backup and firewall in Windows

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Both routes, timed by steps
On paper, draw the two routes as two click-paths, one box per click, in order. Swap with a partner and follow theirs — any missing click is a found mistake.

## Gatekeeper game
One pupil is the firewall with a written rulebook (allow: teacher's files, school site; block: unknown programs). The other holds up traffic cards; the firewall calls 'through' or 'blocked' by the rules — then say why antivirus is still needed at the gate.
