# Counterfeit Digital Data and Fake Identities — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Counterfeit data — records that lie
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Data made or altered to deceive; A raised marks sheet and a payment screenshot never made (also a changed certificate); The copy is perfect — nothing about it looks fake; The original's keepers — the issuing record, the log, the second source; By checking against the source, not by staring at the copy.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fake identities — persons that are not; and the investigators
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A profile of a person who does not exist — or a stolen person's face and name; A history starting yesterday, and photographs found under other names (also stranger-friends, hurrying requests); Reverse image search; Evidence — suspicion is not a verdict; The verdict with its proof.
