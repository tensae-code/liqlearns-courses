# Counterfeit Digital Data and Fake Identities — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is counterfeit digital data?**
**Answer: Data made or altered to deceive**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two examples.**
**Answer: A raised marks sheet and a payment screenshot never made (also a changed certificate)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is digital counterfeiting dangerous?**
**Answer: The copy is perfect — nothing about it looks fake**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Who are counterfeit data's enemies?**
**Answer: The original's keepers — the issuing record, the log, the second source**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is counterfeit data defeated?**
**Answer: By checking against the source, not by staring at the copy**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a fake identity?**
**Answer: A profile of a person who does not exist — or a stolen person's face and name**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two tells of a fake identity.**
**Answer: A history starting yesterday, and photographs found under other names (also stranger-friends, hurrying requests)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which earlier tool exposes reused photographs?**
**Answer: Reverse image search**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must an investigator's verdict carry?**
**Answer: Evidence — suspicion is not a verdict**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do the classroom investigators present?**
**Answer: The verdict with its proof**
