# Hands-on — Counterfeit Digital Data and Fake Identities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Investigate a case** — Your teacher provides a suspect profile or record. Work as investigators: gather evidence, test against sources, and present a verdict with its proof.

## Task two
**The keeper's design** — Design the record-keeping for a school certificate so a counterfeit is easy to expose: what the office keeps, what the certificate carries, how a checker verifies.
