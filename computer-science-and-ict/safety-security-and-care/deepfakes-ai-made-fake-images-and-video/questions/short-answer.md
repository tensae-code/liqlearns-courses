# Deepfakes — AI-Made Fake Images and Video — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a deepfake?**
**Answer: Media generated or altered by AI so a real person appears to say or do what they did not**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two things are typically manufactured?**
**Answer: The face is mapped and the voice is cloned from samples**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why do deepfakes fool casual viewers?**
**Answer: They are cheap and good — and small, compressed, emotional clips hide their faults**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the second, quieter danger of deepfakes?**
**Answer: Every real clip becomes deniable — 'that could be faked' is always sayable**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why is the defence a routine, not a spot-the-error game?**
**Answer: No single tell is reliable**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the fake want from you first?**
**Answer: Your first emotion — because emotion forwards**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does 'check sideways' mean?**
**Answer: Look for the same event in independent, trustworthy sources, and the shown person's own channel**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What should you examine besides the clip itself?**
**Answer: The source — who posted it, when, and what else they post**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the citizen's rule for damaging clips?**
**Answer: Never forward a damaging clip of a real person you have not verified**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is forwarding itself harmful?**
**Answer: Forwarding is publishing — the harm is done by the forwarders, not only the maker**
