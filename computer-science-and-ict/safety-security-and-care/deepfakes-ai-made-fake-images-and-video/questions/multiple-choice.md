# Deepfakes — AI-Made Fake Images and Video — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a deepfake?**
a) Media generated or altered by AI so a real person appears to say or do what they did not  b) The face is mapped and the voice is cloned from samples  c) They are cheap and good — and small, compressed, emotional clips hide their faults  d) Every real clip becomes deniable — 'that could be faked' is always sayable
**Answer: a) Media generated or altered by AI so a real person appears to say or do what they did not**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two things are typically manufactured?**
a) They are cheap and good — and small, compressed, emotional clips hide their faults  b) The face is mapped and the voice is cloned from samples  c) Every real clip becomes deniable — 'that could be faked' is always sayable  d) No single tell is reliable
