# Deepfakes — AI-Made Fake Images and Video — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a deepfake?" is: Media generated or altered by AI so a real person appears to say or do what they did not**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What two things are typically manufactured?" is: They are cheap and good — and small, compressed, emotional clips hide their faults**
**Answer: False** — The face is mapped and the voice is cloned from samples

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why do deepfakes fool casual viewers?" is: They are cheap and good — and small, compressed, emotional clips hide their faults**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the second, quieter danger of deepfakes?" is: No single tell is reliable**
**Answer: False** — Every real clip becomes deniable — 'that could be faked' is always sayable

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why is the defence a routine, not a spot-the-error game?" is: No single tell is reliable**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the fake want from you first?" is: Look for the same event in independent, trustworthy sources, and the shown person's own channel**
**Answer: False** — Your first emotion — because emotion forwards

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does 'check sideways' mean?" is: Look for the same event in independent, trustworthy sources, and the shown person's own channel**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What should you examine besides the clip itself?" is: Never forward a damaging clip of a real person you have not verified**
**Answer: False** — The source — who posted it, when, and what else they post

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the citizen's rule for damaging clips?" is: Never forward a damaging clip of a real person you have not verified**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why is forwarding itself harmful?" is: Media generated or altered by AI so a real person appears to say or do what they did not**
**Answer: False** — Forwarding is publishing — the harm is done by the forwarders, not only the maker
