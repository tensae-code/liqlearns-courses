# Hands-on — Comparing Cipher Methods

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Build the table', 'Draw the comparison table for shift, substitution and transposition with the three grounds as rows. Fill every cell with a reason, not a tick.')

## Task two
('Break the weakest', 'Take a shift-cipher message and break it by trying keys. Count how many tries it took, and enter that number as evidence in the table's strength cell.')
