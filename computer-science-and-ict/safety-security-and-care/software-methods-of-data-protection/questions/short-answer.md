# Software Methods of Data Protection — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the six software methods.**
**Answer: Anti-malware, firewall software, encryption software, backup software, access control, update systems**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does firewall software rule?**
**Answer: The network's traffic in and out**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does backup software do besides copying?**
**Answer: Restores the data**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is the catalogue described?**
**Answer: The threats list read backwards as shields**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does each method answer?**
**Answer: A named threat family**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does anti-malware know best?**
**Answer: Yesterday's weapons**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does encryption not guard?**
**Answer: The password taped to the screen**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do backups faithfully copy too?**
**Answer: Corrupted data**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does unupdated protection become?**
**Answer: A museum of old dangers**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Inside what weave does software work?**
**Answer: Physical means, legal means and human habits**
