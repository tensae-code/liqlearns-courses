# Shut down, Sleep and Sign Out — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Holding the power button is the proper way to shut down.
**Answer: False** — Start → Power → Shut down is the proper route.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sleep and Shut down are reached by the same Start → Power route.
**Answer: True** — same route, different choice.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A computer can sleep through a whole holiday with no risk.
**Answer: False** — the battery eventually runs down — the guide's caution.
