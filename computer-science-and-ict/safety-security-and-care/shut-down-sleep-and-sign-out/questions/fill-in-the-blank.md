# Shut down, Sleep and Sign Out — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Start button → ______ button → Shut down.
**Answer: Power**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A sleeping computer ______ faster than a shut-down one.
**Answer: restarts**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Sign Out lets someone else ______ in at once.
**Answer: sign**
