# Video script — Shut down, Sleep and Sign Out

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Leaving a computer properly.
Shut down: Start. Power. Shut down. Fully off.
Sleep: Start. Power. Sleep. Looks off — wakes fast.
Sign out: your name, top-right. Sign Out. The next person can begin.

Away long? Shut down. Away briefly? Sleep.
Choose the leaving that fits.
