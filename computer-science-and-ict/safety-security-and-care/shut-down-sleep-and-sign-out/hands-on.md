# Hands-on — Shut down, Sleep and Sign Out

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Three leavings drill
Practise all three leavings in turn: shut down fully, start up, sleep and wake, then sign out and sign back in. A partner checks each route against the book's clicks.

## Choose the leaving
Take five situations — lunch break, end of term, five minutes to fetch a book, a classmate waiting to use the machine, an overnight battery. For each, name the right leaving and defend it with the guide's reasons.
