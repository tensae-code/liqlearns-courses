# Shut down, Sleep and Sign Out

## Do it first

Class is over. The computer is on, your work is saved. How do you leave the machine? Pull the plug? Press the power button and hold? Just walk away? There are three proper leavings, each with its own route and its own moment. Learn all three.

## Sub lesson 1 — Shut down

To shut down in Windows 10, in the book's order: click the **Start button**; click the **Power button**; choose **Shut down**. Follow the GIF click by click — Start, Power, Shut down. The computer turns fully off, the proper way; never by the plug or the held power button.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sleep

**Sleep** is reached by the same route — **Start → Power → Sleep**. The computer **looks off, but restarts faster** when you return: your place is kept, waking is quick.

## Sub lesson 3 — Sign Out — and choosing (teacher guide — evidence)

**Sign Out** leaves your account so someone else can sign in at once: click **the signed-in user name at the top-right of the Start screen** and choose **Sign Out**. The teacher guide adds the judgement the routes need: **shut down when stepping away for a longer time** — and as the occasional restart Windows benefits from; **sleep when stepping away only briefly**, because waking is fast, work resumes where it was left, and power is saved. One caution: do not leave a computer asleep for an extended absence — the battery will eventually run down.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the three routes, and which leaving fits a long absence and which a brief one — with reasons.

## Do It Yourself

1. Shut a computer down by the route, from memory.
2. Sleep and wake a computer, and time how fast it wakes against a full start.
3. Explain Sign Out to a classmate who shares one computer with you.
