# Configuring Network Security — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Network security is not one tool but what?**
**Answer: A set of factors working together — who may enter, what may travel, what is watched, what protects when a defence fails**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why are factors taught before configuration?**
**Answer: Settings chosen without the factors are superstition**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is configuration?**
**Answer: The factors turned into settings, one device at a time**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does password authentication demand?**
**Answer: A named user and a strong password for every entry — no open doors, no shared secrets**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do firewall rules decide?**
**Answer: Which traffic may pass and which is turned away**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How should firewall rules be kept?**
**Answer: Set deliberately and reviewed — not left at the installer's defaults**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does encryption do in the network?**
**Answer: Scrambles traffic and stored data for anyone without the key**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does Grade 12 practise the whole?**
**Answer: As a role-play — configuring a simple network's security end to end**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where do the books teach it?**
**Answer: Grade 11 Bab V C.2 and Grade 12 Bab 4 J — network security configuration**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) A strong password on an open door is what?**
**Answer: Superstition — a setting without its factor**
