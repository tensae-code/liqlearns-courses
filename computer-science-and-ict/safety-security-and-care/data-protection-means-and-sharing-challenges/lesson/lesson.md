# Data Protection Means — and the Challenges of Sharing

## Do it first

Protection is easiest when data sits still and alone. But data's whole value is in being shared — and sharing is where protection sweats. This lesson is that sweating.

## Sub lesson 1 — The protection means — gathered

Jordan gathers the unit's means in one view: **technical** means (encryption, access control, anti-malware, backups — the software lesson's catalogue), **physical** means (the locked room's lesson), **organisational** means (policies, permissions, who may share what with whom — decided before the sharing, in writing), and **legal** means (the laws and agreements that bind the receivers). Data sharing is where all four are spent at once: a file leaves by a channel (technical), from a place (physical), under a permission (organisational), into a duty (legal).

In your mind's eye: the protection means — gathered.

## Sub lesson 2 — The challenges of sharing

Sharing's challenges are structural: **control ends at the border** (the receiver can copy, forward, keep — promises replace mechanisms), **copies multiply** (each share is a new home to guard), **purposes drift** (data shared for care is used for marketing), **partners differ** (their security is now your security), and **revoking is near-impossible** (a shared file cannot be unshared). The lesson's craft is sharing **deliberately**: the minimum data, to named receivers, for a named purpose, for a named time — sharing as a designed act, not a reflex of the send button.

In your mind's eye: the challenges of sharing.
