# Data Protection Means — and the Challenges of Sharing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four means families.**
**Answer: Technical, physical, organisational, legal**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do organisational means decide?**
**Answer: Policies and permissions — who may share what with whom, in writing, beforehand**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do legal means bind?**
**Answer: The receivers — laws and agreements**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is a shared file's journey described?**
**Answer: By a channel, from a place, under a permission, into a duty**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where are all four spent at once?**
**Answer: In data sharing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the five sharing challenges.**
**Answer: Control ends at the border, copies multiply, purposes drift, partners differ, revoking is near-impossible**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What replaces mechanisms after the border?**
**Answer: Promises**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is purpose drift's example?**
**Answer: Data shared for care used for marketing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why do partners matter?**
**Answer: Their security is now your security**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is deliberate sharing's formula?**
**Answer: Minimum data, named receivers, named purpose, named time**
