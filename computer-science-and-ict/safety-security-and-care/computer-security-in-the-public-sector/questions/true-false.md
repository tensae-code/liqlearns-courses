# Computer Security in the Public Sector — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Citizens' records the citizen cannot choose to take elsewhere**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A broken trust between the state and its citizens** — as the answer to: Name three kinds of citizen record named.
**Answer: False — Identity, land and tax records (also health, education)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A broken trust between the state and its citizens**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Who may access records, and how media and passwords are handled** — as the answer to: Why must office rules be the same everywhere?
**Answer: False — A citizen's record travels between all the offices**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Who may access records, and how media and passwords are handled**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Access control, backups, firewalls, antivirus and reviewed logs** — as the answer to: How is staff access given in the measures?
**Answer: False — By duty, not by rank or favour**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Access control, backups, firewalls, antivirus and reviewed logs**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Its learners will staff these offices — duty before desk** — as the answer to: What are the measures' three legs?
**Answer: False — Policy, people and systems**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Its learners will staff these offices — duty before desk**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Citizens' records the citizen cannot choose to take elsewhere** — as the answer to: Which chapter teaches this?
**Answer: False — Laos Grade 10 ICT, Chapter 3 §5**
