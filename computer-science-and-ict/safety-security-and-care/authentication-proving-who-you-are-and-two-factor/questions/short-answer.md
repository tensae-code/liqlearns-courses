# Authentication — Proving Who You Are, and Two-Factor Authentication — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is authentication?**
**Answer: Proving who you are to a system, before it lets you in as that person**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three factors.**
**Answer: Something you know, something you have, something you are**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give an example of each factor.**
**Answer: Password/PIN (know); phone/card (have); fingerprint/face (are)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why ask for more than one kind of proof?**
**Answer: Each factor can fail in its own way — guessed, stolen, spoofed**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is two-factor authentication?**
**Answer: Proofs from two different factors — e.g. password plus a phone code**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which app generates the changing codes in the worksheet?**
**Answer: Google Authenticator**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) In which other app do pupils practise 2FA?**
**Answer: The Ministry of Education app**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why does a changing code defeat replay?**
**Answer: It expires in seconds — it cannot be replayed tomorrow**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Authentication stands guard over which security pillars?**
**Answer: The CIA pillars — it is taught as a security procedure for them**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 10 Information Technology Term 1, Unit 1 Theme 3**
