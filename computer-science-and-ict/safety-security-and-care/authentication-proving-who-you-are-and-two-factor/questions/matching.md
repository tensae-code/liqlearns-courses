# Authentication — Proving Who You Are, and Two-Factor Authentication — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is authentication?** → **Proving who you are to a system, before it lets you in as that person**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three factors.** → **Something you know, something you have, something you are**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give an example of each factor.** → **Password/PIN (know); phone/card (have); fingerprint/face (are)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why ask for more than one kind of proof?** → **Each factor can fail in its own way — guessed, stolen, spoofed**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is two-factor authentication?** → **Proofs from two different factors — e.g. password plus a phone code**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why does a changing code defeat replay?** → **It expires in seconds — it cannot be replayed tomorrow**
