# Exercises to Relieve Computer Strain — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why does computer work strain the eyes?**
a) Holding one distance and blinking too little  b) The neck and shoulders  c) Repeat the same small movements  d) None of it hurts in the first minute
**Answer: a) Holding one distance and blinking too little**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What tightens from holding still?**
a) Repeat the same small movements  b) The neck and shoulders  c) None of it hurts in the first minute  d) In small change during the work — breaks and exercises — or all at once later, as pain
**Answer: b) The neck and shoulders**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do the wrists and fingers do hundreds of times?**
a) None of it hurts in the first minute  b) In small change during the work — breaks and exercises — or all at once later, as pain  c) Repeat the same small movements  d) Look far away and back, roll the eyes gently, and rest them closed
**Answer: c) Repeat the same small movements**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is strain called a trap?**
a) In small change during the work — breaks and exercises — or all at once later, as pain  b) Look far away and back, roll the eyes gently, and rest them closed  c) Turning the head slowly side to side, and rolling the shoulders in circles  d) None of it hurts in the first minute
**Answer: d) None of it hurts in the first minute**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is strain paid for?**
a) In small change during the work — breaks and exercises — or all at once later, as pain  b) Look far away and back, roll the eyes gently, and rest them closed  c) Turning the head slowly side to side, and rolling the shoulders in circles  d) Standing, stretching the arms overhead, and bending gently
**Answer: a) In small change during the work — breaks and exercises — or all at once later, as pain**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the eye exercise?**
a) Turning the head slowly side to side, and rolling the shoulders in circles  b) Look far away and back, roll the eyes gently, and rest them closed  c) Standing, stretching the arms overhead, and bending gently  d) Making fists and opening the fingers wide, rotating the wrists, shaking the hands out
**Answer: b) Look far away and back, roll the eyes gently, and rest them closed**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two neck and shoulder exercises.**
a) Standing, stretching the arms overhead, and bending gently  b) Making fists and opening the fingers wide, rotating the wrists, shaking the hands out  c) Turning the head slowly side to side, and rolling the shoulders in circles  d) A short break with exercises every half hour or so — and stop at once if anything hurts sharply
**Answer: c) Turning the head slowly side to side, and rolling the shoulders in circles**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What relieves the back?**
a) Making fists and opening the fingers wide, rotating the wrists, shaking the hands out  b) A short break with exercises every half hour or so — and stop at once if anything hurts sharply  c) Holding one distance and blinking too little  d) Standing, stretching the arms overhead, and bending gently
**Answer: d) Standing, stretching the arms overhead, and bending gently**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What relieves wrists and fingers?**
a) Making fists and opening the fingers wide, rotating the wrists, shaking the hands out  b) A short break with exercises every half hour or so — and stop at once if anything hurts sharply  c) Holding one distance and blinking too little  d) The neck and shoulders
**Answer: a) Making fists and opening the fingers wide, rotating the wrists, shaking the hands out**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the working rule for breaks?**
a) Holding one distance and blinking too little  b) A short break with exercises every half hour or so — and stop at once if anything hurts sharply  c) The neck and shoulders  d) Repeat the same small movements
**Answer: b) A short break with exercises every half hour or so — and stop at once if anything hurts sharply**
