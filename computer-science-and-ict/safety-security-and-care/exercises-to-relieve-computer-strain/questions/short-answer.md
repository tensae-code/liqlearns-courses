# Exercises to Relieve Computer Strain — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why does computer work strain the eyes?**
**Answer: Holding one distance and blinking too little**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What tightens from holding still?**
**Answer: The neck and shoulders**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do the wrists and fingers do hundreds of times?**
**Answer: Repeat the same small movements**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is strain called a trap?**
**Answer: None of it hurts in the first minute**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is strain paid for?**
**Answer: In small change during the work — breaks and exercises — or all at once later, as pain**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the eye exercise?**
**Answer: Look far away and back, roll the eyes gently, and rest them closed**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two neck and shoulder exercises.**
**Answer: Turning the head slowly side to side, and rolling the shoulders in circles**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What relieves the back?**
**Answer: Standing, stretching the arms overhead, and bending gently**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What relieves wrists and fingers?**
**Answer: Making fists and opening the fingers wide, rotating the wrists, shaking the hands out**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the working rule for breaks?**
**Answer: A short break with exercises every half hour or so — and stop at once if anything hurts sharply**
