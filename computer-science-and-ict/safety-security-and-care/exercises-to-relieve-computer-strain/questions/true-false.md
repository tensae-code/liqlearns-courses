# Exercises to Relieve Computer Strain — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why does computer work strain the eyes?" is: Holding one distance and blinking too little**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What tightens from holding still?" is: Repeat the same small movements**
**Answer: False** — The neck and shoulders

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do the wrists and fingers do hundreds of times?" is: Repeat the same small movements**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why is strain called a trap?" is: In small change during the work — breaks and exercises — or all at once later, as pain**
**Answer: False** — None of it hurts in the first minute

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is strain paid for?" is: In small change during the work — breaks and exercises — or all at once later, as pain**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the eye exercise?" is: Turning the head slowly side to side, and rolling the shoulders in circles**
**Answer: False** — Look far away and back, roll the eyes gently, and rest them closed

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name two neck and shoulder exercises." is: Turning the head slowly side to side, and rolling the shoulders in circles**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What relieves the back?" is: Making fists and opening the fingers wide, rotating the wrists, shaking the hands out**
**Answer: False** — Standing, stretching the arms overhead, and bending gently

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What relieves wrists and fingers?" is: Making fists and opening the fingers wide, rotating the wrists, shaking the hands out**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the working rule for breaks?" is: Holding one distance and blinking too little**
**Answer: False** — A short break with exercises every half hour or so — and stop at once if anything hurts sharply
