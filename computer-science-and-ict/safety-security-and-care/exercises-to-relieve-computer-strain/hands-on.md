# Hands-on — Exercises to Relieve Computer Strain

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run the full set
Do the whole exercise set slowly, in order: eyes, neck, shoulders, back, wrists and fingers. A partner watches with this lesson open and names each part as you do it — then swap. Note which parts of your body felt the tightest.

## Build the break habit
Plan your next two hours of real computer work on paper, marking a short exercise break in every half hour. After the work, report honestly: which breaks did you take, and how did your eyes and shoulders feel compared with a day without breaks?
