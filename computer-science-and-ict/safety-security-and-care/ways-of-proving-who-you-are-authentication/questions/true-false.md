# Ways of Proving Who You Are — Authentication — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Proving identity to a system**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Proving identity to a system** — as the answer to: Name the three families of proof.
**Answer: False — Something you know, something you have, something you are**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Password (know); card or phone (have); fingerprint (are)**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Password (know); card or phone (have); fingerprint (are)** — as the answer to: Why combine two families?
**Answer: False — Each family fails differently — together they cover each other's failures**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The methods' characteristics — what can be forgotten, stolen, copied or shared**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The methods' characteristics — what can be forgotten, stolen, copied or shared** — as the answer to: What three proofs can a login ask in a row?
**Answer: False — Password, phone confirmation, fingerprint (know, have, are)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: It can be changed**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: It can be changed** — as the answer to: What is a fingerprint's weakness when lost?
**Answer: False — It is yours for life — it cannot be changed**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The combination whose failures you have thought through**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The combination whose failures you have thought through** — as the answer to: What question judges secrecy?
**Answer: False — How secret is it really — who else could present it?**
