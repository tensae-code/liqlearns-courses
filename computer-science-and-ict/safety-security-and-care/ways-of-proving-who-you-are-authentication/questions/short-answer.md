# Ways of Proving Who You Are — Authentication — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is authentication?**
**Answer: Proving identity to a system**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three families of proof.**
**Answer: Something you know, something you have, something you are**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give an example of each.**
**Answer: Password (know); card or phone (have); fingerprint (are)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why combine two families?**
**Answer: Each family fails differently — together they cover each other's failures**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do the activity's fiches compare?**
**Answer: The methods' characteristics — what can be forgotten, stolen, copied or shared**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What three proofs can a login ask in a row?**
**Answer: Password, phone confirmation, fingerprint (know, have, are)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when a password is lost?**
**Answer: It can be changed**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is a fingerprint's weakness when lost?**
**Answer: It is yours for life — it cannot be changed**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is strong access?**
**Answer: The combination whose failures you have thought through**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What question judges secrecy?**
**Answer: How secret is it really — who else could present it?**
