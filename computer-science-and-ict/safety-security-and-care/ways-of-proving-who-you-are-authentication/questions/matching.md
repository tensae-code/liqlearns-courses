# Ways of Proving Who You Are — Authentication — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is authentication?** → **Proving identity to a system**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three families of proof.** → **Something you know, something you have, something you are**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give an example of each.** → **Password (know); card or phone (have); fingerprint (are)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why combine two families?** → **Each family fails differently — together they cover each other's failures**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do the activity's fiches compare?** → **The methods' characteristics — what can be forgotten, stolen, copied or shared**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What three proofs can a login ask in a row?** → **Password, phone confirmation, fingerprint (know, have, are)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when a password is lost?** → **It can be changed**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is a fingerprint's weakness when lost?** → **It is yours for life — it cannot be changed**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is strong access?** → **The combination whose failures you have thought through**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What question judges secrecy?** → **How secret is it really — who else could present it?**
