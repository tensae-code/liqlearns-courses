# Hands-on — Ways of Proving Who You Are — Authentication

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Sort the proofs** — Cards name twelve proofs (PIN, badge, face, secret question, key fob, voice…). Sort them into know / have / are, then mark each: can be forgotten? stolen? changed?

## Task two
**Design a login** — Design the login for the school library system: choose two factors, and defend the pair by their failure modes — what each covers if the other fails.
