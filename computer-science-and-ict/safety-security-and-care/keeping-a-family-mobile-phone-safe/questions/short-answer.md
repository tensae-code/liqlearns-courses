# Keeping a Family Mobile Phone Safe — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the investigation's title question?**
**Answer: How safe is my family mobile phone?**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three lock kinds.**
**Answer: PIN, pattern and fingerprint (biometrics)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why do updates matter?**
**Answer: They close the holes the news reports**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Give a permission that betrays its app.**
**Answer: A torch reading contacts (or a game watching location)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where are all the answers found?**
**Answer: In the phone's own settings**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three repairs.**
**Answer: A real lock set, permissions withdrawn, updates run (also app locks, backup)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must exist in a second place?**
**Answer: The photographs — a backup**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What code is never shared with a caller?**
**Answer: The OTP — one-time code**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who asks for OTPs by phone?**
**Answer: The criminal — the bank never asks**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is safety, in this lesson's last line?**
**Answer: A family that has rehearsed**
