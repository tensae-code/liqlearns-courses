# Hands-on — Keeping a Family Mobile Phone Safe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Inspect a phone** — With its owner's permission, inspect one family phone against the lesson's checklist and write the findings as a report with evidence.

## Task two
**The repair visit** — Carry out the agreed repairs and write the family's rehearsal card: the first hour after a loss, step by step, on one page for the fridge.
