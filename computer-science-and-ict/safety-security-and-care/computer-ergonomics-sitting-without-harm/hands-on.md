# Hands-on — Computer ergonomics — sitting without harming your body

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The two-sittings test
Sit at a desk twice: first slumped the way the left-behind picture warns against, then sitting appropriately. Stay one minute in each. Say out loud where each sitting puts strain — back, neck, shoulders, wrists.

## Symptom hunt
Write the five overuse-injury symptoms on five slips of paper: pain, swelling, stiffness, weakness, numbness. A partner reads a short story of a pupil who types all day with bent wrists; hold up the slip for each symptom the story names.

## Picture judge
Draw two pupils at desks — one slumped, one sitting appropriately. Swap drawings with a partner and write under each: appropriate, or not appropriate, and one reason why.
