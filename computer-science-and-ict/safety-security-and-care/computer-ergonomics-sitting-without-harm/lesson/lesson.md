## What ergonomics is — and what bad sitting does

**Computer ergonomics deals with the interaction of users and their bodies with a computer, so as to minimise the health risks of improper sitting.** Users spend many hours a day at computers without knowing the consequences. The consequences of inappropriate seating are named plainly: **muscle contraction, pain, injury and backaches**. Sitting is not neutral — the way a person sits at a computer, and the time spent on it, affects physical appearance and health.

## Overuse injury — the named harm (teacher guide — evidence)

Muscles and tendons become painful under **repetitive movements and awkward postures**. That harm has a name: an **overuse injury**. It typically strikes the **elbow, wrist or hand** of a computer user. Its symptoms are five: **pain, swelling, stiffness of the joints, weakness and numbness**. If your hand goes numb at the keyboard, that is not tiredness to ignore — it is the injury announcing itself.
