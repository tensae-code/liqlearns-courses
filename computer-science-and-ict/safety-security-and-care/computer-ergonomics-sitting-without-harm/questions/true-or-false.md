# Computer ergonomics — sitting without harming your body — true or false

Say true or false for each one.

**1.** Ergonomics aims to minimise the health risks of improper sitting.
**Answer: True.**

**2.** The way we sit has no effect on our body structure or health.
**Answer: False** — sitting and time spent affect physical appearance and health.

**3.** Numbness in the hand can be a symptom of an overuse injury.
**Answer: True.**

**4.** Muscle contraction and backaches can result from inappropriate seating.
**Answer: True.**
