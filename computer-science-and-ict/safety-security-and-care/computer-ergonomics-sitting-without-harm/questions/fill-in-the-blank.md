# Computer ergonomics — sitting without harming your body — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1.** Computer ______ deals with the interaction of users and their bodies with a computer.
**Answer: ergonomics.**

**2.** An injury from repetitive movements and awkward postures is called an ______ injury.
**Answer: overuse.**

**3.** The overuse injury typically strikes the elbow, ______ or hand.
**Answer: wrist.**
