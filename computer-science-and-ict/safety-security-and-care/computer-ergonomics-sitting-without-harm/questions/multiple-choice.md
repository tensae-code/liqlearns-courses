# Computer ergonomics — sitting without harming your body — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Computer ergonomics deals with —
a) making computers faster  b) the interaction of users and their bodies with a computer, to minimise the health risks of improper sitting  c) repairing broken screens  d) writing programs
**Answer: b) the interaction of users and their bodies with a computer, to minimise the health risks of improper sitting.**

**2.** Which of these is a named result of inappropriate seating?
a) A faster computer  b) Backaches  c) Better eyesight  d) A bigger screen
**Answer: b) Backaches** — muscle contraction, pain, injury and backaches are the named results.

**3.** An overuse injury is typically found in a computer user's —
a) knee, ankle and foot  b) elbow, wrist and hand  c) shoulder only  d) eyes only
**Answer: b) elbow, wrist and hand** (teacher guide — evidence).

**4.** Which list gives overuse-injury symptoms?
a) Pain, swelling, stiffness, weakness, numbness  b) Hunger, thirst, sleepiness  c) Speed, accuracy, diligence  d) Input, process, output
**Answer: a) Pain, swelling, stiffness, weakness, numbness.**

**5.** Users often spend many hours a day at computers —
a) knowing every consequence  b) without knowing the consequences  c) standing up  d) only at school
**Answer: b) without knowing the consequences.**

**6.** Overuse injury comes from —
a) one long call  b) repetitive movements and awkward postures  c) a cold room  d) a loud speaker
**Answer: b) repetitive movements and awkward postures.**
