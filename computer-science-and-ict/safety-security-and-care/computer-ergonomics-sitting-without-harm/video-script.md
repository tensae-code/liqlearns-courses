# Video script — Computer ergonomics — sitting without harming your body

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sit down at a computer. Now — check your body.

Shoulders up? Back bent? Neck aching?
That is **bad sitting**. And bad sitting harms you.

**Ergonomics** fits the sitting to the body.
Sit the right way, and the harm stays away.

One injury has a name: **overuse injury**.
Elbow. Wrist. Hand. Pain. Swelling. Stiffness.
Do not wait for it. Sit right from the start.
