# Intercepting attacks — man in the middle and eavesdropping — tf

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** True or false: man in the middle — intercepting communication between people and stealing data from their conversation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** True or false: eavesdropping — intercepting, deleting or modifying data between two devices over unsecured communications.
**Answer: True**

**3.** True or false: sniffing / snooping is the opposite: it has nothing to do with the other names for eavesdropping.
**Answer: False** — sniffing / snooping is about the other names for eavesdropping.
