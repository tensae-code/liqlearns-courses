# Intercepting attacks — man in the middle and eavesdropping — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is man in the middle?
a) intercepting communication between people and stealing data from their conversation  b) intercepting, deleting or modifying data between two devices over unsecured communications  c) the other names for eavesdropping  d) the most common place for a man-in-the-middle attack
**Answer: a) intercepting communication between people and stealing data from their conversation**

**2.** What is eavesdropping?
a) intercepting communication between people and stealing data from their conversation  b) the other names for eavesdropping  c) the most common place for a man-in-the-middle attack  d) intercepting, deleting or modifying data between two devices over unsecured communications
**Answer: d) intercepting, deleting or modifying data between two devices over unsecured communications**

**3.** What is sniffing / snooping?
a) intercepting, deleting or modifying data between two devices over unsecured communications  b) the most common place for a man-in-the-middle attack  c) the other names for eavesdropping  d) intercepting communication between people and stealing data from their conversation
**Answer: c) the other names for eavesdropping**

**4.** What is open Wi-Fi?
a) the other names for eavesdropping  b) the most common place for a man-in-the-middle attack  c) intercepting communication between people and stealing data from their conversation  d) intercepting, deleting or modifying data between two devices over unsecured communications
**Answer: b) the most common place for a man-in-the-middle attack**

**5.** Eavesdropping is hard to spot because —
a) it is very fast  b) a listening device may not affect performance at all  c) it uses no computer  d) it deletes itself
**Answer: b) a listening device may not affect performance at all**

**6.** Sensitive data is most at risk when sent over —
a) an encrypted network  b) an open, unsecured network  c) paper  d) a cable at home
**Answer: b) an open, unsecured network**
