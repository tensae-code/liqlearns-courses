# Intercepting attacks — man in the middle and eavesdropping — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is intercepting, deleting or modifying data between two devices over unsecured communications. (Name the term.)
**Answer: eavesdropping**

**2.** _____ is the other names for eavesdropping. (Name the term.)
**Answer: sniffing / snooping**

**3.** _____ is the most common place for a man-in-the-middle attack. (Name the term.)
**Answer: open Wi-Fi**
