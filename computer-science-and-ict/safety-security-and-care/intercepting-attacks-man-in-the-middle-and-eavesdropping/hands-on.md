# Hands-on — Intercepting attacks — man in the middle and eavesdropping

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Pass the note
Pass a paper note across the room through a classmate who may read it. Then agree a sealed-envelope rule. That difference is interception, felt.

## Network check
List the networks you join in a week. Mark each open or secured, and what you send over it.
