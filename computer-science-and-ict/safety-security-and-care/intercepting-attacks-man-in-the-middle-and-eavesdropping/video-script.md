This is **Intercepting attacks — man in the middle and eavesdropping**.

**man in the middle** — intercepting communication between people and stealing data from their conversation.

**eavesdropping** — intercepting, deleting or modifying data between two devices over unsecured communications.

**sniffing / snooping** — the other names for eavesdropping.

**open Wi-Fi** — the most common place for a man-in-the-middle attack.
