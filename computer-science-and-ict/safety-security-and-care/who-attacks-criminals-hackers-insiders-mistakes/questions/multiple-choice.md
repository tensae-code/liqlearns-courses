# Who attacks — criminals, hackers, insiders, mistakes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is online criminals?
a) attackers who steal and sell sensitive data, or hold systems and information to ransom  b) attackers of varying expertise, often untargeted — testing skills or causing disruption  c) people who use their access to an organisation's data or networks against it  d) damage by people with the best intentions — such as emailing sensitive material to the wrong address
**Answer: a) attackers who steal and sell sensitive data, or hold systems and information to ransom**

**2.** What is hackers?
a) attackers who steal and sell sensitive data, or hold systems and information to ransom  b) people who use their access to an organisation's data or networks against it  c) damage by people with the best intentions — such as emailing sensitive material to the wrong address  d) attackers of varying expertise, often untargeted — testing skills or causing disruption
**Answer: d) attackers of varying expertise, often untargeted — testing skills or causing disruption**

**3.** What is malicious insiders?
a) attackers of varying expertise, often untargeted — testing skills or causing disruption  b) damage by people with the best intentions — such as emailing sensitive material to the wrong address  c) people who use their access to an organisation's data or networks against it  d) attackers who steal and sell sensitive data, or hold systems and information to ransom
**Answer: c) people who use their access to an organisation's data or networks against it**

**4.** What is honest mistakes?
a) people who use their access to an organisation's data or networks against it  b) damage by people with the best intentions — such as emailing sensitive material to the wrong address  c) attackers who steal and sell sensitive data, or hold systems and information to ransom  d) attackers of varying expertise, often untargeted — testing skills or causing disruption
**Answer: b) damage by people with the best intentions — such as emailing sensitive material to the wrong address**

**5.** Stealing data to sell to a competitor, using your own access, makes you —
a) a hacker  b) a malicious insider  c) a victim  d) an auditor
**Answer: b) a malicious insider**

**6.** Which attacker is stopped by care and training rather than locks?
a) the online criminal  b) the honest mistake  c) the ransomware gang  d) none
**Answer: b) the honest mistake**
