# Who attacks — criminals, hackers, insiders, mistakes — tf

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** True or false: online criminals — attackers who steal and sell sensitive data, or hold systems and information to ransom.
**Answer: True**

**2.** True or false: hackers — attackers of varying expertise, often untargeted — testing skills or causing disruption.
**Answer: True**

**3.** True or false: malicious insiders is the opposite: it has nothing to do with people who use their access to an organisation's data or networks against it.
**Answer: False** — malicious insiders is about people who use their access to an organisation's data or networks against it.
