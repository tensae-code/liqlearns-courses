# Who attacks — criminals, hackers, insiders, mistakes — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is attackers of varying expertise, often untargeted — testing skills or causing disruption. (Name the term.)
**Answer: hackers**

**2.** _____ is people who use their access to an organisation's data or networks against it. (Name the term.)
**Answer: malicious insiders**

**3.** _____ is damage by people with the best intentions — such as emailing sensitive material to the wrong address. (Name the term.)
**Answer: honest mistakes**
