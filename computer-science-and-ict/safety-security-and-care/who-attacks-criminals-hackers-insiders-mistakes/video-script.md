The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **Who attacks — criminals, hackers, insiders, mistakes**.

**online criminals** — attackers who steal and sell sensitive data, or hold systems and information to ransom.

**hackers** — attackers of varying expertise, often untargeted — testing skills or causing disruption.

**malicious insiders** — people who use their access to an organisation's data or networks against it.

**honest mistakes** — damage by people with the best intentions — such as emailing sensitive material to the wrong address.

Now you try. Say each word. Say what it means.
