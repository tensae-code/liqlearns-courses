# Hands-on — Natural threats to a computer

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Fire drill walk
Walk the lab's real route: main switch, exit, the person in charge, the extinguisher. Say each step aloud at its place.

## Prevention hunt
Inspect a computer room (or a photo of one): find stored boxes, frayed cords and dust. List what must change.


## The school survey
Do the book's group work as a real survey: where do the school's computers sit (flood? fire? theft?), where do the copies live, how are programs protected? Score each against the three measures and report one improvement.
