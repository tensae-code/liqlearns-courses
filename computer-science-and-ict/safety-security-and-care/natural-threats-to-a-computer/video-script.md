# Video script — Natural threats to a computer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fire in the laboratory. Do this, in order.

One — switch off the main power.
Two — leave the room. People first. Always people first.
Three — tell the person in charge.
Four — call for help.
Five — only the right extinguisher. Never water.

And before any fire: no boxes stored here. No frayed cords. No dust. Dust burns.
