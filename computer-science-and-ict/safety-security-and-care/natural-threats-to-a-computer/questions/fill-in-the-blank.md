# Natural threats to a computer — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is heat or cold outside the moderate range malfunctions or breaks components. (Name the term.)
**Answer: extreme temperature**

**2.** _____ is an electric spike through the power supply that can destroy components such as the motherboard. (Name the term.)
**Answer: a lightning surge**

**3.** _____ is get yourself to safety before rescuing the computer. (Name the term.)
**Answer: the first move in a fire**

**4.** The group's survey asks how the school's computers, copied data and ______ are protected.
**Answer: programs**

**5.** Planning against natural threats belongs to the ______ family of prevention.
**Answer: non-technical**
