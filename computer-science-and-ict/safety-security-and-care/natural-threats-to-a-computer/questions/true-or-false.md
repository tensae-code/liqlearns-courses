# Natural threats to a computer — tf

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** True or false: fire damage — flames damage directly, heat melts components, and smoke damages the CPU fan so the machine overheats.
**Answer: True**

**2.** True or false: extreme temperature — heat or cold outside the moderate range malfunctions or breaks components.
**Answer: True**

**3.** True or false: a lightning surge is the opposite: it has nothing to do with an electric spike through the power supply that can destroy components such as the motherboard.
**Answer: False** — a lightning surge is about an electric spike through the power supply that can destroy components such as the motherboard.

**4.** Storing data safely is a non-technical prevention measure.
**Answer: True** — one of the three.

**5.** Natural disasters can be prevented by good planning.
**Answer: False** — their damage can be reduced — they cannot be prevented.
