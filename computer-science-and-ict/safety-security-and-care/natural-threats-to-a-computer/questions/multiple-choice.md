# Natural threats to a computer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is fire damage?
a) flames damage directly, heat melts components, and smoke damages the CPU fan so the machine overheats  b) heat or cold outside the moderate range malfunctions or breaks components  c) an electric spike through the power supply that can destroy components such as the motherboard  d) get yourself to safety before rescuing the computer
**Answer: a) flames damage directly, heat melts components, and smoke damages the CPU fan so the machine overheats**

**2.** What is extreme temperature?
a) flames damage directly, heat melts components, and smoke damages the CPU fan so the machine overheats  b) an electric spike through the power supply that can destroy components such as the motherboard  c) get yourself to safety before rescuing the computer  d) heat or cold outside the moderate range malfunctions or breaks components
**Answer: d) heat or cold outside the moderate range malfunctions or breaks components**

**3.** What is a lightning surge?
a) heat or cold outside the moderate range malfunctions or breaks components  b) get yourself to safety before rescuing the computer  c) an electric spike through the power supply that can destroy components such as the motherboard  d) flames damage directly, heat melts components, and smoke damages the CPU fan so the machine overheats
**Answer: c) an electric spike through the power supply that can destroy components such as the motherboard**

**4.** What is the first move in a fire?
a) an electric spike through the power supply that can destroy components such as the motherboard  b) get yourself to safety before rescuing the computer  c) flames damage directly, heat melts components, and smoke damages the CPU fan so the machine overheats  d) heat or cold outside the moderate range malfunctions or breaks components
**Answer: b) get yourself to safety before rescuing the computer**

**5.** In a laboratory fire you extinguish only with —
a) water  b) the appropriate fire extinguisher  c) a blanket of paper  d) nothing ever
**Answer: b) the appropriate fire extinguisher** — never water on an electrical fire.

**6.** Dust in a computer room matters because —
a) it looks untidy  b) dust is kindling  c) it slows typing  d) it blocks Wi-Fi
**Answer: b) dust is kindling**
