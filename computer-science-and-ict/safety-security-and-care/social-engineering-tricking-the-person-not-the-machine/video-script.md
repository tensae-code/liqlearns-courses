# Video script — Social engineering — tricking the person, not the machine

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **Social engineering — tricking the person, not the machine**.

**social engineering** — creating a social situation to extract information from the user.

**the book's example** — a call supposedly from the mobile company saying 'Your device is in danger'.

**blind trust** — believing the caller without any verification — the victim's failure.

**verification** — checking who is really asking, for example by calling the company back on its own number.
