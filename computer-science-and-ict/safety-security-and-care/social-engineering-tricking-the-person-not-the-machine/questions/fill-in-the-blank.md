# Social engineering — tricking the person, not the machine — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is a call supposedly from the mobile company saying 'Your device is in danger'. (Name the term.)
**Answer: the book's example**

**2.** _____ is believing the caller without any verification — the victim's failure. (Name the term.)
**Answer: blind trust**

**3.** _____ is checking who is really asking, for example by calling the company back on its own number. (Name the term.)
**Answer: verification**
