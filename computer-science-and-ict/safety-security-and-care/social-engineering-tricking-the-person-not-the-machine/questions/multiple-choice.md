# Social engineering — tricking the person, not the machine — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is social engineering?
a) creating a social situation to extract information from the user  b) a call supposedly from the mobile company saying 'Your device is in danger'  c) believing the caller without any verification — the victim's failure  d) checking who is really asking, for example by calling the company back on its own number
**Answer: a) creating a social situation to extract information from the user**

**2.** What is the book's example?
a) creating a social situation to extract information from the user  b) believing the caller without any verification — the victim's failure  c) checking who is really asking, for example by calling the company back on its own number  d) a call supposedly from the mobile company saying 'Your device is in danger'
**Answer: d) a call supposedly from the mobile company saying 'Your device is in danger'**

**3.** What is blind trust?
a) a call supposedly from the mobile company saying 'Your device is in danger'  b) checking who is really asking, for example by calling the company back on its own number  c) believing the caller without any verification — the victim's failure  d) creating a social situation to extract information from the user
**Answer: c) believing the caller without any verification — the victim's failure**

**4.** What is verification?
a) believing the caller without any verification — the victim's failure  b) checking who is really asking, for example by calling the company back on its own number  c) creating a social situation to extract information from the user  d) a call supposedly from the mobile company saying 'Your device is in danger'
**Answer: b) checking who is really asking, for example by calling the company back on its own number**

**5.** Social engineering attacks —
a) the firewall  b) the person's trust  c) the database  d) the password file
**Answer: b) the person's trust**

**6.** The victim in the book's example failed to —
a) own a phone  b) verify who was calling  c) speak the language  d) have information
