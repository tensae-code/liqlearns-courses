# Password attacks — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is decoding a password by trial and error — time-consuming. (Name the term.)
**Answer: brute force**

**2.** _____ is physically looking over the user's shoulder to steal a password. (Name the term.)
**Answer: shoulder surfing**

**3.** _____ is pre-computed hash values used to find passwords. (Name the term.)
**Answer: a rainbow table**
