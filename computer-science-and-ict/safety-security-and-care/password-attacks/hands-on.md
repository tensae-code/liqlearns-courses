# Hands-on — Password attacks

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Guess my word
A partner picks a password from a 20-word list on paper. Try dictionary-style (list order) then random guessing. Count the tries each way — feel why lists work.

## Shoulder watch
In pairs, type an invented code while your partner tries to read it over your shoulder. Then type shielded. Compare.
