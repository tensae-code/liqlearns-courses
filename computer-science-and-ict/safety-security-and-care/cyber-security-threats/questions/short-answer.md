# Cyber Security Threats — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the six threat families.**
**Answer: Malware, intrusion, theft/espionage, denial of service, deception, insider/physical**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three malware kinds.**
**Answer: Viruses, worms, ransomware**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is denial of service?**
**Answer: Systems drowned in false traffic until real users cannot enter**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is deception's phrase?**
**Answer: The human hacked — phishing and fraud**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) At what does each family aim?**
**Answer: One or more of the CIA pillars**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is an electronic attack?**
**Answer: An attempt or act targeting systems or data through electronic means**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is an assault in the book's usage?**
**Answer: The broader aggression — possibly combining electronic, physical or human means**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the distinction matter?**
**Answer: For response and for law**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name the incident report's four questions.**
**Answer: What was attempted, what was done, by what means, against whom**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) From what two sides is one map read?**
**Answer: The threats and the CIA pillars**
