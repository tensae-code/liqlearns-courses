# Security When the System Fails — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a system failure?**
**Answer: The machine or its software stopping, breaking or working wrongly, with no attacker**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three causes of system failure.**
**Answer: A failed disk, a power cut, and a corrupting program (or worn parts)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which two losses does failure inflict?**
**Answer: Data lost (availability) and data damaged (integrity)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is failure certain in a way attacks are not?**
**Answer: Every disk fails eventually**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) What is the first defence against failure?**
**Answer: Backups — copies kept apart and kept current**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When do backups count?**
**Answer: Only if restoring from them has been practised**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a UPS buy in a power cut?**
**Answer: Time to close the machine's work cleanly**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What turns a bad day into procedure?**
**Answer: A written recovery order — what restarts, in what order, by whom**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is failure a security event?**
**Answer: The data does not care whether a thief or a dead disk took it**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 10 ICT, Chapter 3 §2.4**
