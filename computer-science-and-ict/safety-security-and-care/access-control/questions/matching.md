# Access Control — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is access control?** → **A system letting the right people in and keeping everyone else out**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two questions does it answer, in order?** → **Who are you, and what may you do**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name three proofs of identity it can use.** → **A password, a card, a fingerprint**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do permissions govern?** → **What an identified person may do — read, change, administer**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the rule of least power?** → **Each account gets only the smallest permissions its job requires**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why is extra power an extra risk?** → **A stolen password then steals every power the account holds**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the system log?** → **Who entered and what they did**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the chapter teach access control first?** → **The other defences assume a guarded door**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does a system without access control have instead of users?** → **Visitors, any of whom may do anything**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?** → **Laos Grade 10 ICT, Chapter 3 §2.1**
