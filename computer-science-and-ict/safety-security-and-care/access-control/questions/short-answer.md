# Access Control — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is access control?**
**Answer: A system letting the right people in and keeping everyone else out**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two questions does it answer, in order?**
**Answer: Who are you, and what may you do**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name three proofs of identity it can use.**
**Answer: A password, a card, a fingerprint**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do permissions govern?**
**Answer: What an identified person may do — read, change, administer**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the rule of least power?**
**Answer: Each account gets only the smallest permissions its job requires**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why is extra power an extra risk?**
**Answer: A stolen password then steals every power the account holds**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the system log?**
**Answer: Who entered and what they did**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the chapter teach access control first?**
**Answer: The other defences assume a guarded door**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does a system without access control have instead of users?**
**Answer: Visitors, any of whom may do anything**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 10 ICT, Chapter 3 §2.1**
