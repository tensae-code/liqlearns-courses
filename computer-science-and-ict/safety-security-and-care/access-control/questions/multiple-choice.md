# Access Control — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is access control?**
a) A system letting the right people in and keeping everyone else out  b) Who are you, and what may you do  c) A password, a card, a fingerprint  d) What an identified person may do — read, change, administer
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two questions does it answer, in order?**
a) A password, a card, a fingerprint  b) Who are you, and what may you do  c) What an identified person may do — read, change, administer  d) Each account gets only the smallest permissions its job requires
**Answer: b**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name three proofs of identity it can use.**
a) What an identified person may do — read, change, administer  b) Each account gets only the smallest permissions its job requires  c) A password, a card, a fingerprint  d) A stolen password then steals every power the account holds
**Answer: c**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do permissions govern?**
a) Each account gets only the smallest permissions its job requires  b) A stolen password then steals every power the account holds  c) Who entered and what they did  d) What an identified person may do — read, change, administer
**Answer: d**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the rule of least power?**
a) Each account gets only the smallest permissions its job requires  b) A stolen password then steals every power the account holds  c) Who entered and what they did  d) The other defences assume a guarded door
**Answer: a**
