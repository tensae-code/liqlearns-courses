# Access Control — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A system letting the right people in and keeping everyone else out**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A password, a card, a fingerprint** — as the answer to: What two questions does it answer, in order?
**Answer: False — Who are you, and what may you do**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A password, a card, a fingerprint**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Each account gets only the smallest permissions its job requires** — as the answer to: What do permissions govern?
**Answer: False — What an identified person may do — read, change, administer**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Each account gets only the smallest permissions its job requires**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Who entered and what they did** — as the answer to: Why is extra power an extra risk?
**Answer: False — A stolen password then steals every power the account holds**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Who entered and what they did**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Visitors, any of whom may do anything** — as the answer to: Why does the chapter teach access control first?
**Answer: False — The other defences assume a guarded door**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Visitors, any of whom may do anything**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A system letting the right people in and keeping everyone else out** — as the answer to: Which chapter teaches this?
**Answer: False — Laos Grade 10 ICT, Chapter 3 §2.1**
