# Assessing Risk in Information Exchange — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the media information exchanges through.**
**Answer: Spoken, written, printed and digital**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a risk survey walk through?**
**Answer: What we send, by what medium, and who could intercept it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does risk live, by the survey's law?**
**Answer: In the meeting of the information and the medium**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What can happen to a digital message on its path?**
**Answer: It can be intercepted, copied or forwarded by strangers**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What must be recorded in the survey?**
**Answer: What the class finds — its actual exchanges and risks**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Who does the class interview?**
**Answer: An ICT expert**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where do the interview questions come from?**
**Answer: The survey's gaps**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What are the classification's three levels?**
**Answer: Public, private and secret**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What question does classification answer for each exchange?**
**Answer: How much care this information demands — and so which medium it may take**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the human-chain lesson do with this work?**
**Answer: Defends it in public — the assessment made visible**
