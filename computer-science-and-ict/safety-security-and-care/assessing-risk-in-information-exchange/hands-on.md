# Hands-on — Assessing Risk in Information Exchange

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Run the survey** — Survey your class's or family's information exchanges for one day: list what was sent, its medium, its possible watchers, and its classification.

## Task two
**Interview and classify** — Prepare five questions from your survey's gaps for an ICT expert (or a knowledgeable adult). Then classify ten real pieces of information and choose a safe medium for each.
