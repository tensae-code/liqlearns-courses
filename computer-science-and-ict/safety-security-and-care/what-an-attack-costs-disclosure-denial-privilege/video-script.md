The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **What an attack costs — disclosure, denial, privilege**.

**disclosure of information** — dissemination of information to anyone unauthorised — such as a photographed internal letter posted publicly.

**denial of use** — the intentional degradation or blocking of resources so intended users cannot reach them.

**elevation of privilege** — using a system weakness to gain rights that should not be available.

**keystroke logging** — recording the keys struck on a keyboard — one route to stealing a higher-privilege account.
