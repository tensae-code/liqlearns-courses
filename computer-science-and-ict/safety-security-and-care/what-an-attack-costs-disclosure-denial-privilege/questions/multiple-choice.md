# What an attack costs — disclosure, denial, privilege — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is disclosure of information?
a) dissemination of information to anyone unauthorised — such as a photographed internal letter posted publicly  b) the intentional degradation or blocking of resources so intended users cannot reach them  c) using a system weakness to gain rights that should not be available  d) recording the keys struck on a keyboard — one route to stealing a higher-privilege account
**Answer: a) dissemination of information to anyone unauthorised — such as a photographed internal letter posted publicly**

**2.** What is denial of use?
a) dissemination of information to anyone unauthorised — such as a photographed internal letter posted publicly  b) using a system weakness to gain rights that should not be available  c) recording the keys struck on a keyboard — one route to stealing a higher-privilege account  d) the intentional degradation or blocking of resources so intended users cannot reach them
**Answer: d) the intentional degradation or blocking of resources so intended users cannot reach them**

**3.** What is elevation of privilege?
a) the intentional degradation or blocking of resources so intended users cannot reach them  b) recording the keys struck on a keyboard — one route to stealing a higher-privilege account  c) using a system weakness to gain rights that should not be available  d) dissemination of information to anyone unauthorised — such as a photographed internal letter posted publicly
**Answer: c) using a system weakness to gain rights that should not be available**

**4.** What is keystroke logging?
a) using a system weakness to gain rights that should not be available  b) recording the keys struck on a keyboard — one route to stealing a higher-privilege account  c) dissemination of information to anyone unauthorised — such as a photographed internal letter posted publicly  d) the intentional degradation or blocking of resources so intended users cannot reach them
**Answer: b) recording the keys struck on a keyboard — one route to stealing a higher-privilege account**

**5.** A student inside the record system on a teacher's account is —
a) disclosure  b) elevation of privilege  c) denial of use  d) corruption only
**Answer: b) elevation of privilege**

**6.** Halima's first loss, in triad words, was —
a) availability  b) confidentiality of her account information  c) integrity of the bank  d) nothing
**Answer: b) confidentiality of her account information**
