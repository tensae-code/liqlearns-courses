# What an attack costs — disclosure, denial, privilege — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is the intentional degradation or blocking of resources so intended users cannot reach them. (Name the term.)
**Answer: denial of use**

**2.** _____ is using a system weakness to gain rights that should not be available. (Name the term.)
**Answer: elevation of privilege**

**3.** _____ is recording the keys struck on a keyboard — one route to stealing a higher-privilege account. (Name the term.)
**Answer: keystroke logging**
