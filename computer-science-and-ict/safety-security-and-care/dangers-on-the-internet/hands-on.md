# Hands-on — Dangers on the Internet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — 1.3 Какие есть опасности при работе в Интернете? Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach dangers on the internet to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
