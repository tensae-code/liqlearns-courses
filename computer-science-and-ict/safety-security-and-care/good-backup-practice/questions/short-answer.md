# Good Backup Practice — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What decides whether a backup saves you?**
**Answer: Good practice — whether the copy is there, recent and readable on the bad day**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first rule of good backup practice?**
**Answer: Back up regularly, on a schedule**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why must backup be scheduled, not remembered?**
**Answer: Work made since the last backup cannot be saved by it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does separate mean for a backup?**
**Answer: The copy lives away from the original — another device, another place or the cloud**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why keep more than one copy of important work?**
**Answer: Because a single backup can fail too**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is a backup proved?**
**Answer: By checking it — opening the copy and confirming the files are there and readable**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What should be practised before it is needed?**
**Answer: Restoring — bringing a file back from the backup**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which service does Class VII practise backup on?**
**Answer: Google Drive**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What can one accident do to a backup kept beside the original?**
**Answer: Destroy both the original and the copy together**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What three words summarise good practice?**
**Answer: Regular, separate, and more than one**
