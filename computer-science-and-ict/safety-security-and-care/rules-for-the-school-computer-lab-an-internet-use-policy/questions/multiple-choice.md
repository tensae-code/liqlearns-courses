# Rules for the school computer lab — an Internet-use policy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is educational purposes only?
a) the policy's first rule: computers, network and Internet are for education, not entertainment  b) full name, address, telephone number and other personal information — never on a school computer  c) a spotted security problem goes to the teacher or administrator at once; it is not shown to others  d) capitals, small letters, numbers and special characters, more than 8 characters — and never shared
**Answer: a) the policy's first rule: computers, network and Internet are for education, not entertainment**

**2.** What is never revealed?
a) the policy's first rule: computers, network and Internet are for education, not entertainment  b) a spotted security problem goes to the teacher or administrator at once; it is not shown to others  c) capitals, small letters, numbers and special characters, more than 8 characters — and never shared  d) full name, address, telephone number and other personal information — never on a school computer
**Answer: d) full name, address, telephone number and other personal information — never on a school computer**

**3.** What is report, don't demonstrate?
a) full name, address, telephone number and other personal information — never on a school computer  b) capitals, small letters, numbers and special characters, more than 8 characters — and never shared  c) a spotted security problem goes to the teacher or administrator at once; it is not shown to others  d) the policy's first rule: computers, network and Internet are for education, not entertainment
**Answer: c) a spotted security problem goes to the teacher or administrator at once; it is not shown to others**

**4.** What is strong passwords in the policy?
a) a spotted security problem goes to the teacher or administrator at once; it is not shown to others  b) capitals, small letters, numbers and special characters, more than 8 characters — and never shared  c) the policy's first rule: computers, network and Internet are for education, not entertainment  d) full name, address, telephone number and other personal information — never on a school computer
**Answer: b) capitals, small letters, numbers and special characters, more than 8 characters — and never shared**

**5.** You find a way into a restricted folder on the lab network. The policy says —
a) show your friends  b) notify the teacher or administrator, and do not access it further  c) copy one file only  d) say nothing
**Answer: b) notify the teacher or administrator, and do not access it further**

**6.** Copying Internet work as your own is —
a) research  b) plagiarism, forbidden by the policy  c) allowed if short  d) a password problem
**Answer: b) plagiarism, forbidden by the policy**
