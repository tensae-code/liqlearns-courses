# Rules for the school computer lab — an Internet-use policy — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is full name, address, telephone number and other personal information — never on a school computer. (Name the term.)
**Answer: never revealed**

**2.** _____ is a spotted security problem goes to the teacher or administrator at once; it is not shown to others. (Name the term.)
**Answer: report, don't demonstrate**

**3.** _____ is capitals, small letters, numbers and special characters, more than 8 characters — and never shared. (Name the term.)
**Answer: strong passwords in the policy**
