This is **Rules for the school computer lab — an Internet-use policy**.

**educational purposes only** — the policy's first rule: computers, network and Internet are for education, not entertainment.

**never revealed** — full name, address, telephone number and other personal information — never on a school computer.

**report, don't demonstrate** — a spotted security problem goes to the teacher or administrator at once; it is not shown to others.

**strong passwords in the policy** — capitals, small letters, numbers and special characters, more than 8 characters — and never shared.
