# Formulating a Cyber Security Policy — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The policy — rules with reasons
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A written agreement of protection rules, roles and responses; What is protected, the rules, the roles, and the response; Before the panic — in advance; Its reason; A policy whose reasons are known is followed unwatched.
