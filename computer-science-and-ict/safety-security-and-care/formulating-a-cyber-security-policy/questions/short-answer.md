# Formulating a Cyber Security Policy — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a cyber security policy?**
**Answer: A written agreement of protection rules, roles and responses**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the policy's four contents.**
**Answer: What is protected, the rules, the roles, and the response**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When must the response be written?**
**Answer: Before the panic — in advance**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What stands beside each clause?**
**Answer: Its reason**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why do reasons matter?**
**Answer: A policy whose reasons are known is followed unwatched**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is cyber awareness, in the final session?**
**Answer: Carrying the policy's teaching to the school and families**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two awareness roads.**
**Answer: Posters and a presentation (also translation for younger children)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does awareness multiply the policy into?**
**Answer: A school's habits**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who wrote this policy, and why does that bind?**
**Answer: The class itself — citizens obey their own laws**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the course's closing aim here?**
**Answer: Citizens who govern their corner of the digital world — and teach it**
