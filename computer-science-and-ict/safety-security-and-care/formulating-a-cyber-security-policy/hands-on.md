# Hands-on — Formulating a Cyber Security Policy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Write the policy** — In teams, formulate your class cyber security policy: assets, five clauses with reasons, roles, and the response plan. Sign it as its citizens.

## Task two
**Carry it public** — Make the awareness piece — poster or presentation — that teaches your policy's most important clause to younger students.
