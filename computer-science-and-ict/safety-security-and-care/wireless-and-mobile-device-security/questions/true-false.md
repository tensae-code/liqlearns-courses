# Wireless and Mobile Device Security — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It travels through the air, beyond the room — anyone in reach can listen**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: An invitation — any listener may read unencrypted traffic and join** — as the answer to: What does wireless security stand on?
**Answer: False — Encryption and the network password**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: An invitation — any listener may read unencrypted traffic and join**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A lost unlocked phone is every account lost at once** — as the answer to: How can free public wireless be a trap?
**Answer: False — A copied network name harvesting whoever joins**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A lost unlocked phone is every account lost at once**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: From the proper store, with their permissions read** — as the answer to: Name three locks a phone can use.
**Answer: False — A PIN, a pattern, or a fingerprint**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: From the proper store, with their permissions read**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A lost object, not a lost life** — as the answer to: What should be ready before a phone is lost?
**Answer: False — Backups and a find/wipe service**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A lost object, not a lost life**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It travels through the air, beyond the room — anyone in reach can listen** — as the answer to: Which chapter teaches this?
**Answer: False — Laos Grade 12 ICT, Chapter 2 §II.9**
