# Wireless and Mobile Device Security — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why does wireless traffic need special care?**
**Answer: It travels through the air, beyond the room — anyone in reach can listen**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does wireless security stand on?**
**Answer: Encryption and the network password**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is an open wireless network, in effect?**
**Answer: An invitation — any listener may read unencrypted traffic and join**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How can free public wireless be a trap?**
**Answer: A copied network name harvesting whoever joins**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a phone's lock its first security?**
**Answer: A lost unlocked phone is every account lost at once**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three locks a phone can use.**
**Answer: A PIN, a pattern, or a fingerprint**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What care is taken with apps?**
**Answer: From the proper store, with their permissions read**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What should be ready before a phone is lost?**
**Answer: Backups and a find/wipe service**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a stolen phone, when prepared?**
**Answer: A lost object, not a lost life**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 12 ICT, Chapter 2 §II.9**
