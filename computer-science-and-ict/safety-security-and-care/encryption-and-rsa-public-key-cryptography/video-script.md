# Video script — Encryption and RSA Public-Key Cryptography

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A secret on paper can be read by everyone it passes.
Encryption scrambles it. Only the right key can unscramble it.
RSA gives you two keys.
The public key locks — give it to the whole world.
The private key unlocks — keep it in your pocket.
Multiplying primes is easy. Splitting the product is not. That is the lock.
