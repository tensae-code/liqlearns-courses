Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (China, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked for the slug and for near matches before building. It sits in the Safety, Security and Care title (safety-security-and-care/), following the book's own order.

China's own way: the security chapter of Compulsory 2 does not stop at advice — after firewalls and passwords it has the student implement RSA in Python, learning public-key cryptography by making a small working copy of it.

The two Teacher's Guides in China's set (to Compulsory 1 and to Compulsory 2) are record-only and are cited nowhere in this course: their library records are swapped against the student books (mismatched pairings) and both PDFs are partial (front portions only).
