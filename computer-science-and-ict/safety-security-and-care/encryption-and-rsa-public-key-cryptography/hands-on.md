# Hands-on — Encryption and RSA Public-Key Cryptography

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tiny RSA by hand
With the teacher's small primes (for example 5 and 11, product 55), follow the worked locking of one small number-message and its unlocking. Write each step and mark exactly where the private knowledge — the primes — was used.

## Publish the padlock
Write a poster for the classroom wall: a drawing of your public key being handed to everyone, your private key in your pocket, and one sentence explaining why the crowd cannot open your mail.
