# Encryption and RSA Public-Key Cryptography — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Readable text before encryption is called ____.**
**Answer: plaintext** — Plaintext in, ciphertext out.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Encryption that uses one shared key for locking and unlocking is ____ encryption.**
**Answer: symmetric** — One key, shared — hence its hard question.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) RSA builds its keys from two large ____ numbers.**
**Answer: prime** — Multiplied for the public key; kept as the private knowledge.
