# Hands-on — Asymmetric Encryption — Two Keys

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Padlock post
In pairs: each person 'publishes' a padlock (a drawn open padlock labelled with their name) on the class board and keeps a private 'key card' hidden. Send a message: write it, 'lock' it with the receiver's padlock by writing their padlock-name on the envelope, and pass it through three classmates. Only the receiver opens it with the key card. Note what the three carriers learned: nothing.

## Which key? drill
Deal six situation cards (write to Ana; Ana reads it; sign as yourself — next lesson; a shop sends you its lock…) and sort, for the two encryption ones, which key is used and whose it is. Speed matters less than direction: get every direction right.
