# Video script — Asymmetric Encryption — Two Keys

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One key was not enough. So now every person has two.

The public key: give it to everyone. It locks messages written to you.

The private key: keep it forever. It is the only key that unlocks them.

A stranger locks a message with your public key. Only you can open it.

No shared secret ever had to travel. The old problem is gone.

Two keys are slow — so they deliver one fast symmetric key, then step aside.
