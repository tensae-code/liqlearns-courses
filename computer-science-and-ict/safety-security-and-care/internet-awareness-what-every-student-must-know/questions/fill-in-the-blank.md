# Internet awareness — what every student must know — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is what predators do after contact — winning trust to lure a pupil into false security. (Name the term.)
**Answer: grooming**

**2.** _____ is a download judged legal and free of malicious code before it is opened. (Name the term.)
**Answer: a safe download**

**3.** _____ is where you live and where you attend school — never revealed online. (Name the term.)
**Answer: never shared**
