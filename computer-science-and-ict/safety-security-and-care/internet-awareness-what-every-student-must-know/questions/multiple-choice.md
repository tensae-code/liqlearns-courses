# Internet awareness — what every student must know — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is electronic footprints?
a) the traces even messages to known friends leave, which others can misuse  b) what predators do after contact — winning trust to lure a pupil into false security  c) a download judged legal and free of malicious code before it is opened  d) where you live and where you attend school — never revealed online
**Answer: a) the traces even messages to known friends leave, which others can misuse**

**2.** What is grooming?
a) the traces even messages to known friends leave, which others can misuse  b) a download judged legal and free of malicious code before it is opened  c) where you live and where you attend school — never revealed online  d) what predators do after contact — winning trust to lure a pupil into false security
**Answer: d) what predators do after contact — winning trust to lure a pupil into false security**

**3.** What is a safe download?
a) what predators do after contact — winning trust to lure a pupil into false security  b) where you live and where you attend school — never revealed online  c) a download judged legal and free of malicious code before it is opened  d) the traces even messages to known friends leave, which others can misuse
**Answer: c) a download judged legal and free of malicious code before it is opened**

**4.** What is never shared?
a) a download judged legal and free of malicious code before it is opened  b) where you live and where you attend school — never revealed online  c) the traces even messages to known friends leave, which others can misuse  d) what predators do after contact — winning trust to lure a pupil into false security
**Answer: b) where you live and where you attend school — never revealed online**

**5.** A chat contact who says they are a pupil —
a) is always a pupil  b) may not be who they appear to be  c) cannot be a predator  d) is checked by the site
**Answer: b) may not be who they appear to be**

**6.** In a dangerous online situation, a pupil should —
a) handle it alone  b) get adult help  c) delete the account silently  d) reply bravely
**Answer: b) get adult help**
