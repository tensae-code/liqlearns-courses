# Video script — Internet awareness — what every student must know

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Internet is a big library. It is a telephone to the world. It is a place to make things.
But listen. Not everything in it is true. Not everyone in it is who they say.

Never tell where you live. Never tell where you study.
A photo you send can travel without you. It can be changed without you.
A message leaves footprints, even to a friend.

A stranger who is too friendly is a warning, not a friend.
Before you download, ask: is it allowed? Is it clean?
And when something feels dangerous — tell an adult. Always tell an adult.
