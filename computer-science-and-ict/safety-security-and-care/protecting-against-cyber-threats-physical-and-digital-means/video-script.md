# Video script — Protecting Against Cyber Threats — Physical and Digital Means

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Physical protection** guards data by guarding its **carriers and rooms**: locks and guards for server rooms, controlled entry (cards, logs), safes and locked cabinets for media and backups, protection against fire, flood and power failure (alarms, extinguishers, UPS), and care with the machine's body — cables, screens facing walls, devices not left open and logged-in. A stolen, burned or drowned computer needs no hacker: the physical means answer the threats that never touch a network.
**Digital protection** guards the data in its own realm: **passwords and access control** (only the authorised reach it), **encryption** (reached data reads as noise), **anti-malware and updates** (the known weapons blunted), **firewalls** (the network's gate), **backups** (the data survives its own destruction), and **monitoring** (the watch kept). Jordan teaches the two materials as one lesson because real protection is their weave: the encrypted disk in the locked room, the backed-up file behind the guarded account — a threat stopped by whichever material it meets first.
