# Hands-on — Protecting Against Cyber Threats — Physical and Digital Means

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Weave a defence** — For a school office's records, design the protection in two columns — physical and digital — with no pillar of the CIA left to one column alone.

## Task two
**Walk like a threat** — Inspect one real room (your classroom) as three threats in turn — thief, fire, hacker — and list what each meets.
