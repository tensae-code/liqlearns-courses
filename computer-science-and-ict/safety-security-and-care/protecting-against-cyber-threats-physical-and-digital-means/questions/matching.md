# Protecting Against Cyber Threats — Physical and Digital Means — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does physical protection guard?** → **Data's carriers and rooms**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name four physical means.** → **Locks/guards, controlled entry, safes for media/backups, fire/flood/power protection (or screen and device care)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What guards against power failure?** → **A UPS (with alarms and extinguishers for fire)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which threats need no hacker?** → **Theft, fire, flood — the physical ones**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How should a screen face?** → **Toward the wall — not toward passers-by**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the six digital means.** → **Passwords/access control, encryption, anti-malware and updates, firewalls, backups, monitoring**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does encryption do for reached data?** → **Makes it read as noise**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the firewall called?** → **The network's gate**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do backups let data survive?** → **Its own destruction**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How do the two materials work together?** → **As one weave — a threat stopped by whichever it meets first**
