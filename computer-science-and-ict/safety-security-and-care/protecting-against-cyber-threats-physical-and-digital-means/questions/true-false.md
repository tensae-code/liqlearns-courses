# Protecting Against Cyber Threats — Physical and Digital Means — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Data's carriers and rooms**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Data's carriers and rooms** — as the answer to: Name four physical means.
**Answer: False — Locks/guards, controlled entry, safes for media/backups, fire/flood/power protection (or screen and device care)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A UPS (with alarms and extinguishers for fire)**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A UPS (with alarms and extinguishers for fire)** — as the answer to: Which threats need no hacker?
**Answer: False — Theft, fire, flood — the physical ones**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Toward the wall — not toward passers-by**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Toward the wall — not toward passers-by** — as the answer to: Name the six digital means.
**Answer: False — Passwords/access control, encryption, anti-malware and updates, firewalls, backups, monitoring**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Makes it read as noise**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Makes it read as noise** — as the answer to: What is the firewall called?
**Answer: False — The network's gate**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Its own destruction**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Its own destruction** — as the answer to: How do the two materials work together?
**Answer: False — As one weave — a threat stopped by whichever it meets first**
