# Protecting Against Cyber Threats — Physical and Digital Means — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does physical protection guard?**
**Answer: Data's carriers and rooms**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name four physical means.**
**Answer: Locks/guards, controlled entry, safes for media/backups, fire/flood/power protection (or screen and device care)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What guards against power failure?**
**Answer: A UPS (with alarms and extinguishers for fire)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which threats need no hacker?**
**Answer: Theft, fire, flood — the physical ones**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How should a screen face?**
**Answer: Toward the wall — not toward passers-by**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the six digital means.**
**Answer: Passwords/access control, encryption, anti-malware and updates, firewalls, backups, monitoring**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does encryption do for reached data?**
**Answer: Makes it read as noise**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the firewall called?**
**Answer: The network's gate**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do backups let data survive?**
**Answer: Its own destruction**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How do the two materials work together?**
**Answer: As one weave — a threat stopped by whichever it meets first**
