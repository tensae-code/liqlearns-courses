# Spam — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is spam?**
**Answer: Unwanted mail sent in bulk to addresses that never asked for it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Give three kinds of spam content.**
**Answer: Worthless-goods advertisements, false prizes, and chain pleas (also forged letters)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does spam exist economically?**
**Answer: Sending costs almost nothing — a few buyers from a million pay for it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Who pays spam's real costs?**
**Answer: Every inbox — in time, storage, network load and buried true mail**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What dangerous things does spam carry?**
**Answer: Malware (in attachments) and phishing (in its links)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a spam filter do?**
**Answer: Learns the flood's shapes and files spam apart from real mail**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the user's spam discipline.**
**Answer: Never reply, never buy, never click, never open attachments, guard the address**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is replying the worst response?**
**Answer: It proves a person reads that address, which is then sold on as live**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is spam taught in the privacy chapter?**
**Answer: The flood begins where addresses are given away**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 10 ICT, Chapter 3 §4.4**
