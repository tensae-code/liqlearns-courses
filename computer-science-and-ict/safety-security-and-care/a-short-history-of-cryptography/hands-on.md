# Hands-on — A Short History of Cryptography

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Cipher timeline** — Build a timeline with five stops: hidden messages, Caesar, frequency analysis, Enigma, computer keys. At each stop, write who was winning — makers or breakers.

## Task two
**Then and now** — Take one modern secret (a bank card payment). Write its journey through the history's pattern: what locks it, what could break it, what answers the break?
