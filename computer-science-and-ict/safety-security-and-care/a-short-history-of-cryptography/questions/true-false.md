# A Short History of Cryptography — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Hide it; substitute or transpose its letters**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Hide it; substitute or transpose its letters** — as the answer to: What is Caesar's shift?
**Answer: False — Each letter moves a fixed number of places in the alphabet**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Frequency analysis — a language's letter habits**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Frequency analysis — a language's letter habits** — as the answer to: How did cipher-makers answer?
**Answer: False — Polyalphabetic systems that shift by a changing key**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Enquête 5, Fiche 3 — Petite histoire de la cryptographie**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Enquête 5, Fiche 3 — Petite histoire de la cryptographie** — as the answer to: What did rotor machines like Enigma do?

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Computers**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Computers** — as the answer to: What are modern keys?
**Answer: False — Numbers too long to guess**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A stronger cipher, a cleverer break, a stronger cipher again**
