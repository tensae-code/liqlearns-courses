# A Short History of Cryptography — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name two ancient ways to protect a message.**
**Answer: Hide it; substitute or transpose its letters**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is Caesar's shift?**
**Answer: Each letter moves a fixed number of places in the alphabet**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What betrayed simple substitution?**
**Answer: Frequency analysis — a language's letter habits**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How did cipher-makers answer?**
**Answer: Polyalphabetic systems that shift by a changing key**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does this history appear?**
**Answer: Enquête 5, Fiche 3 — Petite histoire de la cryptographie**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What did rotor machines like Enigma do?**
**Answer: Multiplied the shifts beyond hand-breaking**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What broke machine ciphers?**
**Answer: Computers**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What are modern keys?**
**Answer: Numbers too long to guess**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What pattern never changes in the history?**
**Answer: A stronger cipher, a cleverer break, a stronger cipher again**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does today's encryption guard?**
**Answer: Everything from messages to money**
