# Encryption — Keeping Messages Secret — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is encryption?" is: Turning a readable message into a scrambled ciphertext that reveals nothing without the secret**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What are plaintext and ciphertext?" is: A method — the scrambling rule — and a key, the secret value**
**Answer: False** — The readable message, and its scrambled form

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What two things does encryption work with?" is: A method — the scrambling rule — and a key, the secret value**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is decryption?" is: Yes — safety rests on the key's secrecy, not on hiding the method**
**Answer: False** — The journey back from ciphertext to plaintext, possible only with the key

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "May the method be publicly known?" is: Yes — safety rests on the key's secrecy, not on hiding the method**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What ground does encryption let messages cross?" is: A weak method or guessable key falls to patient trying; the key must itself reach the receiver safely (also: content is protected, not the message's existence)**
**Answer: False** — Untrusted ground — networks anyone can listen to

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name two honest limits of encryption?" is: A weak method or guessable key falls to patient trying; the key must itself reach the receiver safely (also: content is protected, not the message's existence)**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What can watchers still see despite encryption?" is: Through Python — pupils write a first encryption program, in the Grade 8 Term 2 unit**
**Answer: False** — Who wrote to whom, and when

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How does Egypt introduce encryption?" is: Through Python — pupils write a first encryption program, in the Grade 8 Term 2 unit**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does making a cipher teach that reading about one does not?" is: Turning a readable message into a scrambled ciphertext that reveals nothing without the secret**
**Answer: False** — Its limits — learned honestly by building it
