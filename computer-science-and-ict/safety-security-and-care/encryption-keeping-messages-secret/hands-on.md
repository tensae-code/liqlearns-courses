# Hands-on — Encryption — Keeping Messages Secret

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Key and method', 'Invent a simple paper cipher with a named method and a key. Encrypt a short message, swap ciphertext and key separately with a partner, and decrypt theirs.')

## Task two
('Limit list', "For your paper cipher, write its three limits: how a patient attacker attacks it, how the key must travel, and what a watcher still learns.")
