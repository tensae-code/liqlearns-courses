# Handling portable devices safely — signal, texts and landlines

## Do it first

Name every wireless switch a phone has. Now say honestly: which of them are switched on right now, doing nothing? Rules 4–7 begin exactly there.

## Rules 4–7

**Rule 4 — Turn off cellular data and wireless.** Turn off the phone's **cellular data, wireless options and Bluetooth when not in use**; and before sleeping, turn off all wireless transmitting devices — apps running in the background keep communicating.

**Rule 5 — Avoid calls where signal reception is low.** A device **raises its transmission power** to stay connected to the tower when reception is poor, and phones continuously signal while travelling in a vehicle — so avoid poor-signal places, and calls while travelling.

**Rule 6 — Fewer calls, more texts.** Texting **keeps the phone away from the body** and **transmits for a shorter time**, making it safer than long calls.

**Rule 7 — Use landline telephones.** Landline calls at office and home are **the safest way** to reduce mobile and cordless phone use for long conversations.
