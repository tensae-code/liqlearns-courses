# Video script — Handling portable devices safely — signal, texts and landlines

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four more rules for the phone in your hand.

**Rule four.** Data off. Wireless off. Bluetooth off — when you are not using them.
**Rule five.** Weak signal? Do not call. The phone must shout to reach the tower.
**Rule six.** Text, do not talk. A text is short — and the phone stays away from your body.
**Rule seven.** A long talk? Use the **landline**.

Signal. Texts. Landlines. That is rules four to seven.
