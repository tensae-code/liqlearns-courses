# Hands-on — Handling portable devices safely — signal, texts and landlines

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Toggle sweep
On a real phone (or a drawn control panel), find and name every wireless switch: cellular data, wireless, Bluetooth. Practise the before-sleep sweep: all off, in the book's order.

## Call or text?
A partner gives you five messages to send — 'I am outside', a long family story, 'call the shop', directions to a house, 'happy birthday'. For each, choose call or text, and say which rule decided you.
