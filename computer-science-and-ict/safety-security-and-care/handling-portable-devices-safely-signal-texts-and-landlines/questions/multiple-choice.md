# Handling portable devices safely — signal, texts and landlines — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Rule 4 says to turn off —
a) the phone's screen only  b) cellular data, wireless options and Bluetooth when not in use  c) the landline  d) the alarm clock
**Answer: b) cellular data, wireless options and Bluetooth when not in use.**

**2.** Where reception is poor, a device —
a) switches itself off  b) raises its transmission power to stay connected to the tower  c) uses less power  d) sends texts only
**Answer: b) raises its transmission power to stay connected to the tower.**

**3.** Texting is safer than long calls because it —
a) is louder  b) keeps the phone away from the body and transmits for a shorter time  c) uses the landline  d) needs no signal
**Answer: b) keeps the phone away from the body and transmits for a shorter time.**

**4.** For long conversations at home or office, the safest choice is —
a) a mobile held to the ear  b) a cordless phone  c) a landline telephone  d) shouting
**Answer: c) a landline telephone.**

**5.** Before sleeping, Rule 4 says to turn off —
a) only Bluetooth  b) all wireless transmitting devices  c) the lights only  d) nothing
**Answer: b) all wireless transmitting devices** — background apps keep communicating.
