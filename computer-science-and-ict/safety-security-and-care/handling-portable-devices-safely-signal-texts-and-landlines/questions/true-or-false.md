# Handling portable devices safely — signal, texts and landlines — true or false

Say true or false for each one.

**1.** Phones continuously signal while travelling in a vehicle.
**Answer: True.**

**2.** A low-signal place is a good place for a long call.
**Answer: False** — Rule 5 says avoid calls where reception is low.

**3.** Apps running in the background stop communicating when you sleep.
**Answer: False** — they keep communicating; that is why the switches go off.
