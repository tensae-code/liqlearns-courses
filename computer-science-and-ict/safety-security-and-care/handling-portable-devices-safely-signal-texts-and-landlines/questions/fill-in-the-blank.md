# Handling portable devices safely — signal, texts and landlines — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1.** Rule 6: fewer calls, more ______.
**Answer: texts.**

**2.** A device raises its ______ power when reception is poor.
**Answer: transmission.**

**3.** ______ calls at office and home are the safest way for long conversations.
**Answer: Landline.**
