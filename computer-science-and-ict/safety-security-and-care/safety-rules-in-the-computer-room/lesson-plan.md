## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Rules before switching on** — The **safety rules** (правила техники безопасности) begin before the machine: enter the computer room calmly and with the teacher's permission, take your own place, touch nothing that is not yours to touch, and never work with wet hands or while eating and drinking near the machines. Cables stay as they lie — a pupil does not plug, unplug or move equipment unless the teacher says so.
2. **Rules while working and after** — While working: sit straight at the set distance, report any smell, spark, strange sound or damage **at once** and stop, and never open the system unit. After work: close your programs, leave the place tidy, and push the chair in. Grade 8 prints the same rules again as an appendix — a pupil who met them at ten meets them again at fourteen, because the machines grew but the rules did not change.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
