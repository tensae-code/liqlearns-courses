# Safety Rules in the Computer Room — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "When do the safety rules begin?" is: Before the machine is switched on**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Who gives permission to enter and begin?" is: Spills and crumbs damage equipment and endanger the pupil**
**Answer: False** — The teacher

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why no eating and drinking near machines?" is: Spills and crumbs damage equipment and endanger the pupil**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What must hands never be at a computer?" is: The cables and equipment**
**Answer: False** — Wet

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What stays as it lies unless the teacher says otherwise?" is: The cables and equipment**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What must be reported at once?" is: The system unit**
**Answer: False** — Any smell, spark, strange sound or damage

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What must a pupil never open?" is: The system unit**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name two after-work duties." is: As a closing appendix**
**Answer: False** — Close your programs; leave the place tidy (or push the chair in)

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "In the Grade 8 book, where do the rules return?" is: As a closing appendix**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why are the rules printed again for older pupils?" is: Before the machine is switched on**
**Answer: False** — The machines grew, but the rules did not change
