# Safety Rules in the Computer Room — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) When do the safety rules begin?**
a) Before the machine is switched on  b) The teacher  c) Spills and crumbs damage equipment and endanger the pupil  d) Wet
**Answer: a) Before the machine is switched on**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who gives permission to enter and begin?**
a) Spills and crumbs damage equipment and endanger the pupil  b) The teacher  c) Wet  d) The cables and equipment
**Answer: b) The teacher**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why no eating and drinking near machines?**
a) Wet  b) The cables and equipment  c) Spills and crumbs damage equipment and endanger the pupil  d) Any smell, spark, strange sound or damage
**Answer: c) Spills and crumbs damage equipment and endanger the pupil**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must hands never be at a computer?**
a) The cables and equipment  b) Any smell, spark, strange sound or damage  c) The system unit  d) Wet
**Answer: d) Wet**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What stays as it lies unless the teacher says otherwise?**
a) The cables and equipment  b) Any smell, spark, strange sound or damage  c) The system unit  d) Close your programs; leave the place tidy (or push the chair in)
**Answer: a) The cables and equipment**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What must be reported at once?**
a) The system unit  b) Any smell, spark, strange sound or damage  c) Close your programs; leave the place tidy (or push the chair in)  d) As a closing appendix
**Answer: b) Any smell, spark, strange sound or damage**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must a pupil never open?**
a) Close your programs; leave the place tidy (or push the chair in)  b) As a closing appendix  c) The system unit  d) The machines grew, but the rules did not change
**Answer: c) The system unit**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two after-work duties.**
a) As a closing appendix  b) The machines grew, but the rules did not change  c) Before the machine is switched on  d) Close your programs; leave the place tidy (or push the chair in)
**Answer: d) Close your programs; leave the place tidy (or push the chair in)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In the Grade 8 book, where do the rules return?**
a) As a closing appendix  b) The machines grew, but the rules did not change  c) Before the machine is switched on  d) The teacher
**Answer: a) As a closing appendix**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why are the rules printed again for older pupils?**
a) Before the machine is switched on  b) The machines grew, but the rules did not change  c) The teacher  d) Spills and crumbs damage equipment and endanger the pupil
**Answer: b) The machines grew, but the rules did not change**
