# Hands-on — Caring for a computer — safety before cleaning

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Precaution drill
In pairs, one reads a cleaning situation aloud ('The monitor is dusty and still warm…'); the other must respond with the precaution that applies before any cleaning starts. Swap after five situations.

## Room check
Walk around a real computer (switched off) and point to each part you may clean later and each part that is trained-people-only, saying the reason for each.
