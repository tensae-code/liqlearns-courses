# Caring for a computer — safety before cleaning

## Do it first

A dusty computer needs cleaning. Before you touch it: what could hurt YOU, and what could hurt the MACHINE? List everything you can think of. This lesson is the book's safety list — check yours against it.

## Safety first, cleaning second

Wrong use of a computer endangers both sides. The **user**: back pain, stiff neck and shoulders, sore hands and wrists, tired legs from sitting long. The **device** can be damaged too. So before cleaning, the precautions come first. **Switch off and unplug** all electrical equipment. **Let monitors and laser printers cool and lose their charge for at least 30 minutes.** High-voltage parts — **power supplies and monitors** — are for **specially trained people only**; never open them. And **use only proper cleaning tools**: unsuitable cleaners can give off dangerous fumes, damage plastics, or simply not clean. Safety is not the delay before the lesson. Safety is the lesson.
