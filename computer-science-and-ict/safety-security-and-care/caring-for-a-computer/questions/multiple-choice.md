# Caring for a computer — safety before cleaning — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Before cleaning electrical equipment you must —
a) keep it running  b) switch it off and unplug it  c) spray it first  d) open the power supply
**Answer: b) switch it off and unplug it** — off and unplugged, always first.

**2.** Monitors and laser printers should cool and lose charge for at least —
a) 1 minute  b) 30 minutes  c) 1 hour exactly  d) no time at all
**Answer: b) 30 minutes** — at least 30 minutes.

**3.** Power supplies and monitors are —
a) for pupils to open  b) for specially trained people only  c) safe when wet  d) toys
**Answer: b) for specially trained people only** — high-voltage parts: trained people only.

**4.** Unsuitable cleaners can —
a) clean better  b) give off dangerous fumes, damage plastics, or not clean  c) save time  d) smell nice only
**Answer: b) give off dangerous fumes, damage plastics, or not clean** — the book's three harms.

**5.** Wrong computer use can bring the user —
a) back pain, stiff neck, sore hands and wrists  b) stronger eyes  c) faster typing only  d) nothing
**Answer: a) back pain, stiff neck, sore hands and wrists** — the body harms are named first in the unit.

**6.** The first step of any cleaning is —
a) wiping  b) safety precautions  c) buying tools  d) opening the case
**Answer: b) safety precautions** — safety before cleaning.
