# Caring for a computer — safety before cleaning — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** A switched-off monitor is instantly safe to open.
**Answer: False** — it must cool and lose charge for at least 30 minutes — and opening is for trained people.

**2.** Unplugging is part of the precautions.
**Answer: True** — switch off and unplug.

**3.** Any cleaner from the kitchen is fine for a computer.
**Answer: False** — only proper tools; unsuitable cleaners harm.
