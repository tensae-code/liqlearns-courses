# Lesson plan — Caring for a computer — safety before cleaning

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Safety, Security and Care · **Lesson:** Caring for a computer — safety before cleaning
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 13 — Safety, Security and Care in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 7 book, Unit 5 — Security and Safety of Computer. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** before any cleaning: switch off and unplug, let monitors and laser printers cool for at least 30 minutes, leave high-voltage parts to trained people, and use only proper cleaning tools.
**This lesson's own level:** it opens the Safety title — it stands on the learner's own body and health first, before any device is touched.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the harms wrong use brings to the user.
2. Give the pre-cleaning precautions in order.
3. Say which parts are for trained people only, and why.
4. Say the three harms of unsuitable cleaners.

## What you need
No equipment for this lesson — it is learned before any cleaning is done.
