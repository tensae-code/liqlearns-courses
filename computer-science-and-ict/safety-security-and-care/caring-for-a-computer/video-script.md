# Video script — Caring for a computer — safety before cleaning

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before cleaning: stop. Safety first.
Switch off. Unplug.
Wait thirty minutes. Parts hold their charge.
Power supplies and monitors: trained people only.

Proper tools only.
Wrong cleaners bring fumes — and damage — and no clean.

Safe hands. Then clean hands.
