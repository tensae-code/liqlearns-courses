# Hands-on — Legal Procedures for Data Protection

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Route map** — Draw the procedure route for a citizen who finds their data wrong at a company: steps, documents, bodies, in order.

## Task two
**Breach clock** — Write a school's breach-report checklist: who is told, what is written, in what order — as a one-page procedure.
