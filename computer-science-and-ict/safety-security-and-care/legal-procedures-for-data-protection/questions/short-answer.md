# Legal Procedures for Data Protection — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name four duties the law sets.**
**Answer: Collection purposes, keeping (secured/accurate/limited), consent, the described person's rights**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three rights of the person described.**
**Answer: To know, to correct, to object**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are legal procedures?**
**Answer: The steps and documents by which the duties are actually done**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name three procedure examples.**
**Answer: Permissions obtained, breaches reported, complaints filed (or penalties applied)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Around what kind of data does the law stand?**
**Answer: Personal data**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What must an organisation register and document?**
**Answer: What personal data it holds, and why**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) To whom must a breach be reported?**
**Answer: The authority and the affected people, within the law's terms**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) By what does a citizen request their data?**
**Answer: A stated route**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do violations meet?**
**Answer: Stated consequences — fines and liability — by stated bodies**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why teach procedures, not slogans?**
**Answer: Protection a citizen cannot operate is protection on paper only**
