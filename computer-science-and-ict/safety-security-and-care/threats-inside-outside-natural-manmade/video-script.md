This is **Threats — inside, outside, natural, man-made**.

**internal threats** — threats that come from inside an institution.

**external threats** — threats that come from outside an institution.

**accidental attacks** — damage done by mistake, an unintended action.

**intentional attacks** — attacks done deliberately.
