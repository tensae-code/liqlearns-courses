# Threats — inside, outside, natural, man-made — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is threats that come from outside an institution. (Name the term.)
**Answer: external threats**

**2.** _____ is damage done by mistake, an unintended action. (Name the term.)
**Answer: accidental attacks**

**3.** _____ is attacks done deliberately. (Name the term.)
**Answer: intentional attacks**
