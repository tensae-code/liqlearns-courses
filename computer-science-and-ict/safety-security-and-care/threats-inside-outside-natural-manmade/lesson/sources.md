Book error, taught corrected: the threats section of the student book cites "section 3.2 above" for the security principles — the principles are taught in §5.2 of this unit, and this build cites 5.2. The lesson keeps the book's own sorting of threats; only the cross-reference is corrected.

Laos's own way (Grade 10 ICT student book): the upper-secondary course turns conceptual — a full information-systems syllabus (organisations, TPS/MIS/DSS/EIS/expert/office systems, the development life cycle), then the series' deepest security chapter (firewalls, cookies, spyware, spam, employee monitoring, privacy law), closing with artificial intelligence, with DOS and Unix taught alongside Windows in the senior years' background.

South Sudan's own way (Grade 9 S1 Student): Opens ICT at secondary level entirely through doing — the whole first year is Microsoft Office application skills (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Publisher, Paint) taught as step-by-step keyboard/mouse procedures with local data (South Sudan state populations, local disease statistics for charts), then internet use, hands-on troubleshooting, and Windows access control/security settings.
