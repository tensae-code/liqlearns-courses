# Fire Extinguisher Types and Colour Codes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How are fires classed?**
a) By what is burning  b) Electrical equipment  c) Electricity or liquid fuel  d) Smothers it
**Answer: a) By what is burning**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which fire class does a computer lab fear most?**
a) Electricity or liquid fuel  b) Electrical equipment  c) Smothers it  d) It smothers electrical fires without leaving residue
**Answer: b) Electrical equipment**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must water never touch?**
a) Smothers it  b) It smothers electrical fires without leaving residue  c) Electricity or liquid fuel  d) By what is burning
**Answer: c) Electricity or liquid fuel**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does foam do to a liquid fire?**
a) It smothers electrical fires without leaving residue  b) By what is burning  c) Electrical equipment  d) Smothers it
**Answer: d) Smothers it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why is CO₂ the lab's extinguisher?**
a) It smothers electrical fires without leaving residue  b) By what is burning  c) Electrical equipment  d) Electricity or liquid fuel
**Answer: a) It smothers electrical fires without leaving residue**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the colour code announce?**
a) Red  b) The extinguisher's type  c) Blue  d) Black
**Answer: b) The extinguisher's type**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which colour means water?**
a) Blue  b) Black  c) Red  d) Get out and raise the alarm instead
**Answer: c) Red**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which colour means dry powder?**
a) Black  b) Get out and raise the alarm instead  c) The extinguisher's type  d) Blue
**Answer: d) Blue**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which colour means CO₂?**
a) Black  b) Get out and raise the alarm instead  c) The extinguisher's type  d) Red
**Answer: a) Black**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What if no colour matches the fire?**
a) The extinguisher's type  b) Get out and raise the alarm instead  c) Red  d) Blue
**Answer: b) Get out and raise the alarm instead**
