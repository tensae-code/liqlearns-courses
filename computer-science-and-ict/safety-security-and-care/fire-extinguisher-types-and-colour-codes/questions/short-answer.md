# Fire Extinguisher Types and Colour Codes — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How are fires classed?**
**Answer: By what is burning**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which fire class does a computer lab fear most?**
**Answer: Electrical equipment**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What must water never touch?**
**Answer: Electricity or liquid fuel**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does foam do to a liquid fire?**
**Answer: Smothers it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why is CO₂ the lab's extinguisher?**
**Answer: It smothers electrical fires without leaving residue**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the colour code announce?**
**Answer: The extinguisher's type**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which colour means water?**
**Answer: Red**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which colour means dry powder?**
**Answer: Blue**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which colour means CO₂?**
**Answer: Black**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What if no colour matches the fire?**
**Answer: Get out and raise the alarm instead**
