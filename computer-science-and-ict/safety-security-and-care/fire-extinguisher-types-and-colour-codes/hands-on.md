# Hands-on — Fire Extinguisher Types and Colour Codes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Read the corridor', 'Find the nearest extinguisher to your classroom (look, do not touch). Write its colour code, its type, and the fire classes it may be used on. Say whether it is the right one for a computer lab.')

## Task two
('Match or run', 'For each fire, answer with an extinguisher colour — or RUN: a waste-paper bin alight; a monitor smoking; cooking oil burning in the school kitchen; an unknown fire already at the ceiling.')
