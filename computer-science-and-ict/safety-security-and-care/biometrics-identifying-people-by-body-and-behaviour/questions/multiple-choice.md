# Biometrics — Identifying People by Body and Behaviour — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is biometrics?**
a) Identifying a person by body and behaviour  b) Fingerprint, hand geometry, iris, face and DNA  c) DNA  d) The iris
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the five body measures.**
a) Fingerprint, hand geometry, iris, face and DNA  b) DNA  c) The iris  d) The fingerprint
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which body measure is the courtroom's standard?**
a) DNA  b) The iris  c) The fingerprint  d) Keystroke rhythm, signature dynamics and voice
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which is readable at a distance and stable for life?**
a) The iris  b) The fingerprint  c) Keystroke rhythm, signature dynamics and voice  d) The making — speed and pressure
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is unique even between twins?**
a) The fingerprint  b) Keystroke rhythm, signature dynamics and voice  c) The making — speed and pressure  d) Attendance and borders (also national ID, banking, phones, elections)
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three behaviour measures.**
a) Keystroke rhythm, signature dynamics and voice  b) The making — speed and pressure  c) Attendance and borders (also national ID, banking, phones, elections)  d) A stolen fingerprint cannot be changed
**Answer: a**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does signature dynamics measure — shape or making?**
a) The making — speed and pressure  b) Attendance and borders (also national ID, banking, phones, elections)  c) A stolen fingerprint cannot be changed  d) Be changed after a theft
**Answer: a**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two places biometrics serve.**
a) Attendance and borders (also national ID, banking, phones, elections)  b) A stolen fingerprint cannot be changed  c) Be changed after a theft  d) Identifying a person by body and behaviour

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does biometric data need the strongest keeping?**
a) A stolen fingerprint cannot be changed  b) Be changed after a theft  c) Identifying a person by body and behaviour  d) Fingerprint, hand geometry, iris, face and DNA
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What can a password do that a fingerprint cannot?**
a) Be changed after a theft  b) Identifying a person by body and behaviour  c) Fingerprint, hand geometry, iris, face and DNA  d) DNA
