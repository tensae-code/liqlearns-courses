# Biometrics — Identifying People by Body and Behaviour — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is biometrics?**
**Answer: Identifying a person by body and behaviour**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the five body measures.**
**Answer: Fingerprint, hand geometry, iris, face and DNA**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which body measure is the courtroom's standard?**
**Answer: DNA**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which is readable at a distance and stable for life?**
**Answer: The iris**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is unique even between twins?**
**Answer: The fingerprint**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three behaviour measures.**
**Answer: Keystroke rhythm, signature dynamics and voice**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does signature dynamics measure — shape or making?**
**Answer: The making — speed and pressure**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two places biometrics serve.**
**Answer: Attendance and borders (also national ID, banking, phones, elections)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does biometric data need the strongest keeping?**
**Answer: A stolen fingerprint cannot be changed**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What can a password do that a fingerprint cannot?**
**Answer: Be changed after a theft**
