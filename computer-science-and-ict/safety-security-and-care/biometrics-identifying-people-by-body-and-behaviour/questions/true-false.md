# Biometrics — Identifying People by Body and Behaviour — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Identifying a person by body and behaviour**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Identifying a person by body and behaviour** — as the answer to: Name the five body measures.
**Answer: False — Fingerprint, hand geometry, iris, face and DNA**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: DNA**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: DNA** — as the answer to: Which is readable at a distance and stable for life?
**Answer: False — The iris**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The fingerprint**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The fingerprint** — as the answer to: Name the three behaviour measures.
**Answer: False — Keystroke rhythm, signature dynamics and voice**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The making — speed and pressure**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The making — speed and pressure** — as the answer to: Name two places biometrics serve.
**Answer: False — Attendance and borders (also national ID, banking, phones, elections)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A stolen fingerprint cannot be changed**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A stolen fingerprint cannot be changed** — as the answer to: What can a password do that a fingerprint cannot?
**Answer: False — Be changed after a theft**
