# Hands-on — Biometrics — Identifying People by Body and Behaviour

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Choose the measure** — For four errands — phone unlock, border control, school attendance, a criminal trial — choose the biometric that fits and defend the trade of speed, cost and certainty.

## Task two
**The unchangeable key** — Write the safety rules for one biometric database: who enrols, who may search, how a false match is appealed, and how the data is destroyed when its purpose ends.
