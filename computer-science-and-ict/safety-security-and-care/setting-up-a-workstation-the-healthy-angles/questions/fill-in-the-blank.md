# Setting up a workstation — the healthy angles — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The backrest should be angled at ______ degrees, with lumbar support in line with the lower back.
**Answer: 90–110.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Wrists should be in line with the ______.
**Answer: forearms.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Headaches, burning eyes, red eyes and a stiff neck together make up computer ______ syndrome.
**Answer: vision.**
