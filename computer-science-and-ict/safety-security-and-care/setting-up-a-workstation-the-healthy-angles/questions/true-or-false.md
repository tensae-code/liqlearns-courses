# Setting up a workstation — the healthy angles — true or false

Say true or false for each one.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Shoulders should be hunched up while typing.
**Answer: False** — shoulders stay relaxed, not hunched.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The keyboard should be aligned with your nose.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Feet may rest flat on the ground or on a footrest.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Prolonged computer work can cause physical stress that could eventually lead to a disability.
**Answer: True** (teacher guide — evidence).
