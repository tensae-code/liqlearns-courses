# Setting up a workstation — the healthy angles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Elbows should rest above the desk at —
a) 30–40 degrees  b) 90–110 degrees  c) 180 degrees  d) any angle
**Answer: b) 90–110 degrees.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** While seated, hips, knees and ankles should be at —
a) 45 degrees  b) 90 degrees  c) 120 degrees  d) straight out
**Answer: b) 90 degrees.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Your eyes should look at —
a) the bottom of the screen  b) the keyboard  c) the top third of the screen  d) the ceiling
**Answer: c) the top third of the screen.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A laptop should be used with —
a) nothing extra  b) a riser, an external keyboard and an external mouse  c) a pillow only  d) a smaller screen
**Answer: b) a riser, an external keyboard and an external mouse.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Computer vision syndrome includes —
a) headaches, burning eyes, red eyes and a stiff neck  b) backaches only  c) numb hands only  d) faster typing
**Answer: a) headaches, burning eyes, red eyes and a stiff neck** (teacher guide — evidence).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In the paired sitting figures, the appropriate style is —
a) the right-hand one  b) neither  c) the left-hand one  d) both
**Answer: c) the left-hand one** — the right-hand style causes back pain and muscle injury (teacher guide — evidence).
