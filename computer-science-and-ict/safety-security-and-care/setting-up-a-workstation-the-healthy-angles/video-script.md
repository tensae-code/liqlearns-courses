# Video script — Setting up a workstation — the healthy angles

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is the workstation set-up, done slowly, in order. Do each step with me.

**One.** Chair first. Sit down. Feet flat on the floor — or on a footrest.
**Two.** Hips, knees and ankles: ninety degrees. Like the corner of a book.
**Three.** The seat must hold under your thighs. Back against the backrest — ninety to one hundred and ten degrees — with the support in your lower back.
**Four.** Shoulders down. Relaxed. Never hunched.
**Five.** Elbows above the desk — ninety to one hundred and ten degrees. Wrists straight, in line with your forearms.
**Six.** Head upright. Ears over shoulders.
**Seven.** The screen: your eyes look at its **top third**. Face it directly.
**Eight.** The keyboard in line with your nose. The mouse close, held loosely.
**Nine.** A laptop? Lift it on a riser — and use its own keyboard and mouse beside it.

Now check me. Check yourself. Same angles?
That is a workstation set up the healthy way.
