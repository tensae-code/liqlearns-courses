# Hands-on — Setting up a workstation — the healthy angles

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Set up for real
At a real workstation (or a desk arranged as one), carry out the eleven principles in order: chair, feet, joints, backrest, shoulders, elbows, wrists, head, screen, keyboard and mouse, laptop riser if there is a laptop. A partner reads each principle aloud; you adjust; they check.

## The angle hunt
Without a protractor: find the corner of a book — that is 90 degrees. Hold it against your elbow, knee and ankle as you sit. Which joints are at the book's corner, and which are not? Adjust until they are.

## Judge the figures
Draw the paired figures yourself — one sitting appropriately, one slumped. Exchange with a partner; each writes the verdict (which is appropriate) and two reasons under the other's drawing.
