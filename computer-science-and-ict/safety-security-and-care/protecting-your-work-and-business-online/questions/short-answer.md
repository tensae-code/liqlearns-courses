# Protecting Your Work and Business Online — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What three assets do online affairs stand on?**
**Answer: Accounts, money and name (reputation)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why is the mail account the key account?**
**Answer: The other accounts can be reset from it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does a scammer steal a business's name?**
**Answer: By cloning its page and trading on its reputation**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why must the defence be one wall, not three fences?**
**Answer: The three assets fall together**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) What password rule protects the accounts?**
**Answer: Strong, separate passwords for each important account**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What proof is added wherever offered?**
**Answer: Two-step proof (two-factor authentication)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How are payments made safely?**
**Answer: Through traced channels, with records kept**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is backed up, and where?**
**Answer: The business's records and pages, kept apart**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the watching habit catch early?**
**Answer: A clone page or a strange transaction**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 12 ICT, Chapter 2 §II.6**
