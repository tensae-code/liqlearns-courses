# Video script — Starting up a computer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Starting up, in order.
Check: parts seated. Cord plugged in.
Connect: keyboard, monitor, mouse — each in its proper place.

Press power on the system unit.
Monitor dark? Press its power too.
Wait. The system loads by itself.
Password? Sign in.

That is start-up.
