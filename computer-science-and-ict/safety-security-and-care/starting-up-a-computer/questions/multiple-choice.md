# Starting up a computer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The first start-up step is —
a) press power  b) check parts are connected and seated, cord plugged in  c) sign in  d) open Word
**Answer: b) check parts are connected and seated, cord plugged in** — checking comes before pressing.

**2.** Keyboard, monitor and mouse connect to —
a) any holes at all  b) their proper connectors on the system unit  c) each other only  d) the wall
**Answer: b) their proper connectors on the system unit** — each device has its proper connector.

**3.** Which power button is pressed first?
a) The monitor's  b) The system unit's  c) The keyboard's  d) None
**Answer: b) The system unit's** — the system unit's power button.

**4.** If the monitor stays dark, you —
a) give up  b) press the monitor's power button  c) unplug everything  d) press system power ten times
**Answer: b) press the monitor's power button** — the monitor may need its own press.

**5.** While the operating system loads, you —
a) keep clicking  b) wait — it loads automatically  c) type your essay  d) clean the keyboard
**Answer: b) wait — it loads automatically** — loading is automatic; wait.

**6.** The last step, when asked, is —
a) shut down  b) sign in with a password  c) open a game  d) disconnect the mouse
**Answer: b) sign in with a password** — sign in if asked.
