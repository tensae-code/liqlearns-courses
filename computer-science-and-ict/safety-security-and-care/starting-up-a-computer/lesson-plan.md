# Lesson plan — Starting up a computer

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Safety, Security and Care · **Lesson:** Starting up a computer
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 13 — Safety, Security and Care in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 7 book, Unit 5 — Security and Safety of Computer. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** start-up in the book's order: check connections and plug in; connect keyboard, monitor and mouse to their proper connectors; press the system unit's power button, then the monitor's if needed; wait for the system to load; sign in if asked.
**This lesson's own level:** it is a doing-lesson — the first of the two machine-routine lessons, standing on the hardware names from the Hardware title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Hardware lessons — the parts of the computer and their connectors.  Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Start a computer in the book's order, every step.
2. Say why checking comes before pressing.
3. Say what happens while you wait: the operating system loads automatically.
4. Sign in when asked.

## What you need
A complete computer set, switched off; a teacher to check connections, as the book directs.

## If the learner is stuck
- Presses power before checking → the check is step one: an unplugged cord makes every later step useless.
- Keeps pressing buttons during loading → loading is the machine's work; your job in that step is to wait.
