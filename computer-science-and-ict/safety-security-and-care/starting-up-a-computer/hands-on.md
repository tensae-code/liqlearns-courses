# Hands-on — Starting up a computer

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Full start-up, observed
Do a complete start-up from a fully disconnected desk set (teacher present): seat the parts, connect the three devices to their proper connectors, plug in, power the system unit, power the monitor if needed, wait, sign in. Your partner follows the strip and ticks each step.

## Fault hunt (teacher-set)
The teacher secretly leaves one thing undone — a loose cable, an unplugged cord, the monitor off. Start the computer and find the fault by working the check step properly.

## If it is hard
Start-up is assessed by direct observation, the guide's pattern for practicals: the teacher checks connections with you — that check is built into the book's own first step.
