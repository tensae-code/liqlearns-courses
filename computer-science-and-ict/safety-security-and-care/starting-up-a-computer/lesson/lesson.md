# Starting up a computer

## Do it first

The computer is off. Everything is on the desk: system unit, monitor, keyboard, mouse, cables. Talk through — out loud — exactly what you would do, in what order, to bring it to life. Then compare your order with the book's.

## The book's start-up order

Follow it exactly. **First, check** that parts are connected and seated properly, and that the **power cord is plugged in** — a teacher checks this with you. **Connect the keyboard, monitor and mouse to their proper connectors** on the system unit — each plug has its own proper place. **Press the power button on the system unit.** If the monitor does not come on by itself, **press its power button** too. Then **wait** — the operating system loads automatically; you do not hurry it. **Sign in with a password if asked.** Watch the GIF run the same order: check, press, load, desktop.
