# Ciphers in Problem Solving — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a cipher problem state?**
**Answer: A situation with a secrecy need — a message that must travel unreadable and arrive readable by one reader**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the solving chain?**
**Answer: State the need, choose the method, apply it exactly, hand over ciphertext and key by separate paths**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is the key handed over?**
**Answer: By a separate safe path — never with the ciphertext**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How exactly is the method applied?**
**Answer: Letter by letter, with the chosen key or table**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What travels through the untrusted hands?**
**Answer: The ciphertext only**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is the solution checked?**
**Answer: From the reader's end — decrypt and recover the plaintext exactly**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) One wrong letter in the recovery means what?**
**Answer: The application failed — rework the encryption**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What judgement follows the check?**
**Answer: Could an unintended hand break this method in the time the secret must last?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) If it could, what was wrong?**
**Answer: The choice was wrong for the need — solve again with a stronger method**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How do Malaysia's problems end?**
**Answer: With that judgement written down**
