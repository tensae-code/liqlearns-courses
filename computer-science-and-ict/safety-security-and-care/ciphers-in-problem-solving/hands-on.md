# Hands-on — Ciphers in Problem Solving

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Solve a whole problem', 'Problem: the class quiz answers must cross the school in a pupil's bag, unreadable until tomorrow's quiz. State the need, choose, encrypt the five-word answer line, and write the closing judgement.')

## Task two
('Examine a claim', 'Swap ciphertext and key with a partner (by separate 'paths'). Decrypt, verify the plaintext, then write your judgement of their method choice for the stated need.')
