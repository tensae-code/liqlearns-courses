# Video script — Ethics of Electronic Communication

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ethics begins in refusals. **Spam** — unwanted bulk messages — is not answered, not forwarded, and not fed by your address given carelessly. **Chain letters** — 'send this to ten friends' — are broken by the reader who stops: the chain lives only by obedience, and deleting is the ethical act. **Strangers online** are met with the standing care of the whole course: no personal information, no meeting, no trust earned by words alone. What is refused costs nothing; what is forwarded cannot be recalled.
What is sent is governed by **polite writing**: words chosen as if the reader stood before you, because behind every address a person does. Mongolia adds its own duty, unique among the course's countries: Mongolian is often typed abroad in **Latin transliteration**, and the book makes writing it **correctly — by the national standard MNS 5217:2003** — an ethics topic in its own right: to write your language carelessly in foreign letters is a small discourtesy to every Mongolian reader, and the standard exists so the letters say the language truly. Ethics here is not imported; it is the nation's own rule for its own tongue, carried into the network.
