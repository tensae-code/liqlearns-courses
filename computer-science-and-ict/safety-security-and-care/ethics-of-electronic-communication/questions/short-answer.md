# Ethics of Electronic Communication — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is spam, and what is done with it?**
**Answer: Unwanted bulk messages — not answered, not forwarded, not fed with your address**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is a chain letter broken?**
**Answer: By the reader who stops — deleting is the ethical act**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What care is kept with strangers online?**
**Answer: No personal information, no meeting, no trust earned by words alone**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What cannot be recalled?**
**Answer: What is forwarded**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What governs what is sent?**
**Answer: Polite writing — words chosen as if the reader stood before you**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is Mongolia's own added duty?**
**Answer: Writing Mongolian correctly in Latin transliteration**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) By which standard is Mongolian transliterated?**
**Answer: The national standard MNS 5217:2003**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is careless transliteration an ethics matter?**
**Answer: It is a discourtesy to Mongolian readers; the standard makes the letters say the language truly**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does this ethics lesson close?**
**Answer: Mongolia's Grade 7 electronic-communication unit**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) In the lesson's phrase, what is this ethics?**
**Answer: Not imported — the nation's own rules carried into the network**
