This is **What an attack costs — loss, corruption, theft, illegal use**.

**destruction or loss of information** — destroyed components, or data lost — for example to a failed hard disk.

**corruption of information** — unauthorised alteration of stored files — such as adding or reducing student marks.

**theft of services** — unauthorised use of computer or network services — such as running private marketing over school Internet.

**illegal usage** — using the system's normal function for the attacker's other, unlawful purpose.
