# What an attack costs — loss, corruption, theft, illegal use — tf

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** True or false: destruction or loss of information — destroyed components, or data lost — for example to a failed hard disk.
**Answer: True**

**2.** True or false: corruption of information — unauthorised alteration of stored files — such as adding or reducing student marks.
**Answer: True**

**3.** True or false: theft of services is the opposite: it has nothing to do with unauthorised use of computer or network services — such as running private marketing over school Internet.
**Answer: False** — theft of services is about unauthorised use of computer or network services — such as running private marketing over school Internet.
