# What an attack costs — loss, corruption, theft, illegal use — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is unauthorised alteration of stored files — such as adding or reducing student marks. (Name the term.)
**Answer: corruption of information**

**2.** _____ is unauthorised use of computer or network services — such as running private marketing over school Internet. (Name the term.)
**Answer: theft of services**

**3.** _____ is using the system's normal function for the attacker's other, unlawful purpose. (Name the term.)
**Answer: illegal usage**
