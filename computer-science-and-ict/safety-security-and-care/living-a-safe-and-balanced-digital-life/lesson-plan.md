# Lesson plan — Living a Safe and Balanced Digital Life

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Safety, Security and Care · **Lesson:** Living a Safe and Balanced Digital Life
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Bangladesh (2026-11-14) — in the title Safety, Security and Care on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Bangladesh's NCTB student books (Bangladesh Grade 8 Digital Technology), because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree and live lesson content were checked for the slug and for near matches before building.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** living a safe and balanced digital life: the closing session of Grade 8's security experience — safety grown into balance: time, body, mind and friendship kept healthy inside a digital life that is lived, not escaped (Bangladesh Grade 8 Digital Technology, Learning Experience 2, Session 6).
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier lessons of its title and on the book's own order — it follows the book's lessons in sequence, so its neighbours in the book are its natural companions. It stands alone for a learner landing on it directly.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Balance — time and body** — **Balance** begins with **time**: the phone's hours measured honestly (the device itself reports them) and budgeted like money — school, family, play and sleep paid first, the feed given what remains, not the reverse. The **body** keeps its accounts too: eyes rested by distance and breaks, posture kept, the device put down before sleep so the night can do its work. None of this is the machine's setting; it is the owner's, which is why the book teaches it as a life skill in a technology book — the technology will not teach it at all.
2. **Balance — mind and friendship; the safe life whole** — The **mind**'s balance is the feed's diet: what angers and frightens is served most because it holds us longest, so the balanced citizen curates — follows what nourishes, mutes what poisons, and remembers the misinformation lessons when the feed performs its certainties. **Friendship**'s balance keeps the digital in its place: present with the people present, the phone face-down in company, online kindness held to the standard of the room. Safe *and* balanced — the experience's two words are the whole curriculum of the digital citizen in miniature: protected from the world's harms, and master of the tool that could otherwise master them.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — The security lessons guard the machine and the data. The last session guards the person: the eyes, the sleep, the temper, the hours. A digital life can be perfectly secure and quietly miserable — this lesson is about the other half of safe. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of balance — time and body as the lesson describes it — the student studies the picture and names what each part shows.

   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key names and definitions aloud, in the order the lesson gives them — the student listens once through, then reads the same lines aloud along with the voice. (Audio is a described slot in this build; no audio file is made.)
