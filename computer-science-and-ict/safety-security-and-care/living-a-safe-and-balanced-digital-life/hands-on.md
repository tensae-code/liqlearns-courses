# Hands-on — Living a Safe and Balanced Digital Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**The honest week** — Record your device's reported hours for one week. Draw your real budget against the lesson's order — necessities first — and set one change.

## Task two
**Curate the feed** — List ten accounts or sources in your feed. Mark nourish, neutral, poison; mute one poison and write what changed in three days.
