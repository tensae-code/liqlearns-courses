# Safety signs and hazards in the computer lab — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Safety signs in the lab are there to —
a) decorate the walls  b) mark dangerous areas and alert pupils and staff  c) show the timetable  d) name the computers
**Answer: b) mark dangerous areas and alert pupils and staff** — they mark danger and alert people.

**2.** Which is one of the four duties?
a) Ignore manuals  b) Identify hazard risks  c) Rush the work  d) Hide problems
**Answer: b) Identify hazard risks** — identify hazard risks.

**3.** The book's activity is to —
a) paint new signs  b) visit the school lab and report the signs and meanings in Word  c) memorise signs only  d) remove old signs
**Answer: b) visit the school lab and report the signs and meanings in Word** — visit, report, in Word.

**4.** Manuals should be —
a) followed  b) thrown away  c) rewritten by pupils  d) read once ever
**Answer: a) followed** — follow manuals.

**5.** The guide's discussion group size is —
a) ten pupils  b) two to four pupils  c) one pupil  d) the whole school
**Answer: b) two to four pupils** — two to four.

**6.** The prevention order begins with —
a) control them  b) running a safe and healthy laboratory procedure  c) buying signs  d) switching off
**Answer: b) running a safe and healthy laboratory procedure** — safe procedure first, then recognise, assess, control.
