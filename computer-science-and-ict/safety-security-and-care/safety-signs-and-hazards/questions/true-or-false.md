# Safety signs and hazards in the computer lab — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** Safety signs protect only teachers.
**Answer: False** — they alert pupils and staff.

**2.** Applying occupational health and safety requirements is part of a safe lab.
**Answer: True** — one of the four duties.

**3.** Hazards should be identified only after an accident.
**Answer: False** — identify risks before work, not after harm.
