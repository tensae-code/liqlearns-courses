# Safety signs and hazards in the computer lab

## Do it first

Picture your school's computer laboratory — or visit it. What signs are on its walls? What is each one warning about, or pointing to? This lesson is about reading the room before you work in it.

## The safe workplace and its signs

Choose a **safe and healthy workplace**. **Identify hazard risks**. **Follow manuals**, and **apply occupational health and safety requirements**. **Safety signs and symbols mark dangerous areas** in and around the computer laboratory, to alert pupils and staff before anything goes wrong. The teacher guide keys the hazard-prevention set in its own order of ideas: run a safe and healthy laboratory procedure, recognise the hazards, assess their risks, control them, follow the manufacturer's requirements, and apply occupational health and safety — discussed in groups of two to four. The book's activity: **visit your school lab and report the safety signs and their meanings in Word.**
