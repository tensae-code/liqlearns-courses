# Lesson plan — Safety signs and hazards in the computer lab

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Safety, Security and Care · **Lesson:** Safety signs and hazards in the computer lab
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 13 — Safety, Security and Care in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 7 book, Unit 5 — Security and Safety of Computer. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a safe lab is chosen and kept deliberately — hazards identified, manuals followed, safety requirements applied — and safety signs mark the dangerous areas to alert pupils and staff.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the precautions lesson — safety moves from the single machine to the whole room.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Caring for a computer — safety before cleaning.  Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say the four duties: safe workplace, identify hazards, follow manuals, apply safety requirements.
2. Say what safety signs are for.
3. Do the book's activity: report your lab's signs and meanings in Word.
4. Give the guide's prevention order.

## What you need
Your school computer lab to visit; Word for the report.
