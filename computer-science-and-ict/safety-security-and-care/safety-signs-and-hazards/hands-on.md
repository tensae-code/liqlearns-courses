# Hands-on — Safety signs and hazards in the computer lab

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Sign survey
Visit the school lab in a group of two to four. Photograph or sketch every safety sign, and write its meaning beside it. No sign found is also a finding — report that.

## Report in Word
Write the survey up in Microsoft Word: sign, where it hangs, what it means, who it protects. Use the Word skills from the Word Processing title.
