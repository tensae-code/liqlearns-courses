# User accounts — Administrator, Standard, Guest — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An account is a user name plus a ______.
**Answer: password**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The ______ account controls the whole computer.
**Answer: Administrator**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A ______ is not recognised by name.
**Answer: Guest**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Guest accounts are common in internet ______.
**Answer: cafés**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Changes to accounts require ______ access.
**Answer: administrator**
