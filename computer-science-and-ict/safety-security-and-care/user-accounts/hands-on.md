# Hands-on — User accounts — Administrator, Standard, Guest

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Who may do it?
Take six actions — install a new program, write a document, change a system setting, open an existing program, let a visitor use the machine, delete another pupil's account. For each, say which account kinds may do it, and which may not.

## Account for a day
Plan the accounts for a family computer: who is Administrator (one trusted person), who is Standard, when Guest is used. Defend each choice in one sentence.

## Route and rank
Run the checking route on a school computer (or on paper). List the accounts found, rank them by power, and say which one daily schoolwork belongs on.
