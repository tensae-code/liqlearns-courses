# Video script — User accounts — Administrator, Standard, Guest

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Security guards three things.
The hardware. The information. The service itself.

The guard is the account. A name — plus a password.
Administrator: controls everything. Installs programs.
Standard: everyday work. No installing.
Guest: anyone. No name known.

Power by need. That is the design.
