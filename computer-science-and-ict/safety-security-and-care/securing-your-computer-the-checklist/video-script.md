# Video script — Securing your computer — the checklist

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **Securing your computer — the checklist**.

**the firewall** — a security guard between the Internet and your local network, to be activated.

**the password rule here** — change passwords regularly; unique mixes of numbers, capitals and small letters — at least 15 characters advised — different for each account.

**daily scans and backups** — daily full system scans, and a periodic backup schedule so data is retrievable.

**think before you click** — treat urgency, too-good-to-be-true offers and requests for personal information as suspicious — when in doubt, do not click.
