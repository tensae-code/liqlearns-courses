# Hands-on — Securing your computer — the checklist

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Checklist in hand
Take the printed checklist to a real computer. Tick what is done, cross what is missing, and fix what you are allowed to fix.

## Too-good-to-be-true drill
Write three scam-style messages using urgency and prizes. A partner marks the suspicion signs in each.
