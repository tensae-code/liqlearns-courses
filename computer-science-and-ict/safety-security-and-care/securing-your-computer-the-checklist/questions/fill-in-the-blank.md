# Securing your computer — the checklist — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is change passwords regularly; unique mixes of numbers, capitals and small letters — at least 15 characters advised — different for each account. (Name the term.)
**Answer: the password rule here**

**2.** _____ is daily full system scans, and a periodic backup schedule so data is retrievable. (Name the term.)
**Answer: daily scans and backups**

**3.** _____ is treat urgency, too-good-to-be-true offers and requests for personal information as suspicious — when in doubt, do not click. (Name the term.)
**Answer: think before you click**
