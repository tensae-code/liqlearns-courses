# Securing your computer — the checklist — tf

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** True or false: the firewall — a security guard between the Internet and your local network, to be activated.
**Answer: True**

**2.** True or false: the password rule here — change passwords regularly; unique mixes of numbers, capitals and small letters — at least 15 characters advised — different for each account.
**Answer: True**

**3.** True or false: daily scans and backups is the opposite: it has nothing to do with daily full system scans, and a periodic backup schedule so data is retrievable.
**Answer: False** — daily scans and backups is about daily full system scans, and a periodic backup schedule so data is retrievable.
