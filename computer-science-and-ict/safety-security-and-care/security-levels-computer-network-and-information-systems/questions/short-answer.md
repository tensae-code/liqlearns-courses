# Security Levels — Computer, Network and Information Systems — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the computer level guard?**
**Answer: One machine — accounts and passwords, updates, antivirus, who may sit at it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the network level guard?**
**Answer: The travelling and the gates — connections, the firewall, attacks on many machines**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a firewall decide?**
**Answer: What may pass**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A guarded network with an unguarded laptop is what?**
**Answer: A wall with the door open**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 7 Pliroforiki A, Unit 6**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the systems level guard?**
**Answer: The whole working system — its data, its people, its procedures**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the data guards.**
**Answer: Who may read/change/delete what, and backups**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the people guards.**
**Answer: Training, duties, the rules they sign**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must be decided before the bad day?**
**Answer: The procedures — what happens when it comes**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why all three rings at once?**
**Answer: Attackers choose the weakest ring**
