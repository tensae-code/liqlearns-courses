# Security Levels — Computer, Network and Information Systems — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the computer level guard?** → **One machine — accounts and passwords, updates, antivirus, who may sit at it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the network level guard?** → **The travelling and the gates — connections, the firewall, attacks on many machines**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a firewall decide?** → **What may pass**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A guarded network with an unguarded laptop is what?** → **A wall with the door open**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches it?** → **Greece Grade 7 Pliroforiki A, Unit 6**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the systems level guard?** → **The whole working system — its data, its people, its procedures**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the data guards.** → **Who may read/change/delete what, and backups**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the people guards.** → **Training, duties, the rules they sign**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must be decided before the bad day?** → **The procedures — what happens when it comes**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why all three rings at once?** → **Attackers choose the weakest ring**
