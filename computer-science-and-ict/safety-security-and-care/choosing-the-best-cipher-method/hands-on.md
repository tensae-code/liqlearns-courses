# Hands-on — Choosing the Best Cipher Method

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Choose and defend', 'Three messages: a birthday surprise note passed in class, a club's membership list kept for a year, a teacher's exam questions kept for a month. Choose a cipher method for each and write the defence in Malaysia's form.')

## Task two
('Attack a choice', 'Swap defences with a partner. Attack their weakest match: name the need their chosen method fails, or the effort it wastes.')
