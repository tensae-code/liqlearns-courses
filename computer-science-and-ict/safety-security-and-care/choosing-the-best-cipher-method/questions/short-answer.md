# Choosing the Best Cipher Method — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does 'best' mean in this lesson?**
**Answer: Best for this purpose — not best in general**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does choosing begin?**
**Answer: With the message's needs**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the needs Malaysia's choice weighs.**
**Answer: How secret it must stay and for how long; who must use the method; what a mistake would cost**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How long must a surprise party's secret last, against an exam paper's?**
**Answer: Days, against until the exam**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a method stronger than the need cause?**
**Answer: Wasted effort and use-errors**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a method weaker than the need?**
**Answer: Not a protection at all**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the choice made from the table?**
**Answer: Match the method whose strengths cover the needs and whose weaknesses the purpose can bear**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What form must the answer take?**
**Answer: A defence — the choice, the need it answers, and each rejected method with its reason**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is an undefended choice?**
**Answer: A guess, not a choice**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is security, in the unit's deepest point?**
**Answer: This judgement — repeated for every message**
