# The map of cyber-attacks

## Do it first

Eight attackers are coming at one connected computer — but each attacks a different way. One floods it, one disguises itself, one listens, one tricks a person. Before the names are given, guess: how many of the eight attack the machine, and how many attack the person using it?

## Why 'cyber'

Most man-made threats now arrive through the Internet, so computer security is used interchangeably with cyber security, an attack is a cyber-attack, and 'cyber' refers to computers and computer networks. Some of the eight kinds combine a psychological attack with a technological one — they work on the person and the machine together.

## The eight, in order

One: denial of service. Two: malware attack. Three: man in the middle. Four: phishing. Five: eavesdropping. Six: SQL injection. Seven: password attack. Eight: social engineering. The lessons that follow take them one by one, in this order — learn the map first, then walk it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: most man-made threats now arrive through the Internet, and the eight main kinds of cyber-attack are: denial of service, malware, man in the middle, phishing, eavesdropping, SQL injection, password attack and social engineering. Then name the key words of this lesson — cyber, a cyber-attack, denial of service, social engineering — and say what each one means.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the eight kinds from memory, in order.
2. Mark each kind M (machine), P (person) or B (both).
3. Say why the map is learned before the details.
