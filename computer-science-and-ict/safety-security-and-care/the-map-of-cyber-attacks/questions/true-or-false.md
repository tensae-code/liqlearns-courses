# The map of cyber-attacks — tf

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** True or false: cyber — referring to computers and computer networks.
**Answer: True**

**2.** True or false: a cyber-attack — an attack arriving through computers and networks, most man-made threats today.
**Answer: True**

**3.** True or false: denial of service is the opposite: it has nothing to do with the first of the eight main kinds, flooding a service shut.
**Answer: False** — denial of service is about the first of the eight main kinds, flooding a service shut.
