# The map of cyber-attacks — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is an attack arriving through computers and networks, most man-made threats today. (Name the term.)
**Answer: a cyber-attack**

**2.** _____ is the first of the eight main kinds, flooding a service shut. (Name the term.)
**Answer: denial of service**

**3.** _____ is the eighth kind, tricking the person rather than the machine. (Name the term.)
**Answer: social engineering**
