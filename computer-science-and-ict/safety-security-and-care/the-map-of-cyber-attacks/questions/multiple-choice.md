# The map of cyber-attacks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is cyber?
a) referring to computers and computer networks  b) an attack arriving through computers and networks, most man-made threats today  c) the first of the eight main kinds, flooding a service shut  d) the eighth kind, tricking the person rather than the machine
**Answer: a) referring to computers and computer networks**

**2.** What is a cyber-attack?
a) referring to computers and computer networks  b) the first of the eight main kinds, flooding a service shut  c) the eighth kind, tricking the person rather than the machine  d) an attack arriving through computers and networks, most man-made threats today
**Answer: d) an attack arriving through computers and networks, most man-made threats today**

**3.** What is denial of service?
a) an attack arriving through computers and networks, most man-made threats today  b) the eighth kind, tricking the person rather than the machine  c) the first of the eight main kinds, flooding a service shut  d) referring to computers and computer networks
**Answer: c) the first of the eight main kinds, flooding a service shut**

**4.** What is social engineering?
a) the first of the eight main kinds, flooding a service shut  b) the eighth kind, tricking the person rather than the machine  c) referring to computers and computer networks  d) an attack arriving through computers and networks, most man-made threats today
**Answer: b) the eighth kind, tricking the person rather than the machine**

**5.** How many main kinds of cyber-attack does the map name?
a) five  b) six  c) eight  d) ten
**Answer: c) eight**

**6.** An attack combining psychological with technological attack is —
a) impossible  b) part of this map — some kinds work on person and machine together  c) only natural  d) a printer fault
**Answer: b) part of this map — some kinds work on person and machine together**
