# Video script — The map of cyber-attacks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Most attacks now come through the Internet.
We call them cyber-attacks. Cyber means computers and networks.

There are eight main kinds. Learn the map.

One — denial of service. The flood.
Two — malware. Bad software.
Three — man in the middle. Someone between you.
Four — phishing. The fake message.
Five — eavesdropping. The listener.
Six — SQL injection. Code slipped into a website.
Seven — password attack. Your password, hunted.
Eight — social engineering. Tricking the person, not the machine.

Eight kinds. Now we walk the map, one by one.
