**Acceptance count: taught in 7 countries (Ethiopia, China, Sudan, Cuba, Bhutan, Sri Lanka, Rwanda).**

Sudan's own way (Grade 6): the final primary book is built around real Sudanese systems — networks and cloud first, then a full security unit taught through named children's everyday internet dilemmas, and a worked example of the ipSchools school-management system (with a real Bakht Al-Ruda school results table) before closing with computers in banks and police work.

Sudan's own way (Grade 11): a university-style secondary text (single named author, Dr Awad Haj Ali of the University of Gezira) that teaches systems work before code — file types and file security in a floppy-disk/UPS world, a complete spare-parts-factory system as the running example, the algorithm named for al-Khwarizmi, desk-checking with real sales data, and then a full BASIC course (numbered lines, READ/DATA, GOSUB, DEF FN) with payroll and mean/variance programs — data processing taught as a school subject in its own right.

Cuba's own way (Grade 9): Grade 9 is the formal programming-foundations year — algorithms, data, variables, counters/accumulators, flowcharts and pseudocode, taught language-independently — paired with a full cybersecurity chapter (passwords/MFA, safe internet, safe social networks).

Bhutan's own way (Class V, Literacy with ICT student book): Word is taught together with typing in Dzongkha, the national language (Dzongkha keyboard software and fonts); communication is taught on the school LAN with Qualia LAN Messenger (chat, file sharing and broadcast with no Internet connection), and the end-of-year folder is sent to the teacher as a LAN-messenger attachment.

Sri Lanka's own way (Grade 6 Reading Book): a story/dialogue-led "Reading Book" with a talking computer character; every chapter cross-refers to numbered Workbook activities; local sectors (school office, ATM, ICU, factories, agriculture) and the 3R e-waste method are taught from Grade 6.

Sri Lanka's own way (Grade 12 Science for Technology, Tamil): the A/L technology-stream ICT volume teaches every office skill twice — once in Microsoft Office 2010 and once in LibreOffice (Writer/Calc/Impress 4.1) — and closes with creating a real Gmail account, mailbox folders, attachments and password safety; hardware goes down to cache levels L1/L2/L3, ROM types and file systems (FAT/NTFS/ext4).

Rwanda's own way (S2 ICT Student Book, KLB): the student companion to the S2 guide — the same nine units, taught through Rwandan financial life (mobile banking, ATMs, stock exchange) and Scratch game projects (Helicopter Game, Moving Eyes Game).
