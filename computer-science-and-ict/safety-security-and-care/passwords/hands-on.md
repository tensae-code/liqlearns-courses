# Hands-on — Passwords — making a strong one

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Password gym
Invent five passwords. Test each against the three rules and mark pass or fail. Rewrite every failed one until it passes — longer, mixed, unique.

## Door test
Draw the guide's comparison: an unlocked house door at night beside an unlocked computer screen. Under each, write the same consequence in your own words: anyone can get in.

## If it is hard
The guide's extension belongs to ready pupils: protect one personal file of your own with a password, with a teacher watching the first time.
