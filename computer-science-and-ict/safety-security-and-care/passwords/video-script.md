# Video script — Passwords — making a strong one

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A password proves you are you.
It is the first line of defence.

A weak password is an unlocked door.
Anyone can walk in.

Strong means: eight characters or more.
Small letters. CAPITAL letters. Numbers. Symbols.
A different one for the account.
And tell it to — no one. Ever.
