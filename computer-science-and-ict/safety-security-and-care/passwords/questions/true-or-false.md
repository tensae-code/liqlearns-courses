# Passwords — making a strong one — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** You may tell your password to a close friend.
**Answer: False** — never tell your password to anyone.

**2.** A strong password is hard for a person or a program to guess.
**Answer: True** — the guide's definition of strength.

**3.** Six characters is enough if they are all numbers.
**Answer: False** — at least 8, and mixed.
