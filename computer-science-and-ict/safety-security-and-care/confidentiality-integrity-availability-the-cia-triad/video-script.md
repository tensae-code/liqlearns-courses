# Video script — Confidentiality, integrity, availability — the CIA triad

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **Confidentiality, integrity, availability — the CIA triad**.

**confidentiality** — information available only to the intended audience — only authorised users can access it.

**integrity** — data that can be trusted — correct, authentic, reliable, not wrongly changed.

**availability** — information available to authorised users whenever they require it.

**the CIA triad** — the three security requirements: confidentiality, integrity and availability.
