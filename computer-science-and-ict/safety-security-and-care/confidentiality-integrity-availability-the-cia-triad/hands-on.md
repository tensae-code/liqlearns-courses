# Hands-on — Confidentiality, integrity, availability — the CIA triad

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Three promises poster
Draw a triangle. Write one principle at each corner and the mark-sheet example in the middle.

## Break one leg
In pairs: one pupil invents a small failure, the other names which leg of the triad broke and why.


## Habit audit
Audit your own (or your family's) devices against the nine habits, one line each: kept, or not yet. End with the single habit you will fix first, and why.
