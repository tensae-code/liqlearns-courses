# Confidentiality, integrity, availability — the CIA triad — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is confidentiality?
a) information available only to the intended audience — only authorised users can access it  b) data that can be trusted — correct, authentic, reliable, not wrongly changed  c) information available to authorised users whenever they require it  d) the three security requirements: confidentiality, integrity and availability
**Answer: a) information available only to the intended audience — only authorised users can access it**

**2.** What is integrity?
a) information available only to the intended audience — only authorised users can access it  b) information available to authorised users whenever they require it  c) the three security requirements: confidentiality, integrity and availability  d) data that can be trusted — correct, authentic, reliable, not wrongly changed
**Answer: d) data that can be trusted — correct, authentic, reliable, not wrongly changed**

**3.** What is availability?
a) data that can be trusted — correct, authentic, reliable, not wrongly changed  b) the three security requirements: confidentiality, integrity and availability  c) information available to authorised users whenever they require it  d) information available only to the intended audience — only authorised users can access it
**Answer: c) information available to authorised users whenever they require it**

**4.** What is the CIA triad?
a) information available to authorised users whenever they require it  b) the three security requirements: confidentiality, integrity and availability  c) information available only to the intended audience — only authorised users can access it  d) data that can be trusted — correct, authentic, reliable, not wrongly changed
**Answer: b) the three security requirements: confidentiality, integrity and availability**

**5.** A mark quietly changed by another pupil breaks —
a) availability only  b) integrity  c) the network  d) nothing
**Answer: b) integrity** — the data is no longer correct and trustworthy.

**6.** An ICT service for disabled students arriving late breaks —
a) confidentiality  b) availability  c) privacy  d) the triad's name
**Answer: b) availability** — it was not there when required.

**7.** How many habits does the book's anti-attack checklist hold?
a) five  b) seven  c) nine  d) twelve
**Answer: c) nine** — from strong passwords to avoiding scams.

**8.** Identity theft is reported to —
a) the local police  b) a computer technician  c) the internet café  d) nobody
**Answer: a) the local police** — crime goes to police; repairs to technicians.

**9.** Denial of service is the failure of —
a) confidentiality  b) integrity  c) availability  d) privacy
**Answer: c) availability** — the service cannot be reached.
