# Confidentiality, integrity, availability — the CIA triad — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is data that can be trusted — correct, authentic, reliable, not wrongly changed. (Name the term.)
**Answer: integrity**

**2.** _____ is information available to authorised users whenever they require it. (Name the term.)
**Answer: availability**

**3.** _____ is the three security requirements: confidentiality, integrity and availability. (Name the term.)
**Answer: the CIA triad**

**4.** Habit one: use ______ passwords.
**Answer: strong**

**5.** Protect your ______ — habit eight guards your identity online.
**Answer: e-identity**
