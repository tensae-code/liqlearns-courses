# Social media — what it gives a student, what it costs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is connectivity?
a) social media keeping people worldwide close — new friends, shared thoughts, family  b) pupils — especially shy ones — working with peers online more easily than speaking in class  c) scrolling and posting instead of attending and studying — a cost of social media  d) personal information (address, photo, phone number, birthday) and passwords
**Answer: a) social media keeping people worldwide close — new friends, shared thoughts, family**

**2.** What is collaboration online?
a) social media keeping people worldwide close — new friends, shared thoughts, family  b) scrolling and posting instead of attending and studying — a cost of social media  c) personal information (address, photo, phone number, birthday) and passwords  d) pupils — especially shy ones — working with peers online more easily than speaking in class
**Answer: d) pupils — especially shy ones — working with peers online more easily than speaking in class**

**3.** What is addiction?
a) pupils — especially shy ones — working with peers online more easily than speaking in class  b) personal information (address, photo, phone number, birthday) and passwords  c) scrolling and posting instead of attending and studying — a cost of social media  d) social media keeping people worldwide close — new friends, shared thoughts, family
**Answer: c) scrolling and posting instead of attending and studying — a cost of social media**

**4.** What is the two sharings never to make?
a) scrolling and posting instead of attending and studying — a cost of social media  b) personal information (address, photo, phone number, birthday) and passwords  c) social media keeping people worldwide close — new friends, shared thoughts, family  d) pupils — especially shy ones — working with peers online more easily than speaking in class
**Answer: b) personal information (address, photo, phone number, birthday) and passwords**

**5.** Monitoring underage pupils' exposure to inappropriate content is named as the responsibility of —
a) the platform only  b) parents  c) classmates  d) no one
**Answer: b) parents**

**6.** Sharing your password risks —
a) better grades  b) hacked accounts and misused information  c) faster Wi-Fi  d) nothing
**Answer: b) hacked accounts and misused information**
