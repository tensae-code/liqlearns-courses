# Social media — what it gives a student, what it costs — tf

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** True or false: connectivity — social media keeping people worldwide close — new friends, shared thoughts, family.
**Answer: True**

**2.** True or false: collaboration online — pupils — especially shy ones — working with peers online more easily than speaking in class.
**Answer: True**

**3.** True or false: addiction is the opposite: it has nothing to do with scrolling and posting instead of attending and studying — a cost of social media.
**Answer: False** — addiction is about scrolling and posting instead of attending and studying — a cost of social media.
