# Video script — Social media — what it gives a student, what it costs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **Social media — what it gives a student, what it costs**.

**connectivity** — social media keeping people worldwide close — new friends, shared thoughts, family.

**collaboration online** — pupils — especially shy ones — working with peers online more easily than speaking in class.

**addiction** — scrolling and posting instead of attending and studying — a cost of social media.

**the two sharings never to make** — personal information (address, photo, phone number, birthday) and passwords.

Now you try. Say each word. Say what it means.
