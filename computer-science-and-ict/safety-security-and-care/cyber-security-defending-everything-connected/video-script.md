# Video script — Cyber security — defending everything connected

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Everything is connected.
Power. Hospitals. Phones. Schools.

CYBER SECURITY guards them all.
Technologies. Processes. Controls.
Systems. Networks. Programs. Devices. Data.

The attacks aim high —
shutdowns, stolen medical records,
networks paralysed.
Mostly for profit. That is cybercrime.

Five guards stand together:
network, information, operational,
disaster recovery — and you, the user, educated.
