# Hands-on — Cyber security — defending everything connected

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Map the dome
Draw your town's connected systems — power, water, phones, hospital, bank, school. For each, write which of the five categories guards it most.

## Attacker's list, defender's answer
Take the guide's target list. For each target, write one defence from this unit's lessons — a habit, a tool, or a category.
