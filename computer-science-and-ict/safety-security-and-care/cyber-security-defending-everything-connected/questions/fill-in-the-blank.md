# Cyber security — defending everything connected — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1.** Cyber security aims to reduce attack ______ and stop unauthorised exploitation.
**Answer: risk**

**2.** Criminal activity involving a computer or network is ______.
**Answer: cybercrime**

**3.** Protecting individuals and families online is the work of ______-fighting.
**Answer: cybercrime**
