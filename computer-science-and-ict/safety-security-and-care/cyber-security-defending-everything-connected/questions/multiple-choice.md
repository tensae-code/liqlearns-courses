# Cyber security — defending everything connected — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Cyber security applies —
a) technologies, processes and controls  b) only passwords  c) only new computers  d) paper locks
**Answer: a) technologies, processes and controls** — all three kinds of defence.

**2.** Which is one of the book's cyber security categories?
a) disaster recovery  b) cooking  c) transport  d) farming
**Answer: a) disaster recovery** — with network, information, operational and end-user education.

**3.** Cyber-attacks can cause —
a) electrical shutdowns  b) better harvests  c) faster typing  d) cheaper computers
**Answer: a) electrical shutdowns** — one of the full-size stakes.

**4.** Cybercrime is carried out mostly —
a) for profit  b) for exercise  c) by mistake  d) for cleaning
**Answer: a) for profit** — some aims only at damaging machines.

**5.** Sensitive data named as a target includes —
a) medical records  b) shopping lists only  c) song lyrics  d) exam timetables
**Answer: a) medical records** — valuable, sensitive — and stealable.
