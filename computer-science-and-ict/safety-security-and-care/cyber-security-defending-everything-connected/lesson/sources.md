**Acceptance count: taught in 5 countries (Ethiopia, North Macedonia, Iraq, Cuba, Egypt).**

North Macedonia's own way (Year 2): a strikingly current first theme (cybersecurity, cryptography, blockchain, robotics, AI/ML and large language models) precedes deep C++ (data representation, functions, one-dimensional arrays), a full databases theme ending in SQL, multimedia with generative AI for graphics, and HTML web authoring.

Iraq's own way (Grade 10): the preparatory stage turns practical and examinable — a whole unit on computer maintenance (preventive vs corrective, hardware and software, hard-disk management with defragmentation, ScanDisk, partitions and FAT32 vs NTFS), then PowerPoint and Microsoft Publisher taught tab by tab, looping control structures in Scratch, and a final unit pairing HTML web-page building with information security.

Cuba's own way (Grade 9): Grade 9 is the formal programming-foundations year — algorithms, data, variables, counters/accumulators, flowcharts and pseudocode, taught language-independently — paired with a full cybersecurity chapter (passwords/MFA, safe internet, safe social networks).

Egypt's own way (Grade 10, Term 1 Programming and Artificial Intelligence student book): A full Japanese-style "Information Study" course adopted for Egypt's new secondary track: 13 chapters running from information theory, ethics and security through number systems, data analysis (frequency, variance, correlation, regression in spreadsheets), simulation and queues, Python, and a final AI-programming project chapter building web pages, a quiz app and a block-breaker game with generative AI.

Egypt's own way (Grade 9, Term 1 student book (Arabic)): Preparatory 3 goes deep on Python — computational thinking, AI algorithms, strings/lists, functions with parameters, input-error handling — then a second unit on AI and cybersecurity ending with deepfakes.
