# Hands-on — Ergonomic guidelines — eight habits at the computer

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The eight-point check
Walk to a workstation and check it against the eight guidelines, one by one, saying pass or fix for each. Fix everything you can fix on the spot — display position against reflections first.

## Reflection hunt
Look at a screen from where a pupil sits. Can you see a window or a lamp reflected in it? Turn the display or move it until the reflection is gone — that is guideline six done with your hands.
