# Ergonomic guidelines — eight habits at the computer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The eight guidelines aim to reduce —
a) stress, physical injury and eyestrain  b) homework  c) screen brightness only  d) typing speed
**Answer: a) stress, physical injury and eyestrain.**

**2.** When talking on the phone at the computer, use —
a) one hand on the phone  b) a hands-free headset  c) the speaker only  d) no phone ever
**Answer: b) a hands-free headset.**

**3.** The display position should be adjusted to prevent —
a) dust  b) reflections of overhead and outdoor lighting  c) sound  d) saving files
**Answer: b) reflections of overhead and outdoor lighting.**

**4.** The mouse should be kept —
a) far from the keyboard  b) on the floor  c) close to the keyboard  d) in a drawer
**Answer: c) close to the keyboard.**

**5.** The monitor should be —
a) as far away as possible  b) close enough to the eyes  c) behind you  d) on the floor
**Answer: b) close enough to the eyes.**
