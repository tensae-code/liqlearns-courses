# Video script — Ergonomic guidelines — eight habits at the computer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Eight habits. Count them with me.

Head and neck **upright**.
Face the screen **directly**.
Elbows **close** to your body.
A chair that holds your **lower back**.
Mouse **close** to the keyboard.
No **reflections** on the screen.
Monitor **close enough** to your eyes.
Talking on the phone? **Headset on.**

Eight habits — less stress, less injury, rested eyes.
