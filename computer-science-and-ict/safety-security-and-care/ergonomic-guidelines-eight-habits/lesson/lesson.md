# Ergonomic guidelines — eight habits at the computer

## Do it first

Look at the place where you sit at a computer. Before any list is given: how many good habits does that place already follow? Guess a number. This lesson's list has eight — check your guess against it.

## The eight guidelines

Tips to **reduce stress, physical injury and eyestrain** while using a computer:

1. **Keep head and neck upright.**
2. **Face the screen directly.**
3. **Keep elbows comfortably close to the body.**
4. **Use a chair that supports the lower back.**
5. **Keep the mouse close to the keyboard.**
6. **Adjust the display position to prevent reflections** of overhead and outdoor lighting on the screen.
7. **Put the monitor close enough to the eyes.**
8. **Use a hands-free headset when talking on the phone.**
