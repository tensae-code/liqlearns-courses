# Encryption and Firewalls — Protecting Data on Networks — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which two defences does this lesson teach together?**
**Answer: Encryption and the firewall**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does encryption do to data?**
**Answer: Scrambles it into a form an interceptor cannot read**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who can restore encrypted data?**
**Answer: Only the intended receiver, holding the means to unscramble it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does encryption protect?**
**Answer: The data itself, wherever it travels**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What danger does encryption answer?**
**Answer: Listening and copying in transit — interception on the network**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a firewall guard?**
**Answer: The gate between a network and the outside**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does a firewall decide?**
**Answer: It examines traffic in and out and allows or blocks it by its rules**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Complete the pair: the firewall decides what may pass; encryption decides…**
**Answer: What passing data reveals**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Are the two defences alternatives?**
**Answer: No — partners; a network needs both**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which books share this section?**
**Answer: Libya's Grade 12 IT books for both the Science and Humanities sections, Chapter 1**
