# Encryption and Firewalls — Protecting Data on Networks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which two defences does this lesson teach together?**
a) Encryption and the firewall  b) Scrambles it into a form an interceptor cannot read  c) Only the intended receiver, holding the means to unscramble it  d) The data itself, wherever it travels
**Answer: a) Encryption and the firewall**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does encryption do to data?**
a) Only the intended receiver, holding the means to unscramble it  b) Scrambles it into a form an interceptor cannot read  c) The data itself, wherever it travels  d) Listening and copying in transit — interception on the network
**Answer: b) Scrambles it into a form an interceptor cannot read**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who can restore encrypted data?**
a) The data itself, wherever it travels  b) Listening and copying in transit — interception on the network  c) Only the intended receiver, holding the means to unscramble it  d) The gate between a network and the outside
**Answer: c) Only the intended receiver, holding the means to unscramble it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does encryption protect?**
a) Listening and copying in transit — interception on the network  b) The gate between a network and the outside  c) It examines traffic in and out and allows or blocks it by its rules  d) The data itself, wherever it travels
**Answer: d) The data itself, wherever it travels**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What danger does encryption answer?**
a) Listening and copying in transit — interception on the network  b) The gate between a network and the outside  c) It examines traffic in and out and allows or blocks it by its rules  d) What passing data reveals
**Answer: a) Listening and copying in transit — interception on the network**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a firewall guard?**
a) It examines traffic in and out and allows or blocks it by its rules  b) The gate between a network and the outside  c) What passing data reveals  d) No — partners; a network needs both
**Answer: b) The gate between a network and the outside**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does a firewall decide?**
a) What passing data reveals  b) No — partners; a network needs both  c) It examines traffic in and out and allows or blocks it by its rules  d) Libya's Grade 12 IT books for both the Science and Humanities sections, Chapter 1
**Answer: c) It examines traffic in and out and allows or blocks it by its rules**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Complete the pair: the firewall decides what may pass; encryption decides…**
a) No — partners; a network needs both  b) Libya's Grade 12 IT books for both the Science and Humanities sections, Chapter 1  c) Encryption and the firewall  d) What passing data reveals
**Answer: d) What passing data reveals**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Are the two defences alternatives?**
a) No — partners; a network needs both  b) Libya's Grade 12 IT books for both the Science and Humanities sections, Chapter 1  c) Encryption and the firewall  d) Scrambles it into a form an interceptor cannot read
