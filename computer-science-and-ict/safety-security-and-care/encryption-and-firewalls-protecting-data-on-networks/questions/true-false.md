# Encryption and Firewalls — Protecting Data on Networks — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which two defences does this lesson teach together?" is: Encryption and the firewall**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does encryption do to data?" is: Only the intended receiver, holding the means to unscramble it**
**Answer: False** — Scrambles it into a form an interceptor cannot read

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Who can restore encrypted data?" is: Only the intended receiver, holding the means to unscramble it**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does encryption protect?" is: Listening and copying in transit — interception on the network**
**Answer: False** — The data itself, wherever it travels

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What danger does encryption answer?" is: Listening and copying in transit — interception on the network**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does a firewall guard?" is: It examines traffic in and out and allows or blocks it by its rules**
**Answer: False** — The gate between a network and the outside

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How does a firewall decide?" is: It examines traffic in and out and allows or blocks it by its rules**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Complete the pair: the firewall decides what may pass; encryption decides…" is: No — partners; a network needs both**
**Answer: False** — What passing data reveals

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Are the two defences alternatives?" is: No — partners; a network needs both**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which books share this section?" is: Encryption and the firewall**
**Answer: False** — Libya's Grade 12 IT books for both the Science and Humanities sections, Chapter 1
