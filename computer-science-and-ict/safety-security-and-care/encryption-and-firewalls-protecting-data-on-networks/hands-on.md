# Hands-on — Encryption and Firewalls — Protecting Data on Networks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Sealed or guarded?', 'For three messages — a password, a school mark list, a public notice — say whether each needs encryption, a firewall, both or neither, and why.')

## Task two
('Draw the road', 'Draw data travelling between two schools across the internet. Mark where a firewall stands and where encryption is applied, and label what each stops.')
