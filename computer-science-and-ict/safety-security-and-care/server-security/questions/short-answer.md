# Server Security — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why is the server the centre of network security?**
**Answer: It holds many people's data, always on and always reachable**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does an attacker gain from a server, against a workstation?**
**Answer: The building, not one desk**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who holds administrative accounts on a secured server?**
**Answer: Only the administrators, each under their own name**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why no shared master account?**
**Answer: Misuse could not be traced to a person — and the key, once leaked, opens everything**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) What does 'services are minimal' mean?**
**Answer: The server runs only the services it exists to give**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why is every extra service a risk?**
**Answer: Every extra service is an extra door**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name three more server defences from the lesson.**
**Answer: Patched software, backups kept apart, and a physically secured room and console**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why must logs be watched, not just kept?**
**Answer: An alarm that rings to an empty street protects nothing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In the chapter's order, what comes after server security?**
**Answer: Security of data sent through the network**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 10 ICT, Chapter 3 §3.1**
