# What security and computer security mean — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is security?
a) freedom from danger, and freedom from fear or anxiety  b) the protection of computer systems and information from harm, theft and unauthorised use  c) using a system or information in a way that is not allowed  d) Ethiopia's federal Information Network Security Agency, the national cyber-security agency
**Answer: a) freedom from danger, and freedom from fear or anxiety**

**2.** What is computer security?
a) freedom from danger, and freedom from fear or anxiety  b) using a system or information in a way that is not allowed  c) Ethiopia's federal Information Network Security Agency, the national cyber-security agency  d) the protection of computer systems and information from harm, theft and unauthorised use
**Answer: d) the protection of computer systems and information from harm, theft and unauthorised use**

**3.** What is unauthorised use?
a) the protection of computer systems and information from harm, theft and unauthorised use  b) Ethiopia's federal Information Network Security Agency, the national cyber-security agency  c) using a system or information in a way that is not allowed  d) freedom from danger, and freedom from fear or anxiety
**Answer: c) using a system or information in a way that is not allowed**

**4.** What is INSA?
a) using a system or information in a way that is not allowed  b) Ethiopia's federal Information Network Security Agency, the national cyber-security agency  c) freedom from danger, and freedom from fear or anxiety  d) the protection of computer systems and information from harm, theft and unauthorised use
**Answer: b) Ethiopia's federal Information Network Security Agency, the national cyber-security agency**

**5.** Using a textbook during an examination is —
a) authorised use  b) unauthorised use  c) computer security  d) a network service
**Answer: b) unauthorised use** — the same book is authorised in class but not in an exam.

**6.** Computer security works in two directions —
a) printing and scanning  b) preventing and detecting unauthorised use  c) buying and selling computers  d) typing and deleting
**Answer: b) preventing and detecting unauthorised use**
