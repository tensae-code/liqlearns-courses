# What security and computer security mean — fill

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** _____ is the protection of computer systems and information from harm, theft and unauthorised use. (Name the term.)
**Answer: computer security**

**2.** _____ is using a system or information in a way that is not allowed. (Name the term.)
**Answer: unauthorised use**

**3.** _____ is Ethiopia's federal Information Network Security Agency, the national cyber-security agency. (Name the term.)
**Answer: INSA**

**4.** InfoSec is short for ______ security.
**Answer: information**

**5.** Computer security is also called ______ security in this book.
**Answer: cyber**
