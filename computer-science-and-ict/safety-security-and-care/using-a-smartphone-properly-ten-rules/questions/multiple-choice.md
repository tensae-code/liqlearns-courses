# Using a smartphone properly — ten rules — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which is one of the ten rules?
a) Play games all the time  b) Do not use a mobile phone when crossing the road  c) Keep the phone loud in public  d) Browse every link
**Answer: b) Do not use a mobile phone when crossing the road.**

**2.** In public places, the phone should be —
a) on silent mode  b) at full volume  c) passed around  d) left ringing
**Answer: a) on silent mode.**

**3.** For calling, the rules say to use —
a) a friend's phone  b) a landline phone  c) two mobiles at once  d) the loudest phone
**Answer: b) a landline phone.**

**4.** The rules say NOT to text —
a) greetings  b) directions  c) insulting messages  d) homework answers
**Answer: c) insulting messages.**

**5.** The phone should be kept —
a) against the body  b) further from the body  c) under the pillow  d) in the shoe
**Answer: b) further from the body.**
