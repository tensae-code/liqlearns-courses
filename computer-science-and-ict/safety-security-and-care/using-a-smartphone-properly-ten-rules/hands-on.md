# Hands-on — Using a smartphone properly — ten rules

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Habit audit
Write your five most common phone habits. Hold each against the ten rules and mark it keep or change. Change one habit today — the road rule and the break rule are the usual first wins.

## Silent-mode drill
Practise putting a phone on silent as you 'enter' three public places you name — a classroom, a place of worship, a clinic waiting room. Entering = silent, every time.
