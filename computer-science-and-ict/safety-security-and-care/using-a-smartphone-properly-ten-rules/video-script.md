# Video script — Using a smartphone properly — ten rules

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A smartphone is commonplace. Use it **properly**.

Do not play games **all the time**.
Do not browse **unnecessary links**.
Do not capture and share photos **carelessly**.
No **insulting** texts.
Take a **break** from your phone.
Keep it **away** from your body.
**Landline** for long calls.
**Never** while crossing the road.
Away from heavy electrical equipment.
**Silent** in public places.

Ten rules. Count them. Keep them.
