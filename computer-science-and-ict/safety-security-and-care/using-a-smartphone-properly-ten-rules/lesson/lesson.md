# Using a smartphone properly — ten rules

## Do it first

Write down five things you actually do with a smartphone — the real habits, good and bad. This lesson's ten rules are the measure. Judge your five against them as you read.

## The ten rules

Tips to **reduce the health risks** of a device that has become commonplace:

1. **Do not play games all the time.**
2. **Avoid browsing unnecessary links.**
3. **Do not capture and share photos** (carelessly).
4. **Do not text insulting messages.**
5. **Take a break from your phone.**
6. **Keep the phone further from your body.**
7. **Use a landline phone for calling.**
8. **Do not use a mobile phone when crossing the road.**
9. **Do not use it in places with a lot of electrical equipment.**
10. **Keep the phone on silent mode in public places.**

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion all ten rules, in order, and which one your own habits break most.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the ten rules from memory.
2. A pupil texts while crossing the road, phone loud in class. Which two rules is he breaking?
3. Take your Do-it-first list: mark each habit keep or change, by rule number.
