# Cleaning a computer — why, how often, with what — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Clean the outside at least once a ______ with a dry cloth.
**Answer: week**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A ______ brush dusts electrical components.
**Answer: small**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Dust blocks air ______, so parts overheat.
