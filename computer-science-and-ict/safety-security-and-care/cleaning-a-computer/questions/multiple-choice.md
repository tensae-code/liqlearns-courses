# Cleaning a computer — why, how often, with what — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dust harms a computer because it —
a) looks untidy only  b) insulates, blocks air, overheats and can short-circuit or cause fire  c) makes it faster  d) cools it
**Answer: b) insulates, blocks air, overheats and can short-circuit or cause fire** — the book's chain of harms.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The outside of the computer is cleaned —
a) once a year  b) at least once a week, with a dry cloth  c) every day with water  d) never
**Answer: b) at least once a week, with a dry cloth** — weekly, dry cloth.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Cleaning helps the computer —
a) run smoothly and last longer  b) use more power  c) get heavier  d) need no care
**Answer: a) run smoothly and last longer** — smooth running and longer life.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The small brush is for —
a) painting  b) dusting electrical components  c) wiping the screen  d) cleaning CDs
**Answer: b) dusting electrical components** — brushing components.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Compressed air is used for —
a) blasting dust out  b) filling the case  c) cooling the room  d) inflating tyres
**Answer: a) blasting dust out** — blasting dust.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** CDs should be kept in cases and handled —
a) by their faces  b) by their edges  c) with wet hands  d) in sunlight
**Answer: b) by their edges** — cases, edges.
