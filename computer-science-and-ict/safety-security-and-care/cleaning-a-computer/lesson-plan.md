# Lesson plan — Cleaning a computer — why, how often, with what

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Safety, Security and Care · **Lesson:** Cleaning a computer — why, how often, with what
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 13 — Safety, Security and Care in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 7 book, Unit 5 — Security and Safety of Computer. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** cleaning keeps a computer running smoothly and lasting longer — dust insulates, blocks air and can short-circuit or cause fire — the outside is cleaned with a dry cloth at least once a week, with a kit of brush, wipers, compressed air and a CD kit.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the two safety lessons — no cleaning happens until safety and signs are known.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Why and how often** — cleaning helps the computer run smoothly and last longer; dust insulates, blocks air, overheats, can short-circuit or cause fire; the outside weekly with a dry cloth
2. **The cleaning kit** — small brush, cleaning wipers, compressed air, CD-cleaning kit — plus the guide's extras: microfiber cloths, screwdriver, cotton cloths

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Caring for a computer — safety before cleaning, and Safety signs and hazards in the computer lab.  Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Give dust's harms: insulates, blocks air, overheating, short-circuit, fire.
2. State the weekly rule: outside, dry cloth, at least once a week.
3. Match the four book kit items to their jobs.
4. Name the guide's three added items.

## What you need

## If the learner is stuck
- Wants to wet-wipe everything → the standing outside clean is a DRY cloth; wet cleaning belongs to the next lesson's careful device procedures.
- Thinks dust is only dirt → dust is insulation, an air-blocker, and a conductor: that is why it can kill a machine.
