# Hands-on — Cleaning a computer — why, how often, with what

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Weekly wipe
With the computer off and unplugged, do the weekly job: wipe the outside with a dry cloth. Note what came off, and where the dust was thickest.

## Kit audit
Lay out the cleaning kit available at school or home. Check it against the book's four items and the guide's three additions, and list what is missing.
