# Video script — Cleaning a computer — why, how often, with what

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dust is not harmless.
It wraps parts warm. It blocks the air.
Things overheat. Dust can short-circuit. Dust can burn.

Clean the outside every week. Dry cloth.
Brush. Wipers. Compressed air. CD kit.
Clean machines live longer.
