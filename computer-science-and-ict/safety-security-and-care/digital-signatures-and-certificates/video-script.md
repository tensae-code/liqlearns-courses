# Video script — Digital Signatures and Certificates

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A signature answers two questions. Who sent this? Was it changed?

Take a fingerprint of the message — a short number that changes if one letter changes.

Lock the fingerprint with your private key. That locked fingerprint is the signature.

Anyone can check it with your public key. Matching fingerprints: truly yours, truly unchanged.

But whose key is it, really? A certificate answers that.

A trusted authority signs the link between a name and its key. Now the key can be trusted.
