# Hands-on — Digital Signatures and Certificates

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Toy signatures
Agree a toy fingerprint rule (e.g. add the letters' alphabet positions, take the last two digits). Sign a message: write the message, its fingerprint, and the fingerprint 'locked' by writing it inside your own secret symbol only you draw. Swap messages, change one letter in one of them, and check both: the changed one's fingerprints must mismatch — catch it and name which proof failed.

## Meet a real certificate
On a browser, open a known shop or bank site and find its certificate (the padlock / tune icon, then the certificate details). Read who it names and which authority issued it, and when it expires. Write the chain in one line: site ← issuer.
