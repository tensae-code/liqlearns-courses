# Digital Signatures and Certificates — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A message's short computed fingerprint is called its ____.**
**Answer: hash**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The two things a valid signature proves are authorship and ____.**
**Answer: integrity**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A certificate is signed by a certificate ____.**
**Answer: authority**
