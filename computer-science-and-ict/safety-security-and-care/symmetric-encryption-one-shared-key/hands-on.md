# Hands-on — Symmetric Encryption — One Shared Key

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pass the note
Write a four-word message, encrypt it with shift key 5, and pass only the ciphertext across the room while a partner carries the key separately, by hand, in a folded note. Decrypt on arrival. Then discuss: which journey — ciphertext or key — was the dangerous one?

## Break a shift
Your teacher or partner encrypts a sentence with an unknown shift. Break it without the key: there are only 25 shifts to try. Time yourself — then say why a 128-bit key ends this game completely.
