# Symmetric Encryption — One Shared Key — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Readable text before encryption is called ____.**
**Answer: plaintext**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Encrypted, unreadable text is called ____.**
**Answer: ciphertext**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The problem of getting the shared key to both sides safely is the key-____ problem.**
**Answer: distribution**
