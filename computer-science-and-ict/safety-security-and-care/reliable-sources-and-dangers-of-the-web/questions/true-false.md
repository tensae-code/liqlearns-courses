# Reliable Sources and Dangers of the Web — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What four questions test a source?" is: Who wrote it, when, why — and whether others agree**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What kind of sites carry the most weight?" is: A claim, not a fact — until a second source confirms it**
**Answer: False** — A school, ministry or maker's own site

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "A claim found in only one place is…" is: A claim, not a fact — until a second source confirms it**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why does the 'why' question matter?" is: Its information is recent enough for the question being asked**
**Answer: False** — A page made to sell or deceive informs differently from one made to inform

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does 'in date' mean for a source?" is: Its information is recent enough for the question being asked**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name two dangers of the web." is: Steal your details by looking trusted**
**Answer: False** — False information, and pages imitating trusted ones to steal details

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do imitating pages try to do?" is: Steal your details by looking trusted**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the defence habit before sharing?" is: Tell a trusted adult**
**Answer: False** — Check the source first

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What should you do when something online frightens or pressures you?" is: Tell a trusted adult**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does sharing an unchecked claim do?" is: Who wrote it, when, why — and whether others agree**
**Answer: False** — It spreads the danger to everyone who receives it
