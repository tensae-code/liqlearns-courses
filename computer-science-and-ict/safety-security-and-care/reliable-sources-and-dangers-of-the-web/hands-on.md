# Hands-on — Reliable Sources and Dangers of the Web

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four-question check
Take one web page your class uses. Put it through the four questions — who, when, why, agreement — and give your verdict with reasons.

## Spot the imitation
Compare a trusted site's real page with a description of an imitation. List three signs that give the imitation away.
