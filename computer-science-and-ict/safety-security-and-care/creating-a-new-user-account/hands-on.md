# Hands-on — Creating a new user account

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Paper PC
Draw the four screens on four sheets. A partner 'clicks' by pointing; you turn the sheets in order and say what is typed on screen three.

## New pupil drill
A new pupil arrives mid-term. Run the whole creation on the school computer (or the paper PC), then find the new account in the list to prove it landed.
