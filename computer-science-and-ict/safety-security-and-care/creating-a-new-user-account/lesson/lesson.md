# Creating a new user account

## Do it first

A new pupil joins your class and needs an account on the school computer. Write the clicks you think it takes, in order. Then follow this lesson's steps exactly — doing-lessons keep the book's step order — and mark where your guess differed.

## The steps, in the book's order

From the accounts screens, in order:

1. Click **“Add a new user in PC settings”**.
2. Scroll down and click **“Add someone else to this PC”**.
3. Enter the **username**, the **passphrase**, and the **security questions and answers**.
4. Click **Next**.

Creation is complete: **the new account appears in the list**.

## Two facts that travel with the steps

First: **by default the new user has no administrator access** — the new account starts at the everyday level, and power is given later only if it is needed.

Second: a **Microsoft account** can also be used to create the account — but that route **requires an internet connection**. The local route above does not.
