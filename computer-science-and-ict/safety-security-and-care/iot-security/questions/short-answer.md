# IoT Security — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why is every smart thing called a door?**
**Answer: It is a computer — a way into its network**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three weaknesses things ship with.**
**Answer: Default passwords, rare updates, owners who think them too small to matter**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a botnet?**
**Answer: Thousands of captured things aimed together at others**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Through what might an attacker enter a whole network?**
**Answer: One weak thing — a camera, a toy**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What key opens a million doors in the opener?**
**Answer: The same factory (default) password**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the five standing measures.**
**Answer: Change defaults, update, segment the IoT network, switch off unneeded features, buy from updating makers**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why segment the IoT network?**
**Answer: So a captured thing cannot reach computers holding data**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What should be switched off?**
**Answer: Features and services a device does not need**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What should a buyer check about a maker?**
**Answer: That it publishes updates**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is security architecture, not decoration?**
**Answer: When it is decided inside the design**
