# Video script — Handling portable devices safely — calls, headsets, distance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your phone is in your hand all day. Handle it with care.

**Rule one.** Short calls. Long talks heat the head — and harm muscle, skin and ear.
**Rule two.** Headset on — or speakerphone. Keep the phone away from your head.
**Rule three.** The phone does not live on your body. Bag, not pocket. And at night — away from the bed, data off.

Three rules. Calls. Headsets. Distance.
