# Lesson plan — Handling portable devices safely — calls, headsets, distance

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Safety, Security and Care · **Lesson:** Handling portable devices safely — calls, headsets, distance
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 13 — Safety, Security and Care in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 8 book, Unit 5 — Computer Ergonomics. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the first three rules for careful handling of portable devices: avoid long conversations, use headsets or speakerphone, and keep mobile devices away from your body.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the ergonomics lessons — the body-first habit now extends from the desk to the phone in the hand.

## Sub lessons

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Ergonomic guidelines — eight habits at the computer. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State rules 1–3 for handling portable devices safely.
2. Say why long conversations without proper headphones are harmful.
3. Say where a phone should rest while sleeping, and what is switched off.

## What you need
A phone (real or drawn) and a bag to practise the distance rule.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner times how long their longest phone call this week was, and says where their phone sleeps at night (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the parts below, in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 12307]: a picture of three panels — a short call timer, a headset in use, a phone resting in a bag away from a bed — the student matches each rule to its panel.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading rules 1–3 aloud — the student holds up one, two or three fingers as each rule is read.
3. **Say it to your companion** — explain the lesson back in your own words (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson retold in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, fill-in-the-blank — served at the learner's level (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a headset is only for music → in this lesson the headset is protection: it keeps the phone's heating effect away from the head during calls.
