# Handling portable devices safely — calls, headsets, distance

## Do it first

Think of the longest phone call you made or took this week. How long was it? And last night — where was the phone while you slept? Keep both answers. The first three rules for portable devices speak directly to them.

## Why portable devices need care

Portable devices are part of daily life — yet smartphones cause inconvenience and can cause **serious health issues with long-term use of handheld devices**. The handling rules begin with calls.

**Rule 1 — Avoid long conversations.** Long use of phones without proper headphones causes a **heating effect on brain cells**, and long conversation can **harm muscle, skin and ear**.

**Rule 2 — Use headsets or the speakerphone option.** Good wired or wireless headsets **reduce the risk of harming brain cells**; use speakerphone whenever possible.

**Rule 3 — Keep mobile devices away from your body.** Avoid direct contact; **carry the device in a bag**; and while sleeping, **keep it away from the bed, with mobile data and wireless options switched off**.
