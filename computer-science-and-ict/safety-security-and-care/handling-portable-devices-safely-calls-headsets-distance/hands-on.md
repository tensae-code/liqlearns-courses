# Hands-on — Handling portable devices safely — calls, headsets, distance

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The night place
Choose tonight's resting place for a phone: in a bag or across the room, away from the bed, with mobile data and wireless switched off. Set it up once for real and describe what you changed.

## Headset check
With a headset (or an imagined one), show the two safe ways to take a long call: headset in, or speakerphone with the phone on the table — never pressed to the ear for a long talk.
