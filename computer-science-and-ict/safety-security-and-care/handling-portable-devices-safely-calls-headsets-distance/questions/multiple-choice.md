# Handling portable devices safely — calls, headsets, distance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Rule 1 for portable devices is —
a) make longer calls  b) avoid long conversations  c) call only at night  d) never use a phone
**Answer: b) avoid long conversations.**

**2.** Long phone use without proper headphones causes —
a) a heating effect on brain cells  b) better hearing  c) faster charging  d) clearer sound
**Answer: a) a heating effect on brain cells.**

**3.** Rule 2 says to use —
a) the loudest volume  b) headsets or the speakerphone option  c) two phones  d) a thinner case
**Answer: b) headsets or the speakerphone option.**

**4.** While sleeping, the phone should be —
a) under the pillow  b) in your hand  c) away from the bed, with mobile data and wireless off  d) charging on the bed
**Answer: c) away from the bed, with mobile data and wireless off.**

**5.** Rule 3 says to carry the device —
a) pressed to the ear  b) in a bag, away from direct body contact  c) in a tight pocket all day  d) in your shoe
**Answer: b) in a bag, away from direct body contact.**
