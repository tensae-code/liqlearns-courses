# Handling portable devices safely — calls, headsets, distance — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1.** Good wired or wireless ______ reduce the risk of harming brain cells.
**Answer: headsets.**

**2.** Long use of phones without headphones causes a ______ effect on brain cells.
**Answer: heating.**

**3.** Smartphones can cause serious health issues with ______ use of handheld devices.
**Answer: long-term.**
