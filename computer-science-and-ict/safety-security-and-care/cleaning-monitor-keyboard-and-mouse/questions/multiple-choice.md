# Cleaning monitor, keyboard and mouse — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The monitor procedure's order is —
a) wipe, unplug, off  b) off, unplug, wipe with a soft cloth moistened with water  c) spray, wipe, plug in  d) unplug, spray, on
**Answer: b) off, unplug, wipe with a soft cloth moistened with water** — off → unplug → wipe.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why never spray the screen directly?
a) It wastes liquid  b) Liquid can leak in and damage internal parts  c) It cleans too well  d) The book forbids fun
**Answer: b) Liquid can leak in and damage internal parts** — sprayed liquid leaks inside.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sticky dirt on a keyboard is removed with —
a) a wet mop  b) cotton moistened with alcohol, gently around the keys  c) a knife  d) compressed water
**Answer: b) cotton moistened with alcohol, gently around the keys** — alcohol cotton, gently.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What gathers in a keyboard?
a) Only dust  b) Dust, hair and lint  c) Sand only  d) Nothing
**Answer: b) Dust, hair and lint** — dust, hair and lint.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The mouse procedure's order is —
a) wipe, dry, unplug  b) unplug, wipe top and bottom, dry, then reconnect  c) reconnect, wipe, unplug  d) spray, wipe, connect
**Answer: b) unplug, wipe top and bottom, dry, then reconnect** — unplug → wipe → dry → reconnect.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Water is dangerous in cleaning because —
a) it is heavy  b) it conducts electricity  c) it is cold  d) it smells
**Answer: b) it conducts electricity** — water conducts electricity — risk of serious injury or death.
