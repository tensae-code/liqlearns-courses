# Cleaning monitor, keyboard and mouse — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Never ______ liquid directly on the screen.
**Answer: spray**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Wipe the keyboard gently with cotton moistened with ______.
**Answer: alcohol**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Let all parts ______ before reconnecting the mouse.
**Answer: dry**
