# Video script — Cleaning monitor, keyboard and mouse

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three devices. Three procedures. Orders matter.

Monitor: off. Unplug. Soft cloth, a little water. Never spray the screen.
Keyboard: cotton with alcohol, gently around the keys.
Mouse: unplug. Wipe top and bottom. Dry fully. Then reconnect.

Water and electricity never mix.
Unplug first. Dry last.
