# Lesson plan — Cleaning monitor, keyboard and mouse

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Safety, Security and Care · **Lesson:** Cleaning monitor, keyboard and mouse
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 13 — Safety, Security and Care in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 7 book, Unit 5 — Security and Safety of Computer. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the book's procedures, in order: monitor — off, unplug, wipe with a soft cloth moistened with water, never spray the screen; keyboard — cotton with alcohol around the keys; mouse — unplug, wipe with alcohol cloth, dry fully before reconnecting.
**This lesson's own level:** it is a doing-lesson — it stands on the safety precautions and the cleaning-kit lessons, and it uses both.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Cleaning the monitor** — off → unplug → wipe with a soft clean cloth moistened with water; never spray liquid directly on the screen
2. **Cleaning the keyboard** — dust, hair and lint gather in it; sticky dirt is removed with cotton moistened with alcohol, wiped gently around the keys
3. **Cleaning the mouse** — unplug → wipe top and bottom with cotton cloth moistened with rubbing alcohol → let all parts dry before reconnecting

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Cleaning a computer — why, how often, with what.  Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Clean a monitor in order, and state the no-spray rule.
2. Clean a keyboard the book's way, gently, with alcohol cotton.
3. Clean a mouse in order, and reconnect only when dry.
4. Say why wet cleaning of electronics is dangerous.

## What you need
The safety precautions applied; soft cloth, water, cotton, rubbing alcohol; devices off and unplugged.

## If the learner is stuck
- Reaches for a spray bottle for the screen → the liquid goes on the cloth, never sprayed on the screen: sprayed liquid leaks inside.
- Reconnects a damp mouse → parts dry fully first; water conducts electricity.
