# Hands-on — Cleaning monitor, keyboard and mouse

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Monitor procedure, watched
Clean a real monitor following the strip exactly: off, unplug, damp soft cloth. A partner reads each step aloud and ticks it only when done in order.

## Keyboard detail
Turn a disconnected keyboard's cleaning into a checklist: brush out dust and lint first, then alcohol cotton gently around the stickiest keys. Compare before and after in good light.

## If it is hard
Every cleaning here is observed doing, as the guide directs for practicals: work with a partner reading the steps, and ask the teacher to check your order before you begin — especially the unplug-first step.
