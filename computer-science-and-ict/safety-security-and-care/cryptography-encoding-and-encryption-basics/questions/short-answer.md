# Cryptography — Encoding and Encryption Basics — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does encoding do?**
**Answer: Rewrites information in another agreed form**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Is encoding about secrecy?**
**Answer: No — about form; anyone with the code can read it back**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give an example of encoding from the lesson.**
**Answer: Letters becoming numbers, or numbers becoming binary**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why must a computer encode before it can protect?**
**Answer: Protection works on the represented form of the information**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Who can decode an encoded message?**
**Answer: Anyone who knows the agreed code**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is plaintext?**
**Answer: The readable message before encryption**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is ciphertext?**
**Answer: The scrambled, unreadable result of encryption**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does decryption need?**
**Answer: The right key**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What makes modern keys safe?**
**Answer: They are long enough that guessing is hopeless**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name two things encryption protects.**
**Answer: Banking and messaging (any two from the lesson)**
