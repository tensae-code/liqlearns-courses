# Hands-on — Cryptography — Encoding and Encryption Basics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Encode and swap
Agree a simple letter-to-number code with a partner. Encode a short message, swap papers, and decode each other's. Note: anyone with your code sheet could do this — that is the point.

## Key or no key
Write two short messages: one only encoded, one described as encrypted with a key only you hold. Under each, write who could read it and why.
