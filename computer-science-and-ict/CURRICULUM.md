# Computer Science and ICT course — CURRICULUM (the path)

Computer Science and ICT is one world course, organised by
**levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they are ready, not
when a school calendar says so. The school grades the books come
from appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as
the course's organisation. The path climbs from the idea of
information itself, to the machine, to the programs, to the
learner's own files and documents, to the connected world, and
at the top to the newest technologies and the society questions
they raise (see TITLES.md): **First Steps → Hardware → Software
→ Files and Folders → Word Processing → Spreadsheets →
Presentations → Internet and the Web → Networks → Multimedia →
Databases → Programming → Safety, Security and Care → Computers
in Society → New Technologies**.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — the first stocked way
is Ethiopia's, from the federal Information Technology student
textbooks and teacher guides for Grades 7–12 — except the **Tip
boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns.
Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the
full book list and the acceptance reckoning.
