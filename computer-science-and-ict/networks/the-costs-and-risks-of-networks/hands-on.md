# Hands-on — The costs and risks of networks

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Germ on the lines
On a drawn star network, trace a virus from one machine to all others in steps. At each step, the class names one place it could have been stopped (Unit 5 will teach how).

## Cost it
List what a small school network costs beyond cables: setup, administration, skilled people. Present the list as the network's price tag.
