## Do it first

## The same openness carries costs

**Viruses and malware spread easily** between networked computers — watch the GIF hop machine to machine. **Remote accessibility introduces vulnerabilities** endangering the **computer, its users, the data, the organisation and the country**. Networks are **complex to set up and manage**, needing **experienced or certified people**, and **setup and administration cost money**. And **social media and unsafe Internet use waste young people's time** and expose them to **cyberbullying, disinformation/misinformation and identity theft** — developed fully in Unit 5.
