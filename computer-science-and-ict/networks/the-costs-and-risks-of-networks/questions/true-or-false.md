# The costs and risks of networks — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** Setup and administration of networks cost money.
**Answer: True** — a named cost.

**2.** A vulnerability can endanger a whole organisation, not only one computer.
**Answer: True** — computer, users, data, organisation and country are named.

**3.** Networks are simple to manage with no experience.
**Answer: False** — complex — experienced people are needed.
