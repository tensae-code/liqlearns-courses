# The costs and risks of networks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** On a network, viruses and malware —
a) cannot spread  b) spread easily between computers  c) spread only by paper  d) die at the switch
**Answer: b) spread easily between computers** — they spread easily.

**2.** Remote accessibility introduces —
a) free hardware  b) vulnerabilities  c) extra printers  d) faster cables only
**Answer: b) vulnerabilities** — vulnerabilities.

**3.** Networks need, to set up and manage them —
a) no skill  b) experienced or certified people  c) only pupils  d) no money
**Answer: b) experienced or certified people** — experienced or certified people.

**4.** Unsafe Internet use exposes young people to —
a) cyberbullying, disinformation and identity theft  b) faster homework only  c) free devices  d) better eyesight
**Answer: a) cyberbullying, disinformation and identity theft** — the three named harms.

**5.** The social risks are developed fully in —
a) Unit 2  b) Unit 5  c) Unit 1  d) the glossary
**Answer: b) Unit 5** — Unit 5.
