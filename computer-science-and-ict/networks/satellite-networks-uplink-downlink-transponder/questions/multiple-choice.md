# Satellite networks — uplink, downlink, transponder — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A satellite network's special strength is —
a) low price  b) reach — transmission to any location however remote  c) speed indoors  d) cable protection
**Answer: b) reach — transmission to any location however remote** — reach to remote locations.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The uplink carries the signal —
a) satellite to earth  b) earth to satellite  c) phone to phone  d) tower to tower
**Answer: b) earth to satellite** — Earth to satellite (keyed in the guide).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The transponder is —
a) a ground antenna  b) the satellite's combined receiver-transmitter  c) a kind of cell  d) a billing office
**Answer: b) the satellite's combined receiver-transmitter** — receiver-transmitter in one.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** EDUSAT is —
a) a weather satellite  b) a satellite dedicated to education  c) Ethiopia's phone company  d) an orbit type
**Answer: b) a satellite dedicated to education** — dedicated to education.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** ETRSS-1 is Ethiopia's first —
a) geostationary satellite  b) low-Earth-orbit, Earth-observation satellite  c) radio station  d) base station
**Answer: b) low-Earth-orbit, Earth-observation satellite** — first LEO satellite, for Earth observation.
