# Satellite networks — uplink, downlink, transponder — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.


**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The signal's journey is uplink, ______, downlink.
**Answer: transponder**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Earth ______ are the ground ends of the system.
**Answer: stations**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** ______ is the satellite system giving navigation and clock synchronisation.
**Answer: GPS**


**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A satellite network divides the planet into ______ like a cellular network.
**Answer: cells**
