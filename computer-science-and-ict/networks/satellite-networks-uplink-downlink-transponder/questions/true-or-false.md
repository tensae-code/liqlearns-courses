# Satellite networks — uplink, downlink, transponder — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Satellite networks need huge ground infrastructure in every village they serve.
**Answer: False** — their strength is reach without it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The downlink carries the signal from satellite to earth.
**Answer: True** — satellite to Earth.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A satellite's power system is its solar panels.
**Answer: True** — keyed in the guide.
