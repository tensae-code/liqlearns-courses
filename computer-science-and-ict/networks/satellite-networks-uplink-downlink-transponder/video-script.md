# Video script — Satellite networks — uplink, downlink, transponder

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A satellite network reaches anywhere on earth.

The earth station sends the signal UP — the uplink.
The satellite's transponder receives it — and sends it back.
DOWN — the downlink — to any place, however remote.

Up. Relay. Down.
