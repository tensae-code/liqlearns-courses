# Lesson plan — Satellite networks — uplink, downlink, transponder

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Networks · **Lesson:** Satellite networks — uplink, downlink, transponder
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Networks in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 10 book, Unit 2 — Computer Network. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A **satellite network** carries communication **from any point on earth to any other**: **up** the uplink, **relayed by the transponder**, **down** the downlink.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the wireless lessons — the satellite is the unguided medium with the widest reach of all.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Nodes that reach anywhere** — the nodes; cells like cellular; reach to remote areas
2. **Uplink, transponder, downlink** — earth stations, uplink, downlink, transponder
3. **Ethiopia in space (teacher guide — evidence)** — EDUSAT; ETRSS-1; a satellite's three systems; GPS

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: How wireless signals travel — ground, sky and line-of-sight. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the satellite network's nodes and its special strength.
2. Describe the signal's journey: uplink, transponder, downlink.
3. Name EDUSAT and ETRSS-1 and what each does, from the guide's key.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Shout a message up to a friend on a high roof, who shouts it down to someone far on the other side. Up, relay, down — you have just run this lesson's network. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the parts in lesson/lesson.md, in order.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 14531]: a picture of an earth station beaming up to a satellite and the satellite beaming down to a remote village — the student traces the signal's whole journey.

   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key words and its one-thing sentence aloud — the student listens and repeats each key word.
3. **Say it to your companion** — explain the lesson back in your own words (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson retold in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, fill-in-the-blank — served at the learner's level (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
