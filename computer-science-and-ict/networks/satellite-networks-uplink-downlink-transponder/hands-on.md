# Hands-on — Satellite networks — uplink, downlink, transponder

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.


## Roof relay
Three students: sender, satellite (on a chair), receiver far away. The satellite must repeat every message exactly — transponder rules — before it may come down.

## Coverage map
Shade a map of Ethiopia's remote and pastoral areas. Write why satellite coverage serves them best, in the guide's words.
