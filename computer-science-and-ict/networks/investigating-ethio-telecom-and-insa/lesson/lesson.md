## Sub lesson 2 — The Dia diagram tool

Search the **Ethiopian Information Network Security Agency (INSA)** site for cyber attacks a student may meet, and discuss how to protect yourselves. The expected findings (teacher guide — evidence): **Misinformation/disinformation** — question the source and intent, check other reliable sources, think before sharing. **Cyberbullying** — monitoring, awareness, platform reporting tools, empathy. **Sex trafficking** — question the source of a job opportunity; healthy relationships and safe homes. **Hate crime** — monitor and report, condemn, strengthen enforcement. **Financial scams** — never click links or give account details, never give account information by phone, inspect an ATM's card reader before use.

Explore the **Dia diagram tool's network-device symbols and functions** — the drawing tool from the earlier practical, now for its symbol library.
