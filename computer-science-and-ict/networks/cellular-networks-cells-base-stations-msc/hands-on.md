# Hands-on — Cellular networks — cells, base stations, MSC

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.


## Ethio telecom visit (the book's field practical)
Visit a local Ethio telecom office, or interview someone who has, and summarise the services of a cellular system: cells, base stations, switching.

## Honeycomb map
Draw a honeycomb of cells over your town. Walk a dot (a caller) across five cells and mark each handover with an X.
