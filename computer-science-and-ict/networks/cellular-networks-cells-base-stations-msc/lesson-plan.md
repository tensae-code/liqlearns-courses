# Lesson plan — Cellular networks — cells, base stations, MSC

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Networks · **Lesson:** Cellular networks — cells, base stations, MSC
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Networks in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 10 book, Unit 2 — Computer Network. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A **cellular network** divides its area into **cells**, each served by a **base station**, all coordinated by a **mobile switching centre (MSC)** that hands calls over as callers move.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the wireless lessons — the cellular network is unguided media organised into a moving system.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Cells and base stations** — cells, base stations, tracking, channel assignment, handover
2. **The mobile switching centre** — the MSC connects calls, records information, handles billing, links to the telephone office
3. **Handoffs and WiMax keyed (teacher guide — evidence)** — hard vs soft handoff; WiMax defined

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Microwaves — aimed beams, dish and horn antennas. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how a cellular network divides its area and what serves each part.
2. List the MSC's jobs.
3. Tell hard from soft handoff and define WiMax from the guide's key.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Cell — the area. Base station — the tower serving it. MSC — the office above them all, connecting, recording, billing. Movement is handled by handing the call to the next base station.
