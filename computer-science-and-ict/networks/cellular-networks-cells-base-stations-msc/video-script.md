# Video script — Cellular networks — cells, base stations, MSC

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A mobile network is built of cells.

Each cell has a base station — its tower.
It finds you. It gives you a channel.
You move — it hands your call to the next tower.

Above them all, the switching centre:
connecting calls, recording, billing.
