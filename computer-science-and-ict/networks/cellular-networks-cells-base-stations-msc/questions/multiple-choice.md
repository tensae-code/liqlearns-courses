# Cellular networks — cells, base stations, MSC — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cellular network divides its service area into —
a) provinces  b) cells  c) channels only  d) orbits
**Answer: b) cells** — small regions called cells.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Each cell is served by —
a) a satellite  b) a base station  c) a modem  d) an access point only
**Answer: b) a base station** — a fixed transceiver — the base station.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The MSC —
a) repairs phones  b) connects calls, records call information and handles billing  c) makes SIM cards  d) prints bills only by hand
**Answer: b) connects calls, records call information and handles billing** — the computerised coordinating office.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The MSC links the cells to —
a) the ionosphere  b) the telephone central office  c) the school LAN  d) a terminator
**Answer: b) the telephone central office** — the telephone central office.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In a soft handoff, a mobile talks to —
a) no base station  b) one base station  c) two base stations at once  d) a satellite
**Answer: c) two base stations at once** — two at once; hard handoff is one at a time.
