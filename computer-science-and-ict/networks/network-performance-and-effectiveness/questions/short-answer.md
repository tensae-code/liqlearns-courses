# Network Performance and Effectiveness — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four performance measures.**
**Answer: Bandwidth, throughput, delay (latency), reliability**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is bandwidth?**
**Answer: How much data the road can carry**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is delay?**
**Answer: How long a message takes to cross**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does reliability measure?**
**Answer: How often the network works, whole and unharmed**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why teach measures beside networks?**
**Answer: So complaints become findings — 'delay is high at noon'**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does effectiveness ask?**
**Answer: Whether the network serves its users' actual work**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give the 3 a.m. example's point.**
**Answer: Performance nobody needs is not effectiveness**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When does the example network fail effectively?**
**Answer: Thirty pupils at 9 a.m.**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is performance improved?**
**Answer: Find the narrow place and widen that, not everything**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name three narrow places.**
**Answer: An old device, a busy hour, a long cable**
