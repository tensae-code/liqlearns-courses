# Network Performance and Effectiveness — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four performance measures.** → **Bandwidth, throughput, delay (latency), reliability**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is bandwidth?** → **How much data the road can carry**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is delay?** → **How long a message takes to cross**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does reliability measure?** → **How often the network works, whole and unharmed**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why teach measures beside networks?** → **So complaints become findings — 'delay is high at noon'**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does effectiveness ask?** → **Whether the network serves its users' actual work**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give the 3 a.m. example's point.** → **Performance nobody needs is not effectiveness**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When does the example network fail effectively?** → **Thirty pupils at 9 a.m.**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is performance improved?** → **Find the narrow place and widen that, not everything**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name three narrow places.** → **An old device, a busy hour, a long cable**
