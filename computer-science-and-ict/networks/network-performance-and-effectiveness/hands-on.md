# Hands-on — Network Performance and Effectiveness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Measure, don't argue** — Measure one school task (opening a page, sending a file) at two times of day and report with measures, not adjectives.

## Task two
**Widen the narrow place** — A class's video calls freeze every morning. List the candidate narrow places and the test that would convict each.
