# Ring topology — and the four shapes compared — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a ring, each node connects to —
a) every other node  b) its two neighbours  c) one switch  d) the bus
**Answer: b) its two neighbours** — its two neighbours.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Intermediate ring nodes act as —
a) printers  b) repeaters, re-sending at full strength  c) hubs  d) routers
**Answer: b) repeaters, re-sending at full strength** — repeaters.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ring traffic flows —
a) both ways equally  b) in one direction  c) through a switch  d) only when asked
**Answer: b) in one direction** — in one direction.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** One failed node in a ring —
a) is bypassed  b) stops the whole ring  c) slows the ring slightly  d) becomes the switch
**Answer: b) stops the whole ring** — stops the whole ring.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which shape's centre failing stops everything?
a) Bus  b) Mesh  c) Star  d) PAN
**Answer: c) Star** — the star's switch.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Which shape uses one long backbone cable?
a) Mesh  b) Ring  c) Star  d) Bus
**Answer: d) Bus** — the bus.
