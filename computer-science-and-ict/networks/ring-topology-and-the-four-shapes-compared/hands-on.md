# Hands-on — Ring topology — and the four shapes compared

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Human ring
Pass a message one way around a circle of learners; each passer-on repeats it at full voice (the repeater job). Then one learner “fails” — watch the ring stop.

## Pick your weakness
For a small office, a school lab, and a tiny home network, pick a topology and defend it from the comparison table.
