# Hands-on — Satellite orbits — GEO, MEO, LEO

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.


## Shelf drawing
Draw the earth and three rings to rough scale order (not to true scale). Shade the two belts and write 'destroyed here' inside them.

## Sort the satellites
Cards: ETRSS-1 (LEO, from the satellite lesson), a GEO broadcast satellite, a MEO navigation satellite. Sort them onto the three shelves.
