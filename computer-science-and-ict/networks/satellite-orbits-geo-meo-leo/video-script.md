# Video script — Satellite orbits — GEO, MEO, LEO

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Satellites sit on three shelves.

Low: LEO — below two thousand kilometres.
Middle: MEO — five to fifteen thousand.
High: GEO — one single height, 35,786 kilometres.

Between the shelves: the Van Allen belts.
Charged particles. A satellite inside them is destroyed.
