# Satellite orbits — GEO, MEO, LEO — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Polar is one of the three orbit paths.
**Answer: True** — equatorial, inclined, polar.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** MEO sits inside a Van Allen belt.
**Answer: False** — MEO sits between the belts.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The gaps between the orbit shelves are empty by accident.
**Answer: False** — the belts bar them.
