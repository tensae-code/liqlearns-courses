# Satellite orbits — GEO, MEO, LEO — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A GEO satellite's orbit is equatorial because it must —
a) avoid the belts  b) stay fixed over one spot  c) save power  d) see both poles
**Answer: b) stay fixed over one spot** — keyed in the guide.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** GEO's single altitude is —
a) 2,000 km  b) 15,000 km  c) 35,786 km  d) 5,000 km
**Answer: c) 35,786 km** — 35,786 km.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** MEO lies between —
a) 0 and 2,000 km  b) 5,000 and 15,000 km  c) 20,000 and 30,000 km  d) 35,786 and 40,000 km
**Answer: b) 5,000 and 15,000 km** — between 5,000 and 15,000 km.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** LEO is normally —
a) above GEO  b) below 2,000 km  c) between the belts  d) at 35,786 km
**Answer: b) below 2,000 km** — below 2,000 km.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The Van Allen belts would —
a) speed a satellite up  b) destroy a satellite orbiting inside them  c) charge its batteries usefully  d) guide its signal
**Answer: b) destroy a satellite orbiting inside them** — charged particles destroy it.
