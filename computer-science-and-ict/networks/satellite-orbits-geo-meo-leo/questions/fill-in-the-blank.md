# Satellite orbits — GEO, MEO, LEO — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.


**1. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ Earth orbit sits at the single altitude of 35,786 km.
**Answer: Geostationary**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** LEO stands for ______ Earth orbit.
**Answer: low**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The belts are named Van ______ belts.
**Answer: Allen**


**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The three orbit paths are equatorial, inclined and ______.
**Answer: polar**
