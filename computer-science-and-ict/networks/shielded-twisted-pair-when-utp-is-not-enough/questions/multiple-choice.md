# Shielded twisted pair — when UTP is not enough — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Crosstalk is —
a) light leaking from fibre  b) interference neighbouring copper wires cause each other  c) a kind of connector  d) a wireless signal
**Answer: b) interference neighbouring copper wires cause each other** — neighbouring wires troubling each other.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In STP, each pair is wrapped in —
a) plastic only  b) metallic foil  c) glass  d) rubber braid
**Answer: b) metallic foil** — each pair in metallic foil.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** STP cuts EMI/RFI —
a) from inside the cable  b) from outside  c) only at the receiver  d) by twisting alone
**Answer: b) from outside** — shielding cuts outside EMI/RFI; cancellation and twisting handle the inside.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** STP serves —
a) only satellite links  b) fast Ethernet and telephone voice/data channels  c) only television  d) only fibre backbones
**Answer: b) fast Ethernet and telephone voice/data channels** — fast Ethernet and telephone channels.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The guide keys UTP as —
a) expensive and hard to install  b) cheap and easy to install, but limited to shorter distances  c) always faster than STP  d) shielded
**Answer: b) cheap and easy to install, but limited to shorter distances** — cheap and easy, limited by attenuation and interference.
