# Shielded twisted pair — when UTP is not enough — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ______ is the interference neighbouring copper wires cause each other.
**Answer: Crosstalk**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** All four pairs together in STP are wrapped in an overall ______ or foil.
**Answer: braid**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** STP cuts crosstalk from ______ and EMI/RFI from outside.
**Answer: inside**


**4. (Sub lesson 3)** STP serves fast ______ and telephone voice/data channels.
**Answer: Ethernet**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** STP is preferred where interference sources are ______.
**Answer: many**
