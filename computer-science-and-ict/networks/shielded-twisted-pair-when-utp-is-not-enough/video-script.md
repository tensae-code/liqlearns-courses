# Video script — Shielded twisted pair — when UTP is not enough

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Wires can trouble each other. That trouble is crosstalk.

Twisting helps. But some places are full of interference.
Motors. Radios. Transmitters.

There, use STP — shielded twisted pair.
Foil wraps each pair. A braid wraps them all.
Crosstalk from inside — cut. EMI and RFI from outside — cut.

STP serves fast Ethernet and telephone channels.
