# Hands-on — Shielded twisted pair — when UTP is not enough

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.


## Shield hunt
List every interference source you can find in the school (motors, radios, wireless devices). For each cable run past them, say: UTP or STP — and why.

## Draw the cut-open
Draw STP in cross-section: four pairs, foil round each, braid round all. Label every layer.
