# Video script — IP addresses — IPv4 and IPv6

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every device needs an address of its own.

The Internet Protocol sets the rules.
The IP address names the device — and where it sits.

IPv4: thirty-two bits.
Four numbers, dots between them. Zero to 255 each.
Like this: 94 . 176 . 117 . 21.
