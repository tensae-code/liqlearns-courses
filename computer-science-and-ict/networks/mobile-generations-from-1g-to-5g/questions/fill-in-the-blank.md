# Mobile generations — from 1G to 5G — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.


**1. (Sub lesson 2)** 4G ______ is the type promising 100 Mbps transfer.
**Answer: LTE**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** 5G promises ______-low latency.
**Answer: ultra**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** GSM enables ______G in the guide's map.
**Answer: 2**


**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Mobile generations are ______ — 1G, 2G, 3G and on.
**Answer: numbered**
