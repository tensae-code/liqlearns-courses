# Mobile generations — from 1G to 5G — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book places the present at —
a) 3G  b) 4G, with 5G emerging  c) 5G everywhere  d) 2G
**Answer: b) 4G, with 5G emerging** — 4G present, 5G emerging.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 4G in a moving car aims at —
a) 1 Mbit/s  b) 100 Mbit/s  c) 1 Gbit/s  d) 10 Gbit/s
**Answer: b) 100 Mbit/s** — 100 Mbit/s moving.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 4G standing still aims at —
a) 100 Mbit/s  b) 1 Gbit/s  c) 1 Mbit/s  d) 56 kbit/s
**Answer: b) 1 Gbit/s** — 1 Gbit/s standing still.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** 5G connects —
a) only phones  b) everyone and everything — machines, objects and devices  c) only computers  d) only satellites
**Answer: b) everyone and everything — machines, objects and devices** — everyone and everything.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The guide keys LTE as the technology of —
a) 2G  b) 3G  c) 4G  d) 5G
**Answer: c) 4G** — LTE enables 4G; NR is 5G.
