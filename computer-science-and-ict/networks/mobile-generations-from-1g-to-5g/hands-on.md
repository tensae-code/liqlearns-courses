# Hands-on — Mobile generations — from 1G to 5G

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.


## Staircase poster
Draw the five steps. On each, write the generation and — from the guide's key — its technology name. Pin 4G's two speeds on the 4G step.

## Elder interview
Ask an elder about the first mobile network they used. Which generation was it, by the lesson's map?
