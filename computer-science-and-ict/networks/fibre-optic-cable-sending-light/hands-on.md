# Hands-on — Fibre-optic cable — sending light

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.


## Backbone walk (the book's practical)
Visit or sketch the school's installation: find where the fibre backbone arrives and where Cat 6 twisted pair runs out to workstations. Label each stretch with its medium.

## Addis to Bahir Dar
On a map of Ethiopia, draw the fibre line from Addis Ababa to Bahir Dar and write why fibre — not copper — is keyed for that join.
