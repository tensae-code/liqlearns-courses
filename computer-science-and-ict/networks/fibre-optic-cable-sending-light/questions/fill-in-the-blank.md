# Fibre-optic cable — sending light — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fibre guides ______ impulses along glass or plastic fibres.
**Answer: light**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The fibre type with the wider core is called ______.
**Answer: multimode**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Joining Addis Ababa to ______ calls for fibre-optic cable, keyed in the guide.
**Answer: Bahir Dar**


**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Fibre opens services such as video ______.
**Answer: conferencing**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In the school installation, a fibre ______ feeds Cat 6 out to workstations.
**Answer: backbone**
