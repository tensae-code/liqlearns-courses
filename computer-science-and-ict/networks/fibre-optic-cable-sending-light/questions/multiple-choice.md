# Fibre-optic cable — sending light — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In fibre-optic cable, bits are encoded as —
a) electric current  b) light  c) sound  d) radio waves
**Answer: b) light** — bits encoded as light.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Electrical interference does not arise in fibre because —
a) it is shielded with foil  b) no electricity flows — it carries light  c) it is buried  d) it uses BNC connectors
**Answer: b) no electricity flows — it carries light** — light, not electricity.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Fibre carries signals, compared with coax and twisted pair —
a) slower and shorter  b) about the same  c) much farther and far faster  d) only indoors
**Answer: c) much farther and far faster** — much farther and far faster.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Single-mode fibre has —
a) a wider core  b) longer reach and higher cost  c) no core  d) the lowest cost
**Answer: b) longer reach and higher cost** — longer reach, higher cost.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Ethiopia's single Internet Service Provider, keyed in the guide, is —
a) Safaricom  b) Ethio Telecom  c) INSA  d) EDUSAT
**Answer: b) Ethio Telecom** — Ethio Telecom.
