# Fibre-optic cable — sending light — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fibre is the standard for joining networks between buildings.
**Answer: True** — immune to moisture and lightning effects.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fibre opens services such as video conferencing.
**Answer: True** — its speed and reach open them.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Copper is immune to the temperature changes that disturb it.
**Answer: False** — the guide keys fibre as the reliable one — copper is disturbed by temperature changes.
