# Video script — Fibre-optic cable — sending light

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fibre-optic cable sends light.

Bits become flashes of light in a hair of glass.
No electricity flows — so electrical interference cannot arise.

Fibre runs between buildings.
It goes much farther, and far faster, than copper.

Wide core: multimode. Long reach: single-mode.
