# Hands-on — Asynchronous, Synchronous and Isochronous Transmission

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Envelope accounting** — Send 100 characters both ways on paper: count the total bits asynchronously (2 extra bits each) and synchronously (one 20-bit frame per 50-character block). Show the tax difference.

## Task two
**Name the agreement** — For five traffic kinds — keyboard, file download, phone call, video stream, sensor bursts — choose its timing agreement and defend it.
