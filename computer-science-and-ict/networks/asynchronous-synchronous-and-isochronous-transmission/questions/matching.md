# Asynchronous, Synchronous and Isochronous Transmission — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What problem does bit synchronisation solve?** → **The receiver knowing where each bit and character begins**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What opens and closes an asynchronous character?** → **A start bit and stop bits**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What traffic suits asynchronous transmission?** → **Irregular traffic like a keyboard's**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is asynchronous transmission's price?** → **Extra bits on every character**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) May the asynchronous line idle between characters?** → **Yes — any length of time**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do synchronous sender and receiver share?** → **A clock**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does synchronous data travel?** → **In large blocks with small control frames**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is synchronous transfer efficient for bulk?** → **The timing tax is paid once per block**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does isochronous transmission guarantee?** → **A rate with bounded delay**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which traffic needs isochronous timing?** → **Live voice and video**
