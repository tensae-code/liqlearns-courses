# Asynchronous, Synchronous and Isochronous Transmission — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What problem does bit synchronisation solve?**
a) The receiver knowing where each bit and character begins  b) A start bit and stop bits  c) Irregular traffic like a keyboard's  d) Extra bits on every character
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What opens and closes an asynchronous character?**
a) A start bit and stop bits  b) Irregular traffic like a keyboard's  c) Extra bits on every character  d) Yes — any length of time
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What traffic suits asynchronous transmission?**
a) Irregular traffic like a keyboard's  b) Extra bits on every character  c) Yes — any length of time  d) A clock
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is asynchronous transmission's price?**
a) Extra bits on every character  b) Yes — any length of time  c) A clock  d) In large blocks with small control frames
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) May the asynchronous line idle between characters?**
a) Yes — any length of time  b) A clock  c) In large blocks with small control frames  d) The timing tax is paid once per block
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do synchronous sender and receiver share?**
