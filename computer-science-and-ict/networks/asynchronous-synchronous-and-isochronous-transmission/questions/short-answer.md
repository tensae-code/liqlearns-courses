# Asynchronous, Synchronous and Isochronous Transmission — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What problem does bit synchronisation solve?**
**Answer: The receiver knowing where each bit and character begins**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What opens and closes an asynchronous character?**
**Answer: A start bit and stop bits**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What traffic suits asynchronous transmission?**
**Answer: Irregular traffic like a keyboard's**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is asynchronous transmission's price?**
**Answer: Extra bits on every character**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) May the asynchronous line idle between characters?**
**Answer: Yes — any length of time**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do synchronous sender and receiver share?**
**Answer: A clock**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does synchronous data travel?**
**Answer: In large blocks with small control frames**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is synchronous transfer efficient for bulk?**
**Answer: The timing tax is paid once per block**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does isochronous transmission guarantee?**
**Answer: A rate with bounded delay**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which traffic needs isochronous timing?**
**Answer: Live voice and video**
