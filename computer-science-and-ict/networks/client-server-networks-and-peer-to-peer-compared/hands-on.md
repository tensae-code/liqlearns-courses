# Hands-on — Client-server networks — and peer-to-peer compared

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Librarian model
One learner is the server holding all resource cards; others are clients who may only request. Then remove the server and replay as peers. Compare the two runs.

## Comparison cards
Make six comparison cards (roles, focus, data, responding, cost, stability). Deal each to “client-server” or “peer-to-peer” correctly.
