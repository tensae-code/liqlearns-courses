## Sub lesson 1 — Client-server networks

In a **client-server network**, **multiple clients (workstations) connect to at least one central server** where **most resources are installed**. **Clients request; the server responds and provides.** Clients **need not share anything themselves**, software is **easier to upgrade** because it is **held on one machine**, **system-wide services run from the server**, and **security is enhanced because the server handles it**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The two kinds compared

The comparison: **differentiated roles** (client vs server) against **none**; client-server focuses on **information sharing**, peer-to-peer on **connectivity**; data is **centralised** on the server against **each peer holding its own**; the **server responds** while **every peer node can both request and respond**; client-server is **costlier** but **more stable**, while peer-to-peer is **cheaper** but becomes **less stable as peers grow**.
