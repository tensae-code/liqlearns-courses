# Client-server networks — and peer-to-peer compared — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Clients ______; the server responds and provides.
**Answer: request**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In client-server, data is ______ on the server.
**Answer: centralised**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Peer-to-peer focuses on ______; client-server on information sharing.
**Answer: connectivity**
