# WAN — country- and world-sized networks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** WAN stands for —
a) Wireless Area Network  b) Wide Area Network  c) Wired Access Network  d) World Address Network
**Answer: b) Wide Area Network** — Wide Area Network.

**2.** A WAN occupies —
a) one room  b) one building  c) a whole country or the whole world  d) one desk
**Answer: c) a whole country or the whole world** — a country or the world.

**3.** A WAN can contain —
a) nothing smaller  b) many LANs and MANs  c) only PANs  d) only printers
**Answer: b) many LANs and MANs** — LANs and MANs inside it.

**4.** The best-known public WAN is —
a) Bluetooth  b) the Internet  c) a school LAN  d) a bus
**Answer: b) the Internet** — the Internet.

**5.** In size order, the ladder runs —
a) WAN, MAN, LAN, PAN  b) PAN, LAN, MAN, WAN  c) LAN, PAN, WAN, MAN  d) MAN, WAN, PAN, LAN
**Answer: b) PAN, LAN, MAN, WAN** — PAN → LAN → MAN → WAN.
