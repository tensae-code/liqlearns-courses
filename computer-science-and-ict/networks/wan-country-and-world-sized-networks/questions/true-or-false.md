# WAN — country- and world-sized networks — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** The Internet is a WAN.
**Answer: True** — the best-known public WAN.

**2.** A WAN is smaller than a MAN.
**Answer: False** — a WAN is country- or world-sized.

**3.** MANs can sit inside a WAN.
**Answer: True** — a WAN contains LANs and MANs.
