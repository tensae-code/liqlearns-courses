# WAN — country- and world-sized networks — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1.** A WAN can cover a whole ______ or the whole world.
**Answer: country**

**2.** The best-known public WAN is the ______.
**Answer: Internet**

**3.** LANs and ______ sit inside a WAN.
**Answer: MANs**
