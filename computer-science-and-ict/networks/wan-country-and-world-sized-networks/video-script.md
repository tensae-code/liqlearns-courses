# Video script — WAN — country- and world-sized networks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A Wide Area Network (WAN) occupies a very large area — a whole country or the whole world. A WAN can contain many smaller networks — LANs and MANs — inside it. The best-known public WAN is the Internet: city networks and building networks, joined and joined again, until the joining covers the planet.

Now look at the picture. Name each part.
That is wan — country- and world-sized networks.
