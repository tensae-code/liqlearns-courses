## The largest size

A **Wide Area Network (WAN)** occupies a **very large area — a whole country or the whole world**. A WAN can **contain many smaller networks — LANs and MANs — inside it**. The best-known public WAN is the **Internet**: city networks and building networks, joined and joined again, until the joining covers the planet.
