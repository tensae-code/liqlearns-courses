# Wireless networks

## Do it first

Unplug — in your mind — every cable from a laptop, then use it from the far corner of the room anyway. What carries its messages now? That is this lesson.

## No physical connection of its own

A **wireless network** has **no physical connection of its own**: devices reach the network **through a WAP**, communicating by **high-frequency radio waves instead of wires**. That **increases mobility**, and lets an existing wired network be **expanded or replaced**.

## The wired backbone behind it

Hold the catch: the **backbone of a wireless network is always a wired network behind it**. The waves cover the last stretch — the WAP itself cables into wires.
