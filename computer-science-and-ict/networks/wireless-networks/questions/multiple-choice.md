# Wireless networks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In a wireless network, devices reach the network through —
a) a cable each  b) a WAP  c) a printer  d) a disk
**Answer: b) a WAP** — through a WAP.

**2.** Wireless devices communicate by —
a) high-frequency radio waves  b) copper wires  c) light in fibre only  d) paper
**Answer: a) high-frequency radio waves** — radio waves.

**3.** Wireless increases —
a) cable length  b) mobility  c) signal weakness  d) cost only
**Answer: b) mobility** — mobility.

**4.** Behind every wireless network stands —
a) nothing  b) a wired network as its backbone  c) a satellite only  d) a printer
**Answer: b) a wired network as its backbone** — a wired backbone.

**5.** A wireless network has —
a) a cable to every device  b) no physical connection of its own  c) only one device  d) no WAP
**Answer: b) no physical connection of its own** — no physical connection of its own.
