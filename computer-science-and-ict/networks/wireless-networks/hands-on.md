# Hands-on — Wireless networks

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Waves, then wires
Draw a wireless network with the cable visible behind the WAP. Trace one message from phone to a wired printer.

## Move and stay joined
Carry a (pretend) laptop to three places in the room. At each, say what you are connected through and what has not moved: the WAP and its cable.
