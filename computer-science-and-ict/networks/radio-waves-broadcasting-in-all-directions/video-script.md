# Video script — Radio waves — broadcasting in all directions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Radio waves go in all directions.

Three kilohertz to one gigahertz — that is the radio band.
No aiming. No alignment.
One sender — many receivers. Radio. Television. Ships. Phones.

The cost? Another antenna on the same frequency interferes.
