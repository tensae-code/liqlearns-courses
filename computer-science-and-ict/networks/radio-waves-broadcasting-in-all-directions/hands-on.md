# Hands-on — Radio waves — broadcasting in all directions

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.


## Stone in water
Drop a pebble in a bowl of water and watch the rings reach every edge at once — the omnidirectional picture. Then cup your hands and shout one direction only: that is NOT radio.

## Receiver census
List every radio receiver in the school or home (radio, TV, phone, ship radio in stories). One antenna could reach them all — say why.
