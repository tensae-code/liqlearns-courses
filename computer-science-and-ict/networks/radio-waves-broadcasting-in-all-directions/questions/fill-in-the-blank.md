# Radio waves — broadcasting in all directions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Radio waves run from 3 ______ to 1 GHz.
**Answer: kHz**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sending one message to many receivers at once is called ______.
**Answer: multicasting**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Radio antennas need no ______.
**Answer: alignment**


**4. (Sub lesson 3)** ______ broadcasting travels long distances by sky-mode radio.
**Answer: AM**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Sky-mode radio is reflected back by the ______.
**Answer: ionosphere**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** Wireless beats wired on expandability without running ______.
**Answer: cables**
