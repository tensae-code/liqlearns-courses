# Radio waves — broadcasting in all directions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Radio waves run from —
a) 1–300 GHz  b) 3 kHz to 1 GHz  c) only 2.4 GHz  d) 300 GHz upward
**Answer: b) 3 kHz to 1 GHz** — 3 kHz to 1 GHz.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Omnidirectional means —
a) aimed at one receiver  b) sent in all directions  c) sent only upward  d) carried by cable
**Answer: b) sent in all directions** — sent in all directions.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Radio suits multicasting because —
a) it needs alignment  b) one sender reaches many receivers at once  c) it cannot be interfered with  d) it is the fastest medium
**Answer: b) one sender reaches many receivers at once** — one sender to many receivers.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The cost of omnidirectional sending is —
a) high price  b) interference from any other antenna on the same frequency  c) short battery life  d) needing tall towers
**Answer: b) interference from any other antenna on the same frequency** — same-frequency interference.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** AM broadcasting travels long distances by —
a) ground cables  b) sky-mode radio  c) infrared  d) fibre
**Answer: b) sky-mode radio** — sky-mode radio.
