# Lesson plan — Radio waves — broadcasting in all directions

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Networks · **Lesson:** Radio waves — broadcasting in all directions
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Networks in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 10 book, Unit 2 — Computer Network. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** **Radio waves** (3 kHz–1 GHz) are **omnidirectional** — sent **in all directions** — which suits **multicasting** to many receivers at once.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on How wireless signals travel — the learner knows the three paths; radio is the first wave family in depth.

## If the learner is stuck
- Omni = all. One sender, every direction, many receivers — that is multicasting. The same openness lets another antenna on the same frequency interfere.
