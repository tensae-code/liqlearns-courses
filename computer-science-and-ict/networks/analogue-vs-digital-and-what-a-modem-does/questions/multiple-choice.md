# Analogue vs digital — and what a modem does — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In an analogue signal, information is carried by —
a) on/off pulses  b) amplitude and frequency of a continuous wave  c) drive letters  d) cell towers
**Answer: b) amplitude and frequency of a continuous wave** — amplitude (loudness) and frequency (pitch).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Digital signals are —
a) continuous waves  b) discrete on/off pulses of 1s and 0s  c) audible sounds  d) light only
**Answer: b) discrete on/off pulses of 1s and 0s** — discrete pulses.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A digital advantage is that pulses can be —
a) never strengthened  b) repeatedly strengthened over long distances  c) heard without devices  d) sent without a medium
**Answer: b) repeatedly strengthened over long distances** — repeatedly strengthened.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A modem converts —
a) analogue to digital only  b) digital to analogue for the line, and back at the other end  c) light to electricity  d) text to pictures
**Answer: b) digital to analogue for the line, and back at the other end** — both ways, in pairs.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Modem is short for —
a) modern emitter  b) modulate/demodulate  c) mobile demodulator  d) mode emulator
**Answer: b) modulate/demodulate** — modulate/demodulate.
