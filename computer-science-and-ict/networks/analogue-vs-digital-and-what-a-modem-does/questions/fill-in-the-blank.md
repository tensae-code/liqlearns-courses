# Analogue vs digital — and what a modem does — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In an analogue wave, ______ (loudness) and frequency (pitch) carry the information.
**Answer: amplitude**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Digital pulses are ______ (on/off), not continuous.
**Answer: discrete**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The public telephone system is shortened to ______.
**Answer: POTS**
