# Analogue vs digital — and what a modem does — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The historic telephone system was built digital.
**Answer: False** — it was built analogue — POTS.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Digital signals need conversion between two computers.
**Answer: False** — no conversion is needed between computers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Modems always work in pairs.
**Answer: True** — one modulates, the other demodulates.
