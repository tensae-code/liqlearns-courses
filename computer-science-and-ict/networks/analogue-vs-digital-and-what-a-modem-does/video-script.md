# Video script — Analogue vs digital — and what a modem does

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two kinds of signal.

Analogue: a smooth, continuous wave.
Loudness and pitch carry the message.

Digital: steps. On, off. Ones and zeros.
Hard for noise to spoil. Strong again and again.

The phone line is analogue. So a modem changes digital to a wave —
and its pair changes it back. Modulate. Demodulate. Modem.
