# Hands-on — Analogue vs digital — and what a modem does

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.


## Hum and clap
Hum a smooth sliding note (analogue), then clap on-off patterns spelling 1-0-1 (digital). A partner says which is which and why.

## Two modems
Two students are modems: one turns a clapped pattern into a hummed wave, the other turns the hum back into claps. The message must arrive identical.
