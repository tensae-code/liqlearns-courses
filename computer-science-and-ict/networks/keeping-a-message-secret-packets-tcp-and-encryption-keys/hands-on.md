# Hands-on — Keeping a Message Secret — Packets, TCP and Encryption Keys

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Whole but read** — Send a paper packet-message through “routers” who must forward reliably (TCP rules: number, acknowledge, resend) but may read everything. Then repeat with the message Caesar-locked. Compare what the routers learned.

## Task two
**Key danger drill** — Write the rules for a class key: where it lives, who holds it, when it changes, what happens if it leaks. Present and defend each rule.
