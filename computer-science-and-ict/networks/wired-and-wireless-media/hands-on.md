# Hands-on — Wired and wireless media

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Guided or unguided?
Hold up six cards (UTP, fibre, radio, microwave, infrared, satellite). The class calls “guided!” or “unguided!” for each.

## Trace the road
For three real messages (phone call, school network print, TV remote), name the medium each message travels through.
