# Wired and wireless media — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The medium's job is to —
a) carry the message from sender to receiver  b) choose routes  c) store files  d) write protocols
**Answer: a) carry the message from sender to receiver** — it carries the message.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Wired media are also called —
a) unguided  b) guided  c) wireless  d) satellite
**Answer: b) guided** — guided media.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** UTP carries electrical signals up to —
a) 10 metres  b) 100 metres  c) 1 kilometre  d) any distance
**Answer: b) 100 metres** — 100 metres.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Fibre optic uses —
a) electricity  b) light  c) sound  d) air
**Answer: b) light** — light — faster and farther.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a wireless medium?
a) UTP cable  b) Fibre optic  c) Infrared  d) Copper wire
**Answer: c) Infrared** — infrared carries through air.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A bit is carried on fibre as —
a) electrical impulses  b) pulses of light  c) sound  d) ink
**Answer: b) pulses of light** — pulses of light on fibre.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Media are not interchangeable for every purpose because they —
a) all cost the same  b) differ in features and benefits  c) are all wireless  d) never carry data
**Answer: b) differ in features and benefits** — features and benefits differ.
