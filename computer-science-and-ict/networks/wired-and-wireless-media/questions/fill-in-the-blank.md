# Wired and wireless media — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** UTP stands for unshielded twisted ______.
**Answer: pair**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fibre optic goes ______ and farther than copper.
**Answer: faster**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Radio waves and ______ are wireless media.
**Answer: microwaves**
