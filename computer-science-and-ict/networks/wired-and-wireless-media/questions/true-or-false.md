# Wired and wireless media — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Satellite communication is a wireless medium.
**Answer: True** — named among wireless media.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** UTP is a fibre cable.
**Answer: False** — UTP is copper; fibre is the other wired medium.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Wireless media can carry through vacuum.
**Answer: True** — air, water or vacuum.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** On metal wire, bits travel as electrical impulses in patterns.
**Answer: True** — electrical impulses in patterns on metal wire.
