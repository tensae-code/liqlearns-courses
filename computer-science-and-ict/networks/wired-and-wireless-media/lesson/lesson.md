# Wired and wireless media

## Do it first

A message is travelling from one device to another right now. What is it travelling *through*? Trace the road — that road is this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Wired media — copper and fibre

The **medium** carries the message **from sender to receiver**. Media split into two families: **wired (guided)** and **wireless (unguided)**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Wireless media — through air, water or vacuum

Wired media are **cables**. **Copper** — the commonest being **unshielded twisted pair (UTP)** — carries **electrical signals up to 100 metres**. **Fibre optic** uses **light**, and goes **faster and farther**. Watch the GIF: a pulse of light travelling down a fibre strand.

Wireless media carry through **air, water or vacuum**: **radio waves, microwaves, infrared and satellite communication**. Detail on both families is promised for Grade 10.
