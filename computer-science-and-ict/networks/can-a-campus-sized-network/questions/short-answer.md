# CAN — a Campus-Sized Network — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does CAN stand for?**
**Answer: Campus Area Network**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a CAN interconnect?**
**Answer: The separate LANs of one institution**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Over what distance does a CAN run?**
**Answer: A campus — a few kilometres at most**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Who owns and runs a CAN?**
**Answer: The one institution itself**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name two things that live on a campus CAN.**
**Answer: The library catalogue and the office records (also laboratory instruments)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Recite the ladder of sizes.**
**Answer: PAN, LAN, CAN, MAN, WAN**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a CAN bigger than, and smaller than?**
**Answer: Bigger than a LAN, smaller than a MAN**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do CAN and MAN differ on similar ground?**
**Answer: Ownership and purpose — one institution's own linking against a city's shared service**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who usually provides a MAN's roads?**
**Answer: A provider**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Bangladesh's book keep CAN in the ladder?**
**Answer: Its students live on campuses and use one daily**
