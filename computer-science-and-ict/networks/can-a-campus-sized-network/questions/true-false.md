# CAN — a Campus-Sized Network — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Campus Area Network**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Campus Area Network** — as the answer to: What does a CAN interconnect?
**Answer: False — The separate LANs of one institution**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A campus — a few kilometres at most**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A campus — a few kilometres at most** — as the answer to: Who owns and runs a CAN?
**Answer: False — The one institution itself**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The library catalogue and the office records (also laboratory instruments)**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The library catalogue and the office records (also laboratory instruments)** — as the answer to: Recite the ladder of sizes.
**Answer: False — PAN, LAN, CAN, MAN, WAN**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Bigger than a LAN, smaller than a MAN**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Bigger than a LAN, smaller than a MAN** — as the answer to: How do CAN and MAN differ on similar ground?
**Answer: False — Ownership and purpose — one institution's own linking against a city's shared service**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A provider**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A provider** — as the answer to: Why does Bangladesh's book keep CAN in the ladder?
