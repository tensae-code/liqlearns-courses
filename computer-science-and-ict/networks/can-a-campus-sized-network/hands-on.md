# Hands-on — CAN — a Campus-Sized Network

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Map your CAN** — Draw your school or a imagined university campus: building LANs as islands, the CAN roads joining them, and three services that ride across.

## Task two
**Ladder drill** — Place eight networks — earbuds, home, classroom, campus, city, two offices in one town, two branches in two countries, a hospital site — on the PAN–WAN ladder with reasons.
