# Hands-on — How a Message Travels — Packets, Routers and the Internet Protocol

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Packet post** — Write a sentence on five numbered slips. Send the slips by different classroom “routers” (pupils who forward by destination name) in mixed order. The receiver reassembles and reads it aloud.

## Task two
**Lose a packet** — Repeat with one slip secretly withheld. Receiver: state what is missing and request exactly that piece — then say why the whole message was not resent.
