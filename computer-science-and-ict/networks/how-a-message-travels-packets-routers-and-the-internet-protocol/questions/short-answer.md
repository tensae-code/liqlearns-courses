# How a Message Travels — Packets, Routers and the Internet Protocol — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Into what is a message cut?**
**Answer: Packets — small addressed pieces**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What address does each packet carry?**
**Answer: Its destination's IP address**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Internet Protocol?**
**Answer: The shared rules for addressing and forwarding packets**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do routers do with a packet?**
**Answer: Read its address and forward it toward its destination, hop by hop**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Must one message's packets take the same road?**
**Answer: No — they may take different roads and arrive out of order**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the receiving machine do first?**
**Answer: Collects the packets and puts them back in order**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does it check?**
**Answer: What is missing**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why do networks survive broken roads?**
**Answer: A lost packet is one piece to resend, not a message to restart**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What matters more than any single machine?**
**Answer: The protocol, agreed by everyone**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How many roads, how many rule-sets?**
**Answer: The roads are many, the rules are one**
