# How a Message Travels — Packets, Routers and the Internet Protocol — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Into what is a message cut?** → **Packets — small addressed pieces**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What address does each packet carry?** → **Its destination's IP address**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Internet Protocol?** → **The shared rules for addressing and forwarding packets**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do routers do with a packet?** → **Read its address and forward it toward its destination, hop by hop**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Must one message's packets take the same road?** → **No — they may take different roads and arrive out of order**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does it check?** → **What is missing**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why do networks survive broken roads?** → **A lost packet is one piece to resend, not a message to restart**
