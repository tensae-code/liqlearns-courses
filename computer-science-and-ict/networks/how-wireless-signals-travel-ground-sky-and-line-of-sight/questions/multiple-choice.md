# How wireless signals travel — ground, sky and line-of-sight — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Wireless media broadcast waves —
a) through copper  b) through free space with no conductor  c) only through fibre  d) inside a conduit
**Answer: b) through free space with no conductor** — through free space, no conductor.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ground propagation waves —
a) bounce off the ionosphere  b) hug the earth's curve  c) travel only in straight lines  d) need directional antennas
**Answer: b) hug the earth's curve** — low-frequency waves hugging the curve.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sky propagation reaches farther on less power because waves are —
a) amplified by the ground  b) reflected back by the ionosphere  c) sent by satellite  d) carried by cable
**Answer: b) reflected back by the ionosphere** — reflected by the ionosphere.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Line-of-sight propagation uses —
a) the lowest frequencies  b) very high frequencies in straight lines  c) ground-hugging waves  d) no antennas
**Answer: b) very high frequencies in straight lines** — very high frequencies, straight antenna to antenna.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Line-of-sight antennas must be tall or close enough to beat —
a) interference  b) the earth's curvature  c) the ionosphere  d) crosstalk
**Answer: b) the earth's curvature** — the earth's curvature.
