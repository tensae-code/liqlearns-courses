# How wireless signals travel — ground, sky and line-of-sight — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Only the intended receiver can receive a wireless broadcast.
**Answer: False** — anyone with a suitable device can receive it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In ground propagation, distance shrinks as power grows.
**Answer: False** — distance grows with power.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Line-of-sight antennas are directional.
**Answer: True** — antennas directional, in straight lines.
