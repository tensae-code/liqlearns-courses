# How wireless signals travel — ground, sky and line-of-sight — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.


**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Waves reflected by the ______ return to earth in sky propagation.
**Answer: ionosphere**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In ______ propagation, low-frequency waves hug the earth's curve.
**Answer: ground**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Very high frequencies travel by ______-of-sight propagation.
**Answer: line**


**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Wireless waves travel through free ______ with no conductor.
**Answer: space**
