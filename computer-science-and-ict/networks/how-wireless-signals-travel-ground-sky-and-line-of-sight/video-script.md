# Video script — How wireless signals travel — ground, sky and line-of-sight

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Wireless signals travel through free space. Three ways.

Ground: low waves hug the earth's curve.
More power — more distance.

Sky: higher waves bounce off the ionosphere.
Farther — on less power.

Line-of-sight: straight, antenna to antenna.
Tall or close — beat the curve of the earth.
