# How wireless signals travel — ground, sky and line-of-sight

## Do it first

Throw a ball three ways: roll it along the ground, bounce it off a high ceiling, throw it straight to a friend. A wireless signal has the same three ways to travel.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unguided media — broadcast through free space

**Wireless media broadcast electromagnetic waves through free space** with **no conductor**, receivable by **anyone with a suitable device**. The signal's path through space comes in three kinds.

[Picture 14512 — one earth curve, three signal paths]

## Sub lesson 2 — Ground and sky propagation

**Ground propagation**: **low-frequency** waves **hug the earth's curve**, and their **distance grows with power**. **Sky propagation**: **higher-frequency** waves are **reflected back by the ionosphere**, reaching **farther on less power**.

[GIF 14513 — the three propagations animating in turn]

## Sub lesson 3 — Line-of-sight propagation

**Line-of-sight propagation**: **very high frequencies** travel in **straight antenna-to-antenna lines**. The antennas are **directional**, and must be **tall or close enough to beat the earth's curvature**.
