# UTP cable, RJ45 and straight-through vs crossover — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** UTP is —
a) a shielded cable  b) the commonest network cable  c) a fibre cable  d) a wireless medium
**Answer: b) the commonest network cable** — UTP is the commonest network cable.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** UTP follows which wiring standard?
a) IEEE 802.3 Ethernet  b) IEEE 802.11  c) RJ-11  d) T568B only
**Answer: a) IEEE 802.3 Ethernet** — IEEE 802.3 Ethernet.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The RJ45 is keyed, so it —
a) fits either way  b) fits only one way  c) needs no connector  d) carries light
**Answer: b) fits only one way** — keyed — it fits only one way.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A computer joins a switch with —
a) crossover  b) straight-through  c) coaxial  d) fibre only
**Answer: b) straight-through** — different devices — straight-through.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Two computers join directly with —
a) straight-through  b) crossover  c) a terminator  d) an RJ-11
**Answer: b) crossover** — similar devices — crossover.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** A cable tester checks —
a) the price of the cable  b) continuity and correct wiring of connectors  c) the colour of the jacket  d) the speed of light
**Answer: b) continuity and correct wiring of connectors** — it verifies connections and wiring.
