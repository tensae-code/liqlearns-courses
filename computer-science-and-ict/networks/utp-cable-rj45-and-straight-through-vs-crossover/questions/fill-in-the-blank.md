# UTP cable, RJ45 and straight-through vs crossover — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.


**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The UTP connector is called the RJ______.
**Answer: 45**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** ______-through cable joins different devices.
**Answer: Straight**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** A straight-through cable's ends follow T568A or T568______.
**Answer: B**


**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The RJ45 joins computers to routers and ______.
**Answer: switches**
