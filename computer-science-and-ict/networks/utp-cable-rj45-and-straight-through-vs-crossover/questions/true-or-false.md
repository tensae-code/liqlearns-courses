# UTP cable, RJ45 and straight-through vs crossover — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** UTP has a physical shield around its pairs.
**Answer: False** — UTP does without a physical shield.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The cable found in school labs is the straight-through cable.
**Answer: True** — computer to hub or switch — the school lab cable.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** A crimper tests whether a cable is wired correctly.
**Answer: False** — the tester tests; the crimper joins cable and connector.
