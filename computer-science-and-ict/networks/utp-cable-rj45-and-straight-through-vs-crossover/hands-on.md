# Hands-on — UTP cable, RJ45 and straight-through vs crossover

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.


## Make the cable (the book's practical, in its order)
1. Rehearse both colour-code orders. 2. Make a UTP cable, crimping an RJ-45 connector at each end. 3. Test it with the cable tester. 4. Link two computers with Cat 5/6/7 cable, then link a computer to a switch, choosing the right wiring for each.

## Which cable? cards
Write six joining jobs on cards (computer–switch, computer–computer, computer–router…). For each, hold up the card and the class calls 'straight!' or 'cross!'
