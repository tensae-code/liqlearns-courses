[FULL cable-making demonstration script — the placements name this video need.]

Watch the hands. We make a network cable.

First, the kit. UTP cable. RJ-45 connectors. A crimper. A cable tester.

The cable is UTP — unshielded twisted pair. The commonest network cable.
It follows IEEE 802.3 — the Ethernet wiring standard.
IEEE: the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers.

Strip the jacket. See the four pairs, twisted, colour-coded.
Untwist only the ends. Lay the wires in the colour order.

For straight-through: the SAME order at both ends — T568A, or T568B.
Straight-through joins different devices: computer to switch.
This is the cable in your school lab.

Slide the wires into the RJ-45. The plug is keyed — it fits only one way.
Put the plug in the crimper. Squeeze. The crimper joins cable and connector.

Now the tester. It checks every connection, end to end.
All lights in order? The cable is true.

For crossover: a different order at the two ends.
Crossover joins similar devices: computer to computer.

Make both. Test both. Say for each one: what does it join?
