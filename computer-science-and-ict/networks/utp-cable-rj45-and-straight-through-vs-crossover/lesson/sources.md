Book error taught corrected: the student book expands IEEE correctly here as the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers, but elsewhere in the book the same letters are wrongly expanded as 'International Enterprise for Electronics Engineering'. This lesson teaches the correct expansion and flags the wrong one, as the placements direct.
