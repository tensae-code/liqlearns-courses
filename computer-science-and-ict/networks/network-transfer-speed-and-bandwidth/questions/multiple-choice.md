# Network Transfer Speed and Bandwidth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does transfer speed count?**
a) How much data crosses a link in one second  b) Bits per second (bps) — scaling to kbps, Mbps and Gbps  c) Eight  d) 12.5 megabytes
**Answer: a) How much data crosses a link in one second**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In what unit is transfer speed measured?**
a) Eight  b) Bits per second (bps) — scaling to kbps, Mbps and Gbps  c) 12.5 megabytes  d) Time = data size ÷ speed, with both sides in the same unit
**Answer: b) Bits per second (bps) — scaling to kbps, Mbps and Gbps**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How many bits are in one byte?**
a) 12.5 megabytes  b) Time = data size ÷ speed, with both sides in the same unit  c) Eight  d) The link's capacity — the widest flow it can carry
**Answer: c) Eight**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the most a 100 Mbps link can move in one second, in bytes?**

**Answer: d) 12.5 megabytes**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the formula for transfer time?**
