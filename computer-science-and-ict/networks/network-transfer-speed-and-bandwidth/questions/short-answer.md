# Network Transfer Speed and Bandwidth — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does transfer speed count?**
**Answer: How much data crosses a link in one second**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In what unit is transfer speed measured?**
**Answer: Bits per second (bps) — scaling to kbps, Mbps and Gbps**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How many bits are in one byte?**
**Answer: Eight**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the most a 100 Mbps link can move in one second, in bytes?**
**Answer: 12.5 megabytes**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the formula for transfer time?**
**Answer: Time = data size ÷ speed, with both sides in the same unit**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is bandwidth?**
**Answer: The link's capacity — the widest flow it can carry**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the speed a user gets usually below the bandwidth?**
**Answer: The link is shared, and the path moves at its slowest link, with distance, interference and devices taking a share**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What decides a multi-link path's speed?**
**Answer: Its slowest link**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Egypt place this lesson?**
**Answer: At the end of the Grade 10 networks chapter, after packets, IP addresses and protocols**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name two planning jobs this number serves**
**Answer: Choosing a connection and judging how long a download will take**
