# Telecommunications in daily life and business — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.


**1.** Telecommunications makes business slower because messages queue.
**Answer: False** — business acts faster — information moves faster.

**2.** Collaboration between people worldwide is one of the applications.
**Answer: True** — people developing solutions together.

**3.** Fax and broadcast television are outside the application range.
**Answer: False** — both are named in the range.
