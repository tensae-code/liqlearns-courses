# Telecommunications in daily life and business — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1.** Telecommunications shrinks —
a) only distance  b) only time  c) time and distance  d) cables
**Answer: c) time and distance** — time and distance.

**2.** Education gains because it can —
a) print more books  b) teach at a distance almost anywhere  c) close schools  d) replace teachers with cables
**Answer: b) teach at a distance almost anywhere** — teach at a distance.

**3.** Business acts faster because —
a) offices are closer  b) information moves faster  c) there are more fax machines  d) meetings are banned
**Answer: b) information moves faster** — information moves faster.

**4.** Which is in the application range?
a) ploughing  b) instant messaging  c) weaving  d) milling
**Answer: b) instant messaging** — instant messaging.

**5.** The book's example of rollout across all regions is —
a) Ethio telecom's 4G LTE Advanced  b) a new railway  c) printed textbooks  d) EDUSAT only
**Answer: a) Ethio telecom's 4G LTE Advanced** — Ethio telecom rolling out 4G LTE Advanced.
