# Telecommunications in daily life and business

## Do it first

## Shrinking time and distance

**Telecommunications shrinks time and distance**: **education can teach at a distance** almost anywhere in the world, and **business acts faster** because information moves faster.

[Picture 14523 — Ethiopia linked by telecom lines]

## The application range

The applications run from **telephony and videoconferencing** through **fax, broadcast and interactive television, instant messaging, email and collaboration** to **data transmission** — people worldwide developing solutions together. The book's example: **Ethio telecom rolling out 4G LTE Advanced across all regions**.
