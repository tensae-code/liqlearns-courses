# BNC connectors — joining coaxial cable

## Do it first

A cable run must join a device in the middle and end somewhere. Three small metal pieces do those three jobs. Find each one's job.

## The BNC family

Coax joins devices through **Bayonet Neill-Concelman (BNC) connectors**, in **three common forms** pictured together.

[Picture 14508 — the three BNC pieces]

## The three forms and their jobs

The **plain BNC connector** joins a cable to a device. The **BNC T connector** joins **a device into a run of cable**. The **BNC terminator** closes **the end of a run**. In the practical, cable and connectors are brought to class and the connection is demonstrated.
