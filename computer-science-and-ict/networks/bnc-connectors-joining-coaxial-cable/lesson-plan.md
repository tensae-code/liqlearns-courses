# Lesson plan — BNC connectors — joining coaxial cable

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Networks · **Lesson:** BNC connectors — joining coaxial cable
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Networks in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 10 book, Unit 2 — Computer Network. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Coax joins devices through **BNC connectors**: the **plain BNC**, the **BNC T** and the **BNC terminator**.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Coaxial cable — the learner already knows the cable these connectors join.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Coaxial cable — a core inside a shield. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Expand BNC and say what the connectors join.
2. Name the three forms and each one's job.

## What you need
For the practical: coaxial cable and BNC connectors, if the school has them; otherwise paper and a pencil.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — A cable run must join a device in the middle and end somewhere. Three small metal pieces do those three jobs. Find each one's job. (lesson/lesson.md).
