# Hands-on — BNC connectors — joining coaxial cable

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.


## Join it (the book's practical)
With real coax and connectors if available: join a cable to a device with the plain BNC, tap a second device in with a T, and close the run with a terminator. Otherwise draw the same run and label each connector.

## Job match
Three cards — plain, T, terminator. A partner names a job; you hold up the right card.
