# BNC connectors — joining coaxial cable — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.


**1.** BNC connectors join ______ cable.
**Answer: coaxial**

**2.** The connector that closes the end of a run is the BNC ______.
**Answer: terminator**

**3.** The ______ connector joins a device into a run of cable.
**Answer: T**
