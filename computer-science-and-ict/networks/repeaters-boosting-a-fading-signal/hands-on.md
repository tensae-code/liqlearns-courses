# Hands-on — Repeaters — boosting a fading signal

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Human repeater
Whisper a sentence along a line of people until it fades; then place a repeater in the middle who repeats each message at full voice. Compare the endings.

## How far?
Measure your classroom in paces. Discuss where a repeater would sit on a cable twice that long.
