# Repeaters — boosting a fading signal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Signals over distance —
a) grow stronger  b) weaken  c) stop being signals  d) turn into noise only
**Answer: b) weaken** — they weaken.

**2.** A repeater —
a) stores files  b) regenerates a weak signal to its original strength and sends it on  c) prints documents  d) chooses routes
**Answer: b) regenerates a weak signal to its original strength and sends it on** — it regenerates the signal.

**3.** A repeater extends —
a) the printer queue  b) the network's coverage  c) the screen size  d) the password
**Answer: b) the network's coverage** — the network's coverage.

**4.** An amplifier, unlike a repeater —
a) boosts noise along with the signal  b) cleans the signal  c) sends packets  d) is software
**Answer: a) boosts noise along with the signal** — it boosts noise too.

**5.** Weakening signals limit —
a) how far a cable segment can reach  b) how many printers exist  c) the size of a file  d) the number of protocols
**Answer: a) how far a cable segment can reach** — the reach of a cable segment.
