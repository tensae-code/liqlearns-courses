# Repeaters — boosting a fading signal

## Do it first

Shout a message down a very long corridor. By the far end your voice is faint. Now put a friend in the middle who hears it and shouts it on at full strength. That friend is this lesson's device.

## Signals weaken over distance

As a signal travels, it **weakens over distance** — and that limits **how far a cable segment can reach**. A long network needs help in the middle.

## The repeater regenerates

A **repeater** takes in the **weak signal**, **regenerates it to its original strength** and **sends it on**, **extending the network's coverage**. Watch the GIF: the wave shrinks along the cable, the repeater rebuilds it full-height, and it travels on.

## Repeater, not amplifier

Its analogue cousin, the **amplifier**, only **boosts** — and it boosts the **noise along with the signal**. The repeater does not: it regenerates a clean signal at full strength. (This contrast is stated in the unit summary.)
