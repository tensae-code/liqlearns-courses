# NMEA Frames — How GPS Devices Report Position — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Every NMEA frame begins with —**
a) A comma  b) The $ sign  c) The checksum  d) The time
**Answer: b) The $ sign — Followed by the talker and sentence type, such as GPGGA.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In a $GPGGA frame, the latitude 4807.038 means —**
a) 48.07038°  b) 480° 7.038′  c) 48° 07.038′  d) 48° 7038′
**Answer: c) 48° 07.038′ — Two digits of degrees, then minutes with decimals, to be divided by 60 for decimal degrees.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The number after the * at the end of a frame is —**
a) The altitude  b) A checksum guarding the line against corruption  c) The satellite count  d) The speed
**Answer: b) A checksum guarding the line against corruption — A receiver can detect and drop a damaged frame.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Converting 01131.000 E to decimal degrees gives —**
a) 11.3100°  b) 11.5167°  c) 113.1000°  d) 1.1310°
**Answer: b) 11.5167° — 011° + 31.000/60 = 11° + 0.5167° = 11.5167°.**
