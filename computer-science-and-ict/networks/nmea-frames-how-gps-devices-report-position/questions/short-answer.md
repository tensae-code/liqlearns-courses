# NMEA Frames — How GPS Devices Report Position — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) A decoder plots a boat in the desert instead of the sea, mirrored east–west of where it should be. What is the most likely bug?**
**Answer: The hemisphere sign was dropped or applied wrongly — the E/W letter was ignored, so a longitude that should be positive (or negative) was plotted with the wrong sign, mirroring the position across the Greenwich meridian.**
