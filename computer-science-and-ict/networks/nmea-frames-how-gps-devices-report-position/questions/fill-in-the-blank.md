# NMEA Frames — How GPS Devices Report Position — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The fields inside an NMEA frame are separated by ____.**
**Answer: commas — Which is why the decoder’s first move is to split the line on commas.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The fix-data frame used in this lesson is named $____.**
**Answer: GPGGA — It carries time, position, fix quality, satellites and altitude.**
