# Switches — sending packets only where they belong

## Do it first

Imagine a post office that copies every letter and delivers a copy to every house in town. Now imagine one that reads the address and delivers each letter to its one house. Two devices in this lesson work exactly those two ways.

## Sub lesson 1 — The switch — learning and forwarding

A **switch interconnects devices** — computers, printers and servers — **in a small network**. It **learns the network's layout into a table**: each computer's **physical address** and its **switch port**. Then it **forwards packets only to their specific destination**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The hub — broadcasting to all

A **hub**, by contrast, is **unintelligent**: it **broadcasts everything it receives to every connected computer except the sender**. Watch the GIF: the switch sends one packet out of one port; the hub sprays copies to all ports. Same wires in — very different behaviour out.
