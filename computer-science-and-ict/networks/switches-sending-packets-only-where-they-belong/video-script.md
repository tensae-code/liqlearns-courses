# Video script — Switches — sending packets only where they belong

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

A switch interconnects devices — computers, printers and servers — in a small network. It learns the network's layout into a table: each computer's physical address and its switch port. Then it forwards packets only to their specific destination.

Now look at the picture. Name each part.
