# Lesson plan — Switches — sending packets only where they belong

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Networks · **Lesson:** Switches — sending packets only where they belong
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 9 — Networks in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 9 book, Unit 2 — Computer Network. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A **switch interconnects devices** — computers, printers and servers — **in a small network**.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title, in the unit's own order — and on the learner's everyday experience of phones, Bluetooth sharing and shared printers.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The switch — learning and forwarding** — the address-and-port table, and forwarding only to the destination
2. **The hub — broadcasting to all** — the unintelligent contrast: copies to every computer except the sender

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
