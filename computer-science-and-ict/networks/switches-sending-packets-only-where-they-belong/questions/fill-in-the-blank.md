# Switches — sending packets only where they belong — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A switch keeps a ______ of addresses and ports.
**Answer: table**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A hub ______ everything it receives.
**Answer: broadcasts**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A switch forwards ______ only to their destination.
**Answer: packets**
