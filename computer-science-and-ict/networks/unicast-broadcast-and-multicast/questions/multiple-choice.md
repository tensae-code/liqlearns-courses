# Unicast, Broadcast and Multicast — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is unicast?**
a) One sender to one receiver  b) A message to one friend (or a page to one browser)  c) A thousand viewers need a thousand streams  d) One sender to every receiver on the network
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name a unicast example.**
a) A message to one friend (or a page to one browser)  b) A thousand viewers need a thousand streams  c) One sender to every receiver on the network  d) It burdens everyone, wanted or not
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is unicast's cost problem?**
a) A thousand viewers need a thousand streams  b) One sender to every receiver on the network  c) It burdens everyone, wanted or not  d) One sender to a chosen group of receivers
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is broadcast?**
a) One sender to every receiver on the network  b) It burdens everyone, wanted or not  c) One sender to a chosen group of receivers  d) Only those who joined the group
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why is broadcast used sparingly?**
a) It burdens everyone, wanted or not  b) One sender to a chosen group of receivers  c) Only those who joined the group  d) One stream branches only where members wait
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is multicast?**
a) One sender to a chosen group of receivers  b) Only those who joined the group  c) One stream branches only where members wait  d) A live lesson streamed to enrolled classes
**Answer: a**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Who receives a multicast?**
a) Only those who joined the group  b) One stream branches only where members wait  c) A live lesson streamed to enrolled classes  d) The audience — one, all, or the group between
**Answer: a**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does multicast save the road?**
a) One stream branches only where members wait  b) A live lesson streamed to enrolled classes  c) The audience — one, all, or the group between  d) One sender to one receiver
**Answer: a**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name a multicast example.**
a) A live lesson streamed to enrolled classes  b) The audience — one, all, or the group between  c) One sender to one receiver  d) A message to one friend (or a page to one browser)
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What decides the mode?**
a) The audience — one, all, or the group between  b) One sender to one receiver  c) A message to one friend (or a page to one browser)  d) A thousand viewers need a thousand streams
