# Unicast, Broadcast and Multicast — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is unicast?**
**Answer: One sender to one receiver**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name a unicast example.**
**Answer: A message to one friend (or a page to one browser)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is unicast's cost problem?**
**Answer: A thousand viewers need a thousand streams**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is broadcast?**
**Answer: One sender to every receiver on the network**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why is broadcast used sparingly?**
**Answer: It burdens everyone, wanted or not**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is multicast?**
**Answer: One sender to a chosen group of receivers**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Who receives a multicast?**
**Answer: Only those who joined the group**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does multicast save the road?**
**Answer: One stream branches only where members wait**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name a multicast example.**
**Answer: A live lesson streamed to enrolled classes**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What decides the mode?**
**Answer: The audience — one, all, or the group between**
