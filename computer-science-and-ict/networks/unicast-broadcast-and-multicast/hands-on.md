# Hands-on — Unicast, Broadcast and Multicast

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Sort the traffic** — Classify eight real messages — a text to a friend, a school announcement, a streamed match, an email to a class list — by mode, with the audience named for each.

## Task two
**Count the copies** — A video must reach 500 students. Count the streams under unicast and under multicast on a shared school link, and explain the saving.
