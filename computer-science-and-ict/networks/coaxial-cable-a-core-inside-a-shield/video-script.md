# Video script — Coaxial cable — a core inside a shield

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Coaxial cable. A core inside a shield.

Centre: a copper core.
Round it: insulation.
Round that: a metal shield. It shields — and it completes the circuit.
Then insulation again, and a plastic cover.

Thick coax: better against noise, for broadband.
Thin coax: smaller, for baseband.
