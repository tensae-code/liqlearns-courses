# Coaxial cable — a core inside a shield

## Do it first

Slice a cake in your mind and count the layers from the middle out. This cable is built in layers too — five of them. Count them as you read.

## Sub lesson 1 — Why coax carries higher frequencies

**Coaxial cable** carries **higher frequencies than twisted pair** because of its **build** — layer inside layer around one centre.

[Picture 14507 — coax cross-section, thick and thin]

## Sub lesson 2 — The five layers, inside out

From the centre: a **central copper core**; an **insulating sheath**; an **outer metallic conductor** that both **shields against noise** and **completes the circuit**; **another insulating sheath**; and a **plastic cover**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Thick and thin

Coax comes **thick** — about **6–18 mm**, with **better noise immunity**, typically carrying **broadband/analogue signals** such as several video channels — and **thin** — about **4 mm**, with **limited noise isolation**, typically **baseband**.
