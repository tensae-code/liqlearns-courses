# Coaxial cable — a core inside a shield — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Coax carries higher frequencies than twisted pair because of —
a) its colour  b) its build  c) its price  d) its length
**Answer: b) its build** — because of its layered build.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At the centre of coax is —
a) a glass fibre  b) a central copper core  c) an air gap  d) a braid
**Answer: b) a central copper core** — the central copper core.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The outer metallic conductor —
a) only carries the signal  b) shields against noise and completes the circuit  c) is the plastic cover  d) stores data
**Answer: b) shields against noise and completes the circuit** — it shields and completes the circuit.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Thick coax is about —
a) 4 mm  b) 6–18 mm  c) 40 mm  d) 1 m
**Answer: b) 6–18 mm** — about 6–18 mm.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Thin coax typically carries —
a) broadband  b) several video channels  c) baseband  d) light
**Answer: c) baseband** — thin coax is typically baseband.
