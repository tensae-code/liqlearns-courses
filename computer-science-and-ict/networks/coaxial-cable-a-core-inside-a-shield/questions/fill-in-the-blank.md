# Coaxial cable — a core inside a shield — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.


**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The central conductor of coax is made of ______.
**Answer: copper**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The outer metallic conductor both shields and ______ the circuit.
**Answer: completes**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Thick coax typically carries ______ signals such as several video channels.
**Answer: broadband**


**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Coax carries ______ frequencies than twisted pair.
**Answer: higher**
