# Lesson plan — Coaxial cable — a core inside a shield

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Networks · **Lesson:** Coaxial cable — a core inside a shield
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Networks in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 10 book, Unit 2 — Computer Network. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** **Coax** carries **higher frequencies** than twisted pair: a **copper core**, insulation, a **metallic shield** that completes the circuit, and covers.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Twisted-pair cable — the learner can compare this build with the twisted pair's.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Why coax carries higher frequencies** — coax carries higher frequencies than twisted pair because of its build
2. **The five layers, inside out** — core, insulation, metallic shield, insulation, plastic cover
3. **Thick and thin** — thick 6–18 mm broadband; thin about 4 mm baseband

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Twisted-pair cable — twisting cancels interference. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say why coax carries higher frequencies than twisted pair.
2. Name the five layers in order from the centre.
3. Tell thick coax from thin coax by size, immunity and signal kind.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.
