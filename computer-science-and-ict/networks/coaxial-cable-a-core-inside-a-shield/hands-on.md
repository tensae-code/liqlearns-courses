# Hands-on — Coaxial cable — a core inside a shield

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.


## Layer drawing
Draw the cross-section as five rings and label each. Beside it draw thick and thin to scale (18 mm and 4 mm) with a ruler.

## Find coax
Find a television aerial cable at home or school. It is coax — say which layer you can see at a cut end.
