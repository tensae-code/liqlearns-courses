# Data communication — delivery, accuracy, timeliness, jitter

## Do it first

Post a letter in your mind. It must reach the right house, arrive unopened and unchanged, arrive on time, and — if letters came in a stream — arrive evenly spaced. Four tests. Data faces the same four.

## Sub lesson 1 — What data communication is

**Data communication** is the **electronic collection, processing and distribution of data between computer hardware devices**. The teacher guide teaches it as **its own section** — the exchange of data **between source and receiver over a transmission medium** (teacher guide — evidence).

## Sub lesson 2 — The four characteristics

A system's effectiveness rests on four characteristics: **delivery** (to the **right destination**), **accuracy** (**unaltered**), **timeliness** (**on time**) and **low jitter** (**even timing of delivery**). The guide's key sharpens delivery: to the **correct destination in correct order**; and jitter as **uneven packet-arrival delay** (teacher guide — evidence).

## Sub lesson 3 — The five components

Its system has **five components**, pictured as **message, sender, receiver, transmission medium and protocol**.
