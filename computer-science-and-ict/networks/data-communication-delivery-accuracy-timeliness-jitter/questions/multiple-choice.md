# Data communication — delivery, accuracy, timeliness, jitter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Data communication is the electronic collection, processing and distribution of data —
a) between computer hardware devices  b) only inside one chip  c) only by satellite  d) on paper
**Answer: a) between computer hardware devices** — between computer hardware devices.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Accuracy means the data arrives —
a) fast  b) unaltered  c) compressed  d) twice
**Answer: b) unaltered** — unaltered.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Jitter is about —
a) the even timing of delivery  b) the size of the file  c) the colour of the cable  d) the price of the service
**Answer: a) the even timing of delivery** — even timing; uneven arrival delay in the guide's words.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Delivery means the data reaches —
a) any device  b) the right destination  c) the nearest device  d) the sender again
**Answer: b) the right destination** — the right destination — in correct order, keyed in the guide.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which is NOT one of the five components?
a) message  b) protocol  c) transmission medium  d) terminator
**Answer: d) terminator** — message, sender, receiver, medium, protocol.
