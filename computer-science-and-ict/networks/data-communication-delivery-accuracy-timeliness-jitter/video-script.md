# Video script — Data communication — delivery, accuracy, timeliness, jitter

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Data communication moves data between machines.

It works when four things hold:
Delivery — the right destination.
Accuracy — unchanged.
Timeliness — on time.
Low jitter — evenly timed.

Five parts carry it: sender, message, medium, protocol, receiver.
