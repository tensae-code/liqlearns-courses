# Routers — joining networks and picking the route — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** A router picks the best route for a message crossing networks.
**Answer: True** — that is routing.

**2.** Every message, even inside one switch, needs a router.
**Answer: False** — same-switch messages do not.

**3.** A router can join different networks together.
**Answer: True** — connecting dissimilar networks is its job.
