# Hands-on — Cable Connectors — RJ45, BNC and Fibre

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Connector hunt

Find three cables around your home or school — a network cable, an aerial or satellite cable, any others. For each one, draw its end, name its cable family if you can, and write which connector from this lesson it is — or write "a different connector" and say what device it fits.

## Match the ends

On paper, draw the three cables of a small school network: twisted pair from a computer to a switch, coaxial for an older link, fibre for the long run between buildings. Label each end with its connector, and beside each label write how that connector locks or joins.
