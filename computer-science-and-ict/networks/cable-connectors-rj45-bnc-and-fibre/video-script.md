# Video script — Cable Connectors — RJ45, BNC and Fibre

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A cable joins nothing until its end fits somewhere.

The end is the connector.

Twisted-pair cable ends in RJ45. Eight pins. Push — click — locked.

Coaxial cable ends in BNC. Push and twist. Locked like a bayonet.

Fibre cable carries light. Its ends must be clean and straight, or the light stops.

Right cable, right connector.
