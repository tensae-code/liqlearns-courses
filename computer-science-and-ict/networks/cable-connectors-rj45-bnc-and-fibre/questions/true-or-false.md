# Cable Connectors — RJ45, BNC and Fibre — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The sound an RJ45 plug makes when it clicks is its clip locking into the port.**
**Answer: True** — The click is the lock engaging.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A BNC connector is made of clear plastic and has eight pins.**
**Answer: False** — That describes RJ45. BNC is a metal connector with a bayonet lock.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) A speck of dust on a fibre end can block the signal.**
**Answer: True** — The signal is light; a dirty end blocks the light, and a blocked light is a lost message.

**4. (Sub lesson 3) Fibre-optic cable carries its signal as electricity in a copper core.**
**Answer: False** — Fibre carries light along a thread of glass.
