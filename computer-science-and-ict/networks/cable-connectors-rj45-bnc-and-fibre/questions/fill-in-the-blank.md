# Cable Connectors — RJ45, BNC and Fibre — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The connector at the end of a twisted-pair network cable is the ____.**
**Answer: RJ45**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A BNC connector locks with a push and a twist — a ____ lock.**
**Answer: bayonet**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) A fibre connector holds two ends of ____ thread face to face so light can pass.**
**Answer: glass**
