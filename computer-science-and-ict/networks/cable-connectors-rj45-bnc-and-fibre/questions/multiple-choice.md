# Cable Connectors — RJ45, BNC and Fibre — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A connector is —**
a) the plug at the end of a cable that joins the cable to a device or another cable  b) the whole cable itself  c) a program that connects to the internet
**Answer: a) the plug at the end of a cable that joins the cable to a device or another cable** — The connector is the cable's end-piece; the join is its job.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many pins does an RJ45 plug have?**
a) Two  b) Four  c) Eight
**Answer: c) Eight** — One pin for each of the eight wires in twisted-pair cable.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) An RJ45 plug is taken out of a port by —**
a) pulling hard until it comes free  b) pressing the clip flat, then pulling gently  c) twisting it a quarter turn
**Answer: b) pressing the clip flat, then pulling gently** — The clip is the lock; pulling against it snaps the clip.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The BNC connector belongs to which cable?**
a) Twisted-pair cable  b) Coaxial cable  c) Fibre-optic cable
**Answer: b) Coaxial cable** — BNC ends coaxial cable.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) A BNC connector locks by —**
a) a clicking plastic clip  b) a push and a quarter-turn twist — a bayonet lock  c) glue
**Answer: b) a push and a quarter-turn twist — a bayonet lock** — The studs slide into the locked end of the slots.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) A fibre connector must hold the two glass ends —**
a) loosely, so they can move  b) exactly face to face, clean and lined up  c) at right angles to each other
**Answer: b) exactly face to face, clean and lined up** — Light must pass straight from one thread into the next; dirt or crookedness blocks it.
