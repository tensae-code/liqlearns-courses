# Virtual Private Networks — VPN — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does VPN stand for?**
**Answer: Virtual private network**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a VPN make?**
**Answer: A private connection across a public network**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is the data protected on the public road?**
**Answer: It is encrypted — wrapped so only the two ends can read it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is it called virtual?**
**Answer: Because no new wire is laid**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why is it called private?**
**Answer: Because encryption keeps everyone else out**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What job does the guide name for a VPN?**
**Answer: Joining separated sites and people as if on one local network**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give one user of a VPN from the lesson.**
**Answer: A branch office reaching head office files**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What road does a VPN use?**
**Answer: The public internet**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the organisation keep on that road?**
**Answer: Its own locked carriage — privacy**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Finish the lesson's summary: the reach of a public network with…**
**Answer: The privacy of a private one**
