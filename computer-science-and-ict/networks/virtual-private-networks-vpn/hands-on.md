# Hands-on — Virtual Private Networks — VPN

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Tunnel on paper', 'Draw two buildings joined by a busy public road. Draw the VPN as a tunnel through the road and label: what travels inside, what the encryption does, and who is kept out.')

## Task two
('Wire or tunnel?', 'A school opens a second site across town. Write two short bids: laying a private cable, and running a VPN. For each give one cost point and one safety point, then say which you would choose and why.')
