## The card that connects a computer to a network

The **Network Interface Card (NIC)** is the **hardware component that connects a computer to a network**. It comes in two forms: a **card plugged into the system**, or **integrated into the board** — built in, as in **most laptops**. Either way, the job is the same: the computer joins the network through its NIC.

## The socket

The NIC **carries the slot into which the network cable plugs**. No NIC, no connection: a computer without one cannot join a wired network, however good its cable is.
