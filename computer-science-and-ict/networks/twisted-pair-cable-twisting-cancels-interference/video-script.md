# Video script — Twisted-pair cable — twisting cancels interference

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guided media carry the signal inside a tube.

Two copper wires. Twist them together.
One wire carries the signal. The other is the ground.

Noise hits the nearer wire harder.
But the twist swaps the nearer wire at every turn.
Both wires suffer the same. The noise cancels.

That is why the wires are twisted.
