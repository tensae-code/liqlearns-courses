# Hands-on — Twisted-pair cable — twisting cancels interference

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.


## Twist it yourself
Twist two strings together. Hold a third string (the noise) beside them and walk along the twist: at each half-turn, say which string is nearer the noise.

## Parallel vs twisted
Draw the same two wires straight and parallel, then twisted. Under each, write which wire the noise strikes harder.
