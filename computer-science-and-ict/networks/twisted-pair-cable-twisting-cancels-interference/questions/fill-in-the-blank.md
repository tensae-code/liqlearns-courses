# Twisted-pair cable — twisting cancels interference — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Media that carry the signal inside a conduit are called ______ media.
**Answer: guided**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One wire carries the signal; the other is the ______ reference.
**Answer: ground**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Interference between neighbouring wires is called ______.
**Answer: crosstalk**
