# Twisted-pair cable — twisting cancels interference — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Guided media carry the signal —
a) inside a physical conduit  b) only through air  c) only by satellite  d) without any pathway
**Answer: a) inside a physical conduit** — the conduit directs and contains it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Twisted pair conductors are —
a) glass  b) copper  c) plastic  d) air
**Answer: b) copper** — copper carries electric current.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In a twisted pair, the second wire acts as —
a) a spare signal wire  b) the ground reference  c) an antenna  d) a shield
**Answer: b) the ground reference** — one carries the signal, the other is ground reference.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Twisting defends the signal because —
a) it makes the cable shorter  b) it swaps which wire is nearer the noise at every turn  c) it blocks all noise completely  d) it turns the signal into light
**Answer: b) it swaps which wire is nearer the noise at every turn** — both wires suffer equally, so the effect cancels.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The unwanted effect cancels —
a) at the sender  b) in the middle  c) at the receiver  d) in the noise source
**Answer: c) at the receiver** — it cancels at the receiver.
