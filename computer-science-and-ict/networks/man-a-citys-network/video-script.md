# Video script — MAN — a city's network

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A Metropolitan Area Network (MAN) is designed to cover an entire city. The book's example: a bank joining three branches in one city over the city's existing telephone network — Ethio-telecom's. Its figure interconnects a hospital, a school/college and a factory LAN across a city: each building keeps its LAN, and the MAN joins the LANs.

Now look at the picture. Name each part.
That is man — a city's network.
