# Hands-on — MAN — a city's network

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## City map join-up
On a simple city map (drawn or printed), place a hospital, a school and a factory. Draw each one's LAN, then the MAN joining them.

## LAN or MAN?
For six networks described by a partner, call “LAN” or “MAN” and say the deciding fact: one building, or a whole city?
