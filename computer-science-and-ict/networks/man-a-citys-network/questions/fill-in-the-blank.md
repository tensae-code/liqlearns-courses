# MAN — a city's network — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1.** A MAN is designed to cover an entire ______.
**Answer: city**

**2.** A bank joining three ______ in one city uses a MAN.
**Answer: branches**

**3.** The city's telephone network in the example is ______'s.
**Answer: Ethio-telecom**
