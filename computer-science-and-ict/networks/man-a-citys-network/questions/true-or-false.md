# MAN — a city's network — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** A MAN is bigger than a LAN and smaller in reach than a WAN.
**Answer: True** — city-sized, between building and country/world.

**2.** In the figure, the hospital, school and factory each have their own LAN.
**Answer: True** — the MAN interconnects their LANs.

**3.** A MAN covers several countries.
**Answer: False** — an entire city.
