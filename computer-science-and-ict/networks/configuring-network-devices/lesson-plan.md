## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **From devices to a working network — installation** — Devices on a bench are not yet a network: **configuration** is the work that makes them one system. The Grade 11 book runs it as the second half of building — after cables are prepared, the network is **installed and configured**: devices connected in the designed topology, each given its place and settings. The SMK book teaches the same craft hands-on in its network chapter — configuring network devices (mengkonfigurasi perangkat jaringan) as a workshop skill with the devices in the pupil's hands, then operating them in practice.
2. **The settings that matter — addresses and agreement** — Configuration's heart is **agreement**: every device must hold settings that match the network's design — its **IP address** within the network's range and unlike any other, the gateway and name-service addresses it should use, and the wireless name and password where air is the medium. A network fails politely or not at all when two devices claim one address or one device keeps a stranger's settings; the configurator's discipline is to set deliberately, record what was set, and test device by device until the whole design answers.
