# Video script — Configuring Network Devices

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Devices on a bench are not yet a network: **configuration** is the work that makes them one system. The Grade 11 book runs it as the second half of building — after cables are prepared, the network is **installed and configured**: devices connected in the designed topology, each given its place and settings. The SMK book teaches the same craft hands-on in its network chapter — configuring network devices (mengkonfigurasi perangkat jaringan) as a workshop skill with the devices in the pupil's hands, then operating them in practice.
Configuration's heart is **agreement**: every device must hold settings that match the network's design — its **IP address** within the network's range and unlike any other, the gateway and name-service addresses it should use, and the wireless name and password where air is the medium. A network fails politely or not at all when two devices claim one address or one device keeps a stranger's settings; the configurator's discipline is to set deliberately, record what was set, and test device by device until the whole design answers.
