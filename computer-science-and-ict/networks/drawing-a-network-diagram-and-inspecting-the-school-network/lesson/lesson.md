# Drawing a network diagram — and inspecting the school network

## Do it first

Draw your classroom's computers, printer and connection points as a diagram — proper symbols, labelled lines. That drawing, and one real inspection, are this whole lesson. Do the drawing first.

## Sub lesson 1 — Drawing with proper symbols

Search for the **Cisco and Huawei symbols** for the devices taught in this unit. **Draw a simple network diagram** from them. Then **redraw the two-networks-and-a-router figure** in **Cisco Packet Tracer, GNS3, or on paper** — in that order: symbols first, your own diagram second, the book's figure third.

[Picture 12916 — a drawn network diagram]

## Sub lesson 2 — Inspecting the real school network

Visit the **school network** and **identify its elements**. Ask the administrator **which protocols and interconnecting devices** it uses. Then examine a **real UTP cable**: its **connectors**, the **wires inside the sheath**, their **colours and their order**. Report back to class — diagram, answers, and cable findings together.

## Say it to your companion

Explain drawing a network diagram — and inspecting the school network back in your own words — the one thing, the picture part by part, and one example of your own. Your companion asks one question; answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Draw a network diagram using proper device symbols.
2. List the school network's elements you identified.
3. Write the UTP cable's wire colours in their order, from your inspection.
