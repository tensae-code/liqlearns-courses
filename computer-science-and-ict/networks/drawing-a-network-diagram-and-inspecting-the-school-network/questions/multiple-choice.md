# Drawing a network diagram — and inspecting the school network — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The diagram symbols to search for are from —
a) Cisco and Huawei  b) two printer makers  c) a games company  d) the school office
**Answer: a) Cisco and Huawei** — Cisco and Huawei.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's figure to redraw shows —
a) one computer  b) two networks and a router  c) a satellite  d) five printers
**Answer: b) two networks and a router** — two networks and a router.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The redraw can be done in —
a) Cisco Packet Tracer, GNS3, or on paper  b) only a games program  c) only chalk  d) a photo editor only
**Answer: a) Cisco Packet Tracer, GNS3, or on paper** — Packet Tracer, GNS3 or paper.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** On the school visit you ask the administrator about —
a) protocols and interconnecting devices  b) exam dates  c) printer paper  d) passwords to copy
**Answer: a) protocols and interconnecting devices** — protocols and interconnecting devices.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Examining the UTP cable, you look at —
a) its price  b) its connectors, the wires inside, their colours and order  c) its length only  d) its box
**Answer: b) its connectors, the wires inside, their colours and order** — connectors, wires, colours and order.
