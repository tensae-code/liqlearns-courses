# Hands-on — Drawing a network diagram — and inspecting the school network

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Packet Tracer redraw
Redraw the two-networks-and-a-router figure in Packet Tracer or GNS3; if neither is available, draw it large on paper with the symbols.

## Cable inspection
With a spare UTP cable (never a live one the school needs), examine both connectors and report the wire colours and order to the class.
