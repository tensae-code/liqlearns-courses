# The Layers of a Network — and What Each Does — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a layer in a network?**
**Answer: One level of the communication work, serving the layer above**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the engineer's trick behind layers?**
**Answer: Splitting a hard journey into simple stages**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do the top layers do?**
**Answer: Prepare what is said, and in what form**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do the bottom layers move?**
**Answer: Signals on wires and air**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What can change without the top layers knowing?**
**Answer: One layer's technology — cable to wireless**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the postal stages in order.**
**Answer: Writing and addressing, sorting, carrying, sorting again, delivery**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Who chooses the route, in the analogy?**
**Answer: The sorting post office**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do trucks and planes correspond to?**
**Answer: The physical journey layer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How much of the journey must one postal worker understand?**
**Answer: Only their own stage**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do layers buy a network?**
**Answer: Specialists at every stage, and a journey no part must understand whole**
