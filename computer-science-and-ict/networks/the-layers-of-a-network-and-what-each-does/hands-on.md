# Hands-on — The Layers of a Network — and What Each Does

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Layer the letter** — Follow one real letter or parcel's journey. List its stages and match each to a network layer's job, in a two-column table.

## Task two
**Swap a layer** — Take a phone call's journey. Change one layer's technology (wired to mobile) and list what the other layers notice — and what they never do.
