# Hands-on — Keeping Our Network Secured

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Door audit** — Audit your class or home network's doors: who can join, what is shared, which passwords guard them. List the three weakest doors and the fix for each.

## Task two
**Write the class rules** — Draft your network's security rules as a one-page agreement — passwords, sharing, strangers, habits — and have every member sign it.
