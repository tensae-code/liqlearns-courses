# Keeping Our Network Secured — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where is a network secured?**
**Answer: At its doors — joining, opening, passwords**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a shared password called in this lesson?**
**Answer: A door with no lock**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How should sharing be set?**
**Answer: Deliberately, file by file**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is shared by accident, if careless?**
**Answer: The whole computer**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to a stranger's request to join?**
**Answer: It is stopped and asked about — never waved through**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What guards a network daily?**
**Answer: Habits**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name three guarding habits.**
**Answer: Logging out, locking screens, reporting strange messages**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What list does the class keep?**
**Answer: Who holds what access**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Whose behaviour is a network's security?**
**Answer: Everyone's on it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is one careless member to the rest?**
**Answer: Every member's risk**
