# Video script — Keeping Our Network Secured

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A network is secured at its **doors**: who may **join** it, who may **open** each shared thing, and what **passwords** guard both. The class's rules from the session: strong passwords that are never shared — a shared password is a door with no lock; sharing set deliberately, file by file, not the whole computer by accident; and care with **strangers' requests** — an unknown device or person asking to join or receive files is stopped and asked about, never waved through to be polite.
Tools guard a network once; **habits** guard it daily: logging out, locking screens, reporting the strange message instead of clicking it, and keeping the class's own list of who holds what access. The session's title is first-person plural — *we* will keep *our* network secured — because a network's security is the behaviour of everyone on it: one careless member is every member's risk. The security lessons of this course grow from this seed: the checklist, the policy, the cyber-intelligence unit all begin as a class keeping its own small network safe on purpose.
