# Mobile Communication Technology — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four generations and each one's gift.**
**Answer: 1G analogue voice; 2G digital voice and SMS; 3G mobile data; 4G fast data**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What arrived with 2G that outlived expectations?**
**Answer: SMS — the short written message**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What carries the subscriber's identity in GSM?**
**Answer: The SIM card**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does CDMA spread calls?**
**Answer: By codes — code division**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a cell?**
**Answer: The ground one base station serves**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Trace a call's journey.**
**Answer: Phone by radio to the nearest station, routed through switches, out at the far station to the other phone**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What ground must Mongolia's coverage cross?**
**Answer: Steppe distances — from Ulaanbaatar across the aimags along roads and settlements**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the technology called cellular?**
**Answer: Each station serves one cell of ground**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What closes the book's mobile lesson?**
**Answer: Safe and polite phone use**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do generation and family together tell you?**
**Answer: What a phone can do and where it can roam**
