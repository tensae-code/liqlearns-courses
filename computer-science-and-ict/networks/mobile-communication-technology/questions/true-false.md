# Mobile Communication Technology — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the four generations and each one's gift." is: 1G analogue voice; 2G digital voice and SMS; 3G mobile data; 4G fast data**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What arrived with 2G that outlived expectations?" is: The SIM card**
**Answer: False** — SMS — the short written message

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What carries the subscriber's identity in GSM?" is: The SIM card**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How does CDMA spread calls?" is: The ground one base station serves**
**Answer: False** — By codes — code division

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a cell?" is: The ground one base station serves**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Trace a call's journey." is: Steppe distances — from Ulaanbaatar across the aimags along roads and settlements**
**Answer: False** — Phone by radio to the nearest station, routed through switches, out at the far station to the other phone

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What ground must Mongolia's coverage cross?" is: Steppe distances — from Ulaanbaatar across the aimags along roads and settlements**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why is the technology called cellular?" is: Safe and polite phone use**
**Answer: False** — Each station serves one cell of ground

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What closes the book's mobile lesson?" is: Safe and polite phone use**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do generation and family together tell you?" is: 1G analogue voice; 2G digital voice and SMS; 3G mobile data; 4G fast data**
**Answer: False** — What a phone can do and where it can roam
