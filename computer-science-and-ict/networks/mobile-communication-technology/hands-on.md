# Hands-on — Mobile Communication Technology

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Place your phone', 'Find your phone's generation and family from its settings or markings. Write what each says it can do, and check one claim (its data speed or its SIM) in practice.')

## Task two
('Draw the call', 'Draw a call between two phones in different aimags: both radio hops and the network between. Mark the cells, the stations and the switch.')
