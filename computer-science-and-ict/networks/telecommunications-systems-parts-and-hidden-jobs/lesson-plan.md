# Lesson plan — Telecommunications systems — parts and hidden jobs

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Networks · **Lesson:** Telecommunications systems — parts and hidden jobs
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Networks in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 10 book, Unit 2 — Computer Network. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A **telecommunications system** moves information between places through **eight components**, doing hidden jobs — routing, error-checking, flow control, security — behind the user's back.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the media lessons — telecommunications is the whole system built on those media.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Telecommunications and its subset** — long-distance communication in any form; data communications is the computer subset
2. **The eight components** — the eight named components and the named applications
3. **The hidden jobs** — transmit, connect, route, error-check, convert, control flow, keep secure
4. **Five components keyed, and multiplexing (teacher guide — evidence)** — the guide's five; multiplexing defined; one-network payoff

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the media lessons of this title (transmission media through infrared). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define telecommunications and name its computer subset.
2. List the eight components.
3. List the hidden jobs the system does.
4. Define multiplexing from the guide's key.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Eight components — count them on fingers: hardware, media, networks, processors, software, providers, protocols, applications. The jobs are what the system does with them while you simply talk.
