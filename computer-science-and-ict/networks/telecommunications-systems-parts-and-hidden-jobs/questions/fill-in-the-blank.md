# Telecommunications systems — parts and hidden jobs — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.


**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The rules component of the system is communications ______.
**Answer: protocols**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The system must ______ by the most efficient path.
**Answer: route**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** The modem converts digital data for an ______ medium such as a telephone line.
**Answer: analogue**


**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Telecommunications is long-distance communication over common ______.
**Answer: carriers**
