# Telecommunications systems — parts and hidden jobs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Data communications is —
a) the whole of telecommunications  b) the computer subset of telecommunications  c) a kind of cable  d) a satellite orbit
**Answer: b) the computer subset of telecommunications** — its computer subset.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How many components make a telecommunications system?
a) five  b) six  c) eight  d) ten
**Answer: c) eight** — eight components.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a communications application named in the lesson?
a) crimping  b) electronic funds transfer  c) drive letters  d) cell division
**Answer: b) electronic funds transfer** — electronic funds transfer.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Routing by the most efficient path is —
a) done by the user  b) one of the system's hidden jobs  c) done by the receiver's screen  d) not the system's work
**Answer: b) one of the system's hidden jobs** — a hidden job.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Multiplexing is —
a) sending one signal twice  b) combining multiple signals into one signal over a shared medium  c) converting digital to analogue  d) dividing a country into cells
**Answer: b) combining multiple signals into one signal over a shared medium** — the guide's definition.
