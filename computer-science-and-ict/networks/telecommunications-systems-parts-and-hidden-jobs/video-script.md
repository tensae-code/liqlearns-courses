# Video script — Telecommunications systems — parts and hidden jobs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Telecommunications moves information far.
Voice. Data. Text. Pictures. Sound. Video.

Eight parts work together:
hardware, media, networks, processors,
software, providers, protocols, applications.

Behind your back, the system routes,
checks for errors, controls the flow —
and keeps it all secure.
