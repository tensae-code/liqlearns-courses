# Bus topology

## Do it first

Picture houses along one water main: each house taps into the same pipe. Now replace the pipe with one long cable and the houses with computers. Draw it before you read on.

## One backbone, every node tapped in

In a **bus topology**, **all nodes tap into one long backbone cable — the bus** — like houses along a water main. Every message travels the one shared cable.

## Cheap and easy — with three costs

It is **cheap, easy to install, and uses little cable**. Against that: **faults are hard to isolate**, **adding nodes is inflexible** (tap spacing is optimised for the bus length), and **a break anywhere in the bus stops the whole network**. One cable is both the bus's strength and its single point of failure.
