# Bus topology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In a bus topology, all nodes —
a) have their own switch  b) tap into one long backbone cable  c) form a ring  d) connect to every other node
**Answer: b) tap into one long backbone cable** — they tap into the one bus.

**2.** The bus is compared to —
a) a taxi route  b) a water main with houses along it  c) a spider web  d) a circle
**Answer: b) a water main with houses along it** — houses along a water main.

**3.** An advantage of the bus is —
a) faults are easy to isolate  b) it is cheap and uses little cable  c) it survives any break  d) it needs no cable
**Answer: b) it is cheap and uses little cable** — cheap, easy, little cable.

**4.** A break in the bus —
a) affects one node  b) stops the whole network  c) slows one printer  d) does nothing
**Answer: b) stops the whole network** — the whole network stops.

**5.** Faults on a bus are —
a) easy to isolate  b) hard to isolate  c) impossible to cause  d) fixed automatically
**Answer: b) hard to isolate** — hard to isolate.
