# Secure Communication Over the Internet — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three dangers on a public road.**
**Answer: Overhearing, changing, impersonation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is impersonation?**
**Answer: A stranger pretending to be the sender or the bank**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why name dangers before cures?**
**Answer: Each cure answers a named danger**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What travels the internet in the opener?**
**Answer: Banks and letters, safely**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What may anyone do beside a public road?**
**Answer: Stand and watch the carts pass**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does encryption make watchers see?**
**Answer: Noise**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What proves who is at the other end?**
**Answer: Certificates and known addresses**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What guards the accounts themselves?**
**Answer: Passwords and second factors**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where do most attacks actually enter?**
**Answer: The human end — user care**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What defeats the strongest lock?**
**Answer: The user handing over the key**
