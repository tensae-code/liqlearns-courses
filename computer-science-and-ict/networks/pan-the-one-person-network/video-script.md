The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

A Personal Area Network (PAN) is the smallest network by size: organised around one person, within about a metre to a room. The distance ladder runs 1 m for a PAN, 10 m for a room, and up the scale to 10,000 km for the planet — the sizes ahead (LAN, MAN, WAN) climb that ladder.

Now look at the picture. Name each part.
That is pan — the one-person network.
