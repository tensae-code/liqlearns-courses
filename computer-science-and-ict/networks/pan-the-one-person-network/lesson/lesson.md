# PAN — the one-person network

## Do it first

Stretch out your arms. Everything you can reach — phone, laptop, the printer beside the couch — is one person's small network. Measure this lesson with your own arms.

## The smallest network — around one person

A **Personal Area Network (PAN)** is the **smallest network by size**: organised **around one person**, within about **a metre to a room**. The distance ladder runs **1 m for a PAN, 10 m for a room**, and up the scale to **10,000 km for the planet** — the sizes ahead (LAN, MAN, WAN) climb that ladder.

## Bluetooth is a PAN

The common example is a computer **wirelessly joined to its peripherals**, and a **Bluetooth network is a PAN**. Devices with Bluetooth need **no cables**: put them down, switch them on, and they work together — sending a document to the office printer from the couch, uploading a phone photo to a desktop, or streaming a film to the TV.
