# PAN — the one-person network — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** PAN stands for —
a) Public Area Network  b) Personal Area Network  c) Planet Area Network  d) Printer Access Network
**Answer: b) Personal Area Network** — Personal Area Network.

**2.** A PAN is organised around —
a) a city  b) one person  c) a country  d) a school
**Answer: b) one person** — one person.

**3.** A PAN spans about —
a) a metre to a room  b) a city  c) a country  d) 10,000 km
**Answer: a) a metre to a room** — a metre to a room.

**4.** A Bluetooth network is a —
a) WAN  b) MAN  c) PAN  d) bus
**Answer: c) PAN** — a PAN.

**5.** On the distance ladder, a room is —
a) 1 m  b) 10 m  c) 100 m  d) 10,000 km
**Answer: b) 10 m** — 10 m.
