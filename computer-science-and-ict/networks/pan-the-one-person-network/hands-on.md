# Hands-on — PAN — the one-person network

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Arm's-reach network
Sit with a phone, a laptop and one more device within reach. List the PAN you could make and what each link would do.

## Ladder steps
Mark 1 m and 10 m on the floor with string. Stand in each and say which network size you are standing in.
