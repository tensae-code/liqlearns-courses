# Video script — What networks are good for

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sharing data and information — documents, images, songs, videos, and data held on a central database server. Communication — chat and instant messaging (Skype, WhatsApp, Viber are named), video conferencing, email, TV and radio; distant communication is telecommunication. Sharing hardware — shared storage (NAS), one printer for all through a print server, fax machines, scanners, supercomputers. Sharing software and Internet services, including e-learning. And transferring money digitally — payroll managed and paid into employees' bank accounts.

Now look at the picture. Name each part.
That is what networks are good for.
