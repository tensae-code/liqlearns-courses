# What networks are good for — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** Networks let many people share one scanner.
**Answer: True** — hardware sharing includes scanners and fax machines.

**2.** Video conferencing is a network communication benefit.
**Answer: True** — named under communication.

**3.** Networks are good only for games.
**Answer: False** — five families of benefit are taught.
