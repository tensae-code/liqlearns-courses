# What networks are good for — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1.** Shared storage on a network is called ______ (network-attached storage).
**Answer: NAS**

**2.** Chat, email and video conferencing are ______.
**Answer: communication**

**3.** ______ can be paid into employees' bank accounts by network.
**Answer: Payroll / wages**
