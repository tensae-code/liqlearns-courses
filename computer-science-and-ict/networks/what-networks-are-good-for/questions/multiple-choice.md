# What networks are good for — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Distant communication is called —
a) telecommunication  b) teleportation data  c) a workgroup  d) routing
**Answer: a) telecommunication** — telecommunication.

**2.** One printer for all works through a —
a) print server  b) repeater only  c) bus break  d) firewall
**Answer: a) print server** — a print server.

**3.** Which is named as a chat / instant-messaging example?
a) WhatsApp  b) a spreadsheet  c) Paint  d) a flowchart
**Answer: a) WhatsApp** — WhatsApp (with Skype and Viber).

**4.** Data for everyone can be held on a —
a) central database server  b) single floppy  c) printer tray  d) cable
**Answer: a) central database server** — a central database server.

**5.** E-learning belongs to —
a) sharing hardware  b) sharing software and Internet services  c) money transfer  d) viruses
**Answer: b) sharing software and Internet services** — sharing software and Internet services.

**6.** Payroll by network means —
a) cash in envelopes  b) pay managed and paid into employees' bank accounts  c) no pay records  d) printing wages
**Answer: b) pay managed and paid into employees' bank accounts** — paid into bank accounts.
