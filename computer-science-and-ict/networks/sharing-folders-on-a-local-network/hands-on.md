# Hands-on — Sharing Folders on a Local Network

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Share and govern', 'Share one practice folder on the school network with read-only permission for the class. From a second computer, open it and prove you can read but not change a file.')

## Task two
('Number the house', 'Run ipconfig on your computer, record its IP address, and write the network path by which a classmate would reach your shared folder.')
