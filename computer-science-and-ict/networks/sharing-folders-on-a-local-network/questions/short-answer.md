# Sharing Folders on a Local Network — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does sharing a folder do?**
**Answer: Offers it to the local network while it stays on its own computer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What should be shared, by the book's habit?**
**Answer: The folder the work needs — not the whole disk**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) By what is a shared folder reached?**
**Answer: Its network path through its owner's computer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do sharing permissions decide?**
**Answer: Who may only read and who may change the folder**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What may the class do with the materials folder?**
**Answer: Read it — only its owner writes there**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a computer known by on the network?**
**Answer: Its IP address**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which command finds your own IP address?**
**Answer: ipconfig, at the command line**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does ipconfig print?**
**Answer: The machine's addresses**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name the lesson's pair.**
**Answer: The door opened and governed; the house numbered**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) In which unit does this lesson stand?**
**Answer: Mongolia's Grade 9 networks unit**
