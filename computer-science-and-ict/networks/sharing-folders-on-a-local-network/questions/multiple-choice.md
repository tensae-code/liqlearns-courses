# Sharing Folders on a Local Network — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does sharing a folder do?**
a) Offers it to the local network while it stays on its own computer  b) The folder the work needs — not the whole disk  c) Its network path through its owner's computer  d) Who may only read and who may change the folder
**Answer: a) Offers it to the local network while it stays on its own computer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What should be shared, by the book's habit?**
a) Its network path through its owner's computer  b) The folder the work needs — not the whole disk  c) Who may only read and who may change the folder  d) Read it — only its owner writes there
**Answer: b) The folder the work needs — not the whole disk**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) By what is a shared folder reached?**
a) Who may only read and who may change the folder  b) Read it — only its owner writes there  c) Its network path through its owner's computer  d) Its IP address
**Answer: c) Its network path through its owner's computer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do sharing permissions decide?**
a) Read it — only its owner writes there  b) Its IP address  c) ipconfig, at the command line  d) Who may only read and who may change the folder
**Answer: d) Who may only read and who may change the folder**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What may the class do with the materials folder?**
