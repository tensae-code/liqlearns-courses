# Organisational Networks — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an intranet?**
**Answer: A private network inside one organisation, using internet ways — pages, browsers, email**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who may enter an intranet?**
**Answer: Only the organisation's own people**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a school intranet carry?**
**Answer: Its notices, shared files and internal mail**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the intranet's value, in Fiji's phrase?**
**Answer: Exactly its wall — internet tools with the public left outside**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Are intranet pages web pages?**
**Answer: Yes — web pages and browsers, on a private network**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is an extranet?**
**Answer: Part of an intranet opened to chosen outsiders**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give an extranet example.**
**Answer: Suppliers checking orders, or parents reading reports**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How are extranet visitors let in?**
**Answer: To their part only, by account and password**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the third circle?**
**Answer: The internet itself — the public network everyone may enter**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name Fiji's three circles.**
**Answer: Intranet (ours), extranet (ours, shared by invitation), internet (everyone's)**
