**Acceptance count: taught in 6 countries (Ethiopia, Laos, Greece, Bangladesh, Jordan, Pakistan).**

The Grade 9 teacher guide's closing answer keys (guide placement item 27; Unit 2 keys at guide printed pp. 36–37) were used as the verified-answer check for this lesson's question files.

Mauritania's own way (Grade 9): The final secondary year is five practical blocks in 30-minute fiches — a full PowerPoint sequence, internet ethics, a first formal algorithms unit (variables, conditions, loops, writing and testing algorithms, no named programming language), simple networks, and email with attachments — each block closed by evaluation fiches.

Laos's own way (Grade 12 ICT student book): the final year is a modern, applied course — problem-solving method, a long English-titled internet-safety sequence (Internet Safety, Email Tips for Scams and Spam, Smart Social Networking, Cyber harassment/Stalking/Addiction), data summaries and PivotTables for decision-making, copyright and Creative Commons licensing, a full PowerPoint course, and choosing technology for your future occupation.

Greece's own way (Grade 8, Pliroforiki B' Gymnasiou, 2024): Grade 8 moves from bits/bytes and data rates into networks, then data analysis in spreadsheets and presentations, then modular Scratch programming (own blocks with parameters, realistic character motion), list data structures, a full AI unit training a neural network, and ICT's changes to the economy (e-commerce, Industry 4.0, new jobs, sustainability).

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 6, Digital Technology): the Competency-based National Curriculum 2022 book is organised as nine project "Learning Experiences" done in groups through stories, games and fieldwork — no conventional chapters; even learners without a computer at home learn technology through experience around school and home. The NCTB Digital Technology line runs in parallel English and Bangla editions; placements are cited from the English Version with the Bangla Version parallel spot-checked.

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 8, Digital Technology): Grade 8 deepens the same project pattern — a public information-verification campaign, a family phone-safety investigation, a planned civic/e-commerce service, Python with binary logic first, and an online fair for the Asia-Pacific experience. The NCTB Digital Technology line runs in parallel English and Bangla editions; placements are cited from the English Version with the Bangla Version parallel spot-checked.

Jordan's own way (Jordan Grade 7 G7T1 Student): Grade 7 starts the whole subject from zero — what a computer is and how it works, hardware inside and out, software, operating systems and human-computer interaction — then networks from definition through topologies to secure communication and network performance.


Pakistan's own way (Grade 8 Computer Education 8): the Grade 8 book is the older National-Curriculum-2006 Punjab book (not SNC): it teaches networks through physical media and cutting-edge wireless (cellular, satellite, GPS, Bluetooth), a whole unit of hands-on antivirus management (definitions, updates, an AVG whole-computer scan), Excel 2007 taught through three full worked reports (monthly expenses, salaries, school results), and programming in GW-BASIC with line numbers — the pre-SNC Punjab way into code.

Pakistan's own way (Grade 9 Computer Science 9): Grade 9 is a full problem-solving course before any coding — candid solutions, the 5 Ws of problem analysis, test data in five types, trace tables, verification vs validation — then number systems carried through to Boolean algebra with truth tables, networks taught through the postal-system analogy for TCP/IP layers, a data-and-privacy unit built on Code.org cipher widgets, NADRA and Pakistan's NR3C cybercrime centre, and a first website built page by page as "Pakistan.html".
