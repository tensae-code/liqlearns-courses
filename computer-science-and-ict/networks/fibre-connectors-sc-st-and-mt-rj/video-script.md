# Video script — Fibre connectors — SC, ST, MT-RJ

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three connectors finish a fibre.

SC — subscriber channel. Push, pull. For cable TV.
ST — straight-tip. Bayonet lock. To networking devices.
MT-RJ — small as an RJ45.

Push. Twist. RJ45-size.
