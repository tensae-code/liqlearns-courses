# Fibre connectors — SC, ST, MT-RJ

## Do it first

Three plugs, three ways of locking on. Push-pull, twist-lock, and one the size of a plug you already know. Match each name to its lock.

## Three connectors for fibre

Three connectors are taught for fibre-optic cable: **SC, ST and MT-RJ**. Each locks on its own way.

[Picture 14511 — SC, ST and MT-RJ in a row]

## SC and ST

The **subscriber channel (SC)** connector is **used for cable TV** with a **push/pull lock**. The **straight-tip (ST)** connector is used to **connect cables to networking devices**, with a **bayonet lock**, and is **more reliable than SC**.

## MT-RJ

**MT-RJ** is a connector the **same size as an RJ45** — the network plug from the UTP lesson.
