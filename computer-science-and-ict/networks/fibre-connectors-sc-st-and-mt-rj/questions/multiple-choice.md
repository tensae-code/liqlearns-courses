# Fibre connectors — SC, ST, MT-RJ — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1.** The SC connector locks by —
a) bayonet  b) push/pull  c) crimping  d) screwing a terminator
**Answer: b) push/pull** — push/pull lock.

**2.** SC is used for —
a) cable TV  b) satellite uplinks  c) crimping UTP  d) telephone only
**Answer: a) cable TV** — used for cable TV.

**3.** The ST connector connects cables to —
a) televisions only  b) networking devices  c) power supplies  d) antennas
**Answer: b) networking devices** — cables to networking devices.

**4.** Which fibre connector is more reliable than SC?
a) MT-RJ  b) ST  c) RJ-11  d) BNC
**Answer: b) ST** — ST — bayonet lock, more reliable than SC.

**5.** MT-RJ is the same size as —
a) a BNC T  b) an RJ45  c) a crimper  d) an SC
**Answer: b) an RJ45** — the same size as an RJ45.
