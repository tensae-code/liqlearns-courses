# Hands-on — Fibre connectors — SC, ST, MT-RJ

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.


## Lock mime
Mime each lock with your hands as a partner names a connector: push-pull for SC, twist for ST, hold up RJ45-size fingers for MT-RJ.

## Connector cards
Three cards, one per connector; write the lock and use on the back. Test each other until all three are instant.
