## Sub lesson 1 — Comparison between internet and intranet
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The internet and an intranet use the same technology but differ in ownership and access: the internet is public and worldwide, an intranet belongs to one organisation and admits only its members; The learner compares the two side by side — who owns it, who may enter, what it is used for — and places real examples on the right side; In Secondary ICT, South Sudan Grade 10 S2 Student, printed p. 21 — Comparison between internet and intranet; Because the similar names hide an opposite difference — open to everyone against open to members only — and confusing them risks real information; The book's own order for comparison between internet and intranet — and how to show it in practice, not only name it.
