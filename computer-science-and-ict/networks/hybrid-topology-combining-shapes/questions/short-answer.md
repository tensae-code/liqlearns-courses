# Hybrid Topology — Combining Shapes — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a hybrid topology?**
**Answer: A network combining two or more basic topologies**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two common combinations.**
**Answer: Star-bus and star-ring (also tree with a mesh core)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is a hybrid chosen?**
**Answer: Different parts of a real network have different needs**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are the pure topologies, in the lesson's image?**
**Answer: The alphabet — the hybrid is the sentence**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Does each part keep its character in a hybrid?**
**Answer: Yes — its strengths come with it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's great hybrid example?**
**Answer: The internet**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the hybrid's two gifts.**
**Answer: Fit to needs, and growth without redesigning the whole**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the hybrid's cost.**
**Answer: Complexity where shapes meet — and faults hiding at the joins**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where do faults love to hide in a hybrid?**
**Answer: At the joining points**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What three verbs end the topologies teaching?**
**Answer: Choose per part, join with care, document the whole**
