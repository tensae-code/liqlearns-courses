# Hands-on — Hybrid Topology — Combining Shapes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Design the hybrid** — Design a network for a school with a science block, an office and a library: choose a topology per part, name the joining devices, and defend the joins.

## Task two
**Read a real hybrid** — Sketch the internet's hybrid nature in one diagram: four different shapes inside it, labelled, with the joins marked as its danger points.
