# Hands-on — Hybrid Networks — Mixing Peer-to-Peer and Client-Server

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Audit a real network** — Take your school's network as far as you know it. List its services and mark each client-server or peer-to-peer; the mix you find is its hybrid map.

## Task two
**Design the mix** — Design the network for a 20-person office: five services, a structure chosen for each, and the reason spoken aloud.
