# Hybrid Networks — Mixing Peer-to-Peer and Client-Server — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In peer-to-peer, what are the computers?**
**Answer: Equals — each shares its own, none is in charge**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name peer-to-peer's weakness.**
**Answer: Hard to secure and back up as it grows**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do servers hold in client-server?**
**Answer: The shared data and services**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is client-server's price?**
**Answer: The server and its keeper**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which lesson weighs the pure forms?**
**Answer: The client-server and peer-to-peer comparison lesson**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a hybrid network?**
**Answer: A mix of peer-to-peer and client-server structures**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where does client-server belong in a hybrid?**
**Answer: Where centrality earns its cost — accounts, records, backups**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does peer-to-peer belong?**
**Answer: Where directness serves better — a workgroup's own files and printer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Are most real networks hybrid?**
**Answer: Yes — named or not**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the designer's real question?**
**Answer: Which structure fits this service — asked service by service**
