# Hands-on — Infrared — short-range invisible light

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.


## Remote test
Press the remote at the TV from near and from far, and through a book held in between. Record what works — infrared is short-range and needs its path.

## Guide's list hunt
Find or name one thing at home for each guide use: heater, cooker, remote, security system, thermal camera.
