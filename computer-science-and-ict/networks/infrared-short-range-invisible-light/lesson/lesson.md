# Infrared — short-range invisible light

## Do it first

Press a TV remote and the channel changes — but you saw nothing travel. Something invisible crossed the room. This lesson names it.

## Longer than visible light, so invisible

**Infrared** is electromagnetic radiation with **wavelengths longer than visible light** — and so **invisible to the eye**.

[Picture 14518 — a remote's invisible beam striking a TV]

## The remote control's light — and its range

Its everyday exhibit is the **television remote control changing channels**. Infrared is the **short-range** medium — used **between a PC and a peripheral** and for **indoor LANs**. The guide also keys infrared to **heaters, cookers, remote controls, security systems and thermal-imaging cameras** (teacher guide — evidence).
