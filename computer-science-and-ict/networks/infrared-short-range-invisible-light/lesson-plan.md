# Lesson plan — Infrared — short-range invisible light

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Networks · **Lesson:** Infrared — short-range invisible light
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Networks in the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); stocked from Ethiopia's Grade 10 book, Unit 2 — Computer Network. The ladder is the recommended order, never a forced one.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** **Infrared** is **invisible** electromagnetic radiation with **wavelengths longer than visible light** — the remote control's beam, and a **short-range** medium.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the unguided-media lessons — infrared is the shortest-range member of the family.
