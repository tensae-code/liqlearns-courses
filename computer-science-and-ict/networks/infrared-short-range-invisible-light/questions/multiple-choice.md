# Infrared — short-range invisible light — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1.** Infrared wavelengths are —
a) shorter than visible light  b) longer than visible light  c) the same as radio  d) made of copper
**Answer: b) longer than visible light** — longer than visible light.

**2.** Infrared is invisible because —
a) it is too fast  b) its wavelength is longer than visible light  c) it travels at night  d) it is blocked by air
**Answer: b) its wavelength is longer than visible light** — longer than the light the eye sees.

**3.** The everyday exhibit of infrared is —
a) a satellite dish  b) the television remote control  c) a fibre cable  d) a microwave horn
**Answer: b) the television remote control** — the TV remote control.

**4.** Infrared is placed as the —
a) longest-range medium  b) short-range medium  c) wired medium  d) satellite medium
**Answer: b) short-range medium** — the short-range medium.

**5.** The guide keys infrared also to —
a) crimpers  b) heaters, cookers, security systems and thermal-imaging cameras  c) drive letters  d) cell towers only
**Answer: b) heaters, cookers, security systems and thermal-imaging cameras** — the guide's keyed list.
