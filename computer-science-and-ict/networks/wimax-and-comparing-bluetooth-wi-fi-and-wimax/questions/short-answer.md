# WiMAX — and Comparing Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and WiMAX — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is WiMAX?**
**Answer: Broadband wireless at metropolitan scale**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How wide an area can one WiMAX base station serve?**
**Answer: Kilometres**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What problem does WiMAX answer?**
**Answer: Internet where laying cables is slow or expensive**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does WiMAX sit in the network ladder?**
**Answer: Where MAN sits — between LAN and WAN**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is WiMAX wired at any stage of its own service?**
**Answer: No — wireless all through**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three compared technologies.**
**Answer: Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and WiMAX**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) By what three measures does the table compare them?**
**Answer: Standard, speed and range**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) State the table's one law.**
**Answer: Range, speed and power trade against each other**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Match the classroom to its technology.**
**Answer: Wi-Fi**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why do all three technologies exist?**
**Answer: No one of them wins range, speed and power together**
