# Hands-on — WiMAX — and Comparing Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and WiMAX

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Build the table** — Draw the comparison table from memory — the three technologies against standard family, range and use — then check it against the lesson.

## Task two
**Match the errand** — For six errands (print from a phone, connect a village school, stream in a café, link two offices across town, wireless earbuds, a farm's sensors) choose the scale and defend it.
