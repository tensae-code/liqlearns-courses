# Intranet a Private Network Inside An Organisation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is intranet?**
**Answer: An intranet is a private network inside one organisation that uses internet technology — web pages, browsers, file sharing — but is open only to the organisation's own people**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the key work in intranet?**
**Answer: The learner identifies what an intranet offers inside a school or office — shared files, internal pages, shared resources — and who may use it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does South Sudan teach intranet?**
**Answer: In Secondary ICT, South Sudan Grade 10 S2 Student, printed p. 21 — Intranet**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does intranet matter?**
**Answer: Because an organisation needs the internet's easy tools without the internet's open door; the intranet is that private version**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What should a learner remember about intranet?**
**Answer: The book's own order for intranet — and how to show it in practice, not only name it**
