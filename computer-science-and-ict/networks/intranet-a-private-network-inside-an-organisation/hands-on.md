# Hands-on — Intranet a Private Network Inside An Organisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Do the book's steps** — Work through Intranet exactly as South Sudan's book orders it, on a real computer if one is available; otherwise write the steps in order and check them against the book's order.

## Task two
**Show it to someone** — Take Intranet and file sharing in an intranet set-up and show a partner what it does: do it once slowly, naming each step, then let the partner do it while you only watch and correct.
