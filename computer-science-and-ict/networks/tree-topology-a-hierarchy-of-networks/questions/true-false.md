# Tree Topology — a Hierarchy of Networks — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Hierarchical topology**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Hierarchical topology** — as the answer to: What stands at the top of the tree?
**Answer: False — A root device**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Stars at the leaves and a bus-like backbone at the trunk**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Stars at the leaves and a bus-like backbone at the trunk** — as the answer to: What does each level's centre manage?
**Answer: False — Its own branch**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A family tree or a company chart**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A family tree or a company chart** — as the answer to: Name two gifts of the tree.
**Answer: False — Growth branch by branch, and fault isolation**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Failure near the top fells everything beneath**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Failure near the top fells everything beneath** — as the answer to: Which links bear the heaviest traffic?
**Answer: False — The top links near the root**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Strong root devices and backup links at the trunk**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Strong root devices and backup links at the trunk** — as the answer to: What does the tree's shape match in human life?
**Answer: False — How institutions themselves are built**
