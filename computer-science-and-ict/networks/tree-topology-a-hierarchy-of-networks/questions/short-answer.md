# Tree Topology — a Hierarchy of Networks — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is tree topology also called?**
**Answer: Hierarchical topology**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What stands at the top of the tree?**
**Answer: A root device**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What shapes is the tree a hybrid of?**
**Answer: Stars at the leaves and a bus-like backbone at the trunk**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does each level's centre manage?**
**Answer: Its own branch**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What everyday charts does the tree resemble?**
**Answer: A family tree or a company chart**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name two gifts of the tree.**
**Answer: Growth branch by branch, and fault isolation**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the tree's weakness?**
**Answer: Failure near the top fells everything beneath**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which links bear the heaviest traffic?**
**Answer: The top links near the root**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do builders protect the tree?**
**Answer: Strong root devices and backup links at the trunk**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the tree's shape match in human life?**
**Answer: How institutions themselves are built**
