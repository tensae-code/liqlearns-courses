# Hands-on — Tree Topology — a Hierarchy of Networks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Grow the tree** — Draw a three-level tree network for a school: root, three branch centres, six device groups. Mark where one branch failure stops and where a root failure reaches.

## Task two
**Topology against errand** — Argue for one topology — bus, star, ring, mesh or tree — for a hospital, a home and a university, naming the weakness you accept in each.
