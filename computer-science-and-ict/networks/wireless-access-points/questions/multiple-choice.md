# Wireless access points — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** WAP stands for —
a) Wired Access Port  b) Wireless Access Point  c) Web Address Page  d) Wide Area Protocol
**Answer: b) Wireless Access Point** — Wireless Access Point.

**2.** A WAP connects —
a) wireless devices to a wireless network  b) printers to paper  c) cables to cables only  d) files to folders
**Answer: a) wireless devices to a wireless network** — wireless devices to the network.

**3.** A WAP works through —
a) Bluetooth and Wi-Fi  b) only cables  c) only satellites  d) only infrared printers
**Answer: a) Bluetooth and Wi-Fi** — Bluetooth and Wi-Fi.

**4.** A WAP is a kind of —
a) end device  b) intermediary device  c) protocol  d) medium only
**Answer: b) intermediary device** — an intermediary device.

**5.** The WAP stands between a network's —
a) two printers  b) wireless and wired devices  c) two screens  d) sender and file
**Answer: b) wireless and wired devices** — wireless and wired devices.

**6.** A wireless LAN carries a wired LAN's service over —
a) fibre only  b) radio frequency  c) infrared only  d) coaxial cable
**Answer: b) radio frequency** — a LAN's service over radio frequency.

**7.** Larger WLAN areas are served by several APs overlapping by —
a) 1–2%  b) 10–15%  c) 50%  d) 100%
**Answer: b) 10–15%** — overlapping by 10–15%.
