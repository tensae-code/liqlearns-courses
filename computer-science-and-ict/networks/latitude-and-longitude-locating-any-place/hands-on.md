# Hands-on — Latitude and Longitude — Locating Any Place

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cross the circles
On a world map or globe, find the place where latitude 0° meets longitude 0° (what is there?). Then find where 90° N meets any longitude — why does the longitude stop mattering there? Write both answers in one sentence each.

## Convert both ways
Convert 48.8566° to DMS (answer near 48° 51′ 24″). Then convert 2° 21′ 00″ E to decimal degrees. Finally, look up or estimate the coordinates of your own school or home town in both writings, with its altitude if you can find it.
