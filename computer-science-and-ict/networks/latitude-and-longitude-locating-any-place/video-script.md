# Video script — Latitude and Longitude — Locating Any Place

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every place on Earth has an address of two numbers.

Latitude first. How far from the equator? Zero at the equator, ninety at the poles. North or south.

Longitude second. How far from the line through Greenwich? Zero there, up to one hundred and eighty, east or west.

One number alone is a whole circle of places. The two together cross at exactly one point. Your point.

Write them as decimals — or in degrees, minutes and seconds, split in sixtieths like a clock. And when height matters, altitude joins as the third number.
