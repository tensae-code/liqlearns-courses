# Latitude and Longitude — Locating Any Place — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Latitude measures a place’s angle from —**
a) Greenwich  b) The equator  c) The North Pole only  d) Sea level
**Answer: b) The equator — 0° at the equator, rising to 90° at the poles, N or S.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The prime meridian — longitude 0° — passes through —**
a) Paris  b) The equator  c) Greenwich, London  d) New York
**Answer: c) Greenwich, London — Chosen by agreement, since nature offers no north–south zero line.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) 0.5° expressed in minutes is —**
a) 50′  b) 30′  c) 5′  d) 500′
**Answer: b) 30′ — Minutes are sixtieths: 0.5 × 60 = 30.**
