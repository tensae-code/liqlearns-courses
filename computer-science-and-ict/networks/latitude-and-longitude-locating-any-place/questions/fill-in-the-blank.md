# Latitude and Longitude — Locating Any Place — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) In DMS writing, 60 seconds make one ____.**
**Answer: minute — And 60 minutes make one degree.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The third number that fixes a place in space, beyond latitude and longitude, is its ____.**
**Answer: altitude — Height above sea level, in metres.**
