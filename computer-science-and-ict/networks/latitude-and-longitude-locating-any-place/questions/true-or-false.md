# Latitude and Longitude — Locating Any Place — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A latitude of 120° N is possible for a place near the pole.**
**Answer: False — Latitude stops at 90°, at the poles themselves; 120° must be a longitude or an error.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) One coordinate alone — only a latitude — fixes a single point on Earth.**
**Answer: False — A latitude alone names a whole circle of places; the pair of coordinates fixes the point.**
