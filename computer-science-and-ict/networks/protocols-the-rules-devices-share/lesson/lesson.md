# Protocols — the rules devices share

## Do it first

Try to hold a conversation where one person follows the rules of a formal letter and the other answers with football signals. It fails — not from lack of words, but lack of shared rules. Devices have the same problem.

## Shared rules for talking machines

A **protocol** is the **set of rules governing data communication**. Devices must **follow the same rules to interact**, and **protocol software instructs devices how to connect and talk to one another**.

## Internet Protocol (IP)

**Internet Protocol (IP)** is named as a key example — it is used for **addressing devices** and **forwarding data between networks**. Its detail is promised for Grade 10; here, plant the name and its two jobs.

## Rules people follow too (teacher guide — evidence)

People follow **written and oral rules** for talking, eating, dressing and letter-writing — and computers likewise need a **common rule**, or the communicating parties **cannot understand each other**. The guide also names the providers on the ground: **Ethio-telecom and Safaricom Ethiopia** install the cables and wireless antennas across cities that connect their customers.
