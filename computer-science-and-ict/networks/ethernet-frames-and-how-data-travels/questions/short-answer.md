# Ethernet Frames and How Data Travels — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) A captured frame begins (after the preamble) with the bytes 3C 7A 9F 10 22 5B B8 21 … State the destination MAC address and how you know it is the destination.**
**Answer: The destination MAC is 3C:7A:9F:10:22:5B — the first six bytes after the preamble are always the destination address; the source address follows them.**
