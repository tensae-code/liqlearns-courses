# Hands-on — Wi-Fi — Wireless LAN Technology

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Survey your air** — With a phone, list the Wi-Fi networks visible at school. Note which are secured and which open, and what that teaches about range.

## Task two
**Harden the home network** — Write the four-step checklist for a family router from this lesson, in the order you would do them, with the reason for each.
