# Wi-Fi — Wireless LAN Technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is Wi-Fi?**
a) Wireless LAN technology — devices joining a network by radio  b) An access point  c) 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz  d) 2.4 GHz
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do devices connect to?**
a) An access point  b) 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz  c) 2.4 GHz  d) 5 GHz, over shorter reach
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name Wi-Fi's two bands.**
a) 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz  b) 2.4 GHz  c) 5 GHz, over shorter reach  d) Radio crosses walls — the network extends into the street
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which band travels farther?**
a) 2.4 GHz  b) 5 GHz, over shorter reach  c) Radio crosses walls — the network extends into the street  d) Listening in (also password guessing, false access points)
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which band carries more speed?**
a) 5 GHz, over shorter reach  b) Radio crosses walls — the network extends into the street  c) Listening in (also password guessing, false access points)  d) A strong network password and WPA2/WPA3 encryption (also changing the router admin password)
**Answer: a**

**Answer: a**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name one attack the open air allows.**

a) A strong network password and WPA2/WPA3 encryption (also changing the router admin password)  b) That other listeners are present  c) The wired network and the internet  d) Wireless LAN technology — devices joining a network by radio

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must a user assume on an open café network?**
a) That other listeners are present  b) The wired network and the internet  c) Wireless LAN technology — devices joining a network by radio  d) An access point
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the access point join the wireless devices to?**
a) The wired network and the internet  b) Wireless LAN technology — devices joining a network by radio  c) An access point  d) 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz
