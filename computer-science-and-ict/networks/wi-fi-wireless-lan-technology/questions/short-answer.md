# Wi-Fi — Wireless LAN Technology — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is Wi-Fi?**
**Answer: Wireless LAN technology — devices joining a network by radio**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do devices connect to?**
**Answer: An access point**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name Wi-Fi's two bands.**
**Answer: 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which band travels farther?**
**Answer: 2.4 GHz**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which band carries more speed?**
**Answer: 5 GHz, over shorter reach**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why is range also risk?**
**Answer: Radio crosses walls — the network extends into the street**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name one attack the open air allows.**
**Answer: Listening in (also password guessing, false access points)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two defences.**
**Answer: A strong network password and WPA2/WPA3 encryption (also changing the router admin password)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must a user assume on an open café network?**
**Answer: That other listeners are present**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the access point join the wireless devices to?**
**Answer: The wired network and the internet**
