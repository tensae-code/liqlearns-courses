# Hands-on — MAC Address — the Physical Address

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a MAC address
Write out a MAC address in six pairs and count the pairs aloud; then say which part of a computer carries it.

## IP or MAC?
Take three statements your teacher reads — an address that changes at a new school, an address fixed in the card, an address that routes across the world — and name each IP or MAC.
