# MAC Address — the Physical Address — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a MAC address?**
**Answer: The physical address fixed into a network card when it was made**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the MAC address name?**
**Answer: The device itself, wherever it goes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a MAC address written?**
**Answer: As six pairs of hexadecimal digits separated by colons or dashes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Can two network cards share one MAC address?**
**Answer: No — each card carries a different one**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does an IP address name?**
**Answer: A device's place on a network — its logical address**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which address changes when a device joins a new network?**
**Answer: The IP address**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which address stays with the device for life?**
**Answer: The MAC address**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the IP address's job in delivery?**
**Answer: To route data across networks to the right building**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the MAC address's job in delivery?**
**Answer: To hand the data to the exact card inside that building**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Say the difference in one line.**
**Answer: IP is where you are; MAC is who you are**
