# MAC Address — the Physical Address — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a MAC address?**
a) The physical address fixed into a network card when it was made  b) The device itself, wherever it goes  c) No — each card carries a different one  d) The IP address
**Answer: a) The physical address fixed into a network card when it was made**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the MAC address name?**
a) As six pairs of hexadecimal digits separated by colons or dashes  b) The device itself, wherever it goes  c) A device's place on a network — its logical address  d) The MAC address
**Answer: b) The device itself, wherever it goes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a MAC address written?**
a) No — each card carries a different one  b) The IP address  c) As six pairs of hexadecimal digits separated by colons or dashes  d) To route data across networks to the right building
**Answer: c) As six pairs of hexadecimal digits separated by colons or dashes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Can two network cards share one MAC address?**
a) A device's place on a network — its logical address  b) The MAC address  c) To hand the data to the exact card inside that building  d) No — each card carries a different one
**Answer: d) No — each card carries a different one**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does an IP address name?**
a) A device's place on a network — its logical address  b) The IP address  c) To route data across networks to the right building  d) IP is where you are; MAC is who you are
**Answer: a) A device's place on a network — its logical address**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which address changes when a device joins a new network?**
a) The MAC address  b) The IP address  c) To hand the data to the exact card inside that building  d) The physical address fixed into a network card when it was made
**Answer: b) The IP address**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which address stays with the device for life?**
a) To route data across networks to the right building  b) IP is where you are; MAC is who you are  c) The MAC address  d) The device itself, wherever it goes
**Answer: c) The MAC address**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the IP address's job in delivery?**
a) To hand the data to the exact card inside that building  b) The physical address fixed into a network card when it was made  c) As six pairs of hexadecimal digits separated by colons or dashes  d) To route data across networks to the right building
**Answer: d) To route data across networks to the right building**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the MAC address's job in delivery?**
a) To hand the data to the exact card inside that building  b) IP is where you are; MAC is who you are  c) The device itself, wherever it goes  d) No — each card carries a different one
**Answer: a) To hand the data to the exact card inside that building**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Say the difference in one line.**
a) The physical address fixed into a network card when it was made  b) IP is where you are; MAC is who you are  c) As six pairs of hexadecimal digits separated by colons or dashes  d) A device's place on a network — its logical address
**Answer: b) IP is where you are; MAC is who you are**
