# Hands-on — Peer-to-peer networks

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Workgroup share
In a small group, each person holds one “resource” card. Practise peer sharing: anyone may ask anyone. Then name what kept it working: trust.

## Count to ten
Add peers one by one to a drawn workgroup, drawing every share link that matters. Stop at ten and say why the book stops there.
