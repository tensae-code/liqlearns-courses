A Local Area Network (LAN) is a privately owned network within a single office, building or campus, spanning a few kilometres. It is built to share resources — storage, printers, programs and data — and to exchange information. The smallest LAN has two computers; large ones hold thousands.

Now look at the picture. Name each part.
That is lan — a building's network.
