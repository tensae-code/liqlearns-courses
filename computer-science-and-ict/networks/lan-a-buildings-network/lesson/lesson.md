## Do it first

## One building, privately owned

A **Local Area Network (LAN)** is a **privately owned** network **within a single office, building or campus**, spanning **a few kilometres**. It is built to **share resources — storage, printers, programs and data** — and to **exchange information**. The **smallest LAN has two computers**; large ones hold **thousands**.

## Wired for speed and security — WLAN

LANs rely **mostly on wired connections for speed and security**, though **wireless parts are possible**. An **entirely wireless LAN is a WLAN**. The pictured LAN in the book joins the building's devices so the sharing above can happen.
