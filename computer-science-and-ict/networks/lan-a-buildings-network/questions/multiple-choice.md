# LAN — a building's network — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** LAN stands for —
a) Large Area Network  b) Local Area Network  c) Long Access Network  d) Linked Address Network
**Answer: b) Local Area Network** — Local Area Network.

**2.** A LAN lives within —
a) a country  b) a single office, building or campus  c) one person's reach  d) a city only
**Answer: b) a single office, building or campus** — one building or campus.

**3.** A LAN is —
a) publicly owned by all  b) privately owned  c) owned by a satellite  d) ownerless
**Answer: b) privately owned** — privately owned.

**4.** The smallest LAN has —
a) two computers  b) ten computers  c) one computer  d) a hundred computers
**Answer: a) two computers** — two computers.

**5.** An entirely wireless LAN is a —
a) WAN  b) WLAN  c) PAN  d) MAN
**Answer: b) WLAN** — a WLAN.

**6.** LANs rely mostly on wired connections for —
a) colour and sound  b) speed and security  c) games  d) printing only
**Answer: b) speed and security** — speed and security.
