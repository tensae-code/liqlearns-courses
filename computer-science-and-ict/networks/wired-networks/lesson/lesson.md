# Wired networks

## Do it first

Follow one cable with your eyes from a computer to where it ends. Follow another. Every device you reach is cabled in. That is this lesson's network.

## Linked by Ethernet cables

A **wired network (Ethernet network)** is **two or more computers, printers and other devices linked by Ethernet cables**. It is the **most common kind of LAN technology** — when a school lab's machines are cabled to a central point, you are looking at a wired network.
