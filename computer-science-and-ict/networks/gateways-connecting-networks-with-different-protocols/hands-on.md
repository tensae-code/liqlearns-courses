# Hands-on — Gateways — Connecting Networks With Different Protocols

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Name the device by its work** — For five joining situations — two rooms one network, two buildings one protocol, a LAN to a differently-built provider, a signal fading, two floors to share files — name the device each needs and why.

## Task two
**Translate like a gateway** — Write a message in one simple code (words as numbers). Act as gateway: receive it, translate it into a second code (numbers as letters), and deliver the meaning intact.
