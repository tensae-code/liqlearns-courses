# Gateways — Connecting Networks With Different Protocols — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a gateway connect?**
**Answer: Networks using different protocols**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a gateway do to a message?**
**Answer: Translates it — form, addressing and rules converted, meaning preserved**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a hub do, against a gateway?**
**Answer: A hub only repeats**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a router join?**
**Answer: Networks of one protocol family, choosing roads between them**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the gateway called in the lesson's phrase?**
**Answer: The protocol converter**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name two places gateways stand.**
**Answer: Between a LAN and a provider's network; between old and new system generations**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What three devices hide in a home 'router' box?**
**Answer: Switch, router and often gateway**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do you name a device truly?**
**Answer: By its work, not its shop name**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must survive translation unchanged?**
**Answer: The meaning**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Is a gateway the shallowest or deepest joining device?**
**Answer: The deepest — it changes the language itself**
