# What a computer network is — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two or more computers ______ together form a network.
**Answer: linked**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Internet is the ______ global network.
**Answer: largest**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethio-telecom is an example of an ______ (Internet Service Provider).
