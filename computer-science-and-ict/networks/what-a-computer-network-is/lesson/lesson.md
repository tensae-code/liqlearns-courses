# What a computer network is

## Do it first

Share a photo from a phone to a laptop — or imagine doing it by Bluetooth. No cable joins them, yet the file arrives. You have just used this lesson's idea. Now learn its name.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a network is — sharing and communicating

A **network** is **two or more computers linked together** so they can **share resources** — printers, files and disks — or **communicate electronically**. Sharing a file by **Bluetooth** from a phone is an everyday network. The **Internet** is the **largest global network** of all.

## Sub lesson 2 — The home network and how we live now

Picture a home: a **laptop, a tablet, a phone and a television** joined through **one central device**. That central device connects onward to a **global network** through an **ISP** — an Internet Service Provider — such as **Ethio-telecom**. Watch the GIF: the file leaves the phone, passes through the central device, and arrives at the laptop. That journey is a network at work.

Four shifts to name: **communication** moves from face-to-face to remote — television, instant messaging and chatting; **learning** moves to online and e-learning; **play and entertainment** move online — games and chatting; and **work** becomes remote, including working from home.
