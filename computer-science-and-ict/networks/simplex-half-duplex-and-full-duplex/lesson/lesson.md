# Simplex, half-duplex and full-duplex

## Do it first

Picture three roads: a one-way street, a single-lane road where traffic takes turns, and a two-way street. Data travels on all three kinds of road. Match each device you know to its road.

## Sub lesson 1 — Simplex — the one-way street

**Simplex** is a **one-way street**: one device **only transmits**, the other **only receives** — a **keyboard sending, a monitor displaying** — using the **whole channel one way**.

[Picture 14535 — the three road pictures]

## Sub lesson 2 — Half-duplex — turns on a one-lane road

**Half-duplex** is a **one-lane road used in both directions in turns**: each station **can send and receive but never at the same time**, the **whole channel serving whoever is transmitting**.

[GIF 14536 — arrows flowing one way, alternating, then both ways at once]

## Sub lesson 3 — Full-duplex — the two-way street

**Full-duplex** is a **two-way street**: both stations **transmit and receive simultaneously**, the channel either **split into two physical paths** or **divided between the directions** — as in an **ordinary telephone conversation**.
