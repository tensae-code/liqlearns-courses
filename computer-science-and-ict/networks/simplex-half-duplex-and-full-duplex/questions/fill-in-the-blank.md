# Simplex, half-duplex and full-duplex — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ______ mode is the one-way street.
**Answer: Simplex**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ______-duplex takes turns on a one-lane road.
**Answer: Half**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** ______-duplex is the two-way street.
**Answer: Full**
