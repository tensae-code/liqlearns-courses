# Simplex, half-duplex and full-duplex — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In simplex, the whole channel is used one way.
**Answer: True** — the whole channel, one direction.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In half-duplex, the whole channel serves whoever is transmitting.
**Answer: True** — turns on the one-lane road.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In full-duplex, stations must wait for silence before sending.
**Answer: False** — they transmit and receive simultaneously.
