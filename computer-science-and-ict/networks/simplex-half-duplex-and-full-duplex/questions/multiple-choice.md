# Simplex, half-duplex and full-duplex — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In simplex mode —
a) both devices transmit at once  b) one device only transmits, the other only receives  c) devices take turns  d) the channel is split
**Answer: b) one device only transmits, the other only receives** — one transmits, one receives.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's simplex examples are —
a) two telephones  b) a keyboard sending and a monitor displaying  c) two radios taking turns  d) a satellite pair
**Answer: b) a keyboard sending and a monitor displaying** — keyboard and monitor.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Half-duplex stations —
a) can never receive  b) can send and receive, but never at the same time  c) always send at once  d) use two channels
**Answer: b) can send and receive, but never at the same time** — both ways, in turns.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** An ordinary telephone conversation is —
a) simplex  b) half-duplex  c) full-duplex  d) broadcast
**Answer: c) full-duplex** — both talk and hear at once.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Full-duplex divides its channel —
a) by time of day only  b) into two physical paths, or between the directions  c) by cable colour  d) it never divides it
**Answer: b) into two physical paths, or between the directions** — split into two paths or divided between directions.
