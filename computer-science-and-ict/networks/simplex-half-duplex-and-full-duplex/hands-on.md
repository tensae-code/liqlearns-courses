# Hands-on — Simplex, half-duplex and full-duplex

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.


## Road walk
Chalk three roads on the ground. Students carry a ball (the message): one-way only on road 1, turns on road 2, both ways at once on road 3. The class names the mode at each road.

## Mode census
List six device pairs (keyboard–computer, walkie-talkies, telephones, monitor–computer…). Assign each its mode and defend it.
