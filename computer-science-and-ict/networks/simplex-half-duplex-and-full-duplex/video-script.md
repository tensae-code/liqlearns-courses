# Video script — Simplex, half-duplex and full-duplex

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three ways for data to flow.

Simplex: one way only. A keyboard sends. A screen shows.
Half-duplex: both ways — in turns. Never at once.
Full-duplex: both ways at the same time.
Like an ordinary phone call.

One-way street. One-lane road. Two-way street.
