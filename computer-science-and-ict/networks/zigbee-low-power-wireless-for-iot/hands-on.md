# Hands-on — Zigbee — Low-Power Wireless for IoT

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Mesh drawing** — Draw a Zigbee mesh for a house: coordinator, two routers, four end devices — and trace one message's hops.

## Task two
**Battery argument** — A door sensor sends 20 bytes a day. Explain in three sentences why Zigbee fits it and Wi-Fi does not.
