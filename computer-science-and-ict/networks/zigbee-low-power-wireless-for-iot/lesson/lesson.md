# Zigbee — Low-Power Wireless for IoT

## Do it first

A sensor that must live five years on a coin battery cannot shout. Zigbee is the wireless standard designed to whisper — this lesson is how it works and where it wins.

## Sub lesson 1 — What Zigbee is — slow, small, long-lived

**Zigbee** is a wireless standard for **low-power, low-data-rate** links over short distances — the traffic of sensors and controls, which is small and patient. Its devices sip power, sleeping most of their lives and waking to send a few bytes, so batteries last years. Zigbee networks are **mesh** networks: messages hop from device to device, so the network reaches farther than any one radio and heals around a failed hop.

## Sub lesson 2 — Coordinator and devices — the network's roles

A Zigbee network is started and managed by one **coordinator**, which chooses the channel and lets devices join. **Routers** relay messages and can parent sleeping children; **end devices** only sense and send, sleeping the rest of the time — the cheapest, longest-lived role. Jordan places Zigbee inside the IoT unit because it is the smart home's and the sensor field's native tongue: lamps, locks, thermostats and meters speak it.
