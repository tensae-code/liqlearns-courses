**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a mesh network?** → **Messages hop device to device, reaching far and healing around failures**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do routers do?** → **Relay messages and parent sleeping devices**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do end devices do?** → **Only sense and send, sleeping the rest of the time**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which role is cheapest and longest-lived?** → **The end device**
