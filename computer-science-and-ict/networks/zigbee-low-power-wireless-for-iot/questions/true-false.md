# Zigbee — Low-Power Wireless for IoT — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Low-power, low-data-rate links — sensors and controls**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Low-power, low-data-rate links — sensors and controls** — as the answer to: How do Zigbee devices save power?
**Answer: False — They sleep most of their lives and wake to send a few bytes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Messages hop device to device, reaching far and healing around failures**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Messages hop device to device, reaching far and healing around failures** — as the answer to: How long can Zigbee batteries last?
**Answer: False — Years**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Short distances**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Short distances** — as the answer to: What does the coordinator do?
**Answer: False — Starts and manages the network, chooses the channel, lets devices join**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Relay messages and parent sleeping devices**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Relay messages and parent sleeping devices** — as the answer to: What do end devices do?
**Answer: False — Only sense and send, sleeping the rest of the time**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The end device**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The end device** — as the answer to: Name four things that speak Zigbee.
**Answer: False — Lamps, locks, thermostats and meters**
