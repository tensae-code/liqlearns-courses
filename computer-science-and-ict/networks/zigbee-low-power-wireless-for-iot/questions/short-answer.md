# Zigbee — Low-Power Wireless for IoT — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What traffic is Zigbee designed for?**
**Answer: Low-power, low-data-rate links — sensors and controls**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How do Zigbee devices save power?**
**Answer: They sleep most of their lives and wake to send a few bytes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a mesh network?**
**Answer: Messages hop device to device, reaching far and healing around failures**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How long can Zigbee batteries last?**
**Answer: Years**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What kind of distances does Zigbee cover?**
**Answer: Short distances**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the coordinator do?**
**Answer: Starts and manages the network, chooses the channel, lets devices join**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do routers do?**
**Answer: Relay messages and parent sleeping devices**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do end devices do?**
**Answer: Only sense and send, sleeping the rest of the time**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which role is cheapest and longest-lived?**
**Answer: The end device**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name four things that speak Zigbee.**
**Answer: Lamps, locks, thermostats and meters**
