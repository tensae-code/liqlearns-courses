# Microwaves — aimed beams, dish and horn antennas — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One-to-one communication is called ______.
**Answer: unicast**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A dish gathers waves into one ______ point.
**Answer: focus**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The ______ is the scoop-shaped microwave antenna.
**Answer: horn**


**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The guide keys microwaves' range as 1–300 ______.
**Answer: GHz**
