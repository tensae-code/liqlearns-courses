# Microwaves — aimed beams, dish and horn antennas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Microwaves travel —
a) in all directions  b) line-of-sight as narrow unidirectional beams  c) through the ground  d) inside cables
**Answer: b) line-of-sight as narrow unidirectional beams** — narrow, unidirectional, line-of-sight.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An aligned microwave pair —
a) always interferes with others  b) does not interfere with another pair  c) needs no antennas  d) works around corners
**Answer: b) does not interfere with another pair** — alignment avoids interference.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The parabolic dish —
a) throws waves in parallel beams  b) gathers waves from a wide area into one focus point  c) stores the signal  d) converts light to current
**Answer: b) gathers waves from a wide area into one focus point** — it focuses gathered waves.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The horn —
a) is a scoop that throws parallel beams and collects incoming waves down its stem  b) is a cable connector  c) closes a cable run  d) is used for cable TV
**Answer: a) is a scoop that throws parallel beams and collects incoming waves down its stem** — the scoop-shaped horn.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The guide keys microwaves' range as —
a) 3 kHz–1 GHz  b) 1–300 GHz  c) only 2.4 GHz  d) below 3 kHz
**Answer: b) 1–300 GHz** — 1–300 GHz.
