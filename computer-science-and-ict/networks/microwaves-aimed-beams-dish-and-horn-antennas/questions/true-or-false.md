# Microwaves — aimed beams, dish and horn antennas — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Microwave links sit low, near the ground.
**Answer: False** — microwave links sit high.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A dish works like a funnel for waves.
**Answer: True** — gathering a wide area into one focus.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The horn can only send, never collect.
**Answer: False** — it throws outgoing waves and collects incoming ones.
