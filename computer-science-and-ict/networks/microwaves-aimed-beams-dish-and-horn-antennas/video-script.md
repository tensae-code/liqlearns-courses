# Video script — Microwaves — aimed beams, dish and horn antennas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Microwaves are aimed.

Narrow beams. Line-of-sight. Antenna to antenna — aligned.
One pair does not trouble another.

The dish gathers waves to one focus, like a funnel.
The horn throws a narrow beam — and gathers what returns.

One to one. Unicast.
