# Hands-on — Microwaves — aimed beams, dish and horn antennas

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.


## Funnel focus
Hold a curved bowl toward a torch beam and find where the light gathers brightest — the focus. Say the dish's job in the same words.

## Aligned or lost
Two torches across a dark room: a message (three flashes) passes only when both aim truly. Turn one torch aside — the link dies. That is alignment.
