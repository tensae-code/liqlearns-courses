# Comparing Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and Zigbee — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the five grounds of comparison.**
**Answer: Reliability, power, cost, range and speed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which standard is fastest?**
**Answer: Wi-Fi**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which sips the least power?**
**Answer: Zigbee**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which is a room's reach?**
**Answer: Bluetooth**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What makes Zigbee stubborn?**
**Answer: Its mesh**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which standard for streaming video?**
**Answer: Wi-Fi**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which for a headset or watch?**
**Answer: Bluetooth**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which for hundreds of battery sensors?**
**Answer: Zigbee**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What five numbers does a designer read?**
**Answer: Data amount, distance, battery life, device count, budget**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the comparison's main lesson?**
**Answer: There is no winner, only fits**
