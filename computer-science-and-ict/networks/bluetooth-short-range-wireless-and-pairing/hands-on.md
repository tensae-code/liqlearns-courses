# Hands-on — Bluetooth — short-range wireless and pairing

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.


## Share by Bluetooth (the book's practical, in its order)
1. Switch Bluetooth on, on two nearby phones. 2. Send a pairing request from one phone. 3. Accept it on the other — the link forms. 4. Share a message between the phones over Bluetooth.

## Cable census
List the cables in one room that Bluetooth could replace (headset, mouse, phone-to-PC). For each, say what pairs with what.
