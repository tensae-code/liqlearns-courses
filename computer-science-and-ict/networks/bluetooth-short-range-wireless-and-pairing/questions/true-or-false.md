# Bluetooth — short-range wireless and pairing — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mobile communication needs a cable for short distances.
**Answer: False** — no physical connection at all.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The 2.4 GHz ISM band is licensed to Bluetooth alone.
**Answer: False** — it is unlicensed and shared with microwaves and Wi-Fi.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Low power use is one of Bluetooth's keyed advantages.
**Answer: True** — built in, no wires, low power.
