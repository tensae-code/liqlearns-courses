# Bluetooth — short-range wireless and pairing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mobile communication means communicating —
a) only in one building  b) between different locations with no physical connection  c) by cable only  d) through satellites only
**Answer: b) between different locations with no physical connection** — no physical connection at all.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Bluetooth is meant to replace —
a) the internet  b) the cables between electronic devices  c) access points  d) cell towers
**Answer: b) the cables between electronic devices** — the cables between devices.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Bluetooth works on —
a) the unlicensed 2.4 GHz ISM band  b) 1–300 GHz  c) 3 kHz  d) fibre
**Answer: a) the unlicensed 2.4 GHz ISM band** — 2.4 GHz ISM, shared with microwaves and Wi-Fi.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Devices join Bluetooth by —
a) crimping  b) pairing  c) terminating  d) multiplexing
**Answer: b) pairing** — pairing — then they communicate freely.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The guide's line is: Wi-Fi connects devices to —
a) each other  b) the internet  c) a headset  d) a printer only
**Answer: b) the internet** — Wi-Fi to the internet; Bluetooth device to device.
