# Bluetooth — short-range wireless and pairing — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.


**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Bluetooth works on the 2.4 ______ ISM band.
**Answer: GHz**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Devices join by ______ and then communicate freely.
**Answer: pairing**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Bluetooth connects devices to ______ other; Wi-Fi connects them to the internet.
**Answer: each**


**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Mobile communication needs no physical ______ at all.
**Answer: connection**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** After ______, the devices communicate freely.
**Answer: pairing**
