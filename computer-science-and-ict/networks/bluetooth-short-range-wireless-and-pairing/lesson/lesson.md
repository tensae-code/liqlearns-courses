# Bluetooth — short-range wireless and pairing

## Do it first

Hold your phone near a friend's phone. No cable joins them — yet a photo can cross. This lesson is the cable-replacer that does it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Mobile communication — no physical connection

**Mobile communication** means communicating **between different locations with no physical connection at all**. Bluetooth is its short-range example.

[Picture 14524 — devices linked by short Bluetooth links]

## Sub lesson 2 — The cable replacer on 2.4 GHz

**Bluetooth** is a wireless technology meant to **replace the cables between electronic devices** — the pictured use is a **phone conversation through a headset**. It works on the **unlicensed 2.4 GHz ISM band**, **shared with microwaves and Wi-Fi**.

[GIF 14525 — two phones pairing: request, acceptance, link]

## Sub lesson 3 — Pairing, then free communication

Devices join by **pairing**, and then **communicate freely**. The class practical **shares messages between nearby phones** over Bluetooth.

## Sub lesson 4 — Bluetooth against Wi-Fi (teacher guide — evidence)

The guide keys Bluetooth as **short-range radio linking devices to each other** — headphones, speakers, printers, laptops, phones — with three advantages: **it is built into most devices, it needs no wires, its low power use is efficient**. And it draws the line: **Wi-Fi connects devices to the internet; Bluetooth connects devices to each other**.
