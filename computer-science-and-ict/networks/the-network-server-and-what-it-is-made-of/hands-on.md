# Hands-on — The Network Server and What It Is Made Of

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the parts
From the book's list, point to or draw each part of a server — processor, motherboard, adaptor, power supply, operating system, UPS — and say its job in one breath.

## Why a UPS?
Explain to a partner why the server, of all computers, is the one that must never lose power suddenly.
