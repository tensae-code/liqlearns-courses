# Hands-on — Parallel and Serial Transmission

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Race the roads** — Send an 8-letter word two ways as a class: eight runners at once, then one runner eight times. Time both over 5 metres and over 50, and explain the results.

## Task two
**Find both at home** — List five connections in your home. Classify each parallel or serial, with the distance clue that decided you.
