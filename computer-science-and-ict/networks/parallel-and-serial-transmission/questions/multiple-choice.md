# Parallel and Serial Transmission — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does parallel transmission send at once?**
a) A whole group of bits, each on its own wire  b) Eight  c) Bits drifting out of step over distance  d) Short distances — inside the computer and to nearby devices
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many wires carry a byte in parallel?**
a) Eight  b) Bits drifting out of step over distance  c) Short distances — inside the computer and to nearby devices  d) Many wires
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is skew?**
a) Bits drifting out of step over distance  b) Short distances — inside the computer and to nearby devices  c) Many wires  d) One after another along a single channel
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does parallel live?**
a) Short distances — inside the computer and to nearby devices  b) Many wires  c) One after another along a single channel  d) They queue in order
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is parallel's cost problem?**
a) Many wires  b) One after another along a single channel  c) They queue in order  d) Nearly everything that travels far — network and internet links
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does serial send bits?**
a) One after another along a single channel  b) They queue in order  c) Nearly everything that travels far — network and internet links  d) Parallel
**Answer: a**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why can't serial bits fall out of step?**
a) They queue in order  b) Nearly everything that travels far — network and internet links  c) Parallel  d) Serial
**Answer: a**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What travels serially?**
a) Nearly everything that travels far — network and internet links  b) Parallel  c) Serial  d) A whole group of bits, each on its own wire
**Answer: a**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which wins inside the machine?**
a) Parallel  b) Serial  c) A whole group of bits, each on its own wire  d) Eight
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which wins every long journey?**
a) Serial  b) A whole group of bits, each on its own wire  c) Eight  d) Bits drifting out of step over distance
**Answer: a**
