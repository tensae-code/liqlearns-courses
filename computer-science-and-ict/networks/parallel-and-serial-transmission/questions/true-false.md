# Parallel and Serial Transmission — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A whole group of bits, each on its own wire**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A whole group of bits, each on its own wire** — as the answer to: How many wires carry a byte in parallel?
**Answer: False — Eight**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Bits drifting out of step over distance**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Bits drifting out of step over distance** — as the answer to: Where does parallel live?
**Answer: False — Short distances — inside the computer and to nearby devices**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Many wires**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Many wires** — as the answer to: How does serial send bits?
**Answer: False — One after another along a single channel**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: They queue in order**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: They queue in order** — as the answer to: What travels serially?
**Answer: False — Nearly everything that travels far — network and internet links**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Parallel**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Parallel** — as the answer to: Which wins every long journey?
**Answer: False — Serial**
