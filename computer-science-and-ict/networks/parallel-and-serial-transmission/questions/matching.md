# Parallel and Serial Transmission — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does parallel transmission send at once?** → **A whole group of bits, each on its own wire**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many wires carry a byte in parallel?** → **Eight**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is skew?** → **Bits drifting out of step over distance**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does parallel live?** → **Short distances — inside the computer and to nearby devices**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is parallel's cost problem?** → **Many wires**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does serial send bits?** → **One after another along a single channel**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why can't serial bits fall out of step?** → **They queue in order**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What travels serially?** → **Nearly everything that travels far — network and internet links**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which wins inside the machine?** → **Parallel**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which wins every long journey?** → **Serial**
