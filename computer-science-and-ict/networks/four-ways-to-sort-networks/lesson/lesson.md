# Four ways to sort networks

## Do it first

Take one school network. Describe it four different ways: what carries it, how big it is, what shape it makes, and who may get in. You have just used this lesson's four ways.

## Four perspectives

Network types are sorted by **four perspectives**: the **connection medium**, the **size or geographical coverage**, the **topology** (the shape), and **resource security and access**. The rest of this unit follows that scheme, **one perspective at a time**: medium first (wired, wireless), then size (PAN, LAN, MAN, WAN), then topology (bus, mesh, star, ring), then access (peer-to-peer, client-server). This lesson is the map for all of them.
