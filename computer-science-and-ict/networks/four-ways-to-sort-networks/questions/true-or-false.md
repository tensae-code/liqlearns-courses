# Four ways to sort networks — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** Topology means the shape of the network.
**Answer: True** — the shape perspective.

**2.** The unit takes the four perspectives one at a time.
**Answer: True** — that is the scheme the unit follows.

**3.** Size and topology are the same perspective.
**Answer: False** — they are two different perspectives.
