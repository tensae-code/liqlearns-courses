# Hands-on — Designing a Simple Network from a Brief

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Brief a classroom network
Write the brief for networking your classroom: who uses it, what for, how many devices, which media. Draw the plan.

## Configure on paper
For each device in your drawn plan, write the one set-up job it needs, and the test you will run to prove two devices can communicate.
