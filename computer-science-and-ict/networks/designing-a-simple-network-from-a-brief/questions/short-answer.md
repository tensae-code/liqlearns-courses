# Designing a Simple Network from a Brief — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the brief for a network state first?**
**Answer: The problem the network must solve**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is listed before anything is connected?**
**Answer: The devices and media needed**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does it mean to configure a network?**
**Answer: Set up each device so the network works**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is tested when the network is built?**
**Answer: That the devices can communicate**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does managing a network include?**
**Answer: Checking it keeps working and fixing faults**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the first research step?**
**Answer: Find out the users' needs and the place**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a simple network connect?**
**Answer: A small number of devices in one place**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why draw the network plan before building?**
**Answer: So every connection is clear before building**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who manages a simple school network?**
**Answer: A named person responsible for it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What proves the design met the brief?**
**Answer: The working network doing what the brief asked**
