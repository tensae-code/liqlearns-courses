# Public and Private Networks — Who Owns the Network — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What divides public from private networks?**
a) Ownership  b) A provider  c) Anyone who subscribes  d) The telephone network (or carrier internet access)
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who builds a public network?**
a) A provider  b) Anyone who subscribes  c) The telephone network (or carrier internet access)  d) Reach and shared cost
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who may use a public network?**
a) Anyone who subscribes  b) The telephone network (or carrier internet access)  c) Reach and shared cost  d) One organisation, for its own people
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name a public network.**
a) The telephone network (or carrier internet access)  b) Reach and shared cost  c) One organisation, for its own people  d) A bank's branch network (or a university CAN)
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do public networks win by?**
a) Reach and shared cost  b) One organisation, for its own people  c) A bank's branch network (or a university CAN)  d) Control of security, speed and rules
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Who owns a private network?**
a) One organisation, for its own people  b) A bank's branch network (or a university CAN)  c) Control of security, speed and rules  d) The whole cost
**Answer: a**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name a private network example.**
a) A bank's branch network (or a university CAN)  b) Control of security, speed and rules  c) The whole cost  d) Travel over them fenced by encryption — the VPN idea
**Answer: a**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the private owner gain?**
a) Control of security, speed and rules  b) The whole cost  c) Travel over them fenced by encryption — the VPN idea  d) Ownership
**Answer: a**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the private owner pay?**
a) The whole cost  b) Travel over them fenced by encryption — the VPN idea  c) Ownership  d) A provider
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How do private networks use public roads?**
a) Travel over them fenced by encryption — the VPN idea  b) Ownership  c) A provider  d) Anyone who subscribes
**Answer: a**
