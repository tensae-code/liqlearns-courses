# Public and Private Networks — Who Owns the Network — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Ownership**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Ownership** — as the answer to: Who builds a public network?
**Answer: False — A provider**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Anyone who subscribes**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Anyone who subscribes** — as the answer to: Name a public network.
**Answer: False — The telephone network (or carrier internet access)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Reach and shared cost**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Reach and shared cost** — as the answer to: Who owns a private network?
**Answer: False — One organisation, for its own people**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A bank's branch network (or a university CAN)**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A bank's branch network (or a university CAN)** — as the answer to: What does the private owner gain?
**Answer: False — Control of security, speed and rules**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The whole cost**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The whole cost** — as the answer to: How do private networks use public roads?
**Answer: False — Travel over them fenced by encryption — the VPN idea**
