# Public and Private Networks — Who Owns the Network — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What divides public from private networks?**
**Answer: Ownership**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who builds a public network?**
**Answer: A provider**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Who may use a public network?**
**Answer: Anyone who subscribes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name a public network.**
**Answer: The telephone network (or carrier internet access)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do public networks win by?**
**Answer: Reach and shared cost**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Who owns a private network?**
**Answer: One organisation, for its own people**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name a private network example.**
**Answer: A bank's branch network (or a university CAN)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the private owner gain?**
**Answer: Control of security, speed and rules**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the private owner pay?**
**Answer: The whole cost**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How do private networks use public roads?**
**Answer: Travel over them fenced by encryption — the VPN idea**
