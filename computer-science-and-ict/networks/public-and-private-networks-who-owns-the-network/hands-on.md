# Hands-on — Public and Private Networks — Who Owns the Network

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Sort by ownership** — List eight networks you use or know. Mark each public or private, naming its owner — and flag any that mix the two.

## Task two
**The fence on public ground** — Explain to a partner, with a drawing, how a company's two offices can be one private network while their traffic crosses the public internet.
