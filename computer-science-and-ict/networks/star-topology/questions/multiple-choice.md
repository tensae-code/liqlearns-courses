# Star topology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In a star, each device links —
a) to every other device  b) to one central controller  c) to two neighbours  d) to the bus only
**Answer: b) to one central controller** — to the central controller.

**2.** The central controller is usually a —
a) printer  b) switch  c) repeater  d) NIC
**Answer: b) switch** — a switch.

**3.** Devices in a star talk to each other —
a) directly  b) never directly — through the switch  c) by Bluetooth only  d) never at all
**Answer: b) never directly — through the switch** — through the switch.

**4.** If the switch goes down —
a) one device stops  b) the entire network is defunct  c) the star becomes a ring  d) nothing changes
**Answer: b) the entire network is defunct** — the entire network is defunct.

**5.** Compared with mesh, star is —
a) dearer  b) cheaper  c) more complex  d) impossible to maintain
**Answer: b) cheaper** — cheaper than mesh.
