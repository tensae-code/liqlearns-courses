# Star topology

## Do it first

Draw a wheel: one hub in the middle, spokes out to every rim point. Now put a switch in the middle and a device at each spoke's end. You have drawn this lesson.

## Every device, its own link to the centre

In a **star topology**, **each device has its own dedicated point-to-point link to one central controller, usually a switch**. Devices **never talk directly**: a node **sends to the switch, which forwards to the right node**. Watch the GIF: the message travels in to the switch, then out to its destination.

## Easy and robust — one weak point

Star is **cheaper than mesh, easy to install, maintain and reconfigure, and robust** — but hold the trade: **if the switch goes down, the entire network is defunct**. The centre is both the star's convenience and its single point of failure.
