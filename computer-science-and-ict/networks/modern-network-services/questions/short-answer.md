# Modern Network Services — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a network service?**
**Answer: A job the network does for its users, through its servers and programs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Road and shop: which is the service?**
**Answer: The shop at the road's end — the connection is only the road**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the modern set.**
**Answer: Email and messaging, the Web, file transfer and sharing, remote access, voice and video**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Every service pairs what two roles?**
**Answer: The client that asks and the server that answers**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 11 Eisagogi Epistimis H/Y, Ch.3.3**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is becoming a service itself?**
**Answer: Computing — programs, storage and platforms rented over the network**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) As services thicken, the user's device grows what?**
**Answer: Thinner**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) State the new judging question.**
**Answer: Which services stand on this network, and how well do they serve?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Not which old question?**
**Answer: Not merely 'is it connected?'**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) The cloud half of this turn is taught where?**
**Answer: In the New Technologies lessons — cloud computing and its service models**
