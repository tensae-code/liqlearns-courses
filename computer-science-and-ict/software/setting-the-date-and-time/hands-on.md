# Hands-on — Setting the Date and Time

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read the clock
Write down the date and time your computer shows. Check them against a phone or a wall clock. Say whether the computer is right or wrong, and by how much.

## Set it right
Open the date-and-time settings, set the correct day, month, year and hour, confirm with OK, and check the taskbar clock and the stamp on a file you save afterwards.
