# Setting the Date and Time — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does the computer's clock sit?" is: At the end of the taskbar**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the computer keep beside the time?" is: By clicking the clock, or through the Control Panel**
**Answer: False** — The date — day, month and year

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How do you open the date-and-time settings?" is: By clicking the clock, or through the Control Panel**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Is the taskbar clock where the time is set?" is: Both the date and the time**
**Answer: False** — No — the clock is only the display; the settings box behind it is where it is set

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the settings box show?" is: Both the date and the time**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How do you set the date?" is: Change the hour, minutes and seconds in the time box**
**Answer: False** — Pick the right month and year, then click the right day in the calendar

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How do you set the time?" is: Change the hour, minutes and seconds in the time box**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What must you press to make the change real?" is: Nothing changes — the old date and time stay**
**Answer: False** — Apply or OK

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens if you close the box without confirming?" is: Nothing changes — the old date and time stay**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why does the right date and time matter?" is: At the end of the taskbar**
**Answer: False** — Files you save carry the date and time stamp, and programs work by it
