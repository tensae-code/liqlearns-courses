# Setting the Date and Time — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the computer's clock sit?**
**Answer: At the end of the taskbar**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the computer keep beside the time?**
**Answer: The date — day, month and year**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How do you open the date-and-time settings?**
**Answer: By clicking the clock, or through the Control Panel**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is the taskbar clock where the time is set?**
**Answer: No — the clock is only the display; the settings box behind it is where it is set**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the settings box show?**
**Answer: Both the date and the time**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How do you set the date?**
**Answer: Pick the right month and year, then click the right day in the calendar**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How do you set the time?**
**Answer: Change the hour, minutes and seconds in the time box**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must you press to make the change real?**
**Answer: Apply or OK**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What happens if you close the box without confirming?**
**Answer: Nothing changes — the old date and time stay**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the right date and time matter?**
**Answer: Files you save carry the date and time stamp, and programs work by it**
