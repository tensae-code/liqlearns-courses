# Setting the Date and Time — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the computer's clock sit?**
a) At the end of the taskbar  b) The date — day, month and year  c) By clicking the clock, or through the Control Panel  d) No — the clock is only the display; the settings box behind it is where it is set
**Answer: a) At the end of the taskbar**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the computer keep beside the time?**
a) By clicking the clock, or through the Control Panel  b) The date — day, month and year  c) No — the clock is only the display; the settings box behind it is where it is set  d) Both the date and the time
**Answer: b) The date — day, month and year**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How do you open the date-and-time settings?**
a) No — the clock is only the display; the settings box behind it is where it is set  b) Both the date and the time  c) By clicking the clock, or through the Control Panel  d) Pick the right month and year, then click the right day in the calendar
**Answer: c) By clicking the clock, or through the Control Panel**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is the taskbar clock where the time is set?**
a) Both the date and the time  b) Pick the right month and year, then click the right day in the calendar  c) Change the hour, minutes and seconds in the time box  d) No — the clock is only the display; the settings box behind it is where it is set
**Answer: d) No — the clock is only the display; the settings box behind it is where it is set**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the settings box show?**
a) Both the date and the time  b) Pick the right month and year, then click the right day in the calendar  c) Change the hour, minutes and seconds in the time box  d) Apply or OK
**Answer: a) Both the date and the time**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How do you set the date?**
a) Change the hour, minutes and seconds in the time box  b) Pick the right month and year, then click the right day in the calendar  c) Apply or OK  d) Nothing changes — the old date and time stay
**Answer: b) Pick the right month and year, then click the right day in the calendar**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How do you set the time?**
a) Apply or OK  b) Nothing changes — the old date and time stay  c) Change the hour, minutes and seconds in the time box  d) Files you save carry the date and time stamp, and programs work by it
**Answer: c) Change the hour, minutes and seconds in the time box**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must you press to make the change real?**
a) Nothing changes — the old date and time stay  b) Files you save carry the date and time stamp, and programs work by it  c) At the end of the taskbar  d) Apply or OK
**Answer: d) Apply or OK**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What happens if you close the box without confirming?**
a) Nothing changes — the old date and time stay  b) Files you save carry the date and time stamp, and programs work by it  c) At the end of the taskbar  d) The date — day, month and year
**Answer: a) Nothing changes — the old date and time stay**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the right date and time matter?**
a) At the end of the taskbar  b) Files you save carry the date and time stamp, and programs work by it  c) The date — day, month and year  d) By clicking the clock, or through the Control Panel
**Answer: b) Files you save carry the date and time stamp, and programs work by it**
