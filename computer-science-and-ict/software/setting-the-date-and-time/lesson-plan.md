# Lesson plan — Setting the Date and Time

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Software · **Lesson:** Setting the Date and Time
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Yemen (2026-11-14) — in the title Software on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Yemen's *Computer* Grade 10, because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The computer keeps a clock and a calendar, shown at the end of the taskbar; setting the date and time means opening those settings, correcting the day, month, year and the hour, and confirming so every file and program uses the right time.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Finding the clock and its settings** — The computer's **clock** sits at the end of the **taskbar**, and beside the time it keeps the **date** — day, month and year. Clicking the clock, or opening the date-and-time settings through the Control Panel, opens a box showing both. You do not set the time by moving the taskbar clock itself: the clock is only the display. The settings box behind it is where the real date and time live and where they are changed.
2. **Setting them right** — To set the date, pick the right **month and year**, then click the right **day** in the calendar. To set the time, change the **hour, minutes and seconds** in the time box. Then confirm with **Apply or OK** — until you confirm, nothing has changed. Afterwards, look at the taskbar clock again: it now shows the corrected time, and files you save from now on carry the right date and time stamp.
