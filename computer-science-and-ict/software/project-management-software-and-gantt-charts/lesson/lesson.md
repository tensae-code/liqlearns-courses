# Project Management Software and Gantt Charts

## Do it first

project management software — planning a project's tasks, schedule, people and budget on a Gantt chart, and following its progress, in the open-source GanttProject (Vietnam Grade 12 specialist, Applied)

## Sub lesson 1 — The project and its chart — tasks on a timeline

**Project management** keeps a project's four questions answered: **what** must be done, **in what order and how long**, **by whom**, and **at what cost**. Its picture is the **Gantt chart**: tasks listed down the side, time across the top, each task a **bar** from its start to its finish — dependencies showing as order (this bar cannot start until that one ends). Vietnam's specialist volume teaches it in **GanttProject**, open-source software: set up the project, enter its tasks, durations and order, and the chart draws the plan.

## Sub lesson 2 — People, budget and progress — managing, not just planning

The volume's later lessons work the plan: **allocating people and budget** — each task gets its workers and its cost, and an overloaded person or an overrun budget shows on the plan before it shows in life; **managing progress** — marking what is actually done, so the chart compares **plan against reality** and the finish date re-forecasts itself; and the productivity close: the software's reports let a small team see the whole project in one look, which is what the tool is for.
