# Hands-on — Project Management Software and Gantt Charts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Chart a real project** — Plan a project you know — a class event — in GanttProject or on paper as a Gantt chart: at least six tasks, durations, one dependency.

## Task two
**Plan against reality** — Halfway through a real task list, mark what is actually done. Recompute the finish date and say what the chart told you that your memory had not.
