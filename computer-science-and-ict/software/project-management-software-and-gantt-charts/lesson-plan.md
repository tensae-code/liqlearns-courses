## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The project and its chart — tasks on a timeline** — **Project management** keeps a project's four questions answered: **what** must be done, **in what order and how long**, **by whom**, and **at what cost**. Its picture is the **Gantt chart**: tasks listed down the side, time across the top, each task a **bar** from its start to its finish — dependencies showing as order (this bar cannot start until that one ends). Vietnam's specialist volume teaches it in **GanttProject**, open-source software: set up the project, enter its tasks, durations and order, and the chart draws the plan.
2. **People, budget and progress — managing, not just planning** — The volume's later lessons work the plan: **allocating people and budget** — each task gets its workers and its cost, and an overloaded person or an overrun budget shows on the plan before it shows in life; **managing progress** — marking what is actually done, so the chart compares **plan against reality** and the finish date re-forecasts itself; and the productivity close: the software's reports let a small team see the whole project in one look, which is what the tool is for.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
