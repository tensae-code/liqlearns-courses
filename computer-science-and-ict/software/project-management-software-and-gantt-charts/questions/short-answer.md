# Project Management Software and Gantt Charts — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are project management's four questions?**
**Answer: What must be done, in what order and how long, by whom, and at what cost**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a Gantt chart?**
**Answer: Tasks down the side, time across the top, each task a bar from start to finish**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a dependency on the chart?**
**Answer: An order rule — this task cannot start until that one ends**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which software does the book teach?**
**Answer: GanttProject — open-source project management software**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is entered to draw the plan?**
**Answer: The tasks, their durations and their order**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is allocated to each task?**
**Answer: Its people and its budget (cost)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the plan show early?**
**Answer: An overloaded person or an overrun budget — before they show in life**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is marked to manage progress?**
**Answer: What is actually done — so plan can be compared against reality**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What re-forecasts itself from progress?**
**Answer: The finish date**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the software give a small team?**
**Answer: The whole project seen in one look — its reports**
