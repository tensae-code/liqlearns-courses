# Hands-on — Working with the Start Menu

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find it from Start
From the Start menu alone, open three different programs one at a time and close each. Write the path you took for each — Start, All Programs, folder, name — the way you would tell a friend over the phone.

## Two ways to leave
Explain to a partner, without touching a computer, the difference between Log Off and Turn Off Computer, and when each is the right way to leave. Then let your partner act it out and correct any mistake.
