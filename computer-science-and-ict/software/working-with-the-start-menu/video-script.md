# Video script — Working with the Start Menu

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Click the **Start button** at the left end of the taskbar (or press the Windows key) and the **Start menu** opens.
Its left side lists programs: **All Programs** opens the full list of everything installed — Word, Paint, games and the rest — some of them grouped in folders that open as you point.
Above the list, Windows keeps the programs you use most, so your common work is one click away.
Clicking a program's name opens it; the menu closes itself behind it.
The menu's right side reaches the rest of your computer: **My Documents** and **My Computer** for your files and drives, **Control Panel** for settings, **Help and Support**, and **Search** for finding things.
At the bottom sit the two leaving buttons: **Log Off**, which ends your session but leaves the computer on for the next person, and **Turn Off Computer**, which shuts Windows down safely.
Always leave by Turn Off Computer — never by pulling the power — so Windows can close every program and save its own work first.
