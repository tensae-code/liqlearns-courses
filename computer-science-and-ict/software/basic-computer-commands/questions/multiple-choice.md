# Basic Computer Commands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What must you do before cut or copy can work?**
a) Select the thing first — click it or drag across it so the program knows what you mean  b) Copy leaves the original and puts a copy on the clipboard; cut removes the original and puts it on the clipboard  c) It puts the clipboard's content in at the place you choose  d) The computer's temporary holding place for the last thing cut or copied
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the difference between cut and copy?**
a) Copy leaves the original and puts a copy on the clipboard; cut removes the original and puts it on the clipboard  b) It puts the clipboard's content in at the place you choose  c) The computer's temporary holding place for the last thing cut or copied  d) It moves — the thing leaves its old place and appears in the new one
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does paste do?**
a) It puts the clipboard's content in at the place you choose  b) The computer's temporary holding place for the last thing cut or copied  c) It moves — the thing leaves its old place and appears in the new one  d) It takes back the last action — and pressed again, the one before that
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the clipboard?**
a) The computer's temporary holding place for the last thing cut or copied  b) It moves — the thing leaves its old place and appears in the new one  c) It takes back the last action — and pressed again, the one before that  d) It does again an action that was undone
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Does cut-paste move or duplicate?**
a) It moves — the thing leaves its old place and appears in the new one  b) It takes back the last action — and pressed again, the one before that  c) It does again an action that was undone  d) They make trying safe — you can try, look, and step back instead of starting over
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does undo do?**
a) It takes back the last action — and pressed again, the one before that  b) It does again an action that was undone  c) They make trying safe — you can try, look, and step back instead of starting over  d) Ctrl+Z
**Answer: a**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does redo do?**
a) It does again an action that was undone  b) They make trying safe — you can try, look, and step back instead of starting over  c) Ctrl+Z  d) Ctrl+Y (in many programs Ctrl+Shift+Z)
**Answer: a**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why do undo and redo matter for a learner?**
a) They make trying safe — you can try, look, and step back instead of starting over  b) Ctrl+Z  c) Ctrl+Y (in many programs Ctrl+Shift+Z)  d) Select the thing first — click it or drag across it so the program knows what you mean
**Answer: a**

a) Ctrl+Z  b) Ctrl+Y (in many programs Ctrl+Shift+Z)  c) Select the thing first — click it or drag across it so the program knows what you mean  d) Copy leaves the original and puts a copy on the clipboard; cut removes the original and puts it on the clipboard
