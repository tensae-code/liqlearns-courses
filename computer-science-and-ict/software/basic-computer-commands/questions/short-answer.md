# Basic Computer Commands — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What must you do before cut or copy can work?**
**Answer: Select the thing first — click it or drag across it so the program knows what you mean**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the difference between cut and copy?**
**Answer: Copy leaves the original and puts a copy on the clipboard; cut removes the original and puts it on the clipboard**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does paste do?**
**Answer: It puts the clipboard's content in at the place you choose**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the clipboard?**
**Answer: The computer's temporary holding place for the last thing cut or copied**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Does cut-paste move or duplicate?**
**Answer: It moves — the thing leaves its old place and appears in the new one**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does undo do?**
**Answer: It takes back the last action — and pressed again, the one before that**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does redo do?**
**Answer: It does again an action that was undone**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why do undo and redo matter for a learner?**
**Answer: They make trying safe — you can try, look, and step back instead of starting over**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the usual keyboard shortcut for undo?**
**Answer: Ctrl+Z**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the usual keyboard shortcut for redo?**
**Answer: Ctrl+Y (in many programs Ctrl+Shift+Z)**
