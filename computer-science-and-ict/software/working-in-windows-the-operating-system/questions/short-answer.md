# Working in Windows — the Operating System — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is Windows, in the book's teaching?**
**Answer: An operating system**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What kind of software is an operating system?**
**Answer: System software**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When does the operating system start?**
**Answer: When the computer starts**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the operating system manage?**
**Answer: The machine's parts**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the operating system run?**
**Answer: The application programs**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do you land on when Windows starts?**
**Answer: The desktop**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) In what do programs open?**
**Answer: Windows**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do you give orders in Windows?**
**Answer: Through menus and icons you click**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Between what is the operating system the layer?**
**Answer: The user, the programs and the hardware**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do the lessons after this one take one piece at a time?**
**Answer: The desktop and its shortcut menus**
