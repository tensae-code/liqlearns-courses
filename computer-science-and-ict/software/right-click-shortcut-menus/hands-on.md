# Hands-on — Right-Click Shortcut Menus

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four clicks, four menus
Right-click the desktop, a file, This PC and the taskbar. Write down one order that appears in each menu and one order that appears in only that menu.

## Rescue from the Bin
Delete a practice file, open the Recycle Bin, and restore it with its shortcut menu. Then say what would remove it for good.
