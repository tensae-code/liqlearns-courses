# Right-Click Shortcut Menus — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a right-click open?**
**Answer: A shortcut menu**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a shortcut menu?**
**Answer: A short list of orders that belong to the thing you clicked**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does right-clicking the desktop give?**
**Answer: The desktop menu**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the desktop menu for?**
**Answer: Arranging the desktop itself**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does right-clicking a file or folder give?**
**Answer: A menu of orders that act on that file or folder**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does right-clicking This PC open?**
**Answer: The menu for the computer itself and its drives**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where do deleted files wait?**
**Answer: In the Recycle Bin**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can happen to a file in the Recycle Bin?**
**Answer: It is restored or removed for good**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does right-clicking the taskbar open?**
**Answer: The taskbar menu**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What one habit serves everywhere?**
**Answer: Right-click a thing to see what you can do with it**
