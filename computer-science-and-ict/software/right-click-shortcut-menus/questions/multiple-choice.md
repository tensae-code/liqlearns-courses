# Right-Click Shortcut Menus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a right-click open?**
a) A shortcut menu  b) A short list of orders that belong to the thing you clicked  c) Arranging the desktop itself  d) The menu for the computer itself and its drives
**Answer: a) A shortcut menu**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a shortcut menu?**
a) The desktop menu  b) A short list of orders that belong to the thing you clicked  c) A menu of orders that act on that file or folder  d) In the Recycle Bin
**Answer: b) A short list of orders that belong to the thing you clicked**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does right-clicking the desktop give?**
a) Arranging the desktop itself  b) The menu for the computer itself and its drives  c) The desktop menu  d) It is restored or removed for good
**Answer: c) The desktop menu**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the desktop menu for?**
a) A menu of orders that act on that file or folder  b) In the Recycle Bin  c) The taskbar menu  d) Arranging the desktop itself
**Answer: d) Arranging the desktop itself**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does right-clicking a file or folder give?**
a) A menu of orders that act on that file or folder  b) The menu for the computer itself and its drives  c) It is restored or removed for good  d) Right-click a thing to see what you can do with it
**Answer: a) A menu of orders that act on that file or folder**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does right-clicking This PC open?**
a) In the Recycle Bin  b) The menu for the computer itself and its drives  c) The taskbar menu  d) A shortcut menu
**Answer: b) The menu for the computer itself and its drives**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where do deleted files wait?**
a) It is restored or removed for good  b) Right-click a thing to see what you can do with it  c) In the Recycle Bin  d) A short list of orders that belong to the thing you clicked
**Answer: c) In the Recycle Bin**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can happen to a file in the Recycle Bin?**
a) The taskbar menu  b) A shortcut menu  c) The desktop menu  d) It is restored or removed for good
**Answer: d) It is restored or removed for good**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does right-clicking the taskbar open?**
a) The taskbar menu  b) Right-click a thing to see what you can do with it  c) A short list of orders that belong to the thing you clicked  d) Arranging the desktop itself
**Answer: a) The taskbar menu**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What one habit serves everywhere?**
a) A shortcut menu  b) Right-click a thing to see what you can do with it  c) The desktop menu  d) A menu of orders that act on that file or folder
**Answer: b) Right-click a thing to see what you can do with it**
