# Hands-on — Adding a Keyboard Language

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Add and switch
On a real computer, open the language settings, look at the languages already installed, and add a second language (or follow the steps as far as your machine allows). Switch to it, type your name, switch back, and type it again.

## Read the bar
Sit at a computer and watch only the language bar while a partner changes the language without telling you. Before each line they type, say which language is active — from the bar alone.
