# Adding a Keyboard Language — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What sets the letters a key produces?**
**Answer: The keyboard language Windows is using**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Can a computer hold more than one keyboard language?**
**Answer: Yes — several at once, and you switch between them**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which language does Afghanistan's book add?**
**Answer: Dari**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is this taught as its own lesson in Afghanistan?**
**Answer: Because adding Dari makes the computer usable in the country's own language**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Through which settings is a language added in the book's Windows XP?**
**Answer: Regional and Language Options in the Control Panel**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do you choose when you add a language?**
**Answer: The language and its keyboard layout, then confirm**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What shows the active language on the taskbar?**
**Answer: The language bar**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do you switch between your languages?**
**Answer: By clicking the language bar or with its key combination, Alt+Shift in the book's Windows**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What should you check before you type?**
**Answer: The language bar — the active language, not the letters printed on the keys**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What follows the active language — the program or the keys?**
**Answer: The program follows the language that is active**
