# Hands-on — Windows and Dialog Boxes — First Steps

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Name the frame', 'Open any program: name its window's title bar and its three working buttons, and say what each button does before you touch any of them.')

## Task two
('Answer the small window', "Make a dialog box appear (for example by starting to save or close something): read what it asks, name its buttons, then cancel it safely and say what your answer would have done.")
