# Unix and Mac Operating Systems — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which two systems complete the guide's map?**
**Answer: Unix and the Macintosh system**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is Unix built for?**
**Answer: Many users and many tasks at once on one machine**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does Unix mainly work?**
**Answer: By the command line**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is Unix famous for?**
**Answer: Stability on servers and large machines**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which later system was made in Unix's image?**
**Answer: Linux**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name two Unix ideas in modern systems.**
**Answer: Files in a tree, and permissions for each user**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Whose system is the Mac system?**
**Answer: Apple's — for its own computers**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What work is the Mac known for?**
**Answer: Design, publishing, picture and sound work**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How are Unix and Mac a family?**
**Answer: The modern Mac system is built on a Unix heart**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which source teaches this lesson?**
