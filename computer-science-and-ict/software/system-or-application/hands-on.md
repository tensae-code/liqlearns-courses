# Hands-on — System or application? Telling software apart

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Sort the shelf
Write eight program names on cards — include an operating system, a word processor, a game, a browser, a utility, a spreadsheet. Sort them into two piles using the three questions, and read your reasons aloud.

## Background hunt
While a computer is used, one person watches for signs of the background worker: the clock keeping time, files being managed, the start-up itself. List three signs of system software at work.
