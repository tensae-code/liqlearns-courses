# The Desktop, Taskbar and Start Menu — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first screen after Windows starts?**
**Answer: The desktop**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is an icon?**
**Answer: A small picture with a name — a door to a program or file**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How do you open a program from its desktop icon?**
**Answer: Double-click the icon**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does Windows Search do?**
**Answer: Finds programs and files by name**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name a built-in program the guide opens from the Start menu.**
**Answer: The Calculator**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where is the taskbar?**
**Answer: Along the bottom of the desktop**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What sits at the start of the taskbar?**
**Answer: The Start button**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the taskbar show for every open program?**
**Answer: A button for it, so you can jump between programs**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What three things sit at the end of the taskbar?**
**Answer: The clock, the date and the language indicator**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What opens when you click Start?**
**Answer: The Start menu — programs are opened from it, and the computer is shut down from it**
