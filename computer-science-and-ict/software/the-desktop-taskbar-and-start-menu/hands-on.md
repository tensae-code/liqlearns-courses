# Hands-on — The Desktop, Taskbar and Start Menu

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Desktop tour
On a real desktop, name aloud: three icons, the taskbar, the Start button, the clock, the date and the language indicator. Then open the Calculator from the Start menu.

## Jump between programs
Open two programs. Use their taskbar buttons to jump from one to the other three times, then find both programs again in the Start menu.
