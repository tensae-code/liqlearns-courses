# Using DOS Commands — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the command prompt?**
**Answer: DOS's working face — drive, path and a waiting cursor**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is work done at the prompt?**
**Answer: Type the command's name, its subject, and Enter — exactly**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does TREE show?**
**Answer: The directory tree — the disk's folders as branches**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does DATE do?**
**Answer: Shows and sets the machine's date**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) What does DEL do — and its danger?**
**Answer: Deletes a named file, with no recycle bin to forgive a slip**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does DISKCOPY copy?**
**Answer: A whole floppy disk to another, track by track**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does FORMAT do?**
**Answer: Erases a disk completely and lays a fresh empty structure**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the standing rule for FORMAT?**
**Answer: Only for a disk whose contents are known and finished with**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do the commands divide by job?**
**Answer: Commands that show, commands that change, and commands that prepare**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) Which source teaches this lesson?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 10 ICT teacher guide, supplementary appendix (teacher guide — evidence)**
