# Hands-on — The Desktop and Its Icons

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name your desktop
On a real computer or a drawing of one, point to the desktop, the taskbar, the Start button and three icons. Say what each icon opens.

## Tidy-up plan
List five things you would keep as desktop shortcuts and five you would file in folders instead. Say why, in one sentence each.
