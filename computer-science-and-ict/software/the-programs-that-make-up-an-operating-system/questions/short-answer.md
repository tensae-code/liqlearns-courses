# The Programs That Make Up an Operating System — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Is an operating system one program?**
**Answer: No — a set of programs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What starts the system at switch-on?**
**Answer: The loader**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the kernel manage?**
**Answer: Memory, the processor's time and the devices**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What speaks to each device?**
**Answer: A driver**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does this lesson sit in the book?**
**Answer: Right after the concept of an operating system**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the loader bring in?**
**Answer: The kernel**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the kernel start next?**
**Answer: The drivers and the interface**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do programs ask the kernel for?**
**Answer: Memory, time and devices**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two things does the kernel do with them?**
**Answer: Shares them fairly and guards them**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is booting, once the parts are named?**
**Answer: A queue of programs, each starting the next**
