# The Programs That Make Up an Operating System — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Is an operating system one program?**
a) No — a set of programs  b) The loader  c) Memory, the processor's time and the devices  d) A driver
**Answer: a) No — a set of programs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What starts the system at switch-on?**
a) Memory, the processor's time and the devices  b) The loader  c) A driver  d) Right after the concept of an operating system
**Answer: b) The loader**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the kernel manage?**
a) A driver  b) Right after the concept of an operating system  c) Memory, the processor's time and the devices  d) The kernel
**Answer: c) Memory, the processor's time and the devices**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What speaks to each device?**
a) Right after the concept of an operating system  b) The kernel  c) The drivers and the interface  d) A driver
**Answer: d) A driver**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does this lesson sit in the book?**
a) Right after the concept of an operating system  b) The kernel  c) The drivers and the interface  d) Memory, time and devices
**Answer: a) Right after the concept of an operating system**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the loader bring in?**
a) The drivers and the interface  b) The kernel  c) Memory, time and devices  d) Shares them fairly and guards them
**Answer: b) The kernel**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the kernel start next?**
a) Memory, time and devices  b) Shares them fairly and guards them  c) The drivers and the interface  d) A queue of programs, each starting the next
**Answer: c) The drivers and the interface**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do programs ask the kernel for?**
a) Shares them fairly and guards them  b) A queue of programs, each starting the next  c) No — a set of programs  d) Memory, time and devices
**Answer: d) Memory, time and devices**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two things does the kernel do with them?**
a) Shares them fairly and guards them  b) A queue of programs, each starting the next  c) No — a set of programs  d) The loader
**Answer: a) Shares them fairly and guards them**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is booting, once the parts are named?**
a) No — a set of programs  b) A queue of programs, each starting the next  c) The loader  d) Memory, the processor's time and the devices
**Answer: b) A queue of programs, each starting the next**
