# Hands-on — Multitasking — Running Several Programs at Once

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Run three at once', 'Open three programs. Switch among them with Alt+Tab only, doing one small task in each. Write which was active at each task and how you knew.')

## Task two
('Ferry the picture', 'Draw a small picture in Paint, copy it into a Word document about the picture, and save the document. State which program owns the picture now, and in what sense.')
