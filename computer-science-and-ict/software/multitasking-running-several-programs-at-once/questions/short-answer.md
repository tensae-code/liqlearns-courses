# Multitasking — Running Several Programs at Once — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a multitasking operating system do?**
**Answer: Runs several programs at once, sharing the machine's time among them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does each open program keep?**
**Answer: Its window, its work and its place**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the active window?**
**Answer: The one that takes the keyboard and stands in front**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is an inactive program closed?**
**Answer: No — it holds its work, waiting**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What switch cycles the windows?**
**Answer: Alt+Tab**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does work travel between programs?**
**Answer: By copying and pasting — the clipboard is the ferry**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's own inter-program journey?**
**Answer: A picture from Paint copied into a Word document**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What should you name before typing?**
**Answer: Your active window**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are most multi-program accidents?**
**Answer: One program receiving another's commands**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who owns the work in your hands — why must you know?**
**Answer: The program it was made in — its tools and saves are that program's**
