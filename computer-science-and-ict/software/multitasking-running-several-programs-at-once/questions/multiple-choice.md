# Multitasking — Running Several Programs at Once — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a multitasking operating system do?**
a) Runs several programs at once, sharing the machine's time among them  b) Its window, its work and its place  c) The one that takes the keyboard and stands in front  d) No — it holds its work, waiting
**Answer: a) Runs several programs at once, sharing the machine's time among them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does each open program keep?**
a) The one that takes the keyboard and stands in front  b) Its window, its work and its place  c) No — it holds its work, waiting  d) Alt+Tab
**Answer: b) Its window, its work and its place**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the active window?**
a) No — it holds its work, waiting  b) Alt+Tab  c) The one that takes the keyboard and stands in front  d) By copying and pasting — the clipboard is the ferry
**Answer: c) The one that takes the keyboard and stands in front**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is an inactive program closed?**
a) Alt+Tab  b) By copying and pasting — the clipboard is the ferry  c) A picture from Paint copied into a Word document  d) No — it holds its work, waiting
**Answer: d) No — it holds its work, waiting**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What switch cycles the windows?**
a) Alt+Tab  b) By copying and pasting — the clipboard is the ferry  c) A picture from Paint copied into a Word document  d) Your active window
**Answer: a) Alt+Tab**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does work travel between programs?**
a) A picture from Paint copied into a Word document  b) By copying and pasting — the clipboard is the ferry  c) Your active window  d) One program receiving another's commands
**Answer: b) By copying and pasting — the clipboard is the ferry**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's own inter-program journey?**
a) Your active window  b) One program receiving another's commands  c) A picture from Paint copied into a Word document  d) The program it was made in — its tools and saves are that program's
**Answer: c) A picture from Paint copied into a Word document**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What should you name before typing?**
a) One program receiving another's commands  b) The program it was made in — its tools and saves are that program's  c) Runs several programs at once, sharing the machine's time among them  d) Your active window
**Answer: d) Your active window**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are most multi-program accidents?**
a) One program receiving another's commands  b) The program it was made in — its tools and saves are that program's  c) Runs several programs at once, sharing the machine's time among them  d) Its window, its work and its place
**Answer: a) One program receiving another's commands**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who owns the work in your hands — why must you know?**
a) Runs several programs at once, sharing the machine's time among them  b) The program it was made in — its tools and saves are that program's  c) Its window, its work and its place  d) The one that takes the keyboard and stands in front
**Answer: b) The program it was made in — its tools and saves are that program's**
