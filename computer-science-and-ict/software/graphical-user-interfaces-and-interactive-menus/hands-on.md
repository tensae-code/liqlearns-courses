# Hands-on — Graphical User Interfaces and Interactive Menus

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the surface
Draw a small program window: title, two icons, a cursor, one button, a menu of three choices. Label every GUI object.

## Menu in words
Write a menu for a school program: four choices, and the action each choice runs. Swap with a companion and follow their menu as its user.
