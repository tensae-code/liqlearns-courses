# The Parts of a Window — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What holds a window in?**
**Answer: Its frame — the border around it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What runs across the top of a window?**
**Answer: The title bar, showing the window's name**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the close button do?**
**Answer: Shuts the window**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does minimise do?**
**Answer: Hides the window down to the taskbar**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does maximise do, and what does its button become?**
**Answer: Fills the whole screen; the button becomes Restore**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Restore do?**
**Answer: Brings the window back to its former size**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What sits under the title bar?**
**Answer: The menu bar — menus holding the program's commands**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where are the scroll bars?**
**Answer: At the right edge and the bottom edge**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are scroll bars for?**
**Answer: Moving through work too big for the window**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the actual work happen in a window?**
**Answer: In the work area — the large middle**
