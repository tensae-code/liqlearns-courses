# The Parts of a Window — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What holds a window in?**
a) Its frame — the border around it  b) Hides the window down to the taskbar  c) Brings the window back to its former size  d) At the right edge and the bottom edge
**Answer: a) Its frame — the border around it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What runs across the top of a window?**
a) Fills the whole screen; the button becomes Restore  b) The title bar, showing the window's name  c) The menu bar — menus holding the program's commands  d) Moving through work too big for the window
**Answer: b) The title bar, showing the window's name**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the close button do?**
a) Brings the window back to its former size  b) At the right edge and the bottom edge  c) Shuts the window  d) In the work area — the large middle
**Answer: c) Shuts the window**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does minimise do?**
a) The menu bar — menus holding the program's commands  b) Moving through work too big for the window  c) Its frame — the border around it  d) Hides the window down to the taskbar
**Answer: d) Hides the window down to the taskbar**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does maximise do, and what does its button become?**
a) Fills the whole screen; the button becomes Restore  b) At the right edge and the bottom edge  c) In the work area — the large middle  d) The title bar, showing the window's name
**Answer: a) Fills the whole screen; the button becomes Restore**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Restore do?**
a) Moving through work too big for the window  b) Brings the window back to its former size  c) Its frame — the border around it  d) Shuts the window
**Answer: b) Brings the window back to its former size**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What sits under the title bar?**
a) In the work area — the large middle  b) The title bar, showing the window's name  c) The menu bar — menus holding the program's commands  d) Hides the window down to the taskbar
**Answer: c) The menu bar — menus holding the program's commands**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where are the scroll bars?**
a) Its frame — the border around it  b) Shuts the window  c) Fills the whole screen; the button becomes Restore  d) At the right edge and the bottom edge
**Answer: d) At the right edge and the bottom edge**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are scroll bars for?**
a) Moving through work too big for the window  b) The title bar, showing the window's name  c) Hides the window down to the taskbar  d) Brings the window back to its former size
**Answer: a) Moving through work too big for the window**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the actual work happen in a window?**
a) Shuts the window  b) In the work area — the large middle  c) Fills the whole screen; the button becomes Restore  d) The menu bar — menus holding the program's commands
**Answer: b) In the work area — the large middle**
