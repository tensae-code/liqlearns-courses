# The Parts of a Window — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What holds a window in?" is: Its frame — the border around it**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What runs across the top of a window?" is: Shuts the window**
**Answer: False** — The title bar, showing the window's name

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the close button do?" is: Shuts the window**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does minimise do?" is: Fills the whole screen; the button becomes Restore**
**Answer: False** — Hides the window down to the taskbar

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does maximise do, and what does its button become?" is: Fills the whole screen; the button becomes Restore**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does Restore do?" is: The menu bar — menus holding the program's commands**
**Answer: False** — Brings the window back to its former size

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What sits under the title bar?" is: The menu bar — menus holding the program's commands**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where are the scroll bars?" is: Moving through work too big for the window**
**Answer: False** — At the right edge and the bottom edge

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What are scroll bars for?" is: Moving through work too big for the window**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does the actual work happen in a window?" is: Its frame — the border around it**
**Answer: False** — In the work area — the large middle
