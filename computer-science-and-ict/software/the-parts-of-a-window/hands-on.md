# Hands-on — The Parts of a Window

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Window hunt
Open a program. Point to and name: frame, title bar, close, minimise, maximise, menu bar, both scroll bars, work area.

## Shrink and restore
Maximise the window, restore it, minimise it to the taskbar and bring it back with its taskbar button. Say what each button did as you press it.
