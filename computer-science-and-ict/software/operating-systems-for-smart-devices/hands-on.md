# Hands-on — Operating Systems for Smart Devices

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the manager
On a phone or tablet you know, find where its operating system is named and which version it is. Write down two jobs it is doing right now.

## With and without
List what a smartphone could still do with no operating system at all. Compare the list with what it does with one — the difference is the operating system's work.
