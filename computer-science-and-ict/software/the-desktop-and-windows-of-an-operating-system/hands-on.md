# Hands-on — The Desktop and Windows of an Operating System

The lesson with your hands, on a real computer where one is available.

## Read the desktop
Look at a started computer. Write down: what the background shows, the name under three icons, and what sits at the left, middle and right of the taskbar. Beside each icon, write what you think opens when it is double-clicked — then check one of them.

## Window moves drill
Open one window. Do each move and tick it off: move it by the title bar; make it wider by an edge; make it smaller by a corner; maximise it; restore it; minimise it to the taskbar; bring it back from the taskbar.

## Two windows, one desk
Open two windows. Put them side by side so half of each is visible. Say out loud which one is at the front, swap them, and say how you know which is in front now.
