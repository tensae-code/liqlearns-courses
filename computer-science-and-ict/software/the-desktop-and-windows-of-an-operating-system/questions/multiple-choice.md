# The Desktop and Windows of an Operating System — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The desktop is —**
a) the wooden table the computer sits on  b) the operating system's home screen  c) one program for drawing
**Answer: b) the operating system's home screen** — It is the screen you land on when the system has started.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) An icon on the desktop is —**
a) a small picture that stands for a program, file or folder, and opens it when double-clicked  b) a warning sign  c) the background picture
**Answer: a) a small picture that stands for a program, file or folder**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The taskbar —**
a) sits across the top of a window  b) runs along the bottom of the screen and shows what is open  c) is the background
**Answer: b) runs along the bottom of the screen and shows what is open**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The title bar of a window —**
a) names the window and carries its control buttons  b) is where you type your document  c) closes the computer
**Answer: a) names the window and carries its control buttons**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Minimise —**
a) deletes the window  b) sends the window down to the taskbar, still open  c) fills the screen with the window
**Answer: b) sends the window down to the taskbar, still open**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) To move a window you drag —**
a) its middle  b) an icon on the desktop  c) its title bar
**Answer: c) its title bar** — Edges and corners are for resizing, not moving.
