# The Desktop and Windows of an Operating System — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three parts of the desktop and the job of each.**
**Answer:** The background (the picture or colour behind everything), the icons (small pictures that open programs, files and folders) and the taskbar (the strip at the bottom that shows what is open).

**2. (Sub lesson 2) How do Minimise and Close differ?**
**Answer:** Minimise takes the window off the screen to the taskbar but leaves it open to return to; Close shuts the window and ends that job on the screen (without deleting the file).

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Two windows are open and the one you need is behind. Give two ways to bring it to the front.**
**Answer:** Click any visible part of the window behind, or click its button on the taskbar.
