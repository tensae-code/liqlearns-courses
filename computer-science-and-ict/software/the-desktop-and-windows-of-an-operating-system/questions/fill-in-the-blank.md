# The Desktop and Windows of an Operating System — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The picture or colour spread across the whole desktop, behind everything else, is the ____.**
**Answer: background**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The bar across the top of a window that names it is the ____ bar.**
**Answer: title**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The button that makes a window fill the whole screen is ____.**
**Answer: Maximise**
