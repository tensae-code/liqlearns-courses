# Video script — The Start Menu and Its Options

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bottom left corner. One round button: Start.

Click it. Or press the Windows key. The menu opens.

Left side: programs. Right side: your places — Documents, Pictures, Computer.

Bottom: a search box. Type a name. The program appears.

All Programs shows everything.

Shut down turns the computer off the right way.

The small arrow holds more: lock your work, log off, restart, sleep.

Pick the one that fits the job.
