# The Start Menu and Its Options — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Pressing the ____ key opens the Start menu.**
**Answer: Windows**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) To see every installed program, click ____ Programs.**
**Answer: All**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The option that switches the computer off and on again in one move is ____.**
**Answer: Restart**
