# The Start Menu and Its Options — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the two sides of the Start menu and one thing on each.**
**Answer:** The left side is programs (for example a pinned program, or All Programs at the bottom); the right side is your places (for example Documents, Computer or Control Panel).

**2. (Sub lesson 2) How do Lock and Log off differ?**
**Answer:** Lock keeps your session and programs open behind your password; Log off closes your session and programs, leaving the computer on for someone else.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the Shut down button better than switching off at the power socket?**
**Answer:** Shut down lets Windows close programs and files in order first; cutting the power can lose work and damage files.
