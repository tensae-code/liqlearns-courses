# The Start Menu and Its Options — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the Start button in Windows 7?**
a) Top-right corner  b) Bottom-left corner  c) Middle of the screen
**Answer: b) Bottom-left corner** — Start sits at the bottom-left of the taskbar.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which keys open the Start menu without the mouse?**
a) Alt+Tab  b) The Windows key, or Ctrl+Esc  c) Shift+Enter
**Answer: b) The Windows key, or Ctrl+Esc** — Both open the same menu as clicking Start.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The right side of the Start menu holds —**
a) only games  b) your places: Documents, Pictures, Music, Computer, Control Panel, Help and Support  c) deleted files
**Answer: b) your places** — The left side is programs; the right side is places.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) All Programs shows —**
a) the full list of everything installed, in folders  b) only today's programs  c) only pinned programs
**Answer: a) the full list of everything installed, in folders**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) You step away for five minutes and your work must stay open but safe. You choose —**
a) Shut down  b) Log off  c) Lock
**Answer: c) Lock** — Lock guards the session behind your password and leaves your work open.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Sleep is —**
a) switching the computer fully off  b) a low-power pause that wakes fast with your work as you left it  c) deleting your session
**Answer: b) a low-power pause that wakes fast**
