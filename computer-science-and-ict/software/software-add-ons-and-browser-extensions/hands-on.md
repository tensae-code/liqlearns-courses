# Hands-on — Software Add-ons and Browser Extensions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Audit the browser', 'List every add-on or extension in a browser you can use. Beside each, write its job in five words or fewer. Mark any you cannot give a job to — those are the lesson's removal candidates.')

## Task two
('Install on paper', 'Write the install walkthrough for one named add-on (a download helper) as numbered steps, then the disable steps and the remove steps under it, so the three are never confused.')
