# Software Add-ons and Browser Extensions — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an add-on?**
**Answer: A small program that adds a feature to a bigger program**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the bigger program called?**
**Answer: The host**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are browser add-ons called?**
**Answer: Extensions (plug-ins in the book's wording)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name one add-on from Rwanda's book.**
**Answer: Download Helper**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does an ad blocker do?**
**Answer: Strips adverts from pages**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where is a Firefox add-on installed from?**
**Answer: The browser's add-ons page**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are the steps to install, in the book's order?**
**Answer: Search the name, choose it, and confirm**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the difference between disabling and removing?**
**Answer: Disabling switches it off but keeps it; removing takes it out completely**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where are add-ons disabled or removed?**
**Answer: From the same add-ons page**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the book's rule of sense for add-ons?**
**Answer: Install only ones you can name a job for**
