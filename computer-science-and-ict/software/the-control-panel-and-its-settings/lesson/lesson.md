# The Control Panel and Its Settings

## Do it first

Every setting Windows owns sleeps in one room. In this lesson you learn the Control Panel — the room where the computer itself is adjusted.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Control Panel — one door to all settings

The **Control Panel** is the place Windows gathers **settings** — the choices that decide how the computer itself behaves and looks. Open it from the Start menu, and its window shows a set of icons (or categories), one per area of the machine: display, mouse, keyboard, sound, date and time, printers, user accounts, and more. Afghanistan's Grade 12 book teaches the Control Panel as its own lesson, late in the course, because by now the learner has met many of its rooms one by one — a language added here, a program removed there — and is ready to see the whole workshop at once: nothing about the computer's own setup lives anywhere else.

## Sub lesson 2 — What its settings cover

Walk the workshop: **Display** sets the wallpaper, screen saver and resolution; **Mouse** and **Keyboard** set those tools' behaviour; **Date and Time** keeps the clock true; **Sounds** chooses what the machine says and how loudly at the system level; **User Accounts** makes and guards the people's accounts; **Add or Remove Programs** installs and removes software — the correct way to remove a program, never by deleting its folder. The workshop's one rule is respect: a setting is a lever on the whole machine, so change one thing at a time, remember what it was, and put it back if the change was wrong. A learner who knows the Control Panel does not fear a strange computer: every unfamiliar machine is adjusted from the same room.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: The Control Panel is Windows' settings workshop: from its one window you reach the settings for the display, the mouse, the keyboard, sound, dates, user accounts, and adding or removing programs. Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.
