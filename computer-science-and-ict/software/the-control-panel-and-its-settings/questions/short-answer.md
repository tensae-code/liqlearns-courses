# The Control Panel and Its Settings — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Control Panel?**
**Answer: The place Windows gathers the settings for how the computer itself behaves and looks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is it opened?**
**Answer: From the Start menu**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does its window show?**
**Answer: Icons or categories, one per area of the machine**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book teach it late, in Grade 12?**
**Answer: By then the learner has met many of its rooms one by one and can see the whole workshop at once**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the computer's own setup live, besides here?**
**Answer: Nowhere else — nothing about the setup lives anywhere else**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Display set?**
**Answer: The wallpaper, screen saver and resolution**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the correct way to remove a program?**
**Answer: Add or Remove Programs — never by deleting its folder**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do User Accounts do there?**
**Answer: Make and guard the people's accounts**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the workshop's one rule?**
**Answer: Respect — change one thing at a time, remember what it was, and put it back if wrong**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does knowing the Control Panel remove fear of a strange computer?**
**Answer: Every unfamiliar machine is adjusted from the same room**
