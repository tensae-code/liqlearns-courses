# Hands-on — The Control Panel and Its Settings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tour the workshop
Open the Control Panel on a real computer and make a map of it: one line per icon or category, saying what it adjusts. Mark the three you have already used in other lessons without knowing they lived here.

## One change, remembered
With leave, change one harmless setting — the wallpaper or the screen saver — noting the original first. Live with it through a short task, then decide and either keep it or put it back, writing what you changed and how you restored it.
