The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

We install the guard.

Step one. Search for Avast anti-virus. Download the exe file.
Step two. Open Downloads. Double-click the exe.
Step three. The box appears. Follow it. Click Run.
Step four. Wait. A few minutes.

Installed. Now the computer is guarded.
