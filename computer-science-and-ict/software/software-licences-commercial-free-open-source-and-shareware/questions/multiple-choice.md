# Software Licences — Commercial, Free, Open-Source and Shareware — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a software licence?**
a) The maker's written permission saying what a user may do with a program  b) Cost, copying/sharing, seeing and changing the code, and how long it may be used (any three)  c) Piracy — whatever the copy cost  d) Software that is sold; its licence is bought and copying/sharing are forbidden
**Answer: a) The maker's written permission saying what a user may do with a program**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What four questions does every licence answer?**
a) Piracy — whatever the copy cost  b) Cost, copying/sharing, seeing and changing the code, and how long it may be used (any three)  c) Software that is sold; its licence is bought and copying/sharing are forbidden  d) Software given at no cost, usable without payment on its maker's terms
**Answer: b) Cost, copying/sharing, seeing and changing the code, and how long it may be used (any three)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Using software outside its licence is called what?**
a) Software that is sold; its licence is bought and copying/sharing are forbidden  b) Software given at no cost, usable without payment on its maker's terms  c) Piracy — whatever the copy cost  d) Its source code is open to study and change, and changed versions may be shared under the licence
**Answer: c) Piracy — whatever the copy cost**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is commercial software?**
a) Software given at no cost, usable without payment on its maker's terms  b) Its source code is open to study and change, and changed versions may be shared under the licence  c) Software given to try for a limited time or with limits, after which the full licence must be bought  d) Software that is sold; its licence is bought and copying/sharing are forbidden
**Answer: d) Software that is sold; its licence is bought and copying/sharing are forbidden**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is free software?**
a) Software given at no cost, usable without payment on its maker's terms  b) Its source code is open to study and change, and changed versions may be shared under the licence  c) Software given to try for a limited time or with limits, after which the full licence must be bought  d) Commercial, trial, free, open-source (tightest to most open as taught)
**Answer: a) Software given at no cost, usable without payment on its maker's terms**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What extra freedom does open-source add?**
a) Software given to try for a limited time or with limits, after which the full licence must be bought  b) Its source code is open to study and change, and changed versions may be shared under the licence  c) Commercial, trial, free, open-source (tightest to most open as taught)  d) No — the licence, not the copy, fixes what you may do
**Answer: b) Its source code is open to study and change, and changed versions may be shared under the licence**
